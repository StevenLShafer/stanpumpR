The **Time** card above the dose table chooses how times are entered and displayed. The simulation is identical either way.

## Elapsed minutes

Everything is counted from zero. The dose table and the plot's time axis are in minutes.

## Actual time

Enter a **Procedure start** as `HH:MM` (24-hour clock). Times in the dose table are then clock times, and the plot's time axis is labelled with the clock. A dose at `09:15` with a procedure start of `08:30` is 45 minutes into the simulation.

When you switch from clock time to elapsed minutes, any clock times in the table are converted to minutes from the procedure start. Switching the other way does not convert existing minutes; enter clock times as you go.

The procedure start defaults to the time on your computer when the app opened.

## Max time

The length of the simulation is set under **Graph Options → Max time**, from one hour to one year, and is independent of the time display.
