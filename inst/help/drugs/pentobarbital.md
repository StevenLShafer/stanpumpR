### The model

From a 2026 study of critically ill children given intravenous pentobarbital (*J Clin Pharmacol*, doi:10.1002/jcph.70204): two compartments, allometric on body weight, with a clearance of 5.21 L/h, a central volume of 37.4 L, an intercompartmental clearance of 18.1 L/h and a peripheral volume of 63.9 L at 70 kg. No covariate beyond weight was kept.

### Route and extrapolation

Intravenous only (`mg`, `mg/kg`, `mg/hr`, `mg/kg/hr`), as pentobarbital is used for refractory status epilepticus as a titrated infusion. The model is paediatric; an adult receives the 70 kg allometric values, which sit at the high end of adult clearance, so an adult's levels during a long barbiturate coma may be underpredicted. A second paediatric model (Ketharanathan and colleagues, *Clin Pharmacokinet* 2023) needs C-reactive protein, which the app does not take, and is not used.

### Band

20 to 40 mg/L, the concentrations reported during barbiturate coma — orientation only, since titration is to the electroencephalogram, not to a level. There is no effect site.
