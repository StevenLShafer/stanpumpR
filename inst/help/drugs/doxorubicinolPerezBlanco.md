### The model

Doxorubicinol formed from [doxorubicinPerezBlanco](help:drugs/doxorubicinPerezBlanco) (Pérez-Blanco and colleagues, *Br J Clin Pharmacol* 2016;82:1517-1527), plotted in its own panel. It cannot be dosed: 22% of doxorubicin's clearance forms it, mass for mass, and two compartments describe it, with a clearance of 26.8 L/h, volumes of 79.8 and 653 L (both fixed in the paper) and an intercompartmental clearance of 424 L/h. Its half-lives are 7 minutes and 20 hours. It was fitted jointly with the parent, so the two curves belong together; it is not the library's [doxorubicinol](help:drugs/doxorubicinol), which goes with Joerger's doxorubicin model.

No therapeutic range of doxorubicinol is established. The parameters were read in the paper's PubMed Central text; the PDF is still to confirm them.
