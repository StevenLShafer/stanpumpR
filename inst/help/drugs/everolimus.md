### The model

Everolimus's parameters come from the population model of the FDA's clinical pharmacology review of Afinitor (NDA 22-334, 2009): 398 patients with advanced solid tumours taking 5-10 mg daily, 1,667 whole-blood concentrations, two compartments. The sponsor's model carried a factor lowering clearance in renal-cell cancer, which its own sensitivity analysis attributed to inconsistent trough measurements; the FDA reviewer's refit without it is used. Apparent oral parameters: clearance 14.8 L/h, central volume 172 L, intercompartmental clearance 48 L/h, peripheral volume 495 L, absorption 6.07 per hour (fixed). The patients sampled intensively in the registration trial had an apparent clearance of 15.4 L/h; healthy subjects cleared more, 20.5 L/h. No weight effect was found, so the values are scaled from the library's reference man.

### Transplantation and cyclosporine

In transplantation (Zortress) everolimus is given with reduced-dose cyclosporine or tacrolimus. The source's patients took neither. Cyclosporine raised everolimus's AUC by 168% in healthy subjects, and the Zortress label's kidney transplant patients on cyclosporine had an apparent clearance of 8.8 L/h, so with cyclosporine the concentration is about 1.7 times what this model shows. Other CYP3A4 and P-glycoprotein inhibitors raise it further (ketoconazole fifteen-fold); inducers lower it. Age (0.33% less clearance a year), black race (20% more), liver disease and haematocrit are not modelled.

### The band

The band is the Zortress label's therapeutic range, 3-8 ng/mL, a whole-blood trough by LC/MS/MS with reduced-exposure cyclosporine or tacrolimus, drawn against the whole curve.

### Dosing

Everolimus is dosed as itself: Zortress tablets of 0.25, 0.5, 0.75 and 1 mg, usually 0.75 or 1 mg twice daily; Afinitor 10 mg daily.
