### The model

9-Hydroxyrisperidone is the active metabolite of [risperidone](help:drugs/risperidone), from the same analysis (Størset and colleagues, *Eur J Clin Pharmacol* 2024;80:1531-1541). One compartment: volume 96 L and clearance 8.0 L/h, falling by 1.3% a year above 39. The half-life is about 8 hours. The age term was checked against the paper; the volume and clearance come from the brief.

### Formed only

Neither risperidone's bioavailability nor the fraction converted to the metabolite could be identified, so these parameters are divided by both, and the whole of risperidone's apparent clearance feeds the metabolite on that scale. That reproduces the concentrations formed from oral risperidone and nothing else. 9-Hydroxyrisperidone is paliperidone, but paliperidone given as itself has its own disposition, which these parameters are not; so this entry has **no dosing unit**. See [Active metabolites](help:models/metabolites).

### Effect

No effect site and no band. D2 occupancy is a function of the risperidone and 9-hydroxyrisperidone concentrations added together; see [risperidone](help:drugs/risperidone).
