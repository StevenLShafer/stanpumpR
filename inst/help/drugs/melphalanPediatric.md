### The model

High-dose melphalan in children comes from Duong and colleagues (*Br J Clin Pharmacol* 2019;85:136-146): 51 children aged 1.7 to 8.3 years with high-risk neuroblastoma, given melphalan over 15 to 30 minutes on three days before autologous transplantation (with carboplatin and etoposide). Two compartments describe it, scaled by weight alone:

- at 15.5 kg, clearance 8.9 L/h, central volume 6.8 L, and a peripheral compartment of 4.6 L exchanging at 4.1 L/h;
- half-lives of 18 minutes and 1.4 hours.

The clearances scale with weight to the ¾ power and the volumes in proportion, about a reference weight of 15.5 kg (see [carboplatinPediatric](help:drugs/carboplatinPediatric) for why). The adults' melphalan is [melphalan](help:drugs/melphalan) (Nath 2010).

### What is plotted

Plasma melphalan in mcg/mL (mg/L). Doses are entered in mg or as an infusion rate. There is no effect site and no band.

### Where to be careful

Weight is the only covariate: renal function, age and sex were tested and dropped. Children 1.7 to 8.3 years. Not a monitoring tool.

Every parameter was read in the paper's PDF.
