### The model

dFdU (2′,2′-difluorodeoxyuridine), the inactive deaminated metabolite of gemcitabine, is formed from [gemcitabine](help:drugs/gemcitabine) and is not dosed. Tham and colleagues (*Cancer Chemother Pharmacol* 2008;63:55-64) modelled it in one compartment receiving 73% of gemcitabine's clearance: volume 52.6 L and clearance 21.5 L/h for a 70-kg, 1.76-m man, scaled by lean body mass. Its half-life is 1.7 hours.

### What is plotted

Plasma dFdU in mcg/mL (mg/L). There is no band.

Every parameter was read in the paper's PDF.
