### The model

Salicylic acid (salicylate) is not dosed directly. It is formed from [aspirin](help:drugs/aspirin) and shown on its own row. Its two-compartment kinetics are from Koh and colleagues (*Drug Des Devel Ther* 2025;19:7853-7863), fitted after 100 mg enteric-coated aspirin in healthy Korean adults:

- apparent clearance 2.76 L/h at the median weight, scaled by (W/68.35)^1.42;
- central volume 7.5 L;
- intercompartmental clearance 0.08 L/h and peripheral volume 1.98 L, both fixed from an earlier study.

About 20% of absorbed aspirin is converted to salicylate before reaching the circulation. The rest is converted in the plasma within minutes.

### Where to be careful

**Linear, so low dose only.** Salicylate is eliminated by saturable conjugation. At analgesic doses its clearance falls and its half-life lengthens from 2–3 hours to 15–30 hours, which this model cannot show. The model fits low-dose aspirin, where salicylate stays far below analgesic and toxic concentrations. There is no effect site and no shaded band.
