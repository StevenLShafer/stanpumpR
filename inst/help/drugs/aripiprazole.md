### The model

Oral aripiprazole and its active metabolite, from Kim J-R and colleagues (*Br J Clin Pharmacol* 2008;66:802-810): 141 steady-state plasma concentrations from 80 Korean psychiatric patients on 10 to 30 mg/day. One compartment for each species, absorption fixed at 1.06 per hour, apparent volume 192 L. Aripiprazole forms [dehydroaripiprazole](help:drugs/dehydroaripiprazole).

Only the abstract could be read. It gives the structure, the absorption rate, a volume of 192 L (the brief gives 193 L from the paper's table) and the ratio of intermediate to normal clearance. The genotype-group clearances and the metabolite parameters come from the brief and **have not yet been checked**.

### CYP 2D6

Apparent clearance is 3.15 L/h in **normal** metabolisers (two functional alleles) and 1.83 L/h in **intermediate** metabolisers. Poor and ultrarapid metabolisers were **not studied**; they take the nearest studied value, which for a poor metaboliser overstates clearance and understates exposure. Kim also reported 2.66 and 2.27 L/h for one functional allele paired with a partly or wholly non-functional one.

### Oral only

All parameters are **apparent**, so aripiprazole is offered as **mg PO** only.

### Effect site

Kim E and colleagues (*J Cereb Blood Flow Metab* 2012;32:759-768) measured striatal D2 occupancy with PET and serial plasma concentrations in 18 healthy men, and fitted an effect compartment driven by **parent** aripiprazole: ke0 0.725 per hour (a 57-minute equilibration half-time), EC50 8.63 ng/mL, Emax 100%. ke0 is supplied directly, so the effect-site curve on this row is that model's effect-site concentration, and occupancy = 100% × Ce / (8.63 + Ce): 50% at 8.63 ng/mL, 80% at 34.5 ng/mL. The EC50 was checked in the abstract; the ke0 and Emax were not. Dehydroaripiprazole must **not** be added to Ce for this calculation. The PET and pharmacokinetic studies used different populations. Occupancy is a receptor biomarker, not a dosing target, so there is no band.
