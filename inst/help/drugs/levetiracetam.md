### The model

No single retrievable model carries both intravenous disposition and an explicit renal equation, so one is assembled from sourced parts (recorded as a reduction in the registry): the structure and typical clearance from Rhee and colleagues (*Epilepsy Res* 2017;132:8-15) — one compartment, absorption fixed at 2.44/h, clearance 3.9 L/h and volume 65.3 L — with clearance split 66 per cent renal (proportional to Cockcroft-Gault creatinine clearance) and 34 per cent non-renal, the split the label and mass-balance work give.

### Routes and renal function

Oral (`mg PO`, with schedules) and intravenous (`mg`, `mg/hr`): bioavailability is complete and the injection is interchangeable mg for mg. Renal function comes from the entered creatinine (a child's on the adult scale) or an assumed normal one. The half-life is about 8 hours; levetiracetam accumulates in renal impairment, which the renal split captures, but haemodialysis is not represented. Ramael 2006 measured a clearance of 3.8 L/h after 1,500 mg; the model gives 3.9. Keppra XR is not offered.

### Band

12 to 46 mg/L (ILAE; Patsalos and colleagues, 2008).
