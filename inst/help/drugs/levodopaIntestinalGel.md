### The model

Carbidopa-levodopa enteral suspension (Duopa, Duodopa) is pumped into the jejunum through a PEG-J tube. The model is the [levodopa](help:drugs/levodopa) disposition of Othman and Dutta (*Br J Clin Pharmacol* 2014), with the gel's own absorption: through one transit compartment at 9.2 per hour, four times as fast as the tablets because the stomach is bypassed, and a bioavailability 0.97 that of the tablets (95% interval 0.95 to 0.98). The motor effect site is Mao 2013's, as for the tablets.

### How to dose it

Enter the morning dose and any extra doses as **mg PO** (or the morning dose as a rate for the 10 to 30 minutes it takes), and the continuous rate over the waking day as **mg/hr PO** (typically 40 to 120 mg/h, 2 to 6 mL/h of the 20 mg/mL gel). Both run through the transit compartment, exactly; set the rate to 0 at night. A continuous rate approaches rate × 0.97 / 24.8 L/h (2.35 mcg/mL at 60 mg/h), the last part slowly because of the slow peripheral phase.

### Threshold and band

As for the tablets: the threshold is the half-maximal motor concentration, 0.81 mcg/mL, and the band 0.9 to 2.0 mcg/mL. On the gel, levels usually sit at 1.5 to 4 mcg/mL.

### What is not in the model

Levodopa-entacapone-carbidopa gel (Lecigon) lowers levodopa's clearance by about 36% (Senek 2020) and is not modelled. Subcutaneous foslevodopa-foscarbidopa (Vyalev) has no published population model.
