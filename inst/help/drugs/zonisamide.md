### The model

From Silva and colleagues (*Eur J Pharm Sci* 2025;207:107023), 64 adults with refractory epilepsy: one compartment, apparent oral, clearance 0.761 L/h, volume 48.1 L, absorption 0.671/h.

### Caveats

The half-life this implies, about 44 hours, is shorter than the 63 to 69 hours healthy adults show (Kochak 1998): the refractory cohort was largely on enzyme-inducing drugs, which raise clearance, so a monotherapy patient's accumulation may be underpredicted. The inducer drug-load covariate is not applied (no co-medication field). Zonisamide binds saturably to red cells, so its plasma kinetics are mildly non-linear at high doses; the model, like the source, is linear. Capsule and oral suspension are label-bridged and share these parameters.

### Band

10 to 40 mg/L (ILAE; Patsalos and colleagues, 2008).
