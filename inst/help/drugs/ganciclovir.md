### The model

Ganciclovir's parameters come from Caldés and colleagues (*Antimicrob Agents Chemother* 2009;53:4816-4824), a population analysis in 20 kidney, liver and heart transplant recipients with cytomegalovirus infection (mean 66 kg, 56 years, creatinine clearance 57 mL/min), each given intravenous ganciclovir and then oral valganciclovir, with rich sampling over a dosing interval on both. Two compartments: clearance 7.49 L/h × (creatinine clearance / 57 mL/min), Cockcroft-Gault; central volume 31.9 L, peripheral volume 32.0 L, intercompartmental clearance 10.2 L/h. Weight had no effect of its own beyond the creatinine clearance, which already carries it.

### Valganciclovir

Oral valganciclovir is entered as prescribed, in milligrams of valganciclovir (**mg PO valganciclovir**, also scheduled once or twice daily), the dose the label gives (Valcyte: 450 mg tablets of valganciclovir, as the hydrochloride). The curve is ganciclovir: each dose is converted at 0.720 mg ganciclovir per mg valganciclovir, the ratio of molecular weights, as the source converted its doses, and absorbed with the source's rate (0.895 per hour) after its lag (23 minutes). Intravenous doses are ganciclovir, as Cytovene-IV is labelled.

### Bioavailability

The bioavailability is the label's, 59.4% for tablets taken with food, a within-subject comparison with intravenous ganciclovir, rather than the source's 82.5%, which compared an intravenous profile on day 5 with an oral one on day 15. With the label's value the model reproduces the label's single-dose exposure in mild renal impairment (AUC 48.9 against 49.5 mg·h/L); with the source's it is a third higher.

### Against the label

At the reference man (creatinine clearance 113 mL/min) clearance is 14.9 L/h; the Cytovene label gives 3.52 mL/min/kg, 14.8 L/h at 70 kg. After 5 mg/kg over an hour the model's AUC is 23.5 mg·h/L, within the label's 22.1-26.8. The label's clearances at reduced renal function (128, 57 and 30 mL/min at creatinine clearances of 50-79, 25-49 and under 25) are close to the model's (142, 81 and 33 at 65, 37 and 15).

### Renal function

Clearance is proportional to the Cockcroft-Gault creatinine clearance, from the Patient Profile's serum creatinine; left blank, a normal creatinine for age and sex is assumed (0.9 mg/dL in a man, 0.8 in a woman). The source excluded creatinine clearances under 10 mL/min, and dialysis is not modelled. In pregnancy the creatinine clearance takes the 50% rise in filtration of late pregnancy when the creatinine is left blank; there are no pregnancy pharmacokinetic studies.

### No band

No concentration range is established. The ESICM/ESCMID position paper (Abdul-Aziz and colleagues, *Intensive Care Med* 2020) proposes an AUC over 24 hours of 40-60 mg·h/L, or a trough above 2 mg/L, if concentrations are monitored, and neither recommends nor discourages monitoring.

### Where to be careful

Twenty patients, all transplant recipients with CMV infection; other populations (HIV, haematopoietic stem-cell transplant, critical illness) may differ.
