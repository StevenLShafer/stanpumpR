### The model

Kadam and colleagues' analysis of 31 healthy Caucasian adults given one 15 mg tablet (*Biol Pharm Bull* 2013): two compartments with first-order absorption, apparent clearance 2.12 L/h, central volume 21.4 L, intercompartmental clearance 0.349 L/h, peripheral volume 26.3 L (fixed) and absorption 1.82/h. Neither sex nor weight was significant, so the parameters are scaled by body size alone.

### CYP2C8

The one covariate was the CYP2C8\*3 allele, which adds 1.09 L/h to the clearance and 1.03 L/h to the intercompartmental clearance (about 51% and 295% higher). The Patient Profile has no CYP2C8 field, so the curve is a non-carrier's (\*1/\*1); a carrier's concentrations would be lower.

### What is not plotted

Concentration only, in ng/mL, of pioglitazone itself. Its active metabolites (M-III and M-IV), which circulate at higher concentrations and carry much of the effect, are not modelled, and the insulin sensitisation, which develops over weeks, is not drawn. See [Antidiabetic models and their limits](help:models/antidiabetics).
