### The model

Etoposide in children comes from Urien and colleagues (*Cancer Chemother Pharmacol* 2011;67:597-603): 67 children aged 14 weeks to 16.7 years (median 3.5 years, 5.3 to 66 kg), given one-hour infusions of 100 or 150 mg/m² a day, each followed by carboplatin, on three or five days. Two compartments describe the disposition, every parameter scaled by body weight (the clearances to the ¾ power, the volumes in proportion, for 70 kg):

- clearance 2.05 L/h, central volume 9.3 L;
- one peripheral compartment, 5.7 L, exchanging at 0.68 L/h.

Weight was the only covariate retained. A maturation term was tested and not kept, nor were dexamethasone or the CYP3A4, CYP3A5 and MDR1 genotypes. For a 15 kg child, clearance is 0.65 L/h. At 70 kg the half-lives are 2.1 and 8.8 hours.

### What is plotted

Plasma etoposide in mcg/mL (mg/L). Doses are entered in mg or as an infusion rate; a dose prescribed per m² or per kg must be converted first. Exposure is judged by the area under the curve (the paper's targeted dosing aims at a course AUC of 371 or 474 mg·h/L), which is not drawn. There is no effect site and no band.

### Where to be careful

- **Children.** Two of the 67 were under six months old and the heaviest weighed 66 kg; the 70 kg reference is an extrapolation, though it lies between the adult clearances the paper cites (1.8 and 2.65 L/h).
- **The terminal phase** rests on samples to 12 hours, about 1.4 of its half-lives.
- **Renal and hepatic function** were not covariates; impaired patients clear etoposide more slowly than this curve shows.
- **Not a monitoring tool.** The curve is a population prediction, not an estimate from measured levels.

Every parameter was read in the paper's PDF. Three misprints there do not affect the model: the Methods give the clearance exponent as ¼ where the abstract, Table 3 and the dosing formula give ¾; Table 3's covariate column names CL70 for every parameter; and its footnote's check, 0.75 L/h at 15 kg, is 0.65 L/h by its own equation. The abstract gives the central volume as 9.21 L, Table 3 as 9.3 L, which is used.
