### The model

Doxorubicinol in children is formed from [doxorubicinPediatric](help:drugs/doxorubicinPediatric) and is not dosed. It comes from the same paper, Völler and colleagues (*Clin Pharmacokinet* 2015;54:1139-1149): one compartment receiving half of doxorubicin's clearance (a fraction fixed from published exposure ratios), with a clearance of 42.5 L/h per m² and a volume of 760 L/m² at the median child, scaled on body surface area like every parameter of the model. Its half-life is 12 hours.

### What is plotted

Plasma doxorubicinol in ng/mL. There is no band.

Every parameter was read in the paper's PDF.
