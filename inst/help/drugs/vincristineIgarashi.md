### The model

Vincristine in adults comes from Igarashi and colleagues (*Cancer Chemother Pharmacol* 2021;87:501-511): 30 Japanese adults with lymphoma, aged 25 to 82, given up to 2 mg as a 10-minute infusion within R-CHOP-like regimens. Three compartments describe the disposition:

- clearance 31.1 L/h × (body surface area / 1.60 m²)^1.74 × (age / 60)^−0.485;
- central volume 13.2 L;
- a shallow compartment of 33.9 L exchanging at 60.2 L/h, and a deep one of 544 L exchanging at 81.1 L/h.

For the typical patient the half-lives are 3 minutes, 36 minutes and 17.6 hours, and 2 mg gives a peak of about 64 ng/mL.

### What is plotted

Plasma vincristine in ng/mL. Doses are entered in mg as labelled (vincristine sulfate); a dose prescribed per m² must be multiplied by the body surface area first, and is usually capped at 2 mg. There is no effect site and no band.

### Covariates

- **Body surface area** (Mosteller's formula; the paper does not name its own), on clearance.
- **Age**, on clearance: older patients clear vincristine more slowly. The term is held at 18 years for younger patients; the youngest studied was 25.

### Where to be careful

- **The early minutes are poorly known.** The first sample was at 10 minutes, after most of the fastest phase was over, and the second compartment's parameters had to be fixed.
- **The terminal phase** rests on samples to 19 hours, about one half-life.
- **Liver disease and CYP3A4 drugs** were not covariates; vincristine is cleared by the liver and CYP3A4, and an azole or a liver impairment will raise exposure well above this curve.
- **Adults only.** Not a monitoring tool.

Every parameter was read in the paper's PDF.
