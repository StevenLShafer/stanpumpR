### The model

Istradefylline's model is the sponsor's population analysis (Knebel and colleagues, *J Clin Pharmacol* 2011;51:40-52) of 1449 subjects, 1219 with Parkinson's disease, as printed in the FDA's pharmacometrics review in the Nourianz file (NDA 022075). Two compartments with first-order absorption (0.464 per hour): apparent clearance 5.76 L/h, central volume 198 L, intercompartmental clearance 21.6 L/h and peripheral volume 307 L, for a White 62-year-old non-smoker of 70 kg. Clearance rises slightly with lean body mass and falls slightly with age; the intercompartmental clearance rises with lean body mass and the peripheral volume steeply with weight. The source does not say how it computed lean body mass; the James formula is used. A dose peaks after about 4 hours and the terminal half-life is about 67 hours (label 83), so steady state takes about two weeks.

### Held at the reference

Smoking raises clearance 63% (exposure 39% lower; the label raises the dose to 40 mg at 20 or more cigarettes a day), and CYP3A4 inhibitors lower it (exposure 35% higher; the label caps the dose at 20 mg). Neither has a Patient Profile field, so both are held at none. No genotype effect is known.

### What is not plotted

The fall in daily OFF time follows the steady-state exposure (at most 5.8 percentage points, half-maximal at an AUC of 1690 ng·h/mL), a relation between doses, not a time course, so there is no effect site and no band.
