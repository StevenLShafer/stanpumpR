### The model

From Diezi and colleagues (*Pharmacol Res Perspect* 2023;11:e01032), a crossover in 12 healthy adults: two compartments, apparent oral, with a clearance of 0.569 L/h, central and peripheral volumes of 31.3 and 13.9 L, an intercompartmental clearance of 10.2 L/h, and syrup absorbed at 5.59/h. The half-life is about 54 hours; 250 mg twice daily averages about 37 mg/L (Warren and colleagues found 32 mg/L).

### Size and formulation

The source's linear weight term turns a volume negative below about 28 kg, so it cannot be extrapolated; it is replaced by the library's [fat-free-mass scaling](help:models/fat-free-mass), and with the switch off the 67 kg values are used for everyone. The US capsule has no published absorption rate and is given the syrup's, so its peak may be later than plotted. The paediatric absence-epilepsy model (Mizuno 2023) was not retrievable; children are extrapolated.

### What is not plotted

Mizuno and colleagues (*Clin Pharmacol Ther* 2023) related the 24-hour exposure to absence-seizure freedom (half the children free at about 1,027 mg·h/mL), but the full equation is not available, so no seizure probability is drawn. There is no effect site.

### Band

40 to 100 mg/L (ILAE; Patsalos and colleagues, 2008).
