### The model

Clozapine's parameters come from Li and colleagues (*Acta Pharmacol Sin* 2012;33:1409-1416), a population analysis of 809 clozapine and 808 norclozapine concentrations from therapeutic drug monitoring in 162 Chinese inpatients with refractory schizophrenia. One compartment each, with **apparent** (oral) parameters: clozapine clearance 21.9 L/h and volume 526 L in a female nonsmoker, 20.8% faster clearance in men, absorption rate constant 1.3 /h (fixed from rich-sampling studies), and 66% of each mole cleared becoming **norclozapine** (clearance 32.7 L/h, volume 624 L; 24.2% faster in men), which is drawn alongside it. Only the oral route is offered.

### Body size, sex and smoking

The source found no effect of weight or age. The model is written for the library's reference man and scaled by fat-free mass, which also carries the source's sex difference (a woman of the same size clears 13.5% less; the source measured 17%). **Smoking is not modelled; the model is a nonsmoker.** Smoking induces CYP1A2 and raises clozapine clearance by 45% (norclozapine by 54%), so a smoker's concentration is about a third lower; when a patient **stops** smoking, as on admission to hospital, the concentration rises toward this curve, a recognised cause of clozapine toxicity. CYP1A2 inhibitors (fluvoxamine, ciprofloxacin) and inflammation also raise it.

### The band

The band is the **AGNP consensus update 2026** (Hart and colleagues, *Pharmacopsychiatry* 2026;59:166-295, Table 5): **350 to 600 ng/mL** in schizophrenia, sampled 12 hours after the dose, laboratory alert level 1,000 ng/mL (above which seizures become more likely). Norclozapine has no range of its own.

### Where to be careful

Most of the samples were drawn late in the dosing interval, so the rise and fall within an interval are understated: at 100 mg twice daily the Clozaril label reports an average steady-state peak of 319 ng/mL and trough of 122 ng/mL, a wider swing than this curve. Pregnancy is not applied: one study found concentrations 23% lower late in pregnancy, with a confidence interval including no change, and case reports go both ways.
