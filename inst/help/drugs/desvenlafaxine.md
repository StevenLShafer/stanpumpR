### Venlafaxine's active metabolite

Desvenlafaxine (O-desmethylvenlafaxine, ODV) appears whenever [venlafaxine](help:drugs/venlafaxine) is given, on its own row. Its parameters, CL/F 22.1 L/h and V/F 238 L (half-life 7.5 hours), come from Wang and colleagues' joint model. All of venlafaxine's clearance forms it, mole for mole, and a further 4.8% of each absorbed dose forms it during first pass.

It **cannot be dosed directly** here. Its parameters are on venlafaxine's apparent scale, divided by venlafaxine's unmeasured bioavailability, and do not describe desvenlafaxine taken as a drug in its own right (Pristiq), whose oral kinetics are its own.

The shaded band, **85 to 380 ng/mL** with 230 as the typical value (its midpoint, not a target), is the trough range of ODV during venlafaxine treatment proposed by the AGNP task force's systematic review (Lense and colleagues, *Psychopharmacology* 2024;241:275-289, https://doi.org/10.1007/s00213-023-06484-7). It applies to ODV formed from venlafaxine, not to desvenlafaxine taken as Pristiq. One PET study found 80% serotonin-transporter occupancy above 85 ng/mL. The range for the active moiety, venlafaxine plus ODV, is 140 to 600 ng/mL; add the venlafaxine row to compare with it.
