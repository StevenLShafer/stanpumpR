### The model

From Bamgboye and colleagues (*J Clin Pharmacol* 2026), who gave 20 adults a stable-isotope-labelled intravenous microdose alongside their oral topiramate: three compartments, linear, allometric on weight, with a clearance of 1.31 L/h, a central volume of 9.84 L and steady-state volume near 58 L at 70 kg. The terminal half-life is about 30 hours.

### Route

Oral (`mg PO`, with schedules). Absolute oral bioavailability is complete (109 per cent against intravenous, Clark 2013; about 100 per cent, Ahmed 2015), so the apparent and true parameters are the same and bioavailability is 1 — the label's figure of 80 per cent is contradicted by both intravenous studies. No absorption rate was estimated; the 1-hour median time to peak sets a label-anchored 2.0/h, recorded as a calibration. The extended-release capsules have no published input model and are not offered, nor is the investigational intravenous product.

### What is not modelled

Enzyme-inducing co-medication (which raises clearance about 1.6-fold), and the empirical dependence of apparent clearance on daily dose that an earlier oral model carried, which a linear engine cannot hold.

### Band

5 to 20 mg/L (ILAE; Patsalos and colleagues, 2008).
