### The model

Opicapone's model is the sponsor's population model of 724 subjects in 21 studies, printed with its equations in the FDA's clinical pharmacology review of Ongentys (NDA 212489): one compartment, apparent clearance 37.6 L/h (14% lower in women), volume 49.7 L, absorbed through one transit compartment. It is set for the product as labelled: the micronised capsule, taken at bedtime and away from levodopa. Bedtime slows absorption (the transit rate 1.24 × 1.245 × 0.405 = 0.63 per hour), and the relative bioavailability depends on the capsule, 1.68 for 50 mg and 1.97 for 25 mg; other doses are joined between these by a power. A 50 mg capsule peaks after about 3 hours and the half-life is about an hour. Bilirubin, which lowers clearance slightly, has no Patient Profile field and is held at the median.

### The plasma is not the effect

Opicapone binds COMT so tightly that red-cell COMT inhibition outlasts it by days: its half-life is 62 hours after one dose and over 100 hours after repeated doses. The curve drawn is plasma opicapone, gone within hours of each bedtime capsule; the inhibition, and the 60 to 80% rise in levodopa exposure it produces, persist over the whole day. No effect site and no band.
