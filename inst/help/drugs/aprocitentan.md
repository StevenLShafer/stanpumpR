### The model

Aprocitentan is the active metabolite of [macitentan](help:drugs/macitentan). At steady state its exposure is three times macitentan's, and it contributes about 40% of the activity (Opsumit label). It cannot be dosed here; it is drawn whenever macitentan is. Aprocitentan is also a product of its own (Tryvio, for hypertension), which is not offered: these parameters were fitted to aprocitentan formed from macitentan.

Its parameters were fitted together with macitentan's by Bartolucci and colleagues (*Clin Pharmacokinet* 2021;60:1605-1619, doi 10.1007/s40262-021-01049-3). It has one compartment:

- volume 23.5 L × (weight/68.8)<sup>0.86</sup>;
- clearance 0.321 L/h × (weight/68.8)<sup>0.70</sup>, 15.7% higher in men and 13.1% lower in moderate or severe renal impairment (MDRD eGFR below 60).

It is formed from 62% of macitentan's clearance, and 6.3% of each dose is absorbed directly as aprocitentan. Its half-life is about 51 hours (label: 48 hours).
