### The model

The base model of Milosheska and colleagues (*Br J Clin Pharmacol* 2016;82:399-411), 100 adults with epilepsy: one compartment, apparent oral, absorption 1.96/h, clearance 2.32 L/h, volume 77.6 L. The half-life is about 23 hours (Cohen 1987 measured 24).

### Why this model

The specification proposed a model with a fixed volume of 1.8 L/kg, which would give a monotherapy half-life near 48 hours against the 24 to 33 observed; Cohen and colleagues measured 1.2 L/kg. Milosheska's base model is complete in one source and consistent with the observed half-life.

### What is not modelled

The curve is a **mixed** co-medication population: valproate roughly halves clearance (half-life 59 to 70 hours) and enzyme inducers roughly double it (13 to 15 hours), but the app has no co-medication field, so neither is applied. Lamictal XR has no published input model and is not offered, nor are the large clearance changes of pregnancy and oestrogen contraceptives. Immediate-release tablets, chewable/dispersible tablets and orally disintegrating tablets share these parameters.

### Band

3 to 15 mg/L (ILAE; Patsalos and colleagues, 2008).
