### The model

From Ingwersen and colleagues (*Eur J Pharm Sci* 2000;11:247-254), 130 patients on tiagabine monotherapy: one compartment, apparent oral, clearance 6.10 L/h and volume 62.0 L at a height of 170 cm, absorption 1.25/h. The half-life is about 7 hours.

### Size and co-medication

Both clearance and volume scale with body **height** in the source, by an exponent in a table that could not be retrieved; for want of it, size is carried by the library's [fat-free-mass scaling](help:models/fat-free-mass) instead, and with the switch off the 170 cm values are used for everyone. This was a monotherapy population; enzyme-inducing co-medication raises clearance about 67 per cent (Samara 1998), which is not applied. There is no effect site.

### Band

20 to 200 ng/mL (ILAE; Patsalos and colleagues, 2008). Tiagabine is 96 per cent protein bound; the range is total drug.
