### Fluoxetine's active metabolite

Norfluoxetine appears whenever [fluoxetine](help:drugs/fluoxetine) is given, on its own row. It cannot be dosed directly: its parameters (CL/F 3.24 L/h, V/F 1.52 L, from Han and colleagues, 2025) were fitted jointly with fluoxetine on the assumption that all of fluoxetine's clearance forms it, and they are apparent on that scale.

### Only the steady-state trough is valid

Like fluoxetine's, these parameters were fitted to steady-state troughs alone. They give norfluoxetine a half-life of 20 minutes, where the label gives 4 to 16 days, so in this model norfluoxetine simply follows fluoxetine at about 0.9 of its concentration in women and rises and falls with it. The trough on a steady once-daily regimen is reproduced (79.5 ng/mL in women and 63.8 in men at 20 mg a day, against 78.9 and 63.7 in the source's own simulations); the time course, and above all the slow washout that makes norfluoxetine clinically important, is not. Read the warning on [the fluoxetine page](help:drugs/fluoxetine) before using either curve.

No band is drawn: the AGNP range, 120 to 500 ng/mL, is for fluoxetine plus norfluoxetine.
