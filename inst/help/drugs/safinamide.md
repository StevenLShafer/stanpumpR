### The model

Safinamide's model is the sponsor's final population model (Model 136) of 1171 patients with Parkinson's disease in three phase 3 studies, as printed in the FDA's pharmacometrics review of Xadago (NDA 207145): one compartment with first-order absorption, apparent clearance 4.54 L/h and volume 168 L for a 70 kg, 60-year-old Asian patient, ka 1.19 per hour. Clearance scales with weight to the power 0.75 and with age to the power -0.199, the volume in proportion to weight; the review prints these exponents without the equations, and the power forms reproduce the ranges it quotes. White patients' bioavailability is 4% higher than Asian patients'; with no race field, the White value is used. The half-life is about 25 hours and a dose peaks after about 3 hours (label 2 to 3).

### What is not plotted

Safinamide inhibits MAO-B reversibly, but platelet MAO-B stays inhibited for days after the plasma falls (still significant at 120 hours), and the trials found no relation between exposure and response, so there is no effect site and no band. Doses are safinamide base, as labelled. Moderate hepatic impairment raises exposure 80%; that is not applied. See [Parkinson disease drugs](help:models/parkinsons).
