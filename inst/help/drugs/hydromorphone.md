### The model

Hydromorphone's intravenous kinetics are from Drover and colleagues (*Anesthesiology* 2002;97:827-836), a study in healthy volunteers that characterised the intravenous disposition and the input of immediate- and extended-release oral formulations. The model is a central volume of 0.16 L/kg with fixed rate constants.

### Covariates

Weight only, scaling the volumes and clearances linearly.

### Effect site

The time to peak effect is 19.6 minutes: slower than fentanyl, much faster than morphine.

### Extravascular routes

Hydromorphone is the one drug in the library with oral, intramuscular and intranasal units. The absorption parameters are **provisional**: a single rate constant of 0.01/min (an absorption half-time of about 70 minutes) and a bioavailability of 0.6 for all three routes, with lag times of 0, 90 and 180 minutes for oral, intramuscular and intranasal doses that the code describes as placeholders. They are being revised in the active-metabolite work, where hydromorphone is also the active metabolite of hydrocodone. See [In development](help:in-development).

### MEAC and typical concentrations

MEAC is 1.5 ng/mL, the shaded band 1.2 to 3 ng/mL. Hydromorphone is five to seven times as potent as morphine by mass.

### Where to be careful

The model describes young healthy volunteers. Hydromorphone-3-glucuronide, a neuroexcitatory metabolite that accumulates in renal failure, is not modelled.
