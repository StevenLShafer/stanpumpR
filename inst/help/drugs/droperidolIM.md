### The model

Intramuscular droperidol, from Foo and colleagues (*Br J Clin Pharmacol* 2016;82:1550-1556): 128 serum samples from 41 acutely agitated patients given 5 or 10 mg intramuscularly. Two compartments with first-order absorption: apparent clearance 41.9 L/h, central volume 73.6 L, intercompartmental clearance 71.5 L/h, peripheral volume 79.8 L, and an absorption rate constant **fixed** at 10 per hour (a 4-minute absorption half-life). The half-lives are 0.31 h and 3.0 h, matching the paper's medians. Clearance and central volume were checked against the abstract; the other two values come from the brief, and they are consistent with those half-lives.

### Intramuscular only

Bioavailability after intramuscular injection was not measured, so the parameters are **apparent** and this drug is offered as **mg IM** only. [Intravenous droperidol](help:drugs/droperidol) is a separate entry.

### Covariates and effect

Body weight was not recorded, so the values are read as the reference adult's and take the library's [fat-free-mass scaling](help:models/fat-free-mass). The data identify no sedation EC50, no ke0 and no QTc slope, so there is **no effect site** and no band.
