### The model

Methadone's parameters are from Inturrisi and colleagues (*Clin Pharmacol Ther* 1987;41:392-401), who studied intravenous methadone in patients with cancer pain. The model is a central volume of 0.18 L/kg with fixed rate constants; its elimination rate constant, 0.008/min, is the smallest in the library.

### Covariates

Weight only, scaling the volumes and clearances linearly.

### Effect site

The time to peak effect is 11.3 minutes. The combination is unusual: an effect site that equilibrates within minutes, like fentanyl's, attached to a disposition that takes a day or more to clear. A dose of methadone works quickly and then stays. See [the methadone scenario](scenario:methadone-bolus).

### MEAC and typical concentrations

MEAC is 60 ng/mL (0.06 mcg/mL in the plotted units); the shaded band is 48 to 120 ng/mL.

### Where to be careful

Methadone's elimination half-life varies enormously between individuals (from about 15 to over 60 hours) and with CYP3A4 and CYP2B6 activity, urinary pH and chronic dosing; a single typical model understates this variability more than for most drugs. Its QT prolongation and NMDA antagonism are pharmacodynamic and not represented. The model is racemic methadone; the enantiomers differ in both kinetics and dynamics.
