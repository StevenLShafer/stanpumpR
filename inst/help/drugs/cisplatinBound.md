### The model

Platinum bound irreversibly to plasma proteins is formed from the unbound platinum of [cisplatin](help:drugs/cisplatin) and is not dosed. Urien and Lokiec (*Br J Clin Pharmacol* 2004;57:756-763) modelled it as a metabolite formed from the central compartment with its own slow elimination (half-life 50 hours). Its volume cannot be separated from the fraction bound, so the two are estimated together: 0.017 per litre at a body surface area of 1.7 m², a creatinine clearance of 81 mL/min, a dose of 25 mg/m² and a serum protein of 70 g/L.

### What is plotted

Protein-bound platinum in ng/mL (µg/L of platinum). Added to the unbound platinum it gives total plasma platinum. It is inactive. There is no band.

Every parameter was read in the paper's PDF.
