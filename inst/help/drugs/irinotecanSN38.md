### The model

SN-38, the active metabolite, is formed from [irinotecan](help:drugs/irinotecan) and is not dosed. It comes from the same paper, Klein and colleagues (*Clin Pharmacol Ther* 2002;72:638-647), in two compartments. The fraction of irinotecan converted is not identifiable, so each volume and clearance is divided by it.

The fit split the patients into two groups, one with a central volume (so divided) of 200 L and one of 466 L. The library uses the 466-L group: the paper's own figure and its validation set put most patients there, and it is the group in which the authors fixed the fraction converted, at 12%. A patient in the other group would have about 2.3 times the SN-38 shown.

The half-lives are 8 minutes and 24 hours.

### What is plotted

Plasma SN-38 in ng/mL. There is no band.

Every parameter was read in the paper's PDF.
