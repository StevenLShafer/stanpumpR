### The model

Tolcapone (Tasmar) is a reversible COMT inhibitor taken with each dose of levodopa, 100 to 200 mg three times a day.

The model is the fluctuator model of Jorga and colleagues (*Br J Clin Pharmacol* 2000). They pooled sparse steady-state samples from 215 patients with motor fluctuations in the phase II dose-finding trials (50 to 400 mg three times a day). The model has two compartments:

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Clearance | 4.8 L/h, with lean body weight to the power 0.73 |
| Central volume | 16 L, with lean body weight to the power 0.65 |
| Intercompartmental clearance | 5.2 L/h |
| Peripheral volume | 12 L |
| Absorption | 1.7 per hour, with no lag |

The authors fixed the absolute bioavailability at 0.6, the value Jorga 1998 measured against an intravenous dose, so these are true rather than apparent parameters. Lean body weight is James's, at a median of 55 kg. A patient 15 kg lighter or heavier in lean weight has 80% or 119% of the typical clearance.

At steady state on 200 mg three times a day, the model's AUC is 25 mcg·h/mL per dosing interval (the paper: 26.5) and the half-life is about 5 hours.

### Patients and volunteers differ

Healthy young men (Jorga 1998) have a clearance of 7.3 L/h, a total volume of 9 L and a half-life of about 2 hours, which is what the label quotes. The patients' total volume was about 28 L in both of the paper's independent analyses. That gives the longer half-life drawn here, although sparse sampling may have contributed. The patients' exposure is nonetheless close to that of healthy elderly volunteers (AUC 24 to 27 mcg·h/mL).

### What is held at the reference

- **Serum protein and albumin.** The Patient Profile does not carry them: protein had a small effect on clearance (at most ±10%) and albumin on the peripheral volume.
- **The volumes' dose dependence.** In the fluctuators the total volume rose with the regimen, from 15 L at 50 mg to 39 L at 400 mg three times a day. The nonfluctuators did not show this, so the volumes are held at their 200 mg values. Clearance did not change with dose, so the exposure is unaffected.
- **Food.** The curve is drawn fasting. Taken with food, tolcapone's exposure is 10 to 20% lower, which the label considers clinically irrelevant.

### Effect and band

Red-cell COMT inhibition follows the plasma concentration without delay: the EC50 was 1.2 mcg/mL in volunteers (Jorga 1998). Tolcapone roughly doubles levodopa's AUC.

The band is the AGNP reference range, 3 to 6 mcg/mL two hours after a dose, although monitoring is not recommended. On 200 mg three times a day the model is at about 5 mcg/mL two hours after a dose.

Age, sex and treatment duration had no effect. The label states that "polymorphic metabolism is unlikely" (glucuronidation by UGT1A9), so no genotype term exists. Tolcapone carries a boxed warning for fulminant hepatic failure; hepatic impairment is outside the model.
