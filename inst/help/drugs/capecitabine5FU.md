### The model

5-FU formed from capecitabine is plotted beside [capecitabine](help:drugs/capecitabine)'s 5′-DFUR and is not dosed. Gieschke and colleagues (*Br J Clin Pharmacol* 2003;55:252-263) modelled it in one compartment receiving all of 5′-DFUR's clearance, with an apparent volume of 17.8 L (fixed from the literature) and clearance of 1190 L/h at an alkaline phosphatase of 140 U/L. Its half-life is 37 seconds.

### What is plotted

Plasma 5-FU in mcg/mL (mg/L), formed mole for mole from 5′-DFUR. It is a separate entry from the library's intravenous 5-FU models. There is no band.

Every parameter was read in the paper's PDF.
