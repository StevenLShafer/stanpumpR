### The model

Cyclosporine's parameters come from Fanta and colleagues (*Br J Clin Pharmacol* 2007;64:772-784), a population analysis in 162 children on peritoneal dialysis before kidney transplantation (6.9-64 kg), each given cyclosporine intravenously (Sandimmune, 3 mg/kg over 4 hours) and orally (Neoral, 10 mg/kg), with whole-blood sampling to 24 hours by a specific assay. Three compartments; at 13 kg clearance 6.1 L/h, central volume 5.3 L, peripheral volumes 19.6 and 4.4 L, scaled by weight to the 0.75 power for clearances and in proportion for volumes, which the source found removed any effect of age. Oral absorption 0.68 per hour. It is the only published model, read in full, with both intravenous and microemulsion parameters.

### Held at the reference

The source's other covariates, cholesterol (5.4 mmol/L), haematocrit (31%) and serum creatinine (524 µmol/L, a dialysis patient's), are held at its reference values. Whole-blood cyclosporine follows the haematocrit: at an adult haematocrit of 40% every parameter would be 6.6% lower.

### One clearance

The source gave the oral route a clearance 23% below the intravenous one, which its authors attribute to the assay. The library uses one clearance, so the oral bioavailability is 0.47 (the source's 0.36 divided by 0.77), which keeps the oral exposure the source fitted.

### Against the labels: be careful with oral doses in adults

Intravenously the model agrees with the labels: at 70 kg its clearance is 5.1 mL/min/kg (the Neoral label: 5 to 7 in adult transplant recipients) and its terminal half-life 17.6 hours (Sandimmune: about 19). Orally it does not: the Neoral label's stable kidney transplant recipients, on 344 mg a day, had an AUC of 6,035 ng·h/mL and a trough of 251 ng/mL over a 12-hour interval; this model gives 3,727 and 80. In an adult taking Neoral the concentration is likely 1.3 to 1.6 times what the model shows, and the trough about three times. Use the model for the shape of the curve and for intravenous dosing, and take oral troughs from monitoring.

### The band

The band is the Sandimmune label's whole-blood 24-hour trough range, 100-200 ng/mL by a specific assay; nonspecific assays read about twice as high. Lower targets are used with everolimus and later after transplantation. It is a trough range, drawn against the whole curve.

### Dosing and interactions

Cyclosporine is dosed as itself: Sandimmune injection, and the microemulsion capsules Neoral and Gengraf ("cyclosporine modified"); the older Sandimmune capsules are not bioequivalent and are not modelled. CYP3A4 and P-glycoprotein inhibitors (azoles, macrolides, diltiazem, grapefruit) raise it and inducers (rifampicin, phenytoin, carbamazepine) lower it; none is modelled.
