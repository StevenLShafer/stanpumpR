### The model

Linezolid's parameters come from Boak and colleagues (*Antimicrob Agents Chemother* 2014;58:2334-2343), a population analysis of 41 adults given 600 mg intravenously or orally every 12 hours, sampled before and 2, 4 and 8 hours after a dose. One compartment: nonrenal clearance 4.55 L/h and renal clearance 2.17 L/h at a standard weight of 65 kg and a creatinine clearance of 120 mL/min, volume 44.3 L. Size is scaled from the lower of the ideal body weight (Devine) and the body weight, clearances to the 0.75 power. Oral doses pass through three lag compartments (a mean lag of 69 minutes) and are absorbed with a half-life of 10 minutes; the oral bioavailability is 100%.

### Renal function

The renal part of clearance follows the Cockcroft-Gault creatinine clearance, which the source computed at a fixed 65 kg, from the Patient Profile's serum creatinine; a blank creatinine is a normal value for age and sex. About a third of clearance is renal at normal function. The Zyvox label says renal impairment does not alter linezolid's pharmacokinetics; three later population analyses found a renal effect, and the label's own single-dose AUCs rise as renal function falls, so the renal term is kept.

### Against the label

At the reference man the model's clearance is 6.5 L/h and half-life 4.7 hours; the label's 600 mg every 12 hours intravenously gives 7.4 L/h, 4.8 hours and an AUC of 89.7 mg·h/L over a dosing interval (this model, 92).

### The MIC line and the band

The MIC line is *Staphylococcus aureus*, including MRSA, at 2 mg/L free drug (2.9 mg/L on the total curve, linezolid being 31% bound): pathogens with an MIC of 2 mg/L or more need higher doses (the ESICM/ESCMID position paper), and the susceptible breakpoint is 4 mg/L.

The band is **not** an MBC band, as other antibiotics' are. Linezolid only stops staphylococci and enterococci from growing; it does not kill them at concentrations the curve reaches. Its band is the trough range the ESICM/ESCMID position paper recommends for monitoring (Abdul-Aziz and colleagues, *Intensive Care Med* 2020;46:1127-1153, doi 10.1007/s00134-020-06050-1, Table 5), 2-7 mg/L of total linezolid in plasma, one sample "30 min or just before the next dosing", taken 48 hours after treatment starts: a trough above 2 mg/L reaches the efficacy target for most isolates, and above 7-10 mg/L thrombocytopenia becomes likely. It is a trough range, drawn against the whole curve.

### Where to be careful

Thrombocytopenia, from about two weeks of treatment, is linezolid's dose-limiting toxicity; it is not modelled. Rifampicin lowers its concentration; liver disease and critical illness are not modelled. In pregnancy the renal clearance takes the rise in filtration of late pregnancy; one pregnant woman's exposure fell by half or more.
