### The model

Nicardipine's parameters come from Modi, Veng-Pedersen, Graham and Dow (*J Pharm Sci* 1993;82:705-713): seven healthy young men, each given 30-minute infusions of 70, 140, 210 and 280 mcg/kg of nicardipine hydrochloride at least six days apart, with venous plasma measured for 24.5 hours. The disposition was linear over the four doses. The authors described each man's response to a unit dose as a sum of two exponentials, and their Table I lists the clearance (0.920 L/h/kg), the central volume (0.291 L/kg), the steady-state volume (1.13 L/kg) and the distribution clearance (0.826 L/h/kg). The model here is the two-compartment system with those mean values, which gives half-lives of about 6 minutes and 1.4 hours. In each of the seven men, the same four numbers reproduce the printed distribution time, the check that identifies the distribution clearance among the table's unlabelled rows.

### What is plotted

Plasma nicardipine in ng/mL, for doses of nicardipine hydrochloride as the injection is labelled, and the effect site, which peaks 1.2 minutes after a bolus. Modi related heart rate and blood pressure to the concentration through its own functions, not an effect site, so the time to peak comes from Cheung and colleagues (*Anesth Analg* 1999;89:1116-1123): in 40 anaesthetised cardiac surgical patients given 0.25 to 2 mg over 5 seconds, with the radial arterial pressure read every 15 seconds, the largest fall in mean arterial pressure came 56 to 101 seconds after the dose, 72 seconds on average. In awake hypertensive patients given the drug over 2 minutes and measured by cuff once a minute (Cheung and colleagues, *Am Heart J* 1990;119:438-442), the largest fall came 2.5 minutes after the end of the injection, later than this effect site puts it (0.6 minutes), so in awake patients the peak may come a little later. No blood pressure is drawn.

### Where to be careful

Seven healthy men. Other studies found clearances from 0.31 to 0.91 L/h/kg (Graham 1985, 0.49), so this model sits at the fast end, and a patient may reach twice the concentration plotted. Cheung 1999's own pharmacokinetic table was read and found unusable; only its timing is used. Every parameter is per kilogram, applied to the fat-free-mass weight when [the scaling switch](help:models/fat-free-mass) is on. See [the vasoactive and resuscitation drugs](help:models/vasoactive).
