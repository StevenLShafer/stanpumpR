### The model

Dehydroaripiprazole is the active metabolite of [aripiprazole](help:drugs/aripiprazole), from the same analysis (Kim J-R and colleagues, *Br J Clin Pharmacol* 2008;66:802-810). One compartment: clearance 8.02 L/h and volume 587 L, a half-life of about 51 hours. Both values come from the brief and have not yet been checked against the paper's table.

### Formed only

The fraction of aripiprazole converted could not be identified, so these parameters are divided by it, and the whole of aripiprazole's apparent clearance is taken to feed the metabolite on that scale. That convention is assumed, not read from the paper. It is consistent with Kim's finding that metabolite concentration per dose did not differ between genotypes, and it predicts metabolite-to-parent ratios of 0.39 (normal) and 0.23 (intermediate) against the 0.20 to 0.34 observed. This entry has **no dosing unit**. See [Active metabolites](help:models/metabolites).

### Effect

No effect site and no band. Kim 2012's occupancy model is of parent aripiprazole only, so this row does not enter it.
