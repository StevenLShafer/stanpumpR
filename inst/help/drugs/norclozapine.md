### The model

Norclozapine (N-desmethylclozapine) is clozapine's main metabolite, drawn when clozapine is given; it cannot be entered in the dose table. Its parameters come from the same analysis as clozapine's (Li and colleagues, *Acta Pharmacol Sin* 2012;33:1409-1416): one compartment, clearance 32.7 L/h and volume 624 L in a female nonsmoker, 24.2% faster clearance in men and 54.3% faster in smokers, with 66% of clozapine cleared becoming norclozapine. The formation fraction was fixed, so these are apparent values, identified only together with it. See [clozapine](help:drugs/clozapine).

### No band

The AGNP consensus update 2026 gives norclozapine no range of its own: evidence on the clinical relevance of norclozapine levels is limited or contrasting. Its ratio to clozapine (norclozapine over clozapine 0.45 to 0.79 in nonsmokers, Table 7 of the same guideline) is used to check adherence and CYP1A2 activity; this model gives about 0.41, at the low edge.
