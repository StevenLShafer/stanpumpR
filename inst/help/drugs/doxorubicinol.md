### The model

Doxorubicinol is formed from [doxorubicin](help:drugs/doxorubicin) and is not dosed. It comes from the same paper, Joerger and colleagues (*Clin Pharmacokinet* 2007;46:1051-1068), fitted jointly with doxorubicin. The fraction of doxorubicin converted is not identifiable, so the metabolite's volume and clearance are each divided by it:

- volume 1580 L;
- clearance 108 L/h × (creatinine clearance / 80)^0.54, the creatinine clearance by Cockcroft-Gault in mL/min.

Its half-life is 10 hours at a creatinine clearance of 80 mL/min.

### What is plotted

Plasma doxorubicinol in mcg/mL (mg/L), formed molecule for molecule from doxorubicin. There is no band.

### Covariates

**Serum creatinine**, from the Patient Profile, in Cockcroft-Gault with the patient's weight (the paper does not say which weight). Left blank, the library's assumed normal value is used.

Every parameter was read in the paper's PDF.
