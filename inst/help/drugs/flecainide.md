### The model

Flecainide's parameters come from Doki and colleagues (*Pharmacogenet Genomics* 2012;22:777-783), a population analysis of 222 trough-to-midinterval serum concentrations in 163 Japanese outpatients with supraventricular arrhythmias (mean 61 years, 66 kg), all genotyped for CYP2D6. One compartment, apparent oral parameters: clearance is a metabolic part, 8.83 L/h (29% lower in women), plus a renal part, 9.79 L/h × (eGFR / 73) × (weight / 66 kg), with the eGFR by the Japanese equation the source used; the volume (6.0 L/kg) and absorption (0.62 per hour) were fixed from earlier studies. In a 66-kg man with an eGFR of 73 the clearance is 18.6 L/h, as the paper says.

### CYP2D6 and age

Genotype has no effect below 55. Above it, the metabolic clearance falls with age in patients with fewer functional CYP2D6 alleles: as (60/age)^1.62 from 60 in heterozygous extensive metabolisers, and as (55/age)^2.83 from 55 in the intermediate and poor group. Between 52 and 70 years the total clearance falls 6% in a homozygous extensive metaboliser and 29% in the intermediate and poor group. The Patient Profile's **CYP 2D6** field is mapped onto the source's groups by inference: normal and ultrarapid as homozygous extensive, intermediate as heterozygous extensive, poor as the intermediate-and-poor group (in which the source had almost only \*10/\*10 and \*10/\*5, and one true poor metaboliser).

### Renal function

The renal half of clearance follows the Japanese eGFR from the Patient Profile's serum creatinine; a blank creatinine is a normal value for age and sex. In pregnancy the eGFR takes the 50% rise in filtration of late pregnancy when the creatinine is left blank.

### Against the label

At the reference man the clearance is 20 L/h and the half-life 15 hours; in a 65-year-old 80-kg man, 18 to 22 hours by genotype, against the label's about 20 hours (12-27) in patients and 14 in healthy subjects. Healthy Western volunteers (Tjandra-Maga 1986) cleared it about a fifth faster, the difference being metabolic, plausibly the Japanese prevalence of CYP2D6\*10.

### The band and the dose

The band is the label's trough range, 0.2-1.0 mcg/mL (200-1000 ng/mL), above which cardiac adverse effects become more likely. Doses are flecainide acetate, as the US tablets (50, 100 and 150 mg) are labelled and as the source dosed. The model is for adults; children clear flecainide faster.

### Where to be careful

CYP2D6 inhibitors (quinidine, fluoxetine, paroxetine, amiodarone) raise flecainide's concentration; they are not modelled. Heart failure slows clearance (the label's half-life in NYHA class III is 19 hours), and urine pH changes the renal part.
