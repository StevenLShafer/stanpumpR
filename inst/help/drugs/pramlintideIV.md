### A research route

Pramlintide (Symlin) is injected subcutaneously. This entry is the **intravenous** model of a research study, offered as its own drug because it is the one complete fitted model of pramlintide; the subcutaneous product has none, and its label's bioavailability (30 to 40%), peak (about 20 minutes) and half-life (about 48 minutes) are not enough to build one. No subcutaneous curve is borrowed from it.

### The model

Fang and colleagues' reanalysis of 25 men with type 1 diabetes given intravenous pramlintide at three dose levels, as a 2-minute bolus and a 2-hour infusion (*AAPS J* 2013): two compartments, clearance 0.955 L/min, central volume 19.1 L, distribution clearance 0.283 L/min and peripheral volume 11.0 L, with no covariates. The study's 26-second lag and the bolus's fitted 4-minute duration describe its sampling and are not applied. Enter doses as `mcg` (bolus) or `mcg/min` (infusion).

### What is not plotted

Concentration only, in ng/mL (1 pmol/L is 0.00395 ng/mL). The same paper's model of meal glucose and endogenous glucose production, with an IC<sub>50</sub> of 23.8 pmol/L, is an endpoint of its own and is not drawn. See [Antidiabetic models and their limits](help:models/antidiabetics).
