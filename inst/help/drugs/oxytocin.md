### Two models, both weakly sourced

**Weight above 1 kg (humans):** a two-compartment model (V1 10.1 L, V2 7.03 L, clearances 0.974 and 0.204 L/min) from **unpublished data of James Eisenach**. There are no covariates.

**Weight of 1 kg or less (rats):** a two-compartment model (V1 0.10 L, V2 0.02 L) attributed in the code to "Tanaka et al", a study using an LC/MS assay, without a journal, volume or year. This is a research setting, reached only by entering a weight of 1 kg or less.

These are the two weakest citations in the library. They are shown rather than tidied away so that a user knows what the oxytocin curve rests on.

### Effect site

The time to peak effect is 5 minutes and is described in the code as a guess.

### Typical concentrations

The shaded band is 0.05 to 0.2 ng/mL. Oxytocin's uterotonic effect is the clinical end point, and the relationship between concentration and uterine tone is not modelled.

### Where to be careful

Oxytocin is in the library to support research on its kinetics, not as a clinical dosing tool. Its concentration-dependent hypotension and the desensitisation of the receptor during prolonged exposure are not represented.
