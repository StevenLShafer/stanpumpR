### The model

Ropivacaine's disposition is from the intravenous deuterium-labelled tracer of Simon and colleagues (*Anesth Analg* 2006;102:276-282), given 25 minutes after an epidural dose, with arterial sampling to 24 hours. The parameters are those of the **oldest group (61 years and over)**, as transcribed and completed for a 70 kg patient by Hartog: a **three-compartment** model with a central volume of 7 L, peripheral volumes of 12.6 and 28.7 L, and a clearance of 0.30 L/min. Simon found clearance about 0.19 L/min lower in the oldest patients than in the youngest, and healthy volunteers' clearance is about 0.40 L/min (Emanuelsson and colleagues, *Ther Drug Monit* 1997;19:126-131), so in a younger adult this model overpredicts exposure. Age is not a covariate here.

### Regional anesthesia (RA)

A dose in **mg RA** is a tissue injection. It enters a depot that is absorbed by first-order kinetics, bioavailability 1, no lag. The absorption rate, 0.00612/min (a half-time of 113 minutes), is **provisional**: it is the single rate that reproduces, with this disposition, the mean peak of 1.28 mcg/mL after axillary block with 30 to 40 mL of plain 5 mg/mL ropivacaine, taken as 175 mg (Vainionpää and colleagues, *Anesth Analg* 1995;81:534-538). The model peaks at about 110 minutes, later than the observed median of 52; matching the peak time instead would give a peak of 2.1 mcg/mL. A single depot cannot give both the early rise and the long tail that slow absorption produces (the half-life after the block was 7.1 hours against 1.7 hours intravenously). After epidural injection Simon found two parallel processes, 27 per cent with a half-time of 11 minutes and 77 per cent with 248 minutes.

Periarticular infiltration with epinephrine is much slower still: Hartog's hip infiltration input corresponds to an absorption half-time of about 17.6 hours. That input is not used here.

A dose in **mg** is intravenous.

### Effect site and typical concentrations

None, as for [bupivacaine](help:drugs/bupivacaine): total plasma concentration only, and no band.

### Where to be careful

Perineural catheter infusions, the postoperative rise in alpha-1-acid glycoprotein, and the effect of epinephrine are not represented.
