## What to look for

Pregabalin 150 mg by mouth at time zero, then 300 mg at 36 hours, by which time the first dose has all but gone: its half-life in this 40-year-old man is 5.8 hours. The second plasma peak is twice the first, 7.2 against 3.6 mcg/mL. Pregabalin is about 90 per cent absorbed whatever the dose, so doubling the dose doubles the concentration. Gabapentin's absorption saturates instead, and [doubling its dose](help:scenarios/gabapentin-saturable-absorption) raises its peak by less than half.

The dashed plasma line peaks 46 minutes after each dose. The flat start is the absorption lag of 19 minutes. Gabapentin's plasma peaks at almost 3 hours.

The solid line is the effect site, and it peaks 4.7 hours after each dose, at 2.2 and 4.5 mcg/mL. That delay comes from the cold pressor test in volunteers (van Esdonk and colleagues, 2018), in which the tolerance of pain followed the plasma concentration with a half-time of about 1.8 hours. One hour after the 300 mg dose the plasma is near its peak, 7.0 mcg/mL, but the effect site has reached only a third of its own peak; two hours after, about 70 per cent. On this model, a dose given an hour before incision is still far from its peak effect when surgery starts.

The shaded band, 1.3 to 5.4 mcg/mL, is the average concentration at steady state in adults taking 150 to 600 mg a day. A single dose of 300 mg peaks above it.

## Try next

- Add gabapentin to the dose table, 600 mg PO at time zero and 1200 mg PO at 36 hours. Its panel shows the other half of the comparison: a later, flatter peak, and a second peak 1.45 times the first rather than twice.
- Enter a **Serum creatinine** of 2.0 mg/dL in the Patient Profile. Creatinine clearance halves, the half-life doubles to about 11.5 hours, and 0.44 mcg/mL of the first dose is still there when the second is given.
- Delete the 300 mg row and change the first dose to 150 mg PO bid. The dose repeats every 12 hours, each peak sitting on the trough left by the one before: the peaks rise by about 30 per cent, to 4.7 mcg/mL, and by the third dose have all but stopped rising: steady state, within about a day.
