## What to look for

Sevoflurane 8 per cent in 6 L/min of oxygen for three minutes, then 3 per cent in 1 L/min, in a 70 kg, 40-year-old man (MAC 2.1 per cent). Press **Visualizer** under the plot and **Play**. The gold line on the circuit is the vaporizer setting; the circuit's fill is what the patient inspires.

**Overpressure fills the brain fast.** At 8 per cent, end-tidal passes 1 MAC in 13 seconds and the vessel-rich group, the effect site, in 1 minute 51 seconds. At three minutes end-tidal is 5.6 per cent (2.7 MAC) and the vessel-rich group 3.3 per cent, still taking up 52 mL/min of vapour.

**At three minutes the vaporizer goes to 3 per cent and the flow to 1 L/min, and the inspired level is now above the setting.** With the fresh gas below the minute ventilation, most of each breath is rebreathed gas, and the exhaled gas, at end-tidal, is richer than the fresh gas. So for three minutes, until 5.9 minutes, the circuit stands above the gold line: 3.5 per cent at 4 minutes against a setting of 3.

**The brain overshoots, then gives agent back for half an hour.** The vessel-rich group peaks at 3.56 per cent (1.70 MAC) at 3.9 minutes, after the turn-down, and from then until 35 minutes its level is above end-tidal. Watch its arrow on the blood line turn red: it is giving agent back, 11 mL/min at five minutes, while muscle, far below, goes on taking up 17. Agent moves from brain to muscle through the blood. By 30 minutes the brain and the lungs are level at 1.0 MAC.

**Low flow saves agent.** In the hour 3.2 L of vapour is delivered and 2.0 L of it vented, two-thirds; the patient holds 1.1 L at the end. [At 6 L/min throughout](scenario:visualizer-sevoflurane-washin), 9.0 L is delivered and 88 per cent of it vented, for a store of the same size.

## Try next

- Keep the overpressure for five minutes instead of three. The vessel-rich group reaches 2.2 MAC at five minutes, and the overshoot is deeper and longer.
- Leave the flow at 6 L/min at three minutes and only turn the vaporizer to 3 per cent. The inspired level falls to the setting at once, with no rebreathing to hold it up, and much more agent is vented.
