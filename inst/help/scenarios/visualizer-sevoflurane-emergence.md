## What to look for

Two hours of sevoflurane 2 per cent at 2 L/min of oxygen, then the vaporizer off and the oxygen up to 8 L/min, in a 70 kg, 40-year-old man (MAC 2.1 per cent). Press **Visualizer** under the plot, drag the time to 2:00:00 and press **Play**.

**At two hours the tissues are far from level.** End-tidal and the vessel-rich group, the effect site, are both at 1.72 per cent (0.82 MAC). Muscle is at 0.94 per cent, a little over half, and fat at 0.07 per cent, a twenty-fourth. But the amounts card shows where the agent is: 113 mL of vapour in the vessel-rich group, 743 in muscle and 357 in fat.

**At the vaporizer's turning off, the lungs empty first, and everything turns round.** At 8 L/min, above the minute ventilation, nothing is rebreathed and the circuit is empty of agent. End-tidal falls to 0.66 per cent in a minute. The vessel-rich group, now far above the lungs, gives agent back to the blood at 20 mL/min, and muscle, above the lungs too, starts giving agent back within half a minute. The arrows on the blood line under the vessel-rich group and muscle turn red.

**Fat goes on taking agent up.** The blood reaching every tissue is at the end-tidal level, and fat's level, 0.07 per cent, is far below it: agent the brain and muscle give back is carried on to fat. Fat keeps filling until 3:13, 73 minutes after the vaporizer was turned off, when end-tidal at last falls below it. Its green arrow is the last to turn.

**The brain empties fast; muscle and fat hold the tail.** From 2:03 muscle is the highest box. The vessel-rich group falls below 0.1 MAC at 2:12, 12 minutes after the vaporizer went off, but at four hours the body still holds 720 mL of vapour: 350 in muscle and 365 in fat, which holds more than muscle from 3:53. Those are what keep a trace of agent in the brain for hours.

## Making a video

A video is the whole run. At the speed the run opens with, 1 s = 2 min, the four hours play in two minutes and the vaporizer goes off a minute in: pause there in the lecture and ask where the agent will go.

## Try next

- Load [the same anesthetic with desflurane at 6 per cent](scenario:emergence-desflurane) and press **Visualizer**: its vessel-rich group falls below 0.1 MAC 9 minutes after the vaporizer is off, against 12 for sevoflurane, and its fat holds 189 L of gas against sevoflurane's 493.
- Change the oxygen at 120 minutes to 2 L/min. Now the exhaled agent is rebreathed, the circuit is no longer empty, and every box empties more slowly.
