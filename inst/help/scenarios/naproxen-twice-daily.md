## What to look for

Naproxen 500 mg by mouth twice a day, entered as one row of 500 mg PO bid, in a 40-year-old, 70 kg man. The app repeats the dose every 12 hours. Naproxen has no effect site in the model: Björnsson and Simonsson found its effect on pain followed the plasma concentration without a delay. The solid line is therefore the plasma.

The first dose peaks at 48 mcg/mL two and a half hours after it is taken. The thin black **time until threshold** line, read on the right axis, times the concentration against 29 mcg/mL. That is the total concentration at which, in their study of wisdom-tooth removal, naproxen halved the pain it could relieve. The first dose is above it from 37 minutes to 10 hours, and by the second dose, 12 hours in, the concentration has fallen to 25 mcg/mL. For two hours on the first night it is below the threshold.

The terminal half-life is 23 hours, nearly twice the 12-hour interval, so each dose adds to what is left of the last. The troughs climb from 25 to 40, 49, 55 and 60 mcg/mL, and the peaks from 48 to 107 mcg/mL by the fourth day. After the second dose the concentration never falls below the threshold again. The daily average is half its steady-state value on the first day, 77% on the second and 89% on the third. The label's 4 to 5 days to steady state reflects the same long half-life.

The steady-state average in the model is 92 mcg/mL: the dose rate divided by the clearance, 0.45 L/h. Healthy volunteers taking 500 mg twice a day average about 75 mcg/mL (van den Ouweland and colleagues, 1987). The difference is protein binding. Naproxen is more than 99% bound to albumin, and the binding saturates at these concentrations, so more of the drug is free and is cleared. The model is linear and does not show this, so at this dose and above it overstates the total concentration. The free concentration, which carries the effect, does keep rising with the dose.

After the last dose of the plot, at 84 hours, the black line reads 33.6 hours at the peak. That is how long the concentration would take to fall back below 29 mcg/mL if no more were given.

## Try next

- Give the same 1 g a day as 250 mg every 6 hours: change the row to 250 mg PO qid. The swing over the last 12 hours of the plot narrows from 65–107 to 76–94 mcg/mL. The first 250 mg never reaches the threshold on its own, though: it peaks at 24 mcg/mL. The concentration first crosses the threshold 23 minutes after the second dose, at 6 hours 23 minutes.
- Stop after the second day: add a row of 0 mg PO bid at 2 days, which ends the repeats. The last dose is then at 36 hours, and at 2 days the concentration is still 55 mcg/mL. The black line there reads 17 hours: the concentration stays above the threshold until 65 hours, more than a day after the last dose.
