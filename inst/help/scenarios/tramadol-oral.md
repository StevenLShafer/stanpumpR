## What to look for

One hundred milligrams of tramadol by mouth. As with codeine, the tramadol line (dashed) is plasma only: this model gives tramadol no effect site, because it represents only the opioid effect of the [desmetramadol](help:drugs/desmetramadol) formed from it. The desmetramadol row appears although none was given, rises to a few tens of ng/mL, and peaks around 90 minutes; its effect site peaks later still, near two and a half hours, which is where tramadol's analgesia is observed.

## Try next

- Change **CYP 2D6** to *Poor*. The desmetramadol curve shrinks, and the tramadol line rises, because formation is a branch of tramadol's own clearance: poor metabolisers have more parent and less metabolite.
- Remember what the model leaves out: tramadol's own monoaminergic analgesia, from serotonin and noradrenaline reuptake inhibition, is real and is **not** shown here. A poor metaboliser still gets that part, so the MEAC panel understates tramadol most in exactly those patients.
