## What to look for

Rocuronium 42 mg (0.6 mg/kg at 70 kg), an intubating dose. The effect site peaks at just over two minutes, the time at which intubating conditions are expected, and then falls. The band, 1 to 2.2 mcg/mL, is the range over which the block goes from deep to recovering.

The **time until threshold** line shows, at each moment, how long until the effect site falls to 1 mcg/mL: at time zero about 35 to 40 minutes, falling to zero as the concentration reaches the threshold. This is the start of recovery of neuromuscular function, not its completion; the model relates concentration to nothing, so there are no twitch heights or train-of-four counts on the plot.

## Try next

- Give 84 mg (1.2 mg/kg, a rapid-sequence dose). The time to the threshold roughly doubles for a doubled dose, because the fall through the band is on the slow part of the curve.
- Add 10 mg top-ups at 30 and 50 minutes and watch the time-until-threshold line jump with each, by more each time as the peripheral compartment fills.
- Replace the bolus with an infusion of 0.6 mg/kg/hr (the units offered are mg and mg/kg/hr) and see how long it takes to reach the band.

## Background

[Rocuronium](help:drugs/rocuronium) is Plaud's two-compartment model with a time to peak effect from Cortínez. Potentiation by inhaled agents, hypothermia and organ dysfunction, and reversal by sugammadex, are not modelled. [Time until threshold](help:models/recovery) explains the black line.
