## What to look for

Sixty milligrams of codeine by mouth. The codeine line (dashed) rises over about an hour and falls slowly, but codeine itself has no effect site: its row is plasma only, because its analgesia is the [morphine](help:drugs/morphine) formed from it. Watch the **morphine** row, which appears although no morphine was given. It rises later than codeine and peaks low, around 1.4 ng/mL near two hours, and it is the morphine effect site, not the codeine, that the MEAC panel scores.

## Try next

- Change **CYP 2D6** in the Patient Profile from *Normal* to *Poor*. The codeine line barely moves (formation is only a few per cent of its clearance), but the morphine curve collapses to almost nothing: this is the poor metaboliser who gets little analgesia from codeine.
- Change it to *Ultrarapid* and the morphine curve rises by about half. This is the phenotype behind the warnings against codeine in nursing mothers and children.
- Compare with 10 mg of oral [oxycodone](scenario:oral-oxycodone), which has its own effect site and does not depend on CYP2D6 for its main effect.
