## What to look for

The time axis runs for 24 hours. The effect site peaks at **11 minutes**, as fast as fentanyl, and the plasma falls steeply for the first hour as methadone distributes into tissue. Then both lines flatten and barely move for the rest of the day: methadone's elimination rate constant is 0.008/min, the smallest in the library, and its terminal half-life is more than a day.

Hover at 1 hour and at 24 hours. The concentration has fallen by much less than half in the intervening 23 hours.

A single 10 mg dose in a 70 kg patient holds an effect-site concentration above MEAC (60 ng/mL) for only a few hours, yet the drug is still largely in the body a day later. A second dose adds to what remains. This is why methadone accumulates with repeated dosing and why its steady state takes days to reach.

## Background

[Methadone](help:drugs/methadone) is Inturrisi's model in cancer-pain patients, with weight scaling. Methadone's half-life varies several-fold between individuals, more than for most drugs; the curve is a typical patient's.
