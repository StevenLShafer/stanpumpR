## What to look for

One 200 mg tablet of carbidopa-levodopa (as 2 × 25/100) in a 68-year-old man with advanced Parkinson's disease. The dashed line is plasma levodopa, the solid line the motor effect site of [levodopa](help:drugs/levodopa), whose equilibration half-life of 0.39 hours Mao and colleagues measured from the UPDRS Part III scores of patients with advanced, fluctuating disease.

- The **plasma** peaks at about 81 minutes, 1.9 mcg/mL, inside the shaded AGNP band. The tablet is absorbed through a transit compartment, so the rise is slow for the first half hour.
- The **effect site** peaks at about 2 hours, 39 minutes after the plasma, at 1.6 mcg/mL. In the clinic, that is when the patient is best.
- The **threshold**, 0.81 mcg/mL, is the effect-site concentration at which Mao's model gives half its maximal improvement in the UPDRS Part III score. It is a modelled half-maximal response, not a measured ON/OFF boundary, but it is the nearest the model has to one: it is crossed upward at about 52 minutes and downward at about 4 hours 15 minutes, as the dose wears off. The *time until threshold* line shows, at each moment, how long is left above it.

## Try next

- Change the dose to **100 mg**. The effect site only just reaches the threshold, for about 20 minutes, although the plasma peak is still almost 1 mcg/mL. In advanced disease the concentration-response relation is steep, so a dose that is too small does little.
- Add a second **200 mg at 4 hours** (240 minutes). The effect site is still at 0.9 mcg/mL when it is taken, so it never falls below the threshold. Move it to 5 hours and a gap of about 80 minutes below the threshold opens, from 4 hours 15 minutes until the second dose takes effect at about 5 hours 33 minutes.
- Try [levodopa intestinal gel](help:drugs/levodopaIntestinalGel) at 60 mg/hr PO after a 100 mg morning dose: the continuous enteral infusion removes the peaks and troughs.
- Add **levodopaSimon**, 200 mg PO at 0, the [model fitted in patients with peak-dose dyskinesia](help:drugs/levodopaSimon). Its threshold is higher (1.41 mcg/mL) and its response steeper, so the same tablet keeps its effect site above threshold for about 90 minutes instead of more than three hours.
- Swap the gel for [Lecigon](help:drugs/levodopaEntacaponeIntestinalGel) at the same 60 mg/hr PO: with entacapone in the gel the plateau is about 44% higher (3.4 against 2.35 mcg/mL), which is why the continuous rate is lowered by about 35% when switching.

This is advanced disease. In early Parkinson's disease the effect of a single dose equilibrates far more slowly and lasts longer, because a long-duration response builds over days; see [Parkinson disease drugs](help:models/parkinsons).
