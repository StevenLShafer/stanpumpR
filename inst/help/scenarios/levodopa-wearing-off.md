## What to look for

One 200 mg tablet of carbidopa-levodopa (as 2 × 25/100) in a 68-year-old man with advanced Parkinson's disease. The dashed line is plasma levodopa, the solid line the motor effect site of [levodopa](help:drugs/levodopa), whose equilibration half-life of 0.39 hours Mao and colleagues measured from the UPDRS Part III scores of patients with advanced, fluctuating disease.

- The **plasma** peaks at about 84 minutes, 1.4 mcg/mL, inside the shaded AGNP band. The tablet is absorbed through a transit compartment, so the rise is slow for the first half hour.
- The **effect site** peaks at about 2 hours, 36 minutes after the plasma, at 1.6 mcg/mL. In the clinic, that is when the patient is best.
- The **threshold**, Mao's half-maximal concentration of 0.81 mcg/mL, is crossed upward at about 52 minutes (the patient turns ON) and downward at about 4 hours 15 minutes (wearing off). The *time until threshold* line shows, at each moment, how long the patient has left.

## Try next

- Change the dose to **100 mg**. The effect site only just reaches the threshold, for about a quarter of an hour, although the plasma peak is still almost 1 mcg/mL. In advanced disease the response is steep: a dose that is too small does almost nothing, and the useful dose has little margin before dyskinesia (Mao's dyskinesia EC50 is 0.60 mcg/mL in the same model).
- Add a second **200 mg at 4 hours** (240 minutes). The effect site is still at 0.9 mcg/mL when it is taken, so the patient stays ON. Move it to 5 hours and an OFF period of about 80 minutes opens, from 4 hours 15 minutes until the second dose takes effect at about 5 hours 33 minutes.
- Try [levodopa intestinal gel](help:drugs/levodopaIntestinalGel) at 60 mg/hr PO after a 100 mg morning dose: the continuous enteral infusion removes the peaks and troughs.
- Add **levodopaSimon**, 200 mg PO at 0, the [model fitted in patients with peak-dose dyskinesia](help:drugs/levodopaSimon). Its threshold is higher (1.41 mcg/mL) and its response steeper, so the same tablet keeps its effect site above threshold for about 90 minutes instead of more than three hours.
- Swap the gel for [Lecigon](help:drugs/levodopaEntacaponeIntestinalGel) at the same 60 mg/hr PO: with entacapone in the gel the plateau is about 45% higher (3.4 against 2.35 mcg/mL), which is why the continuous rate is lowered by about 35% when switching.

This is advanced disease. In early Parkinson's disease the effect of a single dose equilibrates far more slowly and lasts longer, because a long-duration response builds over days; see [Parkinson disease drugs](help:models/parkinsons).
