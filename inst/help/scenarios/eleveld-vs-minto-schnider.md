## What to look for

A 70 kg, 170 cm, 40-year-old man is given propofol 2 mg/kg with 100 mcg/kg/min for an hour, and remifentanil 1 mcg/kg with 0.2 mcg/kg/min for an hour. With **Minto / Schnider** ticked under Graph Options, each dose is run twice: through the library's Eleveld models, and through the Schnider propofol and Minto remifentanil models that most TCI pumps run. Each panel holds two lines of each kind: the Eleveld curve in the drug's own colour, and the Schnider or Minto curve in a darker or lighter shade, named in the legend as *propofol (Schnider)* and *remifentanil (Minto)*.

- **Propofol.** Schnider's central volume is 4.27 L for every patient; Eleveld's is 6.3 L for this one. So the same 140 mg bolus peaks in the plasma (dashed) at 33 mcg/mL on Schnider's model and 22 on Eleveld's. The effect site (solid) is less far apart: 7.9 against 8.7 mcg/mL at the peak, 1.6 minutes after the bolus. Through the infusion Schnider's model sits a little lower (2.8 against 3.0 mcg/mL at 30 minutes), because its larger slow compartment, 238 L against 157, keeps taking drug up, and after the infusion stops it falls a little faster for the same reason.
- **Remifentanil.** The two lines almost coincide. Eleveld's remifentanil model was fitted to pooled data that included Minto's, and for a patient like this one, near the centre of Minto's population, there is little between them: the effect site is 5.3 ng/mL on Minto's model and 5.5 on Eleveld's at the end of the infusion, and both are below 0.3 ng/mL thirty minutes later.
- **Hover** over either panel and the readout gives both values, the library's model first and the other by its name.

## Try next

- Untick *Minto / Schnider* and the second lines go; tick it again and they come back. The box is enabled only while propofol or remifentanil is in the dose table.
- Set the age to 80 and apply. Eleveld's propofol effect site now ends the hour at 3.9 mcg/mL and Schnider's at 3.0: Eleveld's clearance falls with age, Schnider's barely does. Minto's ke0 is a function of age, 0.595 - 0.007 (age - 40) per minute, and at 80 it is 0.315, so the Minto effect site rises more slowly after the bolus (4.6 against 6.0 ng/mL at 1.6 minutes) and ends the hour higher (7.0 against 6.3 ng/mL).
- Set the weight to 120 kg at the same height. The Schnider plasma peak after the 240 mg bolus is 56 mcg/mL against Eleveld's 33, because Schnider's central volume is 4.27 L whatever the weight. This man's BMI is 41.5, just under the pumps' limit of 42 for a man, and just short of the peak of the James lean body mass that the Schnider and Minto models use, a parabola in weight that falls past a BMI of 43. [The obesity scenario](scenario:james-lean-body-mass) shows what happens beyond it.
- Set the age to 6 months (6, with *mo*), the weight to 7 kg and the height to 65 cm. Schnider's propofol clearance comes out negative for an infant this size, so that model is not drawn and the app says why; the Eleveld curve, which was fitted down to premature neonates, is still there. Minto's remifentanil line is still drawn, but Minto's volunteers were 20 to 85 years old, and this is an extrapolation. (A five-year-old of 20 kg still gets a Schnider line, with a clearance of 0.33 L/min that is positive only just, and an extrapolation too: the volunteers were 25 to 81.)
