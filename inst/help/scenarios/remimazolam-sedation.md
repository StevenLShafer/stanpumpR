## What to look for

Three boluses, 5 mg then 2.5 mg at five-minute intervals, as remimazolam is titrated for procedural sedation. Each bolus adds a plasma spike; the effect site (tPeak 2.5 minutes) smooths them into a rising staircase that reaches the band by the third dose.

After the last bolus the effect site falls out of the band within about 15 minutes. Remimazolam is hydrolysed by tissue esterases, so its clearance is high and its offset short; compare midazolam, whose offset from the same concentration takes several times longer.

## Try next

- Replace the three boluses with midazolam 2 mg, 1 mg and 1 mg at the same times (midazolam's band is 0.04 to 0.12 mcg/mL) and compare the tails.
- Add a fourth and fifth dose at 15 and 20 minutes. The staircase keeps rising: each dose arrives before the last has worn off.
- Change the patient to an 80-year-old woman. Remimazolam's clearance is higher in women and its V3 larger with age; see what changes.

## Background

[Remimazolam](help:drugs/remimazolam) is Eleveld's 2025 model with weight, age and sex covariates. The library's typical value (0.2 mcg/mL) sits below its band (0.3 to 0.6); the *Range* band is the one shown here.
