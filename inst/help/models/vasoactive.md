# Vasoactive and resuscitation drugs

The intravenous vasopressors, inotropes, vasodilators, antiarrhythmics and resuscitation drugs of intensive care are listed under **Vasoactive and resuscitation** in the startup menu. Each is a **concentration model only**: no blood pressure, heart rate or rhythm is predicted, and no drug in the group has an effect site or a shaded band.

A drug is offered only where a published pharmacokinetic model could be read and checked against its source. Every drug that was asked for is catalogued, implemented or not, in `inst/extdata/vasoactiveRegistry.csv`, with the source and, for the ones not yet offered, what stands in the way.

### Offered

- [Epinephrine](help:drugs/epinephrine): Abboud 2009, adults in septic shock.
- Clevidipine is offered under **Antihypertensives** ([its page](help:drugs/clevidipine)).
