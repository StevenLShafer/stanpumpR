## What to look for

Rocuronium 42 mg (0.6 mg/kg at 70 kg), an intubating dose. The effect site peaks at about four and a half minutes, the median time to maximum block at the adductor pollicis in Cortínez's patients, and then falls. The band, 1 to 2.2 mcg/mL, is the range over which the block goes from deep to recovering.

The **time until threshold** line shows, at each moment, how long until the effect site falls to 1 mcg/mL: at time zero about 39 minutes, falling to zero as the concentration reaches the threshold. This is the start of recovery of neuromuscular function, not its completion; the model relates concentration to nothing, so there are no twitch heights or train-of-four counts on the plot.

## Try next

- Give 84 mg (1.2 mg/kg, a rapid-sequence dose). The time to the threshold lengthens from about 39 to about 76 minutes: doubling the dose adds the time the concentration takes to halve at that point of the curve, which here is the slow phase, with a half-life of about 38 minutes.
- Add 10 mg top-ups at 10 and 20 minutes and watch the time-until-threshold line jump with each: by about 12.5 minutes the first time and about 13 the second, more as the peripheral compartment fills.
- Replace the bolus with an infusion of 0.6 mg/kg/hr (the units offered are mg and mg/kg/hr), and set *Max time* to 8 hours: the infusion alone reaches the band in about a quarter of an hour, crosses its upper edge at about an hour and a half, and settles near 2.5 mcg/mL, above it.

## Background

[Rocuronium](help:drugs/rocuronium) is Kleijn's two-compartment model, from the same fit as the library's sugammadex, with a time to peak effect from Cortínez. Potentiation by inhaled agents, hypothermia and organ dysfunction, and reversal by sugammadex, are not modelled. [Time until threshold](help:models/recovery) explains the black line.
