### What this is

Free doxorubicin released from pegylated liposomal doxorubicin (Doxil, Caelyx), formed by [doxorubicinLiposomal](help:drugs/doxorubicinLiposomal) and drawn on its own panel. It is not offered on its own.

### The model

Hsu 2018 (*Pharm Res* 35:106) fitted it jointly with the liposome's release: two compartments, 9.52 and 35.1 L, an intercompartmental clearance of 2.87 L/h and a clearance of 2.68 L/h. It receives the released drug and, directly, the 2% of each dose that was never encapsulated.

### Where to be careful

Because release and elimination were fitted together, only their ratio is identified, and the clearance is not conventional doxorubicin's (about a twentieth of it). The panel shows the free doxorubicin the study's assay measured after liposomal dosing; it is not a model of doxorubicin given on its own.
