### The model

M6-SUZ is the active metabolite of [suzetrigine](help:drugs/suzetrigine). It is 3.7 times less potent at NaV1.8, and its exposure is about three times suzetrigine's. The FDA's exposure-response analysis used suzetrigine plus a third of M6-SUZ. It cannot be dosed; it is drawn whenever suzetrigine is.

Its parameters were fitted together with suzetrigine's in the applicant's model 2822 (FDA Integrated Review of NDA 219209, Table 149). The model has two compartments:

- central volume 1.7 L and peripheral volume 29.5 L, both scaled with weight/76.2 kg;
- intercompartmental clearance 8.75 L/h, scaled with fat-free mass to the 0.75 power when the scaling switch is on, anchored at suzetrigine's median subject;
- clearance 0.273 times suzetrigine's clearance.

M6-SUZ is formed from 80% of suzetrigine's clearance. In a suzetrigine simulation its clearance is multiplied by the same induced enzyme as suzetrigine's, so the half-lives in the tables below are those before induction.

The label gives an effective half-life of 33.0 hours at steady state.
