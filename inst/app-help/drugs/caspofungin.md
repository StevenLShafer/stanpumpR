### One name, the model chosen for the patient

stanpumpR holds 1 published model of caspofungin so far. You do not choose between models: stanpumpR picks the one whose study population best matches the patient you entered (a rule of Steven Shafer's, 9 November 2026). The References panel under the plot names the model in use.

| Model | Source | Studied in |
|---|---|---|
| [caspofunginXu](help:drugs/caspofunginXu) | Xu 2026 | 29 critically ill children, 0.33-16 years, 4.9-74 kg |

**What that means for caspofungin now.** Children get Xu's model, the one it was fitted in. **An adult gets it too, as an extrapolation**, until an adult model is added: Würthwein and colleagues' model of 46 adults with invasive aspergillosis given 70 to 200 mg (*Antimicrob Agents Chemother* 2013;57:1664-1671), chosen by Shafer on 28 November 2026, joins when its full text is read. Scaled up to the reference man (1.82 m²), Xu's model gives a clearance of 0.34 L/h, against 0.41 L/h in Würthwein's adults and 0.72 L/h (12 mL/min) in the label. So for an adult it draws about 50 mg / 0.34 L/h = 147 mg·h/L a day on 50 mg, where the label's adults reached 87.

### Models not used

Yang and colleagues (*Front Pharmacol* 2022;13:993330) fitted 299 adults with steps: clearance doubled at 70 kg and rose 46% below 35 g/L of albumin, so the curve would jump between two patients a kilogram apart. Niu and colleagues (*Front Pharmacol* 2020;11:184) fitted 48 children with one compartment, from samples taken mostly late in the dosing interval.

### Target concentrations

No band and no threshold are drawn. The sources judge exposure by the ratio of the daily area under the curve to the organism's MIC, from a mouse model, not by a concentration. The label gives no therapeutic range.

### Pregnancy

No pharmacokinetic study of caspofungin in pregnancy was found. Caspofungin is cleared by slow breakdown, not by the kidneys, so the rise in filtration of pregnancy does not reach it, and nothing is applied.
