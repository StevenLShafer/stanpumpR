### The model

OR-1896 is levosimendan's long-acting active metabolite. It is **not dosed directly here**: it has no dose units and does not appear in the dose table's drug list or the startup menu. Its whole curve comes from the [levosimendan](help:drugs/levosimendan) you give, drawn on a row of its own.

Levosimendan becomes OR-1896 in two steps. A small share of the parent reaches the intestine, where bacteria reduce it to OR-1855, which is absorbed and then acetylated to OR-1896. Some OR-1896 is deacetylated back to OR-1855. The parameters come from Bertin and colleagues in Lausanne (*Clin Pharmacokinet* 2026;65:241-255). They modelled levosimendan, OR-1855 and OR-1896 together in arterial plasma from 21 critically ill patients on veno-arterial ECMO: 15 adults and six neonates and infants. The full-precision estimates are in their supplement. For adults at 70 kg:

- 1.45% of the parent's elimination passes through a delay compartment, at 0.0127 /h (a mean delay of about 79 hours), before it becomes OR-1855;
- OR-1855 is eliminated at 0.01 /h and acetylated to OR-1896 at 0.0722 /h;
- OR-1896 is eliminated at 0.01 /h and converted back to OR-1855 at 0.012 /h;
- both metabolites share a volume of 15.9 L, rising with weight to the power 0.574, and OR-1855's elimination falls with weight to the power 0.611.

The two eliminations and the conversion back were fixed in the source, not estimated.

### How it joins the library's levosimendan

The library's levosimendan is Jonsson 2003's model, fitted in 190 patients with heart failure, not Bertin's. The two are joined through Bertin's share of the parent's elimination that becomes the metabolite, 1.45% at 70 kg, evaluated at the patient's weight and applied to Jonsson's elimination. Concentrations identify only the ratio of a metabolite's formation to its clearance, not the share on its own. So the share is used together with Bertin's own metabolite parameters, which keeps that ratio as Bertin fitted it. The whole chain (parent, delay, OR-1855 and OR-1896) is solved exactly as one linear system by a matrix exponential, as enalapril's transit chain is.

The source's amounts are molar. OR-1896 is drawn in its own mass, ng/mL, using the molecular weights 245.28 for OR-1896 and 280.29 for levosimendan.

### The check against measurements

Antila and colleagues (*Br J Clin Pharmacol* 2004;57:412-415) gave 24 patients with severe heart failure 0.05 or 0.1 mcg/kg/min for 7 days and sampled them for two more weeks. Their data never entered this model. At their patients' weight, about 84 kg:

| | Antila 2004, measured | stanpumpR |
|---|---|---|
| Peak, 0.05 mcg/kg/min | 9.9 ng/mL | 8.3 ng/mL |
| Peak, 0.1 mcg/kg/min | 17.1 ng/mL | 16.6 ng/mL |
| Area, 0.05 mcg/kg/min | 2370 ng·h/mL | 2400 ng·h/mL |
| Area, 0.1 mcg/kg/min | 3890 ng·h/mL | 4790 ng·h/mL |
| Half-life | 81 h | 88 h |
| Time of the peak | 170-180 h (medians) | 216 h |

The measured peaks came 2 to 12 hours (medians) after the infusion ended at 168 hours. The model's peak, at 216 hours, comes about two days after it ended, later than measured.

The area at the higher rate is 23% above the measurement. No linear model can match both areas, because the measured areas are not proportional to the dose. The higher rate gave 18% less than twice the lower rate's area, although its peak was close to double.

A second study, Kivikko and colleagues (*Int J Clin Pharmacol Ther* 2002;40:465-471), gave 12 patients 0.2 mcg/kg/min for 24 hours. Its data never entered this model either. At their patients' size (78 kg, 173 cm, 62 years):

| | Kivikko 2002, measured (mean ± SD) | stanpumpR |
|---|---|---|
| Peak | 5.5 ± 2.2 ng/mL | 5.3 ng/mL |
| Time of the peak | 77 ± 26 h (range 48-120) | 118 h |
| Area | 948 ± 535 ng·h/mL | 1330 ng·h/mL |
| Half-life | 77 ± 31 h | 91 h |

The two studies agree with each other about the model. The height of the peak is right, but OR-1896 rises and falls more slowly than measured: its peak comes about two days later than measured, its half-life is 10-20% long, and its area is high. Bertin fixed the metabolites' eliminations and the conversion back from the literature, and those fixed values are the likely cause. They are kept as published, and the bias is stated here rather than fitted away.

### What is not drawn

- **OR-1855.** Bertin's chain gives OR-1855 about a third of what Antila measured (a peak of 2.4 against 7.8 ng/mL). The likely reason is acetylator status (NAT2), which neither study typed: slow acetylators convert OR-1855 to OR-1896 more slowly and accumulate OR-1855. Bertin's acetylation rate varied 91% between patients. So OR-1855 is carried in the calculation but has no panel.
- **Children.** Bertin found acetylation 73% slower in neonates and infants. It is not applied: the library's levosimendan was fitted in adults.
- **An effect site.** OR-1896 is a calcium sensitiser and vasodilator like the parent, but no concentration-effect relation is plotted.
- **A therapeutic range.** No band is drawn: no therapeutic concentration range is established for OR-1896, or for levosimendan, whose dose is titrated to the haemodynamic response.

### Body size

Bertin's own weight terms (the metabolite volume and OR-1855's elimination) are kept as fitted. The rate constants without a weight term follow fat-free mass when [the scaling switch](help:models/fat-free-mass) is on, as every parameter in the library does. They are anchored at Bertin's adults (12 men and 3 women, 79 kg, 62 years), so a patient like them receives the published values. Bertin's table gives no height, so each sex's height is NCD-RisC's mean for Swiss 19-year-olds in 1985, less the height lost with age: 175.9 cm for men and 162.5 cm for women. Those 19-year-olds were born in 1966, a few years after Bertin's patients, which changes the result by about 0.1%.

### Where to be careful

- The variability is large. Bertin's acetylation rate varies 91% between patients, so a fast or slow acetylator's OR-1896 can be well above or below this curve.
- Bertin's patients were on ECMO. The check against Antila's heart-failure patients is what supports using the chain outside it.
- No pregnancy data exist.
