### The model

ACT-333679 is the active metabolite of [selexipag](help:drugs/selexipag). Carboxylesterases form it by hydrolysis, and it is 37 times as potent as selexipag at the prostacyclin receptor, so it carries most of the effect. It cannot be dosed; it is drawn whenever selexipag is.

Its parameters were fitted together with selexipag's by Krause and colleagues (*CPT Pharmacometrics Syst Pharmacol* 2017;6:477-485, doi 10.1002/psp4.12202). The model has two compartments, all values apparent:

- central volume 4.65 L × (weight/70)<sup>0.88</sup>;
- transfer rate constants 1.04 /h out to the peripheral compartment and 0.18 /h back;
- elimination rate constant 0.49 /h in women and 0.57 /h in men.

The patient is assumed to take no other drug for pulmonary hypertension. With an endothelin receptor antagonist the elimination rate constant was 0.57 /h, with a PDE5 inhibitor 0.52 /h, and with both 0.71 /h.

The label gives a terminal half-life of 6.2 to 13.5 hours and an effective half-life of 3 to 4 hours.
