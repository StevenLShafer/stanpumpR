# Cheng 2026: IVIG population PK in children with PID and SAD

## Study
Retrospective, de-identified electronic-record data from one tertiary paediatric hospital (Great Ormond Street), IVIG given April 2019 to April 2024. 64 children (44 PID, 20 SAD; of SAD, 15 after rituximab and 5 after CAR-T), age median 4.08 y (0.06-16.8), weight median 18.6 kg (3.15-95.3), 444 plasma IgG samples (mostly troughs), immunoturbidimetric assay, LLOQ 0.07 g/L, all samples above it. Median dose 0.56 g/kg (0.24-1.38) every 28 days; local policy 0.3 g/kg every 3 weeks for PID and 0.5 g/kg every 4 weeks for SAD. Products: Privigen 53 patients, Octagam 9, Gamunex 2. NONMEM 7.5.1, FOCEI, Xpose; 1000-sample bootstrap (PDF p.4-5).

## Model and parameters
Two-compartment, first-order elimination, combined additive and proportional error; measured IgG = estimated baseline (endogenous) IgG, CBAS, plus the model's exogenous IgG. Allometry fixed a priori: clearances 0.75, volumes 1.0 exponent, reference 70 kg (estimated exponents 0.783 for CL and 0.743 for V, CIs include 0.75 and 1.0, so fixed; p.7). Total volume V1 + V2 = 10.96 L/70 kg (matches the 10.96 stated in the text, p.7). Simulations: 1000 virtual children at median covariates, targets trough >8 g/L (PID) and 6 g/L (SAD); at 0.4, 0.5, 0.6 g/kg every 28 days AUC over 28 days 200.2, 211.5, 222.4 g/L*day; probability of staying above 6 g/L (SAD) 63.8-72.1%, above 8 g/L (PID) 43.8-51.9%; with a 1 g/kg loading dose and 0.5 g/kg maintenance 62.7% of the interval above 8 g/L vs 48.7% without (p.7-8; abstract gives 44.8-51.9%, text 43.8-51.9%).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD or effect-site model; not an opioid. The "therapeutic" levels are trough IgG targets (total serum/plasma IgG, g/L): BSI-UKPIN at least 8-10 g/L, AAAAI above 5-8 g/L, SAD above 6 g/L (p.2). No MEAC, ke0, tPeak or potency ratio.

## Covariates
Weight (fixed allometry), disease type (SAD vs PID) on CL and on baseline IgG, IgM on baseline IgG. Age, postmenstrual age maturation, sex and CD19+ count gave no significant improvement (p.7).

## Caveats
n = 64, entirely paediatric, mostly trough samples; baseline IgG for PID fixed at 4 g/L and for SAD taken from pre-treatment IgG (median 3.11 g/L) rather than measured under therapy (p.3). Authors note the lower SAD clearance may reflect the heavily pretreated cohort and baseline estimation (p.9). Wide bootstrap intervals for CL (0.23-0.67) and Q.
