## Caveats
Opinion only. Points made: pre-filled syringes reduce error and contamination risk but are costly and some drugs are unstable in syringe. Pre-filled remifentanil syringes are not commercially available. Diprifusor pumps (which required glass pre-filled propofol 1% or 2% syringes) are now sold only in Japan. In NAP5, 18 of more than 160 accidental-awareness cases were drug swaps, only 1 during TIVA. Titrated propofol induction is endorsed for most patients.
