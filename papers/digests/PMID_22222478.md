# Liu 2012: closed-loop propofol-remifentanil guided by M-Entropy

## Study
Single-centre (Hopital Foch, Suresnes), prospective, single-blind RCT, 70 recruited Nov 2006-Apr 2007; 61 analysed (manual n=31, dual-loop n=30). Adults 18-80 y, elective lung/vascular/orthopaedic/gynaecologic/urologic/ENT surgery (Table 1, PDF p5). Median [IQR]: age 55 [41-70] vs 56 [36-71] y; weight 74 [62-82] vs 74 [60-83] kg; height 169 vs 171 cm; M/F 16/15 vs 14/16.
Both arms: TCI of propofol (Schnider 1999 set) and remifentanil (Minto 1997 set), **effect-site targeting**, run on Infusion Toolbox 95 v4.11 with Alaris Asena GH pumps. Target: state entropy (SE) 50, then SE 40-60; remifentanil also to hold RE 40-60 or RE-SE <= 5. Manual arm: clinician-chosen Ce. Dual-loop arm: cascade PID controller changing the two Ce targets every 5 s-scale checks, waiting for effect-site equilibration (from the PK models) between changes; interaction rules between the two drugs (p3). Default Ce limits in the controller, from the text: propofol lower limit 1.3 ug/mL; remifentanil upper limit 12 ng/mL (p5; Table-less, in Results text).
Primary outcome: SE Global Score = (MDAPE + wobble) / %time SE 40-60 (Varvel 1992 measures), lower is better.

## Model and parameters
No PK model was fitted; the paper only **uses** Schnider (propofol) and Minto (remifentanil) with sex, age, weight, height entered. No parameter table.

- Table 2 induction (PDF p6): propofol 0.9 [0.6-1.4] mg/kg in both; remifentanil 1.8 [1.2-2.5] vs 1.1 [0.8-1.8] ug/kg.
- Dual-loop ran 68 h total with 1,250 automatic target changes (519 propofol, 731 remifentanil); no manual overrides apart from raising the remifentanil limit in 2 patients and lowering the propofol limit in 3 (p5). No awareness with recall.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: EEG spectral entropy (SE, 0.8-32 Hz; RE, 0.8-47 Hz incl. facial EMG). SE 40-60 was the "adequate" band; set point SE 50.
- No ke0, t1/2 ke0, tPeak, EC50, MEAC or analgesic concentration is reported. Effect-site delays came from the Schnider/Minto models and are not quoted.
- Remifentanil effect-site medians (5.9 ng/mL, Table 3) are controller outputs during surgery under propofol, not a defined minimum effective concentration; no assay (targets are model-calculated, no measured concentrations).
- No equianalgesic or potency ratio. (Postoperative morphine "median normalized morphine" 0.09 vs 0.07 mg/kg, Table 3.)

## Covariates
None analysed. Demographics (sex, age, weight, height) feed the Schnider/Minto models.

## Caveats
- Single-blind, single centre; one investigator managed 80% of the manual patients (p5). Manual arm had few patients after 3 h (Fig 3).
- RE-SE difference is used by the controller as an amplifier because neuromuscular block suppresses facial EMG; no direct analgesia measure.
- Table 2 propofol induction dose is 0.9 vs 0.9 mg/kg with P=0.07, which looks odd for identical medians (as printed).
- Per-kg basis (actual weight?) for mg/kg/h and ug/kg/min is not stated.
- Authors hold a patent on the controller gain constants.
