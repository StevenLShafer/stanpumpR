# Fabregas 2002: propofol sedation and airway obstruction in Parkinson disease

## Study
21 adults with advanced, drug-resistant Parkinson disease (PD) having functional stereotactic pallidotomy or subthalamic electrode implantation under local anaesthesia (Table 1, p.1379; mean infusion 7.12 h, range 0.99-10.12 h). Premedication diazepam 5 mg orally 2 h before. Propofol by Diprifusor TCI (Marsh PK, central volume scaled to weight), programmed to reach 1 ug/mL plasma in 2 min, then adjusted manually to sedation. No opioid given. Arterial blood (7 mL) every 30 min (if 10 min after a new target) and at 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 10, 15, 20 min after stopping; plasma propofol by HPLC-UV (251 nm), LOD 0.075 ug/mL, CV 4.12% for 0.1-5 ug/mL (p.1379). 355 plasma samples, 86 below the limit of quantification, observed range 0.08-2.58 ug/mL (p.1381). Sedation by Ramsay scale and airway obstruction by a 4-category score (Table 2, p.1380), recorded 10 min after each new target. 356 sedation and 355 airway scores. No patient was in Ramsay 6; 16 observations (6 patients) were airway score 4.

## Model and parameters
Plasma PK profiles were built nonparametrically (linear interpolation of measured concentrations; values below LOQ set to 0.0375 ug/mL), then a proportional-odds (cumulative logistic Emax, gamma fixed to 1) model of P(score >= m) versus Ce was fitted with ke0 estimated, in NONMEM V (Laplacian). No PK model was fitted: the authors say the design is unsuitable.

PK performance of the Diprifusor (Marsh) set against measured plasma (Fig. 1, p.1382): concentrations were systematically overpredicted by the TCI device (most prediction errors below 0%; the Loess line sits around -35 to -45% by eye from the image, derived, no MDPE number printed in the text).

The C50 values are described as steady-state plasma concentrations giving 50% probability of being at or above the score (Table 3 footnote). Using Ce instead of plasma improved the objective function by 38 (sedation) and 25 (airway) points (p.1382). Case-deletion diagnostics (Table 4, p.1383): bias within a few percent for sedation parameters (text layer only), wider for airway (ke0 MDPE about 11% with 95% CI -37 to 59).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End points: Ramsay sedation score (levels 3, 4, 5 vs baseline 2; level 6 not reached) and a 4-level airway obstruction score (1 normal, 2 deep breathing, 3 snoring, 4 severe obstruction).
- ke0: 0.24 /min for sedation, 0.50 /min for airway obstruction (Table 3). The authors say these bracket Schnider's 0.456 /min (their ref 18; the EEG value). Refitting with ke0 fixed to Schnider's: minor change for sedation, significant OBJF rise for airway (p.1385). No tPeak is stated (not calculated here: depends on the PK).
- Probability modes at steady-state plasma concentration (Fig. 4, p.1383): sedation score 3 at 0.35 ug/mL, score 4 at 1.55 ug/mL; airway score 2 at 0.75 ug/mL, score 3 at 8.5 ug/mL.
- Proposed therapeutic target: steady-state plasma propofol 0.35 ug/mL, sedation score 3 with minimal airway obstruction (abstract, p.1386).
- Comparators the authors quote: C50 0.31 ug/mL for PONV reduction (Gan), 1.38 ug/mL EEG (Schnider), 4.5 ug/mL LOC (Wakeling); ICU C50s for Ramsay 2-6 of 0.13, 0.5, 0.74, 1.48, 2.34 ug/mL (Barr), in ventilated patients, some on opioids (p.1385).
- Opioid audit: not an opioid paper (no opioid was given); no MEAC or opioid potency ratio.

## Covariates
Age, weight, height, gender, duration of surgery tested on C50 (equation 6); none significant (P > 0.05). No covariate retained. IIV on C50 significant (P < 0.001); not supported on gamma or ke0.

## Caveats
Plasma-only sampling, no PK model, so Ce depends on the interpolated profile. Ramsay scale has no state for sedated-but-agitated; baseline 2 also occurs at high concentrations. Mental status fluctuates over a 7-h case. Targets could not be raised for safety (airway inaccessible), so deep sedation and severe obstruction were barely sampled and C50 for the top categories sit near the highest observed concentration. Diazepam premedication, PD drugs and no opioid limit transfer. Diprifusor (Marsh) overpredicted plasma concentrations, so any concentration here that is quoted from the pump display would be too high; the C50s use measured plasma.
