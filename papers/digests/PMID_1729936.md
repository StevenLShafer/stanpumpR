# Sebel 1992: Reduction of the MAC of desflurane with fentanyl

## Study
- 134 adults, ASA 1-2, age 39 +/- 13 yr, weight 78 +/- 14.7 kg; Emory and Duke.
- Five randomised groups: desflurane/O2; desflurane/O2 + fentanyl 3 or 6 ug/kg; isoflurane/O2 + fentanyl 3 or 6 ug/kg; stratified 18-30 and 31-65 yr. No isoflurane/O2 group without fentanyl; the isoflurane + fentanyl 6 ug/kg MACs were not reported (unreliable).
- Fentanyl bolus over 20 s one minute before thiopental 2-5 mg/kg induction; succinylcholine; end-tidal agent by up-down method; incision about 25 min after fentanyl.
- Venous plasma fentanyl at skin incision (RIA). End point: movement at skin incision. Logistic regression (Appendix); recovery times also compared.

Isoflurane comparator (Table 1, PDF p.4, printed p.55, visually checked; Results PDF p.3, printed p.54): only the
fentanyl 3 ug/kg isoflurane groups gave MAC values. The study had **no fentanyl-free isoflurane arm**, so no
within-study isoflurane MAC reduction exists. The isoflurane/fentanyl 6 ug/kg determinations were not reported:
the authors judged them unreliable because the conventional vaporizer could not be set smoothly near 0.2%
end-tidal and the analyser read only one significant figure there (PDF p.2, printed p.53). Against historical
isoflurane-only controls (ref. 7) the authors put the reduction at 47%, with the caveat that those controls received
no other drugs (Discussion, PDF p.6, printed p.57); that is a comparison with cited background, not a measured
reduction. Table 1's n are the patients whose pairs gave a MAC determination (76 in all).
Table A1 (dose indicator model, ln desflurane): A 9.3975, B1 4.4405, B2 5.7233, B3 5.0925. Table A2 (continuous ln(fentanyl + 0.01), desflurane on original scale): A 2.8424, B1 1.1059, B2 1.2642; MAC = 2.2484 - 0.8748 ln(F + 0.01) (appendix, p. 59). No effect of age on the response (P usually > 0.5).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
MAC (movement to skin incision) reduction by fentanyl, plasma concentration at incision. No ke0, MEAC or potency ratio. Doubling the dose from 3 to 6 ug/kg gave little further reduction (ceiling). Faster awakening with desflurane than isoflurane at similar fentanyl concentrations. The isoflurane MAC with fentanyl 3 ug/kg (0.61%, logistic) is under Model and parameters; it has no within-study fentanyl-free reference.

## Covariates
Age (strata) - not significant for the fentanyl-MAC relationship.

## Caveats
Single bolus, not steady state: plasma was falling at incision, so plasma and effect site were not necessarily equilibrated. Large SDs in plasma fentanyl. Up-down design with modest numbers per cell.
