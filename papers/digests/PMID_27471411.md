# Rambiritch 2016: population PK of glibenclamide in South African type 2 diabetes

Page convention: printed pp. 83-92 = PDF pp. 1-10 (PDF page = printed page - 82).

## Study
Single centre (Durban), previously published open-label dose escalation. 24 adults with T2D, fasting glucose > 9
mmol/L despite oral therapy, not on insulin. Glibenclamide 0, 2.5, 5, 10, 20 mg/day at 2-week intervals, taken
fasting 10 min before breakfast; sampling at steady state on days 14, 28, 42, 56, 70 at 0, 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2 h (post
breakfast) and 4, 4.5, 5, 5.5, 6, 7 h (post lunch). 841 observations. Four subjects incomplete (samples lost in
transit; subject 16 stopped at 5 mg for glucose 3.6 mmol/L; subject 24 left after 2.5 mg). Table 1 (printed p. 85,
rendered; 22 completers): age 54 +/- 9 (39-73) y; weight 71.1 +/- 14.1 (42.0-107.8) kg; height 156 +/- 9.0 cm;
2 men (BMI 26.48), 20 women (BMI 29.93); fasting glucose 15.4 +/- 3.8 mmol/L; HbA1c 12.2 +/- 3.8%; creatinine 64.1
+/- 10.4 umol/L. Assay HPLC (from the companion PD paper; this paper does not describe it). NCA in WinNonlin (n = 22);
NONMEM FOCE-I; log-transformed data, additive error on the log scale; IIV exponential.

## Model and parameters
One-, two- and three-compartment models tried; two compartments with first-order absorption chosen (OFV -431.164 vs
-243.409 for one compartment); the three-compartment model did not converge; an enterohepatic-recirculation model
(visible in subjects 4, 7, 9, 10, 14) did not converge. No lag in the model equations (Fig. 1). Table 3 (printed
p. 89, rendered; its caption says "final one-compartment" but the right-hand block is the final two-compartment
model):

one-compartment half-life 17.7 h. NCA (Table 2, printed p. 86, rendered), means across 2.5/5/10/20 mg: CL/F 1.94,
2.66, 2.78, 3.09 L/h; t1/2 5.09, 4.42, 8.08, 6.56 h; Tmax 2.05, 2.09, 1.62, 2.04 h; Vz/F 14.63, 16.96, 32.48, 29.24 L;
Cmax 157.97, 242.22, 422.66, 773.61 ng/mL; AUCinf 1376.58, 1861.22, 4276.17, 7513.63 ng.h/mL. AUC and Cmax rose
linearly with dose. The Results and Discussion quote these as "ranges" (for example CL/F 1.94-3.09 L/h, t1/2
4.42-8.08 h), which are the ranges of the dose-level means.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None here; glucose and insulin were measured and modelled in the companion PKPD paper (PMID 27713650), which uses
this paper's CL/F 2.16 L/h.

## Covariates
None: "no formal covariate analysis was conducted" (printed p. 90), justified by the low BSV and no visible trends.
Renal function assumed normal (creatinine 64.1 umol/L).

## Caveats
- Small (24), poorly controlled, mostly women (20 of 22 completers), single centre.
- Peak concentrations about twice those of other studies (Discussion, printed p. 87); ka in the two-compartment
  model differs from literature values (Conclusion).
- Sampling only to 7 h after the morning dose within 24-h dosing; pre-dose levels poorly fitted (unsupervised doses).
- The two-compartment terminal half-life (derived 36.6 h) is far longer than the NCA half-lives (4.4-8.1 h): the
  peripheral volume rests on little information (V3 BSV fixed near zero).
- Table 3 caption typo ("final one-compartment"); Table 4 gives ka in "L/h".
