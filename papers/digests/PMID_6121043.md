# Greenblatt 1982: lorazepam by five routes

## Study
- Randomized single-dose five-way crossover, at least 1 week apart.
- 10 healthy volunteers, 24-39 y, 50.0-79.5 kg; 5 men, 5 women (Table I). One woman was taking oral contraceptives.
- Each subject received 2 mg lorazepam (Ativan) five ways:
  - A: IV, 1 mL of 2 mg/mL over 30 s.
  - B: IM, deltoid.
  - C: oral, two 1-mg tablets (80% in vitro dissolution in 1 h), fasting.
  - D: sublingual, the standard oral tablets held under the tongue for 15 min, fasting.
  - E: sublingual, special tablets (84% dissolution in 1 h), fasting.
- Venous samples at 5 min, then 0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 1, 1.5, 2, 2.5, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12, 24, 30, 36 and 48 h. After the IV dose a sample was also drawn at the end of the injection.
- Assay: electron-capture GLC, oxazepam internal standard.
- Analysis: iterative weighted nonlinear least squares to a sum of exponentials. Absolute availability is AUC(route)/AUC(IV) within each subject.

## Model and parameters
IV (Table I, mean ± SE, n = 10). Biexponential in subjects 1-9, triexponential in subject 10.
- t½α 8.7 ± 1.9 min; t½β 12.9 ± 0.8 h.
- V1 0.52 ± 0.07 L/kg; Vd (area) 1.30 ± 0.07 L/kg.
- CL 1.21 ± 0.10 mL/min/kg.

Table II (mean ± SE):

|---|---|---|---|---|
| Peak, ng/mL | 26.0 ± 1.9 | 24.9 ± 2.4 | 23.3 ± 2.8 | 20.7 ± 1.7 |
| tmax, h | 1.15 ± 0.3 | 2.37 ± 0.32 | 2.35 ± 0.7 | 2.25 ± 0.33 |
| Lag, min | 0 | 11.8 ± 2.1 | 22.7 ± 5.1 | 14.9 ± 3.5 |
| Absorption t½, min | 14.2 ± 4.7 | 32.5 ± 3.8 | 28.5 ± 8.4 | 28.7 ± 7.6 |
| Elimination t½, h | 13.1 ± 1.1 | 14.1 ± 1.0 | 13.2 ± 0.7 | 14.4 ± 1.0 |
| F, % of IV | 95.9 ± 4.0 | 99.8 ± 5.7 | 94.1 ± 6.5 | 98.2 ± 5.8 |

- The IV elimination t½ in Table II is 12.9 ± 0.8 h.
- None of the four extravascular availabilities differed from 100%. Differences between routes were not significant; for peak and tmax, 0.05 < P < 0.1.
- A lag occurred in 9/10 subjects orally, 9/10 with the oral tablets sublingually, 8/10 with the special sublingual tablets, and never after IM.
- Internal inconsistency: the Results text gives the IM peak time as "an average of 1.5 hr", while Table II and the abstract give 1.15 h.

None measured. No ke0 or effective concentration.

## Caveats
- n = 10 healthy young adults.
- Means are of individual fitted values.
- The oral and sublingual absorption half-lives were estimated together with a lag time.
- IM injection was into the deltoid, and all subjects reported discomfort.
- The tablets were taken fasting.
