# Yassen 2006: mechanism-based PK/PD of buprenorphine antinociception in volunteers

## Study
- Randomised, double-blind; 34 healthy volunteers (17 M, 17 F), 18-30 y, no illicit drug history; women on oral contraceptives; each studied once. Weights not reported.
- IV buprenorphine (Temgesic) over 30 s: 0.05 mg/70 kg (n 4), 0.1 (n 4), 0.2 (n 8), 0.3 (n 5), 0.4 (n 8; printed "0.4 mg/kg", evidently /70 kg), 0.6 mg/70 kg (n 5); placebo n 8 (p. 1233).
- Arterial plasma; GC-MS, LLOQ 25.0 pg/mL, linear 25-10,000 pg/mL; buprenorphine free base.
- End point: transcutaneous electrical pain (tibia), 10 Hz, 0.5 mA/s ramp, cutoff 128 mA; pain tolerance (mA) analysed.
- NONMEM V, FOCE interaction; S-PLUS; 1,049 bootstrap replicates for PK only.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: electrical pain tolerance (mA) in healthy volunteers. Final model: effect compartment (ke0) + receptor association/dissociation (kon, koff, Rtot = 1) + linear transduction E = E0/(1 - [CeR]) form (eq. 7). OFVs: Emax+ke0 3407.2, power+ke0 3406.4, receptor model 3458.3, combined 3354.3.
- **ke0 0.00447 /min** (t½ke0 155 min) vs **t½koff 8.8 min**; authors conclude biophase distribution, not receptor kinetics, is rate-limiting (p. 1238, 1240). t½ke0 compared with 171 min reported by Koppert 2005 for analgesia.
- **Observed time to peak effect** approx. 70-100 min after the bolus (p. 1237). No tPeak derived from the model is reported.
- No Emax estimable; no ceiling in 0.05-0.6 mg/70 kg; max predicted receptor occupancy 70% at 0.6 mg/70 kg (Fig. 5). Most concentrations below KD 1.24 ng/mL.
- **MEAC**: none reported (no minimum effective concentration; analgesia was a continuous experimental-pain measure).
- **Potency**: buprenorphine 12-29 times morphine for mu-receptor antinociception, from KD vs morphine's 32.9-76.5 nM (Sarton 2000); consistent with the 20-40× clinical perception (p. 1241-1242).
- Rat comparison: ke0 0.0242 /min, kon 0.0228, koff 0.0731.

## Covariates
None. Sex tested on ke0, kon, koff (ΔOFV < 1). Baseline tolerance 18.9 ± 2.8 (M) and 17.9 ± 2.6 mA (F).

## Caveats
- Young healthy volunteers, single IV doses, experimental pain; no bootstrap for PK/PD.
- PK model fitted to this study only; V1 8.39 L with high IIV; Q2 printed as 155 L/min (typographical).
- ke0 is conditional on this paper's own disposition and on the receptor-binding layer; transplanted to another PK model, the time to peak effect changes (see verification).
