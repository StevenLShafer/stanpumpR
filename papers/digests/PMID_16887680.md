# Yagi 2003: time-kill of linezolid, clindamycin and metronidazole against 11 anaerobes

## Study
In vitro time-kill study (Pharmacia, Kalamazoo). 11 anaerobic strains: Bacteroides distasonis (1), B. fragilis (2),
B. thetaiotaomicron (1), Eubacterium lentum, Fusobacterium nucleatum (ATCC 10953), F. varium, Prevotella
melaninogenica, Clostridium perfringens, Peptostreptococcus anaerobius (2). Banked clinical isolates plus ATCC strains.
- MIC: reference agar dilution on Wilkins-Chalgren agar (NCCLS M11-A3).
- Time-kill: Anaerobe Broth MIC medium, 9.0 mL, anaerobic glovebox, 37 °C; starting inoculum about 10^6-10^7 CFU/mL;
  viable counts at 0, 6 and 24 h by spiral plating, plates counted after 48 h anaerobic incubation.
- Drug concentration: 4 x the MIC, capped at "practical achievable serum concentrations": linezolid 8 mg/L,
  clindamycin 2 mg/L, metronidazole 8 mg/L (PDF p2, section 2.2). Two clindamycin-resistant strains were tested at
  2 mg/L, below their MIC.
- Bactericidal = a reduction of 3.0 log10 CFU/mL or more from the starting inoculum by 24 h (PDF p3, 3.2).
- Broth medium is protein-free; concentrations are total = free drug.

## Model and parameters
No pharmacokinetics. Table 1 (PDF p2, read from the rendered image) — clindamycin and metronidazole:

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: 24-h log10 CFU/mL reduction in broth (bactericidal = >= 3 log). No MBC is reported as such (single
concentration per strain), no MBC50/MBC90. Clindamycin MICs 0.03-2 mg/L for 9 susceptible strains; B. distasonis
(8) and B. thetaiotaomicron (5) resistant (PDF p3, 3.1). Metronidazole MICs 0.10-2 mg/L except P. anaerobius
UC 3928 (64). Authors' conclusion: linezolid is bacteriostatic/bactericidal, like clindamycin.

## Covariates
None (in vitro).

## Caveats
- One concentration per strain, chosen as 4 x MIC up to a cap, so the study cannot give the concentration needed to
  kill; "not bactericidal" at 4 x MIC does not mean not bactericidal at higher concentrations.
- Single run per strain reported; no replicates or variability shown; 11 strains only; industry-sponsored.
- The clindamycin MIC of 5 mg/L for B. thetaiotaomicron 6360 is off the doubling-dilution scale as printed (rendered
  PDF p2); not used by stanpumpR.
- Plating exposed samples to air for up to 30 min (abstract), which can reduce counts of strict anaerobes.

## Verification against stanpumpR
- **"3 of 11 killed 99.9% in 24 h"**: matches Table 1 (3.0-log criterion = 99.9%; three strains). **matches**.
- **"At 2 mg/L"**: **discrepancy**. 2 mg/L was the cap, not the test concentration. The three strains killed were
  exposed to 0.125-0.5 mg/L; the five strains exposed to 2 mg/L (two of them resistant) were not killed (24-h
  change +1.99, 2.68, +1.93, 0.5 and 1.26 log for B. distasonis, B. fragilis 9730, B. thetaiotaomicron, E. lentum,
  F. varium; best 2.68 log). The meaning still supports "not
  reliably bactericidal", arguably more strongly, but the sentence misstates the design.
- **Denominator vs row label**: the row is about non-Bacteroides anaerobes but "3 of 11" includes 4 Bacteroides
  strains; for the organisms the row names the count is 3 of 7. All three killed are in the row's named genera
  (Fusobacterium, Prevotella, C. perfringens). Recorded as open question.
- **Effect on the band**: the row is "excluded" and has no MIC/MBC values (R/antibioticMbc.R:290-292), so it moves no
  band edge (`antibioticMbcBand()` uses only principal/core rows, R/antibioticMbc.R:411-414). No numeric effect.
- **Independent check, clindamycin "Bacteroides fragilis group, clindamycin-susceptible" core row**
  (R/antibioticMbc.R:272-286, MBC90 1.6 mg/L from Levison 1977, "80% of isolates"): not cited there, but in this
  paper the two susceptible B. fragilis strains were not killed by 3 log at 0.5 and 2 mg/L (2.03 and 2.68 log at
  24 h). That is consistent with the row's own note that killing is slow and the MBC is 4-128 x the MIC, and
  argues that 1.6 mg/L is an optimistic upper edge for Bacteroides. Not a transcription discrepancy.
- **Independent check, metronidazole "Other anaerobes" core row** (R/antibioticMbc.R:346-352, MBC90 2 mg/L): here
  Fusobacterium, Prevotella and C. perfringens were killed by >3 log at 0.5-8 mg/L (4 x MIC), E. lentum was not
  (0.70 log at 1 mg/L) and P. anaerobius 3928 was resistant (MIC 64). Broadly consistent; the C. perfringens strain
  (MIC 2) was tested at 8 mg/L, above the row's MBC90 of 2.
- Units: mg/L throughout in both; no conversion needed. Broth = free drug, as the repo header assumes
  (R/antibioticMbc.R:59-60).
