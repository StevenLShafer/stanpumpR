# Al-Habet 1989: methylprednisolone IV (succinate) and oral kinetics

## Study
Five healthy volunteers (4 men, mean age 26.6 +/- 1.8 y, weight 70.4 +/- 16.5 kg; 1 woman, 28 y, 48 kg), fasted.
Randomized crossover, washout >= 2 weeks: IV methylprednisolone sodium succinate (MPSS, Solu-Medrone) 20, 40 and
80 mg over 1 min; oral methylprednisolone (MP) 20 mg as 4 x 5 mg Medrone tablets. Sampling 0.016-24 h (IV) and
0.25-24 h (oral). Assay: HPTLC and HPLC (UV 254 nm), plasma MP and MPSS; sensitivity 10 ng/mL MP, 50 ng/mL MPSS;
CV < 10%. Noncompartmental analysis (trapezoid AUC/AUMC to infinity, MRT, CL = D/AUC, Vss = CL x MRT);
F = AUC(po 20 mg)/AUC(iv 20 mg). Data described by a one-compartment open model at all doses (p. 286). Doses are
as given; the paper does not say whether the IV doses were expressed as MP equivalent or as the succinate salt.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None measured. No ke0 or effect concentration. (Perineal paraesthesia noted with prednisolone phosphate in earlier
work, not with MPSS.)

## Covariates
None.

## Caveats
n = 5 (one woman), healthy young adults; single oral dose level; no absorption rate constant reported. Table 1 is
garbled in the text layer, and the abstract's overall IV values disagree with Table 1. Corresponding author
deceased; the paper was published posthumously for Rogers.
