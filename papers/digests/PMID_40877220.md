# Yuan 2025: paroxetine concentration, remission and adverse reactions in Han Chinese MDD; TRR validated and narrowed

## Study
- Post-hoc analysis of an 8-week multicentre prospective cohort (PMEDA consortium; ChiCTR2000038462). 713 enrolled,
  550 completed week 8, 530 complete cases analysed (Results; Fig. 1a).
- Han Chinese MDD, HAMD-17 >= 17. 74.3% female; age 41.5 +/- 15.5 y; BMI 22.2; CYP2D6 NM 59.1%, IM 39.8%, PM 0.6%,
  UM 0.6%; activity score 1.1 +/- 0.6 (Table 1).
- Paroxetine (Seroxat) monotherapy, 10 mg/day for a week then 20-40 mg/day; mean dose 22.4 +/- 6.1 mg/day.
- Sampling: week 4, after >= 10 days of stable dosing, 20-24 h after the last dose (trough), dried blood spots of
  plasma and whole blood; plasma paroxetine by LC-MS/MS (AB Sciex 5500).
- Outcomes: remission HAMD-17 <= 7 at week 8; ADR by TESS (any item > 2).
- LASSO + logistic regression with 10-fold cross-validation; Firth correction; ROC/Youden for range limits. R 4.3.1.

Remission 67.74% (359/530); ADR 6.42% (34). Within 20-65 ng/mL: 230 (43.4%). Remission by Css below/within/above
20-65 ng/mL: 62.2, 73.0, 64.7%; ADR 5.5, 3.9, 10.5%. Within the updated range: remission OR 1.644 (1.078-2.536),
ADR OR 0.190 (0.051-0.513).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End points: HAMD-17 remission at week 8 and TESS ADRs, against week-4 trough plasma concentration. No ke0, EC50 or
time course. Not an opioid. Earlier ranges quoted: AGNP 20-65 ng/mL; the authors' prior Han Chinese 31.55-52.5
ng/mL; a European 20-70 ng/mL.

## Covariates
Remission: age, baseline HAMD-17, Css within/outside 20-65. Css within range: sex, daily dose, CYP2D6 activity score
(smoking and drinking selected by LASSO).

## Caveats
Observational post-hoc analysis; single trough at week 4 vs outcome at week 8; modest discrimination (AUC 0.6-0.7);
few ADRs (34); Han Chinese only; mostly female; concentrations from dried blood spots.
