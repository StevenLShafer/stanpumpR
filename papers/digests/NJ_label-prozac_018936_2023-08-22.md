# Prozac label 2023: fluoxetine and norfluoxetine pharmacokinetics

## Study
US prescribing information. Pulvules 10, 20, 40 mg fluoxetine (as HCl; MW 345.79; 10 mg = 32.3 µmol)
(section 3, 11). Racemic (50/50 R/S).

Dosing (section 2): MDD adults 20 mg/day in the morning, up to 80 mg/day max; doses >20 mg once daily or
split morning/noon. Pediatric MDD 10-20 mg/day. Lower or less frequent dosing in the elderly and in hepatic
impairment (2.7). At least 5 weeks after stopping Prozac before starting an MAOI; 14 days the other way
(2.9). Full effect may take 4 weeks or longer (2.1).

## Model and parameters
No compartmental model. Label values (section 12.3/12.4):

Fluoxetine metabolism is not dose-proportional (concentrations after 30 days higher than single-dose
prediction); norfluoxetine appears linear (12.3). CYP2D6 poor metabolisers (~7%) have higher S-fluoxetine,
lower S-norfluoxetine, but the sum of the four active enantiomers is not significantly different (12.3).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No concentration-effect relation, no therapeutic range, no effect site. Serotonin uptake inhibition in
platelets (12.2). S-norfluoxetine roughly equipotent to R/S-fluoxetine; R-norfluoxetine much less potent
(animal data, 12.3). Not an opioid; no MEAC/ke0/potency ratio.

## Covariates
Hepatic cirrhosis (prolongs t½), weight in children, CYP2D6 phenotype (enantiomer balance); no renal
adjustment needed; age not significant on single dose.

## Caveats
Values come from older published studies summarised without study detail; ranges, not population
estimates. Nonlinear fluoxetine kinetics.
