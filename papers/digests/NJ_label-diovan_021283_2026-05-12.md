# Diovan (valsartan) prescribing information, FDA, 2026-05-12

## Study
A label, not a study. Tablets 40 (scored), 80, 160, 320 mg. Indications: hypertension (adults and children 1 y
and older), heart failure NYHA II-IV, post-myocardial infarction LV dysfunction. Boxed warning: fetal toxicity
(p.1, 5.1 p.4). The highlights' revision field is the unfilled placeholder "m/202Y" (p.1, rendered); the content
read is the same as the DailyMed SPL NJ_dailymed-5ddba454 in every section compared (2, 8, 12.3).

Dosing (section 2, p.2-4): adult hypertension 80 or 160 mg once daily, range 80-320 mg daily; paediatric 1-16 y
1 mg/kg once daily (up to 40 mg), up to 4 mg/kg (160 mg daily); heart failure 40 mg twice daily up to 160 mg twice
daily (max studied 320 mg/day); post-MI 20 mg twice daily titrated to 160 mg twice daily. Tablets and suspension
are not interchangeable: suspension AUC 60% higher (2.1).

Dose-linear AUC and Cmax over 80-320 mg; no appreciable accumulation at 200 mg once daily (healthy). Recovery
after oral solution about 83% faeces, 13% urine, mostly unchanged; main metabolite valeryl 4-hydroxy valsartan
about 9% of dose, formed by CYP2C9. OATP1B1 and MRP2 substrate.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
12.2 (p.10): 80 mg inhibits the angiotensin II pressor response by about 80% at peak, about 30% at 24 h; no data
on larger doses. Antihypertensive effect substantially present within 2 weeks, maximal by about 4 weeks (2.2).
No concentration-effect relation, no therapeutic range.

## Covariates
Renal: no correlation of AUC with creatinine clearance down to 10 mL/min; not removed by haemodialysis; no dose
change for GFR 30-90; severe impairment not established (8.6, 12.3). Hepatic: about 2-fold AUC; no dose change for
mild-moderate (8.7). Age: AUC +70% (no dose change). Sex: no difference. Paediatric clearance (L/h/kg) similar to
adults (n = 26, 1-16 y, suspension).

## Caveats
Descriptive values only; the half-life and clearance are intravenous, the tmax and bioavailability oral. The
placeholder revision field means the printed revision date is not on the document; the Drugs@FDA date is used.
