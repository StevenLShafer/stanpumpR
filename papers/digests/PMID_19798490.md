# Mondick 2010: ondansetron population PK in children 1-48 months

## Study
- Pooled two GSK multicentre studies; 124 patients analysed, 745 **serum** samples.
  - Study 1 (surgical, 1-24 months, n = 50): 0.1 mg/kg (n = 24) or 0.2 mg/kg (n = 26) IV over 30 s after induction; samples at end of injection, 1, 2, 4, 6, 8 h (+18-24 h in 1-4 month olds at 0.2 mg/kg).
  - Study 2 (oncology, 6-48 months, n = 74): 0.15 mg/kg over 15 min every 4 h × 3; sparse samples around doses 2 and 3.
- Table 1 combined: age 16.3 months (1-48.3), weight 10.2 kg (3.3-20.2), 52% female; 58% White, 19% Black, 23% Hispanic.
- HPLC-MS/MS, LOQ 0.5 ng/mL. NONMEM V, FOCE-I, log-transformed data; 1000-sample stratified bootstrap.

## Model and parameters
Two compartments; allometric scaling to 10.4 kg (exponents fixed 0.75 clearances, 1 volumes); maturation of CL:

CL = θCL·(WT/10.4)^0.75·(1 − βCL·exp(−(AGE − 1)·ln2/TCL)), AGE in months, ages below 1 month set to 1. Table 2 reports parameters per kg after estimation at 10.4 kg:

Text checks: CL reduced 31%, 53%, 76% at 6, 3, 1 months; CL 0.629, 0.770, 0.794 L/h/kg at 6 mo/8 kg, 12 mo/10 kg, 24 mo/13 kg; mature CL extrapolated to 70 kg 0.527 L/h/kg, consistent with adults 0.4-0.5 and 3-12 y 0.39-0.5 L/h/kg; maturity reached at about 19 months (~five half-lives). Derived at 70 kg: CL 37.0 L/h, V1 65.1 L, Q 363 L/h, V2 176 L.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. Not an opioid; no MEAC, ke0 or potency ratio.

## Covariates
Weight (all parameters, allometric) and age (CL maturation). Sex, race, oncology vs surgical, hepatic chemistries not significant.

## Caveats
Infants ≥ 1 month only; V1 IIV inflated by Study 2's design (first sample 2-5 min after infusion); serum, not plasma; dose-preparation inconsistencies noted by authors.
