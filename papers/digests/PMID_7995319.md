# Chetty 1994: population PK of oral chlorpromazine in chronic schizophrenia (weight, cigarette and cannabis smoking)

## Study
- Inpatients with DSM-III schizophrenia, two South African hospitals; eligibility 16-55 y; organic disease
  excluded; admitted only if CPZ and metabolites absent on admission.
- 32 enrolled; 1 withdrawn (non-compliance); 1 with unreliable dosing excluded after initial fits; 2 samples taken
  after an oral contraceptive excluded. Table 1 (31 patients, as printed): 189 plasma samples; age 16-45 y;
  20 male, 11 female; weight 45-70 kg (average 57 kg, Results); cigarette smokers 11; cannabis smokers 5; chronic
  alcohol 7; oral contraceptive 1; orphenadrine 4; CPZ dose 200-1200 mg (route oral, implied by morning/evening
  dosing and fixed ka; not stated explicitly).
- Sampling: 12 h after the evening dose and 2, 3 or 4 h after the morning dose. Plasma CPZ by HPLC, sensitivity
  3 ng/mL.
- NONMEM version 3, ADVAN2 TRANS2 SS2, proportional error; dOBF 3.8 for significance.

## Model and parameters
One compartment, first-order absorption and elimination. ka fixed at 1 /h (from literature tmax 2-4 h and k
0.028 /h) because the free fit flip-flopped; IIV on ka retained. Final model (Results, p.524-525; [SE]):
CL = (P1 x WT + P2) x CIG x CANN; V = P3; ka = 1 /h (fixed). Parameters are apparent oral (the paper does not
write /F).

Typical CL at 57 kg (Discussion): 127 L/h non-smokers, 175 cigarette, 191 cannabis, 263 both. Derived check:
1.82 x 57 + 24.51 = 128.2 L/h; t1/2 at 57 kg non-smoker = 0.693 x 1930 / 128.2 = 10.4 h (derived). Weight
lowered CL CV from 71% to 68%; smoking to 54%. Alcohol (x 1.47 alone) not significant once smoking included;
orphenadrine, age, gender not significant. Oral contraceptive case: CPZ rose from 46 and 34 ng/mL to 839 and
659 ng/mL (single patient).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. Not an opioid; no MEAC, ke0 or potency ratio.

## Covariates
Weight (linear with intercept, on CL), cigarette smoking (x 1.38), cannabis smoking (x 1.50), multiplicative.

## Caveats
n = 31, narrow weights (45-70 kg) and ages (16-45); sparse sampling with almost no absorption-phase data; ka
fixed; one compartment for a drug usually described with more; apparent oral parameters with unknown (and
variable, first-pass) F; IIV large; parent only (metabolites not modelled); OCR-quality text.

## Verification against stanpumpR
- **discrepancy** (R/antipsychotics.R:27-28, :235; docs/antipsychotics.md:70). The full text has a structural
  model and coefficients: one compartment, ka 1 /h fixed, CL = (1.82 x WT + 24.51) x 1.38 (cigarettes) x 1.50
  (cannabis) L/h, V 1930 L, IIV 54% and 57%. The statement was presumably true of the abstract, which is all the
  repo had; it is not true of the paper now archived. Whether to register chlorpromazine remains a judgment: the
  model is weak (31 patients, ka fixed, no absorption data, one compartment, apparent oral, weight 45-70 kg), so
  "blocked" may still be the right status, but the stated reason should change to the model's weakness, not its
  absence.
- Profile fields (R/antipsychotics.R:229-234): oral route, apparent oral basis, 31 patients with chronic
  schizophrenia, plasma, parent analyte. **matches**.
