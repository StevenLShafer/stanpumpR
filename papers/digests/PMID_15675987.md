# Beilin 2005: continuous physostigmine plus morphine PCA

## Study
Randomized, non-blinded trial, Israel. 50 ASA 1-2 patients aged 40-70 y, open lower abdominal surgery (abdominal, gynecology, urology); 25 per group completed (5 transfused patients dropped out). Group PCA: morphine PCA alone. Group PCA+PHYSO: the same PCA plus physostigmine 1.5 mg IV bolus at end of surgery, then 1 mg/h IV infusion for 24 h from PACU arrival. Morphine: loading 2-4 mg IV in PACU, then PCA 1 mg per demand, lockout 6-8 min, max 25 mg per 4 h, no background; extra 1-2 mg bolus if VAS at rest was 4 or more. Anesthesia: fentanyl 2-3 ug/kg induction plus additional fentanyl (total 326 +/- 12.62 ug PCA vs 324 +/- 11.22 ug PCA+PHYSO), propofol, vecuronium, N2O, isoflurane. Outcomes: VAS (10 cm) at 4, 8, 12, 24 h, 24-h morphine use, sedation, nausea, and ex-vivo LPS-stimulated IL-1b and IL-6 from PBMC (ELISA, detection 30 and 15 pg/mL). No drug concentrations were measured.

## Model and parameters
No pharmacokinetic model fitted. ## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Opioid audit: morphine is the opioid here.
- MEAC / analgesic concentration: none; no plasma morphine measured.
- ke0 / t1/2 ke0 / tPeak / hysteresis: none for morphine. For physostigmine only a literature statement that plasma level and central effect on task performance stabilised at about 40 min of 1 mg/h (ref 12).
- Equianalgesic/potency ratios: none given for morphine. Literature mention only: physostigmine 2 mg IV gave analgesia like pethidine 50 mg IV for about 15 min (ref 8).
- Results (text/figures): 24-h morphine consumption lower with physostigmine (t=4.77, P<0.0001; numeric means only in Fig. 2, not read as numbers). VAS lower in both rest and cough (group effect P<0.0001). Sedation not different (scores 2.4/2.2/2.1/1.4 PCA vs 2.2/1.8/1.7/1.2 PCA+PHYSO at 4/8/12/24 h). Nausea higher with physostigmine in 0-2 h (1.7 vs 1.1; P<0.007). IL-1b rose in PCA group, unchanged in PCA+PHYSO (P<0.04).

## Covariates
None modelled. Groups matched for age and weight (Table 1).

## Caveats
Non-blinded; subjective VAS. Small n. Physostigmine plasma levels not measured. Fentanyl at induction and intraoperatively confounds early morphine need but was equal between groups. The morphine saving is a clinical outcome, not a concentration-effect result.
