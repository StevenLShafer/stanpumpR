# Adderall label (NDA 011522 s043, 2016): immediate-release mixed amphetamine salts

## Study
Prescribing information, old (non-PLR) format. The PK statements rest on single doses of 10 or 30 mg Adderall given to healthy volunteers, fasted (Clinical Pharmacology, Pharmacokinetics); n, age and weight not given.

## Model and parameters
No compartmental model. Composition (Description table): each strength is equal masses of the four salts (5 mg tablet = 1.25 mg of each).

Total amphetamine base equivalence per tablet (Description table):

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Base | 3.13 | 4.7 | 6.3 | 7.8 | 9.4 | 12.6 | 18.8 mg |

Other facts: food effect not studied. With normal urine pH about half the dose is recovered as alpha-hydroxy-amphetamine derivatives and 30-40% as unchanged amphetamine; urinary recovery ranges 1-75% with urine pH; acid urine and high flow give renal clearance above GFR (active secretion). CYP2D6 forms 4-hydroxyamphetamine. No Cmax or AUC values are printed.

Dosing (Dosage and Administration): ADHD, 3-5 y start 2.5 mg daily; 6 y and older start 5 mg once or twice daily, increments of 5 mg weekly, rarely above 40 mg/day; extra doses at 4-6 h intervals. Narcolepsy 5-60 mg/day in divided doses.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No concentration-effect data, no therapeutic or toxic concentration range. Not an opioid: no MEAC, ke0 or potency ratio. (Interactions section notes amphetamines potentiate meperidine analgesia; qualitative only.)

## Covariates
None quantified. Qualitative: urinary pH (alkalinizing agents raise, acidifying agents lower levels), CYP2D6 polymorphism, hepatic and renal dysfunction.

## Caveats
- Population of the PK statement ("healthy volunteers") is not described: age (adult vs child) not stated.
- Half-life given as ranges, not a single mean; no clearance or volume.
