# van Lemmen 2025: fentanyl ventilatory depression, PKPD framework

## Study
Single-centre (Leiden), open-label, single-sequence study of 12 healthy volunteers (6 women, 6 men), mean age 22 y (20-25), height 178 cm (153-198), weight 76 kg (55-90), BMI 24 (20-28) (PDF p.4). A 13th was replaced after vomiting. All received five IV fentanyl boluses over 10 s during a 3 h "sham surgery": 100 ug at t = 0, 75 ug at 30 and 60 min, 50 ug at 90 and 120 min (total 350 ug; stated as "per 70 kg"), then 1 h more of monitoring (240 min total). Closed chemoreflex loop; 30% O2 in N2 inhaled; breath-by-breath VE and PETCO2 by mask and pneumotachograph. Arterial blood (radial) at 0, 2, 5, 10, 15, 20, 30 min after each dose and 60 and 120 min after the last; 21-32 samples/subject, 307 total; LC-MS/MS plasma, LLOQ 0.05 ng/mL, ULOQ 50 ng/mL (PDF p.3). NONMEM 7.5.1 (ITS, SAEM, IMP, BAYES); Kalman filters with parallel noise models for the minute-by-minute PD data. No sample-size calculation (exploratory).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Three PD analyses, all with an effect site linked to arterial plasma, inhibitory sigmoid Emax on ventilation (VE = VB / (1+(CE/C50)^gamma)) and a power function for PETCO2 (C100 = concentration for a 100% rise): (1) empirical VE, (2) empirical PETCO2, (3) physiologic combined model with CO2 kinetics (alveolar, tissue storage, cardiac output fixed at 5 L/min) and a ventilatory controller (gain G, time constant tau). - **MEAC / analgesic concentration:** none; no analgesia end point. The C50s are ventilatory depression potencies.
- **ke0 / t1/2ke0 / tPeak:** t1/2ke0 on ventilation (VE) 1.8 min in the empirical model; on PETCO2 11.3 min; 9.0 min for the joint physiologic model (text and Table 2, PDF p.5-7). The authors attribute the difference to the interaction between VE and PCO2. No tPeak is reported; no hysteresis loop is shown. The empirical VE t1/2ke0 is the fastest of the three.
- **C50:** the physiologic C50 2.3 ng/mL is less than one third of the empirical 7.5 ng/mL (derived 2.3/7.5 = 0.31). The authors interpret it as the C50 at brainstem receptors, free of the CO2 feedback, and independent of the infusion rate, whereas an open-loop (empirical) C50 depends on infusion rate. Fig 10 simulation (PDF p.11): 50% depression of isohypercapnic VE55 at 2.3 ng/mL. They compare it with the earlier isohypercapnic values (not given here) and Mildh et al's closed-loop C50 of 6.1 ng/mL (PDF p.8, ref 20).
- **Potency/equianalgesic ratios:** none. Single bolus of 350 ug is predicted to depress VE by 40-50% and raise PETCO2 by 1.5-1.7 kPa (11.3-12.8 mmHg) (PDF p.8). Observed: moderate effects, PETCO2 peaked +0.8 kPa, VE stayed >7 L/min; plasma concentration about 0.5 ng/mL in the PACU period.

## Covariates
None modelled. CO2 production and cardiac output (fixed 5 L/min) are structural; the PD interindividual variances were mostly not estimable after Kalman filtering.

## Caveats
Small n (12), young healthy subjects, exploratory, no formal sample size; two-compartment PK because sampling stopped 2 h after the last dose; the authors say the models may be inadequate for high bolus doses (their ref 15, 10 ug/kg over 90 s gave apnoea); 30% oxygen only (peripheral chemoreceptors not silenced); baseline ventilation high (dead space, anxiety); Kalman filtering removed most interindividual variability so population C50 uncertainty is probably understated; time constant of ventilation noise fixed to 60 min. Funded by Enalare Therapeutics.

## Verification against stanpumpR
Not cited, so there is no citation to verify. The library has a fentanyl model (R/drugs_fentanyl.R, inst/extdata/drugDefaults_global.csv:4), which I compared with this paper's PK and PD.
- **PK:** library = Bae 2020, three compartments at 70 kg: V1 10.1, V2 26.5, V3 206 L, CL 0.704, Q2 2.38, Q3 1.49 L/min (R/drugs_fentanyl.R:77-82). This paper (two compartments, Table 1): V1 17.8, V2 121 L, CL1 1.3, CL2 3.6 L/min. Clearance 1.3/0.704 = 1.85 times the library (derived); Vss 138.8 vs 242.6 L (derived); V1 17.8 vs 10.1 L. Direction: this young-volunteer data set (mean 22 y, 76 kg) gives higher clearance and smaller Vss than Bae's older, mixed set, with a sampling window that cannot see the slow third compartment. Weight scaling in the paper is not described, so absolute values at 70 kg cannot be asserted (see open questions).
- **tPeak** (R/drugs_fentanyl.R:117): library 3.6 min, Shafer and Varvel 1991 EEG. This paper: t1/2ke0 9.0 min (joint), 1.8 min (VE alone), 11.3 min (PETCO2); no tPeak. They are different end points; the paper does not contradict the EEG tPeak. Its joint t1/2ke0 9.0 min is ke0 = 0.077/min (derived ln2/9.0); I did not compute the ke0 the library solves from its tPeak, so no comparison is made.
- **MEAC** (R/drugs_fentanyl.R:114; drugDefaults 0.6 ng/mL, band 0.48-1.2): analgesic concentration from Gourlay/Bae; this paper has none. C50 2.3 ng/mL (physiologic) or 7.5 (empirical) is a ventilatory potency, 4-12 times the library's MEAC, so MEAC is below the concentration for half ventilatory depression, as expected.
- **Verdict:** nothing the repo says depends on this paper; no discrepancy against a stated claim. The cross-study differences above are noted, not resolved.
