# Yin 2026: ceftazidime PopPK in Chinese neonates from dried blood spots

## Study
- Prospective single-centre open-label PopPK study, Xiamen Maternity and Child Health Care Hospital, 2022-2024. Neonates with postnatal age <=28 days, suspected or confirmed bacterial infection, on IV ceftazidime (PDF p2-3).
- 72 neonates, 140 samples (median 2 per patient, range 1-4). Table 1 (p6, median (range)): GA 39.4 (32.6-41.3) wk; PMA 39.7 (32.7-41.9) wk; PNA 1 (1-4) d; weight 3.2 (1.8-4.2) kg; serum creatinine 56.7 (23.7-88.0) umol/L; 43 male / 29 female; 14 (19.4%) preterm.
- Dosing: clinical (not specified per patient in the text; doses 25-100 mg/kg q6-12 h are the range cited in literature).
- Sampling: heel-prick Capitainer qDBS, 10 uL, windows 0-0.5, 2-3 and 4-6 h after dosing, stored at -80 C. Assay UPLC-PDA (text also says UPLC-MS/MS in the abstract), linear 0.25-50.0 ug/mL, accuracy 92.5-101.4%, precision <10.4% (p3). Concentrations are whole blood.
- NONMEM 7.5, FOCE-I; VPC 1000 simulations, bootstrap 1000 (967 converged); simulations in R/mrgsolve.

## Model and parameters
One-compartment, whole-blood concentrations. Weight and PMA included a priori (p4):
CL = TVCL x (WT/70)^0.75 x PMA^Hill/(PMA^Hill + TM50^Hill) x exp(etaCL); V = TVV x (WT/70) x exp(etaV). TM50 fixed 47.7 weeks, Hill fixed 3.4 (Rhodin 2009 renal maturation, ref 25). Exponential IIV and residual error. No stepwise covariate was retained.

OFV -153.8, 31.3 lower than base; the purely data-driven stepwise model (weight on CL only) had OFV -143.9 (p5, p7). VPC: 94.3% of observations inside the 90% prediction interval (p6).

Derived (mine, not the paper's): at WT 3.2 kg, PMA 39.4 wk the maturation fraction is about 0.34 and typical blood CL about 0.66 L/h.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD measured. Target for simulations was 70% fT>MIC (unbound plasma), PTA >=90%; unbound concentration = fu x Cblood x BP (eqs 3-5; fu value not stated in the text). Simulated 12 regimens (25/50/75/100 mg/kg at q6/8/12 h, 30-min infusion), 10,000 virtual neonates per PMA subgroup (32-35, 35-38, 38-42 wk). For MIC <=4 mg/L all doses at q6 and q8 h reached PTA >=90%; at q12 h only 75 and 100 mg/kg. Recommended: 25 mg/kg q6h or q8h, 75 mg/kg q12h; MIC <=8: 25 mg/kg q6h or 50 mg/kg q8h (p6, p8). No difference in PTA across PMA subgroups. Not an opioid; no ke0 or MEAC.

## Covariates
Weight (allometric) and PMA (sigmoid maturation on CL); serum creatinine had 2.8% missing, imputed by mean. No other covariate significant (p5, p7).

## Caveats
- Whole-blood (DBS) based; conversion to plasma depends on an assumed B:P of 0.72 (predicted).
- Narrow age window (PNA 1-4 d in median, PMA 32.6-41.9 wk); 140 samples, 2 per patient; RSE of residual error 45%.
- Reported plasma CL 0.49 L/h/kg (higher than Li 0.224, van der Veer 0.144, Wang 0.301) and V 2.32 L/kg (Discussion p8); these do not reproduce from Table 2 (see open questions). The comparison is against other studies, not stanpumpR.
- No external validation, no CSF penetration, no MIC or clinical outcomes (p9-10).
