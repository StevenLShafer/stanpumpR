# Tsuda 2020: population PK of d-amphetamine after lisdexamfetamine in Japanese and US children

## Study
Pooled data from 6 studies: 2 US (a Phase 1 study, ref 10 = Boellner 2010; a Phase 2 classroom study,
ref 11 = Biederman 2007) and 4 Japanese (Phase 2 and extension; Phase 2/3 and extension). 1365 plasma
d-amphetamine concentrations from 194 patients 6-17 y with ADHD (792 points from 32 non-Japanese,
intensive; 573 from 162 Japanese, sparse); 154 BLQ/missing points excluded. Table 1: all 116 M / 78 F,
age 10.3 (2.8) y, median 10.0 (6-17); weight 37.6 (14.3) kg, median 34.1 (18.2-100.6). Japanese:
weight median 34.3 kg, age 6-17. Non-Japanese: 21 M / 11 F, age 9.3 (1.8), 6-12 y, weight 34.5 (8.3),
median 33.9 (20.5-47.4); 15 Caucasian / 13 African American / 4 other. LC-MS/MS, LLOQ 2 ng/mL.
NONMEM 7.3, FOCE-I; bootstrap 1000 (3.9% failed); pcVPC.

## Model and parameters
One compartment, first-order absorption with lag. Power covariates centred on median weight 34.1 kg;
ethnicity multiplicative (1 = non-Japanese). Table 2:

CL/F = 8.96 x (BW/34.1)^0.600 x 1.26^Ethnicity (L/h); V/F = 133 x (BW/34.1)^0.776 (L).

No IIV covariances retained. Dose units of the dataset (capsule mass vs d-amphetamine base) are not
stated in the paper. Table 3 (Japanese, post hoc): median Cmax,ss 58.0 / 112 / 157 ng/mL and AUCss
885.5 / 1662 / 2479 ng.h/mL at 30 / 50 / 70 mg (all ages).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Exposure-response in the Japanese Phase 2/3 study (n = 49 LDX, 19 placebo): change in ADHD-RS-IV total
score at week 4 vs AUCss, slope -0.00235, p = 0.256, R2 = 0.0274: **no clear exposure dependence**;
all AUCss tertiles better than placebo. No EC50, no ke0, no threshold.

## Covariates
Weight on CL/F and V/F (power); non-Japanese ethnicity on CL/F (x1.26, still 1.27 with fixed
allometry). Age and sex dropped once weight was in. Hepatic/renal markers not tested.

## Caveats
Ethnicity confounded with study (sampling density, ages 6-12 vs 6-17, sites). High shrinkage on V/F,
Ka, ALAG. No inter-occasion variability. Small exposure-response sample. Sponsor-authored (Shionogi).
