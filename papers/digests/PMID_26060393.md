# Teuscher 2015: population PK of Aptensio XR (MPH-MLR) in children, and a Cmax-to-ADHD-RS-IV Emax model

## Study
Sponsor (Rhodes) secondary analysis; Phoenix NLME 1.3, FOCE maximum likelihood.
- Adult PK: two healthy-volunteer studies of MPH-MLR 80 mg vs IR methylphenidate 25 mg x 3 (fasted single dose; fed
  multiple dose). Final adult data: 1124 concentrations from 25 subjects (single-dose study); multiple-dose study used
  for validation. LC assay, 0.05-25 ng/mL.
- Pediatric PK: Quinn 2007, children 6-12 y with ADHD, MPH-MLR 10-40 mg vs IR 10/20 mg, samples 0-24 h; 154
  concentrations from 17 subjects. HPLC-MS/MS (MPH-d3 IS). Analyte: methylphenidate (not described as enantioselective).
- PD: phase III NCT01239030, 6-18 y, ADHD-RS-IV at baseline and weeks 1, 2, 3, 4, 8, 12; 1767 change-from-baseline scores
  from 269 participants; Cmax for each participant simulated from the pediatric PK model (not measured).

## Model and parameters
One compartment, two parallel first-order inputs: fast (IR overcoat) fraction F1 with Ka1; slow (ER core) 1 - F1 with
Ka2 after tlag (Figure 1). CL_i = CL_TV x WT^θ x e^η (Eq. 4; WT not centred). Pediatric final estimates (Table 1):

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Emax model on simulated Cmax (Eq. 5): E = Emax x Cmax / (EC50 + Cmax), end point = change from baseline in ADHD-RS-IV
total score over weeks of treatment (Table 2):
- Emax -34.96 points (%CV 6.8); EC50 5.77 ng/mL of Cmax (%CV 12.6); IIV ωEmax 0.028 (76% shrinkage), ωEC50 1.01 (39%);
  proportional residual 0.44.
- Baseline ADHD-RS-IV 36.3 (SD 9.32); an 18-point fall (50%) taken as clinically relevant; simulated doses to reach it:
  40 mg at 32-36 kg, 50 mg at 41-45 kg, 60 mg at 50-54 kg, 80 mg at 59-68 kg (Table 3).
- Not a concentration-time effect model: no ke0, no time course of effect. Similar results with SKAMP (not shown).

## Covariates
Body weight on CL (power 1.53). Height and BMI tested, not retained.

## Caveats
- Sponsor-funded; small pediatric PK set (17 subjects); PD fitted against simulated, not measured, Cmax from separate
  studies.
- Model for children/adolescents only; authors caution against adult extrapolation.
- CL_TV of 1.3 L/h is the coefficient of an uncentred WT^1.53 term, not a typical clearance.
