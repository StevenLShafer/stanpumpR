# Ku 2018: population PK of IV diazepam in children with status epilepticus

## Study
- Diazepam arm of a randomised trial of lorazepam vs diazepam for paediatric convulsive SE (NCT00621478; Chamberlain 2014), 11 US paediatric EDs, exception from informed consent.
- 87 patients with PK, 162 concentrations (1-4 per patient, median 2, up to 48 h); age 3.9 y (0.4-17.8), weight 15 kg (5-89).
- Dose 0.2 mg/kg IV (max 8 mg) over 1 min; second dose 0.1 mg/kg (max 4 mg) in 28%.
- NONMEM 7.2, FOCE-I; allometric scaling on total weight with fixed exponents.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- Target therapeutic range 200-600 ng/mL at 10 min after dose, adopted from the literature (refs 11, 23-25) as a surrogate end point; not derived or validated here (authors say the range has not been evaluated prospectively in children).
- No significant relationship between PK parameters and efficacy (cessation of SE) or respiratory depression.
- > 2000 ng/mL cited as the adult toxic range (medical examiner reference).
- Simulation: 0.2 mg/kg gives > 200 ng/mL at 10 min in 60% and > 600 in 15%.

## Covariates
Weight only (allometric). SCR, ALT, AST effects driven by 3 outliers and dropped; no age effect.

## Caveats
Sparse sampling, few samples beyond 24 h (terminal phase poorly defined); total drug only; metabolites not measured.
