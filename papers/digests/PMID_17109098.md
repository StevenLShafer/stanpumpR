# Sakata 2006: MIC and MBC of parenteral antibiotics for paediatric blood isolates of S. pneumoniae and H. influenzae

## Study
Short note, single author (Asahikawa Kosei Hospital, Hokkaido). Blood isolates from children admitted to four
hospitals in Kamikawa subprefecture, January 2003 to December 2005.
- S. pneumoniae: 16 isolates, ages 2 months to 9 years (pneumonia 8, occult bacteraemia 5, meningitis 3).
- H. influenzae: 18 isolates, ages 1 month to 4 years (meningitis 6, pneumonia 4, occult bacteraemia 4,
  epiglottitis 2, facial cellulitis 2).
- MIC: broth microdilution (NCCLS), initial inoculum 1 x 10^7 CFU/mL (PDF p2), concentrations 0.06-128 µg/mL.
  Media: cation-adjusted Mueller-Hinton broth + 2% horse blood (pneumococci); cation-adjusted Mueller-Hinton with
  15 µg/mL haematin, 15 µg/mL NAD and 5 g/L yeast extract (H. influenzae).
- MBC: lowest concentration giving >99.9% reduction of the initial inoculum (NCCLS M26-A); incubation time not stated.
- PCR genotyping of pbp1a/2x/2b (pneumococci) and ftsI/blaTEM-1 (H. influenzae).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: >99.9% kill of the initial inoculum (MBC) in broth. The author notes CSF concentrations in treated
meningitis (ABPC, CTX, CTRX, MEPM, VCM, five drugs) as 4.70 ± 1.83, 4.97 ± 2.60, 4.0 ± 2.9 and 3.3 ± 1.1 µg/mL
(four values; PDF p4, printed p341) — which value belongs to which drug is not clear from the text, and these are
cited from elsewhere, not measured here. The author argues the MBC matters more than the MIC in meningitis and that
the MBC was dissociated from the MIC in strains with abnormal pbp genes.

## Covariates
Genotype (resistance class): MIC/MBC gaps were largest in gBLNAR and gBLPACR-II H. influenzae and in pneumococci with
2-3 pbp mutations.

## Caveats
- Small n (16 and 18), one region of Japan, children only.
- High inoculum (1 x 10^7 CFU/mL, versus the usual 5 x 10^5), which tends to raise MICs and MBCs of beta-lactams.
- Internal inconsistency: the text gives the pneumococcal classes as gPRSP 7, gPISP 8, gPSSP 6 (21 for 16 isolates);
  Table 1 shows 7 / 8 / 1. Not used by stanpumpR.
- Pneumococcal broth contained 2% horse blood, so those MBCs are not strictly protein-free.
- Low end of the scale is censored at <=0.06 mg/L, so MBC50s are upper bounds.
