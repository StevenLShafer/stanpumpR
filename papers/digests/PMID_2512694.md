# Greenblatt 1989 (TDM): diazepam kinetics in 48 men after 10 mg orally

## Study
- 48 healthy male volunteers, 18-44 y (Table 1: age 27.0 ± 6.9, height 177 cm, weight 75.0 ± 9.0 kg (59.1-95.0), 97.3% of ideal weight; all within 22% of ideal).
- Single 10 mg oral diazepam (2 × 5 mg Valium) with 240 mL water; fasted 10 h, light liquid breakfast (8 oz orange juice) 2 h before dose; fasting to 4 h after.
- Venous samples 0.25-264 h (21 time points). GC-ECD for diazepam and desmethyldiazepam; LLQ ~ 3 ng/mL, CV ≤ 10%.
- Noncompartmental: β by regression of visually chosen terminal phase; AUC trapezoidal + extrapolation; apparent oral CL = dose/AUC; Vd = CL/β. Distribution tests (Shapiro-Wilk).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points

## Covariates
Vd vs body weight (r = 0.70, P < 0.001); no equation given for use.

## Caveats
- Oral only: CL and Vd are apparent (CL/F, Vd/F); F not measured here.
- t½, CL and Vd distributions skewed/non-normal; log-normal fits better.
- Young healthy men only.
