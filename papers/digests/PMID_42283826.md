# Briki 2026: IPD model-based meta-analysis of IV 5-fluorouracil

## Study
Individual-patient-data model-based meta-analysis of three datasets (Table 2, PDF p. 6): SID1 Terret 2000 (21 pts, 159 samples, LV5FU2, HPLC-UV, LLOQ 0.05 mg/L), SID2 Mueller 2013 (30 pts after one outlier removed, 323 samples used, FOLFOX/FOLFIRI/5FU, LC-MS/MS, LLOQ 0.013 mg/L), SID3 unpublished Dijon routine-care data (675 pts, 1850 samples, LC-UV, LLOQ 0.03 mg/L). Total 726 pts, 2,332 plasma concentrations (text p. 5); 14 below LLOQ removed in the analysis (0.6% censored per Methods, p. 3). Sex 14/7, 20/10, 388/287 men/women (derived: 422 men, 304 women). Median age 63-69 y (range 24-90); weight medians 67-76 kg (32-126); BSA medians 1.7-1.9 m2 (1.2-2.5); eGFR medians 91-99. Regimens: bolus with or without 46-h continuous infusion. NONMEM 7.6, ADVAN13, FOCE-I; BSA by Mosteller, LBM by Janmahasatian. Covariates tested: sex, age, weight, height, BSA, eGFR, DPD profile (categorical), LBM.

## Model and parameters
Two compartments; elimination from central compartment by parallel Michaelis-Menten (Vmax, Km) and linear CL. Residual error: proportional, grouped for SID1+SID2; mixed for SID3. Table 3 (PDF p. 7, printed Page 7 of 14), typical values (%RSE):

Equations (Table 3, p. 7): TVVmax = Vmax (1 + thetaAGE (AGE-60)/60)(1 + thetaMale SEX); TVCL = CL (BSA/1.7)^thetaBSA; TVV2 = V2 (BW/70)^thetaBW. Bootstrap medians within +/-10% of estimates, success >95% (p. 6). Shrinkage Vmax 26%, CL 57%, V1 77%. Text p. 5: men 18% higher Vmax than same-age women; 80-y vs 40-y 17% lower; 80-y woman 30% lower than 40-y man; BSA 1.5 vs 2.0 m2 gives 53% lower CL.
Alternative model (supplement, not read): LBM and age on Vmax, BSA on CL, replacing sex.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD. Exposure target for colorectal cancer: AUC 20-29 mg h/L (AUC = steady-state concentration x infusion duration, Introduction p. 2; dose-adjustment grid Table 1, p. 4). Plasma 5-FU only; no ke0, tPeak or MEAC. (Not an opioid.)

## Covariates
Vmax: sex, age. CL: BSA (Mosteller; allometric exponent estimated 2.61). V2: body weight (exponent fixed 1). eGFR and DPD profile tested and not retained. 5-FU half-life 8-10 min and a plateau needing 18-26 h in a 46-h infusion (Introduction, p. 2).

## Caveats
Three datasets; IIV shrinkage high on CL and V1; DPD genotype not testable; circadian rhythm not modelled; infusion system not tested; one outlier patient removed; simulations (30,000 virtual patients, 400 mg/m2 bolus + 2,400 mg/m2 over 46 h) are in silico only. Weight/BSA of the source patients are actual measured values.
