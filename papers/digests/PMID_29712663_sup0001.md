# van Iersel 2018 supplement: posaconazole tablet PopPK, Figures S1-S5

## Study
Supplementary figures only; no tables, no parameter values and no equations. The population is the main paper's (patients given the delayed-release posaconazole tablet; the main paper's card is `PMID_29712663.md`).

Contents (captions, PDF pages 2-7):
- Fig S1 (p.2-3): goodness-of-fit plots (PRED and IPRED vs observed; CWRES vs PRED and vs time).
- Fig S2 (p.4): box plots comparing observed and simulated AUC and trough (Cmin) in patients.
- Fig S3 (p.5): histograms of bootstrap parameter estimates against the original NONMEM estimate.
- Fig S4 (p.6): simulated concentration-time curves (N = 1000, median and 90% interval) for patient subpopulation on F1, dosing regimen on CL, and high (172.4 kg) or low (45 kg) body weight on F1.
- Fig S5 (p.7): structure of the final model, read on the page image.

## Model and parameters
Structure from Fig S5 (PDF p.7): one-compartment model (volume V, clearance CL); absorption in two parallel paths, a zero-order input of duration D1 and a first-order input with rate constant ka, with relative bioavailability F1 and variability noted on parameters. Covariate links drawn: F1 with tablet A/B and with AML/MDS versus HSCT; ka with food; CL with dosing regimen. The Fig S4 caption adds weight (high 172.4 kg, low 45 kg) acting on F1. No coefficient is printed in the supplement, so the covariate equations the library would need are not here.
