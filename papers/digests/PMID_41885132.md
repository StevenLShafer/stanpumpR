# Ozbek 2026: GLP-1 and GLP-1/GIP agonists in preconception, pregnancy and lactation (systematic review)

## Study
- Systematic review (PRISMA 2020; PROSPERO CRD420251141587) of PubMed, Web of Science, Scopus and Cochrane to 23 Sep 2025 (printed p4504). 1852 records, 934 after de-duplication, 36 studies included (p4505).
- Included designs: large cohorts and registries, RCTs in women with PCOS (preconception exposure only), case reports and series, pharmacovigilance, one lactation PK study (p4505, Table 1 pp4506-4511). Narrative synthesis; no meta-analysis.
- Drugs searched: semaglutide, liraglutide, dulaglutide, exenatide, lixisenatide, tirzepatide (p4504). Table 1 contains no lixisenatide-specific study and no tirzepatide-only study; tirzepatide appears only inside one mixed GLP-1 RA cohort (Maya 2025: semaglutide 45%, liraglutide 26%, dulaglutide 21%, tirzepatide 7%).
- Outcomes: maternal (GDM, hypertensive disorders, weight gain, preterm, caesarean), fetal and neonatal (malformations, growth, hypoglycaemia, jaundice, NICU, Apgar), fertility, lactation.

## Model and parameters
No pharmacokinetic model. The only PK-type numbers are secondary:
- Lactation (Diab 2024, 8 postpartum women, semaglutide): milk at 0, 12, 24 h by LC-MS, LLOQ 5.7 ng/mL, none detected; worst-case relative infant dose 1.26% (printed p4521). Dose NR in Table 1.
- Case report Ivanisevic 2018: umbilical vein liraglutide below the assay limit (< 2 pmol/L) against a maternal 8.4 pmol/L, at 39 weeks, liraglutide 1.8 mg/day throughout pregnancy (Table 2, printed p4516 area; Table 1 p4509).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD model, ke0, MEAC or potency ratio. End points are clinical outcomes (malformations, GDM, etc.). Headline finding: periconceptional or early-pregnancy exposure is not consistently associated with higher risk of major malformations, growth restriction, stillbirth or neonatal death in adjusted analyses; Broe 2025 found an unadjusted liraglutide signal (aOR 3.52) that vanished against prior-exposure controls (aOR 0.74) (Table 2). Not an opioid review.

## Covariates
None modelled. The review describes only exposure window (preconception, first trimester, throughout) and comparator (insulin, other antidiabetics, unexposed).

## Caveats
- Authors: mostly observational and prone to confounding by indication; most exposures stopped at pregnancy recognition, so second and third trimester data are very limited; no dose data in many cohorts; the lactation PK study is a single drug in 8 women, one time frame, no infant serum levels (Table 3).
- The review contains no pregnancy pharmacokinetic study (no drug level or CL change in pregnant women); the search was an outcomes search, so its silence on PK is not an exhaustive PK search.
