# Park 2006: CYP2D6*10 and itraconazole on single-dose oral haloperidol

## Study
- 19 healthy Korean nonsmoking men genotyped in advance (9 CYP2D6*1/*1, 10 *10/*10; other common Asian alleles excluded); 15 completed (8 *1/*1, 7 *10/*10); 4 withdrew for adverse events (1 acute dystonia, 3 akathisia). Age 24.5 +/- 2.2 y, weight 74.1 +/- 8.5 kg.
- Randomized 2-way crossover, 4-week washout: itraconazole 200 mg twice daily (Methods; abstract says 200 mg/d) or placebo for 10 days; single 5 mg oral haloperidol on day 7.
- Plasma at 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10, 12, 24, 48, 72 h; urine 48 h. HPLC-UV, LOQ about 0.2 ng/mL (plasma). Dextromethorphan metabolic ratio for phenotype.
- Noncompartmental (WinNonlin 2.0).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- QTc (Fridericia): mean change about 3-4 ms over 24 h; max change 11.7-16.3 ms at about 4-5 h; no genotype or itraconazole effect; no correlation with concentration (Table 4).
- UKU and BARS: higher with *10/*10 and itraconazole, significant only for BARS total in *10/*10 + itraconazole vs *1/*1 placebo (Table 3).
- Therapeutic concentration cited from literature: 5-17 ng/mL (refs 11, 20). No ke0, no concentration-effect model.

## Covariates
CYP2D6 genotype (*1/*1 vs *10/*10) and itraconazole (CYP3A4 inhibition), by group comparison. Dextromethorphan MR correlated with CL/F and AUC (stronger under itraconazole).

## Caveats
- Small n (8 and 7), 20-fold IIV; genotype effect alone not significant.
- Sampling to 72 h in a drug with a long terminal phase biases t1/2 and CL/F (mean of individual ratios).
- Internal inconsistencies: abstract swaps the genotype and itraconazole AUC comparisons; text calls the itraconazole CL/F change a 25% decrease although 4.2 to 2.8 is 33%; text says *10/*10 decreased CL/F 24% (3.6 vs 4.7 is 23%); abstract gives itraconazole as 200 mg/d, Methods 200 mg twice daily.
- The Discussion says the genotype difference was significant under itraconazole, but Table 1's footnote compares only against *1/*1 placebo.
