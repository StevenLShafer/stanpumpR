# Zuppa 2019: dexmedetomidine in infant cardiac surgery with CPB (phase 1, PK)

## Study
Multicentre (4 Pediatric Heart Network centres) open-label phase 1 dose escalation and model-validation study
(NCT01915277). 124 enrolled, 122 dosed, 119 with complete PK. Neonates 0-21 d (n = 60) and infants 22-180 d (n = 59)
having arterial switch, VSD or tetralogy repair with CPB (no deep hypothermic arrest). Table 2 (medians): age 7 d
(neonates 5, infants 97), weight about 3.8 kg (3.4 / 5.2), length about 54 cm, CPB time 92 min, lowest CPB temperature
33.1 C (column alignment garbled; values assigned by magnitude). Part 1: fixed escalation (CPB-prime-only cohorts,
then loading 0.2-1 ug/kg over 10 min + infusion 0.1-0.75 ug/kg/h + prime dose); Part 2: model-driven dosing to
200-300, 400-500, 600-700 pg/mL with external validation. Plasma DEX by LC-MS/MS, LLOQ 5 pg/mL. 1967 concentrations,
168 BLQ (8.5%). NONMEM 7.2 ADVAN3 TRANS4, FOCE-I.

## Model and parameters
Two compartments, separate parameter sets pre-CPB, on CPB, and post-CPB; size fixed allometric (CL, Q x (WT/70)^0.75;
V x WT/70) or linear (all x WT/70); age maturation on CLpost (Hill); temperature on V1cpb as (T/37)^-1.6. Linear model
had AIC 10 lower (16318 vs 16328) and fewer VPC outliers (5.8 vs 7.6%) and is called "statistically superior".

## Supplementary material
Archived 2026-10-27 as `PMID_31623840_mmc<n>` (Elsevier's numbering). Mapping, from each file's content and the
article's references to it: mmc1 Appendix 1 (sampling times by cohort); mmc2 Appendix 2 (adverse events); mmc3
Appendix 3 (external-validation plots); mmc4 Appendix 4 ("final mathematical forms"); mmc5 and mmc6 Appendix 5 (NONMEM
control streams, allometric and linear); mmc7 and mmc8 Appendix 6a/6b (likelihood profiles, allometric and linear);
mmc9-24 Appendix 7 (simulated concentrations under the Table 9 dosing guidance, neonates and infants, 200/500/700/1000
pg/mL, each model).

**Appendices 4 and 5 do not describe the Table 7 model.** The article calls Appendix 4 the final mathematical model
(PDF p. 10), but both it and the control streams have two periods only, during CPB (`TIME <= CPBT`, which also covers
the pre-bypass samples) and after CPB, with 10 THETAs, and different values. Appendix 4, allometric (per 70 kg):
CL 672 x (WT/70)^0.75 x Age/(1.56 + Age) mL/min after CPB, 12.2 x (WT/70)^0.75 during CPB; V1 179 after, 126 x
(WT/70) x (TEMP/37)^-1.84 during (the -1.84 is a superscript in the Word file, so an exponent); Q 120 after, 2800
during; V2 82.7 after, 221 during. Linear: CL 1220 x Age/(1.29 + Age) and 20.6; V1 148 and 134 x (TEMP/37)^-1.95;
Q 187 and 5540; V2 96.9 and 328. The control streams' `$THETA` blocks are identical in the allometric and linear
files and their comment labels are shifted one line, so they are initial estimates, not results (e.g. THETA(1)
0.631 L/min).

The likelihood profiles (Appendix 6, mmc7 allometric and mmc8 linear, rendered) have 14 THETAs, and their minima
sit at Table 7's values: allometric THETA 1 about 0.62 L/min (CLpost 623), 2 about 0.074 (CLcpb 74.1), 3 about 155
(V1post), 4 about 115 (V1cpb), 5 about 0.21 (Qpost 209), 6 about 2.98 (Qcpb), 7 about 105 (V2post), 8 about 144
(V2cpb), 9 about 1.77 (age), 10 about -1.6 (temperature), 11 about 1.24 (CLpre), 12 about 132 (V1pre), 13 about 2.3
(Qpre), 14 about 79 (V2pre) (read from the plots). So Table 7 is the published final model, with the pre-CPB
parameters added as THETAs 11-14 to the Appendix 5 structure.

**Provenance (Steven Shafer, personal communication, 2026-10-27).** Shafer reviewed the manuscript and pointed out
that the model had only two periods. Zuppa agreed and built the three-period model now in Table 7. Shafer then
implemented stanpumpR's infant model directly from that final model's NONMEM control file. So Appendices 4 and 5
are the superseded pre-review version, published in error as the final model, and Table 7 and stanpumpR both carry
the post-review model.

Maturation: Appendix 4 and both control streams use Age/(TM50 + Age), a Hill coefficient of 1 with no
normalisation to 180 days, age in days. The final model's THETA 9 occupies the same slot, and stanpumpR's form was
taken from the final control file (Provenance above), so the form is the final model's, not only the earlier one's. Neither form gives the article's "90% at about
18 days": Hill 1 gives 90% at 9 x 1.77 = 15.9 d (derived); normalised to 180 d, 14.5 d (derived).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD model, no ke0. Target plasma concentrations 200-1000 pg/mL (Table 9 dosing guidance). Safety: 5/122 adjudicated
events (4.1%, 95% CI 1.8-9.2%: junctional rhythm x2, 2nd/3rd-degree AV block x2, hypotension x1), all infants, at DEX
126-977 pg/mL. Elimination half-life cited as about 2 h (refs 21-23).

## Covariates
Weight (fixed allometric or linear), postnatal age on CLpost (Hill), CPB temperature on V1cpb. CPB duration and
ultrafiltration had minimal effect and were dropped.

## Caveats
Ages 0-180 days only; CPB-specific; Qcpb/Qpre poorly estimated (SE ~96-107%); text-layer table misalignment; the
authors prefer the linear model statistically but present both. Pre-CPB period had a single sample per subject.
