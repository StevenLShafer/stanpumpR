# Vucicevic 2025: efavirenz PopPK-PGx (CYP2B6)

## Study
89 adult Caucasian HIV-1 patients (Belgrade), on efavirenz 600 mg once daily for at least 3 months; one steady-state sample per patient, 2.83 to 13.42 h after the last dose. Median (IQR) weight 76 (66-85) kg, age 40 (32-48) y; 21 (23.6%) male. Carriers: CYP2B6 516G>T 30 (33.7%), c.485-18C>T 12 (13.5%), NR1I3 c.540C>T 53, c.152-1089T>C 58. Exclusions: antituberculosis drugs and other interacting drugs, pregnancy. NONMEM (7.3 in abstract, 7.4 in methods), FOCE-I, PsN, Pirana; bootstrap 1000 and NPDE. Assay not described in this paper (data from Olagunju 2014).

## Model and parameters
One-compartment, first-order absorption (ADVAN2 TRANS2), IIV on CL only (exponential), proportional error. V/F and ka fixed to literature (not estimated).

Covariate model: CL/F = CLp x (1+theta_RSA)^RSA x (1+theta_RSB)^RSB, flags 0/1. Both flags set gives 13.9 x 0.636 x 0.732 = 6.47 L/h (derived). Text: 36.4% lower (GT vs GG, n=30 vs 60), 26.8% lower (TT, n=12). Smoking, weight, BMI, sex and NR1I3 variants not retained.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD. Target trough range used for simulation: 1000-4000 ng/mL (matrix not stated in this paper). Simulation (typical 76 kg, 600 mg/day, with IIV), Table 3, PDF p.6 (text layer, unconfirmed): median Ctrough 3072.75 (516GT+485TT), 2140.05 (GT, CC), 1757.15 (GG, TT), 1179.4 (GG, CC) ng/mL; below 1000: 0, 0, 0.02, 34.8%; above 4000: 5.3, 0, 0, 0%. Missed doses to fall out of range: 3 (both variants), 2 (either), 1 (neither). Not an opioid.

## Covariates
CYP2B6 516G>T and c.485-18C>T on CL/F, as above.

## Caveats
One sample per patient, so V/F and ka are not data-informed and absorption/distribution are assumptions; single-ethnicity (white Caucasian) cohort; low CL/F IIV (13.1%) with 35% RSE; CL/F is apparent (F unknown). Authors compare CL/F 13.9 L/h with published 4-15 L/h.
