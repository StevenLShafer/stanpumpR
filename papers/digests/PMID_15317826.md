# Cox 2004: argatroban population PK-PD with a GP IIb/IIIa antagonist during PCI

## Study
- **Design:** open-label, multicentre, adaptive. GlaxoSmithKline and Encysive supported it.
- **Patients:** 152 undergoing PCI, 106 men and 46 women. Mean (SD) age 63 (11) y and weight 89 (18) kg; the median
  weight was 87 kg. Clinically relevant hepatic dysfunction was an exclusion (p2).
- **Dosing:**
  - Part 1, n=101: argatroban 250 mcg/kg bolus then 15 mcg/kg/min during the procedure, with abciximab (n=99) or
    eptifibatide (n=2).
  - Part 2, n=51: 300 mcg/kg bolus then 15 mcg/kg/min, with abciximab.
  - Up to 3 extra 150 mcg/kg boluses were allowed to reach the target ACT of 275-325 s.
  - All patients also had aspirin and clopidogrel or ticlopidine.
- **Sampling:** sparse, 3-6 samples per patient. The first was 5-15 min after the start, then about every 30 min,
  and every 30 min after the infusion until sheath removal (usually within 3 h). PK was assessed up to 4 h.
- **Assay:** plasma (citrate) LC/MS/MS with LLOQ 5.00 ng/mL; whole-blood ACT by Hemochron or HemoTec.
- **Analysis:** NONMEM V with FO; FOCE and FOCE-I terminated. Sequential PK then PD. A 70% index set and a 30%
  validation set were fitted, then the combined set was refitted.

## Model and parameters
Two compartments (ADVAN3 TRANS4) with exponential IIV and a combined proportional plus additive error. The final PK
model is model 4, refitted to all 152 patients. Table II is on PDF p5 (printed 985).

- **Derived values** (my arithmetic):
  - At 87 kg, k10 = 2.0, k12 = 0.909 and k21 = 0.769 /h, giving λ1 = 3.20 and λ2 = 0.481 /h.
  - The half-lives are therefore 13.0 and 86.5 min. Vss is 24 L.
- **Model building (Table I):**
  - Weight on CL dropped the OF by 76 points; ηCL fell from 41% to 38%.
  - Adding age (to CL in Table I) aborted the covariance step.
  - The text says the residual plots suggested age on V1, not CL, so Table I and the text disagree on where age was
    tried.
- **Healthy-volunteer comparators quoted (p8):** CL 19.3-24.4 L/h and Vss 18.6-25.0 L.
- **Observed:** the mean maximal argatroban concentration was 2673 ng/mL, reached 0.2-0.5 h after dosing whatever
  the bolus.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- **End point:** whole-blood ACT, as a baseline sigmoid Emax in plasma concentration. Individual predicted
  concentrations were the input, with no effect compartment and no ke0. Final model 10 has no IIV on E0. Table IV is
  on PDF p6 (printed 986).
- **Arithmetic check:** the 464 s plateau (p8) is E0 + Emax = 148 + 316.
- **Observed mean maximal ACT:** 288 s (250 mcg/kg bolus) and 297 s (300 mcg/kg). Additional boluses were needed by
  22% and 27% of patients.
- **The paper's EC50 statement:** it says 1920 ng/mL raises the mean ACT by 50%, equating this to a typical healthy
  steady state at 10 mcg/kg/min. On its own Table IV, EC50 gives half of Emax, which is 148 + 158 = 306 s, about a
  107% rise over E0 (derived). The 50% wording is the paper's.
- **Variability inconsistency:** the discussion says IIV was no greater than 26% where estimated, but Table IV gives
  36% for Emax.

  - {name: residual proportional, value: 0.12, unit: fraction, source: "Table IV", note: "precision 8%"}

The paper reports no ke0, tPeak or hysteresis analysis, and no aPTT model.

## Covariates
- Weight on CL, linear and additive. Its effect is small, and the authors say no dosing change beyond weight-based
  dosing is needed.
- Age, gender and race: no effect on CL in their summary, although the covariance step aborted when these were added.
- No PD covariates.
- No hepatic or renal covariate was tested, and hepatic dysfunction was an exclusion.

## Caveats
- Sparse sampling over at most 4 h, so the terminal phase is poorly informed.
- FO estimation only.
- Everyone was on a GP IIb/IIIa antagonist, aspirin and thienopyridines.
- The ACT device was not tested as a covariate.
- Q had no IIV.
- The text, Table I and Table IV disagree in small ways (age on V1 or CL; IIV "no greater than 26%").
- The authors call the relationship best described by sigmoid Emax, though earlier healthy-volunteer work suggested a
  linear one.
- The label's healthy-subject values (`NJ_label-argatroban_020883_2026-06-17.md`) differ from this model:
  - CL is 0.31 L/kg/h, about 27 L/h at 87 kg, against 22.
  - Vss is 174 mL/kg, about 15 L, against 24.
  - Terminal t1/2 is 39-51 min, against the model's 86 min.
  - This is derived, not a statement by the paper.
