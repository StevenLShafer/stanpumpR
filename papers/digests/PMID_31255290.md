# Joosten 2019: closed-loop norepinephrine titration, first in man

## Study
Single-centre proof-of-concept feasibility study, Erasme Hospital, Brussels, May-Aug 2018 (NCT03515161). 20 adults, ASA 1-3 (ASA 2/3 = 7/13), elective moderate/high-risk surgery: 4 each of major vascular, Whipple, lobectomy, endovascular cerebral aneurysm embolisation, and post-cardiac-surgery in ICU before extubation. Table 1 (median [IQR]): age 64 (52-71) y, 7 male (35%), weight 73 (61-79) kg, height 167 (162-169) cm. Exclusions included arrhythmia, LVEF <30%, RV failure, pregnancy.
Anaesthesia: TIVA, propofol (Schnider) and remifentanil (Minto) by TCI (effect-site targets adjusted to BIS 40-60), rocuronium; morphine 0.05 mg/kg at incision. Arterial line to EV1000 monitor; MAP recorded every 20 s.
Controller (Rinehart, UC Irvine): PID plus rules-based (rate and rate-of-change limits), norepinephrine via a Q-Core Sapphire pump; target MAP 70 mm Hg (65-75 band), 80 for aneurysm cases (16 and 4 patients); no pressor boluses allowed. Controller active 3877 min total. Primary outcome: % time MAP >5 mm Hg below target.

Totals: 11,576 rate changes; vasopressor given 97.1% of controller time; total 14,382 (unit see open question), 653 [499-810] per patient. 6 of 20 had pump-communication errors (<2 min each); no major complications; 6 minor.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point is MAP (arterial line) relative to a set target (+/-5 mm Hg). No concentration-effect, ke0, EC50, MEAC or potency data; no norepinephrine concentrations measured. The paper reports the remifentanil and propofol TCI models used as background (Minto, Schnider) but no opioid PK/PD results of its own (opioid audit: none).

## Covariates
None modelled. Doses are absolute ug/min; weight median 73 kg gives a median of about 0.053 ug/kg/min (derived: 3.9/73, mixing a median rate with a median weight).

## Caveats
n=20, single centre, single supervising user, no control arm (historical comparison with ref 17), arterial line required, not tested for acute haemodynamic shifts, pressor and fluid interaction not assessed. Time-in-target gap in vascular and thoracic cases (66-83% in-target in some patients, Table 3). Table 3 lists median/IQR rows that do not align with a per-patient row layout in the text layer, but the rendered table (PDF p6) is clear. Authors declare consulting ties to Edwards Lifesciences; the controller itself is not Edwards-owned.
