# Leemann 1993: CYP2D6 phenotype and quinidine change metoprolol Vss as well as CL

## Study
Seven healthy Caucasian men phenotyped with debrisoquine: 4 extensive (EM; 22-34 y, 58-80 kg) and 3 poor
metabolisers (PM; 25-38 y, 65-86 kg) (p2). (+/-)-Metoprolol tartrate 20 mg (58.4 umol) as a 20-min IV infusion,
fasting, on three occasions at least a week apart: alone; 12 h after quinidine sulphate 50 mg orally; on day 3 of
quinidine sulphate 250 mg twice daily (slow release). Whole-blood (EDTA) samples 5 min to 28 h; racemic metoprolol
by HPLC-fluorescence, enantiomers on selected samples (no stereoselectivity found) (p2-3). Biexponential fits in
ELSFIT and noncompartmental analysis; Vss three ways (central elimination, noncompartmental, peripheral
elimination) (p2).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None measured. Not an opioid.

## Covariates
Debrisoquine (CYP2D6) phenotype and quinidine inhibition, on both CL and Vss.

## Caveats
n = 7. EM CL differed significantly from all other groups (P < 0.05), but the EM vs PM Vss difference did not reach
P < 0.05 (one-tailed P 0.06-0.08), and the quinidine effect on EM Vss was not significant by ANOVA (P 0.10-0.24),
only by paired t tests (p4); the authors call their evidence for a clearance-linked Vss sufficient but weak and
attribute the non-significance to subject 1. They find no satisfying mechanism. Whole blood, not plasma.
