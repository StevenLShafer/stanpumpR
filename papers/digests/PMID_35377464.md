# Cascino 2022: second dose of intranasal diazepam (Valtoco) within 4 h

## Study
Sponsor (Neurelis) analysis with two parts.
1. **Long-term phase 3 safety study** (NCT02721069): 163 of 175 enrolled patients aged 6-65 y with epilepsy
   received 4390 doses for 3853 seizure clusters (median 15.05 months on study). 79 patients gave second doses
   within 24 h for 485 clusters (12.6%): 38 patients gave at least one second dose at or under 4 h (152 such doses,
   31.3%; 44 within 10 min), 41 only after 4 h (Table 1, PDF p4). Mean weight 53.3 kg (under-4 h group) and 66.3 kg
   (over-4 h group); mean age 20.9 and 24.4 y.
2. **Population PK** from three phase 1 studies of the nasal spray: 126 individuals (78 healthy adults, 48 patients
   with epilepsy aged 6-59), doses 5, 10, 15, 20 mg by age and weight (0.3 mg/kg at 6-11 y, 0.2 mg/kg at 12 y and
   over) (Table 3, PDF p5). NONMEM FOCE-I; bootstrap (n = 500) with PsN 3.5.3; VPC.

## Model and parameters
Two compartments, first-order absorption and elimination; parameters CL, V2, V3, Q, ka; weight as an allometric
covariate (exponents not given); population (volunteer vs patient) as a covariate on ka (Section 3.3, PDF p5-6).
**No typical values, covariate exponents, bioavailability or residual error are printed**; only interindividual
variability: CL 41.8%, V2 45.5%, V3 47.1%, Q 66.2%, ka 36.2% (PDF p6, rendered).

Simulations (n = 250 per regimen), modelled: a second dose 1 min to 4 h after the first raised Cmax by about 65%
and AUC about twofold relative to a single dose (text, PDF p6), with overlapping exposures across intervals.
Figure 1 (20 mg, patients 12 y and over, 90 kg) shows medians of roughly 260-300 ng/mL for two doses against about
130 ng/mL for a single dose (read from the figure).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PK/PD model. Safety end points: TEAEs 89.5% (34/38, under-4 h group) vs 80.5% (33/41) vs 82.2% overall;
treatment-related TEAEs 31.6% vs 17.1%, mostly nasal (epistaxis 3/38, nasal discomfort 3/38); no respiratory or
cardiac depression (Table 2, PDF p5, rendered). Planned exposure-response analysis of adverse events of special
interest found no relation to number of doses (no concentrations used). The introduction quotes a target plasma
diazepam of 150-300 ng/mL from a rectal-gel pivotal study (PDF p2), not a result of this paper.

## Covariates
Weight (allometric, on all disposition parameters as stated) and population on ka. Equations not printed.

## Caveats
- The text's "approximately 65%" Cmax increase for a second dose does not match the Figure 1 medians, which read
  as about a doubling (Figure 1 is one scenario, 20 mg at 90 kg; the text does not say which scenario or
  statistic its 65% refers to). Unresolved.
- The long-term study had no control group; second-dose timing was investigator- or caregiver-driven, not
  randomised.
- Model parameters are not reported, so the model cannot be reproduced from this paper.
