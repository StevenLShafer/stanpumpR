# Sintetos 1987: esmolol as an intravenous bolus in healthy men

## Study
- 12 healthy men, 22-44 y (mean 29.9). Two boluses each, over 30 s by hand-held syringe, 2 h apart (9:00 and 11:00).
  Dose escalation: 30 mg first in subject 1, each second dose about 50% larger; subjects 9-12 received 100 and 150 mg
  (PDF p2).
- Blood (both forearms cannulated) at 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 15, 30, 45, 60, 90, 105, 120 min after the first bolus; similar
  after the second, to 6 h. Whole-blood esmolol and its acid metabolite by HPLC-UV (229 nm), sensitivity 0.05 ug/mL,
  linear 0.05-5.0 ug/mL, CV 0.6-5.9% (PDF p2).
- PD: PR, QRS, QTc, cardiac cycle (from 1-min HR count), cuff SBP and DBP; control = mean of five 15-min values in each
  1-h control period; peak change within 15 min of each bolus.

## Model and parameters
No model: only four or five quantifiable samples per subject, so kinetic modelling was not possible (PDF p5).
- Peak blood esmolol in samples within 2.0 min of the bolus; after 150 mg 0.868, 1.16, 1.25 and 1.47 ug/mL (PDF p3).
- Peak concentration poorly correlated with dose (Spearman R 0.348; 0.390 per kg).
- Concentrations fell to 1/20 of peak in under 15 min. The Results say they stayed measurable for under 15 min after
  150 mg, while the Fig. 2 caption says they were below quantification at 15 min in two subjects and at 30 min in the
  other two (both PDF p3).
- Metabolite peak 2.89-3.86 ug/mL at 90-120 min after 150 mg.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- Only the PR interval changed consistently, in the four men given 100 and 150 mg: +4.7 to 9.3% (100 mg), +8.0 to 15.4%
  (150 mg); peak PR 192 +/- 7.9 vs control 177 +/- 10.6 ms, P = 0.00002 (abstract and Results, PDF p1, p3).
- Peak PR prolongation 6-10 min after the bolus, when blood esmolol was frequently negligible; counterclockwise
  hysteresis (Fig. 4); PR back to baseline within 15 min (PDF p3-p4).
- No consistent effect on QRS, QTc, cardiac cycle (heart rate), SBP or DBP; SBP fell after 150 mg (not significant,
  P = 0.15).
- No ke0 or EC50 fitted.

## Covariates
None; dose per kg did not improve correlations.

## Caveats
n = 4 at the informative doses; sparse sampling (first sample at 2 min) relative to esmolol's half-lives; heart rate
from 1-min counts at the sampling times only.
