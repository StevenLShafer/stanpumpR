# Agarwal 2015: oral vs IV baclofen PK in healthy adults

## Study
Randomized, open-label, 2-way crossover pilot study (PDF p.2-3). Twelve healthy
adult volunteers (9 male, 3 female; mean weight 79.6 ± 13.5 kg; age 26-56 y,
mean 39.3 y). Single doses with ≥48 h washout: first 3 participants got 3 mg IV
and 5 mg oral; the other 9 got 5 mg IV and 10 mg oral. IV was the commercial
intrathecal formulation (Lioresal Intrathecal, 2 mg/mL, Novartis) infused over
15 minutes; oral was a commercial 10 mg tablet (Qualitest), halved for the 5 mg
dose (PDF p.2). Plasma sampled pre-dose; 5, 15, 30 min; 1, 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12,
24 h. HPLC-MS/MS assay, linear 20-400 ng/mL, LLOQ 20 ng/mL (PDF p.2).
Noncompartmental analysis in Phoenix 6.2 (PDF p.2-3). For the 3 mg IV dose λz
could not be estimated (r² <0.90 or extrapolated AUC >30%), so AUC0-∞ and t½
are not reported for it (PDF p.3).

Mean absolute oral bioavailability 74% (PDF p.3). Dose-normalized AUC differed
between arms (P=.0024); between-subject CV similar (18%-24%) (PDF p.3). Note the
Results text gives Cmax as 176 (15) oral and 313 (75) IV ng/mL (PDF p.3), ~2 ng/mL
off the Table 1 values (174 ± 16, 310 ± 74) — a within-paper rounding mismatch.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Not an opioid; no MEAC, no ke0, no hysteresis model, no potency ratio.
Pharmacodynamics assessed only clinically (sedation by Stanford Sleepiness Scale,
ataxia by gait, nystagmus). Adverse events mild and not dose- or route-related;
all resolved within 6 h (PDF p.3). Authors note a disconnect between peak plasma
concentration and effect: in dogs toxicity onset was delayed 2-3 h after peak, so
clinicians may need to wait ~2-3 h to see maximum clinical effect of an IV dose
(PDF p.4, Discussion); no human effect-site model is fit.

## Covariates
None modelled (noncompartmental, n=12).

## Caveats
Pilot study, small n (12; only 9 received the 5 mg IV / 10 mg oral pairing that
Table 1 reports). Very low doses; tolerability at clinical doses not established
(PDF p.3-4). Healthy volunteers. IV arm used the intrathecal formulation given IV.
3 mg IV arm excluded from AUC0-∞/t½ for poor terminal-phase fit (PDF p.3).
