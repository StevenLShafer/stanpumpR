# Zhang 2020: teicoplanin population PK in children

## Study
- 159 hospitalised Chinese children (87 boys, 72 girls), 0.2-14.0 years (median 3.7), 2.9-69.0 kg (median 14.8).
- 236 serum samples, 89.8% steady-state troughs (about 1.5 per child). HPLC, LLOQ 2.5 mg/L.
- NONMEM 7.2, FOCE-I, one compartment.

## Model and parameters (Results and Table 2)

```yaml
parameters:
  - {name: CL, value: 0.0694, unit: L/h, note: "x (1 + 2.82 x WT/16.71) x 0.882^(SCr/29.075), SCr in umol/L, the child's own"}
  - {name: V, value: 1.39, unit: L, note: "x 1.75^(WT/16.71)"}
```

Check: at 16.71 kg and SCr 29.075 umol/L, CL 0.234 L/h (0.014 L/h/kg) and V 2.43 L (0.15 L/kg), the paper's per-kg values.

## Caveats
Trough-based: the volume, and so the peaks, are weakly informed. One compartment cannot show the long terminal phase.
