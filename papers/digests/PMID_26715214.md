# Colin 2016: propofol breath monitoring and Bayesian forecasting

## Study
- 20 healthy ASA I volunteers (Table 1, PDF p 3): mean age 27 y (range 22.8-33.3), weight 74.1 kg (63.8-83.0), height 168.0 cm (162.2-178.5), 9 M / 11 F.
- Propofol 0.4 mg/kg/min IV for 10 min, 20-min recovery, then Schnider-model TCI at plasma targets 2, 3, 4, 5 ug/mL, 15 min each; infusion stopped at 90 min.
- Arterial plasma propofol by LC-MS/MS (LLOD 60, LLOQ 100 ng/mL), median 22 samples per subject. Breath propofol by IMR-MS from a laryngeal-mask T-piece (50 mL/min), median 208 values per subject, reported in ppb. BIS (Vista, 10 s smoothing), median 300 values per subject.
- NONMEM 7.3 FOCEI. Plasma PK fixed to individual post-hoc Eleveld 2014 (Anesth Analg 2014;118:1221) values (the IPP approach); the breath model was added. 4-fold cross-validation, AIC. A simulation study blinded to plasma data tested Bayesian forecasting from 1-100 min of breath data.

## Model and parameters
Three-compartment Eleveld plasma PK (fixed per subject), plus a lung effect compartment (ke0Lung) and a scale factor K (ug/mL to ppb) that rises linearly with time (Slope). Final model 8 (Table 2, PDF p 8, printed p 856, typical values, RSE in brackets):

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- Not an opioid paper: no MEAC, no analgesic concentration, no potency ratio.
- ke0Lung 0.152/min is a plasma-to-breath equilibration rate, not a brain ke0 (end point: exhaled propofol, ppb). Derived: t1/2 = ln2/0.152 = 4.6 min.
- ke0BIS 0.107/min (end point: BIS, single effect compartment) (derived: t1/2 6.5 min). The paper gives no tPeak.
- BIS sigmoid Emax: EC50 2.71 ug/mL on Ce, gamma 2.43; EC50 12.4 ppb on predicted breath, gamma 2.49. The authors conclude predicted breath concentration works as well as Ce (AIC 4401 vs 4379; residual SD 0.47 vs 0.45).
- Prior comparison cited in the discussion: Ziaian 2014 reported ke0Lung 0.155/min and K 2.71; Kreuer 2014 ke0Lung 0.209/min and K 0.66 (derived by the authors).

## Covariates
None estimated. The covariates of the Eleveld 2014 prior model (weight, age, etc.) are carried in the fixed post-hoc plasma parameters.

## Caveats
- Healthy volunteers, one dosing regimen, one IMR-MS device; the authors say the linear time drift of K may be study specific and could overstate the benefit.
- A priori Eleveld MdPE was 42.8% (bias) in this cohort; after more than 35 min of breath data MdPE was -1.05% and RMSE fell from 1.63 to 1.39 ug/mL (Results 3.2, PDF p 5). 11/20 subjects improved and 9 got worse.
- Breath concentrations are in ppb with a K that drifts upward in time, so absolute ppb values are device-dependent (Kreuer's values were about 50-fold lower).
