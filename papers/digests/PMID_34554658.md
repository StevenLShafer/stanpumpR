# Inker 2021: CKD-EPI eGFR equations without race

## Study
Development data: CKD-EPI 2009 creatinine set (10 studies, 8254 participants, 31.5% Black) and CKD-EPI 2012 creatinine-cystatin C set (13 studies, 5352, 39.7% Black). Validation: 12 studies, 4050 participants, 14.3% Black. All >= 18 years; GFR measured by clearance of exogenous markers. Least-squares regression of log measured GFR on log markers, age, sex (+/- race), with separate slopes below and above the creatinine knot (kappa 0.7 F, 0.9 M) and cystatin C knot (0.8 mg/L). Seven equations compared (three current, four new). CKD prevalence projected in NHANES 1999-2002 (n = 4563).

## Model and parameters
eGFR = mu x min(Scr/kappa,1)^a1 x max(Scr/kappa,1)^a2 x [cystatin terms] x c^Age x d(female) x e(Black), mL/min/1.73 m2, Scr in mg/dL (Table 2 footnote).

Validation bias (median, mL/min/1.73 m2): current eGFRcr(ASR) overestimates by 3.7 in Black and 0.5 in non-Black participants; new eGFRcr(AS) underestimates by 3.6 in Black and overestimates by 3.9 in non-Black participants; P30 85-90% for all creatinine equations.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Not applicable (renal function estimation).

## Covariates
Age, sex, (race in current equations), serum creatinine, cystatin C.

## Caveats
Adults only; indexed to 1.73 m2; dropping race (ASR-NB) underestimates GFR in Black persons by 7.1. Not a drug paper.
