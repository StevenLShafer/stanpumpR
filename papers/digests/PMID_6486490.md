# Lerman 1984: haematocrit and the blood:gas solubility of volatile anaesthetics

## Study
Four male volunteers (ASA I, 25-52 y, fasting, unmedicated; PDF p1). 190 mL venous blood from each, anticoagulated with
10 mL of 7.6% sodium citrate, centrifuged (1800 rpm) into plasma and packed red cells. Plasma (Hct 0%), packed cells (Hct 80%) and four recombined mixtures
(25%, 40%, 55% and a lowest one) were measured per volunteer; the lowest mixture reads "20%" in the abstract and "10%" in the
Methods text layer, and I did not settle which (the OCR layer is poor here).
Blood:gas partition coefficients of isoflurane, enflurane, halothane and methoxyflurane were measured at 37 C in triplicate
within 24 h of sampling (technique of Lerman 1983). Microhematocrit checked; haematocrit +/- 1.5%. Statistics: linear
regression, comparison of slopes, linearity test. Serum albumin 4.53 +/- 0.1 g/dL, globulin 2.3 +/- 0.1 g/dL,
triglycerides 97.5 +/- 23 mg/dL, cholesterol 183 +/- 8 mg/dL (mean +/- SEM).

## Model and parameters
No PK model. Blood:gas partition coefficients at haematocrit 40% (mean; CV < 6%; PDF p2): isoflurane 1.45,
enflurane 2.05, halothane 2.60, methoxyflurane 16.0. The authors state these are about 3.6% too low because of dilution
by citrate (derived by them, PDF p3).

Effect of haematocrit (Fig. 1, PDF p2): isoflurane decreases linearly (P < 0.01); enflurane increases linearly (P < 0.05);
halothane and methoxyflurane no significant change. Plasma coefficient for isoflurane is 20% greater than in packed cells;
for enflurane plasma is 10% less than packed cells.
Derived slope for isoflurane (derived: lambda(40%) = 1.45 with plasma/cell ratio 1.2 and linear in Hct gives plasma about 1.58, packed
cells about 1.32, slope about -0.0033 per % Hct, or about -0.23% of the 40% value per percentage point).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Not applicable: a solubility study, with no concentration-effect data, MEAC, MAC, ke0 or potency ratio.

## Covariates
Haematocrit (0-80%) is the one variable. The authors expect a normal serum albumin, globulin, triglyceride and cholesterol
content and discuss that these also change solubility; they were not varied here.

## Caveats
n = 4 men. Citrate anticoagulant; per the authors the true coefficients are higher than printed by up to about 3.6%. Plasma and
cells came from the same person, so serum constituents were held constant as haematocrit varied, unlike
saline dilution. The authors report their methoxyflurane value exceeds earlier work by 20%, unexplained. They also caution that
extremes of haematocrit usually accompany other physiological changes.

  +1.5% per Hct point (derived: exp(0.0154) - 1). Experimental designs differ (see open question).
- Sevoflurane, desflurane and N2O were not measured here, so those repo factors get no check.
