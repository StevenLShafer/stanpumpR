# Liesenfeld 2011: population PK of dabigatran in atrial fibrillation (RE-LY)

## Study
RE-LY: patients with non-valvular AF randomised to dabigatran etexilate (DE) 110 or 150 mg twice daily, or to warfarin. CRCL < 30 mL/min was excluded (p. 2). PK samples were drawn before and 2 h +/- 30 min after the morning dose at 4 weeks, with further pairs at 3, 6 and 12 months in a subset (p. 2). Analysed: 27,706 total-dabigatran concentrations (after alkaline cleavage of glucuronides; HPLC-MS/MS) from 9522 patients, 65% male (p. 3). NONMEM VI, FOCE with or without interaction, full-model covariate estimation then backward elimination at P < 0.001 (p. 2).

Table 2 (p. 3), mean (SD), median, range:
- Age 71.2 (8.7), 72, 22-97 y.
- Weight 82.7 (19.5), 80.3, 32.7-222.3 kg.
- CRCL (Cockcroft-Gault) 73.2 (27.5), median 68.6, range 16.1-361.4 mL/min.
- Hb 14.2 (1.5), 14.3, 4.4-23.3 g/dL.

The text gives median CRCL as 68.4 (p. 3), Figure 2 68.64, Table 4 69.

## Model and parameters

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None; PK only. No therapeutic range. Introduction background (refs 4-7, not this analysis): Tmax 2-3 h, terminal t1/2 12-17 h, absolute oral F 6.5% from capsules, 80-85% renally eliminated unchanged (p. 2).

## Covariates
Table 4 (p. 5), effect on the parameter and on AUCss:
- **CRCL:** 1.8-fold AUCss at 30 mL/min and 1.2-fold at 50 mL/min, against the median 69 mL/min.
- **Age:** -0.41%/year on CL/F (AUCss +11.5% at 97 vs 72 y).
- **Female:** -8.3% CL/F (AUCss +9.1%).
- **South Asian:** -20.3% (AUCss +25.5%).
- **HF II-IV:** -6.7% (AUCss +7.2%).
- **Weight and haemoglobin:** +0.77%/kg and -4.0% per g/dL on V2/F, with no AUCss effect.
- **Comedication on F:** verapamil +23%, amiodarone +12%, PPI -12.5%.

Not significant: diltiazem, clopidogrel, H2 antagonists, LVD, HF class I, other ethnicities (p. 6).

## Caveats
- Only two samples per occasion, at trough and 2 h. The absorption and peripheral parameters were therefore fixed to an unpublished model, so the shape of the profile rests on that unpublished fit (p. 3).
- Fixed parameters: the text (p. 3) names ka, lag, Q/F and V3/F, and F = 1 (p. 2). Table 5 also asterisks EC50_CRCL 56.7 as fixed and gives it no RSE, while Table 4 describes the Emax function without saying so. Unresolved within the paper.
- The observed median trough sat slightly outside the 90% prediction interval, with 5.6% underprediction (p. 4).
- The epsilon-shrinkage was 15% (p. 4).
- Severe renal impairment was excluded. The 75 mg twice-daily regimen for CRCL 15-30 is a simulation (p. 5).
