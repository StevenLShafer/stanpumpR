# Grönlund 2011: CYP2D6 and CYP3A4 inhibition and IV oxycodone

## Study
- Randomised, three-phase, placebo-controlled crossover; 4-week washouts (PDF p2, printed p144).
- 12 healthy volunteers (9 M, 3 F), 19-30 y, 52-83 kg; non-smokers, no medication (PDF p2, p4).
  CYP2D6: 10 extensive (6 *1/*1, 4 *1/*4), 1 ultrarapid (*1/*1 duplication), 1 poor (*3/*6) (PDF p4).
- Pre-treatment 5 days: placebo, paroxetine 20 mg daily, or paroxetine 20 mg + itraconazole 200 mg daily.
  On day 4, 1 h after the pre-treatment dose, oxycodone 0.1 mg/kg IV over 2 min (OxyNorm 10 mg/mL;
  salt basis of the per-kg dose not stated) (PDF p2).
- Sampling 0.33-48 h; LC-MS/MS; LLQ 0.1 ng/mL oxycodone and oxymorphone, 0.25 ng/mL noroxycodone and
  noroxymorphone (PDF p3).
- Noncompartmental (WinNonlin 4.1); CL and Vss by statistical moments; oxymorphone and noroxymorphone AUC0-48
  because lambda was often not estimable (PDF p3).

## Model and parameters
No compartmental model. Mean +/- SD (Table I, PDF p6, printed p148; rotated table, confirmed on page image).

| Oxycodone | Placebo | Paroxetine | Paroxetine + itraconazole |
|---|---|---|---|
| AUCinf (ug.min/mL) | 9.4 +/- 1.3 | 10.4 +/- 1.3 | 18.6 +/- 4.3 (197% of control) |
| t1/2 (h) | 3.8 +/- 0.4 | 4.0 +/- 0.5 | 6.6 +/- 1.1 (175%) |
| CL (mL/min/kg) | 9.72 +/- 1.33 | 8.74 +/- 1.02 (90%) | 5.05 +/- 1.01 (52%) |
| Vss (L/kg) | 3.25 +/- 0.23 | 3.11 +/- 0.36 | 2.83 +/- 0.26 (87%) |

Metabolites, placebo phase (Table I): noroxycodone Cmax 4.9 +/- 1.6 ng/mL, tmax median 2.0 h (0.33-6.0),
AUCinf 4.4 +/- 1.6 ug.min/mL, t1/2 6.5 +/- 0.7 h, AUCm/AUCp 0.5 +/- 0.2; oxymorphone Cmax 0.26 +/- 0.09 ng/mL,
AUC0-48 0.10 +/- 0.05 ug.min/mL; noroxymorphone Cmax 0.7 +/- 0.2 ng/mL, AUC0-48 0.7 +/- 0.3 ug.min/mL.
Paroxetine cut oxymorphone AUC0-48 to 34% of control; with itraconazole no subject had measurable noroxymorphone.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End points (PDF p3-4): cold pressor (threshold, pain intensity at 30 and 60 s, NRS 0-100), VAS (drug effect,
  drowsiness, performance, pleasantness), pupil diameter (Cogan pupillometer), Maddox wing, DSST; summarised as
  AUEC0-12 (Table II, PDF p8). Placebo phase: CPT 315 +/- 152 s/h, pupil 35 +/- 10 mm/h, DSST 1495 +/- 220.
- No analgesic difference between phases despite doubled exposure; drowsiness and performance AUEC up 28-30% with
  paroxetine; pupil confounded by paroxetine mydriasis (PDF p8).
- No MEAC or minimum analgesic concentration, no ke0 / t1/2ke0 / tPeak, no hysteresis, no concentration-effect
  model, no potency ratio. Oral bioavailability 60-87% is cited from other studies (PDF p8).

## Covariates
CYP inhibition only (above). Genotype not analysed (too few non-EM subjects); EM-only reanalysis unchanged (PDF p4).

## Caveats
- n = 12 young volunteers; PD underpowered (authors, PDF p9). No CYP3A4-only arm.
- Pharmacokinetic values are per kg; dose salt basis not stated (open question).

## Verification against stanpumpR
- **3.8 h half-life in healthy volunteers** (`drugs_oxycodone.R:343`, registry :310 / csv :24): Table I placebo
  t1/2 3.8 +/- 0.4 h after 0.1 mg/kg IV, healthy volunteers - **matches**. Citation Clin Drug Investig 31:143-153
  - **matches**. That Kokki's comparison used this study is Kokki's claim (see `PMID_22448718.md`), not checkable here.
- **Independent comparison with the library oxycodone model (not cited for it)**: library pooled CL 14.7 mL/min/kg,
  Vss 3.27 L/kg on the hydrochloride basis (`drugs_oxycodone.R:48-49`, :415-416). Grönlund placebo CL 9.72 is
  0.66x as printed, or (derived, if computed on the base like Liukas) 10.8 mL/min/kg, 0.74x; Vss 3.25 as printed
  (1.00x) or 3.63 (1.11x). Grönlund's clearance is lower than the pool and nearest Poyhia's 12.2; it does not
  change any cited claim (open questions).
- **Oxymorphone formation after IV oxycodone** (`drugs_oxycodone.R:295-299`, target AUC ratio 1%): (derived)
  placebo oxymorphone AUC0-48 / oxycodone AUCinf = 0.10 / 9.4 = 0.011, with oxymorphone quantifiable here (LLQ
  0.1 ng/mL, Cmax 0.26 ng/mL) - independent support, **matches** the 1% target (ratio of means; AUC0-48 slightly
  underestimates AUCinf). CYP2D6 inhibition lowering oxymorphone (34% of control AUC) is directionally consistent
  with the poor-metaboliser weight 0.38 (`drugs_oxycodone.R:379`), though inhibition is not genotype.
- **MEAC 12 ng/mL, tPeak 12.36 min** (`drugs_oxycodone.R:461-463`): no concentration-effect data here - **cannot
  verify**.
