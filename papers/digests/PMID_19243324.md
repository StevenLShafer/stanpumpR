# Agarwal 2009: CLADS closed-loop propofol in cardiac surgery

## Study
- Randomised (sealed envelopes) comparison of the CLADS closed-loop propofol system with manual titration, target BIS 50 (range 40-60). Adults with ASA II-III, aged 18-65, scheduled for cardiac surgery with CPB.
- 44 randomised, 22 per arm (Fig 1). 7 were excluded because they needed epicardial pacing, which disturbed BIS (3 CLADS, 4 manual). 37 were analysed: 19 CLADS, 18 manual.
- Premedication was diazepam 0.1 mg/kg. Morphine 0.1-0.2 mg/kg was given for line insertion. Fentanyl 3 ug/kg at induction, then 1 ug/kg/h with 1 ug/kg boluses and supplements. Vecuronium, FiO2 0.6, hypothermia to 28 °C on CPB.
- CLADS computes propofol rate from height, weight, risk status and target BIS, using PK variables "established in the developmental stage", delay factors and peak effect. **No values are given.**
- Table 1 (mean±SD), CLADS vs manual: age 40.6±15.9 vs 41.2±12.7 y; M/F 12/7 vs 9/9; BMI 21.3±4.5 vs 22.4±5.7 kg/m2.

Results (PDF p4 and p7, confirmed on the rendered pages):
- Induction time: 242±55 vs 160±47.5 s, P=0.01. CLADS was slower.
- Induction dose: 1.2±0.3 vs 1.7±0.3 mg/kg, P<0.001.
- Lowest BIS during induction: 45±5 vs 38±9.
- Fall in MAP after induction: 23.6±8.6 vs 33.4±11.3 %.
- Total propofol: 13.3±3.8 vs 17.1±6.9 mg/kg, P=0.041.
- Total fentanyl: 10.5±2.5 vs 12.5±3.2 ug/kg, P=0.046.
- Percent of time with BIS within ±10 of target: 80.4±4.6 vs 69.6±7.6 including CPB; 90.3±3.3 vs 80.8±9.3 excluding CPB.
- MDPE: -2.6±5.1 vs -6.9±10.9 including CPB; -0.2±5.2 vs -5.9±7.4 excluding CPB.
- MDAPE: 9.2±2.4 vs 15.5±5.2 including CPB; 6.8±2.2 vs 12.1±3.3 excluding CPB.
- Wobble: 9.5±2.2 vs 10.5±3.3 including CPB; 6.9±1.7 vs 8.5±2.5 excluding CPB.
- Global score: 25±6 vs 40±12 including CPB; 15±3 vs 26±7 excluding CPB.
- Divergence (including CPB): -0.207±0.15 vs -0.198±0.17.
- Manual group changed the rate a median of 21 times/h (range 16-27).
- Phenylephrine: 6 vs 16 patients needed boluses before CPB, and the cumulative dose was 172.5±144.6 vs 331.3±280.9 ug/kg.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: BIS. No ke0, t½ke0, tPeak, EC50 or hysteresis value is reported.
- BIS drifted below 40 during hypothermic CPB, so the performance measures are given with and without CPB.
- The Discussion reviews propofol PK changes on CPB, with conflicting literature on total and free concentration and on clearance. No concentrations were measured here.
- Opioid audit: fentanyl and morphine were only co-medication. No MEAC, ke0 or potency ratio is reported.

## Covariates
CPB and hypothermia were examined only through the with/without CPB split.

## Caveats
- Unblinded. The authors suggest the Hawthorne effect may have made the manual arm an active control.
- 7 of 44 patients were excluded after randomisation.
- Fig 1 and Table 1 give the manual group as n=18 after exclusions. The Table 1 text layer is scrambled, but the rendered page reads correctly.
