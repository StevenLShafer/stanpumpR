# Fiebrich-Westra 2025: DPYD-guided 5-FU TDM

## Study
Single-centre (Isala, Zwolle) prospective cohort, Jan 2020-Dec 2023. 1,104 patients screened for 4 DPYD variants; 34 heterozygous variant patients with GI cancer (c.1905+1G>A *2A n=8, c.1236G>A/HapB3 n=16, p.D949V n=8, p.I560S *13 n=2) and 26 wild-type controls (Fig 1, Table 1). Mean age 58-68 y, weight 77-82 kg, BSA 1.92-1.97 m2 per group (Table 1; *13 patients 1.69 and 2.21). Regimens: folinic acid 400 mg/m2 2 h then 46-h 5-FU infusion (FOLFOX, FOLFIRINOX, FOLFIRI, FOLFOXIRI), BSA-based. Variant patients started at 50% dose (CPIC) and were titrated in cycles 2-4 to a 5-FU AUC0-46h of 20-30 mg x h/L using the Goirand algorithm; wild-type received 100% with no AUC-guided adjustment. Steady-state sampling at least 16 h into infusion (8-10 AM), 4 mL EDTA plus gimeracil, UPLC-MS/MS on plasma; AUC0-46h = Css x 46 h (derived by the authors). Uracil loading test: 1,000 mg oral, U and DHU at 30 and 120 min; U/DHU ratio below 2.4 = normal, 2.4 or above = poor metaboliser.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No ke0, EC50, hysteresis or effect-site model. End points are clinical: CTCAE v5 toxicity (grade 3 or more; a combined score of 6 or more = moderate to severe). Outcome: moderate-severe toxicity 18% (6/34) of variants vs 27% (7/26) of wild-type over 4 cycles; grade 3 or more in 6/34 variants (Results p4-5, Table 2). 97% (33/34) of variant patients were underexposed at 50% dose. 68% (n=19) of variant patients reached AUC above 20 after escalation; 78% of those still treated tolerated 75% or more after 3 cycles. No concentration-toxicity relationship is fitted. The authors suggest the AUC target might be lower for combination regimens, because the original data come from 5-FU/folinic acid regimens (p8-9). Not an opioid.

## Covariates
Genotype (DPYD variant) and DPD phenotype (U/DHU) are the grouping variables, not model covariates. Grade 3 or more toxicity in variant patients was unrelated to age, sex, tumour type, schedule or WHO status (Appendix Table A1).

## Caveats
Small cohorts (n=2 for *13), non-randomised, heterogeneous regimens (irinotecan unevenly distributed), efficacy not assessable, capecitabine excluded. Several numbers disagree between abstract, text and tables (see open questions). Table 2 labels the *2A column "c.1915vG>A" (a typo for c.1905+1G>A).
