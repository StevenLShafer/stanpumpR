# Byon 2017: apixaban population PK/PD in venous thromboembolism treatment

PDF page = printed page - 339 (PDF p. 1 is printed 340).

## Study
Pooled analysis of 8 phase I studies (healthy, incl. renal-impairment, age/sex and body-weight studies), the phase II
Botticelli DVT study (5 and 10 mg bid) and two phase III trials, AMPLIFY (10 mg bid x 7 days then 5 mg bid) and
AMPLIFY-EXT (2.5 and 5 mg bid) (Table 1, PDF p. 2). 8,323 apixaban concentrations from 970 subjects; 3,139 anti-FXa
observations from 795 subjects. Phase III sampling: about 10% of apixaban subjects, -2, 0, 2 and 4 h at steady
state; 8 AMPLIFY-EXT subjects had serial sampling. Table 3 (PDF p. 5): phase I N = 270 (median 33 y, 18-85; CrCL 112.8,
15-318 mL/min; 71.2 kg, 37.7-175); VTE N = 700 (median 61 y, 18-89; CrCL 99.2, 25.3-322 mL/min; 84 kg, 46.9-210;
60.3% male; 1% Asian; severe renal impairment 3 subjects). Assay LC-MS/MS, LLOQ 1 ng/mL. Anti-FXa: Rotachrom Heparin
on STA-Compact, LMWH-calibrated, range 0.1-18 IU/mL (PDF pp. 2-3). NONMEM 7.2, FOCE-I; IIV exponential; residual
additive on log scale.

## Model and parameters
Two-compartment, first-order absorption and elimination, apparent parameters. Renal term (PDF p. 3):
CL/F = CLNR/F + CLR/F x cCrCL/100 x (1 - FLAG) + CLR/F x 150/100 x FLAG, FLAG = 1 when cCrCL > 150 mL/min
(Cockcroft-Gault), i.e. CLR/F linear in cCrCL and held at its 150 mL/min value above that. Dose-dependent Frel for
doses > 10 mg (inhibitory sigmoid Emax, from ref. 26) was in the base model; its parameters are not printed.
Subject status (VTE vs phase I) was in the full model but dropped (3.5% on CL/F, -7.6% on Vc/F, CIs including 0).

Table 5 (PDF p. 8), simulated steady state in 700 VTE subjects, median (90% CI):
- 2.5 mg bid: daily AUC 1,240 ng*h/mL; Cmax 67.0; Cmin 32.0 ng/mL; anti-FXa 1.07 / 0.51 IU/mL at Cmax / Cmin.
- 5 mg bid: daily AUC 2,446 (2,346-2,554) [5th 1,293, 95th 4,807]; Cmax 132.3 (125.2-139.3) [58.6-302.2]; Cmin 63.2
  (59.0-67.9) [21.7-176.5]; anti-FXa 2.10 / 1.00 IU/mL.
- 10 mg bid: AUC 4,649; Cmax 251.2; Cmin 120.2; anti-FXa 3.99 / 1.91.
Dose-proportional over 2.5-10 mg; peak/trough about 2-fold. The 5 mg AUC is about 25% below NVAF's 3,280 (ref. 25,
a 2014 ACoP abstract by Kowalski et al.; the same value as Cirincione 2018 Table 4), attributed to NVAF's 14% lower CL/F and older, lower-cCrCL population.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Anti-FXa activity (Rotachrom, LMWH units) vs model-predicted plasma concentration: linear, slope 0.0159 IU/mL per
ng/mL (Table 5 anti-FXa = Cmax x slope: 132.3 x 0.0159 = 2.10, derived). Exposure-response (logistic regression,
daily AUCss from empirical Bayes CL/F) for bleeding (major or CRNM; 25 events in 522 acute-treatment and 8 in 178
AMPLIFY-EXT subjects) and efficacy (VTE/VTE death; 5 and 1 events): no significant slope (P > 0.1); no threshold. No
ke0, no therapeutic range. Not an opioid.

## Covariates
cCrCL on CLR/F (linear, cap 150); age (power) and sex on CLNR/F; Asian race and strong/moderate CYP3A4/P-gp
inhibitors on CL/F; weight (power) on Vc/F; evening dosing on ka. Weight on CLNR/F not tested.

## Caveats
- Strong CYP3A4/P-gp inhibitors were prohibited in phase II/III; the inhibitor effect mainly reflects moderate
  inhibitors and is smaller than the phase I ketoconazole (~100%) and diltiazem (~40%) interactions.
- Only 3 VTE subjects had severe renal impairment; the 15 mL/min prediction rests on phase I data (down to 15 mL/min).
- Frel parameters for doses > 10 mg are not reported.
- Few clinical events; exposure-response exploratory.
