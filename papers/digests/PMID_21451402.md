# Volmanen 2011: Alternatives to neuraxial analgesia for labor (review)

## Study
Narrative review (Current Opinion in Anesthesiology) of paracervical block (PCB), pudendal block, nitrous oxide and IV remifentanil for labor pain. No new data. Reviewed literature is mostly obstetric clinical studies; only the remifentanil section has pharmacokinetic/pharmacodynamic content.

## Model and parameters
No PK model or parameter table. Facts stated (PDF p.4, printed p.238):
- Umbilical vein-to-maternal artery remifentanil concentration ratio 0.73-0.88 at caesarean section in humans (refs 63, 64); a ratio of 0.1 at steady state in pregnant ewes (ref 65).
- Table 2 (PDF p.4, confirmed on image): remifentanil PCA doses in five studies comparing with epidural: Volmanen titrated PCA bolus 0.1-0.9 ug/kg; Evron PCA bolus 20 ug + background 0.025 ug/kg/min; Solek-Pastuszka titrated PCA bolus 0.2-0.8 ug/kg; El-Kerdawy PCA bolus 0.25 ug/kg + background 0.05 ug/kg/min; Douma PCA bolus 40 ug. (The text layer prints "mg"; the image shows ug.)
- Nitrous oxide (PDF p.2-3): mean concentration for analgesia with continuous use about 40%; intermittent 50% N2O equals about 26% continuous; end-tidal 7% and 8% at 30 s after starting 50% and 70%, and 36% and 55% at end of intermittent inhalation (refs 35-37). Onset about 30 s, full effect about 50 s.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Opioid audit (remifentanil):
- MEAC / minimum analgesic concentration: none reported (no concentration for analgesia given for remifentanil). For nitrous oxide only inhaled/end-tidal percentages above (about 40% continuous for analgesia), not a MEAC definition.
- ke0 / t1/2 ke0 / tPeak: blood-effect-site equilibration half-time 1.3-1.6 min, from computerized simulations (ref 49, Egan 1996), end point not stated in the review (Egan's end point was EEG). fMRI onset of signal 20-30 s, maximum 80-90 s (ref 50, Leppa); fMRI offset half-time 3.07 min (ref 51). No tPeak reported. No hysteresis analysis of its own.
- Potency ratios: none. Pain-score reduction with remifentanil PCA about 2 units (0-10) better than pethidine/fentanyl (ref 56); Fig 2 post-hoc PID about equal across latent, active, transition phases of labour (n = 52, 44, 16).

## Covariates
None for PK. Labour phase did not change analgesic effect in the post-hoc analysis (Fig 2).

## Caveats
Narrative review; efficacy and safety conclusions rest on small, mostly unblinded studies; the remifentanil half-time values are simulations, not measurements in labouring women.
