# Mendelson 2006: pharmacokinetics and effects of d-, l- and racemic methamphetamine

## Study
- Double-blind, placebo-controlled, 6-session Latin-square balanced crossover, sessions about 1 week apart (p 406, PDF p4).
- 12 male intravenous methamphetamine users, not dependent; age 32.3 ± 7.4 y (23-43), weight 73.5 ± 7.0 kg (68.2-90.9), height 177.6 ± 6.1 cm; 9 white, 3 black (Results, p 408, PDF p6).
- Single IV doses over 1 min by pump: d-methamphetamine 0.25 and 0.5 mg/kg; l-methamphetamine 0.25 and 0.5 mg/kg; racemate 0.5 mg/kg (= 0.25 mg/kg d + 0.25 mg/kg l); saline placebo. Drug from Sigma-Aldrich, recrystallised; whether doses are expressed as base or hydrochloride salt is **not stated**.
- Blood (venous plasma) at 0, 0.5, 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12, 18, 24, 30, 36, 48 h.
- Assay: enantioselective GC-MS (chiral beta-DEXcst column, trifluoroacetyl derivatives); linear 0.5-500 ng/mL; LOQ 1 ng/mL for methamphetamine and amphetamine enantiomers.
- Noncompartmental analysis (WinNonlin 3.1): AUC by linear trapezoid, λ by log-linear regression, t½ = ln2/λ, CL = Dose/AUC, Vd = CL/λ (i.e. Vz, volume during the elimination phase, not Vss).
- PD: HR, SBP/DBP, rate-pressure product, respiratory rate, skin and tympanic temperature; verbal intoxication 0-100; VAS (any drug effect, good, liking, bad, intoxication, high); POMS, BDI, STAI, Buss; monetary value.

## Model and parameters
Noncompartmental; no compartmental model. Table I (PDF p8-9, printed pp 410-411), mean ± SD (95% CI). Values below confirmed on the rendered page.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- No PK/PD model, no ke0, EC50 or hysteresis analysis. Not an opioid; no MEAC.
- HR, BP and rate-pressure product peaked about 5-15 min after the IV dose (p 409); plasma methamphetamine peaked at the first sample (0.5 h) (p 408). Effects of d-containing doses lasted up to about 6 h; l effects returned to baseline in 2-4 h (CV) or about 3 h (subjective).
- Table II (PDF p12), mean peak change (SD): HR placebo 3.7, l 0.25 6.5, l 0.5 10.7, racemate 18.7, d 0.25 18.0, d 0.5 13.5 beats/min. SBP: 4.3, 13.8, 19.5, 33.4, 21.8, 34.5 mm Hg. DBP: 3.9, 6.9, 12.8, 15.7, 13.5, 20.0 mm Hg. VAS intoxication: 1.7, 14.6, 30.3, 24.7, 36.7, 46.0. VAS drug liking: 2.1, 14.9, 28.6, 33.3, 38.9, 47.7. Core temperature unchanged; skin temperature fell with all active doses.
- Potency (cited in vitro, not measured here; Discussion p 417): d-methamphetamine dopamine-release EC50 24.5 ± 2.1 nmol/L vs l 416 ± 20 nmol/L (17-fold); dopamine uptake Ki 114 vs 4840 nM; d about twice l for norepinephrine release. Introduction says d-isomers are 2-10 times more potent for CNS stimulation (literature).

## Covariates
None examined. All doses per kg; all male.

## Caveats
- n = 12 men, experienced users, single IV doses only; no oral, nasal or smoked arm; no women.
- Vd is Vz (CL/λ), not Vss.
- Dose basis (base vs salt) not stated.
- Abstract internal slips: SBP increases reported in "beats/min"; the abstract's paired values for intoxication (46.0 vs 30.3) and liking (47.7 vs 28.6) are, per Table II, d 0.5 mg/kg vs l 0.5 mg/kg, although the sentence reads as if the first number were l. The racemic-d amphetamine Cmax CI (4.9-7.6) has its lower bound equal to the mean, likely a misprint. None of these is used by stanpumpR.
- "30%" is stated two ways: abstract, d AUC 30% smaller than l; Results, l AUC 30% greater than d. The reported ratio 0.679 corresponds to d 32% smaller; mean AUC0-∞ gives 990.7/1406.6 = 0.70 (derived). Either way the comparator is the l-enantiomer **in the same racemic dose**.
- Authors note l clearance fell in the racemate (0.188 vs 0.221 L/h/kg), suggesting d inhibits l metabolism; d clearance unchanged.

## Verification against stanpumpR
- **Half-lives** (`R/drugs_methamphetamine.R:21-22`, help md:7): "l 13.3-15.0 h versus d 10.2-10.7 h" — matches Table I (l 13.6, 13.3, 15.0; d 10.2, 10.3, 10.7) and the abstract. **matches**. (Strictly, the l range across Table I is 13.3-15.0 including 13.6; correct.)
- **"Kinetics broadly similar"**: matches the paper's own conclusion. **matches**.
- **"the d-enantiomer's area under the curve 30% smaller after the racemate than after d alone"** (`R/drugs_methamphetamine.R:22-23`; help md:7 says "exposure 30% smaller after the racemate than after d alone"): **discrepancy — wrong comparator.** The 30% (ratio 0.679) compares d with **l within the racemic dose** (abstract; Results p 408). Against d given alone at the same d dose (0.25 mg/kg), the d-enantiomer from the racemate had AUC0-t 937.6 vs 965.2 ng·h/mL and AUC0-∞ 990.7 vs 1010.3 (Table I, PDF p8-9): about 2-3% lower (derived), and the authors call the same-isomer AUCs "equivalent" (p 418). The difference after the racemate is mainly the l-isomer's **lower** clearance (0.188 vs 0.221 L/h/kg), not a smaller d exposure. The conclusion that l-methamphetamine is not this model still stands on the half-life and PD differences; whether the racemate's d-half is representable is left as an open question.
- **Docs claim "not traceable"** (`docs/illicit-drugs-specification-review.md:97-99`): the clearances 0.257 and 0.259 L/h/kg are in Table I (PDF p8-9) for d 0.25 and 0.5 mg/kg (also 0.266 for d from the racemate). The doc's "suspiciously close to Harris" concern: these are a different study (12 men, IV only, non-deuterated, 2006) from Harris 2003 (8 users, deuterated IV with intranasal/smoked). **discrepancy (stale doc; the values are traceable)**; they are not coded, so no model effect.
- **Independent check of the library model** (not cited for it): `R/drugs_methamphetamine.R:136-137` uses Harris CL 0.272 L/h/kg and V 4.2 L/kg (Vss) giving t½ 10.70 h. Mendelson's d values: CL 0.257-0.266 L/h/kg (2-6% lower than the model), Vz 3.73-3.97 L/kg (5-11% lower than the model's 4.2; Mendelson's is Vz, which for a near one-compartment drug should be close to Vss), t½ 10.2-10.7 h (model 10.70 h). Both per kg, both hours; units agree after the model's /60 to L/min. The uncited source supports the one-compartment d-model within about 10%. **consistent**.
- **`inst/help/investigators.md:81`**: "the difference between the enantiomers (Mendelson)" — **matches**.
- The model's no-effect-site rationale (`R/drugs_methamphetamine.R:31-35`) concerns intranasal dosing and is not attributed to Mendelson; Mendelson's IV data (CV peak 5-15 min, plasma peak at the first 0.5 h sample) provide no ke0 either.
