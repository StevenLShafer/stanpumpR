# Allegaert 2015: pooled paracetamol and metabolite popPK in young women

## Study
Pooled IV paracetamol data, 69 women (Table 1, PDF p4): 47 at caesarean delivery (2 g over 20 min then 1 g q6h to
24 h; 21/47 < 37 weeks); 8 of them again 10-15 weeks post-partum ("early", 2 g single dose); 7 of those 8 again about
1 year post-partum ("late"); 8 healthy volunteers not on oral contraceptives (2 g single dose); 14 Gregoire 2007
volunteers on contraceptives (13 oral, 1 levonorgestrel IUD; 2 g then 1 g q6h, plasma only). Weights (mean, SD): 79.7
(12.9) kg at delivery, 68.8 (11.2) early post-partum, 67.1 (13.5) late, 63.9 (6.6) volunteers, 59.8 (8.9) Gregoire.
Oestradiol 4833 pg/mL at delivery vs 75-86 otherwise. 815 plasma samples and 101 urine collections. HPLC (LLOQ 0.08
mg/L plasma). NONMEM VI FOCE-I, ADVAN6; P < 0.005 forward, P < 0.001 backward; 250-replicate bootstrap.

## Model and parameters
Three-compartment paracetamol (V1, V2 via Q, V8 via Q1) with glucuronidation (CLPG), sulphation (CLPS) and unchanged
renal clearance (CLPU); oxidative clearance (CLPO) could not be identified, so the model has no oxidation pathway.
Metabolite volumes fixed at 18% of V1; k35 = k46 = MF x k17. Reference group: late post-partum plus volunteers not on
contraceptives. Table 2 (PDF p5, rendered), mean (CV%):

pre-term delivery 21.44 (x1.77); early post-partum 8.79 (x0.73). Derived Vss (V1 + V2 + V8): 62.1 L reference vs 78.0

Internal inconsistencies (Table 2 rendered against the text, PDF p8-9): the Discussion gives the delivery
glucuronidation clearance as 15.8 L/h and early post-partum as 4.66 L/h, while Table 2 gives 2.03 x 7.33 = 14.9 and
0.55 x 7.33 = 4.0; the products in the table are the arithmetic of the stated factors. The pre-term sulphation factor is
1.34 in the abstract and Results, but Table 2's 5.61 / 3.86 = 1.45. Unresolved which is right; the table is internally
consistent for CLPG, not for CLPS.

Table 3 (PDF p9) collects literature IV clearances in women, e.g. Kulo 2012 (28 at delivery) 20.3 L/h and 0.72 L/kg
(median, range), against 13.7-22.7 L/h in nonpregnant cohorts.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No ke0 or analgesic concentration.

## Covariates
Delivery (factor 2.03), early post-partum (0.55) and oral contraceptives (1.46) on CLPG; pre-term delivery on CLPS;
urine flow on CLPU; delivery on V1 (more significant than weight on V1, and adding weight did not improve it); weight on
Q; early post-partum on Q1. Oestradiol or progesterone as power functions improved the base model but added nothing to
the delivery covariate. Late post-partum did not differ from volunteers.

## Caveats
Oxidation not identifiable. Single-dose arms sampled only to 6 h. Delivery data are hours after caesarean surgery.
Gregoire volunteers analysed with their own residual error. The text and table disagree on two numbers (above).
