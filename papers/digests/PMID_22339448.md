# van Kuilenburg 2012: 5-FU PK in DPYD c.1905+1G>A carriers

## Study
Retrospective therapeutic-drug-monitoring data (p.2, journal p.164). 30 cancer patients heterozygous for DPYD c.1905+1G>A ("DPD-deficient") and 18 controls without the mutation. Doses: 5FU 300 mg/m2 and/or 450 mg/m2 as a 2-min IV bolus. 26 deficient patients had 300 mg/m2 (15 of them also 450), 4 deficient had 450 only; 10 controls had 300 (7 also 450), 8 controls 450 only. Serum drawn pre-dose and at 5, 10, 15, 30, 45, 60, 90, 120 min (other arm); HPLC. Age table (Table I): medians 55-63 y, ranges 42-76 y; weight not given. Software: MW/Pharm KINFIT (non-compartmental) and KINPOP, iterative two-stage Bayesian (ITSB) population analysis; residual SD = 0.25 + 0.1 C (mg/L). Limited sampling model (LSM): Bayesian Vmax estimate from one sample (30 or 60 min) using the pooled POP-PK model; external validation in 33 colorectal patients. Clinical outcomes retrospective (107 genotyped patients).

## Model and parameters
Two-compartment model with Michaelis-Menten elimination from the central compartment; parameters Km, Vmax, V1, V2, CL12 (inter-compartmental clearance). Units: Km mg/L, Vmax mg/h, volumes L, CL12 L/h. Doses given per m2; no BSA/weight values printed, so no per-kg/per-m2 conversion to absolute dose is possible from the paper.

Table II, population estimates (mean ± SD, p.3, journal p.166):
- POP-PK, all patients: Km 4.74 ± 1.24, Vmax 1040 ± 424, V1 20.2 ± 7.4, V2 15.7 ± 4.0, CL12 66.9 ± 8.4
- POP-PK-CONT: Km 4.55 ± 0.64, Vmax 1512 ± 348, V1 15.3 ± 6.4, V2 15.8 ± 2.9, CL12 68.8 ± 17.8
- POP-PK-DEF: Km 3.69 ± 0.81, Vmax 794 ± 217, V1 21.9 ± 7.8, V2 17.7 ± 4.6, CL12 58.3 ± 15.2

Table III, individual estimates (mean ± SD, journal pp.167-168):
- 300 mg/m2, deficient n=26 vs control n=10: Vmax 942 ± 310 vs 1749 ± 380 mg/h (p<0.001); Km 4.81 ± 0.39 vs 4.39 ± 0.72; V1 20.7 ± 7.3 vs 17.1 ± 4.8; V2 15.4 ± 2.8 vs 17.0 ± 2.1; Vss 36.1 ± 8.0 vs 34.1 ± 4.9; CL12 68.4 ± 7.3 vs 70.4 ± 6.8; AUC 9.1 ± 4.0 vs 6.0 ± 2.1 mg h/L (p=0.029); terminal t½ 0.268 ± 0.116 vs 0.128 ± 0.043 h (p=0.001).
- 450 mg/m2, deficient n=19 vs control n=15: Vmax 900 ± 194 vs 1370 ± 267 mg/h; Km 5.01 ± 0.32 vs 4.58 ± 0.25; V1 21.4 ± 8.3 vs 16.4 ± 6.8; V2 16.2 ± 2.3 vs 15.8 ± 2.2; Vss 37.6 ± 9.0 vs 32.2 ± 8.1; CL12 65.9 ± 4.6 vs 63.6 ± 9.0; AUC 17.7 ± 5.4 vs 13.4 ± 3.9; t½ 0.306 ± 0.103 vs 0.181 ± 0.041 h.
- Text: mean Vmax 46% and 34% lower in deficient patients at 300 and 450 mg/m2 (derived from Table III: 942/1749 and 900/1370); abstract says 40% lower overall (population fit, 794 vs 1512 = 47% lower, derived). AUC 1.5- and 1.3-fold higher, t½ 2.1- and 1.7-fold longer than controls.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD model. Exposure-toxicity: AUC thresholds for severe toxicity discussed from other work (Discussion, p.10), none estimated here. Clinical: all 7 genotyped-before-treatment deficient patients given standard 5FU regimens had grade 3-4 toxicity (1 lethal); 13 given reduced doses had no grade 4 (4 had grade 3); those with grade <=2 toxicity averaged 61 ± 16% of the normal dose (n=10). Authors note this fits the 50% dose reduction in pharmacogenetic guidelines. Not an opioid; no MEAC/ke0.

Classification (Table IV, 300 mg/m2): Vmax from 60-min sample, ROC AUC 0.973, cut-off 1282 mg/h, sens 96%, spec 88%, PPV 96%, NPV 88%; t½ cut-off 0.176 h.

## Covariates
DPD status (c.1905+1G>A heterozygosity) on Vmax; no weight/BSA/age/sex equations. Dose-dependent Km estimate (4-14% higher at 450 mg/m2) the authors attribute to saturation.

## Caveats
Retrospective TDM data, deficient group has only one DPYD variant, no weight/BSA reported, individual Bayesian estimates (shrinkage), sampling stops at 120 min, 5FU undetectable at 60 min in several controls (Vmax from that sample unreliable in controls), doses per m2 so Vmax is not normalised to size.

Not cited, so nothing to verify. Independent comparison with the library's 5-FU models (R/drugs_fluorouracilBriki.R, drugs_fluorouracilTan.R), both Michaelis-Menten models in mg/min or mg/h:
- Briki (Table S3 model): Vmax 411 mg/h, Km 1.89 mg/L, V1 9.58 L, V2 13.6 L, Q 112 L/h, plus parallel linear CL 59.2 L/h (file header, lines 44-45). This paper's control Vmax (1370-1749 mg/h), Km (4.4-4.6 mg/L), V1 (about 16-17 L) and CL12 (64-70 L/h) differ from it; the structure also differs (this paper has no linear clearance). Direction: this paper's Vmax in controls is 3-4x Briki's. Not a transcription issue; different cohorts and structure. The Briki header says DPD deficiency is not represented (line 78) and a patient with DPD deficiency clears 5-FU far more slowly; this paper quantifies that for c.1905+1G>A heterozygotes as Vmax about 0.54-0.66x of controls (derived: 942/1749, 900/1370), plus longer t½ (2.1x and 1.7x).
- Tan (drugs_fluorouracilTan.R lines 32): Vmax 1900 mg/h x (BSA/1.94)^1.14, Km 8.04 mg/L; the Vmax is nearer this paper's control values than Briki's, Km higher than here.
