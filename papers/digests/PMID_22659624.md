# Zhang 2012: population PK-PD (ACT) of bivalirudin in healthy Chinese volunteers

## Study
48 healthy Han Chinese subjects randomised: 36 to bivalirudin, 12 to placebo (placebo not used in the models; p. 2). Four groups of 9 (p. 2):
- 0.5 mg/kg IV bolus;
- 0.75 mg/kg IV bolus;
- 1.05 mg/kg IV bolus;
- 0.75 mg/kg bolus followed by 1.75 mg/kg/h for 4 h.

Table 1 (p. 4): mean age 33.0-34.2 y (range 29-37), weight 55.4-61.6 kg (50-78), RBC 4.3-4.5 x10^12/L, serum creatinine 56.7-73.8 umol/L. All had GFR > 90 mL/min (p. 7). Intensive sampling. Plasma bivalirudin by LC-MS/MS. ACT on a Hemochron Response (p. 2); details are in an earlier paper (ref 13). 423 concentrations (p. 3). NONMEM 7.2, FOCE-I. Bootstrap (1000) and pcVPC validation. Sequential IPP approach for the PK-PD (p. 3).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: ACT (Hemochron Response, kaolin cuvette) (p. 2, p. 7). The model is a direct-effect Emax with no effect compartment and no Hill coefficient. Plasma (central) concentration drives it: E = E0 + [(Emax - E0) x C] / (C + EC50) (p. 3), with Emax 318 s, E0 134 s and EC50 2.44 mg/L. So **318 s is the maximum ACT; the maximal increment over baseline is 184 s** (derived). RBC count raises EC50 (OFV -22.008); the authors suggest this reflects dilution of clotting factors (p. 7). No therapeutic ACT target or concentration range is proposed. The 0.23-1.73 bootstrap interval for θRBC barely includes its 1.70 estimate (Table 3).

## Covariates
None on PK. Doses were per kg, which the authors say may have masked a weight effect (p. 7). RBC on EC50 only.

## Caveats
- 36 young healthy volunteers: age 29-37 (SD about 2 y), weight 50-78 kg, all with GFR > 90 mL/min (p. 7-8). The authors warn against extrapolating to the elderly or to patients (p. 8).
- No renal covariate could be tested. The paper cites Robson (refs 19-20) for CL unchanged in mild and reduced 45% and 68% in moderate and severe renal impairment (p. 7). The 2019 Angiomax label gives only 21% for moderate and severe (see NJ_label-angiomax_020873_2019-07-10).
- One outlier subject in the infusion group was unexplained (p. 7).
- The values in the abstract are those of Table 2.
