# Joosten 2015: closed-loop goal-directed fluid therapy with Nexfin

## Study
Single-centre (UC Irvine) prospective feasibility pilot, n=13 ASA II-III adults, elective moderate-risk surgery >2 h (colorectal 7, hip arthroplasty 3, gynaecologic 3), Feb-May 2014. Six male, seven female. Median [IQR] age 56 [46-62] y, height 165 [161-179] cm, weight 73 [63-91] kg, BMI 25 [24-30]; median case length 275 [206-341] min (p. 888, Results).
Anaesthesia: fentanyl 2 (unit garbled in the text layer, presumably ug/kg, not confirmed on an image), propofol 2 mg/kg, rocuronium 0.6 mg/kg; sevoflurane 0.8-1.2 MAC with fentanyl boluses of 1 (unit likewise garbled) per kg every 30-45 min (p. 887). Postoperative analgesia by epidural or IV morphine.
Closed-loop fluid system: baseline crystalloid 3 mL/kg/h; controller gives 100 mL colloid boluses over 6 min (6% HES 130/0.4 or 5% albumin) guided by non-invasive Nexfin cardiac output/SV/PPV; setpoint a 15% SV rise per 500 mL (adjustable 7.5-23%), scaled to about 3% per 100 mL (p. 888, 890).
Compliance defined as >85% of case time with PPV <13% or mean cardiac index >2.5 L/min/m2.

## Model and parameters
No pharmacokinetic model. Results: 74 boluses of 100 mL in 13 cases (1-20 per case); 12 patients got albumin, one HES; mean cardiac index 2.9 L/min/m2; mean 96.3% (range 84.76-100%) of time preload-independent; all 13 compliant; median hospital stay 5 [3-6] d (p. 888-889, Tables 1-2).
