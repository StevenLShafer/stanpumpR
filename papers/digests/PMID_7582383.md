# de Groot 1995: oral and i.v. methylergometrine in men and women

## Study
Randomised crossover, 6 healthy men and 6 healthy non-pregnant women, one i.v. and one oral session at least 2 weeks apart, both after a standard breakfast. Age 35.7 +- 13 yr (men), 41.2 +- 5 (women); weight 75 +- 4 and 61 +- 7 kg; height 181 +- 8 and 167 +- 7 cm (text, printed p.330). Oral: methylergometrine maleate tablet (Methergin) 0.125 mg, stated as 0.095 mg base (but see open question). I.v.: 0.200 mg maleate = 0.152 mg base over 1 min, line flushed with saline. Plasma sampled 0, 10, 20, 30, 60, 90, 180, 270, 360, 450, 540 min, plus 3 and 5 min after i.v. Assay: HPLC with fluorescence (ex 315, em 430 nm), quantitation limit 150 pg/mL in plasma, intra-day 3%, inter-day 5%. Plasma (heparinised), not whole blood; total, not free. Analysis by MW/Pharm (Mediware): 2-compartment model for i.v., 1-compartment with lag time for oral. F = AUCoral x dose_iv / (AUCiv x dose_oral).

Individual examples (Fig. 1): male #6 F 1.36, lag 30 min; female #3 Cmax 1.28 ng/mL at 38 min, lag 7 min, F 0.73. The i.v. compartment rate constants are not tabulated; only half-lives.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No effect measured (uterine tone not assessed); no ke0, EC50, MEAC or potency ratio. Oxytocic effect is only background. Side effects: 3 men "weird" sensation and 3 women dizzy after i.v.; none after oral. (Not an opioid: audit none.)

## Covariates
Sex compared: i.v. parameters not significantly different; oral lag time, Vss and t1/2beta larger in men, and men weigh more (p 0.0010). No equations.

## Caveats
n = 6 per sex, non-pregnant, fed state, very large oral F variability (CV 50% men, 28.5% women); oral F computed assuming equal CL; oral Table 2 header dose misprint; assay total plasma only; the i.v. maleate injection contained 93.3 +- 6.5% of the claimed content (n = 20), which affects the i.v. dose actually given and so AUC-based F and CL. The authors note postpartum absorption was delayed in a prior study (Allonen 1978), so these volunteer values may not apply postpartum.
