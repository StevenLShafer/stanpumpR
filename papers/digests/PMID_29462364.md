# Coe 2018: absolute bioavailability of oral cocaine

## Study
- Within-subject, three single-dose sessions at least 48 h apart, closed inpatient unit, after a 1-week washout
  (p. 286, PDF p. 2).
- 14 healthy cocaine users completed (6 men, 8 women; 15 enrolled, one discharged for poor venous access). Mean
  age 33.9 y (25-42); mean weight men 84.8 kg (71.1-99.4), women 69.9 kg (50.9-99.2) (p. 287, PDF p. 3; Table I).
  13 of 14 African-American. Pooled mean weight about 76.3 kg (derived: (6 x 84.8 + 8 x 69.9)/14).
- Doses: cocaine 40 mg IV (1 mL over 1 min), 100 mg and 200 mg oral in double-encapsulated capsules, light
  breakfast 2 h before. The paper names cocaine hydrochloride (Mallinckrodt) only as an analytical standard; it
  does not say whether the doses are salt or base mass.
- Sampling: IV -30 min to 24 h (17 times); oral -30 min to 24 h (16 times). Heparinised tubes with NaF and acetic
  acid, plasma frozen at -30 C.
- Assay: GC-MS (SIM) for cocaine, BZE, EME, BNE, NCOC, m-/p-HOCOC, m-/p-HOBZE; calibration 1.25-1,000 ng/mL, LOD
  about 1 ng/mL, controls within 20%. Matrix: venous plasma, total concentrations.
- Analysis: non-compartmental (WinNonlin). F = (AUCoral/AUCiv)(Doseiv/Doseoral); CL (labelled CL/F for oral) =
  Dose/AUC(0-inf) per kg; Vd = CL/kel per kg (p. 287 formulae, PDF p. 3). Two-factor (sex x dose) ANOVA, Tukey.

## Model and parameters
Non-compartmental summaries, mean (SEM), n = 14, from Table II (p. 289, PDF p. 5, confirmed on the rendered page).
For the oral rows CL and Vd are apparent (CL/F, Vd/F).

Bioavailability (abstract and p. 287, PDF pp. 1 and 3, mean +/- SEM): **0.32 +/- 0.04 at 100 mg, 0.45 +/- 0.06 at
200 mg**; dose effect F(1,12) = 11.37, P = 0.006. Women 34.3 +/- 6.2 and 47.9 +/- 8.9 %, men 29.9 +/- 3.8 and
40.1 +/- 6.0 % (not significant). Individual range 0.15-0.93 (p. 289). The Discussion restates the means as
32.4 +/- 3.8 and 44.6 +/- 5.6 (%), slightly different from the Results. 200/100 mg AUC ratio 2.86 (0.94-4.84).

Metabolites (Table II): BZE t1/2 about 6 h, EME 3.2-5.4 h; BZE:cocaine AUC ratio 10.1 (IV), 27.1 and 20.1 (oral
100/200 mg); EME:cocaine 0.8 (IV), 5.2 and 4.3 (oral). NCOC Cmax 4.1 and 23.1 ng/mL after 100 and 200 mg oral.
IV Tmax: 6.3 min in the seven highest-Cmax subjects, 14.3 min in the other seven.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None measured or reported: no ke0, no tPeak of effect, no EC50 or threshold. Not an opioid (no MEAC or potency
ratio applies).

## Covariates
Sex: Cmax higher in women across doses (F(1,12) = 4.81, P = 0.049), no significant pairwise difference; weight-
adjusted Cmax differed by sex. Weight was used only to normalise CL and Vd. No covariate model.

## Caveats
- Small n, experienced users, NCA only; no compartmental or absorption model, so no ka is identified.
- Table II's Vd is not consistent with its own CL and t1/2: CL x t1/2 / ln 2 gives about 3.1 L/kg for IV
  (derived: 0.0327 x 66 / 0.693) against 1.3 reported, and about 12 and 10 L/kg for the oral rows against 4.2 and
  2.9. Means of individual values cannot explain a 2.4-3.4-fold gap; the reported Vd should not be used.
- CL from the mean IV AUC is about 28.5 mL/min/kg at 76.3 kg (derived: 40 mg / 306 ng.h/mL), close to the
  tabulated 32.7.
- Salt or base mass of the doses is not stated; F is unaffected (same material by both routes).
- Results and Discussion give slightly different bioavailability means (0.32/0.45 vs 32.4/44.6 %).
