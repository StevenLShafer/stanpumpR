# Hull 1978: pharmacodynamic (biophase) model for pancuronium

## Study
- 5 healthy adult males (inguinal hernia repair), age 27-65 y, 53.6-82.0 kg (Table I).
- Papaveretum 10 mg + hyoscine 0.2 mg premed; induction fentanyl 100 ug + Althesin 2.5-4.5 mL; N2O/O2; fentanyl 25 ug increments. Pancuronium 4 mg iv once, absolute dose.
- Effect: isometric adductor pollicis twitch (ulnar nerve, supramaximal 100 us pulses at 0.08 Hz), % twitch depression.
- Venous (antecubital) plasma sampled frequently, then to 6 h; fluorimetric "rose bengal" assay. Concentrations corrected for assumed 80% protein binding (free = 20% of total); results are free pancuronium in nmol/L (ln nmol/L in plots).
- Two-compartment bi-exponential fit per patient (non-linear regression), then a third, tiny biophase compartment (1 mL element) attached to compartment 1 with first-order rate constants; k31 chosen so the biophase concentration is equal at the 70% twitch-depression times in onset and recovery (collapses hysteresis).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: % adductor pollicis twitch depression (neuromuscular block). Not an opioid; no MEAC.
- Biophase (ke0-equivalent) rate constant k31 (h^-1), Table II (page image, PDF p7, printed p1119): A 13.09, B 18.97, C 10.80, D 8.46, E 12.36; mean 12.74 (SEM 1.96). (Derived: 12.74/60 = 0.212 /min; t1/2 = ln2/12.74 h = 3.3 min.) The paper reports no t1/2ke0 or tPeak itself.
- Peak twitch depression was 93% at 7.5 min in patient A (text, p1115). Recovery time to 50% twitch: 58, 26, 56.5, 82, 47 min; mean 53.9 (SEM 10.12).
- Log-concentration vs response is linear between 20 and 80% depression; regression coefficient (% per ln nmol/L): A 119.2, B 191.4, C 149.8, D 106.5, E 123.1; mean 138.0 (SEM 33.8). Predicted from guinea-pig KB 25.1 nmol/L and cat margin of safety: 77.82 (57.75-117.85).
- Biophase ln concentration for 50% recovery: A 4.535, B 4.625, C 4.133, D 4.306, E 4.050; mean 4.33 (SEM 0.25) vs predicted 4.746 (4.609-4.897) (Table II; ln nmol/L, free drug). Equals the prediction if binding were 70% rather than 80% (text, p1119). Not a population EC50 in absolute units (derived: e^4.33 = about 76 nmol/L free).
- Hysteresis: plasma and compartment-2 log concentrations showed gross hysteresis against twitch depression (r 0.13 and 0.39 for patient A, p1115).
- Renal-failure simulation (text layer, Table III, printed p1120): for 4 mg, peak 94.7% at 9 min (normal) vs 83.9% at 12.5 min; time to 50% recovery 58 vs 51 min; for 6 mg 122 vs 322 min. Simulated by changing patient A's rate constants per McLeod 1976, not measured.

## Covariates
None estimated (n = 5). Renal failure simulated only: kEL -82.3%, V1 +57.7%, V2 +23.9%, k12 -48.7% (as quoted from McLeod 1976, p1120; the sign/letter assignment in the text layer is garbled, not verified on an image).

## Caveats
- n = 5 healthy males, single 4 mg dose; venous sampling (early samples misleadingly low); 80% protein binding assumed (the authors say 20% or 70% cannot be excluded); twitch transducer linearity; biophase volume arbitrary (1 mL) so only concentration is meaningful.
- Fits and plots are of free drug, so concentrations are not comparable with total-concentration assays.
