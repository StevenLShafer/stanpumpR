# Meyer 2004: striatal SERT occupancy vs dose and plasma level for five SSRIs ([11C]DASB PET)

## Study
77 completers (of 84), age 20-50 (mean 35 ± 9), 33 F / 44 M. Three dose groups: mostly healthy subjects on at most
half the minimum therapeutic dose (n = 37; citalopram 1-10 mg); major depression on usual doses (n = 29;
citalopram 20-40 mg); depression with anxiety/OCD/treatment resistance on higher doses (n = 16; citalopram 40-60
mg). The group sizes count treatment episodes, not people: 37 + 29 + 16 = 82 (derived) because five subjects
completed group 2, responded poorly, and re-enrolled in group 3, so they are in both (Methods, PDF p. 2, printed
p. 827); 82 - 5 = 77 (derived). Two group-2 subjects with early side effects were moved to group 1 (35 of its 37
healthy). [11C]DASB PET before and after 4 weeks; Logan reference-tissue binding potential, cerebellum reference;
occupancy = % reduction in striatal BP. Serum drug level drawn 6-13 h after the last dose, same day as scan 2;
HPLC-fluorescence (Guy's and St Thomas'). Hyperbolic fit f(x) = a·x/(b + x). Test-retest in 6 healthy subjects
(mean absolute difference 8%).

Occupancy at minimum therapeutic dose (Table 2, striatum): citalopram 20-40 mg 81.4 ± 7.2% (n = 7); fluoxetine 20
mg 76.2%; sertraline 50-100 mg 85.0%; paroxetine 20 mg 84.5%; venlafaxine XR 75 mg 83.7%.
(Derived: with a = 96%, 50% occupancy is at 50/46 × b, so the half-maximal concentration b ≈ 11.7 × 46/50 ≈ 10.8
µg/L.)

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: striatal SERT occupancy by PET (a biomarker), not clinical response. No relation between occupancy and
Hamilton change in 36 depressed subjects. ~80% occupancy at minimum effective doses. No ke0 (single time window 6-13
h after dose at steady state). Not an opioid.

## Covariates
None analysed (diagnostic group had no effect on baseline BP).

## Caveats
Single post-dose window; occupancy may include transporter down-regulation; metabolite contributions not
separable; healthy and patient groups pooled; EC50 is defined at 50% absolute occupancy from a fit with Emax < 100%;
the matrix is serum (Methods) though Table 1 says plasma; enantiomers not separated.
