# Schoemaker 2017: brivaracetam population PK in children

## Study
Phase 2a open-label, single-arm, fixed three-step up-titration (N01263, NCT00422422): 96 children with epilepsy aged
1 month to under 16 y (29, 26, 24, 17 patients aged 1 mo-2 y, 2-6, 6-12, 12-16), on 1-3 concomitant AEDs
(levetiracetam excluded). Brivaracetam oral solution, weekly steps of about 0.4/0.8/1.6 mg/kg twice daily (8 y and
over) or 0.5/1.0/2.0 mg/kg twice daily (under 8), capped at 25/50/100 mg twice daily at 50 kg and over. Two samples
on days 7, 14, 21 in one of three time brackets plus one optional: 600 concentrations (PDF p2-3). NONMEM 7.2 FOCE-I,
SCM with PsN 3.4.2, 1000 bootstraps (23 failed). Assay in the supplement.

## Model and parameters
One compartment, first-order absorption and elimination; allometric scaling on lean body weight (LBW, Janmahasatian
via Green and Duffull) with fixed exponents 0.75 (CL) and 1 (V), normalised to a 50 kg LBW adult; WT instead of LBW
raised the OFV by 11.42. Table 1 (PDF p4, rendered):

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PK/PD model. Dosing simulations (NHANES DXA demographics, 11,087 children) compare paediatric Css with the 90%
band for adults on 100 mg twice daily; 2.0 mg/kg twice daily (maximum 100 mg twice daily) puts most children in the
adult range; children under 1 year and under 10 kg had lower exposure, attributed to more frequent phenobarbital
(Figs 1-2, PDF p5-6). Metabolites (no inducers, n = 71): hydroxy 0.36, acid 0.12, hydroxyacid 0.035 mg-eq/L vs
parent 2.10 mg/L (PDF p5).

## Covariates
LBW (fixed allometry); PB/primidone, CBZ and VPA as categorical effects on CL/F. No effect of age, PCA, sex, race,
ethnicity, eGFR (Schwartz bedside), CYP3A or CYP2C19 inhibitors (only 2 and 7 patients). Phenytoin not tested
(one patient).

## Caveats
- V/F and Ka are poorly informed (shrinkage 45.6% and 73.4%; sparse sampling); Ka's bootstrap interval is skewed.
- Covariate effects are associations; the VPA effect is not seen in the much larger adult analysis (PDF p6).
- Apparent (oral) parameters only, from an oral solution.
