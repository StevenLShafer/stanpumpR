# Landersdorfer 2012 (PD companion): vildagliptin effects on active GLP-1, glucose and insulin

## Study
- Randomised, placebo-controlled, double-blind, four-way crossover: 13 adults with type 2 diabetes (7 F, 6 M;
  weight 91 (65-116) kg, height 166 (148-183) cm, age 53.5 (37-64) y); vildagliptin 10, 25, 100 mg or placebo
  orally twice daily for 28 days; 12 completed all four periods (p2 / 374; Results p5 / 377). Data published by NCA
  in He et al. 2007 (Clin Pharmacokinet 46:577-588).
- Day 28 sampling: active GLP-1 at 19 times over 16 h; glucose and insulin at 26 times over 14 h; standard meals
  (55% carbohydrate, 25% fat, 20% protein) about 30 min after the doses. Assays: active GLP-1 ELISA (Linco), LLQ
  2 pmol/L; glucose (Hitachi 747); insulin electrochemiluminescence, LLQ 0.2 mIU/mL.
- Joint population PK/PD in parallelised S-ADAPT 1.56, MC-PEM, Beal M3; bootstrap (200) SEs. Vildagliptin PK and
  DPP-4 binding (TMDD) from the companion paper (Br J Clin Pharmacol 2012;73:391-401) were re-estimated
  simultaneously.

## Model and parameters
Indirect-response system (equations pp3-4 / 375-376, Fig. 1):
- Meals: zero-order input (75 g nominal glucose × meal factor F, over each recorded eating time) into a gut
  compartment, first-order absorption kaB/kaL/kaD/kaS.
- Active GLP-1: dCglp/dt = kin_glp·(1 + S1·GlcGut) − [kout_glp_lin + (RmaxC − DRC)·cf2]·Cglp, with free plasma
  DPP-4 (RmaxC − DRC) from the PK/TMDD model; kin_glp = Bglp·(kout_glp_lin + RmaxC·cf2).
- Glucose: dCglc/dt = kin_glc + absorbed glucose/Vglc − kout_glc·[1 + STins·(Cins − Bins)]·Cglc;
  STins = S5·[1 + S4·(Cglp − Bglp)].
- Insulin: dCins/dt = kin_ins·[1 + STglc·(Cglc − Bglc)] − kout_ins·Cins; STglc = S3·[1 + S2·(Cglp − Bglp)].

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End points: active GLP-1 (pmol/L), plasma glucose (mg/dL), insulin (mIU/L) over a day of meals at steady state.
Drug effect is entirely through free DPP-4: vildagliptin binding reduces GLP-1 elimination; GLP-1 then raises
pancreatic glucose sensitivity (S2) and peripheral insulin sensitivity (S4); the S4 effect was slightly larger in
simulation. Complete DPP-4 inhibition gives about a 2.5-fold longer active GLP-1 half-life (abstract; Results
p5). NCA: glucose AUC below placebo in almost all subjects at vildagliptin AUC ≥ 500 ng·h/mL; insulin AUC showed
no clear trend. No ke0, no EC50 on vildagliptin concentration.

## Covariates
None tested (13 patients).

## Caveats
- 13 subjects; very high BSV and imprecise S2, S3, S5 (BSV 135-188%, SE 41-55%).
- GIP and glucagon not modelled; one gut compartment for stomach and intestine; meal glucose amounts are
  identified jointly from glucose and GLP-1 rises.
- S1 unit printed g^-1 in Table 1 and mg^-1 in Methods (source inconsistency; not used by stanpumpR).
- NONMEM VI could not estimate the system; S-ADAPT only.
