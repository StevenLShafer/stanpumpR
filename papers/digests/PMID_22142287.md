# Honda 2011: vancomycin MIC, MBC and tolerance in MRSA bacteraemia

## Study
- **Design:** prospective cohort, July 2005 to July 2007, Barnes-Jewish Hospital, St Louis (1252 beds, tertiary).
- **Who:** 163 adults with MRSA bacteraemia (at least one positive blood culture) who received vancomycin as
  initial therapy. Patients with MRSA bacteraemia in the previous 3 months or MRSA endocarditis in the previous
  12 months were excluded.
- **End point:** 28-day all-cause mortality, by Cox regression.
- **Microbiology on the stored isolates:**
  - MIC by MicroScan Pos MIC Panel Type 26 microbroth dilution, range 0.25-16 mg/L, read manually at 24 h by one
    investigator.
  - MBC by the Sader method: 99.9% kill of the initial inoculum after 24 h, subcultured from the MIC well and every
    clear well after it. MBCs above 16 were confirmed by repeat testing.
  - Tolerance: MBC:MIC >= 32, or 16 with an MBC >= 32 mg/L.
  - VISA: MIC 4-8 mg/L.

## Model and parameters
There is no PK model; the paper's numbers are susceptibility data.

**MICs (p.3):**
- <= 1 mg/L in 51 isolates (31.3%); >= 2 mg/L in 112 (68.7%); MIC50 2 mg/L.
- 10 isolates (6.1%) at 4 mg/L (VISA); 1 at 0.5 mg/L; none above 4.

**MBCs (p.3):** range 1 to >= 32 mg/L; MBC50 2 mg/L and MBC90 8 mg/L. Table 1 (p.3, image-confirmed), isolates by
MIC (rows) and MBC (columns), in mg/L:

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| <1 | 1 (0.6) | - | 1 | - | - | - | - |
| 1 | 50 (30.6) | 37 | 12 | - | - | 1 | - |

Derived from Table 1:
- MBC column totals are 37, 88, 21, 7, 2 and 8 (sum 163).
- The cumulative share reaches 76.7% at 2 mg/L and 93.9% at 8 mg/L, which confirms MBC50 2 and MBC90 8.
- MIC equals MBC in 37 + 75 + 7 = 119 of 163 isolates (73%). The text (p.6) says "over 70%".

**Other results:**
- Tolerance in 7 of 162 isolates (4.3%).
- MIC and MBC correlated, r = 0.734 (p < 0.001).

**Outcomes:**
- 28-day mortality 21.5% (35/163).
- MIC >= 2: aHR 1.57 (0.73-3.37), not significant.
- Tolerance: crude HR 0.62 (0.08-4.50).
- Initial trough < 15 mg/L: crude HR 0.77 (0.36-1.63), n = 151.
- Independent predictors: age (aHR 1.03 per year), cirrhosis (3.01) and ICU admission within 48 h (5.99).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- **Measures:** in vitro MIC and MBC (24 h, 99.9% kill) and the clinical end point of 28-day mortality.
- **Discussion:** AUC:MIC >= 400 is said not to be achievable at an MIC >= 2 with conventional dosing in normal
  renal function (refs 23-24).
- **Clinical conclusion:** neither reduced susceptibility nor tolerance predicted mortality.
- No PK, no free fraction, not an opioid.

## Covariates
Not applicable to PK. The mortality covariates are listed above.

## Caveats
- **MRSA only;** no MSSA isolates.
- **Method:** a commercial MicroScan panel, not CLSI reference broth microdilution. MICs ran high: 62.6% at 2 mg/L,
  against 16.2% in national surveillance, which the authors note.
- **Single centre.**
- **Limited workup:** TEE in only 17.2%; trough timing not standardised.
- **Power:** not powered for small mortality differences.

## Verification against stanpumpR
- **MBC50 2, MBC90 8 mg/L, 163 MRSA bacteraemia isolates** (antibioticMbc.R:350; drugs_vancomycin.R:53;
  vancomycin.md:15): p.3 and Table 1, confirmed by the derived cumulative distribution. **matches**.
- **"More than 70% of isolates had an MBC equal to the MIC"** (antibioticMbc.R:345): p.6; derived 73%. **matches**.
- **Tolerant minority of 4 to 27%:** Honda supplies the lower figure (4.3%). The 27% is from another source.
  **matches** in part.
- **Organism and MIC source:**
  - The help page (vancomycin.md:15) and drugs_vancomycin.R:53 say *S. aureus*. Honda's isolates are all MRSA.
    antibioticMbc.R:346 says so ("MBCs are from MRSA; MSSA's are lower"); the help page does not.
  - The row pairs Honda's MBCs with a modal MIC of 1 from Diekema. In Honda's own data the MIC50 is 2 (MicroScan)
    and equals the MBC50. The MBC50/MIC50 ratio of 2 implied by the row is therefore an artefact of combining two
    sources, not a finding of Honda. This does not change the band, which uses the MBCs only.
- **Band 2.9-11 mcg/mL total:** that is the MBCs divided by the free fraction, which is not from this paper.
  **cannot verify** here.
