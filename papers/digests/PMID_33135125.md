# Jones 2021: population PK of monthly SC buprenorphine (BUP-XR, SUBLOCADE)

## Study
- Three Indivior studies (Table 1): Study 1 phase IIa MAD (n 89 + SL-only, 103 in PK; SL tablets 8-24 mg/day for 13 days then 4-6 SC injections, 50-300 mg; rich sampling); Study 2 phase III double-blind (504 randomised; 404 in PK; SL buprenorphine/naloxone film run-in, then 300/100 mg or 300/300 mg monthly ×6); Study 3 open-label long-term (287 in PK; up to 12 injections).
- 570 unique subjects with OUD, 19,686 plasma concentrations; LC-MS/MS 0.050-25.0 ng/mL; BLQ < 0.5% excluded.
- Demographics (Table 2, total): age 38.8 (11.5) y, range 19-64; weight 76.5 (15.5) kg, 46.1-132.0; BMI 25.4 (4.2); 67.9% male; 69.5% white, 28.2% Black.
- NONMEM 7.3.0, SAEM + importance sampling; PsN 4.4.0. SL and SC data fitted jointly to resolve flip-flop kinetics. **No intravenous data.**

## Model and parameters
Two-compartment disposition; SL first-order absorption (k14) with bioavailability F1 relative to BUP-XR; SC dual absorption: fast first-order (k24, fraction F2) and slow pathway via one transit compartment (k36, k64). ## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- No PD model; no ke0. Target concentrations of **2-3 ng/mL** (plasma, total buprenorphine) taken from Greenwald's PET work (refs 7, 12, 16, 17): withdrawal controlled at ≥ 50% mu-receptor occupancy, **about 1 ng/mL**; blockade of an exogenous agonist's subjective effects at > 70-80% occupancy, **2-3 ng/mL** (p. 528).
- Steady state (Table 4, model): Cavg 3.00 ng/mL (300/100 mg) and 6.6 (300/300); Cmin 2.6 and 5.4; Cmax 4.9 and 10.1 ng/mL. SL 24 mg/day tablet Cmax 8.3 ng/mL (label).
- **No MEAC** (not an analgesia study); no potency ratio.

## Covariates
BMI on CL/F and k24; weight allometric (fixed exponents). Sex on k36 dropped. Age, race, CYP3A4/2C8, UGT1A1/2B7 genotypes, AST/ALT, creatinine clearance not retained.

## Caveats
- All parameters are apparent relative to BUP-XR bioavailability (no IV arm); peripheral parameters and SL parameters fixed from Study 1; SL dosing between visits imputed in phase III.
- Industry authors (Indivior).
