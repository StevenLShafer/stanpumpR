# Takenaka 2018: population PK/PD of adjunctive perampanel in partial-onset seizures

## Study
- Pooled sparse steady-state data from one phase II (Study 235, adolescents) and four phase III randomized,
  placebo-controlled trials of adjunctive perampanel (Studies 304, 305, 306, 335 Asia-Pacific) in refractory
  partial-onset seizures, age >= 12 y (PDF p1-2).
- 19-week double-blind phase: 6-week titration by 2 mg/week, 13-week maintenance; once-daily oral perampanel
  <= 12 mg (phase II: 8 mg, up to 12 mg). Fed/fasted status unknown; dosing time varied over the day.
- PK sampling at maintenance visits 6, 7, 8, usually two samples 1-2 h apart (one in Study 335); plasma,
  LC-MS/MS (ref 11).
- PK dataset: 6066 concentrations, 1318 subjects (adolescents: 927 concentrations, 210 subjects) (PDF p2).
  PK/PD dataset: 5068 observations, 1748 subjects (1240 perampanel, 508 placebo; Study 235 excluded).
- Table 1 (PDF p3), total PK population: mean age 32.7 (SD 13.6) y, weight 67.1 (17.9) kg, female 51.1%,
  Caucasian 48.9%, Asian (non-Japanese/Chinese) 20.2%, Chinese 15.0%, Japanese 13.1%; mean ALT 20.7 IU/L,
  CrCl 118 mL/min; concomitant carbamazepine 37.7%, oxcarbazepine 16.1%, phenytoin 9.3%.
- NONMEM 7.2 (PDx-POP 5.0), FOCE with interaction; VPC and bootstrap.

## Model and parameters
One-compartment disposition, first-order linear elimination, parameterized as CL/F and V/F (the authors note a
two-compartment model fits rich phase I data, but sparse phase III data support one compartment). V/F and
its variance fixed. Absorption rate constant not reported. Table 2, PDF p5 (printed p404), checked on the
rendered page:

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: log-transformed percent change from baseline in 28-day average seizure frequency; placebo effect
  plus a linear exposure effect on Cav,ss = (dose x 1000 / 24) / (CL/F), ng/mL (PDF p2, Methods 2.5).
- Slope: decrease of 0.502 on the log_e scale per 1 µg/mL increase in Cav,ss (PDF p5); between-subject
  variability of the slope 106% CV; shrinkage 70.9% on the drug effect. Placebo IIV 10.6% CV.
- Typical non-Asian male, no interacting AED, 8 mg/d: predicted Cav,ss 575.2 ng/mL; seizures from 10.7 to 5.8
  per 28 days (PDF p5, Figure 2).
- Responder (>= 50% reduction) probability: logistic, power function of Cav,ss; age and clobazam or lamotrigine
  significant; parameter values are in the supplement (Table S2), not the article.
- No ke0, no effect-site or time course of effect; no therapeutic concentration range proposed.

## Covariates
ALT (power, reference 17 IU/L), sex, Asian race (non-Japanese/Chinese), carbamazepine, oxcarbazepine or phenytoin,
topiramate or phenobarbital, all on CL/F only. Weight, age, CrCl, AST, Japanese and Chinese race, other AEDs were
not significant; CL/F independent of dose (PDF p6-7). The authors judge sex, Asian race, ALT and
topiramate/phenobarbital effects not clinically relevant (Figure 1 simulations).

## Caveats
- Sponsor (Eisai) analysis; all authors are Eisai employees.
- Sparse samples within 1-2 h, bedtime dosing and daytime visits: absorption and distribution are not
  identifiable, so V/F was fixed and ka was not estimated; the model describes steady-state level, not
  the curve's shape after a dose.
- Fed/fasted status unknown.
- PD slope shrinkage 70.9%; responder model parameters only in supplement (not read).
