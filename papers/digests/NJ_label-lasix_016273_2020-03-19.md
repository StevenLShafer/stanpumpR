## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Oral diuresis begins within 1 h, peaks in the first or second hour, lasts 6-8 h (p. 2). No therapeutic or toxic concentration (p. 9).

## Covariates
Age (renal clearance after IV lower at 60-70 y; descriptive).

## Caveats
Dosage (p. 9-10): edema 20-80 mg, repeat or +20-40 mg after 6-8 h, up to 600 mg/day; pediatric 2 mg/kg, +1-2 mg/kg after 6-8 h, <= 6 mg/kg; hypertension 80 mg/day as 40 mg twice daily, cut other antihypertensives by at least 50%. No pediatric PK section.
