# Kulo 2012 (J Biomed Biotechnol): paired paracetamol and ketorolac PK at delivery and post-partum

## Study
Two paired cohorts, each of 8 women at (semi)elective caesarean delivery under combined spinal-epidural anaesthesia
(hyperbaric bupivacaine 7.5 mg + sufentanil 2.5 ug), TAP blocks (ropivacaine 0.375%), restudied with the same dose:
- paracetamol 2 g IV over about 15-20 min after delivery, plasma at 1, 2, 4, 6 h; repeated 10-15 weeks post-partum
  (EudraCT 2010-020164-37);
- ketorolac tromethamine 30 mg IV bolus (stated equal to 20.345 mg ketorolac), plasma at 1, 2, 4, 6, 8 h, racemic HPLC-UV;
  repeated 17-23 weeks post-partum (EudraCT 2011-000367-27).
Non-compartmental: ke from at least 3 final points, AUC trapezoids plus extrapolation, CL = dose / AUC0-inf, Vd = CL/ke.
The paper does not say whether the ketorolac CL uses the 30 mg salt dose or the 20.345 mg free-acid dose. Wilcoxon
paired tests; medians and ranges. 6/8 and 3/8 women were breastfeeding at the post-partum study.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None measured.

## Covariates
State (delivery vs post-partum) only; BSA normalisation reported.

## Caveats
n = 8 per drug; sparse sampling (6 h for paracetamol); post-partum study times differ by drug (10-15 vs 17-23 weeks);
surgery and co-medication confound the delivery values; racemic ketorolac only.
