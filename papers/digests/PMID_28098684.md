# Loeb 2017: Closed-loop anesthesia, ready for prime time? (editorial)

## Study
Editorial accompanying two meta-analyses (Brogi 2017; Pasin 2017) of randomized trials of closed-loop versus manual control of sedation/anesthesia. Summarises 19 discrete randomized comparisons; Brogi 15 trials (484 closed-loop patients), Pasin 12 trials (619 closed-loop patients), about 744 patients after overlap (printed p. 381). Brogi: +17.4% of time within the target range (e.g. BIS 40-60). Pasin: propofol induction dose 0.37 mg/kg lower and recovery 1.67 min faster with closed loop (p. 381). Discusses the FDA position (Oct 2015 discussion paper and workshop) and hazards of automation. It remarks that the US lacks approved target-controlled infusion pumps and calls Stanpump a popular algorithm developed in the US (p. 381, ref 4 Struys 2000).
