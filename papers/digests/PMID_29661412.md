# Eleveld 2018: general-purpose propofol PK-PD (BIS) model

## Study
- Pooled analysis of 30 published/contributed studies (Table 1; mostly from Open-TCI). PK: 15 433 concentrations (11 530 arterial, 3 903 venous) from 1 033 individuals (672 M / 361 F), 27 weeks PMA to 88 y, 0.68-160 kg. Subgroups: 71 young children (<3 y), 128 children, 628 adults, 89 elderly (>=70 y), 121 high-BMI (>=30) (p947).
- PD: 28 639 BIS observations from 122 individuals (56 M / 66 F), 3-74 y, 15-141 kg; only 3 over 70 y; no adolescents 12-19 y; one paediatric BIS study (p947, p956).
- NONMEM 7.3, FOCE-I; sequential PK then PD; covariate entry needed AIC drop >= 20 plus better repeated two-fold cross-validated MdALE averaged across subgroups (p946). Missing PMA assumed age + 40 weeks; missing height (108 people) imputed from same-sex individuals of similar weight (p943, p947).
- Reference individual: 70 kg, 35 y, 170 cm male (p943).

## Model and parameters
Three-compartment mammillary; log-additive residual error. Printed equations (p948):
- V1arterial = Θ1 · fcentral(WGT)/fcentral(70), fcentral = WGT/(WGT+Θ12); V1venous = V1arterial · (1 + Θ17 · (1 − fcentral(WGT))).
- V2 = Θ2 · (WGT/70) · exp(Θ10 · (AGE − 35)).
- V3 = Θ3 · (FFM/FFMref) · fopiates(Θ13); fopiates(x) = 1 without, exp(x · AGE) with concomitant anaesthetic drugs.
- CL = (Θ4 male | Θ15 female) · (WGT/70)^0.75 · fCLmat/fCLmat,ref · fopiates(Θ11); fCLmat = PMA^Θ9/(PMA^Θ9 + Θ8^Θ9) (PMA in weeks).
- Q2arterial = Θ5 · (V2/V2ref)^0.75 · (1 + Θ16 · (1 − fQ3mat)); Q2venous = Q2arterial · Θ18.
- Q3 = Θ6 · (V3/V3ref)^0.75 · fQ3mat/fQ3mat,ref; fQ3mat = (AGE + 40 weeks)/(AGE + 40 weeks + Θ14).
- FFM by Al-Sallami 2015: male [0.88 + 0.12/(1 + (AGE/13.4)^−12.7)] · 9270·WGT/(6680 + 216·BMI); female [1.11 − 0.11/(1 + (AGE/7.1)^−1.1)] · 9270·WGT/(8780 + 244·BMI) (p946).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: BIS (all monitor versions pooled). Sigmoid Emax on Ce; effect-site linked by ke0 (p946, p948).
- Ce50 = Θ1 · exp(Θ7 · (AGE − 35)); ke0 = (Θ2 arterial | Θ8 venous) · (WGT/70)^−0.25; BIS delay (s) = 15 + exp(Θ6 · AGE); gamma Θ4 above Ce50, Θ9 below.

- tPeak not reported. (derived: with the no-opioid reference PK, V1 6.28, V2 25.5, V3 273 L, CL 1.79, Q2 1.83, Q3 1.11 L/min, ke0 0.146/min gives a bolus tPeak of about 2.9 min; computed in R by eigen-decomposition.)
- Targets: Ce50 for anaesthesia (expected BIS 47), Ce10 for MAC sedation (BIS 84) (p949). ke0 0.146 compared with Schnider's 0.456 /min (canonical univariate EEG parameter) and other BIS ke0s 0.17-0.19 /min; authors say ke0s are not comparable across PK models and arterial concentrations should be used for both PK and ke0 (p951-954).
- No MEAC (not an analgesic) and no potency ratio.

## Covariates
Weight (V1 sigmoid, V2 linear, CL and Q allometric 0.75, ke0 −0.25), age (V2; CL and V3 only with concomitant anaesthetic drugs; Ce50; BIS delay), PMA (CL maturation; 95% complete at about 5 months), age + 40 weeks (Q3 maturation; ~11 y to 90%), sex (CL), height via Al-Sallami FFM (V3), arterial vs venous sampling (V1, Q2, ke0), presence/absence of concomitant anaesthetic drugs (opioids or local anaesthesia; dose not modelled).

## Caveats
- Not prospectively validated; PD weakest in children and elderly, none in adolescents/young children (p956).
- Upward bias for very early samples (no front-end kinetics); under-prediction of very late samples (terminal phase mis-modelled) (p948, p956).
- Opioid effect is a presence/absence switch only. BIS-optimal ke0 may not suit haemodynamic or respiratory end points.
- Tables 2-3 in the text layer have offset CI and unit columns; values above realigned by row order (the code's THETA comments agree).
