# Winchell 2024: posaconazole IV and PFS population PK in neutropenic children

## Study
Population PK of posaconazole in the phase 1b study P097 (NCT02452034): 114 children aged 2-17 y analysed (48 aged 2 to <7, 66 aged 7-17; the abstract says 144 participants, the Results and Table 1 say N = 114 with 1,236 PK observations). Median age 8 (2-17) y, weight 28.6 (10.2-102) kg (Table 1, PDF p.3). Documented or expected neutropenia (leukaemia, MDS, aplastic anaemia, HSCT, etc.). Doses 3.5 (n 35), 4.5 (n 31) and 6 (n 48) mg/kg, IV twice daily on day 1 then IV once daily to day 10, then IV or oral powder for suspension (PFS) once daily to day 28; absolute maximum 300 mg. IV only n = 54, IV + PFS n = 60. Sampling days 6-10 (IV: pre-dose, end of infusion, 4, 6, 12, 24 h; PFS inpatients: 0, 2, 4, 6, 8, 24 h; outpatients 0, 2, 4 h). Assay: validated LC-MS/MS, plasma (total drug), LLOQ 5.00 ng/mL. 80 samples excluded. NONMEM 7.2, FOCE, additive error on log concentration.

## Model and parameters

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD model. Exposure targets (not an opioid, so no MEAC/ke0 audit): primary target average steady-state concentration Cavg >= 500 ng/mL in 90% of patients (the adult FDA quartile analysis associated Cavg >= 500 ng/mL with better clinical response); mean Cavg target 1,200 ng/mL with ~90% in 500 to <2,500 ng/mL (abstract says <2,000). Secondary: Cmin >= 500 ng/mL. No upper toxicity limit established; adult Cavg 2,304-9,523 ng/mL showed no relation to adverse events (Introduction, ref 12). All concentrations are total plasma posaconazole.

## Covariates
Age, weight, eGFR, sex and ethnicity tested stepwise (p < 0.01 forward, p < 0.001 backward): none significant beyond the fixed allometric weight terms; the base model was final. Food (no meal vs light/medium/heavy meal, within 2 h before to 1 h after dose) had no significant effect on PFS bioavailability (Fig. 1, PDF p.4).

## Caveats
Simulations: 1,000 virtual children per age group (2 to <7, 7-17 y) at 4.5, 6 and 7.5 mg/kg; 6 mg/kg met the Cavg >= 500 target in >90% of both groups by IV or PFS; the 7.5 mg/kg dose is extrapolated beyond the data and has no clinical safety data. Geometric mean Cavg of 1,200 ng/mL at 6 mg/kg was met in 2 to <7 y only with IV. The model tended to over-predict Cmin for PFS at 6 mg/kg. A 300 mg tablet in >40 kg adolescents (NHANES virtual population) met the target in 90%; tablet Cavg was ~20% below 300 mg PFS (PFS/tablet relative bioavailability 1.2). F1 and Vc show high shrinkage. Text typo "Cavg > 500 mg/mL" for ng/mL. Participant count differs between abstract (144) and Results (114).
