# Fragmin (dalteparin) US label, revised 10/2024

## Study
Not a study: the US prescribing information for dalteparin sodium (porcine-mucosa heparin depolymerised by nitrous acid; mean molecular weight 5,000; about 90% within 2,000-9,000), labelled in anti-Xa international units against the WHO First International LMWH Reference Standard; 2,500 units = 16 mg (Section 11, p. 14). The rendered first page (p. 1) confirms the product, "Revised: 10/2024", and recent changes adding pediatric VTE treatment (1.4, 2.4, 2.5). Routes: subcutaneous only; intramuscular use prohibited (2.7).

Dosing (Section 2, p. 3-6): unstable angina / non-Q-wave MI 120 units/kg (max 10,000 units) SC q12h with aspirin, 5-8 days; hip replacement 2,500 units 4-8 h after surgery or 2,500 units within 2 h before plus 2,500 units 4-8 h after, or 5,000 units the evening before, then 5,000 units once daily, 5-10 days (Table 2); abdominal surgery 2,500 units 1-2 h before surgery once daily, or 5,000 units (high risk); medical patients 5,000 units once daily; cancer VTE 200 units/kg once daily for month 1 (max 18,000 units), about 150 units/kg months 2-6; pediatric VTE starting doses 150 / 125 / 100 units/kg twice daily by age, titrated to an anti-Xa of 0.5-1 unit/mL drawn 4 h after the dose before the 4th dose; CrCl < 30 mL/min (cancer VTE) target anti-Xa 0.5-1.5 units/mL drawn 4-6 h after the dose after 3-4 doses.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point throughout is plasma anti-Xa activity (units/mL). 12.2: up to 10,000 units SC did not change platelet aggregation, fibrinolysis, PT, TT or APTT in healthy subjects; APTT is insensitive (5.4). Therapeutic anti-Xa targets are stated only for pediatric VTE (0.5-1 unit/mL at 4 h) and severe renal impairment (0.5-1.5 units/mL at 4-6 h). Protamine: 1 mg per 100 anti-Xa units, then 0.5 mg per 100 units if APTT remains prolonged; anti-Xa never fully neutralised (maximum about 60-75%) (Section 10, p. 14).

Neuraxial timing (5.1, p. 7): first postoperative 2,500 units 6-8 h after surgery, second dose no sooner than 24 h later; catheter placement or removal at least 12 h after 2,500 units once daily, 15 h after 5,000 units once daily, 24 h after 200 units/kg once daily or 120 units/kg twice daily; next dose at least 4 h after removal; CrCl < 30 mL/min: double the removal delays (24 h and 48 h). Anti-Xa remains detectable at these times.

## Covariates
Renal function (haemodialysis half-life 5.7 h; anti-Xa monitoring when CrCl < 30 mL/min); age in children (CL/F per kg falls with age, Table 12); weight-based dosing for treatment indications. Geriatric (8.5): low weight (< 45 kg) and reduced renal function flagged. Pregnancy (8.1): no PK given; benzyl alcohol vials to be avoided. Lactation (8.2): anti-Xa < 0.005-0.037 units/mL in milk in 11 of 15 women after 2,500 units post-cesarean.

## Caveats
- The adult PK is a set of summary statistics from small healthy-volunteer and haemodialysis studies; dose-dependent IV clearance and more-than-proportional SC AUC mean no single linear parameter set reproduces all of them.
- Anti-Xa activity is an assay of activity, not drug mass; the label states no assay or calibrator detail beyond the WHO standard.
- p. 24-49 not read (patient-facing and approval-stamped labeling pages).
