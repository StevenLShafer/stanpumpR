# Baumann 1976: distribution and clearance of radioiodinated vasopressin in man

## Study
- 10 healthy volunteers, 5 men and 5 women, aged 20-29 yr; mean weight 70.6 kg (SEM 4.0; Table I, PDF p4 / printed p1112).
  Supine 1 h, potassium iodide thyroid block, 20 mL/kg water load to suppress endogenous ADH (PDF p2 / p1110).
- Drug: synthetic arginine vasopressin (Sandoz) monoiodinated with 125I (chloramine T), specific activity 334-492
  mCi/mg; 2-7 uCi (9.5-95 ng) IV bolus, 90 min after hydration, with 131I-albumin for plasma volume (p1110).
  Individual doses: 95, 25.5, 19 (x4), 9.5 (x4) ng (Table I).
- Sampling: frequent venous samples for 60 min; voided urine at 10, 20, 30, 45, 60 min. Intact 125I-AVP separated by
  paper chromatography of plasma extracts at 6, 12, 16 and 20 min, interpolated at other times (p1110-1111).
- Assay: gamma counting of radioactivity (not bioassay, not immunoassay). The 2- and 4-min samples were discarded as
  pre-mixing (mixing time 4-6 min by 131I-albumin; p1111, p1114).
- Analysis: log-linear least squares on the intact fraction over 6-20 min per subject; Vd by back-extrapolation of the
  slope to time zero; MCR = Vd x k (p1111). No compartmental model or software.

## Model and parameters
Plasma decay of intact tracer was at least biexponential; a single exponential fitted 10-20 min (Fig. 3, p1111). The
reported numbers describe that 10-20 min phase only (a one-compartment, back-extrapolated description).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PK-PD model, no ke0 / tPeak. End point observed: urine flow and osmolality. Doses 9.5-25 ng had no detectable
antidiuresis in six subjects; the 95-ng subject's urine flow fell from 16 to 3.5 mL/min and Uosm rose 87 to 270
mosmol/kg within 10 min, peaking at 336 at 30 min (p1111). The authors cannot say whether the iodinated or residual
unlabelled hormone caused it (p1113). No pressor end point. Not an opioid; no MEAC or potency ratio.

## Covariates
None. Clearance and volume reported per kg; no covariate analysis.

## Caveats
- The measured species is 125I-monoiodo-AVP, not native hormone; the authors argue it was not profoundly altered
  (bioactivity in one subject, agreement of the 24-min half-life with radioimmunoassay data for Pitressin by Robertson,
  ref 6), but concede more studies are needed (p1114).
- Transient (bolus) conditions; only 10-20 min of decay used, so the early fast phase is excluded from the half-life.
- Physiological concentrations (2-28 pg/mL), well below pressor-infusion concentrations.
- Hydrated, healthy young adults; renal clearance from voided urine only.
- (derived: MCR 4.14 mL/min/kg x 70.6 kg = 292 mL/min ≈ 0.29 L/min.)
