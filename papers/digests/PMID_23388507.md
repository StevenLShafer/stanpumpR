# Bi 2013: remifentanil-sevoflurane response surfaces (circulatory response to laryngoscopy, circulatory depression)

## Study
- 65 adult Chinese ASA I non-smokers, 30 men/35 women, aged 20-50 yr, within 25% of ideal body weight, elective surgery, no premedication; 57 completed (8 excluded for ventilation difficulty). Data 2007-2009 (PDF p 2).
- Open-label randomized "slices" design: each patient assigned one of 13 fixed end-tidal sevoflurane levels (0.3, 0.5, 0.7, 0.9, 1.1, 1.3, 1.5, 1.7, 2.0, 2.3, 2.6, 3.0, 3.4 vol.%, n = 5 each), then remifentanil TCI stepped 0-10 ng/ml in 1 ng/ml steps, programmed with the Minto 1997 remifentanil PK. 164 paired responses (laryngoscopy), 142 (depression).
- Observation at pseudo-steady state (at least 5 min after remifentanil target, 10 min after stable sevoflurane), after loss of consciousness (OAA/S 1), by laryngoscopy then intubation.
- Binary end points: (1) no circulatory response = HR and MABP rise at most 10% from pre-stimulus; (2) circulatory depression = MABP <= 50 mmHg or HR <= 45 bpm. Modelling was done on the proportion in each sevoflurane subgroup (5 patients), not on individual binary data.
- NONMEM VI, first-order method only (FOCE/FOCEI failed); bootstrap 1000 and VPC 1000.

## Model and parameters
Minto/Dahan hybrid: P = (Us+Ur)^g / [(1/I(Q))^g + (Us+Ur)^g], Us = Csevo/C50,sevo, Ur = Cremi/C50,remi, Q = Us/(Us+Ur) (Q = 1 is sevoflurane only here, reversed from Dahan 2001), I(Q) spline with Imax, Qmax (equations 1-4, Appendix). Concentrations are TCI target (remifentanil, ng/ml) and measured end-tidal sevoflurane (vol.%). Table 2 (printed p 732); RSE is percent relative standard error.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End points: circulatory response to laryngoscopy (HR/MABP), circulatory depression (MABP <= 50 or HR <= 45). Not movement or MAC.
- MEAC / analgesic concentration: none reported. Closest: remifentanil alone C50 for no circulatory response to laryngoscopy 46.2 ng/ml (extrapolated above the studied 0-10 ng/ml; CI 24.2-50.2) and for depression 94.7 ng/ml; TCI target concentrations, not measured blood levels; no free/total issue since not assayed.
- ke0 / t½ke0 / tPeak / hysteresis: none estimated. Remifentanil targeted by the Minto 1997 model, text says "effect site" target; waited at least 5 min; no hysteresis analysis. Sevoflurane end-tidal at least 10 min.
- Potency/interaction: strongly synergistic (Imax 3.40, Qmax 0.75 for laryngoscopy; 1.95, 0.85 for depression). Adding remifentanil 10 ng/ml cut C50,sevo from 2.6 to 0.38 vol.% (to about 15% of the sevoflurane-alone value) for laryngoscopy, and from 3.53 to 1.46 vol.% (about 41%) for depression (Table 3, Fig 3). Authors also note a ceiling effect around 0.75 vol.% sevoflurane and 6.73 ng/ml remifentanil.
- No equianalgesic ratio.

## Covariates
None modelled. IIV only on C50,remi (40.9% and 35.5% CV); IIV on all parameters did not converge.

## Caveats
- Probabilities calculated from only five binary responses per sevoflurane group, so the data points are coarse; FO estimation only.
- Remifentanil C50 values lie far above the studied range (0-10 ng/ml), so they are extrapolations (authors call 94.7 ng/ml extremely high and tie it to the depression definition).
- Circulatory depression thresholds (MABP <= 50, HR <= 45) are arbitrary; Chinese ASA I adults 20-50 yr; no independent validation (authors say it is planned).
- Remifentanil concentration is the Minto-model target, not a measured level.
- Table 3 vs Table 2 disagree at remifentanil 0 for depression (3.57 vs 3.53).
