# Squara 2020: BIS-guided dual closed-loop propofol-remifentanil sedation after cardiac surgery

## Study
Open single-centre pilot (NCT02488486), ICU of Clinique Ambroise Pare. 21 adults aged 18-80 y after elective open-heart surgery were enrolled, 19 analysed (2 excluded for poor BIS signal). Median age 66 y, 18 men and 1 woman, EuroSCORE II 1.2 (Table 1, p. 783). Table 1's height and weight rows print inconsistent brackets (170 [173-180]; 71 [82-89]), so they are not recorded here.
Dual-loop controller (Infusion Toolbox 95 v4.11, Asena GH pumps) targeted BIS 50. It used Schnider and Minto models for effect-site display (p. 780). Limits: propofol 0.1-2 µg/mL and remifentanil 0.1-4 ng/mL. Sedation lasted a median of 139 min (89-205).

Results (median [IQR]; p. 781 and Table 2, p. 783):
- Propofol 2.2 [1.1-2.9] mg/kg/h; remifentanil 0.08 [0.04-0.14] µg/kg/min (micro sign dropped in the text layer; unit inferred).
- BIS 40-60 for 87% [57-95] of the time; BIS above 60 for 10%; below 40 for 2%.
- RASS was -5 in all patients; BPS 3 in 82 of 83 assessments.
- Time from end of infusion to extubation 84 [63-129] min.
- 15 patients (79%) needed volume loading for hypotension; the closed loop was stopped in 3 patients.
- Calculated concentrations of both drugs rose over time (Fig 1c, d; figure values not given in the text).
- Discussion (p. 783): requirements were higher than in a previous surgical-ICU study with the same controller and BIS target (propofol 1.2 mg/kg/h vs 2.2 here, Table 3).

None analysed.

## Caveats
Preliminary, uncontrolled, n=19. BIS 50 gave deeper sedation than needed (RASS -5) with hypotension. The authors note possible carry-over of intraoperative propofol and remifentanil in the early calculated concentrations.
