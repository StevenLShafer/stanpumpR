# Saletu 1986: temazepam pharmaco-EEG, psychometrics and blood levels

## Study
- Double-blind, placebo-controlled, Latin-square crossover; 10 healthy volunteers (5 M, 5 F), 20-36 y (mean 27), 54-93 kg (mean 67),
  160-190 cm (mean 174).
- Single oral doses at weekly intervals, 09:00: placebo, temazepam 10, 20, 40 mg (soft gelatin capsule), flunitrazepam 2 mg.
- Blood, EEG, vital signs at 0, 1, 2, 4, 6, 8 h; psychometric tests at the same times except 1 h.
- Assay: **radio-receptor assay** of plasma (refs 25-26), not a chromatographic assay.

## Model and parameters
No PK model. Peak plasma concentrations in the first hour after all temazepam doses, rapid decline thereafter (Fig. 14, means and
SD, values not legible in the text layer). Cites Fuccella: soft-capsule tmax 0.83 h (vs 1.44 h hard), beta half-life 8.35 h.
Introduction quotes half-life 7-8 h.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Regression of drug-induced change on blood level; the "intercept" is the concentration at which the regression line crosses zero
change (Table 4, ng/mL):
- EEG beta increase r = +0.66 (p < .001), intercept 94.1; EEG alpha attenuation r = -0.47, 204.5; EEG centroid r = +0.39, 168.2.
- Psychometric: alphabetical reaction test r = -0.41, 238.9; Pauli test r = -0.40, 249.6 (Table 4 lists 249.7); numerical memory
  r = -0.20, 239.1; psychomotor activity r = -0.29, 252.1; complex reaction r = -0.40, 251.5; reaction-task errors r = +0.42, 252.7;
  CFF r = -0.25, 232.4. Other Table 4 intercepts 226.7-295.2 (text-layer alignment of the remaining rows is uncertain).
- Conclusion: psychometric variables began to deteriorate above about 250 ng/mL, with improvement below it. Levels > 250 ng/mL were
  seen after 40 mg in hours 1-6 and after 20 mg in hours 1-2, never after 10 mg.
- No correlation with mood (Bf-S) or pupil diameter. No ke0/hysteresis analysis. Pharmacodynamic peak in hour 2 (1 h not tested
  psychometrically); effects to hour 4 after 40 mg, hour 2 only after 20 mg.
- Potency: flunitrazepam at least 10 times as potent as temazepam by weight (EEG dose-efficacy).

## Covariates
None analysed.

## Caveats
n = 10; radio-receptor assay (benzodiazepine-receptor binding activity, may not equal temazepam measured by HPLC); "threshold" is
a regression intercept on pooled observations across doses and times, not an individually titrated concentration; sparse sampling
(no data between 0 and 1 h).
