# McLachlan 1996: population PK of fluconazole in HIV/AIDS

PDF page = printed page - 290.

## Study
- 113 men with HIV/AIDS (covariates available for 109). Age mean 38 (23-60) y, weight mean 63 (42-88) kg, CD4 mean 69 (0-1050) cells/mm3, Cockcroft-Gault CLcr mean 68 (36-138) mL/min (PDF p2).
- Study 1: 13 men, 1-3 oral doses (capsules) plus one IV infusion (50 mg per 15 min), doses 50, 100 or 400 mg, 12-17 samples per dose to 168 h. Study 2: 100 men on routine therapy (50-800 mg orally, twice daily to weekly, at steady state), one plasma sample each (mean 20.9 h after the dose, range 0.42-51.6). Combined 770 observations (670 from Study 1).
- Plasma fluconazole by GC (modified Debruyne), LOQ 0.1 mg/L; 1 mg/L = 3.26 umol/L. Total plasma concentration.
- NONMEM IV (FO) and P-PHARM 1.3; one-compartment, first-order elimination, first-order absorption (oral), zero-order IV input; parameters CL, V, ka, F.

The text states the absorption half-life as 8 or 14 min (Study 1).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No PD, no MEAC, no target concentration (not an opioid). The discussion mentions a concentration needed for antifungal efficacy without giving a value.

## Covariates
- NONMEM final (text p5, best model, delta OFV -41, Table 4): CL (L/h) = 0.25 (33%) + 0.0057 (32%) x CLcr (mL/min) + 0.00068 (10%) x CD4 (cells/mm3); percentages are IIV %CV. V 47.5 L, ka 4.98/h, F 0.98, residual 0.51 mg/L.
- Single-covariate NONMEM: CL = 0.20 (9%) + 0.0068 (34%) x CLcr (delta OFV -34); CL = 0.70 (11%) + 0.001 (38%) x CD4 (-21).
- P-PHARM Bayesian-estimate regression: CL = 0.39 + 0.0025 x CLcr.
- Weight did not influence CL or V in NONMEM (Table 4). Reason for use, zidovudine, didanosine and co-trimoxazole did not.
- CD4 cut-off of 200 cells/mm3 was chosen on clinical grounds (CL split, Table 3).

## Caveats
- All men; CD4<200 group dominates (97 vs 12). CD4 and CLcr uncorrelated (r2=0.04), but the authors suggest the CD4 effect may reflect an undetected renal decline, since CLcr is Cockcroft-Gault in an HIV population.
- Study 2 is one sample per person, mostly late in the interval; this pulled CL down from Study 1 (0.88 to 0.78 L/h) while V stayed put.
- NONMEM could not estimate ka IIV well (234-246% CV).
- CL values are absolute (L/h), not per kg.
- Comparators in the text: CL 1.06 L/h and V 53.8 L in AIDS patients (DeMuria), 0.82-1.20 L/h and 48 L in healthy subjects (Humphery); Table 2 CL is lower than these.
