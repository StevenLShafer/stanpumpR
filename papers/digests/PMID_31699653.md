# Yamamoto 2019: alprazolam in a neonate with abstinence syndrome, with PBPK simulation

This is the publisher's version. The archive also holds the authors' accepted manuscript, digested as
`PMID_31699653_aam.md`; the numbers below agree with it.

## Study
A single **case report** (mother and neonate) plus Simcyp PBPK simulations; not a PK study, no cohort, no
fitted parameters.

- **Mother** (PDF p2, printed 401, section 3): 30 years old, panic disorder. Alprazolam 2.4-3.2 mg/day in
  mid-pregnancy, **2.4 mg/day in late pregnancy** until delivery; lorazepam 0.5-1.0 mg/day as needed in
  mid-pregnancy. Last alprazolam dose 20 min before delivery. Weight not given. Vaginal delivery at 40 weeks.
- **Neonate**: female, 3400 g, Apgar 9. Apnea with oxygen in an incubator at 2 h (Finnegan 7); vomiting and poor
  feeding from 9 h to day 2 (IV fluid); out of the incubator on day 3; Finnegan 0 by day 4.
- **Sampling** (Fig. 1A, PDF p2): neonate at 1, 23.5, 47, 71.5 and 97.5 h; mother once, on day 100 postpartum.
  Assay LC-MS/MS (LSI Medience, Tokyo); plasma, total (unbound not measured). Detection limit not stated.
- **Simulation** (section 2.2): Simcyp v15, alprazolam compound model. Mother: "Sim-Pregnancy" with age and height
  set to hers and hepatic/gut CYP3A4/3A5 abundances from "Sim-Japanese"; 0.8 mg PO three times daily. Neonate:
  "Sim-Paediatrics", 0 year, female, an IV dose chosen to give a 15.2 ng/mL peak, time-varying enzyme abundance.
  Postpartum mother: "Japanese women" population (Fig. 2C). 100 simulated subjects, 90% prediction intervals.

The authors report that the estimated delivery value, the neonatal 24 and 48 h values and the day-100 maternal
value all fell within the Simcyp 90% prediction intervals; the 72 h neonatal point could not be reproduced
(PDF p2-p3). Fig. 2A and 2C (PDF p3) show the simulated pregnancy and postpartum means at a broadly similar level
(roughly 25-35 ng/mL read by eye); no numbers are tabulated.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: Finnegan neonatal abstinence score (Fig. 1B bars, read by eye: 7, then 5, then 4, then 0 by day 4; the text states only 7 at 2 h and 0 by day 4) and the
clinical course; a visual single-case parallel between falling concentration and resolving apnea/GI symptoms. No
exposure-response model, no ke0, no hysteresis. Quoted from other sources (PDF p2, Discussion), not measured here:
EC50 27 ng/mL (sedation) and 30 ng/mL (stimulation) **in rats** (ref 5, Lau 1997), and a "lower threshold" of the
therapeutic range given as 2-25 ng/mL (ref 6, Jones and Holmgren 2013, blood from impaired drivers and autopsies).
No MEAC or potency ratio (not an analgesic).

## Covariates
None fitted. Ethnicity enters only as Simcyp CYP3A abundance choice; age and height set to the patient's.

## Caveats
- n = 1; mid-pregnancy lorazepam co-exposure.
- Maternal delivery concentration back-calculated through a rat placental ratio (the authors' stated limitation).
- Simcyp pregnancy/paediatric populations are Caucasian-derived apart from the CYP3A abundances (authors).
- The neonatal "IV dose" is fictitious, chosen to match the first sample.
- Half-lives come from single pairs of points on nominal 24 h intervals.
- **No pregnant-against-nonpregnant comparison**, no clearance or volume, no exposure ratio.
