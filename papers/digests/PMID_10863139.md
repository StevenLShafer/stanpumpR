# Gidal 2000: variability of gabapentin absorption and bioavailability

## Study
Two prospective studies in healthy adults, fasting.
- Study A: randomised 4-way crossover, 400 mg single doses as 1 × 400 mg capsule, 2 × 200, 4 × 100 mg capsules and
  400 mg solution, weekly; n = 20 (9 M, 11 F; mean age 41 y, weight 75.1 kg). Plasma to 48 h
  (0.25-48 h, 13 samples). NCA (linear trapezoid).
- Study B: 600 mg single dose (2 × 300 mg capsules), n = 50 (15 M, 35 F; 31.1 y, 72.7 kg); 48 h urine; F = Ae/dose.
- HPLC-UV (Lensmeyer 1995); precision CV 0.7-2.6% plasma, 0.8-1.2% urine.

AUC by sex (study A): men 32.1, women 35.8 µg·h/mL. F by sex (study B): men 51.7 ± 7.8%, women 48.1 ± 15.3%.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. Authors note no data support a therapeutic range for gabapentin.

## Covariates
Sex: no significant difference in mean AUC or F; variance of F larger in women (CV 31.8 vs 15.1%).

## Caveats
"Absolute bioavailability" is urinary recovery over 48 h (assumes complete urine collection and renal-only
elimination), no IV reference. Single doses only (400 and 600 mg); absorption is dose-dependent, so variability may
differ at higher doses. Abstract and text disagree on inter-subject CV (22.5 vs 33.5%). Industry co-authors
(Parke-Davis).
