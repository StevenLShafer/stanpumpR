# Obara 2018: external evaluation of nine dexmedetomidine PK models in spinal-anesthesia sedation

## Study
Prospective evaluation (PDF p.2-3). 16 ASA I-II adults having elective surgery under spinal anesthesia (2.4-2.8 mL 0.5% hyperbaric bupivacaine, L3/4) with dexmedetomidine sedation: 9 patients on the manufacturer regimen (6 ug/kg/h for 10 min, then 0.2-0.7 ug/kg/h), 7 on TCI with the Dyck model, initial target 1.5 ng/mL. Infusion delivered by STANPUMP on a Harvard 22 pump, max rate 6 ug/kg/h. Dose titrated to OAA/S 3-4. 84 venous plasma samples (0, 15 min, about every 15 min, and 5, 10, 15 min after the end). Assay: LC-MS/MS, linear 0.02-20 ng/mL, plasma (total, not free). Simulations in NONMEM 7.2; statistics in R. Performance error PE = (measured - predicted)/predicted; pooled-data MDPE (bias), MDAPE (accuracy), wobble per Varvel 1992. Acceptability thresholds cited: MDPE within +-20%, MDAPE < 30%.

## Model and parameters
Table 1 (PDF p.3, confirmed on image) reproduces nine published parameter sets (V in L, CL in L/min; Ht cm, Wt kg, Ag years, alb g/dL, ALT U/L). Transcribed as printed; they are the evaluated models, not new estimates:
- Dyck 1993: V1 7.99, V2 13.8, V3 187; CL1 0.00791 x Ht - 0.927, CL2 2.26, CL3 1.99 (volunteers, n 16).
- Lin: V1 63.4, V2 41.3, V3 284.3; CL1 0.47 x (Ht/160)^6.42, CL2 2.43, CL3 0.086.
- Talke: V1 16.57, V2 85.5; CL1 0.751, CL2 1.37.
- Venn: V1 44.1, V2 104.5; CL1 0.82, CL2 2.255.
- Lee: V1 19.9 x (Ag/27)^0.954, V2 59.4; CL1 0.56 x (alb/4.3)^1.42, CL2 1.13.
- Dutta: V1 13, V2 55; CL1 0.55, CL2 0.833.
- Valitalo: V1 104; CL1 0.65 (other cells as printed are unclear in the table layout).
- Hannivoort 2015: V1 1.83 x (Wt/70), V2 27.8 x (Wt/70), V3 54.4 x (Wt/70); CL1 0.695 x (Wt/70)^0.75, CL2 3.25 x (V2/27.8)^0.75, CL3 0.689 x (V3/54.4)^0.75 (volunteers, n 18, 20-70 y, 51-110 kg, arterial, 408 samples).
- Kuang: V1 17.6 x (Ag/49.7)^-1.43, V2 51.5 x (Wt/63.6)^0.789, V3 44; CL1 0.883 x (ALT/36)^0.113, CL2 2.37, CL3 0.517.

Positive MDPE means measured concentrations exceeded predicted ones, i.e. the model under-predicted. Every model except Lee, Dutta and Hannivoort failed the +-20% / 30% criteria. The Dyck model (the TCI model used for half the patients) under-predicted by 84.9%, as did Venn (83.0%).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD analysis. OAA/S was recorded and used to titrate to a score of 3-4, but no concentration-effect relation, ke0, t1/2 ke0, tPeak, EC50 or target concentration for sedation is reported. The only concentration target is the 1.5 ng/mL initial TCI target (Dyck model), derived from a simulated peak for the manufacturer regimen in a 170 cm patient (PDF p.2). Not an opioid; opioid audit not applicable (no MEAC, ke0 or potency ratio).

## Covariates
None estimated. Covariates in the evaluated models: weight (Hannivoort, Kuang V2), height (Dyck, Lin CL), age (Lee, Kuang V1), albumin (Lee CL), ALT (Kuang CL); the most recent pre-operative albumin/ALT were used.

## Caveats
Authors' own: short surgeries; mostly women in the regimen group; no very high concentrations (dose limited for safety). Further: n = 16 and 84 samples; venous plasma sampled against arterial samples in Hannivoort's volunteer data; randomized to two dosing regimens so the dataset mixes bolus and TCI profiles; PE sign is measured minus predicted over predicted (opposite of some papers' convention). Authors attribute Kuang's poor fit to reduced hepatic flow under spinal block, which is speculation (no hepatic data).

## Verification against stanpumpR
Not applicable (not cited). For information:
- R/drugs_dexmedetomidine.R:107-163 uses Morse 2020 above 1 year (V1 25.2, V2 34.4, V3 65.4 L; CL 0.897, Q2 1.68, Q3 0.62 L/min), not a model Obara evaluated. The Dyck-like set that the file's HISTORY (lines 99-104) says was used until 2026-11-17 (V1 8.06, V2 12.8, V3 178 L; CL 0.445 L/min) is the family Obara found to under-predict by about 85% (Table 3) in this sedation setting. That replacement is therefore supported by this paper in direction.
- tPeak 14.9 min (line 167) is a Colin 2017 ke0 estimated on Hannivoort kinetics; this paper supports Hannivoort's kinetics predicting plasma concentrations in sedated spinal patients (MDPE 5.6%), but not Morse's, whose V1 is about 14x larger (see open question).
- Plasma, total-drug concentrations throughout, matching the library's ng/mL plasma convention.
