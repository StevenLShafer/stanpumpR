# Soy 2004: population 1-compartment PK with missing dosage data

## Study
Simulation methods paper plus one small real data set. No stanpumpR drug is modelled in it.
- Scenario: oral dose nominally every tau (tau fixed at 1). A blood sample C0 is drawn just before an observed test dose Dt, then 5-10 samples (5-7 in the clinical description) within one interval. The pre-test dose Ds is 0 or 1 unit, given at -(tau +/- delta) with delta ~ N(0, SD 0.2); 60% of subjects have Ds = 0. Earlier doses are as prescribed.
- Model (simulation and analysis): 1-compartment, first-order absorption and elimination. Parameters CL, V and Delta = Ka - K (Delta >= 0, avoids flip-flop). Log-normal IIV (eq 1), combined proportional + additive residual error (eq 2). V fixed at 1.
- Study 1: CL fixed 0.7 (t1/2 about tau, heavy accumulation). Study 2: CL fixed 4 (t1/2 about tau/5). Other variables sampled by Latin hypercube: Delta 3-7, omega^2 (CL, V, Delta) 0.01-0.04, sigma1^2 0.01-0.04, N 15-25, n 5-10. 200 trials per study, NONMEM FOCE with eta-epsilon interaction.
- Methods compared: Prescribed Dose (PDM), Missing Dose (MDM; amount in the central compartment at time 0, A0, is an estimated individual parameter), Missing Dose Mixture (MDMM), Extrapolation-Subtraction (ESM), Ideal (IDM, true history).
- Robustness: structural misspecification (2-compartment truth, V1 = V2 = 0.5, Q 0.5-3, fit with 1-compartment; judged on AUC, Cmin, Cmax) and residual-error misspecification (pure additive or pure proportional).
- Real data: 8 subjects, oral caffeine test dose 175 mg, samples at baseline and 30, 60, 90, 120, 240, 360, 480, 660 and 840 min; concentrations 0.08-8.47 mg/L, baseline 0-3.9 mg/L. Prior caffeine exposure unknown.

## Model and parameters
Findings: with accumulation (study 1) MDM and MDMM are the most precise; PDM and ESM are poor. With little accumulation (study 2) the methods do not differ (Figs 2 and 4). Table I gives the percentage of runs that terminated acceptably (option B) per parameter. Structural and residual misspecification did not change the ranking (Fig 5).

## Caveats
- Simple linear 1-compartment oral model only; the authors say more complex models need study.
- Many trials ended with variance parameters on a boundary (Table I), because the design ranges were deliberately broad.
- Real-data example: n = 8. The literature values it compares with come from a different population (Newton 1981 healthy volunteers).
- MDM ignores any drug still in the depot compartment at time 0.
- The caffeine data are oral adult data. They say nothing about neonatal IV caffeine.
