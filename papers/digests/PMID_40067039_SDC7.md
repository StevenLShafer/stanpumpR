# Vellinga 2025, SDC7: predicted sedation and tolerance tables for remimazolam plus remifentanil

## Study
Supplement to the remimazolam-remifentanil PK/PD volunteer study (24 healthy volunteers; see `PMID_40067039.md`). SDC7 holds two model-simulation tables (and matching figures, not viewed): predicted probabilities after 60 min (Table 1, PDF p. 1) and 120 min (Table 2, PDF p. 3) of remimazolam target-controlled infusion, at steady remifentanil targets of 0, 0.1, 0.5, 1, 2 and 4 ng/mL and remimazolam targets of 150, 300, 400, 800, 1300 and 2000 ng/mL (36 rows per table). Columns: BIS (typical value), TOL (tolerance to laryngoscopy, %), TOTS (tolerance to tetanic stimulation, %), and MOAAS 5, 4, 3, 2, 1, 0 (% probability of each score). The supplement gives no simulation details (population, ke0, dosing); no SEs or intervals.

## Model and parameters
No parameters are tabulated; these are model outputs. The time dependence (60 vs 120 min) reflects the acute tolerance (loss of effect with infusion time) that the parent paper attributes to the metabolite CNS7054. Selected rows read from the rendered pages (remimazolam ng/mL; remifentanil 0 unless stated):

- 60 min (Table 1), remifentanil 0: 150 -> BIS 78, MOAAS 5 50.0%, 4 40.4%; 300 -> BIS 68, MOAAS 3/2 20.6/23.0%, MOAAS 0 3.4%; 800 -> BIS 53, MOAAS 0 43.7%; 2000 -> BIS 45, MOAAS 0 74.4%, TOL 3.6%, TOTS 14.2%.
- 120 min (Table 2), remifentanil 0: 300 -> BIS 70, MOAAS 5 10.0%, MOAAS 0 1.8%; 2000 -> BIS 50, MOAAS 0 64.3%.
- Remifentanil 4 ng/mL: 300 remimazolam, 60 min -> BIS 62, TOL 50.6%, TOTS 70.7%, MOAAS 0 99.8%; 120 min -> BIS 65, MOAAS 0 99.0%. TOL and TOTS values are the same in the two tables to within 0.1 percentage point (e.g. TOL 15.0 vs 15.1, TOTS 58.3 vs 58.4), i.e. no time dependence; the parent paper reports acute tolerance for MOAAS and BIS only.
- Remimazolam 2000 with remifentanil 4: TOL 87.2%, TOTS 94.1% (both tables).
- Remifentanil 0.5, remimazolam 300, 60 min: BIS 67, TOL 11.7%, TOTS 24.4%, MOAAS 3/2/1/0 13.5/25.6/36.0/10.7%.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End points: BIS, MOAAS (proportional-odds probabilities), TOTS, TOL. Concentrations are targets (remifentanil in ng/mL, plasma; remimazolam in ng/mL) with remimazolam at 60 or 120 min of TCI. The supplement reports no ke0, t½ke0, tPeak, hysteresis, EC50, MEAC or analgesic concentration, and no equianalgesic/potency ratio. Opioid audit: remifentanil is an opioid, but this file states none of those quantities (remifentanil's interaction C50s are in the parent paper's Table 1: BIS 7.96, MOAAS 2.5, TOL 0.02, TOTS 0.04 ng/mL, per `PMID_40067039.md`, not re-checked here).

## Covariates
None stated (typical-person simulation; the parent paper's simulations used a 70 kg typical person per `PMID_40067039.md`, not stated in SDC7).

## Caveats
Model predictions, not observations; highest remifentanil targets (2 and 4 ng/mL) rest on 4 and 2 volunteers (parent paper), so those rows are extrapolations. BIS is printed to a whole number only. The text layer drops BIS values and misaligns columns, so every number here was read from the rendered pages.
