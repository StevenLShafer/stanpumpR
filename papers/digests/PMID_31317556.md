# Hornik 2019: milrinone population PK in children, with creatinine clearance

## Study
- Pediatric Trials Network PTN POPS (NCT01431326): multicentre, prospective, opportunistic sampling of children < 21 y given IV milrinone per standard of care; dosing and sample times varied (p. 1607-1608).
- 74 patients, 111 evaluable plasma samples (125 drawn; 14 excluded: 9 beyond the validated dilution, 3 BLQ, 2 outliers), 1 (1-11) samples per patient. Table 1 (rendered, median (range) or n (%)): age 2.9 y (0.01-18) [<= 28 d 13, > 28 d-2 y 19, 2-12 y 29, > 12 y 13]; gestational age 39 wk (37-41); PMA 192 wk (37-977); weight 13.1 kg (2.6-157.7); male 39 (53%); serum creatinine 0.5 mg/dL (0.1-3.1); CrCl 117.2 mL/min/1.73 m2 (13.1-261.3) [<= 30: 4; 30-60: 9; 60-120: 32; > 120: 29]; ECMO 17 (23%); obese 11 (15%); infusion rate 0.5 ug/kg/min (0.1-41); duration 12 h (0.02-2647).
- LC-MS/MS (Frontage). Validation range 1 to 3800 ng/mL in Methods (p. 1608) but 1-5000 ng/mL in the abstract; observed concentrations 4-634 ng/mL (abstract).
- NONMEM 7.4.3, FOCE-I; 1-, 2-, 3-compartment models; allometric weight fixed (0.75 CL, 1 V; estimating them changed OFV by -0.02); covariates by forward inclusion (P < .05) and backward elimination (P < .01). CrCl by the updated bedside Schwartz equation (refs. 25-29; the formula and its constant are not printed). Missing covariates: last value carried forward or population median; gestational age 40 wk imputed when missing; CrCl imputed in 5 subjects.

One compartment (Results p. 1610):

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No pharmacodynamics measured. Target steady-state range 100-300 ng/mL, taken from the label and adult studies (refs. 1, 13 = Bailey 1994, 14), and > 500 ng/mL associated with toxicity in adults (hypotension, arrhythmia, thrombocytopenia); the authors note these targets rest on limited PD data in children. Simulations (1000 per subject; virtual 3 kg/PMA 40 wk, 13 kg/144 wk, 40 kg/664 wk, 70 kg/1600 wk; CrCl 30, 60, 120, 160):
- 50 ug/kg load (over 30 min) then 0.5 ug/kg/min: therapeutic at CrCl >= 60 in the 3-kg, 13-kg and 40-kg subjects, except the 40-kg adolescent at CrCl 160, who stayed below 100 ng/mL; above 300 ng/mL in the 3-kg newborn at CrCl 30 (Results p. 1611-1612). In the 13-kg toddler the median Css sat at the 300 ng/mL upper limit (Discussion).
- 25 ug/kg then 0.25 ug/kg/min kept all three subjects in range at CrCl 30; conclusion: 0.25 ug/kg/min for infants and small children with CrCl <= 30.
- The abstract states only the CrCl <= 30 exception. Text typos: loads written "ug/kg/min administered over 30 minutes" (Methods) and the infusion "50 ug/kg/min" (Results) where the figure captions give 25 or 50 ug/kg and 0.5 ug/kg/min.

## Covariates
Weight (allometric, fixed exponents), PMA (sigmoid Emax maturation, estimated), bedside-Schwartz CrCl (power). Tested and not retained: sex, race, ethnicity, obesity, PNA, ECMO, indication, surgery, serum creatinine. No covariate on V beyond weight.

## Caveats
- Sparse, opportunistic data (median one sample per child); high IIV on CL (70%); imprecise maturation parameters; IIV on V fixed to zero.
- Bedside Schwartz limitations in infants < 2 y, obesity and ECMO; CrCl imputed in 5.
- Few infants <= 1 y (23); no adverse-event capture beyond procedures.
