# Barvais 2003: remifentanil effect-site concentration and pupil dilatation to tetanus

## Study
- 12 ASA I/II adults (6 F, 6 M), minor peripheral surgery, no eye disease, no medication, oral alprazolam 0.5 mg.
  Mean (SD) age 40.5 (11.5) yr (22-65), weight 64.7 (12.4) kg (50-78), height 167.7 (7.8) cm (PDF p3).
- Propofol TCI (Schnider set, effect-site targeting, t½ke0 1.8 min stated p2) raised in 1 µg/mL steps from 2 until loss of
  consciousness (LOC); mean propofol CeT at LOC 3.53 (0.43) µg/mL (range 3-4). Cisatracurium 0.15 mg/kg given.
- Remifentanil then targeted by effect site (Minto 1997 set, "TOOLBOX" TCI after Shafer and Gregg) in steps of 1 up to 5 ng/mL,
  with no limit on plasma overshoot. Pupil assessed when 100% of target CeT reached.
- Measures at awake, LOC and each plateau: BP, HR, BIS (A2000); pupil diameter (Colvard infrared pupillometer, 0.1 mm
  resolution) before and after a 100 Hz tetanus (T100) of the ulnar nerve, 60 mA, 10 s. Dilatation = after minus before.
- Control group of 5 ASA I patients on the same propofol profile and no opioid, T100 every 2 min x5: no fade of dilatation.
- All remifentanil concentrations are calculated (Minto model), not measured. No assay.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: pupil dilatation (mm) after T100, under propofol TCI (not isoflurane, not awake). Also pupil size before T100, BP, HR, BIS.
- Dilatation to T100, mean (SD): 1.55 (0.72) mm at LOC (no remifentanil), falling to 0.01 (0.03) mm at CeT 5 ng/mL (text p3, Fig 4A).
  Significantly smaller than at LOC and at 1 ng/mL from CeT 2 ng/mL.
- Linear fit, dilatation vs calculated CeT 0-5 ng/mL: y = 1.46 - 0.324x, R² = 0.68, rho = -0.82; 95% CI of slope -0.377 to -0.271 (PDF p3-4, Fig 4B).
- **50% reduction** of maximal T100 dilatation (vs LOC without remifentanil) at calculated CeT **2.3 ng/mL** (PDF p4). Large inter-individual variation at LOC and at 1 ng/mL.
- Pupil size before T100: 4.14 (1.42) mm at LOC; 3.02 (1.24) at 1 ng/mL; 2.07 (0.48) at 2 ng/mL. After T100: 5.69 (1.42) at LOC, 4.25 (1.21) at 1 ng/mL, below 2.5 mm from 2 ng/mL up.

- **MEAC / analgesic concentration: none stated.** The 2.3 ng/mL value is a pupil EC50-like point on a calculated effect-site concentration, not an analgesic threshold.
- **ke0 / t½ke0 / tPeak for remifentanil: not reported** (uses the Minto set's effect site as built into the pump). Propofol t½ke0 1.8 min (Schnider set, as set in the TCI).
- **Potency ratio: none for remifentanil.** The discussion cites Larson 1997 for alfentanil: 50% reduction of dilatation near 20 ng/mL (plasma, isoflurane), almost abolished near 100 ng/mL; that is a literature citation, not this study.

## Covariates
None analysed. Age 22-65, weight 50-78 kg; the Minto set carries its own age and lean body mass covariates, which set the simulated doses.

## Caveats
- Calculated, not measured, concentrations; Minto set derived in volunteers with an EEG end point.
- 12 young healthy patients; all on propofol at a CeT near 3.5 µg/mL plus alprazolam; no surgical stimulus. Effect of propofol on pupil response is not separated.
- Staircase in one direction in a short time (about 1 min per assessment): concentrations may not have equilibrated and tolerance/time are confounded with dose (the control group addresses fade of dilatation only).
- Haemodynamic fall may reflect delayed propofol effect, as the authors note.

## Verification against stanpumpR
No repo location cites this paper, so nothing is verified against it. Independent comparison with `R/drugs_remifentanil.R:107-120`:
- The library's remifentanil MEAC is 1 ng/mL (typical 1.2, range 0.8-2.0) with tPeak 1.6 min. This paper reports no MEAC and no ke0, so it neither supports nor contradicts either. Its 50% suppression of pupillary dilatation to tetanus at calculated CeT 2.3 ng/mL (under propofol) is above the library's MEAC, consistent with an analgesic-threshold concentration being lower than a half-maximal reflex suppression. This is an observation, not a check, since the end points differ.
- The library's kinetics are Eleveld/Kim, not the Minto set this study used, so its "CeT" values are not the library's effect-site concentrations.
