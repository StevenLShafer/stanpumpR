# Faraj 2022: marzeptacog alfa population PK and phase III dose selection

## Study
- Pooled data from three adult trials (NCT01439971 i.v.; NCT04072237 i.v. + s.c.; NCT03407651 i.v. + s.c., including daily s.c. dosing for 50 days). Doses in µg/kg: i.v. 4.5, 9, 18, 30 (the 0.5 level was excluded, one subject); s.c. 30, 45, 60, 90, 120 (Table 1, PDF p.3).
- 46 male subjects with hemophilia A/B with or without inhibitors, 1225 observations (Table 1). Median weight 75 kg (10th-90th 60-94; range 43-120). Median age 32 y (10th-90th 23-52; range 18-62). 19 BLOQ samples (1.6%) were dropped.
- Assay details are in Supplement Data S2 (not in the PDF). NONMEM; model code in Data S1.
- The paper reports mass concentrations (ng/mL) of drug; the target was set from wild-type rFVIIa in IU/mL (see PD section).

## Model and parameters
- Final model: 2-compartment disposition, linear elimination, zero-order input into a depot followed by first-order absorption (kA) into the central compartment; proportional residual error.
- Allometry with fixed exponents: CL = TVCL x (WT/70)^0.75, Vc = TVVc x (WT/70)^1 (PDF p.4). Same applied to all CL and V terms.
- Baseline endogenous FVIIa is additive: IPRED = BASE x exp(IIVbase) + A/Vc.
- IIV on CL, V, baseline FVIIa, F (CV 54%), kA (CV 34%); IOV on zero-order input duration, F and Vc; covariance of IIV CL-V.
- Michaelis-Menten elimination was tried and dropped (OFV rise 4.3). Its Km estimate was 1352 ng/mL, RSE 68% (Vmax RSE 65%), versus a CL RSE of 7.5% in the linear model (PDF p.9).
- Age on kA was significant (p<0.01) but not clinically significant (Cmax +18% at age 23, -22% at age 52, vs age 32 at 60 µg/kg) and not retained. Weight on absorption parameters and baseline was tested.
- No PK parameter values (CL, V, Q, kA, F) are given in the main text; they are in Supplementary Table S1.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- Not an opioid. No ke0, tPeak or hysteresis analysis; no PD model.
- Target concentration used for dose selection: 24-120 ng/mL MarzAA, defined as 6-30 IU/mL wild-type rFVIIa (NovoSeven) divided by an assumed at-least 5-fold higher potency of MarzAA (preclinical 5-10-fold; in vitro thrombin generation and animal models) (PDF p.4 and p.9). Total plasma concentration, including baseline FVIIa. The 6 IU/mL lower bound comes from literature on hemostasis with rFVIIa; 30 IU/mL from continuous-infusion practice (refs 14-16).
- Simulation results (PDF p.5, Figure 2): fraction above 24 ng/mL after a single dose at 3 h / 6 h: 60 µg/kg 51% / 70%; 90 µg/kg 74% / 87%; 120 µg/kg 86% / 94%. Second dose at 3 h: 90%, 96%, 98% above target at 6 h. Median time to target after one dose: 2.2 h (1.1-5.0), 1.6 h (0.9-3.5), 1.3 h (0.7-2.7) for 60/90/120.
- The 60 µg/kg dose went to phase III.

## Covariates
Body weight by fixed allometry on CL and V (0.75, 1; 70 kg reference). Age on kA found but excluded. The dosing is per kg in all trials. No ADAs were detected.

## Caveats
Small n (46 men, narrow weight range, which is why estimated allometric exponents were not retained). Data come from patients dosed in the non-bleeding state; the target may differ for trauma vs spontaneous bleeds. The target depends on an assumed (not measured human) potency ratio of at least 5x. Parameters not in the main PDF.
