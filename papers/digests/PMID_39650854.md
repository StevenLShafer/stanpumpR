# Zhang 2024: nirmatrelvir PopPK in Chinese COVID-19 patients

## Study
Single-centre retrospective PK study, Changxing People's Hospital, Dec 2022 - Jun 2023 (PDF p.2). Adults >=18 y with mild-moderate COVID-19 on nirmatrelvir/ritonavir 300/100 mg twice daily for 5 days; excluded hepatitis B/C, HIV, and use of CYP450/P-gp inhibitors or inducers in the prior week. Sparse sampling (mainly trough). 130 plasma samples from 129 patients (65 M, 64 F). Table 1: age 73.2 +/- 14.7 y (median 76; 18-97); weight 61.2 +/- 9.3 kg (median 61.1; 37.5-96.0); height 164.6 cm; CrCl 56.7 +/- 33.1 mL/min (median 52.9; 4.8-289.2). Assay: UPLC-MS/MS, plasma, linear range nirmatrelvir 0.1-10.0 ug/mL (total plasma). NONMEM 7.4.1, FOCE-I, PsN SCM, bootstrap 1000.

## Model and parameters
One compartment, first-order absorption, first-order elimination (ADVAN2 TRANS2). V/F and Ka fixed to literature values because only trough data. A-priori allometry: volumes x (WT/70), clearances x (WT/70)^0.75. Final: CL/F = 3.41 x (WT/70)^0.75 x (CrCl/52.9)^theta x exp(eta), theta = 0.429 (PDF p.4-5). Table 2 (PDF p.5, printed 5521; confirmed on rendered page). Residual error additive + proportional, both fixed at 10%. CrCl was the only retained covariate; IIV fell 58.9% to 52.3%; Discussion says CrCl explains 6.6% of IIV.

Simulated exposures (Table 3, 65 kg, 1000 virtual subjects per group, median (95% CI), 300 mg bid with ritonavir 100 mg): CrCl 100 mL/min: first-dose Ctrough 2.36 (0.48-4.72), Cmax 8.31 (4.99-16.16) ug/mL, AUCtau 71.07 (30.32-153.21) ug h/mL; CrCl 15: Ctrough 4.74, Cmax 16.00, AUCtau 164.30. Day-5 (10th dose) Ctrough 3.10 at CrCl 100, 10.10 at CrCl 15. 150 mg bid at CrCl 15: AUCtau 82.11. Table 4 (CrCl 100): 115 kg AUCtau 45.61, 40 kg 100.34 ug h/mL.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No ke0, EC50, Emax or effect-site model; not an opioid, so no MEAC/potency audit applies. Efficacy target quoted from other work: trough above the in-vitro EC90 for 90% inhibition of SARS-CoV-2 replication, 292 ng/mL (= 0.292 ug/mL; Introduction PDF p.2, total concentration). Safety reference "wide safety margin" 26.4 ug/mL (Discussion p.5, from refs 7, 12; basis not described). Simulated >90% of normal-renal-function patients on 300 mg bid had Ctrough >= EC90 on days 1 and 5 (Discussion).

## Covariates
CrCl (Cockcroft-Gault not stated in the text read) on CL/F as a power term; body weight by fixed allometry (0.75 on CL, 1 on V). Age, height, sex, WBC, CRP, procalcitonin, ALP, GGT, ALT, AST not retained.

## Caveats
Authors: sparse (mostly trough) data, so V/F and Ka fixed from earlier work; elderly patients (mean 73 y), limiting generalisation; ritonavir co-administration and medical history not collected; dashboard not validated. Doses are nirmatrelvir with ritonavir (CYP3A4 inhibited), so CL/F is the boosted apparent clearance; F not estimable. Comparator for their "higher exposure" claim: median AUCtau 71.07 vs young Chinese and Western volunteers 32.01 and 33.35 ug h/mL from the Paxlovid label (Discussion, ref 11), not the library. Residual error fixed at 10% rather than estimated.
