# Urien 2011: population pharmacokinetics of etoposide in 67 children

## Study
- **Design:** a randomised open trial (France, multicentre) of ondansetron with or without dexamethasone 5 mg/m²/day, given
  30 min before chemotherapy.
- **Patients:** 67 children with cancer (medulloblastoma 18, neuroblastoma 26, nephroblastoma 7, retinoblastoma 5,
  other 11). From Table 1 (p. 599):
  - age mean 5.35, median 3.52, range 0.27-16.7 years;
  - weight mean 20.6, median 15, range 5.3-66 kg;
  - height median 100, range 60-172 cm;
  - serum creatinine mean 48, median 79, range 15-650 µM (median above mean, printed as is);
  - bilirubin median 6.0, range 2-17 µM.
- **Eligibility:** age 3 months to 16 years, weight above 4.5 kg.
- **Dosing (p. 598):** a 1-h etoposide infusion, then 1-h carboplatin, on 3 or 5 consecutive days.
  - 3-day course: 150 mg/m²/day etoposide.
  - 5-day course: 100 mg/m²/day etoposide.
  - Under 1 year or under 10 kg: 5 or 3.3 mg/kg/day.
  - The 3-day carboplatin dose is printed as "200 mg/kg/day", evidently mg/m²; it is not used by the library.
- **Sampling:** PK on the first and last day, 4 samples per occasion drawn at random from 5, 15 and 30 min and 1-11 h after
  the end of infusion. That gives 566 concentrations, 9 of them BLQ (treated as censored).
- **Assay:** plasma, HPLC with fluorescence and teniposide internal standard, LOQ 0.1 mg/L. It is total etoposide by
  implication, since no unbound measurement is described.
- **Analysis:** Monolix 3.1s (SAEM), with exponential BSV and proportional residual error.

## Model and parameters
- **Structure:** two compartments with linear elimination. Every parameter is scaled allometrically to 70 kg:
  P = P_TYPICAL × (BW/70)^PWR, with PWR ¾ for CL and Q and 1 for Vc and Vp (p. 599). The exponent is printed as ¾ in the
  Methods, the Abstract and Table 3, and in the dosing formula (p. 600).
- **Before weight scaling:** the base model gave CL 0.69 L/h at a mean 20.6 kg, with BSV 53%. The median EBE clearance was
  0.74 L/h, range 0.21-1.84 (Fig. 1).
- **Maturation:** a sigmoid PMA maturation function was tested and dropped, because PMA50 and h had RSE above 50%.

Table 3 (p. 600), typical values at 70 kg:

- The correlation between the CL and Vc BSVs is 0.89.
- The Abstract gives Vc as 9.21 L/70 kg and the CL BSV as 0.26, where Table 3 gives 9.3 and 0.27.
- Table 3's covariate column prints "CL70" in the equations for Vc, Q and Vp, where it means each parameter's own typical value.
- The footnote's worked check, 2.05 × (15/70)^¾ = 0.75 L/h, actually evaluates to 0.646 L/h (derived).

Derived values (not printed in the paper), at 70 kg: the distribution and terminal half-lives are 2.07 h and 8.81 h, and
Vss is 15.0 L.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
There is no PD analysis. The exposure measure is the course AUC: mean 371 (SD 94) mg·h/L for 3-day courses and 474 (SD 127)
for 5-day courses, which differ significantly (P = 0.0003). The authors propose dosing to a target AUC as
Dose = 2.05·(BW/70)^¾·AUC_target (p. 600, Fig. 3). No therapeutic or toxic concentration is given.

## Covariates
- Body weight is the only covariate retained, with the ¾ and 1 allometry above.
- Tested and not retained:
  - PMA maturation;
  - dexamethasone on CL at either occasion (changes under 2%);
  - CYP3A4 \*1B, CYP3A5 \*3 and MDR1 2677/3435 genotypes (CYP3A5\*1\*3 trend +9%, P = 0.09, n = 10; Table 2).
- The paper does not say that creatinine or bilirubin were tested as covariates; they appear only in Table 1.

## Caveats
- Only 8 patients were under 1 year and 2 under 6 months, so maturation could not be identified.
- The heaviest child weighed 66 kg; the 70-kg values are a scaling reference. The authors call them close to adult
  estimates of 1.8 and 2.65 L/h (refs 13 and 14).
- Sampling went to 11 h after the end of infusion, about 1.4 terminal half-lives (derived), so the terminal phase is
  weakly informed. Q and Vp have the highest BSV and shrinkage.
- All patients also received carboplatin and ondansetron.
- Several small misprints in the paper are listed above.
