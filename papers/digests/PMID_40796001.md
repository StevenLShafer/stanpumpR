# Kengo 2025: atazanavir/ritonavir + rifampicin popPK (DERIVE)

## Study
- DERIVE (NCT04121195): open-label, single-arm, dose-escalation, Joint Clinical Research Centre, Uganda. 26 adults with HIV (23 female, 88%), virally suppressed on ATV/r second-line ART >=6 months, no TB. Median age 44 y (range 23-61), median weight 67 kg (range 50-75) (abstract and p.3474 text; Table 1 text layer is scrambled).
- Four visits: (1) ATV/r 300/100 mg QD (day 7); (2) + rifampicin 600 mg QD and dolutegravir 50 mg BID (day 21); (3) ATV/r 300/100 mg BID + rifampicin 600 mg (day 28); (4) ATV/r BID + rifampicin 1200 mg QD (day 35). Plasma sampling pre-dose, 0.5, 1, 2, 4, 6, 8, 12 h (plus 24 h at visit 1); PBMC trough samples at visits 1, 3, 4 and at 12 h at visit 2.
- Assays: HPLC-MS/MS, plasma LLOQ 0.030 mg/L atazanavir, 0.005 mg/L ritonavir; PBMC LLOQ 0.015 mg/L. Total (not free) drug. 857 plasma samples; 3% atazanavir and 2% ritonavir below LLOQ (imputed at LLOQ/2 with inflated additive error).
- External data: ACTG A5231, 13 healthy volunteers (8 male), median age 30 y, 75 kg, atazanavir 300 mg BID (+/- rifampicin 600 mg; one period atazanavir 400 mg BID), 355 samples, no ritonavir.
- NONMEM 7.5.1, FOCE-I; SIR for CIs.

## Model and parameters
Atazanavir and ritonavir each: two-compartment, first-order elimination, transit-compartment absorption, PBMC modelled as an effect compartment on the central compartment (equilibration t1/2 and pseudo-partition coefficient). Clearances and volumes allometrically scaled by fat-free mass; values are for the typical participant (Table 2 footnote: 67 kg, FFM 42 kg). Rifampicin: previously published one-compartment model with saturable hepatic elimination, re-estimated (Table S1, not available).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- No effect-site model for a clinical PD end point. The "effect compartment" is a PBMC distribution compartment: equilibration t1/2 atazanavir 0.963 h (0.546-1.51), ritonavir 1.40 h (1.38-1.63); pseudo-partition coefficient (PBMC:plasma) atazanavir 0.653 (0.538-0.797), ritonavir 1.68 (0.643-1.75). Unaffected by rifampicin (Table 2, p.3476; text p.3475). No ke0 or tPeak.
- Targets used in simulation: protein-adjusted IC90 0.014 mg/L (Ctrough) and a TDM trough of 0.15 mg/L (called MEC in Figure 3/4 captions; ref 9 in the paper, total plasma concentration). These are cited from other sources, not derived here.
- Simulated trough attainment (1225 virtual African HIV/TB adults, p.3477): QD alone 100% >0.014 and 95.4% >0.15 mg/L; QD + rifampicin 72.8% and 2.83%; BID + rifampicin 600 mg 99% and 94%.
- Not an opioid paper: no MEAC, equianalgesic ratio or hysteresis.

## Covariates
FFM allometry on all CL and V (tested against total weight). Rifampicin/regimen as categorical effects on CL, F and ka. Tenofovir DF had no significant effect (CL: dOFV 0.64; F: dOFV 2.66). Higher rifampicin dose (1200 mg) had no further effect on atazanavir CL or F.

## Caveats
Weight limited to 50-75 kg, 88% female; rifampicin 600/1200 mg flat dose (below the proposed 35 mg/kg); rifampicin given without other TB drugs; PBMC data sparse; fixed-dose ATV/r means ritonavir inhibition cannot be separated (explicit ritonavir-inhibition model gave no gain). The A5231 (no ritonavir) atazanavir CL was 2-fold higher than DERIVE visit 1 (p.3476): this comparator is DERIVE visit 1 with ritonavir.
