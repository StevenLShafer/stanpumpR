# Aptiom label 2024: container labels only (NDA 022416/S-021)

**Full label now archived (2026-10-27).** The original approval prescribing information (Revised 11/2013) is
`NJ_label-aptiom_022416_2013-11.pdf`, digested in `NJ_label-aptiom_022416_2013-11.md`: its section 12.3 gives the
apparent half-life of eslicarbazepine as 13-20 h in epilepsy patients and V/F 61 L at 70 kg, so the repository's
"label 13-20 h" matches that label. The 2024 prescribing information itself is still not archived.

**Source file problem.** The archived PDF is not the Aptiom prescribing information: PDF pp. 1-2 are bottle-label
artwork and p. 3 is the FDA signature page (confirmed on page images 2026-10-14). It contains no section 12.3, no
half-life and no dosing. No pharmacokinetic number in this card was verified from it; the "13-20 h" half-life the
repo attributes to the label is unverified (the card's remark that the PI gives it is from general knowledge, not
from any archived file). The only facts checked here are the tablet strengths and that they are stated as
eslicarbazepine acetate.

## Study
Not a study. The archived PDF is 3 pages:
- Page 1 (NDA 022416/S-021 "Page 3"): bottle-label artwork for 200 mg (NDC 63402-202-30, 30 tablets) and
  400 mg (NDC 63402-204-30, 30 tablets).
- Page 2 ("Page 4"): bottle labels for 600 mg (NDC 63402-206-60, 60 tablets) and 800 mg (NDC 63402-208-30,
  30 tablets).
- Page 3: FDA digital signature block (Joyce Crich, 6/20/2024).

Each label states: each tablet contains the stated mg of eslicarbazepine acetate; "once daily"; store at
20-25 °C; see package insert for dosage; manufactured for Sumitomo Pharma America, product of Switzerland.

The full prescribing information (Dosage and Administration, section 12.3 Pharmacokinetics, Use in Specific
Populations) is **not** in this file.
