# Gidal 2003: valproate dose and concentration dependence of lamotrigine clearance inhibition

## Study
- Open-label, randomized, four parallel arms, healthy non-smoking adults (> 18 y), each subject their own control (p. 86-87). Enrolled 32; 30 analysable on Day 1, 27 on Day 9 (protocol violations); 26 women, 6 men, age 34 +/- 13 y, weight 78 +/- 23 kg (mean +/- SD, all subjects; p. 88).
- Lamotrigine 25 mg single oral dose on Day 1 (fasted), serum sampled 0-120 h (0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 10, 24, 48, 72, 96, 120 h). From Day 5, valproate for 8 days: 125 mg qd, 250 mg qd, 375 mg (the Methods text says "350 mg (qd)"; abstract, Results and Table 1 say 375) or 500 mg (250 mg bid). Day 9: lamotrigine 25 mg again, same sampling, valproate continued to the last draw.
- Assays: lamotrigine by time-resolved immunofluorometric assay (DELFIA, GSK; standard curve 0.4-4.0 ug/mL, CV < 10.5% at 4 ug/mL); valproate by Abbott AxSym (CV < 4.3% at 0.7 ug/mL). Serum, total concentrations.
- Non-compartmental analysis (WinNonlin 3.0): AUC0-inf, CL/F, t1/2; geometric mean (95% CI). Percent inhibition = (CL/F Day 1 - CL/F Day 9)/CL/F Day 1 x 100 (Eq. 1). Simple Emax model of percent inhibition against each subject's valproate concentration, the average of the 0 h and 24 h values on Day 9 (Eq. 2; p. 87-88). Sponsor: GlaxoSmithKline.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: percent inhibition of lamotrigine apparent oral clearance (a PK interaction, no clinical end point). Emax 64.9%, EC50 5.6 ug/mL total valproate (Table 2; abstract "approximately 65%" and "approximately 5.6"). Fig. 3 shows the observed points with the fitted curve; one subject shows negative inhibition.
- Arm means plateau at 50-53% from 250 mg/day (13.9 ug/mL) upward; 125 mg/day gives 31% (Results p. 90). Discussion: near-maximal above about 20 ug/mL (about 250 mg/day); below 5.5 ug/mL (about 125 mg/day) less than half-maximal (the Discussion writes 5.5 where Table 2 gives 5.6).
- No ke0 or time course of onset; onset was sampled only after 4 days of valproate.

## Covariates
None; one fit to all Day 9 subjects.

## Caveats
- Healthy volunteers, single 25 mg lamotrigine doses (linearity assumed from the literature), small arms (5-9), 8-day valproate exposure; the authors extrapolate to therapeutic doses.
- The Emax rests on concentrations below 60 ug/mL; the asymptote at therapeutic levels is extrapolated, and the 95% CI of EC50 (0.8-10.4) is wide.
- Methods/Results inconsistency on the third arm (350 vs 375 mg/day); the valproate concentration metric is described three ways (steady-state, trough, mean of 0 and 24 h).
- The authors cite Gidal 2000 and Kanner 2000, in which valproate concentrations in the therapeutic range showed no relation to lamotrigine clearance, consistent with saturation.
