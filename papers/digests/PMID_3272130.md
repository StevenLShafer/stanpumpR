# Jacobs 1988: analytical solution of the three-compartment model

## Study
Short communication (Duke University, Anesthesiology); received July 1987. No subjects or data. Derives exact time-domain equations for the 3-compartment open mammillary model with zero-order input to and elimination from the central compartment, as an alternative to discrete approximations (bilinear transform, Runge-Kutta) used in simulators and CACI-type pumps.

## Model and parameters
- Fig. 1 / Eqs. 1-3: differential equations written in **concentrations** C1-C3 (ng/mL) with rate constants k10, k12, k13, k21, k31 (1/min), infusion k0(t) in ug/kg/min and V1 in L/kg.
- Eqs. 4-6: Laplace transforms with initial concentrations C1', C2', C3'.
- Eq. 7: central concentration vs infusion rate; cubic denominator roots s1, s2, s3 equal -lambda1, -lambda2, -lambda3 of the standard triexponential Cp(t) = A1 e^-l1t + A2 e^-l2t + A3 e^-l3t. Roots may be found by Vieta's trigonometric method (ref 7).
- Eq. 8: C1(t) for a constant Rate from t0 to t (inverse Laplace by tables). Eqs. 9-10: C2(t), C3(t), needed to supply new initial conditions whenever the rate changes.
- Algorithm (p. 764): start with all initial concentrations 0; when Rate changes at t', compute C1, C2, C3 at t' from Eqs. 8-10, reset t0 = t', continue. Use double precision.

## Caveats
More computation per point than discrete methods, but no aliasing, less round-off, no frequency warping, solutions only at times of interest; serves as the standard for checking approximations (Discussion). Method stated to generalise to other compartment structures. The extracted text of Eqs. 7-10 is unreadable; their content is described from the narrative only.
