# Humalog (insulin lispro) U-100 / U-200 US label, May 2025

The PDF is the label it is named as: Humalog U-100 and U-200 full prescribing information, "Revised: 05/2025",
63 pages of which pages 18-63 are the Patient Information and the Instructions for Use for the vial, cartridge, KwikPen,
Junior KwikPen and Tempo Pen. It does not cover Humalog Mix 50/50 or 75/25, which have their own labels.

## Study
Label. Routes (2.2, PDF p2-3):
- SC injection, U-100 or U-200, within 15 min before or right after a meal; pens dial 1 U steps to 60 U (Junior 0.5 to 30 U).
- U-100 may be diluted (U-10, U-50) for SC use under supervision.
- **Continuous SC infusion (pump): Humalog U-100 only.** "Do NOT administer HUMALOG U-200 using a continuous subcutaneous
  infusion pump"; check the pump manual for compatibility; change the reservoir at least every 7 days; do not dilute or
  mix in the pump; not above 37 C. Repeated in 5.8 (PDF p5) and 17 (PDF p16).
- IV: U-100 only, diluted to 0.1-1.0 U/mL in 0.9% saline, under supervision; U-200 never IV.
- 2.5 mixing: U-100 with NPH only, by SC injection; not mixed in a pump; U-200 with nothing.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Equipotent with human insulin on a molar basis; 1 U lispro = glucose-lowering effect of 1 U regular insulin (12.2,
PDF p9). Clamp GIR AUC 126 g (U-100) and 125 g (U-200). IV infusion in 21 T1D patients: mean 129 +/- 14 min to near
normoglycaemia (PDF p10).

## Covariates
Effects of age, sex, race, obesity, pregnancy and smoking on PK not studied (12.3, PDF p12). Renal and hepatic impairment:
no PK difference in type 2 diabetic patients, but greater sensitivity; more monitoring advised (8.6, 8.7, 12.3).
Pregnancy (8.1): no association with major birth defects in published data; no PK change stated.

## Caveats
- Dose-dependent IV clearance and volume (two dose groups only).
- Pump use is conditional on the pump's own labelling ("refer to the ... pump user manual to see if HUMALOG can be used").
