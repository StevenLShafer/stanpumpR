# Galea 2011: IV anakinra (IL-1RA) dose-ranging in SAH

## Study
- Phase I/II dose-ranging, 25 adults with aneurysmal subarachnoid haemorrhage and an external ventricular drain who received the drug (88 screened, 29 recruited, 4 not dosed; Figure 1, PDF p3). Sampling and PK were done at Salford (22) and Cambridge (3); CSF data from the 3 Cambridge patients were dropped for plasma contamination.
- Demographics (Table 1, PDF p3, read on the image): 7 men : 18 women, mean weight 73.4 kg (range 58-110 kg), mean age 53 y (range 37-76, text p2), 100% Caucasian. Dosed a mean of 4 days (2-12) after the bleed. 17 poor grade, 8 good grade.
- Five sequential regimes, 5 patients each: IV bolus over 1 min then a 4 h infusion in 500 mL saline. Bolus/infusion (mg, mg/kg/h): 100/4, 200/2, 300/2, 400/6, 500/10 (Table 2).
- Plasma and CSF sampled at 10, 20, 30, 45, 60, 90, 120, 240, 300, 360, 540 min and days 1, 2, 3, 7. Assay: in-house immunoassay for IL-1RA, calibrated to NIBSC standards (total IL-1RA, endogenous plus drug). NONMEM IV for the remodelling, NONMEM VI for refining the earlier model; simulation in GNU-R.

## Model and parameters
Structural model: two-compartment linear plasma model with elimination from the central compartment, and a one-compartment CSF model (the model of Gueorguieva 2008, refitted). **No parameter table is printed**: the paper reports the model form only, and only these numbers:

Table 2 (PDF p4) gives a priori predicted values: mean peak plasma concentration 34,772 / 41,152 / 61,728 / 82,304 / 102,880 ng/mL and total dose 1220 / 760 / 860 / 2080 / 3300 mg for regimes 1-5, and time to reach 100 ng/mL in CSF of 104 / 141 / 96 / 43 / 30 min. The total doses equal bolus + 4 h x rate x 70 kg (derived), so the table assumes a 70 kg patient. Predictions, not observations.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No pharmacodynamic end point was measured. The target was a CSF IL-1RA of 100 ng/mL within 1 h of ischaemia onset, taken from the rat transient-MCAO neuroprotection work (Clark 2008, cited in Introduction p2); it is an experimental animal-derived target, not a human effect concentration. CSF is the sole index of brain penetration (brain extracellular fluid not measurable). Observed regime 5: 70.4 ng/mL at 30 min (below the prediction), 135.9 ng/mL at 60 min; abstract says the target was reached within 45 min. Opioid audit: not an opioid; no MEAC, ke0, tPeak or potency ratio.

## Covariates
None modelled. Linear mixed-model analysis of regime and time on neutrophil count, temperature and creatinine only (safety). The CSF fraction did not differ among regimes, which the authors read as largely passive transport at these doses. Cambridge CSF data excluded.

## Caveats
- 25 poor- and good-grade SAH patients with drains; likely a leakier blood-brain/CSF barrier than the general stroke population (authors). Endogenous IL-1RA is not separated from drug in the assay and matters at low terminal concentrations.
- CSF concentrations at 30 min were below prediction; the authors' updated simulation says 1 g bolus + 10 mg/kg/h would reach 100 ng/mL in CSF in 30 min.
- Final parameters are not given here; the full fit is Ogungbenro 2016 (digest PMID_26476629).
- Internal inconsistencies: 29 recruited in Figure 1 vs "30 recruited" in the text; CSF crossing 1.6% vs 0.97-1.27%.
