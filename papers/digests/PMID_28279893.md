# Rhee 2017: levetiracetam population PK in Korean patients

## Study
- Retrospective, Comprehensive Epilepsy Center, Seoul National University Hospital, Feb 2011 - Jan 2014 (p. 9).
- 425 patients with epilepsy on oral immediate-release levetiracetam (Keppra) at steady state; 483 concentrations
  (mostly one per patient). 206 M / 219 F; age 37.9 +/- 14.6 y (median 35, range 16-85); weight 65.0 +/- 13.9 kg
  (median 62.8, 40.0-123.6); height 165.5 cm; serum creatinine 0.8 (0.4-2.2) mg/dL; eGFR (MDRD) 97.8 +/- 21.5,
  median 96.0 (29.0-203.7) mL/min/1.73 m2 (Table 1). Dose 125-4000 mg/day.
- Co-medication (Table 1, reassigned): oxcarbazepine 143 (33.6 %), topiramate 112 (26.4 %), valproate 108 (25.4 %),
  carbamazepine 83 (19.5 %), lamotrigine 69 (16.2 %), clobazam 45 (10.6 %), pregabalin 40 (9.4 %), phenobarbital 28
  (6.6 %), zonisamide 20 (4.7 %), phenytoin 13 (3.1 %). The text confirms the first three percentages.
- NONMEM 7.2, FOCE-I, log-transformed data; one compartment, first-order absorption; ka FIXED at 2.44 /h from
  Pigeolet 2007; bootstrap 1000; VPC.

CL/F (L/h) = 3.9 x (WT/70)^0.70 x (eGFR/90)^0.44; V/F (L) = 65.3 x (WT/70) (p. 9, Table 2).
(derived: typical half-life = ln2 x 65.3 / 3.9 = 11.6 h at 70 kg, eGFR 90.)
CL/F falls 26.0, 41.4 and 57.7 % at eGFR 60-90, 30-60 and < 30 (Discussion). Co-medication not significant.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Exploratory dose-response only: > 95 % of seizure-free patients took <= 2000 mg/day; model-predicted troughs did not
differ between seizure-free and seizure groups. Quotes a therapeutic range 12-46 mg/L (trough 6-20 mg/L)
(Introduction). No exposure-response model. Not an opioid paper.

## Covariates
Weight (power 0.70 on CL/F, linear on V/F) and MDRD eGFR (power 0.44 on CL/F). Age and sex were significant
individually but left out as captured by eGFR. Background: about 66 % of a dose excreted unchanged in urine,
> 95 % absorbed, not protein bound (Introduction, Discussion).

## Caveats
- Sparse unplanned sampling; ka fixed; V/F poorly determined (bootstrap upper CI 124 L, IIV 60.8 %).
- V/F 65.3 L (0.93 L/kg) is large compared with healthy-volunteer Vd (about 0.5-0.6 L/kg, e.g. Ramael 2006), so
  the implied half-life (11.6 h) exceeds the 6-8 h usually reported.
- Korean patients; includes adolescents from 16 y; adherence not formally measured.
