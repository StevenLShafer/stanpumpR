# Guay 1988: codeine IV and oral in healthy volunteers and haemodialysis patients

## Study
- 6 healthy volunteers (CrCl > 90 mL/min; age 27.8 ± 3.7 y, 73.1 ± 11.6 kg, 174.0 ± 7.3 cm; 4 M : 2 F) and 6 chronic haemodialysis patients (45.5 ± 18.9 y, 81.5 ± 23.2 kg, 168.3 ± 13.0 cm; 3 M : 3 F).
- Randomised crossover, ≥ 1 week washout: 60 mg oral codeine sulfate (= 51.6 mg base) with 240 mL water; 60 mg IV codeine phosphate (= 45.2 mg base) infused over 15 min. Fasted. Dialysis patients studied off-dialysis.
- Blood at 0.17-12 h and 24 h; urine 0-24 h in fractions.
- Assay: specific radioimmunoassays for free codeine and free morphine; total (conjugated + free) after enzymatic hydrolysis. Codeine antiserum cross-reactivity: codeine-6-glucuronide 0.14%, morphine 0.63%, M6G 0.01%, norcodeine 20%. M3G and M6G not separated.
- Model-independent analysis: terminal k from the 10, 12 and 24 h points; AUC to 24 h plus C24/k; CL = dose (as base)/AUC; Vss and MRT by statistical moments. Mean ± SD; Mann-Whitney U tests.
- PD: respiratory rate, BP, heart rate, consciousness, pupillary light reactivity; pupil diameter by photography before and at 2, 5, 7, 10 h.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End points: pupil diameter (photography) and vital signs. No statistically or clinically significant differences between groups. No concentration-effect model, no ke0, tPeak or hysteresis.
- No MEAC. The Discussion cites toxic plasma codeine > 500 ng/mL (literature) and predicts four of six dialysis patients would exceed it at steady state on 60 mg every 6 h.
- Analgesia was not assessed. No potency ratio.

## Covariates
Renal failure (group comparison only). No correlation of age with any PK parameter.

## Caveats
- n = 6 per group. RIA, which later HPLC studies (Chen 1991) suggest overstates codeine half-life; Chen attributes Guay's longer half-life to nonspecific RIA.
- Terminal slope from only three late points; MRT and Vss after a 15-min infusion.
- Multiple-dose extension stopped for severe nausea and vomiting in the first two dialysis patients.
