# Stoschus 2025: phenobarbital popPK in refractory / super-refractory status epilepticus (LMU Munich)

## Study
- Retrospective, Neurointensive Care Unit, LMU University Hospital Munich, 2015-2024; adults >= 18 y with RSE or
  SRSE and phenobarbital TDM; hypoxic-ischaemic encephalopathy excluded.
- 37 patients: 24 male, 13 female; age median 63 y (5-95%: 24-83); height 173 cm; total weight 78.5 kg
  (51.7-93.3); **IBW median 68.8 kg** (53.3-81.9; cohort range 52.4-82.4); SRSE 95% (Table 1).
- Dosing: loading 10-20 mg/kg, maintenance 1-4 mg/kg; IV as 5-min infusions; oral tablets dispersed in water via
  gastric tube (66% of doses enteral). Median treatment 16 days.
- 301 trough TDM samples (Mon/Wed/Fri), median 6 per patient; median concentration 32.8 mg/L. HPLC-UV, 0.6-150 mg/L,
  serum.
- MONOLIX 2024R1 (SAEM); bootstrap 1000; pcVPC; simulations in mrgsolve (12-h schedule chosen).
- IBW by the formula of Brower et al. (ARDSNet predicted body weight, NEJM 2000).

## Model and parameters
One compartment, first-order absorption and elimination; IIV on V and CL; IOV on CL (occasion = interval between
samples, median 48 h); proportional residual error. ka fixed at 1.9 /h (Viswanathan; absorption phase not sampled).
Covariate: IBW, centred on the median 68.8 kg: V_i = V x (IBW_i/68.8)^1 (Eq. 1); CL_i = CL x (IBW_i/68.8)^0.75
(Eq. 2). IBW lowered OFV by 71.9. Table 2 (estimate; RSE%; 95% CI):

Per kg on median total weight 78.5 kg: V 0.44 L/kg, CL 0.0048 L/h/kg (Results). Bootstrap medians: V 35.2 L,
CL 0.37 L/h. Other covariates (age, sex, renal replacement, creatinine, liver tests, valproate, phenytoin,
metamizole, cenobamate) not significant.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD model. Target trough 18-40 mg/L for SRSE (concentrations > 18 mg/L linked to seizure control; 10-40 mg/L
quoted as the conventional range); > 70 mg/L described as toxic. Table 3 (q12h, by IBW 55-80 kg): IV load
1000-1300 mg, maintenance 100-150 mg q12h; e.g. IBW 70 kg: load 1100 mg, maintenance 150 mg. Target attainment
only about 43-45%. Not an opioid.

## Covariates
IBW (allometric, exponents 1 and 0.75) only.

## Caveats
n = 37, retrospective, 9-year inclusion; trough-only sampling (ka fixed); high IIV; co-medication effects
(valproate, phenytoin) may be absorbed into CL; enteral route via gastric tube in ICU patients; adults only.
Nelson's healthy-volunteer F (94.9%) cited as agreement.
