# Donnelly 1993: effect-compartment linear model of amlodipine's blood-pressure response

## Study
- **Population (Methods, PDF p2, printed p. 304):** 12 patients with mild to moderate essential hypertension
  (160/90 to 210/115 mmHg), 6 men and 6 women, age 25 to 64 years; weight not given. Entry BP after at least 6 weeks
  off treatment 175/103 ± 24/5 mmHg. Glasgow.
- **Design:** single-blind 3-week placebo period, within which single doses of amlodipine 5 mg and placebo were given
  double-blind in random order at the ends of weeks 1 and 3; then amlodipine 5 mg once daily for 6 weeks with a
  third identical study day. Oral, with 100 mL water, fasting.
- **Sampling:** BP (supine after ≥10 min; erect after 2 and 4 min standing; Accutorr) and venous plasma at 0, ½, 1,
  1½, 2, 2½, 3, 3½, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10 and 12 h, and at 24, 32 and 48 h. Ambulatory BP in 6 patients.
- **Assay:** plasma amlodipine by gas-liquid chromatography with electron-capture detection; inter/intra-assay CV
  8% / 6.5%; limit of detection 1 ng/mL. Total (not free) plasma concentration.
- **Analysis:** AUC by linear trapezoids, t½ by log-linear regression; one-compartment first-order-input model
  fitted per patient simultaneously to first-dose and steady-state data; then an effect compartment, with linear
  (E = m·Ce + i) and Langmuir Emax models fitted to placebo-corrected erect systolic BP by nonlinear least squares.
  Linear chosen in all patients by the general linear F test. Values are mean ± SD (Methods, PDF p3).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- **End point:** placebo-subtracted change in erect systolic BP, measured "erect (3 minutes)" (Methods, PDF p3; the
  sampling paragraph says readings were taken at 2 and 4 min standing).
- **Table II (PDF p6, printed p. 308, rendered), per patient m (mmHg per ng/mL) and ke0 (/h):** 1: -3.5, 0.74;
  2: -3.0, 0.10; 3: -4.0, 0.27; 4: -3.5, 0.13; 5: -2.7, 0.29; 6: no values; 7: -2.8, 0.15; 8: -1.6, 1.20;
  9: -2.4, 0.46; 10: -4.9, 0.13; 11: -2.5, 1.04; 12: -2.8, 1.23. Mean ± SD: m -3.1 ± 0.9, ke0 0.52 ± 0.45.
- t½ke0 is not reported (derived from the mean ke0: about 1.3 h; individual values span about 0.6 to 7 h).
- Hysteresis (counter-clockwise loop of fall in erect SBP against plasma concentration) shown for one patient at
  steady state (Fig. 3, PDF p5).
- No Emax or EC50: the Langmuir Emax model was fitted but rejected in every patient.
- **Concentration range (figures, read by eye, not tabulated):** in the three representative patients, first-dose
  concentrations reach about 2-2.5 ng/mL and steady-state concentrations about 3 to 11 ng/mL (Fig. 2, PDF p5);
  Fig. 3's patient spans about 8 to 11 ng/mL with falls of about 25 to 32 mmHg.
- Group BP: no significant fall over the first-dose 10-h day; significant at 24 and 48 h. At 6 weeks, mean 0-10 h
  falls against placebo 12/9 mmHg supine and 15/12 mmHg erect (PDF p3). Heart rate rose 2.5 (supine) and 4 (erect)
  beats/min after the first dose, peak at 5-9 h, not at steady state.
- Fit quality: PK r² 0.992 / 0.834 / 0.935 (best/worst/average); PD r² 0.970 / 0.752 / 0.850 (PDF p4).
- No potency ratio. Not an opioid paper.

## Covariates
m was not related to age or pretreatment plasma renin activity; it correlated with pretreatment blood pressure
(r = 0.71) (PDF p4). No covariate equations.

## Caveats
- 12 patients (11 with PD estimates), one dose level; weight not reported; total plasma concentration.
- Linear model with an unreported intercept and no ceiling; valid only over the narrow concentrations seen.
- ke0 is very variable (CV about 87%, derived) and onset after the first dose was too gradual for the authors to
  quantify the initial response (PDF p6).
- Erect timing stated as both "2 and 4 minutes" and "3 minutes".
