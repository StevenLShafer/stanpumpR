# Hsu 2018: pegylated liposomal doxorubicin joint PK model and BE metrics

## Study
Re-analysis of a randomized, open-label, two-way crossover bioequivalence study from a dossier submitted to the Taiwan regulatory authority (Methods, PDF p. 3). Female patients with ovarian cancer (number, age, weight not printed). Doxil/Caelyx (reference) single dose 50 mg/m2 as a 1-h IV infusion. Samples pre-dose and at 0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 1, 2, 4, 6, 12, 24, 48, 72, 168, 240, 336 h. Liposome-encapsulated, free and total doxorubicin were measured (assay not described). Reference-product data only were used to build the model: NONMEM 7.2, FOCE, ADVAN6 TOL 3, WfN 7.3, log-transformed data with additive error; encapsulated and free fitted simultaneously; bootstrap 200 replicates (196 converged). The model was then used for 500 virtual BE studies per scenario with the release rate (Rel) of the test product varied from 0.50 to 1.50 x reference.

## Model and parameters
Encapsulated doxorubicin: one compartment (V1), infusion rate k0 x E%; emptied by release (Rel) into the free central compartment and by RES uptake (CLres, estimated near the zero boundary and fixed at 0). Free doxorubicin: two compartments (V2, V3, Q), clearance CLfree, plus an unencapsulated fraction k0 x (1-E) entering V2 directly (Fig. 2, PDF p. 5). Total = encapsulated + free. Derived (derived: ln2 x V1 / Rel = 0.693 x 2.52 / 0.0212): release half-life about 82 h. Encapsulated concentrations were about 100-fold higher than free (Results, PDF p. 4).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No ke0, EC50, MEAC or concentration-effect relation. The simulations compare BE metrics (Cmax, AUC0-t, partial AUCs to 24, 48, 72, 96 h) of each analyte for discriminating changes in Rel (Table II, PDF p. 8). Free-drug pAUC0-24h and pAUC0-48h were the most responsive when Rel(test)/Rel(reference) fell; encapsulated AUC0-t was most responsive when it rose. The paper states that the link between partial AUC and clinical safety or efficacy is unestablished. Not an opioid paper.

## Covariates
None. No covariate was tested or reported.

## Caveats
One study only (author's own limitation, p. 9). Free doxorubicin kinetics are release-limited ("flip-flop" kinetics: Rel 0.0212 L/h against CLfree 2.68 L/h), so CLfree and the volumes are not comparable with conventional doxorubicin; the author says as much (PDF p. 6-7). Free doxorubicin Tmax ranges 0.5-168 h, with a double peak in some subjects. V3 has %RSE 33.6. The number and demographics of subjects are not printed. The dose is given as 50 mg/m2 in Methods and 50 mg in the Fig. 6 caption.
