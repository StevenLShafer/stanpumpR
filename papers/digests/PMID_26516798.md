# Absalom 2016: TCI, a mature technology

## Study
Review/survey. Literature search (PubMed, Google Scholar to January 2015, chapters, abstracts) for non-approved TCI software; questionnaire (Appendix 1) to device makers selling TCI pumps 2004-2013; usage estimates from one hospital (UMCG, week of 24-30 Nov 2014, x 48.5) and from the UK/Ireland NAP5 activity survey (Sept 2013). Companion to Struys 2016 and Schnider 2016.

## Model and parameters
None. Table 2 lists the models in commercial pumps: propofol Marsh and Schnider (adult), Kataria and Paedfusor (pediatric), Jung (Aquafol); remifentanil Minto; sufentanil Gepts; alfentanil Maitre (Fresenius: Scott & Stanski); fentanyl Shafer et al. 1990 (Arcomed, Veryark); midazolam Greenblatt (Veryark).

Table 1 (non-approved systems, first-last publication year, number of publications):
CATIA/IVA-SIM 1982-2014, 24; TIAC 1985-1992, 10; CACI/CACI II 1985-2001, 18; Tackley (Bristol) 1989-1999, 2; MINA/Infusion Toolbox 1989-2006, 15; Diprifusor prototypes (Kenny, White) 1991-2013, 43; **STANPUMP (Shafer) 1990-2014, 268**; Leiden platform 1992-2003, 13; PAMO 1997-2005, 9; RUGLOOP 1998-2014, 93; STELPUMP 1999-2012, 51; Bonn platform 2006, 1; AnestFusor 2009-2014, 7; Asan Pump 2010-2013, 5. Total 559 studies (Supplemental Digital Content 1).

Other numbers: first-generation (Diprifusor) about 25,000 units, approval in >50 countries (15,000 before 2004, 10,000 in 2004-2013); second-generation first approved 2003, >36,000 sold 2004-2013 (abstract; "almost 37,000" in text from 5 reporting companies); TCI available in at least 96 countries (Appendix 2; Canada partial); UMCG about 10,800 TCI patients/yr; NAP5: TCI in 237,000 of 303,000 TIVA/sedation patients (78%); extrapolated 2.6 million/yr in Europe, about 5 million worldwide. In the US TCI is only possible with research software in IRB-approved studies (abstract).
