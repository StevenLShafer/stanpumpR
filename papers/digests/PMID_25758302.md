# Johannessen Landmark 2015: gabapentin and pregabalin TDM in Norway

## Study
Short communication. Retrospective anonymous data from the routine TDM service of the National Center for
Epilepsy, Oslo, 2009-2013, most recent measurement per patient; serum, drug-fasting morning samples at assumed
steady state (troughs); validated LC methods; plus Norwegian Prescription Database use figures (PDF p2). Gabapentin
189 patients (66% women, 125 W / 64 M; mean age 53), pregabalin 167 (56% women, 93 W / 74 M; mean age 50). Clinical
effect and tolerability data were not available. Indication stated in 26% (epilepsy assumed where other AEDs were
measured, about 50%).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None measured. The authors suggest a gabapentin lower limit may be unnecessary and the reference range needs
re-evaluation; some pregabalin patients tolerate or need concentrations above the range. C/D-ratio >100-fold
variable for both drugs. Gabapentin mean bioavailability 49% quoted (Gidal 2000). Not an opioid.

## Covariates
Age (elderly C/D higher: gabapentin 92%, pregabalin higher), sex (pregabalin), enzyme-inducing comedication
(carbamazepine, phenobarbital, phenytoin: C/D 38% lower for gabapentin, 35% lower for pregabalin; unexplained for
renally cleared drugs).

## Caveats
- 34 of 167 pregabalin patients above 30 umol/L is 20.4% (derived), against the text's 18%.
- Figure 1a's dashed reference lines sit near 85 and 128 umol/L, not at the stated 70-120 (read from the image).
- No effect data, mixed indications, retrospective, adherence unknown.
