# Tsirizani 2024: once-daily darunavir/ritonavir in African children (CHAPAS-4)

## Study
- Nested intensive PK sub-study in CHAPAS-4 (ISRCTN22964075), open-label randomised trial of second-line boosted PI regimens; Zambia, Uganda, Zimbabwe; Jan 2019-Mar 2021. N=59 children aged 3-15 y, weight >=14 kg, failing first-line; median age 10.9 y (3.8-14.7), weight 26.0 kg (14.5-47.0), 56% female (Table 1, PDF p3).
- Dosing: darunavir/ritonavir 600/100 mg once daily for 14-24.9 kg, 800/100 mg for >=25 kg, oral, with a light breakfast; NRTI backbone TAF/FTC (n=34), ABC/3TC (n=12), ZDV/3TC (n=13). Darunavir dose median 26.7 mg/kg (17.0-41.4).
- Steady-state sampling at week 6: 0, 1, 2, 4, 6, 8, 12, 24 h (plus 0.5 h for TAF arm). 491 darunavir concentrations; 1 below LLOQ. Assay HPLC, total plasma darunavir, LLOQ 0.105 mg/L; ritonavir LLOQ 0.045 mg/L; AAG immunoturbidimetric, LLOQ 0.10 g/L.
- NCA in WinNonlin 8.4; PopPK in NONMEM 7.5.1 (FOCE-I), SIR for uncertainty.

## Model and parameters
Two-compartment disposition, transit-compartment absorption, first-order elimination, F fixed at 1, allometric scaling of CL (0.75) and V (1) on total body weight (preferred over fat-free mass, dOFV 27.9 vs 2.62). Typical values are for a 26 kg child (Table 3, PDF p5). Parameters are apparent (not per kg). BSV on clearance 32.4%; BOV on F, MTT, ka. Residual error proportional + additive.

NCA (Table 2, PDF p4, geometric mean (CV%)), total n=59: AUC0-24 94.3 (50) mg·h/L; Ctrough 1.5 (111) mg/L; Cmax 9.1 (35) mg/L; Tmax 4 h (IQR 3.9-4.0); T1/2 9.1 h (54) (n=45); CL/F 7.4 L/h (54); Vd/F 97.0 L (67). Adults (ARTEMIS III, 800/100 mg): AUC median 69.4 (33.0-88.4), Ctrough mean 1.4 (0.5), Cmax mean 5.5 (1.3), Tmax median 3.0 (1.0-4.1), adult T1/2 not recorded (row ambiguous, see open questions). Ritonavir AUC0-24 9.1 (47), Cmax 1.1 (67) (Table 2 rows read from the image).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD model. Reference concentrations quoted from other work: wild-type EC50 0.055 mg/L, resistant-virus EC50 (Prezista label ref. 27) shown in Figure 2c, EC90 0.495 mg/L (text, PDF p4; protein-adjusted/total basis not stated). All children had Ctrough >0.055 mg/L; 86% above 0.495 mg/L. Simulated WHO 600/100 mg dosing: >99% above twice EC50 in every weight band. Ritonavir exposure and NRTI backbone had no significant effect on darunavir CL. Not an opioid.

## Covariates
AAG on CL: exponential, slope -0.582, dOFV 12.8 (explains part of the 32.4% BSV). A semi-mechanistic AAG binding model (Michaelis-Menten, priors from Schalkwijk) fitted slightly better (dOFV 11) but was not adopted. Weight allometry as above. Age, NRTI backbone, ritonavir AUC: not significant.

## Caveats
- Total (not unbound) darunavir only; ritonavir not varied enough to judge boosting; children only, 3.8-14.7 y; exposures above adult medians (not a deficiency). Table 3 CI for the AAG slope is printed in odd order. Adult reference is a different study (ARTEMIS III), so "higher" exposure means higher than those adults.
