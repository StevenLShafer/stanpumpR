# Zhang 2019: population PK of bupropion XL in healthy Chinese volunteers

PDF pages are the journal pages 339-352 (PDF page n = printed page 338 + n).

## Study
- Open-label, single-centre study (NCT02698553), Shanghai Mental Health Center, May-June 2016; sponsored by GSK China (p. 340).
- 16 healthy Chinese volunteers enrolled (8 men, 8 women), 15 completed (one withdrew for a drug-related rash). Age 26.0 (SD 4.95) y, median 24.5; BMI 22.3 (1.55) kg/m²; weight mean 62.3 (8.31) kg, **median 60.9 kg (range 51.2-82.2)** (Table 1, p. 342).
- Bupropion XL (Wellbutrin XL, extended release) 150 mg orally once daily days 1-5, then 300 mg once daily days 6-14 (p. 340). Doses as labelled (bupropion hydrochloride); the paper does not discuss salt.
- Plasma sampling: day 1 to 24 h; days 5 and 6 to 12-24 h; days 14-19 to 120 h (p. 340-341). Bupropion, hydroxybupropion, threo- and erythrohydrobupropion by validated LC-MS/MS (WuXi AppTec; details in supplement, not archived).
- NCA in Phoenix WinNonlin 6.3; population model (parent only) in NONMEM 7.3, FOCE-I, with concentrations and doses converted to molar units before fitting (p. 341).

## Model and parameters
Two compartments, first-order absorption with lag time, linear elimination; combined proportional + additive residual error (Fig. 4, Table 4, PDF p. 8 / p. 346, confirmed on the rendered page). All parameters are apparent (CL/F, Vc/F, Vp/F; p. 343). Weight on Vc only, as (WT/60.9)^θ; 60.9 kg is the study's median weight. No weight effect on CL.

Other NCA values (Table 2, rendered page, geometric mean (%CVb)): bupropion Cmax 49.9 (29.9) ng/mL day 1 (150 mg), 110.3 (38.9) day 6 (300 mg); Css_max 62.2 (150 mg) and 111.9 (300 mg) ng/mL; Css_av 30.9 and 48.6 ng/mL; Css_min 13.2 and 19.8 ng/mL; AUC0-24 498.2 / 741.6 / 1,184.6 / 1,165.7 h·ng/mL on days 1/5/6/14; median Tmax 5.0 h on every day. Hydroxybupropion Cmax 246.6 ng/mL day 1, Css_max 1,236.7 ng/mL day 14, AUC0-24 23,355.8 h·ng/mL day 14 (20-fold the parent's, p. 342). Threo- and erythrohydrobupropion t½ 43.2 and 36.2 h. Simulated single-dose AUC0-∞ 678.8 h·ng/mL (150 mg) (Table 5, p. 350), lower than SR data in Chinese (845.2-1,870) and Caucasian XL studies (740-1,051) (Table 7, p. 351; text layer).

Autoinduction (not in the final model): an Emax function of time on CL dropped OFV by 128.071; Emax 2.02-fold, T50 about 15.4 days (370 h) (p. 343). This is also visible in the NCA CL/F rising from 202 (day 5) to 257 L/h (day 14).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None measured. Not an opioid. The Discussion cites a mouse antidepressant screen: hydroxybupropion one-half, threo- and erythrohydrobupropion one-fifth as potent as bupropion (p. 346); no human potency data. No ke0, no therapeutic range.

## Covariates
- Body weight on Vc/F: (WT/60.9)^1.3 (Eq. 4, Table 4).
- Tested and not retained: age on lag (ΔOFV 4.79, driven by one 45-year-old), BMI on Vp (ΔOFV 9.424, correlated with weight), sex on CL (ΔOFV 9.002; men 246 L/h at 62.4 kg, women 200 L/h, 81.3% of men's; dropped as inconsistent with Caucasian data) (p. 343, 347).

## Caveats
- n = 16, young healthy Chinese adults, one site; titration design (150 then 300 mg), so single-dose 300 mg profiles exist only by simulation.
- All parameters apparent (no IV reference); F unknown. Vc/F of 2,530 L is very large, consistent with low F and/or the release profile being folded into the disposition.
- Autoinduction is supported by the data but excluded from the final model; the reported typical CL/F (221 L/h) therefore averages over days 1-19 of rising clearance.
- The VPC slightly underestimates variability at the peaks and overpredicts the elimination phase (p. 344).
- The 60.9 kg is the reference weight for Vc only; CL/F has no weight term.
