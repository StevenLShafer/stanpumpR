# Aantaa 1997: dexmedetomidine reduces isoflurane MAC

## Study
Randomised, parallel-group, placebo-controlled; local ethics approval. 49 women, ASA 1-2, age 31-55 yr, abdominal hysterectomy (Table 1, PDF p2): control n=16, low-dose n=17, high-dose n=16. Table 1, mean ± SEM: age 44.1 ± 1.14 / 45.4 ± 0.86 / 47.6 ± 0.93 yr (ANOVA p=0.049, the only demographic difference); height 164.8 / 165.5 / 164.7 cm; weight 67.1 ± 2.50 / 66.9 ± 2.74 / 67.9 ± 2.43 kg (p=0.96). Thiopental induction dose 252 ± 14.1 / 209.9 ± 9.78 / 178.3 ± 10.87 mg (p=0.0002). Isoflurane given 20.1 / 17.9 / 18.0 min before incision.

Dexmedetomidine: two-stage infusion, started 15 min before induction. Low group 75 ug/h for 15 min then 60 ug/h; high group 150 ug/h for 15 min then 120 ug/h (as printed, "u/h" for the first; designed for steady-state plasma targets 0.3 and 0.6 ng/mL, regimen from Dyck 1993, in healthy young male volunteers). Infusion continued until skin incision, at least 30 min allowed before incision.
Induction: alfentanil 15 ug/kg and glycopyrrolate 0.2 mg about 2 min before induction; thiopental 2 mg/kg plus 25 mg boluses every 15 s to loss of eyelid reflex; succinylcholine 1.5 mg/kg. Isoflurane started after intubation, ventilation with 50% oxygen in air.
MAC by Dixon up-down: first patient 1.2% end-tidal (control), 0.7% (low), 0.4% (high); step 0.1%; held at least 15 min before incision; movement = gross purposeful movement of limbs, head or body within 60 s of a vertical skin incision. Statistics: Dixon-Mood (median, SD, CI) and maximum-likelihood logistic regression.
Assays: dexmedetomidine plasma by GC-MS (LOD 10 pg/mL, intra-assay CV 3.3%); alfentanil by capillary GC (LOD 2 ng/mL, CV 3.1%); thiopental by HPLC (LOD 10 ng/mL, CV 5.2%). Samples before induction and at skin incision, plasma, ice, spun within 60 min, -70 C.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: movement response to skin incision (isoflurane MAC, end-tidal %), at steady state with the dexmedetomidine plasma concentration at incision. Table 2:
```yaml
parameters:
  - {name: MAC isoflurane Dixon, value: 0.85, unit: "% end-tidal", source: "Table 2", note: "control, median (SD 0.39), 95% CI 0.54-1.16"}
  - {name: MAC isoflurane Dixon, value: 0.55, unit: "% end-tidal", source: "Table 2", note: "low dexmedetomidine (plasma 0.37 ng/mL), SD 0.33, CI 0.31-0.79; 35% below control (text)"}
  - {name: MAC isoflurane Dixon, value: 0.45, unit: "% end-tidal", source: "Table 2", note: "high dexmedetomidine (plasma 0.69 ng/mL), SD 0.05, CI 0.41-0.49; 47% below control (text)"}
  - {name: MAC isoflurane logistic regression, value: 0.81, unit: "% end-tidal", source: "Table 2", note: "control; 0.51 low; 0.45 high; across-group p=0.06 as printed"}
```
- Control vs each dexmedetomidine group by logistic regression: p=0.022 (high) and 0.025 (low); low vs high p=0.47 (p1058).
- Control MAC (0.85%) is below the 1.2% the authors cite for similar patients; they attribute it to the thiopental and alfentanil induction (residual alfentanil 39-47 ng/mL, which they cite as able to lower isoflurane MAC by up to 50% under steady state, their ref 18). They say the effect cannot be separated from a possible alfentanil-dexmedetomidine spinal synergy.
- No ke0, t1/2 ke0, tPeak, hysteresis, EC50 or Emax is reported. The 30-min wait is assumed to give plasma-brain equilibrium; the authors note the concentration was still rising slightly during that period.
- No MEAC or analgesic concentration; no equianalgesic ratio. The only opioid here is alfentanil as a co-drug (15 ug/kg bolus; plasma 39.2 / 42.9 / 46.9 ng/mL at incision, plasma assay, Table 2).
- Thiopental requirement fell 17% (low) and about 30% (high) versus control (p=0.013 and 0.0001).

## Covariates
None modelled. Age differs slightly between groups (p=0.049).

## Caveats
Alfentanil and thiopental were given at induction in every group, so the dexmedetomidine effect is measured on top of them (the authors say to read results with caution). Up-down method gives a small number of crossings per group (n=16-17), with SD 0.05 in the high group. One dexmedetomidine sample at incision only. Women 31-55 yr, so not directly applicable to older adults (see Fragen 1999, PMID_10526824). Logistic regression assumes independent observations, which the up-down design violates (authors note this). Infusion rates as printed look inconsistent with the stated targets (open question).
