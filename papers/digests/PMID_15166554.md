# Kern 2004: propofol-remifentanil response surfaces in volunteers

## Study
Open-label, randomized, parallel-group crisscross design, 24 healthy ASA I volunteers aged 18-45, within 15% of ideal body weight (PDF p2-3). Two groups of 12. Group 1 (propofol primary): 5 M/7 F, age 29.0 ± 3.8 yr, weight 69.0 ± 11.8 kg, height 169.5 ± 8.9 cm. Group 2 (remifentanil primary): 7 M/5 F, age 31.0 ± 6.0 yr, weight 73.0 ± 10.9 kg, height 175.8 ± 12.8 cm (Table 1, mean ± SD). Overall mean age 30 yr, weight 71 kg (55-92).
Each volunteer had a staircase of the primary drug alone, then two more staircases with a constant background of the other drug. Staircase targets: propofol 0.5-12 ug/mL, remifentanil 0.5-80 ng/mL. Backgrounds: propofol 0.25-4 ug/mL (remifentanil group), remifentanil 1-5 ng/mL (propofol group). Computer-controlled infusion by STANPUMP, remifentanil with Minto PK, propofol with Tackley PK. Each plateau about 5 min. Pretreated with glycopyrrolate 0.2 mg and pancuronium 1 mg. Arterial blood assayed for both drugs (remifentanil: stabilised samples; propofol: plasma HPLC-electrochemical).
End points at each plateau, starting 1 min after the predicted effect-site concentration reached target: OAA/S (sedated = score 1-3), tibial pressure algometry (max 60 lb/in2), tetanic tibial-nerve stimulation (max 90 mA), response to laryngoscopy (movement, grimace or 20% heart-rate rise). Greco interaction model fitted by naive pooling in S-Plus; single-drug staircases fitted to a sigmoid Emax by two-stage WinNonLin.

## Model and parameters
Greco response surface, Emax fixed at 1 (normalised effect): E = U^n/(U^n+1), U = CP/C50P + CR/C50R + alpha*(CP/C50P)(CR/C50R). Concentrations are plasma (measured or predicted from the infusion model; the Greco text says "plasma"). Units: propofol ug/mL, remifentanil ng/mL.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- No ke0, t1/2ke0, tPeak or hysteresis estimated. Effect-site timing was by assumed model only: the concentration was held about 5 min and measured 1 min after the predicted effect-site target (Minto/Tackley PK); the surfaces are against plasma concentration.
- No MEAC or minimum analgesic concentration. The nearest quantities are the C50 values above (end points: OAA/S sedation, pressure algometry, tetanic stimulation, laryngoscopy; plasma, arterial; remifentanil whole-blood assay not stated beyond "stabilised samples", propofol plasma).
- No equianalgesic or potency ratios between opioids. Interaction indices (alpha): 8.2 algometry, 14.7 tetany, 5.1 sedation, 33.2 laryngoscopy; all positive (synergy), P < 0.001.
- Authors' mapping to clinical stimuli (p6): sedation ~ loss of consciousness, laryngoscopy ~ intubation, algometry ~ skin closure, tetany ~ surgical incision.
- Remifentanil alone barely suppresses laryngoscopy (Table 3: 3% at 10 ng/mL; Fig 4 P=0 curve about 12% at 20 ng/mL).

## Covariates
None modelled. Sex and weight reported by group only.

## Caveats
Naive pooled fit (no between-subject variability); fixed exponent for both drugs; algometry and tetany limited by instrument ceilings (censored, so potency possibly underestimated, p9); volunteer surrogates and laryngoscopy alone (not intubation); mean absolute errors 25-34% of response; sedation text uses "OAA/S less than 3" and "1, 2 or 3" for sedated (p3, p5), Fig 3 caption says 0-3, so the cut-off is described inconsistently; tetany C50 propofol is 4.56 in Table 2 and 4.57 in the equation (rounding).
