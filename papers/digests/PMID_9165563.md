# Cheng 1997: intravenous digoxin two-compartment PK across renal function (FPIA)

## Study
Eighteen non-obese adults (<=120% IBW), 18-60 y (none over 60), men and postmenopausal or sterile women, in three groups of six by measured CLcr (24-h urine, U x V / P): group 1 <10 mL/min (haemodialysis), group 2 10-50 (chronic renal insufficiency), group 3 >50 (healthy). Table 1 (PDF p4 / printed 587): age 40 +/- 11, 47 +/- 13, 45 +/- 13 y; M/F 4/2, 5/1, 4/2; weight 64.7 +/- 12.6, 70.3 +/- 11.6, 74.1 +/- 7.8 kg; CLcr 1.9 +/- 1.6, 33.8 +/- 8.8, 115.5 +/- 12.3 mL/min (no ranges printed); albumin 4.2-4.3 (printed "mg/dl"); dose 8.5 +/- 1.6, 10, 10 mcg/kg (printed "mg/kg").

Digoxin (Lanoxin) 10 mcg/kg actual weight IV over 5 min after overnight fast and supine rest (7 mcg/kg in the first three group 1 subjects). 15 serum samples: 0-12 h and days 2-5 (days 6-7 in three subjects). Assay FPIA (TDx Digoxin II), between-day CV 11.9% at 0.64 ng/mL. Two-compartment fits (PCNONLIN 4.1; F test and parsimony); CL = Dose/(A/alpha + B/beta). Table 2 gives mean +/- SD (range) per group, so the parameters are individual fits summarised by group.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. ECGs and telemetry for safety only; no adverse effects. No intercompartmental clearance printed.

## Covariates
Measured CLcr on CL and Vss (linear). Vss was significantly smaller in haemodialysis patients (4.8 vs 6.4-6.6 L/kg), with no difference between renal insufficiency and normal. CLcr explained only 29-40% of the variation in CL (Discussion, p6). One group 1 subject was hyperkalaemic; excluding them did not change the results.

## Caveats
- n = 6 per group; power analysis says 8 per group (group 1 vs 3) and 56 per group (group 2 vs 3) would be needed to detect the CL differences (p5).
- The CL regression's r2 is ambiguous in the source: the equation is printed in L/h/kg but attached to the absolute-CL correlation (0.41); the per-kg correlation is r2 0.29.
- Group 3 AUC range (80-264) is identical to group 2's and cannot be reconciled with 97 +/- 15 in n = 6; the source does not say which is right.
- FPIA may still cross-react with DLIS. Single dose, young-to-middle-aged adults, none with heart failure.
