# Jamois 2025: tocilizumab dosing for bispecific-antibody CRS

## Study
Pooled safety analysis (Table 1, PDF p3) of 733 mosunetuzumab and 772 glofitamab patients in eight phase 1/2 studies: CRS in 232 (31.7%) and 427 (55.3%); tocilizumab given to 36 (15.5% of CRS patients) and 140 (32.8%); 74.9% of tocilizumab-treated received one dose, 11.4% two, 9.1% three, 4.6% four or more.
PopPK dataset (Table 2, PDF p6): 67 adult R/R B-NHL patients given tocilizumab for CRS, 543 samples (289 PK from 66 patients, 254 sIL-6R from 61); 46 glofitamab (NCT03075696), 21 mosunetuzumab (NCT02500407 and four small studies). Development used 16 + 45 patients from the two main studies, the rest for external evaluation. 44 male (72.1%), 17 female; mean age 63.0 y (30-87); mean weight 75.7 kg (53-128).
Dosing in the studies: 12 mg/kg if <30 kg, 8 mg/kg if >=30 kg IV, per-infusion cap 800 mg, repeat every 8 h up to four doses. Sampling: pre-dose, end of infusion, 6, 24, 48, 72 h, day 8, week 8 (tocilizumab). ELISA, LLOQ 0.1 ug/mL (tocilizumab) and 1.0 ug/mL (sIL-6R; total = free + tocilizumab-bound). NONMEM 7.5.0.

## Model and parameters
Two-compartment tocilizumab model with parallel linear and Michaelis-Menten elimination and first-order SC absorption; total sIL-6R by the quasi-steady-state TMDD approximation (indirect response with inhibition of elimination); two-target (sIL-6R and membrane IL-6R) TMDD basis. The model was an unpublished internal update of a published RA/sJIA/pcJIA model; for CRS, CRS-effects were added on CL, Q, Vc, Vp, VM, kint, kdeg and baseline sIL-6R, with parameters otherwise fixed (Table S2). Estimates are in the supplement and not in this PDF.
Relative comparison (Fig. 2, PDF p7; text p5): CL about 1.5-fold and volume about 1.2-fold higher in CRS patients than in RA patients (comparator: typical RA patients from the same internal model, same 8 mg/kg dose). Discussion (PDF p9) quotes literature linear CL: COVID-19 0.725 L/day, adult RA 0.2-0.3 L/day, sJIA 0.17 L/day, CAR-T CRS 0.5 L/day (refs 18, 42).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point is sIL-6R receptor occupancy (%) and free sIL-6R, modelled from total sIL-6R and tocilizumab; no ke0, tPeak or hysteresis. Predicted (Table 3, PDF p6, median (range)): 1 x 8 mg/kg occupancy day 14 99% (89.2-99.6), day 21 98.2%; 2 x 8 mg/kg (8 h apart) day 14 99.6% (97.5-99.8), day 21 99.4% (57.9-99.8); duration of >90% saturation within days 0-28: 28 days (13.8-28) for one dose, 28 days (18.3-28) for two. The FDA-derived tocilizumab concentration ceiling is 649 ug/mL (Cmax of 28 mg/kg MTD in healthy volunteers). IL-6 rises transiently after tocilizumab (displacement from receptor) then falls over about 8 days; CRP falls (Fig. 4, PDF p10). No MEAC or EC50 and no potency ratio.

## Covariates
Study and sex effects on CL and Vc (lower in females, higher in males in NCT02500407) were seen but not included. Missing covariates imputed with median. CRS severity dependence of CL and baseline sIL-6R was investigated; results in supplement.

## Caveats
Small dataset (67 patients, only 16-45 per development study); parameters mostly fixed to an unpublished internal model; the proposed regimen (up to two 8 mg/kg doses per CRS event at least 8 h apart, at most three in 6 weeks, body weight >=30 kg) is a simulation-based proposal and is not an outcome trial. R/R B-NHL on bispecifics only. All authors are sponsor-affiliated except one consultant.
