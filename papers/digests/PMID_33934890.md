# Vellinga 2021: reply defending broad-population TCI models (Eleveld propofol)

## Study
A one-and-a-half-page reply letter, not a study. It answers Schnider, Minto, Egan and Filipovic's comment on Vellinga 2020 (prospective validation of the Eleveld propofol model in general anaesthesia, Br J Anaesth 2020;126:386-94). It reports one worked dosing example and a few performance statements from the 2020 paper; no new data.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: BIS; Ce50 for BIS 3.08 mcg/mL for the stated example patient (Eleveld model). No ke0, tPeak, MEAC or potency ratio is given; no opioid is the subject.
- The letter's argument: one model fitted across children, adults, older and obese patients gives smooth behaviour at covariate boundaries, so clinicians need not learn model switches (e.g. BMI 29.5 versus 30.5). Other points: BIS titration was not a problem in the 2020 study; Varvel measures are preferred over closed-loop measures; divergence was not reported because the authors do not consider it essential; accuracy in the 3-5 min after a target change was not examined.

## Covariates
None reported beyond the example patient's age, weight and height.

## Verification against stanpumpR
Nothing cites it, so there is no citation to check. As an independent check of the worked example, I computed (derived; Python replica of the Eleveld code in `R/drugs_propofol.R:169-259`, not run in R) CL1 for the 35 y, 70 kg, 170 cm male: 1.619 L/min; times 3.08 mg/L gives 4.99 mg/min, which equals the letter's 4.99 mg/min. The library's V1 for this patient is 6.28 L, V2 25.5 L, V3 168.2 L. The letter's Schnider steady-state (4.91 mg/min at 3 mcg/mL) implies CL 1.637 L/min, which equals Schnider's published clearance equation at this patient (derived: 1.89 + 0.0456(70 - 77) - 0.0681(LBM 55.3 - 59) + 0.0264(170 - 177) = 1.64). The induction dose (1.88 mg/kg) and the 3.33 and 5.17 min pauses depend on the effect-site rate constant: the library fixes tPeak 1.6 min (`R/drugs_propofol.R:276`) and solves ke0 for it, whereas the 2020 Eleveld model has its own ke0, so those three numbers were not reproduced and the library's pause could differ. Verdict: matches for the steady-state infusion rate; pause and induction values not verified.
