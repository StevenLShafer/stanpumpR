# van Ruth 2004: mitomycin population PK/PD during HIPEC

## Study
- 47 patients (25 pseudomyxoma peritonei, 22 colorectal peritoneal carcinomatosis; 19 M / 28 F; age 54 +/- 12 y; BSA 1.84 +/- 0.21 m2), cytoreductive surgery then 90-minute hyperthermic intraperitoneal perfusion (Table I, PDF p.4).
- Mitomycin 35 mg/m2 (max 70 mg; mean dose 63 +/- 8 mg) added to the perfusate in three portions: 50% at start, 25% at 30 min, 25% at 60 min. Perfusate at 40-41 degC, basic volume 3 L (mean 3.7 +/- 1.0 L), 1 L/min circulation (PDF pp.2-3 and 13).
- Samples: perfusate at 1-90 min (11 samples); blood at 1-240 min and 18 h (18 h samples all below the limit and excluded). HPLC-UV at 365 nm; LLOQ 400 ug/L in perfusate, 10 ug/L in plasma. Plasma total concentration.
- NONMEM V, FOCE with INTERACTION; all data fitted simultaneously. Covariates by forward addition (OFV drop 3.8, p<0.05) then backward elimination (10.8, p<0.001). Bootstrap with 500 replicates. PD relations fitted in SPSS.

## Model and parameters
Perfusate: one compartment (V1) with first-order transfer to plasma (k12); plasma: two-compartment, central V2, peripheral V3, CL and Q. Because bioavailability is unknown, plasma V and CL are apparent (divided by F), as the authors state (PDF p.4). Interindividual variability proportional; plasma residual error additive (mg/L), perfusate proportional.

Mean individual Bayesian estimates (n=47, abstract and PDF p.8): V1 4.5 +/- 1.1 L, k12 0.014 +/- 0.003 /min, V2 28 +/- 16 L, CL 0.55 +/- 0.18 L/min, V3 36 +/- 8 L. AUC(perfusate) 630 +/- 130 and AUC(plasma) 71 +/- 24 mg.min/L; ratio of the two 10.1 +/- 4.6. Final-model objective function 5124.5 vs 5189.0 basic.

Final covariate equations (Eqs. 9-11):
- V1 (L) = 3.98 x (perfusate volume / 3.0 L)^0.588
- CL (L/min) = 0.502 x (BSA / 1.80 m2)^1.63
- V2 (L) = 21.7 x (duration of surgery / 8.0 h)^1.47

Model-implied: V2 rises from about 20 to 50 L as surgery lengthens from 6 to 12 h (text, PDF p.10).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Haematological toxicity against individual AUC(plasma) (mg.min/L), PDF pp.9-10; not an opioid paper, so no MEAC/ke0/tPeak/potency-ratio items apply and none are reported.
- Leucocyte decrease (% fall from pretreatment to nadir): sigmoidal Emax, Emax fixed at 100% in the Methods but reported as 91% in the Results, AUC50 56 mg.min/L, gamma (Hill) 4.4, r = 0.78 (logarithmic model r = 0.71).
- Thrombocyte decrease: logarithmic model, % decrease = b0 + b1 ln AUC with b0 = 20%, b1 = 10%, r = 0.56.
- No relation between AUC(perfusate) and toxicity.
- Grade 3-4 leucopenia in 13/47 (28%); AUC(plasma) of those 33-123 mg.min/L (Discussion says 32-123). Grade 3 thrombocytopenia in 3; no grade 4. Among 29 patients with AUC < 78, 3 (about 10%) had grade 3/4 leucopenia; 8/41 (20%) with AUC < 100.
- Authors estimate a dose of about 25 mg/m2 (about 30% reduction) would hold grade 3/4 leucopenia near 10%; not tested prospectively.
- Nadir WBC 4.9 +/- 5.0 x10^9/L (pretreatment 8.5 +/- 3.2); platelets 144 +/- 61 (pretreatment 384 +/- 148).

## Covariates
Retained: perfusate volume on V1; duration of surgery on V2; BSA on CL. Age, blood loss, and duration on k12/CL/V3 were significant singly but dropped in backward elimination. Sex was in the methods as an example only.

## Caveats
- Bioavailability from the peritoneum unknown; all plasma PK are apparent (CL/F, V/F). The authors estimate more than 50% absorbed (derived by them from a 0.8 L/min IV clearance literature value and AUC 71, about 57 mg).
- Parameters apply only to this perfusion system (open abdomen, 40 degC, 90 min, three drug additions).
- Emax stated as fixed at 100% in the Methods but quoted as 91% in the Results (see open question).
- Text says the CL IIV fell from 33% to 28% but Table III final shows 26.7%.
- The paper says the bootstrap resampled "approximately 65%" of the data with replacement, which is non-standard.
- Single institution; Q IIV not estimable; plasma sampled to 240 min only.
