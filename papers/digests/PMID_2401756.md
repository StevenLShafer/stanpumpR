# Shah 1990: codeine and morphine after 60 mg oral codeine phosphate

## Study
- 10 healthy men, 18-40 y, within ±15% of desirable weight (weights not given).
- Single oral dose of 60 mg codeine phosphate after an overnight fast; lunch at 3 h.
- Blood at 0, 0.5, 1, 3 and 6 h only.
- HPLC with electrochemical detection; sensitivity 2 ng/mL codeine and morphine, 5 ng/mL norcodeine (none detected).
- Cmax and tmax observed; AUC0-6 by linear trapezoid; elimination rate constant not identifiable, so a literature k of 0.32 /h (Findlay) was borrowed for AUC0-∞, CL/F and Vd.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- No PD measured. No ke0, tPeak, hysteresis or MEAC.
- No potency ratio. The Discussion notes IM codeine was reported more potent than oral despite more morphine after oral (literature) and concludes formed morphine may have only a minor analgesic role after a single dose.

## Covariates
None.

## Caveats
- Four post-dose samples: tmax is effectively the 1 h sample, and AUCs are truncated at 6 h.
- The morphine/codeine AUC ratio excludes the two subjects with undetectable morphine, which biases it upward.
- CL/F and Vd/F rest on a borrowed elimination rate constant.
- Dose stated as 60 mg codeine phosphate; base content not given (about 44-45 mg base, derived; Guay 1988 states 60 mg phosphate = 45.2 mg base).
