# Lehmann 1988: droperidol PK at 5, 10 and 15 mg, and under volatile anaesthesia

## Study
42 ASA I-II adults, elective abdominal, gynaecological or orthopaedic surgery >= 60 min; no significant
hepatic/renal impairment. Premedication diazepam 10 mg PO night before + 10 mg IM 1 h before; induction
atropine, alcuronium, diazepam 5 mg, hexobarbitone 3 mg/kg, suxamethonium. Five randomized groups:
groups 1-3 (neuroleptanalgesia: fentanyl 4 mcg/kg, N2O 60%) droperidol 5, 10, 15 mg IV (n = 10 each);
groups 4-5 droperidol 10 mg with halothane 1.5 then 1 vol% or enflurane 2 then 1.5 vol% (n = 6 each).
Venous plasma at 1, 3, 6, 10, 15, 20, 30, 45, 60, 90, 120, 180, 240, 300, 360 min. Radioimmunoassay
(benperidol kit, Janssen; Fischler method); inter-assay CV 12.3% at 10 ng/mL, 13.9% at 1 ng/mL. Individual
bi- or triexponential fits (SAS, Marquardt, weight 1/C^2); model-independent Cl, Vdss, Vdarea, MRT, t1/2.
Table II (neuroleptanalgesia, n = 30): age 14-72 yr, weight 48-90 kg; group mean weights 65, 73, 65 kg.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None measured. No ke0, no effect concentration. (Not an opioid paper.)

## Covariates
No dose dependence (linear 5-15 mg); no significant effect of halothane or enflurane; Vd and Cl unrelated to
age or weight (r^2 < 0.13); weight normalisation did not reduce variability.

## Caveats
Group sizes small; sampling to 6 h; diazepam/fentanyl co-medication. Internal inconsistencies: pooled Cl and
volumes in the Results text vs Summary/Table II (above); Summary says age 14-65 yr and weight 48-90 kg but
Table II has a 72-year-old (patient 28) and Table III a 100 kg patient (31). Fig. 1 bars are SEM.
