# Darpo 2022: Thorough QT study of cenobamate

## Study
- **Design:** single-centre, randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled, parallel-group thorough QT study
  with a nested moxifloxacin crossover (Celerion, Tempe). Doses were given fasting.
- **Subjects:** healthy adults aged 18-50 y with BMI 18.5-30 kg/m2, N = 108 (mean age about 36, about half
  women).
  - Treatment A (n = 54): oral cenobamate once daily.
  - Treatments B and C (n = 27 each): placebo, with moxifloxacin 400 mg on day -1 (B) or day 64 (C).
- **Titration in arm A:**

  | Days | Dose (mg/day) |
  |---|---|
  | 1-7 | 50 |
  | 8-14 | 100 |
  | 15-21 | 150 |
  | 22-35 | 200 (therapeutic; ECG/PK day 35) |
  | 36-42 | 300 |
  | 43-49 | 400 |
  | 50-63 | 500 (supratherapeutic; ECG/PK day 63) |

  Each evaluated dose had been given for 14 days before its PK day.
- **Sampling and analysis:** serial plasma samples to 23.5 h on days 35 and 63, plus troughs on days 35 and
  61-63. Validated LC assay (Chromolith column), plasma total concentration. Noncompartmental analysis in
  WinNonlin 6.3.
- **Disposition:** 102 completed. One subject in arm A died of DRESS / eosinophilic myocarditis.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- **End point:** QTcF (Fridericia), measured on 12-lead Holter extracts at 8 time points, analysed by time
  point and by concentration-QTc.
- **QTcF change, placebo-corrected:**
  - Day 35: -10.8 ms (90% CI -13.4 to -8.2).
  - Day 63: -18.4 ms (90% CI -21.5 to -15.2).
  - The largest change was at 0.5 h on both days.
- **Linear concentration-QTc model (best fit, with intercept):** slope -0.18 ms per ug/mL (90% CI -0.23 to
  -0.13), intercept -5.75 ms.
  - Predicted effect at the geometric mean peak: -9.85 ms at 23.06 ug/mL and -17.14 ms at 63.96 ug/mL
    (PDF p 7).
  - The x-axis of Figure 4 is labelled ng/mL, but the data and slope are in ug/mL. This is a labelling error
    in the figure.
- **Moxifloxacin (assay sensitivity):** peak QTcF change of 13.3 ms at 3.5 h.
- **No QTc ≤340 ms** was observed in any subject.
- **Not reported:** ke0, effect-site equilibration, therapeutic or analgesic concentrations. Cenobamate is not
  an opioid.
- **hERG:** the introduction cites an hERG IC50 of 1869 uM (500.29 ug/mL), against a steady-state Cmax of
  45.5 ug/mL at 400 mg/day (data on file).

## Covariates
None analysed.

## Caveats
- Healthy young volunteers on cenobamate alone, fasting, after a rapid titration (weekly steps, faster than
  the label).
- Steady state at 200 mg was not statistically confirmed.
- Table 1 does not give the number of subjects with PK on day 63 (arm A lost 4 subjects).
- Funded by the sponsor (SK Life Science).
- Table 1 reports arithmetic means and the text reports geometric means; both are given above.

  **Matches.**
