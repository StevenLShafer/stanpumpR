# Kuhlman 1996: IV, sublingual and buccal buprenorphine PK in six men

## Study
- Crossover, one dose per week: 1.2 mg IV (constant rate over 1 min), 4.0 mg sublingual, 4.0 mg buccal. Preceded by an IV dose-escalation for tolerance.
- 6 healthy men, nondependent but with a history of heroin use; residential research ward. Ages 27-40 y; weights 62.6-72.7 kg (Table II; one subject's data unavailable).
- **Sublingual formulation: 30% alcoholic solution** held under the tongue for 10 min (Ped-Pod dispenser), not a tablet. Buccal: drug-embedded plastic strip between lip and gum for 10 min.
- Plasma to 96 h; GC-MS-MS (negative CI); buprenorphine LOD 0.15, LOQ 0.20 ng/mL; norbuprenorphine LOD 0.016, LOQ 0.031 ng/mL; concentrations between LOD and LOQ were used. Between-run CV at 0.5 ng/mL: 12.8% (buprenorphine), 20.4% (norbuprenorphine).
- Noncompartmental analysis (PCNONLIN). IV buprenorphine data of subject D lost (N = 5 for IV and therefore for F).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD measured. **No MEAC, no ke0/tPeak.** Introduction (background, not measured): buprenorphine 25-40 times as potent as morphine; therapeutic IV/IM doses 0.3-0.6 mg; 4-16 mg effective for heroin withdrawal; oral F ~15%, SL F 55% (Bullingham). 

## Covariates
None analysed (n = 6).

## Caveats
- n = 6 (F from 5 and 4), large variability (SL F 12.8-92.2%); new assay, values below LOQ included.
- SL alcohol solution, not tablet/film; Tmax 0.71 h vs 3.35 h for Bullingham's tablets, authors attribute the difference partly to formulation.
- Long SL/buccal terminal t½ attributed to an oral-mucosal depot ("flip-flop"), proposed model Fig. 2.
- Short IV sampling until LOD (13-48 h) limits terminal phase.

## Verification against stanpumpR
- "51%" at 4 mg (header 84-85; help 19): Table IV mean 51.4%. **matches**.
- "in six men" (header 85): six men were studied, but F was computed in only 5 (IV data of subject D lost). Minor. **discrepancy (n = 5 for F)**.
- Implicit comparison with tablets/films: the paper's 4 mg SL dose was a 30% ethanol solution held 10 min, which likely absorbs more than a tablet (cf. Björnsson F 0.234 at 4 mg by the power law, 0.237 by stanpumpR's fitted form). The repo uses it only as directional support ("nearer the 51%") without stating the formulation. **matches (with caveat: formulation not mentioned; range 12.8-92.2%)**.
- Not used for IV parameters; note this paper's IV CL (76.8 L/h, NCA, n 5) is higher than Björnsson's 52.1 L/h. Informational.
- Potency 25-40× morphine is background in the introduction, not a finding; not used in repo.
