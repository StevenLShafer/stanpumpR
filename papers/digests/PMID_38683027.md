# Vashi 2024: crushed cenobamate tablet, orally or by nasogastric tube, vs intact tablet

## Study
- Phase 1, open-label, randomized, single-dose, three-way crossover (3 periods, 3 treatments, 6 sequences);
  NCT05572255; single site (Worldwide Clinical Trials, San Antonio); sponsor SK Life Science (PDF pp 1-3,
  printed 922-924).
- Healthy adults 18-50 y, BMI 18.5-30.0 kg/m2, non-smokers; women surgically sterile or postmenopausal. 31 randomized,
  9 withdrawn and replaced (mostly protocol deviations: caffeine, ethanol, illicit drugs). Table 1 (PDF p 5): age
  36.3 ± 8.5 y (median 37, 22-49); 26 men (83.9%), 5 women; 19 White, 12 Black. **Weight not reported.**
- Treatments, each a single 200 mg dose after a 10-h fast, with 240 mL water in total:
  A (reference) intact tablet swallowed; B crushed tablet suspended in 25 mL water and drunk; C crushed tablet
  suspension via a 10 Fr polyurethane NG tube (removed 1 h after dosing). Washout 13 days; doses on days 1, 14, 27.
- Sampling: pre-dose and 0.25, 0.5, 1, 2, 2.5, 3, 3.5, 4, 6, 8, 12, 24, 36, 48, 72, 120, 192, 264 h.
- Assay: validated LC-MS/MS in plasma (cenobamate-d4 internal standard), calibration 0.0500-40.0 μg/mL; total
  plasma concentration.
- Analysis: noncompartmental (Phoenix WinNonlin 8.3.4, linear trapezoidal); ANOVA on log Cmax, AUClast, AUCinf;
  bioequivalence limits 80-125%. Two NG subjects excluded (tube blockage; pre-dose concentration > 5% Cmax).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None: no efficacy or concentration-effect end point, no ke0, no therapeutic range. Safety: TEAEs 14.8% intact,
8.3% crushed oral, 25.0% NG (Table 4); all mild, mostly CNS (somnolence, headache, dizziness), plus GI events with NG.
Not an opioid paper.

## Covariates
None examined.

## Caveats
- Single 200 mg doses in healthy, fasted, mostly male volunteers; patients needing crushed tablets may differ
  (authors).
- Bioequivalent by the 80-125% rule, but the AUC point estimates for both crushed forms are 6-9% below the intact
  tablet, with 90% CIs excluding 100% (Table 3).
- Crushed forms reach Tmax earlier (0.5 vs 1.0 h median); authors attribute this to tablet disintegration being
  rate limiting.
- Enteral feeding formula not studied.
