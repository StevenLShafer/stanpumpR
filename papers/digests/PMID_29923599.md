# Beumer 2019: IATDMCT recommendations for 5-FU therapeutic drug monitoring

## Study
Consensus review (not a trial) by the TDM in Oncology Scientific Committee of the IATDMCT, 15 authors. It applies a framework adapted from the AGNP psychiatry TDM guidelines (supplementary Table S2, not in the PDF) to intravenous 5-fluorouracil. Oral fluoropyrimidines (capecitabine, UFT, S-1) are explicitly excluded (PDF p. 1). Conclusion (PDF pp. 12-13, Table 5): TDM is strongly recommended for 5-FU in colorectal and head-and-neck cancer on the common regimens (FOLFOX4/6/7, FOLFIRI, LV5FU, FUFOX, AIO, weekly 1.5 g/m2 over 8 h for CRC; 1.0 g/m2/day D1-4 or D1-5 for SCCHN).

## Model and parameters
No model is fitted. The review tabulates published clearances and exposure targets. Values below are as printed.

- Table 1 (PDF p. 4, printed 601): mean (SD) 5-FU clearance by regimen, L/h per m2 (BSA-normalised). 46-h infusions: 2.4 g/m2, n=589, 117 (53), CV 45%; 2.5 g/m2, n=118, 131 (38), CV 29%; 2.4 g/m2, n=356, 118 (57), CV 49%; 2.4 g/m2 n=48, median 104, range 71-240; 2.4 g/m2 n=47, median 126, CV 31%. 4 g/m2 over 120 h, n=119: 109 (15), CV 41%. 8-h infusion 1.3 g/m2, n=81: 134 (62), range 29-296, CV 46%. Bolus 0.25-0.425 g/m2: 51.5-79.2 (L/h/m2 in most rows; one row 79.2 L/h, n=181).
- Clearance is higher with long continuous infusion than after bolus; the text attributes this to saturation of DPD catabolism as concentrations approach DPD's Km, "~4.6 mg/L" (PDF p. 3, printed 600, ref 27).
- Interindividual variability more than 40% CV (8.3-fold 95% range); intraindividual about 20% CV (2.3-fold) (PDF p. 3; Table 2, PDF p. 4).
- Half-life 10-15 min normally, up to 160 min or more in DPD deficiency (PDF p. 3). Men reportedly have 26% higher elimination than women (PDF p. 3, refs 20, 28).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- Not an opioid; no ke0 or MEAC. Therapeutic window is described as narrow with toxicity and efficacy overlapping (PDF p. 3).
- Exposure metric: AUC (mg h/L); for infusions Css x duration (PDF p. 8, printed 605). Target AUC 20-30 mg h/L, applied to 46-h schedules, widened from Gamelin's 20-24 (8-h infusion) (PDF p. 11). Not appropriate for bolus dosing or infusions of 120 h and longer.
- Exposure-toxicity: transition to toxic exposure about 25-30 mg h/L for 2-weekly infusions, 40-60 mg h/L when summing five daily boluses (Mayo regimen) (PDF p. 8). Table 3 (PDF pp. 5-6) lists single-arm exposure-response studies; e.g. 4 g/m2 over 120 h, NPC, AUC 50 (15) with toxicity vs 31 (11) without (n=119/108).
- Schematic (Figure 1, PDF p. 12): about 60% of BSA-dosed patients underdosed, 25% in window, 15% overdosed.
- Sampling: at least 18 h after the start of infusion (day 2 of a 48-h infusion), peripheral vein away from the infusion line, on ice, plasma separated quickly or a DPD inhibitor (gimeracil) added (PDF p. 11).
- Trial evidence: Gamelin RCT (n=208, 8-h weekly 1500 mg/m2) response 18% to 33%, proportion within target 8% to 94% (PDF p. 10). Meta-analysis response OR 2.04 (PDF p. 10).

## Covariates
Sex (men 26% higher elimination, PDF p. 3), DPYD genotype/DPD activity (PDF pp. 3-5), nutritional status and circadian timing as sources of variability. No equation is given.

## Caveats
Review, not primary data; Table 1 mixes studies of differing design, assays and years, some from the 1990s with handling problems the authors say inflate variability (PDF p. 3). The Table 1 text layer lost some cells (n and years run together), so each stanpumpR-relevant value was read on a rendered page. Only two RCTs of TDM exist (PDF p. 10).

## Verification against stanpumpR
- Target 20-30 mg h/L over 46-h infusion (Briki header, chemotherapy.md:168): **matches** PDF p. 11. Briki's own grid 20-29 is the model paper's, not Beumer's. The code correctly says it is an exposure, not a concentration band, and does not draw it; Beumer agrees (not valid for bolus or 120 h infusions).
- The Briki model (R/drugs_fluorouracilBriki.R:141-150: Vmax 411 mg/h, Km 1.89 mg/L, linear CL 59.2 L/h at BSA 1.7) is an independent check against Table 1: at 0.5 mg/L the model clearance is 59.2 + 411/(1.89+0.5) = about 231 L/h (derived), against Table 1's 46-h means of 117-131 L/h/m2 x 1.7 m2 = about 199-223 L/h (derived, BSA 1.7 assumed). Close, same direction. Tan's model (Vmax 1900 mg/h, Km 8.04 mg/L, R/drugs_fluorouracilTan.R:79-82) gives 1900/(8.04+0.5) = about 222 L/h at 0.5 mg/L (derived), also consistent. The two models' Km values (1.89 and 8.04 mg/L) both differ from the DPD Km "~4.6 mg/L" quoted here; these are fitted whole-body parameters, not the same quantity, so no discrepancy.
- Verdict: **matches** (open question concerns only a convention nothing uses).
