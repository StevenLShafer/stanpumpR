# Grasmäder 2004: mirtazapine population PK and CYP2D6

## Study
- Caucasian depressed inpatients (Bonn, 2000-2003) in a naturalistic TDM study; ICD-10 F3, CGI > 4, mirtazapine monotherapy start. 65 patients overall (abstract), 49 genotyped (CYP2D6, 2C9, 2C19). 331 trough samples, 260 used for model building; genotyped subset 213 observations; patient mean concentrations 14-61 ng/mL.
- Genotyped subset (Table 2): age 48.7 (13.2) y, weight 76.8 (17) kg, height 1.70 m, 27 F / 22 M; CYP2D6 PM/IM/EM/UM 1/18/29/1 (PM pooled with IMs, UM with EMs).
- Samples at steady state, morning, about 10-12 h after the last dose. HPLC-UV, LOQ 5 ng/mL, linear 5-300 ng/mL.
- WinNonMix 2.0.1, FOCE; one-compartment, first-order absorption. Only CL/F estimated: ka fixed 1.5 /h (nomograph from Timmer's kel 0.08 /h and Tmax 2.1 h), V/F fixed 678 L. Exponential IIV on CL/F; proportional residual error.

## Model and parameters
Final model (Table 3): CL/Fi = CL/F x exp(etaCL) x [1 + CL/F1 x (CYP2D6 - 2)], CYP2D6 coded IM = 1, EM = 2.
- CL/F1 = 0.264 (SEM 0.101) (Table 3, final-model row, as aligned in the text layer): IM clearance 26.4% lower than EM (text, p. 477).
- IIV of CL/F 37.4% (basic) -> 32.9% (final) (text).
- CL/F typical values print as ~1171 (basic) and ~1319 (final, SEM ~173); the unit is not legible in the text layer. (derived: 0.08 /h x 678 L = 54 L/h = 1,302 L/day, so the printed magnitudes are consistent with L/day, i.e. ~49-55 L/h; unconfirmed.)
- Residual variability about 27% (discussion).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. Plasma concentrations 5-100 ng/mL quoted from Timmer for 15-80 mg/day. No ke0, EC50 or MEAC. Not an opioid paper.

## Covariates
Only CYP2D6 genotype retained. Smoking (+30% CL/F, OFV drop not significant), CYP3A4 substrates (-20%), age, sex, males <= 48 y, alcohol, coffee, CYP2C9/2C19 genotype and other co-medications not significant; weight, height and CYP2C9 failed to estimate.

## Caveats
Trough-only data (no V or ka information); naturalistic setting with high residual error; small n; one PM only; inhibitors/inducers too rare to test.
