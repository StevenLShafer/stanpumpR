# Choe 2017: propofol-remifentanil response surfaces for rigidity, apnoea, LOC, ApEn, TLMC

## Study
- 60 ASA 1-2 adults (30 M / 30 F) having elective surgery; mean age 50 y (21-69), weight 60 kg (41.6-85.4), height 162.5 cm (PDF p3, printed p31). Randomised to 15 planned propofol/remifentanil Ce combinations, 2 M + 2 F each; because 8 ng/mL remifentanil was not tolerated haemodynamically in some, 19 combinations were actually studied (Table 1, printed p31).
- Target effect-site-controlled infusion (Asan Pump 2.1 driving a Pilot Anaesthesia pump 2): propofol Marsh PK with ke0 set by the tPeak method (ref 33), remifentanil Minto 1997 PK. Propofol Ce 0 / 0.5 / 1.5 / 3 ug/mL; remifentanil Ce 0 / 2 / 4 / 6 / 8 ng/mL. Ce stepped up 0.5 ug/mL and 1 ng/mL each minute, then held at least 15 min at pseudo-steady state, no surgical stimulus, no premedication.
- End points: muscle rigidity (clinical: chest/abdominal wall rigidity, inability to open the mouth, or inflation pressure above 40 cmH2O); apnoea (cessation of oronasal airflow for at least 10 s); LOC (no response to a verbal command repeated every 5 s); EEG approximate entropy (ApEn) and temporal linear mode complexity (TLMC), P4 montage, last 5 min of artifact-free EEG at steady state (Methods 4.3-4.4, printed p38).
- Modelling: Minto 2000 response surface, NONMEM 7.2, naive pooled data, first-order estimation, additive residual error for EEG measures; 2000-replicate non-parametric bootstrap for ApEn and TLMC only. No inter-individual variability (only two observations per person).
- Events: rigidity n=5, apnoea n=22, LOC n=16 (Fig 1 legend).

## Model and parameters
Response surface: U_P = Ce_prop/C50_prop, U_R = Ce_remi/C50_remi, Q = U_P/(U_P+U_R), U50(Q) = 1 - A*Q + A*Q^2, gamma(Q) = gammaR - (gammaP - gammaR - B)*Q + B*Q^2. A = 0 additive, A > 0 synergistic, A < 0 infra-additive. A single gamma was used (separate gammas did not improve fit). Quantal P = [(U_R+U_P)/U50]^gamma / (1 + same); EEG E = E0*(1 - the same fraction).

Quantal end points, Table 2 (PDF p4, printed p33; relative SE % in parentheses; confirmed on the image):
- Rigidity: C50 propofol 130 000 ug/mL* (126.2), C50 remifentanil 9.11 ng/mL (28.4), gamma 3.1 (41.9), Interaction_potency -5060* (16.2). *The authors read the huge propofol C50 as propofol not causing rigidity. A = -C50_prop/25.8 (infra-additive); U50 reduces to 1 + (1/25.8)*PROP*C50_remi/REMI, independent of C50_prop (printed p32, p34).
- Apnoea: C50 propofol 6.26 ug/mL (7.7), C50 remifentanil 8.99 ng/mL (24.9), gamma 7.13 (27.3), Interaction_potency 1.86 (22.7), synergistic.
- LOC: C50 propofol 3.04 ug/mL (16.9), C50 remifentanil 13.9 ng/mL (31.7), gamma 3.4 (27.5), no interaction term (additive, U50 = 1).

EEG end points, Table 4 (PDF p7, printed p36; estimate (RSE %), then bootstrap median, 2.5-97.5 percentile):
- ApEn: E0 0.691 (0.7); C50 propofol 10.4 ug/mL (26), boot 10.4, 7.2-18.8; C50 remifentanil 23.4 ng/mL (23.5), boot 23.2, 15.9-29.8; gamma 1.93 (24.7), boot 1.97, 1.35-2.89; no interaction term.
- TLMC: E0 0.948 (1.4); C50 propofol 6.51 ug/mL (5.5), boot 6.55, 5.29-9.74; C50 remifentanil 14.8 ng/mL (5.7), boot 14.6, 11.5-23.9; gamma 2.54 (9.8), boot 2.57, 1.54-3.66; no interaction term.
- Measured baseline (Table 3, printed p35): TLMC 0.96 +/- 0.03, ApEn 0.70 +/- 0.03 (mean +/- SD).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- Opioid audit (remifentanil): the C50 values above are effect-site concentrations (model-predicted from Minto 1997 kinetics with an unstated ke0 for propofol), by probability of event (rigidity, apnoea, LOC) or half-maximal EEG effect (ApEn, TLMC); concentrations are not measured, assay not applicable. This paper reports **no MEAC / minimum effective analgesic concentration** and no analgesic end point (no noxious stimulus). **No ke0, t1/2 ke0 or tPeak value is reported** for either drug, and no hysteresis analysis; the propofol ke0 was set by the tPeak method (ref 33, value not given) and remifentanil Minto 1997 kinetics were used. **No equianalgesic or potency ratio** is given (a remifentanil-to-propofol C50 ratio for rigidity is implied only through A = -C50_prop/25.8).
- Interaction: rigidity infra-additive (propofol lowers its probability; none occurred at propofol 3 ug/mL); apnoea synergistic (largest U50 drop at Q = 0.5); LOC, ApEn and TLMC additive.
- Remifentanil alone at its rigidity C50 (9.11 ng/mL) corresponds to TLMC 0.474 and ApEn 0.346 (PDF p4, printed p33), i.e. EEG effect is partial.
- Remifentanil C50 for LOC (13.9) and for ApEn (23.4) exceed clinical concentrations; the authors cite 21.4 ng/mL for ApEn in volunteers (Noh 2006, ref 23) and 13.1 ng/mL from Bouillon 2004 (ref 11).
- A single-drug sigmoid model for rigidity gave a remifentanil Ce50 of 186-189 ng/mL, judged unreasonable (printed p34).

## Covariates
None modelled (age and ASA PS did not differ between patients with and without rigidity, printed p34-35).

## Caveats
- Only 5 rigidity events; propofol Ce capped at 3 ug/mL and remifentanil at 8 ng/mL (stepped down to 6 ng/mL for haemodynamics), so the surface does not cover the full clinical range; authors say synergy vs additivity depends on the range studied.
- Naive pooled fit, no IIV; EEG measures vary little, so the surface may be underdetermined.
- Ce depends on the Marsh/ke0 and Minto PK models; patients had no stimulus, so nothing here describes analgesia.
- Table 2 headers: the footnote symbol * marks the rigidity propofol C50 and interaction as values that mean "propofol does not induce rigidity".
