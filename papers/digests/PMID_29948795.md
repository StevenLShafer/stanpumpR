# Sunkaraneni 2018: paediatric eslicarbazepine popPK and adult exposure matching

## Study
Sponsor (Sunovion) modelling for the FDA paediatric extrapolation of eslicarbazepine acetate (ESL). PPK data from
Studies BIA-2093-202 (n = 30 enrolled, 2-17 y, 5/15/30 mg/kg/day QD in 4-week periods, rich sampling 0.5-24 h,
21 samples/subject) and BIA-2093-305 (n = 118, 2-18 y, 10-30 mg/kg/day, max 1200 mg/day, sparse: 3 samples at
weeks 2, 6, 18, mostly 12-24 h post-dose); Study 208 (n = 120) for safety only (Table 1). Final PPK dataset 146
subjects, 857 eslicarbazepine concentrations; mean age 10.0 (SD 4.0) y, height 135.0 (23.0) cm, weight 34.7 (16.0)
kg; 93.2% White (Table 2). All adjunctive therapy. NONMEM 7.1.2, KIWI 1.1. Assay not described.

## Model and parameters
One compartment, formulation-specific first-order absorption, first-order elimination, allometric weight scaling
(exponents fixed 0.75 on CL, 1 on V; estimated 0.648 and 1.19 were not adopted). Additive + proportional residual.

CL = 1.69 × (WT/33.0)^0.75 × (1 − 0.176 × LEVE) × (1 + 0.626 × PHEN) L/h; V = 32.8 × (WT/33.0) L (p. 653).

Adult exposure targets (adult PPK models, refs 12-13, mean Cmin,ss, adjunctive and monotherapy; p. 653):
400 mg QD 3.7 µg/mL (titration target); 800 mg 7.4; 1200 mg 11; 1600 mg 14.7 µg/mL (maintenance range).
Proposed paediatric doses (Table 4): 11-21 kg titration 200, maintenance 400-600 mg/day; 22-31 kg 300, 500-800;
32-38 kg 300, 600-900; > 38 kg 400, 800-1200 mg/day.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD model. Cmin,ss was the exposure metric best related to efficacy in adults (ref 18); Cmax not a significant
predictor of common adverse events (ref 19). No therapeutic range given beyond the adult Cmin,ss targets.

## Covariates
Body weight (CL, V); levetiracetam (−17.6% CL), phenobarbital-like inducers (+62.6% CL), carbamazepine (F × 0.679).
Age, eGFR (Schwartz), height, race, sex, valproate, lamotrigine, topiramate tested and not retained.

## Caveats
Children only (2-18 y), adjunctive therapy only; ka fixed; dose basis (ESL vs eslicarbazepine mass) not explicitly
stated in the model description; adult target values come from earlier adult models (conference abstracts), not
from data in this paper. The levetiracetam effect may be an artefact (authors). Sponsor-funded.
