# Lindemalm 2005: population pharmacokinetics of cladribine

## Study
Retrospective pooled population PK analysis (NONMEM, FOCE with interaction; Xpose, PsN) of earlier
Swedish/UK studies, February 1990 to March 1996 (PDF p. 6). 215 patients/227 courses screened; 173 courses in
163 patients initially included; final analysis 168 courses in 161 patients, 1102 plasma concentrations
(PDF pp. 2, 6). Patients with lymphoid/myeloid malignancies: CLL 63, HCL 84, AML 3, NHL 7, CML 4, LCH 2
(Table 1, PDF p. 5). Demographics (Table 1, mean (SD) [range]): 129 m / 34 f; age 60 (13) [22-89] y; weight
76 (14) [48-118] kg; height 174 (9) cm; BSA 1.9 (0.2) m2. Creatinine clearance (Cockcroft-Gault) 81 (27) mL/min,
range 5-162 (Table 2). No patient had severe hepatic or renal impairment (PDF p. 6).

Dosing (PDF p. 6): 2-h IV infusion 5 mg/m2 or 0.12 mg/kg; oral 10 mg/m2 or 0.24 mg/kg in saline after an
overnight fast; SC 5 mg/m2 (2 mg/mL) into abdominal adipose tissue; six patients had continuous IV infusion for
4-7 days at 4.0-5.6 mg/m2/24 h; three had a 2-h infusion then continuous infusion (4.8-5.7 mg/m2). Data: 93 IV
doses (63 courses), 83 SC doses (83 courses), 24 oral doses (22 courses); 7 patients had both IV and oral data.
Sampling: mean 8 (range 1-27) venous samples per patient up to 36 (3-303) h. Assay: plasma, reversed-phase HPLC
after solid-phase extraction, LOQ 1 nM using 1 mL plasma (about 0.29 ng/mL, derived: MW 285.7 g/mol).

## Model and parameters
Three-compartment disposition model, first-order absorption without lag for oral and SC, exponential IIV on all
disposition parameters plus ka and F, proportional residual error per route with IIV in the residual
(PDF pp. 2-3, 7). Typical values, Table 3, PDF p. 5 (confirmed on the rendered page):

All volumes and clearances are absolute (L, L/h), not per kg or per m2. Route-specific refit without F (PDF p. 3):
IV CL 39.7 L/h (RSE 8%, IIV 59%), oral CL/F 105 L/h (RSE 13%, IIV 55%). Fraction of elimination associated with
the terminal/first/second slopes 65/11/24% (PDF p. 4). Accumulation index (AUC at steady state over first dose)
ranged 1.02-3.05 across individuals (pp. 3-4).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD model, no concentration-effect relation, no ke0. The discussion notes the metabolite 2-chloroadenine has
8-fold lower cytotoxicity than cladribine and is formed about five-fold more after oral dosing (citing ref. 9,
PDF p. 4). CLL response (73 patients): 32% complete, 30% partial, 38% none (PDF p. 6); not related to PK.
Not an opioid paper: no MEAC, potency ratio or analgesic end point.

## Covariates
None. Gender, age, weight, height, BSA, diagnosis, creatinine clearance, liver tests, lymphocyte count, CLL stage
and response were tested (GAM screen, then NONMEM, P < 0.01); none was significant (PDF pp. 3, 7). The authors
conclude BSA- or weight-based dosing is no better than a fixed dose.

## Caveats
- Retrospective pooling over six years and several centres; only 7 patients with both IV and oral data, so the
  low IIV in F (4%) may be an artefact (authors, PDF p. 4).
- Oral F 35.3% is described as slightly lower than previous reports of 37-51% (the comparator is other
  published, mostly non-compartmental, studies, refs 3,4,6,7), not a within-study comparison.
- Q2, Q3 and V3 report identical IIV (61%, RSE 17%), which suggests a shared random effect; the paper does not say.
- V2 475 L has an RSE of only 1.8%, unusually tight for a peripheral volume; transcribed as printed.
- No severe hepatic or renal impairment included; one subject with a seven-fold rise after infusion end and one
  slow absorber were excluded.
