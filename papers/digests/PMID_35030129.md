# Fisher and Shafer 2022: Pharmacokinetics Police

## Study
Critique letter (PDF pages 1-2, printed e7-e8) of two published PK papers; no new data. Followed by a response from the authors of the first paper (signature block truncated on the page; the response says it is from the authors of Dalesio et al).

Critique of Dalesio 2020 (Anesth Analg 131:876-884; morphine PK in children with obstructive sleep apnea and obesity): Figure 1 labelled median but legend says mean; groups dosed differently (obese on ideal body weight, others on weight) so concentrations are not comparable without dose scaling; error bars in Figure 3 not defined as SD or SE; M3G:MS ratio in Table 2 reported without metabolite concentrations or AUC; M6G Cmax missing; M6G:MS ratio reported as <=0.001 (questioned as inconsistent with other publications); some terminal half-lives nearly twice the sampling duration so clearance from AUC/dose is questionable; the "volume of distribution" type is unstated (Vss most relevant).

Critique of Mohamed 2021 (Anesth Analg 132:456-464; oral-mucosal dexmedetomidine gel, 3 doses): non-compartmental AUC and apparent clearance (CL/F) need AUC beyond last sample (last concentration / terminal rate constant, ln2/half-life); some washout curves nearly flat so terminal half-lives exceed the sampling duration and extrapolated AUC is a disproportionate fraction; y-axis said to be logarithmic but is not; smooth curves drawn through points imply a fit when AUC was probably linear-trapezoid.

Response (Dalesio group): units for volume of distribution in their Table 2 should be L/kg; Figure 1 legend should read median; Figure 3 error bars are SD; M6G:morphine ratios corrected; volume reported as Vss/F; adjusted and unadjusted Cmax and AUC540 given for the severe OSAS/obese group; erratum submitted; conclusions unchanged. No numerical PK values appear in the letter.

## Caveats
A critique, not a source of parameters. It is a warning that the Dalesio morphine and Mohamed dexmedetomidine tables have unit and extrapolation problems, so values from them should not be taken without the erratum.
