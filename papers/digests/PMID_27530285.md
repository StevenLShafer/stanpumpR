# Foo 2016: population PK of intramuscular droperidol in agitated patients

## Study
Subgroup of the DORM randomized trial (droperidol vs midazolam for acute behavioural disturbance, emergency
department). 41 patients: 27 F, 14 M; median age 33 (16-62) yr (one 16-year-old recruited inadvertently;
Table 1 gives 18-62); weight not recorded. Doses: text says 24 received 10 mg and 17 received 5 mg (with
5 mg midazolam); Table 1 lists 5 mg 17, 10 mg 19, > 10 mg 5 (5 patients got extra droperidol). 128 serum
samples, median 3 per patient (1-7), empirical then optimal-design sampling (5 min to 10 h). HPLC-UV,
LLOQ 5 mcg/L, 4 samples below LLOQ (M6 method). NONMEM 7.2.

## Model and parameters
Two compartments, first-order absorption, combined error. ka poorly identified (individual estimates
1-140 /h), fixed at 10 /h with BSV 100%. CL and Vc share one random effect (correlation estimated at 100%).

Median (IQR) individual half-lives: alpha 0.32 (0.26-0.37) h, beta 3.0 (2.5-3.6) h. Observed Cmax 22.4-215
mcg/L, peak within ~10 min. Simulations: 10 mg keeps concentrations > 5 mcg/L with 80% probability for ~7 h
(5 mg ~2 h less; 10 + 10 mg at 15 min ~10 h).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None modelled. Authors state no minimum effective concentration or EC50 is known and use the LLOQ as a
surrogate; cite Schulz & Schmoldt therapeutic up to 50 mcg/L. QT not prolonged in the parent trial. No ke0.
(Not an opioid paper.)

## Covariates
Breath alcohol tested, no association with CL or Vc. Weight unavailable.

## Caveats
Sparse early sampling (ka and Q weakly identified), no weight, IM bioavailability not measured (parameters
apparent), co-ingestants varied, serum HPLC-UV with LLOQ 5 mcg/L.
