# Carney 2017: Brain Trauma Foundation severe TBI guidelines, 4th edition (synopsis)

## Study
Synopsis of an evidence-based guideline (Brain Trauma Foundation; endorsed by AANS/CNS). Systematic review of
adult severe TBI (GCS 3-8): 189 publications (5 Class 1, 46 Class 2, 136 Class 3, 2 meta-analyses) supporting
28 recommendations on 18 topics (PDF p1, printed 6); 1 Level I, 7 Level IIA, 10 Level IIB, 10 Level III
(p3, printed 8). Levels follow the quality of the body of evidence. The full guideline, evidence tables and
appendices A-I are online, not in this PDF.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Hyperosmolar therapy (Table 1, PDF p4, printed 9, rendered): no new Fourth Edition recommendation. Two Third
Edition statements are carried forward under the heading of recommendations not supported by evidence
meeting current standards:
- mannitol controls raised ICP at 0.25 to 1 g/kg body weight; avoid systolic BP <90 mm Hg;
- restrict mannitol before ICP monitoring to signs of transtentorial herniation or progressive neurological
  deterioration not due to extracranial causes.
No serum osmolality target, ceiling (e.g. 320 mOsm/kg) or osmolar gap is given; hypertonic saline is not
mentioned. No PK, ke0 or concentration data. Not an opioid paper: no MEAC.

Other thresholds (Table 3, p7, printed 12): treat ICP >22 mm Hg (IIB); CPP target 60-70 mm Hg (IIB); SBP
>=100 mm Hg at 50-69 y, >=110 mm Hg at 15-49 or >70 y (III); jugular venous saturation <50% to avoid (III).
Anaesthetics (Table 1): high-dose barbiturates for refractory ICP (IIB); propofol for ICP control but not
outcome, with caution at high dose (IIB). Steroids not recommended (Level I).

None.

## Caveats
Synopsis only; the evidence summaries live in the online document. The hyperosmolar statements are explicitly
not supported by evidence meeting current standards. Adult severe TBI only. RESCUEicp results arrived after
completion and may change the craniectomy and ICP recommendations.
