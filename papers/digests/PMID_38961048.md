# Chandasana 2024: ABC/DTG/3TC pediatric FDC PopPK external validation

## Study
- IMPAACT 2019 (NCT03760458): phase I/II open-label, children < 12 y with HIV-1, 6 to < 40 kg, once-daily abacavir/dolutegravir/lamivudine fixed-dose combination (dispersible tablet DT, or tablet). Five weight bands: 6-<10, 10-<14, 14-<20, 20-<25, 25-<40 kg (p2).
- 57 enrolled, 55 in the PopPK dataset (590 ABC, 598 DTG, 597 3TC samples); 35 had intensive PK (7 per band; pre-dose, 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 24 h). Median age 6.0 y (1.0-11.0), weight 17.0 kg (8.15-39.3), 45.5% female (PDF p7).
- Assay: LC-MS/MS, plasma, LOQ 5 ng/mL, ULOQ 10,000 ng/mL for all three (ABC also an ultra-sensitive method, LOQ 50 pg/mL) (PDF p3).
- Method: three previously published pediatric PopPK models applied WITHOUT re-estimation (NONMEM 7.3.0, MAXEVAL=0), judged by GOF, VPC, NPDE, then used for 1000 x 1000-subject steady-state simulations (200 per weight band, CDC growth-chart weights/ages).
- Models are from other work: ABC and 3TC from an FDA clinical pharmacology review (ref 16: Ziagen/Epivir PREA review), DTG from Chandasana 2023 Clin Pharmacokinet (ref 15). The parameters below are those prior models, not new estimates.

## Model and parameters
All values are typical values from the prior models, restated in Tables 1-3 (PDF p5-7). Doses are oral, so CL and V are apparent (/F).

ABC: two-compartment, first-order absorption, weight allometry referenced to 15.6 kg (Table 1, PDF p5). Source population n=169, 5 mo-13 y, 4.6-61.3 kg.
DTG: one-compartment, first-order absorption, weight allometry referenced to 70 kg, maturation function on CL, formulation and food effects on F (Table 2, PDF p6). n=239, 2 mo-18 y, 3.9-91.0 kg.
3TC: one-compartment, first-order absorption with lag, weight allometry referenced to 18.5 kg (Table 3, PDF p7). n=209, 4 mo-19 y, 3.1-66.4 kg.

Simulated steady-state exposures by weight band (Table 4, geometric mean; text layer only), proposed daily doses ABC 180/240/300/360 mg DT then 600 mg tablet; DTG 15/20/25/30 mg DT then 50 mg tablet; 3TC 90/120/150/180 mg DT then 300 mg tablet:
- ABC AUC0-24 14.89-18.50 ug*h/mL across bands; target GM 6.3-50.4.
- DTG C24 0.74-0.95 ug/mL (target GM 0.697-2.26); AUC0-24 65.36-72.36 (target 37-134 ug*h/mL).
- 3TC AUC0-24 10.50-13.20 ug*h/mL (target 6.3-26.5).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD model, ke0 or EC50. Exposure targets only (PDF p3): ABC and 3TC AUC0-24, DTG C24 and AUC0-24, taken from adult/pediatric single-entity PK. The paper says AUC is the PK correlate of antiviral effect for ABC and 3TC (refs 19-21). Not an opioid paper; no MEAC, ke0 or potency ratio.

## Covariates
Body weight (allometric, exponents above) on CL and V for all three; DTG also maturation by post-menstrual age on CL and formulation/food on F; 3TC formulation on F; ABC study-specific F. No covariate re-testing was done (Discussion, PDF p12, printed 1888).

## Caveats
- External validation only; no re-estimation. VPCs showed over-prediction of variability and slight misspecification, mainly in sparse data; ABC C24 slightly over-predicted, 3TC C24 under-predicted (PDF p9-12).
- Small sample per weight band (7 intensive). Simulated 25-<40 kg band exposures are higher for ABC and 3TC than the other bands; the authors attribute this to actual participants clustering at 25-30 kg and receiving a higher mg/kg dose.
- Authors are employees of or paid by the sponsor (GSK/ViiV).
- ABC and 3TC source models are cited to an FDA review rather than a journal paper, so parameters cannot be checked against a primary publication from this paper alone.
