# Lohela 2021: rifampin and oral / IV hydromorphone

## Study
Paired, randomised, double-blind, two-phase crossover (6 weeks apart). 12 healthy volunteers (5 F, 7 M), 20-34 y,
56-100 kg, non-smokers. Rifampin 600 mg or placebo nightly for 8 days. Day 6: **2.6 mg oral hydromorphone HCl**
(Palladon IR capsule) at 8 am, fasted 8 h. Day 8: **0.02 mg/kg IV hydromorphone HCl** slow injection (median 1.5 mg,
IQR 1.3-1.9). Route order not randomised (oral always first). Midazolam 0.1 mg oral probe. Samples 0.5-24 h (plus
0.25 h after IV). UHPLC-MS/MS, LOQ 0.05 ng/mL hydromorphone, 0.1 HM3G. Noncompartmental (WinNonlin 6.4), AUC0-last
linear-up log-down; F = AUC0-last,oral·Dose_IV/(AUC0-last,IV·Dose_oral); CL/F = Dose_oral/AUC0-last.

## Model and parameters
Geometric means (geometric CV%); tmax median (range).

Table 1, oral 2.6 mg, placebo phase vs rifampin: Cmax 1.48 (37) vs 0.93 (35) ng/mL; tmax 1.0 (0.5-1.5) vs 0.50
(0.5-1.5) h; t½ 3.06 (22) vs 2.85 (36) h; AUC0-last 6.60 (39) vs 3.77 (46) ng·h/mL; CL/F 5.78 (38) vs 10.07 (46)
L/min; Vz/F 1528 (35) vs 2486 (38) L; F 0.33 (21) vs 0.26 (28). HM3G/HM ratio up 50%.

Table 2, IV 0.02 mg/kg, placebo vs rifampin: CL 1.88 (26) vs 2.55 (25) L/min; Vss 492 (30) vs 631 (29) L;
AUC0-last 11.85 (14) vs 8.72 (14) ng·h/mL; t½ 2.42 (12) vs 2.38 (10) h.

Introduction quotes oral F "13% to 62%" from Vallner 1981 (ref 19), not measured here.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Secondary end points over 6 h, AUC0-6 medians (Table 3): VAS drug effect, pupil diameter reduction, cold pressor
threshold; no significant rifampin effect; large variability.
- MEAC: none.
- ke0 / t½ke0 / tPeak / hysteresis: none.
- Potency: introduction says 5-7 times morphine (refs 13-15), not measured.

## Covariates
Rifampin (enzyme induction) only.

## Caveats
n = 12 young volunteers; oral dose small (2.6 mg) for safety; route order fixed; F and CL/F from AUC0-last, not
AUC0-inf; doses as HCl salt; Figure 1A shows geometric means with 90% CI (values not in the text layer).
