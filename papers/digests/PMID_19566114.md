# Punyawudho 2009: carbamazepine de-induction by an enzyme-turnover model

## Study
- 15 adults with epilepsy (6 women, 9 men; mean weight 78 kg, mean age 39 y) whose oral carbamazepine was being
  discontinued; recruited in Minneapolis, Miami and Atlanta; no interacting co-medications (PDF p2 / printed p314;
  demographics PDF p4 / p316).
- 100 mg stable-labelled (SL) carbamazepine IV over 10 min on three occasions: the morning after the last oral dose
  (that last dose mean ± SD 140 ± 50.7 mg, PDF p2, confirmed on page image), 6-8 days later, and 6-8 weeks later.
  14 completed all three occasions.
- 13 plasma samples per occasion: pre-dose and 0.083, 0.25, 0.5, 1, 2, 4, 6, 12, 24, 48, 72, 96 h after the end of
  infusion; 524 concentrations. LC-MS (Agilent 1100, carbamazepine-d10 internal standard). Total plasma
  concentration.
- NONMEM VI, ADVAN6, FOCE-I; PDx-Pop 2.0; exponential IIV, proportional residual error; bootstrap (500, 493
  minimised) and VPC.

## Model and parameters
One-compartment carbamazepine model linked to a hypothetical enzyme compartment (Figure 1, PDF p2):
- dA1/dt = k0 − CL · C · A2 (Eq. 1): clearance proportional to relative enzyme amount A2 (A2 = 1 at t = 0, the
  first SL dose).
- dA2/dt = kenz,in (1 − FACTOR) − kenz,out · A2 (Eq. 2, PDF p3); at t = 0 kenz,in = kenz,out (steady state).
  De-induction = a fractional fall (FACTOR) in enzyme production. t½ = ln 2 / kenz,out.
- Alternative (increasing degradation) fitted as well by OFV but judged less plausible; concentration-driven
  autoinduction models (linear, Emax) failed (PDF p4).
- Weight was the only covariate tested, linear, centred at 80 kg; retained on Vd only (ΔOFV −19.7). The exact form
  is not printed; presumably Vd = 64.3 + 0.869 (WT − 80) L (not stated).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No pharmacodynamic end point (no concentration-effect, seizure or toxicity data). The "end point" is SL-carbamazepine
clearance over 6-8 weeks after stopping. Clinical inferences (PDF p7): enzymes halve by 3.6 days and fall by
three-quarters by 7.2 days; de-induction complete in 2 weeks (abstract) or 2-3 weeks (conclusion); doses of co-drugs
such as hormonal contraceptives may need adjusting within 3-6 days. Comparator for the enzyme half-life: Magnusson
2008 probe estimates 70 h (CYP3A4) and 105 h (CYP1A2), which bracket it. Not an opioid: no MEAC, ke0 or potency ratio.

## Covariates
Weight on Vd only (linear, 0.869 L/kg about 80 kg). Weight not significant on CL or kenz,out. Nothing else tested.

## Caveats
- n = 15, small; kenz,out without IIV and imprecise (bootstrap upper bound 0.066 h⁻¹, t½ ~10.5 h).
- Patients were tapering (last oral dose mean 140 mg), so the "initial" state may already be partly de-induced; the
  taper history is not given.
- Hybrid enzyme half-life for all carbamazepine-metabolising enzymes, measured for de-induction only. That induction
  shares the rate constant is argued from theory (Abramson 1986), not measured here.
- Deinduction start assumed at the first SL dose; the sensitivity analysis says this matters little.
- Conflict of interest: three authors have a royalty agreement with Ovation (developing IV carbamazepine).
- Lamotrigine (listed in the context's drugs) is not studied in this paper.
