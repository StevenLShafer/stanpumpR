# Kochak 1998: steady-state zonisamide PK in healthy volunteers

## Study
Open, parallel two-regimen, 56-day multiple-dose study, 24 healthy volunteers (sponsor Dainippon). Titration
100 mg/day x 1 week, 200 mg/day x 1 week, then 400 mg/day to day 35. Group A (n = 16 enrolled): 200 mg q12h;
group B (n = 8): 400 mg q24h. 8 withdrew (6 for CNS effects) and 1 was indeterminate, so analysed n = 11 (A) and
n = 4 (B). Weight 83.0 +/- 9.23 kg (A), 88.9 +/- 6.97 kg (B); height 70.1 and 71.5 in; A = 6 black, 4 white,
1 Hispanic; all of B black. Sex not stated. 100 mg capsules, fasting on days 34-35.

Sampling: troughs every 4 days days 15-29 then daily; days 34-35 at 0.5-12 h; washout samples days 36-56.
Serum, reverse-phase HPLC-UV (internal standard N,N-dimethylzonisamide), linear 0.41-64 ug/mL, CV <= 2.6%.
Non-compartmental: Cl/F = dose/AUC(tau), t1/2 from terminal log-linear regression; ANOVA and mixed-effects variance
components.

Also: Cl/F 10.0-11.7 mL/min (Results); steady state by day 29, about 15 days after the last dose change; relative
bioavailability of q24h vs q12h 84.1% (statistically significant). Single-dose literature cited in the Introduction:
tmax 3.2 h, t1/2 52 h, CL/F 0.316 mL/min/kg, V 1.47 L/kg (not measured here). No volume estimated in this study.
Intersubject variance 98.2% of Cl/F random variance (Table III). Cl/F not related to weight (r^2 = 0.174).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD measured. Putative therapeutic range quoted as 15-25 ug/mL. Not an opioid: no MEAC, ke0, or potency ratio.

## Covariates
None modelled; weight not related to Cl/F; regimen (q24h vs q12h) changed exposure.

## Caveats
Very small group B (n = 4); 9 of 24 not analysed; parallel design; heavier-than-70 kg, mostly black healthy men(?)
(sex not reported); the q24h/q12h bioavailability difference may be dissolution-limited absorption. Table II's
confidence-interval column header (A/B) appears inverted relative to its values.
