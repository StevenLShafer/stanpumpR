# Patsalos 2008: ILAE best-practice guidelines for antiepileptic drug TDM

## Study
Position paper of the ILAE Subcommission on TDM (38 pages). Defines "reference range" (a laboratory-quoted range of concentrations below which a response is unlikely and above which toxicity is more likely) as distinct from a "therapeutic range" or an individual therapeutic concentration; the reference range is statistical, not a target. Reviews each AED's pharmacokinetics, interactions, concentration-effect evidence and assays, and gives indications for TDM.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Reference ranges are seizure-control/toxicity ranges in epilepsy, not effect-site or analgesic concentrations. Not an opioid; no MEAC, ke0 or potency.

## Covariates
Narrative: enzyme inducers, valproate inhibition, pregnancy, age, hypoalbuminaemia (use unbound concentrations for phenytoin and valproate in altered binding).

## Caveats
Expert consensus; many ranges rest on small or old studies; reference ranges explicitly are not therapeutic ranges; individual therapeutic concentrations preferred; Table 1 text layer interleaves columns.
