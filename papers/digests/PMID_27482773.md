# Brogi 2017: closed-loop systems meta-analysis

## Study
Systematic review and random-effects meta-analysis (PRISMA; R 3.2.1 with Hartung-Knapp adjustment, RevMan 5.3). MEDLINE, Embase, CENTRAL, 1999-2014. 407 records + 8 hand-found; 44 full texts; 36 RCTs included (Oxford quality score 2: 8 trials, 3: 23, 4: 4, 5: 1). Closed-loop versus manual control. Primary outcome: percentage of time the controlled variable (BIS, glucose, SpO2...) stayed in the target range. Secondary: time or episodes over/under the range.

## Model and parameters
No PK model. Pooled differences in % time in range, closed-loop minus manual (text pp. 448-452):
- Anesthesia drug delivery (15 trials; all BIS except one M-Entropy; BIS target 50 +/- 10 in 11): +17.4% (95% CI 11.7-23.1, P<0.0001, I2 0.86).
- Over/undershoot in anesthesia subgroup: -12.3% (95% CI -18.6 to -6.1, P=0.0009, I2 0.96) more in manual groups.
- ICU glucose control (3 trials): +24.6% (95% CI -20.5 to 69.7, P=0.14, not significant).
- Type 1 diabetes (8 trials): +21.2% (95% CI 11.5-30.9, P=0.001); time out of range -6.5% (95% CI -11.3 to -1.8).
- Ventilation (8 trials): +12.8% (95% CI 1.5-23.1, P=0.03, I2 75%).
- Vasopressor (2 trials): odds ratio 1.44 (95% CI 1.04-2.0) for measurements in range.
Trials of propofol or propofol + remifentanil closed loop (dual loop: 4 trials) are included; no drug concentrations or PK values are given.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No ke0, EC50, MEAC, or potency ratio. BIS target ranges only (50 +/- 10 in most trials). Not a PK/opioid-concentration paper.

## Covariates
None; heterogeneity attributed to age mix (one paediatric trial), target ranges, and observation duration.

## Caveats
High heterogeneity (I2 >75%); mixed domains (anesthesia, insulin, ventilation, vasopressor); mostly intermediate quality; only one trial blinded properly; no patient-outcome studies. The text layer scrambles Table 1; numbers here are from the running text only.
