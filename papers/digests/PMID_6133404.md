# Nielsen-Kudsk 1983: IV and IM lorazepam kinetics in six volunteers

## Study
- Randomized single-dose two-way crossover (IV vs IM), about 14 days apart.
- 6 healthy volunteers: 4 men, 2 women, 22-37 y (mean 30.2), 60-87 kg (mean 71.4) (Table 1).
- Dose about 2 mg (0.03 mg/kg; 0.025 mg/kg IV in subjects 5 and 6). Ativan injection.
  - IV: infused over 10 min (diluted 1:1 in saline).
  - IM: deep gluteus medius.
- Venous samples at 0, 0.083, 0.17, 0.25, 0.33, 0.5, 0.67, 1, 2, 4, 6, 8, 12, 24, 36 and 48 h.
- Assay: total plasma lorazepam by electron-capture GLC, oxazepam internal standard. About half the samples were confirmed by a direct GC method.
- Analysis: each subject's curve was fitted with NONLIN (sum of exponentials), weighted 1/C. Hybrid parameters were derived from the fitted coefficients and exponents.
- The paper prints the equations for the micro-constants: k10 = CL/Vc, k21 = alpha·beta/k10, k12 = alpha + beta − k10 − k21.

- t½β 14.01 ± 2.31 h; t½ absorption 0.67 ± 0.79 h (range 0.08-1.76).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: a binaural stimulation test of attention (recall errors per 5 min), during the IV study only (Fig. 2).
- Three of 6 subjects showed light to moderately severe impairment. It began 10-25 min after the dose and lasted more than 4 h in two of them.
- The effect could not be correlated with plasma concentrations.
- No ke0, EC50 or threshold is reported.
- Two subjects reported amnesia for 8-10 h after the IM dose.

## Covariates
None modelled; per-kg reporting only.

## Caveats
- n = 6. The means of Vc and t½α come from 5 subjects, while CL and t½β come from all 6, so these four means are not one consistent set.
- The IV dose was a 10-min infusion. The paper does not say whether the hybrid parameters were corrected for infusion time.
- Means of individual hybrid parameters are not the parameters of a "typical" subject.
- IM absorption varied widely. In the discussion the authors contrast their slow gluteal absorption with Greenblatt's faster deltoid absorption: t½abs 0.24 h, tmax 1.15 h, no lag, F 95.9%.
- The discussion quotes Greenblatt 1982 as t½β 12.9 h, t½α 0.15 h, Vd 1.30 L/kg, Vc 0.52 L/kg and CL 72.6 mL/kg/h.
