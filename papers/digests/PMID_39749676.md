# Yellepeddi 2025: sotalol after expedited intravenous loading (PEAKS registry)

## Study
- PK/PD substudy of the PEAKS registry (10 US sites, 2022-2023): 22 adults with atrial fibrillation or flutter,
  19 men, 20 white and 2 unknown (Table 1).
  - Age 69 (48-79) years, weight 104 (68.7-185) kg, serum creatinine 1.0 (0.7-1.26) mg/dL.
  - Cockcroft-Gault creatinine clearance 92.4 (64.3-306) mL/min; all medians (range).
  - Enrolment targeted creatinine clearance above 60 mL/min. Only 3 patients were older than 75.
- Dosing by the package insert: 60-120 mg intravenously over 1 h (median 92.3, range 45.6-150 mg), then two
  oral doses (median 100, range 80-120 mg), the first at least 4 h after the infusion and the second 12 h later.
- 99 plasma samples:
  - before the infusion, 0-5 min and 3 h after it, and before the first oral dose;
  - 2-4 h after each of the two oral doses.
  - Assay UPLC-MS/MS, lower limit 5 ng/mL; every sample was quantifiable.
- NONMEM 7.5, FOCE-I, PsN 5.3.0. Covariates were added forward at p < 0.05 and kept on backward elimination
  at p < 0.001. Bootstrap of 1,000 runs (96.7% successful) and a pc-VPC.

## Model and parameters
Two compartments, first-order absorption with no lag, proportional residual error. The between-patient
variability is on ka, CL and Vc.

The paper's own two tables disagree on two values:

| CL (L/h) | 9.5 | 9.5 | 9.5 |
| Vc (L) | 38.2 | 38.2 | 38 |
| Q (L/h) | 71.3 | 71.3 | 71 |
| Vp (L) | 89.1 | 89.1 | 89 |
| ka (/h) | 0.2 | 0.2 | 0.16 |
| F_oral, relative to IV | 1.6 | 1.5 | 1.6 |
| CrCl exponent on CL | 0.7 | 0.7 | 0.65 |
| Weight exponent on Vc | 1.1 | 1.2 | 1.2 |

**Data S1 is the final model.** Its CL and volume values are Table 2's to two figures, so its `$THETA`
values are the final fit's rounded estimates, not starting guesses (derived: comparison of the columns).
The weight exponent is consistent with all three columns only near 1.15: Table 2 rounds it down, Table 3
and Data S1 round it up. F_oral is likewise consistent with all three only near 1.55. The Discussion
quotes F = 1.5.

Covariate equations (Data S1, `$PK`):
- CL = θ × (CRCL/92.4)^θ, with CrCl by Cockcroft-Gault in mL/min.
- V2 (the central volume) = θ × (WTKG/104)^θ, with weight in kg.

Between-patient variability, as Data S1 `$OMEGA` variances:
- 0.23 on ka, 0.068 on CL and 0.21 on V2.
- Q, V3 and F1 are fixed at 0.
- Table 2 prints these as CV 50.5%, 26.6% and 48.1%.

Residual error: σ² 0.023, proportional.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- QTc (Bazett, corrected only above 60 bpm) against the individual predicted concentration, by linear
  regression on 104 QTc values from the 22 patients:
  - QTc = 440 ms + 0.015 ms per ng/mL (R² 0.1);
  - change from baseline = 11 ms + 0.019 ms per ng/mL (R² 0.29);
  - percent change = 1.7% + 0.0045% per ng/mL (R² 0.27).
- Concentrations ranged from 220 to 1,900 ng/mL.
- No effect compartment. QTc samples were not time-matched to concentrations; the authors list this as a
  limitation.

## Covariates
- Kept: creatinine clearance on CL and weight on Vc. Each lowered the OFV by 9.7 and 6.8 alone, and by 14.8
  together (Table S2).
- Tested and not kept: sex, NSAID, heart failure, beta-blocker, calcium-channel blocker, age (normalised to
  69 years) and coronary artery disease. None changed the OFV.
- BSA-normalised creatinine clearance did no better than the raw value.

## Caveats
- **Small sample.** 22 patients, 86% men, all white or unknown, and only 3 over 75.
- **Narrow renal range.** The study targeted creatinine clearance above 60, so the renal term is fitted over
  64-306 mL/min.
- **Oral absorption not identified.** The oral samples were drawn only 2-4 h after each dose, so ka and an
  F_oral above 1 are poorly determined. The labels give 90-100% bioavailability.
- **Bootstrap.** The bootstrap means differ from the estimates (Vc 48.2 against 38.2; Q 53.8 against 71.3).
- **Unexplained example values.** The Discussion quotes Vc of about 19 L at 68.69 kg and CL of about 5 L/h at
  64.3 mL/min. Neither follows from the typical-value equations: 38.2 × (68.69/104)^1.2 = 23.2 L and
  9.5 × (64.3/92.4)^0.65 = 7.5 L (derived). They may be individual estimates.

## Verification against stanpumpR
- **CL 9.5 L/h, Vc 38.2 L, Q 71.3 L/h, Vp 89.1 L at the reference CrCl 92.4 mL/min and 104 kg:** matches
  Table 2.
