# Parab 1988: hydromorphone PK after IV, oral and rectal doses

## Study
- 9 healthy men enrolled, 8 completed; 50-86 kg, 20-30 years (p.188). Individual weights not given.
- Three-way Latin-square crossover, 2-week washouts, overnight fast, standard breakfast at 2 h and meal at 4 h.
- Doses (hydromorphone HCl, Knoll commercial products): IV 2 mg over ≤2 min (t0 = midpoint); oral 4 mg tablet
  with 100 mL water; rectal 3 mg cocoa-butter suppository.
- Samples 0, 3, 7, 15, 30, 45 min, 1, 1.5, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12 h; plasma; radioimmunoassay.
- Analysis: Nonlin84 (IV: biexponential, weight 1/y; oral and rectal: one-compartment with lag), KINPAK
  (model-independent AUC, F, MRT), and Loo-Riegelman absorption analysis (Table 2).
- Partial support from Knoll (the manufacturer).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- No PD measured. No MEAC: the authors state the minimum analgesic plasma concentration of hydromorphone is
  not known (p.195).
- No ke0 / t½ke0 / tPeak / hysteresis.
- Potency (introduction, p.187, cited from Kerri-Szanto 1976, not measured here): hydromorphone 8.5 times as
  potent as morphine IV, 60 times meperidine epidurally.

## Covariates
None. Clearance and volumes reported per kg only.

## Caveats
- n = 8 healthy young men; 3 subjects per period; manufacturer-supported.
- RIA assay (possible metabolite cross-reactivity not discussed).
- The oral tablet released slowly in vitro (biphasic absorption), so oral F and ka may be formulation-specific.
- Huge between-subject spread of F (oral 0.20-0.96 by Loo-Riegelman).
- Several text/table SD mismatches (above); the IV beta SD 0.602 in Table 1 is likely a misprint for 0.062.
- Probably the same cohort as Ritschel 1987 (same group, same n, near-identical F).
