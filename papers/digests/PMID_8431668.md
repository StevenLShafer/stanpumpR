# Rudehill 1993: mannitol PK, serum/CSF osmolality and ICP during tumour craniotomy

## Study
15 patients for intracranial tumour surgery (6 M, 9 F; mean age 59 y, range 25-75; Table 1, mostly glioma). Weight and
height not reported. Thiopentone 3-5 mg/kg, pancuronium 0.1 mg/kg, phenoperidine + droperidol, 60% N2O, PaCO2 ~30 mm Hg.
Mannitol 20%, 1 g/kg over 30 min. Blood and CSF (lateral-ventricle catheter; one lumbar drain) at control, 0, 15, 30, 45,
60, 120, 180, 240, 300, 360, 480 min (Fig. 1). Assay: GC/MS for plasma and CSF mannitol. PK: SIPHAR, two-exponential
model with zero-order infusion, least squares, weight 1; CL = dose/AUC (trapezoidal), Vz = CL/b, Vc = dose/(A+B).
Renal clearance from urine. Data are mean ± SD.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- Serum osmolality peaked at end of infusion: 292 ± 7 -> 310 ± 14 mOsm/kg (p < 0.01), returning toward baseline;
  CSF osmolality essentially unchanged; positive blood-CSF gradient ~10 mOsm/kg for ~30 min (pp. 4, 7, 9).
- Serum Na 138 ± 4 -> 129 ± 4 mmol/L at 30 min; K unchanged.
- ICP (n = 5) 11 ± 4 -> 4 ± 2 mm Hg at 45 min (Table 2). CO 4.2 -> 5.3 L/min, PCWP 7.1 -> 10.2 mm Hg, hematocrit
  40 -> 36% at 30 min (Table 2).
- CSF mannitol never > 12% of plasma; plasma/CSF ratio 679 at 0.5 h, 8.6 at 8 h.
- No ke0, no osmolality-concentration model fitted. (derived: (310 - 292) / (5.91 g/L / 182.17 g/mol x 1000) =
  18 / 32.44 = 0.555 mOsm/kg per mOsm/L of mannitol; not computed by the authors.)

## Covariates
None examined.

## Caveats
- n = 15, tumour craniotomy under 1990s neuroanaesthesia; weights not reported, so CL and V are not per-kg-normalisable.
- Large SDs on A, alpha, k12 (individual two-exponential fits).
- Peak osmolality (n = 13) and peak mannitol (n = 14) are group means of slightly different subsets, not individual
  paired ratios.
- 8 h plasma value from n = 10.
