# West 1996: barometric pressure from altitude with model atmospheres

## Study
Special communication, no subjects and no drug. West compares the ICAO/US Standard Atmosphere with geophysicists' model atmospheres (Valley 1965, millibars converted to Torr by 0.750) for latitudes 15-75 N, July and January, 0-10 km. Fits a quadratic in ln(pressure) to the mean of the 15 N (all months) and 30 N (July) models, and tests it on 14 measured sites (3,094-8,848 m).

## Model and parameters
Equation (Table 2 footnote, PDF p3; abstract p1): P_B (Torr) = exp(6.63268 - 0.1112 h - 0.00149 h^2), h in km.
Table 2 (PDF p3, Torr): mean of the two models vs the equation: 0 km 760.0 vs 759.2; 1 km 678.3 vs 678.3; 3 km 536.1 vs 536.6; 5 km 419.5 vs 419.5; 7 km 324.4 vs 324.1; 9 km 247.5 vs 247.4; 10 km 214.9 vs 215.2.
Table 1 (PDF p2): Standard Atmosphere 760, 674, 596, 526, 462, 405, 354, 308, 267, 231, 199 Torr at 0-10 km; 15 N all months 760, 678, 604, 536, 475, 419, 369, 324, 283, 247, 214.
Table 3 (PDF p4): 14 sites, all predicted within 1% (largest 0.9%, Pike's Peak). Alps (46 N) predicted 3 Torr high; the 45 N July model cut the error to 1 Torr.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None (not a drug paper).

## Covariates
Latitude and season, not in the equation. The fit is for latitudes within 30 degrees of the equator (and 45 N in summer); the author says other models should be used at 45 N and above, especially in winter (45 N January is 13 Torr lower at 3 km). Weather causes 2-3 Torr scatter.

## Caveats
Weather variation not predictable; the equation fits the top of the range of model atmospheres; sites are mostly low latitude and summer. Sea level by the equation is 759.5 (or 759.2 as printed), not 760.
