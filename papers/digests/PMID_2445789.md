# Ritschel 1987: absolute oral and rectal bioavailability of hydromorphone

## Study
Latin-square crossover, 14-day washout. 9 healthy men enrolled, 20-30 y, 50-86 kg (69.11 ± 12.16); one excluded
(blank plasma cross-reacted with the antiserum), so n = 8. Hydromorphone HCl: 2 mg IV over ≤2 min (time zero at
mid-injection); 4 mg oral tablet with 100 mL water; 3 mg rectal suppository (cocoa butter), all Dilaudid (Knoll).
Fasted; breakfast at 2 h. Plasma at 0, 3, 7, 15, 30, 45 min, 1, 1.5, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12 h; saliva after IV.
**Radioimmunoassay** with morphine-6 antiserum (ranges 2.5-20 ng/mL and 250-1000 pg/mL). Model-independent
analysis (AUC-RPP program).

## Model and parameters
Table I (mean ± SD, n = 8): t½ IV 2.36 ± 0.58 h, PO 4.10 ± 0.65, rectal 3.80 ± 0.65 (IV differs, P < .05); AUC0-inf
IV 52.40 ± 29.87, PO 40.21 ± 10.32, rectal 21.10 ± 7.70 ng·h/mL (AUC0-10 50.57, 35.15, 17.54).
Table II: PO Cmax 14.18 ± 4.45 ng/mL, tmax 1.11 ± 0.25 h, F 0.5135 ± 0.2929; rectal Cmax 3.53 ± 1.36, tmax
1.41 ± 0.79 h, F 0.3633 ± 0.2960. Cmax normalised to 1 mg and 70 kg: PO 4.067 ± 1.794, rectal 1.219 ± 0.688 ng/mL.
Saliva/plasma ratio 0.25-2.32; saliva t½ 2.12 ± 0.93 vs plasma 2.36 ± 0.58 h (NS, Table III).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Side-effect counts only (Table IV): dizziness, euphoria, itching more frequent after IV; onset immediate after IV,
30-180 min after PO or rectal.
- MEAC: none; the authors state the minimum analgesic plasma concentration is not known.
- ke0 / tPeak: none.
- Potency: cites 8.5 times morphine IV and 60 times pethidine epidurally (intro, ref 2), not measured.

## Covariates
None.

## Caveats
n = 8 men; RIA with a morphine-6 antiserum (cross-reactivity excluded one subject; metabolite cross-reactivity not
reported). The IV AUC is about three times the LC-MS/MS values of Coda 2003, Davis 2004 and Lohela 2021 for the
same 2 mg, implying a clearance (derived ~0.64 L/min) far below theirs; if the assay over-reads, F may be biased.
Very large SD (29 percentage points). Different dose per route. The rectal discussion quotes "33%, 10-65%", which
does not match its own Table II (36%).
