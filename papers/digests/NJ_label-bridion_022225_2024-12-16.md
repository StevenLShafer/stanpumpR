# Bridion (sugammadex) label, revised 12/2024

## Study
FDA label for sugammadex, a modified gamma-cyclodextrin that encapsulates rocuronium and vecuronium.
Product: 100 mg/mL sugammadex (each mL equivalent to 108.8 mg sugammadex sodium; section 11), 2 and 5 mL
vials; may contain up to 7% mono-OH derivative (about 50% of the affinity; 12.2). MW 2178.01 (sodium salt).

Dosing (2.1-2.2): by **actual body weight**, single IV bolus over 10 s.
- 4 mg/kg: spontaneous recovery to 1-2 post-tetanic counts, no TOF response (rocuronium or vecuronium).
- 2 mg/kg: reappearance of T2 (rocuronium or vecuronium).
- 16 mg/kg: immediate reversal about 3 min after rocuronium 1.2 mg/kg (rocuronium only; not studied in children).
- Paediatric: may dilute to 10 mg/mL.

- Linear kinetics 1-16 mg/kg; no plasma-protein or erythrocyte binding (drug or complex); no metabolites.
- Retained in bone and teeth in animals (mean half-life 172 and 8 days).
- Exposure about 40% lower in children < 6 y than in older children and adults (not clinically relevant per label).
- Obesity (BMI >= 40; ABW n = 76, IBW n = 74): IBW dosing gives about 50% lower exposure than ABW.
- Age: beyond its effect on creatinine clearance, limited impact (popPK).
- No sex or Japanese/Caucasian differences.
- Median time to TOF ratio 0.9 after 2 mg/kg (8.5): 2.2 min (18-64 y), 2.5 min (65-74 y), longer in >= 75 y.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point is recovery of TOF ratio to 0.9 (clinical studies). No concentration-effect relationship or ke0 in the
label; clear dose-response in trials of 0.5-16 mg/kg. QTc not prolonged at twice the maximum dose. Not an opioid.

Measured reversal times, 14.1 (PDF pp.20-23; printed page numbers the same). Adult trials randomized,
safety-assessor blinded; medians and quartiles are product-limit (Kaplan-Meier) estimates with censoring:
- **Moderate block, reappearance of T2** (189 patients): sugammadex 2 mg/kg vs neostigmine 50 mcg/kg, time from
  start of the reversal dose to TOF (T4/T1) 0.9. After rocuronium (Figure 1, PDF p.21): median 1.4 min (Q1-Q3
  1.2-1.7), N=48, vs neostigmine 21.5 min (9.8-42.0), N=48. After vecuronium (Figure 2, PDF p.22): 2.1 min
  (1.8-3.4), N=48, vs 29.0 min (12.2-76.2), N=45.
- **Deep block, 1-2 post-tetanic counts** (157 patients; PDF p.22): sugammadex 4 mg/kg, same end point: median
  2.7 min (25th-75th percentiles 2.1-4.3) after rocuronium, N=37, 7 censored; 3.3 min (2.3-6.6) after
  vecuronium, N=47, 6 censored. The comparator was neostigmine 70 mcg/kg, not expected to reverse block this deep;
  no neostigmine median is printed.
  The FDA Summary Review (`NJ_fda-nda022225_022225_022225Orig1s000SumR.pdf`, PDF p. 23) reprints the 2008 efficacy
  table: in the deep-block study (302) the neostigmine times were 50:22 (rocuronium) and 66:12 (vecuronium) min:s
  against sugammadex 2:52 and 4:28; its statistic is not stated and its sugammadex times differ from this label's
  medians, so the two documents' numbers are not interchangeable.
- **Immediate reversal, a different end point** (110 patients; PDF pp.22-23, Table 5): sugammadex 16 mg/kg given
  3 min after rocuronium 1.2 mg/kg vs spontaneous recovery from succinylcholine 1 mg/kg; end point **T1 recovery
  to 10% of baseline, timed from the neuromuscular blocker** (not from sugammadex): rocuronium/sugammadex median
  4.2 min (range 3.5-7.7), mean 4.4 (SD 0.7), N=55; succinylcholine median 7.1 min (range 3.8-10.5), mean 7.1
  (SD 1.6), N=55. Not comparable numerically with the TOF-0.9 times above.
- Paediatric, moderate block, TOF ratio >= 0.9 (PDF p.23): 2 to <17 y, 2 mg/kg (N=33) geometric mean 1.7 min vs
  neostigmine (N=34) 7.4 min (ratio 0.22, 95% CI 0.16-0.32). Birth to <2 y, 2 mg/kg (N=29) median 1.4 min vs
  neostigmine (N=31) 4.4 min (hazard ratio 2.40, 95% CI 1.37-4.18); 4 mg/kg median 1.1 min. Neostigmine dose
  not stated in these paragraphs.
All are dose-response and comparator results, not a plasma concentration-effect function.

## Covariates
Renal function is the main determinant. Mild/moderate impairment (CrCL >= 30 and <= 80 mL/min): no dose
adjustment (8.6). **Severe impairment (CrCL < 30 mL/min), including dialysis: not recommended** (5.11, 8.6),
because of limited safety data and prolonged, higher exposure.

## Caveats
Label values are rounded summaries; the 88 mL/min and 11-14 L are adult typical values from different
analyses (compartmental vs non-compartmental).
