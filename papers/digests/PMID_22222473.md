# Hendrickx 2008: Is synergy the rule? (anesthetic interactions, hypnosis and immobility)

## Study
Narrative literature review (PubMed search, human and animal data) of pairwise interactions among IV anesthetics, opioids, alpha-2 agonists, ketamine, dopamine antagonists, lidocaine and inhaled anesthetics for two end points: hypnosis (loss of response to command or syringe drop; loss of righting reflex in animals) and immobility (no movement to incision or tetanus; tail clamp in animals). Studies were reanalysed where possible with the normalized-sum rule below, and by response-surface modelling when full data existed. No new experiments; a per-study spreadsheet is a web supplement. Printed pp. 494-506 (PDF pp. 1-13).

## Model and parameters
No PK model. Definitions (printed pp. 495-496): with each drug's dose or concentration normalized to its own ED50 for the end point, synergy = normalized sum < 0.9, additivity = 0.9 to 1.1, infra-additivity (antagonism) = > 1.1. A drug with a ceiling (cannot reach the end point alone, e.g. opioids for immobility) is "synergy by definition" if it shifts the other drug's dose-response curve left by more than 10% (Appendix, pp. 501-503).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No ke0, EC50, MEAC or equianalgesic ratios are reported. Opioid audit: none (no MEAC, no ke0/t1/2ke0/tPeak, no potency ratio). Findings (Fig. 1 grid and text, pp. 496-500):
- Most cross-class pairs are synergistic; ketamine with GABA-A drugs is additive to infra-additive (propofol-ketamine reanalysis infra-additive for immobility, P = 0.02; thiopental-ketamine infra-additive for hypnosis, P < 0.001).
- Propofol with opioids: hypnosis synergistic in 11 of 13 studies; immobility synergistic (isobole bends toward the origin) for fentanyl and remifentanil.
- Opioids with inhaled anesthetics: MAC reduction with a ceiling = synergy by definition (Fig. 2, fentanyl with isoflurane and desflurane). In humans morphine does not change MACawake of halothane, isoflurane or sevoflurane (infra-additive).
- Inhaled anesthetics with each other: additive on MAC; N2O with isoflurane infra-additive in rats. Propofol with sevoflurane additive in humans.
- N2O at 67% or at least half MAC lowered the propofol concentration that blocked movement to incision by 25-35% (infra-additive).
- Midazolam with propofol synergistic for hypnosis; midazolam with ketamine infra-additive for hypnosis on reanalysis.
- Only hypnosis and immobility are assessed; synergy for ventilatory depression or hypotension is noted as possible but not analysed.

## Caveats
Search-term limited; single studies for many pairs; animal data extrapolated across species; classification depends on analysis method ("additivity by default"). Several older studies lack full dose ranges, so classification rests on ceiling arguments.
