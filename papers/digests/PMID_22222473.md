# Heyse 2012: sevoflurane-remifentanil response surface models

## Study
- Randomized, open-label, criss-cross design (PDF p. 2-3). 40 ASA I patients (26 F, 14 M), age 18-60 yr (mean 30 +/- 11), weight 66 +/- 11 kg, height 172 +/- 8 cm; weight 70-130% of ideal body weight. Unpremedicated, before surgery.
- Remifentanil by TCI with the Minto 1997 three-compartment model and effect site; sevoflurane by vaporizer. Targets 0-12 ng/mL remifentanil and 0.5-3.5 vol% sevoflurane, four pairs per patient (160 pairs; 159 analysed), 10 escalating combinations x 3 patients plus 5 sevoflurane-only and 5 remifentanil-only patients (Table 1, PDF p. 3).
- 12 min equilibration at each step; then stimuli in rising order: OAA/S, 5 s tetanic ulnar stimulus (100 Hz, 60 mA), LMA insertion, laryngoscopy. OAA/S 0-1 counted as tolerant of shake and shout.
- Remifentanil Ce taken as the target after 12 min (Minto's age-dependent equilibration half-times 0.94, 1.32 and 2.20 min at 20, 50, 80 yr, quoted not measured); sevoflurane Ce taken as measured end-tidal concentration (reported half-life 2.4 min, from Rehberg 1999).
- Analysis: NONMEM VI, FOCE LAPLACE LIKELIHOOD; 1000-set bootstrap and log-likelihood profiles. Five models compared: Greco, Reduced Greco, Minto, Scaled C50O Hierarchical, Fixed C50O Hierarchical (model equations in the Appendix, PDF p. 11-13).

Model structure (Appendix): P = U^g/(1+U^g); Fixed C50O Hierarchical U = UH(1+UO^gO) with UO = CO/C50O, UH = CH/C50H, C50H scaled per stimulus; equals Reduced Greco when gammaO = 1. ## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End points: quantal tolerance of shake and shout (OAA/S <=1), tetanic stimulation, LMA insertion, laryngoscopy; no EEG or ventilation.
- Opioid audit. No MEAC or analgesic concentration reported. Remifentanil C50O (concentration that halves the hypnotic Ce50, from the Fixed model) 1.69 ng/mL, effect-site concentration, not total/free plasma, and the assay is not applicable (TCI-predicted). Minto-model C50O 14.3 ng/mL is outside the data range.
- ke0: none estimated. Remifentanil equilibration half-times 0.94, 1.32, 2.20 min (20, 50, 80 yr) are quoted from Minto 1997. No tPeak. No equianalgesic or potency ratio.
- Context: the paper says the Fixed model is meant for opioid-hypnotic interaction displays; the Ce50 remifentanil that reduced MAC of sevoflurane was set at 1.5 ng/mL from earlier literature when designing the grid.

## Covariates
Weight, height, age, sex and order of drug administration were tested and none improved the fit.

## Caveats
- Sevoflurane Ce taken as end-tidal after 12 min; no effect-site model. Remifentanil Ce is the TCI target, not measured.
- Sevoflurane Ce50s differ from the same group's 2009 propofol-sevoflurane study (authors' own comparison, Table 4); cause unexplained.
- The authors say the conclusions should not be carried over to other drug classes. Prediction performance of the Reduced Greco, Scaled and Fixed models is similar (RMSE 22.6-23.0%).
