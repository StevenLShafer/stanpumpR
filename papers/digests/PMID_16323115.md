# Jones 2006: microbiology of vancomycin (MIC creep, MBCs, tolerance, breakpoints)

## Study
This is a review plus original SENTRY data from JMI Laboratories.
- **MIC surveillance:** SENTRY Antimicrobial Surveillance Program, 1997-2003, using reference NCCLS broth microdilution. 75,168 gram-positive isolates: 35,458 *S. aureus*, 5902 CoNS, 22,103 *S. pneumoniae*, 3315 *E. faecium* and 8390 *E. faecalis* (the last not tabulated) (PDF p2, printed S14).
- **MBC/tolerance experiment:** 213 *S. aureus* strains: 105 wild-type (wt) MRSA from 150 SENTRY centres, 88 hVISA confirmed by population-analysis profiling, 17 VISA (CDC definition) and 3 VRSA (from NARSA). MIC by reference broth microdilution, then MBC by quantitative subculture of the whole volume of each clear well. Tolerance means MBC:MIC >= 32, or >= 16 together with a resistant-level MBC (>= 32 ug/mL). Daptomycin was tested alongside (PDF p2).
- All concentrations are ug/mL (equal to mg/L). The text layer renders the micro sign as "m", and the PDF images confirm ug/mL.

**MIC surveillance (rendered Tables 1-3, PDF p4-5):**
- *E. faecium*: in vancomycin-susceptible isolates, MIC50 was 0.5-1 and MIC90 was 1 ug/mL. Vancomycin resistance ran 29.0-39.6% (75.2% in 2003, an enriched sample), and the vanA gene was found in 78-88%.
- *S. pneumoniae*: MIC50 0.25 (0.5 in 2000) and MIC90 0.5 ug/mL in every year.
- CoNS: MIC50 2 (1 in 2002-2003) and MIC90 2 ug/mL. 0.8-2.7% had an MIC of 4.
- *S. aureus*: MIC50 and MIC90 1 ug/mL in every year, 1998-2003. 4.7-7.8% had an MIC of 2.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- These are in-vitro end points only: MIC, MBC and the MBC:MIC ratio. There is no concentration-time target, no AUC/MIC, and no serum or free-drug conversion.
- Daptomycin had an MBC:MIC of 1 or 2 in every tested strain (Table 6, PDF p9; wt MRSA 77.1% / 22.9%).
- A kill curve against VISA Mu50 (Figure 5, adapted from Rybak) shows vancomycin is not bactericidal over 24 h.
- Not an opioid: no MEAC, ke0 or potency ratio.

## Covariates
None.

## Caveats
- **MBCs were measured only in *S. aureus*** (four MRSA groups). The paper has no MBC for CoNS, enterococci or streptococci.
- The 213-strain set deliberately over-represents hVISA, VISA and VRSA, and how the 105 wt MRSA were chosen is not described.
- The author's laboratory runs SENTRY, and the conflict-of-interest list is long.
- The historical PK figures in the breakpoint history (Cmax 20-50 ug/mL, half-life 6 h; PDF p5) are background only.
