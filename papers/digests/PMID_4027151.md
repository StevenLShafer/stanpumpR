# Campbell 1985: nicardipine IV bolus PK and haemodynamic PD in normotensive men

## Study
Fourteen normotensive male volunteers, 21-40 y, unrestricted sodium intake (PDF p2, printed 56S). Phase I: ten subjects, two per dose, nicardipine or vehicle in a randomised double-blind design at 10, 20, 40, 80 and 160 ug/kg IV over 1 min, doses ascending, at least 1 week apart. Phase II: four more subjects given 160 ug/kg or placebo (double-blind). PK was fitted only for the six subjects who received 160 ug/kg (lower doses gave concentrations too low to characterise individuals). Whether the dose is nicardipine base or hydrochloride is not stated. Plasma (lithium heparin, on ice, stored at -20 C) assayed by HPLC that separates parent drug from its inactive metabolite (Wu 1984). Blood pressure by Bosomat II sphygmomanometer, heart rate by ECG, systolic time intervals by computer from 30 s of recording, heart-rate corrected. Sampling: haemodynamics at 1-5, 10, 15, 20, 30, 60, 90, 120 min then hourly to 360 min; blood at 0, 3, 5, 10, 20, 30, 60, 90, 120 min then hourly to 360 min. Computer: Nodecrest V77/600. Dose in mg for the six subjects 9.6-11.5 (Table 1).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Not an opioid; no MEAC. Sheiner-type effect-compartment model (Figure 2, Sheiner 1979): linear effect E = m.CE + i, CE from the biexponential plasma curve and keq (the rate constant of loss from the effect compartment, min-1). Individual keq (min-1), Table 3 diastolic BP: 0.734, 0.115, 0.422, 0.201, 0.364, 0.012 (mean 0.308, r2 0.29-0.69); Table 4 heart rate: 0.638, 0.634, 1.560, 0.784, 0.172, 0.112 (mean 0.65, r2 0.88-0.98); Table 5 means for LVET 2.32 and PEP 0.14 (five of six subjects, r2 0.77 and 0.69). Slopes m: DBP -0.037, -0.058, -0.033, -0.047, -0.018, -0.182 (mean magnitude 0.062 mmHg per unit concentration); HR 0.121, 0.055, 0.101, 0.051, 0.155, 0.050 (mean 0.089); LVET 80.0 ms, PEP -83.0 ms. Units of m are inconsistent in the paper (see open question). Equivalent half-lives (derived: ln2 / mean keq): DBP 2.2 min, HR 1.1 min, LVET 0.3 min, PEP 4.9 min; the individual keq values span 0.012-1.56 /min, so the means are poorly determined. No tPeak is reported. Observed effects: DBP fell by an average maximum 22.3 mmHg, significant 1-30 min; HR rose a maximum 34.6 beats/min, significant 1-30 min; systolic BP, QS2-corrected and QT-corrected unchanged; LVET-corrected prolonged (max 26.9 ms at 2 min), PEP shortened (max 17.3 ms at 10 min). Authors read the small lag as close coupling of effect to plasma concentration.

## Covariates
None analysed (six young men, doses 9.6-11.5 mg).

## Caveats
Six men, one dose level with usable kinetics; PK by individual biexponential fits with a single 1-min bolus, so the first sample is at 3 min and lambda1 is poorly sampled (t1/2 lambda1 of 4 min against a 3-min first sample). Effect fits for DBP are weak (r2 0.29-0.69). m units inconsistent (above). Salt form of the dose not stated. Systolic time intervals are indirect measures of contractility; the authors attribute the contractility changes to reflex afterload reduction. Side effects (flushing, palpitation) in the first 3-4 min.

## Verification against stanpumpR
- vasoactiveRegistry.csv:39: **matches** for the one claim made about this paper, that it is an intravenous study with compartmental data (Table 1, Table 2). The claim that Ayub's Table 1 lists it cannot be verified here.
- Uncited comparison with the implemented model (`R/drugs_nicardipine.R`:79-83, Modi 1993 means, per kg, hydrochloride): Modi CL 0.920 L/h/kg and Vss 1.13 L/kg. Campbell's six-man mean CL is 71.5 L/h, about 1.06 L/h/kg (derived, 160 ug/kg assumed), 15% above Modi's; Vss about 0.95 L/kg (derived), 16% below Modi's. This is the same direction as the model header's remark that its clearance sits at the fast end of published studies (header lines 49-51, which quote 0.31-0.91 L/h/kg from Modi's own discussion). Campbell's rapid clearance is a bolus fit with a 3-min first sample, not a 30-min infusion.
- Effect site: the library uses tPeak 1.2 min (Cheung 1999; header says ke0 2.9 /min on its plasma model, line 67). Campbell's mean keq are 0.65 /min (HR) and 0.308 /min (DBP), 4.5 and 9 times smaller (derived), on a different disposition model and a 1-min injection; they would imply a later peak. Campbell does not report a tPeak, and the library does not cite it. Not a discrepancy in the code; a possible independent check on the effect-site time.
