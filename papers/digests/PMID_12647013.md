# Delord 2003: population pharmacokinetics of oxaliplatin

PubMed metadata were empty in the context file; citation and DOI are from the PDF's first page (PDF p1, printed 127).

## Study
40 adults with advanced colorectal cancer (22 M / 18 F; Table 1, PDF p2, printed 128, confirmed on page image):
age mean 59 y (29-82), weight 65 kg (45-96), height 165 cm (148-179), BSA (DuBois) 1.71 m² (1.40-2.09), plasma
creatinine 83 µmol/L (39-153), Cockcroft-Gault CrCl 78 mL/min (28-135), protein 71 g/L (57-80), haemoglobin 12.0
(8.4-16.9), unit printed "g/102 ml".
Oxaliplatin 3-h IV infusion on day 1 with 5FU/folinic acid: 130 mg/m² q3w (17 patients), 80 mg/m² q2w (12), or
100 mg/m² q2w (11). Blood sampled at 1.5, 3, 3.25, 3.5, 4, 5, 8 and 24 h after start; urine 0-5 h. Cycles 1-3
(40, 34, 21 patients). Platinum (expressed as oxaliplatin) by flameless atomic absorption; LOQ 10 ng/mL plasma
ultrafiltrate (30 kDa cut-off) and urine, 25 ng/mL whole blood. NONMEM V; proportional IIV and residual error.
Two separate analyses: plasma ultrafilterable + urine (24-h ultrafiltrate sample excluded, as mostly inactive
amino-acid conjugates) and whole blood (through 24 h after the third infusion).

## Model and parameters
**Plasma ultrafilterable**: 2-compartment, first-order elimination from central; residual error 24% (p2).
Table 2 (PDF p3, printed 129, image-confirmed): CL 18.7 L/h cycle 1 (n=40, IIV 43%); 17.7 L/h cycle 2 (−1%,
95% CI ±11%); 16.5 L/h cycle 3 (−14%, 95% CI ±8%); V1 40.8 L (IIV 43%); V2 21.0 L (IIV NE); urinary elimination
34.3% of total (IIV 35%). Intercompartmental clearance not reported.
Covariate model (p2, image-confirmed): CL (L/h) = θ1·BSA + θ2·BSA·(140 − age)·(1 − 0.15·sex)/Scr, sex 0 = male,
1 = female, Scr in µmol/L; θ1 = 5.49 ± 2.86 (L/h per m²), θ2 = 4.55 ± 3.14 (L/h per m² × µM) (mean ± 95% CI).
IIV in CL falls from 43% to 33% with covariates.

**Whole blood**: 3-compartment, first-order elimination from central, no interoccasion variability, residual 19%.
Table 3 (PDF p3, image-confirmed; the text layer misassigns the IIV column): CL 0.127 L/h (IIV 50%), V1 17.9 L
(IIV 25%), k12 2.97 h⁻¹ (NE), k21 1.77 h⁻¹ (NE), k13 0.0747 h⁻¹ (NE), k31 0.0119 h⁻¹ (IIV 36%).
Covariates (p4, text layer): CL (L/h) = θ1·BSA·(1 − θ2·Hb), θ1 = 0.28 ± 0.07 L/h per m², θ2 = 0.058 ± 0.006
(100 mL/g), Hb in g/100 mL; IIV 50% → 32%.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No ke0, EC50 or concentration-effect model. Neuropathy (grade 1-2 acute in 19/31 after 4 courses; grade 1
cumulative in 6/15 after ≥8 courses) and haematological toxicity were not correlated with AUC, Cmax, Hb or
protein (p4). No therapeutic or toxic concentration given. Not an opioid paper.

## Covariates
Ultrafilterable CL: BSA, age, sex, serum creatinine (Cockcroft-Gault form; alternative age/sex coefficients did
not improve fit). Blood CL: BSA and haemoglobin (negative); authors advise against dosing on the Hb relation.
Apparent dose-dependence of CL disappeared once covariates were included.

## Caveats
Total platinum, not intact oxaliplatin, is measured (mixture of biotransformation products). Urine only 0-5 h, so
renal fraction relies on the model. Small n (40). Internal inconsistencies: text mentions "85 mg/m²" though the
protocols were 80, 100 and 130 mg/m² (p2); Figure 1 says n=20 at 130 mg/m² vs 17 patients in Methods; Table 1's
Hb unit "g/102 ml" is presumably g/100 mL as in the equation. Co-administered 5FU/folinic acid.
