# Lee 2014: serum progesterone vs sevoflurane and analgesic requirement at caesarean

## Study
- Prospective observational study at Chonbuk National University Hospital, Korea, November 2011 to March 2013.
- 100 ASA I-II women aged 20-45 y, singleton pregnancy over 36 weeks, planned caesarean under general anaesthesia.
- 10 were excluded after enrolment (difficult airway 2, severe bleeding 4, PCA stopped for PONV 4), leaving 90 analysed (Fig. 1, PDF p3/printed 903).
- Table 1 (p3/903) reports N = 100, but the paper says 90 were analysed. Age 33 ± 4 y, height 160 ± 6 cm, weight 70 ± 10 kg, GA 37.9 ± 0.9 wk, operation 70 ± 14 min.
- Progesterone: venous serum before induction, by electrochemiluminescence immunoassay (Roche Progesterone II, E170). Measuring range 0.03-60 ng/mL; samples were diluted 5-10x, and 400 ng/mL was the study ceiling.
- Induction: thiopental 4-5 mg/kg and rocuronium 0.8 mg/kg, rapid sequence. Sevoflurane 3 vol% with 50% N2O at 6 L/min until end-tidal sevoflurane reached 2.0%, then 3 L/min.
- Maintenance: end-tidal sevoflurane 0.5-2.0% titrated to MAP/HR within 20% of baseline and BIS 40-60. N2O fixed at 50%. No intraoperative opioid.
- Primary end point: liquid sevoflurane consumption in mL/h, measured by the Dräger Zeus DIVA direct-injection system.
- Postoperative: PCA solution of morphine 40 mg, ketorolac 180 mg and ondansetron 8 mg in 60 mL. 3 mL bolus at skin closure, then 1 mL demand doses, 8-min lockout, no background infusion. VAS at 2, 24 and 48 h.
- Statistics: Pearson correlation on log-transformed data. A median split at 102.1 ng/mL compared by t test or Mann-Whitney.

## Model and parameters
There is no PK or PD model. Results (checked on the rendered pages):
- Progesterone 128.8 ± 84.2 ng/mL, range 23-400 (Table 1, p3/903). The abstract gives 128.2 ± 83.0. Median 102.1 ng/mL.
- Sevoflurane consumption 12.0 ± 1.9 mL/h. It correlates with progesterone at r = -0.26 (95% CI -0.44 to -0.05, P = 0.01; Fig. 2, p3/903).
- Cumulative PCA volume (Table 2, p3/903): 11.9 ± 3.0 mL at 2 h, 30.7 ± 11.6 mL at 24 h and 47.0 ± 14.7 mL at 48 h. Correlations with progesterone: r = -0.20 (P = 0.05), -0.25 (P = 0.02) and -0.28 (P = 0.01).
- 48-h morphine is about 31.3 mg (derived: 47.0 mL × 40 mg/60 mL), given with about 141 mg of ketorolac.
- VAS at rest: 4.8 ± 1.5, 2.9 ± 1.3 and 1.1 ± 1.0 cm. On deep breathing: 5.6 ± 1.7, 3.8 ± 1.5 and 3.0 ± 1.3 cm (Table 2).
- Low vs high progesterone (Table 3, p4/904; n = 45 each; progesterone 69.3 ± 21.9 vs 188.4 ± 81.3 ng/mL):
  - Sevoflurane 12.4 ± 2.0 vs 11.6 ± 1.6 mL/h (P = 0.02).
  - 48-h PCA 50.3 ± 15.2 vs 43.7 ± 13.6 mL. Table 3 gives P = 0.04; the text gives P = 0.02.
  - VAS not different.
- MAP, HR and BIS did not differ between groups (Table 4). BIS after delivery was about 51-53.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- Not a MAC study: the end point is the volume of liquid sevoflurane delivered per hour while BIS and haemodynamics were titrated. That volume depends on fresh gas flow and vaporizer setting, not only on brain sensitivity.
- No end-tidal-to-requirement relation is reported.
- Opioid (morphine, by PCA with ketorolac):
  - MEAC: none reported. No morphine concentration was measured, so there is no total vs free or blood vs plasma assay.
  - ke0, t½ke0, tPeak or hysteresis: none.
  - Equianalgesic or potency ratio: none.
- The only opioid end point is cumulative PCA volume, which is inversely related to progesterone (r = -0.28 at 48 h).
- Nitrous oxide was fixed at 50% and not titrated, so the paper gives no N2O requirement.
- No isoflurane or desflurane was given.

## Covariates
- Serum progesterone, analysed as a log-transformed continuous variable and by a median split.
- Age, weight, height, GA, parity and operation time did not differ between the two groups.

## Caveats
- All patients were at term; there is no non-pregnant comparator. The comparative claim ("lower" consumption) is high vs low progesterone within term parturients.
- The correlations are weak (r of about -0.2 to -0.3), and the authors say the clinical significance is unclear.
- Consumption in mL/h is a flow-dependent surrogate for requirement.
- Other hormones were not measured, nor was the diurnal variation in progesterone.
- The paper is internally inconsistent:
  - The progesterone mean differs between the abstract and Table 1.
  - The 48-h PCA P-value is 0.04 in Table 3 and 0.02 in the text.
  - Table 1 gives N = 100 for a 90-patient analysis.
  - The Fig. 3 axis is labelled "concentration (ml)".
