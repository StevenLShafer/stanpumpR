# Tuo 2025: clobazam and N-desmethylclobazam population PK in Chinese children with epilepsy

Clobazam is not a stanpumpR drug; it is a registry-only entry (`antiseizureRegistry.csv`), so `drugs` is empty.

## Study
- Prospective, single-centre, Wuhan Children's Hospital, December 2022 to March 2024 (Section 2.1, PDF p3).
- 103 children with refractory epilepsy (Lennox-Gastaut, Dravet, infantile spasms, other), 58 M / 45 F. Age mean
  5.94 (SD 3.15) y, median 5.46 (0.85-16.75); weight mean 22.94 (10.48) kg, median 20.00 (6.60-73.00); height median
  112 cm (71-171); eGFR (modified Schwartz) median 126.17 (33.33-240.85) mL/min/1.73 m2 (Table 1, PDF p7).
- CYP2C19 genotype (Sanger sequencing): RM 2 (1.94%), NM 41 (39.81%), IM 45 (43.69%), PM 15 (14.56%); no UM. The 2 RMs
  were not modelled separately (PDF p7).
- Clobazam tablets (Yichang Humanwell), twice daily if the dose exceeded 5 mg; start 5 mg (<=30 kg) or 10 mg (>30 kg),
  then titrated (Section 2.2, PDF p3).
- Co-medication: valproate 80.58%, lamotrigine 28.16%, perampanel 24.27%, levetiracetam 18.45%, topiramate 17.48%;
  ketogenic diet 6.80%; 75.73% on three or more antiseizure drugs (PDF p6).
- Opportunistic sampling from scavenged residual blood: 302 concentrations (154 CLB, 148 N-CLB); 68 patients sampled
  once, 21 twice, 12 three times, one 4 and one 6 times (PDF p6). The abstract says 156 samples were used for model
  development; the Results do not reconcile 156 with 302 concentrations.
- Assay: HPLC-MS/MS (QTRAP 5500), plasma; quantitation range 3-1200 ug/L CLB and 40-16,000 ug/L N-CLB; accuracy CV <5%
  (Section 2.3, PDF p4).
- Phoenix NLME 8.3.4; stepwise covariate search (forward dOFV > 3.84, backward > 6.63); bootstrap n = 1000, GOF, VPC,
  pc-VPC (PDF p5-6).

## Model and parameters
Tandem one-compartment model: CLB central compartment with first-order absorption (Ka fixed), first-order conversion to
one N-CLB compartment (Eqs. 1-3, PDF p5). Fm (fraction converted) was not estimated, so the N-CLB parameters are
CL/Fm and V/Fm. A two-compartment model gave dOFV -9.66 but RSE >100% and was rejected (PDF p7). IIV on CL/F and
CL_N-CLB/Fm only; proportional residual error.

Final model (Eqs. 8-12 and Table 2, PDF p8; typical values for 70 kg, NM):

Derived half-lives from the typical values (derived: ln2 x V/CL): CLB 11.3 h at 70 kg, 8.2 h at 20 kg; N-CLB 1.3 h at
70 kg NM, 4.6 h PM. The paper's own introduction quotes 36-42 h for CLB and 71-82 h for N-CLB (PDF p2), and says N-CLB
takes up to three weeks to reach steady state. The fitted model therefore does not reproduce the known time course,
most clearly for N-CLB; the paper does not comment on this. The ratio V/CL, and so the N-CLB half-life, does not depend
on the unestimated Fm.

Post hoc means by genotype (text, PDF p9 and p14): CL_CLB/F 2.39 / 2.18 / 3.10 L/h (NM / IM / PM, not significantly
different); CL_NCLB/Fm 0.46 / 0.34 / 0.13 L/h (PM lower, p < 0.01; 71.7% lower than NM). Simulated steady-state N-CLB/CLB
ratios (NM / IM / PM): AUC24 6.77 / 8.28 / 27.05; Cmax 5.81 / 6.74 / 18.54; Cmin 8.18 / 10.79 / 51.40 (PDF p11).

Observed concentrations (Table 1, PDF p7): CLB mean 137.01 (SD 92.74), median 114.50 (15.40-495.00); N-CLB mean 1611.56
(1385.85), median 1115.00 (55.90-7060.00). Table 1 labels these ug/mL; the text (PDF p6) and the assay range give ug/L,
which is what the magnitudes fit. The text also calls 137.01 the median CLB concentration; Table 1 gives it as the
mean (median 114.50).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD was modelled (PDF p16). Target trough ranges, taken from the literature: CLB 30-300 ug/L, N-CLB 300-3000 ug/L;
laboratory alert levels CLB 500 ug/L, N-CLB 5000 ug/L (Section 2.8, PDF p6). Under the clinic's own dosing 85.4% (88/103)
had CLB troughs and 79.6% (82/103) N-CLB troughs in range (PDF p15). Not an opioid paper: no MEAC, ke0 or potency ratio.

Simulated dosing (Table 3, PDF p13), mg/kg twice daily for >=80% PTA of both troughs (NM, IM) or of N-CLB (PM):
NM 0.3 / 0.25 / 0.2 / 0.175 / 0.15 at 10 / 20 / 30 / 40 / 50 kg; IM 0.25 / 0.2 / 0.15 / 0.125 / 0.12; PM 0.1 / 0.075 /
0.075 / 0.0625 / 0.06. In PMs the CLB trough PTA (>=30 ug/L) was only 55.9-66.1%.

## Covariates
Body weight on all four disposition parameters, with fixed exponents 1.0 (volumes) and 0.75 (clearances) referenced to
70 kg (Eqs. 9-12). CYP2C19 genotype on CL_NCLB/Fm only. Age, sex, BSA, hepatic and renal markers, co-medication
(including valproate), ketogenic diet and SNPs of ABCB1, CYP3A4 and GABA-receptor genes were not significant (PDF p7-8).

## Caveats
- Opportunistic, mostly elimination-phase samples; Ka fixed; Fm not estimable; no IIV on volumes (PDF p12, p16).
- No external validation; GOF and pc-VPC show poorer prediction at high and low concentrations (PDF p16-17).
- No UM or RM patients modelled; Chinese children only (0.85-16.75 y); 70 kg values are an extrapolation.
- Derived half-lives conflict with the cited literature values (above).
- Table unit misprints (L-1 for L/h in Table 2; ug/mL for ug/L in Table 1).
