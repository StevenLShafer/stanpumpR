# Kulo 2013: paracetamol and metabolite popPK at delivery and post-partum

## Study
Open-label two-period study, Leuven, Aug 2010 - Mar 2011 (EudraCT 2010-020164-37). Period 1: 39 women analysed (41
enrolled, 2 excluded) after (semi)urgent caesarean delivery, IV paracetamol (Perfusalgan) 2 g loading dose over 20 min
within 5 min of delivery, then 1 g over 10 min every 6 h to 24 h. Period 2: 8 of the 39 given a single 2 g IV dose
10.7-15 weeks (mean 12.4) post-partum, 6 h follow-up. Table 1 (PDF p4): all 39 at delivery age 31.1 (5.28) y,
gestational age 35.9 (4.09) wk (13 < 37 wk, 26 at 37-41 wk), weight 79.37 (13.0) kg, BSA 1.92 (0.19) m2, 10 twin
pregnancies (8 pre-term), 5 with pregnancy disease (3 pre-eclampsia, 2 diabetes). Paired 8: weight 77.3 (11.0) kg at
delivery vs 68.83 (11.7) kg post-partum, 12.39 (1.72) weeks post-partum. Co-medication: locoregional anaesthetics and IV
ketorolac. Plasma at 1, 2, 4, 6, 12, 18, 24 h (period 1) and 1, 2, 4, 6 h (period 2); urine 0-6 and 6-24 h (bladder
catheter) and 0-6 h. 255 plasma and 71 urine samples. HPLC for paracetamol (plasma) and paracetamol, glucuronide,
sulphate (urine); plasma LLOQ 0.08 mg/L, urine 1 mg/L. NONMEM VI FOCE-I, ADVAN6; 250-replicate bootstrap.

## Model and parameters
Two-compartment paracetamol (V1, V2, Q) with four elimination pathways: to glucuronide (CLPG = V1 k13), to sulphate
(CLPS = V1 k14), unchanged into urine (CLPU = V1 k17), and the remainder as oxidation (CLPO, not measured; Figure 1).
Metabolite volumes V3 and V4 fixed to 18% of V1; k35 = k46 = MF x k17. Exponential IIV, proportional residual error.

Ratios (Discussion, consistent with Table 2): glucuronidation x2.4, oxidation x1.8, unchanged x1.5, sulphation
unchanged; total 21.1/11.7 = 1.80 (derived). Derived Vss (V1 + V2): 68.1 L at delivery vs 34.4 L post-partum. The
authors say V1 was poorly informed (few early samples) and needs a study with more distribution-phase samples. Observed
(Figure 2 text): 1 h concentration after the 2 g dose 23.1 vs 54.2 mg/L and trough 4.1 vs 7.2 mg/L, delivery vs
post-partum (the sentence says "after delivery" twice; the comparison is with post-partum). Urine recovery in the first
6 h was lower at delivery (36.95 vs 47.62% of dose).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No ke0, no analgesic concentration, no pain scores.

## Covariates
Being at delivery (vs 12 weeks post-partum) on CLPG (OFV 3099.317 to 3056.078), V1 (to 3002.583), CLPO and CLPU; twin
pregnancy lowers CLPU (OFV -6.23). Weight, height, BSA, age, gestational age (trend on CLPG, failed validation), labour,
pre-term delivery, morbidity and urine rate not significant; the authors conclude the difference is not a size effect.

## Caveats
Oxidative clearance is the residual, not measured. Post-partum arm is 8 women, single dose, 6 h. V1 poorly estimated.
Data are hours after caesarean surgery. Total plasma paracetamol; no binding measured.
