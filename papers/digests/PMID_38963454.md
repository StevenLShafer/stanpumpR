# Størset 2024: risperidone / 9-hydroxyrisperidone parent-metabolite popPK with CYP2D6 allele activities

## Study
- Retrospective TDM data, Center for Psychopharmacology, Diakonhjemmet Hospital, Oslo, 2005-2022.
- 512 adults (275 M / 237 F), age median 37 (18-89) y; oral risperidone, daily dose median 3 (0.5-12) mg;
  1565 paired serum risperidone + 9-OH-risperidone measurements, median 2 (1-41) per subject (Table 1).
- Sampling 9-30 h post dose (median 13 h); once (789), twice (758) or three times (18) daily dosing;
  assumed steady state unless recorded otherwise. CYP2D6 inhibitors/CYP3A inducers and recent LAIs excluded.
- Assay: UHPLC-HRMS (Orbitrap), serum; ranges risperidone 1-200 nmol/L, 9-OH 2.5-300 nmol/L; LOD 0.4 and
  1 nM; values below LOD imputed as LOD/2.
- Genotyping: TaqMan for *3, *4, *5, *6, *9, *10, *41, copy number, plus *2 (rs16947), *35 (rs769258),
  rs5758550, NFIB rs28379954, CYP3A4*22.
- NONMEM 7.5.1 FOCE-I; forward p < 0.05, backward p < 0.01; 5000-replicate bootstrap.
- No body size or organ-function data available.

## Model and parameters
One compartment each for parent and metabolite, first-order absorption (ka fixed to 2.01 /h from Korell 2017),
all apparent (F and fmet not identifiable). Risperidone clearance (Table 2 footnote):
CL/F = (CLbase + [CL_allele1 + CL_allele2] x NFIB factor if C carrier) x (1 - CLage x [Age - AgeBP]) for
Age >= AgeBP; CL_met = CLm x (1 - CLage,m x [Age - AgeBP,m]) for Age >= AgeBP,m.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No D2 occupancy, effect site, or therapeutic range modelled. Discussion mentions the DPWG advice to start
CYP2D6 poor metabolisers on 67% of the standard dose.

## Covariates
CYP2D6 allele-specific additive clearances; NFIB-C x1.41 on CYP2D6-mediated CL; piecewise-linear age decline
on both clearances (above 34 y and 39 y). Sex not significant (p >= 0.23); CYP3A4*22 not significant (p = 0.31);
no covariate on volumes.

## Caveats
- TDM troughs (9-30 h), single sample per interval: absorption and V/F weakly informed; no BSV on volumes.
- No body size or renal/hepatic data. No ultrarapid metabolisers reported as a group (copy-number subjects
  only, 2 subjects with xN).
- rs5758550 phase ambiguous in heterozygotes.
