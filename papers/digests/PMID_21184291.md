# de Greef 2011: D2 occupancy linking dose to PANSS and SAS for five antipsychotics

## Study
Model-based meta-analysis by Merck/Pharsight to choose asenapine doses (PDF p1-3, printed pp. 121-123).
- PK: literature/regulatory models for haloperidol, olanzapine, ziprasidone (one-compartment), risperidone and
  asenapine (two-compartment; asenapine from internal reports). Simulated in Pharsight Trial Simulator 2.1.
- D2 occupancy: PET with 11C-raclopride (asenapine in one in-house study used NCQ-115), with plasma samples, in
  healthy volunteers and schizophrenia patients (Table II, PDF p5). Points: asenapine 28, haloperidol 36,
  olanzapine 19, risperidone 43, ziprasidone 8 (PDF p4).
  Olanzapine sources: Kapur 1999 (ref 2) 5-60 mg/day, 17 obs, 43-89% occupancy; Nyberg 1997 (ref 34) 10 mg single
  dose in healthy volunteers, 3 obs, 59-63% (Table II, text layer).
- Occupancy model: direct hyperbolic Emax (Hill 1, zero occupancy without drug), E = Emax·C/(EC50 + C), fitted with
  NLME in S-Plus (PDF p2).
- Clinical: group-mean change from placebo in PANSS (12 studies, 45 arms, 33 active; BPRS scaled by 1.66) and SAS
  (15 studies, 61 arms, 49 active; ESRS mapped to SAS, risperidone: SAS = 0.555·ESRS − 1.1447) (PDF p4-5).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: striatal D2 receptor occupancy by PET (11C-raclopride), against plasma concentration.
- No ke0: the authors found no notable delay between plasma concentration and occupancy (PDF p4), but note mostly
  one occupancy per subject, so a delay of several hours could be missed. No kon/koff model (PDF p8).
- Clinical end points PANSS (efficacy) and SAS (EPS), driven by average daily occupancy in the simulations.
  The paper cites 65-80% occupancy as optimal and EPS risk rising above 80% (PDF p1).
- Not an opioid paper: no MEAC or equianalgesic ratio. Relative in-vivo potency is given only as the PET EC50
  ratios in Table III.

## Covariates
None: individual covariates (smoking, age, sex) assumed minor relative to study-to-study variability (PDF p3).

## Caveats
- Sponsor (Merck) analysis built to pick asenapine doses; group-mean clinical data only.
- PK parameters are borrowed from labels, reports and literature, some unpublished (risperidone expert report,
  asenapine internal); haloperidol and olanzapine one-compartment.
- Emax below 100% for low-affinity drugs is attributed to raclopride competition, an artefact (PDF p7).
- Olanzapine fit rests on 19 PET points from two studies.
- Cubic polynomial clinical models are interpolations with no physiological basis (PDF p7).
