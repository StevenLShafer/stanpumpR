# McCune 2009: cyclophosphamide, HCY and CEPM population PK in children with neuroblastoma

PDF page n is printed page 87 + n (PDF p1 = printed 88).

## Study
- Children's Oncology Group pilot ANBL02P1, 4 institutions. 22 children (17 M, 5 F) with neuroblastoma or
  ganglioneuroblastoma, no prior systemic therapy. Renal function: creatinine ≤1.5 mg/dL and CrCl or GFR
  ≥60 mL/min/1.73 m². Liver function adequate (p2, printed 89).
- Demographics (p5, printed 92): median age 3.16 y (range 1.30-9.37), BSA 0.62 m² (0.45-1.47), weight
  14.2 kg (8.7-52.8), GFR 117 mL/min/1.73 m² (78-238).
- Dosing (p2): cyclophosphamide IV over 30 min, 400 mg/m²/d (>12 kg) or 13.3 mg/kg/d (≤12 kg; 2 children),
  daily ×5. Each dose was followed by a 30-min topotecan infusion, which was pharmacokinetically targeted.
  BSA and weight were computed by each institution's own guideline. No formula is printed.
- Sampling (p3, printed 90): before the dose, at the end of the infusion, and 3, 6 and 24 h after the start of
  doses 1 and 4. 196 samples in all. Cyclophosphamide was detectable in 85%, CEPM in 65% and HCY in 58%. Most
  24-h samples were below the limit (p5).
- Assay (Table I, p3): LC-MS. Cyclophosphamide in 150 µL plasma (LOD 0.6 µM). HCY derivatized with
  phenylhydrazine (LOD 0.25 µM). CEPM (LOD 0.25 µM).
- Model: NONMEM V, first-order method. Log-normal BSV. Additive residual error for CY and CEPM, combined
  error for HCY. Modelling was done in mass units (mg/m²) and converted to µmol/L. MW: CY 261.1, HCY 277.1,
  CEPM 293 g/mol (p3).

## Model and parameters
Four ODEs (p4, printed 91; confirmed on the page image). A1 is CY, A2 a dimensionless enzyme amount with
A2(0) = 1, A3 is HCY and A4 is CEPM:
- dA1/dt = −(CL_NON/V_CY)·A1 − (CL_IND/V_CY)·A1·A2
- dA2/dt = K_ENZ·(1 + E_MAX·(A1/V_CY)/(EC50 + A1/V_CY)) − K_ENZ·A2
- dA3/dt = (CL_IND/V_CY)·A1·A2 − K_HCY·A3 − K34·A3
- dA4/dt = K34·A3 − K_CEPM·A4

EC50 is reported in µmol/L of CY and converted to mass units inside the model (p4).

Model selection (Table III, p6, printed 93): dosing not normalized by BSA (model 2) was clearly inferior. A random
effect on V_CY lowered the OFV by 73.2. GFR on K_HCY and on K34 (model 7) was the final model. Sex on CL_NON
gave no improvement. The reported K_HCY and K34 depend on the fixed metabolite volumes (p6).

Derived exposure (p7, printed 94; page image):
- CY AUC median 431 µM·h (range 258-746) on day 1 and 313 µM·h (171-510) on day 4. The text prints the unit
  as "µM/h". Converted: 112.5 and 81.7 mg·h/L (derived, ×261.1/1000).
- CY clearance from the AUCs: 3.58 L/h/m² (1.73-5.53) after dose 1 and 4.86 L/h/m² (1.93-7.37) after dose 4.
  The model-predicted initial inducible clearance is 1.77 L/h/m² here, against 1.79 in Table II.
- HCY AUC median 26.8 µM·h (day 1) and 37.0 (day 4). CEPM AUC median 16.5 and 23.3 µM·h (pp7-8).
- Table IV (p9, printed 96) gives the individual demographics and AUCs. The day-1 and day-4 CY medians
  recomputed from it are 430.5 and 312.5 µM·h (derived), which agree with the text.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Not an opioid. There are no PD end points, no effect site and no therapeutic range. The authors call the
exposure-response relation unknown and say PK-PD studies are needed (p13, printed 100).

## Covariates
- BSA: every dose and volume is per m² (structural). An absolute-dose model was worse (Table III).
- GFR (mL/min/1.73 m², radioisotope or CrCl, measured before chemotherapy): K_HCY = 47.0 − 26.6·(GFR−117)/117
  and K34 = 0.331 − 0.234·(GFR−117)/117 (/h).
- Age did not enter the final model within this narrow age range. In GEE analysis the CY AUC was associated with
  age, BSA and day.

## Caveats
- n = 22. Two samples a day, on days 1 and 4 only. FO estimation. Topotecan was given with every dose and was
  not tested as a covariate; the authors argue from Table V that it does not alter CY clearance.
- E_MAX, EC50 and both metabolite volumes are fixed rather than estimated. K_ENZ has 126% BSV and 55% SE.
- The BSA formula is not printed (institutional).
- Internal inconsistencies: the Discussion gives K_HCY as 46.5/h (p12, printed 99) against Table II's 47.0.
  The initial inducible CL is 1.77 in the Results against 1.79 in Table II.
- Doses are stated as mg of cyclophosphamide; anhydrous vs monohydrate is not stated.
