# Doufas 2003: effect-site TCI propofol sedation, performance in volunteers

## Study
- 20 healthy volunteers, 18-50 y; median (range) age 26 (19-39) y, weight 64 (50-85) kg, height 168 (158-185) cm; 7 male (PDF p4, printed 947).
- Each volunteer had two trials, nitrous oxide 50% first, then propofol alone ("Air") after a 1-h recovery. Sequential 15-min cycles; propofol effect-site target started at 0.25 mcg/mL (N2O) or 1.5 mcg/mL (Air) and stepped up by 0.25 (N2O) or 0.5 (Air) mcg/mL per cycle until OAA/S 2 (loss of consciousness).
- Delivery: Harvard 2 pump, three-compartment Schnider (1998) covariate model with an effect-site compartment (k41 = 0.456 /min per the Schnider 1999 paper), Euler integration at 1.2-s steps, PID controller on the effect-site target.
- Samples at minute 9 and 15 of each cycle, **venous** plasma, HPLC after Plummer, CV 4.1% at 2 mcg/mL. 205 samples in the N2O trial, 113 in the Air trial. The units are written as "mg ml" in the text layer but the page image shows mcg/mL (µg ml-1).

Plasma concentration at minute 9 versus 15 of a cycle differed by 0.00 +/- 0.16 mcg/mL (N2O, P=0.78) and 0.00 +/- 0.25 mcg/mL (Air, P=0.91).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- ke0 0.456 /min (PDF p2). The Discussion states a corresponding time to peak effect of 1.69 min (PDF p5, text layer); the Rugloop passage says that software targets 1.6 min and uses ke0 0.456 /min. See open question.
- BIS and OAA/S did not change between minute 9 and 15 of each cycle (BIS P=0.43 N2O, 0.15 Air; OAA/S P=0.73, 0.31). No EC50, no MEAC, no opioid, no potency ratio.
- No concentration-effect model is fitted; Fig. 2 (PDF p4) shows BIS traces at targets 1.5, 2.0 and 2.5 mcg/mL (Air) and 0.25, 0.5 and 1.0 (N2O).
- Opioid audit: not an opioid paper.

## Covariates
Age, weight, height, lean body mass and sex enter through the Schnider model. Simulations in Stanpump showed equilibration between plasma and effect site within 4 min for every extreme case tried (e.g. male, 18 y, 135 cm, 41 kg).

## Caveats
- Venous samples against a model built on arterial data: negative bias is expected, and the authors accept it as within 20-30% inaccuracy.
- N2O always preceded the Air trial (not randomised); the N2O trial was at very low propofol targets.
- Pseudo-steady state, 6 min per cycle only.
