# Huestis 2025: MDMA population PK (one compartment, zero-order then first-order input) and PBPK

## Study
- **Data (Figure 1, PDF p3):** two phase I studies in healthy volunteers, pooled.
  - MPKF (NCT05147402; previously unpublished): open-label, randomized two-period crossover food-effect study.
    16 unique participants; 120 mg MDMA HCl fed (n = 14) and fasted (n = 15), high-fat/high-calorie meal.
  - NIDA (NCT01148342; Kolbrich 2008, Hartman 2014): double-blind, placebo-controlled three-period crossover;
    1.0 mg/kg (n = 48) and 1.6 mg/kg (n = 46) MDMA HCl after a light breakfast. Figure 1 gives 51 unique NIDA
    participants; the Results text gives n = 49 in the PopPK dataset (65 = 16 + 49). The two counts are not
    reconciled in the paper.
- **PopPK dataset:** 65 adults, 2012 MDMA and 1871 MDA plasma concentrations (p5, Figure 1). BLQ, missing-time and
  pre-dose values excluded. One MPKF participant vomited within 4 h and was kept in the PopPK set (fasted).
- **Software:** NONMEM 7.4, FOCE-I; PsN 4.8.1; 1000-sample nonparametric bootstrap; pcVPC.
- **PBPK:** Simcyp v21 minimal PBPK with first-order absorption; CYP2D6 mechanism-based inhibition tuned to
  dextromethorphan and paroxetine interaction data; used for split-dose and DDI simulations (pp4-5, 8-10).
- Dose basis throughout is MDMA hydrochloride (Figure 1; text p2-3, p5).

## Model and parameters
MDMA: one compartment, linear elimination. Absorption: lag ALAG1, then zero-order input of duration D1 into the
depot, then first-order ka (Methods p3 and Discussion p10 call it sequential zero- then first-order; Results p6
calls it "parallel first- and zero-order"; Table 2 has ALAG1, D1 and ka and no mixing fraction). IIV on CL/F and
V2/F (block, r = 0.67), ka and D1. MDA: two compartments, fitted sequentially from individual MDMA post hoc
estimates with the fraction metabolized fixed.

**Observed MPKF results (Table 1, p6; 120 mg MDMA HCl, fed n = 14 / fasted n = 15):**
- MDMA AUC0-inf geometric mean 3318 / 3388 h·ng/mL (GMR 0.99, 90% CI 0.94-1.04).
- MDMA Cmax geometric mean 227 / 238 ng/mL (GMR 0.97, 0.92-1.01).
- MDMA Tmax median 4.0 [2.0-6.0] / 2.1 [2.0-8.0] h. The text and abstract round these to 4 and 2 h.
- MDA AUC0-inf 369 / 330 h·ng/mL; Cmax 12.1 / 12.4 ng/mL; Tmax 7.0 / 6.0 h.

**Covariate simulations (p7-8, Figure 2):** reference individual is a white NIDA male, 70 kg, 25 y, CrCl
113 mL/min, light meal. MPKF subjects had 50% higher AUC0-44 and 40% higher Cmax than NIDA. 5th-percentile weight
gave 30% higher AUC0-44; 95th percentile gave 28% lower AUC0-44 and 25% lower Cmax. Clinical-significance band
was ±20%.

**PBPK (p9-10):** simulated AUC0-inf and Cmax within 1.25-fold of observed. Split versus single dose (120 and
180 mg HCl, 2 h apart): AUC0-44 about 1% higher, AUC0-4 about 15% lower, Cmax at most 5% lower. For a CYP2D6
substrate with the second part of a 180 mg split dose, interactions were weak from fmCYP2D6 0.05, moderate from
0.20 and strong from 0.75. Metformin: no interaction with the in vitro Ki values; weak with reduced Ki values.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No concentration-effect model. No ke0, EC50 or effect site. Efficacy and tolerability bounds are stated as
doses: effective from 75 mg HCl and tolerated up to 225 mg HCl split (p7). Peak effects 70-90 min after the first
dose, lasting 4-6 h, are cited from the literature (p10).

## Covariates
- CL/F and V2/F scale with weight as power functions, exponents 1.03 and 0.885.
- Equation 3 normalizes continuous covariates to the median or a standard value, and gives 70 kg as the example
  for weight. The reference individual is 70 kg. Table 2 does not print the reference weight separately.
- Fed/fasted status acts on ka, relative to the NIDA light-breakfast category.
- An empirical study effect acts on CL/F and V2/F.
- Age, sex, race, ALT, albumin and CrCl were tested and are not in the final MDMA model.

## Caveats
- The absorption description is inconsistent within the paper: sequential (Methods, Discussion) versus parallel
  (Results). The parameter set has no mixing fraction.
- The study effect is called an idiosyncrasy of the pooled dataset (p8). Washout, drug-use history and other
  factors are offered as possible causes.
- The fasted ka effect has 72.3% RSE.
- The MDA model rests on a fixed fmet of 0.1.
- The PopPK model is linear and calibrated on single doses. MDMA is a CYP2D6 mechanism-based inhibitor of its
  own metabolism, so the authors used PBPK for split doses.
- Sponsor-funded (Lykos/MAPS); several authors are employees or contractors.
- NIDA participant count differs between Figure 1 (51) and the Results text (49).
