# Bastian 2003: oxaliplatin population PK with enterohepatic recirculation

Context file listed no repo locations (`cited_in_repo: false`), but the library does model this: `R/drugs_oxaliplatinBastian.R` (see below).

## Study
- Pooled re-analysis of two French trials (one phase I, oxaliplatin alone; one phase I-II with folinic acid/5-FU/irinotecan). 56 patients (29 male, 27 female), 66 courses, metastatic cancer, age 41-79, adequate marrow function; 15 had moderate renal impairment (Cockcroft-Gault < 60 mL/min).
- Doses 50, 65, 75, 85, 100 or 130 mg/m2 as 2- or 4-h i.v. infusion (33 courses each per the text). Dose 72-260 mg, infusion 1.5-4.25 h (Table 1, text layer).
- 537 samples (median 6/patient, 4-19) over 28 h. Plasma ultrafilterable platinum by flameless atomic absorption; the paper calls it "oxaliplatin" and states that it stands for all platinum derivatives in plasma (Fig 3 legend).
- MP2 program (Urien), nonlinear mixed effects; proportional error for residual and interindividual variability; 200-sample bootstrap. (NONMEM was not used.)
- Table 1 (means / medians): age 59/59 y; BW 71.4/71.0 kg (41-107); BSA 1.74/1.76 m2; SCr 104/87 umol/L (53-567; the text prints the unit as "mmol/l", Fig 2 uses uM); CGI 72/66 mL/min.

## Model and parameters
Two-compartment model with first-order CL from V1, plus a storage site (gallbladder): drug moves from V1 to the store at k1B, is released as a bolus at time TLAG, then returns to V1 at first-order Ka (Fig 1, p818). Adding the EHR component lowered the objective function from -1083 to -1174; a three-compartment model added nothing. Interindividual variability on k1B was not significant (p820).

Final covariate model (p820, printed equations):
CL = 14.1 x (BW/71)^1.10 x (SCr/87)^-0.57 x 0.60 if female; V1 = 24.9 x (BW/71)^1.29; Q = 34.8 x (BW/71)^1.01. BW in kg, SCr in umol/L. Infusion time did not influence EHR parameters. CL IIV fell from 104% to 62%.

Bootstrap means (Table 3) agree: e.g. CL 16.0, V1 24.8, V2 139, k1B 1.71, TLAG 2.10, Ka 0.25.
Derived by the authors (p822-823): terminal half-lives 10.5 h (men) and 14.4 h (women); median CL 11.3 L/h; target AUC 20.35 mg.h/L = 230 mg / 11.3 L/h (130 mg/m2 x 1.76 m2).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No effect-site model, ke0, EC50 or concentration-response relation. Authors propose AUC as a pharmacokinetic end point and dose by BW with SCr and sex corrections (Table 4: BW change of about 15% or SCr change of -30% to +40% shifts AUC by 20%). Not an opioid paper; no MEAC.

## Covariates
BW (on CL, V1, Q), SCr (negative, on CL), sex (female CL x 0.60). BSA, Cockcroft-Gault index, age, serum proteins tested; BSA in place of BW was slightly worse (OFV -1266 vs -1282); CGI dropped in the full model. Objective-function increase when each is removed (Table 2): BW on V1 35, BW on CL 22, SCr 30, sex 16, BW on Q 26, EHR 91.

## Caveats
- Concentrations are ultrafilterable platinum, labelled oxaliplatin (Fig 3 legend).
- TLAG and Ka have enormous IIV (282%, 391%); the timing of the secondary peak for an individual is poorly determined. Secondary peak/shoulder seen in 20/33 (2-h) and 10/33 (4-h) courses.
- Fitted terminal half-lives (10.5/14.4 h) are shorter than the 16-28 h and >10 day phases quoted from the literature, which the authors attribute to recirculation being ignored elsewhere.
- Paper does not state how the store behaves after TLAG.
