# Osborne 1994: patient-controlled propofol sedation vs infusion (third molar extraction)

## Study
Randomised crossover, 38 ASA 1-2 day-surgery patients (mean age 21 y, SD 3.2; weight 65 kg, SD 15.3; 9 men / 29 women; Table 3, PDF p3 / printed 290). Each had two third-molar extractions under local anaesthesia (lignocaine 2% with adrenaline, 7.1-7.6 mL) 6 weeks apart, one with patient-controlled sedation (PCS), one with a propofol infusion; order randomised (19 per order). No premedication. All patients got fentanyl 0.7 ug/kg IV before the local anaesthetic.
- PCS: propofol 18 mg (1.8 mL) bolus over 5.4 s (1200 mL/h), lockout 1 min.
- Infusion: 3.6 mg/kg/h plus a 20 mg bolus at the start, plus 10 mg boluses each minute until sedation grade 3.
- Sedation scale (Table 1): 1 awake, 2 drowsy, 3 eyes closed rousable to command, 4 rousable to mild physical stimulus, 5 unrousable.
- Outcomes: dose, cooperation, surgeon satisfaction, Stroop and recall tests, patient preference by telephone next day. No blood concentrations were measured.

Dosing results (Table 6): total propofol 2.39 mg/kg (SD 1.28) PCS vs 2.58 (SD 0.84) infusion, not significant. Procedure duration 21.9 vs 23.6 min (Table 5). PCS demands: 7.5 (SD 3.0) and 7.7 (SD 4.4) successful, 4.4 and 2.8 unsuccessful (Table 4).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point is the 5-grade sedation score above. Deepest level was grade 3 in all 76 procedures; grade 3 was reached in 38/38 infusion procedures but 26/38 PCS procedures (p<0.01). Time to grade 3: 6.5 min (SD 3.1) infusion, 9.2 min (SD 4.3) PCS. No concentration, ke0, EC50 or MEAC reported. Preference: 19 PCS, 10 infusion, 9 indifferent; PCS preferences stronger (p<0.05). Not an opioid paper (fentanyl was a fixed premedication dose only).

## Covariates
Age, sex, surgical difficulty, predrug value, test time and propofol dose were covariates in the cognitive-test ANOVA only.

## Caveats
Young, fit adults with a dental procedure; authors say the 18 mg bolus may need reducing for older or less fit patients. Infusion arm got extra fentanyl-independent boluses by anaesthetist. Industry-funded (ICI Australia). Text layer of the abstract is partly garbled by OCR.
