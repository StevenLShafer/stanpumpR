# Maseda 2018: micafungin PK/PD in obese and critically ill patients

## Study
Prospective PK study in 31 adults on micafungin for empirical or directed treatment of invasive candidiasis: 11 critically ill morbidly obese (Hospital La Paz, Madrid), 10 critically ill nonobese and 10 noncritically ill obese (Hospital del Mar, Barcelona) (PDF p.2, Table 1 on p.4). Dose 100 or 150 mg once daily, infused over 60 min in 100 mL saline, dose at the physician's discretion (100 mg for BMI <=45, 150 mg for BMI >45, with three exceptions). Sampling on day 3 at predose, 1, 3, 5, 8, 18 and 24 h; extra samples day 0 and day 7 when feasible. 242 total plasma concentrations. Assay: total micafungin in plasma (not free) by UHPLC-MS/MS, 0.2 to 30 ug/mL. Modelling: Pmetrics (nonparametric adaptive grid) in R, 1- and 2-compartment models; covariates screened by stepwise regression on Bayesian posteriors and -2LL. Monte-Carlo simulation n = 1000, doses 100/150/200 mg, weights 45/80/115/150/185 kg, ages 30/50/70/90 y.

Table 1 (rendered p.4, median unless stated): n 31; age 58 (27-85) y; 71% female; BMI 34.7 (19.6-60.0); weight 95 (44-193) kg. Critically nonobese (n = 10): age 72 (43-85), 40% female, BMI 23.1, weight 65 (44.0-92.5) kg. Morbidly obese 100 mg (n=7): weight 113 (95-121) kg; 150 mg (n=4): 157.5 (142-170) kg. ## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD model. The target is antimicrobial, not a concentration: AUC0-24/MIC of 285 (C. parapsilosis), 3000 (all Candida) and 5000 (non-parapsilosis), from the authors' ref 11 (PDF p.3). PTA >=90% and FTA >=90% were called optimal. MIC distributions from SENTRY (ref 20). Table 3 (PTA by dose, MIC, weight) and Table 4 (FTA, text layer): FTA vs C. albicans 100 mg: 74.6, 62.6, 56.6, 46.4, 37.4% for 45/80/115/150/185 kg; 150 mg: 97.0, 95.9, 90.6, 82.6, 80.6; 200 mg: 99.1, 99.0, 98.9, 98.6, 98.0. C. glabrata 200 mg: 94.2, 91.7, 90.4, 88.1, 85.8. Not an opioid: no MEAC, ke0, tPeak or potency ratio.

## Covariates
CL = TVCL x (Wt/70)^0.75 x (Age/60)^0.75 (PDF p.4; Wt in kg, Age in y). Weight or age alone did not significantly improve fit (p = 0.0586 and 0.597); together p = 0.0238. Severity score not retained. Simulated results said not to change materially with age.

## Caveats
Small groups (3 to 10 per arm); nonparametric fit with large SD and mean/median gaps; weight-based dosing was not randomised; total (not free) concentrations; no outcome data; the abstract says FTA >90% for 150 mg at weights <115 kg but Table 4 gives 90.6% at 115 kg and the Results text includes 115 kg. Funded in part by Astellas.
