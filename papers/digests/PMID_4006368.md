# Hogstedt 1985: metoprolol IV and oral in the same women during and after pregnancy

## Study
Five women aged 20-36 with hypertension in pregnancy (four before week 20, one with pre-eclampsia at week 36),
untreated with other drugs, studied in the third trimester (weeks 35-38) and again 12-25 weeks after delivery
(Table I, PDF p2; weights 74-95.5 kg pregnant, 63-88 kg after). Day 1: metoprolol 10 mg IV (arm vein, overnight
fast); 3 days later 100 mg orally. Plasma sampled to 12 h after each dose (IV: 5 min to 12 h; oral: 20 min to
12 h). Plasma metoprolol by gas chromatography (lowest measurable 1 nmol/L); urinary metabolites by GC-MS; protein
binding by equilibrium dialysis (p2). IV data fitted with a three-compartment model in ELSFIT; CLiv = dose/AUC from
the triexponential fit; oral AUC by trapezoids; paired t tests; data mean +/- SE (p2). The salt is not named.

Results (p2-3): postpartum CLiv 26-97% of the pregnant value (55.0 +/- 12.6%), not significant; postpartum CLo 8-53%
of pregnant (25.3 +/- 7.8%); individual F 0.08-0.43 in pregnancy vs 0.30-0.56 after, "mean difference of 260%"; the
abstract gives F 1.3-3.7 times higher after pregnancy and oral CL 2-13 times higher in pregnancy. Peak oral
concentrations 0.5-3 h after dosing (p3). Total 24 h urinary recovery of the oral dose 187.8 +/- 14.9 vs
171.4 +/- 13.1 umol, not significant (Table III footnote, PDF p4, text layer only). Discussion (p3): oral CL more
than four times higher in pregnancy.
Derived: ratio of mean CLiv pregnant/after 2.1 (1/0.55 = 1.8 from the mean percentage); ratio of mean CLo 5.6;
F ratio of means 0.50; per-kg Vd ratio 1.78. Arithmetic check: 0.017 L/min/kg x 60 x 5.38 h / 0.693 = 7.9 L/kg
vs Vd 6.87 (pregnant); 0.009 x 60 x 5.36 / 0.693 = 4.2 vs 3.85 (after): consistent.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No beta-blockade or blood-pressure end point; no ke0. Not an opioid.

## Covariates
Pregnancy (third trimester) vs the same women postpartum.

## Caveats
n = 5, all hypertensive; mean +/- SE; the rise in systemic clearance was not significant, the fall in F was.
Weight changed between occasions (per-kg values partly reflect it). The authors attribute the change to hepatic
metabolism, not absorption (urinary recovery slightly higher in pregnancy). No CYP2D6 phenotype.

  means (0.21/0.42); the individual F values and the 260% mean difference spread widely around it.
- **Discrepancy (characterisation), R/drugs_metoprolol.R:86-88**: the header says the 1.5 clearance factor

  means give 2.1-fold (1.8-fold from the 55% mean percentage), larger than the model's 1.5, though not significant
