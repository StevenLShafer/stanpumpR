# Mizuno 2023: ethosuximide population PK and AUC-seizure-freedom relation in childhood absence epilepsy

## Study
- Source: the NIH CAE comparative-effectiveness trial (NCT00088452; ethosuximide vs lamotrigine vs valproate), 32 sites, enrolment 2004-2007. Initial double-blind monotherapy phase plus open-label second monotherapy phase.
- PK cohort: 1,320 plasma concentrations from 211 children (143 initial-phase, 68 second-phase), age 7.9 +/- 2.1 y (range 3.5-12.8 y). Exposure-response cohort: 103 initial-monotherapy children with complete PK (age 7.5 +/- 2.1 y, weight 28.1 +/- 9.4 kg, 58% female; Table 2). Adverse-event cohort n = 143.
- Dosing: start 10 mg/kg/day, raised every 2 weeks to seizure freedom, intolerable effects, or max 60 mg/kg/day or 2000 mg/day; twice daily (12-h interval). Syrup and capsule.
- Sampling: one random sample at weeks 4, 8, 12; at week 16-20, predose, 1-3 h and 4-8 h post morning dose.
- Assay: HPLC-UV, plasma, total drug; LLOQ 0.8 mcg/mL, range 0.8-160 mcg/mL.
- Model: NONMEM 7.2 (FOCE-I), Pirana; bootstrap (PsN, n = 1000), pcVPC. Logistic regression (R) of seizure freedom on log AUC; Monte Carlo (5,000 NHANES children 3-13 y).

## Model and parameters
One compartment, first-order absorption and elimination; ka fixed to literature values by formulation (2.52 /h syrup, 1.22 /h capsule; data sparse in absorption), IIV on Vd fixed to 0. Allometry fixed: exponent 0.75 on CL/F, 1.0 on Vd/F, reference 70 kg. Total body weight was the only retained covariate (age, AST, ALT, bilirubin, sex, race tested).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: seizure freedom (parental report + clinic hyperventilation + 1-h awake EEG), binary, at each visit.
- Logistic model: f(log AUC) = 1 / (1 + exp(-(b0 + b1 log AUC))). b0, b1 are not printed in the main text.
- Overall cohort (Fig. 1a,b): EAUC50 1,027 and EAUC75 1,489 mcg.h/mL, where AUC is over the 12-h dosing interval (AUC12h); Ctrough EC50 78 and EC75 114 mcg/mL. AUC for 95% response: 2,776 mcg.h/mL (Discussion).
- Derived (not in paper): the two quantiles fix the curve as P = AUC^n / (AUC^n + 1027^n) with n = ln 3 / ln(1489/1027) = 2.96, independent of log base; this predicts 2,779 mcg.h/mL for 95%, matching the paper's 2,776. For Ctrough, n = 2.90.
- By dosing group (Fig. 1c,d, printed p. 464, PDF p. 6): group 2 EAUC50 914 / EAUC75 1,137; group 3 EAUC50 1,984 / EAUC75 2,436 mcg.h/mL. Ctrough, as printed in the Fig. 1d inset: group 2 EC50 70, EC75 153; group 3 EC50 88, EC75 190 mcg/mL (the text layer matches the page image).
- **Fig. 1d inset probably transposes two values.** The plotted curves cross 75% for group 2 near 88 mcg/mL and 50% for group 3 near 153 mcg/mL (read from the page image). The paper's own regression, Ctrough = 0.080 x AUC - 6.5, applied to the Fig. 1c AUC values gives group 2 EC50 67 / EC75 84 and group 3 EC50 152 / EC75 188 mcg/mL (derived). Both point to group 2 70 / 88 and group 3 153 / 190. This is an inference from the figure; the printed values are kept as printed above.
- Adverse events (Fig. 2): intolerable in 27/143 (18.9%), median AUC 857 (396-1,905); cumulative frequency 11.1% at EAUC50, 16.1% at EAUC75. Tolerable in 52/143 (36.4%), median 617 (338-1,917); 30.8% and 34.9%.
- Dosing (Fig. 3): 40 and 55 mg/kg/day for 50% and 75% probability; by weight cohort 30-45 mg/kg (50%) and 45-65 mg/kg (75%), lower mg/kg in heavier children.
- No effect site, ke0 or hysteresis (not applicable).

## Covariates
Total body weight only: CL/F = 0.82 x (WT/70)^0.75 L/h; Vd/F = 72 x (WT/70) L.

## Caveats
- Sparse sampling, ka fixed from literature, Vd IIV fixed to 0; little data beyond 12 h postdose (twice-daily dosing), so CL may be overestimated if there is deep distribution; authors limit use to AUC12h and steady-state trough.
- Exposure-response excludes discontinuers (re-analysis said not to change results); group 3 shows higher EC50, i.e. heterogeneity in PD.
- Table 1 residual-error entries look misprinted in the source itself (confirmed on the page image, not an extraction artefact). The ka row is legible on the page image: one fixed value per formulation.
- Fig. 1d inset: group 2 EC75 and group 3 EC50 appear transposed (see Pharmacodynamics).
- First author is Kana Mizuno (K); Tomoyuki Mizuno is a different author (ref 4).
