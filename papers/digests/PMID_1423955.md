# Dias 1992: IV diltiazem PK and heart-rate PD in atrial fibrillation or flutter

## Study
- **Patients:** 32 with atrial fibrillation (27) or flutter (5) lasting over 24 h, with a ventricular rate above 120
  for at least 15 min. They were 29 men and 3 women, mean (SD) age 66 (7) y and weight 84.5 (19.5) kg (Table 1, p2).
  None had severe heart failure. 22 had digoxin before the study.
- **Setting:** part of a double-blind placebo-controlled trial (Ellenbogen 1991) at five centres. Sponsored by Marion
  Merrell Dow, the authors' employer (Dias, Weir).
- **Design (Figure 1):**
  - Open-label bolus: 20 mg over 2 min, then 25 mg over 2 min if there was no response within 15 min.
  - Responders then had a 10 mg/h infusion for 24 h, raised to 15 mg/h if the response was lost (or permitted at
    hour 4).
  - Infusion non-responders were rebolused and given open-label 10-15 mg/h.
  - A 10-h washout followed.
- **Groups:** 17 received 10 mg/h only and 15 were raised to 15 mg/h. Five more patients were excluded (assay
  interference or lost samples).
- **Sampling:** plasma at 1, 3, 5, 10, 15, 20 and 24 h of infusion and 1, 3, 5 and 10 h of washout.
- **Heart rate and BP:** after the 2-min bolus and every 5 min to 17 min, then at the plasma times. Heart rate came
  from 1-min lead II strips.
- **Assay:** HPLC-UV. The LLOQ was 6.25 ng/mL for diltiazem and 3.12 for its metabolites.
- **Analysis:** model-independent PK (AUC∞, CL = dose/AUC, Vd = CL/kel). PD fitted per patient with linear,
  log-linear, Emax and sigmoid Emax models.

## Model and parameters
No compartmental model was fitted. Table 2 (PDF p4) gives noncompartmental mean ± SD in 31 patients, split by the
final infusion rate. The p values are two-sample t tests between groups, so this is not a within-subject dose
comparison.

- **Derived checks:**
  - 42.0 × 6.8 / 0.693 = 412 L and 30.7 × 6.9 / 0.693 = 306 L, against Vd of 411 and 299. Consistent.
  - Dose/AUC from the group means is 40.3 and 28.8 L/h. These differ from the means of individual ratios, as
    expected.
- **Metabolites (Table 3):**
  - Desacetyldiltiazem Cmax was 30 and 68 ng/mL; N-desmethyldiltiazem 23 and 45.
  - Their AUC ratios to diltiazem were 0.12-0.14 and 0.09.
  - The authors expect them to contribute little.
- **Steady state:** not reached in 24 h. The authors estimate about 32 h.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- **End point:** percent reduction in heart rate from baseline (1-min ECG strips). Baseline was 131 ± 10 bpm.
- **Sigmoid Emax per patient (Table 5, PDF p6, n=32):**
  - Emax 51.5 ± 17.0% decrease.
  - EC50 110.0 ± 84.3 ng/mL.
  - N 3.8 ± 6.4.
  - r² 0.78 ± 0.2. 27 of 32 had r² > 0.6.
- **By rhythm:** EC50 was 101 ± 76 in atrial fibrillation and 160 ± 117 in flutter (p = 0.15). Emax was similar
  (52 against 50%).
- **Model-predicted concentrations (Table 6):**
  - A 20% heart-rate reduction needs 78.8 ± 72.8 ng/mL (n=31).
  - 30% needs 171.5 ± 241.2 (n=29).
  - 40% needs 294.4 ± 423.6 (n=24).
- **Hysteresis:**
  - No hysteresis in 27 of 32 patients.
  - The 5 with poor fits all showed clockwise hysteresis, which suggests tolerance (p6-7).
  - Peak heart-rate reduction generally came at the time of peak concentration.
- **Bolus response:** response to the bolus came a mean 4.2 ± 3.6 min from the start of the 2-min dose. The mean
  heart-rate fall at response was 28% (p5).
- **Infusion:** heart rate fell 33% at 1 h, 37% at 5 h, 39% at 10 h, 42% at 15 h and 38% at 24 h (Table 4). Thirty
  of 32 maintained their response.
- **Blood pressure:**
  - There was no consistent concentration-BP relation: mean r² 0.35 for SBP and 0.36 for DBP, and a strong relation
    in only 4 patients (SBP) and 3 (DBP).
  - SBP fell 13% after the bolus and 7-9% during the infusion.
- **Not reported:** ke0 and an effect compartment. The paper reports onset ("response"), not time to peak effect.

## Covariates
None modelled. The authors attribute the lower CL than in healthy volunteers to age (66 y) and cardiovascular
disease. The 10/15 mg/h split is a nonlinearity claim, not a covariate model.

## Caveats
- The CL difference between infusion rates compares two different groups of patients. The 15 mg/h group were those
  who lost their response or needed more. Patient selection is therefore confounded with infusion rate.
- The total dose includes the boluses, and the 15 mg/h patients spent their first hours at 10 mg/h.
- Noncompartmental parameters only. The sponsor's staff did the analysis.
- The EC50 SDs are large (CV 77%), and N has a CV of 169%.
- Weir 1989 is quoted from a supplement citation (6:199S), apparently an abstract.
