# Peigné 2018: population PK of stiripentol in children with Dravet syndrome

## Study
Prospective, multicentre, observational population PK study (part of STIPOP, EudraCT 2007-001784-30); a
re-analysis of the paediatric data in the EU SPC (PDF p. 2-3, printed 740-741). Funded by Biocodex.
- 35 children with Dravet syndrome, 18 male and 17 female, age 1.0-17.6 y (mean 8.18, SD 4.78), weight
  9.1-74.0 kg (mean 28.2, SD 14.6), BSA 0.43-1.81 m2 (Table 1, PDF p. 6).
- All on valproate (20.7 +/- 8.6 mg/kg/day) and clobazam (0.41 +/- 0.15 mg/kg/day) for at least 2 weeks.
- Stiripentol oral, around 50 mg/kg/day in 2 or 3 intakes (observed 49.6 +/- 14.7, range 27.1-89.3
  mg/kg/day); powder suspension under 6 y, hard capsules over 6 y.
- Four samples per child at steady state (pre-dose; 1 h; 1-5 h; 5-8 h after the morning dose): 139
  concentrations, none below LLOQ. Observed 12.2 +/- 6.80 mg/L (1.61-35.6).
- Assay: plasma HPLC-UV, LLOQ 0.25 mg/L (total concentration).
- CYP2C19: 26 *1/*1, 9 *1/*2; no *2/*2 and no *3.
- MONOLIX 4.3.3 (SAEM).

## Model and parameters
One compartment, zero-order absorption (duration Tk0), linear first-order elimination; F fixed to 1, so all parameters are apparent (CL/F, Vd/F). Two compartments,
a lag time and saturable (Michaelis-Menten) elimination were not supported. No inter-occasion variability.
Exponential IIV; combined proportional + additive error. Final model (Section 3.4 and Table 2, PDF p. 6):

- Vd/F (L) = 82 x (BW/24.8)^1.2
- CL/F (L/h) = 4.2 x (BW/24.8)^0.42 x (Dose/45)^-0.59

BW is in kg. Dose is the weight-normalised dose (see open questions). Typical values are for a 24.8 kg child
at the reference dose.
