# Gidal 1996: high-protein meal and gabapentin absorption

## Study
- Randomized, unblinded, two-phase crossover; 10 healthy volunteers (5 M, 5 F), 32.1 ± 6.2 y, 72.3 ± 12.95 kg.
- Single 600 mg (2 × 300 mg Neurontin) fasting vs within 5 min of a high-protein meal (80 g protein, 52 g
  carbohydrate, 9 g fat; phenylalanine 4163 mg, leucine 8281 mg, isoleucine 4259 mg, valine 5388 mg). Washout ≥48 h.
- Serum at 0, 0.25-4 h (half-hourly), 5, 6, 8, 12, 24, 30 h. HPLC-UV (TNB derivatization), linear 0.05-10 µg/mL.
- PK by nonlinear regression (PCNONLIN); Cmax/Tmax observed. VAS adverse-effect battery at 2 h.

## Model and parameters
Table 1 (mean ± SD; fasting vs fed; P):

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Serum at 2 h 4.68 ± 1.21 (fed) vs 2.51 ± 0.64 µg/mL (fasting), yet fewer VAS adverse effects fed (composite 6.18 vs
11.73, P<0.03). No concentration-effect model. Not an opioid: no MEAC, ke0, tPeak or potency ratio.

## Covariates
Meal (fed/fasting) only.

## Caveats
n = 10, unblinded, single dose; tlag delay with food only about 14 min; mechanism (trans-stimulation of System-L)
is the authors' speculation.
