# Xarelto (rivaroxaban) prescribing information, revised 03/2026

## Study
Not a study: the US prescribing information (Highlights, full PI, Medication Guide, Instructions for Use). The
document is the Xarelto label it claims to be (title, NDA, March 2026 revision on p.1).

## Model and parameters
No population model. Label facts, adults:

- **Dosage (2.1, Table 1, p.6, image-confirmed)**: AF 20 mg o.d. (CrCl >50 mL/min) or 15 mg o.d. (CrCl <=50),
  each "Take with evening meal"; DVT/PE 15 mg b.i.d. for 21 days then 20 mg o.d., with food at the same time each
  day; recurrence reduction, hip/knee prophylaxis, medical-patient prophylaxis 10 mg o.d., and CAD/PAD 2.5 mg
  b.i.d., all with or without food. CrCl "based on actual weight" (Table 1 footnote).
- **2.4 (p.10)**: stop at least 24 h before surgery or another procedure. **5.3 (p.14-15)**: an epidural catheter
  not removed before 2 half-lives, 18 h (age 20-45) or 26 h (age 60-76); next dose no earlier than 6 h after
  removal.
- **2.6 (p.11-12)**: a crushed 15 or 20 mg tablet must be followed immediately by food or enteral feeding; not
  needed for 2.5 or 10 mg.
- **12.3 Absorption (p.38-39)**: absolute bioavailability dose-dependent; 2.5 and 10 mg 80-100%, not affected by
  food; 20 mg fasted about 66%; food raises the 20 mg mean AUC by 39% and Cmax by 76%; 15 and 20 mg tablets should
  be taken with food. Tmax 2-4 h. Release in the proximal small intestine lowers AUC 29% and Cmax 56%.
- **Distribution (p.39)**: protein binding 92-95% (albumin); Vss about 50 L in healthy subjects.
- **Elimination (p.39-40)**: 51% of a [14C] dose as inactive metabolites (urine 30%, feces 21%); 36% unchanged in
  urine, 7% unchanged in feces; renal secretion:filtration about 5:1; P-gp and BCRP substrate. Systemic CL about
  10 L/h (IV). Terminal t1/2 5-9 h (age 20-45); 11-13 h (age 60-76, p.42).
- **Renal (Table 18, p.38)**: AUC +44% (CrCl 50-79), +52% (30-49), +64% (15-29), +47% ESRD on dialysis, +56%
  post-dialysis.
- **Hepatic (8.7, p.36)**: Child-Pugh B AUC +127%.
- **Overdosage (10, p.36)**: exposure not further increased at single doses above 50 mg (limited absorption);
  not dialyzable.
- **Figure 2 (p.41, image)**: fold-change forest plot (renal, age 75-83 vs 18-43 y, weight <=50 or >120 vs
  70-80 kg, hepatic); read only qualitatively, no numbers transcribed.
- **Pediatric (p.42)**: Tmax 1.5-2.2 h; mean t1/2 4.2 h (adolescents), 3 h (2-12 y), 1.9 h (0.5-<2 y),
  1.6 h (<0.5 y).
- **Clinical studies dosing**: ROCKET AF (14.1, p.46) gave 20 mg (CrCl >50) or 15 mg (CrCl 30-50) o.d. "with the
  evening meal"; EINSTEIN DVT and EINSTEIN PE (phase III, 14.2, p.50) gave 15 mg b.i.d. then 20 mg o.d. with food.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Dose-dependent FXa inhibition and PT/aPTT/HepTest prolongation (12.2, p.37-38); monitoring by anti-FXa or a
clotting test not recommended. No therapeutic concentration range. Not an opioid.

## Covariates
Renal function (dose by CrCl), hepatic impairment, age (half-life), body weight (Figure 2), combined P-gp and
strong CYP3A inhibitors or inducers (avoid). Gender: no effect stated (p.42).

## Caveats
- The label describes ROCKET AF and the phase III EINSTEIN trials; it says nothing about the food conditions of
  the phase II dose-ranging trials (ODIXa, EINSTEIN-DVT dose-ranging, ACS TIMI-46) that make up most of the
  Willmann 2018 pool.
- Derived, not settled by either source: 20 mg fed absolute F about 0.66 x 1.39 = 0.92, which over the 10 mg
  absolute 0.80-1.00 is 0.92-1.15, against Willmann's relative F of 0.84 at 20 mg. The two are not directly
  comparable (single dose vs pooled patient data; the label's 39% is AUC).
