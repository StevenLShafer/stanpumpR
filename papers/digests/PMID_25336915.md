# Gu 2014: multiple-dose evogliptin PK and DPP-4 inhibition in healthy men

## Study
Seoul National University Hospital (NCT00961025). Block-randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled, multiple-dose
escalation: 30 healthy Korean men, three groups of 10 (8 evogliptin, 2 placebo), 5, 10 or 20 mg orally once daily
for 10 days at 9 AM, fasting (PDF p3). Table 1 (PDF p5): age 24.0 +/- 3.5 (placebo, n 6), 24.5 +/- 2.1, 23.3 +/- 2.4,
27.0 +/- 4.9 y; weight 69.1, 65.9, 67.0, 64.4 kg; height 175.8, 173.5, 173.8, 171.8 cm. The text gives the whole
sample as age 27.5 (23-34) y, weight 66.3 (58.0-77.1) kg, height 176.3 (168-181) cm (PDF p5); the weight agrees with
Table 1 (n-weighted mean 66.4 kg, derived) but the age and height do not (24.7 y and 173.6 cm, derived): unresolved.
Plasma sampled to 16 h on day 1, troughs days 2-9, and to 120 h after the day-10 dose; urine 0-24 h and to 120 h
after the last dose. LC-MS/MS (sitagliptin internal standard), LLOQ 5 ng/mL plasma and urine. Noncompartmental PK
(WinNonlin 6.0); sigmoid Imax model of DPP-IV inhibition vs concentration in NONMEM VI.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Plasma DPP-IV inhibition (fluorogenic Gly-Pro-AMC assay, % of baseline): above 80% within 0.5-1 h, maximum 91-96% at
3.5-5.5 h; on day 10 above 80% until 36, 48 and 66 h after the last 5, 10 and 20 mg dose (PDF p5). No hysteresis
between concentration and inhibition (Figure 4), so no ke0. Sigmoid Imax: Imax 93.2%, IC50 1.28 ng/mL; the paper
converts this to 2.32 nM, which implies a molar mass of about 552 g/mol (derived), that of the tartrate salt rather than
the base (about 401 g/mol, which would give 3.19 nM): the paper does not say which it used. Postprandial active GLP-1
AUE 1.7-, 1.5- and 2.4-fold placebo; postprandial glucose AUE reduced 20%, 25%, 35%; insulin not increased (Table 4,
PDF p10).

## Covariates
None analysed (healthy young men, n 8 per dose).

## Caveats
Healthy normoglycaemic men only; noncompartmental PK; the PD model's slope, variability and precision are not
reported. Table 2's placebo AE percentage (25%) disagrees with the text (1 of 6, 16.7%). Demographic summary in the
text disagrees with Table 1 (above).
