# Yao 2023: model-based meta-analysis of dapagliflozin, canagliflozin and empagliflozin (PK, UGE, FPG, HbA1c)

## Study
- **Design:** model-based meta-analysis of published summary-level data. The PubMed search ran to July 2016.
- **Included papers:** 80 in all; 34 dapagliflozin, 23 canagliflozin, 23 empagliflozin.
- **Data points:** 880 PK, 27 ΔUGEc, 848 FPG (195 placebo, 653 drug) and 1219 HbA1c (290 placebo, 929 drug)
  (p5).
- **Populations:** healthy subjects and T2DM patients. Studies in moderate or severe renal or hepatic impairment
  were excluded; PK/PD data are from patients with GFR above 60 mL/min/1.73 m².
- **External validation:** 26 HbA1c points from three ertugliflozin phase III trials.
- **Methods:** NONMEM 7.2 with PsN 4.2.0, FOCE-I. Data were weighted by the square root of each study's sample
  size and digitized from figures where needed.
- **Sequence:** PK models for each drug first. Steady-state AUC0-24h then drove ΔUGEc, the 24-h UGE change from
  baseline divided by baseline FPG (Eq. 1-2, Emax model). ΔUGEc then drove FPG linearly (Eq. 4), and FPG drove an
  indirect-response HbA1c model (Eq. 6-8). Placebo models were fitted by treatment type: naive, non-naive, add-on
  and mixed.

## Model and parameters
- **PK:** two compartments with first-order elimination and transit-compartment absorption (p4; Table 1).
  Dapagliflozin and empagliflozin had two transit compartments and canagliflozin four (p8).
- **Wording:** the Discussion (p8) calls these first-order absorption.
- **Food:** a fed/fasted covariate on Kt for dapagliflozin only.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- **End points:** ΔUGEc (24-h urinary glucose excretion change over baseline FPG), FPG and HbA1c.
- **Exposure driver:** steady-state AUC0-24h; the EC50s are AUC values.
- No effect compartment or ke0.

## Covariates
- **Tested:** subject type, age, sex, weight and fed state in the PK and PK/PD models. Washout, treatment type
  and design were tested in the end-point model.
- **Retained:** only fed state on dapagliflozin Kt, and treatment type on the placebo terms.
- Renal function was not a covariate. Only normal or mildly impaired renal function was included.

## Caveats
- **Units in the abstract:** it prints the EC50s as mg/mL·h; Table 2 and the Results text give ng/mL·h.
- **Figure 2 (p7) against Table 2:**
  - panel (a), "Dapagliflozin", spans AUC0-24h to about 26,000 ng/mL·h and reaches half its maximum near
    2,000;
  - panel (b), "Canagliflozin", spans to about 3,300 and reaches half maximum in the tens;
  - this is the reverse of the Table 2 EC50 assignments (56.6 for dapagliflozin, 2310 for canagliflozin);
  - the long-term AUC0-24h values in the text (51.39, 83.6, 235.3 ng/mL·h) lie below most of the plotted
    exposures;
  - the paper does not reconcile these, and the cause is unresolved from the PDF alone.
- **Other source errors:** CLD units are missing in Table 1. The "How to cite" author order (Yao, Ren, Song,
  Zhou, Hu, Liu) differs from the byline.
- **Data and assumptions:** summary-level data only. Some PK and PD inputs were digitized. ΔUGEc rests on 27
  points. Add-on background therapy cannot be separated from the placebo effect (p11).
- The PK parameters are pooled across studies with very small IIV, and are not individual-level population
  estimates.
