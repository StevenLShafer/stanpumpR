# Yue 2013: acetazolamide population PK/PD (IOP) in peritoneal dialysis patients and healthy volunteers

## Study
- 7 healthy volunteers (HV; 3 M/4 F, age 29 ± 7 y, weight 61 ± 14 kg, CrCl 101 ± 3 mL/min Cockcroft-Gault) and
  8 CAPD patients (2 M/6 F, age 54 ± 6 y, weight 68 ± 16 kg). Baseline IOP 12.2 ± 1.8 vs 15.6 ± 3.4 mmHg; albumin
  reported as 42 ± 4 vs 36 ± 6 "mg/mL" (PDF p2, printed 90; presumably g/L).
- Acetazolamide 500 mg IV over 2 min after an 8 h fast (Novopharm; salt form not stated). Blood at 0, 0.0833,
  0.167, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 1, 1.5, 2, 3, 4, 6 h; urine and dialysate over 24 h. IOP by Schiøtz tonometer at 0, 0.5,
  1, 2, 4, 8 h (p2).
- Assay: HPLC (Chapron method), intra-/inter-assay CV 2.6-5.2% / 2.8-6.3% over 10-100 µg/mL. Matrix called
  "serum" and "plasma" interchangeably; heparinised blood. Free fraction by equilibrium dialysis on pooled samples
  (p2).
- Data: 179 total plasma, 23 urine, 22 dialysate, 60 unbound plasma concentrations, 90 IOP values (p2-3).
- NONMEM VI; sequential PK then PD (individual PK fixed). Covariates tested: age, weight, dialysis.

## Model and parameters
Structure (Fig. 1 and Appendix 1, PDF p3 and p8, rendered): central compartment Vc (total drug) with a
peripheral Vp1 (total, linked by Q1) and a second peripheral compartment Vp2 that holds unbound drug. Transfer
central → unbound is linear (Q2/V1 · A1); return unbound → central is saturable,
Vmax · C5 / (Km + C5) with C5 = A5/V3. All elimination (CLr, CLnr, and CLdialysis × PT, PT = 1 for CAPD) leaves
from the unbound compartment. So CLr, CLnr and CLdialysis are clearances of **unbound** concentration.
Covariates (Appendix 2): dialysis as a categorical switch, θ = [θpatient·PT + θhealthy·(1-PT)]·e^η; age on Q1 as
Q1 · (Age/41.9)^1.08.

Secondary results (p3, text and rendered): terminal t½ of total drug 3.4 ± 0.54 h (HV) vs 20.6 ± 10.5 h (CAPD)
from the model; noncompartmental about 3 ± 0.6 h vs 24 ± 24 h (p6). Mean model-predicted fu 4.2% (HV) vs 8.6%
(CAPD). Measured fu fell from 5.3 to 3.3% over total 54 to 14 (HV) and from 14.3 to 8.0% over 44 to 23 (CAPD);
the text prints the unit as "µm/l", but the methods give these pooled concentrations in µg/mL. Blood/plasma ratio
0.77 → 0.89 (HV) and 0.96 → 1.25 (CAPD) over 10-100 µg/mL.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: intra-ocular pressure (Schiøtz). Direct inhibitory Emax model on **unbound** plasma acetazolamide:
  IOP = IOPbaseline − Emax·Cu/(IC50 + Cu); no effect compartment or ke0 (indirect models tried and rejected; p3).
- IC50 2.47 mg/L unbound (both groups); Emax 7.4 (HV) vs 4.4 mmHg (CAPD) (Table 1b). The authors compare with
  Yano 1998 (Emax 7.2 mmHg, IC50 1.64 µg/mL; p6).
- No anticonvulsant end point and no therapeutic or toxic concentration range.
- Not an opioid paper: no MEAC, ke0, tPeak or potency ratio.

## Covariates
Dialysis on CLr, IOPbaseline and Emax (categorical); age on Q1 (power, reference 41.9 y). Weight not retained.

## Caveats
- n = 15, only 5 men; CAPD patients older than HV, so age and dialysis are confounded (authors' limitation).
- IV only (no absorption information); 6 h of plasma sampling only, short for a 20 h half-life in CAPD.
- fu measured on pooled pairs of samples.
- Typesetting inconsistencies: Fig. 1 labels the Vc-Vp2 link "Q1" where the equations use Q2; Appendix Eq. 2 is
  printed as +Q1/V1·A1 + Q1/V2·A2 (the second term should be negative for mass balance); the Km IIV row carries
  the Vmax description; "µm/l" for µg/mL; albumin in "mg/mL".
- IIV for CAPD CLr (861%) and IC50 (173%) is poorly determined.
- The abstract quotes renal clearance 80.3 vs 1.2 L/h without saying these are unbound-concentration clearances;
  read alone it overstates total clearance about 20-fold (derived: 80.3 × 0.042 ≈ 3.4 L/h relative to total
  concentration, ignoring the nonlinearity).

  paper.

  unresolved (open question). Yue does cite Yano 1998 (ref 4: Eur J Clin Pharmacol 54:63-68) as a

  registry's "renal linear 1C" better.
- **dose_basis "acetazolamide base"**: Yue doses 500 mg IV and does not state the salt. **cannot verify**.
- **pd_status "pk_only"**: consistent for epilepsy. Yue's only PD end point is IOP.
