# Healy 2023: dose rationale for gabapentin and tramadol in paediatric chronic pain

## Study
Modelling and clinical trial simulation (no new patient data) for the GABA-1 paediatric trial (3 months to <18 y).
Gabapentin PK from Ouellet 2001's paediatric model with allometric scaling and CrCL maturation; tramadol from
Garrido (parent + M1) with ka from Payne. Virtual population n = 800 from NHANES/CALIPER (Table 1); 94 patients per
simulated trial; 1000 simulations; t.i.d. regimens.

## Model and parameters
Model parameters are in supplementary tables (S2, S3), not in the main text.
Target exposure (Section 2.1): from the Neurontin NDA 21-397 exposure-response analysis in 690 patients with
post-herpetic neuralgia, the therapeutic range 1200-3600 mg/day appears to correspond to mean steady-state exposure
>32.8 µg·h/mL; Figure 1 legend gives the putative target range 32.8-75.1 mg·L⁻¹·h. 1200 mg/day adult equivalent
≈ AUC0-8 35 mg·L⁻¹·h. Tramadol target 200-300 ng/mL (AUC 1600-2400 ng·mL⁻¹·h).
Recommended gabapentin titration (Table 2, mg/kg/day t.i.d.): 5-15 kg 7 → 14 → 21 → 42 → 63; >15 kg 5 → 10 → 15
→ 30 → 45. Predicted maintenance AUC0-8 35.0 (18.5-66.1) and 37.2 (20.4-71.2) mg·L⁻¹·h (Table 3, medians and 95% CI;
table rows misaligned in text layer).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Exposure (AUC) is assumed to drive analgesia in chronic neuropathic pain; no concentration-effect model, no ke0.
For tramadol (an opioid), the target is 200-300 ng/mL mean steady-state (from Garrido, acute pain), and the authors
quote therapeutic blood levels in adults 100-800 ng/mL and toxic 1000-2000 ng/mL (forensic toxicologists); not a
MEAC, total vs free not specified. No potency ratio.

## Covariates
Weight (allometric) and creatinine clearance (gabapentin); weight on CL and V (tramadol). CYP2D6 not included.

## Caveats
Pure simulation; assumes adult PKPD applies in children, no tolerance, PK unaltered by disease; new liquid
formulation assumed bioequivalent; the AUC target is from the NDA rather than a fitted exposure-response model in
this paper.
