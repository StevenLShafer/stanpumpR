# Bock 2000: rocuronium potency with volatile agents or propofol

## Study
- 84 adults (21 per group; Table 1, PDF p. 2) for major surgery, randomised to propofol 4-6 mg/kg/h, or age-adjusted 1.25 MAC end-tidal isoflurane, desflurane or sevoflurane in 60% N2O. Mean age 49.8-60.3 y (range 21-81), weight 68.2-74.4 kg, no group differences. 1.25 MAC (age-adjusted) was 0.6% isoflurane, 2.8% desflurane, 1.0% sevoflurane (means). Premedication midazolam 7.5 mg; induction fentanyl 3-7 ug/kg and propofol 2-3 mg/kg; intubation without relaxant.
- Monitoring: Relaxograph EMG of adductor pollicis, ulnar nerve, single twitch 0.1 Hz for potency, then TOF 2 Hz every 15 s; temperature kept above 36.0 C core, 32.0 C skin.
- Potency: 40 min after reaching 1.25 MAC, a single bolus of 0.1, 0.15 or 0.2 mg/kg rocuronium (central line), then supplement to a cumulative 0.3 mg/kg. ED50 and ED90 from individual log-dose probit regressions. Infusion started at 5% first-twitch recovery to hold 90-95% block; rate noted at 30, 60, 90, 120 min. Recovery index = time from TOF 0.25 to 0.7 after stopping infusion.
- No plasma concentrations measured; no PK.

## Model and parameters
No PK model. Pharmacodynamic summary, Table 2 (PDF p. 3, printed p. 45), mean (SD):

| | Propofol | Isoflurane | Desflurane | Sevoflurane |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ED50 (ug/kg) | 169 (41) | 126 (32) | 136 (25) | 121 (28) |
| ED90 (ug/kg) | 358 (62) | 288 (29) | 250 (28) | 289 (28) |
| Infusion rate at 120 min (ug/kg/min) | 9.0 (1.9) | 6.3 (1.6) | 6.1 (1.1) | 6.1 (2.0) |
| Recovery index (min) | 22 (13) | 27 (10) | 26 (14) | 28 (13) |

ED50 differences were not significant; ED90 lower with each volatile than propofol (P < 0.05); infusion rate lower with each volatile (P < 0.01); recovery index not different. Authors state infusion rate was reduced 30-40% with volatiles versus propofol (p. 45; derived from table: 6.1/9.0 = 0.68 to 6.3/9.0 = 0.70, i.e. 30-32% lower). ED90 lower by 19-30% (derived: 289/358, 288/358, 250/358). Doses are rocuronium per kg total body weight (bromide basis not stated).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: adductor pollicis twitch depression (EMG), dose-response (dose in ug/kg, not concentration). No ke0, t1/2ke0, tPeak, EC50 in concentration units, hysteresis or effect-site concentration reported. Dose-response curves of the volatile groups had slopes comparable to each other and were shifted left of propofol (Fig 1). Not an opioid paper.

## Covariates
None analysed beyond group (anesthetic). Groups matched for age, weight, sex mix (7/14 to 11/10 M/F).

## Caveats
- Bolus doses 0.1-0.2 mg/kg plus cumulative design: ED values rest on 21 patients per group, each with one dose before supplementation.
- Volatile 1.25 MAC with N2O 60%; propofol group also on N2O. Comparator for all "potentiation" is propofol (4-6 mg/kg/h), not an anesthetic-free state.
- Infusion rate: table versus Fig 2 inconsistent (see open question).
- No PK, so no concentration-effect translation.
