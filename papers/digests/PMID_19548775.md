# Grossherr 2009: propofol blood:gas partition coefficient and pulmonary extraction (goats, pigs)

## Study
Animal study, 10 African Boer goats (64-73 kg) and 10 German landrace pigs (24-33 kg), ketamine/xylazine/atropine premedication, ventilated normocapnic. Propofol infused IV in seven consecutive 10-min steps (T1-T7) after blank samples (T0); at the end of each step arterial and mixed venous plasma (triplicate) and alveolar gas (duplicate) were sampled. Pigs received double the goat dose at every step. Gas analysis n=7 goats (technical losses), n=10 pigs. Non-parametric statistics (Friedman, Wilcoxon with Bonferroni-Holm, Mann-Whitney). Assay methods are by reference to Grossherr 2006 (not described here).

## Model and parameters
No PK model. Two derived ratios (p. 784, eqs 1-2):
BGPC = arterial plasma conc / alveolar gas conc; PER = (mixed-venous - arterial plasma) / mixed-venous plasma.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No ke0, MEAC or potency data. Not an opioid.

## Covariates
Species (goat vs pig) differs in BGPC at T1 and in PER at all steps; PER in goats falls as dose rises.

## Caveats
Animals, not humans; very high BGPC means little propofol is exhaled and steady state between blood and gas is slow; BGPC changed over the infusion; negative PER in pigs; goat gas data n=7.
