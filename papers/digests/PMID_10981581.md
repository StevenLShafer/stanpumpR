# Sakai 2000: BIS-guided PID closed-loop propofol (3 cases)

## Study
Case series, n=3 adults, IRB approved. System: Fujitsu computer, Baxter AS-50 syringe pump, Aspect BIS monitor v1.22; no pharmacokinetic model. PID controller on averaged BIS delivered every 10 s, maximum rate 20 mg/kg/h; P=0.06, I=0.001, D=0.001 (printed p1008, rendered). Designed to hold BIS within +/-5% of target; alarm and revert to manual if BIS >20 units off target.
- Case 1 (balanced): 49 y F, 182 kg, 168 cm; induction propofol 273 mg (1.5 mg/kg), N2O 65%; target BIS 60 (range 57-62), 70 at closure; 180 min; propofol 2352 mg total, mean 3.8 mg/kg/h; fentanyl 200 ug, rocuronium 90 mg; eyes open 1.5 min after stopping, BIS 78.
- Case 2 (TIVA): 31 y F, 108 kg, 165 cm; induction 216 mg (2 mg/kg); target BIS 55 (52-63), 75 at closure; 166 min; propofol 2850 mg, mean 8.8 mg/kg/h; sufentanil 170 ug; eyes open within 60 s, BIS 82; BIS 92 at 2 min.
- Case 3 (MAC): 40 y M, 100 kg, 185 cm; target BIS 80, observed 76-90; 100 min; propofol 445 mg, mean 2.6 mg/kg/h (discussion, p1010); fentanyl 100 ug; awake within 50 s, BIS 96.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point BIS index. No ke0, EC50, propofol concentration or hysteresis reported; the paper notes no PK/PD modelling was used. Weights are actual body weight (rates per kg based on 182, 108, 100 kg; whether per actual or adjusted weight is not stated). Opioids (fentanyl, sufentanil) are co-administered; no MEAC, ke0 or potency ratio reported. Opioid audit: none.

## Covariates
None analysed.

## Caveats
Three cases, no controls, no concentration data; BIS poorly tracks analgesic need per authors. Two morbidly obese/obese patients, so mg/kg/h are not comparable to normal-weight values.
