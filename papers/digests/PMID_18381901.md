# Gründer 2008: aripiprazole serum concentration vs striatal and extrastriatal D2/D3 occupancy ([18F]fallypride PET)

## Study
- 16 inpatients with schizophrenia/schizoaffective disorder on stable oral aripiprazole 5-30 mg/day (mean 18.8,
  SD 7.2) for >= 4 weeks; 15 male; age 30 +/- 10 (19-50). Comedication: antidepressants 7, zopiclone 3,
  lorazepam 5.
- Baseline: 8 drug-free patients with schizophrenia (18-58 y); their mean BP is the common unblocked baseline.
- One [18F]fallypride PET (180 min dynamic, 207 MBq) per patient, 4-78 h after the last dose (Table 1);
  simplified reference tissue model, cerebellum reference. Regions: putamen, caudate, thalamus, amygdala,
  inferior temporal cortex.
- Serum aripiprazole and dehydroaripiprazole by HPLC with column switching and UV detection; LOQ "below 50 ng/mL";
  samples at 8 a.m. trough and immediately before tracer injection.
- Clinical TDM sample: 128 patients, 203 trough serum samples, dose 20 +/- 9 mg/day (10-60).
- Occupancy vs serum concentration fitted with Emax x C / (EC50 + C) (one-site binding, no Hill term),
  SigmaPlot 9 nonlinear regression.

## Model and parameters
No pharmacokinetic model. Concentrations (Results, Table 1): at PET, aripiprazole 245 +/- 307 ng/mL (27-1144),
dehydroaripiprazole 58 +/- 60 (0-210); morning trough 282 +/- 267 (50-862) and 76 +/- 84 (0-261). TDM sample
mean 228 +/- 142 ng/mL, median 196, IQR 134-294; serum level correlated with dose (r = 0.510). Dose did not
correlate with serum level or occupancy in the 16 PET patients. Elimination half-life quoted from the literature
as about 72 h (Discussion) and 60-70 h (later Discussion; ref 29).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: D2/D3 receptor occupancy (% reduction in [18F]fallypride BP vs unmedicated mean), serum (not plasma)
concentration, total drug.
- Parent aripiprazole only (Table 2, mean and SD of the fit; text layer prints the rows out of order, assignment
  below follows printed row order): putamen Emax 92% (4), EC50 10 (4) ng/mL; caudate 92 (4), 9 (4); thalamus
  90 (2), 6 (2); amygdala 88 (2), 4 (2); inferior temporal cortex 89 (3), 7 (3). Text: EC50 range 4-10 ng/mL;
  abstract: 5-10 ng/mL. Mean occupancy 83-85% across regions; no regional difference (repeated-measures ANOVA).
- Aripiprazole + dehydroaripiprazole summed (Results p.991, text): putamen Emax 95%, EC50 20 ng/mL (r = 0.67);
  caudate 95%, 18; thalamus 92%, 12; amygdala 89%, 7; inferior temporal cortex 92%, 15. Correlations slightly
  better in four of five regions with the sum.
- Saturation above about 100-150 ng/mL; authors propose 100-150 ng/mL as an efficacy threshold.
- No ke0, no hysteresis analysis: one concentration-occupancy curve fitted all scan times (4-78 h). Patient 6,
  78 h off drug, 106 ng/mL, occupancy about 75-83% (64% cortex). Authors infer near-saturation for about 1 week
  after stopping when serum > 600 ng/mL (an extrapolation, not measured).
- Not an opioid; no MEAC or potency ratio.

## Covariates
None modelled.

## Caveats
n = 16, mostly male; occupancy uses a group-mean baseline from 8 other patients; possible small cerebellar
specific binding (underestimate < 5%); assay LOQ < 50 ng/mL is coarse relative to EC50 values of 4-20 ng/mL;
occupancy near saturation in almost all patients, so EC50 is poorly informed (lowest serum level 27 ng/mL);
comedications; Table 2 row mapping uncertain from the text layer.

## Verification against stanpumpR
- docs/antipsychotics.md:49-51: **discrepancy** (in the documentation's reasoning, not in any code). The full text
  does support the brief's values: Results p.991 gives Emax 95% and EC50 20 ng/mL for the putamen when aripiprazole
  and dehydroaripiprazole are summed (caudate 95% / 18 ng/mL). The abstract's 5-10 ng/mL is the parent-only fit;
  the two are different driver concentrations, not a contradiction. The decision not to implement it can stand
  (the model uses Kim 2012's parent-only fit), but the stated reason should change from "not supported" to
  "supported for putamen on parent + dehydro; not used because ...". Also note the end point is
  [18F]fallypride D2/D3 occupancy in patients with serum levels mostly at saturation, which is a weak basis for
  EC50.
