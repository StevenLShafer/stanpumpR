# Brenner 2003: steady-state celecoxib and diclofenac in young vs elderly, by CYP2C9 genotype

## Study
Double-blind randomized crossover. 12 young (31.7 ± 5.0 y, 70.7 ± 12.0 kg; 6 F/6 M) and 12 elderly (68.3 ± 2.1 y,
82.0 ± 15.3 kg; 9 F/3 M) healthy Caucasians (Table I; sex counts read from a garbled table). Celecoxib 200 mg bid
and diclofenac 75 mg bid, each 15 days, ≥3-week washout. After the last morning dose, plasma at 0, 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2,
2.5, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12, 24, 25 h. Celecoxib by HPLC-fluorescence (LOQ 0.5 µg/L); free fraction by equilibrium dialysis
at the peak sample. NCA (TopFit 2.0); CL/F = D/AUCτ, CLu/F = D/(AUCτ·fu). Genotypes: *1/*1 10, *1/*2 6, *2/*2 2,
*1/*3 4, *3/*3 1 (one refused).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. Not an opioid.

## Covariates
Age: no effect on celecoxib (total or unbound). CYP2C9 genotype: no association at steady state (two highest AUCs
were *1/*3; the *3/*3 subject was in the normal range). Age effect on diclofenac AUC attributed by the authors to
the elderly group's higher body weight.

## Caveats
n = 12 per group; elderly were fit (to 75 y). Second peaks in 10 celecoxib profiles. Equilibrium dialysis binding
(99.5%) higher than literature 97.4%. Mean CL/F (from individual values) differs from dose/mean AUC.
