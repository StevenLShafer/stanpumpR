# Bruno 1998: docetaxel population PK/PD in phase II

This is the companion to the paper the library cites (Bruno 1996, J Pharmacokinet Biopharm 24:153). The context file lists no repo locations; `R/drugs_docetaxel.R` models docetaxel from Bruno 1996 (ref 7 here), so this card is an independent check on it.

## Study
- 24 open phase II monotherapy studies (May 1992-March 1994), >50 European and 3 US centres. Docetaxel 75 or 100 mg/m2 (95% got 100) as a 1-h infusion every 3 weeks; first course analysed.
- 936 registered, 721 sampled, 640 assessable for PK (68% of treated). Median age 56 (5-95%: 38-71), 270 men / 370 women. Median albumin 41 g/L, AAG 1.34 g/L (0.76-2.59), total protein 71 g/L; 26 (4.1%) with elevated liver enzymes (Table 2, page image).
- Sparse sampling: four randomised schedules of 3 samples each, from mid- or end-infusion to 6 h (Table 1), optional late sample at 12-24 h; D-optimal times 0:30 or 1:00, 1:15, 1:45, 3:45, 8:20, 24:00 h. Mean 2.8 samples per patient (716 points in a 254-patient subset).
- Plasma docetaxel by HPLC-UV after solid-phase extraction. Individual Bayesian estimates by NONMEM IV, using the Bruno 1996 population model as prior (three-compartment, first-order elimination, proportional residual error). PK/PD by logistic and Cox regression (SAS).

Population-model facts quoted (Discussion, p192-193): BSA, AAG and hepatic function are the three main CL predictors; age and albumin minor (about -6.7% CL at age 71); CL 27% lower in 23/547 patients with concomitant ALT/AST >1.5 x ULN and ALP >2.5 x ULN; docetaxel CL unchanged with liver metastases alone or isolated enzyme elevation.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Exposure-response only; no effect-site, ke0, EC50 or MEAC. Not an opioid.
- Breast cancer (n=201): no exposure-efficacy relation.
- NSCLC (n=151): AUC predicted time to progression, P=.0232, risk ratio 0.891 per unit AUC (Table 3); 43% lower risk per 5 AUC units; median TTP 99 d.
- Grade 4 neutropenia (n=582, incidence 64%): CLf odds ratio 4.26 (95% CI 2.46-7.39, P<.0001, Table 4); AAG OR 0.17 per g/L. A 50% fall in CL = 1 unit of CLf.
- Febrile neutropenia (n=582, 4.5%): CLf OR 3.03 (1.55-5.93, P=.0012, Table 5); a 27% CL fall predicted a 1.5-fold rise in odds, while observed incidence in patients with elevated enzymes was 22.6% vs 6.2% (p193).
- Fluid retention (n=631, 53%): cumulative dose strongest (RR 1.005 per mg/m2); t>0.20 umol/L RR 1.087 per h (P=.0029, Table 6).
- Recommended: 25% dose reduction (100 to 75 mg/m2) in elevated hepatic enzymes.

## Covariates
Reported effects from the 1996 model and this analysis: BSA, AAG, hepatic enzymes, age, albumin on CL (above). AAG is also a strong predictor of every PD end point.

## Caveats
- Sparse sampling, mostly to 6 h (outpatients); late samples only 67 from 50 patients.
- Only 25 patients (4%) had the recommended dexamethasone premedication, so its effect could not be assessed.
- AAG's protective association with toxicity was unexplained by the authors; no data beyond 3.5 x ULN (transaminases), 5 x ULN (ALP) or with raised bilirubin.
- "Higher/lower" claims here are within-study: CL 27% lower is against patients with normal enzymes in this population.

## Verification against stanpumpR
- Independent check of the model: the model's mean-covariate CL is 36.70 L/h (header `R/drugs_docetaxel.R:33-36`), this paper's median individual CL is 36.3 L/h (Table 2, page image) and the phase I estimate 35.6 L/h: **consistent**. BSA 1.78 m2 in the model's cohort and dose 100 mg/m2 give AUC about 4.8 ug.h/mL by dose/CL (derived: 178 mg / 36.7 L/h = 4.85), consistent with the median 4.81.
- Hepatic factor: code holds HEP12 at 0 (line 40-43 header) and the header says the HEP12 patient's clearance is a third lower (0.334); this paper reports 27% (different enzyme definition). Not a code error because the field is not applied; see open question.
- Units: this paper reports CL in L/h and AUC in ug.h/mL; the code converts L/h to L/min by /60 (`R/drugs_docetaxel.R:136-138`): **correct**.
- No concentration end point or therapeutic range is drawn (`MEAC` 0); the paper gives only threshold-duration measures (0.05-0.20 umol/L = 0.04-0.16 ug/mL) as exposure metrics, not therapeutic ranges: **consistent** with `therapeuticRanges.csv`'s "no range established".
