# Bouazza 2011: lamivudine population PK from neonates to adolescents

## Study
- Retrospective therapeutic drug monitoring data from France (the Paris AP-HP hospitals). 580 HIV-infected or HIV-exposed
  children (treatment or prevention of mother-to-child transmission), age 2 days to 18 years (median 7.41 y), weight
  1 to 84 kg (median 23 kg). Under French rules no consent was needed (p. 1, printed 3498).
- Oral lamivudine as tablet or solution; median (SD) dose 7.5 (3.2) mg/kg/day; BID and once-daily (OAD) regimens.
  Mean follow-up 2.5 months.
- 2,106 plasma concentrations, 7 below the LOQ. Assay: HPLC-UV (270 nm) on 100 uL of plasma; LOQ 0.02 mg/L,
  interassay precision 10%, overall recovery 65%. Total plasma concentration (lamivudine is less than 36% protein-bound).
- One sample per occasion, so interoccasion variability could not be estimated.
- Monolix 3.1 (SAEM) population model; diagnostics in R; VPC; dose simulations aimed at the adult AUC0-24 range
  of 8.9-16.6 mg·h/L (taken from refs 7 and 24, adults on 300 mg once daily).

## Model and parameters
Two compartments with first-order absorption. Apparent parameters (F unknown). Allometric size scaling at 70 kg,
with exponents FIXED at 0.75 (CL, Q) and 1 (Vc, Vp), plus a sigmoid postmenstrual-age (PMA) maturation function
on CL/F only. IIV (exponential) only on CL/F and Vc/F; residual error proportional. Table 1 (PDF p. 3, printed 3500),
confirmed on the rendered page:

Final model (PDF p. 3): CL/F = θCL·(BW/70)^0.75·PMA^γ/(PMA50^γ + PMA^γ); Vc/F = θVc·(BW/70);
Q/F = θQ·(BW/70)^0.75; Vp/F = θVp·(BW/70). PMA = postnatal age + gestational age; gestational age was set to
40 weeks when unknown.

Other numbers:
- Table 2 (PDF p. 4): median (SD) AUC0-24, Cmin, Cmax. Children 1-11 y (n = 302): BID 4 mg/kg q12h gives
  AUC 12.5 (4.0) mg·h/L, Cmin 0.11 (0.12), Cmax 1.1 (0.23) mg/L. OAD 8 mg/kg q24h gives 11.1 (3.8), 0.05 (0.11)
  and 1.9 (0.47). Adolescents over 12 y (n = 114): BID 150 mg q12h gives 14.8 (3.8), 0.15 (0.09) and 1.2 (0.32).
  OAD 300 mg q24h gives 12.8 (3.6), 0.07 (0.06) and 1.9 (0.44). AUC differences were not significant
  (P 0.75, 0.20); Cmin and Cmax differed at P < 1e-4.
- Table 3 (PDF p. 4), this study's median (95% CI) CL in L/h/kg and AUC0-24 in mg·h/L:
  - newborns under 1 month (n 77): 0.26 (0.15-0.36), 16.7 (7.0-92.5)
  - infants under 1 year (n 45): 0.49 (0.21-0.95), 13.0 (5.7-29.1)
  - children 1-11 years (n 426): 0.64 (0.30-1.04), 12.2 (6.4-27.4)
  - adolescents 12-18 years (n 129): 0.49 (0.24-0.72), 13.1 (5.4-23.3)
  - The table also lists values from the literature (refs 6, 8, 16, 23).
- Total apparent volume Vc+Vp = 205.4 L/70 kg (p. 4). The authors compare it with adult values of 128 L (ref 17) and
  238 L (ref 5), and compare CL/F with adult values of 29.6 L/h (ref 18) and 26.7 L/h (ref 10).
- Dose recommendations (abstract; Table 4, PDF p. 5): 4 mg/kg/day from 0-8 weeks, 5 mg/kg/day from 8-16 weeks,
  6 mg/kg from 16-25 weeks, 8 mg/kg/day from 25 weeks to 14 kg, 150 mg/day for 14-25 kg, 225 mg/day for 25-35 kg,
  and 300 mg/day above 35 kg. Simulated median AUC0-24 was 12.1-14.1 mg·h/L across these groups.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. This is an antiretroviral PK paper, not an opioid paper. No ke0, MEAC or potency ratio. The authors say no
concentration-efficacy relation has been shown in children, so the only target is exposure: the adult AUC0-24 of
8.9-16.6 mg·h/L (p. 5, printed 3503).

## Covariates
Body weight (fixed allometry) on all clearances and volumes; PMA (sigmoid Emax, PMA50 59 weeks, γ 3.02) on CL/F.
Formulation (tablet vs solution) had no significant effect on bioavailability. In the discussion, PMA50 is longer
than the 48 weeks reported for GFR maturation (ref 21), and is said to resemble vancomycin's (ref 3).

## Caveats
- Retrospective TDM with sparse sampling (one sample per occasion). The dose and sampling times come from routine
  records.
- High shrinkage on Vc/F (0.39), so individual volumes are unreliable. Q, Vp and ka have no IIV.
- Gestational age was imputed as 40 weeks when missing, which affects PMA for preterm neonates.
- The "adult" CL/F of 31 L/h/70 kg is extrapolated from children up to 84 kg. Adults were not studied.
- Proportional residual error is large (0.5).
