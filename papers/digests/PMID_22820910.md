# Li 2012: population PK of clozapine and norclozapine in Chinese inpatients

## Study
- Prospective collection of therapeutic-drug-monitoring (TDM) data: 162 inpatients with refractory schizophrenia (DSM-IV), age 18-59 (mean 35.5 +/- 10.6 y), several mental-health sites in China (PDF p.2-4, Table 1).
- Oral clozapine two or three times a day, one brand (Jiangsu Nhwa). 20% of samples 0.5-4.5 h after the last dose, the rest 8.5-15.5 h after (PDF p.3). 809 clozapine and 808 norclozapine concentrations; mean 1.14 +/- 0.73 and 0.54 +/- 0.32 umol/L (Table 1).
- Assay: HPLC-UV, 254 nm, desipramine internal standard; LLOQ 0.08 umol/L for both; CV <5%. Section title says serum, the Methods text calls them plasma (PDF p.3).
- Smoker = 5 or more cigarettes/day in the past week (self-report and nursing assessment). Weight and BSA were recorded but weight is not summarized in Table 1.
- NONMEM 7, ADVAN5, FOCE (FOCE-I deviated more and was rejected); covariate inclusion at dOFV >6.63 (P<0.01), backward elimination at 7.88 (P<0.005); validated internally by NPDE (PDF p.3-4, 6).

## Model and parameters
One compartment for each moiety, first-order oral absorption of clozapine, first-order conversion of clozapine to norclozapine from the clozapine central compartment (fraction KF), remainder to other metabolites (Figure 1, PDF p.3). Parameters are apparent (CL/F, V/F); F is unknown. Table 2 (PDF p.4, printed p.1412, confirmed on the page image); values for a **female non-smoker**:

- Covariate form: TVCL = theta x (1 + SEX x theta_SEX), female = 0, male = 1; the smoking term is applied the same way (PDF p.3). Combined sex and smoking effects are described in the text (PDF p.4, 5).
- No clearance or half-life of the oral dose is derived in the paper (derived, 1-compartment: clozapine t1/2 = 0.693 x 526 / 21.9 = 16.6 h; norclozapine t1/2 = 0.693 x 624 / 32.7 = 13.2 h; female nonsmoker).
- Table 2's footnote labels theta_gender as "effect of smoking status on clearance"; the text and the model form make clear it is sex (apparent typo).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. The authors state their goal is to link the PK to antipsychotic effect later. No therapeutic range, MEAC, ke0 or potency ratio is given (not an opioid). The Introduction recommends routine TDM of both analytes.

## Covariates
- Smoking: clearance higher by 45% (clozapine) and 54.3% (norclozapine), P<0.001 (PDF p.4). Only 2 of 162 patients were female smokers (Table 1), so the female-smoker effect is inferred from the model.
- Male sex: clearance higher by 20.8% (clozapine) and 24.2% (norclozapine), P<0.005. The comparator is female (same smoking status).
- Weight and age were tested and not significant (PDF p.4, 5). The text says clearance is 23.6% higher in female smokers than male nonsmokers (PDF p.5); the Table 2 factors give 1.45/1.208 = 1.200 for clozapine and 1.543/1.242 = 1.242 for norclozapine (derived), so the single 23.6% figure matches neither exactly.
- The authors report Chinese and American patients' clearances were similar to published American values (PDF p.5).

## Caveats
- Authors: smoking by self-report, dichotomous only; concomitant medications not analysed (CYP1A2 inhibitors etc.).
- Sparse TDM data, 80% of samples 8.5-15.5 h post-dose; Ka and KF fixed from other studies, so absorption and formation are not estimated; apparent parameters (F unknown).
- Slight bias in the norclozapine NPDE (PDF p.5-6). Residual error is a mixture (additive + proportional), in umol/L.
- Table 1 count inconsistency (open question).
