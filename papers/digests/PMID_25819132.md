# Zhang 2015: population PK of dolutegravir in treatment-naive HIV patients

## Study
- Pooled population PK from three trials: ING111521 (phase 2a, 10-day monotherapy, 10 and 50 mg once daily; the 2 mg arm excluded; n=19), SPRING-1 (phase 2b, 10/25/50 mg once daily with ABC/3TC or TDF/FTC; n=141) and SPRING-2 (phase 3, 50 mg once daily; n=403). Total 563 adults, 3357 plasma concentrations (Table 1, Table 2; PDF p3-4).
- Demographics (Table 2, all studies): age median 37 y (18-68); weight median 74.5 kg (39.0-135, some imputed); 85% male; 83% Caucasian, 12% Black.
- Sampling: dense on days 1 and 10 in ING111521; sparse and a few intensive profiles in SPRING-1/2 (Table 1).
- Assay: plasma, protein precipitation, LC-MS/MS with labelled internal standard; LLOQ 5 or 20 ng/mL depending on study; BLQ (1%) excluded. Total (not unbound) plasma concentration; DTG is bound >=98.9% to plasma protein (Introduction).
- NONMEM VII, FOCEI; full-covariate approach then backward elimination; PC-VPC and 1000-run bootstrap.

## Model and parameters
One compartment, first-order absorption with lag time, first-order elimination, oral dosing (apparent parameters). IIV (log-normal) on CL/F, V/F, ka; IOV on CL/F; proportional residual error. Reference subject: 40-year-old, 70 kg male, non-smoker, bilirubin 9 umol/L (Table 3 footnote, PDF p6, printed 507). The reference subject is stated to be on a >10 mg dose, which is the baseline for the 10 mg bioavailability factor.

Covariate equations (Table 3 footnotes):
- CL/F = 0.901 x 1.16^SMOK x 1.35^POC x (WT/70)^0.438 x (AGE/40)^0.193 x (BILI/9)^-0.211; POC = 1 only for study ING111521.
- V/F = 17.4 x (WT/70)^0.768.
- F = 1.21^GEND x 1.24^DOSE (GEND = 1 female; DOSE = 1 for the 10 mg dose).

Other facts: correlation of CL/F with V/F (0.375) was dropped (PDF p6). Weight range 39-135 kg gives CL/F 0.697-1.20 L/h and V/F 11.1-28.8 L (p6). Age 18-68 y gives CL/F 0.772-0.998 L/h; bilirubin 3-38 umol/L gives 1.14-0.665 L/h. Pooled 50 mg arms (n=449): AUC 53.6 ug/mL*h, Cmax 3.67, Ctau 1.11 ug/mL (text, p7). Steady-state exposure effects: females Cmax/AUC/Ctau +32/+26/+15%; smokers -7/-13/-22%; study ING111521 exposure 20-50% lower than SPRING at the same dose (p7-8). No two-compartment improvement.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD model. Exploratory graphs of steady-state AUC, Cmax and Ctau against antiviral response (HIV-1 RNA <50 copies/mL at week 48, CD4 change) showed no relationship, which the authors attribute to high potency at 10 mg and above; serum creatinine and creatinine clearance correlated slightly with exposure (OCT2 inhibition). No MEAC, EC50, ke0, tPeak or effect-site model. Not an opioid.

## Covariates
Retained: weight (CL/F, V/F), smoking, age and total bilirubin (CL/F), sex (F), study (CL/F), 10 mg dose (F). Not significant: race, ethnicity, HBV/HCV, CDC class, albumin, creatinine clearance, ALT, AST (p6). Authors judge all effects <32% on exposure and not clinically significant.

## Caveats
- Sparse sampling in the phase 2b/3 trials; few elderly (17 patients >55 y, one >65 y), so the unexpected positive age effect on CL/F is poorly supported (authors' own caveat, p11).
- Unexplained 35% higher CL/F in the monotherapy proof-of-concept study.
- Apparent parameters only; no IV data, so CL and V are not separable from F.
- Only HIV-infected treatment-naive adults; ka 2.24 1/h has RSE 15.4% and wide CI.
