# Liu 2016: IV esomeprazole PopPK and intragastric-H+ PD in healthy Chinese volunteers

## Study
- Single-centre, open-label, randomised 5-way crossover (7-day washout), 20 healthy Chinese volunteers (6 female, 14 male; median age 34 y, range 24-41; total body weight median 61.0 kg, range 48.0-74.0; lean body weight 47.4 kg; BMI 22.1; Table 1, PDF p. 4 printed 820). 11 H. pylori-negative, 9 positive. CYP2C19: 7 homozygote-EM, 11 heterozygote-EM, 2 PM.
- Regimens (PDF p. 2): (1) 40 mg single injection; (2) 40 mg over 30 min at 0 and 12 h; (3) 40 mg over 30 min then 8 mg/h for 23.5 h; (4) 80 mg over 30 min then 4 mg/h for 23.5 h; (5) 80 mg over 30 min then 8 mg/h for 23.5 h.
- Plasma esomeprazole (total, not unbound) at 0, 10, 20, 30, 45, 60, 75, 90, 105, 120 min and 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10, 12, 24 h; LC-MS/MS after solid-phase extraction (accuracy 85-115%, precision <15%). Intragastric pH logged every 10 s for 24 h with a pH electrode about 10 cm below the lower oesophageal sphincter; meals at 4 h and 9 h.
- NONMEM 7.2 FOCEI for PK (mixed error, exponential IIV; VPC and bootstrap n = 500). PD fitted to group means in ADAPT 5, sequentially after PK.

No IIV on Km or CLd (fixed at 0). Vmax is in µg/h and Km in ng/mL (= µg/L), so Vmax/Km is in L/h. The model is not weight-scaled.
PD (Table 3, PDF p. 7, printed 822; HP-negative then HP-positive, group-mean fit): kdeg (H+/K+-ATPase turnover) 0.00827 and 0.00109 1/h; kd (potency, irreversible inhibition) 0.0105 and 0.00505 1/h per ng/mL; kfe 0.694 and 1.05 1/h; tlag 0.396 and 0.521 h; VEf4 300 and VEf9 295 (fixed). See `open_questions` for the other rows. The Discussion quotes kdeg as 0.00827 and 0.00109 1/h, and kd as 0.0105 and 0.00505 1/h per ng/mL.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: intragastric H+ concentration/pH (24 h electrode). Indirect irreversible response (cell-killing) model on relative H+/K+-ATPase activity, with an asymmetric circadian night-time surge (Eq. 5) and a food-dilution term (Eqs 6-7); pH capped at 7.4.
- No effect-site equilibration: a biophase compartment was tried and could not be estimated; a lag time (0.396 h HP-negative, 0.521 h HP-positive) was used instead. No ke0, t½ke0 or tPeak.
- No EC50 or MEAC; pH above 4 and above 6 are used as targets. Observed percent time pH >4 over 24 h (Table 4): 64.6% (HP-negative) and 96.4% (HP-positive) after 40 mg once; 95.3-96.4% with regimens 2-5 in both subgroups. Percent time pH >6 observed 25.0-57.9% (HP-negative) and 45.8-60.9% (HP-positive).
- Not an opioid paper; no potency ratio.
- Authors fitted kdeg about one fifth of a published white-subject value (0.04/h, lansoprazole, ref 12), and the HP-negative/positive difference is preliminary (no placebo arm; the PD fit did not converge on individuals).

## Covariates
CYP2C19 genotype on Vmax (Eq. 12): heterozygote-EM x0.894, PM x0.710, relative to homozygote EM (ΔOFV 6.7). Because Km is shared, the elimination rate falls 10.6% and 29.0%. BMI trend not significant. Body size, age, sex, renal and hepatic indices not retained. HP status is a PD covariate only.

## Caveats
- n = 20, only 2 poor metabolisers, so the CYP2C19 effects have wide intervals (heterozygote RSE 80.6%; its bootstrap CI includes zero). Km fixed. 134 of 500 bootstrap runs hit a boundary and were skipped.
- Residual bias late after dose, possibly self-inhibition, not modelled. PD model preliminary.
- Healthy Chinese volunteers, IV only, 24 h. Total (not unbound) plasma concentration.
