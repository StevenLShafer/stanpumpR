# Sanz-Codina 2023: ceftriaxone total-plasma PK and saturable binding by ultrafiltration vs microdialysis

## Study
Six healthy men, 18-55 y eligible; mean age 31.5 ± 3.6 y, BMI 23.5 ± 3.1 kg/m² (p. 384); weight, height and
serum albumin not reported. Single 2 g ceftriaxone IV over 30 min (Ceftriaxone Kabi). Venous plasma at 0.5, 1, 2, 4,
6, 8 h after start of infusion (total, and free by in vitro ultrafiltration (UF), Vivacon 500 30K, 37 °C); two
intravascular microdialysis (MD) probes per subject, 20 kDa cut-off, sampled in intervals to 8 h, retrodialysis
calibration. HPLC-UV, LLOQ 0.1 mg/L plasma, 0.03 mg/L saline. NCA (WinNonlin 8.0); population model NONMEM 7.4.3 /
PsN, uncertainty by LLP-SIR. PTA by Monte Carlo (n = 5000) at albumin 680 µmol/L (~4.5 g/dL). 180 samples in the
model (plasma 36, UF 36, MD 108).

## Model and parameters
Two-compartment, linear disposition fitted to TOTAL plasma (better than 1-compartment, dOFV -33.2); IIV on CL only;
proportional residual error; no covariate analysis (small n). Free concentration by a saturable single-site
binding model (Eq. 3-4), Ctotal = Cu + NP·CP·Cu/(Kd + Cu), CP = albumin, modelled in molar concentrations;
saturable binding beat constant fu (dOFV UF -123.3, MD -170.7).

NCA (Table 1, mean ± SD): total Cmax 297.42 ± 21.0 mg/L, AUClast 1092.90 ± 90.91 mg·h/L, kel 0.124 ± 0.01 1/h,
t½ 5.66 ± 0.62 h; UF free Cmax 52.83 ± 5.07, AUClast 119.73; MD free Cmax 81.37 ± 26.93, AUClast 189.05. (The CL and
Vd,ss row of Table 1 is garbled in the text layer; its footnote says the values compare UF with MD.)
Observed pairs (p. 384): end of infusion total 297 ± 21.0 and UF free 52.8 ± 5.07 mg/L; at 8 h 75.9 ± 9.6 and
5.84 ± 1.0 mg/L; observed fu ~18% at peak, 7.8% at 8 h. Model-predicted fu (p. 386) at 0.5 h: MD 0.21, UF 0.10; at
8 h: MD 0.055, UF 0.042. AUCfree/AUCtotal 10.96% (UF), 17.25% (MD).
(Derived: 2 g / 1.21 L/h = 1653 mg·h/L, consistent with AUClast 1093 + 75.9/0.124 = 1705.)

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No effect-site model. PK/PD targets 100% fT>MIC and 100% fT>4×MIC; EUCAST resistance breakpoint cited as 2 mg/L.
PTA from MD was at most 27% higher than from UF, largest for twice-daily regimens; only 2 g q12h exceeded 90% PTA at
MIC 2 mg/L (Fig. 6). Not an opioid.

## Covariates
None (not tested).

## Caveats
n = 6 healthy young men; single dose; 8 h sampling only (terminal phase short relative to t½). Albumin of the
subjects not reported. UF and MD give materially different binding (UF lower free, MD higher, especially in
distribution). Model-predicted UF fu at 0.5 h (0.10) is well below the observed ratio (~0.18); the paper does not
reconcile this. MD shows ~15 min apparent delay from interval averaging.
