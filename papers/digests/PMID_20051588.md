# Kaneda 2010: three-compartment population PK of bolus mannitol at craniotomy

## Study
- Randomized, observational PK. 22 adults (18-75 y, ASA 1-3) having elective craniotomy for brain tumour.
- 20% mannitol 0.5 g/kg (n = 10) or 1.0 g/kg (n = 12), infused over 15 min.
- Exclusions included creatinine above 1.2, heart failure and diuretic use.
- Table I, reassembled (0.5 vs 1.0 g/kg):
  - M:F 3:7 vs 3:9; age 56 ± 13 vs 56 ± 14 y.
  - Weight 79 ± 25 vs 93 ± 26 kg; height 167 vs 168 cm.
  - BUN 13 vs 17 mg/dL; Cr 0.7 ± 0.1 vs 0.8 ± 0.1 mg/dL.
  - Cockcroft-Gault CrCL 110 ± 29 vs 120 ± 48 mL/min.
  - Dose 218 ± 69 vs 512 ± 142 mOsm.
- Arterial samples at 5, 10, 15 (end of infusion), 20, 25, 30, 35, 40, 45 and 50 min and at 1, 1.5, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8 and 12 h; 270 samples in all.
- General anaesthesia with volatile agent and N2O; crystalloid 2-3 mL/kg/h.
- Assay: GC-FID of the n-butylboronic acid derivative, dulcitol internal standard, LLOQ < 0.1 mg/mL.
- Analysis: NONMEM VI, FOCE-I, ADVAN6.
  - Forward selection at P < .01; backward elimination at P < .001.

## Model and parameters
Table III (final model; population mean (SE%), IIV CV% (SE%)):
- V1 = θ1 = 2.80 L (4.5). No IIV on V1 (removed before covariate screening).
- V2 = θ2 × exp(θ7 × (WT − 87.0)); θ2 = 8.86 L (7.8), IIV 18.8%.
- V3 = θ3 = 12.0 L (10.3), IIV 28.2%.

- θ7 = 0.0136 /kg (Table IV).
- Proportional residual 10.8%.
- MDAPE 12.9%, MDPE −2.0%.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- None modelled; the paper discusses osmolality and ICP only qualitatively.
- Peak plasma mannitol at the end of infusion: 7.1 ± 1.1 mg/mL (high dose, n = 11) vs 3.3 ± 0.7 mg/mL (low dose, n = 6).
- No osmolality data are reported.

## Covariates
- Weight on V2 (exponential, centred at 87 kg).
- Dose group on CL1 (low dose −34.4%).
- CrCL on CL1 entered in forward selection but was removed in backward elimination (P < .001 criterion).

## Caveats
- n = 22, with obese patients included.
- Two doses only, and "nonlinearity" is a dose-group effect.
- Anaesthetised neurosurgical patients with normal renal function.
- Large bootstrap biases in IIV.
- The authors themselves question whether the small CL1 difference matters clinically.
- In the Discussion, Kaneda quotes Rudehill's CL1 as 0.14 L/min (Rudehill: V1 6.8, V2 25.9 L; Vdss 32.7 L).

## Verification against stanpumpR
- **Parameter values** (R/drugs_mannitol.R:45-50): **matches** the abstract and Table III.
  - V1 2.80, V2 8.86, V3 12.0 L; CL2 2.07 and CL3 0.16 L/min.
  - CL1 0.07 L/min is the rounded high-dose value. The exact θ4 is 0.0683 (2.4% lower) and the exact low-dose value 0.0448.
  - Units are L and L/min, as in the code.
- **Covariate equations and reference weight**: **discrepancy (documentation).** docs/mannitol.md:59-62 and R/drugs_mannitol.R:52-55 say only the abstract was available and that the covariate equations and the reference weight are unknown. The full paper gives them:
  - V2 = 8.86 × exp(0.0136 × (WT − 87)), so the 8.86 L belongs to an 87-kg patient, not 70 kg. At 70 kg Kaneda's V2 would be 7.03 L (derived).
  - The dose effect on CL1 is a factor of (1 − 0.344).
  - The study's mean weight was 79-93 kg.
  - stanpumpR treats all the means as 70-kg values and scales them to fat-free mass. That is a defensible choice, but the statement that the source gives no reference weight is wrong.
- **Renal reference**: R/drugs_mannitol.R:79-81 assigns Kaneda's CL1 to a Cockcroft-Gault clearance of 102 mL/min. The study's patients had a mean CrCL of 110-120 mL/min (Table I). This is a stanpumpR assumption, so the comparison is noted, not judged.
  - "no renal covariate in the source": **matches** the final model.
  - The paper did screen CrCL, which entered at P < .01 and was eliminated at P < .001. The repo does not mention this.
- **Terminal half-life "4.4 hours"** (help:29, docs/mannitol.md:78-79; "7.3 hours" with 0.04): **matches as derived values** (4.41 h and 7.32 h). Kaneda does not report a half-life, so help:29 calling it "Kaneda's terminal half-life" means the model-derived value.
- **"0.04 L/min in the 0.5 g/kg group and 0.07 L/min in the 1.0 g/kg group"**: **matches** the abstract.
- **"22 adults … over 15 minutes for elective craniotomy"**: **matches**.
- **Rudehill clearance**: docs/mannitol.md:82 cites "Rudehill, 87 mL/min". Kaneda's Table V and Discussion quote Rudehill's CL1 as 0.14 L/min. This cannot be settled here (Rudehill is not in this batch) and is flagged for the Rudehill digest.
- **Verdict: discrepancy** (documentation of the covariate model and reference weight; the transcribed values themselves match).
