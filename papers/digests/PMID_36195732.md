# Hart 2022: therapeutic reference range for aripiprazole revised

## Study
Systematic review and meta-analysis (AGNP TDM task force; PROSPERO CRD42020215872) of aripiprazole (ARI)
blood levels (serum or plasma) versus clinical effect, side effects and striatal D2/3 occupancy in
schizophrenia, schizophrenia-spectrum and bipolar disorder (PDF p3). Four databases searched 16 Feb 2021,
updated 31 Jan 2022. 51 articles / 53 study cohorts (2002-2021): 29 after oral ARI, 15 after injectables
(13 LAI, 2 acute), 9 PET (PDF p1, p4). Random-effects meta-analysis in R 4.0.3 (metafor, meta). Evidence
for a concentration/efficacy relationship graded low (level C); for concentration/side effects, absent
(level D) (PDF p1, p6). Active moiety (AM) = ARI + dehydroaripiprazole (D-ARI). No individual PK model.

## Model and parameters
No PK model. Pooled population values (all ng/mL, serum/plasma, steady state, mostly naturalistic TDM):

- Proposed therapeutic reference range: ARI **120-270 ng/mL**; AM **180-380 ng/mL** (abstract, PDF p1;
  conclusion, PDF p12 / printed 3388). Confirmed on page images.
- Population ranges from 8 naturalistic studies of 3,373 patients on flexible oral dosing (text, PDF p8):
  mean ± SD range 71-358 ng/mL; 25th-75th IQR 120-273 ng/mL (Fig. 4, PDF p9; Fig. 4 caption gives N = 3,778
  and pooled mean 214 [191, 238]).
- Combined mean concentration across 17 studies 230 ng/mL [204, 256] (N = 3,778; Fig. 3 gives 230.21
  [204.32, 256.09]); AM 305 [257, 353] (N = 3,332). Mean doses 17 and 16 mg/day (PDF p8).
- Combined mean C/D ratio: ARI 13.8 (ng/mL)/(mg/day) [12.4, 15.3] (7 studies); AM 18.2 [16.6, 19.7]
  (6 studies). Guideline (AGNP 2017) values 11.7 and 16.5 (PDF p8, p12).
- Dose-concentration regression (Fig. 2, PDF p8): y = 25.612 + 12.205 × dose (mg/day), r² = 0.719, N = 3,778.
- Table 3 (PDF p8) expected ARI level at 5/10/20/30 mg/day from C/D 13.82: 69.1, 138.2, 276.4, 414.6 ng/mL;
  AM from C/D 18.18: 90.9, 181.8, 363.6, 545.4 ng/mL.
- Metabolite-to-parent ratio D-ARI/ARI ≈ 0.40, computed as (304.6 − 218.1)/218.1 (PDF p12, suppl. fig. S13).
- Previous AGNP 2017 range: ARI 100-350, AM 150-500 ng/mL (PDF p2).
- Dose to reach range: "average" patient reaches 120 ng/mL at 9 mg/day, 270 ng/mL at 20 mg/day (PDF p11,
  from Table 3). Starting dose 10 mg/day effective in most; 5 mg/day for known CYP2D6 poor metabolizers
  (abstract says "will be sufficient", discussion p12 says "might be sufficient").

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Not an opioid: no MEAC, ke0, t½ke0, tPeak or potency ratio. End point for the range: population
concentration distribution plus PET D2/3 occupancy and scattered clinical response (PANSS/CGI).
- PET (Table 2, PDF p7; confirmed on image): Kim 2012 (healthy, [11C]raclopride) EC50 11.1 ng/mL, EC90
  100 ng/mL striatum, and a PK/PD-model EC90 of 77 ng/mL; Gründer 2008 (patients, [18F]fallypride) EC50
  10 ± 4 (putamen) and 9 ± 4 (caudate), EC90 90 (p) and 81 (c), AM EC90 180 ng/mL; Takahata 2012 EC50 9.9 (s),
  12.2 (p), EC90 89 (s), 110 (p); Kegeles 2008 ED80 5.6 ± 1.0 mg. EC90 values computed from EC50 (×9).
- >90% D2/3 occupancy, described as a prerequisite for partial-agonist efficacy, at ARI 90-110 ng/mL
  (180 ng/mL for AM) (PDF p6, p11).
- Responder ranges from single studies: 134-271 ng/mL (PANSS, Lin 2011), 124-286 ng/mL (CGI, Kirschbaum
  2008) (PDF p11).
- Upper limit is a point of futility, not a toxicity threshold; no concentration-dependent side effects shown.

## Covariates
CYP2D6 genotype: PMs about 50-56% higher dose-corrected levels (Hendset 2007; van der Weide 2015), IMs and
PMs ~40% higher AM (Jukic 2019); authors call for ≥50% dose reduction in PMs. Sex: inconsistent (+10% to +41%
in females in some studies, +28% in males in one). Age: +16% dose-corrected over 65 y in one large dataset.
Body weight: no association (4 studies). Carbamazepine −65%, valproate −23%; paroxetine raises levels
(Table 4, PDF p10).

## Caveats
Low-grade evidence; mostly naturalistic, flexibly dosed, comedicated cohorts; few Caucasian-only samples.
Sampling time for the range is not stated as a fixed window (trough was a quality criterion, Q6a); authors
cite Korell 2018 that 9-14 h vs 20-24 h sampling changes ARI levels only marginally (p12). Internal
inconsistency: text gives 3,373 patients for the population range, Fig. 4 caption gives N = 3,778 (p8-9).
The 0.40 MPR uses 218.1 as the ARI mean, which differs from the 230 combined mean in the text.
