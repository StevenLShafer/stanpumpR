# Murdoch 1999: patient-maintained propofol sedation as premedication

## Study
- 20 adults (ASA I-II, 16-70 y; mean age 40.4, range 16-67; mean weight 62.5, range 45-85 kg; 6 M : 14 F) having day-case surgery (17 varicose vein, 3 minor) under general anaesthesia, Glasgow (PDF p.2 / printed 430).
- Graseby 3400 pump with a backbar running a propofol PK model (their ref 5, White & Kenny 1990; parameters not given), age and weight entered, effect-site concentration displayed.
- Start target 1 ug/mL (1% propofol with lidocaine 20 mg/50 mL), started once the displayed effect-site concentration was within 0.1 ug/mL of target. Patient raises the target 0.2 ug/mL by double press, 2 min lockout, maximum 3 ug/mL; stepped reduction after 6 min without a demand, floor 0.2 ug/mL.
- Outcomes: STAI state anxiety (20-80), 0-100 mm VAS anxiety, modified Steward sedation score, target every 15 min; Wilcoxon signed rank test.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End points: anxiety (STAI and VAS), not a sedation depth. Table 1 median (IQR): STAI 41 (34-51) at 0 min, 26 (23-30) at 15 min; VAS 47 (33-62), 24 (7-34), 17 (1-24), 15 (0-18) at 0/15/30/45 min, 20.5 (5-28) in anaesthetic room; target 1.0 (0.8-1.4), 1.2 (0.8-1.4), 1.3 (0.6-1.6) ug/mL at 15/30/45 min, 1.2 (0.8-1.4) in anaesthetic room. n=20 except 45 min n=9. All P < 0.0001 except 45 min P < 0.01.
- Deepest sedation: rousable to speech, obeying commands. SpO2 fell to 92% and 90% in two patients aged 63 and 67 on room air, corrected by low-flow oxygen.
- Authors compare the 1.2 ug/mL median here with 0.8 ug/mL in Irwin 1997 (PMID_9203877), attributing the difference to the older, higher-ASA population there. They call Irwin's patients "orthopaedic", although Irwin's series was 20 vascular, 8 urology, 8 orthopaedic (Irwin Table 3).
- No ke0, tPeak, MEAC or potency ratio; targets are predicted blood concentrations, not measured.

## Covariates
None analysed.

## Caveats
- Uncontrolled, n=20, no comparator premedicant; predicted concentrations only (the same TCI model over-predicted measured concentrations in Irwin 1997 by 47%, so these targets may be above true concentrations).
- n falls to 9 at 45 min. Patients self-titrated.
