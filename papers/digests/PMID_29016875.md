# Moriguchi 2017: norepinephrine-transporter occupancy by single-dose duloxetine (PET)

## Study
- 8 healthy Japanese men, 25.5 +/- 5.9 yr; 2 smokers. Weight not reported.
- Single oral dose 20 mg (n = 3), 40 mg (n = 3), 60 mg (n = 2); PET with (S,S)-[18F]FMeNER-D2 at baseline and 6 h after
  the dose (median Tmax per Knadler 2011), 120-180 min after tracer injection; ECAT EXACT HR+.
- Thalamus ROI, white-matter reference; occupancy from (AUC ratio - 1) baseline vs drug.
- Plasma duloxetine by GC-MS (LLOQ 0.1 ng/mL), mean of samples just before and after the second scan.
- ED50/EC50 by GraphPad Prism 6, model occupancy % = 100 x C / (C + EC50) (Emax fixed 100%).

Fit quality: RMSE 11.8 for the hyperbolic fit vs 7.3 for a linear fit (linear fitted better; authors kept the
hyperbola as theoretically plausible). Discussion projects steady-state Cmax (Japanese label: 16.2, 31.5, 68.1 ng/mL
at 20/40/60 mg) to NET occupancy 21.8%, 35.2%, 54.0% (as printed; the 20 mg value is below the single-dose mean).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: thalamic NET occupancy (PET biomarker), not a clinical effect. EC50 58.0 ng/mL total plasma duloxetine,
healthy men, single dose; compared with SERT EC50 3.7 (Takano 2006) and 2.62 +/- 0.93 ng/mL (Abanades 2011), and
rat NET EC50 59.0 ng/mL (Bourdet 2012). No ke0 / time course. Not an opioid.

## Covariates
None modelled. Smoking noted to lower plasma concentration in one subject (CYP1A2 induction).

## Caveats
n = 8; occupancies far below Emax (max 46.7%), so EC50 is an extrapolation resting on Emax = 100%; a linear fit was
better. Single dose only. Small n per dose.
