# Fiset 1995: flumazenil EEG pharmacodynamics against midazolam sedation

## Study
- 10 healthy men, 18-50 y (Table I: age 21-47, mean 31.7; weight 73-88 kg, mean 79; height 168-191 cm, mean 177.2; PDF p5, printed p571). Four sessions.
- Session 1: individual midazolam PK (arterial, MKModel, 3-compartment in all) and EEG PD from a 2.5 mg/min infusion (maximum 30 mg). Sessions 2 and 3: STANPUMP target-controlled midazolam with each man's own PK, aimed at 20% (light) or 80% (deep) of his maximal EEG effect, for 210 min. After 20 min, flumazenil 0.5 or 1 mg/min was infused until the EEG returned to baseline, then stopped. Session 4: 80% target for 210 min with no flumazenil, to look for acute tolerance.
- Participation: 7 completed session 2, 6 of those session 3, 4 completed all. Flumazenil PK come from 7 subjects, 13 studies (Table IV).
- EEG end point: aperiodic analysis, total voltage in the 0.5-30 Hz band (uV/s); FFT total power 0.5-30 Hz in two men (subjects 1 and 7) where aperiodic analysis failed. Effect-site analysis used the semiparametric collapse of the hysteresis loop (Verotta) and a sigmoid Emax model.
- Assays: midazolam by GC with nitrogen-phosphorus detection; flumazenil by GC/MS, detection limit 0.5 ng/mL (CV 4.6% intra, 7.3% inter). Both arterial plasma, total concentration (binding not addressed).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Not an opioid paper; the audit items are recorded anyway. No MEAC or analgesic concentration (sedative/antagonist). No potency ratio between drugs.

Midazolam, EEG (aperiodic, 0.5-30 Hz uV/s), session 1, Table II (PDF p6, printed p572), plasma (arterial, total):
- Cp50 range 153-650 ng/mL, mean 384 (SD 140); t1/2 ke0 range 0.7-4.3 min, mean 2.4 (SD 1.3); gamma 1.4-3.5 per subject. n = 9 (subject 8 never reached Emax).
- Per-subject Emax 26-221 uV/s.

Flumazenil, EEG reversal of a fixed midazolam effect, Table III (PDF p12, printed p578), t1/2 ke0 in min and effect-site EC50 in ng/mL; no tPeak is reported:

- Individual rows (t1/2 ke0, EC50): subject 2: 1.1, 18.6 (20%); 5.1, 19.8 (row labelled 70%, achieved 59%). Subject 4: 7, 7.2 (20%); 4.3, 10.5 (80%). Subject 5: 7.4, 7.5 (20%); 1.7, 19.6 (row labelled 66%, achieved 69%). Subject 1 (FFT): 4.4, 17; 5.5, 29. Subject 7 (FFT): 4.9, 18; 3.2, 24.
- Footnote means recomputed from these rows: 4.96, 3.96, 13.66, 20.58 (derived: all agree). The all-session mean t1/2 ke0 is 4.46 min (derived: (24.8+19.8)/10). The deep-sedation rows are not all at 80% (subjects 2 and 5 were 70% and 66% targets for safety; text p570 says the 800 ng/mL cap was below 80% Emax in three men).
- Text says t1/2 ke0 ranged 1.1-7.0 (aperiodic) and 3.1-5.5 (FFT); Table III shows 7.4 and 3.2 (the text numbers differ slightly from the table; the table was used).
- Hysteresis: flumazenil shows a plasma-to-EEG hysteresis (Fig. 5). The authors call the spread of t1/2 ke0 larger than expected and are unsure whether it is method or biology. EC50 was higher at deep than light sedation (paired t, p < 0.05, text p574).
- Session 4: no acute tolerance to midazolam EEG effect in three of four men; one man's effect was not sustained.
- Flumazenil assumed to have no intrinsic EEG effect.
- Comparators in the discussion: flumazenil t1/2 ke0 3.3 min (Digit Symbol Substitution Test, Zhi 1994, a different end point); rat EEG showed no hysteresis (Mandema).

## Covariates
None tested for flumazenil. Men only.

## Caveats
Authors warn that parameter values be treated with caution: no single EEG descriptor worked for all subjects, only 5 subjects contribute to flumazenil PD, mixed aperiodic/FFT, and a 7-fold range of t1/2 ke0. Healthy young men only, arterial sampling, total plasma concentration.
