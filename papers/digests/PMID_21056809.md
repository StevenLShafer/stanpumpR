# Luo 2010: propofol lowers sevoflurane MAC for skin incision

## Study
Prospective, randomised, single-blinded, single-centre study (West China Hospital). 122 ASA I-II adults, age 20-50 y, BMI < 30, elective abdominal surgery (cholecystitis, gastric cancer) with incision > 10 cm. Seven groups (Table 1, PDF p.3): propofol target plasma concentration 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 ug/mL; n = 18, 15, 17, 17, 20, 16, 19. Group means: age 37-41 y, weight 55-63 kg, height 160-165 cm, BIS before incision 55.9-63.1, none different.

Anaesthesia: sevoflurane 6% inhalation induction; remifentanil 1 ug/kg one minute after loss of eyelash reflex; succinylcholine 1.0 mg/kg for intubation; propofol by TCI on a Graseby 3500 pump programmed with the Marsh model (plasma-concentration targeting, not effect-site; no blood sampling). End-tidal sevoflurane held at a preset value for at least 15 min (about 30 min after intubation, unstimulated). Neuromuscular function recovered (train-of-four) before incision. Response: gross purposeful movement within 1 min of incision. Remifentanil 1 ug/kg was given in every group; no other drugs.

Dixon up-and-down: one measurement per patient; step 0.2% sevoflurane (0.1 MAC); first group began at 2.6% (1.3 MAC); starting concentrations for the propofol groups were 2.0, 1.8, 1.6, 1.4, 0.8, 0.6% for 1-6 ug/mL. Six crossover pairs per group; MAC = mean of crossover midpoints.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: immobility (no gross purposeful movement) at skin incision; sevoflurane end-tidal % and propofol Marsh-model plasma target ug/mL. No ke0, tPeak or effect-site model (plasma and effect site assumed equilibrated after at least 15 min, citing Kazama 1997 for propofol). Not an opioid paper in its aims, though all patients had remifentanil 1 ug/kg given before the measurement; no remifentanil concentration is reported (opioid audit: no MEAC, ke0 or potency ratio). The paper states a 50% MAC reduction at propofol 4 ug/mL. The linear (additive) interaction is the authors' interpretation, consistent with Harris 2006. Authors compare the extrapolated 8.47 ug/mL with 15.2 (Smith 1994, fentanyl study) and 10.7 ug/mL (Harris 2006) as propofol-alone skin-incision Cp50.

## Covariates
None analysed. Weight, age, sex balanced across groups; no covariate effect on MAC reported.

## Caveats
Authors: no propofol-alone group, so the curve shape and 8.47 ug/mL are extrapolated; no sample-size calculation; plasma (not effect-site) targets with no measured propofol concentrations. Further, as noticed: remifentanil 1 ug/kg bolus preceded all measurements, so the MACs are with a residual opioid effect, not sevoflurane-propofol alone; no differences found between the 2 and 3 ug/mL groups (P = 0.182); the text gives the regression coefficient as -0.9529 in one sentence and r = -0.9699 elsewhere (R2 0.9407 matches 0.9699); the 6 ug/mL CI is printed with an upper limit 0.78 and the 95% CI of a mean 0.60 +/- 0.17 is plausible, so the "0.78" is the CI limit, not an SD. Linearity is imposed; 12-15 pairs and a single patient per Dixon step per group.
