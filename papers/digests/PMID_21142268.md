# Fineman 2011: exenatide extended-release (Bydureon microspheres), single and weekly dosing

## Study
Two trials in type 2 diabetes (diet/exercise and/or metformin; HbA1c 7.0-11.0%, age 18-75 y, BMI 25-45 kg/m2;
PDF p2, printed 66).
- **Single-dose trial**: 14-day open-label lead-in of exenatide IR 5 µg SC three times daily (n = 68), then
  randomised (n = 62) to placebo microspheres (n = 15) or one SC abdominal injection of exenatide ER 2.5 mg (n = 14),
  5 mg (n = 10), 7 mg (n = 12) or 10 mg (n = 11); 12-week follow-up. Sampling at -0.25, 0.25, 0.5, 1, 2, 4, 8, 12,
  16, 24, 30, 36, 48 h, then weekly (p2-3). PK evaluable n = 58 (4 more excluded for baseline exenatide >100 pg/mL).
  Demographics (Table I, ITT): 52% female, age 53 ± 8 y, weight 90 ± 19 kg, BMI 32.3 ± 4.6 (mean ± SD).
- **Weekly-dosing trial** (NCT00103935; Kim 2007 ref 11): 3-day lead-in of exenatide IR or placebo SC twice daily
  (text layer prints the dose as "5 mg"; the same layer turns "µg" into "mg" on pages verified as µg, so read as
  5 µg; p3 not rendered), then placebo (n = 14), ER 0.8 mg (n = 16) or ER 2 mg (n = 15) weekly for 15 weeks, plus
  12-week follow-up. Table I (ITT n = 45): 40% female, age 53 ± 11 y, weight 106 ± 20 kg, BMI 35.7 ± 5.7.
- **Assay**: two-site sandwich ELISA for intact exenatide, plasma, LLQ 10 pg/mL; no GLP-1 cross-reactivity (p3).
- **Analysis**: noncompartmental PK (linear trapezoid AUC; Cav = AUC/sampling period); nonparametric
  superposition of single-dose data to predict weekly dosing; exposure-response in NONMEM VI, inhibitory Emax;
  no covariate analysis (p3).

## Model and parameters
No compartmental PK model. Noncompartmental results (PDF p5, printed 69; rendered):
- Release is multiphasic: initial release tmax(0-48h) 2.1-5.1 h, then further peaks at about 2 and 7 weeks.
- AUC0-48h (geometric mean ± SE): 460 ± 57, 996 ± 125, 1498 ± 425, 1745 ± 247 pg·h/mL for 2.5, 5, 7, 10 mg,
  about 1-2% of total AUC.
- AUCDay1-study end: 40 378 ± 4903, 89 907 ± 10 416, 95 336 ± 17 176, 110 263 ± 24 769 pg·h/mL; not dose
  proportional (p < 0.001); AUC ratios vs 2.5 mg of 2.2, 2.4, 2.7 (p8).
- Mean tmax(48h-study end) 6-7 weeks (39-48 days). Cmax/Cav 3.36, 3.00, 3.08, 2.93 (2.5, 5, 7, 10 mg).
- Concentrations sustained >60 days; exposure lasts about 9-11 weeks (p8).
- Weekly dosing: steady state approached after week 6-7; 2 mg exceeded ~50 pg/mL by week 2; after stopping,
  mean concentrations near 50 pg/mL at about 6 weeks and near LLQ at about 10 weeks (p6, printed 70).
- Target concentration taken from exenatide IR 10 µg SC Cmax: mean 211 pg/mL [10th-90th percentile 100, 385]
  (cited from the Byetta label, ref 5; p5). Exenatide IR t½ 2.4 h, tmax 2.1 h (from ref 5; p2).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point FPG: 1514 time-matched FPG/concentration pairs from 105 patients (both trials). Inhibitory Emax
  model, EC50 56.8 pg/mL, Emax 57.0 mg/dL, baseline 176 mg/dL (Fig. 4, PDF p8, rendered). Direct (no effect
  compartment, no ke0 or hysteresis term reported).
- Minimally effective concentration for lowering FPG: ~50 pg/mL, quoted from Taylor 2005 (ref 16), not
  estimated in this paper (p5, p6, p8).
- Bodyweight EC50 184 pg/mL (CV 12.7%) in results (p7) but 179 pg/mL in Discussion (p8); poor precision. PPG
  excursion parameters could not be estimated precisely.
- Clinical results after 15 weeks (Table II, mean ± SE, rendered p6): FPG change -42.7 ± 15.7 (0.8 mg) and
  -39.0 ± 9.3 mg/dL (2 mg); HbA1c -1.4 ± 0.3 and -1.7 ± 0.3%; bodyweight 0.0 ± 0.7 and -3.8 ± 1.4 kg; placebo
  FPG +18.2 ± 11.6, HbA1c +0.4 ± 0.3.
- Not an opioid paper: no MEAC, ke0, tPeak or potency ratio.

## Covariates
None tested ("short duration ... small number of patients", p3).

## Caveats
- Sponsor-run (Amylin, Lilly, Alkermes employees among the authors). Small arms (10-16 per dose).
- Noncompartmental only; the release kinetics are described, not modelled.
- Weekly-dosing PK figure uses ITT n = 31 (active arms); ER single-dose figure n = 54 vs evaluable n = 58.
- Internal inconsistencies: bodyweight EC50 184 vs 179 pg/mL; residual SD "653 mg/dL" implausible; Ensure Plus
  printed as 0.0015 kcal/mL (p2), presumably 1.5 kcal/mL.
- Two assay laboratories (Amylin for single dose, Millipore for weekly dosing).
