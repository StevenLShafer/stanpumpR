# Schraag 1999: propofol-sufentanil interaction on loss of consciousness

## Study
Prospective, randomized, double-blind (anesthetist blind to targets). 30 unpremedicated ASA I-II women, elective gynecologic surgery; mean age 49 y (range 28-71); BMI limit 30; 58 data pairs analysed (pooled). Each patient got a propofol and a sufentanil target-controlled infusion (Graseby 3400, Diprifusor-style): propofol 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 or 6 ug/mL and sufentanil 0.1, 0.2, 0.3, 0.5 or 1.0 ng/mL (Greco-Latin square), using Marsh 1991 propofol and Hudson 1989 sufentanil PK sets. After 15 min equilibration LOC was tested (loud verbal command and eyelash reflex); responders had the propofol target raised and were retested after 15 min. Arterial samples at 10 and 15 min. Sufentanil by radioimmunoassay (Janssen kit), propofol by HPLC. No patient got either drug alone. Logistic regression, STATISTICA 4.5, maximum likelihood.

## Model and parameters
Logit model: ln[p/(1-p)] = b0 + b1*Cprop + b2*Csuf + b3*Cprop*Csuf, concentrations measured arterial plasma, steady state (propofol ug/mL, sufentanil ng/mL).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: LOC (no response to loud verbal command and no eyelash reflex), a binary response, on measured plasma (not effect-site) concentration at 10-15 min after the start, taken as steady state.
- Cp50 and Cp95 are the lines on Figure 1 where p=0.5 and 0.95 (the paper gives the lines, not tabulated values).
- Derived from the reduced model (not stated in the paper): Cp50 line 1.501 Cprop + 6.934 Csuf = 6.174, so propofol alone 4.11 ug/mL and sufentanil alone 0.89 ng/mL (both extrapolations outside the data, since no patient had either drug alone); Cp95 line = 9.118, propofol alone 6.07 ug/mL. Check: Hentgen 2002 quotes 44% LOC for propofol 3.5 with sufentanil 0.1 and 76% with 0.3; the reduced model gives 44% and 76%.
- Authors' reading: no supra-additive interaction; sufentanil shows no extra hypnotic effect compared with fentanyl, alfentanil, remifentanil. Opioids lower propofol Cp50 for LOC only 10-20% when at analgesic concentrations (discussion, PDF p. 5).
- Opioid audit: no MEAC / analgesic concentration reported (end point is LOC, not analgesia). No ke0 fitted; the discussion cites sufentanil blood-brain t1/2 ke0 6.2 min versus propofol 2.9 min (source not given in the text; reference list does not attribute it). No equianalgesic ratio reported.

## Covariates
None modelled.

## Caveats
No data at extreme combinations or either drug alone (logit of zero), so curvature could not be tested; the authors say logistic regression is of limited value for hypnotic-analgesic interaction. Only 58 data pairs, women only, plasma not effect-site concentrations. Measured-versus-target error: bias 19.9% (propofol) and 29.0% (sufentanil).
