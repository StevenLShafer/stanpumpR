# Vellinga 2025: remifentanil effect on remimazolam PK/PD

## Study
- Single-centre (UMCG Groningen), three-period crossover, dose-ranging trial; NCT04670471. 24 healthy volunteers, 13 male (54.2%); mean (min, max) age 43 (19, 70) y, weight 74 (51, 106) kg, height 175 (159, 192) cm, BMI 24.1 (20.2, 29.4) (PDF p. 5, Results).
- Eligibility 18-70 y, BMI 18-30, ASA I; stratified by age band (18-34, 35-49, 50-70) and sex. One-week washout between periods (PDF p. 2).
- Remimazolam by effect-site TCI (Zhou 2020 model, keo 0.135 /min, RUGLOOP II), step-up then step-down. Steps in periods 1-2: 150, 300, 400, 800, 1300, 2000 ng/mL up and 1300, 800, 400, 300, 150 down. Period 3 targets were changed after an interim analysis of six volunteers (PDF pp. 2-3, 5).
- Remifentanil by plasma TCI (Eleveld 2017 model): none in period 1, 0.5 ng/mL in period 2, and in period 3 first 2.0 or 4.0 ng/mL, then 0.1 or 1.0 ng/mL after the interim analysis. Remifentanil started 15 min before remimazolam. Final n per remifentanil target: 0.1 (8), 0.5 (24), 1 (10), 2 (4), 4 (2) (PDF p. 5).
- Each step lasted 22-50 min: time to PK steady state (2-30 min), 10 min equilibration, then about 10 min of sampling and assessments. Arterial plasma, EDTA, remimazolam and CNS7054 (metabolite) by UHPLC-MS (LLOQ 2 and 20 ng/mL; ULOQ 2000 and 20,000 ng/mL); remifentanil LLOQ 0.01, ULOQ 10 ng/mL.
- End points: MOAAS (5 to 0), BIS (median of the 60 s before MOAAS), tolerance to tetanic stimulation (TOTS, 100 Hz, 50 mA, up to 30 s, tried when MOAAS <= 4), tolerance to laryngoscopy (TOL, tried when MOAAS <= 1). 1,025 MOAAS, 891 BIS, 611 TOTS, 374 TOL observations.
- NONMEM 7.5 (FOCE-I for BIS, Laplacian for MOAAS, TOL, TOTS); proportional-odds logistic model for MOAAS, logistic for BIS (logit scale), logistic for TOL and TOTS. Significance P < 0.01 (OFV drop 6.64). Simulations in RxODE, 70 kg typical person, remimazolam TCI keo 0.135 /min, maximum 6 mg/min.
- TCI performance, median prediction error (PDF p. 5): remimazolam +5.6% (session 2) and +16% (session 3); remifentanil -8.1% and -11%.

## Model and parameters
- PK: the Vellinga 2024 remimazolam model was refitted (80% of remimazolam assumed converted to CNS7054). Remifentanil did not change remimazolam clearance (OFV change 0.0, P = 1.00) but reduced the apparent clearance of CNS7054 (OFV -315.4, P < 0.001). The remifentanil concentration that halves apparent CNS7054 clearance is 8.0 ng/mL (95% CI 5.5-13.4). The authors say this is about a 40% reduction at the highest studied remifentanil concentration, 4.0 ng/mL (PDF pp. 6-7; Discussion PDF p. 9). The full PK parameter table is only in the supplement.
- PD: Table 1 (PDF p. 10, printed 675) read from the rendered page. Remimazolam and CNS7054 concentrations are plasma, ng/mL.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- Effect-site model used to drive the TCI: keo 0.135 /min (taken from Zhou 2020; it was not estimated here). The paper reports no new ke0 or t1/2ke0 or tPeak for remimazolam or remifentanil. The effect-site targets were not themselves the model end point: PD was fitted to plasma concentrations at steady state.
- Acute tolerance: for MOAAS and BIS, remimazolam effect falls over time, attributed to competitive antagonism by the metabolite CNS7054. Removing it worsened the fit for MOAAS (OFV +328.4) and BIS (+325.3). It was not supported for TOTS (OFV -1.5) or TOL (-1.4). For 2000 ng/mL remimazolam alone, P(MOAAS 0) fell from 83.4% to 74.4% and 64.3% at 10, 60, 120 min, and BIS rose 40 to 45 to 50 (PDF pp. 7-8).
- Remifentanil interaction (all end points, P < 0.01): MOAAS OFV -171.6 (equation 2); BIS: additive model (equation 6, OFV -26.3) fitted better than the synergistic one (equation 2, OFV -8.9); TOTS OFV -134.2; TOL OFV -83.1.
- Remifentanil potency in the interaction terms (ng/mL, plasma, from Table 1): BIS 7.96, MOAAS 2.5, TOL 0.02, TOTS 0.04. These are interaction parameters on remimazolam's C50, not a MEAC. No analgesic concentration (MEAC), equianalgesic ratio, or remifentanil ke0 is reported.
- Table 2 (PDF p. 11, rendered; 10 min of TCI, no remifentanil): at 300 ng/mL, BIS 65, MOAAS 3 15.7%, MOAAS 2 26.6%, MOAAS 0 8.3%, TOL 0.6%, TOTS 2.4%. At 300 ng/mL with 4 ng/mL remifentanil: BIS 60, TOL 50.5%, TOTS 70.7%, MOAAS 0 100.0%. With 0.5 ng/mL remifentanil and 300 ng/mL: BIS 64, MOAAS 0 27.5%.
- Probability of MOAAS 2 or 3 was highest, at 45%, 45% and 44%, for remimazolam targets 275, 250, 200 ng/mL with remifentanil 0, 0.1, 0.5 ng/mL, with predicted BIS 67, 68, 71 (PDF p. 8, text). The highest TOTS and TOL probabilities were 93.3% and 85.5% at 2000 ng/mL remimazolam plus 4.0 ng/mL remifentanil; the authors say these targets were probably too low and give no firm recommendation for TOL or TOTS.
- Comparison with Zhou 2020 (the authors' words): Zhou found a synergistic BIS interaction, this paper an additive one. The authors say a direct comparison is inappropriate because Zhou used remifentanil infusion rate as the proxy for concentration (PDF p. 10).

## Covariates
- Remifentanil concentration (post hoc from the Eleveld 2017 model with measured samples) on CNS7054 apparent clearance and on remimazolam C50. No age, weight or sex covariates are reported in the PDF.

## Caveats
- Healthy volunteers, mostly low remifentanil: only 2 volunteers at 4 ng/mL and 4 at 2 ng/mL after the interim change; TOL and TOTS curves are not fully characterised (maximum probabilities 85-93% at the top targets).
- Plasma concentration used for CNS7054 clearance effect; the authors hypothesise reduced renal blood flow or competition from remifentanil acid, not tested.
- The fitted remimazolam TCI used the Zhou model, so "target" concentrations are on that model's scale.
- Zhou's remifentanil (mg/kg) vs concentration difference noted above.
