# Jeleazcov 2016: TCI-PCA hydromorphone after cardiac surgery

## Study
- Secondary analysis of the hydromorphone PK study (Jeleazcov 2014, ref 12). 50 adults enrolled, 43 analysed for PD (615 NRS ratings): age 66 yr (40-81), weight 79 kg (58-104), height 173 cm, 31 M / 12 F (Table 1). Cardiac surgery with thoracotomy; ASA <=3, BMI <=30, no diabetes/renal/neuro disease, no opioids in prior 14 days.
- Intraoperative propofol TCI (Marsh) plus sufentanil TCI (Gepts) at 0.4 (group 1) or 0.8 (group 2) ng/mL plasma. After surgery, in ICU: hydromorphone TCI (Westerling 2012 PK model; targets 1-2 ng/mL) until extubation, then TCI-PCA for 6-8 h (targets 0.8-10 ng/mL; step 0.2 ng/mL at 0.8-1, 0.5 at 1-5, 0.25 at 5-10; 15 min lockout; automatic decrease after 30 min without request; physician confirmed each request), then PCA 0.2 mg bolus with 10 min lockout overnight. Hydromorphone hydrochloride 1 mg = 0.89 mg base; TCI-PCA syringe 40 ug/mL.
- Plasma hydromorphone (LC-MS/MS, arterial, LLOQ 78 pg/mL) and sufentanil (LLOQ 5 pg/mL), total plasma concentrations; 1,194 hydromorphone and 518 sufentanil samples. Pain: 11-point NRS at rest and on deep inspiration; PD modelled on NRS under deep inspiration. Software NONMEM (ordinal logistic), R, SPSS.

## Model and parameters
The hydromorphone PK model (three-compartment, all volumes and clearances proportional to body weight; CL and V1 decrease with age) is only summarised here (PDF p10); the parameter table is Supplement Table S1 (not read). Population prediction error: MDPE 5.0%, MDAPE 21.0% (individual Bayesian: 1.2% / 9.5%). Sufentanil decline after stopping modelled with a two-exponential function (MDPE 0.1%, MDAPE 5.3%).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- ke0,HM = 0.015 /min, fixed, "corresponding to a time to peak effect of 20 min" (p9, Table 4); end point NRS under deep inspiration (analgesia). Not estimated. No t1/2 ke0 stated.
- ke0,SUF = 0.11 /min, fixed from the literature (ref 29); no end point restated in this paper.
- Hydromorphone EC50 (effect site, population typical): NRS<=4: 4.1 ng/mL (95% CI of individual estimates 0.6-12.8); NRS<=1: 9.6 ng/mL (5.9-18.3). Check (derived): -(a0+a1)/b1 = 3.49/0.85 = 4.1 and -a0/b1 = 8.19/0.85 = 9.6, consistent. These are the concentrations at which the probability of the pain level is 50%, in the presence of the residual sufentanil of the model fit (modal ~0.1 ng/mL falling with time, Fig 6B).
- Sufentanil EC50: NRS<=4: 0.12 ng/mL (0.01-0.79); NRS<=1: 0.29 ng/mL (0.04-1.42) (derived check 3.49/28.4 = 0.123).
- Interaction: concomitant sufentanil 0.1 ng/mL lowers the median hydromorphone EC50 by 3.3 ng/mL (Fig 7, p12 text).
- Observed: median target during TCI-PCA 2.3 ng/mL (0.9-7.0); median individual predicted plasma 2.8 (group 1) and 1.6 (group 2) ng/mL; NRS on inspiration <5 in 83% of ratings; median NRS inspiration 3, rest 0-2. Assays measure total plasma concentration; predicted effect-site from model, not measured.
- Dose: 0.26 mg/h during TCI-PCA (0.07-0.93); PCA 0.19 mg/h. Clearance quoted as 1.7 L/min for the typical-dose steady-state check (0.1-0.2 mg/h -> 1-2 ng/mL, p3). No MEAC as such is stated; no equianalgesic ratio vs another opioid.
- Safety: respiratory insufficiency 3 patients (2 during TCI-PCA at hydromorphone target 1.5 ng/mL, predicted Ce 2.19 and 1.9 ng/mL, Table 3); nausea 30%, vomiting 9%.

## Covariates
PK only (weight, age; Supplement). PD: sufentanil concentration; no demographic covariate in the PD model. Large IIV on all PD parameters.

## Caveats
Cardiac surgery with residual sufentanil and propofol; mixed target groups; ke0 values fixed not fitted; effect-site and Ce from model; NRS collapsed into three levels because adjacent levels overlapped; PCA phase unstandardised; NRS has interval-scale limits (authors). Single-blind, n=43.
