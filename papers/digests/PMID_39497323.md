# Melander 2025: cetirizine PopPK in breast milk

## Study
Extension of the Norwegian human lactation study (ConcePTION). 35 breastfeeding mothers (mean age 30 y, range 22-40; mean weight 78 kg, range 53-110), infants mean 8.2 months (8.3 weeks-21 months), 8.3 kg. 32 took cetirizine 10 mg/day, two took 20 mg/day, one took levocetirizine 5 mg/day; all on drug at least 2 days (steady state). Mothers self-collected 20 mL milk (full pump emptying) pre-dose and at 2, 4, 8, 12, 24 h after a morning dose; 4-6 samples each, 205 samples total. Mean pumping time 14.9 min (2-50). No maternal plasma sampled. LC-MS/MS on milk: LLOQ 0.39 ug/L, detection limit 0.04 ug/L; 17 detectable-but-below-LLOQ samples (3 women) kept in the fit. NONMEM 7.5, FOCE-I, PsN 5.3.1 SCM.

## Model and parameters
Milk-only one-compartment model, first-order input from dose, first-order elimination (all parameters apparent, empirical description of milk concentration, not systemic PK). IIV on clearance only; combined error with the additive part applied only to BLQ data. Table 1 (PDF p.4, image-confirmed):

Derived: ke = CL/V = 25.2/19.9 = 1.27 per h (derived: from Table 1; half-life ~0.55 h, far shorter than the apparent 24 h decline, indicating the ka of 0.095/h is the rate-limiting (flip-flop) step). Model-predicted mean AUC0-24 in milk 408 +/- 129 ug*h/L (compared with 506 in Wilkerson, non-steady-state).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None: no PD, no ke0, no effect-site, no therapeutic or toxic concentration. Exposure end point is relative infant dose (RID), using 150 mL/kg/day milk intake (FDA guidance) divided into the mother's mg/kg dose. Table 2 (PDF p.6): RID 1.99% (SD 0.70, range 1.02-3.53) at average concentration; 3.36% (0.77; 1.65-5.21) at Cmax; 2.02% (0.41; 1.34-2.93) for a feed at 12 h (75% of the feed at that time point, 25% at average concentration). WHO threshold quoted: <10% compatible with breastfeeding.

## Covariates
Pumping duration is the only significant covariate, on Vm: VTpump = (1 + VTpump1) x (TPUMP - 0.22), Vm = VTpump x TVV, TPUMP in hours (PDF p.4). Longer pumping gives a larger apparent Vm (lower concentration; authors suggest fattier hindmilk for a water-soluble drug). Maternal weight, BMI, age, exclusive breastfeeding, infant age were not significant.

## Caveats
Milk only: no plasma, so no milk:plasma ratio and no systemic parameters. Self-collected and home-frozen samples. Milk composition not measured. Mixed cetirizine/levocetirizine and 20 mg doses in a few women. The printed units for the pumping-duration parameter (L) and the "VTpump1" equation do not fit together cleanly (a dimensionless factor times TVV would give L only if TVV were already in L); the text does not resolve this.
