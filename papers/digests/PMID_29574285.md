# Leary 2018: time course of reversal of valproate inhibition of lamotrigine

## Study
- Open, single-sequence study in healthy volunteers (PDF p. 2, sections 2.1-2.5). 10 enrolled and completed
  (7 women, 3 men; age 29 (SD 10) y, weight 77 (22) kg); **9 analysed** (one excluded for a protocol violation).
  Age 18-55, non-smokers, no oral contraceptives.
- Lamotrigine (Lamictal) 10 mg orally every morning for 28 days; valproate (Depakote ER) 500 mg every morning for
  14 days, taken together; valproate stopped **after the morning dose on day 14** (Methods).
- Morning trough serum samples on days 14, 15, 16, 18, 20, 22, 24, 26, 28. Baseline = day 14.
- Assays: lamotrigine by HPLC (the text also mentions a GC method), LOD 0.15 ug/mL, between-run CV 4.9%; valproate by
  immunofluorometric assay, CV 4.3% at 0.7 ug/mL. Serum, total concentrations.
- Statistics: ANOVA with Scheffe post hoc; mean (SD). Powered for a 40% difference in lamotrigine oral clearance.
- Internal inconsistencies: the abstract says lamotrigine for 30 days, sampling to day 30, and valproate stopped after
  the day-15 sample; the Methods say 28 days and the day-14 dose; the Results say day 16 is "two days following the
  final dose". The valproate levels (39.7 on day 15, 10.1 on day 16) fit a last dose on day 14.

- Lamotrigine significantly lower than baseline on days 20-28 (p < 0.05); days 24-28 within 10% of one another
  (new steady state). Decline about 52% (day 28 against day 15, Fig. 1 legend).
- Derived: day 28/day 14 = 0.516 (a 48% fall against the defined baseline); day 28/day 15 = 0.48. Read as a steady-
  state trough ratio at constant dose, lamotrigine CL/F with valproate (trough about 40-44 ug/mL on 500 mg/day ER)
  was about 0.48-0.52 of its value without (derived).
- Apparent offset half-life of the excess over the day-28 level: about 65 h from day 18 to 20 and about 47 h from
  day 18 to 24 (derived, from group means; includes the valproate tail on days 16-18).
- Authors: reversal complete 10-14 days after valproate stops; plateau 7-9 days after valproate disappears.
- Background cited (Introduction/Discussion, from refs 2 and 3, not measured here): valproate 5.6 ug/mL associated
  with 50% of maximal inhibition; near-maximal at troughs of about 20 ug/mL; lamotrigine t1/2 46.5 h (28-64) at
  valproate 500 mg/day.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None: no seizure or clinical end point; the end point is the lamotrigine serum trough. Not an opioid.

## Covariates
None analysed. UGT polymorphisms not screened (authors note this).

## Caveats
- n = 9 analysed, healthy volunteers, a single very low lamotrigine dose (10 mg/day), one valproate dose; group
  means only.
- Abstract and Methods disagree on the durations and the day valproate stopped (see Study).
- "Baseline" is day 14, but the 52% headline uses day 15.
- The offset includes the elimination of residual valproate (10.1 ug/mL on day 16, above the cited 5.6 ug/mL
  half-maximal concentration), so it is not a pure lamotrigine half-life.
- The mechanism section (UGT1A4 vs UGT2B7) is from in vitro literature.

## Verification against stanpumpR
- **antiseizureInteractions.csv:39, "fully reversed 10-14 days later"**: matches the abstract and Conclusion.
  **Matches.**
- **"troughs fall from day 5"**: not in the paper. Fig. 1: unchanged on day 16, 8% lower on day 18 (4 days after
  the last dose, not significant), first significant on day 20 (6 days after). The Discussion says decline begins
  within 3 days. **Discrepancy** (wording; open question).
- **"10 healthy adults"**: 10 completed but 9 were analysed. Minor.
- **evidence_read "abstract; full text not in PMC"**: the full text (open access, CC BY-NC-ND) is in the archive and
  was read for this card. Stale label.
- **Magnitude, against the applied valproate -> lamotrigine row (csv:5)**: fold 0.49 at Cref 75 ug/mL with EC50
  5.6 gives Fmax 0.452 and, at Leary's valproate trough 43.5 ug/mL, a fold of 0.514 (derived). Leary's day-28/day-14
  trough ratio is 0.516 (derived). **Matches** closely, an independent support of the applied row in healthy
  volunteers.
- **"Checks the immediate onset" / offset as lamotrigine's own approach to steady state**: the study measures only the
  offset. The library's lamotrigine (R/drugs_lamotrigine.R:15, CL/F 2.32 L/h, V/F 77.6 L; t1/2 about 23 h, derived)
  under the immediate inhibition row would plateau about 4-5 days after valproate is gone (around day 20-22); Leary's
  troughs keep falling to day 24-26 (apparent half-life 40-65 h, derived). Direction: the model de-inhibits faster
  than Leary observed. Not settled here (open question).
