# Bellon 2020: fibrinogen concentrate in afibrinogenaemia

## Study
- Three LFB studies of Clottafact (FibCLOT), 2004-2015, pooled (Table 1): study 1, 5 adults; study 2, 14 patients 40 kg or more; study 3, 12 children 12 years or under. 31 patients, 18 men and 13 women, age 1.5-48.7 years, weight 10.8-93.5 kg, BMI 12.8-37.0. All given a single 0.06 g/kg infused over about 1 h.
- Clauss fibrinogen activity (STA-R); 8 post-infusion samples per patient in studies 1-2, 3 in study 3, up to 14 days (5 days in children). Antigen by nephelometry, concordant (R2 0.975). LLOQ 0.09-0.3 g/L; 16 BLQ imputed as half the LLOQ.
- NONMEM 7.3, FOCE-I; allometric scaling a priori, exponents estimated; age and sex tested.

## Model and parameters
One compartment (two tested). Table 2 and equations 4-5, weight not centred:

    CL = 0.00288 x WT^0.556  L/h
    V  = 0.0960  x WT^0.808  L

## Checks
- Tables 3-4 (individual Bayes estimates): 40 kg and over CL 0.45 mL/h/kg, Vss 43.2 mL/kg, half-life 66.7 h, recovery 23.1 g/L per g/kg (2.31 mg/dL per mg/kg); under 40 kg 0.74, 52.2, 49.0 h, 18.5. The equations at 70 and 20 kg: 0.44 and 0.76 mL/h/kg, 67 and 49 h, 2.35 and 1.85.
- RiaSTAP label (12.3): CL 0.59 +/- 0.13 mL/h/kg, Vss 52.7 mL/kg, half-life 78.7 h, recovery 1.7 mg/dL per mg/kg; under 16 years 0.73 mL/h/kg and 69.9 h.
