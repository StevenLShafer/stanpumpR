# Bellon 2020: population PK of fibrinogen concentrate (CLOTTAFACT/FibCLOT) in afibrinogenaemia

## Study
- Pooled 3 open-label studies (2004-2015) of LFB's plasma-derived fibrinogen concentrate, single IV dose 0.06 g/kg, max rate 4 mL/min (Table 1, p.331 printed).
- n = 31 afibrinogenaemic patients, age 1.5-48 y (Table 1: study 3 children, median 7.1 y, range 1.5-12.0), weight 10.8-93.5 kg; study 1 n=5 (adults), study 2 n=14 (>=40 kg), study 3 n=12 (all <40 kg, <=12 y). 18 male/13 female derived from the per-study sex counts (derived: 3+8+7 male).
- Sampling: adults pre, 1, 3, 6, 24 h, 3, 6, 10, 14 d; children only pre, 1 h, 3 d, 5 d (3 post-infusion samples). 177 post-infusion concentrations; 16 BLQ imputed as half LLOQ (first BLQ only).
- Assay: Clauss fibrinogen activity (plasma; LLOQ 0.09 g/L first lab, 0.12 or 0.3 g/L second lab) and nephelometric antigen. NONMEM 7.3.

## Model and parameters
One-compartment, IV infusion, first-order elimination, allometric scaling on weight with estimated (not fixed) exponents, uncentred (WT in kg). Exponential IIV on CL and V; one proportional residual error. Age and sex not significant (Table S1, not in PDF). Table 2, printed p.333 (rendered page confirmed):
- TVCL (L/h) = 0.00288 x WT^0.556 (Eq 4)
- TVV (L) = 0.0960 x WT^0.808 (Eq 5)
- Check (derived): 20 kg gives CL 0.0152 L/h (0.76 mL/h/kg) and V 1.08 L (54 mL/kg); 70 kg gives CL 0.0305 L/h, V 2.97 L.

Text states weight-standardised CL is 64% higher in <40 kg (0.74/0.45 = 1.64, derived) and AUCinf 81.3 vs 133.4 g.h/L. Antigen data give similar values (Table 4).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD. No effect-site model, no ke0, no MEAC. Normal plasma fibrinogen cited as 2.0-4.0 g/L (p.330). Incremental in vivo recovery (IR = Cmax / dose, g/L per g/kg) is the dose-planning quantity; dose (g) = (target - baseline g/L) x (1/IR) x kg. Reciprocals quoted: 0.043 (>=40 kg), 0.053 (<40 kg).

## Covariates
Body weight (allometric, estimated exponents 0.556 CL and 0.808 V). Sex and age not significant; age and weight are highly correlated in the growing children, so age could not be assessed independently.

## Caveats
- Afibrinogenaemic patients only (no endogenous fibrinogen), no hepatic or renal impairment, no pregnancy; authors say it does not necessarily extend to very young patients.
- Weight exponent for V (0.808) is not 1, so a per-kg dose gives a lower concentration rise in small patients; CL exponent 0.556 is well below 0.75.
- Children have 3 samples each; two assay laboratories with different LLOQs; larger residuals near LLOQ. IIV/residual precision not estimated.
- The comparison "higher CL, shorter t1/2, lower IR" is for <40 kg against the >=40 kg patients in the same study.
- Funded by the manufacturer, whose employees are authors.
