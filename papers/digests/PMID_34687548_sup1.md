# Franzese 2022 supplement: the final ceftazidime model (adults and children)

## Study
- Pooled population analysis: adult data from 18 phase I-III ceftazidime-avibactam studies plus 154 children 3 months to 17 years (one phase I single-dose study, NCT01893346; phase II cIAI, NCT02475733; phase II cUTI, NCT02497781). Ceftazidime: 9,628 observations, 2,130 subjects (main text).
- Children (Table 1 of the paper): age 0.25-17.7 years (median 7.6), weight 4.1-80 kg (median 25), NCrCL 43-489 mL/min/1.73 m2 (median 104). Adults 18-89 years, about 60% women, CrCL 8-488 mL/min (renal-dose paper).
- NONMEM, FOCE with interaction; ADVAN3 TRANS4.

## Model and parameters
Two compartments, first-order elimination. The control stream ("Ceftazidime final model: NONMEM code") and Table S2:

    CL = 7.75 x CLWT x CLPMA x CLCRCL x CLPOP x CLRCE      L/h
    CLWT = (WT/70) x (WT50 + 70)/(WT50 + WT)               (Supplementary Methods; gamma 1, intercept 0)
    CLPMA = PMA^3.4/(47.7^3.4 + PMA^3.4) at age <= 2 years, else 1
    CLCRCL (age > 2) = 0.0103036 x NCrCL (NCrCL < 100), 1.03036 + 0.00125182 x (NCrCL - 100) (>= 100); 1 at age <= 2
    NCrCL held at 150 mL/min/1.73 m2
    V1 = 11.2 x (WT/70) x V1POP x V1POP5 x V1RCE           L
    Q  = 5.33 x (WT/70)^0.67                                L/h
    V2 = 6.52 x (WT/70)                                     L

POP: 2 cUTI, 3 cIAI, 4 HAP/VAP; POP5 presence of ventilator. The stream's $THETA for CL is 9.1259, an initial value; Table S2's final estimate is 7.75. The other initial values round to Table S2's.

## Covariates
- NCrCL: bedside Schwartz in children over 2 years; Cockcroft-Gault adjusted to 1.73 m2 in adults (paper, Methods); formula for BSA not named.
- Under 2 years, renal maturation by postmenstrual age (postnatal age + 40 weeks where unknown).
- Weight on all four parameters; indication and ventilation; race.

## Caveats
- The paper's Table 2 had the ceftazidime and avibactam columns transposed; corrected in Clin Pharmacol Ther 2024;115:373.
- The renal-dose paper says the model's non-renal ceftazidime clearance is close to zero, and used 0.408 L/h (Leroy 1984) for anuric patients in a sensitivity analysis only.
- No patients under 2 years in the cIAI arm; few children with NCrCL < 50.
