# Overgaard 2021: population PK of oral semaglutide (clinical pharmacology trials)

Page convention: printed pp. 1335-1348 = PDF pp. 1-14 (PDF page = printed page - 1334).

## Study
Six Novo Nordisk clinical pharmacology trials of oral semaglutide in which every dose was supervised on site
(section 2.1): dosing conditions (fasting 15/30/60/120 min, water 50/120 mL), scintigraphy (single 10 mg dose,
300 min fast, 50/240 mL), renal impairment, hepatic impairment, omeprazole DDI, food effect (30/240 min fast,
120/240 mL). Five trials: 10 consecutive daily doses, escalated from 5 to 10 mg after 5 days (10 mg throughout in
the dosing-conditions trial); tablets co-formulated with 300 mg SNAC. Sampling during days 1-9 (7-45 samples) and
after the last dose (16-38 samples); LC-MS, LLOQ 0.729 nmol/L. Table 1 (printed p. 1337, rendered), total: 376
subjects, 14,897 observations; 290 men, 86 women; 333 < 65 y, 43 >= 65 y; 46 with renal and 32 with hepatic
impairment; 360 without and 16 with T2D; weight 80.7 (12.7) kg, range 51.1-122.0; age 47.1 (13.1), 19-74 y; BMI
26.2 (3.7). A separate re-estimation used a trial in 55 subjects with T2D with or without upper GI disease
(3 mg then 7 mg daily for 5 days each; not all dosing supervised): weight 89.3 (17.1) kg, 54.5-131.3; age 60.7.
NONMEM 7.3, PsN 4.60; backward elimination, delta OFV > 6.63.

Supplement (supplement, 2026-10-27): the ESM names the six trials by NCT number (dosing conditions NCT01572753,
scintigraphy NCT01619345, renal NCT02014259, hepatic NCT02016911, omeprazole DDI NCT02249871, food effect
NCT02172313) and the T2D/upper-GI trial as Meier 2019 (ADA abstract 1013-P). It gives no dataset sizes,
covariate ranges or covariate-selection tables of its own.

## Model and parameters
The two-compartment s.c./i.v. disposition model of Overgaard 2019 (including its fixed effects and random-effect
variances) was a fixed component (section 2.2); oral absorption is first order with bioavailability F relative to
i.v. Equations (printed p. 1338, rendered):
- Eq. 1: ka = [(ka,ref - theta_ka.long.fast) x exp(-theta_T.fast,ka x (Tfasting - 30)) + theta_ka.long.fast] x
  exp(eta_ka); theta_ka.long.fast is the ka for fasting of 120 min or longer.
- Eqs. 2-5: CL = CLref x E_weight,CL x E_T2D x exp(eta_CL); Q = Qref x E_weight,CL x exp(eta_CL) (CL's eta);
  Vc = Vc,ref x E_weight,V x exp(eta_Vc); Vp = Vp,ref x E_weight,V x exp(eta_Vc) (Vc's eta).
- Eq. 6: F_ij = Fref x E_weight,F x E_water,F x E_fasting,F x exp(eta_F.i + eta_F.j) (subject and occasion random
  effects).
- Eq. 7: E_weight,CL = (WT/85)^theta_weight,CL; Eq. 8: E_weight,V = (WT/85)^theta_weight,V; Eq. 9: E_T2D =
  theta_T2D,CL^T2D; Eq. 10: E_weight,F = (WT/85)^theta_weight,F; Eq. 11: E_water,F = theta_240mL^(240 mL indicator).
- Eq. 12: E_fasting,F = (1 - exp(-theta_T.fasting,F x Tfasting)) / (1 - exp(-theta_T.fasting,F x 30)), Tfasting in min.

ESM Section 1 (supplement, 2026-10-27; three OMML equations) gives the re-estimation model for the T2D/upper-GI
trial: F = theta_T2D,F^T2D x Fref, F = theta_GI disease,F^(GI disease) x Fref, and an off-site dosing factor
F = theta_Off-site^(Off-site) x Fref (indicators 0/1; Fref is F without T2D or GI disease, and for on-site dosing).
No numerical values for these thetas are printed in the ESM; the main text's 0.841 is theta_T2D,F.

ESM Online Resource Table 1 (supplement, 2026-10-27; parsed from document.xml), "Parameter estimates from the final
PK model" (the 376-subject model; estimate [95% CI], IIV CV%, shrinkage %, IOV CV%):

and 145.7 h with a T2D CL factor of 1.09 (141.9 h with 1.12), so Table 2's 145 h is consistent with 1.09. With the
ESM fasting constant 0.0266/min, Eq. 12 gives F x 0.598 at 15 min, x 1.450 at 60 min, x 1.744 at 120 min (1.39%,
matching the text's plateau of about 1.4%) and x 1.819 as Tfasting -> infinity. With the ESM ka constants, Eq. 1
gives ka 5.32/h at 15 min, 2.09/h at 30, 1.376/h at 60 and 1.350/h at 120 min. F at the recommended conditions

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None in this paper (concentration only). The authors note the most recent dose contributes about 10% of
steady-state exposure, and that missed or single deviant doses change exposure only transiently (Fig. 5).

## Covariates
Weight on CL, Q, Vc, Vp (fixed from 2019) and on F (estimated, ESM 0.741); T2D on CL (fixed, ESM 1.09); post-dose
fasting time on ka (ESM 0.112/min, toward 1.35/h) and F (ESM 0.0266/min); 240 mL water on F (ESM 0.637). F
independent of water from 50 to 120 mL. No significant T2D effect on F (0.841, CI includes 1); the re-estimation
model also carried upper GI disease and off-site dosing factors on F (ESM Section 1, values not printed).
Clinical trials covered 40-188 kg (Discussion), but this dataset is 51.1-122 kg.

## Caveats
- Supervised dosing in clinical pharmacology units; the authors say the estimates may differ from a real-world T2D
  setting and that phase III data are a better indication of exposure (Discussion, printed p. 1346).
- Disposition fixed from a model whose i.v. data are from one trial. The ESM shows 60.7-61.7% shrinkage on the
  disposition etas, so the oral data carry little individual information on them.
- Mostly healthy men (290/376); the T2D re-estimation used a different trial, 7 mg as final dose.
- Table 1's "s.c./i.v. two compartment model total" column prints weight 83.0 (14.7), 54.1-121.2 kg, which are the
  2019 paper's one-compartment-model figures; the 2019 two-compartment total was 81.9 (15.1), 51.9-121.2 kg
  (Overgaard 2019 Table 3). Source inconsistency; immaterial here.
- The T2D factor on CL is "1.09 fixed" in the ESM, while Overgaard 2019 Table 4 prints 1.12 (95% CI 1.09-1.16).
  The evidence: the ESM value, and Table 2's T2D half-life (145 h) agreeing with 1.09 (145.7 h) rather than 1.12
  (141.9 h). The paper does not say why the two differ.
- ESM Table 1's residual-error CI does not bracket its estimate (see above). Units "-" for three parameters with
  dimensions.
