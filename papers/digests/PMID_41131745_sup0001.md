# Towards Goal-Directed Perfusion: consolidated supplement (Parts I & II)

## Study
Supplement to a two-part paper on goal-directed perfusion (GDP) during paediatric cardiopulmonary bypass (CPB). It is not a drug paper: no drug, dose or concentration appears.
- Retrospective data from Great Ormond Street Hospital, 2019-2021, all patients <18 y on CPB (PDF p2-3). 963 operations; 854 with known AKI status.
- Minute-by-minute data: cardiac index (CI), Hb, SaO2, SvO2, temperature (CDI550 sensors, calibrated every 20 min against ABL90 Flex Plus) (p3).
- Training set (AKI-free): 293 operations / 286 patients, 20,443 min, mean 71 min per operation (Table C.1, p5-6). Median age 15.0 months (IQR 4.3-57.7; range 0.1-217.4). Age groups: neonates 43, infants 57, toddlers 96, children 97 (per Table C.1; the text layer's column order is scrambled, so the counts and their group labels were not confirmed on the page). 45% female. Median temperature 33.2 C (IQR 31.3-34.7; range 15.9-36.8). Median OER 0.22 (IQR 0.16-0.27; range 0.01-0.66).
- The full 963-patient set (926 with age and weight, 60,260 min) was used only for comparison with Schofield BMR equations and for age-weight distributions (p4).
- R 4.4; bGARIX fitted by OLS, GARIX by backfitting with the `gam` package (p15).

## Model and parameters
Derived quantities (Section B, p3-4): BSA by Mosteller, sqrt(height cm x weight kg / 3600); CI = pump flow (L/min) / BSA; DO2i = 10 x CI x 1.34 x Hb x SaO2/100; VO2i = 10 x CI x 1.34 x Hb x (SaO2-SvO2)/100; OER = (SaO2-SvO2)/SaO2. Hb-bound O2 only (dissolved O2 about 1.6% of arterial content, ignored).

GARIX structure (Section D, p6-14): change in logit(OER) per minute = autoregressive term group (lags 1..N) + exogenous term group (lagged differences of log CI, log Hb, log SaO2, Temp, including the intended change at lag 0) + equilibrium term (coefficient x [log VO2i - log tVO2i]) + Gaussian noise. tVO2i is a latent oxygen demand.
- bGARIX: log tVO2i = g0 + g1 x Temp (van't Hoff, constant Q10 = exp(10 x g1)); no patient covariates (D.8-D.9).
- GARIX: log tVO2i = g0 + g1 log(age) + g2 log(weight) + g3 log(age) log(weight) + s(Temp), spline in temperature, so Q10 varies with temperature (D.10-D.12). Age and weight units are not stated in the text layer.
- Fitted coefficient values are not tabulated in this supplement (only Figure F.2 plots lagged coefficients by age group).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Not a drug paper; no ke0, MEAC, EC50 or potency ratio. Not an opioid paper, so the opioid audit does not apply. The physiological end point is OER (oxygen extraction ratio) and its steady state, where VO2i = tVO2i. The supplement warns that OER above 0.5-0.6 may be toxic and that training data lay below that (p14).

## Covariates
Age, weight and their log interaction in tVO2i (GARIX only), temperature via spline (GARIX) or linear (bGARIX). The age x weight interaction was reported as statistically significant (p16, no statistic given); at 35 C weight raises predicted tVO2i in neonates and infants but is flat or negative in older children (Figure F.1, p16-17).

## Caveats
Retrospective single-centre data; AKI patients excluded from training; homoscedastic Gaussian noise assumed, and the residual ACF of absolute values suggests heteroscedasticity (Section E, p15-16); extrapolation to high OER not supported.
