# Giraud 2006: stiripentol inhibits clobazam metabolism (CYP3A4, CYP2C19)

## Study
- **In vivo** (re-analysis of the Chiron 2000 randomized placebo-controlled add-on trial, details in that paper):
  children with severe myoclonic epilepsy in infancy (SMEI/Dravet) on clobazam 0.5 mg/kg/day and valproate
  30 mg/kg/day; stiripentol 50 mg/kg/day or placebo added for a 2-month double-blind period. Steady-state
  minimum (trough) plasma concentrations of clobazam (CLB) and N-desmethylclobazam (NCLB) in week 3 of baseline
  and week 7 of the double-blind period. 20 children in the stiripentol group (p 611). Number on placebo, ages
  and weights not given here.
- **In vitro**: cDNA-expressed CYP3A4 and CYP2C19 (BD Gentest, baculovirus/insect cells, 50 nM P450). CLB
  2-100 µM and NCLB 1.5-14 µM with stiripentol 0-5 µM (Ki); IC50 at 2 µM CLB / 14 µM NCLB with stiripentol
  0.001-10 µM; comparators ketoconazole (CYP3A4) and omeprazole (CYP2C19). Two repetitions in duplicate. HPLC
  assay (Giraud 2004). Ki by SigmaPlot nonlinear regression with AIC model choice; IC50 by GraphPad Prism 3.0
  (p 609).

## Model and parameters
No pharmacokinetic model. Inhibition constants, Table 1 (PDF p 2, printed p 609), mean ± SD or (95% CI):

| Reaction | Enzyme | Stiripentol apparent Ki (µM) | Stiripentol IC50 (µM) | Comparator IC50 (µM) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CLB → NCLB (N-demethylation) | CYP3A4 | 1.59 ± 0.07 (noncompetitive) | 1.58 (1.20-2.08) | ketoconazole 0.023 (0.016-0.033) |
| CLB → NCLB | CYP2C19 | 0.516 ± 0.065 (competitive) | 3.29 (1.87-5.79) | — |
| NCLB → OH-NCLB (4'-hydroxylation) | CYP2C19 | 0.139 ± 0.025 (competitive) | 0.276 (0.206-0.371) | omeprazole 2.99 (2.11-4.24) |

In vivo (Results, p 609; means, normalized trough = (mg/L)/(mg/kg)):
- Stiripentol dose 49 ± 2 mg/kg/day; trough 10.0 ± 3.6 mg/L (42.7 ± 15.4 µM). (derived check: 10.0 mg/L ÷
  MW 234.3 = 42.7 µM, consistent.)
- CLB normalized trough 0.39 → 0.84; NCLB 3.6 → 11.6 (both p < 0.001); OH-NCLB 0.258 → 0.063 (p < 0.001).
- NCLB/CLB ratio +269% (p < 0.01); OH-NCLB/NCLB −86% (p < 0.001). OH-CLB not detected. No significant change on
  placebo. The introduction gives the summary as OH-NCLB −83% and CLB, NCLB +173% on average (p 608).
- Usual stiripentol steady-state concentrations 10-60 µM, citing earlier studies (p 609).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Not an opioid paper; no MEAC, ke0 or potency ratio. Clinical end point (from Chiron 2000, restated): responders
(≥50% fall in clonic/tonic-clonic seizure rate) 71.4% (95% CI 52.1-90.7) on stiripentol vs 5% (0-14.6) on
placebo, p < 0.0001. Three of the 20 stiripentol children had no NCLB rise; authors speculate CYP2C19
poor-metabolizer genotype (not tested).

## Covariates
None modelled. CYP2C19*2/*3 genotype discussed as a likely modifier (no data).

## Caveats
- In vitro Ki from recombinant enzymes; Ki "apparent". In vivo values are troughs only, normalized to dose, and
  the in vivo data are a secondary report of Chiron 2000.
- The "Ki ratio approximately 10" (p 609) compares NCLB hydroxylation (0.14) with CYP3A4 CLB demethylation (1.6).
- Ketoconazole IC50 "almost 70 times lower" than stiripentol for CYP3A4; omeprazole IC50 "approximately 10
  times higher" than stiripentol for CYP2C19 (comparators: the same assay, Table 1).
- Funded by Biocodex (stiripentol manufacturer).
