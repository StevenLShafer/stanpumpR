# Hirai 2022: oral digoxin troughs in Japanese patients with AF and HF

PDF page = printed "Page n of 9".

## Study
Single-centre retrospective cohort (Tokyo Women's Medical University), 2008-2016: 391 adults with AF and HF
(ACC/AHA stage C/D) on oral digoxin. Table 1 (rendered): 148 (38%) women; age 67 ± 14 y; height 161 ± 11 cm;
weight 57 ± 15 kg; LVEF 39 ± 14%; SCr 0.94 [0.75-1.15] mg/dL; Cockcroft-Gault CLcr 56.5 [40.7-75.6] mL/min; dose
0.125 mg in 287 (73%), 0.25 mg in 51, 0.0625 mg in 39; amiodarone 63 (16%), diltiazem 33, verapamil 23. 3465 trough
concentrations (at least 6 h post dose, all treated as steady state), median 5 per patient. Two immunoassays (COBAS
to Nov 2016, then Nanopia). Phoenix NLME 8.1; exponential IIV on CL/F only; multiplicative residual error; CLcr
capped at 120 mL/min; stepwise forward selection.

## Model and parameters
One compartment, first-order absorption; ka FIXED 1.0 /h and Vd/F FIXED 6.0 L/kg (from Aronson 1980) because only
troughs were available. Final (Table 3, PDF p5 rendered): CL/F = 6.2 x (CLcr/60)^0.41 x (1 - 0.24 x amiodarone).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Outcome tabulation only (Table 2): digoxin intoxication 3, 1, 5, 8 events in SDC bands < 0.6, 0.6-0.89, 0.9-1.19,
>= 1.2 ng/mL; gastrointestinal symptoms only at >= 0.9. Targets <= 0.9 ng/mL (guideline), 1.2 ng/mL cut-off.

## Covariates
CLcr (power, normalised to 60), amiodarone (binary). Diltiazem and BMI screened, not retained.

## Caveats
Troughs only, V and ka fixed (so no distribution information); the bootstrap CIs in Table 3 are implausibly narrow
(e.g. CL/F 6.207-6.223); the abstract and results print "(CLCR 30 mL/min)" without the "<" the conclusions give;
single centre; assay changed mid-study.
