# Syroid 2002: graphical anesthesia drug display (EC95-normalised effect-site display)

## Study
- Development: interdisciplinary team (bioengineering, architecture, anesthesiology, computer engineering, psychology) built a real-time display of effect-site concentrations normalised to each drug's EC95, with 30 min of history and 10 min of prediction (PDF p.2). Drug effect shown as 0-100 %: effect-site concentration 1.0 x EC95 shows 95 % effect (PDF p.2). Multiple drugs of one class: the combined effect uses the sum of effect-site concentrations (no synergism modelled) (PDF p.2-3).
- Evaluation: 15 anesthesiologists (7 attendings, 8 residents; age 36±4.1 yr) in a crossover (with/without display) on the Anesoft PC simulator; two patients: A (18-yr woman, 52 kg) and B (22-yr man, 74 kg, 180 cm); bolus-only propofol, remifentanil, rocuronium; maintenance phase 15 min 24 s (PDF p.4).
- Concentrations computed with the Shafer and Gregg effect-site algorithms (ref 8 of the paper).
- Results: effect-site concentration kept closer to EC95 with the display (RMSE, Table 4); remifentanil boluses 3.1±1.3 without vs 4.7±1.8 with display (P<0.01); propofol boluses 3.1±2.1 vs 2.7±1.3; heart rate/blood pressure not different; NASA-TLX mental demand, effort and frustration lower; usefulness score 8.7±1.1 of 10 (PDF p.6-7).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Remifentanil audit:
- MEAC / analgesic concentration: none. No numeric EC95 is given for any drug; "EC95" was set by calibration to the simulator: remifentanil EC95 scaled to the dose that made the simulated patient's pupils pinpoint; propofol EC95 so the simulated patient became apneic at that effect-site concentration (1.0 mg/kg propofol bolus) (PDF p.4). These are simulator-defined, not human data.
- ke0: remifentanil 0.441 / 0.469, propofol 0.456, rocuronium 0.168 (Table 3, above), applied from the cited PK/PD papers, not measured; end point of those ke0 not stated here; no tPeak or t1/2ke0 given.
- Potency ratio: none.
- Rocuronium: ED95 stated as 0.6 "ug/kg" (see open question); one ED95 dose abolished train-of-four for over 30 min in the simulator.

## Covariates
Patient weight, height, age, gender entered into the drug delivery program; the PK models adjust to them (PDF p.3-5). No equations given.

## Caveats
- Simulator study; healthy young simulated patients; bolus-only; short cases; no recovery analysis; models are population-based (authors, PDF p.7-8).
- Single EC95 per drug is called oversimplified; no synergism in the prototype.
- Authors disclose a pending patent and possible royalties.

## Verification against stanpumpR
No code location cites this paper; verdict not-applicable. Cross-checks:
- Propofol ke0 0.456 in Table 3 equals the 0.456 /min the library's comment mentions for the STANPUMP propofol file (R/alternativeModels.R:201); the paper's propofol Vc of 0.228 L/kg is the Gepts 1987 scaling (derived), not the Schnider or Eleveld model that stanpumpR uses.
- Remifentanil ke0 0.441/0.469 differs from the library's Minto companion function (R/alternativeModels.R:283; see open question). stanpumpR's default remifentanil uses tPeak 1.6 min (R/drugs_remifentanil.R:116).
- The paper's display idea (effect-site concentration relative to EC95) is not implemented in the cited code and no value from this paper is used.
