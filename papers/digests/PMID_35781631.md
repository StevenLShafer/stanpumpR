# Fokkink 2022: IVIG in Guillain-Barre syndrome

## Study
- Model building: 177 patients with GBS from Erasmus MC cohorts, age 53 (5-89) years, weight 72 (18-122) kg, 57% men (Table 1), all given Gammagard S/D 0.4 g/kg/day for 5 days; plus 15 patients with CIDP (Kiovig/Gammagard liquid, 3 Privigen). 589 + 222 serum IgG levels, 3 (2-7) per GBS patient. External validation: 177 patients with GBS from SID-GBS (Nanogam, Privigen, Kiovig), 689 levels.
- Total IgG by the hospital assay; the endogenous IgG as a baseline concentration CBAS (TVCBAS = theta_CBAS x theta_T^TYPE, TYPE 1 for GBS). NONMEM, FOCE-I.

## Model and parameters
Two compartments, first-order elimination, allometric scaling with fixed exponents (0.75 on CL and Q, 1 on V1 and V2). Covariates kept: methylprednisolone and the GBS disability score on CL. Results 3.1.2 and Table 2:

    CL = 0.28 L/day x (WT/70)^0.75 x 1.31 (MP) x 1.22 (GBS-DS 3-4) or 1.83 (GBS-DS 5)
    V1 = 2.87 L;  Q = 0.25 L/day;  V2 = 2.65 L  (scaled to 70 kg)
    CBAS = 15.90 g/L x 0.65 (GBS) = 10.3 g/L

## Checks
- At 70 kg the half-lives are 2.9 and 18.1 days. GAMMAGARD S/D label (12.3): radio-labelled IgG 21-25 days in healthy people, 17.7-37.6 in immunodeficiency; GAMMAGARD S/D 37.7 +/- 15 days in 10 treated patients with primary immunodeficiency.
- Results 3.1.1: base-model CBAS 10.3 g/L in GBS; the final model's 15.90 x 0.65 = 10.335.
- Simulation (Results, Outcomes): 0.4 g/kg/day for 5 days in a 75 kg patient with GBS-DS 4 peaks after the fifth dose and returns to baseline after about 4 weeks.
- Target: a rise of at least 7.3 g/L at 2 weeks (Kuitwaard et al., reference 12, not read).
