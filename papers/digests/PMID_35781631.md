# Fokkink 2022: IVIg population PK in Guillain-Barre syndrome

## Study
- Population PK of intravenous immunoglobulin (IVIg), measured as **total serum IgG** (endogenous plus infused; the two cannot be told apart), turbidimetry, in adults and children with Guillain-Barre syndrome (GBS). Page 3 (printed 1287).
- Model-building cohort: 177 GBS patients, 589 serum IgG values (Dutch GBS trials: IVIg vs plasma exchange, and IVIg +/- methylprednisolone), plus 15 stable CIDP patients on maintenance IVIg, 222 values, added to define the early post-infusion phase (PDF p.3-4, p.5).
- Validation cohort: 177 GBS patients, 689 samples (SID-GBS trial, good-prognosis non-randomised arm); different IVIg brands.
- Dose: 0.4 g/kg/day for 5 days (2 g/kg total), intermittent infusion; simulation assumes 2 h per dose (PDF p.6). Samples before treatment and at 2, 4, 16, 26 weeks.
- Weight (median, range): 72 kg (18-122) building; 79 kg (52.6-118) validation; age median 53 (5-89) and 55 (18-86) (Table 1, from text layer, row order scrambled).
- NONMEM 7.4.0, FOCE-I; internal evaluation by bootstrap and VPC, external by VPC, npde, RMSE.

## Model and parameters
Two-compartment, linear elimination, baseline endogenous IgG (CBAS) added to the infused amount; allometric scaling with fixed exponents 0.75 (CL, Q) and 1 (volumes) on weight/70 kg. IIV on CL and CBAS; proportional residual error. Table 2 (PDF p.9, printed 1293), final model typical values:

Final CL equation (p.6): TVCL = 0.28 x (WT/70)^0.75 x 1.31 (if methylprednisolone) x 1.22 (GBS-DS 3 or 4) x 1.83 (GBS-DS 5). Bootstrap (876/1000 runs converged) 95% CIs agree: CL 0.24-0.34, V1 2.54-3.34, Q 0.16-0.36, V2 2.05-3.39 (Table 2).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No effect-site model. Clinical target taken from earlier work (ref 12): rise in serum IgG over baseline (delta-IgG) greater than 7.3 g/L at 2 weeks after the start of treatment (total IgG, serum). Simulated target attainment (p.6, Fig. 4): 74% (GBS-DS 0-2), 63% (3-4), 33% (5); 63% falls to 43% with methylprednisolone; 6-day 0.4 g/kg/day regimen 74%, 3-day 12%, 2 g/kg over 2 days 54%. No MEAC, ke0, tPeak or opioid potency data (not an opioid paper).

## Covariates
Retained: methylprednisolone co-treatment and GBS disability score on CL. Tested and rejected: sex, age, FCGRT VNTR (n=71 subset), preceding diarrhoea on CL (diarrhoea affected CBAS in the univariate step only), MRC sum score, mechanical ventilation (represented through GBS-DS 5), dose.

## Caveats
- Total IgG only; endogenous IgG synthesis and catabolism are not separated (authors' limitation).
- CIDP patients were on maintenance IVIg (higher baseline), used to inform early kinetics.
- Validation cohort milder than building cohort; small bias near the 2-week sample (336 h); npde variance not 1, model overestimates variability (RMSE 19.39%, prediction error -0.25).
- Weight is actual body weight; the authors suggest adjusted/ideal weight dosing is under study.
