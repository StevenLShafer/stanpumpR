# Funcke 2021: nociception-monitor-guided remifentanil in prostatectomy

## Study
Single-centre RCT, 96 men (ASA 2-3, mean age 63 yr, BMI about 27; Table 1, p.520) having open radical retropubic prostatectomy under propofol/remifentanil, randomised to remifentanil titrated by clinical signs (control, n=24) or by the surgical pleth index (SPI, n=23), pupillary pain index (PPI, n=24) or nociception level (NOL, n=23) monitor. Two SPI/NOL patients were excluded from analysis for atropine. Remifentanil was an infusion of 0.5 ug/kg/min at induction, then 0.2 ug/kg/min until skin incision. Monitor protocols: NOL below its lower limit for more than 30 s cut the rate by 0.03 ug/kg/min every 5 min until stopped; above the upper limit gave a 30 ug bolus and a 0.03 ug/kg/min step up every 5 min. NOL target 10-25, SPI 20-50, PPI 2-3 (manufacturer ranges). Cortisol and ACTH were sampled at baseline, skin closure, after extubation and end of PACU. No remifentanil concentrations were measured.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- No ke0, t1/2 ke0, tPeak, EC50, hysteresis or MEAC reported; no plasma or effect-site concentration. No equianalgesic or potency ratio.
- Cortisol AUC (ug/L/min): control 37.9, SPI 38.6, PPI 72.1, NOL 54.3 (Table 2). Cortisol rose over surgery in PPI and NOL, fell in control and SPI.
- Fig 3b (PDF p.7, text only): end-of-surgery serum cortisol rose when the mean remifentanil dose was below about 0.2 ug/kg/min; the authors call 0.2 ug/kg/min a possible threshold for an unintended stress response. This is an infusion rate, serum cortisol as the surrogate; it is not a concentration or a MEAC.
- Intraoperative stop of remifentanil: control 0, SPI 1 (4.3%), PPI 18 (75%), NOL 11 (47.8%). Movement: 1, 2, 8 and (NOL not confirmed on image) patients.

## Covariates
None modelled. Rates are per kg total body weight and per min of surgery; surgery lasted about 181-193 min (Table 3, text layer).

## Caveats
- Men only, one operation, concurrent propofol and mivacurium (more of both in low-remifentanil groups). Rates are monitor-driven, not steady-state concentrations. Indices thresholds were manufacturer values.
- Abstract and Table 2 disagree (above). Table 2 ACTH SPI cell reads "12.5 (89.0; 17.4)", an evident misprint of the CI lower bound.
