# Colin 2017: dexmedetomidine PK/PD for BIS and MOAA/S with arousal

## Study
Same 18 volunteers and protocol as Hannivoort 2015 (NCT01879865): 9 M, 9 F, age bands 18-34, 35-54, 55-72 y; 20 s infusion at 6 ug/kg/h, then Dyck-model TCI at 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8 ng/mL. Two randomised sessions with noise-cancelling headphones: silence vs looped operating-room noise. BIS at 1 Hz, median-filtered (60 s span), every 50th value kept (median 372 per subject-session); MOAA/S scored before each target change (median 25 per session). Individual Hannivoort PK parameters fixed. NONMEM 7.3 (FOCE-I for BIS, Laplacian for MOAA/S); CIs by log-likelihood profiling.

## Model and parameters
**BIS**: effect-compartment sigmoid model with Hill coefficient fixed to 1; baseline BIS on a logit scale (Eqs. 6-7). Arousal from MOAA/S stimulation resets a "relaxation" compartment that recovers with rate kin (indirect response), interpolating between an unstimulated C50 and a stimulated C50 x (1 + DC50) (Eqs. 1-4).
**MOAA/S**: proportional-odds logit model with sigmoid Emax on Ce; session (noise) as a covariate on C50.

Removing the arousal component worsened fit (dAIC +2358) and shifted ke0 to 0.991/min, baseline to 89.7, C50 to 4.78 ng/mL. No Hill coefficient for MOAA/S is listed in Table 1 (not reported or fixed; unclear).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End points: BIS (processed EEG) and MOAA/S (0-5). Table 2 (Ce for loss of each MOAA/S level and the BIS50 there; reconstructed alignment), noise cohort vs silent cohort: loss of MOAA/S 5: 0.29 ng/mL, BIS 87 vs 0.43, 83; MOAA/S 4: 0.54, 80 vs 0.79, 74; MOAA/S 3: 0.91, 72 vs 1.34, 64; MOAA/S 2: 4.10, 38 vs 5.99, 29; MOAA/S 1: 9.88, 20 vs 14.4, 15. Loss of response to verbal stimulation: Ce 0.91 ng/mL (BIS 72) with noise, 1.3 ng/mL (BIS 64) in silence (Discussion). BIS 60 to 40 corresponds to Ce 1.6 to 3.6 ng/mL. Fig. 3: Ce 0.01, 1.0, 2.5, 10 ng/mL give predicted BIS 96, 70, 50, 20. Arousal effect lasts up to about 20 min on BIS. Not an opioid paper; no MEAC.

## Covariates
Age, weight, height, sex: no effect on BIS or MOAA/S. Background noise (session) on MOAA/S C50 only. No interoccasion variability retained.

## Caveats
18 healthy volunteers; Dyck-model TCI but analysis on Hannivoort PK (plasma). Internal inconsistency: MOAA/S ke0 0.0428/min (Table 1, as read) implies t1/2ke0 16.2 min, but the text states 14 min. Table extraction is misaligned; values above were matched to the narrative (7.13 = 2.63 x 2.71; 0.29 = 0.43 x 0.684; 5.3 min = ln2/0.130).
