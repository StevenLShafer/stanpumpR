# Bibian 2011: dynamic behaviour of BIS, M-Entropy and NeuroSENSE monitors

## Study
Bench test, no patients and no drug. Pre-recorded EEG segments (awake, sedated, anaesthetised, deep, isoelectric; 80 s each) replayed through a "virtual patient" D/A system into BIS A2000, M-Entropy (RE) and NeuroSENSE NS-701 (WAVCNS v2.1.2). BIS and NeuroSENSE trending rate 30 s, M-Entropy 15 s. A composite step signal identified an LTI model by recursive least squares (order by Rissanen MDL); a second, randomly ordered composite (segments 20-80 s) validated it. Authors are NeuroWave employees and inventors of WAVCNS (conflict declared, p7).

## Model and parameters
Steady-state index values (Table 1, p3): awake 97/97/97; sedated 76/77/77; anaesthetised 48/58/51; deep 18/23/19; isoelectric 0/0/0 (BIS/M-Entropy/NeuroSENSE).
Goodness of fit FIT% (Table 3, p4): identification 81.5 / 87.2 / 95.2; validation 53.2 / 29.3 / 94.8.
NeuroSENSE model is second order (text-layer rendering of Table 2: denominator s^2 + 0.1841 s + 0.0115, numerator 0.0115; coefficients not confirmed on the page image). BIS model included a 5 s delay.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No drug, no PK/PD, no ke0, MEAC or potency ratios. Relevant only as the dynamics of the monitor (a response lag of the index to EEG change). Conclusion: NeuroSENSE is LTI and predictable; BIS and M-Entropy are not.

## Covariates
None.

## Caveats
Bench replay of five EEG segments; scales differ between monitors; vendor-authored.
