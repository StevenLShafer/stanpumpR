# Minichmayr 2021: irinotecan PK/PD (UGT1A1 genotype, neutropenia) for trial-design simulation

## Study
A simulation study, not a patient study. Three published models were combined: the Xie 2002 population PK model of irinotecan (CPT-11), SN-38 and SN-38-glucuronide (109/109/83 patients, median 300 mg/m2, range 100-350, 90-min infusions every 3 weeks); a UGT1A1 covariate on SN-38 clearance taken from Valenzuela 2013 (72 Caucasians, 65-350 mg/m2, 60-90 min, every 2 weeks); and the Friberg semi-mechanistic myelosuppression model (20 patients, 79 observations) re-driven by SN-38. The combined model was then used to simulate parallel and crossover pharmacogenomic trials (500 replicates per scenario; n 50-400 per arm), comparing a chi-square/McNemar test on grade 4 neutropenia against a model-based likelihood-ratio analysis. NONMEM 7.3-7.4, FOCE-I, PsN 4.7.0 (page 597-598). Virtual BSA was normal, mean 1.87 m2, SD 0.25, truncated 1.3-2.5 m2 (page 596).

Structure (Fig. 1, page 596): CPT-11 three compartments; SN-38 two compartments; SN-38G two compartments; first-order formation of SN-38 and SN-38G; Friberg chain (proliferating, three transit, circulating). ## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: absolute neutrophil count, with grade 4 neutropenia defined as under 0.5 x10^9 cells/L (page 597). SN-38 drives myelosuppression with SLO 26.8 uM^-1; the CPT-11-driven original slope was 1.29 uM^-1 (page 602). No effect-site model, ke0 or concentration-response for analgesia; not an opioid paper (no MEAC / potency ratio).
Simulated outcomes (page 598, 1000 patients, 350 mg/m2): grade 4 neutropenia 13.6% (95% PI 11.4-15.7) in *1/*1, 20.5% (18.1-22.9) in poor metabolizers; poor metabolizers at 245 mg/m2 (-30%) 10.7% (8.6-12.5). Power >80% needed about 220 per arm (parallel) or <100 per sequence (crossover) with chi-square/McNemar, and 100 total (parallel) or 15 (crossover) with the model-based test.

## Covariates
UGT1A1 genotype: SN-38 clearance -35.7% for *28/*28 and *1/*28 combined ("poor metabolizers", assumed 62% of Caucasians), from Valenzuela 2013 (page 595); the same authors' *28/*28-only estimate was -40.0% from four patients, not used (page 603). BSA on all distribution and elimination parameters of CPT-11, SN-38 and SN-38G; the exponent is not given in the main text (supplement).

## Caveats
Simulation depends on assumed models; the myelosuppression model came from single-agent irinotecan, so event rates are a best case; Caucasian data only; no inter-occasion variability; other UGT1A1 and transporter variants ignored (page 603). The CPT-11 and SN-38 parameters are for the paper's own combined model, whose SN-38 clearance is an apparent one.
