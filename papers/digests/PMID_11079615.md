# Hwang 2000: quinupristin/dalfopristin and eight comparators against Korean gram-positive isolates

## Study
In vitro susceptibility survey. 360 clinical isolates (one per patient) from two tertiary hospitals in Seoul: 120 *S. aureus* (71 MSSA, 49 MRSA, including one VISA with vancomycin MIC 8 µg/mL, teicoplanin 16), 120 coagulase-negative staphylococci (CNS; 15 methicillin-susceptible, 105 methicillin-resistant; not identified to species), 64 *E. faecalis*, 56 *E. faecium* (7 vancomycin-resistant). MICs by NCCLS broth microdilution (M7-A4); QC strains *S. aureus* ATCC 29213 and *E. faecalis* ATCC 29212. MBC by quantitative subculture of every well without visible growth; tolerance defined as MBC/MIC ≥ 32. MBCs were run on 20 *S. aureus*, 20 CNS, 20 *E. faecium* and 15 *E. faecalis*, chosen at random from strains susceptible to as many agents as possible including Q/D (p. 564). Drugs: Q/D, vancomycin, teicoplanin, clindamycin, ciprofloxacin, rifampin, erythromycin, gentamicin, oxacillin (staphylococci); Q/D, vancomycin, teicoplanin, ampicillin, chloramphenicol, ciprofloxacin, erythromycin, rifampin, tetracycline (enterococci). Supported in part by Aventis.

Notes on the tables: Table 3's CNS rifampin "1/15 (0.7)" is printed so (should be 6.7%); Table 3's footnote says tolerant "when the MBC/MIC ratio was 32", Methods says 32 or greater. The number tested per drug in the MBC subsets varies (12-20), not the 20 Methods states. All seven vancomycin-resistant *E. faecium* had vancomycin MIC > 128 (Table 5). No MBC values themselves are printed anywhere; only ratios.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
In vitro end points only: MIC (growth inhibition, microdilution) and MBC/MIC ratio (kill on subculture; kill criterion not stated). No PK/PD index, no time-kill data of their own (Boswell's time-kill work is cited in the Discussion). Not an opioid paper.

## Covariates
Not applicable. Methicillin resistance raised the vancomycin MIC90 of *S. aureus* from 1 to 2 and teicoplanin's from 1 to 8 (Table 1).

## Caveats
- Korean isolates from 1999-2000; small MBC subsets (12-20 per cell) selected from multiply susceptible strains, which biases towards low MBC/MIC ratios.
- MBC kill criterion and inoculum not stated; medians of ratios only, so MBC50/MBC90 cannot be read off.
- CNS not identified to species; *E. faecium* identification initially wrong in 7 of 8 Q/D-resistant isolates (they were *E. faecalis*).
- Text layer is OCR with scrambled tables; every number here was read from the rendered pages.
