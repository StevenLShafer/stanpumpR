# Patsalos and Perucca 2003: AED-AED interactions (Part I)

## Study
Narrative review (Part I of two; Part II covers interactions with non-AED drugs). Medline/PubMed searches September
2002-February 2003, English only, selected by the authors' judgment (search panel, PDF p8). Covers mechanisms
(absorption, protein displacement, induction, inhibition, renal), CYP/UGT substrates, inhibitors and inducers (Table 1,
PDF p3), and an AED-by-AED matrix of expected changes when an AED is added (Table 2, PDF p5).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Pharmacodynamic interactions discussed qualitatively: valproate with ethosuximide or lamotrigine favourable;
carbamazepine with oxcarbazepine or lamotrigine neurotoxic (PDF p7-8). No concentrations.

## Covariates
Age (children induce more; CYP activity exceeds adult levels by 2-3 y, glucuronidation by 3-4 y), genotype and
others listed qualitatively (panel, PDF p4).

## Caveats
Narrative, non-systematic review; magnitudes are averages from single cited studies. Valproate displacing phenytoin:
total phenytoin falls, free usually unchanged, but can rise with inhibition (PDF p2, p6); no number is given for this.
The "reduced by 76/49/66%" statement is quoted from ref 63; whether the primary paper meant "to" cannot be checked here.
