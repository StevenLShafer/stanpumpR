# Absalom 2003: accuracy of the Paedfusor propofol TCI in children

## Study
Prospective evaluation of the "Paedfusor" paediatric propofol TCI (prototype in a Graseby 3500 pump; its PK set is a preliminary Schüttler model, not his final published one). 32 children ASA II-III enrolled, 29 analysed (3 excluded: two failed arterial cannulation within 3 min, one died). 7 cardiac catheterisation, 22 cardiac surgery (21 on bypass). Median age 5 y (range 1-15), median weight 16 kg (5-53) (Table 2, PDF p3/printed 509). Propofol TCI started at 5 mcg/mL; target then changed by clinical judgement. Premedication oral midazolam 0.5 mg/kg. Catheterisation children got fentanyl 1 mcg/kg; surgical children got an alfentanil TCI (Maitre model) at a target of 100 ng/mL, plus pancuronium; sevoflurane used for induction in some small children. Up to 9 arterial samples (0.5 mL) per child, 212 in all, EDTA tubes, gas-liquid chromatography; the assay matrix is described as "plasma propofol" in the Methods, while the figure axes say "blood" (not reconciled in the paper). LOD 0.05 mcg/mL, CV 4.2% at 0.5 and 4.9% at 8 mcg/mL. Samples at 3 and 8 min, after a target decrease, stable anaesthesia (no target change >30 min), on bypass, and 0, 3, 8 min after stopping and at eye opening. Performance by Varvel: PE, MDPE, MDAPE, wobble.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No ke0, EC50, tPeak, or effect measurement. Only blood/plasma propofol concentration was measured. (Not an opioid paper; alfentanil was given at a 100 ng/mL TCI target and fentanyl 1 mcg/kg, but no opioid concentration was measured.)

## Covariates
Paedfusor: weight (and age, via the preliminary Schüttler structure) are the inputs; the paper does not print the covariate equations, only the 20 kg/5 yr values (Table 1). Compared models: Marsh and Short are linear in weight with fixed rate constants.

## Caveats
Heterogeneous cardiac cohort, mostly of Middle Eastern origin; propofol model derived in Western patients. Concurrent alfentanil/fentanyl, which the authors argue raises propofol concentrations (cited 19-29% with alfentanil 40 ng/mL, 50% with fentanyl), so measured/predicted is biased high. Bias worse in "no flow" periods (+19%), probably redistribution, not clearance. Bypass priming dilution gave a negative bias (-5.5%). Median-based indices, no formal CI on MDPE. Later work in healthy 3-26 month olds (Sepulveda 2011, PMID_21743068) found Paedfusor MDPE +4.97%, MDAPE 22.83%.
