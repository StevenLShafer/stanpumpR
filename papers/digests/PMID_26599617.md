# Okusanya 2016: systematic review of MgSO4 pharmacokinetics in pre-eclampsia/eclampsia

PDF page = printed page − 355 (PDF p. 1 = printed 356).

## Study
- WHO/HRP systematic review following PRISMA, with a protocol (p. 357). Searched MEDLINE, EMBASE, CINAHL, POPLINE and
  Global Health Library in Oct-Nov 2013, updated March 2015; no language or date limits.
- Included 28 studies (7 decades): 1466 women, 91.5% pre-eclampsia and 8.5% eclampsia, 17 MgSO4 regimens. Four
  studies were model-based (p. 359).
- Risk of bias (modified QUADAS-2): 12 studies low, 13 uncertain, 3 high.
- All magnesium concentrations were converted to mmol/L: mg/dL × 0.411 and mEq/L × 0.500 (p. 358).
- Qualitative synthesis by route and regimen; no meta-analysis.
- Measured analyte: serum total magnesium, with some studies reporting ionised Mg.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- Conventional therapeutic range: serum 2 to 3.5 mmol/L (4-7 mEq/L) is "generally held to be therapeutic". The
  authors trace it to Pritchard 1955 (ref 6), not to exposure-response studies (PDF p. 9, printed 364; page image).
  The introduction (p. 357) describes 2 mmol/L as a supposed minimum therapeutic level based on early clinical observation.
- The authors conclude that the minimum effective concentration is probably below 2 mmol/L and that the therapeutic
  window is wider. The Zuspan regimen is clinically effective, yet its mean levels never reached 2.00 mmol/L. They
  warn that titrating to 2-3.5 mmol/L may risk toxicity without better protection (p. 364).
- Typical levels (Table 2, PDF p. 5; Table 3, PDF p. 8; page images):
  - Baseline: <1 mmol/L (0.58-0.95, with one outlier, Chesley 1979, at 2.10).
  - Zuspan (4 g + 1 g/h): ½ h 1.48-1.70 mmol/L; steady state 1.64; "average" 1.70.
  - 4 g + 2 g/h: steady state 1.84 and 1.99.
  - Sibai regimen (6 g + 2 g/h): "average" 2.05 and 2.27.
  - Pritchard regimen: ½ h 1.90-2.79.
- CSF magnesium: 1.23-1.34 mmol/L. CSF ionised fraction 53.6%, unchanged during infusion. Serum ionised fraction at
  baseline 50.0-64.9% (one study 69.8%).
- Toxicity: one death from respiratory depression at serum Mg 9.90 mmol/L (Suvarna 2011, p. 362).
- Simulations in Lu 2002: 1 g/h rarely exceeded 2.00 mmol/L in the first 10 h; 2 g/h more often reached 2.00-4.00
  mmol/L.
- No ke0 or PD model. Not an opioid.

## Covariates
None analysed. The authors note V differed between Indian and Australian populations.

## Caveats
- Heterogeneous designs, sampling times and assays across seven decades; most studies were small. Pre-eclampsia and
  eclampsia data could not always be separated.
- Funded by Merck for Mothers.
- The Table 3 baseline of 2.10 mmol/L (Chesley 1979, 3 g IV + 10 g IM) is implausible for a pre-treatment level.
  Reported as printed.
- The Taber CL/V/t½ triple is internally inconsistent (see derived values above).
- The V and CL attribution between refs 17 and 27 in the text is only implicit.
