# Cengiz 2010: mixing lispro with glargine in one syringe, glucose clamps in youth with type 1 diabetes

## Study
- Randomised crossover, two euglycaemic clamps within 4 weeks (PDF p2, printed 1010). 11 youth with type 1
  diabetes on pump therapy (6 M, 5 F), age 15.1 ± 2.9 y (abstract prints ± 3), A1C 7.6 ± 0.6%; eligibility age 11-21 y,
  BMI < 95th percentile. **Body weight not reported.**
- Dose: 0.2 units/kg lispro + 0.4 units/kg glargine (fixed 1:2 ratio), either mixed in one syringe immediately
  before injection (left arm, deep SC) or as separate injections (glargine left arm, lispro right arm). Pump insulin
  suspended just before dosing; overnight glucose titrated to 80-120 mg/dL.
- Clamp at 80-90 mg/dL for 5 h with 20% dextrose; plasma glucose every 5 min (YSI). Plasma insulin every 10 min
  for 90 min, every 15 min for the next 90 min, every 30 min for the last 120 min.
- Assay: Mercodia iso-insulin ELISA, stated cross-reactivity 89% with lispro, 44% with glargine. Authors estimate
  glargine contributed < 10 µU/mL at any time. Statistics: paired t tests, mixed-model repeated measures; means ± SE.
- No compartmental model: non-compartmental summary measures only.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: glucose infusion rate at a euglycaemic clamp (glucose-space-adjusted GIR every 10 min).
- Mixing lowered GIR between 60 and 190 min and raised it between 270 and 300 min (Figure 1, PDF p2); peak effect
  roughly halved and delayed by ~90 min (Table 1). No EC50, ke0 or concentration-effect model. Not an opioid paper:
  no MEAC, ke0/tPeak or potency ratio.

## Covariates
None studied. Doses per kg.

## Caveats
- n = 11, adolescents on pump therapy; 5 h clamp only, so glargine's own action is not characterised (authors say so).
- Assay cross-reacts with glargine (44%), and residual pump insulin gives a baseline of ~33 mIU/mL (Figure 2), so
  Cmax/Tmax are not pure lispro values.
- Internal inconsistency in P values between abstract, text and table (see open_questions). Received date printed
  as 17 November 2010, after the acceptance date (25 January 2010): an evident misprint, no bearing on the data.
- Comparator for "delayed peak": the same subjects' separate injections (and, in Discussion, an earlier cohort of
  pump-treated adolescents given 0.2 U/kg lispro alone, ref 12), not a population model.

## Verification against stanpumpR
- Not cited, so nothing to verify: **not-applicable**.
- Independent comparison: the library models lispro (`R/drugs_insulinLispro.R`) and glargine
  (`R/drugs_insulinGlargine.R`), both through the Tham 2017 model (`R/insulinTham.R:26-33`, lispro ka 0.989 /h,
  lag 0.265 h, 72.9% first-order + zero-order over 1.06 h, V/F 43.0 L, CL/F 30.5 L/h). That model's lispro peak is at
  about 64 min at the 80 kg reference (derived: numerical simulation of the Table 2 structure in `R/insulinTham.R`,
  single dose, no baseline). Cengiz's separate-injection Tins-max, 55 ± 6 min (SE), in adolescents with residual
  insulin and glargine cross-reactivity, is in the same range: consistent, not a test.
- The library simulates lispro and glargine given together by superposition of independent depots. This paper shows
  that **mixing them in one syringe** breaks that: lispro's absorption is slowed (Tins-max 106 vs 55 min, Cins-max
  53 vs 149). No file in `R/` mentions mixing these two insulins; a superposed pair matches the paper's
  separate-injection arm only. Recorded as a limitation, not a discrepancy.
- No glucose, GIR or HbA1c is plotted by the library (`R/insulinTham.R:7-8`), so the paper's PD values have no
  counterpart in the code.
