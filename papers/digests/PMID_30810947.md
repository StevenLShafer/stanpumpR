# Beechinor 2019: high-dose methotrexate population PK in infants with ALL

## Study
- Children's Oncology Group trial P9407 (PDF p2-3, printed 900-901). 71 infants with newly diagnosed ALL,
  under 366 days postnatal age, born after 36 weeks' gestation; 17 in an intensive PK substudy, 54 with routine
  monitoring only. Age at infusion was imputed from the protocol (exact infusion dates not available).
- Table 1 (PDF p5, printed 903; medians, ranges), substudy / routine group: postnatal age 8.5 (2.9-12.9) /
  7.0 (1.0-12.9) months; weight 8.6 (4.5-11.9) / 7.6 (3.0-12.1) kg; height 69 (51-78) / 66.5 (49.7-79.0) cm;
  BSA 0.40 (0.24-0.48) / 0.36 (0.20-0.48) m2; dose 1.5 (0.62-1.96) / 1.4 (0.60-2.2) g, 4.0 (2.0-4.5) /
  4.0 (2.0-5.6) g/m2; C24h 54.7 (24.2-146.6) / 61.5 (24.0-140.0) uM; C48h 0.20 / 0.20 uM; C72h 0.17 / 0.11 uM.
  Sex 8 M 9 F / 24 M 30 F.
- Dose: 4 g/m2 IV over 24 h as 200 mg/m2 over 20 min then 3.8 g/m2 over the rest of the 24 h, weeks 4, 5, 11
  and 12 (four cycles); alkalinised hydration and leucovorin rescue. 229 cycles; 14.4% of cycles under
  3.5 g/m2, 1.3% over 4.5 g/m2 (reason unknown).
- Sampling: end of infusion, +24 h, then every 12-24 h until < 0.18 uM; substudy also 1, 6, 12, 23 h.
  690 concentrations, 18 (2.6%) below an assumed LLQ of 0.05 uM excluded, leaving 672.
- Assay: routine samples by local FPIA or EMIT; substudy samples by HPLC at a central lab. Concentrations in uM.
- NONMEM 7.4, FOCE-I, ADVAN3 TRANS4; log-transformed data; bootstrap (1000) and VPC.

## Model and parameters
Two compartments, linear elimination, theory-based allometry on total body weight normalised to 70 kg
(exponent 0.75 on CL and Q, 1 on Vc and Vp, fixed; estimated exponents 0.80 and 0.98 did not improve OFV).
No other covariate retained (age, BSA, sex tested; age reduced OFV by < 3.8). No renal function marker was
available; eligibility required creatinine clearance > 70 mL/min/1.73 m2. Table 2 (PDF p7, printed 905):

Simulation (Table 3, PDF p9, printed 907; four cycles, 1000 virtual infants, median (IQR)): C24h 33.5
(27.9-40.0), 67.4 (55.7-79.8), 100.5 (83.7-119), 134.3 (111-159) uM for 2, 4, 6, 8 g/m2; supratherapeutic
at 24 h (> 100 uM) 0.3, 6.28, 51.1, 85.5%; at 48 h (> 1 uM) 3.05, 9.43, 16.4, 23.3%; at 72 h (> 0.1 uM)
24.0, 69.3, 89.0, 95.6%. Bedside algorithm (2-h and 6-h levels): 211/1000 adjusted; median C24h 58.8 vs
59.1 uM with and without adjustment.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PK/PD model. Targets taken from the literature (refs 41, 42), not derived here: C24h < 16 uM
subtherapeutic (relapse); > 100 uM at 24 h, > 1.0 uM at 48 h, > 0.1 uM at 72 h supratherapeutic (toxicity).
The authors note these targets are not prospectively validated. Not an opioid; no ke0.

## Covariates
Weight only (allometric, above). Postnatal age not significant; only 28.4% of cycles in infants under
6 months.

## Caveats
- Q is tiny relative to CL (0.13 vs 11.0 L/h/70 kg): the peripheral compartment barely exchanges, so the
  model is nearly one-compartment over the first days (my reading).
- Mixed assays (FPIA/EMIT, which can cross-react with 7-OH-methotrexate, and HPLC); a uniform LLQ assumed.
- Imputed ages; no renal markers; IOV estimated mainly from routine samples (16/17 intensive profiles in
  cycle 1). VPC: 11.9% of observations outside the 90% PI, the authors suggest CL may be slightly overestimated.
- Allometry to 70 kg is a reporting convention; the data span 3.0-12.1 kg only.
