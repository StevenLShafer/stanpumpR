# Somma 1998: midazolam sedation PD by TCI after CABG

## Study
- Same 90-patient, three-centre CABG cohort as Zomorodi 1998 (PMID 9856717). Of 63 PK-evaluable patients, 9 sufentanil infusion files were unusable -> 54 patients (17 PAVA, 18 Emory, 19 Duke), 1,329 Ramsay sedation scores (Table 3), about 25 per patient.
- Midazolam and sufentanil by TCI using Bührer's and Hudson's kinetics. Ramsay score (modified, 0-6, Table 1) titrated to 5 for >= 2 h, then 3-4.
- Concentrations used in the PD model were post hoc Bayesian midazolam predictions from Zomorodi's model, not measured values; sufentanil predicted from Gepts' kinetics. Predicted midazolam 5-189 ng/mL (mean 53; 91% within 10-100); sufentanil 0.05-2.8 ng/mL (mean 0.24).
- Ordered-categorical logistic model, NONMEM IV; naive pooled (NPD) vs mixed effects; jackknife cross-validation; prediction probability PK.
- Footnote (p. 1432): the STANPUMP version on the lab website incorporates the ICU midazolam kinetics of Zomorodi et al.

## Model and parameters
P(sedation >= ss) = C^n / (C^n + C50,ss^n) (eq. 1); P(ss) by differencing (eq. 2). Models for C (Table 2): A midazolam; B + thetaS x sufentanil; C + interaction; D midazolam + virtual drug thetaV x exp(-K t); E D + sufentanil. t = time since ICU arrival.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: Ramsay sedation score in ventilated SICU patients. C50s refer to plasma (model-predicted) midazolam plus the virtual drug, not effect-site concentrations: **no ke0, t1/2ke0 or tPeak** is estimated. Onset within 2-2.5 min is quoted from the literature (p. 1430).
- Dosing guidance (Table 5, STANPUMP simulations): Ramsay 3 -> start 2 mg/h about 6 h after arrival; Ramsay 5 -> 3 mg/h at arrival, 8 mg/h at 3 h, tapering to 6 mg/h by 72 h. Context-sensitive half-time 1.5 h after 12 h, 4.2 h after 72 h.
- 1-OH-midazolam detectable in only 6 of 54; no effect found. Not an opioid paper: no MEAC or potency ratio (sufentanil potency thetaS in models B/C/E only).

## Covariates
Age and centre tested on C50: not retained. Sufentanil and the midazolam x sufentanil interaction did not beat model D.

## Caveats
Ramsay scale is ordinal, mixes verbal/tactile/painful stimuli; sufentanil and midazolam concentrations correlated and sufentanil range narrow; PK model mostly from 24 h of data; guidelines specific to this anaesthetic protocol.
