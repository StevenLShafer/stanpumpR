# Janssen 2021: chemotherapy population PK in pregnancy (docetaxel, paclitaxel, doxorubicin, epirubicin)

## Study
Pregnant cancer patients from the INCIP registry (NCT00330447) were pooled with non-pregnant controls from earlier trials
and (paclitaxel) the Crombag 2019 dataset; postpartum samples counted as non-pregnant. Chemotherapy was given in the
2nd/3rd trimester only. Plasma, validated assays, NONMEM 7.3 / PsN 4.4.8, FOCE-I, SIR for CIs. Table 1 (PDF p.3; read on image):

## Model and parameters
Docetaxel, doxorubicin, epirubicin: linear 3-compartment, first-order elimination (docetaxel one control dataset, no
body-weight data for controls so no WT scaling; doxorubicin WT-scaled to 70 kg, dOFV -65.3; epirubicin no WT or sex effect).
Paclitaxel: the Crombag 2019 model (saturable elimination from V1; saturable distribution to the first peripheral
compartment; linear distribution to V3 with Q2), refitted with the pregnant data; bilirubin dropped (not measured in pregnant patients).
Exponential BSV, proportional RUV (separate for pregnant and non-pregnant). Allometry fixed 0.75 (CL) and 1 (V), 70 kg (Eq. 3).
Pregnancy effect: one discrete proportional factor per parameter (no continuous gestational-age effect could be identified; no first-trimester data).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No exposure-response, no MEAC or therapeutic range; the authors state no exposure-response is described for these four drugs. Not an opioid.

## Covariates
Pregnancy (discrete factors above; model-selection dOFV: docetaxel -1.6 for 3 factors, p=0.665; paclitaxel -30 for 3 factors, p<0.00001; doxorubicin -5.7, p=0.059; epirubicin -60, p<0.0001 as printed with df=2).
Weight (70 kg allometry; doxorubicin only, not available for docetaxel controls); paclitaxel: BSA, sex, age on VMEL (footnote b); gender tested and rejected for docetaxel, doxorubicin, epirubicin.
Continuous gestational age: not identifiable for any drug.

## Caveats
Small pregnant groups (9 docetaxel patients, almost all in the 3rd trimester); effects small against interpatient variability (authors advise unchanged dosing);
non-pregnant controls from other studies and assays (separate RUV); VPCs overpredict variability in pregnant doxorubicin and epirubicin patients; no first-trimester data;
paclitaxel bilirubin effect dropped; paclitaxel Methods vs Table 2 compartment naming differs. Assay analytes (base vs salt) and dose salt forms are not stated.
Internal count mismatches between text and Table 1 (see open_questions).
