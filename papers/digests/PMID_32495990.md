# Chiang 2021: plasma and CSF ondansetron pharmacokinetics in adults

## Study
- 15 adults scheduled for hip or knee arthroplasty under spinal anaesthesia (NCT02901054, Washington University); inclusion 18-70 y, BMI < 33; no hepatic/renal insufficiency, no strong CYP inhibitors or QT drugs.
- Ondansetron **16 mg IV over 15 min** pre-operatively. Venous plasma at 0, 15, 30, 60, 120, 180 min from start of infusion; one CSF sample per patient (37-83 min) before spinal anaesthesia.
- HPLC-UV, detection limit 10 ng/mL plasma, 5 ng/mL CSF.
- Subject 14 excluded (unquantifiable), so the model has 14: age 58 median (45-70), 50% male, weight 83.0 ± 6.9 kg, CrCl 106 ± 20 mL/min (Table 1, excluding subject 14).
- NCA (WinNonlin) and NONMEM VII FOCE-I; stepwise covariate search (forward P < 0.05, backward P < 0.01).

## Model and parameters
Two compartments with linear elimination from the central compartment plus CSF by partition coefficient (C_CSF = KP·C1). Covariate form (Equation 6, Figure 2): P = A·(COV/COV_median)^B. Table 4:

All absolute (not per kg). CL-Vc covariance estimated, value not reported. NCA (Table 3, n = 14, mean ± SD): t½ 4.9 ± 2.3 h, MRT 6.9 h, AUC 625 ± 296 h·ug/L, Vd,ss 185 ± 50 L, CL 33 ± 20 L/h.
Derived at age 58: half-lives 0.125 h and 5.03 h; Vc 220 L at 45 y and 25.1 L at 70 y (derived).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No effect model. CSF/plasma ratio 0.15 (0.08-0.26); CSF ~35-70 nM at 1-2 h vs ~350 nM plasma peak. No P-gp or OCT1 genotype effect detected. Not an opioid; no MEAC, ke0 or potency ratio.

## Covariates
Age on Vc only (power, exponent −4.91). Weight, height, BMI, sex, CrCl tested and not retained.

## Caveats
n = 14, ages 45-70, 3-h sampling, insensitive HPLC-UV (LOD 10 ng/mL), surgical patients; extreme age exponent; venous sampling.
