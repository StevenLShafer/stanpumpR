# Plank 2005: dose-response of insulin detemir in a 24-h clamp

## Study
Single-centre (Graz), double-blind, randomized, six-period crossover; 24-h isoglycemic clamp at 7.2 mmol/L
(130 mg/dL) (PDF pp. 1-2, printed 1107-1108). 12 C-peptide-negative type 1 diabetic subjects (7 M, 5 F), age
36 ± 12 y, diabetes duration 18 ± 9 y, BMI 23.3 ± 2.3 kg/m2, HbA1c 7.7 ± 1.0%; 11 completed (one withdrawn,
catheter problems). Weight not reported.

Treatments, SC in the thigh at about 22:00 after IV insulin run-in: insulin detemir 0.1, 0.2, 0.4, 0.8 and
1.6 units/kg (1 unit = 24 nmol) and NPH 0.3 IU/kg (1 IU = 6 nmol) (PDF p. 1). Glucose turnover by
[6,6-2H2]glucose (Steele, Powrie modification). Detemir/insulin sampled every 30 min from -60 to 360 min, then
hourly. Detemir assay (Medi-Lab): measures **total** (free plus albumin-bound) detemir, no cross-reaction with
human insulin (PDF p. 2). Duration of action: from 50% reduction of the IV insulin infusion (onset) until glucose
consistently exceeded 8.3 mmol/L after the last glucose infusion, or 24 h. Analysis: ANOVA; dose-response of
AUCGIR with linear plus square-root terms.

Durations at 0.8 and 1.6 U/kg are underestimated because most clamps were stopped at 24 h.
Table 2 PD (mean ± SD): AOCEGP NPH 568 ± 482; detemir 129 ± 150, 447 ± 291, 988 ± 536, 1,454 ± 435,
1,874 ± 345 mg/kg. AUCPGU NPH 324 ± 490; detemir 34 ± 28, 77 ± 84, 387 ± 509, 1,084 ± 958, 3,448 ± 990 mg/kg.

Potency: detemir 0.29 U/kg gave the same AUCGIR as NPH 0.3 IU/kg (interpolated; linear interpolation 0.285),
a detemir:NPH **molar** ratio of 3.9 (PDF p. 4; derived check 0.29 × 24 / (0.3 × 6) = 3.87). At 0.29 U/kg
duration 16.9 h vs 12.7 h for NPH (4.2 h longer; comparator is NPH 0.3 IU/kg in the same subjects); AOCEGP 636
(279-879) vs 584 (323-846) mg/kg; AUCPGU 173 (47-316) vs 328 (39-617). Between-subject variability of duration
and GIRmax was lower for detemir 0.2-0.4 U/kg than for NPH (P < 0.05 / P = 0.07; Table 1 footnote).
No ke0, EC50 or concentration-effect model. Not an opioid paper: no MEAC.

## Caveats
- n = 12 (11 completers), lean type 1 diabetics; weight not reported.
- Clamps truncated at 24 h; onset definition is conservative (authors), so duration is underestimated.
- The 0.29 U/kg equivalence is an interpolation; no dose of detemir near 0.29 U/kg was tested.
- Detemir concentrations are total (mostly albumin-bound) and not comparable molar-for-molar with human insulin
  (NPH Cmax 216 pmol/L); NPH AUC not reported. Industry-sponsored (Novo Nordisk).
