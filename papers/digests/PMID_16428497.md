# Sweeney 2006: pemetrexed with aspirin or ibuprofen

## Study
Two independent, open-label, randomized two-way crossover drug-interaction studies in adults with advanced solid tumours, creatinine clearance 60 mL/min or more (aspirin study, Indiana University; ibuprofen study, San Antonio) (abstract, Methods PDF p2). 29 (aspirin) and 30 (ibuprofen) patients enrolled; 27 per study evaluable for PK (Results, p4). Pemetrexed 500 mg/m2 i.v. over 10 min on day 1 of a 21-day cycle, with folic acid 350-600 ug daily and vitamin B12 1,000 ug i.m. every 9 weeks, and dexamethasone 4 mg twice daily for 3 days. NSAID in one of the first two cycles: enteric-coated aspirin 325 mg or ibuprofen 400 mg orally every 6 h for 9 doses starting 2 days before, last dose 1 h before the infusion. Plasma samples pre-dose, end of infusion, 0.25, 0.5, 1, 2, 4, 6, 8, 12, 24, 48, 72 h. LC-ESI-MS/MS (low range 0.010-2.000 ug/mL; high range 1.000-200.000 ug/mL; interassay precision 6.5-10.3% and 7.4-9.6%). Noncompartmental analysis (WinNonlin 3.1); mixed-effects model on log-transformed AUC, Cmax, Clp, Vss. Baseline mean CrCl 121 (71.1-228) mL/min aspirin study, 117 (61.0-193) mL/min ibuprofen study; mean BSA 1.86 and 1.88 m2 (Results p3).

Background from the Introduction (p1, citing other work): about 80% protein bound, Vss about 16 L, t1/2 about 3.5 h, 70-90% of the dose excreted unchanged in urine within 24 h. Discussion (p6) quotes a 426-patient single-agent reference: clearance 91.8 mL/min, Vss 16.1 L (ref. 5, the label).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD model, no ke0, tPeak, MEAC (not an opioid). Toxicity end points only: grade 3/4 neutropenia in 5 of 29 and 6 of 30 patients (Table 3), no febrile neutropenia; nadirs similar with or without NSAID (Table 4). Renal comparison: patients with CrCl 41-59 mL/min had mean AUC 59.6% higher than 11 with CrCl 80 or more at 500 mg/m2 (Discussion, p6, from ref. 13).

## Covariates
None modelled. Study design bases: ibuprofen advice only for CrCl 80 mL/min or more; only 3 ibuprofen-study patients had CrCl 60-80 (Discussion).

## Caveats
Noncompartmental, crossover with sampling to 72 h; the ibuprofen study's "alone" geometric means (Clp 92.9, AUC 164.7) differ from the aspirin study's (95.1, 163.1) as the studies are separate. Aspirin dose 1.3 g/day only; higher salicylate doses untested. Folate-supplemented patients (unlike the Latz fit). Mechanism not established (tubular secretion competition suggested).

## Verification against stanpumpR
- Independent check of `R/drugs_pemetrexed.R` against Sweeney's "alone" cycles. Latz-model typical values (header lines 40-43): CL 90.2 mL/min, Vss 16.1 L at CrCl 92.6 mL/min and BSA 1.74 m2. Sweeney's pemetrexed-alone means: Clp 95.1 (aspirin study) and 92.9 (ibuprofen study) mL/min, Vss 14.4 and 16.7 L (Table 2), in patients with mean CrCl 117-121 mL/min and BSA 1.86-1.88 m2. The library formula at CrCl about 120 mL/min would give CL = 43.0 + 47.2 x 120/92.6 = about 104 mL/min (derived; the weight-size terms were not evaluated), so Sweeney's observed clearances are about 9-11% lower than the Latz formula at that CrCl. The difference may reflect the CG weight/BSA formula choices or the folate supplementation (Latz was fitted without), not an error; not settled. Vss values agree to within about 10%. Units: mL/min vs the code's L/min conversion by /1000 (`R/drugs_pemetrexed.R:145`) is consistent.
- Library header lines 20-22 and reference text correctly describe Latz as unsupplemented; Sweeney used supplementation, so this is not a like-for-like validation.
- Ibuprofen interaction (clearance 0.84x) is absent from the library: **cannot verify** any code behaviour, see open question.
