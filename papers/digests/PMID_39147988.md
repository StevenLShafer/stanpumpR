# Chu 2024: allopurinol/oxypurinol PopPK in neonates with CCHD, cardiopulmonary bypass

## Study
- CRUCIAL trial (NCT04217421) PK substudy, one centre (Wilhelmina Children's Hospital Utrecht). 14 neonates with critical congenital heart disease, cardiac surgery with CPB at median postnatal age 5.6 days; 13 had surgery at the study centre (Table 1, PDF p5; PDF p7).
- IV allopurinol 20 mg/kg per dose, 5 doses: within 45-60 min of birth, 12 h later, 12 h before surgery, at CPB start, and 24 h after surgery (PDF p3).
- 140 samples (60 postnatal-preoperative, 36 intraoperative, 44 postoperative), 0.5 mL, sparse; plasma LC-MS/MS, LLOQ 0.05 mg/L allopurinol and 0.0467 mg/L oxypurinol; 5.8% of allopurinol values below LLOQ, set to LLOQ/2.
- Model: NONMEM 7.5, FOCE-I; built in three sequential periods split at CPB start and end. Median surgery 320 min, lowest rectal temperature 27.7 C (median).

## Model and parameters
Two-compartment allopurinol linked to a one-compartment oxypurinol, with oxypurinol auto-inhibition of allopurinol metabolism (Emax 1 fixed, IC50 1.1 mg/L fixed). Parameters scaled allometrically to 3.5 kg (fixed exponents 0.75 CL, 1 V). Oxypurinol CL and V are relative to a formation fraction assumed 1. Derived clearances for a typical 3.5 kg neonate (text and Fig. 3, PDF p8): allopurinol/oxypurinol CL 0.95/0.21 L/h at birth, 2.97/0.41 L/h before CPB (day 5.6), 1.38/0.12 L/h during CPB, 2.21/0.05 L/h after CPB. Check: E x birth CL reproduces these (1.46 x 0.95 = 1.39; 2.33 x 0.95 = 2.21; 0.54 x 0.21 = 0.11; 0.23 x 0.21 = 0.048), so the E factors are relative to the birth CL, not to the pre-CPB value. Vd rises about 42% (allopurinol to 3.16 L) and 48% (oxypurinol to 18 L) postoperatively.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD model fitted; no ke0. Targets (PDF p4, p9-11): allopurinol > 2 mg/L in more than 66% of patients (xanthine oxidase inhibition; from refs 30, 31); allopurinol + oxypurinol >= 6.22 mg/L as IC90 for XO inhibition (from the authors' HIE study, ref 11). Target attainment was 100%, 53.8%, 100% at 24 h after birth, 24 h after CPB start, 36 h after surgery; combined IC90 target met in > 90% of patients at the 53.8% time point. Not an opioid paper; no MEAC or potency ratio.

## Covariates
Allometric weight (fixed exponents 0.75 and 1); postnatal age on CL via a sigmoid Hill function (dOFV -80, 2 df); CPB and post-CPB period factors on CL and Vd. Neonatal-only data, so maturation and recovery from illness cannot be separated (authors).

## Caveats
- n=14, sparse sampling, one centre; many parameters fixed (recovery effect, V1, Q1, IC50 of auto-inhibition, which was derived from PNA <= 3 days data).
- Full conversion of allopurinol to oxypurinol assumed; the authors suspect incomplete conversion during CPB (ultrafiltration, circuit sequestration, hypothermia).
- The low post-CPB oxypurinol CL (0.05 L/h) is attributed to likely acute kidney injury, not measured here.
- Allopurinol AUC12 after each dose was compared with an HIE neonate study (ALBINO): median allopurinol 129 mg/L*h, oxypurinol 42.7 mg/L*h.
