# Duong 2019: carboplatin, etoposide and melphalan in children with neuroblastoma

## Study
- Two sites pooled: Study 1, UK (SIOPEN HR-NBL1, NCT01704716), n = 27; Study 2, Children's Hospital at Westmead,
  Sydney, n = 24. 51 children with high-risk neuroblastoma, given high-dose carboplatin, etoposide and melphalan
  (CEM) before autologous transplant (PDF p.2-3, printed 137-138).
- Table 2 (PDF p.4, printed 139; median (range), overall): age 3.5 (1.7-8.3) y; weight 14.5 (8.6-31) kg
  (Study 1 15.0, Study 2 14.1); BSA 0.62 (0.43-1.1) m2; measured GFR_BSA 99 (47-230) mL/min/1.73 m2; GFR
  38 (23-75) mL/min; 31% female.
- Two patients aged >12 y were excluded (a 20-year-old outlier and a 15-year-old without GFR). The text then
  says "n = 53 remaining patients" while Table 2 and the Results give 51 (PDF p.4); carboplatin dosing was
  missing in 5, so carboplatin is fitted in 46 (PDF p.5).
- Dosing (Table 1, PDF p.3): carboplatin 96-h infusion; GFR 60-<100: Newell formula (<=12 kg) or modified Calvert
  (>12 kg; Study 1 used Newell); GFR >=100: 56.8 mg/kg (<=12 kg) or 1700 mg/m2 (>12 kg). Target AUC 16.4 mg/mL·min.
  Etoposide 96-h infusion: 26.8 mg/kg or 800 mg/m2 (GFR <100), 45.2 mg/kg or 1352 mg/m2 (GFR >=100). Melphalan
  15-30-min infusion daily for 3 days: 2 mg/kg/day or 60 mg/m2/day (GFR <100); 2.3 mg/kg/day or 70 mg/m2/day (GFR >=100).
- GFR measured by 51Cr-EDTA (Study 1) or 99mTc-DTPA (Study 2) plasma clearance.
- Sampling (PDF p.3): carboplatin and etoposide pre-dose and 6, 24, 48, 72, 96, 100, 120 h after the start;
  melphalan Study 1 at 5, 20, 90 min after the end of infusion, Study 2 at 20, 30, 40, 50, 60, 90, 120 min post-dose.
- Assays: Study 1 carboplatin as free (unbound) platinum in plasma ultrafiltrate by flameless AAS; etoposide
  LC-MS/MS. Study 2 carboplatin HPLC-UV (matrix not stated), etoposide HPLC-fluorescence, melphalan HPLC-UV.
  LOQ carboplatin 2, etoposide 0.5, melphalan 0.1 ug/mL; all samples above LOQ.
- NONMEM 7.3, FOCE-I, PsN 3.5.3; site-specific residual errors; bootstrap n = 1000 (87% successful).

Residual (proportional %): carboplatin 13 (Study 1) / 34 (Study 2); etoposide 17 / 24; melphalan 16 / 24.

Table 4 (PDF p.9, printed 144), simulated carboplatin AUC (mg/mL·min, median (95% CI)) at 50 mg/kg:
GFR <100: <=12 kg (n 5, 10.8 kg) 15.9 (15.3, 16.1); >12 kg (n 20, 15.4 kg) 17.4 (16.7, 17.7).
GFR >=100: <=12 kg (n 6, 10.5 kg) 15.3 (14.7, 15.9); >12 kg (n 15, 16.8 kg) 17.4 (16.5, 18.5).
Newell and Calvert doses overshoot in GFR >=100 (e.g. Calvert <=12 kg AUC 22.0).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No ke0, no concentration-effect model. Carboplatin is dosed to a target AUC of 16.4 mg/mL·min (±20%), an exposure.
No significant association of AUC with hepatorenal toxicity, ototoxicity or creatinine change (Study 2, small n).
Not an opioid paper.

## Covariates
Allometric weight only (fixed exponents 0.75 and 1; estimating them did not improve fit). Tested and rejected: age,
sex, BSA, BMI, GFR (BSA-normalized and absolute; significant forward at P<0.05 but dropped at backward P<0.01),
study site; in Study 2 also serum creatinine and Schwartz clearance.

## Caveats
- Carboplatin Q and VP, and etoposide VP, are poorly identified (text RSE 143%, 69%, 234%; carboplatin VP bootstrap
  CI up to 1014 L). Table 3 prints 50% and 31% for carboplatin Q and VP, disagreeing with the text.
- Samples begin 6 h into a 96-h infusion: early distribution after a short infusion is extrapolation.
- Two sites measured carboplatin differently (ultrafilterable platinum vs HPLC carboplatin).
- Ages 1.7-8.3 y only; GFR <60 excluded; one patient at 47 mL/min/1.73 m2.
- Patient count inconsistency: 53 in the Methods text, 51 in Table 2.
- Protein binding not measured (etoposide 90-95%, melphalan 60-90% bound per the authors).
