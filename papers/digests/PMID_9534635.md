# Edwards 1998: fuzzy logic closed-loop atracurium with Paragraph monitor

## Study
Clinical feasibility study of closed-loop atracurium infusion. 15 adult ASA 1-2 patients, surgery expected >90 min; 5 withdrawn for inadequate piezoelectric sensor function (PDF p1-3). The 10 completers: mean age 56 y (24-77), mean weight 66 kg (55-92) (printed p138). Controller: self-learning fuzzy logic on an HP 200LX palmtop, Vital Signs Paragraph monitor (piezoelectric, 60 mA TOF every 20 s), Graseby 3400 pump, atracurium 2 mg/mL. End point T1 (first twitch of TOF), setpoint 10% of baseline absolute T1; controller started when T1 recovered to 5-10%; median of last 3 readings, acted on every 60 s. Induction alfentanil 10 ug/kg (unit confirmed on rendered p137) plus propofol, propofol infusion 8-10 mg/kg/h, 67% N2O; atracurium loading dose 0.33 mg/kg; rescue 5 mg bolus if T1 >20% or fails to fall below 10%.

## Model and parameters
No pharmacokinetic model. Control performance (Table 1, printed p138, read on image): duration of control mean 78.2 min (SD 52.6); T1 error from setpoint mean -0.45% (SD 0.44, range -1.06 to +0.13); within-patient SD of error mean 2.40% (range 1.32-3.41); RMSD mean 2.47%; atracurium infusion rate mean 0.34 mg/kg/h (SD 0.14; individual means 0.13-0.67); mean within-patient SD of rate 0.16 mg/kg/h.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point is T1 (% of baseline, piezoelectric mechanomyography-type signal), held at 10%. No ke0, EC50, concentration or potency ratio reported. Not an opioid paper; the alfentanil is only part of the anaesthetic. Opioid audit: none.

## Covariates
None analysed. Weight used only to compute the 0.33 mg/kg loading dose.

## Caveats
Sensor sensitivity varied between patients (33% withdrawn). Infusion rates are per kg actual weight; baseline T1 is the patient's own absolute value. No drug concentrations measured.
