# Lalovic 2004: CYP3A and CYP2D6 in oxycodone oxidation (in vitro)

## Study
In vitro. Recombinant human P450 panel (lymphoblastoid: 1A1, 1A2, 2A6, 2B6, 2C8, 2C9, 2C19, 2D6, 2E1, 3A4; Supersomes
2D6*1, 3A4, 3A5 ± cytochrome b5); microsomes from 5 human livers (pooled Xenotech + HLM 141, 146, 148, 153) and 5
intestinal mucosa preparations (jejunal, duodenal, ileal). Oxycodone 0.1-800 µM; inhibition with ketoconazole (CYP3A)
and quinidine (CYP2D6) at 20/60/200 nM; LC-MS for noroxycodone and oxymorphone (LOQ 0.2 and 0.5 ng/mL of incubate).
One- or two-enzyme Michaelis-Menten models with competitive inhibition fitted in SAAM II.

Intestinal microsomes: noroxycodone formation 2-5-fold lower per mg than liver; oxymorphone formation at least 10-20-fold
lower than liver and undetectable with 100 nM quinidine. CYP2D6 accounted for 79-90% of O-demethylation CLint; CYP3A
> 90% of N-demethylation. Scaled hepatic clearance within 80-112% of Pöyhiä's IV CL of 0.78 L/min. CYP2D6 also
O-demethylates noroxycodone to noroxymorphone (CLint 2.5-fold that of oxycodone O-demethylation, Supersomes).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None (in vitro). Introduction cites oxymorphone µ affinity 3-5× morphine and oral oxymorphone 10× oral morphine potency
(cited, not measured). No MEAC, ke0, tPeak.

## Covariates
CYP3A5 content explained much of the inter-liver variation in N-demethylation; no CYP2D6 PM livers in the panel.

## Caveats
In vitro only; 5 livers, none CYP2D6 PM; intestinal kinetics at a single substrate concentration (10 µM); in vivo
extrapolation assumes microsomal scaling factors.
