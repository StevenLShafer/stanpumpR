# Cortínez 2007: rocuronium ke0 from time of peak effect vs non-parametric and parametric methods

## Study
- 15 adult ASA I patients (9 M / 6 F), age 29 (18-48) y, weight 65 (SD 7) kg, height 169 (7) cm (Table 1);
  within 20% of ideal body weight; no premedication.
- TIVA: fentanyl 3-5 ug/kg + propofol 2 mg/kg induction, intubation without relaxant; propofol 120 ug/kg/min.
- Rocuronium 0.15 mg/kg IV bolus (< 5 s), a deliberately submaximal dose. Evoked compound EMG of the adductor
  pollicis, train-of-four every 10 s (NMT Monitor 221); effect variable is the T4/T1 (TOF) ratio, %.
- NO plasma concentrations measured: Cp predicted from the mean PK parameters of Saldien 2003 (ref 9).
- ke0 by (1) tpeak method (Shafer-Gregg/Minto, Excel Solver, per patient, median taken), (2) non-parametric
  hysteresis minimisation (PKPD Tools), (3) parametric sigmoid Emax in NONMEM. Recovery phase also truncated
  in 20% steps to test robustness.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: adductor pollicis EMG, TOF ratio (T4/T1), not T1 twitch depression.
- Measured tpeak median 4.5 min (2.0-7.7) after 0.15 mg/kg; ke0 ~0.19-0.20 /min by all three methods
  (derived t1/2 ke0 = 0.693/0.19 = 3.6 min). These ke0 values are tied to Saldien's PK.
- Minimum TOF 26% (2-62); full recovery 98% (90-106); study period 20 (9-30) min.
- Two outlier tpeak-method ke0s (0.63, 0.72) attributed to EMG artefacts; without them range 0.09-0.26.

## Covariates
None.

## Caveats
- Predicted, not measured, concentrations (Saldien mean PK); ke0 not transferable to another PK set
  without re-deriving tPeak.
- n = 15, young healthy adults, submaximal dose; underpowered for method comparison (needs ~142).
- TOF-ratio end point differs from the T1 end point of most rocuronium PK-PD studies.
