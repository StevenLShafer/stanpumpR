# Chevassus 2004: single-dose diazepam/clonazepam and glucose metabolism

## Study
- 15 healthy men, 20-29 y (mean 22.5), weight 68.6 kg (55-81), BMI 22.3.
- Double-blind three-way crossover, 7-day washout: diazepam 10 mg, clonazepam 1 mg or placebo, each infused IV over 30 min (min -45 to -15), then FSIVGTT (0.5 g/kg glucose at 0, insulin 0.03 U/kg at ~ 20 min); minimal-model analysis.
- Diazepam, N-desmethyldiazepam, clonazepam by capillary GC-ECD; samples at -45, -15, 0, 15, 30, 60, 120, 180 min.

## Model and parameters
No PK model. Observed (mean ± SE): diazepam peak 370.1 ± 22.6 ng/mL at end of infusion; 229.7 ± 12.6 ng/mL 15 min later (time 0). Desmethyldiazepam 3.2 ± 0.9 (min -15) to 9.1 ± 1.6 ng/mL (min 180). Clonazepam 15.1 ± 2.0 ng/mL peak (min -15); 6.6 ± 0.5 and 4.9 ± 0.4 ng/mL at 0 and 15 min (Results, PDF p. 3). All results are mean ± SE (Methods, p. 3).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- "Effective therapeutic concentrations" quoted, not measured: 300 ng/mL (abstract) / 300-400 ng/mL (Discussion, citing Goodman & Gilman's therapeutics text, ref 14) for diazepam; 5-70 ng/mL for clonazepam (6.0 ng/mL called median and lower limit).
- Clonazepam (PDF pp. 1, 3, 5-7): sedation ARCI PCAG +593% vs placebo (n = 14, P = 0.001), BG (stimulation) -153% (P = 0.004), VAS asthenia-fatigue +70.3% (P = 0.002). AIR was negatively correlated with the plasma clonazepam concentration at time 0, just before the glucose bolus (Spearman r = -0.609, P < 0.05, n = 14; Results p. 5, Figure 4 p. 7). Whole-group mean AIR, Kg, Si and Sg did not differ from placebo (Table 1, p. 7: e.g. Kg 1.78 ± 0.37 vs 2.65 ± 0.31 x10^-2 /min).
- Clonazepam subgroup, post hoc (Results p. 5, Figure 5 p. 8; Wilcoxon tests): in the 7 subjects whose clonazepam concentration at time 0 exceeded 6.0 ng/mL, vs their placebo session: Kg -51.8% (P = 0.028), Si -41.2% (P = 0.018), Sg -49.9% (P = 0.028). Abstract values (p. 1), clonazepam vs placebo, mean ± SE: Kg 1.37 ± 0.3 vs 2.84 ± 0.60 x10^-2 /min; Si 2.18 ± 0.29 vs 3.71 ± 0.89 x10^-4 /(µU/mL)/min; Sg 1.80 ± 0.39 vs 3.59 ± 0.71 x10^-2 /min. Both Sg components also fell in this subgroup (BIE -38.1%, P = 0.018; GEZI -50.9%, P = 0.028; p. 6). A concentration-defined association after a single dose; not a concentration-response model.
- Diazepam increased ARCI PCAG (sedation) scores (+363% vs placebo) and VAS asthenia-fatigue; reduced cortisol at 60 min. No effect on AIR, Kg, Si or Sg; authors attribute this to diazepam falling below 300 ng/mL from 15 min after the infusion.

## Covariates
None.

## Caveats
Single dose, young men; the clonazepam metabolic effect rests on a post hoc subgroup of 7 split at 6.0 ng/mL, with no whole-group difference; the 300 ng/mL value is a secondary citation from a textbook, not a finding of this study.
