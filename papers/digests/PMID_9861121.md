# Schraag 1998: patient-demand remifentanil TCI after propofol-remifentanil anaesthesia

## Study
- Open single-group study, 30 consecutive male ASA I-II patients, elective orthopaedic surgery (Table 1: arthroscopic knee 7, ACL repair 9, partial knee replacement 9, shoulder 3, talus fracture 2). Age 41.6 yr (range 18-65), weight 72 kg (SEM 1.9), surgery 2.07 h (SEM 0.12). Excluded: age >65, BMI >30, chronic drug/alcohol abuse (PDF p1).
- Anaesthesia: oral clorazepate 0.25 mg/kg premedication; propofol TCI (Marsh pharmacokinetics) titrated to loss of consciousness from 2.5 ug/mL; remifentanil TCI (Minto 1997 pharmacokinetics, adjusted for age and weight), initial target 4 ng/mL, raised in 0.5 ng/mL steps for inadequate anaesthesia. Remifentanil 20 ug/mL via Graseby 3400 pump. Targets are PLASMA targets (Cpt); no remifentanil concentration was measured (p4).
- Emergence: propofol stopped ~10 min before end of surgery; remifentanil target lowered by 0.1 ng/mL steps until spontaneous ventilation >=10 breaths/min; that target held until PACU.
- Patient-demand TCI: double press of button within 1 s raises target 0.2 ng/mL; 2 min lockout; no demand in 30 min lowers target 0.2 ng/mL (first 4 h), then 0.2 ng/mL every 45 min (next 4 h). Observed 6 h in PACU. Pain on movement 0-10 VAS every 3 min until analgesia then every 15 min; adequate analgesia VAS <=3; Ramsay sedation; rectal diclofenac 100 mg once VAS <=3.

## Model and parameters
No new model. Programmed pharmacokinetics were from Minto 1997 (ref 6); the paper quotes its bias and performance error of 26% and 19% (PDF p3). No parameters are tabulated.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No ke0 was estimated. Quantities below are mean TCI plasma targets (ng/mL), not measured concentrations; Minto-1997-model Cpt in a population on residual propofol.
- Target at recovery of spontaneous ventilation (>=10 bpm), TC_sv: Table 2 (PDF p3) 1.03 (range 0.6-1.6; CI 0.76-1.48); Results text (PDF p2) gives 1.05 (CI 0.97-1.14), P=0.0001 vs analgesia. Discrepancy inside the paper (see open_questions).
- Target for adequate postoperative analgesia (VAS <=3, pain on movement), TC_VAS<=3: mean 2.02 ng/mL, range 1.2-2.8, 95% CI 1.43-2.69 (Table 2; text CI 1.87-2.16). Between-patient variation >200% (p2-3). This is the nearest thing to a MEAC in the paper; population male orthopaedic, no free/total or blood/plasma assay (none measured).
- Time to adequate analgesia 18.9 min (range 8-45; CI 10.2-28.5, Table 2; text CI 15.8-21.9). All but two patients within 30 min.
- At VAS<=3: ventilatory frequency 16.5 bpm (range 9-23), SaO2 98.2% (Table 3). Target correlated linearly with slowing of ventilatory frequency (r2 given as "90.7", sign garbled in the text layer) and with sedation (median Ramsay 2).
- t1/2 ke0 = 1.6 min is quoted from Egan 1996 (ref 14), a literature value, not measured here (p3; end point of that source not stated in this paper).
- Authors attribute the lower target at spontaneous ventilation (about half of the analgesic target) to residual propofol; calculated residual propofol target then 1.2 ug/mL.
- No potency/equianalgesic ratio. Side effects: nausea 26.6%, vomiting 10%, no hypoxaemia (SaO2 <95%) or apnoea, lowest rate 9 bpm.

## Covariates
None analysed (remifentanil targets age/weight-adjusted by the Minto kinetics in the pump).

## Caveats
n=30 male, short follow-up, no concentrations measured, targets depend on the Minto model; propofol residual confounds the ventilation target; authors say the safety conclusion is preliminary and needs controlled comparison with standard PCA.
