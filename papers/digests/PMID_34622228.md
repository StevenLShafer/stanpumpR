# Overgaard 2021b: semaglutide exposure-response, oral (PIONEER) vs subcutaneous (SUSTAIN)

Page convention: PDF p. 1 is the graphical abstract; article pages 1-9 are PDF pp. 2-10; STAR Methods e1-e4 are
PDF pp. 11-14.

## Study
Exposure-response comparison of oral semaglutide (PIONEER 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 9; 3, 7, 14 mg daily, 26 weeks) with
s.c. semaglutide (SUSTAIN 1, 2, 3, 6, SUSTAIN Japan OAD; 0.5, 1.0 mg weekly, week 30). Oral dosing instruction: up to
120 mL of water, at least 30 min before the first meal and other oral medicines (STAR Methods, PDF p. 12). PK
samples at scheduled weeks, timing at the investigator's discretion (PIONEER 2 aimed at 25 min post-dose); dosing
time of the last dose recorded. Oral PK dataset (STAR Methods): 10,527 observations from 2,345 subjects; 914 BLQ
set to LLOQ/2; 154 excluded. Exposure-response population 3,003 subjects incl. placebo (Table 1, PDF p. 3):
unmatched PIONEER age 59.4 (10.9) y, 44.8% female, weight 88.2 (21.9) kg, HbA1c 8.1%; propensity-matched PIONEER
n = 1,551 (87.2 kg) vs SUSTAIN n = 1,551 (86.3 kg). NONMEM 7.3, PsN 4.60, R 3.2.3.

## Model and parameters
Oral PK model prespecified from the clinical pharmacology model (Overgaard 2021, Clin Pharmacokinet): two
compartments, separate first-order oral and s.c. absorption, shared disposition; **all parameters fixed except
bioavailability**, which was estimated on PIONEER data (STAR Methods, PDF p. 12). Final covariates on exposure:
sex, race (Asian, White), body weight, upper GI disease, trial effects for PIONEER 3 and 5 (lower exposure);
log-normal IIV on F, proportional residual error. Parameter values are in Table S1 / Methods S1 (not archived).
Each subject's Cavg was the average steady-state concentration in a dosing interval at the target dose.
S.c.: the one-compartment model of Carlsson Petri 2018 (CL/F, V/F), same demographic covariates plus injection site.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- HbA1c change at week 26: Emax model with baseline HbA1c and trial population on the drug effect (STAR Methods).
  At 14.6 nM the propensity-matched prediction was -1.58% (oral) vs -1.62% (s.c.); body weight -3.77% vs -3.48%;
  nausea 11.1% vs 11.8%; vomiting 4.2% vs 4.2% (printed p. 4).
- Body weight change: Emax model with sex and baseline HbA1c on the effect; no plateau over the exposures seen.
- Nausea, vomiting: logistic-linear in exposure; females more GI events.
- CRP, triglycerides, systolic BP fall with exposure (Fig. 5).
- Highest baseline HbA1c (>= 9.1%) about -3% at high exposure, vs about -1% for < 7.5%. 19% of 14 mg subjects had
  exposures below the s.c. range but still about -1% HbA1c; 3% below the therapeutic range (< 0.5% expected).
- No ke0 or effect compartment (exposure is steady-state Cavg). No EC50 values printed in the main text.

## Covariates
Body weight is the most important covariate of exposure for both routes; sex, race, upper GI disease and trial had
minor effects. Exposure-response: baseline HbA1c, trial population (insulin, moderate renal impairment smaller
responses); route did not change the relationship.

## Caveats
Oral and s.c. data come from separate programmes; propensity matching only partly corrects. Total (protein-bound)
semaglutide was measured, not free (Limitations). Sparse sampling; F was the only re-estimated PK parameter.
Novo Nordisk employees; the paper does not state in so many words that PIONEER dosing was unsupervised, though the
trials were outpatient phase 3 programmes and the clinical pharmacology paper contrasts its supervised dosing with
phase III data.
