# Caldés 2009: ganciclovir population PK after IV ganciclovir and oral valganciclovir in solid-organ transplant

## Study
- Prospective trial, Hospital Universitari de Bellvitge, Barcelona, March 2004-Feb 2006; clinicaltrials.gov NCT00730769 (PDF p.1-2).
- 21 Caucasian adults with established CMV infection (pp65 antigenemia >=20 positive cells/10^5 PBMC) after kidney, liver or heart transplant; one excluded (pancytopenia), so n = 20 (Table 2, PDF p.2): 10 kidney, 5 liver, 5 heart; 10 men / 10 women. Exclusions included estimated GFR <10 mL/min.
- Table 2 (mean (SD), all 20): weight 66.2 (12.9) kg; age 55.7 (11.8) y; Cockcroft-Gault CLCR 57.0 (25.3) mL/min. By group CLCR: kidney 39.9, liver 75.1, heart 73.2 mL/min.
- Dosing: ganciclovir (Cymevene) 5 mg/kg IV over 1 h twice daily for 5 days, then valganciclovir (Valcyte) 900 mg orally twice daily for 16 days; both reduced for renal function per Table 1 (IV: CLCR >50 5 mg/kg q12h; 25-50 2.5 mg/kg q12h; <25 2.5 mg/kg q24h; oral: >=60 900 mg q12h; 40-59 450 mg q12h; 25-39 450 mg q24h; 10-24 450 mg q48h). Four patients deviated from the table (PDF p.3).
- Sampling: pre-dose and 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12 h after dosing on day 5 (IV) and day 15 (oral); to 24 h in one patient with CLCR 10-16 mL/min. 382 serum concentrations (190 IV, 192 oral); 6 below LLOQ.
- Assay: HPLC-UV, linear 0.5-30 ug/mL, LLOQ 0.5 ug/mL, precision <7%, accuracy <5%. Serum, not plasma; total drug (ganciclovir is not meaningfully protein bound, but the paper does not report binding).
- Analysis: NONMEM VI, FOCE-I; 200-sample bootstrap (PsN); valganciclovir dose converted to ganciclovir equivalent by x 0.720 (ratio of molecular weights) (PDF p.2).

## Model and parameters
Two-compartment open linear model, first-order absorption with lag time, parameterised in CL, V1, CLD (intercompartmental clearance), V2, ka, F, lag. Logit transform on F. Transit-compartment absorption did not improve the fit. Interoccasion variability on disposition parameters was not significant. Residual error combined additive plus proportional. Final-model CL = 7.49 L/h x (CLCR/57), CLCR in mL/min by Cockcroft-Gault (Table 3 footnote c), 57 = mean population CLCR. Final-model values from Table 3 (PDF p.4, printed 4819, confirmed on the page image); variability is variance (omega^2), not CV, unless stated.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No ke0, no effect-site model and no concentration-effect model. Exposure target taken from earlier work (Wiltshire 2005, the paper's ref 27): AUC 40-50 ug.h/mL suppressed viraemia during prophylaxis in D+/R- recipients, with only a weak tendency to more neutropenia at higher AUC; the paper adopts 45 ug.h/mL as the target (PDF p.2, p.8). The authors state no PK/PD relationship or target AUC has been established for treatment of CMV disease in SOT patients. Not an opioid: no MEAC, ke0, tPeak or potency ratio applies.

Simulations (Table 4, PDF p.7, text layer; units ug.h/mL, 66.2 kg): with the manufacturer's CLCR-banded dosing, mean AUC after IV ganciclovir ranged 25.4-128.7 and the percentage reaching target 3.5-100%; oral valganciclovir 38.1-190.4. Overexposure at CLCR <30 mL/min and (IV) underexposure at CLCR >=50, both routes.

## Covariates
- CLCR (Cockcroft-Gault, mL/min) on CL: linear, centred on 57; OFV fell by 33.478 univariately (p<0.001); IIV in CL fell from 68.19% to 32.71% CV (52.03% of IIV explained).
- Gender and tacrolimus co-administration reduced OFV by 9.196 and 6.354 univariately but added nothing once CLCR was in (they are confounded with kidney transplant and low CLCR).
- Body weight on CL: not significant, explained 4.74% of IIV; no pharmacokinetic parameter was related to weight (volumes included).
- The Methods do not say which weight was used in the Cockcroft-Gault equation.

## Caveats
- n = 20, 20 Caucasian adults, Cockcroft-Gault CLCR as the GFR surrogate; one patient with CLCR 10-16.
- F = 0.825 compares an IV day-5 profile with an oral day-15 profile (different days); the authors call it the first absolute F in CMV-infected SOT patients.
- ka reflects absorption and hydrolysis of the prodrug combined.
- Bootstrap CIs for V1 and V2 are wide (V2 11.57-52.42 L).
- Shrinkage <17% for all parameters.
