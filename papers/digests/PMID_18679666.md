# Moisés 2008: epidural lidocaine and MEGX in normal and GDM parturients at caesarean

## Study
- Single-centre (Ribeirão Preto, Brazil), non-randomised, unmatched comparison: 10 normal term parturients (group 1, control) and 6 with diet-controlled gestational diabetes (group 2; insulin-treated women excluded). All delivered by elective caesarean (urgent/emergency deliveries excluded) under single-shot epidural (PDF p2 / printed p1190).
- Demographics, median (25th-75th) (Table 1, PDF p3 / p1191): control age 28.50 y, GA 271.50 d, weight 69.25 kg (66.55-77.65), height 1.61 m, BMI 27.20; GDM age 34.00*, GA 270.50 d, weight 81.55* kg (75.00-90.15), height 1.58 m, BMI 31.86* (*P<0.05).
- Drugs (p1190): lidocaine 2% **without** vasoconstrictor 5 mL (100 mg) skin infiltration; then epidurally, in one injection, lidocaine 2% 10 mL (200 mg) **with** fentanyl 0.1 mg and bupivacaine 0.5% 15 mL (75 mg) **with epinephrine 1:200,000**.
- Sampling: maternal plasma at 1, 5, 15, 30, 45, 60 min and 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 14 h.
- Assay: HPLC-UV (205 nm), plasma; calibration lidocaine 0.5-5.0 µg/mL, MEGX 8-80 ng/mL; linearity to 20 µg/mL and 640 ng/mL; intra/interassay CV <15% (p1191). Total (not unbound) concentrations.
- Analysis: WinNonlin 4.0; lidocaine described as monocompartmental with no visible absorption phase; MEGX bicompartmental (p1192, p1194). Statistics GraphPad Prism 3.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD end point measured (blood pressure and heart rate said to be stable). The Discussion quotes, from Goodman & Gilman (ref 9), lidocaine plasma 1.5-6 µg/mL as therapeutic for systemic effects, 6-10 µg/mL occasionally toxic and >10 µg/mL frequently toxic (p1193); all observed Cmax were below the therapeutic range. Fentanyl was co-injected but not measured; no opioid PK/PD, MEAC, ke0 or potency data.

## Covariates
GDM vs normal pregnancy only (higher Cmax and AUC, lower CL/F and Vd/F, delayed and larger MEGX). Comparator for "reduced clearance": the 10 normal pregnant women in the same study, not non-pregnant women. GDM women were older and heavier. Albumin and α1-acid glycoprotein were higher in the GDM group (no values given).

## Caveats
- Very small, unmatched, non-randomised groups (10 vs 6); no non-pregnant arm.
- All parameters are apparent (/F) after epidural injection with co-injected epinephrine-containing bupivacaine, which slows absorption; with an absorption-limited curve, t½β (≈200 min) may reflect absorption, not elimination.
- CL/F uses the 200 mg epidural dose only; the additional 100 mg skin infiltration also reaches the circulation, so true apparent clearance of the total 300 mg dose would be up to 1.5x higher (derived: 300/200).
- Table footnote P<0.005 conflicts with text P<0.05; abstract Tmax 193.14 vs table 193.15.
- Total concentrations only; protein binding not measured.
