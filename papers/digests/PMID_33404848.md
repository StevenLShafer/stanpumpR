# Badaoui 2021: PBPK of foetal morphine exposure after maternal codeine, by CYP2D6 phenotype and gestational age

## Study
Simulation study (no patients). Simcyp v19.1 with Sim-Paediatric module, full-body PBPK. Codeine: first-order absorption (fa 0.9, Table I), morphine formed in situ by CYP2D6 (plus norcodeine by CYP3A4, codeine-6-glucuronide by UGT2B4/2B7). Morphine cleared by CYP3A4 (normorphine) and UGT2B7 (M3G, M6G). Compound files built from literature and back-calculation (Table I; the text layer scrambles the table, so individual inputs such as fa, fu, B/P and Vss are not transcribed here).
Verification: simulated vs observed AUC and Cmax in healthy-volunteer single-dose trials, 50-100 mg oral codeine (refs 27, 44; Supplemental Table 3, not in PDF). Mean fold error codeine AUC 1.2 (SD 0.42), Cmax 1.14 (0.16); morphine AUC 1.15 (0.29), Cmax 1.15 (0.49) (p.5). Criterion: within 2-fold.
Main simulation: 250 pregnant women aged 20-45 y (Sim-Pregnancy), one-third each CYP2D6 PM/EM/UM (n = 82/71/97 listed for abundance statistics), at gestational weeks 0 (mother only), 6, 12, 24, 36 (n = 2250 assessments), single 30 mg oral codeine at 09:00 fasted, 24 h. An independent cohort of 1000 pregnant women at week 15 was used for foetal vs maternal morphine Cmax. One-way ANOVA.

## Model and parameters
No fitted PK parameters are reported in the PDF beyond Table I inputs (compound files held in Simcyp). Outputs (simulated, 30 mg oral codeine, mean):

Foetal morphine AUC (ng/mL.h), Table IV: wk 6 0.614, wk 12 0.546, wk 24 0.455, wk 36 0.363.
Foetal morphine Cmax (ng/mL), Table IV: 0.225, 0.187, 0.137, 0.093.
Foetal codeine AUC: 287.7, 288.5, 277.1, 252.2; Cmax 111.0, 110.8, 104.1, 90.8.
Maternal morphine AUC, Table V (non-pregnant, 6, 12, 24, 36 wk): 0.092, 0.088, 0.085, 0.077, 0.068; Cmax 0.037, 0.036, 0.035, 0.031, 0.027.
Maternal codeine AUC: 657.7, 630.7, 600.6, 536.8, 474.8; Cmax 254.7, 244.6, 232.7, 205.7, 179.0.
Pooled: foetal morphine AUC 0.535 (SD 0.596) vs maternal 0.082 (0.088) ng/mL.h; foetal codeine AUC 277.6 vs maternal 580.1 ng/mL.h (p.5). Foetal morphine Cmax about 4.08-fold maternal plasma (R2 0.953, Fig. 5).
Text and abstract give the EM-cohort foetal morphine AUC both as 0.546 (abstract) and 0.516 (Discussion, p.9); UM 0.988; ratio 1.8.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No pharmacodynamic end point: exposure only (AUC, Cmax). No ke0, t½ke0, tPeak, hysteresis, MEAC/analgesic concentration or potency ratio is reported. Concentrations are plasma (maternal) and placental/foetal compartment in a PBPK model; assay not applicable (simulated).

## Covariates
CYP2D6 phenotype (PM / EM / UM, via enzyme abundance) and gestational age (physiology changes in Table III: weight 65.7 to 78.7 kg, cardiac output 301.4 to 400.6 L/h, albumin 45.0 to 34.5 g/L from non-pregnant to 36 wk; BSA 1.71 to 1.84 m2). Maternal CYP2D6 abundance rises 2.8-fold from wk 6 to wk 36. UGT2B7 assumed unchanged in pregnancy. Foetal morphine AUC: UM about 1.65-1.87x EM (1.87 at wk 6, 1.65 at wk 36); PM none. Foetal morphine AUC falls with gestational age (1.55-fold EM, 1.75-fold UM from wk 6 to wk 36) because rising volumes offset the CYP2D6 rise. Maternal codeine AUC in PM 1.1x-1.6x that of EM and UM.

## Caveats
Simulation only; no foetal data. Verified against non-pregnant healthy volunteers only, not pregnant women. Foetal CYP2D6 activity not modelled by phenotype (maternal phenotype only). Authors advise caution in applying results. Dose 30 mg single oral. Morphine AUC values are very small (<1 ng/mL.h), so absolute numbers are not clinical concentrations of an analgesic dose.
