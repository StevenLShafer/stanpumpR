# Stoeckel 1981: dose-dependent total ceftriaxone kinetics explained by saturable binding

## Study
Six healthy men, 23-29 y (weights not given). Ceftriaxone 150, 500 and 1500 mg IV over ~5 min (treated as bolus),
randomized crossover, 7 days apart. Plasma at 0, 10, 20, 30, 45 min and 1, 1.5, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 10, 24, 30, 48 h;
urine to 48 h. Binding by equilibrium dialysis (4 h, 37 °C) in each subject's plasma (drawn 2 weeks later), pooled
plasma of 17 adults, and HSA 40 g/L; Scatchard analysis, one site class. HPLC (ion-pair RP), plasma detection limit
0.4 µg/mL. Biexponential fits by NONLIN; free concentrations calculated from each subject's binding parameters (Eq. 5).

## Model and parameters
Total drug fitted biexponentially per dose; analysis by a linear compartment model judged inappropriate because
AUC(total) rose less than dose-proportionally.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None (kinetics and binding only). Not an opioid.

## Covariates
Dose / concentration via binding only.

## Caveats
n = 6 young men. Free concentrations are calculated from ex vivo equilibrium dialysis parameters, not measured in
vivo; the authors note equilibrium dialysis shifts total concentration during dialysis. Urinary fraction rose with
dose, which they attribute to assay sensitivity. ~44% of a labelled dose recovered in faeces (biliary route).
