# Storgaard 2026: Oromucosal THC (Sativex) population PK in older patients with poor appetite

## Study
PK sub-study of a triple-blind, randomised, placebo-controlled crossover trial (EudraCT 2021-002318-15,
NCT05503147), Copenhagen 2022-2024. 20 older medical patients after an acute admission: age >= 65 (median 77, range
66-94), BMI <= 30, poor appetite (SNAQ <= 14); 14 F / 6 M; weight median 56 kg (38-82), height 165 cm, fat-free mass
39 kg (27-55), eGFR 62 (39-98) mL/min/1.73 m2; 65% xerostomia (Table 1, PDF p.5, image-confirmed). Fasted 12 h.
**Sativex oromucosal spray (2.7 mg THC + 2.5 mg CBD per spray), two doses 4 h apart, each 2 sprays (5.2 mg THC; 2
patients) or 3 sprays (8.1 mg THC; 18 patients)**; meals 25 min after each dose. Venous serum, 9-14 samples per
patient **up to 8 h after the first dose (4 h after the second)**; UHPLC-MS/MS, LLOQ 0.25 ng/mL. 370 observations
(184 THC after one outlier, 185 THC-OH); 19.5% below LLOQ (Beal M3). NONMEM 7.5, Laplacian with interaction,
ADVAN13, log-transformed data; bootstrap 1000 samples.

## Model and parameters
THC: **one compartment, absorption through three transit compartments** (Figure 3: MTT = 3/ktr), elimination split
into formation clearance to THC-OH (CLF) and other pathways (CLO). THC-OH (normalised by molecular weight): two
compartments, cleared by CLMET. All parameters apparent (/F); THC-OH volumes unidentifiable in principle. Table 2
(PDF p.9, image-confirmed):

Bootstrap: 338 of 1000 runs successful (33.8%). Observed mean Cmax 1.85 (SD 0.15, n = 2) and 4.25 (SD 1.95, n = 18)
ug/L after 2 and 3 sprays; Cmax came after the first dose in 45% and the second in 55%. Metabolites exceeded THC
(THC-COOH up to about 30 times at peak).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD measured or modelled here. The Discussion cites an inhaled-THC PK-PD study with equilibration half-lives of
7.68-84.8 min (other work) and expects Sativex effects to lag accordingly. Not an opioid; no MEAC.

## Covariates
Screened (age, renal function eGFR/mGFR, body composition, weight with allometric 0.75/1): none retained; allometric
weight gave dOFV -3.31.

## Caveats
Authors: sampling ended 4 h after the last dose, so the terminal phase is not captured and a three-compartment model
could not be supported; the large CL/F (927 L/h, against 616 L/h oral in van Amerongen and 38.8 L/h absolute in
Heuberger) "might suggest a low bioavailability and/or that some distribution is still ongoing"; CLO/F poorly
estimated; low robustness (33.8% bootstrap); variability probably reflects variable bioavailability (F could not be
given IOV stably); most of the dose is probably swallowed rather than absorbed buccally; CBD co-administration.
Noticed: frail, mostly female, low-weight elderly; 18 of 20 on one dose level.
