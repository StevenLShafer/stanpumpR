# Zheng 2022: PBPK model of olanzapine in pregnancy

## Study
Physiologically based PK model (PK-Sim / MoBi, Open Systems Pharmacology), "middle-out" (PDF p. 2-5).
- Adult model: 18-compartment whole body; fu 0.07 (label); elimination fractions from in vivo data: renal excretion 7% of an oral dose, UGT1A4 fm 0.23 (glucuronide recovery, Kassahun 1997), CYP1A2 fm 0.50 (from the fluvoxamine DDI study via the Rowland-Matin equation), CYP2C8, CYP3A4 and FMO3 equal shares of the rest (p. 3-4).
- Test set: single 10 mg oral dose with and without fluvoxamine in adult men; validation set 10 population simulations (incl. children 10-18 y and smokers). GMFE Cmax 1.14, AUC 1.09; > 95% of concentrations within twofold (p. 8).
- Pregnancy model: Dallmann 27-compartment pregnancy physiology; virtual groups of 1000 women at fertilization weeks 6, 20, 34 (gestational age minus 2 weeks), vs non-pregnant women 20-40 y; 10 mg daily at steady state. Enzyme changes by fertilization week: CYP1A2 from caffeine (falls up to about 60% by late pregnancy), UGT1A4 from lamotrigine (rises about 85% on average), CYP3A4 from midazolam, FMO3 +58% late (nicotine N-oxide), CYP2C8 unchanged; albumin-based fu; absorption unchanged; liver blood flow unchanged (p. 4-5, 10).
- Table 2 (PDF p. 5), T1 / T2 / T3: fu 0.075 / 0.085 / 0.091; CLint CYP1A2 22.40 / 14.13 / 9.87 L/h; CYP3A4 0.89 / 1.28 / 1.64; CYP2C8 2.14 throughout; FMO3 4.05 / 4.05 / 7.11; UGT1A4 28.24 / 35.04 / 36.91 L/h.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD model. Reference range 20-80 ng/mL (AGNP) cited; authors note 20 ng/mL cannot be guaranteed throughout a late-pregnancy dosing interval at 10 mg (p. 8). Conclusion: dose adjustment "can hardly be recommended" if treatment was effective before pregnancy (abstract, p. 1).

## Covariates
Gestational age through enzyme activity, protein binding and physiology; CYP1A2 polymorphism not modelled; smoking not modelled in pregnancy.

## Caveats
- The abstract and Results say exposure changes are "less than 28%" / "not more than 28%"; true for Cmax and AUC (Table 5), but the predicted week-34 trough fall is 28.3% (Table 6). Minor.
- Predicted trough decrease is larger than Westin's TDM regression (-28% vs -9% at 34 weeks).
- Absorption in pregnancy unmodified; no pregnant Cmax/tmax data to validate absorption; fu during pregnancy calculated, not measured; CYP3A4 induction magnitude uncertain (a 60% assumption changes little: Cmax 26.3, AUC 488.1); fetal exposure not addressed (p. 10-11).
- Journal volume 12 is the 2021 volume; article published January 2022.
