# Terret 2000: dose and time dependencies of 5-fluorouracil pharmacokinetics

PubMed metadata were empty in the context file; the citation, DOI and pages are taken from the PDF itself

## Study
- 21 patients with advanced colorectal carcinoma (14 M, 7 F), Table I (PDF p 2, printed p 271, confirmed on the
  rendered page): age median 65 y (40-79), BSA 1.71 m² (1.46-2.00), weight 68 kg (48-97), serum creatinine
  81 µmol/L (53-177), proteinemia 71 g/L (55-81), albuminemia 38 g/L (27-44), bilirubin 9.4 µmol/L (6.2-33.4),
  PMNC DPD activity 140 pmol/min/mg protein (44-392). Liver metastatic involvement: none 5, ≤25% 9, >25-≤50% 5,
  >50-≤75% 2. (The text layer scrambles this table; the rendered page is the authority.)
- Regimen LV5FU2, first cycle: folinic acid 200 mg/m² over 2 h, then 5-FU 400 mg/m² over 10 min, then
  600 mg/m² over 22 h; repeated on day 2.
- Sampling (peripheral vein, opposite arm): pre-dose; 0, 0.25, 0.5, 1, 2, 6, 12, 21 h after end of first
  10-min infusion; 0, 1, 21 h after the second. Plasma, HPLC-UV 265 nm; LOQ 50 ng/mL; inter-assay CV 9.2% and
  8.3% at 500 and 2500 ng/mL.
- NONMEM V level 1.1, FO method; proportional IIV; additive + proportional residual error (10.4% and
  0.062 mg/L, p 274).
- 13 covariates tested (weight, height, BSA by DuBois, sex, age, protein, albumin, bilirubin, liver metastasis
  score 0-3, WHO PS, PMNC DPD, Cockcroft-Gault CrCl, clock time).

## Model and parameters
Two-compartment model with Michaelis-Menten elimination from the central compartment (Table II, PDF p 4,
printed p 273: linear elimination +228 OFV, one-compartment nonlinear +92 OFV, both P < .0005). Day-1 means,
Table III (PDF p 7, printed p 276, confirmed on the rendered page). Parameters are absolute (not per kg or per m²);
time unit is hours.

Other values: mean ± SD Cmax (end of 10-min infusion) 31.3 ± 8.9 mg/L and pre-end-of-22-h concentration
0.178 ± 0.046 mg/L (p 274, text layer). Authors note Vmax/Km ≈ 250 L/h approximates linear clearance at
concentrations far below Km (p 277), consistent with 235 L/h from a prior 5-day infusion study (their ref 8).
Table IV alternative models: both constant Km 6.05, Vmax 1260 (ΔOFV 73.8, P < .0005); Km constant / Vmax varying
(ΔOFV 21.7; Vmax 1520 day 1, 1240 day 2; Km 7.24); both varying (ΔOFV 14.9, P < .001; Km 5.83/10.4, Vmax 1370/1490).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No concentration-effect, toxicity or efficacy relationship modelled. Not an opioid paper.

## Covariates
- Vmax = 751 · BSA · (1 + 0.068 · META) mg/h (META 0-3 liver-involvement score). Individually DPD and
  proteinemia also correlated with Vmax but dropped from the final model. No covariate on Km or k21 (day 1).
- Day 2: Km rises (6.08 → 9.83 mg/L; comparator is the same patients on day 1), negatively related to PMNC DPD.

## Caveats
- Cancer patients, small n (21), FO estimation; IIV on k12 fixed to zero.
- External validation (Fig 3) under-predicted later concentrations for 8-h and 96-h infusions (time dependency).
- No circadian effect detected, but only 27 of 176 samples fell 12 PM-9 AM (sic, p 278).
- Text-layer Table III misaligns the IIV column (puts 20% on Vmax and 22% on its own line); the rendered page
  shows k21 27%, Vmax 20%, Km 22%, matching the abstract.
