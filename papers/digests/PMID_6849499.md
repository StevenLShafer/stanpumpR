# Divoll 1983: absolute bioavailability of oral and IM diazepam by age and sex

## Study
- 22 healthy volunteers aged 20-78 y in four groups: 5 young men, 6 young women, 5 elderly men, 6 elderly women; 3 elderly on stable cardiovascular drugs.
- Randomised three-way crossover, ≥ 3 weeks apart: diazepam 5 mg as rapid IV injection, 5 mg oral tablet fasting, 5 mg deltoid IM (2.5 cm needle).
- Venous samples over 24 h then every 24-72 h to 336 h (IV) or 168 h (oral, IM). GC-ECD (diazepam, desmethyldiazepam).
- Sums of exponentials; Vd (area), t½, CL from IV. F = extravascular AUC / IV AUC over the same sampling duration (truncated in the elderly).

Overall oral F 94%, overall oral Tmax 0.9 h (abstract, Discussion); oral peak always within 2.5 h. Age and sex had no significant effect on oral kinetics (ANOVA). IM F lower in women (ANOVA F = 3.60, P < 0.05); slow, incomplete IM absorption in 3 young and 1 elderly woman.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None.

## Covariates
Vd larger in women and rises with age in both sexes; CL falls with age in men (0.46 → 0.24 mL/min/kg, P < 0.05), not significantly in women. No equations.

## Caveats
- Small groups (5-6); bioavailability in the elderly from truncated AUCs; some F > 100% from between-occasion clearance changes (authors).
