# Zhou 2026: gabapentin PK/PD with an OCT2-dependent effect compartment

## Study
- Pooled two Brazilian studies (Ribeirão Preto), n = 94: Study 1, single 300 mg IR capsule with rich plasma sampling
  and VAS (lumbar disc herniation patients); Study 2, 65 chronic neuropathic pain patients on 600-3600 mg/day IR,
  sparse sampling. 568 plasma samples, 392 pain scores.
- 53 F / 41 M, 53 ± 11 y, 81.97 ± 17.34 kg, BMI 29.9; SCr 0.92 ± 0.41 mg/dL; CKD-EPI 2021 eGFR 90.29 ± 22.79
  mL/min/1.73 m² (12% missing, imputed by median).
- SLC22A2 c.808G>T (OCT2): GG 79, GT 15. SLC22A4 c.1507C>T (OCTN1): CC 42, CT 39, TT 13.
- Assay linear 0.2-14 µg/mL (LLOQ 0.2); BLQ 9.15%, left-censored. Monolix 2024R1 (SAEM); bootstrap n = 500;
  Simulx simulations at 5 weeks.

## Model and parameters

84.85 mL/min/1.73 m², weighted mean). PD: inhibitory Imax on VAS driven by effect-compartment concentration;
ke1 (in) and ke2 (out) separate rate constants. Table 1 (estimate, %RSE; bootstrap median, 95% CI):

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: VAS pain 0-10 (neuropathic pain). Delay of pain relief behind plasma peak (Supplementary Fig. S1) handled
  by an effect compartment with ke1 (in) estimated and ke2 (out) **fixed at 1 h⁻¹**; IC50 fixed at 263.11 ng/mL and
  Imax at 1. The paper gives a reason for fixing IC50 (identifiability; study aimed at transport, not potency); no
  separate reason is given for fixing ke2.
- (Derived) With ke1 ≠ ke2, the effect-site time course is governed by ke2 (t½ = 0.69 h at 1 h⁻¹); ke1 scales the
  effect-site level relative to plasma (Ce,ss/Cp = ke1/ke2 = 0.53 for GG, 0.05 for GT).
- No MEAC; not an opioid; no potency ratio.

## Covariates
eGFR (CKD-EPI 2021) on CL/F, exponent 1.34; OCT2 c.808 GT on ke1 (≈10-fold lower). Sex, age, weight, BMI, SCr,
OCTN1 not retained.

## Caveats
Fixed ke2 and IC50 make the delay and potency assumptions rather than estimates; ke1 RSE 56% and IIV SD 2.11;
only 15 GT carriers; mixed single/multiple dose and rich/sparse data; VAS treated as continuous; Brazilian cohort.
