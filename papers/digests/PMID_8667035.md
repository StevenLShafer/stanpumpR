# Chisari 1995: in vitro activity of trospectomycin against anaerobes

## Study
In vitro susceptibility study (Institute of Microbiology, University of Catania, Italy; presented at the 9th
Mediterranean Congress of Chemotherapy, 1994). 151 recent anaerobic isolates from infections of the female genital
tract (Table 1, PDF p3): *Bacteroides fragilis* 15, *B. urealyticus* 5, *B. vulgatus* 5, *Bacteroides* spp. 15,
*Prevotella melaninogenica* 6, *Porphyromonas asaccharolytica* 7, *Mobiluncus* spp. 3, *Peptococcus niger* 3,
*P. variabilis* 9, *Peptococcus* spp. 30, *Peptostreptococcus anaerobius* 5, *P. asaccharolyticus* 3,
*Peptostreptococcus* spp. 25, *Propionibacterium* spp. 7. Identification by API ID 32A.

Drugs: trospectomycin (the subject), clindamycin and ampicillin as comparators, in aqueous solution.
Method (PDF p3): microtitre serial dilution in Wilkins-Chalgren broth, anaerobic chamber (10% H2, 10% CO2, 80% N2),
final inoculum 10^5 CFU/mL, read at 48 h (72 h for *Mobiluncus*), in duplicate. MBC criterion: 99.9% reduction of
the original inoculum (after Pearson 1987). No breakpoint interpretation, no pharmacokinetics.

MBCs equal to the MIC or twice it), and the MICs (1-4 mg/L) lie in trospectomycin's Table 2 ranges, not

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: broth MIC (and, for trospectomycin only, MBC at 99.9% kill). No clindamycin MBC, no time-kill, no
concentration-effect model. Not an opioid paper.

## Covariates
None.

## Caveats
- Small strain counts (3-30 per taxon); genital-tract isolates from one Italian centre, early 1990s.
- MBCs (trospectomycin) are for a single strain per species, not a distribution.
- Clindamycin MICs reflect pre-2000 susceptibility; *Bacteroides* resistance has since risen sharply (the repo cites
  Boiten 2024, MIC90 > 256 mg/L).
- Protein-free broth: the MICs are free-drug values.
- Sponsor (Upjohn, maker of trospectomycin and clindamycin) acknowledged.

## Verification against stanpumpR
- `inst/help/models/antibiotics.md:58`: the help implies this paper holds clindamycin MBCs against anaerobes that
  could not be read. The tables are legible (rendered PDF pp4-5), and the paper gives clindamycin **MICs only**;
  its single MBC table is trospectomycin. It also has no streptococci (only anaerobic cocci). **Discrepancy** in the
  description of the source, not in any number: nothing in the code takes a value from this paper.
- `R/antibioticMbc.R:272-292` (clindamycin band; does not cite this paper): the *Bacteroides fragilis* group row
  (core, MBC90 1.6 mg/L from Levison 1977) is consistent with these MICs (MIC90 0.5 mg/L, free drug), which put the
  1995 isolates well inside the susceptible range; the paper cannot confirm or refute the MBC itself. The row
  `Anaerobes other than Bacteroides (... peptostreptococci)` is excluded from the band because clindamycin is not
  reliably bactericidal (Yagi 2003); this paper has no kill data to test that. Its *Peptococcus* spp. MIC90 of
  8 mg/L (n=30) is notably higher than the other anaerobic cocci, which supports keeping that group out.
- Verdict: **discrepancy** (the help's characterisation of the source); no value in code depends on it.
