# Andrew 2008: maternofetal PBPK model of midazolam in pregnancy

## Study
Conference paper (4 pages; PDF p1-4 = printed 5454-5457). No new subjects: a whole-body PBPK model (20 maternal,
16 fetal compartments, well-stirred, flow-limited; MATLAB) with physiology from Brown 1997 interpolated by
gestational week and midazolam partition coefficients from Björkman 2005. Fetal metabolism set to zero; placental
transfer clearances set equal both ways to an arbitrary value. Validation against digitised published mean data:
- Allonen 1981: 6 men, 0.075 mg/kg IV bolus (Fig. 2, PDF p2).
- Kanto 1984: 6 women after caesarean, 0.075 mg/kg IV (Fig. 3, PDF p3), fetal circuit disconnected.
- Kanto 1983: 6 women after caesarean, 0.075 mg/kg IV (Fig. 4, PDF p3), fetal circuit disconnected.
Hepatic intrinsic clearance was back-calculated from each study's reported plasma clearance, assuming purely
hepatic clearance (the hepatic-flow value used is not stated).

Ratios stated in text: Kanto 1984 CLint 14 times, Kanto 1983 8 times the Allonen value (derived: 14.55 and
8.10). The text says the Kanto 1984 comparison is to "Figure 1"; the Allonen simulation is Figure 2 (paper's slip).
Fig. 2 prediction under-predicts the Allonen data but is within two-fold. Fig. 5 (PDF p4) shows simulated maternal
and fetal venous, muscle and adipose profiles at 38 weeks, 72 kg (illustrative; arbitrary placental clearance).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No ke0, EC50 or effect data. Not an opioid.

## Covariates
Gestational week (organ volumes and flows), maternal weight (linearly scaled to match each study's mean).

## Caveats
Preliminary; no fitting or statistics; data digitised from printed figures; n = 6 per source study. The
"increased clearance" claim is against MALE subjects (Allonen 1981), not nonpregnant women; the authors note
sex differences in midazolam clearance are debated. Post-caesarean (surgery, blood loss, delivery) is not
pregnancy. A back-calculated CLint of 107 L/min implies clearance at the hepatic-flow ceiling, where the
back-calculation is very sensitive to the assumed liver flow. Fetal side unvalidated.
