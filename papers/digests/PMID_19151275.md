# Xu 2009: propofol-remifentanil C50 and BIS in Chinese patients

## Study
Multicentre (5 Beijing hospitals) prospective quantal-response study, 405 ASA I-II unpremedicated Chinese adults (97 M / 308 F), age 18-65 (Table 1: mean 39.5 y, SD 10.1; height 163.7 cm, SD 6.8; weight 62.1 kg, SD 10.5). All patients got propofol; only 301 got the remifentanil step (all Peking Union patients and 51 Beijing Gynecology and Obstetrics patients did propofol only). Propofol by Diprifusor (Marsh model, software 2.0) starting at predicted blood 1.2 ug/mL, +0.3 ug/mL every 30 s until OAA/S = 1 (loss of consciousness, LOC). Propofol then held 3 min and remifentanil TCI (SLGO pump, Minto model) started at predicted blood 2.0 ng/mL, +0.3 ng/mL every 30 s until no purposeful movement to a tetanic stimulus (50 Hz, 80 mA, 4 s, wrist). BIS (A-2000, v3.22, Quattro sensor) recorded manually. Probit (quantal) analysis gave C05/C50/C95. No drug concentrations were measured.

## Model and parameters
No PK model was fitted; PK come from the pump models (Marsh for propofol, Minto 1997 for remifentanil), whose ke0 values are not stated in the paper. Concentrations are the pump-displayed "predicted blood" and "effect-site" values.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- Propofol (alone, no opioid): Ce50 for LOC (OAA/S 1) 2.2 ug/mL; BIS at LOC 58 (Table 4). The authors compare with Milne 2003 (Caucasians, same Diprifusor, same 30 s steps): Ce50 2.8, Ce95 4.1 ug/mL, BIS50 71 (text, PDF p.4-5), and Irwin 2002 (Chinese, 2-min steps): Ce50 2.66, BIS50 65. These are quoted from the discussion, not re-read.
- Remifentanil (on top of a propofol Ce held at the LOC value): Ce50 3.3 ng/mL for no purposeful movement to a 50 Hz tetanus (Table 5); BIS at that point 65, measured after the stimulus, so not directly comparable with LOC BIS (authors' own caveat).
- Respiratory end point (Table 3): Ce50 for respiratory depression 2.1 ng/mL (Cp 3.1); most patients were depressed before losing response; 22 needed mask ventilation.
- Opioid audit (remifentanil): no MEAC/analgesic minimum concentration as such; the tetanus C50 above is an analgesic-type end point defined as quantal non-response, in a propofol background (so it is not a remifentanil-alone value), on predicted (not assayed) concentrations. No ke0, t1/2ke0, tPeak or hysteresis reported (Minto model's own ke0 used by the pump, not printed). No equianalgesic or potency ratio.
- Not stated: gamma/slope of the probit fits (only C05/C50/C95 and Figures 1-3).

## Covariates
No covariate modelling. Text states no sex difference in propofol Ce at LOC; no data shown.

## Caveats
Concentrations are pump predictions (Marsh/Minto), no blood sampling; propofol Ce at LOC is partly a function of the pump's ke0 and of the dose-increment rate (authors cite that longer intervals give higher C50). Remifentanil step was done at a propofol concentration already causing LOC, so values depend on that background. Table 5 n = 301. BIS after stimulus. Funded by AstraZeneca. Table 1 age range prints 18-60 though inclusion was to 65 (text layer; ranges for height/weight scrambled in extraction).
