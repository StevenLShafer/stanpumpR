# Struys 2000: plasma vs effect-site TCI for propofol

## Study
Randomised three-arm comparison, 120 healthy women (ASA I-II, age 18-60, weight 70-130% of ideal), ambulatory gynaecologic surgery, n = 40 per group (Table 1, PDF p.4: age 37 +/- 11, 39 +/- 12, 35 +/- 8 yr; weight 62 +/- 10, 62 +/- 10, 61 +/- 8 kg; height 164 +/- 7, 166 +/- 6, 166 +/- 6 cm). No premedication, no other drugs during the 12 min. Each patient received a 12-min propofol TCI at a target of 5.4 ug/mL (chosen from Wakeling et al.; LOC in about 95% at that level per the authors). All groups used the Marsh kinetics (the Diprifusor set); propofol concentrations were NOT measured, so every concentration is a model prediction. Control software was RUGLOOP (adapted from STANPUMP) driving a Graseby 3500 pump. Effect measured by BIS (Aspect A-1000, version 3.2, 10 s updates); LOC tested every 5 s; also ventilation (apnea = CO2 interval >10 s), blood pressure every 30 s, HR, SpO2.
- Group I: plasma-targeted (Diprifusor-style).
- Group II: effect-site targeted with ke0 = 0.20 /min (Billard et al., ref 18), t1/2 ke0 3.5 min per abstract; with Marsh kinetics this predicts a time to peak effect of 4.5 min after a bolus.
- Group III: effect-site targeted with ke0 solved so that the time to peak effect after a bolus is 1.6 min (Schnider et al. 1999, ref 19). The text gives ke0 = 1.21 /min (t1/2 ke0 34 s per abstract) for the Marsh kinetics.

## Model and parameters
No new model fitted. Structural: Marsh three-compartment kinetics (parameters not tabulated here) plus an effect compartment, driven by the Shafer-Gregg effect-site targeting algorithm as implemented by Jacobs and Williams. Time-to-peak-effect method: ke0 is found by nonlinear regression so the effect-site concentration after a bolus peaks at the stated time (adapted from STANPUMP).

Results, group means +/- SD (Tables 2 and 3, PDF p.4, image-checked):
- LOC, time (s): I 90 (range 44-601); II 68 (45-101); III 71 (43-110). BIS at LOC 67 +/- 12, 78 +/- 11, 77 +/- 11. Calculated Ce at LOC: II 1.8 +/- 0.7, III 4.7 +/- 0.6 ug/mL. Propofol given to LOC (mL, concentration of the syringe not stated): 11.4 +/- 3.9, 20.4 +/- 3.6, 11.7 +/- 2.0.
- tpeak (observed time to lowest BIS, within 2 min of LOC), s: I 218 +/- 86; II 116 +/- 21; III 120 +/- 21.
- teq (calculated time for Ce to equilibrate with Cp), s: II 330 +/- 36; III 130 +/- 17. terror = teq - tpeak: II 207 +/- 73 s; III 9 +/- 15 s (P < 0.0001).
- Maximum calculated Cp (ug/mL): I 5.4; II 14.2 +/- 0.4; III 7.5 +/- 0.2. BIS at tpeak: 39 +/- 11, 27 +/- 11, 41 +/- 16.
- Time to lowest mean arterial pressure (s): I 201 +/- 70; II 165 +/- 66; III 329 +/- 140.
- Apnea: I 9/40, II 34/40, III 17/40; 8 group II patients needed manual ventilation.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: BIS (EEG) for tpeak; LOC to verbal command for onset; mean arterial pressure for the "side effect".
- tPeak: observed BIS tpeak after effect-site targeting was 116 s (ke0 0.20) and 120 s (tPeak-derived ke0) against a calculated teq of 330 s and 130 s. The 1.6 min (96 s) bolus tPeak is a different quantity from this tpeak, which is the time to the lowest BIS during a 12 min TCI with an overshoot. Group III's calculated teq of 130 s matched the observed 120 s within 9 s; group II's did not (207 s error).
- Hysteresis: BIS lags Cp; effect-site targeting speeds onset (116-120 s vs 218 s plasma-targeted). Peak blood pressure effect came later than peak EEG effect in groups II and III (P < 0.05), so effect-site targeting did not worsen hypotension.
- The authors state that ke0 depends on the kinetic model it was estimated with, so a literature ke0 should not be mixed with different kinetics.
- No EC50, Emax, gamma, MEAC or potency ratio reported. Opioid audit: not an opioid paper; none.

## Covariates
None analysed. The Marsh model scales by body weight only (not tabulated here).

## Caveats
Concentrations are predicted, not measured. Healthy women only, 18-60 yr, one target (5.4 ug/mL), 12 min. BIS is smoothed over a 30 s window, so it lags about 15 s (authors), which may lengthen tpeak. ke0 1.21 was found for BIS/EEG with Marsh kinetics only; the authors say future work should find the best combined PK-PD model. The text and Table 2 disagree on group II's LOC range (45-104 vs 45-101 s).
