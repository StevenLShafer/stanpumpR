# Wright 1994: Cumulative characteristics of atracurium and vecuronium

## Study
12 healthy volunteers aged 19-32 yr (non-obese, no medication), alternately allocated to atracurium (n = 6; 4 M/2 F;
27 +/- 3 yr, 77.6 +/- 8.5 kg, 177 +/- 10 cm) or vecuronium (n = 6; 5 M/1 F; 24 +/- 3 yr, 79.7 +/- 14.1 kg,
178 +/- 10 cm) (Table 1, PDF p.3, mean +/- SD). Each studied on three occasions at least a week apart under
propofol 2-3 mg/kg induction, then **nitrous oxide 60% with isoflurane 1% end-tidal** (isoflurane for more than 45
min before the relaxant). Occasion 1: two-dose dose-response (vecuronium 10 ug/kg first dose, then a second aimed at
90% block) to estimate each subject's ED95. Occasions 2 and 3: single bolus of **1.2 and 3.0 x ED95**. Adductor
pollicis twitch (TOF every 12 s, first twitch normalised to final recovery value). Radial arterial samples at 1, 2,
4, 8, 16, 32, 64, 128 min and at about 25, 50, 75% recovery. Vecuronium and 3-desacetylvecuronium by GC with
nitrogen-phosphorus detection (sensitivity 5 ng/mL, CV 15% at 10 ng/mL); atracurium by HPLC. Per-subject PK-PD
(effect compartment) fits in NONMEM, two- or three-compartment PK chosen by AIC, fitted separately for each dose.

## Model and parameters
The PK parameters (CL, volumes) are deliberately **not reported** (p.64: assay could not reach 128 min after the small
dose; atracurium Vss needs tissue elimination). No ke0 or Hill coefficient values are tabulated. Results that are
reported (Table 2, PDF p.4, image-confirmed; mean +/- SD, ranges in parentheses; n = 6 per drug):

| Dose 1.2 ED95 (ug/kg) | 157 +/- 31 (120-198) | 26.2 +/- 9.3 (13.2-39.6) |
| Dose 3.0 ED95 (ug/kg) | 393 +/- 77 (300-495) | 65.5 +/- 23.3 (33.0-99.0) |

The star on "Clinical duration" and on "Time to 75% recovery 1.2 ED95" marks a between-drug difference (P<0.05).
Text p.62 says the ED50 of atracurium was 6.2 times vecuronium's; the table gives ED95 only, ratio 131/21.8 = 6.0
(derived). Plasma concentrations after the large dose were about 2.5 times those after the small dose throughout for
vecuronium (linear PK; Fig. 2); for atracurium the ratio exceeded 2.5 at 1 and 4 min only.

Effect-compartment concentrations (Table 3, PDF p.7, image-confirmed; ng/mL, mean +/- SD; vecuronium 1.2 / 3.0 ED95):
- Parent only (metabolite ignored): Ce25% 112 +/- 29 / 113 +/- 31; Ce50% 88 +/- 23 / 83 +/- 23; Ce75% 65 +/- 18 /
  55 +/- 15 (P<0.05).
- Potency-adjusted sum (vecuronium + 0.8 x 3-desacetylvecuronium): 119 +/- 31 / 124 +/- 33; 94 +/- 25 / 95 +/- 24;
  72 +/- 19 / 66 +/- 17 (no significant difference).
- 3-desacetylvecuronium Ce: 5.7 +/- 2.2 / 14.5 +/- 3.9; 6.2 +/- 2.2 / 14.5 +/- 3.8; 6.3 +/- 2.1 / 12.9 +/- 3.6
  (small-dose values partly imputed: metabolite below detection in 4 of 6).
- Atracurium parent: Ce25% 303 +/- 36 / 268 +/- 39; Ce50% 237 +/- 31 / 218 +/- 35; Ce75% 170 +/- 30 / 167 +/- 30.

Metabolite model assumptions (p.62): formed from (not to) vecuronium, one compartment with volume = vecuronium V1,
plus a component equal to 1% of the predicted vecuronium concentration; same ke0 as vecuronium; potency 80% of
vecuronium (from Caldwell et al., ref 12). Varying the metabolite volume 0.2-5 x V1 did not change the results.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: adductor pollicis first-twitch tension (TOF). Per-subject ke0 and gamma were estimated but are **not
reported**. Ce at 25/50/75% recovery as above. Main findings: vecuronium was cumulative (25-75% recovery time rose
8.2 min with the larger dose) and atracurium slightly so (2.4 min). Recovery-phase half-life rose similarly for both,
so the shift from distribution to elimination explained atracurium's cumulation but only part of vecuronium's.
Parent-only vecuronium Ce75% fell with dose (apparent nonstationarity); once 3-desacetylvecuronium was counted at 80%
potency the potency-adjusted Ce were the same for both doses. Not an opioid; no MEAC.

## Covariates
None modelled (dose comparison within subject).

## Caveats
n = 6 per drug, healthy young volunteers. Isoflurane potentiation is acknowledged (p.66); results may differ without
an inhaled agent. The metabolite content of the administered vecuronium was not measured; small-dose metabolite
concentrations were imputed in 4 of 6. No PK parameters, ke0 or gamma reported, so the paper cannot supply a model.
