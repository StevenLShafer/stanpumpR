# Childress 2025: ODX vs OROS (Concerta) methylphenidate ER bioequivalence

## Study
Two single-dose, open-label, randomized, 4-period, 2-treatment fully replicated crossover bioequivalence trials
(Worldwide Clinical Trials, San Antonio), healthy adults 18-45 y, BMI 18-32 kg/m2, weight >= 59 kg, fasted
(10 h overnight fast, 4 h after dose). Washout >= 7 days.
- Trial 1: ODX-methylphenidate ER (Relexxii) 72 mg x1 vs OROS-methylphenidate ER (Concerta) 2 x 36 mg; 60 enrolled,
  55 completed all 4 periods, 56 in PK analysis; 109 ODX and 111 OROS profiles (Table 1 n).
- Trial 2: ODX 54 mg vs OROS 54 mg; 36 enrolled, 32 completed, 34 in PK analysis; 67 and 67 profiles.
- Sampling: predose, every 30 min to 10 h, then 11, 12, 14, 16, 24, 36 h.
- Assay: plasma (K2-EDTA, iced), liquid/liquid extraction, LC-MS/MS (API 4000, normal-phase HPLC column),
  transition m/z 234-84, methylphenidate-D3 internal standard; LLOQ 0.25 ng/mL. No chiral separation is described,
  so the analyte is methylphenidate without enantiomer distinction (the paper does not say "total" or "d/l").
- Analysis: noncompartmental; BLQ set to 0. Demographics in supplementary Table S1 (not in this PDF).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No PD measured (healthy volunteers; authors note PK-effect correspondence could not be assessed). No ke0,
EC50 or therapeutic concentration. Safety only (TEAEs, Table S2).

## Covariates
None analysed.

## Caveats
Healthy fasted adults, single dose; industry-funded (Osmotica); OROS overcoat fraction (22%) is not given in this
paper. Assay not stated as enantioselective. Table 1/2 layout errors noted above. Mean curves are in Figure 1 only
(read here from the tables, not digitized).
