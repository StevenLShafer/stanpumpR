# Doufas 2001: automated responsiveness test (ART) and propofol conscious sedation

## Study
- 20 healthy volunteers aged 27 +/- 6 y, 66 +/- 10 kg, 169 +/- 8 cm, 7 men (PDF p4, printed 588). Age limit 18-40, fasted >=4 h.
- Each volunteer studied twice, propofol with 50% N2O (always first) then, after >=1 h recovery, propofol with air/30% O2.
- Delivery: Harvard 2 pump with a three-compartment model from Schnider 1998 (ref 9), targeting effect-site concentration; the driver's predicted plasma and effect-site concentrations were "virtually identical" to StanPump (version May 1996). Steady state taken as 9-15 min of each 15-min cycle (model said ~4 min to equilibrate).
- Stepwise target: propofol-air starts at 1.5 mcg/mL and rises 0.5 mcg/mL per cycle; propofol-N2O starts at 0 and rises 0.25 mcg/mL per cycle. Stop at loss of consciousness (OAA/S sum <=10/20), red alert, or loss of ART response.
- ART: computer voice asks to press a button, repeated up to 5 times over 10 s with louder voice and vibration; no press in 10 s = non-response. OAA/S scored after ART at 9 and 15 min.
- Venous blood propofol sampled at 9 and 15 min of each step and at ART failure, loss of consciousness and recovery; HPLC (modified Plummer 1987), samples stored at 4 C.
- Steady-state check: plasma at 9 vs 15 min differed 0.0 +/- 0.2 mcg/mL (P=0.85).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End points: ART non-response (no button press in 10 s) and loss of consciousness (OAA/S <=10/20, i.e. no response to name or mild prodding).
- Measured plasma propofol (mcg/mL, mean +/- SD), Table 3 (PDF p6, printed 590), n=17 N2O, n=18 air:
  - ART failure: 0.7 +/- 0.6 (95% CI 0.5-1.0) with N2O; 1.6 +/- 0.6 (CI 1.3-1.9) with air.
  - Loss of consciousness: 1.2 +/- 0.8 (CI 0.8-1.6) with N2O; 1.9 +/- 0.7 (CI 1.5-2.3) with air.
  - Concentration at recovery score 12: 0.5 +/- 0.5 (N2O), 0.9 +/- 0.5 (air) (text, PDF p4).
- At ART failure and at loss of consciousness HR, SAP, RR, PETCO2 and SpO2 were near normal (Table 3), SpO2 >=97%.
- No ke0, t1/2 ke0, tPeak or hysteresis estimated (effect-site model only used to target); no MEAC; no potency ratio except the observation that N2O shifts both thresholds down by about 0.9 (ART) and 0.7 (LOC) mcg/mL in plasma (derived: air minus N2O means from Table 3).
- Red alerts (8 events, all low respiratory rate / partial airway obstruction, 5 on N2O) clustered between ART-failure P50 and loss of consciousness; only one needed head repositioning; lowest SpO2 91%. No false-normal ART responses.
- Not an opioid paper; MEAC/ke0/potency audit: none.

## Covariates
None modelled. N2O (50%) is the only factor compared.

## Caveats
- Healthy young volunteers (n=20), unblinded single observer, fixed N2O-before-air order, which may confound the comparison; authors expect more variability in patients.
- Concentrations are venous samples; arterial would run higher during rising levels.
- Wide SDs at loss of consciousness (+/-0.7-0.8 mcg/mL), so a single plasma value does not separate sedation from unconsciousness.
