# Lötsch 2001: transfer half-life of M6G (and morphine) from plasma to pupil effect site

## Study
Single-blind, randomized two-way crossover, no placebo arm (p. 1330). Eight healthy volunteers, four men and four
women, 24-37 yr, 54-78 kg, 164-187 cm (Table 2, PDF p. 4; the height column is headed "(m)" but holds values in
cm, e.g. 180). Each received IV morphine (hydrochloride) and IV morphine-6-glucuronide (M6G) on separate days: a
short loading infusion followed by a constant-rate infusion, nominally targeting plasma 25 ng/mL (87.6 nM) morphine
and 1,200 ng/mL (2,589 nM) M6G. Infusions were stopped on pupil size (3.5 mm for morphine, 4.5 mm for M6G) to
achieve submaximal constriction. Median doses: morphine 0.5 mg load + 10.7 mg over 4.7 h; M6G 10.2 mg load + 39.1 mg
over 3.7 h (abstract and p. 1331). In nmol (Table 2): morphine median load 1,872 nmol over 0.035 h, infusion 37,696
nmol over 4.67 h; M6G load 26,497 nmol over 0.200 h, infusion 82,164 nmol over 3.73 h.

Pupil diameter by infrared pupillograph (CIP, Amtech), resolution 0.05 mm, every 20 min for about 18 h, in
standardized dim light (p. 1331). Plasma (EDTA) sampled 28-31 times per subject; morphine, M3G and M6G by LC-MS/MS,
LLOQ 1 ng/mL morphine and 0.05 ng/mL M6G/M3G (p. 1331). Total plasma concentrations (no free fraction).

Analysis: semiparametric PK-PD (linear-spline smoothing of Cp, Unadkat/Sheiner approach) with an effect compartment
dCe/dt = ke0(Cp - Ce) and a sigmoid Emax model on pupil diameter; after morphine, pupil effect = sum of morphine
and M6G (formed) contributions, M6G parameters fixed from the M6G day; M3G ignored (Eqs 1-3, pp. 1332-1333).
Two-stage, NONMEM V 1.1. Emax (smallest pupil) could not be estimated and was fixed from another ongoing study (2.3-
3.0 mm; 2.7 mm average for subjects 1-2) (p. 1333, Table 4).

## Model and parameters
The paper's own PK model (Table 1, PDF p. 3) is only the dosing model: two-compartment parameters from a naive
pooled NONMEM reanalysis of earlier volunteer data (text cites ref 14, Lötsch 1998; the Table 1 footnote cites
ref 13, healthy men). Absolute, not per kg. Morphine plasma concentrations during infusion: median 19.8 ng/mL
(69.4 nM); dosing bias -15.6%, accuracy 25.3% for morphine; M6G 1,140 ng/mL, accuracy 9.6%, bias -1.8% (p. 1334).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- **End point: pupil diameter (miosis)** only; no analgesia, ventilation, EEG or CSF end point was measured. The
  authors caution against carrying these data over to analgesia (p. 1337).
- **ke0 / t½ke0 (pupil)**: morphine median ke0 0.25 h⁻¹, t½ke0 2.8 h (range 1.8-4.4 h); M6G 0.11 h⁻¹, 6.4 h (range
  2.9-16.2 h); every subject's M6G t½ke0 exceeded their morphine t½ke0 (Table 4, PDF p. 8; p. 1335). The authors
  compare the morphine value with 3.8 h from Dershwitz 2000 (p. 1335). Interindividual CV of ke0 56.2% (M6G) vs
  30.7% (morphine) (p. 1335). Plasma assay: total plasma, by LC-MS/MS.
- **tPeak**: not reported.
- **EC50 (pupil, total plasma)**: morphine median 34.4 nM (Table 4) = about 9.8 ng/mL (derived: × 285.3 g/mol);
  the abstract gives 36.2 nM (range 19.7-43.3), which does not match Table 4's median and range (internal
  inconsistency; both confirmed on the page images). M6G 743 nM (Table 4) or 740.5 nM (abstract).
- **MEAC / minimum effective analgesic concentration**: none reported. The pupil EC50 is not an MEAC.
- **Potency ratios**: by EC50, M6G 22 times less potent than morphine at the pupil (range 11-48) (abstract;
  p. 1336); equal pupil effects needed a 2.8-fold greater molar M6G dose (abstract; p. 1337). The introduction
  restates literature that IV morphine is 6-8 times as potent as oral after single doses and 2-3 times after
  repeated dosing (p. 1329, citing Hanks 1987) — background, not a finding of this study.
- Side effects: M6G produced more spontaneous side-effect reports (vomiting, itching) than morphine at comparable
  pupil effect (Table 3, p. 1333).

## Covariates
None modelled. Men rated tiredness and drowsiness after morphine lower than women (p. 1334). No weight or sex
effect on PK-PD parameters reported.

## Caveats
n = 8 healthy young adults; single-blind, no placebo arm (justified by separate placebo-day pupillography in 5 of
8). Emax fixed from another study. Submaximal constriction by design. Infusion length individualized on the
effect itself. Abstract and Table 4 EC50 summaries disagree slightly; M6G t½ke0 is 6.4 h in Results and 6.5 h in
Discussion. Table 1's source is cited as ref 13 in the footnote but ref 14 in the text. Pupil effect may not track
analgesia.
