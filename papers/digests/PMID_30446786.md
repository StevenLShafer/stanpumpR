# Deyme 2019: population PK of the FOLFIRINOX drugs, a review

## Study
Systematic review (PubMed from inception to February 2018) of nonlinear mixed-effects population PK models of
the three FOLFIRINOX cytotoxics given IV: 5-fluorouracil (5FU), oxaliplatin and irinotecan (CPT-11), with
metabolites. Liposomal irinotecan and capecitabine were excluded. 17 papers kept (1999-2015): 6 for 5FU, 6 for
oxaliplatin, 5 for irinotecan (Fig. 1, PDF p. 4). Study sizes 21-86 patients, except Nikanjam 2015 (186, pooled
from five trials). The drugs were studied separately; only one patient (in Mueller 2013) had the full regimen.
Table 1 (PDF pp. 5-6) gives country, cancer, n, age, sampling, dose and comedication; Table 2 (PDF p. 7) software,
algorithm, compartments, elimination type and residual error; Table 4 (PDF p. 10) the covariates tested and kept.
The authors simulated each adult model's typical profile for the standard regimen at BSA 1.73 m2 (Simulx/mlxR):
5FU 400 mg/m2 bolus + 2400 mg/m2 over 46 h, oxaliplatin 85 mg/m2 over 2 h, irinotecan 180 mg/m2 over 1.5 h
(Figs. 2-5).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. A PK review: no effect site, ke0, tPeak or target concentration. The discussion lists the few PK/PD or
PK/toxicity models found (5FU thrombocytopenia in rats, SN-38 and neutrophil count, intraperitoneal oxaliplatin
and neutropenia) without values. Not an opioid paper.

## Covariates
Table 4 (PDF p. 10), summarised in the text. 5FU: sex on clearance (Mueller, Bressolle); BSA, hepatic metastasis
and creatinine clearance once each; DPD deficiency lowers Vmax and Km (van Kuilenburg). Oxaliplatin: renal
function (serum creatinine or GFR) on clearance, body size on CL or volume, plus sex and/or age in some. Irinotecan:
weight (allometry in the paediatric studies; on V1 in Klein); sex on SN-38 clearance; age; bilirubin on SN-38;
EIAEDs, corticosteroids, disease grade (Berg); performance status (Klein). No equations are reproduced.

## Caveats
- A secondary source: values are transcribed from the primary papers, and some are the review's own arithmetic
  (italic) or recomputations (Delord, with the authors' data; Nikanjam at mean covariates).
- Table 3's footnote letter "d" is used twice with two meanings (corrected with the author's approval; k10 + k12
  for Poujol).
- Klein is labelled 2003 in the tables, but reference 38 is Clin Pharmacol Ther 2002;72:638-647.
- Klein's SN-38 V1/F of 311.7 L is printed in bold as a typical value; Klein's fit was a two-group mixture (200 L
  or 466 L), and 311.7 is their proportion-weighted mean (derived), a value no patient in the model has.
- Platinum concentration basis (platinum mass vs oxaliplatin equivalents) is not discussed; profiles from
  different studies are overlaid on one axis.
- Mostly not FOLFIRINOX itself: each drug alone or with one partner.
