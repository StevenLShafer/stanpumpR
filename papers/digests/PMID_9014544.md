# Struys 1997: propofol TCI versus two manual infusion schemes

## Study
- 90 ASA I-II women, day-case gynaecological surgery, randomised to three groups (n = 31 / 29 / 30; age about 31-33 y, weight 62-64 kg, Table 1). Premedication midazolam 0.03 mg/kg IV 7 min and alfentanil 10 µg/kg IV 2 min before induction (units read as µg; the text layer shows "mg").
- Group I: Diprifusor TCI, blood target 4 µg/mL, raised by 2 µg/mL after 3 min and then at 2-min intervals until loss of verbal contact. Maintenance at the induction target, adjusted in 1 µg/mL steps.
- Groups II and III: bolus infusion at 1200 or 600 mL/h until loss of verbal contact, then 10 mg/kg/h adjusted in 2 mg/kg/h steps.
- Graseby 3400 pump. Propofol concentrations were not measured: blood and effect-site concentrations were simulated (IVA-SIM) from recorded pump rates, using the three-compartment Marsh parameter set that the Diprifusor contains (ref 15, Marsh 1991). The paper gives no parameter values and no ke0.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: loss of verbal contact (clinical, titrated). Not an opioid paper; no MEAC, no potency ratio.
- The paper says the simulated effect-site concentration at loss of consciousness did not differ between groups (discussion, PDF p8, printed p49); the numbers are in Table 7, which I did not confirm (see open questions). No ke0, t½ke0 or tPeak is reported. The text names ke1 as the blood/effect-site rate constant (p1) but gives no value.
- Simulated blood concentration at loss of consciousness differed greatly between groups (4.2, 11.1, 6.5 µg/mL, Table 6), which the authors take as the effect of blood/effect-site hysteresis with manual boluses.
- Authors' conclusion: manual boluses at high rate overshoot the effect site; the TCI blood target sat higher during maintenance, hence the larger maintenance dose.

## Covariates
Weight and age were entered into the Diprifusor and used as covariates in the analysis of dose. The Marsh model's weight scaling is the only covariate in the simulations.

## Caveats
- Concentrations are simulated from the Marsh set, never measured, so the "overshoot" claims inherit that model's error.
- Premedicated women only; alfentanil and midazolam lower propofol need.
- Short cases (about 23-27 min).
