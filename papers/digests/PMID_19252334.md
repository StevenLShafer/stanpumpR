# Anderson 2009: Mechanistic basis of size and maturation for clearance

## Study
Review (not a new trial) by Anderson and Holford. Sets out allometry (exponent 3/4 for function, 1 for structure), fat-free mass / normal fat mass (NFM) size descriptors, the sigmoid-hyperbolic maturation function, and an organ-function term, and illustrates them on three pooled analyses: GFR (8 publications, n = 923, 22 wk PMA to 31 y, from Rhodin 2008), morphine clearance (875 ventilated premature neonates, 23-32 wk PMA, plus 184 postoperative infants 0-3 y; ref 71) and paracetamol (n = 509 subjects, 3049 samples, 27 wk PMA/0.5 kg to 24 y/105 kg; oral elixir, rectal and IV; NONMEM VI; 2-compartment, first-order absorption with lag, oral and rectal). The appendix is an NM-TRAN control stream. Vancomycin (214 neonates, PMA 24-34 wk) and levetiracetam are discussed briefly.

## Model and parameters
General form (printed p. 27-28): P = Pstd x Fsize x MF x OF.
- Allometry: CL = CLstd x (W/70)^(3/4).
- FFM (Janmahasatian form): FFM = WHSmax x H^2 x W / (WHS50 x H^2 + W), H in m. Men WHSmax 42.92, WHS50 30.93 kg/m2; women 37.99, 35.98 kg/m2.
- NFM = FFM + Ffat x (W - FFM); Fsize = (NFM/Wstd)^PWR; PWR 1 for structure (V, creatinine production), 3/4 for function (GFR, CL). Ffat for GFR = 0.21 (control stream 0.211).
- Maturation: MF = 1/(1 + (PMA/TM50)^-Hill); asymmetric variants given with parameter d, or separate Hill for young and old.
- GFR maturation (printed p. 28): mature GFR 121 mL/min/70 kg (95% CI 117-125), TM50 47.7 wk PMA (45.1-50.5), Hill 3.4 (3.0-3.8); 90% of adult at 1 year in a 40-wk-PMA birth. Estimated allometric exponent 0.632 improved the fit but the authors prefer 3/4.
- Creatinine production: adult 0.516 mmol/h (70 kg, 40 y male); child form uses exp(Kage x ...), Kage 0.0096 /wk (appendix).

Derived/reported: paracetamol clearance 1.53 L/h/70 kg at 27 wk PCA, 88% of mature by 1 year (printed p. 31; checked: Hill formula at 92 wk PMA gives 0.88 (derived)). Fig. 4 labels mature CL 16 L/h/70 kg. Fig. 5 compares maturation curves (GFR 47.6 wk/Hill 3.4; paracetamol 50.1/3.4; morphine 54.6/3.83). Between-subject correlations (Table 2): CL-V1 0.73, CL-Q -0.319, CL-V2 0.332, V1-Q -0.400, V1-V2 -0.198, Q-V2 0.204. Residual error: additive 0.53 mg/L plus 10.5% proportional.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No pharmacodynamics. No MEAC or analgesic concentration, no ke0, t1/2ke0, tPeak or hysteresis, and no equianalgesic/potency ratio is reported (opioid audit: morphine appears only as a clearance-maturation example). Morphine glucuronidation in fetal microsomes (15-27 wk) is cited as 10-20% of adult microsomes (printed p. 30, ref 37).

## Covariates
Weight (allometric 3/4 for CL, 1 for V), height and sex (via FFM/NFM), postmenstrual age (maturation), postnatal age (volume maturation: V multiplier 1 + BETAV x exp(-ln2/V_TMAT x PNA), BETAV 0.7, V_TMAT 0.3 wk in the appendix), serum creatinine (organ function RF = CLcr/GFR normal). Sex has no effect on GFR beyond size.

## Caveats
Review, so parameter sets come from the authors' earlier pooled analyses. Morphine maturation is a hospital population of ventilated premature neonates (clearance shifted to the right, possibly an illness effect the authors could not separate). The authors say Schwartz-type creatinine clearance over-predicts GFR in children and advise against serum creatinine in premature neonates. Hill/TM50 differences between GFR, paracetamol and morphine are statistical; the authors read them as UGT isoform differences. Table 3's morphine TM50/Hill appear twice (54.2/3.92 separate, 54.6/3.83 common) and the text quotes 54.6/3.83 as the morphine study's own result.

## Verification against stanpumpR
No repo location cites it, so nothing to verify line by line. Independent comparisons:
- **Acetaminophen** (R/drugs_acetaminophen.R:8-40, 79-84): the library is Morse 2022 adult (CL 24.0 L/h/70 kg on NFM with Ffat 0.816, exponent 0.75; V on weight, exponent 1), consistent with this paper's size rules (3/4 for CL and Q, 1 for V). The library has no maturation term; this paper's Table 1 supplies one (TM50 52.2 wk PMA, Hill 3.43), and its CLstd of 16.2 L/h/70 kg is lower than Morse's 24.0 (the paediatric/pooled fit, size on total weight, against Morse's adult volunteers on NFM). The library's header prediction (overpredicts clearance below about a year) agrees in direction: at 40 wk PMA the maturation factor is 1/(1+(40/52.2)^-3.43) = 0.29 (derived).
- **Morphine** (R/drugs_morphine.R): Lotsch 2002 adult volunteers; no PMA maturation. This paper's morphine TM50 54.6 wk PMA, Hill 3.83 would apply only to neonates and infants, outside the library's modelled range (the file bounds BSA at 1.5-2.2 m2).
