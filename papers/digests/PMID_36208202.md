# Gargis 2023: reference agar-dilution MICs for 593 US C. difficile isolates, 2012-17

## Study
CDC Emerging Infections Program (EIP) surveillance, 10 US states. About 100 isolates per year, 2012-2017, randomly
sampled from the 10 most common ribotypes of that year; 7 excluded for WGS quality, leaving N = 593 isolates of
14 ribotypes (RT027 23.1%, RT106 16.9%, RT002 13.3%) (PDF p2, Table 1). MICs by CLSI reference agar dilution
(CLSI M11) for ceftriaxone, clindamycin, meropenem, metronidazole, moxifloxacin and vancomycin; QC strains
B. fragilis ATCC 25285 and C. difficile ATCC 700057. Whole-genome sequencing (MiSeq) for resistance genes
(vanA/B, vanR/vanS, gyrA/gyrB, erm, cfr, pCD-METRO plasmid). Kuiper's test for distribution shifts.
No pharmacokinetics, no patient outcomes, no MBC (bactericidal) testing.

## Model and parameters
No PK model. MIC results (μg/mL = mg/L), all isolates, Table 2 (PDF p4, printed p893; confirmed on the rendered
page) and text p2:

1: 158 (84.5%); 2: 76 (97.3%); 4: 15 (99.8%); 8: 1 (100%). Tested range 0.125-32 μg/mL (Table 2 footnote a).
By year (Table 3, PDF p4, text layer): metronidazole MIC90 2 in 2012, 2014, 2015; 1 in 2013 and 2016; 0.5 in 2017;

The single metronidazole MIC of 8 was an RT027 isolate; pCD-METRO was not found in any isolate (p3).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point is in-vitro growth inhibition (MIC) only. Breakpoints quoted (p4): CLSI metronidazole (all anaerobes)
S ≤8, I 16, R ≥32 μg/mL; EUCAST C. difficile metronidazole and vancomycin S ≤2, R >2; CLSI vancomycin ECOFF ≤2
wild type. The discussion cites a study linking metronidazole MIC ≥1 μg/mL to treatment failure, and fecal
vancomycin concentrations 100-1000 times the MIC90 (p4, refs 33, 34). Not an opioid paper; no MEAC, ke0 or potency ratio.

## Covariates
Ribotype (RT027 vs other) and collection period (2012-14 vs 2015-17).

## Caveats
Convenience sample restricted to the 10 most common ribotypes each year; no repeat AST (MIC may vary one dilution);
fidaxomicin not tested (authors, p5). MIC only: no bactericidal data, so no MBC can be read from it.
