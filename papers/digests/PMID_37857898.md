# Lense 2024: venlafaxine therapeutic reference range revised (systematic review and meta-analysis)

## Study
AGNP task-force systematic review and meta-analysis (PROSPERO CRD42020218248). MEDLINE, PsycINFO, Web of
Science and Cochrane searched (initial search October 2020, updated January 2023). 68 articles (62 cohorts):
29 concentration/effect studies, 29 concentration studies, 4 neuroimaging (PET/SPECT) studies. Blood levels (BLs)
are serum or plasma concentrations of venlafaxine (VEN), O-desmethylvenlafaxine (ODV) and the active moiety
(AM = VEN + ODV). Quantitative synthesis restricted to extended-release formulations and depressive disorders.
No high-quality RCT of concentration/efficacy and no placebo lead-in study was found; most data are naturalistic
TDM studies. Most sampling was at steady-state trough (>75% of studies meeting the sampling-time criterion, p. 278).
Analyses in R (metafor, meta) and RevMan 5.4; random-effects models; Hedges' g for responder vs non-responder.
Published online 19 October 2023 (hence "Lense 2023" in places); volume year 2024.

Table 3 (expected BLs from mean C/D, ER venlafaxine; PDF page 9 image):

| Dose/day | AM ng/mL | VEN ng/mL | ODV ng/mL |
|---|---|---|---|
| 75 mg | 140 [131, 149] | 52 [45, 59] | 85 [79, 91] |
| 150 mg | 281 [261, 299] | 104 [90, 119] | 170 [158, 182] |
| 225 mg | 421 [392, 448] | 155 [135, 178] | 254 [236, 497] (upper CI as printed; see Caveats) |
| 375 mg | 701 [653, 746] | 259 [225, 296] | 424 [394, 454] |

Linear regression of AM BL on daily dose across 28 studies (N = 8211): y = 254 + 0.49x, r2 = 0.152 (Fig. 4);
mean 188 mg/day gave 344 [322, 366] ng/mL AM (p. 281).

How the ranges were derived (p. 285): lower limits 140 (AM) and 85 (ODV) are the expected levels at 75 mg/day
(Table 3). ODV upper 380 ng/mL is the 75th percentile in responders (Engelmann 2021, responder IQR 213-382 ODV,
305-534 AM). AM upper 600 follows from the ODV ceiling and ODV being 62% of AM (derived: 380/0.62 = 613, rounded
by the authors to 600).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- Not an opioid; no MEAC, ke0, t½ke0, tPeak or hysteresis reported. No potency ratio.
- Responders vs non-responders (5 studies, n = 395): AM 412 +/- 208 ng/mL (n = 189) vs 372 +/- 139 (n = 206);
  effect estimate 0.30 [0.00, 0.60] (p. 279, Fig. 2). End point: >=50% HAM-D/MADRS reduction (varied by study).
- Engelmann 2021 reanalysis: ROC ODV threshold 289 ng/mL for response, AUC 0.595 (Fig. 1, p. 279).
- Scherf-Clavel 2020: ROC threshold for remission (HAMD <= 7) 393 ng/mL AM. De Donatis 2021: u-shaped, optimum
  100-400 ng/mL AM (Table 1).
- Transporter occupancy (p. 280): ODV >= 85 ng/mL gives 80% SERT occupancy (EC80, Frankle 2018, PET, healthy
  men); VEN EC80 14 ng/mL (Meyer 2004); authors' inferred EC80 28-42 ng/mL (ODV) and 42-56 ng/mL (AM);
  50% NET occupancy at AM >= 670 ng/mL (Arakawa 2019a). AM 400 ng/mL corresponds to 37% NET occupancy (p. 286).
- ADRs: AM > 400 ng/mL associated with tremor (Engelmann 2021; evidence level C). Evidence for
  concentration/efficacy rated level B (limited) on p. 279 and level C (low) on p. 284 (internal inconsistency).
- No toxic/alert level given (the laboratory alert levels in stanpumpR come from Hart 2026, not this paper).

## Covariates
No model covariates. Reported moderators (p. 281-284): older age (elderly defined as >=59-65 y) raises
dose-corrected AM by 50-61%; women have higher C/D (up to ~70% for women >65 vs men <65); BMI negatively
correlated (differences <50%); CYP2D6 PMs: dose-corrected VEN up 100-1242%, ODV down 62% to absent, AM up >50% to
260%; CYP2D6 inhibitors (melperone +237% VEN, -54% ODV; doxepin +232% VEN), valproate +27-51% ODV.

## Caveats
- Ranges rest mainly on naturalistic TDM and small non-controlled studies; flexible dosing can create artificial
  concentration/effect relations (authors).
- Table 3's ODV 225 mg CI upper bound, 497, is inconsistent with C/D 1.21 x 225 = 272 (derived); a misprint in
  the paper (confirmed as printed on the PDF page 9 image). Not used by stanpumpR.
- Abstract n for meta-analysis is 360; Results says n = 395 (189 + 206).
- Evidence grade stated as level B on p. 279 and level C in the Discussion.
- Matrix is serum or plasma, pooled; total (not free) concentrations.
