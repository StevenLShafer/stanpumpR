# Olesen 2013: population PK/PD of CR665 (IV) and oral oxycodone on visceral pain tolerance

## Study
- Secondary PK/PD analysis of a randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled three-way crossover (Aalborg,
  Denmark; sponsor Johnson & Johnson). Treatments: CR665 0.36 mg/kg IV over 1 h; oxycodone 15 mg oral
  (1.5 mL of 10 mg/mL oxycodone hydrochloride solution in 13.5 mL orange juice, given via the oesophageal tube
  port); double placebo. Washout 1-3 weeks, overnight fast.
- 18 healthy opioid-naive men (17 Caucasian, 1 Asian), 19-43 y (median 25), 62.4-94.5 kg (median 80.0),
  BMI 20.5-27.4 (median 23.6) (Results, p. 128).
- PK sampling: left forearm venous, pre-dose and 5, 15, 30, 60, 75, 120 min (only 2 h). 91 oxycodone samples.
- Oxycodone assay: HPLC-MS/MS (CEDRA), LLOQ 0.1 ng/mL, calibration 0.1-50 ng/mL; BLQ set to zero; inter/intra-day
  CV < 10.2 / 10.5%.
- PD: oesophageal bag distension with water at 10 mL/min; visceral pain tolerance threshold (VPTT, mL at VAS 7
  "moderate pain"); before and at 30, 60, 90 min. 144 oxycodone PD measurements.
- NONMEM VI FOCE-I, PsN 3.4.2 bootstrap (1000) and VPC.

  at about 32 min, about 31 ng/mL (derived: my simulation). The paper itself states no tmax or Cmax.
- CR665 (not in stanpumpR): 2-compartment with N-oxide, V1 8.08 L, k12 4.85 /h, k21 0.0778 /h, k13 = k30 0.501 /h,
  V3 488 L (Table 2), weight-scaled to 80 kg.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: VPTT (oesophageal distension volume at moderate pain, VAS 7/10), experimental visceral pain in
  healthy men.
- Final oxycodone model: **direct linear** on plasma concentration, E = baseline + slopeD x C; slope 0.0793 mL per
  ng/mL (Table 4; abstract says 0.089). **No effect-site delay supported**: model 5 (linear with ke0) raised OFV;
  model 6 gave ke0 "very small (<0.02 h)" (units as printed) and failed covariance; model 7 not significant.
  Direct Emax model 3/4 estimates: Emax 13.2 mL, EC50 166 ng/mL (95% CI -69 to 401), rejected (one dose level).
- **No ke0, t½ke0 or tPeak reported for oxycodone; no MEAC; no equianalgesic ratio.** Oxycodone vs CR665 slopes
  compared (0.0793 vs 0.0035 mL per ng/mL) but explicitly not a potency ratio (doses not equi-effective).
- Metabolites not modelled (parent assumed to drive effect, citing Olesen 2010).

## Covariates
CR665: weight (allometric, volumes WT/80, rate constants (WT/80)^-0.25). Oxycodone: none reported.

## Caveats
- Only 2 h of PK sampling, so the one-compartment model cannot describe distribution or the terminal phase;
  CL/F and V/F are short-window estimates (CL/F 3.35 L/min is about 2.3x Lalovic's 1.44 L/min).
- 3 PD time points per period; single dose level; healthy young men only; experimental visceral pain.
- Dose was 15 mg of the hydrochloride; whether parameters are base-corrected is not stated.
- Abstract/table slope mismatch (0.089 vs 0.0793).
- Sponsor-affiliated authors (J&J).
