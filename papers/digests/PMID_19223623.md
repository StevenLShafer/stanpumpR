# Traczewski 2009: daptomycin, vancomycin and teicoplanin MIC/MBC in 479 MRSA

## Study
In vitro susceptibility survey. 479 unique, nonconsecutive MRSA isolates, mainly blood and abscess, from US
and European hospitals, 1985-2007; all susceptible to the three drugs by prior MIC testing; vancomycin-
intermediate and -resistant strains excluded (PDF p 1). MIC by CLSI broth microdilution (M7-A7, M100-S18),
cation-adjusted Mueller-Hinton broth (daptomycin with 50 mg/L calcium), range 0.015-512 µg/mL. MBC per CLSI
M26-A and Peterson & Shanholtzer: whole 0.1 mL of the MIC well and 4 dilutions above plated, 35-37 °C for 24-48 h;
MBC = lowest dilution giving ≥99.9% (≥3-log10) kill vs the growth control. Tolerance = MBC/MIC ≥32, or ≥16 when
the MBC is at or above the resistance breakpoint. Funded by Cubist (daptomycin maker).

## Model and parameters
No PK. Table 1 (PDF p 2, printed p 1736), µg/mL (= mg/L), total drug in broth:

| Drug | MIC50/MIC90 | MBC50/MBC90 | % tolerance |
|---|---|---|---|
| Daptomycin | 0.5/0.5 | 0.5/1 | 0 |
| Vancomycin | 1/1 | 1/2 | 6.1 (29/479) |
| Teicoplanin | 0.5/1 | 1/32 | 18.8 (90/479) |

Table 2 (vancomycin MBC/MIC ratio, n (%)): 1: 405 (84.6); 2: 38 (7.9); 4: 2 (0.4); 8: 4 (0.8); ≥16: 3 (0.6);
≥32: 27 (5.6). Text: ratio 1 in 84.5%, ≤2 in 92.5% (p 2).
Table 3 (vancomycin MIC distribution, n tolerant/n): ≤0.25 0/2; 0.5 1/120; 1 26/348; 2 2/7; 4 0/2; 8 0/0;
≥16 0/0. Tolerance rose with MIC. Of 29 vancomycin-tolerant isolates, 24 (82.8%) were also teicoplanin-tolerant
(Table 5).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End points are MIC (visible growth inhibition) and MBC (≥99.9% kill at 24-48 h). Not an opioid paper: no MEAC,
ke0 or potency ratio. No clinical outcome data.

## Covariates
None. Year of collection not analysed separately in the tables (Table 2 is across all years).

## Caveats
- **Text and Table 1 disagree** (results, PDF p 1): the text gives vancomycin/teicoplanin MIC50 1 and 1,
  MIC90 0.5 and 1, MBC50 1 and 2, MBC90 1 and 32; Table 1 gives vancomycin 1/1 and 1/2, teicoplanin 0.5/1 and
  1/32. The text's vancomycin MIC90 (0.5) below its MIC50 (1) is impossible, and Table 3's MIC distribution
  gives MIC50 = MIC90 = 1 (derived), so this card takes Table 1. From Tables 2-3, at most ~9 isolates with
  MBC/MIC ≤2 can have MBC >2, so ≥434 of 479 (≥90%) have MBC ≤2 (derived), consistent with Table 1's MBC90 2;
  the text's MBC90 of 1 cannot be excluded from the tables alone.
- Table 4: 12/17 printed as 70.5% (derived 70.6%); trivial.
- Excluding VISA/VRSA lowers MBC90 and tolerance relative to unselected clinical MRSA; isolates span 22 years.
- Tolerance is defined differently from "MBC ≥32 × MIC" alone (also ≥16 at a resistant MBC).
- The authors cite other vancomycin tolerance rates against MRSA: 3.2% (Biedenbach, 76 isolates), 15% (Jones,
  105 isolates), 0% to 47% across studies (p 2).
