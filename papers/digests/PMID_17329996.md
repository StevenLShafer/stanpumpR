# Brockmöller 2007: enantioselective mirtazapine PK by CYP2D6 genotype

## Study
- Healthy volunteers: 12 CYP2D6 UMs (duplication + one active allele; 3 active genes), 12 EMs (2 active), 3 PMs (0 active). Age mean 31 (19-43) y, weight 80 (64-106) kg.
- Single 45 mg oral racemic mirtazapine (22.5 mg per enantiomer), fasting. Plasma at 0-58 h (14 times); urine 0-6 and 6-12 h.
- Enantioselective HPLC with fluorescence, LOQ 1 ng/mL per enantiomer, for parent, desmethyl, 8-OH and N-oxide.
- Noncompartmental (WinNonlin 2.1) and NONMEM V population model: two-compartment, first-order absorption with lag (ADVAN4/TRANS4), CL = non-CYP2D6 part + (active alleles x CL per allele); relative F modelled with F = 1 in PMs.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Adverse effects 4 h post dose and sedation (Table 4, Pearson r with enantiomer + desmethyl concentrations): systolic BP fall r -0.64 (R, P 0.001) vs -0.36 (S, NS); diastolic -0.67 vs -0.40; heart rate -0.39 vs ~0 (NS); sedation 0.76 (R) and 0.63 (S), both P < 0.0001. No hysteresis for sedation (linear concentration-sedation relation per subject); no ke0, EC50 or MEAC. Not an opioid paper.

## Covariates
CYP2D6 active-gene count (strongly for S(+), marginally for R(-)); no dependence on age or weight.

## Caveats
Single dose, healthy young volunteers, only 3 PMs; S(+) oral clearances exceed liver blood flow, interpreted as genotype-dependent first pass; authors warn against physiological reading of peripheral parameters.
