# Fernandez-Candil 2008: rocuronium PKPD with and without chronic phenytoin

## Study
- Observational, open-label, non-randomised; 21 adults for brain surgery at Hospital Clinic, Barcelona. 10 on chronic phenytoin therapy (CPT; phenytoin as the only antiepileptic for at least 7 days), 11 controls on no antiepileptic. Liver or renal insufficiency excluded (PDF p. 2).
- Table 2 (medians, range; PDF p. 6): age 55 (35-76) control vs 39 (19-77) CPT years; weight 65 (52-92) vs 68.5 (52-84) kg; height 165 vs 173 cm; sex M/F 7/4 vs 4/6; serum phenytoin 0 vs 8.05 (4.6-30.2) mg/L (immunoassay, total); albumin 39 vs 37.5 g/L; alpha1-acid glycoprotein 0.91 vs 0.85 g/L.
- Anaesthesia: oral diazepam 5 mg, propofol 2 mg/kg induction and maintenance, remifentanil 0.01-0.5 mcg/kg/min. Rocuronium bolus 3 x ED95 = 0.9 mg/kg (Table 1: 50-80 mg; 63 mg for 70 kg), then an infusion started at 25% twitch recovery and adjusted to keep one TOF response, stopped at skull closure. Infusion rates 10.55-40.46 mcg/kg/min (Table 1, footnote: total dose per kg averaged over infusion time).
- Arterial plasma samples at 1.5, 3, 6, 10, 30, 60 min after the bolus, hourly during the infusion, and 10, 30, 60, 120 min after it stopped. Rocuronium by HPLC with post-column ion-pair extraction and fluorimetry (LOQ 10 ng/mL, range 10-1000, within-day CV 6.8%).
- Effect: adductor pollicis EMG (Datex-Ohmeda M-NMT), supramaximal TOF, T1% = percent of the supramaximal-stimulus response. 16 patients in the PD analysis (5 lost to download errors: 2 control, 3 CPT) but all 21 in the PK.
- NONMEM V, FOCE with interaction, PK fitted first and the individual PK estimates then used for PD (sequential). Exponential IPV, additive (log) residual error, GAM covariate screening. Software for simulation: PKPDtools for Excel.

## Model and parameters
Three-compartment PK, effect-compartment sigmoid Emax PD. Table 3 (PDF p. 7, printed 801): typical values, relative SE in parentheses, IPV as CV%.
- V1 4.04 L (0.07); V2 5.34 L (0.13), IPV 44%; V3 4.93 L (0.26); Cl2 0.36 L/min (0.14), IPV 43%; Cl3 0.04 L/min (0.15); residual 0.3 (logarithmic scale).
- Cl1: 0.26 L/min (0.1) without CPT, 0.75 L/min (0.06) with CPT, IPV 24.4% (0.49). CPT was the only significant covariate (on Cl1, P<0.01). IPV on Cl1 fell from 60% to 24.4% when CPT was included. Individual Cl1 estimates do not overlap between the groups (Fig. 4).
- Fit quality: PK MDAPE 3.63%, MDPE -0.81% (text, p. 5); PD population MDAPE 8.6, MDPE 1.05, post hoc 3.4 and 0.36.
- Derived: Vss = 4.04 + 5.34 + 4.93 = 14.31 L (derived: sum of Table 3 volumes); non-CPT Cl1 per kg at the 65 kg group median = 4.0 mL/kg/min (derived: 0.26 L/min / 65 kg). The paper gives no per-kg values; the text itself quotes V1 4.03 L, Vss 14.6 L and Cl1 0.26 L/min for the non-CPT group (Discussion, p. 7), a small mismatch with Table 3 (4.04, 14.31).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: T1% from adductor pollicis EMG (not force). Concentrations are plasma rocuronium by HPLC (total; assay in plasma, not whole blood).
- ke0 0.073 /min (Table 4, RSE 0.1, IPV 42.4%), same for CPT and non-CPT; t1/2 ke0 = 9.5 min (derived: ln 2 / 0.073). The paper says its ke0 is lower than prior reports (Kuipers 0.24 /min, 2.9 min after 1 x ED95; 0.09-0.17 /min after 2 x ED95) and attributes this probably to the 3 x ED95 bolus: ke0 and IC50 appear dose dependent, so the values are valid only for similar dosing schemes (Discussion, pp. 9-10).
- IC50 836 ng/mL (Table 4); published IC50 range quoted 684-1030 ng/mL. gamma 3.13. CPT did not change IC50 (P>0.05, Fig. 7). Emax of the sigmoid model is not reported in Table 4 (text calls it a sigmoidal Emax model; presumably 100%, not stated). Derived, if Emax is 100%: effect-site concentration for 90% block is 836 x 9^(1/3.13) = about 1690 ng/mL and for 95% block about 2140 ng/mL (derived).
- Time to peak effect (TPE) after a 63 mg bolus (3 x ED95, 70 kg; Fig. 8, results p. 7): 11.23 min control, 7.17 min CPT. Onset to T1 10%: 3.2 vs 5.25 min. Time to 20% recovery: 45.2 vs 16.75 min; to 80%: 85.8 vs 37.75 min; 25-75% recovery 32.2 vs 16.75 min.
- MEAC / analgesic concentration, equianalgesic ratio: not applicable (neuromuscular blocker; not an opioid). Remifentanil was background only.

## Covariates
GAM screened age, height, CPT on Cl1, albumin on V2, alpha1-acid glycoprotein and weight on Cl2; in NONMEM only CPT on Cl1 was significant (P<0.01). No patient covariate (weight, age) entered the PD model. Cl1 is not scaled to weight.

## Caveats
- n = 21 (16 for PD), a brain-surgery group with dexamethasone in 9 and heterogeneous weight and age; the authors note weight could not be tested as a covariate with this design.
- Huge initial bolus (3 x ED95) drives early T1% to zero; authors state ke0 and IC50 depend on dose and the model would give wrong time courses for submaximal doses.
- Phenytoin was measured as total serum concentration (immunoassay), median 8.05 mg/L; its effect modelled only as a binary covariate.
- The predictive check median time to 80% block was 1.4 min observed vs 1.9 min simulated.

## Verification against stanpumpR
No repo location to verify. Independent comparison with `R/drugs_rocuronium.R` (Kleijn 2011 two-compartment model, checked against master 8c1ea1bd):
- Clearance, no phenytoin: paper 0.26 L/min (median weight 65 kg, median age 55); the model gives 0.269 x [1 - 0.00678 (age - 43)] x (W/70)^0.75 (R/drugs_rocuronium.R:162), i.e. 0.284 L/min at the reference man (header, lines 33-35). Close (about 9% higher in the model); same direction as the Kleijn-vs-Wierda agreement the header cites.
- V1: paper 4.04 L vs model 4.77 L at the reference man (line 163); paper Vss 14.3 L (three-compartment) vs the model's V1 + V2 = 4.77 + 6.44 = 11.2 L (header numbers; derived). The model is a two-compartment fit and its header already warns of a fast terminal phase.
- Phenytoin / enzyme-inducer effect: the paper's Cl1 of 0.75 L/min on chronic phenytoin is 2.9 times its own control value (derived: 0.75 / 0.26) and 2.6 times the model's reference clearance (derived: 0.75 / 0.284). `R/drugs_rocuronium.R` has no antiseizure-drug or inducer term and does not mention phenytoin; the repo's antiseizure interaction table (data-raw/antiseizureInteractions.R) was searched and has no rocuronium entry. A phenytoin-treated patient is therefore under-cleared by the model by this paper's account. Whether to add it is a modelling decision, not something this card can settle.
- ke0: paper 0.073 /min (t1/2 9.5 min) at a 3 x ED95 bolus, T1% EMG, TPE 11.23 min at 70 kg without phenytoin. The model uses tPeak 4.5 min (R/drugs_rocuronium.R:182, from Cortinez 2007) and re-solves ke0 to about 0.40 /min (header lines 100-116, a decision of Steven L. Shafer 2026-11-23). The paper's slower equilibration is consistent in direction with the model header's note on Kleijn's own 0.134 /min and the dose-dependence of ke0 stated by this paper and by Chen 2008 (PMID_18974056); it is not adopted by the library.
- Band: library band 1-2.2 mcg/mL (line 126-128, `inst/extdata/drugDefaults_global.csv`: 1 / 2.2 / 1.5) vs paper IC50 0.836 mcg/mL (836 ng/mL; ng/mL to mcg/mL by /1000) and derived 90% and 95% concentrations 1.69 and 2.14 mcg/mL (assuming Emax 100%). The library band sits in the paper's 50%-95% range; the library's own header says its band is an effect-site library band, not taken from this paper.
- Verdict: not-applicable (paper not cited); no discrepancy found against the uncited model beyond the unmodelled phenytoin effect.
