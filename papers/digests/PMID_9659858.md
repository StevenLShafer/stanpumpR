# Kapur 1998: 5-HT2 and D2 receptor occupancy of olanzapine (PET)

## Study
- 12 patients with schizophrenia (DSM-IV, 18-45 y; Table 1: 9 M / 3 F among patients 1-12, ages 19-44) randomly
  assigned to olanzapine 5, 10, 15 or 20 mg/day orally (3 per group), plus 3 additional scans at 30, 30 and 40 mg/day
  (two dose-escalated non-responders, one clinical patient): 15 D2 scans in 13 patients.
- Washout 2-4 days, titration (15 and 20 mg groups from 10 mg, +5 mg/week); scanned after ≥ 5 days at target dose.
- D2: [11C]raclopride bolus + infusion, striatum/cerebellum ratio, 12 h after the last dose; age-corrected baseline
  from 16 controls + 12 neuroleptic-naive patients (no own baseline; estimated error 0-9% at 50% occupancy).
- 5-HT2: [18F]setoperone, prefrontal cortex/cerebellum, 14-15 h after the last dose.
- Plasma olanzapine (heparinized plasma, HPLC with electrochemical detection) drawn at the PET scans.

## Model and parameters
No PK model. Plasma level 12 h post-dose was linear in dose: level = 5.8 + 1.99 × dose (ng/mL, dose mg/day;
R = 0.80) (p. 923). Levels at the setoperone scan averaged 8% lower. Table 1 levels at D2 scan range 9.2-181.4 ng/mL.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: striatal D2 receptor occupancy by [11C]raclopride PET (and cortical 5-HT2 by setoperone).
- Model (p. 923): occupancy (%) = 100 × X / (X + ED50), a saturating rectangular hyperbola with Emax fixed at 100%.
  - ED50 = 4.5 mg/day oral dose (N = 15, R = 0.84, 69-70% variance explained).
  - ED50 (EC50) = **10.3 ng/mL** olanzapine plasma level (N = 15, R = 0.83, 69% of variance) — plasma, total
    (parent), sampled ~12 h post-dose at steady state.
- Mean D2 occupancy: 55% (5 mg), 73% (10 mg), 75% (15 mg), 76% (20 mg), 83% (30 mg), 88% (40 mg); range 43-80% for
  5-20 mg/day, 83-88% at 30-40 mg/day (Table 1 individual 43-88%).
- 5-HT2 occupancy > 90% in all patients, even at 5 mg/day (no fit reported).
- > 80% D2 occupancy (doses > 20 mg/day) associated with EPS and prolactin elevation; > 60% considered needed for
  antipsychotic response (citing earlier work).
- No ke0 / hysteresis (single time point per scan). Not an opioid: no MEAC or potency ratio.

## Covariates
None analysed.

## Caveats
- Small n (15 scans), patients without own baselines (age-corrected population baseline).
- Single time point (12 h post-dose), so the hyperbola mixes inter-individual and dose variation; no time course.
- Two of the high-dose scans are repeat scans in the same patients (1B, 5B), so the 15 points are not independent.
- Striatal D2 only; mesolimbic not visualised.
