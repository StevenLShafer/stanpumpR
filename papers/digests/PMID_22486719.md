# Bouazza 2012: clindamycin population PK, IV and oral, in osteomyelitis

## Study
- Retrospective therapeutic drug monitoring study, Cochin Hospital, Paris, 2008-2010; adults treated for bone and joint infection (osteomyelitis).
- n = 50 (text p 972: age 18-93 y, mean 58.7 y; weight 23-133 kg, mean 70.9 kg). Table 1 prints age 56.7 ± 3.0 and weight 69.9 ± 2.7 kg (inconsistent with the text means); 30 men (60%), 20 women (40%); renal failure 5 (10%, Cockcroft-Gault), hepatic failure 2 (4.2%), rifampicin co-treatment 4 (8%). BMI: 24% normal, 68% overweight, 8% obese (p 973).
- Dosing: 600 mg tablet or IV infusion over 20 min three times daily (one patient 600 mg q6h, two 600 mg once daily). 22 oral only, 22 IV only, 6 both routes. Median dose 26.0 mg/kg/day (13.5-41.8).
- Sampling: routine, all at steady state; 122 plasma concentrations (58 oral, 64 IV); 2 BLQ treated as left-censored.
- Assay: HPLC-UV (200 nm), solid-phase extraction, LOQ 0.1 mg/L, interassay precision 18% at low QC; total plasma clindamycin.
- Software: MONOLIX 4 (SAEM + MCMC). One- vs two-compartment tested; proportional residual error; exponential BSV.

## Model and parameters
One-compartment model with first-order absorption; F estimated jointly from IV and oral data (logit-normal). BSV retained only on clearance (labelled CL/F in Table 2). Table 2 (final model; text layer is row-shifted, values aligned by parameter name and abstract):

  - {name: omega_CL, value: 0.39, unit: SD (exponential), source: "Table 2", note: "RSE 12%; eta shrinkage 0.03"}
  - {name: sigma_prop, value: 0.38, unit: proportional, source: "Table 2", note: "RSE 8%"}
  - {name: half-life, value: 3.0, unit: h, source: "derived: ln2*66.2/15.2"}

- The abstract prints a different vector: CL 16.2 L/h (IIV 0.39), V 70.2 L, ka 0.92 /h, F 87.6%. The table and abstract disagree; the table is the final model.
- Rifampicin increased CL by 43% (SE 0.17, Wald P = 0.04) but LRT was not significant; dropped from the final model.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD model. Dose simulation target: trough (Cmin) 2 mg/L, justified by S. aureus MIC 0.125 mg/L (methods say S. aureus, discussion says MRSA), bone penetration 30%, and a bone:MIC ratio of 5 (p 973, 975). Conclusion: 600 mg q8h adequate up to about 75 kg; 900 mg q8h above (Figure 4). Not an opioid; no MEAC, ke0 or potency ratio.

## Covariates
Total body weight on CL: CL = 15.2 × (BW/70)^0.497 L/h. Age, renal/hepatic failure tested; not retained. Rifampicin not retained (see above).

## Caveats
Retrospective sparse TDM data, steady-state only, 122 samples; 36% RSE on the weight exponent; age/weight summaries in Table 1 disagree with the text; abstract parameters differ from the final table; total (not free) concentrations; IV product is clindamycin phosphate (prodrug) modelled as parent input; only 6 patients contributed both routes to F.
