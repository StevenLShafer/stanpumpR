# Overgaard 2026: oral semaglutide 25 mg model-informed bridging to SC 2.4 mg

PDF page = printed page - 5981 (PDF p1 = 5982).

## Study
- Population PK and exposure-response meta-analysis plus two virtual trials, used for regulatory submission (oral 25 mg for weight management approved December 2025; p2).
- Data: OASIS 1, 2, 4; PIONEER 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 9, PLUS; STEP 1, 2, 6; SUSTAIN 1, 2, 3, JPN OAD, FORTE (p3). Oral PK population 4703 with planned sampling, 4569 with 22,654 PK observations contributed; age < 65 y 70.3%, T2D 86.6%, weight 93.2 (SD 22.6) kg, BMI 33 (6.7) (p4).
- SC semaglutide PK was taken from previous publications (STEP, SUSTAIN), not re-analysed (p3). Oral population PK used steady-state assumption and first-order conditional estimation with interaction (p3).
- Exposure metric: Cavg, the average concentration over a 24-h dosing interval from individual post hoc parameters, at planned and last-visit dose (p3). Units nmol/L (nM).
- Exposure-response population: 11,991 participants (1171 from OASIS); weight 95.3 (23.1) kg; 71.9% T2D (p5).
- Virtual phase 2: STEP 1 as efficacy trial, OASIS 1 as exposure trial, oral 3, 7, 14, 25, 50 mg. Virtual phase 3 (T2D): STEP 2 efficacy, PIONEER PLUS exposure, oral 25 mg. End points: % body weight change weeks 64-68 (treatment policy and trial product estimands), nausea/vomiting in first 26 weeks (p3-4).

- Figure 1 (PDF p3, geometric mean and IQR, read off the graph, approximate): non-T2D SC 1.7 mg about 59, SC 2.4 mg about 76, oral 25 mg about 76 nM; T2D SC 0.5 mg about 15, 1 mg about 28, 2 mg about 54, oral 7 mg about 7, 14 mg about 15, 25 mg about 45, 50 mg about 87 nM. Cavg values in the text are exact; the graph readings are mine.
- Covariates in the full oral PK model: sex, age, race, ethnicity, body weight, renal function, dose, upper GI disease, trial, T2D; final model dropped age, race, ethnicity, renal function (p3). Diabetes status had the largest effect on oral exposure, lower in T2D, outside the 0.8-1.25 range; sex and weight effects within bioequivalence (p4-5). Men and 141 kg vs 100 kg have lower exposure (p6). A slightly higher clearance in T2D gave 12% lower SC exposure in STEP 1 and 2 (p7, cited from a prior paper).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- Exposure-response: body weight % change at weeks 64-68 declines with Cavg (Figure 2, PDF p4); OASIS 4 and STEP 1 curves nearly superimpose (placebo about -2% at 0 nM, falling to about -22% by 300 nM, read off the graph). Central assumption: effect depends on concentration, not route.
- Safety: nausea and vomiting rise with exposure and plateau within the oral 25 mg exposure range (p5, Figure 3).
- No effect-site ke0, t1/2ke0, tPeak or hysteresis reported; no MEAC; no potency ratio other than the Cavg equivalence of oral 25 mg to SC 2.4 mg. Not an opioid paper.

## Covariates
See Model and parameters. T2D and Asian race had a larger influence on oral than on SC exposure (p6); sex and baseline weight modified treatment effect (greater weight loss in females at the same exposure, p6).

## Caveats
- Virtual (modelled) trials, not a measured 25 mg T2D study; empirical models are reliable for interpolation within the studied exposure range (82.2% in range) and less for extrapolation (p7).
- Oral exposure has higher inter- and intra-individual variability than SC (p3, p6).
- T2D exposure difference is not mechanistically understood; inter-trial confounding is acknowledged (p6).
- Novo Nordisk authored and funded.
- The 25 mg oral dose compared here is outside the Rybelsus 3, 7, 14 mg range the library models.

## Verification against stanpumpR
- SC 2.4 mg Cavg: paper 75 nM (STEP 1, p4). Library `R/drugs_semaglutide.R:123,126` with the reference 85 kg woman: Cavg = 2.4 mg / 4113.58 g/mol (583.4 nmol) / (0.0478 L/h x 168 h) = 72.7 nM (derived, bioavailability 1 because CL/F). Within 3% of 75. The paper does not give STEP 1's mean weight or sex mix, so a closer comparison is not possible; the library model was fitted on 0.5-1 mg in T2D and the 2.4 mg obesity dose is an extrapolation. **matches** at this level (derived).
- Oral 14 mg: the library header (`R/drugs_semaglutideOral.R:88-90`) computes 17.1 nM for the 85 kg woman; Figure 1 in this paper shows T2D oral 14 mg about 15 nM (graph reading, interquartile range about 10-25) and the header's PIONEER geometric mean is 14.6. **matches** within the plotted spread.
- Oral 25 mg: library extrapolated linearly gives about 30.6 nM (derived); paper reports 77 nM non-T2D (OASIS 4) and about 45 nM T2D (Figure 1 reading). Exposure in this paper is more than dose-proportional between 14 and 25 mg in T2D, while the library's F is dose-independent (`R/drugs_semaglutideOral.R:132-134`, and the header says dose was tested and not kept in the PIONEER model). Library does not offer 25 mg, so no live error; see open question.
- Direction of any difference: the paper's T2D exposure is lower than non-T2D for the same oral dose (comparator: participants without T2D in OASIS programme, p5-6); the library's oral model is a T2D population model with no T2D switch.
- Unit check: nM to ng/mL is x 4.11358 (`R/drugs_semaglutideOral.R:5`, MW 4113.58); the paper reports nmol/L only. No unit conflict.
