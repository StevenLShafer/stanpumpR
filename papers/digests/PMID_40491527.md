# An 2025: rosuvastatin and ezetimibe co-therapy PK/PD with enterohepatic recirculation

## Study
- Source data: a two-part, open-label, multiple-dose, two-period crossover drug-interaction study (NCT02289430; Kim 2017, ref 3). Healthy Korean male volunteers aged 19-45 y, within +/-20% of ideal body weight, creatinine clearance >= 80 mL/min (Cockcroft-Gault) (PDF p. 2).
- Part A: rosuvastatin 20 mg once daily x 7 d, alone or with ezetimibe 10 mg. Part B: ezetimibe 10 mg alone or with rosuvastatin 20 mg. 14-day washout. 56 enrolled; 50 analysed (25 per part), giving 25 rosuvastatin, 25 ezetimibe and 50 co-therapy profiles (PDF p. 4).
- Table 1 (median, range; PDF p. 4): Part A age 24 y (19-33), weight 69.1 kg (55.0-88.0), height 173 cm (163-182); Part B age 24 (19-37), weight 68.8 kg (44.8-84.0), height 174 cm (159-183).
- Sampling on day 7: 0, 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2, 2.5, 3, 3.5, 4, 5, 6, 8, 12, 24, 48, 72 h post-dose. Standard meals at 4, 10 and 24 h. LDL-C measured before dosing (day 1) and 24 h after the last dose. Doses are the labelled tablet strengths; salt vs base is not stated.
- BLQ: 8.38% of rosuvastatin and 1.25% of ezetimibe samples, all after 48 h, treated as missing.
- Software: Monolix 2024R1, SAEM. Concentrations are plasma (ng/mL; Figure 1). Total ezetimibe = ezetimibe + ezetimibe-glucuronide.

## Model and parameters
Rosuvastatin: two-compartment, first-order absorption, no lag, no zero-order phase; combined error. No covariate significant (all subjects young, healthy men). Rosuvastatin has no enterohepatic recirculation term: the gallbladder loop (Figure 2, PDF p. 5) is in the ezetimibe model only.
Ezetimibe (total): two compartments plus gut and gallbladder, with an on/off bile release at 4, 10 and 24 h, release duration 0.75 h (ke,E fixed 1.33 /h).
All values are typical (population mean) estimates from Table 2, PDF p. 6 (printed 4782), confirmed on the rendered page. Parameters are apparent oral (CL/F etc.); the paper writes them as CL, V.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- LDL-C indirect-response model: production inhibited by both drugs, d(LDL-C)/dt = kin x (1 - C_R/(IC50,R + C_R)) x (1 - C_E/(IC50,E + C_E)) - kout x LDL-C (Figure 2, PDF p. 5). Imax fixed at 1, Hill coefficient 1. Sequential PK then PD; individual PK parameters fixed. End point: plasma LDL-C (mg/dL) 24 h after the day-7 dose. kout is not listed in Table 2.
- Simulated mean LDL-C change at 24 h (1000 virtual subjects, Figure 4, text p. 4783): rosuvastatin 20 mg -51.0 +/- 15.0%; ezetimibe 10 mg -25.3 +/- 16.2%; combination -60.7 +/- 13.9%.
- Not an opioid paper. No MEAC, ke0, tPeak or potency ratio. No therapeutic concentration range is stated.

## Covariates
None retained in any model (age, weight, creatinine, albumin, ALP, ALT, AST, GGT tested; PDF pp. 3-4). Homogeneous young male volunteers.

## Caveats
- Healthy young Korean men only; no women, older adults, patients. Asian ancestry (the label reports about twice the exposure in Asian subjects) is relevant to reading these CL/F values.
- The ezetimibe second peak was weak, so the EHC contribution is poorly identified (AUC ratio without EHC 0.94, p. 4786). Rosuvastatin parameters are not affected, as it has no EHC term.
- Sparse PD sampling (two LDL-C points per treatment).
- Rosuvastatin Vc, Vp and Q are distinctly poorly determined (RSE 13-24%); IIV on Vp (or the second Vc row) is 108% CV.
