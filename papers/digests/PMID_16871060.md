# Manyam 2006: remifentanil-sevoflurane Logit response surface

## Study
- 16 healthy ASA I volunteers (18-45 y, within 25% of ideal weight, nonsmokers), open-label randomised parallel-group crisscross design. Group 1 (primary drug sevoflurane 0.3-6 vol%): age 25.0 +/- 4.2 y, 70.8 +/- 13.0 kg, 174.3 +/- 9.0 cm, 4 M:4 F. Group 2 (primary drug remifentanil 0.5-15 ng/mL): 23.1 +/- 2.7 y, 74.5 +/- 9.3 kg, 177.8 +/- 8.4 cm, 7 M:1 F (Table 2, PDF p4). Pretreated with glycopyrrolate 0.2 mg and pancuronium 1 mg.
- Remifentanil by TCI to predicted effect-site Ce using Minto et al. PK in STANPUMP (Harvard Pump 22); sevoflurane inhaled by mask, end-tidal vol%. Primary drug stepped up three times with 0, low, high background of the second drug. Measurements 5 min after reaching each target (pseudo-steady state); no blood or end-tidal assay of remifentanil (Ce is model-predicted).
- End points: OAA/S score (loss of response <=1 = nonresponsive; >=4 = awake) and three pain surrogates at supramaximal levels (50 mA tetanus, 50 psi pressure, 50 C 5 s), plus response to laryngoscopy.

## Model and parameters
Logit response surface: P = 1/(1+exp(b0 - b1*Cs - b2*Cr - b3*Cs*Cr)), Cs = end-tidal sevoflurane vol%, Cr = remifentanil effect-site ng/mL (Eq 3, Appendix 1, PDF p11). b3 > 0 synergy, = 0 additive, < 0 antagonism. ## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- Opioid audit (remifentanil): no MEAC / minimum analgesic concentration as such; the closest values are the EC95 isobole concentrations above for tetanic stimulation (surrogate noxious stimulus, effect-site Ce as predicted by Minto PK in STANPUMP, no assay), defined as 95% probability of no response to 50 mA.
- No ke0, t1/2 keo, tPeak or hysteresis reported for remifentanil; the paper uses the Minto model's own effect site (not stated numerically). No equianalgesic or potency ratio; reductions are of sevoflurane requirement.
- Sevoflurane MAC reference quoted: 2.2 vol%; plateau 0.75 vol% = 0.34 MAC (about 66% MAC reduction), similar to MAC-awake 0.35 MAC (Discussion, PDF p8-9).
- Remifentanil added to sevoflurane is synergistic for sedation and all analgesic surrogates.

## Covariates
None modelled; group sex imbalance judged not to matter.

## Caveats
Sedation surface from unstimulated volunteers; surrogate pain; supramaximal limits (censoring); hyperalgesic responses at low sevoflurane discarded; Logit leaves a ~0.0007 effect at zero drug; Ce is model-predicted, not measured; 16 subjects, healthy 18-45 y; opposite result from Dahan with alfentanil attributed to BIS vs OAA/S.
