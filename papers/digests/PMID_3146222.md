# Bourgeois 1988: valproate interactions with other drugs (narrative review)

## Study
Narrative review in a valproate supplement (Am J Med 84 suppl 1A, printed pp 29-33 = PDF pp 1-5). No new data;
summarises cited studies (45 references). No PK model.

## Model and parameters
None. Quantitative statements reported from cited work (PDF page / printed page):
- **Inducers -> valproate** (p2/30): Reunanen: valproate levels 38% lower with carbamazepine, 36% lower with
  phenytoin; children on 30 mg/kg/day: levels about 50% lower with phenytoin (De Wolff); Sackellares (children):
  level:dose ratio reduced 45% by phenobarbital, 59% by phenytoin, no further reduction with both; Henriksen:
  levels rose 50-122% after inducer withdrawal (phenytoin 122%, phenobarbital 67%, carbamazepine 50%); Cloyd:
  half-life 7.1 -> 11.8 h, clearance down ~40%, dose down 50-60% after withdrawal (Figure 1, target 75 mg/L);
  Perucca: clearance +88%, half-life -30% on polytherapy; Chiba: clearance +81%, half-life -24%, Vd +36%.
  Summary: valproate dose usually x1.5 to 2 with carbamazepine, phenytoin or a barbiturate.
- **Valproate -> phenobarbital** (p3/31): +81% levels in 5 of 13 patients; concentration:dose ratio +57% in children;
  bound fraction 42.9% -> 31.5%.
- **Valproate -> ethosuximide** (p3/31): Mattson & Cramer: serum ethosuximide +53% (mean) in 4 of 5 patients; the
  non-responder was the only one on no other antiepileptic. Bauer 1982 (normal volunteers at steady state): no rise.
  Pisani 1984 (single-dose): half-life up, total clearance down, renal clearance unchanged (no numbers given here).
  Author's conclusion: inhibition probably real but clinically significant only with additional competing AEDs.
- **Valproate -> carbamazepine** (p4/32): in vitro free fraction 23.5% -> 29.5%; epoxide +101% (valproate) and +330%
  (valpromide); valpromide fourfold epoxide rise.
- **Phenytoin-valproate**: both ~90% bound; displacement then inhibition; free level matters.
- Table I (p2/30, adapted from Browne 1988): qualitative directions only; **ethosuximide is not in Table I**.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None quantified. Notes total phenytoin in the "therapeutic range" may be toxic in the presence of valproate.

## Covariates
Age: inducer effects on valproate more pronounced in children.

## Caveats
Secondary source; all numbers are from cited studies, often with small n. Says nothing about inducers acting on
ethosuximide.
