# Fu 2022: nifedipine PopPK in CKD, effect of PTH

## Study
- Prospective observational study, Third Xiangya Hospital, Changsha, Aug 2017-Apr 2019 (NCT03695107). 157 Chinese adults with CKD and hypertension (KDIGO stage 2/3/4/5 = 11/20/62/64, patients on prior dialysis excluded), 612 plasma concentrations (2-4 per patient). Age median 54 (24-84), BMI 23.4, 63 male / 94 female (Table 1, PDF p.5).
- Drug: Bayer nifedipine controlled-release 30 mg tablets, oral, for 7 days; sparse samples on day 7: pre-dose, 3-4 h, 6-8 h, 24 h post-dose (PDF p.2).
- Assay: LC-MS/MS of plasma (total nifedipine, deuterated internal standard), linear 1-200 ng/mL, LLOQ 1 ng/mL.
- Phoenix NLME, FOCE-ELS; bootstrap 1000, pc-VPC, Monte Carlo (1000 per scenario).

## Model and parameters
One-compartment, zero-order absorption, first-order elimination, additive residual error (base OFV 5141.5, AIC 5155.5; PDF p.4). Final covariate model (PDF p.5-6):
V/F = 2300.26 x (ALT/14)^0.48; CL/F = 49.61 x (Age/54)^-0.22 x (Hb/94)^-0.35 x (PTH/156.84)^-0.22 (all with exp(eta)); K0 with exp(eta). Units: age y, Hb g/L, PTH pg/mL, ALT U/L; reference values are the cohort medians (Table 1).

Bootstrap (1000/1000 successful) medians agree with the estimates: CL 49.53 (95% CI 44.15-54.98), V 2330.24 (1892.23-2779.64), K0 4.13 (3.62-4.77).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None: no concentration-effect model, no blood-pressure end point, no therapeutic range stated. Not an opioid paper.

## Covariates
Retained: age, haemoglobin and PTH on CL/F; ALT on V/F (each by OFV drop > 7.879 on backward elimination). Not retained: sex, BMI, GFR/creatinine, hematocrit, other liver tests, co-medication, and genotypes CYP3A4*1G, CYP3A4 rs4646437, CYP3A5*3, NR1I2, NF-kB, IL-3, MDR1 C3435T. Simulated dosing (Table 4, text layer): 30 mg reference; 22.5 mg at PTH 687.27 pg/mL; 52.5-60 mg at PTH 9.34; 75 mg at Hb 40 with PTH 9.34. The 30 mg tablet is split in simulation to 7.5/15/22.5 mg, which a CR product may not allow.

## Caveats
- The authors name no limitations beyond the need for larger multicentre studies; sparse sampling (2-4 per patient), single CR formulation, no free-drug level, Chinese patients only.
- Because only oral data exist, CL and V are apparent (/F), bioavailability changes in CKD are not separable.
- PTH-CL relation is observational; covariates Hb, age and PTH are partly collinear in CKD (derived: not tested in the paper).
- Text and table directions for AUC vs covariates disagree (see open questions).
