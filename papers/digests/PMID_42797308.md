# González de la Cruz 2026: predictors of sertraline trough concentrations (MedeA cohort)

## Study
Prospective real-world pharmacogenetic (PGx) implementation study (MedeA program, Extremadura, Spain,
Feb 2022 - Apr 2026). 153 Spanish patients on sertraline >= 7 days, self-reported adherent, not pregnant.
Predominantly elderly and multimorbid: median age 73 (Q1-Q3 61-80.5) y, 73.9% women, BMI 27.7 kg/m2, median 10
concomitant medications. Median dose 50 mg/day (25-300); median treatment duration 270 days. One pre-dose (trough)
plasma sample per patient; sertraline and norsertraline by LC-MS/MS (Chromsystems kit; LLOQ 6.44 ng/mL sertraline,
6.42 ng/mL norsertraline; below-LLOQ imputed as LLOQ/2). Genotypes by TaqMan: CYP2C19, CYP2B6, CYP2D6 (with copy
number), CYP2C9, CYP3A4, and the CYP2C:TG haplotype; CPIC/DPWG phenotype assignment. Univariable tests and
multivariable linear regression (140 complete cases), HC3 robust SE sensitivity analysis. Exploratory.

## Model and parameters
NOT a population PK model: no clearance, volume, ka or half-life is estimated. Outcomes are trough concentration,
concentration-to-dose ratio (C/D, (ng/mL)/(mg/day)), and sertraline/norsertraline metabolic ratio (MR).

Dose vs concentration Spearman r = 0.41 for both sertraline and norsertraline. 18 patients (11.8%) > 150 ng/mL,
5 (3.3%) < 10 ng/mL. Two patients who were both CYP2C19 and CYP2B6 gPM had 165.3 and 232.1 ng/mL. Adjusted R2:
concentration 0.08, C/D 0.21, MR 0.05. Univariable: age >= 65 y higher C/D (0.9 vs 0.6, P = 0.02); polypharmacy
higher concentration and C/D.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD; clinical response and adverse effects not assessed. Introduction cites the earlier AGNP reference range
10-150 ng/mL (alert 300) and a more recent AGNP specification of 10-50 ng/mL for depression with alert level
150 ng/mL (ref 5), and a report that < 25 ng/mL may be associated with lower response. No ke0. Not an opioid paper.

## Covariates
CYP2C19 poor-metabolizer status (higher trough). No independent effect of CYP2B6, CYP2D6, CYP2C9, CYP3A4, sex,
age or number of drugs on concentration; smoking and CYP2C:TG associated only with metabolic ratios.

## Caveats
Single trough per patient, self-reported adherence, dose timing unverified; elderly polymedicated cohort; very few
gPMs and strong inhibitor users; heteroscedastic C/D model; exploratory, no prespecified hypothesis; supplementary
tables not read.

  "Models of ... and González de la Cruz (2026) are not combined with this one"): the paper is a regression
  analysis of steady-state trough concentrations and C/D ratios, with no population PK model or PK parameters, so
  it could not be "mixed in" to a compartmental model in any case. **discrepancy** (mischaracterisation, no effect
  on the simulation).

  (R/drugs_sertraline.R:125-136) omits it. **discrepancy** (incomplete citation). Suggested: Pharmaceutics
