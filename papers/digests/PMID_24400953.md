# Spoorenberg 2014: oral vs IV dexamethasone in community-acquired pneumonia

## Study
Randomized open-label parallel-group study (NCT01390012), St Antonius Hospital, Nieuwegein, Sept 2011-Oct 2012.
30 adults hospitalised with CAP: 15 oral (age 68.5 +/- 13.3 y), 15 IV (73.7 +/- 12.6 y); 13/15 male in each arm;
BMI median 27.5 (oral) vs 23.0 (IV); IV group more severely ill (PSI, CRP, leukocytes). Dosing once daily for 4 days:
IV 4 mg dexamethasone bolus (1 ampoule = 5.26 mg dexamethasone disodium phosphate = 4 mg dexamethasone, i.e. base
equivalent) or oral 6 mg (4 x 1.5 mg dexamethasone tablets). Day-1 samples at 0, 20, 40, 60 min, 2, 3, 4, 8, 12 h;
troughs days 2-4. Serum by HPLC-UV (240 nm), LOQ 2.5 ug/L. NCA in MWPharm 3.60 (Kinfit): AUC0-inf trapezoidal,
ke by log-linear regression, CL = dose/AUC, V = CL/ke. Bioavailability = AUC ratio (geometric means) x dose ratio.

## Model and parameters
No compartmental model. Results (medians, IQR):
- AUC0-inf: IV 626 ug.h/L (401-1161); oral 774 ug.h/L (618-1146); P = 0.37 (text and abstract).
- AUC ratio oral 6 mg / IV 4 mg = 1.22 (95% CI 0.81-1.82); bioavailability F = 81% (95% CI 54-121%).
- CV of AUC: 60% IV, 43% oral.
- Oral Cmax 64.4 ug/L (IQR 38.7-77.8) at 2 h.
- Table 2 (row assignment by position; oral vs IV): t1/2 6.9 (6.4-8.6) vs 9.0 (6.2-12.4) h; V 1.09 (0.86-1.42) vs
  0.94 (0.72-1.22) L/kg; MRT 12.4 (9.7-17.1) vs 10.3 (8.2-12.3) h; CL 7.7 (5.2-9.7) vs 6.4 (3.4-10.0) L/h (oral value
  is CL/F). None significantly different. (The Table 2 AUC cells print "774 [146]" / "626 [161]", which do not match
  the text IQRs; the text values are used.)
- Table 3 troughs 24 h after doses about 3-9 ug/L, no route or day differences.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None modelled; IL-6 and CRP in the supplement only. No adverse drug events.

## Covariates
None analysed (n too small); groups imbalanced for severity and BMI.

## Caveats
Parallel groups (not crossover), so F carries between-subject variance (CI 54-121%); n = 15 per arm; elderly, ill
patients; IV group sicker. Healthy-volunteer F quoted as 70-78% (refs 9, 10). The IV clearance here (median 6.4 L/h)
is about a third of Hong 2007's healthy-volunteer 18.1 L/h.
