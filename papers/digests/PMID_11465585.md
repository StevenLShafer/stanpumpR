# Struys 2001: closed-loop BIS propofol vs standard practice

## Study
Twenty female ASA I-II patients (18-60 y) for gynaecologic laparotomy, randomised 10 per group. Both groups: diazepam 10 mg oral, remifentanil 0.50 ug/kg/min before intubation then 0.25 ug/kg/min, started 2 min before induction; rocuronium 0.5 mg/kg after LOC. Demographics (Table 1, PDF p6): age 42 +/- 8 vs 46 +/- 4 y, weight 67 +/- 10 vs 59 +/- 9 kg, height 166 +/- 6 vs 163 +/- 5 cm (group I vs II). Group I: closed loop (RUGLOOP, Ghent), BIS target 50 (A-2000 monitor, v3.4). Induction: plasma-target TCI starting 3 ug/mL, +0.5 ug/mL every 50 s until BIS target; then a patient-specific Hill (sigmoid Emax) curve of BIS against predicted Ce was fitted, loop closed and target switched to effect site. Effect-site model "computed to yield a time to peak effect of 1.6 min after bolus, as published by Schnider" (PDF p3 / printed p7). In maintenance the Hill curve is shifted horizontally to match each measured BIS (Eq 1), plus a derivative term: difference of two successive BIS times a differential factor 0.05 (Eq 2). BIS signal quality index below 15 freezes the controller. Group II: manual propofol 300 mL/h until loss of consciousness, then 10 mg/kg/h, changed by 2 mg/kg/h on haemodynamic or somatic signs (Ausems criteria). Concentration of the propofol syringe is not stated in the text read.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End points: BIS (controlled variable in group I), systolic BP (reference in group I, controlled in group II), recovery times. No ke0 or t1/2 ke0 is reported; the only effect-site timing figure is the 1.6 min time to peak after bolus built into the effect-site targeting (taken from Schnider 1999; not measured here). The observed T(peak,BIS) in Table 3 (290 s group I, 516 s group II) is the time to lowest BIS in a titrated induction, not a ke0 estimate. No EC50 or gamma is given (per-patient Hill fits not tabulated). Not an opioid paper in its aims; remifentanil was a fixed background, so no MEAC, ke0 or potency ratio is reported for it.

## Covariates
None analysed; the Hill curve is individualised per patient from induction data.

## Caveats
n = 10 per group, women only, one BIS target (50), no stress tests, standard-practice group titrated on haemodynamics rather than BIS (authors acknowledge). BIS lags about 15 s (30-s window). Authors own the algorithm.
