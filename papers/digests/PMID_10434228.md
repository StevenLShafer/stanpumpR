# Shader 1999: single-sample population PK of IR methylphenidate in 273 children with ADHD

## Study
Naturalistic, three US sites. 273 children/adolescents 5-18 y (mean 11.1, SE 0.15; range 5.4-18.0), 212 boys / 61 girls,
mean weight 41.6 kg (Table I), DSM-IV ADHD, good responders on stable racemic IR methylphenidate >= 4 weeks: bid n = 109
(mean 12.6 + 12.4 mg, interval 4.3 h, 0.296 mg/kg/dose), tid n = 164 (13.5 + 13.4 + 12.4 mg, intervals 4.1 and 3.7 h,
0.360 mg/kg/dose). 29% on co-medication (clonidine 34, imipramine 20, ...). One venous plasma sample per child at an
assigned time; 16 children (South Dakota) resampled after 1 month. LC/MS/MS, range 0.25-10.00 ng/mL, meperidine IS; the
paper does not state that the assay is enantioselective, and it reports a single methylphenidate concentration (i.e.
total d + l). Unweighted nonlinear regression, SAS PROC NLIN (naive pooled), one compartment, first-order absorption
and elimination, steady state assumed, CL proportional to body weight; equations in the Appendix.

## Model and parameters
Appendix: C = DOSE x F1 x (F2 e^-BETA t - F3 e^-KA t) summed over the day's doses, F1 = BETA x KA x 16666.67 /
(CLKG x WTKG x (KA - BETA)), F2 = 1/(1 - e^-24 BETA), F3 = 1/(1 - e^-24 KA) (dose mg, C ng/mL, t h, 24 h dosing cycle).

r^2 = 0.43. Repeat substudy (n = 16): means 9.56 and 8.84 ng/mL at times 1 and 2, r = 0.83. No lag, no bioavailability
term, no interindividual variability (naive pooled).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None modelled. Background only: IR methylphenidate best effects in first 2-3 h; single-dose literature t½ 2.0-3.5 h,
Cmax 6-15 ng/mL at 0.3 mg/kg. No therapeutic concentration range established.

## Covariates
Body weight (CL proportional to weight, by assumption, not tested). Sex analysed separately with similar estimates; age
not in the model (age-weight relation y = 16.9 + 0.076 x age^2.37, r^2 0.54).

## Caveats
- One sample per child; terminal phase thinly sampled; authors note t½ 4.5 h exceeds single-dose values and may reflect
  sparse terminal data or dose dependence.
- KA from the bid subgroup only, then fixed.
- Naive pooled: no IIV; sampling times sometimes imputed (pre-dose assumed 15 min before dose).
- Co-medications not modelled; responders only.
