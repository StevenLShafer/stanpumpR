# Kramer 1974: digoxin, two- versus three-compartment fit after an IV bolus

## Study
- Five healthy men, 23-28 y, 72.7-85.5 kg, heights 70-75 in, creatinine clearance 89-166 mL/min (Table I, PDF p. 5; the D.L. clearance is printed 112 with a smudge, read as 112).
- 1.0 mg digoxin (Lanoxin injection) by rapid IV injection after a 12 h fast.
- Serum sampled at 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 14, 18, 22, 30, 45 min and 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 16, 24, 48, 72 h (PDF p. 5). Actual times and concentrations in Table III (PDF p. 8); 2-min values 20.5-26.5 ng/mL, 72-h values 0.16-0.53 ng/mL (W.S. last sample 0.16 at 4320 min).
- Assay: 125I radioimmunoassay (Schwarz-Mann), duplicates averaged, serum (not plasma or whole blood), digoxin total. The authors say metabolites interfere little. Sensitivity limit 0.25 ng/mL.
- Fitting: NONLIN, weighted least squares, weight W = 351 x exp(-0.294 x observed conc) (ng/mL) from replicate assays of spiked serum (eq. 15, Table II, PDF p. 6). The microconstants and V1 were iterated; alpha, beta, gamma derived.

## Model and parameters
Two-compartment (eq. 1) and three-compartment mammillary (eq. 4, all elimination from the central compartment) were fitted to each subject. All rate constants are printed as "hr^-1 x 10", so divide the printed number by 10 (e.g. printed 9.8 = 0.98 /h).

Model comparison (Table IV, PDF p. 10): WSSR 2-comp vs 3-comp, F ratio: C.C. 262 vs 202 (F 2.21, not significant); T.F. 51.5 vs 32.4 (4.42); C.J. 307 vs 74.4 (22.0); W.S. 289 vs 72.7 (20.8); D.L. 321 vs 135 (9.66). Critical F at p=0.05: 3.68 (2,15 df), 3.74 (2,14 df). Four of five improved significantly with the third compartment.

Table V, two-compartment (PDF p. 10), rate constants /h after dividing by 10: mean k12 0.76, k21 0.12, k10 0.29, V1 58 L, t1/2 beta 26 h (harmonic mean); individual V1 50, 60, 55, 63, 62 L.

Table VI, three-compartment (PDF p. 12), per subject (C.C., T.F., C.J., W.S., D.L.), printed values /10 in /h:
- k12: 9.8, 7.3, 8.0, 7.9, 7.4 (mean 8.0)
- k21: 0.78, 0.87, 0.28, 0.40, 0.71 (mean 0.61)

Derived by this card (not printed in the paper): clearance k10 x V1 per subject 10.3, 12.4, 9.4, 15.8, 12.9 L/h (mean 12.1 L/h, about 0.16 L/h/kg at 76.6 kg). Vss = V1 x (1 + k12/k21 + k13/k31) per subject about 578, 485, 1420, 964, 683 L (6.7-19 L/kg).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No effect measure, ke0, EC50, or therapeutic or toxic concentration range is reported. ECG, phonocardiogram and carotid pressure were monitored only for safety. The authors call 1 mg about the largest safe volunteer dose. Not an opioid; no MEAC, ke0, tPeak or potency ratio to record.

## Covariates
None modelled. Table I gives age, height, weight, creatinine clearance and serum potassium, but no relationship is fitted (n = 5).

## Caveats
- n = 5 healthy young men; the central-compartment microconstants are poorly determined in some subjects (SD 31-47 on printed k12 and k13 of C.C., Table VI).
- t1/2 gamma ranges 32-131 h. The terminal phase is inferred from 72 h of data against a 0.25 ng/mL assay limit, which the authors acknowledge.
- A 1974 RIA with possible metabolite cross-reaction; concentrations are not comparable to modern fluorescence or LC-MS assays.
- Subject C.C. did not show a significant improvement with the third compartment.
