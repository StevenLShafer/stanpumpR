# Ngan Kee 2017: closed-loop phenylephrine, boluses vs infusion, cesarean spinal

## Study
Randomized, 2-arm, single-blinded (patient-blinded) trial, Prince of Wales Hospital, Hong Kong. 214 enrolled, 204 analysed (102 per arm; 10 excluded for pump/tubing fault 4, shivering 3, inadequate block 2, BP monitor fault 1). Healthy term (>=36 wk) singleton elective cesarean, ASA <3, weight >=50 kg, height 140-180 cm, no hypertension. Table 1 (PDF p4): age 32.8 (4.4) bolus vs 33.4 (4.6) infusion y; weight 66.3 (8.9) vs 68.5 (9.7) kg; height 1.57 vs 1.58 m; BMI 26.7 vs 27.4; baseline SBP 114.4 (9.4) vs 114.3 (9.5) mm Hg (mean SD).
Spinal: 2.2 mL hyperbaric bupivacaine 0.5% + fentanyl 15 ug; cohydration up to 2 L Hartmann's. Noninvasive BP every 1 min from 1 min after injection until uterine incision.
Closed loop: phenylephrine 100 ug/mL in a 50 mL syringe, Graseby 3500 pump driven by a laptop. After each BP measurement, dose I (mL) = (10 - error%)/20, error% = (measured SBP - baseline SBP)/baseline SBP x 100, I clamped to 0-1 mL. Infusion arm: I mL/min (i.e. 0-100 ug/min, derived: 1 mL x 100 ug/mL) re-set at each measurement. Bolus arm: I mL as a rapid bolus at 1200 mL/h after each measurement. Outcome: MDAPE (Varvel performance error), plus MDPE, wobble, divergence, cardiac output (USCOM), hypo/hypertension incidence, neonatal outcome.

Hypertension (SBP >120% baseline) in 7 (6.9%) bolus vs 20 (19.6%) infusion, P=.007; hypotension (<80%) 19 (18.6%) vs 13 (12.7%), P=.25 (Table 4). Cardiac output standardized AUC 587 vs 552 %.min, P=.16 (43 and 39 patients measured). No neonatal differences (Table 5).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point is systolic BP held at the patient's own baseline (pressor effect), not a concentration. No EC50, ke0, t1/2ke0, tPeak, MEAC or potency ratio reported. No phenylephrine concentrations measured. Not an opioid paper (opioid audit: none).

## Covariates
None modelled. Inclusion limits only (weight >=50 kg, height 140-180 cm). Doses are absolute (ug, ug/min), not per kg.

## Caveats
Authors: difference between arms small and with no clinical-outcome difference; the infusion arm received more drug partly because the algorithm ignored the time the BP cycle took (actual interval usually >1 min), so the arms were not dose-matched; comparison specific to these algorithms; requires 1-min BP cycling; CO measured in a subset only. Results text and Table 3 disagree on which P value belongs to MDAPE vs MDPE (see open question).
