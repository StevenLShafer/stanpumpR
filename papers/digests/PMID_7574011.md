# Butterworth 1995: milrinone bolus dose-response and PK after bypass

## Study
- Open-label, dose-escalating, two centres (Wake Forest, Yale). Elective CABG or valve surgery with LVEF >= 40%; excluded if inotropes or balloon pump were needed to separate from bypass, or if cardiac index was >= 3 L/min/m2 after separation (p. 784).
- Of 60 consenting, 30 received milrinone; one (75 ug/kg) had VT within 2 min, needed cardioversion and was dropped, leaving 29 (10, 10, 9 at 25, 50, 75 ug/kg). Table 1 (rendered, medians): age 71 / 64 / 57 y, weight 75 / 72 / 88 kg (ranges 54-110, 65-100, 47-119), females 3 / 1 / 4, serum creatinine 1.0 / 1.2 / 1.1 mg/dL.
- Dose given by hand over 60 s. Haemodynamics at 2.5, 5, 7.5, 10, 20, 30, 45, 60 min; arterial plasma at 1, 2.5, 5, 7.5, 10, 20, 30, 45, 60, 120, 240, 360 min (the 1-min samples excluded for incomplete mixing). HPLC (Harris Laboratories), standards 5-500 ng/mL. Phenylephrine allowed (5 of 10, 5 of 10, 5 of 9 received it in the first 10 min).
- PK: NONMEM with NMVCL, mixed effects, lognormal IIV, extended least squares; also two-stage. One patient's data discarded (mislabelled samples), so Table 3 lists 28 patients. Model choice by Schwarz-Bayesian criterion (p. 785-786).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: thermodilution cardiac index (Fig. 2, rendered), heart rate, MAP, PCWP. All three doses raised CI significantly from 2.5 to 20 min. 25 ug/kg gave lower CI than 50 or 75 at 2.5 min and lower than 50 at 7.5 min; 50 and 75 never differed. Heart rate unchanged at any dose; MAP fell only after 75 ug/kg (P = 0.04 at 2.5 min).
- Time course: the Discussion (p. 790, PDF p. 8) states that most patients had reached their maximal increase in CI at the 5-min measurement. Figure 2's group means (read from the rendered figure, approximate) do not peak at 5 min: 25 ug/kg rises to about 3.5 L/min/m2 at 20 min, 50 ug/kg reaches about 3.75 at 7.5 min and 3.85 at 10 min (about 3.25 at 5 min), and 75 ug/kg is highest at 2.5 min (about 3.65). No ke0 or effect-compartment model was fitted.
- Concentration-response: neither CI nor its % rise at 5 min correlated with the concurrent concentration. Efficacy at 10 min (>= 15% CI rise, or CI >= 3) was > 50% in every concentration stratum, including < 60 ng/mL (Table 2), so no EC50 or threshold could be defined; the authors suggest the effective threshold after bypass may be below the 100 ng/mL reported in chronic heart failure.
- Epinephrine rescue in the first 10 min at 25 ug/kg: 2 of 10 (abstract, p. 783), 1 (Results text, p. 787) or 3 (Fig. 2 caption, p. 787, rendered); none at 50 or 75 in all three places. The three counts disagree; unresolved.

## Covariates
None in the model. Site and dose tested for the haemodynamics; dose did not influence the PK parameters.

## Caveats
- Not blinded, no control group; patients had near-normal function (CI just below 3).
- Elimination clearance poorly identified (CI 0.004-1.061 L/min) and V3 CI not estimable; 6-h sampling; 25 ug/kg group's terminal phase truncated (concentrations detectable after 120 min in 2 of 10; Fig. 5 caption).
- Recommends 50 ug/kg as the bolus after bypass.
