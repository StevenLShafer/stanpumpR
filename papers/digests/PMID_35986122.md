# Nijstad 2022: vincristine population PK in children (saturable beta-tubulin binding)

## Study
Prospective observational study at the Princess Maxima Center (NL; Erasmus MC ethics) combined with a UK Ewing-sarcoma
cohort and three historical cohorts (Lee, n=24 UK only; van de Velde, n=37; Barnett, n=26). Patients up to 18 y (UK
cohort up to 24 y) with a central line; Down syndrome excluded; GFR <60 mL/min/1.73 m2 excluded in the UK study.
Vincristine given per local protocol, 1-2 mg/m2 capped at 2 mg, as bolus or 15-113 min infusion (PDF p.3-4).
Table I (PDF p.4, median (range)): N=206 patients, 253 occasions, 1297 samples (30 below LLOQ, entered as half the
LLOQ), 1 (1-5) occasions per patient, 5 (1-8) samples per occasion; age 8.3 (0.04-33.9) y; 25 patients under 1 y;
weight 27.1 (2.9-126.0) kg; 98 female (48%); dose 1.6 (0.1-2.0) mg, 1.4 (0.4-2.5) mg/m2, 0.05 (0.02-0.09) mg/kg;
214 boluses, 39 infusions; thrombocytes available for 54% of occasions.
Assay: plasma vincristine by LC-MS/MS (LLOQ 0.25 ng/mL) or LC-MS (Newcastle, LLOQ 0.50 ng/mL), and a 200 uL plasma
LC-MS/MS method (LLOQ 0.10 ng/mL) for the Dutch study. NONMEM 7.3, PsN 4.9.0, FOCE-I; CIs by SIR. Missing age/weight/
height (8 and 2 UK patients) imputed from UK growth charts.

## Model and parameters
Final model (Fig. 1, p.5): central Vc, peripheral Vp, clearance CL from the central compartment, plus a saturable
binding compartment fed from the central AMOUNT: dA(bound)/dt = kon x A(Vc) x (1 - A(bound)/Bmax) - koff x A(bound)
(Eq. 1, p.3; the other ODEs are in Supplementary Table S1). Adding the saturable compartment to the 3-compartment
base model dropped OFV by 80. kon was too fast to estimate and was fixed at 1300 /h (the value giving the lowest OFV).
Allometry: exponent 0.75 for clearances and 1 for volumes, normalised to 70 kg; Bmax: weight exponent fixed to 1 (the
estimated exponent was 0.707), age power estimated, normalised to 18 y. IIV on CL, Q, Vc, Vp, kon, koff; IOV on Bmax.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD. No ke0, tPeak, effect-site, MEAC or analgesic concentration, and no potency ratio (not an opioid). No
concentration-response for efficacy or neuropathy was established; the authors say the central-compartment AUC or
Cmax may not be the right exposure measure because the drug acts in tissue (p.8).

## Covariates
Weight (allometric, fixed exponents) on all PK parameters and Bmax; age on Bmax only (power -0.199, 18 y reference).
Thrombocyte count tested on Bmax (imputed 300 x 10^9/L when missing, 46% of occasions) and rejected: unstable models,
no fall in IOV. Sex, CYP3A4/5 inducers, inhibitors and genotype, tumour type: not tested/not available.
Dosing remarks (Fig. 2, p.5): clearance rises with age like a 1.5 mg/m2 dose; authors suggest full mg/m2 dosing in
infants beyond the neonatal period, 0.05 mg/kg for 0-4 weeks.

## Caveats
Beta-tubulin amount was not measured; the saturable compartment is an interpretation (alternative explanations not
excluded). Large IIV on Vc (122.5%) and kon (126.5%). Thrombocytes not usable. Disease type and state not in the
dataset. Only 25 patients under 1 y (88 samples). Doses stated as "vincristine" mg; salt/base not discussed.
No simulated concentrations or AUC are printed. The published text layer scrambles Table I; checked on the image.
