# dos Santos 2009: clonazepam tablet PK and DSST impairment with acute tolerance

## Study
- 23 healthy men (25 entered, 2 dropped out), mean age 28.6 y (19-45), within 15% of ideal body weight (weights not given), no medications.
- Single oral dose 4 mg as two 2 mg immediate-release tablets with 200 mL water after ≥ 10 h overnight fast; breakfast at 3 h, lunch at 5 h.
- Plasma at 0 (pre), 0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 1, 1.5, 2, 2.5, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 24, 48, 72 h (16 samples incl. predose); DSST (90 s) after each sample.
- Assay: on-line SPE-HPLC-MS/MS, linear 0.35-45 ng/mL; plasma (heparin), total clonazepam; 7-aminoclonazepam not measured (inactive).
- MONOLIX 2.4 (SAEM), MLXTRAN ODEs; exponential IIV; additive error on log concentrations.

- Simultaneous PK/PD (Table 2, sigmoid Emax with tolerance; text-layer RSE columns misaligned): Tlag 0.357 h, KA 1.76 /h, VC 130 L, K10 0.0214, K12 0.108, K21 0.285 /h.
- Individual Cmax 6.93-31.8 ng/mL, Tmax 1-8 h (p 568). Text says absorption t½ 31 min, which does not equal ln2/2.21 h⁻¹ = 19 min (derived); internal inconsistency in the paper.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: Digit Symbol Substitution Test score (psychomotor impairment). Observed maximum impairment 72.0 ± 3.7% (mean ± SE), 1.5-4 h (25th-75th percentile) after dose (p 568).
- No effect compartment: PK and PD closely related in the first hours; peak concentration and peak effect at the same time; a model with both effect compartment and tolerance "was not retained" (p 571). No ke0, t½ke0 or tPeak.
- Final PD (Table 2, Appendix): E = BSL·(1 − C^GA/(C50^GA + C^GA)), C50 = EC50 + AC·(1 − exp(−KT·(t − Tlag))). BSL 66.4, EC50 9.33 ng/mL, AC 15.4 ng/mL, KT 0.0322 /h (tolerance half-life about 21.5 h, p 569; RSE 110%), GA 3.57; Emax fixed at full inhibition.
- Hysteresis: clockwise (delayed ~10-12 h) in 15/23, classical clockwise 3, counterclockwise early 3/23, none 3/23.
- Not an opioid; no MEAC or potency ratio.

## Covariates
None tested (n = 23 homogeneous men).

## Caveats
Men only, young, normal weight; single dose; oral only so all PK parameters are apparent; high RSE on K12/K21 and on tolerance parameters (possible over-parameterisation, authors' own caveat); absorption t½ stated in text inconsistent with KA; tolerance not predictable at the individual level.
