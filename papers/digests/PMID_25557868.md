# Nikanjam 2015: population PK of oxaliplatin in children and adults

## Study
Pooled analysis of five earlier trials (Table 1, PDF p2, printed 496): NCI Organ Dysfunction Working Group
hepatic (n = 55) and renal (n = 34) impairment studies in adults >18 y; St Jude phase I (Peds1, n = 26), COG
phase II (Peds2, n = 46) and PBTC phase II (Peds3, n = 25) in children and young adults. 186 patients, 1,508
concentrations; overall median (range) age 20 (0.6-86) y, weight 59 (7-165) kg, serum creatinine 0.8 (0.2-3.4)
mg/dL, 62% male. Oxaliplatin 130 (60-160) mg/m2 over 2 h (4.3 mg/kg at ≤12 months in Peds2/3), every 2-3 weeks.
Sampling to 168-504 h; 1-13 samples per patient. Analyte: plasma ultrafiltrate platinum, by ICP-MS (LLOQ 1 ng/mL)
except the hepatic study (atomic absorption, LLOQ 10 ng/mL). Peds2 had no creatinine (assigned the Peds1/Peds3
median); AST and bilirubin missing outside Peds3 and Hepatic (assigned medians). NONMEM 7.2, FOCE-I, ADVAN11
TRANS4; six-fold cross-validation; 1,000-sample bootstrap (86% converged).

## Model and parameters
Three compartments, allometric in weight with no reference weight (Table 2 and the equations beneath it, PDF p7,
printed 501, read on the page image):

- CL (L/h) = WTKG^0.75 × (0.6/SCR)^θ8 as printed; θ1 does not appear in the printed CL equation.
- Q3 (L/h) = WTKG^0.75 × θ3; Q2 (L/h) = Q3 + WTKG^0.75 × θ2.
- V1 (L) = WTKG × θ4 × (AGE/21)^θ7; V2 = WTKG × θ5; V3 = WTKG × θ6.

Reported summaries (Results, PDF p5): median (IQR) individual CL 0.58 (0.47-0.79) Hepatic, 0.34 (0.23-0.53)
Renal, 0.78 Peds1, 0.74 Peds2, 0.81 Peds3 L/h/kg^0.75; CL varies 6.7-fold over the creatinine range, V1 2.2-fold
over the age range; V (L/kg) 32% larger in adults than children. Monte Carlo at 130 mg/m2: median AUC 14.2 (2-6 y),
16.8 (6-12 y), 16.5 (12-18 y), 17.3 (adults) µg·h/mL. Female CL 86% of male in screening, not retained.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None: the authors state pharmacodynamic data were not consistently available. No ke0, tPeak, target concentration or
toxicity threshold. Not an opioid paper.

## Covariates
SCR on CL (power, exponent 0.67, reference 0.6 mg/dL); age on V1 (power, exponent 0.16, reference 21 y); weight
allometric on all (0.75 on clearances, 1 on volumes). Tested and dropped: sex, AST, bilirubin, race; age on CL is
absorbed by SCR.

## Caveats
- Printed CL equation omits θ1 (0.67); the reported CL values need it (derived: at the cohort medians 59 kg,
  SCR 0.8, θ1 gives 0.67 × 0.825 = 0.553 L/h/kg^0.75, near the Hepatic median 0.58).
- Text calls SCR on CL and age on V1 "linear"; the equations are powers, and the printed 6.7-fold and 2.2-fold
  ranges are (3.4/0.2)^0.67 = 6.67 and (86/0.6)^0.16 = 2.21 (derived), so the equations are what was fitted.
- Methods say "first-order absorption" with ADVAN11 (an IV three-compartment subroutine); an IV infusion model.
- Fig. 2 legend swaps panels a and b (panel a is plotted against creatinine, b against age).
- Dose basis: the paper never says whether doses were entered as mg oxaliplatin or mg platinum; dose/CL
  reproduces the Monte Carlo AUC only with mg oxaliplatin against platinum ng/mL (derived: 70 kg, 1.82 m2,
  SCR 0.8 gives 236.6 mg / 13.38 L/h = 17.7 µg·h/mL vs printed 17.3).
- Ultrafiltrate platinum, not intact oxaliplatin; two assays with tenfold different LLOQs; Peds2 creatinine
  imputed; positive bias in children (median error 13.2%).
