## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No ke0 or effect-site model. Tentative therapeutic plasma concentration 1-4 mg/L (conversion 3.90 umol/L per mg/L), unproven; some patients tolerated >10 mg/L; trough levels did not differ between responders and non-responders in the cited trials (1.7 vs 1.8; 1.91 vs 2.02; 1.53 vs 1.37 mg/L). Adverse effects at isolated high concentrations (nausea 6.0, vomiting 8.0, headache/ataxia >10 mg/L). Not an opioid paper; no MEAC, potency or ke0.

## Covariates
Age: elderly (mean 71 y, n=12) vs young (31 y): clearance 37% lower, V 12% lower, t1/2 31.2 vs 24.9 h (PDF p5, Table I). Gilbert's syndrome: CL 32% lower (30.2 vs 44.2 mL/min), t1/2 31.2 vs 22.8 h. Comedication: inducers (CBZ, PT, PB) shorten t1/2; valproate prolongs it and cut total clearance ~21% in 6 volunteers (Yuen 1992, PDF p6); paracetamol lowered AUC 20% and t1/2 15%. Lamotrigine did not change other antiepileptic concentrations except carbamazepine-epoxide (+45% in one report, ~+10% in another). No autoinduction conclusion (conflicting reports). No pediatric data.

## Caveats
1993 review of small studies; half-lives are single-dose or short-term, from different trials with different comedication groupings; no population PK. Inducer half-life values come from epilepsy patients on polytherapy (some with valproate in the 29.6 h group), whereas the 22.8-37.4 h values are healthy volunteers or monotherapy, so "fold change" depends on pairing.
