# Cherniack 1970: O2 and CO2 gas stores of the body

Review (Physiol Rev 1970;50:196-243, PDF pages 1-48, printed page = PDF page + 195). Not a drug paper. The context file lists no repo locations; the repo cites it only in `docs/gas-engine-expansion.md:190` and `papers/pending-work.csv:97` (a copy supplied in-session, not obtained through the archive).

## Study
Narrative review of the sizes and forms of body O2 and CO2 stores, the physiology that adjusts them, and mathematical models of transient CO2 and O2 storage (Farhi-Rahn multicompartment, lung-single tissue). No new data.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No drug, no ke0, MEAC or potency. Physiological end points are alveolar/arterial/mixed venous PCO2 and body CO2 and O2 stores. The review states that venous PCO2 rises linearly in apnoea while arterial PCO2 shows a brief curvilinear rise, and that after a ventilation step the single-tissue model behaves like one exponential with T3 about 37 min.

## Covariates
Stores scale with body weight (slopes in mL/kg). Stores vary with age and altitude in animals; the renal bicarbonate adjustment takes 18-24 h, complete in 5-10 days (text, PDF p.10).

## Caveats
1970 review; its numbers are estimates assembled from others' work, with a wide spread in the body dissociation slope (Table 6) that motivated the multicompartment model. The oxygen sections were not read in full.
