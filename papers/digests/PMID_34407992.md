# Shum 2021: maternal-fetal PBPK model of fentanyl (IV and epidural)

## Study
- A simulation study with one new in vitro experiment. Maternal-fetal PBPK (mf-PBPK) model in MATLAB/Simulink: 14-compartment maternal model, 7-compartment fetal model, placenta between them (Fig 1). A novel epidural dosing site (epidural fluid, adipose, vein) was added; the non-pregnant model has the same structure without the placenta/fetus.
- Epidural site parameters were tuned on alfentanil (two non-pregnant epidural studies, Coda 1995 and 1999) and scaled to fentanyl by Caco-2 permeability ratio (Eq 2); alfentanil PBPK validated on five IV studies (AAFE 1.20-1.98) and epidural (AAFE 1.21-1.31).
- Fentanyl: non-pregnant IV model validated against four IV studies (McClain & Hug 1980, Ziesenitz 2015, Rauck 2017, Nozari 2019; AAFE 1.25-1.84) and two epidural studies (AAFE 1.21-1.99). Maternal-fetal model validated against seven studies in labour/caesarean (epidural bolus or infusion; Desprats 1991 and 1995, Bader 1995, Loftus 1995, Moises 2005, de Barros Duarte 2009, Haidl 2018): maternal venous AAFE 1.18-1.86, umbilical vein 1.15-2.36, umbilical artery 1.3-1.65. Acceptance criterion AAFE <= 2 (from interstudy CV of CL 22%).
- Fetal liver microsomes (pooled from 14 fetal livers, gestational age 87-137 d) and recombinant CYP3A7 incubated with fentanyl (2, 5, 15, 30 uM); norfentanyl by LC-MS/MS (LLOQ 1 nM).
- Simulated scenario: single 500 mcg IV bolus to a pregnant woman at term (75.2 kg mother, 3.44 kg fetus, Table 1 footnote).

Other fentanyl Kp values (Table 2): bone 1, brain 3.5, gut 8.4, heart 4.5, kidney 12.1, liver 3.8, lung 13.5, muscle 3.1, skin 2.1, spleen 27.6, pancreas 21.3 (read on page image). Epidural fluid volume 10 mL, epidural adipose 3.5 mL, epidural vein 3.5 mL; bioavailability from epidural fluid assumed 100 percent. Adult Vss quoted as 4 L/kg (Introduction).
In vitro: norfentanyl formation CLint 0.20 +/- 0.05 uL/min/mg protein in pooled FLM; 0.019 +/- 0.001 uL/min/pmol CYP3A7 (Fig 5; the text layer prints "ml", the figure axis says uL; scaling 0.20 x 17.8 mg/g x 129 g reproduces 0.028 L/h). CYP3A7 predicted 0.3 uL/min/mg; measured was 0.7-fold of that.
Simulation results (single 500 mcg IV at term; text p.11): Cmax maternal artery 150, maternal vein 3.9, umbilical vein 11, umbilical artery 2.0 ng/mL. UV/MV ratio above 3 in the first minutes, below 1 by about 15 min; epidural 100 mcg bolus: UV/MV peaks about 2.6 then below 1 at about 30 min, while UV/MA rises gradually to 0.7 at 2 h.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- No pharmacodynamic data, no MEAC, no ke0 / t1/2ke0 / tPeak, no equianalgesic or potency ratio from this study. Opioid audit: none measured.
- The Discussion compares the simulated total UV Cmax of 33 nM (11 ng/mL; 11 / 336.47 = 32.7 nM, derived) with a fentanyl EC50 of 20-24 nM quoted from Lotsch 2005 and Kalvass 2007 (about 6.7-8.1 ng/mL, derived); the end point behind that EC50 is not stated in this paper. The authors note the comparison is sensitive to fetal protein binding (fu 0.01-0.34).
- The paper says the model could be coupled to a PD model; none is built.

## Covariates
Pregnancy physiology (term organ volumes and flows, Table 1); CYP3A4 induction 1.99; maternal fu 0.12 vs 0.16 non-pregnant; fetal fu 0.14. No clearance ratio pregnant:non-pregnant is reported. Derived from the paper's own inputs: maternal hepatic CLint,u 1891 vs non-pregnant 1055 L/h = 1.79 (derived), the product of the assumed induction on the CYP3A4 80 percent share only; total CL in pregnancy (hepatic flow-limited, renal added) is not stated.

## Caveats
- The 1.99 induction is borrowed from midazolam, not measured for fentanyl; no human IV fentanyl data in pregnancy were used to check clearance (validation was epidural/labour concentrations).
- Fetal metabolism is negligible by prediction (extraction ratio below 0.01) and a CYP3A7 IVIVE with pooled mid-gestation microsomes (87-137 d) applied to term.
- Epidural site is a new model whose key parameter (CLpd,vein) was fitted to alfentanil then scaled by permeability.
- Validation averages digitised from published mean curves; AAFE up to 2.36 for UV.
- Plasma total concentrations; fu values for fetal blood vary widely.

## Verification against stanpumpR
- pregnancyRegistry.csv:4: the paper indeed reports no pregnant:non-pregnant clearance ratio and no IV human pregnancy PK: **matches**. Worth recording for later readers: the model's maternal hepatic CLint,u (1891 vs 1055 L/h, derived 1.79x) embeds an assumed CYP3A4 induction of 1.99 on the 80 percent CYP3A4 share; it is an input borrowed from midazolam, not a fentanyl finding, so it is not a usable empirical factor, consistent with the registry's note.
- Comparison with the library fentanyl model (R/drugs_fentanyl.R, Bae 2020): the library CL is 0.704 L/min at 70 kg = 42.2 L/h, with V1+V2+V3 = 242.6 L (derived, about 3.5 L/kg) at 70 kg; Shum's non-pregnant model uses total CL 62 L/h (average of four IV studies, comparator being those studies' means) and Vss about 4 L/kg. Shum's baseline is therefore about 1.5 times the library's clearance, so any factor taken from Shum's pregnant-vs-non-pregnant model would be relative to its own baseline, not the library's.
