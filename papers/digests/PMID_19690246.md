# Doufas 2009: automated responsiveness monitor to titrate propofol sedation

## Study
- 21 healthy volunteers, 20-45 y, arterial propofol samples; ramped propofol effect-site concentration (Ce) with a TCI (Shafer-Gregg effect-site algorithm, Schnider 1998 kinetics) until loss of response to an automated responsiveness monitor (ARM, a button-press response to a computer voice plus vibration, 10-s test, 4 tests per min).
- Fixed-percentage protocol (n=10; 32.4 +/- 7.4 y, 69.0 +/- 7.6 kg, 169.7 +/- 8.1 cm): ramp at 0.6 mcg/mL/min, after loss of response Ce cut by 20, 30, 40, 50, 60 or 70% (random order, six trials), held 6 min. Deramping protocol (n=11; 33.4 +/- 5.8 y, 69.9 +/- 12 kg, 172.4 +/- 9.5 cm): ramp 0.5 mcg/mL/min, then Ce lowered at 0.1, 0.2 or 0.3 mcg/mL/min until three successive responses, held 6 min (PDF pp2-3, printed 779-780).
- Sedation also scored by OAA/S (sum of four components, /20; conscious = above 10). BIS A-2000 (BIS 3.3). HPLC propofol, CV 4.1% at 2 mcg/mL. NONMEM V logistic models; 7185 data points for Ce and BIS, 461 for OAA/S.

## Model and parameters
No PK model is derived. The kinetics are Schnider 1998; the TCI ke0 was 0.46 /min, but the reported Ce values were recalculated with ke0 0.17 /min (tPeak 2.7 min), stated to fit better when Ce rises 0.1-0.9 mcg/mL/min (PDF p4, read on the image).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: loss and return of response to the ARM (not loss of consciousness), against predicted Ce (ke0 0.17 /min), BIS and OAA/S. Population: healthy young volunteers; propofol alone, no opioid, no N2O.
- Ce50 for ARM response 1.73 mcg/mL (CI 1.55-2.10), Hill gamma 6.20; protocol type did not change it (Table 2 and text, PDF p5-6). Plasma-vs-effect-site: predicted Ce; arterial plasma assay.
- The Discussion (text layer, PDF p7) says Ce50 for the ARM is about 0.5-1 mcg/mL below the Ce for loss of response to verbal command or tactile stimulation in earlier studies (comparator: other published studies, not measured here), and OAA/S50 12.5/20 lies above the loss-of-consciousness threshold of 10/20.
- Combined model (text layer): Ce alone predicts the response with probability 0.82, BIS alone 0.84, both 0.85; BIS contributed 63% of the fractional model.
- ke0 / tPeak: 0.17 /min = tPeak 2.7 min (adopted), versus 0.46 /min = 1.7 min (Schnider 1999). The end point behind 0.17 /min is cited to Doufas 2004 (response to tactile stimulation/BIS), not measured here.
- No EC95, no MEAC, no opioid, no potency ratio. Opioid audit: not an opioid paper.

## Covariates
None in the PD models. Age, weight and height enter only through the Schnider kinetics used by the TCI.

## Caveats
- TCI over-predicted measured arterial concentration, especially during deramping (MDPE 31%, Table 1), so the Ce axis carries error.
- Rapid ramps (0.5-0.6 mcg/mL/min) cause Ce overshoot after the infusion stops; Ce is a model prediction, not measured at the effect site.
- Healthy young volunteers, propofol only; the authors state wider clinical use, with other stimuli and drugs, is untested.
