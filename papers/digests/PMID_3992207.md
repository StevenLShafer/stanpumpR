# Watanakunakorn 1985: antibiotic tolerance of Staphylococcus epidermidis

## Study
In-vitro microbiology; no patients dosed, no pharmacokinetics. 50 S. epidermidis strains from blood cultures of
50 different patients (PDF p2, printed p59). Drugs: methicillin, nafcillin, oxacillin, cephalothin, cefazolin,
vancomycin. Broth (tube) dilution in Mueller-Hinton broth (MHB) and Brain Heart Infusion broth (BHI); inoculum
1 mL of a 10^3 dilution of an overnight culture, stated as about 10^5 to 10^6 organisms (total, not per mL; tube
volume not given); top concentration 256 µg/mL; 37 °C. MIC read at 24 and 48 h; 0.01 mL from each clear tube
subcultured to agar for 24 h; MBC = lowest concentration giving fewer than 5 colonies. Tolerance defined as
MBC/MIC ≥ 16 (confirmed on the rendered page; the text layer reads "316").

## Model and parameters
No pharmacokinetic model. **No MIC or MBC values are reported**: only whether each strain/drug/medium/time was
tolerant.
```yaml
parameters: []  # in-vitro tolerance study; no PK parameters, and no MIC or MBC values reported
```

Tolerance counts (PDF p3, printed p60, text and Table I, read on the page image):
- Vancomycin: 17 of 50 strains tolerant (34%); 13 tolerant to vancomycin only (5 in MHB only, 4 in BHI only,
  4 in both; 8 at 24 h only, 2 at 48 h only, 3 at both).
- Beta-lactams: 16 of 50 tolerant (32%); 14 tolerant to at least 2 beta-lactams, 13 of these to ≥ 3; 4 cross-tolerant
  to vancomycin. Single-drug cases: one strain to oxacillin only (24 h, BHI), one to nafcillin only (48 h, BHI).
- Per drug among the 14 multiply tolerant strains: nafcillin 13, **cefazolin 13**, methicillin 12, oxacillin 11,
  cephalothin 10 (cefazolin count confirmed by counting Table I: every listed strain except no. 59).
- Tolerance events: MHB 54, BHI 66, both media 48; at 24 h 91, at 48 h only 29, same medium at 24 and 48 h 21.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: in-vitro bactericidal activity (MBC, < 5 colonies from 0.01 mL subculture) relative to the MIC. No
concentration-effect model, no ke0, no therapeutic concentration. Not an opioid paper.

## Covariates
Test conditions only: medium (MHB vs BHI) and incubation time (24 vs 48 h) changed which strains were tolerant;
for many strains tolerance seen at 24 h was gone at 48 h (PDF p4, printed p61).

## Caveats
- Single centre, 1985, S. epidermidis only (not other coagulase-negative staphylococci); methicillin
  susceptibility of the strains not reported.
- No MIC or MBC distributions, so no MIC50/MIC90/MBC90 can be taken from this paper.
- Tolerance is method-dependent (medium, time), as the author stresses; a tube method with < 5 colonies from
  0.01 mL differs from the modern 99.9%-kill microdilution definition.
- The author states the clinical significance of tolerance in S. epidermidis is unknown.
