# Potocka 2011: population PK of human insulin by IV, SC and inhaled (Technosphere) routes

## Study
- Pooled analysis of two open-label, randomised, crossover euglycaemic glucose-clamp studies in healthy
  non-smoking volunteers, 18-40 y, BMI 18-27 kg/m2, normal lung function (PDF p2, printed p1016). Sponsor MannKind;
  two authors are its employees (PDF p10).
- Study 1 (Düsseldorf, n 5): TI 100 U inhaled, regular human insulin (Actrapid) 10 U SC, 5 U IV. Study 2 (Neuss,
  n 12): three single TI doses and 10 U SC (the TI doses pooled are 25, 50 and 100 U). Washout 3-28 days.
- Pooled population (PDF p5): 16 subjects analysed, 14 men, 2 women; age 28 (4) y, weight 76 (11) kg, BMI 24 (2)
  kg/m2, height 179 (9) cm (mean, SD). 651 concentrations, 57 profiles (PDF p6). Exclusions: one IV subject (timing
  errors), one SC profile (three consecutive BLQ), one subject as an inhalation outlier; 51 late concentrations
  dropped where C-peptide suggested endogenous insulin (after 180, 240, 300 min for 25, 50, 100 U TI).
- Clamp at 90 mg/dL by Biostator; endogenous secretion suppressed by a constant IV insulin infusion started 2 h
  before dosing to hold serum insulin at 10-15 µU/mL. All post-dose concentrations were baseline-corrected by
  subtracting the mean of the -120, -90, -60, -30 min samples (PDF p3).
- SC injection in the abdomen. Samples to 360 min. Assay: RIA, range 1.2-400 µU/mL, duplicate (PDF p2).
- NCA in WinNonlin 5.2; population model in NONMEM VI, ADVAN6, FO (FOCE too slow); exponential IIV; combined
  proportional + additive error; inter-occasion variability on F_TI (PDF p3-4).

## Model and parameters
Structure (Figure 1, PDF p4): two-compartment disposition (central V4, peripheral V5), first-order elimination;
inhaled dose enters central from one depot (kaTI); SC dose passes through two sequential first-order depots
(kasc1 into a transit compartment, kasc2 into central). Equations 1-7.

NCA, Table I (PDF p6), mean (%CV): 5 IU IV, n 4: AUC0-last 100.0 (21) µU/mL·h, t½ 1.7 (23) h, AUC0-inf 103.2 (19),
dose-normalised AUC 20.1. 10 IU SC, n 15: tmax 1.5 h (median), Cmax 30.7 (34) µU/mL, AUC0-last 107.0 (26),
dose-normalised 10.7. TI 25/50/100 U: tmax 0.2/0.2/0.33 h, Cmax 54.6/105.3/240.9 µU/mL, AUC0-last
50.0/101.5/218.6, t½ 1.1/1.3/1.4 h, dose-normalised 2.0/2.0/2.2. Bioavailability by NCA about 53% SC and 10-11% TI.

Half-lives (PDF p7): alpha 5 min and beta 93 min, from individual predicted parameters. Derived from the typical
values above: 3.0 min and 85 min (derived: standard two-compartment micro-constants). Distribution phase after IV
about 30 min (PDF p8).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Clamp GIR was collected but no pharmacodynamic analysis is reported in this paper. No effect-site model.

## Covariates
BMI on kasc1 only (eq 15, PDF p7; OFV −9.41; IIV on kasc1 57% to 50%). Weight on V4 (ΔOFV 1.311) and age on V5
(3.893) tested and not retained (Table III, PDF p7). Inhaler device had no effect on F_TI. No covariate on CL or
volumes, so the disposition is absolute, not per kg.

## Caveats
- 16 young, lean, healthy volunteers (14 men); BMI range 18-27, so the BMI effect is extrapolated beyond it.
- IV arm only 4 analysable subjects; mean IV data past 4 h from 2 subjects only (Figure 2 legend).
- Baseline subtraction during an ongoing insulin infusion, and removal of late samples by visual C-peptide
  inspection; the SC NCA AUC is truncated at 6 h, so Fsc may be underestimated (authors).
- FO estimation. Sponsor-employed authors. Equation 15 inconsistency (open question).
- F_TI is per nominal Technosphere unit with the study inhalers, not per SC unit.

- Uncited comparison with the implemented SC regular insulin model (`R/drugs_insulinRegular.R` → Tham 2017,
  `R/insulinTham.R:28`, `:166-168`): Tham regular U100 CL/F 127 L/h at 10 U and V/F 178 L at 80 kg (one compartment),
  ka 0.67 /h. Potocka's equivalents for 10 U SC regular insulin: CL/Fsc 43.4 / 0.52 = 83 L/h and
  (V4 + V5)/Fsc = 69 L (derived), with kasc1 0.63 /h followed by kasc2 1.04 /h. So Tham's apparent clearance is
  about 1.5x and apparent volume about 2.6x Potocka's. Different structures (one vs two compartments, one vs two
  absorption steps), baseline handling (Tham estimated an endogenous baseline; Potocka subtracted the pre-dose
  infusion level) and populations explain some of it; this card does not settle which better fits stanpumpR's
  patients. The library does not cite Potocka for the SC model and no change is implied.
