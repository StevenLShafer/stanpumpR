# Xu 2007: reversible prednisone-prednisolone model on free concentrations

## Study
Model built from digitised mean/typical literature profiles (no individual data): IV bolus prednisolone 16.4 and 49.2 mg (Wald 1992, healthy men), 0.5 h IV infusions of 10 mg prednisolone or prednisone (Ferry 1988), oral prednisone 15 mg (Chakraborty 1999) and 20 mg (Meno-Tetang 2001, women), oral prednisolone (Ferry 1988). Nine datasets fitted simultaneously on a molar basis in ADAPT II (maximum likelihood, proportional variance). Free prednisone = 25% of total (linear binding). Total prednisolone then predicted with Langmuir + linear binding, competitive cortisol binding and a cortisol circadian/suppression model (Rohatagi 1996), solved by Runge-Kutta in Excel.

## Model and parameters
Two species (PN, PNL), each one compartment, first-order elimination from each, reversible linear interconversion (Fig 2). Oral: first-order absorption from a depot, bioavailability F, and an instantaneous first-pass split fPN / fPNL.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Only cortisol suppression (indirect response, Imax 1, IC50 0.98 ng/mL free prednisolone, from Wald 1992). No ke0, MEAC or potency ratio.

## Covariates
None.

## Caveats
Typical-profile fit to digitised literature data across different studies; no inter-individual variability; saturable interconversion not considered (over-parameterisation). Molar fitting basis. Total-prednisolone prediction validated only graphically (r > 0.8, not shown).
