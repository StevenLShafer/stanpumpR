# Ramael 2006: levetiracetam IV vs oral bioavailability, healthy subjects

## Study
- Phase I, SGS Biopharma unit, Antwerp; UCB-sponsored (p. 735).
- 18 healthy white subjects (9 M, 9 F), 19-52 y (mean 35.0, SD 9.3), 50-94 kg (mean 73.3, SD 14.2), BMI 23.9;
  men 84.0 kg, women 62.6 kg (Table I).
- Phase 1: randomized open 2-way crossover, 1500 mg as a 15-min IV infusion (100 mg/min) vs three 500 mg tablets,
  7-day washout, fasted. Phase 2: double-blind, placebo-controlled (2:1), 1500 mg IV every 12 h for 9 doses.
- One subject got about 1200 mg IV and was excluded from single-dose PK (n = 17). Plasma sampled to 36 h; GC with
  nitrogen-phosphorus detection (0.5-40 ug/mL). Non-compartmental (WinNonlin 4.01); Cl = dose/AUC and
  Vd = Cl/lambda_z, both normalized by body weight.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Tolerability only (somnolence, postural dizziness; Doppler echocardiography for pulmonary pressure). No PD model.
Not an opioid paper.

## Covariates
None analysed.

## Caveats
Small, healthy, young; short duration; IV given as 15-min infusion only; the OCR text layer is unreliable (use the
table values above, checked against the image).

## Verification against stanpumpR
- AUC 392 and 428 mg.h/L, 73 kg, 1500 mg (:37-39): Table II 392.4 and 427.9 ug.h/mL, mean weight 73.3 kg.
  **matches**.
- Clearances 3.82 and 3.51 L/h (:38, help :7): not reported in L/h; they are dose / mean AUC (derived 3.82, 3.51).
  The paper's own per-kg means (0.92, 0.85 mL/min/kg) give 4.05 and 3.74 L/h at 73.3 kg. **matches** as derived
  values, but say they are derived.
- Reference (:60-61): authors, journal, volume, pages, DOI correct. **matches**.
- **discrepancy (related)**: the header's half-life check (:37, "Half-life 8.0 h") is set beside this study's
  7.16 h, but the model's own V 65.3 L and CL 3.9 L/h give 11.6 h (derived). The volume is the cause: Ramael's Vd
  is 0.56 L/kg (about 41 L at 73 kg), against the model's 0.93 L/kg (Rhee 2017's V/F).
