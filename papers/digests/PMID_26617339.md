# Naik 2016: alogliptin population PK, weight and renal function

## Study
Pooled population PK of oral alogliptin from three studies (Methods, printed p.701-703; Table 1, Table 2):
- Study 008, phase 3, sparse sampling, T2DM on metformin: 527 randomised (placebo, 12.5 mg, 25 mg daily, 26 weeks); 398 contributed PK.
- Study 002, repeat dose (14 days), T2DM newly diagnosed or diet/exercise only, ages 18-75: 25, 100, 400 mg daily; 45 in the PK set; rich sampling.
- Study 006, single 50 mg dose, rich sampling to 168 h, healthy (CLcr >80) and mild (51-80), moderate (30-50), severe (<30, not dialysis) renal impairment; 48 subjects. Methods also list ESRD on haemodialysis, but Table 2 shows no ESRD row.
- Total n = 491; 3234 plasma samples used (3371 collected). Age 55.0 (SD 10.5) y, weight 87.7 (18.4) kg, height 167 cm, mean CLcr 74.6 (24.9) mL/min, 253 F / 238 M, 385 White (Table 2).
- Assay: validated LC-MS/MS on plasma, linear 1-1000 ng/mL, LLOQ 1 ng/mL. Values below LLOQ were dropped, not imputed.
- NONMEM 7 (FO and FOCE-I), forward (alpha 0.05) / backward (0.001) covariate selection, bootstrap n=1000, VPC.

## Model and parameters
Two compartments, first-order absorption and elimination, apparent parameters (oral CL/F, V/F; bioavailability not separable). Exponential IIV on CL/F, Vc/F, Vp/F, Q/F; proportional residual error. Final model (Eq. 6 and 7, PDF p.5; Table 3, PDF p.6, printed 705), typical values:

- CL/F (L/h) = 17.1 x (CLcr / 72.95)^0.534 x (WTKG / 84.90)^0.345; footnote a: for severe renal impairment the coefficient is 9.61 instead of 17.1.
- Vc/F (L) = 194 x (WTKG / 84.90)^1.11.
- Vp/F, Q/F, ka have no covariate. CLcr in mL/min, weight in kg; CL in L/h, volumes in L.
- The paper's own check (printed p.705): CL/F 20.2, 16.7, 13.2 and 4.1 L/h at CLcr 100, 70, 45 and 15 mL/min.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None modelled. No effect-site, ke0, EC50 or concentration-response analysis; DPP-4 inhibition is only mentioned as background. Not an opioid paper.

## Covariates
- Retained: CLcr (power 0.534 on CL/F, plus a separate lower CL/F coefficient for severe impairment), weight (power 0.345 on CL/F; power 1.11 on Vc/F).
- Tested and not retained: age, sex, race (White vs other), CYP2D6 inhibitors and substrates, renal cation transporter substrates, patient type (healthy vs T2DM), body surface area, serum creatinine (printed p.704).
- Clinical relevance: weight effect on CL/F under +/-8% over 50-125 kg; Vc/F change quoted as +/-48% over 50-125 kg (printed p.706). Derived check (derived: (50/84.9)^1.11 = 0.56 and (125/84.9)^1.11 = 1.54, i.e. -44% and +54%), so the +/-48% is an approximate summary. Simulated AUC varies 33% down to 24% up across weight categories (Table 4).

## Caveats
- The separate severe-impairment CL/F is a simplification: a model with separate renal and non-renal clearance per category was unstable (RSE >50%). Figure 2 shows a discontinuity (about a 44% fall at CLcr just below 30) between the two curves.
- Only 13 subjects have severe impairment (Table 2); 12 are from the single-dose study 006.
- Linear PK assumed; Michaelis-Menten elimination not tested. Apparent parameters only.
- Internal inconsistencies: text cites "equations [3] and [4]" for the covariate model but they are Eq. 6 and 7; text says terminal t1/2 61 h in severe impairment (printed p.703) but Table 6 gives 65.6 h.
- Sponsor-authored (Takeda).
