# Adiwijaya 2017: population PK of nanoliposomal irinotecan (nal-IRI)

## Study
- Pooled population PK from six studies (five phase I-II and the phase III NAPOLI-1, metastatic pancreatic cancer after gemcitabine): 353 patients with PK (258 from NAPOLI-1). 1,792 total-irinotecan (tIRI) samples from 355 subjects and 1,765 total SN-38 (tSN38) samples from 353 subjects (p. 999).
- Doses given as irinotecan free base (70 mg/m2 free base = 80 mg/m2 hydrochloride trihydrate; 100 = 120); 53% received 100 (120) mg/m2, 40% 70 (80) mg/m2 (Table 1, p. 998).
- Baseline: median age 63 y, BSA 1.7 m2 (5th-95th 1.3-2.2), 56% male, 52% Caucasian, 42% East Asian, bilirubin median 7 umol/L, CrCl median 1.36 x10^-3 L/s.
- Sampling: cycle 1 only; intense in early studies, sparse in NAPOLI-1. Analytes tIRI (encapsulated plus unencapsulated) and tSN38. Below-LLOQ data handled by M3 with Laplacian. NONMEM 7.3, FOCEI.

## Model and parameters
- tIRI: two-compartment, first-order elimination. tSN38 = encapsulated SN-38 (a time-invariant fraction of tIRI, estimated 0.01%) plus unencapsulated SN-38 (one compartment, first-order production from irinotecan release/conversion, with irinotecan clearance as a covariate on the input) (p. 1004, Fig. S1).
- Covariates (full approach): BSA, race, bilirubin, albumin, ALT/AST, CrCl, UGT1A1*28, 5-FU co-administration, manufacturing site, liver metastasis. Significant: race and BSA on tIRI; race, BSA and bilirubin on SN-38. UGT1A1*28 not significant.
- Full parameter tables are Tables S6/S7 (supplement, not read).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No ke0, tPeak or hysteresis, no MEAC (not an opioid). Exposure-response only:
- Threshold for time above threshold: uSN38 0.03 ng/mL, chosen from the median in-vitro pancreatic-cell IC50 of SN-38 (thresholds 0.02-0.3 ng/mL gave similar concordance), p. 1004.
- Efficacy (NAPOLI-1, nal-IRI + 5-FU/LV arm): OS and PFS associated with longer tuSN38>thr and higher Cavg of tIRI, tSN38, uSN38; Cmax not associated (P 0.58-0.98) (p. 1000).
- Safety: grade 3+ neutropenia associated with uSN38 Cmax (P<0.001; tSN38 P=0.08); grade 3+ diarrhea with tIRI Cmax (P=0.001 vs Cavg 0.019) (p. 1000-1001). Neutropenia: East Asian 55% vs Caucasian 18% on nal-IRI+5-FU/LV; diarrhea 3.0% vs 19.2%.

## Covariates
- Race: East Asian vs Caucasian, tIRI Cmax 7% and Cavg 78% lower [as printed, p. 1002; the 78% looks large next to 7% and is not checked], uSN38 Cmax 50% and Cavg 20% higher (all P<=0.001).
- BSA: BSA-based dosing reduced the IQR of tIRI and uSN38 Cmax by 3% and 14% versus fixed 116.7 mg Q2W.
- Bilirubin >=1 mg/dL (n=20): uSN38 Cavg +43%, Cmax +35% (text elsewhere says 44%).
- UGT1A1*28 7/7: no effect on SN-38 clearance (ratio 1.000); Caucasian uSN38 Cmax 2.19 vs 1.94 ng/mL (P=0.30).
- Albumin: weak association with tIRI only, judged not clinically relevant.

## Caveats
Terminal tIRI half-life poorly determined; liver-metastasis status imputed "No" outside NAPOLI-1; sparse sampling in NAPOLI-1; encapsulated SN-38 fraction was estimated, not measured; exposure-safety results confounded by race; the manufacturer funded the work and several authors are its employees. Cycle 1 only (post-dose-2 samples excluded for suspect times).
