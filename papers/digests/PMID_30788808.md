# Overgaard 2019: two-compartment population PK of subcutaneous and intravenous semaglutide

Page convention: printed pp. 649-662 = PDF pp. 1-14 (PDF page = printed page - 648).

## Study
Pooled analysis of nine Novo Nordisk clinical pharmacology trials (Table 1, printed pp. 651-652): Japanese/Caucasian
safety-PK, beta-cell function (T2D), hepatic impairment, thorough QT, hypoglycaemic counter-regulation (T2D), energy
intake (obesity), a drug-product-strength trial with one i.v. dose (trial 7: 0.5 mg s.c. at 1, 3 or 10 mg/mL and
0.25 mg i.v.), and two DDI trials. Doses 0.25-1.5 mg s.c. weekly (single and multiple dose); 1.34 mg/mL is the
marketed strength. 353 subjects, 10,573 concentrations (43 excluded for dosing history, 24 more for CWRES > 6).
Table 3 (printed p. 655): 277 normoglycaemic, 76 T2D; 226 men, 127 women; age 44.6 (SD 11.8) y, range 19-70; body
weight 81.9 (15.1) kg, range 51.9-121.2; BMI 26.9 (4.3), range 18.7-42.8; 286 abdomen, 67 thigh injections; 25 with
hepatic impairment. Assay LC-MS/MS. NONMEM 7.1.2; full-model covariate approach, 10% alpha; covariates whose 90% CI
lay within 80-125% were "not important". A one-compartment comparison model used 7 trials, 267 subjects, 8292 samples.

## Model and parameters
Two compartments, first-order s.c. absorption, first-order elimination; i.v. as a bolus into the central compartment.
IIV on ka, on CL/Q (one shared eta) and on Vc/Vp (one shared eta); CL-Vc covariance 0.0172; additive error on
log scale. Reference subject (Methods, printed p. 654): **healthy**, white, non-Hispanic woman, age <= 65 y, 85 kg,
abdominal injection, 1.34 mg/mL product. Covariate equations (printed p. 653, rendered): CL = CLref x (WT/85)^theta x
theta^indicator terms; Q uses the CL weight exponent; Vc and Vp use the V weight exponent; ka by product strength is
written with indicator exponents, so each strength theta is that strength's own ka value, times theta_T2D for T2D.

**Supplement (PMID_30788808_ESM1.docx, read 2026-10-27).** Trial 7 methods: absolute bioavailability from a
randomised double-blind crossover in healthy subjects, 0.25 mg i.v. once and 0.5 mg s.c. on separate occasions,
F = ratio of dose-adjusted AUC0-inf; drug-product strength arm, 0.5 mg s.c. of two of the three strengths (1, 3,
10 mg/mL; injection volumes 500, 170 and 50 uL; the 3 mg/mL dose was 0.51 mg because of device increments,
dose-adjusted to 0.5 mg), equivalence limits 0.80-1.25. Table S1 (above): AUC equivalent for every pair; Cmax
equivalent only for 1 vs 3 mg/mL (lower CI bound 0.84, upper 1.00), and lower with more dilute strengths. Table S2
(above): the one-compartment comparison model, 7 trials with multiple dosing. Figs. S1-S3: individual CL, Vc and
ka against body weight (final two-compartment model), observed profiles by trial, and one- against two-compartment
Cavg and simulated profiles; plots only. **The ESM prints no two-compartment parameter estimates and no T2D factor
for the two-compartment model**: Table 4 of the main text is the only source of the two-compartment values.
All RSE <= 11%, shrinkage < 10%. Covariates explained 58%, 56% and 24% of the IIV variance of CL, V and ka.
No effect of sex, race, ethnicity, age or hepatic impairment on CL (printed p. 657).

0.0411 L/h (healthy, 85 kg), total V/F 9.08 L; one-compartment comparison model ka 0.0296/h, CL/F 0.0473 (T2D) and

Arithmetic check: 0.0348 / 0.847 = 0.0411 (the healthy row), x 1.12 = 0.0460 (the T2D row); (3.59 + 4.10) / 0.847
= 9.08 L. So the Table 4 values are the healthy reference. The one-compartment T2D/healthy ratio is 0.0473 / 0.0415
= 1.14 (derived).

Derived (my calculation, Table 4 values, 85 kg): terminal half-life 158 h healthy, 142 h with the 1.12 T2D factor;
distribution half-life 4.2 h. The paper states only that the half-life is about one week (Introduction) and that it
is driven by disposition, not absorption (Discussion).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. Concentration-only analysis. No ke0, no exposure-response.

## Covariates
Body weight (CL, Q, Vc, Vp), T2D on CL (x 1.12) and ka (x 0.544), injection site on F (thigh x 0.883), product strength
on ka. Hepatic impairment, sex, race, ethnicity, age group (<= 65 / > 65) tested and not retained.

## Caveats
- **Internal inconsistency (source):** the abstract and the Results (printed p. 656) call CL 0.0348 L/h the value "for a
  typical subject with type 2 diabetes", but the Methods define the reference subject as healthy and Table 5's
  arithmetic (above) confirms the healthy reading. The abstract is wrong on this point.
- The i.v. data come from one trial (trial 7), so Q and the Vc/Vp split rest on few profiles (Methods step 3).
- Ka for the 1, 3 and 10 mg/mL strengths are study formulations; only 1.34 mg/mL is marketed.
- Healthy volunteers dominate (277/353); T2D 76.
- ESM now read (supplement, 2026-10-27): it holds the trial 7 methods, the strength-equivalence table and the
  one-compartment comparison fit; nothing in it revises the two-compartment model.
