# Zhou 2002: temperature and haemodilution on volatile anaesthetic blood solubility

## Study
Twelve healthy unmedicated male volunteers (32 +/- 7 y), 230 mL venous blood each, heparinised (PDF p.2). Blood was centrifuged and recombined to haematocrit 40%, then diluted with normal saline to 36, 32, 28, 24 and 20%. Blood:gas partition coefficient (lambda B/G) of desflurane, sevoflurane, isoflurane, enflurane and halothane was measured at 37, 33, 29, 25, 21 and 17 C by two-stage headspace double equilibration with gas chromatography (flame ionisation). Desflurane, sevoflurane and enflurane were measured together in six volunteers; isoflurane and halothane together in the other six. Normal saline:gas coefficients (lambda S/G) were measured six times per temperature. Table 1 values are mean +/- SD, n = 6.

## Model and parameters
Measured at haematocrit 40% (Table 1, PDF p.3, mean +/- SD): lambda B/G at 37, 33, 29, 25, 21, 17 C.
- Desflurane 0.55, 0.62, 0.68, 0.72, 0.80, 1.04; slope of ln(lambda) on T -0.0289.
- Sevoflurane 0.68, 0.77, 0.83, 0.87, 0.96, 1.24; slope -0.0262.
- Isoflurane 1.38, 1.61, 1.83, 2.09, 2.57, 2.89; slope -0.0372.
- Enflurane 1.98, 2.45, 2.76, 3.05, 3.52, 4.91; slope -0.0410.
- Halothane 2.59, 3.11, 3.59, 4.18, 5.24, 6.17; slope -0.0433.
Slopes across all haematocrits and agents lie between -0.0433 and -0.0262 per C, i.e. a 2.62-4.33% rise per C of cooling (PDF p.3). Pooled over all agents and haematocrits the temperature coefficient is -3.61% per C (95% CI -3.41 to -3.80%, PDF p.3).
Saline: lambda S/G at 37 C is lower than blood (R S/B 0.573 desflurane, 0.570 sevoflurane, 0.441 isoflurane at 37 C, Table 2, PDF p.4).
Multiple linear regression (abstract PDF p.1, repeated PDF p.4), T in C, HCT in %:
- desflurane ln lambda = -0.0302 T + 0.0094 HCT + 0.119, R2 0.973
- sevoflurane -0.0295 T + 0.0092 HCT + 0.306, R2 0.961
- isoflurane -0.0382 T + 0.0154 HCT + 1.120, R2 0.997
- enflurane -0.0408 T + 0.0198 HCT + 1.408, R2 0.982
- halothane -0.0417 T + 0.0218 HCT + 1.649, R2 0.994
Validation (PDF p.5, Fig. 3): against literature values, mean difference measured minus predicted ln lambda -0.0307; limits of agreement 0.1528 and -0.3617, which the authors read as predictions from 30% below to 17% above the measured value for 95% of points.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. Pure physical-chemistry measurement in vitro; no MAC, no ke0, no clinical end point. (Not an opioid paper.)

## Covariates
Temperature (17-37 C) and haematocrit (20-40%, saline dilution of blood). Haematocrit coefficient: lambda B/G falls 0.59-2.30% per 1% haematocrit (slopes 0.0059-0.0230, PDF p.3), larger for the more soluble agents (Table 2). No age, sex or lipid covariates; all male volunteers.

## Caveats
- Only male volunteers, n = 12 (6 per agent pair); in vitro, heparinised blood, measured within 12 h.
- Range 17-37 C; nothing above 37 C.
- Table 1 oddities in the page image: isoflurane 20% haematocrit at 33 C reads 1.16 +/- 0.99 (an SD that size is probably a misprint); desflurane 20% reads 0.69 at both 25 and 21 C (non-monotone).
- The paper does not measure tissue:gas coefficients; it says nothing of tissue:blood.
- Predictions can be off by 30% below to 17% above for 95% of literature points (PDF p.5).
