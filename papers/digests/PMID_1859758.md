# Marsh 1991: computer-controlled propofol infusion in children

## Study
Group 1: 20 children, 1-12 y (Table II: 4.8 [2-10] y, 18.3 [12-28.8] kg), ASA I-II, general surgical/urological procedures; TCI (Psion II + Ohmeda 9000) programmed with an adult model, weight-scaled; N2O 66%, regional block, spontaneous breathing; no opioid. Group 2: 10 children (4.9 [1-9] y, 17.4 [12.7-30] kg) prospectively with the derived paediatric model. Venous whole-blood propofol by GLC (Plummer method), detection limit 50 ng/mL. Individual microconstant sets fitted by iterative weighted least squares, then the set minimising population bias chosen.

Clearance (Vc x k10): paediatric 34.30 vs adult 27.36 mL/kg/min (Discussion). Performance: adult set in children bias -18.5% (-14.8 to -22.6), precision 25.4%; paediatric set retrospectively 0.9% / 20.1%; prospectively 2.8% / 16.2%. Saint-Maurice 1989 bolus set (Vc 722 mL/kg) gave bias +33.9%.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No ke0 or PD model. Clinical targets: induction often required 14 ug/mL, reduced to 12 for maintenance in children, vs 4-6 ug/mL usually adequate in adults (with opioid in adults; none here).

## Covariates
Weight only (linear scaling of volumes).

## Caveats
Small n; individual fits chosen from stored candidate sets; whole-blood samples; targets confounded by absence of opioid.
