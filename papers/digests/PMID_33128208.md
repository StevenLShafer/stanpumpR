# Perez-Ruixo 2021: esketamine (IV, nasal, oral) and noresketamine, 820 subjects

## Study
- Pooled population analysis of 13 Janssen studies (Table 1, PDF p4/504): eight phase 1 (healthy, rich sampling), two phase 2 and three phase 3 (treatment-resistant depression, sparse sampling). 820 subjects, 9784 esketamine and 9397 noresketamine concentrations (PDF p7/507).
- Intravenous data come from ONE study, ESKETINTRD1009 (n = 18): 28 mg IV, 84 mg nasal and 84 mg PO in the same subjects, sampled 0.2-48 h. Everything else is nasal (28-112 mg, single and twice-weekly).
- Table 2 (PDF p7/507): 341 men, 479 women; age median 45 (18-86) y; weight median 74 (39-170) kg; White 72.4%, Black 6.8%, Asian 13.7% (72 Japanese); 256 healthy, 564 with TRD; eGFR median 93 mL/min/1.73 m2 (MDRD). Height not printed.
- Plasma, LC-MS/MS, LLOQ 0.5 ng/mL for both analytes (PDF p3/503). NONMEM 7.3, FOCE; log-transformed additive residual error.

## Model and parameters
Esketamine: three-compartment disposition (Vc, Vp1, Vp2, Q1, Q2) with the central compartment linked to a hepato-portal compartment (Vh) by an effective hepatic flow Qh; elimination only from the hepatic compartment, by kmet (to noresketamine) and kel (other routes); intrinsic clearance Vh (kel + kmet), E = CLint/(Qh + CLint), plasma clearance Qh E (well-stirred). Equations PDF p4-5/504-505: kch = Qh/Vc and khc = Qh/Vh (as the well-stirred parameterisation implies; the paper writes kch, khc without defining them numerically).

Inputs: IV into the central compartment. Nasal: a fraction FRn absorbed from a nasal depot into the central compartment at ka,n; the rest swallowed, zero-order over Dsw into an oral depot, then a fraction Fgut absorbed at ka,sw INTO THE HEPATIC COMPARTMENT, so first pass emerges from the structure. Oral solution: zero-order over Dpo, then Fgut at ka,po into the hepatic compartment.

Noresketamine: two compartments, apparent (/F, since the fraction formed is not identifiable without IV noresketamine), formed at kmet from the hepatic compartment.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No effect site, ke0 or tPeak; the paper is pharmacokinetic only. Esketamine is not an opioid; no MEAC.

## Covariates
Retained (p < 0.001 and r2 > 0.10): Asian race on kel (x0.36) and on CLn/F (x0.81); in a sensitivity analysis, Japanese on FRn (x1.34) and age on Qh (decline from 60 y; Qh 25% lower from 77 y). Tested and not retained: sex, body weight, ALT, AST, ALP, GGT, bilirubin, albumin, total protein, eGFR, health status (PDF p7-8).

## Caveats
- The intravenous disposition rests on one study of 18 subjects given 28 mg; the phase 2-3 data are sparse (two samples per occasion).
- No size covariate was retained, so under the library's rule the disposition would be anchored at the cohort (median 74 kg, 45 y, 479 of 820 women; height not printed, so from NCD-RisC or a printed BMI elsewhere).
- Noresketamine is apparent (/F); the fraction formed is not identifiable.
- Pregnancy: the population excluded pregnancy; nothing on it here.
