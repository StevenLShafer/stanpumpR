# Yip 2021: joint population PK of carbamazepine and three metabolites (PICME I / II)

## Study
- PICME I: 8 healthy male volunteers, single oral 400 mg immediate-release carbamazepine; samples pre-dose and 0.25-72 h (rich).
- PICME II autoinduction group: 3 newly diagnosed epilepsy patients, 200 mg IR once daily days 1-14, 200 mg bid days 15-28, 200 mg AM + 400 mg PM days 29-42; rich sampling on day 1 and days 14, 28, 42.
- PICME II maintenance group: 69 patients on stable carbamazepine >= 4 weeks (all controlled-release per Discussion), 80 sparse samples; dose 819 (SD 367) mg/day, range 200-1600 (Table 3).
- Total 80 subjects, 248 sets of assays. Mean age about 45.8 y and weight 80.8 kg (normalising values, methods).
- LC-MS/MS of CBZ, CBZ-10,11-epoxide (CBZE), 2-OH-CBZ and 3-OH-CBZ; CBZ calibration 0.33-15.95 ug/mL.
- 20 SNPs in 11 genes genotyped (ABCB1, ABCC2, UGT2B7, EPHX1, CYP2C8, CYP2C19, CYP2B6, CYP3A4, CYP3A5, MPO, POR).
- NONMEM 7.3/7.4, FOCE-I, closed-form solutions; maintenance subjects assumed to have had 8 weeks of bid dosing.

## Model and parameters
One-compartment parent with first-order absorption (ka = CLTOT/V1 + KASTAR, Eq 9); parent clearance partitioned into formation of CBZE (fraction 1 - FM1 = 0.36), 2-OH-CBZ and 3-OH-CBZ (each FM1/2); each metabolite eliminated first-order, sharing one volume V2. No IV data, so all CL and V are apparent (CL/F, V/F). Autoinduction modelled as a binary induced (IND = 1) vs non-induced state on CLTOT and CL3E (Eqs 10-11, 17-18).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. Motivation is hypersensitivity (reactive metabolites, HLA-B*15:02 / HLA-A*31:01); no concentration-effect model, no therapeutic range used.

## Covariates
- Final: total daily dose on CLTOT (Eq 13 form, reference 400 mg/day), phenytoin co-medication on CLTOT (x1.93), EPHX1 c.416A>G homozygous GG on CBZE clearance (x0.502, about 50% lower; 3 subjects), induction status on CLTOT and CL3E.
- Age, weight, smoking, other AEDs, statins, omeprazole and all other SNPs not significant.

## Caveats
- Small n (80; only 3 subjects with rich autoinduction profiles; 3 EPHX1 GG); few elderly.
- Induction treated as binary, not a time course; a turnover model failed to converge.
- FM1 fixed from literature mass balance; apparent parameters only.
- Under-prediction of 2-OH-CBZ at 48-72 h.
- Internal inconsistencies: V1 161 (Table 5) vs 164 L (abstract); EPHX covariate labelled CL2E (Table 5) vs CL3E (Eq 18).
