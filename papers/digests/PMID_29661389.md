# Jonkman 2018: esketamine counters remifentanil respiratory depression

## Study
- Randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled crossover study, 12 healthy volunteers analysed (6 M, 6 F; age mean 24, range 20-31 yr; weight 68, range 52-102 kg; BMI 22, range 19-30). 16 enrolled; 4 withdrew (2 facemask discomfort, 2 psychomimetic effects), data discarded. Leiden, Nov 2016-Jul 2017. Registered Dutch trial register 6248.
- Visits 1 and 2 (>= 1 week apart): remifentanil by TCI (Minto 1997 PK set, ref 17) started at 0.5 ng/mL and stepped up by 0.1-0.5 ng/mL until ventilation fell 40-50% from baseline, at isohypercapnia (end-tidal PCO2 clamped to give ventilation 20 +/- 2 L/min, end-tidal PO2 14.5 kPa, dynamic end-tidal forcing). After 10 min steady state, esketamine or saline placebo was infused for 60 min in four steps: 4, 8, 12, 16 mg per 70 kg (cumulative 40 mg/70 kg, 0.57 mg/kg), each 15 min. Remifentanil continued 15 min after. Mean target remifentanil 1.0 (0.4) ng/mL (esketamine arm) vs 0.90 (0.3) (placebo arm), P = 0.01.
- Visit 3: esketamine alone, poikilocapnic, same infusion scheme.
- Arterial plasma esketamine (LC-MS/MS; linear range 2.5-2000 ng/mL, LLOQ 0.5 ng/mL), samples to 120 min. Peak plasma esketamine 381 (65) ng/mL (visits 1-2) and 378 (24) ng/mL (visit 3). NONMEM 7.4.1 population PK-PD.
- Results: remifentanil cut ventilation from 19.9 (0.4) to 12.2 (2.3) L/min (esketamine arm) and from 20.1 (0.9) to 12.2 (1.3) L/min (placebo arm). Esketamine raised it from 12.2 to 16.6 (4.1) L/min (+35%); placebo had no effect (12.2 to 12.3). Without opioid, esketamine did not change ventilation (8.9 to 9.4 L/min, P = 0.12) and lowered end-tidal PCO2 by 0.4 kPa. Drug-high VRS 7.2 (esketamine+remifentanil) vs 1.9 (placebo); sedation VRS 7.0 vs 3.7. Nausea 7 occasions, headache 2, anxiety 1.

## Model and parameters
PD model (Eqs. 1-7): ventilation = (1 - lambda) * Y1 + lambda * Y2, with Y1 = baseline-ventilation component = VBLN / [1 + (CREM/C50_1)^G1] (CO2-insensitive) and Y2 = S / [1 + (CREM/C50_2)^G2] (CO2-sensitive, S = ventilatory CO2 sensitivity). Remifentanil effect-site concentration CREM enters through two effect compartments (t1/2 R1, t1/2 R2). Esketamine multiplies C50_2 by (1 + (CKET/CKD2)^Q); the best model is a mixture (responder / non-responder to esketamine and to placebo) with an esketamine effect on C50_2 only, Q fixed at 1, and no esketamine effect on C50_1 identifiable. ## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: minute ventilation at isohypercapnia (end-tidal PCO2 6.5-6.6 kPa), under remifentanil TCI; esketamine and placebo effects are on the remifentanil C50 for CO2 sensitivity.
- Remifentanil, plasma-to-effect-site: t1/2 ke0 12.2 min (slow, component Y1, baseline ventilation) and 2.15 min (component Y2, CO2 sensitivity). Converted: ke0 = ln2 / t1/2 gives 0.057 /min and 0.322 /min (derived: ke0 = 0.693 / t1/2). Whole-body kinetics were Minto 1997 TCI values, assumed to give the correct plasma concentration (p. 1120, step ii), so these ke0 values are tied to that PK set.
- Remifentanil C50 on ventilation: 1.24 ng/mL (baseline-ventilation component, G1 8.44) and 0.46 ng/mL (CO2-sensitive component, G2 4.37); 61% of the opioid effect on ventilation was via Y1 and 39% via Y2 (lambda). The authors compare an earlier open-loop remifentanil study (ref 26): C50 1.6 ng/mL, t1/2 0.53 min, a single component (p. 1124). Concentrations are predicted remifentanil effect-site (TCI), not measured.
- No MEAC or analgesic concentration reported; ventilation (respiratory depression), not analgesia.
- Esketamine: no hysteresis (plasma-driven); doubles remifentanil C50_2 at 127 ng/mL (SEE 33.9; median IQR 84-191 in abstract), measured plasma esketamine (arterial, LC-MS/MS). Esketamine peak plasma 378-381 ng/mL, 400 ng/mL is the highest modelled.
- Potency ratios: none for opioids. Placebo-equivalent esketamine concentration 363 ng/mL, about 3x the esketamine CKD2 (p. 1123).
- Authors extrapolate that esketamine could restore breathing at remifentanil plasma concentrations > 1.25 ng/mL, but flag it as outside the observed range (Fig. 4, p. 1125); suggested infusion 12-24 mg/h for 70 kg (p. 1126).
- S estimated at about 8 L/min/kPa (derived by the authors from lambda * Y2).

## Covariates
None modelled for PK or PD (healthy young adults, 52-102 kg). Responder status (mixture) for esketamine and placebo.

## Caveats
- n = 12, healthy, young, isohypercapnic, short exposure; remifentanil target up to about 1 ng/mL only, so higher concentrations are extrapolation.
- Esketamine is the S(+) enantiomer; racemic ketamine produced respiratory depression in some earlier work (Bourke 1987; Hamza 1989), and the authors do not settle the discrepancy.
- Psychomimetic effects (drug-high VRS about 7) and 2 dropouts for them. 17% of subjects were non-responders.
- The 83% clearance factor and exact PK table sit in the supplement.
