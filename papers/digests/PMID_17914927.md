# Anderson 2008: allometric size and maturation in pharmacokinetics

## Study
Narrative review (Auckland). No new subjects; includes one simulation (Table 4) of the precision of an estimated
allometric exponent (NONMEM, 100 subjects x 1000 bootstrap replicates, one steady-state sample each, CL IIV 30%,
residual 10%).

## Model and parameters
- Allometry (eq 1-5): Y = a W^b; CL_i = CL_std (W_i/W_std)^(3/4) (eq 2); V_i = V_std (W_i/W_std)^1 (eq 3);
  time indices (half-lives) scale with W^(1/4) (eq 4-5). Standard weight 70 kg.
- Worked example (p309): 0.2 L/h/kg in a 70 kg adult and a 4 kg neonate = 14 vs 6.9 L/h/70kg (allometric).
- Remifentanil example (p311): 69.4 mL/min/kg in children vs 34 in adults per kg; both about 2450 mL/min/70kg
  allometrically.
- Per-kg scaling of adult clearance underpredicts by >10% below 47 kg, approaching 50% at 3.5 kg (p311, Figs 3-4);
  BSA model overpredicts >10% below 20 kg.
- Table 3: within-human estimated exponents 0.53-0.88 (e.g. propofol 0.76, busulfan 0.61/0.74, valproate 0.72).
- Table 4: 90% interval of the estimated exponent (true 0.75): 0.48-1.01 (weight log-normal 20% CV),
  0.64-0.86 (50% CV), 0.69-0.81 (uniform 0-140 kg).
- Maturation (eq 7-10): linear, exponential, asymptotic exponential (beta_cl, T_cl; glucuronidation maturation
  half-life 3-4 months), and preferred sigmoid Emax in PMA: F = PMA^Hill / (PMA^Hill + MAT_CL50^Hill).
- Renal (eq 11-14): CPR = 516 x exp(Kage x age term in PMA) x (W/70)^(3/4) umol/h, Kage = 0.00823, reference
  516 umol/h at 70 kg, 40 y male (exact age term garbled in the text layer); CPR = 0.945 W + 0.00237 L^2; CLcr = CPR/Scr;
  Cole: CLcr = 2.21 + 0.115 W - 0.0282 Scr L/h. GFR adult 6 L/h/70kg by 6 months PNA.
- Body composition (eq 15-16): FFM = WHSmax H^2 TWT / (WHS50 H^2 + TWT); WHSmax 42.92, WHS50 30.93 (men);
  37.99, 35.98 (women); NFWT = FFM + Ffat (TWT - FFM); Fsize = (NFWT/NFWT_std)^(3/4), std 70 kg, 1.76 m.
- Table 5: maintenance dose as % of adult (allometric, neonatal value halved for immaturity); column alignment
  garbled in the text layer, so values not transcribed. Rule of thumb: 1/8, 1/4, 1/2, 3/4 of adult dose at about
  1 month, 1 year, 7 years, 12 years (p313).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None quantified; notes age-dependent PD may exist (warfarin, ciclosporin). Remifentanil clearance mentioned only as
an allometry example. No MEAC, ke0, tPeak or potency ratios.

## Covariates
Weight (allometric), PMA (maturation), FFM/NFWT, creatinine-based renal function.

## Caveats
Review; argues 0.75 is theory-fixed because estimates are imprecise. FFM described as possibly better than total
weight when fat varies, not proven. Several tables are garbled in the text layer.
