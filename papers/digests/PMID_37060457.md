# Alshabi 2023: third-trimester hormone mixtures and CYP activity in human hepatocytes (in vitro)

## Study
- **Design:** in vitro. No pregnant women were dosed and no pharmacokinetics were measured.
- **Cells:** freshly isolated primary human hepatocytes from five female donors (Table 1, p3, rendered):
  - ages 67, 31, 31, 31 and 34 years, all Caucasian;
  - BMI 21-38, with one donor not recorded;
  - viability over 87%;
  - one batch (HU12-010) received no growth hormone.
- **Treatment:** 72 h exposure, medium replaced every 24 h, to a hormone mixture at a third-trimester plasma
  ("low", LH) or a projected liver ("high", HH, tenfold) concentration (Table 2, p3):

| Hormone | LH (µM) | HH (µM) |
|---|---|---|
| progesterone | 2 | 20 |
| estrone | 0.2 | 2 |
| estradiol | 0.3 | 3 |
| estriol | 0.8 | 8 |
| 17α-hydroxyprogesterone | 0.1 | 1 |
| hGH | 0.005 | 0.05 |

- **Controls:** rifampin 10 µM and ketoconazole 10 µM.
- **Probe cocktail, 60 min (p4):** phenacetin 100 µM (CYP1A2), diclofenac 90 µM (CYP2C9), S-mephenytoin 50 µM
  (CYP2C19), dextromethorphan 20 µM (CYP2D6) and testosterone 250 µM (CYP3A4). Metabolites were measured by
  LC-MS/MS.
- **Expression:** mRNA by qRT-PCR (four donors, Fig 4-5 captions) and CYP3A4 protein by western blot (three
  donors per the Fig 3 caption; the Results text on p6 says n = 5).
- **Statistics:** duplicates in each batch, mean ± SEM of batches, one-way ANOVA with Tukey's test.

**Results:**
- At projected liver concentrations, the hormone mixture significantly increased CYP3A4 mRNA, protein and
  activity.
- CYP1A2, CYP2C9, CYP2C19 and CYP2D6 activity was not affected at either concentration (Figure 2, p7).
- Their mRNA was not significantly changed.
- CYP3A4 protein and mRNA correlated, r² = 0.7532 (p < 0.001).

In the Discussion (p11) the authors note a non-significant upward trend in CYP2C9 activity. They suggest a longer
or continuous hormone exposure might have made it significant.

## Caveats
- **Cells and culture:** five donors, one aged 67. Earlier hepatocyte reports cited by the authors show the hormones
  deplete from the medium within 4-8 h; here they were replenished only every 24 h (not measured in this study). The 72 h exposure and the dexamethasone in the medium may also have altered enzyme activity.
  Activities in culture are far below in vivo (pp11-12).
- **Clinical context:** the Introduction cites clinical increases in the clearance of CYP2C9, CYP2D6 and CYP3A4
  substrates in pregnancy, and decreases for CYP1A2 and CYP2C19. These hepatocytes reproduced only the CYP3A4
  change.
- **Typos and internal inconsistencies:** the abstract says "third-semester". Figure legends write donor HU1552
  where Table 1 and the figure axes have HU1522. The CYP3A4 activity increase is p < 0.05 in the text (p6) but
  marked **** (p < 0.0001) in Figure 1; the protein increase is p < 0.005 in the text but **** in the Fig 3
  caption; the protein n is 5 in the text and 3 in the Fig 3 caption. None of these touches the CYP2C9 result.
