# McCune 2014 supplement: prior IV busulfan population PK models (Table S1)

## Study
Four-page landscape table summarising earlier busulfan popPK models and Q6h initial-dosing guidelines, used by the main paper for comparison. Values below are as reported by McCune for each study (not re-checked against the originals). Footnotes: b = mean, c = median.

## Model and parameters
All read on rendered pages 1-4.
- **Booth (ref 30)**: n = 24, age 6.3 y (0.25-16.7), weight 23.8 kg; 1-compartment, empirical allometry (exponents 0.742 CL, 0.843 V); CL 0.202 L/h/kg (BSV 23%, BOV 9.5%); V 12.8 L/20 kg (BSV 11%); covariate ABW. Basis of FDA dosing: 1.1 mg/kg ≤ 12 kg, 0.8 mg/kg > 12 kg; adults 0.8 mg/kg on ABW, IBW or AIBW.
- **Nguyen (ref 31)**: n = 24, age 6.0 y; 1-compartment; CL 4.57 L/h (BSV 19%, BOV 9%); V 0.85 L (BSV 12%) [as printed; units presumably per kg, not stated]; covariates weight, BSA. Basis of EMA 5-band dosing (1 / 1.2 / 1.1 / 0.95 / 0.8 mg/kg).
- **Veal (ref 42)**: n = 63, age 3.6 y, neuroblastoma; IV or PO; 1-compartment, exponents fixed 0.75/1.0; CL 0.247 L/h/kg (BSV 14.5%, BOV 11.3%); V 0.757 L/kg (BSV 8.96%).
- **Zwaveling (ref 36)**: n = 77, age 5 y; 1-compartment, empirical exponents 0.84 CL / 0.98 V; CL 4.8 L/h; V 15 L; HPLC-UV.
- **Trame (ref 15)**: n = 94, age 9.2 y; IV or PO; 1-compartment, BW^3/4; CL 4.16 L/h/m² (BSV 10%) or 4.11 L/h/kg^0.75 (BSV 10%, BOV 21%); V 18.4 L/kg [as printed] (BSV 24%, BOV 21%); dose (mg) = 6 × Css_target × 4.11 × (ABW/70)^3/4.
- **Paci (ref 17)**: n = 205, age 2.5 y; 1-compartment, allometric exponent 1.25 if BW < 9 kg, 0.76 if ≥ 9 kg; CL 2.18 L/h (BSV 23%, BOV 11%); V 0.76 L [as printed]; CL by weight band 0.217, 0.235, 0.229, 0.197, 0.156 L/h/kg.
- **Bartelink (ref 16)**: n = 403, age 3.33 y (0.1-26), weight 15.3 kg (3.1-109); 2-compartment, weight-dependent exponent 1.2 (neonates) to 0.55 (young adults); CL 0.227 L/h/kg (BSV 27%, BOV 15%); V 0.725 L/kg (BSV 20%); Q 0.495 L/h/kg (BSV 88%).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. Dosing guidelines only (above).

## Covariates
As listed per study; mostly body weight or BSA, with age, sex, disease, GST polymorphisms, organ function tested and not significant in various studies.

## Caveats
Several volume entries (Nguyen 0.85 L, Paci 0.76 L, Trame 18.4 L/kg) look like unit slips in the table as printed; they are recorded as printed and not used.

## Used by stanpumpR
