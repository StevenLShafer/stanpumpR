# Duffull 1997: carboplatin PK and dose optimisation (review)

## Study
Narrative review of carboplatin pharmacokinetics (Table I, adult and paediatric means with CV), concentration-toxicity and concentration-response relationships (Tables II-IV by tumour type), and dose-individualisation methods (descriptive models, limited sampling strategies, Bayesian methods). Only studies with complete data are tabulated. No new patients. Assays: AAS measures ultrafiltrable platinum (UF Pt = carboplatin plus decarboxylated platinum; limit about 50-100 ug/L); HPLC measures carboplatin itself (limit about 500 ug/L); the two profiles are nearly superimposable for 12 h, after which UF Pt overestimates carboplatin (PDF p.3). Platinum in plasma ultrafiltrate, not whole blood or total platinum, except where "total platinum" is named.

## Model and parameters
Two-compartment open first-order model with elimination from the central compartment; clearance proportional to GFR (about 1.5 to 10 L/h), V1 correlated with extracellular fluid volume (10-20 L), Vss from about 20 L, half-life 2-6 h with normal GFR and up to 18 h in renal impairment (Summary, PDF pp 1, 5). Linear PK over 55-450 mg/m2. Oral absorption poor.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No ke0, effect-site or MEAC (not an opioid; the opioid audit does not apply). The effect measure is the AUC of ultrafiltrable carboplatin in mg/mL.min:
- Toxicity: thrombocytopenia has the strongest AUC relation (Emax-type). Untreated ovarian patients (Jodrell, n 1028): risk of grade 3 thrombocytopenia rises above AUC 5-6, 50% at 7-8, near 100% at >11-12; pretreated 50% at 5-6 (PDF p.6). Neutropenia relation weaker (50% grade 3 leucopenia at 9-10). Carboplatin plus cyclophosphamide: about 100% risk at AUC >=7.
- Response: ovarian AUC 5-7 gave the maximal response in two large retrospective studies, though newer data suggest up to about 12; testicular treatment failure rises below AUC about 5-6 (carboplatin/etoposide/bleomycin); lung and head-neck data insufficient; paediatric: no correlation (Marina, targets 2-8). Typical target AUC for carboplatin 3-7 mg/mL.min (printed 172, PDF p.12, text layer).
- Pretreatment with platinum worsens myelosuppression at a given AUC; constant infusions seem less myelotoxic than bolus at equal mg/m2 (shape of the curve matters).

## Covariates
CL: GFR (CLCR) and, in some models, body weight, age, sex, serum creatinine, lean body weight (Eq 14). Cockcroft-Gault CLCR underestimates GFR relative to 51Cr-EDTA and makes the Calvert formula overpredict AUC (Table VII mean errors with CLCR range from -17% to +11%, depending on the study and CLCR method); the authors advise against putting CLCR into the Calvert equation and suggest Chatelut's formula instead (PDF p.14, 18).

## Caveats
Review of mixed assays (AAS vs HPLC) and patient groups; most AUC-response data retrospective, first-course AUC only, CLCR-derived AUC; Table I mixes means, per-m2 values (back-conversion impossible) and different species. The review's own conclusion: only Calvert's method has been tested against myelotoxicity; none against long-term outcome.
