# De Smet 2007: Bayesian variances for a BIS-guided propofol closed-loop controller

PDF pages 1-10 = printed pages 1629-1638.

## Study
Simulation study (no patients). A closed-loop controller (RUGLOOP) targets propofol effect-site concentration through an effect-site TCI algorithm using the Schnider PK-PD model (refs 13, 14). The controller uses a patient-specific sigmoid Emax model (BIS = E0 - Emax x C^gamma/(C50^gamma + C^gamma), Eq 1), updated continuously by Bayesian fitting (Levenberg-Marquardt) of c50, gamma and an overall delay D against the population reference model, with a forgetting window (SampleTO) on old BIS samples (Eqs 4-5, p 1631-1632). E0 and Emax are fixed at 100. The aim was to choose the Bayesian variances (weights) giving the best control.

Simulation: 625 candidate controllers (Table 2 grid) x 416 virtual patients x 3 BIS targets (30, 50, 70) = 780,000 cases of 3 h (Fig 2 stimulus offset profile). The 416 patients came from 13 real patients (Vereecke 2005, independent dataset), expanded by varying c50, gamma, ke0 and clearance by their coefficients of variation (p 1633). Performance: MDAPE plus induction speed and overshoot plus a cost for freedom, combined into one score Q (Eq 8; 0 best, 0 to -1000 scale, widened to -6000 by penalties).

The overall best is the target-50 winner (Table 4); Fig 4-6 give the Q rankings.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: BIS. The prior is a population BIS Emax model for propofol (c50 4.98 ug/mL effect-site, gamma 2.62, E0 = Emax = 100). No MEAC and no potency ratio. The only effect-site time scale stated is tPeak 1.6 min (p 1630); no ke0 value is given. The authors fold ke0 mismatch, line delay and monitor delay into one estimated delay.

## Covariates
None in the controller. The authors suggest later making the prior c50 depend on age, ASA class and disease.

## Caveats
- Simulation only; the authors say robustness was not tested, only a parameter choice.
- The virtual patients come from 13 real patients and the same sigmoid model type is in both simulator and controller.
- The chosen variances give little inter-patient freedom (p 1637); c50 4.98 is relatively high, which the authors note is a risk for haemodynamically fragile patients.
- Citation 2 in the text reads Struys 2001 and the reference list also repeats Mortier 1998 as refs 6 and 10; irrelevant to the numbers.
