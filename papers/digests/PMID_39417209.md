# Lindauer 2025: PANSS exposure-response and simulated D2 occupancy for monthly IM Risperidone ISM

## Study
- Secondary PK/PD analysis of PRISMA-3 (NCT03160521), phase III, 12-week double-blind, acute exacerbation of
  schizophrenia; Risperidone ISM 75 or 100 mg IM (gluteal or deltoid) or placebo on days 1, 29, 57.
- 438 randomised; PANSS dataset 383 patients (placebo 131, 75 mg 127, 100 mg 125), 2403 PANSS observations.
  Table 1 (n = 390): age mean 41.7 y (18-64), weight mean 83.3 kg (46.8-135.0), BMI 28.1; 220 Ukraine / 170 USA
  (the Sex/Country rows of Table 1 are misaligned in the text layer).
- Individual Cav,ss of the active moiety (risperidone + 9-OH-risperidone) from a prior Risperidone ISM popPK
  model (Laveille 2024, ref 4); Cav,ss = Dose / (CL/F x dosing interval).
- PANSS model: Weibull placebo time course + Emax drug effect on Cav,ss + linear DRIFT (after Ivaturi 2017 and
  Pilla Reddy 2013); NONMEM 7.3 FOCE; SIR for uncertainty; simulations in Campsis.
- Sponsored by ROVI; authors are ROVI employees or paid consultants.

## Model and parameters
PANSS model (Table 2, clearly attributable values only): baseline 92.9; TD (time to 68.3% of max placebo effect)
8.32 days; PMAX 0.117 (fractional, -11.7%); POW 1.39; EMAX 0.0661 (fractional, -6.6%; RSE ~32%);
EC50 4.6 ng/mL active moiety, FIXED to Ivaturi 2017 (could not be estimated); DRIFT -0.0929 PANSS points/day
(active arms only); KTHL (half-life of drug-effect delay) 8.96 day as printed (Discussion says ~9 h; the unit is
ambiguous in the text layer); Ukraine vs US baseline +7.27%; deltoid injection shortens KTHL by 69.1%.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- D2 receptor occupancy was NOT measured. It was simulated with the Uchida et al. 2011 (J Clin Psychopharmacol
  31:318-325) "Emax constraint" model: Emax 100%, EC50 8.2 ng/mL of active moiety in plasma, used for both
  Risperidone ISM and the oral-risperidone comparison (p. 352).
- Target D2RO range 65-80% (Kapur 2000). Simulated median D2RO reached 65% at 4.5 h (100 mg) and ~15 h (75 mg)
  after the first injection, and stayed within 65-80% at steady state; Caverage 38.6 ng/mL for 100 mg at steady
  state (cited from ref 1).
- No effect-site (ke0) model for occupancy. The unconstrained Uchida fit (Emax 88%, EC50 4.9 ng/mL) is not
  stated in this paper.

## Covariates
PANSS model: baseline PANSS on TD, injection site on KTHL, country on baseline. BMI, age, sex, weight, race
tested and not retained.

## Caveats
- Occupancy is simulated from a literature model, not measured; PK from a different model/population.
- EC50 for PANSS fixed; Emax imprecise. Industry-sponsored.
