# Odani 1997: CYP2C9 and CYP2C19 genotype and phenytoin Vmax in Japanese patients

## Study
- 44 Japanese patients with epilepsy, 1-33 y, weight 37.8 +/- 19.5 kg (mean +/- SD); no severe hepatic/renal failure. Oral phenytoin (Aleviatin tablet or ground tablet), mostly 12-hourly, mean dose 5.18 mg/kg/day; 38 on other antiepileptics (carbamazepine, valproate, zonisamide) (p. 288).
- Genotyping by PCR-RFLP: CYP2C9 Arg144Cys (*2) and Ile359Leu (*3); CYP2C19 m1 and m2.
- Retrospective steady-state serum levels, average 6.8 per patient, mostly approximate peaks 2-5 h post dose; FPIA (TDx) total phenytoin.
- Individual V, Km, Vmax by empirical Bayes (NONMEM IV) with the Odani 1996 population model as prior (V 1.23 L/kg, Km 9.19 mcg/mL, Vmax 9.80 mg/kg/day at 42 kg, weight power 0.463, F = 100%). Km computed in the absence of zonisamide (zonisamide raises Km). Scheffe multiple comparisons.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None measured. Four patients had toxic levels (> 30 mcg/mL) at 8.6, 7.1, 6.0 and 4.6 mg/kg/day (subjects in groups 1, 1, 3, 5). Not an opioid paper: no MEAC, ke0, tPeak or potency ratio.

## Covariates
- CYP2C9 *1/*3 (wt/Leu359): Vmax 33% lower; no change in V or Km. No *2 (Cys144) and no *3/*3 found; Leu359 allele frequency 0.0682.
- CYP2C19 m1/m2: Vmax slightly lower (up to 14%), gene-dosage trend; m1 frequency 0.216, m2 0.136.
- Carbamazepine and phenobarbital: no effect detected.

## Caveats
- Small n (only 6 CYP2C9 heterozygotes; no homozygous variant, no *2 carriers); paediatric/young-adult Japanese cohort.
- Individual values are Bayes estimates shrunk toward the 1996 prior, from sparse retrospective TDM samples.
- Total phenytoin; F assumed 1.
