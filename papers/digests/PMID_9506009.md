# Yu 1997: Testosterone PK after transdermal application in hypogonadal men

## Study
- Open-label, randomized, placebo lead-in, three-treatment crossover in 19 hypogonadal men (all completed). Age 55 ± 14 y (27-82), weight 79.5 ± 2.2 kg (as printed, p.2/1140). Entry: history of total serum testosterone below 300 ng/dL; 3 weeks on the scrotal system beforehand.
- Placebo scrotal system for 24 h, then three 7-day treatments with no washout: (1) one scrotal system (Testoderm, 60 cm2, nominal 6 mg/day); (2) one investigational D-TRANS gel system (60 cm2, nominal 5 mg/day) on the upper buttocks; (3) two investigational systems on the upper buttocks. Systems worn about 24 h and replaced daily.
- Sampling on day 7 of each treatment: hours 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 7, 10, 13, 17, 20, 24, then 24.5, 25, 27. DHT, free testosterone and estradiol only at hours 0, 4, 24.
- Assay: RIA after column chromatography for testosterone and DHT; kit RIA for free T and E2. Table I gives testosterone LLOQ <8.0 to <17.6 ng/dL, intra-assay CV <14.3%, inter-assay CV <17.8%; reference range 200-1000 ng/dL. Total serum testosterone, not free, is the quantity modelled. Values below LLOQ set to zero.
- Analysis: DeMonS numerical deconvolution (monotonic cubic spline) fitting a one-compartment disposition with an input rate R(t) (exogenous plus endogenous): Cp(t) = C0 e^(-kt) + integral of R(xi) Cδ(t - xi) dxi (Eq. 1, p.3). k assumed the same across the four treatments for a subject; C0 and R(t) differ by treatment.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD end point; no clinical outcome was measured. The authors compare serum total testosterone, DHT, free testosterone and E2 with reference ranges (Table I). Active treatments raised mean levels into the normal range, with a diurnal-like profile (peak 2-4 h after application, then roughly constant). No MEAC, ke0 or opioid content.

## Covariates
None modelled. Application site and number of systems (surface area) are the only factors.

## Caveats
- Testosterone half-life 1.04 h is an apparent value from deconvolution that assumes endogenous production is absorbed along with exogenous input, in hypogonadal men with residual endogenous secretion; it is not an IV-bolus disposition half-life.
- Sponsor study (ALZA), device investigational; no washout between periods; weight SD of 2.2 kg looks small for an n=19 group with this age range (possible SEM; not resolved).
