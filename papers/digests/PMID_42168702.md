# Tan 2026: population PK of 5-FU with Michaelis-Menten elimination and BSA on Vmax

## Study
Pooled data from four prospective Dutch studies (CombiGaPP-1 and -2, PERFU, FUUT, FLOT) in three centres: 140 adults (3 excluded for unknown bolus duration), 333 dosing records, 435 plasma 5-FU concentrations, none below the quantification limit (Table 1, printed p1032). Median age 63 (IQR 56-70) y; 77 men (55%); mean weight 78.64 kg (SD 16.84); mean BSA 1.94 m2 (SD 0.25); mean creatinine 70.52 umol/L; eGFR (CKD-EPI) 89.72 mL/min/1.73 m2; DPYD wild type 71.4%. Colon 42.1%, pancreatic 34.3%, gastric 17.9%, other GI 5.7%. Regimens FOLFOX/FOLFIRI/FOLFIRINOX/FLOT: median bolus 385 mg/m2 (IQR 293-400), median continuous infusion 2340 mg/m2 (1790-2422) over a mean 42.97 h (SD 7.28; 46 h standard, 24 h FLOT). Median 2 samples per patient (1-17). Blood collected in EDTA with gimeracil 12 ug added immediately as a DPD inhibitor; LC-MS/MS plasma assays validated 50-5000 ng/mL, accuracy 94.2-99.0%, intra-day CV <=5.1%, inter-day <=7.7%. NONMEM 7.4.4, FOCE-I; model-based limited-sampling (LSS) design; mrgsolve/mapbayr Shiny app.

## Model and parameters
Six published models were externally evaluated; none usable as is; the two-compartment MM model of Briki was best (RMSE 82%, MAPE 40.28%; PDF p9). Final model: two compartments with Michaelis-Menten elimination only (linear and mixed elimination overestimated); three-compartment unidentifiable. IIV only on Vmax (IIV on V1 had shrinkage >50%); IOV 12%, not significant (dOFV <3.84). Table 2 (printed p1035), estimate (RSE):

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No effect-site model (not an opioid; no ke0, MEAC or potency ratio). Exposure target: AUC0-inf 20-30 mg h/L (international guideline cited, p1028); only 20.3% of patients reached it with BSA dosing, 60.6% below and 19.1% above (cited from other studies). Table 3 (printed p1037; text layer) simulated median AUC0-inf, with 5-95% PI: continuous 2400 mg/m2 21 [12-36]; 2800 mg/m2 25 [14-43]; 200+2400 bolus+infusion 24 [14-41]; 400+2400 29 [17-50]. Proposed starting doses 200+2400 (bolus+infusion) or 2800 (infusion alone). LSS: best three-point bolus+infusion T = 0.1 (end of bolus), 1, 3 h (MAPE about 2.5 mg h/L); best three-point infusion-only T = 0.8, 2, 5 h (MAPE 2.76), though the Results text elsewhere says 2, 3, 5 h for infusion-only (internal inconsistency, printed pp1036-1037).

## Covariates
BSA on Vmax (allometric exponent 1.14 estimated; fixing it at 1 also improved fit, dOFV >12). DPYD activity score, sex, study/assay and cycle not retained. Renal function not retained as a covariate (creatinine was collected, but no effect is reported on the final model).

## Caveats
Small CombiGaPP samples (median 2 per patient, mostly steady state), DPYD variant carriers pre-emptively dose-reduced, pump-speed variability and circadian DPD variation not modelled, LSS from this model only and not externally validated; most samples at steady state, so IIV only on Vmax. The authors attribute higher 5-FU clearance in older models to ex vivo degradation without gimeracil (Discussion; their model's population predictions at steady state are lower than Briki's).
