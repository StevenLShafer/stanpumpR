# Chamberland 1992: MIC, MBC and tolerance in 941 Gram-negative bacteraemia isolates, Canada

## Study
In-vitro surveillance, no patients dosed. Ten Canadian university hospitals each sent up to 100 consecutive
aerobic Gram-negative blood isolates from septic patients, collected January-November 1988 (Methods, PDF p2;
the Discussion, PDF p11, says 1987: the paper is internally inconsistent on the year). 941 isolates, 31 species;
E. coli 492 (52.5%; the Results text says 494), K. pneumoniae 105, P. aeruginosa 78, S. marcescens 40,
P. mirabilis 37, K. oxytoca 35, E. cloacae 34 (Table 3, PDF p3). Retested centrally at Université Laval.
Disk diffusion (NCCLS, Table 1) and broth microdilution (NCCLS, cation-supplemented Mueller-Hinton,
inoculum 5 x 10^5 cfu/mL, 24 h at 35 °C) for 28 agents. MBC: 10 µL from each clear well plated; MBC = the
concentration killing 99.9% of the inoculum at 24 h; **tolerance = MBC/MIC ≥ 32** (PDF p3, printed p617;
confirmed on the page image). Funded by Squibb Canada.

## Model and parameters
No pharmacokinetics. Key microbiology (all confirmed on page images):

- MIC interpretive criteria used, cefazolin: S ≤ 8, I 16, R ≥ 32 µg/mL (Table 2, PDF p3). Gentamicin S ≤ 4,
  I 8, R ≥ 16; ceftriaxone S ≤ 8, I 16-32, R ≥ 64. These are 1990 NCCLS values, not today's.
- All 941 isolates, broth dilution, % S / I / R / tolerant (Table 4, PDF p4): cefazolin 69 / 2 / 29 / **0**;
  ceftriaxone 89 / 8 / 3 / 0; gentamicin 94 / 4 / 2 / 0; tolerance non-zero only for chloramphenicol (3%) and
  TMP-SMZ (2%).
- E. coli (n = 492), cefazolin MIC range 0.13->128, **MIC50 1, MIC90 8 µg/mL**; disk S 436 (91%)
  (Table 5, PDF p5). Ceftriaxone MIC50 < 0.13, MIC90 < 0.13; gentamicin 0.5 / 2.
- Tolerance by species (Table 13, PDF p13): cefazolin does not appear at all (no tolerant strain of any
  species). Highest overall: chloramphenicol 3.3%, TMP-SMZ 1.4%; cefuroxime 0.2%, cefotaxime 0.3%.
- Text (PDF p12, printed p623): MBCs were in general identical to the MICs.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: 99.9% kill at 24 h (MBC) and the MBC/MIC ratio, in broth. No per-drug MBC50/MBC90 and no
distribution of MBC/MIC ratios below 32 are given; only the tolerant fraction (ratio ≥ 32). No PK/PD target,
no time-kill. Not an opioid paper.

## Covariates
Species and hospital (no hospital-specific resistance pattern, PDF p3).

## Caveats
- Tolerance cut-off ≥ 32 is far above the ratios (1-4) that set band edges in stanpumpR: "0% tolerant" says
  only that no cefazolin MBC was 32 or more times its MIC; it cannot distinguish a ratio of 1 from 16.
- MBC read only from wells without visible growth at 24 h, single plating; 1988 isolates; no ESBL era.
- Collection year inconsistent (1988 in Methods, 1987 in Discussion); E. coli n 492 vs 494.
