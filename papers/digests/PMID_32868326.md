# Vaddady 2020: doravirine popPK/exposure-response in DRIVE-SHIFT switch participants

## Study
- Brief report. DRIVE-SHIFT (P024, NCT02397096): phase 3, open-label, randomized 2:1, virologically suppressed adults with HIV-1 switched to doravirine/lamivudine/TDF (DOR 100 mg once daily) immediately (ISG, n = 447) or after 24 weeks (DSG, n = 223); 670 enrolled (p. 1).
- PK sampling: day 1 predose and week 48 (predose and 0.5-2 h post) in all; ISG additionally week 4 (predose), week 12 (any time), week 24 (predose and 0.5-2 h post). Only ISG data used (DSG sampling sparse). 4 ISG participants excluded (data reconciliation), so n = 443.
- Dataset: original 341 healthy participants + 959 treatment-naive HIV-1 participants + 443 ISG = 1,402 with HIV-1 (p. 2). Prior model: Yee 2019 (AAC 63:e02502-18, reference 9 in the paper).

## Model and parameters
- Previously developed model (one-compartment, first-order absorption, linear CL/F; body weight and healthy-vs-HIV status on V/F; age on CL/F) re-estimated with ISG data; reported to describe the ISG data well and give estimates "very similar" to the earlier model. The parameter table (Table S1) is in the supplement, not in this PDF; no parameter values are given in the main text.
- Steady-state exposures simulated from post hoc estimates, 100 mg once daily (Table 1, p. 2; geometric mean, geometric %CV):

| Treatment naive (P018, P021) | 730 | 38.1 (28.8%) | 932 (62.7%) | 2,290 (18.2%) |
| DRIVE-SHIFT ISG (P024) | 443 | 41.5 (22.8%) | 1,110 (36.1%) | 2,390 (16.5%) |

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: proportion with HIV-1 RNA <50 copies/mL (and <40) at week 48, FDA snapshot and observed-failure approaches (p. 3). Linear exposure-effect model on AUC0-24, Cmax, C24; the C24 slopes are not significant: -4.73e-4 per nM (p = 0.248) for <50 copies/mL; -3.54e-4 per nM (p = 0.366) for <40 copies/mL (Fig. 2, p. 4; N = 443). Interpreted as a flat exposure-response over the 100 mg exposure range.
- No EC50, ke0, tPeak, MEAC or potency ratio reported. Not an opioid paper.
- Matrix: C24 described as plasma concentration, Cmax as "serum" in the Table 1 footnote; not clarified in the main text.

## Covariates
In the re-estimated model (inherited from Yee 2019): weight and HIV status on V/F, age on CL/F (p. 2). No covariate analysis done for exposure-response. Efavirenz switch: transient lower DOR concentrations possible (CYP3A induction washout) but efficacy and PK were similar (p. 4).

## Caveats
Exposure-response is exploratory, snapshot approach, narrow exposure range; fixed 100 mg dose only; no parameter table in the PDF.
