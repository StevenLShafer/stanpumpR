# Hoglund 2015: Artemether-lumefantrine population PK with antiretrovirals

## Study
Pooled popPK of two Ugandan clinical studies in HIV-infected adults **without malaria** (total
n = 89). Study 1 (n = 31): parallel group, single dose artemether 80 mg / lumefantrine 480 mg
(four Coartem tablets), venous plasma at 1, 2, 4, 6, 8, 12, 24, 48, 72 h; arms +/- lopinavir/ritonavir
400/100 mg bid. Study 2 (n = 58): crossover, standard six-dose course (80/480 mg bid x 3 days),
sampling after last dose at 1, 2, 4, 8, 12, 24, 48, 72, 96, 120 h, alone then with nevirapine 200 mg or
efavirenz 600 mg (Table 1, PDF p. 4 / printed 639). Demographics (Table 1): Study 1 age 36.5 (24-51) y,
61.3% female, weight 64 (45-86) kg, BMI 23.7 (17.0-34.0); Study 2 age 36 (20-70) y, 79.3% female,
weight 56 (42-91) kg, BMI 22.3 (17.3-36.5).

Assays: artemether/dihydroartemisinin by SPE-LC-MS/MS (LLOQ 1.4 ng/mL); lumefantrine by SPE-LC-UV
(LLOQ 25 ng/mL); desbutyl-lumefantrine by LC-MS/MS (LLOQ 1.0 ng/mL). Modelled in NONMEM 7.12,
log-transformed concentrations, parent and metabolite fitted simultaneously assuming full conversion.
Concentrations reported in **nM** (plasma). Lumefantrine: 2-compartment with 1 transit absorption
compartment; desbutyl-lumefantrine: 1-compartment. Artemether and dihydroartemisinin:
1-compartment each, 3-transit-compartment absorption, with a maturation model for auto-induction of
artemether demethylation (maturation t1/2 fixed 62 h). HIV drugs entered as categorical covariates on
relative bioavailability (F) and/or elimination clearance.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Not an opioid; no analgesic/MEAC, ke0, tPeak or hysteresis reported. PD surrogate end points are
antimalarial exposure targets: simulated lumefantrine terminal exposure (AUC 72 h to inf),
dihydroartemisinin total exposure (AUC 0 to inf), and lumefantrine day-7 concentration against
published therapeutic-failure cut-offs of 280 ng/mL and 175 ng/mL (Figure 4 dotted lines, text
printed 645). Simulated dose increases to restore exposure under HIV co-treatment: efavirenz +250%,
nevirapine +75%, lopinavir/ritonavir +150% (abstract/Results).

## Covariates
HIV co-medication as categorical covariates (efavirenz, nevirapine, lopinavir/ritonavir) on F and/or
CL/F as above. Body weight, BMI, age, study and gender were tested and had **no** significant effect on
any PK parameter (Results, printed 640/641).

## Caveats
HIV-infected adults **without malaria**; the authors note malaria itself may alter PK and modify the
interactions, so the proposed dose increases are tentative and need prospective malaria-HIV testing.
Large fraction of samples below LLOQ for artemether/dihydroartemisinin (30% and 19% omitted); a
2-compartment artemether model and the M3 method gave unrealistic estimates, so 1-compartment models
were used. High eta-shrinkage on several clearances (40-51%) and a high RSE (106%) on lumefantrine MTT.
Absorption of lumefantrine is saturable (exposure assumed to rise only 70% per 100% dose increase in
simulations).
