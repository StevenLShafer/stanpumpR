# Schnider 1998: propofol PK in adult volunteers (the "Schnider model")

## Study
- Randomized, double-blind, age-stratified two-period crossover; 25 ASA I-II volunteers enrolled, 24 analysed (one withdrew, depression). Three age strata of 8: 18-34, 35-65, >65 y. Abstract: aged 26-81 y (p1170-1171). This paper's Table 1 (PDF p5, printed p1174), 24 volunteers by infusion group, lists ages 25-81 y, weight 44.4-123 kg, height 155-196 cm, 11 women and 13 men (derived: count of Table 1 rows); the companion PD paper's Table 1 gives the same ranges. Gender was not stratified: one woman in the 25 and one man in the 200 µg/kg/min group (p1174).
- Each volunteer studied twice: propofol without EDTA (Diprivan before July 1996) and with EDTA. Bolus over about 20 s of 2.0 mg/kg (<=65 y) or 1.0 mg/kg (>65 y); 1 h later a 60-min infusion at 25, 50, 100 or 200 µg/kg/min (2 per age group per rate) (p1171).
- Arterial samples (radial) at 0, 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 30, 60, 62, 64, 68, 76, 90, 120, 122, 124, 128, 136, 150, 180, 240, 300, 600 min; plasma; HPLC with fluorescence, LLOQ 2.0 ng/mL (p1171).
- Population model built from the EDTA sessions only; non-EDTA sessions used as external validation. Covariates screened by GAM (S-plus), then NONMEM FOCE with eta-epsilon interaction; −2LL drop 6.6 per parameter (P < 0.01); log-normal IIV, constant-CV residual (p1172-1173).
- LBM by James: men 1.1 × weight − 128 × (weight/height)²; women 1.07 × weight − 148 × (weight/height)² (weight kg, height cm) (p1173).

- Goodness of fit (Table 3): no covariates MDWR −1.56%, MDAWR 23.00%, MDPE −3.16%, MDAPE 21.56%; covariate model MDWR −1.86%, MDAWR 17.39%, MDPE −2.98%, MDAPE 18.63%; individual fits MDWR 0.31%, MDAWR 14.18%.
- Bolus phase vs infusion-derived kinetics (Fig 1; Results PDF p5, printed p1174): the 2- and 4-min samples showed a statistically significant negative bias (measured below predicted, i.e. the model OVER-predicted); at all other post-bolus times the bias was positive and significant (UNDER-prediction), including the 1-min peak and the 8, 15, 30 and 60 min values (Discussion PDF p9, printed p1178). Judged small beside interindividual variability (p1174, Conclusion p1181).
- Infusion rate: normalized, corrected concentrations did not differ among the 25, 50, 100 and 200 µg/kg/min groups (Kruskal-Wallis P = 0.12; p1175), and the authors call the kinetics linear over that range (p1175, p1181). This is a failure to detect nonlinearity in 24 volunteers (6 per rate), not a test that proves linearity; the Discussion (PDF p10, printed p1179) notes that subtracting an under-predicted bolus contribution would raise the adjusted concentrations most in the low-rate groups and could mask nonlinearity.
- Residuals (Fig 4 and Results, PDF p8, printed p1177): variability is increased right after the bolus and also when the infusion starts (60 min) and stops (120 min), even in the individual fits; the authors attribute it to the three-compartment model being unable to follow those shapes (model misspecification), not to interindividual variability.
- EDTA made no difference: mean without/with EDTA ratios 0.96 to 1.15, 95% bounds included 1 at every time (p1177); a mean ratio >1.17 or <0.83 was detectable with 80% power (p1181).
- Table 2's title lists gender, but the text says gender on Cl1 did not remain; gender enters only through LBM (p1175).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None in this paper (PK only). The ke0 (0.456/min) and tPeak (1.6 min) are in the companion Schnider 1999 (PMID 10360845). No MEAC (not an analgesic).

## Covariates
Age (V2, Cl2), weight, height and James LBM (Cl1). Reference values in the equations: age 53 y, weight 77 kg, LBM 59 kg, height 177 cm.

## Caveats
- 24 healthy volunteers, no opioids; arterial plasma.
- James LBM is non-monotonic in obesity (not discussed anywhere in the paper; known later problem).
- Misfit where the input changes: over-prediction at 2 and 4 min, under-prediction at 1 min and 8-60 min after the bolus (p1174, p1178), and larger residuals when the infusion starts and stops (p1177); no front-end kinetics. The authors suggest reduced liver blood flow after the bolus and recirculation-related misspecification (p1178).
- Elderly (>65 y) received half the bolus (1 mg/kg), a possible confounder of the age effect that the authors addressed with the normalized concentrations (p1180).
- Linearity rests on a nonsignificant test over 25-200 µg/kg/min, with the bolus-subtraction caveat above (p1179).
