# Graefe-Mody 2011: linagliptin in renal impairment

## Study
Open-label, parallel-group phase 1 (Boehringer Ingelheim). 51 subjects, 18-80 y, BMI 18-40 kg/m2 (PDF p1-2).
Cockcroft-Gault CrCl strata (Table 1, PDF p3, printed p941):

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | normal RF, CrCl > 80 | 6 | 4:2 | 60.2 ± 6.4 | 83.1 ± 5.0 | 0:6 |
| 2 | mild, > 50 to <= 80 | 6 | 4:2 | 65.2 ± 4.1 | 74.0 ± 6.6 | 1:5 |
| 3 | moderate, > 30 to <= 50 | 6 | 3:3 | 60.3 ± 8.9 | 82.4 ± 8.3 | 2:4 |
| 4 | severe, <= 30 | 6 | 4:2 | 57.3 ± 13.2 | 73.7 ± 8.0 | 3:3 |
| 5 | ESRD on haemodialysis | 6 | 4:2 | 41.7 ± 14.9 | 72.6 ± 14.2 | 0:6 |
| 6 | severe + T2DM, <= 30 | 10 | 6:4 | 60.8 ± 7.9 | 81.6 ± 15.2 | 10:0 |
| 7 | normal RF + T2DM, > 80 | 11 | 5:6 | 60.6 ± 9.3 | 83.5 ± 17.0 | 11:0 |

Dosing: 5 mg oral once daily after overnight fast; multiple dosing (7 days; 10 days in Groups 6 and 7) in
Groups 1-3, 6, 7; single dose in Groups 4 and 5, whose steady-state AUC was predicted by orthogonal regression of
log AUCτ,ss on log AUC0-24 from Groups 3 and 6 (PDF p2, p4). Sampling over 24 h after first/last dose, daily
troughs, and 11 days after the last dose; 24-h urine. Assay: HPLC-MS/MS (plasma, urine); DPP-4 inhibition by
fluorescence assay; protein binding in vitro (radiolabel). NCA in WinNonlin 5.2; ANOVA on log parameters, 90% CI.
Dialysis effect not studied.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Plasma DPP-4 inhibition: median > 80% at trough in all groups; PK/PD relationship unaffected by renal function or
T2DM (PDF p4). Baseline DPP-4 concentration not related to renal function. No EC50 reported here. Not an opioid
paper; no MEAC, ke0 or potency ratio.

## Covariates
Renal function (categorical by CrCl) only; r² 0.18 between CrCl and AUCτ,ss. Renal clearance at steady state falls
with CrCl (48.9 -> 27.1 mL/min, G1 -> G3), but renal excretion is < 7% of dose.

## Caveats
n = 6 per renal group; ESRD and severe (non-diabetic) groups single dose only, steady state predicted; ESRD group
younger (41.7 y); T2DM not balanced across Groups 1-5; non-linear (target-bound) kinetics make single-dose and
steady-state ratios differ; sponsor-conducted. Ratios for Groups 2-5 are against healthy volunteers (Group 1), for
Group 6 against T2DM patients (Group 7).
