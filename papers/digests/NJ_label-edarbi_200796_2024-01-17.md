# Edarbi (azilsartan medoxomil) US label, 2024

## Study
This is the current US prescribing information for Edarbi (Revised 01/2024; Reference ID 5310927). PDF page 1 is
the Edarbi label as named, so there is no source file problem.

**Indication and dosing (Highlights and section 2.1, p1 and p3):**
- Indicated for hypertension in adults.
- The recommended dose is 80 mg once daily. Consider starting at 40 mg in patients on high-dose diuretics.
- It may be taken with or without food. Tablets are 40 mg and 80 mg.

**Strength basis (section 11, p7):**
- The drug substance is the potassium salt, azilsartan kamedoxomil, MW 606.62.
- Each tablet holds 42.68 or 85.36 mg of the salt, equivalent to 40 or 80 mg of azilsartan medoxomil. Labelled
  strengths are therefore milligrams of azilsartan medoxomil, not of the salt.
- Ratio check: 42.68/40 = 1.067 = 606.62/568.52 (derived).

**Other PK facts (12.3, p8-9):**
- Azilsartan medoxomil is hydrolysed in the GI tract during absorption and is not detected in plasma.
- Food does not affect bioavailability. CYP2C9 is the major metabolic enzyme.
- Azilsartan is not dialysable (section 10).

**Specific populations (Figure 1, p9, fold change against reference, read from the image):**
- Age >65/18-45 y: Cmax about 1.15 and AUC about 1.25.
- Severe renal impairment against normal: AUC about 1.95. Moderate hepatic impairment: AUC about 1.65.
- For every group shown the recommendation is no dose adjustment. Severe hepatic impairment and pediatrics: no
  experience.

**Arithmetic check (derived):** V 16 L × ln2 / 11 h gives an implied CL of about 1.0 L/h. The label gives no total
CL and does not say whether its V is apparent or absolute.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- **Angiotensin II challenge (12.2, p8):** a single azilsartan dose equivalent to 32 mg AZL-M inhibited the
  maximal pressor effect of angiotensin II by about 90% at peak and about 60% at 24 h.
- **QT:** no QTc prolongation at 320 mg.
- **Efficacy (section 14):** seven double-blind trials, 6 weeks to 6 months, 20-80 mg once daily, 5941 patients.
- No ke0 or concentration target.

## Covariates
- **Labelled populations:** no dose adjustment for elderly patients (8.5), for mild-to-severe renal impairment or
  ESRD (8.6), or for mild-moderate hepatic impairment (8.7). Severe hepatic impairment has not been studied.
- **Pediatrics:** not established (8.4). Not recommended under 2 years of age.

## Caveats
- The label gives no clearance or compartmental volumes, and no study details behind the 11 h half-life or the
  16 L volume.
- Its Tmax and t½ are summary statements, not means with SDs.

- **Half-life, "11 to 12 h"** (`R/drugs_azilsartan.R:63`, `inst/help/drugs/azilsartan.md:46`,
  `tests/testthat/test-drugs-azilsartan.R:120`, `docs/antihypertensives.md:773`): the label says "approximately
  11 hours" (12.3, p8, confirmed on the rendered page). The 11-12 h range comes from Tsai 2016's introduction
  (p989), which cites earlier labels and reviews. Attributing 11-12 h to "the label" is therefore a small
  misquote. The conclusion stands either way: the model's 26 h is more than twice the labelled value.
  **discrepancy** (minor, attribution)
