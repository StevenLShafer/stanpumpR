# Ali-Melkkilä 1990 (BJA): IM glycopyrronium in nonpregnant gynaecological patients

## Study
- **Patients:** 9 women having vaginal hysterectomy (p667). Age 60.6 (SD 8.8) y, weight 73.2 (15.9) kg, ASA I-II.
  Height is not given.
- **Anaesthesia:** spinal plus epidural bupivacaine, with crystalloid 1500-2000 mL.
- **Dose:** glycopyrronium 0.008 mg/kg (8 µg/kg) into the right deltoid, 39.8 (11.6) min before induction. Salt or
  cation is not stated.
- **Sampling:**
  - Venous K-EDTA plasma before the dose and at 5, 10, 15 and 30 min and 1, 2, 3, 6 and 8 h.
  - Urine by catheter to 8 h.
  - CSF and a paired blood sample at 40 (30-65) min.
- **Assay:** radioreceptor assay (Ensinger's ipratropium method). Sensitivity 60 ng/L (= 60 pg/mL). Precision under
  15% at the limit and under 8% at 600 ng/L.
- **Analysis:** AUC by the trapezoidal rule (SIPHAR). Half-life from a semilog plot. CL = dose/AUC, assuming
  complete absorption.

- **Plasma at the spinal block** (about 40 min): mean 6.15 ng/mL. CSF was below detection in all 9 (n = 9).
- **Arithmetic checks (derived):**
  - 8 µg/kg / 14.11 µg·h/L = 0.57 L/h/kg, consistent with Table I's 0.58.
  - V ≈ 0.58 × 1.257 h / 0.693 ≈ 1.05 L/kg.
- **Excretion at 6 h:** the Discussion says about 57%. The Results give 33.1 + 16.2 + 8.3 = 57.6% (derived).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- Heart rate rose significantly from 15 to 60 min (P < 0.05).
- The antisialagogue effect (VAS) was significant from 10 min to 8 h.
- There was no subjective sedation and no change in BP.
- Concentration correlated with the heart-rate increase (r = +0.436, P < 0.001). The correlation with dry mouth was
  poor (r = -0.2152), which the authors put down to its late, gradual onset.
- No ke0 or EC50.

## Covariates
None.

## Caveats
- One of the nine doses was evidently injected into a vein, which gave a very high peak. It is kept in the means:
  hence Cmax SD 10.76 and AUC SD 11.57.
  - If that dose was fully systemic either way, dose/AUC is still valid. But it inflates the Cmax and AUC spread.
- An older surgical population (mean 61 y) with no renal function reported.
- The radioreceptor assay also measures active enantiomers and metabolites; the authors flag this for the kinetics.
- AUC is truncated at 8 h. IM bioavailability is assumed to be 1.
