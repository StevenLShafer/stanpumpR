# Schumacher 2009: propofol-sevoflurane response surface

PDF page = printed page minus 789 (p1 = printed 790).

## Study
- Open-label, randomized crisscross design (after Short 2002). 60 ASA I-II adults, 18-60 yr, 70-130% of ideal body weight, unpremedicated, 33 F / 27 M; median weight 66 kg (50-102), height 172.5 cm (150-190), age 30 yr (18-58) (Table 1, PDF p5; the text layer scrambles the age/gender rows, so treat the age and gender cells as read from text).
- Propofol by effect-site TCI (Schnider PK/PD, Alaris pump, RUGLOOP II), 0-12 ug/mL. Sevoflurane in 50% O2/air, end-tidal measured (Datex S/5), 0-3.5 vol%. 12 escalating combinations x 5 patients (one drug held, other stepped; max 5 steps), plus 5 sevoflurane-only and 5 propofol-only patients. 274 concentration pairs, 3-6 per patient (4.6 mean).
- Each step: 12 min equilibration, then stimuli in fixed order of rising intensity: shake and shout (TOSS, OAA/S < 2), 5 s tetanic ulnar stimulus (TTET), LMA insertion (TLMA), laryngoscopy (TLAR). Response = any purposeful or nonpurposeful movement, grimace, cough, bucking.
- EEG end points: BIS (A-2000, v4.0), state and response entropy (SE, RE), 15 s smoothing, 1-min moving median.
- NONMEM V. Quantal: Greco interaction + logistic; continuous: fractional or interpolated negative sigmoid Emax. Interaction term epsilon tested by likelihood ratio (P < 0.01, delta -2LL 6.84).
- Propofol concentrations are Schnider-model-predicted effect-site values, not measured; sevoflurane is end-tidal, treated as effect-site (p801).

## Model and parameters
Combined potency U = CePROP/Ce50,PROP + CeSEVO/Ce50,SEVO + epsilon * (product); epsilon = 0 (additive) was the final result for every end point. Quantal: P = U^slope/(1+U^slope). Continuous: Effect = E0 - (E0 - REST) * U^slope/(1+U^slope), with REST interpolated between drugs for BIS only.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- Interaction: additive (epsilon not different from 0, P < 0.01) for TOSS, TTET, TLMA, TLAR, BIS, SE and RE (p794-795, Fig 5, Fig 7). Final models: additive for all quantal end points, interpolated additive negative sigmoid for BIS, simple additive fractional Emax for SE and RE.
- Quantal end points are population probabilities of tolerance; 50% = Ce50 above. TOSS is the authors' surrogate for loss of consciousness. The three stimulation end points were measured with no opioid on board (no antinociceptive drug).
- Sevoflurane:propofol Ce50 ratio roughly constant, mean about 0.43 vol% per ug/mL for the quantal end points (p800); the authors' example: propofol 2 ug/mL + sevoflurane 1 vol% = 1.86 vol% sevoflurane-equivalent, "approximately 1 MAC" (derived from the 0.43 ratio: 1 + 2 x 0.43 = 1.86).
- ke0 / equilibration: no ke0 estimated. The authors chose 12 min as 4-5 x t1/2ke0, citing t1/2ke0 of 2.4 min (spectral edge 95%), 1.45 min (BIS), 2.9 min (blink reflex), 2.8 min (tetanic withdrawal), and 3.4 min (BIS, Kennedy) from other papers (p800-801); none is a result of this study. Propofol effect-site concentration uses the Schnider ke0 inside the TCI model.
- No MEAC / analgesic concentration, no opioid and no potency ratio against an opioid. Not an opioid paper.

## Covariates
None tested. Weight 70-130% of ideal body weight was an entry criterion. Between-subject variability: log-normal; a single eta per drug across all stimuli (CVs in Table 2 and Table 3).

## Caveats
- Propofol concentrations are model-predicted (Schnider), not measured; the authors state this (p801). Sevoflurane is end-tidal, assumed equal to effect-site after 12 min.
- Stimuli given in fixed order of intensity, not randomized (ethical); simulation put bias within +/- 4%.
- Single common slope and a single eta per drug across stimuli were imposed to stabilize the fit.
- The authors found the fixed-ratio model statistically inferior to separate Ce50s (AIC, P < 0.01), but suggest the 0.43 ratio for bedside use.
- Printed-text inconsistencies: the Discussion gives the TLMA Ce50 SEVO as "2.55 (0.24)" and TTET as "2.11 (0.16)", whereas Table 2 gives SEs 0.16 and 0.13; the Abstract and Table 2 agree on the Ce50 values themselves. The Discussion writes "ug/ml" as a garbled unit when quoting the Harris 50% isobole (propofol 1.29 at 0.45 vol% sevoflurane).
