# Jung 2023: fractal kinetics in population PK models

## Study
Methods paper. A simulation (R 4.2.2, rxode2) and five previously built population PK models re-fitted in NONMEM 7.5.0 (FOCEI, PsN 5.2.6) with a fractal (time-dependent) rate coefficient added. Simulation: one- and two-compartment models, 100 mcg extravascular dose, 0-400 h, h varied 0-1 in steps of 0.05 (PDF p3, Table 1). Not a study of any stanpumpR drug.

## Model and parameters
Fractal rate (Eq. 1, PDF p3): rate = theta / time^h, where theta is the rate coefficient at time 1, h is the heterogeneity exponent bounded 0-1 (h = 0 gives the classical first-order constant), and time is time since dosing in the model's own time unit (treated as dimensionless). It replaced Ka, Ke, Kcp or Kpc in the simulations; in the five real cases it was applied to the absorption rate only. Time is reset to zero at each new dose in the multiple-dose case (Case 2), with the remaining depot amount emptied.

Simulation grid (Table 1): Ka and CL each 0.033, 0.1, 0.3 (Ka<CL, Ka=CL, Ka>CL); Q 3; V1 10; V2 100 (B) or 1 (C). Units are not stated in the table.

Five cases (PDF p4; Table 2 on PDF p7, confirmed on the page image): 1 single-dose donepezil patch plus oral, 2-cmt, two transit compartments (ref 3); 2 multiple-dose oral plus patch, 2-cmt; 3 in-house IM depot, 2-cmt, two fractions; 4 SC antibody, 2-cmt TMDD (ref 16); 5 SC antibody labelled "anakinra" in the text, 1-cmt TMDD, 48 h (ref 16, which is the HL2351 paper). Fitted h values and the base and fractal parameter estimates are in Supplement Tables S1-S5, which are not in this PDF.

## Caveats
Simulation conclusions are qualitative (h near 1 gives an IV-like absorption profile; fractal elimination traps drug in the central compartment). Case 3 showed no benefit (dAIC +2.11). Only absorption was tested in the real cases, and the models were already minimised, so fit gains are modest. The rate is infinite at time 0 for h > 0, so results depend on the solver's first step (derived: from Eq. 1).
