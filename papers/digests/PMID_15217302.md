# Lichtenbelt 2004: Strategies to optimise propofol-opioid anaesthesia

## Study
Narrative review (Leiden). No new data. Covers propofol and fentanyl, alfentanil, sufentanil and remifentanil: PK/PD, PK and PD interactions, TCI, and BIS. The opioid and propofol numbers are largely from Vuyk 1997 (ref 8) and the cited PK sets.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- **t1/2,ke0 (min), Table I:** propofol 2.40; fentanyl 4.70; remifentanil 0.90; alfentanil 1.10; sufentanil 5.87. The end point for ke0 is not stated in the review.
- **Time to peak effect after a 15 s equipotent bolus (Fig 2 simulation, text printed p585):** propofol 2, remifentanil 1.2, alfentanil 2.3, fentanyl 4.3, sufentanil 7.5 min (ordering remifentanil < alfentanil < fentanyl < sufentanil).
- **EC50 row of Table I (ug/L):** propofol 3400 (loss of consciousness, no opioid; ref 18); fentanyl 1.1, remifentanil 4.7, alfentanil 90, sufentanil 0.14 = "optimal EC50 in the presence of propofol" (ref 8). Table II repeats them as bolus-dose EC50-EC95 in ug/kg (alfentanil 90-130, fentanyl 1.1-1.6, sufentanil 0.14-0.20, remifentanil 4.7-8.0, with propofol 3.2-4.4 / 3.4-5.4 / 3.3-4.5 / 2.5-2.8 mg/L), derived in female patients, lower abdominal surgery. These are effect-site targets for "no response to surgical stimuli" combined with propofol, NOT a MEAC or post-operative analgesic concentration.
- **No MEAC / minimum effective analgesic concentration is reported.** No assay (total vs free, blood vs plasma) is stated for the opioid values.
- **Potency ratio (printed p581, text):** 1 ug/L fentanyl ~ 0.1 ug/L sufentanil ~ 70 ug/L alfentanil ~ 2 ug/L remifentanil, for analgesia and for respiratory depression; plasma concentrations (derived: these are ratios of blood/plasma concentrations, not doses). Alfentanil:remifentanil intraoperative potency 35:1 (printed p584, page image).
- Other: propofol EC50 for LOC 2.4, 1.8, 1.3 mg/L at 25, 50, 75 y (Schnider 1999); propofol alone needs 10-12 mg/L to suppress surgical responses; alfentanil 3 ug/L fentanyl / 122 ug/L alfentanil each lower propofol LOC EC50 by 40%; alfentanil EC50 for adequate ventilation at normocapnia 60 ug/L (Bouillon, ref 52); optimal combination 3.5 mg/L propofol with 85 ug/L alfentanil (50% awake at 16 min after 5 h). Age 20 to 80 y raises opioid t1/2,ke0 ~50% (Minto, ref 36).
- BIS: 40-65 general anaesthesia; BIS rises with ketamine/N2O so is unreliable with them (printed p590).

## Covariates
Propofol set is for 40 y, 180 cm, 80 kg; opioid sets have no covariates in Table I. Text: age lengthens opioid t1/2,ke0; remifentanil dosing should be on lean body mass (refs 15, 37).

## Caveats
Narrative review; values copied from other studies, one set per drug. Opioid EC50s are from one study (Vuyk 1997) in women under propofol. Table I parameters for alfentanil/fentanyl are older sets, not population fits (the table caption says population PK exists only for propofol, remifentanil, alfentanil).
