# Thomson 2009: vancomycin population PK and trough-targeted dosing

## Study
- Model building: 398 adult in-patients (≥ 16 y; routine TDM, Glasgow 1991-2004 and Bristol 1999-2002; 102 from an
  earlier analysis); 1557 concentrations, median 3 per patient. Evaluation: 100 patients (Glasgow 2004-2007), 171
  concentrations.
- Table 1 (median, range), building set: 63% male; age 66 (16-97) y; weight 72 (40-159) kg; initial SeCr 98 (30-573)
  µmol/L; initial CrCL 64 (12-216) mL/min; initial dose 1000 (500-1750) mg; concentration 12.1 (2.0-49.2) mg/L.
- Renal replacement therapy excluded. Serum creatinine below the reference range was set to **60 µmol/L** before
  computing CrCL (Methods). CrCL by Cockcroft-Gault (also Jelliffe, Salazar-Corcoran, MDRD, LBW/IBW variants tested).
- Assay: FPIA (Abbott TDx/FLx), LLOQ 2 mg/L; total serum vancomycin.
- NONMEM 6, FOCEI; one vs two exponentials; GAM screening; LRT p < 0.005; bootstrap 250 runs.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD model. Targets discussed: trough 10-15 mg/L (BNF), 15-20 for resistant strains; AUC24/MIC > 400.
Evaluation set predictions: new guidelines 55% of troughs in 10-15 mg/L and 71% in 10-20 (old 19% and 22%);
AUC24 520 (124) vs 436 (104) mg·h/L; 87% vs 58% with AUC24/MIC > 400 at MIC 1 mg/L.
Loading doses (Table 4): 1000 mg < 60 kg, 1500 mg 60-90 kg, 2000 mg > 90 kg; maintenance by CrCL (Table 5).

## Covariates
Cockcroft-Gault CrCL (TBW) on CL (linear proportional); TBW on V1 and V2. Age, sex, other size metrics not retained.

## Caveats
Routine sparse TDM data; mostly older in-patients; linear CL-CrCL relation gives CL = 0 at CrCL ≈ 1.06 mL/min
(derived: 66 − 1/0.0154); V2 and Q imprecise; FPIA assay; Q unit misprinted.
