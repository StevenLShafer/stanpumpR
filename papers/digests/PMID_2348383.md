# Gupta 1990: lorazepam PK/PD with an effect compartment (oral)

## Study
- 6 healthy nonsmoking men, 22-28 y (mean 26), 71-84 kg (mean 75).
- Double-blind crossover of lorazepam 0.057 mg/kg (3.48-4.90 mg) against placebo, as an oral solution with 200 mL water after an overnight fast.
- Plasma at 0, 20, 40, 65, 85, 100, 135, 180, 240, 360, 480, 600, 660, 1440 and 2880 min.
- Assay: GC (Greenblatt method), LLOQ 1 ng/mL.
- Tasks at 0-690 min:
  - TRKN: continuous tracking, RMS deviation.
  - SWAY OPEN: body sway with eyes open.
  - DSS: digit symbol substitution, as a % change.
- Analysis:
  - PK: individual two-compartment fits with first-order absorption and a lag (JANA, then NONLIN).
  - PD: an effect compartment with a sigmoid Emax model, with baseline E0 for TRKN and SWAY.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Table II, individual values and mean ± SD (half-lives are harmonic mean ± jackknife SD):

| End point | t½ke0, h | EC50, ng/mL | γ | Emax |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| TRKN | 0.48 ± 0.04 (0.31-0.65) | 35.7 ± 8.98 | 6.29 ± 0.85 | 418 ± 115 |
| SWAY OPEN | 0.52 ± 0.03 (0.39-0.79) | 32.2 ± 11.2 | 6.22 ± 0.73 | 416 ± 36.1 |
| DSS | 0.51 ± 0.02 (0.44-0.67) | 27.1 ± 2.75 | 3.06 ± 1.42 | 81.6 ± 14.6% |

- All end points showed counterclockwise hysteresis. The t½ke0 values did not differ between tasks.
- Internal inconsistency: the abstract gives the TRKN t½ke0 as 0.43 h and the EC50 as 35.8 ng/mL. Table II gives 0.48 h (harmonic mean) and 35.7 ng/mL.
  - 0.43 h is subject 2's individual value.
  - Recomputing the harmonic mean of the six printed values gives 0.475 h (derived).
- Concentrations are total plasma.

## Covariates
None.

## Caveats
- n = 6 men; oral solution only.
- Effect-site half-lives from oral dosing are poorly identified in the first hour. The first plasma sample was at 20 min.
- Steep Hill coefficients.
- No IV arm.
