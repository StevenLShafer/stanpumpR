# Yoon 2021: sex differences in zolpidem PK/PD and CYP3A4 activity (the Kim 2026 source trial)

## Study
Open-label, single-dose, single centre (Seoul National University Bundang Hospital); KCT0003934. 30 healthy
non-smoking Korean adults aged 20-45 y, 15 M / 15 F. Table 1 (mean +/- SD; text layer row-shifted, read by order):
age 30.2 +/- 5.9 vs 29.9 +/- 6.2 y; height 173.5 vs 159.9 cm; weight 73.1 +/- 7.6 vs 54.7 +/- 3.9 kg; BMI 24.3 vs
21.4; 4beta-OH-cholesterol 27.0 +/- 9.9 vs 38.7 +/- 15.7 ng/mL (p = 0.030). Single oral zolpidem 10 mg on day 1
in the morning (fasting not explicitly stated; only provided meals); product/salt of the dose not stated (zolpidem
tartrate was the analytical reference standard). Plasma (K2EDTA) at 0, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 1, 1.5, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12 h.
UPLC-MS/MS, linear 0.5-500 ng/mL, LLOQ 0.5 ng/mL. NCA in Phoenix WinNonlin 8.1. PD: DSST (90 s), CRT
(PsyToolkit), VAS sleepiness 0-100, baseline at -23, -22, -20, -12 h and 0.5-12 h post-dose.

CL/F 22% lower in women, not significant (Discussion). Abstract says Cmax 9.9% higher; Results 10.9%; GMR 1.0986.
At 8 h, all but two subjects (69.0 and 60.0 ng/mL) were below 50 ng/mL.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
DSST fell and CRT and VAS rose after dosing; largest changes at 0.5 h for DSST and CRT, coinciding with zolpidem
Tmax (an immediate effect); VAS peak change 0.5-3 h (slightly delayed). Recovery to baseline: DSST 2 h men vs 4 h
women; VAS 3 h men vs 6 h women; CRT similar. Linear concentration-effect regressions (Fig. 3): DSST slope steeper
in men (p = 0.001); CRT, VAS slopes not different. No ke0, EC50 or Emax estimated. Not an opioid; no MEAC. Cites
50 ng/mL as the driving-impairment cut-off (Farkas 2013).

## Covariates
Sex (weight-normalized CL/F and Vd/F not different); CYP3A activity (4beta-OH-cholesterol) correlated inversely
with Cmax and t1/2 and positively with CL/F; women had higher CYP3A activity.

## Caveats
Open-label, no placebo; n = 30; Tmax medians in the text (0.9, 1.1 h) conflict with Table 2 (0.5, 0.75 h); Cmax
percentages differ between abstract and Results. 4beta-OH-cholesterol is a disputed CYP3A marker.
