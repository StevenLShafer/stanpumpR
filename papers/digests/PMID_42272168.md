# Ryder 2026: cephalexin and cefadroxil dosing for skin and soft tissue infection (review + simulation)

## Study
- Narrative review (not formal systematic) of adult SSTI dosing studies of cephalexin and cefadroxil (PubMed to 23 Jan 2025), Table 2 summarising RCTs and observational studies (Johnson 1972, Browning 1981, DiMattia 1981, Cordero 1976, Ballantyne 1982, Gooch 1991, Yadav 2023).
- Plus Monte Carlo PTA/CFR simulations for adults built on the Haynes 2024 pediatric population PK models (ref 19), allometrically scaled to adults and supplemented with oral PK anchors from published adult summary data by a pharmacometric mixture approach.

## Model and parameters
No new model fitted. Simulation method (section 4): the highest weight group (about 70 kg) of the Haynes models used for adults, with CL/F = typical CL x (Weight/70)^0.75 and Vd/F = typical Vd x (Weight/70)^1. Protein binding 15% for both drugs. Regimens: cephalexin 500 or 1,000 mg q6/q8/q12h; cefadroxil 500 or 1,000 mg q8/q12h. MIC ranges in doubling dilutions: GAS 0.06-0.5 mg/L, MSSA 0.5-16 mg/L; distributions for CFR from EUCAST.

Haynes model judged fit for purpose for adult simulation (section 9); pediatric model range quoted as 0.5-18 y, 7.5-79 kg.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- Targets: 40% fT>MIC (primary) and 90% fT>MIC (conservative); PTA > 90%, CFR.
- Cephalexin vs MSSA: 1,000 mg bid or > 500 mg tid-qid gives > 90% PTA to MIC 2 mg/L; at 4 mg/L only 500 mg qid or 1 g tid-qid. No regimen reached CFR 90% for MSSA.
- Cephalexin vs GAS: all regimens > 90% PTA except 500 mg bid at MIC >= 2.
- USCAST cephalexin S. aureus breakpoint <= 8 mg/L for 1,000 mg qid (stasis end point), cited.
- Skin abrasion: cephalexin skin levels about 75% of serum (cited study).
- Authors favour cephalexin 1,000 mg tid or cefadroxil 1,000 mg bid for moderate-severe SSTI.

## Covariates
Weight only (allometric scaling). Renal function not modelled in the simulations.

## Caveats
- Extrapolation of a pediatric model to adults; renal impairment and obesity not represented; plasma not tissue exposure; included clinical trials old and underpowered.
- The figures (PTA curves) could not be read from the text layer; regimen conclusions taken from the text.
