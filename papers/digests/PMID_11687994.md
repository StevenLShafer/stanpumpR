# Hu 2001: blood/gas solubility of desflurane, isoflurane and halothane in cardiac patients

## Study
Descriptive study, West China University of Medical Sciences hospital. 20 healthy adult volunteers, 40 adult cardiac surgery patients (20 coronary artery disease [CAD], 20 rheumatic heart disease [RHD]) and 20 children with congenital heart disease (10 cyanotic, 10 acyanotic). 10 mL fasting venous blood, heparinised; 7 mL for the blood/gas partition coefficient (lambda B/G) at 37 C by two-stage headspace equilibration with a mixed vapour (1.65% desflurane, 1.78% isoflurane, 1.85% halothane in air), measured by gas chromatography; 3 mL for haematocrit, cholesterol, triglyceride, albumin, globulin. ANOVA among adults, t-test between the children's groups, stepwise multiple regression on pooled n = 80.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. Not an opioid paper (no MEAC, ke0 or potency data).

## Covariates
Triglyceride is the main determinant of lambda B/G (Table 2); cholesterol adds for desflurane and isoflurane. Hematocrit had no effect: cyanotic (69%) and acyanotic (38%) children had similar lambda B/G (p > 0.05). CAD lambda B/G was 11% to 19% above volunteers and RHD (text, p3).

## Caveats
Single centre, Chinese patients, venous blood after 10 h fast, small groups; regression r only 0.50-0.69; 37 C only; sevoflurane not studied; not designed as a hematocrit dilution test.
