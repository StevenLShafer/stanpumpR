# Hallik 2020: dobutamine population PK/PD in neonates on the first days of life

## Study
Prospective two-centre dose-escalation study (Tallinn Children's Hospital and Tartu University Hospital, Estonia;
April 2016-December 2017; EU CTR 2015-004836-36). 31 neonates recruited, 28 analysed (2 excluded for therapeutic
hypothermia, 1 consent withdrawn). Table 1 (PDF p. 4, printed p. 321): gestational age 30.4 (22.7-41.0) weeks,
birth weight 1618 (465-4380) g, 18 male (64%), age at recruitment 6 (2-28) h, SNAPPE II 16 (3-89); 5 also on
dopamine. Dobutamine IV infusion started at 5 µg/kg/min and raised by 5 about every 30 min to at most
20 µg/kg/min (maximum reached 10/15/20 in 1/17/10 infants); median infusion 3.5 (1.4-17.7) days; a 10 mL/kg
saline bolus first unless already given. Arterial plasma samples (0.3 mL) 15-30 min after each dose change and
15 min after stopping; 119 concentrations (2 not detected, 9 below LLOQ, imputed as 0.5 µg/L); maximum
330 µg/L. Assay UHPLC-MS/MS, LLOQ 0.97 µg/L. NONMEM 7.3, FOCE-I; PsN bootstrap (1000) and pcVPC.
Echocardiography (RVO, LVO, LVEF) 20-30 min after each step; HR, MAP, SaO2, rScO2 recorded every 2.5 s and
averaged over 1 min for 5 min before to 10 min after each dose change; cFTOE = (SaO2 - rScO2)/SaO2.

## Model and parameters
One compartment, first-order elimination (Michaelis-Menten not better; parallel linear + M-M only ΔOFV 10, set
aside). Equations 1-2 (PDF p. 3, printed p. 320, confirmed on the rendered page):

CLi = θCL · (BWi/Wst)^0.75 · PMAi^Hill / (PMA50^Hill + PMAi^Hill);  Vi = θV · (BWi/Wst)

Wst = median birth weight 1618 g; PMA50 = PMA at which CL reaches 50% of adult values. So θCL is the clearance of a
fully mature 1618 g infant (derived from Eq. 1). Shared BSV for CL and V (r = 1.0), with a scale factor on V.
Allometry and maturation improved OFV by 26 and 15 and lowered BSV by 62%; no other covariate entered.

Table 2 (PDF p. 5, printed p. 322), final PK model, θ (SE), BSV (SE), shrinkage:

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Equations 4-6: linear E = E0 + SL·C; Emax and sigmoid Emax E = E0 + (Emax - E0)·C^γ/(EC50^γ + C^γ). **Emax is the
maximum value of the variable, not the increment** (Eq. 6 and its definition, p. 4). Effect compartment (Eq. 3)
tested for HR, MAP and cFTOE only.
- **Heart rate** (Table 5): sigmoid Emax with an effect compartment; ke0 **6.59 /h** (SE 2.18, no BSV); text gives a
  mean effect time (1/ke0) of 9 min (PDF p. 5) (derived: t½ke0 = 0.693/6.59 h = 6.3 min). E0 138 /min (BSV 15%),
  Emax 172 /min (BSV 5%), EC50 39.2 µg/L (BSV 50%), γ 3.36. Effect compartment improved OFV 7457 → 7411 (Table 3).
- **MAP** (Table 5): sigmoid Emax, no effect compartment (OFV 5076 without vs 5101 with). E0 39.7 mmHg, Emax 41.9 mmHg,
  EC50 25.4 µg/L, γ 13.5.
- **cFTOE** (Table 5): sigmoid Emax, no effect compartment. E0 0.227, Emax 0.206 (a fall), EC50 52.9 µg/L, γ 3.65.
- **LVO** (Table 4): sigmoid Emax. E0 131, Emax 157 mL/kg/min, EC50 117 µg/L, γ 2.82.
- **RVO** and **LVEF** (Table 4): linear, slopes 0.214 and 0.0285; E0 151 mL/kg/min and 63.5%.
- No therapeutic or target range; the authors advise individual titration given the large PD BSV (p. 10).
Not an opioid paper: no MEAC or potency ratio. tPeak not reported.

## Covariates
CL: (BW/1.618 kg)^0.75 × sigmoid PMA maturation (PMA50 37.4 wk, Hill 2.67). V: linear in BW. Birth weight is the
size covariate. Postnatal age, antenatal steroids, dopamine, haemoglobin, albumin, PDA diameter, baseline LVEF
and RVO tested, none retained.

## Caveats
- Table 2 prints the SE of CL as 44.5, larger than the estimate; the PKPD refits give SEs of 3.03-3.61, so 4.45 may be
  meant. It does not affect the estimate.
- PK residual error about 58%, attributed to the accuracy of low-rate neonatal infusions; few samples above
  200 µg/L; sparse sampling (3-5 per infant); BSV of V could not be estimated separately.
- 28 critically ill neonates in the transitional period only; V (3.27 L/kg) reflects high body water in the first days.
- The ke0 comes from the HR model, whose own CL/V (42.3, 5.42) differ slightly from Table 2.
