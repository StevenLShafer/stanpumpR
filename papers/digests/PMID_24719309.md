# Chen 2014: population PK/PD of nab-paclitaxel (Abraxane)

## Study
Pooled analysis of 8 Celgene studies (five phase I, one of them in hepatic impairment; one phase II; two phase III;
Supplemental Table S1, not in the archived PDF). 150 adults with advanced/metastatic solid tumours, nab-paclitaxel
monotherapy 80-375 mg/m2 on days 1, 8, 15 q28d or day 1 q21d, all over 30 min except one 135 mg/m2 q3w cohort
(3 h). Whole blood or plasma sampled after the first dose; paclitaxel by LC-MS/MS, LLOQ 1-10 ng/mL. 1418
concentrations. NONMEM 7.2, FOCEI, ln-transformed data; WB/plasma ratio estimated to bridge matrices; residual
error as two subpopulations (MIXEST). Nonparametric bootstrap (500 runs) and VPC (p. 1099-1100, 1103).

Table 1 (PDF p. 4, printed 1100), PK population, median (range): age 57 (24-85) y; 90 (60%) female; 136 White,
13 Asian, 1 Black; weight 74 (40-143) kg; BSA 1.9 (1.3-2.4) m2; albumin 3.9 (2.1-4.7) g/dL; total bilirubin 8.6
(3.4-81.0) uM; ALP 94 (42-2003) U/L; CrCl 82.7 (29.6-150.0) mL/min. Tumours: breast 24, melanoma 44, other 82.
PD population n = 125 (558 ANC values, first cycle).

## Model and parameters
Three compartments: saturable (Michaelis-Menten) elimination from V1; saturable distribution between V1 and V2;
first-order distribution V1-V3 (Q2). Saturable elimination over linear: dOFV -502; saturable over first-order
distribution: dOFV -141 (p. 1100). Table 2 (PDF p. 6, printed 1102), final estimates (95% CI from bootstrap; RSE%).
**On the rendered page the units are ug/h and ug/L** (the text layer renders the micro sign as "m"); the abstract
(p. 1) and Discussion (p. 9) also print ug/h and ug/L.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Not an opioid: no MEAC, ke0, t1/2ke0, tPeak or potency ratio. End point is neutropenia.
- Friberg semimechanistic myelosuppression model (Fig. 1): drug effect = Slope x C on proliferating cells, three
  transit compartments, MTT = (n+1)/k with n = 3, feedback (BASE/Circ)^FP. Slope 0.00253 per ng/mL (<65 y) and
  0.0038 (>=65 y), i.e. a 25% and 38% fall in proliferation rate per 100 ng/mL (p. 7). Slope is for TOTAL drug
  (Table 3 footnote d; total chosen over free because the free fraction is constant over clinical concentrations,
  data not shown, p. 9).
- Threshold analysis (Fig. 3A/B, p. 8): of cutoffs 1-1000 ng/mL, time and AUC above **720 ng/mL (0.84 uM)**,
  total plasma paclitaxel, best predicted a >=50% fall in ANC in cycle 1. The sb-paclitaxel thresholds of 0.05 or
  0.1 uM were **not** significantly correlated for nab-paclitaxel (p. 9).
- Simulated time above 720 ng/mL: 0.86 h at 100 mg/m2 and 3.75 h at 300 mg/m2 (30-min infusion; Fig. 3C).
- Logistic regression: adjusted OR per ln(Cmax) 2.369 (95% CI 1.189-4.719), per ln(AUC) 2.228 (1.109-4.475); age
  OR 1.030 (p. 8).
- Nadir about 1 week after 260 mg/m2 q3w, about 2 weeks for 150 mg/m2 weekly (p. 7).

## Covariates
- Albumin on VMEL: VMEL = 8070 x (albumin/3.9)^0.554 (p. 4); about 30% lower VMEL at 2 g/dL. Categorical
  (Fig. 2A): median VMEL 12% lower when albumin < 3.5 g/dL.
- Not significant: total bilirubin, CrCl, weight, BSA, age, sex, race, tumour type. Categorically (Fig. 2C), median
  VMEL 22% and 26% lower in moderate and severe hepatic impairment (NCI ODWG bilirubin classes) against normal
  hepatic function in the same dataset. Authors suggest about 20% lower starting dose at bilirubin >1.5-5 x ULN.
- PD: age >= 65 y multiplies Slope by 1.501; albumin on baseline ANC exponent -0.998.

## Caveats
- Industry-sponsored pooled analysis; per-study details in a supplement not archived.
- Saturable distribution return: the paper gives the diagram only, no equations (see open question).
- KMTR (CI 2210-7910) and VMTR (RSE 25.8%) are poorly estimated; Albumin exponent CI 0.15-0.94.
- Single-dose (first-dose) PK only; no pregnancy, children or biliary-obstruction patients.
- Comparisons with sb-paclitaxel are against Joerger 2006's population (different patients, separate analysis),
  not a crossover.
