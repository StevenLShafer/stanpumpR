# Mason 1979: atenolol kinetics and absolute bioavailability

## Study
- 12 healthy adult men, 21-28 yr, 63.6-79.5 kg (Table II mean weight 74.1 +/- 5.63 kg). Open 4-way crossover, >=7 days apart.
- Doses: oral solution 25, 50, 100 mg and 50 mg IV infused over 12 min (0.2 h). Fasted 8 h before to 4 h after dosing.
- Plasma samples to 24 h (IV: 12, 22, 32 min then 1-24 h); urine to 48 h. Assay: HPLC, plasma and urine (CV 9% at 10 ng/mL, 5% at 500 ng/mL). Total drug in plasma (no protein binding or whole-blood measurement).
- Individual IV curves fitted with NONLIN, weight 1/Ci; 11 of 12 fit 3 exponentials (subject 5 two, per Table II). Oral data by model-free analysis plus Loo-Riegelman absorption.

## Model and parameters
3-compartment mammillary model, elimination from the central compartment (Fig. 1, PDF p.4). Values below are Table II means (n=11 for model-dependent terms; printed p.410-411, PDF p.3-4), IV 50 mg.

Absorption is not a single first-order process (biphasic, Fig. 3 p.416, one subject at 100 mg); no ka is reported. Oral Cmax is linear in dose; tmax and t50 do not differ with dose.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No pharmacodynamic end point measured. No ke0, MEAC, therapeutic concentration range, or potency ratio. Not an opioid. The authors remark that peripheral compartments peak about 1 h (shallow) and 2 h (deep) after plasma, "of value in subsequent pharmacodynamic correlations" (p.413).

## Covariates
None analysed. Healthy men only; renal clearance is about 95% of plasma clearance (10.1 of 10.7 L/h; mean CLR 173 mL/min in the Discussion), so renal function would be the expected covariate, but no renal-impaired subjects were studied.

## Caveats
- n=12 healthy young men, no women, no renal impairment, no elderly.
- Early IV sampling was sparse (first sample 12 min); lambda1, k12 etc. are very uncertain (subject 4 lambda1 47.5 /h, SE 1,300; subject 10 SE 2,400), and the means of lambda1, k12, k21 have SD about equal to the mean. Subject 5 fit only 2 exponentials, so Table II means for model terms are n=11.
- Subject 9 has A1 SE "<0.001" (degenerate fit), CLP 27.4 vs about 10 L/h in the model-free rows; CLP mean 10.7 is plotted from the model-free AUC row, which is why it differs from k10*V1 (10.4).
- Table II "CLP" row in the text layer misaligns; confirmed on the rendered page.
- Oral dose in solution, not tablets.
