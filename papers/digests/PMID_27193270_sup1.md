# Overgaard 2016 (Online Resource): liraglutide 3.0 mg population PK, supplement

## Study
Supplement to a population PK analysis of liraglutide 1.8 and 3.0 mg once-daily SC in adults with overweight or obesity from two phase 3 trials (SCALE Obesity and Prediabetes; SCALE Diabetes) (Section S2 and Fig. S3 legend, PDF p3 and p9-10). Baseline weight 60-234 kg (S2, p3). Serum liraglutide in nM; sparse sampling, one sample per PK visit (S1, p2). NONMEM. Main-article demographics are not in this file. Table S2 (PDF p8) is a separate phase 2 dose-finding trial (n=331; 1.2-3.0 mg; 75% women; mean weight 97-100 kg; BMI 35) used only for dose proportionality.

## Model and parameters
One compartment, linear, time-invariant, dose-proportional, first-order absorption (S3, p3). Covariates enter as exp(coefficient) (Table S1 footnote). Only CL/F carries covariates, because V/F is poorly informed (shrinkage 83.2%; S1 and S3).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. Not an opioid: no MEAC, ke0, tPeak or potency ratio. A plasma half-life of about 13 h after SC dosing is quoted from an earlier study (S3).

## Covariates
Weight (power on CL/F), sex, age >=70, race, ethnicity, glycaemic status, dose level (Table S1). Graphical analysis (Fig. S1): exposure falls with rising weight, is lower in men than women, and lower with diabetes; age, race and ethnicity showed no consistent effect. Fig. S3: CL/F against creatinine clearance (post hoc); injection-site effect also post hoc.

## Caveats
V/F poorly identified (sparse sampling, shrinkage 83%), so Cmax is unreliable and simulated profile shape may be slightly inaccurate (S3). Few subjects aged 70 or over and in the Asian and Other race groups. ka fixed. RSEs of 119%, 913% and 904% in Table S1 (dose, Asian, prediabetes) mark effectively null covariates.
