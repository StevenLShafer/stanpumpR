# Zhang 2026: PK-Sim PBPK model of digoxin, healthy adults to children with heart failure

PDF page = printed "n of 18".

## Study
Modelling paper, no new subjects. Digitised literature concentration-time profiles (Engauge); PK-Sim 11.0. Stepwise:
healthy adults (IV and PO, 0.25-1 mg) -> adults with HF (oral 0.1-0.35 mg, single and multiple) -> children with HF
(7 neonates/infants aged 2-81 days, IV 0.014-0.022 mg/kg, one source). Assumptions: P-gp-limited absorption
(Michaelis-Menten), linear Na+/K+-ATPase tissue binding, no metabolism, renal elimination only with "GFR fraction"
1. Acceptance: observed within 0.5-2 fold of predicted, also for AUC0-t and Cmax. Then simulated FDA Lanoxin
paediatric regimens (Table 1: PO loading 0.02-0.03 mg/kg neonates, 0.03-0.04 infants, 0.02-0.03 children; IV 75%).

Results (text): healthy adults most AUC0-t predictions 0.9-1.0 of observed; adult HF AUC ratios 0.84-1.30, Cmax
mostly 0.88-1.03; children AUC observed/predicted 0.59-1.23, Cmax 0.54-0.87 (p8-9).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD. Therapeutic window 0.5-2 ng/mL applied to simulated steady-state troughs: low-dose neonatal regimens fall
below 0.5 ng/mL; infants and children mostly within.

## Covariates
Physiological scaling (age, organ size, GFR); HF as halved blood flows, lower GFR, higher intestinal permeability,
lower Na+/K+-ATPase affinity.

## Caveats
Digitised literature means, only 7 paediatric subjects (IV only), no P-gp ontogeny, no HF severity strata, no PD.
Internal inconsistencies: GFR in children 4.2 "L/min" in text vs mL/min in Table 2; child kidney blood flow 0.07
L/min (text) vs 0.06 (Table 2); Eq 1 defines the ratio as observed/predicted while the text speaks of
prediction-to-observation ratios; "approximately 25% plasma protein binding" vs fu 0.71 (29% bound).
