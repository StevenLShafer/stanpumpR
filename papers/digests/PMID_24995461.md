# Jensen 2014: qCON and qNOX during propofol-remifentanil anaesthesia

## Study
Observational validation study, Hospital Clinic Barcelona (ethics protocol 2009/4969, written consent). n=60 ambulatory surgical patients (inguinal hernia, laparoscopic cholecystectomy, gynaecologic laparoscopy etc.); age, weight and sex not given. General anaesthesia with propofol and remifentanil by effect-site TCI (Base Primea pump): Schnider propofol model and Minto remifentanil model (PDF p2, printed 934). No dosing protocol for preventing movement; movement was only observed. BIS (Vista, 10 s smoothing) recorded with the qCON 2000 monitor (Quantium). Sampling 1024 Hz, 16 bit; samples with signal quality index <50 rejected. Sample size 60 from power 0.9, alpha 0.05, SD 24, difference 20.
End points: loss of eyelash reflex = LOC; movement within 1 min of LMA insertion, laryngoscopy or tracheal intubation (movers vs non-movers); prediction probability Pk (jackknife SE). All 60 completed; 20 moved to at least one stimulus (p4).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Opioid audit (remifentanil): the paper reports no MEAC or minimum effective/analgesic concentration, no ke0, t1/2ke0 or tPeak, no hysteresis analysis and no equianalgesic or potency ratio. Concentrations are model-predicted effect-site values (Ce), not measured: no assay. The only remifentanil numbers are the Ce means above (movers 2.85 vs non-movers 2.96 ng/mL, not significantly different, p5) and the observed range 0-5.3 ng/mL. The authors conclude qNOX predicted movement better than the drug concentrations did. End points are qCON/qNOX/BIS values, eyelash reflex and movement; not an effect-site equilibration study. The qNOX reference scale (Appendix, p8-9) was built from nailbed-pressure response at Ramsay levels 5-6 and OAAS levels 0-1 in a 590-patient database.

## Covariates
None analysed (no age/weight/sex effects reported).

## Caveats
Authors: surgical stimuli were mild; relaxants and frontal EMG may affect qNOX; qNOX not validated against other nociception markers; first validation of qNOX. Three authors are employees of Quantium Medical, the index developer (p6). Movers and non-movers are defined by any movement in 1 min, no blinded pain scoring. n=60 with 20 movers.
