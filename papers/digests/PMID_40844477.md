# Royston 2025: letermovir PopPK in adult HCT recipients (real-world)

## Study
Post hoc pharmacometric analysis of a prospective observational study (Geneva), adult allogeneic HCT recipients on oral letermovir prophylaxis, 1 Mar 2020 to 20 Apr 2021: 480 mg once daily, or 240 mg once daily with ciclosporin (CSA). 40 patients (6 on CSA); 217 trough samples (24 +/- 2 h) plus 79 non-trough samples. Each visit treated as a steady-state occasion; time after dose. Monolix 2023 for fitting, Simulx 2023R1 to simulate the phase III model (Prohn 2021). Assay and matrix are not described in this short form.

## Model and parameters
Phase III model over-predicted: median (IQR) predicted trough 480 (263-838) ng/mL vs observed 260 (123-518) for 480 mg (1.8x); 1,104 (628-1,771) vs 418 (235-945) for 240 mg + CSA (2.6x) (p.2). Refit: one-compartment, ka and lag fixed to phase III values; AIC 4,830 to 3,739. Table 1 (p.3), typical values with RSE:

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No concentration-effect, MEAC or exposure target. The authors note the link between concentration and breakthrough CMV is unclear. Not an opioid.

## Covariates
Univariable only (p.2): higher CL/F with higher weight (allometric), younger age, higher albumin, vomiting; lower CL/F with acute GvHD, prednisone, posaconazole. No association with sex, GFR, liver enzymes, CRP, neutrophils, pantoprazole, or measured CSA concentration. Authors call these hypothesis-generating. No coefficients given.

## Caveats
Sparse sampling mostly at trough; no IV data, so absorption, F and distribution are poorly identified and F cannot be separated from CL; V/F and CL/F are apparent. High IIV/IOV; convergence to a global minimum uncertain. CSA effect imprecise (RSE 69.5%). The comparator for "lower exposure" is the published phase III model simulation, not another cohort.
