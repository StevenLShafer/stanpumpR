# Eger 1965: MAC as a standard of anesthetic potency

## Study
UCSF, dogs, mostly halothane (some fluroxene/methoxyflurane). MAC technique of Merkel and Eger 1963; induction with the agent in oxygen (thiopental abandoned after it reduced MAC 5-20% for 2-4 h). Endotracheal tube, arterial line, esophageal temperature 37.5 +/- 1.0 C. End-tidal halothane by infrared analyzer. Alveolar concentration held >= 15 min before stimulation (3 time constants for halothane assuming CBF 50 mL/100 g/min and brain/blood 2.5). Stimulus applied 30-40 s, usually 1 min; positive = gross purposeful movement of head or extremities (coughing, swallowing, chewing excluded). MAC = midpoint between highest concentration allowing and lowest preventing movement (max error ~10%).

## Model and parameters
Definition: MAC is the minimum alveolar concentration preventing movement in response to a painful stimulus; potency = 1/MAC. Concentrations are vol% relative to 760 mmHg.

Table 1 stimuli (mean of 3 dogs): tail clamp 0.81, tail clamp + CO2 0.80, 10 V 0.66, 30 V 0.83, 50 V 0.84, ET tube movement 0.55, incision 0.69, paw clamp 0.50, spontaneous 0.46%. Tail clamp chosen as a supramaximal stimulus (adding 40 V did not raise MAC).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- Within-dog deviation between successive determinations 0.07 +/- 0.07%; between days 0.07 +/- 0.05% (Fig. 1).
- Unaffected: duration, PaCO2 14-60 mmHg (0.94 -> 0.95 -> 0.91%), phenylephrine hypertension (0.77 -> 0.83%), PaO2 > 30 mmHg.
- Reduced: metabolic acidosis (NH4Cl, 0.90 -> 0.73%, pH 7.38 -> 7.20; bicarbonate to 0.79%), hemorrhagic hypotension (0.96 -> 0.76%), PaO2 < 30 mmHg (fall 0.25 and 0.5%).
- Additivity proposed: PA/POA + PB/POB = 1 (Discussion, p. 762).
- No ke0; not an opioid paper.

## Covariates
None modelled (acid-base, blood pressure, oxygenation tested as perturbations).

## Caveats
Dogs, one agent (halothane) for the perturbation studies, acute stresses only, small groups (3-7 dogs). Paper defines and validates MAC; the original technique is Merkel and Eger 1963. No nitrogen MAC estimate in this paper.
