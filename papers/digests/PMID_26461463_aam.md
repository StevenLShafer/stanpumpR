# Ryu 2016: metoprolol pharmacokinetics in pregnancy and lactation (accepted manuscript)

## Study
Prospective steady-state PK study (Obstetric-Fetal Pharmacology Research Unit network; Univ. Washington, UTMB
Galveston, Univ. Pittsburgh). 22 pregnant women aged 21-40 taking oral metoprolol for clinical reasons
(hypertension 10, arrhythmia 4, others), 25-750 mg/day in divided doses, immediate-release (Caraco) or
extended-release (Toprol-XL); each woman kept one formulation throughout (PDF p2-3). Study windows: early (10-14 wk,
n = 4), mid (22-26 wk), late (34-38 wk) pregnancy, and ≥ 3 months postpartum (n = 9; 4 lactating, 5 not).
Blood at 0, 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 24 h truncated to the dosing interval, urine over one interval,
breast milk every 2-3 h, maternal and cord blood at delivery (PDF p3). LC-MS/MS; plasma LOQ 1.0 ng/mL for
metoprolol and α-hydroxymetoprolol. Noncompartmental: AUCτ,ss by linear trapezoid, **CL/F = dose/AUCτ,ss**;
weight-adjusted values use actual body weight (PDF p3-4). CYP2D6 genotyped: 14 EM, 6 IM, 1 PM (*4/*4), 1 not
fully genotyped; no gene duplications (PDF p5). The PM and a woman on fluoxetine were excluded from PK
comparisons; women on low-dose sertraline or hydroxychloroquine were kept (PDF p5). Wilcoxon signed-rank for paired
comparisons.

Table 1 (PDF p17, text layer): body weight 73 ± 8 (early), 89 ± 25 (mid), 98 ± 26 (late), 81 ± 27 kg
(postpartum); median dose 87.5, 75, 100, 75 mg/day.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No pharmacodynamic measurement: no heart rate or blood pressure end point, no ke0. The discussion notes
α-hydroxymetoprolol has about one-tenth of metoprolol's β-blocking potency (ref 34, a dog study; PDF p10).

## Covariates
Gestational period (paired comparisons), CYP2D6 genotype (EM/IM/PM, descriptive), formulation (IR vs ER; CL/F
not different, so pooled). CLrenal correlated weakly with creatinine clearance (r = 0.38) and urine pH (r = −0.39).

## Caveats
- Small paired groups (n = 5 mid, 8 late); large SDs (CV ~50%).
- Doses were clinical, not standardised (25-750 mg/day); metoprolol bioavailability is dose dependent, which the
  authors address only with the n = 4 same-dose subset.
- Comparator is the same women ≥ 3 months postpartum, some lactating, not nonpregnant controls; patients with
  cardiovascular disease, not volunteers.
- Oral dosing only: CL/F cannot be divided into systemic clearance and bioavailability from this study.
- Accepted manuscript: numbers should be re-checked against the published J Clin Pharmacol PDF when it is archived.
