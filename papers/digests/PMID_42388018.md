# Goeyvaerts 2026: canagliflozin popPK and HbA1c PK/PD in children 10-17 y

## Study
Adult-to-paediatric extrapolation of Hoeben 2016's popPK model and de Winter 2017's HbA1c PK/PD model using two paediatric studies (p3623-3625). Phase 1 DIA1055 (NCT02000700): open-label, 100 or 300 mg once daily for 14 days, rich sampling on Day 14 (pre-dose, 0.5, 1, 2, 4, 8, 12, 24, 48, 72 h); n = 17, median age 15 y, median weight 116 kg (48.2-170), median BMI 41.4. Phase 3 DIA3018 (NCT03170518): placebo-controlled, 100 mg once daily with optional step to 300 mg at Week 13, sparse trough samples at Weeks 12, 26, 52; n = 73, median weight 75.1 kg (44.8-161), median BMI 29.9, eGFR (Schwartz) median 160 (70.3-277) mL/min/1.73 m2 across paediatric patients (Table 1, p3625). Total 90 patients and 330 PK samples; 15.1% BLQ excluded (LLOQ 5 ng/mL, LC-MS/MS, plasma). Only overencapsulated tablets were given. HbA1c: 702 samples from 160 patients. Adult comparators: Hoeben's 1616 people, weight median 86 kg, eGFR 87 (MDRD). NONMEM 7.4, FOCE-I; PK/PD simulations in mrgsolve.

Structure unchanged from the adult model: two compartments (rate constants), lag Tlag, sequential zero-order input into gut over D1, then first-order absorption (p3624). CL/F = Vc/F x ke (text, p3624); the weight effect on Vc/F therefore acts on CL/F. The sensitivity model with fixed allometric exponents (1.0 on Vc/F, -0.25 on ke) had OFV +20.7 and underpredicted Cmax at 100 mg in phase 1 (p3627). Individual steady-state AUC24h, Table 3 (p3628, text layer): 100 mg QD paediatric male 6574 (SD 1774), female 8397 (2155) h*ng/mL vs adult male 7183, female 8984; 300 mg QD paediatric male 22 480 (7858), female 29 930 (11 839) vs adult male 22 954, female 28 391. Exposure in females slightly higher; QD and BID give similar AUC24h, Cmax higher with QD.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point HbA1c (not an opioid; no ke0 or MEAC). Adult turnover model with Emax drug effect on HbA1c formation, re-estimated only for baseline HbA1c, placebo effect and residual error. Table 4 (PDF p9, printed p3630; image-confirmed): baseline HbA1c 8.50 (insulin, placebo), 7.82 (insulin, canagliflozin), 7.91 (no insulin, placebo), 7.22 (no insulin, canagliflozin) %; HbA1c half-life 28.2 days FIX; Efp 0.171% (insulin), -0.238% (no insulin); Emax -0.738% FIX; log EC50 4.12 log(ng/mL) FIX, equal to EC50 61.6 ng/mL (footnote b; derived check exp(4.12) = 61.6); residual SD 0.0773. EC50 is a plasma concentration (total, plasma). Phase 3 placebo-adjusted HbA1c difference at Week 26 was -0.76% (-1.25 to -0.27) (p3623). QD and BID dosing at the same total daily dose give similar simulated HbA1c.

## Covariates
Retained from the adult model: body weight on Vc/F, sex on Vc/F, total daily dose on ke, overencapsulation on Tlag. Removed: age on Vc/F, eGFR on ke (paediatric eGFR higher, Schwartz vs MDRD), BMI on ka and Tlag (p3627-3628, 3631). The authors caution that no children under 40 kg were studied and only six phase 1 patients weighed under 95 kg (p3627, 3632).

## Caveats
Almost all PK parameters fixed to adults; high shrinkage (>39%) from sparse phase 3 data; phase 2/3 residual error doubled (0.559 to 1.14), possibly from dosing-time deviations; only 12 patients up-titrated to 300 mg; caution advised for 300 mg below 50 kg (p3632). eGFR methods differ (Schwartz paediatric, MDRD adult).
