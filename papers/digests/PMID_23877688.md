# Fauchet 2013: zidovudine and G-ZDV population PK in HIV-infected children

## Study
(Printed page = PDF page + 4800.)
- Retrospective therapeutic-drug-monitoring data, Paris area, 1998-2012; no ethics approval or consent (French TDM
  rules, PDF p2).
- n = 247 HIV-infected children, 119 boys / 128 girls; age 0.15-18 y (Table 2 median 10.8 y; text says 10.6 y);
  weight median 32.2 kg (Table 2 range 6-85; text 6.1-84 kg) (Table 2 and text, PDF p3).
- Oral zidovudine only: solution 100 mg/10 mL, capsules 100/250 mg, or Combivir/Trizivir tablets (300 mg ZDV); 94.4%
  q12h, 5.6% q8h; median 300 mg BID (range 18-300 mg); dose/day median 600 mg (36-600). Syrup 33.7%, tablet 66.3%.
  Co-treatments: 3TC 51%, LPV 39%, RTV 41%, NVP 18%, NFV 13%, ABC 11%, EFV 11%, ddI 10%.
- 782 ZDV and 554 G-ZDV (glucuronide) plasma concentrations, mean 3.6 samples/child (1-19), all assumed steady state,
  sampling times uncontrolled. HPLC; LLOQ 0.05 mg/L for both; 5.9% ZDV and 2.3% G-ZDV samples below LOQ, set to LOQ/2
  (M2/M3 did not improve).
- Concentrations converted to molar (ZDV 267.2, G-ZDV 443.3 g/mol). NONMEM 6.2, FOCE. Covariates: OFV drop >= 6.63;
  continuous covariates as power of (BW/median BW). Bootstrap (WfN) and VPC.

## Model and parameters
One-compartment ZDV with first-order absorption and elimination, plus a G-ZDV compartment fed by a first-order formation
rate constant (CLm/Vm) and emptied by kel. A two-compartment model, lag time, or zero-order absorption did not improve
the fit (PDF p4). ka was fixed (insufficient absorption data) to 2.86 h-1 from Panhard 2007 (ref 28).

Table 3 (PDF p4, printed p4804), confirmed on the rendered page; estimate, %RSE, bootstrap 95% CI:
- ka 2.86 h-1, fixed
- CLp/F 89.7 L/h (7.1; 77-102), x (BW/median BW)^0.858 (11.1; 0.67-1.1)
- V/F 229 L (12.4; 181-291), x (BW/median BW)^0.534 (24.5; 0.22-0.81)
- CLm/Vm 12.6 h-1 (20.7; 8.2-18), metabolic formation rate constant
- kel (G-ZDV) 2.27 h-1 (16.2; 1.6-3.1)
- BSV (omega, CV): CLp/F 0.701 (17.9; 0.572-0.820); V/F 0.807 (22.9; 0.597-0.963); CLm/Vm 0.352 (20.7; 0.213-0.473)
- Corr(CLp, V) 0.733 (0.5-0.85)
- Residual: ZDV combined, additive a = 0.1 (fixed, labelled mmol/liter) and proportional 0.56; G-ZDV proportional 0.69

The median BW used for centring is not printed beside the equation; the study median is 32.2 kg (Table 2), so 89.7 L/h
and 229 L are typical values at about 32 kg (inferred from the Methods centring rule, PDF p2). Derived at 32.2 kg:
CL/F 2.79 L/h/kg, V/F 7.1 L/kg, elimination t1/2 = 0.693 x 229/89.7 = 1.77 h. The paper reports the median individual
t1/2 as 1.45 h, Tmax 0.76 h, and median apparent clearance 3.02 L/h/kg (PDF p5); G-ZDV t1/2 = 0.693/2.27 = 0.31 h (derived).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD model, no ke0. Exposure targets taken from other studies (PDF p1, p6):
- Efficacy target: median adult ZDV AUC 3-5 mg.h/L (3 in adults, refs 33-34; 5 in children, ref 9).
- Toxicity: AUC > 8.4 mg.h/L raised anaemia risk from 7.6% to 23.4% (Capparelli 2003, ref 13); AUC > 19.2 mg.h/L
  raised neutropenia risk (Balis 1989, ref 4).
Simulations (400 per patient; Table 4, PDF p7; AUC 0-24 h per footnote c): median AUC 5.2-6.6 (WHO) and 5.2-5.9 (FDA)
in the 20-40 kg bands; >8.4 mg.h/L in 21.5-40% (WHO) and 17.9-26.8% (FDA). Proposed doses 8 mg/kg BID (20-29.9 kg),
250 mg BID (30-39.9 kg), 300 mg BID from 40 kg give median AUC 4.5, 4.8, 4.8, 4.4 mg.h/L and 12.4-17% above 8.4.

## Covariates
Body weight on CL/F (OFV -110) and V/F (additional -15.7), power functions with estimated exponents; fixed allometry
(0.75/1) did not improve the fit. Age (continuous or <2 vs >=2 y), sex, dosage form and co-treatments (incl. EFV, NVP,
ddI, NFV) were not significant; nothing affected CLm/Vm.

## Caveats
- TDM data with uncontrolled sampling, mean 3.6 samples; ka fixed from an adult study; all parameters apparent (oral).
- Unit inconsistency: the additive error is given as 0.1 "mmol/liter" (Table 3) and "0.1 mmol-1" in the text, as
  LOQ/2 = 0.025 mg/L; 0.025 mg/L / 267.2 g/mol = 0.094 umol/L (derived), so micromol/L is presumably meant. It
  affects only the residual model.
- Table 2 and text disagree on median age (10.8 vs 10.6 y) and weight range (6-85 vs 6.1-84 kg).
- The abstract gives BSV with units ("0.701 liters/h"); Table 3 defines omega as a CV (unitless).
- The 3-5 mg.h/L target is called the adult AUC while Table 4 reports AUC 0-24 h; whether the cited adult values are
  per dose or per day is not stated (not relevant to stanpumpR as yet).
- Table 1 footnote c ("for an adult weighing 150 kg") explains the low end of the mg/kg/day ranges.
- No neonates (<6 months); no IV dosing; no pregnant patients.
