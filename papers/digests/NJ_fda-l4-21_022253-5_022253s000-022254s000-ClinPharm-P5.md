# FDA 2008: lacosamide NDA 22-253/22-254 clinical pharmacology review, part 5

## Study
A regulatory review, not one study. What this part contains:
- **Sponsor population PK reports** (pp. 23/2-72/2), all one-compartment models with first-order absorption and
  elimination (NONMEM IV, ADVAN2; FO or FOCE):
  - SP640, healthy subjects in the thorough QT study (final model Table 3, p. 23/2).
  - SP620, healthy young and elderly men and women: 36 subjects, 1169 samples, 100 mg single doses on days 1 and 8,
    then 100 mg twice daily on days 4-7; HPLC-MS/MS, LOQ 0.1 ug/mL (pp. 26/2-32/2).
  - SP755, partial-onset seizures: 1008 concentrations from 292 subjects (p. 35/2).
  - SP754, partial-onset seizures: 1322 concentrations from 278 subjects; LOQ 0.05 ug/mL (p. 42/2).
  - SP665, diabetic neuropathy: 1440 concentrations from 69 subjects (p. 51/2).
  - SP742, diabetic neuropathy: 1660 concentrations from 270 subjects (p. 59/2).
  - SP743, diabetic neuropathy: 1654 concentrations from 264 subjects (p. 66/2).
- **Sponsor exposure-response report SPM927** (pp. 73/2-76/2): partial-onset seizures, pooled SP667, SP754 and
  SP755. 3055 records from 615 subjects. Placebo subjects and non-responders were excluded (baseline-slope criteria).
  The fit was SAS proc nlin with an individual (two-stage) Emax model on AUCtau,ss.
- **FDA reviewer's comments and analysis** (pp. 83/2-87/2): the same three trials, one observation per subject at
  the end of titration (147/161/170 subjects) and at the end of maintenance (132/122/167; Table 19, p. 84/2).
  Pooled Emax fit by nonlinear least squares (S-Plus nls/nlsList or SAS PROC NLIN).
- **Biopharmaceutics review** (PDF pp. 62-90): the BCS class 1 biowaiver, dissolution, and the IV-vs-tablet
  bioavailability studies SP645 and SP658.
- **OCP filing review** (PDF pp. 91-108): a summary of the clinical pharmacology studies.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- **End point**: the reduction in the daily number of partial-onset seizures from baseline, against AUCtau,ss (the
  12-h steady-state AUC).
- **Sponsor (SPM927)**: E(AUC) = Emax·AUC/(AUC50 + AUC). The Emax model beat linear and Emax=100 models on
  weighted sum of squares, but every model's parameters were very variable. Table 17 (N = 615):
  - AUC50 mean 35.9 (SD 185.6), median 1.05, range 0-3998, 1st/3rd quartiles 0 and 27.27 ug/mL·h.
  - Emax mean 71.0% (SD 30.0), median 77.0%.
- **Sponsor's interpretation** (pp. 75/2-76/2):
  - AUC50 35.9 means a 35% seizure reduction, which is half of Emax.
  - 35.9 ug·h/mL is about 110 mg twice daily in a typical subject, assuming V 50 L and ke 0.06/h.
  - 67 ug·h/mL (200 mg twice daily) gives a 46% reduction, 65% of Emax.
  - 100 ug·h/mL (300 mg twice daily) gives a 52% reduction, 74% of Emax.
- **FDA reviewer** (Table 20, p. 86/2), pooled fit, P < 0.0001:
  - end of titration: Emax −70.7, EC50 7.8;
  - end of maintenance: Emax −55.9, EC50 4.3.
  - The reviewer concluded that 400 mg/day gives a response similar to 600 mg/day (Figure 23). Because placebo
    and non-responders were excluded, the curve may be inaccurate outside the observed range.
- **Reviewer's criticisms** (p. 83/2): the two-stage method biases the estimates; responders-only data is a
  limitation; time effects were ignored.
- No therapeutic concentration range, ke0 or effect-site model is given. Not an opioid: there is no MEAC or potency
  ratio.
- **QT** (SP640, filing review p. 10): placebo-corrected QTcI changes were −4.3 ms at 400 mg/day and −6.3 ms at
  800 mg/day, with no QT prolongation.

## Covariates
- **V/F**: lean body weight and/or height, depending on the trial (FFM in SP620; height and weight in SP742).
- **ke**: CLcr and BMI in SP620; age in SP665 and SP743; enzyme-inducing AEDs (carbamazepine, phenobarbital,
  phenytoin) in SP754 and SP755. Equations are in the table above.
- Sex, race and liver tests were not retained. The reviewers concluded that no dose adjustment is needed for the
  tested factors.

## Caveats
- The E-R analyses used responders only, with placebo excluded.
- The sponsor's individual AUC50 values are extremely skewed (mean 35.9 against median 1.05). The FDA's pooled
  EC50 values (4.3-7.8) lie near the bottom of the clinical AUC range (about 67-100 ug·h/mL at 200-300 mg twice
  daily), so the curve is nearly flat over the doses used.
- Table 20 gives no units for Emax or EC50. Figure 23's axes ("Change in Average Number of Partial Seizures",
  "Steady State AUC") suggest % change and ug·h/mL.
- ka was fixed in most models.
- The SPM929 neuropathy E-R pages (77/2-82/2) are absent, and the FDA neuropathy analysis appears to be withheld.
