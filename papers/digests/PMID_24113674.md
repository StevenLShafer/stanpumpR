# Pilla Reddy 2013: oral haloperidol population PK and PANSS PK/PD model

## Study
- PK data: 7 studies, 122 individuals, of whom 20 were healthy volunteers (16 + 4 men) and 102 schizophrenic patients; 538 plasma concentrations; oral doses 1-60 mg/day, single or multiple (Table 1: Kapur x2, Xiberas, Farde, Liem-Moolenaar, Nordstrom, Giegling/LMU).
- PANSS data: 4 studies (3 phase III comparator arms from Janssen and Pfizer trials, plus the open-label LMU study), 473 patients, 2,342 PANSS observations, 4-8 weeks; doses 2.5-40 mg/day.
- NONMEM VII (FOCE +/- I; Laplace for dropout), PsN 3.2.4; log-transformed concentrations; bootstrap (985 / 957 successful) and SSE.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: PANSS total (also positive and negative subscales), weeks of treatment; not a moment-to-moment concentration effect.
- PANSS = Baseline x [1 - Pmax x (1 - exp(-(t/TD)^POW))] x [1 - Emax x Css/(EC50 + Css) x (1 - exp(-KT x t))], Weibull placebo (TD, POW fixed from earlier work), drug effect driven by each patient's average steady-state concentration Css = dose / (CL/F) / tau.
- Table 2: baseline PANSS 91.6; Pmax 0.081; Emax 0.31; EC50 3.58 ng/mL (RSE 39%; IIV 152% CV, shrinkage about 50%); KT 0.116 /day (delay half-time 6 days); IIV Pmax 0.20 SD, baseline 16% CV, Emax 0.29 SD; RUV 8.7 points.
- Dropout: exponential time-to-event, hazard = BHAZ x exp(PANSS x BETA): BHAZ 0.00139 (placebo) and 0.0009 /day (haloperidol), BETA -0.0295 (sign as printed; text says dropout rises with PANSS).
- Effective exposure for a 30% PANSS reduction (Table 3): total 2.7 ng/mL, 5.6 mg/day; positive subscale (Emax 0.41, EC50 1.2) 0.54 ng/mL, 1.2 mg/day; negative subscale (Emax 0.21, EC50 6.4) 31 ng/mL, 65 mg/day. Text gives subscale Emax 0.48 vs 0.21 and EC50 1.28 vs 6.39, differing from Table 3 for the positive subscale. Chronic patients' EC50 8.5 ng/mL (data not shown).
- D2 occupancy (Discussion, from Uchida 2011, not fitted here): ROmax 84%, Kd 0.32 ng/mL plasma; 2.7 ng/mL corresponds to 75% occupancy.
- No ke0 (PD driven by Css), no MEAC.

## Covariates
Age, sex, weight, regimen, healthy vs patient, acute vs chronic tested: none significant at P = 0.05. Nevertheless CL/F carried a fixed allometric weight term, CL/F = TVCL x (WT/70)^0.75, for all fits; no weight term on volumes.

## Caveats
- Pooled heterogeneous studies, many patients without individual PK (Css from population PK for chronic-trial patients), RUV 44%, IIV Vc 116%.
- No reduced-haloperidol metabolite kinetics.
- Table 2 is unreadable as laid out in the text layer; values above were reassembled by order and agree with every value quoted in the Results text.
