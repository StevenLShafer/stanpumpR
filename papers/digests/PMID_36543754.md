# Penland 2023: dapagliflozin popPK in CKD with/without T2DM

## Study
Re-estimation of a sponsor (AstraZeneca) population PK model for oral dapagliflozin with data added from DAPA-CKD (NCT03036150; 1478 patients' PK used). Pooled with adult T2DM studies (NCT00162305, NCT00528372, NCT00736879, NCT00673231), healthy subjects (MB102002) and a paediatric T2DM study (NCT01525238). Final data set 9715 quantifiable plasma concentrations from 3055 patients (of 11,480 samples from 3101 patients). Assay: LC-MS/MS on plasma, LLOQ 0.2 ng/mL (NCT03036150, NCT01525238) and 1 ng/mL (adult T2DM and healthy studies). NONMEM 7.3. Table 1 (PDF p4, medians, range): N 3055; age 60 y (11-93); weight 84 kg (39-168); eGFR (CKD-EPI 2009) 56 (19-154); BMI 30. DAPA-CKD without T2DM n=560, with T2DM n=1218, adult T2DM n=1223, paediatric T2DM n=24, healthy n=30. Height is not given.

## Model and parameters
Previous model refitted, not rebuilt: two compartments, first-order absorption with a fixed lag time, first-order elimination; BSV on Ka, CL/F and apparent peripheral volume; combined error on log data (separate proportional error for the paediatric study). Covariates (Eq 1-2, PDF p4): CL/F = CLref/F x (eGFR/eGFRref)^exponent x (male term or female factor); Vc/F = Vcref/F x (weight/weightref)^exponent. A dichotomous CKD effect on CL/F was tested and not retained (dOFV -2.23; CL/F lower by 4.8% in CKD without T2DM). The full parameter table is Supplementary Table S3 (not in the PDF). Model-derived AUC normalised to 10 mg (PDF p5-6 text; Figure 2): median 757 ng.h/mL CKD with T2DM, 778 CKD without T2DM, 464 adult T2DM without CKD (1.6x and 1.7x higher than the latter). Versus eGFR >=90: 1.6x (eGFR 45-59), 2.0x (30-44), 2.4x (15-29). By race (White 589, Black 614, Asian 751, other 790 ng.h/mL). Weight <70 vs 70-100 kg 1.2x; age >=75 vs 65-74 y 1.1x; women vs men 1.0x.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None modelled. An exposure-response result from another study is quoted (each 100 ng.h/mL AUC rise associated with eGFR change -0.5 mL/min/1.73 m2). No MEAC, ke0 or therapeutic range. A Cmax cut-off of 100 ng/mL (previous T2DM model, 10 mg) was used to flag postdose "troughs".

## Covariates
eGFR (CKD-EPI 2009; the previous model used MDRD) on CL/F, sex on CL/F, body weight on Vc/F. CKD as a separate covariate not retained. Exposure rises as eGFR falls, similarly with and without T2DM (Figure 2b, PDF p6).

## Caveats
Authors: no new high-resolution model, absorption poorly informed (DAPA-CKD mostly troughs), 2009 not 2021 CKD-EPI, high condition number. Sponsor-employed authors. 525 (4.57%) troughs excluded as postdose.

## Used by stanpumpR
The context file lists no repo location (not cited). The library models dapagliflozin from the sister paper (Melin 2023, Br J Clin Pharmacol 2024;90:606-612) in R/drugs_dapagliflozin.R, and a Kobuchi model in R/drugs_dapagliflozinKobuchi.R.

## Verification against stanpumpR
R/drugs_dapagliflozin.R:24-27,109-117 uses CL/F 23.0 L/h x (eGFR/83)^0.584 x exp(-0.174) in women, Vc/F 73.9 L x (weight/89)^0.570, Q/F 8.92 L/h, Vp/F 143 L, ka 2.39/h, lag 0.248 h. This paper cannot confirm those numbers (they are in Supplement S3). What it does support: the same structure (two compartments, lagged first-order absorption), eGFR and sex on CL/F and weight on Vc/F; no extra CKD effect beyond eGFR, consistent with the code's eGFR-only clearance. The 21.6 L/h here is a population average across all studies, not a reference-subject value, so it is not comparable with 23.0 at eGFR 83 in a man; 23.0 L/h is quoted by these authors for the Melin HFrEF model, matching the code's reference CL/F. The code's 0.584 appears in this paper's text, but attribution is unclear (open question). Verdict: parameters **cannot verify**; structure **matches**.
