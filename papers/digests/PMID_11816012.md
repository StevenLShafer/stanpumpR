# Werner 2002: LC-MS assay and single-dose celecoxib NCA in 12 volunteers incl. one CYP2C9 PM

## Study
Assay paper (HPLC-APCI-MS/MS, rofecoxib internal standard, LOQ 5 µg/L, linear 5-2000 µg/L, recovery ~75%) with a
small PK application. 12 healthy volunteers (10 men, 2 women; 22-35 y; BMI 19.1-32.5; weights not given), single
oral Celebrex 200 mg (the paper says "tablet"), plasma at 0, 1, 2, 3, 6, 9, 12, 24, 32, 48, 52, 72 h. CYP2C9 *2/*3
genotyped by TaqMan; 11 extensive metabolizers (EM), 1 poor metabolizer (PM; exact genotype not stated; subject 4,
female, BMI 20.1). NCA (TopFit 2.0), linear trapezoid, λz extrapolation.

Individual EM AUC∞ range 2898-9464 µg·h/L. BMI correlated negatively with AUC∞ (r = -0.76).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. Not an opioid; no MEAC, ke0 or potency.

## Covariates
CYP2C9 (one PM, AUC ~2× EM mean, t½ not prolonged); BMI association noted descriptively.

## Caveats
n = 12, single PM; half-life (7.8 h) shorter than the label's ~11 h, possibly from sampling/LOQ; authors compare
with FDA NDA 20-998 values (n = 7, AUC 5986 ± 4032). Only 2 women. Dosage form stated as tablet although Celebrex
is a capsule.
