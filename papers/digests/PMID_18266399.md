# Campanero 2008: terazosin PK-PD on blood pressure in healthy volunteers

## Study
- 24 healthy Caucasian volunteers (12 M, 12 F), 22-26 y, 50-80 kg, within 20% of ideal weight, normotensive
  (PDF p3, printed p141).
- Single oral terazosin 2 mg tablet (Deflox, Abbott) with 200 mL water after a 10-h fast; fasting to 4 h; sodium- and
  potassium-controlled diet.
- Plasma at 20, 40, 60, 80, 100, 120, 150 min and 3, 4, 5, 7, 9, 11, 13 h. Sitting oscillometric BP and HR before the
  20, 40, 60, 80, 100 min and 2, 2.5, 3, 5 h samples (HP Viridia), and at 0 and 13 h.
- HPLC-fluorescence, prazosin internal standard, LOQ 0.25 ng/mL, calibration 0.25-100 ng/mL, within-day CV < 9.8%,
  between-day < 11.2% (PDF p3-4).
- WinNonlin 1.5, subject-by-subject: one-compartment first-order absorption PK first, parameters fixed, then
  effect-compartment inhibitory Emax E = E0 - Emax x Ce/(Ce + EC50) (Eq. 3, PDF p4). Linear and sigmoid Emax also
  tried; sigmoid gave no advantage (printed p146). Results reported as mean +/- SD of individual estimates
  (Statistical analysis, PDF p5), and tables are headed "Mean +/- SD".

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End points: sitting oscillometric systolic and diastolic BP. Hysteresis observed between plasma concentration and BP
  reduction (direction of the loop not stated); collapsed with an effect compartment, rate constant named ke0 in Tables II-III and the text (PDF p6-7).
- SBP (Table II, PDF p7): E0 125.0, Emax 29.9 mmHg, ke0 0.401 /h, EC50 29.9 ug/L.
  DBP (Table III): E0 79.69, Emax 39.69 mmHg, ke0 0.472 /h, EC50 28.7 ug/L.
- t1/2ke0 (derived: ln2/ke0): 1.73 h SBP, 1.47 h DBP. The text (PDF p6) gives a range of 1.2-1.8 h and the
  Discussion "approximately 1.2" for ke0 0.4 /h, which does not match ln2/0.4 = 1.73 h.
- Observed mean falls: SBP 9.3 +/- 4.3 mmHg (9.78%), DBP 12.0 +/- 7.3 mmHg (19.51%); measured baselines
  118.9/67.1 mmHg, below the fitted E0 of 125.0/79.69. HR unchanged to 3 h, then +6.1 +/- 2.3 /min (PDF p5-6).
- Maximum BP fall said to occur about 2-3 h after dosing. Not an opioid: no MEAC or potency ratio.

## Covariates
None.

## Caveats
- Healthy young normotensive volunteers, single first dose (authors note first-dose responses exceed chronic ones);
  sampling only to 13 h, short against a terminal t1/2 of 6.19 h (the label gives about 12 h).
- Emax (39.7 mmHg DBP) is far beyond the observed 12 mmHg fall: an extrapolated asymptote; EC50 is close to Cmax.
- Internal inconsistencies: CL/F SD 8.1 > mean; ke0 SD vs CVinter; t1/2ke0 "1.2 h" vs ln2/0.4; "CVinter" is
  described as a population-analysis precision although fits were individual.

- **Catalog record** (registry csv :120): label tmax about 1 h, t1/2 about 12 h. This study (comparator: its own 24
