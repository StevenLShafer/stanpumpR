## Study
Descriptive equipment article on Dräger Narkomed machines, from the Journal of Clinical Monitoring "Knowing Your Monitoring Equipment" series. No subjects, no drug, no PK or PD data. Topics: internal pneumatic circuit (yoke and pipeline check valves, DISS connectors, oxygen flush, oxygen failure protection device, low-pressure alarm switch, minimum oxygen flow, flow control valves, tandem flow tubes, oxygen ratio monitor controller) and breathing circuit (CO2 absorber, PEEP valve, APL valve, open-reservoir scavenger).
