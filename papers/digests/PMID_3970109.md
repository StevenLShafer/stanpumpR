# Kuhnert 1985: meperidine and normeperidine in the mother

## Study
12 labouring women (age 22.6 +/- 4.5 y, range 17-32; 9 nulliparous, 3 multiparous), Table I (PDF p.2, printed 407). Meperidine IV as needed for pain; liver or renal disease and substance abuse excluded; mild complications allowed (3 preeclampsia, 1 over 200 lb, 1 sickle cell, 4 smokers, 1 premature rupture of membranes). Doses: 2.58 +/- 0.79 (2-4) injections, total 79.04 +/- 25.9 mg (50-125); drug-to-delivery interval 303 +/- 190 min (93-702) from the first dose and 150 +/- 110 min (51-401) from the final dose. Delivery: 10 vaginal, 2 cesarean. Weight not given.

Samples: intrapartum plasma at various intervals after injection; postpartum plasma every 24 h for 72 h in 4 mothers. Meperidine extracted by the Mather and Tucker method and measured by GC-MS (plasma, total drug). Semilog plots; half-lives from the terminal disappearance; HP-41C calculator curve fitting. No compartmental model.

Meperidine half-life postpartum could not be computed (levels too low by 24 h); detectable for at least 48 h in 2 mothers. Fig. 1: a typical mother given three 25 mg doses at about hourly intervals; meperidine an hour after doses 2 or 3 was 2-3 times that after dose 1; normeperidine rose steadily. Normeperidine level did not correlate significantly with drug-to-delivery interval.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No effect measured. No MEAC or analgesic concentration, no ke0/t½ke0/tPeak/hysteresis, no equianalgesic or potency ratio. The paper calls normeperidine the active metabolite and discusses fetal and neonatal depression qualitatively; no concentration threshold for toxicity is given. Concentrations are total plasma, GC-MS.

## Covariates
None analysed beyond the ratio against interval; the authors saw no difference in patients with medical complications. No renal or weight term.

## Caveats
Small n (12; 4 for half-life; 11 for delivery levels), young women, variable dosing, half-life measured postpartum only (up to 72 h, so a terminal estimate with few points), no meperidine half-life, no non-pregnant comparator in this paper.
