# Beloeil 2005: norepinephrine kinetics and dynamics in septic shock and trauma

## Study
- Bicetre, France. 23 adults already on norepinephrine via central line within 24 h of starting catecholamines: 12
  septic shock, 11 multiple trauma (ISS > 16); sedated with midazolam and sufentanil at constant rates, mechanically
  ventilated; no other drugs (PDF p2).
- Table 1 (PDF p3, rendered), trauma vs septic: female 5 / 6; age 33 (19-66) vs 69 (53-88) y; weight 69 (11) vs
  69 (11) kg; SAPS II 30 (6) vs 42 (16); creatinine clearance 94 (34) vs 35 (17) mL/min (Cockcroft); lactate 1.8 vs 2.7.
- After 60 min, three steps of +0.1 ug/kg/min at 15-min intervals (MAP rise capped at 20%), then back to the initial
  rate. Arterial samples before and at 30 s, 13 and 15 min of each step; in 9 patients also 30 s to 15 min after the
  reset. Baseline doses 0.093-6.3 (median 1.8) ug/kg/min (PDF p3). HPLC with coulometric detection. Dose basis not
  stated.
- NONMEM V, two-stage (PK then PD on Bayesian PK estimates), FOCE-I, heteroscedastic error; one compartment preferred
  to two by AIC.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
MAP = MAP0 + MAPMAX C^gamma/(EC50^gamma + C^gamma), effect in the central compartment (no ke0). Random effects
significant only on MAP0 and MAPMAX; no covariate explained PD variability. End point: invasive MAP. Not an opioid.

## Covariates
SAPS II on CL (inverse). Not significant: group, sex, body weight, creatinine clearance, prothrombin time; none on V.

## Caveats
- The abstract gives the 0.9 L/min and 6.8 min at SAPS II 60; Table 3 and the Results give 66, and 59 600/60 = 993
  mL/min (derived), so the abstract's 60 does not reproduce the stated values.
- The abstract prints the step as 0.1 "mg" kg-1 min-1 (rendered p1); the Results give baseline doses in ug/kg/min.
- Concentration units are inconsistent: Results print 0.99-186 "mg/ml" (median 16.2), Table 3 EC50 in ug/mL, Table 4
  and Figure 2 in ng/mL.
- Small n; patients already on norepinephrine (no true baseline); sedated, ventilated adults.
