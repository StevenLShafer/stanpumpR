# Hemmings 2020: Further retractions of articles by Joachim Boldt

## Study
Editorial of the British Journal of Anaesthesia (BJA). Not a PK or PD paper; no drug data.

Facts reported:
- Boldt's fraud first appeared in the December 2010 notice of retraction in Anesthesia & Analgesia.
- A state medical association review (Rheinland-Pfalz) examined 74 Boldt papers; 30 described human research done
  without IRB approval.
- A Ludwigshafen hospital review found 16 manuscripts with no evidence the research was done as published.
- A Justus Liebig University Giessen report recommended retracting all papers on which Boldt is the responsible author.
- BJA retracts every Boldt BJA article describing research done at Giessen (Table 1, 9 papers, 1990-1997) and four
  Boldt reviews that knowingly cite the fraudulent work (Table 2: 2000, 2007, 2009, 2010).
- Table 3 lists all of Boldt's BJA papers with status (hereby / previously / not retracted). All but one are
  retracted: the Lehmann 1999 case report on dynamic cardiomyoplasty, where Boldt was one of several authors.
