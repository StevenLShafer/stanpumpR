# Alhadab 2020: sertraline two-compartment MBMA with time-dependent ka and dose-dependent single-dose F

## Study
- Systematic PubMed search (October 2015): 27 studies of healthy adults (>= 18 y), 60 mean concentration-time
  profiles, 748 concentrations (Table I, II): 1 IV profile (100 mg over 12 h), 11 oral multiple-dose
  (steady-state, >= 7 days) profiles, the rest oral single doses; 5-400 mg.
- Study-level means: age median 26 (20-72) y, weight median 67 (59-80) kg; 31 profiles male only, 6 female
  only, others mixed or unreported.
- NCA dose-proportionality (log-power model), deconvolution (WinNonlin), then NONMEM 7.3 ADVAN6 FOCEI with
  inter-study, inter-arm and residual variability; arms weighted by 1/sqrt(n). VPC/NPC; SIR uncertainty.
- First author is a Pfizer employee.

## Model and parameters
Two compartments, linear elimination. F = Fmax x DOSE / (D50 + DOSE) (Eq 5), "only after SD
administration"; Ka = Ka,max x TAD^gamma / (T50^gamma + TAD^gamma) (Eq 6). Multiple dosing has a larger
peripheral volume. Table III (estimate with %RSE; SIR median):

IV-only compartmental fit (deconvolution step): CL 66.43 L/h, Q 82.75 L/h, V2 1011.11 L, V3 955.95 L;
alpha 0.207 /h, beta 0.0275 /h. Terminal t1/2 26.6 h (SD) -> 32.7 h (MD) (Discussion). CL/F quoted as 93 L/h
(derived: 59.7 / 0.639 = 93.4). F(D) values (derived): 0.39 at 25 mg, 0.49 at 50, 0.55 at 100, 0.59 at 200.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None.

## Covariates
Dose (on single-dose F) and dosing regimen (SD/MD on V3). Age, weight, sex, food, formulation, ethnicity:
not found or not testable.

## Caveats
- Mean (study-level) data only; ISV/IAV are not between-patient variability.
- One IV profile anchors absolute F; one profile each for 5 and 25 mg SD and MD.
- No ISV/IAV on Fmax, Ka,max, T50 (estimation failed).
- How F is handled with multiple dosing is not written as an equation; the text says bioavailability is
  dose-dependent only after single doses and constant with dose after multiple doses, and quotes CL/F 93 L/h,
  which equals CL / Fmax.
