# Sier 2017: estradiol PBPK linked to a liver metabolic network

## Study
In-silico study (COPASI v4.14 for PBPK and PBPK-LiverODE; MuFINS for PBPK-GSMN). A 16-tissue plus arterial/venous PBPK model of estradiol in women, extended from Plowchalk and Teeguarden (7 compartments) using Bosgra physiology. Intrinsic liver clearance is replaced in turn by (a) a single Clint ODE, (b) a five-compartment mechanistic liver ODE model (LiverODE), (c) Recon2 genome-scale network (7440 reactions). Virtual population: 1495 NHANES 2013-14 women aged 18-45 (weight, height, BMI, BSA drive organ weights and flows). Validated against Kuhnz (0.3 mg IV bolus) and Lyrenas (2 and 4 mg oral) data from the literature; no new patient data.

## Model and parameters
- Endogenous synthesis in gonads fitted to a steady-state total blood estradiol of 0.15 nM (about 0.003 nM free, about 98% protein bound) (PDF p3).
- Plasma binding: albumin Kd 17 uM and SHBG Kd 1.5 nM in the original model; Sodergard values 23.8 uM and 3.18 nM adopted (PDF p5). Free fraction about 2%.
- IV dose 0.3 mg = 1101 nmol, delivered at 250 /h; oral 2 mg = 7342 nmol and 4 mg = 14685 nmol, absorbed at 0.025 /h with 0.01 /h loss (PDF p6).
- Liver permeability-limited: uptake 1000 L/h, efflux 277.8 L/h (PBPK, PBPK-GSMN); 150 and 41.7 L/h for LiverODE; plasma:tissue coefficient 3.6.

RMSE vs IV data: 3.7, 1.2, 0.9 (PBPK, LiverODE, GSMN). Predicted liver Cmax after IV: 50.3, 7.9, 75.7 nM; gonad:plasma ratio 49.7, 21.3, 28.5 (literature 10-133). No classical compartmental parameters (V, CL) are reported; the main text gives none.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None (no PD end point, no ke0, no MEAC; not an opioid). Drug interaction: phenytoin (40-80 uM, 10-20 ug/mL) via PXR induction lowers oral estradiol AUC to about 56%, 54%, 53% of control at 40 uM (0.5, 1, 2 mg) and 47%, 47%, 46% at 80 uM, matching the roughly 50% reduction in the literature (PDF p11). Repeated 0.5-2 mg/day dosing over 10 days: AUC about 55% (40 uM) and 47% (80 uM) of control.

## Covariates
Body weight, height, BMI, BSA scale organ weights and blood flows (Bosgra); no covariate equations on clearance are given in the main text (in Additional file 1).

## Caveats
Simulation study built from literature parameters; one oral subject in the 4 mg data set looked like a 2 mg responder and the models over-predict 4 mg Cmax and AUC. IV AUC under-predicted by LiverODE and GSMN (6.1 and 5.9 vs 10.2). Table 1 has no units. Mouse data used for organ distribution. Different software (COPASI vs MuFINS) can shift timing. Free estradiol, not total, is plotted in Fig. 3.
