# Bruno 1996: Population PK model for docetaxel

## Study
Prospective sparse-sampling population PK, 547 adults at cycle 1: 26 data-rich phase 1 patients (70-115 mg/m2, 1-2 h infusions, 9-15 samples over 2-24 h) and 521 phase 2 patients (75 mg/m2 n=28; 100 mg/m2 n=493, 1-h infusion; mean 2.8 samples, range 1-5, over 24 h). 1,890 plasma concentrations, HPLC-UV after solid-phase extraction, two labs cross-validated. NONMEM IV, ADVAN5/TRANS1. Index set 280 (26 phase 1 + first 254 phase 2) for building; validation set 267 (last-entered phase 2); final refit on all 547. Table I (p.155): age 56.0 y (SD 10.1; 5-95% 39-71), weight 69.3 kg (SD 15.0), height 168 cm, BSA 1.78 m2, albumin 40.1 g/L, AAG 1.46 g/L (SD 0.55, median 1.34), creatinine 83.5 umol/L, 253 M / 294 F, HEP12 = 1 in 18 patients.

## Model and parameters
Three-compartment, linear, first-order elimination; exponential IIV, proportional residual error. Final model (p.168, confirmed on page image):
CL (L/h) = BSA x (theta1 + theta7 AAG + theta8 AGE + theta9 ALB) x (1 - theta14 HEP12); AAG and ALB in g/L, AGE in years, BSA m2. HEP12 = 1 if (SGOT or SGPT >60 IU) and ALKPH >300 IU.

Index-set model (n=280): theta1 36.8, AAG -4.65, AGE -0.139, HEP12 0.418, V1 7.01, k12 1.10, k21 1.43, k13 1.30, k31 0.0793 (ALB not in it). Basic model CL 38.5 L/h. Validation: bias 8.2% / precision 21% overall (Table III), no better than a naive predictor overall but better in low-CL subgroups (AAG >2.27, CL <20.7, HEP12 = 1, age >69).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None; no effect-site model, no concentration targets, no MEAC. Not an opioid.

## Covariates
BSA (proportional), AAG (negative; -19% at 2.6 g/L), HEP12 (-33%), age (-6.7% at 71 y), albumin (within +/-8%) on CL only (p.168). Weight collinear with BSA (r=0.947), BSA chosen. Preliminary phase 1 model had a larger age effect (15% decrease at 65 y).

## Caveats
Sparse data, Bayesian "true" CL shrunk toward priors; only 18 patients with HEP12 = 1; age and albumin CVs 40-50%; no structural covariates on V or inter-compartmental terms; adult cancer patients, 1-h infusions, total plasma docetaxel. Phase 1 plus phase 2 combined for the volumes.
