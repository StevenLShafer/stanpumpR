# Zamir 2022: systematic review of metoprolol pharmacokinetics

## Study
Systematic review (PRISMA, Cochrane handbook) of PubMed and Google Scholar, searched 10 Oct to 26 Nov 2021; open
access English articles only; 676 hits, 41 studies included (p3-4). Quality by Jadad and CASP. Studies tabulated by
route and population: IV and oral in healthy people (Tables 2, 3), disease (Table 4), drug and food interactions
(Table 5). Units converted to common units by the reviewers. No pooling or modelling.

**Supplement (PMID_35764772_ESM1.docx, read 2026-10-27).** No pharmacokinetic numbers. Table S1: 271 numbered
articles excluded at screening, with the basis (title 230, animal 28, abstract 8, not accessible 5; counted from
the table). The main text (PDF p4) says 314 were screened after 362 duplicates and 275 excluded; the table has
271 rows, 4 fewer (derived; cause not stated). Table S2: Jadad scores of the 41 included studies (0 to 5 of 5;
the IV healthy-volunteer and disease sources score 0-2: Jordo 1980 0, Larsson 1984 0, Regardh 1980 2, Regardh 1981
2, Richard 1994 0). Table S3: CASP scores out of 11 (Jordo 1980 7, Regardh 1980 7, Regardh 1981 8, Richard 1994 5,
Larsson 1984 7). Among the exclusions: Lammers 2017 (Clin Pharmacokinet 56:1231, S1 row 122, excluded on title) and
Lammers 2018 (row 121). Leemann 1993 is in neither the excluded (S1) nor the included (S2) list.
Introduction values (p2): oral F 50%, fu 0.88, Vss 3.2 L/kg, B:P 1.13, Mw 267.36 g/mol. Oral F 0.50 in two healthy
studies (p5). Derived: CL 48 L/h with ke 1.01 /h implies V = 48 L (0.7 L/kg) and t1/2 0.69 h, far from the 3.2 L/kg
and 3-4 h quoted elsewhere, so that ke is not a terminal disposition rate.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None tabulated. Not an opioid.

## Covariates
Narrative only: renal impairment (CL 60 vs 48 L/h), cirrhosis (36.6 vs 48), AMI, CYP2D6*10 (Korean study, Cmax
286 / 546 / 873.5 nmol/L for *1/*1, *1/*10, *10/*10), enantiomers, sex, interactions.

## Caveats
Internal inconsistencies: six IV healthy-volunteer studies in the Results and abstract, seven in the Discussion
(PDF p17, printed 1111); the Jordo 1980 and Regardh 1981 control rows carry identical values (48 L/h, 1416 nmol*h/L, 1.01 /h), cause
not stated. In renal impairment CL rises while ke falls (Table 4), unexplained. The 0.16 mg bolus dose for Larsson
1984 is printed in Tables 1-2 and the text; not checked against the primary paper. Doses are not stated as salt or
base. Restricted to open-access papers.
