# Stamer 2007: tramadol and (+)/(-)-O-desmethyltramadol by number of active CYP2D6 genes

## Study
- 187 patients genotyped, ASA I-III, elective major abdominal surgery, Bonn; 174 with complete concentrations; 177 evaluable for response.
- Groups by active CYP2D6 genes (Table 2): PM (0) n = 18, HZ/IM (1, or two reduced-function alleles such as *10, *41) n = 93, EM (2) n = 68, UM (>= 3) n = 8. Age about 53-59 y, weight 73.6-78.8 kg (means).
- Tramadol 3 mg/kg IV (max 250 mg) infused over 15 min about 90 min before end of anaesthesia, with dipyrone 1 g; loading dose 219-226 mg mean. PACU tramadol 50 mg top-ups (12 patients before third sample); then PCA tramadol 20 mg bolus, lockout 8 min, for 48 h; piritramide rescue.
- Samples at 30, 90, 180 min after infusion. Enantioselective LC-MS/MS (Chiralpak AD). (+)ODT detection limit 2-8 ng/mL (p. 45).
- 128 patients on comedication; 26 on CYP2D6 inhibitors.

Mean (SEM) concentrations, all patients, at 30/90/180 min: (+)ODT 22.1/22.8/30.3 ng/mL; (-)ODT 51.9/52.1/57.0 ng/mL (p. 42; "+/-" rendered as 7 in the text layer). Tramadol enantiomers 55-1100 ng/mL, highest at 30 min. (-)ODT about twice (+)ODT; (-)ODT measurable in PMs, (+)ODT not in *4/*4.
Derived ratios to EM: HZ/IM 0.580, UM 2.251, PM Q3 0.171; inhibitor-free subgroup HZ/IM 0.497, UM 2.255.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End points: rescue piritramide in PACU, 48-h non-response (rescue needed and/or negative rating), cumulative consumption (piritramide:tramadol converted 1:10, Fig. 4).
- PM non-response 81.25% vs no difference among HZ/IM, EM, UM (P < 0.001); 10 PMs needed PACU rescue.
- No MEAC measured. Cites Lehmann et al. (ref 22) for minimum effective serum concentration of racemic tramadol 20.2-986.3 ng/mL, median 287.7 ng/mL (secondary, p. 45). No ke0/tPeak/hysteresis. Potency: piritramide:tramadol 1:10 used as a conversion only.
- (+)ODT named as the enantiomer with highest mu affinity and intrinsic efficacy (citing Gillen 2000).

## Covariates
CYP2D6 active gene count; CYP2D6-inhibiting comedication.

## Caveats
- Only 3 samples within 3 h; AUC is a short early window, not formation clearance or whole exposure.
- Group medians include inhibitor users (lowers EM and IM medians); UM n = 8; misclassification acknowledged.
- PACU top-up doses not corrected for.
- HZ/IM group mixes one-active-gene heterozygotes with *10 and *41 carriers.
