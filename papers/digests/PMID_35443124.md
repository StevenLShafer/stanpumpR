# Shafer 2022: Intrinsic severity of the omicron variant (letter)

## Study
Not a pharmacology paper. A letter to the editor (PDF p1 = journal p1867, PDF p2 = p1868) replying to a Perspective by Bhattacharyya and Hanage (NEJM 2022;386:e14). The letter argues that if omicron arose in a single immunocompromised host, reduced virulence may have been favoured by selection. The authors' reply says alpha and delta were thought to have emerged similarly yet were more virulent than ancestors, omicron's immediate ancestor was not delta, and severity probably has little effect on population-level spread. PDF p2 also holds unrelated NEJM corrections (otitis media tubes trial; gilteritinib AML trial) and the letters-to-the-editor instructions.
