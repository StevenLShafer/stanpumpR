# Danielak 2017: clopidogrel and H4 thiol metabolite population PK with genetic co-factors

## Study
- 63 Caucasian patients (Table 1, PDF p4 / printed 1626) before elective coronarography or PCI, on oral clopidogrel 75 mg daily for at least 7 days; sampling on the procedure day.
- Age 65.4 ± 10.5 y; weight 79.1 ± 14.0 kg (n = 55; 8 missing, imputed by sex mean); BMI 28.40 ± 4.89 (n = 55); 42 male / 21 female; 22 diabetic; 60 on statins; 46 on a PPI (43 pantoprazole).
- Genotypes: CYP2C19*2 carriers 20 (31.7%), *17 carriers 34 (54.0%), CYP3A4*1G 10, ABCB1 3435TT 22. No poor metabolizers.
- Sampling: 17 patients full profile (0.5, 1, 2, 3, 4 h); 46 patients two samples (0.5 and 2 h, or 1 and 3 h). 155 clopidogrel and 158 H4 concentrations; 17 below quantitation (M3 method).
- Assay: HPLC-MS/MS, total plasma concentration, EDTA blood stabilized with bromo-3'-methoxyacetophenone; linear 0.25-5 ng/mL (clopidogrel) and 0.25-50 ng/mL (derivatized H4); H4 isomer only; H4 concentrations adjusted to the mass equivalent of parent.
- NONMEM 7.3, FOCE-I, PsN/Xpose; forward-inclusion (dOFV 3.84) / backward-elimination (6.63); VPC and 1000-set bootstrap.

## Model and parameters
Structure (Fig. 1, PDF p4): depot -> clopidogrel central (V2/F) by first order k12; fraction FM of clopidogrel clearance forms H4 (central, V3/F) irreversibly; H4 exchanges/clears with Q2/F; linear elimination. F fixed at 1 (relative bioavailability); FM constrained to at most 0.20. All parameters are apparent (per F). Table 2 (PDF p5, printed 1627), final model; IIV as %CV.

Bootstrap (Table 2): k12 0.556-0.626; V2/F 7310-8240 L; CL/F 13,250-14,970 L/h; FM 0.044-0.048; V3/F 4.65-5.15 L; Q2/F 232-264 L/h.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD end point modelled (no platelet reactivity, no EC50). No ke0, tPeak, MEAC or potency ratio. Not an opioid. H4 is the only isomer with antiplatelet activity (per the paper's introduction). Observed maxima of total plasma clopidogrel did not exceed 5 ng/mL (0.015 uM), PDF p7 discussion.

## Covariates
CYP2C19*2 carriage lowers FM by 45% (FM = TVFM x (1 - 0.45)). Predicted H4 AUC (PDF p5) 13.58 ± 7.10 vs 8.59 ± 6.48 ng h/mL (non-carriers vs carriers as ordered in the text, p = 0.004); abstract gives medians 12.70 (IQR 7.00-19.39) vs 5.94 (3.92-12.51) ng h/mL; discussion says 36.7% lower. Clopidogrel AUC not different. Sex (k12, V2/F), ABCB1 TT (CL/F) and *17 (FM) were significant in the first forward step but dropped after *2 was included. Age, weight, BMI, obesity, diabetes, PPI, statins, CYP3A4*1G: not retained.

## Caveats
Apparent parameters per F with F fixed at 1 and FM capped at 0.20; the very large V2/F and CL/F (7660 L, 14,500 L/h) reflect extensive first-pass loss to the inactive acid not being modelled. Sparse sampling (46 of 63 patients two samples, none after 4 h); 28-36% of 4 h samples below quantitation; patients with cardiovascular disease on chronic therapy, steady-state dosing not modelled explicitly; large unexplained residual variability; no poor metabolizers; only the H4 isomer, no carboxylic acid metabolite; the AUC comparison is a post-hoc test on empirical Bayes clearances.
