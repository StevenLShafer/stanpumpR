# Vuylsteke 2000: clevidipine PK before and during hypothermic CPB, and pulmonary extraction

## Study
- 20 adults having elective CABG at Papworth were enrolled. 17 were treated and evaluated, because arterial
  pressure did not rise enough in three (Results, p685).
- Table 1 (rendered p3), all patients: 14 M / 3 F, age 62 (44-76) y, height 174 (6) cm, weight 81 (14) kg,
  BMI 27 (4), 11 on β-blockers. The CPB subgroup: n = 8, 6 M / 2 F, weight 75 (16) kg.
- Anaesthesia: morphine/scopolamine premedication; midazolam 0.1 mg/kg, fentanyl 15 µg/kg and pancuronium for
  induction; isoflurane ≥1% end-tidal. CPB at 30°C with α-stat management.
- Dosing: clevidipine 0.5 mg/mL in 20% lipid into the right jugular vein.
  - Started at 0.7 µg/kg/min, adjusted every 2 min (maximum 22) to a target MAP of 70-75 before CPB and 55-60 on
    CPB.
  - Held at a constant rate for about 10 min (actual 10-13 min) and then stopped.
  - There was about 20 min of washout before CPB.
- Samples: radial artery at 8, 9 and 10 min of constant infusion and 0.5-10 min after the stop. Pulmonary artery
  (mixed venous) at 8-10 min. CPB inflow and outflow at 5 and 9 min.
- SDS tubes; GC-MS assay, LLOQ 0.23 µg/L.
- Analysis: population PK in P-PHARMA 1.5 with 1/Cpred² error. Reported values are means of the individual
  Bayesian estimates.

Other results:
- There was no extraction by the CPB circuit: the aortic-venous concentration difference was 1.5 (4.7) and
  -1.4 (3.9) µg/L.
- **Infusion rates needed:** 2.2 (0.9) µg/kg/min before CPB and 1.3 (0.4) on CPB. The concentrations required
  were about the same in both phases.
- During CPB, temperature fell from 34.9 to 30.8°C and haematocrit from 34% to 28%. The authors attribute the
  lower clearance mainly to hypothermia, citing in-vitro half-lives of about 6 min at 37°C and 11 min at 30.5°C.
- HR was 72 (13) bpm before and 75 (17) bpm at the end of the pre-CPB infusion.
- **Arithmetic check (derived):** V1 k12/k21 = 0.06 × 0.168 / 0.226 = 0.045, so Vss ≈ 0.105 L/kg, consistent with
  the printed 0.11.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- No concentration-effect model and no ke0. MAP at the start of treatment was 97 (6) mmHg before CPB and 73 (10)
  on CPB.

## Covariates
- Hypothermic CPB, as a condition comparison only. No covariate model was built.

## Caveats
- About 10 min of infusion with 10 min of post-infusion sampling, so the terminal phase is short.
  Two-compartment estimates (Vss 0.11 L/kg) are smaller than the triexponential healthy-volunteer values.
- Anaesthetised patients on several drugs. n = 8 for the CPB comparison.
- Source slips:
  - The text (p686) says half-lives were longer during CPB "than during CPB"; it means than before CPB.
  - The text gives the pre-CPB half-lives as 0.6 (0.2) and 3.8 (0.2), which are group B's. Group A's are 0.7 and
    3.8.
