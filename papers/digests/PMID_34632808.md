# Tveita 2022: Physiological impact of hypothermia (review)

## Study
Narrative review (Physiology, March 2022). Defines hypothermia, summarises therapeutic hypothermia (cardiac surgery, neuroprotection, neonatal encephalopathy, organ preservation) and accidental hypothermia and rewarming shock, with the authors' animal work on rewarming cardiac dysfunction. No new data; no pharmacokinetics or drug concentrations in the part read.

Hypothermia grades used (1994 scheme, PDF p.2): mild 35-32 C, moderate 32-28, severe 28-20, profound <20 C.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No opioid or other drug data in the part read, so no MEAC, ke0, tPeak or potency ratio. Clinical statements only: spontaneous cardiac arrest around 27 C core; unconsciousness reported below about 33 C (text layer, printed p.71 and p.76).

## Covariates
Temperature only: Q10 scaling of metabolic rate; solubility of CO2 and O2 rises on cooling.

## Caveats
Narrative review; the Q10 values are averages quoted from other papers, not measured here. The whole-body 2.0 is "approximately" and rests on one citation (ref 7) whose title concerns cerebral preservation.
