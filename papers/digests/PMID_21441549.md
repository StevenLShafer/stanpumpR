# Lipszyc 2011: remifentanil effect-site TCI-PCA vs morphine PCA after uterine artery embolization

## Study
- Double-blind randomized trial, 19 women ASA I-II having uterine artery embolization (UAE) under local anaesthesia; 10 remifentanil, 9 morphine; one morphine patient lost to computer failure and excluded (per-protocol). Randomized in blocks of four (p. 2, printed 725; Results p. 3, printed 726).
- Morphine arm: PCA pump, 2 mg bolus, 10 min lockout, maximum 30 mg per 4 h; solution 2 mg/mL (text prints "2 mg ml21").
- Remifentanil arm: effect-site TCI (home-made TOOLBOX software, Shafer-Gregg algorithms, Minto 1997 PK/PD, reference 15). Each demand raised the target Ce 0.5 ng/mL while Ce < 2.5 ng/mL, 0.2 ng/mL above that; no lockout beyond reaching the previous target; no demand for 30 min lowered the target by 0.2 ng/mL. Concentration 2 mg/mL ("2 mg ml21"; unit as printed, probably a loss of the micro sign).
- Pain NRS 0-10 at PACU arrival, every 15 min for 2 h, then 4, 8, 12, 16 h. Safety by capnography (nasal prongs), transcutaneous SpO2, heart rate, ventilatory frequency every 5 s. Rescue: paracetamol 1 g and diclofenac 75 mg.
- Analysis: MANOVA for repeated measures; probit analysis of Ce against "satisfactory analgesia" and against E'CO2 >= 45 mm Hg (remifentanil group only).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point for the concentration-response: "satisfactory analgesia" defined behaviourally, as the automatic 30-min-no-demand decrease of the target Ce; "unsatisfactory" is a PCA demand. Not an NRS threshold. Remifentanil Ce (Minto effect site), population 10 healthy women, no assay (Ce is simulated, no blood samples).
- This is a PCA-demand ED95/ED80, **not a MEAC**: no minimum effective analgesic concentration is stated. Nearest values: ED95 2.98 ng/mL, ED80 2.16 ng/mL; mean Ce held 2-3 ng/mL (CI 1.5-3.5, Fig 2, p. 4).
- No ke0, t1/2 ke0, tPeak or hysteresis is reported; the Minto ke0 is implicit and the paper does not state it.
- Pain: NRS lower with remifentanil from 15 min to 2 h (Fig 1: morphine mean about 6.3 at 15 min, 8.1 at 1 h; remifentanil about 3.1 and 2.3, read from the bars). Difference 95% CI at 1 h -7.99 to -3.60 (p. 4). NRS < 4 within 15 min with remifentanil vs 240 min with morphine (Discussion, printed p. 728). Identical after 4 h.
- Respiratory: ventilatory frequency < 8 bpm 3.76% vs 0.91%; E'CO2 > 50 mm Hg 1.3% in both; SpO2 < 90% 0.76% vs 0.96% (Table 2 text layer, see open question). HR 35-50 bpm 2.12% vs 0.11%.
- Potency ratio remifentanil:morphine: none stated.

## Covariates
None analysed.

## Caveats
n = 19 (authors' major limitation); per-protocol analysis with one exclusion; Ce is simulated by the Minto model rather than measured; young healthy women, no co-medication; remifentanil arm had longer procedures (103.2 vs 55.5 min, P=0.1); the authors suggest upper/lower Ce limits be added to the algorithm. Morphine arm had no loading dose. The morphine PCA is described by dose only (2 mg), with no morphine concentration data.
