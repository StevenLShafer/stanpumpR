# Bogason 2011: leukaemic cell burden and daunorubicin plasma PK in AML

## Study
- 40 adults with AML (FAB classified; 33 de novo, 7 secondary), Karolinska, Stockholm, **Sweden**. Mean age 61.3 y (range
  33-83); 19 M : 21 F. Weight and BSA not reported. Baseline WBC mean 39 x10^6 cells/mL blood, range 1-219 (Methods, PDF p2,
  printed 515). (derived from Table 1 on the page image: mean 40.7, median 32.25.)
- Induction: cytarabine 200 mg/m2 2-h infusion days 1-7, then daunorubicin (DNR) 60 mg/m2 as a 1-h infusion days 1-3
  immediately after cytarabine. Table 1 (PDF p3, printed 516, page image): 37 patients 60 mg/m2, one each 45, 30 and 54 mg/m2.
- Sampling: venous heparinised blood at 1 h (end of infusion), 5 h and 24 h after the first DNR infusion; plasma and
  mononuclear cells (Lymphoprep). Intracellular DNR in 24/40.
- Assay: HPLC with fluorescence (ex 485, em 560 nm), idarubicin internal standard; DNR and daunorubicinol (DOL);
  LOD 5x10^-3 uM, quantification range 0.03-20 uM, CV <7% DNR, <13% DOL. Plasma, total drug.
- Analysis: NONMEM 7 FOCE, log-transformed concentrations, proportional residual error, PsN/Xpose, VPC with 500 simulated
  sets. Started from Callies 2004 (3-compartment DNR+DOL model); applied without re-estimation it predicted too rapid a
  fall (Fig 4); refit to DNR only and reduced to two compartments for data sparsity.
- Observed at 1 h: DNR 403.8 +/- 349.2 nM (range 60-1370); DOL 181.1 +/- 162.6 nM (range 0-670) (Results, PDF p4,
  printed 517). Inverse DNR-vs-WBC correlation at 1 h r2 = 0.11, P<0.05; at 5 h r2 = 0.05 (P=0.22); 24 h r2 <0.01 (P=0.9).
  No WBC relation with DOL or with intracellular DNR (n=24, r2 = 0.02).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD model, no ke0/t1/2ke0/tPeak, no effect site. No therapeutic or toxic concentration. Complete remission
(<5% marrow blasts) did not differ in mean WBC or mean plasma DNR/DOL (data not shown). Not an opioid paper; no MEAC or
potency ratio.

## Covariates
Tested stepwise on each parameter: baseline WBC, age, gender, body weight, BSA. Only WBC on Vc retained (linear,
centred at 39). No demographic covariate was significant. Gender would have entered as a fractional change for women.

## Caveats
- Sparse sampling: only 1, 5 and 24 h after the first dose; nothing between 1 and 5 h, so Q and Vp are weakly informed.
- Very high IIV on Vc (127%); CL-V correlation 70%.
- WBC effect is modest and uncertain (RSE 47%; no visible VPC improvement, Fig 5B).
- Centring value 39 is called the median in the Table 2 footnote and the mean in the text; from Table 1 the mean is
  40.7 and the median 32.25 (derived). Irrelevant to the basic model.
- Comparator: CL "about 46% higher" and Vss "33% lower" were against Callies 2004 (10-min infusion, different
  population), not any other reference.
- Dose basis (salt vs base) and molar mass not stated; weight/BSA not reported.
- First-dose data only, given right after cytarabine.
