# Neckebroek 2019: propofol-to-BIS ICU sedation, TCI vs Bayesian vs EPSAC

## Study
Observational open-label pilot, post-off-pump CABG ICU sedation (June 2008 - Sept 2009), adults < 75 y, EF >= 40%, no severe renal/hepatic failure. Propofol titrated to BIS 40-60 (aim ~50) by one of three controllers on the RUGLOOP II system: HUMAN (clinician-titrated effect-site TCI), BAYES (Bayesian-adapted sigmoid Emax controller), EPSAC (model-predictive control with nominal PD). Remifentanil was a TCI for supplemental analgesia, targeted by the clinician (HUMAN) or by the closed-loop system. n = 14 EPSAC (2 of 16 excluded for recording faults), 10 BAYES, 10 HUMAN (Table 1, p. 679). Weight 80 +/- 13, 80 +/- 13, 87 +/- 13 kg; age 62-66 y. Not randomised. Case time about 339-370 min.

## Model and parameters
CePROP and CeREMI were computed with the Schnider propofol and Minto remifentanil PK models (p. 676; refs 31-34). No PK parameters are given in the paper. The controller's nominal PD model (p. 677, Eq. 1): BIS = E0 - Emax * Ce^gamma / (Ce^gamma + C50^gamma); nominal C50 = 2.5 ug/mL, gamma = 3.01, E0 = Emax = 100. Safety cap CePROP 15 ug/mL.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: BIS (40-60 target). Median delivered concentrations (Table 2, p. 680, median (range)): CePROP 1.8 (1.0-3.1) EPSAC, 1.8 (1.1-2.7) BAYES, 2.6 (1.9-3.0) HUMAN ug/mL; CeREMI 1.1 (0-3.0), 0.9 (0-1.3), 0.6 (0-1.5) ng/mL. Median BIS 42-43. Time with BIS 40-60 about 68-75% (Table 4). No ke0, t1/2 ke0 or tPeak is reported, no MEAC (not an opioid-analgesia study; remifentanil only as co-medication), and no potency ratio. Propofol-remifentanil synergy is mentioned qualitatively only.

## Covariates
None analysed.

## Caveats
Small pilot, not blinded or randomised, no power analysis. Concentrations are model-predicted (Schnider, Minto), not measured. Authors disclose patent interest in the Bayesian method.
