# Maurer 2017: MCC/IMS calibration for exhaled propofol

## Study
Bench analytical validation, no patients. A pre-market multi-capillary-column ion-mobility spectrometer (B&S Analytik; OV5 MCC, 12 cm, 90 C, propofol retention 23 s; 1 sample per minute, 20 s collection) was fed gas from a HovaCAL 4836-VOC generator (propofol from a 90 ug/mL aqueous stock, 100% humidity at 37 C, 850 mL/min). Calibration points 5, 10, 20, 30, 40, 50, 60 ppbv, 30 min each, middle 20 of 30 values used. Signal is in volts.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. Not an opioid. No ke0, tPeak, EC50 or MEAC. The paper cites a clinically relevant exhaled range of 0-39 ppbv from other studies and notes one small study (3 patients) reporting 0.0043-0.0335 ppbv. Relation of breath to plasma concentration is not studied here.

## Covariates
None.

## Caveats
Gas-phase generator calibration only, no clinical breath samples. Signal saturates above about 0.5 V, so the device cannot separate concentrations in the upper clinical range. Authors disclose manufacturer funding and equipment loan.
