# Ahmed 2015: topiramate IV + oral population PK and COWA phonemic fluency

## Study
Pooled data from three randomised crossover studies in healthy native-English-speaking adults (Table 1): 32
subjects (20 M / 12 F; 24 Caucasian, 5 AA), weight 76.54 +/- 14.21 kg, age 30.81 +/- 11.66 y (median 26.5,
19-55). Study I (UMN, n = 12): 50 mg (n = 2) or 100 mg (n = 10) stable-labelled IV topiramate over 15 min and oral
tablets, crossover; rich sampling 0-120 h (this is the Clark 2013 cohort). Studies II (UF, n = 11) and III (UMN,
n = 9): single 100 mg oral dose vs placebo (III also lorazepam 2 mg, excluded), one sample after the test.
LC-MS, LOQ 0.04 ug/mL. NONMEM 7, FOCE/Laplace with interaction. 155 IV and 159 oral PK observations; 179 COWA scores.

## Model and parameters
Two-compartment, first-order absorption and elimination, IV and oral fitted together; allometric on actual body
weight (exponents fixed 0.75 / 1); diagonal omega; IIV on Q, Vp and F fixed to 0; separate proportional error per
route. Table 2 (fixed effects, RSE%; IIV %CV):

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: Controlled Oral Word Association (COWA) phonemic fluency, words in three 60-s trials, direct
(no effect compartment) link to predicted plasma concentration. Final model (eq 6, Table 3, continuous data):
COWA = BL x theta(NCOWA>=4) x exp(eta) x exp(-KE x C) + eps.
- BL 42.5 words (RSE 3.48%); KE 0.157 L/mg (RSE 6.97%), i.e. 14.5% fall per 1 mg/L; theta(NCOWA >= 4) 1.12
  (practice effect, about 12% rise); IIV of BL 17%; residual variance 7.1. Poisson model nearly identical
  (BL 42.4, KE 0.163, 1.13).
- Emax model could not estimate IC50 over the narrow 0.05-3 mg/L range. Predicted 27% fall in COWA at the mean
  Cmax of about 2 mg/L after 100 mg.
- No ke0 estimated; authors suggest fast equilibration given immediate effects. Not an opioid paper.

## Covariates
Weight (allometric, fixed exponents) on CL, Q, Vc, Vp. Age, sex, race, formulation not significant on CL.
For COWA: number of tests (>= 4) on baseline; age, sex, race and treatment sequence not significant.

## Caveats
Single low doses (50-100 mg), narrow concentration range; only 12 subjects had rich PK; healthy young adults with
normal renal function; F estimated unconstrained (108%).
