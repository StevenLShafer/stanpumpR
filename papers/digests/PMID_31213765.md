# Lee 2019: population PK of tramadol and M1 with CYP2D6*10

## Study
- 23 healthy Korean men, age 24.8 +/- 4.8 y (20-40), weight 71.6 +/- 8.9 kg (57-90), height 177 cm, BMI 23.0 (Table 1).
- Open label, tramadol sustained-release (Tridol SR) 100 mg orally every 12 h, 5 doses. Blood at 0, 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2, 2.5, 3, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 24, 48, 72 h after the last dose.
- LC-MS/MS; calibration 1-1000 ng/mL (tramadol) and 1-500 ng/mL (M1); accuracy 94.8-109.8% and 89.8-107.5%. Racemic (not enantioselective).
- Genotypes: *wt/*wt 14 (60.9%), *10/*10 8 (34.8%), *5/*5 1 (4.3%, excluded from modelling). 328 tramadol and 323 M1 observations from 22 subjects.
- NONMEM 7.3, ADVAN6, FOCE-I; 1000-replicate bootstrap and VPC.

## Model and parameters
One compartment for tramadol with combined first-order and zero-order absorption (fraction first order Fr, duration D2, lag ALAG2) and a one-compartment metabolite as an extension, first-order elimination (Fig. 2). A two-compartment parent fitted slightly better overall but not within genotype groups, so one compartment was kept.
Covariate equations (p. 1757): CL/F = 16.9 x (1 - 0.351 x G), CLPM/F = 4.11 x (1 - 0.528 x G), where G = 1 for CYP2D6*10/*10. ka also influenced by *10/*10 per the text (p. 1757), although no separate ka covariate estimate is tabulated.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- No PD measured; no ke0, tPeak or hysteresis.
- Introduction, p. 1752, citing reference 8 (Grond S, Meuser T, Uragg H, Stahlberg HJ, Lehmann KA. Serum concentrations of tramadol enantiomers during patient-controlled analgesia. Br J Clin Pharmacol 1999;48:254-257): the minimum effective concentration of M1 is 84 ug/L. No definition, population, analyte (total vs free, racemic vs enantiomer) or assay is given in this paper; it is a one-line secondary citation.
- Introduction also: M1 binds mu receptors with an estimated 300-fold higher affinity than the parent (secondary); 20-30% of an oral dose undergoes first-pass metabolism, bioavailability 68-84%, elimination half-life about 5-6 h, 90% urinary elimination (secondary, refs 1 and 3).

## Covariates
CYP2D6*10/*10 on CL/F and CLPM/F (and ka per text). Age, weight, height and BMI tested, none retained.

## Caveats
- Only 22 modelled subjects, all young Korean men; only two genotype groups (*wt/*wt vs *10/*10); the single *5/*5 subject excluded.
- Sustained-release product, so ka, D2, lag and Fr are formulation specific and do not transfer to immediate-release tramadol.
- Apparent parameters (all /F); formation clearance and metabolite clearance identified only as a ratio.
- Large %RSE on several estimates (Vp/F 42%, Vm/F 67%, CLM/F 66%, CL covariate 81%).
- Racemic assay; authors note the enantiomers and OCT1 polymorphism are not addressed.
