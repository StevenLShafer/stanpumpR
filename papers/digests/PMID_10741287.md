# Chatelut 2000: limited sampling (1 h + 4 h) Bayesian estimate of carboplatin AUC

## Study
Two parts (p 264-265). (1) Bayesian limited-sampling method: NONMEM IV level 1.1 POSTHOC, PREDPP ADVAN3 TRANS4,
built on a database of 103 adults (M/F 68/35; mean (range) age 59 (23-84) y, weight 69 (40-112) kg, BSA 1.77
(1.33-2.24) m², serum creatinine 117 (55-353) µM, dose 451 (150-975) mg; about 9 samples each) after a 1-h
infusion. Single samples at 0, 0.5, 1 and 4 h after the end of infusion compared in a 15-patient screening group;
1 h and 1 h + 4 h tested prospectively in a 13-patient validation group. Reference CL by trapezoidal AUC
extrapolated to infinity (SIPHAR). (2) Clinical application: 5 adult men with metastatic germ cell tumours on
CARBOPEC high-dose chemotherapy (etoposide 450 mg/m²/day, cyclophosphamide 1600 mg/m²/day and carboplatin, 4 days),
targeting total AUC 20 mg/mL×min (5 per day). Assay: plasma ultrafiltrate (Amicon MPS1, 4 °C), flameless atomic
absorption (ultrafilterable platinum) (p 266).

## Model and parameters
Two-compartment, first-order elimination; proportional residual and interindividual error. Typical central and
peripheral volumes proportional to BSA; typical CL (p 265, confirmed on page image):
CL (mL/min) = θ1·weight + θ2·weight·(1 − θ3·age)·(1 − θ4·sex)/serum creatinine (weight kg, age y, creatinine µM,
sex 0 male / 1 female). The fitted θ values and volumes of this NONMEM prior are **not reported**. The Chatelut 1995
coefficients used for initial dosing are printed on p 266: CL = 0.134·weight + 218·weight·(1 − 0.00457·age)·
(1 − 0.314·sex)/creatinine (µM). Covariates reduce IIV of CL from 37% to 16% (p 268, citing ref 4).

Table 1 (screening, n=15, p 266): reference CL 90 (SD 35) mL/min (range 45-149); bias me% (95% CI) and precision
rmse% by sampling time: end of infusion −10.1 (−17.9; −2.2), 16.9; 0.5 h −0.5 (−8.9; 7.8), 14.6; 1 h −0.4 (−4.6;
3.9), 7.4; 4 h −4.3 (−10.1; 1.5), 11.0. Table 2 (validation, n=13): 1 h alone bias −2.0 (−11.1; 9.1), rmse 14.6,
range −26 to +20%; 1 h + 4 h bias −4.5 (−9.4; 0.3), rmse 9.0 (2.3; 12.4), range −21 to +6%, r 0.96.

Day-3/4 dose rule (p 266): [20 − (AUC day 1 + AUC day 2)] × observed CL at day 2 × 0.5. Table 3 (p 267) gives
per-patient weight, BSA, Cockcroft-Gault CLcr, daily doses and AUCs; totals 21.9, (20), 19.5, (19.5), 20.0
mg/mL×min. Patient 1 CL fell from 127 to 82 mL/min and patient 5 from 96 to 80 mL/min over 4 days (p 268).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Target exposure only: total AUC 20 mg/mL×min over 4 days (daily 5). No concentration-effect model; pharmacodynamic
links (thrombocytopenia, response) cited from other work. Not an opioid paper: no MEAC, ke0 or potency ratio.

## Covariates
Weight, age, sex and serum creatinine on CL; BSA on V1 and V2 (equation above).

## Caveats
Small validation (13) and application (5 men) groups; the clinical arm had co-administered etoposide,
cyclophosphamide and in 3 patients amifostine. The prior's fitted parameters are not given, so the model cannot be
reproduced from this paper. The rmse formula is printed with (pej) where (pej)² is meant (p 265). Both the 1995
formula and Calvert with Cockcroft-Gault overpredicted dose relative to monitoring (133-161% and 101-135% of the
administered dose, p 268).
