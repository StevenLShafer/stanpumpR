# Henningsson 2001: mechanism-based PK model for paclitaxel (Cremophor EL binding)

## Study
- 26 cancer patients (23 female, 3 male; 50 courses), age 32-69 y (median 52), BSA median 1.62 m2
  (1.33-2), haematocrit median 36% (24-48) (Table 1, PDF p 3).
- Paclitaxel (Taxol, in Cremophor EL [CrEL]) IV: 3-h infusions 135-225 mg/m2 in 20 patients (41 courses),
  24-h infusions 135 and 175 mg/m2 in 6 patients (9 courses); 5 patients also had doxorubicin, 2 cisplatin
  (pp 4066).
- Unbound (equilibrium dialysis, [3H]-paclitaxel) in 7 patients / 19 courses (3-h only); whole blood in the same
  plus one course; total plasma in all; CrEL (Coomassie dye-binding) in all.
- Assay: HPLC-UV 230 nm, LOQ 10 ng/mL (0.0117 umol/L); free-fraction LOQ 1%.
- NONMEM V/VI, FOCE with interaction; IIV and IOV log-normal. Neutropenia PK/PD in 19 patients (29 courses)
  after excluding doxorubicin courses.

## Model and parameters
Three-compartment linear model for **unbound** paclitaxel; total plasma = unbound + CrEL-proportional binding +
linear + saturable plasma-protein binding (eq 5); blood cells: linear + saturable binding, Cb = H·Cbc + (1−H)·Cp
(eqs 1, 6). Parameters from Table 2 (PDF p 4, rendered page); clearances and volumes refer to unbound drug, in
absolute units (not per kg or m2).

Residual error (Table 2): Cu additive 0.000697 umol/L, proportional 22.4%; Cp additive 0.00296 umol/L,
proportional 18.5%; Cb proportional 17.5%. Mean unbound half-lives 0.21 h (0.073-0.34), 1.4 h (1.2-2.1),
7.0 h (3-20) (p 4069). No dose dependence of unbound CL or volumes. Predicted plasma protein binding without
CrEL 85% (p 4070). Conversion implied by the assay LOQ: 10 ng/mL = 0.0117 umol/L (derived: MW ≈ 854 g/mol).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: neutrophil survival fraction at nadir (no anaesthetic end point; not an opioid). Table 3 (PDF p 8):
- Total plasma, threshold model (best; ΔOFV 8.3 vs AUC model): threshold EC50 0.197 umol/L (RSE 5.3%;
  derived ≈ 168 ng/mL), time above threshold for half-maximal effect AUCE50 14.4 h (RSE 18%), β fixed 20.
- Total plasma, general model: EC50 0.236 umol/L, AUCE50 12.7 h, β 4.11; AUC model AUCE50 18.9 umol·h/L.
- Unbound: general EC50 0.0241 umol/L, AUCE50 8.67 h, β 3; threshold EC50 0.0164 umol/L, AUCE50 11.3 h;
  AUC model AUCE50 0.922 umol·h/L; the three models not distinguishable.
- Eobs,max fixed 1, γ fixed 1; IIV in AUCE50 58-90%. No ke0, MEAC or potency ratio.

## Covariates
Haematocrit (individual values) in the blood model; baseline ANC raised AUCE50 by 7-15% per 10^9/L (p 4071).

## Caveats
- Small n; unbound data only from 3-h infusions, so unbound nonlinearity cannot be excluded (authors).
- Bmax,p implausibly low and Km values poorly estimated (authors, p 4070).
- Table 1 liver-enzyme units printed as µkat/L with values (e.g. AST 18.5) more typical of U/L — likely a
  label error in the table; not used here.
- The CrEL model used for PK/PD was unpublished at the time.
