# Holze 2023: oral psilocybin PK and PK-PD at fixed 15, 25 and 30 mg doses

## Study
- Pooled analysis of two double-blind, placebo-controlled crossover phase I studies in healthy volunteers (Basel), PDF p2-3 (printed 823):
  - Study 1 (Becker 2022): 25 mg psilocybin after placebo pretreatment (escitalopram arm excluded); 23 participants (12 M, 11 F), age 34 ± 10 y (25-55), weight 70 ± 14 kg (50-112), BMI 24 ± 3.5, GFR 110 ± 13 mL/min/1.73 m². Sampling 0.25-7 h.
  - Study 2 (Holze 2022): 15 and 30 mg psilocybin (plus LSD and placebo arms, not analysed here); 28 participants (14 M, 14 F), age 35 ± 9.4 y (25-52), weight 72 ± 12 kg (55-104), BMI 23 ± 2.7, GFR 109 ± 14. Sampling 0.25-24 h, with urine at 0-8, 8-16 and 16-24 h.
- Drug: synthetic psilocybin in 5 mg capsules (psilocybin dihydrate), assayed at 4.61 ± 0.09 mg per capsule (n = 10). Given after a standardised breakfast (two croissants, ~30 min before).
- Assay: validated UHPLC-MS/MS for **unconjugated (free) psilocin** and 4-HIAA in lithium-heparin plasma; psilocin glucuronide by difference after E. coli β-glucuronidase treatment.
- Model: Phoenix WinNonlin 8.3, individual (not population) fits. One-compartment model, first-order absorption, first-order elimination, lag time; 1/y weighting when sampled to 24 h. A two-compartment model did not improve the fit and had higher AIC in all subjects (PDF p4, printed 825). One 25 mg subject could not be modelled, so n = 22 at 25 mg.
- Dose basis of the fit: not stated in words. Table 2 footnote (PDF p6, printed 827): 15 mg nominal = 13.8 mg assayed psilocybin = 9.9 mg psilocin (48.7 µmol); 30 mg nominal = 27.7 mg = 19.9 mg psilocin (97.3 µmol). Psilocin MW given as 204.3 (PDF p5, printed 826).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- Not an opioid; no MEAC, analgesic concentration or potency ratio.
- PK-PD: the individual PK predictions drove an effect-compartment (first-order equilibration constant) link with a sigmoid Emax model (EC50, Emax, γ; Emax bounded 0-100%), for VAS "any drug effect", "good drug effect", "bad drug effect" and "ego dissolution" (PDF p4). **The fitted values (ke0, EC50, Emax, γ) are in supplement Table S3 and are not in the article.** The text says only that EC50s were similar across effects and Emax for "bad drug effect" was 3-5 times lower (PDF p7).
- Table 3 (PDF p9, printed 830), model-predicted "any drug effect" (mean ± SD), 15 / 25 / 30 mg: onset 0.8 / 0.7 / 0.7 h; time to maximal effect 2.1 ± 0.5 / 1.9 ± 0.5 / 2.2 ± 0.7 h; offset 6.3 / 6.2 / 7.1 h; duration 5.6 ± 2.2 / 5.5 ± 1.6 / 6.4 ± 2.2 h; maximal effect 58 ± 25 / 76 ± 22 / 79 ± 19 %. (The abstract gives 73% and 80% for the two higher doses; Table 3 gives 76% and 79%.)
- Effect Tmax (~2 h) sits on plasma Tmax (2.0 h), and the authors report no sign of acute tolerance (PDF p8). No hysteresis value is given in the article.

## Covariates
None in the model. Cmax, AUC and Emax did not correlate significantly with body weight, BMI, GFR or age (Pearson; PDF p5). The authors read this as evidence against weight-adjusted dosing in the range studied (50-112 kg).

## Caveats
- Individual fits summarised as geometric means, not a population model; the V/F and CL/F means are separate summaries, so their ratio only approximately reproduces the mean t½.
- The 25 mg arm comes from a different study, sampled only to 7 h with no urine, so its λz and t½ (1.4 h) rest on a shorter window (authors' limitation, PDF p9).
- Apparent parameters (F unknown); healthy, fed volunteers; capsule of assayed content.
- Comparative claim: the authors call their half-lives shorter than the previously reported 3-5 h (Hasler 1997, Brown 2017, Dahmane 2021, PDF p7). That comparator is other studies with weight-based dosing and, possibly, total rather than free psilocin, not any model in stanpumpR.
