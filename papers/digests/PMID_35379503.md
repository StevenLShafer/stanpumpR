# Heidenreich 2022: AHA/ACC/HFSA heart failure guideline (digoxin passages)

## Study
A 161-page clinical practice guideline (not PK). It gives recommendations with class of recommendation (COR) and level of evidence (LOE) for heart failure. Only the digoxin passages were read.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- Digoxin (section 7.3.9.2, p. e316-e317): recommendation COR 2b, LOE B-R. In symptomatic HFrEF despite GDMT, or when GDMT is not tolerated, digoxin might be considered to decrease HF hospitalisations. The DIG trial (the only large RCT) showed no mortality effect and a modest reduction in the combined death-or-hospitalisation risk.
- Dosing text: usually 0.125-0.25 mg daily; 0.125 mg daily or every other day if age >70, impaired renal function or low lean body mass. 0.375-0.50 mg daily is rarely needed in HF.
- No ke0, EC50 or MEAC reported.

## Covariates
Dose reduction for age >70, renal impairment, low lean body mass (qualitative).

## Caveats
Guideline, not a PK source. The concentrations come from retrospective analyses of trial data, and the text read does not state a sampling time. Adult HF only; there is nothing for children or IV dosing.
