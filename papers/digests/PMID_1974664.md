# Reves 1990: bolus esmolol for tachycardia before bypass

## Study
- 45 patients for myocardial revascularisation, treated for tachycardia with or without hypertension before
  cardiopulmonary bypass. Entry: SBP > 140 and HR > 70, or HR > 80 bpm. All took their last usual beta-blocker dose the
  night before surgery (none that morning). Premedication diazepam 0.15 mg/kg PO, scopolamine, morphine 0.1 mg/kg IM;
  induction midazolam 0.2 mg/kg, vecuronium 0.1 mg/kg, enflurane 0.5-1.5% in oxygen (PDF p2).
- Phase I (15 patients, two dosed twice; Table II, PDF p2): bolus over 15 s, infusion started within 2 min:
  20 mg + 6 or 12 mg/min (0/4 effective), 40 + 6 (2/4), 40 + 12 (3/4), 80 + 12 (4/5). Effective = HR or SBP down 15%
  within 3 min.
- Phase II (30 patients, double-blind): esmolol 80 mg + 12 mg/min (n = 16) or vehicle (n = 14). Table III: esmolol
  group 14 M / 2 F, 57 +/- 9 y, 80 +/- 16 kg, EF 55 +/- 12%.
- Haemodynamics at baseline, 1, 3, 5, 10 min; HR and BP recorded every 15 s for 5 min, then every minute for 15 min.
  Plasma norepinephrine at baseline, 1 and 10 min (HPLC). **No esmolol concentrations.**

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- Phase I (PDF p3): maximum HR effect in all groups within 2 min; maximum SBP effect within 1.5 min, a 20% reduction
  (Fig. 1).
- Phase II (PDF p4): HR fell 9% within 1 min with esmolol, unchanged with placebo at 1 min. The paper states that the
  peak effect of 80 mg came at 1.6 min, a 14% fall in HR and 11% in SBP; how the 1.6 min was derived (from the 15-s
  readings, individual or mean) is not said.
- Table IV (PDF p5), esmolol group mean HR: 78 +/- 11 baseline, 70 at 1 min, 72 at 3, 69 at 5, 71 at 10 min; placebo
  89, 89, 82, 81, 79 (placebo HR also fell, which the authors tie to falling norepinephrine). Fig. 3 (PDF p4): the
  largest mean percent fall in the esmolol group is at 5 min (about -11%, figure reading), then 1 min (about -9%).
  SBP, PAD, RAP, CO and SVR did not change significantly in phase II.
- Norepinephrine unchanged with esmolol (549 +/- 417 pg/mL baseline), fell with placebo (841 to 615).
- Dose about 1 mg/kg and 150 mcg/kg/min (stated equivalents). No ke0, EC50 or concentration-effect relation.

## Covariates
None.

## Caveats
Small groups; baseline HR differed between arms; HR fell with placebo; peak time reported once without method; no
drug levels; patients chronically beta-blocked but without a morning dose.
