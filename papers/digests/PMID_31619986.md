# Ali 2019: propensity score methods in health technology assessment (not an enalapril paper)

**Source file problem.** stanpumpR cited "Faisal 2019 (Front Pharmacol 10:973), doi 10.3389/fphar.2019.00973"
for its enalapril/enalaprilat serum-and-urine model. That DOI resolves to this paper, a methods review on
propensity scores (PDF p.1, rendered: Frontiers in Pharmacology, published 18 September 2019, Volume 10, Article
973). It contains no pharmacokinetics and no enalapril. The enalapril model is Faisal M, Cawello W, Burckhardt BB,
de Hoon J, Laer S, Front Pediatr 2019;7:281, doi 10.3389/fped.2019.00281 (PMID 31338356), which this PDF is not.
Nothing in this card is verified from it about enalapril.

## Study
Narrative review (19 pages) of propensity score methods for observational comparative-effectiveness and safety
research in HTA: the potential-outcomes framework, estimation of the score, covariate balance diagnostics,
matching, stratification, inverse-probability weighting and covariate adjustment, extended applications (e.g.
multiple treatments, high-dimensional scores), limitations, and reporting.
