# Nord 2011: striatal D2 occupancy after quetiapine IR vs XR (PET, healthy men)

## Study
- Open-label, one-sequence crossover; AstraZeneca/Karolinska; NCT00832221 (p1358, p1365).
- 11 healthy men, 21-29 y (25 ± 2.5); 8 completed every PET. One withdrew before the 4th PET (panic/anxiety, so no IR data); one XR-trough PET lost (radioligand synthesis failure); one IR-peak PET delayed 3.5 h and excluded (p1360).
- Dosing: quetiapine XR once daily, titrated 50 → 300 mg over 4 d, then 300 mg/d XR for 4 d, then switched directly to 300 mg/d IR once daily for 4 d (morning) (p1358).
- PET: [11C]raclopride, HRRT; baseline, at expected Cmax (2 h after IR, 5 h after XR, day 4 of each) and at trough (next morning). Putamen ROI, cerebellum reference, SRTM; occupancy = (BPbaseline − BPdrug)/BPbaseline × 100 (p1358-1359).
- Plasma quetiapine and norquetiapine by LC-MS/MS, LLOQ < 0.70 ng/mL (< 1.8 nmol/L) (p1359).

## Model and parameters
Direct (no delay) hyperbolic relation of occupancy to plasma concentration (Eq 2, p1359): Occ = Occmax · Cp/(Ki,app + Cp), Occmax fixed at 100%, nonlinear least squares over all PET measurements.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: striatal (putamen) D2 occupancy by [11C]raclopride PET.
- Peak occupancy: IR 50 ± 4% (range 44-59%, n = 9); XR 32 ± 11% (13-45%, n = 11); P = 0.0003. Trough: IR 7 ± 7% (−3 to 17%), XR 8 ± 6% (−1 to 18%); NS (Fig 3).
- Derived by the authors with Eq 2: 50% occupancy at about 638 mg/d XR; 400-800 mg XR → 39-56%; 65% at peak would need 1185 mg XR; 80% (EPS threshold) needs > 4 × Ki,app, about 2550 mg XR (p1363-1364).
- No ke0, no hysteresis estimated (occupancy treated as a direct function of plasma; PET at peak and trough only). Adding norquetiapine (interaction model) did not improve fit.

## Covariates
None.

## Caveats
- Healthy young men, small n; 300 mg once daily (IR is usually given twice daily).
- Occmax fixed, not estimated; only two concentration levels per formulation, so Ki,app rests mainly on 44-59% and low-trough points.
- Assumes plasma-brain equilibrium at the PET times; Ki,app is total plasma, quetiapine alone.
