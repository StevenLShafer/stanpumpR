# Liou 2018: NLMAZ response surface for midazolam-alfentanil-propofol sedation

## Study
Single-centre observational study (Taipei), ASA 1-2 adults 20-80 yr for upper endoscopy or colonoscopy. Modelling group: 56 enrolled (33 female), 4 excluded, mean age 53.3 yr, BMI 23.1, weight 61.6 kg, 227 concentration sets. Validation group: 47 patients (32 female), age 49.3 yr, weight 62.9 kg, upper endoscopy only, 188 sets. Anaesthesiologist-chosen IV boluses; MOAA/S scored at start, end and critical events; loss of response (LOR) = MOAA/S <2. Effect-site concentrations (Ce) from TIVA Trainer 9.1: Maitre (alfentanil), Zomorodi (midazolam), Schnider (propofol); t1/2 ke0 from Scott 1985 (alfentanil, EEG) and Buhrer 1990 (midazolam, EEG), values not printed. Ce ranges 0-108 ng/mL midazolam, 0-156 ng/mL alfentanil, 0-2.6 ug/mL propofol. Respiratory depression (RD) = SpO2 <90%.

## Model and parameters
Non-linear mixed amount with zero amount (NLMAZ) response surface: P(LOR) = (U/U50)^n / (1 + (U/U50)^n), Emax 1, E0 0; U = sum of Ce/C50 over the three drugs; n and log10 U50 are cubic polynomials in the drug fractions (23 parameters); fmincon with bootstrap, Matlab. AUC 0.87 (95% CI 0.83-0.92) modelling, 0.80 (0.72-0.88) validation; accuracy 83% and 76% (Results, PDF p4).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: LOR to prodding or noxious stimulus (MOAA/S <2) during endoscopy; binary, not EEG. The C50s are model-based Ce, not measured. Synergy: minimum U50 0.74 at fractions 0.35/0.37/0.28 (midazolam/alfentanil/propofol); midazolam-alfentanil strongest pair (24.3% fall in U50, abstract), others weaker; synergy lost above about 72-76% of C50 (authors' values: alfentanil 480 ng/mL, midazolam 39 ng/mL, propofol 1.9 ug/mL). RD: 2 patients (3%) in the modelling group, both above an alfentanil fraction of 0.12 with LOR probability <0.5; authors conclude alfentanil is the main cause.
Opioid audit (alfentanil): no MEAC or analgesic concentration; no ke0, t1/2 ke0 or tPeak of their own (EEG t1/2 ke0 from Scott 1985 inside TIVA Trainer, not printed). Potency ratio alfentanil:remifentanil printed as 0.0625:1.2 (Discussion, PDF p9 / printed p1217), used to turn LaPierre's remifentanil C50 for RD (4.1 ng/mL) into alfentanil 78.72 ng/mL (authors' derivation; implies a factor 19.2). Midazolam 150 ng/mL steady state caused no RD at MOAA/S 2-3 (cited from Flogel 1993).

## Covariates
None in the NLMAZ model; the covariates sit in the source PK models used to compute Ce.

## Caveats
Patients not volunteers; non-steady state; clustered concentrations; alfentanil C50 is outside the data (0% of sets above it) and the authors say opioid alone cannot give LOR; only 2 RD events; 23 parameters with several RSE >50%; validation AUC lower. MOAA/S <2 differs from the verbal end point of Minto 2000.
