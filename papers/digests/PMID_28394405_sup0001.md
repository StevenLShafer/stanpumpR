# Tham 2017 (supplement): the covariate and structural equations of the eight insulin models

## Study
No new study. A 3-page document, "Supplement Methods: Pharmacokinetic Model Equations", for the parent article (card
`PMID_28394405.md`: 16 pooled Lilly PK and clamp studies, NONMEM 7.3 FOCE-I, one-compartment models). It holds the
equations of the final model in four sections (glargine; regular U100, U500 and NPH; lispro and lispro protamine
suspension, ILPS; the common endogenous baseline), then an abbreviation list and the definitions of theta1-theta7.
It prints no parameter values: the numbers are in the article's Table 2.

**This PDF print has lost every equation.** Pages 1-2 show the section headings and the bare lines "When t <=
DUR_Glar, K0_Glar =", "When t <= t_lag,NPH," and so on, with blank space where each equation object should be
(rendered pages 1 and 2; the text layer agrees). Page 2 (lower half) and page 3 are the abbreviations and theta
definitions, intact. Every equation below is from `PMID_28394405_sup0001.docx`, the same document, whose OMML
objects survive. The stanpumpR header (R/insulinTham.R:52-53 at e36536c) already says the PDF had lost the equation
objects.

## Model and parameters
Equations as the Word file's OMML gives them (subscripts flattened; t is time after dose in hours):

Glargine:
- F_Glar = (TVF_Glar x (Dose_Glar/35))^theta1. In the OMML the superscript's base holds the product TVF_Glar x
  (Dose/35), not Dose/35 alone; TVF_Glar is fixed at 1 (Table 2), so the two readings give the same number.
- Ka_Glar = TVKa_Glar; CL_Glar = TVCL_Glar (no covariate).
- Vd_Glar = TVVd_Glar x (1 + theta3 x (BW - 71.4)) (linear, reference 71.4 kg).
- ABS_Glar = Ka_Glar x Dose_Glar x FRAC_Glar x F_Glar.
- For t <= DUR_Glar, K0_Glar = (1 - FRAC_Glar) x F_Glar x Dose_Glar / DUR_Glar; for t > DUR_Glar, K0_Glar = 0.
- dConc_Glar/dt = (ABS_Glar + K0_Glar)/Vd_Glar - (CL_Glar/Vd_Glar) x Conc_Glar. No lag term anywhere.

Regular U100, U500 and NPH:
- Ka_RegU100 = TVKa_RegU100 x (Dose_RegU100/10)^theta2; Ka_RegU500 = TVKa_RegU500; Ka_NPH = TVKa_NPH.
- Vd_Reg/NPH/F = TVVd_Reg/NPH x (BW/80)^theta4 (one volume shared by U100, U500 and NPH).
- CL_Reg/NPH/F = TVCL_Reg/NPH x (Dose_Reg/NPH/10)^theta6.
- For t <= t_lag,NPH, ABS_NPH = 0; for t > t_lag,NPH, ABS_NPH = Ka_NPH x Dose_NPH.
- dConc_Reg/NPH/dt = (Ka_Reg100 x Dose_RegU100 + Ka_RegU500 x Dose_RegU500 + ABS_NPH)/(Vd_Reg/NPH/F) -
  (CL_Reg/NPH/F)/(Vd_Reg/NPH/F) x Conc_Reg/NPH. Regular U100 and U500 carry no lag.

Lispro and ILPS:
- Ka_Lispro = TVKa_Lispro; Ka_ILPS = TVKa_ILPS; CL_Lispro/ILPS/F = TVCL_Lispro/ILPS (no covariate).
- Vd_Lispro/ILPS/F = TVVd_Lispro/ILPS x (BW/80)^theta5.
- For t <= t_lag,Lispro, ABS_Lispro = 0; for t > t_lag,Lispro, ABS_Lispro = Ka_Lispro x Dose_Lispro x FRAC_Lispro.
- For t <= DUR_Lispro, K0_Lispro = (1 - FRAC_Lispro) x Dose_Lispro / DUR_Lispro; for t > DUR_Lispro, K0_Lispro = 0.
  The zero-order condition is on t, the time after the dose, not on t - t_lag. No F term for lispro.
- dConc_Lispro/ILPS/dt = (ABS_Lispro + K0_Lispro + Ka_ILPS x Dose_ILPS)/(Vd/F) - (CL/F)/(Vd/F) x Conc. ILPS
  carries no lag.

Common endogenous baseline: ENDO_INS = TVENDO_INS x (BW/80)^theta7, "common parameter shared by all insulins"
(abbreviations, PDF p3), its unit given there as U/mL.

Theta definitions (PDF p3): theta1 power, dose on F_Glar; theta2 power, dose on Ka_RegU100; theta3 linear slope, BW
on Vd_Glar; theta4 power, BW on Vd_Reg/NPH; theta5 power, BW on Vd_Lispro/ILPS; theta6 power, dose on CL_Reg/NPH;
theta7 power, BW on TVENDO_INS. Their values are Table 2's (parent card), not printed here.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. Plasma (serum immunoreactive) insulin only; no glucose, GIR or effect site.

## Covariates
Body weight: power on the human-insulin V/F (reference 80 kg), power on the lispro/ILPS V/F (80 kg), linear on the
glargine V (71.4 kg), power on the endogenous baseline (80 kg). Dose: power on glargine F (reference 35), power on
regular U100 ka (10), power on the human-insulin CL/F (10). No covariate on any clearance other than the human
insulins' dose term, and none on glargine or lispro clearance; no weight term on any clearance.

## Caveats
- The PDF print carries no equations at all (blank objects); only the Word file is usable.
- Dose units are not given for the reference values 10 and 35. The parent's Methods (PDF p2, printed 1127) say molar
  units were used for dose and concentration in the analysis, while the outputs were expressed in U. The supplement
  does not say whether 10 and 35 are U or molar amounts. Glargine's 35 sits beside Table 1's mean glargine dose of
  36 U; the regular insulins' 10 is not their Table 1 mean (35 U for U100).
- "Dose_Reg/NPH", the dose on the shared human-insulin CL/F, is not in the abbreviation list (which defines only
  Dose_NPH, Dose_RegU100 and Dose_RegU500), so for a 70/30 premix the document does not say whether it is the whole
  injection or a component.
- The absorption terms are written Ka x Dose, with the dose amount rather than an amount remaining in a depot; read
  literally they are constant inputs, not the first-order absorption the article and Figure 1 describe.
- Typos: "Ka_Reg100" for Ka_RegU100 in the regular dConc equation; "Vd/F" without subscript in the lispro dConc
  equation; "IPLS" for ILPS in two abbreviations; TVKa_NPH listed twice.
- ENDO_INS is given "in U/mL" here, while Table 2 gives the baseline as 79.7 pmol/L.
- The supplement settles two of the article's internal inconsistencies (parent card, Caveats): the weight power on V/F
  belongs to the volume U100, U500 and NPH share, and the endogenous baseline is common to all insulins (the article
  text said regular and NPH only). It also confirms that no clearance carries weight, against the article text's
  statement that CL/F rose with weight.
