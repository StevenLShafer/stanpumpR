# Choi 2015: propofol PK/PD in children, externally validated

## Study
- 39 ASA 1-2 children aged 2-12 y (Table 1: median age 5 y, range 2-12; weight median 21 kg, range 11.9-39.1; height 115.7 +/- 17.1 cm; 35 M/4 F), elective urologic or orthopaedic surgery, Seoul National University Hospital (PDF p2-3).
- Premedication midazolam 0.1 mg/kg (max 5 mg) + atropine 0.02 mg/kg. Induction propofol 3 mg/kg bolus + remifentanil 1 mcg/kg bolus; then propofol 200 mcg/kg/min and remifentanil 0.2 mcg/kg/min, titrated to BIS < 60. Rocuronium 0.6 mg/kg; 65% N2O in O2. Mean remifentanil infusion 0.24 +/- 0.17 mcg/kg/min (range 0.203-1.214).
- Arterial samples (1 mL, EDTA) pre-induction, 2, 5, 10, 15, 30 min after bolus and 0, 2, 5, 10, 30, 60, 120 min after stopping: 405 concentrations (7-15 per child), 30 (7.5%) within 5 min of bolus, 77 (19%) within 10 min. Plasma propofol by HPLC-fluorescence, LLOQ 100 ng/mL, range 100-10,000 ng/mL. 1159 BIS values (Aspect 2000).
- NONMEM VII, FOCE-I, ADVAN6; sequential PK then PD (post hoc PK). Randomization test and 2000 bootstrap replicates (fit4NM).
- External validation: 155 children (age median 6 y, range 0.2-12; weight median 21 kg, range 5.2-65; 78 M/77 F) given effect-site TCI (Asan pump v2.1.3) at stepwise targets 4, 5, 3, 2 mcg/mL, each held at least 10 min; 599 plasma samples; BIS paired for 78 (intermediate PD model) and 77 (final PD model) children.

Basic (no covariate) PK model, Table 2: V1 2.12 L, V2 24.4 L, CL 0.86 L/min, Q 1.32 L/min (RSE 9.9, 6.7, 14.0, 29.4%); CV 47.8, 37.0, 45.6, 92.5%; sigma 5.2%. Covariate steps: weight on V2 dOFV 16, then weight on CL dOFV 28 (PDF p7-8). Eta-shrinkage V1/V2/CL/Q 36.4/42.9/2.8/11.2%.

Doses are per kg but V1 is absolute (L), so a lighter child gets a higher initial concentration per mg/kg; the authors argue this explains higher weight-based induction needs in small children (PDF p9, p12).

Basic PD model (Table 3): E0 76.9, Emax 35.6, Ce50 2.61 mcg/mL, gamma 3.05, ke0 0.557 /min. Effect equation is Eq. 2: BIS = E0 + (Emax - E0) x Ce^g / (Ce50^g + Ce^g), with Emax the minimum achievable BIS (PDF p3). REMI in Ce50 is the mean remifentanil infusion rate over the whole case (bolus plus infusion doses divided by elapsed time, mcg/kg/min), a quantity known only at the end of anaesthesia.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: BIS (Aspect 2000), children given midazolam premedication (mean baseline BIS 82.2 +/- 7.0, PDF p13) and remifentanil co-infusion; propofol concentration is arterial plasma.
- ke0 (blood-brain equilibration, BIS): 0.557 /min in the basic and intermediate models, 0.372 /min in the final model (Table 3). The authors give a half-life of 1.87 min for the final ke0 (PDF p13; ln2/0.372 = 1.86 min, derived). They state this is faster than adult values of 5.68 and 5.13 min that they cite. No tPeak is reported (derived tPeak not computed). The ke0 was carried into the Asan pump TCI.
- Ce50 on BIS: 2.61 mcg/mL (basic); 3.78 - 0.183 x age (intermediate); final per Table 3 3.65 - 0.102 x AGE - 1.72 x REMI (abstract version: 3.47 - 0.095 x age - 1.63 x REMI). Authors: intermediate Ce50 fell 53.6% from age 2 to 12 y; final fell 29.0% over the same range with REMI 0, and fell 74.3% as REMI rose from 0.2 to 1.2 mcg/kg/min (age 10 y) (PDF p10).
- No MEAC, no analgesic end point, no opioid concentration is reported; this is a propofol paper. Remifentanil effect enters only through the mean infusion rate covariate; remifentanil concentrations were not measured.
- No potency ratio. Midazolam was given at a fixed 0.1 mg/kg so its effect is absorbed in E0 and the parameters; not modelled separately in the final model (an interaction variant is in supplementary Table S3, not in this PDF).

## Covariates
- PK: weight on V2 (linear) and CL (power, exponent 0.97). None on V1. Age, sex, height, BSA, LBM, BMI, fat-free mass, eGFR, protein, albumin, remifentanil and midazolam dose were tested (PDF p3).
- PD: age on Ce50 (intermediate); age and mean remifentanil infusion rate on Ce50 (final). Age and weight effects were simulated to be independent: PK depends on weight, PD potency on age.

## Caveats
- Sampling: only 7.5% of samples within 5 min of the bolus and none beyond 120 min after stopping; two compartments were adequate partly for that reason (authors, PDF p13). V1 is lower than other paediatric models (they cite a range of 1.74-10.3 L for a 10 kg 1-year-old).
- External validation of the PK (Table 4, PDF p10): bias (MDPE) -20.21% (-23.31 to -18.12), inaccuracy 30.35% (28.62-32.72), divergence 19.14 %/h, wobble 11.48% - plasma concentrations were over-predicted by TCI. BIS: intermediate model bias 1.46%, inaccuracy 18.87%, divergence -8.57 %/h, wobble 8.50%; final model bias -6.81%, inaccuracy 19.13%, divergence -6.99 %/h, wobble 9.97%.
- The final PD model needs the whole-case mean remifentanil rate, unknown during real-time use; the authors recommend the intermediate model for TCI.
- Final-model parameters disagree between the abstract, Table 3 and the Table 4 footnote (see open_questions). Table 3's final-model sigma is labelled sigma^2 (8.4) but the text layer of the intermediate gives sigma 8.5; labels not reconciled.
- The text layer of Tables 2 and 3 is scrambled (columns interleaved); values above were read from rendered pages.
- Patient set is skewed male (35 of 39); BMI/obesity not studied; arterial sampling.
