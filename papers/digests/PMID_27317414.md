# Glund 2017: age, renal function and idarucizumab PK / dabigatran reversal

## Study
Randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled crossover phase Ib (NCT01955720; SGS Antwerp, Sep 2013 to Aug 2014).
46 volunteers in four groups, all completing: middle-aged 45-64 y (n = 12), elderly 65-80 y (n = 16), mild renal impairment RI 60-90 (CrCl 60 to under 90 mL/min Cockcroft-Gault, age 45-80, n = 6 per dose), moderate RI 30-60 (CrCl 30 to under 60, n = 6). BMI 18.5-29.9 (middle-aged) or 18.5-32 kg/m2 (others). Dabigatran etexilate (DE) 220 mg twice daily (middle-aged, elderly) or 150 mg twice daily (RI groups) for 4 days; idarucizumab or placebo as a 5-min infusion starting about 2 h after the last DE dose (1 h 55 min after, per Methods). Doses 1, 2.5, 5 g, or 2 x 2.5 g one hour apart (RI 30-60 only). Crossover after a 6-day or longer washout. Sampling to 120 h (longer in RI).
Demographics (Table 1, text layer): weight (SD) 65.5 (12.2) to 80.8 (7.0) kg, mean CrCl 97.5 / 110.4 (middle-aged), 84.8 / 83.3 (elderly), 79.9 / 72.8 (RI 60-90), 58.7 (RI 30-60) mL/min; weight lowest in RI 30-60.
Assays: idarucizumab by ELISA (LLOQ 1 ug/mL, plasma and urine); total and unbound dabigatran by LC-MS/MS (LLOQ 5.00 and 1.00 ng/mL); dTT (Hemoclot), ECT, aPTT in a central laboratory. Noncompartmental PK; geometric means; post hoc t tests of the 5 g groups against the middle-aged 5 g group.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Not an opioid; no MEAC, ke0 or tPeak. Effect measured on dabigatran-prolonged clotting times (dTT, ECT, aPTT; also thrombin time and ACT in the supplement). Reversal was defined as the clotting time returning to or below a threshold equal to 20 ng/mL unbound dabigatran, from a linear regression of unbound dabigatran on dTT and ECT using this and an earlier study. Placebo: peak unbound dabigatran about 170 ng/mL at about 2 h in middle-aged volunteers (dTT x1.8, ECT x3, aPTT x2.2); 210 ng/mL in elderly (x2.1, x3.6, x2.2); about 140 (RI 60-90) and about 220 ng/mL (RI 30-60). Unbound is about 30% below total. Idarucizumab 2.5 g or more gave immediate, complete, sustained (at least 24 h) reversal; unbound dabigatran fell to under 1 ng/mL (LLOQ); total dabigatran rose about 4.5- to 5.8-fold within 30 min (bound to idarucizumab). 1 g gave partial return of anticoagulation at about 2 h. Binding is 1:1 stoichiometric. AUEC2-12 reductions are in Table 3 (not read on the image, see open question). No concentration-effect model or EC50 reported.

## Covariates
Renal function (CrCl) lowers idarucizumab clearance and prolongs the initial half-life (+48.7% in RI 30-60 versus middle-aged at 5 g, p = 0.0003); age alone did not change PK significantly; sex: profiles similar (data not shown). No equation given.

## Caveats
Small groups (6-8) of mostly White volunteers, healthy apart from renal impairment; the RI 30-60 group received a split dose (Cmax not comparable); noncompartmental; CL in the 2.5 g and 5 g middle-aged groups differs, and 1 g elderly shows high CV (terminal half-life CV 60.6%). Idarucizumab is a protein with a concentration-dependent distribution; do not generalise the 1 g groups.
