# Luzier 1999: sex differences in metoprolol enantiomer PK and exercise PD

## Study
Randomised crossover (drug vs drug-free control, 7-day washout), 20 healthy white volunteers, 10 men
(26.0 ± 4.9 y, 83.9 ± 10.7 kg, 66-97) and 10 women (25.3 ± 3.7 y, 62.0 ± 7.3 kg, 54-77); all
dextromethorphan-phenotyped CYP2D6 extensive metabolisers; women in follicular phase, no oral
contraceptives (PDF p2, printed 595). Metoprolol tartrate (Lopressor) 100 mg orally twice daily, 9 doses;
PK/PD after the 9th. Plasma at 0, 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12, 24 h. Stereospecific HPLC (UV 256 nm),
LOQ 5 ng/mL each enantiomer, range 5-500, CV 4% within-day, 10% between-day (PDF p3).
PD: submaximal upright bicycle exercise (workload set at 75% of age-predicted max HR) at 0, 2, 4, 6, 8, 12,
24 h; exercise HR (ECG) and cuff SBP; effect = % reduction from the drug-free (control) phase at the same
time.
Analysis: iterative two-stage population method (ADAPT II subroutines), separation then comodelling,
AIC; Kruskal-Wallis for comparisons (PDF p3).

## Model and parameters
Linear two-compartment, first-order absorption with lag; (R) and (S) comodelled assuming similar
elimination, with an S/R factor. Apparent (÷F) parameters, normalised to total body weight.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Sigmoid Emax (Hill) model, E = Emax·C^H/(EC50^H + C^H), C = **plasma (S)-metoprolol** concentration,
effect site **in the central compartment** (i.e. no ke0; an effect compartment was tested and not
selected) (PDF p4, printed 597). End points: % reduction in **exercise** HR and **exercise** SBP vs the
control phase — not resting BP. Table II (PDF p5, mean (SD), page image): see YAML. AUEC0-12 SBP 295
(141) vs 358 (109) %·h, HR 200 (85) vs 250 (37)* %·h. No PD parameter differed by sex (P > .20).
No ke0/t½ke0/tPeak, no therapeutic range, no potency ratio.

## Covariates
Sex (the study's subject): women had higher Cmax and AUC, lower weight-normalised Vp/F and a trend to lower
CL/F; comparator is the study's own men. Authors attribute it to higher bioavailability (lower first pass)
in women.

## Caveats
- n = 10 per sex, healthy young CYP2D6 extensive metabolisers; not hypertensive patients.
- Apparent parameters only (F unknown). The Discussion says the "greater volume" in women, but Table I
  shows lower Vp/F in women; and the per-kg clearance range quoted (10.76-98.08) is inconsistent with the
  Table I means (open question).
- SBP Hill coefficient 6.15 with SD 6.77: poorly determined.
- PD fitted to (S) only, though Table I lists both enantiomers.
