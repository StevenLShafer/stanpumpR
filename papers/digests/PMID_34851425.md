# Alvarez-Jimenez 2022: dexmedetomidine PK with concentration-dependent (nonlinear) clearance

## Study
Pooled analysis of two published healthy-volunteer step-up TCI trials (PDF p. 2): Hannivoort 2015 (n = 18, two sessions each, targets 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8 ng/mL, 30 min each, Dyck model driving the pump) and Weerink 2019 (n = 30, dexmedetomidine-alone period only, targets 1, 3, 4, 5, 8 (amended to 6) ng/mL, 40-50 min each, Hannivoort model driving the pump). Infusion rate capped at 6 mcg/kg/h, and 10 mcg/kg/h for the highest targets. Arterial plasma samples during and up to 300-420 min after infusion; 762 observations, 29 below LLOQ (M3 method), range 0.02-14.4 ng/mL. 48 subjects, stratified by age and sex: 24 M / 24 F, age mean 42.2 yr (18-70), weight mean 74.8 kg (SD 13.3; 49-110), height mean 176.2 cm; BMI 18-30 allowed. NONMEM 7.3, LAPLACE, ADVAN13; allometric a priori scaling; AIC for selection.

## Model and parameters
Three-compartment mammillary, elimination from the central compartment, with concentration-dependent clearance: CL = CL0 x (1 - Imax x Cp^g / (Cp^g + C50^g)), Imax fixed at 1 (Eq. 3). Volumes scale linearly with FSIZE, Q2/Q3 and CL0 with FSIZE^0.75 (FSIZE = WGT/70 for males; females x (1 + 0.180)); age on CL0 only: FAGE = exp(-0.00519 x (AGE - 35)). Model development (Table 1, PDF p. 5): AIC -663.1 (linear 3-cmt) to -831.8 (nonlinear CL, change -168.7), -842.8 (sex on FSIZE), -849.8 (age on CL0, final). Performance: median PE -2.3%, median absolute PE 21.7%. Parameter uncertainty 4-32.7% (fixed), 30.5-40.8% (random).
Nonlinearity check (derived from text, PDF p. 11): refit excluding samples taken during or within 5 min of infusion (419 observations, 55%): nonlinear model still better, change in OFV -63, P < 0.001, so incomplete arterial mixing does not explain it. Mean maximal fall in CL across individuals 55.2% (range 23.3-82.7%).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD was estimated. For effect-site TCI simulations the authors used ke0 = 4.85e-2 /min (their ref 17, Colin 2017, MOAA/S sedation score; PDF p. 3, printed p. 281). No MEAC, tPeak, t1/2ke0 or potency ratio reported. Not an opioid.

## Covariates
Total body weight (allometric, exponent 1 on volumes, 0.75 on clearances), sex (females have FSIZE 1.18x larger; change in AIC -11), age (CL0 only; AIC -7). No significant covariate on C50. Cardiac output (time-varying, Eq. 1) did not explain between- or within-subject variability in CL (change in AIC +1.2, LRT P = 0.25; theta_BSV 0.0211, theta_WSV 0.0037, i.e. negligible).

## Caveats
Healthy volunteers aged 18-70, BMI 18-30; no children, very old or ill patients. Imax fixed at 1 (could not be estimated); the authors state higher concentrations than studied would be needed. Mechanism hypothesised (concentration-dependent change in liver blood flow / cardiac output ratio), not shown. Authors' simulation conclusions: the linear Hannivoort model agrees with the nonlinear model up to about 2 ng/mL; above, 62.1% of simulated subjects exceed a 3 ng/mL plasma target and 80.4% a 5 ng/mL target (Fig. 4; PDF p. 8); ratio of nonlinear-model concentration to target 0.89 (1 ng/mL), 0.97 (2), 1.1 (3), 1.2 (4), 1.4 (5). With the EMA ICU loading dose (1 mcg/kg over 10 min) predicted Cmax is 3.2 ng/mL versus 1.5 ng/mL linear (PDF p. 9).

## Verification against stanpumpR
Independent comparison with R/drugs_dexmedetomidine.R (Morse 2020, linear), my arithmetic (derived):
- Clearance, 70 kg man of 35 yr: Alvarez CL0 0.799 L/min at Cp -> 0; at 0.6 ng/mL (the library's `typical`, :169) CL = 0.799 x (1 - 0.60^1.34/(0.60^1.34 + 5.75^1.34)) = about 0.762 L/min. Morse (header :70) gives 0.873 L/min for a 70 kg, 176 cm man of 35 yr, so Alvarez is about 13% lower at the library's typical target. Direction: the library's clearance is higher than this paper's at sedation concentrations, so the library would predict LOWER concentrations than this paper implies, and real plasma concentrations would run ABOVE the library's prediction. For a dose chosen from the library that is an OVER-EXPOSURE risk, not a conservative one: a clinician targeting a sedation concentration would reach more than intended. This card first called it "the conservative direction for under-dosing", which is backwards and was corrected after CodeRabbit raised it on PR #903 (2026-11-25); the clause that follows always said the right thing, since the paper's own finding is that the linear Hannivoort/TCI-type models UNDERPREDICT high concentrations, which is the same direction. At 2 ng/mL the factor is 1 - 0.0814 = about 0.92 (derived).
- Volumes: Alvarez V1 1.87, V2 17.8, V3 67.4 L (sum 87 L at 70 kg) against Morse's 25.2, 34.4, 65.4 L (R/drugs_dexmedetomidine.R:108-110; sum 125 L at its reference NFM). The central volume differs about 13-fold; the paper's V1 is the Hannivoort-lineage small V1. Different data sets (Morse pooled 5 sets and simulated Hannivoort's), so not a transcription issue, but it affects bolus-driven peak concentrations and ke0-derived tPeak.
- Effect-site: ke0 mismatch recorded in `open_questions` (0.0485 here against 0.0428 at :81-84).
- No repo location depends on this paper; verdict partly-verified because only the independent comparison above was done.
