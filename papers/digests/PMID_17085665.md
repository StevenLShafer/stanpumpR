# Ekhart 2006: flat dosing of carboplatin

## Study
Retrospective pooled population PK of ultrafilterable platinum (free carboplatin, measured as platinum by flameless atomic absorption) from 178 adults with cancer, 280 courses, 3,119 samples (abstract, p1; Methods p4). Pooled from five earlier studies (refs 6, 20-23): conventional dosing (AUC 6 mg/mL min over 30 min, or 300-600 mg/m2 over 30 min, with paclitaxel) and high-dose CTC/tCTC (cyclophosphamide, thiotepa, carboplatin 267-400 mg/m2/d or AUC 13.3-20 mg/mL min over 1 h for 4 days) (Table 1, PDF p2; table text layer scrambled, protocol details approximate). Case mix from the abstract and Table 1: 107 women/71 men; SCR median 51 umol/L (range 18-124) (abstract). NONMEM V 1.1, FOCE with INTERACTION, log-transformed data; exponential IIV and IOV; exponential (proportional) residual error. Ultrafiltrate by Amicon YMT-14 30 kDa membrane, 2,500 g, 20 min. Noncompensated Jaffe creatinines were adjusted by subtracting 26 umol/L (Methods, p4). Inclusion criteria gave Cockcroft-Gault CrCl above 50 mL/min in all patients (Discussion, p7).

## Model and parameters
Two-compartment, first-order elimination (Cl, Vd, k12, k21); IIV on all four, IOV on Cl and k12 (Results, p5). No covariate remained: no effect of SCR, GFR/CrCl estimate, age, weight, sex, pretreatment, protocol or repeat courses (Results, p5; Figs 4-6).

Covariate models (Table 3, p6, objective function values; text layer): model 1, no SCR relation, OFV -4,764.722; model 2, Calvert with CG or other GFR, -4,457.087; model 3, Calvert with the constant refitted, -4,460.216; model 4, CG form refitted, -4,789.303 (dOFV -24.6 against model 1, 2 df). Refitted model 4: Cl (mL/min) = 115.5 + 0.212 x (140 - age) x weight x 0.736^gender / SCR (p5; gender 1 = female), against Calvert with CG: 25 + 1.23 x (140 - age) x weight x 0.85^gender / SCR. The authors still conclude that the relation is weak (intercept 4.6x higher, slope 6x smaller than Calvert with CG) and that dosing formulae do not beat a flat dose.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD. No ke0, tPeak, MEAC or effect-site model (not an opioid). Exposure target quoted: carboplatin AUC (ultrafilterable) 5-7 mg/mL min typical for Calvert dosing (p1); flat dose = target AUC x 138.8 mL/min, e.g. AUC 5 gives 694.2 mg (p7).

## Covariates
None retained for the fitted population model. Tested: SCR, CG CrCl, Jelliffe CrCl, Wright GFR (each unadjusted and truncated at 150 or 250 mL/min), age, weight, BSA, gender, cisplatin pretreatment, protocol, multiple courses, Chatelut formula (Fig. 6). All non-significant at dOFV above 7.8 (P < 0.005).

## Caveats
Retrospective pooled data from patients with relatively normal renal function (CrCl above 50 mL/min); small IIV (19.1%) may reflect that, and the authors say so. More than half the patients had SCR below 60 umol/L (CG CrCl above 150 mL/min, physiologically implausible). Mixed creatinine assays (kinetic Jaffe vs compensated Jaffe) corrected retrospectively by 26 umol/L. The result does not apply to renal impairment. Analyte is free platinum by atomic absorption, not intact carboplatin.

## Verification against stanpumpR
- `R/drugs_carboplatinDuffull.R:8-9` ("fitted, but with no renal term"): **matches** the paper (the authors tested four renal-term models and found no SCR relation, Table 3).
- `docs/chemotherapy.md:181-182` ("fitted in creatinine clearance above 50 mL/min"): **matches** (Discussion p7).
- Comparison with the library's Duffull model (carboplatinDuffull) at its cohort mean (header lines 38-41: Cl 6.5 L/h, V1 14.6 L, Vss 21.3 L; Table 2 means 6.8, 14.8, 21.6): Ekhart's mean CL 8.33 L/h is about 22% higher than Duffull's 6.8 (derived: 8.33/6.8 = 1.23). Ekhart's patients were mostly normal renal function (CG above 50, many above 150 mL/min) in a heterogeneous cohort including high-dose regimens; Duffull's were 12 women with adjusted GFR 35-98 mL/min (mean adjusted ClCr 74.2 mL/min). The direction is expected from the renal-function difference; the analytes also differ (see open questions). The library's carboplatin default is not tested against Ekhart's flat-dose value in the repo.
- Paper's flat-dose clearance 138.8 mL/min = 8.33 L/h x 1000/60 (derived: confirmed). No unit problem found.
- The "erratum" or other claims in the repo about Ekhart were not found beyond the above. Cannot verify parameter V2/Vss use (open question).
