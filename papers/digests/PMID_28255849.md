# Savic 2017: population PK of oral/IV cladribine in multiple sclerosis

## Study
Pooled population PK of four studies in relapsing-remitting MS (PDF pp. 2-3; Tables 1-2): 25803 (phase I, n = 16, 3 mg IV over 1 h versus 10 mg tablet, crossover), 26127 (phase I food crossover, n = 16, 10 mg tablet fed/fasted), 26486 (n = 16, tablets 1.75 mg/kg over 8 weeks with and without IFN beta-1a), 25643 CLARITY (phase III, n = 125, sparse, 3.5 or 5.25 mg/kg cumulative over 2 years). 173 patients, 2619 plasma and urine observations of cladribine (CdA) and 2-chloroadenine (CAde) after 45% of records (mostly BLQ) were excluded. Median age about 40 y (range 19-65), median weight 69.2 kg (range 48.5-116.1), about 34% men (Table 3; scrambled in the text layer, not confirmed on an image). Assay LC-MS/MS, LLOQ 100 pg/mL, in plasma and urine. NONMEM VI+, FOCE/FOCEI, PsN covariate search, bootstrap and VPC.

## Model and parameters
CdA: three-compartment, linear elimination, parent total clearance = renal (proportional to creatinine clearance) plus non-renal; oral first-order absorption (fasted), transit-compartment absorption delay (fed), lag time in phase III. CAde: one compartment with saturable renal clearance, fed by CdA. Final model, Table 4 (PDF p. 6, confirmed on the rendered page; RSE from bootstrap n = 100):

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No concentration-effect, therapeutic range or effect-site model. Not an opioid; no MEAC, ke0, tPeak or potency ratio.

## Covariates
- Creatinine clearance (Cockcroft-Gault) on CdA renal clearance, linear (Table 4 note c). Retained.
- Food: lowers CdA bioavailability (about 11-14%) and delays absorption through the transit model.
- IFN beta-1a: CLNR x 1.21 (total CL about +11%); could equally be a bioavailability effect, the design cannot separate them (PDF p. 7).
- Weight, age and sex not significant except through CLCR; liver tests not associated with CLNR.

## Caveats
- Stated by the authors: urine data are noisy (RUV about 87-104%); CAde second compartment suspected but unsupported; IFN effect confounded with period/occasion.
- Phase III data are sparse and carry no renal-clearance information.
- Patients with MS, mostly mild renal impairment at most; CLCR typical 6.31 L/h versus 4.86 L/h in the earlier oncology analysis (Discussion, PDF p. 7).
- Comparison with Lindemalm 2005: the paper says its total clearance of 40.5 L/h at CLCR 4.86 L/h agrees with Lindemalm's 39.3 L/h; the earlier population's lower raw clearance was because of lower CLCR.
