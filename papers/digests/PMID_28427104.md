# Kasahara-Ito 2017: tofogliflozin phase 1 PK/PD (SAD, MAD, food effect) in healthy men

## Study
Three phase 1 studies in healthy men, all in Japan (PDF p2, printed 350):
- **SAD**: double-blind, randomised, placebo-controlled; single oral doses 10, 20, 40, 80, 160, 320, 640 mg
  (Japanese) and 10, 20, 80 mg (Caucasian), fasting; 6 active + 2 placebo per cohort. Plasma to 48 h
  (0.25 ... 48 h), urine to 48 h. (Text says 42 Japanese and 18 Caucasian active; Table 1 lists N 56 and 24
  including placebo.)
- **MAD**: 2.5, 20, 80 mg once daily for 7 days, 15 min before breakfast; 6 active + 2 placebo per cohort.
- **Food effect**: open-label three-period crossover, 20 or 40 mg (15 per dose) fasting, pre-meal (15 min
  before) and post-meal (30 min after) a high-fat, high-calorie breakfast.
- Table 1 (PDF p4, printed 352), mean ± SD: SAD Japanese age 24.8 ± 4.36 y, weight 61.2 ± 5.91 kg, eGFR
  107 ± 12.8; SAD Caucasian 31.0 ± 5.27 y, 73.2 ± 10.1 kg, eGFR 121 ± 14.6; MAD 27.4 ± 5.55 y, 62.9 ± 5.46 kg;
  food effect 27.0 ± 3.83 y, 62.9 ± 8.28 kg (eGFR in mL/min/1.73 m2).
- Assay: LC-MS/MS; plasma LLOQ 0.200 ng/mL for tofogliflozin in all studies. Metabolites (carboxylated,
  ketone, acyl-glucuronide) measured in MAD/food-effect studies. Non-compartmental analysis (WinNonlin 6.1);
  Emax analysis in SAS 9.4.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: urinary glucose excretion, UGE0-24h (g), and UGE rate (g/h, Fig 3).
- Emax model UGE0-24h = Emax·Cavg/(EC50 + Cavg), Cavg = AUC0-24h / 24 h (total plasma, ng/mL): **Emax 67.8 g,
  EC50 29.2 ng/mL** (p4 Results; Fig 4 legend, PDF p7). No SE given. Pooled Japanese and Caucasian, no ethnic
  difference in the relationship.
- Authors' conclusion: near-maximal effect at Cavg above about 100 ng/mL, i.e. doses between 20 and 40 mg.
- Table 3 (PDF p6, printed 354, page image), UGE0-24h mean ± SD: SAD Japanese placebo 0.0644; 10 mg 45.2 ± 9.13;
  20 mg 56.8 ± 5.43; 40 mg 59.1 ± 10.9; 80 mg 66.2 ± 11.2; 160 mg 64.2 ± 8.64; 320 mg 73.3 ± 9.88; 640 mg
  78.8 ± 10.9. Caucasian 10/20/80 mg 44.6/47.3/66.2. MAD day 1 2.5/20/80 mg 27.8/53.8/66.1; day 7 22.1/46.0/59.6.
  Food effect 42.4-54.5 g (no food dependence).
- No ke0, hysteresis or effect-compartment analysis. Not an opioid; no MEAC or potency ratio.
- Safety: no hypoglycaemia; blood ketone bodies rose dose-dependently under fasting (181-531 µmol/L means).

## Covariates
No covariate model. Ethnic difference in exposure attributed to body weight (GMR about 1 after weight
normalisation). Food lowers Cmax ~30% without changing AUC.

## Caveats
Healthy young men only (no women, no T2DM, no renal impairment); n = 6 per dose; NCA only; Emax fitted to
per-subject Cavg with no reported precision. Text p2 counts 42 Japanese/18 Caucasian active subjects while the
abstract and Table 1 give 56/24 (including placebo). MAD t½ (3.81-4.37 h) is from 0-24 h data only (footnote c),
so shorter than SAD. Sponsor (Chugai) authors.
