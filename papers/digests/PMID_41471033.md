# Park 2025: GB-5001 long-acting injectable donepezil, phase 1 PK/PD and population PK

## Study
- Phase 1, open-label, active-controlled, dose-escalation, parallel groups, Chungnam National University Hospital, Jan-Oct 2024 (NCT06127368). Healthy Asian male volunteers, 19-55 y, weight >= 55 kg, BMI 18.5-30. Part B excluded CYP2D6 poor metabolisers.
- Part A (n = 34 dosed; 8-9 per cohort): GB-5001A 70 mg IM, GB-5001A 70 mg SC, GB-5001D 70 mg SC, Aricept 10 mg oral. Part B (n = 16): GB-5001A 140 mg IM and 280 mg IM. Mean age 29.5 y (Part A) and 31.0 y (Part B); weight 73.3 kg (Part A) and 71.8 kg (Part B); height about 173 cm (Table 1).
- Sampling: pre-dose to 2352 h (D99) for IM cohorts (Cohort C to 1512 h); Aricept intensive to 240 h. Plasma donepezil by LC-MS/MS (total concentration). 6-O-desmethyl donepezil measured for the 280 mg IM group only. AChE activity measured in washed red cells (Cohort F only) by a Sigma colorimetric kit.
- NCA with WinNonlin 8.5; population model with NONMEM 7.5 on the GB-5001A IM cohorts (A, E, F) plus the Aricept cohort (D): 32 participants.

## Model and parameters
GB-5001A IM model (Figure 2, Table 6): two-compartment, absorption into central from an IM depot via one zero-order input and two parallel first-order depots (KA3, KA4), each with a lag. Combined residual error. Q was fixed at 100 L/h because it was not estimable (the authors cite 185 L/h from an earlier formulation, ref 14). Apparent (per-F) parameters; no weight scaling reported. No covariate effects are reported in Table 6 (sex, age, weight and CYP2D6 phenotype were considered in the dataset).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: erythrocyte AChE inhibition (% change from baseline) in Cohort F only (280 mg IM, n = 7): Emax 37.06% (95% CI 28.94-47.44), AUE(last) 52,373.11 h*% (41,037.97-66,839.15) (Table 3, p13). Geometric means.
- No EC50, Emax model, ke0, t1/2ke0, tPeak or hysteresis analysis was fitted; concentration-effect was not modelled.
- The authors cite earlier literature that >= 65% AChE inhibition is associated with ADAS-cog improvement and note that 37% is below that (p19-20, ref 18); no MEAC or therapeutic concentration is derived by the authors. Not an opioid.

## Covariates
Sex, age, weight and CYP2D6 phenotype were put in the modelling dataset, but no covariate relationship is reported in the final model (Table 6). CYP2D6 phenotype (Part B only: EM and intermediate metabolisers, no PM/UM): EM showed lower early concentrations but a slower decline after 1512 h in Cohort E (Figure S4); too few subjects to conclude.

## Caveats
- Healthy males only, n = 7-9 per group; single dose, so steady-state results are simulations. IIV in the model is small for CL (about 31% CV, derived) and V1 is poorly determined (SE 47.9 on 58.2).
- Apparent PK (per F) from a depot formulation with flip-flop kinetics: the terminal t1/2 (270-425 h, Table 2) reflects absorption, not elimination. The oral Aricept NCA CL/F 9.27 L/h and t1/2 89.77 h are the closest thing in the paper to disposition values; the IM model CL/F of 9.57 L/h is similar. The authors' "3-4 fold longer half-life" is against the Aricept arm of this study (89.77 h).
- CYP2D6 poor metabolisers excluded from Part B. Q fixed. AChE assay in red cells, not brain.
