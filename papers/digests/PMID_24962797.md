# Hutmacher 2015: covariate selection in pharmacometric analyses

## Study
Narrative methods review with no data of its own (no subjects, drug, dose or assay). It covers selection of nested predictor effects in nonlinear mixed-effects models (NONMEM-style), with one worked illustration (Figure 2: 30 subjects, one-compartment model after a single bolus, four candidate effects: weight and sex on CL and V; true model weight on CL and V plus sex on V).

## Model and parameters
No fitted model. Figure 1 (printed p. 135) shows template submodels: PK with CL and V as log-linear functions of sex, weight and CLcr with a random effect; PK-PD with E0, Emax and ED50 as functions of sex, comorbidity and age.

## Covariates
Methods content, summarised:
- Selection criterion: general information criterion GIC = ΔOFV + penalty. Penalty from chi-square cut-offs (df 1: alpha 0.05 -> 3.841, 0.01 -> 6.635, 0.001 -> 10.828); AIC penalty 2 per effect (equal to alpha 0.157 for df 1); BIC penalty ln(nE) per effect with effective sample size nS < nE < nT (printed p. 137).
- Procedures: forward selection, backward elimination, classical stepwise and combined forward/backward (p. 138-140); all-subsets (including the Wald approximation method, WAM) and genetic algorithm (p. 140-141); lasso, SCAD, Bayesian and resampling methods (p. 141-143).
- Advice: pre-specify predictor-parameter pairs in an analysis plan, avoid correlated covariates (for example weight and BMI) together, examine eigenvalues or condition numbers of the predictor and estimate-covariance matrices, report base, full and final models with standard errors, and consider keeping the full model (p. 135-146).
- One cautionary example: a covariate effect can be assigned to the wrong parameter (hysteresis vs potency; p. 135).

## Caveats
Opinion and review; no new data. Written about drug-development pharmacometrics; it speaks of OLS simulation results from the older statistics literature.
