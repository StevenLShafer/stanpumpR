# Hamp 2013: IV lidocaine bolus and sevoflurane MAC

## Study
Randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled; 90 ASA I-II adults aged 30-65 (91 recruited, one replaced because the laryngeal mask could not be placed), 30 per group, elective surgery with a >=3 cm truncal incision. Groups: placebo (saline), lidocaine 0.75 mg/kg, lidocaine 1.5 mg/kg, single IV bolus over 30 s, 3 min before skin incision, after >=15 min of constant end-tidal sevoflurane (equilibration). Mask induction 8% sevoflurane; LMA; normothermia (nasopharyngeal ~36.3-36.5 C). MAC by Dixon up-and-down: first patient per group at 2.0% end-tidal, steps of 0.2%; movement = gross purposeful head/limb movement within 1 min of incision (cough/bucking excluded). Expired agent by Draeger Primus (accuracy 0.1%). Sample size assumed 11 crossovers per group for a 20% MAC change. Bonferroni-adjusted tests, bootstrap check. No lidocaine concentrations were measured.
Table 1 (PDF p.3, printed 325), mean +/- SD: age 47.0 +/- 9.3 / 47.0 +/- 7.9 / 51.0 +/- 9.7 y (high / low / placebo); weight 69.8 / 68.0 / 70.0 kg; F/M 29/1, 28/2, 29/1; LMA-to-incision 22.2 / 22.0 / 22.3 min. Table 1's column header reads "1.75 mg/kg" for the high group; the text, abstract and Fig 2 say 1.5 mg/kg (printing error in the header).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: movement at skin incision (MAC for immobility), with end-tidal sevoflurane as the exposure. MAC reduction at 1.5 mg/kg is about 12% (stated; derived 0.23/1.86 = 12.4%), less than the hypothesised 20%. 0.75 mg/kg: no reduction (-0.01 difference).
- No lidocaine plasma, CSF or effect-site concentration, no ke0 / t1/2ke0 / tPeak, no hysteresis estimate. The authors chose 3 min after the bolus on literature that lidocaine reaches the CNS within ~2.5-3 min, and say the peak effect may have been missed and effect-site concentration varied (discussion p.326). No MEAC for lidocaine. Not an opioid paper; no potency ratio.
- Context cited by the authors: animal infusion studies (200-400 mcg/kg/min) lowered MAC by 28-43%; a human study at 1 mg/kg bolus + 1.5 mg/kg/h (Hodgson 1999) is dismissed as confounded.
- Haemodynamics (Table 2): BIS prestudy to preincision in the 1.5 group 41.8 to 37.0 (P 0.02); SAP fell in both lidocaine groups.

## Covariates
None modelled. Age restricted to 30-65 on the premise that MAC is uniform there.

## Caveats
Single bolus and single time point (3 min), not steady state; a 12% effect with lower CI limit 0.03% sevoflurane; the Dixon method with ~11 crossovers; no plasma levels; authors judge the effect of questionable clinical value. Population overwhelmingly female (about 95%).
