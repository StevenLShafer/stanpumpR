# Eap 2002: review of methadone pharmacokinetic variability

## Study
Narrative review (41 pages, ~235 references) of methadone pharmacokinetics, CYP metabolism, interactions,
maintenance dosing and therapeutic drug monitoring, focused on opioid-maintenance (MMT) patients.

## Model and parameters
Summary values (racemic unless stated), with the review's own location:
- Detectable in blood 15-45 min after oral dosing; **tmax 2.5-4 h** (refs 47, 48, 57, 60), range 1-5 h between
  patients, independent of dose; tablet and solution absorption comparable; possible second peak ~4 h
  (enterohepatic) (Abstract; section 2.1, p. 1156).
- **Oral bioavailability mean around 75%, range 36-100%** (Abstract); tablets around 70-80% for 10-60 mg doses
  (refs 47, 49, 53, 63), range 36-100%; 95 ± 9% falling to 81 ± 10% after 25 days of MMT (ref 49); predicted
  intestinal first-pass extraction about 20% (p. 1157).
- Absorption not stereoselective (similar F for both enantiomers).
- Free fraction about 13% (R,S), 10% (S), 14% (R); 3- to 6-fold variation; bound mainly to alpha1-acid glycoprotein.
- Vd about 4 L/kg (range 2-13); total CL about 0.095 L/min (range 0.02-2); t½ about 22 h (5-130); (R) about 40 h
  (Abstract; Table I).
- Table I includes Inturrisi 1987 (ref 51): Vc 0.16 ± 0.08 L/kg, Vβ 3.6 ± 1.2 L/kg, t½ 23 ± 12 h.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- MEAC for analgesia: none stated. TDM targets for MMT non-response: trough **400 µg/L (R,S)** or **250 µg/L (R)**
  plasma (Abstract; section 7). These are maintenance (withdrawal-suppression) targets in opioid-dependent
  patients, not analgesic thresholds.
- ke0 / tPeak: none.
- Potency: (R)-methadone about 50 times as potent as (S) in human analgesia (p. 1155, ref 27); receptor IC50s
  (R) 3.0 vs (S) 26.4 nmol/L at mu1.

## Covariates
Reviewed qualitatively: CYP3A4 (and 2D6, 2B6) activity, inducers (rifampicin, phenobarbital, phenytoin,
carbamazepine, nevirapine, efavirenz), inhibitors (fluconazole, paroxetine), alpha1-acid glycoprotein, urine pH.

## Caveats
Secondary source; values pooled from small studies, mostly in MMT patients whose opioid use may slow gastric
emptying (longer tmax than healthy subjects).
