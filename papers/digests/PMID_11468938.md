# Huitema 2001: cyclophosphamide autoinduction and thioTEPA inhibition model

## Study
- 34 patients (4 men, 30 women; median age 46, range 16-58), high-dose CTC (n=17) or "tiny" CTC (n=17), first courses only. Normal cardiac, renal, hepatic, haematological and pulmonary function required (PDF p. 4, printed p. 214).
- Regimen, 4 consecutive days: cyclophosphamide (CP) 1500 mg/m2/day over 1 h, then carboplatin 400 mg/m2/day over 1 h, then thioTEPA 120 mg/m2/day as two 30-min infusions 12 h apart. tCTC is two thirds of that dose. In 3 patients the order was reversed on one day.
- Plasma sampling before each day's infusions and at 30, 60, 90, 120, 150, 165, 180, 210, 285, 390 and 660 min on day 1 and one of days 2-4; one more sample about 22 h after the last CP.
- Assays: CP and thioTEPA by GC (LLQ CP 0.2 uM, thioTEPA 0.03 uM); 4-hydroxy-CP (HCP) by HPLC-UV (LLQ 0.2 uM); phosphoramide mustard (PM) by HPLC-UV (LLQ 0.2 uM), PM in only the last 7 patients.
- NONMEM V, first-order estimation, proportional IIV, combined error. thioTEPA fitted first (two compartment), its individual Bayesian estimates then drove the CP/HCP model, then PM.

## Model and parameters
- CP: one compartment (VCP) with a noninducible clearance (CLnon) and an inducible clearance (CLind) that forms HCP. CLind is proportional to the amount of a hypothetical enzyme, set to 1 at t=0.
- Autoinduction: the total enzyme amount rises at a zero-order rate kenz while CP is present (an on-off Emax switch with IC50 fixed very low). No enzyme elimination could be estimated (Table IV).
- thioTEPA: reversible, concentration-dependent inactivation of the active enzyme (kinh x thioTEPA concentration), reactivation at kreact. About 80% of the active enzyme is inhibited after each thioTEPA infusion; reactivation half-life 6.5 h (derived by the authors from kreact 0.107/h; ln2/0.107 = 6.5 h).
- HCP: one compartment, first-order elimination kHCP; PM: one compartment, first-order formation from HCP and elimination.
- Volumes of HCP and PM fixed at 1 L, so only apparent clearances were estimated (PDF p. 6).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No pharmacodynamic end point (no effect-site, no ke0, no MEAC, no potency ratio). The "pharmacodynamic" content is enzyme induction and inhibition, which acts on the formation clearance of HCP. In vitro, thioTEPA IC50 against CP activation in human liver microsomes was 1-40 uM, quoted from reference 17 (PDF p. 3). Not an opioid paper.

## Covariates
None tested or reported; no body-size term. Doses were per m2 but the model is in absolute L and L/h.

## Caveats
- Not formally validated; bootstrap impractical (PDF p. 16, printed p. 226).
- PM data from 7 patients only, and the PM model is described as not completely satisfactory.
- HCP trough concentrations were near the assay LLQ.
- Short infusions, 4-day schedule: no induced steady state could be seen, so enzyme elimination was not estimable. The decline of induction after treatment was not quantified.
- Normal organ function required; 30 of 34 were women with breast, germ-cell or ovarian cancer.
