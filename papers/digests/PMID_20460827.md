# Suzuki 2010: target-mediated (5alpha-reductase) binding model of finasteride PK and serum DHT

## Study
Modelling study of published/sponsor mean data, no new subjects. PK fitted to mean plasma profiles of healthy male
volunteers given oral finasteride 0.2 mg or 1 mg once daily for 17 days (day 1, n = 12; day 17, n = 11; Banyu interview
form and PMDA Propecia review, refs 6 and 10). PD fitted to mean serum DHT after single oral doses of 5, 10, 20, 50 and
100 mg (Ohtawa 1991, ref 7; n = 6 to 12 per dose, Table 1). Age, weight, assay not given in this paper. Fitting by
nonlinear least squares in MLAB on mean data (naive pooled; no population/IIV). Molecular weight 372.55 used for
nmol conversion (PDF p. 2, printed p. 209).

Table 1 (PDF p. 2, p. 209; mean, single oral dose, healthy volunteers, from refs 6 and 7):

Introduction (p. 208): AUC on day 17 of 0.2 mg/day is about 4.7-fold day 1; unchanged with 1 mg/day. In vitro IC50
500 nM (5alphaR1) and 4.2 nM (5alphaR2).

## Model and parameters
One-compartment, first-order absorption (ka) and elimination (ke) of drug unbound to enzyme, plus a target-binding
compartment: free drug binds 5alphaR (taken as 5alphaR2) 1:1 with kon and koff, total enzyme Etot (Fig. 1; eqs 1-3,
PDF p. 2):
- dCc/dt = ka·F·Xg/Vc + koff·XcE/Vc − ke·Cc − kon·Cc·(Etot − XcE)
- dXcE/dt = kon·Cc·Vc·(Etot − XcE) − koff·XcE
- Xg in nmol, Cc in nM (free of enzyme, not free of plasma protein), XcE and Etot in nmol. F fixed at 0.8 (ref 11, Steiner 1996).
Binding-to-5alphaR1 neglected in the PK. Irreversible model (koff = 0) failed to converge (p. 212).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: serum DHT as % of baseline (indirect-response, inhibition of production). Eq 6 (PDF p. 3):
d[DHT]/dt = Kin·F2·(1 − Φ2) + Kin·(1 − F2)·(1 − Φ1) − kout·[DHT], Kin = 100·kout; Φ2 = XcE/Etot (eq 4);
Φ1 = Cc/(Cc + Ki1) (eq 5). (The text layer renders Φ as "F", colliding with F2; read from page image.)
- Ki1 220 nM total; corrected for 15% free fraction, Ki1,f = 33.1 nM (Discussion p. 212), vs ~300 nM in vitro.
- Ki2,f = koff/kon/Vc corrected for binding = 0.00129 nM (derived check: 0.0185/0.0293/73.7 × 0.15 = 0.00129), vs 2-7 nM in cell lines.
- Simulations (Figs 6, 7): single 0.2-5 mg gives almost the same DHT fall; 14-day dosing of 0.01, 0.05, 0.2 and 5 mg
  lowers DHT about 10, 30, 60 and 60%. 5alphaR2 almost saturated from 1 mg; 5alphaR1 inhibition negligible ≤ 1 mg.
- No ke0, no effect compartment, no EC50 for a clinical end point. Not an opioid.

## Covariates
None (mean-data fit, no IIV).

## Caveats
- Fitted to published means, not individual data; "SD" ranges are on estimates from MLAB, not population variability.
- Etot is a fitted amount with no independent biochemical check (authors say so, p. 212).
- Ki2,f differs from in vitro values by about 3 orders of magnitude; authors attribute it to assay conditions.
- PD model is fitted to single-dose 5-100 mg data and then used to simulate 0.01-5 mg repeated dosing (extrapolation).
- Healthy male volunteers; ethnicity, age and weight are not reported in this paper.
