# Armstrong 1982: arterial-venous nitroglycerin gradient during IV infusion

PDF pages 1-4 = printed pages 1273-1276.

## Study
- 20 patients with congestive heart failure (13 M, 7 F; mean age 61, range 43-84). The summary says "chronic", but
  Methods (PDF p1) say 11 had heart failure complicating acute myocardial infarction within 48 h and 9 had chronic
  failure (8 atherosclerotic, 1 cardiomyopathy). Weight not reported.
- GTN 100 ug/mL in D5W (glass), from 50 mL glass syringes through Teflon tubing by Harvard pump (no loss, unpublished
  assay), into an arm vein. Start 15-20 ug/min, increased by 50% every 10-15 min until wedge pressure fell by >= 25% or
  the rate exceeded 10x the start ("peak rate"); peak rate held >= 15 min. Peak rates 15-440 ug/min, mean 120.7 +/- 156
  (Table 1). Infusion duration 265 +/- 9 min (as printed), total dose 13.0 +/- 16.8 mg (PDF p3).
- At the peak rate, simultaneous plasma samples from the pulmonary artery (Swan-Ganz), radial artery, and a peripheral
  vein of the arm not infused (the arm carrying the radial cannula). Arterial samples at 0.5, 1, 2, 5, 10 min after the
  stop for t1/2 (semilog plot).
- Assay: GLC (detector not stated; dinitrobenzene internal standard, double hexane extraction), plasma, lower limit 0.3 ng/mL, CV 12% at
  0.3, 7% at 5, 5% at 10 ng/mL.
- PK: non-compartmental, CL = rate / Css; Vd = CL x t1/2 / 0.693 (venous Vd assumes the arterial t1/2).

## Model and parameters
Table 1 (PDF p2, rendered), means +/- SD, n = 20 (PA n = 19, patient 5 missing; t1/2 n = 17):
PA 29.8 +/- 52.8, radial arterial 25.1 +/- 48.4, peripheral venous 7.3 +/- 15.4 ng/mL (text p3 prints the venous SD
as 14.3); AV extraction 60.8 +/- 27.2%; arterial t1/2 1.4 +/- 0.8 min (range 0.3-3.7).
Within patients, arterial 83 +/- 19% and venous 36 +/- 30% of PA; pulmonary extraction 17.4 +/- 19.1% (PDF p3).
AV extraction unrelated to arterial concentration, duration or total dose (r = 0.22, 0.10, 0.15).
Rate vs concentration r = 0.84 (PA), 0.81 (venous), 0.74 (arterial).

Table 2 (PDF p3, rendered): arterial CL 12.0 +/- 8.9 L/min (range 2.5-30.4), arterial Vd 23.0 +/- 21.5 L (n = 17);
venous CL 39.1 +/- 39.3 L/min, venous Vd 77.0 +/- 79.2 L. Arterial < venous (p < 0.001 CL, < 0.01 Vd).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point was haemodynamic titration (wedge pressure fall >= 25%). At peak rate (PDF p2): MAP 90 +/- 21 to 83 +/- 17
mmHg, PCWP 27 +/- 6 to 20 +/- 6, RAP 12 +/- 4 to 8 +/- 4, TPR 24.3 to 20.8 units, CI 2.0 to 2.2 L/min/m2, HR unchanged.
No concentration-effect model, EC50, ke0 or hysteresis. Not an opioid: no MEAC or potency ratio.

## Covariates
None analysed.

## Caveats
- Concentrations span two orders of magnitude; means are dominated by patients 16-20 (220-440 ug/min). The ratio of
  means (7.3/25.1 = 0.29, derived) differs from the mean within-patient ratio (1 - 0.608 = 0.39, derived).
- Patient 4's venous concentration was 0.0 (below 0.3 ng/mL) yet Table 2 prints venous CL 0.0 and includes it in the
  39.1 mean (derived: sum/20 = 39.07); the true value is undefined/very large, so the venous mean is understated.
- Patient 6 had a negative AV extraction (-6.3%); patient 19 low concentrations at 440 ug/min (delivery suspected).
- Steady state judged haemodynamically; mixed acute-MI and chronic failure population; weights not given.
