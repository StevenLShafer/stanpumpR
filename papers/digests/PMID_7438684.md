# Warren 1980: carbamazepine induction of ethosuximide clearance in healthy men

## Study
- 6 healthy men, 22-44 y, weight 72.3 +/- 11.5 kg (individuals 61.4, 64.4, 70.5, 72.7, 70.7, 94.1 kg); no other drugs, controlled diet.
- Ethosuximide one 250 mg capsule twice daily for 55 doses (days 1-28); carbamazepine 200 mg each evening days 11-27.
- Sampling: 8 samples (0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12 h after morning dose) on days 10, 17, 21, 28; troughs days 11, 18, 22, 29; washout samples days 30-31.
- Assay: GC with flame ionization (Dill method), plasma, total.
- Analysis: noncompartmental. Css,avg = AUC(0-12)/12 h; CL = F.D/AUC with F assumed 1; ke from monoexponential fit days 28-31 (post-induction); Vd = F.D/(ke.AUC). Induction time course fitted to the Levy-Lai-Dumain exponential-clearance model (BMDX85).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None measured (healthy volunteers, kinetic study). No therapeutic range given.

## Covariates
None modelled. Carbamazepine raised clearance by about 20% (0.664 to 0.800 L/h, P < 0.05) and lowered Css by 17%; individual increases 0-55% (subject B none).

## Caveats
- n = 6 healthy men; F assumed 1; Vd and ke only measured post-induction, pre-induction t1/2 assumes Vd unchanged.
- Day 10 is about 4.5 half-lives into twice-daily dosing (derived), so "steady state" may be about 95% complete.
- Text-layer misprints: abstract SD 0.0154 (text 0.154), Vd 49.7 vs 49.6, Discussion's "preinduction t1/2 (44.6)".
