# Davies 1977: clonidine IV and oral PK and concentration-effect

## Study
Five healthy normotensive men, 29-35 yr, 62.1-82.6 kg (Table I, PDF p3). Crossover, two days in random order: 300 ug clonidine HCl (258.9 ug base; 3.13-4.17 ug/kg base) IV over 10 min, or 300 ug orally (3 x 100 ug tablets). IV samples at 0 (end infusion), 5, 10, 20, 30, 45, 60 min and 1.5-24 h; oral samples 0.5-24 h. Plasma assay by GC-MS with clonidine-d4 internal standard, limit of detection 0.15 ng/mL. Postinfusion data fitted per subject to a biexponential (Eq 1-3, infusion-corrected). Effects: BP (Arteriosonde), heart rate, 100 mm sedation VAS, salivary flow (g/min).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- No ke0, t1/2ke0, tPeak, EC50 or Emax is estimated. No hysteresis analysis; the discussion notes the maximum BP fall (26 +/- 2 / 16 +/- 3 mm Hg IV; 21 +/- 4 / 15 +/- 4 oral) occurs at 1.5-2 h after both routes (Fig 4).
- Sedation (VAS) and salivary flow track plasma concentration over 0-4 ng/mL; maximum sedation (sleep) at plasma 1.5-2 ng/mL and plateaus above it (Fig 7); maximum salivary reduction at about 1 ng/mL.
- BP fall relates to plasma concentration up to about 1.5-2 ng/mL, then attenuates at higher (early post-IV) concentrations (Fig 8, Subject 5), so the concentration-BP relation is non-monotone. Authors offer two explanations (delayed norepinephrine fall; peripheral alpha stimulation above 2 ng/mL) and say the data cannot choose.
- Not an opioid: no MEAC or potency ratio.

## Covariates
Dose and volumes per kg only; no covariate model.

## Caveats
n = 5 men, single dose, normotensive, one oral tablet product; interindividual alpha-phase variation large (t1/2 alpha 2.2-28.7 min) with sparse early sampling after a 10-min infusion; concentrations below ~0.15 ng/mL unmeasurable so late terminal data thin (no samples quantified beyond 24 h).
