# Glass 1997: Nomenclature for computer-assisted infusion devices (letter)

## Study
Correspondence (accepted 26 March 1997) from Glass (Duke), Glen (Zeneca), Kenny (Glasgow), Schüttler (Erlangen-Nuremberg), Shafer (Stanford).

## Model and parameters
No data. Recommendations:
- Lists earlier acronyms: CATIA (computer-assisted total intravenous anesthesia), TIAC (titration of intravenous agents by computer), CACI (computer-assisted continuous infusion), CCIP (computer-controlled infusion pump). Recommends **target-controlled infusion (TCI)** as the common term, because it does not imply the computer rather than the anesthesiologist controls depth, and commercial systems will not need an external computer.
- Notation: C_T target, C_CALC calculated (preferred over C_P "predicted", to avoid confusion with plasma), C_M measured. Site subscripts: Cp_T, Ce_T, Cp_M, Cp_CALC, Ce_CALC. A second subscript names the parameter set (e.g., Cp_CALC,MAITRE).
- Cp50_M remains the device-independent index; ideally Ce50_CALC or Ce90_CALC (with PK/PD model stated), Cp50_CALC/Cp90_CALC acceptable if the calculated plasma concentration was held long enough for effect-site equilibration.
