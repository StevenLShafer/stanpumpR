# Yamamoto 2019: alprazolam concentrations in a neonate with abstinence syndrome, with a PBPK simulation

**This digest is from the authors' accepted manuscript**, the only open-access version (filed by
`_build/unpaywall_fetch.py`). It has no journal pagination, and its tables and figures could have moved at proof,
so every page reference below is a **PDF page of the archived file**, not a printed page.

## Study
A single **case report** of one mother and her neonate, with a physiologically based pharmacokinetic (PBPK)
simulation. Not a pharmacokinetic study: there is no cohort, no control group and no fitted parameter set.

**The mother** (PDF p7): 30 years old, taking benzodiazepines for panic disorder. Alprazolam 2.4-3.2 mg/day in
mid-pregnancy and **2.4 mg/day in late pregnancy until the day of delivery**, plus lorazepam 0.5-1.0 mg/day as
needed in mid-pregnancy. The last alprazolam dose was **20 min before delivery**. Vaginal delivery at 40 weeks;
birth weight 3400 g; Apgar 9.

**The neonate** (PDF p7-p8): supplemental oxygen in an incubator for apnea at 2 h, Finnegan score 7; vomiting and
poor feeding from 9 h to day 2, treated with intravenous fluid; out of the incubator on day 3; **Finnegan score 0
by day 4**. Blood sampled right after birth and daily (Fig. 1A gives the times as 1, 23.5, 47, 71.5 and 97.5 h,
and day 100 for the mother). Alprazolam by LC-MS/MS (LSI Medience, Tokyo).

**The simulation** (PDF p6-p7): Simcyp population-based ADME simulator version 15, compound model for alprazolam.
For the mother, the "Sim-Pregnancy" population with age and height set to the patient's and hepatic and
gastrointestinal CYP3A4 and CYP3A5 abundances replaced by the "Sim-Japanese" values; oral dose 0.8 mg three times
a day. For the neonate, "Sim-Paediatrics" at 0 year, female, given **intravenously** at whatever dose made the peak
15.2 ng/mL, with enzyme abundance redefined over the period. A third simulation used "Japanese women" parameters
for the mother after delivery.

## Model and parameters
No pharmacokinetic parameters are estimated - no clearance, no volume, no population model. What the paper
supplies is three measured neonatal concentrations, one measured maternal concentration, one derived maternal
concentration, and two half-lives calculated from the neonatal points.

The authors report that the estimated maternal concentration at delivery, the neonatal concentrations at 24 and
48 h and the measured maternal day-100 concentration all fell within the Simcyp 90% prediction intervals. They
state they **could not successfully simulate the neonate's concentration at 72 h**, and suggest CYP3A4/3A5
maturation in this neonate was faster than the published values they used.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
The end point is the **Finnegan neonatal abstinence score**, 7 at birth falling to 0 by day 4, alongside the
clinical course (apnea to about 24 h, gastrointestinal symptoms to day 4). The claim is a parallel between the
falling concentration and the resolving symptoms - a visual, single-case association, with **no exposure-response
model, no EC50 fitted here, no ke0 and no hysteresis analysis**. The authors say explicitly that symptoms may
persist after the drug has gone and that further investigation is needed.

Two concentration benchmarks are **quoted from other papers**, not measured here (PDF p9):
- EC50 for sedation **27 ng/mL** and for stimulation **30 ng/mL** - in **rats** (their ref 5, Lau and
  Heatherington 1997).
- The lower threshold of the alprazolam therapeutic range "estimated as 2-25 ng/mL" (their ref 6, Jones and
  Holmgren 2013 - impaired drivers and forensic autopsies).

On that basis they argue the neonate's concentrations were high enough to be pharmacologically active.

## Covariates
None fitted. Ethnicity enters only as a Simcyp population choice (CYP3A4/3A5 abundances switched to the Japanese
set), and age and height were set to the patient's own values. Gestational age 40 weeks, birth weight 3400 g.

## Caveats
- **n = 1**, mother and neonate. A case report.
- **The maternal concentration at delivery is not measured.** It is back-calculated from the neonate's value
  through a **rat** fetal:maternal ratio of 0.72, which the authors list as a limitation.
- The mother also took lorazepam as needed in mid-pregnancy.
- The neonatal "dose" in the simulation is fictitious: an intravenous amount chosen to hit 15.2 ng/mL, since the
  real exposure was transplacental.
- Sim-Pregnancy and Sim-Paediatrics are derived from Caucasians; only the CYP3A4/3A5 abundances were switched to
  Japanese values, leaving placental and tissue volumes, other CYP activities and GFR unchanged - the authors'
  own stated limitation.
- The 72 h neonatal point could not be reproduced by the simulation.
- The two neonatal half-lives come from two concentration pairs each, on nominal 24 h intervals, while the
  recorded sampling times were 1, 23.5 and 47 h.
- **No pregnant-against-non-pregnant comparison is reported**, and no clearance, volume or exposure ratio.

  placental ratio, the postpartum dose is not stated, and n = 1. Under `docs/pregnancy.md` a change known only in
  direction is recorded, never applied, so the row's `applied` status is correct either way; the pair is worth
  recording in the row's notes as weak corroboration of the assumed direction. See the `[source]` question.
