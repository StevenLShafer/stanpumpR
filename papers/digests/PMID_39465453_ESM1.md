# Li 2024 (supplement): log-likelihood profiles for the norepinephrine population PK model

## Study
Supplement to the Li 2024 article (36 healthy volunteers, norepinephrine infusions awake and under
propofol-remifentanil anaesthesia; NONMEM). It holds no new data or tables, only figures (4 PDF pages):
- PDF p1: title page.
- PDF p2: **S1**, log-likelihood (OBJ) profiles of the eight thetas of the final model; **S2**, profiles of the
  five omegas.
- PDF p3: **S3**, profiles of the two sigmas; **S4**, profiles of the eight thetas of the "STM" model (the model with
  the electrical-stimulation correction factor that the article later removed).
- PDF p4: **S5**, spaghetti plots against time (min) by session (1 awake, 2 anaesthesia) of BIS, cardiac output
  (L/min), heart rate, mean arterial pressure, measured propofol (mcg/mL, about 0.5-9) and remifentanil (ng/mL,
  0-11) concentrations.

Each profile panel prints the 95% CI and an RSD (%). Point estimates are not printed; they can only be read
approximately from each panel's grey vertical line.

### Checks against the article's Table 3 (PDF p8, printed 1604; rendered)
- **Omegas agree.** Converting each S2 CI with Table 3's footnote formula, sqrt(exp(omega) - 1), gives
  (derived) CL 7.9-14.2%, Vc 33.9-63.6%, Vp 32.2-57.6%, prod 24.8-41.3%, CPROP~CL 68.3-1515%. Table 3 prints
  7.9-14.2, 33.8-63.6, 32.2-57.6, 24.8-41.3 and 68.3-1515.1.
- **Lag CI agrees and shows the unit.** S1's ALAG1 CI of 0.225-0.569, times 60, gives 13.5-34.1 s (derived).
  Table 3 prints the lag CI as 13.485-34.131 s, so ALAG1 is in minutes and 13.7 s is about 0.228 min. Table 3's
  own footnote says the ALAG1 CI is not reported, yet the table prints one. The article also says the lag search
  was restricted to 10-16 s. The profile's upper bound of 34 s lies outside that range.
- **Theta CIs do not agree.** Table 3 prints these lower-upper bounds, and S1 prints different ones:

  | Parameter | Table 3 | S1 |
  |---|---|---|
  | CL | 2.073-2.201 | 2.035-2.231 |
  | Vc | 1.988-2.850 | 2.005-2.853 |
  | Q | 0.534-0.729 | 0.540-0.727 |
  | Vp | 3.058-4.196 | 3.047-4.160 |
  | Prod | 487.905-545.814 | 447.140-557.111 |
  | CPROP | -4.312 to -2.780 | -4.354 to -2.765 |
  | AGE | -0.598 to -0.091 | -0.598 to -0.087 |

  The differences are small except for Prod's lower bound: 487.9 against 447.1. Neither document says which run
  is which, so the cause is unresolved. The point estimates are not in question.
- **Sigmas.** Table 3 says within-subject CV = sqrt(estimate). sqrt of S3's CIs gives 16.0-17.8% (proportional)
  and 27.3-38.1 (additive) (derived). Table 3 prints 15.7-17.6 and 27.2-38.1, so the proportional bounds differ
  slightly. The additive term is an SD in concentration units, not a %.
- **Rounding.** Table 3 prints CL 2.1, Vc 2.4, Q 0.6 and Vp 3.6 to one decimal. The S1 minima sit near 2.13 and
  0.63 (my reading), and the midpoint of Table 3's own CL CI is 2.137 (derived). So the printed 2.1 and 0.6 are
  probably rounded down from about 2.13 and 0.63. That is about 1.5% on CL and 5% on Q.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. The supplement has no PD model, ke0 or concentration-effect relation. S5 shows only raw time courses of
BIS, cardiac output, HR, MAP and measured propofol and remifentanil concentrations.

## Covariates
Profiles of the article's covariate coefficients: propofol concentration on CL (theta7) and age on CL (theta8).
The equations are in the article (Eq. 2 and 4), not here.

## Caveats
- The figures carry no table of point estimates. Every central value above is read from a plot.
- The ALAG1 profile is bimodal: the lag is poorly identified, as the article's footnote says.
- The CIs printed here differ from Table 3's for the thetas (above). The supplement and the table may come from
  different profiling runs; nothing in either says so.
