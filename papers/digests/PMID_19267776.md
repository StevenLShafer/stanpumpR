# Delavenne 2009: acenocoumarol PK-PD and amoxicillin-clavulanate interaction

## Study
- Eight healthy male Caucasian volunteers; median age 23 y (20-29), height 178.5 cm (164-186), weight 65 kg (60-76), BSA 1.8 m2 (1.7-2), baseline PTr 89.5% (72-100) (Table II, PDF p5).
- Open, sequential (not cross-over) design: day 1 single oral 8 mg acenocoumarol; amoxicillin 1 g + clavulanic acid 250 mg orally twice daily for 7 days (days 3-9); day 8 a second 8 mg acenocoumarol dose with the antibiotic (Figure 1, p2-3).
- Samples at 0, 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2, 3, 5, 7, 13, 24, 31, 48 h after each dose (p2). Plasma acenocoumarol by HPLC (reversed-phase C18), LLOQ 15 ng/mL, ULOQ 500 ng/mL, inter-assay CV <10% (p2-3). The assay is achiral: racemic (total) acenocoumarol, enantiomers not separated.
- PD end point: prothrombin time ratio PTr = PTcontrol/PTpatient, expressed in % (Neoplastin reagent), so PTr falls as anticoagulation rises (p3). INR not used.
- Structural PK model identified on a pooled individual-data meta-analysis of three healthy-volunteer studies (this study n=8 first period only; a pharmacogenetic study n=23 given 4 mg day 0, 4 mg day 1 (Verstuyft ref 14); Mismetti PAGE 1998 n=8 given 12 mg) (Table I, p3), then applied to both periods of this study.
- NONMEM VI, FOCE with interaction; bootstrap (100) for precision; VPC (500 replicates).
- Non-compartmental: AUC0-inf geometric mean ratio with/without antibiotic 1.18 (90% CI 1.08-1.29), outside 0.8-1.25; PTr area-above-curve ratio 1.14 (0.99-1.31). Per-subject AUC increase mean 20.6 +/- 16.9%, AAC change 3.6 +/- 48.7% (Table III, p5).

## Model and parameters
Two-compartment, first-order absorption with lag, log-normal IIV on Cl/F, V2/F, V3/F with Cl-V2 covariance; proportional residual error (p5). All parameters are apparent (/F) for racemic acenocoumarol. Values from Table IV (PDF p6, printed 132), confirmed on the rendered page; SE given as CV%.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: PTr (%) on plasma, 0-48 h after single 8 mg doses.
- Model (equations 1-2, PDF p4, confirmed on image): dCF/dt = kin*(1 - Cp^gamma/(Cp^gamma + C50^gamma)) - kout*CF; PTr = PTr0 * CF^lambda/(CF50^lambda + CF^lambda). Indirect response, inhibition of clotting-factor production, nested in an Emax link to PTr. gamma and lambda fixed to 1.
- kin = kout = 0.175 /h (derived: clotting-factor turnover t1/2 = ln2/0.175 = 3.96 h).
- C50 1.69 mg/L, CF50 0.0510 mg/L as printed (unit doubtful).
- No covariate (including the antibiotic) affected PD.
- No ke0, tPeak, therapeutic concentration or potency ratio reported. Not an opioid paper.

## Covariates
- Body weight on V2/F: V2 = 24.5 x [Wt/median(Wt)] (linear; reference median weight not stated).
- Amoxicillin + clavulanic acid on Cl/F: x 0.875 (Table IV); text says a 15% decrease (see open_questions).
- BSA was tested (power form shown in Methods) but is not in the final model.

## Caveats
- n = 8, young healthy men, single doses; not a cross-over, so a period effect cannot be separated from the interaction and inter-occasion variability could not be estimated (authors, p7).
- Racemic, achiral assay; R and S enantiomers not separated.
- Absorption poorly estimated (Ka IIV 79; Discussion says >90%).
- Internal inconsistencies: 0.875 vs 15% vs "increased"; mg/L labels on concentrations that are evidently ng/mL in Figure 3.
- Authors call this the first PK/PD model for acenocoumarol.

## Verification against stanpumpR
- **inst/help/drugs/acenocoumarol.md:40** says: two-compartment, indirect-response, body-weight covariate, corroborating the structure. Paper: two-compartment first-order with lag (Table IV), indirect response with inhibition of production (eq 1), weight on V2/F. **Matches.** Notes: (a) "independent" is true of the data set in part only -- the meta-analysis pooled a 23-subject Verstuyft pharmacogenetic study (Table I, ref 14: two 4 mg doses 24 h apart, the same design as the library's Verstuyft 2012 source, R/drugs_acenocoumarol.R:15-16, so the data may overlap), and Delavenne is a co-author of Verstuyft 2012 (R/drugs_acenocoumarol.R:11); (b) the PD end point here is PTr, not INR, and the link to PTr is an Emax on clotting factor, whereas the library turns over 1/INR (inst/help/drugs/acenocoumarol.md:15-19).
- **Comparison with the library model (R/drugs_acenocoumarol.R:26-34, 130-135; not citing this paper).** The library models S-acenocoumarol only, from half of each racemic dose (CL/F 15.3 L/h, V1 24.7 L x WT/69, ka 0.936 /h, lag 0.269 h). Delavenne's values are for racemic total acenocoumarol, so CL is not comparable: 4.08 L/h racemate vs 15.3 L/h S-enantiomer (the S-form 3.75x higher). Neither this paper nor the library header states enantiomer-specific clearances, so the card does not attribute the gap; it is the size expected only if the S-enantiomer is cleared much faster than the R, which this paper cannot show. V2 is close (24.5 vs 24.7 L), both with linear weight on the central volume (Delavenne's reference weight unstated vs 69 kg). Delavenne's ka (4.04 /h) and lag (0.404 h) are faster/longer than the library's; absorption was poorly estimated here. The library's C50 (0.0023 mg/L, S-enantiomer, INR) is not comparable with Delavenne's C50 (racemate, PTr, unit doubtful).
