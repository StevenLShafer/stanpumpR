# Cirincione 2018: apixaban population PK in nonvalvular atrial fibrillation

PDF page = printed page - 727 (PDF p. 1 is printed 728).

## Study
Pooled NONMEM 7.1 (FOCE-I) analysis of 12 studies: 8 phase I (healthy, incl. renal-impairment, age/sex, body-weight
and race studies; single doses 2.5-50 mg and multiple doses up to 25 mg bid), APPRAISE-1 and Japan ACS phase II
(acute coronary syndrome), Japan NVAF phase II, and an ARISTOTLE subset (Table 1, PDF p. 3). Two stages: stage 1 on
phase I/II data, then re-estimated with phase III (updated stage 1) and extended (stage 2). Stage 2 dataset: 11,968
concentrations from 4,385 subjects (270 phase I, 3,071 NVAF, 1,044 ACS); ARISTOTLE contributed 2,932 concentrations
from 2,932 subjects (Results, PDF p. 4). Table 2 (PDF p. 5), total population: age median 68 (18-94) y; weight
median 81.4 (32-198.2) kg; Cockcroft-Gault CrCL mean 85.2 (SD 34.5), median 79.3 (11.9-319.7) mL/min (NVAF only
18.8-279.8); 70% male; 14.9% Asian. Assay LC-MS, LLOQ 1 ng/mL (PDF p. 2). Total plasma apixaban.

## Model and parameters
Two-compartment, first-order absorption and elimination, apparent (/F) parameters. Total CL/F split into renal
(CLR/F) and non-renal (CLNR/F). Eq. 1 (PDF p. 2, stage 1 base model): CLR/F x (cCrCL/80)^theta1 when cCrCL <= 150
mL/min and CLR/F x (150/80)^theta2 above it (FLAG = 1 when cCrCL > 150), i.e. a 150 mL/min breakpoint, stated as
avoiding Cockcroft-Gault limits at extremes of weight. The base model was then simplified so that CLR/F is
proportional to cCrCL (PDF p. 5); Table 3 lists cCrCL (theta7) as "1 FIXED". The main text does not say whether the
150 mL/min breakpoint was kept or dropped in the final models.

Stage 2 dose-dependent relative bioavailability (Eq. 4, PDF p. 6), replacing the stage 1 Imax/ED50/gamma form (Eq. 2)
because of parameter correlations > 0.95: Frel = 1 - I50 x ((Dose - 2.5)/47.5)^gamma for doses > 2.5 mg,
I50 = e^theta9/(1 + e^theta9), I50 the fall at 50 mg.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None modelled. No ke0, no exposure-response, no therapeutic range. Not an opioid.

## Covariates
cCrCL (Cockcroft-Gault, linear on CLR/F), age and sex on CLNR/F; Asian race, NVAF, ACS and strong/moderate
CYP3A4/P-gp inhibitors on total CL/F; weight, NVAF and ACS on Vc/F; evening dosing on ka (43% lower). Strong
inducers were tested and dropped (small, poorly estimated). Predicted AUCss rises vs normal renal function: 9%, 28%,
55% at cCrCL 65, 40, 15 mL/min (PDF pp. 8-9); Vc/F 23.3% lower at 50 kg and 22% higher at 90 kg (PDF p. 6).

## Caveats
- ARISTOTLE contributed one sample per subject; strong inhibitors had only 3 subjects, so inhibitor categories were
  collapsed; authors defer to phase I interaction studies (ketoconazole +99%, diltiazem +40% AUC; PDF p. 9).
- IIV was placed on microconstants after CL/Vc IIV correlation near 1.
- Frel fitted from doses up to 50 mg (phase I).
- "Model assessment" cites Table 2 for the parameter estimates; Table 3 holds them (source slip, PDF p. 6).
- Abstract and text agree on the headline values checked (55% severe renal; 27% lower with dose reduction; Asian
  <15%).
