# Rodrigues 2017: parent-metabolite population PK of oxcarbazepine and MHD in children

## Study
- Ancillary PK data from an add-on efficacy trial (previously analysed non-compartmentally by Rey et al.). 31 children with
  inadequately controlled partial-onset and/or generalised seizures on 1-3 unchanged AEDs; 13 girls, 18 boys; median age
  8.08 y (2.25-12.5), median weight 23 kg (12.7-56) (PDF p4 / printed 2698).
- Single oral dose of oxcarbazepine suspension, 5 mg/kg (n = 14) or 15 mg/kg (n = 17), after an overnight fast. Samples at
  about 0, 1, 2, 4, 6, 8, 12, 24, 36, 48 h; 277 OXC and 279 MHD concentrations.
- Co-medication (Table 1, PDF p5): carbamazepine 19 (61.3%), vigabatrin 14, clobazam 8, phenytoin 5, valproate 4,
  clonazepam 3, lamotrigine 3, diazepam 2, phenobarbital 2, ethosuximide 1, progabide 1. 24 of 31 took at least one
  enzyme-inducing AED (EIAED: carbamazepine, phenobarbital, phenytoin).
- Assay: non-enantioselective HPLC of total OXC and MHD in plasma (S and R not distinguished); LLOQ 0.05 mg/L (OXC),
  0.1 mg/L (MHD); 32% of OXC and 11.5% of MHD observations BLQ, handled by M3.
- Monolix 4.3.2, SAEM. F fixed to 1 (from Flesch 2011's 0.99), fraction metabolised to MHD fixed to 1. Simulations
  (NONMEM 7.3) at 10-50 kg, 10-90 mg/kg/day bid.

## Model and parameters
OXC: two compartments, first-order absorption without lag, first-order elimination entirely to MHD. MHD: one compartment,
first-order elimination, with first-order back-transformation of MHD to OXC (KBT). A first-pass model put 5.4% of the dose
directly into MHD but was not retained. IIV on all parameters except Ka; proportional error for OXC, combined for MHD.

Final ("empirical allometry") model (equations PDF p5 / printed 2699; Table 3 PDF p8 / printed 2702), all apparent (/F):
- Ka = 1.83 /h
- CL_OXC/F = 140 × (WT/70)^0.798 L/h
- Vc_OXC/F = 337 × (WT/70)^2.4 L
- Q_OXC/F = 62.5 L/h; Vp_OXC/F = 60.7 L (not weight-scaled)
- CL_MHD/F = 4.11 × e^(−0.257 × MED) × (WT/70)^0.549 L/h, MED = 1 when NO EIAED is taken (Table 3 labels the covariate
  "absence of EIAEDs"). So 4.11 L/h/70 kg with EIAEDs and 3.18 without (prose, PDF p7); the abstract states EIAEDs raise
  MHD clearance by 29.3% (derived check: e^0.257 = 1.293).
- Vc_MHD/F = 54.8 × (WT/70)^1.09 L
- KBT = 0.0622 /h

Fixed-exponent alternative (0.75 / 1, Table 3): Ka 1.81, CL_OXC 116, Vc_OXC 55.7, Q 119/70 kg, Vp 235/70 kg, CL_MHD 5.1,
Vc_MHD 47.7, KBT 0.0476, nEIAED effect −0.276. Not chosen: it did worse on external evaluation against Li et al. TDM troughs.

Simulated steady-state MHD troughs (Table 5, PDF p10, median [95% CI], mg/L; doses 10, 20, 40, 60, 90 mg/kg/day), e.g. at 40 mg/kg/day:
10 kg without EIAEDs 8.9 [2.5-18.1], 50 kg without 25.1 [13.5-43.7], 10 kg with EIAEDs 5.9 [1.1-13.7]. AUC0-12 in Table 4.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No concentration-effect model. The target is the ILAE 2008 MHD trough reference range **3-35 mg/L** (total, plasma,
racemic MHD), with >35 mg/L taken as the toxicity threshold (introduction, methods); a narrower 15-35 mg/L (May 2003) is
discussed. The authors note the MHD concentration-effect relation is not well established. Dose conclusions: 50 kg children
without EIAEDs need about 20-30 mg/kg/day; 10 kg children with EIAEDs about 90 mg/kg/day for >95% probability in range.

## Covariates
Body weight (power, reference 70 kg) on CL_OXC, Vc_OXC, CL_MHD, Vc_MHD with estimated exponents (0.798, 2.4, 0.549, 1.09).
EIAED co-medication on CL_MHD only (none on CL_OXC). Age (power or maturation function) did not improve the fixed-exponent
model; separate CL_MHD exponents for <6 and >6 y were similar (0.541, 0.498). Sex not retained.

## Caveats
- 31 children, single dose, oral suspension only; steady-state predictions are extrapolated (autoinduction not an issue for
  OXC, but chronic dosing not observed).
- The Vc_OXC exponent of 2.4 is far from theory; the authors attribute it to sparse distribution-phase sampling and say the
  model should not be used under 2 years. Applicable to 2-12 y, non-obese, non-malnourished children.
- Racemic MHD, not enantiomers; total concentration.
- KBT and CL_MHD/F are identifiable only with F fixed and Fm fixed to 1.
