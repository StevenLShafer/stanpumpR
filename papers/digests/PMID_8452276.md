# Inagaki 1993: midazolam reduction of halothane MAC

## Study
Fifty ASA 1-2 women (23-50 yr, 42-66 kg) for hysterectomy, no premedication, randomised to four groups (Table 1, PDF p3: n = 12, 13, 12, 13; age 37-39 yr; weight 51.4-53.2 kg; height 154.7-157.3 cm). Group A: no midazolam. Groups B/C/D: IV bolus 0.1/0.2/0.4 mg/kg then infusion 1/2/4 ug/kg/min. Halothane by mask, intubation without adjuvant drugs, end-tidal halothane held at least 15 min. Movement at surgical incision 30-50 min after the infusion started (observer blinded) gave MAC by the Dixon up-and-down method (20% steps). Arterial blood every 10 min for 60 min and at incision; SERUM midazolam and 1-/4-hydroxy metabolites by gas chromatography (8-methoxylamoxapine internal standard; sensitivity 5 ng/mL; intra- and inter-assay variation <3%). Total serum concentration; free drug not measured. A preliminary group of 15 women set the doses.

## Model and parameters
No PK model fitted. Fig 1 (PDF p2, n = 5 per point): serum midazolam about 120, 235, 570 ng/mL (groups B, C, D), steady between 30 and 50 min. At incision (PDF p3, mean +/- SD): 134 +/- 26, 250 +/- 24, 539 +/- 98 ng/mL. Metabolites <5 ng/mL throughout.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: movement or not at skin incision (halothane MAC). MAC reduction vs control 39.7%, 51.3%, 70.5% at mean serum midazolam 134, 250, 539 ng/mL (PDF p3 discussion). Relationship exponential and saturable (the authors' fit equation is garbled in the text layer; not recorded). Authors calculate that 172 ng/mL corresponds to 0.44 MAC halothane (derived by them). Other studies cited for context, not this study's data: EEG deep hypnosis >=500 ng/mL (Lauven), sedation about 400 ng/mL (Crevoisier), satisfactory intra-operative hypnosis >250-300, verbal response 150-200, post-operative sedation 100-150 ng/mL (Persson); dog enflurane MAC reduction 36.9% at 355 and 50.4% at 610 ng/mL (Hall). No ke0, t1/2 ke0, tPeak or hysteresis reported (sampling at near-steady state). Not an opioid paper; no MEAC or potency ratio.

## Covariates
None analysed (women only, narrow age and weight range).

## Caveats
n = 12-13 per group, one up-and-down MAC per group; group-mean concentrations. Serum, total drug. Women 23-50 yr only.
