# Melhem 2013: population PK of hydrocodone from extended-release capsules (Zohydro ER)

## Study
- Pooled 7 Zogenix/Elan studies (5 phase 1, 2 phase 2; Table 1): healthy adults (single 20 mg, 50 mg with 0% alcohol
  arm only), bunionectomy patients (10-40 mg single), osteoarthritis patients (10-40 mg BID escalation to steady state,
  21 days), normal-hepatic-function subjects from a hepatic study, and a renal-impairment study (20 mg). Dose range
  10-50 mg of hydrocodone bitartrate ER (SODAS bead capsule, 12-h product).
- 220 subjects, 4714 plasma hydrocodone concentrations; 95% had >= 11 samples; ~90% of samples within 24 h of a dose.
  Demographic summary (weight, age, sex distribution) not tabulated in the text.
- LC-MS/MS (method improved across trials); BLQ excluded.
- NONMEM 7.1.2, ADVAN6, FOCE-I; log-transformed data with additive log error. Covariates screened: BSA (Gehan-George),
  CLCR (Cockcroft-Gault), IBW, age, height, weight; forward p < 0.01, backward p < 0.001; pcVPC.

## Model and parameters
Two-compartment mammillary disposition, linear elimination; absorption by two sequential first-order processes: the
first (fraction F1) delayed through 4 transit compartments (ktr), the second with a conventional lag time (Fig. 1,
Eqs. 3-10; Eq. 5 as printed contains a duplicated ka2 term, an apparent typesetting error). Table 2:

Covariate equations (p. 912, Eqs. 11-12): CL/F = 64.4 x (CLCR [mL/min/1.73 m2] / 91.48)^0.223;
V1/F = 714 x (BSA [m2] / 1.87)^0.486. Paper: CLCR 15 vs 100 mL/min/1.73 m2 gives about 53% higher exposure.
Absorption half-lives 18 min (ka1) and 96 min (ka2) (Discussion).
Derived: disposition eigenvalues 0.0916 and 0.00594 /h (t½ 7.6 h and 117 h).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Opioid audit: no PD measured. No MEAC, no ke0 / t½ke0 / tPeak, no hysteresis, no potency ratio. The discussion
states that hydromorphone formation is small (3.5% of dose in urine as hydromorphone, citing Otton) and that hydrocodone
plasma exposure is > 25 times hydromorphone's (data on file), so the authors treat hydrocodone as the active entity.

## Covariates
CLCR on CL/F (power 0.223, reference 91.48 mL/min/1.73 m2) and BSA on V1/F (power 0.486, reference 1.87 m2). No others
significant after these. Authors judge neither warrants a priori dose adjustment.

## Caveats
Industry-funded (Zogenix); ER product only, so apparent parameters (F unknown); complex absorption specific to the
SODAS formulation; CLD/F small with V2/F poorly estimated (SEM 34.8%); assay cross-validation across studies not
reported; demographic ranges not given in the text.
