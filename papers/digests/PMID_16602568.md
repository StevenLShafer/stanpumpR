# Zikov 2006: WAVCNS, a wavelet-based EEG depth-of-anaesthesia index

## Study
Methods paper plus an observational comparison of the wavelet-based anesthetic value (WAV_CNS) with BIS v3.4. Index derivation used five EEG reference sets: awake (15 min, 5 healthy adults), light REM sleep (15 min, 3 subjects), general anaesthesia (18 min, 6 arthroscopy patients), deep/delta (9 min, 5 patients) and isoelectric (5 min, 2 patients) (p.3 of PDF). Clinical study: 25 ambulatory knee-surgery patients, 5 rejected (3 incomplete data, 1 poor signal, 1 event-log errors), 20 analysed; propofol induction, then sevoflurane or desflurane with N2O or propofol infusion, opioids as needed, no muscle relaxants, LMA. Raw EEG taken from a BIS A-1050 at 128 S/s.

## Model and parameters
Not a PK model. Algorithm: 1-s epochs, detrend and RMS-normalise, stationary wavelet transform, Daubechies #6 filter on the 32-64 Hz band, PDF of coefficients compared with awake and isoelectric reference PDFs, scaled 100 (awake) to 0 (isoelectric), smoothed by a 30-s average (to match BIS) or a second-order IIR low-pass (0.02 Hz cutoff, damping 1). Dynamics are expressed as a linear time-invariant transfer function (Eq. 24; coefficients not legible in the text layer).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: EEG-derived index; no drug concentrations, ke0, EC50 or hysteresis are reported (the authors suggest tuning against plasma concentration in future work). Findings: steady-state WAV vs BIS correlation 0.969 over 13 h of steady-state data (over 45,000 epochs; abstract and p.11); WAV leads BIS by about 15 s at induction and about 30 s at emergence; at the "responding to verbal command" event WAV was 90.6 (+/-10.2) vs BIS 79.0 (+/-18.8).

## Covariates
None.

## Caveats
Single-site, 20 patients, anaesthetic regimens uncontrolled; intermediate-state labels set by one anaesthesiologist's judgment; "responding to command" event was not assessed at defined intervals, so it is not a marker of return of consciousness; authors hold pending patents.
