# Ericsson 1999 (BJCP): first-in-man dose escalation of clevidipine

## Study
- First study of clevidipine in man. Placebo-controlled, single-blind tolerability study (Methods, p532).
- 25 healthy male Caucasians: age 29 ± 5 y, weight 75 ± 6 kg, height 179 ± 5 cm. Twenty-one were enrolled twice,
  so there were 46 study entries.
- 20-min IV infusions at target rates of 0.12, 0.24, 0.5, 1.5, 3, 6, 12, 24, 36 and 48 nmol/min/kg, in a 20%
  lipid emulsion (Intralipid vehicle; Intralipid as placebo).
  - Two clevidipine subjects and one placebo subject at each of the two lowest rates; four and one at each later
    step.
  - Only three subjects received clevidipine at 24 (a protocol deviation) and at 48 nmol/min/kg, where the
    escape criterion (HR > 120 bpm in more than 50%) stopped the study.
- Venous whole blood from the forearm opposite the infusion, put into SDS + ascorbic acid to stop hydrolysis.
- Assays: clevidipine by GC-MS, LOQ 5 nmol/L, intra-day CV 0.7-11.4%. Metabolite H 152/81 by LC, LOQ 50 nmol/L.
- **PK analysed only at 1.5-48 nmol/min/kg** for clevidipine and 6-48 for the metabolite: at lower rates the
  concentrations could not be measured precisely enough (Methods, p533).
- Analyses:
  - NCA: CL = Rinf/Css, with Css the median of the last 10 min of infusion.
  - Population: P-PHARMA 1.4 (nonlinear mixed effects), 1/Cpred error, mono- vs biexponential compared by AIC.
  - CL, V1, k12 and k21 were fitted and log-normal. Every other parameter was calculated from the population
    means. Individual values came from MAP Bayesian fits.

**Arithmetic check (derived):** with CL 0.121 and V1 0.36, k10 = 0.336/min. The two half-lives give
λ1 0.385 and λ2 0.073/min, hence k21 0.084 and k12 0.038/min. That gives V2 about 0.17 and Vss about 0.53 L/kg,
against 0.56 printed. The rounded entries are close to each other but not exactly consistent. The C1 and AUC
shares reproduce to within about one point.

Other results:
- **Dose linearity:** NCA clearance was unaffected by dose rate from 1.5 to 48 nmol/min/kg (Results, p534). The
  Discussion (p536) states linearity "over the entire dose range".
- The NCA mean is about 20% above the population mean, which the authors attribute to outlying concentrations in
  a few subjects.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- **End point:** the percentage fall in MAP/HR (an indirect index of SVR), recorded during the last 10 min of
  infusion, against the blood concentration (Figure 6, p6). A simple Emax model was fitted to pooled data in
  WinNonlin. A sigmoid model was not favoured by AIC or SBC.
- **Emax 45.4%, EC50 12.1 nmol/L** whole blood (Results, p5). The Discussion places the EC50 at the steady state of
  about 1.5 nmol/min/kg.
- Haemodynamics:
  - MAP fell about 10% at the highest rates (abstract). HR rose from 54 ± 9 to 86 ± 10 bpm at the three highest
    rates (p537). Mean basal MAP was 83 mmHg and HR 55 bpm (Figure 4).
  - MAP/HR was back to pre-dose values within 5 min of stopping, for rates up to 12. MAP was back at 10 min.
- No ke0 or effect compartment was estimated, and no hysteresis analysis was done.
- Adverse effects: flush 40%, headache 20%.

## Covariates
None. Parameters are per kg, and no covariate model was tested.

## Caveats
- Healthy young men with an intact baroreflex, so the blood-pressure effect was marginal.
- Sparse sampling, so the terminal half-life is uncertain (p536). The authors note that elimination may also
  occur from the peripheral compartment.
- Source inconsistency: the Results text (p534) gives the NCA clearance range as 0.092-0.276. Table 1 (same page,
  rendered) gives a minimum of 0.090.
- k12 and k21 are not printed, so the model cannot be rebuilt exactly from the paper.
