# Prazosin hydrochloride capsules (Cardinal Health / Novitium generic), DailyMed label

## Study
- **Document:** generic US label for prazosin HCl capsules (ANDA210971).
- **Indication:** hypertension.
- **Strengths:** each capsule contains prazosin HCl equivalent to 1, 2 or 5 mg prazosin base, as the
  polyhydrate. This relabel supplies the 1 mg capsule only (NDC 55154-4156-0).
- **Molecular weight:** 419.87 for C19H21N5O4·HCl.

- The drug is "highly bound" to plasma protein; no percentage is given. The label says prazosin is not dialyzable
  for this reason (Overdosage, p8).
- Metabolism, by demethylation and conjugation with biliary and faecal excretion, is described from animal
  studies. Human data are said to be less extensive.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- No concentration-effect data. Blood pressure falls supine and standing, most on the diastolic.
- **First-dose syncope:** about 1% of patients started on 2 mg or more, usually 30-90 min after the dose
  (Warnings, p3).
- **Dosing (p8-9):**
  - start 1 mg two or three times a day;
  - maintenance up to 20 mg/day in divided doses, usually 6-15 mg/day;
  - some patients up to 40 mg/day;
  - reduce to 1-2 mg three times a day when another antihypertensive is added.

## Covariates
None quantified. There is no renal or hepatic dosing section.

## Caveats
- The PK statements are short summaries with no study size or dose.
- The 90% figure is absorption relative to an oral solution, so it is not absolute F.
- **Use in specific populations:**
  - pregnancy category C; prazosin is excreted in small amounts in human milk;
  - pediatric safety and effectiveness are not established.
