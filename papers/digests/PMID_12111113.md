# Herben 2002: topotecan urinary and fecal excretion (IV and oral)

## Study
- 11 patients with refractory solid tumours (4 men, 7 women; median age 58, range 39-66; ovarian 5, colorectal 3, small-cell lung 2, unknown primary 1), ECOG <= 2, serum creatinine <= 1.5 mg/dL or CLcr >= 60 mL/min, bilirubin <= 2.0 mg/dL. 9 evaluable for PK.
- Randomised, course 1: topotecan IV 1.5 mg/m2/day (30-min infusion) or oral 2.3 mg/m2/day, once daily for 5 days. Course 2 crossed over; IV on day 1 then oral days 2-5 for the oral-first patients.
- Plasma sampling (IV): pre, 15, 30, 45, 60, 90 min, 2, 4, 6, 8, 12 h; (oral): pre, 30, 60 min, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 14 h. Urine in 24-h periods and all feces in course 1, up to 9 days after the first dose. HPLC assays for topotecan (lactone and carboxylate) and N-desmethyl topotecan.
- Non-compartmental analysis; CL = dose/AUC, Vdss from AUMC, F = AUC ratio dose-corrected, CLR = urinary amount/AUC.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None: an excretion/mass-balance study with no concentration-effect analysis. Not an opioid paper.

## Covariates
None analysed (n=9). No weight, BSA or CLcr values are reported for the 9 patients.

## Caveats
- Small n (IV 8-9, oral 4); IV and oral doses given in different patients.
- Plasma AUC to infinity; for N-desmethyl topotecan more than 20% was extrapolated so AUCt is reported.
- Fecal collection to 9 days; about 28% (IV) not recovered.
- One patient took domperidone on days 4-5 (assessed on day 1, kept evaluable); one took valproate with no visible effect.

## Verification against stanpumpR
- No cited location to check. Independent comparison with R/drugs_topotecan.R (Mould 2002, two-compartment, total plasma topotecan): the model's reference patient (70 kg, CLCR 70 mL/min, ECOG 1) has CL 18.57 L/h, 69% renal, Vss 65.0 L (R/drugs_topotecan.R:36-39). Herben's IV means (CL 28.8 L/h, Vdss 95.3 L, renal clearance 13.6 L/h = 47% of CL, derived) agree in the renal fraction (about 0.47 vs 0.69, still lower) but Herben's total clearance is 1.55x and Vdss 1.47x the library's reference values. The reference patient is a different one from the Herben patients (no weight reported), so the comparator is the model's own reference patient, not a fitted fixed effect. Both measure total (lactone plus carboxylate) plasma topotecan; concentrations are ng/mL in both. Units agree (h*ug/L = ng*h/mL; dose per m2 vs mg in the model).
