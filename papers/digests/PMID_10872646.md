# Pollak 2000: population PK of long-term oral amiodarone and desethylamiodarone

## Study
Halifax, Nova Scotia; prospective open-label clinic follow-up. 77 patients (50 men, 27 women) on oral amiodarone for
ventricular and supraventricular arrhythmias, doses set by the cardiologist (maintenance 100-600 mg/d, most often
300 mg/d), taken with food; adherence checked by pill counts (pp. 643-644). Table I: men age 61.3 (22-81) yr, weight
81.4 (52-133) kg, height 173 cm; women 69.0 (40-86) yr, 71.7 (39-120) kg, 158 cm. 605 trough serum samples
(fasting, before the morning dose), mean 24 months of therapy (0.2-92 months), 8 (1-21) per patient. HPLC (cyano
column, UV 241 nm), LOQ 0.1 mg/L, amiodarone and desethylamiodarone together. NONMEM FOCE with η-ε interaction;
exponential IIV; combined proportional + additive residual error (proportional only for the metabolite).
Input: each daily oral dose as a constant 24-h rate (400 mg/d = 16.7 mg/h), because no within-interval sampling
(no ka estimable), t½ is 60 × the dosing interval, and within-interval swing should be < 10% (p. 643).
Metabolite input: the amount of amiodarone permanently cleared from serum, from each patient's post hoc parent
parameters (p. 644). No IV dosing, so all parameters are apparent (÷ F).

## Model and parameters
Two-compartment mammillary model for parent and metabolite (3 compartments no better; 2 vs 1 compartment ΔOFV 164).

Text equivalents: CL ≈ 160 mL/min parent, 180 mL/min metabolite (p. 646).
Simulations (STANPUMP; RECOV for decrements): target 1.5 mg/L; proposed regimen 1600 mg/d × 2 d, 1200 × 5 d, 1000 × 7 d,
800 × 7 d, 600 × 7 d, 400 × 62 d, then 343 mg/d (= 1.5 mg/L × 229 L/d; 400 mg/d on 6 of 7 days). Label regimens:
maximum 1600 mg/d × 3 wk, 800 mg/d × 1 month, then 600 mg/d (half the population above 2.5 mg/L at steady state;
some > 5 mg/L in loading); minimum 800 mg/d × 1 wk, 600 mg/d × 1 month, then 400 mg/d (6-9 months to approach steady
state; typical steady state 1.73 mg/L) (p. 646). Steady-state context-sensitive decrements: 25% in 3 days, 50% in
36 days (abstract) / nearly 5 weeks (Results), 75% in 98 days (p. 647, Fig. 6). Proposed regimen keeps 90% of
patients in the 1.0-2.5 mg/L window at steady state (abstract).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD model, ke0, tPeak or MEAC (not an opioid). Therapeutic window 1.0-2.5 mg/L from the product monograph
(serum amiodarone). Metabolite stated to be of equal potency (ref 40) and toxicity (ref 41). Serum reflects the
effect site only at steady state (p. 650).

## Covariates
Age, sex, height, weight, lean body mass tested (P < .01 criterion); none significant.

## Caveats
Apparent parameters only (÷ F; F 30-80%); sparse early data (11 samples in week 1, 64 in month 1) → large, poorly
determined V1, model ill-suited to single doses or short IV infusions; overwhelmingly Northern European population.
Internal inconsistencies: abstract CL2/F 599 vs Table II 588; Table I observation counts 342 + 363 = 705, not 605
(363 is probably 263; 27 women × mean 10 ≈ 270); IIV quoted as "30% to 108%" though metabolite V1 CV is 21.2%;
the metabolite's 62-day and the 3/36/98-day decrements are not reproduced by Table II (derived: 60.4 days;
2.2/31.2/86.6 days).
