# Grasela 1999: population PK of adjunctive lamotrigine in adults

## Study
Retrospective NONMEM IV analysis pooling three US add-on, placebo-controlled phase II/III trials in refractory
partial seizures (Matsuo 1993, Messenheimer 1994, Schachter 1995; Table I, printed p 375). 556 enrolled, 29 dropped
for poor dosing/sampling records: **527 patients, 2,407 plasma concentrations** (p 377). Age 34.0 (SD 10.0), range
18-64 y; weight 73.5 (18.0) kg, range 38.2-159; height 167.7 (10.8) cm; 54.8% female; 10.1% non-Caucasian (origin not
recorded); 22 women on oral contraceptives (p 377).
- All on concomitant enzyme-inducing AEDs (carbamazepine, phenytoin, phenobarbital, primidone); **valproate excluded**
  by protocol (p 374). Doses titrated from 100 mg/day in 100 mg steps to 300-500 mg/day; tablets (Matsuo) or capsules.
- Intended trough samples, but not all at trough (Fig 1); samples > 20 h after the last dose deleted (p 375).
- Assay: immunofluorometric (IFA), lower limit 0.1 ug/mL (p 374).
- Model: one compartment, first-order absorption and elimination; proportional IIV and residual error (p 375).

## Model and parameters
Equation 1 (p 378): CL/F = (Theta_INT + Theta_SLP x WT) x (1 + Theta_RACE x RACE) x (1 + Theta_NAED x NAED),
RACE 1 if non-Caucasian, NAED 1 if two or more of CBZ/PHT/PB/PRM (0 if one or fewer).
Equation 2: V/F = Theta_V x (1 + Theta_SEX x SEX), SEX 1 if female. Table II (printed p 379, image-confirmed):

IIV on CL fell from 32% to 28.7% with these terms (p 380).

Other drugs (p 380-381): phenytoin Vm changed +0.9% with lamotrigine (CI -1 to 3%, NS); Vm 6.9 mg/kg/day, Km fixed
5.7 mg/L, IIV 24.4%, residual 6.5% (56 patients, 377 levels). Carbamazepine CL/F +0.8% with lamotrigine (CI -2 to 3%,
NS); CL/F 1.12 mL/min/kg, IIV 36.3%, residual 16.1% (208 patients, 1438 levels).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No concentration-effect, therapeutic range, ke0 or tPeak. Not an opioid: no MEAC or potency ratio.

## Covariates
Weight (linear on CL/F), race (-25% CL), >= 2 inducers (+13% CL vs one inducer), female (-27% V). Age (18-64), sex
on CL, height, oral contraceptives, time on study: not retained (p 378, 381).

## Caveats
- Every patient on at least one inducer and none on valproate: **no uninduced baseline**; the 0.98 mL/min/kg is an
  induced clearance.
- Comparator for the 13%: patients on any ONE of the four inducers, a mix; the NAED group includes "two or three".
- Mostly trough samples: V/F and ka weakly informed (ka IIV %SEM 137).
- PB/PRM grouped; PB/PRM alone did not raise CL vs CBZ alone.
- Authors note a prior 1.25 mL/min/kg and 1.4 L/kg in small studies (n = 17 / < 20); their V is higher.
- Funded by Glaxo Wellcome.

## Verification against stanpumpR
- **Transcription** (all three locations): 527 patients and 13% **match** (p 377, Table II Theta_NAED 0.131, p 379).
  "A second inducer" is a fair paraphrase of "two or more ... vs one".
- **What the 13% supports** — **discrepancy (interpretive)**. The 13% is measured against single-inducer clearance,
  not the uninduced clearance eq. (3) counts excess in. Read against the library's own factors (Rivas 2008, in
  `inst/extdata/antiseizureInteractions.csv:2-4`: PHT 1.941, PB 1.8, CBZ 1.595), additive excess predicts
  (derived) vs CBZ alone: PHT +21.7% (Grasela +34.0%, inside CI), CBZ+PHT 2.536/1.595 = +59% (Grasela +25.8%, CI to
  37%), PHT+PB 2.741/1.595 = +72% (Grasela +23.1%, CI to 36%), PB 1.8/1.595 = +13% (Grasela -8.5%, CI to +1.3%),
  CBZ+PB 2.395/1.595 = +50% (Grasela +5.8%, CI to 15%). Grasela shows combinations far below multiplicative
  (CBZ+PHT multiplicative +94%) but also below additive excess; in Grasela CBZ+PHT is lower than PHT alone. So the
  paper supports "sub-multiplicative", as `used_for` says, but not additive excess specifically, and the repo's
  "Two lamotrigine sources say so" (R/antiseizureInteractions.R:72) overstates it. Caveats on the comparison:
  Grasela's population (US refractory, trough IFA) differs from Rivas's (German/Spanish TDM), and Rivas's own
  factors relative to one another (PHT/CBZ 1.22) differ from Grasela's (1.34). See `open_questions`.
- **Lamotrigine model (uncited comparison)**: `R/drugs_lamotrigine.R` / `inst/extdata/antiseizureParameters.csv:42-44`
  use Milosheska 2016 base: CL/F 2.32 L/h, V/F 77.6 L, ka 1.96 /h. Grasela on one inducer: 4.32 L/h at 73.5 kg
  (derived), 1.86x the library's base, within the single-inducer factors the library applies (1.60-1.94), so
  consistent. Grasela V/F 132 L (males) / 97 L (females) is 1.3-1.7x the library's 77.6 L, and ka 1.30 vs 1.96 /h;
  both weakly informed by trough data. No change suggested.
- Phenytoin and carbamazepine: Grasela finds lamotrigine does not change their clearance (Vm +0.9%, CL +0.8%, NS);
  `inst/extdata/antiseizureInteractions.csv` has no row with lamotrigine as perpetrator (searched), consistent.
