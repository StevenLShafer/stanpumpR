# Kates 1981: verapamil disposition in chronic atrial fibrillation

PDF page n is printed page 43 + n.

## Study
Stanford, single centre. 12 patients with chronic atrial fibrillation (Table I, PDF p2): 8 men, 4 women (patients
4, 7, 10, 11); age 52-69 years, mean 59.8; weight 58-115 kg (derived mean 81.0 kg); NYHA class I (n = 3), II (4),
III (5; the text says the five class III patients had congestive heart failure). All on oral digoxin with serum
digoxin above 1 ng/mL; no antiarrhythmics or beta blockers; no renal or hepatic dysfunction.

IV study: 15 mg verapamil by Harvard pump at 1 mg/min (a 15-minute infusion; the abstract calls it a "bolus"),
venous samples for 24 h, plasma by HPLC (Harapat and Kates 1980) for verapamil and norverapamil. Each patient's
data fitted to a three-compartment model in MLAB, weights 1/C², with the 15-min infusion modelled as a continuous
input. Model-independent t1/2, CL and Vdss (Benet and Galeazzi) also computed. Dose salt (base or hydrochloride) is
not stated anywhere.

Then a multistep infusion to targets 15, 30, 60 and 120 ng/mL designed from each patient's model; at least 48 h
later a single 120 mg oral dose (fasted); then at least 10 weeks of oral therapy with a steady-state dosing interval
sampled.

## Model and parameters
Table II (PDF p3, rendered) prints each patient's k10, k12, k21, k13, k31 (/min) and Vc (L), with Vc·k10 (L/min) and
Vdss (L/kg). Means printed: k10 0.05915, k12 0.1547, k21 0.0502, k13 0.0736, k31 0.00658 /min, Vc 28.6 L, Vc·k10 0.99
L/min, Vdss 3.50 L/kg.

**Patient 2's Vc is printed 92.72 L.** Its own derived columns require 9.272 L: 9.272 × 0.06796 = 0.630 L/min
(printed 0.63; 92.72 would give 6.30), and 9.272 × (1 + k12/k21 + k13/k31) / 58 kg = 4.35 L/kg (printed 4.36; 92.72
would give 43.5). The printed mean Vc 28.6 L equals the mean with 92.72 (derived 28.58); with 9.272 it is 21.6 L. The
other printed means (Vc·k10 0.99, Vdss 3.50) were computed from the consistent derived columns. Evidence points to a
source typo in the Vc cell carried into the Vc mean.

Results text (PDF p5): model-calculated average CL 12.5 ± 5.4 mL/min/kg and Vdss 3.50 ± 1.3 L/kg; all patients
triphasic; three compartments needed. Table III (PDF p4), model-independent: t1/2 6.3 ± 4.0 h, CL 13.3 ± 7.7
mL/min/kg, Vdss 4.3 ± 1.9 L/kg (these are the abstract's numbers). Model-dependent and model-independent CL did not
differ; Vdss differed (p < 0.02, paired t); the discussion attributes this to the terminal slope's sensitivity in
the Benet-Galeazzi formula.

Derived (mine, from Tables I-II with 9.272 L): arithmetic means of per-kg V1 0.2751, V2 0.8594, V3 2.3560 L/kg,
CL 12.516 mL/min/kg (SD 5.36), Q2 0.03820, Q3 0.01408 L/min/kg, Vss 3.490 L/kg (SD 1.26). The model built from those
means has half-lives 2.62 min, 36.9 min and 4.76 h. At 81 kg: CL 60.8 L/h, Vss 283 L.

Infusion validation (Table IV, PDF p4): predicted 15, 30, 60, 120 ng/mL against measured 19.6 ± 1.2 (n = 5), 31.1 ±
2.1 (11), 63.7 ± 4.1 (12), 118.5 ± 7.0 ng/mL (12), mean ± SEM. Oral 120 mg: Cmax 148.6 ± 65.5 ng/mL, within 90 min in
all but two patients (120 and 360 min). Chronic oral: mean steady-state concentration 2 ± 0.8 times that predicted
from the single doses.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None reported. The paper says the infusions were for "measurements of drug effect" but reports no effect data, no
ke0 or time to peak effect. Norverapamil was not detected after the IV dose (PDF p4); during the final infusion step
it reached at most 28 ng/mL (mean 15.1).

## Covariates
None modelled; individual fits only. Weight, NYHA class and co-medications per patient in Table I.

## Caveats
Twelve older patients with AF, all digitalised, five with heart failure: not a healthy-volunteer model. Individual
fits only, no population analysis; the mean of fits is not a population typical value. Dose basis unstated. One
misprinted Vc (patient 2), demonstrated by the table's own derived columns. Abstract reports the model-independent
CL and Vdss, the Results the model-dependent ones; they differ for Vdss (4.3 vs 3.50 L/kg).
