# Giaccone 1996: enzyme-inducing anticonvulsants and ethosuximide pharmacokinetics

## Study
Parallel-group comparison (not crossover), Pavia and Messina, Italy.
- **Controls**: 12 drug-free healthy adults, 6 M / 6 F, age 41 ± 10 y, weight 63 ± 13 kg (Table 1, PDF p. 2, printed p. 576).
- **Patients**: 10 adults with partial epilepsy on chronic enzyme-inducing antiepileptic drugs, 7 M / 3 F, age 29 ± 12 y,
  weight 74 ± 12 kg (Table 2, PDF p. 3, printed p. 577). Co-medication (daily dose, serum concentration in µg/mL):
  phenytoin alone in 2 (subjects 13, 16), phenytoin + vigabatrin in 1 (22), phenobarbitone alone in 2 (17, 21),
  and two-inducer combinations in 5 (CBZ+PB 14, 20; PHT+PB 15; PHT+CBZ 18, 19). **No patient took carbamazepine alone.**
  Comedication unchanged during the study.
- Drug: single 500 mg oral ethosuximide (2 × 250 mg Zarontin capsules) after an overnight fast, no food for 4 h.
- Sampling: plasma at 0, 2, 4, 6, 9, 12, 24, 31, 47, 71, 95, 119 h.
- Assay: HPLC (Kabra), LOQ 0.4 µg/mL, intra-/inter-day CV 1.0-6.7% / 3.2-13.7%; non-stereospecific (justified as
  enantiomer kinetics being non-stereoselective). Total plasma concentration (ethosuximide is described as not protein bound).
- Analysis: non-compartmental (Cmax, tmax, t½, AUC, CL/F, V/F), Student's t-test, 95% CI of the difference.

## Model and parameters
Non-compartmental; controls show monoexponential decline after peak within 2 h (text, PDF p. 2). Means ± SD from the
rendered tables.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No effect or seizure end point, no therapeutic range stated; not an opioid paper (no MEAC, ke0, tPeak or potency ratio).

## Covariates
Group only (inducer comedication vs drug-free healthy). The authors could not separate drugs or doses (PDF p. 4).
Smoking recorded but not analysed.

## Caveats
- **Comparator**: the "higher clearance" is patients on inducers against a *separate group of healthy volunteers*,
  not the same subjects before and after induction. Groups differ in age, weight, sex mix and disease.
- Inducers pooled; no carbamazepine-alone patient, so any carbamazepine-specific factor is not measured here.
- n=10 vs 12; single dose; apparent (oral) parameters only.
- The per-kg ratio (×1.66) and the absolute ratio (×1.94, derived from mean AUCs) differ because patients were heavier.
- Controls agree with earlier adult values the authors cite (refs 8, 9, 22, 23).
