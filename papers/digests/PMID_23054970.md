# Fang 2013: population PK/PD reanalysis of intravenous pramlintide in type 1 diabetes

## Study
- Reanalysis of the data of Colburn et al. 1996 (J Clin Pharmacol 36:13-24). Randomized, single-blind,
  placebo-controlled, ascending-dose crossover (PDF p. 2, printed 16).
- 25 men with type 1 diabetes (24 white, 1 Hispanic); mean ± SD age 29.6 ± 6.8 y, weight 170.4 ± 17.0 lb,
  height 71.6 ± 2.5 in (derived: about 77.3 kg and 181.9 cm); HbA1c 6.1-13.0%, basal C-peptide < 0.6 ng/mL.
- Three groups: 30, 100 or 300 µg pramlintide given both as a "bolus" (2-min IV infusion) and as a 120-min IV
  infusion, two-period crossover within 2 weeks; two subjects per group received placebo only. Injected into a
  forearm vein contralateral to the sampling cannula. Insulin about 30 min before dosing; breakfast 30 min after
  dose start.
- PK sampling: bolus at end of the 2-min dose and 5-240 min after; infusion 30-300 min after start.
- **PK data set: 342 observations in 18 individuals** (placebo subjects excluded; PDF p. 2). Glucose data set:
  1028 observations in all 25.
- Assay: two-site immunoradiometric "pramlintide equivalent" assay; mean minimum detectable concentration
  3.7 pmol/L (range 1.0-10.8); intra-/inter-assay CV < 10% / 15%. Cross-reacts with endogenous amylin and the
  active des-lysine metabolite, so the measurement is pramlintide plus metabolite. 13.8% of PK samples were
  below the LLOQ (M3 method; the control stream uses LOQ = 2 pmol/L, PDF p. 11).
- NONMEM VII level 2.0, FOCE-I (control stream: METHOD=1 INTER LAPLACE), ADVAN3, S1 = V1; Phoenix WNL 6.1
  for NCA; SAS 9.1.

## Model and parameters
Two-compartment linear model, zero-order input, first-order elimination, with a lag time. IIV on CL, V1, V2,
D1; IOV on CL (bolus vs infusion occasion); CL-V1 correlation. No covariates (age, weight, BMI screened).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: plasma glucose after a meal (not plotted by stanpumpR). Two-compartment indirect-response glucose
model; pramlintide prolongs the zero-order gastric-emptying duration Tin by a dose-independent factor
S = 1 + Smax, and inhibits endogenous glucose production kin by a sigmoid Imax function of Cp (Eq. 8 has no
Hill exponent printed). Table III (PDF p. 9, printed 23; rendered), bootstrap 95% CI:
- Imax 0.995 (0.429, 1.0); IC50 23.8 pmol/L (1.53, 162); Smax 1.26 (0.725, 2.98).
- Tin 85.9 min (IIV 39.7%); kout 0.0146 /min (0.00987, 0.0271); ka (glucose) 0.0282 /min; F (glucose)
  0.843; G0 161 mg/dL (IIV 38.4%, IOV 37.1%); VGc 9.33 L, VGp 8.56 L, QG 0.442 L/min fixed from Silber 2007.
- Abstract and Table III agree on Imax 0.995 and IC50 23.8 pmol/L. NONMEM covariance step failed for the PD
  model, hence bootstrap; the IC50 CI spans two orders of magnitude.
- AUCnet fell at least 40% for all regimens except the 30-µg bolus; significant vs placebo only for the 100-
  and 300-µg groups; glucose Tmax delay significant only in the 100-µg group (Figs 3-4).
- No ke0, no effect-site, no MEAC (not an opioid).

## Covariates
None in the final PK model; the authors note the narrow covariate spread (body weight) of 18 young men.

## Caveats
- PK from 18 young men with type 1 diabetes; measured analyte includes the active des-lys metabolite and any
  endogenous amylin.
- The 2-min "bolus" needed a fixed 4-min duration plus a lag to fit; the population D1 was sensitive to
  initial values, which is why it was fixed (PDF p. 5).
- Overprediction in the 100-µg group and especially the 30-µg bolus group (Fig. 2); one 30-µg bolus subject
  had much lower concentrations and was kept.
- Funding in part by Amylin Pharmaceuticals; one author an Amylin employee.
- Derived check: the model's terminal half-life (39.3 min) is shorter than the SC label's ~48 min
  (NJ_label-symlin_021332_2019-12-19), which is consistent with some absorption limitation after SC dosing but
  the paper does not address SC. Using CL 0.955 L/min and MW 3949.4 (label), the label's SC AUCs (Table 5) imply
  F of about 0.36-0.47 (derived), close to the label's 30-40%.
