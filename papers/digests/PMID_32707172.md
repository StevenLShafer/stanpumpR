# Teixeira-da-Silva 2020: phenobarbital PopPK in Caucasian outpatients with epilepsy

## Study
- Retrospective TDM data, Salamanca University Hospital: development 1992-2012 (288 patients, 675-677 trough
  samples), external validation 2013-2015 (107 patients, 178 samples); 395 patients, 855 samples in all. Ambulatory,
  > 15 y, BMI 16.0-39.9, steady state (≥ 1 month after start or change), compliant (level/dose CV ≤ 20%).
- Development set (Table 1, median (range) [mean ± SD]; text-layer rows realigned): 174 M / 114 F; age 45.0
  (15.6-90.1) y; weight 66.0 (37-99) kg; height 165 cm; BSA (Mosteller) 1.7 (1.2-2.2) m²; FFM 47.4 kg; dose 150
  (50-315) mg/day; concentration 23.2 (8.2-48.0) mg/L. Co-medication: carbamazepine 45, phenytoin 81, valproate 9
  patients (as best read from the scrambled table).
- Oral tablets, usually once daily; samples mostly steady-state troughs. Serum by FPIA (Abbott TDx), CV < 10%.
- NONMEM 7.3, FOCE-I, ADVAN2/TRANS2; stepwise covariates (forward ΔOFV > 3.84, backward > 6.63); bootstrap 1000;
  external validation.

## Model and parameters
One compartment, first-order absorption and elimination. **Only CL/F estimated**: V/F fixed at 0.61 L/kg
(Patsalos 2013) and ka fixed at 3.0 /h (Vučićević 2015), because trough data cannot inform them (p. 2).

Final model, PopPK1+2 (both datasets), Table 2 / Table 4 (p. 4-5):

CL/F (L/h) = (0.236 + 0.115 × (BSA − 1.7)) × 0.822^PHT × 0.711^VPA   (PHT, VPA = 0/1)

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None measured. Target range quoted as 15-40 µg/mL (introduction) and 15-30 mg/L (simulation, Fig. 2). Not an
opioid. Simulation: 150 mg/day for BSA 1.7 m² monotherapy; with PHT or VPA about 100 mg/day (average Css 21.5 and
24.8 mg/L).

## Covariates
BSA (linear, centred on 1.7 m²; chosen over weight, FFM, height, age for the largest OFV drop, 16.4); phenytoin
(−17.8%) and valproate (−28.9%) co-medication. Carbamazepine, sex, age not retained. No genotype data.

## Caveats
- V/F and ka fixed, so half-life and absorption are not data-driven; troughs only.
- FPIA assay; retrospective; valproate group small (< 10%).
- Apparent oral parameters (F not estimated; the intro cites ~90% bioavailability).
