# Cortinez 2014: propofol TCI models in the obese, PK performance and BIS PD

## Study
- 20 morbidly obese adults (BMI > 35), ASA II-III, laparoscopic bariatric surgery. Mean (range): age 33.7 (21-53) y, weight 106 (85-141) kg, height 160 (148-178) cm, BMI 41.4 (35-52); 3 male / 17 female (Table 2, PDF p3).
- Propofol by effect-site TCI, initial target 4 mcg/mL, Cortinez 2010 model with ke0 0.21 /min (tPeak about 2.2 min) (PDF p2). Remifentanil by manually controlled infusion, 0.3 mcg/kg/min at induction (TBW basis); mean consumption 0.36 (SD 0.09) mcg/kg/min; propofol 6.53 (SD 1.11) mg/kg/h (TBW). Titrated to BIS 40-60; mean BIS in maintenance 37.4 (SD 8.7).
- 294 arterial propofol samples (plasma, HPLC, LLOQ 0.1 mcg/mL) and 1431 BIS values (BIS VISTA, 15 s smoothing, recorded every 10 s).
- Analysis: Varvel MDPE/MDAPE for 5 published models (Marsh, Schnider, Cortinez 2010, van Kralingen, Eleveld 2014); then NONMEM 7.2 FOCE-I 3-compartment PK without covariates, post hoc parameters fed to a PD fit (Sheiner sequential). Positive MDPE = model underestimates.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: BIS (sigmoid Imax, Emax fixed 0), propofol with remifentanil on board; ke0 effect-site model plus an estimated BIS-monitor time lag (Table 5, PDF p5, printed 306, values confirmed on the image):

```yaml
pd:
  - {name: E0, value: 96.8, unit: BIS units, source: "Table 5", note: "95% CI 94.8-99.0"}
  - {name: Ce50, value: 3.29, unit: mcg/mL, source: "Table 5", note: "plasma-derived effect-site; BSV 21.9% CV; CI 2.95-3.69"}
  - {name: gamma, value: 1.59, unit: none, source: "Table 5", note: "BSV 42.8% CV; CI 1.29-2.00"}
  - {name: ke0, value: 0.190, unit: 1/min, source: "Table 5", note: "BSV 79.2% CV; CI 0.13-0.26"}
  - {name: lag, value: 0.44, unit: min, source: "Table 5", note: "CI 0.39-0.46; OFV fell 111.2"}
  - {name: tPeak, value: 2.1, unit: min, source: "PDF p5 text", note: "median after a rapid bolus, derived by authors from ke0; plus about 0.4 min lag to see it on BIS"}
```

- Effect-site target for BIS 50 in a typical patient: 3.2 mcg/mL (abstract SE 0.17); bolus 1.4 mg/kg TBW then 5 to 4 mg/kg/h (Fig. 7, PDF p5). Boluses of 1.1, 1.4 and 1.8 mg/kg for BIS 60, 50, 40.
- Comparisons the authors make: Ce50 3.34 mcg/mL in lean adults (Rigouzzo, ref 20); ke0 0.17 /min = tPeak 2.7 min in nonobese (Doufas, ref 19, no lag parameter). No weight, age or sex effect on any PD parameter (OFV drop < 2).
- No MEAC, no opioid kinetics; remifentanil was only co-administered. No potency ratio.

## Covariates
- PK/PD fit: none retained. Weight, age, sex tested on PD: no effect.
- For the tested models: TBW, ABW, FFM size scalars; ABW and the Schnider (FFM) results are in Table 3.

## Caveats
- All five models underestimated concentrations (positive MDPE); the authors suggest deep hypnosis, high remifentanil dose, laparoscopy (pneumoperitoneum, reverse Trendelenburg) and low targets, none proven.
- n = 20, mostly women, one centre; PD estimated with remifentanil and "cannot be extrapolated" to no-opioid settings (PDF p7).
- Study PK fit is a post hoc step for the PD analysis, not a TCI-grade population model, per the authors.
- Authors' recommended options for obese TCI: Eleveld (2014) with TBW, or Marsh/Schnider with ABW, the latter only with EEG monitoring.
