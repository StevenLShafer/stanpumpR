# Brunner 1994: MAC reduction of isoflurane by sufentanil

## Study
- 76 ASA I-II unpremedicated adults 18-60 yr randomised; 66 analysed (10 excluded: pre/post-incision sufentanil differed by >30% or >0.05 ng/mL). Analysed: mean age 38.3 yr (19-60), mean weight 74.2 kg (43-116), 42 men.
- Sufentanil by CACI (Bovill PK) to target 0, 0.1, 0.3 or 0.6 ng/mL, held >= 20 min before incision; within each group five predetermined end-tidal isoflurane concentrations in O2, held >= 15 min.
- Venous plasma samples at 10 min, pre-incision and 1 min post-incision; RIA (LOD 0.1 ng/mL; 0.02 ng/mL with sorbent extraction). Pre-incision concentration used (range 0-1.39 ng/mL).
- End point: purposeful movement within 1 min of skin incision. Logistic regression with sufentanil, isoflurane and their product.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- Potency (MAC reduction, plasma at steady state): sufentanil 11.5 x fentanyl (0.145 vs 1.67 ng/mL).
- Cited ratios: EEG IC50 1:12 (Scott 1991); MEAC-based sufentanil:fentanyl 1:15 (Gourlay 1988, Lehmann 1991); dose-based 3-10x.
- Sufentanil t1/2ke0 6.2 (SD 2.8) min, cited from Scott 1991 (EEG end point); used to justify the 20-min (about 3 x t1/2ke0) equilibration.
- No MEAC measured. Manual regimens suggested: 0.15 ug/kg + 0.003 ug/kg/min for 0.15 ng/mL; 0.5 ug/kg + 0.008 ug/kg/min for 0.5 ng/mL.

## Covariates
None examined.

## Caveats
CACI with Bovill parameters had ~60% performance error; measured concentrations tended to exceed target. Wide CI on the C50 (0.04-0.26). MAC CI for isoflurane alone reported inconsistently. Fentanyl value is from a different (though same-method) study. Interaction terms individually non-significant.
