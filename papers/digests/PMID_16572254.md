# Melo-Cristino 2006: Viriato surveillance, Portugal 2003-2004

## Study
National prospective multicentre surveillance (29 hospital laboratories, central testing in Lisbon) of
community-acquired respiratory pathogens, 2003-2004: 2945 isolates: *S. pyogenes* 513 (acute tonsillitis/pharyngitis;
449 paediatric < 18 y, 64 adult), *S. pneumoniae* 1300, *H. influenzae* 829, *M. catarrhalis* 303 (PDF p8, printed p16).
Disc diffusion per NCCLS M100-S14; MICs by Etest (AB Biodisk). **MICs were determined for all pneumococci for
penicillin, amoxicillin, amoxicillin/clavulanate and levofloxacin, and for the other organism-drug pairs only when
resistance was detected** (PDF p7, printed p15). Macrolide phenotype (M vs MLSB) by erythromycin-clindamycin
double-disc test. Funded by GlaxoSmithKline Portugal. No patients dosed; no PK.

## Model and parameters
No PK model. *S. pyogenes* (Table I, PDF p9, printed p17; confirmed on rendered page), resistant strains of 513:
penicillin 0, amoxicillin/clavulanate 0, erythromycin/clarithromycin/azithromycin 97 (18.9%) each,
**clindamycin 31 (6.0%)** (adults 5, 7.8%; paediatric 26, 5.8%), tetracycline 29 (5.6%), levofloxacin 0.
Macrolide-resistant phenotype: M 76.6% (2003), 58.0% (2004); MLSB 23.4% and 42% (PDF p10, printed p18); the abstract
gives M 67% over both years. **MIC90 erythromycin 16 mg/L, clindamycin 0.094 mg/L, two years pooled** (PDF p10,
printed p18; abstract PDF p2, printed p10, ties both to the M-phenotype strains).

*S. pneumoniae* (Table II, printed p19, text layer only): clindamycin resistance 207/1300 (15.9%); MLSB 83.7% of
macrolide-resistant; erythromycin and clindamycin MIC90 > 256 mg/L in resistant strains (printed p20). Tetracycline
resistance 14.5% in Table II and the Portuguese abstract, 14.9% in the English abstract (internal inconsistency).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End points are in vitro MIC (Etest) and NCCLS susceptibility categories. No MBC measured. Not an opioid paper:
no MEAC, ke0 or potency ratio.

## Covariates
Adult vs paediatric (< 18 y) isolates tabulated separately; year (2003 vs 2004).

## Caveats
- MIC90s reported without the n they were computed on; MIC testing was conditional on disc resistance.
- 0.094 mg/L is an Etest half-dilution reading; no MIC50 for clindamycin.
- Portuguese isolates, 2003-2004, mostly paediatric; tonsillitis only.

## Verification against stanpumpR
- **MIC90 0.094 mg/L** (`R/antibioticMbc.R:261,264`): matches the paper (PDF p10 and abstract, rendered). **matches**.
- **"in 513 isolates"** (`R/antibioticMbc.R:264`): **discrepancy**. 513 is the total *S. pyogenes* collection, but
  the paper measured MICs only on strains with disc-detected resistance and links the 0.094 to M-phenotype
  macrolide-resistant strains (abstract). The erythromycin MIC90 of 16 mg/L in the same sentence cannot be over all
  513 (81% erythromycin-susceptible), so the clindamycin value is very probably over the same resistant subset (about
  two-thirds of 97, derived). M-phenotype (efflux) strains carry no clindamycin resistance mechanism, so 0.094 is
  still a plausible clindamycin-susceptible GAS value, but it is not a population MIC90 over 513. See open question.
- **"modern ... MIC90s are lower"** (`:261`): the comparator is the 0.25 mg/L of Muller 1996 (mixed tonsillar flora,
  probably the lowest concentration tested); the Viriato value is from different isolates and method (Etest), and is
  2003-2004 data. The direction (lower) holds; "modern" is the repo's wording.
- Units mg/L in both; no conversion. Page range: PubMed 9-30, printed footer 9-29; the repo's 9-30 follows PubMed.
