# Lu 2002: magnesium PK and blood-pressure effect compartment in preeclampsia

## Study
51 hospitalised women with preeclampsia (gestational hypertension plus proteinuria >= 2+), Jinling Hospital,
Nanjing; MgSO4 the only treatment; preexisting hypertension and cardiac or renal disease excluded; no
antihypertensives allowed (PDF p3). Age 27 +/- 3 y, weight 68 +/- 10 kg, 34-41 weeks pregnant. MgSO4 as two
consecutive infusions, 120 mg/kg over 1 h then "24 mg/kg for 5 hours" (read most naturally as 24 mg/kg/h; the text
does not say per hour). Serum Mg2+ and supine BP at 0, 0.5, 1, 2, 4, 6, 7, 9, 11, 13 and 15 h. Salt form
(heptahydrate or not) and assay not stated. NONMEM; two-step PK then PD with individual posterior PK fixed.

## Model and parameters
Two compartments, instantaneous input, first-order elimination (better than one compartment). Central and
peripheral volumes depended on body weight, but the weight function and reference weight are not given (PDF p5).
No other covariate (age, gestational duration) significant. Mean endogenous Mg2+ estimated 1.08 +/- 0.21 mmol/L,
identical to the mean initial measurement; residual CV 5.5% (PDF p5).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: supine sphygmomanometer systolic and diastolic BP (mean of 2nd and 3rd of three readings). Emax model
(better than sigmoid Emax) on effect-site Mg2+ above baseline, with a 12-h cosine baseline. Mean initial BP 157/105
+/- 13/8 mmHg. Equilibration half-time 0.693/ke0 described as about 1 h (derived: 0.91 h systolic, 1.39 h diastolic).
At 2 mmol/L, 52-57% (mean 54%) of Emax systolic and 50-55% (53%) diastolic (PDF p8). Therapeutic range used: 2-4
mmol/L. Derived here: with Table I's PK, a bolus puts the effect-site peak at 111 min (systolic ke0) and 144 min
(diastolic ke0). Simulations (Fig. 4, 4 g over 15 min loading): 1 g/h rarely exceeds 2 mmol/L; 3 g/h risks > 4 mmol/L
after 10 h; 2 g/h or IM 4-5 g q4h best. Optimal loading 8 g over 15 min or 5 g over 30 min (PDF p9).

## Covariates
Body weight on Vc and Vp (form not reported).

## Caveats
Placebo not studied (authors: placebo component presumed minimal). Fewer than 24 h of BP data. The IM maintenance
dose is "5 g" every 4 h in the Fig. 4 caption (PDF p7) but "4 g every 4 hours" in Results and Discussion (PDF p7, p9).
Weight scaling unspecified, so the typical values apply to about 68 kg. Endogenous baseline 1.08 mmol/L is high for
the usual range.
