# Kapil 2015: paroxetine (CYP2D6 block) and extended-release hydrocodone

## Study
- Single-centre (PPD Phase I, Austin TX), randomised, double-blind, 2-period 2-way crossover; Purdue-funded.
- 24 healthy adults randomised (23 completed); age 36.0 ± 8.14 yr, 50% men (Table I text; Table I row labels are
  scrambled in the text layer), weight 74.3 ± 12.02 kg, height 168.5 ± 8.81 cm, BMI 26.1 ± 2.81 (Table I, all
  patients). CYP2D6 genotype not reported.
- Paroxetine 20 mg or placebo daily for 12 days; single hydrocodone bitartrate 20 mg ER tablet (Hysingla ER, once
  daily product) on day 10 (or 23), fasted; no naltrexone block.
- Plasma (K2EDTA) at 0, 0.5, 1, 2.5, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 14, 16, 18, 24, 30, 36, 48, 72, 80 h. HPLC-MS/MS; linear
  0.1-100 ng/mL (hydrocodone, norhydrocodone), 0.05-50 ng/mL (hydromorphone). BLQ set to 0.
- Noncompartmental analysis (WinNonlin 6.2.1); ANOVA on ln parameters.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Opioid audit:
- MEAC / analgesic concentration: none.
- ke0 / t½ke0 / tPeak / hysteresis: none; no PD end point measured (tolerability only).
- Potency ratios (Introduction and Discussion, from cited sources): hydrocodone about equianalgesic to oxycodone and
  twice oral morphine (refs 1-2); oral hydromorphone 2.5-fold as potent as hydrocodone (ref 16, Exalgo label).
  Hydromorphone systemic exposure "up to 3%" of parent (ref 5).
- Urinary recovery after 15 mg (cited, Cone 1978): hydrocodone 11%, hydromorphone 3.5%, norhydrocodone 5.2%.

## Covariates
None examined.

## Caveats
n = 24 healthy adults; single 20 mg dose of an ER tablet; hydromorphone mostly near LLOQ, BLQ set to 0, so
hydromorphone AUC0-t (and the ratio) is uncertain and probably underestimated; no AUC0-inf for hydromorphone;
CYP2D6 genotype not reported; industry-sponsored.
