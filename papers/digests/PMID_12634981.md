# Hornestam 2003: IV digoxin PK and the effect compartment for ventricular rate in acute AF

## Study
Post hoc population analysis of the DAAF trial (13 Swedish hospitals; randomised, double-blind, IV digoxin vs placebo in AF of <=7 days). 117 randomised to digoxin; 105 in the PK analysis; 122 placebo patients as controls for the PD. Table 1 (PDF p3 / printed 749): age 67 +/- 12 (21-89) y; weight 77 +/- 15 (47-139) kg; Cockcroft-Gault CrCL 71 +/- 27 (20-140) mL/min (values >140 set to 140; three patients); heart rate at inclusion 120 +/- 22 (74-173) bpm; beta-blockers 23%, sotalol 15%, rate-limiting calcium blockers 9%, ACE inhibitors 17%.

Dosing: 0.5 mg at inclusion (0.25 mg under 50 kg), 0.25 mg at 2 h and 6 h (up to 0.5 mg over 75 kg), optional extra dose; target total 0.015-0.020 mg/kg. Mean doses 0.48, 0.31, 0.32 mg (Table 2, p4). Samples: 49 patients early (0.35 +/- 0.06 h) and late (16.1 h), 56 late only; early 9.6 +/- 3.2 nM, late 1.6 +/- 1.0 nM. Assay Abbott TDX, LOQ 0.4 nM (1 mcg/L = 1.3 nM). Heart rate from digitised ECGs at 0, 2, 6, 12, 16 h in patients still in AF (n 98, 89, 67, 56, 53). NONMEM V, FO method; ADVAN3 TRANS4 for PK; ADVAN5 TRANS1 for PD; sequential (individual PK then PD).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: ventricular heart rate during ongoing AF (ECG), linear in the effect-compartment concentration: HR = baseline x (1 + theta_placebo x time) x (1 + theta_digoxin x Ce). Emax model tested; linear chosen (range of concentrations too narrow, Discussion). ke0 0.184 /h, equilibration half-time about 3.8 h. **No time to peak effect is printed.** Derived: after a single bolus on the typical plasma model the effect site peaks at 87 min (derived, 86.9 min). The authors note that with the trial's regimen maximal heart-rate reduction follows each dose by about an hour (Discussion p6, Fig 3). Placebo: about 0.5 bpm/h fall at baseline 125 bpm. Per ng/mL: -12.2% at the effect site (derived, x 1.3 nM). No inotropic end point; no conversion-to-sinus-rhythm model (no drug-placebo difference).

## Covariates
CrCL (Cockcroft-Gault, capped 140) on CL, linear, at the largest slope that kept population CL non-negative (negative below CrCL 20 if steeper). Gender, age and weight tested on CL, not retained. Heart failure history or presence did not influence the response.

## Caveats
- One or two samples per patient; peripheral parameters without BSV; FO estimation.
- The CrCL slope is a bound, not an estimate.
- PD residual error: 9.0% in Table 3 against "approximately 6%" in the text (p4).
- Elderly AF population (mean 67 y); patients on beta-blockers (23%) and calcium blockers (9%) included.
