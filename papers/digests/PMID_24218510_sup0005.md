# McCune 2014 supplement: Figures S1-S2 (weights, pcVPC)

## Study
Supplementary file to McCune 2014 (main card `PMID_24218510.md`); 3 pages: legends (p1), Supplemental Figure 1
(p2), Supplemental Figure 2 (p3). No tables, no numeric parameters.

## Model and parameters
- **Figure S1** (PDF p2): actual body weight (ABW) and predicted total body weight (TBW) plotted against dosing
  weight (DWT), with identity line; (A) whole data set (axes to 150 kg DWT, 160 kg ABW/TBW), (B) patients < 40 kg.
  DWT followed each institution's practice; ABW was known only for the FHCRC/Seattle "ABW-available cohort"; TBW was
  estimated from DWT by the main paper's Equation 3. Visually, adult ABW points lie mostly above identity
  (ABW > DWT) for DWT above about 50 kg; points below 40 kg cluster on identity.
- **Figure S2** (PDF p3): prediction-corrected VPC, (A) 0-24 h and (B) 0-6 h after start of infusion; log
  concentration axis 50-5000 ng/mL. Dashed lines observed 5th/95th percentiles, solid lines simulated; open
  circles and crosses the observed and simulated medians. In the figure as rendered, simulated and observed
  medians overlap closely through 0-6 h; beyond about 8 h the percentile bands diverge widely (sparse data).
