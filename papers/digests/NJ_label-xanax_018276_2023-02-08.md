# XANAX / XANAX XR label 2023: alprazolam

## Study
Product labels. No individual study design given for the PK statements; dose proportional 0.5-3.0 mg (XANAX
12.3); linear to 10 mg once daily (XANAX XR 12.3).

Metabolites (CYP3A4): 4-hydroxy and alpha-hydroxyalprazolam, < 4% of parent (IR label), "less than 10% and 4%"
respectively (XR label); relative potencies 0.20 and 0.66. Asians: Cmax ~15% and t1/2 ~25% higher. Smokers: up to
50% lower concentrations. AUC increase with ketoconazole 3.98-fold, itraconazole 2.66, nefazodone 1.98,
fluvoxamine 1.96, erythromycin 1.61; cimetidine CL -42%; fluoxetine CL -21%; oral contraceptives CL -22%.
Ritonavir, time dependent and in opposite directions (XANAX 12.3, PDF p. 18 = printed p. 18; XANAX XR 12.3, PDF
p. 43 = printed p. 19; both read on page images 2026-10-14): short-term low-dose ritonavir (4 doses of 200 mg)
raised mean alprazolam AUC about 2.5-fold, did not significantly change Cmax, and prolonged t1/2 to 30 h vs 13 h;
extended ritonavir (500 mg twice daily for 10 days) lowered alprazolam AUC by 12% and Cmax by 16%, t1/2 not
significantly changed, as CYP3A induction offsets the inhibition. Dosing (XANAX 2.6, PDF p. 4): halve the
alprazolam dose when ritonavir and alprazolam are started together, return to the target dose after 10-14 days,
no reduction in patients already on ritonavir for more than 10-14 days; ritonavir is the one strong CYP3A
inhibitor excepted from the contraindication.
XR: high-fat meal Cmax +25%, AUC and t1/2 unchanged. Volume of distribution is not given numerically.
Dosing: GAD 0.25-0.5 mg tid, max 4 mg/day; panic disorder 0.5 mg tid, trials 1-10 mg/day (mean 5-6); geriatric and
hepatic impairment 0.25 mg bid-tid; taper no more than 0.5 mg every 3 days; XR 0.5-1 mg once daily start, 3-6 mg
once daily usual; switch IR to XR at the same total daily dose.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No concentration-effect data, no ke0, no therapeutic concentration range. Not an opioid; no MEAC or potency
ratio (the metabolite relative potencies above are receptor binding / seizure models).

## Covariates
Age (elderly), obesity, alcoholic liver disease, Asian ancestry, smoking, CYP3A inhibitors/inducers (ritonavir both,
depending on duration): qualitative or summary-statistic effects only.

## Caveats
Summary values without study identity or n (except the n=16 young adults). The IR label's young-adult range
"6.3 to -15.8" is a typo for 6.3-15.8 (the XR label prints it correctly). Overall 6.3-26.9 h range spans young and
elderly subjects.
