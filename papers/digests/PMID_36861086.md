# Sader 2023: CLSI 2023 aminoglycoside breakpoints and US Enterobacterales susceptibility

## Study
SENTRY surveillance: **9809 Enterobacterales isolates**, one per patient, 37 US medical centres, 2017-2021 (UTI 39.5%, bloodstream 26.2%, pneumonia 19.6%, intra-abdominal 8.3%, skin 5.0%). Reference broth microdilution (CLSI M07-A11) at JMI Laboratories. Susceptibility by CLSI 2022, CLSI 2023 and US FDA 2022 criteria; AME and 16S rRNA methyltransferase genes by whole-genome sequencing in aminoglycoside-nonsusceptible isolates.

Amikacin 2023 breakpoints <= 4 / >= 16 (from <= 16 / >= 64); tobramycin <= 2 / >= 8.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
In vitro MIC only. The authors state the old breakpoints dated from the 1970s and were based on toxicity-related blood levels; the new ones on PK/PD (USCAST 2019). No clinical outcomes.

## Covariates
None.

## Caveats
Surveillance isolates from infection sites judged causal locally; US only; JMI is industry-funded (plazomicin focus). MIC90 is a population descriptor, not a target.
