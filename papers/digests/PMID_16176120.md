# van Hest 2005: population PK of mycophenolic acid in renal transplant recipients

## Study
- Retrospective analysis of a randomised concentration-controlled trial (RCCT) of oral mycophenolate mofetil (MMF) in adult renal transplant recipients; 140 patients, 6523 MPA plasma concentrations (PDF p1-2).
- Three MPA AUC target groups: 16.1, 32.2, 60.6 mg·h/L; starting MMF 450, 950, 1700 mg twice daily, then adjusted to target. All received ciclosporin and corticosteroids (PDF p2).
- Demographics (Table I, PDF p4, median (range), day 3): age 50 (19-70) y; weight 69 (37-104) kg; 88 male/52 female; 131 Caucasian; CLCR 31 (4-142) mL/min; albumin 34 (22-45) g/L; ciclosporin 600 (0-1400) mg/day. By day 140: CLCR 60 (25-123) mL/min, albumin 40 (32-53) g/L, ciclosporin 300 (150-700) mg/day. 23 delayed graft function.
- Sampling on 9 occasions (days 3, 7, 11, 21, 28, 56, 84, 112, 140): full profiles (pre-dose, 0.33, 0.66, 1.25, 2, 6, 8, 12 h) on the first three days; pre-dose to 2 h on the rest. Fasting overnight and 2 h post dose (PDF p3).
- Assay: validated HPLC, LLOQ 0.1 mg/L; total MPA (free MPA not measured).
- NONMEM V level 1.1, FO method, log-transformed data, inter- and intra-patient (inter-occasion) variability; bootstrap 1000 replicates (Wings for NONMEM).

Equations (PDF p8): V1 = 91·(CLCR/48)^−0.62·(Alb/30)^−1.13; CL = 32.5·(CLCR/48)^−0.12·(Alb/30)^−1.07·(CsA/450)^0.31·1.11^gender.

Structural model (no covariates), Table II: ka 3.8 /h, V1 98 L, V2 302 L, CL 31 L/h, Q 44 L/h, tlag 0.21 h. Bootstrap means match the final model (ka 4.1, V1 92, V2 239, CL 33, Q 35, tlag 0.21).

Checks (derived): V1 at CLCR 60 vs 10, Alb 30 = 79 and 241 L (text: 80 and 242); CL at CLCR 60 vs 10 = 31.6 and 39.2 L/h (text: 32 and 39), confirming the text's worked examples use the female reference.

Note: the abstract lists IIV (111/91/102/31%) and IOV (116/53/20%) from the final model, matching Table II.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD model. Exposure target cited from the literature: MPA AUC(0-12) 30-60 mg·h/L (ref 11, Shaw 2001) as minimising rejection and adverse effects. No ke0; not an opioid. Protein binding ~97.5% to albumin (cited, ref 6-7); free MPA not measured.

## Covariates
CLCR (Cockcroft-Gault, mL/min) and plasma albumin (g/L) on V1 and CL; ciclosporin daily dose and sex on CL (Eq. 6-7). Tested but dropped: age, race, weight, diabetes, AST/ALT, bilirubin, haemoglobin, MMF dose. Body weight was not retained. CLCR < 25 mL/min (n = 56 at day 3) had 34% higher CL on day 3 than CLCR > 25 (n = 84) (Fig. 5, PDF p11, printed 1093), the comparator being the other group in the same study.

## Caveats
- Early post-transplant population (days 3-140), with renal function and albumin recovering; all on ciclosporin (which lowers EHC). Not applicable to tacrolimus co-therapy or stable patients without adjustment; authors say EHC was not modelled and Cmax is slightly under-predicted.
- FO estimation; very high ka, V1 and V2 variability.
- Total MPA only; free MPA, the active moiety, was not measured, so higher CL in renal failure/hypoalbuminaemia may not mean lower free exposure (authors' point, PDF p12).
- Comparative claims: "higher clearance" with low CLCR is against the CLCR > 25 group of this study; the 18.1 L/h day-140 median CL is compared with Le Guellec 2004 stable patients (15.7 L/h for 64 kg).
