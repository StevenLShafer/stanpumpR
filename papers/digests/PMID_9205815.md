# Cheymol 1997: propranolol, labetalol and nebivolol IV in obese and normal-weight volunteers

## Study
Nine obese (157 +/- 24% of IBW, BMI 34 +/- 5.6, 99 +/- 23 kg) and nine normal-weight healthy volunteers (98 +/- 10% IBW,
BMI 21.4 +/- 2.6, 60 +/- 11 kg); each group 4 men and 5 women, aged 32 +/- 9 (controls) and 31 +/- 9 y, each with one
debrisoquine poor hydroxylator (Table 1, PDF p2). Randomized crossover, supine, fasting: single IV infusion over
5-10 min of rac-propranolol 0.108, rac-labetalol 0.99 or rac-nebivolol 0.073 mg base per kg IBW; 2-3 week washout.
Venous samples to 48 h. HPLC with fluorescence; labetalol LOQ 5 ng/mL; all concentrations as base (PDF p2).
Non-compartmental (SIPHAR): CL = dose/AUC, Vss = dose AUMC/AUC^2, Vz = CL/lambda_z. Nebivolol analysed with n = 8
(poor metabolisers excluded).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Haemodynamics monitored for safety: SBP, DBP, HR and CO fell after each drug, maximal at 1-2 h (BP, HR) and 0.5-2 h
(CO), ended by 4-5 h; CO fell less in the obese (P < 0.05) (Results PDF p3; Table 3 PDF p5, not re-read on image). No
concentration-effect analysis, no ke0.

## Covariates
Obesity: total Vss of labetalol higher in the obese, per-kg Vss not significantly different (lower), CL and t1/2
unchanged; Vss correlated with % IBW (r = 0.643, P < 0.01). Across five beta-blockers, log Vss = 0.23 log D7.4 + 2.1
(controls) and 0.25 log D7.4 + 2.2 (obese), r2 0.89-0.90 (PDF p4). Labetalol 50% protein bound (PDF p5, cited).

## Caveats
- Nine per group, non-compartmental; single dose sized by IBW. Table 1 prints creatinine as "mmol l-1" (80, 87),
  which must be umol/L (rendered PDF p2; source unit slip).
- Doses and concentrations are both as base, so CL is base/base.
