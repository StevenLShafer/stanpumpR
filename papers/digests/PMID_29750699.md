# Joosten 2018: closed-loop-assisted GDFT in major abdominal surgery

## Study
Single-centre (Erasme Hospital, Brussels) propensity-matched before-after study. Closed-loop GDFT (from Apr 2015; patients pooled from a randomised crystalloid-vs-colloid trial) n=104 vs historical non-GDFT controls (Jan 2013-Apr 2015) n=104 (Fig 1, p3); elective open major abdominal surgery, ASA <=3. Matching: GLM on age, sex, ASA, weight, P-POSSUM morbidity and mortality, anaesthesia time; caliper 0.02. Closed-loop: baseline 3 mL/kg/h balanced crystalloid plus 100 mL boluses over 6 min when predicted SV rise >=15% (p1-2).
Anaesthesia (p2): propofol 1.5-2.5 mg/kg (lean-body-weight-adjusted), remifentanil target 2-6 ng/mL using the Minto model (induction and maintenance) or sufentanil, rocuronium 0.5 mg/kg; titrated to BIS/entropy 40-60. Remifentanil effect-site vs plasma target is not stated.

## Model and parameters
No PK model is reported. Results (abstract): net intra-operative fluid balance median [IQR] 2.9 [1.6-4.4] vs 6.2 [4.0-8.3] mL/kg/h, P<0.001; total fluid in 5.9 [4.9-7.6] vs 9.5 [7.0-11.5] mL/kg/h (p4). Major complications 17% vs 32% (P=0.015), minor 31% vs 45% (P=0.032), hospital LOS 10 [6-15] vs 12 [9-18] days (P=0.022).
