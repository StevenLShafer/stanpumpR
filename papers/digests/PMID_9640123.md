# Servin 1998: Diprifusor TCI vs manual propofol, multicentre

## Study
Prospective, randomised, open, 29 centres, n=562 (TCI 283, manual 279), age 18-85 (one 12-year-old included by error), ASA I:II:III 183:88:12 TCI, 175:94:10 manual (Table 1, PDF p2). Propofol 1% by Diprifusor TCI (plasma-targeted) or manual syringe pump. TCI induction target 4-6 ug/mL recommended or at anaesthetist's discretion; manual bolus 1200 mL/h to loss of verbal contact. Opioids/premedication at local discretion. No concentrations were assayed.

## Model and parameters
No PK model fitted (Diprifusor's built-in model is used, not described here).

Key outcomes (mean (SD)): dose to loss of consciousness 1.69 (0.50) TCI vs 2.31 (0.75) mg/kg manual (p<0.001); mean TCI target at LOC 5.7 ug/mL (range 2.5-12.0); time to LOC 71 (54) vs 61 (31) s; overall rate 12.1 (5.1) vs 11.0 (6.0) mg/kg/h; eye opening 15.4 vs 13.8 min. TCI target at LOC by ASA: I 6.07, II 5.08, III 4.46 ug/mL (p=0.06). Fig 1: target at LOC = 7.18 - 0.03 x age ug/mL (r=0.32), TCI group (formula from the figure legend, PDF p3; text layer only).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: loss of consciousness = loss of verbal contact; the TCI target at that moment is a plasma (not effect-site) value during a rising target, so it is not an EC50 or steady-state value.
- Premedication lowered target at LOC (Fig 2, p84: roughly 5.3 hydroxyzine, 5.2 benzodiazepine, 6.5 none ug/mL, bar heights read by eye; difference p<0.001).
- Opioid audit: no MEAC, ke0, t1/2ke0 or tPeak measured. Equianalgesic/potency weights used to convert pre-induction opioid to fentanyl equivalents: fentanyl 1 : alfentanil 7.5 : sufentanil 0.1 (Methods, p83, citing Brunner 1994 and Stanski & Hug 1982). Low opioid group < 2 ug/kg fentanyl equivalents (n=269), high >= 2 (n=266). Table 3 (image-confirmed): mean doses low/high fentanyl 1.38/3.45, alfentanil 10.4/25.6, sufentanil 0.16/0.47 ug/kg. Opioid amount did not change TCI target at LOC (5.66 vs 5.62 ug/mL) but high dose gave larger SAP fall (TCI -17.7% vs -13.3%; manual -14.4% vs -12.0%). Sufentanil lowered target more than fentanyl (Fig 3, not read). Discussion says fentanyl and sufentanil time to peak effect are similar (cites Shafer & Varvel 1991; no value given).

## Covariates
Age (target at LOC falls with age), ASA class, premedication, pre-induction opioid.

## Caveats
Open design, prototype TCI systems, investigators inexperienced with TCI, often a single target for all patients; heterogeneous co-medication. Paper's own table units: opioid doses ug/kg, TCI dose mg/kg.
