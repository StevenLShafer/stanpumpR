# Short 2002: efficient trial design for a two-drug response surface

## Study
Simulation study (Excel 7.0 spreadsheet, Solver) of three designs for eliciting a PK-PD response surface for two synergistic IV drugs (printed p400-401). Hypothetical drugs A and B take PK/PD from published alfentanil (Scott & Stanski 1987; Maitre 1987) and midazolam (Buhrer 1990) models, with an interaction term approximated from benzodiazepine-opioid studies. Designs: "slices" (drug A at pseudo-steady-state, 16 min zero-order infusions of B), "crisscross" (slices, with drug roles swapped in half the patients), "radial" (both drugs infused together at fixed ratios). 5, 10, 20 and 40 cumulative "patients", 10 simulated trials each. Variability: normal, heteroscedastic, CV 0.2 or 0.3 on parameters; homoscedastic CV 0.2 on observed effect. Pooled-data analysis, extended least squares, ke0 recovered by a semiparametric hysteresis collapse.

## Model and parameters
Response-surface model (eqs 1-5): each drug normalised to its C50 (Ue = C/C50); theta = UeB/(UeA+UeB); effect = E0 + (Emax-E0)*(Ue/C50(theta))^n / (1+(Ue/C50(theta))^n); C50(theta) = 1 - b2*theta + b2*theta^2 (b0=b1=1 by definition; simulated b2 = 2, b3 = b4 = 0). Simulated drug parameters (Table 4 "Original" column, printed p407, text layer partly scrambled): drug A C50 450, Emax 100, n 4; drug B C50 150, Emax 100, n 4 (unit only given as ng/mL in the Fig 1 legend). Table 1 (printed p403) lists target concentrations (e.g. drug A pseudo-steady-state 0, 56, 113, 225, 450; drug B 16 min infusion peaks 332, 273, 209, 95, 65 for slices).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No real ke0 or end point. Equilibration times quoted for planning: three to five equilibration half-lives, about 15 min for alfentanil and 45 min for midazolam (printed p407-408). Not an opioid PD study; no MEAC or potency ratio reported. Opioid audit: none.

## Covariates
None.

## Caveats
Simulation only; the surface was symmetric, which may have favoured the slices design; assumes sigmoidal responses at all ratios and no time-dependent interactions; variability smaller than a heterogeneous patient population. Result: crisscross needs 20 subjects, the others 40 (abstract, printed p400).
