# Schomerus 1976: physiological disposition of verapamil in man

## Study
Five male volunteer patients, 51-69 years, normal renal and hepatic function by examination and laboratory tests, normal
ECG, fasted overnight (PDF p2-3). 14C-D,L-verapamil HCl (labelled in the nitrile group) given as 10 mg IV infused over
45 min by constant-rate syringe (30 mL saline) in three men (J.H., R.N., M.K.; weights 87, 54, 66 kg), and as 80 mg oral
solution (100 mL water, 8 am) in three men (J.H., J.S., W.S.; weights 87, 60, 90 kg); J.H. had both. Plasma verapamil by
mass fragmentography (limit about 1 ng/mL; measurable to 24 h after IV, 12 h after oral); 14C-activity by liquid
scintillation; urine and faeces 5 days. Protein binding by equilibrium dialysis at 37 C, pH 7.4, plasma from lab staff,
verapamil 50-500 x 10^-9 g/mL. Two-compartment open model fitted by least-squares regression of log concentration; the
IV curve was corrected for infusion duration (Loo-Riegelman, eqs 1-2). Desk calculator (Olivetti 652), no population fit.
Dose basis: the labelled compound is the HCl salt, and the dose is stated as 10 mg and 80 mg of the labelled compound
(whether HCl or base is not stated for the mg).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No pharmacodynamic measurement, no ke0 or tPeak, no concentration/effect relationship and no MEAC (not an opioid).
IV infusion of 10 mg over 45 min gave plasma 45-90 ng/mL within 20 min, constant during infusion (PDF p4). Oral peak 30-45 min
after dosing (PDF p5). Metabolites: the authors report preliminary data that the N-demethylated and N-dealkylated metabolites
are at least twice the parent's plasma level and might contribute to effect (PDF p8, text layer). 14C-activity beta half-life
about 24 h versus about 3 h for unchanged drug; verapamil AUC 0-24 h was only 14.9-18.2% of 14C AUC after IV, and 0.9-3.8% in
the first 12 h after oral (PDF p4-5).

## Covariates
None analysed (n = 3 per route, ages 51-69). Weight is given but parameters are absolute, not per-kg.

## Caveats
n = 3 per route, one man in both; racemate; IV given as a 45-min infusion and the oral dose 8 x the IV dose, so oral PK use
an assumed F; no oral 10 mg data because the assay found no measurable concentrations; verapamil measurable only 24 h (IV) /
12 h (oral), so the terminal beta phase is defined over a short span (about 2-3 half-lives). Table 1 constants are not
mutually consistent (see open question). Whole-plasma verapamil by mass fragmentography; protein binding is in vitro with
non-patient plasma.
