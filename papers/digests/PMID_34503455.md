# Jang 2021: propofol Ce and UMSS sedation score in children

## Study
Prospective single-centre (Seoul National University Hospital) PD modelling study, 30 children aged 3-6 y (mean 4.8, SD 0.95), 19.2 kg (SD 4.8), height 109.6 cm, 16 M / 14 F, scheduled for surgery >1 h under general anesthesia (Table 2); 32 enrolled, 2 lost. Premedication midazolam 0.1 mg/kg IV; lidocaine 0.5 mg/kg; then propofol by effect-site TCI (Kim and Choi model, ref 16 = Choi 2015) starting at Ce 1 ug/mL, held 2 min, then +1 ug/mL steps up to 6 ug/mL. UMSS (0-4) assessed by a blinded anesthesiologist every 20 s during induction; 237 Ce-UMSS pairs used (Ce at start of infusion, at each score increment, and mid-period). NONMEM 7.4.4 Laplace, logistic model, no IIV (naive pooled; IIV gave >30% shrinkage), 1000 bootstraps. Rocuronium and remifentanil given after UMSS 4. Plasma propofol not measured, no EEG monitor.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: UMSS (0 awake, 1 minimally sedated, 2 moderately, 3 deeply, 4 unarousable; Table 1), response defined as score >= n. Ce is the TCI-displayed effect-site concentration from the Kim and Choi pediatric model (ke0 not printed in this paper).
- Highest-probability UMSS by Ce (Table 4, PDF p.8): 0 below 1.9; 1 for 1.9-<2.7; 2 for 2.7-<4.0; 3 for 4.0-<4.8; 4 for >=4.8 ug/mL.
- Authors' suggested initial targets (discussion, p.7): 1.5-2.0 (UMSS 1), 2.0-3.0 (UMSS 2), 3.5-4.0 ug/mL (UMSS 3). They suggest slightly lower targets on Kataria or Paedfusor pumps.
- Comparison models (Bland-Altman, 23 patients, 166 points, Fig. 4 / text p.5): Kim and Choi Ce is higher than Kataria by 24.2% (CI 21.8-26.6) and than Paedfusor by 27.9% (CI 25.1-30.7).
- Cited external values: Munoz 2006 ECe50 3.65 ug/mL at BIS 50 in children 3-11 y; Ce 4.3 ug/mL for immobilisation in proton therapy.
- Not reported: ke0, t1/2ke0, tPeak, hysteresis (steps of 2 min, so equilibration assumed). Not an opioid paper; no MEAC, no potency ratio.

## Covariates
None modelled (IIV and covariates not estimated).

## Caveats
Authors: induction of general anesthesia rather than procedural sedation; data stop at UMSS 4; no EEG; no plasma concentrations; midazolam premedication; naive pooled analysis with no IIV, so the CI reflects between-child scatter only approximately. Ce scale depends on the Kim and Choi model, which is not commercially available. 3-6 y only (19 kg mean).
