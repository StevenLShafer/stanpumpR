# Tse 1987: oral [14C]tizanidine in healthy men

## Study
- 18 healthy men. Single-dose arm: 12 men (age 21-48 y, 57-86 kg), randomised 6 to 5 mg and 6 to 20 mg oral [14C]tizanidine tablets, fasted 8 h before and 2 h after (PDF p. 2). Multiple-dose arm: 6 men (age 20-37 y, 63-79 kg), 4 mg every 8 h x 13 doses (about 07:00, 15:00, 23:00), open label (PDF p. 2).
- Single-dose blood samples: pre, 0.5, 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 9, 12, 24, 36, 48, 72, 96, 120 h. Multiple-dose samples to 216 h, with dense sampling 96-108 h.
- Total radioactivity by combustion and liquid scintillation, in blood, urine and faeces. Parent drug in the single-dose study by HPLC with reverse isotope dilution (6.6% overall CV; limit 0.034 ng/mL for the 5 mg dose and 0.14 ng/mL for the 20 mg dose). Parent drug in the multiple-dose study by a radioimmunoassay (minimum detectable 0.05-0.42 ng/mL), specific for tizanidine (PDF p. 3). Whole blood, not plasma, is the matrix.
- Noncompartmental analysis only (trapezoidal AUC, terminal log-linear half-life). No compartmental model, no software named. First-pass estimated with Gibaldi's equation using hepatic blood flow 1.7 L/min.

Not reported: clearance, volume of distribution, any compartment rate constants, absorption rate constant, plasma concentrations, protein binding, weight-normalised values. The bioavailability figure is an estimate that depends on an assumed hepatic blood flow and on faecal parent drug standing for unabsorbed drug.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD end point measured and no ke0, MEAC, EC50 or potency ratio. Not an opioid paper. The Introduction mentions antinociceptive activity in animals that is stronger than morphine's, with no concentration given.

## Covariates
None analysed. Healthy men only, weight 57-86 kg, no sex, age, renal, hepatic or CYP1A2 comparison.

## Caveats
- Single oral doses of 5 and 20 mg and 4 mg q8h only. Linear over 5-20 mg judged from dose-proportional levels. Linearity is not tested for a wider range.
- Whole-blood concentrations of parent drug; parent is about 5% of blood radioactivity at Cmax.
- The radioactivity terminal phase (61-85 h) is attributed by the authors to a metabolite reversibly bound to tissue; effective half-life (16 h) is a better guide to accumulation.
- Parent drug was assayed by different methods in the single-dose study (isotope dilution) and the multiple-dose study (RIA), and the tizanidine terminal half-life differed (about 3 h vs 2.1 h).
- Standard deviation of tizanidine Cmax is large (CV about 40-90%).
