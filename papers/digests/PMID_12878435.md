# Patsalos & Perucca 2003, Part II: AEDs and other drugs

## Study
Narrative review (MEDLINE/PubMed search 2 Sept 2002 to 10 Feb 2003, English, authors' selection; PDF p7). The
companion Part I (Lancet Neurol 2003;2:347-56, PMID 12849151) covers AED-AED interactions; this part covers AEDs
with non-AED drugs, grouped by therapeutic class. No new data and no meta-analysis.

## Model and parameters
None. Quantified statements, each a quoted literature value (page given):
- Praziquantel plasma concentration reduced on average 74% (phenytoin) and 90% (carbamazepine); albendazole
  metabolism increased 50-66% by inducers (p1).
- Macrolides: erythromycin and troleandomycin up to fourfold carbamazepine concentration (p2, Panel 1).
- Enzyme inducers lower itraconazole more than tenfold (p2); carbamazepine lowered indinavir up to 16-fold;
  ritonavir raises carbamazepine two- to threefold (p2).
- Phenytoin lowers losartan's active metabolite E3174 by 63% (p4); valproate raises nimodipine 50% (p4).
- Sucralfate lowers phenytoin bioavailability 20-30% (p4); omeprazole raises phenytoin about 25% (p4).
- Valproate raises amitriptyline and nortriptyline 50-60% (p5); risperidone raises carbamazepine 20% (p5).
- Phenytoin may raise sirolimus metabolism up to fourfold (p5); danazol raises carbamazepine 50-100% (p6).
- **Oral midazolam concentrations fall 95% with enzyme-inducing AEDs**; the authors expect much less effect after
  IV midazolam, whose clearance depends more on liver blood flow (p6).
- Oral contraceptives lower lamotrigine 40-65% at steady state (p7).
- Inducers also stimulate the metabolism of fentanyl, methadone, pethidine, paracetamol, theophylline, vecuronium
  and other non-depolarising relaxants (p7; no magnitudes).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Not a PK/PD paper. No MEAC, ke0 or potency values. Clinical end points are toxicity or loss of effect of the
affected drug.

## Covariates
Not applicable.

## Caveats
- Selective narrative review; most magnitudes come from single studies or case reports quoted without CIs.
- Valproate is said to raise diazepam and lorazepam (p6), with no magnitude.
