# Chan 2021: pooled adult + paediatric pregabalin population PK (Pfizer)

## Study
- Pooled 10 Pfizer studies: 2 paediatric FOS studies, 3 adult phase III FOS studies, 4 phase I healthy-adult studies, 1 renal-impairment study (Randinitis 2003).
- 724 adults (median age 38, range 17-75 y; weight 75.5, 40-180 kg; 50.7% female; 4,317 samples) and 255 children (3 months-16 y; 941 samples) (Table 1).
- Adult CLcr median 108 (42.2-261) mL/min, renal-impairment study 46.5 (10.0-122); adult NCLcr 101 (49-227) and 42 (9.94-117) mL/min/1.73 m^2. Children: CLcr 15.5-293 mL/min; NCLcr median 149 (74-315).
- NONMEM 7.3, FOCE-I. Assay not described in the paper.
- Renal function: Cockcroft-Gault (SCr, age, weight, sex) at >= 13 y; modified Schwartz, K = 0.55 (1-12 y), K = 0.45 (< 1 y), in mL/min/1.73 m^2 (Methods p.133). For the absolute-CLcr model Schwartz was converted to mL/min with individual BSA. NCLcr = BSA-normalised CLcr. BSA formula not given (in Appendix).

## Model and parameters
One compartment, first-order absorption with lag, first-order elimination. ka "modeled as a fraction of the elimination rate constant to avoid flip-flop" (Methods); Table 2 footnote f repeats this, yet tabulates ka as 10.0 /hr. Clearance proportional to NCLcr up to an estimated breakpoint, constant above. Weight on CL/F and V/F as power functions normalised to 70 kg; male reference sex. Covariate equations are in the Appendix (not available).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Exposure-response on log 28-day seizure rate (not analgesia): common Emax -0.924, EC50 4.69 ug/mL (children + adults, Table 3); adult-only EC50 5.93 ug/mL. No ke0, no effect delay, no MEAC (not an opioid).

## Covariates
NCLcr (breakpoint 96.4), weight (power, 70 kg), sex on CL/F and V/F, food on ka and Tlag. Age/race not retained beyond these.

## Caveats
- Covariate equations and BSA formula only in an unavailable Appendix.
- ka footnote vs tabulated value is internally ambiguous (absolute 10/h vs 10 x k).
- High V/F shrinkage (60.7%), sparse sampling around Tmax in phase III; authors flag Cmax prediction as a limitation.
- Breakpoint partly reflects Cockcroft-Gault overestimation in overweight subjects (Methods).
