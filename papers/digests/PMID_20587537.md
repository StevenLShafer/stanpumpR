# Soehle 2010: PSI vs BIS during propofol

## Study
- 17 ASA I-II adults, 18-65 y, ENT surgery (7 F, 10 M); age 36 (12) y, weight 74 (17) kg, height 172 (11) cm, BMI 25.0 (4.4) (p174). Midazolam 7.5 mg po 45 min before.
- Propofol 2% at a fixed 2000 mg/h until burst suppression >60% (BIS) or MAP <60 mm Hg, stopped until BIS >60, repeated up to 3 cycles (1.5 cycles in 1 patient, 2 in 9, 3 in 7). No surgical stimulus; spontaneous breathing.
- PSI (PSA 4000, release 3.00.09, 25 s averaging) and BIS (A-2000, v4.0, 15 s smoothing) recorded together every 5 s.
- No blood sampled. Plasma concentration was calculated with the Schnider 1998 propofol PK set (Excel spreadsheet of Bruhn and Bouillon); Ce from dCe/dt = ke0 (Cpl - Ce). So ke0 and C50 are conditional on the Schnider PK set.
- Two fits: individual two-stage (Excel solver, ke0, E0, Emax, Ce50, gamma fitted together to collapse hysteresis) and population NONMEM V (FOCE; IIV on Ce50 and ke0; additive residual error).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End points: PSI and BIS (processed EEG), not clinical response. Not an opioid; no MEAC.
- ke0 (min^-1): PSI 0.088 individual, 0.103 population; BIS 0.104 individual, 0.131 population (Table 1). Authors give t1/2ke0 about 7 min for propofol (p176, text); (derived: ln2/0.104 = 6.7 min for BIS, ln2/0.088 = 7.9 min for PSI). No tPeak reported.
- Ce50 1.38-1.94 ug/mL, low because no stimulus (authors' explanation, p176).
- Recommended index ranges map to Ce: PSI 25-50 to 1.2-2.6 ug/mL, BIS 40-60 to 1.4-2.7 ug/mL (population fit, Fig 4, p175).
- PSI is 10-15 points lower than BIS at high Ce (lower Emax).

## Covariates
None modelled (no weight, age, sex effects on PD). Schnider PK used for Cpl.

## Caveats
- Ce is predicted from a PK set (Schnider 1998) with no measured propofol; the PD parameters (ke0 especially) are tied to that set. Authors note Marsh would be expected to differ in elderly and obese patients, none enrolled.
- Healthy, no surgical stimulus, midazolam premedication, slow ramp-up at a constant rate: ke0 reflects the BIS/PSI processing delay as well as drug equilibration (authors discuss time lag and averaging).
- Fisherian parameters are means of 17 individual fits; the sigma and Emax estimates are near bounds (Emax PSI 0.3 with SD 1.1).
