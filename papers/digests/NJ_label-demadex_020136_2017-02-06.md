Metabolism by CYP2C9, minor CYP2C8 and CYP2C18; M5 (carboxylic acid, inactive) is the major metabolite, M1 and M3 have
some activity at much lower exposure (PDF p9). CHF: total clearance about 50% of healthy (PDF p10). Digoxin raises
torsemide AUC 50% (PDF p10).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
12.2 (PDF p8-9): oral onset within 1 h, peak in hour 1-2, duration 6-8 h; sodium excretion linear over 2.5-20 mg;
hypertension effect near-maximal at 4-6 weeks. No concentration-effect model or therapeutic range. Not an opioid.
Drug interactions (PDF p5-6): CYP2C9 inhibitors (amiodarone, fluconazole, miconazole, oxandrolone) raise and inducers
(rifampin) lower torsemide concentrations; NSAIDs blunt the effect; cholestyramine: give torsemide 1 h before or 4-6 h
after.

Dosing (2.1-2.2, PDF p2-3): HF edema 10 or 20 mg once daily, doses above 200 mg not adequately studied; CKD 20 mg;
cirrhosis 5 or 10 mg with an aldosterone antagonist, above 40 mg not studied; hypertension 5 mg, then 10 mg after 4-6
weeks.

## Covariates
Qualitative: renal and hepatic impairment, elderly, heart failure (12.3, PDF p9-10). Pregnancy: no human data (8.1,
PDF p6); pediatric safety not established (8.4, PDF p6-7). Contraindicated in anuria, hepatic coma, hypersensitivity
to torsemide or povidone (4, PDF p3).

## Caveats
- Tablet label only; no IV PK. No study sizes given.
- "This label may not be the latest approved" banner on every page.
