# Henthorn 2018: ketamine arterial and venous, recirculatory-compartmental model

PDF page = printed page - 259 (printed 260-270 = PDF 1-11).

## Study
Ten healthy men (age 24-62, mean 32 +/- 11 y; weight 68-92 kg, mean 75.6 +/- 7.5 kg). Each received a 30-min constant
infusion of about 7 mg of S(+) ketamine hydrochloride and, at least 3 days apart, of R(-) ketamine hydrochloride
(means 7.1 +/- 0.5 and 7.1 +/- 0.3 mg; doses corrected for measured solution concentration; PDF pp. 2-4). Simultaneous
radial artery and forearm vein samples: S(+) at 0, 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, 40, 50, 60, 120, 180, 300 min; R(-) at 0-30 every 5,
36, 43, 50, 180, 300, 420 min; venous only at 540 and 660 min. Enantioselective HPLC, plasma, LLOQ 2 ng/mL (CV 4.1-8.5%),
values below LLOQ censored (M3 method as a check). Phoenix NLME 7.0, FOCE-ELS; log-normal parameters; relative residual
error; model choice by BIC; VPC of 1000 subjects. Plasma (not blood) concentrations; total concentrations.

## Model and parameters
Final model (Fig 1, PDF p. 4): three-compartment mammillary model shared by both enantiomers (same V2, V3, Cl2, Cl3;
VC and ClE linked by eq. 9: VC,S = VC,R x ClE,S / ClE,R); infusion enters a 1 L unmixed first-pass volume that
empties into VC at the total pharmacokinetic flow (sum of clearances, eq. 2: arterial = R0/SigmaCl + central); venous
= F x PCarm x Carm + (1 - F) x Ccentral (eq. 4) with the arm compartment linked to the central one by karm0, and
PCarm = V2/VC (3.04, derived).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None: purely pharmacokinetic. No ke0, tPeak, hysteresis, MEAC or analgesic concentration, no potency ratio. (Not an
opioid.) The authors note that arterial samples during an infusion contain an unmixed "infusion artifact" of R0/SigmaCl,
which would distort PK-PD fits and the context-sensitive half-time (PDF pp. 7-9).

## Covariates
None tested for the final model beyond enantiomer (shared V2, V3, Cl2, Cl3; ClE differs) and occasion; all subjects male
and healthy. No weight scaling: 10 men, 68-92 kg.

## Caveats
Healthy, awake men only; doses about 7 mg (about 0.09 mg/kg) with sampling to 11 h, so many late values fall below the
2 ng/mL LLOQ and V3 and ClE are the least secure (M3 vs censoring changed them slightly, PDF p. 6). Table 2's volumes
describe an arterial/mixing model and are not interchangeable with a standard model: the paper's Table 3 shows a
3-compartment fit to arterial data alone has VC 15.4 L and to venous data 54.2 L. Dose basis (salt vs base) not stated.
Total pharmacokinetic flow 7.59 L/min converts to cardiac output of about 6.5 L/min only with an assumed
erythrocyte:plasma ratio and haematocrit 0.45 (PDF p. 8).
