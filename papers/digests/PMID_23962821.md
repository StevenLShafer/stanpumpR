# Cowley 2013: Marsh model in effect-site mode, neurosurgical propofol TCI

## Study
Prospective observational study, Queen Elizabeth Hospital Birmingham, Nov 2011-May 2012. 50 ASA I-III adults (of 57 consented) undergoing elective neurosurgery with propofol TCI by the Marsh model in effect-site mode plus remifentanil (Minto, effect site), on an Orchestra Base Primea pump. Total body weight went into the Marsh model. Table 1 (PDF p.3, confirmed on the image): age 53 (15) y, weight 78 (16) kg, height 167 (10) cm, BMI 28 (5) kg/m2, ASA I/II/III 6/30/14 patients, sex M:F 18:32 (mean (SD)). Median propofol target 3.5 ug/mL (range 2.5-5). Arterial samples at least 10 min after any change in target, typically at 15, 30, 60 min then hourly; 254 samples (mean 5.1 per patient, range 3-9), sampling period mean 197 min (56-650). Plasma (not whole blood) propofol by Pelorus 1000 analyser (colorimetric, 0.7 mL), CV 1.4% at 2.84 ug/mL and 1.2% at 6.68 ug/mL, bias 0.15 ug/mL against HPLC (95% CI -0.11 to 0.41), linear 0-12 ug/mL (page 2 image). No depth-of-anaesthesia monitor was used by any of the 18 anaesthetists.

## Model and parameters
No model was fitted. The model under test was Marsh 1991 (propofol, plasma/effect-site TCI); its V1 is proportional to body weight (discussion). Performance indices follow Varvel 1992 (PMID_1588504): PE = (measured - predicted)/predicted x 100 (PDF p.2 image).

Further pooled results (text): mean PE 51.6% early (15-30 min) vs 26.9% later (>30 min). BMI under 30 (n = 159 samples) 23% vs BMI at least 30 (n = 95 samples) 47%, P < 0.001; regression of PE on BMI R2 0.13. Higher ASA (class 3), age over 70 y and female sex had significantly lower mean PE (P = 0.01, 0.004, 0.02); numbers small, particularly over 70 y. Anaesthetists changed the propofol target 0.3 times/h and remifentanil 2.7 times/h.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No pharmacodynamic measurement (no BIS or EEG). Only the pump's setting is stated: ke0 1.21 /min, t½ke0 0.57 min, tPeak 1.6 min (propofol, effect-site mode, Marsh); these are model settings, not measured, and not tied to an end point in this paper. Concentration end point: measured plasma propofol (ug/mL). No MEAC or potency ratio. Remifentanil (Minto model) was co-infused, with no remifentanil concentrations reported; no opioid audit values.

## Covariates
Pre-specified categorical groups: sex, age (over 70 y), BMI (30 kg/m2 cut), ASA status (over 2); t-tests on pooled samples. Obesity contributed around half the bias.

## Caveats
- Sampling only in maintenance and at least 10 min after target changes; plasma stands in for the unmeasurable effect-site concentration.
- Co-administered remifentanil and other drugs were not controlled; authors cite conflicting evidence on a propofol-remifentanil PK interaction.
- Plasma rather than whole blood: red-cell binding could explain the early bias (authors' hypothesis, citing an abstract).
- Pooled samples treat the repeated samples of one patient as independent; subgroup n is small for age over 70 y.
- Text-layer oddities: the Table 1 and Table 2 layout scrambles in the text, so the rendered page was used.
- A positive PE here means measured above predicted, so propofol concentrations were underpredicted (the model's concentrations were too low).

## Verification against stanpumpR
- Nothing cites the paper, so there is no repo claim to verify.
- The library has no Marsh model (R/ has no Marsh model; "Marsh" appears only in unrelated comments, e.g. R/drugs_enoxaparin.R:110). Propofol in the library is Eleveld 2018 (R/drugs_propofol.R:276-277) and a Schnider companion (R/alternativeModels.R, named in drugs_propofol.R:78-79). The paper's performance numbers therefore describe a model the library does not run and cannot be compared with its curves.
- tPeak: the paper's 1.6 min equals the repo's tPeak 1.600 min (R/drugs_propofol.R:276, attributed there to Schnider 1999). Same value, independent source; the paper's ke0 1.21 /min belongs to Marsh's own PK and is not applicable to Eleveld's.
- Weight basis: Marsh with total body weight overshoots in obesity (BMI at least 30: 47% against 23%); the library's Eleveld model carries its own fat-free-mass covariate (drugs_propofol.R:75-76), so this finding is a motivation, not a check.
