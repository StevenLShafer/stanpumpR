# McKay 2006: sevoflurane effect-site model on spectral entropy

## Study
- 20 ASA I-II adults (16 women), age 18-63 y (mean 38 ± 13), weight 81 ± 17 kg, elective gynaecological/general/orthopaedic surgery, unpremedicated (PDF p.2, p.4).
- Cycle 1 (clean, no other drugs): facemask, semi-closed circuit, fresh gas 4 L/min; 3% inspired sevoflurane for 2 min, then 7% inspired until RE <= 20, held 2 more min, then off until RE returned to 70 (lightening). Cycle 2: re-deepening after airway placement, with propofol, fentanyl and relaxants at the anaesthetist's discretion; only 11 of 20 had a second sevoflurane cycle (PDF p.2, p.4).
- Effect measured: Datex-Ohmeda M-Entropy Response Entropy (RE, 0-100) and State Entropy (SE, 0-91), sampled at 0.2 Hz. End-tidal sevoflurane from the minimum expired value, sampled at 100 Hz. Analysis in MatLab 6 (nlinfit).
- Method: ke0 found by stepping ke0 and picking the value giving the greatest R² of measured vs modelled entropy per subject (i.e. minimising hysteresis); end-tidal concentration is taken as a stand-in for arterial partial pressure. Sigmoid inhibitory Emax fitted per subject; deepening and lightening phases then fitted separately with ke0 held at the full-cycle value (PDF p.3-4).

## Model and parameters
Effect-site: dCeff/dt = ke0 (Cet - Ceff). Effect = Emax - (Emax - Emin) × Ceff^γ / (EC50^γ + Ceff^γ), with Emax and Emin the individual's maximum and minimum entropy (PDF p.3).

Table 1 (PDF p.5, printed p.95; first cycle, whole loop, mean ± SD, n = 20):

| SE | 87.9 ± 2.9 | 17.7 ± 5.6* | 2.65 ± 1.75 | 7.4 ± 3.5* | 1.7 ± 0.4 |

Goodness of fit: median weighted residual 0.05 (no bias); median absolute weighted residual 11.7% in the Results (PDF p.4), 11.6% in the abstract (a discrepancy within the paper), lower when phases were fitted separately.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: processed-EEG spectral entropy (RE and SE) of the Datex-Ohmeda module; hysteresis between end-tidal sevoflurane and entropy was minimised to get ke0. t½ke0 = 2.4 ± 1.5 min (RE), 2.65 ± 1.75 min (SE), Table 1. The authors say this agrees with Ellerkmann 2004 (ke0 2.1, EC50 1.6; units not given in this paper) (PDF p.4).
- Hysteresis is shown for one subject in Fig. 1a. A 5 s shift of entropy relative to end-tidal did not change ke0 (2.4 ± 1.5 and 2.3 ± 1.5 min).
- γ: 5.9 full cycle (Ellerkmann about 4); path dependent, 61.1 deepening vs 5.7 lightening (RE). The authors attribute this mainly to pharmacodynamics (sudden state transition), not kinetics: shifting ke0 by ±30% changed γ by only 11-12% (PDF p.4-5). They note γ is a tangent at the steepest point, so it is very unstable for steep curves.
- Entropy was almost unresponsive above 3% effect-site sevoflurane (PDF p.4).
- Loss of consciousness (response to voice) occurred at RE 81.5 ± 24.1 (awake 97.1 ± 1.7) and SE 73.0 ± 25.0 (awake 88.0 ± 2.9), P < 0.01; the entropy value at LOC varied widely between patients, usually before the rapid fall (Fig. 4).
- Cycle-1 parameters predicted cycle-2 entropy poorly (Bland-Altman limits 33.8 ± 13.8 vs 17.4 ± 8.3 for cycle 2 predicting itself, P < 0.05), possibly due to supplemental drugs and patients being already unconscious.
- No MAC, MEAC or potency ratio is reported. Not an opioid paper.

## Covariates
None tested (age and weight only described).

## Caveats
- End-tidal partial pressure assumed equal to arterial; end-tidal sampling during assisted ventilation was by minimum expired value.
- Small n, one centre; γ and EC50 varied widely between subjects; entropy plateau above 3%.
- Eleven of 20 patients contributed a second cycle; supplemental drugs were uncontrolled.
- Equipment supplied by Datex-Ohmeda.
- The EC50 unit is never stated in the tables.
