# Kim 2015: paroxetine population PK from Korean TDM data, and exposure versus outcome

## Study
- Retrospective TDM data, Samsung Medical Center, Seoul, 2005-2011: 271 steady-state serum concentrations from
  127 Korean psychiatric outpatients (MDD or anxiety disorder) (Table 1).
- Age 67 +/- 13 y (median 71, range 24-90); weight 59.4 +/- 10.0 kg (median 58, 37.0-86.9; n = 106);
  albumin 4.1 g/dL; 83 male (65.4%), 44 female.
- Serum paroxetine 61.6 +/- 76.6 ug/L (median 39.9, range 4.4-761.6). Daily doses from >=10 to <=52.5 mg; IR
  (63.8% of concentrations), CR (12.9%), or both. Once daily in all but one subject (twice daily).
- Concentrations per subject: 1 (52.8%), 2 (18.1%), 3 (8.7%), >=4 (20.5%).
- Assay: HPLC-MS/MS (API 4000), range 5-300 ug/L, CV < 10%. Samples below LLOQ excluded.
- NONMEM, FOCE-I. Assumptions: no lag, sample drawn just before a dose (trough), dosing every 12 or 24 h, CR has
  the same ka as IR but relative F 0.67 (from the Paxil CR monograph). Exponential IIV, additive error on log scale.
- Outcome subset: 83 MDD patients, 6 weeks of monotherapy, HAM-D-17 response (>= 50% fall at week 6), UKU for ADR.

## Model and parameters
One compartment, first-order absorption, elimination from central. V/F and ka **fixed** to literature values
(V/F 1020 L described as 17 L/kg for a 60 kg subject). Covariates as power functions centred on medians.

CL/F = 13.1 x (daily dose/25)^-0.363 x (age/71)^-0.702 L/h. Daily dose reduced OFV by 7.5. Weight (dOFV -1.53),
albumin (-6.35) and removal of sex (+5.13) did not meet the p < 0.01 (6.63) criterion. Diagnosis had no effect.
Derived: t1/2 = ln2 x 1020/13.1 = 54.0 h at 25 mg/day, 71 y.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PK/PD model, no ke0. Logistic regression of response or ADR on log(daily dose) or log(AUC) (AUC = dose/CL):
all bootstrap 95% CIs of slopes include zero (Table 4). Response 72%, ADR 30%. In subjects with >= 2
concentrations, ADR rose with exposure; mean AUC > 1800 h*ug/L (average 75 ug/L, about 58 ug/L trough, authors'
conversion) associated with ADR > 40% (Discussion). The 20-60 ug/L reference range (Tomita 2014) had a positive
predictive value of 78.9% for response. Not an opioid; no MEAC or potency ratio.

## Covariates
Daily dose and age on CL/F (power forms above). No size, sex, albumin or genotype term. CYP2D6 not genotyped.

## Caveats
Retrospective; sparse troughs (53% one sample); V/F and ka fixed, so the half-life is not identifiable; slight
over-prediction of low and under-prediction of high concentrations; nominal 24 h interval used; co-medication
allowed; outcome and concentrations not simultaneous; high response rate suggests selection bias. Population
weight median 58 kg and mostly elderly.

## Verification against stanpumpR
- ka 0.908/h -> 0.908/60 /min; V 1020 L; CL/F 13.1 L/h /60; exponents -0.363 (dose, ref 25 mg) and -0.702 (age,
  ref 71 y); IIV 40.2%: all **match** Table 2, units converted correctly (h -> min).
- Population, n (271/127), Korean outpatients, NONMEM, fixed V and ka, trough sampling: **matches** Methods.
- Steady-state equivalence D x (D/25)^0.363 / CL25 = D/CL(D): algebra correct (derived). 46 ng/mL at 20 mg, 50 y
  and 160 ng/mL at 50 mg: derived values, correct (20 x 0.8^0.363 / (24 x 16.76) x 1000 = 45.9). Note these are
  stanpumpR calculations, not Kim's reported numbers (Kim's observed median was 39.9 ug/L).
- 54 h half-life "artefact of fixed V": supported (V/F fixed, troughs only). The label's 21 h is not from this paper.
- "Kim retained no size covariate": **matches** (weight dOFV -1.53, not significant). Caveat (not an error): the
  fixed V/F was defined for a 60 kg subject and the population median weight was 58 kg, yet the code takes the
  values as the 70 kg reference man's for FFM scaling, so with the switch on a 58 kg patient gets less than Kim's
  typical CL/F and V/F. Worth recording as a judgement, not a transcription fault.
- Not represented in the code: CR formulation relative F 0.67 (Kim's assumption). Help offers "mg PO" only; a user
  dosing CR paroxetine would be over-predicted by about 1/0.67 under Kim's assumption. **Note**, not a discrepancy
  in what is implemented.
- Help says paroxetine "inhibits its own metabolism by CYP2D6": consistent with Kim's Discussion (saturation and
  auto-inactivation of CYP2D6).
