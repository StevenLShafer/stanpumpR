# Boswell 2013: pain relief tracks hydromorphone, not hydrocodone, after Cesarean section

## Study
- Prospective observational study, University of Louisville, Dec 2008 to Feb 2011. 177 women enrolled
  (aged 18-45, Cesarean section under bupivacaine spinal); 158 with complete OPRM1 genotype analysed.
  Exclusions: morbid obesity, drug-abuse history, non-English/Spanish speakers; 2 with no relief from
  hydrocodone excluded.
- First 40: spinal without intrathecal morphine, parenteral morphine for 24 h; remaining 118: intrathecal
  morphine (Duramorph). Then oral hydrocodone/acetaminophen 5/325 or 7.5/325 mg every 4 h as needed, plus
  ibuprofen 800 mg every 8 h.
- One venous sample per patient on postoperative day 3 (morning of discharge), time since last dose not
  recorded. Serum LC-MS/MS (Mayo Clinic), LOQ 0.5 ng/mL, linear to 100 ng/mL, inter-assay CV < 10%.
- Pain: nurse-administered VAS 0-10 every 4-6 h; outcome is the recorded pain level. 13 side effects scored
  1-3 and summed. Regression (Stata) of pain on serum concentration adjusted for total dose, age, BMI,
  stratified by OPRM1 A118G (AA vs AG/GG).
- Hydrocodone: 7.1 ± 2.5 doses (1-13), total 79.3 ± 37.8 mg (7.5-190); treatment duration 4-79.5 h, median 49 h.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: nurse-recorded VAS pain level (0-10), single day-3 observation, against a single serum sample.
- Table 3 (AA, n = 129): serum hydromorphone beta -0.085 (SE 0.029), P = 0.004; total dose beta 0.009
  (SE 0.005), P = 0.010. AG/GG (n = 27): hydromorphone beta 0.019, P = 0.724.
- Table 4: serum hydrocodone beta -0.007 (SE 0.011), P = 0.511 in AA; -0.008, P = 0.742 in AG/GG.
- Fig. 1: dose-adjusted hydromorphone vs pain in AA R² = 0.05, P = 0.045 (weak).
- No MEAC, no minimum effective concentration, no ke0 / t½ke0 / tPeak / hysteresis.
- Potency/affinity (discussion, citing Volpe 2011, not measured here): mu Ki hydromorphone 0.36 nM,
  hydrocodone 41.58 nM (115-fold lower affinity), morphine 1.168 nM; an earlier rat-brain study (Chen 1991) gave
  30-fold. No equianalgesic ratio.
- Side effects higher in G carriers (Table 5); respiratory depression OR 5.29 (P < 0.05).

## Covariates
OPRM1 A118G genotype (AA 82.9%, AG 13.9%, GG 3.2%; G allele 17.1%). Regressions adjusted for total dose,
age, BMI. CYP2D6 not genotyped.

## Caveats
- One random-time sample per patient; no dosing-time record, so concentration and pain are not time-matched.
- Tiny explained variance (R² 0.05); pregnant/postpartum women only; acetaminophen and ibuprofen co-therapy;
  residual intrathecal or parenteral morphine possible; 43 patients had unprescribed opioids.
- Association, not a concentration-effect model; the prodrug conclusion is the authors' interpretation.
- Authors acknowledge hydrocodone may have intrinsic mu activity at usual doses.
