# Kadam 2013: pioglitazone two-compartment population PK and CYP2C8*3

PDF page = printed page minus 244.

## Study
- Monotherapy phase of a gemfibrozil interaction study (ref 10, Aquilante 2012). 31 healthy Caucasian non-diabetic adults,
  prospectively enrolled by genotype: 16 CYP2C8*1/*1, 14 *1/*3, 1 *3/*3 (Table 1, PDF p3).
- Table 1, mean +/- SD (range): 10 men, 21 women; age 36 +/- 10 (22-56) y; weight 73.1 +/- 15.9 (51.1-107.2) kg; height
  170 +/- 10.4 cm; BMI 25.2 +/- 3.6 (19.5-33.4); serum creatinine 0.9 +/- 0.1 (0.8-1.3) mg/dL.
- Single 15 mg oral dose after an overnight fast, standard breakfast 2 h later; samples pre-dose and 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 7,
  9, 12, 18, 24, 48 h; 351 plasma concentrations; validated LC/MS (PDF p2, p4).
- NONMEM 7.2, FOCE-I, ADVAN4/TRANS4; exponential IIV and residual error; categorical covariates additive
  (TVCL = theta1 + theta6 x CYP2C8); forward dOFV > 3.84, backward > 7.88; VPC (100 replicates) and 1000-sample bootstrap
  (875 of 1001 runs minimised) (PDF p2-6).

## Model and parameters
Two compartments, first-order absorption and elimination, apparent (/F) parameters. Lag time did not help (sampling
started at 1 h). Vp/F fixed to the base-model mean because of convergence problems; covariates not tested on Ka
(PDF p4). Table 3 (PDF p3), final model original estimates (%RSE):

Text equations (PDF p4) agree with Table 3 except that they round Q/F to 0.35. Abstract and Results say CL/F and Q/F
are 52% and 286% higher in carriers; from Table 3, 1.09/2.12 = 51% (the Discussion, PDF p6, says "about 51%") and
1.03/0.349 = 295% (derived). The 286% does not follow from the tabulated estimates; the source of the 9-point gap is not
stated. Derived from the typical non-carrier values: alpha half-life 5.9 h, terminal half-life 62 h (micro-constants
k10 0.099, k12 0.0163, k21 0.0133 /h), the terminal phase lying well beyond the 48 h sampling.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. Active metabolites M-III and M-IV not measured or modelled.

## Covariates
Screened: CYP2C8 genotype, sex, weight, BMI, age, serum creatinine, liver tests. Only CYP2C8*3 (carrier vs *1/*1) retained,
on CL/F (dOFV 12.21, P = 0.0005) and Q/F (dOFV 8.19, P = 0.004); BMI on CL/F, Vc/F or Q/F not significant (Table 2,
PDF p3). Sex and body weight not significant (abstract, PDF p6). The *3 carriers pool 14 heterozygotes and one homozygote.

## Caveats
- Single 15 mg dose, healthy volunteers, all Caucasian; genotype-enriched sample, so the carrier fraction is not a
  population frequency.
- Ka poorly identified (first sample at 1 h; bootstrap RSE 1353%); Vp/F fixed.
- Reduction of IIV by the covariate is in CV percentage points (43.0 to 37.8 = 5.2; 130 to 116 = 14).
