# de Bruijn 1987: esmolol pharmacokinetics in anaesthetised CABG patients on chronic beta blockers

## Study
- 19 patients for elective CABG (17 men, 2 women), all on chronic beta blockers (atenolol 8, metoprolol 1, nadolol 1,
  propranolol 9), regular morning dose taken 4-6 h before the study; 11 on oral nitrates, 11 on calcium entry blockers.
  Weight 77.6 +/- 12.8 kg (53-100), BSA 1.89 +/- 0.17 m2, age 57 +/- 8.4 y (40-69), ejection fraction 58 +/- 10 %
  (Table 2, PDF p3, mean +/- SD, range).
- Premedication oral diazepam 0.15 mg/kg, IM scopolamine, IM morphine 0.1 mg/kg. Anaesthesia induced after 7 min of
  esmolol with diazepam 0.5 mg/kg IV, pancuronium 100 mcg/kg, enflurane 0.4% (PDF p1).
- Esmolol into a peripheral vein, stepped regimen (Table 1, PDF p2): 500 mcg/kg/min 1 min, 100 for 4 min, 500 for 1 min,
  200 for 4 min, 500 for 1.5 min, then 300 until 5 min after maximal sternal spread. "The total duration of the infusion
  at the final infusion rate" was 54.8 +/- 5.6 min (Results, PDF p2). (Derived: with the 11.5 min of earlier steps the
  whole infusion was about 66 min, consistent with Fig. 2, where the decay begins at about 63-65 min after the start.)
- Venous blood from a short cannula in the right internal jugular vein at baseline, induction, intubation, incision,
  sternotomy, maximal sternal spread, end of infusion, then 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 9, 11, 13, 15, 18, 21, 25, 30 and 40 min
  after the end; all before cardiopulmonary bypass. Fluoride syringes, immediate methylene-chloride extraction; GC-MS
  (Sum and Yacobi). Assay sensitivity printed as "0.025 ng/mL" with CVs at 0.1, 1 and 5 "ng/mL" (PDF p2), although all
  concentrations are reported in ug/mL: the unit is printed as written.
- Two-compartment open model, NONLIN 84, weight 1/c, constant IV input; only the decay phase was fitted (Fig. 2
  caption). Six of the 19 were excluded for incomplete sampling or sampling errors, leaving 13 (PDF p2).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None measured as a concentration-effect relation. Cardiac index did not change significantly (2.3 +/- 0.3 baseline vs
2.2 +/- 0.5 L/min/m2 at incision). No ke0, tPeak or EC50.

## Covariates
None. All parameters per kg total body weight.

## Caveats
- Means of individual fits in 13 patients; very wide scatter (Vc CV 75%), and the printed CL and Vc means do not
  reproduce from the printed rows (above).
- Sampling site matters: the authors cite an unpublished animal study (ACC study 8052-82-14-02) with arterial esmolol
  3-7 times peripheral venous. These samples were internal jugular (central venous), infusion peripheral.
- The lower clearance than Sum's volunteers (285 mL/kg/min) is attributed by the authors to reduced hepatic and renal
  blood flow under anaesthesia and to chronic beta blockade; they also allow methodological differences.
- Table 4 also prints Sum 1983's values: alpha 0.364 +/- 0.095, t1/2 alpha 2.030 +/- 0.544 min, beta 0.085 +/- 0.031,
  t1/2 beta 9.190 +/- 3.510 min, Css 1.59 +/- 0.605 ug/mL, CL 285 +/- 104 mL/kg/min, V-beta 3.430 +/- 1.420 L/kg,
  Vc 0.867 +/- 0.259 L/kg.
