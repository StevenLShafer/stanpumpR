# Castillo 2024: sertraline population PK in dual pathology, CYP2D6*2 and CYP2C19 phenotype

## Study
- Outpatients >12 y with a psychiatric disorder plus substance use disorder, Centros de Integración Juvenil, San Luis Potosí, Mexico.
- n = 59 (13-76 y; median age 29), 55.9% male; 20.3% under 18; weight 71.9 ± 13.9 kg, height 1.7 ± 0.1 m, BMI 26.3 ± 4.8 (Table 1).
  Serum creatinine median 0.82 mg/dL (0.49-4.18); CrCL median 113.0 mL/min (17.6-150.9).
- Doses 12.5-100 mg/day (abstract); median daily dose 50.0 mg (12.5-100.0) (Table 1, layout scrambled).
- 75 plasma concentrations: one sample >= 7 days after start and a second >= 30 days later (sparse, mostly elimination phase).
- Assay: UPLC-MS/MS, plasma (EDTA), linear 5-800 ng/mL. Genotyping CYP2D6 *2 *4 *10 *17 *41, CYP2C9 *2 *3, CYP2C19 *2 *17,
  CYP2B6 *9 (TaqMan); phenotypes by CPIC.
- NONMEM 7.4, FOCE, PsN 5.3.0; forward P<.05, backward P<.01; bootstrap 1000; VPC.

## Model and parameters
One-compartment, first-order absorption, linear elimination (ADVAN2 TRANS2). Only CL estimated; ka fixed at 0.855 1/h (from Alhadab
& Brundage 2020, ref 29) and V fixed at 20.2 L/kg (value giving the best prediction/lowest OFV) (Results p1270).
Final model (Table 4 footnote): CL (L/h) = θ2 x (1 + θ4 x CYP2D6*2) x (1 + θ5 x CYP2C19 PM) x (1 + θ6 x CYP2C19 IM); V = θ3 x weight.
Base model CL 57.1 L/h, IIV 38.2%, RV 12.89%.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD model, no ke0. Simulations target the AGNP range 10-150 ng/mL for steady-state troughs; proposed doses 75 mg/day (CYP2D6*2 +
CYP2C19 PM) to 200 mg/day (CYP2C19 EM) (Figure 3, Table 5). Beck inventories collected but not related to concentration (most
patients missed the second visit). Not an opioid.

## Covariates
CYP2D6*2 carrier (-23.1% CL), CYP2C19 IM (-19.06%), CYP2C19 PM (-48.26%), relative to EM/RM. Age, sex, weight, liver function,
grapefruit, CYP2B6 not significant. V scales with total body weight (fixed coefficient).

## Caveats
Small n (59), 75 sparse samples, mostly elimination phase so ka and V not estimable (both fixed). Population with substance use
(urine positives: marijuana 24%, methamphetamine 10.7%). CYP2C19 PM n small (CYP2C19 *2/*2 frequency 1.7%). No external validation.
