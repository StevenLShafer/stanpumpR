# Son 1998: lincomycin and clindamycin binding to plasma, AGP and albumin across species

## Study
- In vitro protein binding by ultrafiltration (Centrifree, 37 °C, 10 min incubation; filtrate about 10% of volume) of lincomycin (LM) and clindamycin (CM) spiked over a range of concentrations into pooled plasma, purified AGP solution and albumin solution (3.5 g/dL) from humans, dogs, cattle and sheep.
- Human plasma: pooled from 2 women and 2 men, healthy, 20-35 y. Dogs n = 6, cattle n = 3, sheep n = 3.
- Assay: HPLC-UV (CM at 198 nm). AGP by radial immunodiffusion; albumin by bromcresol green.
- Analysis: Rosenthal plots; nonlinear least squares (MULTI) for saturable (Kd, Bmax) plus non-saturable (PC) binding.
- Units: the text layer prints "mmol/L" and "mg/mL", where the micro sign has been lost; from Bmax/AGP = 1.42 with AGP 430 and MW 50 000 (derived: 430 µg/mL / 50 000 = 8.6 µmol/L; 12.24/8.6 = 1.42), Kd and Bmax are µmol/L and AGP is µg/mL.

Other species (CM, plasma): dog Kd 1.48, Bmax 9.52, PC 2.91; cattle Kd 1.22, Bmax 4.45, PC 2.40; sheep PC 1.48 only. Crossover from specific to non-specific dominance for CM in human plasma at about 17 µmol/L total. Simulated rise of AGP to 2000 µg/mL lowers human CM fu about 4.0-fold (abstract, p 40).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. Not an opioid; no MEAC, ke0 or potency.

## Covariates
AGP concentration (simulation, Eqn 7; Bmax proportional to [AGP]).

## Caveats
Pooled plasma from 4 people; in vitro ultrafiltration, single pool; no individual variability; units printed ambiguously in the text layer; PC for CM in human plasma (4.98) is large relative to albumin solution, attributed to another binder (lipoprotein); salt form of CM (base vs hydrochloride) not stated, so the mass conversion assumes the base.
