# Caritis 2025: buprenorphine pharmacology during and after pregnancy

## Study
Single-centre prospective cohort (Magee-Womens Hospital, Pittsburgh; NCT02863601; May 2017 to July 2021). 22 pregnant
people on stable sublingual (SL) buprenorphine maintenance for opioid use disorder (at least 1 week), 2-8 mg every 6 h
(QID) or 8 h (TID), frequency chosen by the patient. 33 studies (6-8 h, one dosing interval each); 5 excluded for
non-adherence, leaving 28 (derived: 33 - 5). Epochs (PDF p2): first half of pregnancy 15.6 +/- 2.9 wk (10 TID, 4 QID);
second half 30.1 +/- 6.4 wk (3 TID, 4 QID); postpartum 5.6 +/- 1.0 wk after delivery, 12 participants, all QID.
Table 1 (PDF p3, image read): age 29 +/- 5.2 y, BMI 27.2 +/- 5.3 kg/m2; 21 non-Hispanic White, 1 biracial; all 22 smokers;
average daily dose 13.2 +/- 4.5 mg (1st half), 17.7 +/- 4.5 mg (2nd half), 16 +/- 0 mg (postpartum); number of studies
14 / 7 / 12. Weight not given. Morning dose withheld; baseline then 8-10 plasma samples over one interval; pupil
diameter (mean of 3, low light), COWS and craving (0-10 Likert). Assay UPLC (ref 20 is a UPLC-MS/MS method), LLOQ
0.05 ng/mL, CV < 10%; total plasma buprenorphine. Non-compartmental: individual dose-normalised AUC (concentration
divided by the dose taken at the study start), groups compared by one-way ANOVA. No model fitted; PD curves smoothed
with third-order polynomials (R2 0.70-0.91).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- **MEAC / effective concentration (opioid audit):** about 1 ng/mL total plasma buprenorphine (UPLC; plasma, not whole
  blood) is the level at which pupil diameter, COWS and craving returned to baseline (abstract; PDF p3-5). It is a
  threshold for **suppressing withdrawal and craving in opioid use disorder**, not an analgesic MEAC; the authors take
  it as a minimal therapeutic concentration in nonpregnant people from Greenwald 2014 (ref 18), and observe the same
  level in these pregnant and postpartum women (mild withdrawal only at baseline). Not a fitted value.
- **Hysteresis:** the pupil-diameter nadir lagged the plasma peak by 30-60 min after an SL dose (PDF p3; Discussion
  p5 applies the same 30-60 min delay to COWS and craving). No ke0, t1/2ke0 or tPeak was estimated.
- No EC50, Emax or potency/equianalgesic ratio.

## Covariates
Gestational epoch only (first half, second half, postpartum). Dosing frequency (TID vs QID) did not change troughs
(Fig. 3, PDF p5). No weight or other covariates.

## Caveats
Smaller than planned (powered for 40 studies; single site after 3 centres withdrew). Unpaired between-group comparison
with 7 studies in each of the decisive groups; postpartum n is 7 in Fig. 1 but 12 in Table 1. AUC over 0-360 min for all,
including TID participants. Postpartum at about 6 weeks is the comparator, not nonpregnant women. Second-half doses
were higher (17.7 vs 16 mg/day), and SL bioavailability is dose dependent, so dose normalisation is not exact. All
smokers. Only mild withdrawal at baseline, which weakens the PD relationships. Apparent (SL) exposure only; no IV
data. The authors attribute the change to raised CYP3A and UGT activity, citing ref 21 (Zhang H, Bastian JR, Zhao W et
al., Ther Drug Monit 2020;42:264-270).

  all four agree with PDF p3 (page image). 218/119 = 1.832, rounded 1.83, as the test asserts. "22 women, 33 studies"
  matches, though only 28 studies were analysed.
- **Authorship: discrepancy.** The paper's authors are Caritis SN and Venkataramanan R only (PDF p1; PubMed agrees).
  The repo cites it as "Zhu Y, Bastian JR, Caritis SN et al." (R/drugs_buprenorphine.R:294, :398;
  pregnancyRegistry.csv:36), "Zhu 2025" (R/drugs_buprenorphine.R:248, :318, :343; man/buprenorphine.Rd:25; test name

- **Design: discrepancy (registry).** pregnancyRegistry.csv:36 gives comparator "postpartum (same women)" and design
  "paired NCA / popPK". This paper compares groups by one-way ANOVA (each woman contributed 1-3 studies, not all
  epochs), and fits no population model. Bastian 2017 was the paired study.
- **Comparator.** The 1.83 is second half of pregnancy against about 6 weeks postpartum in the same cohort; the library
  applies it to Bjornsson's nonpregnant disposition. That baseline difference, the small n, and the use of the
  second-half rather than whole-pregnancy ratio are recorded as an open question; the paper does support the
  direction and approximate size (45% lower AUC).
- **Mapping to elimination clearance.** This paper reports only apparent SL exposure; it gives no absorption or IV
  data, so the mapping to CL1 with absorption unchanged (R header :254-262) rests on Bastian 2017 and the 2020 TDM
  paper, not on this one. Note that the paper's ref 21 gives that TDM paper as Zhang H, Bastian JR, Zhao W et al.,
  whereas the repo cites it as "Liu Y et al." (R/drugs_buprenorphine.R:252, :296; registry :36); not checked here.
  The registry also calls the 2018 PBPK "Zhu 2018" while its own source list and this paper's ref 17 give Zhang H.
- **PD.** The 30-60 min pupil lag after SL dosing is consistent in scale with the library's 61.2 min tPeak after an IV
  bolus (:305), but is a different measure (lag of nadir behind plasma peak after SL input), so it neither confirms
  nor contradicts it. The ~1 ng/mL withdrawal threshold is not used by the library, whose analgesic band is 0.2-1
  ng/mL with MEAC 0 (:172-224); no conflict.
