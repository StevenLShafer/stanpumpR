# Han 2025: joint fluoxetine / norfluoxetine population PK from steady-state troughs

## Study
- Part 1: systematic review of fluoxetine PopPK models (PubMed, Embase, Web of Science to 7 May 2025); two retained:
  Panchaud 2011 (perinatal women; CL/F 8.42 L/h, V/F 690 L, ka 0.3 /h fixed) and Wilens 2002 / FDA review (children;
  CL/F 0.181 x BW L/h, V/F 37.4 x BW L, ka 0.666 /h fixed) (Table 2).
- Part 2: external evaluation of both against a retrospective Chinese TDM dataset; both biased at population level
  (PRED MPE 50.7% and 78.3%; Table 3).
- Part 3: new joint parent-metabolite model on the same dataset. Hunan Brain Hospital, 2021-2024: 198 patients
  (146 women, 52 men; 73.7% women), median age 17 yr (12-56), median weight 59 kg (35.9-115); pediatric < 18 yr n = 102,
  adult n = 92 (sic, sums to 194). 241 fluoxetine + 241 norfluoxetine plasma concentrations, all steady-state troughs
  before the next dose. Dose 20-60 mg QD (Table 1; Section 3.3 says 20-40 mg). LC-MS/MS, LLOQ 1 ng/mL.
  Median observed fluoxetine 140.97 ng/mL (7.43-979.93), norfluoxetine 129.2 ng/mL (9.6-490.41).
- NONMEM 7.5, PsN 5.2.6; stepwise covariates (forward p < 0.01, backward p < 0.001); 1000-sample bootstrap; pcVPC.
- Monte Carlo (n = 1000 per arm) of 10-60 mg QD for PTA of active-moiety trough 120-500 ng/mL.

## Model and parameters

Derived (not stated in the paper): fluoxetine t½ = ln2 x 24.9 / 2.91 = 5.93 h (5.09 h in men); norfluoxetine
t½ = ln2 x 1.52 / 3.24 = 0.325 h (19.5 min). Weight-normalised CL 0.053 L/h/kg (paper, Discussion). The paper does
not comment on these half-lives against the 4-6 day label value it cites in the Introduction.

Simulation (Section 3.6): median active-moiety trough at 60 mg/day 502.32 ng/mL (women) vs 372.84 (men); PTA > 70%
for women at 20-40 mg and men at 30-50 mg; abstract gives the maximum effective dose as 30 mg (women) and 40 mg (men).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD modelled. Target: fluoxetine + norfluoxetine trough 120-500 ng/mL (adult TDM range, refs 7-8); pediatric
preliminary 208-328 ng/mL. Not an opioid; no ke0.

## Covariates
Sex on fluoxetine CL/F only (men +16.5%). Weight not retained (narrow IQR 48.15-67.75 kg, Discussion). Age not
named as tested in the main text (limitations say homogeneity may have hidden age and weight effects); Table S3
(not archived) lists the covariate steps. No genotype data.

## Caveats
Trough-only data: ka fixed, V/F and the half-lives poorly identified (V/F RSE 38% and 57%); the fitted half-lives are
hours, far shorter than the known days, so only the steady-state trough is supported. Retrospective, single centre,
mostly adolescent women; dose range stated inconsistently (20-40 vs 20-60 mg). FM = 1 is an assumption.
Supplementary Tables S3/S4 are not in the archive.
