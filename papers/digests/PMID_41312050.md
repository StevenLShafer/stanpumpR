# Choi 2025: maternal-fetal PBPK model of venlafaxine and O-desmethylvenlafaxine

## Study
- **Design:** a physiologically based PK (PBPK) model of venlafaxine and its active metabolite
  O-desmethylvenlafaxine (ODV), built in Simcyp version 21 in three stages: non-pregnant women, pregnant women
  (gestational-age-dependent physiology, Simcyp pregnancy population), and a fetoplacental unit (pp3-5).
- **Data, all from published studies (no new subjects):**
  - Non-pregnant model: healthy volunteers aged 18-45 in the source trials (female fraction 0 to 0.5), simulated
    as women at gestational week 0, 12-22 subjects per trial, 10 replicate trials. Single 75 or 150 mg doses, two
    75 mg tablets, or 75 mg repeated for 4 days (p4, Table S1).
  - Pregnant model: trough concentrations per trimester from ter Horst 2014 (ref 16), women aged 26-34, one to three
    per dose group, 37.5-225 mg once daily (p5). The abstract gives n = 7 for plasma.
  - Fetoplacental model: cord blood at delivery, women aged 28-40, gestational week 37-39, n = 9 (abstract), doses
    37.5-225 mg (p6).
- **CYP2D6:** population-level Simcyp default phenotype frequencies (EM 56.8%, IM 32.5%, PM 8.2%, UM 2.5%), not
  individual genotypes (p4).
- **Evaluation:** predicted/observed Cmax and AUC within 0.7-1.3, and observed points inside the predicted 95%
  interval. Morris global sensitivity analysis and a local CYP2D6 sensitivity analysis (p6).

**Predicted trimester course (Table 2, p10; arithmetic means, multiple doses):** venlafaxine AUC falls 7.8%,
24.4% and 38.5% in the first, second and third trimesters, and ODV AUC 7.7%, 24.2% and 40.3%. The percentages are
the same at 37.5, 75, 150 and 225 mg, so the model is linear in dose over this range. At 150 mg, for example,
venlafaxine AUC is 2063.97 ng·h/mL at baseline and 1268.56 in the third trimester; ODV 4562.47 and 2727.64. The
paper does not state the AUC interval (presumably one dosing interval at steady state; not stated).

**Mechanism given (p11):** simulated CYP2D6 abundance rises 62.59% by the third trimester (Table S7, not seen).

**Arithmetic check (derived):** a 38.5% AUC fall implies an apparent clearance factor of 1/0.615 = 1.63 for the
parent and 1/0.597 = 1.67 for ODV formation-plus-elimination at steady state.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD model. Dose selection uses the AGNP therapeutic range for venlafaxine + ODV, 100-400 ng/mL (p7, ref 18).
- 37.5 and 75 mg/day: the sum never reaches the range in any trimester (Figure 5, Table S6).
- 150 mg/day: Cmin and Cmax in range in the first and second trimesters; Cmin slightly below 100 ng/mL in the third.
- 225 mg/day: in range in the third trimester.
- 375 mg/day (maximum dose): predicted umbilical cord Cmax 195.26 ng/mL, Tmax 5.43 h, AUC 2805.17 ng·h/mL (Figure
  S6), which the authors propose as near a fetal-toxicity threshold on the basis of animal studies (p13).

**Abstract versus discussion:** the abstract says 225 mg "was necessary" in the third trimester. The discussion
(p12) calls the gain from 225 over 150 mg minimal, the framework (p13) offers 225 mg only if therapeutic
concentrations are not reached, and the conclusion recommends 150 mg/day throughout pregnancy.

## Covariates
Gestational week, through the Simcyp equation in plasma volume, hepatic CYP abundance, renal function and blood
flow (Eq. 1, p4). CYP2D6 phenotype only as population frequencies. Fetal clearance via swallowing 0.00844 and
fetal renal clearance 0.055 L/h/kg fetal weight (Eqs. 2-3).

## Caveats
- Validation in pregnancy rests on 7 women's troughs and 9 cord samples; the authors call the results exploratory
  (p13). Some observed points (patients 3, 6 and 7) fall outside the 95% prediction interval (p10).
- Several clearance terms were tuned to the curves rather than measured ("additional systemic clearance" 22 L/h is
  larger than the CYP-predicted part), so the pregnancy prediction depends on Simcyp's default changes in CYP2D6
  and renal function.
- The therapeutic range was not validated for perinatal depression (p13).
- This contrasts with observed TDM: the authors cite ter Horst (venlafaxine 98.9% to 87% of postpartum, T1 to T3,
  ODV/venlafaxine ratio 76.9% to 196.7%) (p2), much smaller than the model's -38.5% parent and -40.3% ODV AUC.
