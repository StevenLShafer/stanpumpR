# Haynes 2024: population PK/PD of oral cephalexin and cefadroxil in children

## Study
- Prospective open-label crossover, Children's Hospital Colorado, May 2019 - Sept 2020. Ages 6 months - 18 y with musculoskeletal infection (osteomyelitis, septic arthritis); excluded eGFR < 60 mL/min/1.73 m2, weight < 5.5 kg or > 95th percentile.
- 17 enrolled, 16 dosed: cephalexin n = 15, cefadroxil n = 14 (13 crossover). One cephalexin subject with GI dysmotility excluded from the cephalexin analysis.
- Cephalexin: single oral 50 mg/kg (max 1,250 mg capsules / 1,375 mg suspension); median 50.3 mg/kg, 1,250 mg in >= 25 kg subjects. Cephalexin group median age 12.2 y (0.82-17.78), weight 51.8 kg (7.15-79.5) (Table 1, column reading reconstructed).
- Cefadroxil: 75 mg/kg (max 2,000 mg), reduced to 50 mg/kg (max 1,500 mg) for vomiting.
- Samples about 1, 2, 6, 8 h (cephalexin) and 1, 2, 6, 8, 12 h (cefadroxil); 53 cephalexin, 66 cefadroxil plasma samples. LC-MS, LLOQ 0.05 mg/L cephalexin, total concentrations.
- NCA (WinNonlin 8.3), then NLME in Phoenix NLME 8.3 (FOCE-ELS); Monte Carlo 1,000 subjects per 10-80 kg band.

## Model and parameters
One compartment, first-order absorption; lag only for cefadroxil. Weight fixed allometry at 70 kg: CL/F x (WT/70)^0.75, V/F x (WT/70)^1. No other covariate retained (age, height, sex, eGFR, food, emesis tested).

Absorption half-life cephalexin 0.39 h (Discussion; derived ln2/1.79 = 0.39 h).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- Primary PD target: free (unbound) T>MIC >= 40% of the dosing interval with PTA >= 90% for MIC <= 4 mg/L; secondary 60% fT>MIC. Unbound concentration assumed from 15% protein binding for both drugs. MIC distribution from Haynes 2022 (local MSSA isolates); about 80% of isolates <= 2 mg/L.
- Cephalexin 25 mg/kg (max 750 mg) three times daily met the primary target; 1,000 mg qid or high-dose tid needed for MIC 8 mg/L.
- Murine thigh data cited: cephalexin fT>MIC 26% for stasis, 34% for 1-log kill.

## Covariates
Weight only (fixed allometric exponents 0.75 / 1). Fed vs fasted: cephalexin dose-normalised AUC 53.8% lower when fed (not retained in model).

## Caveats
- n = 15 children, single dose, not steady state; normal renal function only; total concentrations with assumed binding.
- Apparent parameters (no IV arm).
- 70 kg is a normalisation weight; the heaviest child was about 80 kg.
