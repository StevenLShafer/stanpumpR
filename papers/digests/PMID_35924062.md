# Liu 2022: escitalopram population PK in Chinese psychiatric patients (CYP2C19, age)

## Study
- Retrospective TDM, inpatients of the Affiliated Brain Hospital of Guangzhou Medical University, 2018-2021.
- 106 patients, 337 **serum** concentrations (steady and non-steady state, mostly near trough); patients with a single
  level excluded. Conventional tablets 5-20 mg qd, 5 or 10 mg bid; median 10 mg/day (5-30).
- Table 1 (median, range): age 45 yr (12-83); 59 men (55.66%), 47 women; weight 61 kg (37-97); height 165 cm
  (150-180); BMI 22.4 (14.87-33.91); 3 smokers. Missing weight/height imputed to the median.
- CYP2C19 (Table 2): *1/*1 47 (EM), *1/*2 48 + *1/*3 1 (IM, 49), *2/*2 7 + *2/*3 3 (PM, 10). Allele frequencies *1
  67.5%, *2 30.6%, *3 1.9%. *17 not genotyped (low frequency in Chinese).
- HPLC-MS/MS, linear range 3-300 ng/mL. NONMEM 7 FOCE-I; forward p < 0.01, backward p < 0.001; bootstrap 1000; NPDE.

## Model and parameters
One compartment, first-order absorption and elimination, apparent oral; ka fixed at 0.6 /h from Chen 2013 (Chinese
subjects) because absorption-phase data were lacking; additive error fixed 0, proportional error only.

Covariate equations (Methods): continuous covariates Eq 3, P = TV x e^eta x [1 + theta x (Cov_i - Cov_median)];
CYP2C19 Eqs 5-7, CL = TVCL x e^eta x theta_EM / theta_IM / theta_PM (EM is the reference; theta_EM not tabulated).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. Therapeutic window cited: AGNP 15-80 ng/mL, laboratory alert 160 ng/mL (Hiemke 2018). Simulations: 5 mg/day
may leave EM/IM troughs below 15; PM > 80 at 20 mg/day; PM maximum 10 mg/day recommended. Not an opioid; no ke0.

## Covariates
Age (linear, Eq 3) and CYP2C19 phenotype on CL/F. Not significant: sex, height, weight, BMI, smoking, co-medication,
liver/kidney indices.

## Caveats
Retrospective, sparse, mostly troughs; ka fixed; few PMs (n = 10); *17 not genotyped so rapid/ultrarapid absent;
serum not plasma; the age-effect sign and magnitude are internally inconsistent (above). "IM" is printed as
"immediate metabolizer".
