# van der Mast 2019: In vitro intravenous microdialysis of gentamicin and vancomycin

## Study
In vitro only, no patients. EIRUS 14G triple-lumen central venous catheter with a 5000 Da microdialysis membrane (~330 mm2), perfused with 0.9% NaCl at 5 uL/min, placed in stirred media at 37 C spiked with gentamicin 8-22 mg/L or vancomycin 15-22 mg/L. Media: saline, ammonium acetate buffer, phosphate buffer, phosphate buffer with albumin 40 g/L (pH 6.9-7.1), human plasma (Omniplasma, Sanquin) and commercial human serum (Millipore). Dialysate fractions over 3 h. Unbound concentration in the medium by ultrafiltration (Centrifree); total and unbound measured by immunoassay (Abbott Architect c8000; gentamicin LLOQ 0.5 mg/L, CV < 5%). Triplicate experiments (gentamicin serum repeated twice, plasma four times). Relative recovery of total (RRT) and unbound (RRU) concentration, mean after 60 min.

## Caveats
The paper does not report an unbound fraction as a result; the free fractions above are derived from its ultrafiltration columns. Spiked commercial serum/plasma in vitro, no patient samples; ultrafiltration conditions (temperature, pH, divalent cations) not described in detail; small number of replicates. Table layout in the PDF text layer is scrambled; assignments were checked by arithmetic.
