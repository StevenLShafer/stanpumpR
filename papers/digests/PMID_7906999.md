# Kearns 1994: cladribine pharmacokinetics in children with acute leukemia

## Study
- 25 patients with acute leukemia, 12 M / 13 F, age 8 months to 23 years (mean and median 9.6 y); 21 White, 4 Black (Table 1, PDF p. 2). Weight mean 39.1 kg (8.6-110.5), surface area mean 1.18 m2 (0.43-2.34), serum creatinine mean 0.76 mg/dL (0.2-1.4), bilirubin mean 0.57 mg/dL (0.1-2.3).
- 12 relapsed (6 AML, 6 ALL) in a phase II study; 13 newly diagnosed AML given one course as a single agent before conventional chemotherapy.
- Dose: 8.9 mg/m2/day for 5 days by continuous IV infusion (total 44.5 mg/m2, derived). Drug in 100 mL saline or D5W/0.45% saline, by pump.
- Blood: pre-dose; 24, 48, 96, 120 h during infusion; 0.5, 1, 2, 4, 8, 12, 24, 48, 72 h after. Urine in 24 h aliquots. One lumbar puncture on day 4 or 5.
- Assay: plasma cladribine by radioimmunoassay, standard curve 1.25-50 nM made from each patient's own pre-dose plasma; intra-assay CV 8.7% (37.5 nM) and 5.6% (225 nM), inter-assay 9.8%. Discussion gives sensitivity about 0.2 nM. Total drug, plasma (not blood).
- Analysis: two-compartment open model by ADAPT II (maximum likelihood), parameters Vc, Kc (elimination), Kcp, Kpc; CL, Vdss and half-lives are secondary. Because most AUC fell during the infusion, Kcp and Kpc were poorly identified, so they were fixed at the group means (0.52 /h and 0.05 /h, as printed in the text layer) and Vc and Kc re-estimated.

- Renal clearance = urinary excretion rate / mean plasma concentration over the collection interval, averaged over intervals per patient.
- Derived: CL 39.4 L/h/m2 times the cohort's mean BSA 1.18 m2 is about 46 L/h (derived; mean of products would differ).
- No significant difference in any PK parameter between relapsed and newly diagnosed, among cytological subtypes, or among response groups (CR 8, PR 6, none 10 of 24 evaluable).
- Not reported: oral or SC data (IV only); metabolites (none identified in urine or plasma, per Discussion).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- No ke0, t1/2ke0, tPeak or hysteresis (not an effect-site study).
- No MEAC or analgesic concentration (not an opioid).
- No concentration-effect relation: no PK parameter correlated with response, and all patients had grade 3-4 haematological toxicity at this dose (Discussion).
- CSF: lumbar sample day 4 or 5 of the infusion; mean CSF:plasma ratio 18.2% (SD 10.0). Day 5 mean 22.7% (range 12.4-38.0%, n = 7) vs day 4 mean 7.6% (5.5-9.4%, n = 3), P = 0.03 (Mann-Whitney). The authors call the day-4 vs day-5 comparison cautious because n is tiny.

## Covariates
- CL per kg falls with weight, BSA, height and age (R2 58.4%, 55.7%, 42.6%, 41.8% respectively, P < 0.0005); CL per m2 is only weakly related to weight (R2 19.6%, P < 0.027) and to nothing else. Authors conclude BSA-based dosing gives more uniform exposure across ages than weight-based dosing.
- Serum creatinine (R2 32.7%, P = 0.003) and total bilirubin (R2 20.9%, P = 0.021) predicted CL per kg but not CL per m2. All creatinine values were normal; only one bilirubin was abnormal (2.3 mg/dL), and that patient had the lowest CL.
- Patients 15 y or older (n = 7): mean CL 0.71 (SD 0.42) L/h/kg, range 0.31-1.27.
- No race or sex effect found. No equation published.

## Caveats
- Sparse post-infusion sampling; Kcp and Kpc fixed; terminal half-life (19.7 h) is longer than Liliemark's adult 6.7 and 9.9 h after 2-h infusions, which the authors attribute to sampling and assay sensitivity.
- Radioimmunoassay (cross-reactivity not discussed in the text read); n = 7 for renal clearance; CSF n = 10-11.
- Paper compares its mean CL of 1.44 L/h/kg with Liliemark's adults (0.74, 0.93 and 1.08 L/h/kg, values the authors recalculated from those papers).
- Table 2 misprint risk noted in open_questions.

## Verification against stanpumpR
- "About half of clearance is renal": **matches**. Paper mean 51.0% (range 11.0-85.1%, n = 7, PDF p. 2 and abstract). The code comments say "about half", which is fair as a mean, but the individual range is wide.
- "Children... Kearns is the paediatric source": **matches** (8 months-23 y).
- Cladribine model (Lindemalm 2005) compared with Kearns (not a citation of the paper, an independent check): library CL 39.3 L/h and Vss = 71.7 + 475 + 73.6 = 620 L (R/drugs_cladribine.R:85; derived Vss). Kearns weight-normalised CL 1.44 L/h/kg vs library 39.3/76 = 0.52 L/h/kg (derived) in a 76-kg adult. Kearns older patients (>= 15 y) 0.71 L/h/kg are close to Liliemark's adults, and the library's 0.52 L/h/kg is lower still. Kearns Vdss 356.6 L/m2 times 1.9 m2 is about 680 L (derived) vs library 620 L. Terminal half-life 19.7 h (Kearns, children, 120-h infusion) vs 16.4 h (library, adult, header lines 37-39). Direction: children have higher CL per kg than the adult model, as Kearns attribute to body-size dependence; the library's FFM scaling goes the same way but I did not compute its predicted value for a child (see open question).
- Renal term: header lines 45-50 say no renal term was found; Kearns's creatinine correlation applies to weight-normalised CL in children with all-normal creatinine, so it does not contradict the statement.
- Unit conversions: not applicable (no Kearns value enters the code).
