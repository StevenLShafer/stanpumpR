# Philipson 1987: cephradine and cefazolin during and after pregnancy

## Study
- Paired (same women) comparison, Danderyd Hospital, Sweden. 12 pregnant women with asymptomatic lower urinary tract infection, otherwise healthy (p. 2, printed 137).
- Cephradine (Velosef) 0.5 g IV over 2 min (dose A, the first dose of a 10-day oral course) and, at least 1 week after the course, 0.5 g orally fasting (B); after pregnancy, when breast-feeding had stopped and menstruation had returned, 0.5 g IV (C) and orally (D). Six of the 12 also had cefazolin (Kefzol or Celmetin) 0.5 g IV during (E) and after (F) pregnancy.
- Gestation at the IV cephradine dose 10-29 weeks (Table I); at the cefazolin dose 19-33 weeks (Table II). Weight during pregnancy 62.9 (SD 9.1) kg (cephradine), 67.9 (6.9) kg (cefazolin).
- Sampling: IV 0, 10, 20, 30, 45 min, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8 h; oral 0.5-8 h. Assay: disc-diffusion bioassay (Sarcina lutea ATCC 9341), standards in pregnant and non-pregnant plasma; lower limit 0.2-0.6 mg/L cephradine, 0.2-0.5 mg/L cefazolin. Total concentrations only.
- Analysis: one-compartment, terminal slope by regression, AUC by trapezoids with extrapolation, CL = dose/AUC, Vdarea = CL/beta; paired two-tailed t-test.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None measured. The aim is to keep the concentration-to-MIC ratio of non-pregnant women; the authors suggest raising both drugs' doses by about 40 to 60% in pregnancy (Therapeutic Implications, PDF p. 9, printed 144), with the caveat that a rise in cefazolin's free fraction might offset its lower total concentrations. No MIC target, binding or free concentration was measured.

## Covariates
Gestational week: significant negative correlation with cephradine CL per kg (Fig. 3; r printed as 0.742, p < 0.01) and with Vd per kg (Fig. 4; r = 0.607, p < 0.05), so the authors expect cephradine clearance to be highest early and to approach non-pregnant values near term. Albumin fell with gestational week (r = 0.811, p < 0.01). Binding quoted from the literature: cephradine 14%, cefazolin 84%. Renal mechanism argued from both drugs being excreted mainly unchanged (Discussion, p. 7).

## Caveats
Small (6 for cefazolin, none of the cefazolin changes except t1/2 significant), bioassay, total concentrations only, one-compartment, sampling to 8 h with several cephradine samples unmeasurable from 5 h. Gestation mostly second trimester (cephradine median about 15 weeks), so the women are not a term population. The post-pregnancy reference is after lactation and the return of menstruation, a true non-pregnant state. The abstract rounds the cephradine AUC fall to 39% where Table I prints -38%.
