# Stanczyk 2022: LNG/EE transdermal system, 12-week extended regimen (popPK simulation)

## Study
- Simulation study built on a published phase 1, open-label, single-centre, randomized trial, ATI-CL14 (NCT01243580): 36 healthy non-smoking women, 18-45 y, BMI 18-32 kg/m2, 3 cycles of 28 days (p.2-3).
- popPK models used cycle 2 data from the 18 women treated with the LNG/EE patch in cycles 1 and 2. The patch delivers LNG 120 ug/day and EE 30 ug/day (2.6 mg LNG and 2.3 mg EE per patch), applied weekly for 3 weeks, then 1 week patch-free.
- Sampling (weeks 1 and 3 of wear): pre-dose and 6, 12, 24, 48, 72, 120, 144, 168 h. After the third patch also 174, 180, 192, 216, 240 h. 23 samples per subject (p.3).
- Assay: LC-MS/MS on human plasma (500 uL, K oxalate/NaF). LLOQ was 2 pg/mL for EE and 50 pg/mL for LNG, with CVs of 5.17-15.3% (EE) and 2.28-15.8% (LNG) (p.3).
- Software: Phoenix NLME 8.1 for modelling and simulation, WinNonlin 8.1 for NCA, R 3.4.0.
- Simulation: 1000 virtual women, hourly concentrations for weeks 1, 3 and 12 of a 12-week continuous regimen (84 days) followed by 7 patch-free days. IIV was included and residual variability was not. BMI and weight were not modelled (p.4).

## Model and parameters
- EE: one-compartment model. Zero-order input into the central compartment for a fixed 168 h, plus a first-order route from a dosing compartment. Overall F fixed at 30%. Frel was estimated for the zero-order and first-order routes (Frel x F and (1-Frel) x F). IIV on V, CL and ka. Parameter RSEs 7-14% (p.3-4).
- LNG: one-compartment model. Zero-order input into the central compartment for a fixed 168 h, plus zero-order infusion into a dosing compartment with first-order absorption (duration estimated). Overall F fixed at 27%. A week-3 effect on V and CL was included (RSE 57% and 47%). IIV on V, CL and Frel. Other RSEs 10-31% (p.4).
- Final parameter values are in S1 Table (EE) and S2 Table (LNG), which are not in this PDF. No parameter values are available from this file.
- Simulated NCA summaries (prose, p.5-6; the table layout is scrambled in the text layer):
  - EE geometric mean Cmax: 40.8 (wk 1), 47.9 (wk 3), 48.0 (wk 12) pg/mL.
  - EE geometric mean Css(48-168): 33.5, 35.7, 35.7 pg/mL.
  - EE AR Cmax: 1.17 at weeks 3 and 12. EE AR AUC: 1.17 (wk 3), 1.18 (wk 12).
  - EE mean concentration peaks at 24 h.
  - LNG geometric mean Cmax: 1420, 2310, 2330 pg/mL.
  - LNG geometric mean Css(48-168): 1250, 1840, 1860 pg/mL.
  - LNG AR Cmax: 1.63 (wk 3), 1.64 (wk 12). LNG AR AUC: 1.68 and 1.70.
  - Week-3 vs week-12 AUC(0-168) is 0.2% lower (EE) and 0.9% lower (LNG) at week 3.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. This is a PK simulation, with no PD end point, MEAC or ke0.

## Covariates
None tested (n=18). BMI/weight were not evaluated. SHBG is suggested as a possible cause of between-cycle accumulation (p.8).

## Caveats
- Simulation from one cycle of data in 18 women. The authors call for confirmation in a clinical PK study.
- Funded by the patch's manufacturer. Several authors are its employees or consultants.
- Residual variability was not simulated.
- The model underpredicts EE above 0.1 ng/mL (p.4).
