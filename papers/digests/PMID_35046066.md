# Abduljalil 2022: Maternal-placental-fetal PBPK for cefuroxime, cefazolin, amoxicillin

## Study
Bottom-up whole-body maternal-placental-fetal PBPK model in Simcyp Simulator V20 (14 fetal tissue compartments, 3-compartment permeability-limited placenta, amniotic fluid compartment), run for three renally cleared antibiotics: cefuroxime, cefazolin, amoxicillin. Nonpregnant compound models were built and checked first, settings were kept, and pregnancy was predicted with no fitting; placental passive permeability came from polar surface area and H-bond donors (eq. 1, 2). Predictions were compared with published observed concentration-time and PK-parameter data (virtual trials matched to each study: 20 trials, size 20x the real study in Table 3). Reported: all predictions within 2-fold of observed. No new patients.

## Model and parameters
Cefazolin (PDF pp. 4-5, printed pp. 389-390): full-PBPK distribution, Rodgers-Rowland Kps with a global Kp scalar of 0.7 (matched to a 1000 mg IV dataset, Rattie and Ravin 1975); elimination by MechKiM kidney model (basolateral OAT1/OAT3, apical MRP4) plus nonspecific hepatic metabolism; IM absorption rate 1.0 /h (Scheld 1981). Placental CLPD 0.0012 L/h/mL placenta at term; fetal CLR 0.0130 L/h/kg (0.0172 L/h/kg including intramembranous flow). OAT3 activity in pregnancy 2.2-, 1.7-, 1.3-fold nonpregnant in trimesters 1-3 (from Peng 2021; fitted as a polynomial in gestational week, eq. 4); MRP4 and OAT1 left at nonpregnant values.

Pregnancy results for cefazolin (Table 3, PDF p. 11, read on the page image; Pred/Obs ratios are predicted/observed). Systemic CL L/h, observed vs predicted (means):
- Postpartum/nonpregnant: Philipson 1987 (6 F, GW 0) obs 4.66 (0.74), pred 4.3 (1.2); Elkomy 2014 (20 F, GW 0) obs 4.12, pred 4.2 (1.3). Healthy-male IV studies (Smyth 1979, 2-4 g) obs 3.58-4.56, pred 4.16-4.81.
- Pregnant: Philipson 1987 (19-33 GW) 7.3 (3.0) vs 6.9 (2.6); Allegaert 2009 (17-40 GW) 7.44 (1.34) vs 7.03 (2.22); van Hasselt 2014 (17-27 GW) 7.48 vs 6.94 (2.7) and (28-40 GW) 7.54 vs 6.8 (2.3) (the observed means are derived by the authors from reported means); Elkomy 2014 (36-40 GW) 7.18 (0.56) vs 6.3 (1.8); Grupper 2017 (39 GW) 7.38 (5.34) vs 9.25 (2.58), ratio 1.25.
- Text (PDF p. 9, printed p. 395): predicted systemic CL rises about 70% over nonpregnant at 20 GW, falling to about 60% at term; observed rises quoted 57% (Philipson 1987) and 74% (Elkomy 2014) vs postpartum. The comparator is the same women postpartum (Philipson, Elkomy) or the model's own nonpregnant adult (prediction).
- Cord/maternal ratio: predicted umbilical vein about 50% of maternal plasma; Table 2 (not image-checked) lists observed cefazolin ratios by study.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No pharmacodynamics. No MIC, target attainment, effect-site or ke0 content. Not an opioid.

## Covariates
Gestational week via continuous physiology (maternal GFR and renal blood flow, albumin, fetal growth: Abduljalil 2012, 2018-2021) plus the renal OAT3 function above. No creatinine covariate; GFR is gestational-age dependent inside the Simulator.

## Caveats
Authors: placental permeability uses an equation built for intestinal membranes and small molecules; amniotic extramembranous flow set to zero (early amniotic underprediction); many observed datasets lump wide gestational ranges, small n, or mixed doses; cord/maternal ratios depend on sampling time. Amoxicillin umbilical concentration was overpredicted. Whether CL is total or unbound is not stated in the text read; the text does state a Kp scalar and drug properties live in Supplemental Table 1 (not in the PDF).
