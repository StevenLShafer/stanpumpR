# Sharabiani 2026: GARIX Part II, temperature dependence of oxygen demand in paediatric bypass

## Study
Retrospective, single centre (Great Ormond Street), minute-by-minute data from paediatric cardiopulmonary bypass (CPB). Dataset 963 procedures (2019-2021); training on 293 procedures from 286 patients without postoperative AKI; 20,443 minutes of data. GARIX is an autoregressive model of oxygen extraction ratio (OER) with exogenous cardiac index, Hb, SaO2 and temperature, plus an equilibrium term for indexed oxygen demand (tVO2i) as a function of age, weight, their interaction and a spline in temperature. Age groups: neonate <1 month, infant 1-6 months, toddler 6 months-3 y, child >3 y. Uncertainty by 1000 patient-level bootstrap refits. Full model details are in Part 1 and the supplement, not in this PDF.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No drug data (not an opioid paper: no MEAC, ke0 or potency). Physiological end points are OER and oxygen demand. OER responds to a step in Hb or cardiac index with a lag, and the immediate response to Hb is weaker in neonates and infants than in older children (Fig 3). Simulated steps in oxygen delivery give transient over- or under-oxygenation (Fig 6).

## Covariates
tVO2i depends on log age, log weight, their interaction, and a spline in temperature; it is non-monotonic in age, peaking near 3 y. GARIX predicts BMR per kg with a pattern like Schofield's but about 58% lower (text, discussion).

## Caveats
Retrospective, single centre; data sparse below 25 C; Hb and SaO2 not truly exogenous; no outcome analysis; congenital anatomy not modelled; demand is a latent quantity inferred from equilibrium, not measured.
