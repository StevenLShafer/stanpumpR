# Kaila 1990: radioreceptor assay for glycopyrrolate, with three IM pilot patients

## Study
Methods paper (Turku). Radioreceptor assay (RRA): displacement of ³H-N-methylscopolamine (NMS) from rat cerebral
cortex muscarinic receptors, after extraction of plasma into dichloroethane with sodium picrate (plasma proteins
otherwise interfere, Table 1). Urine diluted 1:50 and assayed directly; CSF assayed directly.

Pilot PK: 3 women for vaginal hysterectomy under combined epidural/spinal anaesthesia, mean age 56.7 yr (48-68),
mean weight 83.5 kg (73-95), ASA I-II. Glycopyrrolate (Gastrodyn 0.2 mg/mL) 8 µg/kg into the right deltoid about
40 min before anaesthesia. Venous K-EDTA plasma serially to 8 h (Fig. 3); urine by catheter to 8 h; one CSF sample
with a matching blood sample about 40 min after injection (PDF p1, printed 313; confirmed on page image).
Dose is the labelled (bromide) dose; the paper does not state salt vs cation.

Table 3 (PDF p3, printed 315; confirmed on image), mean renal excretion as % of dose: 0-1 h 27.90, 1-3 h 17.61,
3-6 h 10.32, 6-8 h 4.00 (derived: 0-3 h 45.5%, the abstract's "almost 50% within 3 hr").
Assay precision (Table 2, plasma standards 50-5000 ng/L): intra-assay CV < 3%, inter-assay 8-16%. Plasma calibration
curve spans about 50-7000 ng/L (Fig. 1); how samples above that (Cmax ~14 µg/L) were handled is not stated.
No cross-reaction of de-esterified or hydroxylated derivatives below > 10⁵ µg/L. CSF: all below detection limit.
Discussion: RRA about 200 times more sensitive than the GC method of Murray 1984; the two agree above 10 µg/L.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None measured. No ke0, EC50 or effect end point. Not an opioid (no MEAC or potency ratio). The RRA measures
muscarinic-binding activity, i.e. active drug, which the authors present as an advantage over RIA/chemical assays.

## Covariates
None (n = 3).

## Caveats
- n = 3, older nonpregnant women with neuraxial blocks; a pilot to show the assay works, not a PK study.
- Internal dose inconsistency: Table 3 title says 0.08 mg/kg vs 8 µg/kg everywhere else (see open question).
- RRA is non-specific: any antimuscarinic drug (anticholinergics, some neuroleptics, antihistamines, tricyclics)
  interferes.
- Cmax lies above the top plasma calibration standard; dilution not described.
