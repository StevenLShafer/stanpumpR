# Ho 2026: intact oxaliplatin exposure and AUC from end-of-infusion concentration

## Study
Re-analysis of two datasets. (1) ChAMPION trial (ACTRN12611000738921, earlier published as Han 2013): 19 adults with advanced colorectal cancer (12 M, 7 F, age 33-77 y, weight 47.2-114 kg, serum creatinine 54-107 umol/L), 38 cycles (cycles 1 and 2 of oxaliplatin-based chemotherapy, calcium/magnesium vs placebo crossover, no effect on PK). Oxaliplatin 85 or 130 mg/m2 over 2 h (patient 18 97.5 mg/m2 both cycles; patient 17 118 mg/m2 cycle 2). 13 samples: 0, 20, 40, 60, 90 min, end of infusion, then 5, 10, 20, 30, 60, 120, 180 min after the end. Plasma was methanol-deproteinised at the bedside (plasma:methanol 1:2), stored at -80 C. Intact oxaliplatin [Pt(DACH)oxalato] by HPLC-ICP-MS; total unbound platinum by ICP-MS. (2) External validation: 10 patients with metastatic colorectal cancer, 85 mg/m2 over 2 h, 9 samples, intact oxaliplatin in plasma ultrafiltrate by LC with post-column derivatisation and UV (Ehrsson 2002, Table 2 of that report); AUC values from WinNonlin. Reference AUC: linear trapezoid to the last sample (log-linear within 1%); values below LOQ set to 0; 4 of 494 missing values interpolated with a one-compartment infusion model (Pkweb).

## Model and parameters
No population model. A one-compartment constant-rate-infusion model (Eqs. 8-9) was fitted to dose-normalised (to 130 mg/m2) mean concentrations, intact oxaliplatin (Fig. 1a, PDF p. 6; R2 = 0.99). Two AUC estimators: steady-state (AUC = CT x infusion duration) and linear (AUC = m x CT).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No pharmacodynamics, effect-site, ke0, EC50 or MEAC. The paper says exposure-response and therapeutic-range data are lacking, and that no exposure-response data were in these datasets. Not an opioid paper.

## Covariates
None modelled: interpatient CV of dose-normalised AUC is under 20%, judged too small for covariate analysis.

## Caveats
- Intact oxaliplatin and total unbound platinum are separate analytes; both are unbound (methanol-deproteinised plasma, validated against ultrafiltration for total unbound Pt).
- The AUCs are truncated at 3 h after the end of infusion (last sample at 5 h), so total unbound platinum AUC is time-dependent (authors note AUC0-inf is about twice AUC0-48 in an earlier study); the intact-fraction numbers refer to this window only.
- External set used a different assay, units and AUC method; AUC was taken from the published table, and Cmax was assumed equal to end-of-infusion concentration.
- 38 cycles from 19 patients, so cycles are not independent; one dataset of advanced colorectal cancer only.
