# Antognini 1994: hypothermia and nitrous oxide MAC in rats

## Study
Female Sprague-Dawley rats (2-3 months, 250-300 g) in a hyperbaric chamber (~600 L) with N2O and O2 (O2 0.4 +/- 0.1 atm,
inspired CO2 0-1 mm Hg). Group N (normothermia, n=10): rectal temperature 37.5 +/- 1 C; Group H (n=9): 29.7 +/- 1.8 C;
three further rats at about 34 C for the regression (22 rats in all). Stimulus: tail electrical current 50 Hz, 70 mA,
0.2 ms/pulse, 30 s (40 mA in four rats at 35.2 +/- 0.9 C). Gross purposeful movement positive. Chamber pressure stepped
about 15% up or down, 10-15 min equilibration, MAC the mean of the pressures just permitting and just preventing
movement. Gas by Datex 254; chamber gauge accuracy +/- 2%. Unpaired t-test; linear regression.

## Model and parameters
No PK model. Results (PDF p2, printed 981, image-confirmed): N2O MAC 1.9 +/- 0.2 atm (N) and 1.6 +/- 0.2 atm (H),
P<0.01 (mean +/- SD). The four 40 mA rats: 1.8 +/- 0.3 atm. Figure 1 regression over all rats:
MAC (atm) = 0.03827 x T(C) + 0.3836, r = 0.59, P<0.01 (confirmed as a plus sign on the image).
Derived: zero MAC at T = -0.3836/0.03827 = -10.0 C; MAC(28)/MAC(37) = 0.81.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: movement to electrical tail stimulus (MAC). N2O MAC fell about 20% over 38 to 28 C (Table 1, extrapolated
for some entries), against 41-63% for halothane, isoflurane, desflurane, 33% for cyclopropane in rats (Table 1, PDF
p3). The authors predicted about 13% from N2O's lipid-solubility change over 38-28 C (refs 8, 9); the observed 20% is
larger. They place a physiological "knee" below 28-30 C. Figure 2: change in MAC vs change in lipid solubility across
agents, r = 0.90. No ke0, EC50 or potency ratios. (Not an opioid.)

## Covariates
Body temperature only (rats; no human data).

## Caveats
Rats, not people; between-group design (not paired); stimulus lower than ideal because 70 mA is near burn
threshold (authors argue it was not submaximal); N2O MAC may be slightly underestimated in both groups; r = 0.59;
the range tested was about 29-38 C and the hypothermia group is a single temperature; no measurement below 28 C.
