# Randinitis 2003: pregabalin in renal impairment and haemodialysis

## Study
Open-label, parallel group, single 50 mg oral dose (two 25 mg capsules), fasted. 26 subjects in CG-defined CLcr groups (> 80, 51-80, 30-50, < 30 mL/min) plus 12 haemodialysis patients (Table II: ages 30-75, weights 44.5-115.5 kg). Sampling to 72 h (CLcr >= 30) or 168 h; dialysate sampled. Validated LC assay, 0.05-20 ug/mL. Noncompartmental (WinNonlin 2.1).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None (not an opioid; no MEAC, ke0 or potency).

## Covariates
CLcr (Cockcroft-Gault, equation in Table I footnote). Dosing (Table I): CLcr >= 60: 150-600 mg/day; 30-<60: 75-300; 15-<30: 25-150; < 15: 25-75; supplemental post-HD dose 25-100 mg. Recommendation: halve the dose per halving of CLcr below 60 mL/min.

## Caveats
Small groups (one subject < 15 mL/min); single dose; older subjects (normal-function group t1/2 9.1 h, longer than young volunteers).
