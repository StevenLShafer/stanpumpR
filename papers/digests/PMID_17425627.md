# Linnebjerg 2007: effect of renal impairment on exenatide pharmacokinetics

## Study
- Open-label, single-dose, parallel-group study at three Belgian sites (printed 318-319). 31 subjects aged 25-80,
  BMI 19-40, stratified by Cockcroft-Gault CrCL: normal > 80 mL/min (n 8), mild 51-80 (n 8), moderate 31-50 (n 7),
  ESRD on haemodialysis ≥ 1 month (n 8). One subject (mild RI) had type 2 diabetes; the rest were nondiabetic.
- Table 1 (PDF p4 / printed 320): mean CrCL (range) 111 (83-156), 68 (60-78), 45 (34-50) mL/min, ESRD N/A; age
  46 ± 5.5, 56 ± 9.9, 64 ± 9.6 (P < 0.05 vs control), 52 ± 18.3 y; weight 73.3 ± 10.9, 76.9 ± 13.3, 76.1 ± 12.6,
  64.1 ± 13.5 kg; male:female 3:5, 5:3, 5:2, 3:5.
- Dose: 10 µg SC (normal, mild), 5 µg (moderate; 2 of 7 inadvertently got 10 µg), 5 µg (ESRD, dosed in the long
  interdialytic interval). Sampling to 12 h (normal), 18 h (mild, moderate), 48 h (ESRD). Immunoenzymetric assay,
  10-500 pg/mL.
- Noncompartmental analysis (WinNonlin 3.1); one moderate-RI subject (10 µg) excluded as an outlier (low
  concentrations). Combined analysis with four earlier single-dose crossover studies in 67 patients with T2D
  (age 52.3 ± 8.77, BMI 33 ± 4.71, weight 97.1 ± 18 kg; CrCL 140 ± 47.5 mL/min in 63, four with mild RI); linear
  mixed-effects model, subject random.

Dose-weight-normalised exposure ratios vs normal, combined data (Table 5): AUC mild 1.18 (P 0.156), moderate 1.63
(P 0.003), ESRD 6.24 (P < 0.001); Cmax 1.17, 1.34, 3.28. Within the renal study alone (Table 4): CL/F ratios mild
1.30, moderate 1.12, ESRD 0.26; AUC ESRD 3.37.

**Abstract vs table**: the abstract lists combined CL/F as 8.14, 5.19, 7.11 and 1.3 L/h for normal, mild,
moderate and ESRD; Table 5 (rendered) and the Results text (p6) give mild 7.11 and moderate 5.19, and the stated
reductions (13% mild, 36% moderate, 84% ESRD; Discussion) agree with the table (7.11/8.14 = 0.87,
5.19/8.14 = 0.64). The abstract has mild and moderate transposed.

Why combine: four of eight renal-study controls had unexpectedly low clearance (CL/F 2.13-3.16 L/h, Cmax
960-1996 pg/mL) against the usual about 9 L/h and 200 pg/mL after 10 µg (p6).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD model. A reference range of 50-350 pg/mL is used as "efficacious and tolerable" in normal renal function
(refs 19, 20); above 350 pg/mL nausea increases (p8). Nonparametric superposition: moderate RI 5 µg bid stays below
350, 10 µg bid exceeds it for 2-4 h; ESRD 5 µg qd mostly above 350, 2.5 µg qd below (Fig. 3, untested clinically).
Tolerability: ESRD poorly tolerated (nausea, vomiting; antiemetics in 7 of 8; Table 2).

## Covariates
CL/F vs Cockcroft-Gault CrCL: linear mixed-effects regression, r = 0.63, P < 0.001 (Fig. 2); the slope and
intercept are not printed.

## Caveats
- Single doses; small groups (6-12 in the combined analysis outside normal); the normal group mixes healthy
  controls with T2D patients from other studies.
- Different doses by group (10 vs 5 µg); dose-weight normalisation assumes linearity.
- No severe-RI (non-dialysis) group; its clearance is interpolated.
- Dialysis effect not evaluable (BLQ at 48 h).
- Abstract transposes mild and moderate CL/F (above).

  L/h to moderate impairment. Table 5 gives mild 7.11 and moderate 5.19. The repo follows the abstract's order, which

  L/h, about 2.7-fold) is unaffected. "ESRD on dialysis" **matches** (haemodialysis group).

  3.5 L/h are the repo's model values (Cirincione 2017 card), arithmetic correct; 3.5/1.3 = 2.7.
- Context note: the archive context's `used_for` string repeats the abstract's transposed order.
- Note: the study's renal measure is Cockcroft-Gault CrCL in mL/min, not the MDRD eGFR the model uses; the
