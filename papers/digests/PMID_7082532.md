# Tomson 1982: pethidine kinetics and placental passage in labour

## Study
- 30 term pregnant women (38-42 weeks), normal pregnancies, Huddinge Hospital, Stockholm. Group I (n=16): maternal kinetics. Group II (n=14): foeto-maternal relation only (Table 1, PDF p.4). Group I age 26 y (19-35), 8 nulliparous / 8 multiparous, all vaginal delivery; birth weight 3500 g (2970-4670). Maternal weight not given in the paper.
- Pethidine 1.5 mg/kg intramuscularly (total 70-130 mg), single dose; patients needing a second injection were excluded. Plasma sampled through an arm cannula at 10, 20, 30, 60, 120, 240 min and at delivery (fewer samples when dose-delivery interval < 180 min). Dose-delivery interval 30 min to 8.5 h in Group I.
- Group II: foetal scalp blood (FSB) twice per patient plus maternal vein simultaneously, and umbilical artery/vein at delivery; dose-to-sampling 20 min to 9.5 h (cord up to 10.5 h).
- Assay: GC-mass spectrometry (LKB 2091), pethidine standard curves 5-250 and 50-1000 ng/mL; norpethidine detection limit about 10 ng/mL. Maternal samples were plasma; foetal and cord samples were stored as whole blood (small volumes). Blood/plasma ratio, 14C-pethidine: 0.904 +/- 0.156 (n=17, pregnant women), 0.964 +/- 0.056 (n=5, cord) (PDF p.3).
- Half-life: least-squares regression on the apparently monoexponential part (at least 3 points); terminal phase reached 1-1.5 h after dose.

Norpethidine was below 10 ng/mL (not measurable) in maternal plasma of the 7 women analysed (2.7-8.5 h intervals) and in 7 cord samples. Foetal pethidine reached a plateau between 1 and 5 h after the maternal dose (Fig 2); maternal concentration peaked at 30-60 min.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- No pharmacodynamic measurement, no MEAC, no ke0 / t1/2ke0 / tPeak, no equianalgesic ratio. Opioid audit: none measured.
- Only a cited clinical benchmark: the Discussion (p.6) quotes an average analgesic level of 550 ng/mL in postoperative patients from Tamsen 1979 (patient-controlled analgesia; matrix and assay not stated here) and says the highest umbilical artery value here is about two thirds of it. That is a secondhand number, not a result of this paper.
- tmax of 28.6 min after IM injection is an absorption feature of the intramuscular route, not an effect-site delay.

## Covariates
Weight-based dosing only (1.5 mg/kg). The authors note the weight-based dose gave only a two-fold spread in peak concentration. No covariate analysis. Time from dose to delivery is the determinant of the foeto-maternal ratio (positive correlation).

## Caveats
- Single IM dose, intramuscular absorption confounds the early profile; terminal half-life is apparent.
- Half-life in only 10 of 16 patients; 6 patients delivered before 180 min and had fewer samples.
- Foetal data are whole blood while maternal data are plasma (corrected with the measured blood/plasma ratio).
- Norpethidine not detectable, unlike other groups' reports (the authors discuss assay or enzyme-induction differences).
- No non-pregnant control group in this paper.

- pregnancyRegistry.csv:8: n=16, IM, in labour, half-life 3.4 h and the "not different from healthy controls" wording all match the abstract and Table 2: **matches**. The registry's "evidence_read" says abstract; the full text adds that the half-life was calculable in only 10 patients and that the control comparison is to earlier literature (see open question).
- Independent check against the library model (R/drugs_pethidine.R, Bjorkman rate constants, V1 18.1 L at 70 kg, CL = V1*k10 = 0.763 L/min = 45.8 L/h): the model's slowest disposition half-life is 270 min (4.5 h), its intermediate half-life 22 min (derived from the rate constants). Tomson's 3.4 h is an apparent half-life taken from about 1-1.5 h to 4-9 h after an IM dose, so it sits between the two and cannot be compared cleanly with either; it does not contradict "unchanged".
- The library MEAC (R/drugs_pethidine.R:25, 0.46 mcg/mL, whole blood, from Austin 1980) is not supported or contradicted by this paper; Tomson reports no MEAC. The cited 550 ng/mL (Tamsen) is a different study and not in the code.
- The pethidine row applies no factor (cl_factor NA), consistent with "unchanged"; nothing else in the repo cites this paper.
