# Franzese 2022: ceftazidime-avibactam PopPK in children

## Study
Pooled adult plus pediatric PopPK analysis (NONMEM 7.3, FOCE-INTER; avibactam final model by SAEM/IMP with MAP; R 3.4.3 for simulation; PsN, Xpose). Adult two-compartment models (ref. 15) were updated with pediatric data from one phase I single-dose study (NCT01893346) and two phase II multiple-dose studies, cIAI (NCT02475733) and cUTI (NCT02497781). 153 pediatric patients contributed 509 ceftazidime and 488 avibactam observations; totals 9,628 observations / 2,130 subjects (ceftazidime) and 14,223 / 2,403 (avibactam). Pediatric baseline (Table 1, PDF p4, printed 638): n = 153, 55.6% female, age median 7.57 y (0.25-17.7), weight median 25.0 kg (4.1-80.0), BSA 0.961 m2, NCrCL median 104 mL/min/1.73 m2 (43-489). Doses (CrCL >= 50): 2,000-500 mg (12-<18 y), 50-12.5 mg/kg (3 mo-18 y), 40-10 mg/kg (3-6 mo), capped at adult dose for >= 40 kg; 2-h IV infusion q8h, ceftazidime:avibactam 4:1. LC-MS/MS on total (free plus bound) plasma; LLOQ 50 ng/mL ceftazidime, 10 ng/mL avibactam.

## Model and parameters
Two-compartment, first-order elimination from the central compartment, for both drugs. Final parameter estimates are in the Supplement (Tables S2, S3), not in this PDF. Structure given in the text (PDF p3-4):
- Allometric weight, reference 70 kg: CL and Q exponent 0.67 (changed from 0.75 after estimation); Vc and Vp exponent 1. For ceftazidime, the weight effect on CL was refined to an Emax-type function because a power model biased CL in low-weight children (details in the supplement); Q, Vc, Vp ceftazidime exponents fixed 0.67, 1.0, 1.0.
- Renal function: children > 2 y use BSA-normalised CrCL (updated bedside Schwartz); adults Cockcroft-Gault normalised to 1.73 m2; children <= 2 y use a maturation function on postmenstrual age (50% maturation at 47.7 weeks, sigmoidicity 3.4; Rhodin), PMA = postnatal age + 40 weeks if unknown.
- Text states cIAI patients have 33% higher ceftazidime CL (PDF p9; comparator = non-cIAI patients in the same model).
- Free fractions assumed for simulation: ceftazidime 85%, avibactam 92%.

Selected exposures (geometric mean, %CV; simulated, 50-12.5 mg/kg q8h, normal renal function, cUTI; Table 3, PDF p6-7): ceftazidime AUCss,0-24 760-864 ug*h/mL and Cmax,ss 86-92 ug/mL in children >= 1 y; adults 2,000-500 mg q8h 828 ug*h/mL, 74.0 ug/mL. Avibactam children 118-136 ug*h/mL; adults 113 (Table 3).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD model, ke0 or concentration-effect parameter. Antibacterial PK/PD target: 50% of the dosing interval with free ceftazidime > 8.0 mg/L (with avibactam) and free avibactam > 1.0 mg/L (threshold CT), simultaneously (joint target), MIC 8 mg/L. Joint PTA at MIC 8 mg/L (Table 4): cIAI 82% for 1-<2 and 2-<6 y, 90-96% other pediatric ages, adults 95%; cUTI >= 94%; HAP/VAP (NP) >= 92%. Not an opioid; no MEAC.

## Covariates
Body weight (allometric), NCrCL or postmenstrual age (renal), indication (cIAI on CL in adults; mechanical ventilation on Vc in adults in the HAP/VAP trial); forward p = 0.05, backward p = 0.01.

## Caveats
No pediatric HAP/VAP PK data (extrapolated from adults); no pediatric cIAI patients < 2 y; few cIAI children <= 6 y (6 patients 2-6 y); mild renal impairment only (moderate/severe reported separately); tissue penetration assumed equal to adults; total-drug assay with assumed free fractions; outliers (CWRES > 4) excluded.
