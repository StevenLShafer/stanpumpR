# Liu 2014: N2O sparing of propofol and remifentanil in BIS-guided closed-loop anaesthesia

## Study
Randomized, double-blind, 10-centre trial (NCT00547209). Adults aged 18-80 y, ASA I-IV, elective surgery over 60 min needing intubation. Jan 2008 to Jun 2009. 672 randomized; 601 analysed (302 N2O, 299 AIR; Fig 1, p. 844). Group N2O: 60% N2O with 40% O2. Group AIR: 40% O2.
Propofol and remifentanil were given by a dual PID closed-loop controller on BIS (set point 50), run on Infusion Toolbox 95 v4.11. The controller used Schnider (propofol) and Minto (remifentanil) effect-site calculations and TCI. Limits: propofol 1.3-5 µg/mL and remifentanil 3-12 ng/mL effect site (p. 844). Remifentanil gain constants were cut 15% from the earlier controller.
Endpoints were consumption between start of ventilation and end of infusion, and time with BIS 40-60. Sex interaction was a secondary analysis requested by the German ethics committee.

## Model and parameters
No PK model was fitted. The paper only cites the Schnider propofol model and the Minto remifentanil model as used inside the controller (p. 843). No parameter table.

Consumption results (median, IQR; abstract p. 842 and Table 2 p. 849; text layer only):
- All patients, propofol N2O 4.5 (3.7-5.5) vs AIR 4.8 (4.0-5.9) mg/kg/h, P=0.032 (a 6% reduction). Remifentanil 0.17 (0.12-0.23) vs 0.18 (0.14-0.24) µg/kg/min, not significant.
- Men: propofol 4.2 vs 4.4 mg/kg/h and remifentanil 0.19 vs 0.18, both not significant.
- Women: propofol 4.7 (4.0-5.8) vs 5.3 (4.5-6.6) mg/kg/h, P=0.004 (11% less). Remifentanil 0.18 vs 0.20 µg/kg/min, P=0.029 (14% less).
- BIS 40-60: 79% vs 76% overall.
- Induction (p. 846): propofol 1.1 vs 1.0 mg/kg; remifentanil 1.8 vs 1.8 µg/kg.
- Reference values from the earlier controller study cited for power: propofol 4.7 mg/kg/h and remifentanil 0.22 µg/kg/min (p. 845).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Not an opioid PD paper. No MEAC, ke0, t½ke0, tPeak, hysteresis or potency ratio is reported. The paper only says that time to peak effect is shorter for remifentanil than propofol, and that the controller step interval equals each drug's time to peak (p. 844). The only end point is BIS (set point 50). The paper does not report a remifentanil analgesic concentration. The remifentanil limits of 3-12 ng/mL (effect-site, calculated) are controller limits, not MEAC values.
Opioid audit: none.

## Covariates
Sex: a significant treatment-by-sex interaction (Table 3, p. 849). N2O sparing was seen in women, not men. No other covariate model.

## Caveats
Noxious stimulation was not recorded. Results depend on the controller gain constants. BIS may under-read N2O effect. Only intraoperative consumption was measured. The authors' conclusion is that the sparing effect is statistically but not clinically important (propofol 6%).
