# Hedaya 2015: irbesartan and hydrochlorothiazide PK/PD in normotensive men

## Study
Open-label, randomized, three-way crossover, 2-week washout, single oral doses under fasting: A irbesartan (IRB) 300 mg, B hydrochlorothiazide (HCT) 25 mg, C fixed-dose combination 300/25 mg (p. 218-219, PDF 3-4). n = 18 healthy normotensive Egyptian men, age 25.2 +/- 8.1 y, weight 77.6 +/- 6.1 kg, BMI 21 +/- 2.3 (PDF 7). Plasma sampled 0.25 to 48 h (16 samples) by HPLC (IRB fluorescence, LLOQ 0.01 ug/mL; HCT UV, LLOQ 1 ng/mL; both total plasma, no free-drug assay). Seated BP (mercury sphygmomanometer) and heart rate measured at the same 15 times. Analysis with WinNonlin: non-compartmental and a two-compartment first-order-absorption model; PK/PD fitted simultaneously (effect-compartment, sigmoid Emax) for IRB only (HCT 25 mg did not lower BP; heart rate unchanged).

## Model and parameters
Two-compartment with first-order absorption and elimination for both drugs (p. 220). **Volumes and intercompartmental clearance are not printed**; Table 1 gives only ka, Cmax, tmax, AUC0-inf, half-life, MRT and CL/F (mean +/- SD, n = 18). Interaction: IRB PK unchanged by HCT (all differences not significant); IRB lowered HCT AUC about 25% and raised HCT CL/F about 25% (p. 223-224 text, Table 1). Between-subject CV for IRB Cmax 42.0-43.1% and AUC 38.9-44.3% (PDF 9).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: absolute fall in seated systolic (SBP) and diastolic (DBP) blood pressure from baseline (baseline 119.3 +/- 9.4 and 79.1 +/- 8.1 mmHg, PDF 9), in mmHg, not percent. Model: E = E0 - Emax Ce^n/(EC50^n + Ce^n), effect compartment linked to the central compartment (eq. 4, Figure 1, PDF 6). Concentrations are total plasma IRB. - ke0 (keo) is "the rate of equilibration of the effect compartment concentration with the central compartment concentration" (Table 2 footnote); no t1/2keo or tPeak is stated by the authors (t1/2keo above is derived: 0.693/keo).
- Observed: peak IRB concentration about 2 h, maximum BP fall 4-5 h (PDF 9) or 4-6 h (PDF 13); anticlockwise hysteresis in Figure 4, collapsed when plotted against modelled effect-site concentration (Figure 5). Mean maximal fall, IRB alone: SBP 9.7 +/- 5.1, DBP 6.1 +/- 2.7 mmHg; FDC: 11.1 +/- 4.3 and 7.2 +/- 3.9 mmHg (PDF 9).
- Authors' reading: combination Emax about 25% higher and EC50 about 40% lower for SBP; DBP only EC50 significant (abstract, PDF 1).
- The text quotes an IRB "IC50 1.3 nm" (PDF 13), a receptor-level literature value, not the fitted EC50 (2.1 ug/mL is about 4.9 uM at MW 428.5; derived).
- HCT: no measurable effect on BP or heart rate at 25 mg, so no PD parameters. Not an opioid; no MEAC or potency ratio.

## Covariates
None tested in the PK/PD model. Cohort restricted to men, 18-33 y, BMI 20-26, IBW > 60 kg (PDF 3).

## Caveats
n = 18 healthy normotensive men, single dose, so the effect is small (9.7 mmHg SBP) against 16.4/11.7 mmHg reported by others for repeated 300 mg in hypertensives (PDF 13). Parameters are means of individual fits with large SDs (EC50 SD 33-87% of mean). The fixed-dose arm was the only one given HCT, so the EC50/Emax shift is confounded with HCT's own PD. Irbesartan concentration units are misprinted in Table 1 (ng/ml for ug/mL). No volumes, Q or V/F are reported. Effect-site concentration is derived from the authors' own PK fit; applying EC50 to a different PK model changes the effect (see below).
