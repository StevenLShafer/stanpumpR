# Avidan 2011: BAG-RECALL, BIS versus end-tidal anesthetic-agent protocol

## Study
Prospective, randomized, evaluator-blinded trial at three centres (Washington University St. Louis, University of Chicago, University of Manitoba), May 2008 to May 2010. Adults 18 y or older having elective surgery under isoflurane, sevoflurane or desflurane, with at least one awareness risk factor (open-heart surgery, ASA 4, ejection fraction below 40%, opiate, benzodiazepine or anticonvulsant use, daily alcohol, etc.; Table 1, p. 4). 6041 randomized, 5809 analysed, 5713 (98.3%) interviewed at least once (Figure 1, p. 6): BIS group n = 2861, ETAC group n = 2852. Mean age 60 (BIS) and 61 (ETAC) y.

Intervention: BIS group had an audible alarm when BIS was above 60 or below 40 (0-100 scale). ETAC group had an alarm when end-tidal agent was below 0.7 or above 1.3 age-adjusted MAC (MAC from Eger 2001 and Nickalls 2003 charts, p. 3). Both groups had checklists and education; protocols did not prescribe agent dose. Awareness assessed with a modified Brice questionnaire within 72 h and at 30 days; events graded by blinded experts (Michigan Awareness Classification).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No MEAC, ke0 or potency ratio. Relevant anaesthetic-depth facts:
- Definite awareness (primary): BIS 7/2861 (0.24%) vs ETAC 2/2852 (0.07%); difference 0.17 percentage points (95% CI -0.03 to 0.38); one-sided P = 0.98 (Table 3, p. 8). Superiority of BIS not shown.
- Definite or possible awareness: 19 (0.66%) vs 8 (0.28%); difference 0.38 (95% CI 0.03 to 0.74) (Table 3).
- Distressing awareness (post hoc): 8 (0.28%) vs 1 (0.04%) (Table 3).
- Overall incidence of definite awareness 9 patients (0.16%); definite or possible 27 (0.47%) (p. 6).
- During maintenance: median ETAC 0.9 age-adjusted MAC (IQR 0.8-1.0) and median BIS 41 (IQR 38-45) in both groups; BIS below 60 a median 94.0% of the time, ETAC above 0.7 MAC a median 84.8% (p. 6). Of 9 definite-awareness patients, 5 had no BIS above 60 or ETAC below 0.7 MAC (p. 6).
- Background stated in the introduction (p. 2): MAC is the concentration preventing movement in 50%; at about 0.33 MAC half of subjects do not respond to oral commands; maintaining ETAC above 0.7 MAC is thought to lower awareness.
- No difference in sedative, hypnotic, opioid or relaxant doses; midazolam given to 80.8% vs 79.7%; 30-day mortality 1.96% vs 2.21%.

## Covariates
Risk-factor count was higher in patients with awareness (median one more inclusion criterion; p. 6). No dosing equations.

## Caveats
Authors' limitations (p. 9): high-risk population receiving mainly inhaled agent only; one EEG monitor tested; alarm fatigue; few missing interviews (most deaths); unidentified factors such as genetic resistance. The direction of the primary result is opposite to the hypothesis.
