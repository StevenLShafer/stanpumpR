# Teh 1994: midazolam-propofol free-concentration interaction

## Study
- 12 adult Chinese patients, 18-60 y, ASA I-II, within 20% of ideal body weight, elective surgery, under general anaesthesia (fentanyl 1.5 ug/kg, thiopentone 3-5 mg/kg, atracurium, 70% N2O). Randomised to two groups of six.
- Group A: propofol by computer-controlled infusion to hold a plasma target of 1504 ng/mL, then midazolam added at 30 min targeting 94 ng/mL. Group B: midazolam first, propofol added at 30 min. Targets were the predicted concentrations at the ED50 hypnotic doses of Short & Chui 1991 (midazolam 0.14 mg/kg, propofol 1.01 mg/kg), delivered with published adult kinetics (Marsh propofol, Short midazolam; refs 5 and 6).
- Two venous 5 mL samples at 2-min intervals for 10 min before and 10 min after the second drug (six before, five after used in the test). Both drugs by HPLC; protein binding by equilibrium dialysis (5 h, 37 C, pH 7.40). Midazolam free concentration was below assay accuracy (10 ng/mL), so samples were spiked with midazolam (500 ug) to measure binding. Propofol assayed in whole blood (limit of accuracy 2 ng/mL).
- Midazolam group: mean age 32 y (18-46), 59 kg (47-71), 4 F/2 M. Propofol group: 40 y (19-60), 54 kg (48-65), 2 F/4 M. Mean concentration held: midazolam 84 ng/mL, propofol 1304 ng/mL.

Free fractions are about 2.3% midazolam and 1.4% propofol (derived: these are the values at the measured, not spiked, concentrations as reported). Free midazolam rose 20% on adding propofol, not significant. Power 0.8 to detect an 80% rise, the change needed to explain the 44% fall in hypnotic ED50 for the combination (p63).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD measured. The paper concludes the synergism is not explained by altered free plasma concentration and is probably pharmacodynamic (GABA-A). No ke0, MEAC, EC50 or potency ratio reported. The only PD numbers are cited from Short & Chui 1991: the combined hypnotic ED50 was about 44-45% below that expected from the single agents. Free concentration was measured in plasma only, not CSF or brain.

## Covariates
None analysed.

## Caveats
Small n (6 per group), venous samples, anaesthetised patients receiving other drugs, midazolam binding measured on spiked samples, power only excludes an effect of about 80% in free concentration. Concentrations are steady-state.
