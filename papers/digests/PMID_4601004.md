# Wagner 1974: V/Q distributions in normal subjects on air and 100% O2

## Study
Multiple inert gas elimination technique (six gases: SF6, ethane, cyclopropane, halothane, diethyl ether, acetone) infused IV at 2-5 mL/min (usual 5 mL/min) in dextrose or saline for about 20 min to reach steady state; arterial blood and mixed expired gas sampled (duplicate sets 5 min apart), cardiac output by indocyanine green dilution, minute ventilation by respirometer; gases measured by gas chromatography. Distributions use 50 compartments over V/Q 0.005-100 (p. 8-9).
Twelve healthy non-smoking men: group I 4 young (21-24 y), group II 5 older (39-60 y), both semirecumbent 30-40 degrees on air and on 100% O2 (O2 for at least 35 min exposure; N2 washout confirmed end-tidal N2 below 1%); group III 3 young (21-22 y) on air supine and erect only (Table I, p. 3-4). Of 10 older applicants, 5 were rejected for previously unknown abnormalities.

Across the nine subjects studied on O2, 8 developed a shunt within 30 min, reversible on returning to air (p. 10 and p. 15). (Derived: weighted by group, about 2% mean over the nine, if 0.5% is the mean over all four young subjects.)

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Not applicable (no drug). Other findings: 95% of both blood flow and ventilation lay at V/Q 0.3-2.1 in the young; ln SD of blood flow rises with age (p. 14, Fig. 7); in older subjects 7-15% of blood flow sat at V/Q below 0.3 in some individuals (W.C. had V/Q 0.01-0.1 regions). Shunt on O2 was greatest where air-breathing blood flow was in V/Q below 0.1; W.C. went from 0 to 10.7% on O2 while a 9.6% low-V/Q region disappeared, attributed by the authors to absorption atelectasis (pp. 9, 14-15). Limits: V/Q below 0.005 cannot be separated from shunt (p. 15).

## Covariates
Age (ln SD of blood flow, P<0.05 older vs young); posture (erect shifts to more high V/Q, narrower blood flow); FIO2 (oxygen produces shunt).

## Caveats
Twelve selected healthy men, awake, spontaneously breathing, no anaesthesia, small groups; cardiac output and ventilation error shifts V/Q position but not shape (p. 15). Shunt measured includes intrapulmonary and intracardiac shunt but not bronchial/Thebesian venous admixture (p. 6). None of this is under general anaesthesia.
