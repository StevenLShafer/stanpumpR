# Urien 2004: population PK of total and unbound plasma cisplatin

## Study
- 43 adult cancer patients (25 M / 18 F) in two phase I combination studies (cisplatin + irofulven 0.4 mg/kg
  over 30 min, n = 18; or + 5-fluorouracil 1 g/m²/day as a 120-h infusion, n = 25); metastatic disease,
  second/third-line chemotherapy (p 757 / PDF p 2; p 758 / PDF p 3).
- Demographics (Table 1, p 759 / PDF p 4; mean, median, range): age 55, 58, 21–76 y; weight 63, 64, 40–102 kg;
  height 167, 168, 150–181 cm; BSA 1.69, 1.62, 1.38–2.10 m²; serum protein 68, 70, 47–80 g/L; serum creatinine
  77, 76, 43–120 µmol/L; CLCr (Cockcroft–Gault) 84, 81, 44–155 mL/min; dose per infusion 35.5, 34.4, 20–80 mg;
  dose 25, 25, 15–40 mg/m² per infusion.
- Cisplatin IV over 30 min (actual 0.25–1 h over 146 infusions), daily ×5 or twice a month; total dose per
  infusion 15–80 mg (Methods) — Table 1 gives the range as 20–80 mg. One to five consecutive daily infusions
  per patient analysed.
- Sampling: end of infusion and 0.25, 0.45, 0.75, 1, 1.5, 2, 4, 6, 8, 12, 24 h after start; 483 samples →
  396 unbound and 477 total concentrations.
- Assay: platinum by flameless (Zeeman) atomic absorption spectrophotometry; unbound = plasma ultrafiltrate
  (Amicon MPS-1, YMT membrane, 4 °C). Lower limit of determination printed as "20 mg l-1" in the text layer
  (p 757); the concentration data are in µg/L, so this is presumably 20 µg/L (not confirmed on an image).
- Software: MP2 (extended least squares, Yamaoka); 200-sample bootstrap validation; OSP-Fit for optimal
  sampling times. Measured species is platinum, not intact cisplatin.

## Model and parameters
Two-compartment linear models fitted sequentially to total and to unbound (ultrafilterable) platinum; then an
integrated model in which unbound platinum in V1 is irreversibly bound to plasma protein, modelled as
"metabolite" formation with clearance CL1m = fm·CL into a bound compartment eliminated by CLm0 (Fig. 1,
p 758). Vm is not identifiable; fm/Vm and CLm0/Vm are estimated. Proportional ISV; additive residual error.

Final covariate equations (p 760 / PDF p 5): V1 = 23.4 × (BSA/1.70)^2.3; CL = 35.5 × (BSA/1.70)^0.83 ×
(CLCr/81)^0.36; CL1m = fm/Vm × CL = TV(CLm) × (dose m⁻²/25)^−0.48 × (PROT/70)^1.33, equivalently
fm/Vm = TV × (dose m⁻²/25)^−0.48 × (PROT/70)^1.33 × (BSA/1.7)^−0.83 × (CLCr/81)^−0.36.

Optimal sampling (p 761): unbound 0.5, 1, 3.3, 4.5 h; total 0.5, 1, 4, 24 h; both: 4 and 24 h.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No exposure–toxicity or exposure–response analysis; no ke0, EC50 or therapeutic range reported.

## Covariates
Table 2 (p 759 / PDF p 4; OFV decrease, ISV): total V1 — gender −11 (36%), BSA −24 (27%), BW −20 (30%); unbound
V1 — gender −16, BSA −22, BW −14; unbound CL — gender −12, BSA −17, BW −12, CLCr −15; fm/Vm — serum protein −11,
dose/m² −17 (inverse). BW dropped for BSA (r = 0.953); gender not significant once BSA was in. Concomitant
irofulven or 5-FU was tested; not retained.

## Caveats
- Internal inconsistency: the unbound-V1 BSA exponent is +1.60 in Table 4 (bootstrap +1.53) but 2.3 in the
  printed equation on p 760; 2.30 is the total-platinum exponent in Table 3. The table value is more likely
  correct for unbound V1, but the paper does not settle it.
- Dose range per infusion 15–80 mg (Methods) vs 20–80 mg (Table 1).
- Small n (43), Phase I combination regimens; MP2 (ELS), not NONMEM; platinum (all species) measured, not
  intact cisplatin.
- The authors' comparisons ("in the order of" / "in the range of" previous estimates, p 762) are against
  literature values rescaled by 1.7 m² or 64 kg, not against any model here.
