# Ward 2021: SCEPTER III sedation trial design in ventilated ICU adults

## Study
Consensus recommendations from a 2-day in-person meeting (Washington DC, 28-29 March 2019; 36 participants from academia, industry and FDA, plus two former ICU patients) followed by a three-round online modified Delphi (February-June 2020) (PDF p.2-3). Consensus: at least 70% rated an item Critical (7-9 on a 9-point scale) and at most 15% Not Important. Fifteen recommendations resulted. Shafer is a co-author.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No MEAC, ke0 or potency ratios. Recommended trial end points (Table 1, PDF p.4, printed 1687):
- Sedation level assessed at least every 4 h with a valid scale (RASS or SAS); the Ramsay scale is not recommended.
- Pain at baseline and at least every 4 h (numeric rating scale; CPOT or BPS if the patient cannot self-report).
- Delirium at least every 12 h (CAM-ICU or ICDSC).
- ICU and hospital mortality, length of stay, ventilation duration, 30-day mortality (possibly to 180 d).
- Enrollment as soon as possible, preferably within 24 h of starting sedation; record a severity score (APACHE, SOFA or SAPS); standardize and report rescue medication.
- Processed EEG may be added only if it does not displace validated measures (p.5).

None.

## Caveats
Authors note panel composition may bias the recommendations, and that they may not suit every ICU trial. Expert opinion, not data.
