# Vadiei 1996: single-dose IV amiodarone in normal and impaired LV function

## Study
Three parallel groups given a single **5 mg/kg IV amiodarone over 15 min** (peripheral line, volumetric pump):
normal LV function (n = 12 enrolled, 11 completed; EF assigned 67%, not measured), moderate CHF (EF > 30% to 45%,
n = 6), severe CHF (EF <= 30%, n = 6). NYHA II-III. Adults roughly 29-66 y (Table I scrambled). Serum sampled at 0,
15, 18, 23, 30, 45 min, 1, 2, 4, 8, 14, 24, 34 h, and days 2-76. HPLC-UV, LLOQ 5 ng/mL. Noncompartmental analysis
(SAS); ANOVA between groups. Wyeth-Ayerst sponsored (manufacturer).

Amiodarone parameters not estimable in 5 of 11 normals and 1 severe patient (too few terminal points). Table II gives
mean serum amiodarone by time (e.g., normal group 16546 ng/mL at 0.25 h, 1357 at 1 h, 169 at 24 h); Table IV gives DEA
(peaking about 40 ng/mL at 8-14 h in normals). Terminal phase reached 6-10 days after dosing. Prior literature
quoted: CL 90-158 mL/h/kg, Vss 40-100 L/kg.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No concentration-effect model or ke0. ECG: in severe CHF, QRS 109 ± 9.9 to 118 ± 7.7 ms on day 1 (5 of 6), QTc
417 ± 33 to 470 ± 47 ms on day 2; normals QTc 400 ± 22 to 421 ± 27 ms on day 1. No arrhythmias. Not an opioid.

## Covariates
Ejection fraction / CHF group: no significant effect on amiodarone PK; DEA half-life about 60% longer in severe
dysfunction; weak inverse correlation of DEA t½ with EF (r² = 0.26, P = 0.02).

## Caveats
Small groups; normals' EF assumed; incomplete profiles bias the normal group; DEA t½ shorter than parent's, which
authors attribute to estimation difficulty (formation-rate-limited, parallel decline). Sponsor-run.
