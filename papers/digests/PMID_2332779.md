# García-Rodríguez 1990: amoxicillin/clavulanate and metronidazole killing kinetics against B. fragilis group

## Study
In-vitro study, Hospital Clínico Universitario, Salamanca. Amoxicillin-resistant, beta-lactamase-producing
clinical isolates of the *Bacteroides fragilis* group: 13 *B. fragilis*, 4 *B. thetaiotaomicron*, 4 *B. vulgatus*,
3 *B. distasonis*, plus *B. fragilis* ATCC 25285 and *B. thetaiotaomicron* ATCC 29741 (26 strains; the summary
counts 14 and 5, including the type strains) (PDF p2, printed p11). Amoxicillin/clavulanate 2:1 and
metronidazole standard powders. MIC by broth macrodilution, anaerobic, 35 °C, 48 h; MBC = lowest
concentration giving 99.9% reduction of the original inoculum (incubation time for the MBC not stated);
time-kill (Krogstad & Moellering) on 6 strains; post-antibiotic effect (PAE, time to a 10-fold rise after drug
removal, exposure at the MIC) and IMAC (lowest concentration giving 90% reduction vs the initial inoculum after
5.5 h, Lorian) on 5 strains (PDF p3, printed p12).

B. distasonis 4/4. No strain resistant to either drug.

MBC: **no MBC table**. Summary (p2): MBC two- to four-fold the MIC for amoxicillin/clavulanate and one- to
two-fold for metronidazole "for most strains". Results (p3) state, for both drugs together, that every strain
was killed at no more than two- or four-fold the MIC, and that the MBCs of 16% of B. fragilis were above the
breakpoint (drug not named; the context suggests amoxicillin/clavulanate).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- Time-kill at the MIC (Fig 2, p4): metronidazole reduced the inoculum 10^3-10^5-fold within 4 h, faster than
  amoxicillin/clavulanate (100-fold at 2 h, 10^2-10^5-fold at 6 h, Fig 1).
- PAE: metronidazole 4-6 h, amoxicillin/clavulanate 2-4 h, both after exposure at the MIC.
- IMAC: metronidazole 1/4 MIC in all strains; amoxicillin/clavulanate 1/2 MIC in all but one (1/4).
- Not an opioid paper: no MEAC, ke0 or potency ratio.

## Covariates
Species within the B. fragilis group only.

## Caveats
- Small n (26 strains for MIC/MBC, 5-6 for kinetics); only beta-lactamase-producing, amoxicillin-resistant isolates.
- The MBC/MIC ratio is a summary statement, without a table or a per-strain distribution, so no MBC50/MBC90 can be read.
- The summary's MIC range "0.5 and 32 mg/l" does not match Table 1 (0.25-16).
- Broth, protein-free: free-drug values.

- **"MBC one to two times the MIC"**: matches the summary (PDF p2) for metronidazole, which qualifies it "for most

  discrepancy, since the repo does not attribute it here.
- MIC90 = 1 (Boiten 2024) is one dilution above this paper's 0.5 for B. fragilis (n=13, 1990 isolates);
  direction expected for a modern, larger series.
