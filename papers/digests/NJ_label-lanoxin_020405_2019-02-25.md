# LANOXIN (digoxin) tablets prescribing information, 2019 (s015)

## Study
A label, not a study. It is the 2019 revision the repo cites by its Reference ID, 4394702. The ID is printed in
every page footer. It replaces the 2024 file the fetcher first archived, which was carton and foil artwork with no
PI text.

## Model and parameters
```yaml
parameters:
  - {name: serum digoxin lower limit, value: 0.5, unit: ng/mL, source: "section 2.5 (PDF p5)", note: "below it, diminished efficacy"}
  - {name: serum digoxin upper limit, value: 2, unit: ng/mL, source: "section 2.5 (PDF p5)", note: "above it, increased toxicity without increased benefit"}
  - {name: sampling time, value: "just before the next dose, or at least 6 h after the last", unit: none, source: "section 2.5"}
```
Section 2.5 also says a trough taken 24 h after a dose runs 10-25% below a sample taken 8 h after it, with
once-daily dosing; with twice-daily dosing the timing matters little. The maintenance-dose tables
(Tables 3-5) give doses by lean weight and creatinine clearance. They print the daily body-store loss as
14 + CrCl/5 percent.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
The 0.5-2 ng/mL range is clinical association, not a fitted concentration-effect model. Toxicity is described as
more common above 2 ng/mL, though it can occur at lower levels. Peak cardiac effects come 3-6 h after a dose, in
the overdosage and toxicity text.

## Covariates
Renal function (creatinine clearance) and lean body weight, through the dosing tables. P-glycoprotein inducers and
inhibitors (7.1). Age, through renal function (8.5).

## Caveats
A label summary of older literature. The range is total serum digoxin. Digoxin-like immunoreactive substances and
spironolactone interfere with some immunoassays (laboratory-test interference text).
