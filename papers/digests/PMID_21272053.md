# Björnsson 2011: naproxen pain-intensity and dropout model after wisdom-tooth removal

## Study
- Randomized, double-blind dose-finding study (AstraZeneca; Eastman Dental Centre, London). Mandibular wisdom-tooth removal under local anaesthesia; patients needing pain relief within 6 h with VAS PI >= 40 mm were randomized.
- 242 patients, 48% male, age 19-38 y, BMI 18-31 kg/m2 (Table 1). Arms: naproxcinod 375/750/1500/2250 mg (n = 41/37/42/41), naproxen 500 mg (n = 39), placebo (n = 42). Hard gelatine capsules.
- Rescue: ibuprofen 400 mg on request (asked to wait 1.5 h). PI on 100 mm VAS at 0, 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2, 2.5, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8 h and before rescue.
- PK subgroup: venous plasma in 15/12/18/15/16 patients (naproxcinod 375-2250, naproxen) at the same times. Total naproxen by RP-HPLC fluorescence (LOQ 0.5 umol/L; accuracy over 1.5-400 umol/L); unbound by ultrafiltration at 1, 3, 8 h (LOQ 5 nmol/L; 12.5-3200 nmol/L). The text layer renders the micro sign as "m"; the assay ranges show the units are umol/L.
- NONMEM VI, Laplace. Sequential PK then PK/PD (PK parameters fixed, PK data retained).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: pain intensity on a 100 mm VAS after third-molar extraction. PI = PIbaseline·(1 − Placebo)·(1 − Drug); Drug = Emax·Cu^γ/(EC50^γ + Cu^γ) on **unbound plasma** concentration, **no effect compartment / no ke0** reported.
- EC50 0.135 umol/L unbound, γ 1.61, Emax fixed 1.
- Derived: total concentration at the unbound EC50 = 0.135 + 643·0.135/(0.549 + 0.135) = 127 umol/L = 29.3 mg/L (MW 230.26) (derived).
- Dropout: Weibull hazard, COV = θPI·(1 + θbaseline·(PIbaseline − 55))·PIt; hazard zero for first 1.5 h.
- Not an opioid; no MEAC; no potency ratio.

## Covariates
None on PK or PD other than baseline PI in the dropout hazard.

## Caveats
- PK measured in about one third of patients; sampling only to 8 h; single dose; acute pain.
- EC50 has 120% IIV; Emax at a bound.
- In the -layout text the IIV column is shifted one row (it would put 43% on EC50); the content-stream (-raw) order gives kpl 43%, EC50 120%, γ no IIV.
