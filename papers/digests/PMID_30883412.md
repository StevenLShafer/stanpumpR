# Polaner 2019: Falling Dominoes (editorial)

## Study
Editorial (PDF p1 = journal p613, p2 = p614) accompanying Egunsola et al's systematic review of gabapentin and pregabalin for paediatric pain (Anesth Analg 2019;128:811-819). It flags two fraudulent studies by Amin (Saudi J Anaesth 2014;8:317-322, retracted 2018; Anesth Essays Res 2011;5:167-170) found by that review. Evidence cited: identical reported values across the two papers, e.g. anaesthesia duration 32.75 ± 6.58 min, weight 16.1 ± 1.71 kg, age 4.8 ± 1.24 y. It discusses unexplained homogeneity as a fraud signal (Carlisle, Zintzaras/Ioannidis Monte Carlo methods), the persistence of citations to retracted work (e.g. 20 Reuben papers cited 274 times 2009-2014), a roughly 10-fold rise in fraudulent publication since 1975, and a call to check PubMed retraction notices and teach ethics.
