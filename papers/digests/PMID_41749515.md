# Kim 2026: population PK-PD of zolpidem (DSST, CRT, VAS) in healthy Koreans

## Study
Secondary population analysis of the Yoon 2021 trial (KCT0003934): open-label single oral 10 mg zolpidem dose
(formulation/salt not restated here; Yoon used zolpidem tartrate reference standard) to 30 healthy Korean adults,
15 M / 15 F. Table 1 (median, range): age 29.5 (20-44) y; weight 60.05 (50.0-83.0) kg (men 75.4, women 54.9);
albumin 4.4 (3.9-5.0) g/dL; creatinine 0.73 mg/dL. Plasma sampled 0-12 h (0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 1, 1.5, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8,
12 h): 325 PK observations. DSST, CRT and VAS sleepiness at baseline (Day -1) and 0.5-12 h: 388 observations each.
NONMEM 7.5.1, FOCE-I; PsN 5.4.0 (SCM, SIR); R 4.4.2. BLQ handled by M1 (ignored).

## Model and parameters
One compartment, first-order elimination, transit-compartment absorption (Ktr parameterized by MTT and NN, Savic
2007), then first-order Ka into central; relative bioavailability BIO fixed at 1 with IIV. First-order absorption
with lag gave IIV on Ka of 599.7% and was rejected. With BIO, IIV on Vc/F estimated zero (Discussion). No PK
covariate retained: sex entered forward selection (dOFV -5.137) and was removed in backward elimination.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
All three end points modelled as DIRECT sigmoid Emax functions of plasma concentration (no effect compartment).
Effect compartment tested for VAS: dOFV -2.524, not retained (Section 3.2.4). No ke0 / t1/2ke0 / tPeak reported.
Table 3 final model (footnote equations):
- DSST: DSST = Elearn x (1 - Edrug); IC50 205 ug/L (= ng/mL) at weight 60.05 kg and albumin 4.4 g/dL;
  IC50 = 205 x (WT/60.05)^-1.57 x (1 + 0.541 x (ALB - 4.4)); HILL 2.54; BASE 68.2 x (1 - 0.0143 x (Age - 29.5));
  MAXlearn 14.4 score; LRPAR 4.3 h; IIV IC50 22.3%, IIV BASE 13.1%; additive error 6 score.
- CRT: CRT = BASE x (1 + MAXdrug x Edrug); EC50 282 ug/L x (1 - 0.0193 x (WT - 60.05)) x (ALB/4.4)^3.89 x
  (CRTB1/438.5)^-1.28; HILL 6 x (ALB/4.4)^-5.26 x (1 + 0.00637 x (CRTB1 - 438.5)); BASE 437 ms x (1 + 0.0112 x
  (Age - 29.5)); MAXdrug fixed 5.6 (max/min observed CRT 2219/337 ms); IIV EC50 21.1%, BASE 9.4%; prop. error 9.42%.
- VAS: EC50 146 ug/L, HILL 1.92; baseline spline H1-H4 0.303, 0.362, 0.198, 0.127 (x100 mm); IIV EC50 53.9%;
  additive error 14.4 mm.
(Table 3's text layer is column-shifted; values above follow the footnote equations and the Results text.)
Rank of potency VAS > DSST > CRT. DSST and CRT return to baseline about 12 h after 10 mg (Fig. S14).
Not an opioid: no MEAC, no potency ratio. Note: at the CRT EC50 the reaction time is BASE x 3.8 (derived), i.e.
half of the maximum drug effect, not "50% impairment".

## Covariates
PK: none. PD: weight and albumin on DSST IC50; weight, albumin and baseline CRT on CRT EC50; albumin and baseline
CRT on CRT HILL; age on BASE of DSST and CRT. VAS: none.

## Caveats
30 young healthy Koreans, single dose, 12 h sampling; exploratory. Ka IIV 330.8% (very poorly identified).
PD sampling not fully aligned with PK. Weight effect on PD may absorb unexplained PK variability (authors).
No tolerance modelled (single dose).
