# Neckebroek 2020: remifentanil and BIS closed-loop propofol performance

## Study
- Prospective observational cohort (NCT00764855), 163 patients analysed (11 men, 152 women), ASA I-III, age 42 +/- 13 y, height 167 +/- 7 cm, weight 69 +/- 13 kg; elective gynaecological or head-and-neck surgery under TIVA, one anaesthesiologist. (PDF p2-3)
- Remifentanil started by effect-site TCI 3 min before propofol; initial CeREMI chosen by the clinician between 2 and 7.5 ng/mL, adjustable. Propofol by the Bayesian BIS closed-loop system (BIS target 40-50, as clinically required). Rocuronium 0.6 mg/kg after LOC. Escape bolus propofol 30 mg allowed; max CePROP 15 ug/mL; loop opens when BIS SQI < 50%.
- Propofol PK-PD for the controller: Schnider (refs 10, 11). Initial PD priors: E0 100, Emax 0, gamma 2.62, EC50 4.98 ug/mL, BIS delay 10 s, BIS sample fade-out 120 s, BIS sampled every 3 s (PDF p3, printed 1663).
- Analysis: Varvel PE criteria from LOC to end; Spearman correlation of each criterion with each patient's mean CeREMI; loess curves.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: BIS (propofol hypnosis), with CeREMI as an exposure covariate. No remifentanil ke0, t1/2ke0, tPeak, hysteresis, EC50, MEAC or analgesic concentration reported; no potency ratio. Audit: none for remifentanil as a PD parameter.
- Remifentanil effect-site concentration (a model-calculated TCI target, not an assay) during maintenance 4.9 +/- 1.4 ng/mL; propofol Ce 3.5 +/- 0.8 ug/mL; at end Ce propofol 2.7 +/- 1.0 ug/mL (Table 1, image-confirmed).
- Table 1 induction: BIS at LOC 67 +/- 16; TLOC 110 +/- 34 s; TBIS TARGET 215 +/- 79 s; BISpeak 31 +/- 7; TPEAK,BIS 259 +/- 85 s; TEQ 412 +/- 245 s. Maintenance: BIS 41 +/- 3 vs target 44 +/- 3; % time within +/-10 of target 82 (75-88), within +/-5 60 (52-68); case time 5066 +/- 2482 s.
- Table 2: PE -6.705 +/- 5.218; MDPE -6.634 +/- 5.492 %; MDAPE 11.243 +/- 5.461 %; divergence -0.001 +/- 0.004 %/min; wobble 9.746 +/- 3.988 %.
- Table 3 Spearman rho vs mean CeREMI: PE 0.237; MDAPE -0.234; divergence 0.168; MDPE 0.208; wobble -0.187 (all |rho| < 0.4, weak). Loess curves (Fig 3): stable across CeREMI, deteriorating below 2.8 ng/mL (MDPE about -15%, MDAPE about 18% at 2 ng/mL, per text, derived from figure by authors). Authors treat this as hypothesis-generating, few observations.
- Haemodynamics: HR 50-90 for 94% (84-98) of case time; MAP < 65 mm Hg for 7% (0-17); SBP > 140 for 0% (0-7).

## Covariates
CeREMI (mean per patient) only. No effect on propofol PK-PD estimated.

## Caveats
- Observational; CeREMI chosen by the clinician, so confounded with patient factors (authors). 152 of 163 are women; mostly ASA I-II; no power calculation.
- Text cites "Table 2" for haemodynamics and for the confidence intervals; those are in Table 1 and Table 3 (internal mislabels, numbers unaffected).
- The remifentanil model behind CeREMI is not stated (open question).
