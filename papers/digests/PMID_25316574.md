# Heuberger 2015: population PK of THC across oral, intravenous and pulmonary dosing

## Study
Pooled population PK analysis (CHDR Leiden; NONMEM 7.2, FOCE-I, ADVAN5; PsN bootstrap). Model-building
datasets (Table 1, PDF p3 / printed p211):

| Dataset | Route | THC dose (mg) | Subjects | Obs/subject | Sampling | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| CHDR 0828 | oral (Namisol tablet) | 5, 6.5 or 8, single | 13 (6 M, 7 F) | 18.7 | to 11 h | mean age 22.4, BMI 22; LOQ 0.1 ng/mL |
| CHDR 0826 | inhalation (Volcano vaporiser, nose clip) | 2, 6, 6 at 1.5 h | 22 (11 F) | 7.7 | to 2.97 h after last dose | LOQ 1 ng/mL |
| CHDR 0830 | inhalation (Volcano, nose clip) | 2, 4, 6 at 1.5 h | 40 M | 8.7 | to 0.95 h after last dose | LOQ 1 ng/mL |
| Ohlsson 1982 | IV 2-min infusion / smoked cigarette | 4.55 IV / ~9 smoked | 9 M (5 heavy, 4 light users) | 16.8 | 0.2-48 h | deuterium-labelled THC; LOQ 0.1 ng/mL |

All subjects healthy cannabis users (CHDR: no more than weekly). IV: 5 mg of 91% labelled THC, modelled as
4.55 mg (p7) and as a bolus into the central compartment; smoking modelled as a bolus with an apparent fraction;
smoked dose = cigarette content minus residue in the butt (p3). Assay HPLC-MS/MS (CHDR); plasma.
External validation (not used in fitting): CHDR 0508 (28 M, inhaled 2/4/6 mg), 0916 (24 M, inhaled 4 mg x5),
King's College IV 2.5 mg (n = 23) and 1.25 mg (n = 13) 5-min infusions (p4).

## Model and parameters
Three compartments plus an oral depot with lag time (Appendix, PDF p10 / printed p218): central V2, elimination
k20, fast peripheral (k24/k42), slow peripheral (k23/k32), first-order ka. IIV on k20, V2, ka; proportional
residual error. Final estimates, Table 2 (PDF p7 / printed p215), confirmed on the page image:

(shrinkage 4.74%). Text (p5): CL 38.8 L/h; terminal half-life 21.5 h.

(3.4 min), 1.08 h and 21.5 h (eigenvalues of the rate matrix); fast peripheral Q = 25.2 L/h, V = 24.9 L;

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None modelled. No ke0, EC50 or effect end point. Fig. 7 (p9) marks 5 ng/mL as an "intoxication level" and
0.5 ng/mL as a detection level in plasma, without source or derivation; the model predicts THC still present
7 days after one ~54 mg joint and above 0.5 ng/mL for about 2 days. 11-OH-THC said (Introduction, citing
others) to be at least as potent as THC; not modelled. Not an opioid paper.

## Covariates
None. Route indicators only; no weight, sex or user-status covariate (heavy vs mild users pooled; authors
think the effect negligible). Weights not reported (BMI only).

## Caveats
- Absolute disposition rests on the 9-subject Ohlsson IV arm; the terminal phase is seen only in Ohlsson data
  (to 48 h); the authors say the terminal half-life may still be underestimated (Ohlsson thought > 20 h, rabbit
  data 31-66 h).
- Oral and vaporiser arms were not crossed with IV, so their F values are apparent fractions, not absolute
  bioavailability (p5, Discussion).
- Large shrinkage on k20 (40.1%) and ka (62.7%) IIV.
- Lag-time CI (0.197-0.198 h) is implausibly narrow, suggesting the parameter sat near a fixed or boundary value.
- Clearance compared by the authors with an unpublished CHDR model (53.0 L/h) and earlier short-sampling models
  with terminal half-lives of 68-150 min: "lower" and "longer" are relative to those models.
- IV 2-min infusion and inhalation modelled as boluses.

`inst/extdata/drugDefaults_global.csv` at cb2f313d. So the checks are of the documents' claims.
- **CL 38.8 L/h, terminal t½ 21.5 h** (spec review :164; help :59, :61): paper p5 states both; Table 2 gives
  k20 x V2 = 38.7 L/h and a 21.5 h slowest exponential (derived). **matches.**
- **Vss 236 L** (spec review :179-180): derived from Table 2 = 236 L. **matches** (the repo derived it before
  the table was read, presumably from another source; it agrees).
- **"Three compartments, absolute, fitted across oral, IV and pulmonary"** (spec review :164; help :61): matches
  the model structure (Appendix, p10). Qualification the repo should carry: only the disposition is absolute; the
  oral F (0.0325) is an apparent fraction from subjects who never received IV THC, and the paper says it does
  not equal absolute bioavailability. **matches**, with that caveat.
- **"No new engine, no reduction"** (spec review :212-214): three compartments and a first-order oral input with
  lag; the engine already applies `tlag_PO` (`R/advanceClosedFormPO_IM_IN.R:20`). Plausible, not tested here.
  Units: Table 2 rate constants are per hour and V2 is in litres (absolute, not per kg); per-minute values are
  given above. The pulmonary F values cannot be used: the library has no pulmonary route, as the help page says.
- **"Parameter table behind a paywall... a copy would settle it"** (spec review :164, :212; help :61): now
  superseded by this card. Not a numerical discrepancy; whether to implement is the `[decision]` question.
- **Li & An comparison factors** (2.2, 6.8): Heuberger's side confirmed (38.8 L/h, 236 L); Li & An's side not
  checked here.
