# Jiao 2004: population CL/F of carbamazepine and its 10,11-epoxide (Chinese TDM)

## Study
Prospective routine TDM, 1999-2001, five neurology centres in Shanghai, Nanjing and Beijing. Index set 408
outpatients, 459 steady-state trough samples (245 men, 163 women); validation set 40 patients, 40 samples
(Table 1). Conventional carbamazepine tablets for at least 4 weeks at a fixed dose. Index set (mean ± SD,
range): dose 457 ± 195 mg/day (100-1200), 9.68 ± 5.74 mg/kg/day; carbamazepine 5.17 ± 1.85 mg/L; epoxide
0.85 ± 0.46 mg/L; weight 52.39 ± 18.09 kg (5-101); age 22.55 ± 14.89 y (1.3-85.1), so children are included.
Comedication (index): valproate <= 18 mg/kg/day 52, > 18 mg/kg/day 32, phenytoin 28, phenobarbital 16,
valproate + phenobarbital 11. HPLC for both analytes (calibration 0.4-16 and 0.2-8 mg/L). NONMEM V, FO, checked
with FOCE-I for carbamazepine; one compartment, first-order absorption, ka fixed 1.2 /h; epoxide ka set to the
parent's elimination rate; formation fraction fixed 0.65; parent and epoxide fitted sequentially.

## Model and parameters
Carbamazepine (Table 4, PDF p 6, printed 252; matches the abstract, PDF p 1):
CL/F (L/h) = 0.141 x Dose(mg/d)^0.406 x TBW(kg)^0.117 x 1.44^PHT x 1.26^PB x 1.23^(VPA > 18 mg/kg/d);
V/F 72.0 L. The abstract writes it 1.23^VPA x 1.44^PHT x 1.26^PB, the same values.
- **Eq. 3.1 (p 5) numbers the thetas in a different order** (theta4 VPA, theta5 PHT, theta6 PB) from Table 4
  (theta4 PHT, theta5 PB, theta6 VPA). The labelled Table 4 rows and the abstract agree with each other, so
  the values by drug are PHT 1.44, PB 1.26, VPA 1.23; the equation's numbering is the odd one out.
- The abstract says "PB = 1 for patients comedicated with phenytoin", a source typo for phenobarbital.
- Valproate at <= 18 mg/kg/day dropped out (Table 2, delta OFV 0). The 18 mg/kg/day cut-off was picked by
  scanning 10-30 mg/kg.

Epoxide (Table 5, p 6): CL/F (L/h) = 0.686 x TBW^0.440 x Dose^0.311 x 0.693^VPA (any valproate dose); V/F 175 L.
Phenytoin on the epoxide dropped out at backward elimination (Table 3, delta OFV 5.7). The Discussion (p 8)
gives the epoxide V/F as 150 L, but its own 3.34 L/kg at the mean 52.4 kg is 175 L (derived: 175/52.4 = 3.34),
so the 150 is a source error and 175 stands.

Check (derived): 0.141 x 457^0.406 x 52.4^0.117 = 2.69 L/h = 0.0514 L/h/kg, the paper's monotherapy value at
mean dose and weight (p 6).

IIV: carbamazepine CL 10.3% (omega2 0.0106), V 42.9%; epoxide CL additive 4.75 L/h, residual 29.6% (p 5).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. The paper quotes therapeutic windows of 4-12 mg/L (carbamazepine) and 0.8-3.2 mg/L (epoxide) from the
literature (p 1).

## Covariates
Dose and weight as power terms (age dropped for collinearity with weight); comedication as categorical
factors above. Valproate raises parent CL/F only above 18 mg/kg/day and lowers epoxide CL/F (x 0.693,
consistent with epoxide hydrolase inhibition). Phenytoin and phenobarbital had no significant effect on
epoxide clearance.

## Caveats
Sparse troughs, so V/F is poorly determined; ka and formation fraction fixed; F unknown (all parameters
apparent). Chinese population, children and adults pooled. Epoxide IIV and the FOCE run were unstable. Bias
towards underestimating high concentrations (p 5).
