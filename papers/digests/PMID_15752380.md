# Csajka 2005: ephedrine, norephedrine and caffeine population PK (oral)

## Study
Pooled reanalysis of two earlier UCSF single-dose crossover studies (Haller 2002, ref 13; Haller 2004, ref 14) in
24 healthy adults (Table 1, PDF p7): study 1, n = 8 (5 F / 3 M, 25-38 y, 52-88.9 kg), two capsules of Metabolift
(analysed: 17.3 mg ephedrine, 0.2 mg norephedrine, 5.3 mg pseudoephedrine, 0.42 mg norpseudoephedrine, 175 mg
caffeine); study 2, n = 16 (9 F / 7 M, 22-39 y, 58.6-91.5 kg, 144-188 cm), oral 25 mg ephedrine sulphate and/or
200 mg caffeine, alone and together. 6 women took oral contraceptives. **All dosing oral; no intravenous data.**

Sampling: plasma before and at 30 min, 1, 1.5, 2, 4, 6, 8, 11, 12, 14 and 18 h (as printed); urine 0-14 h (study 1)
and 0-24 h (study 2). 379 ephedrine, 352 norephedrine, 417 caffeine plasma concentrations, 40 urine collections.
Assay LC-MS/MS; limit 0.5 ng/mL ephedrine and norephedrine, 25 ng/mL caffeine; within-run CV 0.16-2.5%,
accuracy 97-105%.

Model: NONMEM, FOCE-INTERACTION, ADVAN6; caffeine fitted first, then ephedrine/norephedrine conditional on
individual caffeine predictions. Caffeine: one compartment, first-order absorption with lag. Ephedrine: absorption
compartment, one plasma compartment (a second distribution compartment gave ΔOBJ -1, PDF p5), renal elimination to
a urine compartment, and Michaelis-Menten conversion to norephedrine (ΔOBJ -124 against first-order); the abstract
calls the whole thing "four-compartment". Caffeine in the absorption compartment slows ephedrine absorption
(eq. 10b/10e, ΔOBJ -183).

Other results (PDF p5-6): plasma ephedrine 1.59-101.40 and norephedrine 0.51-8.18 (printed "mg l-1"; almost
certainly µg/L, cf. Fig. 4 axis in mcg/L); urinary ephedrine 7.35-22.2 mg. Renal clearance against urine pH:
CLR = 0.4723 (SE 0.045) − 0.0172 (SE 0.0069) × pH L/min, p = 0.013. Separate per-study fits: CLR 37.7 vs 22.7 L/h
and V 320 vs 217 L (the text says "herbal compared with pharmaceutical", direction as printed). Simplest caffeine
interaction model: ka_E 0.033 /min without caffeine, 0.020 /min with (40% lower). Caffeine t½ 5.3 h (Results;
5.4 h in the Discussion), 11.8 h with oral contraceptives (11.7 h in the Discussion); oral CL 5.0 L/h.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No haemodynamic or other effect data, no ke0, no therapeutic or toxic concentration range.

## Covariates
- Oral contraceptives on caffeine clearance: CL_C × (1 − θOC × OC), θOC 0.54 (Table 3).
- Formulation on ephedrine bioavailability (FE,pharm vs FE,herbal), relative F 0.78/0.59 = 1.32.
- Caffeine amount in the absorption compartment on ephedrine ka (eq. 10b).
- Urine pH on renal clearance (post hoc regression only, not in the model). Weight, age and sex not tested.

## Caveats
- Oral only: V and CL are apparent (÷F); absolute bioavailability not estimable. A single disposition compartment
  can reflect sparse early sampling after oral dosing rather than true one-compartment kinetics.
- "Higher bioavailability" of herbal ephedrine is relative to the pharmaceutical sulphate tablet in study 2, and
  the dose basis (salt vs base) is not stated (open question 1).
- Typographical problems: Vmax/VNE printed as 1.96^-4; keNE in 1/h (table) vs 1/min (text); ka printed "l min-1".
- 24 subjects, single doses, healthy, two pooled studies with different formulations and urine intervals.

- "one compartment with first-order absorption (a second did not improve the fit)" - **matches** (ΔOBJ -1, PDF p5;

- "plus a minor saturable conversion to norephedrine" - **matches** (eq. 6; Discussion calls the pathway minor).
- "Table 3 gives V/F 181 L and CL_RE/F_E 0.34 L/min" - **matches** Table 3 (rendered).
- "because the fitted CL_RE is the absolute renal clearance, CL_RE/F_E is the apparent total clearance CL/F" -
  **cannot verify**: the paper never says CL_RE is absolute, and labels the 0.34 itself CLRE/FE. The conclusion
  (0.34 L/min is an apparent clearance on the same basis as V/F) agrees with the paper's 6.1-h half-life
  (ln2 × 181/0.34 = 6.15 h, derived). Open question 2.
- "Taking F = 1 turns those into V 181 L and CL 0.34 L/min ... removed" - arithmetic matches; the reasoning (oral
  data cannot show a distribution phase) agrees with the paper's design. Nothing is implemented, so no numeric
  path to check.
- AGENTS.md:92 (not a context location): "Dividing again by F_E (V 232 L, CL 0.436 L/min)" = 181/0.78 and
  0.34/0.78 (FE,herbal), arithmetic matches; "doses are ephedrine sulfate (base factor 0.771)" matches the study-2
  product, but the paper does not say which basis its model used (open question 1).

Verdict: **partly-verified** - every number the registry quotes matches Table 3; one interpretive sentence goes
beyond the paper, and no stanpumpR model depends on it.
