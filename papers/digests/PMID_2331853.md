# Chin 1990: cephalosporin MICs against coagulase-negative staphylococci

## Study
In-vitro susceptibility study (a short "Note"). 80 clinically significant coagulase-negative staphylococcal
isolates, one per patient, from blood cultures, sternotomy wounds and hip-prosthesis infections at the
Presbyterian Hospital, New York, collected over 4 years and identified by API; 40 methicillin-susceptible and
40 methicillin-resistant (oxacillin MIC ≥4 µg/mL = resistant). The tables call them all *S. epidermidis*;
the title and Table 3 say coagulase-negative staphylococci. Agar dilution, Mueller-Hinton agar pH 7.4 with 3%
NaCl, inoculum 10^4 CFU by multipoint inoculator (NCCLS 1988), 35 °C, read at 24 and 48 h (p1).
Agents: cefazolin, cefamandole, cefuroxime, vancomycin, teicoplanin, daptomycin, ofloxacin, ciprofloxacin,
erythromycin (oxacillin for classification). **No MBC, time-kill or tolerance was measured.**

Table 1 cefazolin 24-h distribution (MS): 1 at 64, 2 at 16, 2 at 8, 5 at 4, 9 at 2, 7 at 1, 12 at 0.5, 2 at

stayed at 4; 9 of 80 isolates had vancomycin MIC 8 (p2).

1 g gives peak serum about 20 µg/mL, t½ 6 h (p1, p3).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point is MIC (no visible growth on agar) at 24 and 48 h; no MBC, no PK/PD index, no clinical outcome.
Not an opioid paper: no MEAC, ke0 or potency ratio.

## Covariates
Methicillin (oxacillin) resistance; incubation time (MICs roughly doubled from 24 to 48 h for MS isolates).

## Caveats
- Internal inconsistency on the headline number (cefazolin MSSE 24-h MIC90 8 vs 4); also vancomycin 48-h MS.
- Agar with 3% NaCl and a 10^4 CFU spot inoculum; single centre, 1980s isolates.
- Text p2 says cefazolin inhibited 90% of MR isolates at ≤8 µg/mL, contradicting Table 2's MIC90 of 64.
- The paper is about MICs only; tolerance or MBCs cannot be drawn from it.

## Verification against stanpumpR
- **MIC90 = 4 at 24 h, 32 at 48 h**: matches the abstract, the text on p1 and Table 3 for **methicillin-susceptible**
  isolates; Table 1 itself says 8 at 24 h (open question). The row is labelled for all coagulase-negative
  staphylococci but omits the methicillin-resistant half of the same study, whose MIC90 is 64 at both times,
  16-fold higher. **Discrepancy (scope of the value)**, though harmless to the plotted band: the row is
  "excluded" and `antibioticMbcBand()` (`R/antibioticMbc.R:411`) uses only principal and core rows, so neither
  the band nor the CSV depends on it. The paper's MR data strengthen, not weaken, the reason for exclusion.
- **"about a third ... tolerant to beta-lactams"**: **cannot verify** from this paper (no MBCs); attributable only
  to Watanakunakorn 1985.
- Units: µg/mL = mg/L, no conversion issue. The repo takes these MICs as given; no free/total question arises
  for an excluded row.
