# Charbit 2008: droperidol, ondansetron and their combination on QTcF

## Study
Prospective, double-blind, randomized, placebo-controlled 4-period crossover (ICH E14 principles). 16 healthy
volunteers (8 M, 8 F; 2 replaced after period 1), age 29 +/- 8 yr, height 171 +/- 9 cm, BMI 24 +/- 3.
Treatments: droperidol 1 mg IV as 10 s bolus; ondansetron 4 mg IV over 120 s; both; placebo. Washout median
5 (2-11) days. Plasma at 3, 5, 7, 9, 13, 23, 30, 45, 60, 120, 240, 420, 600 min; LC-ion-trap MS/MS,
LOQ 0.50 ng/mL. Noncompartmental PK (WinNonlin). ECG every minute for 15 min, then to 600 min; manual QT,
Fridericia (cube-root) correction; primary end point mean maximal placebo- and baseline-subtracted QTcF
(ddQTcF) in the first 30 min.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point QTcF (ECG). Mean maximal ddQTcF: droperidol 25 +/- 8 ms, ondansetron 17 +/- 10 ms, combination
28 +/- 10 ms; droperidol > ondansetron (P = 0.014); combination not different from droperidol (P = 0.33).
Bazett: 42 +/- 21, 24 +/- 12, 43 +/- 19 ms. Droperidol QTc prolongation significant from end of infusion to
20 min. Concentration-QTc: weak linear correlation for droperidol on post-distribution samples > 50 ng/mL
(r = 0.34, P = 0.005; Fig. 4), none for ondansetron; no slope reported, and not significant in multivariable
analysis. Cited hERG IC50: droperidol 32 nM (~12 ng/mL), ondansetron 808 nM. Peak droperidol ~400 nM, ~150 nM
at 10 min. No ke0. (Not an opioid paper.) Adverse: one subject had dyskinesia/agitation after 1 mg.

## Covariates
No sex or period effect on ddQTcF.

## Caveats
n = 16 healthy young adults, single low doses, different infusion rates for the two drugs. Concentration-
effect analysis is a correlation without a reported slope or model.
