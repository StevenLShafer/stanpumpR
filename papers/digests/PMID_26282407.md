# Valade 2015: emtricitabine blood and seminal plasma population PK

## Study
EVARIST ANRS EP 49 cohort, 6 Paris centres. HIV-1-infected men who have sex with men, on stable cART with FTC 200 mg once daily (all with tenofovir DF; 52% plus an NNRTI, 29% a boosted PI, 14% raltegravir), blood HIV RNA < 50 copies/mL for at least 6 months.
- n = 122 (blood plasma) and 117 (seminal plasma). 236 BP and 209 SP concentrations at steady state, from two visits (inclusion and 1 month later) at varied times after dose. One BP value below LLOQ set to LLOQ/2.
- Table 1 (median, range): age 43 y (27-63), body weight 73 kg (range not read reliably: the text layer scrambles Table 1's ranges), serum creatinine 78 umol/L, CLcr (Cockcroft-Gault) 113 mL/min.
- Assay: validated HPLC-MS/MS, LLOQ 0.00625 mg/L, range 0.00625-2.0 mg/L, blood plasma and seminal plasma (total concentration).
- Software Monolix 4.1.4 (SAEM + MCMC). BP model fitted first, parameters fixed, then SP model, then all estimated together.

## Model and parameters
BP: two-compartment, first-order absorption and elimination. SP: virtual effect compartment of negligible volume fed from the central compartment (rate k1e) with elimination ke1 (different input and output constants); a transit compartment did not improve fit. Equations: A(1) gut, A(2) central, A(3) peripheral, Ae the effect-compartment concentration; k12 = Q/Vc, k21 = Q/Vp, k10 = CL/Vc. ka was not estimable and fixed at 0.53 /h from the authors' earlier adult FTC model (ref 21).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No pharmacodynamic model. The "effect compartment" is a pharmacokinetic seminal-plasma compartment, not a drug effect; k1e/ke1 are distribution rates, not a ke0 (equilibration half-life of ke1 = ln2/0.113 = 6.1 h, derived). End point examined: detectable seminal HIV RNA (> 100 copies/mL), 16 of 116 men (13.8%) and 18 of 207 samples (8.7%) detectable; ROC AUC 0.503 (0.376-0.631) for SP AUC0-24 and 0.547 (0.406-0.688) for the ratio; mixed logistic P = 0.943 and 0.893: no association. The authors cite a BP AUC0-24 > 10 mg·h/L as the exposure tied to maximal anti-HIV activity (ref 23); 91% of patients exceeded it. No MEAC, EC50 or Emax. Not an opioid.

## Covariates
CL/F = 14.8 x (CLcr/113)^0.178, CLcr in mL/min (Cockcroft-Gault). Age, weight, creatinine and co-medications did not improve the BP model beyond CLcr; none explained variability on k1e.

## Caveats
- Apparent parameters; F unknown. Sparse sampling, two visits per man; ka fixed. Vp/F and Q/F poorly determined (RSE 44% and 26%).
- Median SP/BP ratio 2.91 versus 4.5 in a single-dose study of 8 men (AUC from 24 h to 14 d), a different interval.
- Seminal plasma only; intracellular phosphorylated FTC not measured.
- Text layer of Table 2 swaps Vc/F and Vp/F ordering relative to the image; the image values are used here.
