# Chandasana 2024: dolutegravir and lamivudine once-daily FDC popPK (TANGO)

## Study
- TANGO (NCT03446573), phase 3 open-label noninferiority switch study in virologically suppressed HIV-1 adults; this analysis uses the dolutegravir/lamivudine FDC arm only (50 mg / 300 mg once daily, oral). Page 3.
- 362 participants, 2,629 dolutegravir and 2,611 lamivudine plasma samples over 48 weeks (the simulations use 361). Table 1 (p. 5), median [range]: age 40 [20-74] y, weight 78.8 [50.2-153.0] kg, BMI 25.3, serum creatinine 0.96 mg/dL, eGFR 99.0 [44-147] mL/min/1.73 m2, CrCL 113.9 mL/min; 93.1% male, 80.4% White, 13.5% Black, 19.3% Hispanic/Latino.
- Sampling: intensive subset n=30 at week 4, fasted (pre-dose, 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2, 3, 4, 6, 10, 24 h); sparse samples in most others at weeks 4, 8, 12 (fed state not controlled), 24, 36, 48 (pre-dose). Page 4.
- Assay: protein precipitation, UHPLC-MS/MS, total plasma; LLOQ 20 ng/mL dolutegravir, 2.5 ng/mL lamivudine. BLQ 0.19% and 0.11%, excluded.
- NONMEM 7.3, FOCE-I; covariates by forward (p<0.01) and backward (p<0.001) selection; pcVPC, bootstrap, NPDE.

## Model and parameters
Dolutegravir: one-compartment, first-order absorption and elimination, no lag, proportional error, IIV on CL/F and on the error magnitude. Lamivudine: two-compartment, first-order absorption, V3/F fixed equal to V2/F (the full-data fit could not estimate V3/F; the alternative of fixing 59.8 L from the intensive subset gave an OFV 21.8 points worse, p. 7-8); allometric weight on CL/F and Q/F (exponent 0.75, fixed) and on V2/F (exponent 1.0, fixed). All volumes and clearances are apparent (per F); F is not estimated separately. Absolute units (L, L/h), not per kg. Table 2, PDF p. 6 (typical values at the reference subject).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No pharmacodynamic model, no concentration-effect relationship, no ke0 / tPeak / hysteresis, and no MEAC or therapeutic range. Not an opioid paper (opioid audit: none). The authors call AUC0-tau the best predictor of lamivudine antiviral activity (p. 8) without giving a target.

## Covariates
Dolutegravir CL/F: weight (power 0.427, centred 79 kg), bilirubin (power -0.153, centred 8 umol/L), Hispanic/Latino ethnicity (x0.844). V/F: weight (power 0.917). Over observed ranges (p. 7): weight 50-153 kg gives CL/F 18% lower to 33% higher and V/F 34% lower to 83% higher than 79 kg; bilirubin 2-34 umol/L gives CL/F 24% higher to 20% lower. Lamivudine CL/F: eGFR (power 0.533, centred 99), Black/African American race (x0.789), weight allometric 0.75 (fixed); eGFR 44-147 gives CL/F 35% lower to 24% higher; weight 50-153 kg gives CL/F and Q/F 22% lower to 80% higher, V2/F 29% lower to 119% higher. Age, sex, smoking, albumin, CDC class, concomitant CYP/UGT inhibitors and metal cations, food: not significant. Simulated covariate effects on exposure were below 20% in forest plots (Fig 3, p. 9), so the authors recommend no dose adjustment.

## Caveats
- 93% male, 80% White, treatment-experienced and virologically suppressed; one study; covariate categories are confounded by correlations within the dataset (authors, p. 10).
- Absorption phase of dolutegravir not captured well in the median (p. 7); lamivudine trough overpredicted because about 2% of "pre-dose" samples have high concentrations (p. 8).
- Lamivudine V3/F was fixed at V2/F because it was not estimable.
- Table 2 gives %RSE of the variance terms but the IIV column in %CV; the ethnicity and race entries are multiplicative factors, the bilirubin, weight and eGFR entries are exponents.
- Dolutegravir post hoc Cmax (5.08) exceeds treatment-naive historical values (3.67), noted by the authors as not clinically significant.
- Sanity check (derived): 50 mg / 0.858 L/h = 58.3 ug*h/mL, consistent with the reported AUC 59.2; 300 mg / 19.6 L/h = 15.3 against the reported 14.1 (F and the post hoc geometric mean make them differ).
