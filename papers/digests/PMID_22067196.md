# Välitalo 2012: plasma and CSF naproxen pharmacokinetics in children

## Study
- 53 generally healthy children (ASA 1-2), 3 months to 12 years, lower-body surgery under spinal anaesthesia, Kuopio. Boys n = 39: age 5.4 y (0.25-12), weight 20 kg (5-49); girls n = 14: 6.1 y (0.42-12), 24 kg (8.3-69) (Table I, median (range)).
- Single oral naproxen suspension 10 mg/kg (Pronaxen 25 mg/mL) preoperatively; midazolam-ketamine premedication, intrathecal levobupivacaine.
- 270 plasma samples 13 min to 51 h; 52 unbound (ultrafiltration) and 52 CSF samples (one each, 41 min to 20 h). GC-MS; plasma linear 0.125-20 mg/L (diluted as needed).
- NONMEM VI, FOCE-I; 250-sample bootstrap; VPC. One outlier (flat profile, peak 4 mg/L) excluded from the final fit; estimates with it also given.

## Model and parameters
Two compartments, first-order absorption, plus a CSF compartment (QCSF scaled by fu and an uptake factor). Final "empirical" model: CL/F and volumes linear in WT/70; Q/F not weight-scaled (fixed exponent 1 worsened OFV by 13; estimated exponent −0.16, RSE 240%).

- Vss/F 12.5 L/70 kg (abstract). Typical 20 kg child: Tmax 2.3 h (95% PI 0.7-6.0), Cmax 64 mg/L (26-148) (text).
- Mechanistic alternative: allometric CL/F and Q/F (exponent 0.75), volumes linear, plus maturation of CL/F (TM50 24.5 months postmenstrual, 33% RSE). In this model **CL/F was 0.72 L/h** (per 70 kg) and Vdss 11.7 L; OFV 0.8 lower with one more parameter. The authors preferred the empirical model because the TM50 was high and imprecise.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- No ke0, EC50 or concentration-effect model.
- Plasma naproxen at onset of wound pain after inguinal surgery (n = 21): median 51 mg/L (range 13-77; IQR 32-63); called preliminary.
- CSF: about 4-fold the unbound plasma concentration; model CSF Tmax 8 h, Cmax 430 ug/L in a 5-year-old, 20 kg child.
- Not an opioid; no MEAC; no potency ratio.

## Covariates
Weight (linear on CL/F, V1/F, V2/F). Age on CL tested; only the mechanistic model retained maturation.

## Caveats
Children only (none under 3 months; few infants); suspension may not have been evenly mixed (outlier); sparse sampling; apparent (/F) parameters only; analgesic concentrations preliminary.
