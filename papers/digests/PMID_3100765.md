# Scott 1987: fentanyl and alfentanil kinetics and EEG dynamics across age

## Study
Stanford. 37 unmedicated men, ASA I-II, elective surgery, no significant obesity or organ disease: fentanyl n = 20
(20-88 yr), alfentanil n = 17 (20-89 yr). Glycopyrrolate 0.2 mg, pancuronium 1 mg; O2 by mask. Infusion fentanyl
150 µg/min or alfentanil 1500 µg/min until delta waves were well established in the raw EEG; succinylcholine 1 mg/min
from loss of response. Then thiopental, N2O/enflurane surgery. Arterial samples every 0.5-1 min during infusion,
2-4 min after, then every 1-2 h to 24 h (fentanyl) or 12 h (alfentanil); about 40 per patient. Serum RIA (fentanyl
LOQ 0.25 ng/mL, alfentanil 1.0 ng/mL). Individual bi-/triexponential fits by extended least squares (ELSFIT);
triexponential preferred in all. EEG spectral edge (95%) with an effect compartment and inhibitory sigmoid Emax
model, fitted per patient with that patient's PK fixed.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Opioid audit (end point throughout: EEG spectral edge frequency, arterial serum, total concentration, RIA; adult men):
- t½ke0 (effect-compartment equilibration half-time): fentanyl mean 4.7 min (SD 1.5, Table 1, n = 16);
  alfentanil 0.9 min (SD 0.3, Table 2, n = 16); significantly different (P < .001). Not age-related.
  ke0 (derived: 0.693/t½ke0) ≈ 0.147/min fentanyl, 0.77/min alfentanil. No tPeak reported.
- IC50 (steady-state serum concentration for half-maximal spectral edge decrease): fentanyl 7.8 ng/mL (SD 2.6);
  alfentanil 479 ng/mL (SD 271; discussion p. 165 says 497) (Tables 1-2). Both fall with age (about 50% from 20 to 85):
  fentanyl IC50 = −0.0675·age + 11.4 (r −0.52); alfentanil IC50 = −7.69·age + 886 (r −0.66) (Fig. 6 legend).
- E0 18.9 / 19.2 Hz; Emax decrease 13.0 / 13.5 Hz; gamma 4.3 / 4.8 (fentanyl / alfentanil).
- EEG dose (dose to first delta waves): fentanyl 771 ± 414 µg (text; Table 1 mean 717 harmonic), alfentanil
  3898 ± 2280 µg; vs age fentanyl −14.76·age + 1560, alfentanil −65.7·age + 7381 µg (Fig. 5).
- Potency ratios: alfentanil:fentanyl EEG dose ratio 5.44:1; steady-state serum (IC50) potency about 61:1 (p. 163).
- No MEAC measured. Discussion quotes literature (not measured here): fentanyl analgesia for mild-moderate pain
  2-4 ng/mL, respiratory depression 1.5-5 ng/mL, with N2O 5-10 ng/mL adequate anaesthesia; alfentanil 108 ng/mL
  postoperative analgesia with ventilatory depression (O'Connor 1983), 200-500 ng/mL with N2O for abdominal surgery.

## Covariates
Age: no significant change in V1, Vdss or CL for either drug; fentanyl fast intercompartmental clearance fell with
age (r −0.62); alfentanil terminal half-life rose with age (r 0.50, P < .05). Pharmacodynamic sensitivity (IC50)
increased with age. No PK parameter correlated with weight.

## Caveats
Men only; individual two-stage estimates; anaesthesia and surgery (enflurane) during the sampling may have lowered
clearance (authors); fentanyl CL lower and t½ longer than earlier studies; alfentanil CL less than half of Bovill
and Camu. IC50 for alfentanil is quoted as both 479 and 497 ng/mL.
