# Domino 1984: racemic ketamine kinetics with and without diazepam

## Study
- Double-blind randomised crossover; 8 healthy men aged 26-41 (mean 32.3) y, 59.9-81.8 (mean 72.8) kg, prison volunteers; fasted. Subject 4 did not complete, so paired data n = 7.
- Diazepam 0.3 mg/kg IV or saline 10 min before racemic ketamine 2.2 mg/kg IV over 1 min; sessions 5-24 days apart.
- Arterial (radial) and venous samples at 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 10, 20, 30 min, 1, 2, 4, 5, 6, 8, 12, 24 h; GC-mass fragmentography for ketamine, KI (norketamine) and KII (decomposition product).
- Three-exponential fits per subject (NONLIN, weighted 1/C^2, F test); micro-constants and volumes from coefficients/exponents (Wagner); values are means of individual parameters.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No ke0. Plasma concentration at which subjects first followed commands: 1080 ± 40 ng/mL (placebo) and 1070 ± 120 ng/mL (diazepam) (Table VI/text). Time to follow commands 15.0 ± 1.6 min (placebo) vs 21.7 ± 3.2 min (diazepam). Duration of anaesthesia tracked the predicted second compartment more than plasma; authors note a separate effect compartment would be preferable. Mean ketamine plasma clearance was lower after diazepam (848 vs 719 mL/kg/h, paired n = 7): significant only by a one-tailed paired t test (P < 0.05), a trend by the two-tailed test (P < 0.08) (Table IV footnote, PDF p3, printed p647, checked on the rendered page; the Results text, PDF p6, printed p650, calls it a decrease citing only the one-tailed P). No differences in the rate constants. Diazepam was pretreatment only (no diazepam PK here). Diazepam raised early ketamine levels; 24 h urine (ketamine, KI, KII) ~20% of dose.

## Covariates
None modelled; parameters per kg total body weight.

## Caveats
- n = 7-8 young healthy men; single 2.2 mg/kg bolus; per-subject curve stripping; large SDs (k10 CV ~90%).
- Mean micro-constants are not a coherent model (see derived checks).
- 1 min sample near end of the 1 min injection dominates V1 and k10.

- Resulting model: CL = 4.41 L x 0.438 = 1.93 L/min at 70 kg (help ketamine.md:3 says "about 1.9 L/min"). The paper's measured plasma clearance is 0.848 L/kg/h = 0.99 L/min at 70 kg, and the raw Table I data support ~0.8 L/kg/h. **discrepancy**: the model built from mean micro-constants has about twice the paper's clearance, V3 1.7x (2.55 vs 1.51 L/kg) and Vss 1.55x (2.77 vs 1.78 L/kg). The help's "high clearance ... about 1.9 L/min" is therefore an artefact of averaging, not Domino's estimate. Terminal half-life 1.91 h vs paper 2.17 h (harmonic) / 2.25 h (mean). Early (minutes) behaviour is roughly preserved (half-lives 24 s, 4.6 min); infusion-scale and late concentrations will be underpredicted roughly two-fold.
- "volunteers", "racemic", "per kilogram of total body weight": **matches**. "young and healthy": men only, 26-41 y. **matches**.
