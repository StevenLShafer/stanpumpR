# Comets 2012: fluindione PK/PD in octogenarian inpatients (PREPA)

## Study
PREPA: prospective, observational, multicentre study (Sept 2005 to Sept 2007), consecutive inpatients aged >=80 y
starting fluindione in six medical and one cardiac-surgical acute-care units of three French hospitals (PDF p. 6,
printed 784). 151 recruited, 131 with PK/PD data (p. 2, printed 778). Table 1 (p. 2): age median 85 y (80-98);
weight 65 kg (32-112; missing 14); creatinine clearance 68 mL/min (11-179); 87 women (66%), 44 men; 52 (40%) after
cardiac surgery; indications AF 84, VTE 27, valve prosthesis 43. On average eight co-medications on day 1.

Dosing by routine practice (no algorithm), evening (~6 PM). Initial doses 5, 10 and 15-20 mg in 28, 94 and 10
patients; median stay 8 days (2-31). Target INR 2-3 for all; only 52 (40%) left the study in range. Ten (8%) bled,
nine of them after heparin. Samples: morning INR before the first dose and after 2, 4, 6 and 8 doses, then twice
weekly, with an extra 5 mL for fluindione concentration each time. 493 concentrations, 477 INR values. Assay: HPLC
(ref. 48, plasma micromethod; details in the online supplement, not read). Model: nonlinear mixed effects, SAEM in
MONOLIX 3.2; R 2.11 for the rest.

## Model and parameters
One compartment, first-order absorption, no lag (p. 2-3). ka was **fixed** at 2.42 /h from a healthy-volunteer
study (ref. 21: Tmax 2.0 h, t1/2 35 h); the text writes "2.42 h", Table 2 gives the unit h^-1. IIV on CL and V only,
no covariance. All V and CL are apparent (oral, F unknown), as the authors say (p. 6).

PD: indirect response on coagulation activity F = 1/INR, inhibition of production (Eq. 4, p. 7):
dF/dt = Rsyn·(1 − Imax·C^γ/(C^γ + IC50^γ)) − kout·F, parameterised by kout and I0 = INR0 (Rsyn = F0·kout).
Proportional residual error for INR; combined error for PK.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: INR (as 1/INR), indirect-response inhibition of production. IC50 2.18 mg/L total plasma fluindione,
Imax 0.94, γ 1.71, kout 0.03 /h (Table 2). No effect compartment or ke0; precursor-pool models were tried for the
delay and did not improve the fit (p. 3). Not an opioid; no MEAC or potency ratio.

Steady-state relations (p. 7): Css = D/(24·CL); INRss = I0 / (1 − Imax / (1 + (24·CL·IC50/D)^γ)). Doses of 7.5 and
10 mg keep a typical 65-kg, 85-year-old patient in range (p. 4). Table 3 (PDF p. 7, image): dose giving INRss closest
to 2.5, with % of 1000 Monte Carlo runs choosing it: 80-y man 60 kg 7.5 mg (17%); 80-y woman 60 kg 7.5 (17%);
80-y man 50 kg 7.5 (16%); 80-y man 70 kg 10 (17%); 90-y man 60 kg 5 (22%); after cardiac surgery 5 (24%); with
cordarone 5 (22%); with deroxat 5 (19%).

## Covariates
Eq. 7 (p. 3): V_i = V·(Weight/60)^0.57·Men^0.24; CL_i = CL·(Age/90)^−3.26·Cordarone^−0.18; IC50_i = IC50·Surgery^−0.54;
γ_i = γ·Surgery^−0.46·Deroxat^0.88, each with exp(η). The indicators are 0/1, so the printed power form must mean
exp(β·indicator); the text's percentages fit that reading (derived: exp(0.24) = 1.27, "men ~25% higher V";
exp(−0.18) = 0.84, "20% decrease" in CL with cordarone; exp(−0.54) = 0.58, "50% reduction" in IC50 after surgery).
Age: a 90-year-old has ~30% lower CL than an 80-year-old (p. 3) (derived: (80/90)^3.26 = 0.68). Weight: +10 kg gives
~9% more V. Cordarone (amiodarone) counted active for 1 month, deroxat (paroxetine) for 15 days. CYP2C9 and VKORC1
genotypes (about one-third genotyped) showed no relationship.

**Comparators**: the age effect is within this cohort (all >=80 y, centred on 90); it says nothing below 80. The
"increased exposure" is against the authors' earlier ADAP study in younger patients (mean age 60, range 29-89):
there V 37 L and CL 0.49 L/h vs ~8 L and 0.10 L/h here, with similar half-lives (52 h vs 56 h); IC50 2.18 here vs
1.35 mg/L in ADAP (~60% higher); kout 0.03 vs 0.18 (p. 6-7; unit printed "h", presumably h^-1).

## Caveats
- Sparse, routine-care data; high shrinkage (V 44%, CL 32%, PD parameters 47-86%); short stays, few at steady state.
- ka fixed from a volunteer study; apparent parameters only, F unknown. The authors cannot tell a fourfold rise in F
  from a fall in CL as the cause of the higher exposure versus ADAP (p. 6).
- Covariate equation printed with 0/1 indicators raised to a power (see above).
- Albumin, CRP and genotypes were missing in more than half of patients and were excluded from covariate testing.
- Supplementary tables (S1-S3, imputation ranges, assay details) not read.
