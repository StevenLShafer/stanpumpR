# Dyck 1993a: Dexmedetomidine IV and IM pharmacokinetics and hemodynamics

(Context file had no PubMed metadata; identity and citation read from the page images. DOI not printed on the PDF; the one above is inferred from the journal pattern and should be checked.)

## Study
Stanford IRB; crossover. 10 healthy men, mean age 35.5 y (29-44), weight 79 kg (60-98). Dexmedetomidine HCl 2 ug/kg IV over 5 min by pump; >= 2 weeks later the same dose IM into the deltoid over 30 s. 500 mL saline preload then 125 mL/h. Arterial sampling to 4 h (IV: 0.5-240 min, 22 times; IM: 2-240 min), venous 180-1440 min. Assay GC/MS (negative-ion CI), LLQ 50 pg/mL, CV 7.5-17.1%. Moment analysis (trapezoid linear-up/log-down); IM absorption by deconvolution against each subject's IV unit disposition function (constrained positive, unimodal). Arterial waveform at 128 Hz; median MAP and HR per 60 s.

IM absorption biphasic (early rapid, then slow; Figs 4-5). Suggested maintenance for 1.0 ng/mL: 0.511 ug/min; loading 194 ug over 30-45 min, not as a bolus (Discussion).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Hemodynamics only (no sedation score, no ke0). IV: MAP +22% at 5 min, HR -27% at 4-5 min; by 4 h MAP -20%, HR -10% (text p. 818; abstract says HR 5% below baseline). IM: no acute rise; MAP -20%, HR -5% by 4 h (abstract reverses the HR percentages). Two subjects had vasovagal loss of consciousness on standing ~5 h after IV. Authors suggest keeping plasma concentration below 1.0 ng/mL. Not an opioid paper.

## Covariates
None.

## Caveats
Ten men only; venous samples after 4 h read higher than arterial; possible nonlinearity from concentration-dependent hemodynamics. Printed bioavailability formula is inverted (AUC_IV/AUC_IM x 100; the results are clearly AUC_IM/AUC_IV). HR percentages after IV vs IM are inconsistent between abstract and text. Funded partly by Orion-Farmos.
