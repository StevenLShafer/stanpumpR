# Patel 2022: imipenem and relebactam PopPK in HABP/VABP

## Study
Update of the Bhagunde 2019 population PK models for imipenem (IPM) and relebactam (REL), adding RESTORE-IMI 2 (PN014, HABP/VABP, n = 261; IMI/REL 500/500/250 mg every 6 h, 30 min infusion) and PN017 (n = 81, Japanese cIAI/cUTI) to 855 participants from 10 earlier studies (Table S1). Final data set 1197 participants, 6100 IPM and 6531 REL quantifiable plasma concentrations (p. 400). Median age 55 y (18-96), weight 75 kg (27-180), CrCl 106 mL/min (8-452) (Table 2, p. 401). Infection: healthy, cIAI, cUTI and pneumonia (261: nonventilated HABP 139, ventilated HABP 30, VABP 92); the per-group counts of the first three are scrambled in the text layer and were not re-read. Software NONMEM, covariate forward p<0.01 (OFV drop 6.63), backward p<0.001 (10.83); bootstrap n = 1000. Simulations: joint PTA (IPM 30% fT>MIC; REL fAUC0-24/MIC >= 8 at fixed REL 4 ug/mL) at MIC <= 2 ug/mL, plus an exposure safety simulation.

## Model and parameters
Two-compartment, zero-order IV infusion, first-order elimination, for each drug (p. 399). Proportional residual error. BSV on CL, V1, V2 (CL~V1 correlated). Footnote g: the ventilation effect applies to pneumonia patients receiving ventilation, reference = pneumonia without ventilation. Units: weight in kg, CrCl in mL/min (Cockcroft-Gault), volumes L, clearance L/h, absolute (not per kg).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No effect-site model; antibacterial PK/PD targets only: IPM >= 30% (sensitivity 40%) of the dosing interval with free concentration above MIC; REL free AUC0-24/MIC >= 8 at fixed REL 4 ug/mL (p. 399). Joint PTA > 99% (30% fT>MIC) in all renal groups at MIC <= 2 ug/mL; > 98% at 40% (p. 402). Safety thresholds: IPM AUC0-24 3229.8 uM*h, Cmax 625.1 uM; REL AUC0-24 2941.0 uM*h, Cmax 367.9 uM; 12.2% of ESRD patients exceeded the REL AUC (p. 399, 403). Not an opioid; no MEAC, ke0 or potency ratio.

## Covariates
CrCl and body weight on CL and V1 (a priori), infection type (pneumonia vs pooled healthy/cIAI/cUTI) on CL and V1, ventilation on V1 only; race and age/sex not retained. Fold change in AUC0-24 vs normal renal function (CrCl 90-150): IPM 1.23 / 1.59 / 2.18 and REL 1.39 / 2.05 / 3.35 for mild / moderate / severe impairment. Healthy participants had AUC0-24 0.62x (IPM) and 0.57x (REL) that of pneumonia patients, after adjusting for weight and CrCl (p. 401). No time-varying CrCl.

## Caveats
Covariates fixed at baseline; ESRD effect from n = 5 of 1194 (and MODIFY data for simulation); BLOQ values excluded (mostly phase I); the authors note over-large BSV estimated for healthy participants; cIAI/cUTI could not be estimated separately from healthy participants (imprecise), so they were pooled as the reference; CL~V1 BSV correlation 0.97. "Higher exposure in pneumonia" is relative to healthy participants and cIAI/cUTI patients in the same model, with weight and CrCl adjusted.
