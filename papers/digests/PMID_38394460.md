# Boiten 2024: Dutch national susceptibility survey of Bacteroides, Phocaeicola, Parabacteroides, Prevotella

## Study
Prospective surveillance, February-July 2021: seven university hospitals and one regional laboratory in the
Netherlands consecutively collected 20-25 Bacteroides/Phocaeicola/Parabacteroides and 10-15 Prevotella clinical
isolates each (max 3 months). 298 received, 280 included (184 Bacteroidales: B. fragilis 62, B. thetaiotaomicron/
faecis 31, B. ovatus/xylanisolvens 25, Bacteroides spp. 22, Phocaeicola 26, Parabacteroides 19; Prevotella 96).
Sources: abdominal 43%, head/neck 11%, blood culture 11% (Table 1). MICs by the reference agar dilution method
(Brucella/FAA-HB agar, 0.008-256 mg/L two-fold dilutions, 48 h anaerobic, CLSI reading; QC B. fragilis ATCC 25285,
B. thetaiotaomicron ATCC 29741); clindamycin by ETEST because of QC failure. MIC50/MIC90 per species with >= 10
isolates. Isolates with metronidazole MIC > 4 mg/L, high carbapenem MIC, or cfiA underwent WGS. Resistance
percentages by EUCAST v11.0, v13.1 and CLSI 2023 are in Table S1 (supplement, not in this PDF).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
In vitro MIC only (agar dilution). No PK/PD target. Breakpoint versions named (EUCAST v11.0/v13.1, CLSI 2023) but
values are in the supplement.

## Covariates
Not applicable.

## Caveats
Single country, single 2021 collection window; MIC50/MIC90 only for groups with >= 10 isolates; percent resistance
in a supplement not read here. The paper does not define a "wild-type" (ECOFF) distribution.
