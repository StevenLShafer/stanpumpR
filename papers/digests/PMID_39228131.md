# Ding 2024: amodiaquine and piperaquine PopPK in African pregnant women

## Study
- PREGACT sub-study (NCT00852423), non-inferiority, open-label, randomised multicentre trial, Burkina Faso, Ghana, Malawi, Zambia, 2010-2013 (PDF p3).
- Second/third-trimester pregnant women with P. falciparum mono-infection. Artesunate-amodiaquine 200/540 mg daily (as base 270 mg amodiaquine per tablet pair, 2 tablets/day; n=771 in the AQ analysis) or dihydroartemisinin-piperaquine 40/960 mg daily (3 tablets, 171 mg PQ base each, n=755), for 3 days, oral, directly observed. Vomiters excluded (13 and 8).
- Sparse sampling: 2 mL venous plasma on day 7 plus other visits when possible; mostly after the last dose, little absorption-phase data. 1071 AQ, 1087 DEAQ, 976 PQ concentrations modelled (982 PQ collected, 6 below LLOQ). 848 of 1071 AQ (79.2%) were below LLOQ; those were omitted (M1 method).
- Assay: LC-MS/MS of plasma (total drug). NONMEM 7.4, FOCE-I, log-transformed data.
- Demographics (Table 1, PDF p5, median [range]): AQ group age 22 y (15-43), weight 55 kg (37-104), GA 24 wk (13-36), daily dose 12.8 mg/kg; PQ group age 20 y (15-43), weight 54 kg (35-115), GA 24 wk (16-36), daily dose 17.8 mg/kg.

## Model and parameters
- AQ: one-compartment, first-order elimination, 2 transit compartments, ka, MTT fixed to literature. DEAQ: two-compartment, complete bioconversion from AQ assumed. PQ: three-compartment, 2 transit compartments, MTT fixed, dose-occasion effect on F fixed at +23.7% per dose.
- Allometry fixed a priori on all CL (exponent 0.75) and V (exponent 1); typical values are for a 70 kg woman with acute malaria (Table 2/3 footnote). Population-mean Tables 2 and 3 are apparent (per F). F fixed to 100% with IIV on F.
- Residual error: additive on log scale (RUV given as variance).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No effect-site model, EC50, MEAC or ke0; the paper compares exposure (AUC, day-7 concentration) only. Not an opioid.

## Covariates
- Gestational age on AQ F, linear: F = 1 + 0.0128 x (GA - 24 wk) (Table 2); dOFV -15.6. F is 90% at 16 wk and 115% at 36 wk (90% CI 54-148% and 70-191%).
- GA and trimester not significant on any PQ parameter. Baseline parasitaemia on PQ F as above (dOFV -20.7). Age not significant for either drug.
- Simulation (Figures 3, 4): DEAQ AUC 2.8%-32.2% higher in pregnant women (16-36 wk) than non-pregnant (literature model, Ali 2018), day-7 DEAQ -10.1% to +15.5%; PQ AUC +10.0% and day-7 +20.7% vs non-pregnant (Hoglund 2017 model). Comparator is the published non-pregnant meta-models, not women in this study.

## Caveats
- No non-pregnant women; comparison is by simulation. Sparse sampling, no absorption phase, so ka/MTT fixed. 79% of AQ samples below LLOQ. Allometry kept although it worsened fit. Bootstrap omitted 67 (Table 2) and 36 (Table 3) of 1000 runs near a boundary. DEAQ Vc IIV 205% with 70% shrinkage.
- Authors note their AQ half-life is longer than previous reports, attributed to sparse sampling.
