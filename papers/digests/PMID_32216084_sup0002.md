## Model and parameters
**Panel A, visual predictive checks** (x: TADNOM, nominal time after dose, h; y: "concentration of SXB in units of
ug/mL" per the caption). Four strata: Adult visit 1, Adult visit 2, Pediatric visit 1, Pediatric visit 2. Visit 1 is
paediatric PK night 1 (half dose) and the first period of the adult studies; visit 2 is paediatric PK night 2 and the
second adult period. Red solid line, observed median; red dashed, observed 2.5th/97.5th percentiles (the caption says
"upper and lower 95% range"); red band, 95% PI of the simulated median; blue bands, 95% PIs of the simulated outer
percentiles. Read from the rendered page (approximate):
- Adult visit 1: observed median about 80 ug/mL near 0.75 h, about 60 after the 4 h dose; upper percentile about 150
  after each dose; x axis to 12 h.
- Adult visit 2: observed median about 95 near 1 h; upper percentile about 165 after dose 1 and 175 after dose 2;
  after 4 h the observed median runs at about 25, below its simulated band at several bins.
- Pediatric visit 1: observed median about 25 before 4 h and about 45 at about 5.5 h; upper percentile about 120 at
  about 5.5 h.
- Pediatric visit 2: observed median about 60 at 0.75 h, about 55 at about 5 h; upper percentile about 160.
Observed central tendencies sit mostly inside the median bands; the outer percentiles fall partly outside their blue
bands in several bins (visual reading; the paper gives no numeric VPC statistic here).

**Panel B, goodness of fit** (all data): observed vs population predictions and vs individual predictions (0-230
ug/mL, LOESS lines close to identity, PRED wider scatter); |IWRES| vs IPRED (mostly below 0.6, flat LOESS at about
0.17); CWRES vs TAFD (elapsed time from first dose, 0-12 h; mostly within -3 to +4, LOESS near 0).

## Caveats
- The y-axis basis is labelled SXB (sodium oxybate) in ug/mL in the caption; the panels say only "Observations".
- Strata pool studies with different regimens (single 4.5 g, 2 x 2.25 g, 2 x 3 g, 2 x 4.5 g; Table S2 in
  sup0006.docx), so the adult VPC shape mixes single- and two-dose profiles; per-study VPCs are not shown.
- The |IWRES| plot's flat LOESS at about 0.17 is consistent with a proportional residual error of that order; the
  residual-error estimate itself is not printed in Table S4 or here.
