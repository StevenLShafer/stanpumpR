# Short 1994: revised Marsh-type propofol model for Chinese children

## Study
Hong Kong, ASA I full-term children aged 4-10 yr, minor surgery, 67% N2O (the text says 70% in methods), spontaneous breathing, no premedication, haemoglobin >=11.5 g/dL. Part 1: n=10, 5.2 (4-7) yr, 18 (15-22) kg, 6 M/4 F, infusion 38 (18-108) min; initial target 10 ug/mL for the first child then +/-2 ug/mL by response to incision. Part 2 (prospective test): n=20, 6.8 (4-10) yr, 23.7 (15-43) kg, 19 M/1 F, infusion 30 (18-56) min; initial target 8 ug/mL (equal to a 3.5 mg/kg bolus), local block before surgery. Ohmeda 9000 pump driven by Marsh-style 3-compartment algorithm. VENOUS WHOLE BLOOD (opposite limb) at incision then every 5-10 min and up to 5 samples in the first 2 h of recovery (max 12 x 2 mL); HPLC-fluorescence, limit 2 ng/mL. Part 1: 67 samples; part 2: 177 samples (Fig 1, n=177, r=0.86). Revised rate constants found by iterative linear least squares minimising squared prediction error per patient on part 1 (not a population fit).

Revised set on part 1 (theoretical): bias -0.3%, precision 23.1%. Authors warn the revised constants are best treated as a fitting algorithm, not true compartmental parameters (order-dependent regression). Vc is 25% above Marsh's. Marsh's own precision was 20.1% against 24.8% here.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Not an opioid paper. No ke0, tPeak, EC50 or MEAC. Clinical end points only (part 2, with N2O, no opioid): mean whole-blood concentration for satisfactory anaesthesia 6.6 ug/mL (3-11), infusion 474 ug/kg/min (125-737); part 1 range 5-13 ug/mL, 497 (225-776) ug/kg/min. Eye opening to command 40 (17-80) min after stopping the infusion and N2O (35 min (19-68) in part 1), at a MODEL-PREDICTED concentration of 0.86 ug/mL (0.40-1.45). Authors compare with 1.1 ug/mL for waking in adults (Shafer 1988) and note a second concentration peak in 4 patients at 52-103 min post-infusion.

## Covariates
Weight only (everything per kg); no age term.

## Caveats
Authors: small n; Chinese children, no premedication; constants fitted to 10 children; recovery sampling only to about 1 h, so elimination not fully tested. Also: whole blood, not plasma; bias -0.1% is a mean while individuals ranged -30 to +42%.
