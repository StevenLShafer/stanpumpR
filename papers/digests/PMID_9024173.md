# Billard 1997: SE95, delta power and BIS as EEG measures (alfentanil, propofol, midazolam)

## Study
Retrospective re-analysis of EEG from 31 adult men (patients or volunteers) in earlier Stanford PK/PD studies, each given one drug by brief infusion to maximal EEG effect: propofol 0.5 mg/kg/min until 3 s of burst suppression (n=12 patients, mean 366 mg over 8.6 min); alfentanil 22 ug/kg/min until a plateau in spectral edge (n=11 volunteers, mean 8.5 mg over 4.7 min); midazolam 30 mg over 12 min (n=8 volunteers; only the first, midazolam-alone session of the Fiset study). Table I (PDF p. 4, printed p. 48) gives age, weight, infusion duration, dose, CL and Vss: alfentanil 48.3 y, 80.7 kg, CL 155.1 mL/min, Vss 21.8 L; propofol 49.8 y, 85.6 kg, CL 1869.6 mL/min, Vss 706.6 L; midazolam 32.1 y, 78.3 kg, CL 377.1 mL/min, Vss 91.6 L (means). EEG from posterior leads, 128 samples/s, SE95, delta power and BIS version 1.1. Sigmoid Emax (Hill) model with an effect-site compartment (ke0), least squares in Excel Solver; fit quality as r2.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Opioid audit (alfentanil): no MEAC or analgesic concentration measured. ke0 0.64/min, t½ke0 1.09 min, on the EEG (SE95, delta power, BIS 1.1 together), effect-site equilibration from plasma, estimated individually in volunteers (n=11); equals the 1.1 min of Scott 1985 (their ref 7) for SE95. EEG IC50 (519 delta, 574 SE95, 634 BIS ng/mL) was higher than the clinical IC50s the authors quote from Ausems (their ref 26): 475 intubation, 279 skin incision, 150 skin closure, 223 return of adequate ventilation ng/mL, and Lemmens' 208 ng/mL (breast surgery maintenance). Hill gamma 7.0. No tPeak, no potency ratio. Whether the concentrations are plasma or whole blood and total or free is not stated in this paper; alfentanil assay is in the earlier studies.
Propofol: biphasic EEG (activation then slowing then burst suppression), poorly fitted by a monophasic model. Midazolam: EEG ke0 about 0.019/min (t½ 36 min), far slower than the 2.2 min of Breimer; a semilinear canonical correlation reanalysis gave t½ke0 2.9 min and r2 0.78, which the authors regard as more consistent with clinical effect. Table II gives baseline and maximal effect of each measure (e.g. BIS at maximal alfentanil effect 35.5 (CV 58%) against baseline 90.4).

## Covariates
None.

## Caveats
Retrospective; mixed patients and volunteers; BIS version 1.1, since revised (the authors say). Propofol and midazolam activation phases not captured by the sigmoid; midazolam t½ke0 of 36 min is implausible for clinical effect (authors' own view). Posterior EEG leads only.
