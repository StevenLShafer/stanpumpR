# Kontny 2013: doxorubicin and doxorubicinol population PK, adults and children over 3 years

## Study
Retrospective pooled analysis of three earlier NONMEM datasets (PDF p. 3, printed p. 751; Table 1):
- Dataset 1 (Wilde 2002): 30 patients (21 M, 9 F), 29 adults plus one 17-year-old, advanced Hodgkin's lymphoma (BEACOPP);
  capillary electrophoresis with laser-induced fluorescence, LOQ 2 µg/L; 258 doxorubicin and 252 doxorubicinol samples.
- Dataset 2 (Callies 2003): 30 adults, 27-73 y, metastatic or locally advanced cancer, given with zosuquidar; HPLC-fluorescence,
  LOQ 2.5 µg/L; 350 + 357 samples; BSA was the only covariate available.
- Dataset 3 (Thompson 2009): 22 patients (16 M, 6 F), 17 aged 3-17 y and 5 adults 18-22 y, various cancers; HPLC-fluorescence,
  LOQ 2 µg/L; 326 + 326 samples.
- Combined: 82 patients (64 adults, 18 under 18 y), 934 doxorubicin and 935 doxorubicinol concentrations, ranges 2-2,701 and
  2-61 µg/L.
- External evaluation: dataset 4 (Jörger 2007), 59 women 30-81 y with breast cancer, LOQ 1 µg/L, 232 + 196 samples.

Table 1 (median (range)), by dataset: age 31 (17-59), 57 (27-73), 15 (3-22) y; weight 72 (54-99), 83 (43-137), 52 (12-80) kg;
BSA 1.9 (1.6-2.3), 2.0 (1.4-2.7), 1.5 (0.6-2.1) m²; dataset 4: 56 (30-81) y, 70 (51-95) kg, 1.8 (1.5-2.1) m². The "Combined"
row prints age 21 (3-73), weight 59 (12-99) kg, BSA 1.8 (0.6-2.3) m², height 168 (92-196) cm, although its footnote says it
includes dataset 2, whose weight reaches 137 kg and BSA 2.7 m² (an internal inconsistency of the table; the weight and BSA
ranges look like datasets 1 and 3 only).

Doses (Table 2): 22-36, 32-110 and 20-60 mg/m² over 0.03-1.67, 0.50 and 0.08-6.00 h (datasets 1-3); dataset 4 50-66 mg/m²
over 0.25 h. No salt (hydrochloride or base) is stated. Sampling to 21 h (dataset 1), 96 h (dataset 2), 48 h (dataset 3).

Software: NONMEM 7.2, FOCE with interaction, PsN, Pirana, Xpose. Predefined building strategy (Table 3); covariate
selection on datasets 1 and 3 (n = 52); age groups (3-11, 12-17, ≥18 y) compared by Kruskal-Wallis / Mann-Whitney.

## Model and parameters
Doxorubicin three compartments, then a metabolite compartment for doxorubicinol added with the doxorubicin parameters
fixed (ADVAN5, four compartments). Appendix 1 control stream (PDF p. 14, printed p. 762, page image):
- k10 = CL/V1 (doxorubicin elimination other than to doxorubicinol), k14 = Q4/V1 (formation), k40 = CLM/V4; S4 = V4;
  no molar-mass conversion between parent and metabolite.
- Every parameter × (1 + (BSA − 1.8) × θ13), θ13 = 0.643 FIX.
- Q4 = 32.1 L/h FIX (formation clearance), **not reported in Table 4**. Total doxorubicin elimination as run is therefore
  53.3 + 32.1 = 85.4 L/h (derived), fraction converted to doxorubicinol 32.1/85.4 = 0.376 (derived).
- Proportional error; IOV on CL and V1 (θ 0.13 and 0.205 FIX in the stream; Table 4 prints 13% and 21%).

Covariances (Table 4): CL-V1 78%, CL-Q2 68%, V1-Q2 48%. Bootstrap (1,000 runs, 34% failed to minimise) means agree
(CL 53.5, V1 17.8, V2 1815, CLdox'ol 44, Vdox'ol 1152). External evaluation on dataset 4 with Q3 and V3 fixed: CL 47.1,
V1 13.4, Q2 44.6, V2 672 L, θBSA 2.2, CLdox'ol 30, Vdox'ol 642 (Table 4). Derived: Vss = 17.7 + 1830 + 71.6 = 1919 L at
1.8 m²; the BSA factor reaches zero at BSA = 1.8 − 1/0.643 = 0.245 m².

Table 4 also re-expresses the four source studies' own models at 1.8 m² (footnotes a-c). Dataset 2's column prints
Q3 = 93.6 and V3 = 93.6; footnote b gives Q3 35.6 L/h and V3 104 L at BSA 2, i.e. 32.0 and 93.6 at 1.8 m² (derived), so
the printed Q3 looks like a misprint. Not used by stanpumpR.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No concentration-effect, toxicity or ke0 analysis. Background (citing others): doxorubicinol is 1.5- to 10-fold less
potent than doxorubicin in tumour-cell growth inhibition and is thought to contribute to cardiotoxicity (p. 2, printed 750).

## Covariates
BSA (linear, as above) on all parameters. Body weight (OFV 3,094), allometric weight (3,095; estimated exponent 0.77,
OFV 3,094), height, age, BMI and lean body mass (3,093) did not improve on BSA (3,096) at p < 0.01; sex not significant;
analytical centre lowered OFV (4,797 to 4,780) but the model was not robust and it was dropped. Age-group comparison:
V1 per m² smaller and Vdox'ol per kg larger in children under 12 than in adults (p < 0.05, Fig. 4); no age effect on CL.

## Caveats
- Retrospective, three assays at different centres; covariates other than BSA missing for dataset 2.
- No children under 3 years; only 7 children aged 3-11 in the age analysis.
- Dataset 1 sampled only to 21 h; the external evaluation needed Q3/V3 fixed and missed by >15% on most parameters; the
  authors advise sampling to at least 48 h.
- Formation clearance Q4 is in the control stream only; Table 4's CL is not total doxorubicin clearance.
- Linear BSA factor goes to zero at 0.245 m² and negative below it.
- Dose salt form unstated.
