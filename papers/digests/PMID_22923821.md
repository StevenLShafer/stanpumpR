# Puri 2012: BIS closed-loop propofol (CLADS) at 3505 m

## Study
Single-arm performance study, Leh, Ladakh (3505 m), 20 ASA I-II adults, elective abdominal/orthopaedic surgery (Table 1, p.240): age 37.69±15.25 y, M:F 9:11, BMI 17.2±3.5 kg/m2 (as printed), room-air SpO2 89±1.0 %, pre-induction EtCO2 25.5±3.48 mmHg. Premedication midazolam 0.5 mg and fentanyl 2 ug/kg IV; fentanyl then 1 ug/kg/h; vecuronium 0.1 mg/kg; lidocaine 1.5 mg/kg; 66% N2O. Propofol delivered by the authors' closed-loop system (CLADS): BIS (target 50, 15 s averaging, EEG data requested every 5 s) feeds a PID-type controller that sets the syringe-pump rate; signal-quality index used to reject invalid BIS (Fig 1, p.239). No propofol PK model parameters are given.

Total CLADS time 58.28±20 min, valid time 55.2±21 min (p.240). Median time to obeying command 3 min (IQR 6); median 15 min (IQR 25) to baseline oximetry.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No ke0, EC50 or concentration-effect relation estimated. The controller's "effect site delay" appears only as a box in Fig 1. End point is BIS (target 50); the authors cite their own earlier work that high-altitude dwellers need more propofol for a given BIS (ref 10), but this paper does not quantify it.

## Covariates
None analysed (altitude is the setting, not a modelled covariate).

## Caveats
Single arm, n=20, short cases (~58 min); MDPE/MDAPE worse than the authors' earlier report; N2O, fentanyl and midazolam co-administered, so propofol doses are not single-agent. Dose basis: mg/kg, weight type not stated; BMI 17.2 is low.
