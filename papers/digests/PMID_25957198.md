# Cawello 2015: clinical PK/PD of lacosamide (review)

## Study
Narrative review by a UCB Pharma author, summarizing sponsor phase I-III data. Not a new study.

## Model and parameters
Key PK statements (section and page):
- Absorption: Cmax 0.5-4 h after single (100-800 mg) and multiple (up to 400 mg bid) doses; accumulation factor
  about 2.3; no food effect (s. 3.1, p. 903).
- Absolute oral bioavailability 100 % (Fig. 2; ref 27); abstract: 100 % for doses up to 800 mg. Tablet, syrup and
  15-, 30- and 60-min IV infusions give equivalent AUC; Cmax higher only for the 15-min infusion (p. 903).
- Mass balance with [14C] 100 mg IV (60-min) vs oral solution: plasma total radioactivity curves similar, confirming
  complete absorption; unchanged drug 38 % (IV) and 34 % (oral) of urinary radioactivity; O-desmethyl 28 % (p. 905).
- Protein binding < 15 %; Vd about 0.6 L/kg; about 50 % removed by a 4-h haemodialysis (s. 3.2, p. 904).
- About 40 % of the dose excreted unchanged in urine; about 60 % metabolized (CYP2C19, 2C9, 3A4 and non-CYP);
  metabolites inactive (s. 3.3, p. 904).
- t1/2 12-14 h young, 14-16 h elderly (abstract: about 13 h young); about 18 h (mild-severe renal impairment) and
  20 h (ESRD). Systemic CL about 2.5 L/h healthy; total CL 2 L/h in POS patients (p. 904).
- Renal impairment AUC ratios: mild 1.3, moderate 1.2, severe 1.6, ESRD 1.6-fold (s. 4.4, p. 905).
- Moderate hepatic impairment (Child-Pugh B, n = 8): AUCss up 50-60 % (s. 4.5).
- Women: AUCtau,ss about 14 % higher in elderly women than men at about 70 kg (s. 4.2).
- Healthy-subject popPK (n = 47, 100 mg): one compartment; V/F 42.4 L (man, height 1.69 m), 35.5 L (woman);
  ke 0.044 /h (t1/2 15.8 h) men, 0.051 /h (13.6 h) women at CLcr 89 mL/min (s. 4.7, p. 906).
- Patient popPK (565 POS patients, 2370 samples): one compartment; V/F 64.8 L, CL/F 1.97 L/h without inducers;
  inducers (CBZ, phenytoin, phenobarbital) lower concentrations 15-20 % (s. 5.3 and 7.1, p. 908, 910).
- Phase II/III steady-state values (mean +/- SD), 200 / 400 / 600 mg/day: Cmax 3.88 +/- 1.82, 8.76 +/- 3.31,
  12.33 +/- 4.69 ug/mL; Cmin 2.48 +/- 1.00, 5.28 +/- 1.92, 7.41 +/- 2.78 ug/mL (p. 907).
- CSF:serum ratio about 0.5 (IV, naive), 0.7 (pretreated), about 0.85 at oral steady state (s. 5.2).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- Emax exposure-response model, 1308 patients from three phase III trials: trough concentration giving half the
  maximum seizure-frequency reduction 4.6 ug/mL (90 % CI 3.5-5.7) (s. 6.1, p. 908; ref 57).
- Median seizure reduction 33.3, 36.8, 39.4 % at 200, 400, 600 mg/day vs 18.4 % placebo.
- PR interval increase at tmax (placebo-subtracted) 7.3 ms (400 mg/day) and 11.9 ms (800 mg/day).
- No therapeutic range stated. Not an opioid paper; no ke0.

## Covariates
Reported (from cited models): CLcr and sex on ke, height and sex on V/F (healthy); sex on V/F and sex, weight and
inducers on CL/F (patients; sex and weight dropped from the final model, < 15 % effect).

## Caveats
Single-author review by a sponsor employee; secondary source for nearly every number; most values approximate.
