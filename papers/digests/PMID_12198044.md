# Hentgen 2002: propofol-sufentanil TCI for thyroid surgery

## Study
Open, randomized (blocks of 6, stratified by sex), 3 groups of 15, ASA I-II, age 18-70, thyroid surgery (31 total thyroidectomy, 14 lobectomy). Median age 46 y (25-70), weight 62 kg (46-112); 35 women. Sufentanil effect-site TCI via Stanpump, Gepts PK (3-compartment, no weight/age/sex adjustment), fixed target (STC) 0.1, 0.2 or 0.3 ng/mL until thyroid removal. Propofol TCI (Diprifusor, Marsh model, plasma target) started at 4 ug/mL, raised 1-2 ug/mL every 2 min until LOC, then titrated to MBP within 70-130% of reference and BIS 40-60. Atracurium 0.5 mg/kg. Concentrations are model-predicted, not assayed. Interaction fitted per Vuyk 1996 (additive vs interaction coefficient e) by least squares in Excel.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End points: BIS 40-60, MBP, somatic movement, recovery (spontaneous ventilation, response to command, extubation). Propofol requirement fell linearly with STC; the interaction model was not significantly better than additive (e not demonstrated), correlations r=0.16/0.18 (intubation), 0.46 (incision), 0.63/0.64 (thyroid removal).
- Recovery times (Table 4, median, min, STC 0.1/0.2/0.3): spontaneous ventilation 9/9/16 (p significant for 0.3), response to commands 15/14/16, extubation 16/14/22 (NS).
- Authors' recommendation: STC 0.3 ng/mL for intubation in paralysed patients; 0.2 ng/mL optimal during surgery; 0.1 inadequate (4/15 moved); 0.3 delays recovery.
- Opioid audit: no MEAC or analgesic concentration defined (clinical STC range 0.1-0.3 ng/mL, effect-site, predicted, not assayed). ke0 / t1/2 ke0 / tPeak not reported; the effect site is Stanpump's default for sufentanil. Potency ratio mentioned only as a literature assumption (sufentanil/alfentanil = 630, from Vuyk), not derived here (PDF p. 1).

## Covariates
None modelled; Gepts and Marsh used unadjusted for sufentanil, weight-adjusted for propofol (per the text).

## Caveats
Open design, n=15 per group, concentrations predicted not measured, no neuromuscular monitoring so movement may be under-detected, only three STC levels, a surgery-specific stimulus.
