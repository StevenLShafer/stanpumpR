# Mu 2018: TCI vs manual propofol infusion in children

## Study
Randomised, unblinded, single-centre trial (Shenzhen), Aug 2015 to Mar 2016, ChiCTR-IOD-16010147. ASA I-II children aged 1-12 yr for elective surgery with tracheal intubation. 92 assessed, 85 randomised (41 TCI, 44 manual), 74 analysed, 37 per group (Fig 1, PDF p.4). Table 1 (PDF p.5): age 4.3 yr (range 1-11 TCI, 1-9 manual); weight 18.8 (7.1) vs 19.5 (8.3) kg; M:F 28:9 vs 20:17; sevoflurane induction 16 (43.2%) vs 11 (29.7%).

Propofol arms (both aimed at BIS 40-60, BIS recorded every 5 min):
- TCI: Paedfusor model on an Alaris PK pump (Absalom 2003), plasma target Cp 3-5 µg/mL, adjusted to BIS; no effect-site model.
- Manual: 2.5 mg/kg load then 15, 13, 11, 10, 9 mg/kg/h (15 min, 15 min, 30 min, 60 min, then remainder), the McFarlan 1999 scheme, said to give blood propofol about 3.0 µg/mL.
- All: fentanyl 1 µg/kg, atracurium 0.5 mg/kg, remifentanil infusion 0.15 µg/kg/min plus 1 µg/kg bolus 30 s before intubation (titrated to heart rate within 20% of post-induction), dexmedetomidine 0.5 µg/kg, flurbiprofen, paracetamol, wound infiltration.
- Propofol stopped 5-10 min before the end of surgery. Primary end point: time to extubation after stopping propofol; power 33/group for a 25% reduction from 19.1 (6.9) min.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: BIS 40-60 and recovery times, not a PK-PD fit. Table 2: % time BIS <40 was 10.4 (18.3) TCI vs 10.4 (15.3) manual; % time BIS >60 was 10.2 (18.4) vs 23.2 (26.3), P 0.016; median setting changes 2 [2-4 (0-7)] vs 0 [0-1 (0-4)].
- Duration of propofol infusion 69.7 (26.9) vs 74.4 (40.1) min; Aldrete >=9 after stopping 55.2 (19.1) vs 48.5 (17.4) min (as printed; see open question); PAED >=10 in 2 (5.4%) vs 0.
- Authors note the Paedfusor has no effect-site (ke0) component, so a high plasma target was needed at induction to avoid movement; no ke0, t½ke0 or tPeak is given. They say 2.5 mg/kg gives an initial plasma concentration of about 4-5 µg/mL on Paedfusor or Kataria.
- Audit (remifentanil): MEAC / analgesic concentration none; ke0/tPeak none; potency ratio none. Remifentanil given by fixed rate, no concentration reported.

## Covariates
None analysed beyond group allocation; dose expressed per kg.

## Caveats
Authors: unblinded, BIS-guided in both arms (pushes doses together), BIS logged every 5 min, dexmedetomidine confounds recovery, not generalisable to comorbid patients or very long cases. Also: Paedfusor, not a model for Chinese children; effect-site absent; TCI dose slightly higher (difference about 1.8 mg/kg/h).
