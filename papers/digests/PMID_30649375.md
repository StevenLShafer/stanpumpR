# Wasmann 2019: micafungin PK and target attainment in obese adults

PDF pages = printed pages minus 977 (PDF p.1 = printed 978).

## Study
Open-label, single-dose, multicentre PK study in healthy volunteers (NCT03102658): 24 subjects (all Caucasian, 50% female), 8 normal weight (BMI 18.5-25) given 100 mg IV, 16 morbidly obese (BMI > 40, bariatric surgery) randomised to 100 mg (n=8) or 200 mg (n=8) IV; every dose infused over 60 min. Age 18-65. Table 1 (PDF p.3, printed 980), median (range): weight 70.8 (61.5-81.5) normal, 156 (112-184) obese 100 mg, 141 (126-180) obese 200 mg kg; BMI 22.5, 44.4, 43.5; LBW (Janmahasatian) 46.3, 65.2, 65.2 kg. Nine samples per person over 24 h (0.5, 0.95, 1.25, 1.5, 2, 4, 8, 12, 24 h from start of infusion), plus 48 h in some. 223 plasma samples analysed (2 excluded). Assay: validated UPLC with fluorescence detection, plasma 0.01-32.40 mg/L. NONMEM 7.4.0, FOCE-I. Observed geometric mean AUC0-24 (range), 100 mg: 96.9 (80.8-119.0) mg.h/L normal weight vs 55.5 (39.9-74.1) obese; obese 200 mg: 114 (97.7-139) (p. 980).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No concentration-effect model and no ke0 (not an opioid). PK/PD target from Andes 2011 (ref 6): AUC/MIC > 5000 (AUC0-24 in mg.h/L over MIC in mg/L), for all Candida except C. parapsilosis; quoted as associated with 97.8% mycological response (98% vs 87% below 5000). Candida MICs simulated 0.008, 0.016, 0.032, 0.064 mg/L (CLSI). Dose nomogram (Fig 4, p.984): 100 mg suffices up to 125 kg for MIC 0.016; 200 mg above 125 kg (0.016) or for MIC 0.032 up to 125 kg, 300 mg above; MIC 0.064 needs 300 mg up to about 90 kg then alternative therapy. A loading dose of twice the maintenance dose helps on day 1. Prior bedside rule (Pasipanodya): dose (mg) = weight (kg) + 42; the authors find it adequate to 190 kg only for MIC up to 0.016. No MEAC or therapeutic range reported; no plasma-concentration target apart from AUC/MIC.

## Covariates
TBW on CL, Vc, Vp (power, standardised to 70 kg male 1.8 m). LBW, BMI, ideal body weight, age and sex were also tested; TBW was best; age and sex gave no improvement (p. 980). Inclusion threshold dOFV >= 10.8 (p<0.001).

## Caveats
n=24, relatively young and healthy, obese subjects had minor laparoscopic surgery (fluids, co-medication) on the sampling day; sampling only to 24 h (48 h in a few). Authors note that the CL-weight relationship agrees with Hebert 2005 (mean weight 71.7 kg, CL 0.72 L/h; model predicts 0.70). Observed AUC0-24 is a 24-h exposure, not AUCinf, so it is not simply dose/CL (derived: 100 mg / 0.690 L/h = 145 mg.h/L). No standard error is given for the 70 kg anchor, the cohort covers 61.5-184 kg, 70 kg is within the normal-weight group's median (70.8 kg). Supplementary Figs S1-S2 were not in the PDF.
