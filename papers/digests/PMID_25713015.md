# Freeman 2015: remifentanil PCA vs epidural in labour (RAVEL trial)

## Study
- Multicentre (15 Dutch hospitals) randomised, open-label equivalence trial, women of intermediate/high obstetric risk (ASA 1-2, age >=18, vaginal delivery planned after 32 weeks). Randomised antenatally (1414: 709 remifentanil, 705 epidural); analysed 1358 (687 / 671) by intention to treat; 447 (65%) and 347 (52%) actually received analgesia (RR 1.32, 1.18-1.48).
- Remifentanil: PCA bolus 30 ug (20 ug/mL solution), 3-min lockout, no background infusion; could be raised to 40 ug or lowered to 20 ug. Of 447 users, 92% (411) started at 30 ug; dose increased once in 13%, decreased once in 3%. Epidural: local protocol (ropivacaine/sufentanil 37%, bupivacaine/sufentanil 46%, levobupivacaine/sufentanil 6%, bupivacaine/fentanyl 11%).
- Pain and satisfaction VAS hourly (0-100 mm), AUC as primary outcome. No blood sampling, no assay.
- Baseline (Table 1, text layer scrambled, not re-rendered): maternal age about 31.5 (5.1) vs 31.7 (4.8) y, median BMI 23.7 vs 23.8 (21.4-27.6), 21.7% and 20.4% BMI missing. Weight not reported.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- No MEAC, ke0, t1/2ke0, tPeak, EC50 or equianalgesic ratio: none reported (no concentrations).
- Primary (Table 3, PDF p.10), mean AUC, remifentanil vs epidural, imputed: satisfaction during active labour 30.9 vs 33.7, difference -2.8 (-6.9 to 1.3), equivalence not shown; satisfaction after start of analgesia (447/347) 25.6 vs 36.1, -10.4 (-13.9 to -7.0). Pain AUC 30.9 vs 27.2 (3.8, 0.92 to 6.6) and 26.7 vs 20.3 (6.4, 3.5 to 9.4). Non-imputed values are in the table.
- Table 4 (text layer, not re-rendered), mean (SD): pain after pain relief 6.1 (1.9) vs 4.2 (2.3); lowest pain after relief 4.0 (2.6) vs 1.7 (2.3); pain at request 7.7 vs 7.7.
- Safety (Table 6, PDF p.13), during analgesia, remifentanil n=447 vs epidural n=347: SpO2 <95% 154 (37) vs 37 (12); SpO2 <92% 71 (18) vs 14 (5); minimum SpO2 median 95 (93-97) vs 97 (96-98), range 50-100 vs 76-100; respiratory depression (<8/min) 4 (1) vs 0, P=0.15; nausea 62 (21) vs 25 (12); percentages use valid observations, with 19-40% missing for some items.

## Covariates
Adjusted regression (age, race, education, parity, onset of labour, dilation, prematurity) gave similar results (satisfaction AUC after relief -8.7, -12.0 to -5.5). Subgroups (parity, induction, age and others) showed no interaction. No PK covariates.

## Caveats
AUC computable for only 57% / 43% of women (handled by multiple imputation); primary outcome changed from a single time point to AUC mid-trial (Feb 2013); open label with about 10% crossover (remifentanil to epidural 13% of starters, 53/402). Not a PK study.
