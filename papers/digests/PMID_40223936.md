# Zhang 2025: fluconazole popPK in ARF patients on CRRT

## Study
Literature-data popPK: 297 plasma concentrations from 16 patients (15 acute renal failure, 1 liver-transplant patient with normal renal function) in five published CRRT studies (Wolter 1994, Valtonen 1997, Muhl 2000, Kishino 2001, Yagasaki 2003, Lopez 2014 appear in Table 1), digitized with WebPlotDigitizer 4.3 (p2-3). 9 male / 7 female; median age 59.5 y (32-82), median weight 77 kg (48-272); doses 50-1,200 mg over 0.5-6 h; CVVH/CVVHD/CVVHDF (13/1/14 per Table 1, as printed); Qb 75-180 mL/min; CRRT dose 8.0-64.8 mL/h/kg (Table 1, p3). Missing values imputed by mean or median. NONMEM 7.3, FOCEI, PsN 4.60; GOF, pc-VPC, SIR (p2-4). External validation only in a few samples from two other studies (Supplement, per p7).

## Model and parameters
One-compartment central model in series with a CRRT compartment (Q exchange); total clearance = CLbody + CLcrrt; additive residual error (Fig 2, p4). Table 2 (PDF p5, read on image):

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No ke0 or effect-site model (not an opioid paper; no MEAC). PK/PD target: free-drug fAUC0-24/MIC >= 100 (EUCAST), 12% protein binding assumed, mean sieving coefficients used; probability of target attainment >= 90% = optimal; MIC 0.06-32 mg/L examined, breakpoints 2 mg/L (EUCAST) and 4 mg/L (CLSI SDD) (p4). Monte Carlo n = 1,000 across weights 45/70/95/120 kg and CRRT doses 0/20/35/50 mL/kg/h (Tables 3, 4, p8 image). Minimum doses meeting target, MIC 2 mg/L (LD/MD mg), 0 mL/kg/h: 45 kg 800/200; 70 kg 840/200; 95 kg 1,000/200; 120 kg 1,200/200. At 20: 800/360; 1,000/400; 1,140/570; 1,440/600. At 35: 1,000/400; 1,200/560; none/760; none/800. At 50: 1,000/540; 1,200/700; none/950; none/1,000. MIC 4 mg/L maintenance (Table 4): 0: 360/400/400/400; 20: 600/800/950/1,200; 35: 800/1,200/none/none; 50: 1,000/none/none/none. No loading dose met target on day 1 at MIC 4.

## Covariates
Renal failure on CLbody (two typical values); total body weight on Vc by a power function with exponent 0.799 (see open question for form). Age, sex, filter membrane area/type not retained.

## Caveats
Authors: digitized data and literature-derived dosing/CRRT info; 16 patients only; limited external validation. One patient supplies the non-ARF clearance and a 272 kg patient widens the weight range (Table 1). CRRT clearance is an input computed outside the model. Reference values quoted from literature: healthy clearance 15-24 mL/kg/h, half-life 30 h; ARF 10 mL/kg/h, half-life 96 h (p2, secondary citations).
