# Ostermann 2004: plasma and CSF population PK of temozolomide

## Study
- 35 adults with malignant glioma from two phase II trials (Table 1, p 3730 / PDF p 3; mean (range)):
  - Pilot study (newly diagnosed GBM, n = 23): age 50 (36–65) y, weight 72 (46–105) kg, height 169 (150–188) cm,
    BSA 1.80 (1.46–2.11) m², 15/8 M/F, creatinine 84 (61–108) µmol/L.
  - Recurrent study (n = 12; 8 GBM, 4 AA/AOA): age 49 (30–79), weight 72 (53–85), height 170 (151–181),
    BSA 1.83 (1.59–2.06), 9/3 M/F, creatinine 84 (53–129).
- Oral temozolomide (TMZ): continuous 75 mg/m²/day × 42–49 days with radiotherapy (fasting, 1 h before RT), then
  adjuvant 200 mg/m²/day × 5 every 4 weeks; or intermittent 150–200 mg/m²/day × 5 every 4 weeks (p 3729 / PDF p 2).
- Co-medications common: CYP-inducing anticonvulsants 17/23 and 8/12, corticosteroids 18/23 and 9/12 (Table 2,
  PDF p 4).
- Sparse sampling: 3 plasma samples 0.2–8.4 h post-dose on day 1 and the last day (continuous) or days 1 and 5
  (intermittent); CSF by lumbar puncture on two occasions 1.3–6.6 h post-dose (one patient with an Ommaya
  reservoir gave serial CSF). 227 plasma and 47 CSF samples. Plasma acidified with HCl.
- Assay: RP-HPLC-UV (330 nm) after C18 solid-phase extraction; linear 0.4–20 µg/mL (plasma), 0.1–2 µg/mL (CSF);
  LOQ 0.2 µg/mL plasma, 0.1 µg/mL CSF; inter-assay CV and bias <15% (PDF p 3).
- Low-dose (75 mg/m²) concentrations were normalised to 200 mg/m² (factor 2.67) for display; observed ranges
  0.10–13.99 µg/mL plasma, 0.16–1.93 µg/mL CSF (p 3732 / PDF p 5).
- NONMEM V, FO method, ADVAN5; F fixed to 1 (no IV data; literature bioavailability 97%).

## Model and parameters
Three compartments: gut (A1) → plasma (A2, volume VD) ⇄ CSF (A3, volume Vp), with first-order Ka, oral CL from
plasma, K23 plasma→CSF, K32 CSF→plasma (Fig. 1 and equations, PDF p 3). Vp fixed to 140 mL from literature.
Final estimates (Table 4, p 3732 / PDF p 5; s.e. as % of estimate):

Covariate model (Table 3 multivariate rows, PDF p 4): CL = θa·(1 + θb·sex) + θc·BSA with θa 10.0, θb 0.2
(Table 4: 0.19), θc 4.2, sex male = 1; VD = θa + θb·BSA with 30.3 and 19.9; BSA (DuBois) expressed as relative
deviation from the population mean (footnotes Table 3/Table 4). Doubling BSA gives CL 14.2 L/h and VD 50.2 L
(text). The population mean BSA itself is not stated (study means 1.80 and 1.83 m²).

Base model without covariates (Table 3 footnote c): CL 11.2 L/h, VD 29.3 L, Ka 5.2 h⁻¹, K23 6.78 × 10⁻⁴,
K32 0.72, Vp 0.14 L.

Exposure (Table 5, p 3735 / PDF p 8; mean ± SD of post hoc AUC, mg·h/L, printed "mg/liter/h"): daily dose
75 mg/m² AUCplasma 12.1 ± 1.1, AUCCSF 2.5 ± 0.3; 200 mg/m² 30.1 ± 6.1 and 6.1 ± 1.2; AUCCSF/AUCplasma
20.6 ± 2.0% (continuous, n = 23) and 20.3 ± 1.9% (intermittent, n = 32). AUCplasma = Dose/CL; AUCCSF =
Dose/CL × (VD·K23/K32)/Vp (PDF p 4). (Derived: typical (K23/K32)·(VD/Vp) = 0.205, consistent with ~20%.)
Observed CSF/plasma concentration ratio median 30.1% (range 2.1–65.2%), rising across the dosing interval.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- No ke0, EC50 or target concentration. CSF is modelled as a PK compartment (K23/K32), not an effect site.
- Survival and PFS (pilot study) correlated with cumulative dose, AUCplasma and AUCCSF (Spearman P 0.0003–0.0065),
  but the predictors were inter-correlated (r > 0.86) and AUC added nothing to cumulative dose; with daily or
  per-cycle dose, P > 0.1; logistic regression P > 0.19 (PDF p 6).
- Haematological toxicity (nadir % decrease, intermittent schedule, first two cycles): mean ANC decrease 49%,
  platelets 47%; no correlation with AUCplasma (P = 0.599 ANC, 0.812 platelets) (PDF p 7).
- Radiotherapy: non-significant trend to 15% higher K23 (ΔOF −2.6). Corticosteroids: no effect on K23/K32 (ΔOF −0.1).

## Covariates
BSA on CL and VD, sex on CL (Table 3). Age, creatinine, CYP inducers (ΔOF −0.2) and inhibitors (−2.2), antiemetics,
antacids, sucralfate, corticosteroids: no significant effect. Authors' comparison: oral CL 11.2 L/h with 15% ISV
in Jen et al. 2000 (a different population model), versus 10.0 L/h and 4.7% here.

## Caveats
- Oral CL and VD are apparent (CL/F, V/F) with F fixed to 1.
- Ka imprecise (few early samples); CSF only 47 samples from lumbar puncture, mostly one time point per occasion;
  Vp fixed, so K23 and K32 are conditional on 140 mL.
- FO estimation; ISV on CL (4.7%) has a large s.e. (80.5%).
- Table 3 footnote d describes BSA oddly ("relative deviation of the individual body weight, height, age").
- TMZ is a prodrug; the active MTIC was not measured.
