# McCune 2014: busulfan population PK from infancy to adulthood

## Study
- Retrospective pooled TDM data, June 1999 to September 2011, FHCRC / SCCA busulfan PK laboratory; 51 institutions (p761).
- 1,610 HCT recipients aged 0.1-65.8 y (mean 9.8 ± 13.0 y; 92% < 20 y; 466 < 2 y, 256 < 1 y, 25 < 3 months PNA). 904 male, 689 female, 17 not reported. Malignant 978, non-malignant 632 (Table 1, p3/printed 756; p5).
- Actual body weight (ABW) available for only 133 patients (108 adults, 25 children; "ABW-available cohort"), mean age 42.9 ± 20.7 y. For the rest only the institution's dosing weight (DWT) was known.
- IV busulfan, zero-order infusion of institution-reported duration; dosing frequency Q6h 88%, Q24h 11%, Q8h/Q12h 1% each (of 1,570 with known frequency).
- 12,380 concentrations. GC-MS, dynamic range 25-4,500 ng/mL, interday CV < 8%; 91 points (0.7%) below LLOQ (62 ng/mL) kept in the data (p2/printed 755).
- NONMEM 7.2, FOCE-I; parameter uncertainty from 100 bootstrap replicates; pcVPC.

## Model and parameters
Two compartments, first-order elimination (no evidence of mixed-order elimination). Size by normal fat mass (NFM) with theory-based allometry (exponent 3/4 on CL and Q, 1 on V1 and V2, fixed; estimated exponents in Table S4 confirmed these, p6/printed 759). Equations (p3/printed 756):
- (A) FFM = WHSmax·HT²·TBW / (WHS50·HT² + TBW); WHSmax 42.92 (M) / 37.99 (F) kg/m²; WHS50 30.93 (M) / 35.98 (F) kg/m²; HT in m.
- (B) NFM = FFM + Ffat·(TBW − FFM), Ffat parameter-specific.
- (C) TBW = DWT·FDW·FFEM_DW when ABW missing; FDW estimated indistinguishable from 1 and fixed to 1.
- (D) Fsize = (NFM/70)^Pwr; the reference NFMs of 62 kg (CL) and 59 kg (V) correspond to 70 kg TBW (Table 2 footnote a).
- (E) Fmat = 1/(1 + (PMA/TM50)^−Hill); PMA = PNA + 40 weeks (all infants assumed term).
- (F) CLGRP = CLpop·Fmat·Fsize·Fsex; Q has Fmat and Fsex fixed to 1; V1 and V2 have Fmat fixed to 1.
- Random effects exponential with BSV and BOV (G); BSV on TBW proportional (H); combined residual error with BSV on RUV (I, J).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Not an analgesic/opioid paper; no PD model, no ke0. Exposure target used for simulations: Css 770 ng/mL (equivalent to AUC 1,125 µmol/L·min with Q6h dosing), therapeutic window 592-963 ng/mL (−20% / +25%, bioequivalence convention) (p4/printed 757). Css = AUC/dosing interval. The paper cites FDA (Booth) simulations targeting 615-925 ng/mL and EMA/Trame windows of 615-1,025 ng/mL (p761-762).

Dose-prediction comparison (Table 3, p8/printed 761), % of patients in window: age- and size-dependent model 72% overall; FDA dosing 57% (P < 0.0001); EMA dosing 70% (P = 0.214). Note: the text-layer column alignment of Table 3's age rows looks shifted (eight model values for eight rows plus "All"); the age-group values were not confirmed on a rendered page and are not relied on here.

Label Q6h doses quoted (Table 3 footnote b): FDA 1.1 mg/kg ≤ 12 kg, 0.8 mg/kg > 12 kg; EMA 1 mg/kg < 9 kg, 1.2 mg/kg 9 to < 16 kg, 1.1 mg/kg 16-23 kg, 0.95 mg/kg > 23-34 kg, 0.8 mg/kg > 34 kg.

## Covariates
- Size: NFM with Ffat 0.509 (CL, Q) and 0.203 (V1, V2); i.e. CL scales with FFM plus 51% of fat mass, V with FFM plus 20% (p6).
- Maturation of CL by PMA (sigmoid, TM50 45.7 wk, Hill 2.3): 50% of adult at 46 wk PMA (6 weeks after term birth); size-standardised CL reaches 95% of adult at 2.5 postnatal years (p6, Fig. 2) (derived check: PMA 40 + 2.5·52 = 170 wk gives 170^2.3/(45.7^2.3+170^2.3) = 0.95).
- Female sex: V1 and V2 × 1.07; no sex effect on CL (p6).
- Time within course: CL from 6-36 h 6.8% lower and from 36-83 h 8.1% lower than 0-6 h (p6). The paper does not state explicitly whether time is from the first dose of the course.
- Disease (malignant vs not): no effect on CL or V.
- Not tested: actual gestational age (unavailable), organ function, concomitant drugs.

## Caveats
- ABW in only 133 patients; Ffat estimated in that subset (RSE 43-52%) then fixed for the rest, whose TBW was estimated from DWT.
- Gestational age unknown; all infants assumed 40 weeks.
- Adults few (n ≈ 128 ≥ 20 y), and mostly on daily dosing; authors call for validation in adults and the obese.
- Abstract CL (12.4 L/h) differs from Table 2 (11.4 L/h).
- Supplement tables S2-S7 referenced in the text are not in the archived supplement (which holds Table S1 only).
