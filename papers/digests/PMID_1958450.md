# Pöyhiä 1991: IV oxycodone kinetics in young surgical adults

## Study
Open single-dose study (short report). n = 9 otherwise healthy appendicectomy patients (4 M / 5 F), age 27 ± 5.3 y
(19-37), weight 71 ± 11 kg (57-90), height 175 cm (Table 1). Studied on the first postoperative morning, 13.1 ± 3.9 h
after anaesthesia (premedication pethidine 1 mg/kg IM; thiopentone or propofol, enflurane/N2O, alfentanil or fentanyl).
Dose: oxycodone hydrochloride ("chloride") 0.07 mg/kg IV bolus, stated as = 0.05 mg/kg free base; Table 1 dose column
3.1-4.7 mg (mean 3.8 mg), which matches free base. Venous samples 5 min to 12 h (15 samples). Assays: oxycodone GC,
LLOQ 3 ng/mL; noroxycodone GC, limit 1 ng/mL; oxymorphone HPLC-coulometric, limit 0.5 ng/mL. Individual bi- (6) or
tri-exponential (3: subjects 1, 5, 7) fits by nonlinear least squares (Systat), equal weights, AIC; t½z, Vss, CL by
standard (Wagner) methods; AUC(0-12) by linear trapezoids.

## Model and parameters
No population or compartmental parameter table; only per-subject noncompartmental-type summaries (Table 1, mean ± SD,
95% CI). Per-subject exponents/coefficients are not reported.

Individual CL ranged 0.38-1.33 L/min and t½z 110-580 min (Table 1). No kinetic parameter correlated with age, weight
or duration of anaesthesia. Oxymorphone was never detected (< 0.5 ng/mL).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD measured: no MEAC, no ke0 / t½ke0 / tPeak, no hysteresis. The introduction says oxycodone is regarded as similar
in potency to morphine (qualitative, cited); no equianalgesic ratio is given. Discussion: longer t½ than morphine
reflects lower clearance, volumes being similar.

## Covariates
None identified (no correlation with age, body weight, anaesthesia duration; n = 9).

## Caveats
Small n; postoperative patients ~13 h after general anaesthesia with other opioids; GC assay LLOQ 3 ng/mL limits the
terminal phase; mixed bi/tri-exponential fits; CL reported in absolute L/min while Vss is per kg; dose expressed both
as salt (0.07 mg/kg) and base (0.05 mg/kg) - which one CL was computed on is not stated explicitly, though the dose
column is base.
