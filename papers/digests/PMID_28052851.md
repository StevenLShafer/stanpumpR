# Hopkins 2017: population PK of oral doxycycline (Doryx formulations)

## Study
Pooled analysis of eight phase 1 trials (Mayne Pharma, all US, healthy volunteers, normal renal function). 188 enrolled; 178 with PK data (Table 1: 120 male, 58 female; age mean 28 y, range 18-73; weight mean 75.9 kg, range 47.2-114.4; FFM mean 56.3 kg; BMI 25.25; 22 smokers). 651 oral doses, 7,093 concentrations; 12.7% BLOQ, excluded (M1 method). Formulations: 100 mg conventional doxycycline hyclate capsule ("Doryx capsule"), 75/100/150/200 mg Doryx delayed-release tablet, 120 mg Doryx MPC tablet; fed and fasted. Six single-dose two-period crossovers, two multidose studies (study 40: 7 daily doses; study 70: doses at 0, 72, 96, 120, 144, 168 h). Plasma doxycycline by validated HPLC, LLOQ 0.01-0.1 ug/mL by study (Table 6). NONMEM 7.3, FOCE. All doses oral, no IV data, so volumes and clearance are apparent (/F).

## Model and parameters
Two-compartment model, two transit absorption compartments (rate KTR), allometric scaling to fat-free mass (70 kg reference; exponents 0.75 for clearances, 1 for volumes), exponential BSV and BOV, combined proportional/additive error, fixed between-study effects. Covariances (Table 3): CL-KTR 0.420, CL-VP1 0.363, CL-V 0.720, KTR-VP1 -0.234, KTR-V 0.884, VP1-V -0.089. Base-model estimates are only in supplement File S1 (not in this PDF). Absolute bioavailability is not estimable (oral data only).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No PD, no MEAC, no ke0 or effect-site model. The discussion says AUC/MIC is the efficacy driver (citing another reference); no target concentration is given. Not an opioid paper.

## Covariates
Retained: fed status on relative bioavailability (10.5% decrease per text), female sex on CL (14.4% increase; the reference group is not labelled, presumably male). Screened and not retained (Table 2, PDF p4): age, race, smoking, dose, tablet strength, sex on V (dOBJ 15.1 on screening, only 1.05 after sex on CL), sex on VP1. Weight enters only through FFM allometry. Renal function not assessed (all normal). Fed status also delays absorption (0.203 h) and slows KTR (tablet/capsule -20.9%, MPC -54.9%).

## Caveats
Healthy volunteers only, mostly young; apparent parameters only (oral); between-study effects as large as 0.516 on F (studies 20, 70, 80) show study-to-study differences (assay or formulation); the bioequivalence power results (Tables 4-5) are simulation findings of no use to stanpumpR. Shiny app: isop.shinyapps.io/DoxycyclineSimulation.
