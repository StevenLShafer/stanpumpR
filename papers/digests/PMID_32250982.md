# Eley 2020: cefazolin in plasma and subcutaneous ISF of obese women at cesarean delivery

## Study
- 12 women, BMI > 35 at delivery (median 41.5, IQR 39.7-46.6), elective cesarean at >= 37 weeks under neuraxial anaesthesia, Royal Brisbane and Women's Hospital, Jul 2016 - Feb 2017. Age mean 32.8 (SD 4.8) y; weight at delivery mean 119.1 kg (range 93.7-150.0); 5 women > 120 kg; serum creatinine 53.5 (SD 11.2) umol/L; albumin 27.3 (SD 4.9) g/L (Table 1). Exclusion: creatinine > 70 umol/L.
- Cefazolin 2 g IV slow push within 30 min of incision (one woman 3 g).
- Plasma total and unbound (ultrafiltration, Centrifree, 37 C) at 0, 10, 30, 60, 90, 180 min; subcutaneous microdialysis (CMA 60) every 15-30 min; UHPLC-MS/MS; mean catheter recovery 26%.
- Pmetrics 1.5.0, nonparametric adaptive grid; Monte Carlo n = 1,000.

## Model and parameters

- Observed: minimum total plasma 31.7 mg/L with unbound 7.7 mg/L (same participant); maximum total 164.1, unbound 73.1 mg/L; minimum ISF 0.7 mg/L; 2 of 12 did not keep ISF > 2 mg/L.
- Assay validation fu: 15.0%, 19.1%, 54.6% at total 20, 100, 400 mg/L (spiked plasma).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- MIC90 of cefazolin for S. aureus taken as 2 mg/L (EUCAST); FTA over the EUCAST distribution to the ECOFF (0.125-2 mg/L).
- Pre-specified target: 95% FTA for unbound ISF concentration > 2 mg/L throughout 3 h of surgery. PTA target for simulations 100% fT>MIC.
- Result: 2 g plus 2 g redose at 2 h gave FTA > 95% at 90-150 kg; 3 g + 3 g at 2 h > 99%.

## Covariates
Weight at delivery on tissue (ISF) volume only.

## Caveats
n = 12; obese term pregnant women with low albumin; single centre; microdialysis recovery by retrodialysis of an internal standard; empirical binding model.
