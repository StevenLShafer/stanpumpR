# Soy 2003: enfuvirtide pediatric population PK and viral PD

## Study
- Source study: PACTG P1005, phase 1/2, open label, multicentre. 15 HIV-1-infected children enrolled (12 in part A, 3 more in part B); the PK analysis used 12 (part A) and 14 (part B), 11 in both (p. 570-571).
- Age median 8.2 y (range 4.0-12.1). Part A: 8 girls, 4 boys, mean weight 30 kg (12.5-76.2). Part B: 8 girls, 6 boys, mean 21.3 kg (12.5-76.2) (p. 572).
- Part A (intensive): single SC dose of 15, 30 or 60 mg/m2 (n=4 each) plus a separate IV dose of the same size at least 1 day later; plasma at 0, 0.5, 1, 2, 4, 6, 8, 12 h. Part B (sparse): 30 or 60 mg/m2 SC every 12 h, one random-timed sample per occasion (mostly troughs) on days 1, 3, 7, 10 and weeks 4, 16, 24, 48. Dose raised from 30 to 60 mg/m2 on day 7 if the 0.7 log10 RNA fall or trough >= 1000 ng/mL was not met (p. 570-571).
- Assay: plasma enfuvirtide by sandwich electrochemiluminescence immunoassay, LLOQ 5 ng/mL, QC 7.5/30/120 ng/mL, CV 2.5-11.4% (p. 571). Viral RNA by RT-PCR (Amplicor), LLOQ 50 copies/mL.
- Software NONMEM, first-order conditional estimation; covariates (weight, sex) kept at P<.05 by likelihood ratio.

## Model and parameters
One-compartment, first-order SC absorption and first-order elimination; two-compartment tested and not better (p. 572). Weight enters CL and V as power functions (appendix: mu_P = theta_P1 * WT^theta_P2). A sparse-sample (part B) multiplier on V (theta_V,3) and on residual error (theta_SP,3). Derived: ke = CL/V = 1.30/5.16 = 0.25 1/h, stated in the text (p. 575).
Exposure (steady state, MAP, mean +/- SD, p. 575): AUC 33.51 +/- 17.22 ug h/mL, Cmin 1.80 +/- 1.02 ug/mL, Cmax 3.46 +/- 1.76 ug/mL in these children, against 49 +/- 13, 2.7 +/- 0.8, 5.7 +/- 1.8 in adults on 90 mg SC twice daily (Zhang et al, ref 31).

Viral PD model (Table II, PDF p7, printed 575; appendix): one compartment, unit volume, zero-order viral input R for a time tau after drug start, first-order viral clearance CLv. Estimates (SE fraction): Av0 4.59 (0.23), tau 1.95 d (0.29), R 4.05 (0.41), CLv 0.44 mL/d (0.10); IIV 83, 54, 26 %; residual 16 %. Units of Av0 and R are printed as copies/mL and copies/mL per day (see open question).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- Not an opioid; no MEAC, ke0, tPeak or potency ratio.
- The end point is plasma HIV-1 RNA. No effect-site model, EC50, Emax or gamma was fitted.
- The design target was a 12-h trough above 1000 ng/mL, the "expected effective concentration" taken from preclinical data (p. 570). Three of 15 subjects on 30 mg/m2 missed it by day 7; all reached it on 60 mg/m2 (p. 578).
- No exposure-response relationship was significant (AUC, tmax, Cmin, Cmax against CLv, tau, RNA change, time below baseline, success/failure); trends were in the expected direction only (p. 573, 577-578).

## Covariates
Weight on CL and V (power 0.44 and 0.43). Sex tried, not retained. Time on therapy was not significant (no induction or inhibition). An offset for sparse versus intensive samples on V and on residual error.

## Caveats
- n=14; model misfits early IV concentrations in the highest mg/kg children (p. 573, 577); authors suspect noncompliance inflates V in the sparse data.
- The pediatric model extrapolated to 70 kg exceeds the adult two-compartment values of Zhang et al (Fig 4); authors caution against allometric extrapolation (p. 575).
- Pediatric CL/F 1.42 L/h conflicts with 0.95 L/h from the noncompartmental Bellibas abstract (p. 575).
