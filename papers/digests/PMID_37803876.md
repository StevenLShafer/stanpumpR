# Chan 2023: nirmatrelvir/ritonavir integrated population PK

## Study
Pooled population PK of nirmatrelvir given with ritonavir 100 mg, from 8 studies (7 phase I with rich sampling, 1 phase II/III EPIC-HR with sparse sampling), Table 1 (PDF p3-5). N = 1237 adults (1087 COVID-19, 150 healthy; 87.9% from phase II/III), 5149 plasma samples; 745 samples (14.5%) were BLQ (LLOQ 10 ng/mL) and were excluded (p6, p7). Demographics (Table 2, PDF p7): median weight 79.4 kg (42.0-158), median age 45 y (18-86), median baseline nCLCR 119 mL/min/1.73 m2 (15.8-318); 53.1% male. Formulations: suspension, 100 mg tablet, 150 mg tablet. Assay: plasma LC-MS/MS (total drug). Software: NONMEM 7.5.0, PsN 5.2.6 (SCM, pcVPC, SIR), R 4.1.1. Covariate selection: forward alpha 0.05, backward alpha 0.001.

## Model and parameters
Two-compartment, first-order absorption and elimination, oral. Allometric scaling on baseline weight normalised to 70 kg, fixed exponents 0.75 (CL, Q) and 1 (V2, V3). Typical values are at 300 mg dose, 70 kg, age 45 y, nCLCR at or above 70 mL/min/1.73 m2 (Table 3, PDF p10, read on the page image). CL, V and Q are apparent (oral dose, F1 fixed to 1 at 300 mg).

Dose-effect, formulation and COVID-19 effects partly confound (150 mg tablet was only given to healthy volunteers as a single dose), as the authors note (p9). All eta-shrinkage above 30% because most data were sparse.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD model, no ke0. The only target is the in vitro EC90 of 292 ng/mL (SARS-CoV-2 replication inhibition; plasma total concentration), used as a trough target: at least 90% of simulated subjects with Cmin at or above it. Not an opioid; no MEAC or potency ratio. Safety margin: highest observed concentration 26,400 ng/mL (750 mg x 3 doses, healthy) and 16,100 ng/mL in EPIC-HR (p9); 90th percentile predicted Cmax under 10,000 ng/mL.

Simulated Cmin (Table 4, PDF p11, read on the page image), 5000 subjects per group, with ritonavir 100 mg b.i.d.: adults at least 40 kg normal renal function, 300 mg: Day 1 median 827 ng/mL (10th 445, 90th 1313), 96.7% at or above EC90; Day 5 median 1417 (593; 2731), 98%. Mild renal impairment 300 mg: Day 5 median 1478. Moderate 150 mg: Day 5 median 1839, 99.7%. Pediatric 12 to under 18 y, at least 40 kg: 300 mg Day 5 median 2177 (ratio to adult 1.54); 150 mg Day 5 1445 (1.02).

## Covariates
Weight (allometric, structural), nCLCR on CL (breakpoint model), carbamazepine and itraconazole on CL, COVID-19 on CL, age on V2, dose on F1, formulation on F1. Sex, race, BMI, hepatic impairment tested; not retained in the final model (p8-9). Severe renal impairment (n = 10) could not be dosed.

## Caveats
Phase II/III sparse data with many BLQ samples and 139% residual error; V3 and IIV V2 lie just outside the SIR CIs (p9); BLQ handled by exclusion, not M3; ritonavir exposure not modelled (nirmatrelvir PK is boosted-state). Authors are Pfizer employees.
