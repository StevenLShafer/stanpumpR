# Wan 1978: amphetamine enantiomer kinetics and the effect of urinary pH

## Study
4 healthy adults (3 M, 1 F), 22-26 y, 48-65 kg. Oral amphetamine HCl, 10 mg base, fasted, four occasions >= 2 wk
apart:
1. 10 mg racemic with urine alkalinised (NaHCO3 2 g q4h from the day before);
2. 10 mg racemic with urine acidified (enteric-coated NH4Cl 2 g q4h);
3. 10 mg (+)-amphetamine, alkaline urine;
4. 10 mg (-)-amphetamine, alkaline urine.
Plasma and saliva to ~48 h; enantioselective GC-chemical-ionisation MS (chiral derivatisation), deuterated IS.
One-compartment fits (NONLIN, weight 1/C^2) to the post-absorptive decay. Protein binding by ultrafiltration.

Absorption complete by 4-6 h; peaks dose-proportional; urinary pH did not change rate or extent of absorption.
Isomer t1/2 difference 39% (alkaline, p < 0.05) and 13% (acid, p < 0.05); no isomer interaction (Studies 1 vs
3/4 not different). Plasma (+)/(-) ratio ~1.00 early, falling to 0.66 at 48 h (alkaline; Table II).
Saliva/plasma ratio post-absorption 2.76 (SE 0.12) vs predicted 2.21 (pKa 10, saliva pH 6.98).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None measured.

## Covariates
Urinary pH (by design); no covariate model.

## Caveats
n = 4 young adults; urinary pH forced to extremes; 10 mg base, not mixed salts; no IV data, so V is apparent.
