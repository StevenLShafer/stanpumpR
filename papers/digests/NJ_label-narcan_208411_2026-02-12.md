# NARCAN Nasal Spray OTC carton 2026: Drug Facts only

**Source file problem.** The archived PDF is not the NARCAN prescribing information: PDF pp. 1-3 are OTC carton
and Drug Facts artwork and p. 4 is the FDA signature page (confirmed on page images 2026-10-14). It contains no
pharmacokinetics, so no PK number in this card comes from it. The 4 mg nasal values the repo attributes to "the
label" are checked instead against the archived FDA clinical pharmacology review of the same NDA,
`NJ_fda-review-naloxone_208411_208411Orig1s000ClinPharmR.pdf` (digest `NJ_fda-review-naloxone_208411_208411Orig1s000ClinPharmR.md`), PDF p. 17 = printed p. 11, Table 2.4.1b (study Naloxone-Ph1a-002, healthy
subjects, n = 29, geometric mean (CV%)). That table supports AUC 7.9 ng.h/mL, Cmax 4.8 ng/mL and relative
bioavailability 47% vs IM for 4 mg; it does not support the upper figure 0.52, which stays unverified. The repo's
wording "label" is therefore backed by the review, not by an archived label.
Since 2026-10-27 the original NARCAN PI is archived as `NJ_label-narcan_208411_2015-11`: its Table 1 (PDF p10) prints the same Cmax 4.83 ng/mL, AUC 7.87/7.95 h.ng/mL and relative BA 46.7% (43.9% for 8 mg), so it verifies those values; it prints no 0.52 either.

## Study
Not a study and not a prescribing information document. This is the OTC Drug Facts / carton labeling for NARCAN 4 mg nasal spray (4 mg naloxone hydrochloride in 0.1 mL per single-dose device). Content: uses (emergency treatment of opioid overdose), lay directions (check, give 1st dose in one nostril, call 911, wait 2-3 min, repeat a dose every 2-3 min until the person wakes, stay), storage 2-25 C, inactive ingredients (benzalkonium chloride, edetate disodium, HCl, NaCl, water).

## Verification against stanpumpR
Nothing below is verified from the archived carton file; the evidence is the FDA review `NJ_fda-review-naloxone_208411_208411Orig1s000ClinPharmR.pdf`, PDF p. 17 (printed p. 11), Table 2.4.1b, read on the page image 2026-10-14. Lines re-checked on master 781013d.
- AUC near 7.9 ng.h/mL after 4 mg (R/drugs_naloxone.R:47-48; inst/help/drugs/naloxone.md:9): review AUCt 7.9 (CV 37%), AUCinf 8.0 h.ng/mL, geometric means, n = 29. **matches** (the review, not a label). Arithmetic (derived): 4 mg / 8.0 ng.h/mL = 500 L/h (4 mg / 7.9 = 506 L/h), the code's "CL/F about 500 L/h" if the dose is taken as the hydrochloride (as base, about 3.6 mg, 460 L/h).
- 0.47-0.52 relative-to-IM bioavailability (R/drugs_naloxone.R:49-50; naloxone.md:9): review gives 47% (4 mg, one spray) and 44% (8 mg) dose-normalized vs IM. **0.47 matches; 0.52 unverified**: it is not in Table 2.4.1b. The review's study synopsis (PDF p. 26 = printed p. 20) gives a 43.9% to 53.6% range across four nasal treatments that include 2 mg and a 20 mg/mL formulation, so 0.52 is not a value printed for the marketed 4 mg spray in any archived file.
- Label peak about 4.8 ng/mL (tests/testthat/test-drugs-naloxone.R:112): review Cmax 4.8 ng/mL (CV 43%) after 4 mg. **matches** (the review).
