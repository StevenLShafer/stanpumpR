# Weerink 2019: remifentanil-dexmedetomidine interaction (sedation, laryngoscopy)

## Study
Three-phase crossover in 30 healthy volunteers (18-67 yr, 49.2-98.3 kg, 158-193 cm, BMI 18.2-28.7; 5 men + 5 women in each of 3 age strata 18-34, 35-49, 50-70), p. 1005, 1008. Session 1: dexmedetomidine TCI, effect-site targets 1, 3, 4, 5, 8 ng/mL (Hannivoort PK with Colin MOAA/S ke0; the highest target was lowered to 6 in the interim analysis). Session 2: remifentanil TCI (Eleveld PK-PD), effect-site targets 1, 2, 3, 5, 7 ng/mL, then after washout dexmedetomidine 2 ng/mL plus remifentanil steps 0.5-4.0 ng/mL ("interaction phase"). Arterial samples at pseudo-steady state; plasma (EDTA), UHPLC-MS, LLOQ 0.05 ng/mL, upper limit 20 ng/mL, both drugs (p. 1007). Effects: MOAA/S-derived binary end points (no response to name, tolerance of shake-and-shout, tolerance of trapezius squeeze, tolerance of laryngoscopy) and Patient State Index (PSI-2). 464 steady-state observation sets. NONMEM; binary end points by naive pooling (no IIV), PSI by FOCE-I with IIV.

## Model and parameters
Binary end points: final model is a Hill-type logistic in dexmedetomidine concentration alone (gamma fixed to 1; remifentanil and the interaction terms dropped), P = (C/EC50)/(1 + C/EC50) (Eq. 3); the full hierarchical model had overparametrised opioid gamma and EC50 (p. 1010). Age covariate: EC50D = EC50Dtyp x exp(-0.048 x (age - 35)) (Eq. 4); Table 2 (PDF p. 9; EC50 is for a 35-year-old per the footnote).
PSI: hierarchical interaction model, PSI = Base x (1 - Emax x U^gD/(1 + U^gD)), U = (CD/EC50D) x (1 + CR/EC50R)^gR (Eqs. 5-6, p. 1011; text layer scrambles the equation, form here from Eq. 1 on p. 1007 and the Results text; remifentanil has no effect without dexmedetomidine).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- Opioid audit (remifentanil): no MEAC / minimum effective or analgesic concentration reported. No ke0, t1/2ke0, tPeak or hysteresis estimated: the TCI used an Eleveld remifentanil effect-site model (value not printed here) and the PD fits used steady-state plasma concentrations, with washout/recovery data excluded because plasma and effect site are not in equilibrium (p. 1015). No equianalgesic or potency ratio reported. Remifentanil EC50 on PSI = 1.6 ng/mL (Table 2, plasma, PSI end point, only in the presence of dexmedetomidine under the hierarchical model); remifentanil alone did not change probability of any of the four binary responses (p. 1010-1012).
- Dexmedetomidine: EC50 values above are for 35-year-olds on the four binary end points; PSI EC50 0.49 ng/mL. Age lowers EC50 (e.g. at 2.5 ng/mL, predicted P(no response to name) 83% at 65 yr vs 36% at 20 yr, p. 1015). The ke0 used in the TCI is Colin's MOAA/S ke0 (value not printed in this paper).
- Laryngoscopy: dexmedetomidine phase 34 attempts in 22 subjects, tolerated by 13 subjects; interaction phase 43 attempts in 19 subjects, tolerated by 15 (p. 1008).
- TCI accuracy (p. 1010): dexmedetomidine median PE 27% (dex phase) and 5.7% (interaction), MDAPE 34% and 20%; remifentanil MDPE 3.6% and 35%, MDAPE 32% and 45%.

## Covariates
Age on dexmedetomidine EC50 (above). Height, weight, sex were tested and not retained (p. 1008). No covariates retained on PSI base or EC50.

## Caveats
Healthy volunteers; supraclinical dexmedetomidine to 6-8 ng/mL with TCI under-predicting at the top target; naive pooling for binary models; analgesia not assessed directly and authors say interaction on analgesia remains unclear (p. 1015); possible PK interaction (dexmedetomidine reducing remifentanil clearance) suggested by higher remifentanil prediction error in the interaction phase.
