# Shafer 2008: Mixed effect modeling in analgesia trials (editorial)

## Study
Two-page editorial (PDF pp. 1-2; printed pp. 9-10) on two papers in the same issue: Aasvang et al. (capsaicin wound instillation, postherniotomy pain; Anesth Analg 2008;107:282-91) and Zeidan et al. (intra-articular tramadol plus bupivacaine after arthroscopic knee surgery; 107:292-9). No new data.

## Model and parameters
No PK model. The editorial says NONMEM was used for both trials, with the analgesic response modelled as a simple decrement in VAS score persisting over several days.
```yaml
parameters: []  # editorial, no parameters
```

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Not an opioid paper. No MEAC, ke0, t1/2ke0, tPeak or potency ratio. Only quantitative statement: capsaicin gave about 50% reduction in VAS score, P < 0.0001 once inter-individual variability was modelled (printed p. 10); the original traditional analysis showed a non-significant trend.

## Covariates
None.

## Caveats
Opinion piece. Author discloses teaching a NONMEM workshop. Argues mixed-effect models should be the standard for analgesic trials.
