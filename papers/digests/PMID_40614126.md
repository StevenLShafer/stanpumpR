# Sun 2025: maribavir population PK and exposure-response

## Study
- Update of a prior maribavir popPK model (Song 2024, ref 22) with new data: a phase 1 study in healthy Japanese and non-Hispanic white adults (NCT05319353) and the randomised double-blind phase 3 AURORA trial (NCT02927067) in hematopoietic cell transplant (HCT) recipients with a first asymptomatic CMV infection (PDF p. 2).
- 7431 concentrations from 930 people: 206 healthy volunteers (4231 records) and 724 transplant patients with CMV (3200 records); 297 BLQ values (3.5% after dosing), all excluded (M1 method) (PDF p. 4). Table 1 (PDF p. 5): weight mean 74.2 kg (SD 17.4), median 73.0 (36.1-141); 59% male; 77% Caucasian; 82% aged 18 to <65 y; one person <18 y.
- Dose in the exposure simulations: maribavir 400 mg twice daily, oral. Assay: plasma by LC-MS (different LLOQs in phase 1 vs phase 2/3). NONMEM 7.5.1, IMPMAP estimation.
- Exposure-response used 238 AURORA maribavir-arm patients (PDF p. 4).

## Model and parameters
Two-compartment, first-order absorption with lag, first-order elimination; additive plus proportional error. Parameters are apparent oral (/F). Table 2, PDF p. 7 (printed 1352), confirmed on the rendered page. Typical values are for a 70 kg person without covariates; covariates multiply (formulas are in the table note). Maribavir is about 98% bound to plasma protein (intro, PDF p. 3); whether concentrations are total is not stated beyond "plasma".

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- No effect-site model; no ke0, tPeak or MEAC. Not an opioid.
- Exposure-response (AURORA, AUCss, Cmax,ss, Cmin,ss): flat for the primary end point (confirmed CMV viremia clearance at week 8; 178 of 238, 74.8%) and the key secondary (maintained to week 16; 136 of 238, 57.1%); logistic p = 0.632 and 0.131 against AUCss quartiles (Figure 3, text layer). Quartile AUCss range 69.0-817 ug*h/mL.
- Safety: nausea and vomiting rise with AUCss (nausea p = 0.0394, vomiting p = 0.00296; Figure 4). No therapeutic or toxic concentration range is stated; dose 400 mg BID is supported.

## Covariates
Weight (allometric, estimated exponents 0.301 on CL and Q, 0.536 on volumes, not the fixed 0.75/1), strong CYP3A4 inhibitors and inducers on CL/F, CMV status on CL/F (32% lower than in other people, comparator: healthy volunteers and non-CMV participants in this dataset), dose on Ka, PPI on F and Ka. No effect of age, sex, race, transplant type, refractory vs first-line (Figure 2, PDF pp. 8-9). Underweight geometric mean AUCss 1.17 x normal weight.

## Caveats
Weight range includes one participant <18 y, so pediatric use is untested. Q/F and Vp/F IIV are large and shrinkage about 55%. The covariate exponents are derived from this adult dataset.
