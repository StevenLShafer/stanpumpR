# Yue 1991: codeine and seven metabolites in debrisoquine EM and PM

## Study
- Single dose: 14 healthy Caucasians, 8 extensive (EM) and 6 poor (PM) hydroxylators of debrisoquine (PM: metabolic ratio > 12.6). EM mean age 33, weight 66 ± 10 kg; PM age 30, weight 69 ± 11 kg (Table 1).
- 50 mg codeine phosphate (123 µmol) oral, fasted. Blood at 0.17, 0.33, 0.5, 0.67, 1, 1.5, 2, 3, 4, 5, 7, 9, 11 and 24 h; urine to 48 h.
- Multiple-dose substudy: 3 EM + 3 PM, 50 mg every 6 h for nine doses.
- Ion-pair HPLC for codeine, C6G, NC, NCG, M, M3G, M6G, NM.
- Codeine AUC by linear trapezoid, extrapolated to infinity with the terminal slope; CL = dose/AUC (complete absorption assumed); partial clearances from urinary recovery/AUC.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- No PD measured. No ke0, tPeak or hysteresis.
- MEAC cited, not measured: mean minimum effective plasma morphine concentration for pain after abdominal surgery 16 ng/mL (42.7 nmol/L), Dahlström 1982 (Discussion p.640); plasma morphine after codeine is far below it.
- Potency ratios cited from the literature (Discussion): M6G about twice morphine subcutaneously in mice, about 90-fold intracerebroventricularly and 650-fold intrathecally (Paul 1989); normorphine about one-fourth as potent as morphine for postoperative pain in man (Lasagna & de Kornfeld 1958), equipotent intrathecally in rats.

## Covariates
Debrisoquine phenotype (CYP2D6): no effect on codeine AUC, t½ or CL; large effect on O-demethylated metabolites.

## Caveats
- Small groups (8 EM, 6 PM); oral only, so CL is CL/F with F assumed 1.
- M AUC very variable (SD equal to mean) and undetectable in five of six PM.

## Verification against stanpumpR
- **Morphine:codeine AUC ratio 0.020** (`R/drugs_codeine.R:69, 102`; `codeine.md:19`): the paper does not print 0.020; it is the ratio of the EM mean AUCs in Table 2 (20/1010, derived, molar), consistent with the text's "50 times". **Matches** as a derived value, but it is EM only (n = 8), molar rather than mass (0.019 by mass), and a ratio of means with an SD as large as the morphine mean.
- **"whose AUCs are truncated at six hours"** (`R/drugs_codeine.R:109-110`) and "both truncated" (`tests/testthat/test-drugs-codeine.R:181`): Yue sampled to 24 h and extrapolated the codeine AUC to infinity. Only Shah 1990 was truncated at 6 h. **Discrepancy**. It also undercuts the code's comparison, which uses a model AUC ratio over the first six hours against Yue's full-length ratio.
- **No clearance difference EM vs PM** (`R/drugs_codeine.R:198-200`; test lines 113-115): Table 1, 2.10 ± 0.84 vs 1.92 ± 0.63 L/h/kg, not significant. **Matches**.
- **"The CYP2D6 branch is only about 3% of codeine clearance"** (`R/drugs_codeine.R:197-198`): this is the stanpumpR formation fraction (Ashraf, rescaled), not Yue's. Yue's EM O-demethylation partial clearance is about 5.7% of oral clearance (derived) and 6% of the dose is O-demethylated. Not attributed to Yue, so not a citation error, but the paper puts the branch at roughly twice the code's value.
- **"two studies of eight and ten subjects"** (`R/drugs_codeine.R:109`): Yue's ratio is from the 8 EM. **Matches**.
- **Citation** (`R/drugs_codeine.R:135-136`): **matches**.
