# Wigal 2009: 13-hour laboratory classroom study of lisdexamfetamine in children

## Study
Phase IIIb, multicentre (7 US sites), NCT00500149. Children 6-12 y with ADHD (all combined subtype at
baseline). Open-label dose optimisation of lisdexamfetamine (LDX) 30, 50 or 70 mg/d over 4 weeks,
then randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled 2-way crossover (1 week each). 129 enrolled, 117
randomised, 113 ITT, 111 completed. Safety population mean age 10.1 (SD 1.5) y, mean weight 72.8
(17.3) lb (derived: about 33.0 kg), 76.0% male. Final doses in the randomised population: 30 mg
n = 46, 50 mg n = 50, 70 mg n = 21. Dosing at 7 AM; SKAMP and PERMP at 0.5 h predose and 1.5, 2.5,
5, 7.5, 10, 12 and 13 h postdose on laboratory-school days.

## Model and parameters
None. **No pharmacokinetic samples were collected** (Discussion). The Discussion quotes, from other
studies, d-amphetamine 86.7 ng/mL at 12 h after 70 mg in children, and in adults intact LDX Tmax 1.1 h
and d-amphetamine Tmax 3.7 h.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End points: SKAMP deportment (primary), SKAMP attention, PERMP attempted/correct, CGI. LDX better than
placebo at every postdose time from 1.5 h through 13.0 h (P < .005). LS-mean SKAMP-D difference vs
placebo -0.45 (95% CI -0.62, -0.28) at 1.5 h and -0.26 (-0.43, -0.08) at 13 h. In the LDX group alone,
improvement vs predose was significant at all times except 12 and 13 h. No concentration-effect
analysis, no EC50, no threshold.

## Covariates
None analysed for PK. Dose-group summaries only.

## Caveats
No PK; the effect window is a group comparison against placebo, not a concentration. Predose scores
worse in the LDX group (possible carry-over). Analogue classroom; children only; Shire-sponsored.
