# Chan 2008: population PK meta-analysis of oral maraviroc

## Study
- Pooled NONMEM meta-analysis of 17 Pfizer Phase 1/2a studies (Table 1, PDF p. 3 / printed p. 78): 15 in healthy
  volunteers (n = 365) and 2 in asymptomatic HIV-infected subjects (n = 48); 413 subjects, 690 profiles,
  8951 plasma concentrations (929 from HIV+ subjects) (PDF pp. 2, 5).
- Age median 30 y (18-54), weight median 71 kg (46-109) (Table 2); 317 male (76.8%), 95 Asian (23.0%), 302 White,
  14 Black (Table 3).
- Oral maraviroc tablets, single or multiple doses, 100-1800 mg/day (max 900 mg twice daily); fasted and fed
  (high-fat meal) conditions; 46/690 profiles (6.7%) fed. Drug-interaction studies contributed only maraviroc-alone
  or placebo arms.
- Sampling to 48 h (one study 24 h) after single doses; after first and last multiple doses to >= 12 h plus troughs.
- Assay: solid-phase extraction, LC-MS/MS, LLOQ 0.5 ng/mL, same method in all 17 studies (PDF p. 2).
- NONMEM V level 1.1, ADVAN4 TRANS4, FOCE, log-transformed data; 1000-run nonparametric bootstrap; VPC.

## Model and parameters
Two-compartment disposition, first-order absorption with lag. Bioavailability partitioned (Eq. 1-4, PDF p. 3-4):
F = FABS * FHEP; CL_H = FQ * EH (FQ hepatic plasma flow fixed 59.59 L/h, from hepatic blood flow 101 L/h and
blood:plasma ratio 0.59); FHEP = 1 - EH; CL = CL_H + CL_R with CL_R fixed 12 L/h. FABS = ABSEmax * Dose^g /
(ED50^g + Dose^g), ABSEmax fixed 1 (fasted). ka = ka1mg * Dose^theta_ka. All dose nonlinearity is put on absorption;
disposition is linear.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No concentration-effect, ke0 or target concentration reported. Not an opioid paper.

## Covariates
- Asian race (vs all non-Asians): on EH (F up 17.7%, AUC up 26.5% vs typical non-Asian), Vp and CLic.
- Age on CLic (power, reference 30 y); no effect on AUC.
- Dose on FABS (sigmoid Emax) and on ka (power); food (high-fat meal) on ABSEmax, ED50 and ka.
- Weight, sex, HIV status: no influence found (weight not used; no allometry).

## Caveats
- Internal inconsistency: the text (PDF p. 6) gives ka 0.277 1/h as the value for a 100-mg fasted dose, but Table 5
  gives 0.614 1/h at 100 mg; 0.277 * 100^0.173 = 0.614 (derived), so 0.277 is ka at 1 mg (ka1mg, Eq. 5).
- The functional form of the race effects on Vp and CLic is not printed; exp() reproduces the EH effect in Table 5.
  Asian Vp would be 277*exp(-0.637) = 146 L if exponential (derived, unconfirmed).
- No IV data: CL_R, FQ and blood:plasma ratio are fixed from an unpublished mass-balance analysis; absolute F
  depends on those fixed values (sensitivity runs: parameters changed by at most 16%).
- Mostly healthy, male, single-agent data; no interacting antiretrovirals included. Data from doses >= 100 mg only.
- Authors are Pfizer employees/consultants.
