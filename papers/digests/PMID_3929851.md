# Curry and Aburawi 1985: review of nitroglycerin analysis, disposition and kinetics

## Study
Narrative review (University of Florida), no new data. Covers assays, stability, infusion-set losses, metabolism,
IV, sublingual, oral and transdermal kinetics, and the sources of conflict among published plasma data.

The review concludes that IV doses in older studies were likely lower than calculated and that sets that do not
interact may make this "a problem of the past" for new studies (PDF p8). It also cites Wei and Reid (1.6 ng/mL in
MI patients at 37.5-175 ug/min) and Imhof (0.46 and 0.81 ng/mL at 34 and 75 ug/min) (PDF p21), and posture and
liver blood flow as possible sources of variability (PDF p29-30).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No concentration-effect parameters relevant here.

## Covariates
Posture, liver blood flow and dose dependence discussed qualitatively.

## Caveats
Secondary source; bench (in vitro) losses, not patient exposure. The infusion-set figures come from 1979-1983 studies
of sets and bags no longer typical.
