# Hornuss 2007: propofol in expired air by IMR-MS during TIVA

## Study
Pilot study at the University of Munich, neurosurgery. (1) Venous blood from 5 healthy volunteers (mean age 38.0 SD 6.8 yr, 3 F) spiked with propofol 0, 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2, 2.5, 5, 10 ug/mL; headspace measured at 37 C. (2) Off-line headspace above arterial blood in 6 patients (A-F, ASA II, age 26-75 yr, 63-95 kg; Table 1). (3) On-line end-expiratory measurement in 11 patients (A-K, ASA I-III) through a T piece, with CO2 used to define end-expiration. Propofol was given by TCI with the Marsh model (ref 5), target 6 ug/mL at induction then 2.5-4.0 ug/mL, with sufentanil and remifentanil 0.5 ug/kg/min. Blood sampled at TCI targets 2.5, 3.0, 3.5, 4.0 and again 2.5 ug/mL after 20-30 min at each. Whole-blood propofol by LC-MS/MS (dansyl derivative; LOD 85 ng/mL, LOQ 297 ng/mL). Breath by ion-molecule reaction MS, measuring intact propofol (m/z 178) and a 163 fragment. Sevoflurane interfered with m/z 178 but not 163 (Fig 2).

## Model and parameters
No PK model. Signals were uncalibrated counts, standardised to mercury ion counts (dimensionless).
- Spiked blood: Pearson r 0.982-0.990 (m/z 163) and 0.825-0.987 (m/z 178) (Table 2).
- Off-line: r 0.846-0.957 (163), n = 6 (Table 3).
- On-line vs measured whole blood, individual r: 0.784-0.985 (163); 0.541-0.995 (178) (Table 3).
- Pooled regression across 11 patients, 49 measurements: r = 0.850, r2 = 0.723; Pblood (ug/mL) = 0.164 + 129.92 x Pexpiratory (standardised counts) (Fig 5, PDF p8; confirmed on image).
- Mean intraindividual r: TCI target vs measured blood 0.873 SD 0.089; breath 163 vs blood 0.938 SD 0.055.
- Response: changes of about 0.5 ug/mL in blood detectable within about 90 s of an infusion change (Fig 6: measured whole blood 3.5, 4.1, 4.9, 5.4 then 3.9 ug/mL).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No pharmacodynamics. No MEAC, ke0, tPeak or potency ratio. The only temporal information is the roughly 90 s breath response after TCI changes (Fig 6), measured against whole-blood propofol; it is not an effect-site equilibration constant.

## Covariates
None modelled. The authors list lung disease, V/Q mismatch, tidal volume and pulmonary metabolism as untested influences.

## Caveats
Uncalibrated (arbitrary units), so only within-patient relative change is supported; patient E had high headspace counts, probably from probe overheating. Small n. Whole blood, not plasma. The relation between TCI target and measured concentration was weaker than breath vs measured blood. The study was funded by the instrument's maker, and three authors have financial interests in the system.
