# Schüttler 2000: population PK of propofol (multicenter)

## Study
NONMEM V population analysis of 4,112 propofol concentrations from 270 individuals (150 M, 120 F; age 2-88 yr, weight 12-100 kg; 256 surgical patients, 14 volunteers) pooled from five groups (Schüttler, Cockshott, Glass, White, Shafer; Table 1, PDF p2). Bolus or computer-controlled infusion; arterial (groups 1, 2, 3, 6) and venous (4, 5, 7-9) sampling; sampling periods 0.25-24 h. Assay: HPLC (groups 1-6, 9) or GLC (7, 8); whole blood in groups 1-8, plasma in group 9. Data split at random into two 135-subject halves: model built on the first, tested on the second (MWR -0.4 %, MAWR 25.5 %). Interindividual variability log-normal; residual error constant CV. Covariate retained if objective function fell by at least 7.8 (P < 0.005).

## Model and parameters
Three-compartment, linear, elimination from the central compartment. Final model (Table 3, PDF p6, printed 732; confirmed on the rendered page). Weight BW in kg, age in yr; ven = 1 for venous samples, bol = 1 for bolus data (both 0 for arterial infusion data).
- Cl1 = θ1·(BW/70)^θ7 if age ≤ 60; θ1·(BW/70)^θ7 − (age − 60)·θ10 if age > 60
- Cl2 = θ3·(BW/70)^θ8·(1 + ven·θ14)·(1 + bol·θ16)
- Cl3 = θ5·(BW/70)^θ11·(1 + bol·θ18)
- V1 = θ2·(BW/70)^θ12·(age/30)^θ13·(1 + bol·θ15)
- V2 = θ4·(BW/70)^θ9·(1 + bol·θ17)
- V3 = θ6 (no covariate)

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No ke0, EC50, tPeak or effect measure. Simulations (infusion rates for 1 µg/mL over 2 h; context-sensitive half-times, Figs 5-7) are PK only. Not an opioid paper: no MEAC/potency audit applies.

## Covariates
Weight enters Cl1, Cl2, Cl3, V1, V2 as a power below 1 (0.55-0.75), so per-kg parameters rise as weight falls. V3 independent of weight and age. Cl1 falls linearly with age above 60. V1 falls with age, (age/30)^−0.39. Sampling site: only Cl2 lower in venous samples. Administration mode: bolus data had larger V1, V2, Cl2 and smaller Cl3 (Fig 8: infusion kinetics over-predict the first 10 min after a bolus and under-predict the next 4 h). No gender effect. Lean body mass not tested (heights unavailable for all subjects).

## Caveats
Authors' own: V1 is smaller than in other adult studies and poorly determined (little early data, mostly bolus); model under-predicts measured concentrations above about 8 µg/mL (possible nonlinear PK or incomplete mixing, Fig 3); assay mixed (blood vs plasma, HPLC vs GLC); age effects valid only over 2-88 yr (Cl1 would reach zero near 90 yr); interindividual CV stays 37-55 %. Weight power functions may partly absorb age effects across the child-adult range. Combined children and adults. Note that the bolus factors (θ15-θ18) mean a single parameter set fits infusion or bolus, not both.
