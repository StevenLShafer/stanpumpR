# Simon 2006: ropivacaine epidural absorption and IV (D3-tracer) disposition by age group

## Study
- 24 ASA I-II surgical patients (22 M / 2 F) in three age groups: 18-40 y (n = 5), 41-60 y (n = 10),
  >= 61 y (n = 9); overall age median 54 (20-80) y, weight median 80 (61-100) kg, height 180 cm (Table 1).
  Group 3: age 74 (61-80) y, 8 M / 1 F, weight median 85 (61-100) kg, height 178 cm (re-aligned Table 1).
- Epidural L3-4: 15 mL ropivacaine 1.0% (150 mg HCl) at 1 mL/s; temazepam premedication; no GA.
- 25 min later: ~50 mL of 0.44 mg/mL D3-ropivacaine IV at 5 mL/min (foot vein), i.e. a ~10-min infusion of
  ~22 mg (derived).
- Arterial samples to 24 h; total plasma ropivacaine and D3-ropivacaine by LC-MS/MS (AstraZeneca); LOQ 2.74
  and 2.77 ng/mL.
- Disposition: 2- or 3-exponential fits to D3 data (WinNonlin 4.1; 3-compartment in 20, 2 in 3, 1 in 1
  patient). Absorption by deconvolution; bi-exponential cumulative-fraction-absorbed fit (two parallel
  first-order processes). Results are group summaries of individual estimates (no population model).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None (no effect or toxicity model). Total, not unbound, concentrations.

## Covariates
Age group (ANOVA): longer t1/2 and lower CL in >= 61 y; larger F1 in the youngest vs middle group. No
covariate model.

## Caveats
- Small groups (n = 5 in the youngest); 22 of 24 men.
- Disposition structure varied between patients (1-3 compartments); group means of parameters from mixed
  structures do not form a single fitted model.
- The D3 tracer was given during ongoing epidural absorption (deconvolution assumption of linearity).
