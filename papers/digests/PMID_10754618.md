# Ericsson 2000: clevidipine arterial PK after 20-min and 24-h infusions, arteriovenous differences

## Study
- Open study (Methods, p994). 15 healthy men were randomised (age 28.3 ± 4.7 y, weight 77.8 ± 6.0 kg) and
  **12 completed**. The randomisation was to three groups of four:
  - **20-min infusion** at 7 nmol/kg/min.
  - **24-h infusion, low:** escalated from 0.5 to 4.0 nmol/kg/min in 0.5 steps every 15 min, then 2 nmol/kg/min
    after 120 min.
  - **24-h infusion, high:** escalated from 0.5 to 7.0 in 0.5 steps every 10 min, then 7 nmol/kg/min.
- Emulsion of 0.3 or 1.0 mg/mL in 20% Intralipid.
- Paired radial arterial and forearm venous samples (venous from the arm opposite the infusion). For safety, the
  arterial catheter was removed during the 24-h infusion and reinserted for the last 30 min.
- 1 mL blood into 10% SDS within 15 s. GC-MS assay, LOQ 0.1 nM.
- Bi- and triexponential models were fitted to each subject's **arterial** data in WinNonlin, weighted 1/Cpred².
  Decrement times were simulated from the 24-h, 7 nmol/kg/min parameters.

## Model and parameters

  Css = 2.13 + 13.38x, R² = 0.960 (Fig 2, p4, rendered).

Other results:
- **Decrement times:** under 1 min to a 50% fall, at any infusion duration, and about 5 min to a 90% fall (Fig 5).
- **Arteriovenous difference:**
  - Arterial concentrations reach steady state within 2 min, venous ones in about 10 min.
  - At steady state, arterial concentrations are **about twice venous**, during 20-min and 24-h infusions alike.
  - After the stop, arterial concentrations fall to, or briefly below, venous ones within about 1 min.
- The authors expect clearance to differ about twofold between arterial and venous sampling, which explains the
  higher venous-based clearances of earlier studies.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- After the 20-min infusion (Finapres), MAP fell from about 90 to 75 mmHg and HR rose from about 53 to 75 bpm.
  Both returned to baseline within 10 min of stopping.
- Effect against venous concentration showed **clockwise** hysteresis, and against arterial concentration
  **counterclockwise** hysteresis, with a short delay (Fig 8, one subject).
- No ke0 or effect-compartment model was fitted. The authors say the data did not allow one.
- The 24-h Finapres data are not reported (cuff discomfort).

## Covariates
None.

## Caveats
- n = 4 per group, healthy young men.
- Enantiomers were not separated (a non-enantioselective assay). The authors cite essentially identical
  enantiomer PK.
- **Source inconsistency (rendered p4 and p5):** the Results text gives the arterial mean clearance range as
  0.066-0.075 L/kg/min, but the Table 1 group means run 0.066-0.072. The Fig 2 slope, 13.38 nmol/L per
  nmol/kg/min, implies about 0.075 (derived: 1/13.38), but the text does not say that is the source of 0.075.
  Unresolved.
