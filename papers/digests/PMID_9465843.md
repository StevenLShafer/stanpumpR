# Mentré 1998: population PK/PD of fluindione in patients (indirect-response INR model)

## Study
Inpatients of the cardiology department, Pitié-Salpêtrière, Paris, fluindione-naive. Learning group n = 49
(13 women; age 60.2 ± 13.3 yr, range 29–89; weight 70.8 ± 11.7 kg, 45–98; creatinine 105.4 ± 24.6 µmol/L;
Table I, PDF p5 / printed p68); 38 low-intensity (INR 2–3: AF, dilated cardiomyopathy, VTE), the rest mechanical
valves (INR 3.5–4.5). Usually 20 mg once daily from day 0, adjusted by physicians (26 stayed on 20 mg, 6 on 15 mg).
Administration at 8 AM, noon or 8 PM (randomized); one 8 AM sample on days 0, 1, 3, 5, i.e. 12, 18 or 24 h after
dose. Validation group n = 24 (12 women), INR and PCA only, 12 h after dose on days 2, 3, 4, 6. Plasma fluindione by
HPLC-UV 280 nm, linear 0.05–10 mg/L, LOQ 0.025 mg/L. Factors II, VII, IX, X, PCA and INR (ISI 1.28). Nonparametric
maximum likelihood (NPML) population analysis.

## Model and parameters
One-compartment PK, absorption as a bolus (first sample 12 h after dose), F fixed at 1, parameters oral CL and
Varea: C(t) = D/Varea · exp(−CL/Varea · t) (Eq. 1, PDF p3, printed p66; image-confirmed).

Indirect response on each effect F (Eq. 2, image-confirmed):
dF/dt = Kd · [F0 / (C^γ/C50^γ + 1) − F(t)], F0 standardized to 100 (factors, PCA) or 1 (INR).
For the INR, INR(t) = 1/F(t), F from Eq. 2 with the INR's own parameters. A delay of onset tlag applies after the
first dose only (model 2, better likelihood than lag-after-every-dose). Final model 2.4: γ fixed at 1, tlag and Kd
identical across effects within a patient, C50 effect-specific (Table III: identical C50 rejected, P < 10^-7).
Error model: proportional, CV 10% (concentration) and 20% (effects), fixed.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Not an opioid. End points: clotting factor II, VII, IX, X activity, PCA and INR, all through the indirect model on
synthesis. Potency order by median C50: VII < X < IX < II (abstract 0.25–2.05 mg/L are medians; the Discussion's
"0.56 to 1.92" are the Table IV means). Protein C and S C50 medians 1.43 and 8.42 mg/L (Discussion, data not shown).
No effect compartment / ke0: the delay is handled by tlag. Kd is not the clotting-factor half-life (authors).

## Covariates
Exploratory only (expected variance reduction > 10%, Table V): V and CL bell-shaped with age; CL tends to fall with
weight; Kd inversely with weight and directly with creatinine; C50 IX rises with creatinine, C50 X with fibrinogen.
No covariate equation adopted. No genotypes.

## Caveats
Sparse design (one sample a day, 12–24 h after dose; at most five days), so rate constants are poorly informed
(authors). Bolus absorption, F = 1, apparent parameters. PK differs widely from the single-dose volunteer study
(half-life 69 vs 31 h). INR validation biased at day 6, largely one patient (INR 6.2). Some patients on heparin.

## Verification against stanpumpR
- **`R/inr.R:244-266`**: dR/dt = kin(1 − E) − kout R, E = C^γ/(C^γ + C50^γ), R0 = kin/kout, INR = 1/R. Mentré Eq. 2
  is dF/dt = Kd[F0/(1 + (C/C50)^γ) − F]; since 1/(1 + (C/C50)^γ) = 1 − E, this is the same equation with kout = Kd,
  kin = Kd·F0, and INR = 1/F as in the paper. The attribution of the structure is **supported (matches)**. The
  library has no tlag; Mentré's first-dose lag (median 7.7 h) is not part of the Verstuyft parameter set the code
  uses, so its absence is consistent with the cited Verstuyft model, not an error.
- **`inst/help/drugs/fluindione.md:41`**: citation (authors, journal, 1998;63(1):64-78, DOI) matches PubMed and the
  PDF; description (patient-based, multiple-dose, indirect response) is accurate; the help says it is not

  carries them.)
