# Tsen 1999: bupivacaine serum protein binding across gestation

## Study
In vitro binding study on donor serum. 81 women: 11 non-pregnant controls (no oral contraceptives) and 70
pregnant women, 7 to 42 wk gestation (28 first, 21 second, 21 third trimester); all taking no drugs other than
vitamins (p 965-966). Age, weight and race not given. Venous blood clotted, serum (not plasma, to avoid in-vitro
lipolysis) stored at -20 °C; bupivacaine HCl spiked to 1 µg/mL ("therapeutic") or 5 µg/mL ("toxic"), pH adjusted
to 7.40 ± 0.02, ultrafiltration (method of Denson 1984, ref 11). Bupivacaine by gas chromatography, LOD 10 ng/mL,
standard curve 10 ng/mL to 8 µg/mL; CV 6% in serum, 10-12% (low) and 2-5% (high) in ultrafiltrate. Samples more
than 10% off the target concentration were discarded, leaving 69 at 1 µg/mL (8/24/18/19 by group) and 67 at
5 µg/mL (7/22/19/19) (p 966). Albumin and alpha-1-acid glycoprotein (AAG) by Beckman Array nephelometer.
Statistics: linear regression on gestational age; ANOVA with Fisher's PLSD (p 966).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. 1 µg/mL and 5 µg/mL are labelled therapeutic and toxic systemic (total) concentrations on the authority of
refs 9-10 (Datta 1979; Munson 1975, monkey seizure thresholds); the paper does not measure toxicity. Not an opioid
paper: no MEAC, ke0 or potency ratio.

## Covariates
Gestational age (weeks) by regression; trimester groups. No equations given.

## Caveats
In vitro spiking of frozen serum, not in vivo free concentrations. Small control group (7-8 analysed). Comparisons
marked significant are against the first trimester, not the non-pregnant controls. Regression coefficients are not
reported, only plotted. "P < 0.05%" is printed in Methods (p 966), presumably meaning P < 0.05. Reference 1 (Denson,
Clin Pharmacol Ther 35:702-9) is dated 1985 here; volume 35 is 1984. The 1 µg/mL data do not show the term rise in
unbound fraction that Wulf 1991 reported.
