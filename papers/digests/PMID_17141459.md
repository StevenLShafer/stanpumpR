# Pottumarthy 2007: bactericidal activity of cefepime and ceftriaxone against S. pneumoniae

## Study
In-vitro microbiology; no patients, no pharmacokinetics. 50 S. pneumoniae strains from the SENTRY programme
(2003-2005, 37 centres, 3 continents), deliberately stratified by penicillin category: 10 susceptible
(MIC <= 0.06), 10 intermediate (0.12-1), 30 resistant (>= 2 ug/mL) (PDF p2, printed p346). Sources: sputum 32%,
invasive pulmonary 28%, middle ear 18%, upper respiratory 12%, blood 6%.
- MIC: CLSI reference broth microdilution, nonmeningitis criteria (CLSI 2006).
- MBC: whole 100 uL from the MIC well and each log2 dilution above it plated; MBC = lowest concentration
  killing >= 99.9% of the inoculum (p346).
- Time-kill: 14 strains (10 penicillin-resistant, 2 intermediate, 2 susceptible), duplicate, at 2x, 4x, 8x MIC,
  counts at 0, 2, 4, 8, 24 h (p346).
- Concentrations are in protein-free broth (free drug); units ug/mL (= mg/L).

## Model and parameters
No PK model. Susceptibility results (Table 1, PDF p2, confirmed on rendered page):

| Ceftriaxone, 50 strains | MIC | MBC |
|---|---|---|
| 50th percentile | 1 (80% cumulative at 1; 40% at 0.5) | 1 (52% cumulative at 1; 30% at 0.5) |
| 90th percentile | 2 (96% at 2; 80% at 1) | 2 (92% at 2; 52% at 1) |
| Range | 0.015 to 8 | 0.03 to > 64 |

Cefepime: MIC50/90 1/2, MBC50 1 (62% at 1), MBC90 2 (92% at 2). MBC50 1 for both and MIC50/MIC90 1/2 for
both are stated in the abstract and p347; the MBC90s are (derived: from the Table 1 cumulative percentages).

MBC/MIC ratios (Table 2, PDF p3, rendered): ceftriaxone ratio 1 in 30 (60%), <= 2 in 88%, <= 4 in 94%,
<= 8 in 96%, >= 32 in 2 strains (4%, "tolerance"). Cefepime <= 4 in 96%, >= 32 in 2 (4%). The 2 tolerant
strains: 025-114B (penicillin-intermediate; ~2 log10 kill only) and 085-901B (MIC 2, MBC > 64 for both drugs).

Time-kill: >= 3 log10 kill at 24 h at 8x MIC in 13/14 strains (92.9%) for both drugs; killing did not increase
with concentration (p347). Regrowth after 8 h at 2x-4x MIC in one susceptible strain.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End points are in-vitro: MIC (growth inhibition), MBC (>= 99.9% kill), and >= 3 log10 CFU/mL reduction at 24 h
in time-kill. Not an opioid paper: no MEAC, ke0, tPeak or potency ratio. No serum concentrations or
PK/PD targets reported.

## Covariates
Penicillin susceptibility category only (MIC90 0.06 in penicillin-susceptible and 0.5 in intermediate strains
for both drugs, p347). 26.7% of penicillin-resistant strains had a ceftriaxone MIC of 2 and 6.7% were
ceftriaxone-resistant (MIC 8).

## Caveats
- The collection is enriched for penicillin resistance (60% resistant), so its MIC/MBC percentiles are higher than
  in an unselected population; the abstract's "MIC50 1" is not a population value.
- Small (50 strains); industry-associated laboratory (JMI); funding not stated in the text layer.
- Table 3's caption says 4x MIC, while the text reports the bactericidal result at 8x MIC; Table 3 has typesetting
  errors ("1.5R6", "0.5E1") and one implausible rise (021-3807B cefepime 1.7E4 at 2 h, 9.5E4 at 4 h). None of
  these is used by stanpumpR.
