# Langenhorst 2019: fludarabine (F-ara-A) in children and adults before HCT

## Study
- Retrospective, single centre (UMC Utrecht), myeloablative conditioning May 2010 - January 2017; 258 patients,
  2605 plasma samples over 596 doses (PDF p.4-5).
- Table 1 (PDF p.5, printed 631), median (range, IQR): children/adolescents <=20 y n = 134, age 9.6 (0.2-20) y,
  weight 29 (4.3-96) kg, eGFR 140 (40-140) mL/min/1.73 m2; adults n = 124, age 54 (22-74), weight 78 (47-130), eGFR
  110 (25-140); total age 18 (0.2-74), weight 60 (4.3-130), eGFR 120 (25-140); 38% female. Indications benign,
  leukaemia, lymphoma, MDS, plasma cell disorders.
- Regimen (PDF p.2-3): fludarabine phosphate 1-h infusion days -5 to -2, then busulfan 3-h infusion. 197 patients
  cumulative 160 mg/m2 (40 mg/m2/day); 61 patients 40 mg/m2 cumulative (10 mg/m2/day, Fig. 1) with 120 mg/m2
  clofarabine. ATG in unrelated-donor HCT.
- Analyte: plasma F-ara-A (called "fludarabine" throughout) by LC-MS, LLOQ 0.001 mg/L; none below LLOQ. Samples
  mostly 4-7 h after the end of infusion, some 7-24 h, and from 2016 15-45 min after the end.
- NONMEM 7.3.0, FOCE-I; bootstrap 1000 (95% successful); NPDE, pcVPC.

## Model and parameters
Three compartments, first order. Weight (actual body weight) allometric on all parameters, exponents fixed 0.75
(clearances) and 1 (volumes), reference 70 kg. Clearance (Eq. 7, PDF p.7, printed 633; Table 2):
CL = (CL70,non-renal + eGFR × Slope_pop) × (BW/70)^0.75 × e^IIV. The text says eGFR is "used in this equation as
l/h/kg" and calls Slope_pop unitless (the fraction of eGFR that is renal clearance of fludarabine). The abstract gives
the terms as 3.24 ± 20% L/h/70 kg and eGFR × 0.782 ± 11% L/h/70 kg.

Observed cumulative AUC0-inf (p.6 text, Fig. 1b): 10-66 mg·h/L at 40 mg/m2/day, 2.7-12 at 10 mg/m2/day; median
21 mg·h/L (range 5.7-42) in children and 26 (13-65) in adults at 40 mg/m2/day; Fig. 1b medians 25.7 and 5.98.
Key Points (PDF p.2): renal clearance about 65% of total in adults.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None: no concentration-effect relation, no ke0, no target concentration. Exposure is expressed as cumulative AUC.
The authors note the label's 50% dose cut below eGFR 70 and that clearance already falls >25% below eGFR 120.
Not an opioid paper.

## Covariates
- Weight (actual) beat FFM (McCune equation) and BSA (ΔOFV +60 and +68); estimated exponents near 0.75 and 1.
- eGFR: Cockcroft-Gault (mean of creatinine values day -7 to 0); Schwartz below 17 y (female) or 14 y (male);
  capped at 140 mL/min/1.73 m2, the cap rising from 35 at birth to 140 at 1.5 years and falling 8 per decade after
  30 years (PDF p.4). Adding eGFR: ΔOFV -172, IIV on CL 34% -> 23%.
- No effect of age (after weight and eGFR), clofarabine or ATG.

## Caveats
- The eGFR units in Eq. 7 ("l/h/kg") are inconsistent with the abstract (L/h/70 kg) and the 65% renal share.
- Children under 0.5 y with eGFR <60 (n = 4) poorly predicted (clearance over-predicted); maturation possibly
  not captured.
- Sparse sampling, mostly 4-7 h after dose; peak samples in 19% of doses.
- Single regimen (busulfan ± clofarabine); typo "mL/min/1.83 m2" for the renal strata (PDF p.6).
- Dose basis (phosphate vs F-ara-A mass) not stated.
