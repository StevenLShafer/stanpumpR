# Plomp 1992: amiodarone in pregnancy and lactation (case series)

## Study
Case series: four mothers, five pregnancies/infants (cases 1, 2, 3, 4a, 4b; 4a and 4b are sisters from the same
mother). Mothers aged 26-33 y (case 4 mother 30 y), all on oral amiodarone (AM) 200 mg/day: before conception and
throughout pregnancy (cases 1, 3, 4a, 4b), or only late in pregnancy with metoprolol (case 2: text says last 6 weeks,
Table I header says "last 5 weeks"; therapy stopped 1 week after delivery). Cases 1 and 3 continued AM while
breast-feeding; case 2 breast-fed until 5 weeks; 4a and 4b bottle-fed. Samples of maternal plasma, cord plasma,
infant plasma, breast milk and placenta assayed for AM and desethylamiodarone (DEA) by HPLC; thyroid hormones by
RIA. Infants followed 8 months to 5 years. No PK modelling; concentrations only.

confirmed on the rendered page:

Summary statistics (text, p 3-5):
- Pre-partum maternal plasma, case 4 (both pregnancies): AM 0.76 ± "13" µg/mL as printed (p 3; evidently 0.13,
  derived SD of 0.60/0.90/0.73/0.80 = 0.13), DEA 0.69 ± 0.07 µg/mL.
- Maternal at birth (n = 3): AM 0.51 ± 0.11, DEA 0.78 ± 0.07 µg/mL. Post partum (two mothers): AM 0.50 ± 0.32,
  DEA 0.73 ± 0.34 µg/mL.
- Cord at delivery (n = 3): AM 0.14 ± 0.08, DEA 0.32 ± 0.16 µg/mL. Cord/maternal ratio AM 0.15-0.61 (mean
  0.31 ± 0.26), DEA 0.29-0.63 (mean 0.41 ± 0.19).
- Milk: AM 1.7-3.0 (mean 2.3) µg/mL, DEA 0.8-1.8 (mean 1.1) µg/mL; milk/plasma ratio AM 4.6-13.0 (mean 7.8),
  DEA 1.1-2.9 (mean 1.9). Placenta/plasma ratio AM 35, DEA 95.
- Estimated infant intake via milk 0.5-1.5 mg/kg/day (150 mL/kg/day milk); infant plasma about 0.1 µg/mL or less.
- Comparator quoted from the authors' earlier work (ref 9): non-pregnant cardiac patients on 200 mg/day,
  steady state AM 0.99 ± 0.40, DEA 0.85 ± 0.31 µg/mL (p 5). Physicochemical: MW 645, pKa 6.6 (AM), 5.6 (DEA); AM
  39.3% iodine w/w (p 2, 6).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No concentration-effect data. End points were clinical/thyroid: one infant (case 2) transiently hypothyroid (cord
T4 65 nmol/L, normal 80-200), one (4a) cord T4 slightly high (277 nmol/L). Maternal T4 105-169 nmol/L, T3
1.26-1.45 nmol/L. Not an opioid: no MEAC, ke0 or potency ratio.

## Covariates
None analysed.

## Caveats
Four mothers; sampling times scattered and unstandardised; maternal steady state not documented; no non-pregnant
measurements in the same women. Internal inconsistencies: case 2 duration (6 wk text vs 5 wk table); "± 13"
misprint.

## Verification against stanpumpR
- **csv:66 finding** ("only a five-case series measuring maternal/cord/milk concentrations ... without PK
  parameters"): **matches** (five cases/infants from four mothers; no PK parameters). The note "Desethylamiodarone
  metabolite also unaffected" is **not supported** by Plomp: no pregnant-vs-non-pregnant comparison exists for DEA
  or AM (open question). The cross-reference to R/pregnancy.R mentioning Ansari/amiodarone was not found in the
  checkout (open question).
- **csv:67** (no IV amiodarone pregnancy PK): **matches** (Plomp is oral only). Its note "96%" binding is not
  in Plomp (open question).
- **Independent check against the library model** (`R/drugs_amiodarone.R:154-157`, Pollak 2000 apparent oral,
  CL1/F 229 L/day): predicted steady state for 200 mg/day = 200/229 = 0.87 mg/L (derived). Plomp's pre-partum
  maternal AM 0.60-0.90 µg/mL (mean 0.76; Table I, case 4) is about 13% below this, and below the authors' own
  non-pregnant 0.99 ± 0.40 µg/mL. Direction is consistent with a modest rise in apparent clearance in pregnancy, but
  with one mother, no confirmed steady state and no paired control, it cannot support a factor; "unknown" is
  appropriate. All Plomp concentrations lie below the library's 1.0-2.5 mg/L window (`R/drugs_amiodarone.R:213-215`),
  as do the non-pregnant reference values at 200 mg/day.
