# Arikci 2025: absolute oral bioavailability of LSD; base and tartrate bioequivalent

## Study
Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, double-dummy five-period crossover, washout >= 10 days (NCT04865653) (PDF p. 2).
- 20 healthy adults completed (10 M, 10 F), age 37 +/- 11 y (25-57), weight 70 +/- 12 kg (50-93); 11 had prior psychedelic use.
- Treatments, about 80 ug LSD freebase equivalent each: (i) LSD base in 1 mL 96% ethanol, analytically 83.1 +/- 1.59 ug base; (ii) LSD tartrate 117 ug in water (oral), analytically 81.0 +/- 1.43 ug base; (iii) LSD base rapid dissolvable tablet (RDT), content declining with storage, estimated 80.5 +/- 4.1 ug (73.8-86.5); (iv) IV LSD tartrate, same solution as (ii) diluted to 2 mL, given as four ~20 ug portions every 3 min over 9 min; (v) placebo (p. 2). Analytically determined doses used for dose correction.
- Plasma at 0.25-24 h (21 times). HPLC-MS/MS, LLOQ 10 pg/mL; LSD and 2-oxo-3-hydroxy-LSD (O-H-LSD). Sampling covered 96-98% of AUC (p. 8).
- Non-compartmental analysis in Phoenix WinNonlin 8.4; IV dosing time set to 5.5 min. F = AUCinf oral / AUCinf IV. Bioequivalence by EMA rules, ethanolic base as reference. A one-compartment (oral) and two-compartment (IV) compartmental analysis and a sigmoid Emax PK-PD analysis are reported only in the supplement (Table S1, Table S2) (p. 3-5).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- Table 2 (PDF p. 6), VAS "any drug effect", mean +/- SD: onset 0.7 h (base), 0.8 h (RDT), 0.7 h (tartrate) vs 0.04 +/- 0.04 h IV; time to maximal effect 2.4, 2.5, 2.6 h vs 1.2 +/- 0.8 h IV; duration 8.1, 8.3, 7.6 h vs 8.0 h IV; max effect 85, 82, 79 vs 94% IV.
- About 1 h lag between plasma peak and subjective peak after IV, similar to oral; cause unclear (p. 8).
- IV gave more anxiety (vs all oral), nausea and bad drug effect (vs base and tartrate solutions), and higher heart rate. No tolerance across repeated sessions >= 10 days apart.
- EC50 / Emax / gamma of the PK-PD analysis are in Table S2, not read. No ke0 or EC50 in the main text. Not an opioid; no MEAC or potency ratio.

## Covariates
None modelled in the main text. Discussion mentions CYP2D6 poor metabolisers having longer half-lives (prior work).

## Caveats
- NCA estimates; the IV dose was a 9-min staged infusion through the catheter later used for sampling, with a contamination risk for the first sample (authors' limitation, p. 8).
- RDT content fell 18% over 18 months; doses estimated assuming a linear decline.
- Three participants dropped out (two after a challenging IV first session, one for poor veins); 20 completers analysed.
- Abstract and text agree on F = 80% (text: 80-81%). Earlier indirect estimate was about 71%.
- Funding in part from Mind Medicine, Inc.; M.E.L. a consultant.
