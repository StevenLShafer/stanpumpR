# Vashi 2023: cenobamate population PK, monotherapy vs adjunctive

## Study
Sponsor (SK Life Science) population-PK simulation analysis (p1-3). Pooled data: eight phase 1 studies (healthy or
special populations) plus phase 2 C013 and C017 and phase 3 C021 in adults with uncontrolled focal seizures on 1-3
ASMs (Section 2.1; the abstract says "three phase 2 and 3 studies"). Doses: single 5-750 mg, multiple 50-600 mg/day,
100 mg/day in an oral-contraceptive DDI study, 200 mg single doses in elderly, renal and hepatic studies. Table 1
(p2-3): N per study 77 (single ascending dose, 5895 observations), 35, 14, 21, 26, 24, 31, 26, 113 (C013, 534
observations), 329 (C017, 1054), 445 (C021, 1217). In all 960 subjects and 8700 concentrations; 67.5% of subjects at
200 mg/day, 14.2% at 100, 9.3% at 400 (Section 3.1, p3). LC-MS/MS assay; NONMEM 7.3.0 and R 3.2.2. Covariates on CL/F
and V1/F stepwise (Supplemental Table 1); concomitant ASMs used by more than 10% of patients tested
(levetiracetam, lamotrigine, oxcarbazepine, lacosamide, topiramate, carbamazepine, valproate, clobazam); phenytoin and
phenobarbital not tested (too few patients). Simulation: 1000 virtual subjects per arm at 100, 200 and 400 mg once
daily.

## Model and parameters
Structure (Sections 2.2 and 3.1, p2-3): two compartments, first-order absorption with an absorption lag, exponential
IIV, combined proportional and additive residual error, and a dose-dependent effect on CL/F. The parameter estimates
are in Supplemental Table 2 (supplement, 2026-10-27; see PMID_37429218_mmc1.md for the whole table): ka 2.74 /h,
CL/F 0.533 L/h, V1/F 42.4 L, lag 0.240 h, Q/F 1.84 L/h, V2/F 7.27 L, final model (typical values, 95% CI and RSE
there).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. Equivalence criterion: 90% CI of the simulated AUC GMR within 0.8-1.25. Lacosamide, lamotrigine, levetiracetam,
oxcarbazepine, topiramate and valproate had no significant effect. The Discussion quotes an earlier DDI study:
cenobamate exposure -28% with phenytoin and -15% with phenobarbital, judged not clinically meaningful (p3).

## Covariates
Clobazam and carbamazepine on CL/F as above (categorical, effect as a fraction). **Supplement (2026-10-27):**
- **Supplemental Methods (p1):** a continuous covariate enters as P_i = P_TV (1 + theta (X_i - X~)), with X~
  "typically the median". A categorical one enters as P_i = P_TV (1 + theta_m I_m), so P_TV is the first
  category. The co-medication effects are therefore multiplicative factors 1 + theta: carbamazepine x1.150,
  clobazam x0.810. They are not exp(theta).
- **Final covariates (Supplemental Table 2):**
  - On CL/F: daily dose (labelled "exponential"), BSA, clobazam, bilirubin, carbamazepine, African American,
    Hispanic.
  - On V1/F: weight and African American.
  - No median or reference value is printed for any continuous covariate. The form of the "exponential" dose
    term is not written out, so it is the one term that the linear rule in the Methods does not cover.
- **Supplemental Table 1 (p2):**
  - Runs 16-23, from run 16 (the stepwise final, dOFV -359.9 from run 12) through Black race on CL/F (run 17,
    -23.2) and V1/F (run 21, -21.9; run 20, -22.3 from run 16) to Hispanic on V1/F (run 22, -22.8).
  - Hispanic on CL/F was not significant (run 19 -0.2, run 23 -0.7).
- **Table 1's footnote** says weight, clobazam, carbamazepine, bilirubin and BSA affected CL/F. Supplemental
  Table 2 has no weight-on-CL/F row. The supplement's two tables disagree here, as they do on the Hispanic
  parameter.

## Caveats
- Sponsor analysis and short communication.
- Monotherapy was never observed; it was simulated.
- Phenytoin and phenobarbital were not evaluated.
- **Supplement (2026-10-27):**
  - It prints no covariate medians and no diagnostics beyond figures.
  - The unit printed on the dose row (m⁻²) repeats the BSA row's.
  - Bilirubin's unit is printed as "IU", and its RSE as negative.
  - IIV is on ka, CL and V2, not V1.
