# Sica 1990: methocarbamol PK and protein binding in haemodialysis patients and normals

## Study
- Non-blinded single-dose study (PDF p1, printed 193).
- Haemodialysis: n = 8 functionally anuric patients on maintenance haemodialysis (7 Black/1 White; 6 M/2 F),
  age 32 (SD 6.8) y, weight 68.7 (14.1) kg; studied on an off-dialysis day.
- Normals: n = 17 healthy male volunteers studied concurrently with a similar protocol, age 25 (5.7) y,
  weight 78.6 (8.1) kg (weight significantly greater, p = 0.035).
- Dose: methocarbamol 1.5 g orally (two 750 mg tablets), fasted from the evening before until 2 h post-dose.
- Sampling: venous EDTA plasma at 0, 0.33, 0.66, 1, 1.5, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12, 15, 18, 24 h (plus 0.17, 0.5, 5,
  10 h in normals). Protein binding samples at 1, 8, 12 h (Centrifree ultrafiltration).
- Assay: HPLC with fluorometric detection; sensitivity 0.2 mg/l.
- Analysis: non-compartmental. k10 from the log-linear terminal slope; t½ = ln2/k10 (harmonic mean);
  AUC by linear trapezoids extrapolated to infinity (extrapolated fraction <2% normals, <4% HD).
  Cmax and AUC·k10 normalised to 72 kg. Relative availability = (AUC·k10)HD/(AUC·k10)normal × 100%.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None measured. Two haemodialysis patients had self-limiting drowsiness. No therapeutic or toxic concentration
range. Not an opioid paper: no MEAC, ke0 or potency ratio.

## Covariates
Group comparison only (haemodialysis vs normal renal function); weight normalisation to 72 kg. Haemodialysis did
not significantly change tmax, normalised Cmax, k10 or normalised AUC·k10 (comparator: the concurrently studied
healthy male volunteers, who were younger, heavier and all male).

## Caveats
- Two-page short communication; small n; groups differ in age, sex, race and weight.
- Internal inconsistency: the Results paragraph (PDF p2) assigns 1.24 h to normals and 1.14 h to haemodialysis,
  but the Abstract, Discussion and Table 1 k10 values (ln2/0.56 = 1.24, ln2/0.61 = 1.14) all give 1.24 h for
  haemodialysis. The Results paragraph has them swapped.
- Abstract unit typo: weight-adjusted Cmax "27.0 mg·m-1" (Table 1: mg/l).
- The AUC·k10 "bioavailability" comparison assumes V/F proportional to weight and equal between groups.
- Metabolites (glucuronide/sulphate conjugates, expected to accumulate in renal failure) not measured.
- Oral data only: no absolute bioavailability, CL or V.
