# Krumpl 2018: 24-hour landiolol and esmolol infusions in healthy whites

## Study
- Single-centre, double-blind, randomised two-period crossover, 7-day washout; 12 healthy white volunteers (Table 1,
  PDF p3: 5 women, 7 men; 34.3 +/- 9.5 y; 72.8 +/- 8.9 kg; 173.8 +/- 7.9 cm; BMI 24.0 +/- 2.2).
- Esmolol hydrochloride (Brevibloc 2500 mg/250 mL) 50 mcg/kg/min for 2 h, 100 for 2 h, then 200 for 20 h, with no
  loading dose (PDF p2; the text layer prints "mg/kg", the rendered tables give ug/kg/min). Landiolol 10/20/40 in the
  other period.
- Venous blood from the arm contralateral to the infusion at 0, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12, 16, 20, 30, 60, 90, 120 min after each
  dose level, at 4-20 h of the high dose, and to 360 min after the end. 0.5 mL whole blood into chilled ethanol within
  30 s (lysed whole blood), HPLC-MS/MS. Non-compartmental analysis (1- and 2-compartment models also run in WinNonlin,
  but not reported).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End points: resting HR, SBP, ECG. Esmolol's HR fall became significant only at 30 min (vs 16 min for landiolol);
  minimum median HR 63.0, 62.0, 61.0 bpm (changes -2.5, -4.5, -3.0) at 50, 100, 200 (PDF p4). Maximum mean HR change
  -4.7 +/- 6.7, -6.2 +/- 7.8, -4.6 +/- 8.2 bpm; maximum SBP change -5.1, -6.5, -12.5 mmHg (Table 5, PDF p7, rendered).
- Tolerance: high-dose esmolol gave no significant HR reduction at any time; its bradycardic effect was lost hours
  before 16 h. Rebound after stopping, up to +7.5 bpm, significant until 1 h (PDF p7).
- No ke0, tPeak, EC50 or effect-compartment analysis.

## Covariates
None.

## Caveats
Healthy volunteers at bed rest; HR influenced by meals ("lunch bump"); non-compartmental only; two subjects withdrew
after esmolol (one for infusion-site reactions).
