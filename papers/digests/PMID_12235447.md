# Xie 2002: population PK of irinotecan, SN-38 and SN-38G, and diarrhea

## Study
- 109 patients (65 M, 44 F), age 34-75 y, solid tumours (69 colorectal); 153 evaluable courses (44 patients had a second course). Treated at Erasmus MC-Daniel den Hoed, Rotterdam, 1996-2001. Table I.
- Irinotecan 100-350 mg/m2 (median 300) as a 1.5-h IV infusion (range 0.75-2.25 h) every 21 days; 82 patients also had cisplatin (56) or R115777/tipifarnib (26).
- Sampling: before dose and about 0.5, 1.5, 1.67, 1.83, 2, 2.5, 3.5, 4.5, 5, 5.5, 6.5, 8, 12, 25.5, 49 and 56 h. Plasma, HPLC with fluorescence detection, LLOQ 2.0 ng/mL; total (lactone plus carboxylate) drug reported as the surrogate for the lactone. Concentrations in ng/mL.
- SN-38G measured in only 57 patients; diarrhea scores (NCI CTC, worst grade in 3 weeks) in 104 (51 with SN-38G).
- Model: NONMEM VI, FOCE-I, sequential (irinotecan first, then post hoc parameters fixed for SN-38, then for SN-38G); first-order formation of each metabolite; exponential IIV; additive plus proportional residual error. Covariates by generalised additive models with AIC: age, sex, BSA, cisplatin, R115777.

- Absolute irinotecan values: CL 31.6 L/h, Vss 263 L (text, PDF p. 6). Derived check: 16.9 x 1.87 = 31.6 and 140.4 x 1.87 = 262.5, so the conversion uses BSA 1.87 m2; and 36.7 + 35.9 + 67.9 = 140.5, matching the printed 140.4.
- SN-38 and SN-38G parameters are apparent (divided by the unknown formation fraction Fm). SN-38 terminal half-life from Table III, derived: k10 = 712/408, k12 = 1530/408, k21 = 1530/71,600 gives beta about 0.0068 /h, t1/2 about 100 h (derived), poorly supported by sampling that ended at 56 h.
- Exposure: AUC(0-60) mean (SD) 17,226 (8,016) ng/mL.h for irinotecan (range 4,043-46,164); 403 (214) for SN-38 (126-1,229); 4,416 (3,816) for SN-38G (197-22,734). Text, PDF p. 6.
- Cisplatin: no effect on irinotecan PK; R115777 effect on irinotecan CL under 15%. Sex and age: no significant PK covariate.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: diarrhea grade 0-4, proportional-odds logistic model, logit(P(Y >= m)) = Int_m + Slope x AUC(0-60). No ke0, t1/2ke0, tPeak, hysteresis, EC50 or Emax (an Emax replacement did not improve fit, data not shown).
- Grade frequencies: 46% (0), 28% (1), 20% (2), 4% (3), 2% (4); in the SN-38G subset 46/24/20/5/5%.
- Table V (page image): intercepts m = 1 to 4 for irinotecan -0.55, -1.23, -1.69, -1.16; SN-38 -0.31, -1.22, -1.68, -1.15; SN-38G -0.62, -1.10, -1.58, -0.90. Slope (logit per ng/mL.h): irinotecan 4.26E-05 (SE 2.0E-05), SN-38 1.20E-03 (SE 7.9E-04), SN-38G 1.87E-04 (SE 6.0E-05). n = 104, 104, 51.
- Table IV dOFV: irinotecan -4.29 (P = .04) in the full set (n = 104) but -3.514 in the reduced set (n = 51); SN-38 -2.468 and -1.672 (not significant); SN-38G -8.43 (P = .0038); biliary index -0.522. SN-38G AUC is the strongest predictor; SN-38 AUC is not significant. Dichotomised diarrhea showed no significant relation.
- No concentration threshold, MEAC or potency ratio (not an opioid).

## Covariates
- Body surface area, direct proportionality on every irinotecan PK parameter (CL, Q, V). No other covariate retained. Cisplatin, R115777, sex, age, tumour type and tumour number tested; none significant.

## Caveats
- SN-38G data from only 57 of 109 patients; the irinotecan-diarrhea relation is borderline and lost in the reduced set. Sequential, post hoc fixed individual parameters for the metabolites. Total (lactone plus carboxylate), not lactone. Q2 and the Q3 IIV are imprecise (RSE 58% and 71%). Absolute V and CL here are BSA-based conversions.
- The Table II layout is scrambled in the text layer (Q3 and V1 values appear in the wrong rows); the page image above is the authority.

## Verification against stanpumpR
Not a cited source in code, so this is an independent comparison with the Klein 2002 model the library ships (R/drugs_irinotecan.R:81-82, R/drugs_irinotecanSN38.R:23-26), with both read as population typical values:
- Irinotecan CL: Xie 31.6 L/h (BSA 1.87 m2; 16.9 L/h/m2) vs library 5.52 x 4.56 = 25.2 L/h (header line 32, Klein). Xie is about 25% higher (derived). The comparator is the library's Klein cohort (median 80 kg, 78 patients, 100-340 mg/m2), whose BSA is not stated in the code.
- Vss: Xie 263 L vs library 262.4 L (header line 32): agree.
- Central volume: Xie V1 = 36.7 L/m2, about 69 L at 1.87 m2 (derived), vs library v1 = 5.52 L. The structures differ: Klein has a tiny fast central compartment (k12 57.8 /h) that Xie's first sample at about 0.5 h could not resolve; early-peak predictions will differ accordingly.
- SN-38: Xie CL/Fm 712 L/h and Vss/Fm 72,008 L vs library 466 x 2.52 = 1,174 L/h and Vss/Fm about 21,200 L (derived: 466 + 466 x 2.6 / 0.0584). Xie CL/Fm is lower (0.61x, derived) and Vss/Fm about 3.4x higher (derived); the apparent terminal half-life is about 100 h (derived) against Klein's 24.3 h, with sampling to 56 h only. Fm is unknown in both, and both parameter sets are Fm-scaled.
