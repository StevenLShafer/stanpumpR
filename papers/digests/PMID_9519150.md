# Ritchie 1998: effect compartment on myocardial metoprolol (and sotalol) content

## Study
22 patients with ischaemic heart disease at diagnostic cardiac catheterisation (p1): metoprolol 4 mg IV bolus
(n = 12, patients 1-12; Mw 267, the base) or sotalol 20 mg (n = 10). Salt of the 4 mg not stated. Myocardial drug
uptake = coronary arteriovenous whole-blood concentration difference x coronary sinus blood flow (thermodilution),
sampled 0.25-20 min; cumulative myocardial content by trapezoids, normalised to resting coronary sinus flow (p1).
Effects: reduction in spontaneous heart rate, prolongation of the PR interval at fixed (paced) rate, reduction in
LV +dP/dt max. Data re-analysed from the group's earlier reports (refs 7, 8). Multifit nonlinear least squares,
individual fits, mean +/- SEM (p2). No plasma concentrations are modelled.

## Model and parameters

Linear PD, E = S x Ae (eq 5). Observed time of peak myocardial content 2.67 +/- 0.38 min (p1, from ref 7).
Sotalol (Table 1/2): two-compartment myocardial model; ke0 0.60-0.66 /min for the three effects. Example patient 1
(Fig 3): Ka 1.549 /min, K 6.08 x 10^-2 /min, ke0 for dP/dt 0.021 /min, much smaller than the group mean.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- ke0 values above are on the amount in a myocardial effect site driven by MYOCARDIAL content, not plasma.
- The paper states peak haemodynamic and ECG effects of both drugs were not seen until 5-10 min after injection,
  citing refs 7, 8 (p1).
- Derived (mean Ka, K, ke0 in eq 6): Ae peaks 3.28 min after the bolus for heart rate (ke0 1.30), 7.26 min for
  paced PR (0.30), 7.40 min for dP/dt (0.29); peak myocardial content of the mean model 1.9 min.
- ke0 did not differ significantly between effects or drugs (power about 0.36-0.39). Not an opioid; no MEAC.

## Covariates
None.

## Caveats
Patients at catheterisation, small n, effects followed for only 20 min; myocardial content is indirect (mass
balance); heart-rate ke0 has a 95% interval that includes zero; low doses, so a linear PD (no Emax) was used.
