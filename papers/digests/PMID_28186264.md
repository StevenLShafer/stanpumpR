# Hara 2017: predictive performance of 11 propofol PK models in children, long infusions

## Study
Prospective observational study, Chiba Children's Hospital. 21 ASA I-II children, 3-11 yr (mean 7 (SD 3)), 24 (7) kg (15-40), 120 (16) cm, BMI 16 (1); 15 M / 6 F. Lean children (morbid obesity excluded); expected anaesthesia over 2 h. Propofol bolus 1.5 or 2.0 mg/kg then 4-14 mg/kg/h (propofol or sevoflurane induction), with remifentanil (0.34 (0.06) ug/kg/min) and fentanyl (1.8 (0.7) ug/kg/h). Infusion 272 (131) min (88-545). 85 VENOUS plasma samples (0.5 mL, opposite limb), 30-120 min apart and at stop, none within 5 min of a rate change (all but two >10 min); HPLC assay; plasma stored at -30 C. Measured Cp 1.1-4.1 ug/mL (p4). All samples at steady state, none from the early bolus phase or from recovery after a long infusion. Metrics: Varvel MDPE, MDAPE, divergence (slope of PE vs time), wobble; "acceptable" = MDPE within +/-20%, MDAPE <=30%, divergence PE within +/-4 %/h; also a performance metric (% with APE<=20% minus % with APE>60%).

## Model and parameters
No new model. Table 1 (PDF p3, printed p417) transcribes eleven published 3-compartment sets in L, min, TBW in kg: Paedfusor, Kataria, Marsh (children 1-12 yr), Short, Rigby-Jones, Coppens, Saint-Maurice, Shangguan, Murat, Schuttler (venous, <=60 yr), Eleveld 2014. Only Short and Schuttler met all three criteria. Positive PE = measured above predicted, so negative MDPE = model overpredicts.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No effect-site data, ke0, tPeak, EC50, MEAC or potency ratio (propofol; remifentanil and fentanyl were co-administered but not studied). Not an opioid paper.

## Covariates
Only those inside the published sets (TBW; age in Kataria/Schuttler/Eleveld; BOL, GEN, PMA in the general-purpose models). The authors note the two acceptable models lack a fat-free-mass term, so may underpredict in obese children.

## Caveats
Authors: lean children only; no infants or adolescents; steady-state venous samples only, so not valid right after a bolus or rate change; population (not individual) performance; models fit to bolus-only data drifted most. Also: n=21, 85 samples; comparison against measured venous Cp (Schuttler carries a venous/arterial covariate); concomitant remifentanil and fentanyl. Measured Cp 1.1-4.1 ug/mL, so most models read as overpredicting at low concentration (authors' suggestion).

## Verification against stanpumpR
Not applicable as a citation check. For the independent-check rule: the library's propofol model is Eleveld 2018 (`R/drugs_propofol.R:81-84, 172-259`), plus a Schnider companion (`R/alternativeModels.R:213`). The library has no Short, Marsh, Kataria or Paedfusor model, so those results touch nothing. Hara's Schuttler row is the 2000 model, not Schnider's. Hara's Eleveld set is the 2014 version (see open question). Hara tested venous Cp; the library sets `A1V2 <- 1` (arterial prediction, `R/drugs_propofol.R:169`), so the venous offset terms in Eleveld are not applied. Short parameters in Table 1 agree with Short 1994 Table II (see `PMID_8130049.md`).
