# Akil 2016: R- and S-citalopram and desmethylcitalopram population PK in CitAD (Alzheimer's agitation)

## Study
CitAD trial: 94 patients with Alzheimer's disease and agitation received racemic citalopram, titrated from 10 mg to
a target 30 mg once daily (morning, three 10 mg capsules) over 2 weeks; plasma at weeks 3, 6, 9. 81 analysed (41 M,
40 F), age 77.8 ± 8.2 y (47-90), weight 71.5 ± 17.2 kg (40-122.3), BMI 26.3 ± 5.2 (Table 1). CYP2C19: EM 53.1%,
RM 3.7%, IM 21%, PM 3.7%, missing 18.5%. 205 observations (~2.5/subject; S-citalopram 109). Chiral HPLC-UV, LOQ 5
ng/mL per enantiomer (S-DCIT 10). NONMEM 7.1, ADVAN6, FOCE-I; 100-replicate bootstrap.

## Model and parameters
Four compartments (one per compound), first-order absorption into each parent compartment, complete parent→
metabolite conversion, linear metabolite elimination; parent and its metabolite share one volume per enantiomer;
ka equal for both enantiomers and fixed at 1 /h. Continuous covariates as centred power functions (age/60,
weight/70); sex and CYP2C19 (EM/RM, IM/PM, missing) as group values. The weight exponent 0.75 appears as a fixed
allometric value (no estimate in Table 3).

interval, as printed "mg/L/h").
(Derived: 60 y, 70 kg man, normal CYP2C19: t½ R = 0.693 × 1830/13 = 97.6 h; S = 0.693 × 1390/22.1 = 43.6 h.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None in this paper (exposure-response referred to Ho et al. 2015 abstract: R-citalopram AUC linked to QTc and
cognition). Not an opioid.

## Covariates
CL_R: sex, age. CL_S: age, weight (fixed 0.75), CYP2C19 (EM/RM vs IM/PM, ~36% higher in EM/RM). Metabolite CLs:
weight. No weight, BMI or CYP2C19 effect on CL_R.

## Caveats
Sparse sampling (1.3 S-citalopram samples per patient), elderly AD population (47-90 y), one-compartment parents,
no IV data (all apparent), CYP2C19 groups pooled (EM with RM, IM with PM), 18.5% genotype missing. Whether each
enantiomer receives D/2 is not written explicitly; the equations show "Ka × Dose". Missing-genotype CL_S differs
between text and table. Bootstrap medians differ from final estimates for CL_R and V_R by 6-14%.
