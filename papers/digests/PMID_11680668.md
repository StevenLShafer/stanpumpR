# Barrett 2001: tinzaparin anti-Xa population PD in DVT patients

## Study
Population (NONMEM V, FO) model of plasma anti-Xa activity (chromogenic Kabi assay, LLOQ 0.2 IU/mL; citrated platelet-poor plasma; PDF p3) in two phase III trials. Not a pure PK model: anti-Xa is a surrogate for LMWH activity, not drug mass.
- DVT treatment (Hull 1992): 187 patients, 1,724 observations in the final set; tinzaparin 175 anti-Xa IU/kg SC once daily; samples at entry, 4-6 in the first 24 h, then daily ~4 h post dose. Mean age 63.2 y, weight 76.6 kg (37-151), BMI 26.4 (Table 1).
- DVT prophylaxis, total hip replacement (Planes 1999): 238 patients, 887 observations; fixed 4,500 IU SC daily; sparse samples taken at nearly the same post-dose time per patient. Age 38-86, weight 46-95 kg.
- Index set 70% of the treatment data for building, 30% for validation, then refit on all (Run 101). The prophylaxis data were too sparse to build a model, so the treatment base model was applied to them.

## Model and parameters
Two-compartment, first-order SC absorption, per-kg parameterization (CL l/h/kg, Vc and V3 l/kg, Q l/h/kg, KA 1/h; Methods, PDF p5), plus an individual constant baseline endogenous anti-Xa (ENDO) added to each prediction. Bioavailability is not separated, so CL and V are apparent (CL/F, V/F). IIV is exponential; Vc IIV fixed to zero; CL-KA covariance included. Residual error combined additive plus proportional.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No effect-site model: the only "pharmacodynamic" quantity is plasma anti-Xa activity (IU/mL), modelled directly. No ke0, t1/2 ke0, tPeak, hysteresis, EC50, MEAC or therapeutic concentration is reported; the paper notes that anti-Xa has not tracked antithrombotic efficacy and that routine monitoring is generally not advised (PDF p2). Not an opioid paper. Background values quoted from other work (not this analysis): Cmax 0.25 IU/mL after 4,500 IU, 0.87 IU/mL after 175 IU/kg, Tmax 4-5 h, F 86.7%, half-life about 4.4 h (PDF p2). The abstract's elimination half-life is 3.9 h (CI 2.5-5.2).

## Covariates
Tested on CL: sex, age, weight, race, PIBW, diabetes, creatinine, warfarin. Kept: plasma creatinine (OFV drop 14.5) and PIBW (5.0), exponential models centred on the median: CL = CL0 * exp(-0.213*(CREA-median)) * exp(-0.006*(PIBW-median)), median CREA about 1 mg/dL, PIBW 14% (Table 5; equation form Eq. 10). Sex (1.1), age (0.2), warfarin dose (0.4) and warfarin yes/no (0.1) not significant. Weight not significant, but dosing and parameters were already per kg. PIBW uses IBW 50 (male) or 45 (female) + 2.3 kg per 2.5 cm above 152 cm. Creatinine clearance uses Cockcroft-Gault with IBW. Individual-estimate analysis: CL about 22% lower in BMI >30 (median 0.0143 vs 0.0176 L/h/kg) and 24% lower with CrCl <30 mL/min. No covariates were significant in the prophylaxis data.

## Caveats
Anti-Xa is a surrogate; population is DVT patients (and elective hip replacement), excluding dialysis and hepatic encephalopathy. V3, Q and their IIV are imprecise (V3 RSE 81%, CI includes zero; V3 fixed in Table 4). FO estimation. Fixed-dose study data sparse. Authors conclude covariate effects are not clinically important.
