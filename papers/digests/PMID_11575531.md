# Kuizenga 2001: EEG PKPD of propofol over three sequential infusions

## Study
10 adult patients (5 M; ASA I-II; age median 58 yr, range 22-75; weight 81 kg, 60-90; height 1.71 m) for elective knee surgery under epidural analgesia (0.5% bupivacaine, T8 median sensory level). Three propofol infusions, each started when the patient regained responsiveness: 25 mg/kg/h for 10 min, 22 mg/kg/h for 10 min, 12.5 mg/kg/h for 20 min (PDF p. 2). Arterial whole-blood propofol (HPLC, detection limit 5 mcg/L, CV 4.3%), sampled every 2 min (4 min during slow infusion, sparse to 130 min after the third). EEG: 11-15 Hz amplitude by aperiodic analysis (30 s epochs) and Bispectral Index (A-1000, BIS 3.12, 15 s smoothing); effect times set to epoch midpoints. After exclusions 7 patients were fully analysed (Results, p. 4). Spreadsheet Solver fits; MDPE, R2, -2 log likelihood.

## Model and parameters
No pharmacokinetic model fitted: measured arterial blood concentrations (linear interpolation up, log-linear down) drove a single effect compartment (Eq. 1). Pharmacodynamics, derived from the first infusion only, then used to predict infusions 2 and 3. Table 2 (page image, PDF p. 5, printed p. 611), medians over patients:

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End points: EEG 11-15 Hz amplitude (biphasic response: baseline 33 microV/s, peak 193, then fall to 13) and BIS (baseline 98, minimum 40). Equilibration rate constants keo above (/min; t1/2ke0 and tPeak are not reported; t1/2 = ln2/keo would be about 3.9 min for BIS keo 0.18 and 2.0 min for amplitude keo 0.34, derived). Concentration is arterial whole blood, in mg/L. No MEAC or potency ratio. Clinical events (Table 1): loss of responsiveness at median 2-3.5 min from infusion start, EEG maxima at 3-4 min, return of responsiveness at 25-32 min. Main finding: parameters fitted to infusion 1 predicted later infusions poorly (R2 median 0.00 for amplitude and 0.15 for BIS in the abstract), so the blood concentration-EEG relation was not stable over time under non-steady-state conditions.

## Covariates
None evaluated.

## Caveats
n = 7 evaluable; surgical patients with epidural block (block level may regress); the epoch-length time correction uses future EEG data and could not be applied in real time; the Mandema model is unvalidated per the authors; fits were of a spreadsheet Solver; keo estimates differ by method (0.34 amplitude vs 0.17 BIS). Whole-blood concentrations, not plasma.

## Verification against stanpumpR
Independent comparison only. R/drugs_propofol.R takes tPeak = 1.6 min from Schnider 1999 (line 276) on the Eleveld 2018 PK. These keo values (BIS 0.18 /min, amplitude 0.27-0.34 /min, whole-blood PK-free effect-site delay) imply slower equilibration than a 1.6 min tPeak would for Schnider-like kinetics, but they were estimated against measured arterial concentrations, include the processed-EEG epoch delay handling described above, and are not comparable to a tPeak without the PK model; no discrepancy asserted. Nothing in the repo relies on this paper.
