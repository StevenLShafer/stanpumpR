# Lafolie 1996: plasma and urine codeine and metabolites after 25 and 50 mg oral codeine

## Study
- 13 healthy volunteers (10 M, 3 F), 22-61 y, 54-93 kg (Table I); no drug abuse history.
- Open randomised crossover: codeine 25 and 50 mg orally (salt not stated for the dose; codeine phosphate listed under chemicals), 2-week washout, fasted.
- Plasma at 0, 0.5, 1, 2, 4, 6, 7, 24 h; urine 96 h.
- Plasma: HPLC with UV and electrochemical detection for codeine, C6G, morphine, M3G, M6G; LOQ codeine 15, C6G 10, morphine 0.6, M3G 4.0, M6G 0.4 nmol/L. Urine: EMIT screen and GC-MS (total, acid hydrolysis) codeine, morphine, norcodeine.
- Noncompartmental (Siphar); AUC0-7 by linear trapezoid; Cmax/Tmax observed.
- CYP2D6 not genotyped; 3/13 (subjects 3, 8, 13) judged poor metabolisers from low morphine formation.

Derived molar morphine:codeine AUC0-7 ratio: 0.020 (50 mg), 0.027 (25 mg).
Urine (Table II): total morphine 5.44% / 5.55% of dose, codeine 65.8% / 57.5%, morphine:codeine ratio 8.8% / 10.9% (overall range 2.3-23.3%, mean 9.8%). Urinary t1/2 codeine 3.48-3.71 h, morphine 6.90-8.27 h (Table IV).
Text p. 544: all compounds peaked at about the same time, 1-2 h.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No MEAC, no ke0/tPeak, no potency ratio (forensic toxicology paper).

## Covariates
None modelled; dose (25 vs 50 mg) and putative CYP2D6 phenotype discussed.

## Caveats
- Sparse early sampling (0.5, 1, 2, 4 h): a peak earlier than 30 min, or between 30 and 60 min, cannot be resolved; reported Tmax values are sampling times.
- AUC truncated at 7 h; morphine t1/2 imprecise (range up to 19 h at 25 mg).
- Small n; 3 putative poor metabolisers among 13; dose salt not stated.

## Verification against stanpumpR
- **"Every measured compound, morphine included, peaking 1-2 h"** (codeine.R:86-87): text p. 544 says peaks at about the same time, 1-2 h. Table V means are 1.0-2.0 h. **matches** as a paraphrase.
- **Use as a bound on first pass** (codeine.R:84-92, 226-229; test ~162 asserts 60 < morphine tmax < 120 min): Lafolie's mean morphine Tmax (1.0 h at 50 mg, 1.2 h at 25 mg) is equal to or earlier than codeine's (1.1 / 1.3 h), with individual peaks as early as 0.5 h. The model puts codeine at 60 min and morphine near 110 min (codeine.R:96-97), i.e. morphine lags codeine by about 50 min where Lafolie shows no lag. The data are compatible with a morphine peak anywhere from 0.5 to 2 h, and cannot exclude an early component because the first sample is at 30 min. So the paper supports "peak within 1-2 h" but does not support a lag of morphine behind codeine, and arguably favours more early (first-pass) morphine than 0.0015 of the dose. **partly-verified**: cited fact matches; the inference drawn from it is weaker than the code states.
- **Morphine:codeine ratio** (not attributed to Lafolie in the code): derived AUC0-7 molar ratios 0.020-0.027 agree with the Shah 1990 / Yue 1991 values (0.027, 0.020) that the code compares against; model gives 0.014 (codeine.R:101-103).
- **Citation**: the repo carries only the short form "Lafolie 1996"; this archived paper (J Anal Toxicol 1996;20:541-546) is consistent with the use and is the likely intended source. No full reference in R/drugs_codeine.R's reference list (lines 127-145). **cannot verify** intended identity; recommend adding the full reference.
