# Manberg 2008: regulatory challenges for closed-loop anesthesia infusion devices

## Study
Policy commentary ("public policy" section), no data. Authors are full-time employees of Aspect Medical Systems (maker of the BIS monitor), who state a patent interest in BIS closed-loop use. Argues the US FDA will approve a closed-loop anaesthetic infusion system if clinical benefit outweighs risk, citing closed-loop nitroprusside (PMA P860035, 1987) and arbutamine (1997) systems as precedents, the IEC 60601-1-10 closed-loop collateral standard (2007), and the absence of any FDA-cleared TCI pump in the US as of 2008 (PDF p.1-2). Table 1 lists potential benefits of closed loop (less over/underdosing, faster recovery); Table 2 lists product-development issues (component choices: propofol, a TCI pump such as Asena PK, BIS target of 50, PID or Bayesian controller; hazard analysis; trial design). Suggests demonstrating a safety benefit such as fewer awareness events would need larger trials than several hundred patients (PDF p.3).
