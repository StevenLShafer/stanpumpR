# Sahinovic 2018: Clinical pharmacokinetics and pharmacodynamics of propofol (review)

## Study
Narrative review (MEDLINE search of propofol MeSH headings, English, post-1985, plus snowballing). No new data. Covers formulation, dosing, PK (adult, obese, paediatric, unified, PBPK models), PD (CNS, cardiovascular, respiratory, PD modelling) and drug interactions (midazolam, dexmedetomidine, volatiles, opioids). Eleveld's general-purpose model is presented as the most recent unified model (p.1548, PDF p10).

Other facts stated in the text (text layer): free fraction 1.2-1.7% (p.1541); up to 50% bound to erythrocytes, so clinical PK is often measured in whole blood (p.1541); mean clearance about 2.2 L/min, hepatic blood extraction 90%, extrahepatic sites about 40% of clearance, kidneys extraction 60-70% (up to one third of metabolism) (p.1542); non-compartmental ranges for adults: Vdss 159-771 L, t1/2 elimination 116-834 min, Clb 1.78-2.28 L/min (p.1542); 80% context-sensitive decrement time under 50 min for infusions under 3 h and up to 3.5 h for infusions over 12 h (p.1542); Diprivan label induction 2-2.5 mg/kg, maintenance 6-12 mg/kg/h (healthy adults under 55 y) (p.1541).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
(Page image read, PDF p13, printed 1551.) Hypnotic half-time of plasma-effect-site equilibration (T1/2 keo) 2.5 min, independent of age; haemodynamic T1/2 keo 5 min in young and up to 10 min in elderly patients, citing Kazama 1999 (ref 132). Ranges of published BIS-based PD parameters (refs 65, 67, 71, 149-152): ke0 0.01-0.45 /min; Ce50 2.71-3.44 mg/L; gamma 1.47-2.961. The review reports no tPeak. BIS is the end point for the PD range; the 2.5 min value is called "hypnotic" without naming the measure.

Opioid interactions (PDF p15, printed 1553, image read), propofol with an opioid, not an opioid paper itself:
- Fentanyl: ceiling at an effect-site concentration of 3 ng/mL; propofol plasma concentration for loss of responsiveness to verbal command falls 40% and for suppression of movement at incision 90% (ref 184). Concentration matrix (plasma vs whole blood, total vs free) not stated.
- Sufentanil: additive for loss of responsiveness; Hentgen (ref 186): sufentanil Ce 0.3 ng/mL with propofol Ce "4 ng/mL" (as printed; the unit is probably ug/mL, not confirmed) for intubation, sufentanil Ce 0.2 ng/mL during surgery.
- Remifentanil: supra-additive for hypnotic and analgesic end points (no values).
- No MEAC, no potency ratio and no opioid ke0 in the review.

## Covariates
Eleveld model (Table 2): weight (V1 sigmoid, V2 linear, CL and Q allometric 0.75), fat-free mass (V3), age (V2 decline; V3 and CL decline only with opioids), sex (CL), maturation (PMA sigmoid on CL; age sigmoid on Q3), arterial vs venous sampling, opioid comedication. Text (p.1548): female metabolic clearance is higher than male in the Eleveld model, with no comparator beyond the male reference of the same model.

## Caveats
Review; prospective evaluation of Eleveld's model "still required" (abstract). Table 2 has printing errors: Eleveld FFM is shown as [0.88 + (1 - 0.88)/1 + (AGE/13.4)^-12.7], where the division should enclose the 1 and the age term, and fsigmoid is printed without its division sign; AGE and the 40-week offset are mixed units in fQ3maturation (the library resolves this as years x 52 + 40 weeks). The AGEref and WGTref of Eleveld are not given. Marsh's V3 is shown as 2.89 x TBW, as in the original. In Table 2 the Schnider formula uses cm for height as printed; the LBM equations are the James equations.
