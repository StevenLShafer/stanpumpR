# Berg 2015: enzyme-inducing antiepileptics and irinotecan/SN-38 pharmacokinetics

## Study
Population PK analysis pooling two NCCTG phase 2 glioma trials (N997D, newly diagnosed glioblastoma; 96-72-51, recurrent glioma). n=86 patients (PDF p. 3, Table 1): 54 men, 32 women; age 53.1 +/- 13.8 y (mean +/- SD); BSA 1.97 +/- 0.26 m2; 56 on trial 96-72-51 and 30 on N997D; concomitant EIAED 56 (65.1%) (phenytoin 36, carbamazepine 9, phenobarbital 3, two or more 8), none/non-inducing 30; concomitant corticosteroid 67 (77.9%). Weight and height not tabulated.
Irinotecan 90-min IV infusion, weekly, 100-400 mg/m2 (dose levels 100, 125, 250, 300, 400 mg/m2; Table 1); N997D 400 mg/m2 with EIAED or 125 without (PDF p. 2). Samples before, at end of, and 1, 2, 4 and 24 h after infusion, day 1 cycle 1; HPLC for irinotecan, APC, SN-38 and SN-38G; 1723 concentrations; 21 SN-38 values below the 1 ng/mL LLOQ imputed as LLOQ/2. 425 observations each for irinotecan and APC, 426 each for SN-38 and SN-38G; one subject removed (times wrong) and single observations removed from three. NONMEM 7 (IMPMAP), bootstrap n=1000 (959 successful), VPC.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None: no effect site, no concentration-effect relationship, no ke0, MEAC or therapeutic range. The authors translate the exposure change into a dose: since EIAEDs cut mean SN-38 AUC0-t by 41%, a 1.7-fold irinotecan dose matches AUC (PDF p. 8-9); published MTDs with EIAEDs were 225-411 mg/m2, 1.8-3.3-fold the 125 mg/m2 MTD without (PDF p. 9). SN-38 is described as over 100x more active than irinotecan (PDF p. 1, ref 3).

## Covariates
Fractional changes versus the reference patient (Table 3, PDF p. 6; theta, bootstrap mean (95% CI), P):
- EIAED present: CL irinotecan +0.194 (0.193; 0.088-0.293; P<.0001); CL SN-38/F +1.03 (1.06; 0.678-1.51; P<.0001); CL SN-38G/F +0.248 (0.270; -0.027 to 0.641; P=.001). CL APC unaffected. The text's 19.3%, 106% and 26.9% match the bootstrap means (PDF p. 4). The three EIAEDs were pooled because there were too few on carbamazepine and phenobarbital to separate them.
- Corticosteroid: CL APC/F +0.262 (0.267; 0.051-0.504; P=.006); no effect on irinotecan or SN-38; APC is inactive.
- Female sex: CL SN-38/F -0.247 and CL SN-38G/F -0.381 per Table 3, but the text states 37.7% and 24.3% in the reverse order (see open_questions). Simulation: women's mean SN-38 AUC0-t only 8% higher.
- Grade 2 disease: CL irinotecan +0.171 (0.169; 0.072-0.273; P=.001); the authors attribute it to more EIAED use in lower grades (15 of 16 grade 2 patients on an EIAED).
- Simulated: SN-38 AUC0-t down 41% with EIAEDs; SN-38G/SN-38 AUC ratio 3.5 to 5.6; APC median Cmax 345 vs 309 ng/mL (PDF p. 9). Age, BSA, ECOG status, dose level were not retained. Covariates cut BSV in CL by 4.0% (irinotecan), 2.4% (APC), 18.1% (SN-38), 7.5% (SN-38G).

## Caveats
Authors' own: no UGT1A1 genotype, no individual EIAED or steroid doses, sparse sampling (no enterohepatic recirculation), EIAED effect on irinotecan CL only about 19%, smaller than expected, possibly from other inhibiting comedications (PDF p. 8). Glioma patients only; model SN-38 exposure 17% lower than observed because 3 non-EIAED patients had unusually high SN-38 (P=.024). Vc/F SN-38 is poorly identified (95% CI 34.7-666 L). Table 2 contains stray characters ("Table?2", "Q?") in print.
