# Jäger-Roman 1986: valproate in pregnancy, fetal outcome and maternal free fraction

## Study
- Prospective longitudinal Berlin cohort (started 1976) of epilepsy, pregnancy and child development
  (PDF p4, printed 1000). 14 women on valproate (VPA) monotherapy and 12 on VPA with other antiepileptics
  (primidone, phenytoin, phenobarbital, ethosuximide); 26 matched-pair controls and 116 controls from the
  wider study. Maternal age 25.4 ± 5.4 years; pre-pregnancy weight 64.3 ± 10.3 kg on VPA monotherapy,
  56.8 ± 8.1 kg on combinations, 58.0 ± 11.7 kg controls; individual weights not given.
- Oral VPA monotherapy 300-2000 mg/day (Table I). Blood for total VPA and protein binding sampled at
  random times; assay methods only by reference (ref 11, Nau 1984).
- End points: fetal distress, Apgar, birth measurements, major malformations, minor anomalies (blinded
  observer in 16 of 26 infants).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- Therapeutic range quoted as 50-80 ug/mL total (PDF p5); first-trimester totals of 100-184 ug/mL in two
  women without evident toxicity.
- Monotherapy outcomes (Table II, PDF p6): major malformations 4/14 (28.5%) vs 2/26 (7.7%) matched and
  7/116 (6%) entire controls; median minor anomalies 4.0 (0-19) vs 1.1. Fetal distress 6/14 (43%) vs 2 (9%)
  matched controls; low Apgar 4/14 (28%). Minor anomalies correlated with prescribed dose, r = 0.88
  (P < 0.01). The authors attribute perinatal distress possibly to the raised free fraction at birth.
- Not an opioid; no ke0, MEAC or potency ratio.

## Covariates
None modelled. Dose relation of minor anomalies (above).

## Caveats
- Very small n; random-time sampling with large between-dose fluctuation, so trimester totals are not
  averages. Only two women have both first- and third-trimester totals.
- "At birth" free fraction is maternal serum during labour/delivery, when free fatty acids displace VPA.
- Pair 7's cord level 4.9 against a maternal 67.6 ug/mL is out of line with the other pairs (possible
  misprint); not used by the repo.
- Free-fraction n and sampling times not reported.
