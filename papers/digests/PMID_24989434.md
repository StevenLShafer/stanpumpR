# ter Horst 2014: venlafaxine and ODV concentrations through pregnancy

## Study
- Observational TDM case series from the Dutch ADAP adherence study (Isala, Zwolle). Ten venlafaxine users were eligible and 7 were included. Two gave no samples. One had no post-partum sample, but the paper still lists post-partum values for all 7 IDs in Table 1.
- Mean age 30 ± 4 y; weight 76 ± 15 kg and height 168 ± 6 cm in the first trimester (p2/printed 542). No co-medication, smoking or alcohol.
- Oral venlafaxine at 37.5-225 mg/day (the highest was 225 mg). The paper does not say which formulation. Dosing times were recorded with MEMS caps, and 86% of women took more than 80% of doses on time.
- Sampling: one trough sample per trimester (T1 weeks 0-13, T2 weeks 14-27, T3 > 28 weeks) and one about 3 months post-partum. The post-partum sample was assumed to reflect the pre-pregnancy state and served as the baseline.
- Assay: HPLC with fluorescence detection on plasma (EDTA whole blood, centrifuged). Linear from 50 to 1000 µg/L. LLOQ 50 µg/L for both analytes. Within- and between-day CV < 5.9%.
- Values below the LLOQ were set to 25 µg/L. Concentrations were corrected for dose changes, then expressed as a % of each woman's post-partum value. Friedman test.

Summary results (abstract; text on p3/printed 543). All are medians of each woman's % of her post-partum value:
- Venlafaxine: T1 98.9% (54.2-292.0), T2 100.0% (46.5-264.0), T3 87.0% (61.5-217.2). Friedman P = 0.028. See open_questions: the T3 figure is suspect.
- ODV: no significant change, P = 0.565. No numbers are given in the text. Fig. 1 bars read about 77% (T1), 77% (T2) and 87% (T3).
- ODV/V metabolic ratio: T1 76.9% (32.8-142.0), T2 116.0% (24.0-177.0), T3 196.7% (83.3-427.6). P = 0.004 on p3, but P = 0.018 later in the same section.
- Sum below the 100-400 µg/L reference range: 3 women in T1, 1 in T2 and 2 in T3. None was below the range post-partum.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- Therapeutic reference range: 100-400 µg/L for venlafaxine plus ODV in plasma. The paper takes it from the AGNP 2011 guideline (ref 3, p1/printed 541) and did not measure it itself.
- The paper has no exposure-response data.
- Venlafaxine is not an opioid, so there is no MEAC, ke0 or potency ratio.

## Covariates
- Trimester only. Weight was recorded only at inclusion.
- CYP2D6 was not genotyped. All metabolic ratios fell between 1 and 10, which the authors read as extensive metabolizers.

## Caveats
- Very small (n = 7). Many venlafaxine values were below the LLOQ (11 of 28 cells were imputed as 25), so ratios built on them are mostly artefact.
- Three women had dose changes, and correcting for them assumes linear PK.
- The post-partum value (about 3 months) stands in for the non-pregnant state; there is no pre-pregnancy sample.
- The paper is internally inconsistent: the MR P-value is given as 0.004 and as 0.018, and the T3 venlafaxine summary appears to repeat the ODV values (see open_questions).

## Verification against stanpumpR
- **ter Horst sentence in the finding, "venlafaxine ~87-100% of postpartum across trimesters (NS to -13%)": discrepancy.**
  - The paper's own text gives 98.9%, 100.0% and 87.0%, so "87-100%" transcribes it.
  - But the paper calls the change significant (P = 0.028), not NS.
  - The T3 87.0% appears to be the ODV values: Table 1 gives a T3 venlafaxine fall to about 34-51% of post-partum (derived). See open_questions.
- **"ODV unchanged": matches** (P = 0.565).
- **"ODV/venlafaxine ratio rising from 77% (T1) to 197% (T3)": matches** (76.9% to 196.7%, p3).
- **Comparator "own non-pregnant baseline (same women) / postpartum": matches.** The baseline is the same woman at about 3 months post-partum.
- **Group "unchanged" with no factor applied:** this rests mainly on Westin 2017 (active moiety) and Schoretsanitis 2020, which this card does not check. ter Horst does not contradict an unchanged active moiety: ODV is unchanged, and the parent, the smaller share, falls. A corrected T3 parent fall would strengthen the row's "parent declined" wording but would not change the active-moiety decision.
- **Library venlafaxine model:** `R/drugs_venlafaxine.R:90-95` uses a 140-600 ng/mL active-moiety range (Lense 2024, replacing AGNP 2017's 100-400) and the 85-380 ng/mL ODV band. ter Horst's 100-400 µg/L comes from the older AGNP 2011. Units are the same (µg/L = ng/mL). The paper has no PK parameters to compare with the model.
