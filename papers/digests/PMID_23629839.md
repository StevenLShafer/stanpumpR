# van Heusden 2013: propofol response variability in children (WAVCNS)

## Study
Control-engineering paper. Identifies per-patient pharmacodynamic models of propofol on depth of hypnosis (DOH) measured by the WAVCNS index (NeuroSENSE monitor; about 90 awake, 40-60 general anaesthesia) in children 6-16 y, for robust PID controller design. Data: 30 open-loop cases (elective general surgery, propofol bolus then manual infusion, remifentanil 0.5 mcg/kg bolus then 0.03 mcg/kg/min) and 69 closed-loop cases (ASA I-II, endoscopy; age 11±3 y, 34 male, 43±15 kg, 150±17 cm; remifentanil as above). Selected: 14 open-loop (first 8 min of infusion) and 36 closed-loop (first 10 min) cases, after discarding cases with stimulation or poor data; 50 subjects identified, 3 models judged invalid, 47 reported (Table I, PDF p8). Propofol infusion rates recorded manually (open loop) or by controller; no blood propofol assay.

## Model and parameters
Two structures per subject, both with a Hill output nonlinearity E = E0 - E0*C^g/(EC50^g + C^g) (Eq 2, 3):
- PKPD: Paedfusor PK model (Absalom and Kenny, ref 17) FIXED to predict Cp; PD = first-order effect-site lag kd (min^-1) plus time delay Td (s), EC50 (mg/L), gamma, E0. Monitor trending filter GM = 1/(8s+1)^2 (30 s filter).
- FOPTD: first-order plus time delay directly from infusion rate: Td (s), k (min^-1), E50 (mcg/kg/min), gamma.
E0 = mean of the first 50 s; Td capped < 120 s; kd values adjusted for negative discrete poles are shown in italics in Table I (value 60 min^-1, PDF p8).

Fit: mean rms residual open loop 3.61 (FOPTD) vs 3.79 (PKPD); closed loop 3.55 vs 3.68 (p6). Closed-loop validation: induction time (to WAVCNS 60) 3.6 min (PKPD) and 3.7 min (FOPTD); overshoot 8 and 9; settling to 45-55 in 13 and 13.4 min (p7). Three PKPD outliers (including the subject whose fit was insufficient) excluded, leaving 47.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: WAVCNS (EEG wavelet index), not BIS. The PKPD kd is a first-order lag Cp to Ce (kd as ke0-like constant, 0.33-60 min^-1 in Table I) combined with a delay Td; no ke0, t1/2 ke0 or tPeak is stated by the authors, and they say the PD parameters have limited physiological meaning (identifiability from step-like induction data; p7). EC50 is in Paedfusor plasma-concentration units (mg/L) and "should be used in combination with this PK model" (Table I footnote). No MEAC, opioid potency ratio or analgesic concentration (propofol is not an opioid). Remifentanil synergy is not modelled; models are valid only for similar remifentanil dosing.

## Covariates
Age, weight, height, sex listed per subject in Table I; no covariate model is fitted. Models are normalised to weight (infusion in mcg/kg/min).

## Caveats
Linear approximation from induction data; nonlinearity gamma, Td and kd are not separately identifiable; tradeoff expected to bias toward larger Td, slower kd, smaller gamma (p5). Stimulation bias (33 of 69 closed-loop cases discarded). EC50 and kd are tied to Paedfusor, not to propofol blood measurements. Small n, one centre, remifentanil co-administration.
