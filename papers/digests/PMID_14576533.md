# Donati / Egan / Gropper / Zeilhofer 2003: four Anesthesiology editorial views

PDF page = printed page - 1036 (1037 = p1 ... 1044 = p8). The DOI is not printed in the PDF text and is left blank.

## Study
Four commentaries; no original data.
- **Succinylcholine dose (PDF p1-2, Donati).** Discusses Naguib 2003 and Kopman 2003. Naguib: 0.56 mg/kg predicted to give acceptable intubating conditions in 95% (the 95% and the definition of acceptable are arbitrary; about 90% at 0.3 mg/kg; more than 1 mg/kg for 99%). Excellent conditions: about 55% (0.3 mg/kg), 60% (0.5), 80% (1 mg/kg); acceptable 30% with no drug, 98% at 1 mg/kg. Kopman: reducing 1.0 to 0.6 mg/kg shortens duration by about 90 s (adductor pollicis recovery 9.3 min at 1 mg/kg, 7.6 min at 0.6; longest recoveries 10, 10.5, 11 min for 0.4, 0.6, 1 mg/kg). Benumof: about 8 min apnea to SpO2 90% after preoxygenation. Mean apnea after 1 mg/kg: 5.2 min (Heier, 12 volunteers); first diaphragm movement 4.7 min (Hayes, n=100). Succinylcholine half-life stated as under 1 min (cites Roy 2002). Table 1 (p2): excellent conditions after 1 mg/kg 63-80% and poor/failed 2-8% (Andrews 74/3, Sparr 73/3, Blobner 63/8, Fleming 65/7, Naguib 80/2; the "good" column is unreadable in the text layer).
- **TCI (PDF p3-5, Egan and Shafer).** Commentary on Avram and Krejcie 2003 (front-end kinetics): standard compartment models put the peak plasma concentration at the moment of injection although there is a 30-45 s delay. Account of the FDA's stance on TCI (Diprifusor). Fig. 2 and text (p4-5): constant propofol infusion 100 ug/kg/min, Schnider model, effect-site concentration 0.5 ug/mL at 1.5 min, 1.0 at 2.9, 2.0 at 9.9, 3.0 at 87, 4 at 747 min. Example regimen: 2-2.5 mg/kg bolus, 100-150 ug/kg/min for 15 min, 80-100 for 30 min, then 70-90 ug/kg/min.
- **Sepsis and hypothermia (PDF p5-7, Gropper)** and **COX-1 in neuropathic pain (PDF p7-8, Zeilhofer and Brune):** animal-model commentary; not pharmacokinetic.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Succinylcholine: intubating conditions (Copenhagen consensus scoring) and adductor pollicis twitch recovery; no ke0, EC50 or concentration. Propofol: no EC50 or ke0 stated (Schnider refs cited). Not an opioid paper; no MEAC or potency ratio.

## Caveats
Opinion pieces reporting other trials. The editorial itself notes the dose conclusions depend on arbitrary thresholds and do not apply when a nondepolarizing defasciculating dose is given.
