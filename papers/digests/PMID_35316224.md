# Olofsen 2022: buprenorphine reduction of fentanyl-induced respiratory depression

## Study
- Two-part PK/PD study, Leiden (Mar 2018 - Jan 2019); NL7028; funded by Indivior. Endpoint: iso-hypercapnic, iso-normoxic minute ventilation (end-tidal forcing, breath-by-breath averaged to 1 min). PDF p. 1-2, 9-11.
- Part A: 14 opioid-naive healthy volunteers (7 M/7 F, mean age ~24 y, 18-45 y, BMI 18-30), single-blind 2-sequence crossover, buprenorphine or placebo infusion (periods 1, 2), optional period 3 buprenorphine alone (n = 5). 2 volunteers did only period 1.
- Part B: 8 chronic opioid users (3 M/5 F, mean 42 y, 18-55 y, BMI 18-32, 6 chronic pain, 2 OUD; mean 220 +/- 134 mg oral morphine equivalents/day, mean 7 y), open-label single sequence; placebo+fentanyl then buprenorphine+fentanyl; oxycodone substitution, last dose >= 15 h before each period.
- Buprenorphine IV, 6 h: loading dose over 15 min then per-hour infusion, per 70 kg (Table 1, PDF p. 4): naive low 0.05 mg then 0.02 mg/h (target 0.2 ng/mL); naive high 0.125 mg then 0.05 mg/h (0.5 ng/mL); chronic low 0.25 mg then 0.1 mg/h (1 ng/mL); medium 0.5 mg then 0.2 mg/h (2 ng/mL); high 1.25 mg then 0.5 mg/h (5 ng/mL). Measured plasma (mean +/- SD): 0.28 +/- 0.05, 0.54 +/- 0.08, 1.08 +/- 0.33, 2.28 +/- 0.40, 6.12 +/- 1.26 ng/mL (p. 2).
- Fentanyl IV boluses over 90 s at t = 120, 180, 240, 300 min, mg/70 kg: naive 0.075, 0.15, 0.25, 0.35; chronic 0.25, 0.35, 0.50, 0.70 (Table 1). Naive volunteers mostly got only 2-3 doses (apnea); one got all 4 (p. 2).
- Arterial blood, 8 mL, plasma, LC-MS/MS (buprenorphine-d4, fentanyl-d5 internal standards); calibration range 0.020-10.0 ng/mL buprenorphine and 0.100-50.0 ng/mL fentanyl; QC accuracy/precision within 5.3% (p. 13-14). Plasma, total drug (no free fraction).
- Analysis: sequential PK then PK/PD, NONMEM 7.4.4, SAEM/IMP; PK on log-transformed data; M3 censoring of negative ventilation at zero; Kalman filter for correlated residuals (sigma, process noise sigma_v, tau).

PD structure (p. 15): receptor kinetics d[BR]/dt = kON,B [B] [R] - kOFF,B [BR] with [B] the effect-site concentration; fentanyl assumed in rapid equilibrium, [FR] = (1 - [BR]) ([F]/C50,F) / (1 + [F]/C50,F); VE = VB (1 - alphaB [BR] - alphaF [FR]).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point throughout: iso-hypercapnic minute ventilation (and probability of apnea); not analgesia, EEG or pupil. Concentrations are effect-site (biophase) via ke0; the measured PK is arterial plasma total drug.
- Fentanyl: ke0,F 0.10 /min, t1/2 ke0 = 6.9 min (stated p. 6; Table 3). Without the Kalman filter it was 18.7 min (p. 8). Authors note the 6.9 min agrees with a 6.4 min EEG-power-spectrum t1/2 ke0 (their ref 18, not re-measured here). C50,F: 0.68 ng/mL naive, 2.23 ng/mL chronic (3.3-fold), effect-site conc for 50% fall in ventilation.
- Buprenorphine: ke0,B 0.0028 /min; text gives t1/2 ke0 251 min (derived here: ln2/0.0028 = 248 min, rounding of the table value). kOFF,B 0.019 /min = t1/2 37 min (43 min without Kalman). Apparent K = kOFF/kON: 0.22 ng/mL chronic and 0.072 naive per the text (p. 5-6); derived from Table 3: 0.019/0.26 = 0.073, 0.019/0.085 = 0.22. t1/2 ke0 did not differ between populations.
- alphaB 0.81 naive, 0.48 chronic: buprenorphine alone gives a ventilatory ceiling (mild depression).
- Protective buprenorphine concentration against fentanyl respiratory depression: plasma >= 2 ng/mL (chronic users), from the simulations of Fig 9 and the p. 4 text; 1, 2, 5 ng/mL target correspond to 50%, 70%, 80% MOR occupancy (their ref 24).
- Simulated apnea probability after 2 mg/70 kg fentanyl in chronic users: 2.1% at 5 ng/mL, 6.4% at 1 ng/mL buprenorphine; 59% under placebo (p. 4).
- MEAC / minimum effective analgesic concentration: not reported (ventilation study). Equianalgesic or potency ratio: none reported. Tolerance shift quoted as fentanyl C50 3.3-fold, buprenorphine kON 3.1-fold lower (derived: 0.26/0.085).

## Covariates
Opioid-use state (naive vs chronic) only, on kON,B, alphaB, C50,F in the PD model (Table 3). PK: no covariate retained beyond allometric body-weight scaling.

## Caveats
Small n (14 naive, 8 chronic; only 2-3 per buprenorphine dose group in chronic users); naive volunteers' fentanyl escalation limited by apnea; fixed dosing sequence in part B (placebo always first); buprenorphine alone given only in 5 naive; alphaF fixed to 1; most IIVs fixed to 0.001; sequential PK then PD; Kalman filter changed fentanyl t1/2 ke0 from 18.7 to 6.9 min, so the value is model dependent; sponsor-funded. Table 3 prints fentanyl C50 as an effect-site concentration while the plasma assay is total plasma.
