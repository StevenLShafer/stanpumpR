# Retlich 2015: linagliptin population PK (TMDD) and PK/PD in type 2 diabetes

## Study
- **Data:** four Boehringer Ingelheim studies in type 2 diabetes (Table 1, p3):
  - Study 1, phase 1: powder in bottle, 1-10 mg for 12 days, 35 patients.
  - Study 2, phase 1: tablet formulation 1, 2.5-10 mg for 4 weeks, 61 patients.
  - Study 3, phase 2b: tablet formulation 2, 0.5-5 mg for 12 weeks, 170 patients.
  - Study 4, phase 2b: tablet formulation 2, 1-10 mg for 12 weeks as add-on to metformin, 196 patients.
- **Sampling:** rich profiles in phase 1 (day 1 and steady state, 0.5-12 h, plus washout samples); trough and 1
  and 2 h samples at four visits in phase 2b.
- **PK set:** 6907 linagliptin concentrations from 462 patients (Table 3, p6). Median (range): age 60 (30-78)
  years, weight 89 (57-132) kg, BMI 30.6; 65.4% men, 92.9% Caucasian; 42.4% on metformin.
- **PK/PD set:** 9674 DPP-4 activity values with concentrations from 607 patients, including placebo.
- **Software:** NONMEM V; GAM screening in S-Plus; AUC simulations in Berkeley Madonna. Because of high
  η-shrinkage, the forward/backward covariate search was adapted (p = 0.01 to include, parameter by parameter)
  (p4). The paper does not describe the bioanalytical assay.

## Model and parameters
**Structure (Fig. 1, p3):** depot → central compartment (VC) with first-order absorption (F, Ka), and a
peripheral compartment (VP) linked by QP. Each compartment has unbound and bound linagliptin. Binding is
saturable to DPP-4 sites: Bmax,C (a concentration) in the central compartment and Amax,P (an amount) in the
peripheral, with one affinity constant Kd. CL is the clearance of the **unbound** concentration. The model is
carried over from Retlich 2010 (ref 15); QP, VP, Kd and Amax,P were fixed to that model's estimates (Table 4,
footnote d). The binding equation itself is not printed.

**Supplement (PMID_25637172_ESM1.docx, read 2026-10-27).**
- Appendix Table 1 (PK covariate selection): finally selected are weight and study 4/metformin on F (the latter
  in base-model development), GGT on CL/F, study/formulation and dose group on Ka, pre-dose DPP-4 activity, dose
  group, age and sex on Bmax,C; nothing on VC/F. CLCR was tested on CL/F (GAM-indicated, special interest) and on

- Appendix Table 2 (PK/PD covariates): BSL on FPG, ALT, GGT, cholesterol, triglycerides and sex; EC50 on

- Appendix Table 3: observed vs predicted median DPP-4 inhibition by dose, 2 h and 24 h (above for 5 mg).
- Appendix Figs: 1 and 4 goodness of fit; 2, VPC of studies 3 and 4 at steady state (**base** model); 3A/3B,
  posterior predictive checks of median Ctrough and Cmax per dose group (**final** model; the authors note the 0.5
  and 10 mg Cmax medians fall slightly outside the 90% interval).
- **Not in the ESM:** the binding equation, the observation equation, the assay, any control stream.

**Covariate effects on exposure (pp7-8):** every single covariate changed AUCτ,ss at 5 mg by less than 20%.
- Combined extremes: +63% for an elderly woman of 67 kg with high GGT and high DPP-4 activity on metformin;
  -26% for a young man of 117 kg with low GGT and low DPP-4 activity.
- Weight changed exposure by +5.9% and -8.7% at its 5th and 95th percentiles; age 73 vs 42 by +13.8%. Exposure
  in study 4 was about 20% higher than in the monotherapy studies (p11).

**Missed-dose simulation (p9, Fig. 7):** final models, the 607 PK/PD patients, 5 mg once daily.
- Median concentration 24 h after a regular steady-state dose: 5.58 nM (90% PI 3.55-9.69).
- 48 h after the last dose, when a dose is missed: 4.21 nM (2.65-6.86).
- Trough after the next dose following the missed one: 5.29 nM (3.38-8.82).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- **End point:** plasma DPP-4 activity, in RFU, related directly to plasma linagliptin by a sigmoid Emax model.
  There was no hysteresis, so no effect compartment and no ke0 (p4). Table 5 (PDF p10, rendered):
  - Baseline: BSL 10,700 RFU in men, 11,565 in women (BSLmale + 865).
  - Emax 92.4% (maximum decrease), EC50 3.06 nmol/L, Hill 3.22.
  - BSL_EC50 0.00792% per RFU from 11,600. Covariates on BSL: GGT, ALT, FPG, triglycerides and cholesterol.
    Triglycerides also act on EC50 (-0.0153% per mg/dL).
  - IIV: BSL 16.9%, EC50 15.4%; residual 14.8%.
- **Typical EC50 by sex:** 2.84 nM for men and 3.05 nM for women (p12). Consistent with Table 5 through the
  baseline term: 3.06 × (1 + 0.0000792 × (10,700 − 11,600)) = 2.84, and 3.05 at 11,565 (derived).
- **Covariate extremes (Fig. 6, p10):** EC50 2.49-4.13 nM, EC80 4.44-7.38 nM.
- **DPP-4 inhibition after a missed dose (p9):** median 81.7% at 24 h, 69.6% at 48 h, and 80.3% trough after
  resuming.

## Covariates
- **F:** weight (linear, centred at 88 kg) and study 4.
- **Ka:** formulation/study and dose.
- **CL:** GGT.
- **Bmax,C:** sex, age, baseline DPP-4 activity and dose.
- **Without effect:** CLCR and liver enzymes other than GGT, explored but showing no or minor effect. Only
  patients with normal liver function and normal renal function or mild impairment were included (p11).

## Caveats
- High η-shrinkage on F, CL, Ka, Bmax,C and VC (22-58%); only two absorption-phase samples per visit in the
  phase 2b studies, so the model suits overall exposure better than absorption (p11).
- Four structural parameters are fixed from the 2010 model.
- Mostly Caucasian patients. No renal or hepatic impairment beyond mild.
