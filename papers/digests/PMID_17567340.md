# Barakat 2007: propofol sedation, Marsh vs Schnider effect-site predictions

## Study
Randomised (sealed envelopes), two-group, 40 un-premedicated ASA I-III adults (age 18 or more) having orthopaedic surgery under spinal anaesthesia with propofol sedation. Group M (n=20): Marsh model, plasma-site targeting; group S (n=20): Schnider model, effect-site targeting. Both groups targeted 2 ug/mL on an Alaris Asena PK pump. Demographics, mean (range), Table 2 (PDF p2): age 67 (47-88) M vs 64 (38-80) S; weight 82.2 (62-108) vs 81 (46-123) kg; height 162.9 vs 167 cm; sex M/F 6/14 vs 9/11; no significant differences.
OAAS (responsive component, 5 to 0) and BIS (A2000, 15 s smoothing) were recorded every 30 s for up to 15 min (stopped if OAAS reached 0). No propofol concentrations were measured. The effect-site concentration (Cep) of each model was calculated afterwards with Tivatrainer from the infusion actually given; the pump's own model was one of the two. OAAS and BIS were inverted (5-OAAS, 100-BIS) for plotting and correlation. Correlation was between group median time courses and Cep; the overall R2 is the mean of individual R2 values (PDF p2).

Both are described as three-compartment models (Marsh from venous sampling, Schnider from arterial, with age, weight, sex and lean body mass covariates for Schnider).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End points: OAAS responsiveness score and BIS, against model-predicted Cep (not measured). Table 3 (PDF p4, printed p663), r and R2:
- Marsh-driven group, Cep vs OAAS: Marsh r 0.87, R2 0.65; Schnider r 0.10, R2 0.05. Cep vs BIS: Marsh r 0.97, R2 0.65; Schnider r 0.32, R2 0.14.
- Schnider-driven group, Cep vs OAAS: Marsh r 0.83, R2 0.64; Schnider r 0.30, R2 0.14. Cep vs BIS: Marsh r 0.97, R2 0.62; Schnider r 0.48, R2 0.23.
- OAAS vs BIS (Table 4): r 0.91 (R2 0.61) Marsh group, r 0.90 (R2 0.57) Schnider group.
The authors conclude that sedation was reached more slowly with Schnider effect-site targeting than with Marsh plasma targeting at the same 2 ug/mL, and that Marsh Cep tracked effect better. They suggest Schnider's PD came from a bolus and its PK from infusions, and that Schnider derived ke0 from time to peak effect after a bolus while Marsh used infusion data. No EC50, MEAC, tPeak or potency ratio is reported. Not an opioid paper.

## Covariates
None analysed.

## Caveats
Sedation only (target 2 ug/mL, up to 15 min), spinal anaesthesia in place, older patients, ASA I-III; the effect-site comparison rests on simulated Cep; correlations are on group medians over one rising time course. Two BIS records lost (Marsh group, faulty cable). Printed r and R2 do not follow r squared (e.g. r 0.97 with R2 0.65) because R2 is a mean of individual values. The Marsh t1/2 ke0 of 2.60 min does not equal ln2/0.26 = 2.67 min.
