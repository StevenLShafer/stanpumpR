# Maximova 2022: IV acyclovir population PK in oncologic children

## Study
- Prospective single-centre observational study, Trieste (IRCCS Burlo Garofolo), 2011-2020; 120 children aged 0-18 y with haematological malignancy, receiving IV acyclovir for prophylaxis (n = 94) or HSV/VZV treatment (n = 26), around allogeneic HSCT or high-intensity chemotherapy. 73 M / 47 F.
- Table 1 (PDF p3, text layer): age 8.6 ± 5.0 y (median 9.5); weight 32.4 ± 19.1 kg (median 27.8); height 128.2 ± 30.0 cm; serum creatinine 0.361 ± 0.206 mg/dL (median 0.320); eGFR (Schwartz) 228.1 ± 79.7 mL/min/1.73 m2 (median 209.4); BSA 1.058 ± 0.426 m2.
- Drug: acyclovir IV over 60 min every 6-8 h; median starting dose 40.7 mg/kg/day (range 15.6-136.7). TDM: dose adjusted to keep Cmin > 0.5 and Cmax < 25 mg/L.
- Sampling: two samples per occasion after >= 4 days of therapy (steady state): 10 min before a dose (Cmin) and 30 min after the end of the infusion (Cmax). 120, 54 and 13 patients on occasions 1, 2, 3; 187 pairs, 374 concentrations. Plasma, LC-MS/MS.
- Software: NONMEM 7.4, PsN, Xpose; 1000-run bootstrap; pcVPC.

## Model and parameters
One-compartment, first-order elimination, proportional residual error (starting additive-error OFV 1372.979; proportional improved by -141.269). IIV on CL and V, IOV on CL. Covariates retained: eGFR on CL (dOFV -12.386); body weight with allometry on CL (-22.811) and V (-12.664). Final model Table 2 (PDF p4, read on the rendered page); covariate centres 209.4 mL/min/1.73 m2 and 27.8 kg (the cohort medians).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- Not an opioid; no MEAC, ke0, tPeak or potency ratio, and no effect-site model.
- Concentration targets used (Introduction/Methods, PDF p2-4): Cmin > 0.5 mg/L for TDM; in simulation Cmin > 0.56 or > 1.125 (printed 1.156 in places) mg/L in at least 50% of patients, and Cmax > 25 mg/L in under one quarter of patients as the toxicity boundary. Background quoted from the literature: concentration above 1 mg/L for at least 50% of the dosing interval for HSV killing; peaks above 50 mg/L tied to neurotoxicity (Introduction).
- Observed (Results, PDF p4): first-occasion Cmax 7.6 ± 5.4 mg/L; Cmin 1.0 ± 1.1 mg/L (6-h schedule) and 0.85 ± 1.3 mg/L (8-h schedule). About 75% of patients had recovery/improvement/infection control; 3 of 4 poor responders had Cmin > 1 mg/L (the fourth 0.78).
- Simulation (Tables 6-7, text layer only): 20 mg/kg 1-h every 6 h gave Cmin > 0.56 in about 62.5% and > 1.125 in about 50.9% (eGFR <= 250) with Cmax > 25 in about 28.6%; prolonged 2-3 h infusions raised target attainment and cut Cmax > 25; continuous infusion of 10 mg/kg every 8 h gave Cmin > 0.56 in all, > 1.125 in 96.1% (eGFR <= 250, Cmin 3.18 ± 1.69 mg/L) and 90.5% (eGFR > 250, 2.51 ± 1.26 mg/L). Simulated with eGFR drawn from mean 228.1, SD 79.7 and weight fixed at 27.8 kg.

## Covariates
- eGFR (Schwartz) on CL, power model, exponent 1.627 centred on 209.4 mL/min/1.73 m2.
- Body weight on CL (exponent 0.75) and V (exponent 1), centred on 27.8 kg.
- No sex difference in the PK parameters (Table 3 legend). Age, creatinine, BSA and treatment indication are not retained.

## Caveats
- Sparse design: two samples per occasion at fixed TDM times (Cmin, Cmax), not dense sampling; GOF showed over-prediction in the first hours after the dose and pcVPC did not fit the lowest Cmin and highest Cmax well; about 90% of bootstrap runs converged.
- eGFR centre (209.4) is far above the usual paediatric normal, because Schwartz values were high in this cohort; the authors note this versus an earlier study (median 164).
- Paediatric oncology only; the simulation targets are the authors' choice.
- Clearance per kg and L/kg figures in the Discussion (0.54 L/h/kg, 0.97 L/kg) are the authors' own and are consistent with Table 3 means (derived: 14.0/26 and 25.4/26 are about 0.54 and 0.98 for a 26 kg child, so a weight near 26 kg was presumably used; not stated).
