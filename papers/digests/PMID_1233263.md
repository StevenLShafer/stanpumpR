# Berlin 1975: clonazepam after single IV and oral 2 mg doses, and 0.5 mg bid

## Study
- 8 healthy volunteers, 4 women and 4 men, 22-59 y, 49-80 kg (Table 1).
- Crossover single doses: 2 mg IV as a rapid injection (Rivotril 1 mg/mL) and 2 mg oral as **4 × 0.5 mg Rivotril tablets**; breakfast 2 h after oral dose.
- Sampling: IV at 10 and 30 min, 1, 2, 4, 8, 12, 24, 48, 72, 96 h; oral the same without 10 min.
- Repeated dosing: 5 subjects, 0.5 mg at 8 am and 8 pm for 15 days; troughs on days 10, 11, 12, 15; washout sampling over 80 h.
- Assay: GC with 63Ni electron-capture detector, sensitivity 0.5 ng/mL; SD 5-8% at 1-20 ng/mL; plasma.
- Analysis: biexponential fit to IV data (two-compartment), iterative least squares (PDP-8); AUC trapezoidal + Clast/β; F = AUCoral/AUCiv; Vdβ = D/(β·AUCiv).

- IV distribution phase lasted 8-10 h; a three-compartment model "requires very frequent sampling during the first 30 min", which was not done (p 157). A and H: IV curves too irregular for A, B, α.
- Highest IV concentration 36.8 ng/mL at 10 min (subject D). Steady-state troughs predicted with CV 6%, max deviation 12%.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Qualitative only: marked sedation about 30 min after IV; slurred speech, incoordination, sleepiness about 3 h after oral. No concentration-effect analysis, no ke0. Not an opioid; no MEAC or potency.

## Covariates
None analysed.

## Caveats
n = 8; IV sampling began at 10 min, so the central volume and first phase are poorly identified; F > 1 in three subjects (assay/AUC extrapolation error); 1970s GC-ECD assay; oral dose was four 0.5 mg tablets, not a 2 mg tablet.
