# Gosselin 2018: ICSH recommendations for laboratory measurement of DOACs

## Study
Consensus guidance document (no new data) from an ICSH ad hoc committee of 10 clinical/laboratory experts,
covering pre-analytical, analytical and post-analytical DOAC testing for dabigatran, rivaroxaban, apixaban and
edoxaban. Recommendations rest on published literature, author experience and good laboratory practice; a
recommendation is "consensus" when all authors agreed (PDF p2, printed p438). Received Aug 2017, accepted Dec 2017.

## Model and parameters
No model is fitted. Table 1 (PDF p2, printed p438; read on the rendered page) summarises literature PK:

| | Dabigatran | Rivaroxaban | Apixaban | Edoxaban |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bioavailability | 3-7% | 80-100% | 50% | 62% |
| Protein binding | 35% | 92-95% | 87% | 55% |
| Primary clearance | 80% renal | 67% renal | 56% faecal | 50% renal |
| Tmax | 1.5-3 h | 2-3 h | 3-4 h | 1-2 h |
| Half-life (a) | 12-14 h | 5-13 h | 12 h | 10-14 h |

(a) half-life rises with renal impairment. The text (p438) gives dabigatran Tmax as 1.25 to 3 h, against
1.5-3 h in Table 1; rivaroxaban peak "in 2 hours" in text, 2-3 h in the table.

Other numbers: PT doubling needs rivaroxaban 66-750 ng/mL, apixaban 480 to >1000 ng/mL, edoxaban 97-296 ng/mL;
PT may be normal (ratio <1.20) with apixaban up to 200 ng/mL (p440); dabigatran <30 ng/mL prolongs the TT
(p442); LC-MS/MS LLOD/LLOQ 0.025-3 ng/mL, reportable range 5-500 ng/mL (p442); chromogenic anti-Xa LLOQ around
30 ng/mL ex vivo for rivaroxaban and apixaban, 15 and 10 ng/mL reported for apixaban and edoxaban (p444);
dTT LLOD 2-8, LLOQ 20-30 ng/mL; ECA LLOD 14-46 ng/mL; C-FIIa LLOD about 15 ng/mL (p443). Dabigatran
glucuronide adds about 20% anticoagulant activity (p443). Sample stability: dabigatran 24 h at room temperature
(4 h for TT); rivaroxaban/apixaban at least 8 h room temperature, 48 h at 5 °C, 30 days frozen (p440).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No exposure-response model. Rivaroxaban maximal FXa inhibition 1-4 h after dosing (p438). The document
recommends that every DOAC result carry a comment that there are no DOAC therapeutic ranges, citing expected
trough levels (Table 2) instead (p439). Reporting unit: ng/mL. Trough sampling recommended for nonemergent
testing. LC-MS/MS is the gold standard; drug-calibrated dTT/ECA/ECT/anti-FIIa (dabigatran) and drug-calibrated
anti-Xa (FXa inhibitors) are suitable for rapid quantitation. Not an opioid paper.

## Covariates
None modelled. Half-life rises with renal impairment (Table 1 footnote); dabigatran dose by indication, age and
renal function (p438); food raises rivaroxaban absorption.

## Caveats
Consensus, not systematic review; Table 2 mixes means and medians with different interval definitions, and its
footnotes say to consult regional labelling before using the numbers. Several authors declare industry ties and
litigation consulting (p446). Text and table differ slightly on dabigatran Tmax (1.25 vs 1.5 h lower bound).
