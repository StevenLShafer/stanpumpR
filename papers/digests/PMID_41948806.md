# Sullivan 2026: TNX-102 SL (sublingual cyclobenzaprine) vs oral ER, steady state

## Study
- Single-centre (Quebec), open-label, randomised 1:1, parallel-group, multiple-dose PK bridging study (NCT03443960), healthy adults, fasted, morning dosing, 20 days once daily, 21-day confinement (PDF p2-3).
- Arms: sublingual cyclobenzaprine HCl 5.6 mg (two 2.8 mg tablets) vs oral ER capsule (AMRIX) 30 mg. n = 30 per arm, all completed. Primary analysis in age 18-65 (SL n = 26, ER n = 30); 4 SL participants aged 65-75 analysed descriptively only (Table 1, p4).
- Demographics (Table 1): mean age 46.6 (23-75) SL, 40.3 (25-60) ER; 50% female SL, 43.3% ER; BMI 25.8 (2.4) and 25.3 (2.7) kg/m2; weight >=50 kg men / >=45 kg women (inclusion); no absolute weights reported.
- Sampling Days 1 and 20: hourly 1-12 h, then 14, 16, 18, 20, 22, 24 h; Day 20 also 36-168 h; norcyclobenzaprine to 648 h. Trough samples Days 3-19. Assay not described in this PDF (plasma; LC method not stated). Non-compartmental analysis, SAS ANOVA of ln AUC/Cmax (p3-4).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None measured. No ke0, t1/2ke0, tPeak, hysteresis, MEAC/EC50 or potency ratio is reported; cyclobenzaprine is a muscle relaxant/sleep agent, not an opioid. The Discussion only asserts receptor occupancy (5-HT2A, alpha1, H1, M1) as a prediction. Safety end points: somnolence 33.3% SL vs 6.7% ER (Table 4).

## Covariates
Age only, descriptively: 4 participants aged 65-75 had about 10-11% higher cyclobenzaprine AUC and a longer t1/2 (43.6 vs 40.3 h) and later tmax (8.5 vs 5.0 h) than ages 18-65 (Discussion p8). No weight, sex or other covariate analysis.

## Caveats
- Healthy volunteers, n = 26 vs 30 in the primary comparison; only 4 older adults; no weights reported.
- Dose-normalised "higher bioavailability" is an inference; no IV reference.
- Plasma concentrations; assay (LLOQ, method) not given in the PDF.
- Trial sponsored by the manufacturer; the authors are employees.
- Terminal t1/2 of 36-40 h with steady state over 20 days; AUC0-t to 168 h so Day 20 AUC0-t is not a dosing-interval value.
