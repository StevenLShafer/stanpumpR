# Ochs 1984: temazepam single and multiple oral dosing

## Study
- 10 healthy volunteers, 5 men / 5 women, 4 smokers; three women on oral contraceptives.
- Age 23.6 ± 0.8 (SE) y, range 20-30 in Table I (abstract says 22-30); weight 66.8 ± 3.3 kg (55-85).
- Single 20 mg oral temazepam, **soft gelatin capsule** (Planum, European), fasting; serum at 0.25-48 h.
- Then 20 mg once daily (08-09 h) for 7 days; predose troughs and full profile after the last dose.
- Electron-capture GLC. Terminal slope β by log-linear regression; AUC trapezoidal + C_last/β;
  CL = dose/AUC assuming 100% availability; Vd = CL/β. Accumulation ratio = AUC(0-24) last dose / first dose.

## Caveats
- Means of individual ratios: CL is not Vd × ln2/t½ of the means (derived: 1.45 × 0.693/8.6 h = 1.95 mL/min/kg).
- CL range 0.71-5.79 is wide; mean CL is about twice the other temazepam studies'.
- Soft gelatin capsule absorbs faster than the US hard capsule (authors' discussion).
- One-compartment summary only; no distribution phase reported.
