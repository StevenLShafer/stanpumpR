# de Jonge 2004: integrated popPK of cyclophosphamide and thiotepa with mutual interaction

## Study
- 49 patients (7 M, 42 F), 86 courses of high-dose CTC or tiny-CTC chemotherapy with stem-cell support
  (Table I, PDF p6): breast cancer stage II/III 16, stage IV 21, ovarian 5, germ cell 7. Median (range) age 45
  (16-59) y, BSA 1.8 (1.55-2.3) m2, weight 69 (52-96) kg, height 170 (156-194) cm, creatinine 62 (40-88) µM,
  albumin 41 (7-49) g/L.
- Regimen (PDF p5): CP 1500 mg/m2/day as a 1 h infusion, then carboplatin 400 mg/m2/day over 1 h, and thiotepa
  (TT) 120 mg/m2/day split into two 30-min infusions 12 h apart, for 4 days (totals CP 6000, TT 480, carboplatin
  1600 mg/m2); tCTC = two-thirds of each dose.
- About 20 plasma samples per course from a Hickman catheter; full profiles on day 1 and usually day 3 or 4
  (30-660 min), plus a day-5 sample. GC assay for TT, tepa (T), CP; HPLC for 4OHCP (semicarbazone).
- NONMEM V 1.1, FO method (FOCE too slow); IIV and IOV exponential; significance ΔOFV 10.8 (p 0.001).

## Model and parameters
TT and T two-compartment; CP two-compartment; 4OHCP one-compartment (V fixed 1, formation-limited). TT clearance
split into non-inducible and inducible parts; the inducible part forms T and is multiplied by a hypothetical
enzyme amount ENZTT (initially 1) whose first-order elimination is inhibited by CP (Emax, effectively on/off).
CP autoinduction by a separate enzyme pool; TT reversibly inactivates the CP enzyme (kass/kdiss). Equations in

Residual error (PDF p14): TT 21.7% proportional + 0.0645 µM additive; T 16.7% proportional; CP 0.242 and 4OHCP
0.270 µM additive (log scale for CP and 4OHCP). Induction raised inducible TT clearance 20-50% and total TT
clearance 10-25% over a course (PDF p10). Table III's label for kenzTT says "first-order formation and zero order
elimination", the reverse of the text and abstract (zero-order formation, first-order elimination).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None: no PD, ke0 or effect measure. Not an opioid paper.

## Covariates
None tested beyond the CP-driven enzyme induction; doses per m2 BSA.

## Caveats
FO estimation; few day-2 TT data, so the time course of ENZTT and its 200% IIV are uncertain (authors, PDF p17).
Two patients without CP motivated the CP-induction hypothesis; TT autoinduction or co-medication cannot be
excluded with these data (PDF p16). Oncology patients on a high-dose regimen.
