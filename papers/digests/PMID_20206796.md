# Salem 2010: population PK of a single 10 mg oral loratadine dose in 72 Jordanian men

## Study
- Retrospective pooled analysis of 2 single-dose PK studies at the International Pharmaceutical Research Center,
  Amman (36 subjects each), 72 healthy white Jordanian men, age 23 (SD 3.57) y, within 15% of ideal body weight.
  **Weight, height not reported.**
- 10 mg loratadine tablet (Claritin, two batches) after a 10 h fast with 240 mL water. Venous samples pre-dose and at
  0.33, 0.66, 1, 1.5, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 16, 24, 36, 48, 72, 96 h.
- Plasma (lithium heparin) loratadine by HPLC-MS/MS (ESI+, 383.11 > 337.20 m/z), linear 0.10-20.00 ng/mL, LLOQ
  0.1 ng/mL, precision 7.44% CV. Metabolite desloratadine (descarboethoxyloratadine) **not measured**.
- Model: 2-compartment with first-order absorption and lag, 6 parameters (Vd/F, ke, k01, lag, k12, k21), Kinetica 4.1
  EM algorithm, uniform weighting. Plus per-subject non-compartmental ke, Cmax, Tmax.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No PD end point, no concentration-effect relation, no metabolite. Not an opioid paper: no MEAC, ke0/tPeak or
potency ratio.

## Covariates
None (all healthy young men, single ethnicity).

## Caveats
- "Shorter half-life" (3.65-5 h) is against literature values of 7.8-11 h (refs 1-4: drug compendia and Hilbert 1987,
  RIA-based), and the population figure is 0.693/ke, not a terminal half-life; the paper's own k12/k21 imply a long
  terminal phase (derived ~93 h), which the 96 h sampling barely reaches. The authors attribute the difference to
  ethnicity/variability; no direct comparator group was studied.
- Described as a re-analysis of "prior noncompartmental analysis results", yet a concentration-time fit was done; what
  data entered the fit (individual concentrations, presumably) is not explicit.
- Vd/F %CV 0.03 is implausible; k12 %CV 241 shows the peripheral parameters are poorly determined. Below-LLOQ handling
  is not described beyond "Undetectable" points in the Figure.
- Extensive first-pass metabolism; F unknown, so all parameters are apparent (/F).
