# Courlet 2021: amlodipine PK and antiretroviral interactions in people living with HIV

## Study
- Population PK (NONMEM 7.3, FOCEI, PsN, Pirana) in 55 people living with HIV (PLWH), 163 amlodipine plasma concentrations (Swiss HIV Cohort Study project 815, Lausanne and Basel, plus intensive-sampling PK study NCT03515772: 8 patients, 84 samples, median 11 samples each; others median 1 sample, range 1-3). Samples up to 28 h after the last dose; steady state assumed.
- Table 1 (PDF p.3, printed p.982), median [IQR]: age 61 [53-70] y; male 41 (75%); weight 79 [71-91] kg (9 missing, imputed to median); CrCl 82 [50-101] mL/min (Cockcroft-Gault).
- Amlodipine 2.5-10 mg once daily; three patients 5 mg twice daily. Measured concentrations 0.4-70 ng/mL.
- Assay: UHPLC-MS/MS, plasma (EDTA), protein precipitation with methanol, LLOQ 0.3 ng/mL. Total (not free) concentration.
- Comedications: ritonavir-boosted darunavir 27 samples; cobicistat-boosted darunavir 1; ritonavir-boosted atazanavir 1; cobicistat-boosted elvitegravir 7; efavirenz 7; etravirine 18; nevirapine 8; rilpivirine 2; dolutegravir 103; raltegravir 8 (Table 1, counted in the 163 samples). No patient had inhibitor plus efavirenz. Etravirine effect not estimable (two patients on etravirine also took ritonavir-boosted darunavir).

## Model and parameters
One-compartment, first-order absorption with lag time (lag: dOFV -7.39). BSV on CL only. Additive residual error. Apparent parameters (CL/F, V/F), not corrected for bioavailability. Base-model estimates in the text (PDF p.3): ka 0.66 /h, ALAG 0.86 h, V 980 L, CL 15.7 L/h, BSV CL 61% CV.

Final model (Table 2, PDF p.5, printed p.983; confirmed on rendered page):
CL/F = 17.0 x (1 - 0.49 x CYP3A4 inhibitor) x (1 + 1.40 x efavirenz).

Shrinkage: 9% on BSV CL, 15% on residual error. Bootstrap n=2000; all estimates within the bootstrap 95% CI, within 7% of the bootstrap median except ka (17%).

Model-based simulations (1000 virtual PLWH, Table 3, PDF p.6, median (95% PI)), 5 mg qd alone: Cmax 13.6 (7.3-29.0) ng/mL; Ctrough 10.2 (3.6-25.5) ng/mL; AUC0-24 290.8 (129.3-658.4) ng h/mL (AUC = dose/CL, derived by the authors). 2.5 mg qd with CYP3A4 inhibitor: Cmax 12.7, Ctrough 10.9, AUC 285.7; GMR 0.92 / 1.08 / 0.98. 10 mg qd with efavirenz: Cmax 13.7, Ctrough 6.5, AUC 242.3; GMR 1.01 / 0.62 / 0.83. Text: at 5 mg qd, inhibitors raise AUC 96% and efavirenz lowers it 59% (derived check: 1/0.51 = 1.96; 1/2.4 = 0.417, i.e. -58%). Text (PDF p.4): efavirenz with 15 mg qd gives Cmax +51%, AUC +25%, Ctrough -7%; 5 mg bid gives Ctrough -15%, Cmax -17%, AUC -16% (supplementary, not seen).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD end point was modelled. The authors note that PK-PD relations between amlodipine concentration and blood-pressure effect exist in other work (refs 7, 9) and that no upper tolerability concentration has been established. No MEAC, ke0, tPeak, EC50 or potency ratio. (Not an opioid.)

## Covariates
Tested: age, sex, weight, albumin, AST, ALT, CrCl (none significant, dOFV > -1.1, p > 0.29). Retained: strong CYP3A4 inhibitors (ritonavir- or cobicistat-boosted regimens; dOFV -20.9) and efavirenz (dOFV -10.8). Together they explained 48% of CL variance. Nevirapine tested; not retained. Limits: small n, sparse sampling, one-compartment model, individual inhibitors not separable.

## Caveats
- Apparent CL/F and V/F; steady state assumed; dose timing from records.
- Sampling to 28 h only; one-compartment model misses the long terminal phase of other papers' two-compartment models. The authors' CL/F of 17 L/h is lower than values in other published analyses (their statement; the comparators are refs 6-9).
- Efavirenz effect imprecise (RSE 37%); etravirine not estimable; no data on inhibitor plus inducer.
- Weight not significant here, unlike other amlodipine population models.
