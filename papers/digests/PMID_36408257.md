# Yang 2022: caspofungin PopPK in all patients and ICU patients

## Study
Retrospective PopPK (NONMEM 7.20, PsN 3.4.2). General model: 299 hospitalised adults (ICU 51, solid-organ transplant 163, haematology 42, other 43), 921 plasma samples (median 3 per patient: 242 troughs at 22-24 h, 247 at 0-12 h, 432 at 12-22 h), LC-MS/MS. Mean weight 62.3 +/- 11.5 kg (30-100), albumin 35.6 g/L; 161 of 299 had albumin <35 g/L (Table 1). ICU-subgroup model: 136 ICU patients (51 own + 85 from seven publications), mean weight 68.1 kg. Dosing: 1 h IV infusion; mostly 70/50 mg (loading/maintenance), 70/35 mg for Child-Pugh B, 70/70 mg for weight >75 kg (Sec 2.1). Monte-Carlo (10,000 replicates) for PTA/CFR using fAUC24/MIC targets of 20 (C. albicans) and 7 (C. parapsilosis, C. glabrata), 97% protein binding; AUC24 = Dose/CL.

## Model and parameters
One-compartment, first-order absorption and elimination; exponential IIV, proportional residual error. Covariates are dichotomised (Table 3 footnotes, PDF p6): ALB* = 1 if albumin <35 g/L; WT* = 1 if weight >=70 kg. General: CL = th1 x (1 + th3 x ALB*) x (1 + th5 x WT*) x e^eta1; V = th2 x (1 + th4 x ALB*) x (1 + th6 x WT*) x e^eta2. ICU: CL = th1 x (1 + th3 x WT*) x e^eta1; V = th2 x e^eta2. Ka is not reported in the main paper.

Model-derived individual values (Table 4, text layer): mean CL 0.42 +/- 0.12 L/h and AUC 126.46 mg h/L for all 299; ICU subgroup CL 0.56 +/- 0.22 L/h, AUC 101.83. Bootstrap: 867/1000 (general) and 895/1000 (ICU) runs converged (p. 7). OFV drop final vs base 135.35 (general), 11.02 (ICU).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No clinical PD or effect-site model; no ke0. The target is a preclinical fAUC24/MIC (20 for C. albicans, 7 for C. parapsilosis and C. glabrata, free fraction 3%), with PTA/CFR >=90% as success. At MIC 0.12 mg/L for C. albicans, 70/50 mg PTA was 86.1% (all) and 73.5% (ICU) (Sec 3.5). CFR values are in Table 5 (scrambled in the text layer; not read). Concentration ranges: only cited troughs of 0.52-4.08 mg/L in ICU patients and 1.12-1.78 mg/L in healthy subjects (Introduction). (Not an opioid.)

## Covariates
Albumin <35 g/L (raises CL and V) and weight >=70 kg (raises both) in the general model; weight alone on CL in the ICU model. Albumin was dropped from the ICU model because >90% of ICU patients were hypoalbuminaemic. Continuous forms did not lower OFV (Discussion). Mycophenolate, cyclosporine, methylprednisolone, CMT, surgery, SOT, ICU group were significant only in forward steps (Table 2).

## Caveats
Sparse sampling, mostly elimination phase; dichotomised covariates; few patients above 70 kg; fixed 97% binding in PTA although albumin changes it; targets from murine data; two-compartment fit was worse (not shown); retrospective, single centre; ICU data partly taken from other papers.
