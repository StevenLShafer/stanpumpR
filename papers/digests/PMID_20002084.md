# Jacobsen 2009: liraglutide pharmacokinetics in renal impairment

## Study
Single-centre (Christchurch, NZ), single-dose, open-label, parallel-group trial (Novo Nordisk). 30 adults aged 31-82:
6 normal renal function (Cockcroft-Gault CrCL > 80 mL/min), 6 mild (>50-80), 7 moderate (>30-50), 5 severe (<=30),
6 ESRD on CAPD (haemodialysis excluded). Three had type 2 diabetes (2 moderate, 1 ESRD); the rest did not. One
**0.75 mg SC** dose into the abdomen, given 21:00-22:00. Plasma sampled -30, -15 min and 2-72 h (24 samples).
Total (bound + free) intact liraglutide by validated ELISA, LLOQ 18 pM. Protein binding by equilibrium dialysis of
spiked pre-dose plasma. Noncompartmental AUC (trapezoidal + extrapolation), Cmax, tmax, t1/2, CL/F; ANOVA on logs
adjusted for renal group, age and log(weight); regression of log AUC on log(CrCL), log(weight) and age (SAS 8 PROC MIXED).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None measured beyond safety glucose (one healthy subject 2.5 mmol/L at 15 h; one severe-group subject four events,
2.8-3.6 mmol/L). No exposure-response, no ke0, no potency ratio. Not an opioid paper.

## Covariates
Renal function (Cockcroft-Gault CrCL): no significant effect on AUC by regression; group comparisons showed lower AUC
and faster CL/F in every impaired group **relative to this study's own 6 healthy subjects** (younger, mean 41.8 y).
Body weight significant on AUC; age not. Albumin: no association (exploratory, data not shown).

## Caveats
- Small groups (5-7), healthy group younger than the others; mostly non-diabetic subjects.
- Equivalence (0.70-1.43) not shown for any group; the authors read the regression as no renal effect and say a
  minor lowering of exposure with impairment cannot be excluded.
- Single 0.75 mg dose, not steady state at 1.2-1.8 mg.
- Author-employer funded (three authors Novo Nordisk employees).

## Verification against stanpumpR
- **CL/F 0.79 L/h, 0.75 mg, normal renal function** (`R/drugs_liraglutide.R:45-46`, help line 11): matches Table 2
  (healthy group mean 0.79, SD 0.29). Read directly here, not through Scheen 2015. **matches**
- **No trend from mild impairment to dialysis**: matches the authors' regression conclusion. The group ratios, however,
  run the other way from accumulation: CL/F 1.16-1.48x and AUC 0.67-0.86x of the healthy group (Table 3), against this
  study's own younger healthy subjects. Not modelling a renal effect is consistent with the paper. **matches**
- **Comparator in "(the model at 70 kg: 0.77 L/h)"**: the healthy group weighed 84.7 kg on average (Table 1). At 84.7 kg
  the Røge model (CL/F 1.83e-4 L/min/kg) gives 0.93 L/h (derived: 1.83e-4 x 60 x 84.7), so the observed 0.79 is
  0.85x the model at the study's weight, not 1.03x. Also derived for a single 0.75 mg dose at 84.7 kg: model AUC
  215 nmol·h/L vs observed 274.3; model Cmax 5.74 nmol/L at 13.3 h vs observed 9.25 at a median 12.5 h; model apparent
  half-life ~12 h vs 14.25 h. The Watson model (`R/drugs_liraglutideWatson.R`, 0.013 L/h/kg) gives 1.10 L/h at 84.7 kg.
  The Røge fit was in type 2 diabetes at steady state, this cohort mostly non-diabetic after one dose, so the
  comparison is indicative only. The repo applies no factor from this paper, so no plotted curve depends on it;
  the 70 kg comparison simply flatters the agreement.
