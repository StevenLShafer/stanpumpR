# Klepser 1996: clindamycin time-kill against Bacteroides fragilis

## Study
In vitro time-kill study (a journal Note); no patients, no pharmacokinetics. Five *B. fragilis* isolates: clinical
strains 11, 13 and 266, reference strains ATCC 25285 and ATCC 23745 (p. 1941). MIC by NCCLS broth microdilution for
anaerobes, inoculum about 5 x 10^5 CFU/mL, prereduced enriched soybean-casein digest broth, 48 h anaerobic.
Kill curves: starting inoculum about 10^6 CFU/mL in 30 mL BACTEC NR7A vials; clindamycin at 1, 4, 16 and 64 x MIC plus
a drug-free control; samples at 0, 4, 8, 12, 24, 36, 48 h; lower limit of quantitation 100 CFU/mL; duplicate runs,
mean log CFU/mL plotted. Bactericidal = >= 3-log fall from the starting inoculum.

## Model and parameters
No PK and no fitted PD model. Table 1 (PDF p. 2, printed p. 1942), confirmed on the rendered page; mean time (h) to
99.9% reduction from the starting inoculum:

| Isolate | MIC (ug/mL) | 1 x MIC | 4 x | 16 x | 64 x |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ATCC 23745 | 0.03 | 24 | 24 | 24 | 24 |
| ATCC 25285 | 0.5 | 48 | 48 | 48 | 48 |
| 11 | 0.5 | 24 | 24 | 24 | 24 |
| 13 | 1.0 | not obtained | 36 | 12 | 12 |
| 266 | 8.0 | not obtained | not obtained | not obtained | not obtained |

Table 1 footnote: NCCLS breakpoints for *B. fragilis* <= 2 susceptible, 4 intermediate, >= 8 ug/mL resistant.
Strain 13 MBC 2 ug/mL (MIC 1) (p. 1942). Strain 266 (resistant): about a 2-log fall, not bactericidal, not improved
above the MIC (Fig. 1E). Strain 13 at 1 x MIC: bacteriostatic early, then regrowth to control counts by 36 h (Fig. 1D).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: log CFU/mL over 48 h and time to 99.9% kill. For three susceptible strains (ATCC 23745, ATCC 25285, 11)
the rate and extent of kill were the same from 1 to 64 x MIC; for resistant strain 266 likewise (no extra kill above
the MIC). Exception: strain 13, where kill rate rose with concentration up to 16 x MIC and not beyond (p. 1942).
The authors conclude clindamycin is concentration-independent (time-dependent) in killing *B. fragilis* and that
dosing should aim at time above MIC (p. 1942). Killing is slow: 24-48 h to 99.9% even at 64 x MIC. No Emax/EC50 fitted.

## Covariates
None.

## Caveats
- Five isolates only, one resistant; duplicate runs, means only, no statistics.
- Static concentrations; no protein binding, no simulated serum profile.
- Strain 13 does not fit the headline (dose-dependence up to 16 x MIC).
- The serum bactericidal study in volunteers mentioned in the Discussion (ref 11b) is an abstract, not data here.
