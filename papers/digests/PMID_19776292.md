# Ide 2009: pravastatin population PK with enterohepatic recirculation and SLCO1B1*15

## Study
57 healthy male Japanese volunteers, 20–40 y, 49.7–97.8 kg, pooled from the group's three earlier studies (refs 16, 33,
34) and recruited by SLCO1B1 haplotype after prescreening ~100 volunteers, so haplotype frequencies are enriched, not
population frequencies (PDF p2 / printed 1310). Single oral pravastatin 10 mg with 150 mL water after ~12 h fast; food at
4 h. Plasma sampling 0–24 h (13 times in 33 subjects, 11 times in 24). 636 samples; 52 pravastatin and 92 RMS-416 values
BQL were omitted (LOQ not stated). Assay: LC-MS/MS of pravastatin and its gastric degradation product 3′α-isopravastatin
(RMS-416). NONMEM VI level 1.0, FOCE-INTERACTION; Xpose 4; PsN bootstrap, 200 replicates.

Genotypes (Table I, PDF p4 / printed 1312, rendered — the text layer misaligns the haplotype rows): *1a/*1a 7, *1a/*1b 6,
*1b/*1b 15, *1a/*15 7, *1b/*15 16, *15/*15 6 (derived: 28 non-carriers, 23 heterozygotes, 6 homozygotes). ABCC2 1446C>G
not observed (57 C/C).

Observed (PDF p3 / printed 1311): Cmax at ~0.5–1 h; about half had a second peak near 6 h. AUC mean ± SD pravastatin
56.2 ± 26.3 ng·h/mL (range 15.9–131), RMS-416 42.3 ± 28.8 ng·h/mL (2.09–125).

## Model and parameters
One-compartment model with an Erlang (transit-chain) absorption, Ndepot = 8, Ktr = Ndepot/MTT, and an enterohepatic loop:
central → gallbladder (K12), gallbladder → central (K21) after gallbladder emptying time tg (Figure 2, PDF p6). OFV:
first-order absorption 1585.7, with lag 1450.2, Erlang EHC model 938.6. Input is an **apparent dose** = 10 mg × Fa, with
Fa = AUCpra/(AUCpra + AUCrms) per subject, to remove gastric isomerisation; so CL/F and V1/F are relative to that apparent
dose, not the nominal 10 mg. Exponential IIV; combined proportional + additive residual error.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No lipid or other effect measured; no target concentration. Not an opioid paper.

## Covariates
Tested: body weight (power function), SLCO1B1*15 (HT/HM indicators), ABCC2 −24C>T, 1249G>A, 3972C>T. Retained only
SLCO1B1*15 on Frel: Frel = 1 · 1.50^HT · 1.95^HM (PDF p4). Comparator: participants **without** a *15 allele
(*1a/*1b haplotypes) in this same study. Backward-elimination ΔOFV 32.2 (heterozygote) and 33.7 (homozygote). No effect
on CL or V1; no ABCC2 effect.

## Caveats
- Healthy young Japanese men, single 10 mg dose; cohort enriched for *15 carriers; no women, elderly or patients.
- CL/F and V1/F are scaled to a per-subject apparent dose (Fa from the RMS-416 ratio), so they cannot be used with the
  nominal dose without an Fa; the mean Fa is not reported (only the mean AUCs, from which a ratio of means would be
  56.2/(56.2+42.3) = 0.57, derived, not the paper's).
- Oral-only data: absolute bioavailability, CL and V are not identifiable.
- CWRES trend at 24 h, attributed to 43 of 57 values at that time being BQL (PDF p6).
- tg was estimated near 3.6 h with the meal given at 4 h.
