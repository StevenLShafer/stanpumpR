# Dilantin-125 oral suspension label (2022)

## Study
Not a study: US label for phenytoin oral suspension, 125 mg phenytoin (free ACID) per 5 mL (sections 3, 11).

## Model and parameters
- Absorption (12.3): peak levels 1.5 to 3 h after a dose of the suspension.
- Steady state at least 7-10 days (5-7 half-lives) at 300 mg/day (12.3; 2.4).
- Elimination (12.3): oral plasma half-life averages 22 h, range 7-42 h.
- Metabolism: CYP2C9, less CYP2C19; saturable; a 10% dose increase can cause disproportionate rise (12.3).
- Salt/acid (2.5): suspension and Infatabs are free acid, ~8% more drug than the sodium salt.
- Dosing (2.2, 2.3): adult start 5 mL (125 mg) three times daily, max 25 mL/day; pediatric 5 mg/kg/day start,
  4-8 mg/kg/day maintenance, max 300 mg/day.
- No V, CL, Vmax, Km or ka.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Therapeutic range total 10-20 mcg/mL (unbound 1-2) (2.4). Overdose section: nystagmus ~20, ataxia ~30,
dysarthria/lethargy >40 mcg/mL. No ke0/MEAC (not an opioid).

## Covariates
As the capsule label: clearance 20% lower over 70 y; CYP2C9 variants lower clearance; pregnancy raises
clearance; unbound fraction higher with renal/hepatic disease or low albumin; sex and race no effect.

## Caveats
Tmax range has no study population or n.
