Acid stable, may be given without regard to meals. Metformin: Cmax +34%, AUC +24%, renal CL -14% (single 500 mg
doses). Half-life, volume and clearance not given.
Dosing (2.1-2.3): adults and >= 15 y 250 mg q6h or 500 mg q12h for 7-14 days, up to 4 g/day in 2-4 doses;
children > 1 y 25-50 mg/kg/day (50-100 severe; otitis media 75-100); renal impairment (Table 1): CrCl 30-59 mL/min
max 1 g/day; 15-29 250 mg q8-12h; 5-14 250 mg q24h; 1-4 250 mg q48-60h.
12.4: active against methicillin-susceptible S. aureus, S. pneumoniae (penicillin susceptible), S. pyogenes,
E. coli, H. influenzae, K. pneumoniae, M. catarrhalis, P. mirabilis; MRSA and most enterococci resistant;
interpretive criteria referred to FDA STIC (no MIC breakpoint printed).

- Binding 10-15%, section 12.3: label 12.3 Distribution, approximately 10% to 15% bound. **matches** (section
  number right). The label does not say "linear"; that comes from the other source (Singhvi 1977).
- Free fraction 0.85: uses the upper binding bound (15%), so the 4.7 mg/L threshold (derived 4 / 0.85 = 4.71) is the
  conservative end of the label range; 10% binding would give 4.44 mg/L. **matches** (a defensible choice).
- The help's "No CLSI or EUCAST cefalexin breakpoint for staphylococci" (antibioticThresholds.R) is not addressed by
  the label, which defers to FDA STIC: **cannot verify** here.
- Not a cited location, for context: the label's 500 mg peak of about 18 mcg/mL at 1 h is higher than the plotted
  model gives for a 70 kg adult (derived from CL/F 13.98 L/h, V/F 26.63 L, ka 1.79/h: tmax 0.97 h, Cmax about 11.3
