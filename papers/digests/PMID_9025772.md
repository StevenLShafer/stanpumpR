# Duffull 1997: sequential Bayesian dose individualisation of carboplatin

## Study
Single-centre PK study in 12 women with ovarian cancer, single-agent carboplatin (PDF p. 2-3, Table 1, p. 5). Age 59.5 +/- 6.9 y (49-68); height 164 +/- 6.8 cm (152-177); weight 62 +/- 10.6 kg (46-85); LBW 56 +/- 6 kg (48-64); GFR by adjusted Cockcroft-Gault 74.2 +/- 20 mL/min (35-98); GFR by DTPA 89 +/- 19 mL/min (54-124). Dose by Calvert formula, target AUC 5-7 mg/mL.min, infused in 500 mL D5W over 30 min. Samples before, at 10 min, 1, 3, 6, 12 and 24 h after the end of infusion (plus 48 h if GFR < 60 mL/min). Assay: HPLC of plasma ultrafiltrate (unbound carboplatin); standard curve 2.5-50 mg/L, CV 6.3% within day and 8.4% between days, limit of detection 0.25 mg/L. Analysis: TOPFIT then NONMEM (ADVAN3/TRANS3), two compartments, lognormal parameters, proportional plus fixed error; covariates kept if objective function fell > 5. The paper also develops a sequential Bayesian dosing algorithm (Metropolis-Hastings error model for the a priori model, Eq. 6; assay error, Eq. 7.0-7.1; Table 3 worked example).

## Model and parameters
Two-compartment, parameterised as Cl, Vss, V1, Clic. LBW = (height cm - 150) x 0.9 + 45 kg (+5 kg men) (Pesola; Table 1 footnote). ClCr = Cockcroft-Gault with LBW (or total weight if lower) and serum creatinine floored at 60 uM (Table 1 footnote, p. 5). Equations (p. 5):
- (5.1) Cl (l/h) = 0.15 x LBW^0.75 + 0.78 x ClCr, CV 15.1%
- (5.2) Vss (l) = 0.38 x LBW, CV 17.3%
- (5.3) V1 (l) = 0.26 x LBW, CV 4.1%
- (5.4) Clic (l/h) = 0.15 x LBW^0.75, CV 0.4% (conflicts with Table 2; see open questions)
- (4) Cl (l/h) = 1.08 x ClCr + 28, r2 = 0.65, slope not different from 1; non-renal 28 (95% CI -14 to +70) not different from Calvert's 25.
Other: r2 of Cl with DTPA 0.32, with age 0.36. AUC by NONMEM 89.8 +/- 21 mg/L.h (about 5.6 mg/mL.min) vs 87.8 +/- 22 by non-compartmental analysis. t1/2 alpha 0.85 +/- 0.3 h, t1/2 beta 3.9 +/- 1.9 h (mean of individuals). Prediction error of the a priori model vs NCA AUC: ME -1.5, RMSE 21.3; Calvert ME -4.2, RMSE 24.8 (no significant difference).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None modelled. The Introduction cites an AUC window of about 5-7 mg/mL.min (from Jodrell 1992) as the target. No MEAC, ke0 or tPeak. Not an opioid paper.

## Covariates
LBW (from height) on Cl, Vss, V1, Clic; adjusted Cockcroft-Gault ClCr on Cl. Weight and height entered NONMEM as the two extra covariates beyond ClCr (p. 5). Age and DTPA GFR correlated singly with Cl but were not retained.

## Caveats
Twelve women only; small n inflates the a priori error (Discussion). CVs for Clic (0.4%) and V1 (4.1%) are implausibly small, so the model carries almost no between-patient variability on those. GFR range 35-98 mL/min. Doses were conventional (AUC 5-7), not high-dose. Unbound drug only, by ultrafiltration.
