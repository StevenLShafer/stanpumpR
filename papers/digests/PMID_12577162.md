# Adams 2003: esketamine-methohexital TIVA vs sevoflurane vs spinal for caesarean section

## Study
- Prospective, single-blind phase IV study; 64 women, ASA I-II, >18 y, elective or urgent caesarean (Misgav-Ladach).
  Exclusions included pre-eclampsia, hypertension >180/100, glucocorticoids incl. betamethasone. (PDF p2-3)
- Randomized general anaesthesia arms (n = 21 each); spinal (SPA) group n = 22, non-randomized control.
  Table 1 (PDF p4): mean age 31/32/32 y, weight 82/82/79 kg (ranges 59-118), height 166-169 cm.
- Esketamine group: atracurium 5 mg, esketamine 0.5 mg/kg, methohexital 1.5 mg/kg (mean given 1.6 mg/kg),
  succinylcholine 1.5 mg/kg; FiO2 1.0 until cord clamping; methohexital ~0.5 mg/kg top-ups as needed.
- Sevoflurane group: methohexital 1.5-2.0 mg/kg (mean 2.0), N2O/O2 FiO2 0.5 + sevoflurane ~1.0 vol% end-tidal.
- Both GA groups after cord clamping: fentanyl 2.5 ug/kg (0.2 mg at 75 kg) + sevoflurane ~1.0 vol% in N2O/O2.
- SPA: 2.6-3.0 ml isobaric bupivacaine 0.5%. Postoperative piritramide 50 ug/kg then ~35 ug/kg boluses on demand.
- Sampling: maternal venous catheter (cephalic/basilic), 5 time points (before induction, skin incision, cord
  clamping, end of skin closure, 30 min after surgery); neonatal blood from the placental part of the umbilical
  arteries at cord clamping. Esketamine by HPLC with UV detection (ref 5), plasma; total (no free fraction).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Clinical end points only: first analgesic request after extubation 14.6 ± 10.9 (esketamine), 22.9 ± 28.1
(sevoflurane), 151.2 ± 208.4 min (SPA); total piritramide 19 ± 14, 21 ± 16, 10 ± 11 mg (Table 1, PDF p4). Recovery
times, haemodynamics (Table 2), catecholamines/ADH/ACTH/cortisol (Table 3), Apgar and umbilical pH (Table 4) similar
between GA arms; no awareness; 3 esketamine patients recalled pleasant dreams.
Opioid audit (fentanyl, piritramide): no MEAC or analgesic concentration, no ke0 / t½ke0 / tPeak, no potency ratio;
no opioid concentrations measured.

## Covariates
None analysed for PK.

## Caveats
- Esketamine (S-enantiomer), not racemic ketamine; HPLC-UV, which may not separate enantiomers but only S was given.
- Mean of a skewed distribution (maternal range 152-2399); neonatal sample is umbilical arterial, not venous.
- No non-pregnant comparator, so nothing about pregnancy-related changes in ketamine clearance or volume.
- SPA group not randomized; Abb. 2 caption gives SPA n=2 (misprint for 22).
