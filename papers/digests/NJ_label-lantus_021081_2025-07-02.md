# Lantus label 2025: insulin glargine U-100

## Study
FDA prescribing information for Lantus vial (10 mL) and SoloStar pen (3 mL), 100 units/mL (U-100), subcutaneous once daily. Revised 5/2025. No new PK study; section 12 is qualitative.

## Model and parameters
The label reports no quantitative PK: no tmax, Cmax, AUC, half-life, bioavailability, clearance or volume. Section 12.3 says only that after subcutaneous injection the serum concentration profile is slower, more prolonged and relatively constant over 24 h with no pronounced peak, compared with NPH insulin. Metabolism: partly at the B-chain carboxyl terminus in the subcutaneous depot to M1 (21A-Gly-insulin) and M2 (21A-Gly-des-30B-Thr-insulin), both active in vitro like human insulin. Effects of age, race, BMI, sex on PK not evaluated; renal and hepatic impairment not studied (8.6, 8.7).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Section 12.2: molar glucose-lowering effect of IV glargine is about the same as human insulin. Glucose-clamp study in type 1 diabetes (glucose infusion rate, 24 h observation): median time to end of effect 14.5 h (range 9.5-19.3) for NPH and 24 h (range 10.8 to >24, observation ended at 24 h) for Lantus. Duration similar after abdominal, deltoid or thigh injection; time course varies between and within patients. No ke0, EC50 or therapeutic concentration range.

## Covariates
None quantified. Starting dose in type 2 diabetes not on insulin 0.2 units/kg or up to 10 units once daily (2.3); type 1 about one-third of total daily insulin. Switching: from Toujeo 80% of the Toujeo dose; once-daily NPH same dose; twice-daily NPH 80% (2.4). Rat carcinogenicity text equates 0.2 units/kg/day with 0.007 mg/kg/day (13.1), so 1 unit is 0.035 mg here (derived: 0.007/0.2).

## Caveats
No PK data to fit a model. The label is for Lantus; the repo's glargine model is fitted to Basaglar.
