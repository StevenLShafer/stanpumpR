# Volz-Zang 1994: esmolol receptor occupancy, PK and exercise effects

## Study
Randomized, single-blind, three-way crossover, 8 healthy non-smoking men (age 23-31 y, mean 27.7; weight 69-87 kg, mean 73.7; height 1.72-2.00 m), PDF p2 (printed p400). Esmolol (Brevibloc) in running saline over 70 min at 100, 250 or 500 ug/kg/min, each preceded by a bolus (600 ug/kg, 1.50 mg/kg, 3.0 mg/kg respectively) to reach steady state within 30 min; days at least 7 d apart. Bicycle ergometry (supine, 10 min, 25 W steps) before (ex 1), during the last 10 min of infusion (ex 2) and 45 min after stopping (ex 3). Plasma esmolol and acid metabolite by HPLC (UV 229 nm; LOD 0.025 ug/mL esmolol, 1 ug/mL metabolite); blood drawn with tricresylphosphate to stop ex vivo hydrolysis. Beta1/beta2 occupancy by in vitro radioreceptor assay (RRA) on plasma (concentration as multiples of Ki, i/Ki). Hill-equation fits for IC50.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End points: exercise-induced rise in heart rate (HR) and systolic BP (SBP); resting HR/SBP; beta1 occupancy (RRA).
- Exercise-induced rise without esmolol: HR +84 bpm, SBP +80 mmHg; with esmolol HR +75, +61, +52 bpm and SBP +52, +37, +34 mmHg at 100, 250, 500 ug/kg/min (PDF p4).
- IC50 (Hill fit, Fig 6, PDF p5 image-confirmed): infusion rate 113.5 ug/kg/min (HR) and 134.3 ug/kg/min (SBP) (text rounds to 114 and 134). Corresponding plasma esmolol IC50 0.54 ug/mL (HR) and 0.62 ug/mL (SBP), i.e. i/Ki 2.5 and 3.2 (printed p403). Plasma, HPLC, esmolol parent only; total (not free) not specified.
- Resting HR fell 8.8, 4.9, 3.9 bpm (not dose dependent); resting SBP fell 8.2 mmHg (250) and 14.2 mmHg (500).
- Metabolite: no effect (effects identical at ex 1 and ex 3 when metabolite 80 ug/mL, esmolol undetectable).
- No ke0, t1/2 ke0, tPeak, hysteresis, MEAC or potency ratio reported; effect was assessed at (quasi) steady state. Beta1/beta2 affinity ratio 34-fold quoted from an earlier abstract (ref 19).

## Covariates
None analysed (all rates per kg body weight).

## Caveats
n = 8 young healthy men; 4 of 8 withdrew from exercise at the higher rates (1 at 250, 3 at 500); plasma not whole blood; IC50 values in vivo are higher than in vitro Ki because of competing sympathetic agonist (authors' explanation); no PK parameters.

## Verification against stanpumpR
- Registry row: EC50 113 and 134 mcg/kg/min as infusion rates, end point exercise HR and SBP, healthy adults: **matches** (Fig 6, PDF p5; the paper calls them IC50, 113.5 and 134.3).
- Registry limitation text says a concentration EC50 would need clearance the study does not report: **discrepancy (minor)**. The paper reports plasma IC50 0.54 and 0.62 ug/mL directly and Css per rate; it is true that no clearance is reported.

Plasma vs whole blood is a confound. No error found.
- The model sets MEAC = 0 and has no band; this paper offers IC50 0.54/0.62 ug/mL (plasma) for exercise attenuation, but it is a concentration at steady state during stress, not a minimum effective level.
