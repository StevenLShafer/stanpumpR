# DeVane 1993: alprazolam population PK from postmarketing sparse sampling

## Study
- 94 psychiatric inpatients on alprazolam (Gainesville VA and Shands, University of Florida): 70 men, 24 women;
  age 47.7 +/- 13.3 y; weight 175.5 +/- 38.1 lb (79.6 kg, derived). Indications: panic, GAD, PTSD, anxious
  depression and others. Only 14 had no concurrent illness; 38 cardiovascular disease; 50 (53%) on tricyclics or
  antipsychotics (Table I; table layout partly garbled).
- Two blood samples per patient at random times in two different dose intervals, presumed steady state;
  183 concentrations (20.8 +/- 18.7 ng/mL, range 1.0-129.3).
- Assay: HPLC-UV 214 nm (Miller and DeVane 1988); sensitivity 2.0 ng/mL (1.0 with 2 mL plasma); CV 2.7-7%.
- NONMEM; one-compartment, first-order absorption and elimination. Covariates on CL tested as fractional
  indicator effects (univariate forward, then backward elimination; delta OBJ 3.84).

## Model and parameters
Equation 1: CL/F = theta_WT x WT x (1 + theta_SEX x SEX)(1 + theta_GI x GI)(1 + theta_CARD x CARD)(1 + theta_TA x TA)
x (1 + theta_MULTI x MULTI)(1 + theta_AGE x AGE)(1 + theta_SMOKE x SMOKE), indicators 0/1 (AGE = 1 if > 60 y;
MULTI = two or more disease states). V/F modelled only as a function of weight; ka a population constant;
no IIV estimable on V.

Phase I comparison column (Upjohn unpublished, Table II): CL 0.06 L/h/kg, V 1.0 L/kg, ka 1.3 /h, IIV CL 35%,
IIV V 20%, residual 20%.

Univariate results (Table III; label column misaligned in the text layer; values per text): women +57.9%, tricyclic
or antipsychotic +25.9%, multiple organ disease -34.4%; GI disease and age > 60 marginal; heart disease and smoking
not significant. Tricyclics dropped out in multivariate analysis.

Dose-concentration regression (Fig. 1): Cpss (ng/mL) = 2.3 + 1317.8 x daily dose (mg/kg/day); the text says this
implies CL 0.05 L/h/kg. Check (derived): 1 mg/kg/day / 1317.8 ng/mL = 0.032 L/h/kg, so the printed slope does not
reproduce 0.05.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None (PK only). No ke0, EC50 or MEAC.

## Covariates
Weight (proportional on CL and V), age > 60 (-23% CL), female sex (+59% CL), multiple concurrent illness (-26% CL).
Smoking, cardiac disease, GI disease and tricyclics not retained.

## Caveats
- Sparse, mostly steady-state sampling: CL is well identified; V and ka are poorly identified (V below phase I,
  no IIV on V; ka a single constant).
- Apparent oral parameters (divided by F); heterogeneous inpatients with comorbidities and co-medication;
  24 women only.
- Authors note a sex difference has been seen inconsistently in other studies.
- Internal inconsistency between the regression slope and the stated 0.05 L/h/kg (above).
