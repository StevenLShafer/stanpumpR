# Kaiser 1989: benazepril and benazeprilat, single and repeated oral doses

## Study
Healthy MEN. Single dose: 59 volunteers from five studies (19-32 y, 56-91 kg, mean 73.2 +/- 8.2 kg) given 10 mg
benazepril HCl fasting, plus 3 subjects' first dose from the repeated-dose study, 62 in all (PDF p3, p5). Repeated dose:
15 men (20-33 y, 58-89 kg, mean 75.4 +/- 8.8): 11 on 5, 10 or 20 mg once daily for 28 days, 4 on 20 mg single then six
daily doses (14C-labelled first and last dose) (PDF p3). Capsules 5 mg (10.85 umol), 10 mg (21.69 umol), 20 mg
(43.39 umol). Plasma and urine to 24, 48 or 72 h; GC-MS for benazepril and benazeprilat (PDF p3). Model-independent
analysis: trapezoidal AUC, half-lives by log-linear regression, benazeprilat t1/2,beta from levels at 24 h and later,
t1/2,alpha from 4-12 h by residuals; AUC(0-inf) = AUC(0-t) + Ct x t1/2,beta / ln2; CLr = TUE(0-24h)/AUC(0-24h)
(PDF p3-4). No compartmental model.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD model. Plasma ACE fell below 10% of baseline within 1 h of >= 5 mg and stayed there at least 12 h (Introduction,
citing ref 3, PDF p1-2); maximum ACE inhibition 1-2 h after 2-20 mg (Discussion, PDF p10). The authors state that
unchanged benazepril has virtually no ACE-inhibitory activity at plasma concentrations and that parent kinetics are of
minor importance (PDF p10). Accumulation ratios that fall with dose are offered as support for ACE-bound,
fixed-amount terminal-phase drug and possible non-linearity (PDF p11-12). Effective accumulation half-lives compared
with 11.1 h for enalaprilat (PDF p11). Not an opioid.

## Covariates
None analysed. Renal clearance 22.7 mL/min vs 158 (enalaprilat) and 106 (lisinopril) from other sources; animal data
suggest biliary as well as renal excretion (PDF p11).

## Caveats
- Non-compartmental summaries; AUC(0-24h) (N 60) and AUC(0-inf) (N 45) come from different subject sets.
- Cmax is a mean of individual peaks with tmax 1-6 h.
- Repeated-dose groups are small (n 2-8 per dose); the 10 mg RAUC is two individual values.
- Men only, 19-33 y.
