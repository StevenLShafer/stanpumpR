# Cerebyx (fosphenytoin) label (2022)

## Study
Not a study: label for fosphenytoin sodium injection, 50 mg PE/mL (2 mL = 100 mg PE, 10 mL = 500 mg PE),
IV or IM. Includes Figure 1, a crossover in 12 healthy subjects of 1200 mg PE at 100 or 150 mg PE/min vs
1200 mg Dilantin at 50 mg/min (unbound phenytoin, 96 h sampling).

## Model and parameters
- Dose units (2.1, 11): always mg phenytoin sodium equivalents (PE); 1.5 mg fosphenytoin sodium = 1 mg PE.
  MW of fosphenytoin 406.24 (11). One mmol fosphenytoin yields one mmol phenytoin (12.3).
- Rates (2.3, 2.4): IV max 150 mg PE/min adults; pediatric 2 mg PE/kg/min or 150 mg PE/min, whichever slower.
- Doses: status epilepticus load 15-20 mg PE/kg (Table 1); non-emergent load adult 10-20, pediatric 10-15 mg
  PE/kg (Table 2); adult maintenance 4-6 mg PE/kg/day (Table 3).
- Fosphenytoin PK (12.3): IV peak at end of infusion; IM completely bioavailable, fosphenytoin peak ~30 min;
  protein binding 95-99% (saturable; displaces phenytoin); V of fosphenytoin 4.3-10.8 L, rising with dose
  and rate; conversion half-life to phenytoin about 15 min; not excreted in urine.
- Phenytoin after Cerebyx (12.3): conversion complete; after IM, total phenytoin peaks about 3 h; maximal
  serum phenytoin ~10-20 min after end of IV infusion (2.3); conversion essentially complete ~2 h after IV,
  4 h after IM (2.5); unbound fraction ~12% normally, up to 30% during conversion (0.5-1 h post-infusion);
  phenytoin half-life 12.0-28.9 h, longer at higher concentrations; AUC rises more than proportionally with
  dose (400-1200 mg PE); 1-5% unchanged phenytoin in urine.
- Substitution (2.6): Dilantin capsules ~90% bioavailable orally; phenytoin from Cerebyx 100% IV and IM.
- 15-20 mg PE/kg at 100-150 mg PE/min approximates free phenytoin from phenytoin sodium at 50 mg/min.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Target total phenytoin 10-20 mcg/mL (unbound 1-2) (2.5). Immunoassays (TDx, Emit) overestimate phenytoin
before conversion is complete. Overdose concentrations as for phenytoin; formate and phosphate toxicity. No
ke0/MEAC (not an opioid).

## Covariates
Age 5-98 y no effect on fosphenytoin PK; phenytoin clearance 20% lower over 70 y; renal/hepatic disease or
low albumin may speed fosphenytoin conversion without raising phenytoin clearance; CYP2C9 variants (12.5).

## Caveats
Conversion half-life is a rounded label figure; Boucher 1989 measured shorter (code cites 8.0 min).
