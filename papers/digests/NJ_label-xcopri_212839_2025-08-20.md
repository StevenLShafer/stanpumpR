Dosage (Table 1, p2, rendered): 12.5 mg qd weeks 1-2; 25, 50, 100, 150 mg qd for two weeks each (weeks 3-10);
maintenance 200 mg qd from week 11; maximum 400 mg qd, rising by 50 mg every two weeks above 200 mg. Taken with or
without food; whole or crushed in water by mouth or nasogastric tube (2.4, p3). Discontinue over at least 2 weeks.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No ke0, no exposure-response numbers, no therapeutic or toxic concentration range. QTcF: mean ddQTc -11 (-13, -8) ms
at 200 mg qd and -18 (-22, -15) ms at 500 mg qd; QT shortening > 20 ms in 31% (200 mg) and 66% (500 mg) against
6-17% on placebo; no QTc below 300 ms (12.2, p17). Dose-dependent somnolence/fatigue (31%, 36%, 57% at 100, 200,
400 mg/day vs 19% placebo) and dizziness/gait (21%, 31%, 52% vs 18%) in Studies 1 and 2 (5.5, p7).

## Covariates
"No clinically significant differences" by age (18-77 years), sex, or race/ethnicity (Asian, Black, Caucasian,
Hispanic, Other) (12.3 Specific Populations, p18). Renal: 8.6 (p15) and Highlights: use with caution, dose reduction
"may be considered" in mild to moderate (CLcr 30 to < 90 mL/min) and severe (< 30) impairment; not recommended in ESRD
on dialysis. **No numerical renal maximum is given.** Hepatic: maximum 200 mg qd in Child-Pugh A or B (2.3, 8.7),
not recommended in Child-Pugh C. Drug interactions (Table 5, p11-13, rendered p11): lamotrigine and carbamazepine
concentrations down (increase their dose as needed); phenytoin up, "potential 2-fold", decrease phenytoin dose
gradually by up to 50%; phenobarbital and desmethylclobazam up (consider dose reduction); CYP2B6 and CYP3A
substrates and oral contraceptives down; CYP2C19 substrates up. Valproic acid, levetiracetam and lacosamide: no
clinically significant change (12.3, p19). Valproate, phenobarbital and carbamazepine: no significant effect on
cenobamate exposure (p19). In vitro: inhibits CYP2B6, 2C19, 3A; induces CYP2B6, 2C8, 3A4; does not induce 2C19
(p19-20).

## Caveats
A label: summary values without variability or study sizes. The renal and hepatic numbers are single-dose
ratios. The lamotrigine range is population-PK prediction, not a dedicated DDI study. Mechanisms of the AED
interactions are not stated for lamotrigine or for phenytoin's effect on cenobamate. The label's 12.3 phrasing of the
omeprazole effect (Cmax +83%, AUC +107%) is the reverse of the 2019 FDA review (AUC +83%, Cmax +107%); unresolved
here and not used by the repo.
