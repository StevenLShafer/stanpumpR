# Liukas 2011: IV oxycodone clearance by age group

## Study
Open comparative study, n = 41 Caucasian orthopaedic patients in four age groups: 20-40 y (n 11, ACL arthroscopy),
60-70, 70-80, 80-90 y (n 10 each, knee prosthesis). ASA I-IV; BMI > 35 and significant hepatic/renal disease excluded.
Spinal bupivacaine, then epidural bupivacaine 1 mg/mL + fentanyl 5 µg/mL postoperatively. Dose: oxycodone 5 mg IV
bolus (OxyNorm) in the recovery room immediately after surgery (salt vs base not stated). Venous samples 2.5 min to
24 h (18 samples). LC-MS/MS; LLOQ 0.1 ng/mL oxycodone and oxymorphone, 0.25 ng/mL noroxycodone and noroxymorphone.
CYP2D6 genotyped (37 EM, 1 UM, 1 IM, 2 PM). GFR estimated by the abbreviated MDRD equation, constant 186 with creatinine
in µmol/L/88.4, exponent -1.154, age^-0.203, × 0.742 if female (Eq. 1; cited to Levey 2003 K/DOQI guidelines, ref 15).
Noncompartmental analysis (WinNonlin 4.1); ANOVA/Tukey; Pearson correlations with eGFR.

Table I (mean ± SD): age 27.1 ± 4.8, 66.3 ± 2.5, 76.5 ± 2.7, 83.5 ± 2.6 y; weight 81 ± 14, 83 ± 9, 82 ± 13, 68 ± 8 kg;
height 177, 169, 169, 160 cm; women/men 3/8, 5/5, 4/6, 9/1; eGFR 108 ± 10, 82 ± 16, 76 ± 17, 64 ± 19 mL/min/1.73 m^2.
eGFR was available for all patients > 60 y but for only 3 of the 11 young patients.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD measured: no MEAC, no ke0 / tPeak, no potency ratio. Four of the oldest patients desaturated (< 90%) in the
first 35 min. The authors state the parent drug appears responsible for analgesia (cited). Discussion: the oral route
produces more oxidative metabolites than IV (attributed to their earlier oral study and Lalovic 2006).

## Covariates
Age group (CL); eGFR (MDRD) correlated with CL, Vss and AUC; no sex effect; CYP2D6 effect not assessable.
About 10% of oxycodone is excreted unchanged (Discussion, cited).

## The dose basis: decided by the paper's own summary statistics
The paper names a product and never a salt: "The study medication, 5 mg of intravenous oxycodone (OxyNorm;
Mundipharma, Bard Pharmaceuticals, Cambridge, UK)" (PDF p3, printed 43). OxyNorm is the ANHYDROUS hydrochloride, so
5 mg of it contains 4.48 mg of base. CL and Vss were computed "using standard noncompartmental methods" with
WinNonlin 4.1 (p4) and no basis is stated. **Steven L. Shafer ruled on 2026-10-29 that the computation is on the
base**, and the reason is arithmetic the paper itself supplies.

**The bound, which is exact and needs no assumption about the data.** Per subject CL_i/w_i = D/(A_i w_i) with A the
AUC to infinity. Then

- by Jensen's inequality, which is exact for positive x: mean(1/x) >= 1/mean(x), so mean(CL/kg) >= D / mean(A w);
- mean(A w) = mean(A) mean(w) + cov(A, w), and cov(A, w) <= sd_A sd_w by Cauchy-Schwarz (using n-1 SDs makes this
  conservative);
- therefore **D <= mean(CL/kg) x (mean(A) mean(w) + sd_A sd_w)**, whatever the correlation between AUC and weight.

With Table II's CL and AUC-inf and Table I's weights, the ceiling on the dose is 5.04, **4.66**, 5.06 and 4.97 mg in
the 20-40, 60-70, 70-80 and 80-90 y groups. Push every rounding toward 5 mg (means +0.05, SDs +0.05 and +0.5,
weights +0.5) and it is 5.15, 4.76, 5.16 and 5.08. **The 60-70 y group alone excludes 5 mg**: 8.6 x (6.4 x 83 +
1.2 x 9) = 8.6 x 542 = 4661 ug = 4.66 mg, against the 4.48 mg of base in a 5 mg OxyNorm tablet-equivalent dose.

**Supporting, approximate.** The second-order expectation E[1/X] ~ (1 + CV^2)/mean, with AUC and weight independent,
predicts on 4.48 mg a mean CL/kg of 12.1, 8.8, 8.6 and 7.9 against the reported 11.9, 8.6, 8.6 and 7.9; on 5 mg it
predicts 13.6, 9.9, 9.6 and 8.8, which is 11-15% high in every group.

**The alternative, recorded.** The arithmetic shows only that CL x AUC is about 4.5 mg; "base" is the natural reading
of that, not a statement in the paper. A mis-scaled AUC column would do the same, but it would have to land within
2% of the base figure in all four groups independently.

**Conversion to the app's basis**: x 351.83/315.37 = **1.1156**, so the young group's CL 11.9 -> 13.28 mL/min/kg and
Vss 3.7 -> 4.13 L/kg. The ratios between the age groups are unchanged, so the age model is unaffected.

## Caveats
Postoperative setting with neuraxial bupivacaine/fentanyl; different surgery in young vs old; women over-represented
in the oldest group; youngest group's eGFR from n = 3; abbreviated MDRD (186, non-IDMS) rather than CKD-EPI; dose
salt form not stated (see above - it is inferred, not read); Table II text layer interleaved.
