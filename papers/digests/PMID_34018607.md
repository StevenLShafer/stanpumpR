# Sun 2021: population PK of olanzapine and samidorphan

## Study
- 10 OLZ/SAM studies (9 phase 1 intensive, 1 phase 3 sparse) in healthy subjects and patients with schizophrenia; olanzapine dataset 601 subjects, 9905 concentrations (5.6% < LLOQ, handled by M3).
- Olanzapine 5-30 mg orally, as OLZ/SAM bilayer tablet (65%) or olanzapine-alone tablet (35%).
- Table 1 (olanzapine set): age median 36 (18-73) y; 32% women; 42% Black, 55% White; 41% healthy, 59% schizophrenia; weight 76.9 (44-141) kg; smokers 48%, not recorded 29%.
- Samidorphan dataset (Results p. 4, printed 1433; Methods p. 2): 521 subjects, 9321 concentrations, 11.5% < LLOQ; the 10 OLZ/SAM studies plus 1 phase 1 samidorphan-alone study (11 in all, as the abstract says). Samidorphan 5-30 mg. Table 1 (samidorphan set): age median 34 (18-73) y; 29% women; 50% Black, 47% White; 57% healthy, 43% schizophrenia; weight 76.4 (46.5-130.0) kg; BMI 25.6; smokers 35%, not reported 43%; bilayer tablet 78%.
- LC-MS/MS, LLOQ 0.250 ng/mL. NONMEM 7.3, IMP/IMPMAP.

## Model and parameters

### Samidorphan (Results pp. 4-5, Table 4 p. 7, Discussion pp. 8-9)
Same structure as olanzapine: two compartments, first-order absorption with lag, linear elimination (6 structural parameters). Final model: 8 covariates, weight on CL/F and Vc/F (fixed allometric) and 6 categorical (Table 2, p. 5: rifampin +170%, moderate hepatic -19%, severe renal -43% on CL/F; ALAG +10-fold in study ALK3831-A305, +41% for the samidorphan-alone tablet; fed -90% on ka). Typical values above (Table 4). Reference subject as for olanzapine; samidorphan reference Cmax,ss 33.4 ng/mL and AUCtau 284 ng·h/mL at 10 mg qd (Figure 3 legend, p. 10) (derived: 10 mg / 284 = 35.2 L/h, consistent with CL/F 35.4).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None (PK only). No receptor occupancy, ke0 or threshold. Not an opioid (samidorphan is an opioid antagonist; no opioid PD reported).

## Covariates
Above. Table 2 says Black vs non-Black −10% while Table 3 (1.10) and the Discussion (10% higher) say +10%: an internal inconsistency; Table 3/Discussion taken as correct.

Samidorphan covariate results (simulated steady state, 10/10 mg qd; Results p. 5, Discussion pp. 8-9): no effect of age, sex, race or smoking. Weight: AUCtau ratio 1.39 to 0.60 and Cmax,ss ratio 1.49 to 0.52 over 44-141 kg relative to 70 kg, judged not clinically meaningful; note 44-141 kg is the olanzapine data set's weight range (the samidorphan set spans 46.5-130.0 kg, Table 1). Model vs dedicated clinical study (ref 25/26): rifampin CL/F x2.7 (study x3.7); severe renal impairment CL/F -43% (study -56%); moderate hepatic impairment predicted AUCtau x1.23 (study AUCinf x1.5 after a single dose); fed state predicted Cmax,ss -29% (food study -15%).

## Caveats
Apparent oral parameters; samidorphan ka is poorly determined (IIV 219%, fed effect RSE 36.9%) and its Vp/F IIV is 98.8%; lag fixed from an earlier model; 29% smoking status missing (pooled with nonsmokers as reference); combination product data; adults only.
