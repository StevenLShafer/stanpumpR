# Man 2019: benzonatate and BBA PK, healthy Chinese volunteers

## Study
Mainly a bioanalytical-method paper with a small PK application. Two HPLC-MS/MS methods (ESI+, MRM m/z 194.2 -> 138.1, telmisartan internal standard) for 4-(butylamino)benzoic acid (BBA), the hydrolysis product/metabolite of benzonatate. Method A measures total BBA (blood left at room temperature >= 2 h so that benzonatate hydrolyses completely to BBA, shown by about 100% appearance at 2 h, Fig. 1); method B measures original BBA (blood collected with neostigmine metilsulfate esterase inhibitor, ice bath, EDTA-K2; sodium fluoride was less effective). Benzonatate is then calculated as (total BBA - original BBA) x MW(benzonatate, average) 603.74 / MW(BBA) 193.24 (Eq. 3, printed p. 136). Both methods: linear 10-10000 ng/mL, LLOQ 10 ng/mL, plasma 50 or 100 uL, protein precipitation with methanol; accuracy and precision within FDA limits (Tables 1-6); incurred-sample reanalysis within about -5 to +11%.

PK study: n = 4 healthy Chinese volunteers (2 men, 2 women), fasted, one 100 mg benzonatate soft capsule with 240 mL water; samples 0 to 10 h (16 times, first 0.167 h); weights, ages and doses per kg not given. Approved by Beijing Anzhen Hospital ethics committee; registration 2016L06776.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No ke0, effect-site, MEAC or potency data. Not an opioid. Assay: total plasma concentration by LC-MS/MS; the paper reports no free fraction.

## Covariates
None examined (n = 4).

## Caveats
Four subjects; no weights or ages; one dose; fasted single capsule only. Benzonatate is a polymer mixture of 15 components (average MW 603.74), so its concentration is inferred indirectly from BBA, and benzonatate esters hydrolyse ex vivo (temperature, anticoagulant and esterase inhibitor all matter; Section 3.1). Benzonatate and BBA Tmax values are identical, as the means come from the same subjects/time points. The authors call the study preliminary.
