# Léger 2002: creatinine assay (Jaffé vs enzymatic) and Chatelut carboplatin clearance

## Study
Prospective PK study, Institut Claudius-Regaud, Toulouse (PDF p2, printed p53). 40 cancer patients,
age 23-83 y, 20 M / 20 F, mean (range) weight 64 (41-96) kg, serum creatinine 91 (61-172) µM. Carboplatin
60-min IV infusion, 265-1465 mg, with 5-FU (11), paclitaxel (9) or other drugs. Dose = target AUC × CL, CL
predicted a priori by the Chatelut formula with Jaffé creatinine corrected to the enzymatic scale by
Scr = (Jaffé − 1.6)/1.08 (µM), from the authors' earlier comparison on 244 sera (ref 8), in which kinetic Jaffé
read 13.9% higher on average than the enzymatic (creatinine amidohydrolase, Vitros/Ektachem) assay.
Two samples per patient, 1 h and 4 h after end of infusion (limited sampling strategy, ref 10); plasma
ultrafiltrate (free) platinum by flameless atomic absorption. NONMEM V, two-compartment model, proportional
residual and inter-patient error; the 40 patients pooled with a 103-patient database (n = 143). Analysis (i):
FOCE POSTHOC individual CL with no covariates; analysis (ii): FO with the Chatelut covariates plus MET.

## Model and parameters
Chatelut formula as used (PDF p2, printed p53; confirmed on page image), CL in mL/min:
CL = 0.134·weight(kg) + 218·weight(kg)·(1 − 0.00457·age(y))·(1 − 0.314·sex) / Scr(µM), sex 0 = male, 1 = female;
weight = actual weight, or mean of actual and ideal (Lorentz) weight for obese patients (ref 9).
Covariate model with assay (PDF p4, printed p55): denominator Scr(µM)·(1 − θ·MET), MET = 0 enzymatic (database,
n = 103), MET = 1 Jaffé (present study, n = 40). θ = +0.116 (95% CI −0.014 to +0.246); objective function fell
by 9.4 (P < 0.01). No structural PK parameters (V1, k12, etc.) are reported.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. AUC-targeted dosing only; no toxicity or efficacy end point, no concentration-effect data.
Not an opioid paper.

## Covariates
Weight, age, sex, serum creatinine (Chatelut), plus creatinine assay (MET) as a multiplicative factor on Scr.

## Caveats
- The Jaffé-enzymatic relation is one laboratory's (Toulouse kinetic Jaffé vs Vitros enzymatic); other Jaffé
  and enzymatic (e.g. Roche PAP) methods differ, as the discussion notes (Ando 2000 adds 17.7 µmol/L to PAP).
- θ's 95% CI includes 0; the "uncorrected" bias's CI also just includes 0, though the one-sided test was
  significant (P < 0.05).
- Precision (rmse ~27%) is unchanged by the correction; one patient over-predicted by +96% (predicted 222,
  actual 114 mL/min, Scr 61 µM).
- Only two samples per patient; individual CL is Bayesian on a 143-patient prior.
- Pre-IDMS era; "enzymatic" here is not necessarily IDMS-traceable.
