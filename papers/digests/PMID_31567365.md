# Kuizenga 2019: propofol and sevoflurane PD with clinical score and PSI

## Study
Re-analysis of a published crossover trial (Kuizenga 2018, Br J Anaesth 120:525-536; NCT02043938). 36 healthy ASA I volunteers (stratified by age, sex and remifentanil group; weight 70-130% of ideal; demographics in the online Supplement, not in this PDF), four sessions at least 1 week apart in random order: propofol, sevoflurane, remifentanil plus step-dose propofol, remifentanil plus step-dose sevoflurane. Remifentanil effect-site target 2 or 4 ng/mL (Minto model; between-subject groups), started 2 min before the hypnotic. Propofol by effect-site TCI (Schnider) with steps 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2.5, 3.5, 4.5, 6, 7.5 mcg/mL; sevoflurane end-tidal 0.2, 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2.5, 3.5, 4, 4.5 vol% (closed-loop). 12 min equilibration per step (PDF p.3). Responses: MOAA/S below 3 = no response to name (NRCN), below 2 = tolerance to shake and shout (TOSS), tolerance to tetanic stimulation (TOTS, up to 30 s electrical stimulus); PSI-1 and PSI-2 computed post hoc from SedLine EEG. Data: 891 NRCN/TOSS and 781 TOTS observations; 451 propofol and 204 remifentanil arterial concentrations (LC-MS/MS), 440 matched end-tidal sevoflurane values. Analysis used measured arterial propofol (mcg/mL, plasma) and end-tidal sevoflurane (vol%) as the concentration (steady state, so no effect-site model); remifentanil used predicted effect-site target. NONMEM 7.3 (FOCE for PSI, LAPLACE for binary), sigmoid Emax; lognormal IIV.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Not an opioid paper; remifentanil is a covariate (a co-administered opioid), but for completeness: no MEAC, no ke0/t1/2 ke0/tPeak and no equianalgesic ratio is reported for remifentanil. It was targeted at 2 or 4 ng/mL predicted effect-site (Minto model, whose ke0 is not restated); remifentanil Ce effects on C50 are given above. End points are clinical responsiveness and PSI, not analgesia, EEG-ke0 or CSF.
Hypnotic-opioid interaction: remifentanil 2 ng/mL lowered the propofol C50 for PSI-2 by 13.5% (1.63 to 1.41 mcg/mL); 4 ng/mL lowered it by 81.6% (RSE 62.3%); sevoflurane PSI C50 unchanged. No ke0 is estimated because steady state was used; hysteresis is not analysed.
The authors recommend an upper PSI-2 limit near 35 with propofol; PSI-2 50-25 is adequate for sevoflurane (p.12).

## Covariates
Only remifentanil group (0, 2, 4 ng/mL). No age, weight or sex covariate effect reported in the text (the design stratified for them).

## Caveats
Healthy volunteers; 36 subjects; remifentanil groups are between-subject (stratified), not crossover; predicted rather than measured remifentanil used; C50 depends on observer and stimulus (the authors say comparing with other studies is of limited relevance); MOAA/S end points are binary. Measured arterial propofol (plasma) used as the concentration; end-tidal sevoflurane for the gas.
