# Gordon 1972: Serum protein binding of aminoglycosides

## Study
In vitro ultrafiltration of undiluted pooled human serum from healthy volunteers, pH adjusted to 7.4-7.5 by bubbling 5% CO2, 37 C, cellophane membrane (Union Carbide), vacuum ultrafiltration (0.8 mL filtrate in 45 min). Concentrations: gentamicin and tobramycin 5 ug/mL; kanamycin 5 and 15; streptomycin 15. Assay: agar well microbiological assay with Bacillus subtilis ATCC 6633, standards in serum and in drug-free filtrate. Membrane retention checked with buffered saline (< 3%). Percent bound = (serum - filtrate)/serum x 100.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. Discussion: earlier reports of 0-30% gentamicin binding attributed to method (e.g. trichloroacetic acid precipitation, high concentrations); serum calcium rather than binding explains reduced activity against P. aeruginosa in serum.

## Covariates
None. Divalent cations not controlled beyond native serum (Myers 1978 later showed binding depends on Ca/Mg).

## Caveats
Single concentration (5 ug/mL); microbiological assay variability; serum, not plasma; no patient sera.
