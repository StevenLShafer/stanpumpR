# Kirvela 1993: Glycopyrronium pharmacokinetics in uraemic patients

## Study
11 uraemic patients undergoing cadaveric renal transplantation (dialysed within 24 h; serum creatinine 731 (SD 267) umol/L) vs 7 ASA I controls having general surgery (creatinine 75 (17) umol/L); groups comparable in age and weight (values not given). Glycopyrronium **4 ug/kg i.v.** about 15 min before induction (thiopentone, fentanyl, vecuronium, isoflurane/N2O). Venous samples 2 min to 24 h (central venous catheter in uraemic, peripheral cannula in controls); urine to 24 h. Assay: radioreceptor assay (sensitivity 60 pg/mL, CV < 10%). Biexponential fit (PCNonlin 3.0); AUC by trapezoid; Mann-Whitney tests. One graft had immediate function.

At 6 h plasma concentrations were about 10 times higher in the uraemic patients; 200-300 pg/mL remained at 24 h.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None measured. Discussion: low residual concentrations have little heart-rate effect but may prolong antisecretory and gut effects; repeated or large doses should be avoided in uraemia.

## Covariates
Uraemia (group comparison only); no continuous renal-function relationship.

## Caveats
Small groups; different sampling sites (central vs peripheral); terminal half-lives far shorter than later LC-MS/MS studies (e.g. Bartels 2013 i.v. t1/2 6.2 h), reflecting assay sensitivity and 24-h sampling; AUC units appear misprinted.
