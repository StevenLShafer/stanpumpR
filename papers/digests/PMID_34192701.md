# Zhang 2021: remifentanil PCA vs epidural analgesia in labor, meta-analysis

## Study
- Meta-analysis (PRISMA) of RCTs, PubMed/EMBASE/ScienceDirect/Cochrane, searched to 1 April 2020; 507 records, 11 RCTs (2008-2019), 3,039 parturients (1,525 rPCA, 1,514 epidural); healthy term pregnancies (one trial gestation >32 wk).
- Random-effects pooling (Stata 15, RevMan 5.3); Cochrane risk-of-bias: half of trials high risk for performance and detection bias.
- rPCA regimens (Table 1): bolus 20-40 ug fixed in most (Douma 40 ug; Evron 20 ug; Freeman and Logtenberg 20-40 ug; Stocki 20-60 ug; Stourac 20-40 ug), weight-based in Ismail and Karadjova (0.1-0.9 and 0.1-1 ug/kg), Tveit 0.15-1.05 ug/kg, Volmanen 0.1-0.9 ug/kg; lockout 1-3 min; background infusion none in most (saline in some). Comparators: epidural ropivacaine/bupivacaine/levobupivacaine with sufentanil or fentanyl.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No MEAC, ke0, t½ke0, tPeak, EC50 or equianalgesic ratio is reported (the introduction only says remifentanil has rapid onset and short time to peak effect, citing others; no numbers).
Pooled results (rPCA vs epidural; Figs 3-4 and p. 235):
- Satisfaction with pain relief (7 trials, 1,921 women): SMD -0.19 (95% CI -0.57 to 0.18), I² 92%; re-expressed -0.33 (-0.96 to 0.30) using pooled SD 1.69.
- Average labor VAS (5 trials, 1,746; 0-10 cm): WMD +1.41 (0.32 to 2.50) higher with rPCA, I² 98%; MCID 0.72 (derived by authors).
- Spontaneous delivery (9 trials, 2,860): RR 1.02 (0.98-1.06). Oxygen desaturation <94% (5 trials, 1,120): RR 3.23 (1.98-5.30). Hyperthermia >=38.0 C (4 trials, 1,213): RR 0.49 (0.24-1.01). Apgar <7: RR 1.12 (0.86-1.47) at 1 min, 1.08 (0.57-2.06) at 5 min.

## Covariates
None analysed (no meta-regression); authors suggest bolus dose and lockout may explain heterogeneity (Ismail's 0.9 ug/kg bolus with 1 min lockout was the outlier, VAS WMD -0.13).

## Caveats
Few trials, very high heterogeneity for the primary outcomes with no source found, mixed satisfaction scales, no publication-bias test, no data on respiratory depression, apnea or hypertension.
