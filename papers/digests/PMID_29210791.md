# West 2018: closed-loop propofol and remifentanil with a safety system

## Study
- Two-phase, single-centre, non-randomised feasibility study (Royal Columbian Hospital, BC; NCT01771263), adults 19 y or older, ASA I-III, elective surgery under TIVA. Excluded: chronic opioid use, BMI <15 or >45, sedative premedication, neurological or EEG abnormality (p.884).
- 138 participants studied (Table 1: 58 phase I, 80 phase II); 127 analysed (52 phase I, 75 phase II) after excluding switches out of closed loop and technical failures. Overall median age 64 y, range 22-86 (abstract). Phase I: age 63 (51-69) y, BMI 28.5; phase II: age 64 (55-69) y, BMI 27.8 (Table 1, PDF p.6).
- Device: iControl-RP (NeuroSENSE WAVCNS EEG index, two Alaris TIVA pumps). Propofol 10 mg/mL and remifentanil 50 ug/mL syringes. Infusion rates updated every 5 s (p.885).
- Phase I: propofol by robust PID on WAVCNS (setpoint default 50); remifentanil by TCI (Minto model) with an anaesthesiologist-set target, default 2 ng/mL, adjusted in 0.5 ng/mL steps. Phase II: mid-ranging controller, propofol the slow actuator and remifentanil the fast one, with a baseline remifentanil infusion (default 0.1 ug/kg/min, adjustable in 0.02 steps, floor 50% of baseline) (p.885, p.887).
- Lidocaine 0.5 mg/kg before induction; induction complete when WAVCNS <60 for 30 s (p.887). Boluses available: propofol 0.25/0.5/1.0 mg/kg, remifentanil 0.25/0.5/1.0 ug/kg.

## Model and parameters
No PK model was fitted. Effect-site concentrations (Ce) were calculated from published models: Schnider (ref 21) for propofol, Minto (ref 22) for remifentanil (p.886; Table 2 footnote). Patient sex, age, weight and height were typed into the interface. The paper gives no covariate details, ke0 values or parameter table for these models. Phase I data were used to identify patient models of the propofol response for controller design (details in van Heusden, ref 30, not in this PDF).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: WAVCNS depth-of-hypnosis index (0 isoelectric, 90-95 awake, 40-60 clinical range; p.884) and burst-suppression ratio. Primary outcome was control performance (global score, % time within 10 units of setpoint).
- Phase II vs phase I (Table 2): global score 14.64 (11.63-20.74) vs 18.30 (14.26-27.23), P=.019; time within +/-10 of setpoint 88.2% vs 84.3%, P=.043; burst suppression (BSR >10% for 1 min or more) 73% vs 65%, P=.431; hypotension treated 60% vs 25%, P<.001.
- Propofol lower safety bound (1.5 ug/mL) reached in 81 of 127 cases (64%); propofol Ce below 1.6 ug/mL for 8.0% (0-29.5) of maintenance. Authors cite 1.6 ug/mL as a Ce associated with awakening after stopping propofol (ref 35). Upper bound (8 ug/mL) reached in 30 of 127 (24%) (p.888; Table 2: 16 and 14 cases).
- Opioid audit (remifentanil):
  - MEAC / minimum analgesic concentration: none reported. The 1-15 ng/mL remifentanil Ce limits are engineering safety bounds, not a derived analgesic concentration. The ~2 ng/mL Ce per ~10 WAVCNS unit figure is a controller design gain.
  - ke0, t1/2 ke0, tPeak, hysteresis: none reported or estimated; the Minto model supplies Ce but its ke0 is not stated.
  - Equianalgesic or potency ratio: none.
  - Remifentanil dose-sparing statement (p.892): even small doses (up to a Ce equivalent of about 2 ng/mL) significantly reduce propofol requirements, with less additional effect above that (ref 44, not data from this study).
- Median remifentanil Ce at maintenance (Table 2): 3.75 ng/mL (3.08-4.62) in phase I; 5.43 (4.43-6.22) in phase II. Text on p.888 says the phase II baseline Ce equivalent was 3.75 (3.19-4.17).

## Covariates
Age: burst suppression BSR >10% for 1 min or more in 94% (16 of 17) of those 70 y or older, 78% of 55-69 y, 41% of under 55 y (Table 3, P=.002 per text); hypotension treatment 82%, 66%, 24%. No PK covariate model.

## Caveats
- Not randomised; phase I served as a comparator for phase II; phases not matched for age, BMI or surgery. Learning curve for anaesthesiologists; 14 anaesthesiologists in phase II.
- The authors note that the propofol lower bound was reached often and suggest the Schnider model may underestimate concentrations in the elderly (ref 37), or that the bounds were too conservative.
- Ce values are model-calculated (Schnider, Minto), not measured, and depend on those models.
- Table 3 numbers are scrambled in the text layer and were not re-read on an image.
