# Overgaard 2016: liraglutide 3.0 mg population PK (SCALE trials)

## Study
Population PK of once-daily subcutaneous liraglutide in adults with overweight/obesity. Data: SCALE Obesity and Prediabetes (Trial 1, 3.0 mg, 2339 subjects in PK analysis) and SCALE Diabetes (Trial 2, 3.0 and 1.8 mg, 584 subjects); total N = 2923 (Table 1, printed p. 1417). Dose titration 0.6 mg/day with 0.6 mg weekly steps. One sample each at weeks 2, 12, 28; plus a week-16 substudy (N = 52) with samples at ~10, 11, 12, 14 h post dose. Records with incomplete dosing history over the preceding 3 days were excluded (1185 of 8859). A phase II dose-finding trial (Trial 3, N = 331 in the dose-proportionality analysis; 1.2, 1.8, 2.4, 3.0 mg) was used to check dose proportionality using the fixed full model (Fig. 1).
Population (Table 1): mean age 47.1 y (SD 12.3), 72% female, 85% White, 90% non-Hispanic; weight 106 kg (SD 21), range 65-234 (overall PK dataset text says 60-234 kg); BMI 38 (SD 7); 20% type 2 diabetes (all in Trial 2). Renal function by CKD-EPI: 51% normal, 44% mild, 5% moderate, 2 subjects severe.
Assay: total liraglutide in plasma by ELISA; LLOQ 30 pmol/L (Trials 1, 2) and 18 pmol/L (Trial 3). Software: NONMEM 7.1.2, S-PLUS 8.2.

## Model and parameters
One-compartment, first-order absorption and elimination; covariates on CL/F only (Sect. 2.2):
CL/F = TVCL x E_weight x E_dose x E_sex x E_age x E_ethnicity x E_disease x exp(eta). Proportional residual error. Reference subject: female, <70 y, 100 kg, White, non-Hispanic, no diabetes/prediabetes, 3.0 mg daily. ka fixed at 0.0806 (printed "L/h-1", evidently /h) from a separate clinical pharmacology study. The full parameter table is in the Online Resource (Table S1), not in this PDF. BSV of CL/F 34.8% CV, 24.7% after covariates (p. 1418).
Values stated in the PDF text:
- CL/F range 0.9-1.4 L/h: 0.9 (non-diabetic woman) and 1.0 (diabetic woman); 1.1-1.4 for men (Sect. 3.1.1, p. 1416), other covariates at reference.
- Geometric mean individual CL/F 0.94 / 1.22 / 0.93 L/h in Trials 1/2/3; covariate-adjusted 0.86 / 0.86 / 0.88 L/h (Sect. 3.3.5, p. 1419).
- Predicted AUC: high-exposure scenario 1631 nM*h, low-exposure scenario 297 nM*h (Sect. 3.3.7).

Fig. 2 ratios are steady-state AUC24/dose relative to the reference subject, geometric mean with 90% CI by likelihood profiling. Injection site (upper arm 1.04, thigh 0.97) and renal impairment (mild 1.03, moderate/severe 1.05) are post hoc and small.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No ke0, tPeak, MEAC, analgesic concentration or potency ratio (not an opioid; no effect-site model). Exposure-response for weight loss is in a separate paper (Wilding 2016, ref. 41); the text states weight loss rose with exposure and did not support differential dosing by sex or weight.

## Covariates
Body weight and sex are the only relevant ones (90% CI outside 0.80-1.25): 60 kg 1.41, 234 kg 0.56, men 0.76 (equivalently women 32% higher exposure at the same weight). Age >=70, race, ethnicity, glycaemic status, dose, injection site, renal function: within or marginal to the limits. Absolute body weight predicts exposure better than BMI (Sect. 3.3.3). Diabetes is confounded with trial (all T2DM from Trial 2); the Trial 2 geometric mean CL (1.22 L/h) is higher than Trials 1 and 3 before adjustment. Dose proportionality 1.2-3.0 mg (Fig. 1).

## Caveats
Sparse sampling; ka fixed from another study. Diabetes/trial confounding. Renal function by CKD-EPI eGFR (may overestimate GFR in obesity); only 2 subjects with severe impairment. Injection-site and renal analyses post hoc. Adults only; 60-234 kg. Industry authors.
