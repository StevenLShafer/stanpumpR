# Schwartz 2009: the CKiD ("bedside Schwartz") equations for GFR in children

## Study
Cross-sectional derivation in the Chronic Kidney Disease in Children (CKiD) cohort: 349 children aged 1 to 16 years
at enrolment (Table 1, PDF p2: median age 10.8 y [IQR 7.7-14.3], height 1.4 m, weight 35.0 kg, BSA 1.2 m2; 61% male;
69% white, 15% black; Tanner I 60%, II 11%, III 8%, IV 14%, V 7%; 20% glomerulonephritis). Eligibility was mild to
moderate CKD, original-Schwartz eGFR 30-90 mL/min/1.73 m2 (Concise Methods, PDF p7). Reference GFR: iohexol plasma
disappearance (5 mL iohexol IV; samples 10, 30, 120, 300 min; two-compartment), BSA-adjusted to 1.73 m2 (Haycock BSA).
Median iGFR 41.3 mL/min/1.73 m2 (IQR 32.0-51.7; 95% between 21.1 and 75.9). Serum creatinine **enzymatic**, central lab
(Siemens Advia 2400), shown elsewhere to agree closely with an HPLC method traceable to IDMS; median 1.3 mg/dL. Cystatin
C by Dako turbidimetric kit (median 1.8 mg/L); BUN median 27 mg/dL. Regression of log(iGFR) on logs of predictors
centred at their medians. Test set: 168 of the children with a second iGFR at the 1-year visit (Table 5).

## Model and parameters
Updated ("bedside") Schwartz: the exponent of height/Scr constrained to 1 (Results, PDF p3 / printed 631; Table 3):
- eGFR = 41.3 x height (m) / Scr (mg/dL), mL/min/1.73 m2; with height in cm, **eGFR = 0.413 x height / Scr**.
- The paper calls this a 25% reduction from the previous k 0.55 of Jaffe-based creatinine.
- Within 30% of iGFR: 79.4%; within 10%: 37.0%; RMSE 0.223, R2 59.6% (Table 3, PDF p5 / printed 633).
- The free exponent was 0.775 (Figure 1: iGFR = 41.2 [ht/Scr]^0.775, R2 65.0%), significantly below 1; the authors
  state the exponent of 1 is not correct, though the bedside form is offered as a good approximation (Abstract).

Full CKiD model III (Table 3; Abstract):
eGFR = 39.1 [ht(m)/Scr]^0.516 x [1.8/cystatin C]^0.294 x [30/BUN]^0.169 x 1.099^male x [ht(m)/1.4]^0.188;
R2 75.2%, RMSE 0.176, 87.7% within 30%, 45.6% within 10%.

Test set (Table 5, PDF p6): updated Schwartz ("CKiD" row of Counahan) bias -1.75 mL/min/1.73 m2, 95% LOA -19.7 to 16.2,
r 0.84, 73% within 30%, 26% within 10%; model III bias -2.23, LOA -17.1 to 12.7, r 0.88, 83% / 41%.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Not applicable: a renal-function estimating equation, no drug. No MEAC, ke0, tPeak or potency ratio.

## Covariates
Height, serum creatinine (enzymatic), cystatin C, BUN, sex (model III). After BSA adjustment, age, weight, Tanner stage,
race and BMI added nothing (Table 2; Results).

## Caveats
- Derived in children with **reduced** GFR (median ~41); the authors consider the formulas useful from 15 to
  75 mL/min/1.73 m2 and untested at higher GFR (Discussion, PDF p7). Others' k for enzymatic creatinine in populations
  with normal function were 0.430-0.500 (Discussion), higher than 0.413.
- Ages 1-16 at enrolment (Table 4 lists 1.2-17.1 y); no infants under 1 year, no adults. Growth-retarded cohort
  (median height percentile 22.8), only 21% Tanner IV-V.
- Valid only with an enzymatic / IDMS-traceable creatinine; Jaffe values would need the older k.
- The bedside form constrains an exponent the data put at 0.775.
