# Franzese 2022 supplement: ceftazidime and avibactam paediatric/adult PopPK

## Study
Supplement (43 PDF pages) to the population PK analysis of ceftazidime-avibactam (CAZ-AVI) in adults (phase III cUTI, cIAI, HAP/VAP) pooled with children >=3 months to <18 y (phase I single dose NCT01893346; phase II cIAI NCT02475733; phase II cUTI NCT02497781). Contents: NONMEM code of the final ceftazidime and avibactam models; derivation of the WT Emax covariate function; Table S1 (paediatric studies); Table S2 (ceftazidime estimates); Table S3 (avibactam estimates); Figures S1-S9 (observed data, GOF, VPCs, joint PTA by MIC); references.

Paediatric dosing (Table S1, CrCl >=50 mL/min): 2000-500 mg q8h (>=40 kg) or 50-12.5 mg/kg q8h (<40 kg); infants 3 to <6 months 40-10 mg/kg q8h; CrCl 30 to <50: half doses. Paediatric patients in PK analysis: 32 (phase I), 58 (cIAI), 63 (cUTI). Sampling 3-7 per subject (Table S1 footnotes). Doses simulated as 2-h infusions in the PTA figures.

## Model and parameters
Both: two-compartment, ADVAN3 TRANS4, NONMEM; concentrations in ng/mL, dose mg, time h. Ceftazidime estimation FOCEI (METHOD=1 INTER); avibactam SAEM then IMPMAP.

Ceftazidime (Table S2 and code): CL 7.75 L/h; Vc 11.2 L; Q 5.33 L/h; Vp 6.52 L (70 kg). V1 and V2 scale as (WT/70)^1 (fixed); Q as (WT/70)^0.67 (fixed). CL weight effect is a normalised Emax: WT50 = 53.5 kg (53.4 in the text of the derivation), gamma 1, normalised to 1 at 70 kg, so the maximum effect is 1 + WT50/70. Renal maturation for age <=2 y: PMA^3.4/(47.7^3.4+PMA^3.4) (fixed, Hill 3.4, TM50 47.7 weeks). NCrCL effect on CL (age >2): piecewise linear, slope 0.0103036 per mL/min below 100 and 0.00125182 above 100, NCrCL capped at 150. BSV (omega^2): CL 0.154, Vc 0.108, Q 0.203, Vp 0.0236. Residual: proportional 0.172 and additive 125 ng/mL (phase I); proportional 0.374 and additive 2560 ng/mL (phase II/III).

Avibactam (Table S3): CL 10.7 L/h; Vc 11.5 L; Vp 7.56 L; Q 6.94 L/h; weight exponent 0.75 on CL and Q (code comment; the code line reads THETA(30) with value 0.67 FIX, an internal inconsistency), exponent 1 on volumes; CL ESRD factor; dialysis CL 0.0674 L/h. Text-layer table is scrambled (see open questions); code shows renal terms (NCrCL/80)^1.05 below 80 and 1 + 0.00279*(NCrCL-80) above 80 as initial values.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
PK/PD target: 50% fT>8 mg/L (ceftazidime) with 50% fT>1.0 mg/L (avibactam) simultaneously; PTA by MIC in Figures S8-S9 (primary target at MIC 8 mg/L). No ke0 or MEAC; not an opioid.

## Covariates
Ceftazidime CL: weight (Emax), renal maturation by PMA (<=2 y), NCrCL, population (cIAI x1.33, HAP/VAP x1.10), Asian non-Chinese/non-Japanese -0.136 and Chinese -0.0844 (fractional). Vc: cUTI x1.49, cIAI/HAP-VAP x1.83, ventilator +0.202, Asian race -0.135. Avibactam: weight, NCrCL, ESRD, dialysis, APACHE II on CL (+0.214), ventilator on Vc, indication/phase on Vc.

## Caveats
High shrinkage for some random effects (Vp 83%, Q 80% for ceftazidime). Residual theta values are standard deviations in code. Several structural covariate values are fixed from earlier model iterations (slopes from MS-06).
