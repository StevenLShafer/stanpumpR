# Absalom 2007: Domino vs Hijazi vs Clements ketamine models in low-dose TCI

## Study
Prospective use of the Domino ketamine model to drive a Graseby 3500 TCI pump, with retrospective prediction by three other models. 58 paid healthy volunteers (ASA I-II, right-handed, non-smokers) in four cognitive/fMRI studies; 53 supplied samples (30 M, 23 F; mean age 32 y, range 18-64; mean weight 72 kg, 55-102; mean height 173 cm, 145-190) (PDF p4, printed p618). Racemic ketamine 1 mg/mL, targets 50, 100 or 200 ng/mL, venous blood, 2-3 samples per target plus one after the infusion stopped (Table 2, PDF p3). Study 1: n=14, 120 min, targets 50 and 100, samples 15/120/210 min. Study 2: n=12, 70 min, samples 15/70/130. Study 3: n=15, 140 min at 100, samples 30/140. Study 4: n=17, 100 ng/mL for 105 min, 5 min at zero, then 200 ng/mL for 100 min (210 min total), 6 samples. Plasma ketamine by GC-MS, LOD 1 ng/mL, linear 10-400 ng/mL, CV <15%. 254 samples obtained, 252 analysed (2 from an extravascular cannula); 14 more (6 with no recorded time, 8 anomalous) dropped for the cross-model comparison, leaving 238. Predicted concentrations for the three comparator models were computed retrospectively from infusion histories regenerated in Stanpump (mean dose error 0.5%, study 4). Performance by Varvel MDPE, MDAPE, wobble, divergence, plus slope of PE over time.

Table 3 (PDF p5, printed p619), Domino alone, all 252 samples, studies 1-4 then overall: MDPE 5.0, 1.2, -16.5, -8.3, -4.6 %; MDAPE 37.3, 43.2, 29.0, 30.8, 34.7 %; wobble 33.7, 35.8, 14.6, 21.3, 23.5 %; divergence 0.0920, 1.4791, -0.0790, 0.0647, 0.3855 % per h; slope of PE 0.5031, 2.1068, 0.1883, 0.4837, 0.8316 % per h. Measured concentrations (Fig 1) rose steadily over time at a constant target, reaching 150-370 ng/mL by 200 min in study 4 at the 200 ng/mL target. The Clements 250 model was the only one whose predictions all lay within 2 SD of the measured means; ANOVA P <= 0.0001 for every statistic, Dunn post hoc P < 0.002 (MDAPE), < 0.0001 (wobble, slope) Clements 250 vs Domino (PDF p6).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. The paper reports no ke0, t½ke0, tPeak, EC50 or MEAC and no analgesic or cognitive concentration threshold; the cognitive and fMRI outcomes are published elsewhere (refs 11-17). Targets used (50, 100, 200 ng/mL) are experimental, not defined as effective concentrations. Not an opioid paper; no potency ratio.

## Covariates
No covariate model. Mean weight 72 kg; V1 of each comparator is expressed per kg (Table 1) and Stanpump scaled by weight for each volunteer (derived: dose scaling, not stated per subject). Sex, age not modelled.

## Caveats
Domino was the controlling model, comparators retrospective, so Clements's superiority is not a prospective test (authors say so). Sampling times limited by fMRI schedule; few samples per subject (2 for study 3's slope); six study-3 samples lacked a time; eight anomalous samples dropped. Clements models came from 5 volunteers after boluses only. Measured plasma, venous. The Domino set tested is the mean rate-constant set of Table 1 as programmed in the pump.

## Verification against stanpumpR
No repo citation, so nothing to check for transcription. Independent comparison with `R/drugs_ketamine.R:38-43` (not a discrepancy, a context note):
- The paper's Domino constants (V1 0.063 L/kg; k10 0.4381, k12 0.5921, k13 0.5900, k21 0.2470, k31 0.0146 per min) are exactly the "mean rate constants" the file header (lines 15-17) says the library used until 2026-10-14. The library has since moved to a refit (V1 0.0583 L/kg x 70; k10 0.226, k12 0.657, k13 0.206, k21 0.0676, k31 0.00489 per min). The paper's finding (early over-prediction, late under-prediction, positive slope of PE 0.61 % per h) therefore describes the superseded set, and says nothing direct about the refit.
- Direction is consistent with the file header: the old set's clearance was 0.4381 x 0.063 x 60 = 1.66 L/kg/h (derived), about twice the 0.79 L/kg/h of the refit and of Domino's 0.848; the paper observed measured concentrations rising above predictions at late times, i.e. clearance too high in the pump model.
- Clements 250 clearance (derived: 1.7522 L/kg x 0.0109 per min x 60) is 1.15 L/kg/h and V1 1.75 L/kg; the library's V1 is 0.058 L/kg. The two-compartment Clements V1 is not a like-for-like quantity with a 3-compartment V1; compare Vss or concentration curves instead.
- No MEAC/tPeak in the paper, so the library's tPeak 3 min and band 0.1-0.16 mcg/mL (lines 45-52) cannot be checked against it. The study's 50-200 ng/mL range brackets that band's lower end.
