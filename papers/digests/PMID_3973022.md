# Jaski 1985: milrinone dose-response in severe heart failure, against nitroprusside

## Study
- 11 patients with chronic congestive heart failure despite digitalis, diuretics and vasodilators (Table I, PDF p. 2): 10 M / 1 F, age 24-74 y (individual ages printed; mean not given), CAD 8 / idiopathic cardiomyopathy 3; LVEF < 0.30 in all (0.18 +/- 0.08, given as mean +/- SEM). The text calls them NYHA class III or IV, but Table I lists patient 11 as class II (6 III, 4 IV, 1 II).
- Brigham and Women's catheterisation laboratory; vasodilators withheld 48 h. Haemodynamics, Fick cardiac output, micromanometer LV dP/dt.
- Nitroprusside titrated first (10-300 ug/min, 10-min steps) to a 15-20 mmHg fall in MAP, washed out, then milrinone IV boluses over 15-60 s of 12.5, 25, 50 and 75 ug/kg at 10-min intervals (the protocol line on PDF p. 2 prints the doses "ug/kg per min", but they are boluses, given as ug/kg everywhere else). 7 patients received all four doses; 4 stopped after the third because MAP had fallen 15-20 mmHg.
- Serum milrinone (HPLC, Edelson) at the recontrol point and 7-10 min after each bolus, with the haemodynamic measurements. Statistics mean +/- SEM.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- At the maximum dose (all 11): HR 92 +/- 4 to 99 +/- 4 bpm (P < 0.01); MAP 82 +/- 3 to 71 +/- 3 mmHg; RAP 15 to 7; LVEDP 26 to 18 mmHg; cardiac index 1.7 to 3.0 L/min/m2; SVI 20 to 30 mL/m2; SWI 14 to 21 g.m/m2; peak +dP/dt 858 +/- 54 to 1,130 +/- 108 mmHg/s (+32%) (Results p. 645).
- Seven patients with all four doses: dP/dt rose with concentration, significant at 12.5 ug/kg and +35% at the top dose (Fig. 4).
- First three doses, all 11 (Fig. 5): at 12.5 ug/kg (63 +/- 4 ng/mL) dP/dt and SVI rose while HR and SVR were unchanged; SVR fell significantly only at the third dose; HR rose only at the two highest doses.
- Against nitroprusside at a matched MAP (n = 10; one patient's MAP did not fall with milrinone): equal MAP (70 mmHg), milrinone gave higher HR, lower LVEDP, higher CI (3.0 vs 2.5 L/min/m2, P < 0.02), higher SWI and a 32% rise in dP/dt that nitroprusside did not; at matched CI, milrinone's MAP was 42% less reduced.
- No threshold, EC50, Emax, ke0 or time to peak effect is estimated; responses were read 7-10 min after each bolus. Adverse events after the protocol: VT 17 min after 50 ug/kg (patient 8), atrial fibrillation after 75 ug/kg (patient 3).

## Covariates
None.

## Caveats
- Incremental cumulative dosing with 10-min spacing: each sample reflects all earlier boluses, and response and concentration were not separated in time.
- Small n; afterload not measured directly (MAP used); dP/dt is load- and rate-sensitive (discussed by the authors).
- The two lowest-dose concentrations (63 +/- 4 and 66 +/- 3 ng/mL) are both printed for the 12.5 ug/kg step; the paper does not say which patients each covers (unresolved).

- **Discrepancy:** drugs_milrinone.R:47 and vasoactiveRegistry.csv:46 call 427 ng/mL the concentration "after the fourth" dose.
