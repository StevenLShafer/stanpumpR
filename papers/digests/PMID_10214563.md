# Park 1998: PK/PD model to design an optimal dose input profile

PDF page = printed page minus 470 (PDF p. 1 = printed 471).

## Study
- Double-blind, placebo-controlled crossover in 32 chronic-pain subjects (PDF p. 2). "Drug X" is deliberately disguised, and the paper says it is not necessarily an analgesic (footnote, PDF p. 2). Not an opioid that the library can identify.
- Treatments, oral, 8 mg at t=0 on all but TID:
  - ASC: 8 mg at 0 h, then a small dose every 0.5 h to 8 h, 36.3 mg total.
  - TID: 10 mg at 0, 4 and 8 h.
  - AM: 10, 6.4 and 13.6 mg at 0, 2 and 8 h.
  - PM: 10, 11.8 and 6.2 mg at 0, 6 and 8 h.
  - Placebo matched to ASC (lactose q30 min). Everyone had ASC, TID and placebo, plus either AM or PM.
- Pain scores (higher is worse) at 0.5, 1.5, ..., 9.5 h. No drug concentrations were measured; the plotted concentrations are simulated.
- Fitted with NONMEM V, full population model, FOCE-type; nonparametric (NONPAR) distribution of random effects for the optimisation.

## Model and parameters
- Total effect = placebo effect (natural cubic longitudinal spline, 3 breakpoints at 0.5, 5.0, 9.5 h chosen by AIC, with per-subject distortion splines) + drug effect (Eq. 2, text equation not legible in the text layer) + residual error. Drug effect has an effect-compartment concentration ce, a tolerance-compartment concentration ctol (Porchet nicotine model), and a time-drug term.
- PK for simulation is fixed, not estimated (PDF p. 3): one-compartment, first-order absorption, [ka, ke, V/F] = [1.37 1/h, 0.37 1/h, 1370 L]. The PK is assumed, not fitted from concentrations. 1370 L is a V/F in litres for an unnamed drug.
- Parameterisation: ktol = theta7, keo = ktol + theta8 (both >0, constraint keo > ktol).
- Estimates (SE) theta1..theta8 (printed p. 480, PDF p. 10, confirmed on image): 0.95 (0.15), 1.59 (0.20), 1.58 (0.20), 0.34 (0.07), 0.18 (0.10), 0.02 (0.03), 0.49 (0.15), 0.53 (0.20). The paper says these are hard to interpret. Units are not stated for theta7 and theta8; since ke is per hour they are presumably 1/h (derived: not stated).
- Table I (PDF p. 10, confirmed on image): -2lnL full 158.0; no tolerance 183.6 (delta 25.6, critical 5.99, significant); no time effect 159.3 (delta 1.3, critical 3.84, not significant); both deleted 188.8 (delta 30.8, critical 7.81, significant). Steady-state drug effect theta4*css: 1.61 full, 1.04 no tolerance, 1.66 no time effect, 0.85 both deleted. Tolerance factor 1/(1+theta5*css) = 0.54 (full), 0.58 (no time effect). Time factor 1+theta6*(5 h) = 1.10 full, 0.86 no tolerance. css = 4.73 ng/mL (mean of min and max steady-state concentration, a crude figure).
- Optimal regimens, bolus plus ramped infusion (Table II, PDF p. 11, confirmed on image), constraints total dose <= 39 mg, bolus <= 15 mg, lag 0.5 to 2 h, slope >= 0:
  - IND target: b* 8.1 mg, lag 0.50 h, r0 2.69 mg/h, s 0.00 mg/h^2, total 39.0 mg.
  - TYP target: b* 8.2 mg, lag 0.75 h, r0 2.73 mg/h, s 0.00, total 39.0 mg.
- Unconstrained optima (Table III) and the faster-kinetics cases (Table IV) are in the paper but not read from an image; not recorded.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: pain score (higher is worse), subtracting placebo. Not CSF, pupil, EEG or ventilation.
- Effect-compartment rate keo = ktol + theta8, so with the estimates above keo is about 0.49 + 0.53 = 1.02 and ktol about 0.49, in whatever time unit theta uses (derived; units and whether theta7 and theta8 are rates in 1/h are not stated). The paper does not state ke0, t1/2 ke0 or tPeak for drug X directly.
- No MEAC, no analgesic concentration, no EC50 or Emax (a saturable effect was tested and judged not to explain the time pattern, PDF p. 5), no potency or equianalgesic ratio. Concentrations are simulated, not assayed (no total/free or blood/plasma issue arises).
- Tolerance is significant: it roughly halves effect at steady state (1/(1+theta5*css) = 0.54). Time-of-day effect not significant (Table I).

## Covariates
None in the model. Random effects eta1..eta4 (unstructured covariance) cover placebo-curve distortion and drug-effect variability; the nonparametric support points were the 32 subjects' empirical Bayes estimates.

## Caveats
- Drug identity disguised; concentrations simulated from assumed PK, not measured; chronic-pain patients with a large placebo effect.
- Optimal profile conditional on point estimates (parameter uncertainty ignored, as the authors note).
- Placebo time-trend is matched to ASC, so it may not be an adequate control for the other regimens.
- Optimal total dose blew up in the 50% more tolerance case (Table III footnote text, PDF p. 17).
