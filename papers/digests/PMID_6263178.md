# Corrado 1980: inoculum effect on MIC and MBC against Pseudomonas aeruginosa

## Study
In-vitro study. 40 clinical isolates of *P. aeruginosa* (blood or urine, Kings County and Brooklyn Jewish hospitals),
tested against gentamicin, N-formimidoyl thienamycin (imipenem, MK0787), moxalactam, cefotaxime, cefoperazone and
piperacillin. Microtiter broth dilution in Mueller-Hinton broth (cation-supplemented, 5 mg Ca2+ and 2.5 mg Mg2+ per dL,
for gentamicin), final inocula 5 x 10^3, 5 x 10^5 and 5 x 10^7 CFU/mL; macrotiter tests confirmed results within
twofold, and only microtiter results are reported (p. 894). MIC: no visible growth overnight at 37 C. MBC definition
depends on inoculum (p. 894): 99.9% reduction at 5 x 10^5; no growth on 1.5-uL subculture at 5 x 10^7; no more than one
colony on subculture of the whole well at 5 x 10^3 (99.9% not measurable there).

Other drugs, MBC90 at 5 x 10^5 (Table 2): imipenem 8, moxalactam 32, cefotaxime 128, cefoperazone 64, piperacillin 32;
all >128 at 5 x 10^7 except gentamicin and imipenem (8).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
In-vitro MIC (visible growth) and MBC (definitions above). Gentamicin MIC90 virtually unchanged from 5 x 10^3 to
5 x 10^5 (p. 894). MIC100 of every drug except imipenem, gentamicin included, was 64 ug/mL or more even at
5 x 10^5 (p. 895). Not an opioid paper: no MEAC, ke0 or potency ratio.

## Covariates
None.

## Caveats
- Gentamicin values exclude 2 of 40 isolates (resistant); the denominator is 38.
- MBC criterion differs by inoculum, so MBC90 at 5 x 10^3 is not comparable with those at higher inocula.
- 1980 isolates from two Brooklyn hospitals; total drug in protein-free broth (equivalent to free drug).
