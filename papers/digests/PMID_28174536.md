# Smith 2017: remifentanil PK in mid-trimester fetal surgery

## Study
- **Patients:** 10 women having laser photocoagulation for twin-twin transfusion syndrome (UTHealth Houston) were
  enrolled. Only 8 had complete sampling, and these 8 are the study population.
- **Demographics** (Table 1, p4): gestation 22.2 (± 2.7) wk (18.9-26), age 27.8 (± 5.1) y, BMI 29.6 (± 6.3). Five
  were Hispanic and three white; TTTS stage I-IV. **Weight is not reported.**
- **Infusion:**
  - Methods say remifentanil started at 0.05-0.08 mcg/kg/min "with a higher dose" first, then titrated to maternal
    and fetal sedation.
  - Table 1's starting infusions are 0.05-0.5 "mcg·kg⁻¹·h⁻¹", with starting doses of 241-2280 mcg/h.
  - Durations were 62-85 min. Mean total dose was 975.3 µg.
- **Sampling:** plasma at baseline; at 5, 15, 30, 45 and 60 min after the start; and at 15, 30 and 60 min after the
  end.
  - Samples were put on ice and processed within 15 min. No esterase inhibitor is mentioned.
- **Assay:** HPLC with UV (210 nm) after solid-phase extraction, with fentanyl as internal standard.
  - Linear over 2-100 ng/mL (r = 0.997); CV 0.16-14.9%.
  - AAG measured by ELISA.
- **Analysis:** ADAPT II, two-compartment model chosen by AIC/SC and the fits. AUC = total dose / CL.

- **Comparisons made by the authors** (Discussion p4), all against Westmoreland 1993 non-pregnant values:
  - V is about 3.3-fold the 25-40 L.
  - Clearance is lower than the 250-300 L/h in non-pregnant women, a "1.8 fold decrease". Derived: 275/170.7 = 1.6
    and 300/170.7 = 1.76.
  - t½ 0.6 h against 0.2-0.4 h, about 1.5-fold.
  - The later Discussion calls it "a two-fold decrease".
- **AAG:** nearly twice Chu 1981's 62.6 mg/dL. The authors link the lower clearance and the slower fetal
  immobilisation to high AAG from surgical stress, and the larger V to blood-volume expansion and placental transfer.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- **Opioid audit:** no MEAC or analgesic concentration, no ke0, t½ke0, tPeak or hysteresis, and no potency ratio.
- **Fetal immobilisation:** inadequate immobilisation at non-pregnant doses is described only as the surgeons'
  impression; it was not measured.
- **Placental transfer quoted from others:** UV/MA 0.88 ± 0.78 (Kan 1998), 0.73 ± 0.17 (Ngan Kee 2006), and 0.64
  ± 0.52 from a concurrent study by the same group.

## Covariates
None modelled. AAG was measured but not related to PK statistically.

## Caveats
**Internal inconsistencies:**
- **Infusion units:** Methods (p2) say starting rates of 0.05-0.08 mcg/kg/min. Table 1 lists 0.05-0.5 labelled
  mcg/kg/h. The paired mcg/h column fits per-minute rates at about 76-83 kg (derived: 1480/(0.3 × 60) = 82 kg;
  2280/(0.5 × 60) = 76 kg) and does not fit per-hour rates. So the Table 1 unit label looks wrong, and the range
  exceeds the Methods' 0.08.
- **Mean concentration:** the Results text gives 12.6 ± 7.8 ng/mL "(Table 2)", but Table 2 gives 4.8 ± 2.7. This
  is unresolved.
- **Fold change:** the clearance is called both a 1.8-fold and a two-fold decrease.

**Design limits:**
- n = 8, no control group, and comparison only with published non-pregnant values.
- Weight is not reported, so per-kg clearance is unavailable.
- The HPLC-UV lower limit (2 ng/mL) equals the mean Cmin, so the washout samples are near or below quantification.
- No esterase inhibition is described for an ester drug.
- CL and V are very variable (CV 108% and 137%). The lowest CL, 17.7 L/h, is not physiological for remifentanil.
- All women were mid-trimester and had surgery under maternal stress, with high AAG.

## Verification against stanpumpR
- **Values in the finding:** n = 8, 22 weeks, 170.7 L/h, 250-300 L/h, 1.8-fold, V about 3.3×, AAG about doubled,
  and the AAG explanation. All as stated in the paper. The "1.8-fold" is the authors' own figure; derived, it is
  1.6-1.76. **matches**
- **`design` field "NCA mid-trimester (Smith 2017)":** Smith fitted a two-compartment model in ADAPT II and derived
  AUC from dose/CL. That is not non-compartmental analysis. **discrepancy (minor, design label)**
- **The decision to keep × 2 (Kan) and not use Smith:** a reasonable reading. Smith is uncontrolled, mid-trimester,
  in a surgical-stress population, and has the assay and unit problems above. Not a transcription matter.
