# De Smet 2008: Bayesian closed-loop propofol control with BIS

## Study
- Randomised (permuted blocks of 4, 20 per group) comparison, 40 ASA I-II women aged 18-45 y, ovum retrieval, spontaneous ventilation by facemask. Weight 70-130% of ideal body weight. (PDF p3, printed 1202)
- All patients: midazolam 1 mg IV 7 min and alfentanil 0.5 mg IV 2 min before propofol; paracetamol 1 g at end. No other drugs.
- Closed-loop (CL) group: RUGLOOP II software, BIS target 50 for the whole case, induction included. Manual group: anaesthesiologist titrated the effect-site-controlled TCI to BIS near 50, starting at Ce 5.0 ug/mL (equal to the controller's initial EC50).
- Actuator: Schnider three-compartment propofol model with effect compartment; time to peak effect fixed at 1.6 min (the Schnider 1999 / Minto 2003 convention). The paper cites Schnider 1998 and 1999 and does not tabulate the PK parameters.
- BIS from A-2000 monitor, BIS-XP v4, sampled every 5 s; BP every 1 min.
- Outcomes: induction (time to LOC, BIS at LOC, TBIS TARGET, BISpeak, TPEAK,BIS, TEQ), maintenance (% time BIS 40-60 and 45-55, Varvel PE / MDPE / MDAPE / divergence / wobble), recovery times, haemodynamics, movement, gynaecologist's quality score.

## Model and parameters
Controller PD model (Eq 1, PDF p2, printed 1201): sigmoid Emax, BIS = E0 - (E0-Emax)*Ce^gamma/(Ce^gamma+EC50^gamma). Four parameters named (E0, Emax, EC50, gamma). The only population value printed is the initial EC50 = 5.0 ug/mL (stated indirectly: the manual start target "exactly similar to the initial EC50 in the initial population model", PDF p4). E0, Emax and gamma are not given here (they are in the companion simulation paper, De Smet 2007, and in PMID_31107260: E0 100, Emax 0, gamma 2.62, EC50 4.98 ug/mL).
Bayesian objective function (Eq 2): weighted squared BIS error with a time-forgetting factor, plus squared penalties for EC50, gamma and system delay D relative to the population values, each divided by its own variance. Gamma was held constant in practice (Fig 4 text: gamma, E0, Emax not estimated); EC50 and delay were estimated.
The "moving curve controller" shifts the sigmoid along the Ce axis to find the Ce that gives target BIS. No PK parameters are estimated.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: BIS (frontal EEG). ke0 / t1/2ke0 are not reported; the effect compartment is fixed by tPeak 1.6 min. No hysteresis analysis.
- EC50 (initial prior) 5.0 ug/mL; delay (system delay) estimated per patient but typical value not printed. Figure 4 shows time courses only.
- Mean propofol Ce at stop: 3.1 +/- 0.8 (CL) vs 3.5 +/- 0.9 ug/mL (manual) (Table 2, PDF p5).
- Induction (Table 2): induction time 66 +/- 25 vs 49 +/- 9 s; propofol to LOC 91 +/- 22 vs 106 +/- 10 mg; BIS at LOC 73 +/- 11 vs 82 +/- 7.
- Table 3: TBIS TARGET 93 +/- 34 vs 89 +/- 64 s; BISpeak 40 +/- 7 vs 33 +/- 10; TPEAK,BIS 125 +/- 61 vs 148 +/- 112 s; TEQ 176 +/- 101 vs 204 +/- 124 s (only 16/20 manual patients returned to target); % time BIS 40-60: 75 +/- 13 vs 43 +/- 17; 45-55: 47 +/- 13 vs 20 +/- 16; BIS <40: 15 +/- 12 vs 48 +/- 17.
- Table 4 (start to stop of propofol): average PE -2.91 +/- 24.49 vs -14.17 +/- 28.88 %; MDPE -7.78 +/- 3.46 vs -19.96 +/- 8.38 %; MDAPE 11.51 +/- 4.0 vs 24.06 +/- 8.01 %; divergence -0.009 +/- 0.012 vs 0.004 +/- 0.025 %/min; wobble 8.44 +/- 2.84 vs 11.48 +/- 4.19 %.
- Recovery (Table 2): eyes open 215 +/- 133 vs 316 +/- 125 s; orientation 259 +/- 128 vs 343 +/- 112 s.
- Opioid audit: alfentanil 0.5 mg single bolus 2 min before induction is the only opioid. No alfentanil concentration, MEAC, ke0, tPeak or potency ratio reported. Audit: none.

## Covariates
None analysed (homogeneous population; weight 62.2 +/- 9.0 vs 65.4 +/- 8.0 kg, age 31.6 vs 32.5 y, height 165.0 vs 168.8 cm, Table 1).

## Caveats
- Small feasibility study, young healthy women, short cases (about 17 min), minimal opioid; the authors warn against extrapolating. Authors and patent holders include Shafer, who has an interest in the controller (PDF p1).
- Negative MDPE in CL (-7.8%) means BIS tended below target; authors attribute it to the controller acting only on input, not elimination.
- Table 4 footnote spells MDAPE as "median absolute performance" (sic).
- Manual comparator was a single anaesthesiologist who could see BIS.
