# Bailey 1994: milrinone pharmacokinetics after cardiopulmonary bypass

## Study
- Emory University; adults having elective cardiac surgery with CPB (hypothermia 23-28 C, cold hyperkalaemic cardioplegia). No patient had hepatic or renal disease (p. 617).
- Pharmacokinetic cohort, n = 25 (Table 1, mean +/- SD): age 61.3 +/- 10.3 y, weight 86.8 +/- 18.4 kg, height 175.4 +/- 9.3 cm, 6 F / 19 M; 19 CABG, 6 AVR.
- Five groups of 5: single doses of 25, 50 or 75 ug/kg into the venous reservoir after rewarming, before separation from CPB; 50 ug/kg plus 0.5 ug/kg/min continued for the rest of the study (dose 15 +/- 8 min before separation); 50 ug/kg immediately after separation (p. 617).
- Arterial plasma at 2, 5, 7, 10, 20, 30, 60, 90, 120, 180, 240, 600 and 960 min (3 min instead of 2 in the post-separation group). HPLC (Edelson), CV 6.8% over 5-500 ng/mL; data below 5 ng/mL omitted.
- Two- and three-compartment models fitted per patient by extended least squares (MK-MODEL), variance proportional to prediction, model choice by the Schwarz criterion; parameters did not differ by dose group (ANOVA, 5 per group), so the pooled data of all 25 were fitted to one three-compartment model ("naive pooled", not NONMEM; p. 617, 620).
- Pharmacodynamic cohort, a separate n = 11 with cardiac index < 2.5 L/min/m2 after separation (Table 1: 62.6 +/- 10.4 y, 79.9 +/- 16.9 kg, 2 F / 9 M; 9 of 11 on norepinephrine), given 50 ug/kg; cardiac index and plasma at 3, 5 and 10 min (p. 618).

Internal checks (derived): A + B + C = 489.7 ng/mL against dose/V1 = 50/0.102 = 490 ng/mL; the eigenvalues of the six-parameter model are 0.2472, 0.01497 and 0.000754 /min, and the 50 ug/kg coefficients 366.3, 110.5 and 13.4 ng/mL, so Table 2's two halves agree to their printed precision; the abstract's Vss (1,698 mL/kg) and clearance (1.88) match the table. Terminal half-life ln2/0.000754 = 919 min, 15.3 h (derived).

Other results: peak concentration > 150 ng/mL in every dose group and proportional to dose; without an infusion, concentration fell below 100 ng/mL within 30 min in 19 of 20 single-dose patients; with 50 ug/kg + 0.5 ug/kg/min the mean stayed above 100 ng/mL (Fig. 5, to 960 min). Three compartments fitted better than two in 13 of 25 individuals and in the pooled data. Simulated context-sensitive half-times at a 120 ng/mL target for infusions of 30, 45, 60, 120, 180, 300, 400 and 600 min: 36, 43, 45, 49, 61, 78, 120 and 138 min (amrinone, for comparison, 86 to 120 min) (p. 621).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: percentage increase in thermodilution cardiac index from the post-bypass baseline, at 3, 5 and 10 min after 50 ug/kg, in 11 patients with CI < 2.5 L/min/m2. Model: % increase = 100 x C^gamma / (C^gamma + C50^gamma) (Emax fixed at 100%); gamma 1.16, C50 167 ng/mL (Results p. 620, Fig. 6 rendered). A linear fit gave r = 0.47, y = 0.126x + 28.3 (p. 621).
- No controls, so a spontaneous rise in CI cannot be excluded (authors). Effect and concentration were sampled together; no ke0, no hysteresis analysis, no time to peak effect.
- The authors take C50's interval (100-235 ng/mL) to be close to the ~100 ng/mL threshold reported in chronic heart failure (Jaski 1985, ref. 25) and call the 50 ug/kg + 0.5 ug/kg/min regimen typically therapeutic.

## Covariates
None tested; parameters per kg of (total) body weight. Dose group and timing relative to separation from CPB had no detectable effect (5 per group).

## Caveats
- Naive pooled fit: no interindividual variability; individual model order differed (2 vs 3 compartments).
- Patients not selected for low output; all had normal renal and hepatic function. Concomitant catecholamines (2 epinephrine, 11 norepinephrine of 25).
- Pharmacodynamics from 11 patients over 10 min with an arbitrary CI threshold; authors call it approximate.
- delta printed as .0008 (one significant figure).
