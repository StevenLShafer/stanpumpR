# Ketharanathan 2023: pentobarbital one-compartment popPK in PICU children (status epilepticus, severe TBI)

## Study
- Retrospective, single centre (Erasmus MC-Sophia, PICU), January 2007-February 2023. 48 treated; model building
  n = 36 (SE 22, sTBI 14), 178 samples; external validation n = 9 (sTBI 6, SE 3), 60 samples.
- Model group: median age 1.3 y (SE 0.31 y, sTBI 12 y), median weight 10 kg (SE 6.3, sTBI 40 kg).
- Continuous IV pentobarbital with loading doses; SE median infusion 3 mg/kg/h (0.5-10), sTBI 2 mg/kg/h (0.05-5).
  Median measured concentration 27.5 mg/L (0.1-106). ECMO and haemodialysis samples excluded; no hypothermia.
- Assay: HPLC-DAD, 100 uL plasma, LLOQ 0.5 mg/L, ULOQ 90 mg/L.
- NONMEM 7.2, FOCE-I; bootstrap 1000, VPC, NPDE, external VPC. Backward elimination p < 0.001.

## Model and parameters
One compartment (two compartments fitted equally well; one chosen for simplicity). IIV on CL only; additive
residual error. Allometry fixed: 0.75 on CL, 1 on Vd (estimated exponent's CI included 0.75). Table 2, final model
(RSE%):

CL (L/h) = 3.59 x (Weight/70)^0.75 x (Creatinine/36)^-0.919 [x (CRP/70)^-0.883 if CRP > 70 mg/L]; creatinine in
umol/L. Half-life reported as 24.68 h (Discussion). Derived: ln2 x 142/3.59 = 27.4 h at 70 kg, creatinine 36.
Obese children (weight-for-age >= 2 SD) had a 71% higher Vd (Discussion; not in the model).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PK/PD; EEG and ICP effects not modelled. Simulation target 25 mg/L, "within the known safety margin"; dose
adjustments aimed to keep concentrations within a **safety margin of 20-40 mg/L** (section 3.5.1). Three sTBI
patients needed CVVH for levels > 40 mg/L with refractory shock. Doubling creatinine roughly halves CL; CRP-based
dose reductions in Table 4 (73% at 100, 40% at 200, 28% at 300, 21% at 400 mg/L). Steady state took >= 48 h without
loading. Not an opioid.

## Covariates
Weight (fixed allometry), serum creatinine, CRP > 70 mg/L on CL. Not retained: age, sex, diagnosis, urea, eGFR
(Schwartz, k 0.365; heights 67% missing), albumin, bilirubin, ASAT, ALAT, gamma-GT, ALP, KDIGO.

## Caveats
Retrospective, single centre; two diagnoses with different protocols pooled; sampling sparse at start and after
stopping (may hide a second compartment); creatinine may mark illness severity rather than renal function; heights
often estimated; no PD.
