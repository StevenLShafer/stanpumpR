# Müller 1987: temazepam after morning and evening oral doses

## Study
- Open randomised crossover; 12 healthy non-obese Caucasian men, mean age 22.8 y, mean weight 74.4 kg.
- 20 mg temazepam soft gelatin capsule with 50 mL water at 09:00 and at 22:00, 7 days apart; fasted >= 4 h; recumbent 10 h.
- Samples pre-dose, 10, 20, 30, 40, 60, 90, 120 min, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12, 24, 36 h. GC with electron-capture detection, plasma.
- Cmax and tmax observed; half-lives (absorption, distribution, elimination) by least-squares exponential fitting (method of
  residuals type); AUC trapezoidal; Steinijans-Diletti CIs.

No lag time reported. No volumes or clearance (no i.v. reference). Derived: ka (day) = ln2/0.38 = 1.82 /h.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None measured. No adverse reactions. Not an opioid.

## Covariates
Time of day only. Men only.

## Caveats
Small n; men only; half-lives from curve stripping of individual oral data (absorption and distribution phases overlap); Cmax/tmax
are means of individual observed values, not the peak of the mean curve; night-time dosing in recumbent subjects. Abstract says all
half-lives were longer at night, but the elimination difference was not significant.
