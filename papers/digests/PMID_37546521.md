# Wang 2023: teicoplanin in critically ill adults (not used)

## Study
- 108 ICU patients (70 men, 38 women), median 56.5 years (21-91), 61 kg (40-115), eGFR 29.2 mL/min/1.73 m2 (4.89-170), 43 on CRRT. 304 venous plasma samples; 1-h infusions. NONMEM 7.3, FOCE-I.

## Model (Table 2)
Two compartments: CL 0.838 L/h x (1 + (eGFR - 50) x 0.00823) (CKD-EPI), Vc 14.4 L, Q 3.08 L/h, Vp 51.6 L. The notes print "Vp = 51.6 x e^0.387", where 0.387 matches the variance of Vp's 62.2% CV and no fixed effect.

## Why stanpumpR does not use it
The assay measured the A2-2 component only ("We chose A2-2 to be detected in place of all components"; A2-2 is about 50% of the drug), and the paper does not say whether the results were scaled to total teicoplanin. Its clearance is about twice Kim 2024's. Rejected by Steven L. Shafer, 2026-11-27, in favour of Kim 2024 for every adult (R/drugs_teicoplaninKim.R).
