# Lemon 2016: ondansetron exposure across gestation in one woman

## Study
Single-patient case report (Obstetric-Fetal Pharmacology Research Unit Network). A 26-year-old Caucasian
pregnant woman with normal liver and kidney function, maintained on **oral ondansetron 8 mg three times daily**
throughout pregnancy. Weight not reported. Serial plasma samples over one 8-hour dosing interval (0, 0.5, 1, 1.5,
2, 3, 4, 6, 8 h) at **14, 24 and 35 weeks' gestation** (early, mid, late). Assay: LC-MS/MS (Waters 2695 /
Quattro micro, positive ESI), a minor modification of a published granisetron method (ref 5); total plasma
ondansetron. Non-compartmental values; the AUC method is not stated. No postpartum or non-pregnant
measurement was made (PDF p2, e140; p1 abstract).

## Model and parameters
No model. Table 1 (PDF p2, e140), steady-state observed values, single subject:

|---|---|---|---|
| Early (14 wk) | 109 | 634 | 12.6 |
| Mid (24 wk) | 100 | 553 | 14.5 |
| Late (35 wk) | 80 | 387 | 20.7 |

Late/early CL/F ratio 1.64 (derived: 20.7/12.6); late/mid 1.43 (derived). Bioavailability not reported, so
systemic clearance cannot be obtained from these values. Figure 1 (p2): trough (time 0) about 70, 66 and 35 ng/mL
at 14, 24 and 35 weeks (read from figure, approximate); tmax about 1-1.5 h.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No antiemetic end point, no effect-site or concentration-response data, no therapeutic concentration.
(Not an opioid; no MEAC, ke0 or potency ratio.)

## Covariates
Gestational age only, as three descriptive time points. The authors attribute the falling exposure to
increased CYP3A4 and CYP2D6 activity in pregnancy (CYP1A2 decreases); this is their inference, not measured.

## Caveats
- n = 1; no postpartum or non-pregnant baseline, so the "increase" is relative to her own 14-week value, not
  to non-pregnant women.
- Oral dosing: CL/F mixes bioavailability (first pass) and clearance; F not measured.
- Weight, adherence and timing of the last dose before sampling are not reported; AUC method not stated.
- Return to baseline postpartum is the authors' expectation, not observed.
