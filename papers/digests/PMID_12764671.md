# Ralph 2003: population PK of epirubicin and AST-based dosing

## Study
Pooled data from three trials at Guy's Hospital, London: 109 women with advanced or metastatic breast cancer,
single-agent epirubicin by rapid IV infusion (median 3 min, range 0.4-19). Trial 1: 50 women, normal or near-normal
liver tests, 12.5-120 mg/m2 every 3 weeks; trial 2: 23 with liver metastases, 25 mg/m2 weekly; trial 3: 36 with
liver metastases, 20-75 mg/m2 every 3 weeks by AST and performance status. First cycle only; mean 12 samples
(8-18), typically predose, 6, 10, 15, 30 min, 1, 2, 4, 6, 12, 24, 48 h. Plasma HPLC. Demographics (Table 1, PDF p3):
dose median 78 mg (20-228); age 57 (35-79) y; height 157 cm; BSA 1.65 (1.25-2.2) m2; weight 62.8 (37-89) kg;
bilirubin 10 (1-282) umol/L; AST 90 (7-815) IU/L; 72 with liver metastases. NONMEM V, FOCE-INTER; 105 patients
analysed (4 with missing covariates excluded).

## Model and parameters
Three-compartment, zero-order input, proportional residual error; IIV on CL, Q3 and V3 only. Final model
(Table 3, PDF p6, printed 39; confirmed on page image): TVCL = θ1·(1 − θ7·ln AST), AST in IU/L.

Derived: at the median AST 90 IU/L, CL = 72.9 × (1 − 0.135 × 4.50) = 28.6 L/h; CL falls to zero at AST ≈ 1640 IU/L
(e^(1/0.135)), beyond the data (max 815). Individual Bayesian CL 4-86 L/h. Full model (with dose, BSA, liver
metastases on CL; ln dose, metastases on Q3; bilirubin, height, albumin on V3) in Table 3 was not retained because
each extra covariate cut IIV by < 2%.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Not an opioid. Target AUC 4000 ng·h/mL, chosen from Jakobsen 1991 to give a nadir WBC of 1-2 × 10^9/L. Proposed fixed
doses (Table 4): AST < 150 IU/L 125 mg; 150-250 90 mg; 250-500 60 mg; > 500 30 mg. Precision of AUC vs target:
rmse 39% (new) vs 63% (UK label), 62% (US label), 59% (Dobbs equation); bias −0.1%, 0.9%, 4.9%, 18.9%.

## Covariates
ln AST on CL only (ΔOFV 56.4, Table 2). Bilirubin weaker (ΔOFV 12.1); creatinine clearance not significant; no
positive BSA effect (weak negative in full model).

## Caveats
Women with breast cancer only; first cycle only; evaluated on the same data used for development (authors say
external validation is needed); 2-compartment model failed under FOCE so structure chosen with FO; women with normal
liver tests were not scanned for metastases.
