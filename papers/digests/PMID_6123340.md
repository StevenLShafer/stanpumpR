# d'Hollander 1982: age and the steady-state dose of vecuronium (Org NC45)

## Study
- 24 adults for elective surgery, no hepatic or renal disease, in three age groups of 8: group I < 40 yr (mean 28 +/- 2 SEM, range 18-36), group II 40-60 yr (54 +/- 3, 40-60), group III > 60 yr (69 +/- 3, 63-85) (Table I). Weight, height and sex not reported.
- Anaesthesia: oral diazepam 10 mg premedication; methohexitone 1 mg/kg and fentanyl 5 ug/kg; 33% O2 in N2O with supplementary fentanyl. **No volatile agent.** Normocapnia, warming mattress (rectal 37 degC).
- Monitoring: adductor pollicis isometric force, ulnar nerve single twitch at 0.1 Hz.
- Dosing: bolus Org NC45 (vecuronium) 0.07 mg/kg, intubation at minimum twitch, then an on-demand infusion (160 ug/mL) adjusted by hand to hold twitch height (TH) at 10% of baseline (i.e. 90% block), for at least 90 min; the dose was measured every 10 min.
- **No plasma concentrations measured** (the authors say so, printed p.656).
- End points: steady-state dose requirement (SSDR, ug m^-2 h^-1, i.e. per body surface area); TH10-25 (end of infusion to 25% twitch); TH25-75 (recovery index).

## Model and parameters
No pharmacokinetic model. Results (Table I, PDF p.3, printed 655; mean +/- SEM, range):

| | Group I (<40) | Group II (40-60) | Group III (>60) |
|---|---|---|---|
| SSDR, ug m^-2 h^-1 | 2791 +/- 434 (1348-4469) | 2702 +/- 325 (1546-3973) | 1775 +/- 213 (889-2816) |
| TH10-25, min | 8.44 +/- 1.16 (4-14) | 9.18 +/- 1.40 (5-14) | 14.70 +/- 1.82 (7.8-17.8) |
| TH25-75, min | 16.24 +/- 4 (3-37.1) | 21.88 +/- 2.2 (16-33) | 44.94 +/- 6.91 (27.5-82) |

(The Results text on printed p.654 gives different group I and II SSDR: see open_questions.) Group III differed from I and II (SSDR P < 0.05; TH10-25 P < 0.05; TH25-75 P < 0.01); groups I and II did not differ. Steady state was reached about 30 min after the start; no time-related decline in requirement over the next 60 min (Fig. 2), i.e. no cumulation.

Pooled regressions (printed p.654, rendered page): SSDR (ug m^-2 h^-1) = -27.31 x age (yr) + 3803, r = -0.51, P = 0.01; TH25-75 (min) = 0.557 x age (yr) + 0.189, r = 0.58, P < 0.01.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: single-twitch height (adductor pollicis), held at 10% of control. No ke0, EC50 or gamma; no concentrations.
- Ratios (derived from Table I): SSDR group III / group I = 0.64; TH10-25 III / I = 1.74; TH25-75 III / I = 2.77.
- The authors attribute the age effect to reduced elimination rather than sensitivity, by analogy with pancuronium, but say they could not separate the two.
- Not an opioid paper: no MEAC, ke0 or potency ratio.

## Covariates
Age only (grouped and linear regression). Dose scaled by body surface area; how BSA was calculated is not stated.

## Caveats
- n = 8 per group; SEM, not SD; no concentrations, so PK and PD changes cannot be separated.
- Text and Table I disagree for groups I and II SSDR (see open_questions).
- Recovery was measured after a steady-state infusion of varying length (at least 90 min), not after a bolus.
- Fentanyl/N2O anaesthesia, no volatile potentiation.

## Verification against stanpumpR
- **Direction (R/drugs_vecuronium.R:75, vecuronium.md:9): matches.** The paper found slower recovery and a lower maintenance requirement over age 60.
- **"A larger difference still" (vecuronium.md:9): matches, with a caveat on comparability.** The model's age ratio for time to 25% after a bolus is 45/32 = 1.41 (derived); the paper's nearest quantity, TH10-25 after stopping an infusion, is 1.74 for group III against I, and TH25-75 is 2.77. These are different end points (recovery from a 90% steady-state block vs from a bolus), so the comparison is qualitative, as the repo presents it.
- **Age shape (not a repo claim, for the record):** the model interpolates linearly from 40 to 70 years (`R/drugs_vecuronium.R:107`); d'Hollander found no difference between < 40 and 40-60 and the change only above 60, though the pooled linear regression was significant.
- **Independent check of the library's clearance (derived, assumptions mine):** at steady state the infusion rate is CL x Css. With the library's CL 0.51 L/min and its 90% block concentration 134 ng/mL (`R/drugs_vecuronium.R:87`), a 70 kg, 170 cm adult (Du Bois BSA 1.81 m2) needs about 68 ug/min = 2270 ug m^-2 h^-1 at 40, and x 0.71 (Rupp's CL ratio, line 109) = about 1610 at 70. The paper's 2791 (Table I) or 3046 (text) for group I and 1775 for group III are in the same range and the age ratio (0.64) is close to the library's 0.71. This ignores the library's fat-free-mass scaling and assumes no potentiation difference; it is a plausibility check, not a test.
- The repo carries no year/journal for this citation (context notes); the PDF confirms the Br J Anaesth 1982;54:653-657 paper is the one meant (steady-state on-demand infusions, age groups).
