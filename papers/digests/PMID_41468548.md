# Xu 2026: Caspofungin population PK in critically ill Chinese children (PICU, with/without ECMO)

## Study
- Prospective single-centre observational study, Children's Hospital of Fudan University PICU, 1 Nov 2022 - 30 Dec 2024 (NCT04961593). Eligible: age 3 months-18 y on caspofungin.
- n = 29 patients, 138 plasma samples (median 7.475 mg/L, range 0.155-58.300; all above the LLOQ). Median age 5.33 y [0.33, 16.0], weight 16.0 kg [4.90, 74.0], BSA 0.660 m2 [0.286, 1.89] (Table 1; see open questions on the text/table mismatch). 12 M / 17 F. Four patients on ECMO, one on CRRT, 2 hypoalbuminaemia, 2 severe malnutrition. About 5 had abnormal liver tests and 5 moderate renal impairment.
- Dosing: 1-h IV infusion once daily. Loading dose 70 mg/m2 (max 70 mg) on day 1, then 50 mg/m2 (max 70 mg). BSA by Mosteller.
- Sampling: intensive stage (pre-dose, 1, 2, 4, 8 h, 16 h if feasible after the 6th dose) then an optimal-design sparse stage (pre-dose 0-1 h, 0.5-1.5, 6-7, 23-24 h). No samples after the first dose (so no loading-dose PK).
- Assay: LC-MS/MS, total (not free) plasma caspofungin, EDTA-K2 plasma, range 0.05-50 ug/mL, LLOQ 0.05 ug/mL.
- Software: NONMEM 7.5 FOCE-I; PopED 0.6.0 for design; stepwise covariate modelling (forward P < 0.05, backward P < 0.01); bootstrap n = 1000; Monte Carlo n = 1000 for PTA.

## Model and parameters
Two-compartment model, allometric scaling on BSA (reference 0.79 m2), exponent FIXED at 1 for volumes and 0.66 for clearance. BSA gave lower OFV/AIC than weight, lean body weight or fat-free mass. IIV exponential; CL-V1 IIV correlation 0.802 (text, PDF p4) and 72% in Table 2. Combined residual error. ECMO a covariate on V1 (dOFV -13.262).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD study. PK/PD targets used for simulation: total AUCss,24h/MIC > 450 (C. glabrata), 865 (C. albicans), 1185 (C. parapsilosis), from a mouse model (ref 21, Andes 2010; 1-log10 kill); and Cmin,ss above MIC90 (0.06 mg/L C. albicans and C. glabrata, 1.0 mg/L C. parapsilosis). PTA >= 90% was taken as success. No MEAC, ke0, tPeak or hysteresis (not an opioid).

Simulation results (PDF p5-6): by AUC/MIC, BSA <= 1.4 m2 needed >= 30 mg/m2/day for C. albicans and C. glabrata, and no regimen reached the target for C. parapsilosis at MIC90 1 mg/L; BSA > 1.4 m2 needed a flat >= 50 mg/day (C. glabrata and C. parapsilosis, see open questions on MIC) and more for C. albicans. By Cmin,ss, non-ECMO needed 70 mg/m2 (capped at 70 mg) for BSA <= 1.4 m2 and 60 mg/day for BSA > 1.4 m2; ECMO patients reached no PTA > 90% on any regimen.

## Covariates
BSA (allometric, a priori; V exponent 1, CL exponent 0.66, reference 0.79 m2); ECMO on V1. Age, sex, WBC, RBC, Hb, AST, ALT, bilirubin, creatinine, uric acid and CRRT were screened and not retained.

## Caveats
- n = 29, only 4 on ECMO; ECMO V1 effect has RSE 62% and a bootstrap CI of 4-298; authors call ECMO findings preliminary.
- No post-first-dose sampling, so no loading-dose evidence. Single-day steady-state sampling, no inter-occasion variability. Total drug only, no unbound concentrations (caspofungin is highly protein bound).
- PK/PD targets come from animal/in vitro data. BSA exponent of 0.66 for CL is fixed, not estimated.
- Comparator for 'higher': the authors say BSA-normalised CL (0.248 L/h/m2) and V1 (2.81 L/m2) were higher than other published paediatric studies (median CL 0.208, V1 2.19), i.e. relative to those studies, not to any stanpumpR model.
