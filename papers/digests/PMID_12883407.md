# Mertens 2003: propofol-remifentanil response surfaces, perioperative

## Study
- 30 unpremedicated women, ASA I-II, lower abdominal surgery (abstract says 18-65 yr, Methods say 20-65 yr, PDF p1-2); 29 evaluable (one group C sample mishandled). Table 1 (PDF p4): n 10/10/9, age 35/39/39 yr, weight 68/65/67 kg, height 165-168 cm.
- Randomised, double-blind to constant target propofol 2, 4 or 6 ug/mL (groups A, B, C) by TCI (Gepts three-compartment set), plus remifentanil TCI (Minto 1997 three-compartment set) titrated up 1-10 ng/mL on signs of inadequate anaesthesia, down 1-10 ng/mL when none. Atracurium kept at train-of-four 1-3. Morphine 0.2 mg/kg given 30 min before skin closure.
- Stimuli: laryngoscopy (repeated 2-3 times at different remifentanil targets, 5 min after each new target), intubation, skin incision, peritoneal opening, intra-abdominal surgery, and return of consciousness (verbal command) after stopping both infusions.
- Samples (arterial) for measured **whole-blood** concentrations: propofol HPLC (LOQ 11.7 ng/mL, CV <=3%); remifentanil tandem MS after citric-acid esterase inactivation (LOQ 0.1 ng/mL, CV <10%).
- Analysis: NONMEM V, Bol et al. response surface (Berenbaum-style epsilon) for laryngoscopy and intubation; Minto-type surface for return of consciousness; per-patient logistic regression then a mechanistic isobole for intra-abdominal surgery. AIC chose between additive and nonadditive.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Opioid audit (remifentanil):
- **MEAC / analgesic concentration:** no MEAC is defined. The nearest quantities are the remifentanil C50 for no response to surgical stimuli (a 50% probability of no response, logistic/response-surface, whole blood, women ASA I-II; see above and Table 3/Fig 7). Stated recommendation: effect-site propofol 2.0 ug/mL with effect-site remifentanil 6.3 ng/mL in unpremedicated women without major muscle relaxation (printed 358, PDF p12); 3 ug/mL propofol or more advised when full relaxation is required.
- **ke0 / t1/2ke0 / tPeak / hysteresis:** none measured or reported. The design cites published T1/2ke0 (propofol Kazama 1999, remifentanil Egan 1996) and says 10 min is 4-5 times propofol's T1/2ke0 and 5 min is 6-7 times remifentanil's (derived: propofol t1/2ke0 about 2-2.5 min and remifentanil about 0.7-0.8 min; the paper gives no number). The model concentrations are blood, and samples were taken after equilibration. The ke0 values drive only the simulations in Figs 10-11 (not read in detail).
- **Potency ratio:** alfentanil:remifentanil 31.1 +/- 7.53 for intra-abdominal response suppression with propofol (pooled with Vuyk 1995, printed 356, PDF p10); the paper compares it with 30 (EEG, Egan 1996) and 41 (as supplement to nitrous oxide, Drover/Ausems). Whole-blood concentrations (remifentanil) and plasma alfentanil from Vuyk, so the basis differs between studies.
- Propofol: C50 for return of consciousness 3.4-3.5 ug/mL (matches 3.4 ug/mL for loss of consciousness in an earlier study, ref 7). Propofol C50 alone for laryngoscopy by extrapolation 7.3 ug/mL (compare Kazama 9.8 for laryngoscopy, 17.4 for intubation, as quoted).
- Simulation (printed 357-358, PDF p11-12, text layer): the fastest awakening after 180 min of infusion is about 6.5 min at propofol 1.5 ug/mL plus remifentanil 9.0 ng/mL on the 50% no-response isobole.

## Covariates
None modelled. All female, ASA I-II, no premedication, morphine given before emergence.

## Caveats
- No remifentanil-alone data (the authors say it was unsafe), so C50,rem is not identifiable; the fitted C50,rem is orders of magnitude above the studied range for several end points and the model collapsed to eq 2 or 3. For intra-abdominal surgery and laryngoscopy remifentanil acts as a partial agonist (ceiling) in this design.
- Many patients had no overlap of response and non-response data (17 of 29 for intra-abdominal), so per-patient C50s are midpoints or 0.
- Morphine 0.2 mg/kg 30 min before the end probably lowered the propofol concentration at awakening and delayed awakening.
- Internal disagreements in the printed text: intubation remifentanil C50 start 4.4 (abstract) vs 4.7 (Results); return-of-consciousness range 0.6 over 0-7 ng/mL (abstract) vs 0.4 over 0-10 (Results); age range 18-65 vs 20-65. Unresolved, not used by stanpumpR.
- One value quoted in the text (propofol 7.3 ug/mL for laryngoscopy C50 alone) is the x-axis intercept of Fig 1, not a fitted C50.
