# Short 2016: pharmacodynamic TCI of propofol and remifentanil on a BIS response surface

## Study
Prospective observational study, Auckland City Hospital. A convenience sample of 50 adults (ASA 1-3, 18-80 y, elective surgery with TIVA and neuromuscular block) was recruited; 5 were excluded (4 received midazolam or morphine, 1 lost infusion data), leaving 45 (28 F, 17 M; Table 2, PDF p.4). Mean (range): age 48 (19-73) y, weight 80 (45-169) kg, BMI 28 (19-45) kg/m2.
Drugs: propofol 10 mg/mL and remifentanil 40 ug/mL by two Graseby 3400 pumps run from a laptop (CCIP v2.4). The software gave effect-site TCI of both drugs, the user choosing a BIS target and a propofol:remifentanil ratio, and the software solved for the Ce pair that gives the targeted BIS on the response surface. The authors say the effect-site algorithm was checked against Stanpump (PDF p.2).
No drug concentrations were measured. The outcome is how well the surface-predicted BIS matched observed BIS (BIS DSC-XP, 15-s epoch, recorded every 30 s, reduced to 1-min epochs, SQI >= 50 only). Metrics follow Varvel 1992: performance error PE = 100 x (BIS observed - predicted) / predicted, MDPE (bias), MDAPE (inaccuracy), wobble.

## Model and parameters
All models are published ones (Table 1, PDF p.3, printed p.92). Units are L and L/min.
Propofol PK is Schnider; remifentanil PK is Minto; lean body mass is James (formulas in the Table 1 footnote).

Combined effect (PDF p.3): U_P = Ce_P/C50_P and U_R = Ce_R/C50_R; theta = U_P/(U_P + U_R); U = (U_P + U_R)/(1 - beta*theta + beta*theta^2); with beta = 0, U = U_P + U_R; predicted BIS = E0 - Emax * U^gamma/(1 + U)^gamma (as printed).
Performance (Table 3, PDF p.4; mean (SD) (range) of the individual medians, %):

| Phase | MDPE | MDAPE | Wobble |
|---|---|---|---|
| All data | 8 (24) (-40 to 70) | 25 (13) (11-70) | 15 (7) (5-39) |
| Induction, 0-20 min | 14 (30) (-31 to 113) | 31 (18) (11-113) | 27 (16) (8-77) |

| Recovery | 13 (35) (-40 to 110) | 31 (22) (4-110) | 23 (19) (4-90) |

Seven patients had a median bias above 50% (PDF p.4). Positive MDPE means observed BIS was higher than predicted, i.e. the model over-predicted drug effect.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: BIS (hypnosis). No other end point was modelled.
- Propofol: ke0 0.46 /min (Schnider, fitted on EEG spectral edge); BIS EC50 4.47 ug/mL, gamma 1.43 (Table 1). Concentration basis is the model's effect-site concentration; no assay, since none was drawn.
- Remifentanil: ke0 = 0.595 - 0.007*(age - 40) /min (Minto, fitted on spectral edge); BIS EC50 19.3 ng/mL, gamma 1.43 (Table 1).
- t1/2 ke0 and tPeak: none reported for either drug.
- MEAC / minimum effective or analgesic concentration: none reported. The 19.3 ng/mL is an EC50 for the BIS decrease, not an analgesic concentration.
- Equianalgesic or potency ratio: none; the potency "ratio" is the user-chosen theta = U_P/(U_P + U_R) in normalised C50 units (Fig. 6).
- Hysteresis: the authors link the large induction bias (MDPE 14%) partly to a misspecified ke0 and to over-prediction of concentration by Schnider in the first 21 min (Discussion, PDF p.6-7).
- They cite Billard 1997 for similar ke0 for BIS and spectral edge in adults; that is their argument, not shown here.

## Covariates
Age and BMI did not affect bias (Spearman rho -0.119, P = 0.44, and -0.09, P = 0.56, n = 45; PDF p.4). The model over-predicted by about 10% in the young and the slim relative to the elderly or obese (Figs 7-8). Covariates inside the PK models are age, weight, height and lean body mass (Table 1); the PD model has none.

## Caveats
- No concentration data, so the PK and PD contributions to the error cannot be separated.
- PK, ke0 and the surface come from different volunteer studies; the surface came from healthy adults aged about 20-43 y with no stimulus (PDF p.7).
- Convenience sample; no sample-size justification beyond past TCI studies.
- Table 1 prints the propofol CL1 height term as (hgt - 18); see open_questions.
- Remifentanil PD in this paper is for BIS, not for analgesia.
