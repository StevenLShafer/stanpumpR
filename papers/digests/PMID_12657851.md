# Stucke 2003: halothane and glutamatergic drive to inspiratory premotor neurons

## Study
Basic-science animal neurophysiology, not a PK/PD paper. Decerebrate, vagotomized, paralyzed, mechanically ventilated dogs (28 animals; 21 complete neuron protocols), hyperoxic (FIO2 > 0.8) and hypercapnic (PaCO2 55-65 mmHg) (PDF p.2-3, printed 898-899). Extracellular single-unit recording from caudal ventral respiratory group (cVRG) inspiratory neurons with localized picoejection of AMPA (7.5 uM), NMDA (200 uM) and the GABA-A blocker bicuculline (200 uM) at 0 and 1 MAC halothane in randomized order. Isoflurane (1.3-1.8 MAC) was used for surgery only. Halothane 1 MAC = 0.9% (from ref 10, dog; isoflurane 1 MAC = 1.4%). Reproducibility sub-study: 15 neurons from 3 animals (14 protocols analysed after outlier removal).

## Model and parameters
No pharmacokinetic model and no drug concentration in blood or brain. The only "concentration" is end-tidal % at 1 MAC. Statistics: Wilcoxon signed-rank, two-way repeated-measures ANOVA, mean +/- SD.
```yaml
parameters: []   # no PK parameters; neuronal results below are descriptive
```
Results (pooled, n = 21 neurons unless stated; PDF p.4-5, printed 900-901):
- Spontaneous firing (Fcon) reduced 20.6 +/- 18.0% by 1 MAC (P = 0.012 in abstract; text and Fig. 8 say P < 0.001; internal inconsistency in the paper).
- Overall excitatory drive (Fe, at complete bicuculline block) reduced 15.4 +/- 20.2%.
- Overall GABAergic inhibition alpha 0.60 +/- 0.1 at 0 MAC and 0.62 +/- 0.1 at 1 MAC (delta +5.2 +/- 14.9%, P = 0.19, not significant).
- AMPA response slope reduced 18.6 +/- 35.7% (net increase at D40Hz 40 -> 33.6 +/- 14.3 Hz); NMDA slope reduced 22.2 +/- 26.2% (n = 20; 40 -> 31.1 +/- 10.5 Hz).
- Reproducibility: run 2 slope 99.5 +/- 27.5% of run 1; method can detect about a 20% difference with 90% power.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Not an opioid paper. End point is neuronal discharge frequency (Hz) of cVRG inspiratory premotor neurons. No ke0, t1/2 ke0, tPeak, EC50, hysteresis, MEAC, or potency ratio is reported. The only exposure level is 1 MAC (0.9% in dog) versus 0 MAC.

## Covariates
None.

## Caveats
Decerebrate dog, single anesthetic level (no concentration-response), large between-neuron variability (SDs near or above means), outlier removal by Dixon test, residual isoflurane/halothane possible at 0 MAC (authors argue negligible). Cannot separate pre- from postsynaptic mechanism fully. Findings are mechanistic and do not give anything quantitative for a PK/PD model.
