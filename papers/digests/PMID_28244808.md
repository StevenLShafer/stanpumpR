# Balyan 2017: CYP2D6 genotype and oral oxycodone / oxymorphone PK in children

## Study
- Prospective observational pilot, part of a Phase IV safety/PK study (NCT01959204), three US sites; funded partly
  by Vistapharm (assay) and NIH R01 HD089458.
- 30 children, ASA I-II, after orthopaedic, thoracic, urological or colorectal surgery, once cleared for oral
  analgesia. Table 1: all 11.9 +/- 4.2 y, 45.6 +/- 17.4 kg; 2-6 y n=5 (16.8 kg); 7-12 y n=8 (41.7 kg); 13-18 y
  n=17 (56.0 kg). Sex not reported. CYP3A4 inhibitors/inducers within 14 days excluded.
- Single dose of oxycodone oral solution: 0.1 mg/kg (2-6 y), 0.08 mg/kg (7-12 y), 0.07 mg/kg (13-17 y). Salt vs
  base not stated.
- Sampling: 0, 0.5, 1, 2, 4, 6, 8, 12, 24 h. Plasma oxycodone and oxymorphone by LC-MS/MS, LLOQ 0.02 ng/mL.
- NCA (Phoenix WinNonlin 6.1), linear-up/log-down AUC; AUC0-6, 0-12, 0-24; all PK parameters normalized by the
  actual oxycodone dose (units printed as "arbitrary units", AU).
- CYP2D6 genotyped (TaqMan; long-PCR for *5 and duplications); total activity score (TAS, Gaedigk 2008) and a
  descriptive phenotype. Table 2: PM n=1 (*4/*41, TAS 0.5); IM n=13 (TAS 1: *1/*4 x5, *1/*6 x2, *17/*41 x1,
  *2A/*4 x4, *2A/*5 x1); EM n=16 (TAS 1.5: *1/*9, *2A/*9; TAS 2: *1/*1 x5, *1/*2A x6, *2A/*2A x3). No UM.
- Mann-Whitney comparisons, PM/IM (n=14) vs EM (n=16).

- Oxymorphone/oxycodone ratios by phenotype (Cmax, AUC0-6, AUC0-24) are shown only as plots (Figs 6-8, mean +/-
  95% CI, log axes); **no numeric group means are printed**. Text: oxymorphone concentrations were 1-5% of
  oxycodone's; oxymorphone formation "about 1-5%" of oxycodone elimination.
- Unit inference: Fig. 2A shows oxycodone peaks of several ng/mL after ~3 mg, which fits AU = ng/mL per mg, not per
  mg/kg.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- **None measured.** No MEAC, no analgesia or pain scores, no ke0/t½ke0/tPeak, no hysteresis.
- Background statements (citing others, ref 16): oxymorphone has 40-fold higher mu affinity and is 14-fold more
  potent than oxycodone; noroxymorphone 3-fold and 10-fold higher affinity than oxycodone and noroxycodone
  (Introduction, p. 338). Fig. 1 schematic gives oxymorphone 2.9%, noroxycodone 74%, noroxymorphone 21% of
  oxycodone metabolism (cited, not measured here).

## Covariates
- CYP2D6: no significant genotype effect on dose-normalized oxycodone Cmax (p = 0.498), AUC0-6 (p = 0.847) or
  AUC0-24 (p = 0.591) (Figs 3A-5A). Oxymorphone higher in EM: Cmax p = 0.028, AUC0-6 p = 0.0162, AUC0-24 p = 0.026;
  TAS effect on oxymorphone AUC0-24 p = 0.0091 (TAS 1 vs 2). Ratios higher in EM: Cmax p = 0.0007, AUC0-6
  p = 0.001, AUC0-24 p = 0.004.
- Age: no noticeable tmax difference across age groups (p. 343). No weight or age model.

## Caveats
- Small n; one PM, no UM. Oxymorphone AUC0-24 called unreliable by the authors (>20% extrapolated).
- Phenotype labels are Gaedigk-2008 TAS: TAS 1.0 (e.g. *1/*4) is "IM" here but is "normal metaboliser" under the
  2020 CPIC/DPWG consensus; the "PM/IM" group is mostly such TAS-1.0 children.
- Bioavailability not measured; postoperative children with possible delayed absorption; sex not reported.
- Units printed as arbitrary units; dose salt form not stated.
