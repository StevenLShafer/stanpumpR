# Overdyk 2019 / Doufas 2019: Opioid-induced ventilatory depression in sleep apnea (comment and reply)

## Study
Letters, no data. Overdyk et al. (printed p.936-937) say the Doufas 2019 remifentanil study (Anesthesiology 130:213-26) should not be generalized to other opioids or settings: a remifentanil TCI of 4 ng/ml for 10 min in a noisy operating room differs from morphine PCA on a night-time ward. The reply (p.937-938) restates the original design (remifentanil infusion 0.2 ug/kg ideal body weight/min, awake patients with moderate-severe OSA) and says missing plasma concentrations did not materially change the estimated interindividual variability of the effect-site concentration for half-maximal ventilatory depression (qualitative). It agrees findings should not be extrapolated. The third letter (p.938) is about chlorhexidine oral care in ventilated patients and is irrelevant to stanpumpR.
