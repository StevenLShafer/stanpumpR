# Yacobi 1983: esmolol pharmacokinetic profile (letter)

## Study
- 8 healthy men, 21-27 y, 62.4-76.2 kg. Constant IV infusions of 50, 150 and 400 mcg/kg/min for 2 h on three days.
  Isoproterenol challenge (dose giving a 50% HR rise) during and up to 60 min after each infusion (PDF p1).
- Blood esmolol by GC-MS, metabolite ASL-8123 by HPLC. Esmolol fitted with a two-compartment open model, the metabolite
  with a modified one-compartment model, by nonlinear least squares.

## Model and parameters
- Steady-state blood esmolol (mean +/- SD): 0.164 +/- 0.068, 0.569 +/- 0.204, 1.59 +/- 0.605 ug/mL at 50, 150 and 400
  mcg/kg/min; proportional to dose (r = 0.866, n = 24).
- Total clearance 363 +/- 184, 298 +/- 112, 285 +/- 104 mL/min/kg at the three rates, not dose-related (r = 0.210).
- Table I (400 mcg/kg/min, n = 8, PDF p1): esmolol terminal t1/2 9.19 +/- 3.51 min, CL 285 +/- 104 mL/min/kg,
  volume of distribution 3.43 +/- 1.42 L/kg. ASL-8123: peak 77.9 +/- 3.93 ug/mL at 146 +/- 11.1 min (26 min after the
  end), t1/2 223 +/- 14.0 min, formation t1/2 2.82 +/- 0.592 min, CL 1.28 +/- 0.19 mL/min/kg, V 0.411 +/- 0.057 L/kg,
  fraction formed 0.829. (The text layer reads the metabolite peak as "17.9"; the rendered table and the text both
  give 77.9.)
- The central volume and the distribution half-life are not printed. Clearance about 4 times cardiac output
  (70 mL/min/kg) and 14 times hepatic blood flow.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: reduction of the isoproterenol-induced increase in HR and BP, correlated with log blood esmolol. Blood
0.3 and 1 ug/mL went with 50% and 80% reduction in HR response and 30% and 50% in BP response. No significant effect
30 min after the end of infusion at any dose. No ke0 or time to peak.

## Covariates
None.

## Caveats
A one-page letter: no individual data, central volume or sampling schedule printed; sampling site not reported (as de
Bruijn 1987 notes for the companion Sum 1983 paper).
