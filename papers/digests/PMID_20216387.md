# Luginbuhl 2010: Noxious Stimulation Response Index (NSRI)

## Study
- Post hoc re-analysis of the Struys 2003 data set (ref 4): 45 ASA 1 women for ambulatory gynaecologic surgery (44 with complete data; 263 data sets), age 33 (5) to 34 (4) y, weight 63 (10) to 66 (11) kg, height 167 (6) to 168 (6) cm across three groups (PDF p2, printed 873).
- Propofol effect-site targets stepped up by 0.5 mcg/mL every 4 min, starting at 1.5 mcg/mL (no remifentanil) or 1.0 mcg/mL with remifentanil at constant effect-site targets 0, 2 and 4 ng/mL. Rugloop II control with Schnider propofol PK/ke0 and Minto remifentanil PK/ke0 (values not printed here).
- Measured at the end of each 4-min step: eyelash reflex, OAAS (modified, 0-5, Table 1), BIS, AAI, and motor response to a 2-s 100 Hz, 50 mA tetanic stimulus of the volar forearm.
- Analysis: prediction probability (PK) by a 1,000-sample bootstrap (one random point per patient), NONMEM V naive-pooled logistic regression for the NSRI at 50% response to tetanus.

## Model and parameters
Modified hierarchical interaction model (PDF p2-3, equations 1-7; equations 1-6 confirmed on the page image):
- Opioid attenuates stimulus intensity: postopioid = preopioid x (1 - Ce_opioid/(Ce50_opioid + Ce_opioid)) (fractional Emax, slope fixed at 1) (eq. 1).
- P(no response) = N^phi/(1 + N^phi), N = Ce_hyp/(Ce50_hyp x postopioid) (eqs. 4-5); Ce50_hyp is for the reference stimulus "shake and shout" with preopioid intensity 1.
- Relative intensity of laryngoscopy R_lar = Ce50_TOL / Ce50_TOSS (eq. 3). P_TOL = (N/R_lar)^phi/(1 + (N/R_lar)^phi) (eq. 6).
- NSRI = 100 x (1 - (N/R_lar)^sl/(1 + (N/R_lar)^sl)), slope factor sl = 2.18 calibrated so P_TOL 0.9 maps to NSRI 20; P_TOL 0.5 maps to NSRI 50 (eq. 7, text layer; Figure 2 on p4).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End points: loss of response to 2-s tetanic stimulation (analgesic state), OAAS, eyelash reflex, BIS, AAI (hypnotic state).
- Opioid audit (remifentanil): no MEAC or minimum analgesic concentration. Ce50 remifentanil 1.16 ng/mL (SE 0.48) in the modified model is the effect-site concentration reducing stimulus intensity by 50%; defined on the model, not as a clinical concentration; effect-site from the Minto model (not an assay; total/free, blood/plasma not applicable). The same quantity, in the original Bouillon model, is the 1.01 ng/mL that stanpumpR uses (see below). Remifentanil Ce 0, 2 and 4 ng/mL were the study targets. No ke0, t½ke0, tPeak or hysteresis is estimated or printed (Minto ke0 is used inside the Rugloop controller but its value is not given). No potency or equianalgesic ratio.
- Appendix claim: reanalysis of the earlier propofol-remifentanil data gave Ce50 propofol for hypnosis 4.82 mcg/mL in the original model, which the authors regard as high compared with other studies; the modified model gives 2.99 mcg/mL.

## Covariates
None. Women only, ASA 1.

## Caveats
- Post hoc, one study population (44 women), only two non-zero remifentanil concentrations, propofol and remifentanil targets not independent, so the effect of remifentanil is partly confounded (PK of remifentanil Ce for OAAS 0.43 shows reversed prediction, per the authors).
- The 2-s tetanus is weaker than surgical stimuli (NSRI50 61 against 50 for laryngoscopy); the index needs prospective validation.
- Bootstrap CIs are approximate; the authors give no P values.
- Several authors hold the NSRI patent via Dräger (financial disclosure, PDF p1).

## Verification against stanpumpR
- R/modelInteraction.R:18-23 takes Bouillon 2004 Table 4 Bayesian values: C50 remifentanil 1.01 ng/mL, C50 propofol 6.68 mcg/mL, opioid steepness 0.72, propofol steepness 6.9, pre-opioid intensity 0.83 (laryngoscopy). R/modelInteraction.R:31-33 implements postopioid = 0.83 x (1 - U^0.72/(U^0.72 + (1.01 x 0.83)^0.72)) and P(no response) = Prop^6.9/(Prop^6.9 + (6.68 x postopioid)^6.9). This is the original model that Luginbuhl's appendix equations A-B describe; the code's structure matches that description. Units agree (propofol mcg/mL, remifentanil ng/mL).
- Luginbuhl's modified model is not the same function: slope on the opioid is 1 (code 0.72), the remifentanil C50 is 1.16 ng/mL (code 1.01), and the laryngoscopy propofol C50 for no opioid is 8.46 mcg/mL (code 6.68 x 0.83 = 5.54 for propofol alone at R/modelInteraction.R:36). These differences reflect two parameterisations of overlapping data, not a transcription error in the code. Luginbuhl states that in the original model only the product Ce50_hyp x postopioid is identifiable, which is why the code's two factors cannot be checked individually against Luginbuhl's values. Open questions above record the choice.
- Remifentanil-equivalent conversion of other opioids via MEAC (R/modelInteraction.R:50-61) is stanpumpR's own extension, not in either paper.
- Verdict: **partly-verified**; no discrepancy with the paper; the choice of parameterisation is open.
