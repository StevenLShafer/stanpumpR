# Joerger 2007: population PK/PD of doxorubicin and cyclophosphamide (AC) in breast cancer

PDF page n is printed page 1050 + n (PDF p1 = 1051).

## Study
- 65 women with early or advanced breast cancer enrolled at 12 EORTC centres (1998-2002); **59** had complete PK and toxicity data and form the analysis set (p8/1058). 50 had metastatic disease evaluable for response, 9 were adjuvant.
- Table I (p8, n = 59; medians, 25th-75th percentiles): age 56 y (49-63; range 29-81 from p1065), BSA 1.81 m2 (1.68-1.91), weight 70 kg (62-80), AST 24 U/L (15-38), ALT 20 (15-29), bilirubin 7 umol/L (5-8.5), albumin 44 g/L (42-47), Cockcroft-Gault CLcr 82 mL/min (66-106).
- Dosing: doxorubicin 60 mg/m2 IV over 15 min, then cyclophosphamide 600 mg/m2 over 15 min, every 3 weeks (p4/1054). Salt/dose basis not stated.
- Sampling, first cycle only (limited sampling): doxorubicin (n = 290 samples, EDTA) before, at end of infusion, 0.5, 1.5 and 24 h after the end; cyclophosphamide (n = 250, heparin) before, at end of infusion, 1 and 24 h after the **start** (p4-5).
- Assays: doxorubicin and doxorubicinol by isocratic HPLC, LLQ 1.8-2.4 nmol/L; cyclophosphamide by capillary GC; cross-validated between centres (<15% deviation). 4-hydroxycyclophosphamide not measured.
- NONMEM V, FOCE, log-transformed concentrations in umol/L; BLQ handled as zero; covariates forward p < 0.01 (6.7), backward p < 0.005 (7.9).
- PD: Friberg semiphysiological neutrophil model (proliferating compartment, 3 transit compartments, feedback (ANCbase/ANCt)^gamma), linear drug effect on proliferation for both drugs, additive (Eq. 9).

## Model and parameters
Doxorubicin: two compartments; all doxorubicin clearance forms doxorubicinol (one compartment), parameterised as CL_DOL/fm and V3/fm (fm not identifiable). Cyclophosphamide: one compartment (two over-parameterised).

Covariate equations (p9/1059, confirmed on page image):
- Eq. 10: CL_DOX [L/h] = 47.6 x (BSA/1.8)^1.4 x (AST/21)^-0.24 x (AGE/56)^-0.54
- Eq. 11: CL_DOL/fm [L/h] = 108 x (CLCR/80)^0.54
- Eq. 12: CL_CYC [L/h] = 4.23 x 1.89^EIAD (EIAD = 3 patients on carbamazepine; their CL 5.9-10.1 L/h)

Text examples (p9): BSA +0.2 m2 -> CL_DOX +16%; AST +20 U/L -> -15%; age +10 y -> -9%; CLcr +20 mL/min -> CL_DOL/fm +13%; EIAD -> CL_CYC +89%.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: absolute neutrophil count (weekly), Friberg model, linear drug effects (Table VI, p12/1062, confirmed on image):
- MTT 113 h (RSE 7.6%; IIV 28.5%) = 4.7 days; gamma (feedback) 0.18 (RSE 5.5%).
- Slope doxorubicin 16.7 L/umol (RSE 27.9%; IIV 71.8%); slope cyclophosphamide 0.035 L/umol (RSE 63.8%; IIV 27.4%).
- CV on baseline ANC 43.5%.
- Population nadir 640/uL at day 10 (p10). Emax models were tried and rejected for precision.
- Exposure-response (Table VII, p13): AUC0-inf (umol h/L, first dose, NONMEM) doxorubicin CR 4.2, PR 3.9, SD 4.7, PD 4.5 (n 3/27/14/6), no difference (p 0.43); cyclophosphamide CR 1062, PR 916, SD 974, PD 602; CR/PR/SD 945 vs PD 602, p = 0.0002. The footnote says "mean (range)", but the column head says 95% CI.
- Not an opioid paper: no MEAC, ke0, tPeak or potency ratio. No effect site.

## Covariates
BSA, AST and age on CL_DOX; CLcr (Cockcroft-Gault) on CL_DOL/fm; EIAD on CL_CYC (Table V, p11: OFV drops 7.9, 7.2, 6.6, 7.7, 20.3). Bilirubin not significant (p = 0.7; nearly all within the normal range). No body-size covariate on cyclophosphamide or on any volume.

## Caveats
- One first-cycle profile per patient with sampling to 24 h: the doxorubicin terminal phase (literature 20-48 h) is truncated; the doxorubicinol and doxorubicin terminal half-lives are close (10.1 vs 11.1 h, derived), so they separate weakly.
- The EIAD covariate rests on 3 patients. The Discussion (p15/1065) says EIADs went with a higher CL_DOX, a slip for CL_CYC (Eq. 12, Table V).
- Eq. 5 prints MTT = (n+1)/ktr, then says MTT equals "(ktr)^-4"; with n = 3 that should be 4/ktr. Table VI glosses MTT as "median transition time" and RSE as "residual standard error". These are presentation errors.
- The dose basis is not stated for either drug (doxorubicin HCl or base; cyclophosphamide anhydrous or monohydrate), though the model is fitted in umol.
- Women only, adults 29-81 y, nearly normal liver function. 4-hydroxycyclophosphamide not measured, so cyclophosphamide autoinduction cannot be identified.
