# PMID_40067039 SDC1: remimazolam/remifentanil step protocol

## Study
Supplement (2 PDF pages) describing a volunteer trial of remimazolam, with and without a constant remifentanil target (the supplement writes "Cp"). Outcome score: Modified Observer's Assessment of Alertness/Sedation (MOAA/S).

- Table 1 (p.1): MOAA/S 5 = responds readily to name in normal tone; 4 = lethargic response; 3 = responds only after name called loudly/repeatedly; 2 = only after mild prodding or shaking; 1 = only after painful trapezius squeeze; 0 = no response to trapezius squeeze.
- Figure 1 (p.1) step-up/step-down remimazolam concentration steps (ng/mL), with constant remifentanil Cp:
  - Period I, no remifentanil, N=24: up 150, 300, 400, 800, 1300, 2000; down 1300, 800, 400, 300, 150.
  - Period II, remifentanil 0.5 ng/mL, N=24: same remimazolam steps.
  - IIIa, remifentanil 0.1 ng/mL, N=12: up 125, 225, 275, 525, 925, 1700; down 925, 525, 275, 225, 125.
  - IIIb, remifentanil 1.0 ng/mL, N=12: up 50, 100, 125, 225, 400, 1300; down 400, 225, 125, 100, 50.
  - IIIa*, remifentanil 2.0 ng/mL, N=12: up 100, 200, 250, 500, 850, 1350; down 850, 500, 250, 200, 100.
  - IIIb*, remifentanil 4.0 ng/mL, N=12: up 75, 150, 200, 400, 650, 1000; down 650, 400, 200, 150, 75.
  - The caption's wording on the starred arms (IIIa*/IIIb*, "where applicable") is garbled in the text layer: they apparently replace IIIa/IIIb after the first 6 volunteers. The remimazolam targets are ng/mL as printed; whether plasma or effect-site is not stated here.
- Figures 2 and 3 (p.2) are images (interventions per step; PK sampling scheme); not read.
