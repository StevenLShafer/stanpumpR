# Orliaguet 2015: BIS-guided dual closed-loop propofol/remifentanil in children

## Study
Single-centre, single-blind randomized trial (NCT00778505), Necker-Enfants Malades, Paris, July 2009 to March 2012. Ages 6 to 16 yr, elective surgery expected to last at least 60 min. 46 recruited, 42 with usable data: auto (closed loop) n=23, manual n=19 (PDF p. 3, Fig. 1, Table 1). Median age 12 (10-14) yr auto vs 14 (7-14) manual; weight 35 (30-51) vs 45 (27-57) kg; height 145 vs 157 cm (Table 1, PDF p. 4, medians with IQR).

Both groups: TCI in effect-site mode, Schnider propofol and Minto remifentanil (adult models, used unmodified in children), run on Infusion Toolbox 95 v4.11. Oral hydroxyzine 1 mg/kg premedication. No nitrous oxide. Manual group: clinician targets, BIS as close to 50 as practical. Auto group: cascade dual PID controller (BIS set point 50, 5-s cycle) from Liu 2011, with the upper target limits raised to 5-7 ug/mL propofol and 12-15 ng/mL remifentanil. Minimum interval between target changes equals the time to peak effect of each drug in its PK model (Discussion, PDF p. 5). Primary outcome: percentage of maintenance time with BIS 40-60.

## Model and parameters
No PK or PD model was fitted. The paper uses published adult models for the TCI and reports only consumption and effect-site targets achieved.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: BIS (EEG), a hypnosis measure; no pain end point other than FLACC/VAS in recovery. No ke0, t1/2ke0, MEAC or analgesic concentration, no EC50 or potency ratio is reported. tPeak is mentioned only as the controller's minimum interval between changes (equal to each drug's time to peak effect in its PK model, shorter for remifentanil), and no value is given. The paper cites Park 2007 (ref 41) for remifentanil halving the propofol EC50 for supraglottic airway insertion in children; that is another study's finding, not data here.

Mean remifentanil effect-site concentration targets reached 4.0-5.2 ng/mL (Minto, Ce) during maintenance with propofol Ce about 3 ug/mL (Table 3). These are concentrations the BIS-guided anaesthetic needed, not a MEAC.

## Covariates
Age, weight, height and sex are entered into the Schnider and Minto models (adult covariates; Schnider uses weight, height, sex, age; Minto uses age and LBM). No paediatric covariate analysis.

## Caveats
Authors: adult PK models in children; auto group used the 2006 adult controller; remifentanil use higher in auto group (could be controller gains or real need); Hawthorne effect likely in manual group; majority of children over 10 yr; regional anaesthesia used in 36-42%; underpowered for awareness; Liu and Chazot hold a patent on the controller gain constants and co-founded MedSteer. The Table 3 text layer is scrambled (columns misaligned); the numbers above are from the page image. The Table 3 propofol dose (mg/kg/h) and remifentanil dose (ug/kg/min) use different time units.
