# Douma 2011: remifentanil PCA vs epidural ropivacaine/sufentanil in labour

## Study
- Single-centre-style randomised, open-label comparison (computer-generated numbered envelopes). 26 enrolled, 20 analysed: remifentanil n=10, epidural n=10 (Fig. 1, PDF p.3: 5 excluded for delivery within 1 h of randomisation, 1 failed epidural).
- Healthy ASA I-II parturients, active labour, singleton cephalic, no prior opioid, cervix <=5 cm, BMI <40 kg/m2.
- Remifentanil arm: IV PCA, 40 ug loading dose, 40 ug boluses, 2-min lockout, 36-s bolus duration, maximum 1200 ug/h (Graseby 3300 pump). Stopped at full dilation. Epidural arm: 12.5 mL 0.2% ropivacaine load, then 0.1% ropivacaine with sufentanil 0.5 ug/mL at 10 mL/h.
- Pain VAS (0-10 cm) during contractions hourly, observer sedation (1-5), SpO2 continuous, neonatal outcome. No blood sampling, no assay.
- Demographics (Table 1, PDF p.4, printed p.121; mean (SD)): remifentanil age 32.7 (5.9) y, height 168.4 (9.4) cm, weight 83.3 (16.7) kg; epidural 31.0 (5.2) y, 168.0 (7.6) cm, 78.9 (11.9) kg. Remifentanil given: 2817 (1564) ug over analgesia duration 286 (145) min (derived: about 9.9 ug/min average, about 0.12 ug/kg/min at 83.3 kg). Ropivacaine 69.8 (23.7) mg, sufentanil 22.4 (11.9) ug in the epidural arm.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- No MEAC, no analgesic concentration, no ke0 / t1/2ke0 / tPeak, no EC50: none reported (no concentrations were measured).
- No equianalgesic or potency ratio stated.
- Clinical end points (Table 2, PDF p.4), mean (SD), remifentanil vs epidural: VAS pain 0 h 7.8 (1.6) vs 8.4 (0.9); 1 h 4.0 (2.0) vs 1.6 (2.2); 2 h 6.7 (1.5) vs 1.7 (1.3); 3 h 5.7 (3.0) vs 1.4 (1.0). Pain rebounded toward baseline in the remifentanil arm (no longer different from baseline at 2 h). Authors suggest rising labour pain, tolerance, or both, and that the dose may need to increase over time.
- SpO2 (%) 0 h 98.0 (1.6) vs 98.7 (1.6); 1 h 95.2 (2.4) vs 99.0 (1.1); 2 h 96.8 (1.7) vs 98.6 (1.3); 3 h 95.5 (3.3) vs 99.1 (0.7). One remifentanil patient needed supplemental oxygen. Sedation score at most 1.7 (0.8), no group difference. Nausea 5/10 vs 2/10, vomiting 5/10 vs 1/10 (NS).

## Covariates
None analysed.

## Caveats
n=10 per group; powered for a 3-point VAS difference only. Observation effectively limited to about 1 h for some patients (many left the analysis by end of study, Fig. 1: remifentanil 10, 9, 6 analysed at 1, 2, 3 h). Open label. Remifentanil stopped at full dilation, so no second-stage data. Dose is a PCA bolus regimen, not a concentration target.
