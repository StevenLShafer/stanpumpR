# Tran 2017: gabapentin population PK with saturable absorption (healthy Korean men)

## Study
- Pooled reference-formulation arms of seven single-dose, randomized, two-way crossover bioequivalence studies
  (Chonnam National University). 173 healthy **men**: 96 at 300 mg (4 studies), 26 at 400 mg (1), 51 at 800 mg (2).
- Age 19-30 y (mean 23.5 ± 1.9); weight 52.0-88.4 kg (67.7 ± 8.4); BSA 1.6-2.1 m² (Mosteller); Cockcroft-Gault
  CrCL 66.0-170.4 mL/min (mean 108.1 ± 17.1, **median 106.3**) (Table 1; the text-layer table columns are shifted,
  medians read by matching ranges).
- Fasted overnight, single oral dose with 240 mL water. Serum samples 0, 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12, 24 h.
- Assay: HPLC with OPA-MPA derivatization, fluorescence; linear 0.1-10 µg/mL, LLOQ 0.1 µg/mL.
- ABCB1 1236C>T, 2677G>T/A, 3435C>T genotyped by PCR-RFLP. 2677: GG 54 (31.2%), GT/GA 96 (55.5%), TT/AT 23 (13.3%).
- NONMEM 7.3, FOCE-I, ADVAN2 TRANS2; log-transformed data, additive error on log scale. Bootstrap (1000) and VPC.

## Model and parameters
One compartment, first-order absorption with lag; bioavailability an inhibitory-Emax function of dose
(Eq. 3/6): F1 = 1 − Imax·Dose/(ID50 + Dose), Dose in mg per administration. Covariates (Eq. 7, 8):
CL = 11.1·(CrCL/106.3)^0.331 L/h; ka = 0.860·(1 + 0.339·[2677 TT/AT]) h⁻¹.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No effect site, no concentration-effect data. Not an opioid; no MEAC, ke0, tPeak or potency ratio.

## Covariates
CrCL (Cockcroft-Gault) on CL, power form normalized to the median 106.3 mL/min, exponent 0.331.
ABCB1 2677G>T/A (TT/AT) on ka, ×1.339. Tested and not retained: age, weight, height, BSA, BMI, albumin,
ABCB1 1236 and 3435 (on CL); age, BSA, BMI, weight (on V).

## Caveats
- Healthy young men only (authors); narrow, normal CrCL range, so the 0.331 exponent is weakly determined
  (Fig. 2 R² 0.022).
- No IV reference: F is relative to the model's assumption that a vanishingly small dose is completely absorbed;
  V and CL are apparent on that scale.
- Only three dose levels (300/400/800 mg); ID50 RSE 31%, bootstrap CI 75.5-1440 mg.
- TT/AT n = 3 and 4 at 400 and 800 mg (authors).
- Population-prediction GOF under-predicts at low concentrations (authors, Fig. 4a).
