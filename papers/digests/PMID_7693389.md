# Otton 1993: CYP2D6 phenotype and hydrocodone O-demethylation to hydromorphone

## Study
- Addiction Research Foundation, Toronto. Open label.
- In vitro: microsomes from 3 extensive-metaboliser (EM) livers and 1 poor-metaboliser (PM) liver (D6-B/D6-B).
  EM: Km 300 ± 46 µmol/L, Vmax 50 ± 13 pmol/mg/min; PM liver formed 1.7 vs 18.4 ± 5.1 pmol/mg/min at 150 µmol/L.
  Quinidine Ki 0.015 µmol/L, quinine 11 µmol/L (competitive).
- Cosegregation: 11 EM + 7 PM (dextromethorphan ODMR phenotyped) took 10 mg hydrocodone bitartrate (6.05 mg base);
  0-8 h urine hydrocodone/hydromorphone ratio vs ODMR, rs = 0.85 (p < 0.001, n = 18).
- PK/PD: healthy volunteers (6 women, 5 men; age 26.3 ± 5.7 yr, 19-36; weight 71.9 ± 16.8 kg, 50-108), white ancestry.
  10 mg hydrocodone bitartrate orally (Hycodan syrup); 5 EM, 5-6 PM (subject 11 genotyped wild-type but phenotypically
  PM, analysed as PM), 4-5 EM repeated after quinidine 100 mg. Plasma to 24 h, urine to 48 h.
- Assay: HPLC with UV (hydrocodone) and electrochemical (hydromorphone) detection, after beta-glucuronidase hydrolysis
  of both plasma and urine, so hydromorphone is TOTAL (conjugated + unconjugated).
- PK by NCA: CL = dose(base)/AUC; CLHM (partial metabolic clearance to hydromorphone) = urinary hydromorphone 0-24 h /
  hydrocodone AUC0-24.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Opioid audit:
- MEAC / analgesic concentration: none. Analgesia not measured.
- ke0 / t½ke0 / tPeak / hysteresis: none estimated. Effects sampled at 30, 60, 90, 120 min.
- End points: heart rate fell in all groups (-10.7 to -18.4 beats/min), miosis in all subjects, no phenotypic
  difference in any physiologic measure. ARCI-Cole summed "good" effects over the first hour: EM 49.2 ± 12.16 vs PM
  12.0 ± 11.10 (mean ± SEM, p < 0.05); "bad" effects EM -1.6 vs PM 19.5 (NS). In the second hour PMs reported good
  effects but continued to report bad effects (Fig. 3).
- Potency (Introduction, cited): hydromorphone 7-8 times as potent as morphine subcutaneously; 7 times in suppressing
  morphine abstinence. Authors suggest most of hydrocodone's opiate effect probably resides in hydromorphone.

## Covariates
CYP2D6 phenotype (dextromethorphan ODMR) and quinidine inhibition; no other covariates.

## Caveats
Very small groups (5 EM, 5-6 PM); 10 mg single dose; total (hydrolysed) hydromorphone, so plasma levels and CLHM
include conjugates; CLHM from urinary recovery is a lower bound on formation clearance; subject 11 phenotype/genotype
discordant; drug-naive subjects for subjective effects. Table I OCR is unreliable, check the PDF for individual values.
