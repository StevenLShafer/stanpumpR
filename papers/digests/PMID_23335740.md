# Würthwein 2013: caspofungin population PK at 70-200 mg daily in adults with invasive aspergillosis

## Study
- Phase II dose-escalation trial at three German university hospitals (EudraCT 2006-001936-30, NCT00404092). Adults with proven or probable invasive aspergillosis, first-line.
- Caspofungin once daily as a 2-hour (120-min) IV infusion at 70, 100, 150 or 200 mg, up to 28 days; 9, 8, 9 and 20 patients.
- Sampling: day 1 (pre-dose, 2 h [peak], 3 h, 5-7 h, 24 h [trough] after the start of infusion) and peak and trough on days 4, 7, 14 and 28. 468 samples, 462 analysed from 46 patients; none 7.5-22 h after a dose on later days.
- Assay: LC-MS/MS, total plasma caspofungin, LLOQ 84 ug/L.
- Table 1: 21 men, 25 women; median age 61 years (18-74); median weight 76 kg (43-104), 14 above 80 kg; bilirubin 0.8 mg/dL (0.3-2.3); creatinine clearance 102 mL/min (39-260). No height or BMI.
- NONMEM 7; bootstrap, pcVPC and SVPC.

## Model and parameters
Linear two-compartment model, proportional error, IIV on CL, V1 and V2, CL-V1 correlation, IOV on CL. Weight on CL and V1 with one fractional change: CL_i = theta1 [1 + theta2 (BW - BW_median)] (Methods), BW_median 76 kg.

Table 3 (steady state, geometric mean, GCV): AUC 170, 243, 365, 487 mg h/L; Cmax 13.8, 19.7, 29.6, 39.4 mg/L; Cmin 4.2, 6.0, 9.0, 12.0 mg/L at 70, 100, 150, 200 mg. The AUCs equal dose / 0.411 L/h.

## Covariates
Weight only. Gender on V1, age on CL and V1 and creatinine clearance on CL were significant in the forward step but had RSEs of 34-46% and were dropped. A Michaelis-Menten model gave Vmax 118 mg/h and Km 289 mg/L, far above the concentrations, so elimination was taken as linear. An allometric exponent estimated at 0.952 led to the linear form.

## Caveats
- Sparse later sampling (peak and trough), no samples beyond 25 h after the last dose: the terminal gamma phase is not identified.
- One patient with an extreme V2 (43.8 L) was excluded.
- CASLAMB comparison: CL 0.462 L/h, V1 8.33 L, V2 3.59 L, Q 1.25 L/h there; external validation showed a small bias toward overestimating CASLAMB concentrations.
