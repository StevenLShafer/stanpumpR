# Marino 2012: absolute carbamazepine bioavailability and clearance at steady state (IV stable label)

## Study
- 92 adults (18-64 y) with epilepsy on stable maintenance carbamazepine (monotherapy or non-interacting co-medication), five US sites; 45 M / 47 F; age 41 (SD 11) y; weight 80.4 (20.1) kg; daily dose 819 (514) mg (range 200-2400 mg/day); race 37 African American / 1 Asian / 54 Caucasian; 31 immediate-release, 61 extended-release (Table 1).
- 100 mg stable-labelled (13C, 15N) carbamazepine in 22.5% HP-beta-cyclodextrin, IV over 10 min, replacing 100 mg of the usual morning oral dose (methods).
- Plasma samples pre-dose and 0.083, 0.25, 0.5, 1, 2, 4, 6, 12, 24, 48, 72, 96 h. LC-MS (labelled m/z 238 vs unlabelled 236; d10 internal standard), total and free (ultrafiltration) concentrations; standard curve 0.5-20 ug/mL.
- Noncompartmental analysis (WinNonlin 5.1): CL = Dose/AUCinf of the labelled drug; Vd = Dose/(ke x AUCinf); F from oral AUC(0-tau) vs IV, in 42 subjects dosed q8h or q12h.

- Sex and race together explained 21.7% of the variance in weight-adjusted CL (Results). Caucasian women had 67% greater CL/kg than African American men.
- The authors note F is slightly underestimated (about 10%) because 100 mg of the morning oral dose was withheld (Discussion). F > 1 in some subjects.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. Therapeutic range quoted from the literature as 4-12 ug/mL (Introduction). No PD model.

## Covariates
Sex and race (ANOVA on CL/kg, t½, Vd); no interaction between sex and race. Formulation did not affect F. No covariate equation.

## Caveats
- Induced steady state only (patients on chronic therapy); noncompartmental; single 100 mg IV tracer.
- Race confounded with site and formulation (Miami subjects mostly IR; CL there 0.038 vs 0.052 Emory and 0.049 Minnesota L/h/kg).
- Text layer of Table 2 is mis-aligned; L/h values per column are reconstructed.
- Repo and PMC differ on page range: PubMed gives 483-488.
