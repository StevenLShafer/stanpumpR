# Tobin 2026: atomoxetine population PK in children and adolescents (CYP2D6 / CYP2C19)

## Study
- 86 children and adolescents with ADHD (6-17 y; mean age 12.6 +/- 3.2 y, weight 54.5 +/- 26.4 kg, BMI 21.8 +/- 6.4), pooled from three studies (p2-3, Table 1 p5). CYP2D6 phenotype by CPIC activity score: 6 PM, 41 IM, 35 NM, 4 UM; CYP2C19 phenotypes also recorded. 159 visits, 1946 concentrations. Participants were 81% male; all 6 PMs were male.
- Study A: single oral 0.5 mg/kg dose, sampling up to 72 h (PM), 24 h (IM, NM), 12 h (UM). Study B: single dose to validate an unpublished model, with a repeat visit (15-386 days later). Study C: model-individualized dosing aimed at a steady-state Cmax of 400 ng/mL, with a single-dose session and up to two steady-state sessions at weeks 6 and 18; steady-state sampling up to 12 h (UM/NM) or 24 h (IM/PM) (p3). Subjects fasted from midnight to 2 h post dose.
- Assay (matrix, total vs free) is not described in the main text. It refers to the earlier papers (Brown 2016, Cheng 2024), so treat "plasma, total" as unconfirmed.
- Analysis: NCA plus Pumas 2.5.1 nonlinear mixed effects, using a two-stage approach in which each participant-occasion is a separate "individual", because absorption was erratic between and within subjects (p4). Inter-occasion variability is therefore merged into BSV.

## Model and parameters
One compartment, first-order elimination, zero-order input into a depot, then first-order absorption (p7). Combined additive plus proportional error. Covariates (p4, Eqs 3-5; Table 3 p8):
- V/F = Vtv x (wt/70)^1.0 x (1/Frel,CYP2D6) x (1/Frel,CYP2C19) x exp(eta)
- CL/F = CLtv x (wt/10 as printed; text says 70)^0.75 x (1/Frel,CYP2D6) x (1/Frel,CYP2C19) x (1 + E,CYP2D6 PM) x exp(eta). See open questions.
- Frel is relative bioavailability compared with NMs (NM = 1).

NCA (Table 2, p6; mean +/- SD; dose-normalized): single-dose Cmax in ng/mL per mg was 275 (PM), 79 (IM), 40 (NM), 23 (UM); AUC0-24 in ng.h/mL per mg was 21.8, 12.8, 9.5, 7.0; half-life 17.3, 4.5, 2.5, 2.3 h; tmax 4.32, 1.73, 1.46, 0.92 h. Steady-state in PM/IM/NM: Cmax per mg 512, 92, 50; half-life 12.2, 3.3, 3.8 h (UM n=1).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD model and no ke0. The only concentration target is a proposed therapeutic target of a peak plasma concentration of 400 ng/mL (p3, p10-11), described as the "target theoretical therapeutic concentration"; it is a Cmax target used for dosing, not a measured MEAC, and no assay type is stated. Observed steady-state Cmax in Study C was 362 +/- 25 in PMs, 626 +/- 286 in IMs and 444 +/- 242 in NMs (p6). The text also gives 452 ng/mL for a fourth group labelled "PMs", which looks like a labelling slip (probably UM). Not an opioid; no opioid audit items.

## Covariates
Body weight by allometry (0.75 on CL, 1.0 on V, centred 70 kg); CYP2D6 PM and IM relative bioavailability; CYP2D6 PM clearance effect; CYP2C19 PM relative bioavailability. CYP2D6 UM effects, IM clearance effect, and obesity were not retained. A single Asian participant showed lower ka, V and CL but was not modelled (p8).

## Caveats
- Very small PM (n=6) and UM (n=4) groups; PM/UM excluded from the qualification VPC/QPC (p9). The 6 PMs are all male and mostly White.
- Two-stage method: BSV likely over-estimated, and covariates are screened on EBEs, which the authors acknowledge (p12). Parameters at bounds (ka, duration).
- Observed IM steady-state median Cmax exceeded the 95th percentile of simulated medians; prediction error -13.8% (95% CI -33.5 to 1.0) (p9-10, Figure 5).
- The authors compare their 99 L V/F with a label IV volume of 110 L (0.63 bioavailability for NMs).
