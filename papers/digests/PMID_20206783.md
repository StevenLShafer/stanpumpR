# Boellner 2010: single-dose lisdexamfetamine 30/50/70 mg in children, d-amphetamine and intact LDX PK

## Study
Single-centre (Arkansas), randomised, open-label, 3-period crossover (two 3 x 3 Latin squares, 6
sequences of 3). 18 children 6-12 y with ADHD: 10 boys, 8 girls; age 9.6 (SD 1.9) y; weight 36.0 (7.6)
kg (range 25.2-47.0); height 139.2 (10.2) cm; 8 white, 8 black, 1 Native American, 1 Asian American.
Single oral LDX 30, 50 and 70 mg, 7-day washout of prior medication and 6-day washouts between
periods. Venous samples at 0, 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 10, 12, 24 and 48 h. HPLC-MS/MS
(Cedra), d-amphetamine range 2-200 ng/mL (LLOQ 2), intact LDX 1-100 ng/mL. Non-compartmental analysis
(WinNonlin 4.0). PK population n = 16, 17, 17 at 30, 50, 70 mg.

## Model and parameters
Non-compartmental only. d-Amphetamine, Table I, mean (SD) [%CV]:

| | 30 mg | 50 mg | 70 mg |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cmax, ng/mL | 53.2 (9.62) [18.1] | 93.3 (18.2) [19.5] | 134 (26.1) [19.4] |
| Tmax, h | 3.41 (1.09) [31.9] | 3.58 (1.18) [33.0] | 3.46 (1.34) [38.6] |
| t1/2, h | 8.90 (1.33) [15.0] | 8.61 (1.04) [12.1] | 8.64 (1.32) [15.3] |
| AUC0-inf, ng.h/mL | 844.6 (116.7) | 1510.0 (241.6) | 2157.0 (383.3) |
| AUC0-t, ng.h/mL | 745.3 (129.3) | 1448.0 (246.7) | 2088.0 (394.0) |

Dose proportionality: power-model b about 1 (P < 0.001) for Cmax and AUC. d-Amphetamine > LLOQ at 1 h
and still > LLOQ at 48 h. Intact LDX: Tmax about 1 h, t1/2 about 0.5 h, < LLOQ at 48 h, exposure
more than dose-proportional (b > 1.5). AUC 10-20% higher in girls and in 6-9 vs 10-12 y; differences
shrank when normalised by mg/kg (Table III).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None (tolerability only: anorexia, insomnia, etc.). No concentration-effect data.

## Covariates
Descriptive only: sex and age-group exposure differences, largely explained by mg/kg dose.

## Caveats
n = 18, single doses, healthy-appearing children 6-12 y only. Non-compartmental.

## Verification against stanpumpR
- Numbers: 18 children, mean 36.0 kg, US, Cmax 53.2 / 93.3 / 134.0 ng/mL, Tmax about 3.5 h, dose
  proportionality: **match** (abstract, Table I).
- Model predictions at 36 kg non-Japanese (44.3 / 73.9 / 103.5 ng/mL; Tmax 4.8 h): reproduced by hand
  (derived: CL/F 11.63 L/h, V/F 138.7 L, 8.903 mg base -> Cmax 44.4 ng/mL at 4.84 h). **matches**.
- **Discrepancy (characterisation)**: code line 37-38 and help line 13 call Boellner "an independent
  study". It is not: Tsuda 2020's dataset includes "a Phase 1 study [10]" conducted in the US, and
  Tsuda's ref 10 is this paper (Tsuda Methods 2.1 and reference list). Boellner's children are among
  Tsuda's 32 non-Japanese patients. The check is therefore an in-sample comparison, which makes the
  17-23% Cmax shortfall more notable, not less, and still supports the base-equivalent dose basis over
  capsule mass. Test file line 5 ("external check") has the same wording issue.
