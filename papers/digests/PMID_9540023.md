# Catalano 1998: insulin metabolic clearance rate by clamp, before and during pregnancy

PDF pages 1-6 are printed pages 403-408.

## Study
- Prospective longitudinal study, Medical Center Hospital of Vermont, 1986-1991 (page 2 / p404). 16 healthy nonobese
  women planning to conceive, all with a normal 75-g OGTT before conception. Two groups:
  - **normal glucose tolerance (control), n 7**: normal 100-g 3-h OGTT in the index pregnancy;
  - **abnormal glucose tolerance, n 9**: 8 with gestational diabetes by Carpenter-Coustan, 1 with one abnormal OGTT
    value; 2 eventually needed insulin therapy. Figures label this group "GDM".
- Pregravid demographics (Table 1, page 3 / p405, rendered; means ± SD, normal vs abnormal): age 31 ± 5.5 vs
  31.7 ± 2.6 y; weight 57.0 ± 7.8 vs 58.9 ± 6.0 kg; height 166.4 ± 6.4 vs 168.9 ± 8.3 cm; BMI 20.5 ± 2.1 vs
  20.6 ± 1.2 kg/m2; fat mass 10.1 ± 3.6 vs 12.9 ± 2.5 kg; body fat 17.5 ± 5.2 vs 21.8 ± 3.3%; lean body mass
  46.8 ± 5.8 vs 46.0 ± 4.8 kg; months to conception 3.0 ± 2.5 vs 3.8 ± 3.3. No group difference (P 0.07 to 1.0).
  No pregnancy weights, body surface areas or creatinine clearances are tabulated.
- Each woman studied pregravid, early (12-14 weeks) and late (34-36 weeks) gestation, after an 11-h fast and a
  standardised diet: 2-h hyperinsulinaemic-euglycaemic clamp (DeFronzo) at **40 mU/m2/min** of insulin; basal samples
  and the last 40 min of the clamp analysed (abstract, page 1).
- Assay: insulin by double-antibody RIA (interassay CV 7.1%, intra 6.0%), C-peptide by RIA (7.2%, 3.5%); the insulin
  assay does not distinguish infused from endogenous insulin (page 2). Each subject's three visits run in one batch.
- Statistics: mean ± SD; repeated-measures ANOVA (time, group, group-by-time), SAS.

## Model and parameters
No PK model. The metabolic clearance rate (MCR) of insulin is the clamp infusion rate over the steady-state insulin
concentration, corrected for residual beta-cell secretion through the C-peptide suppression ratio (modified from
Minaker; page 2 / p404). **As printed** (zoomed render) the equation reads I / (SSI × FI × (SS C-pep / F C-pep)),
which does not give mL/m2/min dimensionally; the prose and abstract describe the denominator as steady-state insulin
corrected for residual secretion. A subtraction form, I / (SSI - FI × SS C-pep/F C-pep), with I = 40 mU/m2/min
taken as 240 pmol/m2/min (the conversion 6 pmol/mU is my assumption; the paper states none), gives about 432 and
392 mL/m2/min from the pregravid group means (derived, from Tables 2-3 and the C-peptide percentages), near the
printed 440 and 410. That supports subtraction but does not prove it; the printed form is recorded as printed.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PK/PD model, ke0 or concentration-effect relation. End points are basal and clamp insulin and C-peptide, C-peptide
suppression by infused insulin, and MCR of insulin. Insulin sensitivity is from the group's earlier papers (refs
10-12) and not tabulated here. The authors cite saturable insulin clearance at about 200 µU/mL (Minaker; page 5).

## Covariates
MCR did not correlate with maternal weight, fat-free mass, percent body fat, insulin sensitivity or placental weight
at any visit (data not shown). The only correlation was with creatinine clearance before conception (r = 0.64,
P = 0.01), not during gestation (page 3). MCR is per m2 of body surface area; BSA itself is not reported.

## Caveats
- Small n (7 + 9), lean women only (BMI about 20.5); authors say the results may not apply to obese women.
- MCR at one supraphysiological clamp rate (40 mU/m2/min), steady-state, IV insulin; a clamp clearance of infused
  human insulin, not an apparent CL/F of a subcutaneous product.
- The printed MCR equation is dimensionally inconsistent (see above); the assay cannot separate infused from
  endogenous insulin, so the correction rests on C-peptide.
- Per-group early and late MCR are figure-only. P = 0.0005 is the overall change over time across three visits,
  not a pairwise late-vs-pregravid test.
- Abstract vs body: P for group difference 0.76 vs 0.77; the basal insulin and C-peptide P values are swapped in
  the abstract relative to Table 2.
