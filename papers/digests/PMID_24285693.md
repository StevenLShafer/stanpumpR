# Fu 2014: propofol EC50 for loss of consciousness by menstrual phase

## Study
Prospective, double-blinded up-and-down (Dixon) study, 42 women ASA I-II, age 18-40, regular cycles, BMI 18-33, elective gynaecological laparoscopy, no premedication. Follicular group (cycle days 1-10, n=22) and luteal group (days 18-24, n=20); phase confirmed by progesterone assay (competitive chemiluminescent enzyme immunoassay, Roche E170; serum/plasma type not stated). Propofol and remifentanil by TCI (Base Primea pump) using Schnider's propofol PK-PD model and Minto's remifentanil model. First target 3.5 ug/mL, steps of 0.5 ug/mL between patients; within a patient, +0.5 ug/mL every 4 min until LOC (loss of response to verbal command). Remifentanil (Ce 4 ng/mL) started only after LOC. Anaesthesia then maintained to BIS 50 (Ce propofol floor 2.0 ug/mL). No propofol concentration was measured; every concentration is a model-predicted effect-site value (PDF p.1-2, 4; printed 507, 509, 512).

## Model and parameters
No model was fitted. EC50 is the mean of the crossover midpoints (ineffective then effective) in the up-down sequence; probit analysis gave EC5 and EC95 (values not given in the text).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point is LOC, loss of response to verbal command, within 4 min of each 0.5 ug/mL step (the paper holds each target 4 min to approach effect-site steady state). Secondary: BIS at LOC and emergence, eye opening. Concentrations are Schnider-model predictions, so the EC50s are tied to that model's ke0 and PK; no ke0 or tPeak is reported or estimated. Remifentanil at LOC was not yet running, so the LOC EC50s are propofol alone (premedication none). Spearman correlation of predicted Ce at LOC with progesterone r = -0.49, P<0.001 (all 42 patients); no significant correlation with FSH, LH, oestradiol (Fig 2, p.510). The BIS at 95% probability of LOC (44 vs 51) and of emergence (80 vs 79) did not differ by phase. Not an opioid paper; remifentanil is a co-drug, with no MEAC, ke0 or potency value reported for it.

## Covariates
Menstrual phase (luteal vs follicular), progesterone level. No equation; the Schnider model used has no menstrual covariate, as the authors note.

## Caveats
Authors: no plasma propofol measured, so Ce is predicted; phase assignment by cycle day plus progesterone; EC50 by Dixon method has little practical use for dosing. Notes: n of 20-22 per arm, all women of 51 kg average weight (Chinese), no male or mid-cycle comparator. Methods say cycle length 23-35 days; the Limitations paragraph says <22 or >36 days excluded (p.2 vs p.512): minor inconsistency. Table 2 p-values: FSH differs (0.04), LH 0.17, oestradiol 0.18 (read on the rendered page; the text layer scrambles them). Emergence-time and PONV differences (45% vs 15%, P=0.047, Table 4) are secondary.

## Verification against stanpumpR
Nothing to verify; the paper is not cited. `R/drugs_propofol.R` (Eleveld 2018 PK and PD, tPeak 1.6 min at line 276) has no menstrual-phase covariate, and none is implied: these EC50s are expressed in Schnider-model effect-site concentration, a different baseline from the library's Eleveld default (Eleveld's effect-site and Schnider's differ in ke0 and PK), so the 3.58 vs 4.17 ug/mL gap should not be applied to an Eleveld Ce without conversion. The paper's direction (luteal lower by 0.59 ug/mL EC50, 0.83 ug/mL mean Ce at LOC) is against the follicular group of the same study, not against any population model.
