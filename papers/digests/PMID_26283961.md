# Barrett 2015: K-PD model of nitroprusside and MAP in anaesthetised children

## Study
NICHD-2003-09-DR (BPCA; design in Drover et al. 2015): multicentre, randomised, double-blind, parallel, dose-ranging,
effect-controlled trial then open-label titration; 203 children (birth to 17 y) needing deliberate hypotension or
controlled normotension under anaesthesia for at least 2 h. Blinded phase: SNP 0.3, 1.0, 2.0 or 3.0 ug/kg/min for up
to 30 min (stepped titration); open label up to 180 min. MAP every 2 min. **No SNP concentration could be measured;
every plasma cyanide sample was below the 0.10 ug/mL detection limit** (p.3). Model building on the blinded phase:
**3038 MAP measurements from 202 patients** (Results p.4; the abstract says 3464); evaluation on the open-label phase
(10,303 MAP from 197). Model-building population (Table 1, PDF p.5, image-confirmed): age mean 8.83 y (median 11.3,
0.01-17.01), weight 33.8 kg (2.8-112.2), 73 M / 129 F; dose rate mean 0.87 ug/kg/min (0.09-3.04); 4 neonates, 51
infants, 47 children, 100 adolescents. NONMEM 6.2 FOCE/FOCE-I; bootstrap 1000.

## Model and parameters
K-PD: dose drives a one-compartment "effect compartment" of **nominal V 1 L/70 kg (fixed)** with clearance CL; both
allometric on weight (exponents 1 and 0.75). Inhibitory sigmoid Emax on MAP with a **two-group mixture on EC50**,
baseline S0 a power function of age, plus a linear drift ("disease-progression") term (eq. 1-5). Table 2 (PDF p.6,
image-confirmed):

EC50's bootstrap average (104.3) is well below the model estimate (138).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: MAP. Simulations (deterministic, Tables 3-4): infusion for 5 min to lower MAP 10 mmHg needs 0.346-0.432
ug/kg/min (low-EC50 group) or 1.51-1.88 (high-EC50 group) across five age/weight strata; 15 mmHg needs 0.407-0.537 or
1.73-2.38. A 3-min loading then 30-min maintenance to MAP 60 mmHg in a 7-month, 8 kg child: first dose given as
0.566 (text), 0.577 (Table 4) and 0.557 (Figure 5 legend) ug/kg/min, second 0.158; high-EC50 2.472 / 0.691. Authors
suggest assuming low EC50 first, then increasing. The FDA-approved starting dose 0.3 ug/kg/min is said to bisect the
model's recommendations. Toxicity not modelled. Not an opioid; no MEAC.

## Covariates
Weight (allometric CL and V, fixed exponents); post-natal age on baseline MAP. No maturation of CL beyond size was
found. The two sensitivity groups could not be predicted from any covariate.

## Caveats
No drug concentration: CL and EC50 are on a nominal scale set by the fixed V. Population predictions were high
against the open-label data (unrecorded stimuli, rescue drugs). Anaesthetised children only. Several internal
inconsistencies (3464 vs 3038 observations; CL 3.12 vs 3.13; PwrAge 0.0333 vs 0.0338; the 7-month loading dose; a
"10 mm Hg" label repeated where 15 is meant in the Simulation 1 text).
