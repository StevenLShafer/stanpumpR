# Coreg (carvedilol) tablets label, NDA 020297, 2023

The document is what its name says: the Coreg immediate-release tablet label (not Coreg CR), Highlights "Revised
07/2023", every page footed "Reference ID: 5265063" and headed "This label may not be the latest approved by FDA".
Strengths 3.125, 6.25, 12.5 and 25 mg (section 3, p4).

## Study
Not a study. Regulatory label. Indications: chronic heart failure, LV dysfunction after myocardial infarction,
essential hypertension (section 1, p2).

## Model and parameters
No compartmental model. Section 12.3 (PDF p20-21) gives:
- Absolute oral bioavailability about 25% to 35%, from first-pass metabolism.
- Apparent mean terminal elimination half-life "generally" 7 to 10 hours (oral). The Overdosage section (p18)
  repeats a 7- to 10-hour half-life.
- Concentrations proportional to dose; food delays time to peak with no significant change in extent.
- Steady-state volume of distribution about 115 L; plasma clearance 500 to 700 mL/min; more than 98% protein bound
  (mainly albumin).
- Parent about 7% of plasma radioactivity AUC; under 2% of dose excreted unchanged in urine; metabolites mainly in
  bile/faeces.
- Stereoselective first pass: R(+) plasma levels about 2 to 3 times S(-); terminal half-lives R(+) 5 to 9 h,
  S(-) 7 to 11 h.
- No Cmax, tmax, AUC or concentration value in ng/mL appears anywhere in the label (text search and section read).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- Beta-blockade (S(-) enantiomer only) usually seen within 1 hour; alpha1-blockade (both enantiomers, equal
  potency) within 30 minutes (12.1, 12.2, p19-20). Carvedilol reduces exercise- and isoproterenol-induced
  tachycardia (p20).
- 4'-hydroxyphenyl metabolite about 13 times more potent than parent for beta-blockade (preclinical); the 3
  active metabolites reach about one-tenth of parent concentrations (12.3, p21).
- No therapeutic or toxic concentration range is given.

## Covariates
- CYP2D6: debrisoquin poor metabolisers have 2- to 3-fold higher R(+) and 20% to 25% higher S(-) concentrations;
  CYP2C19 poor metabolisers unchanged (12.3, p21). Enzymes: CYP2D6 and CYP2C9, lesser 3A4, 2C19, 1A2, 2E1.
- Heart failure: AUC and Cmax raised, up to 50% to 100% in 6 NYHA IV subjects; half-life similar (12.4, p21).
- Elderly: about 50% higher levels (p22). Severe cirrhosis: 4- to 7-fold higher (contraindicated). Moderate to
  severe renal impairment: AUC 40% to 50% higher, peak 12% to 26% higher; not dialysed (p22).
- Interactions (12.5, p22-23): rifampin lowers AUC and Cmax about 70%; cimetidine raises AUC 30%; amiodarone at
  least doubles S(-) troughs.

## Dosing (section 2, p2-3)
Take with food. Heart failure: 3.125 mg twice daily for 2 weeks, then 6.25, 12.5, 25 mg twice daily at intervals
of at least 2 weeks (up to 50 mg twice daily over 85 kg). Post-MI: 6.25 mg twice daily, then 12.5 and 25 mg after
3 to 10 days. Hypertension: 6.25 mg twice daily, up-titrate every 7 to 14 days to 25 mg twice daily; total daily
dose not over 50 mg. Paediatric effectiveness not established (8.4).

## Caveats
- Half-life "generally" 7 to 10 h is a summary of apparent terminal values after oral dosing; sampling durations
  are not given.
- The label's IV-derived clearance and volume come from studies not described here.
