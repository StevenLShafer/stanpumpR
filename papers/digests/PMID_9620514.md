# Sethna 1998: ketamine-alfentanil combinations after intradermal capsaicin

## Study
Randomized, double-blind, six-session crossover in healthy paid volunteers (PDF p. 1-3). 12 subjects completed (10 men, 2 women, aged 20-26 yr; PDF p. 3); 10 more started but did not finish (3 did not develop >=5 cm2 pinprick hyperalgesia on repeated capsaicin tests, 3 could not do testing on ketamine test doses, 2 found capsaicin pain too severe, 1 urticaria, 1 personal reasons). Capsaicin 250 ug (10 mg/mL solution) injected intradermally into the volar forearm; infusions begin 25 min later. Minimum 24 h between sessions, a new injection site each session. Weight not given beyond "doses adjusted linearly to weight" (Table 1 is for a 70 kg subject).

Six infusions (Table 1, PDF p. 2, mg for a 70 kg subject): alfentanil 2 mg, ketamine 20 mg, combination 1 mg alfentanil + 10 mg ketamine (large-dose triad); alfentanil 0.5 mg, ketamine 5 mg, combination 0.25 mg + 2.5 mg (small-dose triad). The two triads differ by a factor of 4; each combination is half the dose of each component. All doses given over 35 min: 14% as a loading bolus over the first 3 min, then 86% over the next 32 min by pump (PDF p. 3). (derived: for the 2 mg alfentanil arm, bolus 0.28 mg in 3 min then 1.72 mg over 32 min = 53.8 ug/min, about 0.77 ug/kg/min at 70 kg; for 20 mg ketamine, 2.8 mg then 17.2 mg over 32 min = 0.54 mg/min, about 7.7 ug/kg/min.) Large single doses were taken from an earlier study (Park 1995, ref 17) in which they cut pain/hyperalgesia 50-75%; the doses there were titrated to side effects (mean ketamine 32 mg, alfentanil 3.1 mg) and were reduced by about one third here.

Outcomes: 100-mm VAS background pain, area and magnitude of pinprick hyperalgesia (PPH), mechanical allodynia, Digit Symbol Substitution Test (DSST), Perception Speed Test (PST), side effects. Primary outcome = mean of the 30, 40, 50, 60 min observations as % of the pre-infusion baseline (mean of 10 and 20 min). Tests of synergy: paired t-tests of combination versus each component (Plummer-Laska / Laska design). No drug assay.

## Model and parameters
No pharmacokinetic or pharmacodynamic model, no concentrations.

```yaml
parameters: []   # no PK/PD parameters; dose-response only (Table 1 doses, Table 2 p-values)
```
Dose-response significance (Table 2, PDF p. 5, one-tailed paired t-test): alfentanil DSST 0.002, PST 0.003, pain 0.36, PPH area 0.06, PPH magnitude 0.16; ketamine DSST 0.02, PST 0.16, pain 0.47, PPH area 0.36, PPH magnitude 0.25; combination DSST 0.02, PST 0.014, pain: footnote (smaller dose apparently had greater effect), PPH area 0.17, magnitude 0.27. Only the cognitive tests (except ketamine PST) showed significant dose-response.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- Opioid audit (alfentanil): no MEAC, no minimum effective or analgesic concentration, no plasma or effect-site concentration of any kind, no ke0, t1/2 ke0, tPeak or hysteresis. Nothing in the paper to compare with the library alfentanil MEAC.
- Potency/equianalgesic ratio: none measured. The design ratio is ketamine : alfentanil = 10 : 1 by mg (20:2, 5:0.5, 10:1, 2.5:0.25; Table 1). The abstract calls the single-drug doses equianalgesic, but the basis is the earlier study's 50-75% reductions, not a computed ratio, and here the dose-response relations for pain were not significant (Table 2).
- Result: effects of the combination were approximately additive (combination near the mean of its two components, Figure 3, PDF p. 5); no synergy shown on pain, PPH or cognition. Large-dose figure values are only on the graph (not tabulated); not recorded. The authors say synergy cannot be excluded because variances were large (a significant synergy test would have needed about 20 percentage points more PPH-area reduction and about 40 more for PPH magnitude and background pain).
- Mechanical allodynia was too variable to analyse (no subject had >=5 cm2 in all six sessions; six had essentially none).
- Side effects (Table 3, PDF p. 6; n = 12 each): sense of intoxication (not able to drive) 9-11 per arm; dizziness 9-12; blurred vision 2-6; itching 1-4; nausea 1-4; "unreal/spacey" 0-2. Highest dizziness (12) with alfentanil 2 mg.

## Covariates
Dose scaled linearly to weight only. No covariate analysis.

## Caveats
Small n (12 completers of 22), young healthy mostly male volunteers; capsaicin pain variable; skin temperature not controlled (authors later controlled it at 36 C); infusion given after capsaicin, not pre-emptively; only two dose levels per drug; effect measured during infusion, with no concentration data, so nothing here says what plasma or effect-site concentration produced any effect.
