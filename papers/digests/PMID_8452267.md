# Crankshaw 1993: preprogrammed alfentanil infusion by plasma drug efflux

## Study
- 33 adult coronary-artery-surgery patients, either sex, studied between intubation and cardiopulmonary bypass (about 1 h). Premedication papaveretum 15-20 mg and hyoscine 0.3-0.4 mg IM; induction fentanyl 200-500 mcg, diazepam 5-10 mg, thiopental 50-150 mg, vecuronium; N2O 65% in O2 with intermittent fentanyl (PDF p2).
- Goal: a variable-rate infusion that rapidly reaches and holds a target arterial plasma alfentanil concentration CT = 100 mcg/L, using the "Plasma Drug Efflux" method (no compartmental model).
- Method: Ep = R / CM, the infusion rate (mcg/min/kg LBM) divided by the arterial concentration measured 1 min later (a 1 min vein-to-artery transit correction), at each sampling time; Ep averaged over the group and linearly interpolated at 1-min steps; later groups were infused at Ep x CT x LBM. Iterated through 5 groups (4, 4, 5, 8, 12 patients).
- Group 1: 10 mcg/min/kgLBM for 1 min then 1 mcg/min/kgLBM for up to 60 min (from Geerts 1987).
- Dosing weight: lean body mass by James 1976: males 1.10 TBW - 128 (TBW/Ht)^2; females 1.07 TBW - 148 (TBW/Ht)^2 (TBW kg, Ht cm).
- Sampling: radial artery at 1, 2, 3, 5, 7, 10, 15, 20, 30, 40, 50, 60, 70 min; HPLC-UV assay of total plasma alfentanil, precision 7.0% at 5 mcg/L and 6.3% at 200 mcg/L.
- Stopping rule: Wilcoxon matched pairs (bias) and Kruskal-Wallis (skew) both non-significant.

Performance (Table 1, PDF p3): MDPE / MDAPE by group: 1: 17 / 33%; 2: 8 / 17%; 3: 25 / 30%; 4: -8 / 18%; 5: 3 / 10% (points 42, 38, 55, 99, 129). Final group (Table 2): n = 12, 11 men and 1 woman, age 60.9 +/- 7.9 y, height 167.2 +/- 7.6 cm, weight 75.8 +/- 11.1 kg, LBM 55.7 +/- 5.4 kg, fat 25.6 +/- 8.5%, total dose 3.05 +/- 0.84 mg over 40-70 min; per-patient mean concentrations 93-114 mcg/L.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Opioid audit: no MEAC, ke0, t1/2 ke0, tPeak or equianalgesic ratio is measured or reported. The 100 mcg/L target is a chosen plasma target, with the paper pointing to Ausems 1986 for "effective plasma concentrations" without restating them; end point of that target not defined here. Concentrations are total arterial plasma (HPLC), not free and not blood.

## Covariates
Lean body mass (James) used as the only dose scalar; gender enters only through the LBM formula. In three patients with estimated fat above 34% concentrations were within 5% of CT, which the authors take to support LBM dosing (dosing on total weight would have given 20-30% higher concentrations, by the authors' estimate).

## Caveats
- Small, non-random, cardiac-surgery cohort, 11 of 12 final patients male, single target, about 60-70 min, with fentanyl, diazepam, thiopental and N2O on board; the authors say the scheme should not be extrapolated beyond that CT, group and duration.
- Group 3 bias was worse (25%) than group 2 (8%); convergence was judged by non-parametric tests.
- The table unit is ambiguous (open question).
