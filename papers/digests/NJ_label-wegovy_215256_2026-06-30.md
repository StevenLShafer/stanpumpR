# Wegovy label (NDA 215256, revised 6/2026): semaglutide injection and tablets

Combined label for WEGOVY injection (single-dose pens 0.25, 0.5, 1, 1.7, 2.4 and 7.2 mg (HD); single-dose syringes to 2.4 mg; FlexTouch 9.6 mg/3 mL
delivering four 2.4 mg doses) and WEGOVY tablets 1.5, 4, 9, 25 mg (Section 3). Indications: CV risk reduction, weight reduction (adults; injection also
age 12 and over), MASH with F2-F3 fibrosis (injection, accelerated approval). Molecular formula C187H291N45O59, molecular weight 4113.58 g/mol (Section 11).

## Study
Not a study. Dosing (Section 2):
- Injection, SC once weekly, abdomen, thigh or upper arm, any time of day (2.1). Table 1 (about PDF pp. 7-8, page not confirmed): 0.25 mg weeks 1-4, 0.5 mg weeks 5-8, 1 mg weeks 9-12,
  1.7 mg weeks 13-16, then maintenance: 2.4 mg (recommended) or 1.7 mg; up to 7.2 mg weekly in adults for weight reduction after at least 4 weeks on 2.4 mg (2.2).
- Tablets, once daily fasting with up to 4 ounces of water, wait at least 30 minutes before food, drink or other oral drugs (2.1). Table 2 (PDF p. 8, text
  and structure read from text layer): 1.5 mg days 1-30, 4 mg days 31-60, 9 mg days 61-90, 25 mg from day 91 (maintenance).
- Switching (2.5): injection 2.4 mg to tablets 25 mg one week after the last injection; tablets 25 mg to injection 2.4 mg the day after the last tablet.

Water and fasting (12.3, PDF pp. 30-31): single tablet doses gave higher AUC and Cmax with 50 mL than 240 mL; over 10 days, a longer post-dose fast
(15, 30, 60, 120 min) raised absorption, and 50 vs 120 mL did not differ.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
12.2 (PDF pp. 29-30): lowers body weight (more fat than lean mass), reduces calorie intake, glucose-dependent insulin and glucagon effects, delays gastric
emptying; no QTc prolongation at SC doses up to 1.5 mg at steady state; decreases liver fat (MRI-PDFF), stiffness, ELF and Pro-C3. No concentration-effect
value, EC50, ke0, t1/2ke0, tPeak or MEAC is reported. 8.6 says some T2D patients on the tablets may have subtherapeutic concentrations (no threshold given).
Not an opioid.

## Covariates
12.3 (PDF p. 31): no clinically significant PK differences by age (<65, 65-74, 75 and over), sex, race, ethnicity, body weight, upper GI disease, eGFR 30 to <90,
Child-Pugh A-C, or MASH fibrosis F2-F3. SC exposure no different in MASH than in obesity. Injection site: similar exposure. T2D: lower Css (68 vs 75 nmol/L
SC; 44 vs 77 nmol/L oral). Drug interactions (tablets): levothyroxine total T4 AUC +33%, Cmax unchanged; no meaningful effect with omeprazole, lisinopril, warfarin,
metformin, digoxin, ethinyl estradiol, levonorgestrel, furosemide or rosuvastatin.

## Caveats
- All label PK values are "approximate" or "average" with no SD, no statement of arithmetic vs geometric mean and no model named.
- "No clinically significant difference" by body weight is a clinical-relevance statement; the Carlsson Petri model the library uses has clearance
  proportional to (weight/85)^0.774. The two are not contradictory, but the label does not quantify the weight effect.
- Oral values are for the Wegovy tablet, which the label says is co-formulated with SNAC; its strengths (1.5/4/9/25 mg) differ from Rybelsus.

## Verification against stanpumpR
Compared as an independent check (the repo does not cite this label):
- **SC model** (`R/drugs_semaglutide.R:121-128`: CL/F 0.0478 L/h x (weight/85)^0.774, V/F 12.2 L, ka 0.0286/h, F = 1 apparent; population: type 2 diabetes,
  0.5 and 1 mg). Label: CL/F about 0.05 L/h, V about 12.5 L, t1/2 about 1 week, in overweight or obesity (PDF p. 31). Model half-life 0.693 x 12.2 / 0.0478
  = 177 h (derived; the file header states the same). **Agrees within 5%.** Weekly 2.4 mg Cavg for the 85 kg reference woman: 2.4 mg / (0.0478 x 168 h)
  = 0.299 mg/L = 72.7 nmol/L (derived; 1 nmol/L = 4.11358 ng/mL); label 75 (obesity) and 68 (T2D) nmol/L, so **matches** at the label's rounding. At 7.2 mg the
  same arithmetic gives 218 nmol/L against 230 (5% lower). Direction: the model's weight-dependent clearance means a patient heavier than 85 kg is predicted
  lower than the label's average; the label gives no body-weight effect to compare, and the label's mean weight is not stated.
- The model is built for 0.5-1 mg in T2D and the registry says Wegovy dosing is out of scope; the label's agreement is evidence the extrapolation is reasonable,
  not a validation.
- **Oral model** (`R/drugs_semaglutideOral.R:106-134`: CL 0.039 L/h, F 0.471% for an 85 kg woman, from Rybelsus PIONEER data to 14 mg). The label's Wegovy tablets
  show F 1-2% and Css 44 (T2D) or 77 (no T2D) nmol/L at 25 mg. Derived model Cavg 30.6 nmol/L at 25 mg for the 85 kg woman: **lower than both label values**,
  as expected if the library's F is an outpatient estimate and the product differs (see open_questions). Not a transcription discrepancy.
- Units: label V and CL are absolute (L, L/h), not per kg; concentrations in nmol/L; molecular weight 4113.58 g/mol agrees with the 4113.58 in `drugs_semaglutide.R:5-6`
  and `drugs_semaglutideOral.R:6`.
