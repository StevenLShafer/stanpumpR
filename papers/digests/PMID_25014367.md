# Brvar 2014: tramadol population PK with inverse-Gaussian oral input (IR and PR)

## Study
- Pooled reference-arm data from two phase I bioequivalence studies (Krka); 52 healthy Caucasian non-smoking men,
  age 18-42 y, BMI 19-29 kg/m2, 26 per study (p. 171).
- Study I: single dose, Tramal 50 mg IR capsule (tramadol hydrochloride), fasted; 17 samples 0.5-36 h; LC-MS/MS,
  LLOQ 4 ng/mL (ULOQ 256).
- Study II: Tramal 200 mg PR tablet (hydrochloride) every 12 h x 5 doses; samples to 48 h after the day-3 dose;
  GC-MS validated 10-800 ng/mL.
- NONMEM 7.3, FOCE-I; BLQ handled by M3 (Laplacian); bootstrap n = 1000 (stratified by formulation).
- Only tramadol measured (no M1). No IV arm, so all disposition parameters are apparent (divided by F).

## Model and parameters
Two-compartment disposition, first-order elimination; final input = inverse Gaussian function directly into the central
compartment (MIT, CV2I), separate for IR and PR, with relative bioavailability Fr. Allometric weight scaling, exponent
0.75 on clearances and 1 on volumes, centred on 70 kg. CYP2D6 mixture models (2 or 3 subpopulations) did not improve
the fit.

Input function (Table 1 footnote): fi(t) = dose x sqrt(MIT / (2 pi CV2I t^3)) x exp(-(t - MIT)^2 / (2 CV2I MIT t)).

Intermediate models (Results, p. 173): first-order ka in the dissolution + absorption model was 6.29 /h (RSE 40.4 %)
with MDT 0.512 h; direct inverse-Gaussian input gave MIT 0.692 h at that stage (before pooling). AIC: lag 2115,
inverse Gaussian 2077, transit 2088, direct IG 2075. Mixture CL values: 26.9 / 35.4 L/h (5.1 / 94.9 %) for two groups;
27.6 / 35.3 / 44.5 L/h (30.9 / 35.2 / 33.9 %) for three; neither improved the fit.

Not reported: tmax of the typical IR profile (derived below), absolute F (literature ~70 %, range 64-86 %, cited in
the Introduction), any M1 data.

Steady-state simulation PR/IR geometric mean ratios: AUCss 0.98, Cmax,ss 0.91, Cmin,ss 0.93.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. Opioid audit: no MEAC, no ke0 / t½ke0 / tPeak, no potency ratio.

## Covariates
Body weight (allometric, fixed exponents 0.75 / 1, 70 kg). CYP2D6 mixture tested, not retained. No genotyping.

## Caveats
- Apparent parameters only (no IV data); F not estimable.
- Doses are tramadol hydrochloride; no salt-to-base conversion stated.
- Two assays (LC-MS/MS and GC-MS) in different labs; one residual error sufficed.
- Healthy fasted young men; n = 26 per formulation, different subjects per formulation (so no IIV on Fr).
- Most samples at 36 h (IR) and 48 h (PR) were BLQ.
