# Boosman 2022: age and the population PK of gemcitabine and dFdU

## Study
- Pooled data from a prospective observational study (80 patients aged 70 or more, planned for gemcitabine; Amsterdam and Nijmegen hospitals, 2012-2021) plus four earlier trials at the Netherlands Cancer Institute (PDF p.2).
- 197 patients in total, 83 aged 70 or more (42%). 789 gemcitabine and 789 dFdU concentrations; 41 gemcitabine values below LLOQ were dropped, leaving 748 (p.4).
- Table 1 (PDF p.4, text layer only): median age 66 (27-85) y; weight 74 (46-121) kg; BSA 1.87 (1.47-2.44) m2; CrCl 76.7 (32.3-205.4) mL/min; 51.3% male; dose 974 (297-1318) mg/m2. Younger group (n = 114) had median dose 735 mg/m2, older group (n = 83) 1000 mg/m2. Samples per patient: younger median 5, older median 1.
- Infusion up to 1 h; samples after infusions longer than 1 h were excluded. Flexible limited sampling (first sample at end of infusion, 1 to 10 samples). Tetrahydrouridine added within 5 min to block ex vivo deamination. LC-MS/MS; LLOQ 0.5 ng/mL gemcitabine, 5.0 ng/mL dFdU.
- NONMEM 7.5; R 4.1.0; Pirana; pcVPC (n = 1000) and sampling importance resampling for uncertainty. Significance P < 0.01 (OFV drop 6.63). Exponential IIV; combined proportional plus additive error written in Methods, but Table 2 reports proportional only.

## Model and parameters
Two compartments with linear clearance from the central compartment for both gemcitabine and dFdU. The dFdU parameters are "apparent", divided by the unknown converted fraction fm. Allometric scaling on total body weight to 70 kg: exponent 1.0 on V, 0.75 on CL and Q. Table 2 (PDF p.6, printed p.702), covariate model with age; (RSE %):

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD end point. The paper compares exposure (simulated concentration-time curves, Figure 3) between age groups and cites other work on haematological toxicity. No effect site, ke0 or concentration target. Not an opioid paper; no MEAC, potency or hysteresis to record.

## Covariates
- Tested, from earlier analyses: allometric scaling on total body weight; creatinine clearance (Cockcroft-Gault, standardised to 70 kg and 100 mL/min) on renal dFdU clearance; gender on CL dFdU and Vc dFdU; NSCLC on Vc dFdU. Missing weight, CrCl: median imputed with IIV added (n = 15 or 16, 8%).
- **Age** (continuous, standardised to the median, and categorical < 70 vs >= 70): no statistically significant effect. Categorical age on Vc gemcitabine: +20% (95% CI -3.5 to +46%, P = 0.117). The authors judge the exposure difference clinically irrelevant (Figure 3, p.6-7).
- In the base model the age effect on Vc was significant; it vanished once weight scaling and other covariates were added (the older group was heavier, p.5).

## Caveats
- Older patients had 1 sample per patient (median) against 5 in younger patients; late distribution phase poorly covered for the older group.
- Younger patients all came from combination-therapy trials and received lower gemcitabine doses (median 735 vs 1000 mg/m2); age is confounded with regimen and tumour type (Table 1).
- Fit patients only (selection bias, acknowledged in Discussion).
- Allometry is on total body weight, not lean mass.
- Gemcitabine IIV shrinkage for Vp is 67%.

## Verification against stanpumpR
No citation to check. As an independent comparison with `R/drugs_gemcitabine.R` (Tham 2008, per 70 kg and 1.76 m, lean-mass scaling, lines 71-77):
- **CL gemcitabine:** 220 L/h here against 305.2 L/h in Tham (CLG = 70.2 / 0.23; line 98). The library's value is about 39% higher than this paper's. The two weight bases differ (total body weight here; lean body mass over a 56.1 kg reference in the library), so the numbers are not strictly like for like.
- **V1:** 43.9 L here against 2.96 L. **V2/Vp:** 75.1 L against 47.6 L. **Q:** 7.14 L/h against 156 L/h. The central distribution differs by an order of magnitude or more; the library's own header calls Tham's V1 essentially unidentified. Differences here run in the direction of a slower, larger distribution, so early plasma peaks after a 30-min infusion will differ between the two models.
- **dFdU:** this paper is two-compartment (Vc 28.2 + Vp 58.4 = 86.6 L per fm; non-renal CL 7.02 L/h per fm). The library's dFdU is one compartment, V 52.6 L, CL 21.5 L/h (Tham, lines 75-76, 148-158). With fm unknown, a direct comparison is not possible (open questions).
- The library holds no age or sex covariate beyond lean-mass scaling; this paper finds no age effect, which supports that choice.
