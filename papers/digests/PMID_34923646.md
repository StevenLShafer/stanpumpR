# Ohk 2022: sex differences in oral sumatriptan PK (Korean volunteers)

## Study
38 healthy Korean volunteers (29 male, 9 female) from two studies with the same protocol (one previously published as Lee 2015). Single oral dose 50 mg sumatriptan tablet with 150 mL water, fasting. Plasma sampled pre-dose and at 0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 1, 1.5, 2, 2.5, 3, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12 h; UPLC-MS/MS, calibration 0.5-50 ng/mL (total plasma). 532 observations. Table 1 (PDF p4): age 24.8 +/- 2.7 y (21-31), height 171.1 +/- 7.8 cm, weight 64.0 +/- 9.8 kg (50-84), BMI 21.8 +/- 2.2, CrCL 119.2 +/- 17.9 mL/min (Cockcroft-Gault). NONMEM 7.3, FOCE-I, exponential BSV, combined error; bootstrap (1000) and VPC (1000). NCA with WinNonlin 7.1.

## Model and parameters
One-compartment, first-order elimination, absorption by a transit-compartment chain (Savic) combined with a first-order depot absorption with lag time. Parameters are apparent (oral, divided by F), absolute not per kg. Table 4 (PDF p7, printed p29, read on the image):

Population structural-model CL/F before the sex covariate: 374 L/h (p29, text). NCA (Table 2, PDF p4, geometric mean unless stated; text layer): total AUC0-inf 131.2 ng.h/mL, Cmax 31.6 ng/mL, Tmax median 1.5 h (0.5-4.0), t1/2 2.8 h (arithmetic), CL/F 390.7 L/h (6.1 L/h/kg). Males vs females: Cmax 29.0 vs 41.4, AUC0-inf 117.1 vs 188.9, t1/2 2.9 vs 2.6 h, CL/F 427.8 vs 271.2 L/h (6.4 vs 5.1 L/h/kg).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD end point, ke0, EC50 or concentration target measured (not an opioid; no MEAC). The authors say sex-related PD could not be assessed.

## Covariates
Tested on CL/F and V/F: age, height, weight, BMI, CrCL, sex (Table 3, PDF p7). dOFV vs structural: weight on CL -10.541 (p < 0.005), sex on CL -26.174 (p < 0.001); sex + weight added to sex model: only -0.001 to -0.011 for height/BMI/CrCL; sex added to the weight model -15.634. Final model: sex on CL/F only, CL = (1 - SEX) x 444 + SEX x 281 L/h (SEX 0 = male, 1 = female). Sex-on-CL/F is 1.58-fold higher in males (444/281). The comparator is the female subgroup of this study.

## Caveats
n = 38, only 9 women, healthy volunteers (not migraineurs), single dose, Korean subjects, one tablet formulation (Sumatran 50 mg); F unknown (CL/F, V/F apparent). Large IIV on V/F (99.9%) and poorly determined NN (RSE 48%). Weight effect on CL not carried into the final model. Concentrations are total plasma (plasma binding 14-21% per the label, cited).
