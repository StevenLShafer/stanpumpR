# Zydus bumetanide tablets US label (DailyMed), 2023

## Study
This is the generic Bumetanide Tablets USP label from Zydus (ANDA 202900; marketing start 05/01/2018; revised
12/2023). It is the oral bumetanide tablet the registry names, so there is no source file problem.
- **Strengths:** 0.5, 1 and 2 mg tablets.
- **Indication:** edema of heart failure and of hepatic and renal disease.
- **Dosage (p5):**
  - 0.5-2 mg daily, usually as one dose.
  - Repeat at 4-5 h intervals if needed, up to 10 mg/day. Intermittent schedules are recommended.
  - Substitute for furosemide at about 1:40.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- **Time course (p1):** oral onset of diuresis in 30-60 min, peak activity at 1-2 h, largely complete in 4 h at
  1-2 mg.
- **Potency:** 1 mg ≈ 40 mg furosemide. The ototoxicity warning (p3) says 40-60 times.

## Covariates
Age (elderly, neonates and infants). Renal and hepatic impairment are qualitative only.

## Caveats
- A generic label with no Tmax, F, adult CL or V.
