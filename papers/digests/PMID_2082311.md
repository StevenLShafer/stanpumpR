# Mroszczak 1990: ketorolac absorption, kinetics and metabolism (Syntex summary)

## Study
A Syntex review of its own studies (racemic ketorolac tromethamine; plasma assayed as ketorolac):
- Study 1: 4 volunteers (2 M, 2 F), 14C-ketorolac 1.7 mg/kg oral and IV solution crossover; 8 men 1.3 mg/kg IM.
- Study 2 (Jung 1988): 15 volunteers, 10 mg IV, 10 mg tablet, 10 mg IM crossover.
- Study 3 (Jung 1989): 12 men, 30 mg oral solution, 30, 60, 90 mg IM.
- Unpublished (Syntex data on file): 12 men, 10 mg tablet, NONLIN two-compartment fit of mean plasma levels (Table 2, Fig. 2).
- Food/antacid study: 12 volunteers (6 M, 6 F), 10 mg capsule fasting, with Maalox, or 1 h after a high-fat breakfast.
- Animal kinetics (monkey, rabbit, rat, mouse), mouse tissue distribution, protein binding, metabolism.

Table 1 (mean, SD): study 1 IV AUC 35.6, oral 28.8 mcg.h/mL, F oral 80.5% (urinary recovery suggests 99.4% absorbed), IM F 108%; t1/2 5.0-6.0 h. Study 2: Cmax oral/IM 0.77-0.81 mcg/mL, Tmax 46-53 min, t1/2 5.0-5.1 h, F complete. Study 3: F complete for oral and IM; t1/2 5.2-5.6 h; IM Cmax 2.24, 4.48, 6.88 mcg/mL at 30, 60, 90 mg; AUC linear in dose.
Food study: Tmax 30.0 (fasting), 33.3 (Maalox), 91.7 min (high-fat meal); Cmax 1.14, 1.13, 0.57 mcg/mL; AUC 4.92, 4.39, 4.44 (not different).
Derived (not in paper): ln 2 / 11.0 h^-1 = 3.78 min, matching the 3.8 min.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None (kinetics and metabolism). Metabolite p-hydroxyketorolac is inactive (less than 1% of analgesic activity in animals), plasma levels 1-2% of ketorolac. Protein binding 99.2% (human). No analgesic concentration, no ke0.

## Covariates
None analysed. Food delays (does not reduce) absorption.

## Caveats
- A sponsor review; the 3.8 min absorption half-life comes from an unpublished, mean-data NONLIN fit (12 men, 10 mg tablet) that includes a 0.2 h lag.
- Racemate only; no enantiomer data.
- Several table entries scrambled in the text layer; reassembled values agree with the narrative.

## Verification against stanpumpR
- ka = ln 2 / 3.8 min = 10.9 /h (R/drugs_ketorolac.R:46-47, 93): text says 3.8 min, Table 2 ka 11.0 /h. **matches**; per-minute conversion correct.
- Complete oral bioavailability (R/drugs_ketorolac.R:44-45; help :20): studies 2 and 3 report complete F (study 1, 81%, judged less reliable). **matches**.
- "no lag" (R/drugs_ketorolac.R:47, :106 tlag_PO = 0): the 3.8 min half-life was estimated together with a 0.2 h (12 min) absorption lag in the same fit. Taking the rate without its lag moves the peak about 12 min earlier than the source model; the source's own observed Tmax for this tablet was 35 min (Table 2). The repo's note that "no single first-order rate reproduces both" peak time and height does not consider restoring the source's lag. **discrepancy** (partial transcription of the absorption model).
- "food delays absorption" (help :20; R/drugs_ketorolac.R:49): food study Tmax 91.7 vs 30 min, AUC unchanged. **matches**.
