# Courlet 2021: rosuvastatin PK/PD in people living with HIV

## Study
- Population PK/PD, NONMEM 7.4.2 (FOCEI, ADVAN4 TRANS4), Swiss HIV Cohort Study, two sites (Lausanne, Basel).
- 65 adults living with HIV on rosuvastatin 5-20 mg once daily (median 10 mg). 6 richly sampled (65 concentrations,
  median 11 samples each) plus 62 sparsely sampled at routine visits (89 concentrations, 1-3 each); 154 concentrations
  in all, 0.2-38.5 h after the last dose (PDF p4/printed 383).
- Table 1 (PDF p4, printed 381-382): median age 55 y (IQR 49-64), 8 women (12%), weight 75 kg (66-85, 2 missing),
  creatinine clearance 87 mL/min/1.73 m^2 (79-119). Comedication by concentration (n=154): ritonavir-boosted darunavir
  70 (46%), cobicistat-boosted darunavir 4, etravirine 38, efavirenz 21, dolutegravir 47, raltegravir 38.
- Assay: plasma (EDTA), UHPLC-MS/MS, total rosuvastatin, stored at -80 C. Log-transformed concentrations fitted;
  additive error on the log scale.
- PD part: 403 non-HDL-cholesterol values (253 before, 150 after starting rosuvastatin).

## Model and parameters
Two-compartment, sequential zero- then first-order absorption (Fig. 1), apparent (oral) parameters, no lag time
(delta OFV -0.2). ka and D1 were estimated from the rich data alone and then fixed. Table 2 (PDF p7, printed 385;
confirmed on the rendered page), final model estimates:

- Derived (mine): Vc+Vp = 1754 L; terminal half-life from the Table 2 rates = about 25.6 h; steady-state
  AUC0-24 = dose/CL: 20 mg -> 164 ng.h/mL, matching the paper's simulated median 164 (p5/printed 384); 5 mg -> 41
  and 10 mg -> 82 also match the paper's medians.
- Simulation (p5/384): boosted PIs raised Cmax 29%, lowered trough 6%, no change in AUC0-24.
- Literature range of Vd quoted by the authors: 1255-4870 L (p9/386).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- Indirect-effect model: inhibition of non-HDL-cholesterol production, d(nonHDL)/dt = kin(1 - Ct/(IC50+Ct)) - kout
  nonHDL, kout = kin/baseline (PDF p3/printed 381). Ct is the model-predicted plasma rosuvastatin concentration
  (total, plasma, ng/mL). End point: non-HDL-cholesterol (mmol/L).
- IC50 15.8 ng/mL (Table 2); no Emax or gamma (maximum inhibition is 100%); no ke0 or hysteresis separate from the
  indirect-response turnover (kout = kin/baseline = 0.02/3.6 = 0.0056 /h, derived).
- Baseline covariates: ARVs with negative lipid effect +12% (theta 0.12, RSE 65%, CI includes 0); etravirine -14%;
  age: baseline 3.6 x (1 - 0.81 x (age-60)/60) (Table 2 footnote).
- Target 2.8 mmol/L non-HDL-C reached by 31% (5 mg), 44% (10 mg), 64% (20 mg), 84% (40 mg) of simulated patients (p5/384).
- Not an opioid; no MEAC or potency ratio.

## Covariates
Tested on PK: sex, age, weight, creatinine clearance, ALT, AST, boosted PIs. None on CL (delta OFV >= -2.89). Univariate
on Vc: boosted PIs -65% (delta OFV -3.88, p=0.05) and women three-fold (delta OFV -6.12), neither kept in the
multivariate step (only 2 of the 22 boosted-PI patients were women, p5/384). Authors say the data could rule out a CL decrease of
more than 22% with boosted PIs. The final PK model has no covariate.

## Caveats
Authors: small numbers for each ARV, no transporter genotypes (OATP1B1, BCRP), high unexplained between-subject
variability (Vc IIV CI 2-451%). Absorption parameters were fixed from six rich profiles, and the Vc IIV absorbs the
absorption variability. Mostly male, mostly white; creatinine clearance range narrow. Half of the samples were on
boosted darunavir, which raises rosuvastatin Cmax in other studies. Sparse data have 1-3 samples per patient.
