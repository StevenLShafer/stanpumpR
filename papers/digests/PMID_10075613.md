# Levey 1999: the original MDRD Study prediction equations

## Study
Cross-sectional analysis of the MDRD Study baseline cohort: 1628 patients with chronic renal disease (training sample
1070, validation 558). Mean age 50.6 ± 12.7 y, 60% male, 88% white, 6% diabetic; mean weight 79.6 kg, BSA 1.91 m^2;
mean GFR 39.8 ± 21.2 mL/min/1.73 m^2; mean serum creatinine 2.3 mg/dL (203 µmol/L). GFR = renal clearance of
125I-iothalamate, indexed to 1.73 m^2. Creatinine by kinetic alkaline picrate (Jaffe; not IDMS-standardized). Stepwise
regression on log GFR; coefficients refitted on all 1628.

## Model and parameters
Table 3 (GFR mL/min/1.73 m^2; Pcr, SUN mg/dL; Alb g/dL; UUN g/d):
- Equation 6: GFR = 198 × Pcr^-0.858 × Age^-0.167 × 0.822 [female] × 1.178 [black] × SUN^-0.293 × UUN^0.249.
- Equation 7 (the recommended "MDRD Study equation"): GFR = 170 × Pcr^-0.999 × Age^-0.176 × 0.762 [female] × 1.180
  [black] × SUN^-0.170 × Alb^0.318.
- Comparators: Cockcroft-Gault overestimated GFR by 16% (bias factor 0.84); measured creatinine clearance by 19%
  (factor 0.81).

## Covariates
Serum creatinine, age, sex, ethnicity, SUN, albumin (eq 7); urine urea nitrogen (eq 6).

## Caveats
Developed only in CKD patients (no normal GFR, no persons > 70 y, no children, no type 1 diabetes, no transplant
recipients); validation sample from the same population; non-standardized Jaffe creatinine.
