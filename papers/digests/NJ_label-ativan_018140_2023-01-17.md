# Ativan injection label 2023: intravenous and intramuscular lorazepam

## Study
Not a study: US prescribing information for lorazepam injection (status epilepticus, preanesthetic
sedation). Summary PK, with small special-population studies described by n. No assay given.

Hepatic 3-O-phenolic glucuronidation; enterohepatic recirculation; glucuronide inactive, renally cleared.
Unbound drug crosses the blood-brain barrier (confirmed by CSF sampling).

Special populations (pp. 4-5): neonates with asphyxia (0.05 or 0.1 mg/kg IV, n=4/6): CL/kg 80% lower,
t½ 3-fold longer, V 40% lower. Children 2-12 y with ALL in remission (n=37): V/kg 50% higher, t½ 30%
longer, unbound CL/kg comparable to adults. Adolescents 12-18 y (n=13): V/kg 50% higher, t½ two-fold.
Elderly 60-84 y (n=15 vs 15 aged 19-38): total CL 20% lower. Sex: no effect. Race: young Americans
(n=15) and Japanese (n=7) CL 1.0 mL/min/kg; elderly Japanese 0.59 vs elderly Americans 0.77 mL/min/kg.
Renal impairment (CLcr 22 ± 9 mL/min, n=6; dialysis n=4): V and t½ +40% / +25% (+75% both on dialysis),
total CL unchanged; 8% removed as lorazepam in a 6 h dialysis; glucuronide t½ +55% / +125%, metabolic
clearance −75% / −90%. Cirrhosis (n=13 vs 11, 2 mg IV): no substantive difference. Smoking: no difference.

Dosing (Dosage and Administration): status epilepticus 4 mg IV at 2 mg/min (≥18 y), repeat 4 mg after
10-15 min if needed. Premedication: IM 0.05 mg/kg up to 4 mg at least 2 h before surgery; IV 2 mg total
or 0.044 mg/kg (0.02 mg/lb) whichever is smaller, up to 0.05 mg/kg / 4 mg total, 15-20 min before surgery.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- No concentration-effect relation, no EC50, no ke0 or tPeak.
- Timing of effect: lack of recall optimal 15-20 min after IV and within 2 h after IM; intended effects
  of 2-4 mg last 6-8 h. Visual tracking impaired for a mean of 8 h after 4 mg IM and 4 h after 2 mg IM
  (n=6). IV up to 3.5 mg/70 kg does not alter CO2 ventilatory response. 8-10 mg IV produces loss of lid
  reflexes within 15 min.
- Status epilepticus (Clinical Studies; responder = seizures stopped within 10 min and seizure-free a
  further 30 min): dose-comparison trial (n=119) 61% at 1 mg, 57% at 2 mg, 76% at 4 mg (4 vs 1 mg p=0.08);
  active-control trial (n=58) lorazepam 2+2 mg 80% vs diazepam 5+5 mg 57% (p=0.04).

## Covariates
Qualitative/percentage effects only (above). No equations.

## Caveats
- "Initial concentration" after 4 mg IV is undefined in time; the label does not say when it was sampled.
- The 1.1 mL/min/kg clearance and 1.3 L/kg volume have no stated n or population.
- Paediatric data come from children with leukaemia in remission and asphyxiated neonates.
