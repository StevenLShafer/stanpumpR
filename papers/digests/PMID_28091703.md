# Krumpl 2017: bolus landiolol vs esmolol in healthy Caucasians

## Study
- Single-centre, double-blind, randomised, three-period crossover; 12 healthy volunteers. Table 1 (PDF p3): 5 women,
  7 men; 24.5 +/- 6.9 y, 71.2 +/- 11.0 kg, 173.9 +/- 11.0 cm, BMI 23.6. (The abstract says 7 women and 5 men; Table 1
  and the Results text say 5 women and 7 men.)
- Esmolol (Brevibloc 100 mg/10 mL) 0.5, 1.0 and 1.5 mg/kg, injected at 0.5 mg/kg per 15 s (abstract), so 15, 30 and
  45 s (derived). Within a period the three doses were given in ascending order at 0, 60 and 120 min (Fig. 3 caption,
  PDF p6); no parent drug was quantifiable before the second and third doses. Landiolol 0.1, 0.2, 0.3 mg/kg in other
  periods.
- Whole-blood ("blood") esmolol; Fig. 3's axis says plasma. Assay and sampling times are in the online supplement
  (not archived).
- Non-compartmental PK only.

**Supplement (PMID_28091703_ESM1.docx, read 2026-10-27).** Methods: esmolol hydrochloride (Brevibloc, racemic)
10 mg/mL as boluses of 0.5 mg/kg (15 s), 1.0 mg/kg (30 s) and 1.5 mg/kg (45 s), three ascending doses 1 h apart in
one period; supine from 20 min before the first bolus to 4 h after the last. Blood (whole blood, 0.5 mL into
ethanol within 25-30 s) at 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 16, 20 and 30 min after each bolus, plus 60, 120, 180 and
240 min after the last. HR from the ECG, BP and ECG intervals recorded at the same times; continuous ECG from 10 min
before the first dose to 120 min after the last. Whether "after each bolus" means from its start or its end is not
said. Assay: HPLC-MS/MS of landiolol, LM1, LM2, esmolol and EM1 in precipitated human whole blood, GLP, LLOQ
0.00111 ug/mL for esmolol. NCA in Phoenix WinNonlin 6.1/6.4; PD descriptive (time to maximal change, maximal
change, AUE derived, but the ESM prints no PD results). Section 3.3 and Tables 1-2: metabolite NCA. The
metabolites' t1/2 rows are printed in "min" but match ln2/lambda-z in hours (LM1 0.88 with lambda-z 0.79 /h; EM1
3.82 printed vs 2.23 h derived from 0.311 /h, which does not match either unit): a source error in units or
values, cause not stated. The assay range line names "esmolol-M2" where every other line names EM1 (source typo).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: resting heart rate (and systolic BP). Table 4 (PDF p5), esmolol; predose HR 60.8 bpm, SBP 105.3 mmHg:

| | HR 1 min | HR 2 min | lowest HR | duration of significant HR fall | max SBP fall | SBP duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.5 mg/kg | 60.3 (p 0.784) | 59.3 (0.568) | 57.6 (0.025) | 0 min | 7.7 mmHg | 13 min |
| 1.0 mg/kg | 60.0 (0.094) | 56.0 (0.004) | 56.0 (0.008) | 1 min | 8.6 | 9 min |
| 1.5 mg/kg | 61.6 (0.852) | 56.7 (0.033) | 54.4 (0.001) | 2 min | 10.1 | 59 min |

- At 1.0 mg/kg the lowest mean HR equals the 2-min value. At 1.5 mg/kg the lowest (54.4) is below the 2-min value
  (56.7), so it came at another time; Fig. 3 (PDF p6) places it at about 3 min (figure reading). At 0.5 mg/kg the
  lowest (57.6) also differs from the 1- and 2-min values, and no significant HR reduction was found at that dose.
- Text (PDF p6): HR fell earlier with landiolol (1 min) than esmolol (2 min, except the low dose); HR decreases
  overlapped the concentration peaks.
- Potency: landiolol:esmolol bradycardic potency 10:1 to 15:1 (Discussion, PDF p9).
- No ke0, effect-compartment model, EC50 or hysteresis analysis. Fig. 4 plots maximal HR reduction against log dose and
  log Cmax without fitted parameters.

## Covariates
None examined.

## Caveats
Healthy young adults at rest (little sympathetic tone to block); n = 12; doses in fixed ascending order 1 h apart;
very variable esmolol PK; heart-rate sampling times are only in the supplement.
