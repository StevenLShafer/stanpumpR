# Ngan Kee 2009: placental transfer of phenylephrine and ephedrine at cesarean delivery

## Study
Randomised, double-blind trial (Hong Kong; registry CUHK_CCT00079). 104 ASA 1-2 women, term singleton, elective
cesarean under spinal anesthesia (bupivacaine 10 mg hyperbaric + fentanyl 15 ug), 52 per arm (PDF p2). Table 1
(PDF p3): age 32 (4.7) y both arms; weight 70 (15.7) kg phenylephrine, 70 (10.5) ephedrine; height 156 (5.3) /
157 (4.9) cm; induction-to-delivery 27.6 [22.3-30.9] / 27.6 [23.0-31.9] min; uterine incision-to-delivery 88 [60-119]
/ 87 [60-125] s. Infusions: phenylephrine 100 ug/mL or ephedrine 8 mg/mL (80:1 potency ratio from Saravanan 2006),
started at 60 mL/h at intrathecal injection, then on/off by systolic pressure checked every minute until uterine
incision; rescue phenylephrine 100 ug IV bolus for more than two consecutive hypotensive readings (PDF p2). The
phenylephrine salt is not stated. Single samples: maternal radial arterial (MA) at delivery, umbilical arterial (UA)
and venous (UV) from a double-clamped cord segment immediately after delivery. Assay (Appendix 2, PDF p6-7):
HPLC-MS/MS on 0.5 mL plasma, LOD phenylephrine 0.2 ng/mL, linear 0.2-50 ng/mL, intra-assay CV mean 6.39%,
inter-assay 8.88%; ephedrine LOD 0.05 ng/mL, corrected by a mean 2.07% for interference from its metabolite
norephedrine (the internal standard). Mann-Whitney, Wilcoxon, chi-square; SPSS 15.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End points were fetal acid-base and metabolic markers, not a concentration-effect relation. Ephedrine arm: lower UA
pH 7.25 vs 7.33 and UA base excess -4.8 vs -1.9 mmol/L (Table 2, PDF p3); higher UA/UV lactate, glucose,
epinephrine and norepinephrine (Table 3). Haemodynamics (Table 4, PDF p5): hypotension 2 (4%) phenylephrine vs 13
(25%) ephedrine; rescue phenylephrine 1 (2%) vs 11 (22%); bradycardia 6 (12%) vs 0; minimum heart rate 58 vs 70
beats/min; nausea or vomiting 1 (2%) vs 18 (35%). The authors suggest the true phenylephrine:ephedrine potency ratio
is below the 80:1 assumed, since less ephedrine solution was given (PDF p6). No ke0, EC50 or target concentration.

## Covariates
None analysed.

## Caveats
One maternal sample per woman at delivery, after a titrated on/off infusion that ended at uterine incision (median
about 1.5 min before delivery), so there is no steady state and no dosing history per sample; a clearance cannot be
derived. Term healthy women only. The 100 ug median dose (range 0-1200 ug) in the Discussion (PDF p5) is from the
authors' earlier nonelective-cesarean study (their ref 14), not this one.
