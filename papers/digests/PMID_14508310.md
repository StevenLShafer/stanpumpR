# Struys 2003: BIS, AAI and Ce(propofol) vs responsiveness, with and without remifentanil

## Study
- Design: randomized, three parallel groups of 15 (n = 45), staircase target-effect-site propofol (PDF p.3, printed 803). Groups: remifentanil effect-site target 0, 2, 4 ng/ml. Remifentanil Ce target started 4 min before propofol.
- Population: 45 female ASA I patients, aged 18-60, ambulatory gynecologic surgery; weight 70-130 % of ideal. Table 2 (mean ± SD): age 33±5 / 33±5 / 34±4 yr; weight 63±10 / 66±11 / 65±9 kg; height 167±6 / 168±6 / 167±6 cm (groups 0 / 2 / 4 ng/ml).
- Propofol Ce started at 1.5 µg/ml (0 remi group) or 1 µg/ml (other groups), +0.5 µg/ml every 4 min until loss of response to all stimuli. Spontaneous ventilation, no premedication.
- Delivery: RUGLOOP, three-compartment model plus effect compartment; propofol Schnider 1998/1999 with Ce computed for a time to peak effect of 1.6 min; remifentanil Minto 1997 with t1/2ke0 of 1.020619 min (p.3). Steady state defined as plasma-effect equilibration, reached after 4 min per step.
- Measures: BIS (A-2000, v3.4), AAI (A-Line ARX index from MLAEP), calculated Ce propofol (CePROP) and remifentanil (CeREMI); responses OAA/S (0-5), eyelash reflex, 100 Hz / 50 mA 2 s tetanic stimulus. LORverbal = OAA/S 0-2; LORlash; LORnoxious.
- Statistics: prediction probability PK (Smith), probit ED50/ED95, sensitivity/specificity cutoffs, ordinal logistic regression composite indices.

## Model and parameters
No new PK model. Models applied (from other papers): Schnider propofol, Minto remifentanil. Appendix ordinal-logistic composite indices (Tables 8-9, printed p.811-812) predict OAA/S; they are an index construction, not a PK/PD model.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Remifentanil audit:
- MEAC / minimum effective or analgesic concentration: none reported. Remifentanil was held at calculated effect-site targets of 2 and 4 ng/ml (model Ce, not assayed; no blood assay in the study).
- ke0 / t1/2ke0 / tPeak: no estimate in this paper. t1/2ke0 1.020619 min applied from Minto (end point of that estimate not stated in this paper). Propofol Ce uses tPeak 1.6 min (PDF p.3).
- Potency ratio: only a quoted aside that alfentanil:remifentanil potency is "around 40:1", cited in the discussion of Iselin-Chaves' alfentanil 50 and 100 ng/ml doses (printed p.808, PDF p.7); no source or end point given here.

Propofol / remifentanil interaction (Table 3, PDF p.5, printed 806; ED50 (95 % CI)/ED95; CePROP in µg/ml, BIS and AAI index units; group 0 / 2 / 4 ng/ml remifentanil):
- LORverbal CePROP: 2.9 (2.7-3.1)/3.8; 2.4 (2.2-2.6)/3.1; 2.0 (1.8-2.2)/2.7.
- LORlash CePROP: 2.8 (2.5-3.0)/3.4; 1.8 (1.7-2.0)/2.6; 1.7 (1.6-1.9)/2.5.
- LORnoxious CePROP: 4.1 (3.8-4.4)/6.6; 1.8 (1.4-2.1)/3.8; 1.3 (1.1-1.5)/3.3.
- LORverbal BIS ED50/ED95: 61/55; 66/58; 77/65. AAI: 26/20; 33/18; 40/25. LORlash BIS: 67/55; 80/66; 84/71. AAI: 34/21; 56/44; 56/32. LORnoxious BIS: 41/29; 72/56; 85/68. AAI: 16/11; 42/23; 55/27.
- Remifentanil lowered CePROP at LOR in a dose-dependent way, and BIS/AAI at LOR rose; LORnoxious was most affected.

Prediction probability (Table 4, PDF p.7, printed 808; mean (SE); 0 / 2 / 4 ng/ml / pooled): OAA/S AAI 0.88/0.87/0.88/0.87; BIS 0.93/0.90/0.88/0.90; CePROP 0.92/0.92/0.92/0.90. LORlash AAI 0.94/0.96/0.87/0.94; BIS 0.96/0.92/0.94/0.95; CePROP 0.94/0.95/0.94/0.93. LORnoxious pooled 0.72 (AAI), 0.75 (BIS), 0.72 (CePROP). Composite BIS+CePROP+CeREMI PK 0.91 (0.01); AAI+CePROP+CeREMI 0.93 (0.01) (Table 10).
- OAA/S at LOR (Table 7, printed p.809): LORlash 3.6 / 4.2 / 4.1; LORnoxious 0.87 / 3.4 / 4.0 (0 / 2 / 4 ng/ml).
- Sensitivity/specificity cutoffs: Tables 5-6, printed p.808.

## Covariates
None modelled (adult women only). Composite regression coefficients (Tables 8-9) are for BIS/AAI, CePROP, CeREMI; not read further, not used by the repo.

## Caveats
- Calculated, not measured, concentrations; remifentanil and propofol Ce depend on the Minto and Schnider models.
- Healthy women only, staircase design, tetanic stimulus as noxious stimulus; OAA/S and eyelash reflex themselves shift with opioid (authors' own caveat).
- Author Jensen is the A-Line patent holder and consultant (title footnote).

- Propofol tPeak 1.6 min here matches the library's tPeak 1.6 min (R/drugs_propofol.R:276, citing Schnider 1999); the library's model is Eleveld kinetics with that tPeak, the Schnider companion is in R/alternativeModels.R.

- No MEAC or potency ratio from this paper is used in the repo.
