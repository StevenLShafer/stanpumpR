# Heffernan 2022: total and unbound ceftriaxone in critically ill adults

## Study
- 36 critically ill adults from two PK studies and a TDM programme (Brisbane; Hong Kong); 474 concentrations (207 paired unbound/total, 60 unpaired total).
- Mean age 52 y, hypoalbuminaemic (mean albumin 23.9 g/L), median creatinine clearance 117.9 mL/min (Results text). Table 1 (as read): weight median 65 kg (IQR 59.5-80.0), albumin 23 (20.0-28.3) g/L, CLCR 104 (85-157.88) mL/min; mostly respiratory infections; mostly 1 g twice daily.
- Pmetrics (nonparametric), Monte Carlo PTA for unbound Cmin/MIC > 1.

## Model and parameters
Two compartments, unbound central compartment with dynamic albumin binding (Kon/Koff, binding number N), elimination of unbound drug = TVNR + TVR x CLCR/118 (Eq 1). Table 3 (PDF p. 5; mean (SD)), confirmed on the rendered page:

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- Target unbound Cmin/MIC > 1 over the first 24 h. Albumin <= 20 g/L lowers PTA by up to 20%.
- Dosing recommendations (simulation-based PTA, not measured outcomes). Table 7 (PDF p. 12; rendered page checked) assumes MIC 1 mg/L and PTA >= 90%: CLCR <= 60 mL/min, 1 g twice daily; 61-139 mL/min, 1 g three times daily; >= 140 mL/min, 1 g loading dose then 2-4 g/day continuous infusion or 2 g twice daily. Its footnote allows lower doses for MIC < 1 mg/L.
- Conclusions (PDF p. 12; also Discussion, PDF p. 5): at normal CLCR (~100 mL/min), 1 g twice daily is likely sufficient only for highly susceptible isolates (MIC <= 0.25 mg/L). Conclusions: MIC > 0.25 mg/L or augmented renal clearance needs 3-4 g/day, divided or as a continuous infusion. 1 g twice daily does not cover MIC 1 mg/L near 100 mL/min.
- The abstract (PDF p. 1) states this more loosely: 1 g twice daily for CLCR < 100 mL/min and unspecified "susceptible isolates" (PTA ~90%), and 4 g/day continuous infusion for augmented renal clearance (> 130 mL/min) with MIC >= 0.5 mg/L. Table 7 and the Conclusions are the explicit statements.
- MSSA (breakpoint <= 8 mg/L) poorly covered; only 4 g/day CI reaches target at MIC <= 4.
- Toxicity proxy: total Cmin > 100 mg/L (Table 5).

## Covariates
Creatinine clearance on renal clearance (linear, normalised to 118 mL/min); albumin enters through the binding model.

## Caveats
- n = 36, heterogeneous sources and assays; nonparametric.
- Authors state their volume estimates did not differ appreciably from healthy volunteers, unlike earlier ICU studies (attributed to modelling binding explicitly).
- Table 1 values in the text layer conflict with the Results text (median CLCR 104 vs 117.9).
