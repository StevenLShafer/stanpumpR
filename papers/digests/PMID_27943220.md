# Gonzalez 2017: lorazepam population PK in paediatric status epilepticus

## Study
- Data from two NICHD trials in children with status epilepticus (Status 1 and Status 2).
- 145 patients, 439 samples; median age 5.4 y (0.3-17.8); median dose 0.10 mg/kg (0.02-0.18), with a 4 mg maximum.
- Samples per age group: 125 (3 mo to < 3 y), 184 (3 to < 13 y), 130 (≥ 13 y).
- Assay: LC-MS/MS, calibration 1-500 ng/mL.
- Analysis: NONMEM 7.2, two-compartment, allometric weight scaling, power residual error, bootstrap (n = 1000), VPC.

- Between-subject variability: about 40% on V1, 47-57% on CL and about 90% on V2. The exact column alignment is uncertain in the text layer; the text says it exceeded 40%.
- Eta shrinkage: CL 9%, V1 20%, V2 61%.

Post hoc medians (Table 3):
- CL 1.02 L/h; CL_WT 0.056 L/h/kg.
- Vss_WT 1.40 L/kg.
- α half-life 0.28 h; β half-life 17.36 h.
- Weight-normalised CL is 0.058 L/h/kg below 13 y and 0.048 from 13 y (Discussion).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- Exploratory. CL, Vss and V1 were not associated with efficacy (cessation of seizures) or safety (need for ventilation).
- Higher predicted concentrations were found in patients who failed. The authors attribute this to their extra doses.
- Simulation target: 50-100 ng/mL, from the literature.
  - The Discussion cites adult seizure improvement at 20-30 ng/mL (Walker), an ICU sedation target of 50 ng/mL (Barr), and the label's initial 70 ng/mL after 4 mg.
- 0.1 mg/kg (maximum 4 mg) gave more than 50 ng/mL at 5 min in about 90% of simulated children.

## Covariates
- Total body weight (allometric, with exponent 0.75 on clearances).
- Age on CL (power 0.133, centred at 4.7 y).
- Serum creatinine on V2 fitted similarly but was rejected (less precise in the bootstrap).

## Caveats
- Children aged 3 months to 18 years only.
- Sparse sampling during emergencies.
- Concomitant antiepileptic drugs.
- V2 is poorly identified (shrinkage 61%).
