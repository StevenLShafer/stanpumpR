# Diezi 2023: ethosuximide granules vs Zarontin syrup, with a popPK model

## Study
Phase I, Lausanne (CHUV), two panels of 6 healthy adults, randomised 3-way crossover: ethosuximide granules
(A in panel A, optimised B in panel B) vs placebo granules (double-blind) vs Zarontin syrup 50 mg/mL (open), single
10 mg/kg oral doses, fasting. Panel A: 4 F / 2 M, 26 ± 7 (21-40) y, 67 ± 10 (60-87) kg. Panel B: 1 F / 5 M,
22 ± 2 (19-26) y, 69 ± 7 (56-75) kg. Plasma at 0, 10, 20, 30, 45 min, 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 9, 24, 48, 72, 96, 168, 336,
504 h; saliva in panel A. HPLC-UV 203 nm, LOQ 0.1 µg/mL (linear 0.1-25). NCA in WinNonlin; popPK in NONMEM 7.1.0,
FOCEI, PsN bootstrap (2000), pcVPC, NPDE.

## Model and parameters
NCA (Table 1; mean ± SD, dose-normalised to 10 mg/kg; tmax median (range)):

| | Granules A | Granules B | Syrup |
|---|---|---|---|
| tmax h | 0.63 (0.3-1.0) | 0.75 (0.5-4.05) | 0.5 (0.3-0.8) |
| Cmax µg/mL | 18.2 ± 2.1 | 15.8 ± 1.9 | 18.2 ± 1.0 |
| AUC0-∞ µg·h/mL | 1350 ± 423 | 1100 ± 100 | 1210 ± 125 |
| t½ h | 62.3 ± 15.3 | 54.4 ± 10.5 | 57.1 ± 8.9 |
| CL/F mL/h/kg | 7.6 ± 2.0 | 8.9 ± 0.8 | 8.3 ± 0.8 |
| Vz/F mL/kg | 646 ± 83 | 695 ± 107 | 678 ± 103 |

Relative exposure vs syrup (Section 3.3, PDF p. 6, and abstract, PDF p. 1; mean log-transformed ratios of
dose-normalised values, 90% CI; measured, n 6 per panel):

| | Granules A | Granules B |
|---|---|---|
| Cmax | 93.7% (76.3-115.1) | 87.6% (81.6-94.0) |
| AUC0-∞ | 96.1% (91.0-101.5) | 92.5% (88.5-96.6) |

- Granules B: lower, more blunted peak than syrup (p < .02) and later median tmax (0.75 vs 0.5 h); the authors say
  B lies entirely within the 80-125% bioequivalence range (p. 6), and B had palatability similar to placebo granules
  and low adherence to glass (abstract, p. 1).
- Granules A: its Cmax lower bound, 76.3%, is outside 80-125%, although the Discussion (PDF p. 7) says both granules
  remained bioequivalent at Cmax and that A met the criteria better than B. The numbers do not support formal Cmax
  bioequivalence for A. A was also judged suboptimal (bitter, adhered to the beaker; abstract).

PopPK (pooled 12 subjects, 3 formulations): two compartments, first-order absorption with separate ka for syrup
and granules, combined additive + proportional error. Vc = 31.3 × (1 + 1.71 × (WT − 67.2)/67.2) (Table 2 note;
linear weight on Vc only; ΔOF −33.9). No covariate on CL; age not influential (range 18-40).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD model. Dizziness VAS vs plasma concentration shows clockwise hysteresis after syrup (Figure 4); CNS VAS peaked
at 30 min; differences between formulations not significant. Saliva/plasma slope 0.67 (Figure 5). No therapeutic
range data.

## Covariates
Linear body weight on Vc (slope 1.71, centred on 67.2 kg). Weight, age, sex tested on CL: none.

## Caveats
n = 12 healthy adults, single dose, narrow weight range (56-87 kg); the linear weight term is not extrapolable (goes
to zero at 67.2 × (1 − 1/1.71) ≈ 27.9 kg (derived)). BSV values in the text (CL 18%, ka1 110%, ka2 61%) differ
from Table 2 (17.6, 153, 66). Parameters are apparent (F not identified); granule formulation is not marketed in
the US. Industry co-authors (Advicenne).
