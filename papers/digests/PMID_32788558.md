# Dahan 2020: oliceridine vs morphine utility (analgesia vs respiratory depression)

## Study
Reanalysis of Trevena study 1003 (Soergel 2014): healthy male volunteers (30 enrolled, 29 analysed), 19-50 y, BMI
19-32. Each received IV oliceridine 1.5, 3 and 4.5 mg and IV morphine 10 mg on separate visits (placebo data not
used). Venous samples 2, 12, 30 min, 1, 2, 3, 4, 8 h; oliceridine LC-MS/MS LLOQ 0.05 ng/mL; morphine LLOQ 0.5 ng/mL.
End points: cold pressor hand-withdrawal latency (2 °C, cutoff 180 s) at 10, 30 min, 1-8 h; hypercapnic ventilatory
response (5% CO2 rebreathing, 5 min) at 0.5-4 h. NONMEM 7, two-stage (individual PK posthocs → PD). Plus
extrapolation to phase III studies 3001 (bunionectomy) and 3002 (abdominoplasty): 309 oliceridine, 159 morphine
patients, 96 respiratory events.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- Opioid audit: no MEAC in the clinical sense. Analgesic potency: oliceridine C100 27.9 ng/mL, morphine 34.3 ng/mL
  (effect-site concentration doubling cold-pressor tolerance, healthy men, venous plasma, total drug); potency ratio
  0.81 (95% CI 0.39-1.56), i.e. equianalgesic by concentration.
- Respiratory: morphine 50% VRH reduction at 33.7 ng/mL; oliceridine 25% reduction at 27.4 ng/mL; respiratory
  potency ratio 2.48 (1.65-3.72) in favour of morphine being more depressant.
- ke0: oliceridine t½ke0 for analgesia not quantifiable (fast); for ventilation 0.25 h (dependent on baseline VRH);
  morphine t½ke0 0.6 h (analgesia) and 1.2 h (ventilation). No tPeak reported.
- Utility P(analgesia ≥ 0.5) - P(resp depression ≥ 0.25): oliceridine positive 0-35 ng/mL, morphine negative;
  86% of clinical respiratory events at utility ≤ 0.2.

## Covariates
None reported (all male, narrow range).

## Caveats
Men only; venous sampling; sponsor (Trevena) supplied data, two authors Trevena employees; thresholds arbitrary;
PK parameters only in the supplement; respiratory t½ke0 depends on baseline VRH.
