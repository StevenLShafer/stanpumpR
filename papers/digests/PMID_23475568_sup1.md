# Duong 2013, supplement: metformin study design, transporter genotypes and CL/F

This file is the electronic supplement to Duong 2013 (the main paper is `PMID_23475568.pdf`, digested in
`PMID_23475568.md`). It holds no model parameters: one figure and three tables.

## Study
Supplementary Table I (PDF p. 3), the three pooled studies:

| Study | Subjects | Metformin dose | Samples/subject, median (range) | Observations |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | 120 T2DM patients | multiple and single dose, IR and XR, 250-3000 mg daily (no restriction on regimen) | 2 (1-18) | 462 |
| B | 16 healthy Caucasians | multiple and single dose, IR and XR; escalation 500 to 1500 mg, then randomized to 2000 mg XR or 1000 mg IR bd; 35 days | 120 (52-120) | 1833 |
| C | 169 healthy Malaysians | single dose IR, randomized 500, 850 or 1000 mg | 15 (10-18) | 2600 |

(derived: 462 + 1833 + 2600 = 4895 observations, the total the main paper gives.)

Supplementary Table III (PDF pp. 8-13, text layer only): forward and reverse primer list for 59 SNPs genotyped in the
T2DM patients, in three multiplexes (29, 23 and 7 SNPs). The main-paper card records 57 SNPs tested; the 59 here
are those genotyped (the difference is not explained in the supplement).

## Model and parameters
None in this file; the structural model, Table 2 and the covariate equations are in the main paper.

Supplementary Table II (PDF pp. 4-7): apparent metformin clearance (CL/F, L/h, mean ± SD) by genotype for OCT1
(18 markers including G465R, plus "number of inactive OCT1 alleles"), OCT2, OCT3, MATE1 and PMAT, in study A's genotyped
patients (about 103 per marker; derived: e.g. rs1867351 76 + 23 + 4). Wild-type means run about 43-56 L/h with
SDs about 20-31 L/h. No marker reached significance: the smallest P values are 0.07 (PMAT rs6963810: WT 55.7 ± 28.4,
WT/V 43.6 ± 20.5, V/V 47.2 ± 26.7; n 36/54/11; PDF p. 7) and 0.09 (OCT1 rs3798167). Number of inactive OCT1
alleles: 0, 1, 2 gave 50.2 ± 22.0, 49.0 ± 29.0, 34.9 ± 11.4 L/h (n 66/30/2), P = 0.68. P values by one-way ANOVA,
or unpaired t test where only two genotype groups were present. The text layer splits the last PMAT rows' WT/V
column onto separate lines; the values above were read on the rendered page.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Supplementary Figure 1 (PDF p. 2): scatter of metformin average steady-state concentration (Cav,ss, mg/L, about
0.2-7.7) against (a) HbA1c (%) and (b) plasma lactate (mmol/L), with a dotted line at the upper reference for
lactate (about 2.7 mmol/L, read from the plot). No correlation coefficient, threshold or concentration-effect model
is given. No therapeutic or toxic range is stated. Not an opioid: no MEAC, ke0 or potency ratio.

## Covariates
Transporter genotypes were tested against CL/F and none was associated (Table II). Renal-function covariates are
in the main paper.

## Caveats
- The supplement does not say whether Table II's CL/F values are post hoc model estimates or non-compartmental,
  nor the patients' renal function, so its means cannot be set beside a typical-value model directly.
- Many homozygous-variant cells have n = 1-4 (SD printed 0.0 for n = 1).
- Does not resolve the main card's open question on the ALAG unit (no NONMEM code here).

- Independent check: `R/drugs_metformin.R:136-139` (Chae 2012) gives CL/F = 52.6 L/h x (CLcr/106.5)^0.782.
  Table II's wild-type means (about 43-56 L/h, T2DM patients) lie in the same range as Chae's typical value at
  normal renal function. At study A's median CLcr of 67 mL/min (main paper, Table 1), Chae's model predicts about
  37 L/h (derived: 52.6 x (67/106.5)^0.782), below these means; but Table II's subset, renal function and how
  CL/F was obtained are not given here, and Duong's CLcr uses lean body weight, so this is not a discrepancy, only
  a direction to note. The library's choice of Chae and its omission of genotype are consistent with this
  supplement (no transporter effect found).
- The library models IR only (`R/drugs_metformin.R:35-37`); this supplement's studies A and B mix IR and XR.
