# Chae 2026: Remimazolam in obstetric anaesthesia, a focused review

## Study
Narrative focused review (6 pp.), not a PK study. It covers remimazolam's pharmacology, case reports and small trials in labour, cesarean (neuraxial and general), termination of pregnancy, safety, compatibility and cost. Cited obstetric evidence: case reports, one RCT of 80 (remimazolam 5 mg vs midazolam 2 mg, spinal cesarean), a pilot of 22, retrospective series (n=13 and n=51), and termination trials (n=22 up-and-down; n=200 RCT). The paper states that most studies were in non-pregnant surgical patients.

Pregnancy: the paper says there are no human PK data in pregnancy (p.5, "Future research direction"). It says pregnancy may suppress hepatic CES1 in animals, while an oseltamivir study found no change in human CES1 activity (p.2). It reports no clearance or volume change for pregnancy.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- No ke0, t½ke0, EC50 or MEAC reported. Not an opioid paper.
- Time of peak effect 3-3.5 min after a single dose, 11-14 min after multiple doses (Table 2); "maximal effect about 5 min" in the text (p.4). Sedation time 11-14 min (Table 2). End point: sedation (MOAA/S), not measured as an effect-site constant.
- ED50 0.145 mg/kg and ED95 0.242 mg/kg for suppressing body movement, given with sufentanil 0.1 µg/kg, during surgical abortion (22 patients, up-and-down design; Liu 2024, p.4).
- Dosing (manufacturer, p.2): 5 mg over 1 min, then 2.5 mg every 2-5 min (ASA I-II); 2.5 mg initial then 1.25-2.5 mg (ASA III-IV). General anesthesia: 6 mg/kg/h induction, 1-2 mg/kg/h maintenance (non-obstetric ASA III, with remifentanil TCI 0.2-0.3 µg/kg).
- The review says TCI models exist but are not validated in pregnancy (p.2, Table 4).

## Covariates
Reported only as qualitative risk factors: severe hepatic impairment (reduced clearance), age, obesity, hypoalbuminemia, Asian ethnicity (flumazenil re-sedation or delayed emergence). No renal dose adjustment is needed (p.2). No equations.

## Caveats
Narrative review with no systematic methods described. Table 2 comes from a secondary source (APSF) and has misaligned rows. The half-life figures differ between Table 2 (37-53 min) and the text (about 20 min). Case reports and small trials mostly come from Asia.
