# Voller 2015: age-dependent doxorubicin pharmacokinetics in children

## Study
EPOC-MS-001-Doxo, multicentre phase II PK trial (20 centres, Italy, France, UK, Germany; NCT01095926). 101
recruited, 94 with PK data (48 female, 46 male): 23 aged <3 y, 46 aged 3 to <12 y, 25 aged 12 to <18 y (p2).
Table 1 (PDF p3, printed 1141; page image), median (range): age 5.32 y (0.2-17.7), BMI 16.3 (12.7-31.4) kg/m2,
BSA 0.77 (0.23-2.05) m2, serum creatinine 0.40 (0.06-0.80) mg/dL, bilirubin 0.30 (0.12-1.20) mg/dL, height 111
(52-194) cm, weight 19.3 (3.6-88.1) kg, infusion duration 3.92 (0.25-24.0) h, administered dose 25.0 (2.4-57.0) mg
(salt not stated). Solid tumours and leukaemia per national/European protocols. Samples during and after two
administrations per patient: 5 samples (3 + 2) in children <3 y, 8 (5 + 3) in older children. Plasma doxorubicin
and doxorubicinol by HPLC with fluorescence (epirubicin internal standard), validated range 2-1000 ug/L. 620
doxorubicin and 588 doxorubicinol observations of 665 samples evaluable; one outlier >3000 ug/L excluded; 17% of
the during-infusion samples missing. NONMEM 7.2.0, FOCE+I, log-transformed data; OFV drop of 6.63 (p<0.01) for
selection. 17 SNPs (carbonyl reductases, transporters, others) tested.

## Model and parameters
Three-compartment linear doxorubicin model with a one-compartment doxorubicinol model fed by a fraction
metabolised fM fixed at 0.5 (from published doxorubicinol/doxorubicin AUC ratios; 0.3-0.7 tried, none better)
(p6, Fig. 2). Proportional residual error; exponential IIV on CL, V1, Q2 (correlated CL-Q2-V1); IOV on V1.
All parameters scale linearly on BSA with one factor 1.30; CL also carries a power of age (Eq. 1, p6, printed
1144, page image):

CL_i = thetaCL x exp(eta_i) x (1 + (BSA_i - BSA_median) x 1.30) x (1 + (Age_i / Age_median)^0.286)

Table 2 (PDF p5, printed 1143, page image): values for the median child (BSA 0.77 m2, age 5.32 y) converted
from L/h/0.77 m2 or L/0.77 m2 to per m2 (table footnote). Model-building estimate (RSE); bootstrap estimate
[90% CI].

Empirical Bayes CL normalised to BSA: 21.1 L/h/m2 in children <3 y vs 26.6 L/h/m2 in children >=3 y
(p=0.0004, Welch t test; p7 Results; the Discussion, p8, prints p=0.004). The 18-year-old value equals 52.4 L/h
(p8). Derived (this card): thetaCL = 24.1 x 0.77 / 2 = 9.28 L/h, i.e. 12.05 L/h/m2 on the per-m2 scale; half-lives
at the median child 0.10, 1.96 and 31.2 h; Vss 597 L/m2; doxorubicinol t1/2 0.693 x 760 / 42.5 = 12.4 h. Note the
slope 1.30 is almost exactly 1/0.77 (1.299), so the "linear" BSA factor is in effect proportional to BSA.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No exposure-response, no ke0, no concentration target; cardiotoxicity is discussed only as a motive.

## Covariates
BSA (linear, every parameter, slope 1.30 about 0.77 m2; better than weight, allometric weight, height, BMI or
lean body mass; IIV on CL fell from 85% to 31%). Age as a power (0.286) inside "1 +" on CL only; age categories did
not help. BSA on CLM and VM (dOFV 86). No effect of sex, renal or hepatic markers, BMI, tumour type, fluid volume,
or any of the 17 SNPs; age did not affect fM, CLM or VM.

## Caveats
Only 23 children <3 y (authors ask for confirmation). 17% of during-infusion samples missing, so volumes are
poorly determined (V1 IIV RSE 88.4%, wide bootstrap CIs). IOV on V1 (124%) exceeds IIV. fM fixed, so CLM and VM
are apparent values. Population doxorubicinol under-predicted above 15 ug/L. BSA formula in the supplement only;
dose salt not stated. Table 2's bootstrap CL (11.7) is on a different scale from the 24.1 beside it (see below).
The age p value is printed 0.0004 (Results) and 0.004 (Discussion).
