# Prohn 2021: letermovir population PK (healthy participants and HSCT recipients)

## Study
Two-stage NONMEM 7.3 analysis. Phase I model: 9020 plasma observations (174 below LLOQ excluded) from 280 healthy participants in 12 phase I studies, doses 30-960 mg, oral and i.v., single and multiple; over 90% female; median age 30 y (range 18-59), weight 66 kg (45-99) per Table 1 (text layer, unconfirmed on an image). HSCT-recipient model: 2888 observations from 399 HSCT recipients (phase III n=350, phase IIb n=13, phase I n=36) at steady state on clinical doses (240-480 mg, with or without cyclosporine A, CSA); 53 below LLOQ excluded. Concentrations are plasma, ng/mL.

## Model and parameters
Phase I model: four-compartment, concentration-dependent (Michaelis-Menten) clearance and intercompartment clearance Q1, auto-induction of CL, transit-compartment absorption; proportional error. Table 2 (p. 260, printed): CLmax 12.3 L/h; weight exponent on CLmax 0.566 and on Vd 0.667 (reference 65.8 kg, Figure 1 legend); V1 7.46 L; V2 61.6; V3 12.1; V4 19.0 L; Q1max 4.39, Q2 31.3, Q3 4.91 L/h; KMCL 2.68e3 and KMQ 5.63e3 ng/mL; NTR 3.58; MTT 1.04 h (dose effect 0.344 relative to 240 mg); Kout 0.00783 1/h; IMAG 0.0829; F1 0.938; Asian effect on Vd -0.281 (27.6% lower); proportional error 0.283. Fixed-effect numbers come from the text layer of a clean table; not re-read on an image.

HSCT-recipient model: two-compartment, linear elimination, first-order absorption with lag, CL and F depending on CSA (Table 3, PDF p. 8, printed 262, read from the page image):

Predicted steady-state AUC0-24 (p. 263): oral 480 mg/day median 34,400 ng.h/mL (90% PI 16,900-73,700); oral 240 mg with CSA 60,800 (28,700-122,000); i.v. 480 mg 100,000 (65,300-148,000); i.v. 240 mg with CSA 70,300 (46,200-106,000). Combined across regimens median 49,200 (26,900-87,400).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No PD, MEAC, ke0 or potency data. (Not an opioid.)

## Covariates
Phase I model: body weight on CLmax and Vd (allometric-type exponents above, reference 65.8 kg); Asian descent on Vd. Gender, OATP1B1 and UGT1A1 genotypes, and age had no significant effect. HSCT model: CSA on CL and F; Asian descent on V2; weight on CL did not improve fit.

## Caveats
Nonlinear (supra-proportional) PK across 30-960 mg; the HSCT model is a linear simplification valid near the clinical dose. Phase I model fit poorly at 30 and 960 mg. The HSCT model underpredicts Cmax (no IIV on V1, two-compartment structure; Figures S5). Healthy cohort was over 90% female. F in healthy participants was fixed to 1 in the HSCT model, so F in HSCT recipients is relative to that.
