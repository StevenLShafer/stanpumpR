# el-Desoky 2005: two-sample population PK of IV digoxin in Egyptian children with heart failure

## Study
40 inpatients with heart failure, Assiut University Hospital, Egypt. Table 1 (PDF p2 / printed 321): age 5.94 (median 5.50, 0.33-15.00) y; weight 17.86 (15.00, 4.25-42.00) kg; height 99.18 (54-160) cm; serum creatinine 0.63 (0.6, 0.2-1.3), printed "g/dL"; Schwartz CrCL 91.57 (30.68-130) mL/min/1.73 m2 (capped 130); 19/21 male/female; prednisone 7, furosemide 10. Only 9 of 40 were under 2 years (Discussion p7). IV digoxin 0.01 mg/kg/day in two doses (5 mcg/kg every 12 h), at steady state. Two samples per child: 30 children trough plus 4 h post-dose; 10 children 0.5 and 6 h. FPIA (TDxFLX), CV <8%. NONMEM V, FOCE, log-transformed data, exponential IIV, additive residual; GAM screening, forward P < 0.01, backward P < 0.001; 1000-replicate bootstrap.

## Model and parameters
Two compartments (one compartment worse by 59 OFV units). Only CL, V1 and Q population values estimated; V2 fixed (flat likelihood profile); IIV on V1 and V2 fixed to zero. Serum creatinine the only retained covariate (Table 3, p5): the final model is written as **CL = theta1 x [1 + theta5 x (SCR - 0.6)]** (OFV -87, dOFV -14). Sex on Q was dropped on backward deletion.

Bootstrap (Table 6, PDF p7): final model CL 0.38 +/- 0.02, V1 1.36 +/- 0.11, Q 0.47 +/- 0.09 L/h/kg, V2 8.46 fixed, original and bootstrap alike; base model original CL printed 0.31 +/- 0.02 (Table 4 says 0.37).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. Predicted Css 0.83-2.4 ng/mL, median 1.09, mean 1.17 (p5). No toxicity seen.

## Covariates
Serum creatinine on CL (above). Age and weight not significant (few infants). Furosemide and prednisone no effect.

## Caveats
- The clearance equation is printed three ways: (1) abstract: "CL (L/h) = 0.388 - [0.78 x (SCR-0.6)]", Q 0.48; (2) Results (PDF p4, rendered): TVCL = 0.38 - 0.77 x (SCR - 0.6), additive; (3) Table 3's final model CL = theta1 x [1 + theta5 x (SCR - 0.6)] with Table 4's theta SCR -0.76, a proportional form (0.38 x (1 - 0.76 (SCR - 0.6))). The additive forms give zero clearance at SCr 1.09 (derived), and negative clearance at 1.3, inside the studied range. Only the proportional form gives positive clearance across the data (zero at 1.92, derived). The source does not say which is right.
- V1 and V2: Table 4 gives 1.38 and 9.11 (fixed), Table 6's final-model "original data" 1.36 and 8.46 (fixed). The text's Vss of 9.82 L/kg equals Table 6's sum; it does not equal Table 4's.
- Sampling: the Methods say 30 children gave a trough **and** a 4-h sample, but the Discussion (p6) says about 75% (n = 30) "had only the late samples taken (trough samples)".
- Two samples per child; SCr range 0.2-1.3 mg/dL; single centre; SCr units misprinted g/dL.
