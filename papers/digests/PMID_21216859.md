# Murphy 2011: closed-loop insulin aspart delivery in early and late type 1 diabetic pregnancy

PDF page = printed page minus 405.

## Study
- Exploratory safety study, ISRCTN62568875. 10 pregnant women with type 1 diabetes recruited March 2009 to March 2010
  (Cambridge 7, Norwich 2, Ipswich 1) (PDF p2).
- Results text (PDF p3), median (IQR): age 31.1 (28.7-31.7) y; diabetes duration 19 (13.5-24) y; weight 66.6
  (64-73.9) kg; booking A1C 6.9% (6.2-8.0); BMI 24.1 (23.1-26.3). Visits at 14.8 and 28.0 weeks (abstract).
- Each visit 24 h at the Wellcome Trust Clinical Research Facility: Deltec Cozmo pump delivering **insulin aspart**;
  basal rate set every 15 min by a model predictive control (MPC) algorithm from FreeStyle Navigator CGM, entered by a nurse;
  prandial boluses by the woman's insulin/carbohydrate ratio; standardised dinner (80 g carbohydrate) at 1800 h and
  breakfast (60 g) at 0700 h (PDF p2). Plasma glucose (YSI) every 15 min from 1400 h.
- Statistics: Wilcoxon tests; linear mixed-effects models with ARMA(2,2) correlation (PDF p3).

## Model and parameters
No pharmacokinetics. Plasma insulin concentrations, time to peak insulin and insulin clearance are not reported in this
paper; the MPC algorithm's internal model (competing absorption models run in parallel) is not specified numerically.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End points are glucose control (abstract, PDF p1): median plasma glucose 117 (100.8-154.8) mg/dL early vs 126
(109.8-140.4) late, P = 0.72; overnight time in target (63-140 mg/dL) 84% vs 100%, P = 0.09; no differences in postprandial
glucose, glucose variability, insulin infusion rates or CGM accuracy between early and late pregnancy. Sensor MARD 13.3%
(14.7% early, 11.9% late) (PDF p4).

## Covariates
Gestation (early vs late) as a fixed effect on glucose; none on insulin kinetics.

## Caveats
- n = 10, no power calculation; insulin dosing per weight trended up in late pregnancy, not significantly.
- This trial's data were later reused as "study 1" by Goudie 2014 and 2015 for the aspart PK analysis.
