# Hengstmann 1982: tritiated phenylephrine IV and oral in volunteers

## Study
Bonn. 7-3H-phenylephrine (191 uCi/mg). 15 subjects with no evidence of kidney or liver impairment, counting one
patient with a porto-caval anastomosis (PDF p1): 4 volunteers IV, about 1 mg in 2 mL saline over 12.5-20 min
(Table 2: 0.842 +/- 0.172 mg); 10 volunteers oral, 1 mg in 100 mL water fasting (Table 2 serum in 3 of them, 0.986
+/- 0.15 mg); plus the shunt patient oral. No age, weight, height or sex is reported. Serum sampled to 480 min IV,
360 min oral (Table 2); urine to 48 h. Analyte: tritium in the free-amine fraction of serum after column
chromatography ("free" = unconjugated, not unbound), assumed to be only phenylephrine by analogy with urine (PDF
p2); recovery 96 +/- 0.6%, results corrected. Two-compartment open model fitted to each IV subject; oral
bioavailability from the ratio of dose-normalised AUCs (Dost).

Urine (Table 1, PDF p2): total 3H 86.3% IV (n 3; four were infused) and 79.5% oral (n 10) at 48 h; free
phenylephrine 16.0% IV vs 2.6% oral; conjugates 8.3% vs 45.7%; m-hydroxymandelic acid 56.9% vs 24.2%. The text gives
the IV conjugate as 3.6% in the first period and 8.0% in total (PDF p2), against 1.4% at 2 h and 8.3% at 48 h in
Table 1.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No concentration-effect model, no ke0. The Discussion (PDF p5) relates Horvath 1954's pressor data to serum levels:
10-15 ng/mL were seen when the effect of a 0.75 mg IV bolus had gone at 5 min; oral 1 mg gave peaks near 1 ng/mL.

## Covariates
None (n 4 IV).

## Caveats
Four IV subjects, demographics unreported. The analyte is radioactivity in a chromatographic fraction, not a
specific assay. Renal clearance changed with time after IV dosing (540 mL/min in the first 2 h vs 72 mL/min on day
2). Abstract errors: total clearance "2 l/h" (Summary) against 2095 mL/min (Table 3) and "about 2l/min"
(Discussion); the beta half-life range in the text (124-206 min) is close to but not exactly 0.693/beta (126-210 min,
derived). The salt or base of the dose is not stated.
