# Jin 2014: simvastatin and simvastatin acid popPK with three-depot absorption

## Study
- Pooled data from 4 studies at Asan Medical Center, Seoul (p.1802-1803, Table I, text layer): 133 healthy Korean
  males; age 25.0 ± 2.6 y, weight 69.8 ± 6.7 kg, height 174.8 ± 5.4 cm, BMI 22.8 ± 1.7 kg/m² (mean ± SD, total column).
  - Study I (PK crossover, n = 20): two oral doses each, randomly from 10, 20, 30, 40, 80 mg (Zocor), 3-day washout,
    fasted; dense sampling 0-24 h (10, 20, 30, 40, 50 min, 1-2 h every 15 min, then 3, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 24 h).
  - Studies II-IV (bioequivalence 2×2, n = 30, 30, 53): single 60 mg Zocor (reference arm only used), sampling 0-12 h.
- 2,182 simvastatin and 2,130 simvastatin acid concentrations. Assay HPLC-MS/MS; LLOQ 0.2 ng/mL (simvastatin),
  0.1 ng/mL (acid); BLQ handled as LLOQ/2 for the first value in the disposition phase.
- NONMEM VII level 2, FOCE-I; amounts and concentrations converted to molar units (residual error in nmol/L).
  Sequential parent then metabolite fit (metabolite model used individual MAP parent estimates).

## Model and parameters
Parent: 2-compartment, linear elimination, with three parallel depot compartments, each fed by a zero-order input
(duration D1, D2, D3) and emptying by first order (Ka1-Ka3), lags ALAG2 and ALAG3 (Fig. 2; no lag on depot 1 is
listed). Depot fractions F1 = 1/(1+BA1+BA2), F2 = BA1/(1+BA1+BA2), F3 = BA2/(1+BA1+BA2) (Eq. 2, p.1804). All
parameters are apparent (oral; absolute F not estimated). IOV on BA1, BA2, D1-D3.

Metabolite (simvastatin acid): 2-compartment (V6 central, fixed 1 L for identifiability; V7 peripheral via K67/K76),
fraction FM of parent CL forms acid, reversible acid→parent clearance Q64, acid clearance CLm. Non-equilibrium
interconversion preferred (OFV 13,140.4 vs 16,274.5 equilibrium, p.1806). The acid volume is not physiological; the
authors say so (p.1811).

Absorption-model comparison (Table II, PDF p5): Model I 5,757.0 (17 parameters), II 5,652.9 (25), III 5,274.1 (35,
selected), IV 6,682.5 (21), V Weibull 5,999.0 (19); first-order-only 2-compartment 7,615.9.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No PD, no concentration-effect relation, no ke0, no target concentration. Not an opioid paper.

## Covariates
Age, weight and height were tested for parent and metabolite; none retained (p.1806).

## Caveats
- Healthy young Korean men only, single doses, fasted; no CYP3A4/SLCO1B1 genotype or interacting drugs.
- Empirical 35-parameter absorption model. Read literally, Fig. 2's DES empties depot 1 only by Ka1 = 0.00126 /h
  (derived: about 3% of depot 1 absorbed in 24 h), which cannot produce the early peak the text attributes to depot 1;
  a unit or printing problem is likely (see `open_questions`).
- Several internal inconsistencies (BA values, final disposition values, Q64 CV, CI scales): see `open_questions`.
- Acid volume fixed at 1 L, so acid CL and FM are only relative; acid concentrations are model-scaled.
- Prior study (Kim 2011) gave Vd 8,980 L and CL 1,740 L/h; the authors attribute differences to the absorption model
  (p.1806, 1810): the comparator is that study's structural model, not a reference population.
