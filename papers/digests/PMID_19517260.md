# Hegde 2009: BIS-guided closed-loop propofol (CLADS) in pheochromocytoma

## Study
Retrospective analysis of 13 adults (7 M / 6 F, 15-65 y) who had adrenalectomy for pheochromocytoma over 4 years under one surgeon (PDF p2-3). Table 1 (mean ± SD): age 38 ± 15 y, weight 55 ± 12 kg, height 160.4 ± 9.3 cm, BMI 21.4 ± 3.5.
Premedication oral diazepam; fentanyl 2 µg/kg then 1 µg/kg/h and magnesium sulphate 60 mg/kg then 10 mg/kg/h (both stopped at tumor excision); vecuronium 0.1 mg/kg; epidural bupivacaine/lignocaine. Propofol induction and maintenance was delivered by CLADS, a BIS-driven closed-loop controller (BIS target 50, BIS averaged over 15 s, Fresenius Pilot-C syringe pump, Aspect A-2000 monitor). The controller algorithm and its PK model are not given in this paper (cited to Puri 2007, ref 12).
Performance measured by Varvel 1992 metrics (MDPE, MDAPE, wobble, divergence).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No ke0, EC50, MEAC or potency ratio. The only end point is BIS (target 50). Minimum BIS during induction 45.6 ± 4.4. BIS rose significantly after intubation, incision and tumor handling, and fell after tumor excision (p. 3-4, Fig 1). The authors speculate (not tested) that falling catecholamines reduced hepatic blood flow and propofol clearance after excision. No propofol concentrations were measured (stated as a limitation).

## Covariates
None analysed (the system itself scales infusion by weight; method not described here).

## Caveats
Retrospective, n = 13, no control group, single centre. No plasma propofol, catecholamine or magnesium levels. Haemodynamic control worse than other CLADS series (MAP within ±25% of baseline 76.3 ± 13.2% of time). Six of 13 needed nitroprusside during tumor handling.
