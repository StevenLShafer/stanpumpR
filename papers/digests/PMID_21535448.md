# Kleijn 2011: population PK-PD of sugammadex reversal of rocuronium

## Study
- Pooled data from 8 Organon/MSD trials for model building (2 more for external validation; Table 1): 426 patients + 20 healthy
  volunteers = 446 in the PK data set; 59 rocuronium-only (placebo) patients for the rocuronium PD; 338 for sugammadex PD.
- Weight mean 72.5 kg (9.60-139), median 74.5; age 42.4 y (1-91), median 43; CLCR mean 118 mL/min (4.30-239), median 119;
  289 M / 157 F; non-Asian/Asian 393/53; 14 renally impaired (CLCR < 30) (Table 2).
- Rocuronium 0.6-1.2 mg/kg; sugammadex 0.1-16 mg/kg (study range 0.1-96 mg/kg dose-proportional); propofol or sevoflurane.
- Assays: LC-MS/MS, **total** (free + complexed) sugammadex and rocuronium; LLOQ 2 ng/mL rocuronium, 0.1 µg/mL sugammadex (text
  layer prints "mg ml-1"; the µ is lost throughout, see Caveats).
- CLCR: Cockcroft-Gault for adults (>= 18 y); Schwartz denormalised by DuBois BSA for children (Table 2 footnote). Missing creatinine
  imputed: adults CLCR = 183 - 1.34 x age; children 53.8 + age^1.75.
- NONMEM VI, FOCE (final), PsN 2.2.4; bootstrap 1000; posterior predictive checks on TOF90/TOF70 reversal time.
- PD: acceleromyography (TOF-Watch SX), T4/T1 ratio; reversal time = start of sugammadex to TOF 0.9 (primary), 0.7, 0.8.

## Model and parameters
Two-compartment models for rocuronium, sugammadex and the complex (three-compartment unstable). The complex has free sugammadex's
PK parameters. Dynamic binding in the central compartment: kd = k2/k1 fixed 0.0559 (in vitro), k2 = exp(-3.38) = 0.034 /min,
k1 = 0.61 /min per concentration unit; association/dissociation half-lives 1.1 and 20.4 min. Allometric exponents fixed: 0.75
(clearances), 1 (volumes), -0.25 (rate constants). Reference subject: non-Asian, 43 y, 74.5 kg, CLCR 119 mL/min.

Sugammadex (Table 4):
- CL = [1 + θ(BW - 74.5)] x [2 CR/(CR + 119)]^θ x TVCL (no allometric weight term on CL; renal term carries size)
- V1 = [1 + θ(BW - 74.5)] x RAC x θ x (BW/70); Q2 = θ x (BW/70)^0.75; V2 = exp[θ(CR - 119)] x θ x (BW/70)

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- Rocuronium effect compartment, end point T4/T1 ratio (sigmoid Emax, Emax = E0): keo 0.134 /min (t1/2 5.2 min) without sevoflurane,
  0.058 /min (12 min) with sevoflurane (keo sevo effect -0.567); keo x (BW/70)^-0.25; IIV keo 41.7%. EC50 1.62 (printed "mM";
  µM by magnitude, derived: about 1 µg/mL rocuronium) falling to 0.98 with sevoflurane (-0.395, a 40% potentiation); IIV 24.9%;
  Hill 7.52 (IIV 41.1%); E0 1.04 x 100 (IIV 11.1%) (Table 4, Results p424).
- Sugammadex effect: extra elimination of rocuronium from the effect compartment, rate ks x free central sugammadex;
  ks = exp(-3.43) = 0.033 /min per µM at 70 kg, x(BW/70)^-0.25; IIV 114%. With 2 or 4 mg/kg the effect-site rocuronium half-life
  falls to 1.43 and 0.71 min (vs 5.2 min).
- Simulated TOF90 in typical adults: 0.8 min (16 mg/kg at 3 min after 1.2 mg/kg rocuronium), 1.5 min (4 mg/kg deep block),
  1.4 min (2 mg/kg at T2) (abstract, Table 6). External validation Table 5. Not an opioid.

## Covariates
Sugammadex: weight (linear on CL beyond the renal term; on V1 with allometry), CLCR (CL via saturating power term; V2), race
(V1). Rocuronium: age (CL, V2), CLCR (V1), race (Q2). Sevoflurane on keo and EC50. No gender effect.

## Caveats
Assay measures total drug; complexation only in central compartment; paediatric CLCR by Schwartz, not Cockcroft-Gault; no infants
under 1 y in fact; TOF90 under sevoflurane poorly predicted (bias 23.25 min for spontaneous recovery); k2 bootstrap biphasic. The
text layer renders µ as "m" (µM -> "mM", µg/mL -> "mg ml-1"); units above are corrected by magnitude.
