# Karanam 2018: lamotrigine CL/F rises from 5 weeks' gestation

PDF page n = printed page 555 + n.

## Study
- Prospective multicentre observational study (P-PEP, a supplement to WEPOD; 4 US epilepsy centres), women with
  epilepsy aged 18-40 planning pregnancy, on lamotrigine **without interacting co-medication** (PDF p2, printed 557).
- 23 women (26 pregnancies) eligible; 1 excluded (incomplete dose data); **22 women, 25 pregnancies** analysed,
  2 miscarriages (PDF p4). Preconception age 32.6 +/- 3.7 y, height 64.7 +/- 2.7 in, weight 73.6 +/- 18.7 kg;
  20 Caucasian (Table 2, PDF p4, printed 559).
- Formulation: 19 extended release, 1 immediate release, 5 unknown (PDF p4).
- Sampling: convenience (not necessarily trough) plasma samples at preconception baseline and at 5 visits about
  2 weeks apart between 5 and 13 gestational weeks (dated from last menstrual period). 141 LTG concentration-
  gestational week pairs (52 preconception) and 128 estradiol-LTG pairs (48 preconception) (PDF p2, p4).
- Assay: plasma LTG by validated LC/MS (within-run CV 5.7%, between-run 2.6%); estradiol by LC-MS/MS (PDF p2-3).
- Analysis: NONMEM 7.2 + Pirana; all concentrations treated as steady state, CL/F = dosing rate / Cpred
  (Equation 1, PDF p3, printed 558). No volume or absorption estimated. No body-size covariates tested
  (justified by the earlier, larger Polepally 2014 model).

## Model and parameters
Linear models (Table 1, PDF p3): preconception CL/F = CL_BL; pregnancy CL/F = CL_BL + slope x GW, or
CL_BL + slope x estradiol. Table 3 (PDF p5, printed 560), estimate (RSE%), bootstrap median (2.5th-97.5th):

Other results: pregnancies split at a 24% mean CL/F change (geometric mean of the Polepally 2014 subpopulations'
7.5% and 76% first-trimester changes); about 44% of the 25 pregnancies fell in the low-change group (36% of women,
counting the miscarriage-then-pregnancy pairs); the high-change group's slope was steeper (p < 0.001), with no
difference in estradiol change between groups (p = 0.523) (PDF p4-6).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None: no concentration-effect analysis, no seizure-frequency model, no therapeutic range. Not an opioid; no
MEAC, ke0, tPeak or potency ratio.

## Covariates
Gestational week (linear) or estradiol concentration (linear) on CL/F during pregnancy only; nothing else tested.

## Caveats
- Small (22 women), first trimester only (to 13 weeks); no later-pregnancy data, so the linear slope is not for
  extrapolation to term.
- Steady state assumed with convenience samples; mostly XR formulation; doses were adjusted during the study.
- Internal inconsistencies, both checked on the rendered pages: the Results text gives 26.4% unexplained
  variability for the gestational-week model where Table 3 gives RUV 23.2% (PDF p4 vs p5); the Discussion's
  "average increase in clearance by week 13 was 3.7L/hr" (PDF p5) matches the population CL/F *level* at
  13 weeks in Fig 2 (derived 3.69 L/h), not the increase (derived 1.5 L/h).
- No mixture model could be fitted (sample size).

## Verification against stanpumpR
- The registry row's numbers (2.79 L/h baseline, Emax 4.9 L/h, half-max at 12 weeks, factor 2.3) come from
  Karanam 2025 (Pharmacotherapy, PMID 39764656), as does the factor in `R/drugs_lamotrigine.R:103-105`. Nothing
  in the row is attributed to Karanam 2018 specifically, and nothing in it contradicts this paper. **matches**
  (supporting citation only).
- Comparator: Karanam 2018's increase is against the same women's preconception CL/F, a within-woman ratio,
  which is the kind of comparison the library's factor applies (to its nonpregnant model). This paper does not
  inform the third-trimester size of the factor (data stop at 13 weeks); its linear model at 13 weeks gives
  x1.68 of baseline (derived), consistent with a rise that continues toward the 2.3 used at 36 weeks.
- The registry's finding text does not mention this paper's distinctive result (onset by 5 weeks' gestation);
  that is an omission, not an error.
- **Independent check of the base model**: Karanam 2018 preconception CL/F 2.19 L/h (women, no interacting
  co-medication, mean 73.6 kg) vs the library's Milosheska 2016 CL/F 2.32 L/h (`R/drugs_lamotrigine.R:126`,
  mixed co-medication, 70 kg reference): 0.94x, i.e. close. The library header predicts its own model is a
  mixed-co-medication population; the two agree within Karanam's bootstrap interval (1.9-2.6 L/h).
- Not about this paper but seen in the same registry row: its `notes` field says the lamotrigine header still lists
  pregnancy under NOT OFFERED; `R/drugs_lamotrigine.R:38-40` now says pregnancy is applied, so that note is stale.
