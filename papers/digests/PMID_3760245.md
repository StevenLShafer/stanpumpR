# Blyden 1986: IV and oral diphenhydramine and N-demethyl metabolite

## Study
Ten healthy volunteers (six men, four women), 26-41 y, no other drugs; weights not reported. Two-way randomised crossover, at least 1 week apart: 50 mg diphenhydramine hydrochloride (43.75 mg base) IV (1 mL of the parenteral product infused over 5 min into an antecubital vein) and 50 mg orally, fasting. IV samples (contralateral arm, heparinised) at 5, 15, 30, 45 min, 1, 1.5, 2, 2.5, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10, 12, 15, 18, 24 h (some also 36, 48 h). Plasma assayed by GC with nitrogen-phosphorus detection (a longer column than Abernethy and Greenblatt's, separating parent from the N-demethyl metabolite DMDP, internal standard orphenadrine); DMDP measured in 7 of 10 subjects. IV data fitted to two or three exponentials with infusion correction (BMDP derivative-free nonlinear regression); oral terminal slope by log-linear regression, trapezoidal AUC.

Table II (printed p. 533, text layer) compares Vd / half-life / clearance (L/kg, h, mL/min/kg) across studies: Albert 1975 (n = 2) half-life 5.6, F 0.50; Carruthers 1978 (n = 6) 3.29, 3.3, 11.2, F 0.43; Berlinger 1982 (n = 5) 4.17, 4.1, 12.1, F 0.61; Meredith 1984 (n = 8) 8.04, 9.3, 9.8; this study 4.54, 8.4, 6.2, F 0.72.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No ke0, t1/2ke0, tPeak, EC50 or MEAC, and no effect measurements were made. Not an opioid; no potency ratio.

## Covariates
None analysed. DMDP AUC after IV dosing correlated with parent IV clearance (r = 0.79, P < .05, Figure 3).

## Caveats
Weights not reported, so mg-dose versus per-kg clearance cannot be reconciled from the paper. Parent and DMDP co-elute on standard GC columns, so assays presumed specific for diphenhydramine may be biased; the authors resolved them only with a longer column. DMDP apparent half-life matches the parent's, suggesting formation-limited elimination. The IV bolus was infused over 5 min and first sample was at 5 min; no compartment parameters are printed.
