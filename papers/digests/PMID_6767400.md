# Baaske 1980: nitroglycerin losses to filters, plastic bags and IV administration sets

## Study
In vitro (bench) study from the manufacturer's laboratory (Arnar-Stone). No patients, no pharmacokinetics.
PDF p1 is printed p201 (its top third is the end of the preceding article); PDF pN = printed 200+N.

- **Filters** (PDF p2): 250 mL of a 485.6 ug/mL solution (= 100%) through 142 mm, 0.2 um filters. Loss 55%
  (Gelman GA, cellulose triacetate), 5% (Millipore GS, cellulose acetate/nitrate), 2% (Gelman Tuffryn).
- **Containers** (PDF p2-3): investigational 0.5 mg/mL ampuls (potency printed as 47.8 ug/mL on PDF p2,
  a tenth of nominal, probably a misprint for 478; treated as 100%; nothing in the repo depends on it) added to D5W or 0.9% NaCl to a nominal 50 ug/mL in glass bottles or Viaflex (PVC) bags; stored at 4, 25,
  40 C and under light (35-37 C). Bag sizes 50-1000 mL at room temperature for 7 days.
- **Administration sets** (PDF p2, p4): four US sets (Travenol Minidrip, McGaw Pediatric AdditIV, Cutter Saftiset,
  Abbott Venoset) and four European (Baxter, Avon A-10, Avon A-11, Braun Intrafix), fed from a glass bottle at a
  nominal 100 ug/mL, 1 mL/min; effluent sampled initially and at 10, 20, 30, 45, 60, 120 min. Flow-rate study with the
  McGaw set at 0.5, 1.0, 2.0 mL/min.
- **Assay**: stability-indicating HPLC (218 nm), duplicate samples, isosorbide dinitrate internal standard.

## Model and parameters
No pharmacokinetic model. Bench results, read from the rendered pages:

- Glass bottles: no change over 48 h at 4, 25, 40 C or under light (PDF p3, text).
- Table 1 (PDF p3), PVC bags, nominal 50 ug/mL, ug/mL at initial / 24 h / 48 h:
  4 C D5W 45.1/28.5/25.4, saline 37.7/27.7/23.3; 25 C D5W 46.2/18.2/14.0, saline 39.1/22.9/16.7;
  40 C D5W 41.8/9.5/6.0, saline 39.7/16.8/12.5; light D5W 42.6/9.6/..., saline 40.6/10.0/....
- Table 2 (PDF p3), PVC bags at room temperature, 50 / 100 / 150 / 250 / 500 / 1000 mL:
  contact area per volume 2.72/1.87/1.26/0.98/0.73/0.59 cm2/mL; time zero 38.6/44.9/45.3/45.9/45.9/50.1 ug/mL;
  24 h 7.5/11.5/15.3/19.8/20.5/27.0; 48 h 4.5/7.7/11.4/14.6/19.2/22.0; 7 days 2.0/3.8/6.2/8.1/11.5/13.9;
  drug lost by 7 days 2.4/4.62/6.53/10.48/19.25/36.1 mg. Equilibrium not reached at 7 days.
- Table 3 (PDF p4), McGaw set, % of labelled 100 ug/mL delivered (decrease from initial value, %):
  0.5 mL/min first sample 51.9 (48.1), second sample (10-20 min, maximum loss) 25.7 (74.3), 2 h 36.5 (63.5);
  1.0 mL/min 73.3 (26.7), 50.3 (49.7), 71.3 (28.7); 2.0 mL/min 63.8 (36.2), 61.6 (38.4), 78.6 (21.4).
- Figure 1 (PDF p4): all eight sets at 1 mL/min fall to a nadir of roughly 45-72 ug/mL at 10-20 min, then rise slowly
  to roughly 60-90 ug/mL by 2 h (read from the figure).
- Abstract/conclusion: filter + plastic bag + set could reduce the concentration by as much as 80%; worst case
  (Gelman GA filter, 250 mL bag, set at 0.5 mL/min) under 20% of anticipated potency. This combined figure is the
  authors' projection from the separate experiments, not a measured combined run.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None (in vitro). No MEAC, ke0, tPeak or potency ratio (not an opioid paper).

## Covariates
Not applicable. Determinants of loss: filter material, PVC contact area per unit volume, temperature, diluent
(saline slower than D5W), time, and flow rate (slower flow, larger loss).

## Caveats
- Manufacturer's laboratory; the set results are for 1980 PVC sets and do not apply to non-sorbing (polyethylene /
  polyolefin) sets developed later.
- Initial bag concentrations were already below nominal (overfill, fast adsorption), so losses relative to time zero
  and to the theoretical 50 ug/mL differ.
- Flow-rate study used one set (McGaw); the 2.0 mL/min first-sample loss (36.2%) exceeds the 1.0 mL/min one (26.7%),
  so the flow dependence is not monotonic at the first sample.
- PDF p5 prints the flow rate as "0.5 mg/ml"; context shows mL/min is meant.

## Verification against stanpumpR
- "filters up to 55%": **matches** (Gelman GA 55%, PDF p2; other filters 2% and 5%).
- "bags ... > 50%": **matches** for PVC bags at 25 C and above within 24 h (Table 1: 46.2 to 18.2 ug/mL in D5W at
  25 C, a 61% fall from initial, derived) and for small bags (Table 2); not at 4 C over 48 h (45.1 to 25.4, 44%).
- "giving sets > 50%": **matches only at the slowest flow**: Table 3 maximum loss 74.3% at 0.5 mL/min, 49.7% at
  1.0, 38.4% at 2.0; Figure 1's sets at 1 mL/min lost roughly 30-55% at nadir. The repo line is Curry's summary and is
  fair as an upper figure.
- "up to 80% together": **matches** the abstract and conclusion (PDF p1, p5), noting it is a projection.
- "none from glass": **matches** (PDF p3).
- The context note calls the citation doubtful (secondary, no year); it is correctly attributed: the numbers are this
  paper's. No unit or transcription issue. The model assumes the full dose reaches the vein; this paper, like Kim 2018,
  supports the header's warning that PVC delivery lowers it.
