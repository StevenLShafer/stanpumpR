# Kerbusch 2000: population PK of ifosfamide autoinduction (NONMEM)

## Study
- 15 adults with soft-tissue sarcoma (9 M, 6 F), mean age 49 y (23-72), mean weight 59 kg (49-82) (PDF p3, printed 557).
- Single-agent ifosfamide as a 72-h continuous IV infusion every 4 weeks: first 6 patients 12 g/m² per 72 h; after unacceptable central neurotoxicity, the next 9 got 9 g/m² per 72 h. Individual total doses 10.3-23.5 g (PDF p2, printed 556). One patient's infusion stopped at 48 h for severe neurotoxicity (66% of planned dose).
- Co-medication: mesna, hydration, bicarbonate, antiemetics, methylene blue when indicated, plus a mean of 7 other drugs per patient; authors state none is a known CYP3A4 inhibitor or inducer.
- Sampling: whole blood at 0, 3, 10, 20, 24, 34, 48, 58, 68, 72, 73, 76, 80, 92 h; plasma assayed. 176 concentrations in total.
- Assay: GC with nitrogen-phosphorus detection after alkaline liquid-liquid extraction; validated 0.192-383 µM (accuracy 93.3-104.5%, intra-assay CV 0.8-5.5%, inter-assay 2.6-5.1%).
- Software: NONMEM V level 1.0, ADVAN6 TOL=5, first-order method; proportional IIV; combined additive + proportional residual error; POSTHOC individual estimates.

## Model and parameters
One-compartment model with an indirect-response "enzyme" compartment (Figure 2, Equations 1-4, PDF p3, printed 557):
- dA1/dt = R − CLapp·Cp; Cp = A1/V (A1 in µmol, R in µmol/h, Cp in µmol/L).
- CLapp = CL·A2 (CL = initial clearance; A2 = relative enzyme amount, 1 at t = 0).
- dA2/dt = Kenz,out − Kenz,out·A2·(1 − Cp/(Cp + IC50)) (Kenz,in = Kenz,out because A2(0) = 1). Ifosfamide inhibits enzyme degradation; no lag time.
- t½,enz = ln2 / Kenz,out.

- Individual CL range 2.22-4.96 L/h (PDF p5, Discussion).
- Model predicts on average a doubling of the relative enzyme amount over the first 24 h; Figure 5 shows A2 reaching about 3 by 72 h (PDF p5).
- Simulation (Figure 6, Table 2, PDF p6): doubling the dose from 10 to 20 g per 72 h raised AUC by only 56% (see open question on Table 2's units).
- Derived (not stated): elimination t½ before induction = 0.693 × 43.5 / 2.94 ≈ 10.3 h.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD model, no effect-site constant. Toxicity was clinical (neurotoxicity); the authors state neurotoxicity was not explained by parent-drug PK (the patient with severe neurotoxicity had the highest AUC 0-48 h but low CL 2.25 L/h and Kenz,out 0.0471 /h). Not an opioid paper; no MEAC, ke0 or potency ratio.

## Covariates
None tested (authors list covariates as future work). Dose basis is g/m², but the model parameters are absolute (L/h, L).

## Caveats
- n = 15, rich sampling; no validation (no data splitting, cross-validation or bootstrap), as the authors note.
- Comparison of initial CL (1.77 L/h/m²) is against Boddy 1995's breast-cancer patients (3.48 L/h/m², 5 g/m² over 24 h, lag-time model), not against a stanpumpR model.
- Table 2 AUC units are implausible (open question).
- Parent drug only; active and toxic metabolites not measured.
