# Hwang 2023: carvedilol population PK-PD and CYP2D6 in healthy Korean men

PDF pages equal the printed page numbers (1/13 to 13/13).

## Study
- Open-label, one-sequence, multiple-dose study (NCT02286934; design detailed in Jung 2018, ref 12), Seoul
  National University Bundang Hospital (p2, p5).
- n = 21 healthy men, all male (Table 1, p5): age 27.3 ± 4.7 y, height 174.2 ± 6.6 cm, weight 70.4 ± 9.6 kg,
  BMI 23.1 ± 2.4 (mean ± SD). CYP2D6 groups: EM (*1/*1, *1/*2) n = 6, weight 79.3 ± 3.9 kg; IM-1 (*1/*10, *2/*10)
  n = 7, 69.5 ± 10.3 kg; IM-2 (*10/*10) n = 8, 64.5 ± 7.4 kg. The paper reports no significant demographic
  difference between groups (p5), although mean weight falls from EM to IM-2.
- Dosing: single 12.5 mg oral carvedilol, then once-daily 25 mg (number of days not stated in this paper; the
  multiple-dose VPC, Fig. 2C, covers 168-192 h). Samples at 0, 0.25, 0.5, 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12 and 24 h after the
  single dose and after the multiple doses (p2). 450 plasma concentrations and 1003 heart-rate observations (p5).
- PD: isoproterenol sensitivity test (IST) at baseline (0.25, 0.5, 1, 2 μg) and after single and multiple
  carvedilol (5, 10, 20, 40 μg), injected until HR > 140 bpm or a 30 bpm rise (p2). Injected doses, not an
  infusion.
- Assay: not described in this paper (refers to ref 12). No statement that enantiomers were measured.
- NONMEM 7.4.4, FOCE-I; sequential PK then PD with post hoc individual PK (p3-4). 1,000-sample bootstrap.

## Model and parameters
Two compartments, zero-order absorption of duration D1 after a lag Tlag, first-order elimination (p5, Fig. 1).
First-order, lagged first-order and dual absorption were tried and "did not well describe" the data (p6). All
parameters are apparent (oral data only; no IV arm). IIV exponential on CL/F, Vc/F and Tlag (none on D1, Vp/F, Q);
proportional residual error. Table 2 (PDF p6, rendered):

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: isoproterenol-induced heart-rate rise, not resting HR or blood pressure. Baseline isoproterenol
  model: simple Emax on dose with γ fixed at 1 (direct effect, eq 3, p3, p6). Carvedilol enters as a competitive
  antagonist, ED50 × (1 + C/IC50) (eq 4, p3). Direct effect; no ke0, no effect compartment, no hysteresis term.
- Table 3 (PDF p10, rendered): E0 60.4 bpm (RSE 3.1%; bootstrap 60.4, 56.9-64.0); ED50 0.685 μg (30.9%; 0.709,
  0.387-1.512); Emax 30.7 bpm (21.9%; 31.7, 20.2-52.1); IC50 16.5 ng/mL (34.4%; 16.6, 9.3-39.4); ωE0 13.5% [0%
  shrinkage]; ωEmax 65.4% [8%]; additive error 64.2 (5.9%). IIV on E0 and Emax only.
- Labelling inconsistency in the paper: the text (p3) and Table 3 footnote call Emax the maximal effect "for HR
  reduction" or "of carvedilol", while equations 3-4 and the Discussion (p11) make it the maximum rise in HR caused
  by isoproterenol, 30.7 bpm above baseline. The equations govern.
- No covariate, including CYP2D6, on any PD parameter (p8). Discussion (p11) cites Tenero's S(-)-carvedilol
  half-maximal concentration of 7.7 ng/mL (a different analyte) and a flat response above 20 nmol/L S(-).
- Metabolite 4'-hydroxyphenyl carvedilol: 13 times parent beta-blocking potency at about 10% of parent
  concentration (Introduction, p2, citing refs 4-7); metabolic ratios "for most of subjects" below 0.1, so only
  the parent was modelled (p10).
- No MEAC, therapeutic range or potency ratio of the analgesic kind (not an opioid).

## Covariates
- CYP2D6 IM-2 on CL/F only: −32.8% (Results p6 and abstract), −32.7% (Discussion p10 and p11); table value 0.328.
  Adding it dropped ωCL/F from 26.8% to 14.6% (ΔOFV −27.50).
- Age, height and weight screened (power model normalised to the median) and not significant (p4, p6).

## Caveats
- 21 healthy young Korean men; narrow weight range. The authors contrast heart-failure models in which body
  weight, α1-acid glycoprotein, digoxin and smoking mattered (p10).
- The reported RSEs and bootstrap intervals disagree in both directions: Tlag (RSE 0.6%) and CL/F (0.7%) have
  bootstrap 95% intervals of roughly ±20% and ±13%; Vp/F (2.3%) has 553-992 L; while D1 (70%) has a fairly narrow
  interval, 0.330-0.515 h. The paper does not explain this; unresolved. The authors themselves treat D1's 70% RSE
  as signalling uncertainty (p9).
- ωTlag 56.8% with IIV on Tlag but none on D1.
- Racemate only; the enantiomer and assay used are not described here.
- Abstract and Results say 32.8%, Discussion 32.7%.
