# Pond 1985: methadone PK in methadone-maintained pregnant women

## Study
Longitudinal within-subject study, n = 9 healthy pregnant women on oral methadone maintenance for at least 2 months (mean 1.4 +/- 0.9 y), each studied in up to four phases: I = 20-34 wk gestation (mean 27 wk, n = 8), II = 35-40 wk (mean 37 wk, n = 9), III = 1-4 wk postpartum (mean 2 wk, n = 9), IV = 8-9 wk postpartum (n = 8). Home dose unchanged within the 24 h study; given as the hydrochloride in solution after an overnight fast. Blood sampled pre-dose and at 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12 and 24 h; plasma, EDTA, gas-liquid chromatography (two laboratories, r = 0.956 on 24 split samples); 24 h urine for methadone and metabolites; protein binding by equilibrium dialysis (4 h, 37 C) of the pre-dose sample. Total (bound plus unbound) plasma concentration. Total clearance = dose / AUC(0-24 h) (trapezoidal, steady state, so oral CL/F in effect; F not measured). Unbound clearance = dose / AUC of unbound. Statistics: one-way repeated-measures ANOVA, Scheffe test. Two breast-feeding subjects gave milk samples.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- No MEAC, minimum effective or analgesic concentration, no ke0, t1/2 ke0, hysteresis or equianalgesic/potency ratio reported. Methadone here is a maintenance (withdrawal-suppression) dose, not analgesia.
- Only effect measure: a symptom questionnaire (Horns 1975) completed pre-dose; mean scores 40 +/- 7, 44 +/- 8, 36 +/- 7, 38 +/- 5 in phases I-IV, N.S. (n = 6). In phase II three of nine women reported withdrawal symptoms 10-12 h after the dose, with pre-dose total plasma concentrations lower than postpartum.
- Time to peak concentration (total plasma, oral solution, fasting) 2.0-2.1 h in all phases (Table 1): an absorption tmax, not tPeak of an effect site.

## Covariates
Pregnancy phase versus the same women postpartum; weight, albumin and hematocrit shown in Table 1. No regression equations. Unbound fraction fell with advancing postpartum time (21% to 16%); albumin was lower in pregnancy.

## Caveats
n = 9; dose not constant across phases (changed in 7 of 9 women), so concentrations are also shown normalised; clearance is dose/AUC of an oral dose, so it is CL/F and a change in F cannot be excluded (authors argue absorption is unlikely to account for it). Total plasma (EDTA) assay, two laboratories. Phases are not the same as trimesters (phase I is 20-34 wk, II 35-40 wk). Authors conclude enhanced metabolism (higher ratio of pyrrolidine plus pyrroline metabolites to AUC).

## Verification against stanpumpR
- pregnancyRegistry.csv:10: the row says Pond 1985 "agree[s]" with the 1.71 factor and quotes a "weight-adjusted CL 0.17 to 0.21 L/h/kg across pregnancy". Direction **matches**: total clearance was higher in pregnancy than postpartum in the same women (1.65x absolute and 1.44x weight-adjusted for phase II vs IV, derived). Magnitude: 1.71 is slightly above Pond's phase II ratios and about 15% below Pond's phase I absolute ratio (2.0); consistent, third trimester (phase II) CL/F 1.44-1.65 is somewhat lower than 1.71. The quoted 0.17-0.21 L/h/kg **cannot be verified** from Pond (0.16 postpartum to 0.29 L/h/kg phase I, derived); see open question. Comparator in Pond is the same women 2-9 wk postpartum, on maintenance dosing, not Henthorn's non-pregnant volunteers that the library model is based on (R/drugs_methadone.R:324-327).
- Model parameter basis: the library plots total plasma racemic methadone; Pond is total plasma methadone, matching in assay matrix.
