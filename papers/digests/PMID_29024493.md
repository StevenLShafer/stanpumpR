# Aoyama 2017: population PK of oral meloxicam with CYP2C9 and lean body mass, plus a TXB2 PK/PD model

## Study
Population PK: reanalysis of Hasunuma 2016 data, 119 healthy men (30 Japanese, 30 Chinese, 29 Korean, 30 white), single
oral 7.5 mg meloxicam, single drug lot, matched meals. Samples pre-dose and 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 12, 24, 36, 48, 60, 72 h;
LC-MS/MS plasma (total) meloxicam. Age medians 24-31 y (range 21-35); weight medians 63.5-74.5 kg (range 51.0-100);
James LBM medians 52.5-59.8 kg (Table 1; overall median 55.0, range 43.6-75.2 kg, p. 826). CYP2C9: 95 *1/*1, 6 *1/*2,
1 *2/*2, 15 *1/*3 (derived: sum of the per-ethnicity counts, pp. 824-825). NONMEM 7.2 FOCE-I, PsN, 1000-sample bootstrap,
pc-VPC.
PK/PD: digitised mean data from Bae et al. (21 Korean men, 15 mg oral; CYP2C9 *1/*1 n = 12, *1/*13 n = 9), serum TXB2.

## Model and parameters
Two-compartment, parallel zero-order and first-order absorption: fraction F of the dose enters by zero-order over DT; the
rest by first-order ka after a lag tlag, with tlag fixed equal to DT (ΔOFV +49.9 for that constraint, accepted as
physiologically more plausible; p. 826). Enterohepatic recirculation model failed to converge. Proportional residual error.

Final model (Table 2 footnote): CLpop (L/h) = 0.391 x (1 - n*2 x 0.147 - n*3 x 0.400); Vcpop (L) = 7.79 x (LBM/55.0)^1.05.
LBM by the James formula. Model-predicted CL reductions vs *1/*1: 15% (*1/*2), 29% (*2/*2), 40% (*1/*3), 55% (*2/*3),
80% (*3/*3) (p. 827).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Indirect response model with inhibition of kin (Eq. 7) on percent serum TXB2 generation (COX-1 biomarker), fitted to
digitised Bae data with a separate PK fit (Table 3: CL 0.320 L/h *1/*1, 0.147 L/h *1/*13; Vc 5.24 / 3.38 L; Q 3.41 /
3.38 L/h; Vp 5.18 / 4.55 L; ka 5.01 / 5.27 /h; DT 2.99 / 2.96 h; F 0.653 / 0.713).
PD: kout 0.912 /h (%RSE 17.2), gamma 0.739, IC50 1390 ng/mL (total plasma meloxicam), kin = 100 x kout (Table 3).
No ke0, no analgesic concentration, no MEAC. Simulated 24 h time-weighted TXB2 generation at steady state, 7.5 mg/day:
60.5% (*1/*1) ... 31.8% (*3/*3) (p. 828).

## Covariates
CYP2C9 genotype on CL/F (per-allele proportional, Eq. 5); James LBM on Vc/F (power, centred 55.0 kg). No ethnic
difference. Age, weight, eGFR, albumin not retained (albumin dropped in backward step).

## Caveats
- Healthy young men only; one 7.5 mg dose; narrow age/weight ranges (authors: may hide weight/age effects on CL).
- Rare diplotypes (*2/*2 n = 1; no *2/*3 or *3/*3) extrapolated; predicted *3/*3 CL 0.0782 L/h exceeds an observed
  0.043 L/h in 3 Korean *3/*3 subjects (p. 831).
- External validation: model tends to underpredict external data (p. 827).
- Apparent parameters (F not estimated). PD built from digitised group means only.
