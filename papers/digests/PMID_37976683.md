# Liu 2024 (J Clin Anesth): ciprofol PK-PD in elective surgery

## Study
- Population PK on nine trials (Table 1, PDF p.2): three Phase 1 SAD studies in Australian healthy subjects (HSK3486-SAD n=35, SAD_02 n=25 women, SAD_03 n=12 men), Chinese healthy-subject studies -101 (n=18) and -103 (n=21, with and without rifampin), and elective-surgery studies -202 (n=23, Phase 2a), -204 (n=30, Phase 2), -302 (n=88, Phase 3) and -306 (n=86, Phase 3). 338 enrolled; 334 subjects with 3092 concentrations in the PK set (Table 2, p.5).
- PK-PD and exposure-response: only -204 (n=29 in the E-R set) and -306 (n=86): 115 surgical patients, 5640 BIS values. Doses: induction 0.4 mg/kg then maintenance 1.0 (+-0.5) mg/kg/h in -204; 0.4 mg/kg then 0.8 mg/kg/h, adjusted 0.1-0.4 mg/kg/h (max 2.4) in -306. Infusion titrated to BIS 40-60. BIS read every 2 min early, then every 5 min (Table 1).
- PK-PD cohort (Table 2, PDF p.5, medians): age 40 y (19-63), weight 58.6 kg (41.6-97.9), height 161 cm, 34 men (29.6%) and 81 women (70.4%), all Asian, all patients; venous sampling 89, arterial 25, both 1. PK cohort: age 33 y (18-64), weight 61.7 kg, height 165 cm, 150 men and 184 women, 79.6% Asian, 109 healthy volunteers and 225 patients.
- Venous and arterial plasma samples; assay not described in the text layer (total vs free not stated; ciprofol concentrations in ng/mL; lower limit of quantitation not stated here). Software: NONMEM 7.4.3, FOCE-I, PsN 4.2, R. Sequential PK-PD: PK parameters fixed, PD estimated (p.3).
- Not a two-stage fit: pooled nonlinear mixed-effects. Individual parameters are not printed; no concentration-time data in tables (pcVPC in Fig. 2 only).

## Model and parameters
Three compartments, first-order elimination from the central compartment (Fig. 1). Log-transformed concentrations, proportional (log-additive) residual error separate for vein and artery. Table 3 (PDF p.6, population estimate, %RSE) is the typical value for a 62 kg, 33-year-old male, VENOUS sample, SHORT-term-infusion study (study type = 0, reference); the covariate equations are eq. 12 (p.6).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: BIS. Effect-compartment inhibitory sigmoid Emax: BIS = E0 x (1 - Emax CE^gamma / (EC50^gamma + CE^gamma)), dCE/dt = ke0 (CA - CE) (eqs. 5-6, p.4). Emax fixed at 1 because protocols kept BIS at 40-60 and Emax was not estimable; a freely estimated Emax was 48.5 (lowest BIS 41.9, p.11). E0 = individual's observed baseline BIS.
- ke0 7.04 /h (Table 3, p.6) = 0.1173 /min (derived). t1/2ke0 = ln2/7.04 h = 5.9 min (derived). No tPeak is reported by the paper.
- (Derived by me, scipy matrix exponential, not in the paper) time to peak effect after a bolus with ke0 7.04 /h on the paper's own typical 62 kg, 33-year-old male, venous, long-infusion parameters: 2.94 min; on the short-infusion reference set: 2.82 min; with the arterial CL x1.23 on the long-infusion set: 2.78 min.
- EC50 582 ng/mL (effect-site, whether plasma total or free not stated), gamma 2.14, IIV 23.8% and 28.3%. Population: 115 Chinese elective-surgery patients. No covariate (sex, age, weight, baseline heart rate) on any PD parameter (p.6, Supplemental S3).
- Simulations (p.7-9, Figs. 3-4): induction 0.4 mg/kg over 30 s; time with BIS 40-60 highest (60.3%) at 0.8 mg/kg/h started 2 min after induction (36.5% at 0.6, 45.5% at 1.0); starting 2, 3, 4, 5 min after gave 60.3-60.8%. Authors conclude 0.8 mg/kg/h begun 4-5 min after induction.
- No MEAC, therapeutic range or equianalgesic ratio. Ciprofol is said to be about 4-5 times as potent as propofol (Introduction, p.2, citing other work). Not an opioid; opioid audit not applicable.
- Exposure-response (115 pts): no meaningful exposure relationship with efficacy or hypotension (41.7%), bradycardia (24.3%) or injection pain (6.1%) (Table 4, p.9); Cmax and AMTindu showed statistically significant but small (4-5 min) recovery-time associations.

## Covariates
Weight and arterial sampling and sex and study type on CL; age on V1; study type on V2, Q3, V3 (eq. 12). Not significant: AST, ALT, ALP, bilirubin, total protein, creatinine clearance, health status, race (p.10). Study type is a design artefact (long maintenance infusions vs single-dose induction studies), not a patient covariate; the authors say it most likely reflects study design (p.10).

## Caveats
- 79.6% Asian in the PK set, 100% in the PD set; PD data only in surgical patients titrated to BIS 40-60, so Emax cannot be estimated (authors' main stated limitation, p.11).
- High eta-shrinkage for V1, Q2, Q3, V3 (40.1-45.4%) and CL 31.1% (p.10); the short-infusion studies' small deep volume (V3 79.7 vs 436 L) is a study-type effect that the authors do not explain beyond design.
- Sponsor-funded (Haisco); several authors are employees.
- Typical baseline BIS, assay LLOQ and plasma vs blood are not stated in the main text.
