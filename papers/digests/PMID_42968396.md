# Rohilla 2026: propofol PK-PD across CKD stages 1-4

## Study
- Prospective, single centre (PGIMER Chandigarh, 2017-2020). 38 enrolled, 35 analysed (CKD stage 1 n = 10, stage 2 n = 11, stage 3 n = 8, stage 4 n = 6). Age 18-65 y (stage means 32-52 y), BMI 18-25, mean weight 55-67 kg; stage 4 all men (Table 1).
- Renal function: Cockcroft-Gault creatinine clearance, stage means 124, 77, 51 and 20 mL/min (Table 1).
- Elective urological surgery. Fentanyl 1-2 mcg/kg; propofol 1.5-2 mg/kg bolus, 200 mcg/kg/min for 10 min, then 100-150 mcg/kg/min titrated to BIS 40-50; atracurium.
- Samples: antecubital VENOUS blood, contralateral arm, plasma; at LOC, seven maintenance times, and at eye opening (the last sample). No samples after emergence.
- Assay: HPLC-fluorescence after a partial validation; LLOQ 0.062 mcg/mL; inter-day CV 11.6-12.7%.
- Pumas 2.2, FOCE-I; three compartments; BIS by a one-compartment effect site and sigmoid Emax.

## Verification against stanpumpR
- Not implemented as a model (`R/drugs_propofol.R`, section RENAL IMPAIRMENT). Against Eleveld (`propofol()`) at a 62 kg, 165 cm, 32 y man: Vc 6.21 vs 6.03 L agrees, but CL 4.46 vs 1.49 L/min is 3.0x higher, and the slowest half-life of the Table 4 parameters is 37 min. Both follow from venous sampling that ends at eye opening (no washout observed). Only clearance compares across sources, and it does not agree here.
- The Discussion compares the clearance with Schnider's as only slightly higher; Schnider's is about 1.9 L/min, so it is more than twice as high.
- Fig 7 (simulated plateau about 1.3 mcg/mL at 0.1 mg/kg/min) is consistent with CL 4.46. The simulated BIS of about 52 at that plateau is not: Table 5's parameters give about 83 at Ce 1.3 mcg/mL. The PD tables and figure do not reproduce each other.
- The ratio is physiologically plausible: (20/75)^0.19 = 0.78, within the kidney's reported share of propofol clearance (up to about 30%; Takizawa 2005, Hiraoka 2005, cited by the paper).
- Pregnant women were excluded; nothing for `inst/extdata/pregnancyRegistry.csv`.
