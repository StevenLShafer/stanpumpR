# Shafer 2018: letter on the PILLAR liposomal bupivacaine trial

## Study
A letter (PDF p. 1) criticising the statistical analysis of the PILLAR randomized trial of liposomal bupivacaine (Mont et al., J Arthroplasty 2018;33:90-96) after total knee arthroplasty. No new data.

Claims, as facts:
- Prespecified plan (Dysart 2016): 130 subjects/group, 90% power to detect a 0.3 difference in geometric mean total opioid dose (mg IV morphine equivalents), SD 0.670, 2-group t test, 0.025 two-sided alpha (Bonferroni for 2 coprimary end points).
- Mont et al. reported AUC12-48 of VAS pain 180.8 (SD 94.80) with liposomal bupivacaine vs 209.3 (78.97) without; LS mean difference -26.88, one-sided P = .0381. The letter says the two-sided P is .076, above 0.025, so the pain reduction was not significant.
- The plan tested total opioid consumption only if the pain comparison was significant; the paper tested it anyway.
- Authors' Reply (partial): the control arm is bupivacaine HCl infiltration and the experimental arm is liposomal bupivacaine in addition to it; both are FDA approved.
