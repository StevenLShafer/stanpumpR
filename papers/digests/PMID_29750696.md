# Kreienbuhl 2018: patient- vs clinician-controlled propofol sedation, meta-analysis

## Study
Systematic review and meta-analysis (searched to Oct 2017) of parallel-group RCTs comparing patient-controlled sedation (PCS) with clinician-controlled sedation (CCS), propofol the main sedative in both arms. 13 trials, 1103 patients (CCS 567, PCS 536), median age 47 y, ASA I-III, no children; median mean weight 75 kg (10 trials). Random-effects (DerSimonian-Laird), alpha 0.02 with 98% CI, trial sequential analysis (PDF p3-4, printed 875-876).

## Model and parameters
No pharmacokinetic model. Regimens in the Table (PDF p4 / printed 876): PCS boluses of 3-30 mg or 0.25-0.33 mg/kg, lockout 0 s-3 min, two trials with PCS plus background infusion (0.05 mg/kg/min), one patient-controlled effect-site TCI (0.8 +/- 0.1 ug/mL, Stonell). CCS: boluses, infusion (0.05-0.15 mg/kg/min or 2-8 mg/kg/h) or TCI 2 +/- 0.5 ug/mL.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No ke0, EC50 or concentration-effect data. Results (PDF p5-6 / printed 877-878):
- Desaturation: 31/448 PCS vs 46/481 CCS, RR 0.74 (98% CI 0.35-1.56).
- Rescue intervention: 29/449 vs 74/482, RR 0.45 (0.25-0.81).
- Hypotension: 21/320 vs 40/348, RR 0.56 (0.34-0.93); bradycardia 11/214 vs 12/237, RR 0.86 (0.35-2.09); neither reached trial-sequential information size.
- Oversedation (no response to loud repeated command): 68/407 vs 193/433, RR 0.37 (0.21-0.63).
- Propofol dose: median of means 122 mg PCS vs 126 mg CCS; WMD -21.8 mg (98% CI -44.3 to 0.73), not significant.
- Satisfaction (0-10 cm VAS): no difference for operator or patient.
GRADE quality very low for primary outcomes and dose.

## Covariates
None modelled; authors note patient response to propofol is not predicted by sex, age and weight alone and PCS typically uses a fixed bolus/lockout scheme.

## Caveats
Mostly high risk of bias, small trials; bolus-vs-infusion comparators differ across trials; the bolus-only subgroup did not confirm the hypotension or rescue results; few elderly or obese patients. Not an opioid paper.
