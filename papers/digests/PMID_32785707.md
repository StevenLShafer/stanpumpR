# Smit 2020: Gentamicin in real-world obese patients with renal (dys)function

## Study
Training set: all patients with BMI >= 25 given gentamicin at St Antonius Hospital (Oct 2017-Apr 2019; TDM data from the electronic record) plus the prospective AMIGO rich-sampling study (non-obese n = 8 at 5 mg/kg TBW, morbidly obese n = 20 at 5 mg/kg LBW; normal renal function; 10 samples each to 24 h). **1187 concentrations from 542 individuals**. External validation: Spaarne Gasthuis 2013-2018, **321 concentrations from 208 individuals**. Exclusions: renal replacement therapy, no weight within 6 months.

Training set (Table 1, median and range): age 69.5 (19.0-94.0) y; 347 M / 195 F; ICU 70 (13%); height 175 cm; BMI 29.3 (18.2-65.1); TBW 90.0 (53.3-220.5) kg; LBW 62.1 kg; BSA 2.1 m2; serum creatinine 96 (24-763) umol/L; indexed CKD-EPI 63.1 (5.1-141.7) mL/min/1.73 m2; **de-indexed CKD-EPI 77.3 (6.0-215.6) mL/min**; dose 360 mg (4.3 mg/kg). Validation: TBW 100 (68.6-180.4) kg, CKD-EPI 5.3-130.0.

Assay: immunoassay (Roche training, LLOQ 0.4 mg/L; Abbott validation, LLOQ 0.5 mg/L); BLQ by M3. NONMEM 7.4.3, Laplacian; 1-, 2-, 3-compartment models. Covariates screened: TBW, LBW, ABW, BSA, creatinine, MDRD, CKD-EPI, Cockcroft-Gault, age, sex, ICU (time-varying). **De-indexing: CKD-EPI x BSA/1.73.** The CKD-EPI and BSA formulas are in supplementary Tables S1-S2 (not read); the main text does not say which CKD-EPI version.

## Model and parameters
Two-compartment model. Table 2 (final model, RSE%; bootstrap median and 95% CI):

CL (L/h) = TVCL x (CKD-EPIdi / 74) x F_IC^ICU; V1 (L) = TVV1 x (TBW / 70).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD model. Target for dosing: median AUC0-24 of lean (TBW < 100 kg, CKD-EPI > 60, non-ICU) patients given 6 mg/kg TBW = **85.9 mg.h/L**, equivalence 80-125%. Target trough < 1 mg/L (drives the interval, Fig. S8). Table 3 dose nomogram for obese (> 100 kg): 6, 4.8, 3.6, 2.4, 1.8 mg/kg TBW for CKD-EPI > 120, 90-120, 60-90, 30-60, < 30; interval 24 h, extended to 24-36 and 36-48 h below 60 and 30; consider 25% reduction in ICU.

## Covariates
De-indexed CKD-EPI (linear, on CL) chosen over indexed CKD-EPI + TBW (similar fit, but correlated); ICU admission on CL (factor 0.751); TBW on V1 (linear).

## Caveats
Retrospective TDM data with sparse sampling (median 1 sample per patient in TDM cohorts); renal replacement excluded; obese-dominated population (BMI >= 25 for TDM patients); CKD-EPI version and BSA formula only in the supplement. High V1 shrinkage (55%).
