# Thoueille 2023: lopinavir population PK in COVID post-exposure prophylaxis

## Study
Population PK of lopinavir (LPV, with ritonavir, 400/100 mg twice daily) in 105 HIV-negative adults exposed to SARS-CoV-2 (COPEP trial; one dried-blood-spot sample each, taken on day 5 of 5-day prophylaxis) plus 170 sparse plasma samples from 119 people living with HIV from therapeutic drug monitoring (Swiss HIV Cohort, 2010-2022). All assumed at steady state. LC-MS/MS assay; NONMEM 7.4.3, R 4.0.2. DBS blood concentrations converted to plasma: Cplasma = CDBS x FBP/(1 - HCT), with protein binding 98.5% and haematocrit assumed 0.4 (women) or 0.45 (men) because it was not collected (p2). Samples below 1000 ng/mL (12) were removed as non-adherence. COPEP: 45 male/60 female, age 39 y (17-67), weight 73 kg (47-157); PLWH: 50 male/69 female, age 41 y (19-78), weight 66 kg (40-147) (Table 1, p3; text layer).

## Model and parameters
One-compartment, first-order absorption and elimination, parameters apparent (oral, F unknown). Table 2 (PDF p4), final model with bootstrap (n=2000):

Covariate equation (Table 2): CL = CL_typical x (1 + 0.447 x (BW - 70)/70). Text: 19% higher CL at 100 kg than 70 kg (p5); allometric scaling fitted equally well, linear kept for simplicity. Derived by the authors from the base model: Tmax 3.9 h, t½ 13.5 h (p6). CL IIV shrinkage 37%. Population type (COPEP vs PLWH) was not significant on any parameter.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Not an opioid; no ke0, MEAC, tPeak or potency ratio. No concentration-effect relationship was found: model-predicted day-5 Cmin (trough) versus a positive SARS-CoV-2 test at day 21, ANOVA p = 0.142 (p5-6, Fig 3).

## Covariates
Body weight on CL (linear, reference 70 kg). Age, sex, height, BMI, smoking and population type tested and not retained. Concomitant medications not analysed.

## Caveats
Single DBS sample per COPEP participant, often close to dosing; haematocrit assumed; self-reported dosing times; high shrinkage; ka bootstrap CI very wide (0.37-66.41); published models (Alvarez, Niu, Fuchs, Dickinson, Lubomirov, Moltó) validated on these data with MPE -19.4% to 8.0%, RMSE 3.4-49.5% (p3-5, details in Table S1, not read). Apparent clearance and volume, so not comparable per kg or to IV values.
