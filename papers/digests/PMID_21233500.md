# Liu 2011: dual closed-loop propofol-remifentanil on BIS

## Study
Randomized multicentre (4 French hospitals) trial, 196 randomized, 167 analysed (83 dual-loop, 84 manual; 29 excluded, Fig. 2). Adults 18-90 y, ASA I-IV, elective surgery under general anaesthesia (some combined with regional) expected to last over 30 min with tracheal intubation. Exclusions: psychiatric illness, supraspinal neurological disorders, cranial neurosurgery, pacemaker.
- Both arms: TCI with Schnider (propofol) and Minto (remifentanil) models in Infusion Toolbox 95 v4.11, effect-site targets, BIS set point 50 (acceptable 40-60). No assay: concentrations are model-calculated.
- Manual arm: clinician-chosen targets, no limits.
- Dual-loop arm: clinician picks the initial propofol Ce; controller sets initial remifentanil Ce (propofol Ce below 2.5 ug/mL gives 4 ng/mL; 2.5-2.9 gives 5; above 3 gives 6; printed p.547). Then a cascade of two PID controllers adjusts the effect-site targets within limits propofol 1.3-5 ug/mL and remifentanil 3-12 ng/mL (p.547).
- Primary outcome: global score = (MDAPE + wobble) / % of time BIS 40-60 (lower is better).

## Model and parameters
No new pharmacokinetic model. Controller details and results (numbers confirmed on rendered pages):
- Gain constants K by BISerror (printed Table 1, PDF p.3): propofol 50 at -10 to -8, 65 at -7 to -4, infinite at -3 to 3, 90 at 4 to 7, 60 at 8-9, 50 at 10 or feedforward; remifentanil 80 at -10 to -4, 65 at -3 and -2, infinite at -1 to 1, 90 at 2-3, 70 at 4-9 (the table prints 70 at 4-7 and 70 at 8-9), 40 at 10 or feedforward. New target = current target x (1 + BISerror/K).
- Minimum interval between target changes = time to peak effect for each drug, plus 60 s in maintenance. Propofol delay varies linearly from 96 s (age 20) to 120 s (age 80); remifentanil nonlinearly from 80 s (age 20) to 151 s (age 80), both from the PK models (Appendix, printed pp.554-555).
- Table 3, induction (manual vs dual-loop, mean +/- SD; PDF p.5): duration 345 +/- 166 vs 289 +/- 122 s (P 0.01); propofol dose 1.3 +/- 0.7 vs 1.3 +/- 0.6 mg/kg; initial propofol Ce 3.1 +/- 1.2 vs 3.1 +/- 0.5 ug/mL; maximal propofol Ce 4.1 +/- 1.6 vs 4.0 +/- 0.9 ug/mL; remifentanil dose 1.7 +/- 0.8 vs 2.3 +/- 1.2 ug/kg; initial remifentanil Ce 4.0 +/- 1.8 vs 5.4 +/- 1.2 ng/mL; maximal remifentanil Ce 5.3 +/- 1.9 vs 7.5 +/- 2.6 ng/mL (all three remifentanil P < 0.0001).
- Table 4, maintenance (manual vs dual-loop; PDF p.6, printed 551): duration 150 +/- 81 vs 140 +/- 78 min; propofol 5.0 +/- 1.6 vs 4.7 +/- 1.6 mg/kg/h (P 0.32), mean Ce 2.3 +/- 0.6 vs 2.4 +/- 0.7 ug/mL (P 0.37); remifentanil 0.16 +/- 0.07 vs 0.22 +/- 0.07 ug/kg/min (P < 0.0001), mean Ce 4.8 +/- 1.6 vs 6.1 +/- 1.4 ng/mL (P < 0.0001); time in BIS 40-60 71 +/- 19% vs 82 +/- 12%; global score 43 +/- 40 vs 26 +/- 11; MDAPE 15.0 vs 11.4; wobble 9.2 vs 8.7 (P 0.283); burst suppression in 27 (32%) vs 9 (10%) patients; extubation 11 +/- 5 vs 10 +/- 4 min (P 0.02) (the abstract prints 11 +/- 5 vs 10 +/- 4 as well).
- Subgroup with combined general/regional anaesthesia: remifentanil use fell in the manual arm 0.17 to 0.12 ug/kg/min (P 0.003), not significantly in dual-loop (printed p.552).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: BIS (EEG), set point 50; the paper treats BIS 40-60 as adequate hypnosis. Analgesia is not measured directly; the authors note no specific analgesia monitor exists and cannot judge whether remifentanil was optimal.
- Opioid audit (remifentanil): no MEAC / minimum effective or analgesic concentration reported. The mean model-calculated remifentanil Ce during BIS-guided maintenance was 4.8 ng/mL (manual) and 6.1 ng/mL (dual-loop) (Table 4), both effect-site, Minto model-calculated (no assay), alongside propofol Ce about 2.3-2.4 ug/mL; these are the concentrations the clinicians or controller reached at BIS about 46-47, not thresholds defined by the authors. No ke0 / t1/2ke0 is stated; tPeak is used only implicitly as the controller's dwell time, and only as delays of 80-151 s (remifentanil) and 96-120 s (propofol) from the models (Appendix). No equianalgesic or potency ratio.
- Hemodynamic responses (heart rate, systolic pressure, BIS) to laryngoscopy and incision did not differ between arms (Fig. 6, subset of 31-49 patients).

## Covariates
Age sets the controller delay (above). Sex, age, weight and height go into the Schnider and Minto models. No covariate analysis of results.

## Caveats
Authors list: long induction (interquartile 3.4-6.1 min), higher remifentanil use in dual-loop, 29 dropouts partly from the prototype's computer failures, unblinded multicentre design with variable care, comparison against clinician TCI not a fixed-dose design. Table 4 prints the interquartile range of remifentanil increment as "78-1.07" (a missing decimal point, probably 0.78). Text states undershoot as BIS above 60 but Table 3 labels BIS above 70.
