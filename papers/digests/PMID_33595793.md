# Würthwein 2021: PopPK of PEG-asparaginase in children with ALL

## Study
AIEOP-BFM ALL 2009 trial (German and Czech centres), children aged 1 to under 18 years with newly diagnosed ALL. PEG-asparaginase (Oncaspar) 2500 U/m2 per dose (maximum 3750 U) as a 2 h IV infusion: two doses in induction (day 12 and day 26), and for non-high-risk patients a third in reinduction (about 18 weeks after the last induction dose). Serum asparaginase activity measured weekly, scheduled at 7 and 14 days after each dose; LLOQ 5 U/L (activity in U/L, not mass). Samples after hypersensitivity, with "silent inactivation" (< 100 U/L within 8 days and/or undetectable within 15 days) or implausible rises were excluded (PDF p.3).
Datasets: Model Building Dataset 1374 patients (6069 samples >= LLOQ, 115 < LLOQ); Testing Dataset 1253 patients (5523 samples, 107 < LLOQ); Final Dataset 2545 patients, 11,486 samples (11,707 incl. 221 < LLOQ, 1.9%, omitted) (PDF pp.4, 8).
Table 1 (PDF p.4), median (range), Model Building / Testing: sex M/F 779/595 and 741/512; age 5.13 (1.03-17.9) and 5.06 (1.03-18.0) y; height 113 (71-195) and 112 (71-199) cm; weight 19.55 (8.5-128) and 19 (7.7-118) kg; BSA 0.78 (0.41-2.42) and 0.77 (0.39-2.44) m2 (Mosteller); dose 1940 (710-4850) and 1900 (620-5300) U, 2494 (902-4559) and 2492 (984-4988) U/m2.
Software NONMEM 7.3.0/7.4.4, FOCE-I, PsN, Pirana, R, SAS. Covariate selection forward alpha 0.05, backward 0.01; bootstrap 1000 runs (92.9% successful); pcVPC; external validation in the Testing Dataset; Bayesian forecasting with artificially deleted samples (PDF pp.3-4).

## Model and parameters
Empirical transit model (Fig. 1, PDF p.1, journal p.289): a chain of compartments (14 shown, "1 2 3 ... 14"), each with first-order elimination CLinitial and transfer Qtr between them; the last compartment has CLinitial + Qtr, i.e. CLinduced is set equal to Qtr (simplified from the 2017 model, Würthwein 2017). Clearance therefore rises with time after dose (de-PEGylation). Observed activity is serum activity; BSA is a covariate in the base model (F_BSA, linear, centred at 0.79 m2).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD model, ke0 or exposure-response analysis. The paper's derived exposure measures are AUC0-inf and time above 100, 250, 500 and 1000 U/L of serum activity per treatment phase; it cites 100 U/L as the level considered sufficient for complete asparagine depletion (Discussion, PDF p.9) and mentions 400 U/L in the discussion of non-parametric comparisons. Opioid audit: not applicable (not an opioid paper).

## Covariates
BSA (linear, centred 0.79 m2) on V, CLinitial and Qtr; treatment phase (second induction dose, reinduction) on V and CLinitial; age above 8 y (linear increase in CLinitial); female sex (-7.1% CLinitial). Body weight and BSA correlated (r = 0.988) so only BSA retained. Equations in Table 3 footnotes (PDF p.6).

## Caveats
Only 0.9% of samples were taken in days 0-5, so peak levels and early V are poorly informed; slight under-prediction of high activities and of day 14 levels after the second induction dose (PDF pp.5, 8). Individual predictions of a deleted sample were within +-20% for only 52-65% of samples (residual error 18.9%); AUC0-inf was predicted within +-20% for > 91% of scenarios. Patients with silent inactivation or hypersensitivity were excluded, so the model describes the normal elimination process only. The authors attribute the treatment-phase difference in V to statistical description (V in reinduction median 44.4 mL/kg, close to plasma volume); Kloos 2020's model (comparison in PDF p.9) has V 0.94 L/m2 and smaller phase effects. Authors disclose funding and consultancy ties to the marketing-authorisation holders.
