# Shoushtarian 2016: CI, CCS, BIS and CVI under propofol-remifentanil

## Study
Reanalysis of the raw EEG of the Sahinovic 2014 study (see `PMID_24892803`; NCT01053611). The BIS 30 groups were dropped because of burst suppression. 80 patients were eligible (BIS 50 or BIS 70 targets x remifentanil 0, 2, 4, 6 ng/mL, n = 10 per group); 78 analysed (p385). 23 were responders and 55 nonresponders. Propofol: closed-loop BIS-guided, effect-site control with the Schnider model, then open-loop TCI at the achieved Ce. Remifentanil: Minto model, effect-site targets 0/2/4/6 ng/mL (p383). Infusions ran 17.5 min, OAA/S at 18 min, tetanic stimulus (100 Hz, 60 mA, 30 s) at 19 min. Note the methods text also says a "50-mA" stimulus once (p384) while giving 60 mA elsewhere. Indices: BIS, CVI, and the Liley-model EEG indices CCS (cortical state; 0 to -2, more negative = more hypnosis) and CI (cortical input, in uV).

## Model and parameters
No PK model fitted; propofol (Schnider) and remifentanil (Minto) were used only to drive TCI.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- Opioid audit (remifentanil): no MEAC or analgesic concentration reported, no ke0, t1/2ke0, tPeak or hysteresis reported, and no potency or equianalgesic ratio. Remifentanil levels are predicted Ce targets (Minto), not assayed.
- Baseline, remifentanil vs none: CI Cohen's d 0.65 (0.48-0.83), P = 0.0217; CVI d 0.72 (0.56-0.88), P = 0.0034 (p386). Neither showed a concentration-dependent relation across 2, 4, 6 ng/mL. Authors suggest PK-model variability or a ceiling effect (about 2 ng/mL, citing earlier work) (p389).
- Baseline CI at BIS 70 (Table 2, p386, mean (SD) uV): 4.47 (0.72), 3.24 (0.86), 2.79 (0.84), 3.01 (0.97) for REMI 0/2/4/6. At BIS 50: 4.31, 4.17, 4.11, 4.47. Baseline CVI at BIS 70: 2.55, 2.09, 2.33, 2.01.
- CCS vs BIS correlation: R2 = 0.55 baseline, 0.77 response, 0.68 combined (Fig 2, p387). CI vs BIS R2 = 0.07 baseline and 0.01 response.
- Responder vs nonresponder (Table 3, p387), Pk: baseline CCS 0.660, CI 0.594, BIS 0.725, CVI 0.743. Response period CCS 0.796, CI 0.769, BIS 0.791, CVI 0.880. Delta CCS 0.792, CI 0.825, BIS 0.726, CVI 0.813.
- Pairwise k-means at T2 (Table 5, p389): CI+CCS sensitivity 75.8% (47.1-90.0), specificity 52% (34.4-67.2); CVI+BIS sensitivity 42%, specificity 24%. The abstract's intervals differ from the table's (99% CI, abstract 52.7-98.8 vs Table 5 47.1-90.0); not reconciled. AUROC 0.807 (CI+CCS) and 0.786 (CVI+BIS).

## Covariates
None modelled; hypnotic level (BIS 50 vs 70) affected the stimulus-induced change in every index (p386).

## Caveats
Responder definition mixes the OAA/S and tetanic responses, so T2 classification may reflect response to OAA/S rather than the tetanus. Artifact rejection removed about 30% of epochs. Dose-independent behaviour of CI/CVI limits use as an opioid-concentration index. Authors affiliated with Cortical Dynamics Ltd. (CI/CCS developer).
