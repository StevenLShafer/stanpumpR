# Daher Abdi 2014: capecitabine and metabolites in patients 75 years and over

## Study
CAPAGEC (NCT00812864), single centre, Limoges: 20 patients aged 75-92 (median 80.5) with metastatic breast (5) or
colorectal (15) cancer, oral capecitabine 1250 mg/m2 twice daily for 14 days every 3 weeks, up to 6 cycles. PK on
cycle 1 day 1 and cycle 2 day 14: pre-dose and 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2, 4, 6, 8 h. Pooled with 40 younger adults (30-73 y)
from two phase I studies (Urien 2005; capecitabine 1400-2300 mg/m2/day every 12 h, combined with irinotecan or
irofulven; PK days 1 and 15). LC-MS/MS for capecitabine, 5'-DFUR, 5-FU, FBAL; calibration 6-27,828 nmol/L
(capecitabine), 41-20,309 (5'-DFUR), 77-7,688 (5-FU), 19-93,385 (FBAL); 5'-DFCR only in the phase I patients, FBAL
only in CAPAGEC. NONMEM 7.2, **FO** method; 1000-sample bootstrap. 2213 concentrations (p. 4, printed 1288).

Table 1 (PDF p. 4), phase I vs CAPAGEC, median [range]: n 40 / 20; female/male 15/25 vs 12/8; age 54.5 [30-73] vs
80.5 [75-92]; weight 68 [41-95] vs 74 [48-113] kg; BSA 1.80 [1.40-2.10] vs 1.82 [1.41-2] m2; total bilirubin 8.8
[3-22] vs 5.1 [2-13.3] umol/L; CLCR (Cockcroft-Gault) 71 [28-115] vs 66 [23-137] mL/min.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Not an opioid: no MEAC, ke0, tPeak or potency ratio. Exposure-effect in the 20 CAPAGEC patients only:
- Cycle 2 AUCs of capecitabine, 5'-DFCR, 5'-DFUR and 5-FU (not FBAL) higher in patients with grade 2-3 hand-foot
  syndrome (p = 0.012, 0.031, 0.0064, 0.0090; Fig. 2); cycle 1 AUCs not associated (only 2 HFS events then).
- No AUC difference for grade >=2 diarrhoea, or between responders/stable and treatment failure.
- No concentration threshold or target range reported. AUC linear with dose (r2 0.53 in >=75 y, 0.41 in younger).

## Covariates
Final model: age group (>=75 y) on ka (x0.45) and PK occasion on k40 (x0.77). Univariate only (not retained):
bilirubin on k34, BSA and occasion on CL10, age group and occasion on CL12, sex and occasion on k40. CLCR, weight
not significant. Both retained covariates' bootstrap intervals include 1 (0.31-2.42; 0.56-1.15).

## Caveats
- Small (20 elderly); age group is confounded with study, dose, schedule and combination therapy (the authors say an
  age effect cannot be told from a study effect).
- FO estimation; very large ISV on tlag (148%) and V1 (107%); IOV on ka 129%.
- No metabolite volumes, so metabolite concentrations need Urien 2005's scaling convention (not in this paper).
- Typical capecitabine disappearance (CL10+CL12)/V1 = 0.78 /h (derived), far slower than 5'-DFUR's own k34 6.0 /h:
  5'-DFUR decline is formation-limited in this model.
- 5'-DFCR measured only in the young group, FBAL only in the old.
