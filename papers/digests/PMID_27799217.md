# Aouri 2017: rilpivirine population PK and pharmacogenetics in HIV patients

## Study
- Observational TDM data from adults on rilpivirine 25 mg once daily (with emtricitabine and tenofovir DF as one tablet), several Swiss centres, April 2013 to January 2015. 249 patients, 325 plasma samples (1-4 per patient, mostly one), drawn 1.25-36 h after the last dose at steady state (at least 4 weeks on treatment). Concentrations 12-255 ng/mL.
- Assay: LC-MS/MS of plasma after protein precipitation, LLOQ 5 ng/mL (total concentration).
- Table 1 (PDF p3, text layer): 72.7% male; median age 46 y (22-80); weight 74 kg (42-112); height 175 cm (150-198); 51.4% Caucasian. 119 patients consented to genotyping (CYP3A4*22, CYP3A5*3, CYP2C19*2 and *17, UGT1A1*28, UGT1A4*2).
- NONMEM 7.1.0, FOCE-I. Structures tested: 1- and 2-compartment, first- and zero-order absorption. Bootstrap 2,000 samples (PsN); VPC with 1,000 simulated subjects; external validation on 33 samples from 31 other patients.

No IIV on V or D1 improved the fit. Text states a mean absorption time of 2 h (derived, = D1/2 for a zero-order input; 4 h is the duration). Simulations (PDF p4-5): steady-state 24-h trough at 25 mg QD averages 69 ng/mL (95% PI 33.5-125.6); 50 mg QD gives a median 140 (PI 39-446); 25 mg BID gives a median 167 (PI 59-406).
External validation: population prediction bias 4% (95% CI -2 to 12), imprecision 22%; individual prediction bias -2% (-5 to 8), imprecision 12%.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD model and no ke0. Targets cited from other sources (not estimated here): trough of 50 ng/mL, associated with virological success in ECHO/THRIVE (Cmin below 44 ng/mL, first quartile, had about twice the virological failures of above 123 ng/mL); protein-adjusted in vitro IC90 of 12 ng/mL; QTc prolongation (>10 ms) above about 500 ng/mL at 3-12 times the dose. All are total plasma concentrations. Model prediction: 29% of patients on 25 mg QD fall below 50 ng/mL at trough; 2.4% at 50 mg QD; none at 25 mg BID; all concentrations stayed above 12 ng/mL and below 500 ng/mL in every regimen. Not an opioid; no MEAC or potency ratio.

## Covariates
None retained. Sex: females had 13% lower CL (95% CI 3-25%), delta OFV -5.3, not significant at the 7.88 threshold. No genotype improved the fit (delta OFV above -3.05). Also tested: weight, height, age, race, AST, ALT, HCV and HBV status, comedication.

## Caveats
Food intake and adherence were not recorded, and both are known to change exposure. Mostly one sample per patient, so inter- and intra-subject variability are hard to separate. Most data fall within 28 h of dosing, so terminal redistribution is not captured: the authors' half-life (24 h) and V (401 L) are lower than the manufacturer's 45 h and 1,064 L (two-compartment). Genetic study powered only for marked effects. Plasma, total concentration.
