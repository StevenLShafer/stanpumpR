# Lee 2018: deep learning prediction of BIS during propofol-remifentanil TCI

## Study
Retrospective registry study, Seoul National University Hospital, general surgery June-September 2016. 1,223 registry cases; 417 TIVA; 231 kept after exclusions (volatile agent added, BIS <80 at start, >300 s data loss, cumulative volume not 0 at first BIS, pump reset). Split: 101 training, 30 validation, 100 testing (PDF p. 4, printed 496). No premedication. Effect-site TCI of propofol (Schnider or modified Marsh, clinician's choice) and remifentanil (Minto) by Orchestra Base Primea pump. Induction targets: propofol 3-5 ug/mL, remifentanil 4-6 ng/mL. Propofol titrated to BIS 40-60. Rocuronium given. Data at 1 s: BIS (VISTA), infused volumes and pump-calculated Ce. No blood samples or concentration assays.

Table 1 (printed p. 497, mean ± SD (range); training / validation / testing):
- Age yr: 58±14 (18-87) / 53±16 (20-85) / 55±14 (23-85). Sex M/F: 44/57, 15/15, 39/61.
- Weight kg: 61±9 (41-88) / 62±10 (47-88) / 62±11 (38-92). Height cm: 161±8, 163±10, 162±8.
- Anaesthesia duration h: 2.6±1.7 / 2.6±1.7 / 2.5±1.6. Median BIS: 44±6, 44±6, 44±5.
- Propofol total dose g: 1.0±0.6, 1.1±0.8, 1.1±0.7; median Ce 3.0±0.5, 3.1±0.4, 3.1±0.5 ug/mL.
- Remifentanil total dose mg: 1.3±1.3, 1.5±1.4, 1.2±1.1; median Ce 3.8±1.0, 4.0±0.9, 3.8±1.0 ng/mL.

## Model and parameters
Empirical model, not a PK/PD model: an LSTM layer (8 memory cells per drug, input the preceding 1,800 s of infusion history as 10-s doses, 180 points; dose divided by 12 for normalisation) feeds a feed-forward net (16 hidden nodes) together with age, sex, weight, height; output BIS/100. Trained with ADAM in Keras. Hyperparameters chosen by 5-fold cross-validation over 18 combinations. Final training error 6.23 and validation error 6.46 BIS units. Weights are in the supplement and at osf.io/d8gs2.
Comparator: response-surface model of Short 2016 (based on Bouillon 2004 hierarchy), using pump Ce from the Schnider or modified Marsh propofol model and Minto remifentanil.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point is BIS only. No ke0, t½ke0, tPeak, EC50, Emax or gamma are reported, and no MEAC or potency ratio. The authors state these cannot be identified in the network. The remifentanil input is a dose history (pump-driven by the Minto model), not an analgesic end point.
Performance, testing set (Table 2, printed p. 497, mean ± SD; deep learning vs response surface):
- MDPE %: All -0.1±11.9 vs 25.0±17.6; Induction -3.1±14.6 vs -15.7±15.3; Maintenance 1.1±13.1 vs 28.2±19.1; Recovery -5.3±14.9 vs 19.9±21.0.
- MDAPE %: All 13.9±5.3 vs 28.5±14.3; Induction 15.2±7.3 vs 22.6±8.9; Maintenance 14.1±6.5 vs 30.2±16.8; Recovery 14.8±7.8 vs 23.7±16.8.
- RMSE: All 9±2 vs 15±4; Induction 11±4 vs 19±6; Maintenance 9±2 vs 14±5; Recovery 11±6 vs 15±6.
- Concordance correlation coefficient 0.561 (0.560-0.562) vs 0.265 (0.263-0.266); Pearson 0.622 vs 0.378; bias correction factor 0.902 vs 0.701 (p. 496).
Performance error is defined as (measured BIS - predicted BIS)/predicted BIS (p. 496), so a positive MDPE means measured BIS above predicted.

## Covariates
Age, sex, weight, height enter only the feed-forward (PD) part. No equations; effects are not interpretable.

## Caveats
Authors: not interpretable; depends on training data and extrapolates poorly (obesity, other infusion schemes not covered); residual serial correlation; retrospective with possible carrier-fluid or line problems. Added by this digest: single centre, adults, mean weight about 62 kg, propofol-remifentanil TCI only, no effect-site drug concentrations measured, so the response-surface comparator is driven by pump-predicted Ce that may itself be the error source. The Table 2 response-surface MDPE is strongly positive (+25%) while the earlier Short 2016 value quoted in the Discussion is 8±24%.
