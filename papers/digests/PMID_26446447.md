# Bergmann 2016: clopidogrel-paclitaxel interaction, case report

## Study
Single-patient case report plus an in vitro experiment. The patient was a 60-year-old woman with FIGO stage IIIc ovarian carcinoma, the only clopidogrel user (dose unknown) among 93 patients in a previously reported paclitaxel PK cohort (paclitaxel 175 mg/m2 with carboplatin AUC 5-6 mg.min/mL every 3 weeks, 6-9 courses). Genotype CYP2C8*1/*3. Comedication: metoclopramide, ondansetron, lisinopril, hydrochlorothiazide, salicylic acid, citalopram, simvastatin, paracetamol, tramadol, esomeprazole. Clopidogrel was measured in all three of her stored plasma samples (LC-MS/MS), so non-compliance was excluded. In vitro: human liver microsomes (2 mg/mL) with NADPH 1 mM, preincubated 30 min with 100 uM clopidogrel acyl-beta-D-glucuronide or control, then 0.5 uM paclitaxel for 1 h; paclitaxel and 6alpha-hydroxypaclitaxel by LC-MS/MS; triplicates. Time-dependent depletion constants over 0-30 min; 6alpha-hydroxypaclitaxel formation at 7.5 min.

Cohort toxicity (p2): 18% hospitalised, 17% stopped paclitaxel for toxicity; grade 3 neuropathy 17%, nausea 2%, vomiting 4%.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD model, no ke0, no concentration-effect relationship. Not an opioid. The only effect measures are clinical: grade 3 neuropathy (CTCAE) led to stopping paclitaxel after four cycles; grade 3 nausea and vomiting caused three hospitalisations. The authors cite (ref 1) low paclitaxel clearance as associated with neuropathy. The 77% in vitro CYP2C8 inhibition is stated to resemble the estimated inhibition at clinical clopidogrel doses; no calculation is shown.

## Covariates
Concomitant clopidogrel (via its acyl-beta-D-glucuronide, a time-dependent CYP2C8 inhibitor) and CYP2C8*1/*3 genotype in this patient; no equations.

## Caveats
One patient; clopidogrel dose unknown; the second-lowest clearance in the cohort could also reflect the *3 allele or other causes (nothing separates them); other drugs (simvastatin, citalopram, esomeprazole) were co-administered. Clearance is an unbound value from the prior model, not measured from this patient's own full profile. In vitro inhibition uses the glucuronide at a single concentration (100 uM) and a single paclitaxel concentration (0.5 uM), so there is no IC50 or Ki. The authors call the interaction likely and plausible, not proven.
