# Gurjar 2023: raltegravir popPK and UGT1A1/SLC22A6 pharmacogenetics (NEAT001)

## Study
- NEAT001/ANRS143, treatment-naive HIV adults on darunavir/ritonavir 800/100 mg daily plus raltegravir 400 mg twice daily (oral). One plasma sample per visit at week 4 and week 24 (time post-dose 0.17-16 h).
- 386 patients (726 samples) supplied data; 602 samples from 349 patients modelled (313 week 4, 289 week 24); 124 samples (17.1%) excluded (missing time/concentration, time >16 h, below LLQ). Concentrations 0.012-17.3 mg/L. LC-MS/MS, LLQ 0.0117 mg/L, plasma.
- Table 1 (n=349, median (range)): 87.7% male; age 37 y (20-71); weight 72 kg (41-135); CD4 340 (5-780) cells/mm3; HIV RNA 4.82 log10 (3.11-6.31); 82.5% Caucasian, 12.6% Black.
- NONMEM 7.3, FOCE-I, $PRIOR using Arab-Alameddine 2012 estimates for all fixed effects except CL/F. VPC with PsN (1000 sims).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- Not an opioid; no ke0, tPeak, MEAC or potency ratio. No PK/PD model.
- PK-clinical exposure-response (C12 or AUC0-12 vs time to virological failure, CD4 change, integrase resistance): no significant association (e.g. HR 0.72 per log10 mg/L C12, 95% CI 0.44-1.17, p=0.181). Higher AUC0-12 was associated with grade 2+ triglycerides (HR 6.24 per log10 mg/L, 95% CI 1.88-20.72, p=0.003); no association with creatine kinase or LDL.
- No concentration threshold for efficacy or toxicity is given. The discussion cites other-study median Cmin of 543 nM (400 mg twice daily) vs 113 nM (1200 mg daily) (ONCEMRK, ref 36), which are not this study's data.

## Covariates
Weight, age, sex, ethnicity, UGT1A1*28, SLC22A6 453G>A and 728C>T were tested univariably on CL/F; none gave a significant OFV decrease (Supplementary Table S1, not in the PDF), so none stayed in the model. Low UGT1A1 activity (*28/*28; n=40) gave 21% lower CL/F than normal/reduced (n=224, not significant); post-hoc GMR (90% CI): AUC0-12 1.34 (0.99-1.84), C12 1.32 (0.99-1.77), both p=0.062 (Fig. 2). Virological failure by week 96: 1 of 40 (2.5%) vs 42 of 224 (19.2%), p=0.012; lost after adjusting for baseline viral load (p=0.082) or both (p=0.089).

## Caveats
One sample per visit, so priors were needed and IIV and residual error are weakly separated; absorption-phase fit imperfect; priors may not suit this population; Vp/F RSE 0.6% reflects the prior. Parameters are apparent (per F), oral, whole-population (87.7% male); no food data. Authors note high intra-individual variability masks PK-PD links.
