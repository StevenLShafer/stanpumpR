# Smith 2022: expert consensus on adjusting concomitant ASMs when adding cenobamate

## Study
Modified Delphi consensus of seven US epileptologists (survey, then virtual meetings on 15 April and 5 October 2021),
convened by the sponsor SK Life Science, which the authors say had no role in the recommendations (PDF p4). Inputs:
investigator dose changes in the phase 3 open-label safety study (C021), cenobamate PK and DDI papers, the US
prescribing information. Adults >= 18 years with uncontrolled focal seizures. No new patient data, no PK
measurements, no model.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No concentration-effect data. The recommendations (Table 2, PDF p6-7; Table 4, PDF p10; rendered) use dose and two
blood-level thresholds:
- Phenytoin: standard < 15 ug/mL blood level, high >= 15 ug/mL. High: proactive decrease 25-33% at cenobamate 25
  mg/day and again 25-33% at 50 mg/day (text: 100 mg/day each step). Standard: reactive 25-33%, usually at cenobamate
  50-100 mg/day.
- Phenobarbital: proactive decrease 25% on starting cenobamate 50 mg/day; if the level is > 25 ug/mL, at 25 mg/day.
- Clobazam: <= 40 vs > 40 mg/day; stepwise 5-20 mg/day reductions from cenobamate start to 100 mg/day.
- Lacosamide: <= 400 vs > 400 mg/day; high dose: decrease 100 mg/day at cenobamate 50 and again at 100 mg/day
  (pharmacodynamic sodium-channel interaction).
- Carbamazepine: <= 1200 vs > 1200 mg/day; high dose: consider decrease 100-200 mg/day when cenobamate reaches
  200 mg/day; otherwise reactive.
- Lamotrigine: <= 500 vs > 500 mg/day; high dose: consider decrease 100 mg/day when cenobamate reaches 200 mg/day;
  otherwise reactive decrease 100 mg/day for adverse effects (e.g. dizziness).
- Other ASMs at high dose: consider a 25% decrease at cenobamate 150 mg/day; reactive 25% for adverse effects.
The panel states that although the label suggests raising carbamazepine and lamotrigine doses, in their experience
they usually do not (PDF p10). The lamotrigine interaction mechanism is called "unspecified" (PDF p10).
Phase 3 post hoc dose reductions quoted (PDF p12): clobazam -25.3 mg/day (66%), lacosamide -176.6 mg/day (35%),
phenytoin -251.6 mg/day (61%), phenobarbital -40.8 mg/day (40%), lamotrigine 23%, carbamazepine 46%.

## Covariates
Concomitant ASM dose level and, for phenytoin and phenobarbital, blood level. None else.

## Caveats
Expert opinion, sponsor-convened, all panellists involved with the drug. Interaction magnitudes are second-hand from
the label. Dose thresholds, not concentration thresholds, for lamotrigine and carbamazepine.

  lamotrigine or carbamazepine doses, which differs from the label advice the registry summarises.
