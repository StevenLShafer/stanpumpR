# Cai 2025: diamorphine / 6-MAM / morphine popPK (IM and IN), extrapolated to children

## Study
- Model-building data: two published NIDA studies (Cone 1993, Skopp 1997; Table 1) in male regular heroin users after
  3 days' abstinence; double-blind double-dummy crossover, 1-week washout: IN diamorphine HCl 6 and 12 mg vs IM 6 mg.
  Results text: 3 cohorts in 10 male volunteers, 28 sessions, 385 plasma concentrations (diamorphine, 6-MAM,
  morphine). Table 1 (layout garbled) gives median age 28.5 [23-32] and 35 [29-41] y, weight 74.75 [63.6-81.4] and
  69.4 [60.4-80] kg, 0% female for the two studies. 6 mg is roughly 0.07-0.09 mg/kg (p. 440).
- Assay: GC-MS (development data). External verification: Girardin 2003 (8 adults, IM <200-250 mg, LC/MS morphine)
  and Kidd 2009 (12 children 4-13 y, IN 0.1 mg/kg, RIA morphine). Neither used for fitting.
- Concentrations converted ng/mL -> nM using MW 369.4 (diamorphine), 327.4 (6-MAM), 285.34 (morphine) (p. 438).
  Whether doses were converted as base or HCl salt is not stated.
- Monolix 2021R2 (SAEM), ODE model; Simulx and Simcyp v22 virtual children; R 4.3.1.

## Model and parameters
Integrated 4-compartment model (Fig 1b): DIAM 1-cpt (V1) -> MAM 1-cpt (V2 = V1) -> MOR 2-cpt (V3, peripheral),
all conversions unidirectional first order (K12 DIAM->MAM, K23 MAM->MOR). DIAM and MAM have no other elimination
(renal elimination discarded, p. 444). First-order absorption without lag, common Ka for IM and IN; IM is the
F reference (F = 1); IN F logit-normal. Absorption t1/2 ~13.6 min (p. 440). Proportional error per analyte.
Ka-V1 random-effect correlation 0.854. Table 2 (p. 441), typical values "per 70 kg", FIM estimates (RSE%) and BSV:

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD model, no ke0 / t1/2ke0 / tPeak, no hysteresis. No MEAC for diamorphine. Targets used are morphine
concentrations taken from the literature: steady-state morphine 10-20 ug/L (35-70 nmol/L) as the effective
analgesic window, with >20 ug/L associated with respiratory depression (refs 32-37, paediatric postoperative
data; plasma, total). Paediatric simulation end points from Kidd 2009: morphine Cmax 13.7-82.7 nmol/L and
AUC0-1h 10.13-58.36 (units as printed, nmol/L). Introduction (citing ref 6): diamorphine about twice as potent as
morphine; 6-MAM peak effect within 10 min, morphine within 1 h (cited, refs 7-8). Recommended paediatric IN regimen
0.3 mg/kg load + 0.1 mg/kg hourly (simulation only).

## Covariates
Body weight via "theory-based" allometry, exponents fixed (estimating them worsened the fit); the equations are in
Supplementary S2, not in the main text. Morphine CL has an empiric sigmoid age-maturation function (Holford);
equation not in the main text. Dose and route not significant (beyond F).

## Caveats
Ten male opioid-experienced adults only; GC-MS vs LC/MS vs RIA across studies; model under-predicts early DIAM
(0-0.12 h) and late MAM; BOV not estimable (each session treated as a subject); high BSV on Ka, F, V1; metabolite
volume unidentifiable (V2 fixed = V1); linear kinetics only shown at low doses; no neonatal/infant validation;
supplementary material (allometric exponents, maturation equation) not archived here.
