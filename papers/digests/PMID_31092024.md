# Lamminsalo 2019: oxycodone population PK in plasma and CSF after epidural and IV dosing

PDF page 1 is a Taylor & Francis cover sheet; PDF page n = printed page 647 + n (PDF 2 = p. 649).

## Study
- Randomised, double-blind, double-dummy trial (EudraCT 2011-000125-76), Kuopio, Finland; the Kokki 2014 BJA data set (12 EPI + 12 IV) plus 6 new EPI patients (p. 649-650).
- 30 women, elective gynaecological surgery with epidural analgesia. IV group n = 12: age median 53 (26-60) y, weight 74 (55-110) kg, height 165 cm, BMI 26.6. EPI group n = 18: 56 (27-64) y, 67 (53-100) kg, 161 cm, BMI 24.2 (Table 1, p. 651, rendered page).
- Dose: 0.1 mg/kg oxycodone hydrochloride trihydrate, maximum 10 mg, as a 5-min infusion either epidurally (catheter T12/L1) with IV placebo, or IV with epidural placebo, postoperatively in recovery (p. 650). Rescue IV fentanyl for 4 h, then an epidural epinephrine-fentanyl-levobupivacaine infusion.
- Parallel plasma (3.5 mL) and lumbar CSF (1 mL, spinal catheter at L3/4) samples at 0, 2, 5, 15, 30, 45 min and 1, 2, 4, 8, 12, 24 h after the end of injection (p. 650).
- UPLC-MS/MS, LOD 0.1 ng/mL, LOQ 0.2 ng/mL; total oxycodone (no free fraction). 634 concentrations; 12 BLOQ (M6 method), 24 above 10,000 ng/mL excluded (all CSF), 607 used (p. 650-651).
- NONMEM 7.3, FOCE-I, PsN, Pirana; log-normal IIV; separate proportional errors for plasma and CSF; 1000-sample bootstrap (p. 650).

## Model and parameters
Five compartments (Figure 1): plasma central and peripheral, CSF central and peripheral, and an epidural depot feeding both plasma central (k31) and CSF (k34). IV dose into plasma central. No epidural bioavailability term (plasma AUC same by both routes, per Kokki 2014). No covariates. Values from Table 2 (p. 651), confirmed on the rendered page. Volumes and clearances are absolute, not per kg.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- **No PD measured or modelled.** No MEAC or minimum effective concentration, no ke0, t½ke0 or tPeak, no hysteresis, no potency or equianalgesic ratio. The Discussion mentions only that rescue fentanyl use (from Piirainen 2018) could feed a future repeated time-to-event model (p. 653).
- CSF is a sampling compartment, not an effect site: Q2 (0.0537 L/h) is a plasma-CSF exchange clearance, and the paper gives no time of peak CSF concentration after IV dosing as a number (only VPC plots, Figure 4). CSF peaks after epidural dosing reached >10,000 ng/mL (p. 653).
- Not reported: free fraction, whole blood; all concentrations are total oxycodone in plasma or CSF.

## Covariates
None. Age and weight were tested against the etas and showed no correlation (Supplement 2, not archived) (p. 651).

## Caveats
- 30 women only, postoperative, median 53-56 y; IV arm only 12 patients.
- CSF central volume fixed; spinal catheter may have let epidural drug pass to CSF (observer effect, p. 653).
- Single dose; ALOQ CSF samples excluded rather than modelled.
- Dose basis (trihydrate vs base) of the fitted parameters not stated (see open question).
- Bootstrap medians for IIV and residual error differ markedly from the estimates.
