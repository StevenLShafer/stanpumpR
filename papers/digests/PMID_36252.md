# Greenblatt 1979: lorazepam kinetics in the elderly

## Study
- IV arm: 15 healthy elderly subjects (60-84 y, mean 69.8) and 15 healthy young subjects (19-38 y, mean 27.3).
  - Single IV doses of 1.5-3.0 mg, roughly adjusted for weight.
  - The drug was diluted to 50 mL and infused over 5 min.
- Crossover arm: 10 of the elderly subjects also received the same dose IM (deltoid) and orally (fasting, tablets) in a three-way crossover. IM was always the third trial.
- Samples up to 72 h. Urine was collected for 72 h for lorazepam glucuronide.
- Assay: electron-capture GLC, sensitivity 1-3 ng/mL, CV ≤ 5%.
- Analysis: weighted nonlinear least squares, two or three exponentials.

## Model and parameters
IV, group means ± SE (Table I and abstract):

| | Young (n = 15) | Elderly (n = 15) | P |
|---|---|---|---|
| Vd, L/kg | 1.11 ± 0.03 | 0.99 ± 0.03 | < 0.01 |
| t½β, h | 14.1 ± 1.1 | 15.9 ± 1.1 | NS |
| CL, mL/min/kg | 0.99 ± 0.08 | 0.77 ± 0.06 | < 0.05 |
| V1, L/kg | 0.22 ± 0.04 | 0.13 ± 0.04 | NS (as printed) |

- Clearance in the elderly is 22% lower. Vd is 11% lower.
- Smoking explained part of the age difference.
  - Excluding moderate-to-heavy smokers: CL 0.76 (elderly) vs 0.93 (young) mL/min/kg, not significant.
  - Excluding all smokers: 0.78 vs 0.96, not significant.
- Sex had no effect.

Absorption in 10 elderly subjects (Table II and text):
- IM: peak within 2 h in all, mean tmax 1.0 h; lag in only one subject; t½abs 15.6 min (range 1.0-40.0).
- Oral: lag in 8/10, mean 10.9 min; tmax 1.0 h; t½abs 10.8 min (range 1.2-44.9).
- Availability by AUC: IM 92.1 ± 3.7%, oral 89.7 ± 3.4%.
- Availability by urinary glucuronide: IM 87.9 ± 8.6% (94.1 ± 6.7% with one incomplete collection removed), oral 97.2 ± 7.0%.
- First-pass-corrected oral absorption 93.1 ± 3.3% (derived by the authors, using an extraction ratio of 3.4 ± 0.3%).
- Abstract summary: IM and oral absorption 80% to 100% complete; peak within 2.5 h; t½abs never above 45 min.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- All subjects became drowsy, and most slept intermittently for 1-6 h; effects were gone within 12 h.
- Not quantified. No ke0 or concentration-effect relation.

## Covariates
- Age (group comparison only, no equation).
- Smoking (multiple regression: age explained 17% of the variance in clearance, smoking 8%).

## Caveats
- Group comparison, not a model.
- Some elderly subjects had compensated cardiovascular disease.
- Table I's individual columns cannot be read reliably from the text layer. The group means above match the abstract.
