# Bouillon 2004: propofol-remifentanil interaction (hypnosis, laryngoscopy, BIS, AE)

## Study
- 20 healthy volunteers, 10 M / 10 F, median age 33.5 yr (20-43), median weight 69.3 kg (50-120) (p. 1353).
  Stanford; randomized, open-label, modified crisscross design.
- TCI by STANPUMP with Schnider propofol and Minto remifentanil parameters. Phase 1: one drug alone ramped to
  apnoea or PECO2 > 65 mmHg (respiratory data published separately). Phase 2: the second drug at a constant target
  (remifentanil 0-4 ng/mL, Table 1; propofol 0-4 ug/mL, Table 2) while the first was stepped up until laryngoscopy
  was tolerated. 15 min at each target before assessment.
- Stimuli in order: name in normal voice, shouting, shaking and shouting, LMA insertion, laryngoscopy (one
  observer, SLS). 100 assessments, 430 stimulus-response pairs; one point (remifentanil 40 ng/mL) removed.
- Concentrations: TCI-predicted and individual post hoc Bayesian (from the companion PK analysis); TCI
  over-predicted propofol by about 30% on average (p. 1358).
- EEG: BIS (A1000, v3.22) and approximate entropy (m = 2, r = 0.2 SD, 8-s epochs), averaged over 56 s before each
  assessment. NONMEM V; log-normal IIV; additive residual error.

## Model and parameters
Hierarchical model (p. 1356-1357): the opioid attenuates stimulus intensity,
postopioid = preopioid x (1 - opioid^γ/(opioid^γ + (opioid50 x preopioid)^γ)) (eq. 3); propofol then acts against
the remaining intensity with C50 x postopioid and steepness φ (eq. 4). As printed, eq. 4 reads "probability of
nonresponsiveness = 1 - hypnotic^φ/(hypnotic^φ + (hypnotic50 x postopioid)^φ)"; with that leading "1 -" it would
fall as propofol rises, so the printed form appears to be a typesetting slip (eq. 6, p. 1370, repeats it) and the
non-response probability is the fraction itself (consistent with Figs. 3-6 and the text). Discussion (p. 1369):
15 ug/mL propofol needed for 95% no response to laryngoscopy without remifentanil; C50 propofol x preopioid
intensity reaches 8.44 ug/mL (TCI). P(hypnosis) and P(laryngoscopy) differ only in preopioid intensity.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End points: no response to shaking and shouting (hypnosis); no response to laryngoscopy (clench, cough, buck);
  BIS; AE. Concentrations are TCI-predicted or Bayesian plasma concentrations at pseudo-steady state (15 min per
  step); no ke0 estimated or used.
- Remifentanil alone did not ablate either response at clinically relevant concentrations (two subjects still
  responded to laryngoscopy at 24 ng/mL target). Synergy for both clinical end points (Minto model P < 0.001 TCI,
  P < 0.05 Bayesian). Remifentanil 4 ng/mL reduces the propofol needed by about two thirds (p. 1364).
- EEG interaction additive; remifentanil has little EEG effect below 8 ng/mL.
- 95% no-response combinations: AE 0.35-1.2 and BIS 27-72 for hypnosis; AE 0.14-0.80 and BIS 15-54 for
  laryngoscopy, for propofol > 1 ug/mL (p. 1363-1364).
- No MEAC, no ke0/t½ke0/tPeak, no equianalgesic or potency ratio.

## Covariates
Sex tested on all parameters, not significant. No other covariates.

## Caveats
- Healthy young volunteers, n = 20; one observer; LMA and laryngoscopy only after loss of response to shaking.
- Propofol C50 depends strongly on whether TCI (Schnider) or Bayesian concentrations are used (30% TCI bias).
- Propofol steepness IIV is large (CV 90-106%).
- Printed eq. 4 carries an apparent "1 -" slip (see above).
