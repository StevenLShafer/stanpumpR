# Cooper 2001: placental transfer of temazepam and fentanyl at 12-17 weeks

## Study
- 40 women recruited (ASA 1-2), surgical termination of pregnancy for psychosocial reasons, gestation 12-17 weeks
  (ultrasound biparietal diameter); median gestation 14 weeks (IQR 13-15). Age and weight not reported. (PDF p2)
- Temazepam 10 mg orally 1-2 h pre-operatively; fentanyl 2 ug/kg IV at induction, with propofol 2-3.5 mg/kg;
  maintenance isoflurane 0.5-2% and N2O 60-65%, spontaneous breathing via laryngeal mask. (PDF p2)
- One fetal blood sample (2 ml, ultrasound-guided transabdominal intracardiac puncture, 22G) per woman, randomized to
  5, 10, 15 or 20 min after fentanyl, with a simultaneous 10 ml maternal antecubital venous sample. (PDF p2)
- Usable pairs: 37 for fentanyl (5-23 min after dose), 36 for temazepam (55-120 min, median 80 min, after dose).
  Three fetal samples failed; one woman did not receive temazepam. Five women had benzodiazepine in pre-dose urine.
  (PDF p2)
- Assays (serum): temazepam "and its main metabolites" by fluorescence polarization immunoassay (Abbott), limit
  5 ng/ml; fentanyl "and its metabolites" by solid-phase RIA (DPC), detection limit 0.1 ng/ml, intra/inter-assay
  CV <7%. Immunoassays, so cross-reacting metabolites may be included. (PDF p2)
- Statistics: medians and IQR (non-normal), least-squares regression and multiple regression (Statgraphics).

## Model and parameters
No pharmacokinetic model. Table 1 (PDF p3, printed 167), medians (IQR), serum:

| | Maternal (ng/ml) | Fetal (ng/ml) | F:M ratio |
|---|---|---|---|
| Temazepam | 109.5 (78-139.75) | 42 (30-49.25) | 0.38 |
| Fentanyl | 0.475 (0.33-0.75) | 0.89 (0.7175-1.19) | 1.87 |

The F:M ratios equal the ratio of the medians (derived: 42/109.5 = 0.384; 0.89/0.475 = 1.87), not a median of
individual ratios. Fentanyl F:M rose from 1.42 at 5 min to 2.6 at 20 min (text, PDF p2).

Correlations (PDF p3): maternal fentanyl fell with time (r = -0.71), fetal fentanyl too (r = -0.34); fentanyl F:M rose
with time (r = 0.48) and fell with gestational age (r = -0.38). Temazepam concentrations did not change with time;
temazepam F:M rose with gestational age (r = 0.47); maternal-fetal temazepam r = 0.76, fentanyl r = 0.35.
Terminal half-lives quoted from the literature (Discussion): temazepam 5-12 h, fentanyl 3 h.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None measured. Opioid audit (fentanyl): no MEAC or analgesic concentration, no ke0 / t½ke0 / tPeak / hysteresis, no
potency ratio.

## Covariates
Gestational age (12-17 weeks) and sampling time, by regression only (no equations given for prediction).

## Caveats
- Immunoassays that include metabolites; single sample per subject; weight not reported, so fentanyl doses in mg are
  unknown; early second trimester under general anaesthesia, not term.
- Fetal sample is intracardiac (mixed), maternal is peripheral venous.
- Fentanyl F:M >1 is unusual (authors compare 0.7 at delivery, Rayburn 1989) and they cannot fully explain it.
- Small inconsistency: abstract gives the temazepam window as 60-120 min and 50-120 min; results say 55-120 min.
