# Iprivask (desirudin) label, NDA 021271, 11/2014

## Study
Not a study. Regulatory label: recombinant hirudin (65 amino acids, MW 6963.52; 15.75 mg per vial, about
20,000 antithrombin units per mg), a direct thrombin inhibitor for DVT prophylaxis in elective hip replacement.

## Model and parameters
No population model. Section 12.3 (PDF p7-8):
- Plasma concentrations by a **non-specific ELISA** that does not separate desirudin from its metabolites.
- SC absorption complete at 0.3 and 0.5 mg/kg; Tmax 1 to 3 h after 0.1-0.75 mg/kg; Cmax and AUC dose-proportional.
- IV disposition two- or three-compartment; Vss 0.25 L/kg, independent of dose (extracellular).
- Total clearance about 1.5 to 2.7 mL/min/kg, SC or IV, dose-independent, close to GFR; 40-50% of the dose excreted
  unchanged in urine, under 7% as C-terminally truncated metabolites.
- IV: about 90% of the dose leaves plasma within 2 h; terminal half-life 2 to 3 h. SC terminal half-life about 2 h.
- Figure (p8, rendered; 15 mg SC, 12 healthy subjects): mean concentration peaks near 33 nmol/L at about 2 h and
  falls to about 2 nmol/L at 12 h (read from the figure; derived about 230 ng/mL at 6963.5 g/mol).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point aPTT. Mean peak aPTT about 1.38 times baseline (range 0.58 to 3.41) on 15 mg SC twice daily (12.2,
  p7). The p8 figure shows mean aPTT peaking at about 1.5 times baseline at 1.5 h after a single 15 mg dose.
- Thrombin time often exceeds 200 s even at low concentrations: unsuitable for monitoring (12.2).
- Thrombin binding: 1:1, inhibition constant about 2.6 x 10^-13 M (12.3).
- Monitoring target: peak aPTT not above 2 times control (2.3, p2-3). No therapeutic plasma concentration range.

## Dosing (2.1-2.2, PDF p2)
15 mg SC every 12 h, first dose 5 to 15 minutes before surgery and after induction of regional block if used,
up to 12 days. CrCl 31-60 mL/min/1.73 m2: 5 mg every 12 h; under 31: 1.7 mg every 12 h (the Highlights call these
reductions by factors of three and nine), with daily aPTT and creatinine.

## Covariates
- Renal (12.3, p8): single IV 0.5/0.25/0.125 mg/kg in mild/moderate/severe impairment; dose-normalised aPTT
  AUCeffect about 3-fold (moderate) and 9-fold (severe) higher; no increase in mild; terminal half-life up to 12 h
  in severe vs 2 to 4 h otherwise. Here CrCl is in mL/min, while 2.2 uses mL/min/1.73 m2.
- Age: clearance 28% lower at 65 and over (derived check: 1 - 110/153 = 0.28); population PK in 301 hip-replacement
  patients found no age or sex effect once creatinine clearance was included (no model printed).
- Hepatic: not studied.

## Caveats
- ELISA is not specific for parent drug. Most PK figures are healthy-volunteer summaries without n or variability.
- Section 5 is numbered differently in the contents (p1: 5.2 Renal Insufficiency, 5.3 Hemorrhagic Events, 5.5
  Hepatic Impairment/Liver Injury, 5.6 Laboratory Tests) and in the body (p3-4: 5.1-5.4 only, renal at 5.3), so
  the cross-references to 5.5 and 5.6 (12.3, 8.6) point at sections absent from the body; 8.6 spells "aPPT".
  Formatting only, no bearing on PK.
