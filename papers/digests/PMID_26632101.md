# Tsai 2016: azilsartan and chlorthalidone population PK and SBP exposure-response

## Study
- **Design:** a phase 3, 8-week, double-blind, placebo-controlled, randomised factorial trial of the azilsartan
  medoxomil (AZL-M)/chlorthalidone (CLD) fixed-dose combination. Arms were AZL-M placebo, 20, 40 or 80 mg QD
  crossed with CLD placebo, 12.5 or 25 mg QD (11 combinations; no double placebo). 1714 subjects randomised, with
  moderate to severe essential hypertension (p990).
- **PK population (p990):** 1481 subjects gave plasma samples. 1201 gave 5432 AZL concentrations, 1202 gave 5460
  M-II concentrations and 1059 gave 4807 CLD concentrations. 54% female, 69% white, 20% black. Mean age 57 y, mean
  weight 88 kg, eGFR 85 mL/min/1.73 m2, baseline ABPM 151/91 mm Hg. The exposure-response population was 1310.
  The age range is in Supplemental Table 1, which this PDF does not hold.
- **Sampling (p990):** sparse. Troughs at weeks 4 and 6, one sample 1-4 h post-dose at week 4 and two samples
  (at least 1 h apart) 4-12 h post-dose at week 6.
- **Assay:** validated LC-MS/MS on plasma. LLOQ 10.0 ng/mL for AZL, 2.00 ng/mL for M-II and CLD.
- **Software:** NONMEM VI, FOCE with interaction. Covariates screened by GAM, then stepwise (forward α 0.01,
  backward α 0.001). Evaluated by VPC and nonparametric bootstrap. Exposure-response was fitted in SAS 9.1 by
  nonlinear least squares.
- **Fixed parameters (p990):** some base-model parameters were fixed from earlier models of extensively sampled
  phase 1 data, to avoid over-parameterisation and instability.

## Model and parameters
- **AZL:** two compartments with first-order absorption, fitted simultaneously with a one-compartment model of the
  inactive metabolite M-II. CL/F and Vd/F carry a covariance term. IOV on Vd/F was not retained. Residual error
  is additive on the log scale.
- **CLD:** two compartments with first-order absorption. Residual error is combined additive and proportional.
- **Values:** all from Table 1 and Table 2 (PDF p4, printed 991), confirmed on the rendered page. Brackets in the
  tables are %RSE. The footnote says roman type marks fixed values. Fm, Q and Vp/F of AZL carry no RSE and an NA
  bootstrap interval, so they are the fixed ones.
- **Abstract check:** the abstract's CL/F and Vd/F (1.47 L/h and 3.98 L for AZL; 4.13 L/h and 62.1 L for CLD)
  agree with the tables.
- **Dose basis:** the paper does not state whether a modelled dose is in mg of AZL-M or of AZL.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- **End point:** change from baseline to week 8 in trough ambulatory (ABPM) SBP.
- **Model:** additive Emax, ΔSBP = Emax·AUC/(EC50+AUC) for AZL plus the same term for CLD (Figure 3, p994). The
  exposure is the steady-state AUC over the dosing interval, from NCA of profiles simulated from individual
  Bayesian estimates (p990). An interaction term was not significant.
- **Overall estimates (Table 3, p994):**
  - Emax: AZL -15.6 mm Hg (95% CI -18.0 to -13.2); CLD -23.9 (-29.8 to -18.1).
  - EC50: AZL 3564 ng·h/mL (558 to 6570); CLD 2743 (1000 to 4487). The EC50s are poorly estimated.
  - Bootstrap medians (Table 4): -15.6, -23.9, 3596 and 2729.
- **Subgroup Emax (Table 3):**
  - Black: AZL -11.6, CLD -31.1. Nonblack: AZL -16.5, CLD -22.6.
  - Emax was larger at higher baseline clinic DBP, ABPM DBP and ABPM SBP. The EC50s were not compared between
    subgroups.
- **Observed against predicted (Table 5, p995):** observed mean ABPM SBP change at 40 mg AZL-M + 25 mg CLD was
  -29.8 mm Hg (n=156), against -29.8 predicted.
- No ke0, effect site or concentration target. Not an opioid, so no MEAC.

## Covariates
- **AZL (p991):** CL/F = (1.47 - 0.0149·[Age - 58]) · (Weight/86)^0.267. eGFR was significant univariately but
  dropped out once age and weight were in. Liver tests were not significant.
- **CLD clearance (p992):** CL/F = (4.13 - 0.0451·[Age - 58]) · (1 + Sex·0.253) - 2.61·(Cystatin C - 0.73 mg/L).
- **CLD volume (p992):** Vd/F = 62.1 · (1 + Sex·1.19) · 2.41 · (BMI - 30.9) as printed. Read literally, this gives
  zero at BMI 30.9, although the text says Vd/F rose linearly with BMI. The printed equation is not usable as
  written. The intended form cannot be recovered from this PDF.
- **Sex coding:** not stated explicitly. The text says males are higher, and the Figure 2 reference subject is a
  58-year-old female (86 kg, BMI 30.9, cystatin C 0.73 mg/L). Male = 1 is therefore implied.
- **Exposure effects:** the Results (p993) say exposure changes up to ±20% over ages 40-75 for both AZL and CLD.
  Males have CLD Cmax about 30% and AUC about 20% lower than females.

## Caveats
- **±20% against ±30%:** the abstract and Results give ±20% for both drugs. The Discussion (p996) says CLD exposure
  changes up to ±30%. The two statements disagree; the paper does not reconcile them.
- **Prior CLD clearance:** the Discussion calls the CLD CL/F of about 4 L/h "close to" earlier estimates of about
  10 L/h, citing small (n ≤7) single-dose studies.
- **Sparse sampling:** sampling was sparse and within one dosing interval. AZL Q and Vp/F were fixed from phase 1
  models, so AZL's terminal phase rests on those fixed values. Most subjects had normal renal and liver function.
- **Exposure-response:** it is AUC-driven, the EC50s are imprecise, and it was sponsor-run (Takeda).
