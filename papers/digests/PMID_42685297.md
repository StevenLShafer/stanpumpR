# Duan 2026: gestational change in ASM concentration/dose ratios, WECARE cohort, China

## Study
- WECARE: prospective, multicentre (93 centres) observational cohort of women with epilepsy aged 18-45 y, China; samples October 2019 to August 2025 (Methods p. 3; Results p. 4). Visits preconception, at least once per trimester, peripartum, postpartum (1-9 months, then yearly); timing clinical.
- 947 women, 1638 samples: 1187 nonpregnant samples from 821 women (age 29.31 +/- 8.38 y) and 451 pregnancy samples from 228 women (28.67 +/- 4.30 y); some women in both. Polytherapy in 64.3% at least once (51.5% of pregnant women). 118 distinct regimens.
- Steady-state troughs (>= 7 days on a stable dose, before the next dose). Total serum concentrations by UPLC-MS/MS (KingMed central lab). Oxcarbazepine as its MHD, divided by the oxcarbazepine dose. End point: C/D ratio, serum mg/L divided by daily dose; tabulated in mg/L per g/day.
- Analysis: LMMs on ln(C/D), random intercept per woman. Whole cohort: pregnancy status as a 4-level factor (nonpregnant reference), adjusted for age, time-varying weight and each concomitant ASM dose. Pregnancy subcohort: piecewise model (pregnancy-onset step + GA + GA squared) with prepregnancy baselines. Full and conditional model averaging over AICc-ranked candidate models (MuMIn); GMR = exp(beta). R 4.5.1, lme4, lmerTest.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD. The Introduction cites seizure-risk thresholds from other studies (concentrations below about 65% / 46% / 58% of the prepregnancy level for lamotrigine / levetiracetam / oxcarbazepine); not measured here.

## Covariates
Pregnancy (trimester or GA), weight (lower C/D with higher weight for most ASMs; higher for perampanel), concomitant ASM doses; age not significant. No pregnancy x co-medication interaction found. Marginal R2 0.586 (lamotrigine pregnancy model), 0.555 (lacosamide), about 0.27-0.37 (MHD), 0.298 (perampanel); conditional R2 up to 0.882.

## Caveats
- Authors: sparse troughs; total concentrations only; heavy polytherapy (confounding); small numbers for newer ASMs; limited structured postpartum follow-up; large unexplained between-woman variability.
- C/D is a concentration ratio, not a clearance estimate; the whole-cohort contrast is partly between women (nonpregnant reference includes women never pregnant).
- Lacosamide rests on few pregnant women (Table 1: 8, 9 and 6 by trimester; the Discussion says 43 observations from 8 women, which does not match Table 1's 36 samples).
- Abstract inconsistencies: lamotrigine T1 CI; and lacosamide's "-10.8%" is the full-averaging GMR (not significant) printed beside the conditional-averaging beta (-0.207) and P (0.035), which correspond to -18.7%.
