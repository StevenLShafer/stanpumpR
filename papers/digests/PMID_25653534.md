# Kunisawa 2015: landiolol TCI and population PK in peripheral arterial disease

## Study
- Eight patients for peripheral arterial surgery (UMIN000015077); inclusion age > 20 y, weight < 80 kg, ASA 2-3; no arrhythmia, alpha-methyldopa, clonidine or beta blocker (PDF p2).
- Table 1 (PDF p4): 6 men, 2 women; weight 58.9 (10.9) kg, 40.4-71.8; lean body mass 46.4 (8.1) kg; height 159 (7) cm, 147-170; age 73 (7) y, 64-84; albumin 3.8 g/dL; creatinine 1.00 (0.52) mg/dL.
- Anaesthesia by TCI of propofol (Diprifusor, BIS 40-60) and remifentanil (STANPUMP, Minto; 2 then 8 ng/mL); dopamine 3 mcg/kg/min from 20 min before incision; rocuronium.
- Landiolol hydrochloride by STANPUMP TCI with Honda 2008's two-compartment parameters, without the lag (STANPUMP cannot take one), to plasma targets 500 then 1,000 ng/mL for 30 min each, via an 18-G forearm catheter.
- Radial arterial samples at 1, 2, 5 and 25 min after each start and 1, 2, 5, 10, 15 and 20 min after the end; 1 mL whole blood into chilled ethanol and neostigmine with EDTA (esterase stopped); plasma by HPLC with fluorescence (Suno 2009). 112 concentrations.
- NONMEM; two compartments with ALAG best by AIC (1,247.171); one- and three-compartment models did not converge. Covariates tested: body weight, lean body mass, age; none retained. IIV on CL only (exponential); exponential residual error.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Heart rate fell significantly 2 min after the start of landiolol and stayed below baseline until 20 min after the end; systolic pressure was lower at five time points; diastolic unchanged (Fig. 3, PDF p5-6). Ephedrine 10.6 (6.8) mg and phenylephrine 0.23 (0.15) mg were given. No ke0 or concentration-effect model. Not an opioid; no MEAC.

## Covariates
Weight, lean body mass (James) and age tested, none significant (8 patients). Laboratory values not tested. Per-kilogram parameters throughout.

## Caveats
- n = 8, elderly, anaesthetised, on dopamine; predictions fell below the observed values above 1,000 ng/mL, which the authors attribute to slight nonlinearity (PDF p7).
- The lag on an intravenous infusion is a property of the delivery (dead space), not the drug.
- Plasma with esterases stopped at the bedside; whole-blood studies (Krumpl 2017, 2018) give roughly twice the clearance.
