# ELIQUIS (apixaban) label, NDA 202155, 2025

## Study
Not a study: the FDA prescribing information for apixaban (2.5 and 5 mg tablets, 0.5 mg tablets for oral
suspension, 0.15 mg SPRINKLE capsules). PDF page = printed page + 2 in the full prescribing information
(e.g. printed p. 24 is PDF p. 26).

Dosing (section 2.1, printed pp. 2-3): nonvalvular AF 5 mg twice daily, or 2.5 mg twice daily with at least 2 of
age >= 80 y, weight <= 60 kg, serum creatinine >= 1.5 mg/dL; DVT prophylaxis after hip/knee replacement 2.5 mg twice
daily, first dose 12 to 24 h after surgery (35 days hip, 12 days knee); DVT/PE treatment 10 mg twice daily for 7 days
then 5 mg twice daily; recurrence reduction 2.5 mg twice daily after at least 6 months. Pediatric weight-tier
dosing, Table 1 (printed p. 3). Combined P-gp and strong CYP3A4 inhibitors: halve 5 or 10 mg doses (2.6).
Perioperative (2.4): stop at least 48 h before procedures with moderate/high bleeding risk, at least 24 h before
low-risk ones. Neuraxial (5.3, printed pp. 6-7, text layer): remove indwelling epidural/intrathecal catheters no
earlier than 24 h after the last dose; next dose no earlier than 5 h after removal; delay 48 h after a traumatic
puncture. Pharmacodynamic effect expected to persist at least 24 h after the last dose, about two half-lives (5.2).

Crushed tablets: in 30 mL water exposure similar to intact; in 30 g applesauce Cmax and AUC 20% and 16% lower;
crushed 5 mg in 60 mL D5W by nasogastric tube similar (PDF p. 26). Activated charcoal 2 h and 6 h after a 20 mg
dose lowered mean AUC by 50% and 27% (section 10, printed p. 22). Interactions (Figure 2, PDF p. 27): ketoconazole
and clarithromycin raise AUC (ketoconazole about 2-fold, read off the forest plot), rifampin lowers it (about
0.5-fold); no numeric table is given. Specific populations (Figure 3, PDF p. 28): forest plot only; ESRD on
hemodialysis AUC 36% higher (dosed post-dialysis) and 17% higher (dosed 2 h before dialysis), 14% lower on vs off
dialysis (PDF p. 29, text layer).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Section 12.2 (PDF pp. 25-26, page images): PT, INR and aPTT changes at therapeutic doses are small, variable and
not useful for monitoring. Anti-FXa activity (Rotachrom Heparin chromogenic assay) rose with concentration, similar
in healthy subjects and AF patients, but the label states this test is not recommended for assessing apixaban's
anticoagulant effect; with PCC use, clotting tests or anti-FXa monitoring are "not useful and is not recommended"
(5.2). Pediatric: anti-FXa vs plasma concentration linear with slope close to 1 (units not given). 4-factor PCC
in healthy volunteers: ETP back to baseline 4 h after PCC start vs 45 h with placebo, then overshoot 34%-51%.
Enoxaparin or naproxen co-administration: 50% to 60% higher anti-FXa activity. No QTc effect up to 50 mg.
**No therapeutic, target or toxic plasma concentration range, and no ng/mL value, appears in the sections read.**
Not an opioid: no MEAC, ke0 or potency ratio.

## Covariates
Figure 3 (PDF p. 28): renal impairment, age >= 65 y vs 18-40 y, body weight >= 120 kg or <= 50 kg vs 65-85 kg,
hepatic impairment; all "no dose adjustment" except moderate hepatic impairment (no recommendation possible).
Severe hepatic impairment not recommended (8.7). Sex and race: no meaningful difference (PDF p. 28).

## Caveats
- The label's 3.3 L/h is called "total clearance" next to an absolute F of about 50%; the label does not say
  whether it is systemic (IV-derived) or apparent oral clearance. Not relevant to any repo claim (the repo models
  use their own published CL/F).
- The PDF cover date (FDA action 2025-12-12) differs from the label's own "Revised: 04/2025"; both are 2025.
- Figures 2 and 3 are forest plots without numeric values; any fold change taken from them is read off a graph.
