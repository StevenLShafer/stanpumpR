# Uchida 2011: pooled PET D2 occupancy vs plasma level, one-site model per antipsychotic

## Study
- Systematic review (MEDLINE, PubMed, EMBASE to February/March 2010) of PET studies reporting individual plasma (or serum) antipsychotic levels with D2 occupancy in schizophrenia or related psychosis; drugs with >= 20 subjects pooled; first scan only; long-acting injectables in the prior 6 months excluded.
- 34 treatment arms from 23 studies, 281 subjects: risperidone 98, clozapine 75, olanzapine 42, haloperidol 35, ziprasidone 31. Mean ages 28-62 y. All but one arm striatal occupancy; antagonist radiotracers, mostly [11C]raclopride; SRTM.
- Fit: occupancy = a x [C / (C + ED50)], unconstrained and with a fixed at 100% (GraphPad Prism); better model chosen by F test; leave-one-out prediction; mean prediction error (bias) and RMSE (precision).

## Model and parameters
Risperidone driver: plasma risperidone + 9-hydroxyrisperidone (active moiety), ng/mL.

For risperidone the unconstrained model fitted better (F[1,96] = 27.12, P < 0.001); leave-one-out bias 0.0 (95% CI -1.8 to 1.8), RMSE 8.9 (7.6-10.2), mean absolute error 6.6 +/- 6.1%, r = 0.77.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: striatal (and in some arms extrastriatal) D2 receptor occupancy by PET.
- Plasma ranges for a 60-78% occupancy window (Discussion): risperidone 10.5-38.2, olanzapine 13.9-43.6, haloperidol 0.8-4.2, ziprasidone 70.0-251.6 ng/mL.
- Note: ED50 is called the level for 50% occupancy, but with a < 100% the model gives a/2 (44% for risperidone) at C = ED50.
- No time course, no ke0 or hysteresis. Not an opioid.

## Covariates
None (pooled cross-sectional). P-glycoprotein variability discussed as a source of scatter.

## Caveats
Mixed radiotracers, regions and plasma vs serum; mostly steady-state single scans; timing of samples relative to dose not modelled; 9-hydroxyrisperidone treated as equipotent by summation.
