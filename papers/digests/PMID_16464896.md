# Sandoe 2006: ampicillin, vancomycin and linezolid against enterococcal biofilms

## Study
In vitro microbiology; no patients dosed, no pharmacokinetics. 58 enterococcal isolates (49 *E. faecalis*,
9 *E. faecium*) from 58 of 61 consecutive episodes of intravascular catheter-related bloodstream infection (CRBSI),
Leeds (p768). MIC by broth microdilution; MBC = lowest concentration killing 99.9% of the inoculum (5 µL subcultured
onto horse blood agar). MBIC (minimum biofilm inhibitory concentration) by a simplified Calgary-biofilm-device
method: biofilm grown on a 96-peg lid 18-20 h, exposed 20 h to antibiotic 0-8192 mg/L, then pegs moved to fresh
broth; MBIC = lowest concentration with no regrowth (p768). Tolerance = MBC/MIC ≥ 32 (p768). Gentamicin added at a
fixed 10 mg/L for combination testing. **Vancomycin was tested on only 24 of the 58 isolates (21 *E. faecalis*,
3 *E. faecium*)**, those whose infections were treated with vancomycin (p769). Funded partly by Pharmacia.

## Model and parameters
No PK model. Susceptibility results, Table 2 (PDF p3, printed p769; confirmed on rendered page), all mg/L:

| *E. faecalis* linezolid (49) | 4 | 2-8 | 2048 | 64-2048 | 4096 | 1024-4096 |

| *E. faecium* vancomycin (3) | 4 | 2-4 | >128 | – | 4096 | 2048-4096 |

Tolerance (p767 abstract, p768-769): ampicillin 93% (48/49 *E. faecalis*, 6/9 *E. faecium*), vancomycin 100%
(all 24 tested; every MBC > 128 mg/L, the top concentration quantified), linezolid 93% (54/58).
Gentamicin 10 mg/L reduced vancomycin MBC in 50% and MBIC in 38% of isolates (p769).
Table 1: peg biofilm load 6.2 × 10^4 to 1.7 × 10^5 cfu/mL before exposure (7 *E. faecalis*, ampicillin).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End points are in vitro MIC, MBC (99.9% kill) and MBIC (no regrowth from biofilm). Not an opioid paper: no MEAC,
ke0 or potency ratio. The authors state MBIC90s were about 10^3 times the MIC90, at least twice the MBC90, and well
above achievable serum concentrations (p769). No correlation of MBIC with clinical outcome (13 catheter-retained
cases, too few to analyse).

## Covariates
None (in vitro).

## Caveats
- Vancomycin n = 24 only, *E. faecium* n = 3; MBC censored at > 128 mg/L.
- MBIC method simplified (visible regrowth, not peg counts); single centre; isolates partly clonal (Strain-type A
  caused 22 of the 61 episodes).
- Total drug in broth; no protein binding.
