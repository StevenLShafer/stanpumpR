# Milne 2003: propofol-sparing effect of remifentanil (closed-loop)

## Study
- Randomized, three-arm, 60 unpremedicated ASA I/II day-case patients (inguinal hernia or varicose-vein groin surgery), 20 per arm, age 16-70 yr, BMI <=30. Table 1 (PDF p3): age 42.9/40.4/41.5 yr, weight 69.8/71.9/69.2 kg, height 171.1/168.3/171.9 cm for the 2/4/8 ng/ml groups.
- Remifentanil by TCI ("Remifusor", Diprifusor-type) to a constant **plasma (blood) target of 2, 4 or 8 ng/ml**, using the Minto 1997 PK set (age, weight, sex adjusted). Stated approximate maintenance infusion rates 0.08, 0.15, 0.3 µg/kg/min (units per the check question above). Remifentanil was started 8-10 min before propofol so blood and effect site were in equilibrium.
- Propofol TCI (Marsh set, Diprifusor). Induction by stepping the target from 4 by 0.5 µg/ml every 30 s until loss of consciousness (LOC); then closed-loop (CLAN) control on the auditory evoked potential index (AEPex), set point 35, to give equal anaesthetic depth in all arms. Propofol effect-site concentration was **calculated** with ke0 0.2 /min (Billard 1997, applied to the Marsh model), as built into the Diprifusor.
- LMA airway, spontaneous-type ventilation to ETCO2 5 kPa, no neuromuscular blocker. At end of surgery both targets were set to zero; recovery times and calculated propofol Cp/Ce at LOC and recovery of consciousness (ROC) recorded. Stats: ANOVA, Kruskal-Wallis, t-test.

## Model and parameters
No new PK model. Propofol (Marsh) and remifentanil (Minto 1997) models were taken from the literature.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- Opioid audit, remifentanil: **no MEAC or analgesic concentration** is reported. The three fixed plasma targets (2, 4, 8 ng/ml; Minto-model blood concentration, not measured) are experimental levels. End point for propofol requirement is the AEPex 35 (an hypnosis/arousal surrogate), not analgesia.
- **No remifentanil ke0, t½ke0, tPeak or hysteresis** is reported; equilibration was assumed after 8-10 min.
- Propofol ke0 0.2 /min (Billard 1997 with the Marsh model) was used only to compute Ce; it is not estimated here.
- Potency ratio: the discussion (PDF p4, printed 626) notes Vuyk's alfentanil-propofol curve was converted to remifentanil with an alfentanil:remifentanil equipotency ratio of 30:1, and suggests the ratio may be greater than 30:1. Not measured here.
- Propofol requirement fell with remifentanil: Ct for adequate anaesthesia 4.96 -> 3.46 -> 3.01 µg/ml for 2/4/8 ng/ml (Table 2); effect-site Ce fell with remifentanil level at LOC (P=0.029) and ROC (P=0.001). Remifentanil added little hypnotic effect without stimulation (LOC Ct 6.1 -> 5.1).
- Propofol Ce at ROC close to Ce at LOC; mean absolute difference 0.68 µg/ml (0.52-0.83). Authors suggest maintaining Ce at least 1 µg/ml above the LOC value.

## Covariates
Remifentanil dosing used Minto's age/weight/sex covariates (as implemented in the pump); no covariate analysis of the results.

## Caveats
- Minor surgery, local-anaesthetic infiltration at the end, AEPex as input (authors say it may be less sensitive to opioid graded effects); Ce is model-calculated, not measured; concentrations are model targets, not assays.
- Targets were mean values among adequately anaesthetized patients, not EC50/EC95.
- Text layer drops micro signs (see open questions).
