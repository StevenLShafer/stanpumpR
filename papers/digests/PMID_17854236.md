# Perez-Ruixo 2007: trabectedin population PK meta-analysis

## Study
- Pooled 14 clinical studies (5 phase I with rich sampling, 9 phase II with sparse sampling) in cancer patients: 603 patients, 945 cycles, 6613 plasma concentrations (text, Methods; Table I, PDF p4).
- Index dataset (model building): 274 patients, 4251 concentrations, 7 studies (studies 1-4, 8, 9, 11, 13 per Table I). Test dataset (external qualification): 329 patients, 2362 concentrations, 6 studies. Final fit to the combined set.
- Dosing: IV trabectedin monotherapy 0.024-1.8 mg/m2 as 1 h, 3 h or 24 h infusions q21d; 3 h infusions days 1, 8, 15 of a 28-day cycle; or 1 h daily x5 q21d.
- Baseline (Table II, combined): age median 54 y (19-81); weight 72 kg (41-148); BSA 1.8 m2 (0.9-2.8); 240 male, 363 female; CrCl (Cockcroft-Gault, capped 150) 91 mL/min (34-150).
- Assay: LC-MS/MS on heparinised plasma, LLOQ 0.01 ng/mL (ecteinascidin-729 internal std) or 0.025 ng/mL (d3-trabectedin); CV < 13%. Total (not free) plasma concentration; free fraction about 2.48%, blood:plasma 0.89 (Background).
- Software: NONMEM V 1.1, FO (FOCE also tried); log-additive residual error with a two-subpopulation mixture; IIV and IOV lognormal.

## Model and parameters
Open four-compartment model, zero-order input, linear elimination from the central compartment (CL/V1), linear distribution to a deep peripheral compartment (k12/k21 pair as drawn; compartment 3 via k31), saturable (Michaelis-Menten-type) distribution to a shallow peripheral compartment (rate Tmax*C/(Tm+C)), and a catenary compartment 4 off the shallow one (k34, k43) (Fig 1, PDF p8). The text names compartment 2 as the deep and 3 as the shallow peripheral; the figure's labelling of which of k12/k21/k31 belongs to which is not unambiguous in the text layer (see open_questions).

Derived/quoted values from the text (not Table IV): Vss about 6070 L (male) and 5240 L (female) (abstract; p878); terminal t1/2 mean 180 h (SD 61.4) overall; male 168 h with and 189 h without dexamethasone, female 153 h and 171 h (p877); t1/2 variability 34.1%. Structural-model (pre-covariate) CL 35.1 L/h (p878). Simulated cycle-2 median AUC about 10% above cycle 1 (limited accumulation, q3w).

Simulation percentiles, Table V (text layer only, p879): 1.5 mg/m2 24 h q3w median AUC21d 62.0 mg*h/L, Cmax 1090 ng/mL; 1.3 mg/m2 3 h q3w AUC 53.9, Cmax 5839; 0.58 mg/m2 3 h d1,8,15 AUC 17.0, Cmax 2552.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No concentration-effect model is fitted in this paper. Cited from other analyses (refs 36, 37) only: neutropenia and transaminitis depth/duration relate to dose level and plasma concentration; ANC nadir 0.58, 1.22 and 2.17 x 10^9/L at 1.7, 1.3 and 0.9 mg/m2 q3w. No ke0, no effect-compartment, no MEAC. Not an opioid paper; opioid audit not applicable.

## Covariates
- Screened: age, weight, BSA, sex, ALT, AST, ALP, LDH, bilirubin, albumin, total protein, CrCl, liver metastases, ECOG status.
- Retained: sex on V1 (dOFV 21.1, additive male increment 2.22 L); dexamethasone on CL (dOFV 9.95, df 2, p = 0.007, just above the preset 0.005 criterion, kept because of the size of the effect and a significant effect in study 11 by a GEE analysis). Only 4% of patients were known to receive no dexamethasone.
- No significant effect of age, weight, BSA, hepatic or renal indices, albumin or ECOG on any parameter.

## Caveats
- Tm was fixed because Tmax and Tm were not independently estimable; the saturation is only evident in study 1 (1 h infusions), and most data lie in the linear range of the distribution.
- Dexamethasone CL effect rests on few non-users and misses the preset significance level; text sign inconsistency noted in open_questions.
- Concentrations are total plasma; the drug is about 97.5% protein bound.
- Inpatient oncology population with normal organ function by eligibility; no renal or hepatic impairment data.
- Earlier cited estimates (CL 75 L/h, V1 52 L) came from 48 h sampling; the authors attribute their own lower CL and V1 to the longer sampling (>=168 h).
