# Owellen 1977: pharmacokinetics and metabolism of vinblastine

## Study
Patient 1 (metastatic hypernephroma, normal renal function, ideal body weight 59 kg) received ring-labelled [3H]vinblastine 0.167 mg/kg IV bolus (10.0 mg, 205.6 microCi); blood radioactivity followed 4 h (Table 1 line A, treated as 2-compartment), urine and stool for 6 days. Two other patients received 0.20 mg/kg unlabelled VLB; serum assayed by radioimmunoassay to 72 h (the assay cross-reacts equally with VLB, VCR, deacetylvinblastine and vindesine, not vinleurosine). The second patient was re-dosed at 24 h and followed 72 h more, giving three runs (Table 1 lines B, C, D). Three-compartment open mammillary model by curve stripping (Wagner 1975). Also reported: deacetylvinblastine (DVLB) identified as a human metabolite (TLC in three systems, acetylation), no glucuronide or sulfate, cytotoxicity of excreta, mouse LD50 and P388 activity.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PK/PD model, no ke0, effect-site or hysteresis data and no MEAC (not an opioid). Biological activity only: B14-150 hamster cells ED50 2.2 ng/mL (cited as unpublished); DVLB is active (lower mouse LD50 and lower optimal P388 dose than VLB, Table 6); no other active metabolite found in urine or stool.

## Covariates
None modelled; dose weight-based. Radiolabel in urine 13.6% and stool 9.9% of dose at 72 h; about 73% retained at 6 days (Results/Discussion).

## Caveats
Three RIA runs in two patients and one radiolabel patient; wide spread (Vc 6.4-22.5 L); the RIA cannot separate VLB from DVLB, so concentrations are VLB plus DVLB; the second-dose run is not corrected for residual drug.
