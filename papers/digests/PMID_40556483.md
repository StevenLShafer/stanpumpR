# Chigutsa 2025: tirzepatide exposure-response for fat mass and fat-free mass (SURMOUNT-1)

PDF page = printed page - 1488 (PDF p1 = 1489).

## Study
- SURMOUNT-1 (NCT04184622), phase 3, adults with BMI >= 30 or >= 27 plus a weight-related complication, without T2DM; n = 2539, 1:1:1:1 to placebo or tirzepatide 5, 10, 15 mg SC once weekly, up to 72 weeks, body weight monthly (p2).
- Extra data: phase 3 T2DM trials (SURPASS, 5,727 participants with T2DM, with or without obesity) pooled for the population/BMI covariate tests (p5).
- Sequential PK/PD: individual post hoc concentrations from the published population PK model (Schneck 2024; ref 22): two compartments, first-order absorption and elimination, body weight time-varying; allometric exponents fixed on CL and V; CL tracks total weight, V tracks fat-free mass plus 48% of fat mass; exposure changes ~1.1% per kg over 70-120 kg (p2). Not re-fitted here.
- Dependent variables: FFM and fat mass, both CALCULATED from weight, height and sex with Janmahasatian 2005 (not measured). Fat mass = weight - FFM (p2, p3 image).
- Indirect-response model, drug inhibits zero-order formation (Kin) with an Imax model; placebo effect additive, decaying exponentially. NONMEM SAEM then importance sampling; joint model with residual-error correlation (L2 data item). Half the data (random) for development, half for external evaluation; a ~10% subset with DXA used for external validation only (p3).

## Model and parameters
Equations (p2 text, p3 image): FFM male = 9.27e3 x Bwt / (6.68e3 + 216 x BMI); FFM female = 9.27e3 x Bwt / (8.78e3 + 244 x BMI); Bwt in kg, BMI in kg/m2.
Kin(t) = Kin0 x (1 - Imax x conc / (IC50 + conc) - Plac(t)); Plac(t) = Plac0 x exp(-decay x t).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: weight change decomposed into FFM and fat mass (calculated, not measured); validated against DXA in ~10% (Figure 2, Figure S3 not in PDF).
- Fat mass is reduced about 3 times as much as FFM (Imax higher and IC50 lower for fat mass). Weight "half-life" 22 weeks (about 2 years to a new steady state).
- Imax model, saturable. Tirzepatide is a GIP/GLP-1 agonist; the model cannot separate the two (p9).
- T2DM raised IC50 about 3-fold for both fat mass and FFM (p5); higher BMI lowered Kout (slower plateau) (p5).
- Not an opioid paper: no MEAC, ke0, tPeak or potency ratio. No hysteresis/effect-site delay: the delay is carried by the indirect-response Kout.
- Concentration unit is ng/mL of tirzepatide in plasma (Table 1); total vs free not stated in this PDF.

## Covariates
Sex (baseline FFM, fat mass, Imax, IC50: females lose more weight), Asian race (baseline FFM and fat mass), population (T2DM vs obesity without T2DM, on IC50), BMI (on Kout). No significant difference Black vs White (Figure S5, not in PDF). Age and GI adverse events tested; the PDF names them as assessed (p3) and does not report them as retained.

## Caveats
- FFM and fat mass are formula outputs, so their correlation is built in; the authors argue the DXA comparison supports identifiability (p9). Valid only for populations/drugs where the Janmahasatian formula holds (not children, not drugs acting on muscle).
- Table 1, Kout row: the bootstrap median prints as -3.43, which cannot be a median of a positive rate whose interval is 0.0295-0.0348; it appears to be a misprint (read on the page image, PDF p4).
- Table 1 prints the sex effects on Imax and IC50 as "fractional change" but the values (1.78, 0.809, 1.14, 2.05) look like multipliers rather than fractional changes; the PDF does not settle this (Imax FFM 1.78 would be +178% if a fractional change). NONMEM code is in the supplement (not read).
- The text says exposure is about 22% higher at 70 kg and 33% lower at 120 kg vs 90 kg (p2). (Derived: (90/70)^0.8 = 1.22 and (90/120)^0.8 = 0.79 from CL alone gives 21% lower, not 33%; the 33% must include other terms or is a different metric.)
- Employee-authored (Lilly), industry-funded.
