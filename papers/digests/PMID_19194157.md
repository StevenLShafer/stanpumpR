# Veselis 2009: propofol/midazolam ERP study of familiarity and recollection

## Study
- Healthy volunteers, 18-50 y, BMI < 30; 55 analysed (36 M/19 F; age 29.1 +/- 8.1 y; weight 71.5 +/- 12.4 kg; BMI 24.5; Results, PDF p. 6; Table 2). Groups: placebo 11, thiopental 11, propofol 10, midazolam 12, dexmedetomidine 11. Of 79 recruited, 67 took part and 12 were excluded (6 asleep or unable to perform, 6 technical EEG loss).
- Randomized parallel groups. Each person got two sequential doses: first aimed at 40% memory impairment (40AmnDose), then at 60% (60AmnDose). Testing began when predicted effect-site concentration was within 5% of target. A continuous recognition task (CRT) of IAPS pictures (3 blocks per dose, recognition 6 s or 27 s after first showing) with Cz (familiarity, 300-400 ms) and Pz (recollection, 400-600 ms) ERPs. Drug stopped after the CRTs; end-of-day yes/no recognition 225 min after the end of infusion (Table 1 timeline, PDF p. 5).
- Drug delivery: STANPUMP, effect-site targets, with models: thiopental Shafer; propofol Schnider; midazolam Greenblatt; dexmedetomidine "Markku Anttila" (printed p. 300).
- Targets (Methods, printed p. 300, rendered): thiopental 1.5 and 3 ug/mL; propofol 0.45 and 0.9 ug/mL; midazolam 20 and 35 ng/mL; dexmedetomidine 0.2 and 0.4 ng/mL. The abstract says midazolam 20 and 40, dexmedetomidine 0.25 and 0.4 (see open questions).
- No blood concentrations measured.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Not an opioid paper; no MEAC reported.
- No ke0/t1/2 ke0/tPeak/EC50 analysis. Hysteresis not examined. End points are behavioural (reaction time, false alarms, % correct recognition, forgetting to end of day) and ERP (old/new amplitude and area between waveforms, uV x ms).
- Table 3 (means, SD; text layer only): reaction time CRT overall placebo 824, thiopental 866, propofol 845, midazolam 950, dexmedetomidine 833 ms. Midazolam RT 187 ms (70-301) slower than placebo (P = 0.001).
- % correct recognition, higher dose (60AmnDose): midazolam 23.8% lower than placebo, propofol 11.4%, thiopental 11.1%; dexmedetomidine not different (p. 303). At the lower dose only midazolam differed (9.8% lower than placebo; P = 0.003).
- False alarms increased with drug by 6.2% overall and by 6.5% with dose.
- Forgetting from CRT to end of day: placebo 33.9% (SD 18.3); all active drugs more (14.7% for dexmedetomidine to 23.4% for propofol over placebo), no difference among active drugs (P = 0.40).
- ERPs: working-memory (6 s) areas not affected by any drug. At 27 s, propofol and midazolam cut the old/new area, about 50% by the authors' summary (familiarity P = 0.03; recollection P = 0.12). Propofol shifted ERP amplitude to smaller values (recollection 3.5 uV lower at higher dose, P < 0.001). Thiopental no ERP effect. Dexmedetomidine possibly reduced familiarity more than recollection (P = 0.10).
- Audit: MEAC none; ke0/t1/2 ke0/tPeak none (STANPUMP targets effect site, but the ke0 used is not reported); potency ratio none.

## Covariates
Age, weight (and presumably height, sex) as STANPUMP inputs. No covariate analysis on outcomes.

## Caveats
- Five parallel groups of 10-12 reduced power; several key effects are P = 0.09-0.12, treated by the authors as "suggestive".
- Targets, not measured concentrations; two inconsistent target values between abstract and Methods.
- Midazolam and propofol doses were not matched for equal memory effect; propofol produced ERP changes at a dose where behavioural memory impairment was smaller.
- Only correct trials contribute to ERPs, so they describe successful recognition.

## Verification against stanpumpR
Not applicable (no citation). Orientation only: the library's midazolam sedation band is 0.101-0.304 mcg/mL (101-304 ng/mL, Ramsay, `R/drugs_midazolam.R:179-193`), well above the 20-35 (or 40) ng/mL used here for a memory end point. Library midazolam kinetics (Zomorodi 1998, lines 150-157, tPeak 3.0 min, line 195) differ from the Greenblatt set the authors used. Propofol targets (0.45 and 0.9 ug/mL) can be compared with the library's propofol model only after identifying which of the library's propofol sets is applicable; not attempted here. Dexmedetomidine: library is Morse 2020 with tPeak 14.9 min (`R/drugs_dexmedetomidine.R:167`), the paper used a different set; the study's 0.2-0.4 ng/mL targets are in the library's units (ng/mL, header line 7).
