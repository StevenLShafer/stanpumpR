# Richard 1994: metoprolol IV kinetics, inter- and intra-subject variability

## Study
Retrospective pooled analysis of three bioavailability cross-over experiments (PDF p. 1-2). 18 healthy volunteers (12 M, 6 F), 20-34 y, 43-80 kg; 6 per experiment, two periods each, giving 36 IV profiles. Each period: an oral metoprolol formulation (OROS, conventional or slow-release tablet) plus, starting 55 min later, 15 mg deuterium-labelled metoprolol tartrate (6 D, isopropylamino group) IV over 5 min by syringe pump. Labelled and unlabelled drug measured together by GC-negative-ion MS in plasma, 10 mL blood samples to 31 h; LOQ about 20 nmol/L (5 ng/mL base). Labelled drug equals unlabelled (concentration ratio 0.98 +/- 0.03, n=18 in 2 volunteers on 50 mg oral). Analysis: point-area deconvolution of the 5-min infusion to an instantaneous injection, then non-linear least squares of a sum of exponentials (Survo 76, weight 1/conc^2); CL = Dose per kg / AUC (trapezoid, last concentration taken as 0); Vz = CL x t1/2 / ln2; variability by ANOVA with subject and period effects.

## Model and parameters
Biexponential (two-compartment, elimination from central) fit; a short departure from biphasic decline (median 0.75 h, 33 of 36 profiles) just after the rapid phase was omitted from the fits; triexponential fits gave half-lives over 14 h or negative parameters. 2 profiles were monophasic (subjects 12 and 15, one period each).
Table II (printed p. 161, PDF p. 5; mean, range; inter/intra-subject variability %): k10 1.11 /h (0.30-3.04; 66/55); k12 6.82 /h (0.57-13.6; 58/50); k21 2.34 /h (0.95-4.56; 36/35); Vc 1.00 L/kg (0.33-2.11; 52/44) (n=32 profiles, subjects 12 and 15 excluded); t1/2 3.4 h (1.6-8.8; 74/20); CL 0.913 L/h/kg (0.264-1.892; 57/15) (n=36). Median t1/2 2.8 h (text p. 160).
Table III (printed p. 162, PDF p. 6): median of subject means (n=18): k10 1.11, k12 6.52, k21 2.15 /h, Vc 0.87 L/kg; mean n=32: 1.11, 6.82, 2.34, 1.00; Regardh (n=4, 5 mg tartrate over 10 min): 0.45, 2.23, 2.10, 2.15; Jordo (n=6, 20 mg over 10 min): 1.01, 5.27, 2.06, 0.96. Table I (PDF p. 4) lists every profile's k10, k12, k21, Vc; read on the page image.
Regression through the origin: CL (L/h/kg) = 2.55 / t1/2(h), r = 0.94, n=18 (Fig. 3, p. 161); implied mean Vz 3.7 L/kg (text p. 161).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD measured, no effect-site constants, no MEAC. (Not an opioid.) The only concentration-related datum is the LOQ above.

## Covariates
None modelled. The authors attribute the wide t1/2 range to the debrisoquine (CYP2D6) polymorphism (ref. 11) but genotype was not measured. Intra-subject t1/2 is more reproducible (20%) than the model constants (35-55%), so t1/2 is the subject characteristic.

## Caveats
Healthy volunteers 20-34 y; concomitant oral dose in each period; the 5-min infusion converted to a bolus profile by deconvolution; fits omit the post-distribution bump; table values are per-kg and dose basis (tartrate vs base) is not stated for the 15 mg IV dose, which the text calls "labelled drug" (ampoules were tartrate).
