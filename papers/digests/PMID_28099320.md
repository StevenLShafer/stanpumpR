# Pasin 2017: closed-loop vs manual propofol TIVA, meta-analysis

## Study
Systematic review and meta-analysis of RCTs of BIS-guided closed-loop propofol delivery versus manually controlled propofol in adults (any surgery). Searches were updated to 15 April 2015 (PubMed, EMBASE, Cochrane Central, BioMedCentral, Scopus per the abstract). 313 records led to 12 trials with 1284 patients (642 closed loop, 642 control). Control was manual TIVA in 7 trials and TCI in 5. Trials were of medium quality (5 low risk of bias, 4 multicentre). Analysis used RevMan 5.2, random effects when I2 > 25%, 99% CI, alpha 0.01. Funding: none.
Devices (Table 1, p.458): CLADS, RUGLOOP II, Infusion Toolbox 95, CONCERT-CL, McSleepy, a self-designed interface and others. One included trial (Dussaussoy 2014, see `PMID_23494090`) used remifentanil in both arms. Table 2 (p.459) lists opioid doses per trial, but the text layer scrambles it (e.g. fentanyl 12.5, 10.5, 5.1, 4.8, 3.0 ug/kg; remifentanil 0.22, 0.19, 2.3, 1.7 ug/kg/min).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No ke0, EC50, MEAC or potency ratio. End point is BIS (target usually 40-60). Performance targets quoted from other work: Global Score 20-50 and median absolute performance error 10-20 are called clinically acceptable. Opioid audit: no opioid PD or MEAC; opioid doses per trial are listed in Table 2 only as background (fentanyl and remifentanil in unspecified columns).

## Covariates
Subgroup analyses by control type (TIVA vs TCI), device, mean age (< vs >= 55 y), and excluding cardiac and ambulatory surgery. Recovery-time benefit was larger with mean age >= 55 (2.51 min) than < 55 (0.89 min) (Table 3).

## Caveats
Few, small, medium-quality RCTs, high heterogeneity (I2 up to 90%); the propofol saving is small and statistically but perhaps not clinically meaningful; no outcome data on delirium, cognitive dysfunction or mortality; recovery judgement subjective; BIS is hard to interpret with opioids; results depend on controller algorithm. Versus TCI the authors find no clinically relevant advantage. Internal inconsistencies noted under open_questions.
