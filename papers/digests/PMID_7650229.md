# Sun 1995: 15N-nitroglycerin IV infusion alongside three transdermal patches

## Study
Single-centre, open-label, randomised three-way crossover (Ciba-Geigy / Medical College of Pennsylvania; PDF p2).
18 healthy men, 21-35 y (no weights given). A wash-in tolerance phase with Transderm-Nitro patches over 2 days, the
last removed 2 h before dosing. Each period: one 0.4 mg/h patch (Transderm-Nitro, Nitrodisc or Nitro-Dur II) for
14 h, with a simultaneous IV infusion of 15N-labelled nitroglycerin, 7.1 ug/mL at 35 mL/h = 0.25 mg/h, for 14 h (PDF
p2). Blood (7 mL) at 0, 0.25, 0.5, 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 14 h (end of infusion and patch removal), 14.08, 14.17,
14.5, 15 and 16 h; **sampling site not stated**. GC/MS for labelled and unlabelled GTN, 1,2-GDN and 1,3-GDN. Non-
compartmental: CL_IV = IV dose / AUC of [15N]GTN; transdermal amount = AUC x CL_IV (PDF p3).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None measured (no haemodynamics reported).

## Covariates
None.

## Caveats
Healthy, nitrate-tolerised (wash-in) men lying with a patch on; IV labelled drug given beside transdermal drug, and
both stop at 14 h; sampling site unstated; Figure 1 is the only source of IV concentrations and is small (values are
readings, +/- ~10 pg/mL). The first post-stop sample is at 4.8 min, so the early fall is one point per arm; the
residual 25-30 pg/mL after 30 min sits near the predose labelled reading (~15 pg/mL). Wide intersubject AUC range
(1621-21500 pg.h/mL, PDF p5).
