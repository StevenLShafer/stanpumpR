# Deferm 2021: mannitol clearance (GFR) in asphyxiated neonates under therapeutic hypothermia

## Study
PK sub-study of the ALBINO trial (NCT03162653; allopurinol vs mannitol placebo for hypoxic-ischaemic
encephalopathy), four centres (Amsterdam, Nürnberg, Tübingen, Utrecht). Only the placebo (mannitol) arm analysed:
17 (near-)term neonates, 13 given therapeutic hypothermia (TH, 33.5 °C for 72 h); the 4 without TH had mild HIE.
Table 2 (PDF p4), medians [IQR]: GA 39.5 [1] weeks, birthweight 3.4 [0.5] kg, length 54.1 cm, age at start of
cooling 2.1 h, inotropes 8/17. Dosing (PDF p2): first IV dose within 45 min of birth, stated in the Methods as
20 mg/kg (allopurinol or mannitol); neonates who went on to TH had a second 10 mg/kg dose over 10 min at 12 h (the
abstract mentions only the 10 mg/kg second dose). Sampling schedules A/B (Table 1, PDF p3, rendered): 15-60 min,
1.5-4 h, then 8-12 h (no TH) or a 12-h trough and 13-14 h (TH), and 18-24/60-72 h or 36-48/96-168 h. 77
observations. Assay: LC-MS after derivatization, total plasma mannitol, range 0.2-200 µg/mL, LLOQ 0.2 µg/mL.
NONMEM 7.4, FOCE-I; SIR (2000/1000) and NPDE evaluation.

## Model and parameters
One-compartment, first-order elimination (OFV 450 vs 454 for two compartments), combined residual error. No
covariate was significant in stepwise selection; BW, GA and TH were added on prior knowledge (PDF p4-5). Final
model, Table 3 (PDF p5, rendered), footnotes: CL = PopCL x (BW/3.4)^0.75 x GA^3.4/(47.7^3.4 + GA^3.4) x thetaTH;
V = PopV x (BW/3.4)^1.

Base model: CL 0.0760 L/h, V 1.440 L, IIV CL 176%, V 49.7%. Derived: typical CL with TH 0.147 x 0.399 = 0.059 L/h;
no-TH half-life about 1.4 x ln2 / (0.441 x 0.345) = 6.4 h (derived, BW 3.4 kg, GA 39.5 wk). NPDE mean 0.004, variance 1.003, slight
non-normality.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. Mannitol clearance is used as a GFR surrogate: freely filtered, not metabolised, renal elimination only,
with limited tubular reabsorption (10%) in adults and immature tubular transport in neonates (Introduction, PDF p1).

## Covariates
Birthweight (allometric, fixed), gestational age (sigmoid maturation, fixed to Rhodin), TH (estimated, about 60%
decrease). LDH and ALT trends were driven by one outlier and dropped; PNA gave a non-physiological negative effect
and was dropped (PDF p8).

## Caveats
n = 17; TH perfectly confounded with asphyxia severity (only mild HIE without TH); covariates not statistically
significant; very large IIV on CL; about one sample per neonate before cooling, so pre-TH CL not estimable; authors
advise against extrapolating to other renally cleared drugs (PDF p8). No pregnant or adult subjects.

## Verification against stanpumpR
- **Mannitol clearance as a GFR marker (R :16, registry :64)**: **matches** the paper's rationale (PDF p1).
- **"filtered at the glomerulus and not reabsorbed" (R :14-15) / "without reabsorption" (pregnancyRegistry.csv:64,
  data-raw :741)**: the paper says there is limited tubular reabsorption, about 10%, in adults: **discrepancy**
  (minor wording; it does not change the code).
- **The 1.5 factor**: not from this paper, which has no pregnant subjects and no GFR-in-pregnancy data; the repo
  labels the factor physiological, not measured for mannitol, and the registry confidence low, which is accurate.
  Listing a neonatal hypothermia study as the "first source" (docs/pregnancy.md:85) is a citation for the marker
  property only: **matches** as worded, but the paper supplies no pregnancy evidence.
- The registry's evidence_read is "abstract"; this card read the full text. Citation, journal, pages, DOI, PMID:
  **matches**.
- The library's mannitol model is Kaneda 2010 (adults); no Deferm parameter is used, so nothing numeric to check.
