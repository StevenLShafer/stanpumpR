# Zaouter 2017: automated propofol sedation for TAVI (pilot)

## Study
- Prospective observational single-center pilot, CHU Bordeaux, 1 Feb to 1 Jun 2015; ethics board waived written consent (PDF p2). 20 consecutive patients aged 65 y or more having transfemoral TAVI; 19 analysed (one conversion to general anesthesia for pericardial tamponade).
- Table 1 (PDF p4): age 82 (8) y; 12 female (60%); BMI 29 (6) kg/m2; sedation time 102 (17) min; logistic EuroSCORE 11 [9-19]%; LVEF 60 (11)%; COPD 20%.
- Premedication oral midazolam 0.1 mg/kg one hour before; no opioids. Abdominal-wall blocks with ropivacaine 0.5% and lidocaine 1%; acetaminophen 1 g after valve. Hardware: Philips IntelliVue BIS module, Graseby 3400 pump, RS-232 control.
- Primary outcome: success without manual override, 19/20 = 95% (99% CI 68-100%). No propofol concentration measured.

## Model and parameters
No PK model. Same hybrid sedation system as PMID_26970975, with details (PDF p2-3):
- Bolus 250 ug/kg over 2 min, then 50 ug/kg/min for 3 min.
- Profile from three BIS values: below 60 light, 60-90 moderate, above 90 profound. Initial maintenance 100 (light), 125 (moderate), 150 (profound) ug/kg/min. RR threshold 13 (light, moderate) or 12 (profound).
- Dose = previous dose x Km x Kh, every 2 min from the last six BIS values. When SpO2 below 92 or RR below 8: mean BIS below 53 cuts the rate 50%; BIS 58 or less cuts it 25%; above 58 no cut. RR below 8 or SpO2 below 92 for more than 2 min cuts 50%; over 5 min stops it until safe for over 30 s. DSS button cuts the rate by 10%.
- Delivered: median 65 (IQR 53-75) ug/kg/min; 15 (SD 3) rate changes/h (PDF p4).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: BIS target 65; no ke0, tPeak, EC50, hysteresis, MEAC or potency ratio. (Not an opioid paper.)
- Bands: excellent within 10%, very good 11-20%, good 21-30%, inadequate above 30% of 65.
- Table 2 (PDF p5, page image), mean (SD), n=19: excellent 21.5 (12.5)%, very good 28.6 (8.8), good 19.2 (9.5), inadequate 30.7 (12.7); MDPE -2.4 (11.6)%, MDAPE 23.8 (4.9)%, Wobble 20.3 (4.4)%, Divergence -0.13 (0.15) %/min. Excellent to good together 69% (99% CI 53-77; IQR 59-79) (abstract).
- Table 3: % of time BIS<55 37.2 (21.9); <60 42.4 (22.6); >75 25.5 (14.1); >80 14.8 (8.9).
- Safety: 15 patients had critical respiratory events (median 3/h), 6 had MAP<60 mm Hg (median 56 mm Hg), all treated with ephedrine plus 5 mL/kg crystalloid. Nine had an automatic 50% rate cut; one had a 2-min stop (PDF p4).
- Comparison to the orthopedic cohort (PDF p5): excellent-to-good 69% vs 90%, more time with BIS<55; authors attribute this to elderly sensitivity (median dose 65 here vs 87 in the orthopedic study), an inference, not measured.

## Covariates
Age is contrasted informally (82 y here vs 64 y in the orthopedic trial); no covariate model.

## Caveats
- n=20 pilot, no control arm, observational. Authors list small size and non-generalisability to morbid obesity (PDF p7).
- Control quality was worse than in the orthopedic trial (inadequate 30.7%), so the "frail patients" claim rests on a cross-study comparison.
- Midazolam premedication and regional blocks confound sedation depth.
