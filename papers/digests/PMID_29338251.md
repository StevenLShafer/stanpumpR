# Tanaka 2018: oxytocin IV and nasal kinetics and nose-to-brain delivery in rats and mice

## Study
- PK study: male Wistar/ST rats, 230-250 g, pentobarbital anaesthesia (52 mg/kg ip) for femoral cannulation.
  Oxytocin 2.0 mg/kg IV (2.5 mg/mL, 200 uL, femoral vein) or the same dose intranasally (50 mg/mL, 10 uL; rats
  conscious after dosing). Arterial plasma sampling; n = 3-4 per group (Fig. 1 legend).
- Nasal degradation: in situ nasal perfusion (5 ug/mL) under pentobarbital, 180 min (Fig. 2).
- Brain distribution: male ddY mice, 25 g; 5 uL in, 100 uL IV or ip (40 and 2 mg/mL solutions); plasma and three
  brain sections at 5 min (Fig. 3, Table 2).
- PD: restraint-stress mice (2 h), plasma corticosterone by LC/MS (Fig. 4).
- Assay: LC/MS (Shimadzu LCMS-2020), linear 2 ng/mL to 10 ug/mL, r2 > 0.998. Plasma is the matrix; no
  immunoassay.
- PK analysis: WinNonlin, IV data fitted to a two-compartment model (lowest AIC, weight 1/Y^2); nasal data by
  moment analysis. Data are mean +/- SE.

## Model and parameters
Two-compartment model chosen for IV, but **only CL and central volume are reported**; no V2, Q or micro-constants
are given anywhere in the text or tables. Table 1 (mean +/- SE, per rat, not per kg):

Discussion: CL per kg = 16 mL/min/kg (authors, on a 250 g rat). Derived: V_central per kg about 87-95 mL/kg;
Vss = CL x MRT = 4.01 x 6.97 = 28 mL per rat (derived, about 0.11-0.12 L/kg).
Brain study (mice, 5 min): plasma 0.19 (in), 3.64 (IV), 3.57 (ip) ug/mL; olfactory bulb 0.70 / 0.02 / 0.04 ug/mL.
Direct transport percentage (Table 2): olfactory bulb 99.6%, frontal half 97.1%, occipital half 96.2%.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No concentration-effect model, no ke0, no tPeak. End point: plasma corticosterone after restraint stress in mice
(naive 10-100, stressed 136-232 ng/mL); intranasal but not IV oxytocin suppressed the rise (Fig. 4). Human
oxytocin half-lives of 10.3 and 15.3 min and a human blood clearance of about 200 mL/min (4 mL/min/kg) are quoted
from other papers in the Discussion. Not an opioid; no MEAC or potency ratio.

## Covariates
None (single body-weight range; dose scaled per kg).

## Caveats
Rodents only; n = 3-4; anaesthetised for IV sampling; mean +/- SE; peripheral compartment parameters not
reported; abstract (about 2%) and Table 1 (1.41%) differ on nasal F; brain-delivery calculation uses single 5 min
concentrations rather than AUCs.
