# Lim 2013: haloperidol D2 receptor occupancy ([18F]fallypride PET) PK/PD model

## Study
- 8 healthy Korean men, 19-45 y, 57.0-89.9 kg, within 20% of ideal weight.
- Open, parallel: oral haloperidol 0.5 mg (n = 3), 1 mg (n = 2) or 3 mg (n = 3) once daily for 7 days.
- PET ([18F]fallypride, 0.1 mCi/kg, two dynamic sessions over about 3 h) at baseline, 24 h (day 8) and 120 h (day 12) after the last dose; binding potential by simplified reference tissue model (cerebellum reference) in putamen and caudate.
- Plasma: predose, 6, 24, 48, 96, 144 h after first dose; 0.5-168 h after the 7th dose. LC-MS/MS, LLOQ 0.05 ng/mL, total plasma.
- NONMEM VII (FOCE-I), ADVAN6; sequential PK then PD with individual PK.

## Model and parameters

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: striatal D2 receptor occupancy from [18F]fallypride PET; RO% = 100 x (E0 - BP)/E0.
- ke0 0.213 /h (putamen), 0.209 /h (caudate); text gives the effect-compartment half-lives as 3.25 and 3.32 h (= ln2/ke0). Hysteresis is between plasma and D2 binding, informed by the two post-dose scans (24 h and 120 h after the last dose).
- C50 (Ce at half-maximal inhibition) 0.791 ng/mL putamen, 0.650 ng/mL caudate; the abstract calls these plasma concentrations (equal at steady state). No separate Imax is listed; the inhibitory model with RO computed from E0 implies inhibition to zero BP (Emax 100% occupancy).
- Observed occupancy 24 h after the 7th dose (Table I, mean, CV%): putamen 15.1 (0.5 mg), 35.7 (1 mg), 46.3 (3 mg); caudate 17.3, 39.6, 52.4.
- Target window cited: 65-80% (or 70-80%) occupancy; >80% linked to EPS (refs 9, 10, 23). Simulation: 2 mg every 12 h (and 3 mg every 24 h) near the window; 2 mg q12h called near optimal.
- No MEAC, no potency ratio.

## Covariates
None tested (n = 8).

## Caveats
- n = 8, three dose groups of 2-3; only two post-dose PET scans per subject, so ke0 rests on few points; caudate ke0 RSE of 0.9% is implausibly small.
- Observed occupancies (15-52%) lie below the C50-to-Emax region; Emax is implicit, not estimated.
- PK model is nonlinear (saturable binding), so its ke0 and C50 belong to that model's plasma predictions.
- Table II 95% CIs are scrambled in the text layer and not used here.
