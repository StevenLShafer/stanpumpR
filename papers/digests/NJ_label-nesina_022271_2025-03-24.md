## Study
Not a study: the US prescribing information for the DPP-4 inhibitor alogliptin (adults with type 2 diabetes; not for type 1). Sections quoted below are those read.

- Exposure is dose-proportional from 25 to 400 mg single and multiple dose. PK in healthy subjects and in patients with T2DM was similar (12.3).
- Metabolism is limited: M-I (N-demethylated, active DPP-4 inhibitor, under 1% of parent) and M-II (N-acetylated, inactive, under 6% of parent); CYP2D6 and CYP3A4 contribute in vitro. The drug is more than 99% the (R)-enantiomer with little chiral conversion.
- Food: a high-fat meal gave no significant change in total or peak exposure (12.3).
- Dialysis removes about 7% of the drug in a 3 h session (10 and 12.3).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- DPP-4 inhibition (12.2): single doses of 12.5 to 800 mg in healthy subjects gave peak inhibition within 2 to 3 h, above 93%, and above 80% at 24 h for doses of 25 mg or more. In patients with T2DM, peak inhibition came within 1 to 2 h, above 93% at 25, 100 and 400 mg after one dose and after 14 days, and above 81% at 24 h after 14 days.
- Active GLP-1: peak and 24 h exposure three- to four-fold greater than placebo at 25 to 200 mg.
- QTc (12.2): no increase at 50 mg or 400 mg once daily for 7 days (n = 257 over four arms); peak concentration at 400 mg was 19-fold the peak after 25 mg.
- No ke0, tPeak, hysteresis time course, EC50, MEAC or therapeutic or toxic plasma concentration range is stated. No concentration target exists; dosing is by renal function and glycaemic response.

## Covariates
- Renal impairment (12.3, single-dose 50 mg study): AUC about 1.2-fold (CrCl 60 to under 90 mL/min), about 2-fold (30 to under 60), about 3-fold (15 to under 30) and about 4-fold (ESRD, CrCl under 15 or dialysis) versus healthy subjects. The healthy comparator is not defined in the label.
- Dose by CrCl (2.2): 25 mg once daily at 60 mL/min or more; 12.5 mg at 30 to under 60; 6.25 mg at 15 to under 30 and in ESRD. CrCl is not defined (equation, weight) in the label.
- Moderate hepatic impairment (Child-Pugh B): AUC about 10% lower and Cmax about 8% lower versus healthy subjects; Child-Pugh C not studied.
- Age (18 to 80 y), sex and race (White, Black, Asian): no clinically meaningful effect. Weight is not mentioned in the sections read.
- Pregnancy (8.1): no human PK; limited human data; placental transfer in rats; no adverse developmental effects in rats and rabbits at 180- and 149-times the 25 mg clinical AUC. Lactation (8.2): no human data; present in rat milk. Not established in children (8.4).
- Dose basis (2.1, 3, 11): tablets are 25, 12.5 and 6.25 mg of alogliptin, supplied as 34, 17 and 8.5 mg alogliptin benzoate. Doses are therefore in mg of alogliptin base.

## Caveats
- Label PK is given as rounded means with no confidence intervals or study sizes.
- The renal-impairment fold changes are class averages of a 50 mg single-dose study and say nothing about CrCl within a class.
