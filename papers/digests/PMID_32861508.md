# Bae 2020: allometric fentanyl PK model and MEC/MEAC after open abdominal surgery

## Study
Two trials at Asan Medical Center, Seoul (KCT0003273), ASA 1-2, age 20-80 yr, PDF p2 (printed 977).
- **PK study** (n = 30): fentanyl citrate 100 µg i.v. bolus before incision under propofol-remifentanil TCI;
  14 arterial samples to 12 h. Pooled with 65 subjects from five earlier studies supplied by Steven L. Shafer
  (McClain & Hug n = 7, Scott & Stanski 19, Hudson 10, Varvel 8, Shafer 21; Table 1, p3). Total 95 subjects,
  2092 concentrations analysed (3 excluded). NONMEM VII, FOCE-I, 1-3 compartments, log-normal IIV, constant-CV
  residual error; bootstrap 2000 replicates.
- **MEAC study** (n = 30, 120 samples): open stomach (17), colorectal (10), hepatobiliary (3) surgery;
  laparoscopy excluded. Fentanyl 1 µg/kg 30 min before end of surgery. In PACU, VAS (0-10) every 10 min at rest
  and with 20 N wound compression. Sample 1 when VAS ≥3 at rest or ≥5 with compression; then fentanyl 50 µg
  every 10 min until <3 at rest and <5 with compression → sample 2 (first MEAC). When pain returned → sample 3
  (MEC). Titrated again → sample 4 (second MEAC). No NSAIDs.
- Assay: **venous** (MEAC study) or arterial (PK study) **plasma** (EDTA), total fentanyl, LC-MS/MS, LLOQ
  0.01 ng/mL, range 0.01-20 ng/mL (p3).

## Model and parameters
Three-compartment mammillary, allometric with estimated exponents (θ7 on volumes, θ8 on clearances),
reference 70 kg. Table 2 (PDF p5, printed 980; confirmed on page image). Population fit MDPE -2.1%,
MDAPE 22.7%; post hoc 1.2% / 8.3%.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Opioid audit (all from PDF p6, printed 981, page image checked; plasma = venous EDTA plasma, total fentanyl,
LC-MS/MS; population: 30 Korean ASA 1-2 adults in PACU after open abdominal surgery):
- **MEC** (pain returning after titrated relief, sample 3; VAS ≥3 rest or ≥5 with 20 N compression):
  median **0.72 ng/mL** (IQR 0.58-1.05). Range 0.31-1.56 (Discussion p8).
- **MEAC** (pain relieved after 50 µg titration, VAS <3 rest and <5 compression): first MEAC 0.97
  (0.70-1.20), second 1.04 (0.81-1.34); across both, median **0.99 ng/mL** (range 0.35-1.79). Abstract
  gives IQR 0.76-1.28.
- First sample on arrival in pain: 0.15 (0.13-0.17) ng/mL.
- Effect-site estimates: Ce at MEC 1.09 (0.72-1.57), Ce at MEAC 1.73 (1.44-2.55) ng/mL, computed with
  **ke0 0.147 /min** taken from Scott & Stanski 1987 (EEG end point; not estimated here). No tPeak observed
  or estimated (Discussion p9).
- Logistic (probability of no need for rescue fentanyl vs measured Cp): Cp50 0.63 ng/mL, γ 2.24; IIV
  negligible.
- **Potency ratio**: MEAC ratio oxycodone:fentanyl **75:1**, from oxycodone median MEACs 74.1 and 76.1 ng/mL
  in the same group's earlier study (Choi 2017, ref 14), same design and investigator (p7, printed 982-983).
  Fig 3b re-plots that study with oxycodone MEC 63.4 ng/mL.
- Doses: 250 (100-350) µg to first MEAC, 50 (50-100) µg to second.
- MEC/MEAC did not differ by surgical group (P = 0.682 / 0.517).

## Covariates
Weight only: volumes × (WT/70)^1.23, clearances × (WT/70)^0.313 (estimated; chosen over fixed 1/0.75
exponents by ΔOFV 12.61, P = 0.002; MDAPE 22.7 vs 23.7%).

## Caveats
- Results text calls the MEC the "second blood sample" (p6), but Methods and Fig 3a place the MEC at the
  third sample; Methods/Fig 3 are consistent with each other and with the values.
- The MEAC IQR 0.76-1.28 appears only in the abstract; Results give a range.
- ke0 borrowed from another population (Scott & Stanski), so the Ce values are model-dependent.
- PK in anaesthetised surgical patients with propofol/remifentanil; bolus-only data in the new cohort.
- Single-centre, n = 30 Korean patients, open surgery only; authors call their MEAC an upper reference value.
- Oxycodone ratio pairs two separate cohorts.
