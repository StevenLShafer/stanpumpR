# Komatsu 2024: unbound cefazolin population PK in urological surgery

## Study
- Prospective single-centre cohort, Kitasato University Hospital, Dec 2021 - Jul 2023: 152 patients (139 men, 13 women), 101 laparoscopic radical prostatectomy, 51 laparoscopic partial nephrectomy.
- Table 1 medians (IQR): age 66 (61-73) y; weight 66.5 (59.4-71.9) kg; CLcr 70 (67-86) mL/min; albumin 32 (30-34) g/L; bilirubin 17.1 umol/L.
- Dose: 1 g (< 80 kg) or 2 g (>= 80 kg, n = 17) IV "over a 10 min period" (Methods); redosing after 3 h in 113 patients (3 had a second redose). Simulations used 15-min infusions.
- Samples at incision, resection, redosing, skin closure; 614 total and 610 unbound serum concentrations. HPLC-UV, unbound by Nanosep 10K ultrafiltration; LLOQ 0.5 mg/L.
- NONMEM 7.3, FOCE-I; bootstrap 1,000 (921 successful).
- CLcr by Cockcroft-Gault (Methods); weight used in CG not stated.

- Unbound about 12% of total (Discussion). Observed medians (Table 2, as read): at incision total 128.6, unbound 14.9 mg/L at 0.40 h.
- Half-life 1.8-2.2 h with normal renal function (cited). Weight not a significant covariate (narrow range).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- Target: unbound trough above MIC at 3, 4, 6, 12 h after 1 g over 15 min; MIC scenarios 0.5 mg/L and 1 mg/L. The paper labels these inconsistently (abstract: MIC90 0.5 and MIC50 1.0 for MSSA or E. coli; Methods: MIC50 1 mg/L E. coli and 0.5 mg/L MSSA; Fig 4 caption: both "for E. coli" and "mg/mL").
- Redosing interval: 3 h for CLcr >= 50, 6 h for CLcr 20-45, 12 h for CLcr <= 15 mL/min.

## Covariates
CLcr (Cockcroft-Gault) on CLu, power 0.586 normalised to 70 mL/min; albumin on Bmax, power 0.401 normalised to 32 g/L. Age, weight, bilirubin, serum creatinine tested.

## Caveats
- Mostly older Japanese men, median 66.5 kg; only 16-17 patients > 80 kg; sparse data beyond 6 h.
- Low-blood-loss laparoscopic surgery; albumin changes small.
- Infusion duration inconsistent between Methods (10 min) and simulations/abstract (15 min).
- Article was corrected on 30 May 2024 (Table 3 column headings).
