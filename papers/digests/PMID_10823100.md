# Strachan 2000: remifentanil added to propofol, BIS and sedation

## Study
- Double-blind, placebo-controlled, 40 adult patients (16-80 y, ASA I-II, within 20% of ideal body weight, no premedication) before routine surgery, in the anaesthetic room, 30 min. 10 per group.
- All: propofol TCI 2 ug/mL (Graseby Diprifuser) for 15 min. Then randomised for a further 15 min to placebo or remifentanil 0.01, 0.05 or 0.1 ug/kg/min added to propofol. O2 by facemask.
- BIS (Aspect 1000, v2.51, bifrontal F7/F8, ref Fp2), 30-s average every 5 min; sedation by the OAAS responsiveness scale (5 alert to 1 no response to prodding) every 5 min. Venous propofol at 10, 20, 30 min by HPLC with fluorimetric detection (not reported in numbers; no group difference). Spearman correlation, ANOVA (SPSS 7.5.1).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: change in BIS from the value at 15 min of propofol alone (baseline), measured 5, 10 and 15 min after starting the remifentanil dose (Fig 1, PDF p. 2, printed 490; bars read from the chart, approximate).
- Mean BIS at 15 min before the randomised dose: 74.1 (placebo), 82.8 (0.01), 75.1 (0.05), 71.6 (0.1 ug/kg/min); no significant difference.
- Mean reduction in BIS at 15 min (read from Fig 1): about 5 (placebo), 7 (0.01), 10 (0.05), 16.5 (0.1). Dose vs BIS change at 15 min: Spearman r=0.415, P<0.01. BIS vs sedation score across all patients: r=0.768, P<0.01.
- Respiratory rate lower in the 0.1 group at 20, 25, 30 min (P<0.01); 4 of 10 at 0.1 became apnoeic and needed chin lift and hand ventilation, so that rate was judged too great. Haemodynamics stable.
- ke0 / t½ke0 / tPeak / hysteresis: none reported. MEAC: none. EC50/Emax: none (infusion rates only, no concentrations). Potency ratio: none. The BIS was still falling at 15 min for the higher doses (Fig 1), so the values are not steady state.
- Population: awake adult surgical patients on propofol 2 ug/mL; EEG (BIS) end point, not analgesia.

## Covariates
None analysed.

## Caveats
- Short communication, n=10 per group, no concentrations, apnoeic patients may have been aroused by stimulation (authors note this raised BIS), and baseline BIS differed numerically between groups.
- Propofol target is a pump prediction (the paper reports venous HPLC concentrations only as "no difference").
