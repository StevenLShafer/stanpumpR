# Goke 1995: Voglibose (AO-128) and the enteroinsular axis in healthy volunteers

## Study
Double-blind, randomised, parallel-group study in 72 healthy male volunteers aged 18-40 y (within 15% of ideal weight; mean weight about 74-77 kg, Table 1). Six groups of 12: voglibose 0.5, 1.0, 2.0 or 5.0 mg or placebo (two groups of 12, pooled to n = 24), one tablet three times daily with meals (08:00, 13:00, 18:00) for 7 days. Higher doses (2, 5 mg) were run in a second, later section. Standard breakfast (800 kcal; 55.5% carbohydrate, 42.7% fat, 1.8% protein) on days -1, 1 and 7; blood at 0, 15, 30, 45, 60, 90, 120, 150, 180 min. Measured: glucose, insulin, C-peptide (serum), GIP (commercial RIA) and GLP-1 (competitive RIA, antibody GA 1178; sensitivity 2 fmol/tube; inter-/intra-assay CV 10.2/3.4%). AUC0-3h and peak by model-independent methods; ANOVA, Bonferroni.

## Model and parameters
No PK model; voglibose plasma concentrations were not measured (authors state it acts locally and gives no measurable circulating concentrations, citing refs 4 and 20). No PK parameters.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Dose-dependent reduction of postprandial glucose, insulin (maximum 60-70% reduction at 5 mg) and C-peptide (up to about 50% at 5 mg), AUC0-3h and Cmax (Figs 1-3). Effect at 0.5 and 1 mg larger on day 7 than day 1; at 2 and 5 mg already maximal on day 1. GIP postprandial rise reduced, significant at 2 and 5 mg from day 1 and at lower doses by day 7. GLP-1 increased: first dose 1 mg gave over 80% above control (p < 0.001); 1 mg three times daily for 7 days gave about 90% above control, not increased by 2 or 5 mg. GLP-1 elevation also prolonged over the 3 h. No ke0, EC50, MEAC or potency ratio. Not an opioid.

## Covariates
None analysed (healthy young men, narrow weight range).

## Caveats
Healthy normoglycaemic men, not diabetics. Single standardised breakfast. Adverse events (flatulence, diarrhoea, etc.) in 43 of 48 voglibose subjects (59.7% of 72 as printed) and 4 of 24 placebo (47 of 72 overall); transient SGPT rises in placebo and voglibose groups without dose dependence. No PK sampling.
