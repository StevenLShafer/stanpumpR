# Navani 2013: in vivo opioid activity of norhydrocodone (mouse)

## Study
- Animal study: male Swiss Webster mice, 28 ± 2 g. St. John's University, New York.
- Receptor binding in mouse spinal cord homogenate ([3H]DAMGO mu, [3H]DPDPE delta, [3H]U69,593 kappa).
- Analgesia by tail-flick (10 s cut-off; quantal for ED50 by probit, BLISS-21) after subcutaneous (SC), intrathecal (IT)
  and intracerebroventricular (ICV) hydrocodone (HYC), hydromorphone (HYM) and norhydrocodone (NHYC); naltrexone
  antagonism; IT seizure dose-response. Doses as free base. n = 5-15 per group.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Opioid audit:
- MEAC / analgesic concentration: none; no concentrations measured, doses only.
- ke0 / t½ke0 / tPeak: none estimated; only time of peak tail-flick effect after each route (above).
- Potency ratios (mouse tail-flick ED50): SC hydromorphone ~5.4x hydrocodone, norhydrocodone ~70x less; IT
  hydromorphone ~174x; ICV hydromorphone ~96x, norhydrocodone similar to hydrocodone. All analgesia naltrexone-
  reversible; SC norhydrocodone analgesia fully blocked by ICV naltrexone (supraspinal).
- Neuroexcitation: IT seizures with all three, not naltrexone-reversible; norhydrocodone ~4.6x and hydromorphone ~3.7x
  as potent as hydrocodone. No seizures after SC or ICV dosing at the doses used.

## Covariates
None.

## Caveats
Mouse only, tail-flick end point; no plasma or brain concentrations, so potency differences mix intrinsic activity
with CNS access. The text layer drops the Greek mu, so IT/ICV doses printed as "mg/mouse" (e.g. IT hydrocodone 1-5)
are very probably µg/mouse; check the PDF before quoting absolute IT/ICV values. Human relevance of norhydrocodone
activity is unestablished (authors call for CNS concentration studies).
