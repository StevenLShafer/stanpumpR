# Bihorel 2023: molnupiravir (NHC) population PK

## Study
Pooled PopPK of plasma NHC (the circulating metabolite of the prodrug molnupiravir; parent not detectable) after oral molnupiravir. N = 1207 individuals, 4202 NHC concentrations (of 4750 post-dose samples; 88% quantifiable): phase I MK-4482-004 (n = 100 healthy; single doses 50-1600 mg, food effect, 50-800 mg q12h for 6 days), phase IIa MK-4482-006 (n = 66), MOVe-IN (n = 196 hospitalised), MOVe-OUT (n = 845 non-hospitalised; 194 phase II plus 651 phase III). COVID-19 doses 200/400/800 mg q12h for 5 days. LC-MS/MS, LLOQ 5 ng/mL (004, 006) and 1 ng/mL (MOVe-IN/OUT). Doses and concentrations converted to molar (molnupiravir 329.31 Da, NHC 259.2 Da). NONMEM 7.3, FOCE-I. Population: 51.7% men; age median 46 (18-91) y; weight median 85 (36.1-172) kg; BMI mean 30.4. Sparse sampling in patients (72.8% had two or fewer samples) (Methods, Results, Table 1, PDF pp. 3-6).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD model, no ke0. The exposure metric tied to clinical response, by the sponsor's exposure-response analysis (ref 15; also unpublished data), is AUC0-12. Clinical comparability bounds are 0.7-2.0 times reference, about 23,800-68,000 nmol*h/L at 800 mg q12h; the lowest exposure octile in MOVe-OUT had median AUC 21,020 nmol*h/L (95% CI 15,650-23,780). Plasma NHC is a surrogate; the active NHC-triphosphate is intracellular and not measured. Not an opioid.

## Covariates
Final: body weight on CL/F (power 0.412); BMI and sex on Vc/F; food status and formulation on MTT. No effect of age, race, ethnicity, renal function (48% mild, 7% moderate impairment by eGFR) or hepatic function (modified Child-Pugh; 60 mild, 3 moderate); the hospitalisation shift was removed. Forest plot (Figure 3, text layer): BMI over 40 gives AUC0-12 GMR 0.787 (0.73-0.848); women have about 15% higher Cmax but similar AUC; moderate renal impairment AUC about +18%, judged not clinically relevant by the authors.

## Caveats
Authors: few participants with moderate hepatic impairment; food status not recorded in phase II/III and imputed by mixture model (25% of unknown set to high-fat); IIV/IOV on Vc and MTT estimable in only about a third; no allometric scaling on Q and Vp, so not for paediatric use. Parameters are apparent and conflate F with CL and V. The weight exponent is less than proportional (0.412).
