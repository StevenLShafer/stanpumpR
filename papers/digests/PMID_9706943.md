# Koblin 1998: MAC of noble gases, nitrogen and SF6 in rats

## Study
- Adult male Sprague-Dawley **rats** in pressure chambers; compression at 2 atm/min; oxygen held at 0.5-1.5 atm; temperature 36.5-39 °C.
- End point: MAC = partial pressure preventing movement to electrical tail stimulation in 50% (per-animal average of the pressures just permitting and just preventing movement).
- Gases with lethal pressures overlapping their MAC (nitrogen, SF6) were assessed by **additivity with desflurane**: MAC = applied partial pressure / (1 − desflurane MAC with gas / control desflurane MAC).

Helium and neon: no anesthesia; convulsions at 84.6 ± 22.2 and 91.3 ± 7.0 atm; helium raised desflurane MAC 19% (P = 0.018). Nitrogen oil/gas 0.069, MAC × oil/gas 7.45 atm (Table 2) — the largest deviation from Meyer-Overton.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
MAC for immobility to tail stimulation in rats; no kinetics, no ke0. Not an opioid; no MEAC.

## Covariates
None.

## Caveats
Rat, not human; nitrogen MAC is an extrapolation by assumed additivity with desflurane (the direct value, 46.5 atm, is confounded by lethality); nitrogen MAC by additivity rose with the nitrogen pressure used (108 to 180 atm).
