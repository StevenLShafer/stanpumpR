# Haynes 2022: MSSA MIC distributions for cefalexin, cefadroxil and comparators

## Study
- 48 unique MSSA isolates (one per patient; 49 identified, 1 excluded) from children admitted to Children's Hospital Colorado with musculoskeletal infection, Jan 2018 - May 2020; from blood 81%, bone 15%, synovial fluid 4%. Patients: mean age 9.1 (SD 4.8) y, 52% male, 88% osteomyelitis.
- Broth microdilution, custom Sensititre panel 0.25-16 (units printed as "mg/mL", evidently ug/mL = mg/L), standard inoculum, duplicates; the higher of duplicate MICs taken; QC with ATCC 29213. Comparators: oxacillin, cephalothin, ceftaroline, cefazolin (n = 47 for cefazolin), cefadroxil, cephalexin.

No PK. MIC distributions (Table 1, counts cross-checked with Table 2 for the two oral drugs):

- Cefadroxil vs cephalexin paired MICs: equal in 29 (60%); cefadroxil one dilution lower in 10 (21%), cephalexin lower in 9 (19%); Wilcoxon P = 0.28.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PK/PD analysis. Discussion cites a pediatric cephalexin study in which 20% of patients missed 40% T>MIC at 4 mg/L; target named is T>MIC > 40% of the dosing interval.

## Covariates
None.

## Caveats
- Single institution, pediatric isolates; standard inoculum only (inoculum effect acknowledged).
- No CLSI cephalosporin breakpoints for S. aureus; MSSA defined by oxacillin.
- The paper labels MICs "mg/mL" throughout (Table 1, abstract); the Discussion uses mg/L. Read as mg/L.
- Literature MIC90s for cephalexin range 4-16 mg/L (Discussion), so 4 mg/L is at the low end.
