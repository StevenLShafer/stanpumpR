# Csajka 1999: angiotensin challenge as a pharmacodynamic surrogate for RAS blockers

## Study
The paper pools 13 phase I studies from the Lausanne (CHUV) unit, covering nine angiotensin II receptor antagonists
(losartan, tasosartan, candesartan cilexetil, TAK-536, SC-52458, L-159,282, LRB-081, UP 269-6, CS-866), one ACE
inhibitor (CS-622) and one dual ACE-NEP inhibitor (MDL 100 240). Subjects were healthy volunteers, all male
(p. 596). Blood pressure was measured beat-to-beat with a Finapres finger photoplethysmograph. Each subject's
angiotensin I or II bolus dose was titrated to raise blood pressure by about 30 mmHg.
- Dose-response: 1144 angiotensin-induced peaks in 228 volunteers (p. 595; the Fig. 1 legend says 185 subjects).
  Bolus doses were 0.49-5.4 µg. Fitted in NONMEM IV (first-order method) with linear, Emax and log-linear models.
- Stability: 1592 challenges during 167 placebo periods in 101 volunteers, analysed by hierarchical ANOVA.
- Literature review (Table 2): 13 published phase I studies, covering 5 ACE inhibitors and 5 AT1 antagonists, with
  invasive or Finapres blood pressure. The ID75 (the dose inhibiting the pressor response by 75% at maximum effect)
  was compared with the labelled therapeutic dose (Table 3, Fig. 2).

**This paper has no drug concentrations and no PK.** Every quantity is a dose of angiotensin or of the blocker.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- **End point**: the peak rise in finger blood pressure (Finapres) after an IV angiotensin bolus. The blocker's
  effect is the % inhibition of that peak.
- After placebo, the mean pressor response was 29±5 mmHg systolic (study means 24.4-35.5) and 25±4 mmHg diastolic
  (20.5-30.8) (p. 598). Diurnal drift was at most ±5.8 mmHg SBP and ±3.9 mmHg DBP.
- The mean angiotensin dose was 2.1±0.8 µg (CV 37%). Correcting for body weight did not reduce the variability
  (CV 36%). In one study, the Ang I dose was 1.63±0.32 µg against 1.15±0.41 µg for Ang II. Time to peak did not
  differ between Ang I and Ang II.
- **Drug tmaxE** (time to maximal inhibition; Table 2 and p. 598): ACE inhibitors 0.2-1.5 h, but enalapril,
  lisinopril, cilazapril and CGS 13928C took 3-4 h. AT1 antagonists 1-8 h. These are effect times after an oral
  dose, not an effect-compartment ke0.
- After angiotensin I infusions, the dose-response curves showed an apparent elimination half-life of the shift of
  about 4 h for cilazapril and 2 h for captopril (p. 598).
- **ID75 against therapeutic dose** (Table 3, PDF p. 8, printed p. 601; read from the page image):

- Not an opioid paper. There is no MEAC, no ke0 and no concentration-effect relationship. There is no potency ratio
  beyond the dose comparisons above.

## Covariates
In the dose-response model, Ang I against Ang II mattered: it was absorbed by Q = 0.78, and a Q estimated from the
data came close to 0.78. Age, weight, height and ethnic group had no influence (p. 598). Angiotensin dose was not
explained by any demographic. Intrasubject variation (27%) was smaller than intersubject variation (P = 0.001), in
27 subjects studied 2-3 times.

## Caveats
- The dose range covered only the rising part of the curve, so the authors prefer the log-linear model over Emax.
- Printing inconsistencies, found by reading the page image:
  - Table 1 prints the Emax-model SBP Emax as "0.3±5.9", but the text (p. 596) gives 40.3±5.9 mmHg.
  - Table 1's linear-model SBP a and b (9.1±3.7, 23.8±6.0) are identical to the log-linear row.
  - The text on p. 597 assigns 20.2 to systolic and 24.5 to diastolic, the reverse of Table 1. The abstract
    (24±6/20±5 systolic/diastolic) agrees with Table 1.
  - The abstract's ED50s (0.67 SBP, 0.84 DBP) differ slightly from Table 1 (0.65, 0.8).
- Subjects were healthy men only. Table 2 values are mostly read graphically ("~") from heterogeneous studies, and the
  text layer scrambles Table 2. Its columns were not transcribed beyond the summary above (PDF pp. 6-7 not rendered).
- Captopril is the stated outlier: its labelled dose is far above its ID75, which the authors attribute to its
  short duration of action.
