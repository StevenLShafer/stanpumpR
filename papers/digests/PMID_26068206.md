# Hannivoort 2015: dexmedetomidine PK in volunteers by step-up TCI

## Study
UMCG Groningen; NCT01879865. 18 healthy ASA I volunteers (9 M, 9 F), stratified 3 M + 3 F per age band 18-34, 35-54, 55-72 y; actual age 20-70 y, weight 51-110 kg, BMI 20.6-29.3 kg/m2 (BMI 18-30 required). Two sessions 1-3 weeks apart; 2 withdrew after session 1, so 34 sessions. Dosing: 20 s initial infusion at 6 ug/kg/h, then (from 10 min) TCI with the Dyck model via RUGLOOP II and Fresenius Orchestra, targets 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8 ng/mL, 30 min each; rate capped at 6 ug/kg/h (10 for 6 and 8 ng/mL); stop on safety criteria (MOAA/S 0 in 22 sessions, bradycardia 6, hypertension 2, airway obstruction 3; only 1 session reached 8 ng/mL); 5 h recovery. Arterial samples at baseline, 2 min, before each step, and 2-300 min of recovery: 408 samples, 29 below LLOQ excluded, 379 analysed. Assay HPLC-MS/MS, range 0.020-20 ng/mL. NONMEM 7.2; AIC (decrease >= 9.2 to add a parameter) plus out-of-sample MDAPE from repeated 2-fold cross-validation.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None in this paper (PD in the companion Colin 2017, PMID 28854538). Context-sensitive decrement times plotted (Fig. 4). Not an opioid paper.

## Covariates
Total body weight only (allometric). Age, sex, FFM as size descriptor, and session order did not improve the model.

## Caveats
Healthy volunteers, BMI <= 30; few observations above 4 ng/mL; escape drugs in 4 of 34 sessions (atropine, ephedrine, ondansetron, dexamethasone), mostly late in recovery. Small V1 may partly reflect dexmedetomidine vasoconstriction and the early arterial sampling. Authors call for prospective validation in patients.
