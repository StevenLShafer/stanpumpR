# Carlsson Petri 2015 (ESM): liraglutide population PK, paediatric and adult T2D

## Study
Supplement to the population PK analysis of liraglutide in paediatric (Trial 1; age and weight histograms in Figs 1-2, PDF pages 2-3, captions only) and adult subjects with type 2 diabetes, once-daily 1.8 mg SC. Concentrations in nM; the VPC (Fig 4, PDF p4) shows steady-state data at 1.8 mg: median about 13 nM at 0 h rising to about 16 nM near 6-10 h and falling to about 1 nM at 72 h (read off the figure, approximate); 11.4% of points outside the 90% prediction interval. Sample size, assay and software are not in this supplement.

## Model and parameters
One-compartment, first-order absorption. CL/F_i = CL/F x (BWT/90 kg)^CovCLBWT x exp(CovCLGen if male) x exp(CovCLPaed if paediatric) x exp(eta_i) (formula as printed, PDF p5). Table 1 (rendered page read):

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None (PK only; not an opioid). No effect-site model, MEAC or potency ratio.

## Covariates
Body weight (power on CL/F, exponent 0.929, reference 90 kg), sex (male CL/F x exp(0.365)), age category (0.107). No covariate on V/F or ka.

## Caveats
Full model CL/F 1.06 L/h is for the reference adult woman at 90 kg, not a population mean (base model 1.49 L/h is the unconditioned estimate). Age-category RSE 91%. The KA unit is misprinted. Only a supplement; design and population counts are in the main paper.
