# Asaumi 2019: expanded rifampicin PBPK model (OATP1B/CYP2C8 induction, MRP2 inhibition)

## Study
Modelling paper, no new clinical data. A whole-body PBPK model of rifampicin (previously published by the same group, ref 5) was extended with OATP1B and CYP2C8 induction and MRP2 inhibition, then tested against published rifampicin drug-interaction data for pravastatin, glibenclamide (= glyburide), repaglinide and the endogenous OATP1B biomarker coproporphyrin I (CP-I). Pioglitazone data (Jaakkola 2006, 30 mg after 600 mg rifampicin daily for 6 days) were used to fit the CYP2C8 induction. Software: Numeric Analysis Program for Pharmacokinetics v2.31; least squares weighted by square root of value. All models had five-liver-compartment structure; absorption by segregated-flow (rifampicin) or first-order models. Acceptance: within twofold and geometric mean fold error (GMFE).

Predicted relative activities at steady state on 600 mg rifampicin daily (p.849): OATP1B 240% (falling to 34% at next dose, 180% at 12 h); CYP3A 400%, CYP2C8 270%, CYP2C9 230%; MRP2 40% at trough, 75% at 12 h.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD end point in the clinical sense. Induction Emax/EC50,u/kdeg are enzyme-activity ratios, not drug effects. Glibenclamide AUC ratios (observed, Figure 4b): single IV 600 mg rifampicin 2.18 +/- 1.09; day 7 of repeated oral 600 mg 0.72 +/- 0.32; 48 h after 7 days 0.35 +/- 0.19. Predicted with beta 0.2/0.5/0.8: 2.35-2.54; 0.70/0.91/1.10; 0.28/0.36/0.44. No MEAC, ke0, tPeak, or potency ratio (not an opioid paper).

## Covariates
None (average healthy adult; physiological parameters in the supplement Table S1).

## Caveats
Recommends small beta (0.2-0.5) values for victims; beta cannot be identified from single-dose DDI data. CP-I delay after rifampicin not captured (Figure 6d). Degradation rate of OATP1B assumed. CYP3A/2C8 inhibition by rifampicin judged negligible in vivo.

## Verification against stanpumpR
Not cited in the repo. The library's glyburide model (R/drugs_glyburide.R:13-21) uses Rambiritch 2016 apparent PK and its pioglitazone model (R/drugs_pioglitazone.R:13-23) uses Kadam 2013; neither depends on this paper, and no rifampicin interaction is modelled for either (inst/app-help/models/antiseizure-interactions.md:52 says rifampicin is not modelled in the antiseizure group). Asaumi's pioglitazone/glibenclamide values are PBPK intrinsic parameters (per kg, unbound), not apparent two-compartment values, so they cannot be compared with the library's.
