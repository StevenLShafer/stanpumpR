# Kanto 1981: atropine PK and placental transfer at caesarean section

## Study
- 44 healthy parturients (ASA 1, uncomplicated pregnancies, 38-41 weeks) having caesarean section, Turku (Table 1, PDF p. 2, printed 86; confirmed on page image).
  - Group 1, n = 10: IV atropine, maternal PK. Age 24.9 (21-29) y, weight 66.3 (48-78) kg, height 161.0 (150-168) cm.
  - Group 2, n = 22: IV atropine, placental transfer only. 29.5 (19-42) y, 69.0 (54-105) kg, 160.5 (147-171) cm.
  - Group 3, n = 12: IM atropine; maternal PK in 8. 29.4 (22-42) y, 70.9 (54-94) kg, 163.0 (155-173) cm.
- Dose 0.01 mg/kg, single IV or IM injection, IV shortly before or after induction. Salt/base basis not stated.
- Anaesthesia: thiopentone up to 350 mg, suxamethonium 100 mg, N2O 70% + halothane 0.5-1.0% until delivery; after cord clamping droperidol + fentanyl, pethidine, N2O, suxamethonium drip.
- Samples: maternal antecubital vein, umbilical vein and artery; amniotic fluid in 5 (IM). Methods say one sample from each patient for the placental study; the Group 1 serial sampling schedule is not printed (Figure 1 runs to about 6 h).
- Assay: radioimmunoassay for atropine (dl-hyoscyamine), LOD 9 nmol/L (2.5 ng/mL) in 0.1 mL plasma; intra-assay CV 5.1-7.2%, inter-assay 5.0-7.1%. Plasma.
- Analysis: two-compartment open model by standard methods on an HP-97 calculator; AUC by trapezoids with extrapolation. Individual fits, means +/- SEM.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No concentration-effect data, ke0 or tPeak. The discussion links the 1.02-min distribution half-life to the clinical onset in the mother within a few minutes (citing Hellman 1961, 1963), and fetal vagal blockade at 5-15 min (citing others). Not an opioid; no MEAC or potency ratio.

## Covariates
None examined. All values per kg of total (term-pregnant) weight.

## Caveats
- Small n (10 for IV PK), term pregnancy under general anaesthesia (thiopentone, halothane, delivery and blood loss during sampling); the authors attribute the second peak to circulatory changes.
- RIA of racemic dl-hyoscyamine; LOD 9 nmol/L, so the terminal phase rests on concentrations a few times the LOD.
- Dose basis unstated; sampling schedule for Group 1 not printed; "mean half-life" not reconciled with mean slopes.
- Figure 1's legend marks IV as open circles, but the open-circle curve rises from zero like an IM curve; the symbols appear swapped.
- The authors say their half-life and clearance agree with Gundert-Remy 1980 (GC-MS, an abstract); that comparator is the Hinderling group's early report, not the 1985 paper the library uses.
