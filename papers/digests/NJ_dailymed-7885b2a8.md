# AVAPRO (irbesartan) US label, 2026

## Study
This is the innovator PLR label for Avapro (irbesartan), an angiotensin II AT1 receptor blocker, in 150 mg and
300 mg film-coated tablets. Initial US approval was 1997. PDF page 1 is the Avapro Highlights, so there is no
source file problem.

**Dosage (2, p4):**
- Hypertension: start at 150 mg once daily, up to a maximum of 300 mg once daily.
- Diabetic nephropathy: 300 mg once daily.
- Volume- or salt-depleted patients, including haemodialysis: start at 75 mg.
- Take with or without food.

- PK is linear over the therapeutic range. Steady state comes within 3 days, with less than 20% accumulation
  (p11).
- More than 80% of circulating radioactivity is unchanged irbesartan. The inactive glucuronide is about 6%.
- Oxidation is mainly by CYP2C9; CYP3A4 is negligible (p12). About 20% of radioactivity appears in urine, the
  rest in faeces.
- Arithmetic check (derived): ln 2 × V/CL over the printed ranges gives 3.5-6.8 h, not the 11-15 h terminal
  half-life. A terminal-phase volume would be about 150-230 L. So 53-93 L is not Vz, and the three ranges are not
  one jointly estimated one-compartment set.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- **Angiotensin II pressor response (12.2, p11):** inhibition was 100% at 4 h after 150 or 300 mg in healthy
  subjects, and 40% and 60% at 24 h.
- **Chronic dosing:** angiotensin II rises 1.5-2-fold and renin 2-3-fold. GFR and renal plasma flow are
  unchanged up to 300 mg.
- **Blood pressure (14.1, pp13-14):**
  - 7 placebo-controlled 8-12-week trials, 1915 on irbesartan (1-900 mg) and 611 on placebo.
  - Trough falls against placebo were about 8-10/5-6 mmHg at 150 mg and 8-12/5-8 mmHg at 300 mg, with no further
    effect above 300 mg.
  - Peak effect at 3-6 h, trough/peak 60-70%, near-full effect at 2 weeks.
  - About two-thirds of the effect remained 1 week after stopping.
- **Not given:** no concentration-BP coefficient. Not an opioid: no MEAC or ke0.

## Covariates
All from 12.3 (p12):
- Females: plasma concentrations 11-44% higher.
- Elderly (65-80 y): AUC and Cmax 20-50% higher, half-life unchanged.
- Black subjects: AUC about 25% higher.
- Renal impairment and haemodialysis: no change, and irbesartan is not removed by haemodialysis.
- Mild-moderate cirrhosis: no change.
- Children 6-16 y: no effective BP lowering at up to 4.5 mg/kg/day (8.4, p9).

## Caveats
- All PK descriptors are printed as ranges of averages, from unidentified studies.
- The kind of volume is not stated, and it is inconsistent with the half-life in one compartment (derived
  above).
