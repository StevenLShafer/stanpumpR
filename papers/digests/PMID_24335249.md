# DeJongh 2014: recombinant antithrombin (ATryn) in hereditary deficiency

## Study
- Development: AT III 009-00, a single-dose study in 15 patients with hereditary antithrombin deficiency not in a high-risk situation, 13 women and 2 men, mean baseline activity about 51%; 50 IU/kg (n = 9) or 100 IU/kg (n = 6) as a bolus; samples to 72 h.
- External validation: AT III 01002 (14 patients) and AT HD 012-04 (18), 11 surgery and 21 delivery in all, a 15-minute loading dose then a continuous infusion, adjusted to activity (Table 1). Delivery patients were over-predicted by the first model; their CL and Vss were re-estimated (Table 3), and the updated model checked against AT HD 012-04 (Table 4).
- No weight, height or age printed for any cohort; the simulations assumed 76 kg. Activity by a functional assay, % of normal; the baseline estimated as a parameter and the infused activity added to it. NONMEM, FOCE-I; a covariate search was not done formally for want of patients.

## Model and parameters
Two compartments, linear elimination from the central compartment, proportional residual error. Table 3:

One sentence of the Results calls 1.38 L/h and 14.3 L the lower 95% confidence boundaries; Table 3, the Discussion and the ATryn label (12.3) give them as the delivery estimates.

## Checks
- Table 2 and the ATryn label (12.3), non-compartmental, baseline-corrected: incremental recovery 2.24 and 1.94 %/IU/kg, half-life 11.6 and 17.7 h after 50 and 100 IU/kg; the label adds CL 9.6 and 7.2 mL/h/kg and Vss 126 and 156 mL/kg. The model at 70 kg: CL 9.5 mL/h/kg, Vss 110 mL/kg, recovery 2.27 at the end of a bolus.
- Dosing rules: loading (100 - baseline) x weight / 2.3 and maintenance / 10.2 per hour in surgery, / 1.3 and / 5.4 at delivery (the paper and the label agree).
- Table 4: with the delivery update, 92% of delivery samples above 80% in AT HD 012-04 against 60% with the original rule in AT III 01002.
- Funded by rEVO Biologics; two authors its employees. No Gideon Koren among the authors.

## How stanpumpR uses it
R/drugs_antithrombinAlfa.R: the surgery values for a non-pregnant patient, the delivery values under the Pregnant flag; Vcentral = Vss/2.51; fixed values scaled by fat-free mass at the reference man (no cohort size printed). Plotted as the Antithrombin baseline (blank 44.7) plus the infused, in IU/dL; band 80-120 from the label.
