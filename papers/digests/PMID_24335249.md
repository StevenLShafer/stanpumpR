# DeJongh 2014: population PK of recombinant human antithrombin (ATryn) in hereditary deficiency, surgery vs delivery

## Study
Population PK and dosing paper for recombinant human antithrombin (rhAT, antithrombin alfa, ATryn) in patients with hereditary antithrombin deficiency (HD). 47 patients in three trials (p.2, PDF p.2):
- AT III 009-00: open-label single-dose PK study, 15 non-pregnant HD patients not in a high-risk situation, female:male 13:2, mean baseline AT about 51%. Single IV bolus 50 IU/kg (n=9) or 100 IU/kg (n=6). Samples at screening, 1 h and 15 min before, and 0, 5, 10, 15, 30, 45, 60 min and 2, 4, 6, 8, 24, 48, 72 h after the infusion.
- AT III 01002 (NCT00056550): 14 patients, 5 surgery and 9 delivery (Table 1).
- AT HD 012-04 (NCT00110513): 18 patients, 6 surgery and 12 delivery (Table 1).
- Dosing in the two trials: 15-minute loading infusion followed at once by continuous infusion; doses from baseline AT activity and body weight.
- Analysis: NONMEM V.2 and 7.2, FOCE with interaction, S-Plus post-processing. Two-compartment model, linear elimination from the central compartment, baseline AT activity a separate parameter with the infused activity added to it; proportional residual error. Model built on the 15 PK-study patients only; no formal demographic covariate analysis (small n). External validation on the two trials; delivery was the only covariate that mattered.
- Assay: AT activity as % of normal (assay not described in the text read).

Dosing formulas (PDF p.4 and p.6), pretreatment AT activity in %, body weight in kg:
- Original (surgery): loading dose IU = (100 - pretreatment AT) / 2.3 x weight; maintenance IU/h = (100 - pretreatment AT) / 10.2 x weight.
- Delivery (revised): loading (100 - pretreatment AT) / 1.3 x weight; maintenance (100 - pretreatment AT) / 5.4 x weight.
- Target steady state 80-120% of normal, not exceeding 200%. TDM: first sample 2 h after the start, second at 6 h if in range; correct by 0.7x (above 120%) or 1.3x (below 80%) and recheck 2 h later (PDF p.6).
- Simulation: 10 000 patients each for surgery and delivery, typical weight 76 kg, variance 204 kg^2 (p.3). With the first regimen 79.5% in range without adjustment, 14.5% too high, 6.4% too low (p.5).

External validation (Table 4, PDF p.7): share of non-baseline samples above 80% / within 80-120%: AT III 01002 surgery 93% / 91% (of 69); delivery 60% / 40% (of 121); AT HD 012-04 surgery 83% / 81% (of 42); delivery 92% / 61% (of 79). Internal VPC: 84% and 94% of observations within the 10th-90th percentiles at 50 and 100 IU/kg (p.4).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Not a pharmacodynamic paper; the end point is plasma AT activity (% of normal), and the therapeutic target is 80-120% of normal. No ke0, hysteresis, MEAC or potency ratio (not an opioid). Audit: none.

## Covariates
Delivery (vs surgery) is the only covariate found significant: larger CL (1.38 vs 0.665 L/h, ratio 2.07, derived) and Vss (14.3 vs 7.72 L, ratio 1.85, derived), P < .05 by a chi-square test (p.5-6). Age, sex, body weight, centre and concomitant medications were examined graphically on residuals and had no significant effect (p.3, p.8). No weight scaling is in the model; weight enters only through the dose formulas. The comparator is the surgery group of the same HD cohort, not non-pregnant women in general.

## Caveats
- Model is for the recombinant product, whose half-life is 11.6-17.7 h (Table 2), against 2.5 days the authors quote for pooled plasma-derived AT (Discussion, p.8). Not transferable to a plasma-derived concentrate.
- Built on 15 patients (13 female); no formal covariate model; Q is imprecise (SE 0.646 on 0.613).
- The delivery group is a high-risk labour and peripartum state, treated for about 3 days; the authors suggest volume and hepatic changes of pregnancy as possible reasons but do not know.
- Industry-funded (rEVO Biologics); two authors are company employees.
- The paper does not state whether the HD patients' weight was total body weight (no weight scaling).
