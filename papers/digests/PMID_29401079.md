# Cortinez 2018: propofol PKPD model for effect-site TCI in the obese

## Study
- Step 1 (derivation): PK from three studies of obese adults pooled with BIS data from one: 47 patients (13 M / 34 F), 770 propofol concentrations, 1510 BIS values. Median (range) age 39 (21-66) y, weight 104 (76.8-160) kg, height 160 (139-185) cm, BMI 40.1 (33.3-52.8) (Table 1, PDF p4, text layer). Study 1: Servin, 8 morbidly obese, 97-169 kg, stepwise infusion, blood HPLC converted to plasma with blood/plasma 1.18. Study 2: Cortinez 2010, 19 obese ASA II-III, 2 mg/kg bolus then 10-8-6-5 mg/kg/h. Study 3 (PK + PD): 20 obese, BMI >35, 85-141 kg, plasma TCI adjusted to BIS 40-60, remifentanil 0.3 ug/kg/min, BIS every 15 s, arterial plasma HPLC (LLOQ 0.1 ug/mL).
- Step 2 (validation): 14 new obese patients (BMI >35, ASA II, 20-60 y; median weight 114 kg, range 90.5-197), plasma TCI with the Cortinez 2010 model; bolus target 12-16 ug/mL (mean bolus 1.3 mg/kg TBW, range 0.98-1.68), then 4 ug/mL x30 min, then 2.5 ug/mL, with remifentanil 0.3 ug/kg/min; 269 arterial samples for PK, 1356 BIS values for PD.
- NONMEM 7.3, simultaneous PKPD, exponential IIV, proportional PK error, additive PD error; bootstrap 1000 for CIs.

## Model and parameters
Three-compartment PK, effect compartment, sigmoid inhibitory Emax on BIS with a lag time. Volumes and clearances scale **linearly with total body weight**, normalised to 104 kg (the median weight of the study population); fat-free mass / normal fat mass (Ffat estimated 0.99) and empirical allometry did not improve the fit (delta OBJ 0.708 and 0.309). ## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: BIS (propofol hypnotic effect), arterial plasma propofol, in obese adults on remifentanil; Emax fixed at BIS 0.
- ke0 (keo) = 0.292 /min (Table 3; CI 0.17-0.61). Derived: t1/2 ke0 = ln2/0.292 = 2.37 min. tPeak not reported and not derived here (needs the PK model).
- Ce50 = 3.4 ug/mL (mg/L), gamma 2.17, E0 96.5, time lag 0.49 min. Using Ce50 as the effect-site concentration, the authors state BIS 60-40 corresponds to effect-site concentrations of 2.6-3.8 ug/mL.
- Hysteresis: an effect-site link improved the fit over a direct Cp-BIS link by delta OBJ -1245.
- PD validation: MDPE 0.4% (IQR -10.39 to 3.85), MDAPE 11.9% (IQR 9.62-18.76), 1356 BIS values (p869).
- Not an opioid paper (opioid audit: none); remifentanil 0.3 ug/kg/min was co-administered in studies 2-3 and the PD estimates apply to propofol with that background.

## Covariates
Total body weight only, linearly (reference 104 kg). No age or sex effect found. The authors say the model should be used only in obese patients (BMI >35); in lean patients it risks underdosing, mainly from underestimated clearance. The linear form is flattened because weights spanned only 77-160 kg.

## Caveats
- Small derivation set (47) and validation set (14); wide CIs on V1, V3, Q2, keo.
- Two datasets had only 2 h of post-infusion sampling (could overestimate CL); Servin's data run to 8 h.
- Linear weight scaling was chosen for simplicity, not because it was statistically better than allometry.
- Step-2 PK result: the Eleveld 2014 model had the lowest PK errors (MDPE <10%, MDAPE <25%, abstract); Marsh showed +24.3% bias in this population (positive means measured above predicted).
- Abstract interval for PD MDAPE appears misprinted (see open_questions).
