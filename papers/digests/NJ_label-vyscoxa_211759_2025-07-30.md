# VYSCOXA label 07/2025: celecoxib pharmacokinetics

## Study
Product label. The VYSCOXA-specific absorption data are Study 915/22 (52 healthy subjects, 200 mg); the rest of 12.3
is carried from the Celebrex label.

## Model and parameters
Section 12.3 (all apparent, oral):
- Half-life about 11 h (effective, fasted); low solubility prolongs absorption and makes terminal t½ variable.
- CL/F about 500 mL/min (derived: 30 L/h).
- Vss/F about 400 L. Protein binding about 97% (mainly albumin, also α1-acid glycoprotein); not bound
  preferentially to red cells.
- Tmax median 1.5 h (0.67-8.00) suspension, 2.5 h (1.00-8.00) capsules; suspension AUC equivalent, Cmax 22% lower.
- Capsules dose-proportional up to 200 mg BID; less than proportional above (poor aqueous solubility).
  Suspension vs capsules above 200 mg not determined. **No absolute bioavailability study.**
- Steady state on or before day 5.
- Food (suspension): median Tmax +1.5 h; AUC0-t +50%, AUC0-inf +35%, Cmax +144%.
- Metabolism mainly CYP2C9 to inactive alcohol, carboxylic acid and glucuronide; <3% unchanged in urine and feces;
  57% of a radiolabelled dose in feces, 27% in urine; carboxylic acid metabolite 73% of dose.
- Antacids (Al/Mg): Cmax -37%, AUC -10%. Fluconazole 200 mg daily: about twofold higher concentrations.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No concentration-effect data, no therapeutic range. Not an opioid: no MEAC.

## Covariates
- Pediatric (12.3): popPK in 152 JRA patients aged 2-17 years, weighing 10 kg or more, investigational suspension:
  CL/F rises less than proportionally with weight; 10 kg and 25 kg patients predicted 40% and 24% lower CL/F than a
  70 kg adult RA patient. Dosing 50 mg BID (10-25 kg), 100 mg BID (>25 kg).
- Geriatric (>65 y, steady state): Cmax +40%, AUC +50%; higher in elderly women mainly from lower body weight; start
  at the lowest dose below 50 kg.
- Race: meta-analysis, AUC about 40% higher in Black than Caucasian subjects.
- Hepatic: steady-state AUC +40% (Child-Pugh A), +180% (B); halve the dose in B; C not studied.
- Renal: AUC about 40% lower in chronic renal insufficiency (GFR 35-60 mL/min); no relation of GFR to clearance.
- CYP2C9 (12.5): *3/*3 systemic levels 3- to 7-fold higher (8 subjects from 4 reports); *3/*3 frequency 0.3-1.0%;
  start at half the lowest dose.

## Caveats
Typical values are rounded ("about"). Weight relation comes from a pediatric popPK (Krishnaswami), not restated as an
equation in the label.
