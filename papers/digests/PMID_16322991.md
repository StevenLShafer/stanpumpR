# Latz 2006: population PK of pemetrexed from ten phase II trials

## Study
- Ten single-arm, nonrandomised phase II trials of single-agent pemetrexed (Eli Lilly), one tumour type each
  (Table 1, PDF p. 2). 287 patients (128 F, 159 M), 441 cycles. Index (model-building) set: 8 studies, 209 patients,
  324 cycles, 1,184 concentrations. Validation set: 2 NSCLC studies, 78 patients, 117 cycles, 412 concentrations
  (p. 4, printed 404).
- Population (Table 2, PDF p. 4): index set age mean 57.3 (median 57.3, range 26.3-79.1) y; BSA 1.76 (median 1.74,
  1.26-2.50) m2; weight 68.3 (median 66.0, 34.0-138) kg; CrCLcg.std 96.9 (median 92.6, 44.3-225) mL/min.
  Validation set: age 36.6-80.2, BSA 1.28-2.35, weight 36.0-127, CrCL 40.7-162 (median 94.9). Index set 77%
  Caucasian, 17% African descent.
- Dose: 500 or 600 mg/m2 as a 10-min IV infusion every 21 days, **without** folic acid or vitamin B12
  supplementation; later cycles reduced for toxicity. Doses given 126-838 mg/m2 (mean 561); modelled on absolute
  dose, 244-1,494 mg (mean 990) (p. 4). Dose expressed as "mg pemetrexed".
- Sampling: 3-5 samples per patient in cycles 1 and 3, drawn at random within windows 0-2, 2-6, 6-12 and 12-36 h
  from start of infusion (p. 2).
- Assay: plasma; HPLC-UV (4 studies) or LC-MS/MS (6 studies), low range 10-2,000 ng/mL and high range
  1,000-200,000 ng/mL. ~6% of samples BQL, excluded (p. 3).
- NONMEM V, FOCE with interaction. Covariates normalised to population median; forward inclusion at dOFV >= 3.84,
  backward elimination at >= 10.8 (P < 0.001).

## Model and parameters
Two-compartment, linear, constant-rate input. Final model, Table 3 (PDF p. 5, printed 405):

- CL (mL/min) = 43.0 + 47.2 x CrCLcg.std / 92.6 (92.6 = median baseline CrCLcg.std, mL/min)
- V1 (L) = 6.13 x BSA^1.32 (BSA in m2, not centred)
- Q 14.5 mL/min; V2 3.38 L
- BPV (proportional/exponential): CL 19.3%, V1 16.6%, V2 24.5%; none on Q. Residual error proportional 28.4%.

Other reported values: predicted CL 90.2 mL/min at the median CrCL 92.6 and 61.3 mL/min at the minimum CrCL 36.1
(p. 5); ~95% of patients' CL within 53.2-146 mL/min (31.2-81.3 mL/min/m2) (Discussion, p. 7). Covariates explain
~25% of BPV in CL (25.8% -> 19.3%) and ~30% in V1 (24.0% -> 16.6%). PK linear (dose-independent) and
time-invariant. Protein binding ~81% (cited from the label); population CL about four times fu x GFR (23 mL/min),
so tubular secretion contributes (p. 9). Model-predictive performance: MPE 1.33 index vs 1.44 validation;
concentrations above ~75 ug/mL are underpredicted (Fig. 3, Table 4).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No effect-site model, no ke0. Not an opioid. The paper names AUC (not Cmax) as the main driver of neutropenia
(citing refs 35, 18, 19); no concentration target is given. Figure 4 simulates fixed, BSA-based and renal-function
(target-AUC) dosing: renal-based dosing puts >90% of patients within 25% of a target AUC vs <75% for BSA or fixed
dosing.

## Covariates
- CrCL (standard Cockcroft-Gault, mL/min, not normalised to 1.73 m2) on CL, linear (equation above). The
  weight used in Cockcroft-Gault is not stated; the lean-body-mass variant was tested and not adopted (p. 5).
- BSA on V1, power. BSA formula not named (refs 8, 40 "standard formulae").
- Not retained: sex, age, ethnicity, smoking, alcohol, cancer type (NSCLC study 2 V1 30% lower, breast V2 20% lower,
  judged not clinically relevant). Folate deficiency (n = 5, single cycles; Discussion says n = 4) gave CL 33%
  lower and was left out because patients now receive vitamins.

## Caveats
- Fitted in patients given no folic acid / B12; clinical use now always supplements.
- The linear intercept keeps CL >= 43 mL/min however poor the kidneys; the authors suggest a sigmoid or split-line
  model might fit better (p. 8).
- The paper is internally inconsistent on the CrCL range: Table 2 gives 44.3-225 (index) and 40.7-162
  (validation), while the Results give a population minimum of 36.1 and the Discussion a range 36.1-254 mL/min.
  Possibly all cycles vs baseline; not explained.
- Typical CL 91.6 mL/min (Abstract) does not equal the equation at median (90.2) or mean (derived: 43.0 + 47.2 x
  96.9/92.6 = 92.4) CrCL; its basis is not stated.
- Sparse sampling to 36 h; a ~5% subset of high concentrations is underpredicted.
