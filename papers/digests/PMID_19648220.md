# de Haas 2010: zolpidem 10 mg PK, CNS effects, and sigmoid-Emax vs acute-tolerance PK/PD

## Study
Double-blind, randomised, placebo-controlled; zolpidem 10 mg was the active control (n = 14) within a 70-subject
trial of a novel sleep compound (almorexant; Brisbare-Roch 2007). Healthy MEN (all subjects male), Leiden (CHDR);
ages/weights not given in the text read. Single oral dose, fasting, daytime. Plasma (heparin) at 10, 20, 40, 60, 80
min and 2, 2.5, 3, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12 h; HPLC-fluorescence, LOQ 1.00 ng/mL. PD (saccadic eye movements, smooth pursuit,
EEG Fz-Cz/Pz-Oz, body sway, adaptive tracking, Bond-Lader and Bowdle VAS, word recall) pre-dose and 10 min - 12 h.
NONMEM, FOCE; empirical Bayes PK used to drive PD.

## Model and parameters
NCA: median Tmax 0.78 h (range 0.33-2.50); geometric mean Cmax 132.0 ng/mL (printed range 59.2-136.0, which cannot

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
AUE 0-4 h vs placebo (Table 1): SPV -22.9 deg/s; smooth pursuit -8.3%; VAS alertness -12.0 mm; body sway +261 mm;
adaptive tracking -4.6%; EEG alpha Pz-Oz -1.31 uV; EEG beta Fz-Cz +0.36 uV; VAS "feeling high" increased; memory not
affected. A linear concentration-effect model showed effects peaking earlier and declining faster than predicted
from plasma; CNS effects normalised faster than concentrations fell (Abstract). Two models fitted (Table 3):
- Sigmoid Emax on plasma (no effect compartment): EC50 113 ng/mL (log body sway), 149 (VAS alertness), 150 (log EEG
  beta), 108 (log EEG alpha); Hill 2.81, 3.42, 2.15, 2.42 respectively (column alignment of the other end points is
  uncertain in the text layer).
- Transit tolerance model, 15 transit compartments to a hypothetical antagonising factor; e.g. transit times 190 min
  (body sway), 161 min (VAS alertness); IC50 of tolerance factor 8.52-1390 ng/mL by end point.
Both fit most end points comparably; tolerance less suitable for SPV and EEG alpha. Authors lean to tolerance
because 5 mg doses have effects that a steep sigmoid model would not predict, but say a repeated low-dose study is
needed. No ke0, no effect-compartment model, no hysteresis analysis reported. Not an opioid; no MEAC.

## Covariates
None examined (n = 14 men).

## Caveats
n = 14, men only, daytime fasting; zolpidem was an active control, not the study focus. Tables poorly extracted.
The two PD models are not distinguished by the data.

  direct sigmoid Emax can, which is relevant to stanpumpR's plasma-only (direct-effect) approach.
