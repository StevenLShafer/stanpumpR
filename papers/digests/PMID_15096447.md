# Martinoni 2004: Model-based closed-loop control of ventilation

## Study
- Clinical validation of a model-based controller that adjusts minute volume (MV, via f and VT) to hold end-tidal CO2 (FE'CO2) at a setpoint during mechanical ventilation under i.v. anaesthesia (PDF p1-3).
- 16 ASA I-II patients enrolled, 15 analysed (one excluded for lung secretions); age 38 (20-59) yr, weight 66 (54-87) kg, 11 F / 4 M (Table 1, PDF p3). Excluded COPD, emergency/pulmonary/intracranial surgery, operations under 2 h.
- Anaesthesia: oral omeprazole 40 mg and midazolam 7.5 mg premedication; propofol 2 mg/kg and fentanyl (the text layer prints the dose as "0.3 mg/kg", a likely lost micro sign, not used here) for induction, then propofol and remifentanil infusions "according to clinical needs"; mivacurium 0.3 mg/kg. No concentrations, PK or PD of these drugs are reported.
- Setpoints: normoventilation FE'CO2 4.5% (35 mmHg), hyper 3.5%, hypo 5.5%; steps of 1 and 2 vol% held at least 15 min. Compared with a historical fuzzy-logic group (n=30, Schaublin 1996).
- Controller: simplified three-compartment (lung, brain, tissue) CO2 model from Chiari et al., observer, state feedback plus integral action; MV computed every 5 s; MV change limited to 10% per cycle.

Performance (printed values): rise time after a 1 vol% setpoint increase 313 (90) s fuzzy-logic vs 144 (17.3) s model-based (Table 3, PDF p4); after a 1 vol% decrease 355 (127) vs 177.1 (35.7) s; 2 vol% steps in model-based group: increase 311 (85) s, decrease 215 (18) s (Table 4). Static precision not different between controllers (Table 2). Mean overshoot 0.14-0.39 vol%.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None for any drug. Remifentanil, fentanyl and propofol appear only as background anaesthetic: no MEAC, ke0, t1/2ke0, tPeak, hysteresis or potency ratio is reported (opioid audit: none). The controlled variable is FE'CO2, not a drug effect.

## Covariates
Minimum MV imposed by body weight (2.1-2.6 L/min) is mentioned in the discussion (PDF p6); no covariate equations.

## Caveats
- Historical (not concurrent) control group; different age/sex mix (Table 1).
- Only ASA I-II patients; median-based performance measures were insensitive (many zeros), as the authors note.
- Controller cannot detect dead-space ventilation (e.g. pulmonary embolism); it lowers MV when FE'CO2 falls.
