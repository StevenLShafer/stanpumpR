# Short 1991: propofol-midazolam synergy (bolus induction, hypnosis and tetanus end points)

## Study
- 200 unpremedicated Chinese women, 18-40 yr, ASA I-II, weight within 20% of ideal, elective gynaecological surgery. Mean ages 31.6 (midazolam), 30.5 (propofol), 30.5 yr (combination); mean (SD) weights 51.0 (9.4), 50.3 (7.7), 50.7 (8.3) kg (p541).
- Part 1: 120 patients, 12 groups of 10, one dose each: propofol 0.7, 1.0, 1.3, 1.6, 1.9, 2.2, 2.5 mg/kg or midazolam 0.1, 0.125, 0.15, 0.175, 0.2 mg/kg. Bolus over 10 s into a forearm vein, 10 mL saline flush (p539). Observer blind.
- Part 2: 80 patients, combination at a fixed dose ratio, propofol/midazolam mg/kg = 0.21/0.03, 0.29/0.04, 0.36/0.05, 0.46/0.065, 0.60/0.085, 0.71/0.10, 0.92/0.13, 1.2/0.17 (Table I). Midazolam given 2 min before propofol, then 2 min to assessment (p540-541).
- End points, assessed 2 min after propofol and 4 min after midazolam ("approximate time to peak effect" of each as a bolus; assumed, no effect-site measurement): hypnosis = failure to open eyes on verbal command; anaesthesia = no response to 5-s tetanic stimulus over the ulnar nerve (50 Hz, 80 mA, 0.25 ms), tested only in those hypnotic.
- Statistics: probit of log dose for ED50; Plummer-Short method (non-parallel curves, additive vs non-additive logit model, eqs 1-3) for hypnosis; logistic horizontal shift for anaesthesia; SPSS-X. No drug concentrations measured.

Hypnosis, combination: P < 0.01 synergy; combination 1.44 times as potent as the individual agents (Summary); ED50 44% below additive (p543); at equi-effective combination doses 85% hypnotic at a total dose that gave 50% with either alone (Fig 3). Anaesthesia: propofol ED50 1.93 to 0.93 mg/kg with midazolam 0.13 mg/kg, P < 0.01 (Summary) / P < 0.001 (Fig 4 caption).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End points are loss of response to verbal command and to tetanus, not EEG or ventilation. No ke0, t½ke0, tPeak, EC50 or Emax; concentrations never measured.
- Assessment times of 2 min (propofol) and 4 min (midazolam) are described as approximate bolus time to peak effect, with no source given in this paper; they are a design choice, not a measurement.
- Table I MAP fall at 2 min: no dose relation; same for combination as for single agents at equal hypnotic potency.
- Not an opioid paper: no MEAC or equianalgesic ratio. Potency ratio, hypnosis, propofol:midazolam by dose = 1.01/0.14 (derived: about 7:1 by mass).

## Covariates
None analysed (age and weight did not differ between groups).

## Caveats
- Dose, not concentration, response; interaction defined at one time point after bolus, so a pharmacokinetic interaction is not excluded (authors say so, p544).
- Young Chinese women only; doses per kg of actual weight, patients within 20% of ideal.
- Midazolam alone never reached the anaesthesia end point, so no ED50 exists for it.
- Internal inconsistencies: coefficient 0.78 vs 0.87 (above).
