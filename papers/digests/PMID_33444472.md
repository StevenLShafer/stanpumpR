# Nijstad 2021: clofarabine population PK for paediatric HCT conditioning

## Study
Retrospective PK analysis, myeloablative conditioning before allogeneic HCT, UMC Utrecht and Princess Maxima Center, October 2011 to January 2019. 81 patients: 80 paediatric (0.5-18 y) plus one adult (37.8 y); median age 11.1 y, median weight 36.6 kg (6.6-102.9), 30 (37%) female, five <12 months (Table 1). Clofarabine 1-h infusion, cumulative 120 mg/m2 over 4 days (days -5 to -2), followed by fludarabine (1 h) and busulfan (3 h). Plasma sampled during busulfan TDM, mostly 5, 6, 7, 8 h after end of the clofarabine infusion, some to 24 h and some about 1.5 h; 805 samples (median 10 per patient), none below LLOQ (1 ng/mL), LC-MS/MS (plasma, total). NONMEM 7.3.0, FOCE-I, SIR for CIs.

## Model and parameters
Linear two-compartment, first-order elimination. Clearance = non-renal + renal component: CL = (CLnonrenal + CLrenal x RF) x (BW/70)^0.75, where RF = eGFR(L/h) x (70/BW) / eGFRstd, eGFRstd = 6 L/h (100 mL/min) (Table 2 and Eqs 2-3). V1 and V2 scale linearly with BW/70 (exponent 1); Q exponent 0.75 (exponents fixed a priori). Parameters are for a 70 kg patient with RF = 1 (Table 2, rendered PDF p5):
CLnonrenal,70kg 24.0 L/h (95% CI 13.7-34.4); CLrenal,70kg 29.8 L/h (23.9-36.1); V1,70kg 268 L (234.8-296.6); V2,70kg 186 L (165.4-210.7); Q70kg 33.2 L/h (27.5-40.9). IIV: CL 17.8%, V1 12.6%, Q 64.5%. IOV: CL 9.7%, V2 39.1%. Proportional residual error 8.3%. Derived by the authors: renal clearance about 55% of total at normal renal function (29.8/(24.0+29.8) = 0.554, derived), t1/2alpha 1.7 h, t1/2beta 8.1 h (p. 3222). Observed AUC T0-inf (cumulative over four days) median 3.1 mg/L*h, range 1.8-6.0.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD model; no target AUC exists for clofarabine (the paper says it is undetermined). Dose algorithm (Eq 5): cumulative 4-day dose (mg) = AUCtarget x (24.0 + 29.8 x RF) x (BW/70)^0.75, with AUC in mg/L*h; an AUCtarget of 3.1 mg/L*h (cohort median) was used for illustration. Not an opioid; no ke0, MEAC or potency ratio.

## Covariates
Body weight (allometric) and renal function (eGFR) on clearance; adding renal function dropped OFV by 34 and IIV on CL fell from 46.2% to 17.8%. eGFR by Cockcroft-Gault, Schwartz for <17 y (female) and <14 y (male); capped at 8.4 L/h (140 mL/min/1.73 m2); in infants assumed to rise from 2.1 L/h (35 mL/min) at birth to the cap by 1.5 y. BSA and FFM did not improve on BW. Maturation (PMA, Hill) tested and not retained.

## Caveats
Retrospective; no patient with eGFR <60 mL/min/1.73 m2, none under 6 months; only 7.6% of samples <4 h after dose (model underpredicts early points). Lower exposure seen in children <20 kg only partly explained by better renal function. Exposures are the cumulative 4-day AUC. Parent drug only (the active triphosphate not modelled).
