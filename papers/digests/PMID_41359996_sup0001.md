# Giamarellos-Bourboulis 2025: ImmunoSep supplement

## Study
Supplement to a multicentre randomized, placebo-controlled trial of biomarker-guided immunotherapy in adult sepsis (pneumonia or primary bacteremia). Randomized 276 patients (precision immunotherapy n=131, placebo n=145; eTable 5-6 headers). Arms by immune state (eTable 7): macrophage activation-like syndrome (MALS) anakinra n=25 vs placebo n=23; sepsis-induced immunoparalysis recombinant human interferon-gamma (rhIFNg) n=106 vs placebo n=122. Allocation by ferritin and monocyte HLA-DR (eTable 4, PDF p14): MALS ferritin >4,420 ng/mL with HLA-DR >=5,000 antibodies/monocyte; immunoparalysis ferritin <=4,420 with HLA-DR <5,000 (eTable 10 footnote). Eligibility: age >=18, Sepsis-3 (SOFA >=2), within 72 h of sepsis onset; exclusions include stage IV malignancy, neutropenia <1,500/mm3, HIV, prednisone >=0.4 mg/kg/day >15 days, anti-cytokine biologics within 1 month, pregnancy/lactation (PDF pp 3-4). Ferritin by ELISA (lower limit 75 ng/mL); HLA-DR by flow cytometry (p5).

Outcome facts (eTable 9, PDF p22): primary endpoint (>=1.4-point fall in mean SOFA days 2-9) : precision immunotherapy was given to 46/72 (63.9%) patients who met it and 85/204 (41.7%) who did not; precision immunotherapy OR 2.48 (95% CI 1.42-4.31) univariate, 2.49 (1.42-4.36) adjusted, P=.001. eTable 10 (p23): MALS 12/25 (48.0%) vs 4/23 (17.4%), OR 4.38 (1.15-16.64); immunoparalysis 34/106 (32.1%) vs 22/122 (18.0%), OR 2.15 (1.16-3.97). Co-medication eTable 6 (p16-17): norepinephrine 84.0 vs 86.9%, mechanical ventilation 88.5 vs 89.0%, hydrocortisone 200 mg/day 42.7 vs 49.7%.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Not a PK/PD paper. No ke0, MEAC or potency ratio. End points are SOFA change and immune-dysfunction reversal.

## Covariates
Logistic regression (eTable 9): APACHE II, Charlson index, baseline SOFA and enrolment-site size had no significant effect on the primary endpoint.
