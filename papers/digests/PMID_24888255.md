# Heise 2014: IDegAsp at steady state, glucose clamp in type 1 diabetes

## Study
Single-centre (Profil, Neuss), open-label, single-arm trial, NCT01590836, Novo Nordisk sponsored. 22 white adults
with type 1 diabetes (18 men, 4 women) of 25 screened, none withdrew; inclusion 18-65 yr, insulin for 12 months or
more, basal requirement at least 0.2 U/kg/day, total below 1.2 U/kg/day, BMI 18.0-28.0, HbA1c at most 9.5%,
fasting C-peptide at most 0.3 nmol/L (PDF p3-4, printed 257-258). Table 1 (PDF p6, printed 260): age 40.0 (20-56)
yr, BMI 24.6 (20.2-27.9), diabetes duration 23.1 (8.9-42.9) yr, HbA1c 7.9 (5.8-9.0)%, C-peptide 0.02 (0.02-0.08)
nmol/L. Weight not reported.

Dosing: insulin degludec (IDeg) 0.42 U/kg SC once daily at about 8 p.m. for 5 days (plus insulin aspart as needed),
then on day 6 a single SC dose of IDegAsp 0.6 U/kg (0.42 U/kg IDeg + 0.18 U/kg IAsp; 70:30) into the lower abdomen.
30-h euglycaemic clamp (Biostator, target 5.5 mmol/L). Serum IAsp (specific ELISA) to 12 h; serum IDeg (specific
sandwich ELISA) to 120 h. PK/PD model in NONMEM 7.1.2 used only to simulate twice-daily dosing (0.3 U/kg BID).

## Model and parameters
The paper describes a PK/PD model structure but reports no parameter values (PDF p5-6): IDeg, depot + one transit
compartment, absorption and transit rates, one-compartment disposition (CL, V), insulin-action compartment with
turnover and sensitivity parameters linked to GIR; IAsp, the same with four transit compartments. GIR contributions
are added. Reported exposures and times (PDF p7, printed 261):

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: glucose infusion rate in a euglycaemic clamp. Rapid onset and a distinct IAsp peak, then a flat basal
IDeg effect; GIR already about 2 mg/kg/min at time 0 from earlier IDeg doses (Discussion). No subject reached end
of action (blood glucose consistently above 8.3 mmol/L) in 30 h, so duration exceeded 30 h in all 22. IDeg steady
state reached after 2-3 days of once-daily dosing. The simulation (Fig. 4) suggests twice-daily dosing keeps both
components distinct. Hypoglycaemia: 22 confirmed episodes in 13 subjects during IDeg run-in; 6 in 5 after IDegAsp.

## Covariates
None analysed.

## Caveats
- Uncontrolled, single-arm; 4 women; all white; some overweight subjects (authors).
- No PK parameter estimates published; the BID profile is a model simulation, not measured.
- Industry-sponsored (Novo Nordisk), medical writing funded by the sponsor.
