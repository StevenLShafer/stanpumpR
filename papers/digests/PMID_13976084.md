# Said 1963: venous admixture on 100% oxygen

## Study
Physiology study, no drug. Resting recumbent subjects: 6 normal (age 18-34), 15 obstructive emphysema (group I), 14 diffuse pulmonary fibrosis/infiltration (group II), 12 markedly obese (group III; mean weight 112 kg, height 170 cm, BSA 2.22 m2) (PDF p1-2, printed 507-508). Arterial blood and expired gas sampled on room air, then after about 10-30 min of near-100% O2 (FiO2 0.95-0.99, Tables I-IV); both within 1 h. PaO2 by needle electrode (or Riley bubble method), PaCO2 by bubble or Severinghaus electrode; gases by Scholander; DLO2/DLCO in a few patients. Four deep breaths at end of N2 washout tested in 15 patients (Table VI).

## Model and parameters
No PK model. Venous admixture equations (PDF p2, printed 508):
- Qva/Qt = (SA - Sa)/(SA - Sv) (Eq 3), with SA-Sv assumed 25% during air.
- On O2: Qva/Qt = [(PA-Pa) x 0.0031] / [(Ca - Cv) + (PA-Pa) x 0.0031] (Eq 4); 0.0031 is O2 solubility in whole blood, volumes per 100 mL per mmHg; the (Ca-Cv) denominator term assumed 5 volumes per 100 mL.
- PAO2 = PIO2 - (PaCO2/PECO2)(PIO2 - PEO2) (Eq 5).

Results (Table V, printed 511, mean +/- SD; mean Qva/Qt on O2 in %): normal AaD 26 +/- 12 mmHg, Qva/Qt 1.6 +/- 0.7; emphysema AaD 120 +/- 52, 7.4 +/- 4; fibrosis 134 +/- 62, 8.3 +/- 4; obesity 147 +/- 58, 9.1 +/- 5. On air: normal AaD 7 +/- 3 mmHg, Qva/Qt 3.3 +/- 1.8%; emphysema 25 +/- 10 and 20.7%; fibrosis 31 +/- 8 and 21.3%; obesity 26 +/- 10 and 16.3% (text layer, sd of the %-columns garbled). Four deep breaths lowered obese Qva/Qt from 12.4 +/- 5.8 to 9 +/- 2.3% (p<0.05, Table VI); no significant fall in emphysema or fibrosis.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Not a drug paper. No ke0, MEAC or potency ratio. Oxygen breathing causes CO2 retention in 6 emphysema, 2 obese and 4 fibrosis patients (p509-510).

## Covariates
Disease group (emphysema, fibrosis, obesity) and lung recruitment by deep breaths; no regression equations.

## Caveats
Assumed temperature 37.5 C, assumed A-v content difference, shunt treated as all-venous-admixture lumped, O2 breathing itself may produce atelectasis, 10-30 min may leave poorly ventilated alveoli with incomplete N2 washout (authors, p512-513). Small groups, resting only.
