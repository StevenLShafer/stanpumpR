# Yassen 2007: buprenorphine-naloxone competitive interaction on ventilation

## Study
Placebo-controlled adaptive naloxone dose-selection study (data partly reported by van Dorp 2006). 44 healthy subjects
enrolled, 18-30 y (Table I: bup 0.2 mg arm 11 M/11 F, mean 70.5 kg; 0.4 mg arm 7/7, 73.6 kg). Buprenorphine 0.2 or
0.4 mg/70 kg IV: half as a 90-s bolus at t = 0, rest over 60 min. Naloxone at t = 30 min: half as 90-s bolus, half over
30 min; totals 0-7 mg/70 kg. External validation: 8 subjects (4 M/4 F), bup 0.2 mg, naloxone 3 mg bolus then 4 mg/h
for 90 min. Arterial sampling 2-480 min. Naloxone HPLC-ECD, LLOQ 1.00 ng/mL, free base. End point: inspired minute
ventilation at end-tidal PCO2 clamped 7 kPa (dynamic end-tidal forcing). NONMEM V FOCE-I; two-stage (individual PK
posthocs drive the PD model); bootstrap 1000.

Naloxone elimination t½ (0.693/β) quoted as 33 min (p.977); derived from Table III typical values: 36.9 min.
Naloxone receptor binding assumed fast (in vitro dissociation t½ 0.82 min, cited). Bup-naloxone interaction:
competitive receptor occupancy, linear transduction E = E0(1 - α·ρbup).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- Naloxone ke0 0.106 min⁻¹ (t½ke0 6.5 min), end point: reversal of buprenorphine-induced depression of minute
  ventilation (isohypercapnic). Estimated with this paper's own arterial two-compartment naloxone PK.
- Buprenorphine (opioid audit): t½ke0 173 min (ke0 0.00404 min⁻¹) on minute ventilation in presence of naloxone;
  koff t½ 40.3 min; KD 0.18 nM; partial agonist α 0.67. No MEAC (ventilation study, not analgesia). No potency ratio.
- Clinical conclusion: naloxone 4 mg/70 kg/h infusion recommended; >4 mg/h gave less reversal (bell-shaped curve).

## Covariates
Sex, age, dose, weight, height tested; weight and demographics not significant for buprenorphine PK; naloxone
covariates not reported as retained. Doses per 70 kg.

## Caveats
Two-stage PK-PD; healthy young volunteers; buprenorphine only; arterial sampling (early concentrations higher than
venous); naloxone LLOQ 1 ng/mL; poorer prediction above 4 mg/h naloxone. ke0 is tied to this PK model.
