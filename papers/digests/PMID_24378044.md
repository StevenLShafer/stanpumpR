# Rinehart 2014: first closed-loop goal-directed fluid therapy

## Study
Prospective pilot, 12 patients at two sites: Paris (n=5, aortobifemoral bypass, LiDCO Rapid; 3 M/2 F, age 69 +/- 8 y, height 165 +/- 13 cm, weight 65 +/- 16 kg) and Irvine (n=7, Whipple/hepatobiliary, Edwards EV-1000/FloTrac; 3 M/4 F, age 61 +/- 17 y, height 163 +/- 13 cm, weight 71 +/- 22 kg) (p. e37-e38).
Anaesthesia: Paris propofol TCI (target garbled in the text layer, printed as 2-4 mg/mL, almost certainly ug/mL) and remifentanil TCI 1-2 ng/mL, atracurium; Irvine propofol, fentanyl 2 (unit garbled), sevoflurane 0.8-1.2 MAC, fentanyl boluses, epidural bupivacaine 0.25% 6-10 mL before extubation (p. e36). The TCI model/software is not named in the text.
Fluids: 5 mL/kg/h isotonic crystalloid baseline plus 6% HES 130/0.4 given by the LIR closed-loop controller (Q-Core Sapphire pump). Compliance: >85% of case with SVV <13% or mean CI >= 2.5 L/min/m2.

## Model and parameters
No PK model. Results: all 12 met at least one criterion; mean CI 3.2 +/- 0.8 L/min/m2; mean 91% (76-100%) of time SVV <13%; both criteria met in 8 of 12; median case time 447 [309-483] min, EBL 200 [100-1000] mL; hospital stay 7 +/- 1 (Paris) and 7 +/- 3 d (Irvine) (p. e37-e39, Tables 1-2).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None for drugs. The remifentanil target of 1-2 ng/mL is a dosing choice, not a measured effect-site end point; no MEAC, ke0, tPeak or potency ratio is reported.

## Caveats
Pilot, no control; two different monitors; HES used; no inotrope arm (p. e40).
