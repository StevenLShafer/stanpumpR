# van Steveninck 1994 (part II): IV temazepam reproducibility over 6 months

## Study
- 9 healthy non-smokers (4 men, 5 women), 18-24 y; weight 66 (47-80) kg occasion 1, 67 (47-87) kg occasion 2.
- 0.8 mg/kg/h IV (about 0.4 mg/kg) for up to 30 min, stopped early if too sedated for the eye-movement test;
  same subjects twice, 6 months apart. Research formulation (5 mg/mL in 60% PEG 400) diluted to 0.1 mg/mL.
- Venous plasma (other arm) at 0, 7, 14, 21, 28, 35, 42, 56, 70, 120, 180, 240, 360, 420, 480, 600, 840,
  1440 min; HPLC, LOD 1.1 ng/mL, CV 3.3% at 95 ng/mL.
- Individual two-compartment fits (SIPHAR, weight 1/y²); r 0.98-0.99. End points: Cmax (observed), t½
  (from the fitted model), AUCs by trapezoid, AUC(0-∞) extrapolated by C_last/λz.
- Free fraction by ultrafiltration at 37 °C at 7, 21, 42, 120 min (occasion 2).
- PD to 8 h: saccadic peak velocity, EEG beta (Fz-Cz), alpha (Pz-Oz); Spielberger anxiety.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- Concentration-effect relations were fitted as **linear** (sigmoid Emax not reliably estimable).
  Saccadic peak velocity slopes -0.12 to -0.25 deg/s per ng/mL (Table III); EEG beta slopes 0.81-8.27
  µV per ng/mL. Intercepts for velocity 481 vs 438 deg/s (occasion 2 lower because the target angle was
  accidentally changed from ±20° to ±15°).
- **Proteresis**: slight for saccadic velocity (5 subjects occasion 1, 6 occasion 2); for EEG beta in all
  subjects occasion 1 and 7/8 occasion 2. Correcting for the changing free fraction reduced it only slightly;
  arteriovenous differences offered as another cause.
- **No ke0, t½ke0, tPeak or EC50** estimated (EC50 for EEG beta could not be derived).
- Free fraction 2.0 ± 0.7% (7 min) → 3.3 ± 0.7% (21 min) → 1.7 ± 0.2% (120 min); unbound concentrations 8, 31,
  27 and 8 ng/mL.
- Trait anxiety correlated with saccadic-velocity slope (r = -0.63).

## Covariates
None modelled.

## Caveats
n = 9 young adults; venous sampling; end points only; wide t½ variability (one 29.4 h value); research
formulation; target-angle error on occasion 2.
