# Taylor 2023: HDMTX covariates for MTXPK.org

## Study
Retrospective population PK of high-dose methotrexate (>0.5 g/m2) in 1668 pediatric/adolescent-young-adult cancer patients (age 0.1-32.3 y, median 10.9) from three US centers (CCHMC, CHOA, TXCH), 2010-2020 (Table 1, PDF p3). 7506 administrations, 30,250 plasma concentrations (after 734 post-glucarpidase immunoassay values were excluded), sampled to 380 h. Infusions 3, 4, 24 or 36 h; doses 1-18 g/m2 (short, <=8 h) and 0.5-9.5 g/m2 (long). Cancers: ALL, lymphoblastic lymphoma, NHL, osteosarcoma, other. Prior MTXPK.org model (three compartments, BSA and time-varying SCR) used as the starting model; NONMEM 7.2.0, ADVAN11 TRANS4, FOCE-I, exponential IIV, log-transformed-both-sides residual error; covariates added forward at p<0.01 (PDF p5). Between-occasion variability not modelled. Missing BSA was imputed from other courses; missing baseline albumin imputed as 3.5 g/dL. Site of treatment was added to the base model (dOFV -806.66) but is not in the final equations.

Table 2 footnote describes Q3 as between V2 and V3 (a chain); the Methods give ADVAN11 TRANS4, which is the mammillary three-compartment model. IIV and residual error estimates are not in the main text (Table S1/S2 are in the supplement). Final OFV -2825.89 (dOFV -187.59 against adjusted base).

Table 5 (PDF p10) simulated mean/2SD above mean concentrations (umol/L): 5 g/m2 over 24 h (N 3193): 24 h 84.8/127.3, 36 h 2.46/10.08, 42 h 0.96/5.34... (24 h mean 84.8; 48 h mean 0.48, 2SD 2.63; 72 h mean 0.09, 2SD 0.54). Other regimens are in Table 5.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No effect-site, ke0 or concentration-effect model. Clinical thresholds quoted: glucarpidase consensus >120 uM (>50 uM short infusion) at 24 h, >30 uM at 36 h, >10 uM at 42 h, >=5 uM at 48 h (PDF p8); delayed elimination >0.2 umol/L at 72 h (short) or 96 h (long); sampling stops at 0.1 umol/L (Fig 2 legend). Not an opioid paper.

## Covariates
BSA (Mosteller) on every parameter, serum creatinine (time-varying) on CL, short infusion and Down syndrome on CL, pleural effusion on V1 and V2, severe hypoalbuminemia (<2.5 g/dL) on Q2 and Q3. Categorical factors enter as multipliers. Tested but not retained: sex, race, ethnicity, cancer type, age, BMI, administration number, time of day (Tables 3-4; differences under about 5% in clearance). Prevalence: Down syndrome 63 courses (0.8%), severe hypoalbuminemia 212 (2.8%), pleural effusion 44 (0.5%) (Table 1).

## Caveats
Unexplained variability remains; protocol, hydration and bicarbonate not modelled; genotype not included. Severe hypoalbuminemia effect on CL was only about 2% and not significant; the hypoalbuminemia effect is on intercompartmental clearance. Down syndrome n=17 patients, all with ALL. Pleural effusion has only 44 courses. Observed-versus-predicted median deviates at late times because sampling stops once concentration reaches 0.1 umol/L (Fig 2).
