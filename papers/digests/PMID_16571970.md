# Zanderigo 2006: the well-being drug interaction model

## Study
Theoretical/simulation paper, no patients and no drug data. It extends the Minto 2000 response-surface interaction model so that each drug has a positive (desired) and a negative (side) effect surface, and "well-being" is their weighted difference. Sensitivity analyses on the interaction parameters, on therapeutic window, and a bolus simulation combining PK with the surface. Software and any numerical method are not named in the text read.

## Model and parameters
Single drug (Eqs 1-3, PDF p2): E = Emax*(C/EC50)^g/(1+(C/EC50)^g); EN likewise with EmaxN, EC50N, gN; W = E - w*EN, w = weight of negative effects (w = 1 in all simulations). C is effect-compartment concentration (ug/L).

Two drugs (Eqs 4-12, PDF p3): normalized concentrations UA = CA/EC50,A, UB = CB/EC50,B; theta = UB/(UA+UB); surface E = Emax(theta)*[(UA+UB)/U50(theta)]^g(theta) / (1 + same), with second-order polynomials in theta; U50(theta) = 1 - bU50*theta + bU50*theta^2 (Eq 12; theta=0 and 1 give U50=1). The same construct is applied to the negative effect with its own theta_N (Eqs 13-19). Simplifications (Eqs 21-22): bEmax = bEmaxN = 0 and bgamma = bgammaN = 0, so only b (positive effects) and bN (negative effects) are varied (values -2, 0, 2).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No real drug, no measured end point. ke0 is stated to be the same for positive and negative effects (p5). Not an opioid paper: no MEAC, analgesic concentration, tPeak or potency ratio. Optimal concentration range = set of Ce with W >= 0.8 (Fig 3). Results: b > 0 (synergy in positive effects) and bN < 0 enlarge the optimal range and the "area above 0.8"; b < 0 and bN > 0 shrink it; e.g. delta area +178% for (b=2, bN=0) and -54% for (b=2, bN=2), +90% for (b=-2, bN=-2) (Discussion, printed 751). No surface with more than one maximum was found for two drugs. Bolus of 1.5 mg of each drug: the Ce loop enters the optimal range at 10-11 min (b=0) or 7-8 min (b=2).

## Caveats
Purely theoretical; no experimental parameters; interactions in Emax and gamma set to zero; second-order polynomial only; the authors say tests on real drug combinations were still under way. Only two-drug combinations explored.
