# Falcão 2012: eslicarbazepine population PK, interactions and exposure–response

## Study
Population PK and PK/PD analysis pooling 641 adult partial-onset-seizure patients from three
phase III studies of eslicarbazepine acetate (prodrug of eslicarbazepine, the active moiety) 400,
800 and 1200 mg once daily, taken with 1–3 concomitant AEDs. **Sparse** plasma samples (mostly
pre-dose troughs, Cmin,ss) of eslicarbazepine and co-AEDs; efficacy (seizure frequency per 4 weeks)
from 1048 patients. Eslicarbazepine measured by chiral LC-MS/MS. NONMEM V (FO/FOCE), SAS 8.2. Most
analyses fitted log-transformed trough data; a one-compartment model with first-order absorption and
elimination; ADVAN1 without an absorption parameter failed. Baseline (Table I, PDF p.7): mean weight
70.7 ± 15.1 kg, age 37.5 y, 52% male, mean CLcr 117.7 mL/min.

## Model and parameters

**CL/F = (θ1 + DCAR·θ2 + AEDPB·θ3) · (weight/70)^0.75**, where DCAR = carbamazepine daily dose (mg)
and AEDPB = 1 if phenytoin or barbiturates are co-administered.

Carbamazepine dose effect: over 200–2400 mg/day, CL/F rises 2.66 → 5.94 L/h (+152% at 2400 mg vs
none); at the average 937 mg, +1.40 L/h. Phenytoin/barbiturates add 1.41 L/h (+60% for a typical
subject). No effect of lamotrigine, valproate, topiramate, gabapentin, clobazam, levetiracetam;
**no effect of age, sex, ethnicity or creatinine clearance** on eslicarbazepine CL/F. Terminal
half-life ~13–20 h (text, from phase I). Eslicarbazepine acetate's effect on co-AEDs was small:
carbamazepine CL/F up to +14% (Table IV), lamotrigine +12%, topiramate up to +16% (Table V).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Not an opioid; no MEAC/ke0/potency ratio applies. Exposure–response: seizure-frequency reduction
best described by an **Emax** function of eslicarbazepine Cav,ss. Baseline (ln) seizure frequency
0.888 (2.4 seizures/28 days); maximum effect a decrease of 0.668, half at **EP50 = 1080 ng/mL**
(Cav,ss), so near-maximal effect is reached at the lowest maintenance concentrations (400 mg).
Responder-rate model: max probability increase 0.947, half at **5870 ng/mL**. Seizure-free model:
Emax 0.446 on logit scale, half at **1370 ng/mL** (text, PDF p.8). Co-AEDs did not affect the
exposure–response relationship.

## Covariates
Eslicarbazepine CL/F: bodyweight (allometric ^0.75), carbamazepine daily dose (continuous), and
presence of phenytoin/barbiturates (binary AEDPB). Height also noted as weight-correlated. No CLcr,
age, sex or ethnicity term.

## Caveats
Sparse trough data with an assumption that Cmin,ss after oral ≈ IV (justified by short tmax ~1 h
relative to 24 h interval); absorption rate reflects appearance, not true absorption (prodrug).
Five co-AED interaction estimates rest on <100 patients each (clobazam, gabapentin, levetiracetam,
phenobarbital, phenytoin). Industry-sponsored (BIAL). The structural intercept is a clearance,
not a full disposition model — V/F and ka are not estimated here (stanpumpR takes them from a poster).
