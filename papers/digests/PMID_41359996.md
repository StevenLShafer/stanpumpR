# Giamarellos-Bourboulis 2026: ImmunoSep precision immunotherapy in sepsis

## Study
Multicentre (33 sites, 6 countries), randomized, double-blind, double-dummy, placebo-controlled phase 2b trial. Adults with Sepsis-3 sepsis from community-acquired, hospital-acquired or ventilator-associated pneumonia, or primary bacteraemia, enrolled within 72 h of sepsis onset. Blood ferritin and monocyte HLA-DR receptors at enrolment (6 mL EDTA blood): ferritin >4420 ng/mL = macrophage activation-like syndrome (MALS); ferritin <=4420 ng/mL and HLA-DR <5000 per CD45/CD14 monocyte = sepsis-induced immunoparalysis; others unclassified and not enrolled.
672 screened, 281 randomized 1:1, 276 analysed (5 withdrew data): 131 precision immunotherapy, 145 placebo. Mean age 70 (SD 13) y; 93 women (33.7%); median baseline SOFA 9 (IQR 7-11). 48 MALS (25 anakinra, 23 placebo); 228 immunoparalysis (106 interferon gamma, 122 placebo).
Interventions (PDF p.2, printed 776, confirmed on page image): one IV injection every 8 h and one SC injection every 48 h for 15 days. MALS: anakinra 200 mg IV (in 0.9% saline to 20 mL) plus SC placebo. Immunoparalysis: recombinant human interferon gamma 100 microgram SC (0.5 mL) plus IV placebo. Placebo: 20 mL saline IV and 0.5 mL saline SC. Creatinine clearance by Cockcroft-Gault daily; clearance <30 mL/min got half the IV dose (printed p.777, text layer).
Primary end point: a fall of at least 1.4 points in mean SOFA (days 2-9) from day-1 baseline. Patients dying before day 9 were assigned SOFA 24.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Not a PK/PD paper: no MEAC, ke0 or EC50. Clinical results (Table 2, printed p.780): primary end point 46/131 (35.1%) vs 26/145 (17.9%), difference 17.2% (95% CI 6.8 to 27.2), unadjusted OR 2.48 (1.42 to 4.32), P=.002. By stratum (printed p.781): MALS 12/25 (48.0%) vs 4/23 (17.4%), P=.04; immunoparalysis 34/106 (32.1%) vs 22/122 (18.0%), P=.02. 28-day mortality 57/131 (43.5%) vs 72/145 (49.7%), P=.34; 90-day 68.7% vs 67.6%, not different. Serious adverse events: more anaemia in the anakinra group, more haemorrhage in the interferon gamma group (printed p.782).

## Covariates
Renal function (Cockcroft-Gault creatinine clearance) only for dose halving. Baseline CCI and SOFA were subgroup interactions for outcome, not PK.

## Caveats
Authors: surrogate primary end point; ferritin and HLA-DR assays may not be feasible everywhere; subtype stability unknown; only pneumonia and bacteraemia sepsis; 355 of 672 screened were unclassified. The primary end point pools two different drugs and was not designed to separate them. Table 1 is scrambled in the text layer; its values were not used.
