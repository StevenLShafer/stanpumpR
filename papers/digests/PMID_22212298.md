# Krogh-Madsen 2012: population PK of cytarabine, etoposide and daunorubicin in AML

## Study
- Prospective population PK study, two Danish hospitals (Rigshospitalet, Herlev). 24 AML patients enrolled (14 M, 10 F);
  one woman excluded, so **23 analysed** (PDF p2-3, printed 1156-1157). Four had two treatment series about 1 month apart.
- Table 1 (PDF p3, page image; means and ranges): age 52.3 (22-68) y; weight 79.4 (46-117) kg; BSA 1.91 (1.5-2.2) m2;
  serum creatinine 74.1 (49-140) umol/L; Cockcroft-Gault creatinine clearance 116 (47-189) mL/min; bilirubin 9.87 (2-55)
  umol/L; baseline WBC 26.9 (0.9-167) x10^6 cells/mL; ALAT 40.0 (8-152) U/L.
- ADE 10+3+5: cytarabine 100 mg/m2 b.i.d. days 1-10 as a 5-min IV push; daunorubicin 50 mg/m2 q.d. days 1, 3, 5 and
  etoposide 100 mg/m2 q.d. days 1-5, each as 1-h infusions; order chosen by the nurse.
- 8-10 central venous samples per patient per series (tubes with tetrahydrouridine); 162 / 177 / 109 observations for
  ara-C / etoposide / daunorubicin. Plasma, simultaneous HPLC: ara-C UV 280 nm (LLoQ 13 ng/mL); etoposide (LLoQ
  52.5 ng/mL) and daunorubicin (LLoQ 15 ng/mL) by fluorescence. Precision 0.2-13.5% RSD.
- NONMEM VI 2.0, FOCE-Laplacian, M3 for BLQ data (28 / 53 / 17 BLQ for Eto / ara-C / Dnr); PsN SCM (forward P<0.05,
  backward P<0.01), 200-sample bootstrap, VPCs. Daunorubicin fitted with the $PRIOR subroutine using Bogason 2011
  (Br J Clin Pharmacol 71:514) as prior, degrees of freedom 2.

## Model and parameters
All three drugs: two compartments, additive error on the log scale. Table 2 (PDF p5, printed 1159; bootstrap estimates;
confirmed on the page image). Covariate form (PDF p4): P = theta x exp(eta) x (1 + theta_j x (COV - median));
sex: x (1 + theta_j) in women. Etoposide final clearance split: TVCL = (theta1 + theta2 x cCrCL) x bWBC term.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No effect site, ke0, concentration-effect relation or therapeutic range; the paper names AUC (etoposide,
anthracyclines) and daunorubicin peak as possible effect drivers from other work, without values. Not an opioid paper.

## Covariates
- Etoposide: cCrCL (Cockcroft-Gault, unbounded) and bWBC on CL; sex on Vc. Text (PDF p6): cCrCL 116 -> 60 gives -30%
  total CL, 116 -> 160 gives +24%; AUC0-24 122.5 / 90.1 / 74.0 mg/L.h at cCrCL 60 / 116 / 160; bWBC 9 -> 90 gives +15%.
  Renal CL is 54% of total at 116 mL/min (vs 24-40% in earlier literature). Bilirubin and ALAT dropped.
- Cytarabine: bWBC on CL (inverse; -17% at 90 vs 9 x10^6/mL). Women's 9% higher CL (RSE 87%) not kept.
- Daunorubicin: Vc-bWBC, +1.9% per 10^6 cells/mL, kept though not significant.
- Age, BSA, weight and serum creatinine not significant for any drug.

## Caveats
- 23 patients, sparse sampling; ara-C BLQ by about 2 h, daunorubicin all BLQ after 9 h (Vp of ara-C RSE 32%).
- Table 2's etoposide renal slope is inconsistent with the paper's own text, footnote and Fig 4 (open question).
- Text calls women's Vc 32% lower, while -0.2793 in the stated form gives 27.9%.
- Daunorubicin estimates depend heavily on the Bogason prior; small RSEs reflect the prior, not the data.
- bWBC medians for etoposide and cytarabine are not printed.
