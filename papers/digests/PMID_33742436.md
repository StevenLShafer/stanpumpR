# Douxfils 2021: ICSH 2021 update, laboratory measurement of DOACs

## Study
Consensus guidance (practice guideline/review, ICSH working party, 10 authors) updating the 2018 ICSH document. Based on peer-reviewed literature, expert opinion and good laboratory practice (PDF p. 2). Covers apixaban, dabigatran, rivaroxaban, edoxaban; betrixaban is excluded as discontinued. No new patient data.

## Model and parameters
No PK model. The paper gives no tabulated PK parameters in its text (Fig. 1, PDF p. 6, shows expected plasma ranges per DOAC from product labels and trials; numbers not read). Numerical values stated in the text:

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Not an opioid paper; no ke0, EC50 or MEAC. Concentration-based decision thresholds (30 and 50 ng/mL, above) are plasma DOAC concentrations measured by drug-calibrated assays (anti-Xa, dTT, ECT); the paper does not say whether total or free, and does not name which DOAC they apply to. Tests: anti-Xa/dTT/ECA for quantitation; PT/APTT/TT screening are insensitive; DOACs interfere with clot-based thrombophilia and lupus anticoagulant assays even at low levels.
Trough sampling advised: just before next dose, after 5 or more doses (Table 1).

## Covariates
Table 1 lists groups where testing may help (non-urgent): advanced age, severe renal failure/dialysis, prior high-bleeding-risk interventions, BMI above 40 kg/m2 (peak and trough), drug interactions. Paediatric patients may have lower DOAC levels than adults (p. 1011); no equations.

## Caveats
Consensus/expert opinion; the authors state data are insufficient for dose adjustment from levels alone. Several authors report conflicts with DOAC-testing manufacturers (one is co-inventor of a DOAC filter). The text says the 30/50 ng/mL thresholds lack prospective clinical validation.
