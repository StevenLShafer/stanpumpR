# Lexapro label 4/2024: escitalopram pharmacokinetics

## Study
Product label, section 12.3; section 6.1 ECG Changes for QT.

## Model and parameters
- Linear, dose-proportional 10-30 mg/day; steady state in about one week; accumulation 2.2-2.5 x the single-dose
  concentrations in young healthy subjects.
- Absolute bioavailability **of citalopram** about 80% (none given for escitalopram); tablet and solution
  bioequivalent. Peak blood levels about 5 h after 20 mg; no food effect.
- Protein binding about 56%. Volume of distribution of citalopram about 12 L/kg (no escitalopram value).
- **Mean terminal half-life about 27-32 h.** **Oral clearance 600 mL/min** (derived: 36 L/h), about 7% renal.
- Metabolism by CYP3A4 and CYP2C19 (N-demethylation) to S-DCT (about one-third of parent at steady state) and S-DDCT
  (mostly undetectable); metabolites at least 7- and 27-fold less potent.
- Urine: about 8% as escitalopram, 10% as S-DCT.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No concentration-effect relation for antidepressant efficacy and no therapeutic range. Not an opioid.

QT safety exposure-response (section 6.1, ECG Changes, PDF p. 14; no EC50 or concentration target given):
- Randomized, placebo- and moxifloxacin (400 mg)-controlled crossover, escalating multiple-dose study in 113 healthy
  subjects. Maximum mean placebo-adjusted QTcF change (95% upper confidence bound): 4.5 (6.4) ms at 10 mg and
  10.7 (12.7) ms at a supratherapeutic 30 mg once daily (measured).
- From the established exposure-response relationship the label predicts 6.6 ms at the 20 mg Cmax (bound given as
  7.9 ms) (model prediction, not measured at 20 mg).
- Mean Cmax on 30 mg was 1.7-fold the 20 mg steady-state mean Cmax, and similar to the exposure expected in CYP2C19
  poor metabolizers on 20 mg.
- Same paragraph, clinical trials: no Lexapro patient (N=625) had QTcF > 500 ms or a prolongation > 60 ms, against
  0.2% of placebo patients (N=527).

## Covariates
- Elderly (>= 65 y): AUC and t½ about 50% higher, Cmax unchanged; 10 mg/day recommended.
- Sex: no adjustment needed.
- Hepatic (citalopram data): oral clearance -37%, t½ doubled; 10 mg/day.
- Renal mild-moderate (citalopram): oral clearance -17%; no data below CrCl 20 mL/min.
- Pediatric 7-11 y (popPK, 20 mg/day): Cmax +93%, AUCtau +86% vs adults; 12-17 y single 10 mg: AUC -19%, Cmax +26%.

## Caveats
Several values are carried over from racemic citalopram (bioavailability, volume, hepatic and renal effects).

## Verification against stanpumpR
- Half-life 27-32 h (R:29-30; help:11): **matches**; the model's ln 2 x 815 / 16.3 = 34.7 h (derived, confirmed).
- Observation not in the repo: the label's oral clearance, 600 mL/min = 36 L/h, is 2.2 times Liu 2022's CL/F of
  16.3 L/h for CYP2C19 normal metabolizers. The model's steady-state average on 10 mg/day (25.6 ng/mL, R:33, help:11)
  would be about 11.6 ng/mL with the label's clearance (derived: 10 mg / 24 h / 36 L/h). The half-life agreement
  hides this, because the model's V/F (815 L, about 11.6 L/kg at 70 kg) is also smaller than 36 L/h x 30 h / ln 2
  (about 1560 L, derived). Worth stating in the help as a known difference (Chinese TDM cohort vs label populations).
- Elderly AUC and t½ about 50% higher: the repo gates age out (R:44-50); the label effect is not modelled.
