# Li 2012: PK/PD of effervescent vs standard ibuprofen after third-molar extraction

## Study
- Single-centre, randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled, parallel-group, 8 h inpatient trial (NCT00631111). 262 healthy subjects 16-40 y randomised after extraction of >= 2 third molars (>= 1 bony mandibular impaction), moderate-severe baseline pain (>= 50 mm VAS).
- PK/PD analysis: ibuprofen effervescent tablet 400 mg (n = 30), Nurofen 2 x 200 mg (n = 22), placebo (n = 37, efficacy only).
- Plasma sampled predose, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, 45, 60, 90 min, 2-8 h hourly, and before rescue. Racemic ibuprofen by HPLC-MS/MS (PPD).
- PD: 5-point categorical pain relief (0-4) treated as continuous; stopwatch TFPR and TMPR; time to remedication.
- PK: one compartment, first-order absorption with lag (S-plus nlme), separate fits per formulation. PD: placebo + sigmoid Emax on effect-site concentration driven by linearly interpolated observed plasma concentrations (NONMEM VI).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Table II (n = 89), estimate ± SE as re-assembled: Pmax 1.54 ± 0.12 pain-relief units, k (placebo) 1.26 ± 0.06 /h, **keo 1.49 ± 0.20 /h (equilibration half-life 28 min, p 94)**, Emax 1.80 ± 0.24 units above placebo, **EC50 10.2 mcg/mL effect-site** (SE printed 0.23), gamma 2.0 ± 0.43. End point: categorical pain relief score (0-4) after third-molar extraction, adults/adolescents 16-40 y. Effect-site peak ~1 h (effervescent) and ~2 h (standard).
Hazard models: placebo TFPR/TMPR hazards 1.04 and 0.30 /h; slopes 0.088 and 0.11 mL/mcg (Table III); ~10 mcg/mL plasma doubles the hazard. Remedication hazard 1.34 /h at relief 0, 0.034 /h at relief 3 (Table V). 8 h remedication 47% / 55% / 81% (effervescent / standard / placebo). Not an opioid; no potency ratio.

## Covariates
None in PK (healthy subjects, by design).

## Caveats
- Small PK/PD groups (30 and 22); one-compartment PK; plasma driven by interpolation of observed values.
- Pain relief treated as continuous.
- Industry-funded (Pfizer Consumer Healthcare).

## Verification against stanpumpR
- EC50 10.2 mg/L effect-site, adult third-molar extraction: Table II. **matches**.
- "equilibration rate could not be retrieved" (R/drugs_ibuprofen.R:81-82; inst/help/drugs/ibuprofen.md:21): the paper reports keo 1.49 /h, t1/2ke0 28 min (Table II and p 94). **discrepancy**: the statement is stale now that the paper is archived. Not a numeric error in the model (stanpumpR uses Hannam 2018's 1.04 h), but the adult dental estimate (28 min) is about half the paediatric 62 min and is relevant to the code's flagged assumption of applying the paediatric value to adults. Note Li's ke0 is tied to a one-compartment PK and interpolated plasma data, so it is not directly transferable to Morse's two-compartment model either.
