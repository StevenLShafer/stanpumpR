# Tenero 2006: S(-)-carvedilol concentration and exercise heart rate (inhibitory Emax)

## Study
- Purpose: show carvedilol CR once daily gives 24-h beta1 blockade comparable to IR twice daily (PDF p1, printed 27L).
- Model-building data, healthy subjects (age, sex, weight not reported):
  - GSK study: single oral carvedilol IR 12.5 mg (n = 24), 37.5 mg (n = 20), 50 mg (n = 19), IR 25 mg every 12 h (n = 22), given sequentially; then a pilot CR formulation (n = 11). Bicycle tests and PK sampling to 11 h after single IR doses and to 24 h for the twice-daily IR and the CR.
  - de Mey 1994 (outside data): 12 healthy volunteers, single IR 12.5, 25, 50, 100 mg, crossover; sampling and bicycle tests at 0, 2, 4, 6, 9, 12, 22 h.
- End point: heart rate at the end of a supine 6-min bicycle ergometry test at the workload set at screening to give 140 beats/min (continuous ECG).
- Analyte: plasma **S(-)-carvedilol** (the beta-blocking enantiomer). The assay is not described.
- Software: NONMEM V (first-order method mainly; FOCE and FOCE-I also tried); 1,000-sample bootstrap (96% converged); condition number 42.
- Prediction set: 173 patients with mild-to-severe heart failure or post-MI LV dysfunction, IR 3.125-25 mg twice daily then CR 10-80 mg once daily, sampled over 24 h on day 14 (Packer 2006); PD predicted from their S(-) concentrations with the healthy-volunteer model.

## Model and parameters
Direct-effect inhibitory Emax model (no effect compartment); individual concentration-effect plots showed no hysteresis (p 28L, citing data on file). E = E0 − Emax·C/(EC50 + C), with a linear time trend on E0 (baseline slope) and a 24-h correction θ. The paper does not print the equation; the form is inferred from "direct effect inhibitory Emax" (derived).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- EC50 7.71 ng/mL **S(-)-carvedilol** in plasma, for the blunting of exercise-induced heart rate (supine bicycle ergometry), healthy subjects. Emax 19.2 beats/min from E0 146 (about 13% maximum decrease, abstract). (Derived: 7.7 ng/mL ÷ 406.5 g/mol ≈ 19 nmol/L S(-)-carvedilol.)
- Direct effect: no ke0, t½ke0 or tPeak; hysteresis not seen. Effect plateaus at higher concentrations (Figure 1, PDF p3: observations to about 140 ng/mL).
- The IR and CR concentration-effect relationships overlapped (Figure 2, PDF p4): input rate did not change the relation.
- Predicted CR:IR ratios in patients (Table 3, PDF p5): AUEC 1.07 (90% CI 1.04-1.10), PDmax 0.99 (0.96-1.02), PDmin 1.01 (0.95-1.06).
- Not an opioid; no MEAC or potency ratio. No therapeutic range given.

## Covariates
None tested besides study (separate baseline slopes) and occasion (IOV on E0). No age, weight, sex or CYP2D6 effects.

## Caveats
- Short supplement paper; no PK parameters, no demographics, no assay description, no model equation.
- No placebo arm; the 24-h θ is unexplained by the authors.
- EC50 IIV 44% with a %SE of 82% on that IIV; EC50 bootstrap range 3.0-11.8 ng/mL.
- The analyte is the S(-)-enantiomer; the result cannot be applied to racemic concentrations without the R/S ratio, which this paper does not give.
- The heart-failure "results" are model predictions, not measured effects.
