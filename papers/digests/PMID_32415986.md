# Abd-Rahman 2020: chloroquine PK/PD in a P. vivax volunteer infection study

## Study
- Phase Ib, single-centre, open-label volunteer infection study (ACTRN12616000174482), 3 cohorts of 8; 24 healthy adults inoculated IV with ~564 viable P. vivax-infected erythrocytes on day 0 (p. 1056).
- Chloroquine phosphate (Avloclor) orally over 3 days starting day 8, 9 or 10: 1.55 g base total for adults >=60 kg, or 25 mg base/kg for <60 kg. Table 1 (p. 1057): base doses 620 mg at 0 h, 310 mg at 6 h, 310 mg at 24 h (one subject 232.5 mg), 310 mg at 48 h (two subjects 232.5 mg). Phosphate-to-base factor 0.62.
- Subjects (Table 1): 13 M / 11 F; age mean 25.6 y (SD 6.6, range 19-44); weight mean 73.3 kg (SD 10.8, range 57.2-99.5); BMI mean 23.7; hematocrit mean 0.4.
- Sampling for chloroquine (CQ) and desethylchloroquine (DCQ) in plasma and whole blood: predose and 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 12, 24, 48, 72, 96, 168, 240, 480 h after the first dose. Parasitemia by 18S qPCR (LOD 10 parasites/mL), daily to twice daily before treatment and out to 480 h after.
- LC-MS/MS; LLOQ 1.35 ug/L (CQ plasma), 1.0 ug/L (CQ whole blood), 1.3 ug/L (DCQ plasma), 1.75 ug/L (DCQ whole blood). Data: 1,084 concentrations (271 per analyte/matrix), 619 parasitemia values. Left-censored below LLOQ/LOD.
- Monolix 4.3.3, MATLAB/IQMtools; sequential PK then PD (empirical Bayes PK estimates as regressors). Plasma and whole blood modelled separately. Concentrations converted to molar using 319.8 (CQ) and 291.8 g/mol (DCQ).

## Model and parameters
Two-compartment, first-order absorption and elimination for CQ; DCQ two-compartment with first-order input from the CQ central compartment (p. 1057-1058, Fig. 1). 18% of CQ assumed converted to DCQ (fixed, from urinary recovery data) for identifiability. All parameters are apparent (/F); Frel fixed at 100%. No covariate (age, sex, weight) retained.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: log10 P. vivax parasitemia (qPCR, parasites/mL). Not a clinical or analgesic end point; not an opioid paper.
- Effect-compartment model (delayed response). ke0 (Table 3, p. 1061): plasma 0.0212 1/h (95% CI 0.0171-0.0253); whole blood 0.0288 1/h (0.0212-0.0364). Equilibration half-lives given in the text: 32.7 h (27.4-40.5) plasma and 24.1 h (19.0-32.7) whole blood (derived check: ln2/0.0212 = 32.7 h, ln2/0.0288 = 24.1 h). The text-layer row labels of Table 3 are scrambled; the ke0 values were assigned to plasma/whole blood by this half-life match. No tPeak reported.
- Emax (max killing rate) 0.213 1/h (0.196-0.230), shared by plasma and whole blood fits; EC50 fixed: whole blood 0.28 umol/L (from a published relapse analysis), plasma 0.047 umol/L (assumed six-fold lower, from the whole blood:plasma ratio); Hill 2.5 fixed. EC50 and ke0 were poorly identifiable because no subject had recrudescence; sensitivity of estimates to initial values is reported (p. 1061).
- Secondary: parasite multiplication rate 17.4 per 48 h (95% CI 14.3-20.4); log10 PRR48 2.6 (2.5-2.7), i.e. a PRR of ~400 per 48 h; parasite clearance half-life 4.5 h (4.1-5.0); time above EC50 14.3 d (plasma), 18.3 d (whole blood).
- Resistance simulations (Fig. 4, p. 1062): treatment success at day 28 fell below 90% when Emax < 0.16 1/h (plasma) or < 0.184 1/h (whole blood); EC50 needed <= 19.5 ug/L (0.061 umol/L) plasma or <= 146 ug/L (0.456 umol/L) whole blood for >90% success.
- No MEAC or analgesic potency ratio (not applicable). Matrix: plasma vs whole blood concentrations differ ~6-fold, so any threshold must carry its matrix.

## Covariates
Age, sex, body weight tested; none retained in the final PK model (p. 1058). PD: no covariates; IIV on baseline parasitemia, kgrow, Emax.

## Caveats
- EC50, Hill fixed and ke0 weakly identified (no recrudescence). DCQ activity ignored in the PD model. Model assumes CQ acts on all asexual stages, though trophozoites/schizonts may be insensitive (authors). Hypnozoite relapse not modelled.
- Small n (24), healthy volunteers, oral data only so all parameters apparent. Residual bias in the first 100 h (absorption). Authors note the model may be used to simulate COVID-19 dosing; this was a speculation, not tested.
- Supplementary material S1 not read (not in the PDF).
