# Boer 2010: serum creatinine reference values under one year

## Study
- Retrospective hospital-database study at Erasmus MC-Sophia, Rotterdam, 2003-2008. 29,839 creatinine determinations in 5,608 infants under 1 year.
- Exclusions (Table 2): kidney disease, cytostatics, prematurity (< 37 wk), small for gestational age, syndromes, major surgery, prerenal problems, urological problems, hyperbilirubinaemia, haemolysis, hypertriglyceridaemia and muscle disease.
- One random assay per patient.
- 442 infants were included: 67 in week 1, 15 per week to week 13, then 5 per week. 57.7% were boys.
- Assay: enzymatic (Roche Creatinine Plus, Hitachi 912), calibrated to HPLC; bias 0.65%; interassay CV < 2%.
- Model: a "broken stick" of three log-linear segments, with Y = log10(creatinine, µmol/L) and X described as log2(age in years).
  - Intercept A 1.75.
  - Breakpoints at day 65 and day 216.
  - Initial slope −0.07 (15% fall per doubling of age).
  - Final slope 0.045 (11% rise per doubling).
  - Plateau geometric mean 20 µmol/L; R² 0.63; residual SD 0.084 (log10). The 2.5th/97.5th percentiles are 10^(pred ± 2 × 0.084).
- No relation to length, weight or sex (residual correlations NS; sex P = 0.11).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Not applicable.

## Covariates
Age only; sex, weight and length were not significant.

## Caveats
- Hospital population, retrospective.
- Few samples in the first days (5 on day 1).
- Term infants only; enzymatic assay only.
- Applying Pottel's selection method raises the means by about 20%.
