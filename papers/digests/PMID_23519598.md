# Abduljalil 2013: steady-state population PK/PD of phenprocoumon (CYP2C9, VKORC1)

## Study
Multicentre cross-sectional study (Tübingen, Cologne, Mannheim) in outpatients on long-term oral phenprocoumon
(PDF p3, printed 361). 350 Caucasian adults enrolled; 72 excluded for unstable dosing, leaving 278 (p6, printed
364). Inclusion required a stable dose over the 4 weeks before enrolment (±3 mg/week from the mean weekly dose).
Indications: atrial fibrillation 151, prosthetic valve 37, thromboembolism 37, ischaemic stroke 11, other 42.
Table 1 (p6, image): age 68.6 ± 11 y (26-90); 173 M / 105 F; weight 81 ± 15 kg (46-132); BMI 27.9 ± 4.6;
steady-state INR 2.54 ± 0.57 (1.10-5.00); weekly dose 13.62 ± 5.75 mg (3.75-37.50); trough phenprocoumon
2744 ± 1057 ng/mL (256-6510); 4'-OH 56.1 ± 27.3 ng/mL; 7-OH 63.8 ± 31.9 ng/mL. CYP2C9 *1/*1 172, *1/*2 61,
*1/*3 36, *2/*2 5, *2/*3 4, *3/*3 0. VKORC1 1173C>T: CC 97, CT 130, TT 51.

One plasma sample per patient, taken just before the next dose (trough). LC-MS/MS of racemic phenprocoumon (sum
of enantiomers) and 4'- and 7-hydroxy metabolites; LLOQ 400 ng/mL parent, 20 ng/mL metabolites (p4, printed 362).
INR measured by routine laboratories. NONMEM VII, FOCE-I, PRED routine; 1000-replicate bootstrap (PsN).

## Model and parameters
Steady-state model (p5, printed 363): input = constant infusion (dose/dosing interval), so volumes and
absorption are not estimated. One compartment for parent, one each for the two metabolites (their clearances
fixed at an arbitrary 100 mL/h, so formation fractions are relative), and a linear INR model with E0 fixed at 1
and a VKORC1-dependent slope. Total CL = sum over the two CYP2C9 alleles of the per-allele clearance + a
non-CYP2C9 clearance; covariates weight (allometric to 70 kg, mainly on total CL), CYP3A comedication (on
CL_N2C9, categorical multipliers 0.158 to 6.325), atrial fibrillation (on total CL), age (power, reference 40 y,
on the VKORC1 slope). Exact equations are in ESM Appendix A (not in the archive). Residual errors fixed from
assay limits (single sample per patient).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: steady-state INR (single trough-time value). Linear model, INR = E0 + slope × concentration, E0 = 1
fixed; slope depends on VKORC1 genotype and age. Trough phenprocoumon needed for INR 2.6: 2.84 (CC), 1.97 (CT),
1.33 µg/mL (TT) (p7-8, printed 365-366); the reference covariates for those numbers are not stated in the
main text (derived: 1.6/(0.316 × 2.84) = 1.78 and 1.6/(0.675 × 1.33) = 1.78, so both rows imply the same
unstated age factor). TT carriers need 2.13-fold lower exposure than CC for the same INR. No effect
compartment, ke0, turnover of clotting factors or time course of INR: the authors state the model cannot
predict non-steady-state INR. Not an opioid paper: no MEAC, ke0, tPeak or potency ratio.

Empirical doses (Table 2, p7, image): VKORC1 CC/CYP2C9*1/*1 17.89 ± 5.47 mg/week (n 61); CT/*1/*1 14.50 ±
5.42 (n 78); TT/*1/*1 9.47 ± 5.41 (n 33). Mean INR per group 2.23-2.90 (single-patient cells excluded).

## Covariates
CYP2C9 alleles (per-allele additive CL), weight (allometric, 70 kg reference), CYP3A-modifying comedication
(on CL_N2C9), atrial fibrillation (×0.833 on total CL), VKORC1 1173C>T genotype (PD slope), age (power on
PD slope, reference 40 y). Covariates explained 50.0% of the variability. Exact equations only in ESM.

## Caveats
One sample per patient; residual error fixed from assay limits; IIV on CL heavily shrunk (53%). Steady-state
only, no volumes, no absorption, no INR dynamics. Metabolite formation fractions are relative to arbitrary fixed
metabolite clearances. Racemate measured (no enantiomers). Comedication effects categorical and arbitrary
(authors' own word). No *3/*3 patients. Within-paper inconsistencies: *3 CL 5.83 (Table 3) vs 5.7 (abstract,
text); CL_N2C9 5.13 vs 5.3 / 5.25; TT slope 0.675 vs 0.673. Table 1 lists the BMI range in reverse
(45.6-16.7). The methods give a phenprocoumon LLOQ of 400 ng/mL (p4), yet Table 1's lowest concentration is
256 ng/mL.
