# Kim 2017: remifentanil pharmacokinetics in obesity

## Study
- Pooled reanalysis of nine published data sets (Egan 1993, 1996, 1998, 2005; Minto 1997; Drover 1998; Mertens
  2003; Kern 2004; La Colla 2010): 229 adults (107 patients, 122 volunteers), 97 M / 132 F, 4,455 arterial blood
  concentrations, 0.054-245.4 ng/mL, sampling 2-523 min (p. 1020-1021, Tables 1-2).
- Combined demographics (Table 1): age 41.4 (SD 16.7) yr, range 20-85; weight 81.3 (32.9) kg, 45-215; height
  150-195.6 cm; BMI 27.9 (11.9), 16.1-73.7. La Colla 2010 contributed 15 morbidly obese women (mean 188.2 kg,
  BMI 69.4). Bolus, infusion and TCI dosing.
- NONMEM 7.2, ADVAN6, FOCE-I; log-normal IIV; combined additive + proportional residual error. Covariates (weight,
  age, sex, BMI, Janmahasatian FFM) by forward inclusion (ΔOFV -6.63, P < 0.01) and backward elimination
  (+10.83, P < 0.001). 2,000-replicate nonparametric bootstrap; repeated twofold cross-validation.
- FFM (Janmahasatian; p. 1022, eq. 3-4): men 9.27e3 TBW/(6.68e3 + 216 BMI); women 9.27e3 TBW/(8.78e3 + 244 BMI).

Base model (no covariates, Table 4): V1 4.72, V2 7.47, V3 3.46 L; CL1 2.71, CL2 1.78, CL3 0.201 L/min.
Bootstrap medians close to the estimates (e.g. θ1 4.8 [4.31-5.31], θ4 2.77 [2.68-2.87]; Table 4 right column,
garbled in the text layer). Performance (Table 5): total MDPE 2.5%, MDAPE 21.2%; BMI > 35 MDPE 2.8%, MDAPE 21.7%
(Minto model MDPE -37.7% in BMI > 35 is shown in the third row of Table 5 per the layout; the row labels are
scrambled, so subgroup attribution of individual Minto/La Colla values is uncertain).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- PK only. No ke0, no EC50, no MEAC, no potency ratio. The authors state that no ke0 can be estimated from these
  data and point to time of peak effect (Minto 2003, ref 43) as one way to obtain effect-site predictions
  (p. 1030). They assume remifentanil PD is not influenced by obesity.
- Simulations (Table 6, 175 cm, 25-yr-old man, Cp 5 ng/mL TCI): 120-min totals 1868.7 ug at 75 kg to 2328.2 ug
  at 150 kg; a 150 kg patient needs about 125% and a 75-yr-old about 75% of a 25-yr-old 75 kg patient's 5-h dose.

## Covariates
Weight (power on V1, CL1), FFM (power on V2), age (linear on V2, V3, CL1, CL2). Sex and BMI not significant once
weight was in the model. No children; authors call the model unsuitable for younger patients (p. 1031).

## Caveats
- No PD; no ke0.
- Unknown number of below-quantitation samples (believed < 2%); mixed assays across nine studies.
- All obese subjects were patients (volunteer/patient covariate not pursued because of confounding).
- No prospective validation. Linear age terms extrapolate poorly outside 20-85 yr.
