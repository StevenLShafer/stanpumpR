# Fagan 1982: early kinetics of intravenous propranolol

## Study
- Letter. Six healthy male volunteers, 20-31 years, 60-90 kg; excluded asthma, heart failure, beta-blocker allergy, other
  medicines (PDF p1-2).
- Propranolol (Inderal) 0.05 mg/kg IV at 1 mg/min (infusion 3.0-4.5 min) after an overnight fast, into a forearm vein
  through running 5% dextrose (PDF p2). The salt is not stated; Inderal is the hydrochloride.
- Venous samples from the contralateral antecubital vein at 1, 2, 5, 10, 20 and 30 min after the end of the infusion, then
  (heparin lock) at 1, 2, 3, 4, 6 and 8 h (PDF p2).
- Plasma propranolol (racemic, total) by GC-MS with hexadeuterated propranolol internal standard (PDF p2).
- Analysis: nonlinear least squares on an HP-2100, fitted first to the group mean data and then to each subject; one, two
  and three compartments tried, three fitted best; infusion intercepts converted to bolus-equivalent intercepts (Loo and
  Riegelman). Cp(t) = P e^(-pi t) + A e^(-alpha t) + B e^(-beta t). Vd = Dose/(beta AUC), Vc = Dose/(P + A + B),
  QB = Dose/AUC (PDF p2).

Derived (mine) from the Table 1 mean column taken as one triexponential per 3.8 mg: V1 = 3800/343.5 = 11.06 L;
AUC = sum(C/lambda) = 3433 ng min/mL = 57.2 ng h/mL; CL = 1.107 L/min; Vss = 263.9 L; half-lives 0.81 min, 12.6 min,
3.41 h. These differ from the group-fit half-lives the paper prints (1.4 min, 16 min, 2.8 h), because the mean column is
an average of individual parameters, not the fit to the mean data. The mean of the individual Vc (0.41 L/kg, about 31 L
at 76 kg) is also far from Dose/(mean P + A + B), because subjects 2 and 3 have very large P. Per subject (derived,
triexponential subjects 1-4 and 6): Vss 253, 266, 418, 319, 376 L; subject 5 (biexponential) 206 L.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No effect was measured. The authors relate the fast (pi) phase to uptake into highly perfused tissue such as the heart and
cite rapid onset of heart-rate, dP/dt and nodal effects from other studies (Hesse 1979, Riva 1980); they say the time
course of effect was then under study (PDF p3-4). No ke0, tPeak or effective concentration. Not an opioid.

## Covariates
None. Doses were per kg; Vd and Vc are reported per kg, QB in absolute L/min.

## Caveats
- Six young men; letter format, no individual weights (doses imply 60-90 kg at 0.05 mg/kg).
- Venous sampling; heparin lock after 30 min (authors note heparin can alter binding, not total concentration; PDF p4).
- Large between-subject spread in P and pi (subject 3 pi 2.445/min, P 1007 ng/mL), so the mean column is dominated by
  subjects 2-3 for P and pi.
- The fitted coefficients of the group mean curve are not printed, only its three half-lives.
