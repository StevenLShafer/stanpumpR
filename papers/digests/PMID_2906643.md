# Greenblatt 1988: desmethyldiazepam (nordiazepam) kinetics and formation from diazepam

## Study
- 12 healthy adults (7 women, 5 men), 34-79 y, mean 62 y; one on digoxin.
- Four treatments, ≥ 3 weeks apart: IV DMDZ 10 mg over 1 min (n = 12); IV diazepam 5-15 mg over 1 min (n = 11); oral clorazepate dipotassium 15 mg (n = 10); oral DMDZ 10 mg tablet (n = 11).
- Venous samples to ≥ 14 days (DMDZ) or ≥ 7 days (diazepam). GC-ECD.
- Fraction of diazepam converted to systemic DMDZ: F = (DMDZ AUC after IV DZ × IV DMDZ dose) / (DMDZ AUC after IV DMDZ × IV DZ dose) × 1.05 (MW ratio DZ/DMDZ). Computable in 9 of 11.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. DMDZ > 96% protein bound (cited).

## Covariates
DMDZ Vd vs body weight (r = 0.73) and % ideal body weight (r = 0.91). Age effects discussed, not modelled.

## Caveats
- Mean age 62 y; diazepam t½ longer than in young adults (authors).
- Conversion from 9 subjects; ~ 27% of diazepam to temazepam cited from unpublished data.
