# Davies 1997: Clinical pharmacokinetics of naproxen (review)

## Study
Narrative review (no new data, no meta-analysis). Tables I (absorption: Cmax, tmax, AUC by formulation/dose), II
(healthy volunteers: t½, Vd/F, CL/F) and III (disease states) compile the literature.

## Model and parameters
Key facts stated by the review (page cited):
- Absorption rapid and complete orally, whether suspension, capsule or tablet; oral AUC similar to IV (p. 269,
  refs 63-64). Single regular-release doses up to 4 g absorbed with Cmax at 0.5-3 h (p. 269).
- AUC linearly proportional to dose up to a total dose of 500 mg; above 500 mg the unbound fraction rises, so
  total (renal) clearance rises while unbound clearance is unchanged (Summary p. 268; p. 280; p. 279 sect 1.4).
  Section 2.1 also notes a suggestion of dose-dependence within 250-500 mg (ref 62), and states linearity "up to
  500 mg twice daily" from older refs: the review is internally loose on per-dose vs per-day.
- Each 500 and 1000 mg/day increment raised mean trough by 34.5% and 14.6% (p. 280).
- Vd/F 5-10 L (0.1-0.2 L/kg), about plasma volume (p. 274).
- Binding > 99.9% in plasma, high affinity to albumin (K 5-8 × 10^6 L/mol); 99% bound at 100 mg/L (96% to
  albumin), 93% at 500 mg/L (87% to albumin) (p. 274). Free fraction rises over 23-800 mg/L (p. 277).
- Healthy-volunteer half-lives in Table II mostly 12-19 h; Vree 1993 entries 25.8 / 25.7 h (p. 275-277).
- Metabolism: CYP2C9 and CYP1A2 O-demethylation to 6-O-desmethylnaproxen; urinary acyl glucuronides ~51%,
  6-DMN acyl glucuronide ~14%, isoglucuronides ~7% and ~6%; < 1% unchanged (p. 279). Renal clearance about 10% of
  total clearance (p. 285).
- Elderly (Upton 1984): unbound fraction doubled, intrinsic clearance halved, steady-state totals unchanged
  (p. 285); another study 40% lower unbound clearance in the elderly; another 37% lower intrinsic clearance (p. 286).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- Rheumatoid arthritis: no clinical response with trough serum < 18 mg/L (p. 280); good response with serum
  naproxen above 50 mg/L (trough) (p. 280); efficacy linear in total trough concentration (ref 100).
- Platelet COX-1 (thromboxane B2) inhibition: sigmoid Emax, EC50 7.7 ± 4.4 mg/L total, 25.3 ± 22 µg/L unbound
  (p. 280, ref 74). End point: TXB2 inhibition, not pain.
- Synovial-fluid free concentration correlates with morning stiffness / Lee Index but not with pain (p. 280).
- No ke0, no MEAC, no potency ratio. Not an opioid.

## Covariates
Reviewed qualitatively: age, renal failure (lower binding), hepatic disease/cirrhosis (binding down 60%, intrinsic
clearance down), RA activity and hypoalbuminaemia, diabetic microangiopathy (30% lower absorption), probenecid,
cimetidine/ranitidine/famotidine (shorter t½), salicylates. No equations.

## Caveats
Review; tables poorly preserved in the text layer. Some contradictions on the dose at which nonlinearity begins.
