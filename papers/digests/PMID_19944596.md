# Kitzen 2010: vinorelbine PK with liver dysfunction

## Study
Open-label randomised crossover PK study in cancer patients (solid tumours; age 18-75, KPS >=70, creatinine clearance >60 mL/min or creatinine <=1.5 x ULN), by liver function cohort. Cohort I: bilirubin <1.5 x ULN with transaminases 1.5-2.5 x ULN; cohort II: bilirubin 1.5-3 x ULN; cohort III: bilirubin >3-10 x ULN (Table 1). 16 patients treated, 12 completed both routes, 35 cycles. IV vinorelbine 17, 21 or 25 mg/m2 (infused over 6-10 min in 50 mL saline) and oral capsule 40, 50 or 60 mg/m2 with food, one route on day 1 and the other on day 8; BSA capped at 2.0 m2. Blood samples (2.5 mL, heparinised) pre-dose, 10, 30 min, 1, 1.5, 3, 6, 9, 24, 48, 96, 168 h after IV start (oral: 15, 30, 45 min then same). Whole blood (blood concentrations; assay not described in this paper). Non-compartmental parameters; exploratory ANOVA only (p. 267). Cohort III enrolled one patient who left after the IV dose.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No effect, toxicity thresholds or concentrations related to response; safety was haematological and gastrointestinal toxicity, similar across cohorts and routes. Introduction cites a sharp oral therapeutic threshold between 60 and 80 mg/m2 (p. 266, from other studies). Not an opioid.

## Covariates
Liver function by bilirubin and AST/ALT cohort. Conclusion: IV CL and Vd were very similar for cohorts I and II (and to previously reported values for normal liver, refs 1, 9, 10); oral F was lower, not higher, in liver dysfunction; no dose change suggested for mild-moderate impairment. Cohort III (severe, bilirubin >3 x ULN) has n=1, so no conclusion for it.

## Caveats
Very small n (8 and 5-6 per route and cohort); only exploratory statistics; whole-blood, assay not described here; one cohort III patient (CL 11.7 L/h, t1/2 22.7 h, atypically low); F in cohort II rests on 3 patients; no healthy/normal-liver control group in the study; Table 1 versus Table 2 dose levels differ (25/20/15 vs 25/21/17 mg/m2).

## Verification against stanpumpR
Independent comparison with R/drugs_vinorelbine.R (Variol 2002 Table 3, whole blood ng/mL, so same matrix):
- CL: model 41.9 L/h (R/drugs_vinorelbine.R:58) vs Kitzen IV 43.2 (cohort I) and 40.3 L/h (cohort II): **matches** (within 4%). Units: both absolute L/h; model divides by 60 for L/min (:93).
- Volume: model Vss 1074 L (header :35, V1 22.2 + V2 136 + V3 916) vs Kitzen Vdz 2705/2854 L. Different quantities (terminal vs steady state); model terminal t1/2 36.9 h gives Vz = 41.9 x 36.9/ln2 = 2231 L (derived), within about 20% of Kitzen. **Not a discrepancy**; see [check] question.
- Terminal t1/2: model 36.9 h vs 44.6/51.2 h IV (cohorts I/II): model shorter by 20-30%; both are long-tail values sensitive to sampling to 168 h vs 120 h.
- Oral F: model 0.36 (:62) vs 24.7% and 31.6% in impaired liver: lower in Kitzen. The comparator is hepatic-dysfunction patients in an open-label study with no normal-liver arm; the paper itself cites an earlier F of 36-43% (p. 267). See [decision] question.
- Oral Tmax: Kitzen 2.08 and 1.39 h (text: peak 1.5-2 h); model ka 0.481/h with 0.15 h lag gives Tmax of roughly 2-3 h (not computed here). **Cannot verify** without running the model.
- Help page statement "liver impairment lowers clearance": the paper does not support that for mild-moderate (cohorts I-II: no change in IV CL or Vd); only the single cohort III patient had low CL. The statement is **not supported by this paper** for mild-moderate; severe impairment untested here (n=1).
