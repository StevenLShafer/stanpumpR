# Brstilo 2025: population PK of cannabidiol (oral) in children with DEE, food and formulation effects

## Study
- Two PK studies pooled (Hospital de Pediatria JP Garrahan, Buenos Aires), 2019-2023. Children 2-18 y with drug-resistant developmental and epileptic encephalopathy; 46 patients analysed (abstract says N = 48; text and Table 1 say 46). Table 1: age median 12.1 y (2.4-18.0); weight 32.5 kg (11.1-70.0); height 130.5 cm; 25 female, 21 male.
- Oral CBD twice daily, at least 21 days, stable dose over 1 week. Two formulations: CBD-enriched oil in palm oil (25 mg/mL CBD, THC up to 2 mg/mL; Rideau/Aphria) and purified CBD in sesame oil (100 mg/mL; Convupidiol). Median dose 10.2 mg/kg/day (2.5-20.5) purified, 11.1 mg/kg/day (5.8-25.0) enriched.
- Food-effect design: two morning visits on the same maintenance dose, fasted (water only, no food for 2 h) vs immediately after a normocaloric breakfast eaten within 30 min of the dose. Samples (2 mL) pre-dose and 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 h.
- 276 plasma concentrations (216 intensive, 60 trough-only). 39 intensive profiles: 11 enriched fasted, 16 purified fasted, 12 purified fed.
- Assay: UHPLC-MS/MS on plasma; linear 1-300 ng/mL, LOQ 0.9 ng/mL (page 4 image). Total CBD in plasma.
- Model: MonolixSuite 2024R1, SAEM; covariates by OFV (3.84 forward, 10 backward).

## Model and parameters
One compartment, transit-compartment absorption, first-order elimination, proportional error. Allometric exponents fixed (0.75 CL/F, 1 V/F), reference weight 32.5 kg. Parameters are apparent (divided by F); F is relative, purified CBD fasted = 1. Table 2 (PDF p6, printed 1148, read on the rendered page):

F = TPV x exp(beta_FED x FOOD) x exp(beta_ECBD x FORMULATION) (the typeset formula is garbled in the text layer; the footnote values confirm the product form). V/F = TPV x (BW/32.5)^1 x exp(beta_MALE x SEX). CL/F = TPV x (BW/32.5)^0.75. FOOD 0 fasted, 1 fed; FORMULATION 0 purified, 1 enriched; SEX 0 female, 1 male.

- Per kg (derived: footnote values / 32.5 kg): V/F 58.2 L/kg and CL/F 4.4 L/h/kg for purified fasted; the authors state the same in the Discussion.
- Simulated exposure (Table 3, median, dose-normalised): AUC0-12/D 88.1 (enriched fasted), 149.1 (purified fasted), 284.5 (purified fed) (ng.h/mL)/(mg/kg); Cmax/D 11.6, 28.6, 40.2 (ng/mL)/(mg/kg); Tmax about 2.0-2.3 h. Fed/fasted in 12 matched patients: Cmax 2.0-fold (0.8-4.7), AUC 1.9-fold (0.8-4.4) (Table 4).
- Not reported: tPeak/transit number (main text), IOV (not supported by data), exponents other than fixed ones.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No effect-site, ke0, hysteresis, EC50, minimum effective concentration or potency ratio; not an opioid. Concentration-response is not analysed (seizure outcome is not modelled).

## Covariates
Retained: food (F up 44.8%), formulation (enriched F down 49.3% vs purified), sex on V/F (male lower, beta -.39), body weight allometric. Univariate only, dropped in backward elimination: ketogenic diet, levothyroxine, sulthiame (higher F). Nasogastric/gastrostomy route: no difference in dose-normalised AUC (text p6 lists the fed figures as identical to the fasted ones, 206.8 vs 191.1, apparently a repeat).

## Caveats
Sparse elimination-phase data (6 h sampling) so one compartment only; the authors note CBD is likely multiphasic. Real-world cohort, polypharmacy (median 3 other ASMs), meals self-chosen and lower fat than FDA high-fat meals (food effect smaller than the 2-4x in adults). Pre-dose timing from parents' report. V/F and CL/F are 'apparent' and confounded with F by formulation and food. Authors' V/F and CL/F are lower than two earlier paediatric studies (80-650 L/kg, 10-15 L/h/kg).
