# Gin 1991: propofol at caesarean section and 2-3 days postpartum

## Study
Hong Kong (Prince of Wales Hospital). 8 healthy women with uncomplicated singleton pregnancies for elective
caesarean section, and 8 ASA I women 2-3 days postpartum for mini-laparotomy tubal ligation (PDF p1). Both:
ranitidine, sodium citrate, rapid-sequence induction with propofol 2 mg/kg over 20 s, suxamethonium 1.5 mg/kg,
atracurium 0.5 mg/kg. Caesarean: 50% N2O + 1% enflurane, changed at delivery to 70% N2O + 0.5% enflurane, oxytocin,
morphine 0.2 mg/kg IV. Postpartum: 70% N2O + 1% enflurane throughout, no opioids. Venous blood (2 mL) before and at
2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 20, 30, 60, 120, 240, 360, 480 and 600 min in both groups; maternal venous, umbilical venous and
arterial samples at delivery; neonatal samples at 30-240 min. Whole-blood HPLC with fluorescence (as in Gin 1990).
Non-compartmental analysis (statistical moments). Protein binding by equilibrium dialysis. Demographics, mean (SD)
(PDF p2): caesarean 27.4 (4.3) yr, 72.5 (9.1) kg; postpartum 30.8 (4.3) yr, 62.0 (11.8) kg (P < 0.05); anaesthesia
43.1 (6.2) vs 30.2 (8.7) min (P < 0.01). Height not reported. Mann-Whitney for derived variables.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None for the mother; no ke0. Neonates (37-39 weeks, 2.6-3.9 kg, induction-delivery 8.7-12.3 min): satisfactory
blood gases, Apgar and NACS (median 33 at 15 min); neonatal propofol exceeded maternal by 2 h and was 0.05-0.11
ug/mL at 4 h (abstract).

## Covariates
Group (caesarean vs postpartum) only.

## Caveats
- The comparator is not nonpregnant: postpartum women retain much of pregnancy's physiology.
- Comparison with the 1990 nonpregnant group (Discussion p4): 1990 nonpregnant mean 1.57 L/min was less than the
  1991 caesarean clearance (P < 0.05) but not significantly less than the 1991 postpartum clearance (P = 0.16); the
  authors say methodological differences between the studies make further conclusions difficult, and only suggest
  a larger trial "may show" postpartum kinetics closer to caesarean than to nonpregnant.
- Sampling to 600 min gives larger Vss and half-life than their 1990 study (authors' explanation).
- Small groups; medians and ranges; different anaesthetics between groups.
