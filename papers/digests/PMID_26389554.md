# Kharasch 2015: CYP2B6 genotype and methadone clearance

## Study

Single-centre, open-label, single-session genotype-cohort study in healthy volunteers
(NCT01648283; Methods, printed p. 1143 / PDF p. 2). 489 potential subjects were genotyped for
four SNPs (516G>T rs3745274, 785A>G rs2279343, 983T>C rs28399499, 1459C>T rs3211371) and
cohorts were assembled. 64 subjects studied (34 M : 30 F; 42 Caucasian, 10 African American,
10 Asian, 2 other/unknown), age 29 ± 8 yr, weight 74 ± 13 kg (Table 1, printed p. 1144 /
PDF p. 3; confirmed from the page image).

Cohorts (Table 1): *1/*1 n = 21 (age 28 ± 7, 72 ± 14 kg), *1/*6 n = 20 (28 ± 8, 78 ± 11 kg),
*6/*6 n = 17 (32 ± 9, 71 ± 13 kg), plus *1/*4 n = 1 (22 yr, 68 kg), *4/*6 n = 3 (29 ± 2,
68 ± 15 kg) and *5/*5 n = 2 (28 ± 1, 84 ± 0 kg). The one *1/*4 and three *4/*6 subjects are
reported together as "CYP2B6*4/X" (n = 4) in the figures.

Dosing (printed p. 1144 / PDF p. 3): simultaneous IV racemic unlabelled (d0)-methadone HCl
6.0 mg (5.4 mg free base) and oral deuterated (d5)-methadone HCl 11.0 mg (9.86 mg free base)
in water. Entry criteria 18-50 yr, within 30% of ideal body weight (BMI < 33), no hepatic or
renal disease, no CYP2B6-affecting drugs. Plasma sampled to 96 h, urine pooled at 24, 48, 72,
96 h. Chiral LC-MS/MS for methadone and EDDP enantiomers; interday CV 6-13% (plasma), 3-10%
(urine). Analysis was **noncompartmental** (Phoenix), assuming complete absorption; no
population or compartmental model and no PK parameter table are reported. Results are
arithmetic mean ± SD; one-way ANOVA with Student-Newman-Keuls across the three main cohorts;
the rare-variant groups were deliberately **not** tested formally.

Allele frequencies among the 489 genotyped (printed p. 1144 / PDF p. 3): *4 0.02, *5 0.07,
*6 0.22, *7 0.02, *9 0.002, *16 0.002, *18 0.016.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points

**None.** This is a disposition study. No pharmacodynamic end point was measured (no pupil,
EEG, ventilation, analgesia or sedation), so there is **no MEAC or minimum effective analgesic
concentration, no ke0 / t½ke0 / tPeak / hysteresis, and no equianalgesic or potency ratio** in
this paper. The only potency statement is qualitative, in the introduction (printed p. 1142 /
PDF p. 1): R-methadone primarily confers µ-opioid receptor activity, both enantiomers act at
NMDA receptors. The discussion (printed p. 1150 / PDF p. 9) adds, without numbers, that S-
methadone affects R-methadone's metabolism and that QTc is mostly an S-methadone concern.

## Covariates

CYP2B6 genotype is the only covariate analysed, as cohort membership rather than a fitted
equation: *1/*1, *1/*6, *6/*6, with *4 carriers and *5/*5 described but untested. Race
(Caucasian vs African American) is a secondary comparison (Table 2) and is attributed to the
genotype distribution — more *6 carriers and no *4 carriers among the African Americans; with
*4 carriers removed, EDDP formation clearance stayed significantly lower and R- but not
S-methadone apparent oral clearance remained lower (printed p. 1147 / PDF p. 6). The paper
maps genotype to phenotype only as "poor versus extensive metabolizer" (discussion, printed
p. 1148 / PDF p. 7): *6 = poor (516G>T plus 785A>G), *4 = an apparent extensive metaboliser
phenotype. No CPIC phenotype vocabulary, no activity score, no dosing equation. Weight, age
and sex were not analysed as covariates; clearances are already per kg.

## Caveats

Authors' limitations (printed p. 1151 / PDF p. 10): healthy volunteers and a **single dose**,
so steady-state metabolism and clearance were not assessed; because methadone causes about
twofold autoinduction of its own clearance and CYP2B6 is **less inducible in *6 carriers**,
genotype differences would be expected to be larger at steady state than measured here; R and S
methadone influence each other's metabolism, again more at steady state; the *4 and *5 groups
are very small and incidental, to be interpreted conservatively; and the absence of a
bioavailability difference in *6 carriers despite different oral clearances is unexplained.

Noticed here: hepatic clearance is reported only for *1/*1 and *6/*6, so any heterozygote
hepatic multiplier must be inferred; R-methadone systemic clearances are given only as box
plots; subjects were 18-50 yr and BMI < 33, so elderly, obese, hepatically or renally impaired
patients are extrapolation; doses were 6 mg IV / 11 mg oral HCl, far below therapeutic
maintenance; and the three measures of the genotype effect (abstract percentages, Results oral
clearances, Results systemic clearances) do not agree in magnitude — e.g. S *1/*6 oral CL/F
1.6/2.3 is 30% lower (derived) while the abstract says 35%, because the abstract averages
individual ratios rather than the ratio of means.
