# van den Berg 2017: Bayesian adaptation of propofol TCI

Printed pages 918-927 = PDF pages 1-10 (printed page = PDF page + 917).

## Study
- Double-blinded randomized trial, UMCG Groningen, NTR4518. 120 adults (60 intervention, 60 control) having elective off-pump coronary artery bypass; age 18-75, BMI 18-35, ASA I-III. Table 1 (PDF p6, printed 923): control age 62.0 (SD 7.4) yr, weight 91.5 (13.8) kg, height 178 (9) cm, BMI 27.6 (3.1), 54 M/6 F; intervention age 62.5 (7.5), weight 81.5 (12.7), height 178 (8), BMI 26.3 (3.1), 56 M/4 F (median with SD as printed; no group difference, P 0.157-0.827).
- Propofol by TCI (RUGLOOP II) using the Eleveld model, **patient version** (not the volunteer version); sufentanil TCI by the Gepts model; rocuronium. Targets chosen by the clinician for BIS 40-60.
- Arterial blood propofol measured at the bedside (Pelorus 1500, visible absorption spectrometry, whole blood; lower limit 0.25 ug/mL, bias 0.15 ug/mL; PDF p3). Samples at baseline, 10, 35, 50 min, then 5 and 15 min after the adjustment, then every 60 min and at end of infusion (870 observations).
- Intervention arm: Bayesian adjustment of volumes and clearances (algorithm in the online supplement, not read) at 60 min using the first samples; control arm kept population values. The pre-adjustment period is the first 60 min.
- Performance error PE = (measured - predicted)/predicted x 100%; MdPE (bias), MdAPE (inaccuracy), divergence (%/h), wobble (%). Pooled medians per group.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No ke0, tPeak, EC50 or MEAC reported. BIS (40-60 target), heart rate and invasive MAP did not differ between groups (Fig 5, p8; exception: a short BIS difference at induction). Not an opioid paper (sufentanil used as background, Gepts model; no opioid concentrations reported).

## Covariates
None estimated. Bayesian step let V1-V3, CL, Q2, Q3 vary per patient; Q3 and V1 changed most (Fig 3, p7), Q3 left unconstrained.

## Caveats
- Cardiac surgery, mostly men (110 of 120), one centre; propofol sampled as whole blood, model predicts plasma (the paper does not discuss the difference); venous/arterial site was arterial.
- The sample-size simulation assumed cardiac patients had 15% lower clearance (p3), an assumption not tested.
- The Table 2 comparison models were all run without Bayesian adjustment on the same cohort (all on sufentanil). The paper gives no reason for Marsh's very high MdPE (85%); it only reports it.
- Authors note the Bayesian result depends on the model's own variances, and device failure caused dropouts.
- The text layer's Table 2 row labels are offset against the numbers; the rendered page was used.
- Some figure values (Fig 4) are read from graphics with text labels; the abstract prints the rounded versions.
