# Emanuelsson 1997: dose linearity and variability of IV ropivacaine in healthy volunteers

## Study
- Double-blind, randomised, complete crossover (cyclic 3 x 3 Latin square), washout >= 1 week; Huddinge Hospital, Karolinska.
- 9 healthy males, 20-37 y, 65-104 kg; debrisoquine phenotyped (2 poor metabolisers, PK no different).
- Ropivacaine HCl 20, 40 and 80 mg IV as 30-min infusions (Methods; the Fig. 1 legend says 5 min, an internal inconsistency). Doses as base used for CL/Vss: 18, 35, 71 mg.
- Venous (antecubital, contralateral) samples to 8 h. Total ropivacaine base by GC-NPD (LOQ 0.01 mg/L); free by coupled-column LC after ultrafiltration (LOQ 0.003 mg/L); AAG by radioimmunodiffusion.
- Non-compartmental: CL = dose/AUC, Vss from AUMC with infusion-time correction, t1/2 by log-linear regression; ANOVA for crossover.

No compartmental model, no intercompartmental parameters. Variance split (Table 2, % of total, inter/intra): CL 93/7, CLu 60/40, Vss 60/40, Vss,u 81/19, t1/2 80/20.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- One subject (80 mg) had lower-lip numbness 2 min after the infusion ended: extrapolated total 1.7 mg/L, free 0.08 mg/L (venous). Authors cite a prior study where CNS symptoms appeared at free arterial concentrations above 0.34 mg/L (mean 0.6 mg/L).
- Not an opioid: no MEAC, ke0, tPeak or potency ratio.

## Covariates
None modelled. fu rose slightly with dose (4.8 to 5.5%), suggesting binding may become concentration-dependent above about 2 mg/L total. AAG 12-23 umol/L, stable.

## Caveats
- n = 9 young healthy men; venous sampling; 8-h sampling for a 1.7 h half-life.
- Infusion duration inconsistent between Methods (30 min) and Fig. 1 legend (5 min).
- Healthy-volunteer CL (400-500 mL/min IV) is higher than the apparent CL after epidural block (about 300 mL/min), per the discussion.
