# Nie 2025: population PK of epidural sufentanil in labouring women, with an umbilical-cord compartment

## Study
Secondary analysis of the sufentanil arm of a multicentre RCT of epidural nalbuphine vs sufentanil with ropivacaine
(ChiCTR1800018810), six Chinese centres, Nov 2018-Feb 2019. Inclusion: ASA II-III, 20-40 y, BMI 18.5-30, primiparas,
singleton, cervix 2 to <6 cm, VAS 50 mm or more. 90 recruited, 41 analysed: age 27.2 ± 2.9 y, weight 70.2 ± 7.5 kg
(PDF p4). Dosing (PDF p2, rendered): 5 mL test dose of ropivacaine 0.1% with sufentanil and 1:400,000 epinephrine,
10 mL bolus 5 min later, then PCEA 30 min later (6 mL/h infusion, 6 mL bolus, 15-min lockout, 30 mL/h maximum) until
delivery. The sufentanil concentration is 3 µg/mL in the Methods but 0.3 µg/mL in the abstract; the paper does not
resolve which. Samples: maternal venous at 0.5 h and 1 h after the loading dose, at delivery and 2 h after;
umbilical venous at cord clamping; 79 maternal and 37 cord samples. LC-MS/MS, LLOQ 1 pg/mL, CV < 15%. NONMEM 7.4.0,
FOCE-I, bootstrap 5000, pcVPC.

## Model and parameters
The epidural compartment could not be estimated and was merged with the central compartment (PDF p3), so the
"two-compartment" model is one maternal compartment (CL, V1) plus an umbilical-cord compartment (V2) linked by CL2
(K12 = CL2/V1, K21 = CL2/V2). No absorption or bioavailability term. IIV on CL and V1 only; proportional residual
error for each compartment. Table 1 (PDF p5, rendered), estimates (CV%):

Derived: k10 = 176/519 = 0.34/h (maternal half-life about 2.0 h); K21 = 0.0134/0.187 = 0.072/h (cord half-life about
9.7 h), which is the model's slow fall in cord concentration after the infusion stops. Observed concentrations are a
few to about 30 pg/mL maternal and about 0.5-15 pg/mL cord (Fig. 2, PDF p5). Protein binding 93% and octanol/buffer
coefficient 1737 are cited (PDF p8, ref 11).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No MEAC or minimum effective concentration, no ke0, t1/2ke0 or tPeak, no potency ratio. The only analgesic end point
is the VAS entry and exclusion rule (VAS 40 mm or less, or a 50% fall, at 30 min). Cord-to-maternal ratio of 0.81
cited from Loftus 1995 (ref 10), not measured here.

## Covariates
None tested or reported.

## Caveats
Sparse sampling (at most four maternal samples, one cord sample per woman); merged epidural compartment, so CL and V1
include absorption and bioavailability; ropivacaine and epinephrine co-administered; the 3 vs 0.3 µg/mL conflict; the
model's cord compartment is a statistical construct with one sample per subject. Authors list small dataset and
possible interactions (PDF p9).
