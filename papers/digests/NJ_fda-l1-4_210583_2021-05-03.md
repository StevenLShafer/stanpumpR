# ANJESO label 2021: intravenous meloxicam

## Study
Not a study: US prescribing information for ANJESO, a nanocrystal meloxicam injection, 30 mg once daily
by IV bolus over 15 s, for moderate-to-severe pain in adults. 12.3 summarises healthy-volunteer NCA
(Tables 4, 5) and population PK conclusions; n, assay and study identities are not given.

Linear PK over 15-180 mg IV; accumulation slightly more than 2-fold on repeat dosing without a change
in t½. Metabolism: CYP2C9 (minor CYP3A4) to 5'-hydroxymethyl (9% of dose) and 5'-carboxy (60%) meloxicam;
peroxidase metabolites 16% and 4%; all inactive. Excretion: metabolites equally in urine and feces;
unchanged 0.2% urine, 1.6% feces. Cholestyramine raised clearance 50% (t½ 19.2 to 12.5 h, AUC −35%),
indicating enteral recirculation. Population PK: age, sex and race not clinically meaningful. Mild renal
impairment (eGFR 60-90, elderly): Cmax +5%, AUC +7%. Moderate/severe renal impairment and severe hepatic
impairment not adequately studied. 12.5: AUC substantially higher in CYP2C9 poor metabolisers (*3/*3).
The label states no absolute oral bioavailability.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No concentration-effect relationship, no ke0, no analgesic concentration. Dose 30 mg once daily
(maximum 30 mg/day). QT: no effect at 30-180 mg IV (12.2).

## Covariates
Qualitative only: renal function (mild impairment small effect), CYP2C9 genotype, cholestyramine.

## Caveats
- NCA from unspecified healthy-volunteer studies; large SDs (AUC CV 32-53%).
- Tables 4 and 5 give different single-dose Cmax and AUC for the same 30 mg dose (different studies).
- Vz is a terminal-phase volume, not Vss.
