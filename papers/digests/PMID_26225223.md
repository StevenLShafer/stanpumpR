# Røge 2015: liraglutide PK and its effect in an integrated glucose-insulin model (T2DM)

## Study
- 29 adults with type 2 diabetes from two Novo Nordisk crossover trials (Results p1-2 / printed 28-29; Methods
  p8 / 35):
  - Trial 1 (Flint et al.): 18 recruited; liraglutide or placebo for 3 weeks each (3-4 week washout), weekly
    escalation 0.6 → 1.2 → 1.8 mg once daily (evening); a 2 MJ meal tolerance test (MTT) at each dose; liraglutide
    sampled sparsely (6, 8, 10, 12 h after dosing; 4 per subject per dose).
  - Trial 2 (Hermansen et al.): 18 completed (7 excluded from the meal-test end points); same escalation to
    1.8 mg; 4 MJ high-fat MTT at 1.8 mg; liraglutide sampled −15 min and 4-60 h after the last dose (12 samples).
- NONMEM; stepwise: liraglutide PK first, then fixed for the glucose-insulin (IGI) model. ΔOFV 10.83 criterion;
  log-additive residual error.

## Model and parameters
Liraglutide: one compartment, first-order absorption and elimination; IIV on CL and V; CL/F and V/F proportional to
body weight (p2 / 29). A zero-order then first-order absorption model (Watson's) was tried and did not improve the
fit. Bootstrap (100) SEs agreed with NONMEM's.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- Liraglutide multiplies insulin secretion by 1 + p_lira·C (linear; an Emax form was not better), p_lira
  0.0665 per nM.
- Gastric emptying (paracetamol): delay in trial 1 only, modelled as a lower glucose absorption rate with
  liraglutide (0.0151 vs 0.0278 /min); none in trial 2; no concentration dependence detectable.
- Endogenous glucose production: linear and Emax inhibition tested, not significant, not included.
- No ke0 or hysteresis term; the drug effect acts on concurrent concentration.

## Covariates
Body weight (proportional, on CL/F and V/F). No renal, age or sex covariates tested for liraglutide.

## Caveats
- Small (29), short (3-week) studies at 0.6-1.8 mg; trial 1 PK sampling sparse (6-12 h post dose).
- The authors note the VPC overestimates variability and a plateau near Cmax from pooling the two trials.
- ka < k (flip-flop), so the terminal slope is absorption; V/F and CL/F are apparent.
- Meal composition (fat, protein) not handled by the IGI model.
