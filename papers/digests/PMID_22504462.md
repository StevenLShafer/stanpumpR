# White 2012: morphine background infusion plus low-dose PCA bolus vs bolus-only PCA

## Study
- Randomized double-blind trial after open colorectal cancer surgery, 90 consented (100 invited), 86 completed (Fig 1 CONSORT: B0 41, BI 45; four B0 drop-outs). Age 26-95 y, 51 men (abstract). Standardized general anaesthesia (fentanyl 1.5 ug/kg induction, isoflurane/N2O). Table 1 (PDF p. 4, printed 188; header says 45 patients per group, the sex counts 23/18 sum to 41 for B0): age 62 +/- 13 vs 65 +/- 10 y; weight 76 +/- 13 vs 71 +/- 11 kg (P=0.078); surgery 216 +/- 64 vs 198 +/- 68 min; intraoperative fentanyl 428 +/- 204 vs 397 +/- 162 ug.
- Every patient: titrated loading morphine 0.1 mg/kg IV over 15 min in PACU, then PCA, diclofenac 75 mg IM twice daily as scheduled rescue, 48 h of PCA (p. 2-3, printed 186-187).
- B0: 1.5 mg morphine bolus, 7 min lockout. BI: 0.01 mg/kg bolus (about 0.7 mg at 70 kg) plus continuous 0.01 mg/kg/h (about 0.7 mg/h at 70 kg), same 7 min lockout. Bolus ratio about 1:2.
- Pain NRS (0-10) at deep breath twice daily and at 6 h; PCA "effective applications" were the efficacy measure.

## Model and parameters
No pharmacokinetic model or parameter. Dose regimens only.

Dose facts (all printed pp. 186-189): loading 0.1 mg/kg; BI infusion 0.01 mg/kg/h; BI bolus 0.01 mg/kg; B0 bolus 1.5 mg (about 0.02 mg/kg at 70-75 kg); lockout 7 min.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- No MEAC, minimum effective or analgesic concentration; no ke0, t1/2 ke0, tPeak or hysteresis; no equianalgesic/potency ratio; no assay. The authors call 0.7 mg/h "sub-analgesic" and say this is not a recognized pharmacological term (Discussion, printed p. 190).
- Results: bolus morphine by PCA 16.8 +/- 11.7 mg (BI) vs 50.9 +/- 26.2 mg (B0) (Fig 4, P=0.034 in the caption); total morphine including infusion about 25% lower in BI after weight adjustment. Effective PCA use 19% less in BI (Table 2: 0-24 h 0.92 +/- 1.6 vs 1.07 +/- 1.24 per h; 24-48 h 0.52 +/- 0.98 vs 0.83 +/- 1.1). PACU cumulative pain 3.41 vs 5.72; 48 h cumulative pain 1.40 vs 4.07; satisfaction 6.95 vs 4.69 (Table 2, P < 0.0001 for the last two).
- Safety: no hypoxaemia, deep sedation or ICU admission; PONV needing treatment 4 BI vs 3 B0.
- Cites Lotsch 2006 for a mean 0.17 +/- 0.10 mg/kg morphine at unacceptable pain relief (reference 16), a dose, not a concentration.

## Covariates
Doses are per kg in the BI arm (0.01 mg/kg bolus, 0.01 mg/kg/h); B0 bolus is fixed at 1.5 mg. Age and weight were compared across groups only.

## Caveats
Open label comparison of two dose structures, not an infusion-alone arm, so the effect of the infusion cannot be separated from the halved bolus; scheduled diclofenac in both arms; Table 1 header and CONSORT disagree on group size (45 vs 41 for B0); PCA use stands in for pain as the primary efficacy measure; single centre, single operation.
