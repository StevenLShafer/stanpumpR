# Wagner 1987: nicardipine, IV tracer and steady-state oral pharmacokinetics

## Study
- Six healthy male non-smokers, 24-33 years (printed 134). **Body weights are not reported.**
- Oral nicardipine hydrochloride capsules 10, 20, 30, 40 mg every 8 h for 3 days each, ascending; 24-h profile after the 10th dose on day 4 of each regimen (extended to 72 h after the last 40 mg dose).
- **Intravenous tracer**: 0.885 mg (31.6 uCi) 14C-nicardipine HCl in 2 mL over 2 min, given *together with the oral dose on day 4 of the 30 mg q8h regimen*, i.e. at oral steady state (printed 134).
- Assays: nicardipine and its pyridine metabolite M5 by capillary GC-ECD (LLOQ 1 ng/mL); the IV radiolabelled drug separated by HPLC and counted by LSC (LLOQ 0.2 ng/mL) (printed 135). All concentrations and doses are expressed **as the hydrochloride**. Plasma (venous).
- Blood/plasma partition measured in vitro (r, Table 6).
- Analysis: IV data fitted to a biexponential; Michaelis-Menten fit of the oral steady-state data by Wilkinson's method.

## Model and parameters
IV model (Scheme I, PDF p4): **two compartments with elimination from the PERIPHERAL compartment** (Rowland's model), dose into V1, kel out of compartment 2. Equations 3-5 give k12, kel, k21 for that structure; CLs = D/AUC, V1 = D/(C1 + C2), Vbeta = CLs/l1 (l1 is the SMALLER exponent here, so l1 is the terminal and l2 the distribution exponent).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Not an opioid. Blood pressure 1 h after the morning dose on days 1 and 4 in normotensive subjects; Figure 3 (PDF p14, printed 146) gives partial correlations of the fall in BP with log Cp: supine diastolic -0.79 (p < 0.01), erect diastolic -0.88 (p < 0.01), supine systolic -0.26 (p 0.28; the text layer reads -0.28, the page image -0.26), erect systolic -0.45 (p 0.05). No ke0, tPeak, EC50 or concentration-effect model.

## Covariates
None modelled. Dose rate (saturable first pass): oral clearance falls linearly with dose rate (equation 17, CLpo = 125 - 2.39 Ro, mL/kg/min).

## Caveats
- Six young men; weights not given (per-kg values cannot be converted back to the doses' absolute scale except by inference).
- The IV tracer was given at oral steady state (oral-dose nicardipine about 5.7-75 ng/mL over the 0-8 h interval at 30 mg t.i.d., Table 2), so the IV clearance was measured during concurrent drug exposure; the paper attributes the non-linearity to first pass, not systemic clearance.
- IV sampling was effectively limited to 8 h by tracer sensitivity; the oral terminal half-life (about 9-11 h) shows a slow phase the IV biexponential misses, so the IV clearance (AUC extrapolated on l1) may be overstated and the volumes understated.
- Peripheral-elimination parameterization: its k12, k21, kel cannot be dropped into a central-elimination model; only the biexponential (or the derived equivalents) carry over.
- Internal inconsistencies in Table 4 (open_questions).
