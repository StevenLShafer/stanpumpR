# Mazer 2005: Androderm patch vs AndroGel 1% gel, steady-state PK in hypogonadal men

## Study
- Randomized, open-label, two-way crossover; no washout (authors assumed residual skin testosterone gone by day 7
  of the next period). Single centre (PDF p 2-3, printed 214-215).
- n = 28 hypogonadal men (morning screening TT <= 300 ng/dL, one exception at 305); age 52.4 (SD 12.2) y,
  range 28.2-71.1; weight 94.0 (11.3) kg, range 72.0-114.2; BMI 30.2 (3.8) kg/m2 (Table 2, PDF p 5).
  25/28 (89.3%) hypogonadotropic (LH <= 9 IU/L); diabetes in 16 (57.1%), pituitary disease in 6 (21.4%).
- Treatments, 14 days each: Androderm patch 5 mg/day nominal delivery (24.3 mg total T content) applied about 2200 h;
  AndroGel 1% 5 g/day (nominal delivery 5 mg/day; 50 mg T applied) applied about 0800 h. Application sites rotated by
  a fixed schedule (Table 1, PDF p 3); abdomen on the PK days 7 and 14. Subjects showered <= 10 min before each
  application.
- Sampling on days 7 and 14 at 0, 1, 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 18, 24 h after application; 24-h baseline before treatment
  (0800, 2000, 0800 h).
- Assay: serum TT, DHT, E2, SHBG by immunoradiometric assay (Esoterix); LH by ICMA (screen only); free T
  *calculated* (Vermeulen method), not measured. Cavg = AUC(0-24)/24 by trapezoid. Bioequivalence by Liu's ANOVA
  for repeated crossover on log data. No compartmental or population model.

## Model and parameters
No structural PK model, clearance or volume. Non-compartmental steady-state exposure only.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Not an opioid paper; no PD model, no effect-site parameters, no therapeutic concentration beyond the reference
ranges the authors adopt: TT 306-1031 ng/dL (Meikle 1996), cFT assumed equal to equilibrium-dialysis free T
52-280 pg/mL (Esoterix), DHT 28-85 ng/dL, E2 0.9-3.6 ng/dL (PDF p 3). Skin tolerability and patch adhesion only
as clinical outcomes.

## Covariates
None modelled. Discussion attributes lower patch TT than in earlier studies to older, more obese subjects and the
abdominal site (PDF p 11), without analysis.

## Caveats
- Sponsor-run (Watson, maker of Androderm); first author employed by Watson.
- Free T is calculated, not measured; SHBG for intermediate time points is the mean of 0 and 24 h, albumin from the
  screen sample.
- No washout between periods; endogenous baseline not subtracted from Table 4 values (BLavg about 212 ng/dL), so
  Cavg mixes endogenous and exogenous T.
- Gel exposure here is lower than in the earlier Swerdloff study the authors cite (gel Cavg 565.8 ng/dL there);
  they suggest daily showering before gel application may explain it. "Lower Cmin for the patch" is against the gel
  in the same men, not against an absolute reference.
- Apparent misprints, not used by stanpumpR: Table 2 screening TT range "1.5-305.0" ng/dL (PDF p 5; 1.5 is
  implausible for a screening value); baseline DHT/TT SD printed "0.084 (0.61)" (PDF p 7).
- PubMed metadata in the context file is empty; the PMID-to-paper link rests on the archive file name and on
  `docs/library-expansion/downloads.md:106`, which lists PMID 16422889 as the testosterone patch/gel comparison.
