# Inderal tablets FDA label, 2011 (NDA 016418)

## Study
Not a study: the FDA-archived label for Inderal immediate-release tablets (propranolol hydrochloride 10-80 mg; PDF p1,
p16-17). The Drugs@FDA file name bundles three application numbers (016418, 016762, 017683), but the document's content
is the tablet label only; no injection dosing is given (the only IV dose is glucagon in Overdosage, PDF p15).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No concentration-effect relationship or target range. Clinical-effect summaries as in the generic label (BHAT post-MI,
migraine, tremor, etc.; PDF p7-8). Not an opioid.

## Covariates
Elderly half-life 11 vs 5 h; renal failure Cmax 161 +/- 41 vs 26 +/- 1 ng/mL after 40 mg; cirrhosis (7 vs 9, 80 mg q8h)
unbound steady state 3-fold higher, half-life 11 vs 4 h; African-American R(+)/S(-) clearance about 76%/53% higher;
Chinese unbound fraction 18-45% higher (PDF p3-4).

Dosing (PDF p15-16): hypertension 40 mg bid, usual 120-240 mg/day, up to 640 mg/day; angina 80-320 mg/day; AF 10-30 mg
tid-qid; post-MI 180-240 mg/day; migraine 160-240 mg/day; tremor 120 mg/day (240-320 occasionally); HSS 20-40 mg tid-qid;
pheochromocytoma 60 mg/day preoperatively.

## Caveats
- 2011 label; the page header warns it may not be the latest. No PK table. Values are rounded label statements.
