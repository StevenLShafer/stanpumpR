# LaPierre 2011: remifentanil-propofol response surfaces for esophageal instrumentation and ventilatory depression

## Study
Open-label, randomized, parallel-group crisscross design (Short 2002) in 24 healthy volunteers (12 M, 12 F; ASA I-II, nonsmokers, BMI 18-28; mean±SD height 174±8 cm, weight 71±12 kg, BMI 23±3, age 25±4 y; PDF p4 / printed 492). Each got 3 of 6 sets of escalating target effect-site concentrations (Ce) while the other drug was held constant; target Ce ranged 0-6.4 ng/mL remifentanil and 0-4.3 ug/mL propofol (Appendix 1, PDF p9 / printed 498; 61 possible pairs). Delivery: STANPUMP with Minto 1997 remifentanil and Schnider 1999 propofol PK; measurements began 5 min after the predicted Ce reached target. Spontaneously breathing on facemask O2.
End points: loss of response to esophageal instrumentation (LREI; 42F bougie to 40 cm; no gag, movement, or HR/BP change >20%); loss of responsiveness (LOR; OAA/S = 1); intolerable ventilatory depression (IVD; respiratory rate <=4/min in 1 min, by capnography).

## Model and parameters
Greco response-surface model adjusted for categorical data (Eq 1), naive pooled fit by -2LL, bootstrap CVs (1000 resamples), chi-square goodness of fit. Concentrations are STANPUMP-predicted Ce (ng/mL remifentanil, ug/mL propofol), not measured. The IVD model used 407 points: this study plus 31 earlier unpublished points from Kern 2004 (Appendix 2). LOR is the earlier Johnson 2008 model, not refit here.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Opioid audit (remifentanil): no MEAC or analgesic concentration; no ke0, t1/2 ke0, tPeak or hysteresis reported (Ce comes from Minto 1997 inside STANPUMP; its ke0 is not printed). No equianalgesic or potency ratio. The pharmacodynamic C50s are in Table 2 above (end points LREI and IVD; LOR from Johnson 2008). They are on predicted Ce, not plasma or whole blood, in healthy volunteers.
Take-home (Discussion, PDF p8 / printed 497): about 2 ng/mL remifentanil with 2 ug/mL propofol gives about 50% probability of LREI, LOR and IVD; 1 + 1 gives about 5%; >3 + >3 about 95%. The isoboles for all three overlap: no pair had >95% LREI with <5% IVD and LOR. Synergy is shown by positive alpha for LREI and IVD.
Cited comparators (other populations, plasma concentrations, looser response criteria): Kazama 2000 propofol C50 for EI 2.8 ug/mL (gag reflex 3.0); Drover 2004 in children 3.7 ug/mL.
Observed (abstract, PDF p1): 105 of 367 EI assessments unresponsive; 9 assessments had no IVD and no LOR.

## Covariates
None modeled (no age, sex or weight terms).

## Caveats
Healthy young volunteers; strict LREI definition (any movement or HR/BP change counts). The IVD propofol C50 (7.0) and the LOR remifentanil C50 (33.1) lie outside the tested range, so the authors say the magnitude of alpha should not be interpreted. Ce are model-predicted with no concentration assay. IVD was assessed unstimulated; respiratory rate ignores tidal volume and CO2. 31 of the IVD points were pooled from a prior study.
