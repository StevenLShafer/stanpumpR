# Hornuss 2012: expiratory propofol time course after a bolus (IMR-MS) vs BIS

## Study
Observational, single-centre (Munich), 21 adults ASA I-III, routine gynaecological or spinal surgery. Table 1 (PDF p 2, printed 556), mean±SD: age 54±13 y, height 170±6 cm, weight 74±17 kg, BMI 27±6 kg/m2, male/female 4/17. Premedication midazolam 7.5 mg orally. Induction: propofol 2.5 mg/kg (Propofol-Lipuro 1%) over 10 s. After loss of consciousness, remifentanil TCI at 6 ng/mL or sufentanil 20 mcg bolus; cisatracurium 0.1 mg/kg in the 9 who were intubated. Breath propofol measured by ion molecule reaction mass spectrometry (side-stream 50 mL/min, 200 ms per analysis, expiratory CO2 plateau only, propofol fragment mass 163 D used). Calibration gas 35±7 ppb propofol in nitrogen. BIS recorded every 10 s (smoothed over 15 s). Data for the first 600 s. No blood propofol was measured.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point is BIS (processed EEG) against expired propofol (ppb), pooled data of 18 patients, 30-s averages. Sigmoid Emax fit with E0 fixed at 99 (Results, Fig 3): Emax 10.33±8.41 (SE), Cexp50 3.49±0.57 ppb (SE), gamma 1.30±0.23 (SE), R 0.786, R2 0.62. The Emax is the BIS at maximal effect. This is a breath-concentration vs BIS relation, not a plasma or effect-site one; no ke0, t1/2 ke0 or tPeak was estimated (the authors say a PK/PD model is needed). Time to lowest BIS (219±62 s) is an observed time on a 2.5 mg/kg bolus in midazolam-premedicated patients receiving opioid after loss of consciousness, not a model tPeak. Not an opioid paper: no MEAC, ke0 or potency ratio for remifentanil or sufentanil (they were co-administered only). Discussion cites Takita (2.0 mg/kg bolus, already anaesthetised, intubated): detection 41.8±8.0 s, time to peak 333.8±70.0 s, peak 3-10 ppb.

## Covariates
Age, weight, lean body weight, height and sex showed no significant correlation with time to detection, time to peak or peak concentration (Results).

## Caveats
Breath propofol was not calibrated against plasma; midazolam premedication and later opioids confound BIS; BIS sampled at 10 s; time to peak read by eye; n reduced by exclusions; sponsor-supplied instrument and a co-author employed by the manufacturer. Gas-phase ppb has no stated conversion to plasma mcg/mL.
