# Salcedo-Mingoarranz 2023: digoxin in patients over 90

PDF page n = printed page 1724 + n.

## Study
Retrospective TDM, Severo Ochoa University Hospital (Spain), 2000-2018. 326 patients aged over 90 (73.9% women):
modelling 231 (57 men, 174 women), 441 concentrations; external validation 95 (192 concentrations). Table 1,
modelling group (mean ± SD (range)): age 93.1 ± 2.8 (90.0-102.4) y; TBW 61.1 ± 13.6 (40-100) kg; height 158 ± 8 cm;
SCr 1.09 ± 0.45 (0.43-3.81) mg/dL; Cockcroft-Gault 37.1 ± 16.0 (11.3-115.6) mL/min (TBW used); K 4.3 ± 0.6 mEq/L;
dose 138 ± 47 (50-250) mcg/day; SDC 1.35 ± 0.69 ng/mL; loop diuretics 83.5%, PPIs 67.5%, NSAIDs 29.9%,
amiodarone 2.2%, CCB 10.8%. Oral tablets 0.25 mg, oral solution 0.05 mg/mL and IV; about 70% of samples troughs.
Two immunoassays (turbidimetric 2000-2010, ECLIA 2011-2018), cross-validated. NONMEM 7.4, FOCE+I, PsN.
Covariate forward dOFV > 3.84, backward > 10.83. Abstract's overall means (age 93.2, SCr 1.11 mg/dL, TBW 61.2 kg)
are for all 326.

## Model and parameters
One compartment, first-order absorption (ADVAN2 TRANS2); a 2-compartment model gave Vp 0.002 L and was rejected.
ka and F were estimated in the base model, then fixed (Table 2). Final (Table 3, PDF p9 rendered):
CL/F (L/h) = 4.55 (CG/36.4)^0.468 x 0.830^LD x 1.21^SEX (SEX 1 = male); Vd/F (L) = 355 (K/4.3)^-0.849.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. Target 0.8-1.2 ng/mL (Vamos meta-analysis), about 1 ng/mL, up to 1.2 accepted.

## Covariates
Cockcroft-Gault (TBW), loop diuretic, sex on CL/F; serum potassium on Vd/F.

## Caveats
LD and SEX were retained although their dOFVs (-6.655, -8.288) are below the stated backward-elimination threshold
of 10.83. Section 3.5 says the syrup has F "around 0.893", which Table 3 assigns to tablets (syrup 0.977).
Retrospective, mixed routes and assays, ka/F fixed from the base model, women predominate.
