# Simons 1989: hydroxyzine PK and wheal/flare PD in the elderly

## Study
Nine healthy elderly subjects (7 women, 2 men), age 69.5 +/- 3.7 y, weight 70.0 +/- 9.6 kg, normal hepatic and renal function, within 10% of ideal weight, non-smokers, no antihistamine history. Single oral hydroxyzine HCl syrup (Atarax) 0.7 mg/kg (mean dose 49.0 +/- 6.7 mg), fasted, 250 mL water. Serum sampled before and at 1-6, 8, 10, 12 h, then 24, 48, 72, 96, 120, 144 h. HPLC (UV 229 nm); LLOQ 1.0 ng/mL hydroxyzine, 2.0 ng/mL cetirizine; CV 10.6 +/- 3.7%. PK by noncompartmental methods (PKCALC), compared with the authors' earlier young-adult and paediatric studies. At each sample time, 0.01 mL of 0.1 mg/mL histamine phosphate was injected intradermally; wheal and flare areas traced at 10 min.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Wheal and flare suppression by histamine skin test; no concentration-effect model, no ke0, EC50, t1/2ke0, tPeak or hysteresis analysis. Wheal area significantly suppressed vs predose from 1 to 144 h (p < 0.01); maximum wheal suppression 4-10 h (74.9-80.4%); flare suppressed 1-144 h, maximum 2-72 h (73.6-83.0%) (PDF p. 5, Figure 2). Suppression persisted at 144 h when hydroxyzine was below 1 ng/mL in 7 of 9 subjects and mean cetirizine 2.5 +/- 0.7 ng/mL, so effect outlasts serum concentration. At 48 h: hydroxyzine 9.2 +/- 9.0, cetirizine 72.3 +/- 26.4 ng/mL. Comparisons quoted from other studies: young adults 36 h after 39.5 mg (hydroxyzine 9.6 ng/mL, suppression significant, gone by 60 h at 5.6 ng/mL) (ref 13). Sedation: 8 of 9 fell asleep 2-6 h post dose. No MEAC. Not an opioid.

## Covariates
None analysed within the sample (n = 9). Age comparison against the authors' earlier young-adult series (ref 13): t1/2 29.3 vs 20.0 +/- 4.1 h; Varea/F 22.5 vs 16.0 +/- 3.0 L/kg (significantly larger, p < 0.05); CL/F 9.6 vs 9.78 +/- 3.25 mL/min/kg (not different). The authors attribute the longer t1/2 to the larger volume.

## Caveats
Small n; syrup oral only so CL and V are apparent; no IV reference; cross-study comparison with historical young adults rather than a concurrent control; cetirizine reported only as formed from hydroxyzine (no dose basis for its own CL or V); 7 women and 2 men.
