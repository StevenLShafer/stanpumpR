# Glen 1998: how the Diprifusor propofol TCI system and its Marsh model were chosen

## Study
Narrative account (Zeneca) of the Diprifusor development programme:
- Model selection in two stages by simulation (program EXPLICIT) of the infusion scheme of Servin et al.: 10 healthy adults, 21 mg/kg/h x 5 min, 12 mg/kg/h x 10 min, 6 mg/kg/h thereafter; frequent arterial whole-blood propofol samples, including at eye opening.
- Stage 1: two-compartment and bolus-derived models (e.g. Adam) underpredicted much more than three-compartment infusion-derived models.
- Stage 2: Tackley, Marsh and Dyck & Shafer models compared by Varvel performance error, PE = (Cm - Ccalc)/Ccalc x 100.
- Clinical programme: 428 patients in 8 studies to support the Diprivan data sheet.
- Bench delivery testing (Graseby 3400, Ohmeda 9000) and a delivery specification.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- Clinical guidance from the trial programme: targets about 4-8 mcg/mL for induction and 3-6 mcg/mL for maintenance in healthy adults (blood, Diprifusor-calculated).
- Fig. 1 (from Vuyk): with the same target, Adam and Gepts models gave blood 3.9 (0.7) and 2.6 (0.4) mcg/mL, predicted to produce loss of consciousness in 78% and 8%.
- No ke0 or effect-site targeting is described (targets are blood concentrations). Not an opioid: no MEAC or potency ratio.

## Covariates
Marsh: weight-proportional V1 (body weight range accepted 30-150 kg in delivery tests). No age term discussed. Paediatric use would need an amended model.

## Caveats
Company review; model choice made by simulating one 10-patient data set; Marsh chosen partly for accumulated prototype experience; Diprifusor underpredicts measured concentrations by about 16%.
