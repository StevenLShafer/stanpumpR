# van der Stoep 2019: treosulfan population PK in paediatric HSCT

## Study
Prospective observational multicentre study (Leiden, Rome), June 2011 to March 2017. 91 paediatric patients (58 male); 7 had a second transplant and are counted twice as separate individuals. Median age 4.3 y (0.1-18.2), median weight 15.6 kg (3.8-75.0), 33 (36%) aged <=2 y (Table 1, PDF p5). IV treosulfan, 3-h infusion on 3 consecutive days: 14 g/m2/day (total 42 g/m2) in patients >1 y (73 patients, 80.2%), 10 or 12 g/m2/day in patients <1 y. Serum sampled on day 1 (some also day 3) at 1.5, 3.5, 4, 5, 7, 9 h after start of infusion (rich) or 4 and 7 h (sparse); 410 samples, 4 (1%) below LLOQ (6.8 mg/L) kept as measured. HPLC-UV assay (serum, total drug). NONMEM 7.3.0, log-normal IIV, interoccasion variability on Cl (24 patients), proportional residual error. Median AUC0-inf 1658 mg*h/L (range 643-3371) (Table 1).

## Model and parameters
Two-compartment, first-order elimination from central compartment (dOFV -127.78 vs 1-compartment). Allometric scaling to 70 kg, Fsize = (BW/70)^a with a fixed at 0.75 for Cl and Q and 1 for V1 and V2 (rendered PDF p3, Eq 4 and text). Maturation of Cl and Q: Fmat = 1/(1+(PMA/TM50)^-Hill), PMA = postnatal age + 40 weeks (Eq 5). Cltot = Clpop x Fsize x Fmat (Eq 6). Cl, Q in L/h, V in L; the values are standardised to 70 kg (Table 2 footnote a).

Final model (Table 2, PDF p6): Cl 18.8 L/h (RSE 7%), V1 20.2 L (18%), Q 21.3 L/h (31%), V2 16.8 L (16%), Hill 1.2 (34%), TM50 38 weeks PMA (19%). IIV (CV%): Cl 31.8, V1 45.9, V2 17.3, Q 41.4. IOV Cl 13.9%. Proportional error 12.3%. Bootstrap medians (Table 2): Cl 19.4, V1 19.8, Q 22.0, V2 16.8, Hill 1.1, TM50 43. Base (unscaled) model: Cl 5.94 L/h, V1 0.77 L, Q 24.6 L/h, V2 8.73 L. Table 2 V1 row label prints "V1 (L/)".

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD model. Target exposure for dosing table: AUC0-inf 1650 mg*h/L (median of cohort at 14 g/m2/day); the authors cite their earlier work that exposure >1650 mg*h/L raised risk of grade >=2 mucositis and skin toxicity (p. 2041). Not an opioid; no ke0, MEAC or potency ratio.

## Covariates
Body weight (allometric) and PMA (sigmoid maturation) on Cl and Q. Clearance reaches 50% of adult at 38 weeks PMA and about 90% at about 4 years (text p. 2036, Fig 2). eGFR (revised Schwartz, capped 120 mL/min/1.73 m2) lowered OFV by 16.72 but was left out because the VPC worsened and IIV rose. Sex, disease, conditioning regimen, Hb, Hct, albumin not significant.

## Caveats
RSE of IIV on V2 and Q is 201% and 153% when scaled to 70 kg (the authors say the RSE fell to 53%/46% when normalised to 15.6 kg). Only 5 patients with eGFR <60. Parent drug only; the active epoxide metabolites were not modelled. Co-administered fludarabine/thiotepa levels not available. Sparse and rich data mixed.
