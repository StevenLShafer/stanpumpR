# Zhang 2025: remdesivir and metabolite PK simulation, renal impairment

## Study
In-silico modelling, no new subjects. Data were digitized (Engauge Digitizer 12.1) mean concentration-time profiles from two published sources: (1) a Gilead phase I parallel-group study (Zhang et al. 2020 in the reference list as cited, i.e. the renal-impairment trial) with healthy matched controls and mild (n=12), moderate (n=11), severe (n=10) renal impairment and kidney failure (n=6 dialysis, n=4 non-dialysis); single IV doses of 100 mg (mild/moderate), 40 mg (severe or predialysis kidney failure), 20 mg (postdialysis or non-dialysis kidney failure) (p3, p5); (2) a case report (Sörgel 2021) of one man in his mid-70s, eGFR 0, on renal replacement therapy, 200 mg day 1 then 100 mg daily; only day 1 was used (p3, p6). Only means, no individual data. Monolix, SAEM, lognormal random effects on all parameters, combined error model; renal impairment a 0/1 categorical covariate (p5).

## Model and parameters
Six-ODE model: remdesivir (RDV), intermediate metabolite GS-704277 (IM) and nucleoside monophosphate (NUC), each with a central and a peripheral compartment; RDV is eliminated from the central compartment; RDV to IM and IM to NUC conversion occurs in both compartments; NUC converts to NTP (GS-443902) in the peripheral compartment (Fig 2, Eqs 1-6, p3-4). Linear kinetics assumed. No volumes are reported. Table 1 (PDF p4, read on image) gives point values for three columns, all "/h":

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No ke0, EC50, MEAC or effect measure; the paper is PK only (not an opioid paper).

## Covariates
Renal impairment (0 = control, 1 = impaired, degrees not distinguished) was assigned to KC,IM, KP,IM and KP,NUC (p6). The authors nevertheless state that RDV and metabolite PK show no significant difference across degrees of renal impairment including dialysis (p7).

## Caveats
Authors: linear kinetics assumed; model more complex than a 2-compartment one, chosen to reproduce the biphasic decay of RDV and IM (Fig 6); no NTP data to validate; hepatic impairment not covered. Mean digitized data only, so population variability is not identified; goodness-of-fit is shown with 90% intervals (Fig 4, 5). The eGFR 0 column derives from one patient. No volumes, doses-to-concentration scaling or units for concentrations are given in the article.
