# Bartels 2013: Population PK of glycopyrronium, systemic and lung

## Study
Novartis healthy-volunteer data. Model building: Study 1 Part 2 only - **i.v. glycopyrronium 120 ug as a 5-min infusion (n = 20)**, and inhaled 200 ug via Breezhaler with (n = 17) and without (n = 18) oral activated charcoal; plasma to 72 h. Validation: Study 1 Part 1 (oral 400 ug, n = 8) and Study 2 (inhaled 50 ug once daily for 14 days, n = 24). All doses and concentrations refer to the **active glycopyrronium moiety (cation)**, not the bromide salt. (The text layer prints "mg" for these doses; the plasma concentrations of hundreds to thousands of pg/mL and the Breezhaler 50 ug product show they are micrograms.) No covariates considered. NONMEM VI, FOCE-I; log-transformed data, additive error.

## Model and parameters

Observed i.v. terminal t1/2 6.2 h (NCA); inhaled 52.5-57.2 h (absorption-limited). Disposition half-times from the Table 2 parameters (derived): 0.093, 0.81, 7.2 h.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None modelled. No ke0, no heart-rate or secretion model. The paper attributes once-daily bronchodilation to the slow lung absorption.

## Covariates
None.

## Caveats
Healthy volunteers; a single small i.v. dose (120 ug); four-compartment model rejected on stability; the i.v. arm was below LLOQ after 24 h, so the slow systemic tail is defined by few points. Industry authors.
