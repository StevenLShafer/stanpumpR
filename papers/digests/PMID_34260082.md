# Tikiso 2022: abacavir PK in African children (pooled)

## Study
- Pooled analysis of four studies (ARROW, CHAPAS-3, DNDi, MATCH): 230 HIV-infected African children, 2760 plasma concentrations (285 = 10.3% below LLOQ). Median (range) age 2.1 (0.1-12.8) y, weight 9.8 (2.5-30.0) kg; 115 malnourished (weight-for-age and height-for-age Z < -2). 154 on lopinavir/ritonavir (LPV/r), 76 on efavirenz; 104 on rifampicin-based TB therapy (101 with super-boosted LPV/r 4:4, 3 with efavirenz). 227 sampled on twice-daily dosing, 41 of them also once daily, 3 once daily only.
- Dosing by WHO weight bands, target 8 mg/kg twice daily or 16 mg/kg once daily. Oral liquid or abacavir/lamivudine fixed-dose tablet.
- Assay: LC-MS/MS (UCT; LLOQ 0.0243 ug/mL DNDi, 0.0238 ug/mL CHAPAS and MATCH); ARROW by HPLC/MS (GSK; 0.0243 ug/mL). Plasma, total drug. BLQ by Beal M6. NONMEM 7.4.4, FOCE-I; SIR for uncertainty.
- Simulation: 57,014 virtual children 3-35.9 kg, 8 mg/kg q12h, AUC0-12 against an adult median of 6.02 mg h/L (EMA).

## Model and parameters
Two-compartment, first-order elimination, transit-compartment absorption. Allometric weight scaling (exponent 3/4 for CL and intercompartmental CL, 1 for volumes) on total body weight (fat-free mass no better). Maturation of CL as a sigmoid of postmenstrual age (PMA), assuming 9 months gestation: PMA^g / (PMA50^g + PMA^g). Typical values refer to a 9.8 kg child on standard LPV/r (4:1) at steady state (Table 4, PDF p.9, printed p.411; confirmed on rendered page). CL and volumes are apparent (F fixed to 1).

Allometrically scaled CL/F of 46.8 L/h per 70 kg is listed for this pooled model in Table 3 (read from the garbled text layer, not confirmed on an image); (derived: 10.7 x (70/9.8)^0.75 = 47). Key effects (text pp.5-6): rifampicin + super-boosted LPV/r lowers abacavir AUC 29.4%; malnutrition raises AUC more than 4-fold at start of rehabilitation, about 2-fold after 14 days; first dose exposure about 22% higher (text p.7; derived 1/0.816 = 1.225).

Simulated AUC0-12 at 8 mg/kg (Fig. 4, p.11): above the 6.02 mg h/L adult median with LPV/r 4:1 or efavirenz, within the adult range with rifampicin + super-boosted LPV/r; higher at 3-4.9 kg (immature CL), higher at 25-35.9 kg, low at 7-10 kg. Numerical AUC values are in the figure only.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD end point. Exposure is compared to the adult AUC0-12 target of 6.02 mg h/L (EMA). No MEAC, ke0, tPeak or potency ratio. (Not an opioid.)

## Covariates
Retained: total body weight (allometric), PMA (maturation), efavirenz (CL +12%), rifampicin + super-boosted LPV/r (F -29.4%), malnutrition with exponential waning (F and CL), fixed-dose tablet (slower absorption), first dose (CL), evening-dose absorption delay. Extra variability in MATCH (BOV on F 1.39-fold, BVV on CL 3.35-fold).

## Caveats
- Few children under 3 months, so the neonatal maturation is largely extrapolation (authors' statement).
- Only three children on efavirenz with rifampicin, so that combination was not confirmed.
- Pooling of heterogeneous studies; first-dose data only in malnourished children; liquid mostly in younger children and with LPV/r; two laboratories.
- The reference group for comparative words ("12% higher clearance with efavirenz", "first dose lower") is children on standard LPV/r at steady state, not abacavir alone. The authors contrast their first-dose result with Waters 2007 (adults, abacavir alone versus with LPV/r, about 30% lower exposure).
- Gestation of 9 months assumed for all children.
