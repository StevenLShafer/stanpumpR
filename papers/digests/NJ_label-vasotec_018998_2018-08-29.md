# VASOTEC (enalapril maleate) tablets, US label (Valeant, FDA 2018-08-29)

## Study
Not a study: the brand enalapril maleate tablet label. Enalapril maleate MW 492.53; a prodrug hydrolysed to enalaprilat
(PDF p1).

Prolonged terminal phase attributed to a small, saturable ACE-bound fraction (amount bound does not rise with dose;
PDF p2). Renal impairment: disposition unchanged until GFR <= 30 mL/min, then higher peak and trough, later Tmax,
prolonged effective half-life (PDF p2). No metabolites other than enalaprilat.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- Single oral dose: antihypertensive onset at 1 h, peak BP reduction at 4-6 h, effect maintained 24 h at recommended
  doses (PDF p2-3). Smaller response in black patients (PDF p2).
- CONSENSUS survival 74% vs 56% at 6 months, 64% vs 48% at 1 year (n = 127 vs 126; PDF p4); SOLVD-Treatment 11% lower
  all-cause mortality (PDF p3).
- No concentration-effect model, no ACE-inhibition parameters, no therapeutic range. Not an opioid.

Dosing (PDF p16-17): hypertension 5 mg once daily (2.5 mg with diuretics), usual 10-40 mg/day in one or two doses;
CrCl <= 30 mL/min (serum creatinine >= 3 mg/dL) start 2.5 mg, dialysis 2.5 mg on dialysis days; heart failure 2.5 mg,
2.5-20 mg twice daily, maximum 40 mg/day; pediatric 0.08 mg/kg (to 5 mg) once daily, above 0.58 mg/kg or 40 mg not
studied, not recommended in neonates or GFR < 30 mL/min/1.73 m2 (PDF p17).

## Covariates
Renal function (dose table, PDF p16); age (pediatric PK consistent with adults, PDF p4). Pregnancy: boxed warning
(PDF p1) and Warnings, Fetal Toxicity (PDF p7): RAS drugs in the second and third trimesters reduce fetal renal function
and raise fetal and neonatal morbidity and death; discontinue when pregnancy is detected. Enalapril and enalaprilat are
detected in human breast milk (PDF p11).

## Caveats
- No PK study sizes for the adult statements; the 11 h is an accumulation half-life after oral dosing, not a terminal or
  intravenous elimination half-life.
- The last page carries a placeholder revision date ("Rev. XX/201X", PDF p18); identity rests on the FDA file name
  (018998s083) and date.

## Verification against stanpumpR
- Boxed warning for fetal toxicity: **matches** (PDF p1; Warnings, Fetal Toxicity, PDF p7).
- "PREGNANCY CONTRAINDICATION": **discrepancy (wording)**. This label's Contraindications section (PDF p5) lists
  hypersensitivity, history of ACE-inhibitor-related or hereditary/idiopathic angioedema, aliskiren in diabetes, and
  neprilysin inhibitors; pregnancy is not among them. The label handles pregnancy through the boxed warning and
  "discontinue as soon as possible", and allows continued use in the unusual case with no alternative (PDF p7).
  "Strongly warned against / boxed warning" would match the label; "contraindication" does not.
- "No pregnancy pharmacokinetic study exists": the label says nothing about pregnancy PK studies; this is the repo's
  inference, **cannot verify** from this source.
- Corroborating, not cited at these lines: the registry's enalapril label record (`antihypertensiveRegistry.csv:43`)
  quotes Tmax about 1 h, accumulation half-life about 11 h and BP peak 4-6 h, all of which this label states (PDF p2-3).
