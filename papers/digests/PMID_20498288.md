# Kaneda 2011: population PK/PD of a brief etomidate infusion (BIS, OAA/S)

## Study
20 ASA I-II volunteers (18-55 y, 50-80 kg) recruited; 2 excluded for moderate/severe myoclonus; 18 analysed
(Table II: age 38.9 ± 8.5 y, 10 M / 8 F, weight 63.6 ± 8.2 kg, height 166.8 ± 5.8 cm, ASA I/II 11/7). Etomidate
0.2% (Etomidate Lipuro) at 300 mg/h (5 mg/min) until loss of eyelash reflex, then spontaneous recovery. Induction
dose 24.0 ± 10.8 mg (0.38 ± 0.17 mg/kg); time to LOC 227 ± 81 s; whole study 503 ± 143 s. Arterial plasma, BIS
and OAA/S every minute until response to verbal command. HPLC (diode array). NONMEM VI, FOCE-I, ADVAN6;
sequential PK then PD; 1000-sample bootstrap. Data collected at Hallym University, Korea.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End points BIS and modified OAA/S (effect = 100 − BIS and 5 − OAA/S). t½ke0 1.55 min (ke0 0.447/min), estimated on
plasma with a two-compartment PK model; EC50 0.526 µg/mL (BIS) and 0.554 µg/mL (OAA/S), effect-site. No
"therapeutic range", no awakening concentration and no 0.4-0.8 µg/mL band are reported. Figure 3 simulates the
curves over 0.05-2.00 µg/mL. Authors restrict use to infusions under 10 min.

## Covariates
None retained.

## Caveats
Slow infusion and 1-min sampling leave V1 poorly defined and CL1 poorly estimated (SE 88.9%; CI nearly to zero);
short sampling (about 8 min) so elimination not captured (authors). Two myoclonic subjects excluded. Healthy young
adults. Plasma not whole blood.

## Verification against stanpumpR
- typical 0.5 µg/mL (R/drugs_etomidate.R:15): consistent with the EC50s 0.526 (BIS) and 0.554 (OAA/S) µg/mL.
  **matches** (approximately; an EC50, not a "typical" level).
- Band 0.4-0.8 µg/mL (R/drugs_etomidate.R:16-17; help :15; CSV Lower 0.4 Upper 0.8): the paper reports no such
  range. It might be read off Figure 3, but it is not stated. **cannot verify**.
- R/drugs_etomidate.R:16-17 names are inverted: `upperTypical <- 0.4`, `lowerTypical <- 0.8`. Harmless at run time, because
  R/getDrugPK.R:1007-1008 overwrites both from the CSV (Lower 0.4, Upper 0.8), but the function's own values are
  swapped. **discrepancy** (labelling, cosmetic).
- The citation in the code has no author or title; it should name Kaneda K et al.
- Not used, but relevant: Kaneda's t½ke0 1.55 min is a t½ke0 (two-compartment plasma PK), not a tPeak; it agrees
  with Arden's 1.6 min t½ke0 and does not support the code's tPeak = 1.6 min (see PMID_3729056 digest).
  Their Figure 4 shows near-identical Cp to Arden's model (STANPUMP) for 10 min after 0.3 mg/kg.
