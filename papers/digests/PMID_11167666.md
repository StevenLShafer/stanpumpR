# Huitema 2001: thioTEPA and TEPA population PK, high-dose regimen

## Study
- 40 patients (37 F, 3 M; breast, ovarian, germ-cell cancer), 65 courses of high-dose CTC or tCTC (cyclophosphamide + carboplatin + thioTEPA) with progenitor-cell rescue (Table 1, PDF p5). TEPA data from 30 patients, 50 courses.
- thioTEPA 60 mg/m2 as twice-daily 30 min infusions on 4 days (CTC; total 480 mg/m2), tCTC two thirds of this (total 320 mg/m2); one course 240 mg/m2.
- Median (range): age 46 y (16-58), BSA 1.79 m2, weight 68 kg (52-90), height 170 cm, CLcr (Cockcroft-Gault) 102 mL/min, alkaline phosphatase 57 U/L, albumin 42 g/L, total protein 61 g/L (Table 1).
- Plasma, GC assay; LLQ 0.026 uM thioTEPA, 0.029 uM TEPA. 1115 thioTEPA and 915 TEPA concentrations. Samples to 660 min on one day plus predose samples.
- NONMEM V, FO, ADVAN6. Concentrations in uM, doses in umol, time in h. Two-stage covariate search; IIV and interoccasion variability (IOV) proportional.

## Model and parameters
Two-compartment thioTEPA (CL, V1, k12, k21) with first-order conversion to TEPA (fraction fm) and a two-compartment TEPA model. V3 (TEPA volume) was fixed to 1, so fm is reported as fm* = fm/V3 (L^-1). Dose and concentration in molar units, so volumes are for molar dosing as given.

Basic model (Table 2, PDF p6): CL 34.5 L/h, V1 45.8 L, k12 0.273, k21 0.459 (IIV 47%), fm* 0.0298 L^-1, k 0.584, k34 2.79, k43 0.855 (all /h), prop error thioTEPA 27.7%, additive 0.0631 uM, prop error TEPA 20.9%.
Derived by the authors: thioTEPA distribution t1/2 0.6 h, elimination t1/2 2.4 h; population TEPA AUC / thioTEPA AUC ratio 1.76 (p66). AUC(thioTEPA) = dose/CL; AUC(TEPA) = dose x fm*/k.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No effect measure, ke0, MEAC or potency ratio. The introduction cites Przepiorka's finding that summed AUC and TEPA peaks above 10 uM relate to non-haematological toxicity (cited, not measured here). Not an opioid paper.

## Covariates
Final covariates (Table 3/4): CL = th1 + th9 x (AP - 57) + th10 x (ALB - 43); V1 = th2 + th11 x (TP - 61); k = th6 + th12 x (WGT - 68). Linear, centred at medians. Tested and dropped: age, BSA, height, creatinine, CLcr, ALT, AST, LDH, bilirubin, GGT. The text calls the V1 covariate "HGT" at one point (p66) and total protein elsewhere; Tables 3 and 4 use total protein.

## Caveats
- Selected young, mostly female cohort with normal renal and hepatic function; two dose levels only, so nonlinearity cannot be excluded.
- First-order estimation method (FO).
- Co-medications (cyclophosphamide, carboplatin, azoles) not modelled as interactions.
- The TEPA volume is not identifiable; fm* cannot be converted to a fraction without it.
