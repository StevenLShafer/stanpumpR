# Stratton 1992: time-kill of the Bacteroides fragilis group by beta-lactam/inhibitor combinations, cefoxitin and metronidazole

## Study
In-vitro. MIC by NCCLS anaerobe broth microdilution (0.125-256 µg/mL, 10^5 CFU/well, 48 h anaerobic, 35 °C)
for 300 consecutive beta-lactamase-producing B. fragilis group isolates (B. fragilis 181, B. thetaiotaomicron
53, B. ovatus 34, B. distasonis 17, B. vulgatus 14, B. uniformis 1) (PDF p2). Time-kill: **26 isolates only**,
two cohorts of 12 clinical isolates chosen by cefoxitin MIC (≤ 16 vs ≥ 32 µg/mL; each 2 B. fragilis,
5 B. thetaiotaomicron, 2 B. distasonis, 3 B. ovatus) plus 2 ATCC strains (PDF p2; Table 2 footnote a, PDF p9).
Time-kill in 30 mL BACTEC 7D anaerobic bottles, inoculum about 10^6 CFU/mL (Table 2 title: 5 x 10^6), sampled
0, 4, 12, 24 h; killing = ≥ 3 log10 (99.9%) fall at 24 h; carryover checked (PDF p3).

## Model and parameters
No pharmacokinetics.

Metronidazole, 300 isolates (Table 1, PDF p4, page image): range 0.5-1, mode 1, **MIC50 1, MIC90 2 µg/mL**,
0% resistant (breakpoint ≥ 32). (The range 0.5-1 and an MIC90 of 2 are inconsistent as printed.)

Metronidazole time-kill, 26 isolates, cumulative number (%) with ≥ 99.9% kill at 24 h (Table 2, PDF p9,
page image): **1 µg/mL 8 (31%); 2 µg/mL 23 (88%); 4 µg/mL 26 (100%)**; higher not done.
(derived: MBC50 2, MBC90 4 mg/L in this 26-isolate set.)

Comparators at 24 h, all 26 killed by (concentration of the beta-lactam): ampicillin-sulbactam 16,
amoxicillin-clavulanate 16, cefoperazone-sulbactam 16, piperacillin-tazobactam 64, ticarcillin-clavulanate
128; cefoxitin killed only 19/26 even at 256 (Table 2; Table 2 labels the first combination
"Amoxicillin-sulbactam" but footnote c says ampicillin).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: ≥ 99.9% reduction of the inoculum at 24 h in time-kill (bactericidal), and visible growth (MIC).
Metronidazole was the most rapidly bactericidal agent and equally active against cefoxitin-susceptible and
-resistant isolates (PDF p4, p8); regrowth at lower concentrations was common for beta-lactams, survivors kept
their MIC within one dilution (PDF p5). No PK/PD index or clinical target. Not an opioid paper.

## Covariates
Cefoxitin susceptibility cohort; species.

## Caveats
- The time-kill set is 26 selected isolates (half cefoxitin-resistant), not the 300 MIC isolates, and only
  2 of 24 clinical ones are B. fragilis proper.
- High inoculum (about 1.5 x 10^8 total CFU per bottle, PDF p7) in enriched broth; concentrations tested in
  doubling steps, so MBCs are on the dilution scale.
- MIC range in Table 1 (0.5-1) conflicts with its MIC90 (2).

## Verification against stanpumpR
- Quote and MBC90 (`R/antibioticMbc.R:335, 343-344`): MBC90 = 4 mg/L, "measured" — **matches** Table 2
  (26/26 killed at 4, 23/26 = 88% at 2, so 4 is also the measured 90th percentile). The abstract wording
  quoted in the repo is accurate (PDF p1).
- Note text (`R/antibioticMbc.R:337-338`) says the MBC90 is the concentration that killed every one of
  **300 isolates**: **discrepancy**. 300 isolates had MICs measured; killing was measured in 26 (24 clinical,
  2 ATCC; Table 2 footnote a). Should read 26.
- MBC50 (`R/antibioticMbc.R:335`, 1 mg/L = Boiten 2024 MIC50 0.5 x 2): not cited to this paper, but this
  paper's direct measurement puts 1 mg/L at 31% killed and 2 mg/L at 88%, so its measured MBC50 is 2. The
  repo value is one dilution lower; see open question. The plotted band (drugDefaults_global.csv:56, Lower 1,
  Typical 4.2, Upper 4.2) carries the 1.
- Independent MIC check: this paper's MIC50/MIC90 1/2 is one dilution above the repo's 0.5/1 (Boiten 2024),
  different era and method; not a discrepancy, but it runs in the same direction as the MBC50 point.
- The repo's "MBC is the MIC to four times it" (line 337) is consistent: MIC50 1 vs measured MBC50 2, MIC90 2
  vs MBC90 4.
- Free vs total: broth values, free drug, as the repo assumes.
- Verdict: **discrepancy** (isolate count in the Note); MBC90 matches.
