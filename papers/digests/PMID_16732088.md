# Sebel 2006: propofol plus sevoflurane at the GABA-A receptor are additive

## Study
In-vitro. Human alpha1-beta2-gamma2s GABA-A receptors transiently expressed in HEK-293 cells; whole-cell patch clamp at -60 mV, room temperature (21-24 C), fast solution exchanger. GABA 0.3-1000 uM (8 concentrations), propofol 0-10 uM, sevoflurane 0-1650 uM, 33 anaesthetic combinations (PDF p2-4, printed p1177-1179). Clinical reference concentrations used: 1 MAC sevoflurane taken as 330 uM aqueous; propofol anaesthetic EC50 taken as 2 uM (printed p1177). Losses in the perfusion system 5-10% (gas chromatography, ref 22). The measured quantity is the fractional shift of the GABA EC50: 1 - EC50'/EC50 (printed p1178). Concentrations were nominal (not re-assayed in this study). Surface fitted with the Minto 2000 response-surface model (MATLAB nlinfit, verified in NONMEM by Shafer, acknowledgements).

## Model and parameters
Single-agent concentration-effect (Hill) on the fractional EC50 shift (printed p1178-1179, text and Tables 1-2):

- Sevoflurane: C50 306 +/- 15 uM, slope 1.00 +/- 0.04. Fractional effect at 330 uM 0.54 +/- 0.06.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: potentiation of GABA-A receptor currents (fractional reduction of the GABA EC50), not a clinical end point; no ke0, tPeak, hysteresis, MEAC or equianalgesic ratio (not an opioid paper).
- Propofol and sevoflurane have additive potentiation on this receptor; the authors link this to a companion clinical study (Harris et al., ref 25) showing additivity for loss of consciousness and movement to incision, and to separate binding sites (propofol beta subunit, sevoflurane alpha subunit).
- Propofol 2 uM reduced GABA EC50 by a factor 0.59 (fractional effect 0.41); sevoflurane 330 uM by a factor 0.46 (0.54).
- Propofol alone (not sevoflurane) and the combination also caused a small decrease in peak currents at 100-1000 uM GABA; sevoflurane alone did not.

## Covariates
None (cell line, single receptor subtype alpha1-beta2-gamma2s, about 40% of CNS GABA-A receptors per the authors).

## Caveats
- Recombinant human receptors in HEK cells at room temperature, nominal bath concentrations, EC50 shift as the end point; 29 of 33 combinations differed from control (P < 0.05).
- The b2U and b2g standard errors (0.49, 0.47) are about twice the estimates, so additivity is "no detectable interaction", not proof of exact additivity.
- Aqueous concentration of 1 MAC sevoflurane (330 uM) and propofol EC50 (2 uM) are the authors' reference values, not measured here.
