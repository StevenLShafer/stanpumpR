# Reimers 2018: zonisamide serum concentrations during pregnancy

## Study
Retrospective multicentre case series, "six hospitals in Norway and Denmark" (abstract); the Methods (p.2) list
seven sites, five Norwegian (St. Olavs 10 pregnancies, National Center for Epilepsy 2, Ullevål 1, Drammen 1,
Stavanger 2; the National Center and Ullevål are both Oslo University Hospital) and two Danish (Copenhagen 3,
Aarhus 4). Pregnancies September 2005 to September 2017, data recorded prospectively to the EURAP protocol. 23 pregnancies in 15 women (5 with two, 2 with three
pregnancies), age at conception 27.9 ± 4.7 y; 6 pregnancies on zonisamide (ZNS) monotherapy, 17 with other AEDs
(8 + levetiracetam, 2 + lamotrigine, 3 + valproate, 2 + lamotrigine + clobazam, 2 + carbamazepine + clobazam)
(Table 1, p.2, rendered). 144 serum concentrations, drug-fasting morning samples, mostly monthly; steady state
ascertained for 138 and probable for 6. Assays: routine in-house LC-MS, LC-MS/MS or HPLC-UV at each site.
Concentration in µmol/L; C/D ratio = serum concentration (µmol/L) / total daily dose (mg). Reference: the latest
pre-conception value within 12 months (post-partum value > 4 weeks and < 12 months in two cases). One clearly
non-adherent pregnancy excluded from the concentration analysis; GM1 omitted (3 samples). Repeated-measures ANOVA
with mean imputation and Bonferroni correction. Maternal weight not recorded. Trimesters defined as weeks 1-14,
15-28, 29-delivery from the last menstrual period.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Seizure frequency recorded trimester-wise (not analysed statistically): increases in 2 pregnancies in the first
trimester, 6 in the second and 6 in the third; at those times concentrations were about 66% (56-77%) and 65%
(45-95%) of pre-pregnancy values (p.3, rendered). Breakthrough seizures in 4 of 10 previously seizure-free
pregnancies. The Discussion (p.4) gives a 35-36% C/D decline when seizures increased. No therapeutic range is
defined in this paper. Dose adjustments 21 times in 10 pregnancies (19 increases).

## Covariates
None modelled. Carbamazepine (an inducer) in 2 pregnancies of one woman, stable before pregnancy; its own
concentrations fell (48 to 29 and 42 to 28 µmol/L).

## Caveats
Retrospective; small n per month (10-19); several assays; adherence not controlled; maternal weight missing; mostly
polytherapy. The authors attribute the fall to raised CYP3A4 activity, raised renal excretion (renal clearance about
30% of total) and possibly reduced absorption, so the C/D fall is not purely clearance. Obstetric outcomes: mean
gestation 278 ± 7.6 days, birthweight 3500 ± 491 g (n = 20), head circumference 35.5 ± 1.5 cm (n = 14); two
malformations, both on polytherapy.
