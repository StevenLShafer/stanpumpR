# Shuster 2014: glyburide disposition across gestation in pregnant mice

## Study
Animal study (no human subjects). Wild-type FVB mice, 7-10 weeks old; nonpregnant (called gd 0) and pregnant on
gestation days 7.5, 10, 15 and 19. Glyburide **20 µg per mouse** (fixed, not per kg; the text layer drops the µ
and reads "20 mg", the rendered PDF p5 Fig. 1 legend reads µg) by retro-orbital injection under isoflurane,
i.e. intravenous. Sparse destructive sampling: maternal plasma by cardiac puncture at 0.5, 5, 10, 20, 40, 60, 120,
180, 240 min, n = 3-5 mice per time point, about 30 mice per gestation day (PDF p2-3, printed p426-427). Whole
fetuses on gd 15 and 19. HPLC-MS (single quadrupole); LLOQ in maternal plasma 10 ng/mL glyburide and 1.2 ng/mL for
metabolites M1-M3 (PDF p3). Plasma protein binding by ultrafiltration of [3H]glyburide. Liver microsomes (n = 5-6
per gestation day, from undosed mice) for substrate-depletion intrinsic clearance.

Analysis: pooled-data bootstrap (Mager and Göller 1998), 10,000 resamples, biexponential fit
C = A·e^(−αt) + B·e^(−βt) with A + B ≤ 12,500 ng/mL; permutation tests versus gd 0 (PDF p4, printed p428).
Metabolites and fetal AUCs by noncompartmental trapezoidal AUC.

with 95% CI. All values are for mice; volumes in **mL**, clearances in **mL/min** (absolute, per mouse) or
mL/min/g.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No glucose or insulin end point, no effect compartment; concentrations only. The authors note the
pharmacodynamics and therapeutic window of glyburide are not well characterised (PDF p8, printed p432).

## Covariates
Gestational age (categorical, gd 0/7.5/10/15/19). Body weight used only to normalise CL and V.

## Caveats
- Mice, IV dosing: the clearance is systemic, not the oral CL/F measured in women.
- Pooled sparse sampling (one sample per animal); CIs from bootstrap.
- A fixed 20 µg dose per mouse whatever the body weight; per-gram clearance rose only about 1.5-1.7-fold, the
  absolute clearance 2.2-2.3-fold, because pregnant mice weighed 32-34 g against 24 g.
- Mouse fu is about 2%, not the ~1% reported in women; the rise is attributed to hepatic CYP3A induction
  (microsomal CLint doubled), and mouse metabolism is mainly Cyp3a whereas in women CYP2C9 also contributes.
- The MBRT unit is printed as min⁻¹ in Table 1; the values (50-59) only make sense in minutes.
