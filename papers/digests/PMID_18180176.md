# Westin 2008: levetiracetam concentration/dose ratio in pregnancy

## Study
Retrospective review of 21 consecutive pregnancies in 20 women with epilepsy taking levetiracetam (two Norwegian
clinics in EURAP); 19 completed, 2 first-trimester miscarriages. Mean age at delivery 29 y (21-38). Monotherapy
in 5 pregnancies, polytherapy in 16 (carbamazepine, oxcarbazepine, valproate, lamotrigine, topiramate,
gabapentin, pregabalin; Table 1, PDF pp. 3-4 / printed 194-195). Levetiracetam doses 500-3500 mg/day. Serum
drawn drug-fasting, 10-14 h after the last dose. LC-MS; calibrated 5-500 umol/L, LOQ 5 umol/L, between-day RSD
< 16.9% at 25 umol/L and 7.7% at 250 umol/L (p. 2 / 193). Weight not reported.

Outcome: C/D ratio = serum concentration (umol/L) / total daily dose (mg), not weight-adjusted. Baseline = last
sample before pregnancy, or if missing a sample 2 or 4 weeks after delivery (p. 2).

## Model and parameters
No model. C/D ratio, (umol/L)/(mg/day), all samples (Table 2, PDF p. 5 / 196; confirmed on the page image):

| | Prior | T1 | T2 | T3 | 3-5 d pp | 2 wk pp | 4 wk pp |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mean | 0.043 | 0.026 | 0.022 | 0.021 | 0.029 | 0.031 | 0.040 |
| SD | 0.022 | 0.008 | 0.005 | 0.009 | 0.009 | 0.005 | 0.018 |

Paired analyses (p. 4 / 195): 7 women with all trimesters: baseline 0.036 ± 0.015, T3 0.022 ± 0.010 (p = 0.005);
T1 and T2 lower but not significant. 11 women with baseline and T3 (Figure 2): baseline 0.042 ± 0.018, T3
0.021 ± 0.009 (p < 0.001), i.e. 50% (abstract). Postpartum vs T3 (independent t-test): 2 wk 0.031 ± 0.005 (n = 8,
p = 0.02), 4 wk 0.040 ± 0.019 (n = 9, p = 0.01), 3-5 d p = 0.06.

Derived checks (mine): from Table 2 the 11 women of Figure 2 (patients 3, 5, 7, 8, 9, 11, 12b, 13, 14, 16, 20,

umol/L per mg/day = ~7.2 ug/L per mg/day at trough.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PK/PD analysis, no ke0, no therapeutic concentration. Seizure frequency rose in 7 of 19 completed pregnancies
(5 in T3), fell in 5; one woman had convulsive (T2) and non-convulsive (T3) status epilepticus. The fall in C/D was
not larger in women with more seizures (p. 6 / 197).

## Covariates
None modelled. Polytherapy with enzyme inducers and dose changes of co-medication are present but not analysed.

## Caveats
Small and retrospective; incomplete sampling (only 7 complete sets). C/D is not weight-adjusted and uses troughs
10-14 h post dose. Baseline is partly 2-4 weeks postpartum (patients 5, 8, 20), which may not yet be fully
nonpregnant. Mostly polytherapy. Pronounced interindividual variability. Text misprints: 3-5 day mean given as
"0.29" in the text (Table 2: 0.029); 4-week SD 0.019 in text vs 0.018 in Table 2. The text layer renders umol/L
and uM as "mmol/L" and "mM"; the page image shows micromoles.
