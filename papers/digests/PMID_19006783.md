# Allegaert 2009: cefazolin in maternal plasma and amniotic fluid

## Study
- Pooled population PK (NONMEM V, FOCE-I, ADVAN6), three sources (p. 2-3, printed 170.e2-e3):
  1. New data, Leuven: 49 women having in utero surgery (fetoscopic laser for twin-to-twin transfusion 24, tracheal occlusion or its reversal for diaphragmatic hernia 19, shunts 6, amniodrainage 2); cefazolin 2 g IV over a median 0.5 h (0.08-1), 1-2 h before surgery, then every 8 h for 2 days. Median GA 25 (17-34) weeks, weight 72 (54-95) kg, height 165 (152-180) cm, creatinine 0.62 (0.37-0.9) mg/dL, albumin 36.6 (33.9-39.3) g/L (Table 1). 153 plasma samples (41 of 49 interventions) and 66 amniotic fluid samples (47 of 49).
  2. Brown 1990: 7 women, 10 fetal transfusions for Rh isoimmunisation, 2 g IV bolus, mean GA 27 (SD 3) weeks, weight 72.9 (12.1) kg.
  3. Mitchell 2001: 26 term elective caesareans, 1 g IV bolus.
- Total: 82 pregnancies, 17-40 weeks, 187 plasma and 96 amniotic fluid samples. HPLC after solid-phase extraction, linear 0.1-100 ug/mL, LLOQ 0.1 ug/mL (total concentrations; no free drug measured).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD measured. Amniotic-fluid exposure against an MIC: the authors take the coagulase-negative staphylococcal MIC90 as 0.5-4 mg/L and note 20 of 96 amniotic observations below 0.5 mg/L (Comment, p. 6, printed 170.e6). In the visual predictive check, 95% of women without polyhydramnios exceed 0.5 mg/L in amniotic fluid 2 h after 2 g IV (Fig. 3). Simulated alternatives: a single 6 g dose (Fig. 4) or 4 g twice 8 h apart in polyhydramnios (Fig. 5), not evaluated clinically.

## Covariates
Gestational age on Teq only (Teq about 20 h at 24 weeks, 4.4 h at term; Fig. 2), polyhydramnios on Teq (x 9.09, no GA relation within that group). No gestational-age trend in CL or volume was found (Comment, p. 5). Weight, creatinine and albumin were collected (Table 1) but the paper reports no covariate model on CL or V.

## Caveats
- There is no non-pregnant arm: the statement that clearance and volume are raised rests on comparing 7.44 L/h with 3.6 L/h in healthy young adults from an older study (Rattie and Ravin 1975) and 12.04 L with 6.94 L in non-pregnant adults (Comment, p. 5; ref. 16 and unreferenced). The authors note their CL matches Philipson's 7.3 L/h in pregnancy.
- The authors judge the size of the clearance rise too large to be explained by the rise in GFR alone, and suggest altered binding or tubular function contribute (Comment, p. 5).
- A population undergoing fetal surgery, many with polyhydramnios; the term data are a published caesarean study with sparse sampling (mean 1.85 h after dose). Total concentrations only, although cefazolin binding is saturable (Introduction).
- V1 has 46.7% PPV and a wide CI; Teq CI 1.3-16.7 h.
