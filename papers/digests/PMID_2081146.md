# Rocha 1990: IV nicardipine kinetics and haemodynamics after chronic oral propranolol

## Study
- Open, parallel two-group study (no randomisation stated) in 16 patients with coronary artery disease undergoing
  diagnostic catheterisation; no heart failure, AV block, bradycardia < 50/min or hypertension (p1, printed 1525).
- Group N (n 8): no beta-blocker for at least 1 week. Group P + N (n 8): oral propranolol 160-480 mg/day for at
  least 1 week, last dose 2 h before study; other drugs stopped 48 h before (PDF p2).
- Table 1 (PDF p2, mean ± SD): age 51 ± 9 vs 55 ± 10 y; weight 78 ± 14 vs 74 ± 13 kg; BSA 1.88 vs 1.84 m2.
  Baseline HR 68 vs 60/min (p < 0.05), CI 3.14 vs 2.33 L/min/m2 (p < 0.01), SVR 1,347 vs 1,990 dyn·s·cm-5
  (p < 0.01). Sex not reported.
- Dose: nicardipine 5 mg in 20 mL 5% dextrose IV over 5 minutes (salt form not stated).
- Sampling: plasma at end of infusion and at 10, 15, 20, 45, 60, 120, 180, 320 and 360 min later (as printed, PDF p2;
  Figure 1 shows points near 2, 3, 4 and 6 h, so "320" may be a misprint for 240).
- Assay: unchanged nicardipine by capillary GC with electron-capture detection after ether extraction;
  sensitivity 0.5 ng/mL; reproducibility 12.0, 9.8, 6.2, 5.9% at 5, 25, 50, 100 ng/mL (PDF p3).
- Analysis: per-patient two-compartment model with zero-order 5-min input, extended least squares (PHARM program);
  AUC by trapezoids extrapolated with beta; CL = dose/AUC; Vss by the Perrier formula (PDF p3).
- Haemodynamics: Swan-Ganz, Millar micromanometer, LV angiography at control, 10 and 20 min.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- No ke0, tPeak, EC50 or concentration-effect model. Effects measured only at 10 and 20 min after the infusion.
- At 10 min (PDF p3): HR +18% (N) and +25% (P + N); mean aortic pressure −10% (N) and −25% (P + N), still −17% at
  20 min only in P + N; SVR −39% and −47%; PVR unchanged in N, −36% (10 min) and −40% (20 min) in P + N; cardiac
  index +44% and +46%; PR interval, filling pressures, LV dP/dt max and Vmax unchanged; EF +11% and +22%.
- The authors attribute the larger, longer fall in pressure in P + N to higher nicardipine concentrations
  (Discussion, PDF p4-5); no exposure-response analysis supports it directly.

## Covariates
Only the propranolol co-treatment: CL 0.68x and Vss 0.61x of group N (derived from the means), t½beta and alpha
not significantly changed. The comparator is the untreated group N in the same study. The authors propose reduced
hepatic blood flow and microsomal enzyme activity as mechanism (not measured).

## Caveats
- n 8 per group, patients rather than volunteers, non-randomised groups; large variability (CV of CL 48% in P + N).
- Sampling only to 6 h, about three t½beta, so a slower terminal phase, if present, would be missed (Modi 1993
  sampled for 24.5 h). "320 min" sampling time likely misprinted.
- Plasma concentration as unchanged drug; no protein binding.
- Salt form of the 5 mg dose not stated.
