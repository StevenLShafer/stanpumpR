# Patsalos 2018: TDM of antiepileptic drugs, 2018 update

## Study
Narrative review (PubMed/Google Scholar to March 2018, 171 references) of indications, PK, interactions and
TDM for 27 AEDs. Defines the reference range as a laboratory-quoted population range (a lower limit below which
response is unlikely, an upper limit above which toxicity is likely), not a therapeutic range.

## Model and parameters
Eslicarbazepine acetate (section text): prodrug, Tmax of eslicarbazepine 2-3 h, F > 90%, Vd 2.7 L/kg, protein
binding 44%, t1/2 13-20 h;
metabolites R-licarbazepine 5%, oxcarbazepine 1%; glucuronidation (UGT2B17 mainly).
Lacosamide: linear PK, Tmax 1-2 h, Vd 0.6-0.7 L/kg, F 100%, protein binding 14%, t1/2 12-14 h; CYP2C19
(also 3A4, 2C9) to O-desmethyl lacosamide (30%).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Reference ranges only (seizure control vs toxicity, population-based). No concentration-effect model.
Not an opioid paper.

## Covariates
Inducers (carbamazepine, phenytoin, topiramate) lower eslicarbazepine; carbamazepine, phenytoin, phenobarbital
lower lacosamide.

## Caveats
Review; the eslicarbazepine range is borrowed from oxcarbazepine's MHD rather than derived for eslicarbazepine.
Table 3 is mis-aligned in the text layer; the section text is unambiguous.
