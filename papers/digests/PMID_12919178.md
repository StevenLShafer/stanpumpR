# Olubodun 2003: zolpidem kinetics in elderly vs young adults

## Study
40 healthy Caucasian volunteers: 24 young (8 M, 16 F; ages 21-42 in Methods, 22-42 in Abstract) and 16 elderly
(8 M, 8 F; 66-85 in Methods, 61-85 in Abstract). Table 1 (text layer shifted; read by order) mean weights: young men
76.4, elderly men 74.0, young women 66.3, elderly women 72.1 kg. 8 of 16 young women on oral contraceptives.
Single oral 5 mg zolpidem tartrate (4.2 mg zolpidem base) after overnight fast; breakfast at 2 h. Serum at 0.5, 1,
1.5, 2, 2.5, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8 h. HPLC with fluorescence; sensitivity 1 ng/mL; CVs <= 10%. Non-compartmental: CL/F =
dose of zolpidem BASE / AUC(0-inf). Plus in vitro testosterone studies (liver microsomes, hepatocytes).

Overall CL/F range 66-1595 mL/min, mean 411 +/- 345 (Results). Elderly men: CL/F about one third of young men's,
AUC about 4x, Cmax > 2x, t1/2 about doubled (Discussion). Elderly women: CL/F 209 vs 376 mL/min (ratio 0.56,
derived); t1/2 unchanged. Pooled young vs elderly CL/F (derived, n-weighted): 524 vs 243 mL/min, ratio 0.46.
Background: absolute oral F about 70% in young men (cited); CYP3A4 about 60% of hepatic CYP clearance (in vitro).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None measured. Not an opioid.

## Covariates
Age (lower CL/F in elderly); in men, free testosterone correlated with CL/F (r2 = 0.46, power function) and log AUC
(r2 = 0.43); age plus testosterone multiple r2 = 0.48. Sex: men had higher CL/F within each age group (not tested).

## Caveats
Small groups (8 per elderly sex, 8 young men); low 5 mg dose; sampling only to 8 h (t1/2 up to 2.7 h, so modest
extrapolation); clearance on base mass, so tartrate-based figures differ by 4.2/5. Age ranges inconsistent between
Abstract and Methods. Very large CV of CL/F.
