# Miller 2013: Closed-loop systems in anesthesia (editorial)

## Study
Editorial introducing three papers in the same issue on closed-loop control (Dumont primer; Rinehart in silico and pig fluid studies of the "learning intravenous resuscitator"). Discusses closed-loop neuromuscular block, BIS-guided closed-loop isoflurane/IV anesthesia (reported to hold BIS in a narrower range than clinicians), and closed-loop stroke-volume-guided fluid. Notes BIS limitations as an input signal (EMG, ketamine). No new data.
