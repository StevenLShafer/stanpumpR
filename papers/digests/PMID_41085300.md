# Rathmell and Shafer 2025: Opioid ventilation models (editorial)

## Study
Editorial on van Lemmen et al.'s population PK-PD model of fentanyl-induced ventilatory depression in healthy volunteers (interlocking equations for fentanyl PK, CO2 kinetics and ventilatory control). The editors adapted the authors' Python code into a Shiny simulator (steveshafer.shinyapps.io/Fentanyl_Vent_Depression/). No new data; the model's parameter tables are in the van Lemmen paper, not here.

Simulation results stated in the text (PDF p2-3; printed 1143):
- 100 ug bolus then 1 ug/min for 2 h (Fig 1): ventilation falls modestly early; by 10 min, CO2 has risen and depression is about half the initial; max fall about 15% (Fig 1 caption); about 2 h after stopping, ventilation mostly recovers.
- 200 ug as bolus: about 30% ventilation decrease over first 5 min; the same dose over 10 min: about 25% decrease, peak shortly after infusion ends (Fig 2).
- 500 ug bolus: predicted 50% reduction; the editors say clinical experience suggests apnea, so the model underpredicts at high dose. The model mathematics do not predict apnea. Largest bolus in the source study was 100 ug.
