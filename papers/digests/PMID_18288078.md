# Hebert 2008: oral midazolam and digoxin at 28-32 weeks vs 6-10 weeks postpartum

PDF page n = printed page 247 + n.

## Study
14 healthy pregnant women enrolled, 13 completed all four study days (one withdrew after one day and was
excluded). Paired design: oral digoxin 0.25 mg and oral midazolam 2 mg, in random order 1-2 weeks apart, at 28-32
weeks' gestation (mean 30.5 ± 1.3 wk digoxin, 30.2 ± 1.0 wk midazolam) and the same order 6-10 weeks postpartum
(10.0 ± 3.6 and 9.9 ± 4.0 wk; two women later). Weight 67.2 ± 18.3 kg prepregnancy, 76.4 ± 17.1 third
trimester, 71.6 ± 19.3 postpartum; age 28 ± 6 (22-40) y. Albumin 2.5-3.1 g/dL pregnant, 3.4-4.4 postpartum.
Digoxin plasma sampled to 48 h, urine collected 0-48 h; midazolam to 6 h, urine 24 h. LC-MS for drug; protein
binding by ultrafiltration (3H-digoxin). Noncompartmental: CLrenal = Ae/AUC0-48; CLsecretion = CLrenal - fu x
CrCL (24-h measured creatinine clearance); unbound clearances = clearance / fu. Wilcoxon signed-rank. Digoxin
CL/F was deliberately not computed (only about 1.5 half-lives sampled).

Figure 3: digoxin renal clearance vs creatinine clearance, y = 1.1x - 15.5, r = 0.8 (13 subjects, both
periods). Discussion (p251): renal clearance is 80-85% of digoxin total clearance (authors' statement);
creatinine clearance a good surrogate for the change in digoxin renal clearance.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None (probe-drug PK study).

## Covariates
Pregnancy (paired). CYP3A5*3*3 women (n = 9): midazolam AUC 8.9 vs 16.1 ng.h/mL, unbound CL/F 637 vs 368 L/min.

## Caveats
n = 13; oral only, so bioavailability and systemic clearance cannot be separated; digoxin sampled only 48 h
(no CL/F); "percent difference" columns are means of individual changes and differ from ratios of means (renal
clearance: 61% vs 57.4%). Abstract/Table 1 disagree slightly on postpartum unbound midazolam CL/F (345 vs 343).
