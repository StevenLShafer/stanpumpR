# Zheng 2021: PBPK prediction of quetiapine and aripiprazole exposure in pregnancy

## Study
Pure modelling study, no new subjects (published online 30 Nov 2020; issue 2021;60(5)). PK-Sim/MoBi (Open
Systems Pharmacology 8.0), default 18-compartment adult model extended to a 27-compartment pregnancy model
using Dallmann's gestational physiology. Adult models built from digitised published data (quetiapine 25 mg
single and 25→300 mg bid; aripiprazole 2 mg i.v. and 10 mg oral), verified against further studies, then
extrapolated. In pregnancy only fu (albumin, Eqs 1-2) and enzyme activity changed: CYP3A4 by a cubic in
gestational week (Eq 3), CYP2D6 +100% in the third trimester (p4, printed 626). Virtual populations: nonpregnant
women, mean age 30 yr; trimesters at mean gestational weeks 6, 20, 34. Doses simulated: quetiapine 200 mg bid,
aripiprazole 15 mg qd, to steady state. Predictions compared with TDM data (ESM Figs S4-S6, not read).

Dose recommendations (Table 3, p10, printed 632): quetiapine 300 mg bid (T2), 500 mg bid (T3) give AUCτ 2464 and
2522 vs 2786 ng·h/mL at baseline; aripiprazole 25 mg qd (T2), 40 mg qd (T3) give active-moiety AUC 6286 and 5954 vs
6265; 30 mg qd in T3 gives 4715 (~25% lower) with trough 148 ng/mL. Conclusion: at least 2.5× (quetiapine) and 2×
(aripiprazole) the baseline dose late in pregnancy.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None modelled. Therapeutic reference ranges used (from Hiemke 2018): quetiapine 100-500 ng/mL, aripiprazole
100-350 ng/mL, active moiety 150-500 ng/mL. Authors assume unchanged concentration-response in pregnancy.

## Covariates
Gestational week (fu via albumin; CYP3A4 activity cubic; CYP2D6 +100% in T3). No genotype-by-pregnancy
interaction modelled.

## Caveats
- Predictions, not measurements; comparator is the model's own simulated nonpregnant 30-year-old women, not the
  same women before pregnancy.
- Authors say the model under-predicts the quetiapine decline seen in TDM (Westin) and slightly over-predicts
  aripiprazole's.
- Table 2 misprints: caption swaps drugs/doses; quetiapine T3 Cmin change "-4%" and aripiprazole T3 Cmax change
  "-5" are inconsistent with the printed means (derived -45% and -54%).
- CYP2D6 +100% is a single literature assumption; dehydroaripiprazole clearance assigned to CYP3A4 arbitrarily.
- N-desalkylquetiapine (norquetiapine) not modelled.
