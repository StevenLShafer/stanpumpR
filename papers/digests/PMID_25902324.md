# Puri 2016: multicentre CLADS vs manual propofol control

## Study
Stratified randomised, patient-blinded, 6 Indian centres (Jan 2010-Sep 2012), n = 242 (121 per arm), ASA low-risk adults 18-60 y, 1-3 h non-thoracic/vascular/neuro surgery. Excluded weight <70% or >130% of ideal. Table 1 (PDF p 5): age 41 +/- 13 vs 42 +/- 13 y; weight 61 +/- 14 vs 62 +/- 13 kg; height 158 +/- 8 cm; M:F 38:83 vs 41:80. Premedication lorazepam 1-2 mg orally; fentanyl 2 ug/kg 3 min before induction then 1 ug/kg/h; N2O/O2 (FiO2 0.4); vecuronium. CLADS (closed-loop BIS-guided propofol; BIS target 50; PID, rate changes every 30 s, BIS sampled every 5 s) vs propofol titrated manually to BIS 50.

## Model and parameters
No PK model is reported. The controller is described only qualitatively (uses distribution/clearance and the delay to peak effect; parameters not printed; algorithm in earlier CLADS papers, refs 7 and 13).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: BIS (target 50). No ke0, t1/2 ke0, tPeak, EC50 or MEAC reported; no plasma or effect-site concentrations measured or calculated. Not an opioid study (fentanyl 2 ug/kg + 1 ug/kg/h is co-medication).
- Table 2 (PDF p 5), median (IQR) CLADS vs manual: propofol induction dose 1.4 (1.2-1.8) vs 1.8 (1.6-2.2) mg/kg, p < 0.0001; induction time 160 (125-213) vs 105 (55-150) s; minimal BIS 42 vs 37.
- Table 3: % time within +/-10 of BIS 50: 82 (76-89) vs 61 (41-74); MDAPE 10 (10-12) vs 18 (14-24); wobble 9 vs 10; global score 24 vs 51; total propofol 5.4 (4.5-6.7) vs 5.3 (4.3-6.9) mg/kg/h, p = 0.57; fentanyl 3 vs 3 ug/kg. The abstract's 81.4% vs 55.3% are means; the tables give medians. (The abstract's MDAPE 10 vs 18 matches Table 3; the abstract's wobble "9 vs 10" and global 24 vs 51 also match.)
- The high-altitude site (Leh) needed a larger induction dose and longer induction time than the other sites (results text).

## Covariates
Site/altitude only (descriptive). No equations.

## Caveats
Anaesthesiologist not blinded (Hawthorne effect acknowledged); all patients low-risk so the high-risk rate limits were not exercised; the manual comparator varied widely between sites; Table 4 not independently checked here.
