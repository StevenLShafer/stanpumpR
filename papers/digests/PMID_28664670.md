# Moffett 2017: population PK of antithrombin concentrate in children

## Study
- Single-centre retrospective cohort, Texas Children's Hospital, January 2011 to December 2015; patients under 19 y who got plasma-derived antithrombin (AT) concentrate (Thrombate) and had a predose and at least one postdose AT activity level; mechanical circulatory support excluded.
- n = 184 (46.7% male); age median 0.35 y (IQR 0.07-3.9); weight median 5.2 kg (IQR 3.4-14.8); height median 58 cm; postconceptional age median 55 weeks (Table 1, PDF p.3). Age groups: 50 neonates, 85 infants, 30 children, 19 adolescents.
- Dosing: median 2 doses (IQR 1-4), mean 46.3 units/kg (SD 13.6) per dose, infused over 15 min, IV. Continuous-infusion unfractionated heparin (UFH) in 87.5%, mean 34.1 units/kg/h (SD 22.7).
- Sampling: median 3 postdose levels per patient (IQR 2-7), median 20.8 h after a dose. Baseline AT activity mean 59 (SD 17) units/dL (Table 2, PDF p.4).
- Assay: plasma chromogenic anti-thrombin activity (STA-Stachrom AT, STA-R analyser), CV 9.35%; reported as % of normal = units/dL. This is activity, not mass concentration.
- Software: NONMEM 7.2, FOCE-I; exponential IIV; model selection by OFV (3.84 forward, 10.83 backward); baseline AT handled by the B3 method; 1000-sample bootstrap; external validation in 30 patients from the following 6 months.

## Model and parameters
One compartment, exponential residual error, allometric weight (exponent 0.75 on CL, 1 on V, reference 70 kg). Final model (Table 3, PDF p.4, rendered; units dL/h and dL, so activity in units/dL times volume in dL gives units):

- CL (dL/h) = 0.917 x (WT/70)^0.75 + 0.129 x (UFH/173), UFH in units/h.
- V (dL) = 67.9 x (WT/70) x (BASE/60)^-0.389, BASE in units/dL.
- IIV 47.5% (CL), 28.5% (V); residual 11.1%.

Bootstrap (Table 4, PDF p.5, text layer only): CL 0.917 (RSE 16.8%), V 67.9 (4.9%), UFH 0.129 (15.5%), BASE -0.389 (37.5%), omega1 47.5 (26.5%), omega2 28.2 (31.2%), residual 11.1 (26.9%).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None modeled. The end point is plasma AT activity, not an anticoagulant effect. The authors mention a usual goal of 120 units/dL and a pediatric normal range of 85-130%, but no PD model, ke0, MEAC, or potency ratio. Validation: median prediction error -1.75% (IQR -11.75 to 6.5%), median absolute error 10.1% (IQR 4.8-19.6%) for the first postdose level (n = 30).

## Covariates
- Weight (allometric) on CL and V; UFH dose on CL (higher heparin, higher AT clearance: 0.129 dL/h per 173 units/h); baseline AT activity on V (higher baseline, smaller V). Albumin, platelets, age, creatinine and others were tested (Appendix, PDF p.7) and dropped. Baseline on CL and V together gave unacceptable IIV, so it was kept on V only.

## Caveats
- Retrospective, clinically timed samples, not a controlled PK study; only the first postdose level and later declining levels were kept.
- Endogenous AT production is handled only via the baseline method, so V and CL absorb it.
- Text-vs-table half-life mismatch (open question). Minor print slips: validation AT level written as units/mL (should be units/dL), hemoglobin as mg/dL (Table 2, PDF p.4; likely g/dL).
- Pediatric-only, mostly infants (median age 0.35 y).
