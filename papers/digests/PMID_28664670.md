# Moffett 2017: plasma-derived antithrombin in children

## Study
- Retrospective, Texas Children's Hospital, 2011-2015: 184 inpatients under 19 years given Thrombate (Grifols) and not on mechanical circulatory support, with a predose and at least one postdose activity (Table 1): 46.7% boys, age 0.35 (IQR 0.07-3.9) years, weight 5.2 (3.4-14.8) kg; 50 neonates, 85 infants, 30 children, 19 adolescents. Baseline activity 59 +/- 17 IU/dL (Table 2).
- 87.5% received antithrombin for a low activity during continuous heparin, 34.1 +/- 22.7 units/kg/h. Doses 46.3 +/- 13.6 units/kg over 15 minutes, a median of two; three levels per patient, a median 20.8 h after a dose.
- Activity by STA-Stachrom AT (chromogenic, functional), reported as % of normal = IU/dL. NONMEM 7.2, FOCE-I; the baseline entered as a covariate and in the error model (Dansirikul's B3 method). Validation cohort of 30 (Table 6): median prediction error 1.75%.

## Model and parameters
One compartment, exponential residual error. Table 3:

    CL (dL/h) = 0.917 x (WT/70)^0.75 + 0.129 x (UFH/173)
    VD (dL)   = 67.9 x (WT/70) x (BASE/60)^-0.389

UFH the continuous heparin in units/h, BASE the baseline activity in IU/dL. The exponent's sign is printed on the page image (the text layer drops it, and Table 4's bootstrap interval for it, -0.675 to -0.103, loses its minus signs too); the Discussion says a higher baseline gave a smaller VD.

## Checks
- Table 5 (5.2 kg, BASE 60, means of 10 000 simulated patients): CL 2.8 mL/kg/h without heparin and 5.7 with 34 units/kg/h, VD 101 mL/kg, half-lives 31.6 and 15.5 h. The typical values are 2.51 and 5.04 mL/kg/h and 97.0 mL/kg; with the variability (log-normal) their means are 2.81, 5.64 and 101.
- Text: half-life ~27 h without heparin (26.8 by the equations) and "~22 h" with it, which the equations do not give (13.3 h).
- Thrombate III label (12.3, adults with hereditary deficiency, functional assay): recovery 1.4 IU/dL per IU/kg, half-life 3.8 days. This model at 70 kg, BASE 60: 1.03 and 2.1 days.
- No Gideon Koren among the authors.

## How stanpumpR uses it
R/drugs_antithrombin.R: Table 3 with the heparin term at 0, both weight terms as fitted, BASE from the Antithrombin baseline field (blank 60) held to 25-93 in VD. Plotted as baseline + infused in IU/dL; band 80-120 IU/dL from the label.
