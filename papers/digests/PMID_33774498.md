# Fukushima 2021: lacosamide before, during and after one pregnancy (case letter)

## Study
Clinical letter, single case, National Epilepsy Center, Shizuoka, Japan. A 23-year-old non-smoking woman
with temporal lobe epilepsy after limbic encephalitis, **CYP2C19*1/*1** (extensive metaboliser; *2 and *3
genotyped by real-time PCR). Lacosamide was cut from 400 to 200 mg/day before conception; throughout
pregnancy and after delivery she took lacosamide 200 mg/day, **carbamazepine 800 mg/day** and clobazam
45 mg/day, unchanged. Serum lacosamide by LC-MS/MS, samples 4-5 h after the morning dose, May 2017 to June
2020. Comparator: TDM data, 94 samples from 27 nonpregnant women (17-45 y) on lacosamide plus carbamazepine.
Body weight is not reported.

## Model and parameters
No model. Values (text p 2 and Fig 1, rendered):
- Lacosamide 5.2 µg/mL at week -4 (200 mg/day); 2.6 to 4.7 µg/mL in weeks 6-36.
- CD ratio (concentration / weight-adjusted dose): 1.46 at week -4, 1.28 "in week 18" (text), 1.57 at week 36.
  Mean 1.19 before pregnancy (14 samples, weeks -72 to -4), 1.28 during, 1.37 after (5 samples, weeks
  44-90) (Fig 1 caption).
- Fig 2 (control and patient): concentration linear in mg/kg/day dose, R² = 0.78; RMSE 1.01 (controls), 0.98
  (patient before) and 0.41 (patient during pregnancy). Fig 2 counts 4 samples during pregnancy and 19 before
  and after.
- Delivery at 39 weeks, healthy infant; no focal impaired-awareness seizures during pregnancy.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Seizures only: one focal impaired-awareness seizure after the pre-conception dose cut; none in pregnancy. No
concentration-effect relation.

## Covariates
The authors suggest (Discussion p 3) that a pregnancy fall in CYP2C19 activity (a PBPK prediction by Ke 2014)
could offset the rise in renal clearance in extensive metabolisers, and that poor metabolisers might show a
larger fall. This is their hypothesis; nothing in the case tests it.

## Caveats
One woman; on carbamazepine, an inducer, throughout (so were the controls); weight and gestational sampling
sparse (Fig 2 counts 4 pregnancy samples); sampling 4-5 h post dose. The authors conclude little change in
lacosamide, unlike the falls reported by MONEAD for women not on inducers.
