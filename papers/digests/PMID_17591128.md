# Puri 2007: CLADS closed-loop propofol by BIS vs manual control

## Study
- Randomised comparison of a PID-based closed-loop propofol system (CLADS) with manual titration, both targeting BIS 50. 40 patients, 20 per group. The abstract says ASA I-II; the Methods say ASA 1-3, aged 18-65, elective non-cardiac surgery.
- Premedication was lorazepam 1-2 mg orally. Fentanyl 2 ug/kg was given 3 min before induction, then 1 ug/kg/h for the whole case. Vecuronium 0.1 mg/kg, with controlled ventilation.
- The controller reads BIS every 5 s (15 s averaging) from an A-2000 monitor and drives a Pilot-C syringe pump. It does not change the rate more than every 30 s outside induction. Fig 1 shows the algorithm: PID, adaptive, with an "effect delay".
- The Methods describe the controller as built on a clinical pharmacokinetic-dynamic profile. **No PK or PD parameters are given.**
- Demographics (Table 1, mean±SD), CLADS vs manual: age 41.4±15.9 vs 37.0±12.9 y; weight 60.8±14.6 vs 65.4±18.7 kg; height 156.3±18.7 vs 158±7.4 cm; BMI 23.9±4.4 vs 26.3±6.0; M/F 13/7 vs 12/8.
- Performance measures are those of Varvel 1992: MDPE, MDAPE, wobble, divergence.

Results (PDF p4, Tables 2 and 3, confirmed on the rendered page):
- Induction time, median (IQR): 175 (85-305) s CLADS vs 191 (95-320) s manual, P=0.11.
- Induction dose: 1.48±0.53 vs 2.0±0.48 mg/kg, P=0.03.
- Lowest BIS during induction: 46.66±7.25 vs 38.14±10.8 (the text says ±10.18), P=0.03.
- Fall in MAP: 18.3±10.1 vs 17.9±9.5 %, P=0.9.
- Percent of time with BIS within ±10 of target: 87.32±9.1 vs 77.3±14.3, P=0.01.
- MDPE: 1.0±4.4 vs 0.93±2.2.
- MDAPE: 9.45±1.85 vs 16±5.3 %.
- Wobble, median (IQR): 8.4 (4.3-9.5) vs 13.2 (5.1-17.6).
- Divergence: -0.0024±0.005 vs -0.019±0.009 %/min.
- Total propofol (text only): 5.03±1.68 vs 7.33±2.07 mg/kg/h, P<0.01.
- Recovery (end of drug to extubation): 440 (250-620) vs 535 (300-835) s, P<0.05.
- Manual group changed the infusion rate a median of 30 times (IQR 12-45).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: BIS, target 50. No ke0, t½ke0, tPeak, EC50 or hysteresis value is reported. The Discussion only says the controller's built-in delay allows for hysteresis.
- Opioid audit: not an opioid paper. Fentanyl was a background infusion (1 ug/kg/h), with no concentration, MEAC or potency data.
- No propofol concentrations were measured.

## Covariates
None analysed.

## Caveats
- Small n, no blinding, and the control arm was also set to BIS 50.
- ASA classes are inconsistent between the abstract (I-II) and the Methods (1-3).
- The text and Table 2 differ on the SD of lowest BIS (10.18 vs 10.8).
- The Discussion cites "Table 2" for the divergence and wobble results, which are in Table 3.
- The induction-dose P value is <0.05 in the abstract and 0.03 in the text and table.
