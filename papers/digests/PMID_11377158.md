# Ropcke 2001: propofol-remifentanil interaction on BIS during surgery

## Study
Prospective open-label study, 20 ASA 1-2 adults for orthopaedic surgery (Table 1, PDF p3; ages 19-72 y, weights 46-95 kg, heights 155-184 cm, 8 men and 12 women, counted from the table), 7.5 mg oral midazolam premedication. Propofol (Gepts PK set) and remifentanil (Minto 1997 PK set) both by TCI with IVA-PUMP software. Targets were alternately changed to hold BIS 45-55 (propofol 1.5-6 ug/mL maintenance, remifentanil 2-15 ng/mL); after at least 20 min at constant targets with BIS in range, venous propofol was sampled (HPLC fluorescence, plasma, total) and BIS recorded. Infusion rate = median between minutes 10 and 20 after the last change. Eighteen patients completed; patients 1 and 7 (parenthesised values in Table 1) never held BIS in range for 20 min and were excluded. No intraoperative recall. Remifentanil was not assayed (assay failed to be set up, text PDF p5).

Goodness of fit, Table 2 (PDF p4 text layer, not re-read on an image): synergistic model likelihood 14.9, SD 21.4%; additive model likelihood 2.09, SD 23.9%; P < 0.05 for synergy. Validity range: propofol 1.5-8 ug/mL, remifentanil 1-30 ng/mL (text PDF p6). Propofol PK set underpredicted measured concentrations, median absolute prediction error 30.0% (text PDF p5).

Derived (computed from the first isobole, not printed): remifentanil needed for BIS 50 falls from about 43 ng/mL at propofol 2 ug/mL to about 4.9 at 4 and 2.5 at 5, and about 0.57 at 8 ug/mL. Outside the data, F and G were not significant so the curve has no single-drug intercept.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: BIS held at 45-55 (target 50) during orthopaedic surgery with surgical stimulation (hypnotic EEG surrogate, not analgesia or movement).
Opioid audit (remifentanil): no MEAC or minimum effective concentration stated. No ke0, t1/2 ke0, tPeak or hysteresis estimate; instead the authors held targets for at least 20 min to allow plasma-effect-site equilibration (text PDF p3). Remifentanil concentration is the TCI-predicted blood concentration (Minto PK), not measured; the authors note a median absolute weighted residual of 20% for that set. Potency ratio: the text mentions an alfentanil-to-remifentanil potency ratio used by Vuyk (reference 4) but gives no value. Vuyk-model effect-site predictions quoted for comparison (text PDF p7): remifentanil 4.7 ng/mL with propofol 2.5 ug/mL for 50% probability of no response to surgical stimuli, and 8.0 ng/mL with 2.8 ug/mL for 95%; propofol-alone concentrations quoted for suppressing movement at skin incision 10.0-15 ug/mL (refs 12, 13).

## Covariates
None modelled. Table 3 contrasts weight-adjusted and non-weight-adjusted infusion rates: weight adjustment reduced between-patient variability (SD 24.9% vs 33.1%) with patient 1 (the outlier on weight-adjusted rates; 74 kg, 72 y in Table 1) excluded from the weight-adjusted fit; including that patient the SD is 29.9%.

## Caveats
- n=18 analysed, no data on either drug alone, so single-drug intercepts are extrapolation (authors say as much).
- Remifentanil never assayed; Cprop measured venous, plasma. Propofol was underpredicted by the TCI set; venous sampling shortly after infusion changes versus arterial.
- Steady state approximated by the infusion rate between minutes 10 and 20 after the last target change (rate change -0.5 +/- 2.1% propofol, -1.5 +/- 3.1% remifentanil).
- BIS 50 isobole only; the authors say it cannot be extrapolated to other BIS values.
- Midazolam premedication in all patients.
