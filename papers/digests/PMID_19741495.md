# Sigtermans 2009: S(+)-ketamine PK-PD, experimental pain and cardiac output

## Study
- 20 healthy volunteers, 10 men and 10 women, 18-30 y, BMI < 28 kg/m2. Table 1 (PDF p4): all 69.4 +/- 2.4 kg (men 75.2 +/- 2.4, women 63.6 +/- 3.2), height 177.2 +/- 2.4 cm, age 21.7 +/- 0.5 y (mean +/- SEM). All women on oral contraceptives. Leiden.
- S(+)-ketamine IV for 2 h in stepped rates designed to raise the concentration by 40 ng/mL every 15 min to a maximum of 320 ng/mL; doses per 70 kg: 1.25 mg over min 0-5 and 5-15, 2.2 mg at 15-20 and 20-30, 3.1, 4.1, 4.6, 5.4, 6.0, 6.6 mg in the following 5 and 10 min blocks; 66.5 mg total in 70 kg (Fig 1A, PDF p3; from the text).
- Arterial plasma sampling (radial line): before, at each step (8 samples), and 2-180 min after the infusion (13 samples). Assay: HPLC with UV at 195 nm, LLOQ 10 ng/mL, LOD 3 ng/mL, for S(+)-ketamine and S(+)-norketamine (Appendix 1).
- Pain measured at 0, then every 15 min during and for 3 h after: heat pain (Medoc TSA-II, 3x3 cm thermode, VAS 0-10 cm, peak temperature 46-49 C, the lowest giving VAS > 6) and electrical pain tolerance (tibial, mA, 10 Hz, 0.2 ms, cutoff 128 mA); drug high (VAS) and cardiac output (FloTrac/Vigileo, 5-min values).
- NONMEM VI 1.2, ADVAN6, two-stage (PK first, then PD with PK fixed to Bayesian estimates); exponential IIV; covariates sex and weight (dose and PK per 70 kg).
- No placebo arm in the main study; an extra placebo set (n=10, data not shown) showed no heat effect and a small slow electrical effect (maximum about 20% above baseline).

## Model and parameters
S(+)-ketamine: three compartments; S(+)-norketamine: formed through three sequential transit compartments (mean transit time 3/KM), then a two-compartment norketamine model; norketamine central volume set equal to ketamine's (not identifiable) and its formation rate set to the ketamine elimination rate (Fig 2, Appendix 2). Weight scaling (volumes L/70 kg, clearances L/h/70 kg) improved the fit (delta MOFV 21); sex on CL1 improved it (delta MOFV 17); a third transit compartment improved MOFV by 8; a peripheral norketamine compartment by 539.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Not an opioid; no MEAC, equianalgesic or potency ratio to an opioid. ke0 was **not estimated**: the study was not designed to (Discussion, PDF p8). Effects followed plasma concentrations almost instantly, suggesting a small t1/2ke0; the authors cite Herd (racemic ketamine, children, EEG slowing) as 11 s (95% CI 7-20 s) and Schuttler (S(+)-ketamine, EEG median frequency) as agreeing. Rapid onset and offset of all effects (Fig 4, PDF p6).

- Heat pain C50 373 ng/mL vs electrical 2.2 ug/mL: the authors call S(+)-ketamine six times more potent on heat pain (derived 2200/373 = 5.9).
- Cardiac output rose from about 5.5 to 8.2 L/min over 0-320 ng/mL (a 40-50% rise; Discussion). A 20% rise needs 134 ng/mL.
- Heat pain unpleasantness and drug high fit poorly and were discarded (systematic lag; PDF p5). Norketamine added no consistent effect on any end point; the S(+)-norketamine contribution term (Eq 5) was significant in a minority of subjects only.
- No sex effect on any PD parameter; higher concentrations in men account for the sex differences in effect.
- Side effects: nausea 16/20, dizziness 20/20, hypertension 20/20 (mean BP 150/110 at end of infusion).
- Literature C50 values the authors quote: Schuttler S(+)-ketamine EEG 800 ng/mL or more for adequate anaesthesia; Herd racemic, children, 500 (arousal) and 440 (recall) ng/mL.

## Covariates
- Weight: all PK per 70 kg; weight scaling beat none (delta MOFV 21). Scaling form (linear per 70 kg) is by parameterisation, no allometric exponent estimated.
- Sex: on CL1 of ketamine and norketamine only; women have the higher clearance, hence lower concentrations (Table 2; text says 20%, Table 2 combined gives 1.30, open question).
- No sex effect on PD parameters.

## Caveats
- Healthy young volunteers, single 2-h ramp, no bolus, no placebo arm in the main data.
- Norketamine PK conditional on assumptions (formation rate fixed to ketamine elimination); no norketamine administration data.
- Hyperalgesia after the infusion modelled as a trend with large IIV; heat pain TRD has a wide SE.
- Pain tolerance modelled although 'arbitrarily' restricted; electrical data only for tolerance.
- S(+)-enantiomer, not racemic ketamine.

## Verification against stanpumpR
Not cited, so no transcription check applies; the comparison below is an independent check of the library ketamine model (R/drugs_ketamine.R:38-77) with the caveat that Domino is **racemic** and Sigtermans **S(+)**:
- Library volumes at 70 kg (derived from lines 38-43, 63-65): V1 4.08, V2 39.7, V3 171.9 L (Vss 215.6 L, 3.08 L/kg). Sigtermans combined: V1 15.4, V2 26.6, V3 121 L (Vss 163 L). Central volume is 3.8x larger in Sigtermans (arterial sampling in both; Domino's measured at 1 min).
- Library clearance 55.3 L/h per 70 kg (0.79 L/h/kg; derived from line 66, k10 0.226 x V1). Sigtermans CL1 75.1 (men) and 97.3 (women) L/h/70 kg, i.e. 36% and 76% higher than the library's (derived). Direction: S(+)-ketamine, which the paper says is eliminated faster in women; the comparator here is a different enantiomer and cohort, not an error in the library.
- Library analgesic band 100-160 ng/mL, typical 120 (lines 45-51), as a threshold for analgesia; this paper's heat-pain C50 of 373 ng/mL for S(+)-ketamine is 50% inhibition of VAS on a different scale (experimental heat pain), not the same quantity, and not a MEAC. No conflict stated; no S(+) MEAC in this paper.
- tPeak 3 min is 'assumed' (line 52). Sigtermans did not estimate ke0 but reports near-instantaneous equilibration (small t1/2ke0, cited 11 s for EEG), which is compatible with a short tPeak on the heat-pain, tolerance and cardiac output end points. Not a measured tPeak.
