# Xie 2002: population PK of irinotecan (CPT-11) lactone/carboxylate and metabolites

PDF page = printed page − 3292 (PDF p1 = p3293).

## Study
- 70 adult cancer patients (46 men, 24 women), Erasmus MC Rotterdam, 1996-1999; age median 54 y (36-71); BSA
  median 1.86 m² (1.35-2.5); mainly colorectal cancer (n = 38). Eligibility: age <75, ECOG ≤2, adequate marrow,
  liver and renal function (PDF p2-3).
- Irinotecan 175-300 mg/m² as a 90-min IV infusion (actual 1.3-1.9 h) every 3 weeks; 16 patients studied in a
  second course. Premedication ondansetron 8 mg + dexamethasone 10 mg IV.
- Sampling to 56 h (about 0, 0.5, 1.5, 1.67, 1.83, 2, 2.5, 3.5, 4.5, 5, 5.5, 6.5, 8, 12, 25.5, 49, 56 h).
  Plasma, reversed-phase HPLC with fluorescence: lactone and total CPT-11 and SN-38; total only for SN-38G, APC, NPC.
  Concentrations in ng/mL.
- Sequential NONMEM modelling (FOCE with interaction; "NONMEM VI" as printed), Xpose 3.0; exponential IIV;
  combined proportional + additive residual error; P < .001 (ΔOFV 10.8) for model selection.

## Model and parameters
Step 0 (lactone alone, no interconversion): three-compartment, CL 107 L/h (57.5 L/h/m²), Vss 722 L (388 L/m²)
(PDF p3, p5).

Step 1 (final, Table 1, PDF p6, rendered): CPT-11 lactone three-compartment and carboxylate two-compartment,
with reversible interconversion in central and peripheral compartments. BSA was the only covariate (ΔOFV 26.99);
parameters are per m² BSA.

Typical patient (BSA 1.86 m², PDF p1 and p3): lactone CL 74.3 L/h and Vss 445 L; carboxylate CL 12.3 L/h and Vss
78 L (derived check: 39.9 × 1.86 = 74.2; (20.7 + 47.4 + 171) × 1.86 = 444.7; 6.6 × 1.86 = 12.3; 41.8 × 1.86 = 77.7).
Post hoc CL ranges 24.8-149.9 L/h (lactone), 6.6-19.5 L/h (carboxylate). Simulated terminal half-lives
(350 mg/m² over 1 h): CPT-11 lactone 18.3 h, total 18.1, SN-38 lactone/total and SN-38G 24.2, APC 18.3, NPC 33.3 h;
74% of the dose eliminated as lactone, 26% as carboxylate (PDF p5). Model-predicted total CPT-11 CL 18 L/h/m²
(online appendix, not in this PDF).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No pharmacodynamic end point, no ke0, no exposure-toxicity model. Not an opioid paper. The only "equilibration"
quantity is chemical: CPT-11 lactone/carboxylate interconversion half-life of 14 min in the central compartment,
hydrolysis about five times faster than lactonisation (abstract). Derived: ln2/k_L1C1 = ln2/3 h⁻¹ = 13.9 min,
whereas ln2/(k_L1C1 + k_C1L1) = 11.4 min, so the quoted 14 min matches the hydrolysis constant alone;
3/0.64 = 4.7. For SN-38, lactonisation is 2.6-fold faster than hydrolysis (0.835 vs 0.319 h⁻¹).

## Covariates
BSA on CPT-11 parameters (form of the BSA relation beyond per-m² normalisation not printed); age on APC CL
(−1.42% per year from 55). None for SN-38, SN-38G or NPC.

## Caveats
- Metabolite parameters are identifiable only relative to the formation fraction (CL/Fm, V/Fm) or formation
  clearance (ratios to CLf), so they are not absolute.
- Carboxylate peripheral volume fixed arbitrarily; central-to-peripheral rate fixed at 0.
- Enterohepatic recycling seen as rebounds but not modelled. NPC estimates exclude one outlier.
- Full model and total-drug predictions are in an online appendix not in the archived PDF.
- Cancer patients with normal organ function; no hepatic or renal impairment studied.
