# Bockbrader 2011: Pfizer adult pregabalin population PK

## Study
- 18 studies: 5 serial-sampling (4 healthy-volunteer, 1 renal impairment, n = 123, 2,868 samples), 4 partial-seizure studies (n = 626, 1,513 samples), 9 chronic-pain studies (n = 974, 1,202 samples). Final 5,583 samples from 1,723 subjects.
- Mean age 50.8 y (13-100), weight 83.7 kg (37.7-180), CLcr 101.1 mL/min (10-281), 47% male, 89% white (Results p.251-252).
- Doses 1-900 mg/day; b.i.d./t.i.d.; fed/fasted (fasted = meal > 2 h from dose).
- HPLC-UV (derivatised), LLOQ 0.05 ug/mL. CLcr by Cockcroft-Gault.
- NONMEM V; 75% index / 25% validation split.

## Model and parameters
One compartment, first-order absorption with lag, first-order elimination.
CL/F = thCLcr x CLcr x CFLG + thCLcr x thCLcrBP x (1 - CFLG), CFLG = 1 when CLcr <= breakpoint (no intercept).
Vd/F = thWT x (WT/ref)^thpwr x (GDER + thGDER x (1 - GDER)); text gives ref 80.5 kg, Table 3 header gives 79.5 kg (internal inconsistency).
Ka = (k x thfast) x (1 + thfed x (1 - FFA)), k = CL/F / Vd/F.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. Not an opioid; no MEAC, ke0 or potency.

## Covariates
CLcr (CG) on CL/F with breakpoint; weight and sex on Vd/F; food on Ka. Abstract states CL/F independent of gender, though Table 4 shows a 7.7% lower female ratio judged not clinically relevant.

## Caveats
- Breakpoint may reflect CG overestimation in heavy subjects (up to 180 kg) and 32 patients with SCr < 0.6 mg/dL (Discussion).
- Vd/F poorly individualised in sparse data. Reference weight 80.5 vs 79.5 kg inconsistency; ka fold 76 vs 85.5 inconsistency.
