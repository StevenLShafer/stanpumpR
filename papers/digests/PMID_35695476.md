# Han 2022: cabotegravir oral and long-acting IM population PK

## Study
- 16 phase 1, 2 and 3 studies, 1647 adults (72% HIV-1 infected, 28% uninfected; about 25.7% female), 23 926 plasma concentrations; oral tablets 10, 30, 60 mg and LA IM injections 200, 400, 600, 800 mg (12 169 injections). Studies listed in Table 1 (printed p. 4609-4610).
- External validation set: ATLAS-2M, 647 subjects, 5097 concentrations (Q4W and Q8W arms), not used for model building.
- Assay: LC-MS/MS on plasma, LLOQ 0.01 ug/mL (earlier studies) or 0.025 ug/mL (later); total (not unbound) concentration; >99% albumin-bound.
- NONMEM 7.3, FOCE-I, PsN; BQL data (537 values after LA injection, about 3.1% of post-injection concentrations) handled by the likelihood-based M3 method. Forward addition P < .01, backward elimination P < .001. Bootstrap and prediction-corrected VPC, 500 replicates.

## Model and parameters
Two-compartment model, first-order oral absorption and first-order LA IM absorption, linear elimination. Oral bioavailability relative to LA, F1, is estimated (LA IM bioavailability assumed 100% when F1 is read as absolute oral F). Residual error combined additive and proportional, the same for oral and LA. Interoccasion variability on KALA and F1 did not improve the fit. Typical subject is 76.6 kg. Base model (Table 3): KAoral 1.12 /h, KALA 0.000642 /h, CL/F 0.16 L/h, V2/F 5.5 L, Q/F 0.563 L/h, V3/F 2.45 L, F1 0.746, exponents 0.611 (CL, Q) and 0.675 (V).

Final equations, text of printed p. 4618-4619 (text layer only): KALA = 0.00073 x (1 - 50.9% if female) x (1 + 47.8% if split) x (BMI/25.4)^-0.766 x (NDL/1.5)^0.478; CL/F = 0.151 x (BWT/76.6)^0.618 x (1 + 17.4% if current smoker); V2/F = 5.27 x (BWT/76.6)^0.702; Q/F = 0.507 x (BWT/76.6)^0.618; V3/F = 2.43 x (BWT/76.6)^0.702. All clearances and volumes are apparent (divided by F, with F the LA IM bioavailability).

Derived by the authors (text, p. 4619): oral terminal half-life 36.5 h nonsmoker, 31.2 h current smoker (76.6 kg); LA IM terminal half-life 5.6 weeks male, 11.5 weeks female (BMI 25.4, unsplit, 1.5-inch needle); 6.4 weeks for the base model in the building population. LA kinetics are absorption-limited (flip-flop). After the last LA injection, concentrations are predicted to fall below the 0.025 ug/mL quantification limit in about 12-14 months and below the PA-IC90 in about 7-9 months (median). Ctau-1 median 1.43 ug/mL (5th-95th 0.54-2.73) overall; 0.87 (0.32-1.98) in females with BMI >= 30; a 2-inch needle raises that subgroup's median by 11%.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD model. The efficacy reference is the in vitro protein-adjusted IC90 (PA-IC90) 0.166 ug/mL (total concentration), drawn on the simulated profiles (Fig. 6); the paper reports no concentration-response analysis. Not an opioid: no MEAC, ke0, tPeak or potency ratio.

## Covariates
Final: baseline body weight (power, allometric-like exponents estimated, 0.618 and 0.702, not the fixed 0.75 and 1; the fixed version gave comparable estimates but a worse OFV, P < .001); current smoking on CL/F; sex, BMI, split injection and needle length on KALA. Race and adult age were not significant. Effects on trough and peak were at most 32% (printed p. 4607 abstract). Weight scaling by fat-free mass, lean body mass or ideal weight fitted similarly; total body weight chosen for practicality. Albumin, bilirubin, HIV status and UGT1A1 effects were removed under the 15% refinement rule.

## Caveats
- Female and BMI >= 30 subjects slightly underpredicted in the pcVPC (and in external validation); small share of females in the building set (authors).
- No transgender subjects; shrinkage of KAoral 69.1% and F1 40%.
- Rifampin DDI data (study 117010 period 2) were excluded.
- Total, not unbound, plasma concentration; adults only (age 18+ ; weight range in Table 2 not re-read from an image).
- The "higher/lower" statements in the abstract are against the reference subject defined above (male, nonsmoker, unsplit, 1.5-inch needle, BMI 25.4, 76.6 kg), not against another study.
