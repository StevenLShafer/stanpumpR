# Pennell 2022 (MONEAD): dose-normalized antiseizure drug concentrations in pregnancy

## Study
Prospective observational cohort, 20 US sites, enrolment 19 Dec 2012 to 11 Feb 2016. Women 14-45 y with
epilepsy, IQ > 70, pregnant cohort enrolled before 20 weeks' gestation. Analysed: **326 pregnant women**
(median age 29, range 19-43) and **104 nonpregnant controls** (29, 16-43), on monotherapy or noninteracting
polytherapy. Pregnant women: visits in each trimester (second 21-27 wk, third 30-36 wk), at delivery (counted
as third trimester) and at 1.5-3, 6 and 9 months postpartum; controls 7 visits over 18 months. Convenience
samples at least 3 days after a dose change, about 60% taken 3-7 h after a dose. LC-MS assays (University of
Minnesota). End point: dose-normalized concentration (DNC) = concentration / total daily dose, reported in
µg/L/mg. **The nonpregnant baseline is the last postpartum sample, a median 38 (range 14-48) weeks after
delivery.** Repeated-measures ANOVA with Tukey tests within the pregnant cohort, plus linear mixed-effect models
against the controls (R 3.6.1, nlme).

Table 1 (PDF p 5, printed 374, rendered), women and samples, pregnant / control: carbamazepine 18 / 4 women
(101 / 28 samples); lacosamide 16 / 11 (67 / 58); lamotrigine 162 / 49 (891 / 277); levetiracetam 151 / 46
(821 / 261); oxcarbazepine 20 / 8 (111 / 37); topiramate 15 / 6 (53 / 26); zonisamide 22 / 9 (112 / 52).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD. The introduction cites earlier work that concentrations below 65% of the preconception baseline raise
seizure risk (p 2). Seizure outcomes are not reported here.

## Covariates
Pregnancy, gestational week (Table 2), estrogen-containing medication (lamotrigine, levetiracetam tested;
lamotrigine significant).

## Caveats
DNCs from random-time samples, not clearances; the authors plan formal PK modelling. The postpartum baseline is
late (median 38 weeks), and the pregnant cohort's postpartum DNCs differed from the controls' for unbound
carbamazepine and lacosamide (supplement eTable, 2026-10-27: unbound carbamazepine in its intercept, β2 -3.34,
P < .001, and slope, β3 0.05, P = .02; lacosamide in its slope only, β3 0.32, P = .01, intercept P = .07). Small numbers for every drug except lamotrigine and levetiracetam; not powered
for between-cohort comparisons (authors). Doses were higher in pregnancy than postpartum.
