# Goyal 2022: vancomycin popPK in pregnant women

## Study
- **Design:** retrospective TDM data from Texas Children's Hospital inpatients, January 2011 to May 2019.
- **Who:** pregnant women given IV vancomycin with at least one measurable level. Excluded: renal replacement
  therapy, vancomycin by another route, or dosing before admission.
- **Cohort, n = 34 (Table 1, p.3, image-confirmed), median (range):**
  - age 28 (17-38) y; height 163 (147-173) cm; total weight 74 (43-157) kg; BMI 28 (19-70);
  - gestation 27 (7-40) weeks: 2 first, 15 second and 17 third trimester;
  - serum creatinine 0.56 (0.27-1.97) mg/dL;
  - CrCL 176 (43-389) mL/min, Cockcroft-Gault, or modified Schwartz under 19 y, uncapped;
  - FFM 45 (30-60) kg, Janmahasatian, or Al-Sallami under 18 y.
- **Doses:** median total daily dose 3000 mg (IQR 2000-4000).
- **Samples:** 91, of which 9 were below the 5 mg/L LLOQ and excluded, though handled with M2 censoring. Most were
  troughs.
- **Assay:** VITROS enzyme immunoassay, range 5-50 mg/L, CV <6%.
- **Software:** Pumas 2.0, Laplacian with interaction. The base model was a non-pregnant two-compartment model with
  covariates changed to CrCL and FFM (Supplementary Table S1, not in the PDF).

## Model and parameters
Two compartments with first-order elimination. IIV was estimable only on CL; shrinkage on Vc, Vp and Q exceeded 90%.
No covariate beyond the structural CrCL and FFM terms was retained.

Q, Vp and the CrCL exponent were fixed to the non-pregnant model's values.

**Sensitivity analysis on capping CrCL.** Uncapped OFV 472, against 483 capped at 120 mL/min and 489 capped at
150 mL/min. Capping biased CL upward. FFM fitted better than total weight (12 OFV points, IIV about 8% lower).

**Comparison with the non-pregnant model** (p.6), for the typical subject (FFM 45 kg, CrCL 175):
- CL 7.64 against 9.9 L/h, which the authors call similar. Derived ratio 0.77.
- Vc 67.3 against 37.5 L, which they call "approximately 80% higher". The discussion prints both volumes in "L/h",
  a unit typo.
- The non-pregnant Vc of 37.5 L equals the fixed Vp of 37.5 L. With Table S1 unavailable, it cannot be checked
  whether this is a coincidence.

**Simulated AUC0-24** from the 34 patients' own regimens: geometric mean (IQR) 223 (170-273) ug.h/mL with the
pregnant model, against 226 (178-290) with the non-pregnant model (p.4). Abstract, text and table agree on every
estimate.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- **Targets:** the reference index is AUC/MIC > 400 (Rybak 2020). Clinical dosing at the time aimed at troughs of
  5-20 mg/L.
- **Predicted troughs:** median individual predicted trough 10.1 (IQR 7.0-14.5) mg/L.
- **AUC:** the calculated AUC0-24 was below 400 at an MIC of 1.
- **Binding:** vancomycin is quoted as 55% bound (p.2).
- No ke0. Not an opioid.

## Covariates
- **CrCL:** linear on CL (exponent fixed at 1), uncapped.
- **FFM:** exponent 0.75 on CL and Q, 1 on Vc and Vp.
- **Gestational age:** not retained; post-hoc eta plots showed no correlation.
- **Albumin:** not available.

## Caveats
- Small n, with mostly trough data.
- Only CL had estimable IIV. Vc is imprecise (CI 42-113 L). Vp and Q are fixed.
- Hospitalised women, with no measured GFR.
- One centre in the USA.
- Supplementary Table S1 is needed to reproduce the non-pregnant comparison.
- Infection status is not described.

## Verification against stanpumpR
- **Cohort:** 34 women (2/15/17 by trimester), median creatinine 0.56 mg/dL, CrCL 176 mL/min, uncapped. Table 1 and
  pp.3, 5. **matches**. The paper used modified Schwartz for patients under 19, not Cockcroft-Gault for everyone;
  this is immaterial to the median.
- **CL formula: discrepancy (transcription).** The registry writes "(FFM/45)". Table 2 has **(FFM/45)^0.75** on CL,
  and also on Q, which the registry leaves unscaled. This makes no difference at the typical FFM of 45 kg, which is
  the only point where the registry uses the numbers. Vc and Vp do scale linearly with FFM, as the registry implies.
- **Vc 67.35, Q 9.06 (fixed), Vp 37.5 (fixed) L:** **matches**.
- **Vss 67.35 + 37.5 = 105 L:** correct arithmetic at FFM 45 kg (104.85 L).
- **"Clearance at a given CrCL was close to the non-pregnant model":** the paper says "similar". The numbers are
  7.64 against 9.9 L/h, so the pregnant estimate is 23% lower. "Close" understates that, though the paper's own
  wording is similar.
- **AUC 223 vs 226 mg.h/L:** **matches** (ug.h/mL = mg.h/L).
- **IIV on CL only, mostly troughs:** **matches**.
- **The 1.5 GFR factor (docs/pregnancy.md:80):** the paper gives no factor. The registry's reasoning is its own:
  CrCL in pregnancy is about 1.5 times a non-pregnant woman's, and Goyal's CL is linear in CrCL at near
  non-pregnant CL per unit CrCL. That reasoning is consistent with the paper's findings: CL is linear in uncapped
  CrCL, the cohort's median CrCL is 176, and AUC is unchanged. **matches** as an interpretation.
- **Thomson-model numbers (8.01 L/h; Vss 104 L at 74 kg):** repo computations, not in this paper.
