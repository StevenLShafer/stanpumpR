# Lee 2018: bioequivalence of two 0.5 mg lobeglitazone (Duvie) tablets

## Study
Open-label, randomized, single-dose, two-period crossover bioequivalence study (PDF p2-4, printed 427-429).
- 28 healthy Korean men (38 screened); age 23.1 ± 2.5 y, height 174.1 ± 5.0 cm, weight 68.6 ± 8.2 kg
  (median 67.9, range 51.9-94.4), BMI 22.6 ± 2.3 kg/m² (Table 1, PDF p4). All 28 completed.
- Test: new harder-tablet formulation of Duvie 0.5 mg lobeglitazone sulfate (for export); reference: the
  originally approved Duvie 0.5 mg tablet (Chong Kun Dang). Single oral dose with 240 mL water after ≥10 h fast;
  7-day washout.
- Plasma (EDTA) at 0, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 1, 1.5, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12, 24, 48 h.
- Assay: LC-MS/MS (lobeglitazone-d3 internal standard), calibration 1-250 ng/mL. Total plasma concentration.
- NCA in Phoenix WinNonlin 6.3 (linear trapezoidal; AUCinf = AUClast + Clast/λz). BE by ANOVA on log AUClast
  and Cmax, 90% CI within 0.80-1.25. Sponsored by the manufacturer.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None measured. Serum glucose stayed 81.43-100.43 mg/dL; no hypoglycaemia (p7). No PD model, EC50 or
exposure-response data.

## Covariates
None examined.

## Caveats
- Healthy young Korean men only; single 0.5 mg dose; no women, patients, elderly or organ impairment.
- Salt vs free-base basis of the apparent clearance not stated.
- Industry-sponsored formulation-bridging study; the PK is descriptive.
