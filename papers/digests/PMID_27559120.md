# Biedenbach 2016: ceftaroline vs ceftriaxone MICs, AWARE respiratory isolates

## Study
In vitro surveillance (AWARE programme), 2012-14, 39 countries outside the USA: Asia-Pacific (APAC), Europe, Latin
America, Africa-Middle East (AFME). 13 009 respiratory isolates (abstract says 13005): S. aureus 7213 (4387 MRSA),
S. pneumoniae 4719, H. influenzae 753 (124 beta-lactamase positive), M. catarrhalis 324; mostly sputum, endotracheal
aspirate and BAL (PDF p1-2). Identification by MALDI-TOF; CLSI broth microdilution at one central laboratory (IHMA);
CLSI and EUCAST interpretation; QC strains in range. Funded by AstraZeneca (ceftaroline's maker). No PK.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
In vitro MIC only; no MBC, no PK/PD target, no clinical end point. Breakpoints quoted: CLSI H. influenzae ceftaroline
0.5 and ceftriaxone 2 mg/L; EUCAST 0.03 and 0.125 mg/L (PDF p2). Potency comparison: ceftaroline 16-fold more potent
than ceftriaxone against MSSA and S. pneumoniae by MIC90, >=16-fold against MRSA, 4- to 8-fold against M. catarrhalis,
similar against H. influenzae (abstract).

## Covariates
Region, methicillin status, penicillin phenotype (S. pneumoniae), beta-lactamase status (H. influenzae).

## Caveats
Surveillance isolates, US excluded; sponsor-funded with a sponsor employee as author. APAC H. influenzae had higher MICs
(beta-lactamase positive 25%; 43.8% in Taiwan). Table 2, Latin America ceftriaxone S. pneumoniae (all), 0.12 column
prints "3528 (67.6)", a typesetting error (from the cumulative percentages the count is 28, derived). Abstract gives
13005 isolates, Methods 13 009.
