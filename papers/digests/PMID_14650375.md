# Björkman 2003: simplifying PBPK models of fentanyl and pethidine

## Study
- Methods paper: whole-body perfusion-limited (and, for fentanyl, diffusion-limited) PBPK models with 11-12
  organ/tissue compartments, successively simplified (formal lumping into lungs, liver, pre-hepatic, rapid lean,
  slow lean, fat; two- or three-kp models), each simulated per patient in MATLAB 6.0 and compared with measured
  arterial plasma concentrations (median prediction error and median absolute prediction error, Wilcoxon).
- Pethidine clinical data (Redke and Björkman 1999 abstract, Malmö): 8 patients (thoracotomy for pneumothorax n=6,
  3 M 3 F, 25-56 y; jaw surgery n=2, 19 and 35 y), each given pethidine base 26-44 mg IV over 2 min twice, 12 h apart.
  Radial arterial samples 1, 5, 10, 30 min, 1, 2, 4, 6, 8, 12 h. GLC-NPD. Blood:plasma ratio 0.91-1.04. Five
  further patients given midazolam were excluded (lower clearance). Weights not listed individually.
- Fentanyl clinical data: Hudson 1986, 10 aortic-surgery patients (55-80 y), 100 µg/kg over 2 min, radial arterial.
- Kp values for pethidine: rat steady-state (Gabrielsson 1986, protein-binding corrected) or calculated (Davis and
  Mapleson 1993); human lung kp 28.5 (Persson 1988) as alternative.
- Hepatic intrinsic clearance from the patients' own blood clearance and estimated hepatic blood flow.

## Model and parameters
The paper publishes **no compartmental parameter set** (no V1, no intercompartmental clearances, no rate constants)
for pethidine. Three-compartment fits (PCNONLIN, 1/pred^2) were done to the arterial data and only summaries are
given:

- Lumped time constants (rat kp): rapid lean 14 min, slow lean 93 min, pre-hepatic 14 min (p. 298).
- Fentanyl (not pethidine): allometric CL prediction 720 vs measured 784 mL/min; Vdss from fits 3.0-7.5 L/kg.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- No PD measured. **No MEAC; no measured ke0.**
- PBPK-derived brain equilibration half-life for pethidine: 13 min (rat kp) or 1.6 min (calculated kp) (Discussion
  p. 305). Cited animal values: 6.3 min cerebral equilibration in sheep; ke0 half-life 6-7 min on evoked potentials
  in goats. Author: only direct human measurement can settle it.
- Fentanyl: PBPK brain equilibration half-life 6.6 min vs EEG t½ke0 6.4, 4.7, 5.4 min cited. **No potency ratio.**

## Covariates
Physiological inputs from sex, weight, height (organ weights via lean body mass, Hallynck formula; flows by sex and
age). No covariate model in the compartmental sense.

## Caveats
- Small n (8) with exclusion of midazolam co-recipients; source data only in an abstract.
- Renal excretion ignored (<10% at normal urine pH; urine pH 4.7-7.4 here).
- Arterial sampling: early concentrations and central volume differ from venous-based literature.
- Pethidine hepatic clearance could not be predicted allometrically; model used the patients' own median CL10.
