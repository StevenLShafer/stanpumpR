# Knorr 2001: montelukast 4 mg chewable in children 2-5 y, population PK vs adults

## Study
- Open, one-period, single-dose, multicentre (Chile, Peru, USA; 1997) population PK study (PDF p2, printed 613).
- 15 children aged 2-5 y with physician-diagnosed asthma, otherwise healthy. Table I (PDF p4, printed 615): age
  median 4 (2-5) y [2 y: 5, 3 y: 1, 4 y: 2, 5 y: 7]; 7 male (47%); weight median 17 (12-22) kg; height median 106
  (84-118) cm; 6 Caucasian, 3 Black, 6 Mestizo.
- Dose: single 4 mg chewable tablet (CT), fasting from midnight, chewed with 50 mL water; snack at 2 h, meals at 4 and
  8 h.
- Sampling: two randomised sparse schedules of 4 samples each (A: predose, 1.5, 4, 12 h; B: predose, 2, 8, 24 h),
  3 mL each, sodium heparin, plasma at -70 °C protected from light (PDF p3).
- Comparator: historical adult study (ref 27), 16 healthy men 18-45 y, single 10 mg film-coated tablet (FCT), samples
  predose and 0.5, 1, 2, 4, 6, 8, 12, 16, 24 h.
- Assay: HPLC with fluorescence detection, LLOQ 3 ng/mL; values below LLOQ set to 0 (PDF p3).
- Analysis: nonlinear mixed-effects one-compartment model, first-order absorption and elimination, S-plus 3.4
  (nlme), fitted separately to the paediatric and adult data; t1/2 from a linear mixed-effects fit to log
  concentrations at the last 2 points per child / last 5 per adult. Primary end point AUCpop; GMR 90% CI against a
  prespecified 0.5-2.0.
- Dose choice: weighted linear regression of AUC on weight from five earlier studies (Figure 1, PDF p3), assuming
  dose proportionality and a 20% higher AUC for CT than FCT.

derived quantities (rendered pages):

- Adult AUC0-inf by conventional methods gave GMR 1.11 (0.94-1.31) (p5); adult t1/2 by conventional methods 4.6 h
  (p5) against 4.09 h from the mixed model (Table III).
- CV of Cmax 53% (child) vs 76% (adult); of tmax 56% vs 71% (p5).
- Adult t1/2 range in Merck's own studies 2.7-5.5 h (data on file, p7). Adult 2 mg FCT trough 0.6 ng/mL; 10 mg FCT
  9.7 ng/mL (p7).
- Highest tolerated exposures cited (ref 34): AUC0-inf up to 173,000 ng·h/mL, Cmax up to 19.5 µg/mL (p7).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD measured; exposure matching (AUC) was the dose-selection criterion. Authors note trough has not been shown to
track efficacy, and that adult 2 mg FCT (trough 0.6 ng/mL) was already effective (p7). No effective concentration,
ke0 or potency ratio. Not an opioid paper.

## Covariates
Weight only, in the pre-study regression of AUC on weight (Figure 1); no covariate model in the population fit. The
"shorter t1/2" and "larger Cmax / shorter tmax" in children are against the **historical adult FCT group**, and the
authors attribute the Cmax/tmax and variability differences to the dosage form (CT vs FCT) rather than age (p7).

## Caveats
- n = 15, sparse sampling (7-8 children per time point); paediatric t1/2 from two points per child.
- Historical adult control (healthy men, different formulation, different study); CT vs FCT confounds age.
- Model parameters themselves unpublished here (ref 28 is an abstract).
- Sponsored and largely authored by Merck.
