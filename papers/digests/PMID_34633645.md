# Moes 2022: tocilizumab PopPK in ICU COVID-19

## Study
Single-centre (Leiden UMC), open-label, observational PopPK plus descriptive PD study, Dec 2020 - Mar 2021. n = 29 adult ICU patients with PCR-confirmed COVID-19 on high-flow oxygen or ventilation. Mean age 64 y (45-80), 72.4% male, mean weight 96.4 kg (58-130) (PDF p4, printed 234). All received dexamethasone 6 mg daily (max 10 days) and a single IV tocilizumab dose of 8 mg/kg (max 800 mg) within 24 h of ICU admission. One patient was double-dosed by accident; one received the dose in two parts within 12 h. 139 EDTA-plasma samples (mean 4.8 per patient, range 1-11), day 1 to day 20, leftovers from haematology tubes. Assay: ELISA (Sanquin), LLOQ 0.2 ug/mL, ULOQ 250 ug/mL, precision 8%, accuracy 93%; the paper calls the analyte free tocilizumab in plasma. sIL-6R by Quantikine ELISA in 80 samples. NONMEM 7.4.4, FOCE-I, PsN 5.0.0; pcVPC 500 sims, bootstrap n = 1000.

## Model and parameters
One-compartment disposition with parallel linear (CL) and Michaelis-Menten elimination (Fig. 1, Table 2 model 5; final model = base model, no covariates retained). A two-compartment version was unstable (RSE > 1000%, Table 2 model 3). Table 3 (PDF p9, printed 239) values are final-model estimates:

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Descriptive only; no ke0, EC50, tPeak or hysteresis model. CRP fell rapidly after dosing and stayed low >= 10 days in all patients; in 8 of 29 CRP rose again after day 10 despite detectable drug (4 of the 8 with a documented secondary infection). sIL-6R rose after dosing. All patients stayed above 1 ug/mL (a receptor-saturation threshold taken from RA data by the authors, citing Nishimoto) for >= 15 days. Simulated fixed 200 mg kept all patients above the 1 and 5 ug/mL thresholds for >= 7 days. Not an opioid; no MEAC.

## Covariates
Tested: sex, age, weight, BMI, BSA, height, creatinine, eGFR, urea, albumin, LDH, ASAT, ALAT, GGT, bilirubin, CRP (also time-varying). None significant on CL or Vd (all p > 0.05). Weight as power on CL: exponent 0.002, no OFV gain. Posterior power to detect an exponent of 0.5 was 82%, 99% for 0.8.

## Caveats
n = 29, one centre, all on dexamethasone; sparse first-day sampling (hence no peripheral compartment); observational, no efficacy end point; text compares with RA/CRS literature (CL 0.25, 0.29 and 0.5 L/day) and states CL here is faster than those - comparator is other studies' RA/CRS populations. Simulations to 600 mg fixed dosing assume the model above.
