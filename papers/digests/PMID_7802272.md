# Jacobs 1995: aging increases midazolam hypnotic sensitivity

## Study
- 39 CABG patients, age 39-77 y, double-blind; 33 analysed (median age 66, range 39-77; ages 39-48 n=4, 50-59 n=8, 62-69 n=12, 70-77 n=9). Midazolam 1-4 mg IV given earlier for sedation as needed; no other premedication.
- CACI midazolam to a random set plasma concentration of 50, 100, 150 or 200 ng/mL, held 10 min, using the group's own 3-compartment model (refs 5, 6; parameters not given here). Arterial (radial) samples at 5 and 10 min; GC-ECD assay in plasma (total).
- End point: at 10 min, blinded assessor judged response (eyes opened/purposeful reaction within 30 s) to loud verbal request. Patients whose 10-min concentration differed from 5-min by >30% excluded (6 patients). Measured 10-min concentration (Css) taken as effect-site concentration; range 64-796, median 279 ng/mL (Table 1). Measured concentrations ran well above the CACI setpoints (model underpredicted; attributed to overestimated central volume).

## Model and parameters
Logistic regression of P(no response) on ln(Css, ng/mL) and age (y); ln(C) fitted better than C. Eq 3: P = 1/(1+exp(-(b0 + 3.054 ln C + 0.1115 Age))), b0 about -23.96 (text layer garbled); chi2 = 15.5, P = 0.0004; ln(C) P = 0.0052, age P = 0.034.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- Cp50 (verbal command, steady-state plasma assumed equal to effect site) falls with age, ~595 ng/mL at 40 y to ~135 ng/mL at 80 y. Age range studied 39-77, few <50 y (n=4), so young-end estimates are uncertain (Fig 2 CIs).
- No ke0 estimated. Literature t1/2ke0 for midazolam (EEG) quoted as 0.9 min (Mandema) to 5.6 min (Buhrer); if >~3 min, effect site would lag plasma at 10 min and Cp50 would be overstated. Authors recommend effect-site targeting in future.
- Opioid audit: not an opioid paper.
- Comparators: pharmacokinetics claimed "not markedly" age-dependent apart from lower clearance; no PK values reported.

## Covariates
Age (continuous) on Cp50, as above.

## Caveats
Cp50 uses measured total plasma, not effect-site; equilibration assumed; CABG patients on cardiac drugs and pre-treated midazolam 1-4 mg; CACI poorly predictive here.
