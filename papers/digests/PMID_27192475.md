# Liou 2016: midazolam-alfentanil response surfaces during combined EGD and colonoscopy

## Study
- 33 analysed adults (40 enrolled, 7 excluded: 5 incomplete records, 2 neurologic disorders), age 48.3 ± 9.6 y (enrolment cohort), ASA I-II, 18 men, BMI 20-28 (21.77 ± 2.21) (PDF p4, printed 302). Patients under 65 y, no sedative/opioid/alcohol history. Consent waived by the IRB.
- Combined EGD then colonoscopy under bolus midazolam (initial 0.03-0.04 mg/kg) and alfentanil (initial 6-9 ng/kg [as printed; probably mcg/kg, see Caveats]), supplementary doses about half the initial dose. Mean cumulative dose 0.048 ± 0.016 mg/kg midazolam and 0.013 ± 0.005 mg/kg alfentanil (PDF p4).
- Phases: EGD (3.0 ± 1.5 min), colonoscopy (6.5 ± 2.6 min), intersession (2.7 ± 1.4 min). 218 concentration pairs: 68 EGD, 75 colonoscopy, 75 intersession. Ce range 1-76 ng/mL midazolam, 5-80 ng/mL alfentanil.
- End point: OAA/S scored every 2 min by two observers (Table 1). Binary response: OAA/S below 4 (intersession) or below 2 (EGD, colonoscopy) = "loss of response".
- Ce computed offline in TIVA trainer 9.1: Maitre alfentanil PK, Zomorodi midazolam PK, t½ke0 from Scott 1985 EEG (alfentanil) and Bührer 1990 EEG (midazolam); neither t½ke0 is printed. Fitting in MATLAB R2013a (fmincon, bootstrap), minimising -2LL; AICc and Spearman rho.

## Model and parameters

- Other printed values: Minto beta_U50 0.1 (intersession), 1.3 (colonoscopy), 1.4 (EGD) (text on PDF p4 gives 1.3-1.4 for the two painful phases and 0.124 intersession); Scaled/Fixed hierarchy PreOI 1.37/1.5 (EGD) and 1.52/1.93 (colonoscopy) from the text. ## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- Opioid audit (alfentanil): no MEAC or minimum analgesic concentration is reported. The C50alf values are Ce for 50% of patients reaching the sedation end point in a response-surface model (OAA/S below 4 or below 2 in combination with midazolam), not an analgesic concentration. Free vs total and whole blood vs plasma not stated; concentrations are model Ce from a simulator, not assays. The full Greco and Minto C50alf (164-288 ng/mL, Tables 2-4) are outside the observed range (5-80 ng/mL) and the authors attribute them to an opioid failing to produce hypnosis alone. A bolus of 2 mg would be needed for a plasma concentration above 250 ng/mL and a Ce of 160 ng/mL in a 70 kg man (PDF p6, printed 304, derived by the authors by simulation).
- ke0 / t½ke0 / tPeak: none estimated. t½ke0 values come from the Scott 1985 (alfentanil) and Bührer 1990 (midazolam) EEG studies via the simulator and are not printed. The discussion (printed 306) quotes midazolam t½ke0 2.6-4.2 min (Mould 1995) and notes that the phases (about 3-6.5 min) preclude steady state; they attribute onset and offset to redistribution.
- Potency/equianalgesic ratios: none.
- Interaction: strong synergy during EGD and colonoscopy (Minto beta_U50 1.3-1.4), additive in the intersession (beta_U50 0.124, alpha 0.393; reduced Greco alpha' 0.028-0.04 close to additive). Figure 1 (PDF p6) shows the 50% isoboles.
- Safety observations: one patient desaturated (SpO2 80-90% for about 2 min) at Ce 48 ng/mL midazolam and 45 ng/mL alfentanil, peak Ce 59 ng/mL both (printed 302).

## Covariates
None modelled (no age, weight or sex effect on the PD parameters).

## Caveats
- Binary sedation end point, so the clinical share of analgesia, anxiolysis and hypnosis cannot be separated (authors, printed 307).
- Non-steady-state Ce, multiple boluses, small observation count (218 pairs from 33 patients); parameter CVs are bootstrap-derived and several are 50-80%.
- Concentration range limited; extremes of the surface are extrapolated.
- Dose of alfentanil is printed as "6 to 9 ng/kg" for the initial dose (PDF p2); the cumulative dose is 0.013 mg/kg (13 mcg/kg). The initial dose unit looks like a misprint for mcg/kg; not settled here.
- Parameters are for sedation depth in Asian adults under 65; not an analgesic or MEAC study.
