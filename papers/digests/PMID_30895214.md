# Diekema 2019: 20 years of SENTRY S. aureus susceptibility

## Study
- SENTRY Antimicrobial Surveillance Program, 1997-2016: 191 460 clinical S. aureus isolates from 427 centres in 45 countries (North America, Latin America, Europe, Asia-Pacific); consecutive episodes of bacteraemia, pneumonia in hospitalised patients, intra-abdominal, urinary and skin/skin-structure infection.
- Central-laboratory reference broth microdilution (CLSI M07), CLSI breakpoints (FDA or EUCAST where CLSI absent); CLSI 2018 criteria in Table 4.
- 77 146 (40.3%) MRSA overall; 26.8% Europe to 47.0% North America; peak 44.2% in 2005-2008, 39.0% in 2013-2016.

- Vancomycin MIC > 1 mg/L in < 3% overall, < 1% in 2013-2016; > 99.9% of MSSA and MRSA at ≤ 2 mg/L; one isolate at 8 mg/L in 20 years; no MIC creep (p S49, S50).
- No clindamycin MIC distribution or MIC50/90 is given; only % susceptible.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None (in vitro susceptibility only). Not an opioid; no MEAC, ke0 or potency.

## Covariates
Not applicable. MRSA proportion higher in nosocomial isolates, age > 80 y (48.0%), pneumonia and UTI (Table 1).

## Caveats
Sentinel, not population-based; centres changed over time; Africa unrepresented; no molecular typing; clindamycin susceptibility reported only as a percentage (inducible resistance testing not described); Table 2 and Table 4 text layers are misaligned.
