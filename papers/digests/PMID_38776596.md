# Liao 2024: CYP2D6 metabolizer status, paroxetine exposure and a therapeutic reference range (PMEDA cohort)

## Study
8-week multicentre single-drug prospective cohort (PMEDA consortium, 17 Chinese hospitals; ChiCTR2000038462),
enrolment March 2021 to April 2023. Han Chinese outpatients aged 18-65 with MDD, GAD or panic disorder (DSM-5),
drug-naive or after a >=2-week washout, no CYP2D6 inducers/inhibitors. 1056 enrolled; 921 in the primary
(concentration) analysis: MDD 633, PD 72, GAD 216 (Fig. 1); 841 with 4-week efficacy/ADR data, 805 at 8 weeks.
Paroxetine hydrochloride IR (Seroxat) 10 mg/day for week 1, then 20-40 mg/day; mean daily dose 20.0-23.3 mg/day by
group (Table 1). Mean age ~41 y (UM 34.4), ~70% female, weight ~62 kg, BMI ~22.5 (Table 1, PDF page 8 image).
Sampling: trough, 20-24 h after the last dose, at week 4 after >=10 days of stable dosing; dried blood spots of
plasma and whole blood; paroxetine in plasma by LC-MS/MS (LLOQ 0.1 ng/mL; LOD stated as 2 ng/mL).
CYP2D6 genotyped by MALDI-TOF (14 alleles) plus TaqMan copy number; activity score (AS) categories UM >2.25,
EM 1.25-2.25, IM 0-1.25, PM 0. Efficacy: % improvement on HAMD-17/HAMA/PDSS; responder >=50% improvement.
ADR: TESS item >2. Meta-analysis of 12 studies (1570 participants) for dose-adjustment ratios.

## Model and parameters

smoking, drinking).

35/40/143/241; non-East-Asian 62/68/131/159 (p. 10-11, Fig. 4).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- Not an opioid; no MEAC, ke0, t½ke0, tPeak or hysteresis; no potency ratio.
- Therapeutic reference range (TRR), defined as plasma trough Css with response unlikely below and tolerability
  falling above: 31.95-79.65 ng/mL for depressive or anxiety disorders. Within range: responder OR 1.41 [1.05,
  1.88], 59.9% vs 51.2%; ADR OR 0.56 [0.32, 0.98], 5.84% vs 9.94% (Fig. 3b-c). Response measured at 4 weeks
  (matched to the 4-week concentration). MDD alone: 31.55-52.5 ng/mL.
- Discussion (p. 13) cites ~85% SERT occupancy at 30 ng/mL and ~90% at 80 ng/mL (ref 44), and the earlier
  guideline range of 20-65 ng/mL.
- ADR risk rose as AS fell below 1.63 (OR per SD 0.38, P = 0.005).

## Covariates
CYP2D6 metabolizer status / AS (above); sex interaction (in women IM 1.23x and UM 0.24x EM; no significant effect in
men); CNV deletion; ethnicity in the meta-analysis. Daily dose as covariate in sensitivity model.

## Caveats
- TRR from ROC cut-offs in a single observational cohort (Youden index), with adjustment only for age and sex;
  response rates were high and secondary analyses unadjusted for multiplicity (authors).
- Han Chinese adults 18-65 at low doses (mean ~23 mg/day); few PMs (6) and UMs (5).
- Concentrations from dried plasma spots, not conventional serum; LOD (2 ng/mL) printed larger than LLOQ (0.1
  ng/mL), which is odd but does not affect the range.
- Some patients with intolerable ADRs declined sampling, which may bias the upper limit.
- Authors disclose a pending patent on predicting paroxetine outcome.
