# Kim 2012: lacosamide in healthy Korean men

## Study
- Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, parallel-group, dose-escalation phase I trial at Seoul National
  University Hospital (NCT01450111). Healthy Korean men, 20-45 y, BMI 19-28 (p. 173).
- 3:1 active:placebo in three groups: 50 mg single dose (A), 100 mg (B) or 200 mg (C) single dose on day 1, then
  B and C twice daily days 5-8 plus the morning of day 9. 12 on lacosamide per group (Table 1).
- 36 received lacosamide: age 27.4 (5.7) y, weight 70.1 (6.6) kg, height 175 (5.1) cm (p. 174, 177).
- Plasma sampling 0-96 h after the single dose and over day 9; urine collections. HPLC-MS/MS (calibration
  0.005-20 ug/mL). Non-compartmental analysis in SAS; population PK in NONMEM VI, pooled with 12 Caucasian men
  from a 100 mg study (ref 11).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None measured (authors state pharmacodynamics not investigated). Not an opioid paper.

## Covariates
CL/F = 1.92 (CLcr/107)^0.534 L/h; V/F = 41.6 (BSA/1.856)^1.37 L; ka = 6.47 /h (Table 2 footnote).

## Caveats
- Healthy young men only (n = 36 active, plus 12 Caucasians in the pooled model); narrow CLcr range.
- ka poorly identified (IIV 181%, wide bootstrap interval).
- Sponsor-funded (UCB); three authors UCB employees.
