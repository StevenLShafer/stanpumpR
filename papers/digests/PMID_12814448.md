# Jonsson 2003: levosimendan PopPK in NYHA III-IV heart failure

## Study
- Two efficacy trials pooled, 193 treated patients, PK in 190 (Methods, PDF p2-3; Table 1, PDF p2). Study 1: stable CHF, dose-finding, 95 patients on levosimendan (plus placebo 36, dobutamine 20); loading bolus (10-min infusion) 3, 6, 12, 24 or 36 ug/kg, then the same hourly dose as a 24 h infusion 0.05, 0.1, 0.2, 0.4 or 0.6 ug/kg/min. Study 2: stable and unstable CHF, 98 on levosimendan (placebo 48); 6 ug/kg load over 5 min, then ascending rates 0.1, 0.2, 0.3, 0.4 ug/kg/min (55 min steps), maintenance to 24 h, then 6-48 h total.
- 1793 concentrations (768 and 1025); about 10 samples per patient over 0-60 h; 2 patients dropped and 41 concentrations omitted (PDF p3). Re-adding the omitted points changed estimates only marginally but raised residual error from 25% to 33%.
- Population: age 30-84 y (mean 60.6), 161 male / 32 female, weight 46-130 kg (the Table 2 range printed alongside CRCL is ambiguous in the text layer), NYHA III 157, IV 36; digoxin 106; beta-blockers 32. Creatinine clearance 46-130 mL/min (Cockcroft-Gault).
- Assay: HPLC, plasma, 5-3000 ng/mL linear, LOQ 5 ng/mL (PDF p2). Total plasma concentration (97-98% protein bound per introduction).
- Software: NONMEM V, first-order estimation; Xpose 2.0 for GAM covariate search; backward deletion at DeltaOFV 10.8 (p < 0.001).

## Model and parameters
Two-compartment, zero-order infusion input, first-order elimination (PDF p6). Exponential (proportional) IIV; log-transformed residual error. Covariance modelled for CL, Q and V1. Parameters for a 76 kg patient (Table 5, PDF p6, confirmed on page image).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None: no ke0, t1/2ke0, tPeak, EC50, MEAC or concentration-effect relationship is reported (haemodynamic effects of the two trials are in refs 14 and 15). Not an opioid.

## Covariates
Final model: body weight on CL and V1 only (DeltaOFV 35.9 and 11.9 on deletion, Table 4). Rejected by backward deletion: sex (on CL 7.5, Q 8.5, V1 1.2), digoxin (CL 5.4, Q 0.3), beta-blockers on V1 (0.7), age on Q (0.3). Not influential: age, race, hepatic function, creatinine clearance (46-130 mL/min), NYHA class, aetiology.

## Caveats
- NYHA III-IV only, 32 women, 2 Asian patients; weight 46-130 kg.
- First-order estimation; 41 observations excluded; residual error 25% (33% with all data).
- Huge IIV on Q and V2 and wide RSEs on the weight slopes.
- Only total plasma levosimendan measured (active metabolite OR-1896 not covered here).
