# Bullingham 1980: buprenorphine kinetics

## Study
- 24 "fit" patients for elective total hip replacement, Oxford, < 75 yr; the same cohort as McQuay 1980 (Br J Anaesth 52:1013) plus 4 who did not complete all phases (PDF p.3, printed 669).
- Table I (PDF p.2, printed 668, mean ± SEM): phase I n = 24, age 64.5 ± 1.6 yr, 14 M : 10 F, weight 67.7 ± 2.4 kg; phase II IV n = 10, 67.5 ± 6.5 yr, 6:4, 67.5 ± 2.1 kg; phase II IM n = 11, 62.1 ± 2.6 yr, 7:4, 66.5 ± 3.6 kg.
- Anaesthesia: diazepam premed, thiopental 4 mg/kg, pancuronium 0.1 mg/kg, N2O:O2 2:1 + halothane 0.5%, normocapnia.
- Phase I: 0.3 mg IV over 1.0 min (time zero = end of injection), arterial samples 2-180 min (14 times), under anaesthesia/into recovery. Phase II at 180 min: second 0.3 mg IV (n = 10) or IM vastus lateralis (n = 11), awake postoperative; arterial samples 2-180 min.
- Assay: RIA in heparinized plasma (antibody to N-hemisuccinyl-norbuprenorphine–BSA; Bartlett 1980); intra/inter-assay CV < 5%; metabolite cross-reaction said not significant. Total plasma drug; plasma/RBC ratio ~1 (unpublished).
- Analysis: individual sum-of-exponentials fits (stripping, least squares); first-dose terminal tail subtracted from phase II data; Wagner equations for derived parameters; IM availability by AUC 0-180 (spline) + extrapolation.

Plasma levels (Table II, PDF p.3, printed 669, mean ± SD, ng/mL): phase I 18.62 at 2 min (n = 23), 4.60 at 10, 1.59 at 30, 0.85 at 60, 0.49 at 120, 0.37 at 180 min; phase II IV 14.62 at 2 (n = 9), 3.15 at 10, 0.77 at 60, 0.38 at 180; IM 3.56 at 2 (n = 8), 3.61 at 5, 3.31 at 10, 0.79 at 60, 0.36 at 180.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- No PD measured in this paper; clinical effects deferred to McQuay 1980.
- Discussion (PDF p.6, printed 672): effect far outlasts the plasma level, with no direct relationship between plasma level and effect; poor IM availability did not imply poor analgesia. No numbers given here.
- **MEAC:** none. **ke0 / t½ke0 / tPeak / hysteresis:** none quantified. **Potency ratio:** none.

## Covariates
Anaesthesia vs awake (same patients, phase I vs phase II IV): clearance about 30% lower under anaesthesia (text p.671; derived 901.3/1275 = 0.71), plasma levels higher up to 30 min (p < 0.025), attributed to lower initial volume. No weight, age or sex covariates.

## Caveats
- 3 h sampling only, so terminal half-life (2-3 h) is an estimate (authors).
- Phase II fits depend on subtracting the first dose's extrapolated tail (~0.4 ng/mL at 180 min).
- Phase I under halothane; hepatic extraction near 1 (CL ~ hepatic blood flow), so anaesthesia effects matter.
- IM availability: 4 of 11 poor (40-60%); IM parameters from 7.
- Elderly surgical patients, 24 at most; total drug only.
