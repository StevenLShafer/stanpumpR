# Ibarra 2023: population PK of high-dose methotrexate in adults

## Study
Retrospective analysis of anonymised routine-care data from 101 US community oncology practices (Guardian Research Network). 243 adults (108 F, 135 M; ALL 14, NHL 173, other 56), 693 HDMTX courses (Table 1, printed p664). Age 60 (18-88) y, weight 79.9 (43.9-161.6) kg, height 170.2 (142.2-200.7) cm, BSA 1.92 (1.43-2.72) m2, baseline serum creatinine 0.74 (0.20-1.78) mg/dL, baseline albumin 3.3 (1.9-4.7) g/dL (Table 1 Panel A, "All patients"). Doses 0.5-13.4 g/m2 as 3-, 4- or 24-h infusions (466 courses short, <=4 h; 227 long, 24 h). Plasma MTX sampled up to 400 h; only samples up to 100 h used for modelling; mean 3 samples per course (1-8); BLQ/ALQ handled with M4; concentrations after glucarpidase excluded. Missing creatinine imputed (0.92 M / 0.77 F mg/dL, or the patient's own mean; linear interpolation for time-varying values). Software: MonolixSuite 2020R1, SAEM with MCMC (not NONMEM). External evaluation on Kawakatsu (135 pts, 454 courses) and Barreto (80 pts, 80 courses) data. Exposure-toxicity analysis by logistic regression.

## Model and parameters
Three-compartment, first-order elimination, exponential residual error. No IOV supported; IIV covers between-patient and between-course variability. Weight scaling: 3/4 power on CL, Q2 and Q3 (the text says "CL, Q1 and Q2", a labelling slip), linear on V1, V2, V3, centred on 70 kg. BSA did not improve the fit (dOFV 9) vs weight (dOFV -7).

CL_i = CLpop x (WT/70)^0.75 x (SCRpop/SCR_t)^betaSCRt x (ALB/4)^betaALB (printed p663). SCR is time-varying; ALB is the patient's average baseline value. The text writes the albumin reference as "4 mg/dL"; Table 1 reports albumin in g/dL (a unit slip).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No effect-site model, ke0, MEAC or potency ratio (not an opioid). Toxicity link (Table 4, printed p667): model-estimated dose-normalised concentrations at 24 h and 48 h (C24/D, C48/D, umol/L per g/m2) are higher in courses with stage 2/3 AKI. Odds ratios for stage 2/3 AKI: short infusions C24/D 1.16 (1.09-1.24), C48/D 1.57 (1.25-2.03); long infusions 1.08 (1.02-1.14), 1.72 (1.36-2.24). No association with AUC. Stage 2/3 AKI in 62 of 693 courses (8.8%). Hypoalbuminaemia: predicted CL reductions of 15, 25 and 34% at albumin 3.0, 2.5 and 2.0 g/dL (printed p668; derived check from the equation: 15.7, 24.4, 33.8%).

## Covariates
Retained: body weight (allometric), time-varying serum creatinine, albumin on CL. Tested and dropped for low clinical impact: infusion length (CL -12% for shorter infusions), prior AKI (-8%), age; also platins, TKIs, diuretics, bilirubin. Volumes: weight only. Covariates explain 39% of IIV on CL.

## Caveats
Real-world sparse data, imputed creatinine, high residual variability. External validation: a priori rBias -3.73% / rRMSE 66.4% (Kawakatsu) and 6.78% / 84.1% (Barreto); a posteriori rRMSE 17.6% and 57.7%, so predictive performance is centre-dependent. Data truncated at 100 h. Plasma MTX in umol/L in the paper.
