# Sweeney 2017: in vitro MIC, MBC and time-kill of oritavancin and comparators against S. aureus

## Study
In vitro (PDF p1-2, printed 121-122). Funded by The Medicines Company, which markets oritavancin; three of the
authors are its employees. Isolates came from the Micromyx repository, ATCC and NARSA: **12 MSSA and 15 MRSA**,
including ATCC 29213 (MSSA), ATCC 43300 (MRSA) and USA100-800 pulsed-field types. Drugs: oritavancin, dalbavancin,
telavancin, daptomycin, linezolid (MRSA only), tedizolid, vancomycin and ceftaroline. Meropenem, nafcillin and
**cefazolin were tested against MSSA only** (cefazolin from USP, lot LOC345).

MIC: CLSI broth microdilution in CAMHB, read at 16-20 h, or at 24 h for vancomycin. Duplicate independent inocula.
MBC (CLSI M26-A): 10-µL aliquots from the MIC well and the 3 wells above it. The MBC is the lowest concentration
giving at least a 99.9% (3-log) fall from the starting inoculum. Where replicates differed, the higher MIC, or the
higher MBC, was used. An MBC:MIC ratio of 4 or less counted as bactericidal.

Time-kill: 2 MSSA and 2 MRSA, at 4x, 8x and 16x the MIC up to the fCmax, and at the fCmax itself. The fCmax was
taken from label Cmax and protein binding at skin-infection doses. Inocula were standard (about 5.5-7.5 × 10^5
CFU/mL) and high (7.5 × 10^7). Bactericidal meant a sustained 3-log kill through 24 h.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End points: the MBC (3-log kill at 24 h) and time-kill. This is not an opioid paper, so there is no MEAC, ke0 or
potency ratio.
- MSSA (p3): every agent was bactericidal for all 12 isolates except cefazolin, where 1 isolate had a ratio above 4.
  Tedizolid was bacteriostatic for all 12.
- MRSA: vancomycin was bactericidal for 15/15 by MBC:MIC. At fCmax it gave a 3-log kill by 24 h against both MRSA
  strains at standard inoculum (p5).
- Cefazolin at fCmax 32: a 3-log kill by 4-6 h against both MSSA strains at standard inoculum (p5, Figs 3-4). At
  high inoculum, killing was largely bacteriostatic for MMX 7908 and generally bactericidal by 24 h for MMX 7790
  (p5). Read from the text; the figures were not digitised.

## Covariates
None.

## Caveats
- Small panels (12 MSSA, 15 MRSA) from a repository, not consecutive clinical isolates. Funded by the sponsor.
- The tables give ratio categories only. MBC50 and MBC90 can only be bounded from the MIC and the ratio, not read.
- Where duplicates differed, the higher MIC or MBC was kept, which biases ratios and MBCs upward.
- The fCmax values come from labels, and the regimens behind them are not stated (e.g. the cefazolin dose).

## Verification against stanpumpR
- **antibiotics.md:55** lists the paper as a cefazolin source for MSSA, streptococci and Enterobacterales.
  Cefazolin was tested against MSSA only (12 isolates). The paper gives MBC:MIC ratio counts, not an MBC table. It
  was readable in full. The citation (Diagn Microbiol Infect Dis 2017;87:121-128) is correct. **matches**, except
  that the paper has no streptococcal or Gram-negative cefazolin data.
- **Discrepancy:** the library calls Jones 1977 the only study that measured cefazolin MBCs:
  - `inst/help/drugs/cefazolin.md:7`: "in the one study that measured cefazolin's MBCs"
  - `inst/help/models/antibiotics.md:30`: "in the one study that measured its MBCs"
  - `R/antibioticMbc.R:93-94`: "No modern measured cefazolin MBC for MSSA could be read", and "the one cefazolin
    series"

  This paper measured cefazolin MBCs against 12 MSSA by the CLSI method (Table 2), so it is a second, modern series.
  Its result runs the other way from the library's x4: 11/12 isolates were within one dilution of the MIC and 1/12
  had a ratio above 4. That puts the 90th-percentile ratio at about 2 (derived). Jones had 2/14 at 4 or more. Both
  series show a tail; with 12 isolates, Sweeney's single outlier falls just above the 90th percentile. See
  `open_questions`.
- MIC check against the cefazolin MSSA row (`R/antibioticMbc.R:91`: MIC50 0.5, MIC90 1, from EUCAST's 17,120
  isolates). Sweeney has MIC50 0.5 and MIC90 0.5. The MIC50s agree, and Sweeney's MIC90 is one dilution lower (small
  panel). The row note already says smaller series put the MIC90 at 0.5 or 2.
- Vancomycin cross-check (not cited for it; `R/antibioticMbc.R:362-371`: MIC50 1, MIC90 1, MBC50 2, MBC90 8). The
  MRSA MICs agree (1/1). No MRSA isolate had a ratio above 4, so no tolerant isolates appeared in 15; the library's
  MBC90 of 8 reflects a tolerant minority (Honda 2011). Sweeney's ratios suggest a lower MBC50 (73% at a ratio of 1,
  so about 1; derived). The row's MBC50 of 2 is from a larger bacteraemia series. No change is implied.
- fCmax: cefazolin 32 µg/mL free comes from a label at an unstated skin-infection dose, so it was not compared with
  the unbound Komatsu model (`R/drugs_cefazolin.R`).
