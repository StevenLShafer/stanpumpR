# Ho 2016: R- and S-citalopram exposure and agitation/cognition scores in CitAD

## Study
- Secondary exposure-response analysis of CitAD (randomised, placebo-controlled, 9 weeks; 94 citalopram, 92 placebo randomised). Patients with Alzheimer's disease and agitation, without major depression.
- Analysed: 81 citalopram (41 M, 40 F; age 78 ± 8.7 y) and 80 placebo (45 M, 35 F; 78 ± 8.5 y) (Table 1). Baseline MMSE 17 vs 14.9, NBRS-A 7.2 vs 7.5, NPIA 7.6 vs 7.8.
- 331 plasma samples at weeks 3, 6, 9; doses 10 mg (5% of samples), 20 mg (20%), 30 mg (75%) daily. Chiral HPLC-UV, LOQ 5 ng/mL per enantiomer, CV < 15%.
- Exposure: individual predicted R- and S-AUC(0,24 h) from the Akil 2016 population PK model (cited here as J Pharm Pharmacol 2016;43:99-109, a journal-name error in this paper's reference 18).
- NONMEM 7.3; time-in-study (placebo/disease) functions plus linear drug terms; logistic model for mADCS-CGIC responder status.

## Model and parameters
No PK model of its own. PD structures: MMSE X(t) = X0·e^(k1·t) + k2·AUCr + k3·AUCs; NBRS-A and NPIA X(t) = α·e^(k1·t) + β·e^(k2·t) + k3·AUCr/1000 + k4·AUCs/1000 (k1 fixed −2); CGIC logit C1 = k1 (AUC = 0) or k2·AUCr/1000 + k3·AUCs/1000 + k4.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End points: NBRS-A (agitation), NPIA, mADCS-CGIC responder status, MMSE (cognition), over 9 weeks. Exposure metric is AUC(0,24 h), not concentration; no time-course link, no ke0/EC50, no concentration threshold or therapeutic range. Not an opioid; no MEAC or potency ratio (S-citalopram has ~40-fold higher SERT affinity than R, cited from literature).

## Covariates
Age and sex were not significant on response. (PK covariates — age and sex on R, CYP2C19 and age on S — belong to Akil 2016.)

## Caveats
Post hoc; not designed for enantiomer effects; small effects with wide CIs (several CIs printed with the wrong sign); predicted, not measured, AUCs; time in study dominated all scores; Alzheimer's agitation without major depression, so nothing here concerns antidepressant response.
