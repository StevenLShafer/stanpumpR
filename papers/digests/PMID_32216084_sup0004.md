# Chen 2020 (supplement): Figure S4, simulated dose-exposure regressions

## Study
No new data. Simulated exposures "in pediatric and adult participants" from the parent article's final population
model (card `PMID_32216084.md`), plotted against the dose in mg/kg on log-log axes, with a linear regression of the
log-transformed values. The simulated population, the number of simulated subjects and the regimen are not stated;
the panels name Cmax1 and Cmax2, so the simulation gave two doses. One PDF page.

## Model and parameters
Four panels, x: Log(Dose) (mg/kg), 2.00-5.25 (natural log, so about 7.4-191 mg/kg; derived: exp(2) and exp(5.25),
natural log inferred because log10 values would put Cmax near 10^6 ug/mL). Y axes in log ug/mL or log h*ug/mL, basis
"SXB" (title). Equations as printed under each panel:

```yaml
parameters:
  - {name: Cmax1 log-log slope, value: 1.19, unit: "-", source: "Figure S4A", note: "Y = -0.719 + 1.19 X; 90% CI of slope 1.17-1.21; SE 0.0143; residual SD 0.326; simulated first-dose Cmax"}
  - {name: Cmax2 log-log slope, value: 1.24, unit: "-", source: "Figure S4B", note: "Y = -0.326 + 1.24 X; 90% CI 1.23-1.26; SE 0.0106; residual SD 0.242; simulated second-dose Cmax"}
  - {name: AUCinf log-log slope, value: 1.68, unit: "-", source: "Figure S4C", note: "Y = -0.609 + 1.68 X; 90% CI 1.65-1.71; SE 0.018; residual SD 0.41"}
  - {name: AUC0-24h log-log slope, value: 1.68, unit: "-", source: "Figure S4D", note: "Y = -0.605 + 1.68 X; 90% CI 1.65-1.71; SE 0.0179; residual SD 0.408"}
```

Implied ratios for a doubled mg/kg dose (derived: 2^slope): Cmax1 2.28, Cmax2 2.36, AUC 3.20. The scatter is
curved at the low end (AUC panels: points above the line below about log 2.5, i.e. the regression under-predicts at
small doses), so a single slope is an average over the range.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None.

## Covariates
Dose normalised by weight; paediatric and adult simulated participants pooled in one regression. No covariate split.

## Caveats
- Regimen, population size and covariate distribution of the simulation are not stated.
- Natural log is inferred, not printed.
- A slope fitted across weights mixes the dose effect with the weight-scaling of VC and Vmax (exponents 0.650 and
  0.633, Table S4); it is not a within-subject dose-proportionality estimate.
