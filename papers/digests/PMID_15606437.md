# Hussein 2005: population PK of levocetirizine in young atopic children (ETAC)

## Study
Retrospective population PK within ETAC, a randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial.
343 children (of 399 treated), 12-24 months old at entry, atopic and at high risk of asthma.
Oral racemic cetirizine dihydrochloride drops (10 mg/mL), 0.25 mg/kg twice daily for 18 months;
levocetirizine dose taken as 50% of the racemic dose (PDF p3). Sparse steady-state plasma samples at
months 3, 12 and 18 (Table 1 and abstract; the Methods on PDF p2 say months 3, 6 and 18); 248, 279
and 226 children per visit; 753 concentrations; 66, 144 and 133 children gave 1, 2 and 3 samples;
75% of samples 1-5 h post-dose. Demographics (Table 1, PDF p3, page image): age mean 19.8, 29.0,
35.3 months (range 14.4-46.3); weight mean 11.9, 13.9, 15.0 kg (range 8.2-20.5); BSA 0.52-0.63 m2;
CLcr (Traub-Johnson) 28.2-32.8 mL/min. Assay: chiral LC-MS/MS (Chiralcel OD), range 3-1000 ng/mL;
4-fold dilution raised the limit to 12 ng/mL; samples at or below 12 ng/mL excluded (PDF p2-3).
NONMEM V level 1.1, FO (FOCE no better). Sponsor: UCB.

## Model and parameters
One compartment, first-order absorption and elimination. ka = θka + CL/V (to prevent flip-flop; CL/V
adds under 5%). A relative bioavailability F1 = θF1 for samples judged non-compliant (92 of 753,
12%), F1 = 1 otherwise. Exponential IIV; two additive residual errors (concentrations ≤400 and
>400 ng/mL). Final model, Table 3 (PDF p7, printed 34; read on the page image):

Reported typical values: CL/F 0.60 and 1.13 L/h, V/F 5.1 and 12.8 L at 8 and 20 kg (abstract, page
image); CL/F 0.77, 0.86, 0.91 L/h and V/F 7.60, 8.88, 9.59 L at 11.9, 13.9, 15.0 kg (PDF p9). These
agree with the equations (derived: 0.244 + 0.0442 x 8 = 0.598; 0.639 x 11.9 = 7.60). Implied half-life
at 12 kg about 6.9 h (derived: ln2 x 7.67 / 0.774); it rises slowly with weight because V/F has no
intercept. AUCss in compliant children 1952 (1227-3319) µg·h/L, mean (min-max), vs 2036 (1414-2827)
µg·h/L in adults after 5 mg oral solution (abstract, page image; the text layer misprints µg as mg).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No concentration-effect, wheal/flare or therapeutic range data. Not an opioid. The levo:dextro
concentration ratio was mostly 2-4 (Fig. 4).

## Covariates
Weight on CL/F (linear with intercept) and V/F (proportional). Age, BSA and CLcr were significant
alone but not after weight; sex, allergen sensitization, eosinophilia (>500/µL), concomitant
corticosteroids, penicillins, macrolides, hydroxyzine and diarrhoea had no detectable effect (Table 2;
few positive cases).

## Caveats
- Sparse sampling (1-3 per child), steady state only, mostly 1-5 h post-dose: ka is poorly informed
  (IIV 105%); IIV on V/F poorly estimated (precision >100%).
- Apparent parameters only; no i.v. data. Dose and sampling times recorded by parents.
- "Weight-normalized CL/F and V/F about 60% higher than adults" (PDF p9): the comparator is the adult
  volunteer study of Baltes et al. 2001 (ref 7), not a model fitted here.
- Methods list sampling months 3, 6 and 18 (PDF p2); everywhere else 3, 12, 18.
- The 400 ng/mL split in residual error was chosen empirically; the authors state it has no clinical meaning.
- Hydroxyzine (metabolised to cetirizine) could interfere with the assay (PDF p9).
