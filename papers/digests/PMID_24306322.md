# Cannesson 2014: closed-loop systems editorial

## Study
Three-page editorial introducing a special issue of J Clin Monit Comput on closed-loop perioperative systems. No data, no patients, no pharmacokinetics. It argues that regulatory approval, clinician acceptance and development pathway are the hurdles to automated anesthesia. It cites the Sedasys propofol-sedation system for colonoscopy (FDA condition: an anesthesia professional must be immediately available). It introduces six papers: Dumont (feedback control overview), a simulation environment, two Hemmerling-group papers (an anesthesia robot, and decision support), Dussaussoy (propofol/remifentanil automated titration and workload), and Wysocki (closed-loop ventilation).
