# Loft 1986: metronidazole 0.5 and 2.0 g IV and oral, with metabolites

## Study
8 healthy volunteers (2 F, 6 M; age 30 +/- 6 y; weight 68 +/- 11 kg), non-smokers, no drugs. Four-way crossover:
metronidazole 0.5 g and 2.0 g IV (5 mg/mL solution, DAK, infused over 20 min) and orally (250 mg DAK tablets, 100 mL
water, overnight fast + 1 h). Plasma sampled to 60 h (IV: 10, 20, 30, 40, 60, 80 min, 2-60 h; oral: 15, 30, 60, 80 min,
2-60 h); urine 0-60 h. Assay: HPLC-UV 320 nm for metronidazole, hydroxymetronidazole (HM) and metronidazole acetic
acid (MAA); urine with/without glucuronidase; CV 3-8%. Curve stripping (ESTRIP); IV fitted by an open
two-compartment model with infusion correction; oral lag, ka and t1/2 by ESTRIP; F = AUCpo/AUCiv per dose.

CL 9% lower after 2.0 g IV (95% CI 4-14%, p < 0.05); 7% (5-9%) lower after 2.0 g oral assuming complete absorption.

Table 2, oral (mean +/- SD; F with 95% CI):

| Dose | Lag (min) | ka (1/h) | t1/2 (h) | F | AUC (ug.h/mL) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.5 g | 4.6 +/- 5.2 | 9.6 +/- 9.0 | 8.0 +/- 1.6 | 0.99 (0.94-1.04) | 100 +/- 16 |
| 2.0 g | 3.0 +/- 4.5 | 7.8 +/- 4.7 | 7.6 +/- 0.9 | 0.96 (0.92-1.00) | 427 +/- 53 |

ka independent of dose; authors note it exceeds previously reported values (Jensen 1983, Bergan 1980).
Urinary recovery ratio po/iv 0.85 (0.72-0.92) at 0.5 g, 0.94 (0.83-1.05) at 2.0 g.
Metabolites (Table 3): HM t1/2 11.1-11.8 h; HM AUC 62-65 (0.5 g), 227-239 (2.0 g) ug.h/mL; HM Cmax 2.2-2.4 ug/mL
at 9.5-9.7 h (0.5 g). MAA only in trace amounts after 0.5 g.
Clearance pathways (Table 4, 0.5 g IV): renal 12.0, MAA 13.3, HM 32.6, glucuronidation 5.1 mL/min; together 77% of CL.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None.

## Covariates
Dose only (small, statistically significant reduction in CL at 2.0 g; authors judge it clinically unimportant).

## Caveats
n = 8 healthy young adults; curve-stripping estimates (ESTRIP) not population modelling; ka very variable (SD
~equal to mean at 0.5 g) and early oral samples only at 15, 30, 60 min; 250 mg Danish (DAK) tablets.

## Verification against stanpumpR
- "essentially complete in Loft 1986" (R:26). Paper: F 0.99 (0.5 g) and 0.96 (2.0 g). **matches**. The code's
  chosen F 0.841 (Bergan 1984) is lower than Loft's; the header states the choice, so this is a judgment, not an
  error.
- **Discrepancy**: R:26-28 and help (inst/help/drugs/metronidazole.md:7) say Elkomy 2022's ka 1.336 /h is "the only
  published first-order estimate found". This paper, cited in the same header, reports first-order oral ka of
  9.6 +/- 9.0 /h (0.5 g) and 7.8 +/- 4.7 /h (2.0 g) with a 3-5 min lag, from commercial 250 mg tablets (Table 2),
  and itself refers to earlier ka values (Bergan & Arnold 1980; Jensen & Gugler 1983). The Loft value is 6-7 times
  faster than the 1.336 /h in the code, which would give an earlier, higher oral peak.
- Not used but available: two-compartment IV disposition (Vc 36 L, Vss 50 L, CL 4.98 L/h) in healthy adults.
