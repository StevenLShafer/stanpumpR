# Drover 2004: zaleplon, zolpidem and zopiclone kinetics and dynamics (review)

## Study
Narrative review (single author, Stanford) of the three non-benzodiazepine hypnotics: receptor pharmacology,
effects on sleep, anxiolysis, amnesia, psychomotor effects, adverse effects, metabolism, special populations,
interactions, and a NONMEM simulation of typical oral profiles from literature parameters (no variability; Section
2.4). Medline search by drug name and investigational number. No new data.

One-compartment first-order absorption, parameterised as CL/F, V/F, ka (Section 2.4). Figure 2 (rendered) simulates
10 mg zolpidem peaking at about 90 ug/L near 1.4 h and about 13 ug/L at 8 h (read from the figure). (derived: with
Table I's CL/F, V/F and ka, Cmax 91.5 ug/L at 1.34 h and 12.8 ug/L at 8 h, agreeing with Figure 2 and not with
Table I's 120 ug/L, which is a summary of observed peaks, not the model's prediction.)

Zolpidem facts in the text (PDF p5-6): F about 70%, not dose- or duration-dependent; extensive hepatic CYP
metabolism, 1% or less excreted unchanged, metabolites inactive; Cmax and AUC rise in the elderly; shorter tmax and
lower AUC in children; clearance falls in hepatic failure; renal function has little effect.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No range, threshold or EC50 stated for any of the three drugs. Effect-site equilibration judged clinically
insignificant after oral dosing (tmax 1-2 h; hysteresis not significant in ref 23, Drover 2000); duration of effect
attributed to kinetics alone (PDF p7, p9). Zolpidem produced more psychomotor effect than zaleplon at similar
plasma concentrations (ref 23). Not an opioid.

## Covariates
None modelled; qualitative age, hepatic and interaction effects only.

## Caveats
- Internal inconsistencies: text (PDF p6) gives the zaleplon/zolpidem volumes as 275 vs 66 L, Table I 285 vs 69.6 L;
  zopiclone kel 0.203/h (t1/2 3.41 h) does not equal its CL/F / V/F (derived 0.159/h, t1/2 4.4 h); Table I gives
  zaleplon tmax 1.4 h while the text gives 0.7-1.1 h and about 1 h.
- Table I Cmax values are literature observations, not the simulated peaks of Figure 2 (above).
- Parameters are apparent (/F) and from different studies; no variability.
