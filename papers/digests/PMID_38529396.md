# Zhou 2024: cetagliptin multiple-dose PK/PD in Chinese type 2 diabetes

## Study
Single centre (First Affiliated Hospital of Nanjing Medical University; CTR20190599), randomised, double-blind,
placebo- and open-label sitagliptin-controlled. 32 Chinese adults with T2DM (22 men): two dose cohorts of 16,
randomised 10:2:4 to cetagliptin (50 or 100 mg), placebo or sitagliptin 100 mg, once daily fasting for 14 days
(PDF p3). Inclusion: 18-65 y, BMI 19-30, HbA1c 6.5-9%, no hypoglycaemic drugs for 12 weeks. Table 1 (PDF p6):
cetagliptin 50 mg age 47.8 +/- 4.3 y, weight 69.55 +/- 7.63 kg, 90% male; 100 mg 45.2 +/- 9.8 y, 72.56 +/- 7.62 kg,
70% male. Plasma sampled to 24 h on day 1 and to 120 h after the day-14 dose; LC-MS/MS 0.5-5000 ng/mL. NCA in
WinNonlin 8.1; population PK then sequential PK/PD in Phoenix (NLME).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Plasma DPP-4 inhibition (Gly-Pro-AMC fluorescence). Table 3 (PDF p8): day 1 Rmax 86.39% (50 mg), 88.78% (100 mg),
90.10% (sitagliptin); DUR80% 8.39, 21.1, 16.3 h; day 14 Rmax 89.47, 89.99, 90.43%, DUR80% 21.9, 32.3, 18.6 h, E24h
78.43, 83.91, 77.72%. Active GLP-1 AUEC0-24h 2.20- and 3.36-fold placebo (sitagliptin 2.90). No FPG or HbA1c
difference over 14 days. Population PK/PD: direct-effect sigmoid Emax (equation PDF p5), 554 concentrations after
excluding |CWRES| > 5; Supplementary Table 1 gives Emax 91.78%, EC50 5.120 (no unit), gamma 1.008 (supplement,
2026-10-27); the authors note CWRES trends needing further optimisation (PDF p9). No ke0 (direct effect).

## Covariates
Total bilirubin on V2 only ("V2 increases with TBIL"). Supplementary Table 1 gives the coefficient dV2dTBIL = 0.3723
(positive, consistent with the text) but neither the functional form nor a reference bilirubin (supplement,
2026-10-27).

## Caveats
n 10 per dose, 14 days, Chinese patients. Observed dose ratio is supra-proportional on day 1 (2.55) but proportional
at steady state (2.04); the paper does not discuss it. Because the TBIL term's centring is not given, the supplement
does not establish whether tvV2 = 558 L is V2 at a typical patient's bilirubin or at some other value of the
covariate.
