# Chen 2026: PBPK model of ceftriaxone in pregnancy (OSP Suite)

## Study
- Modelling study, no new subjects. PK-Sim 12 (Build 440) and MoBi 12 (Build 434), Open Systems Pharmacology; data digitized from the literature (WebPlotDigitizer) (p. 3).
- Healthy adult model: drug parameters from Alasmari 2023 (Table 1); calibration to Patel 1981 (0.5, 1, 2 g IV over 30 min), external validation against 11 datasets from 7 studies (Borner, Harb, McNamara, Meyers, Pollock, Seddon, Zhou; 0.5-3 g, bolus to 35-min infusions, single and multiple dose). Virtual populations of 100, demographics matched per study.
- Pregnancy model (Dallmann workflow): adult model exported to MoBi with the pregnancy spatial structure (placenta, fetus, amniotic fluid and others) and the "Pregnant (Dallmann et al.)" physiology database; renal clearance re-expressed as a GFR fraction, optimized to 0.52 against Bourget 1993 (9 women, 28 4/7 to 40 5/7 weeks, 2 g IV over 60 min, single dose). Validation: Bourget multiple-dose (2 g q24h for 7 days) and Kafetzis 1983 (17 women at 38-41 weeks, 1 g IV bolus, bioassay) (p. 3, 5).
- Applications: steady-state comparison of non-pregnant women (White American females 20-40 y) with women at 35 weeks (1 and 2 g q24h IV over 30 min, 7 days, last interval 144-168 h); gestation-week simulations (7, 21, 35 weeks; 2, 2.5, 3 g q24h and 2 g q12h); PTA against Treponema pallidum (MIC 0.0025 ug/mL) with fu,p fixed at 0.2963 (p. 6-7).
- Model files public: github.com/UFPBPK/CeftriaxonePregnancy (PK-Sim and MoBi formats).

Derived checks: at 1 g, Ctrough 17.2 umol/L x 0.55458 mg/umol = 9.5 mg/L total, x 0.2963 = 2.83 mg/L free, the non-pregnant 100% fT>MIC breakpoint (2.81), as the authors explain (p. 12). If AUC0-168 is taken as proportional to 1/CL (linear model near steady state), the CL ratio is 708,602 / 587,464 = 1.21; with t1/2 6.8 / 8.0 = 0.85, the implied volume ratio is 1.21 x 0.85 = 1.03. These CL and V ratios are my derivations: the paper prints no total clearance or volume for either population.

Healthy adult performance (Table 2, abstract): Cmax S/O 0.60-0.95 and AUC 0-t S/O 0.64-0.99, systematic underprediction at low doses, which the authors attribute to fixing a single fu,p for saturable binding. The abstract says the adult model was calibrated "using data from 193 subjects"; the Results give n = 193 as concentration pairs (33 calibration + 160 evaluation; p. 8-9), not subjects.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
PTA only (no clinical end point): free ceftriaxone, % fT>MIC (60% "bacteriostatic", 100% "bactericidal", 100% fT>4xMIC). Both 1 and 2 g q24h keep 100% fT>MIC against T. pallidum (MIC 0.0025 ug/mL) at 35 weeks, across fu,p 0.10-0.40; the breakpoint falls by about 38% in pregnancy at every fu,p (p. 12). Gestation-week simulations: Cmax falls about 8.5% from week 7 to week 35 for once-daily regimens.

## Covariates
Gestational age (through the Dallmann physiology; encoded as virtual age 30.0 y = conception to 30.75 y = 38 weeks). No trimester-varying binding: fu,p fixed, with albumin change only implicit in the framework.

## Caveats
Authors (p. 14): renal and biliary clearance inputs come from parameter identification, not mass balance; the Kafetzis discrepancy; late-pregnancy validation only (no first or second trimester data); fu,p fixed; gestation simulations are upper bounds; no placental or fetal exposure. Also: pregnancy calibration rests on 9 women from one 1993 study; the GFR rise in the framework (21%) is smaller than measured pregnancy GFR rises; the healthy reference is a virtual White American female population, not a measured control. The Figure 3 caption describes the multiple-dose regimen as "every 60 min" where Table 3 and the Methods give 2 g q24h over 60 min (a caption slip). An ongoing IMPAACT 2044 study (NCT07207876) is named as the planned external check.

## Verification against stanpumpR
- pregnancyRegistry.csv:52, 17% lower AUC and 38% lower trough at 35 weeks: **matches** (17.1% and 37.8%, Results p. 11). The registry's CL ratio 1/0.83 = 1.20 is the same derivation as mine (1.21); it is an inference from a cumulative 7-day AUC, not a reported clearance.
- pregnancyRegistry.csv:52 missing-data field and docs/pregnancy.md:243, that the manuscript gives "the predicted CL and V" separately: **discrepancy**. The published paper reports no total CL or V for pregnant or non-pregnant women; it gives the GFR fraction (0.52), Cmax, trough, cumulative AUC and half-life. The CL and V ratios can only be derived (about 1.21 and 1.03, above). The calibration data it was fitted to are named (Bourget 1993, n = 9; validated on Kafetzis 1983, n = 17), which answers the other half of the docs line.
- pregnancyRegistry.csv:52, "manuscript under review" and evidence "abstract": out of date; the paper is published (J Clin Pharmacol 2026;66:e70205) and archived in full.
- The registry's decision not to apply a factor is a judgement the paper does not contradict: the PBPK prediction is a model of a 9-woman calibration set with a fixed binding fraction. Note also that the plotted repo curve is total ceftriaxone (Sanz-Codina), while Chen's model predicts total concentration with fu fixed, so any applied CL x 1.2 would be on the same total scale.
