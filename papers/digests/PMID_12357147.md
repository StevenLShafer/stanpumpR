# Drover 2002: IV, immediate-release and OROS extended-release hydromorphone

## Study
- Stanford; 12 healthy volunteers (6 men, 6 women), age 21-34 yr; weights not given in the main text (web table 1).
  Nonsmokers, fasted. Funded by Knoll/Abbott.
- Three stages, six sessions, crossover: (1) IV hydromorphone 8 mg over 10 min (0.8 mg/min), arterial samples
  1-370 min then venous to 1450 min; (2) 8 mg IR tablet (Dilaudid), venous to 1440 min; (3) randomised double-blind
  8, 16, 32 mg OROS or placebo, venous to 48 h. PD published separately (Angst 2001).
- Assay: LC-MS/MS, linear 0.05-10.0 ng/mL, LOQ 0.05 ng/mL, between-day CV 1.4-11.2%.
- Analysis: NONMEM V; individual 1-, 2-, 3-compartment fits to IV data (proportional error, Hannan criterion); oral
  input by deconvolution with a constrained linear spline using each subject's IV disposition.

## Model and parameters
- IV disposition: three-compartment mammillary model preferred (P < 0.001 vs two-compartment), first-order
  elimination from the central compartment. Individual parameter values are in web-enhancement Table 2 and are NOT
  reported in the main text; no population typical values (V1, CL, rate constants) appear in the article.
- Absolute oral bioavailability from the area under the spline input function (Table 1):

| Formulation | Mean F | SD | Range of 12 |
|---|---|---|---|
| IR 8 mg | 0.189 | 0.054 | 0.106-0.274 |
| OROS 8 mg | 0.241 | 0.059 | 0.177-0.381 |

  OROS > IR, P = 0.025. Trapezoidal-AUC check: IR 0.194 (SD 0.050), OROS 0.251 (SD 0.073). F the same for 8, 16 and
  32 mg OROS.
- IR input: initial rapid release then slower phase; mean maximum release rate 17 mg/h (SD 10); first-order absorption
  (with or without lag) misspecified. OROS: maximum input about 10 h, mean maximum rate 1.2 mg/h (SD 0.38) for 8 mg.
- Simulation (48 mg/day): peak 9.2 ng/mL (IR 16 mg q8h) vs 6.7 ng/mL (OROS 48 mg q24h); fluctuation 130% vs 39%.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Opioid audit: no PD in this paper (published separately, Angst et al. 2001). No MEAC, no ke0 / t½ke0 / tPeak, no
hysteresis. Potency stated in the Introduction: hydromorphone about five times as potent as morphine (no source or
route given).

## Covariates
None examined (n = 12).

## Caveats
Small healthy young sample; individual (not population) disposition fits, typical values not published in the main
text; spline deconvolution still showed some misspecification (Fig. 3); long OROS release may reflect enterohepatic
cycling or sparse late sampling (authors); earlier higher bioavailability estimates (Vallner, Parab, Ritschel) used
RIA and shorter sampling.
