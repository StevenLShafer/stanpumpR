# Schneck 2024: population PK of tirzepatide (19 studies, 5802 participants)

## Study
Eli Lilly authors and sponsor. Pooled 19 studies (Table 1, PDF p3): phase 1 (healthy, T2DM, renal and hepatic
impairment, oral contraceptive, metabolism, absolute bioavailability with 5 mg SC and IV, injection site, device),
phase 2 dose-ranging/finding, phase 3 SURPASS-1 to -5, J-mono and J-combo. SC doses 0.25-15 mg, single and once
weekly up to 109 weeks. 39,644 observations from 5802 participants. Table 2 (PDF p5): phase 3 T2DM n 5354, age 58.1
± 10.5 (18-91) y, weight 90 ± 20.6 (43.1-227) kg, BMI 32.5 ± 6.39, 43% female. Assay LC-MS (intact tirzepatide),
range 2-500 ng/mL; about 16% of samples below LLOQ, excluded. NONMEM 7.4.2, FOCE-I; bootstrap (1000) with PsN 4.8.1;
pcVPC; RxODE simulations.

## Model and parameters
Two compartments, first-order absorption and elimination, IIV on ka, CL and Vc, proportional residual error (PDF
p5). Absolute F fixed at 0.8 from the IV study; between-study differences in F as F = 0.8 x (1 + Fraction) (PDF p2,
Table 3 footnote b). Size: CL and Q x (BW/70)^0.8 (footnote d); Vc and Vp x (FFM + FracFM x FM)/70 (footnote e,
exponent 1), with Janmahasatian FFM: male 9270 BW/(6680 + 216 BMI), female 9270 BW/(8780 + 244 BMI), FM = BW - FFM
(Equations 5-8, PDF p4). Body weight time-varying. Fraction of fat mass on CL approached 1, so total weight was used.

Derived: 0.0329 x (90/70)^0.8 / (0.8 x 0.819) = 0.0614 L/h, so the published apparent CL is reproduced at 90 kg
only if the -0.181 applies to the T2DM studies. Other results: dose-proportional 0.25-15 mg; tmax 8-72 h; similar
exposure in abdomen, thigh, arm; accumulation 1.7-fold; exposure about 1.1% per kg over 70-120 kg (22% higher at 70
kg, 33% lower at 120 kg vs 90 kg) (PDF p5-6).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None modelled (PK only). Figure 4 simulates delayed or missed doses (a dose 4 days late gives a transient 20% higher
concentration).

## Covariates
Weight (time-varying) and fat mass as above. Not significant: age, creatinine, eGFR, AST, ALT, bilirubin, albumin,
anti-drug antibodies, race, ethnicity, sex after weight.

## Caveats
Industry analysis. BLQ samples (16%) dropped. High ka shrinkage (57%). The meaning of the between-study F effect
(which studies) depends on the supplement's control stream, which is not in the archive.
