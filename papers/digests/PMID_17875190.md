# Gueorguieva 2008: IL-1Ra in plasma and CSF after subarachnoid haemorrhage

## Study
- Eight patients recruited (six women, two men), White, mean age 51 years (39-69), mean weight 70 kg (range printed as 64-150), with subarachnoid haemorrhage and an external ventricular drain; seven analysed, one of them with renal impairment fitted apart.
- 100 mg IV bolus, then 2 mg/kg/h for 24 h; plasma and CSF sampled during and after the infusion.

## Model and parameters
Plasma: two compartments, linear, elimination from the central compartment (Table 1): CL 6.96 L/h (RSE 23%), V1 5.14 L, V2 4.96 L, Vss 10.1 L. The renally impaired patient: CL 1.41 L/h, V1 3.94 L, Vss 8.25 L. CSF: one compartment, kin 0.0019 /h and kout 0.1 /h. At 24 h the CSF concentration was 358 ng/mL (115-886), 1-4% of plasma.

## Covariates
None in a model; renal impairment cut clearance about fivefold in one patient.
