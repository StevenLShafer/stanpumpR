# Krzyzanski 2021: population PK of IM and oral dexamethasone and betamethasone

## Study
Open-label randomized partial crossover (NCT03668860; data from Jobe 2020), fasting, 10-day washout. 48 healthy
nonpregnant Indian women, 22-39 y, BMI 20.6-25.0, height 144-167 cm, weight 47.0-68.7 kg (mean 56.8 kg). Eight
sequences of 6; single 6 mg doses (stated as free-alcohol equivalents, i.e. base) of: A DEX-P IM solution (Fresenius,
n = 12), B BET-P IM (n = 12), C BET phosphate+acetate 3+3 mg IM (Celestone, n = 24), D DEX-P PO 0.5 mg tablets
(Cadila, n = 24), E BET-P PO tablets (n = 24). Samples 0, 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2, 3, 4, 6, 12, 18, 24, 30, 36, 48, 60, 72, 96 h,
with phosphatase inhibitor. LC-MS/MS on a chiral column, LLOQ 0.1 ng/mL. NONMEM 7.4 Laplacian with interaction,
Beal M3 for BLQ (103 of 578 DEX observations BLQ).

## Model and parameters
Two-compartment, separate first-order IM and PO absorption (eq 1-6); no IV arm, so all clearances/volumes are divided
by F_IM, and F_PO is relative: Fr = F_PO/F_IM (eq 7). Log-additive residual. DEX, Table 3 (estimate (%RSE); IIV as
variance (%RSE) (CV%)):

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None in this paper (PD planned separately: cortisol, glucose, cell trafficking).

## Covariates
None; homogeneous women; CL vs weight r2 = 0.005 (DEX).

## Caveats
No IV reference: absolute F and true central volume unidentifiable (authors attribute the large Vp/F to the absorption
phase hiding the early disposition). Their DEX CL/F (9.29 L/h) is about half of earlier IV values (16.3 L/h; Hong
2007 18.2 L/h), which they attribute to longer sampling here (96 h, LLOQ 0.1 ng/mL). They also cite Hong's 41.6 L as
a Vss, although Hong's 41.6 L is a central volume. Small ethnic/sex-specific population; ka_PO highly variable.
