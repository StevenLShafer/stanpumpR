# Liechti 2016: GHB pharmacokinetics and subjective effects in healthy men

## Study
- Randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled, crossover (GHB vs placebo within subject, dose between groups) in 32
  healthy non-smoking men (NCT02342366) (p. 2). 20 mg/kg group: n = 16, 24 +/- 3 y, 74 +/- 8 kg. 35 mg/kg group: n = 16,
  25 +/- 5 y, 75 +/- 9 kg. Fasting morning; Xyrem solution in juice at 09:00; 7-day washout.
- Dose basis: "GHB (Xyrem solution in juice)" at 20 or 35 mg/kg; the paper does not say whether mg/kg is of sodium
  oxybate or of GHB.
- Sampling: -20, 35, 60, 100, 135, 190 min (p. 3). GC-MS, plasma, LLOQ 2 nmol/mL; concentrations in nmol/mL, so molar
  GHB; endogenous (placebo) levels subtracted at each time point before NCA (WinNonlin 6.4) (pp. 3-4).
- PD: VAS 0-10 general drug effect, sedation, stimulation, dizziness at -15, 40, 60, 100, 120, 180 min; BP and HR.

differed between groups (P = 0.008). The abstract and discussion call this about +40%; Table 1's normalised AUC gives

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End points: VAS general drug effect, sedation, stimulation and dizziness; dose-dependent sedation and dizziness,
  stimulation not dose-dependent; effects lasted about 2 h; no change in HR or BP (Table 2, p. 5).
- Hysteresis (results p. 6; Figure 4 and caption, p. 7, rendered): no clockwise hysteresis, i.e. no acute tolerance.
  Maximal sedation coincided with peak plasma levels at 35-60 min; maximal stimulation and dizziness came at 60 min
  while plasma levels were already falling, a slight counterclockwise hysteresis the authors attribute to distribution
  to the effect compartment.
- No ke0, EC50 or model fitted; no threshold concentration.

## Covariates
None examined (single sex, narrow age and weight).

## Caveats
- The abstract's Methods and first Results sentence say 25 and 35 mg/kg; the body, Table 1 and the abstract's AUC
  sentence say 20 and 35 mg/kg (rendered p. 1 and p. 3). The 25 is an error in the abstract.
- Few samples around tmax (first at 35 min); the authors call Cmax and tmax not fully reliable (p. 7).
- Elimination looked first order at these doses (Figure 1B). Liechti could not confirm Palatini's capacity-limited
  absorption (p. 7).
- Effect sampling (40, 60, 100 min...) is coarse for detecting a short equilibration delay.

## Verification against stanpumpR
- **tests/testthat/test-drugs-GHB.R:98-111**: 32 healthy men, mean 74.5 kg (average of the two groups' 74 and 75 kg,
  derived), age 25, Cmax 218 and 453 nmol/mL converted x 104.10/1000, peak within 15-60 min against "median 35 min
  (range 35-60)". Values **match** Table 1. The test enters the doses as "mg PO oxybate" (sodium oxybate mg/kg); the
  paper does not state the basis of its mg/kg (Xyrem is sodium oxybate, so the reading is plausible but is an
  assumption; read as acid, the input would be 21% higher). **Cannot verify** the dose basis.
- **inst/help/drugs/GHB.md:39**: "reported peak concentrations of 22.7 and 47.2 mcg/mL". **Matches** as a conversion of
  the geometric-mean nmol/mL values (the paper reports nmol/mL only, and these are geometric means).
- **R/drugs_GHB.R:110-113; GHB.md:47** ("Liechti found subjective effects tracking plasma with NO clockwise hysteresis,
  so an effect-site delay would be the wrong shape even if a fitted one existed"): **discrepancy**. The paper reports no
  clockwise hysteresis (no acute tolerance), but it also reports a slight counterclockwise hysteresis for stimulation and
  dizziness and attributes it to distribution to the effect compartment (Figure 4 caption, p. 7). Counterclockwise
  hysteresis is the shape an effect-site delay produces, so the paper does not support the stated reason; it supports
  "no tolerance" and "at most a small delay, not estimated". The repo's choice of no effect site can stand on the lack
  of a fitted ke0, not on this argument.
- **R/drugs_GHB.R:154-158** (reference entry: 32 healthy men, 20 and 35 mg/kg orally, independent validation, absence of
  hysteresis): numbers **match**; "absence of hysteresis" overstates it, as above.
- **inst/help/models/illicit-drugs.md:88** (at 8baa733; the context's line 82 is now the mass-basis paragraph):
  "within 6% and 1% of the two peak concentrations Liechti measured" is the repo's own simulation result; the observed
  values it compares against match Table 1.
