# Cha 2024: zolpidem population PK from digitized curves, and an overdose web simulator

## Study
Re-analysis of the KCT0003934 trial (Yoon 2021): open-label single 10 mg zolpidem (film-coated immediate-release
tablet) to 30 healthy Korean adults (15 per sex), plasma at 0, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 1, 1.5, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12 h.
Individual curves were digitized (WebPlotDigitizer) from an MFDS research report; 7 subjects excluded as duplicated
graphs, leaving 23 (12 M, 11 F). Individual demographics unavailable. NCA (R NonCompart 0.7.0) of digitized vs
original data (Table 1) showed no significant differences. NONMEM 7.5.0 ADVAN14, FOCE-I; 1000 bootstrap; R 4.3.3;
converted with nonmem2rx 0.1.3 to rxode2 2.1.0; Shiny app (pipettox.shinyapps.io/Zolpidem).

## Model and parameters
One compartment, first-order absorption with lag time (ALAG improved OFV by 25.3), first-order elimination; no
covariate (sex tested, not significant). Abstract and Table 2 (estimate; RSE where legible):

Derived: k = 16.9/61.7 = 0.274 /h, t1/2 2.53 h.
Table 1 NCA, mean (SE), original vs digitized: male Cmax 161.21 vs 162.72 ng/mL; female 187.64 vs 189.22; male
t1/2 2.93 vs 3.58 h; female 3.05 vs 3.02 h; Tmax median 0.94 / 0.96 h (M), 0.83 / 0.84 h (F).
Introduction background (cited to Uges, TIAFT reference list): therapeutic range 80-200 ng/mL; toxic > 500,
comatose > 1500, fatal > 4000 ng/mL. Also cites oral F 70%, t1/2 1.5-2.5 h, peak within 2 h (Salva and Costa).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None modelled. Simulated overdoses (280, 560, 1120 mg) against the TIAFT thresholds (Table 3). Not an opioid.

## Covariates
None retained (sex tested). Individual demographics unavailable.

## Caveats
Data digitized from graphs, 23 of 30 subjects; NCA values in Table 1 (original N = 15) do not exactly equal Yoon
2021 Table 2 (e.g. male Cmax 161.2 vs 154.0 ng/mL), presumably because of the MFDS report's own analysis. Table 2
text layer is scrambled. Overdose absorption (large tablet loads) not represented. The cited original MFDS analysis
used a two-compartment Erlang plus zero-order absorption model.
