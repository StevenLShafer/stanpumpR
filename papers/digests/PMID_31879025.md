# Hammer 2020: Playing with dexmedetomidine pharmacokinetics

## Study
A 3-page editorial accompanying Zuppa et al. (Br J Anaesth 2019;123:839-852), not a primary study. It takes Zuppa's
dexmedetomidine PK model for neonates and infants on cardiopulmonary bypass (CPB) and its dosing table (Zuppa Table 8)
and simulates them in stanpumpR (digital supplement and the app help page, example "Dexmedetomidine BJA 2020").
No new data, no assay, no fitted parameters.

Simulated patients (Fig 1 caption, PDF p. 2):
- neonate: 2-week-old girl, 3.8 kg, 52 cm;
- infant: 2.5-month-old girl, 5.2 kg, 58 cm.

Scenario: CPB starts at 60 min, weaning from bypass at 180 min (Fig 1a, 1c; 240 min in Fig 1b), infusion off at 6 h.
Targets 0.2, 0.5, 0.7, 1.0 ng/mL (taken from Zuppa Table 8).

## Model and parameters
No PK parameters are printed. The editorial says only that Zuppa's model depends on age, weight and temperature, with
separate behaviour at the start and end of CPB (it cites "table 6" of Zuppa).

Table 1 (PDF p. 3, printed p. 240; confirmed on the page image): infusion for the 2.5-month-old, 5.2 kg, 58 cm girl to
hold 1 ng/mL, CPB 60-180 min. Dose unit is ug/kg/h (the text layer garbles it to "mg").

Two rows at 180 min are as printed; the paper does not say how they are applied. The authors say building the table
took about 10 minutes of trial and error.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD. No ke0, tPeak, MEAC, EC50 or potency ratio is reported (opioid audit: not an opioid paper). The targets
0.2-1.0 ng/mL are Zuppa's Table 8 plasma concentrations (Fig 1 caption); the editorial gives no end point for them
and says the optimal concentration for each phase of the case is not known.

## Covariates
Age, weight, temperature, bypass state (per Zuppa). Findings stated in the editorial (simulation, Fig 1):
- Zuppa's own dosing guide gives about half of target before CPB, a spike at CPB onset (priming-volume drug and a
  smaller central volume), and about 80% of target if the infusion is restarted an hour after bypass.
- Cooling to 34 C at 90 min and 32 C at 120 min has a very modest PK effect (Fig 1b); the authors call it ignorable
  although statistically significant in Zuppa's model.

## Caveats
- Fixed-assumption dose tables (when bypass starts and stops) cannot fit real cases; the authors argue for simulation.
- Written by the stanpumpR author; the simulations are a demonstration, not a validation.
- The help-page example is named "58 inches" in the text (PDF p. 3) while the Fig 1 caption gives 58 cm; the card
  follows the caption.

## Used by stanpumpR

"Dexmedetomidine BJA 2020" and uses stanpumpR's dexmedetomidine infant model (Zuppa).
