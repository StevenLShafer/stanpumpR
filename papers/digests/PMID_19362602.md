# López-Fraile 2009: levetiracetam levels through pregnancy and 2 and 12 months postpartum

## Study
- Brief communication; prospective series, Zaragoza, Spain. 5 consecutive women recruited before gestational week 16,
  none excluded; ages 29-40 y; all first pregnancies (p. 372-373). Weight not reported (body weight and creatinine were
  monitored but not at the same times as levels; creatinine clearance not measured).
- Levetiracetam (LEV) twice daily: 1 woman on monotherapy (patient 1, 3000 mg), 3 with lamotrigine (patients 2-4) and 1
  with clobazam (patient 5); LEV 2000-3000 mg/day, patient 2 2000-2500 mg (Table 1, PDF p2). LTG doses were changed
  during pregnancy.
- Sampling: monthly fasting morning samples 9-10 a.m. before the first LEV dose (trough), at delivery (maternal and
  umbilical cord), and at 2 and 12 months postpartum.
- Assay: total plasma LEV by HPLC with UV/VIS detection, solid-phase extraction, internal standard; detection limit
  1.0 mg/L, CV < 10%.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Not an opioid paper. Seizure frequency unchanged in 4 of 5; patient 2 worsened in TM1 on reduced LTG and LEV and settled
when LTG was raised (Table 1, p. 374). Usual LEV range quoted from literature, 8-26 µg/mL (35-120 µmol/L) (p. 374).
Neonatal outcomes normal; one Apgar 6-9 with the lowest cord level (5.7 mg/L). No ke0, MEAC or potency ratio.

## Covariates
Pregnancy period only; co-medication (LTG, clobazam) described, its effect on LEV not determinable per the authors.

## Caveats
- n = 5, only one on monotherapy; the concentration comparison rests on 4 women with unchanged LEV dose.
- The baseline is 12 months postpartum, not pre-conception (only two pre-conception levels exist, Fig. 1); the authors
  argue 2-month postpartum levels overstate baseline.
- Trough-based "apparent clearance" (dose/C_trough) overstates true CL/F: 153.4 L/day is 6.4 L/h (derived), against
  about 3.9 L/h in typical adult models. Ratios between periods are less affected.
- Lamotrigine doses changed during pregnancy in the dual-therapy patients.
