## Study
A label. Pramlintide (as acetate) 1000 mcg/mL, SymlinPen 60 (1.5 mL) and SymlinPen 120 (2.7 mL), p. 4.
Dosing (section 2, pp. 2-3): mealtime insulin cut by 50% at initiation; type 2 start 60 mcg SC before major
meals, increase to 120 mcg; type 1 start 15 mcg, 15-mcg steps to 30, 45 or 60 mcg, at least 3 days between
steps; abdomen or thigh, arm not recommended.

Other 12.3 statements (pp. 15-16): dose-linear Cmax and AUC; arm injection in obese patients 20-36% higher
exposure with AUC CV 73-106%; no effect of obesity, BMI, skinfold or needle length (6.0 vs 12.7 mm) on
relative bioavailability; active metabolite des-Lys1 pramlintide; no accumulation.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Gastric emptying, postprandial glucagon and food intake (12.1-12.2, pp. 12-14); no concentration-effect
relationship, no EC50. Acetaminophen Cmax down 29/23/20% and Tmax delayed 72/48/48 min when dosed 0/1/2 h
after 120 mcg (n = 24); norgestrel Cmax down about 30%; ampicillin Tmax delayed about 60 min (p. 16-17).

## Covariates
Renal impairment: single 60 mcg in type 1 diabetes (4 normal, 9 mild, 5 moderate, 3 severe; ClCr 15-29 for
severe), no significant AUC or Cmax difference, high variability; no dose change for ClCr 15-89 (8.6, p. 11);
ESRD, hepatic, geriatric, sex and race PK not studied (pp. 15-16).

## Caveats
Approximate label statements; Table 5 gives means only, without n or SD.
