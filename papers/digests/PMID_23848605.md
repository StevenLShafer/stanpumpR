# Battino 2013: seizure control and dose changes in pregnancy (EURAP registry)

## Study
Prospective observational international registry (EURAP, 42 countries, from 1999) whose primary aim is
malformation risk. This analysis: 3,806 pregnancies in 3,451 women entering pregnancy on monotherapy with
carbamazepine (CBZ), lamotrigine (LTG), phenobarbital (PB) or valproate (VPA), enrolled before gestational week
16 (mean 8.9 ± 3.3 weeks); age 29.5 ± 5.0 y (15.3-43.5) (PDF p2, printed 1622). Physician reports each
trimester, at birth and at 12 months; seizures categorised as GTCS or other, frequency in six categories.
Dose groups set at conception (PB < 150 vs ≥ 150 mg/day). Chi-square tests (JMP 7.0.2). **No serum drug
concentrations were collected**, nor the reasons for dose changes (p6, printed 1626).

## Model and parameters
No pharmacokinetic model or parameters. The only quantitative dose data are prescribing changes.
```yaml
parameters: []  # no PK parameters; registry of seizure control and prescribed dose changes
```
Treatment-change results (text p4, printed 1624; Tables 2-3 read from page images):
- Mean dose increase, first to third trimester: CBZ 5%, LTG 26%, **PB 11%**, VPA 6% (text p4 and abstract;
  no SD, no denominator, no definition of how the mean was computed).
- Dose increase and/or added AED, first to third trimester: CBZ 16.7%, LTG 47.7%, PB 29.3%, VPA 20.5%
  (p < 0.0001). Table 2, PB: < 150 mg/day 53/166 (31.9%), ≥ 150 mg/day 10/49 (20.4%) (derived: 63/215 =
  29.3%); dose increased in 49 and 9, other AED added in 4 and 1.
- Table 3 (seizure-free pregnancies only): AED load increased third vs first trimester in PB 43/157 (27.4%), LTG
  253/722 (35.0%), CBZ 80/914 (8.8%), VPA 106/728 (14.6%).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End points are clinical: seizure freedom, GTCS, change in seizure-frequency category, status epilepticus.
Table 1 (p3, image), PB: < 150 mg/day n 165, seizure-free 126 (76.4%), GTCS 20 (12.1%); > 150 mg/day n 49,
seizure-free 31 (63.3%), GTCS 10 (20.4%). Whole cohort 66.6% seizure-free, GTCS 15.2%; LTG worst (58.2%
seizure-free, GTCS 21.1%). Worsening after the first trimester: PB 11.7%, LTG 19.9%, CBZ 14.6%, VPA 13.2%. 21
status epilepticus cases (10 convulsive), no maternal deaths. No concentration-effect relation, no therapeutic
range. Not an opioid paper: no MEAC, ke0, tPeak or potency ratio.

## Covariates
Epilepsy type (idiopathic generalised 73.6% seizure-free vs localisation-related 59.5%), AED and dose group at
conception, first-trimester seizures (more dose increases when present: 35.4% vs 15.3%).

## Caveats
Not population based; not randomised (higher doses track more severe epilepsy); seizure classification broad;
no serum levels and no reasons for treatment changes, so dose changes are prescribing behaviour, not a
measure of clearance. The 11% PB mean dose increase is not a pharmacokinetic ratio. Labels differ: Table 1
prints "PB > 150"; Table 2 and the footnotes use "PB ≥ 150". Small wording mismatches between text and Table 1 (CBZ
≥ 1,000 seizure-free 41.9% in text vs 42.3% in table; LTG ≥ 300 41.2% vs 41.3%).
