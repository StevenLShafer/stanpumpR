# Fung 1987: pharmacokinetics and pharmacodynamics of organic nitrates (symposium review)

## Study
Symposium review (SUNY Buffalo) of nitroglycerin (NTG), isosorbide dinitrate and isosorbide-5-mononitrate kinetics,
the factors that change them, and nitrate tolerance. Includes a summary of the author's rat study of NTG clearance
against cardiac output (Fung, Blei, Chong, J Pharmacol Exp Ther 1986).

ISDN: half-life 10 and 65 min, venous CL 4 L/min, Vd 4 L/kg, small AV extraction; 5-ISMN: 276 min, 0.1 L/min,
0.6 L/kg (Table I).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Qualitative: tolerance shifts the concentration-response curve right while concentrations rise; proposed mechanism
reduced vascular metabolic activation (PDF p4-5). No numbers usable for a model.

## Covariates
Cardiac output (rats only, quantitatively). Heart failure: Fung cites Armstrong 1980, where the more congested CHF
group had lower NTG clearance, but notes that group also received a much higher dose (mean 268 vs 38 ug/min) so dose
dependence could explain it (PDF p3). Noonan 1985's dose and time dependence cited (PDF p4).

## Caveats
Review; the cardiac-output relation was measured in rats, with arterial and venous samples, during NTG +/- vasopressin.
Table I values are summary figures without stated sources.
