# Martin-Cerezuela 2024: isavuconazole PK in the critically ill

## Study
Prospective observational study, one 24-bed medical ICU in Valencia, Jan-Dec 2023 (PDF p. 2). 24 adults on IV isavuconazole for at least 96 h. Regimen: 200 mg q8h for six doses, then 200 mg q24h; 60-min infusion. Samples after 48-96 h of treatment: predose (Cmin), 1 h after the end of infusion (Cmax), 12 h after (C50), EDTA plasma, HPLC with fluorescence detection (ChromSystems kit; excitation 261 nm, emission 366 nm); total concentration only, no free fraction. 71 samples in all. Population PK in NONMEM 5 (FOCE-I, ADVAN1 TRANS2), forward stepwise covariate selection (p<0.05).

Patients (Table 1, PDF p. 4): age 53.2 (16.4) y, 70.8% male, weight 75.1 (12.6) kg, BMI 26.03 (3.77), SAPS3 median 63; 95.8% ventilated; 8 on CRRT (33.3%), 7 on ECMO (29.1%); creatinine median 0.61 (0.47-0.79) (unit not stated in the table); albumin 2.4 (0.4) (the table gives g/L, which cannot be right for 2.4: probably g/dL); ICU mortality 58.3%. Fungal infection confirmed in 11 of 24.

## Model and parameters
One compartment, first-order elimination, IV input, no covariates retained (PDF p. 5; Table 2 confirmed on the page image).

Derived: k = CL/V = 0.044 /h, half-life about 15.8 h (derived: ln 2 x 122/5.36).

Observed concentrations (PDF p. 5): Cmin mean 1.76 (1.02) mg/L, median 1.7, range 0.1-4.5; Cmax (1 h after infusion) 3.99 (1.96), median 4, range 0.7-7.4; C50 (12 h) 2.26 (1.20), median 2, range 0.3-4.6 mg/L.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD model, ke0 or EC50. Target used: trough 1-5 mg/L ("most authors accept", refs 3, 20, 22, 23; PDF p. 2); 87.5% (21) of patients had a Cmin in that range, 12.5% (3) below 1 mg/L, none above 5; six patients (20.8%) had Cmax >5 mg/L without toxicity. Cmin did not differ between responders and non-responders (clinical 1.85 vs 1.45 mg/L, p 0.558; microbiological 1.66 vs 1.8, p 0.871; Table 3, PDF p. 6) or by ECMO or CRRT. No relationship of concentration to toxicity (p 0.779). Clinical response 6 of 11 with a confirmed diagnosis; microbiological response 8 of 11. Total (not free) plasma concentration, HPLC.

## Covariates
Tested: age, sex, weight, SAPS3, creatinine, bilirubin, AST, ALT, GGT, albumin, CRRT, ECMO. None significant for V or CL (Fig 2, PDF p. 7); ECMO and CRRT did not change V or CL. The group means do not exclude a real effect: n = 24.

## Caveats
- 24 patients, no pharmacokinetic follow-up beyond one profile of three samples at 48-96 h; IIV (67% and 45.3%) and SEs are wide.
- Sampling window 12 h after dose limits the terminal phase (see open question).
- No protein-binding measurement; total concentration only; no drug-interaction analysis.
- Few patients below 1 mg/L, so concentration-response could not be tested at low levels.
- The table's SD of AUC (48.5) exceeds its mean (37.31).
- PDF p. 5's text layer is duplicated and corrupted; I used the page image for every number here.
