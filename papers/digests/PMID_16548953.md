# Chapman 2006: effect-site controlled, patient-maintained propofol sedation in dental patients

## Study
Open single-arm safety/efficacy study, 40 ASA 1-2 patients (10 M, 30 F, age 32.4 (12.7) y) for oral surgery (37 third-molar extractions), Glasgow dental school, anaesthetist present. Patient presses handset (double press within 1 s) to raise the effect-site target by 0.2 mcg/mL from an initial 1.0 mcg/mL; the button is enabled once predicted plasma concentration is within 10% of the target; plasma overshoot up to 100% above target is allowed. When the patient is ready for the local anaesthetic, the handset is removed and the effect-site concentration held to the end. Outcomes: lowest SpO2 without oxygen, patient and surgeon questionnaire scores.

## Model and parameters
Marsh PK model (ref 11) with t1/2 ke0 2.6 min; no parameters tabulated. No estimation.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Not an opioid paper: no MEAC, opioid ke0 or potency ratio. Propofol end point: modified OAA/S sedation score and procedure tolerance. Final effect-site concentration before local anaesthetic, held to the end: mean 1.5 (SD 0.4) mcg/mL, range 1.0-2.5. 55.7 (37.7)% of procedure time at score 5; 24 patients (60%) reached score 4; one reached 3; no one reached 2. Mean time from start of sedation to start of procedure 8.9 (4.8) min (compared by the authors with 13 min in an earlier plasma-controlled study). Lowest SpO2 mean 96 (2.4)%; none below 90%. 39/40 completed. No EC50/gamma.

## Covariates
None.

## Caveats
Single arm, healthy young mostly female patients, anaesthetist present; authors say safety needs further confirmation. Predicted, not measured, concentrations.
