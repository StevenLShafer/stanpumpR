# Edwards 1989: in vitro activity of SM-7338 (meropenem)

## Study
In vitro susceptibility study, ICI Pharmaceuticals, UK. 1,102 unselected clinical isolates, mostly UK hospitals since 1983 (many beta-lactam or gentamicin resistant), tested against SM-7338 and comparators (imipenem with cilastatin source, ceftazidime, cefotaxime, piperacillin, gentamicin; metronidazole and clindamycin for anaerobes). MIC by agar dilution, Iso-Sensitest agar (+5% horse blood for fastidious organisms or anaerobes), inoculum about 10^4 CFU per spot, overnight 37 C; MIC = lowest concentration with no visible growth (p. 216, methods).
MBC (97 strains: 30 reference strains plus clinical isolates; 6 S. aureus, 5 CoNS, 5 E. faecalis, 57 Enterobacteriaceae, 5 Acinetobacter, 7 P. aeruginosa, 5 P. cepacia, 1 X. maltophilia, 6 Bacteroides): MIC first by broth dilution (IST broth aerobes, Wilkins-Chalgren anaerobes), final inoculum 10^5 CFU/mL; MBC = lowest concentration giving >=3 log10 kill of the starting inoculum by viable counts after overnight incubation (p. 216). No PK, no human data.

Other text-stated MICs: 0.13 ug/mL inhibits 90% of methicillin-susceptible S. aureus; 0.5 ug/mL for methicillin-resistant strains; 90% MIC range <0.008 to 0.13 ug/mL for Haemophilus, Branhamella, Neisseria and Enterobacteriaceae; all 141 Pseudomonas spp. inhibited at 8 ug/mL; E. faecalis 100% at 8 ug/mL; ESBL/plasmid beta-lactamase strains (TEM-3, TEM-5) all inhibited at 0.5 ug/mL (Table 2); 10 or 25% human serum, pH 6-8 and inoculum 10^4-10^6 did not significantly change MICs (p. 221-222). Concentrations are total drug in broth/agar (serum added only at 10 or 25%, heat-inactivated).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point is visible growth (MIC) or >=3 log kill (MBC); no ke0, EC50, MEAC or hysteresis. Not an opioid. MBC/MIC ratio distribution (Table 4): ratio 1 in 59% (57/97), <=2 in 90% (87/97, derived), <=4 in 99% (96/97, derived), 8 in 1.

## Covariates
None (in vitro). Medium dependence noted only for X. maltophilia.

## Caveats
Mixed panel; Table 4 not split by organism; MBC read after overnight incubation (not 24 h time-kill); single inoculum 10^5 CFU/mL for MBC; 1989 methodology, pre-CLSI/EUCAST standardisation; drug identity given only as SM-7338.
