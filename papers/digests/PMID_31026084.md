# Saito 2019: ipragliflozin population PK and a renal-function-dependent UGE Emax model

## Study
Seven Astellas studies, all Japanese (pp2-3). All authors are Astellas employees.

| Study | Subjects | Design and dosing | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | healthy men, n = 84 (60 active / 24 placebo) | single fasted doses of 1-300 mg; multiple fed doses of 20-100 mg | PK/PD |
| B | T2DM | 50 or 100 mg for 14 days | PK/PD and PopPK base |
| C | T2DM | single 50 mg; normal, mild and moderate renal impairment | PK/PD and PopPK base |
| D | T2DM | phase II, 12.5-100 mg for 12 weeks | PopPK, troughs |
| E | T2DM | phase III, 50 mg for 16 weeks | PopPK, troughs |
| F | T2DM | 52-week, 50 → 100 mg | PopPK, troughs |
| G | T2DM with mild or moderate renal impairment | 52-week (first 24 weeks used) | PopPK, troughs |

- **T2DM cohort (Table 1, p5):** 887 patients in total.
  - Age: mean 58.7 years (range 26-86).
  - Weight: mean 68.19 kg (41.5-128.0).
  - BSA: mean 1.732 m².
  - GFR, BSA-corrected: mean 84.46 mL/min (24.1-181.5).
  - Renal function: 318 normal, 466 mild, 103 moderate, none severe.
  - FPG: mean 168.3 mg/dL.
- **Healthy subjects:** mean GFR 101.28 mL/min, FPG 92.6 mg/dL.
- **Renal function:** eGFR by the Japanese MDRD equation, converted to mL/min with the Du Bois BSA.
- **Assay:** LC-MS/MS, LLOQ 1 ng/mL.
- **Software:** NONMEM 7.1.0, FOCE-I.

## Model and parameters
**PK/PD (Table 2, p8):** ΔUGE24h = Emax · AUC24h/(EC50 + AUC24h). The data were 155 ΔUGE24h values from 111
subjects (65 healthy, 46 T2DM); the 1 and 3 mg doses were excluded. AUC24h was by noncompartmental analysis.

- If FAC = FPG × GFR ≤ 18,000: Emax = 72.3 × (FPG/100)^1.37 × (GFR/90)^0.623 g/24 h.
- If FAC > 18,000: Emax = 107 g/24 h.
- No IIV on Emax or EC50.

**PopPK (Table 3, p8):**
- Structure: two compartments with first-order absorption (ADVAN4 TRANS4).
- Prior model: 534 concentrations from 43 patients in studies B-C.
- Final model: 3714 troughs from 630 patients in studies D-G, fitted with NONMEM $PRIOR.
- Covariates (Eq. 4): CL/F = 9.47 × (GFR/90)^0.233 × (TPRO/7.0)^−0.417 × (TBIL/0.8)^−0.0681 × (BSA/1.73)^0.610.

**By renal function at 50 mg (Table 5, p10):**
- AUC24h: 5083 normal, 5474 mild, 5969 moderate ng·h/mL.
- ΔUGE24h: 86, 89 and 65 g.
- Derived moderate/normal ratios: 1.17 for AUC and 0.76 for ΔUGE, as the abstract states.

**Label-level background (p2):** oral F above 90%, protein binding about 96%, cleared mainly by
glucuronidation, about 1% excreted unchanged in urine.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- **End point:** 24-h urinary glucose excretion change from baseline (ΔUGE24h, g), driven by AUC24h.
- **Effect size:** the Discussion gives Emax about 140 g/24 h at FPG 160 mg/dL and GFR 90 mL/min. That is
  consistent with Eq. 2: derived 72.3 × 1.6^1.37 = 138 g/24 h, with FAC 14,400 below the threshold.
- No effect compartment and no FPG or HbA1c model.

## Covariates
- **CL/F:** GFR, total protein, total bilirubin and BSA (Eq. 4). GFR is the dominant one; each of the others
  changes exposure by 10% or less (p10).
- **Emax:** FPG and GFR, with a threshold at FAC 18,000.

## Caveats
- Japanese subjects only. No severe renal impairment was studied, and the authors caution against extrapolating
  to it.
- The late-phase data are troughs only, so IIV is on CL/F alone.
- Each subject contributed only 1-2 ΔUGE values, so there is no IIV on the PD.
- In Table 1 the total GFR range (24.1-181.5 mL/min) exceeds the ranges of both subgroups (29.8-169.8 and
  24.1-175.4). The paper does not explain this.
