# Drake 1991: temazepam Gelthix vs liquid-filled soft gelatin capsules

## Study
Open, randomized, two-period crossover bioequivalence study (sponsor: Farmitalia Carlo Erba). 24 healthy
volunteers, 16 men and 8 women, aged 18-43 y, mean age 29 (SD 7) y, mean weight 70.8 (SD 11) kg (p. 347).
Single oral dose of temazepam 20 mg as Gelthix (stiff-gel soft capsule designed to resist intravenous abuse) or
the reference liquid-filled (PEG 400) soft gelatin capsule, washout at least 7 days; fasted 10 h before to 4 h
after the dose, 200 mL water, dosed about 08:00 (p. 346). Venous plasma (lithium heparin) at 0, 10, 20, 30, 40,
50, 60, 75, 90 min and 2, 3, 6, 9, 12, 24 h. Assay: ether extraction at pH 9, reversed-phase HPLC with UV
detection at 230 nm, diazepam internal standard; LOQ 30 ng/mL, linear 30-500 ng/mL, CV 13.5% at 30 ng/mL
(p. 347). Analysis: each subject and formulation fitted by the method of residuals to a triexponential
(two-compartment, first-order absorption with lag) equation; ANOVA of log-transformed parameters; Tmax by
Wilcoxon test (pp. 346-347).

## Model and parameters
Individual two-compartment fits; Table 1 (PDF p. 4, printed p. 348, confirmed on the rendered page) reports
geometric means (back-transformed log means; p. 350) and the 95% CI of the Gelthix/liquid ratio:

| Parameter | Gelthix | Liquid-fill | 95% CI ratio |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cmax, ng/mL | 616.6 | 707.9 (P<0.05) | 73.5-100 |
| Tmax, min (median) | 40.0 | 30.0 (NS) | n/a |
| log10 AUC | 5.23 | 5.26 (P<0.05) | 88.7-100 |
| Ka, min^-1 | 0.123 | 0.138 (NS) | 64.0-126 |
| t1/2 alpha, min | 29.5 | 32.4 (NS) | 68.0-122 |
| t1/2 beta, h | 6.3 | 6.6 (NS) | 77.0-115 |

Other values: Tmax range 20-75 min (Gelthix) and 20-120 min (liquid) (p. 347). Fitted lag time 20 min (Gelthix)
and 15 min (liquid) (p. 347). By 24 h, 83.6% and 82.5% of AUC-infinity was reached (Fig. 2, p. 348); t1/2 beta
exceeded 18 h in two subjects. The authors note that the "conventional" slope method on the same data gives
distribution half-lives of 1.35 h (Gelthix) and 1.52 h (liquid), close to earlier published 1.3-1.4 h; the
29.5-32.4 min values come from resolving absorption, distribution and elimination by residuals (p. 350).
The unit of AUC is not stated. No volumes, clearance or absolute bioavailability are reported.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No pharmacodynamic measurements, no ke0 / t1/2ke0 / tPeak. The discussion cites (ref. 3, a 1984 Launchbury
monograph) plasma temazepam 200-300 ng/mL in the first hour as effective for hypnotic and anxiolytic activity;
every subject's Cmax exceeded 300 ng/mL (p. 350). This is a cited threshold, not measured here. Not an opioid; no
MEAC or potency ratio.

## Covariates
None examined (formulation and period only).

## Caveats
- Industry bioequivalence study; healthy, fasted, morning dosing; n = 24.
- Cmax values are geometric means of individual maxima, which run higher than the peak of the mean curve
  (Fig. 1's mean liquid-fill curve peaks near 600 ng/mL, read by eye; the Gelthix curve lower).
- The 0.5 h distribution half-life is a method-dependent number: the same data give 1.35-1.52 h by the
  conventional method (p. 350).
- Sampling stopped at 24 h, so t1/2 beta (6.3-6.6 h) may be underestimated; two subjects exceeded 18 h.
- AUC unit not given. If it were ng.min/mL, 10^5.26 would be about 3030 ng.h/mL (derived: an assumption, not
  stated by the paper).
- The 30 ng/mL LOQ censored the earliest samples (only 1-4 subjects quantifiable at 10 min).
