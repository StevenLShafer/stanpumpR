# Drake 1991: temazepam Gelthix vs liquid-filled soft gelatin, oral bioequivalence

## Study
Open, randomised, two-period crossover in 24 healthy volunteers (16 men, 8 women; mean age
29 y, SD 7; mean weight 70.8 kg, SD 11), no significant disease. Each received a single oral
20 mg dose of temazepam in two forms — the abuse-resistant Gelthix stiff-gel softgel (TG) and
the reference liquid-filled softgel (TL) — in randomised order, ≥7-day washout, fasted 10 h
before to 4 h after, 200 mL water. Blood at 10, 20, 30, 40, 50, 60, 75, 90 min and 2, 3, 6, 9,
12, 24 h. Temazepam by HPLC/UV at 230 nm (diazepam internal standard); limit of quantification
30 ng/mL, linear 30-500 ng/mL. Two-compartment analysis by the method of residuals, fitting a
triexponential (absorption + distribution + elimination) per subject; means are geometric.

## Model and parameters
Summary PK, Table 1 (printed p.348, PDF p.4; geometric means from log-transformed data).
Confirmed on the rendered page image (the text layer interleaves the two columns). Both
formulations are 20 mg oral softgels.

| Parameter | Gelthix | Liquid | 95% CI ratio (TG/TL) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cmax (ng/mL) | 616.6 | 707.9 * | 73.5-100 |
| Tmax (min), median | 40.0 | 30.0 (NS) | N/A |
| Log10 AUC | 5.23 | 5.26 * | 88.7-100 |
| Ka (min⁻¹) | 0.123 | 0.138 (NS) | 64.0-126 |
| t½α distribution (min) | 29.5 | 32.4 (NS) | 68.0-122 |
| t½β elimination (h) | 6.3 | 6.6 (NS) | 77.0-115 |

(* P<0.05; NS = not significant.) Cmax and AUC differed significantly between formulations but
AUC stayed within 80-120% bioequivalence limits. Distribution phase complete within 6 h; by
24 h ~83.6% (TG) / 82.5% (TL) eliminated; t½β exceeded 18 h in two subjects (text, p.347).
The authors note their true distribution half-life (29.5/32.4 min, from curve-fitting) is lower
than the 1.3-1.4 h of earlier studies that read it off the descending slope; conventional
analysis here gave 1.35 h (TG) / 1.52 h (TL) (Discussion, p.350).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD end point measured (a bioequivalence PK study). No ke0, effect site or hysteresis. The
Discussion (printed p.350) cites a received efficacy threshold: plasma temazepam 200-300 ng/mL
in the first hour is regarded as effective for hypnotic/anxiolytic activity (citing ref 3), and
both formulations' Cmax exceeded the 300 ng/mL threshold in all 24 subjects. This is a cited
reference range, not an EC50 measured here.

## Covariates
None. Single fixed 20 mg dose; no body-size or demographic modelling.

## Caveats
Small healthy-volunteer study (n=24), single dose. Means are geometric from log-transformed
data. Distribution and elimination half-lives had high variability (wide CIs). The true
distribution half-life depends on the curve-fitting method used (method of residuals), and
differs from slope-read values in older studies. No intravenous arm, so absolute
bioavailability and systemic clearance are not determined.
