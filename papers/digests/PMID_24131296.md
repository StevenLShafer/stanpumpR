# Matsumura 2014: IV nicardipine in severe pre-eclampsia, placenta and breast milk

## Study
Observational, single centre (Saitama), June 2011-Oct 2012. 18 women with severe pre-eclampsia (BP >= 160/110 and
proteinuria >= 0.3 g/24 h after 20 weeks), 15 singleton and 3 twin pregnancies; mean age 35 y (25-42); GA at
start of nicardipine mean 32-3/7 weeks; all delivered by caesarean at mean 34-4/7 weeks (Table 1, p3). Maternal
weight not reported. IV nicardipine started at 0.5 mg/h, increased by 0.5 mg/h to SBP < 160 and DBP < 110, maximum
80 mg/day; infused 11.9 +/- 10.5 days (1-70) before and 4.6 +/- 1.6 days (1-10) after delivery (p2, p4). Maternal
plasma at delivery and postpartum days 2-7 (on infusion), cord arterial and venous plasma at delivery, breast milk
on the postpartum days. LC-MS/MS, LLOQ 0.1 ng/mL in plasma and milk (p2-p3).

Regressions: MPd = 19.75 + 28.97 x dose (mg/kg/day), r2 0.300 (Fig 1); UaP = 1.07 + 0.08 MPd, r2 0.396; UvP = 1.04 +
0.08 MPd, r2 0.591; milk = -0.95 + 0.15 x postpartum plasma, r2 0.72 (p5-p7).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
BP fell from 166.2 +/- 9.1 / 100.5 +/- 5.1 before infusion to 138.4 +/- 10.8 / 89.6 +/- 7.5 mmHg at delivery; HR
83.9 -> 87.9 (n.s.) (Table 2, p4). No concentration-effect analysis, no ke0, no MEAC.

## Covariates
None modelled. Authors: delivery and postpartum clearance did not differ clearly and overlap the healthy-male range
they quote (319-920 mL/kg/h, citing Modi 1993 and Higuchi 1980), so pregnancy CYP3A up-regulation is judged not to
change nicardipine PK markedly (p8).

## Caveats
- Table 4's dose rows are internally impossible: a mean (0.23) below the median (0.39) with a minimum of 0.14 and 18
  patients cannot hold (half the values >= 0.39 force a mean >= 0.265), and the postpartum mean (0.26) lies below
  its own minimum (0.43). The printed dose means are probably wrong; the medians fit the other rows (0.39 mg/kg/day
  / 24 / 34.35 ng/mL = 473 mL/h/kg, near the 579 median clearance; derived). Unresolved source error.
- UvP median 4.79 lies near its range maximum (5.34) while its mean is 3.73; possible but odd.
- Clearance is dose/concentration from one sample per period, steady state assumed, total body weight not
  reported; no healthy control group. Table 1 "GA at admission: mean 31-3/7" vs text "median 31 weeks 2 days".
- The Discussion quotes Bartels 2007 milk levels as "5.1 to 18.5 mg/l"; Bartels reports ng/mL (a unit slip).
