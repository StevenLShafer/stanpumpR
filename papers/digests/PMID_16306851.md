# de Jonge 2005: population PK of cyclophosphamide and three metabolites with thiotepa and carboplatin

## Study
- 35 patients (13 men, 22 women), 70 courses; metastatic or high-risk breast, relapsed germ-cell or ovarian cancer.
  Table 1 (PDF p3, printed 758): age median 41 y (16-59), BSA 1.8 m2 (1.6-2.3), weight 71 kg (55-129), height
  172 cm (156-198).
- CTC: cyclophosphamide (CP) 1500 mg/m2/d as a 1-h infusion, then carboplatin 400 mg/m2/d over 1 h, then thiotepa
  120 mg/m2/d in two 30-min infusions 12 h apart, for 4 days. "Tiny" CTC (tCTC): two-thirds of each dose
  (CP 1000 mg/m2/d). Three patients had a reversed sequence on one day. Mesna, dexamethasone, granisetron,
  ciprofloxacin, amphotericin B co-medication.
- About 20 plasma samples per patient per course (full profiles on day 1 and day 3 or 4, troughs daily, day 5 and
  day 7). Assays: CP, thiotepa, tepa by GC; 4OHCP by HPLC-UV as the semicarbazone; 2DCECP by GC; PM by HPLC-UV.
  2DCECP missing in 5 courses, PM in 24.
- NONMEM V (FO), log-transformed data, extending the authors' 2004 CP-thiotepa model (de Jonge 2004, J
  Pharmacokinet Pharmacodyn 31:135); thiotepa/tepa individual estimates fixed from a first step.

## Model and parameters
Structure (Fig 3, Table 2, PDF p4, printed 759; confirmed on the rendered page):
- CP two compartments. Before induction CL splits 75% to 4OHCP (x active enzyme ENZ1act), 20% non-inducible (renal),
  5% to 2DCECP (x enzyme ENZ3); fractions fixed from literature.
- Enzymes start at 1. ENZ1act and ENZ3 rise at **zero order** (kenz1, kenz3) while CP is present
  (factor [CP]/(0.01 + [CP]), 0.01 umol/L, so in effect an on-off switch); no enzyme degradation term (turnover could
  not be estimated). Thiotepa converts ENZ1act to ENZ1inact (kass x A(TT) x A(ENZ1act)) and it returns at kdiss.
  The text layer prints the switch constant as "0.018"; the rendered page shows 0.01 with a degree-sign footnote mark.
- 4OHCP one compartment, V fixed at 1 L (flip-flop), fully converted to PM. PM and 2DCECP one compartment each.

tCTC: CP 4257 (2169-6376) 47; 4OHCP 123 (83-194) 47; 2DCECP 515 (120-978) 44; PM 509 (219-1433) 31.

Simulated induction over 4 days (Results, Discussion): CP AUC down about 50%; 4OHCP and PM up about 50%, 2DCECP up
about 35%. Derived: k73 = 0.00833 /h gives a peripheral return half-time of about 83 h; k37/k73 implies a
peripheral volume of about 267 L (derived: 43.7 x 0.0509/0.00833), poorly determined (RSE 89%).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No exposure-toxicity or exposure-response analysis; concentrations are plasma (total) in umol/L. Not an
opioid; no MEAC, ke0 or potency ratio.

## Covariates
None reported (no BSA, weight, sex or age effects in the final model). Thiotepa co-administration is structural.

## Caveats
- Every parameter was estimated with thiotepa (and carboplatin) present; thiotepa individual estimates came from a
  prior step. The "apparent" clearances assume fixed literature fractions (75/20/5%) and full 4OHCP-to-PM conversion.
- Enzyme amounts grow without bound while CP is present (no turnover), valid only over the 4-day course studied.
- 5 patients (8 of 70 courses) had unexplained high 2DCECP that the model under-predicted (Fig 4E).
- FO estimation; k73 imprecise (RSE 89%). Table 4 title says 72 courses vs 70 elsewhere.
