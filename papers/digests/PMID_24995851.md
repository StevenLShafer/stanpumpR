# Valade 2014: emtricitabine population PK in pregnancy

## Study
Population PK of emtricitabine (FTC) in HIV-infected women, combining a therapeutic-drug-monitoring (TDM) cohort at Cochin Hospital (chronic oral **200 mg** once daily; routine sparse sampling) with the TEmAA ANRS 12109 study (single oral **400 mg** at onset of labour; samples at 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 12, 24 h). **179 women** (148 Cochin, 31 TEmAA): 103 non-pregnant, 83 pregnant (7 appeared in both groups on different occasions); among the pregnant, 1 first-trimester, 26 second-trimester, 67 third-trimester (48 on day of delivery). **457 FTC plasma concentrations** (307 in pregnant women). Demographics (Table 1, PDF p.4 / printed 1381, image-confirmed), median (range): whole population age 35 y (16–72), BW 69 kg (37–130), Scr 60 µmol/L (27–183), CLcr 135 mL/min (35–335), GA 37 wk (5–41); pregnant women age 31 (19–43), BW 70 (47–106), Scr 49 (27–119), CLcr 160 (70–335); non-pregnant age 40 (16–72), BW 69 (37–130), Scr 66 (35–183), CLcr 106 (35–247). Assay: LC-MS/MS, LOQ 0.01 mg/L, intra-assay 3.6%, inter-assay 7.9% (PDF p.2). CLcr by Cockcroft–Gault. Software: Monolix 4.1.3 (SAEM); NPDE/VPC validation.

## Model and parameters

Final covariate model: **CL/F = 22.3 × (CLcr/135)^0.33** (CLcr in mL/min; PDF p.4, image-confirmed). CLcr was the only retained covariate; pregnancy acted on FTC PK only through its effect on CLcr (median CLcr 160 vs 106 mL/min pregnant vs non-pregnant → CL/F geometric mean 24.1 vs 20.5 L/h, +18%).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Not an anaesthetic/opioid drug — no ke0, no MEAC, no analgesic concentration. PD anchor is antiviral: the **in vitro IC50 = 0.004 mg/L** (refs 6, 22); 100% of pregnant-women C24 values exceeded it (Table 4). A prior exposure–response (ref 6) showed anti-HIV activity plateauing at a daily **AUC ≈ 10 mg·L⁻¹·h**; a secondary target of **AUC ≥ 7 mg·L⁻¹·h** was used (92% of pregnant women met it vs 100% non-pregnant). ## Covariates
Only **creatinine clearance on CL/F** (power 0.33). Pregnancy/GA, age, BW, Scr all individually decreased OFV, but after CLcr was added none improved the model further — i.e. the pregnancy effect is fully mediated by the ~50% rise in estimated GFR (median CLcr 160 vs 106 mL/min).

## Caveats
Oral, apparent model (F unknown). Two very different dosing regimens pooled (200 mg chronic TDM; 400 mg single-dose labour) with separate residual errors. Cockcroft–Gault may overestimate GFR in pregnancy (weight gain is water/fat, not muscle). Only one first-trimester woman, so no first-trimester estimates. Authors conclude the 15% AUC drop and 18% CL/F rise are not clinically relevant and **no dose adjustment** is needed in pregnancy, including the second trimester.
