# Koh 2019: amitriptyline and nortriptyline PK by CYP2C19/CYP2D6 genotype

## Study
Single oral 25 mg amitriptyline (Etravil) to 24 healthy Korean men, 6 in each of four genotype groups (Table 1, PDF p2): I CYP2D6 *1/*1 with CYP2C19 *1/*1, *1/*2 or *1/*3; II CYP2D6 *1/*10 or *1/*5; III CYP2D6 *10/*10, *10/*5 or *5/*5 (all with CYP2C19 not poor); IV CYP2C19 *2/*2, *2/*3 or *3/*3 with any of the CYP2D6 genotypes. Age 25.00 ± 3.84 y, height 175.22 ± 5.60 cm, weight 72.06 ± 7.24 kg (Table 2 total, text layer). Samples 0, 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12, 24, 48, 72, 96 h; 373 amitriptyline and 347 nortriptyline plasma concentrations; LC-MS/MS, plasma (total, not free). NONMEM 7.3, ADVAN13, FOCEI, molar concentrations; VPC 500 replicates and bootstrap 500 (Methods, PDF p2-3).

## Model and parameters
Two-compartment, linear model for each moiety, mixed first- and zero-order absorption (zero-order release D1 into a depot, then first-order ka after a lag), enterohepatic recirculation (K24, K41, MTIME), nortriptyline formed from amitriptyline's metabolic clearance (CL_M); nortriptyline central volume fixed equal to amitriptyline's (identifiability, Discussion). Proportional residual error. Parameters are apparent (oral, F unknown). Table 3 (PDF p6, printed 537), NONMEM estimates, confirmed on the page image:

Genotype effect: adding CYP2C19 genotype to the formation pathway dropped the objective function 34.12 (Results, PDF p6). CYP2D6 on the other pathways was not significant (MAP-clearance ANOVA and Kruskal-Wallis, Results and Discussion).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No concentration-effect, MEAC, ke0 or potency ratio is reported. Not an opioid.

## Covariates
Only genotype: CYP2C19 poor (*2/*2, *2/*3, *3/*3) versus others on formation clearance; CYP2D6 *10/*10 and *10/*5 versus others on non-formation clearance. No weight, age or sex effect (the groups did not differ in them; all subjects male). *17 was not genotyped.

## Caveats
Healthy Korean men, single dose, n = 6 per group; CYP2D6 effect not statistically significant, and its CI for the low group (0.72-25.88 L/h) is very wide; Table 3 CIs for several parameters (K41, MTIME2) are extremely wide; nortriptyline Vc fixed to amitriptyline's, so the split between formation and nortriptyline clearance is arbitrary (only their ratio is identified). The salt of the dose is not stated. The Discussion refers once to 'CYP2C9' where CYP2C19 is meant (Results, PDF p5).
