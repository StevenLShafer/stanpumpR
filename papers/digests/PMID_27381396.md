# Desai 2016: isavuconazole population PK and target attainment

## Study
Nine phase 1 studies (healthy subjects, single and multiple doses, oral and 1 h IV, 40-400 mg isavuconazole, including hepatic or renal impairment and elderly) plus the phase 3 SECURE trial (patients: 200 mg IV every 8 h x 6 doses, then 200 mg daily IV or oral; sparse sampling, mostly troughs). 421 individuals (189 healthy, 232 patients), 6,363 concentrations (PDF p2-3). Doses are isavuconazole equivalents (372 mg isavuconazonium sulfate = 200 mg isavuconazole). Plasma concentrations. NONMEM 7.2, FOCE, ADVAN4 TRANS4, log-log error, F fixed to 1.

## Model and parameters
Two-compartment, Weibull absorption, first-order elimination; IV and oral share parameters with F = 1. Table 5 (PDF p6, printed 5488, confirmed on image):

Other IIV (%): Vp 31.78, RA 40.24, Q 49.09, GAM1 45.71. Derived values in the text: V at steady state about 460 L; terminal half-life in patients mean 130 h (median 110 h, 5th-95th 53.5-248.1 h); AUC0-24 mean 97 mg h/L (healthy 92.0, patients 101.0; Table 4). Absorption complete by 2-3 h.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No measured PD. The probability-of-target-attainment analysis uses murine total-drug AUC/MIC targets (nonneutropenic model): EI50/EI80/EI90 = 24.7/29.8/33.3 (EUCAST MICs) and 50.4/55.7/59.0 (CLSI MICs). In 5,000 simulated patients on the SECURE regimen, over 90% attained the target at MIC up to 1 mg/L (EUCAST) and 0.5 mg/L (CLSI). Total, not unbound, AUC used because protein binding exceeds 99%. Not an opioid; no MEAC, ke0 or potency ratio.

## Covariates
Race on CL (the only significant CL covariate); BMI and healthy-vs-patient on Vp. Weight, age, sex, dose, liver chemistry and CYP3A inhibitors were not retained.

## Caveats
Patient data are mostly troughs; the Asian group is small (14 healthy, 39 patients) and the lower clearance is unexplained. "Lower" means lower than the predominantly Caucasian group in this same pooled model. 13% of bootstrap runs failed.
