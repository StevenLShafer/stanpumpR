# Zhou 2001: temperature and tissue solubility of volatile anesthetics

## Study
Tissue/gas partition coefficients of desflurane, sevoflurane, isoflurane, enflurane and halothane in human brain, heart (left
ventricle), liver, skeletal muscle (quadriceps) and subcutaneous fat, at 37, 33, 29, 25, 21 and 17 C (PDF p1-2).
Specimens came from 10 male traffic-accident cadavers, age 27 +/- 8 yr, taken within 6 h of death, prepared within 10 h; not all tissues from
all cadavers. Tissue homogenised in saline, two-stage syringe equilibration in a water bath, gas-phase concentration by gas
chromatography (GOW-MAC 580, FID); the tissue/gas coefficient comes from homogenate/gas corrected for the saline/gas coefficient
(Eq 4), the latter taken from the authors' earlier abstract (ref 2). Table 1 reports mean +/- SD with n = 6 per cell (the methods say 10 donors; this is the
only n given for the table).

## Model and parameters
Not a PK model. Table 1 (PDF p3, printed p236), mean +/- SD, n = 6; the tissue/gas coefficients are dimensionless. Values at 37 C and 17 C
(confirmed on the page image), with R^2 and slope. The slope is that of the regression of ln(coefficient) on temperature, stated in the paper to be the
percentage increase per C of cooling (the sign printed is negative; derived check: brain desflurane 0.62 to 1.15 over 20 C is about 3.1 %/C, so natural log).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No MEAC, ke0 or potency ratio (not an opioid paper). The paper cites a hypothermia MAC study (ref 11, Liu 2001) only to say
decreased anesthetic requirement tends to offset the slower rise in alveolar concentration (p5).

## Covariates
Temperature only (6 levels, 37-17 C). Age and race discussed as possible reasons for lower muscle values than Yasuda 1989 (young
27 +/- 8 yr here vs 66 +/- 21 yr there; Lerman 1986 found muscle coefficients rise with age), not studied.

## Caveats
Cadaver tissue, young Chinese men only; homogenate method with a saline correction taken from an abstract; n = 6 per cell although 10 donors; white/gray matter
mix in brain not controlled; muscle desflurane, sevoflurane, isoflurane and halothane values are 50-150% below Yasuda 1989's (authors' own words); no blood
sample could be taken from the cadavers, so the claim "tissue:blood does not change" rests on historical blood slopes (ref 2 abstract, Eger and Eger),
not paired blood. The measured range is 37 to 17 C; nothing above 37 C. Enflurane brain 17 C (4.93) jumps from 3.30 at 21 C (R2 0.89).
Text-layer caution: tissue labels in the extracted Table 1 sit one row off; the rendered image was used.

## Verification against stanpumpR
- R/physiology.R:514-522, 593-595, 649-668 (`gasPropertiesAt`): multiplies lambda_blood, tg_brain, tg_muscle, tg_fat of desflurane, sevoflurane and isoflurane
  by exp(-slope (T-37)), slope = the Zhou 2002 blood slopes 0.0302/0.0295/0.0382 per C. Direction and the structure (ln-linear in T, per C)
  **match** this paper. The magnitude is **partly verified**: this paper's own tissue slopes (Table 1) are not the blood slopes. Tissue slopes
  run 3.03-4.34 %/C for des/sevo/iso across tissues (brain 3.22/3.07/3.03, muscle 3.93/3.82/4.05, fat 3.25/3.78/4.34), against the
  repo's 3.02/2.95/3.82 and this paper's quoted blood values 2.86/2.65/3.73. So tissue:blood is not strictly constant in the paper's own data: muscle
  desflurane rises about 0.9 %/C faster than the repo's factor (derived: about 8% over 9 C; about 20% over 20 C). Raised as open questions
  rather than called a discrepancy, since the authors themselves conclude "proportional" and the Zhou 2002 values were not read here.
- Repo-vs-paper blood slopes (0.0302/0.0295 vs 2.86/2.65 %/C quoted on p4 of this paper from an abstract) differ; whether they are the same dataset (different haematocrit) cannot be settled from this PDF.
- inst/app-help/models/physiology-and-pharmacokinetics.md:398-399 ("tissue:gas coefficients rise in proportion, leaving tissue:blood unchanged"): **matches** the authors' conclusion.
- Halothane and enflurane (paper has data) are not temperature-adjusted by the repo's table (`GAS_SOLUBILITY_TEMPERATURE_SLOPE` has only three agents); not a discrepancy, just unused data.
- The repo adjusts only brain, muscle and fat tissue:gas columns; heart and liver, measured here, are not separate tissue columns in `gasPropertiesAt` (physiology.R:667). Not checked whether the engine
  groups them with the vessel-rich compartment.
- Transcription of 37 C coefficients: not compared; the repo's base partition table (`getGasProperties`) is not cited to this paper.
