# Bergmann 2011: CYP2C8*3 and unbound paclitaxel clearance

## Study
Prospective pharmacogenetic population-PK study in 93 evaluable Caucasian women (Denmark 89, Sweden 4) with epithelial ovarian, peritoneal or tubal cancer, WHO/ECOG 0-2. Paclitaxel 175 mg/m2 as a 3 h IV infusion in Cremophor EL (5 mL per 30 mg paclitaxel), every 3 weeks with carboplatin. Three plasma samples in one cycle: about 3 h (end of infusion), 5-8 h and 18-24 h. Total plasma paclitaxel by HPLC-UV (LOQ 25 nmol/L); Cremophor EL by colorimetric dye-binding. Genotyping of 22 SNPs in 10 genes (pyrosequencing, TaqMan, allele-specific PCR). One further patient was excluded for an implausible sample. Median (range) age 60.1 (32-79.4) y, BSA 1.71 (1.27-2.37) m2, bilirubin 5 (2-28) umol/L, albumin 42 g/L, alpha-1 acid glycoprotein 1.08 (0.5-2.75) g/L; ECOG 0/1/2 = 53/31/9 (Table 2, PDF p4).

## Model and parameters
NONMEM VI (FOCE-I) two-compartment model of **unbound** paclitaxel (Cu) from total (Cp) concentration (PDF p3): Cp = Cu + 3.31 Cu + 4.46 [CrEL] Cu + Cu Bmax/(Km + Cu), with Bmax = 0.2311 x [AAGP] - 0.017 and Km = Bmax/9.41, constants fixed from Henningsson 2003. Covariates: BSA on CL and volumes, bilirubin on CL, age and performance status added here (OFV drops 13.5 and 18.5). ## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD. Not an opioid paper. The end point is the individual empirical-Bayes clearance of unbound paclitaxel against genotype.

Key genotype results (Table 4, PDF p6, read on page image; geometric mean unbound CL in L/h with 10th-90th percentiles; P is for trend adjusted for BSA, age and performance status, uncontrolled P in brackets):
- CYP2C8*3 (1196A>G, K399R): reference N=74, 395 (297-490); heterozygote N=19, 350 (238-458); no homozygotes; P 0.03 (0.04). Text: 11% lower clearance in carriers.
- CYP2C8*4 (792C>G, exploratory): 86 at 391 (297-490), 7 at 321 (270-374); P 0.04 (0.03); text: 18% lower. One patient with both *3 and *4: 270 L/h.
- ABCC1 rs504348 (exploratory): 65 at 394 (297-548), 27 at 368 (291-470), 1 at 332; P 0.04 (0.15); text: 7% lower. Exploratory P-values are uncorrected for multiple testing.
- ABCB1 C1236T, G2677T/A, C3435T (confirmatory): no significant association (P 0.25, 0.20, 0.83). Diplotype 1/2/3 mean CL 399/381/355 L/h, trend P 0.12 (Table 1, PDF p3).
- Performance status: mean CL 397 L/h (PS 0-1) vs 291 L/h (PS 2) in the raw data (PDF p6).
- Three patients on CYP3A4/5 inhibitors: 383, 456, 282 L/h (none *3 carriers).

## Covariates
BSA, age, performance status and bilirubin on CL as above (Table 3). Tested without effect: smoking, alcohol, albumin, alkaline phosphatase, ALT (PDF p3). Genotype was not in the PK model; it was tested on the empirical-Bayes clearances afterwards.

## Caveats
Observational, Caucasian women only, mainly stage III; three sparse samples per patient; unbound clearance derived from a published binding model with fixed constants; the *3 effect is small (11%) and the authors judge genotyping not rational on that basis; exploratory SNPs uncorrected for multiplicity; the *3 effect was only just significant (P 0.03, adjusted; 0.04 uncontrolled) and the Figure 1 distributions overlap widely; no *3 homozygotes. Comparison is with CYP2C8 wild-type carriers in this same cohort, not with other studies.

## Verification against stanpumpR
No location cites it. Independent comparison with the library:
- `R/drugs_paclitaxelJoerger.R:150-168` (Joerger 2006) fits **total** plasma paclitaxel with saturable elimination and saturable distribution; the Crombag model in `R/drugs_paclitaxelCrombag.R` and `R/drugs_paclitaxelProteinBound.R` are also in the library. Bergmann's CL of 396 L/h (BSA 1.71 m2) is the linear clearance of the UNBOUND fraction, a different quantity from the total-plasma Vmax/Km terms, so no numerical reconciliation is possible without the binding equation.
- Direction of covariates agrees in sign with Joerger's: CL rises with BSA (Bergmann +6.2% per 0.1 m2) and falls with age (-6% per 10 y, as the Bergmann age term). The library's Joerger model has a bilirubin power of -0.167 held at its median; Bergmann's bilirubin factor is -0.012 per umol/L from Henningsson, a linear form.
- The library has no CYP2C8 genotype factor; Bergmann's -11% (*3) applies to unbound clearance of a heterozygote-versus-wild-type comparison in women with ovarian cancer.
