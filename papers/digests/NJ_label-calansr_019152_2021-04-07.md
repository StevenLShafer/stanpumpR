## Model and parameters
Immediate-release verapamil: more than 90% absorbed, bioavailability 20-35%, Tmax 1-2 h; mean half-life 2.8-7.4 h
single-dose, 4.5-12.0 h after repeated doses; about 90% protein bound; hepatic insufficiency half-life 14-16 h and
clearance about 30% of normal (PDF p3). CALAN SR 240 mg single dose: with food Cmax 79 ng/mL, Tmax 7.71 h, AUC0-24
841 ng·h/mL; fasting 164 ng/mL, 5.21 h, 1478 ng·h/mL (p3). Norverapamil about 20% as active, steady-state levels
about equal to verapamil's.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No concentration-effect model; no relation established between concentration and blood-pressure fall (p3). PR
prolongation correlates with concentration early in titration.

## Dosing (PDF p13-14)
With food; start 180 mg each morning (120 mg in the elderly or small people); titrate weekly to 240 mg each morning,
then 180 + 180 or 240 + 120 mg, then 240 mg every 12 h.

## Covariates
Age, hepatic function, food and CYP3A4 interactions, as in the S-045 card.

## Caveats
A duplicate of the S-045 label under a file name whose date the document does not carry.
