# Thoueille 2023: tenofovir after tenofovir alafenamide, effect of CKD

## Study
- Swiss HIV Cohort Study (project 815) plus routine TDM data from Lausanne, to Jan 2021. 569 people living with HIV: 486 for model building, 83 for validation (Table 1, PDF p3).
- Data: 877 plasma tenofovir and 100 tenofovir alafenamide (TAF) concentrations (793 tenofovir and 100 TAF in the building set; 84 tenofovir in validation). Steady state assumed; TAF 25 mg q24h, or 10 mg q24h with cobicistat, oral.
- Rich sampling in a few patients: pre-dose, 0.5, 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 24 h (44 samples from four patients); the rest sparse, 1–2 samples per person (PDF p3, p5).
- Assay: LC-MS/MS, plasma, LLOQ 1 ng/mL for tenofovir and TAF (total plasma). Doses in nmol, concentrations in nmol/mL.
- NONMEM 7.4.3, FOCE-I, PsN 4.8.0.
- Median age 51 y; Cockcroft–Gault CLCR range in the building set roughly 42–142 mL/min per the text-layer Table 1 (text-layer columns are scrambled; not confirmed).

## Model and parameters
Tenofovir alone: one compartment, first-order absorption (ka fixed at 2 /h because of TAF flip-flop kinetics), linear elimination, additive error. All parameters apparent (relative to F). Table 2 (PDF p6, read on the rendered page), full final model:

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD. No PBMC tenofovir-diphosphate model. Reference plasma tenofovir trough with TAF in normal function: 9.7 ng/mL, matching the usual TDM target cited by the authors (PDF p7); no formal therapeutic range is derived. Not an opioid.

## Covariates
CLCR (Cockcroft–Gault, reference 100 mL/min), age (median 51 y), Black/Hispanic American ethnicity (+12%), potent P-gp inhibitors (−12%; carvedilol and sertraline named), cobicistat on F. Only CLCR judged clinically relevant (accounts for 53% of the 59% of BSV on F explained). The model was fitted to oral data: CL and V are apparent.

## Caveats
Plasma tenofovir is only an indirect marker of intracellular exposure (authors, p9); TAF absorption not estimable (ka fixed); no IV data, so BSV on F stands in for all BSV; steady state assumed; hepatitis B not studied. Intracellular-driven dosing suggestions (q48h or q72h in stage 3 or 4 CKD) were not validated prospectively.
