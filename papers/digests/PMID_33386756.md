# Alvarez 2021: population PK of smoked THC and metabolites, chronic vs occasional users

## Study
Randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled crossover (NCT02061020); three sessions (placebo, 10 mg, 30 mg THC in
a cigarette with 1 g tobacco), ≥ 7 days apart. 30 healthy **male** cannabis users aged 18-34 (Methods says 20-34):
15 chronic (1-2 joints/day) and 15 occasional (1-2 joints/week); mean age 21.5 y, BMI 21.5 (Table 1, PDF p. 5).
Controlled smoking: 2 s puff every 40 s for 10 min (15 puffs); THC left in the butt measured, and the actual
quantity consumed used in analysis (CC 9.0 vs OC 8.0 mg at 10 mg; 26.4 vs 22.6 mg at 30 mg; PDF p. 5).
Whole-blood sampling (heparin) pre-dose and 5, 15, 30 min, 1, 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 24 h after end of smoking.
LC-MS/MS on whole blood: LOQ THC 0.1, 11-OH-THC 0.2, THC-COOH 1 ng/mL (PDF p. 3). 582 THC concentrations, 9% BLQ
discarded. NONMEM 7.4.1, FOCE-I, ADVAN5; concentrations modelled in µmol/L. CYP2C9 genotyped (80% *1/*1).
Oral-fluid DrugWipe 5S (THC cut-off 10 ng/mL) related to blood THC by logistic regression (690 tests).

## Model and parameters
THC: three compartments, zero-order input over the smoking duration (D1 fixed), first-order elimination from V1.
11-OH-THC and THC-COOH: two compartments each, fed first-order from THC / 11-OH-THC; their volumes not estimable,
fixed (11-OH-THC = V1/F; THC-COOH printed "= 1" L). Table 2 (PDF p. 8, printed 3146), confirmed on the rendered page:

F1 = 2.41^GRP x 0.68^DOSE x exp(eta) (Table 2 footnote). The paper's own AUC check: dose/CL = 1600 µg·min/L for
10 mg (derived check: 10 mg / 374 L/h = 1604 µg·min/L, agrees). Discussion re-parameterises assuming 25% absorbed:
Vss 2529 L and CL 1.06 L/min (10 mg), 3719 L and 1.56 L/min (30 mg) (PDF p. 7).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PK-PD model, ke0 or effect concentration. End point modelled: probability of a positive oral-fluid DrugWipe 5S
test vs blood THC (Table 3, PDF p. 9): intercept -1.197, THC (per ng/mL) 0.782, chronic group -1.812; AUROC 0.927,
accuracy 0.845; optimum blood THC cut-off 1.895 ng/mL with sensitivity 63.5%, specificity 85.1% (Fig. 5). All confirmed on the
rendered page. Not an opioid.

## Covariates
Group (chronic vs occasional) and dose (30 vs 10 mg) on relative bioavailability F1 only. CYP2C9 genotype, age,
weight, BMI, CO rise and tobacco not retained. Note "higher bioavailability" in chronic users is relative to the
occasional users of this study; it is relative, not absolute, F.

## Caveats
- All parameters apparent on an unmeasured pulmonary bioavailability (relative F only); healthy young men only;
  24 h sampling limits the terminal phase; prior use assumed away (steady-state predose not retained).
- Assay is whole blood, though figures and Table 3 say "plasma".
- Table 2 oddities on the rendered page: IIV KM1_M2 estimate 0.55 lies outside its printed CI 0.31-0.35; COV_GRP CI
  0.11-4.65 is very wide; "V THC-COOH (L) = 1" while the text says it was set to V1.
- Derived: Vss/F 14877 L x 0.25 = 3719 L and CL/F 374 L/h x 0.25 = 1.56 L/min, i.e. the typical (10 mg) values give
  the pair the Discussion labels 30 mg; the 10 mg/30 mg labels there may be swapped (not used by stanpumpR).
