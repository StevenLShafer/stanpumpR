# Adams 2004: single-dose and steady-state PK of oxymorphone extended release 5-40 mg

## Study
- Randomized, three-period, four-sequence incomplete-block crossover (each subject 3 of 4 doses: 5, 10, 20, 40 mg
  oxymorphone ER, TIMERx matrix, Endo). Sponsor Endo/Penwest; authors from SFBC and Endo.
- 24 healthy volunteers (14 M, 10 F; 18 white, 4 Hispanic, 2 black), 20-45 y (mean 35.5), mean 72.6 kg, 168 cm;
  23 completed at least one period, 21 all three.
- Each 8-day period: single dose day 1; q12h days 3-7; last dose day 8; 7-day washouts. Fasted 10 h before to
  4 h after on days 1 and 8. Naltrexone 50 mg daily throughout (stated not to alter oxymorphone PK, data on file).
- Sampling: day 1 to 48 h (0.5-48 h, 15 times); troughs days 6-8; day 8 over 12 h.
- Assay: LC-MS/MS; oxymorphone and 6-hydroxyoxymorphone 0.1-20 ng/mL; oxymorphone-3-glucuronide 5-250 ng/mL.
- NCA; ANOVA on dose-normalized (to 20 mg) ln AUC and ln Cmax.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None measured (healthy volunteers on naltrexone blockade). **No MEAC, no ke0/t½ke0/tPeak.** Background only:
oxymorphone has "several times" morphine's analgesic potency (Introduction, citing refs 7, 8); no numeric
equianalgesic ratio. Metabolites: under 2% of oxymorphone excreted unchanged in urine (cited); OXM-3-G activity not
evaluated; 6-OH-OXM active in animals (data on file).

## Covariates
None examined.

## Caveats
- Extended-release product only; naltrexone co-administration; healthy young adults.
- Lower-dose AUCs uncertain (assay limit 0.1 ng/mL); 5 mg CL/F very variable.
- Doses are oxymorphone hydrochloride; CL/F is computed on the salt dose (not stated as base).
- Text-layer table columns are scrambled; values above should be confirmed against the PDF image if load-bearing.
