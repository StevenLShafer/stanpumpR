# Bockbrader 2010: single/multiple-dose and food-effect PK of pregabalin in volunteers

## Study
Five healthy-volunteer studies, 112 subjects on drug (Table I):
1. Single rising dose 1-300 mg (n = 29; 40.1 y; 77.4 kg; 14 M/15 F); 1-2 mg solution, others capsules.
2. 25-300 mg q8h or 300 mg q12h for 2 weeks (n = 45).
3. 300 mg q8h for 28 days (n = 12).
4. Food (standard breakfast) and capsule vs solution, 100 mg (n = 12, mostly women, 54 y).
5. High-fat breakfast, 150 mg (n = 14, mostly men, 85.3 kg).
Fasting >= 8 h for PK days. HPLC-UV after derivatisation; LLOQ 0.005 or 0.05 ug/mL. Noncompartmental analysis.

## Model and parameters
Noncompartmental only. Key values:
- Single dose 1-300 mg: mean tmax 0.7-1.3 h; t1/2 4.6-6.8 h; Cmax and AUC proportional to dose (Table III, Fig 2).
- Table III, e.g. 125 mg Cmax 3.59 ug/mL; 200 mg 5.96; 300 mg 9.46 ug/mL, AUC 66.3 ug.h/mL, CL/F 75.6 mL/min, Vd/F 36.3 L.
- Urinary recovery unchanged ~90% at 1-300 mg, taken as oral bioavailability (< 0.1% in faeces, 98% of urinary radioactivity unchanged). CLr 58.0-80.9 mL/min.
- Vd ~36 L after correcting for F; mean t1/2 6.3 h (Discussion).
- Steady state within 24-48 h; accumulation 1.48-2.03 (q8h), 1.21-1.50 (q12h).
- Food (Table V): 100 mg standard breakfast Cmax 2.59 vs 3.78 fasted (68.6%), tmax 3.17 vs 0.615 h, AUC0-inf 25.4 vs 26.7 (94.9%). 150 mg high-fat: Cmax 2.60 vs 3.47 fasted (74.8%), tmax 2.29 vs 1.25 h, AUC0-inf 25.5 vs 27.3 ug.h/mL (93.3%).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PK/PD in this study. Discussion cites a slight delay between plasma concentration and efficacy (preclinical/clinical) and argues AUC rather than Cmax predicts efficacy. No ke0, MEAC or potency (not an opioid).

## Covariates
None modelled.

## Caveats
n = 3 per dose group in study 1; bioavailability inferred from urine, not an IV reference; study 5 subjects mostly male and heavier.

## Verification against stanpumpR
- Fasted tmax 0.7-1.3 h: **matches** (Table III). Model peak 0.76 h lies in that range.
- Bioavailability ~90%, independent of dose: **matches**.
- The adjacent header numbers "3.85-4.65 mcg/mL after 150 mg" and AUC "23.7-29.8" (R/drugs_pregabalin.R:49-50, 67-68; help pregabalin.md:7 "3.9 to 4.7" and "24 to 30") are **not from this paper** and cannot be verified here. This paper's own fasted 150 mg values are Cmax 3.47 ug/mL and AUC 27.3 (Table V), closer to the model's 3.57 than the quoted range. Calleja 2025 cites 3.85 ug/mL and AUC 27.9 for 150 mg from Tjandrawinata 2015, which is probably the lower bound's source. Not a discrepancy in the code, but the source of the range is unstated.
- Food effect in help (peak a quarter to a third lower, about 3 h, AUC unchanged): **matches** Table V (25-31% lower; tmax 2.3-3.2 h; AUC 93-95%).
