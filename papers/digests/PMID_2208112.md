# Reid 1990: idarubicin and idarubicinol in children with leukaemia

## Study
- Phase I CCSG trial; 21 patients aged 1-21 years with leukaemia (ALL or AML) in second or later marrow relapse, normal organ function, <325 mg/m2 prior anthracycline (PDF p. 1). 14 had CNS radiation and 15 intrathecal therapy before; 3 had CSF blasts.
- Idarubicin 10, 12.5, 13.5 or 15 mg/m2 **IV only**, infused over 10-45 min, either weekly x 3 or daily x 3 every 3 weeks. Dose basis (hydrochloride vs base) not stated.
- Plasma sampled pre-dose and 0.5, 1, 2, 4, 6, 8, 12, 24, 48 h after dose on day 1 (weekly) or day 3 (daily), plus trough and 12-h samples on days 1-2 of the daily schedule. CSF (2 mL aliquot of a clinical lumbar puncture) 18-30 h after a dose; urine 0-24 and 24-48 h (weekly only).
- Assay: HPLC, fluorescence; plasma/urine linear 0.25-100 ng/mL, CSF 0.15-5.0 ng/mL; recoveries 91-93%.
- Analysis: each patient's plasma idarubicin fitted to a triexponential (NONLIN, 1/Y weighting); CL and Vdss corrected for infusion duration and multiple dosing. Idarubicinol half-life by graphic analysis.
- No weights or heights reported; parameters normalised to BSA.

## Model and parameters

- Idarubicinol exceeded parent within 4-6 h; on the daily schedule it was 5-10-fold above parent for most of the 3 days. 48-h urinary recovery: idarubicin 2.4%, idarubicinol 10.1%.
- No dose- or schedule-dependence; no age relation found.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- No PD model, ke0 or effect-site data. Not an opioid.
- CSF (Table 2, PDF p. 3): idarubicin detected in 2 of 21 (0.14 and 1.57 ng/mL); idarubicinol in 20 of 21, 0.22-1.05 ng/mL, mean 0.51 ng/mL; CSF/plasma idarubicinol ratio about 0.04 on average (0.00-0.13). Daily schedule means 0.57 (0.29) and 0.70 (0.27) ng/mL at 12.5 and 13.5 mg/m2.
- In vitro (cited from the authors' abstract): 0.5-1.0 ng/mL idarubicinol for 3-5 days is cytotoxic to leukaemia cells. Idarubicinol is described as almost equitoxic with idarubicin in vitro.

## Covariates
None modelled; no age relation found.

## Caveats
- Individual fits with very large between-patient variability; several Vdss values (3-16 L/m2) are implausibly small beside terminal half-lives of 10-40 h (CL/Vdss would imply half-lives far shorter).
- Infusion duration assumed (15 min) for four patients; two-compartment fits for two.
- Children with heavy prior treatment; small n per dose.
