# Mouksassi 2015: population PK of thrombomodulin alfa (ART-123)

## Study
- Phase I: 24 healthy volunteers, 0.02 and 0.06 mg/kg IV, samples to 144 h. Phase IIb (Protocol 2-001): 750 randomised adults with sepsis and suspected DIC, 0.06 mg/kg/day (max 6 mg) or placebo for six days, IV bolus or 15-min infusion; 368 treated with plasma samples (sparse: days 1, 3, 7, 14, some 28). Patients on renal replacement excluded.
- Combined (Table 1): age 55.8 (18-93) years, height 167.7 (130-200) cm, weight 70.54 (30-150) kg, median 68; serum creatinine 1.44 (0.2-11.0) mg/dL; Cockcroft-Gault CrCL 74.6 (5-150) mL/min, truncated at 150; 142 women, 238 men.
- ELISA, LLOQ 1 ng/mL. NONMEM; one compartment better than two (Supplemental Table S1).

## Checks
- Typical CL at CrCL 105, 75, 45, 22 mL/min: 0.158, 0.145, 0.128, 0.105 L/h; V 5.44-5.54 L (Results). The equations at 68 kg give 0.155, 0.141, 0.123, 0.101.
- Japanese DIC patients (ref 8): CL 0.19 L/h, V 4.83 L at 70 kg.
- 54-74% of a dose recovered in urine in healthy subjects (Discussion).
- Therapeutic range 300-5,400 ng/mL (Introduction): effective > 300 ng/mL from non-clinical and clinical data; 5,400 ng/mL the highest with no bleeding in non-clinical toxicology.
