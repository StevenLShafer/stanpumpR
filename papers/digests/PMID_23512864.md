# Sigmond 2013: Performance of eight alfentanil PK sets in bolus and infusion data

## Study
Retrospective evaluation of how well eight published alfentanil PK parameter sets predict measured plasma concentrations (PDF page 2-3, printed pp. 198-199).
- Data set I (infusion): 30 orthopaedic patients, anaesthetised with xenon 70% or N2O 70% plus desflurane 2%; alfentanil given by TCI using StanPump with the Shafer parameter set, targets 5-400 ng/mL; 649 arterial samples. Mean (range) age 33 (18-54) yr per text on printed p. 201.
- Data set B (bolus, unpublished): 40 orthopaedic patients, propofol TCI (Schnider set) plus repeated alfentanil boluses of 0.5-1.0 mg; 201 arterial samples. Text says "40 (51-76)" for age in Group B (printed p. 201), which is not self-consistent with the Table 1 age range; not resolved here.
- Total 850 samples, all used. Median predicted Cp 72 (54-104) ng/mL (infusion) vs 143 (113-175) ng/mL (bolus) (p. 201).
- Assay: plasma, GC-MS, lower detection limit 2 ng/mL (p. 198). Samples stored at -22 C (text-layer "2268C" is a corrupted minus-22; not checked on image).
- Dosing histories were run through the eight sets in PKPD Tools for Excel. Measures: Varvel MDPE and MDAPE, Masui log bias and log accuracy, Wobble, Divergence. Acceptance: MDAPE <=30%, MDPE within +-20%, log accuracy <0.15, log bias within about +-0.1, and not different between bolus and infusion.

## Model and parameters
No new model is fitted. Sets compared: Scott 1987 (original and weight-adjusted), Fragen 1983, Shafer (as in StanPump), Maitre 1987, Lemmens 1990, Mertens 2001, Hudson 1991. Table 2 (PDF page 4, printed p. 200, read on the image) lists them as published; Table 3 evaluates them for a 70 kg, 170 cm, 30-year-old man.

Performance (text, printed pp. 201-202; the sign convention is PE = (Cm - Cp)/Cp, so negative PE is overprediction):
- MDAPE, infusion data: Scott 22.3%, Fragen 26.5%, Scott weight-adjusted 26.5%, Maitre 41%.
- MDAPE, bolus data: Scott 30.2%, Fragen 26%, Scott weight-adjusted 29.3%, Maitre 24.1%.
- MDPE, infusion: Scott -18.4%, Fragen -6%, Scott-WA -24%, Maitre +39.4%. MDPE, bolus: Scott -30%, Fragen -18.3%, Scott-WA -24.6%, Maitre +0.6%. (Minus signs are corrupted in the text layer; read as printed 2 = minus; Figure 3 not checked on image.)
- Scott, Fragen and Scott-WA met MDAPE <=30% in both modes; Maitre and Lemmens met it only in bolus data. Scott sets overpredicted; Maitre, Hudson and Mertens underpredicted.
- Divergence <=5 %/h for Scott, Fragen, Maitre, Lemmens, Mertens; Hudson 12 (infusion) and 18 (bolus) %/h; Shafer 9 and 7 %/h (p. 201).
- Hypnotic (xenon vs N2O plus desflurane) did not change any index within the infusion group (P>=0.263).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Audit: no MEAC or analgesic concentration, no ke0 / t1/2 ke0 / tPeak / hysteresis, no equianalgesic or potency ratio is reported. Concentrations are plasma (arterial), assayed by GC-MS (total drug). The paper's "50% decrement time" after a 2 h infusion at a 200 ng/mL target is 45 min (Maitre) vs 47 min (Scott) (derived by simulation, p. 206), a PK quantity, not a PD end point.

## Covariates
Maitre carries sex and age effects (Table 2); Lemmens has sex and age (female <=50 vs >50); Fragen, Mertens, Hudson scale with weight; Shafer scales V1 with BSA; Scott is fixed (weight-adjusted variant scales V1 linearly with weight). The authors found weight did not affect PE of the Scott set (p. 202). Mertens' set encodes a propofol effect: propofol 1.5 mcg/mL lowered Cl1, Cl2, Cl3 by 15, 300 and 50% (p. 204).

## Caveats
Sample timing and number unbalanced between data sets, mostly in the flat part of the curve; the two groups differ in age, ASA, hypnotic (propofol vs xenon/N2O-desflurane) and duration, so mode of administration is confounded with those (authors, pp. 206-207). Initial and terminal phases are not well predicted by any set. Healthy, non-obese orthopaedic patients. Infusion cohort was driven by the StanPump Shafer set, which may bias the sampled concentrations. Authors' simulation: 120 min TCI at 200 ng/mL, 40-year-old 70 kg 170 cm man, cumulative dose 9 mg (Scott), 14 mg (Maitre, man), 14.8 mg (Maitre, woman) (p. 206).
