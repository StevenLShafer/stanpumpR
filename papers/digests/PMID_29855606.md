# Vujkovic 2018: CYP polymorphisms and efavirenz PK / CNS toxicity, Botswana

## Study
Cohort of HIV-infected black African adults (>=21 y, not pregnant) starting efavirenz 600 mg/day plus two NRTIs, Gaborone, Botswana, June 2009 to November 2013 (PDF p.3). 941 enrolled, 814 genotyped for CYP2B6 516G>T, 742 with one mid-dosing-interval steady-state plasma EFV sample at month 1, 562 with a second at month 6 (Table 1, PDF p.16). Median age 37 y, BMI 22.0, CD4 196, 49% male (Table 1). Assay: total EFV in plasma by LC-MS/MS, LLOQ 1 ng/mL. 21 SNPs in CYP2B6, 2A6, 3A4, 3A5 (TaqMan OpenArray). Population PK in NONMEM VII, ADVAN2, FOCE-I. Outcomes: 35-item Subject Experience Questionnaire (CNS items), and a composite of death, HIV RNA >25 copies/mL at month 6, or loss to follow-up.

Median plasma EFV 2.17 ug/mL at month 1 and 2.05 ug/mL at month 6 (p<0.01) (PDF p.5; IQR at month 1: 1.62-3.88, Table 1).

## Model and parameters
One-compartment, first-order absorption. Vd 150 L and ka 0.18 h-1 fixed from the authors' earlier cohort paper (ref 13); only CL/F estimated (PDF p.4). Weight allometric on CL/F, exponent fixed 0.75, 70 kg reference. Base-model results (PDF p.5), CL/F in L/h/70 kg:

CYP3A4 and CYP3A5 SNPs showed no clearance change above about 8%. Threshold used: a SNP counts as extensive or slow if CL changes by 20% (p.4).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No concentration-effect model and no MEAC. Cited concentration thresholds (from other work, not derived here): plasma EFV <1 ug/mL associated with virologic failure, >4 ug/mL with CNS adverse effects (PDF p.2, intro; the Discussion says "mg/mL", a typo). The paper's own finding: composite CYP2B6 metabolizer class (Table 3) vs CNS score at month 1, p = 0.041 (more severe symptoms in extensive, fewer in slow metabolizers despite higher plasma EFV); not significant at month 6 (p = 0.425). Table 4 ORs for composite failure were not significant (e.g. very extensive 0.99 (0.65-1.50), slow/very slow 1.18 (0.80-1.72)). No ke0, tPeak or potency ratio. Not an opioid.

## Covariates
Weight (allometric 0.75 fixed), CYP2B6 516G>T genotype, visit (month 1 vs 6). Sex, tuberculosis history and adherence did not improve MOFV. SNPs tested one at a time.

## Caveats
Exploratory; 21 SNPs with no multiplicity correction (stated); one sample per visit, sparse; Vd and ka fixed; adherence from pharmacy refills; SEQ not validated in Africans; loss to follow-up substantial; internal inconsistencies noted in open_questions.
