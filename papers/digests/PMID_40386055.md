# Kobuchi 2025: dapagliflozin trough PK and HbA1c in Japanese type 2 diabetes

## Study
- Single centre (Tonami General Hospital, Japan), real-world, 1 year. 85 Japanese outpatients with type 2 diabetes
  (72 from an earlier report plus 13), all on metformin >= 1000 mg/day and a DPP-4 inhibitor, given dapagliflozin
  5 mg once daily after breakfast; none on 10 mg (PDF p2).
- Table 1 (PDF p4): 61 M / 24 F; age 59.1 +/- 10.0 (median 59, 37-75) y; height 165.9 +/- 8.1 cm; weight 78.1 +/- 13.4
  (median 77.0, 49.0-118.0) kg; BMI 28.3; HbA1c 6.8 +/- 0.5%; eGFR 74.6 +/- 18.5 (median 74.3, 39.2-137.8)
  mL/min/1.73 m2; SCr 0.81 mg/dL.
- Visits at 1, 3, 6, 9, 12 months after an overnight fast: 415 trough plasma concentrations (below-LLOQ excluded) and
  508 HbA1c values (PDF p4-5). LC-MS/MS, LLOQ 0.5 ng/mL in 100 uL plasma.
- Phoenix NLME 8.4, FOCE-ELS; stepwise covariates (forward p < 0.01, backward p < 0.001); pc-VPC and bootstrap
  n = 1000 (PDF p2-3).

## Model and parameters
One compartment, first-order absorption, multiplicative error. Because every sample was a trough, ka and V/F were
fixed to "the previous report of population PK analysis" (reference 11 = Melin et al., Br J Clin Pharmacol
2024;90:606-612; PDF p3). Equation 6 (PDF p4, rendered): CL/F = 229.3 x (Body weight / 77.0)^0.41, with body weight at
the time of sampling (baseline weight was tried first). Table 2 (PDF p6, rendered):

5000 / 229.3 = 21.8 ng/mL. The abstract gives no PK numbers, so there is no abstract-table conflict to report.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
HbA1c turnover (de Winter 2017 form; equations 2-5, PDF p3): dH/dt = kin - Ef - kout H, kout = ln2 / t1/2HbA1c,
Ef = Efc (H(0) - 5)/(8 - 5), Efc = Emax C/(C + EC50), a Hill factor did not improve the fit. End point: HbA1c (%), not
glucose. Simulation (Figure 4, PDF p7): baseline 6.8% to 6.5% (5 mg) and 6.4% (10 mg) at 1 year. No ke0, no MEAC.
Not an opioid.

## Covariates
Body weight at sampling on CL/F (power 0.41). Not retained: sex, age, height, AST, ALT, gamma-GTP, eGFR (Japanese
equation). The authors judge the weight effect clinically negligible (median weight change -1.5 kg).

## Caveats
- Troughs only; ka and V/F borrowed. The shape within a dosing interval is the borrowed parameters', not these
  patients'.
- Methods say continuous covariates were normalised to the mean, but Equation 6 uses 77.0 kg, the median (mean 78.1).
- The text prints kin = H(0)/kout (PDF p3, rendered); for dH/dt = 0 at baseline Equation 2 requires kin = kout x H(0).
  As printed; unresolved.
- The simulation section heading reads "10 mg/kg" (PDF p3); the text and Figure 4 are 10 mg/day.
- In Table 2 the CV% of omega_CL/F (21.3) equals that of theta_BW; as printed.
- Single centre, single arm, no drug-interaction analysis; all on metformin and a DPP-4 inhibitor.
