# Tachibana 2014: Marsh-model propofol TCI bias in obese Japanese patients

## Study
- Open-label comparative study, Sapporo Medical University Hospital, Oct 2011 - Dec 2013. Adults 30-65 y, ASA 1-2, elective surgery (cranial, orthopaedic neck, gynaecological). 70 enrolled, 1 excluded (no radial arterial cannula), 69 analysed: 37 non-obese (BMI < 25) and 32 obese (BMI >= 25) (Figure 1). Study 2: 5 further obese women, BMI >= 30.
- Propofol by Diprifusor TCI with the Marsh model, constant predicted concentration (Cp) 4.0 ug/mL, actual body weight as the set weight; remifentanil 0.25 ug/kg/min maintenance (the text prints "mg", read as ug on the unit symbol rendering); rocuronium 0.6 mg/kg. No other premedication.
- Arterial samples at 15, 30, 60, 90, 120, 150, 180 min (Study 2 from 30 min); 333 samples from 69 patients. Assay: Pelorus 1000 bedside analyser, WHOLE BLOOD propofol, colorimetric (Gibbs reagent), LLOQ 0.25 ug/mL, linear below 12 ug/mL against HPLC.
- Performance error PE% = (Cm - Cp) / Cp x 100; MDPE, MDAPE per time point; acceptable if MDPE within +/-20 % and MDAPE < 30 % (ref 9).
- Table 1 (text layer): non-obese vs obese weight 56.1 +/- 8.5 vs 79.4 +/- 19.0 kg and BMI 21.5 +/- 2.3 vs 30.0 +/- 5.9 (p < 0.001); no difference in age, height or blood loss.

## Model and parameters
No new PK model is fitted. The only derived quantity is an empirical correction of the Marsh target from a linear regression of measured concentration Cm on BMI (264 samples, the 15-min samples excluded because of the Marsh early overshoot): slope 0.083 per kg/m2 (p < 0.001), intercept 2.789 ug/mL (Figure 3 and Results text, PDF p6). Corrected Cp = 4 x 4 / (0.083 x BMI + 2.789) ug/mL. This is a statistical fit, not a physiological model, and is based on one fixed target.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD end point except BIS values recorded for comfort (Table 4: BIS 29-59 in the five women, none had autonomic response, movement or recall). No ke0, t½ke0, tPeak, effect-site target or MEAC. Not an opioid paper (remifentanil is background only); no potency ratio.

## Covariates
BMI only (also total body weight as the TCI weight). Sex not tested; Study 2 was all women. Age 30-65 only.

## Caveats
- Authors: only 5 validation patients, all female, no control at the fixed 4.0; correction derived at one target and not tested while changing targets; only the Marsh model examined; whole-blood bedside assay rather than HPLC plasma; no comparison with Schnider or other models.
- The corrected Cp predicts concentrations ABOVE the 4.0 target as BMI rises (measured propofol exceeded target in obese patients), yet the corrected Cp gave measured concentrations that were still 20-40 % above corrected Cp (Table 5), i.e. the corrected targets remain biased against the lowered setting. The note "Cm vs 4.0 ug/mL" in Table 5 is simply the measured concentrations against the original 4.0 target, which are near it by construction.
- The 15-min samples were dropped from the regression for the Marsh overshoot (Discussion).
- Tension between the abstract (MDAPE 8.8-11.5) and Table 5 (max 13.0): see open_questions.
