# Joerger 2006: paclitaxel population PK with sex, age, bilirubin and BSA

## Study
- Retrospective pooled analysis of 168 adults (86 M / 82 F), 280 courses, with solid tumours: NSCLC 84, ovarian 40,
  various 44 (Abstract; Table 1, PDF p. 2, printed 2151). Age median 56 (33.1-86.2) y; BSA median 1.80
  (1.35-2.25) m2; weight 68.5 (44-125) kg; total bilirubin 7 (2-24) umol/L; Cockcroft-Gault CrCL 78 (37-144) mL/min.
  8/40 ovarian patients had ascites; 16/84 NSCLC had pleural effusion.
- Drug: Taxol (Cremophor EL/ethanol), 100-250 mg/m2 over 3 h (n = 163; some NSCLC with carboplatin 300-400 mg/m2)
  or 135/175 mg/m2 over 24 h (n = 5). PVC-free sets. Dexamethasone/clemastine/H2-blocker premedication.
- Sampling: rich, to 48 h after a 3-h infusion and 30 h after a 24-h infusion (PDF p. 2).
- Assay: total paclitaxel in heparin plasma, HPLC with solid-phase extraction, 10-10,000 ng/mL.
- NONMEM V, first-order (FO) method, ADVAN6, log-transformed data; two residual-error subpopulations (15% with the
  larger error). Forward inclusion P < 0.01 (dOFV > 6.7), backward elimination P < 0.005 (dOFV > 7.9).

## Model and parameters

peripheral compartment (VMTR, KMTR, returning at first order K21), and linear distribution (Q) to a second
peripheral compartment V3 (Methods, PDF p. 3). Amounts in umol, time in h. Table 2 (PDF p. 3, printed 2152):

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No effect-site model; not an opioid. Exposure measures only: Cmax and time above 0.1 umol/L (TC0.1), simulated for
175 mg/m2 over 3 h in 1,000 virtual patients per covariate (Figs. 3-4). Cited thresholds for neutropenia: time above
0.05, 0.07, 0.1 or 0.2 umol/L (refs 2, 8, 21, 26, 29); >15 h above 0.1 umol/L linked to survival in NSCLC (ref 30).
These are literature values, not estimated here. Women had higher Cmax and TC0.1 than men (P < 10^-3).

## Covariates
See equations. Stated effects (p. 4): men +20% VMEL; +0.2 m2 BSA +9% VMEL; +10 y age -5% VMEL; +10 umol/L bilirubin
-14% VMEL; VMEL 22% lower at BSA 1.35, 19% lower at bilirubin 24, 14% lower at age 86 vs the typical patient. KMTR
>2x higher in men; K21 11% lower in men. Third-space effusions and weight not retained. OFV fell 247.4 with all
covariates.

## Caveats
- Empirical model of total (Cremophor-entrapped) paclitaxel; nonlinearity is largely the vehicle's (Discussion).
  Not transferable to nab-paclitaxel.
- Label swap in the Results (p. 4): the 0.83/1.74 umol/L sex-dependent constant is called "K_EL" and 0.53 "K_TR"; by
  Table 2, Eq. D and the Discussion, 0.83/1.74 is KMTR and 0.53 KMEL.
- Methods say Q "in L/min"; Table 2 and Eq. F give L/h. Methods' covariate example "(WT - 68)^theta" is presumably
  (WT/68)^theta; weight is not in the final model.
- Table 2's KMTR 0.83 vs Eq. D 0.826 (rounding).
- FO estimation; some patients on concurrent carboplatin, not tested as a covariate. Patients had near-normal liver
  function (bilirubin max 24 umol/L).
- No molecular weight stated; conversion to mg needs MW (853.9 g/mol is the standard value, not from the paper).
