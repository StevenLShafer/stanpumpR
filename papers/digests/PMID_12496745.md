# Klein 2002: population PK of irinotecan, SN-38 and SN-38G

## Study
- Two pooled dose-escalation studies: University of Chicago, 44 patients (solid tumours or lymphoma),
  irinotecan 100-175 mg/m2; Mayo Clinic, 34 patients (solid tumours), 240-340 mg/m2. All by 90-min IV infusion
  (PDF p3, printed 640). Total n = 78 cancer patients; age, weight, sex split not tabulated in the text layer
  (median body weight 80 kg is quoted in the covariate results, p6/643).
- Plasma sampling at 0.5-25 h from start of infusion (17 times), plus 33.5 and 49.5 h at Mayo; mean 16 samples
  per subject. HPLC for irinotecan, SN-38 and SN-38G, LLOQ "at least 5 ng/mL" (p3/640).
- No premedication in either trial (p8/645).
- Data splitting: 52 model-building, 26 validation (random one third). NONMEM V, FOCE; proportional error,
  separate for each site. Metabolite models fitted sequentially with parent (then SN-38) parameters fixed.
  Covariates by graphics, GAM (S-PLUS), then stepwise NONMEM (add P < .05, delete P < .01); bootstrap 100
  data sets for SN-38 parameters, 1000 for covariate inclusion.
- LBM formulas (James) given on p3/640 for men (1.10 W - 128 W^2/H^2) and women (1.07 W - 148 W^2/H^2).

## Model and parameters
Irinotecan: linear 3-compartment; SN-38: 2-compartment with first-order formation from irinotecan central
compartment; SN-38G: 1-compartment with first-order formation from SN-38 (Fig 3, p6/643). Because the fractions
metabolised are unknown, metabolite volumes are apparent (Vc/Fmsn, Vc/Fmsng). SN-38 uses a mixture model with two
subpopulations of Vc/Fmsn. Enterohepatic recycling not identifiable, not modelled.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No exposure-response, toxicity or efficacy model; no ke0. SN-38 is stated (from the literature) to be
300-1000 times more potent than irinotecan (p2/639, refs 8,9). Dose-adjusted SN-38 Cmax bimodal: about 0.09 vs
> 0.13 ng/mL per mg irinotecan (Fig 2, p4/641).

## Covariates
(p5-6/642-643, confirmed on the p6 image)
- Irinotecan CL: -10.5 L/h for ECOG performance status 2 vs 0-1 (stated as 33%); -2.1 L/h per 10 y above 60 y,
  +2.1 L/h per 10 y below (stated as 7.2%).
- Irinotecan Vc: +/-4% per 10 kg above/below median 80 kg.
- SN-38: CL/Fmsn -30% (stated 252 L/h) and Vc/Fmsn -38% (stated 212 L) in women; Vc/Fmsn a further -30% with
  bilirubin > 0.9 mg/dL. Dose excluded.
- SN-38G: CL/Fmsng -10% for performance status 2; Vc/Fmsng +30% for weight > 80 kg.
- Neither weight nor BSA predicted irinotecan or SN-38 exposure; the authors say this argues against mg/m2 dosing.

## Caveats
- The percentages and absolute values in the covariate text do not agree with Table I's typical values:
  10.5/25.2 = 42%, not 33%; 2.1/25.2 = 8.3%, not 7.2%; 252 L/h is 50% of 504, not 30%; 212 L is not 38% of 200 or
  466 (derived). Exact covariate equations are not printed. Not used by stanpumpR, so not raised as a question.
- Table I's column headings label the model-building set "PBVS" and the validation set "SMVS", although the Methods
  define both PBVS and SMVS as fits to the validation set (p4/641).
- Fmsn not identifiable: metabolite volumes/clearances are apparent. Cancer patients, mixed tumour types, two sites,
  doses 100-340 mg/m2; no UGT1A1 genotype.
- The Table II bootstrap CIs are much narrower than, and in places do not contain, the final-model point estimates
  (e.g. kSN21 0.0584 vs 0.062-0.066).
