# van den Nieuwenhuyzen 1999: TCI alfentanil, plasma protein binding and variability

## Study
- 10 ASA I-II patients (5 M), age median 36 (18-65) y, weight 70 (SD 15) kg, orthopaedic surgery >1 h (Table 1, rendered). Exclusions as in the 1997 paper plus rheumatoid arthritis.
- Standardised anaesthesia: temazepam 0.3 mg/kg PO, pancuronium 0.02 mg/kg, alfentanil TCI target 150 ng/mL plus etomidate 0.15-0.3 mg/kg, succinylcholine, enflurane 0-1.2% and 66% N2O; droperidol 1 mg at end. Intraoperative alfentanil 9.8 (SD 2.9) mg; anaesthesia 145 (86) min.
- Postoperative TCI alfentanil (Maitre 1987 PK set in the pump). On pain, restarted at CT = predicted concentration if >50 ng/mL, else 50 ng/mL; CT +10 ng/mL for requests every 10 min; loading ended when CT unchanged 30 min; then step 5 ng/mL, lockout 10 min; after 2 h unchanged, CT -5 ng/mL every 15 min until a request. Observed until 21:00 on the first postoperative day, median 30 (28-32) h.
- Alfentanil total plasma concentration and protein binding (equilibrium dialysis, 37 C, 4 h, Fu = CD/(CMEAS - R*CD) with R = 2) at four times: arrival in recovery, 21:00 on day of surgery, 09:00 and 21:00 on day 1. GC assay, detection limit 0.1 ng/mL.
- Effective analgesic concentration (EAC) defined as the measured total plasma concentration at which the patient needed no additional analgesia with VAS <=3.0.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: quantal analgesia (no demand and VAS <=3.0) in the postoperative period, total plasma concentration (arterial plasma; unbound fraction measured separately).
- EAC per patient (Table 2, PDF p3 rendered; ng/mL total plasma): arrival RR median 73 (range 40-100); 21:00 day 0 median 50 (4-128); 09:00 day 1 median 36 (3-140); 21:00 day 1 median 7 (0-209). Per-patient values are printed in Table 2 (e.g. arrival: 75, 59, 83, 48, 93, 71, 68, 78, 100, 40). Abstract states EAC range 33-140 at onset; Table 2 arrival values range 40-100 (inconsistent: see caveats).
- Fu explained 39% of inter-individual variability in EAC at onset of therapy, 25% at 21:00 on day 0 (largely one patient); no significant relation on day 1 (Fig 1).
- Free-concentration illustration in the paper: unbound EAC 4 ng/mL gives total 50 vs 100 ng/mL at 92% vs 96% binding. This is the authors' arithmetic, not a measured value.
- The authors call EAC at onset a MEAC ("MEAC during the early postoperative phase"); defined by titration. Later-time EACs were set equal to the plasma concentration at the observation time, which may overestimate the true EAC.
- ke0 / t1/2ke0 / tPeak / hysteresis: none reported. Equianalgesic or potency ratios: none reported.
- Time from start of therapy to satisfactory analgesia 20 (10-60) min; loading period 42 (31-74) min (Table 1).

## Covariates
Plasma protein binding (Fu) vs EAC by linear regression; no PK covariate model. Fu fell on day 1 (more AAG after surgery) yet EAC was lower on day 1, so binding did not explain intra-patient change.

## Caveats
- n=10; correlations driven by single patients; no full PK evaluation.
- Abstract and text give EAC range 33-140 at onset; Table 2 arrival range is 40-100 and the 33 and 140 match other columns (33 at 21:00 patient 2; 140 at 09:00 patient 4). Treat the abstract range as unreliable.
- Table 3 text layer is scrambled; MDPE/MDAPE read from the rendered image.
- Authors infer a pharmacokinetic change over time (measured below predicted on day 0, above on day 1) from binding changes, not directly shown.
