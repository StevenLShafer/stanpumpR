# Rinehart 2015: closed-loop vs manual goal-directed fluid therapy

## Study
Single-centre (UC Irvine, Sept 2013-Feb 2014) observational study with 1:1 propensity-score matching (calipers 0.04 wide, 20% of SD of the score). Adults having hepatobiliary, pancreatic or splenic surgery with an arterial line. 40 enrolled, 5 excluded, 35 closed-loop (CL) patients analysed; 25 matched pairs fell within the caliper (text p6; Table 1 on PDF p6 gives n=25 per arm: age 61.5 vs 61.6 y, weight 73.9 kg both). Control (M) patients had manual goal-directed fluid therapy (GDFT) by protocol (Fig 1). Closed-loop system (Sironis software, EV-1000/FloTrac, Q Core Sapphire pump): 100 mL colloid boluses over 6 min (6% HES 130/0.4 or 5% albumin), target scaled stroke-volume increase 7.5-22.5% over 500 mL (default 15%), baseline crystalloid 3 mL/kg/h.
Anaesthesia: fentanyl 2 ug/kg and propofol 2-3 mg/kg induction, sevoflurane maintenance with fentanyl boluses at the team's discretion (no concentrations or PK).

## Model and parameters
No pharmacokinetic model. Primary outcome: percent of case time with stroke volume variation <= 12%: CL 95 +/- 6% vs M 87 +/- 14%, P=0.008 (abstract, p6). Case-mean SVV 7.0 +/- 1.2 vs 8.8 +/- 2.5 (Table 2, p7; the text on p6 prints 7.7 +/- 12, inconsistent with the table). End HR 77 +/- 10 vs 88 +/- 13, P=0.003. Fluid volumes did not differ (crystalloid 4.6 vs 4.9 mL/kg/h; colloid 1.4 vs 1.4 mL/kg/h, Table 2).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Not an opioid paper. No MEAC, ke0, t1/2ke0, tPeak or potency ratio. Haemodynamic end points only (SVV, SVI, CI, MAP, HR).

## Covariates
Propensity model used age, height, weight, sex, HR, SVI, MAP, SVV, procedure, surgeon (p3).

## Caveats
Non-randomised; two surgeons; investigators present in CL cases; arterial-waveform CO monitoring; authors hold interest in Sironis. Printed text and Table 2 disagree on case-mean SVV and CL boluses (214 boluses across "25" cases, 8.5 per case).
