# Le Jouan 2005: quinine PK-PD in children with falciparum malaria

## Study
- Monocentric, prospective, open, noncomparative study, Yaounde Central Hospital, Cameroon (PDF p1).
- 30 children (17 boys, 13 girls) with uncomplicated P. falciparum malaria; age 2.8 ± 1.7 y (range 0.55-6.7), weight 13.6 ± 3.8 kg (mean ± SD; PDF p3). Initial parasitemia median 16,500/µL (range 1,404-176,000).
- Oral quinine 8.3 mg/kg **as quinine base** every 8 h for 5 days (15 doses), as a 2% quinine formiate salt syrup given by syringe (PDF p1).
- PK sampling: venous plasma at 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 8, 24, 48 and 56 h after onset of treatment (abstract says five to nine per patient). Assay: liquid chromatography with fluorescence detection after liquid-liquid extraction; LOQ 1 mg/L; interday CV < 10%, bias < 5% (PDF p1-2). Total (not unbound) plasma concentration; unbound fraction was not measured.
- PD: parasitemia on thin smears at 0, 12, 24, 48, 72, 96 h and days 7, 14 (PDF p2).
- PK: NONMEM, FOCE; PD: Bayesian three-stage hierarchical model in WinBugs 1.4 (PDF p2).

## Model and parameters
One-compartment, first-order absorption and elimination, apparent oral parameters (F not estimated). Time-varying protein binding: fu = b·(t − 36) + 0.15, t in h over 0-72 h, 0.15 being the assumed typical fu at 36 h. Typical values: CL/F = fu·(θ1 + θ5·BW), V/F = fu·(θ2 + θ6·BW), BW in kg; θ's are **unbound** clearance/volume terms (Table 2 footnote a). Residual error exponential. The authors compare the 15-kg values with White et al. 1982 adults with uncomplicated malaria scaled to 15 kg (CL 1.21 L/h, V 25 L) and call them similar (PDF p4).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: Ter, time to a 10^4-fold (4-log) fall in initial parasitemia, = 4/k, k the slope of log10 parasitemia vs time over the first 3 days (PDF p2).
- Exposure metric: Cav = AUC(0-72 h)/72 from post hoc PK (total plasma quinine).
- Model: Ter = Tmin·[1 + (C50/Cav)^s]; s fixed, best fit s = 2 (PDF p5).
- Table 3 (posterior, PDF p3, confirmed on image): mean Tmin 35 h (5th-95th 32-38); IIV CV Tmin 45% (33-59); mean C50 6.6 mg/L (4.3-9.2); IIV CV C50 67% (42-107). Priors (Table 1): Tmin mean 36 h, C50 mean 6 mg/L, CV 50%.
- All individual C50 < 8 mg/L; Cav ranged 5.9-18.3 mg/L. Median Ter 47 h (10th-90th percentile 39-76 h). Ter does not shorten much above Cav 10 mg/L (Fig. 4).
- No ke0, no effect-compartment model; not an opioid. No therapeutic/toxic range estimated here; the paper cites 6 mg/L as the lower bound of the therapeutic range for continuous-infusion quinine (Davis 1990, ref 3; PDF p2-3).

## Covariates
Body weight (linear, intercept-plus-slope, on unbound CL and V; CL intercept fixed 0, so CL/F is proportional to BW). Time since treatment start via fu. Body weight explained IIV better than age. PD parameters not correlated with age.

## Caveats
- n = 30, African children 0.55-6.7 y with uncomplicated malaria; PK only days 1-3.
- Unbound fraction never measured; b fixed and fu 0.15 assumed, so the time trend in CL/F and V/F is largely imposed by assumption.
- Disease-state PK (alpha1-acid glycoprotein falls as malaria resolves); not applicable to non-malarial patients.
- Dose basis is quinine base, not salt.
- Stated half-life 9.1 h vs derived 9.9 h (open question).
- Efficacy conclusion for 5-day course is explicitly preliminary (small n, 14-day follow-up).
