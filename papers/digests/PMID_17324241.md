# Nath 2007: melphalan population PK in children (BMT)

## Study
Prospective single-centre study, Children's Hospital at Westmead, 59 children aged 0.3-18 y undergoing autologous or allogeneic BMT 1994-2003. Randomly split into a development set (39 children, 571 concentrations) and a validation set (20 children, 277 concentrations); Table 1 (PDF p.3, text layer): development median age 5.4 y (1.0-15.8), weight 18.8 kg (IQR 13.5-28.1), height 110 cm, BSA 0.76 m2, GFR 115 mL/min/1.73 m2 (IQR 94-139), 28 male / 11 female, 17 with prior carboplatin; validation median 6.9 y, 20.3 kg, GFR 105, 10/10, 9 with prior carboplatin. Whole cohort weight range 7.7-104 kg (Discussion, PDF p.12). Melphalan (Alkeran) as a 15-min IV infusion: single doses of 140 or 180 mg/m2, or divided 70 mg/m2 x 3 d or 30 mg/m2 x 4 d; sampling on one day only. Median 15 lithium-heparin samples per child, before the infusion and at 0, 5, 10, 15, 20, 30, 40, 50 min, 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 12, 24 h after the infusion end. Plasma melphalan by HPLC (LOQ 0.5 ug/mL, linear 0.5-40 ug/mL). NONMEM 5.1.1, FOCE with eta-epsilon interaction, exponential IIV, combined additive and proportional error. Some children had prior carboplatin (CPT, 5 days of Calvert-dosed carboplatin before melphalan), busulphan or TBI.

## Model and parameters
Two-compartment linear model with first-order elimination, parameterized by CL, V (central), k12, k21. Final covariate model (Table 4, PDF p.7, image-read), development dataset:

- CL (L/h) = 0.34 x WT(kg) - 3.17 x CPT + 0.0377 x GFR(mL/min/1.73 m2); the CL constant theta1 is fixed to zero.
- V (L) = 1.12 + 0.178 x WT(kg)
- k12 = 1.70 (h-1), k21 = 1.84 (h-1) (the units are not printed in Table 4; Table 2 prints h-1 for these)

Basic (no covariate) model, Table 2 (PDF p.6, text layer only, not image-checked): CL 10.1 L/h, V 4.81 L, k12 1.81 h-1, k21 1.86 h-1, IIV 60/58/61/64%. Derived at the median child (Table 6, image-read, median (IQR)): CL 0.47 L/h/kg (0.36-0.57), 12.5 L/h/m2; V 0.23 (0.16-0.29) per kg and 6.0 (4.3-7.5) per m2 (see open question 2 on the printed units); k 2.1 h-1; t1/2 alpha 0.12 h, t1/2 beta 0.83 h (total group); no-carboplatin group CL 0.50 L/h/kg, 14.9 L/h/m2, t1/2 beta 0.73 h; carboplatin group CL 0.40 L/h/kg, 8.6 L/h/m2, t1/2 beta 0.96 h. AUC by dose group (ug/mL.h, no carboplatin): 140 mg/m2 9.0 (7.9-10.7, n = 20); 70 mg/m2 5.0 (3.7-6.3, n = 6); 180 mg/m2 with carboplatin 20.7 (15.3-23.9, n = 26).

Derived by me (not in the paper) for a child of 15.5 kg, GFR 115, no carboplatin: CL = 0.34 x 15.5 + 0.0377 x 115 = 9.61 L/h (6.44 with carboplatin); V = 1.12 + 0.178 x 15.5 = 3.88 L; Q = k12 x V = 6.6 L/h; V2 = V x k12/k21 = 3.58 L.

Validation (Table 5, PDF p.9, image-read, n = 277): bias -0.0260 mg/L (95% CI -0.2669 to 0.2148), precision (RMSE) 2.1056 mg/L (1.2752-2.6909). The model under-predicts concentrations above 20 mg/L (Discussion, PDF p.12). Limited-sampling model for AUC (15-min infusion only): AUC = 0.238 + 5.301 x C(2h) + 1.184 x C(15min) - 0.942 x C(1h) (text layer, PDF p.11). Dosing nomogram: dose (mg) = target AUC x CL, target AUC 9 ug/mL.h (median of the 140 mg/m2 no-carboplatin group); Table 7 (text layer) lists doses by weight and GFR, with a footnote that it is population-mean based and needs test-dose individualization.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No PD end point, no MEAC, no ke0; the toxicity-exposure relationship is cited only from earlier work, and the authors state a pharmacodynamic study is still needed to choose a target AUC. Not an opioid paper.

## Covariates
Retained (forward then backward, dOFV > 6.63 forward, backward increases of 12.8 to 371 per Table 3 text layer): weight on CL and V, prior carboplatin on CL (-3.17 L/h), GFR on CL. Weight, weight^0.75, height, BSA, age, gender, GFR, CPT, TBI, busulphan and dose group were screened; TBI was significant alone but not retained; dose group (mg/m2) and busulphan not significant (so linear kinetics are suggested). Carboplatin reduces CL and makes the PK dose-nonlinear, so a test dose does not predict a full dose after carboplatin (Discussion, PDF p.13). The authors caution the nomogram may not apply below 1 y (one infant, the highest-AUC outlier).

## Caveats
- Development n = 39 only; one child under 1 y; weight 7.7-104 kg, so the linear-in-weight CL is a population-specific fit, not allometric (intercept fixed to 0).
- Some children probably fit a three-compartment model better (authors).
- Median of 15 samples per child but only a single dosing day per child; melphalan hydrolyses in vitro, handled by rapid freezing at -40 C.
- Interindividual variability computed from sqrt(variance) x 100.
- Table 6 prints incorrect volume units (open question 2); Table 4's text layer mis-aligns the rows, so only the image-read values are used.
- Table 7 was read from the text layer only.
