# Gupta 1992: droperidol IV infusion vs rectal osmotic module

## Study
Technical note (ALZA). Open-label randomized crossover, 7-day washout. 8 healthy men, 19-29 yr, 64-76 kg
(Table I mean 70.8, SD 4.2 kg). Each received 3 mg droperidol (a) IV as a 24 h constant-rate infusion of
125 mcg/h (Droleptan) and (b) rectally from an Osmet osmotic drug delivery module (ODM; 3 mg, designed to
deliver at constant rate for 15 h; inserted 24 h). Plasma at 0, 0.5, 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 14, 18, 24, 26, 28, 30,
33, 36 h. Radioimmunoassay (Janssen), detection limit 0.20 ng/mL. Noncompartmental analysis (Watari & Benet);
kel by log-linear regression after the infusion stopped; CL = IV dose / AUCinf. IV data also "fitted to a
one-compartment infusion model" (no parameters given).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None measured. No concentration-effect, ke0, or effective concentration. (Not an opioid paper.)

## Covariates
None examined (n = 8 homogeneous young men).

## Caveats
Pilot study, n = 8 young healthy men, radioimmunoassay. The 3.2 ng/mL is the mean of individual observed
peaks (Tmax mean 17.8 h), not a fitted steady state; the mean curve sits a little below it. The text has a few
inconsistencies (rectal F range 39-65 in Discussion vs 38-65 in Table I; table title misspells droperidol).
The paper compares its 9.3 mL/min/kg with Fischler's 14.1 in anaesthetised patients.

## Verification against stanpumpR
- Design (8 healthy men, 3 mg as 125 mcg/h for 24 h): **matches**.
- CL 39.4 L/h = 657 mL/min (derived: 39.4/60*1000 = 656.7): **matches**.
- "Plateau about 3.2 ng/mL": the paper's 3.2 (SD 0.6) is the mean IV Cmax (Table I), the mean of individual
  peaks, not a plateau; Fig. 1's mean curve is about 2.5-2.9 ng/mL over 6-24 h. The test comment
  (test-drugs-droperidol.R:53) correctly says "intravenous Cmax 3.2 +/- 0.6", but R/drugs_droperidol.R:45,
  help :18 and docs :56 call it a plateau. Also, Gupta's own CL implies a steady state of 125/39.4 = 3.17
  ng/mL (derived), so 3.2 is a fair target either way. Verdict: **discrepancy (wording, minor)** - "plateau"
  should be "mean peak"; the numerical check is sound.
- Model prediction 3.22 ng/mL (derived: 0.125 mg/h / (0.646 L/min * 60) = 3.22 ng/mL at true steady state):
  arithmetic **matches**.
