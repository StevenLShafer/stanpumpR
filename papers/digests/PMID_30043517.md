# van Esdonk 2018: pregabalin PK/PD on experimental pain models

## Study
Part 2 of a randomised placebo-controlled crossover (Okkerse 2017). 16 healthy volunteers (8 M/8 F) given 300 mg pregabalin orally or placebo. Table 1 (read by position): weight 68.0 (8.22) kg, height 176 cm, age 21.75 (19-25) y, BMI 21.89, FFM 50.26 kg, SCr 82.19 umol/L, CG GFR 112.7 mL/min. PK samples predose, 0.5-10 h (136 used; LLOQ 20 ug/L). Pain battery 10 times per visit: cold pressor PTT, electrical PTT, pressure PTT, heat PDT. NONMEM 7.3, sequential PK then PD.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- Cold pressor PTT: turnover (indirect response) model; a linear relationship between pregabalin concentration and **kin** was superior (dOFV -88.8) to a direct effect (dOFV -27.3); Emax no better (Results p.576). kout 0.39 /h, t1/2 = 1.78 h (derived).
- Electrical stimulation PTT: turnover with effect on kin, EC50 < 1 ug/L, so modelled as on/off; kout 0.494 /h.
- Pressure PTT: effect compartment, ke0 RSE 84.4%, abandoned (value not reported). Heat PDT: abandoned.
- No MEAC, no potency ratio (not an opioid). Derived: with the paper's own PK and ke0 = 0.39 /h, an effect-site analogue peaks 4.71 h after the dose.

## Covariates
Weight (allometric, fixed exponents). GFR not retained (collinear with weight, small n).

## Caveats
n = 16, single dose level, young lean subjects; sparse absorption-phase sampling; IIV on kout 105%.

## Verification against stanpumpR
- 300 mg, 16 volunteers, cold pressor PTT, kout 0.39 /h (RSE 21%), slope 0.135 per mg/L, half-time 1.8 h, electrical kout 0.49 /h: **matches** Table 3.
- 283 min tPeak: derived on Chan's PK; with van Esdonk's own PK the same ke0 gives a 4.71 h peak (derived), so 4.7 h is robust: **matches**.
- **Discrepancy (statement, not number):** code and help say the paper's equations are in an unretrieved supplement and that whether the drug stimulates production (ke0 = kout exactly) or slows loss is unknown (R/drugs_pregabalin.R:154-157; inst/help/drugs/pregabalin.md:19). The main text (p.576) says the linear effect is on kin, the stimulated-production case, so by the code's own argument ke0 = kout. The ke0 value used is unaffected. One nuance: the model also has a placebo baseline drift (-0.07 s/h) that an effect site does not represent.
