# Cortínez 2010: propofol PK in obesity

## Study
Three data sources pooled (51 adults, 1482 samples; weight 93 ± 24 kg, range 44-160; age 46 ± 16 yr, 25-81; BMI 33 ± 9, range 16-52; PDF p1 abstract and p4). Study 1: Schnider 1998, 24 healthy volunteers 26-81 yr, 44-122 kg, bolus then 60 min infusion at 1.5-12 mg/kg/h, arterial plasma, LLOQ 0.02 µg/mL. Study 2: Servin 1993, 8 morbidly obese patients 25-66 yr, 97-169 kg, stepwise 21/12/6 mg/kg/h by corrected weight, arterial blood converted to plasma with blood/plasma ratio 1.18, LLOQ 0.04. Study 3 (new, "index"): 19 ASA II obese bariatric patients, age 40 ± 8.7 (28-56), weight 106 ± 18 kg (82-134), height 163 ± 13 cm, BMI 39.7 ± 4.1, 11 F / 7 M (Table 1 as printed; the 11/7 sums to 18, not 19), propofol 2 mg/kg bolus then 10-8-6-5 mg/kg/h for 5-20-40-120 min by total body weight, with remifentanil TCI; 163 arterial plasma samples, HPLC-fluorescence, LLOQ 0.025 µg/mL. NONMEM VI, FOCE-I, ADVAN 11 TRANS 4, bootstrap 1000, prediction-corrected VPC.

## Model and parameters
Three-compartment mamillary model, parameters standardised to 70 kg by allometry: P = Pstd·(X/70)^PWR, PWR 0.75 for clearances and 1 for volumes (allometric model). Size descriptors compared: TBW, lean body weight, free fat mass, normal fat mass (NFM). Age: FAGE = exp(SL·(AGE − 50)).

Pooled-model results stated in text only: allometric TBW model beat linear TBW (ΔOBJ 15.371); adding age to V2 and Q2 improved fit by ΔOBJ 43.484; FFM, LBW and NFM gave no improvement over TBW (NFM fat fraction Ffat came out at 1, i.e. TBW). In the index study alone, linear TBW scaling was best (OBJ 178.098). The pooled numeric table is in Supplementary S2 (not available).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None: no ke0, EC50, tPeak or effect measure. Not an opioid paper (remifentanil was co-administered only), so no MEAC/potency audit applies. Simulation (30-yr woman, 140 kg, 160 cm, target 3 µg/mL for 3 h; Figs 4-5): infusion rates 10.9 → 8.8 mg/kg/h (Schnider), 11.1 → 5.8 (Marsh), 10.5 → 5.1 (pooled model); Marsh with Servin corrected weight 7.1 → 3.7. Context-sensitive half-time after 3 h: 0.7 min (Schnider), 3.6 (pooled), 8.8 (Marsh); 80 % decrement time 8.2, 51 and 55 min.

## Covariates
TBW, allometric (0.75 clearances, 1 volumes), is the size descriptor; age lowers V2 and Q2 (about 1.6 % and 1.5 % per year from 50 yr). No gender effect found. Height not required in the final model, which removes the need for the James lean-mass equation.

## Caveats
Authors': index study had short infusions and 2 h of post-infusion sampling, so V2, V3, Q2 are imprecise (V3 CI 31.1-566); only 51 subjects pooled (covariate selection bias possible below 100); elderly data are limited and come mostly from Schnider's study; model valid for 25-81 yr. Mine: index-study sex counts as printed total 18, not 19; Table 1 PDF text layer is scrambled and was read from the image.
