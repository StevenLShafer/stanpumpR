# Kawedia 2025: busulfan clearance with phenytoin vs levetiracetam

## Study
- Single-centre (MD Anderson) timed-sequential myeloablative regimen protocol, NCT01572662. 150 patients: 98 on phenytoin, 52 on levetiracetam (levetiracetam patients all in the 20,000 uM*min arm). Median age 61 y (24-75); 91 male, 59 female (Table 1, PDF p5).
- Busulfan IV, fixed test dose 80 mg/m2 over 3 h on days -13 and -12, then PK-guided doses days -6 to -3 (course target AUC 20,000 +/-12% uM*min, 82.1 mg*h/L; or 16,000 uM*min, 65.7 mg*h/L). Fludarabine 40 mg/m2 daily days -6 to -3.
- Phenytoin 600 mg orally, then 600 or 300 mg twice daily (300 mg if BSA <1.82 m2), starting the night before the first busulfan dose and stopping a day after the last. Levetiracetam 500 mg on the same schedule.
- Sampling: pre-dose, 0.75 and 2.91 h during infusion, then 3.15, 3.5, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 16 h after start. Plasma busulfan by LC-MS. One-compartment individual fits in ADAPT; AUC from CL. PK on day -13 and day -6. One patient's CL (>150 mL/min/m2) was capped at 150.

P-values (Table 2): day -13 CL P=0.47, half-life P=0.17; day -6 CL P=0.011, half-life P=0.058. Dose adjustment after the day -6 PK averaged +29.05 +/-24.52% (phenytoin) and +9.74 +/-14.83% (levetiracetam). If only a day -13 PK had been used, predicted course AUC would be 18,488 +/-1086 (phenytoin; 17 patients below 17,600) vs 19,492 +/-930 uM*min (levetiracetam) (Table 3, text p5-6); with only day -6, 20,857 +/-645 vs 20,327 +/-504 uM*min.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD. Exposure target only: course AUC 20,000 uM*min +/-12% (17,600-22,400), plasma busulfan. No seizures in either arm. Not an opioid; no MEAC, ke0, tPeak or potency ratio. Interaction magnitude: CL rose 14.7% (phenytoin) vs 4.93% (levetiracetam) between day -13 and day -6.

## Covariates
Antiseizure drug (phenytoin vs levetiracetam) is the comparison; phenytoin started 7 days before the day -6 dose, allowing enzyme induction. Fludarabine given in both arms (authors cite it as lowering busulfan CL moderately, a reference-based statement). Body size via BSA (per m2 clearance).

## Caveats
- Non-randomised, sequential cohorts (phenytoin first, then levetiracetam), so era effects are possible; day -13 CL did not differ.
- Day -12, -5, -4, -3 CL not measured, assumed equal to the adjacent measured day; course AUC is an estimate.
- Authors note phenytoin is now rarely used with busulfan.
- Table text uses "mM*min" in some places but the rendered page and the 16,000/20,000 conversion to 65.7/82.1 mg*h/L indicate uM*min.

## Verification against stanpumpR
Library has R/drugs_busulfan.R (McCune 2014; CL 11.4 L/h per 62 kg normal fat mass, V1 13.9 L, plasma, CL x0.932 after 6 h and x0.919 after 36 h of the course). Independent comparison:
- Kawedia CL (derived, assuming BSA about 1.8 m2, not given in the paper): 96.9 mL/min/m2 = about 10.5 L/h on day -13; 110.9 = about 12.0 L/h on day -6. McCune 11.4 L/h at 62 kg NFM (R/drugs_busulfan.R:91) falls between them, so agreement is fair. The library's own header predicts a falling clearance over the course (R/drugs_busulfan.R:63-70); Kawedia's day -6 vs day -13 change is a RISE of 14.7% on phenytoin and 4.9% on levetiracetam, the opposite direction, within a regimen that has a 7-day gap and fludarabine. The library's course factors were fitted to 4-day dosing and do not apply to this schedule.
- Half-life about 2.6-2.8 h (Kawedia, one-compartment) is a different structural model to McCune's two-compartment one; not directly comparable.
- Library has no phenytoin-busulfan interaction (no busulfan match in the antiseizure interaction files, data-raw/antiseizureInteractions.R and docs/antiseizure-interactions-review.md). This paper supports one but with no value for a stable phenytoin level.
- Verdict: partly-verified (nothing cited; comparisons are derived and BSA is assumed).
