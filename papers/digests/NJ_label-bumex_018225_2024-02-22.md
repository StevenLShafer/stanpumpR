# Bumex (bumetanide) tablets US label, 2024

## Study
This is the US prescribing information for BUMEX tablets, 0.5, 1 and 2 mg (Validus; dated January 2024; Reference
ID 5311711). It is the Bumex tablet label as named, so there is no source file problem. It is an old-format
(non-PLR) label; pages 10-15 are carton art and the signature page.
- **Description (p1):** bumetanide is a loop diuretic, MW 364.42.
- **Indication:** edema of heart failure and of hepatic and renal disease, including nephrotic syndrome. Not
  hypertension.
- **Dosage (p8):**
  - Usual oral dose is 0.5-2 mg daily, usually as a single dose. If needed, a 2nd or 3rd dose at 4-5 h
    intervals, up to 10 mg/day.
  - Intermittent schedules are recommended. Keep doses minimal in hepatic failure.
  - Substitute for furosemide at about 1:40.
  - Parenteral (IV/IM) bumetanide is for when oral use is impaired or impractical.

- **Elderly:** nonrenal clearance, bioavailability and V did not differ from younger subjects. The label gives no
  numeric bioavailability.
- **Neonates:** cord-serum binding is about 97%, and bumetanide displaces bilirubin at 0.5-50 µg/mL.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- **Time course (p2):** oral onset of diuresis in 30-60 min, with peak activity at 1-2 h. At 1-2 mg diuresis is
  largely complete in 4 h; at higher doses it lasts 4-6 h. IV diuresis starts within minutes and peaks at
  15-30 min.
- **Infants (p3):** in 56 infants, the maximal diuretic effect came at a bumetanide excretion rate of about
  7 µg/kg/h, at doses of 0.035-0.040 mg/kg.
- **Potency:**
  - Clinical Pharmacology (p1) says 1 mg ≈ 40 mg furosemide.
  - Warnings, Ototoxicity (p4) says the diuretic potency is about 40-60 times furosemide.
  - The two statements are inconsistent within the label; it states both.

## Covariates
Age (elderly and neonates; see the table above). Renal and hepatic impairment are discussed qualitatively.

## Caveats
- A descriptive old-format label, with no Tmax, F, adult CL or V.
- The pediatric values come from small published studies.
