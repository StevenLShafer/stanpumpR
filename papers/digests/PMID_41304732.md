# Gu 2025: PBPK model of 8 drugs in heart failure (digoxin, midazolam among them)

## Study
Whole-body PBPK model (stomach, 3 absorbing intestinal segments, liver, kidney, lung, heart, brain, muscle, adipose, skin, arterial/venous blood, rest of body) coded in Phoenix WinNonlin 8.4 (PDF p.3-4). Eight drugs: digoxin, furosemide, bumetanide, torasemide, captopril, valsartan, felodipine, midazolam. First validated in healthy subjects (70 kg virtual, 1000 subjects), then moved to HF by replacing tissue blood flows only; intrinsic hepatic and renal clearance assumed unchanged by HF (p.4). Virtual populations: normal and NYHA II, III, IV, each 1000 virtual 70-kg subjects; CLli,int, CLk,int, fu,b, Peff and ka sampled within 80-120% of baseline (p.5). Validation against published HF concentration data (literature; refs 5, 6, 21, 29, 32, 48-53). Success criteria: AUC or Cmax within 0.5-2.0-fold of observed, or observations inside the 5th-95th percentile band (p.5). No new clinical data.

## Model and parameters
HF blood flows (mL/min, 70 kg) come from Leithe 1984 (ref 2): hepatic flow 76/54/46% of normal for class II/III/IV, renal 78/55/63%, skin/adipose/muscle 57/44/28% (p.4); GI and splenic flow assumed to follow hepatic flow. Table 1 (PDF p.5, read on the page image; classes II / III / IV):

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD end point. Not an opioid paper; no MEAC, ke0, t1/2ke0, tPeak, hysteresis or equianalgesic ratio. Concentration thresholds used for digoxin (plasma, units ng/mL, assay not stated): toxic threshold 2.0 ng/mL (refs 58, 59); guideline therapeutic range in HF 0.5-0.9 ng/mL (ref 60); 0.5 ng/mL used as the minimum effective concentration in the dose optimisation (p.12). Reported 4-week mortality by digoxin level: <1.0 ng/mL 2.0%; 1.1-2.0, 5.0%; 2.1-4.0, 8.6%; 4.1-6.0, 7.9%; >6.0, 50% (p.13, from ref 59).

## Covariates
HF class (II/III/IV) acts only through tissue blood flow (Table 1). Age, sex, body weight, drug-drug interactions were not modelled (stated limitation, p.14). Midazolam: the Introduction cites Patel 1990 for 30% lower systemic clearance in HF (p.2); the model itself does not alter clearance.

## Caveats
Virtual 70-kg population only; IIV imposed as 80-120% uniform-type sampling, not estimated from data. HF assumed to leave intrinsic clearance unchanged, which the midazolam under-prediction (and the drug's reported 30% clearance fall) argues against. Validation is by 0.5-2-fold criterion, which is loose. Local sensitivity (Fig 7) and Sobol analysis (Fig 8) for digoxin steady-state AUC: renal intrinsic clearance dominates, then fu,b, hepatic intrinsic clearance, arterial flow; Cmax is driven by muscle flow and Peff (p.10-11).
Digoxin dose optimisation (p.12, Fig 9): at 0.25 mg once daily, steady-state Cmax exceeded 2.0 ng/mL in over 50% of HF II-IV patients; time above 2.0 ng/mL was 0.7, 1 and 1.7 h; Cmin above 0.5 ng/mL in 43.8%, 44.0%, 40.6%. Adjusted regimens: 0.13 mg bid (II), 0.12 mg bid (III), 0.105 mg bid (IV), i.e. 0.26, 0.24, 0.21 mg/day (derived); Cmax then below 2.0 ng/mL in all; Cmin above 0.5 ng/mL in 51.7%, 47.2%, 36.7%. These were simulated, not clinically tested. Internal inconsistency: the text gives the standard-regimen Cmin<0.5 incidence in class II as 56.6% (p.13) while 100-43.8 = 56.2% (derived).

## Verification against stanpumpR
Not cited, so nothing to verify; compared as an uncited independent source.
- Digoxin (R/drugs_digoxin.R:1-80; oral, Yukawa 2001, apparent parameters, constant daily rate): the code's band is 0.5 to 2.0 mcg/L serum (lines 77-79), numerically the same as the paper's 0.5 ng/mL floor and 2.0 ng/mL toxic threshold (mcg/L = ng/mL), and the header's note that practice targets the lower half in HF agrees with the 0.5-0.9 ng/mL guideline range the paper cites. The paper's finding that standard 0.25 mg/day gives Cmax >2 ng/mL in over half of simulated HF patients concerns a model with a daily peak; the library draws only the average concentration for a constant rate (header lines 43-56), so peaks are not compared. The paper provides no digoxin PK parameter table in the PDF (Supplementary).
- Midazolam (R/drugs_midazolam.R): no heart-failure covariate in the library (no match for heart failure/cardiac/CHF). The paper's own midazolam HF prediction under-predicted observed exposure (AUC Obs/Pre 1.50, Cmax 1.62) and so cannot support a cardiac-output adjustment.
- drugDefaults_global.csv digoxin rows list 0.5 and 2 as the band values, matching the paper's thresholds. Verdict: not-applicable.
