# Cockcroft 1976: the Cockcroft-Gault creatinine clearance equation

## Study
- Records of 534 consecutive patients (96% male) at Queen Mary Veterans' Hospital, Montreal, with two or more 24-h creatinine clearances; 29 excluded for non-steady state (Scr differing > 20%) -> group I, n = 505.
- Group II: 236 of group I with consistent paired 24-h creatinine excretion (within 20%), excretion >= 10 mg/kg/24 h and adequate records; measured CCr 11 mL/min to normal, mean 72.7 (SD 36.6) mL/min.
- 24-h creatinine excretion in 249 males aged 18-92 (226 of group II plus 23 others).
- Creatinine by Technicon AutoAnalyzer method N-11B (alkaline picrate / Jaffe), fasting serum. Upper normal 1.4 mg/100 mL in males, 1.2 in females (cited for this method).

## Model and parameters
Derivation (Results): mean creatinine excretion per kg by decade (Table II) regressed on mean age:
- Eq 1: CrUV24/kg (mg/kg/24 h) = 28 - 0.2 x age (years).
- Eq 3: CCr (mL/min) = (28 - 0.2 age) x wt x 100 / (1440 x Scr) = (140 - age) x wt (kg) / (72 x Scr (mg/100 mL)).
- Women: 15% less (Discussion: authors of other formulae proposed 10-20%; 15% chosen, not derived from female data).
- For an average 72 kg man, CCr = (140 - age)/Scr.

Performance in group II (Tables III-IV): mean predicted 72.8 vs measured 72.7 mL/min; r = 0.83, SEE 19.8, intercept 14.0, slope 0.81. 95% of predictions within 35% and 67% within 20% of measured. Comparators: Jelliffe formulae I and II, Edwards-Whyte III, Siersbaek-Nielsen nomogram (r 0.84).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Not applicable (renal function estimator). Reference measurement: mean of two 24-h urine creatinine clearances.

## Covariates
Age, total body weight, serum creatinine, sex (fixed 15% reduction). Height omitted deliberately (no BSA normalisation).

## Caveats
- Derived and tested in the same group II patients (authors note optimistic validation); 96% men; female factor assumed.
- Adults only: the authors state no experience with children.
- Requires steady-state creatinine; overestimates in early acute renal failure, muscle wasting (falsely low Scr), paraplegia.
- Marked obesity or ascites: less creatinine per kg; authors advise using lean or ideal body weight when pronounced.
- Jaffe (non-creatinine chromogen) creatinine assay, not IDMS-traceable enzymatic; CCr exceeds inulin clearance through secretion.
