# Glund 2019: idarucizumab and dabigatran PK in RE-VERSE AD patients

## Study
Prospective, multicentre, single-arm, open-label RE-VERSE AD study (NCT02104947); this paper is the PK/PD/immunogenicity analysis. Adults >=18 years on dabigatran with uncontrolled or life-threatening bleeding (group A, about 290 in the PK figures) or needing urgent surgery or an invasive procedure (group B, about 190). Mean age 76.6 years, median 78 (Discussion). Dose: idarucizumab 5 g IV, as two 50-mL bolus infusions of 2.5 g each, at most 15 minutes apart. Samples: baseline, after the first infusion, 10-30 min, 1, 2, 4, 12 and 24 h after the second infusion; ADA samples at baseline, day 30, day 90. Assays: idarucizumab concentrations by a central-laboratory method (reported in nmol/L); total and unbound dabigatran including active metabolites (reported in ng/mL; unbound means not bound to idarucizumab or plasma protein); dTT (Hemoclot) and ECT; ADA by bridging electrochemiluminescence. Idarucizumab PK by non-compartmental analysis in Phoenix WinNonlin; AUC0-24 only if the 24 h sample and at least one of the 4 h and 12 h samples existed. Dabigatran below the 1 ng/mL LLOQ was set to 1 ng/mL (unbound). Renal categories by Cockcroft-Gault CrCl: normal >=80, mild 50 to <80, moderate 30 to <50, severe <30 mL/min.

## Model and parameters
No compartmental or population model. Non-compartmental geometric means (gMean). Idarucizumab concentration units nmol/L; AUC nmol*h/L.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Unbound dabigatran fell immediately after idarucizumab to the LLOQ; gMean stayed below 20 ng/mL over 24 h in both groups and in all renal categories except severe renal impairment, which rose slightly above 20 ng/mL at 24 h. The paper calls under 20 ng/mL a level presumed to give little or no anticoagulant effect (not a measured threshold). Recurrence of unbound dabigatran at or above 20 ng/mL occurred in 114 patients (redistribution suggested); earlier and larger with baseline unbound at or above 1000 ng/mL and with renal impairment. Linear relation of dTT and ECT to unbound dabigatran up to about 700 ng/mL (Fig. 3, PDF p. 6): dTT intercept 32.04 s, slope 0.124 s per ng/mL, R2 0.8765; ECT intercept 43.588 s, slope 0.4935 s per ng/mL, R2 0.8733. At least equimolar idarucizumab is needed to bind dabigatran. No ke0, no hysteresis modelling, no effect-site model, no MEAC, no potency ratio. Not an opioid.

## Covariates
Renal function (Cockcroft-Gault) is the only effect on idarucizumab exposure: AUC0-24 up 38% (mild), 90% (moderate), 146% (severe) versus normal; Cmax up 20%, 29%, 33%. Hepatic impairment (ALT/AST), region, bleeding versus surgical group, and ADA status had no relevant effect. Age effect is cited from an unpublished population analysis (none seen in it).

## Caveats
Sparse, descriptive; no model; no clearance or volume. Group comparison is a single-arm cohort. Idarucizumab in hepatic impairment was judged by liver enzymes only, which the authors say was not formally studied. Ethnicity not assessed. ADA: 28 of 501 (5.6%) positive overall, of which 19 (3.8%) pre-existing and 9 (1.8%) treatment-emergent; the figures are consistent (28 = 19 + 9), though 3.3.1 words 28 as "pre-existing" and the next sentence gives 19. Boehringer Ingelheim sponsored and employs most authors.
