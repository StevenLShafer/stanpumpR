# Thompson 2025: unbound and bound platinum PopPK after cisplatin, with 5-HT3 antagonists

## Study
- Prospective, randomised to a 5-HT3 antagonist (ondansetron 8 mg oral, granisetron 2 mg oral or palonosetron 0.25 mg IV, 1-2 h before cisplatin); NCT03817970. 33 adults in cycle 1 or 2 of cisplatin (at least 25 mg/m2, 0.5-2 h infusion), University of Colorado and MSKCC.
- Demographics (text, PDF p6): 70% male, 85% Caucasian, 64% head and neck; age mean 61 (32-75); cisplatin dose 66 (20-100) mg/m2; BMI 26 (17-39); BSA 1.9 (1.4-2.6) m2; baseline eGFR 90 (60-118) mL/min/1.73 m2. 149 total and 147 unbound platinum concentrations.
- Samples (lithium heparin plasma): pre-dose and 0.5, 1, 2, 4, 48, 72, 240 h from start of infusion.
- Assay: ICP-MS for total platinum and unbound platinum (10 kDa ultrafiltrate, 5000 g, 4 C, 30 min). Bound = total - unbound; fb = bound/total.
- Software Phoenix NLME 8.3 (QRPEM for unbound-only and bound-only models, FOCE-ELS for the combined model). Covariates by forward (dOFV > 3.84) and backward (> 6.64) steps.

## Model and parameters
Table 1 (PDF p6, printed p768), read on the page image. The table prints "Mean +/- SD" and CV%; the text calls them estimates. Three model families:

Unbound Pt, two compartments, additive error. Final covariate model: V1_u 37.7 L, V2_u 347 L, CL1_u 27.3 L/h, CL2_u 25.1 L/h, visit effect on V1_u 1.25 (exponent, first dose), lorazepam effect on CL2_u 0.643 (exponent), -2LL 2290. Final model without covariates: V1_u 45.1, V2_u 347, CL1_u 27.4, CL2_u 32.1, -2LL 2311. Equations: V1_u = 37.7 x e^(1.25 x [visit==1]) x e^eta; CL2_u = 25.1 x e^(0.643 x [lorazepam]) x e^eta.

Bound Pt, two compartments: V1_b 94.3 L, V2_b 35.1 L, CL1_b 0.411 L/h, CL2_b 4.95 L/h (final); no covariates; -2LL 2538.

Combined: unbound two-compartment plus one bound compartment, one-way flow from central unbound to central bound at fraction fb (upper bound 1). Final covariate model: V1_u 31.2, V2_u 484, V1_b 1.10, CL1_u 1.64, CL2_u 64.0 L/h, CL1_b 0.508, fb 0.990, visit effect on V1_u 2.04, lorazepam on CL2_u 0.298 (-2LL 5009). Base combined: V1_u 50.7, V2_u 1180, V1_b 7.37, CL1_u 6.82, CL2_u 56.1, CL1_b 0.521, fb 0.948 (-2LL 5025).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD end point. Antiemetic efficacy (MASCC antiemesis tool at 24 h and 4 days) did not differ between the three agents; only 4 of 33 vomited. Dose-normalised AUC (AUC_d, per 100 mg cisplatin): ondansetron versus palonosetron +331% unbound (P=.233) and +188% bound (P=.023); granisetron versus palonosetron +114% unbound (P=.020) and +143% bound (P=.060) (PDF p8). The palonosetron group is the comparator. Mechanism discussed: MATE1 inhibition IC50 0.1 uM (ondansetron), 5.0 uM (granisetron), 1.6 uM (palonosetron), cited from another paper.

## Covariates
Visit (first or second cisplatin course) on V1_u; concomitant lorazepam on CL2_u. Tested and not retained: age, BSA, albumin (all but one patient normal), cisplatin dose. Bound-only model: none.

## Caveats
- n = 33, 147-149 observations; early scatter 0-2 h, possibly infusion-line contamination; bound platinum calculated, not measured.
- The authors say the covariate models had CVs over 20% and, in the first attempt, V2_u 1.19 x 10^9 L with CV 709%. Eta shrinkage is very high for several parameters (V2_u 0.92-0.98, CL1_b 0.88).
- Antagonist assignment is not in the final models (only in the AUC_d comparison, Table S5).
- The V2_u and CL2_u values far exceed the published Urien 2004 values (the authors say 12.0 L and 8.64 L/h) and they attribute this to the 240 h sampling.

## Verification against stanpumpR
- **docs/chemotherapy.md:746-748**: the ambiguity of CL1_u is real (27.3 L/h unbound-only versus 1.64 L/h combined, Table 1). Confirmed; left open. First-cycle and lorazepam covariates indeed have no field in the model.
- **Comparison with the library's cisplatin model** (`R/drugs_cisplatin.R:33-36`, Urien 2004): V1 23.4 L x (BSA/1.7)^1.60, CL 35.5 L/h x (BSA/1.7)^0.83 x (CLCr/81)^0.36, Q 8.64 L/h, V2 12.0 L. The Thompson values quoted in the paper's own Discussion are in agreement: its unbound-only V1_u (37.7-45.1 L) is larger than Urien's 23.4 L (about 1.6-1.9x, a typical patient of BSA 1.7 for Urien against BSA 1.9 here); CL1_u 27.3 is lower than 35.5 L/h (0.77x); V2 347 L against 12 L (29x) and CL2_u 25.1 against 8.64 L/h (2.9x). The paper's text values for Urien match the library header (23.4, 35.5, 8.64, 12.0). The large V2 and CL2 differences run in the direction the authors give: longer sampling (240 h versus 24 h).
- Both papers measure platinum by assay (Urien atomic absorption, Thompson ICP-MS); Thompson's unit and dose basis is not stated in the text read (open question), whereas the library works in ug/L platinum for a dose in mg cisplatin.
- The Thompson model has no BSA or creatinine clearance term, so it cannot replace Urien's covariate structure.
