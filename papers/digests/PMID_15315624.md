# Calvo 2004: propofol formulation, PK/PD in anaesthetized patients

## Study
Retrospective NONMEM V analysis of 77 ASA I-II surgical patients (age 18-65, mean 45; weight 47-100 kg, mean 72.6; Table 1, PDF p.6) at Galdakao Hospital, each given one of five commercial propofol 1% products ("form 1" Diprivan, 2 Recofol, 3 Ivofol, 4 Propofol Abbott, 5 Fresenius). Group I (n=48; 23 M/25 F; model development; all five forms) was induced by rapid infusion (600 mL/h) to BIS 60; group II (n=29; validation; forms 1, 2 and 5 only) to clinical loss of signs (eyelash reflex, verbal contact, tone; MOAA/S grade 2). Premedication atropine 0.01 mg/kg, midazolam 0.02 mg/kg, alfentanil 15 ug/kg; alfentanil 1 ug/kg/min, cisatracurium. Maintenance by Roberts' scheme: 10 mg/kg/h immediately after induction, 8 mg/kg/h after 10 min, 6 mg/kg/h after 20 min (p.1039-1040). Blood samples: pre-dose, at the end point, and 15 and 30 min later (four per patient in Methods; "three" in the abstract), from the contralateral arm vein; total and unbound (ultrafiltrate) blood concentration by HPLC with fluorescence; detection limit 25 ng/mL total, 5 ng/mL unbound. Group I gave 142 Cb and 132 (Table 5 caption says 137) BIS observations.

## Model and parameters
One-compartment PK (two- and three-compartment fits did not lower the objective function; p.1043); proportional residual error; lognormal IIV. Emax effect model on 100-BIS (Emax fixed at 100, Hill = 1) with an effect compartment linked to the **blood** (central) concentration, dCe/dt = ke0 (Cb - Ce) (Eq 4-5, p.1041). Concentrations are **blood** (not plasma), venous, **total** unless stated. PK was fixed to its final model when PD was fitted.

Formulation effects: form 3 had V 3.34 L vs 9.77 L and a lower ke0 (see open_questions for the two reported values). Form 2 had the highest induction dose (195.1 mg vs form 1 147.2 mg, Table 1, P<0.05 vs form 1) and, in the rejected Model 5.5, V 12.98 L (p.1047). Forms 2, 4, 5 were not significant covariates in a valid run (standard errors large, small n of about 10 each).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: BIS (EEG), modelled as 100-BIS, in group I measured at the induction end point (BIS 60) and at 15 and 30 min (BIS about 40-60 expected); group II end point was clinical (MOAA/S 2), with BIS recorded. Basic ke0 0.19 /min (t1/2ke0 3.64 min), final 0.23 /min (t1/2ke0 3 min) for forms other than 3, Table 5; hysteresis was seen on the effect vs Cb plot. ECe50 3.96 mg/L of total blood propofol (Table 5; see open_questions). Not reported: tPeak, MEAC, any opioid potency. Mean predicted Ce did not differ by form (form 1 2.50, 2: 2.81, 3: 2.70, 4: 3.06, 5: 2.35 mg/L; p.1044). Alfentanil was background analgesia, not studied. Protein binding: mean unbound fraction 0.82% (range 0.42-1.58, Table 1), no difference by form.

## Covariates
Weight on CL (linear, final model above); formulation (form 3) on V and ke0. Age, sex, cholesterol, triglycerides, haematocrit, baseline haemodynamics were not significant PK covariates. Induction dose regressed (GAM) with baseline SBP, age and formulation: dose = 110 + 0.18 SBP0, or 119 + form terms - 1.1 age (p.1042).

## Caveats
Sparse sampling (3-4 samples/patient, within 30 min), one-compartment fit so V is a blend and CL is only that of a 30-min window; venous whole blood; BIS endpoint differences between groups; formulation effects rest on about 10 patients per form, with large standard errors except for form 3; Tables 3-5 contain internal inconsistencies (see open_questions); the Methods say four samples, the abstract three. The paper's own interpretation that form 3's different molecule size explains the low ke0 is speculative.

## Verification against stanpumpR
No citation to verify. Independent comparison with the library's propofol (`R/drugs_propofol.R`, Eleveld 2018, arterial plasma-type, three compartments, tPeak 1.6 min at line 276; Schnider in `R/alternativeModels.R`): for a 72.6 kg, 45-year-old patient the Eleveld equations at lines 233-259 give, by hand arithmetic (derived, approximate), CL about 1.6 L/min and V1 about 6.4 L, against Calvo's 2.31 L/min and a one-compartment 8.4-9.8 L. Calvo is higher in both; the comparator is the library's model, and the difference is expected because it is venous whole-blood, one-compartment, 30-min sampling, with 15 ug/kg alfentanil and midazolam on board (a high-extraction drug). A one-compartment V is not comparable with V1 of a three-compartment model. Calvo's ke0 0.19-0.23 /min (t1/2 3-3.6 min) is slower than the library's tPeak of 1.6 min, but tPeak depends on the PK model and this one-compartment blood link does not map onto it (comparison not computed; different baseline). Neither the formulation effect nor weight-linear CL is represented in stanpumpR, which is consistent: this card does not recommend change.
