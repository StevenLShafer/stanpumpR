# Agema 2021: population PK of oral oxycodone, noroxycodone and noroxymorphone in cancer pain

## Study
- Observational study, Erasmus MC Cancer Institute, Rotterdam (MEC 09.332; NTR4369). Hospitalized adults with
  moderate-severe nociceptive cancer pain on oral oxycodone IR and/or ER (opioid-naive, already on oxycodone, or
  rotating; fentanyl-patch users with IR oxycodone allowed).
- n = 28 (one participated twice); 12 female (43%). Table 1 (median, range; the text layer is column-shifted, so
  the pairing of values to labels is my reading of the layout): age 62.5 y (39-81); weight 80.0 kg (46-135);
  BMI 27.7 (18.9-42.6); follow-up 80.7 h (24.1-211.4); samples per patient 12 (4-51). eGFR 86.0 mL/min (37->90; 3 below 60);
  albumin 42.5 g/L. CYP2D6 EM 12, IM 10, missing 6 (no PM/UM). CYP3A4*22 heterozygous 6. UGT2B7*2 variant 5.
- Doses: ER 5-100 mg twice daily; IR 5-30 mg.
- Sampling: 08:00 and 20:00 (ER dose times); at most once daily pre and 5, 15, 30, 60 min after an IR tablet.
  302 samples, 1207 measurements across 4 analytes; 352 (29.2%) BLQ; M3 method.
- Assay: UPLC-MS/MS; LLOQ 0.200 ng/mL oxycodone and oxymorphone, 1.00 ng/mL noroxycodone and noroxymorphone.
  Model concentrations in nM.
- NONMEM 7.4, Laplacian with interaction; PsN 4.2.0, Pirana, Xpose; bootstrap n = 1000.
- PD: NRS average and maximal pain over previous 12 h, twice daily; nine adverse events on 4-point Likert scale.

## Model and parameters
One-compartment parent with separate first-order IR and ER absorption depots, first-order formation of
noroxycodone (K34) and of noroxymorphone from noroxycodone (K45), each metabolite one-compartment (Fig. 2).
Oxymorphone could not be modelled (179/309 BLQ; 12 patients all BLQ). Table 2 (estimate; RSE; bootstrap median;

- No covariate retained (age, sex, weight, eGFR, albumin, liver tests, CYP2D6, CYP3A4*22, UGT2B7 all tested).
- Model AUCs: noroxymorphone about one eighth and noroxycodone about two thirds of oxycodone's (Discussion p. 10).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- Joint PK/PD NONMEM model attempted (linear, Emax, effect compartment, turnover) but not feasible: pain did not
  track exposure. Mixed-effects regression (Table 3): oxycodone 12-h AUC vs average pain beta 1.28 (95% CI
  -3.71 to 6.29), maximal pain 0.58 (-5.74 to 6.89); adverse events vs oxycodone -2.72, noroxycodone -3.29,
  noroxymorphone -12.13 (all p > 0.05). 5 fentanyl co-users excluded from PD.
- **No MEAC, no ke0, t½ke0 or tPeak, no hysteresis.**
- Literature statements (cited, not measured, Discussion p. 10): oxymorphone plasma concentrations about 2% of
  oxycodone's; oxymorphone thought to contribute 15-20% of oral oxycodone analgesia; oxymorphone mu affinity
  8-44 times oxycodone's (Introduction says 10- to 45-fold). Metabolites could theoretically account for 40% of
  peripheral adverse events (authors' estimate). **No equianalgesic dose ratio.**

## Covariates
None significant (see above). Non-significant trend for UGT2B7 T allele; no CYP2D6 EM vs IM difference in
noroxycodone -> noroxymorphone conversion.

## Caveats
- Small n, sparse twice-daily sampling (most ER samples at trough), no biphasic ER absorption identified.
- K30 and two metabolite volumes fixed from intermediate models; high IIV on metabolite clearances.
- Oxymorphone mostly BLQ at 0.2 ng/mL and not modelled; the "about 2%" ratio is a literature citation, not this
  study's result.
- Co-medication (dexamethasone in one patient changed noroxycodone; 8 on neuropathic agents); 12-h recall pain scores.
- Table text layer is column-scrambled; parameter-row assignment above should be checked against the PDF image.

## Verification against stanpumpR
- R/drugs_oxymorphone.R:70-71 reference "Agema BC et al., Cancers 2021;13:2768", DOI correct. **matches**.
- The context's claimed use (calibrating formation to ~2%) is no longer what master does: formation is now
  calibrated to 1% IV (Finnish studies) and 3.35% oral (Balyan 0.027 / Lalovic 0.04) in R/drugs_oxycodone.R:170-188;
  oxycodone.md, oxymorphone.md and test-drugs-oxymorphone.R:84 no longer mention Agema. Agema's 2% is in any case a
  literature-cited figure, not a measurement (oxymorphone mostly BLQ here). **not-applicable** for that use.
- R/drugs_oxymorphone.R:84-86 (no citation attached in text) "binds the mu receptor 8 to 44 times more tightly ...
  thought to carry 15 to 20% of oral oxycodone's analgesia": both figures appear in Agema's Discussion (p. 10) as
  citations of others (refs 8, 9, 37-39). **matches** Agema's wording, but Agema is a secondary source for them.
  Agema's own Introduction says 10- to 45-fold, an internal inconsistency in the paper.
- R/drugs_oxymorphone.R:132-133 "about 1-3% of oxycodone concentrations": consistent with Agema's cited "approximately
  2%". **matches** (secondary).
- Not used: Agema's oral CL/F (derived ~1.0 L/min) is lower than the code's Lalovic-based 1.44 L/min oral CL/F in
  healthy young adults; cancer patients (median ~62 y) — consistent direction with the code's age factor. Not a
  discrepancy, since stanpumpR does not cite Agema for oxycodone PK.
