# Iselin-Chaves 1998: propofol-alfentanil interaction on BIS, consciousness and recall

## Study
Healthy volunteers, 40 evaluable, two sites (Duke, Emory); Table 1 (PDF p4): propofol alone n=20 (age 30±5 yr, 71±16 kg, 171±9 cm, 9 M/11 F); propofol + alfentanil 50 ng/mL n=10 (Emory; 30±5 yr, 75±16 kg, 6 M/4 F); propofol + alfentanil 100 ng/mL n=10 (Duke; 31±7 yr, 81±17 kg, 8 M/2 F). The 20 propofol-alone subjects were also in an earlier multicentre study (Glass 1997). Radial arterial line.
Drug delivery: computer-assisted infusion to a target EFFECT-SITE concentration, each step held at least 10 min; propofol steps 1, 2, 4, 6 µg/mL (up and down, at least two conscious/unconscious cross-overs) using Gepts PK; alfentanil preselected effect-site 50 ng/mL (Emory) or 100 ng/mL (Duke) using Scott and Stanski PK (PDF p3).
Measures at each steady state: BIS (Aspect A-1000; real-time rev 2.0, recomputed offline as rev 3.0, bifrontal channels), OAA/S responsiveness score (0-5; scores 3-5 conscious, 0-2 unconscious), two arterial samples per level, picture recall/recognition (Duke only), and a tibial periosteal pressure pain stimulus (Duke only); ΔBIS = max BIS in 3 min after the stimulus minus BIS 60 s before.
Assay: propofol by HPLC with fluorometric detection (plasma, arterial), standards within 20%, interassay variability 12%; alfentanil in plasma by radioimmunoassay (Janssen kit), interassay variability 11.2%. Statistics: logistic regression for quantal end points, prediction probability (Pk), ANOVA for ΔBIS.

## Model and parameters
No PK model was fitted; the paper used published sets (Gepts propofol, Scott and Stanski alfentanil) only to drive the infusion. No parameters.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End points: loss of consciousness (OAA/S ≤2) and lack of recall; concentrations are MEASURED arterial PLASMA propofol (µg/mL), not effect-site or target.
Table 2 (PDF p4, printed p952; mean, 95% CI):
- Loss of consciousness, propofol alone / + alfentanil 50 ng/mL / + alfentanil 100 ng/mL:
  - BIS50 64 (61-66) / 67 (64-69) / 72 (67-76)
  - BIS95 49 (45-54) / 63 (57-70) / 54 (45-64)
  - propofol Cp50 2.52 (2.27-2.78) / 2.02 (1.58-2.45) / 1.66 (1.23-2.09) µg/mL
  - propofol Cp95 4.16 (3.57-4.74) / 2.87 (2.01-3.73) / 3.69 (2.50-4.88) µg/mL
  The authors report that the shifts with alfentanil were not statistically significant.
- Lack of recall (no Emory data, memory not tested there): BIS50 89 (85-93) propofol alone, 83 (77-90) with alfentanil 100; BIS95 79 (70-88) and 67 (51-83); propofol Cp50 1.10 (0.76-1.44) and Cp95 1.68 (0.96-2.40) µg/mL with alfentanil 100 only. For propofol alone Cp50/Cp95 for recall could not be computed (all recall at 0 µg/mL; lowest no-recall concentration 0.7 µg/mL).
Table 3 (PDF p5): prediction probability for consciousness, BIS / target / plasma: propofol (n=222) 0.965±0.012 / 0.921±0.015 / 0.950±0.013; propofol/alfentanil (122) 0.961±0.017 / 0.918±0.022 / 0.904±0.030; with alfentanil 50 (49) 0.999±0.003 / 0.976±0.016 / 0.981±0.017; with alfentanil 100 (73) 0.937±0.031 / 0.863±0.037 / 0.835±0.052.
ΔBIS after pain: 6±4 (n=61) propofol alone vs 3±4 (n=73) with alfentanil 100 ng/mL (SE; P<0.001); lowered by propofol concentration (P=0.040) and alfentanil presence (P=0.002) (Figure 3).
Opioid audit (alfentanil): no MEAC / minimum analgesic concentration defined. The alfentanil levels (50 and 100 ng/mL) are the TARGET effect-site values set by the design (assay: plasma RIA); measured alfentanil concentrations are not tabulated in the text read. No ke0, t½ke0, tPeak or hysteresis is estimated (steady-state design, 10 min holds). No potency ratio.
Propofol: no ke0 or tPeak estimated either.

## Covariates
None analysed beyond group (alfentanil level). Demographics did not differ among groups.

## Caveats
Alfentanil dose was fixed at two levels at two different sites (50 at Emory, 100 at Duke), so site and alfentanil level are confounded; memory and pain testing were Duke only. Several authors consult for Aspect (BIS maker) and Aspect funded part of the work. Non-significant trends in Cp50 reflect small n (10 per opioid group). One BIS value of 95 at sedation score 1 was judged an electrode artefact.
