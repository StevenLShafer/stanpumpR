# Chae 2026: teicoplanin PK in Korean post-HSCT neutropenic adults

## Study
- Retrospective TDM data, one hospital (Seoul St. Mary's), 2015-2017. 417 adults screened; 12 dropped for missing covariates and 1 for an erroneous concentration, leaving 405 patients (222 M / 195 F in the 417) with 568 trough observations (p5). Almost all samples were troughs about 1 h before the next dose; the text says 581 measurements were taken (p5).
- Dosing: usual regimen was 400 mg IV q12h x3 loading, then 400 mg daily, but doses were at the physician's discretion and the real dosing history was used (p2).
- Assay: UHPLC-MS/MS, calibration range 3.9-52.9 mg/L, LLOQ validated at 0.72 mg/L (p3). Total (not free) concentration; assay matrix is not stated in the text.
- Because troughs alone cannot define distribution, 360 simulated concentrations were added (6 times 0.3, 1, 1.5, 2.5, 4, 6 h x 60 virtual subjects) from two published Korean models: a 2-compartment model in 15 elderly critically ill patients, and a 3-compartment model in 12 healthy volunteers (200 mg single dose). Stochastic simulation and estimation (SSE) was repeated 1000 times, with FOCE-I in NONMEM 7.5 (p3-4). Table 1 (p5): weight 64.06 +/- 12.22 kg, age 49.29 +/- 14.86 y, serum creatinine 0.63 +/- 0.30 mg/dL, albumin 3.04 +/- 0.41 g/dL, CKD-EPI eGFR 113.38 +/- 20.80 mL/min/1.73 m2.

## Model and parameters
Two-compartment, first-order elimination, IV dosing. CL = theta1 x (eGFR/113.31)^theta5 x (albumin/3.10)^theta6 (Table 3, p8; albumin in g/dL, eGFR in mL/min/1.73 m2 per Table 1). A 3-compartment model fit better by OFV but failed to converge in SSE, so the 2-compartment structure was kept (p5). IIV on CL and V2 only (Table 2, p7). Residual error is combined additive plus proportional.

Base model (Table 3, p8, single fit with 1000-sample bootstrap): CL 1.25 L/h, V1 4.58 L, V2 47.1 L, Q 5.53 L/h, eGFR exponent 0.596, albumin exponent -0.801, omega CL 35.7 CV%, omega V2 78.9 CV%, additive error 0.741, proportional error 0.236.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD. No target concentration is analysed; the paper only says TDM aims to keep troughs above a target (p2, no value given). Not an opioid, so no opioid audit items.

## Covariates
eGFR (CKD-EPI) and albumin on CL as power functions (above). Tested and not retained: height, weight, sex, serum creatinine. No covariate on V1, V2 or Q.

## Caveats
- Almost all real data are troughs; the distribution phase comes from simulated points built on two other Korean models. The authors say a structural misspecification (3-compartment was better) was accepted for stability.
- CL 1.26 L/h is about 2x the two source Korean models (0.63 and 0.69 L/h) (p8). The comparators are those two published Korean cohorts: elderly critically ill patients with mean eGFR 64 and healthy volunteers with eGFR 103 (p9). The authors attribute it partly to high eGFR and low muscle mass, and warn that an enlarged volume may have been folded into CL.
- eGFR is indexed per 1.73 m2; there is no weight in the model, so absolute CL is not per kg.
- Table 4 has the same parameters as Table 3 but the omega values are printed as 0.118/0.458 (probably variances) whereas Table 3 is in CV%; the unit is not stated.
