# MacGregor 2000: two-compartment dopamine pharmacokinetics in nine healthy men

## Study
Wake Forest. Nine healthy non-obese men (10 planned, 1 withdrew). Table 2 (PDF p3, rendered): age 23-45 (mean 33)
y, height 166-186 (mean 175) cm, weight 73-116 (mean 90) kg, creatinine 0.8-1.1 mg/dL; BMI 25.6-35.4 (29.2 ± 3.0)
(PDF p3). Dopamine IV (dominant arm) 10 µg/kg/min for 10 min, 30-min washout, 3 µg/kg/min for 90 min, 30-min washout
(fixed order, not randomized). 32 radial arterial samples per subject (Table 1, PDF p2); 9 samples missing. HPLC
with electrochemical detection after alumina extraction, detection limit 0.8 ng/L, interassay CV 4.6% at 10 ng/L.
NONMEM (Laplacian), lognormal IIV, model choice by Schwartz-Bayesian criterion; a two-stage fit also done. Dose basis
(salt or base) not stated.

## Model and parameters
Best model D (Table 3, PDF p6, rendered): two compartments, absorption lag, V1 weight-adjusted, constant-CV residual
error, 10 parameters; -2LL 1088.10, SBC -571.80, AIC -554.05, APE50 ±59%, APE95 ±514%. Alternatives: model A
(1 compartment) APE50 78%, APE95 100,000%; two-stage one-compartment fit (k10 0.44/min, V1 55.5 L) APE50 81%,
APE95 186,741% (Table 3; text says > 100,000%).

IIV magnitudes and the residual CV are not reported. Derived checks: V1 x k10 = 0.265 L/kg/min (printed 0.27);
k12 V1 = 0.0588; k12 V1/k21 = 0.840; alpha 1.364, beta 0.0567 /min from the micro constants; ln2/beta = 12.2 min
(printed 12.3, presumably from unrounded estimates); 1/(V1 k10) = 3.77. Cl10 at 0.265 L/kg/min is about 24 L/min
for the mean 90 kg man (derived).

Observed (Results, PDF p4): basal 3-8,023 ng/L, not different from end-washout values; 7-10 min samples at 10
µg/kg/min 5,616-201,513 ng/L; 3 µg/kg/min plateau 367-83,745 ng/L (abstract: 12,300-201,500 and 1,880-18,300 ng/L,
apparently subject-level summaries). Per-subject APE50 35-319%, APE95 84-648% (Table 5, PDF p7).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No concentration-effect model. Heart rate rose during 10 µg/kg/min but not above baseline at 3 µg/kg/min (PDF p5);
monitored for safety only. Authors note no published concentration-effect data for dopamine (PDF p8).

## Covariates
Weight on V1 (all parameters per kg since the rate constants are not scaled). Age, sex, serum creatinine had no
significant effect on Css (PDF p5).

## Caveats
n = 9, healthy men only; 10- to 75-fold variability at identical rates; endogenous baseline not modelled; the model
overpredicts the 10 µg/kg/min phase, suggesting dose-dependent (nonlinear) kinetics; fixed dose order; authors warn
against extrapolating to critically ill patients. Cites Le Corre 1993: Vss 0.78 ± 0.22 L/kg at 3 and 1.58 ± 0.62
L/kg at 6 µg/kg/min in ICU patients (PDF p8).
