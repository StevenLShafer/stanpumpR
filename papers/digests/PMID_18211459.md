# Schnider and Minto 2008: Alaris pump uses a constant tPeak, not ke0, for propofol

## Study
A one-page letter, not a study. The Schnider and Minto authors answer Barakat 2007 (Anaesthesia 62:661), who gave propofol by TCI on an Alaris Asena PK pump and simulated effect-site concentrations in Tivatrainer. No data, subjects or assay. Only the printed first page is in the archive; the Pandit reply is cut off at the page end.

## Model and parameters
No PK parameters. The letter states that the Alaris "Schnider model" uses the Schnider 1998 PK (ref 2) but not the published ke0 (ref 3).

  - {name: tPeak_Alaris, value: 1.6, unit: min, source: "p.206", note: "median tPeak the letter says came from visual inspection of the EEG data; Alaris holds tPeak fixed and recomputes ke0 per patient"}

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- Published ke0 0.456 min^-1 combined with the covariate-adjusted PK gives a LONGER tPeak in the elderly than in the young (comparator: young patients under the same published ke0; no values given). End point of the underlying ke0: EEG (the Schnider 1999 propofol EEG work; the letter says only "EEG data").
- Alaris pump: constant tPeak = 1.6 min (median) for everyone; the tPeak algorithm (Minto 2003, ref 4) then gives each patient their own ke0. Age-invariant tPeak 1.6 min.
- ke0 is one of the inputs that fix Vdpe (volume of distribution at the time of peak effect-site concentration), so the choice also changes loading-dose calculations.
- Tivatrainer's default Schnider model uses constant ke0; the letter says that differs from the Alaris "constant tPeak" implementation, so Barakat's Tivatrainer predictions for the Schnider model are wrong (the letter's claim).
- Pandit reply (same page): the Alaris implementation changes the delivered drug slightly; at the highest age in their group the induction dose differs by 0.5 mL between the two Schnider variants; effect site still equilibrates in under 2 min in the "true Schnider" simulation.
- Opioid audit: not an opioid paper; no MEAC, ke0 for an opioid or potency ratio.

## Covariates
Age is the covariate in play: under constant ke0 tPeak lengthens with age; under constant tPeak, ke0 rises with age to compensate (the direction follows from the letter's description; no numbers).

## Caveats
Opinion piece by the model's authors. The 0.456 min^-1 value carries no source table in the letter. "Median tPeak 1.6 min" came from visual inspection of EEG per the letter, not a formal estimate. Reply truncated in the archive.
