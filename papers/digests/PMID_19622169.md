# Abboud 2009: one-compartment epinephrine pharmacokinetics in adult septic shock

## Study
Prospective, 18-bed medical ICU, Hôpital Européen Georges Pompidou, Paris, Jan-Jun 2006. 38 consecutive adults with
septic shock, no catecholamine in the previous 24 h, no renal replacement, not pregnant; epinephrine the only
catecholamine, no hydrocortisone or activated protein C. Table 1 (PDF p3): age 64 ± 15 y, 25 men (65.7%), weight 68
± 19 kg, SAPS II 64 ± 23, ICU mortality 65.7%. Epinephrine (1:10 dilution) started at 0.15 µg/kg/min and titrated to
MAP 65-75 mmHg (PDF p2). Samples: C0 within 15 min before the infusion; C1 when the cumulative dose reached 0.15
mg/kg with the rate steady for 15 min (median 415 min, range 90-1260, after the start). 73 epinephrine
concentrations (3 C1 tubes lost). HPLC with coulometric detection, LOQ 0.10 nmol/L; C1 is endogenous plus exogenous.
NONMEM VI, FOCE, bootstrap (387 runs), NPDE.

## Model and parameters
One compartment, first-order elimination, with a baseline (endogenous) input rate R0; Michaelis-Menten elimination
did not improve the fit and gave no reliable Vmax or Km (linear kinetics, PDF p4). Basic model: CL 108 L/h (omega
0.44), V 9.1 L, R0 43.5 nmol/h (omega 1.21), half-life 3.5 min; residual error fixed at 10% and 0.1 nmol/L.
Covariates BW and SAPS II at ICU admission lowered the OFV by 20 and omega CL to 0.33. Plateau prediction:
Cplateau (nmol/L) = (infusion rate + R0) / CL.

Observed (Table 2, PDF p4, rendered): infusion at C1 2 (0.1-7) mg/h, 0.52 (0.026-1.7) µg/kg/min (abstract: 1.67),
10,090 (545-38,208) nmol/h; epinephrine 0.34 (0.10-4.3) nmol/L at C0 and 95.8 (4.40-540) at C1; norepinephrine fell
from 8.8 (0.99-56.7) to 4.5 (0.30-38.9) nmol/L. The text calls the range of rates "greater than 100-fold", but
Table 2's range is 70-fold in mg/h and 65-fold per kg (derived).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No concentration-effect model; no ke0 or tPeak. Hemodynamics C0 to C1 (Table 2): HR 109 to 117, SBP 80 to 116, DBP
38 to 56, MAP 52 to 76 mmHg. Baseline aldosterone, renin, cortisol, norepinephrine and fluid volume (median 4650 mL)
had no effect on CL.

## Covariates
CL = 127 x (BW/70)^0.60 x (SAPS II/50)^-0.67 L/h; none on V. Sex, age, fluid, hormones not retained.

## Caveats
One concentration per patient during infusion, at steady state, so V (and the approach to plateau) is poorly
determined; septic shock only; endogenous epinephrine adds to C1. Authors list the single infusion rate per patient
and restriction to septic shock (PDF p6).
