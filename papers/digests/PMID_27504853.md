# Heo 2016: valsartan and amlodipine population PK, and their blood-pressure interaction

## Study
- **Data for the combination (PDF p2, printed 1558):** 48 healthy volunteers from a Korean comparative PK study of two
  fixed-dose combination tablets (ref. 7, Kim 2013, whose title says healthy male Korean volunteers). Each subject
  took a single tablet of valsartan 160 mg with amlodipine 10 mg as the besylate, and the same with amlodipine
  orotate, in a crossover with a 14-day washout.
- **Sampling:** 0, 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 16, 24, 48, 72, 96, 144 and 192 h. Valsartan was measured to
  48 h, amlodipine to 192 h. Sitting SBP and DBP were taken at 0, 2, 4, 8, 12, 16 and 24 h; later readings were
  outpatient and were not used.
- **Observations (Results, PDF p4):** 816 amlodipine and 624 valsartan concentrations; 0.61% and 6.5% below the
  limit of quantitation. 336 SBP and DBP measurements, none missing. The assay and its LLOQ are not described.
- **Demographics (supplement Table 3 = Supplementary Table S3, mean +/- SD):** age 29 +/- 5.98 y; weight 68.7 +/-
  7.63 kg; AST 14.6 +/- 4.07 IU/L; ALT 12.8 +/- 6.20 IU/L; GGT 18.1 +/- 8.62 IU/L; creatinine 0.814 +/- 0.0873 mg/dL;
  smokers 20/28; drinkers 31/17. Sex is not given.
- **Monotherapy blood pressure (Methods, PDF p3):** mean SBP and DBP time courses to 24 h, taken from the literature
  as a naive pooled analysis. Valsartan: Czendlik 1997, 12 healthy volunteers given 160 mg, six time points.
  Amlodipine: Kim 2010, 24 volunteers given 10 mg besylate, 12 time points (supplement Table 1).
- **Software:** NONMEM 7.3, FOCE with interaction; PsN SCM, forward P < 0.05 and backward P < 0.001; VPC with 1000
  simulated datasets (Wings for NONMEM); 200 bootstrap replicates. PK and PD were fitted sequentially (PPP&D), with the
  PK parameters fixed.

## Model and parameters
- **Structure (PDF p4):** two compartments, first-order elimination, and **zero-order absorption of duration D1**
  for both drugs.
  - Amlodipine: OFV -387.497 for zero-order against -29.849 for first-order absorption.
  - Valsartan: OFV 7712.434 against 7874.858.
  - Both models include a CL-V1 correlation and a combined proportional and additive residual error.
- **Size (Equations 1-2, PDF p3):** theory-based allometry at WTSTD = 70 kg. (WT/70)^(3/4) multiplies CL and Q, and
  (WT/70)^1 multiplies V1 and V2. Nothing is said about D1.
- **Covariates screened:** age, serum creatinine, smoking and drinking; ALT, AST and GGT were screened for amlodipine
  only. Apart from body weight, none was significant (PDF p4).
- **No bioavailability term and no lag are reported.** The parameters are apparent ("/F").
- **Final estimates:** supplement Table 4, which the article cites as Supplementary Table S4. The values are typical
  values with %RSE. The footnote says the PPV values were calculated from the square root of the NONMEM OMEGA, so they
  are on the SD scale, not variances.

- **VPC (Figure S3, supplement):** observed and predicted median valsartan concentrations peak about 3.2 ug/mL at
  about 4 h, and the 95th percentile peaks about 7.7 ug/mL (read from the figure). The authors say both drugs' median
  peak concentrations were "somewhat underpredicted" (PDF p4).
- **Derived (mine):** at 70 kg a 160 mg dose over 4.39 h gives Cmax 3.42 mg/L at 4.39 h. AUC = 160/6.18 = 25.9
  mg·h/L. The disposition half-lives are 1.96 and 8.90 h.
- **Comparator (Discussion, PDF p8):** Lim 2007 reported valsartan CL/F 6.07 L/h, Q 2.02 L/h, Vc 13.8 L and Vp
  14.2 L. Earlier amlodipine CL/F values were 32-39 L/h.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- **End point:** sitting SBP and DBP; the drug effect is a fractional fall, BP = Baseline × (1 - PD(Ce)) (Eq. 3).
- **Models tested:** linear, log-linear, Imax and sigmoid Imax.
- **Selected model:** an effect compartment with an Imax model for each drug.
  - Valsartan: delayed against immediate response, ΔOFV -8.4.
  - Amlodipine: ΔOFV -11.5.
- **Valsartan monotherapy (Table 2, PDF p7, rendered; from Czendlik 1997's mean data):**
  - Baselines: SBP 126 (RSE 1.58%; bootstrap 119.6, 117-125); DBP 71.3 (2.52%; 67.52, 65.1-71.8).
  - Imax 0.164 (RSE **102%**; bootstrap 0.228, 0.0623-1).
  - IC50 1200 ng/mL (53.3%; 1823.05, 1020-4000).
  - Keq 0.542 /h (teq 1.3 h; 59.5%; 0.403, 0.1-0.777).
  - Residual SD: SBP 3.18 mmHg, DBP 0.41 mmHg.
  - Equations 11-12 apply one Imax and one IC50 to both SBP and DBP. Imax in pressure units is 20.7 mmHg SBP and
    11.7 mmHg DBP.
- **Amlodipine monotherapy (Table 1, PDF p6, rendered; from Kim 2010's mean data):**
  - Baselines: SBP 119; DBP 71.3.
  - Imax **0.164 FIXED** to valsartan's estimate, because the data were not informative.
  - IC50: SBP 8.27 ng/mL (32.2%); DBP 2.97 ng/mL (36.8%).
  - Keq: SBP 0.211 /h (teq 3.3 h; 38.3%); DBP 0.821 /h (teq 0.84 h; 104.7%).
  - The printed teq values equal ln2/Keq (derived).
- **Combination (Eq. 8; Table 3, PDF p8, rendered):** BP = Baseline × (1 - (PD(ACe) + PD(VCe)) - α × PD(ACe) ×
  PD(VCe)), as printed.
  - SBP: baseline 117 mmHg; α -0.171 (RSE 11.6%; bootstrap -0.178, -0.218 to -0.143); additive residual 7.84 mmHg.
  - DBP: baseline 72.8 mmHg; α -0.0312 (57.8%; -0.0336, -0.0774 to -0.00283); additive residual 4.46 mmHg.
  - Both are infra-additive. Imax and the remaining PD parameters were fixed from the monotherapy models.
  - The Table 3 PPV rows are labelled IC501, IC502 and AML/VAL Keq. Their values are not explained beyond the
    footnote.
- **Supplementary drug-interaction model (supplement p1):** BP = Baseline × (1 - Imax × ((UA+UV)/U50)^γ / (1 +
  ((UA+UV)/U50)^γ)), with θ = UV/(UV+UA) (OMML, decoded). This is a Minto-Schnider common-receptor model. The
  article (PDF p7) says it gave implausible estimates (Imax 0.03) and was dropped.
- **Validation against patients (Table 4, PDF p10):** relative SBP fall, patients against healthy predicted, was 12%
  against 9% (amlodipine) and 11% against 8% (valsartan). Relative DBP fall was 13% against 13% and 11% against 8%.
- **Supplement Tables 5-6:**
  - Amlodipine maximum fall: SBP 7.5 against 6.5 predicted; DBP 9.2 against 7.4.
  - Valsartan maximum fall: SBP 10.4 against 14.18 predicted; DBP 8.3 against 8.03.
- No therapeutic concentration range. Not an opioid.

## Covariates
Body weight only, by fixed allometry (Equations 1-2). Age, serum creatinine, smoking and drinking (and for
amlodipine ALT, AST and GGT) were screened and not retained. There is no renal function term. The population was 48
healthy young adults with a mean creatinine of 0.814 mg/dL.

## Caveats
- Healthy young volunteers, a single dose, and a fixed-dose combination tablet. The PK of each drug comes from
  combined dosing; the article cites ref. 4 for the absence of a clinically relevant PK interaction.
- The monotherapy PD rests on literature means from 12 (valsartan) and 24 (amlodipine) other subjects, as a naive
  pooled analysis. Valsartan's Imax has an RSE of 102%, and it was carried as amlodipine's fixed Imax.
- The bootstrap CI for amlodipine SBP Keq (0.1-0.211) ends at the point estimate. Several bootstrap medians differ
  from the estimates (valsartan IC50 1823 against 1200).
- D1's unit is printed "/h/70kg" in supplement Table 4 for both drugs. A duration in hours is the only reading
  consistent with "duration for zero order absorption" in the footnote.
- The text calls the selected PD model both Emax (Results) and Imax (tables, equations).
- The article cites the supplement's tables as S1-S7, but the supplement file numbers them Tables 1-7.
- No assay, LLOQ or valsartan bioavailability is given.
