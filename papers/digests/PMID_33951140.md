# Joosten 2021: closed-loop norepinephrine plus fluid decision support vs manual goal-directed therapy

## Study
Single-center (Bicetre, France), parallel two-arm randomized single-blind (patient-blinded) superiority trial, 38 adults (19 per arm) having elective intermediate/high-risk abdominal or orthopedic surgery (Oct 2019 - Jun 2020). Both arms: radial arterial line to an EV1000/Flotrac pulse-contour monitor; sufentanil 2 ug/kg + propofol 2 mg/kg induction, sevoflurane maintenance (BIS 40-60), atracurium. Goal: MAP within 10% of the preoperative baseline, with 100 mL mini-fluid challenges to maximize stroke volume index.
- Control: anesthesiologist titrated norepinephrine by hand.
- Intervention: a closed-loop hybrid PID/rules controller (Chemyx Fusion 100 syringe pump, MAP read from the EV1000) titrated norepinephrine, with the "assisted fluid management" decision-support system for fluid boluses.
- Primary outcome: % of case time with MAP < 90% of baseline. Powered on 12 +/- 8% control hypotension, 50% reduction, 80% power.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Not an opioid paper (no opioid audit entries). Reported outcomes (abstract, text p.262-263, Tables 3 and 4):
- Hypotension, % of case time: median 1.2 [0.4-2.0] computer vs 21.5 [14.5-31.8] manual (P < 0.001).
- MAP < 65 mmHg: 0.0 vs 1.9% (Table 4).
- Time within +/-10 mmHg of target: 97.2 vs 58.8%.
- Mean SV index 40.9 +/- 9.7 vs 33.5 +/- 7.5 mL/m2; mean cardiac index 3.2 +/- 0.6 vs 2.4 +/- 0.4 L/min/m2 (Table 4).
- Minor complications 42 vs 58% (P = 0.330).
- Text says total norepinephrine dose was >40% lower with the computer, and that the controller made >1,000 rate changes per case vs a median of 15 manually. The Table 2 values are scrambled in the text layer and are not recorded here.
No norepinephrine dose-response, EC50 or ke0 is reported.

## Covariates
None modelled. Baseline characteristics in Table 1 (age 63-64 yr, weight 70 vs 79 kg).

## Caveats
Authors list: principal investigator supervised the system, no blinding of providers, post-induction start so induction hypotension is unrecorded, higher blood loss in the computer arm, single center, n = 38. Authors declare commercial interests in closed-loop systems.
