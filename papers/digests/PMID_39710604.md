# Fromage 2025: multi-indication popPK of EC-MPS

## Study
Retrospective popPK and MAP Bayesian estimator for enteric-coated mycophenolate sodium (Myfortic) from the Limoges ISBA TDM platform. 153 profiles (863 concentrations) from 129 patients: 116 solid-organ or HSC transplant, 13 autoimmune disease; median age 45 y (6-80); EC-MPS 180-1440 mg per intake; 3-11 samples per profile (PDF p. 3-4). Split 75/25 by profile into development (112 occasions, 95 patients), validation, and a later external set (11 patients, 360-720 mg). Total MPA concentrations (mostly HPLC-MS/MS), LOQ 0.5 mg/L handled by M4. Monolix SAEM. Steady state assumed for all profiles (C0 = estimated trough). No weight, albumin or renal function available.

## Model and parameters
One compartment, first-order elimination, absorption by a double gamma distribution (fraction r fast, 1-r slow): C(t) = C0 + FAIV*D*[r*... + (1-r)*...] (PDF p. 3). Derived: mean absorption time MATi = ai/bi; V/F = 100/FAIV; CL/F = Dose/AUC. Table 2 (PDF p. 8, confirmed on the image; estimate with %RSE):

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD. Exposure target is the MPA AUC0-12 (not given in this paper as a number). LSS: 0.3, 2, 4 h post-dose; validation rMPE -2.67%, RMSE 13.55%, 12.5% of patients beyond ±20% (Table 3A, PDF p. 10); external rMPE -4.91%, RMSE 13.47%, 10% (Table 3B). Not an opioid; no MEAC or ke0.

## Covariates
Retained: treatment indication on b2 (renal/hepatic reference; cardiac, pulmonary, bone marrow; autoimmune), which reduced -2LL by 177 and IIV on b2 from 1.32 to 0.52. Tested and not retained: ciclosporin co-treatment (only 11% on it), analytic method, sex, age, time since transplant. Weight, albumin, creatinine clearance were not available.

## Caveats
Retrospective, steady state assumed, immunoassay vs LC-MS methods mixed, heterogeneous indications grouped for convergence (heart/lung/bone marrow combined, not physiologically justified), no heart or lung transplant in the validation or external sets, a1 and b1 far above the MMF literature range (a1 1280.97 versus up to 21.20), gamma absorption is empirical.

## Verification against stanpumpR
Not cited in code, so nothing is transcribed from this paper. Independent comparison with R/drugs_mycophenolate.R: that model is a two-compartment MMF model (CL 32.5 L/h woman reference at CLCR 48, albumin 30, ciclosporin 450 mg/day; V1 91 L, V2 237 L, ka 4.1/h, tlag 0.21 h, per mg MPA = 0.739 x MMF, lines 120-143). Fromage's apparent CL/F 4.99 L/h (about 6.5x lower) and V/F 12.60 L (far below 91 + 237 L) refer to a different formulation (EC-MPS), patients of whom only 11% took ciclosporin (the van Hest cohort all did), a one-compartment structure with a steady-state trough term, and an unstated dose basis, so the difference cannot be called a discrepancy in either model without those answered (see open questions). Absorption: Fromage's mean absorption times (about 3.1 h and 4.1 h in the reference group) are consistent with the header's remark (lines 54-56) that EC-MPS absorption is delayed; the library's lag 0.21 h would not represent it. Verdict: partly-verified.
