# Hui 1991: fosinoprilat kinetics across renal function

## Study
- Open-label; renally impaired patients got a randomised crossover of IV 14C-fosinoprilat 7.5 mg (over 90 s;
  equimolar to 10 mg fosinopril sodium) and oral 14C-fosinopril sodium 10 mg capsule, ≥9 days apart; patients with
  normal renal function got the IV dose only (PDF p2, printed 458).
- Groups by mean of two 24-h creatinine clearances (Table I, PDF p4, mean ± SEM): none 98.8 ± 10.6, mild
  59.6 ± 3.2, moderate 31.8 ± 2.0, severe 16.3 ± 2.1 mL/min/1.73 m²; ages 54 to 68 y; weights 77.0 to 80.9 kg.
  Groups n = 4 to 6 (Table IV footnote); one patient withdrawn, four partly excluded (p4). Fasted 8 h before to 4 h
  after dose.
- Sampling: plasma to 96 h (5, 15 min IV; 0.5 to 96 h), urine to 120 h, faeces 5 days.
- Assay: total radioactivity by scintillation; fosinoprilat by TLC radiochromatography (sensitivity 2 ng/mL);
  metabolites by HPLC (p3). Protein binding by ultrafiltration.
- Analysis: statistical moments; three-compartment fits (PC NONLIN, chosen by AIC) for IV fosinoprilat; elimination
  from the central compartment (p3).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Not an opioid paper. No ACE inhibition or concentration-effect model; only maximal mean falls in seated diastolic
BP in the first 24 h after IV fosinoprilat: 8, 13, 15, 9 mm Hg (none, mild, moderate, severe) (PDF p8). No ke0,
EC50 or potency ratio.

## Covariates
Renal function (group comparison only): CL lower in all impaired groups than in normal function, but not related to
creatinine clearance within 11 to 72 mL/min/1.73 m² (r = 0.23, Fig. 3). No covariate equations.

## Caveats
n = 4 to 6 per group; SEM not SD; normal group received no oral dose; parameters are means of individual fits, so
the half-lives, V_init, CL and Vss need not form one consistent model; radiolabel assay; one patient fitted poorly.
