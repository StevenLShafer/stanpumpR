# Joachim 2024: norepinephrine PK/PD on MAP after low-dose peripheral boluses under anaesthesia

## Study
- Lariboisiere Hospital, Paris; NCT03454204. Adults for elective neurosurgery needing vasopressor for intraoperative
  hypotension, no severe cardiovascular disease or shock. Propofol and remifentanil TCI to BIS 40-60, atracurium,
  ventilated (PDF p2).
- 34 included, 5 excluded, 29 analysed (PDF p5). Table 1 (PDF p3, rendered): female 20 (69%); age 52 [46-64] y; weight
  71 [62-84] kg; height 163 [158-173] cm; BMI 26 [22-29]; GFR 100 [88-128] mL/min; hypertension 12 (41%); smoking 8
  (28%); prior norepinephrine infusion 14 (48%); baseline NE 1.24 [0.59-1.36] nmol/L (patients without prior infusion).
- Dilute norepinephrine tartrate 5 ug/mL by syringe pump through a peripheral line: an initial 10 ug bolus, then
  boluses or infusions at the clinician's discretion (PDF p2). Median prepared concentration 5.35 [4.77-5.85] ug/mL.
  35 boluses, 82 infusions, 717 arterial plasma samples, 34 736 MAP values over 499 min (PDF p5). Concentrations
  converted to nmol/L to avoid base/tartrate confusion (PDF p3).
- Monolix 2021R1 (SAEM, MCMC individual estimates); BIC; SAMBA covariate search.

## Model and parameters
Two compartments plus a depot (the infusion line/mixing), lag, and a nonlinear depot outflow:
dAd/dt = -ka Ad^beta + I(t - Tlag); dAc/dt = ka Ad^beta - (k12 + kel) Ac + k21 Ap; dAp/dt = k12 Ac - k21 Ap;
Cc = Cendo + Ac/Vc (PDF p4, rendered). Units nmol, L, s. BIC 2313 (with depot) vs 2561 (1 compartment) and 2469
(2 compartments). Table 2 (PDF p4, rendered; the second column is labelled standard deviation):

**Supplement (sup0001.docx, read 2026-10-27).** Supplement 1: the infusate was diluted at the bedside from
commercial norepinephrine tartrate 8 mg/4 mL to a theoretical 4.76 ug/mL, and each solution was assayed. Supplement 2:
arterial EDTA microtubes, bar-code time-stamped, iced; HPLC with coulometric detection (Chromsystems kit) after
aluminium-hydroxide extraction. Supplement 3: syringe pumps logged by RS232 at 0.001 mL resolution; the amount given was
computed from the measured solution concentration and entered in nmol. Supplement 5: the model-selection BIC values
equal those printed in the article (2561/2469/2313; 18 716/19 229/18 714), with AIC, BICc and -2LL added. Its two
covariate tables give single beta coefficients by parameter (listed above) and are not the same as the article's
Table 3: different covariates on different parameters (e.g. height on Tlag and ka, LBM on V1, an S0 parameter not in
Table 2). Neither table is labelled with its method; the article does not reconcile them. No time to peak effect is
in the supplement.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Observed after a 10 ug bolus (PDF p5, rendered): MAP +24% [15-31] from 68 [63-73] mmHg; HR -8% [2-11] from 65; CO
-20% [15-28] from 4.2 L/min; SV -8%. Times to peak effect: MAP 74 [53-94] s, HR 74 [63-102] s, CO 61 [51-81] s.
During infusion, a plateau after a median 360 s with MAP +13%. End point: continuous arterial MAP (125 Hz). PD
model 1: sigmoid Emax on Cc (no effect compartment; the delay is carried by the lag and depot). Model 2: second-order
Windkessel driven by Cc, which captured the post-bolus undershoot seen in 12 patients. BIC 18 716 (Emax) vs 19 229
(Windkessel); mixture 18 714, P(Emax) 62.8%. No ke0. Not an opioid.

## Caveats
- Neurosurgical patients only; boluses and infusions at clinician discretion; ~half on norepinephrine before data
  capture.
- Recruitment dates differ: June 2018 to December 2021 (abstract, Methods) vs November 2019 to March 2021 (Results).
- "12 patients (35%)" with undershoot: 12/34 = 35%, 12/29 = 41% (derived).
- Figure 2 caption gives a 39 nmol bolus; 10 ug as base would be 59 nmol and as tartrate about 28-30 nmol (derived);
  the caption does not say which dose it is. Unresolved.
- The Discussion describes Oualha 2014 as neonates, which that paper's 2-85 kg children do not fully fit.
- (supplement, 2026-10-27) Nominal infusate 5 ug/mL in the article (PDF p2) against a theoretical 4.76 ug/mL in
  Supplement 1; the measured median 5.35 ug/mL (PDF p5) differs from both. The supplement says the delivered amount
  was taken from the measured concentration, which bears on (but does not settle) the Figure 2 caption's 39 nmol.
- (supplement, 2026-10-27) The supplement's covariate tables and the article's Table 3 report different covariate
  relations (see Model and parameters); which one belongs to the final model is not stated.
