# Pennell 2008: lamotrigine clearance, TDM and seizures in pregnancy

## Study
Prospective observational cohort (Emory Women's Epilepsy and Mental Health Programs, Dec 2002 to Mar 2006).
53 pregnancies in 53 women (39 epilepsy, 14 psychiatric); mean age 31.0 y (17-42); 71.7% white, 17.0% black
(Table 1, PDF p. 4 / printed 2133). Women taking drugs known to alter lamotrigine metabolism were excluded
(p. 2 / 2131). Visits every 1-3 months through pregnancy and the first postpartum year; 305 serum samples, with
the hours since the last dose recorded. Total and free (Centrifree YM-30 ultrafiltrate) lamotrigine by HPLC-UV
(Chromsystems); LOD 0.25 ug/mL, linear to 20 ug/mL; inter-assay CV 7.5-12.5% (p. 3 / 2132).
Apparent oral clearance = daily dose (mg/kg) / serum concentration (mg/L), for total and for free drug; compared
across nonpregnant baseline and trimesters (T1 < 14 wk, T2 14-28 wk, T3 29-42 wk) by a linear mixed model with
linear and quadratic terms for hours post dose. Baseline: 6 women had a preconception sample; for 19 more,
samples > 6 weeks postpartum in women not breastfeeding (p. 3-4). No weights reported.

## Model and parameters
No compartmental model. The quantitative result is Figure 2 (PDF p. 5 / 2134), mean (± SE) apparent clearance in
(mg/kg)/(mg/L), i.e. L/kg per day; values read from the bar chart are approximate:

| | Baseline | T1 | T2 | T3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Total CL (read from figure) | ~1.05 | ~1.27 | ~1.7 | ~2.0 |
| Free CL (read from figure) | ~2.7 | ~3.85 | ~4.8 | ~5.15 |

Stated in text: third-trimester total CL +94%, free CL +89% above nonpregnant baseline (abstract p. 1; Discussion
p. 5). All trimesters differ from baseline (p < 0.001); T2 and T3 differ from T1 (p < 0.03). Free CL higher in
white than black women (p = 0.031); not significant for total. Age no effect. Clearance returned to baseline
within the first postpartum month (Figure 3, p. 6 / 2135). Lamotrigine described as about 55% protein bound and
> 90% glucuronidated, mainly UGT1A4 (p. 2 / 2131).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No ke0, EC50 or concentration-effect model. End point: monthly seizure frequency relative to preconception
baseline (n = 36 with epilepsy). Ratio to target concentration (RTC = total concentration / individual
preconception target) below 0.65 in the second trimester predicted seizure worsening (ROC; at 0.78 sensitivity
100%, false-positive 25.0%). No association in T1 or T3. Over the whole pregnancy 39% had more seizures, 33% fewer,
28% no change (p. 4). Postpartum toxicity (dizziness, imbalance, blurred/double vision): 4/6 not following the
empiric taper vs 3/21 following it (p = 0.040). No population therapeutic range is given.

## Covariates
Trimester (categorical); race (free CL only); hours post dose (linear + quadratic, adjustment). Age not
significant. No equations published.

## Caveats
Apparent oral clearance from single, untimed-to-steady-state samples, per kg of an unstated weight. Baseline mixes
preconception (n = 6) and late postpartum (n = 19) samples; the remaining women have no baseline. Doses were
being changed under TDM. Mixed epilepsy and psychiatric population. Figure values are read off a chart.
