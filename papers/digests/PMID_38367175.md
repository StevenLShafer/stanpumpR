# Valke 2024: PK-PD of thrombin and plasmin generation after VWF/FVIII (Haemate P) in hemophilia A

## Study
External-validation (replication) study of the Bukkems 2022 population PK-PD model (Br J Clin Pharmacol 2022;88:2757-68). 29 adult men with severe hemophilia A (FVIII < 1 IU/dL) in Mashhad, Iran, 2011-2012; sub-study of IMPALA (NL2808). Age median 27 (19-53) y; weight mean 62 (SD 7) kg, median 62 (48-73) kg; baseline VWF 117% (SD 46%, Table 1; text says SD 43%, see Caveats); 1 patient NBA-positive (1.1 NBU/mL), 7 NLTIA-positive (0.04-0.05 NLTIU/mL). Single IV bolus of Haemate P, median 1600 IU (IQR 1500-1700) = 25 IU/kg (IQR 24.6-25.4), after 72 h wash-out. Plasma sampled pre-dose and at nine times to 24 h. FVIII activity by one-stage (OSA, 258 samples) and chromogenic (CSA, 258 per Table 1 as printed) assays; thrombin and plasmin generation by the Nijmegen hemostasis assay (NHA), normalised to normal pooled plasma (NPP). NONMEM 7.4, FO for the predictive-performance step. Pre-bolus FVIII was subtracted as endogenous baseline; the pre-bolus sample was also used as if drawn at 72 h.

Observed (medians, Fig. 3, text p.5): FVIII activity 52 IU/dL at 15 min, 46 at 1 h, 29 at 6 h, 7 at 24 h; FVIII half-life 10.6 h (IQR 8.3-12.9, Table 1). Thrombin peak height 142 nM at 15 min, 109 at 6 h, 62 at 24 h; thrombin potential 1823, 1886 and 1440 nM-min at 15 min, 6 h and 24 h; normalised thrombin potential still 71% of NPP at 24 h while FVIII was 7 IU/dL.

## Model and parameters
PK: the Bukkems model over-predicted FVIII (MPE 60.9%, MAPE 61.3%), so a new two-compartment model was fitted: IIV on CL and V1, combined proportional + additive error, CSA read 0.939 times OSA, positive NBA raised CL by 153% (NLTIA had no effect; VWF not measured after dosing, so not tested). ## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End points are NHA parameters as % of NPP against FVIII activity (IU/dL); not an opioid paper, so no MEAC/ke0 audit applies. There is no effect-site delay (no ke0, tPeak or hysteresis) reported: effect is modelled directly on plasma FVIII, and the paper notes thrombin generation outlasts FVIII activity (Tmax of thrombin peak identical to FVIII; potential Tmax 9 h).

Sigmoid-Emax forms (Table 3, PDF p.11). Peak height: E = Ebase x (1 + Emax Cn/(EC50n + Cn)); potential: E = Ebase + (1 + ...) as printed; plasmin: E = Ebase x (1 - ...). External-dataset re-estimates vs Bukkems:

## Covariates
PK: positive NBA on CL (+153%). Age and NLTIA tested, NLTIA not kept; VWF could not be tested. PD: age and body weight tested, none significant. IIV was kept on only one PD parameter per model.

## Caveats
Samples frozen in Iran and shipped on dry ice; 3-h samples lower than expected in some; only 24 h of sampling; no bleeding-rate data; VWF not measured post-dose; all men. Table 1 as printed gives 258 samples for both OSA and CSA and 287 PD samples, while text gives 285 PD values and Table 1 the SD of VWF as 46 against 43 in text (a text/table inconsistency within the paper). The paper's comparative statements ("higher clearance", "larger volume") are against the Bukkems model, not against any other model.
