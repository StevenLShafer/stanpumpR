# Vernillet 2020: cenobamate single and multiple ascending doses in healthy subjects

## Study
Four phase 1, single-centre, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled studies, sponsor-run (SK Life Science),
210 healthy adults 18-55 years, capsule formulation (abstract; Methods, PDF p2-3).
- SAD: 110 men (77 cenobamate, 33 placebo), 11 cohorts of 7 active + 3 placebo, single fasted doses 5, 10, 25, 50,
  100, 200, 300, 400, 500, 600, 750 mg. Mean age 35.5 years, weight 81.6 kg (SD 9.6), BMI 26.4; race 51.9% Black,
  29.9% Caucasian, 14.3% Hispanic among active subjects (Table 1A, PDF p4, rendered). Sampling to 72 h (5-25 mg), 96 h
  (50 mg) or 144 h (100-750 mg).
- MAD 1: 50 subjects; 50, 100, 150 mg/day (men), 150 mg/day (women), 200 mg/day, 14 days.
- MAD 2: 20 subjects; 250 or 300 mg/day for 17 days.
- MAD 3: 30 subjects; 400, 500, 600 mg/day after titration, 15 days at target (total 29, 30, 35 days); 600 mg/day
  stopped on day 29 for CNS adverse events.
Assay LC-MS/MS, ranges 0.020-25.0, 0.020-10.0, 0.20-10.0 or 0.50-40.0 ug/mL by study (PDF p3). Noncompartmental
analysis (Phoenix WinNonlin 6.3); dose proportionality by ANOVA and power model (R 3.3.3).

Other Table 3 geometric Cmax (ug/mL): 5.10, 11.3, 18.4, 18.7, 23.9, 30.6, 38.4, 45.5, 59.2 for the nine rows; median
tmax 2.0-3.5 h. Derived steady-state CL/F = dose/AUCtau: 0.52, 0.43, 0.41, 0.42, 0.41, 0.45, 0.40, 0.46, 0.45 L/h.

Text: single-dose AUC0-inf rose more than dose-proportionally (power model failed over 5-750 and 100-400 mg), Cmax
dose-proportional; multiple-dose AUCtau and Cmax dose-proportional 50-500 mg/day (adjusted reference 0.90309-1.0969)
(PDF p5, p7). Steady state by day 13 (MAD 1, 2) and day 11 (MAD 3). The Results text gives CL/F falling from about
1.4 to 0.40 L/h with dose (PDF p5), the abstract says 1.4 to 0.50 L/h; Table 2's lowest mean is 0.396 at 750 mg, so
the abstract's 0.50 disagrees with its own Results and table. The authors' hypotheses for the single-dose
nonlinearity (transporter saturation or a low-capacity binding/trapping system) are untested (PDF p13). No
compartmental V or ka reported.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. Safety only (TEAEs, mostly CNS; DRESS in one MAD 3 subject at 500 mg/day; 600 mg/day not tolerated). No
concentration-effect data, no ke0.

## Covariates
Sex: exploratory, no difference at 150 mg/day (Table 3; Supplemental Table 5 for day 1, not archived). No other
covariates analysed.

## Caveats
Healthy, mostly male volunteers, about half Black in the SAD study; small cohorts (n = 6-7 active per dose);
capsules, not the marketed tablet. Supplemental Tables 3-5 (dose-proportionality statistics, day-1 PK by sex) are not
in the archived file. MAD 3 subjects reached the target dose after titration, so their day-14-at-target values follow
about 29-30 days of dosing.
