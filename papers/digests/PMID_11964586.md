# Struys 2002: ARX auditory evoked potential index (AAI) vs BIS during propofol

## Study
- 20 female ASA I patients, 18-60 yr, ambulatory gynaecologic surgery; weight 63 +/- 6 kg, age 32 +/- 5 yr, height 167 +/- 10 cm (mean +/- SD, Results, PDF p6; text layer). Weight 70-130% of ideal.
- Propofol by effect-site-controlled infusion (RUGLOOP) using the Schnider 1998/1999 three-compartment model plus effect site tuned to **time to peak effect 1.6 min** after bolus. Target Ce started at 1.5 µg/ml and raised 0.5 µg/ml every 4 min until loss of all clinical signs. No premedication, no other drugs, spontaneous ventilation by mask.
- At each step (end of 4 min) recorded BIS (A-2000, v3.4), AAI (A-Line), HR, BP, modified OAA/S, eyelash reflex, then response to a tetanic stimulus (100 Hz, 50 mA, 2 s, forearm). Analysis: Spearman, prediction probability (PK), probit ED50/ED95, sensitivity/specificity tables. A 15-patient interference study found no effect of the A-Line clicks on BIS.

Other: BIS ED50 for the same end points 85/74/66/71/41 (ED95 80/58/52/54/20); AAI ED50 63/39/28/30/16 (ED95 40/20/19/11/4). Spearman correlation with Ce propofol: BIS -0.905, AAI -0.794, HR -0.144, SBP -0.477, MAP -0.484, DBP -0.497 (Table 2). Prediction probability for OAA/S (Table 3): BIS 0.93, AAI 0.89, Ce propofol 0.91 (row mapping from the text layer is scrambled; confirm on the page). Median Ce 1.5 to 4 µg/ml as OAA/S fell 5 to 0 (Results). Sensitivity/specificity cut-offs: 100% sensitivity for unconsciousness needs BIS just below 55, AAI just below 20 or Ce just above 3.5 µg/ml (Results; Tables 5-7).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End points: OAA/S (levels 5, 4, 3; LOC = 3 to 2), loss of eyelash reflex, loss of movement to tetanic stimulus, all in the presence of propofol alone (no opioid). BIS, AAI and Ce all discriminated sedation and LOC well; none predicted response to noxious stimulus (Table 3, PK 0.5-0.9 with large SE).
- ke0/tPeak: tPeak 1.6 min for the propofol effect site (Schnider), clinically supported by Struys 2000 (ref 17). No ke0 value printed; no hysteresis fitted. Equilibration assumed after 4 min per step.
- No opioid in this study: no MEAC, no potency ratio. (Opioid audit: none.)
- After the stimulus, BIS and AAI rose: maximum mean increase 8.2 (SD 5.0) and 15.4 (SD 11.1); time to peak 39 s (SD 12) for BIS and 20 s (SD 15) for AAI.

## Covariates
None analysed (single homogeneous female group).

## Caveats
Authors: observer not blinded to index values; PK statistic assumes independence of repeated measures; steady-state, single-drug conditions only; hemodynamics only recorded before the stimulus. Added: 20 young healthy women, 4-min steps may not reach complete effect-site equilibrium; Ce is model-calculated, not measured.
