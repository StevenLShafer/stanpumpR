# Heyse 2014: sevoflurane-remifentanil response surface on BIS, entropy, CVI, SPI

## Study
- 40 ASA I patients (26 F, 14 M), age 30 +/- 11 y, 66 +/- 11 kg, 172 +/- 8 cm; weight 70-130% of ideal (PDF p2, 5). Secondary analysis of Heyse 2012.
- Crisscross design (4 concentration pairs per patient, 160 pairs, 159 periods analysed): remifentanil 0-12 ng/mL effect site by TCI (Minto 1997 PK-PD, RUGLOOP II), sevoflurane 0.5-3.5 vol% end-tidal. Effects read 12 min after reaching target, median of the minute before OAA/S, and after laryngoscopy (moving median, highest value). No muscle relaxant, no premedication.
- NONMEM 7.2.0 FOCE; 2000-sample bootstrap. Negative sigmoid Emax; Greco, reduced Greco, Minto and hierarchical interaction models (p3-4).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End points: BIS, state entropy, response entropy, CVI, SPI (not opioid analgesia as such). Remifentanil has a weak EEG effect: C50remi is about 10-fold higher than for dichotomous responsiveness endpoints in Heyse 2012 (PDF p8).
- Remifentanil C50 for NSRI (laryngoscopy suppression, Luginbuhl 2010) quoted as 1.16 ng/mL versus 7.5-27.3 ng/mL here (PDF p8, a comparison, not a result of this study).
- Not defined: no MEAC / minimum analgesic concentration; no ke0, t1/2ke0, tPeak or hysteresis estimated (effect site taken from Minto 1997 and assumed equilibrated 12 min after target); no equianalgesic ratio. Concentration is predicted effect site, not measured; no assay.
- Potency: 1.5 vol% sevoflurane + 5 ng/mL remifentanil gives U = 0.94 (BIS), 1.13 (SE), 1.07 (RE), 2.29 (CVI) (p8).

## Covariates
None tested.

## Caveats
Young healthy ASA I; remifentanil C50 for BIS extrapolated beyond the data range (12 ng/mL max); CVI fit poor (residual 27%, 46% after stimulation); stimulation sequence may carry over; end-tidal sevoflurane, not effect site.
