# Hoymork 2003: TCI propofol/remifentanil, measured concentrations and BIS

## Study
Open study, 21 analysed of 22 enrolled (one woman excluded for surgery > 200 min), ASA I-II, age 18-55, normal weight, laparoscopic cholecystectomy. Table 1 (PDF p2, printed 774; mean (range) age, mean (SD) otherwise): men n = 7, age 41.3 (25-50), weight 81.0 (7.8) kg, BMI 24.1 (2.6); women n = 14, age 36.5 (23-53), weight 68.0 (12.3) kg, BMI 24.5 (3.5). Surgery mean 67 min (41-125).
Dosing: remifentanil by a prototype TCI (Remifusor) using the Minto 1997 parameters, blood target 7.5 ng/mL at induction, kept at 7.5 ng/mL (stated as roughly 0.30 µg/kg/min) until gall bladder removal, then 7.5 or 5.0 ng/mL (5.0 in nine patients). Propofol by Diprifusor TCI, target 5 µg/mL at induction, then 2.5 µg/mL after intubation and titrated to BIS 45-60 (steps down to 1.5-1.6, up to 4.0 µg/mL). Arterial samples: first 5 min after start (targets 7.5 ng/mL and 5 µg/mL), before and 5 min after each propofol target change, after gall bladder removal, and at emergence.
Assay: remifentanil in whole blood, LC/MS/MS, LOQ 0.1 ng/mL, CV < 15% over 0.1-40 ng/mL, citric-acid stabilised. Propofol in plasma, HPLC-fluorescence, LOQ 2 ng/mL, CV < 5.5%. (Paper says "serum" in text; the methods say plasma for propofol.) BIS: Aspect A-1050 v1.21 (algorithm 3.3), delay about 45 s.

## Model and parameters
Not a model-building study. It evaluates the predictive performance of the Diprifusor (Marsh-type propofol set) and of Minto's remifentanil set in a TCI pump. MDPE = median of 100 x (Cmeasured - Cestimated)/Cestimated, so positive means the algorithm UNDERpredicts. PDF p4 (printed 776, signs read on the page image):

Measured range at a 2.5 µg/mL propofol target 2.2-8.1 µg/mL; at a 7.5 ng/mL remifentanil target 3.3-13.9 ng/mL (Discussion, PDF p6). Total drug: propofol 653 (253) mg, remifentanil 1.64 (0.58) mg. No individual PK parameters estimated.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: BIS (not analgesia, not EEG-derived ke0). No EC50, gamma, ke0, t1/2ke0 or tPeak reported.
- Measured propofol (plasma) vs BIS during surgery: inverse regression r2 = 0.13, slope -1.4 BIS per µg/mL (SD 0.38), P < 0.001; restricted to BIS 45-60 r2 = 0.01, slope -0.15, P = 0.66 (PDF p4-p5). BIS did separate awake (above 70) from asleep (below 65) states.
- No remifentanil MEAC or analgesic concentration: 7.5 ng/mL was a fixed anaesthetic-maintenance target adjusted for haemodynamics (bradycardia, hypotension, hypertension), not a defined minimum effective concentration.
- Emergence (response to verbal command) 8.3 (4.1) min, women 6.6 (3.5) vs men 11.6 (3.2) min (P < 0.01); no gender difference in measured concentrations at emergence; BIS at emergence 94.5 (4.3) women vs 89.1 (7.8) men (PDF p5). Emergence concentrations are given only in figures, not tabulated in the text.
- Potency/equianalgesic ratios: none.

## Covariates
Gender examined for emergence time only; no covariate model.

## Caveats
n = 21, only 7 men; open study with BIS-guided adjustment of the propofol target, so repeated measures per patient are pooled in the regression. Concurrent remifentanil may reduce propofol clearance (authors' speculation, citing other work). Fixed arterial sampling compared with plasma-site TCI predictions, not effect-site. A remifentanil target of 7.5 ng/mL is high compared with typical analgesic needs.

No citing location, so nothing to verify against a citation. Independent comparison with the library:
- Propofol (R/drugs_propofol.R): the library model is Eleveld 2018 (header, line 277); the Diprifusor/Marsh set evaluated here is not the library model, so the +60% MDPE (measured above Diprifusor prediction) says Marsh underpredicts in this setting, not that the library model does. Cannot check the library model against these samples.
- Remifentanil (R/drugs_remifentanil.R): the library default is Eleveld 2017 (Kim 2017 for obese adults), with Minto 1997 as the companion model in R/alternativeModels.R. The −7% Minto MDPE here (measured below predicted, in whole blood) supports Minto being roughly unbiased in this setting; it says nothing about Eleveld. Library MEAC is 1 ng/mL (R/drugs_remifentanil.R:117) and tPeak 1.6 min (line 116); the paper offers neither.
