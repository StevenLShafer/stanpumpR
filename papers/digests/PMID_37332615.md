# Bauer 2023: PK/PD of recombinant vs plasma-derived VWF in type 3 VWD

## Study
Population PK/PD (NONMEM 7.3.0/7.4.0, R 3.5.3) of VWF:ristocetin-cofactor activity (VWF:RCo) and its effect on endogenous FVIII activity (FVIII:C), after IV recombinant VWF (rVWF, vonicog alfa) or plasma-derived VWF/FVIII (pdVWF/FVIII, Humate-P, VWF:RCo/FVIII:C 2.4:1). Funded by the manufacturer (two authors employed by it).
- rVWF model: 79 patients, 1664 VWF:RCo samples from four studies (NCT00816660 phase 1, NCT01410227 and NCT02283268 phase 3, EudraCT 2011-004314-42 phase 1 in severe haemophilia A); doses 2-80 IU/kg VWF:RCo; types 1 (5), 2 (7), 3 (57), haemophilia A (10). Median age 35 y (18-70), weight 74 kg (43.8-145), haematocrit 0.417 (Table 2, PDF p3). FVIII PK/PD: only the two phase 3 studies (41 patients, 686 samples), because phase 1 patients received exogenous FVIII (p2-3, Table 1).
- pdVWF/FVIII model: 20 type 3 patients, 281 samples, single 50 IU/kg VWF:RCo dose in a crossover with rVWF (study NCT00816660), sampled to 96 h; median age 30 y, weight 66.3 kg (43.8-132), haematocrit 0.406 (Table 3, p4). 49 VWF:RCo samples were below the LLOQ (1 IU/dL).
- Excluded: 7 patients with VWF/FVIII antibodies, bleeding-event samples, samples after plasma-derived co-treatment. FVIII by one-stage clotting assay. VWF:RCo LLOQ 1 IU/dL (phase 1) and 8 IU/dL (others).

## Model and parameters
VWF:RCo: two-compartment, first-order elimination, with an endogenous baseline; allometric weight (fixed), haematocrit on Vc. FVIII: indirect-response model in which VWF:RCo inhibits FVIII elimination (kout); pdVWF/FVIII model adds a FVIII volume term because the product contains FVIII. Table 4 (PDF p8, printed p406), typical values; RSE in parentheses; IIV and RUV as CV or SD scale fractions as printed.

Derived by the paper (p403-405): half-life and MRT of VWF:RCo are 1.74x and 1.76x longer for rVWF than pdVWF/FVIII (abstract says ~1.75); AUC0-inf 1.97x greater; FVIII t½ ratio rVWF:pdVWF 2.4-3.8 over a 72 h interval at steady state (Fig 5).
Simulations (typical 75 kg, haematocrit 0.4): 50 IU/kg every 72 h of rVWF keeps FVIII:C above 40 IU/dL for the whole interval; at steady state median FVIII:C of type 3 patients is 40-100 IU/dL, and at least 80% / 97.5% stay below 150 / 250 IU/dL peak (p403, Fig 2, Fig 4).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Not an opioid. The PD end point is FVIII:C (IU/dL) via inhibition of FVIII elimination: Imax 0.998 (rVWF), 0.994 (pdVWF/FVIII); IC50 0.0658 and 0.0577 IU/dL of VWF:RCo (Table 4). No ke0, MEAC or potency ratio; the effect is modelled by indirect response (kout 15.9 1/h), not an effect-site delay.

## Covariates
Body weight (allometric, exponent 0.75 on CL and Q, 1.0 on volumes; fixed), haematocrit on Vc and on baseline FVIII. No formal covariate analysis was possible for pdVWF/FVIII (n=20); covariates were carried over from the rVWF model (p408). Once endogenous VWF:RCo was accounted for, VWD type and haemophilia A did not change rVWF PK (p403).

## Caveats
Single plasma-derived product (Humate-P); small pdVWF dataset (IIV CL RSE 38%); in silico comparison; type 3 patients' simulated profiles; manufacturer-funded; supplement not read.
