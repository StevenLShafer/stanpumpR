# Escher 2007: PK and antinociception after 0.002 mg/kg IV buprenorphine

## Study
- Randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled, three-period crossover (part of a naltrexone-reversal study): (1) buprenorphine then naltrexone 50 mg PO at 45 min, (2) placebo + naltrexone, (3) buprenorphine + placebo. Washout ≥ 2 weeks.
- 12 healthy white men, 21-34 y (mean 26, SD 3.5), 58-86 kg (mean 67, SD 9); no opiate history.
- Buprenorphine 0.002 mg/kg IV (0.15 mg/70 kg; Temgesic) in 1 mL over 1 min.
- Plasma at 0.5, 1, 1.5, 1.75, 2, 2.5, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8 h; LC-MS/MS, LLOQ 0.05 ng/mL (range 0.05-50 ng/mL, 0.25 mL plasma).
- PD before dosing and at 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8 h: sedation 0-10, DSST, thermal QST, nociceptive flexion RIII reflex (sural nerve; objective threshold) with subjective threshold, cold pressor test (cutoff 2 min). PK by WinNonlin 4.1 compartmental; Wilcoxon tests.
- It is not stated in the text which arms' data (with or without naltrexone) the PK/PD summaries come from.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- RIII reflex (objective) threshold: baseline 31.6 (9.5) mA; max increase +14.1 (17.5) mA (53.7% of baseline), P = 0.02.
- Subjective electrical pain threshold: baseline 45.5 (22.3) mA; max +24.2 (21.7) mA (74.7%), P = 0.02.
- **Both thresholds peaked at 120 min** and stayed elevated > 4 h.
- **Cold pressor pain tolerance peaked at 30 min** (latency 69 (10) s, +63.8%), lasting about 2 h.
- DSST max decrease at 60 min (41 → 35 symbols, 14.5%); sedation peak at 120 min (2.9 → 6.4).
- Authors: onset of effects during the distribution phase; prolonged effects attributed to high mu affinity and possibly brain distribution.
- **No ke0, t½ke0, or modelled tPeak**; peak times read directly from the data. **No MEAC** or analgesic concentration. **No potency ratio** (intro cites usual SL 0.2-0.8 mg, parenteral 0.3 mg; SL bioavailability 30-50%; Ki 0.31 nM; in vitro dissociation t½ 5.6 and 166.4 min).
- AEs: nausea 9/12, lightheadedness 8/12, drowsiness 7/12, vomiting 5/12.

## Covariates
None studied. Men only, young.

## Caveats
- n = 12 men, single low dose; sparse early sampling (first sample 30 min), so the fast distribution phase was missed (authors' explanation for 2 vs 3 compartments).
- Table I units/magnitudes look erroneous; Cmax 207 ng/mL at 0.15 mg is implausible for a first sample at 30 min.
- Concurrent naltrexone arms complicate interpretation; dose was weight-adjusted (mean 67 kg → about 0.134 mg, derived).
