# van Iersel 2018: population PK of posaconazole delayed-release tablet

## Study
Six studies pooled: five phase 1 (healthy volunteers, n = 16, 25, 21, 23, 19; 104 total) and one phase 3 (n = 231 patients at high risk for invasive fungal disease: AML/MDS and allogeneic HSCT recipients with GVHD). 335 subjects, 5,756 concentrations (5 outlier samples with CWRES > 6 excluded during development, reinstated in the final model). Tablet formulations A-D (D = intended commercial), delayed-release with a pH-sensitive polymer. Doses 100, 200, 300, 400 mg, single and multiple (Table 1); sampling in Table 3 (rich in phase 1, rich/sparse in phase 3). NONMEM VII, FOCE, log-transformed both-sides, additive residual error. Assay not described; presumably total plasma concentration (binding not discussed).

## Model and parameters
One-compartment, sequential zero-order then first-order absorption (duration D1, rate ka), first-order elimination. Table 2 (PDF p4, printed p4; confirmed on the rendered page), final model with all samples; ka is printed "liter/h" in the table but is a rate (1/h).

Base-model shrinkage (Table 2): CL 12.4, ka 62.4, D1 65.9, F1 23.5, residual 9.34. Base-model IIV for D1 is 40.0 (final column blank). Bootstrap (500, stratified) was stable (<5% not converged; Fig. S3 is in the supplement, not read). Model underpredicts Cmax versus NCA, more after single doses.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD model, no ke0. Exposure target > 0.5 mg/L (500 ng/mL) for Cmin or steady-state Cavg, the MIC90 for the most important Aspergillus species (ref 17) and the target used to choose the IV dose; also bands < 0.5, 0.5 to < 2.5 and >= 2.5 mg/L. Infections were most frequent below the first Cavg quartile (cited from the label). A cited retrospective study used > 0.7 mg/L as a stricter target. Total plasma concentration in patients at high risk (AML/MDS, HSCT). Not an opioid paper.

## Covariates
Retained: AML/MDS on F1 (-0.165), body weight on F1 (-1.03), tablet formulation A/B vs C/D on F1 (0.247), food on ka (0.530), single vs multiple dose on CL (0.750). Race and others were tested; race was forward-significant but dropped in backward exclusion. Simulation (300 mg bid day 1, then daily to day 28): Cmin > 0.5 mg/L in 94% of AML/MDS and 96.4% of HSCT; Cavg >= 0.5 in 98.6% and 99.4%; with median weight 172.4 kg, 66.1% of AML/MDS had Cavg > 0.5. Body weight is the only covariate the authors consider clinically relevant.

## Caveats
Population mostly white and AML/HSCT; VPC shows phase 1 variability overestimated; high shrinkage on ka and D1 (62-66%); Cmax underpredicted; oral apparent parameters (F unknown); all authors are Merck employees.
