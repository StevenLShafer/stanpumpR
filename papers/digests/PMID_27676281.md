# Dexter 2017: statistical reporting checklists and statistical editing

## Study
Narrative review of published evaluations of editor instructions, reporting checklists (SQUIRE, STROBE) and statistical review, found by PubMed and Google Scholar searches to 29 March 2016, plus the authors' experience at Anesthesia & Analgesia 2006-2016. Not a pharmacology paper.

## Caveats
Reported findings (PDF pp1-5): error-bar use in Memory & Cognition rose from about 10% to about 40% under an editor's instruction, then fell to 21% by 2000; SQUIRE items reported 40% (SD 10%) before vs 41% (SD 14%) after (Howell 2015); STROBE score 48% to 58% with no effect of publication (Bastuji-Garin 2013); basic statistics frequently missing (e.g. sample size calculations in 10 of 137 papers, 7%); 100% statistical review judged necessary. Authors list three recommendations: designated statistical reviewers, 100% statistical review, checklists are no substitute. Limitation: reviewed journals were mostly outside anaesthesia.
