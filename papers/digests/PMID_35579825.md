# Bukkems 2022: doravirine in pregnancy, placenta perfusion plus PBPK

## Study
- Ex vivo dual-side perfused human term cotyledons, closed-closed circuit, n = 8 placentas (4 maternal-to-fetal, MTF; 4 fetal-to-maternal, FTM); 7 of 8 from caesarean section (p. 4, PDF p. 6 for design/results).
- Perfusion flows: maternal 12 mL/min, fetal 6 mL/min; albumin 29 g/L (maternal buffer), 32 g/L (fetal buffer); reservoirs 200 mL each. Doravirine 0.96 mg/L added to one circulation (chosen to mimic Cmax in nonpregnant adults with HIV); 180 min sampling; antipyrine 100 mg/L as overlap control (accepted if ratio > 0.75). Total and unbound concentrations measured.
- A mechanistic 3-compartment placenta model (plus reservoirs) fitted in NONMEM 7.4 (FOCE-I) gave intrinsic transfer clearances; those were fed into the Simcyp v20 permeability-limited placenta / full-body maternal-fetal PBPK model.
- Simulations at 26, 32 and 40 weeks of pregnancy, n = 100 virtual subjects each, vs matched nonpregnant women; doravirine 100 mg once daily (QD) and 100 mg twice daily (BID), steady state.
- Closed-open perfusion (3 MTF, 3 FTM) done as a sensitivity comparison (ESM, not in the PDF).

## Model and parameters
Perfusion results (text, PDF p. 6-7): at 180 min after maternal dosing, median total concentrations maternal 0.34 mg/L (0.30-0.39), fetal 0.28 mg/L (0.24-0.32), mean FTM concentration ratio 0.82. After fetal dosing, maternal 0.28 (0.24-0.29), fetal 0.42 (0.40-0.56); mean MTF ratio 0.61. Antipyrine FTM ratio 0.89 (Discussion). Ex vivo free fraction 52.9%. Recovery 46% (MTF) and 53% (FTM); placental tissue 0.76 ug/g (0.53-1.07), about 15% of the dose.

Check of the conversion (derived): 37.2 mL/min x 60 / 1000 / 44.02 mL = 0.0507 L/h/mL; 5.5 x 0.06 / 44.02 = 0.0075. Both agree with the paper. Closed-open fit (ESM): CLpdm 11.0 mL/min (5.6-22.7), CLpdf 4.3 mL/min (2.3-8.3), quoted in the text on PDF p. 7.

Maternal PBPK parameters for doravirine (the nonpregnant three-compartment model, FDA/Yee) are in ESM 10, not in this PDF. Stated in the text: log P 3.0, plasma protein binding 76%, CYP3A4 main clearance, minor renal; unbound fraction in plasma 27% nonpregnant, 31% at 40 weeks.

Simulation results (text, PDF p. 7-8, no table): maternal geometric-mean AUC0-24 down 55% at 40 weeks vs nonpregnant. 100 mg QD GM total Ctrough 0.10, 0.07, 0.05 mg/L at 26, 32, 40 weeks (decreases 65%, 75%, 84%). 100 mg BID: 0.46, 0.37, 0.28 mg/L. Fetal predicted mean Ctrough below 0.23 mg/L on QD; about 0.23 mg/L on BID; fetal concentrations above 0.23 mg/L until about 8 h post-dose. Predicted placental-tissue Cmax almost 2x maternal plasma Cmax. Sensitivity: only a 10-fold lower CLpdf, CLpdm or FUp changed fetal Cmax (about 20% lower); maternal curves unchanged.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD model. The only efficacy reference is a target total trough of 0.23 mg/L: the 10th percentile Ctrough in a population PK study of phase IIb/III data, associated with lower clinical efficacy (virologic) (ref 33, Yee 2019; the authors call the relationship flat and possibly driven by non-adherence). Total plasma concentration, not unbound. Not an opioid; no MEAC, ke0, or potency ratio.

## Covariates
Pregnancy physiology only (gestational age 26, 32, 40 weeks, built into the Simcyp pregnancy population). The perfusion model scales placental volumes to individual cotyledon weight. No patient covariates were fitted.

## Caveats
- Transfer parameters come from term placentas only; none at 26 or 32 weeks. Variability of mechanistic inputs set pragmatically to 30% CV, so only mean PK is reported.
- Fetal clearance not modelled (fetal CYP3A4 assumed negligible).
- 54% (MTF) and 47% (FTM) of the dose was not recovered, only about 25% of that loss explained by tissue uptake.
- Active P-gp transport could not be separated from diffusion. IIV fixed at 100% (the table's IIV rows say "fixed"). Funding: Merck Sharp & Dohme paid for the perfusion experiments.
- The comparative claim "decrease" in pregnancy is against matched nonpregnant simulations in the same model, not observed data.
