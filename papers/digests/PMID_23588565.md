# Kim 2013: population PK of cyclophosphamide with genetic effects in HSCT

## Study
21 adults (14 M, 7 F; median age 32, range 18-50; median weight 56.8 kg, range 47.3-84.5; height 171.2 cm) with
haematological disease (diagnosis counts in Table 1, PDF p. 4) receiving allogeneic HSCT at Seoul National University
Hospital 2009-2011. Conditioning: busulfan 0.8 mg/kg IV over 2 h every 6 h on days -8 to -5, then cyclophosphamide
(CY) 60 mg/kg IV over 1 h on 2 consecutive days. Diphenylhydantoin (phenytoin) was given during busulfan for seizure
prophylaxis (dose not stated); mesna 4 x 1,000 mg. CY doses 2,555-4,800 mg (PDF p. 5). Plasma CY and 4-hydroxy-CY (HCY)
by LC-MS/MS, after semicarbazide derivatisation; sampling before and at 0.5, 1.5, 2.5, 5, 7, 9, 24 h or 1, 3, 6, 24 h
from the start of infusion, plus 48 h in 15 patients. 74 CY and 66 HCY concentrations. NONMEM 6.1, FOCE-I, ADVAN6.
Genotyped CYP2B6, CYP2C19, CYP3A5, GSTA1/M1/P1/T1, ABCB1, ABCC2. Only 3 patients were ABCC2 1249 GA.

## Model and parameters

CY concentration, first-order enzyme degradation Kenz); HCY as a metabolite compartment. Final estimates, Table 2
(PDF p. 6, printed 1548):

Equations (PDF p. 5): CL_UI = 15.5 x 0.492^ABCC2 (ABCC2 = 1 for GA, 0 for GG); CL_I = 0.683 x ((AST + 0.00001)/25)^-0.0488.
Observed means (text p. 5): CL_UI 17.3 +/- 8.87 L/h (GG) vs 8.45 +/- 3.22 (GA). Fig. 1 (p. 6) shows the individual
non-induced CL by genotype. Observed 1-h concentrations 28.2-55.3 ug/mL (CY), 0.115-3.01 ug/mL (HCY).
Derived (mine): AST effect is tiny: AST 100 vs 25 IU/L changes CL_I by (4)^-0.0488 = 0.93.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No pharmacodynamics, ke0, MEAC or potency ratio (not an opioid). Autoinduction is the only dynamic element:
Emax 5 (maximum fold induction), EC50 0.6 (unit unstated), Kenz 0.0213 /h, giving induced-enzyme half-life 32.5 h
(p. 7), said to be similar to earlier work.

## Covariates
Retained: ABCC2 1249 GA on CL_UI (dOFV -5.985 on adding; +7.933 on removal) and AST on CL_I (-7.721; +4.763). Not
significant: other genotypes (CYP2B6, CYP2C19, CYP3A5, GST, ABCB1), azole use, ALT, bilirubin, GFR, creatinine, albumin,
urine pH. A trend (P = 0.054) towards higher CL_I with CYP2B6*6 heterozygotes (1.51 +/- 1.01 vs 2.92 +/- 1.64, text p. 7).
Forward p < 0.05 / backward p < 0.01 selection. No body-size covariate.

## Caveats
n = 21, three ABCC2 GA patients, Korean cohort; CL_I IIV 200%; several parameters fixed to literature; all patients
also received busulfan and phenytoin (both can interact with CY metabolism), so the model's CL is for this co-medicated
setting; ABCC2 is not known to transport CY itself (authors' discussion). The printed Table 2 labels disagree with the
equations (open questions).
