# Guidi 2019: artesunate-mefloquine FDC population PK in African children

## Study
Open-label randomised non-inferiority trial (DNDi, PACTR201202000278282) of the fixed-dose artesunate-mefloquine (ASMQ) dispersible tablet versus artemether-lumefantrine in children 6-59 months, six centres in Tanzania, Burkina Faso and Kenya (p. 2). 472 children received ASMQ; 21 removed by PK exclusion criteria. Dose: 1 tablet (25 mg AS / 55 mg MQ) once daily for 3 days at 6-11 months, 2 tablets at 12-59 months (p. 2). Model building on 48 Kenyan children with intensive sampling (day 0 to 6 h, day 2 to 6 h, day 3, day 7 and one late day); MQ model validated on 378 other children (538 concentrations) (pp. 3, 5-6, 9). Plasma LC-MS/MS, LOQ 2.5 ng/mL MQ, 2 ng/mL AS and DHA (p. 3); total drug (no binding measurement). NONMEM 7.3, FOCE-I; AS/DHA modelled in molar units; BQL by M6 (p. 3, 5). Table 1 (p. 6): model-building children median age 2.6 y (0.6-5.0), weight 12 kg (7-17) (the table's text layer is scrambled; the 12.2 kg median weight is stated in the Table 2 and 3 footnotes). Of the model-building data 71% of AS and 57% of DHA samples were BQL (p. 6).

## Model and parameters
AS and DHA fitted together: first-order absorption, AS eliminated only by irreversible conversion to DHA, DHA one compartment (p. 3-4, 6). Ka was fixed at 3.2 /h (mean of published values) because the data could not estimate it (p. 3). Parameters are apparent (oral). Weight: allometric with exponents fixed at 0.75 (clearances) and 1 (volumes), normalised to the median weight 12.2 kg, applied to MQ (all parameters) and DHA (CLM, VM) only; AS CL and VC had no weight term (Table 2 footnote d, p. 7). MQ: two compartments, first-order absorption (Table 3, p. 9).

Table 2 (p. 7, typical values; AS/DHA, final model): F1 100% fixed (BSV 56%); theta day-F1 -0.29, applied as (1 + theta*Q1) with Q1 = 0 on day 1 and 1 after (so F1 about 29% lower on later days); theta age-F1 -0.68, applied as 1 + theta*(AGE - 2.6)/2.6; CL (AS) 146 L/h; VC (AS) 139 L; Ka 3.2 /h fixed; CLM 11 L/h and VM 11 L (DHA, at 12.2 kg; BSV on VM 60%); sigma prop AS 79%, DHA 60%; additive 0.0023 and 0.0042 nmol/mL. Because DHA is apparent to the AS dose in molar terms, CLM and VM are not true DHA values.

Table 3 (p. 9, MQ, at 12.2 kg): F1 1 fixed (BSV 28%); CL 0.45 L/h (BSV 39%); VC 95 L; Ka 0.17 /h on day 1 (BSV 91%) and 0.40 /h after day 1; theta age-Ka -0.67, as 1 + theta*(AGE - 2.6)/2.6; Q 0.35 L/h; VP 60 L; sigma prop 21%. Individual values: CL and Q x (BW/12.2)^0.75, VC and VP x BW/12.2.

Table 4 (p. 9, derived medians with PI95%): AS Cmax 0.52 (0.17-1.43) nmol/mL, tmax 0.52 h, t1/2 40 min; DHA Cmax 3.9 (1.0-11.4) nmol/mL, tmax 1.4 (1.0-1.7) h, t1/2 40 (20-81) min; MQ Cmax 2874 (1099-4994) ng/mL, tmax 56 (35-62) h, t1/2 12 (9-24) days, AUC0-inf 650 (251-1619) (printed unit ng/L/h; see open question). Simulated MQ AUC0-day63 725 mg/L/h (310-1718) in a 12.2 kg child on 110 mg/day x 3 versus 865-1080 in adults on 400 mg/day x 3 (p. 9-10).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No concentration-effect model. An exploratory logistic regression of PCR-confirmed recrudescence (15 children, 7% of failures) on model-predicted MQ cumulative AUC at days 7, 28, 42 and 63 found no significant association (p > 0.05; day-7 median AUC 286 mg/L/h with and without recrudescence) (p. 10). No MEAC, EC50 or ke0 reported. Not an opioid paper.

## Covariates
Weight (allometric, MQ and DHA), age (on AS F1 and on MQ Ka), treatment day (AS F1; MQ Ka). Sex, liver enzymes, parasitaemia, creatinine not retained (pp. 6-8).

## Caveats
Only 48 children in model building; most AS/DHA samples BQL; AS Ka fixed; AS/DHA pcVPC showed misspecification, accepted for sparse data (p. 7); AS/DHA could not be externally validated (p. 3). MQ clearance and volumes are much lower than adult values, which the authors attribute to body weight (p. 11-12). Children 6-59 months only.

## Used by stanpumpR

## Verification against stanpumpR
Nothing cites this paper, so there is nothing to check line by line. As an independent comparison of DHA: library DHA is CL/F 255 L/h, VC/F 64.1 L at 45 kg (R/drugs_dihydroartemisinin.R:9-11), weight-scaled with exponents 0.75 and 1, the same scheme as Guidi. Scaled to 45 kg Guidi's DHA would be CLM about 29 L/h and VM about 41 L (derived: 11*(45/12.2)^0.75 and 11*45/12.2), about 9-fold lower clearance and a longer half-life (about 42 min against about 10 min). The parent drug and population differ, so this is not a discrepancy in the code; see the second open question. Guidi's literature range for DHA half-life, 30-186 min (p. 10, Discussion), also lies above the library's value. Verdict: not-applicable (no citation) with the open question flagged.
