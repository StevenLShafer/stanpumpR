# Wermeling 2001: intranasal, IV and IM lorazepam

## Study
- Randomized three-way crossover, 1-week washouts. Funded by Intranasal Technology, Inc.
- 12 enrolled (6 M, 6 F); 11 completed (subject 3, a woman, dropped out).
- Completers: 20-26 y (mean 22.0), 70.4 ± 8.7 kg.
- 2 mg by each route:
  - IV: 2 mg in 2 mL over 4 min plus flush (5 min total).
  - IM: 1 mL into the anterior thigh.
  - IN: one 100 µL spray of 1 mg/100 µL into each nostril (Pfeiffer unit-dose pump).
- Fasted. Samples at 0, 5, 15, 30 and 45 min and at 1, 2, 3, 4, 8, 12, 18, 24 and 36 h.
- Assay: LC-MS/MS, LLOQ 0.10 ng/mL.
- Analysis: noncompartmental (WinNonlin).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None measured. Adverse effects were tabulated (drowsiness was commonest). No concentration-effect data.

## Covariates
- Women had significantly higher AUC by all routes (P = 0.0001).
- No weight normalisation was done. Cmax did not differ by sex.

## Caveats
- n = 11 young adults; industry funded.
- Five subjects showed a second IN absorption peak, attributed to swallowed drug.
- The IV Cmax CV of 57.8% reflects the timing of the 5-min sample.
- No compartmental absorption model.
