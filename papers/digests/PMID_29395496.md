# Petrenaite 2018: UGT1A4 / UGT2B7 genotype, foetal sex and lamotrigine C/D in pregnancy

**Citation mismatch.** stanpumpR cites, from memory, a 2018 paper by "Petrenaite V, Ohman I, Thi Nguyen Larsen P, et al." on pregnancy-induced changes in oxcarbazepine/MHD pharmacokinetics, and this PMID has been attached to that citation in the archive. The archived paper is a different study: lamotrigine and UGT genotype, with a different author list and no oxcarbazepine or MHD data. Nothing in this card verifies the MHD citation.

## Study
- Retrospective cohort, Danish women with epilepsy followed at Rigshospitalet-Glostrup and Blegdamsvej, 2008-2014 (p. 200). 40 Caucasian non-smoking women, 47 pregnancies (7 women with two); lamotrigine monotherapy in 37 pregnancies, polytherapy (no interacting drugs) in 10; mean age 32 (21-43) y (Table 1). Women had prior UGT genotyping; smokers and those on interacting drugs excluded.
- Genotypes: UGT1A4*3 142T>G (L48V), UGT1A4*2 70C>A (P24T), UGT2B7*2 802C>T (H268Y). Trough lamotrigine before the morning dose (>= 10 h post dose), modified HPLC. C/D = plasma lamotrigine (umol/L) / daily dose (mg). Periods: T0 prepregnancy, T1 < 13 wk, T2 13-28 wk, T3 >= 28 wk, PP >= 14 days after delivery (mean 18.4 days). Women are their own controls; C/D analysis limited to the 33 pregnancies with a T0 level.
- Statistics: paired t / Wilcoxon, Bonferroni (P < 0.0125), ANOVA / Kruskal-Wallis, multiple regression (T0 ratio, foetal sex, their interaction).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None (no seizure or concentration-effect outcome). End point is the C/D ratio.

## Covariates
UGT genotypes and foetal sex, tested on C/D change. 142TG had a smaller T3 fall than 142TT; 802TT a larger T1 (P = 0.015) and T3 (P = 0.04 vs CT) fall; none survived Bonferroni correction. Foetal sex: female-foetus pregnancies had larger falls, but their T0 C/D was already higher (0.070 vs 0.044, P = 0.002/0.001, Table 3A); after adjusting for T0 the sex effect disappeared, while the T0 x sex interaction was significant for T2 (P = 0.007) and T3 (P = 0.028).

## Caveats
- Authors: small, retrospective, unstandardised sampling times and PP timing, adherence not assured.
- Abstract vs tables: the abstract's T3 range "-53% to -74%" does not match Table 2, whose most negative T3 value is -73% (70CA, n 3). The abstract attaches P = 0.002 (T2) and P < 0.001 (T3) to the percentage changes by foetal sex, but those P values belong to the absolute differences in Table 3A; the percentage comparison in Table 3B gives P = 0.28 and 0.03.
- Genotype subgroups are small (3-10 pregnancies in most variant groups); 802TT was concentrated in female-foetus pregnancies, confounding the sex effect.
