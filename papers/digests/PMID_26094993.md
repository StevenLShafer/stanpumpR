# Murata 2015: zonisamide for wearing-off in Parkinson's disease

## Study
Multicentre (65 hospitals, Japan), randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled, parallel-group, Aug 2010 - Dec 2011. 4-week single-blind placebo run-in, then 12 weeks double-blind. Oral once daily, absolute doses: placebo, zonisamide 25 mg/d, 50 mg/d (not per kg), added to unchanged antiparkinson therapy. 422 enrolled, 389 randomised (131/130/128), 354 completed; efficacy set (FAS) 375 (129/125/121). Adults 20-74 y with Parkinson's disease and wearing-off, on levodopa plus decarboxylase inhibitor >= 6 months, mean OFF time >= 2 h/d. FAS: age 63.6 y, PD duration 8.5 y, wearing-off 2.7 y, OFF time 6.37 h/d (Table 1 text; PDF p. 4-5). Primary end point: change in daily OFF time (patient diary, 30-min entries, last 7 d) at week 12; ANCOVA with LOCF, fixed-sequence testing (50 mg first); MMRM as sensitivity analysis.

## Model and parameters
No PK model, no concentrations, no assay. The paper reports no PK measurements; the only PK statement is the uncited sentence "long half-life (25 mg; t1/2 90 h)" in the Introduction (PDF p. 2).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Change in daily OFF time at week 12, LS mean +/- SE (PDF p. 6): placebo -0.011 +/- 0.173 h/d; 25 mg -0.436 +/- 0.176; 50 mg -0.719 +/- 0.179. Difference 50 mg vs placebo -0.709 h/d (95% CI -1.198 to -0.219), P = 0.005. 25 mg not significant by the fixed sequence (abstract reports P = 0.005 for 50 mg only). MMRM: -0.725 vs -0.034 h/d, P = 0.007. Responders (>= 20% OFF-time reduction): 36.8% (46/125) at 25 mg, 40.5% (49/121) at 50 mg, 20.9% (27/129) placebo. UPDRS II (off) change at 50 mg -2.0 vs -0.9 placebo (P = 0.021). Dyskinesia duration change +0.197 (25 mg) and +0.138 h/d (50 mg) vs -0.027 placebo, not significant. Earlier phase 2b/3: 50 mg reduced OFF time 1.395 h/d vs 0.178 placebo. No therapeutic concentration, MEAC, ke0 or tPeak (not an opioid paper).

## Covariates
None analysed for PK. Baseline OFF time was an ANCOVA covariate.

## Caveats
Modest effect (0.7 h/d), smaller than the earlier phase 2b/3 study; the authors give more background therapy (about 90% on a dopamine agonist) as one reason. LOCF used for the primary analysis. Japanese population; sponsor-employed authors. Adverse events: somnolence 3.1% (25 mg), 6.3% (50 mg), placebo 2.3%; dyskinesia 7.6/6.9/7.0%.

## Verification against stanpumpR
- "OFF time 0.7 h shorter on 50 mg/day": **matches** the placebo-adjusted difference of 0.709 h/d (the within-arm reduction is 0.719). The comparator is placebo, not baseline.
- Doses 25-50 mg/day: **matches** the trial arms.
- Half-life "about 63 h" and "2-6 h": not in this paper (see open questions); **cannot verify** here.
- R/drugs_zonisamide.R (Silva 2025 model, CL/F 0.761 L/h, V/F 48.1 L; half-life 44 h per its header) does not cite this paper. The paper's 90 h half-life at 25 mg is uncited text, so it is not a usable independent check; it is longer than the model's 44 h and than Kochak's 63-69 h, which the header already notes the model underpredicts.
- The registry's statement that the 10-40 mcg/mL band does not apply at these doses is the repo's inference; the paper reports no concentrations.
