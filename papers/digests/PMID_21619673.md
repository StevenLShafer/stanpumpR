# Paule 2011: PopPK/PD of hydroxyurea in sickle cell anemia

## Study
- Two datasets pooled for PK (PDF p2-4): (1) PK-PD study, 81 adult HbSS patients, 30-month open-label observational study, HU started at 20 mg/kg orally (10 to 15 mg/kg in renal insufficiency), final dose usually at most 30 mg/kg, sparse sampling up to 9 samples over up to 30 months; (2) rich-PK bioequivalence study, 16 SCA or S/beta-thal adults, 1000 to 2000 mg, 10 samples each (baseline, 45, 90, 120, 150, 180, 240, 360, 480 min, and 24 h trough).
- Table 1: PKPD set age median 30 (18 to 54), 24 men/57 women, weight 60 kg (45 to 163), creatinine 65 uM; rich set age 32, 5 men/11 women, weight 63 kg (42 to 71) (text layer).
- Assay: HPLC-UV 449 nm on heparinised plasma, linear 5 to 1000 uM, LOQ 7 uM (0.532 mg/L).
- NONMEM VII, Laplacian for PK, FOCE-I for PD; BLQ handled by Beal M3; bootstrap n = 1000; PC-VPC and NPDE.
- PD end points: fetal haemoglobin % (HbF%) and mean corpuscular volume (MCV), sequential PK-then-PD using individual mean steady-state concentration.

## Model and parameters
Two-compartment, first-order absorption and elimination. Values are for a 70-kg patient; scale Vc by weight/70 and CL by (weight/70)^0.75 (PDF p5). CL and Vc are apparent (CL/F, Vc/F; F not estimated). The 0.75 exponent fit better than 1 (p = 0.0016).

Models: dHbF%/dt = Kin x exp(theta x dMCV) - Kout x (1 - Imax) x HbF% (inhibition of elimination); dMCV/dt = Kin - Kout x (1 - beta x C^gamma) x MCV. Time to 95% of PK steady state about 35 days; HbF% steady state median about 18.6% reached in about 26 months, MCV about 104 fL in about 90 days (PDF p6-8).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- Exposure metric: mean steady-state plasma HU concentration (mg/L). Observed average concentrations in the study ranged about 2 to 9 mg/L (PDF p10); at those extremes MCV Kout was multiplied by 0.88 and 0.84.
- HbF%: no concentration-effect relationship identifiable; all patients at maximal effect (Imax typical 0.57, so Kout reduced by 57%) at 500 to 2000 mg/day. Kout 0.013 /day (derived half-life about 53 days; the authors quote about 265 days to steady state for HbF% and 83 days for MCV).
- No MEAC, no analgesic or toxic concentration, no ke0, tPeak or hysteresis (indirect-response turnover models only). Not an opioid; no potency ratio.
- No dose-limiting toxicity occurred, so no toxicity model.

## Covariates
Body weight on Vc (linear) and CL (power 0.75), kept although the OFV improvement was only near-significant. Creatinine (Cockcroft-Gault-like form), age and sex were tested. PD: dMCV (rate of MCV change between the last two observations) on HbF% Kin; dHbF% on MCV beta.

## Caveats
- Oral doses only, adults, 500 to 2000 mg/day; weights partly imputed (mean 62 kg women, 65 kg men).
- Parameters are apparent (CL/F), and the ka row is hard to interpret (see open questions).
- The PD models are weak on concentration dependence (flat exposure-response in the studied range).
- Simulations ignore genetic polymorphisms.
