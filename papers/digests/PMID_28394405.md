# Tham 2017: one-compartment population PK of eight subcutaneous insulin products

PDF page = printed page minus 1125 (PDF p1 = printed 1126).

## Study
- Pooled data from 16 clinical PK and euglycaemic clamp studies (Lilly), largely healthy subjects without diabetes;
  single SC doses with washout between occasions (p2, printed 1127). Table 1 (PDF p3), mean (range):

| Formulation | N (healthy) | Age, y | Dose, U | Weight, kg | BMI |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Regular U100 | 30 | 37.8 (21-58) | 35 (3-100) | 81.9 (63.5-119) | 28.0 (20.0-38.6) |
| Regular U500 | 12 | 36.7 (23-59) | 75 (50-100) | 100 (78.6-124) | 33.8 (30.1-37.8) |
| NPH | 24 | 28.7 (19-49) | 30 (21-42) | 73.8 (53.1-95.8) | 25.2 (19.7-31.6) |

- Assay: serum immunoreactive insulin by validated RIAs (total, bound plus unbound); compound-specific assays for
  glargine and lispro, converted to regular-insulin units with a factor of 1 (p2). Molar units in the analysis, outputs
  expressed in U of regular insulin; premix doses split by their percentages (10 U of 70/30 = 7 U NPH + 3 U regular).
- Glargine concentrations C-peptide corrected (Equation 1, PDF p3); regular and NPH fits estimate an endogenous baseline
  at time 0.
- NONMEM 7.3, FOCE-I; log-normal IIV, proportional residual error; covariates retained at dOFV >= 6.635 (P < .01).
- Glargine model built on Basaglar and externally checked on Lantus; lispro built on Humalog U-100 and checked on
  Humalog U-200 (PDF p3). Further external checks in obese nondiabetic, T1DM and T2DM patients (Figure 3, PDF p7).

## Model and parameters
One compartment, first-order absorption and elimination; one ka per formulation, V/F and CL/F per insulin type
(Figure 1, PDF p4: regular U100, U500 and NPH share Vd/F_reg and CL/F_reg; lispro and lispro protamine share
Vd/F_lis and CL/F_lis; glargine its own). Premixes feed two SC depots (70/30: regular U100 + NPH; 50/50 and 75/25:
lispro + lispro protamine). Glargine and lispro add a zero-order input (k0) drawn beside ka from the SC compartment
into the central volume (Figure 1). Table 2 (PDF p5), final estimates; the parentheses are not labelled in the table
(the footnote defines CV and "SEE, relative standard error of estimate"):

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None modelled: the paper is PK only (limitation stated in the Discussion, PDF p9). No glucose, GIR or ke0. The authors
treat the insulin concentration profile as a stand-in for the time-action profile.

## Covariates
Tested: age, weight, sex, BMI, dose. Retained: weight on V/F (regular insulin, lispro, glargine) and on endogenous
baseline (regular, NPH); dose on glargine F, regular-insulin ka and regular-insulin CL (PDF p4). The equations (reference
weights and doses, power vs linear forms) are in the online supplement, which is not in the article PDF.

Supplement (2026-10-27; Word file `PMID_28394405_sup0001.docx`, OMML; the PDF print `sup0001.pdf` lost them):
- Human insulins: Vd_Reg/NPH/F = TV x (BW/80)^theta4, ONE volume for U100, U500 and NPH; CL_Reg/NPH/F = TV x
  (Dose_Reg/NPH/10)^theta6; Ka_RegU100 = TV x (Dose_RegU100/10)^theta2; U500 and NPH ka have no covariate.
- Lispro and ILPS: Vd/F = TV x (BW/80)^theta5; CL/F and both ka without covariates.
- Glargine: Vd = TV x (1 + theta3 (BW - 71.4)), linear; F = (TVF x Dose/35)^theta1 with TVF fixed at 1; CL and ka
  without covariates; F multiplies both the first-order part and the zero-order part.
- Endogenous baseline: TV x (BW/80)^theta7, common to all insulins.
- No clearance carries weight. Reference doses 10 and 35 are given without a unit (see Caveats).

## Caveats
- Healthy volunteers, single doses; diabetic and obese populations only as external checks.
- Internal inconsistencies: the text (PDF p4) says CL/F and Vd/F both rose with weight, but Table 2 has no weight term on
  CL/F. The text says weight acts on V of "regular insulin", while Table 2 prints 1.62 only under Regular U100 although
  U500 and NPH share the same V/F. The text puts the endogenous-baseline weight effect on regular and NPH only, while
  Table 2 prints 0.739 in all six columns.
- (supplement, 2026-10-27) The supplement's equations settle three of these: no clearance has a weight term (so the
  text's "CL/F rose with weight" is not in the model); the weight power is on the volume U100, U500 and NPH share
  (Table 2 prints it in one column); the baseline's weight term is common to all insulins (as Table 2's six columns
  print it, not the text's regular and NPH only).
- (supplement, 2026-10-27) Dose units for the reference doses are not stated: the Methods (PDF p2) say molar units
  were used for dose and concentration in the analysis, and the supplement writes 10 and 35 with no unit. Glargine's
  35 sits beside its Table 1 mean dose of 36 U. Nor does the supplement define "Dose_Reg/NPH" (the dose on the
  human-insulin CL/F) for a 70/30 premix.
- Weight and weight-based dosing are confounded (authors, PDF p9).
- Sponsor Eli Lilly; all authors employees.
