# Crevoisier 2003: clonazepam 2 mg IV, IM and oral in healthy volunteers

## Study
- Open-label randomised three-way crossover; 12 healthy volunteers (6 men, 6 women), mean age 30 y (23-50), weight 63 kg (52-90), height 168 cm (158-182); washout ≥ 21 days.
- 2 mg clonazepam by IM (Rivotril ampoule, buttock, over 4 min), oral (Rivotril tablet, 100 mL water, after ≥ 12 h fast) and IV (Rivotril ampoule over 4 min). Low-fat meals at 2 and 4 h.
- Plasma (heparin) predose and 5, 10, 20, 30 min, 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 24, 48, 72, 96, 120 h.
- Assay: electron-capture GLC (De Silva), flunitrazepam IS, LLOQ 0.5 ng/mL, duplicate analyses.
- NCA in TOPFIT; F* half-life-corrected absolute bioavailability; Schuirman TOST for equivalence.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD measured. One subject had transient global amnesia 4-6 h after IV. Discussion: predicted steady-state trough about 40 ng/mL for 2 mg twice daily (7.5 ng/mL at 12 h × accumulation 5.5), compared with a reported panic-disorder threshold of 17 ng/mL (Beauclair 1994, cited). Not an opioid; no MEAC, ke0 or potency.

## Covariates
None analysed.

## Caveats
n = 12; NCA only; F* uses half-life correction; older GLC assay; IV given over 4 min, but no compartmental model reported, so the early IV phase is not characterised; Table 1 text layer misaligned.
