# Tan 2025: injection site, sex and long-acting cabotegravir/rilpivirine PK

## Study
- Prospective, non-interventional (JABS-PKInSITE), Royal Perth Hospital, Perth. n = 31 adults with suppressed HIV on routine long-acting CAB + RPV (23 men, 8 women), 4 Oct 2023 to 21 Mar 2024 (PDF p4).
- Overall (Table 1, PDF p3): median age 45 y (23-72), weight 84 kg (52-114), height 173 cm, BMI 26.3 (19-38.1); 8 had BMI >30.
- Drug: fixed-dose co-packaged CAB and RPV, gluteal (ventrogluteal) intramuscular injection every 8 weeks, 23 G 38 mm needle. Dose amounts are not stated in the paper.
- Samples: 141 serum samples (78 troughs just before an injection, about 8 weeks post dose; plus optional 24 h, 2, 4, 6 week samples). 134 injection sites were ultrasounded (<15 min after injection) and classified intramuscular (75, 56%), subcutaneous (40, 30%) or mixed (19, 14%); skin-to-muscle thickness median 18.5 mm (3.7-50.6). Subcutaneous depots in 72.4% of women vs 18.1% of men (PDF p4). All injections subcutaneous when thickness >30 mm.
- Assay: serum LC-MS/MS (total concentration, serum), quantification range 100-10 000 ng/mL CAB, 5-500 ng/mL RPV.
- Software: NONMEM 7.5.1; forward (P>0.05) / backward (P>0.01) covariate selection; bootstrap 1000; pcVPC.

## Model and parameters
CAB and RPV each: one compartment, first-order absorption (flip-flop-type depot absorption with very small ka). CAB: IIV on CL/F and ka, IOV on ka. RPV: IIV on CL/F and ka, IOV on CL/F. Table 2 (PDF p5, confirmed on page image). Parameters are normalised per 70 kg (the paper does not say how: allometric exponents are not stated in the main text).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD model, ke0, tPeak, MEAC or potency ratio (not an opioid paper). Observed trough concentrations (Table 1, PDF p3/text p4): CAB median 1390 ng/mL (340-4423), higher in women (1727) than men (1292); RPV median 56.0 ng/mL (20-257), women 62.5 vs men 53.0. No virological failure; detectable HIV RNA associated with lower CAB troughs. The authors compare with PAIC90-based targets (11.5% of CAB and 38.5% of RPV troughs under 4 x PA-IC90) and say a therapeutic target remains incompletely defined (PDF p7).

## Covariates
- CAB: subcutaneous depot location reduces ka by 56.3% (dOFV -7.237); skin-to-muscle thickness >=20 mm as a categorical covariate was also significant (dOFV -6.824) but was not retained. Sex and BMI lost significance once depot site was included.
- RPV: no covariate met criteria (a small sex difference in trough concentrations was seen in exploratory analysis).
- Comparator for "slower absorption": subcutaneous vs intramuscular depot within this cohort (not vs another study).

## Caveats
Small n (31; 8 women); ultrasound may compress tissue; shortest skin-muscle distance underestimates needle path; few early samples after injection (no rapid-phase absorption identified); morbid obesity and underweight under-represented; one-compartment model with per-70-kg normalisation not described in detail; supplements not in PDF.
