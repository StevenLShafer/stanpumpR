# Agersø 2004: desmopressin PK in renal impairment

## Study
Open-label, single-IV-dose study, 24 subjects aged 49-68 y (BMI 23-32 kg/m2, height 152-184 cm): 6 healthy and 18 with mild/moderate/severe renal impairment (none on dialysis). Groups 1-4 by CrCL quartile (Table 2). Dose 2 µg desmopressin acetate IV over 1 min (rendered abstract p.1 and Table 2 caption say µg; the text layer shows "mg" because µ is mis-extracted). Plasma sampled predose to 24 h (15 samples); urine in 3-h pieces to 24 h. Radioimmunoassay after acetone extraction; plasma LLOQ 2.5 pg/mL, urine LLOQ 20 pg/mL. CrCL from 24-h urine, normalised to 1.73 m2 (Gehan-George BSA). Two subjects excluded (apparent absorption phase). NONMEM V, FO then FOCE-interaction; plasma and urine amounts fitted simultaneously; covariates screened: CrCL, age, weight, BMI, height. Page 3-4 (printed 353-355).

## Model and parameters
Three-compartment model (V1, V2, V3, Q2 and Q3 both from V1), clearance split into renal (CLr) and non-renal (CLnr); urinary amount = CLr x AUC. CrCL was the only significant covariate, on both CLr and CLnr (Table 1, PDF p.5, printed 356). Clearances are for CrCL 60 mL/min (population median). Effects are % change per 1 mL/min CrCL change (the paper's wording; the functional form is not given in the text, so linear vs power is not stated).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None measured. No ke0, MEAC or effect concentration; no antidiuretic effect data. Not an opioid.

## Covariates
CrCL on CLr and CLnr only; age, weight, BMI and height did not significantly improve the fit. Discussion notes 240% rise in terminal t½ from healthy to lowest-CrCL group; systemic CL fell from 10 to 2.9 L/h (abstract).

## Caveats
n = 22 analysed, one dose (2 µg), ages 49-68 only; RIA assay, and the authors attribute differences from an earlier uraemic study (CL 5.88 L/h, t½ 3.3 h) to assay sensitivity. Reason for CLnr dependence on CrCL is unexplained. Table 1 labels V3 "volume of the peripheral compartment" (same as V2). Table 2 individual CL entries are as printed (e.g. subject 7 CL 8.4 L/h with CrCL 33, an outlier in Group 3).
