# Kim 2024: teicoplanin population PK in healthy adults

## Study
- 12 healthy Korean adults, 6 men and 6 women; inclusion 19-55 years. Table 1 medians: age 32 years, height 164 cm, weight 67.9 kg, BMI 24.8.
- 200 mg IV over 30 min. Venous plasma at 33, 36, 45 and 90 min, 4 and 8 h, then 48-120 h and 168-240 h. Total teicoplanin by HPLC-MS/MS.
- NONMEM 7.5, FOCE-I, ADVAN11 (three compartments).

## Model and parameters (Table 2)

```yaml
parameters:
  - {name: CL, value: 0.693, unit: L/h, note: "x (CE/105.27)^0.785, CE = CKD-EPI 2021 eGFR x BSA/1.73 in mL/min (Table 1 footnotes c, e)"}
  - {name: V1, value: 3.96, unit: L}
  - {name: Q2, value: 4.45, unit: L/h}
  - {name: V2, value: 8.24, unit: L}
  - {name: Q3, value: 1.76, unit: L/h}
  - {name: V3, value: 69.8, unit: L, note: "x (WT/67.85)^1.73"}
```

Table 3: mean terminal half-life of the population model 103 h, against the NCA's 54.6 h over sampling that ended at 7-10 days.

## Pharmacodynamics
Probability of target attainment against EUCAST's MRSA MIC distribution, 0.25-2 mg/L (Kim's simulations). No concentration-effect model.
