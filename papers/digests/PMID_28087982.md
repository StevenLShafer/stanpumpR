# Stage 2017: CES1 genotype and d-methylphenidate exposure after 10 mg Ritalin in healthy Danes

## Study
200 healthy Danish Caucasian volunteers genotyped (100 M / 100 F); 44 selected into six predefined CES1 groups
(19 M / 25 F; 43 completed): control (two wild-type CES1A1, n = 17 enrolled, 16 analysed), four CES1 copies (5), G143E
(rs71647871) heterozygotes (6), three copies with high-activity CES1A2 promoter (2), CES1A1c (4), three copies with
common CES1A2 (10). NCT02135263 (Lyauk 2016 "Study I", same 44 subjects and sampling schedule). Single oral 10 mg racemic
Ritalin 1 h after a standardised breakfast following an overnight fast. Samples 0, 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2, 2.5, 3, 4, 6, 8, 10,
24, 33 h. Chiral LC-MS/MS (CHIRALPAK-AGP), LLOQ 0.205 ng/mL for MPH enantiomers. Non-compartmental (PKSolver 2.0),
AUC by linear-log trapezoid; BLQ as zero. Control group median age 24 y, height 171 cm, weight 66.5 kg (54.5-104.0),
8 F / 8 M (Table 1).

l-MPH below LOQ at every time in 38 of 43 subjects. Allele frequencies: CES1A2 haplotype 14.7%, 143E 1.6%, CES1A1c 15.5%.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None measured. No concentration-effect data; authors note conflicting literature on concentration-response.

## Covariates
CES1 genotype groups only; sex, age, BMI not adjusted (small groups).

## Caveats
- Small, unblinded genotype groups; single 10 mg dose; fed (1 h post breakfast); non-parametric statistics without
  multiplicity adjustment.
- Four-copy result may reflect undetected CES1A1 variants beyond exon 5.
