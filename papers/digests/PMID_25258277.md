# Allegaert 2015: pooled tramadol / M1 maturation, size and CYP2D6 phenotype model

## Study
- Pooled IV tramadol studies, 295 human subjects (postmenstrual age 25 weeks to 84.8 years, weight 0.5-186 kg) plus dog
  studies (codes 20-22, including IV M1 in dogs, used to identify the M1 volume) (Tables 1-2).
- Human studies: neonates/infants (code 1, n 56), children (codes 2, 4, 6), healthy adults (code 3, Lintz studies,
  n 41; code 5, Pedersen, n 16), postoperative adults (code 7, Stamer 2007, n 56 of 93 used).
- CYP2D6 genotype in 137/295: low activity (active allele score 0 or 0.5) n 20; normal (>= 1) n 117.
- Doses converted to tramadol base (1 mg HCl = 0.8784 mg base). M1 converted to tramadol mg-equivalents (MW 249.38
  M1, 263.38 tramadol; molar ratio 0.947). Enantiomer M1 measurements summed to racemic.
- NONMEM VII level 1.3, FOCE-I; bootstrap SEs; pcVPC.

## Model and parameters
Tramadol: two compartments (VP1, VP2, CLICP), clearance split into formation to M1 (CLPM) and other routes (CLPO).
M1: two compartments (VM1, VM2, CLICM), elimination CLMO. Allometry (W/70)^0.75 clearances, ^1 volumes. Sigmoid
maturation on PMA for CLPO, CLPM, CLMO. Two-group phenotype mixture on CLPM.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None measured. Introduction states (from refs): tramadol mu affinity 6,000 times lower than morphine; (+)-M1 mu
affinity about 200 times tramadol's. These are receptor affinity ratios, not equianalgesic potencies.

Opioid audit: no MEAC, no ke0 / t½ke0 / tPeak, no equianalgesic ratio.

## Covariates
Size (allometric), postmenstrual-age maturation (Fmat = 1 / (1 + (PMA/TM50)^-Hill)), study code 2 (CLPO 34.6 %, CLMO
52.7 % of others), phenotype mixture on CLPM; CYP2D6 genotype activity explored.

## Caveats
- Internal inconsistency on the genotype/phenotype overlap: abstract says only 32 % of low-genotype-activity subjects
  were slow metabolizers; Results say 8 (42 %) of 19 low-activity subjects; Discussion says 8 of 19 phenotypic slow
  metabolizers (42 %) had low activity. (derived: 8 / 20 low-activity = 40 %.) The 32 % is not reproduced by any count
  in the body (32.4 % is the three-group model's poor-metabolizer share).
- VM1 fixed from dogs; M1 eliminated mostly renally (maturation like GFR).
- Racemic concentrations, while only (+)-tramadol to (+)-M1 is CYP2D6-specific; genotype available in under half.
