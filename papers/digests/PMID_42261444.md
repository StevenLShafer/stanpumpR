# Yan 2026: mirtazapine population PK in Chinese depressed inpatients

## Study
- Retrospective TDM data, Affiliated Brain Hospital of Guangzhou Medical University, May 2024 - Sep 2025. 105 Chinese inpatients with depressive disorders, 210 **serum** concentrations. 43 M / 62 F; age 48.52 +/- 20.80 y (**13-79**); weight 62.19 +/- 13.27 kg (25.4-104.0); height 1.63 m; BMI >= 28 in 20 (19%) (Table 1). Daily dose 7.5-45 mg once nightly (mean 31.9 mg); concentrations 53.29 +/- 31.48 ng/mL (8.25-160.17). Co-medication: venlafaxine 26, paroxetine 13, duloxetine 12, risperidone 10, fluvoxamine 6, fluoxetine 5. Smokers not included; no genotypes.
- Sampling: steady state, 8-9 AM the morning after the nightly dose; most samples about 12 h after the last dose (elimination phase), so absorption not identifiable.
- LC-MS/MS, linear 7.56-453.6 ng/mL. NONMEM 7.3 ADVAN2, FOCE-I; exponential IIV on CL/F and V/F; proportional residual. Stepwise covariates (forward dOFV > 6.63, backward > 10.83). Bootstrap (924/1000 converged), NPDE, external validation on 49 new patients (43/49 within 95% PI) and 8 literature sources.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD model. Target range quoted from AGNP 2017/2018: 30-80 ng/mL, alert 160 ng/mL. Simulated probability of target attainment: typical patient 16.0% at 15 mg/d, 70.0% at 30 mg, 74.6% at 37.5 mg. Recommended doses (Table 3): none 30-37.5 mg/d; BMI >= 28 or paroxetine 22.5-30; fluvoxamine 15; obese + paroxetine 15; obese + fluvoxamine 7.5-15. No ke0 or MEAC. Not an opioid paper.

## Covariates
CL/F = 28.9 x (1 + theta) for each present covariate: BMI >= 28 (Chinese obesity cut-off) -0.294, paroxetine -0.269, fluvoxamine -0.511. Age, sex, AST, ALT, eGFR, creatinine not retained. Combination effects are simulated multiplicatively in the paper (obese + fluvoxamine etc.).

## Caveats
- Abstract and Table 2 disagree (29.3 vs 28.9 L/h; 348 vs 310 L).
- ka fixed at 1.2 /h "based on previous study" citing Grasmäder 2004 (ref 13), but Grasmäder fixed ka at 1.5 /h; the source of 1.2 is unclear.
- V/F poorly identified from ~12 h post-dose samples (derived half-life ln2 x 310/28.9 = 7.4 h vs 20-40 h label).
- Fluvoxamine n = 6; no smokers; no genotypes; Chinese patients only; includes adolescents (from 13 y).
