# Kharasch 2004: CYP3A and CYP2B6 in methadone disposition; oral bioavailability 0.70

## Study
- Randomised, balanced 4-way crossover, ≥2 weeks apart. 12 healthy volunteers completed (8 M, 4 F; age 25 ± 7,
  18-39 y; weight 75 ± 14 kg; BMI < 30; smokers and non-smokers). 2 further subjects withdrew after the
  troleandomycin session.
- Pretreatments: none (control); rifampin 600 mg/day × 5 days before and 4 after (CYP3A induction);
  troleandomycin 500 mg every 12 h (hepatic + intestinal CYP3A inhibition); grapefruit juice (intestinal
  CYP3A inhibition). IV midazolam 1 mg as CYP3A probe.
- Methadone, given simultaneously 1 h after midazolam: IV racemic d0-methadone HCl 5.5 mg (4.5 mg base) plus oral
  racemic d5-methadone HCl 11.16 mg (10.0 mg base). Fasted; breakfast at 2 h. Ondansetron 4 mg + droperidol
  0.625 mg prophylaxis.
- Venous plasma 0.033 to 96 h (17 samples); urine to 96 h. LC-MS (d0/d5 methadone and EDDP); calibration 0.5-200
  ng/mL methadone. Racemate only (no enantiomer assay).
- Noncompartmental analysis (WinNonlin 4.01). F = (AUCoral/Doseoral) × (DoseIV/AUCIV). Hepatic plasma flow
  assumed 15.2 mL/kg/min.
- Pupil: dark-adapted pupil diameter (Pupilscan II infrared pupillometer) at each blood sample.
- In vitro: human liver microsomes and expressed CYPs.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: dark-adapted pupil diameter (miosis = baseline minus current). Peak miosis after the IV dose within
  10-15 min (p.258), secondary peak at about 4 h from the oral dose; miosis Cmax 3.5 ± 1.5 mm in controls.
- Counterclockwise hysteresis visible in all groups (Fig 5), but the authors state the protocol was not designed
  to estimate plasma-effect-site transfer (IV bolus rather than slow infusion). **No ke0, t½ke0 or tPeak was
  estimated.**
- No MEAC or analgesic concentration (healthy volunteers, no pain end point).
- Potency: R-methadone stated (citing Eap 2002) to be 10-50 times more potent than S-methadone (p.266). No
  equianalgesic ratio.
- Rifampin effects were pharmacokinetic only (no rightward shift of the miosis-concentration curve).

## Covariates
None modelled. Drug interactions (rifampin, troleandomycin, grapefruit) as above.

## Caveats
- Single low dose (15 mg HCl total); steady state not studied; racemate only (no enantiomers).
- Healthy young volunteers, n = 12; compliance with pretreatments not verified by drug levels.
- The table labels IV Vc as 5.25 L/kg, larger than Vss 4.74 L/kg, which is implausible for a true central
  volume; probably a mislabelled terminal volume. Not used by stanpumpR.
- Mixed salt/base dosing (IV 4.5 mg base, oral 10.0 mg base); F assumes both doses expressed in the same terms.
- Intestinal extraction assumes Fabs = 1.
