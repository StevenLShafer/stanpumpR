# Vree 1993: naproxen, O-desmethylnaproxen and their acyl glucuronides; cimetidine

## Study
- 10 healthy subjects (4 men, 6 women, 20-50 y); weights not reported. Naproxen (Naprosyn) 500 mg orally after an
  overnight fast. One month later 6 of them repeated 500 mg on cimetidine 400 mg twice daily (from 2 days before, for
  the 4 days of elimination). One subject took O-desmethylnaproxen 100 mg orally.
- Fingertip blood (2 mL) at 1, 2, 4, 6, 8 h, then twice daily at 12 h intervals for 4 days. Urine to 140 h, kept at
  pH 5.0-5.5 with ammonium chloride 1 g tid.
- Direct gradient HPLC of naproxen, O-desmethylnaproxen and their acyl and iso-acyl glucuronides; plasma LOQ
  1.5 µg/mL; intra/interday CV < 5%. Binding in vitro by ultrafiltration (Amicon MPS-1) of spiked blank plasma.
- Non-compartmental (MW/Pharm; linear trapezoid, extrapolation C(last)/λz).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. Not an opioid; no effect, threshold or potency data.

## Covariates
Cimetidine co-medication only: half-life fell 47.3 ± 11.5% (range 39-62%), oral clearance rose; mechanism unknown
(authors suggest altered enterohepatic cycling). No weight, age or sex analysis.

## Caveats
- Small n; sex-mixed; weights not given. Sparse sampling after 8 h (twice daily).
- Authors attribute the long, variable half-life (CV 27%) to enterohepatic cycling of the acyl glucuronide.
- Abstract compares the 10-subject baseline (24.7 h) with the 6-subject cimetidine value (13.2 h); the paired
  6-subject baseline is 25.8 h (Table 3).
- Binding measured in vitro only, at 8-20 µg/mL, below therapeutic peaks.
