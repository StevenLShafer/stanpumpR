# Liebowitz 1982: N. gonorrhoeae susceptibility to 14 agents, Johannesburg

## Study
In-vitro surveillance study (printed pp 598-603, PDF pp 1-6). 100 N. gonorrhoeae isolates from STD clinic patients in
Johannesburg, 1979-1980; none produced beta-lactamase (chromogenic cephalosporin test). CDC strains F18 and F44 as
controls. 14 agents including ceftriaxone (Hoffmann-La Roche).
- MIC and MBC: microtiter broth dilution, Mueller-Hinton broth with 6% saponin-lysed blood; suspension about 10^8
  CFU/mL, 0.01 mL per well (final inoculum about 10^6 CFU); 37 C, 10% CO2, 24 h (PDF p 1, printed p 598).
- MBC: 0.1 mL from each overnight dilution plated on chocolate agar, 24 h. The MBC end point (percentage kill) is not
  defined in the paper.
- Statistics: log2 MICs, correlation (BMDP 1R) and linear regression (SAS GLM).

## Model and parameters
No pharmacokinetics. Table 1 (PDF p 2, printed p 599; read on the rendered page), ceftriaxone, **91 strains tested**:
number inhibited at 0.007 / 0.015 / 0.03 / 0.06 / 0.125 ug/mL = 78 / 8 / 0 / 2 / 3; MIC50 < 0.007, MIC90 0.01 ug/mL.
(derived: 78/91 = 86% at the lowest dilution, so the 0.007 column is "0.007 or less"; the 90th percentile isolate,
the 82nd of 91, falls in the 0.015 column, so the reported 0.01 is not a dilution step and appears interpolated.)
Other cephalosporins for comparison (Table 1): cefotaxime (100) MIC50 < 0.007, MIC90 0.01; ceftazidime (95) < 0.007,
0.03; cefuroxime (90) 0.009, 0.11; cefoxitin (100) 0.22, 0.90. Benzylpenicillin (100) 0.01, 0.22, range up to 0.5.

MBC: no per-drug MBC values are reported. Results (PDF p 2) say that, with few exceptions, MBCs were within a log2
dilution of the MICs; the abstract says the MICs and MBCs were "within a log2 concentration of each other".

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End points are in-vitro growth inhibition (MIC) and bactericidal concentration (MBC, definition not stated). Not an
opioid paper: no MEAC, ke0 or potency ratio. Ceftriaxone MICs correlated poorly with penicillin MICs (probably
because nearly all were at the lowest dilution); best correlation was with ceftazidime, r = 0.6793 (p 599).

## Covariates
None.

## Caveats
Lowest dilution 0.007 ug/mL, so ceftriaxone MIC50 is censored. Ceftriaxone tested in 91, not 100, isolates. MBC
reported only as a general statement across agents, with exceptions not itemised. High inoculum (~10^6 CFU per well).
No beta-lactamase producers; 1979-1980 South African isolates.

  times 2, consistent with "within a log2" — **matches** as an upper estimate (the code's note does not say it is censored).

  (wording; no number changes).
- Note "no MIC90 or MBC90 was readable": Table 1 reports ceftriaxone MIC90 = 0.01 ug/mL (stated also in Results, p 599)
  — **discrepancy**; see open question. Leaving MBC90 NA has no plotted effect: the upper edge takes

  0.02-0.03 would not move it.
- The quoted phrase "within a log2 concentration of each other" is verbatim from the abstract — matches; it applies to
  all agents collectively, not to ceftriaxone specifically.
- Units: ug/mL = mg/L, no conversion issue.
