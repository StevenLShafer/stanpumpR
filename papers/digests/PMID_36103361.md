# Leutritz 2023: psychotropic TDM in pregnancy and breastmilk (Wuerzburg, Frankfurt)

## Study
Naturalistic observational study in two university mother-child psychiatric clinics, 2011-2019 (PDF p2-3): a
retrospective sample of 40 patients (routine TDM) and a prospective sample of 20 mothers with timed milk samples;
60 patients, 77 data sets (second pregnancies and medication changes counted separately). Mean age 33.26 +/- 2.45 y
(n 58), BMI 26.55 +/- 6.23 (n 31) (Table 1, PDF p3). 13 drugs; duloxetine 1 data set. Serum trough levels at steady
state (stable dose at least 5 days), drawn 8-11 AM before the dose (24-h trough for morning dosing, 12-h for
evening). TDM measurements in 9 patients in the first trimester, 24 in the second, 22 in the third. Assay: isocratic
reversed-phase HPLC, Wuerzburg TDM laboratory with external quality control (PDF p4). Statistics descriptive
(concentration/dose ratio C/D, milk/serum ratio M/P), Spearman, Kruskal-Wallis, chi-square; SPSS 26.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None for drug concentrations. Child outcomes (Table 6): no significant differences in APGAR, weight, length, head
circumference or cord gases between exposed and unexposed newborns; more moderate postpartum abnormalities in the
pregnancy-exposed group (chi-square P = .01) (PDF p7).

## Covariates
None.

## Caveats
One duloxetine data set; its dose, concentrations, sampling dates and the reason for the missing TR3 value are not
given. The Methods define C/D as "daily dosage/serum or breastmilk concentration" (PDF p4) while tables and text call
it concentration/dose; units are never stated. The authors call the trimester results preliminary and exploratory.
The Spearman coefficients 1.93 and 2.63 printed for the dose-milk correlations (PDF p5) are impossible for a
correlation coefficient: a source error.
