# Yee 1983: bleomycin disposition in children with cancer

## Study
14 children (11 boys, 3 girls; median age 14.5 y, range 1-17), solid tumours or B-cell NHL, all with bleomycin in
combination chemotherapy. 14 IV bolus courses (Table I: 2 at 20 mg/m2, 12 at 30 mg/m2) and 7 prolonged infusions
(5 at 15 mg/m2/day, 2 at 30 mg/m2/day; 24 or 48 h). Bolus samples at 5, 15, 30 min and 1, 4, 8, 12, 24 h (heparinised
plasma, 2-3 mL). Plasma and urine by radioimmunoassay (limit 8 ng/mL; precision 8% within day, 10% between days).
Fitted with DRUGMODEL (PROPHET), 1/Y weights; Vss and CL_T by model-independent methods; CL_R from the slope of
cumulative urinary excretion against plasma concentration at the collection midpoint (urine after 8 bolus courses in
7 patients). Creatinine clearance by the Schwartz equation. Bolus courses grouped by cumulative cisplatin (<300 vs
>300 mg/m2) and, within <300, by age (<3 y vs >8 y).

## Model and parameters
Two-compartment first-order model for bolus data; one-compartment for the infusions. Per m2 of body-surface area
(the paper names no BSA formula). Table II (PDF p3, printed 670), mean +/- SEM:

| parameter | all (n=11) | <3 y (n=3) | >8 y (n=8) | cisplatin >300 mg/m2 (n=3) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| t1/2 alpha (h) | 0.3 +/- 0.1 | 0.2 +/- 0.1 | 0.3 +/- 0.1 | 0.4 +/- 0.2 |
| t1/2 beta (h) | 3.2 +/- 0.7 | 3.5 +/- 2.2 | 3.1 +/- 0.6 | 6.0 +/- 1.0 |
| Vc (L/m2) | 4.3 +/- 0.5 | 4.7 +/- 1.0 | 4.1 +/- 0.6 | 3.7 +/- 0.8 |
| Vss (L/m2) | 9.9 +/- 1.1 | 10.6 +/- 3.2 | 9.7 +/- 1.0 | 8.1 +/- 0.7 |
| CL_T (mL/min/m2) | 51.8 +/- 6.1 | 70.5 +/- 15.1 * | 44.8 +/- 4.0 | 18.0 +/- 3.3 * |
| CL_R (mL/min/m2) | 33.5 +/- 2.4 | 26.2 (one patient) | 34.7 +/- 2.4 | 8.2 (one patient) |

* P < 0.05 (the <3 y column against the >8 y column, the >300 column against <300).
Table III (infusions, PDF p4, text layer): normal renal function n=6, t1/2 2.1 +/- 0.1 h, Vd 11.0 +/- 2.6 L/m2,
CL_T 57.1 +/- 13.5, CL_R 33.2 +/- 6.4 mL/min/m2; one course with ureteral obstruction (CrCl about 10 mL/min/1.73 m2):
t1/2 7.9 h, Vd 3.3 L/m2, CL_T 4.8, CL_R 4.1 mL/min/m2. Steady-state concentrations 188 and 320 ng/mL at 15 and
30 mg/m2/day (children, normal renal function); about 1600 ng/mL in the obstructed course.
Other text: recalculated Vd-beta 14.8 L/m2 (adult value cited 17.5); CL_T in adults cited 50.6 mL/min/m2.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No effect, toxicity or concentration-response data, no MEAC, ke0 or potency ratio (not an opioid). Concentrations are
radioimmunoassay plasma values; steady-state infusion concentrations are listed above.

## Covariates
Renal function: CL_T and CL_R fall after cumulative cisplatin >300 mg/m2 (CrCl 77 vs 122 mL/min/1.73 m2, P<0.05);
within the bolus data no significant correlation of CrCl with t1/2 beta, CL_T or CL_R (P>0.05). Age: CL_T higher in
<3 y (70.5, n=3) than in >8 y (44.8), against the older children of this study; only one patient aged 3-14 y, so the
transition age was not assessed. Obstructed ureters: CL_T 4.8. Children's CL_T 51.8 is described as nearly identical to
the adult value (50.6) cited from Alberts 1978.

## Caveats
Small n (3 courses under 3 y; 3 courses after high-dose cisplatin from 2 children; one obstructed infusion). Table II
columns are means of per-course fits, so five parameters for a four-parameter model do not close exactly. Early
sampling was inadequate to resolve a multicompartment shape after infusions. Other chemotherapy given concurrently.
Adolescents dominate the older group (14-17 y, one 8-y-old).
