# Mazzucchelli 2006: oxcarbazepine, R- and S-MHD in pregnancy and the puerperium

## Study
- Design: prospective observational study in five women with partial epilepsy on oxcarbazepine (OXC) monotherapy, from centres in Italy and Turkey.
- Patients: aged 22-34 y (mean 27), weight at inclusion 54-62 kg (mean 58).
- Dosing: 150-1,500 mg/day OXC in two divided doses. Three patients had dose changes during follow-up.
- Enrolment: four women from the first trimester, one (patient 5) 2 weeks before delivery.
- Sampling: plasma at least once per trimester, at delivery and within 3 months postpartum, mostly 12-15 h after the last dose (trough).
- Assay: enantioselective HPLC on a Chiralcel ODR column, UV detection at 210 nm.
  - Analytes: OXC, R-(−)-MHD, S-(+)-MHD, and racemic DHD (carbamazepine-10,11-trans-dihydrodiol).
  - LOQ: 0.06 µM for OXC and DHD, 0.12 µM for each MHD enantiomer.
  - The text gives between-assay CV as ">15%", probably a misprint for <15%.
- Active moiety: the molar sum OXC + R-MHD + S-MHD.
- Dose normalization: concentrations divided by (dose mg/day)/100, i.e. µM per 100 mg/day.
- Analysis: repeated-measures ANOVA and trend tests. No pharmacokinetic model, and no clearance was computed.

Derived from Table 1 (none of these is stated in the paper):
- S-MHD as a fraction of total MHD (S/(S+R)) is 0.85, 0.86, 0.85 and 0.86 in T1, T2, T3 and the puerperium.
- Mean S-MHD, T3/T1 = 0.52 and T3/puerperium = 0.42.
- Active moiety, T3/puerperium = 0.43.
- At steady state, a dose-normalized concentration is inversely proportional to CL/F. These ratios therefore correspond to CL/F about 1.9× (against T1) and about 2.4× (against the puerperium) in the third trimester.
- These are cross-sectional means with unequal n per column, not paired estimates.

Results reported in the text:
- Postpartum rise: in four of five women, dose-normalized S-MHD and active moiety rose 1.5- to 13-fold from the last pregnancy sample to the first puerperal one. Patient 4 had no late-pregnancy samples.
- ANOVA across periods: significant for R-MHD (p < 0.02); borderline for active moiety (p = 0.086).
- Excluding T1: active moiety p < 0.05, R-MHD p < 0.01, S-MHD p = 0.06.
- MHD protein binding is about 40%, cited from May 2003 (ref. 9).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- Clinical observations: low active-moiety concentrations coincided with seizures in patients 1 and 2, and control improved after dose increases.
- Not reported: an effect site, ke0, a therapeutic range, and any concentration-effect analysis.

## Covariates
- Pregnancy period (trimester or puerperium) only.
- There was no pre-pregnancy baseline. The authors cannot say whether puerperal levels are a return to baseline or an overshoot above it (p. 508).

## Caveats
- n = 5 in total, with few samples per woman, dose changes in three, and one woman enrolled only at term. No pre-conception baseline.
- The authors give these limitations themselves (p. 508).
- The comparison is within the same women: pregnancy against the first trimester and the puerperium.
- Concentrations are total plasma.
- What the paper does not do: it does not study eslicarbazepine acetate, and it computes no clearance.
