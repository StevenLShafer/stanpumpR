# Douxfils 2018: laboratory testing of the direct oral anticoagulants

## Study
Narrative review (no new data) on which coagulation test to use for each DOAC (apixaban, dabigatran etexilate,
edoxaban, rivaroxaban; betrixaban excluded), when to measure, how to interpret results for bleeding,
emergency, thrombolysis and perioperative decisions, and DOAC interference with routine and thrombophilia
assays. Open access (CC BY-NC). Several authors declare industry fees (Stago, Daiichi Sankyo, Bayer, BI,
BMS/Pfizer); first author is CEO of QUALIblood.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- Time to maximal effect: about 3 h for dabigatran, apixaban and rivaroxaban, 2 h for edoxaban (PDF p2-3).
- Thresholds quoted from others (PDF p6, printed p214, rendered page): ISTH SSC guidance (ref 29), drug level
  > 30 ng/mL before high-bleeding-risk urgent intervention likely warrants an antidote; > 50 ng/mL with serious
  bleeding. Thrombolysis cut-offs (ref 31): 10 (apixaban), 50 (dabigatran), 100 (rivaroxaban) ng/mL; a
  rivaroxaban study (ref 32): < 20 (or 30) ng/mL thrombolysis recommended, 20-100 considered, > 100 precludes (PDF p7).
- Perioperative residual level < 30 ng/mL: proposed, still debated (PDF p7).
- Assay ranges: dTT accurate 50-500 ng/mL; limit of quantitation of most specific tests about 30-50 ng/mL;
  a normal dRVVT can exclude DOAC > 50 ng/mL (Table 1, PDF p3). PT/APTT ratio > 1.2 taken as relevant prolongation (Fig. 1).
- The authors state there is no consensus therapeutic range for these drugs, and that thresholds derived from
  PK studies still need prospective validation (PDF p8-9).
- No ke0, EC50 or exposure-response model.

## Covariates
Not modelled. Mentions renal function, drug interactions, body weight extremes, liver dysfunction and
malabsorption as reasons concentrations may depart from expected; 12-13% of routine-care patients had levels
above the phase III 95th percentile, 50-60-fold interpatient variation (ref 58; PDF p9).

## Caveats
Narrative review; all concentration values are secondary, from SmPCs and other papers, with mixed summary
statistics (mean vs median, IQR vs 5th-95th). Assay- and reagent-dependent interpretation. Industry conflicts.
