# Vega-Villa 2014: vecuronium pharmacokinetics in major burns

## Study
- 20 adults with > 20% TBSA burns (TBSA median 51%, range 27-81; studied 4-57 days post-burn, median 13) and 20
  age- and sex-matched non-burn patients having elective surgery (Table 1, PDF p2 / printed 305).
- Non-burn controls: M:F 17:3, age 40 (25-58) yr, height 170 (7) cm, **weight 70 (13) kg**, ASA 17/2/1/0,
  albumin 4.6 (0.7) g/dL, CrCL 103 (18) mL/min. Burns: 16:4, 36 (23-58) yr, 169 (7) cm, 65 (10) kg.
  Mean (SD) or median (range). No renal or hepatic dysfunction.
- Anaesthesia: propofol 2.0-2.5 mg/kg then 0.1-0.2 mg/kg/min, intermittent fentanyl 1-2 µg/kg, N2O 50%. **No
  volatile agent.**
- Vecuronium 0.12 mg/kg IV bolus over < 10 s. Plasma (EDTA) at 0, 1, 2, 3, 5, 10, 15, 30, 45, 60, 90, 120, 150,
  180, 240, 300, 360, 480, 600, 720 min. HPLC-MS/MS, accuracy 95.5%, precision < 9.1%, LOQ 5.0 ng/mL.
- NONMEM 7.2, ADVAN11 TRANS4, LAPLACIAN with INTERACTION, log-transformed data; below-LOQ data (26% overall)
  handled by the YLO likelihood adjustment. Exponential IIV (removed from V3, shrinkage > 50%); V1-CL correlation
  0.77. Combined proportional + additive error. Covariates from Tables 1-2 (which include weight, age, sex)
  screened stepwise (forward P < 0.05, backward P < 0.01). One burn patient's data after a second dose excluded;
  565 observations.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No pharmacodynamic measurement: no twitch data, ke0, tPeak, EC50 or gamma. Not an opioid; no MEAC or potency ratio.
The discussion cites a paediatric study relating effective vecuronium dose to TBSA, and a free fraction of 31-35%
from another source (ref 15), neither measured here.

## Covariates
BURN (0/1) on CL3: TVCL3 = theta_nonburn x (1 + BURN x theta_burn_effect); BURN on the IIV of V2. TBSA, albumin
and blood loss were significant alone but dropped once BURN was in. No age, weight or sex effect retained.

## Caveats
- Few terminal-phase data: by 720 min only 5 of 20 non-burn and 3 of 20 burn patients had measurable concentrations
  (printed 306), so V3, CL3 and the 6.6 h half-life rest on sparse data; V3 has no IIV and a very wide bootstrap CI.
- Several typical values lie at or outside their own bootstrap 90% CI (CL3 non-burn 0.095 vs 0.075-0.092; V1 10.25
  vs 8.55-10.66; V3 43.79 vs upper bound 44.08), and the %RSEs (1-6%) look small beside IIVs of 45-49%.
- Patients under propofol/fentanyl/N2O; no hepatic or renal disease; 17:3 male in controls.
- "Shorter half-life" and "higher CL3" in burns are against this study's own matched non-burn controls.
- Burn-group CL3 is 27% higher in the text, 24% in the Fig 2 caption.
