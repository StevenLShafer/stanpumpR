# Yu 2026: vancomycin in kidney transplant recipients

## Study
- Retrospective, University of Michigan 2007-2024. PK analysis 1539 hospitalised adults: 469 kidney transplant recipients (courses > 30 days after transplant) and 1070 non-transplant patients frequency-matched on age, sex and weight. Excluded: renal replacement therapy, multi-organ transplant.
- Recipients (Table I, medians): age 60.7 y, weight 84.5 kg, height 172.7 cm, creatinine 1.83 mg/dL, CLcr 44.9 mL/min.
- Routine TDM (troughs and random samples), kinetic immunoassay. Infusion durations imputed by dose where not recorded.
- CLcr: Cockcroft-Gault, time-varying, with actual weight or, above 120% of the Devine IBW, adjusted weight IBW + 0.4 x excess; truncated at 150 mL/min.
- Monolix 2024R1, SAEM. External validation on 479 MIMIC-IV patients without re-estimation.

## Verification against stanpumpR
- Implemented as `vancomycinKidneyTransplant` (`R/drugs_vancomycinKidneyTransplant.R`) with KT = 1; tests reproduce the paper's typical CL of 1.5 L/h (recipients) and 3.9 L/h (non-transplant) at the group median CLcr.
- Independent check of `vancomycin` (Thomson 2009), which does not cite it: the non-transplant clearance agrees with Thomson's at the same Cockcroft-Gault CLcr to within about 5% over 45-150 mL/min (Thomson slightly higher), tested in `test-drugs-vancomycinKidneyTransplant.R`. The volumes are not comparable (one compartment from trough-dominated data against two).
- External validation over-predicted recipients' concentrations (mean prediction error +16.8% against +7.6%), so the transplant effect may be smaller elsewhere; stated in the drug's header and help page.
