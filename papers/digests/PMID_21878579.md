# Krishnaswami 2012: celecoxib population PK (weight, sex) in JRA children pooled with adult RA

## Study
Study I: 12-week randomized double-blind trial, celecoxib oral suspension ~3 or ~6 mg/kg bid (fixed mg by weight
band, Table I) vs naproxen in JRA patients aged 2-16 (abstract: 2 to 17), ≥9 kg (none <10 kg enrolled); sparse PK
(one sample per visit at weeks 2, 4, 8, 12; predose, 0.5, 3, 6 h). Plus a separate adult RA cohort (n = 36, 200 mg
bid suspension, rich sampling at week 2). PK data: 73 + 79 JRA patients and 36 adults (188; 797 observations).
HPLC-MS/MS, LLOQ 1 ng/mL. NONMEM V, FO-type with log-transformed error; covariates by WAM. Studies II and III:
healthy-adult relative bioavailability of suspension and capsule sprinkles vs capsule.

## Model and parameters
One-compartment, first-order absorption; CL/F and V/F reference: 41 kg (median weight of the PK data set), CL/F
reference a female (Eq. 15-16): CL/F = θ3·(W/41)^θ7·(1 + θ8·male); V/F = θ2·(W/41)^θ6; Frel = 1; Ka = θ1.

Full model also tested age, race, methotrexate (CL/F) and age, race, sex (V/F), lot and food (Frel); not retained.
Abstract: typical CL/F 40% and 24% lower at 10 and 25 kg than at 70 kg. Mean Bayes CL/F by age group (Table IV):
30.6 (2-5 y, 15.4 kg), 33.4 (5-11 y), 46.0 (11-17 y), 44.9 L/h (adult RA, 81.7 kg). Suspension/capsule AUC ratio
0.848 (200 mg), 0.867 (400 mg); Cmax ratio 0.476 and 0.575 (Study II). Sprinkles ≈ capsule (Study III).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Exposure-response: mixed-effects logistic model of JRA-30 DOI responders vs time with linear dose or typical AUC0-12
slope (Table V; e.g. exponential time model + typical AUC θ4 0.253 per µg·h/mL). No ke0, EC50 or analgesic
concentration. Not an opioid.

## Covariates
Weight (power on CL/F and V/F), sex (CL/F). An allometric (0.75/1) plus age alternative was examined and gave similar
typical clearances; authors caution the weight exponent reflects net allometry + age.

## Caveats
Sparse sampling; one-compartment (authors note 2-compartment describes single doses); suspension formulation; JRA
inflammation may affect clearance; no placebo for E-R. θ3 is for a female.
