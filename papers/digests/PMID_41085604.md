# Vuong 2025: fluconazole in critically ill adults, a pooled population analysis

## Study
- Individual data from eight published ICU studies (Van Daele, Muilwijk, Bergner, Buijk, Sandaradura, Sinnollareddy, Alobaid, Boonstra; Belgium, the Netherlands, Germany, Australia), obtained from the authors: 177 patients, intravenous fluconazole, 1616 plasma concentrations (median 7 per patient), none below the limit of quantification.
- Table 1, overall: age median 62 years (Q1-Q3 50-70), total body weight 80 kg (70-92; 34-142 in the text), 115 men (65%), APACHE II 18; 34 patients (19.2%) on CRRT at least once; eGFR off CRRT median 82.7 mL/min/1.73 m2 (49.8-104; 7-213 in the text), available in 40.7% of dosing intervals, the rest multiply imputed (70 datasets, pooled by Rubin's rules). No height or BMI printed.
- NONMEM 7.5, ADVAN13, FOCE-I; stepwise covariate search (forward 0.05, backward 0.01) over total body weight, BMI, FFM (lean body weight), time-varying CRRT and eGFR (CKD-EPI without race). BMI and FFM were tested and not retained.
- 2000 bootstraps (200 on each of 10 imputed datasets). Simulations: 1000 virtual patients, 1000 simulations each. The assays differed between the contributing centres, which did not change the error model.

## Model and parameters
Two compartments, linear elimination, proportional error (16.7%). Equations 3-4 and Table 2 (final model, pooled), matching the ESM1 control stream (clearance separate on and off CRRT; covariates normalised to 80 kg and 80 mL/min/1.73 m2 by power functions):

    CL (off CRRT) = 0.614 L/h x (eGFR / 80)^0.532
    CL (on CRRT)  = 1.56 L/h
    Vc = 39.4 L x (TBW / 80 kg)^0.908
    Q  = 12.1 L/h,  Vp = 8.47 L

Base model, before covariates: CL on CRRT 1.56, CL off CRRT 0.633 L/h, Vc 38.7 L, Q 13.5 L/h, Vp 8.66 L -- so the covariates move the point estimates very little, and what they buy is the 8.1% and 7.1% of variability below.

The variances printed with the equations are omega^2 0.217 for CL off CRRT and 0.312 for Vc, which agree with Table 2's %CV as they should: ln(1 + 0.493^2) = 0.217 and ln(1 + 0.605^2) = 0.312. The CL off CRRT-Vc covariance is 26.4 in the final model (33.9 in the base, which is the figure the Results text quotes).

The control stream's $THETA values (1.7, 0.641, 38.2, 13.4, 9.02, 0.401, 1) are initial estimates for one imputed dataset, not the pooled finals.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Target fAUC0-24/MIC of 100, i.e. fAUC0-24 of 200 mg h/L (MIC 2 mg/L) and 400 mg h/L (MIC 4 mg/L), unbound fraction 0.89 (protein binding 11-12%, from the label); a trough of 80 mg/L treated as a toxicity threshold, taken from two case reports of convulsions. Under the proposed regimen fewer than 1.2% of virtual patients reached that trough. No effect compartment, ke0 or MAC concept applies.

## Dosing recommendation (Table 3, PDF p.11, read on the page image)
Maintenance from day 2, every 24 h: 400 mg off CRRT, 800 mg on CRRT. Loading dose on day 1 by weight band, off CRRT / on CRRT: 30-60 kg 800 / 1000 mg; 60.1-80 kg 1000 / 1200; 80.1-100 kg 1200 / 1400; 100.1-120 kg 1400 / 1600; 120.1-150 kg 1600 / 1800. The label regimen (800 mg then 400 mg) missed 90% target attainment above 60 kg off CRRT and above 30 kg on CRRT on day 1; 12 mg/kg then 6 mg/kg missed it below 80 kg off CRRT and below 120 kg on CRRT (PDF p.6).

## Covariates
eGFR (time-varying) on CL off CRRT; total body weight (baseline) on Vc; CRRT status selects the clearance. They explained 8.1% and 7.1% of the variability.

## Caveats
- Critically ill patients only; clearance about 40% below the Diflucan label's healthy-volunteer value at a normal eGFR.
- eGFR missing in 59% of intervals and imputed.
- The ESM1 simulation code renames a column "FFM" to "BW" in its virtual dataset; the model itself (control stream) uses total body weight.
- Intravenous data only: no absorption.
- Not a systematic literature search, and the eight source studies are heterogeneous.
- Types of CRRT are not distinguished, and no covariate interactions were tested.
- CKD-EPI is unreliable in critical illness, which is the population it is applied to here.
- No external validation; the authors say so.
- Q and Vp have wide confidence intervals (3.7-20.5 L/h and 2.73-14.20 L).
- The regimen is poor for an MIC of 4 mg/L or for C. glabrata.
