# Vuong 2025: fluconazole in critically ill adults, a pooled population analysis

## Study
- Individual data from eight published ICU studies (Van Daele, Muilwijk, Bergner, Buijk, Sandaradura, Sinnollareddy, Alobaid, Boonstra; Belgium, the Netherlands, Germany, Australia), obtained from the authors: 177 patients, intravenous fluconazole, 1616 plasma concentrations (median 7 per patient), none below the limit of quantification.
- Table 1, overall: age median 62 years (Q1-Q3 50-70), total body weight 80 kg (70-92; 34-142 in the text), 115 men (65%), APACHE II 18; 34 patients (19.2%) on CRRT at least once; eGFR off CRRT median 82.7 mL/min/1.73 m2 (49.8-104; 7-213 in the text), available in 40.7% of dosing intervals, the rest multiply imputed (70 datasets, pooled by Rubin's rules). No height or BMI printed.
- NONMEM 7.5, ADVAN13, FOCE-I; stepwise covariate search (forward 0.05, backward 0.01) over total body weight, BMI, FFM (lean body weight), time-varying CRRT and eGFR (CKD-EPI without race).

## Model and parameters
Two compartments, linear elimination, proportional error (16.7%). Equations 3-4 and Table 2 (final model, pooled), matching the ESM1 control stream (clearance separate on and off CRRT; covariates normalised to 80 kg and 80 mL/min/1.73 m2 by power functions):

    CL (off CRRT) = 0.614 L/h x (eGFR / 80)^0.532
    CL (on CRRT)  = 1.56 L/h
    Vc = 39.4 L x (TBW / 80 kg)^0.908
    Q  = 12.1 L/h,  Vp = 8.47 L

The control stream's $THETA values (1.7, 0.641, 38.2, 13.4, 9.02, 0.401, 1) are initial estimates for one imputed dataset, not the pooled finals.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
fAUC0-24 of 200 mg h/L (MIC 2 mg/L) and 400 mg h/L (MIC 4 mg/L), unbound fraction 0.89; a trough of 80 mg/L treated as a toxicity threshold. Recommends a weight-based loading dose and 400 mg (off CRRT) or 800 mg (on CRRT) daily.

## Covariates
eGFR (time-varying) on CL off CRRT; total body weight (baseline) on Vc; CRRT status selects the clearance. They explained 8.1% and 7.1% of the variability.

## Caveats
- Critically ill patients only; clearance about 40% below the Diflucan label's healthy-volunteer value at a normal eGFR.
- eGFR missing in 59% of intervals and imputed.
- The ESM1 simulation code renames a column "FFM" to "BW" in its virtual dataset; the model itself (control stream) uses total body weight.
- Intravenous data only: no absorption.
