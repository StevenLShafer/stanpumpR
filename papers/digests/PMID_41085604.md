# Vuong 2026: fluconazole popPK in critically ill patients

## Study
Pooled analysis of eight published ICU studies of IV fluconazole: 177 critically ill adults, 1616 plasma concentrations (median 7 per patient), body weight 34-142 kg (median 80 kg, IQR 70-92), eGFR CKD-EPI 7-213 mL/min/1.73 m2 (median 82.7), 34 patients (19.2%) on CRRT at some time (Table 1, PDF p.5, text layer). Weight missing in 5 (2.8%) and eGFR present in only 40.7% of dosing intervals; missing values handled by multiple imputation (m = 70). NONMEM 7.5, FOCE-I, ADVAN13; 2000 bootstraps (200 on each of 10 imputed sets). Simulations: 1000 virtual patients, 1000 simulations each. Assays differed between centres; they did not change the error model.

## Model and parameters
Two-compartment, linear elimination, proportional error. CRRT is a time-varying categorical covariate with a separate clearance. Table 2 (final-model pooled estimate, 95% CI; PDF p.6, confirmed on the rendered page):
- CL on CRRT 1.56 L/h (1.25-1.87); CL off CRRT 0.614 L/h (0.548-0.679) at eGFR 80 mL/min/1.73 m2.
- Vc 39.4 L (33.1-45.7) at 80 kg; Q 12.1 L/h (3.7-20.5); Vp 8.47 L (2.73-14.20).
- Exponent of eGFR on CL off CRRT 0.532 (0.131-0.933); exponent of body weight on Vc 0.908 (0.498-1.317).
- IIV (%CV): CL on CRRT 57.1, CL off CRRT 49.3, Vc 60.5; covariance CL off CRRT-Vc 26.4 (final; base 33.9, which the Results text quotes); proportional error 16.7%.
- Base model (no covariates): CL on 1.56, CL off 0.633, Vc 38.7, Q 13.5, Vp 8.66.
Equations (text layer, PDF p.3): Vc = 39.4 L x (BW/80 kg)^0.908 x exp(eta), omega^2 0.312; CL off CRRT = 0.614 x (eGFR/80)^0.532 x exp(eta), omega^2 0.217. The text prints the CL off CRRT unit as "L", an evident typo for L/h (Table 2). The two omega^2 agree with the %CV values (derived: ln(1+0.605^2) = 0.312; ln(1+0.493^2) = 0.217).
Body weight explained 7.1% of IIV in Vc and eGFR 8.1% of IIV in CL off CRRT (PDF p.4).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Not an opioid paper. PKPD target: fAUC0-24/MIC of 100 (fAUC0-24 200 mg h/L at MIC 2 mg/L; 400 mg h/L explored for MIC 4 mg/L), unbound fraction 89% (protein binding 11-12%, from the label). Toxicity threshold used: trough 80 mg/L, from two case reports of convulsions. Under the proposed regimen fewer than 1.2% of virtual patients had a trough of 80 mg/L or higher. No ke0 or MAC concept applies.

## Covariates
Total body weight on Vc (power, 80 kg reference); CRRT status on CL (separate estimates); eGFR CKD-EPI on CL off CRRT (power, 80 reference). Body weight and eGFR were statistically significant, but with the proposed regimen only CRRT and weight were clinically relevant for target attainment. BMI and fat-free mass were tested and not retained.

## Dosing recommendation (Table 3, PDF p.11, confirmed on the page image)
Maintenance from day 2, every 24 h: 400 mg off CRRT, 800 mg on CRRT. Loading dose on day 1 by weight, off CRRT / on CRRT: 30-60 kg 800 / 1000 mg; 60.1-80 kg 1000 / 1200; 80.1-100 kg 1200 / 1400; 100.1-120 kg 1400 / 1600; 120.1-150 kg 1600 / 1800. The standard label regimen (800 mg then 400 mg) missed 90% target attainment above 60 kg off CRRT and above 30 kg on CRRT on day 1; 12 mg/kg then 6 mg/kg missed it below 80 kg (off CRRT) and below 120 kg (on CRRT) (PDF p.6).

## Caveats
Not a systematic literature search; types of CRRT not distinguished; heterogeneous source studies; no covariate interactions tested; CKD-EPI unreliable in critical illness; no external validation (authors say so); eGFR missing in about 60% of intervals so its effect is an imputation result; regimen is poor for MIC 4 mg/L or C. glabrata. Q and Vp have wide CIs (Table 2).
