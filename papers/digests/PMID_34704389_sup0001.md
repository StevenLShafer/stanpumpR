# Patel 2022 supplement: imipenem/relebactam population PK, final and alternative models

## Study
Supplement to the imipenem/cilastatin/relebactam (IMI/REL) popPK/PD paper. Data (Table S1, PDF pages 5-9): phase 1 healthy-volunteer trials (PN001 n = 83 men, PN002 n = 18 elderly and young women, renal-impairment study n = 30 plus 18 matched controls, intrapulmonary study n = 16, three-period crossover n = 36, Japanese studies n = 16 and 14) and phase 2/3 patients: cUTI n = 261, cIAI n = 313, 50 patients with IMI-resistant infection (HABP/VABP, cUTI or cIAI), 261 hospitalized adults with HABP/VABP, and 81 Japanese patients with cIAI or cUTI (PN017); protocol numbers for the middle rows are not reliably aligned in the text layer. Dosing in patients: 500/250 mg IMI/REL (fixed-dose combination) every 6 h. The row-to-protocol alignment in the text layer is partly shifted; protocol numbers are given only where legible.
- Method (page 1-2): NONMEM 7.3.0 with PsN 4.7.0; data prep in SAS 9.4 and R 3.3.1. The earlier final models were first checked with VPCs/simulation on the new trials PN014 and PN017 (Figure S2 shows the dose-corrected VPCs), then re-fitted; observations with absolute CWRES over 6 were removed; BLQ values treated as missing; missing weight and CrCl imputed with the population median. Cilastatin not modelled. PK sampling in PN014/PN017: about 30 min and about 4 h after start of the first infusion on days 1 and 3 (PN014 also day 6).
- Alternative model (page 2): the final model plus covariance of imipenem CL with relebactam CL, fixed allometric exponents 0.75 on CL and Q and 1.0 on V1 and V2 for both drugs, and separate residual error for healthy participants and infected patients.
- Table S2 (page 10): renal dose adjustment of IMI/CIL/REL by CrCl (mL/min): 90 or more 500/500/250 mg q6h; 60-89 400/400/200; 30-59 300/300/150; 15-29 200/200/100 (derived reading of the scrambled table; piperacillin/tazobactam comparator: CrCl 40 or more 4 g/500 mg, 20-40 3 g/375 mg, 15-under 20 2 g/250 mg q6h). Table S3 (page 11): CrCl categories for simulation, augmented renal clearance 150-250 mL/min through ESRD under 15 mL/min.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No MEAC, ke0 or opioid-style PD. The PK/PD target is probability of target attainment: 30% fT>MIC (free imipenem; sensitivity 40%, Table S6) and relebactam fAUC0-24/MIC of 8 or more with relebactam fixed at 4 ug/mL, computed from simulated patients (Tables S5, S6, pages 16-18). The free fractions used are not given in the supplement. Not an opioid paper.

## Covariates
CL: creatinine clearance (power), weight (power, imipenem only in the final model; fixed 0.75 in the alternative), pneumonia (reference: healthy + cIAI + cUTI). V1: weight (power 1.0 imipenem, 0.7 relebactam), pneumonia, ventilation within pneumonia. Table S4 only; no equations printed.

## Caveats
- The text layer of Table S4 and the page-4 to 9 tables is scrambled; Table S4 was confirmed on images, Tables S1-S3 and S5-S6 were not.
- Final and alternative models differ materially in Q (imipenem 23.1 vs 9.9 L/h), V1 and BSV; the authors present the alternative as a robustness check (page 2).
- Mean estimates only; no reference covariate values in the supplement; pneumonia/ventilation effects are low-precision (ventilation RSE 32-46%).
- Healthy and patient data pooled; effects of pneumonia are on the pooled reference.
