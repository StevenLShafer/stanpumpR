# Periclou 1996: population PK of cytarabine (ara-C) in children with leukaemia

## Study
- Retrospective NONMEM analysis of four clinical studies run over 12 years at Childrens Hospital Los Angeles (PDF p. 3, printed 44).
- 52 paediatric patients with leukaemia (ALL, AML) or lymphoma, aged 0.17-19 years (methods; the abstract says 2 months to 19 years). Weight, sex and surface-area distribution are not tabulated.
- Regimens: high-dose ara-C (HDara-C), 3 g/m2 every 12 h x 8 doses, infused over 3 h in most children and over 1 h in a smaller share; or a loading bolus (LB) followed at once by a 72-h continuous infusion (CI). LB/CI doses are not printed in this paper.
- Sampling: 3-h HDara-C at 0, 3, 4, 5, 7, 9 h; 1-h HDara-C at 0-8 h hourly; LB+CI at 0, 2, 12, 24, 36, 48, 60, 72 h; a few hourly post-infusion samples.
- Assay: ara-C and ara-U in heparinised plasma by reverse-phase HPLC (muBondapak C18, UV 280 nm), external standard; same assay in all studies. No LLOQ printed.
- Software: NONMEM IV level 2. Model I: ADVAN3/TRANS4 (CL, V1, Q, V2). Model II (ara-C plus ara-U): ADVAN6 differential equations; Model II-4: ADVAN3/TRANS1. IIV: constant-CV (proportional) etas, diagonal OMEGA; residual: combined additive + proportional (Eq. 5). Covariate acceptance at P < 0.01 by likelihood ratio.

## Model and parameters
Model I, ara-C alone, two compartments, first-order elimination from the central compartment. The basic model (I-1) was biased (Appendix 1); the final model (I-2) makes every parameter a linear function of the product P = AGE x SA (age in years, surface area in m2).

Final estimates as the abstract and Results text print them (PDF p. 1 and p. 4, printed 42 and 45; confirmed on page images): CL = 2.59 x AGE x SA L/h, Q = 2.01 x AGE x SA L/h, Vd1 = 0.48 x AGE x SA L, Vd2 = 38.1 x AGE x SA L. CVs of interindividual variability (text): CL 83.79%, Q 12.08%, Vd1 40.0%, Vd2 52.54%.

Model II, ara-C (two compartments) with ara-U (one compartment) formed by first-order deamination k13 (Table 2, PDF p. 5, printed 46; confirmed on page image). Basic II-1 estimated k10 = 3.0e-9 /h (about zero), so k10 was dropped (II-2): ara-C leaves the central compartment by deamination. Final II-3: k12 1.16 (0.30) /h, k21 0.08 (0.01) /h, k13 = 1.53 x AGE (0.25) /h (Eq. 7; the intercept of Eq. 6 was about zero), Vd1 1.37 (0.51) L, k30 0.41 (0.06) /h (ara-U t1/2 1.69 h), Vd3 4.98 (1.50) L. II-4 (ara-C alone, ADVAN3/TRANS1): k12 1.37, k21 0.07, k10 = 1.19 x AGE /h, Vd1 2.17 L. Model II has no surface-area term.

Derived (not printed): the Model I parameters all scale with P, so the half-lives are the same at every age: alpha 9.61 /h (t1/2 4.3 min), beta 0.0296 /h (t1/2 23.4 h). The introduction quotes 17 min and 4 h from earlier two-stage analyses (ref 13).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD model, ke0 or effect site. Concentration thresholds quoted from earlier work (introduction, PDF p. 2, printed 43; confirmed on page image), all plasma ara-C peaks: above 300 uM most likely CNS toxicity; below 100 uM none; 101-299 uM about 50% probability of neurotoxicity (ref 20); infants reach 300-2500 uM; one case of 5 mM. A Css of 10-20 uM by LB+CI is called tolerable for 3 days and probably enough to saturate activation to ara-CTP. Not an opioid; no MEAC or potency ratio.

## Covariates
AGE x SA (product) on all four Model I parameters, linear through the origin; AGE alone on k13 in Model II. Sex, weight and organ function were not tested.

## Caveats
- Every parameter is proportional to age, so the model predicts zero clearance and volume at birth and growth without a plateau; the paper does not discuss this.
- Large standard errors (Vd2 34.6 +/- 22.6; Q 1.82 +/- 0.86) and the Results/Table 1 mismatch above.
- Surface-area formula unnamed; LB/CI doses and patient demographics not tabulated; retrospective data from four trials over 12 years with co-administered chemotherapy and TBI.
- Sampling for HDara-C stops at 8-9 h, far short of the derived 23-h terminal half-life, which is an extrapolation.
- The text's CVs do not match Table 1's variances.
