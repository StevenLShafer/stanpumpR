# Karnes 2021 (CPIC 2020 update): CYP2C9 and HLA-B genotypes and phenytoin dosing

## Study
Expert-consensus pharmacogenetic guideline updating CPIC 2014; evidence in supplementary Tables S1-S2 (not read).
Applies to phenytoin and fosphenytoin.

## Model and parameters
No PK model. CYP2C9 activity score (AS): allele values 0 (no function, e.g. *3), 0.5 (decreased, e.g. *2), 1 (normal,
*1), summed per diplotype (p. 303). Table 2 (p. 304):

| Phenotype | AS | Example diplotypes |
|---|---|---|
| Normal metabolizer (NM) | 2 | *1/*1 |
| Intermediate metabolizer (IM) | 1.5 | *1/*2 |
| Intermediate metabolizer (IM) | 1 | *1/*3, *2/*2 |
| Poor metabolizer (PM) | 0.5 | *2/*3 |
| Poor metabolizer (PM) | 0 | *3/*3 |

Change from 2014: *2/*2 (AS 1) is now IM (was PM). No increased-function CYP2C9 alleles exist.

General dosing context (p. 304): adult outpatient start 5-7 mg/kg/day; therapeutic range typically 10-20 mcg/mL;
IV load 15-20 mg/kg at ≤ 1-3 mg/kg/min or 50 mg/min.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Recommendations (Table 3; p. 306-307):
- HLA-B*15:02 positive, phenytoin-naive: do not use phenytoin/fosphenytoin (strong); unless > 3 months prior
  continuous use without cutaneous reaction.
- HLA-B*15:02 negative or unknown, by CYP2C9:
  - NM (AS 2) and IM AS 1.5: no adjustment from typical dosing.
  - IM AS 1.0: typical initial/loading dose; then "~25% less" than typical maintenance (narrative: at least 25%).
  - PM (AS 0.5 or 0): typical initial/loading dose; then "~50% less" maintenance (narrative: at least 50%).
- The guideline says available evidence does not clearly indicate the size of dose reduction needed; the numbers
  are described as conservative.
No exposure-response model, no concentration targets beyond 10-20 mcg/mL. Not an opioid.

## Covariates
CYP2C9 genotype, HLA-B*15:02. CYP2C19 and other genes mentioned but not used for recommendations.

## Caveats
Consensus guideline; reductions are dose guidance, not estimated Vmax ratios. Applies to initiating maintenance
therapy; TDM-guided adjustment thereafter.

- Diplotype → AS: code (R/drugs_phenytoin.R:111-115) gives *1/*1 2, *1/*2 1.5, *1/*3 and *2/*2 1, *2/*3 0.5, *3/*3 0;
  Table 2 identical. **matches**.
- No adjustment at AS 1.5 (R/drugs_phenytoin.R:111-112): Table 3 / p. 306. **matches** for dosing.
- Phenotype label: R/drugs_phenytoin.R:111 calls AS 1.5 "normal", and inst/help/drugs/phenytoin.md:15 says
  "a score of 1.5 is entered as normal" and describes intermediate metabolisers as "activity score 1, the *1/*3
  genotype". CPIC Table 2 classes AS 1.5 (*1/*2) as an **intermediate** metabolizer that needs no adjustment.
  **discrepancy** (terminology only; the multiplier 1.0 is right).
- PM ×0.50 (R/drugs_phenytoin.R:114-117, PHENYTOIN_CYP2C9_VMAX line 170): CPIC "at least 50%" reduction of starting
  maintenance dose. With Michaelis-Menten kinetics the dose for a target C is Vmax·C/(Km + C), so a 50% dose cut at
  fixed Km is exactly Vmax × 0.5. **matches** (guideline-anchored, as the code says).
- AS 1 ×0.67 (Odani 1997): CPIC recommends at least 25% lower maintenance; a 33% Vmax reduction is consistent with
  "at least 25%". **matches** (source is Odani 1997, not CPIC; code attributes it correctly).
- R/constants.R:57-60 and R/drugs_phenytoin.R:148-149, 223: citation and DOI correct. **matches**.
