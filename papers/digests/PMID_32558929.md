# Taylor 2020: MTXPK.org, a three-compartment high-dose methotrexate model in Nordic children

## Study
- Retrospective population PK of high-dose methotrexate (HD MTX) from the NOPHO ALL2000 and ALL2008 protocols (Denmark, Finland, Lithuania, Norway, Sweden), 2002-2014 (PDF p2, printed p636).
- 820 Philadelphia-negative ALL patients screened; 48 patients / 679 concentrations excluded for missing dosing; patients given glucarpidase excluded (DAMPA interferes with immunoassay). Final: **772 patients, 4,986 courses, 31,672 plasma concentrations**, 5,535 of them ≥ 96 h after the start of infusion (PDF p3, printed p637).
- Table 1 (PDF p3, page image): median age 4 y (range 1-18.83); BSA 0.745 m2 (0.4-2.31); weight 17.8 kg (7.2-105); serum creatinine 29 µmol/L (4-155); dose 5 g/m2 (0.6-10.1); 333 female (43%); courses per patient 8 (1-8); Sweden 428 (55%), Denmark 207 (27%), Finland 78 (10%), Norway 59 (8%).
- Dosing: 6-8 courses of 5 or 8 g/m2 as a **24-h IV infusion**, folinic acid rescue at 36 or 42 h; levels at 24, 36 h and every 6 h until ≤ 0.2 µmol/L.
- Assay: EMIT or fluorescence polarization immunoassay (plasma). Creatinine recorded before each infusion and at least daily (time-varying covariate).
- NONMEM 7.2.0, validated with KinPop++; MTXPK.org Bayesian module built in Edsim++ 1.80. Exponential (log-normal) residual error. BSA formula **not stated** in the paper.

## Model and parameters
Three compartments, linear elimination from V1. The 3-compartment base model beat the 2-compartment one by ΔOFV −6,136.43 (PDF p3). All six parameters normalised to 1.73 m2 (Table 2 model 2, "Normalize", i.e. linear in BSA/1.73; this beat normalising CL alone by ΔOFV 2,605.79, PDF p4). Final clearance equation (PDF p5, page image):

CLi = CLpop × (BSA/1.73) × (SCR/29)^−0.247, SCR in µmol/L (29 µmol/L = 0.33 mg/dL, the population median).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No effect compartment, no concentration-effect model. The thresholds discussed (glucarpidase label "> 2 SD above the mean excretion curve" with > 50% creatinine rise; consensus-guideline actionable concentrations; discharge at ≤ 0.2 µmol/L in this protocol) are management criteria, not fitted quantities. Not an opioid: no MEAC, ke0 or potency ratio.

## Covariates
- BSA on all parameters (linear normalisation to 1.73 m2): ΔOFV −2,358.81; weight normalised to 70 kg was weaker (−1,260.02) (Table 2). Figure 3: R2 0.54 for BSA vs CL; weight R2 is given as 0.36 in the text (PDF p4) and 0.25 in the Figure 3 caption (PDF p5) — an internal inconsistency.
- Serum creatinine, time-varying, power on CL normalised to 29 µmol/L, exponent −0.247 (ΔOFV −702.73 on top of BSA).
- Age and sex not significant (Figure S2). Finnish patients had 26% faster clearance than Swedish, Danish and Norwegian patients (Figure S3) — not in the final model.

## Caveats
- Children with ALL on a single protocol family, 24-h infusions only; mostly European; glucarpidase recipients excluded.
- IIV on V1 and Q2 not estimable: the early distribution phase is poorly informed (authors' own limitation).
- No albumin, eGFR, co-medication, SLCO1B1, hydration or urine pH data.
- BSA formula not named; MTXPK.org itself defaults to DuBois (docs/chemotherapy.md:262, from the API, not from this paper).
- Supplement (model-building details, Tables S1-S3 comparing Treviño and Kawakatsu models) not read.
