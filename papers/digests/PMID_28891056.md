# Cho 2017: Minto remifentanil TCI during cardiopulmonary bypass

## Study
Prospective randomized (Ultiva vs Remiva, 1:1, blinded) predictive-performance study, Seoul National University Hospital, Jan-May 2016. ASA 1-3 adults aged 20-80 yr undergoing cardiac surgery with CPB. 61 screened, 56 studied (28 + 28; Table 2's column header prints "n = 29" for Ultiva, a misprint against Table 1's n = 28). Table 1 (PDF p.5, printed p.1200), pooled mean (SD): age 61 (15) yr; 30 (54%) male; height 161.8 (9.9) cm; weight 61.3 (12.3) kg; BMI 23.2 (3.2) kg/m2; BSA 1.7 (0.2) m2; LV EF 58 (8)%; haematocrit 39 (6)%; GFR 75.1 (23.7) ml/min/1.73 m2.

Anesthesia: midazolam 1 mg/kg and sufentanil 1 ug/kg induction (midazolam unit as printed, see Caveats), propofol by Marsh TCI 1-4 ug/ml, remifentanil by **effect-site TCI with the Minto model, 5-15 ng/ml**, titrated to haemodynamics; BIS <60. Moderate hypothermic bypass 23-29 C, non-pulsatile, a-stat; 6 patients (all in the Ultiva group) had hypothermic circulatory arrest (HCA) with selective cerebral perfusion at core <25 C.

Sampling: arterial, before bypass (1 h after induction), 30, 60, 90 min of bypass, and at end of bypass (1 h after protamine); at least 5 min after any target change. 275 samples (138 Ultiva, 137 Remiva); 168 during bypass; 13 under HCA. Assay: LC-MS/MS (API 4000), LOQ 0.1 ng/ml, plasma stabilised with formic acid; intra-assay CV 6.89% (0.1), 7.32% (0.3), 1.87% (15), 2.58% (75 ng/ml); inter-assay 5.10-5.46%. PE = (Cm - Cp)/Cp x 100; acceptable if MDPE within ±20% and MDAPE <30% (Schuttler criterion, their ref 14).

Results (Table 3, PDF p.6, printed p.1202; mean (SD) ng/ml, pooled; predicted | measured): before CPB 8.5 (1.9) | 11.4 (4.7); CPB 30 min 7.1 (0.9) | 9.7 (6.3); 60 min 7.0 (0.5) | 9.9 (6.1); 90 min 7.1 (0.6) | 10.9 (8.6); after CPB 7.7 (1.4) | 8.5 (2.6). Note these are plasma concentrations predicted by the Minto model at effect-site targets of 5-15 ng/ml.

Table 4 (PDF p.8, printed p.1203), median (IQR [range]), MDPE / MDAPE:
- Pooled before CPB (n=52): 23.2% / 26.0%; during CPB (n=168): 22.1% / 22.3%; during CPB excluding HCA (n=155): 21.6% / 21.8%; after CPB (n=55): 8.4% / 18.4%.
- Ultiva during CPB (n=84): 29.4% / 29.4%; Remiva during CPB (n=84): 15.8% / 16.9%.
- Whole surgery pooled (text, PDF p.4): MDPE 21.3%, MDAPE 21.8%; excluding HCA 18.5% / 21.4%. Under HCA (n=13) both 148.4%; measured 26.9 (17.0) vs predicted 7.1 (1.6) ng/ml, against 9.2 (3.2) vs 7.5 (1.3) ng/ml at other times (text, PDF p.4).
- Positive PE means measured above predicted; the Minto model underpredicted during CPB. The authors' comparator for the "bias" is a 2003 non-cardiac study (Mertens), MDPE -15% (95% CI -17 to -12), where Minto overpredicted.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD end point was modelled. **No ke0, t1/2 ke0, tPeak, MEAC or analgesic concentration is reported, and no potency ratio.** Targets were effect-site 5-15 ng/ml (Minto) at BIS <60 under propofol and a sufentanil induction dose; the paper gives no concentration-effect relationship. Plasma remifentanil (LC-MS/MS, total concentration, plasma) is the only measured quantity. Cited, not measured here: remifentanil elimination clearance falls 6.37% per degree C below 37 C (Michelsen 2001, their ref 25); porcine esterase hydrolysis was faster at higher temperature (ref 24).

## Covariates
Group (formulation Ultiva vs Remiva) and HCA considered; no PK covariates analysed. Temperature (hypothermic bypass, mean nasopharyngeal 26.9 C at 30 min, Table 3) is the proposed explanation; haemodilution and esterase activity are discussed without data.

## Caveats
HCA cases were all in the Ultiva arm (randomization did not balance them), so the Ultiva-vs-Remiva difference is confounded; few samples before and after CPB (authors' own caution); bias before CPB (MDPE 23.2%) was as large as during CPB, which the authors attribute partly to un-equilibrated induction; one study site, adults of mean BMI 23; effect-site targets with samples drawn at least 5 min after target changes; Minto PK was derived in healthy volunteers. Induction midazolam is printed as 1 mg/kg (and vecuronium 1 mg/kg) on the rendered Methods page (PDF p.2, printed p.1197), implausibly large for a per-kg dose; probably a misprint for per-patient doses, and irrelevant to the library.

## Verification against stanpumpR
Not cited, so no transcription to verify. Comparison with the library: `R/drugs_remifentanil.R` runs Eleveld 2017 (BMI <30 and children) or Kim 2017 (adult BMI >=30) kinetics, with tPeak 1.6 min and MEAC 1 ng/ml (lines 116-117); Minto 1997 is the companion model `remifentanilMinto` (R/alternativeModels.R:261, selectable at line 94). The paper's finding applies to Minto in CPB and HCA, not to Eleveld/Kim, and the library has no CPB or temperature term, so the paper identifies a situation the library does not model. Pregnancy factor and other covariates are unrelated. No numeric value in the library depends on this paper.
