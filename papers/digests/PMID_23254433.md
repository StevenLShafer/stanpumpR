# Leeds 2013: tecovirimat (ST-246) PK/PD and human dose selection under the animal rule

## Study
- Sponsor analysis (SIGA Technologies with Pharsight). Population PK in Phoenix NLME 6.2.0.416, FOCE-ELS
  (PDF p. 2, printed p. 1137). Beal M6 for BLQ data.
- **NHPs (cynomolgus)**: six studies; study 1 uninfected PK (0.3-30 mg/kg PO QD), study 2 PK before and
  after i.v. monkeypox virus (MPXV, ~5 x 10^7 PFU) at 3/10/20 mg/kg; studies 3-6 efficacy (survival) with
  0.3-300 mg/kg/day for 14 days started day 3, 4 or 5 post-infection (PDF p. 3). 96 NHPs in the survival
  analysis (23 placebo); most treated animals male (n = 82). 1,558 plasma concentrations in the NHP model.
- **Humans**: one double-blind placebo-controlled trial (ref. 23, Chinsangaram 2012): 107 healthy volunteers,
  400 mg (45), 600 mg (46) or placebo (16) PO once daily for 14 days, non-fasted; age 19-74, roughly equal
  sexes; rich sampling days 1 and 14 plus sparse days 2-13 (PDF p. 4). 1,395 concentrations in the human
  model. Mean weight 78.4 kg.
- Assay: LC-MS/MS on plasma; range 5.00-2,000 ng/mL (NHP) and 50.0-4,000 ng/mL (human); LLOQ 5.0 / 50 ng/mL
  (PDF p. 3). Protein precipitation for infected samples, liquid-liquid extraction for uninfected.

## Model and parameters
Two-compartment, first-order absorption with lag time, allometric weight scaling (0.75 clearances,
1 volumes; reference 78.4 kg humans, 3.105 kg NHPs). All parameters are apparent (oral, /F). Table 1,
PDF p. 4 (printed p. 1139), confirmed on the rendered page.

NHP (Table 1; dose = mg/kg; wt in kg):
- ka 0.868 x (dose/10)^0.160 /h infected, 0.586 x (dose/10)^0.160 uninfected (IIV 11%, IOV 32%).
- Tlag 0.302 h.
- CL/F 2.809 x (wt/3.105)^0.75 x (dose/10)^0.093 L/h infected, 2.827 x (same) uninfected (IIV 31%, IOV 14%).
- Vc/F 20.054 x (wt/3.105)^1 x (dose/10)^0.623 L (IIV 47%); Q/F 3.244 x (wt/3.105)^0.75 L/h (IIV 75%);
  Vp/F 13.34 x (wt/3.105) L (IIV 55%). Error: additive 0.133 ug/L, proportional 30%.
- Single-dose dose-normalised AUC uninfected vs infected NHPs 1.15 vs 0.9 mg·h/L/(mg/kg), P = 0.025, n = 18/group
  (non-compartmental); no significant difference at steady state (PDF p. 4).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: survival of MPXV-infected NHPs (not a concentration-effect model). No ke0.
- Lowest efficacious NHP dose 3 mg/kg/day (survival 92.9%, 13/14; 10 mg/kg 96%, 24/25); 0.3 mg/kg 80% and
  1 mg/kg 100% death; placebo 100% death, median time to death 14 days (PDF p. 5-6, Fig. 2).
- ROC cutoffs (PDF p. 6, rendered): AUC/wt 0.50 mg·h/(L·kg) (Fig. 3 legend prints "mg · h/(liters/kg)"),
  sensitivity 92.86%, specificity 97.44%, n = 67; Cmin after first dose 21.63 ug/L; Cmin at steady state
  21.22 ug/L. Excluding the two deaths at >= 3 mg/kg, survival was 100% above 0.5 mg·h/(L·kg) and Cmin 40 ug/L.
  The Fig. 3 tree itself shows the split on dose (< 3 mg/kg), not on exposure.
- Bridging: 3 mg/kg NHP predicted equivalent to 100 mg in a 78.4 kg infected human; 10 mg/kg NHP about
  equivalent to 400 mg. Mean steady-state AUCinf in NHPs at 3 mg/kg 5,370 h·ng/mL; every human at 400 or
  600 mg exceeded it (Fig. 4B, PDF p. 7). Selected human dose 600 mg (predicted > 95% of population above
  the NHP 3 mg/kg median exposure).
- Not an opioid; no MEAC, tPeak or potency ratio.

## Covariates
Humans: weight (allometric, above) and sex on Vc/F only; sex had no clinically relevant effect on exposure
or half-life. NHPs: weight; infection on ka and CL/F; dose (mg/kg) on ka, CL/F, Vc/F. Age, race, BMI tested
(PDF p. 2).

## Caveats
- Human model in healthy volunteers only; infection effect taken from NHPs and assumed to apply to humans.
- The comparative "apparent clearance slightly higher in infected NHPs" (PDF p. 4) is a single-dose
  non-compartmental comparison within NHPs (uninfected vs infected); the final model's typical CL/F is in fact
  marginally lower infected (2.809 vs 2.827), while infected ka is higher.
- Two different extraction methods for infected vs uninfected samples. Sponsor-authored. Outlier exclusions
  in the clinical data (high troughs, double peaks) are only partly quantified.
- Survival analysis pooled 5 studies with different start days; small groups (3 per arm at 30 and 100 mg/kg).
