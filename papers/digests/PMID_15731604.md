# Hu 2005: TCI variability is bounded by bolus variability

## Study
Linear-systems proof plus simulation plus re-analysis of old rat data. Assumptions: linear, time-invariant PK
(superposition; any infusion = sum of infinitesimal boluses), every patient receives the same dosing function.
Simulation (Excel): Schnider 1998 propofol PK for a 50 y, 70 kg, 170 cm man (V1 4.27 L, V2 20.07 L, V3 238 L,
Cl1 1.64, Cl2 1.36, Cl3 0.84 L/min), 1,000 individuals with volumes and clearances varying 30% (log-normal);
regimens: 10 mg bolus, 10 mg/min infusion, plasma TCI at 1 ug/mL. Experimental: thiopental in rats (Gustafsson
1992): bolus n = 9, first TCI n = 7, refined TCI n = 8.

## Model and parameters
- Theorem 1 (eq 7/17): with M = max over time of the CV of the unit disposition functions, the CV of
  concentrations under any dosing regimen I(t) satisfies CV{U*I} <= M (for t up to the last bolus). Proof via
  the lemma sigma(sum x) <= sum sigma(x) (Appendix eq A9).
- The bound is a maximum over the same time period: e.g. variability in the first 10 min of TCI is bounded by the
  maximum variability in the first 10 min after a bolus (Discussion).
- Simulation result: bolus CV about twice that of infusion or TCI; infusion and TCI CVs indistinguishable (Fig 2).
- Rat thiopental: average CV 31% after bolus; 11% first TCI (persistent overshoot); 18% refined TCI (Fig 3).
- Table 1 toy example ("Duzitol"): two boluses 10 min apart have lower maximum CV than one bolus.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None; concentrations only. Discussion: TCI target is effectively the dose; target-response relations are
dose-response relations. Not an opioid paper: no MEAC, ke0 or potency.

## Covariates
Not modelled; argues TCI can reduce variability by incorporating covariates.

## Caveats
Ignores assay error, pump mechanical accuracy and software fidelity; applies only to linear time-invariant PK;
the bound is "less than or equal", and is relative to the maximum CV after a bolus, not the CV at each time.
Rat data from a single older study. Author SLS consults for TCI device companies (disclosed).
