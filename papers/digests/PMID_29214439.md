# Yoneyama 2018: emicizumab PopPK and repeated time-to-event model, phase III dose selection

## Study
Pooled analysis of Japanese and Caucasian healthy male volunteers (24 Japanese at 0.01, 0.1, 0.3 or 1 mg/kg single SC; 18 Caucasian at 0.1, 0.3 or 1 mg/kg; 0.001 mg/kg group excluded, all below the 0.05 mcg/mL quantitation limit) plus 18 Japanese male adult or adolescent patients with severe hemophilia A, with or without FVIII inhibitors, given weekly SC emicizumab in three cohorts (0.3, 1, 3 mg/kg QW; loading doses 1 and 3 mg/kg in cohorts 1 and 2) (Methods 2.1, Table 1, PDF p.3-4). Healthy-volunteer data complete; patient interim data to November 2014 (later data to February 2016 used only for validation). Patient age 12-58 y, weight 33-66 kg medians (Table 1, text layer only). NONMEM 7.2.0, FOCE-I for PopPK, Laplacian for the RTTE model.

Derived typical patient values (derived): CL/F = 0.222 x 1.26 = 0.280 L/day and Vd/F = 10.2 x 1.19 = 12.1 L at 70 kg, NAb-negative.
RTTE (Eqs. 3-6): hazard = lambda x (1 - (C/EC50 + PLX x thetaPLX)/(1 + C/EC50 + PLX x thetaPLX)), repeated events, each reset at the last event.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: time to repeated bleeding events (annualized bleeding rate) against plasma emicizumab concentration; aPTT and thrombin generation showed no relation to bleeding because they plateau at low concentrations (Results 3.1, PDF p.5). No ke0, tPeak or effect-site model, no hysteresis analysis (concentration is used directly). EC50 1.19 mcg/mL (Table 3) is for the hazard model with a very wide CI and 24.7% shrinkage on its IIV.
Target efficacious exposure: simulated constant plasma concentration of 45 mcg/mL gives zero annual bleeding in at least 50% of patients; simulated median ABR without treatment 22 (Results 3.4, Fig. 4, PDF p.6). The authors say 45 mcg/mL is not a threshold for each patient (Discussion, PDF p.7). Steady-state peak concentrations remain below 120 mcg/mL, the observed mean trough at 3 mg/kg QW (Results 3.5). Equivalent FVIII activity conversion quoted from ref. 18: at least 0.3 IU/dL per mcg/mL, so 1.5 mg/kg QW is at least 15 IU/dL (Discussion).
Selected phase III regimens: 3 mg/kg QW for 4 weeks, then 1.5 mg/kg QW, 3 mg/kg Q2W or 6 mg/kg Q4W. No opioid content; no MEAC.

## Covariates
Body weight on CL/F (exponent 0.75, fixed) and on Vd/F (exponent 1, fixed); NAb positivity on CL/F (time-varying onset 33.4 d); subject type (healthy vs patient). Age (12 y and over) and FVIII inhibitor status were not significant on PK or on EC50.

## Caveats
Small n (18 patients); dose-ascending, non-randomized with imbalanced baseline; the 45 mcg/mL target depends on a flat, poorly bounded exposure-response (EC50 CI 0.308-3.77, 24.7% shrinkage); the prophylaxis-effect estimate is less than published effect sizes (authors); simulations ignored NAbs and discontinuation; 6 mg/kg Q4W extrapolates linear PK beyond 3 mg/kg; paediatric dosing out of scope (Discussion, PDF p.8).

## Verification against stanpumpR
Comparison with `R/drugs_emicizumab.R:81-93` (Retout 2020: CL/F 0.272 L/day, V/F 10.4 L, KA 0.536/day, CL weight exponent 0.911, V exponent 1.00, 70 kg reference):
- CL/F: Yoneyama 0.222 L/day (healthy) or 0.280 (patient, derived) vs library 0.272: library is 3% below the patient value and 23% above the healthy-volunteer value. Different populations (Yoneyama pooled 24+18 healthy and 18 Japanese patients; Retout 389 patients from HAVEN 1-4 plus the Japanese study). No disagreement worth a flag.
- V/F: 10.2 (healthy) or 12.1 L (patient, derived) vs library 10.4: library is 14% below the patient value.
- Absorption: t1/2,abs 1.56 d (ka 0.444/day, derived) vs library ka 0.536/day (t1/2 1.29 d); Retout's own individual-mean half-life 1.61 d per the library header. Agrees with the library header's statement that a mean of individual half-lives exceeds the typical value.
- Weight exponent on CL/F: Yoneyama fixed 0.75 (a priori), Retout fitted 0.911 (library). Over the library's ranges this gives different size scaling for very light or heavy people; not a discrepancy, Retout is the fitted value.
- Elimination half-life: Yoneyama 30.1 d (patients), 31.8 d (healthy) vs library typical ln2 x 10.4/0.272 = 26.5 d (library header) and the label 26.9 d. Yoneyama's half-life is 14-20% longer than the library's (derived), from the smaller CL/F (healthy) or larger V/F (patients) in a small sample; the library's shorter value agrees with the label, so no error is implied.
- Concentration target 45 mcg/mL: the library plots concentration only and states no therapeutic range (`inst/extdata/therapeuticRanges.csv`, emicizumab row: `none_established`, with the 51.2/46.9/38.5 mcg/mL label troughs as the only numbers). Yoneyama's 45 mcg/mL is a simulation-based target for phase III dose selection, not a measured therapeutic range; do not copy it into the table as one. Retout 2020 found a flat exposure-bleeding relation at the exposures reached (per that row).
