# Tsuchiya 2017: omarigliptin single and multiple dose PK/PD in healthy Japanese men

## Study
Two-part, double-blind, randomized, placebo-controlled, single-site study (Protocol 005) in healthy Japanese men aged 18-45 y, BMI 18-30 kg/m2, creatinine clearance (Cockcroft-Gault) > 80 mL/min (p2). Weight not reported beyond the BMI range. Part 1: 16 men in two panels of 8 (6 active, 2 placebo), single rising doses 5, 25, 100 mg (panel A) and 10, 50 mg (panel B), fasted, with 2 week washout; panel B period 3 gave 10 mg after a 411 kcal Japanese breakfast (food effect). Part 2: 32 men, four panels of 8 (6 active), 1, 10, 25 or 50 mg once weekly x 3 (days 1, 8, 15), fasted. Plasma (K2EDTA, frozen -20 C) assayed by high-turbulence LC online extraction, LLOQ 1.0 ng/mL plasma and 40 ng/mL urine. Urine collected over 168 h after the day 15 50 mg dose. Plasma DPP-4 activity: substrate glycyl-prolyl-p-nitroanilide, absorbance at 390 nm, LOQ 0.6 mOD/min. Noncompartmental PK; mixed-effects models on log scale.

## Model and parameters
No compartmental model fitted. Noncompartmental results, Table 1 (PDF p4, printed p87). Concentrations in nmol/L, AUC in umol/L*h. Single-dose values are back-transformed LS means; Tmax median; t1/2 geometric mean (%CV).

Terminal half-life is >100 h for the long beta phase (p3, p8; the abstract says so), but the reported geometric mean t1/2 values in Table 1 range 33-144 h because sampling windows differ (authors explain the fed/fasted 10 mg difference by sampling to 336 h vs 240 h). Dose proportionality: AUC and Cmax about proportional; C168h less than proportional (p3). Steady state reached after the first dose.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: percent inhibition of plasma DPP-4 activity from baseline (not an opioid; no MEAC, ke0, t1/2ke0 or tPeak is reported and none was fitted; no hysteresis analysis).
- 168 h post single dose (Table 2, PDF p6, printed p90): LS mean % inhibition 79.20 (5 mg), 77.39 (10 mg fasted), 73.90 (10 mg fed), 85.97 (25 mg), 86.76 (50 mg), 94.01 (100 mg). Placebo -8.69 (n=4, period 1 data excluded for carryover).
- Day 15, 168 h after the third weekly dose: 51.33 (1 mg), 83.26 (10 mg), 87.52 (25 mg), 90.23 (50 mg); placebo 8.85.
- Inhibition above 80% was reached rapidly and held for 168 h at 25 mg and above (p3, Fig 2).
- PK/PD relation: Emax model, Emax 96.5% inhibition, EC80 11.4 nmol/L (p3, from single-dose data per Supplement Fig S2/Table S3; the supplement is not in this PDF). EC50 and Hill slope are not given in the main text.
- The Discussion says 80% DPP-4 inhibition correlates with maximal glycemic efficacy (citing ref 11, Krishna 2016).
- No potency ratio.

## Covariates
None modelled. Creatinine clearance was measured only for the 50 mg group (6.9 L/h, 115 mL/min). Weight not reported.

## Caveats
Healthy Japanese men only, 6 active per dose, n=4-5 at some 168 h points (Table 1 footnote). Fed arm sampled longer than fasted (to 336 h vs 240 h), so t1/2 is not comparable between them. Free-fraction figures are unpublished. Supplementary material (Emax parameter table, food effect table) not available here.

## Verification against stanpumpR
No implemented model uses this paper, so there is nothing to verify (verdict not-applicable). As an independent check on the registry's unimplemented Jain model: unbound CL/F e^1.1 = 3.0 L/h (x (WT/76)^0.75 x eGFR term) at 76 kg; this paper's 50 mg weekly steady-state data give total apparent CL about 3.0 L/h (derived: dose / AUC0-168h, MW 398.4 g/mol assumed, not stated in the paper) and about 3.1 L/h from CLr/fe. With free fraction 0.56-0.76 an unbound CL/F would be 1.3-1.8x larger, so either Jain's figure is total clearance or the models differ; see the `[source]` question. Registry text "Complete, not implemented" is consistent with the paper's PK being the same order of magnitude: Cmax of 1.3 umol/L at 50 mg and a terminal half-life above 100 h fit a two-compartment drug with slow redistribution.
