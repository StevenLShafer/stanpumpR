# Lim 2023: vilobelimab and C5a concentrations in PANAMO

## Study
Phase 3 PANAMO (NCT04333420), double-blind, randomised, placebo-controlled, 46 hospitals; adults, invasively ventilated, COVID-19, P/F 60-200 mm Hg. 368 randomised (177 vilobelimab, 191 placebo; abstract and PDF p2). PK sampling only at Western European sites (NL, FR, DE, BE). PK analysis set n = 197 (96 vilobelimab, 101 placebo) (Table 1, PDF p4); drug samples available for 93 and 99 patients, C5a for 94 and 99.
Dose: vilobelimab 800 mg IV (fixed, not per kg) on days 1, 2, 4, 8, 15, 22 while in ICU/hospital (PDF p3).
Samples: pre-infusion at screening, day 8, and hospital discharge only. Assay: ELISA for vilobelimab (trough, plasma) and C5a (menal GmbH); ADA by MSD electrochemiluminescence bridging assay in serum. Statistics descriptive (SAS 9.4); no PK modelling, no NONMEM.
Population (Table 1): age mean 61.4 (SD 12.3) y total, 73.1% male; BMI mean 30.5 kg/m2; eGFR <60 in 31.0%. Body weight not reported.

## Model and parameters
No structural PK model, no CL, V, t1/2 or Ka reported. Only observed concentrations (Table 2, PDF p8, ng/mL).

Hospital-discharge vilobelimab geometric means by days since discharge window (Table 2, text layer): day 1-10 185,361 (n=3); day 11-20 130,592 (n=11); day 21-30 91,086 (n=10); day 31-40 16,579 (n=4); after day 40 493 (n=3). Time since last infusion is plotted in Fig. 3 (PDF p7), not tabulated.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
PD end point is plasma C5a (ng/mL), not clinical. No EC50, Emax, ke0 or hysteresis fitted; Fig. 4 (PDF p9) is a scatter of C5a against vilobelimab, no model. Authors state drug levels were sufficient for complete C5a suppression (day 8 C5a down 87% vs 11% rise on placebo, Wilcoxon p<0.001). C5a began to rise after end of treatment for patients discharged after day 30 but stayed below baseline. No MEAC or potency ratio. ADAs: one treatment-emergent case on vilobelimab (day 40) and one on placebo (day 25).

## Covariates
None modelled. Baseline C5a showed no association with P/F ratio, ARDS severity, WHO scale, mortality, eGFR or thrombosis. Day-8 C5a vs CRP Spearman 0.56 (vilobelimab arm) and 0.32 (placebo).

## Caveats
Trough samples only, three time points, sparse after day 8; Western Europe only; 5 vilobelimab and 1 placebo values excluded for timing or implausibility; values above ULOQ set to ULOQ and below LLOQ set to zero; sponsor-affiliated authors; day-8 "mean concentrations ranged" wording ambiguous. Table 1 text layer is scrambled (placebo column values interleaved with other rows). Total vs free drug assay not stated.
