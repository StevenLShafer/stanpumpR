# Kitchen 1997: pediatric phase I of continuous-infusion thioguanine

## Study
Phase I, pharmacokinetically guided dose escalation. 18 patients 4-25 y (median 18), 12 M / 6 F, refractory cancers (16 solid tumours, 2 ALL; Table 1, PDF p. 3). Stage 1: TG 10 mg/m2/h for 24 h (start), escalated to 20 mg/m2/h to reach Css > 1 uM (the in vitro maximally cytotoxic concentration). Stage 2: duration increased in 12-h steps at 20 mg/m2/h. TG given as sodium salt (reconstituted vials, 75 mg). Blood (heparinised) at 3 and 6 h, end of infusion, then 0.25, 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12 h after; plasma assayed by reverse-phase HPLC (SPE extraction, UV 320/340 nm, MP riboside internal standard). PK sampling in 17 patients: 4 at 10 mg/m2/h x 24 h; 13 at 20 mg/m2/h (9 for 24 h, 4 for 36 h) (Results). Css = concentration in the last hour of infusion. Model fitted with MLAB simultaneously to the 24-h data from both dose rates.

## Model and parameters
Two compartments, Michaelis-Menten elimination from the central compartment (Fig. 1, PDF p. 3):
dCc/dt = k0/Vc - Vm Cc/(Km + Cc) - kcp Cc + kpc Xp/Vc; dXp/dt = kcp Vc Cc - kpc Xp. Parameters (mean +/- SD as printed, Results, printed p. 715):

```yaml
  - {name: Vm, value: 3.7, unit: uM/h, source: "Results p. 715", note: "+/- 1.1; a concentration rate, so Vmax = Vm x Vc"}
  - {name: Km, value: 2.5, unit: uM, source: "Results p. 715", note: "+/- 0.5"}
  - {name: kcp, value: 0.42, unit: 1/h, source: "Results p. 715", note: "+/- 0.23"}
  - {name: kpc, value: 0.29, unit: 1/h, source: "Results p. 715", note: "+/- 0.08"}
  - {name: CL_at_10mg_m2_h, value: 987, unit: mL/min/m2, source: "Results p. 715", note: "computed by the authors from the fitted model; the Css used is not stated (open question)"}
  - {name: CL_at_20mg_m2_h, value: 608, unit: mL/min/m2, source: "Results p. 715", note: "computed by the authors from the fitted model; the Css used is not stated (open question)"}
```
Clearance formula given: Cl = Vm x Vc / (Km + Css) (Methods). Css at 10 mg/m2/h: 0.7, 0.9, 1.2 uM (n = 3; mean 0.9, range 0.7-1.2); at 20 mg/m2/h mean 4.1 uM (range 1.0-8.3). A doubled rate gave about 4-fold Css, so elimination is saturable. An unidentified metabolite eluted at 6.5 min. Discussion: adult bolus data show CL > 2000 mL/min/m2 at 30-65 mg/m2 (ref 22) vs 320 at up to 1200 mg/m2 (ref 16). The authors warn parameters came from a narrow dose range. Units: concentrations in uM (not mass); body-size basis is m2 (no BSA formula named).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD model. Target: Css > 1 uM, chosen as the in vitro concentration giving maximum cytotoxicity in human leukaemic cell lines (ref 5), not measured here. Total plasma TG (plasma, HPLC; free vs bound not stated). Toxicity (not an effect-site model): dose-limiting myelosuppression (AGC < 500/mm3 or platelets < 25,000/mm3) in 2 of 3 evaluable at 20 mg/m2/h x 36 h (Table 2, median AGC nadir 490/mm3, platelets 48,000/mm3); none at 24 h (n = 6 evaluable). Recommended: 20 mg/m2/h for 24 h. No responses (3 stable disease of 17 evaluable). No MEAC, ke0, tPeak, hysteresis or potency ratio. Not an opioid.

## Covariates
None analysed beyond BSA normalisation. Dose-dependent clearance (nonlinearity) is the finding.

## Caveats
Small n (13 in the 24-h fit; 17 with PK), 16 of 18 with solid tumours, wide Css range (1.0-8.3 uM). Fig. 2 shows the model underpredicting the early (3 and 6 h) 20 mg/m2/h means. Parameters estimated from two dose rates only; extrapolation cautioned. Plasma TG, not intracellular thioguanine nucleotides, was measured. Metabolite unidentified.
