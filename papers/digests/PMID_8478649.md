# Cicman 1993: Anesthesia systems, Part II (Narkomed components)

## Study
Equipment-education article from the Department of Education, North American Dräger. It describes, for Narkomed
anesthesia machines: the Dräger 19.n vaporizer (concentration dial, on/off switch that opens above 0.2%,
temperature-compensating bypass, pressure compensator, wick, control cone) (PDF p1-2); the electronic anesthesia
ventilator AV-E (a time-cycled, pneumatically driven double-circuit controller; four phases: inspiratory flow, inspiratory pause,
expiratory flow, expiratory pause) (p3-5); the effect of rate, flow control and I:E ratio on the pressure waveform
(p6); the pressure limit control (p6-7); and the three built-in monitors for oxygen (electrolytic cell), airway
pressure (transducer) and exhaled volume (Spiromed rotor sensor with Hall-effect pickups) (p7-8).
No patients, no drugs, no pharmacokinetic or pharmacodynamic data.

## Model and parameters
None. Engineering numbers only, none a drug parameter: drive gas about 12.5 L/min O2 entraining room air to
about 82 L/min at about 33% O2 at the medium flow setting; end-expiratory pressure of about 2 cm H2O from a 2.4-g ball valve (p3-5).
