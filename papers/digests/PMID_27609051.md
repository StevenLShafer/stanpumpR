# Jullien 2017: micafungin population PK in ICU patients

## Study
- EMPIRICUS trial ancillary PK study, 19 French ICUs. Adults (>18 y) with ICU stay >5 days, mechanical ventilation >4 days, SIRS, Candida colonisation and broad-spectrum antibacterials; randomised 1:1 to micafungin 100 mg daily (1-h infusion, 14 days) or placebo. 100 micafungin-arm patients recruited, 99 analysed (one excluded for unrecorded sampling times).
- Table 1 (text layer, columns partly scrambled): 66 men, 33 women; median age 61.4 y (29.9-92.7); median body weight 84.5 kg (48-141); SOFA score median 7 (range 0-19 as read). 19 patients on intermittent haemodialysis and 11 on ECMO on the PK day.
- 5 plasma samples per patient after the FIRST dose (end of infusion, 30-60 min after, 2-4 h, 8-16 h, and before dose 2). 436 concentrations, range 0.31-17.8 mg/L, none below the LOQ. Plasma (heparin), HPLC with fluorescence detection, LOQ 0.2 mg/L, total drug (free fraction not measured).
- NONMEM 7.3, FOCE-I; VPC (500 sims), bootstrap (1000), NPDE. Monte Carlo PTA: 1000 simulated patients per dose (100-300 mg)/BW (50-120 kg)/SOFA (<10, >=10)/albumin (<=25, >25 g/L)/MIC cell.

## Model and parameters
Two compartments, linear elimination from the central compartment, proportional residual error. Typical values are for an 84 kg patient (the BW reference in the equations), SOFA <10 and albumin >25 g/L (abstract and PDF p3-4, final covariate model equations).

Final model (PDF p3): CL (L/h) = 1.34 x (BW/84)^0.59 x 1.14 (if albumin <=25 g/L) x 0.75 (if SOFA >=10); Vc = 11.8 x (BW/84)^0.61 x 1.14 (albumin <=25); Vp = 7.68 x (BW/84)^0.67 x 1.14 (albumin <=25); Q = 4.67. Eta shrinkage 4.0% (CL), 6.5% (Vc), 30.0% (Vp), 58.4% (Q); epsilon shrinkage 28.9%; no covariance between etas estimable.

Table 3 (mean AUC, mg.h/L, 84 kg, 100 mg dose): SOFA >=10: 87.3 (albumin <=25), 99.5 (>25); SOFA <10: 65.5 (<=25), 74.6 (>25). Comparators quoted from the literature: ICU 78 (Lempers), non-ICU 100, healthy 134.5. (derived check: 100 mg / (1.34 x 0.75) = 99.5 and 100 / 1.34 = 74.6 mg.h/L, consistent with the table.)

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No concentration-effect model. Efficacy target is the previously published AUC/MIC threshold (Andes 2011, ref 8): >5000 for non-parapsilosis Candida (C. albicans, C. glabrata) and >=285 for C. parapsilosis. Results (text pp. 4-7): for C. albicans / C. glabrata, PTA >=90% for MIC below 0.004 / 0.0075 mg/L at any BW and SOFA; at MIC >=0.015-0.016 mg/L, doses of 150-300 mg were needed. For C. parapsilosis at MIC 2 mg/L (I/R breakpoint) PTA was 0% at 100 mg and at best 15% at 300 mg; PTA >90% at 100 mg only for MIC <=0.125 mg/L. No MEAC, ke0, tPeak or potency ratio (not an opioid).

## Covariates
BW (allometric, exponents estimated), albumin <=25 g/L (cutoff chosen by objective function; +14% on CL, Vc, Vp), SOFA >=10 (-25% on CL). Tested and not retained: age, sex, SGOT, SGPT, ALP, bilirubin, prothrombin time, total protein, haemodialysis, ECMO. Bilirubin and the liver tests were not individually significant.

## Caveats
- Total, not free, concentration; micafungin is 99.85% albumin-bound, so the albumin effect on total CL may not change free exposure (authors' argument).
- Only first-dose data; the authors argue linear PK allows steady-state prediction, supported by agreement with Lempers (CL 1.47 vs 1.5 L/h).
- Eta shrinkage for Q is 58%; Table 2 RSD columns were not read on a page image.
- The "lower exposure" claim compares with healthy and non-ICU AUC values from other studies (Table 3), not with a library model.
