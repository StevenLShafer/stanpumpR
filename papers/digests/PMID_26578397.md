# Joosten 2015: closed-loop goal-directed fluid therapy review

## Study
Narrative review of perioperative goal-directed therapy (GDT) for fluids and of a closed-loop fluid-bolus controller (UC Irvine/Brussels) driven by an EV-1000 cardiac output monitor. Fluid management only; no drug pharmacokinetics or pharmacodynamics.

Figures quoted from the review (secondary statements, not original data):
- About 240 million anaesthetics per year worldwide; about 10% (24 million) are high-risk and account for more than 80% of perioperative mortality (PDF p. 1).
- Moderate-risk surgery is about 40% of procedures (96 million patients), with about 30% having a minor complication (p. 1).
- EUSOS mortality 4% (p. 2).
- Pulse pressure variation (PPV) gray zone 9-13%, affecting about 25% of patients under general anaesthesia; tidal volume of 8 mL/kg is generally required for PPV validity (p. 3).
- Closed-loop success was defined as more than 85% of case time in a preload-independent state (PPV < 13% and/or cardiac index > 2.5 L/min/m2) (p. 5).
