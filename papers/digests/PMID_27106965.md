# Hannivoort 2016: PTOL and NSRI for sevoflurane, propofol, remifentanil

PDF page = printed page minus 623 (p1 = printed 624, p3 = 626).

## Study
- Re-analysis of pooled raw data from three earlier crisscross response-surface studies (n = 120, 521 laryngoscopy observations): propofol-remifentanil (Bouillon 2004, n = 20, 95 obs), sevoflurane-propofol (Schumacher 2009, n = 60, 274 obs; PMID_18633028), sevoflurane-remifentanil (Heyse 2012, n = 40, 152 obs). Counts from Table 1, PDF p3.
- Mean weight 69 / 66 / 64 kg, age 34 / 30 / 26 yr, F/M 10/10, 33/27, 26/14 (p4).
- Propofol and remifentanil by TCI (Schnider; Minto) at effect-site targets (Bouillon used plasma targets, 15 min equilibration; the other two used effect-site, 12 min). Sevoflurane titrated to end-tidal. Only the response to laryngoscopy (TOL, movement vs none) used. No patient received all three drugs.
- NONMEM 7.2 (Laplace), naive pooling (no inter-individual variability), bootstrap 2000 sets for 95% CIs, objective-function drop 3.84 for significance. Prediction probability (PK, Smith) with two-fold cross-validation.

## Model and parameters
Eq 1: PTOL = U^g/(1+U^g). Eq 2: U = (CeSEVO/Ce50SEVO + CePROP/Ce50PROP) x (1 + (CeREMI/Ce50REMI)^go). The pooled data supported g common to both hypnotics, Ce50REMI common, and go = 1, so the final model (Eq 8, "reduced Greco") is U = (CeSEVO/Ce50SEVO + CePROP/Ce50PROP) x (1 + CeREMI/Ce50REMI). NSRI = 100 / (1 + (PTOL/(1-PTOL))^s), s = 0.63093 (Eq 7; the text layer shows the exponent garbled, so s and the form are from the text and not image-confirmed).

Validation (p4, text layer): mean absolute prediction error of PTOL for the final model 1.8 / 2.3 / 3.9% (propofol-remifentanil: published / reanalysis / pooled), 14.6 / 6.8 / 6.9% (sevoflurane-propofol), 5.3 / 3.0 / 4.1% (sevoflurane-remifentanil). Prediction probability for tolerance (Table 2, p630, text layer): PTOL/NSRI 0.92 (0.90-0.94) in all 120; CeSEVO 0.73, CePROP 0.46, CeREMI 0.67, BIS 0.71; in the sevoflurane-remifentanil subgroup (n = 40) PTOL/NSRI 0.95.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: tolerance of laryngoscopy (absence of movement), quantal; 50% of the population tolerating = Ce50. This is a population probability, not an individual threshold.
- Opioid (remifentanil) audit: no MEAC or analgesic concentration. Ce50REMI = 1.36 ng/mL (Table 1, pooled) is the effect-site remifentanil concentration that halves the Ce50 of sevoflurane and propofol (doubles U). It is an interaction parameter on the laryngoscopy end point, not a concentration that gives analgesia. Concentrations are Minto-model-predicted effect-site values (targets), with Schnider/Minto accuracy referenced to earlier blood sampling in the propofol-remifentanil study (p625); assay not applicable here (total vs free, blood vs plasma not stated).
- No ke0, t1/2ke0 or tPeak is estimated. Equilibration of 12 or 15 min was used (p625). No hysteresis analysis.
- Potency ratio: sevoflurane vs propofol, 0.342 vol% per ug/mL (pooled, Table 1). Worked example (p629): CeREMI 3 ng/mL with CePROP 3 ug/mL or CeSEVO 1.03 vol% gives PTOL 0.7 and NSRI 31, called equipotent. (Derived check with pooled values: U = 1.27, PTOL = 0.78, NSRI = 31.3; the printed 0.7 is a rounding of 0.78 and the NSRI 31 agrees.)
- Remifentanil interacts strongly synergistically and equally with both hypnotics; sevoflurane and propofol interact additively (Abstract, p4-5).
- Other predictors: BIS, SE, RE moderate (about 0.7-0.78); composite variability index 0.74; surgical pleth index 0.57 (n = 40, Table 2, text layer).

## Covariates
None. Population is adults of 50-120 kg, age 18-58.

## Caveats
- No volunteer received all three drugs, so a triple interaction is neither detected nor excluded (p630).
- Predicted (not measured) propofol and remifentanil effect-site concentrations; sevoflurane end-tidal.
- Naive pooling: no inter-individual variability, so the slope (5.22) is shallower than an IIV-based fit; PTOL describes a population.
- Laryngoscopy only; the authors say clinical use needs a prospective study with surgical stimuli.
- The sevoflurane-propofol data had a maximum U of only 1.56 without remifentanil, giving a larger prediction error (14.6% for the published model).
