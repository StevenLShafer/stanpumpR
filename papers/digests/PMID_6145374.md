# Dailey 1984: Vecuronium and pancuronium pharmacokinetics at caesarean section

## Study
18 healthy women at term having caesarean section under general anaesthesia (previous section, breech/transverse lie,
failure to progress, CPD; fetal distress excluded); vecuronium n = 11, pancuronium n = 7 (allocation method not stated).
Vecuronium group 26.5 +/- 1.6 yr, **73.3 +/- 4.2 kg**, 39.9 +/- 0.4 weeks; pancuronium 28.1 +/- 3.2 yr,
74.1 +/- 4.3 kg, 40.0 +/- 0.4 weeks (Table 1, PDF p.2, mean +/- SE). Glycopyrrolate and antacid premedication;
d-tubocurarine 3 mg, thiopental 4 mg/kg, succinylcholine 1.5 mg/kg, then **vecuronium or pancuronium 0.04 mg/kg IV**
after intubation (vecuronium 2.7 x and pancuronium 1.8 x the ED50 under N2O-halothane); 50% N2O in O2 before delivery,
butorphanol after; neostigmine reversal. Maternal venous samples 2-240 min; maternal vein, umbilical vein and artery
at delivery. Serum by single-ion-monitoring mass spectrometry (parent only, sensitivity 2.0 ng/mL, CV < 10%). Two- and
three-compartment fits (weighted by concentration^3/2); **two-compartment** selected. Mean +/- SE throughout.

## Model and parameters
Table 5 (PDF p.4, image-confirmed), mean +/- SE; **PK in vecuronium n = 5, pancuronium n = 3** (of 11 and 7 dosed; the
paper does not say why the rest were excluded):

| | Vecuronium (n = 5) | Pancuronium (n = 3) |
|---|---|---|
| Distribution half-life (min) | 5.1 +/- 0.9 | 3.8 +/- 1.5 |
| Elimination half-life (min) | 36 +/- 1.8 | 72 +/- 6 (P<0.01) |
| Vdss (mL/kg) | 251 +/- 7.0 | 283 +/- 28 |
| Total clearance (mL/kg/min) | 6.4 +/- 0.4 | 3.0 +/- 0.1 (P<0.001) |

Placental transfer (Table 4, PDF p.3, image-confirmed; vecuronium n = 9 measured): drug-to-delivery 6.7 +/- 0.6 min,
maternal vein 162 +/- 10 ng/mL, umbilical vein 17.9 +/- 2.0 ng/mL (range 8.5-26.4), umbilical artery 9.1 (n = 2),
**UV/MV 0.11 +/- 0.02**. Pancuronium (n = 5): MV 115 +/- 10.5, UV 21.6 +/- 4.0, UV/MV 0.19 +/- 0.03.

The authors' comparison (p.573) is with **non-pregnant patients from their earlier study (ref 5, Cronnelly 1983),
not a concurrent arm**: CL 5.2 vs 6.4 mL/kg/min and t1/2 beta 71 vs 36 min for vecuronium; 1.8 vs 3.0 mL/kg/min and
140 vs 72 min for pancuronium, Vdss similar. They conclude Cl is greater and t1/2 beta shorter at caesarean, and
discuss blood loss, removal of fetus/placenta and progesterone induction of hepatic metabolism as causes.

Check (derived): t1/2 = ln 2 x Vdss/CL = 0.693 x 251/6.4 = 27 min, below the 36 min reported, as expected for a
two-compartment model where Vbeta > Vss.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Maternal neuromuscular effect not studied (succinylcholine given first; reversed early). Neonatal end points: Apgar
8-10 at 1 and 5 min 45% and 91% (vecuronium) vs 57% and 100%; NACS 35-40 at 15 min, 2 h, 24 h 73/73/100% vs
29/57/100% (Table 2), no significant differences; vecuronium infants weighed less (3459 vs 4020 g). Cited
comparators (p.572, ref 5): plasma concentrations for 50% block in adults under N2O-halothane 94 ng/mL vecuronium and
88 ng/mL pancuronium. Not an opioid; no MEAC.

## Covariates
Pregnancy (term), versus a historical non-pregnant group only.

## Caveats
PK in only 5 vecuronium patients, sampled for 4 h (a later 12 h study found a much longer terminal phase); no
concurrent non-pregnant control; SE not SD. Acute blood loss and fluid replacement during surgery.

## Verification against stanpumpR
- **CL 6.4 +/- 0.4 mL/kg/min, Vss 251 mL/kg, t1/2 36 min, 0.04 mg/kg, 4 h, mass spectrometry, 5 women**: Table 5 and
  Methods. **matches**.
- **"No nonpregnant group"**: true of the study design, but the paper does compare with non-pregnant patients from
  ref 5 (CL 5.2 vs 6.4, t1/2 71 vs 36 min) and concludes clearance is **greater** at caesarean. The registry's
  `unchanged` rests on stanpumpR's own comparison with the Vega-Villa model, not on the authors' conclusion. The
  registry does not mention the authors' own comparison. **discrepancy** (interpretive; the numbers themselves
  match).
- **"Dailey 1984: 11 women at caesarean section ... CL 6.4"** and **n = 11**: 11 women received vecuronium, but the
  clearance comes from 5 (Table 5). The registry text contradicts itself (5 and 11). **discrepancy** (minor).
- **5.7-6.2 mL/kg/min from the library model**: a stanpumpR calculation; consistent with an FFM-scaled 0.51 L/min for
  70-80 kg women of average height (rough check, derived), not verifiable from this paper.
- **UV/MV 0.11**: Table 4. **matches**.
- **"Smaller volume and shorter half-life reflect 4 h against 12 h of sampling"**: stanpumpR's inference; plausible,
  not stated by the paper (which attributes the shorter half-life to the higher clearance).
