# Panchaud 2011: fluoxetine popPK in breastfeeding women, infant exposure simulation

## Study
Retrospective pooled data from two earlier studies (Kristensen 1999; Taddio 1996): 24 breastfeeding women on
fluoxetine 7.5-80 mg/day at steady state (≥ 2 weeks of therapy) and 25 infants (one twin pair). 49 maternal plasma
and 112 milk fluoxetine concentrations. Maternal age 31.8 ± 5.6 (22.7-44) y, weight 64.5 ± 13.5 (31-85) kg; infants
6.3 ± 6.8 months (0.13-25), 5.3 ± 2.1 kg. Intensive plasma+milk sampling (1-24 h) in 4 women, paired samples in 10,
intensive milk in 10. Assay not described here (from source studies). NONMEM VI (6.2.0), FOCE-I, ADVAN5;
200-sample bootstrap; external validation of milk/plasma ratio (MPR) against 8 published studies (59 patients).

## Model and parameters
One compartment, first-order absorption (2-cmt ΔOF −3.0, not significant); ka fixed at 0.3/h (from Altamura 1994);
milk = MPR × plasma; no IIV on V; exponential residual error. Norfluoxetine could not be modelled.

(derived: t½ = ln2 × 690 / 8.42 = 56.8 h; Css at 20 mg/day = 20/(8.42 × 24) = 0.099 mg/L, matching the paper's
0.0988 mg/L.)

Simulation (1000 pairs, 20 mg/day, infant CL 20% of allometric adult value): infant dose median 0.0094 mg/kg/day;
RID median 0.031 (99th pct 0.089); infant/maternal Css ratio median 0.085 (99th pct 0.25).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. RID ≤ 0.1 used as a reference exposure level.

## Covariates
Maternal weight, infant weight, dose tested (linear): none retained.

## Caveats
Small n (24), lactating women only, mostly sparse samples at steady state; ka fixed; V poorly estimated (bootstrap CI
192-1187 L); F assumed 1; no norfluoxetine (the main active, long-lived metabolite); infant clearance an assumption.
