# Westin 2009: topiramate concentration/dose ratio in pregnancy

## Study
Retrospective/prospective TDM series from two Norwegian epilepsy clinics: 12 women, 15 pregnancies (14 completed,
1 spontaneous abortion), mean age at conception 28 years (21-38). Weight not reported. Topiramate monotherapy in 7
pregnancies, with other antiseizure drugs in 8 (lamotrigine, carbamazepine, primidone, valproate, levetiracetam,
oxcarbazepine; Table 1, p2). Serum drawn 10-14 h after the last dose. Assays: fluorescence polarisation
immunoassay (Innofluor/TDx, detection 0.3 µg/mL = 0.89 µmol/L, CV < 5%) at one site, LC-MS (detection 0.34 µg/mL,
CV 7.5%) at the other. Outcome: C/D ratio = serum concentration (µmol/L) / total daily dose (mg). Baseline:
last nonpregnant sample before pregnancy (n = 11) or first sample at least 1 month after delivery (n = 3). 62
samples; paired t-tests of each trimester against the same women's baseline, Bonferroni (p ≤ 0.017).

First trimester not different from baseline (p = 0.45, n = 12). Example given: 200 mg/day gives about 14 µmol/L at
baseline, 10 (CI 8-12) in T2 and 9 (CI 7-10) in T3. The text's paired means (0.071, 0.073) are subsets of Table 2's
all-pregnancy baseline mean (0.0732). Fig. 1 regression shows a gradual linear decline over gestation.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Not an analgesic; no ke0 or concentration-effect model. Seizure frequency rose in 7 of 15 pregnancies; among the 8
with third-trimester samples, mean C/D fall was 34% in the 4 with more seizures and 26% in the 4 without (not
significant). No therapeutic range stated.

## Covariates
Gestational stage only. Co-medication and dose changes recorded but not modelled.

## Caveats
Small n (11 and 8 paired pregnancies), wide interindividual variability, two different assays, mixed inducer
co-medication (carbamazepine in one; oxcarbazepine, primidone in others), and a sampling time of 10-14 h rather
than a fixed trough. Misprints: the T2 difference CI is printed "0.01-0.31" (presumably 0.031), and "ztrimester".
The text says 62 samples from 11 patients, Fig. 1 says n = 14 patients. The comparator for every "lower" is the
same women's nonpregnant C/D.
