# Beck 2001: sevoflurane vs propofol, shunt during one-lung ventilation

## Study
Randomised, 40 adults for thoracic surgery needing one-lung ventilation (OLV); 38 analysed (19 per group; one per group excluded for hypotension needing vasoactive drugs). Propofol group: infusion 9 mg/kg/h for 10 min then 6 mg/kg/h (abstract says 4-6 mg/kg/h). Sevoflurane group: end-expiratory 1.8 vol% ("1 MAC"). Premedication midazolam 0.1 mg/kg po; induction etomidate 0.2 mg/kg + fentanyl 0.1-0.2 mg; cisatracurium 0.15 mg/kg; left double-lumen tube; PA thermodilution catheter. FIO2 started at 0.5 (oxygen/air), adjusted to keep SpO2 above 91%; 7 propofol and 8 sevoflurane patients needed FIO2 above 0.5. Tidal volume 10 mL/kg, no PEEP.
Measurements (before surgical manipulation of the lung): TLV (two-lung, supine, after 30 min), OLV-1 (supine, 30 min of OLV), OLV-2 (lateral, chest open). Shunt = Qs/Qt = (Cc'O2 - CaO2)/(Cc'O2 - CvO2) (Riley three-compartment), Cc'O2 from PAO2 with R = 0.8, Hb 100% saturated; so it is venous admixture (true shunt plus low V/Q), not true shunt (discussion, PDF p.4). Age 62 (46-78) propofol, 58 (34-78) sevoflurane; 6 and 7 females; Hb 13.2 and 11.9 g/dL; PaO2 66.9 and 76.1 mmHg (Table 1, PDF p.3; FIO2 not stated).

Increases from TLV, as stated in the text: OLV-1 17.4 and 17.2 percentage points (P=0.94); OLV-2 18.3 and 16.5 (P=0.59), propofol and sevoflurane (derived check: Table 3 differences agree). Other Table 3 values (propofol/sevoflurane): PaO2 TLV 168/184, OLV-1 91/103, OLV-2 104/100 mmHg; CI 2.5/2.4, 2.7/2.6, 2.7/2.8 L/min/m2; PvO2 41/43, 39/40, 40/41 mmHg; PaCO2 42/41, 46/43, 44/42 mmHg.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: calculated shunt fraction, PaO2, haemodynamics. No concentration-effect model, no ke0, EC50 or MEAC. Not an opioid paper. Sevoflurane given at 1 MAC (1.8 vol%); the discussion cites an animal/in-vitro estimate that HPV is reduced about 25% at 1 MAC (ref 2, rabbit lung), with an obligatory OLV shunt of about 25% cited from other work, so about 25% to 30%, a 5-point rise (discussion, PDF p.5; reasoning, not a measurement in this study).

## Covariates
Linear regression of shunt on cardiac index: y = 19.775 + 2.95x (P=0.06, r=0.230). Best-subsets regression: cardiac index (P=0.02) and PaCO2 (P=0.04) contributed; PvO2 (P=0.09), MAP, MPAP did not.

## Caveats
Shunt by the oxygen method, venous admixture; patients had lung disease (pre-op PaO2 67 to 76 mmHg); position of PA catheter not imaged; FIO2 not fixed; double-lumen tube with open chest, not accidental endobronchial intubation. Post hoc power: 80% to detect an 8.5-point difference.
