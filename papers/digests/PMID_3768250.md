# Miners 1986: oral paracetamol metabolism in the third trimester

## Study
Short report, Flinders Medical Centre. 8 women in the third trimester (gestation 31-38 weeks: three at 31, three at 33, two at 38; Table 1),
age 26-32 y, weight 61-77 kg (mean 68 +/- 5), against 12 non-pregnant women, age 19-31 y, weight 54-72 kg (mean 63 +/-
7), drawn from this group's earlier and concurrent studies (unpaired). Fasted, 1 g oral paracetamol (2 x 500 mg Panadol)
with 150 mL water; **saliva** at 0.5-8 h (10 samples); urine 0-24 h. HPLC for saliva paracetamol and urinary
paracetamol, glucuronide, sulphate, cysteine and mercapturate conjugates. CLpo = D/AUC (apparent oral clearance, taken
as intrinsic clearance for a completely absorbed, hepatically cleared drug); partial clearances = fraction recovered x
CLpo. Mann-Whitney. Dose 13-16 mg/kg (pregnant) and 14-19 mg/kg (controls).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. The authors suggest analgesic dosing in pregnancy needs study.

## Covariates
Pregnancy only (unpaired groups). Variability in CLpo similar (1.62- vs 1.84-fold range); half-life more variable in
pregnancy, which the authors read as variable distribution volume.

## Caveats
Small unpaired groups; saliva rather than plasma; oral apparent clearance (bioavailability assumed complete); mostly
early third trimester (31-33 weeks); not at delivery.
