# Gepts 1995: linearity and model estimation of sufentanil

## Study
- 25 ASA 1-2 patients for head and neck surgery with postoperative ventilation (Brussels); 2 discarded for
  surgical complications, 23 analysed, aged 14-68 yr (p. 1195, 1197).
- Demographics by dose group (Table 1): weight means 60-75 kg, overall range 47-94 kg; height range
  154-182 cm; lean body mass means 47-55 kg; 14 male, 9 female (derived from Table 1 counts).
- Sufentanil as a short infusion: 25 ug/min x 10 min (250 ug), 50 ug/min x 10 min (500), 75 ug/min x 10 min
  (750), 66.7 ug/min x 15 min (1,000), 75 ug/min x 20 min (1,500). Induction etomidate 0.2 mg/kg, atracurium,
  35% O2 in N2O; isoflurane 0.3-0.8% if haemodynamics rose > 30% (p. 1195).
- Arterial samples every 2 min during infusion, then at 1-120 min, hourly to 8 h, 2-hourly to 36 h, 4-hourly to
  48 h. Plasma RIA (Janssen), detection limit 0.02 ng/mL, interassay CV 8.5-10.5% (0.05-10 ng/mL).
  788 of 801 concentrations used (3 outliers, 10 values at the detection limit after undetectable ones
  excluded).
- Three approaches, all three-compartment mammillary: two-stage (Excel Solver, extended least squares,
  constant-CV error), naive pooled (NONMEM, IIV fixed at 0), mixed-effect (NONMEM with NMVCL). The two-stage
  model excluded the 250 ug group (n = 18 from 500-1,500 ug) because its concentrations fell below detection
  before the terminal phase.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- PK only. No ke0, t½ke0, tPeak, EC50, MEAC or potency ratio measured or reported.
- Fig. 6 (p. 1203): simulated 30/50/70% plasma decrement times vs infusion duration (to 16 h) for this model,
  Bovill 1984 and Hudson 1989; the 50% decrement time rises for about 2 h then flattens.
- Terminal phase fractional intercept 0.41% (p. 1202).

## Covariates
None. Age (linear regression of each parameter) had no effect; scaling volumes and clearances by weight or by
lean body mass (James formula, given p. 1197) did not improve the fit but did not worsen it either (p. 1198,
1202). Authors note this may not hold at extremes of weight.

## Caveats
- Small n (18 in the final model), head and neck surgical patients with N2O; RIA assay.
- The 250 ug group shows apparent dose dependence that the authors show is a sampling-duration artefact;
  sampling of at least 24 h and doses above 500 ug are needed.
- Mixed-effect model judged inferior (negative bias after 600 min); two-stage chosen as best.
