# Plaud 1995: rocuronium PK and PK-PD at the laryngeal adductors and adductor pollicis

## Study
- 8 ASA I-II patients, age 31-58 y (mean 47 +/- 9), weight 59 +/- 9 kg, height 165 +/- 5 cm, 6 F / 2 M
  (Table I; individual rows garbled in the text layer).
- Propofol-alfentanil anaesthesia, no volatile agents or N2O; intubation without relaxant.
- Rocuronium 100 ug/kg/min IV for 5 min (total 500 ug/kg).
- Arterial samples at 0-360 min (21 samples); HPLC; LLOQ 10 ng/mL; metabolites (17-desacetyl, desallyl)
  not detected.
- Effect: T1 depression (% of control), adductor pollicis by force transducer (ulnar TOF) and laryngeal
  adductors by tracheal-tube cuff pressure (recurrent laryngeal nerve TOF).
- Individual two- or three-compartment fits (MultiFit; F test / AIC); PK-PD by Sheiner effect compartment +
  Hill equation per patient (PkPdFit). Results are means +/- SD of individual estimates (no population model).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: T1 twitch depression (% control) at each muscle.
- Onset (infusion start to max block): larynx 5.8 +/- 1.3 min, adductor pollicis 7.1 +/- 2.5 min; max block
  72 +/- 11% vs 95 +/- 9% (Table II). Recovery to 90%: 24.5 +/- 6.5 vs 32.7 +/- 8.2 min.
- ke0 and EC50 above; EC50 ratio larynx/AP 1.73 (vs ED50 ratio 1.52 cited from Meistelman 1992).
- No tPeak after a bolus reported (the dose was a 5-min infusion).

## Covariates
None (8 patients; per-kg reporting).

## Caveats
- n = 8, mostly women, middle-aged, healthy; no population model, mean of individual estimates.
- Some individuals better fitted with three compartments; the summary is not a single 2-compartment model.
- Text layer is OCR-quality; Table I and Table IV individual rows misaligned.
