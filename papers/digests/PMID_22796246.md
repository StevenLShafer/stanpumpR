# Kim 2012: methotrexate PopPK after HSCT, ABCB1 3435 genotype

## Study
- Prospective, single centre (Seoul National University Hospital), 2009-2011. 20 adult allogeneic HSCT recipients, all Korean; 12 men, 8 women; median age 28 y (18-49), median weight 55.6 kg (44.8-80.8), height 168.9 cm (Table II, PDF p5).
- Conditioning: busulfan 0.8 mg/kg IV q6h x4 days, cyclophosphamide 60 mg/kg x2 days. GVHD prophylaxis: cyclosporine plus short-course IV push methotrexate, 15 mg/m2 on post-HSCT day 1 and 10 mg/m2 on days 3, 6, 11 (Methods, printed 1817).
- Samples: baseline, 5 min, 2, 3, 6, 12, 24 h after start; again 6, 12, 24 h on day 11; a few extra on day 3. 26 PK profiles, 94 concentrations (about 3.3 per profile). Plasma MTX by LC-MS/MS, quantification limit 2 ng/mL.
- Analysis: NONMEM 6.1, ADVAN3/TRANS4, FOCE with interaction; exponential IIV, proportional residual error; forward addition (dOFV >3.84) then backward elimination (dOFV >10.8); 2000-sample bootstrap (PsN). GFR by Cockcroft-Gault.

## Model and parameters
Two-compartment linear model, IV bolus. Final model, Table IV (PDF p7, printed 1822), typical values:

Covariate equation as printed (Results): CL (L/h) = 6.93 x [1 + 0.376 (GFR/116)] x 0.613^PEN x 1.3^HOMZ (PEN=1 with penicillin; HOMZ=1 for ABCB1 3435 TT). Mean CL by genotype: 10.07 L/h (CC or CT) vs 8.26 L/h (TT), P<0.001 (Fig 2). Q and V2 variances were fixed; Q and V2 covariates were not tested.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No effect, toxicity or target concentration is reported. Not an opioid paper; no MEAC, ke0, tPeak or potency ratio.

## Covariates
GFR (Cockcroft-Gault, mL/min) on CL, concurrent penicillin (6 on piperacillin-tazobactam, 1 on ampicillin-sulbactam) on CL, ABCB1 3435 genotype on CL. Tested and not retained: sex, age, BSA, donor type, creatinine, albumin, AST, ALT, bilirubin, urine pH, folic acid, other SNPs (ABCB1 2677, ABCC2, ATIC, GGH, MTHFR, TYMS). dOFV on removal: GFR 15.257, penicillin 22.497, genotype 10.93 (Results, printed 1821). IIV on CL fell from 36.5% (base) to 21.6%.

## Caveats
- n=20 patients, one ethnicity, low-dose MTX (10-15 mg/m2), so not applicable to high-dose MTX.
- IIV on V1 is large (73.3%); IIV on V2 and Q not estimated.
- Several internal inconsistencies (see open_questions): CL 6.93 vs 7.08, GFR coefficient 0.376 vs 0.382, genotype direction, dose unit, V2.
- Table II is partly scrambled in the text layer; not needed here.
