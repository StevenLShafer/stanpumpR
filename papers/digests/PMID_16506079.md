# Takano 2006: serotonin-transporter occupancy by duloxetine ([11C]DASB PET)

## Study
- 17 healthy Japanese men enrolled, 2 excluded (PET technical), 15 completed; age 24.1 +/- 2.4 yr; non-smokers.
  Weight not reported. Part of the Japanese phase I programme (partly supported by Shionogi).
- Part A (n = 12): single oral dose of 5, 20, 40 or 60 mg (3 each); PET at baseline and 6 h; the 60 mg subjects also
  at 25 h and 53 h.
- Part B (n = 3): 60 mg daily for 7 days; PET at baseline, 6, 49 and 78 h after the last dose.
- [11C]DASB, ECAT 47, 90-min dynamic scans; MRTM2 with cerebellum as reference; occupancy measured in the
  **thalamus**. Plasma duloxetine by LC-MS/MS, drawn just before and after each scan.

## Model and parameters
No PK model. Occupancy = 100 x (BP_baseline - BP_drug)/BP_baseline. Occupancy-concentration model (Methods,
Fig. 1): occupancy % = 100 x C / (ED50 + C), i.e. **Emax fixed at 100%**, C = dose or the plasma concentration just
before the scan.

Mean occupancy by dose (6 h, Part A): 43.6 +/- 8.8% (5 mg), 71.3 +/- 5.3% (20 mg), 80.6 +/- 4.8% (40 mg),
81.8 +/- 4.3% (60 mg); range across subjects 35.3-86.5%.
Time course after 60 mg single dose: 81.8% (6 h), 71.9% (25 h), 44.9% (53 h). After 7 days of 60 mg: 84.3% (6 h),
71.9% (49 h), 47.1% (78 h). Linear fits: about 70% (single) and 78% (repeated) at 24 h (Discussion).
Plasma concentration time course shown only in Figs. 2-3 (mono-exponential fit; values not tabulated).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: 5-HTT occupancy in thalamus by PET; a biomarker, not a clinical effect. EC50 3.7 ng/mL total plasma
duloxetine (Emax 100% assumed by the model form). Occupancy falls more slowly than plasma concentration (hysteresis
noted qualitatively; no ke0 estimated). NET occupancy not measured (no ligand at the time); from in vitro Ki (0.8 vs
7.5) the authors estimate about 30% NET occupancy at a dose giving 80% SERT occupancy. Not an opioid.

## Covariates
None.

## Caveats
n = 3 per dose group; healthy young men only; Emax fixed at 100%; single region (thalamus); plasma concentrations
not tabulated. Authors' clinical threshold (80% occupancy) is borrowed from SSRI literature.
