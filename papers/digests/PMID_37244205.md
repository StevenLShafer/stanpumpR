# Shibata 2023: duloxetine population PK in Japanese children and adolescents with MDD

## Study
- Open-label long-term extension trial (NCT03395353), 36 Japanese sites, Jan 2018 - Jul 2020; 40 or 60 mg/day for
  50 weeks. Five steady-state samples per patient from week 4 (one or two pre-dose troughs); samples at least 2 weeks
  after any dose change.
- 512 samples from 141 patients; 490 from 138 analysed. 11 children (9-11 yr; weight 36.9 +/- 6.9 kg, range
  27.6-46.6) and 127 adolescents (12-17 yr; 52.5 +/- 11.3 kg, range 27.9-102.7); 30% male overall (Table 1).
- Plasma LC-MS/MS, LLOQ 0.100 ng/mL. NONMEM 7.4.4, PsN 4.9.0; pcVPC (200 sims), bootstrap (1000).
- Sponsor-authored (Shionogi, Eli Lilly Japan).

## Model and parameters
One compartment, first-order absorption, apparent oral parameters; dose-proportional; IIV on CL/F only (V/F IIV
dropped, shrinkage 68%); proportional residual error. Reference patient: male. Table 2:

Mean post-hoc CL/F (Discussion): at 40 mg children 97.9, adolescents 82.6, Japanese adults 93.7 L/h; at 60 mg 71.3,
68.5, 67.0 L/h. Table 3: steady-state Cmax/AUC similar to or above adult values at 40 and 60 mg. US pediatric
model (Lobo 2014) reference values for comparison: CL/F 79.7 L/h, V/F 1200 L (BSA 1.55 m2, 60 mg).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. Not an opioid.

## Covariates
Sex on CL/F only. Weight, height, BMI, BSA, IBW, LBW, ABW, age, liver tests, CrCL, age group all not significant.

## Caveats
Few children (n = 11), mostly adolescent girls; narrow body-size range; ka fixed; V/F IIV not estimable.
Bootstrap CI and asymptotic CI for the variance terms look misprinted.
