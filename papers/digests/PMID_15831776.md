# Venkatakrishnan 2005: IV alprazolam, adinazolam and NDMAD kinetics and EEG dynamics

## Study
- 9 healthy Caucasian men, 20-40 y (mean 29), mean weight 80 kg; randomized, blinded 4-way crossover (after a
  no-treatment practice trial), >= 1 week apart.
- Treatments, each IV over 30 min (constant-rate pump, diluted to 50 mL): alprazolam 1 mg; adinazolam mesylate 10 mg
  (7.9 mg base); N-desmethyladinazolam (NDMAD) mesylate 10 mg (7.8 mg base); placebo.
- Plasma at 0.5-24 h (19 times). Alprazolam by GC-ECD (sensitivity 1 ng/mL); adinazolam/NDMAD by HPLC (5 ng/mL).
- PD: EEG (Fz, Cz, Pz, Oz; FFT; relative beta 12-30 Hz amplitude, change over baseline), DSST, VAS mood and sedation
  (self and observer).
- PK: per-subject two-compartment fits (derivative-free nonlinear least squares, weights 1/C^2).
- PK-PD: **subject-averaged (naive pooled) data**; alpha, beta, k21, V1 fixed from the PK fit; sigmoid Emax on
  effect-site concentration (Eqs. 5-6), iterating Emax, EC50, Hill A and kEO.

## Model and parameters
Alprazolam and adinazolam: two-compartment IV fits per subject; NDMAD: one compartment in 4 of 9 subjects (Results,
p. 5). Table I (PDF p. 5, printed 533; mean +/- SEM; rendered page, columns plainly labelled):

|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Distribution t1/2, h | 1.6 +/- 0.04 | 0.1 +/- 0.03 | -- |
| Elimination t1/2, h | 14.6 +/- 1.6 | 2.9 +/- 0.3 | 2.8 +/- 0.2 |
| Vd (area), L | 100 +/- 6.8 | 106 +/- 6.1 | 77 +/- 4.0 |
| CL, mL/min | 84 +/- 5.6 | 444 +/- 37 | 321 +/- 10 |

The abstract (p. 1) lists adinazolam / NDMAD / alprazolam as Vd 106 / 100 / 77 L, t1/2 2.9 / 2.8 / 14.6 h, CL 444 /
321 / 84 mL/min: half-lives and clearances agree with Table I, but the alprazolam and NDMAD volumes are swapped.
Table I is the internally consistent one (derived: CL x t1/2 / ln 2 = 5.04 L/h x 14.6 h / 0.693 = 106 L for
alprazolam, 19.26 x 2.8 / 0.693 = 78 L for NDMAD; an alprazolam Vd of 77 L would need t1/2 about 10.6 h), so the
alprazolam Vd is taken as 100 L. Fraction of IV adinazolam reaching the circulation as NDMAD 0.82 +/- 0.13 (Results p. 5).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- EEG beta (12-30 Hz) vs plasma alprazolam showed counterclockwise hysteresis; an effect-site model removed it.
  Equilibration half-life 4.8 min in the abstract; 4.7 min in the Results and Figure 5 (ke0 0.144-0.147 /min,
  derived). End point: relative beta amplitude change over baseline, averaged across leads.
- Sigmoid Emax: Emax 16.1%, EC50 7.5 ng/mL, A 2.7 (Figure 5).
- DSST: no hysteresis could be shown because the first DSST was at 1 h; fitted to a modified exponential model
  (Figure 7).
- Three-drug comparison (abstract p. 1; Results p. 5; Figures 3-4 p. 6; Table II p. 7): all three increased EEG beta
  over placebo; alprazolam had the largest effect, NDMAD a similar maximum but shorter, adinazolam the weakest, and
  4.5-h EEG effect areas differed significantly among all treatments. DSST fell after alprazolam and NDMAD; after
  adinazolam it was like placebo. Observer-rated sedation (4.5-h VAS effect area, Table II): placebo -3.49,
  adinazolam 16.15, NDMAD 46.4, alprazolam 64.1 (F 4.56, P = .01); all three differed from placebo. The authors
  conclude the effects after adinazolam are mediated mainly by NDMAD. These are dose (single IV dose) comparisons,
  not potency estimates.
- NDMAD: little hysteresis, exponential model (E = B C^A, Figure 6); adinazolam: no model fitted (its concentrations
  fit no effect model). More than 80% (0.82 +/- 0.13) of IV
  adinazolam appeared systemically as NDMAD.
- No MEAC, no potency ratio.

## Covariates
None (9 young men).

## Caveats
- n = 9 healthy young men; kEO from naive-pooled mean data, so no between-subject variability or SE for kEO.
- 30-min infusion; the earliest PD samples are at 0.5 h, so a 5-minute kEO is constrained by few early points.
- Abstract (4.8 min) and Results/Figure 5 (4.7 min) disagree slightly.
- Table I's text layer is garbled; the rendered table and the abstract disagree on which of alprazolam and NDMAD has
  Vd 100 vs 77 L (Table I preferred, see above).
- Not every two-compartment microconstant (k21, alpha) is tabulated, so the IV disposition model cannot be fully
  rebuilt from the paper.
