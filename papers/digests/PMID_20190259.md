# Coppens 2010: constant Ce at LORNC vs BIS, Marsh / Schnider / Schnider-tPeak

## Study
60 ASA I adults, 18-65 y, 70-130% of ideal body weight, minor ambulatory surgery, no premedication or other drugs, spontaneous breathing on face-mask O2. Randomised to three groups of 20 (Table 2): age about 33 y, weight 62.2 / 65.75 / 67.55 kg, height 170.8 / 169.3 / 170.3 cm, sex M/F 2/18, 3/17, 7/13 (groups M, S1, S2; S2 had more men).
Propofol 1% was run at 300 mL/h (3000 mg/h) until loss of response to name calling (LORNC, OAA/S 3 to 2; name called every 15 s). At LORNC the model's predicted effect-site concentration (CePROP) became the target for effect-site TCI (RUGLOOP II driving an Asena GH pump), held for up to 20 min unless the patient regained responsiveness (ROC). BIS XP (15 s smoothing) throughout. No propofol assays; all concentrations are model predictions.
Groups: M = Marsh PK with ke0 0.26 /min (Marsh-Schuttler set; not commercial for effect-site TCI); S1 = Schnider PK with fixed ke0 0.456 /min; S2 = Schnider PK with ke0 set per patient so that time to peak effect (TTPE) is 1.6 min (101 s), after Minto 2003.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End points: LORNC (OAA/S 3 to 2) and BIS. No opioid.
- ke0 / TTPE per group (Table 1): M 0.26 /min, 270 s; S1 0.456 /min, 92 (SD 4) s; S2 0.362 (SD 0.04) /min, 101 s (1.6 min).
- Predicted CePROP at LORNC, mean (SD), text PDF p3: M 1.96 (0.55), S1 5.54 (0.75), S2 5.08 (1.20) ug/mL (the text layer prints the unit as mg ml-1, read as ug/mL from the axis labels of Fig 1); M differs from both (P<0.0001), S1 and S2 do not. Time to LORNC and BIS at LORNC did not differ between groups; BIS at LORNC 59 (Table 3, SD 14 and 13 for S1 and S2).
- Holding CePROP constant at that value: every group M patient regained consciousness (after 401 (SD 102) s); one S1 patient woke after 505 s, none in S2. BIS reached steady state after 6 min in M and 11 min in S1 and S2; at min 11 mean BIS was 29 (SD 6) in S1 and 37 in S2 versus 59 at LORNC (Table 3, P<0.001), i.e. effect deepened at constant CePROP.
- Mean pump inactivity after LORNC (Table 4): M 233 s, S1 102 s, S2 81 s.
- No EC50, Emax, MEAC or potency ratio. The authors conclude that a CePROP fixed at the LORNC value does not give a constant effect with any of the three sets, and suggest falling asleep may need more propofol than staying asleep.

## Covariates
Schnider PK: age, weight, height (via lean body mass); Marsh volume per kg. The authors list induction speed as a possible covariate (3000 mg/h here; above 6000 mL/h said to disturb ke0 estimation).

## Caveats
Single induction rate; BIS is a surrogate; follow-up stopped at ROC (surgical patients); young ASA I patients (mean age about 33 y) so age terms are untested; Marsh set's large V1 slows the predicted Cp rise so CePROP at LORNC is lower. Group S2 had more men.
