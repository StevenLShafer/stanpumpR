# Ambien label 2022: zolpidem tartrate prescribing information

## Study
Regulatory label. Product: zolpidem tartrate immediate-release tablets 5 and 10 mg (strengths are of the
tartrate salt; Section 3). Key PK study (12.3): single-dose crossover in 45 healthy subjects, 5 and 10 mg
tablets. Food study: 30 healthy men, 10 mg fasting vs 20 min after a meal. Elderly study: 8 subjects >70 y vs
20-40 y, 20 mg. Hepatic: 8 patients with chronic hepatic insufficiency, 20 mg. Renal: 11 ESRD patients on
haemodialysis, 10 mg daily for 14 or 21 days.

No absolute bioavailability, clearance or volume is given. Renal failure: no significant change; not
dialysable; no dose adjustment. Metabolised to inactive metabolites, renally excreted.

Dosing (2.1-2.2): initial 5 mg for women, 5 or 10 mg for men, once nightly immediately before bedtime with
7-8 h before waking; maximum 10 mg; elderly/debilitated and mild-moderate hepatic impairment 5 mg; avoid in
severe hepatic impairment. 10 mg raises next-day driving impairment risk in some patients (no concentration
given in the label).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No concentration-effect relation, no therapeutic range, no ke0 in the label; no 50 ng/mL figure (that is in
the 2013 Drug Safety Communication). Not an opioid: no MEAC or potency ratio.

## Covariates
Sex, age, hepatic impairment, food, CNS depressants and CYP3A4 interactions (qualitative/percentage).

## Caveats
Single-dose summary statistics; Cmax ranges are wide (58-272 ng/mL at 10 mg).
