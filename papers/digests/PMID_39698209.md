# Zheng 2024: quetiapine popPK in bipolar inpatients, initial dose by Monte Carlo

## Study
- Retrospective real-world TDM study, Xuzhou Oriental Hospital, July 2020 to August 2023.
- n = 99 patients with bipolar affective disorder (BAD) on quetiapine; 66 men / 33 women; age 17.05-69.47 y,
  weight 43.00-119.00 kg (text, section 2.1). Table 1 (demographics, labs) is badly garbled in the text layer;
  the weight median appears to be 72.00 kg (43.00-119.00) but the column alignment is unreliable.
- Formulation: "quetiapine" (tablets implied); the paper does not state IR vs XR explicitly, nor dose range,
  sampling times, or whether samples were at steady state. Assay not described.
- 23 co-medications tabulated (Table 2) and tested as covariates; laboratory indices also collected.
- NONMEM; stepwise covariate search (forward dOFV > 3.84, backward > 6.63); bootstrap and VPC evaluation.
- Monte Carlo: 1,000 virtual patients at 40, 60, 80, 100, 120 kg, doses 1-28 mg/kg/day; therapeutic window
  100-500 ng/mL (cited from ref 22, Lin 2024).

## Model and parameters
One compartment, first-order absorption, ka fixed at 1.46 /h from Zhou 2015 (ref 23). Final model (Eq 6-7):
CL/F = 76.1 x (weight/70)^0.75; V/F = 530 x (weight/70). Allometric exponents fixed (0.75, 1), citing Anderson
and Holford 2008. Table 3:

Dose recommendations (Table 4): 40-66 kg, 16 mg/kg/day (78.8-82.2% in window, 5.2-10.3% above 500 ng/mL);
66-120 kg, 12 mg/kg/day (81.5-85.5% in window, 3.6-8.1% above). Discussion quotes literature values: Tmax ~1.5 h,
t1/2 ~7 h, protein binding ~83%, tablet bioavailability close to 100% (ref 28, DeVane 2001).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None measured. No ke0, EC50, receptor occupancy or clinical end point. Only the cited 100-500 ng/mL
therapeutic reference range (ref 22), used as the simulation target.

## Covariates
Weight only (fixed allometry). No co-medication or laboratory value was retained. Genotype not tested
(stated limitation).

## Caveats
- Retrospective TDM data; sampling times, dose history and steady-state status not reported.
- ka fixed, not estimated; V/F very imprecise (SE 59.6%, bootstrap 90% CI 210-2088 L).
- Parameters are apparent (/F); F not estimated.
- Recommended doses (12-16 mg/kg/day, i.e. ~840-1000 mg/day at 66-70 kg) exceed usual labelled maxima
  (derived: 12 x 70 = 840 mg/day); the paper does not comment.
- Table 1 is unreadable in the text layer (columns misaligned).
- No CYP genotype; Chinese population only.
