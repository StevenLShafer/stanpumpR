# Ben-Menachem 1992: gabapentin CSF/plasma after a single oral dose

## Study
- 5 patients (4 M, 1 F), 22-38 y, intractable complex partial seizures on 1-2 enzyme-inducing AEDs
  (CBZ, PHT, PR, PB); all had been on placebo in a double-blind add-on trial (Table I). Weight not given.
- Single oral dose after overnight fast: 600 mg (patients 1-4) or 1200 mg (patient 5), 300 mg capsules.
- Plasma at 1, 2, 3, 6, 12, 24, 72 h; lumbar CSF at baseline, 6, 24, 72 h. HPLC (Hengy & Kölle);
  LLOQ 0.10 µg/mL. Also CSF free/total GABA, HVA, 5-HIAA.

## Model and parameters
No model. Observations (Table II, text): 600 mg plasma peaks 3.27-5.90 µg/mL within 180 min;
AUC 20.1-52.7 µg·h/mL; plasma t½ 4.9-6.5 h (abstract says 4-6 h).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- CSF/plasma ratio at 6 h: 0.09, 0.14, 0.11, 0.09 (600 mg) and 0.13 (1200 mg) (Table III); summary "about 0.1".
- At 24 h CSF gabapentin was measurable only in the 1200 mg patient (0.10 µg/mL; plasma 0.43; ratio 0.23 derived);
  the abstract gives 0.3 and the misaligned Table III shows 0.22: the paper is internally inconsistent here.
  600 mg CSF at 24 h and all at 72 h: BLQ.
- No change in CSF free/total GABA; HVA and 5-HIAA rose at 24 and 72 h in 3 patients.
- No effect measure linked to concentration; no ke0, tPeak, EC50. Not an opioid: no MEAC or potency ratio.

## Covariates
None analysed.

## Caveats
n = 5; one CSF time with drug present for most patients, so CSF kinetics are not characterized; patients on
enzyme inducers; lumbar CSF only (authors discuss possible transport mechanisms, not shown). Patient 3's "6 h"
plasma sample was taken at 5 h.
