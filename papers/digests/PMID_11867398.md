# Gin 2002: prolonged rocuronium block postpartum is explained by dosing on total body weight

## Study
Brief report (PDF pp. 1-4 = printed pp. 686-689), Prince of Wales Hospital, Hong Kong. Randomised, double-blind
allocation of dose basis (sealed envelopes), 2 x 2 design, n = 44 women, 11 per cell:
- **Postpartum**: elective tubal ligation, scheduled 24-120 h after delivery (Methods, p. 686); in Table 1 time
  after delivery was median 55 h (range 41-67) in the TBW arm and 59 h (31-79) in the LBM arm.
- **Nonpregnant controls**: gynaecological laparoscopy.
- ASA I, aged 25-45 y; excluded neuromuscular disease, drugs affecting neuromuscular function, oral contraceptives.

Anaesthesia: no premedication; propofol 2 mg/kg + alfentanil 5 µg/kg induction, then a manually stepped-down
infusion of a propofol 10 mg/mL / alfentanil 50 µg/mL mixture (1, then 0.8, then 0.6 mL/kg/h). After 15 min
stabilisation, **rocuronium 0.6 mg/kg** over 5 s, either per kg **total body weight (TBW)** or per kg **lean body
mass (LBM)**. LBM by the formula LBM = 1.07·TBW − 148·(TBW/height)², kg and cm (p. 687; the James female formula,
citing Morgan & Bray 1994 and Bouillon & Shafer 1998). Ventilation to end-tidal CO2 34-38 mm Hg.

Monitoring: adductor pollicis EMG (Datex Relaxograph), ulnar nerve train-of-four every 10 s, palmar skin
temperature > 33 °C, GCRP guidelines; T1 allowed to recover spontaneously to ≥ 95% (all reached 96-100%).
End points: onset (start of injection to 95% T1 suppression), duration (to 25% T1 recovery), 25-75% recovery
interval. Analyst blinded. Mann-Whitney U tests; linear regression of duration on TBW, LBM, BMI.
No drug concentrations were measured.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: adductor pollicis EMG T1 (train-of-four), onset to 95% suppression, duration to 25% recovery,
  25-75% recovery interval.
- TBW dosing: duration 35.3 vs 24.8 min (postpartum vs nonpregnant, medians, P < 0.001); 25-75% interval 14.9 vs
  11.7 min. Onset similar (92 vs 94 s).
- LBM dosing: duration 18.9 vs 19.5 min, 25-75% 7.6 vs 6.8 min, not different.
- No ke0, tPeak, EC50 or concentration data. Not an opioid paper (alfentanil was only part of the background
  anaesthetic): no MEAC, ke0 or potency ratio.
- Comparator: every "longer" is postpartum women versus concurrently studied nonpregnant gynaecological patients
  given the same per-kg rule; postpartum women were heavier (TBW 65 vs 52 kg) with BMI ~28 vs ~21.
- Authors' interpretation (Discussion, p. 688): the prolongation is a relative overdose from per-kg-TBW dosing
  of a still-heavier patient; they had no rocuronium PK data and say altered elimination cannot be excluded.
  Discussion also suggests ~0.85 mg/kg LBM as an alternative equivalent dose (not tested).

## Covariates
Dose basis (TBW vs LBM) and postpartum state; TBW, LBM and BMI examined by linear regression against duration
(no equations reported beyond r and P).

## Caveats
- Small (11 per cell); medians/ranges only; no concentrations, so PK vs PD cannot be separated.
- LBM-dosed groups received less drug (about 0.6 x 41/65 ≈ 0.38 mg/kg TBW in postpartum women; derived from
  medians), so shorter blocks there make differences harder to detect, as the authors acknowledge.
- Height row labelled "m" but values are cm (typographical).
- Chinese women in Hong Kong; propofol/alfentanil TIVA (no volatile agent).
- Postpartum 31-79 h; not informative about pregnancy itself.
