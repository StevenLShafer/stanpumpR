# Choi 2015: bioequivalence of two naproxen 500 mg / esomeprazole 20 mg combinations

## Study
- Open-label, randomized, two-period crossover, single dose, 7-day washout (NCT00938132; Seoul National University Hospital).
- Healthy Korean **male** volunteers 20-50 y; 70 randomized, **66 completed** and analysed. Age 29.8 ± 5.9 y (22-51), weight 69.0 ± 7.0 kg (55.2-84.1), height 174.2 ± 5.1 cm, BMI 22.7 ± 2.0.
- HCP1004 (enteric-coated naproxen 500 mg + immediate-release esomeprazole strontium 20 mg) vs VIMOVO (naproxen EC 500 mg + esomeprazole magnesium 20 mg); fasting, ~9 am.
- Naproxen sampling to 72 h (0, 0.5, 1, 2, 3, 3.5, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 10, 12, 16, 24, 36, 48, 72 h). LC-MS/MS, LLOQ 0.3 ug/mL. Non-compartmental (WinNonlin 6.4, linear-log trapezoidal).
- Esomeprazole sampling to 12 h (0, 0.17, 0.33, 0.5, 0.75, 1, 1.5, 2, 2.5, 3, 3.5, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10, 12 h; pp. 2-3, printed 4128-4129); LC-MS/MS, LLOQ 5 ng/mL. Sample size (68) was set from an assumed 45.5% intrasubject CV for esomeprazole AUC; bioequivalence limits 0.80-1.25 (p. 2).

Intersubject CVs of 61-100% (Discussion, p. 16) are similar to an earlier VIMOVO study (81% Cmax, 91% AUC0-t). Derived CL/F = 500/1369.64 = 0.365 L/h (derived). A previous study quoted: Cmax 66.9 ug/mL, AUC0-t 1226 h·ug/mL.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None (bioequivalence/tolerability only). No MEAC, ke0 or potency ratio.

## Covariates
None studied.

## Caveats
Single dose, Korean men only, enteric-coated formulation (median Tmax 5 h), co-administered esomeprazole; NCA only. Esomeprazole arithmetic means carry SDs about as large as the means (CV 61-100%), so the arithmetic Cmax/AUC differences between products mean little; the geometric-mean ratios are the comparison. Esomeprazole sampled only to 12 h.
