# Launay 1989: mitoxantrone population PK by NONMEM

## Study
21 cancer patients, 22 concentration-time curves (317 plasma samples), Institut Paoli-Calmettes. Group Ia (n=5, one patient twice): 12 mg/m2 over 10 min; group Ib (n=5): 30 mg/m2 over 1 h (mean 55 mg over 1.15 h; 50-60 mg, 1.07-1.35 h); group II (11 patients, ALL/ANLL): 12 mg/m2/day as a 48 h continuous infusion with high-dose cytarabine. Sampling to 72 h (to 196 h for high dose/long infusion). Assay: HPLC with visible detection (655 nm), plasma, range 2-50 ng/mL, sensitivity 0.5 ng/mL with repeated injection. Individual fits (weighted nonlinear least squares): 5 curves biexponential, 17 triexponential; terminal half-life 8-84.2 h and correlated with the length of observation (r=0.755). Population fit by NONMEM (in-house software, first-order linearisation) with a single three-compartment model, biexponential curves treated as fragmentary data.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None reported; not an opioid. No effect concentration. Concentrations at 100 h were below 0.5 ng/mL in 8 of 11 curves (p879).

## Covariates
None tested; the population mixes three regimens and diseases (breast, ovarian, nasopharynx, ALL/ANLL).

## Caveats
Pooled heterogeneous protocols; terminal half-life depends on sampling duration; per-patient CL in absolute L/h with no weight or BSA given; unusually long half-life partly reflects a poorly defined third phase; 1989 assay.
