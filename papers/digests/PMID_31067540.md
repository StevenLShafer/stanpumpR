# Alqahtani 2019: phenytoin Michaelis-Menten PopPK in Saudi epileptic patients

## Study
- Retrospective chart review, King Saud University Medical City; routine TDM of stable outpatients on oral
  phenytoin. 43 patients, 81 trough concentrations (sparse, mostly single point).
- Table 1, mean (SD) [range]: age 41 (18.7) [18-84] y; 70% male; weight 65.4 (17.7) [25-115] kg; height 160.3
  (11.1) cm; phenytoin dose 330.5 (104.5) [50-600] mg/day (salt form not stated); concentration 11.2 (10.3)
  [0.5-37.9] mg/L; CrCl 106.7 mL/min; albumin 26.5 (6.2) g/L (units implied); carbamazepine 4 (9.3%), valproate 8
  (18.6%). Dosing once, twice or three times daily.
- Assay: Abbott ARCHITECT chemiluminescent immunoassay, CV ≤ 10%. The methods say it quantifies "free" phenytoin,
  but the therapeutic range used is 10-20 mg/L, i.e. total (the ARCHITECT phenytoin assay is a total assay; the
  word "free" looks like an error in the paper).
- Monolix 4.4 (SAEM); F assumed 1; one vs two compartments compared.

## Model and parameters
One compartment, first-order absorption (ka not reported), Michaelis-Menten elimination. Table 2 (p. 63):

| Parameter | Estimate | RSE % |
|---|---|---|
| V (L/kg) | 0.61 | 14 |
| Vmax (mg/kg/day) | 6.12 | 18 |
| Km (mg/L) | 5.33 | 16 |
| IIV V (CV%) | 18 | 10 |
| IIV Vmax (CV%) | 23 | 19 |
| IIV Km (CV%) | 25 | 20 |
| Residual a / b | 0.15 / 0.05 | 16 / 18 |

Covariates (Table 2 footnote): Vmax = 6.12 × (Age/41)^−0.16 × (BW/65.4)^0.36; Km = 5.33 × 2.66 with valproate.
(The abstract misprints V as "0.61 L/h/kg".) Whether the Vmax equation's 6.12 stays per kg after the weight power
is not stated.

Comparisons quoted (p. 63): Abduljabbar (Saudi) Vmax 7.99 mg/kg/day, Km 6.52 mg/L; Deleu 9.1 and 7.3; Odani
(Japanese) 9.8 mg/kg/day and 9.2 mg/L.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. Therapeutic range 10-20 mg/L trough (methods). Not an opioid.

## Covariates
Age (negative on Vmax), body weight (positive on Vmax), valproate co-therapy (Km × 2.66). Sex, dose, albumin,
bilirubin, liver enzymes, carbamazepine not retained. No genotype.

## Caveats
Small n, 81 sparse troughs, retrospective, compliance not stated, dosing interval varied, ka not estimable, dose
salt form unspecified, assay wording inconsistent (free vs total).

## Verification against stanpumpR
- R/drugs_phenytoin.R:41-42 (43 adults, V 0.61 L/kg, Vmax 6.12 mg/kg/day, Km 5.33 mg/L): Table 2 and abstract.
  **matches**. ("Adults": age range 18-84, but weight range 25-115 kg.)
- R/drugs_phenytoin.R:57-58 (65 kg, 330 mg/day, mean trough 11.2 mg/L): Table 1 65.4 kg, 330.5 mg/day, 11.2 mg/L.
  **matches**.
- R/drugs_phenytoin.R:59, "their own parameters give 10.5": **discrepancy (cannot reproduce)**. At the typical
  patient (age 41, 65.4 kg: Vmax = 6.12 × 65.4 = 400 mg/day, Km 5.33 mg/L), Css = Km·R/(Vmax − R) is 25.3 mg/L for
  R = 330.5 mg/day taken as phenytoin acid, or 16.8 mg/L if the dose is sodium salt (× 0.92 = 304 mg/day)
  (derived). Neither is 10.5. The mean of individual troughs (11.2) is expected to sit below the typical-patient
  prediction because Css is convex in Vmax; the code's comparison number should be recomputed or its method stated.
- R/drugs_phenytoin.R:58, model "15.4 mg/L at their mean dose and weight": by hand at total weight 65.4 kg Odani gives
  Vmax 505 mg/day and Css 17.4 (acid) or 13.9 mg/L (× 0.92) (derived); 15.4 presumably reflects the pharmacokinetic
  (fat-free-mass) weight; not checked (package not run).
- R/drugs_phenytoin.R:152-153: citation and DOI correct. **matches**.
- Supports the code's Odani values: the paper cites Odani's Vmax 9.8 mg/kg/day and Km 9.2 mg/L.
