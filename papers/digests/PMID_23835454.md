# Rinehart 2013: robustness of the LIR closed-loop fluid controller (simulation)

## Study
In silico Monte-Carlo study of the Learning Intravenous Resuscitator controller on a modified Upton and Ludbrook constrained Frank-Starling baroreceptor model (2-compartment, arterial/venous). Phase 1: 1000 runs, weight 35-100 kg, starting blood volume 65-100% of mean circulatory pressure (MCP) volume, contractility (CNT) 3000. Phase 2: 1000 runs, 75 kg, CNT 1500-6000 mm Hg/s, starting volume 70-100% (Table 2). Each run 2 h. Controller: boluses of 100 or 250 mL, reference target SV rise 15% to a 500 mL bolus; no bolus below a predicted 8% rise, test bolus in the 8-13% gray zone. Success = final blood volume within 250 mL of the optimal blood volume (OBV).

## Model and parameters
OBV = 61 x kg + 634 mL at CNT 3000; for varying CNT a cubic polynomial in CNT is printed (PDF p. 6, printed 1114; coefficients not transcribed because the text layer may corrupt exponents). Table 1 lists model parameters (venous compliance 0.45 L/mm Hg, compliance ratio 20, HRgain 80, etc.); the text layer misaligns its rows, so none are recorded.
Results: phase 1 volume error 25.4 +- 59.3 mL (Table 3), 1000/1000 within 250 mL, lower 95% Clopper-Pearson 99.6%; phase 2 error -60.7 +- 89.7 mL (Table 4), 982/1000 within 250 mL (lower CI 97.2% in the body, 97.1% in the abstract), 1000/1000 within 350 mL. Fluid given 858 +- 526 mL (phase 1), 764 +- 464 mL (phase 2).
