# SYMLIN label 2019: pramlintide acetate, subcutaneous

The file is what its name says: the full US prescribing information for SYMLIN, "Revised: 12/2019"
(Highlights, p. 1), 49 PDF pages, of which pp. 24-30 are the Medication Guide and pp. 31-49 the SymlinPen 60
and 120 Instructions for Use. Each chunk of the text layer is one PDF page and the printed page numbers equal
the PDF page numbers. The repository's registry links the 2015 version (s025,
NJ_fda-021332s025lbl); every PK number below is identical in that version.

## Study
A label, not a study. Product: pramlintide (as acetate), 1000 mcg/mL in SymlinPen 60 (1.5 mL; 15, 30, 45,
60 mcg doses) and SymlinPen 120 (2.7 mL; 60, 120 mcg doses), section 3, p. 4. Initial US approval 2005.
- Dosing (section 2, pp. 2-3): with every start, cut mealtime insulin by 50%. Type 2: 60 mcg SC immediately
  before each major meal, then 120 mcg; type 1: 15 mcg, titrated in 15-mcg steps to 30, 45 or 60 mcg, wait
  at least 3 days between steps. Major meal = at least 250 kcal or 30 g carbohydrate. Abdomen or thigh; arm
  not recommended (variable absorption). Never mixed with insulin.
- Boxed warning: severe hypoglycemia with insulin, mainly type 1, within 3 h of injection (p. 2).

- Dose-linear Cmax and AUC over 30-120 mcg (12.3). Arm injection in obese patients: exposure 20-36% higher,
  AUC CV 73-106%. Relative bioavailability not different by obesity, BMI or skinfold; 6.0-mm and 12.7-mm
  needles similar.
- Primary metabolite des-Lys1 pramlintide (2-37), active in vitro; no accumulation on repeat dosing.
- No IV data, no clearance or volume in the label.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Sections 12.1-12.2 (pp. 13-15): slows gastric emptying, lowers postprandial glucagon, reduces food intake.
Postprandial glucose fell in a crossover of 19 type 2 (lispro), 19 type 1 (regular insulin) and 21 type 1
(lispro) subjects (Figure 2). Caloric intake at a buffet fell about 21% (30 mcg, type 1) and 23% (120 mcg,
type 2), placebo-subtracted. No concentration-effect relationship, no EC50. Interactions (12.3, pp. 17-18):
acetaminophen Cmax down 29/23/20% and Tmax later by 72/48/48 min when given 0/1/2 h after 120 mcg
(n = 24, type 2); premixing with insulin changed pramlintide Cmax by up to -40% and AUC by up to +36%.

## Covariates
Section 8 and 12.3 Specific Populations (pp. 11-12, 16-17): renal impairment (single 60 mcg, type 1; 4
normal, 9 mild, 5 moderate, 3 severe, ClCr 15-29) showed no significant AUC or Cmax difference, with high
variability; no dose change for ClCr 15-89; ESRD, hepatic, geriatric, sex and race not studied for PK.
Not recommended in children. Clinical-trial ages 15-84 y.

## Caveats
- The 30-40% bioavailability and 48-min half-life are approximate label statements without the study n or
  population detail; Table 5 gives means only, no SD and no n.
- The overdose section (10 mg in 3 healthy volunteers) repeats the 48-min half-life.
