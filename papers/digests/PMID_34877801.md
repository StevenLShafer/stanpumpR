# Greene 2022: cenobamate on a CYP probe cocktail in healthy subjects

## Study
Phase 1, single-centre, open-label, fixed-sequence drug-interaction study (NCT03234699), US, March-July 2017, sponsor
SK Life Science (PDF p3). 24 healthy adults enrolled (38 screened), 21 completed; age 23-50 y (mean 35.3), BMI
19.9-29.7 kg/m2, 13 men / 11 women, 16 African American (66.7%) and 8 White (PDF p6, Results). Probes alone on day 1
(bupropion 150 mg) and day 7 (cocktail: midazolam 2 mg oral syrup, warfarin 5 mg, omeprazole 20 mg delayed-release),
then with cenobamate: midazolam on day 69 (cenobamate 100 mg/day), bupropion day 99 and cocktail day 105 (200 mg/day).
Cenobamate titration (Figure 2, PDF p4): 12.5 mg/day days 13-26, 25 mg days 27-40, 50 mg days 41-54, 100 mg days 55-70,
150 mg days 71-84, 200 mg days 85-110; self-administered outside confinement. Cenobamate trough samples within 1 h
of dosing on day 13 and within 1 h before the next dose on days 20, 27, ... 106 and 111 (PDF p4). Assay: HPLC-MS/MS (Worldwide Clinical
Trials for cenobamate, PPD for the probes). NCA in Phoenix WinNonlin 6.3; GMRs from a mixed model on the log scale;
no-effect bounds 0.80-1.25.

The abstract's GMRs (midazolam 0.734 and 0.277, bupropion 0.615, S-warfarin 1.14, omeprazole 2.07) agree with
Table 1 to rounding. Individual troughs by dose are in Figure 6 (PDF p10, rendered): at 200 mg/day roughly 8-25 ug/mL,
rising and more scattered toward the end of the study; the paper prints only the medians quoted above. The Discussion
(PDF p11-12) adds: mean trough 19.0 ug/mL at 200 mg/day in the earlier ascending-dose study (Vernillet 2020); in vitro
Ki for CYP2C19 110 uM, "near" a 200 mg/day steady-state exposure of about 89 uM.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None for cenobamate; end points are probe AUC0-last and Cmax. Safety (Table 2, PDF p10): 20/24 subjects had 59 TEAEs;
somnolence 5 subjects (20.8%) on cenobamate alone; 2 discontinued for TEAEs (rash, abscess).

## Covariates
None. The authors conclude cenobamate induces CYP3A4/5 dose-dependently (weak at 100, moderate at 200 mg/day), weakly
induces CYP2B6, moderately inhibits CYP2C19 and has a negligible effect on CYP2C9.

## Caveats
Healthy subjects, two thirds African American, n 20-21 in the cenobamate periods; fixed sequence (time and
titration confounded); no transporter assessment (authors). The 18.4 ug/mL is a median of troughs on 200 mg/day
over days 85-111 (which day not stated), with probes co-dosed. The "phase III" 15.5 ug/mL is quoted from Krauss 2020
(ref 11), which Vashi 2023 Table 1 lists as phase 2 study C017; the Discussion also writes the current study's range
as "15.5-18.4", which it does not explain.
