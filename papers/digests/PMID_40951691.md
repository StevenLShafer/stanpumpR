# Koh 2025: popPK/PD of enteric-coated low-dose aspirin and salicylate

## Study
Pooled period 1 of two open-label phase I crossover studies (NCT05481307, NCT05699070) in healthy Korean adults:
100 mg enteric-coated aspirin once daily for 5 days, as capsule (Astrix, Boryungbio) or tablet (Aspirin Protect,
Bayer). 44 subjects, 20-49 y, 157.7-185.5 cm, 55.6-89.5 kg, BMI 19.5-26.9 (Table S1, demographics, is in the
supplement, not this PDF). PK sampling 0-24 h on days 1 and 5; UPLC-MS/MS for ASA (LLOQ 10 ng/mL) and SA (LLOQ
200 ng/mL); concentrations converted to molar units for fitting. TXB2 (serum, ELISA) pre-dose day 1 and 24 h day 5
only. Monolix 2021R1 (SAEM), BLQ ignored (M1). Observation count: abstract says 669 plasma ASA+SA concentrations;
Results give 317 ASA + 379 SA (= 696) + 83 TXB2 = 779 (internal inconsistency in the paper). External validation:
16 volunteers, 80 and 160 mg EC aspirin for 7 days (ref 17).

## Model and parameters
ASA one compartment (V3/F), SA two compartments (V4/F central, V5/F peripheral). Dual absorption into a pre-systemic
compartment (2): fraction fr zero-order (duration Tk0, with Lag0), remainder first-order (ka). Pre-systemic -> ASA
central (k23) or -> SA central (k24). ASA -> SA by k34. SA eliminated by CLm/F. Weight on k34 and CLm/F as power
functions normalised to the covariate median (Methods). Table 1:

stated in the main text (Table S1/Appendix 1 not in this PDF).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Serum TXB2 by an indirect-response (turnover) model with ASA inhibiting production (Emax form; IC50 and gamma fixed
from literature). Predicted steady-state TXB2 inhibition at 168 h: 160 mg 87.7% (capsule) / 89.3% (tablet); 80 mg
84.9% / 91.1%. 80-90% inhibition taken as adequate. No ke0, no analgesic concentration. Not an opioid; no MEAC.

## Covariates
Formulation on ka; body weight (power) on k34 and CLm/F. Age, height, BMI not retained.

## Caveats
100 mg only (validated at 80/160 mg; linear model will fail at analgesic doses, where salicylate elimination
saturates); healthy Korean adults 19-50 y; only two TXB2 time points; Table S1 and model code only in the
supplement; observation count inconsistent (669 vs 696).
