# Silva 2025: zonisamide population PK in refractory epilepsy (Coimbra)

## Study
Retrospective observational TDM study, Refractory Epilepsy Centre, CHUC, Coimbra, Portugal, Jan 2018-Apr 2022.
64 adults (28 M / 36 F), age median 31.5 (18-74) y, weight 71 (45-110) kg, height 165 (150-194) cm, BMI 24.74
(17.21-41.40), CKD-EPI GFR 109.44 (76.81-143.71), haematocrit 40.8%, RBC 4.60 x 10^12/L (Table 1). Daily ZNS dose
25-600 mg (200 mg n = 21, 300 n = 17, 400 n = 17; 61/64 at <= 400 mg); 0.4-9.1 mg/kg/day. 92.19% on polytherapy
(1-5 other ASDs).

Sampling: 114 plasma concentrations, about 1 h post-dose (0.5-3.3 h) and pre-dose (8.6-14.4 h), last day of
admission. Assay: lithium-heparin plasma, liquid-liquid extraction, HPLC-DAD (Gonçalves 2018), LLOQ 0.5 mg/L,
linear 0.5-50 mg/L. NONMEM 7.4, FOCE-I; bootstrap (100) and pcVPC (1000).

## Model and parameters
One compartment, first-order absorption and elimination (better than two-compartment and Michaelis-Menten);
exponential BPV on CL/F, V/F, ka; proportional residual error. Final model Table 2:

The paper gives CL/F 12.68 mL/min and V/F 0.69 L/kg for a typical 71 kg patient (Discussion), and a half-life of
about 44 h (Discussion). Derived: ln2 x 48.10/0.761 = 43.8 h. Shrinkage: eta CL 13.63%, V 37.48%, ka 55.06%;
epsilon 49.49%. Condition number 6.29.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD model. Therapeutic reference range used: 10-40 mg/L (Patsalos 2008/2018), applied to simulated steady-state
troughs. Simulations (Tables 3-4), INDDL = 0, median trough: once daily 100/200/300/400/500/600 mg = 4.84, 9.40,
13.74, 18.62, 23.19, 28.10 mg/L; twice daily 5.18, 10.14, 15.25, 20.11, 25.41, 30.80 mg/L. Best target attainment
without inducers: 400 mg qd or bid, 500 mg qd (81.5%, 84.4%, 80.2% within range). Not an opioid: no MEAC, ke0 or
potency ratio.

## Covariates
Only the inducer drug load on CL/F. INDDL = sum of (prescribed daily dose / WHO defined daily dose) over
carbamazepine, oxcarbazepine, phenytoin, phenobarbital (Methods 2.1). Theta 1.63. The equation is not printed, but
the stated CL/F increases of about 28%, 108%, 239% at INDDL 0.5, 1.5, 2.5 (Discussion) equal 1.63^INDDL (derived),
i.e. CL/F = 0.761 x 1.63^INDDL L/h. Covariate explained 16% of CL BPV. Weight and BMI were significant univariately
but dropped in stepwise selection; none on V/F; haematocrit, RBC, GFR, eslicarbazepine, daily dose not significant.

## Caveats
Sparse TDM data (about 2 samples per patient), so the large BPV on ka and high shrinkage; retrospective; adherence
not verified. Internal inconsistency: 114 concentrations, yet sample-window counts n = 143 and n = 173 are given.
Table 1 co-medication rows are offset in the text layer; reconciled with the Results text: carbamazepine 18 (28.13%),
clobazam 17, clonazepam 9, diazepam 3, eslicarbazepine 15, lacosamide 2, lamotrigine 5, levetiracetam 23 (35.94%),
oxcarbazepine 6, perampanel 12, phenobarbital 2, phenytoin 1, pregabalin 2, topiramate 1, valproate 15 (my
reconstruction). Table 4 rows are interleaved in the text layer; values above follow the Results text (4.84, 9.40
once daily). Table 4 once-daily INDDL 2.5 at 600 mg (3.35) is lower than at 500 mg (4.46), apparently a misprint.
V/F 0.69 L/kg is below the 1.1-1.7 L/kg the paper itself cites. Whole-plasma concentrations; ZNS concentrates in
red cells (up to 8-fold). Adults only.

  (derived). Not applying it is still consistent with having no co-medication field.
- R/drugs_zonisamide.R:23 / help :7, half-life 44 h. **Matches** (paper about 44 h; derived 43.8 h).
- R/drugs_zonisamide.R:24-26 and help :7 attribute the short half-life to a cohort "mostly" / "largely" on enzyme
  inducers, whose effect the no-inducer intercept "still carries". **Discrepancy**: at most 27 of 64 patients took an
  inducer (CBZ 18, OXC 6, PB 2, PHT 1, possibly overlapping; under 42%), and 0.761 L/h is already the INDDL = 0
  value (the cohort-wide base estimate was 1.01 L/h). The low volume (0.69 L/kg) is the main reason the half-life is
  shorter than in healthy volunteers; that part of the header (volume on the low side) is supported.
- R/drugs_zonisamide.R:31-33 (linear; source linear). **Matches**: one-compartment linear model, Michaelis-Menten
  rejected, dose not a covariate.
- Body-size scaling with FFM (R/drugs_zonisamide.R:36-37, 60): the paper found no size covariate in the final model;
  scaling is a stanpumpR policy, not from the source. Reference weight median 71 kg (code's 70 kg reference is close).
- Band 10-40 (R/drugs_zonisamide.R:39, 74): **matches** the range the paper uses (10-40 mg/L).
