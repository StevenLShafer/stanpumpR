# Tracy 2005: CYP1A2, CYP2D6 and CYP3A activity across pregnancy

## Study
Prospective longitudinal phenotyping (NICHD MFMU Network; Pittsburgh and West Virginia). 35 pregnant women aged
18-35 recruited before 14 weeks; smokers and users of inducers/inhibitors excluded. Two CYP2D6 poor metabolisers
(urinary dextromethorphan/dextrorphan > 0.3) excluded; 28 extensive metabolisers completed all periods; 3 with
incomplete urine collections (> 5 SD outliers) excluded, leaving **n = 25**. Weight not reported.
Studied at 14-18, 24-28 and 36-40 weeks of gestation and at 6-8 weeks post partum (the reference state).
Oral probes after overnight fast and 48 h caffeine abstinence: caffeine 100 mg, dextromethorphan 15 mg,
acetaminophen 500 mg (n = 20; reported elsewhere). Saliva at 0, 1, 4, 8, 12, 24 h; 24-h urine. HPLC assays.
- CYP1A2: apparent oral caffeine clearance, Dose/AUC0-inf from saliva (WinNonlin NCA).
- CYP2D6: urinary dextromethorphan/dextrorphan ratio (O-demethylation).
- CYP3A: urinary dextromethorphan/3-hydroxymorphinan ratio (N-demethylation). A lower ratio means higher activity.
Statistics on log-transformed data; repeated-measures ANOVA; Wilcoxon signed-rank on within-subject % change,
significance P < .016 (0.05/3).

## Model and parameters
No PK model. Results (within-subject % change in activity versus the same woman's postpartum value, mean ± SD;
abstract p 1 and Figures 1B, 2B, 3B, PDF pp 4-5, printed 636-637; all P < .001 for % change):

| Enzyme (probe) | 14-18 wk | 24-28 wk | 36-40 wk |
|---|---|---|---|
| CYP1A2 (caffeine CL/F) | -32.8 ± 22.8% | -48.1 ± 27% | -65.2 ± 15.3% |
| CYP2D6 (DTM/DT) | +25.6 ± 58.3% | +34.8 ± 41.4% | +47.8 ± 24.7% |
| CYP3A (DTM/3-HM) | about +35% | about +35-36% | about +38% |

CYP3A values are given numerically only as "35%-38%" (abstract; results p 4); per-period bars read from Figure 3B
are roughly 35, 36 and 38%, with SD whiskers reaching about 69-73% (read from figure, approximate).
Figure 1A medians (caffeine apparent oral clearance, read from the box plot): about 61, 49, 31 mL/min at the three
periods vs about 97 mL/min post partum. Caffeine volume of distribution did not differ across periods (data not
shown). No correlation of CYP1A2 or CYP3A activity with serum estrogen or progesterone.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No pharmacodynamics. Opioid audit: no MEAC or analgesic concentration, no ke0 / t½ke0 / tPeak / hysteresis, no
equianalgesic or potency ratio. The only opioid content is the authors' mention that codeine (CYP2D6) and
methadone (CYP3A) are affected substrates (p 6), with no drug-specific data.

## Covariates
Gestational period only. No relation to estrogen or progesterone.

## Caveats
- Comparator is the same women at 6-8 weeks post partum, not non-pregnant women or any published model.
- The metrics are urinary metabolic ratios (CYP2D6, CYP3A) and an oral caffeine clearance; none is a clearance of
  an anaesthetic drug. A % change in a ratio is not a % change in a drug's systemic clearance, especially for
  high-extraction drugs.
- Large between-subject SD (CYP2D6 at 14-18 wk: SD 58.3% around a 25.6% mean).
- Internal inconsistencies: Figure 3B legend says the CYP3A change was assessed by dextromethorphan/dextrorphan
  (the methods and Figure 3A use 3-hydroxymorphinan); the Comment (p 6) gives CYP3A as "approximately 30%" while the
  results give 35-38%; Comment cites mean caffeine-clearance reductions 40/37/68% versus within-subject 33/48/65%.
- 5 West Virginia subjects did all sampling as outpatients; 4-12 h saliva collected at home.
