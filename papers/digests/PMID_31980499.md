# Liao 2020: metformin in pregnant women with GDM against nonpregnant women with T2DM

## Study
Multicentre, prospective, randomised phase I-II PK study (OPRU network; NCT01329016). Pregnant women with GDM randomised before 33 weeks to metformin monotherapy (n = 24) or metformin + glyburide (n = 30) (glyburide-alone arm not reported). Comparators: 24 nonpregnant women with T2DM, 18-45 y, on metformin for clinical reasons: 13 enrolled here plus 11 from "another similar study" for which the paper cites Eyal 2010 (PDF p2 / printed 265). Results (p3): 57 GDM subjects at 26-38 weeks of gestation took part; doses 1000-2000 mg/day in two divided doses (four took it three times a day). Metformin immediate release, initiated at 500 or 1000 mg bid and titrated. Exclusions included serum creatinine >1.2 mg/dL.

Steady-state PK day: fast >=5 h, dose given with a standardised meal (toast, margarine, 240 mL Boost Plus); plasma at 0-12 h (13 times), urine in 4-h blocks. LC-MS/MS, LLOQ 4.95 ng/mL. NCA: CL/F = dose/AUC; Vbeta/F = (CL/F)/k; CLR = Ae/AUC; F = Ae/dose (metformin is excreted unchanged); CL = (CL/F) x F; CrCL measured from urine; CLsec = CLR - CrCL (fu = 1). Per-kg values use actual weight. Mann-Whitney by dose level; then a linear mixed-effects model on all data with dose and weight as random effects.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None (no glucose end point in this paper). Cord: arterial 290 +/- 233 ng/mL, venous 331 +/- 257, maternal 262 +/- 231; cord/maternal ratios 1.5 +/- 0.5 (arterial), 1.4 +/- 0.5 (venous); 14 arterial and 11 venous of 38 below LLOQ (p4).

## Covariates
Pregnancy (by group) and dose level. In the pooled linear mixed-effects model pregnancy raised F, CL, Vbeta, CLR and CrCL significantly but did **not** significantly alter half-life, Tmax, Vbeta/F or CL/F (Results p3). Renal CL correlated with CrCL (r = 0.67) and CLsec (r = 0.96); CL/F correlated poorly with CrCL (r = 0.18, P = 0.06). Transporter genotypes (OCT1, OCT2, MATE1, MATE2-K, PMAT) not associated with any PK measure.

## Caveats
- Comparator is nonpregnant women with T2DM, not the same women postpartum; the 500 mg nonpregnant group is n = 9.
- The 27% and 33% are 500-mg-only results. At 1000 mg CL/F was lower (not higher) in pregnancy (P = 0.4), and the pooled mixed model found no significant effect on CL/F or Vbeta/F.
- Abstract and Results disagree on the dose effect within pregnancy (500 to 1000 mg): abstract Vbeta/F +28%, weight-adjusted +32%, CL/F +25%, weight-adjusted +28%; Results (p4) +22%, +25%, +29%, +33%. Table 1 supports the Results (450/368 = 1.22; 4.99/4.00 = 1.25; 1405/1085 = 1.29; 15.6/11.7 = 1.33, derived).
- Doses given with a meal; steady state; GDM treated in late second and third trimester (26-38 weeks).
