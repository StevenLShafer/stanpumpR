# Tompson 2008 (COMPASS): lamotrigine IR twice daily to XR once daily

## Study
- Open-label conversion study, 12 US centres (8 enrolling), Oct 2005 - Jun 2006; GSK-sponsored (p. 411).
- 44 subjects with epilepsy, >= 13 y (enrolled median age 38.5, range 18-88 y), 25 F / 19 M, 98 % white; stable
  twice-daily LTG-IR plus up to two AEDs (p. 412).
- Groups by co-medication: neutral (n = 15; monotherapy or non-inducing, non-inhibiting AEDs), induced (n = 15;
  carbamazepine, phenytoin, phenobarbital, primidone), inhibited (n = 14; valproate). VPA + inducer excluded.
- Baseline median LTG dose: neutral 400 mg/day (200-600), induced 600 (200-1200), inhibited 200 (50-800) (p. 413).
- Phases: 2 weeks IR bid, 2 weeks XR qd at the same total daily dose, 1 week IR. Serum sampled over 24 h on day 14
  (IR, 21 samples), day 15 and day 28 (XR, 13 samples). HPLC-MS/MS, LLOQ 4 ng/mL. Non-compartmental analysis;
  dose-normalized mixed-model relative bioavailability (Table 2).

Background half-lives quoted from the 2006 Lamictal label: about 15, 30 and 60 h with inducers, neutral and
inhibiting AEDs (Discussion); Introduction: single-dose t1/2 33 h, about 24 h on multiple dosing; inducers 24 -> 13 h,
valproate 24 -> 60 h.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Seizure diaries only (no change between phases); no concentration-effect analysis. Not an opioid paper.

## Covariates
Co-medication group only (descriptive).

## Caveats
Open label, small groups (35 completed); wide dose ranges; not powered for bioequivalence; Table 1 not normalized
for dose; sponsor-authored.
