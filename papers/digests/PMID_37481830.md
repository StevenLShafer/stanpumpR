# Li 2023: treosulfan population PK, adults and children (BSA covariate)

## Study
- Pooled analysis of 9 trials: 7 historical (adult and a few paediatric; the sponsor's earlier trials and literature) plus the two paediatric allo-HSCT trials MC-FludT.16/NM and MC-FludT.17/M (83 paediatric PK subjects). Analysis set 2435 samples (p4). Table 2 (n = 198; one subject with an unknown dose excluded): age median 16.0 y (0.3-77.0), adults 51.0, children 5.3 (0.3-17.0); weight median 54.6 kg (5.6-114.0), children 19.3 (5.6-82.0); BSA median 1.62 m2 (0.30-2.38), children 0.78 (0.30-2.00); 110 male, 88 female; 162 malignant, 36 non-malignant.
- Treosulfan IV over 2 h on 3 days at 10, 12 or 14 g/m2 (SOLCA3 single doses 8 or 10 g/m2; SOLCA5 single 20-47 g/m2 before autologous HSCT).
- Plasma assays differ: historical trials by HPLC with refractometric detection; the two recent trials by HPLC-MS, corrected for haematocrit and dilution (p2). A SHIFT parameter absorbs the difference.
- NONMEM 7.1.0, FOCE with interaction; covariates by PsN stepwise covariate modelling (forward p<0.05, backward p<0.01); 500-profile simulations per BSA level 0.3-2.0 m2; non-parametric bootstrap (97.8% successful) (pp3-5).

## Model and parameters
Two compartments, dosing into the central compartment, linear elimination. Covariate form (PDF p4, read on the page image): PK = TVPK x (BSA/1.75)^theta for CL, V1, V2 and Q, so typical values are for a 1.75 m2 patient. Residual error constant CV. IIV log-normal on CL, V1, V2, Q with covariances V1-CL and CL-V2. Table 3 (PDF p5, read on the page image):

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD model. Target exposure interval AUC 760-3600 ug*h/mL (read on the page image; the text layer prints it as mg), chosen because no AUC relation with time to granulocyte engraftment was seen in that range; linear regression of engraftment time on AUC p = 0.16 (p5, Fig 4). Simulations: 0.77% of simulated AUCs below 760 and 0.03% above 3600. No ke0, no concentration-effect model. (Not an opioid paper: no MEAC, ke0, tPeak or potency ratio.)

## Covariates
BSA on all four PK parameters (power, reference 1.75 m2). Tested and not retained: age (maturation function of van der Stoep, OFV gain about 5 points), weight, sex, CCG (creatinine clearance or GFR; RSE 33% on CL so dropped), data-set flag (replaced by the SHIFT). CREG excluded for over 5% missing; SITEID excluded for correlation with the data flag (p4).

## Caveats
- Two assay methods pooled; SHIFT 1.36 is an empirical correction and the authors say interpretation needs caution (p7).
- Paediatric data dominate the new trials; the historical set is largely adults. Weight versus BSA decided by OFV only (17665 vs 17627).
- The authors are mostly sponsor (medac) employees; dose recommendations: 10, 12 and 14 g/m2 for BSA <0.4, 0.4 to <0.9 and >=0.9 m2 (abstract, Table 4).
- The text layer prints the AUC target as mg*h/mL; the rendered page shows ug*h/mL.
