# Zomorodi 1998: midazolam population PK by TCI after CABG

## Study
- Multicentre (Duke, Emory, Palo Alto VA), 90 consenting adults (>= 35 y) needing >= 6 h ventilation after elective CABG; LVEF > 25%; baseline creatinine 1.2 mg/dL (0.5-2.4). 27 excluded (12 assay interference, 6 missing infusion files, 9 infusion files not matching concentrations) -> 63 analysed (Table 2): Duke 19, Emory 22, PAVA 22; ages 60-67 (means), weights 84-87 kg, BSA 1.97-2.05 m2, 7 women. Midazolam infusion 8.1-11.6 h, total dose 28.7-48.4 mg (means by centre).
- Midazolam and sufentanil both by TCI (STANPUMP at PAVA and Emory, CACI II at Duke; identical internal algorithms). Midazolam TCI used Bührer's kinetics (ref 26); sufentanil Hudson's. Induction target 150 ng/mL, intraoperative 75-150 ng/mL; ICU restart at 50 ng/mL, titrated to Ramsay 5 for >= 2 h, then down to 3-4 for >= 4 h more (p. 1420).
- Arterial sampling at baseline, before each target change, 5-120 min into each target, and 5 min to 24 h after stop. 1,708 samples, 27 excluded -> 1,681 analysed. GC-MS assay (Tufts), CV 4.7% midazolam, 7.2% 1-OH-midazolam; LOQ 3 and 10 ng/mL.
- NONMEM, naive pooled and mixed-effects; two- and three-compartment; constant-CV intraindividual error; log-normal IIV. Covariates height, weight, age, BSA, BMI. Cross-validation (appendix 1).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD model, no ke0 or tPeak (PD is in the companion paper, Somma 1998, PMID 9856718). Context-sensitive 50% decrement times (Fig. 3, present-study kinetics): 1-2 h for infusions < 12 h, 3 h at 24 h, 4 h at 36 h; an 80% decrease after a 24 h infusion could take ~20 h. 1-OH-midazolam above LOQ in only 7 patients, at most 20% of parent. Not an opioid paper: no MEAC or potency ratio.

## Covariates
BSA linearly on V2 and Cl1 (Table 4) improved MDWR from 1.39 to -0.08% and OFV by 21, but not MDAWR or cross-validated performance; final recommended model has no covariates.

## Caveats
- Bührer's volunteers were younger (mean 38 y) and got 3.75-25 mg (mean 13 mg) over 3-5 min (p. 1426); the Bührer set predicted the infusion phase reasonably but under-predicted increasingly after the infusion stopped (Fig. 2A).
- The paper prints the Bührer column without saying how it was derived from Bührer's data.
- Table 3's Bührer rate constants are rounded and not perfectly self-consistent (derived: Cl2/V1 = 2.01/3.3 = 0.609; Cl3/V3 = 0.83/96.76 = 0.0086 vs printed 0.0085).
- Mostly 24 h of sampling; elderly cardiac surgical patients on sufentanil.
