# Reeder 2025: piperacillin-tazobactam PopPK and PTA in critically ill adults

## Study
Prospective, opportunistic-sampling study at Univ. of Iowa and Vanderbilt (PDF p.2-3). Adult ICU patients (>=18 y, non-pregnant) given only piperacillin-tazobactam by intermittent IV infusion. 112 enrolled; 10 on CRRT (8 CVVHDF, 2 CVVH; Table 1 footnote, Results). Table 1 medians (range): CLcr,TBW 82 (10-289) mL/min; CLcr,LBW 51 (8-173); TBW 92 (41-160) kg; LBW 57 (30-89) kg; age 64 (21-88) y; 50 F / 62 M; 90% white. Doses 3000/375 mg (pip/tazo) in all but one patient (4000/500), every dose a 4-h infusion; 764 doses (Results, PDF p.3-4). Samples at non-standard times, 1-8 per subject, mean 2.2. Piperacillin: 239 concentrations, 111 subjects; tazobactam: 229 concentrations, 108 subjects (outliers with |WRES|>5 removed). Assay: LC-MS/MS on **total plasma** concentration; calibration 0.1-150 mg/L (pip), 0.25-150 mg/L (tazo). NONMEM 7.4.3, FOCE-I, ADVAN2; pc-VPC with PsN; bootstrap.

## Model and parameters
One compartment, zero-order input, first-order elimination, for both drugs; combined proportional + additive residual error; exponential IIV on CL and V (PDF p.4-6). Covariates: piperacillin CLcr,LBW on renal clearance, LBW on non-renal clearance, CRRT on total clearance; tazobactam CLcr,LBW on renal clearance, CRRT on total clearance. IIV on CL fell 60% to 47% (pip) and 57% to 40% (tazo). Table 2 (PDF p.6, printed 457; confirmed on rendered page). Covariate form: CLR x (CLcr,LBW/51 mL/min)^theta; CLNR x (LBW/57 kg)^theta (pip). V is not weight-scaled.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No ke0, EC50 or effect-site model (antibiotic; not an opioid, so no MEAC/potency audit applies). Targets were PK/PD indices on **free** piperacillin (assumed 30% protein binding): 50% fT>MIC, 100% fT>MIC, 100% fT>4xMIC; MIC range simulated 0.125-128 mg/L (PDF p.2-3). Breakpoint = highest MIC with >=90% of 11,100 virtual subjects (111 x 100 replicates) at target. For tazobactam, free fraction taken as 0.70 and the minimal critical concentration (MCC) threshold was 4 mg/L (CLSI breakpoint); no target attainment computed. Key results with 100% fT>MIC (Table 3, text layer): 4 g q6h 30-min infusion breakpoints 16/8/4 mg/L for CLcr <60 / 60-129 / >=130 mL/min; 4 g q6h 4-h infusion 32/16/8; 16 g/day continuous 64/32/16 (all patients 32). Continuous 6 g/day recommended for CLcr <60, 9 g/day for 60-129, 12 g/day for >=130 mL/min at MIC 16 mg/L (Abstract). Tazobactam free concentrations stayed above 4 mg/L through the interval in >50% of patients on 0.5 g q8h (30-min or 4-h), >95% on 1.5 g/day continuous.

## Covariates
CLcr,LBW (Cockcroft-Gault with lean body weight), LBW, CRRT as above. Not retained: age, sex, TBW, SOFA, sepsis, septic shock, AKI, hepatic function, ventilation.

## Caveats
Authors: no outcomes recorded; one compartment chosen because frequent 4-h infusions masked distribution (differs from prior two-compartment models); large residual IIV so TDM still needed. Observed V about twice healthy-volunteer values. Sparse (mean 2.2 samples), only 10 CRRT patients (CLCRRT tazo RSE 70%), 90% white, all but one given 3 g by 4-h infusion so the 30-min and continuous results are extrapolations from the model. Patients enrolled while on 4-h infusions only.

## Verification against stanpumpR
No repo location cites this paper, so nothing to transcribe-check. Independent comparison with R/drugs_piperacillin.R (Kong 2025, two compartments, CL 10.6 L/h at 70 kg, 35 y, normal creatinine; V1 10.4 + V2 11.6 L at 70 kg; plotted as total plasma mg/L, lines 6-8, 123-126):
- Reeder's total clearance without CRRT, 4.55 L/h (Table 2), is much lower than Kong's 10.6 L/h reference. A rough hand estimate (derived, not run through the code): Kong's age-decline term alone for a 64-year-old gives about 0.77 of the 35-year value, and a 60-kg fat-free weight about 0.89, so about 7 L/h at normal creatinine. Reeder's population is ICU patients with median CLcr,LBW 51 mL/min, i.e. impaired renal function, which would plausibly lower clearance further; the direction (Reeder lower) fits that but I did not compute the code at Reeder's covariates.
- Volume: Reeder V 23.5 L (one compartment, mean ICU patient, no weight scaling) is close to Kong's V1 + V2 = 22.0 L at 70 kg. Reeder's own comparison (Discussion) was to healthy-volunteer volumes, not to Kong.
- Both report total plasma piperacillin; the repo's MIC lines are free-drug (header line 119), consistent with Reeder's use of 30% binding.
