# Brenzavvy (bexagliflozin) label, 09/2023

The document is what its name says: the US prescribing information for BRENZAVVY (bexagliflozin) 20 mg tablets, Initial US Approval 2023, Revised 09/2023, from the FDA's 214373s001 label file.

## Study
Label, not a study. Indication: adjunct to diet and exercise for glycaemic control in adults with type 2 diabetes; not for type 1 (section 1, PDF p2).

Dosage and administration (section 2, p2): 20 mg orally once daily in the morning, with or without food; do not crush or chew. Assess renal function first; **not recommended if eGFR <30 mL/min/1.73 m2**. Correct volume depletion first. **Withhold at least 3 days, if possible, before major surgery or procedures with prolonged fasting**; resume when clinically stable and eating (2.3). Section 5.1 (p2-3): ketoacidosis risk (including euglycaemic, glucose <250 mg/dL), surgery a precipitant. Urinary glucose excretion persists 3 days after stopping, and postmarketing reports describe ketoacidosis or glucosuria lasting more than 6 days, up to 2 weeks, after stopping SGLT2 inhibitors.

Metabolism by UGT1A9 and, less, CYP3A; inactive 3'-O-glucuronide is 32.2% of parent AUC. Mass balance ([14C] solution): 91.6% recovered, 51.1% faeces (mostly parent), 40.5% urine (mostly glucuronide); parent 1.5% urine, 28.7% faeces. P-gp substrate.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
12.2 (p11): dose-dependent urinary glucose excretion (UGE) with more urine; 20 mg gives near-maximal UGE, maintained on repeated dosing. No QTc prolongation at 5x the dose. In renal impairment (single 20 mg dose, T2DM), 24-h UGE was 17%, 60% and 83% lower (mild, moderate, severe) than with normal function, while AUC was 7%, 34% and 54% higher (p12). No concentration-effect parameters (EC50, Emax) are printed.

## Covariates
- Renal (p12): AUC +7%, +34%, +54% for eGFR 60-89, 30-59 and <30, "not clinically meaningful"; the glucose-lowering effect declines with renal function; haemodialysis not studied.
- Hepatic (p12): Child-Pugh B AUC +28%, Cmax +6.3%; Child-Pugh C not studied, not recommended (8.7).
- Age, weight, sex and race: no clinically relevant effect (population PK).
- Interactions (Figure 1, p13, rendered), geometric mean ratio (90% CI) of bexagliflozin AUC / Cmax: metformin 88 (80-97) / 109 (94-125); glimepiride 96 (81-114) / 89 (70-114); sitagliptin 114 (printed "10, 120") / 127 (112-143); exenatide 148 (130-167) / 125 (104-150); probenecid 144 (131-158) / 109 (98-120); verapamil 97 (88-106) / 106 (89-127); rifampin 600 mg x 5 days 86 (70-106) / 120 (94-152), the regimen possibly not the maximal effect. Section 7 nonetheless warns that UGT inducers may significantly reduce exposure. Figure 2: bexagliflozin raised glimepiride AUC to 127 (110-148); no relevant effect on metformin, sitagliptin or digoxin (AUC 103, 92-115).
- Specific populations: not recommended in the second and third trimesters (rat renal findings, 8.1) or while breastfeeding (rat milk:plasma about 2, 8.2); paediatric safety not established (8.4).

## Caveats
- The printed half-life (about 12 h) is longer than CL/F and V/F imply (9.5 h) and than the 20% accumulation implies; the label does not reconcile these.
- Figure 1's sitagliptin AUC lower bound "10" cannot be right for a point estimate of 114 drawn with a narrow bar; it is printed that way.
- Section 5.1 cites 12.2 for the 3-day persistence of UGE, but 12.2 as printed does not state it.
