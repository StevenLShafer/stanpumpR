# Barbhaiya 1994: buspirone in renal and hepatic impairment, single and multiple doses

## Study
Open-label, parallel-group, six groups, 41 volunteers/patients: Group 1 normal (6 M, 6 F; age 34, weight 65 kg, CLcr 98.2 mL/min/1.73 m2); renal impairment by creatinine clearance, not dialysed: Group 2 mild (CLcr 58.0, 40-75), Group 3 moderate (24.4, 20-40), Group 4 severe (9.54, <20); hepatic: Group 5 compensated cirrhosis, Group 6 decompensated cirrhosis (BSP clearance coefficient 0.071 and 0.053 1/min vs 0.13 normal) (Table 1; group sizes in the table are scrambled in the text layer; 17 renal and 12 hepatic patients completed). Dosing: 10 mg buspirone HCl tablet on Day 1, then 10 mg every 12 h from 36 h later for 9 days, last dose the morning of Day 10 (20 mg/day). Sampling on Days 1, 5, 10 (0 to 12 h; to 36 h on Days 1 and 10) plus 12-hourly troughs Days 3-9. Plasma buspirone and 1-pyrimidinylpiperazine (1-PP) by GC-MS, LLOQ 0.1 ng/mL (buspirone), 0.2 ng/mL (1-PP). Noncompartmental analysis; weighted repeated-measures ANOVA.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD end point; safety only. Adverse events (anxiety, difficulty concentrating, dizziness, headache, insomnia, vertigo) were mild to moderate and similar in frequency and intensity across groups (p.43). No therapeutic or toxic concentration range is given. Not an opioid; no MEAC, ke0, tPeak or potency ratio.

## Covariates
No model equations. Correlation of average steady-state buspirone concentration with BSP clearance coefficient (log fit, r = 0.678, P = 0.0003, Fig 3, 24 subjects), with serum albumin (r = 0.862) and total bilirubin (r = 0.842) (p.46). No correlation between creatinine clearance and apparent clearance (dose/AUC) or half-life. The paper states a clearance-vs-renal-function relation was found for 1-PP only in moderate to severe renal failure. Mild renal impairment had the highest buspirone levels of the renal groups, not in order of severity.

## Caveats
Very large intra- and inter-individual variability (SDs near or above means), small groups, mixed sexes and ages (impaired groups older), so the authors made no dosing recommendations beyond starting cautiously. Steady state was reached by day 3 in all groups. The single-dose comparator from other studies is only cited: earlier work reported buspirone clearance in renal impairment about a third to a half of normal (Caccia 1988) and a 16-fold plasma increase in cirrhosis (Dalhoff 1987). Total (not free) plasma concentrations; protein binding is cited as about 85% (buspirone) and 60% (1-PP).
