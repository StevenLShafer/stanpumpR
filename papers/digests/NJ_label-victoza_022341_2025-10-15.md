# VICTOZA label 2025: liraglutide

## Study
US prescribing information for liraglutide 6 mg/mL (18 mg/3 mL pen delivering 0.6, 1.2 or 1.8 mg), a once-daily
GLP-1 receptor agonist for type 2 diabetes in adults and children >= 10 y. The document is what its name says.

## Model and parameters
No compartmental model. Dosing (2.1, PDF p. 3): adults 0.6 mg SC daily for one week (not glycaemically effective),
then 1.2 mg, maximum 1.8 mg; pediatric >= 10 y start 0.6 mg, 0.6 mg steps weekly to 1.8 mg. Missed dose > 3 days:
restart at 0.6 mg (2.2, p. 4). Any time of day; abdomen, thigh or upper arm (2.3).

Dose-proportional over 0.6-1.8 mg. Thigh AUC 22% lower than abdomen; sites considered comparable. Molecular weight
3751.2 Da (11, p. 12). Intact drug not found in urine or faeces (6% and 5% of radioactivity as metabolites).

Internal consistency (derived, not resolved): 0.6 mg / 960 ng*h/mL gives CL/F 0.63 L/h; 1.8 mg/day at Css 128 ng/mL
gives 0.59 L/h; 0.693 x 13 L / 13 h gives 0.69 L/h. The printed apparent clearance of 1.2 L/h is about twice these.
The label does not say which population each figure comes from; recorded as an unresolved inconsistency within the
label.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Postprandial glucose AUC0-300min 35% (1.2 mg) and 38% (1.8 mg) below placebo; glucose-dependent insulin secretion and
preserved glucagon response at 7.5 mcg/kg; delayed gastric emptying; no QTc prolongation to 1.8 mg (12.2, pp. 13-14).
No exposure-response or therapeutic concentration. Not an opioid.

## Covariates
Age: no effect (65-83 y study; population PK 18-80 y). Females 25% lower weight-adjusted clearance. Race: no effect.
Body weight: exposure falls as weight rises; 1.2 and 1.8 mg adequate from 40 to 160 kg. Renal: single-dose AUC 35%,
19%, 29% and 30% lower in mild, moderate, severe impairment and ESRD than in healthy subjects; no dose adjustment
(8.6, 12.3). Hepatic: AUC 11%, 14%, 42% lower in mild, moderate, severe; no adjustment, use with caution (8.7, 12.3).
Pediatric (72 patients 10-17 y): PK consistent with adults (12.3, p. 15).

## Caveats
Summary values without SD or population; the CL/F figure conflicts with the label's own AUC, Css and V/F-half-life
arithmetic (above). One posting (Reference ID 5676386); later revisions may differ.
