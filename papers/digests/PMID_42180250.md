# Feng 2026: imipenem PBPK in children with renal impairment

## Study
GastroPlus 9.9 PBPK model, built first for healthy adults (digitised published concentrations; single and multiple IV infusions, 0.5 or 2 h, plus bolus), then adults with renal impairment (RI), then children with normal function, then extrapolated to children with RI (p3-4). RI classes by GFR (mL/min/1.73 m2): >= 90 normal, 60-89 mild, 30-59 moderate, 15-29 severe. Renal clearance = fraction unbound x GFR; no non-renal clearance (p3, p9). Population simulator n = 200 (1:1 sex). Validation: fold error predicted/observed < 2 for AUC and Cmax (p4). Adult RI validated on one 250 mg infusion (Gibson 1985) (p3). Paediatric validation on Claesson 1992 and Bradley 2023 (Supp Table 2). Applies to children >= 3 years, normal growth; no observed data in children with RI (p10-11). Drug is given with cilastatin; the model describes imipenem concentrations.

## Model and parameters
Inputs (Table 1, PDF p3): MW 299.35 g/mol, logP -3.9, pKa 3.44 (acid) and 10.88 (base), solubility 0.78 mg/mL, Fup 80% (default software values used in RI, which rise with RI), renal clearance (CLr) 10.71 L/h (University of Washington database, with cilastatin). Model outputs: adult CLr 10.726-11.176 L/h, children 7.886-8.489 L/h; Vss about 3.259 L/kg children vs 11.663 L/kg adults as stated (p4-5; units as printed, the L/kg for adults looks implausible, see below). ## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Not an opioid paper. No ke0 or MEAC. PK/PD index: %fT > MIC, target 70% (conservative; the literature range quoted 50-100%, p4), PTA >= 80% accepted, Monte Carlo. Table values (p7-8): at 15 mg/kg q6h with a 30 min infusion, PTA at MIC 2 mg/L was 48.81%, 50.71%, 60.95% and 99.52% for normal, mild, moderate, severe RI; at MIC 4 mg/L 39.28%, 41.19%, 49.52%, 80%. With a 3 h infusion at MIC 4 mg/L: 78.57%, 80%, 86.19%, 100%. Proposed doses (3 h infusion, q6h): 15 mg/kg normal and mild RI, 12 mg/kg moderate, 7 mg/kg severe; PTA at MIC 4 mg/L 78.57%, 80%, 81.19%, 85.71% (Table 4 and Figure 6C). Note the normal and mild PTAs stay below the paper's own 80% criterion.

## Covariates
Age and weight drive the software's built-in physiology; GFR sets renal clearance (CLr = Fup x GFR). Sensitivity (+/-10%): AUC0-inf sensitive to Fup and renal clearance (+10% CLr gives about -8% AUC, -1% Cmax); Cmax to Fup and Rbp (p6-7).

## Caveats
Authors: no PK data in children with RI, so the extrapolation is unvalidated; only renal-clearance changes modelled; no concomitant drugs; not for neonates, infants < 3 y or over- or underweight children (p10-11). Predicted paediatric clearance 7.886 L/h is between the 5.31 (2-6 y) and 9.60 (6-12 y) L/h reported by Bradley 2023 (p9). Table 4 labels AUC0-24h but values match single-dose AUC0-t (see open question). Vss in L/kg for adults (11.663) is implausibly large and probably L; not checkable.
