# Bryan 1985: once-daily ceftriaxone vs ampicillin + chloramphenicol for bacterial meningitis

## Study
Two-phase study at Hospital Couto Maia, Salvador, Bahia, Brazil (p361-362).
- **Phase A** (PK): 13 patients (9 under 1 year, 3 aged 2-5 years, 1 aged 29), all given ampicillin 300 mg/kg/day IV in six doses plus ceftriaxone 75 mg/kg load then 50 mg/kg every 12 h; on days 4-6 and 10-12 a single 80 mg/kg ceftriaxone dose was given and CSF and serum drawn 24 h later. Aetiology: H. influenzae 8, S. pneumoniae 1, purulent sterile CSF 4.
- **Phase B** (open randomized trial, random-number table): 52 enrolled, 16 excluded (Table 1), 18 per arm. Ceftriaxone 100 mg/kg load then 80 mg/kg IV every 24 h vs ampicillin 75 mg/kg load then 50 mg/kg every 4 h plus chloramphenicol 50 mg/kg load then 25 mg/kg every 6 h. Ages: ceftriaxone 3 months to 59 years, control 4 months to 14 years (Table 2 footnote). Treatment at least 10 days (7 for meningococcal disease). Exclusions: under 2 months, pregnancy, beta-lactam allergy, recurrent meningitis.
- **Assay**: agar well diffusion bioassay, E. coli 1346 indicator, samples deproteinized with acetonitrile, ampicillin inactivated with penicillinase (verified by parallel standards), triplicate. Phase A standards in pooled serum and 0.85% NaCl; phase B in pooled serum and CSF. Total drug.
- **Susceptibility**: macrotube dilution (NCCLS methods); MBC = concentration killing 97.0% of a 10^5 inoculum, read after 24 h. Repeat in Charlottesville with a 99.9% end point gave identical results or at most one dilution higher (p362). CSF bactericidal titre = dilution killing 97.0% of 10^5 CFU/ml.

## Model and parameters
No PK model; concentrations only (all ceftriaxone, total, bioassay):
- Phase A CSF 24 h after 80 mg/kg: days 4-6 mean 4.2 ± 1.9 ug/ml (range 1-5.4, n = 7); days 10-12 2.3 ± 0.9 (range 1.0-3.7, n = 7) (p363). One ventricular fluid level 12.5 ug/ml at 24 h, day 4.
- Phase B CSF after the 100 mg/kg load: 19.7 ± 10.6 ug/ml after 2 h, 10.5 ± 5.5 at 24 h (p364).
- Phase B CSF 20-24 h after 80 mg/kg: mean 1.4 ug/ml on days 2-4, 1.5 ug/ml on days 8-12 (p364; Figs. 1-2 give serum and CSF means ± SD in 4-h bins, small n per bin, values only readable approximately from log plots).
- Ventricular fluid 100 and 18.9 ug/ml at 12 and 21 h after 80 mg/kg (two patients, p364).
- Background cited (not measured here): CSF ~3-9% of serum; serum t½ 4.2-6.5 h in infants/children, 5.8-8.8 h in adults (p361, from refs).

MBC data:
- Abstract (p361): MBC90s of ceftriaxone against 54 CSF isolates were <=0.06 to 0.25 ug/ml. The abstract names pneumococcus, meningococcus and H. influenzae; Results (p364) list the 54 as H. influenzae n = 33, S. pneumoniae n = 9, N. meningitidis n = 7, Salmonella group B n = 5. No per-species MBC90 is tabulated.
- Table 4 (p364, rendered): individual phase B ceftriaxone-group isolates: H. influenzae x3 <=0.06, S. pneumoniae x2 <=0.06, Salmonella group B <=0.06, Klebsiella 0.5 ug/ml. No meningococcal isolate in Table 4.
- Table 5 (p365, rendered): control group, ampicillin and chloramphenicol MBCs only (N. meningitidis: ampicillin <0.06, chloramphenicol 2.0).
- Fold over MBC90 (authors): phase A 24-h CSF 83- and 38-fold above the MBC90 (p365); load 24-h CSF at least 90-fold (p364).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Not an opioid paper; no MEAC, ke0 or potency ratio. PD end points: CSF bactericidal titres 16-24 h after ceftriaxone 1:64 to >=1:2,048 for non-Enterobacteriaceae, 1:4 to 1:256 for Klebsiella and Salmonella; control group 1:8 to 1:256 (Tables 4-5, p364). CSF sterile within 24-48 h in 83-85% of both arms. Case fatality 17% (control) and 22% (ceftriaxone). Authors note titres were higher than predicted from CSF concentration/MBC (unexplained).

## Covariates
None analysed.

## Caveats
Small trial (n = 36), mostly infants; open label; bioassay; MBC end point 97% kill, not 99.9%; MBC90s are pooled ranges with small per-species n (meningococcus n = 7, so its MBC90 is effectively its highest or second-highest MBC); CSF, not plasma, is the compartment measured; Fig. bins have 1-10 samples.
