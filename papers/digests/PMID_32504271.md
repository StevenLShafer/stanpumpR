# Retout 2020: population PK of emicizumab in haemophilia A

## Study
- 389 persons with haemophilia A with (49.9%) or without factor VIII inhibitors, from HAVEN 1, 2, 3, 4 and the Japanese phase I/II study (Tables 1-3). Age 30.0 [1.22-77.0] years, weight 69.1 [9.50-156] kg, BMI 23.6 [13.1-52.4], BSA 1.81 [0.479-2.58] m2, albumin 45.0 [16.8-56.6] g/L (Table 2, median [min-max]). Race: white 62.7%, Asian 22.9%, black 8% (label 12.3).
- Doses: 3 mg/kg SC weekly x 4 then 1.5 mg/kg weekly (QW), 3 mg/kg every 2 weeks or 6 mg/kg every 4 weeks; phase I/II 0.3-3 mg/kg weekly. 8-71 samples per person.
- LLOQ 50 ng/mL (phase I/II), 100 ng/mL (phase III). NONMEM, FOCE-I; covariates by GAM then a full model, reduced on 95% CIs.

## Model and parameters
One compartment, first-order absorption and elimination, apparent parameters. The authors' reason for one compartment: an IgG's distribution phase is masked by SC absorption (Discussion).

Table 4 (reference BW 70 kg, ALB 45 g/L, age <= 30 years) and the equations printed under it:

    CL/F = 0.272 L/day x (BW/70)^0.911 x (1 - 0.0157 x (ALB - 45))
    V/F  = 10.4 L x (BW/70)^1.00 x (1 - 0.215 x BLK)
    KA   = 0.536 /day
    F    = 1 if AGE <= 30, else 1 - 0.00651 x (AGE - 30)

IIV (CV%): CL/F 28.7, V/F 25.9, KA 72.5; correlations CL-V 0.217, CL-KA -0.341. Residual: additive 0.025 ug/mL fixed, proportional 14.6%.

## Checks the paper prints (Results 3.2)
- Reference person on 1.5 mg/kg QW: Cav,ss 55.1 ug/mL; at ALB 33 g/L 46.4 (-16%), at 57 g/L 68.0 (+23%); 77 years -31%. All reproduced from the equations (Cav = dose x F / (CL/F x tau)); 57 g/L lies just past the studied maximum, 56.6.
- Individual mean elimination half-life 26.8 (SD 9.16) days, absorption 1.61 (0.957) days (Table 5). The typical values are 26.5 and 1.29 days; a mean of individual absorption half-lives exceeds the typical one when KA varies 72.5%.
- Infants under 1 year deferred to "a future publication" (Discussion).

## Label (HEMLIBRA, BLA 761083, revised 7/2025; papers/labels/NJ_label-hemlibra_761083_2025-07-21.pdf)
- 12.3: CL/F 0.27 L/day (28.4%), V/F 10.4 L (26.0%), half-life 26.9 +/- 9.1 days, absorption half-life 1.6 +/- 1 day; absolute SC bioavailability 80.4-93.1% at 1 mg/kg; dose-proportional 0.3-6 mg/kg. Table 4 steady-state troughs 51.2, 46.9 and 38.5 ug/mL (QW, Q2W, Q4W).
- 12.3 says PK is "not influenced by age (1 year to 77 years)", which the population analysis contradicts on F; predicted concentrations 19-33% lower below 6 months of age.
