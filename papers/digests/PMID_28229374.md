# Lammers 2017: CYP probe cocktail (incl. metoprolol IV and PO), fasting vs control

## Study
Open-label randomised crossover in 12 healthy Caucasian men (Table 1, PDF p5: median age 23 y, range 19-33;
height 186 cm, 175-194; weight 80 kg, 68-103). Each was to receive a five-probe cocktail on four occasions 4 weeks
apart: oral and IV after an overnight fast (control), oral and IV after 36 h of fasting. Only 8 completed all four;
the dataset pools an earlier oral-only study (9 subjects, 6 of whom also had IV) plus 3 oral-only and 1 IV+IV+oral
subject (p4, section 3.1). Metoprolol doses: 100 mg tablet (Teva) orally, 20 mg IV (1 mg/mL, 5 mL ampoules,
AstraZeneca) (p2-3). **The salt (tartrate or succinate) is not named**; the IV administration time is not stated.
Sampling: oral pre-dose and 1-10 h; IV pre-dose, 2 min to 9 h; further samples on days 2, 3, 8 and 15 (mainly for
warfarin) (p3). LC-MS/MS plasma, metoprolol LLOQ 1 ng/mL, ULOQ 200 ng/mL (p3); racemic (no enantiomers).
NONMEM 7.2 FOCE-I, log-transformed data, bootstrap n = 1000, VPC (p3-4). CYP2D6 genotype in 9 men (Table 1):
*1/*1 1, *1/*1xN (UM) 1, *1/*2 1, *1/*3 1, *1/*4 4, *4/*4 (PM) 1 (page image; the text layer misaligns these rows).

PM/UM factors were estimated in a control-only NONMEM run (ill-conditioning in the full model, dOFV -87.6) and
then fixed (p6). Text p6 agrees with the table: PM CL 56% lower, F more than twofold; UM CL doubled, F 19%
(0.45 x 0.43 = 0.19, derived). Post hoc medians (p6): oral arm CL 65.7 L/h control vs 92.7 fasting; IV arm 75.2 vs
86.2 L/h. Derived from Table 3: terminal half-life 3.4 h, Vss 285 L (3.6 L/kg at 80 kg). Fasting did not change F.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None measured. No ke0, tPeak or effect concentration. Not an opioid.

## Covariates
CYP2D6 PM and UM (fixed factors on CL and F, one subject each); fasting (theta_CL 1.13). No size, age or sex
terms (all men, narrow age range). For metoprolol, genotype was the only covariate that improved the model (PDF
p12, printed 1242).

## Caveats
Twelve young men; unbalanced design; the PM and UM factors each rest on one person. The Results text (p5) says a
three-compartment model was used "for metoprolol and S-warfarin", but Table 3 gives metoprolol V1/Q/V2 only and the
V3/Q2 columns are midazolam's and S-warfarin's: a source typo for midazolam, shown by Table 3. The 95% CI for Q is
wide (40-108 L/h). Salt not stated.
