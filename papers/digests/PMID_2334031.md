# White 1990: Glasgow computer-controlled propofol infusion (Atari / Imed 929)

## Study
- Device: Atari 1040ST microcomputer, program in GFA BASIC (compiled, auto-boot), driving an Imed 929 infusion pump over RS-232 (p205). Inputs: patient weight and target blood concentration.
- Controller (p205): three-compartment PK model; induction (and any upward target change) by a rapid zero-order infusion at 1200, 600 or 300 ml/h (anaesthetist's choice); model concentration computed twice per second; when predicted concentration reaches target the system switches to a maintenance mode compensating for elimination and redistribution. A lower target is reached by stopping the infusion until the model predicts the new target. Data logged at each change and each minute in maintenance.
- PK parameters: taken from Gepts et al. 1988 (ref 2, propofol during alfentanil anaesthesia), derived from 9 male and 2 female patients, mean weight 73.5 kg, mean age 35 years (p208). The parameter values themselves are not printed in this paper.
- Patients (Table 1, mean (SD) and range): n = 33 general-surgery patients, 12 M / 21 F; age 45.2 (14.5) y, range 20-72; weight 68.8 (11.4) kg, range 50-95. IPPV group n = 13 (7/6), age 48.4 (13.8) y, 20-69; weight 64.5 (10.0) kg, 52-81. Spontaneous breathing n = 20 (5/15), age 43.1 (14.5) y, 22-72; weight 68.3 (11.9) kg, 50-95. (Table layout garbled; assignment of rows reconstructed from the text "mean weight 68.8 kg, mean age 45.2 years", p208.)
- Co-medication: temazepam 20-40 mg premedication; alfentanil 0.5 mg bolus before induction then 0.25 µg/kg/min; N2O 66% in O2; IPPV patients atracurium 0.5 mg/kg then 0.25 mg/kg/h (p206).
- Sampling/assay: 410 blood samples, gas-liquid chromatography (p206). Whole blood ("blood propofol").

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- Clinical end point only (adequacy of anaesthesia, no formal scoring). Predicted target 3-6 µg/ml needed for induction; 2-6 µg/ml for maintenance with alfentanil 0.25 µg/kg/min and 66% N2O; no volatile supplement needed (p206).
- Case examples (Figs 5, 6): 34-y, 59-kg woman, spontaneous breathing, needed 6 µg/ml for induction and maintenance, maximum infusion 133 ml/h; 68-y, 65-kg woman, IPPV, induced at 3 µg/ml, maintained at 2-3 µg/ml.
- No ke0, effect-site targeting, EC50 or potency ratio reported.

## Covariates
Weight only (dose scaling of the model). No other covariates.

## Caveats
- PK parameters from a small, younger, mostly male group (n = 11) applied to an older, mostly female cohort; authors expect imperfect agreement (p208).
- Imed 929 resolution 1 ml/h and 1 ml; 10 ml priming volume (p208). Authors moved to an Ohmeda 9000 syringe pump (0.1 ml/h steps).
- Regression/correlation, not prediction-error statistics (no MDPE/MDAPE); repeated samples per patient treated together.
- Paper does not mention the Diprifusor (1990, predates it).
