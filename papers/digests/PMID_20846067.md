# Van Calsteren 2010: chemotherapy pharmacokinetics in pregnant and nonpregnant baboons and women

## Study
Two parts (Methods, PDF pp 2-3; printed pp 1339-1340).

- **Baboons** (Papio anubis, Institute of Primate Research, Nairobi): 5 animals each dosed once in pregnancy
  (mean gestational age 142 +/- 9 days) and again postpartum (56 +/- 11 days), under general anaesthesia
  (ketamine 10 mg/kg + xylazine 0.5 mg/kg IM, then halothane/N2O/O2). Weight 16.1 +/- 1.0 kg pregnant,
  14.3 +/- 1.5 kg postpartum. Three received doxorubicin 25 mg/m2 within ABVD; two received paclitaxel
  175 mg/m2 + carboplatin (Calvert target AUC 6, GFR by Schwartz). Samples over 24 h. (Table 1, p4.)
- **Women**: 22 patients (Table 4, p6), 16 pregnant cycles and 11 nonpregnant cycles (4 postpartum,
  7 unrelated to pregnancy). Pregnant/control patients: paclitaxel 5/2, doxorubicin 7/5, epirubicin 4/4,
  carboplatin 2/2. Standard BSA-based regimens (FAC, AC, TAC, ABVD, (R-)CHOP, FEC, paclitaxel +/- carboplatin
  or cisplatin); carboplatin by Calvert (AUC 5 or 6), GFR by Cockcroft-Gault. Gestational age 19-32 weeks.
  Three women were sampled both in and after pregnancy (patients 3 and 9, doxorubicin; patient 21,
  paclitaxel + carboplatin, postpartum sample at 30 days in Table 4, "4 weeks" in Table 5).
- **Carboplatin patients** (Table 4): pregnant = patients 21 (23 wk) and 22 (25 wk); control = patient 19
  (nonpregnant, AUC6) and patient 21 at 30 days postpartum (AUC5).
- **Sampling**: to 24 h (baboons) or 48 h (women); for short infusions at the end of infusion, then 15, 30 min,
  1, 2, 4, 6, 8, 12, 24, 48 h. EDTA plasma.
- **Assays**: doxorubicin and epirubicin by HPLC-fluorescence (LLOQ 2.5 ng/mL, linear 2.5-1000 ng/mL);
  paclitaxel by HPLC-UV (LLOQ 25 ng/mL, linear 25-5000 ng/mL); **total platinum** by graphite-furnace atomic
  absorption (calibration linear 178-1048, detection limit 10; the text layer gives the unit as mg/L, not checked
  on the page image, and this text layer drops the micro sign elsewhere).
  All are **total** (bound + unbound) plasma concentrations.
- **Analysis**: non-compartmental (WinNonlin, linear up/log down); t1/2 from the last four points;
  CL = Dose/AUC; Vd = CL/lambda (i.e. Vz). Cmax corrected for dose and infusion time, AUC for dose.
  Wilcoxon rank-sum for the clinical pooled comparison; no statistics on the baboons.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No effect measure, no ke0, no concentration target. Not an opioid paper.

## Covariates
Pregnancy only (pregnant vs postpartum or nonpregnant). Dosing by BSA; carboplatin by Calvert. No covariate
model.

## Caveats
- Very small numbers (2 vs 2 for carboplatin, 5 vs 2 for paclitaxel); the authors say significance was out of
  reach. Only epirubicin CL, Cmax and AUC and doxorubicin Cmax reached p < 0.05.
- "Control" mixes nonpregnant women and postpartum samples; the paclitaxel/carboplatin postpartum sample was at
  about 4 weeks, which the authors note may be too early to be free of pregnancy effects.
- Concentrations are total drug; platinum is **total** platinum by AAS, which includes protein-bound platinum.
- NCA over 24-48 h; Vd is Vz (CL/lambda), not a compartmental volume. Paclitaxel is nonlinear; NCA CL depends on
  dose and infusion time.
- Table 5's carboplatin clearance unit is inconsistent with Table 6 (open_questions). Baboon data under general
  anaesthesia.
- Comparator: every "higher clearance" here is pregnant women against this study's own control women (and
  baboons against themselves postpartum), not against any population model.

## Verification against stanpumpR
- **Carboplatin row and Duffull header** (`data-raw/pregnancyRegistry.R:2424`, `R/drugs_carboplatinDuffull.R:76-80`):
  "2 pregnant and 2 nonpregnant women, lower AUC and higher clearance and volume". Table 6 (p7): 2 vs 2, AUC-D
  88.9 vs 152.7, CL 11.4 vs 6.9 L/h, Vd 378.8 vs 272.7 L. **Matches**, with one nuance: one of the two
  "nonpregnant" controls is patient 21 herself at 30 days postpartum (Table 4), not a never-pregnant woman.
  The row was written from the abstract (`evidence_read = "abstract"`); the full text confirms it.
- **The factor applied**: x 1.5 on the Cockcroft-Gault ClCr of Duffull's renal term (only that term; total Cl
  therefore rises less than 1.5-fold). Van Calsteren's derived total-platinum clearance ratio is 1.65 pooled
  (between different women) and 1.17 within patient 21. Direction agrees, as the row says; the size is not
  settled by these data. **Species differ**: the library's model is unbound intact carboplatin in ultrafiltrate
  (`R/drugs_carboplatinDuffull.R:23-24`); Van Calsteren measured total platinum, so its absolute clearances
  are not comparable with the model's 6.5 L/h and only the ratio carries over, if at all.
- **"To obtain"** (`data-raw/pregnancyRegistry.R:2432`): the paper gives only the pooled mean +/- SD for the two
  pregnant women and patient 21's own values; individual values for patient 22 are not printed (derivable
  only from the n = 2 mean and SD). See open_questions.
- **Bleomycin row** (`data-raw/pregnancyRegistry.R:2864`): the paper reports doxorubicin, epirubicin,
  paclitaxel and platinum only; bleomycin, vinblastine and dacarbazine were given (ABVD) but not measured.
  **Matches.**
- **Uncited comparisons** (the library's pregnancy factors come from Janssen 2021, same Leuven group, with
  nonpregnant patients from a popPK reference; the women here likely overlap with Janssen's data):
  - doxorubicin: library CL x 1.14, V1 x 1.08 (`data-raw/pregnancyRegistry.R:2590-2591`); here CL 1.27 and
    Vz 1.30 (derived, NCA, n = 7 vs 5, not significant). Same direction, larger here; Vz is not V1.
  - epirubicin: library has no clearance factor, V1 x 1.79 (`data-raw/pregnancyRegistry.R:2897-2899`); here CL
    is 1.43-fold higher (p < 0.05, 4 vs 4). Janssen's larger fit did not confirm a clearance effect, as the
    row already says of van Hasselt 2014; Van Calsteren's 4 vs 4 does not overturn that, but runs the
    other way on clearance.
  - paclitaxel: library KMEL x 1.32, V1 x 2.27 (`data-raw/pregnancyRegistry.R:2534-2536`); here NCA CL 1.21,
    Vz 1.68 (5 vs 2). Same direction.
  None of these are discrepancies; the repo does not cite this paper for them.
