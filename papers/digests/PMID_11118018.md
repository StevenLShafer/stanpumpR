# Lepore 2000: glargine vs NPH vs ultralente vs lispro CSII in type 1 diabetes (24-h clamp)

## Study
- **Patients**: 20 C-peptide-negative type 1 diabetic patients (12 men), age 32±2 y, diabetes duration 15±2 y,
  BMI 22.2±0.4 kg/m², HbA1c 6.9±0.1% (mean ± SE). **Body weight is not reported.**
- **Design**: two crossover pairs, each patient studied 4 times.
  - Pair 1: SC glargine 0.3 U/kg against SC NPH 0.3 U/kg, randomised and double-blind.
  - Pair 2, 6-9 months later: SC human ultralente 0.3 U/kg against CSII of lispro at 0.3 U·kg⁻¹·24 h⁻¹, open.
    The authors give the CSII rate as about 0.8 U/h (p. 2147).
  - Injections were into the thigh at 17:00 (time 0); CSII was into the abdomen.
- **Clamp**: before time 0, IV regular insulin held plasma glucose at 130 mg/dl. After the injection, IV insulin
  was tapered off and 10% glucose was infused to keep glucose at 130 mg/dl for 24 h. A study stopped early if
  glucose exceeded 200 mg/dl with no glucose being infused.
- **Sampling and assay**: arterialised venous blood. Free insulin by RIA after PEG extraction.
  - No glargine-specific assay existed. The antibody's cross-reactivity with glargine is 56%, so glargine-study
    values from 3 h onward were multiplied by 1.8.
  - In the other studies, the 1-2 h values were corrected for the IV insulin contribution. In the glargine study,
    those values are indeterminate.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End points:
  - **Onset of action**: the time at which IV insulin needs fell consistently by 50% from the 20 min before
    injection.
  - **End of action**: the time at which glucose rose consistently above 150 mg/dl. It is set to 24 h if that never
    happened, which underestimates it; 16 glargine patients were still below 150 mg/dl at 24 h.
  - **GIR**: the glucose infusion rate at the 130 mg/dl clamp.
- Glucose: with NPH, held to 11 h, then a peak of 178±17 mg/dl at 20 h. With glargine, held to 15 h, then
  141±5 mg/dl from 16 to 24 h. With CSII, 130±2 mg/dl throughout. With ultralente, a peak of 166±7 mg/dl at 24 h.
- Intersubject variability (the SD across subjects, mean ± SE; Fig. 5 and p. 2145):
  - GIR: glargine 0.64±0.05 against NPH 1.05±0.18 mg·kg⁻¹·min⁻¹ (P < 0.05).
  - Ultralente against CSII: insulin 8.3±0.28 vs 4.1±0.24 µU/ml, and GIR 1.5±0.2 vs 0.65±0.04 (P < 0.001).
- No ke0, EC50 or concentration-effect model. Not an opioid paper: no MEAC and no potency ratio.

## Covariates
None analysed.

## Caveats
- Glargine concentrations are an immunoreactive estimate: total insulin by human-insulin RIA × 1.8. The 56%
  cross-reactivity comes from a manufacturer's internal report.
- The glargine/NPH and ultralente/CSII pairs were studied 6-9 months apart. The authors say the four arms can be
  compared only indirectly.
- Each insulin was studied once per patient, so there is no intrasubject variability.
- In the NPH study, n fell from 20 at 12 h to 2 at 19 h as glucose exceeded 200 mg/dl.
- Measured insulin includes residual bedtime NPH from the night before and, early on, the IV insulin.
- The text (20.6) and Fig. 2 (~25) disagree on the CSII plateau; see open_questions.
- Body weight is not given, so absolute doses are unknown.
- Funded in part by Hoechst Marion Roussel, the maker of glargine.

## Verification against stanpumpR
Nothing cites the paper, so the verdict is **not-applicable**. The library does model three of these insulins with
the Tham 2017 population model (`R/insulinTham.R`; `R/drugs_insulinGlargine.R`, `R/drugs_insulinNPH.R`,
`R/drugs_insulinLispro.R`). Ultralente is not modelled. Tham's subjects were mostly healthy volunteers; Lepore's are
C-peptide-negative type 1 patients. Independent checks:

- **Lispro by CSII**:
  - Tham's lispro CL/F is 30.5 L/h, with no size or dose covariate (`R/insulinTham.R:169`). At the paper's own
    ~0.8 U/h, the model's steady state is 0.8 / 30.5 = 0.0262 U/L = 26 µU/ml (derived).
  - That agrees with Fig. 2, which plots about 24-26 µU/ml. It is 1.27× the 20.6 µU/ml in the text.
  - Which number is right is an open `[check]`. The model is unaffected either way.
- **NPH**:
  - The model's time to peak at Tham's reference (80 kg, 10 U) is 3.3 h (derived). It uses ka 0.129/h, ke 127/178
    = 0.71/h and lag 0.378 h (`R/insulinTham.R:191`, `:167`).
  - Lepore observed a mean-curve peak at 4 h, and a mean individual peak time of 4.5±0.5 h (abstract).
  - Lepore's dose (0.3 U/kg) is larger than the reference, which by the dose covariate lowers CL/F and delays the
    peak. The direction is consistent; it is not checked numerically, because weight is not reported.
  - Fig. 2 also shows NPH concentration below the pre-injection baseline by 13 h. This fits the model's short
    terminal phase.
- **Glargine**:
  - The model's ka 0.083/h and ke 74.5/768 = 0.097/h (`R/insulinTham.R:170`, `:195`) give a broad,
    nearly flat profile with a late, low maximum. This is qualitatively consistent with Lepore's peakless plateau
    from 3 to 24 h.
  - Levels are not compared. Tham fitted Basaglar on an immunoreactive assay. Lepore's values are a
    cross-reactivity-scaled estimate in patients of unknown weight.
- `inst/extdata/therapeuticRanges.csv:101,103` give no therapeutic concentration range for NPH or lispro. This paper
  supports that: it doses by glucose and defines no target concentration.
