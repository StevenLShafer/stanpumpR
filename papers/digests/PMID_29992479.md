# Combes 2018: everolimus PopPK and seizure-count PK/PD in tuberous sclerosis

## Study
- Sponsor (Novartis) analysis of the three phase 3 EXIST trials (PDF p2, printed 708):
  EXIST-1 (111 adults and children, subependymal giant cell astrocytoma, troughs 5-15 ng/mL vs placebo),
  EXIST-2 (65 adults, renal angiomyolipoma, 10 mg daily), EXIST-3 (366 patients with treatment-refractory
  partial-onset seizures with TSC, 80% children; placebo vs low exposure, LE, trough target 3-7 ng/mL, vs high
  exposure, HE, 9-15 ng/mL; 8-week screening, 6-week titration, 12-week maintenance, then extension on everolimus).
- PK dataset: 6649 concentrations from 531 subjects (PDF p5); trough and peak samples in all studies, full profiles
  in an EXIST-1 subset. 613 observations and 8 patients (75 observations) excluded, including BLQ (<10% of data).
  Age range of the EXIST patients 1-61 years, median 11 (PDF p10).
- Matrix and assay are **not stated** in the main text (the word "blood" appears only in a reference title).
- Software: NONMEM 7.2 FOCE-I (PopPK, ADVAN4 TRANS4); Monolix 2016R1 SAEM (PK/PD), R 3.2.3 (PDF p3).
- PK/PD dataset: 163,963 daily seizure counts, 126,202 doses, 366 EXIST-3 patients (PDF p6). Exposure input =
  individual daily Cmin predicted by the PopPK model.

Table 3 (PDF p11, printed 717): means of individual post hoc CL/F by age and inducer status. Adults (>= 18 y):
32.98 L/h with inducer (n obs 419) and 24.88 L/h without (1279), i.e. 18.85 and 13.66 L/h/m2. Children < 3 y:
12.36 (with) and 12.31 L/h (without). All patients: 25.23 (with) and 22.61 L/h (without). BSA-normalised means
are near 20 L/h/m2 in every group except adults without inducers. The authors read the inducer effect as about
none in children and about 30% in adults (PDF p10; derived: 32.98/24.88 = 1.33).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: daily seizure count (Poisson; no overdispersion or Markov term needed, PDF p7, p12).
- Baseline: log(lambda_i) = log(1.45) + (-0.347) x [log(AGE0_i) - log(AGE0_median)] + eta (Table 2, PDF p8).
- Placebo: lambda multiplied by 1 + (MaxPCB - 1)(1 - exp(-SlopePCB (d - Tlag))); MaxPCB 0.50, half-life 18.3 weeks
  (95% CI 11.2-44.5); MaxPCB < 1 in 322 patients and > 1 in 44 (PDF p8).
- Everolimus: E = 1 - Emax(t) x Cmin/(Cmin + C50), C50 **1.77 ng/mL (trough, Cmin)**, RSE 1%; Emax rises toward 1
  as 1 - exp(-SlopeTimeEmax (d - TlagEVE)), half-life 122.1 weeks (95% CI 83.2-396.7) for a typical 10.1-year-old;
  70.7 weeks at 6 y and 224 weeks at 18 y; age exponent -1.05 on SlopeTimeEmax; omega 4.27 (PDF p8-9).
  The units of the two slopes are printed as h^-1 although the equations use day d; the stated half-lives agree
  with h^-1 (derived: ln2/2.26e-4 h = 3067 h = 18.3 weeks; ln2/3.38e-5 h = 20,507 h = 122 weeks).
- Simulations (Fig 5, PDF p10; Fig 6, PDF p11): median 6-month responder rate (>50% reduction) about 30% at a Cmin
  of 5 ng/mL and about 50% at 15 ng/mL (Conclusions, PDF p13); steeper below 5 ng/mL; 5% probability of a seizure
  increase of 84.5% or more at 3 ng/mL vs 58.6% at 5 ng/mL. The authors conclude the 5-15 ng/mL trough target is
  supported; 15 ng/mL is the upper bound because safety above it is not established.
- Not an opioid; no effect-site (ke0), no tPeak, no MEAC.

## Covariates
- PK: time-varying BSA on CL/F and Q/F (exponent 0.609), BSA on V2/F and V3/F (0.494), each by a spline:
  allometric up to 1.39 m2, quadratic in ln(BSA) between knots, constant (adult values) above about 1.54 m2;
  CYP3A/P-gp inducer x1.1 on CL/F. Age, sex, race, height, weight were tested (PDF p3).
- PD: age on baseline seizure mean and on SlopeTimeEmax. AEDs not retained.

## Caveats
- Sponsor analysis; ka fixed; matrix/assay not stated; spline coefficients and full code only in the ESM.
- Exposure is predicted Cmin, not measured; seizure model assumes stable disease (no progression term).
- Emax onset half-life (122 weeks) exceeds the trial duration; the authors warn against extrapolating it.
- HE arm mostly missed its target (median Cmin 8.3 ng/mL, PDF p10).
- Mostly children; adults were a minority.

## Verification against stanpumpR
- **Structural model "2C first-order (Combes 2018)"**: matches (PDF p5, two compartments, first-order absorption).
- **"531-patient"**: matches (6649 concentrations from 531 subjects, PDF p5).
- **"typical values not retrievable"**: **discrepancy**. Table 1 (rendered page, PDF p6) prints CL/F 20.0 L/h,
  V2/F 219.0 L, Q/F 15.4 L/h, V3/F 335.7 L, ka 10.8 /h. What the paper does not print is the BSA reference and the
  between-knot quadratic; for adults above about 1.54 m2 the splines are flat, so if Table 1's values are the adult
  plateau the adult model is complete in the paper (see open_questions). Supporting this reading (derived, not
  stated): adult no-inducer mean post hoc CL/F 24.88 L/h (Table 3) = 20.0 x exp(0.409/2) = 24.6 if 0.409 is a
  log-scale variance.
- **"whole-blood trough target 5-15 ng/mL"**: the 5-15 ng/mL trough target is supported (abstract, PDF p1-2, p12-13).
  "Whole blood" is **not stated in this paper** (cannot verify here; it is the standard matrix per the labels).
- **Comparison with the library model** (`R/drugs_everolimus.R:72-76`, NDA 22-334 reviewer's model in adults with
  solid tumours, which does not cite this paper): library CL/F 14.8 L/h, Vc/F 172 L, Q/F 48 L/h, Vp/F 495 L,
  ka 6.07 /h. Combes' typical CL/F 20.0 L/h is 1.35x the library's (adult TSC no-inducer post hoc mean 24.88 L/h,
  1.68x); Vc/F 1.27x; Vp/F 0.68x; Q/F 0.32x; ka 1.8x. The comparison is across populations (TSC, mostly children,
  some on inducers, vs oncology adults); the library header itself notes healthy subjects at 20.5 L/h, close to
  Combes' value. No direction-of-effect claim from this paper is applied by the library.
- **Band**: the library draws the Zortress transplant range 3-8 ng/mL (`inst/extdata/drugDefaults_global.csv:305`,
  `inst/extdata/therapeuticRanges.csv:297`); this paper supports 5-15 ng/mL for TSC seizures, a different
  indication. therapeuticRanges.csv:297 says "Afinitor (oncology) gives no range"; that is outside this paper,
  but Combes describes 5-15 ng/mL as the approved TSC-seizure target (PDF p2) -- worth checking against the
  Afinitor Disperz label.
