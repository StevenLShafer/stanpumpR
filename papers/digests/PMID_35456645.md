# Teixeira-da-Silva 2022: valproic acid population PK, children and adults (Salamanca)

## Study
- Retrospective TDM data, University Hospital of Salamanca; ambulatory patients on mono- or dual therapy at steady
  state (≥ 1 month); BMI 16-39.9 kg/m² (WHO criteria for children).
- Development: 836 patients, 1751 samples (the abstract says 776 patients, which is the external set's sample count);
  age 33.2 ± 23.9 y, 0.11-89.42; weight 55.8 ± 26.1 kg (6.7-125); 53.9% male; dose 1107 ± 587 mg/day.
  External evaluation: 368 patients, 776 samples, age 0.67-92.92 y.
- Products: gastro-resistant tablets (200, 500 mg), coated prolonged-release tablets (300, 500 mg), oral solution
  (200 mg/mL). 97.5% of samples were steady-state troughs.
- FPIA (Abbott TDx), LOD 0.7 mg/L; total serum VPA.
- NONMEM 7.5.1 FOCEI, log-transform both sides; one compartment, first-order absorption; stepwise covariates
  (p 0.05 / 0.01); bootstrap 1000 (994 successful); external Bayesian evaluation.

## Model and parameters
Equation 1: CL/F (L/h) = 0.646 × (TBW/70)^0.75 × 1.640^PHT × 1.386^PB × 1.512^CBZ × (AGE/15)^−0.0154
Equation 2: V/F (L) = 14 × (TBW/70)^1 (V/F **fixed**, ref. 5)
ka **fixed**: 2.64 h⁻¹ syrup, 0.78 h⁻¹ gastro-resistant tablet, 0.38 h⁻¹ modified-release coated tablet (refs 7, 8).

Merged-dataset re-estimate: CL/F 0.641, AGE −0.0107, CBZ 0.549, PB 0.349, PHT 0.642. The RUV estimate and its
bootstrap median disagree (57.7 vs 28.1), which the authors acknowledge.
Worked examples (Discussion): monotherapy CL/F 0.010, 0.103, 0.538 and 1.484 L/h at 1 y/9.4 kg, 6 y/22.9 kg,
15 y/53.3 kg and 35 y/73.5 kg; these do not follow from Equation 1 (derived: 0.646 × (73.5/70)^0.75 × (35/15)^−0.0154
= 0.66 L/h at 35 y), so the worked examples, not the equation, are internally inconsistent. Their dual-therapy ratios
are 1.52 (CBZ; derived 2.258/1.484 = 1.522), 1.64 (PHT), 1.39 (PB).
Introduction: F > 90% for all oral forms; CL/F 6-10 mL/h/kg; t½ 12-16 h adults, 8.6-12.3 h children; reference range
50-100 mg/L.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None modelled. Reference range 50-100 mg/L cited (trough).

## Covariates
TBW (fixed allometry); age (power, centred at 15 y); phenytoin, phenobarbital, carbamazepine (multiplicative).
Sex (< 10% effect) and lamotrigine not retained. Daily dose deliberately not tested: the authors regard it as
confounded by the TDM effect (dose follows level) and by saturable protein binding.

## Caveats
Sparse troughs: ka and V/F fixed, not estimated; total VPA only (saturable binding not modelled); FPIA; poor fit
under 24 kg / 6 y before the age term; worked examples inconsistent with the equation.
