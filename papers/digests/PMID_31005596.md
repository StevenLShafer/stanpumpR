# Zebala 2019: oral desmetramadol vs tramadol at steady state, with and without paroxetine

## Study
- Sponsor (Syntrix) first-in-human trials A (2014) and B (2017), single centre, Salt Lake City; double-blind, randomised, 3-period crossover: placebo, tramadol 50 mg (Ultram), desmetramadol 20 mg (racemic M1, oral, overencapsulated); 9 doses q6h per period.
- Trial A: 43 healthy men, age 28.4 +/- 8.0 y, BMI 25.5; CYP2D6 by activity score: 1 PM, 17 IM (AS 1.0), 23 NM, 1 UM (Table 2). Trial B: 60 (42 M, 18 F), age 28.0 y, NM/IM only, plus paroxetine 20 mg daily x 3 per period (paroxetine 11-12 ng/mL).
- PK: samples before and 1.0, 1.5, 2.0, 2.5, 4.0 (and 8.0 in B) h after dose 9; chiral LC-MS/MS, LLOQ 5.0 ng/mL per enantiomer. t1/2 from C4 and C8.
- PD: cold pressor (pain at 30 s, tolerance) at 1, 2, 3 h after dose 9; pupillometry after dose 7 (trial A); abuse VAS; respiratory rate.

No tramadol enantiomers after desmetramadol. Dose relation: 20 mg desmetramadol q6h gives the same steady-state (+)-M1 as 50 mg tramadol q6h in unselected (mostly NM/IM) men.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- Analgesia (cold pressor, pain at 30 s, men): trial A tramadol -0.46, desmetramadol -0.60 vs placebo (P = .022, .0005), not different from each other; trial B tramadol not different from placebo, desmetramadol -0.75 vs placebo and -0.62 vs tramadol (P = .003). Tolerance +12.4 / +12.6 s (A).
- Pupil (trial A, after dose 7): 5.5 (tramadol) and 5.1 mm (desmetramadol) vs 6.2 mm placebo; paired reductions -0.7 and -1.1 mm.
- No MEAC, ke0, tPeak or hysteresis estimated. Equianalgesic/equi-exposure ratio: desmetramadol 20 mg : tramadol 50 mg (by (+)-M1 and cold pressor).
- Stated in the introduction (secondary): therapeutic M1 levels 50-100 ng/mL; fatal blood M1 means 1300 and 1900 ng/mL (p. 2, p. 14). In vitro (+)-M1 Ki 0.0034 umol/L (citing Gillen/Raffa).
- Respiratory rate: decreases of 0.30-0.34 breaths/min vs placebo significant only in trial B (Table 6).

## Covariates
CYP2D6 activity score (descriptive), paroxetine inhibition; sex (no analgesia signal in women in B).

## Caveats
- Industry-sponsored, healthy young adults, experimental pain only, oral only (no IV desmetramadol, no volume or clearance reported).
- Sparse 4-8 h sampling; t1/2 from two points with very large SD.
- Table 3 in the text layer is scrambled; values above taken from the results text.
