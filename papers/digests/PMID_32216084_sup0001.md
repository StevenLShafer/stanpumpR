# Chen 2020 (supplement): Figure S1, population PK model schema

## Study
No new study. One-page box diagram of the final population PK model of the parent article (card `PMID_32216084.md`:
174 participants, five pooled studies, NONMEM 7.3). No numbers.

## Model and parameters
The diagram (PDF p. 1, rendered):
- Oral dose into a central compartment by first-order absorption, Ka, which varies with fed state.
- Central compartment labelled "(Plasma)", its volume scaling with weight (allometric) and varying with age category.
- One peripheral compartment, exchanging with the central one in both directions (arrows; no rate constants named).
- Michaelis-Menten clearance (Vmax and Km) out of the central compartment; Vmax said to vary with weight, **age
  category**, and time of day (AM vs PM).
- No lag time, no second absorption path, no saturable absorption drawn.

## Covariates
As drawn: fed state on Ka; weight and age category on the central volume; weight, age category and time of day on
Vmax. No equations, reference values or effect sizes.

## Caveats
- **The schema and Table S4 disagree on the age effect.** Figure S1 puts age category on Vmax as well as on the
  central volume; Table S4 (sup0008) lists age-category effects only on VC ("Pediatric age category (7-17 y) on VC",
  "Child age category (7-11 y) on VC") and no age term on Vmax, and the main article (p. 5) also places them on VC.
  The figure is evidently the less specific of the two; the table carries the estimates.
- No parameterisation is shown for the peripheral exchange; Table S4 gives it as K23/K32 with VP derived.
