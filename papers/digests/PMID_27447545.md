# Pérez-Blanco 2016: doxorubicin and doxorubicinol population PK in non-Hodgkin lymphoma

## Study
- 45 adults with non-Hodgkin lymphoma (36 diffuse large B-cell, 2 Burkitt-like, 2 follicular, 5 other), 23 M / 22 F,
  treated with R-CHOP every 21 days, Salamanca University Hospital (30) and the GEL-R-COMP-2013 trial (15, NCT02012088),
  June 2009 to June 2015 (pp1518-1519). Normal hepatic, renal and cardiac function (Results, p1521).
- Table 1 (PDF p3, p1519), mean (SD), range: unlabelled row (presumably age, see open question) 66 (15), 26-84; weight
  71 (12) kg, 43-110; height 1.64 (0.01) m, 1.43-1.92; BSA 1.8 (0.2) m², 1.3-2.3; BMI 26.5 (3.9); LBW (Janmahasatian)
  47.9 (10.0) kg; dose 51 (7) mg/m², 25-71, = 89 (14) mg, 53-130; infusion 0.5 (0.2) h, 0.2-1.3; CLcr (Cockcroft-Gault)
  91 (40) mL/min; bilirubin 0.44 (0.19) mg/dL; ALT 23 (18), AST 25 (13) IU/L.
- Dose: 50 mg/m² by 0.5-h infusion (p1518). Salt/base not stated.
- Sampling: plasma (5 mL peripheral venous blood) at 0, 30, 90 and 180 min after the end of infusion (D-optimal design,
  WinNonlin 5.3) (p1519). 125 doxorubicin and 120 doxorubicinol observations (p1521).
- Assay: UHPLC with fluorescence; LLOQ 8 ng/mL doxorubicin, 3 ng/mL doxorubicinol; intra/inter-day CV 7.6/9.2% and
  6.3/7.8% (p1520).
- NONMEM 7.3, FOCE; PsN 4.4.0, Xpose, NPDE; 1000-sample bootstrap, pcVPC, NPDE (p1520). One patient removed as an outlier
  (|CWRES| > 4), so the final model has n = 44 (p1521; Table 2 header).

## Model and parameters
Five linear compartments: three for doxorubicin (V1 central, V2, V3) and two for doxorubicinol (V4 central, V5); a fraction
Fm of doxorubicin is converted to doxorubicinol (abstract: "fraction metabolized"; Table 2 footnote: "conversion rate to
DOXol"). Qn is the intercompartmental clearance of compartment n (Table 2 footnote). No covariates retained (bilirubin and AST
tested on CL and CLm, not significant). All volumes FIXED (bold in Table 2): doxorubicin's "to those proposed by Kontny et
al. [26]" (p1521; but ref 26 is Rudek 2004, Kontny 2013 is ref 15), doxorubicinol's from a sensitivity analysis. IIV of Q2,
Q3 and CLm fixed. Exponential IIV, proportional residual error.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Not an opioid paper. No ke0, effect site, EC50 or therapeutic concentration range. End point: haematological toxicity
(CTCAE v3.0 grade 0-2 vs 3-4 leukopenia and neutropenia; leucocyte and neutrophil counts 21 days after dosing), analysed
against AUC, AUCm and AUCtotal in 36 patients (8 removed: 6 without haematology, 2 given G-CSF) (p1523). Table 3 (PDF p9,
p1525): group mean AUC 1.44 vs 1.78 (leukopenia, P = 0.120), 1.45 vs 1.55 (neutropenia, P = 0.563) mg·h/L; AUCm 0.75 vs
0.63 and 0.70 vs 1.08 mg·h/L; AUCtotal 2.18 vs 2.41 and 2.14 vs 2.62 (P = 0.065), printed in "ng ml⁻¹" (evidently a unit
misprint: AUCtotal is defined as AUC + AUCm, and the values are their sums in mg·h/L). Best correlation: AUCtotal vs
neutrophil count, exponential, r = -0.287, P = 0.089. Nothing reached P < 0.05. The paper states doxorubicinol's activity is
about 10% of the parent's (Introduction, p1518). No potency ratio otherwise.

## Covariates
None in the final model. Tested: age, sex, weight, height, BSA, BMI, LBW, CLcr, AST, ALT, bilirubin (SCM, forward α 0.05,
backward α 0.01; p1520).

## Caveats
- Sparse sampling to 3 h after a 0.5-h infusion: the 45.8-h terminal phase and the large V2 are those of the fixed literature
  volumes, not these data. CLm is imprecise (RSE 42.9%; bootstrap mean 37.0 vs 26.8).
- The fixed volumes are cited to ref 26 (Rudek 2004) in the Results, while the Methods name Kontny et al. [15] as the
  three-compartment reference model; ref 15 is the right one.
- Table 1: age row unlabelled; height SD 0.01 m against a 1.43-1.92 m range looks like a misprint (perhaps 0.1). Neither
  number enters the model.
- Homogeneous population (authors' explanation for finding no covariates); single cycle; G-CSF-treated patients excluded
  from the toxicity analysis (possible selection bias, authors).
- Plasma only; no salt/base statement; mass units throughout.
