# Horta 2000: epidural droperidol and epidural-morphine pruritus at cesarean delivery

## Study
- Randomized, double-blind, four parallel groups of 35 (n = 140), ASA I-II women having cesarean delivery under lumbar epidural anesthesia (L2-3 or L3-4), Pelotas, Brazil (p. 638-639). Gestational age not reported.
- All received 150 mg (30 mL) 0.5% bupivacaine with 1:200,000 epinephrine plus 2 mg epidural morphine sulfate, and epidural droperidol 0, 1.25, 2.5 or 5.0 mg (Groups 1-4), made up to 2 mL with distilled water (preservative-free droperidol 2.5 mg/mL). Before the block, 10 mg metoclopramide and 50 ug fentanyl IV to all (p. 638).
- Demographics (Table 1, mean (SD), groups 1-4): age 27-28 y, height 161-163 cm, weight 70-75 kg, BMI 27.1-28.4; no group differences (P > 0.05).
- Outcome: pruritus graded absent / mild / moderate / severe (worst grade reported), assessed at least twice in the first 24 h and daily to discharge by blinded observers; other side effects recorded. Severe pruritus treated with IM promethazine 50 mg on request. Analysis: Mantel-Haenszel test for linear association (pruritus), chi-square (other effects). Sample size 32 per group (from their IV droperidol work), plus 10%.
- No drug concentrations were measured. No pharmacokinetics.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: graded pruritus (ordinal), dose-related reduction with epidural droperidol 0-5 mg (P < 0.001, Table 2).
- Somnolence rose with dose: 2 (6%), 1 (3%), 6 (17%), 9 (26%) in Groups 1-4; only Group 4 differed from control (Results p. 640; abstract P < 0.05). The authors contrast this with 11% somnolence after 5 mg IV droperidol in their earlier study, where 5 mg IV reversed the anti-pruritic effect seen at 2.5 mg.
- Nausea and vomiting no different: 4 (11%) in Groups 1-3, 2 (6%) in Group 4 (P = 0.81). No respiratory depression, constipation or herpes activation observed; urinary retention not assessable (catheters). No neonatal problems attributed to the drugs.
- No ke0, EC50, MEAC or potency ratio. Morphine was a fixed 2 mg epidural dose in every group, so no opioid concentration-response is given.

None analysed.

## Caveats
- Clinical pharmacodynamic trial of the epidural route, combined with epidural morphine; not intravenous droperidol and not maternal pharmacokinetics.
- No concentrations; the Discussion's remarks on droperidol kinetics (Fischler 1986, Lehmann 1988, Naji 1990, Bach 1986) are citations, not data.
- Doses above 5 mg not studied (sedation). Ordinal outcome, single centre pair, no gestational-age or timing details of the assessments beyond the schedule.
