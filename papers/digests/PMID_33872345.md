# Vogt 2021: midazolam vs ketamine, fMRI memory/pain paradigm

## Study
- Randomized, single-blind, within-subject crossover fMRI study; 26 healthy adults, 18-39 y (mean 25.3 y, 15 men; mean 171 cm, 71.4 kg; Table 1). Eight did not return for the second drug session; two sessions lost to scanner error (PDF p. 4).
- Each scanning visit: a saline segment first, then drug. Drug order randomized (Fig. 1, PDF p. 3). Next-day memory test (20-32 h after).
- Drug delivery by STANPUMP, with an initial bolus and infusion simulated beforehand; targets were **effect-site** concentrations of **10 ng/mL midazolam** and **200 ng/mL ketamine** (PDF p. 3, printed p. 71). PK models cited: refs 11 (Greenblatt, midazolam) and 12 (Domino 1984, ketamine), "accounting for age, sex, height and weight". Saline carrier at 75 mL/h.
- Total dose per subject (Table 1, mean): midazolam 1.50 mg (range 0.79-2.08), ketamine 34.5 mg (25.3-48.7).
- Painful stimulus: 100 Hz tetanic nerve stimulation of the left index finger, titrated to 7/10 pain (mean 16.7 mA midazolam sessions, 17.5 mA ketamine sessions, Table 2).
- Primary outcome: recollection d' (Remember responses), next-day recognition. Secondary: familiarity d', response time, task fMRI, seed-to-voxel functional connectivity (17 seeds).
- No blood sampling; no concentrations measured.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Not an opioid paper; no MEAC reported (the audit items below are "none").
- No ke0, t1/2 ke0, tPeak or hysteresis analysis; no EC50/gamma.
- Memory (recollection d', median [95% CI], Fig. 4B, p. 75): saline 1.78 [1.61-1.96]; midazolam 0.73 [0.43-1.02]; ketamine 1.55 [1.12-1.97]. Midazolam vs saline P < 0.0001, vs ketamine P < 0.001; ketamine vs saline P = 0.081. Familiarity d' did not differ (0.36 / 0.31 / 0.31; P = 0.540).
- Pain score (0-10 NRS, Table 2): reduced under ketamine (median decrease 1.6, P < 0.001; mean 6.6 saline to 5.1/5.0/4.9 over the drug blocks), not under midazolam (P = 0.194; mean 6.8 to 6.7/6.5/6.4). Large inter-individual variation in ketamine analgesia (several subjects 0-2.5, others unchanged).
- Response times did not differ between drugs (P = 0.065), taken as equal psychomotor impairment. Dissociation/dysphoria reported in 6/22 ketamine sessions, none with midazolam; sedation reported in 4/22 ketamine and 8/22 midazolam sessions.
- Connectivity: midazolam changed connectivity from 3 seeds (precuneus, posterior cingulate, left insula): 28 changes, 20 increased. Ketamine: 8 seeds, 19 clusters, 17 decreased (Tables 3-4).
- Audit: MEAC none; ke0/t1/2 ke0/tPeak none; potency ratio none (doses chosen for "roughly equal sedation", p. 80, not an equipotency ratio).

## Covariates
Age, sex, height and weight were inputs to the PK models (per the paper). No covariate analysis on the outcomes.

## Caveats
- Single dose of each drug; targets, not measured concentrations; considerable between-subject variability in drug effect (authors, p. 80).
- Single-blind; sedation levels not quantified by a scale (self-report only).
- Ketamine's lower recollection effect (P = 0.081 vs saline) is not significant; activation contrasts saline vs drug had no surviving clusters.
- Unequal completion: 8 of 26 did not return; 22 sessions per drug.

## Verification against stanpumpR
Not applicable (no citation). For orientation, the paper's targets against the library:
- Ketamine (`R/drugs_ketamine.R:1-54`) uses Domino 1984, the same source as the paper's ref 12 (ref 12 is cited by the Vogt text as a PK model for ketamine). The library's band is 0.10-0.16 mcg/mL with typical 0.12 (lines 49-51); the 200 ng/mL (= 0.2 mcg/mL) target here is above the band's upper end, and its end point (sedation/memory) differs from the library's analgesic-threshold band. tPeak is 3 min in the library, assumed (line 52); the paper reports none.
- Midazolam (`R/drugs_midazolam.R:162-195`): library kinetics are Zomorodi 1998 (Buhrer data), tPeak 3.0 min, band 0.101-0.304 mcg/mL (Ramsay sedation, plasma; lines 179-193). The 10 ng/mL (0.010 mcg/mL) target here is about one tenth of the library's lower band edge (0.101), reflecting a memory/anxiolysis end point at low sedation rather than Ramsay 3. Library concentrations are total plasma; the paper does not say what the model's compartment 1 assay basis was. See the open question about which midazolam model the authors used.
