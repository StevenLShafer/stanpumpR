# Reimer 1980: moxalactam, cefazolin and five other agents against 420 blood isolates

## Study
In-vitro susceptibility study, University of Colorado (Denver). 420 blood-culture isolates from patients with true
bacteraemia (judged by an infectious disease consultant), one isolate per species per episode, in proportion to their
occurrence in blood at the authors' hospitals (p1, printed p412). Drugs: moxalactam (LY127935), cefazolin, amikacin,
tobramycin, carbenicillin, piperacillin, ticarcillin; laboratory standard powders.

Method (p2, printed p413): MIC by microtiter broth dilution in duplicate (modification of the International
Collaborative Study method); final inoculum 10^5 to 10^6 bacteria/mL; Mueller-Hinton broth (cation-supplemented for
P. aeruginosa); streptococci in Trypticase soy broth, H. influenzae in Schaedler broth with Fildes reagent, anaerobes
in Schaedler broth with hemin and vitamin K1; 35 °C. MIC = lowest concentration without turbidity. **MBC = lowest
concentration with no growth (>99.9% killing) on antibiotic-free agar after subculture of 3 µL from each well and
overnight incubation.** N. gonorrhoeae and Corynebacterium spp. by agar dilution (MIC only). The test media contain no
serum, so these are free-drug values. Funded by Eli Lilly (maker of moxalactam and the cefazolin powder used).

## Model and parameters
No pharmacokinetic model. Table 1 (PDF p3, printed p414): geometric mean (range) MIC, µg/mL, 420 isolates.

streptococci 2.2 and L. monocytogenes 2.2. NCCLS 1975 susceptible breakpoint used for cefazolin: MIC ≤16 µg/mL.
With those breakpoints, 79% of the 420 isolates were inhibited and 76% killed by cefazolin (moxalactam 87% and 82%).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End points are in-vitro inhibition (no turbidity) and killing (>99.9%, overnight). No serum concentrations, no PK/PD
index, no time-kill data. Not an opioid paper: no MEAC, ke0, tPeak or potency ratio.

## Covariates
None (organism only).

## Caveats
- Geometric means and ranges only: no MIC50/MIC90 or MBC50/MBC90, no per-isolate MBC/MIC distribution.
- Each species group mixes susceptible and resistant isolates (cefazolin ranges reach >64), so the geometric means are
  not values for "susceptible" isolates.
- S. aureus methicillin status and Streptococcus species are not reported.
- 1980 isolates and methods; inoculum 10^5-10^6/mL; streptococci in a different broth. Sponsor-funded.
