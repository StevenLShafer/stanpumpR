# Tackley 1989: Bristol BET-type computer-controlled propofol infusion

## Study
- Device: BBC model B microcomputer driving an IMED 929 pump (20-1200 ml/h) over RS232; rate and delivered volume checked and adjusted every 10 s (p48).
- Algorithm (p47-48): BET scheme (after Schüttler, Schwilden and Stoeckel) on a linear three-compartment model: (1) loading dose = target x V1, given over 20-30 s; (2) constant infusion = loading dose x k10; (3) declining infusion = loading dose x (k12 e^-k21 t + k13 e^-k31 t), recomputed every 10 s.
- Target 3 µg/ml whole blood throughout (chosen as close to the EC95 in morphine-premedicated patients, ref 14; p52).
- Group 1: 8 healthy patients aged 24-65 y (herniorrhaphy 2, varicose veins 6), spontaneous breathing. Group 2: 8 patients aged 28-58 y, ASA I-II, body-surface surgery, IPPV to normocapnia (Penlon-Oxford ventilator). Both: morphine 0.15 mg/kg i.m. 1 h before; 66% N2O in O2 from 15 s; suxamethonium 1 mg/kg at 6 min for intubation (p48-49).
- Sampling: venous (antecubital, contralateral arm) at 0, 2, 5, 10, 15, 20, 30, 40, 50, 60, 80, 100, 120 min. Assay: whole-blood HPLC with fluorescence (Plummer 1987); spiked 1 µg/ml gave 1.107, CV 6%; LOD 20 ng/ml (p49).
- Model development: a pilot with single-dose kinetics (Kay 1985, ref 6) overshot early (Fig 2); group 1 used parameters from infusion studies (Cockshott, refs 7-8); group 2 parameters were hand-adjusted by visual fitting to group 1 data with clearance (k10 x V1) held constant (p48).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- No PD model, ke0 or effect-site concept. Clinical: induction in 1-2 min, no apnoea > 15 s, good intubating conditions at 6 min, no awareness; 2 of 8 group-1 patients needed brief volatile supplement (p49).
- Group 1: mean measured within 6% of target by 10 min, close to target to 120 min; inter-patient CV 43.8% in first 2 min. Group 2: within 4% by 5 min, then 5-20% above target from 20 min (Summary; p51).
- Table II (mean, SD or range): group 1 weight 73 (10.1) kg, infusion 102 (64-129) min, total dose 11 (2) mg/kg, final rate 41 (6.3) ml/h, calculated steady-state rate 35 (5) ml/h, time to correct date of birth 12 (3.3) min; group 2: 76 (13.6) kg, 77 (45-135) min, 10 (2.6) mg/kg, 44 (7.5), 36 (6.5), 13 (3.7) min. Waking 8-17.5 min after stopping.
- Haemodynamics: systolic pressure about 33% and heart rate about 12% below baseline at the end; gradual fall plateauing about 15 min (Fig 6).
- EC95 quoted only as a rationale (ref 14); no value beyond "close to 3 µg/ml".

## Covariates
Body weight (per-kg parameters) only.

## Caveats
- n = 8 per group; group 2 parameters were fitted by eye to group 1 data, not estimated statistically (no NONMEM).
- Venous sampling lags arterial during the rapid rise (authors' note, p52).
- Group 2 over-target drift attributed speculatively to IPPV reducing hepatic flow.
