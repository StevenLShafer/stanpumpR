# Helfer 2026: pentobarbital two-compartment popPK in children (PTN POP01), allometric on total weight

## Study
- Pediatric Trials Network POP01 (NCT01431326), multicentre, opportunistic standard-of-care sampling. 42 enrolled,
  39 analysed (one oral dose, one single BLQ sample, one dosing error excluded), **70 plasma samples**.
- Age median 1.18 y in < 2 y group; groups < 2 y (n 12), 2-6 (11), 6-12 (10), >= 12 (6); weights 9.2, 16.2,
  28.5, 53.7 kg medians (Table 3). Youngest 2 days, others >= 6 months. 5 obese; 4 on ECMO; 4 on vasopressors.
- Indications: sedation most common, then seizure control. IV bolus median 2 mg/kg (0.39-8.9; 4.7-290 mg);
  infusion median 2.4 mg/kg/h (0.00175-16.1); infusion duration median 7.2 h (0.3-146.42).
- Assay: HPLC-MS/MS (OpAns), range 50-50,000 ng/mL, plasma.
- NONMEM 7.5, FOCE-I; PsN bootstrap (1000, 90.4% successful) and pcVPC; simulations in mrgsolve with PK-Sim virtual
  children aged 1-18 y.

## Model and parameters
Two compartments, linear, proportional residual error; IIV on CL with **shared eta for CL and V1**. Allometric
scaling to total body weight, exponents **fixed** 0.75 (CL, Q) and 1 (V1, V2). Total weight beat LBM and FFM
(OFV -52.984 vs -52.642 vs -50.569). Table 2:

Obesity on V1 entered in forward selection (V1 94.9 vs 33.2 L/70 kg) and was removed in backward elimination. Age
had no effect beyond weight. Derived micro-constants at 70 kg: half-lives 0.81 h and 15.1 h (derived:
k10 0.139, k12 0.484, k21 0.283 /h).
EBEs (Table 3): CL 0.13, 0.11, 0.11, 0.076 L/h/kg by age group; V1 0.58, 0.51, 0.7, 0.51 L/kg.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PK/PD; no ke0. Simulation targets taken from the literature: preoperative sedation Cmax 1-5 mg/L; deep sedation
Css 5-15 mg/L; seizure control Css 4-21 mg/L (EEG burst suppression, ref 7); "potentially toxic" > 45 mg/L.
Recommended: 1 mg/kg bolus for preoperative sedation; 5 mg/kg + 1 mg/kg/h for seizures; 0.75 mg/kg/h for deep
sedation at 6-18 y. Not an opioid.

## Covariates
Total body weight only (fixed allometry). Tested and not retained: postnatal age (sigmoid maturation), BMI, obesity,
sex, race, ethnicity, surgery, ECMO, indication, interacting drugs. CRP not available; creatinine missing in 48.7%.

## Caveats
Sparse (70 samples in 39 children); standard-of-care dosing and timing; few < 1 y and few adolescents; ECMO patients
retained; no PD; laboratory covariates largely missing. Adult values quoted in the Introduction (healthy adults
V about 1 L/kg, CL 0.02-0.07 L/h/kg; severe head injury V 1.03 L/kg, CL 0.043 L/h/kg).

## Verification against stanpumpR
- CL 5.21 L/h, V1 37.4 L, Q 18.1 L/h, V2 63.9 L per 70 kg; fixed 0.75 / 1 exponents; /60 for L/min: **matches**
  Table 2 and Methods.
- "No covariate beyond weight was retained (obesity on V1 entered and left)": **matches**.
- "critically ill children": loosely supported; POP01 children received pentobarbital per standard of care, mostly
  for sedation (some on ECMO/vasopressors). Minor wording.
- **discrepancy**, R/drugs_pentobarbital.R:36-38 ("CHECKS"): the code's micro-constants give half-lives of 0.81 h
  and 15.1 h, not "about 1.5 h and 23 h" (derived), and 10 mg/kg over 1 h then 1 mg/kg/h in a 70 kg patient gives
  about 11 mg/L at 24 h (derived, Euler simulation; 14.2 at 1 h, 8.3 at 6 h), not "about 20 mg/L"; steady state
  is 70/5.21 = 13.4 mg/L, below the 20-40 band. The comment does not describe the implemented model.
- Band 20-40 mg/L "reported for burst suppression": not from this paper, which uses 4-21 mg/L for EEG burst
  suppression and 45 mg/L as potentially toxic. (Ketharanathan 2023 gives 20-40 mg/L as a safety margin.)
  **cannot verify from this paper**; note the tension.
- Adult clearance "quoted in both papers' discussions" (R/drugs_pentobarbital.R:29-31): Helfer quotes 0.02-0.07 and
  0.043 L/h/kg in its **Introduction**; Ketharanathan does not quote them at all (see PMID_37247187 digest).
  **discrepancy** (attribution).
- Body size: the paper chose total body weight over FFM and found volume rising more than proportionally with weight
  (exponent 1.3, obesity on V1). With the switch on, stanpumpR evaluates the allometry at an FFM-based pkWeight,
  which lowers volumes in obese patients, the opposite direction. A repo design choice, not a transcription error;
  worth flagging. With the switch off it reproduces the paper.
- Citation: repo never names the authors; full citation above.
