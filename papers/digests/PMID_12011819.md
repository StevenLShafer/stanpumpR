# Mould 2002: population PK of topotecan and neutropenia risk

PDF page = printed page − 333 (PDF p1 = p334).

## Study
- Pooled analysis of 9 phase I-III trials (Table II, PDF p3). Single-agent topotecan (Hycamtin), 30-min IV
  infusion daily x 5 days every 3 weeks; doses 0.2-2.0 mg/m² per day.
- PK data set: 245 adult patients with solid tumours (mainly ovarian and small-cell lung cancer), 2408 plasma
  concentrations, about 12 samples per patient over 1-4 occasions (p336). Table I (PDF p2, rendered): age mean
  58.9 y (34-82); measured weight mean 68.5 kg, median 70.9 (36.5-121), n = 197; BSA mean 1.80 m² (1.3-2.62);
  Cockcroft-Gault CLCR mean 73.5, median 68.7 mL/min (15.5-150, capped at 150); 77 male, 165 female;
  ECOG 0/1/2/3 = 76/109/39/1; 204 of 245 had prior platinum.
- Missing covariates (48 weights, 20 ECOG, 3 sex) imputed with a joint model (Table IV).
- Assay: total topotecan (lactone + open-ring carboxylate), plasma, HPLC with fluorescence; LLOQ 1 ng/mL in the
  early dose-ranging study, 0.1 ng/mL in the others (p336).
- Adverse-event data set: 438 patients, 1778 cycle-level neutropenia grades (NCI CTC 0-4 plus a sixth category,
  grade 4 with clinical consequence) (p337).
- NONMEM V level 1.1, FOCE with interaction (PK); FOCE Laplace/likelihood (logistic model) (p340).

## Model and parameters
Two-compartment, first-order elimination, estimated infusion duration (D1). Allometric weight scaling, exponent
0.75 on CL and Q, 1 on volumes. CL split into nonrenal and renal parts; renal function RF = (CLCR/70 mL/min) ·
(70 kg/wt), i.e. CLCR normalised for weight. Final model, Table V (PDF p9, rendered):

CL = (θ1 + θ2 · RF) · (wt/70)^0.75 · exp(θ3 · [ECOG − 1]); V1 = θ4 · (wt/70); Q = θ5 · (wt/70)^0.75;
V2 = θ6 · (wt/70). Standard patient: 70 kg, CLCR 70 mL/min, ECOG 1.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: worst neutropenia grade per cycle, cumulative-logit model with exposure = AUC(0-24) = daily dose /
  individual CL. Table VII (PDF p10, rendered): baseline log-odds grades 0-4: 1.46, 0.715, 1.05, 1.92, 3.77;
  AEmax 8.16 (95% CI 7.15-9.17); E50,AUC 101 ng·h/mL (53.1-149); platinum effect 0.521 (0.458-0.584), per prior
  platinum course; IIV SD 1.012. The footnote equation is printed truncated (ends with "·").
- Dose-based fits: E50,dose 2960 µg (≈1.64 mg/m² at BSA 1.80 m²); E50 normalised dose 1.5 mg/m²; both ≈ AUC
  119 ng·h/mL (p343).
- Grade 4 risk: platinum-naive <10% up to AUC 90, ~15% at ≥120 ng·h/mL; ≥1 prior platinum course <10% below 30,
  >40% above 120 ng·h/mL (p345). AUC at 1.5 mg/m² for median patient 145.9 ng·h/mL; 86 ng·h/mL with CLCR 120 and
  ECOG 0 (p347).
- No ke0, no effect-site model, no concentration-effect relation in the anaesthetic sense. Not an opioid paper.

## Covariates
CLCR (via weight-normalised RF) on CL; weight allometrically on all parameters; ECOG on CL. Covariate inclusion
reduced CL IIV 49% → 40% (CLCR) → 35% (weight) → 33% (ECOG) (PDF p9). Sex, age, race not significant.

## Caveats
- Total topotecan (lactone + carboxylate) assay, not the active lactone.
- Early dose-ranging study used a research formulation; fitted factor 0.489 on its dose.
- ~20% of weights imputed by the joint model.
- Internal inconsistency: text p340 gives V1,std 19.2 L, V2,std 44.5 L and IIV "32% for both V1 and V2", while
  Table V gives 19.3 L, 45.7 L, 31.8% and 30.6%. Table V is taken as the final estimates here.
- Discussion (p346) calls 12.8 L/h "CLR ... per 70 kg" and compares it with GFR of 6 L/h per 70 kg (tubular
  secretion).
- Cancer patients, many heavily pretreated; neutropenia graded weekly, so nadir timing is coarse.
