# Yano 1998: oral acetazolamide popPK and IOP Emax in elderly post-surgical eye patients

## Study
17 Japanese ophthalmology inpatients (11 F, 6 M) with transient IOP elevation, all but one after intraocular
surgery; 7 diabetic. Age 67.4 (SD 10.5) y, range 49-85; weight 55.0 (9.1) kg; serum creatinine 1.56 (0.66) mg/dL;
Cockcroft-Gault CCR 36.7 (13.7) mL/min, range 15.4-58.3 (Table 1, PDF p.2). Oral acetazolamide tablets (Lederle
Japan 250 mg; whole, half or quarter) after breakfast: single 62.5 mg (n=1), 125 mg (1), 250 mg (8); repeated
b.i.d. 125 mg (1), 250 mg (5), 500 mg (1). IOP (Goldmann) and venous plasma at about 1, 3, 5, 9 h after the last
dose; 69 concentrations, 79 IOP values. HPLC, total and unbound (ultrafiltration) plasma, linear 0.1-50 µg/mL,
interday CV < 5%. NONMEM IV: FO for PK, FOCE for PD; log (exponential) error for PK, additive for PD.

## Model and parameters
One compartment, first-order absorption with lag (equation 1; superposition for multiple doses). CL = θ1 × CCR,
CCR in mL/min (Cockcroft-Gault), CL in L/h (apparent oral, CL/F); V scaled linearly by body weight (V/F). Table 2
(PDF p.4, confirmed on page image):

Mean CL/F 1.70 L/h at CCR 36.7 mL/min (p.67; derived 0.0468 × 36.7 = 1.72) and V/F 0.231 L/kg (derived about 12.7
L at 55 kg; t½ about 5.2 h, derived).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: IOP (mmHg). Direct (no effect compartment): hysteresis seen in only 2 of 17 (ID 10, 14), so no ke0.
Emax model IOP = IOP0 − Emax·Cp/(EC50 + Cp), total plasma Cp (unbound fitted equally well). Table 3 (PDF p.4,
confirmed): IOP0 30.9 mmHg (28.7-33.1); Emax 7.2 mmHg (4.2-10.2); EC50 1.64 µg/mL (95% CI −1.16 to 4.44);
IIV IOP0 4.6 mmHg; residual 3.8 mmHg. Without the non-surgical patient: IOP0 30.7, Emax 7.4, EC50 1.82 µg/mL
(p.67). Target: 4 µg/mL total plasma gives 70% of Emax (p.67; derived 4/(1.64+4) = 0.71); dosing (Table 4) keeps
steady-state trough above 4 µg/mL for a 55-kg elderly patient: CCR 70 → 250 mg t.i.d.; 50 → 125 mg t.i.d.; 30 →
125 mg b.i.d.; 10 → 125 mg once daily. No toxic concentration defined. Not an opioid.

## Covariates
CL/F = 0.0468 × CCR (Cockcroft-Gault, mL/min); V/F = 0.231 × weight (kg). Linear, not allometric, on weight.

## Caveats
n = 17, elderly, mostly renally impaired, post-surgical, Japanese, mean 55 kg; sparse (≤ 4-5 samples) and the
one-compartment assumption was imposed, not tested against two compartments. Apparent oral parameters only (F
unknown). EC50 CI crosses zero; Emax small (23% of IOP0) in post-surgical eyes. Authors say the nomogram may not
suit young patients. No seizure end point.
