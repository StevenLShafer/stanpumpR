# Sum 1983: esmolol and its acid metabolite in healthy volunteers

## Study
- 8 healthy men, 21-27 yr (mean 24.5), 62.4-76.2 kg (mean 65.8) (Methods, PDF p.2, printed 428).
- Esmolol IV infusion for 2 h at 50, 150 and 400 ug/kg/min on three different days, ascending order, at least 22 h apart. Salt not stated.
- Blood sampled at -1, 29, 59, 119, 121, 123, 125.5, 138, 150, 164.5, 270, 390, 690 and 1110 min from start; **sampling site not stated**. **Whole blood**, immediately extracted with methylene chloride (stops esterase hydrolysis). Esmolol by GC-MS; metabolite (ASL-8123 acid) by HPLC-UV.
- Two-compartment open model for esmolol, a modified one-compartment model for the metabolite, nonlinear least squares (SAS NLIN), weights 1/C^2. Compartmental fit **only at 400 ug/kg/min**: at 50 and 150 the phases could not be resolved.
- PD: isoproterenol dose raising HR by 50%, given at 30, 60, 120 min during and 6, 45, 60 min after each infusion; placebo arm.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: blockade of the isoproterenol-induced rise in HR and systolic BP (Table IV, PDF p.6, printed 432). Effect correlated with log blood esmolol (HR r = 0.915, SBP r = 0.854; Fig. 3); not with metabolite.
- Concentration-effect (Discussion, PDF p.7, printed 433): blood 0.3 and 1 ug/mL gave about 50% and 80% reduction of the HR response and about 30% and 50% of the SBP response; expected at 150 ug/kg/min or more.
- Offset: effect gone within 6 min after 150 ug/kg/min; no significant effect at 45 min after any dose.
- No ke0, EC50 fit, Emax or gamma. Not an opioid: no MEAC or potency ratio.

## Covariates
None (8 young men; per-kg parameters only).

## Caveats
- Small n, young healthy men, awake. Compartmental parameters only from the 400 ug/kg/min arm; Table I values are means of individual fits, so derived combinations close only approximately.
- Sampling site unreported; arterial vs venous matters for an esterase-cleared drug.
- Dose basis (salt vs base) not stated.
- Large intersubject variability (CV 36-41%).
