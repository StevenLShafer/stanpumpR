# Greenblatt 1998: zaleplon vs zolpidem kinetics and dynamics

## Study
Double-blind, single-dose, 5-condition crossover (PDF p.2, printed 554): placebo, zaleplon 10 mg and 20 mg, zolpidem tartrate 10 mg and 20 mg, by mouth with 250 mL water, 48 h apart. 11 healthy male volunteers aged 21-44 y, nonsmokers, enrolled; 1 dropped out and was replaced, 10 completed. Fasting until 3 h after dosing. Venous plasma sampled predose and at 1/4, 1/2, 3/4, 1, 1.5, 2, 2.5, 3, 4, 5, 6 and 8 h. Zolpidem assay: HPLC with fluorescence, 0.5 mL plasma, limit of sensitivity 1.0 ng/mL (zaleplon limit of quantitation 0.5 ng/mL). Both are total plasma concentrations; no protein binding measured. Non-compartmental analysis: terminal slope (beta), t1/2, linear-trapezoid AUC extrapolated to infinity, apparent oral clearance = dose/AUC. Body weight not given.

## Model and parameters
No compartmental model. Table I (PDF p.4, printed 556; mean +/- SE, n=10, confirmed on the rendered page):

|---|---|---|
| Cmax, ng/mL | 125 +/- 15 | 232 +/- 17 |
| tmax, h | 1.7 +/- 0.3 | 2.0 +/- 0.3 |
| t1/2, h | 2.0 +/- 0.15 | 2.2 +/- 0.26 |
| AUC, ng/mL.h | 408 +/- 48 | 889 +/- 110 |
| Apparent oral clearance, mL/min | 378 +/- 52 | 341 +/- 38 |

Zaleplon (10 / 20 mg): Cmax 26.0 / 49.0 ng/mL, tmax 1.1 / 1.1 h, t1/2 1.01 / 0.98 h, AUC 42.1 / 93.2, CL/F 4436 / 4299 mL/min. Kinetics did not differ significantly between the two doses for either drug (dose-normalised comparison for Cmax and AUC). Text: overall zolpidem t1/2 about 2.1 h, individual range 1.2-4.0 h (p.4). Oral bioavailability of zolpidem about 70% is quoted from another source (ref 26), not measured here.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End points: self- and observer-rated sedation (100-mm VAS), other mood VAS items, digit symbol substitution test (DSST), word-list learning/recall, and scalp EEG relative beta (12-30 Hz) amplitude, each as 4-h effect area of change from baseline (Tables II, III). Zolpidem 20 mg was always the largest effect; order placebo < zaleplon 10 < zaleplon 20 < zolpidem 10 < zolpidem 20. Observer sedation effect area: placebo 2.3, zolpidem 10 mg 21.8, zolpidem 20 mg 61.7. DSST: -6.6, -32.1, -60.0. Concentration-effect relation fitted per drug with exponential functions (Figures 5-6, mean concentration vs placebo-corrected mean effect, EEG beta, observer sedation, DSST); fitted coefficients are not tabulated in the text read. No clear clockwise or counterclockwise hysteresis was seen. No ke0, t1/2ke0, tPeak, EC50 or Emax reported. No therapeutic or minimum effective concentration range stated. No equianalgesic ratio (not an opioid); the paper's potency statement is that 20 mg zaleplon was comparable to 10 mg zolpidem on some measures.

## Covariates
None. Healthy young men only; no sex, weight or age effects tested.

## Caveats
n=10, males only, single doses, a 20 mg dose above the 10 mg usual dose. Concentration-effect analysis uses group means, not individual fits. Some subjects had values treated as missing (one zolpidem 20 mg subject had 0 ng/mL at 6 h, p.4). Zaleplon deethyl metabolite was below the assay limit.
