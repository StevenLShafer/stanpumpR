# Halliday 1987: intravenous temazepam formulations

## Study
- Volunteers: 11 fit fasting non-smoking students, mean age 22 y, mean weight 68 kg, no medication. Each received temazepam 20 mg
  on four occasions in random order (>= 2 weeks apart): elixir (Euhypnos), soft capsule (Euhypnos), i.v. in 90% propylene glycol,
  i.v. in 40% sodium salicylate. Injection into a large antecubital vein over 20 s.
- Sampling 0, 5, 10, 15, 30, 60, 90, 120 min (contralateral cannula); HPLC-UV at 231 nm (Ho 1983); CV 3% within, 5% between batch
  at 800 ng/mL. Plasma, total drug.
- Patients: 72 unpremedicated women for gynaecological surgery, PG formulation only; 10 in a 2.5 mg increment pilot, then 0.5, 0.6,
  0.75 or 1.0 mg/kg over 20 s (n 15-16 per group; age 31-34 y, weight 57-61 kg, Table I). Thiopentone induction 10 min later;
  39 controls.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- Volunteers: 20 mg i.v. gave no more than slight drowsiness over 2 h; oral forms moderate sedation in 7-8 of 11.
- Patients: sedation maximal at 3 min at all doses; adequate sedation 15/31 at 0.5-0.6 mg/kg vs 26/31 at 0.75-1.0 mg/kg
  (P = 0.007); only 4/16 at 1 mg/kg needed no thiopentone. Thiopentone dose 2.4 ± 0.16 vs 4.6 ± 0.18 mg/kg in controls
  (P < 0.001). No concentration-effect analysis, no ke0.
- Tolerance: volunteers, salicylate pain 6 and thrombosis 2; PG pain 5, thrombosis 9 (of 11). Patients: pain in 60%, moderate to
  severe 36%. Authors stopped evaluation; venous intolerance precludes clinical use.

## Covariates
None.

## Caveats
2-h observation only; means without SD; figure-only data; small n; research formulations never marketed.
