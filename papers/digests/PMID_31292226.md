# Kasteleijn-Nolst Trenite 2019: cenobamate in the photosensitivity model

## Study
Phase 2a, single-blind (patients blinded; EEG read by a blinded expert), single-dose, sequential-cohort study at 4 US
centres, August 2007-January 2009 (NCT00616148; PDF p2). Adults 18-60 y, BMI 18-35, photosensitive epilepsy with a
reproducible IPS-induced photoparoxysmal response (PPR) of at least 3 points in one eye condition, on 0-2 AEDs (a second
AED only levetiracetam, gabapentin or pregabalin). Placebo day -1, single oral cenobamate day 1 (100, 250 or 400 mg;
600 mg planned, not given), placebo day 2. IPS at 14 flash frequencies in 3 eye conditions before and 1, 2, 3.5, 5, 6.5
and 8 h after dosing on days -1, 1 and 2 (and on day 3), scored as the standardized photosensitivity range (SPR,
0-14). Cenobamate PK samples predose and 1-54 h after the dose (PDF p3); NCA.

7 patients enrolled, 6 evaluable unique patients; 5 re-entered at a higher dose with new identifiers; ITT 3 (100 mg),
4 (250 mg), 4 (400 mg) (PDF p4). Table 1 (PDF p5, rendered): age 19-28 y (mean 22.1), weight 44.5-83.6 kg (mean
61.5), all White, 5 of 7 women, 6 generalized and 1 focal epilepsy; concomitant levetiracetam 5, valproate 2,
ethosuximide 1, topiramate 1 of 7 unique patients.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: SPR on EEG under IPS (photoparoxysmal response), most sensitive eye condition. Complete suppression = SPR
0 at one or more time points in all 3 eye conditions; partial = a fall of 3 or more points at 3 or more time points
against the same times on day -1. Table 2 (PDF p5): 100 mg partial in 1/3; 250 mg complete 1/4, partial in all 4 in
some eye condition; 400 mg complete 1/4, partial 2/4. Figure 2 (PDF p6, rendered): the 1-hour SPR on day -1 was about
8-9 points in each cohort, and the mean change at 1 h on day 1 and day 2 was about -1.7 to -2.8 points at 250 and
400 mg, with SDs as large as the means. The text (PDF p4) states the PPR was consistently reduced 1 h after 250 and
400 mg on days 1 and 2 (day 2 is more than 24 h after the dose). Exposure-response by individual (PDF p6-7):
Cmax 1.0-4.0 ug/mL partial in 1/3, 4.1-9.0 partial in 4/6, 9.1-16.0 complete in 2/2. No ke0, hysteresis analysis or
EC50 is reported. Figure 3 (mean concentrations) shows a concentration at 1 h well below the cohort peak, which comes
at about 2.8-7.9 h (median tmax).

## Covariates
None.

## Caveats
Very small (6 unique patients), young, mostly generalized epilepsy, no formal statistics (authors). Table 1 and the
text disagree on comedication counts (text, PDF p6: ethosuximide 2, levetiracetam 5, valproate 4; Table 1 totals 1, 5
and 2, while its footnote names 2 patients on ethosuximide plus levetiracetam): unresolved. Cohorts share patients.
