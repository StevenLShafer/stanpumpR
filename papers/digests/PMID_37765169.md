# Sharma 2023: nitrofurantoin PBPK (rabbit, rat, human)

## Study
Cross-species PBPK model (MCSim 6.1, MCMC fitting, normal/truncated-normal priors, 10% CV normal likelihood) for nitrofurantoin (NFT), built on digitised published data, no new data. Compartments: gut (with lumen), liver, fat, kidney (with filtrate and delay compartments), plasma, rest of body; later a bile compartment (enterohepatic recirculation, EHR). Data: rabbit IV/oral 0.25, 1.25, 2.5, 5, 10, 15 mg/kg plasma and urine (ref 17); rat IV/oral plasma, urine, bile (refs 23, 28); human oral plasma after single 50, 100, 200 mg (ref 4). Tissue:plasma partition by the Berezhkovskiy method; liver metabolism Michaelis-Menten. Model versions: V1 (GFR only) failed dose dependence; V2 (+saturable active tubular secretion) and V3 (+first-order reabsorption) each insufficient; V4 (both) fit rabbit; V5 adds EHR (rat); V5a fits EHR parameters hierarchically across rat and rabbit, then allometrically scaled to human (Sections 2.2, 3.1-3.4, PDF pp. 3-9).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD model, no effect-site equilibration (no ke0/tPeak), no MEAC. Not an opioid. The Introduction cites a literature claim that at least 30% of the dose must reach the bladder for therapeutic effect (PDF p. 2). Simulated human regimen 50 mg four times daily for 5 days: falling GFR (normal >90, moderate 70, mild 45, severe 20 mL/min) raises plasma Cmax about 1.3-fold and Ctrough about 2-fold, with about 30% less urinary excretion (Figure 7 and Discussion, PDF pp. 11-13).

## Covariates
GFR (scenario analysis only, categories above); age (25-40, via published physiology equations for body weight, cardiac output, organ volumes, blood flows); species allometry. No sex effect modelled.

## Caveats
Authors: no in vitro data for metabolism, absorption or renal elimination (top-down calibration); no gastric emptying or formulation effects; transporter role in humans unknown; EHR poorly constrained (biliary data for one rat dose); model predicts oral uptake too fast in rats; rabbit urine fraction mis-predicted before the hierarchical EHR fit. Human validation is plasma curves from one study, compared visually only. The GFR-exposure conclusions are model predictions, not observations.
