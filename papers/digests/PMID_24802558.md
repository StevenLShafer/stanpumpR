# Oualha 2014: norepinephrine PK/PD in hypotensive critically ill children

## Study
- Prospective, 18-bed neonatal and paediatric ICU, Necker, Paris, January 2011 to December 2012. Children < 18 y
  needing norepinephrine for hypotension (septic shock 16, non-traumatic cerebral injury 6, heavy sedation 8, severe
  congenital diaphragmatic defect 8); 50 enrolled, 38 analysed (PDF p4).
- Table 1 (PDF p5, rendered): age 7.6 (0-182) months; 27 (71%) male; weight 6.7 (2-85) kg; creatinine clearance 53.5
  (5-300) mL/min/1.73 m2; > 3 organ dysfunctions 14 (37%); baseline NE 0.54 (0.03-2.16) ug/L; MAP 46 (25-57) mmHg.
  11 neonates, 7 premature; 17 (45%) died.
- Noradrenaline 2 mg/mL (Renaudin) diluted to 20-200 ug/mL in 5% glucose, central venous catheter, 1-2 mL/h; dose 0.5
  (0.05-2) ug/kg/min for 1.5 (1-13) days (PDF p2, p4). The dose basis (base or tartrate) is not stated.
- Samples: C0 before infusion (22 patients), C1 >= 60 min after start or >= 40 min after a rate change (38), C2 (16):
  76 concentrations. HPLC with coulometric detection, LOQ 0.10 nmol/L. 431 MAP values. MONOLIX 4.13s, SAEM.

## Model and parameters
One compartment, first-order elimination, endogenous production: dA/dt = q0 + Rate - A x CL/V, C = A/V (Eq. 1-2,
PDF p3). V fixed to the circulating volume 0.08 x BW (not estimable from steady-state data). CL and q0 scale as
BW^0.75 (exponents first estimated, then fixed). Table 2 (PDF p5, rendered):

Worked example (PDF p5): at 10 kg CL 37.1 L/h, q0 17.5 ug/h, V 0.8 L, t1/2 0.9 min. Derived checks: 6.6 x 10^0.75 =
37.1; 0.693 x 0.8/37.1 x 60 = 0.90 min; q0/CL = 0.47 ug/L at 10 kg, against the observed baseline median 0.54 ug/L.
Figure 8 (PDF p11, rendered): concentration increase at 2 ug/kg/min about 51 ug/L at 70 kg and 23 ug/L at 3 kg;
derived steady state 8400/(6.6 x 70^0.75) = 52.6 and 360/(6.6 x 3^0.75) = 23.9 ug/L.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
MAP = MAP0 + dMAP x C/(C + EC50), direct Emax on plasma concentration, no hysteresis or ke0 (steady-state data).
MAP rose from 46 (26-57) to 50 (25-92) mmHg; HR unchanged (136 to 137). End point: MAP (invasive or non-invasive). Not
an opioid.

## Covariates
Body weight (allometric 0.75) on CL and q0; post-conceptional age on MAP0; number of organ dysfunctions (<= 3 vs >= 4)
on dMAP. Not significant on PK: sex, pH, temperature, PELOD, SNAP-II, organ dysfunctions, other catecholamines,
renal replacement, creatinine clearance, factor V, malnutrition.

## Caveats
- Supplement (sup0001.doc, 2026-10-27): definitions of the four inclusion diagnoses only. Septic shock by the
  paediatric Task Force criteria (suspected infection with cold or warm shock signs, including urine output below
  1 mL/kg/h); heavy sedation as non-hypovolaemic hypotension under midazolam for ventilation after other causes were
  excluded; non-traumatic cerebral injury (meningoencephalitis, status epilepticus, coma); neonatal non-hypovolaemic
  hypotension as a mean pressure below the gestational age in weeks after preload normalisation and hydrocortisone. No
  PK, PD, dataset or covariate numbers.
- 76 concentrations in 38 children, most of them infants; V fixed, so the 0.9 min half-life follows from the assumed
  volume.
- Inclusion weight printed as > 1500 g in the abstract and > 1200 g in the Methods (PDF p1, p2).
- Units printed per kg for theta_CL and theta_q0 although they multiply BW^0.75.
- Other catecholamines (epinephrine 5, dobutamine 6) ran at constant rates in some children.
