# Kang 2003: population apparent oral clearance of sustained-release verapamil

## Study
Population PK of long-term oral sustained-release racemic verapamil in 186 patients (hypertension, coronary
artery disease, supraventricular arrhythmia). Group 1: 81 clinical-care patients (Northwestern), stable dose
> 3 months, usually one sample 2–20.5 h post-dose (61 had several). Group 2: 105 patients from ALZA phase III
trials (80 hypertension, verapamil CR 120/180/360/540 mg daily for 12 weeks; 25 angina, verapamil SR
180/360/540 mg daily for 7 weeks), trough and 10–12 h samples (pp 32–33).
Table I (PDF p4): men 135 (age 63 ± 12 y, IBW 70.7 ± 6.6 kg, height 69.0 ± 2.9 in), women 51 (60 ± 17 y,
IBW 53.7 ± 7.2 kg, 63.6 ± 3.2 in). Age range 31–93 y. Formulations (Table II): Covera SR 105, Calan SR 67,
Verapamil SR 5, Verelan 7, Isoptin SR 2; mean dose 280 ± 139 mg (abstract).
Assay: group 1 total verapamil by HPLC-fluorescence, LLOQ 2 ng/mL; group 2 R/S-verapamil and norverapamil by
stereospecific capillary electrophoresis, LLOQ 2.5 ng/mL per analyte; plasma (p33). NONMEM, FOCE.

## Model and parameters
Steady-state model only: Css = Dose/CL, log-normal IIV on CL, proportional residual error (p33). No volumes,
absorption rate, bioavailability or intravenous data; CL is apparent oral clearance (CL/F), **per kg of ideal
body weight** (IBW = 50 kg + 2.3 kg/inch over 5 ft for men, 45.5 kg + 2.3 kg/inch for women; p34).

Final model M1 (all 186): CL = θ1 + θ2·SEX (SEX = 1 male, 0 female). Table IV (PDF p5, rendered):

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. The authors state they did not examine pharmacodynamic end points (p38). No ke0, tPeak or
therapeutic concentration.

## Covariates
Sex (M1, all patients). In group 1 (M14): smoking (+9.76), black race (+6.71), male (−10.4), smoking × black
(−10.2) mL/min/kg IBW. Nonsmoking black women 36.4 vs non-black 26.6 mL/min/kg (text p35). Age, formulation,
alcohol and dose not significant.

## Caveats
Sparse, mostly single samples; steady state assumed; dose and compliance self-reported or by pill count. Mixed
assays (total vs sum of enantiomers) between groups. Only apparent oral clearance; F unknown so not usable for an
intravenous model. Per-kg IBW normalisation chosen by the authors; multiple testing in group 1.
