# Tham 2008: tumour-shrinkage model for gemcitabine + carboplatin in NSCLC

(The context file carries no PubMed metadata; the citation and DOI are from the PDF, PDF p. 1.)

## Study
- 56 adults with stage IIIB (9) or IV (47) non-small cell lung cancer, from a randomised phase II trial
  (NCT00212043; Soo 2006). 39 men, 17 women; 45 Chinese, 10 Malay, 1 other. Age 58.2 ± 9.7 y, weight
  58.1 ± 11.9 kg, height 1.61 ± 0.08 m, CLcr 74.7 ± 18.9 mL/min (mean ± SD, Table 1, PDF p. 4).
- Gemcitabine 1000 mg/m² over 30 min or 750 mg/m² over 75 min (fixed dose rate) on days 1 and 8 every 3 weeks;
  carboplatin to AUC 5 mg·min/mL over 1 h before gemcitabine on day 1. Median gemcitabine dose 1382.5 mg
  (975–1990); median empirical Bayes gemcitabine AUC 5074.9 µg·h/L (2904.8–10589.8) (Table 1).
- PK exposures (AUC of gemcitabine, dFdU, PBMC dFdCTP) from individual Bayesian clearances of a four-compartment
  population model published separately (Tham, Cancer Chemother Pharmacol 2008).
- End point: longest unidimensional diameter (or sum over lesions) of the primary tumour on imaging, at baseline,
  after cycles 2, 4, 6 and bimonthly after; 202 measurements, follow-up 132.9 ± 113.1 days.
- NONMEM VI, FOCE; bootstrap (988 of 1000 runs successful); VPC with 500 replicates.

## Model and parameters
Dose of gemcitabine at each cycle enters an "exposure" compartment that decays with half-life t½,eq; inhibitory
Emax (Emax fixed to 1) on a Gompertz-like turnover:
dSize/dt = (RateIn·Effect − Size/Tturnover)·Size, Effect = 1 − Exposure/(Amt50 + Exposure),
RateIn = Size0/Tturnover (no net growth at baseline, assumed) (equation and text, PDF p. 3, printed p. 4215,
read on the page image).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: unidimensional primary-tumour size (cm), over weeks. t½,eq 7.67 weeks on that end point; it is a
  K-PD "exposure" half-life, not a plasma–effect-site ke0 in the usual sense.
- Amt50 10,600 mg of gemcitabine (dose amount, not a concentration). No EC50 in concentration units; no
  plasma or PBMC concentration threshold.
- Gemcitabine clearance BSV quoted as 32% (Discussion, PDF p. 4).
- Not an opioid paper.

## Covariates
None. A three-subgroup mixture on Amt50 did not improve the fit.

## Caveats
- Combination therapy: gemcitabine's effect cannot be separated from carboplatin's (authors).
- Baseline growth rate assumed from baseline size, not estimated.
- Very wide CIs (Tturnover BSV CI down to 0.58×10⁻⁵ %; Amt50 705–19,897 mg).
- Asian population, small body size (58 kg).
- Internal inconsistency (PDF p. 3): the Figure 2 diagram writes k2 = ln2/Tturnover, while its caption and the
  text equation use 1/Tturnover. The text equation is taken as the model here; no repo code depends on it.
