# Jiang 2008: gemcitabine and dFdU population PK in cancer patients

## Study
Pooled analysis of three clinical studies (Table 1, PDF p.3): Study 1 phase I dose escalation (n = 21, solid tumours, gemcitabine 30-min infusion then oxaliplatin 120 min, gemcitabine 750-1500 mg/m2 with oxaliplatin 40-80 mg/m2), Study 2 randomized sequencing study in NSCLC (n = 43, gemcitabine 1250 then oxaliplatin 70 mg/m2, or the reverse order), Study 3 randomized crossover of gemcitabine 1000 mg/m2 alone as a 30-min vs 100-min infusion (n = 31). Total 94 patients, 122 concentration-time profiles, 652 gemcitabine and 1130 dFdU plasma concentrations (PDF p.4). Table 2 (PDF p.4, image-read): age median 65 y (range 33-85, n = 94), weight 73 kg (37-120, n = 93), BSA 1.8 m2 (1.2-2.5), estimated Cockcroft-Gault CLcr 83 mL/min (37-150, n = 91), 68 men / 26 women, tumour NSCLC 47 / pancreatic 14 / diverse 33, gemcitabine-then-oxaliplatin 38, oxaliplatin-then-gemcitabine 25, gemcitabine alone 31, infusion rate median 55 mg/min (15-95). First-cycle samples at 0, 10, 25, 40 min, 1, 1.5, 2, 2.5, 4, 7, 24 h and up to 192 h after the dose; typically 3-6 gemcitabine samples and 12 dFdU samples per profile. Plasma, HPLC-UV at 272 nm, LOQ 0.2 ug/mL for both analytes. NONMEM V (FO, ADVAN6), forward inclusion / backward elimination at dOFV >= 10.83 (P = 0.001).

## Model and parameters
Two-compartment model for gemcitabine and a two-compartment model for dFdU with first-order formation from gemcitabine; proportional between-subject (BSV), between-occasion (BOV) and residual error. The fraction converted to dFdU could not be estimated, so the dFdU parameters are apparent (divided by F). Table 3 (PDF p.4, image-read), typical values; clearances in L/min (volumes in L):

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. Pure PK (plasma gemcitabine and dFdU); no effect, toxicity or response end point, no MEAC, no ke0. Not an opioid paper. The Discussion (PDF p.6) states dFdU is less active than gemcitabine (IC50 45-200 uM vs 3-5 nM, from the investigator brochure) and that dFdU AUC was about 15 000 vs 400 ug/mL.min for gemcitabine ("data not shown"); text layer only.

## Covariates
Table 4 (PDF p.5, image-read): CLcr on CL(dFdU)/F dOFV -17.7; BSA on Vc,dFdU/F -31.6; oxaliplatin on Vc,dFdU/F -107.0; NSCLC on Vc,dFdU/F -24.8 (OFV 5864.6 to 5683.5). Final equations as printed (CLcr in mL/min):

- CL(dFdU)/F (L/min) = 0.04 x (1 + 0.48 x CLcr/70)
- Vc,dFdU/F (L) = 46 x (BSA/1.73)^0.93 x 0.65^GEMOX x 0.54^OXGEM x 1.24^NSCLC

GEMOX = 1 when gemcitabine precedes oxaliplatin, OXGEM = 1 when oxaliplatin precedes gemcitabine, NSCLC = 1 for NSCLC. The abstract reports the oxaliplatin effects as 35% and 46% lower Vc,dFdU. BSA also affected Q(dFdU)/F (Results, PDF p.4) but the equation is not printed. No covariate on gemcitabine PK was significant; infusion rate (30 vs 100 min) did not alter gemcitabine or dFdU PK.

## Caveats
- Only 3-6 gemcitabine samples per profile (sparse), and Vc,gem has BOV 93%; the central volume is poorly characterized. First-order estimation (FO), not FOCE.
- Plasma only; intracellular dFdCTP, the active species, is not modelled (authors' stated limitation).
- F is not estimated, so every dFdU parameter is apparent.
- Dose basis in mg/m2 per Table 1; the dose basis for the model (mg of gemcitabine HCl vs base) is not stated.
- The CL(dFdU)/F equation's reference CLcr is ambiguous (open question 1).

|---|---|---|

| dFdU volume | Vc 46 L, Vp 192 L (apparent) | single compartment 52.6 L |

(derived: unit conversions and ratios are mine.) Jiang's CL is about 0.53x the library's; total gemcitabine volume 30 L vs 50.6 L. Jiang's CL is for patients with median weight 73 kg and no size covariate; the library scales to lean body mass. Jiang's dFdU clearance is far lower, and Jiang sampled to 192 h whereas the library header says Tham sampled only to 2 h after the infusion, so a one-compartment dFdU with half-life 1.70 h (header) cannot capture the long terminal phase that a 192-h profile does; dFdU time courses beyond a few hours will therefore differ. Tham's V1 is described in the header as essentially unidentified (90% CI 0.20-7.8 L), and Jiang's 15 L lies outside that interval. Direction and size of these differences are facts of the two studies (different populations: Asian NSCLC vs Australian mixed tumours; different sampling); whether the library should respond is open question 2. Verdict: partly-verified (no citing location; comparison only).
