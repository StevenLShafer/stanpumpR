# Panetta 2003: temozolomide population PK in children with CNS tumours

## Study
Phase II studies at St. Jude. 39 patients (20 boys, 19 girls), age median 7.1 y (range 0.7-21.9), BSA median 1.1 m2 (0.45-2.1) (Table 1). Normal age-adjusted creatinine, bilirubin, albumin, AST/ALT. Oral temozolomide (TMZ) 140-200 mg/m2 once daily x 5 days every 21 days, given 2 h before or after a meal (most at 200 mg/m2, n=88 studies). Plasma sampled pre-dose and at 0.25, 0.5, 1, 2, 2.5, 3, 6, 8 h on days 1 and 5 of courses 1 and 3. 132 studies: 109 training, 23 validation (last 10 patients' course 1 day 5, course 3 days 1 and 5). Assay: HPLC-UV for TMZ, MTIC and AIC (plasma, lithium heparin). Model: one compartment per analyte fitted per study with ADAPT II (maximum likelihood), then a second-stage linear mixed-effects model of the log parameters in S-Plus (two-stage approach). F fixed at 1, so V and CL are apparent (V/F, CL/F). Metabolite volumes fixed to V/F.

## Model and parameters
First-order absorption (ka) into one TMZ compartment (volume Vc/F, rate ke); MTIC and AIC compartments in series (Fig. 2). Population values (Table 2, PDF p. 4 / printed p. 438; mean of the individual estimates with range, %CV between and within patients, training set):

Table 2's values are population means of the individual parameters, not the typical (geometric) values of the Table 3 equations; the paper does not say which is which beyond the heading "Population mean". Table 2 gives no rate constants for MTIC and AIC.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No concentration-effect model, ke0, MEAC or therapeutic range. Exposure and toxicity (neutropenia, thrombocytopenia) are discussed in the Introduction only as background from adult studies. Not an opioid paper.

## Covariates
Table 3 (PDF p. 4):
- ln CL/F (L/h) = 0.42 + 1.08 x BSA.
- ln CL/F per m2 = 1.38 + 0.024 x age (y), P<0.02.
- ln Vc (L) = 1.34 + 1.09 x BSA.
- ln Vc per m2 = 2.36 + 0.019 x age, P<0.05.
- ln Cmax (mg/L) = 2.49 - 0.24 x BSA, or 2.44 - 0.026 x age.
- ln TMZ AUC = 3.9 - 0.026 x age.
- MTIC AUC = 1.12 - 0.014 x age (P=0.05).

Only BSA and age were significant "independently and in combination"; no combined equation is printed (Results, PDF p. 4). No effect of liver or renal indices. Dose: CL/F fell about 17% when the dose went from 200 to 150 mg/m2 (P<0.03, two-stage comparison; 4 of 11 patients with reductions of 40-100%). Day and course number had no effect. Validation (23 studies): CL and AUC unbiased; median accuracy 19% (quartiles 11-43%) for CL, 20% for AUC, against 9% in the training set.

## Caveats
Two-stage approach, not a joint NONMEM fit. F not identifiable (fixed at 1). The ka estimate is very imprecise (range 0.14-31.6, intrapatient CV 125%). Both BSA equations are exponential in BSA, fitted over 0.45-2.1 m2 only. Adult TMZ clearance is reported 25-41% greater than this cohort's 4.9 L/h/m2, compared with adult studies the authors cite, not with a library model. Paediatric comparison 4.3 L/h/m2 is Estlin 1998 (ref 8).
