# Lang 1996: isoflurane MAC reduction by remifentanil

## Study
- Two-centre (Duke, Emory), open-label, randomized; ASA 1-3, 18-65 yr, both sexes, within 50% of ideal body
  weight; elective surgery with a skin incision of at least 2 inches (or 1 inch laparoscopic) (p. 722).
- 220 enrolled; 198 analysed, mean age 39.3 (SD 12.8) yr (p. 724). 25 received no remifentanil (isoflurane MAC
  alone); 173 used for the MAC-reduction analysis; 3 given remifentanil without isoflurane excluded (log 0);
  12 withdrawn for technical problems, 7 excluded for protocol violations.
- Remifentanil by computer-assisted continuous infusion (CACI) to target whole-blood concentrations of
  0, 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32 ng/mL, started before isoflurane. Patients stratified by age (18-30, 31-55,
  56-65 yr). Isoflurane end-tidal target set by up/down (Dixon) with 0.1% steps; initial age-adjusted
  estimates 1.28, 1.15, 1.05% (p. 722). Group sizes Table 2/3: 26, 25, 26, 24, 28, 26, 24, 28, 13.
- CACI PK (Table 1, from Glass 1993, ref 8): k10 0.376 /min, k12 0.3090 /min, k21 0.1920 /min,
  V1 0.12 L/kg.
- Three arterial samples: at least 10 min before incision, just before incision, 1 min after incision. Whole
  blood (citric acid stabilised), GC high-resolution MS selected-ion monitoring; LLOQ 0.1, ULOQ 250 ng/mL,
  interday CV < 11.5% (p. 723). Mean of the last two samples used in the response analysis.
- End point: gross purposeful movement within 1 min of skin incision (coughing, chewing, swallowing not
  movement). Logistic regression on log(remifentanil) and log(isoflurane); age, weight, sex, ethnicity tested.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: movement to skin incision under isoflurane (MAC reduction). Concentrations are **arterial whole
  blood** remifentanil, measured, not target.
- C50 for 50% MAC reduction: 1.37 ng/mL whole blood, 40-yr-old (Table 4/6). Ceiling: 91% at 32 ng/mL; the
  reduction is incomplete even at 32 ng/mL.
- Abstract states 77% reduction at 4 ng/mL; Table 4 gives 72% (internal inconsistency).
- Potency (Table 6, 50% isoflurane MAC reduction, plasma except remifentanil): fentanyl 1.67 ng/mL (potency 1),
  sufentanil 0.14 (12), alfentanil 28.8 (1/16; 1/55 corrected for thiopental), remifentanil 1.37 whole blood
  (1.2). p. 726 also cites remifentanil 30-75 times alfentanil from other studies.
- t½ke0 of remifentanil quoted from other work as 1-1.5 min (p. 726, refs 8, 9); not measured here.
- No MEAC measured. Remifentanil > 2 ng/mL (whole blood) noted as causing significant respiratory depression
  (p. 726, from ref 8).

## Covariates
Age not significant on MAC alone or on MAC reduction (authors attribute this to too few patients at the age
extremes). Weight, sex, ethnicity tested; none reported as significant.

## Caveats
- Logistic coefficients not given; Table 4 is a modelled 40-year-old curve only.
- Whole-blood, not plasma, remifentanil; Table 6 compares it with plasma values of other opioids.
- Table 4 row for 16 ng/mL (67%) inconsistent with its MAC (0.17%); abstract vs Table 4 disagree at 4 ng/mL.
- Up/down design with only 13 patients at 32 ng/mL.
