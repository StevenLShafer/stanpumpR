# Lee 2017: Marsh and Schnider propofol models in underweight patients

Printed pages 883-891 = PDF pages 1-9 (printed page = PDF page + 882).

## Study
- Asan Medical Centre, Seoul; KCT0001502. 30 underweight (BMI < 18.5) ASA I-II adults, elective surgery, randomized to propofol effect-site TCI with the **modified Marsh** (n=15) or **Schnider** (n=15) model (Asan Pump 2.1.3). Remifentanil Ce-TCI (Minto model) at 3-20 ng/mL for haemodynamics, cisatracurium 0.2 mg/kg.
- Target Ce in order 2.5, 3, 4, 5, 6, then 2 ug/mL, each held at least 7 min after pseudo-steady state; arterial sample at the end of each step (3 mL, EDTA, plasma stored at -70 C). 172 samples (84 at Ce 2/2.5/3, 88 at 4/5/6); 8 missing from 4 patients (short surgery).
- Assay: ultrafast LC-MS/MS (API5500), plasma, validated 50-10000 ng/mL, LLOQ 50 ng/mL, within-run accuracy 98.3-99.6%, CV < 2.1% within / < 3.0% between run (p3-4).
- Table 1 (text layer): BMI 16.6 (1.1) Marsh group vs 16.1 (1.5) Schnider group; height 165.4 (9.7) vs 164.5 (7.6) cm; weight 45.3 (5.1) vs 43.6 (4.7) kg; age median 32 (26-41) vs 37 (30-53) yr. Sex and ASA split are interleaved in the text layer and not recorded.
- Performance: PE = (measured - predicted)/predicted, MDPE (bias), MDAPE (inaccuracy), divergence (%/h), wobble, pooled with fit4NM (Varvel). The predicted value is described as the predicted effect-site concentration (p3) while the measured value is plasma; for Eleveld "plasma concentrations retrospectively estimated" from the actual infusion histories (p3-4).
- Simulations (Figs 3-4, Supplementary Fig S2): male, 35 yr, 165 cm; BMI 16.5 (underweight) vs 20.5; clearance vs BMI; 10-min cumulative weight-normalized doses at Ce 2-6; 60-min infusion rates at Ce 3.

## Model and parameters

Divergence (all patients, % per h), overall: Marsh 13.7, Schnider 3.83, Eleveld 11.2. Wobble overall: Marsh 14.2, Schnider 16.8, Eleveld 12.9 (Table 2). Table 2 also splits results by randomized group. The abstract's figures agree with the all-patient rows. Note the abstract's labels "Ce 3" mean "Ce <= 3" and "Ce >= 4" in Table 2.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD end point measured. The only PD-related number is the Marsh model's ke0 of 1.21 per min, cited from Struys 2000 (p2), not estimated here. No MEAC, tPeak, EC50. Remifentanil (Minto model, Ce 3-20 ng/mL) was a background drug, not studied. Not an opioid paper.

## Covariates
Body size only through the models: simulation shows Schnider clearance exceeds modified Marsh clearance at BMI < 18.5 (Fig 3, p7), and Schnider's weight-normalized dose is higher in underweight than in normal patients, which the authors offer as the cause of the opposite biases (Marsh under-, Schnider over-predicts).

## Caveats
- n=30, 172 samples, pooled analysis; all biases had 95% CIs excluding zero but fell within the authors' acceptable range (MDPE < 10-20%, MDAPE 20-40%).
- Compared effect-site predictions with plasma samples at pseudo-steady state; concurrent remifentanil, ephedrine and phenylephrine could influence kinetics and prevented a per-group analysis for Eleveld.
- No underweight-specific model proposed; the Eleveld version is not stated (presumably the published 2014 Anesth Analg model, ref 17) and it is not the 2018 BJA model that stanpumpR implements; the stanpumpR model's performance in underweight patients is therefore not tested here.
- Bias shifts with target Ce (less negative at higher Ce) which the authors attribute to incomplete mixing, lung uptake or sampling error.
