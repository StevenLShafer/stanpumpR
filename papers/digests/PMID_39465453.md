# Li 2024: norepinephrine population PK in healthy volunteers, awake and anaesthetised

## Study
- Single centre (UMC Groningen), crossover, NL9312. 36 healthy volunteers, 12 in each age band 18-34, 35-50, 51-70 y,
  6 M / 6 F per band (PDF p2). Table 1 (PDF p4): median weight 66.9 / 72.3 / 69.6 kg, height 174.5 / 175.0 / 173.0 cm,
  BMI 22.9 / 24.4 / 23.0; mean age 42 (SD 16), mean BMI 23.2 (PDF p4).
- Norepinephrine tartrate (Centrafarm) 20 mcg/mL by peripheral IV: 5 mcg bolus, then 0.04, 0.08, 0.12, 0.16,
  0.20 mcg/kg/min, 15 min each, awake; after a 30 min washout, repeated under propofol (Eleveld TCI, age-adjusted
  EC50) and remifentanil (Eleveld, Ce 3.6 ng/mL), with a 30 s tetanic stimulus at each step (PDF p2-3). The dose basis
  (base or tartrate) is not stated.
- Radial arterial line; samples at baseline, end of each step, 2, 5, 30 min after stopping (awake) and additional ones
  under anaesthesia. LC-MS/MS (ref 20). 1219 samples after excluding 4 outliers (PDF p4); observed 0.25-67.09 nmol/L.
- NONMEM 7.5, FOCE-I; profile-likelihood CIs; pvcVPC (PDF p3-4).

## Model and parameters
Two compartments, first-order elimination, lag time (bounded 10-16 s), combined error. Endogenous production:
baseline = Prod/(CL/Vc) as amount (Eq. 1), i.e. Prod/CL as concentration. Allometry a priori, exponents 0.75 on
clearances and Prod, 1 on volumes, 0.25 on lag; reference weight not printed. Age: F_AGE = exp(theta_AGE/100 x
(AGE - 35)) (Eq. 2, rendered). Propofol: F_COV = exp((theta + eta)(COV - COV baseline median)) (Eq. 4, rendered; no
/100 printed). Table 3 (PDF p8, rendered):

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None in this paper (pressure response in de Keijzer 2026, PMID 41481868). No ke0. Not an opioid.

## Covariates
Weight (allometric, fixed exponents), age on CL, measured propofol concentration on CL (time-varying, with IIV).
Cardiac output explained less of the session effect than propofol.

## Caveats
- Healthy volunteers; propofol/remifentanil only; the authors say not directly translatable to cardiovascular
  patients.
- The text gives median baseline NE 2.92 nmol/L with IQR 1.3-2.2 (PDF p4-5), a median outside its own IQR; Table 2
  gives 1.40-1.97 by band, and the companion paper (PMID 41481868, Table 2) gives 1.5 [1.3 to 2.2]. Unresolved in
  this paper.
- Table 1 sample counts (711 + 682 + 685 = 2078) do not equal the 1219 samples analysed; not explained.
- Remifentanil Ce target 3.6 ng/mL here; de Keijzer 2026, same study, prints 4.0 ng/mL.
- The abstract's 10% CL decrease is the session factor of an intermediate model, not the final propofol model.
