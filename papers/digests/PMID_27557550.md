# Kim 2017: escitalopram PK and regional SERT occupancy ([11C]DASB PET), healthy Korean men

## Study
- 12 healthy Korean men, 19-30 yr; age 23.0 +/- 2.7 yr, height 173.1 +/- 6.9 cm, weight 69.4 +/- 7.9 kg.
- Single oral dose, parallel groups: 5 mg (n = 4), 10 mg (n = 4), 20 mg (n = 1), 30 mg (n = 3), fasting.
- PET with [11C]DASB the day before and 3, 24 and 46 h after the dose (Biograph 40); MRTM2, cerebellum reference with
  correction for its 33% displaceable fraction; ROIs putamen and dorsal raphe nucleus (DRN).
- Plasma escitalopram by LC-MS/MS (LLOQ 0.05 ng/mL; range 0.05-50) at 0, 1, 2, 3, 4.5, 6, 8, 10, 22, 24, 46, 72 h.
- NONMEM 7.3, FOCE-I, sequential PK then PD.

## Model and parameters
PK: two compartments, first-order absorption, proportional error. PD: direct-link sigmoid Emax on central
concentration, Emax fixed 100%, additive error. An effect-compartment model gave ke0 = 9660 (unit not stated), i.e.
instantaneous equilibration, so the direct link was kept.

Observations: Tmax 2.1 +/- 0.5 h; Cmax 4.8 +/- 0.8 (5 mg), 9.5 +/- 0.7 (10 mg), 21.7 (20 mg), 24.1 +/- 1.5 ng/mL
(30 mg). Max occupancy putamen 51.2, 58.7, 64.9, 62.2%; DRN 55.6, 73.7, 81.2, 78.7% (by dose). Extrapolated putamen
occupancy at 64.4 ng/mL (steady-state 30 mg, Sogaard 2005) = 77.6%.
Derived: terminal t1/2 from CL, Q, V1, V2 = 31.3 h (beta 0.0222 /h).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: SERT occupancy by PET in putamen and DRN (biomarker). EC50 4.30 ng/mL (putamen) and 2.89 ng/mL (DRN), total
plasma escitalopram, with Hill coefficients < 1. ke0 effectively infinite (9660, unit not given): no hysteresis
between plasma and SERT occupancy. Not an opioid.

## Covariates
None (homogeneous small sample).

## Caveats
n = 12 men, single doses (one subject at 20 mg); Emax fixed at 100% with shallow Hill slopes, so EC50 depends strongly
on that assumption; DRN is small (partial-volume effects); occupancies lower than most published SSRI studies
because of the reference-region correction.

## Verification against stanpumpR
- "Units could not be verified": Table 1 labels EC50 as ng/mL (4.30 putamen, 2.89 DRN), and Fig. 8 relates them to
  plasma escitalopram in ng/mL. The abstract omits the unit, which may be why it was unverified. **discrepancy**
  (documentation now outdated; the units are ng/mL of plasma escitalopram). Whether to plot remains a design choice;
  the Emax-100% and Hill < 1 caveats are a sound reason not to.
- Not an implemented source; no parameters to check numerically. Kim's healthy-volunteer CL/F (33.4 L/h) is about
  twice Liu 2022's patient CL/F (16.3 L/h) used by stanpumpR, as Liu's own Discussion notes for intensive-sampling
  studies.
