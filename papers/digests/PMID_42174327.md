# Blackman 2026: adult high-dose methotrexate population PK model (Vanderbilt)

## Study
- Retrospective EHR cohort, VUMC, Nov 2017 to Dec 2022; 208 adults (age 18-87, mean 51.6, 55.8% female), each given at least one IV MTX dose of 200 mg/m2 or more (Table 1, PDF p3-4). Glucarpidase patients excluded.
- 2,448 concentrations, 53 removed (line contamination etc.), 2,395 kept, split 70:30 at random: train N=145, test N=63. Train: weight mean 84.5 kg (42.4-141), height 170 cm, BSA 1.97 m2 (1.32-2.66), SCR median 68.5 umol/L (44.2-132) (Table 1). Median dose 3.5 g/m2 (0.2-12.1), infusion median 4.7 h (2.0-27.9). Disease: lymphoma 51%, leukaemia 41%, sarcoma 7%.
- Samples from 24 h after the start of infusion, then every 24 h until under 0.1 umol/L; concentrations beyond 96 h dropped. Concentrations in mg/L. Time-varying weight, height, BSA, SCR (SCR converted mg/dL to umol/L). Leucovorin from 24 h in all.
- Monolix 2024R SAEM for fitting; NONMEM 7.5 for MAP Bayesian forecasting. Assay not named in the text read.

## Model and parameters
Three-compartment, proportional residual error, IIV on all six parameters, inter-occasion variability (IOV) on CL. Final model (Table 2, PDF p6, confirmed on image):
CL = 9.41 x (BSA/1.97)^0.61 x (SCR/68.08)^-0.56 x exp(0.01 x female); all other parameters x (BSA/1.97)^1 (fixed exponent 1).
BSA beat weight on every likelihood measure. Final model OFV -2512.1, AIC -2478.1, BIC -2407.3.

Base model (no covariates, Table 2): CL 7.63 L/h, V1 17.88 L, Q2 0.75, V2 5.32, Q3 0.08, V3 5.37. BSA-adjusted: CL 9.08 x (BSA/1.97)^0.46, V1 30.21, Q2 0.71, V2 4.99, Q3 0.14, V3 12.05.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No PD, no effect-site model. Concentration thresholds appear only as the 0.1 umol/L stop level for monitoring and the 48-60 h glucarpidase window (cited from another paper).

## Covariates
BSA (all parameters, exponent on CL estimated, others fixed at 1), SCR (umol/L, time-varying) and sex on CL. Weight (allometric 0.75 or 1), height, age were tested and not kept.

## Caveats
- Predictive performance (test set, Table 4, PDF p12): own model RMSE 5.91 mg/L, MPE 10.61%, MAPE 46.88%, F20 24.51%, F30 35.52%. It did not meet the authors' criteria (MPE <= 20%, MAPE <= 30%, F20 >= 35%, F30 >= 50%); no model did. External models compared: Arshad, Beechinor, Gallais, Gao, Hui (ALL and OS), Maximova, Medellin-Garibay, Mei, Olivo, Taylor (Table 3 and 4).
- Bayesian forecast (Table 5, PDF p12): predicting the first (24 h) concentration, the model had MIPE 0.10% and F30 44.39%, the best of the six; the Hui ALL model was better for the 48 h prediction.
- The authors attribute the advantage at 24 h to adult versus mostly paediatric development populations of the comparators.
- Single centre, EHR data, no albumin, eGFR, genetics or comedications; samples only from 24 h, so Q2, Q3, V3 are weakly determined (V3 SE 2.43 on 10.60). Sex effect unexpected (authors' own word).
