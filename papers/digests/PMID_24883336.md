# Polepally 2014: population model of lamotrigine CL/F through pregnancy and postpartum

## Study
- Prospective observational cohort (Emory Women's Epilepsy and Women's Mental Health Programs), women >= 17 y on lamotrigine for epilepsy and/or a psychiatric disorder (mostly bipolar), pregnant or planning pregnancy (p. 100). Some subjects were in Pennell 2008.
- 60 women, 64 pregnancies (a second pregnancy treated as an independent subject), 600 concentrations; 42 women with epilepsy, 18 without; pregnancies 43 epilepsy, 19 psychiatric, 2 both (Results p. 102; Table 1 gives 43 / 21 by epilepsy vs non-epilepsy). Age 31.1 +/- 5.5 y, weight 64.3 +/- 12.7 kg, height 1.65 +/- 0.06 m, BMI 23.9 +/- 4.9 (Table 1, baseline). Race: 44 White, 10 Black, 4 Asian, 2 Native American.
- Samples by stage (Table 2, rendered): preconception 13 subjects / 35 samples; T1 (0-14 wk) 36 / 88; T2 (14-28 wk) 45 / 139; T3 (28 wk to delivery) 54 / 161; postpartum (to 70 wk) 51 / 177. Weight rose from 66.1 kg (T1) to 75.5 kg (T3).
- Daily doses 50-1400 mg, clinically adjusted; 73% twice daily. Samples not necessarily troughs, drawn at least 7 days after a dose change. Total lamotrigine by HPLC-UV (Chromsystems), LLOQ 0.25, ULOQ 20 ug/mL, CVs < 12.5%; plasma or serum.
- NONMEM 7; every observation modelled as a steady-state concentration, C = (daily dose / 24 h) / (CL/F) (Eq. 1); no volume estimated. Covariates tested: age, gestational age (GA), body size, indication, race. Bootstrap and standardized VPC.

## Model and parameters
Table 3 (rendered, PDF p. 5): preconception CL/F = CL_BL; pregnant CL/F = CL_BL + Slope x GA, with Slope1 (population I) or Slope2 (population II) assigned by a mixture model; postpartum CL/F = dCL_DEL x exp(-k x PPW) + CL_BL (PPW, postpartum weeks).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None modelled; no concentration-effect analysis. The authors infer clinical monitoring advice (monthly levels in population I; taper to preconception dose within 3 weeks of delivery).

## Covariates
Only GA (linear, two slopes by mixture) and postpartum week. Weight, age, indication (epilepsy or not) and race had no effect (chi-square, P > 0.05). No covariate identified population membership; women with two pregnancies fell in the same group both times. No co-medication (inducer, valproate, oestrogen) covariate reported.

## Caveats
- Steady-state model with no volume; non-trough samples; dosing changes clinical, not protocol-driven.
- Mixed indication (30% of women not epileptic); single-centre US cohort; 13 women had preconception data.
- Linear rise with GA (no plateau) is the authors' chosen form; it predicts the largest change at term only.
- The slope of population II is imprecise (RSE 53%, bootstrap CI 0.0012-0.0872).
- Supplementary modelling details not archived.
