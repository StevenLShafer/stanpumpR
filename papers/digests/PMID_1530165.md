# Telford 1992: fentanyl does not alter the "asleep" plasma concentration of thiopental

## Study
- 46 unpremedicated adults (age >=18; thiopental-only n=24, age 32 +/- 14 y, range 18-71, 9 F/15 M; thiopental+fentanyl n=22, age 41 +/- 14 y, range 18-64, 8 F/14 M), IRB approved, consent. Excluded: drug/alcohol abuse, current hypnotics or opiates.
- Thiopental infused by computer-assisted continuous infusion (CACI) to a set plasma concentration of 5, 7.5, 10, 15, 20 or 25 ug/mL (random allocation). In 22 patients fentanyl was also infused to a set plasma concentration of 1 ng/mL. CACI used Ghoneim & Van Hamme thiopental kinetics and McClain & Hug fentanyl kinetics (p.524).
- Venous blood (contralateral antecubital) at 5 and 10 min. Plasma fentanyl by RIA (LOD 0.2 ng/mL); total thiopental by HPLC (sensitive to 100 ng/mL); CV <10%. Whole/free: total plasma thiopental.
- End point: at 10 min, no eye-opening to a spoken name/command = "asleep". Only patients whose 10-min concentration was within +/-30% of the 5-min one were analysed (thiopental-only 17/24; thiopental+fentanyl 18/22). Logistic regression, maximum likelihood.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: loss of response to a verbal command to open eyes, 10 min into a constant-plasma-concentration infusion. Thiopental Cp50(asleep) 7.3 ug/mL (CI 5.5-10.9); Cp90(asleep) ~9.8-9.9 ug/mL.
- Fentanyl, at 0-2.1 ng/mL measured plasma, did not shift the thiopental Cp50 (P>0.5); age (18-71 y) was not a significant covariate (P>0.5).
- No ke0 estimated. The authors cite published keo values (thiopental, Hudson 1983; fentanyl, Scott 1985) to say equilibration at 10 min is predicted ~100% for thiopental and ~75% for fentanyl (Discussion p.526). Hudson's EEG IC50 for thiopental is quoted as 16 ug/mL.
- Opioid audit (fentanyl): no MEAC measured. 1 ng/mL was chosen as an "analgesic concentration" on the basis of earlier work (ref 24; MAC-reduction studies 0-1 ng/mL). No potency ratio. No fentanyl ke0 of its own (only the 75% at 10 min citation).

## Covariates
Age: none (P>0.5). Fentanyl concentration: none (P>0.5).

## Caveats
Venous sampling (arterial thiopental is higher early; venous hysteresis is less); only ~30% concentration stability enforced; total not free thiopental; fentanyl only up to 2.1 ng/mL, so interaction at higher concentrations is not excluded; end point is hypnosis, not response to incision.
