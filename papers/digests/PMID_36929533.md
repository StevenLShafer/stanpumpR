# Lin 2023: PopPK of intramuscular JZP458 (recombinant Erwinia asparaginase) in ALL/LBL

## Study
- Source data: phase II/III study AALL1931 (NCT04145531, with the Children's Oncology Group). Patients with ALL or lymphoblastic lymphoma who had grade 3 or greater allergy or silent inactivation to a long-acting E. coli asparaginase. Six i.m. doses replaced each remaining E. coli dose; two weeks = one course (p. 2, PDF p3).
- Cohorts: 1a 25 mg/m2 MWF (n = 32), 1b 37.5 mg/m2 MWF (n = 83), 1c 25/25/50 mg/m2 MWF (n = 51). Total 166 patients (PDF p4).
- Population (Table 1): age 1.7 to 25 y, mean 10.21 (SD 5.72); weight 9.33 to 131.0 kg, mean 41.62; BSA 0.44 to 2.53 m2, mean 1.23; 103 male (62.0%), 63 female (38.0%). Race, ethnicity, subtype and ADA counts are not read here (columns interleave in the text layer).
- Sampling: 8 PK samples in course 1 (predose; 48 or 72 h after doses 1 to 5; 2.5 h after doses 1 and 4), 3 per later course. Assay is serum asparaginase activity (SAA), enzyme activity assay; calibration 0.0349 (LLOQ) to 0.2096 IU/mL (ULOQ), dilution linearity to 467.72-fold (PDF p3).
- Data: 2687 SAA points (2145 quantifiable, 542 BLOQ, M3 method). NONMEM 7.3, FOCEI with Laplacian. Absorption parameters (Ka, zero-order rate, F) were fixed from the phase I healthy-volunteer model (ref 9) because sampling was sparse (PDF p4).
- Simulations: 2000 virtual subjects from NHANES 2017 to March 2020 (1000 paediatric, 1000 adult; weight 4.2 to 181.0 kg, BSA 0.25 to 3.03 m2) and 2000 resampled from AALL1931 patients.

## Model and parameters
One-compartment, simultaneous zero-order plus first-order absorption, linear elimination. Exponential IIV on CL and V; proportional plus additive residual error. BSA scaled to 1.2 m2 as an allometric covariate (PDF p6):

CL (mL/h) = 146 x (BSA/1.2)^1.48 x 0.674 [if Black/African American] x 0.771 [if T-ALL]
V (mL) = 445 x (BSA/1.2)^1.61

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- No PD model. The surrogate end point is nadir serum asparaginase activity (NSAA) at or above 0.1 IU/mL, taken as adequate asparagine depletion (intro, PDF p2; refs 6, 7). This is a target activity threshold, not a MEAC.
- Response rate = proportion with NSAA at or above 0.1 IU/mL at the last 48 h or last 72 h. Observed (Table 3, text layer): 25 mg/m2 MWF last 72 h 64.3%, 37.5 mg/m2 MWF 90.8%, 25/25/50 MWF 89.8%. Simulated NHANES last 72 h: 76.4%, 88.5%, 92.1%; 25 mg/m2 q48h x7: 93.8% (last 48 h). Last-48 h simulated: 93.7%, 96.3%, 93.8% for the three MWF regimens.
- No ke0, t1/2ke0, tPeak or hysteresis (PK only). Not an opioid paper; no potency ratio.
- Exposure-response: AUC336, Cmax,6, C48,L, C72,L versus five adverse events; no significant relationship found (small event numbers).

## Covariates
BSA (allometric, exponents 1.48 on CL and 1.61 on V, reference 1.2 m2), Black/African American race (CL x 0.674), T-ALL subtype (CL x 0.771). Tested and not retained: age, sex, height, weight, ethnicity, ALL vs LBL, ADA status. Retention threshold p 0.01 forward, 0.005 backward.

## Caveats
- Ka, zero-order rate and F were fixed from a different (healthy volunteer) study; sparse sampling.
- Table 1 and Table 3 values were read from the text layer only.
- Terminal t1/2 19.1 h is absorption-limited (flip-flop): Ka 0.0369/h gives an absorption half-life of about 18.8 h (derived), whereas V/CL from Table 2 would give an elimination half-life near 2 h (derived).
- Sponsor-funded; several authors are Jazz employees.
