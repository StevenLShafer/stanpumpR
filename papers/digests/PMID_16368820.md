# Enggaard 2006: IV tramadol analgesia in CYP2D6 extensive vs poor metabolizers

## Study
- Two randomized, balanced, double-blind, placebo-controlled crossover trials (same site, same investigators), one per
  sparteine phenotype; washout at least 1 week (p. 147).
- 10 extensive metabolizers (EM; sparteine MR 0.1-0.8; 4 M / 6 F, age 21-30 y) and 10 poor metabolizers (PM;
  MR >= 20; 6 M / 4 F, age 22-33 y), selected from >2000 phenotyped healthy volunteers. Weight not reported.
- Treatments: tramadol 100 mg IV bolus injected over 5 min vs saline (Grünenthal). Salt/base not stated.
- Pain tests before and 15, 30, 60, 90 min after dosing: single electrical sural-nerve stimulation (pain detection
  and tolerance thresholds, mA), repetitive stimulation (pain summation threshold, mA), cold pressor test
  (1.0 °C, 2 min; peak pain VAS and discomfort VAS, cm).
- Blood after each test session; serum (+)/(-)-tramadol and (+)/(-)-M1 by a modified gas chromatographic method
  (refs 2, 11). M1 limit of determination 5.1 ng/mL (Fig. 4 legend; values below set to 5 ng/mL).
- Statistics: sums of pre-minus-post differences, Wilcoxon paired; Mann-Whitney between phenotypes; Spearman vs AUC0-90.

## Model and parameters
No PK model fitted. Descriptive concentrations only (Figs 3-4, means; no tabulated values):
- Tramadol enantiomer concentrations did not differ between EM and PM (p. 147).
- (+)-M1: below the limit of determination in all PMs; in EMs from below the limit (all times, one subject) to
  30 ng/mL (p. 147-148); stated elsewhere as 5.5-29 ng/mL (p. 148) and 5-30 ng/mL (Discussion, p. 149).
- (-)-M1 below the limit at all times in 6 of 10 PMs; significantly higher in EM than PM at every time
  (P < 0.01 at 15 min, P < 0.001 at 30-90 min).
- Discussion compares with their earlier oral 2 mg/kg study: (+)-M1 20-60 ng/mL after oral vs 5-30 ng/mL after IV,
  attributed to absence of first-pass formation.

- EM vs PM differed in cold-pressor discomfort at 90 min (P < 0.02).
- Reduction in cold-pressor peak pain correlated with (+)-M1 AUC0-90 in EMs (rs = -0.758, P < 0.02; Fig. 5); no
  relation with tramadol AUC0-90 in either phenotype.
- Interpretation: cold pressor is opioid-sensitive and reflects (+)-M1; the sural-nerve tolerance threshold rose in PMs
  and is taken as a marker of the parent's monoaminergic effect.

## Covariates
CYP2D6 phenotype (sparteine MR) is the design factor. No other covariates.

## Caveats
- n = 10 per phenotype; healthy young volunteers; experimental pain; 90 min observation window only.
- Single 100 mg IV dose; (+)-M1 concentrations near the assay limit (5.1 ng/mL), so M1 exposure is poorly resolved.
- EM tramadol produced no significant change in sural-nerve thresholds, which the authors cannot explain by PK.
- This is a phenotype-comparison study, not an antagonist study.
- Funded and drug supplied by Grünenthal; one author (Ossig) from Grünenthal.
