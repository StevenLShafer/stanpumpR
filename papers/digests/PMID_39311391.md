# Punyawudho 2025: dolutegravir PopPK with rifampicin (Thai PLWH)

## Study
Cross-sectional analytical study (HIV-NAT, Bangkok; NCT03731559). 40 Thai adults (>18 y) with new HIV/TB co-infection on rifampicin-based TB therapy (450 mg if 35-49 kg, 600 mg if >=50 kg), randomised to dolutegravir (DTG) 50 mg once daily with food (n=20) or 50 mg twice daily without food (n=20). Intensive sampling at week 4: pre-dose, 1, 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12 h (b.i.d. arm); extra 24 h sample in the OD arm. Plasma DTG by LC-MS/MS (LLOQ 0.1 mg/L; accuracy 83.5-118.4%; precision <15%). 332 concentrations from 40 people analysed (160 OD arm, 140 b.i.d. arm per Table 1; one participant's data removed except pre-dose because all values < LLOQ). BLQ replaced by LLOQ/2. NONMEM 7.4, FOCEI; 1000-sample bootstrap and VPC. Demographics (Table 1, PDF p.4): 85% / 90% male; weight 59.3 / 60.2 kg (reported as mean +/- SD with range 41.1-78.4 / 47.1-86.0, though labelled "median"); total bilirubin 0.380 / 0.350 mg/dL.

## Model and parameters
One-compartment, first-order absorption with lag time, first-order elimination. F fixed to 1 (so CL/F and V/F are apparent). Allometric weight on CL/F (exponent 0.75) and V/F (exponent 1), fixed; reference weight 60 kg (equation PDF p.4). Only significant covariate: total bilirubin on CL/F. IOV on F and Ka (each dosing occasion). No IIV on V/F or lag time (not estimable). Proportional residual error. Food had no detectable effect on F or lag time.

CL/F = 2.82 x (weight/60)^0.75 x exp(-0.297 x (total bilirubin - 0.38)); bilirubin in mg/dL (PDF p.4). Authors state 1 mg/dL of bilirubin lowers CL/F by 25.7%.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD model, no ke0. Targets used for simulation (not measured here): in vitro protein-adjusted IC90 0.064 mg/L; in vivo EC90 0.3 mg/L trough (taken from the SPRING-2b/phase IIb 10 mg dose trough, per the Introduction and refs 7, 8, 20). Simulations: 10,000 steady-state troughs per regimen, weight groups 41-60 and 60.1-86 kg, five bilirubin grades (normal 0.1-1.29 mg/dL ... grade 4 >6.1), weight and bilirubin allocated randomly. Table 3 (PDF p.7, image-confirmed), % troughs above target, normal bilirubin:

| Weight | Regimen | >0.064 mg/L | >0.3 mg/L |
|---|---|---|---|
| 41-60 kg | 50 mg OD | 79.2 | 20.9 |
| 41-60 kg | 50 mg b.i.d. | 99.9 | 95.5 |
| 41-60 kg | 100 mg OD | 92.6 | 49.7 |
| 60.1-86 kg | 50 mg OD | 78.4 | 16.2 |
| 60.1-86 kg | 50 mg b.i.d. | 99.9 | 92.7 |
| 60.1-86 kg | 100 mg OD | 92.9 | 45.7 |

Higher bilirubin grades raise the percentages (grade 4 all 100%). Toxicity thresholds quoted from other work: trough >1.34 mg/L (Yagura) and 1.7 mg/L linked with neuropsychiatric adverse events; simulated fractions above 1.34 mg/L for 41-60 kg, normal bilirubin: 0.15% (50 OD), 25.9% (b.i.d.), 2.91% (100 OD) (text p.102, data not shown in a table).

## Covariates
Weight (allometric, fixed exponents) and total bilirubin on CL/F, as above. Sex, age, food, serum creatinine tested and not retained. Fat-free mass was tested and was worse than total weight.

## Caveats
All participants were on rifampicin, so the effect of rifampicin cannot be isolated; no rifampicin concentrations. Model overpredicts DTG during 0-5 h (food-treated people). Small n (40), 90.6% IOV on F. Weight range 41-86 kg, mostly men. Clinical efficacy of 100 mg OD not tested. Table 1 labels mean +/- SD as "median".
