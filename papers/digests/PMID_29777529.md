# Hauser 2019: ADS-5102 (amantadine ER, Gocovri) pharmacokinetics

## Study
Sponsor (Adamas) report of three studies (Table 1, PDF p3):
- **Study 1** (phase I, crossover): 30 healthy volunteers, mean age 30.4 y, 53% male, 18-45 y, 50-100 kg;
  single morning fasted doses of ADS-5102 68.5, 137 and 274 mg (= 85, 170, 340 mg amantadine HCl).
- **Study 2** (phase I, two-period crossover): 24 healthy volunteers, mean age 31.8 y, 79% male; ADS-5102 137 mg
  at 8 pm single dose then once daily for 7 days, versus amantadine IR 161 mg at 8 pm single dose then 81 mg twice
  daily (8 pm, 8 am) for 7 days; at least 7-day washout.
- **Study 3** (phase II/III EASED, NCT01397422): 83 Parkinson's disease (PD) patients with dyskinesia, 30-85 y
  (mean 66.0), randomised to placebo (22) or ADS-5102 210, 274, 338 mg at bedtime (20, 21, 20) for 8 weeks;
  samples on day 1 and weeks 1, 2, 4, 6/8, drawn 9 am to 4 pm.
- Assay: LC-MS/MS (API 4000 / API 5000), plasma, amantadine-d6 internal standard. No LLOQ printed in the article.
- Doses are stated as amantadine free base ("ama"): 137 mg base = 170 mg HCl, 161 mg base = 200 mg HCl (Fig. 1
  caption; Section 2.1).

Dose proportionality (68-274 mg, power model): slope 0.994 (90% CI 0.942-1.046) for Cmax, 1.081 (1.032-1.129)
for AUCinf. The Cmax/dose and AUC/dose rows are per mg of amantadine **HCl** (derived: 141.4/85 = 1.66,
4254/85 = 50.0, 464.0/200 = 2.32), not per mg of base; the table does not say so.

Steady state, healthy volunteers, day 7 (Table 3, PDF p6, rendered): ADS-5102 137 mg once daily vs IR 81 mg twice
daily: Cmax,ss 536.1 vs 611.7 ng/mL; Cmin,ss 284.8 vs 366.3; Cavg,ss 414.4 vs 480.5; median Tmax,ss 8.00 h
(4-18) vs 14.00 h (2-16; 2 h after the second IR dose); AUC24,ss 9947 vs 11531 ng·h/mL; accumulation 2.52 vs
1.82; degree of fluctuation 0.62 vs 0.54. Predose on days 8, 9, 10: geometric means 317.3, 307.6, 290.7 ng/mL
(Section 3.2, PDF p5).

Relative bioavailability ADS-5102 vs IR (single dose): dose-normalised AUCinf ratio 93.0% (90% CI 87.3-99.0);
dose-normalised Cmax 34% lower (Section 3.4, PDF p8). Steady state not bioequivalent: Cmin,ss ratio 77.18%.

Models (no parameter values printed): a one-compartment first-order absorption model with lag (equation, PDF p4)
for renal-function simulations, with ke tied to creatinine clearance from four older papers; for the Fig. 4
"real-world" simulations, one-compartment fits to individual study-2 data with parallel first- and zero-order
absorption for ADS-5102, scaled by a correction factor of 1.6 for the lower clearance of PD patients
(CrCl 121 vs 74 mL/min/1.73 m2).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- No PK/PD model fitted. Preclinical (animal) dyskinesia EC50 about 1400 ng/mL (about 9 µM), quoted from an
  unpublished study under review (ref. 6, PDF p2); NMDA IC50 in striatal neurons 12 µM (ref. 5).
- PD patients on 274 and 338 mg (significant dyskinesia reduction over placebo) had mean plasma concentrations
  of about 1500 ng/mL, observed (Section 3.3, PDF p6-7; Fig. 2a). Healthy-volunteer scaling predicted Cmax,ss
  about 1000 ng/mL at 274 mg.
- CNS adverse events: 41% placebo, 40/52/55% at 210/274/338 mg; median concentration about 2100 ng/mL in the 10
  patients (16%) who discontinued for CNS events (PDF p7).
- Simulated daytime (6 am-6 pm) average 1536 ng/mL for 274 mg at 10 pm versus 751 and 1125 ng/mL for IR 81 mg
  twice and three times daily (modelled, PDF p9).

## Covariates
Renal function is the only covariate discussed: baseline median eGFR 74 mL/min/1.73 m2 (range 50-123) in PD
patients vs 121 (84-151) in study-2 volunteers. No equation is printed.

## Caveats
- Sponsor-authored; the PK/PD correlation it leans on (EC50 1400 ng/mL) is an animal value from a paper under
  review at the time.
- Section 3.4 (PDF p8) calls the single-dose Tmax values medians of 12.0 and 3.0 h; Table 2 labels the same
  arms' Tmax as mean (range), 11.5 and 3.2 h. Unresolved; both are reported.
- In Table 2 the 137 mg am Cmax/dose carries %CV 32.7, not the 24.9 of its Cmax; with the 137 mg pm column also
  at 32.7 this looks like a copied value, but the page does not show which.
- The Fig. 4 and Fig. 2b profiles are simulations, not observations; the 1.6 clearance factor is an assumption
  from eGFR ratios.
