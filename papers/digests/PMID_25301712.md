# Hu 2014: levornidazole, metabolites and comparators against anaerobes in vitro

## Study
- 375 clinical anaerobic isolates (Huashan Hospital, Shanghai, January 2006 to February 2013), 34 species:
  181 Gram-negative bacilli, 11 Gram-negative cocci, 139 Gram-positive bacilli, 44 Gram-positive cocci.
- Agents: levornidazole, ornidazole, metronidazole, dextrornidazole and levornidazole metabolites M1-M5.
- MIC: CLSI agar dilution (M11-A8), Brucella agar with 5% laked sheep blood, haemin, vitamin K1; inoculum about
  10^8 CFU/mL by multipoint inoculator; 35 °C anaerobic, 48 h. Valid only if metronidazole MIC for B. fragilis
  ATCC 25285 was 0.25-1 mg/L.
- MBC: levornidazole and metronidazole only, broth microdilution, 22 clinical B. fragilis plus ATCC 25285; CFU counted
  at 48 h; MBC = concentration reducing the initial viable count by ≥99.9% (p. 517).
- Susceptibility by the CLSI metronidazole breakpoint (≤8 mg/L). Funding and test materials from the
  levornidazole manufacturer (competing interest stated).

## Model and parameters
No pharmacokinetics. In vitro MIC/MBC only, mg/L of drug in medium (total; no protein).

Table 3 (PDF p. 5, printed p. 518), 22 clinical B. fragilis:

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Levornidazole | 0.25-2 | 0.5 | 1 | 1-4 | 2 | 4 |

MBC50/MIC50 = MBC90/MIC90 = 4 for both drugs (p. 518).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End points: MIC (no visible growth, agar dilution, 48 h) and MBC (≥99.9% kill, microdilution, 48 h). No PK/PD index,
no concentration-time data. Not an opioid; no MEAC, ke0 or potency ratio.

## Covariates
None (in vitro).

## Caveats
- Single centre, Chinese isolates 2006-2013; MBC on only 22 B. fragilis strains.
- MIC (agar) and MBC (broth microdilution) used different methods; Table 3 does not say how its MICs for the 22
  strains were measured.
- MBC is at a 48 h count, longer than the classic 24 h subculture; values in drug-free medium, no protein.
- Manufacturer-supplied materials and funding.

## Verification against stanpumpR
- **MIC50/MIC90 0.5/1** (R/antibioticMbc.R:335, sourced to Boiten 2024): Hu agrees for B. fragilis in both Table 2 and
  Table 3. **matches** (independent check).
- **MBC/MIC ratio of 4** (R/antibioticMbc.R:344-345): Table 3 and p. 518 give 4 for both MBC50/MIC50 and
  MBC90/MIC90. **matches**.
- **MBC90 = 4 mg/L "measured"** (R:335-336): Hu's metronidazole MBC90 is 4 mg/L (Table 3). **matches**.
- **MBC50 = 1 mg/L, "MIC x 2"** (R:335-336): Hu's measured metronidazole MBC50 against B. fragilis is 2 mg/L, not 1.
  The row cites Hu's ratio of 4 while setting MBC50 at a ratio of 2 (from Garcia-Rodriguez 1990's 1-2x). Hu does not
  support MBC50 = 1. **discrepancy** (see open question).
- **"Comparison with levornidazole"** (R:345): the ratio is metronidazole's own, measured in parallel with
  levornidazole; it is not a ratio derived relative to levornidazole. Wording only.
- **Free drug** (inst/help/models/antibiotics.md:3): Hu's MICs/MBCs are in protein-free medium, consistent with the
  help's free-drug convention.
- **"abstract gives an MBC/MIC ratio of 4 but not the values"** (inst/help/models/antibiotics.md:59): the full table
  is now read: metronidazole MBC50 2, MBC90 4 mg/L (MBC range 1-8). The help entry can be closed when the row is revised.
- Other repo metronidazole rows, compared with Hu as an uncited check: "Other anaerobes" MIC90 1 (R:347; Boiten) vs
  Hu Prevotella and Peptostreptococcus MIC90 2 and C. perfringens 2 (Hu one dilution higher); C. difficile MIC90 2
  (R:357; Gargis 2023, US) vs Hu 0.5 (Chinese isolates; that row is excluded from the plot anyway).
