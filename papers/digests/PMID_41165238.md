# Müller 2026: pregabalin 300 mg and the propofol Cp50 for skin incision

## Study
RCT, double-blind. 80 women (ASA I-II, 25-65 y, BMI 18.5-30) for breast surgery, Vienna, Dec 2022-Nov 2023. Pregabalin 300 mg or placebo: abstract says 2 hours before induction; Methods say 1 to 2 hours before surgery. Propofol alone by Schnider effect-site TCI at assigned targets 5.5-14.5 ug/mL, >= 20 min equilibration, then standardised skin incision; movement scored. Arterial samples: pregabalin immediately before incision (LC-MS/MS, MassTox TDM kit); propofol at 15 and 20 min.
Pregabalin group: age 49.0 (11.6) y, BMI 24.8 (3.00); equilibration time 22.7 min (Table 1). Weight and height not reported.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: movement to skin incision under propofol alone (logistic regression, delta and bootstrap). The paper gives two reductions, on two concentration scales:
- **44%** on measured arterial plasma propofol just before incision (16.9 to 9.4 ug/mL, delta method; Table 2, printed
  p. 378; derived: 7.5/16.9 = 44.4%). This is the figure in the abstract Conclusions (printed p. 373) and the
  Discussion (printed p. 380). On this scale the bootstrap CI of the difference crossed zero (6.96, -2.91 to 18.9);
  the delta-method CI did not (7.53, 0.39 to 14.7).
- **34%** on the Schnider target effect-site concentration (8.47 to 5.62 ug/mL; Table 4, printed p. 380; derived:
  2.85/8.47 = 33.6%). This is the figure in Key Points (printed p. 373, unlabelled there) and the Discussion; both
  delta and bootstrap CIs of the difference exclude zero.
Measured plasma propofol ran about twice the Schnider targets at the Cp50 (derived: 16.9/8.47). Secondary: piritramide PCA 2.52 vs 4.51 mg, NRS 1.7 vs 3.1 at 2 h. No ke0, MEAC or potency ratio for pregabalin.

## Covariates
None.

## Caveats
Women only; single dose; dose-to-sample interval not exactly stated (1-2 h before surgery plus ~23 min equilibration); measured plasma propofol differed significantly from Schnider targets.
