# Coppens 2011: using population PK models to estimate propofol/BIS PD in children

## Study
28 ASA 1 children (19 boys, 9 girls), 4 groups of 7 by age 3-5, 5-7, 7-9, 9-11 y; median [range] age 6.5 y (4-11), weight 21.5 kg (18-54), height 119 cm (105-152) (printed p.87). Dental treatment under general anaesthesia, no premedication, no opioid. Propofol by TCI (Kataria model, RUGLOOP II): target 7 ug/mL for 15 min, then 1 ug/mL for 15 min or until awakening signs, then 5 ug/mL for 15 min. Venous plasma samples (bound plus free, HPLC-fluorescence, LOQ 0.2 ug/mL) at baseline and 1, 2, 5, 10, 15 min after each target change: 443 samples. BIS (A-2000, version 4.0 XP, smoothing 15 s). NONMEM VI, three-compartment PK plus effect site (ke0) and sigmoid Emax on BIS with E0 fixed at 95 and Emax fixed at 0; BIS delay 10 s assumed. Two strategies: "classic" sequential PK-PD (population PK estimated from the children's own samples, then PD) versus fixing PK to nine literature propofol models (Kataria, Paedfusor, Marsh, Short, Rigby-Jones, Rigby-Jones multicenter, Schuttler, ShangGuan, Schnider) and estimating PD only.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point is BIS (frontal EEG), with E0 fixed at 95 and Emax fixed at 0; concentration is venous plasma, total (bound plus free) propofol; effect site linked to the central compartment by ke0, with a 10 s monitor delay assumed (which the authors say changes the absolute ke0; printed p.91).
- Classic PK-PD: ke0 0.79 /min (SE 0.12), Ce50 3.85 ug/mL (SE 0.11), gamma 1.50 (Table 3). The authors compare Ce50 with Rigouzzo's "measured" 4.03 ug/mL and their Kataria-based 2.94 ug/mL (2.98 here) and Schnider-based 2.64 (2.80 here) (printed p.91).
- The PD parameters swing widely with the PK model used (ke0 0.33-3.30 /min, Ce50 2.78-4.52 ug/mL, Table 4); no PK(predicted)-PD value tracked the classic one, and the lowest objective function (Schnider 74334.83, with the worst PK error, MDPE 41.4%) selected the worst PK model. Headline conclusion: a PD fit using a borrowed PK model does not identify true ke0, Ce50 or gamma.
- No tPeak or t1/2ke0 stated; no MEAC; not an opioid paper.

## Covariates
Classic model was scaled to total body weight (per kg). No significant effect (P < 0.05) of age, height or sex on estimated PK or PD parameters (printed p.88). Literature models in Table 1 carry their own covariates (weight, age, and for Schnider lean body mass and height); not transcribed here.

## Caveats
Venous sampling (the authors suggest the Paedfusor error was worse than in an arterial-sampled study for this reason); TCI dosing gave little PK information, so variances for V2, V3 and Q2 were fixed to 0 and Q3 and V3 simplified; BIS-only PD with E0 and Emax fixed; no opioid or other drug. Sample is one dental population, 28 children, European (Ghent).
