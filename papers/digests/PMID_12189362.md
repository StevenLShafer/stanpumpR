# Lötsch 2002: morphine and M6G population PK in young volunteers

## Study
- Open, single-occasion study; 8 healthy volunteers, 4 men and 4 women, age 23-30 y (mean 26.4), weight 54-98 kg (mean 70.5), height mean 175.1 cm, BSA mean 1.85 m² (Table I).
- IV bolus of 5.64 mg morphine base (7.5 mg morphine sulfate), injected as a short infusion (mean 102 s), together with 1 mg D3-labelled M6G. Fasted.
- Samples at 2, 5, 10, 15, 20, 30, 45, 60, 90, 120, 150, 180 min, then hourly to about 9 h.
- Assay LC-MS-MS; LLOQ morphine 1 ng/mL, M6G/D3-M6G/M3G 0.5 ng/mL; CV < 5%.
- NONMEM V, FO method, log-transformed data, log-normal IIV. Covariates screened: weight, height, age, sex, LBM, BSA, creatinine clearance, liver enzymes, albumin.
- External validation on 8 other young men (mean 80.1 kg) from another centre.
- D3-M6G data of subject 2 excluded (probable dosing error).

## Model and parameters
Model A: standard 3-compartment morphine, 2-compartment M6G, M6G formed as a fraction Fmp of morphine clearance via a first-order "metabolism" compartment (λM). Model B: alternative with morphine metabolic clearance from the first peripheral compartment, renal clearance fixed from literature (2.3 mL/min/kg). Other results: CL corresponds to 18.5 mL/min/kg on average (Model A, p.159); terminal half-life 9 h, effective half-life 2.8 h morphine, 1.2 h M6G (p.159); median M6G formed 12.6% of dose by the AUC method; prediction bias for morphine 14.8% (A) and -2.4% (B), inaccuracy 28.4% (A) and 19.7% (B) (p.157).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- No pharmacodynamic measurement. No ke0, t½ke0, tPeak or hysteresis reported.
- No MEAC or minimum analgesic concentration reported.
- No potency ratio estimated; M6G is described as active and M3G as having little clinical effect (discussion, from literature).

## Covariates
Model A: BSA on Q3M, multiplicative power, exponent 4 (Q3M = 19.5·(BSA/1.83)^4). Model B: BSA on V3M, exponent 2.3. Weight was not retained and was not proven a covariate of renal clearance (Model B). No per-kg parameters.

## Caveats
- n = 8 young healthy volunteers; authors say no indication the parameters hold for older, younger, hepatic or renal patients.
- Single IV dose of 5.64 mg; 9 h sampling is about the terminal half-life.
- Model A Fmp and M6G parameters were up to 25% smaller after excluding subject 2's D3-M6G data.
- Model B depends on a literature renal clearance.

## Verification against stanpumpR
- **Rate constants** (`R/drugs_morphine.R:79-83`): k10 0.070505618, k12 0.127340824, k13 0.018258427, k21 0.025964108, k31 0.001633166 /min. Model A gives 75.3/17.8/60 = 0.0705056, 136/17.8/60 = 0.1273408, 19.5/17.8/60 = 0.0182584, 136/87.3/60 = 0.0259641, 19.5/199/60 = 0.0016332 (derived). **Matches** to all printed digits (Q3 at the median BSA).
- **V1 0.25 L/kg** (`R/drugs_morphine.R:78`): the paper gives V1 = 17.8 L absolute, with no weight term. 17.8/70.5 kg = 0.2525 L/kg (derived), so 0.25 L/kg is a rounded per-kg restatement. At 70 kg the code has V1 17.5 L and every clearance 1.7% below the paper's (CL 74.0 against 75.3 L/h). The header at line 32-33 says so (74.0 L/h). **Matches**, as a documented restatement.
- **Help volumes and clearances** (`inst/help/drugs/morphine.md:3`): 17.5, 86, 196 L and 1.23, 2.23, 0.32 L/min equal the paper's values times 17.5/17.8 (derived). **Matches** the code; the paper's own figures are 17.8, 87.3, 199 L and 1.255, 2.267, 0.325 L/min.
- **"as Lötsch's per-kilogram model did"** (`inst/help/drugs/morphine.md:15`) and "weight-scaled V1" (`inst/help/investigators.md`, morphine row): the published model is not per kilogram. All parameters are absolute, weight was not a covariate, and the one covariate is BSA on Q3 (exponent 4). **Discrepancy** (description of the source).
- **BSA covariate omitted**: the code never applies (BSA/1.83)^4 to Q3; it uses the median-BSA value and the stanpumpR size scaling instead. Not stated in the header or help. **Discrepancy** (undocumented omission; small at typical BSA, a factor of about 2 at BSA 2.2).
- **tPeak 93.8 min** (`R/drugs_morphine.R:8-9`, 85; `inst/help/drugs/morphine.md:19`): the header reads as if Lötsch 2002 supplies it. This paper has no PD and no ke0, so tPeak cannot come from it. **Cannot verify / misattributed**: the tPeak needs its own source.
- **MEAC 8 ng/mL** (`R/drugs_morphine.R:86`): not from this paper (none reported). **Cannot verify** here.
- **"eight young volunteers"** (`R/drugs_morphine.R:8`), "study of morphine and its glucuronide metabolites in volunteers" (`morphine.md:3`): **matches** (8 volunteers, 23-30 y, morphine + D3-M6G).
- **Codeine rescaling with 75.3 L/h** (`R/drugs_codeine.R:57, 201`; test line 134): 75.3 L/h is the Model A CLM. **Matches** the paper. Note that the morphine model actually runs at 74.0 L/h at 70 kg, so the rescale is 1.7% off the clearance the simulated morphine actually has (minor inconsistency inside stanpumpR, not with the paper).
- **Oral F 0.290 from "Lotsch's clearance (74.0 L/h at the 70 kg reference)"** (`R/drugs_morphine.R:32-33`): consistent with the 0.25 L/kg restatement; the paper's own CL is 75.3 L/h. **Matches** as documented.
- **Citation** (`R/drugs_morphine.R:64-65, 91`; `docs/users-guide.md:366`): authors, journal, volume, pages and DOI **match**.
