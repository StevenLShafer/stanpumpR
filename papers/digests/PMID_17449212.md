# Rouse 2007: ceftobiprole, daptomycin, linezolid and vancomycin against methicillin-resistant staphylococci

## Study
In-vitro susceptibility study (Mayo Clinic; funded by Johnson & Johnson). Stored isolates from the Mayo
Endocarditis Registry and Orthopaedic Infectious Diseases Registry (PDF p1-2):
- Infective endocarditis (blood or cardiac tissue, 1985-2005): 37 MRSA, 51 methicillin-resistant
  coagulase-negative staphylococci (MRCNS).
- Bone and joint infection (joint tissue or fluid, 1999-2005): 31 MRSA, 65 MRCNS. Total 184 isolates.
- MIC: CLSI broth microdilution, 0.1 mL cation-adjusted Mueller-Hinton broth (daptomycin with 50 mg/L Ca2+),
  10^5 CFU, 37 C, 18-24 h. MBC: 50 uL subcultured from each negative well and the highest positive well. The
  kill criterion for the MBC is not stated beyond citing NCCLS M26-A. QC strain S. aureus ATCC 29213.
- "Lacked bactericidal activity" / tolerance defined as MBC/MIC >= 32 (p364).
- Broth, protein-free: values are free drug, ug/mL (= mg/L).

Other drugs, MIC90/MBC90 in the same order: ceftobiprole 1/2, 1/1, 1/2, 1/2; daptomycin 1/2, 0.25/1, 1/4,
0.5/2; linezolid 2/128, 2/64, 2/>=128, 2/>=128.

Abstract (pooled, all 184 isolates): vancomycin MIC90 1, MBC90 8; ceftobiprole 1/2, daptomycin 1/4,

(derived: 2/31 = 6.5% of that subgroup; 2/68 = 2.9% of all MRSA); one of them was also daptomycin-nonsusceptible
(MIC 2).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
In-vitro end points only: MIC (growth inhibition) and MBC (subculture of MIC wells; kill threshold not restated).
Not an opioid paper: no MEAC, ke0, tPeak or potency ratio. No serum concentrations or PK/PD targets.

## Covariates
Infection site (endocarditis vs bone/joint) and species (S. aureus vs CoNS), reported as separate columns.

## Caveats
- Selected registry isolates spanning 20 years; small subgroups (31-65), so an MBC90 rests on 3-6 isolates.
- Only methicillin-resistant isolates; no MSSA.
- The MBC kill criterion is not stated in the text; the abstract's pooled values do not agree cleanly with the
  subgroup table (see open questions); one printed MBC90 is below its MIC90.
- Industry-funded (ceftobiprole's sponsor).

## Verification against stanpumpR
- `R/antibioticMbc.R:362-371`: MIC50 1, MIC90 1, MBC50 2, MBC90 8, Basis "measured". The MBC90 8 is taken from
  Honda 2011 and supported by this paper's abstract value 8 for all methicillin-resistant staphylococci, which
  the source string words correctly ("in methicillin-resistant staphylococci"). Units ug/mL = mg/L, broth (free
  drug). For MRSA alone this paper gives MBC90 4 (endocarditis) and 8 (bone/joint) (Table 1), so 8 is the upper
  of its MRSA values, not a pooled MRSA figure: **matches** as cited, with that nuance. The row's MIC90 1 comes
  from Diekema 2019; this paper's MRSA MIC90s are 2 and 1.
- `R/antibioticMbc.R:383-385`: the CoNS row says no usable MBC50 or MBC90 was found and that Rouse 2007 pools
  CoNS with MRSA. Only the abstract pools them; Table 1 reports MRCNS separately: vancomycin MBC90 4 (range
  0.5-8) in 51 endocarditis isolates, and 1 (range 1-16, as printed, likely misprinted) in 65 bone/joint
  isolates: **discrepancy**. The row stays "excluded", so no band edge changes; whether to use the
  endocarditis MRCNS MBC90 of 4 is a modelling choice.
- The row's statement that CoNS MICs run about one dilution above S. aureus is only partly borne out here:
  MRCNS MIC90 2 in both groups vs MRSA 2 (endocarditis) and 1 (bone/joint).
- Context line numbers (351, 367) have drifted; the citation is at line 369.
