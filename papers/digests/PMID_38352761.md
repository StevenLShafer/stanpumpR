# Zhang 2024: sertraline population PK in Chinese psychiatric inpatients

## Study
- Retrospective TDM study, Affiliated Brain Hospital of Guangzhou Medical University, 2018-2022 (p2).
- 140 hospitalised patients with psychiatric disorders, 70 M / 70 F; 298 trough serum concentrations (Table 1, §3.1).
- Age median 22 (range 11-79), mean 27.46 (SD 16.43); <18 y n = 52, 18-65 n = 75, >65 n = 13. Weight median 60 kg (40-110);
  height 166 cm (145-185); BMI 36 (25-62) as printed in Table 1 (the BMI row is garbled in the text layer). 54 patients aged
  13-18 per abstract (Table 1 says 52 under 18).
- Comedications: lamotrigine 99 (33.22%), quetiapine 12 (4.03%), venlafaxine 4 (1.34%) of samples (Table 1).
- Doses not tabulated; regimens mainly QD and BID (§3.5). Inclusion: non-zero concentrations at two or more dosages.
- Assay: LC-MS/MS (Shimadzu), serum, sertraline-d3 internal standard, linear range 5-500 ng/mL (§2.2).
- Software: NONMEM 7, FOCE-I, Pirana 2.9.0, PsN 3.4.2 (bootstrap n = 1000), NPDE in R 4.2.2 (§2.3).

## Model and parameters
One-compartment, first-order absorption and elimination. Because all samples were troughs the absorption phase could not be
estimated and ka was fixed at 0.098 from Li 2013 (DIADS-2, ref 20) (§2.3). Proportional residual error (additive fixed to 0).
Final model (§3.2, Table 2): CL/F = 76.1 x [1 - 0.0068 x (AGE - 22)]; V/F = 803; Ka = 0.098.

**Units are not printed anywhere in the paper** for CL/F, V/F or ka (equation and Table 2 give bare numbers). L/h, L and 1/h are the
natural reading; the discussion's "CL/V" of 0.095 (unit not given) equals 76.1/803 (derived: 0.0948).

Derived: t1/2 = ln2 x 803 / 76.1 = 7.3 h at age 22 (derived); CL/F falls to 76.1 x 0.62 = 47.3 at age 78 (derived).
Covariate addition dropped OFV by 7.081 (P < 0.01). Base model OFV 2356.936.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD model, no ke0. Uses the AGNP therapeutic reference range 10-150 ng/mL (laboratory alert 300 ng/mL) for simulations (§2.5,
§3.4). Reports mean adult concentration 67 ng/mL from the TDM literature (ref 24). Not an opioid.

## Covariates
Age on CL/F only (linear, centred at 22 y). Sex, height, weight, BMI and comedication not significant (P < 0.01 criterion).
CYP2C19 tested in a subset (n = 38) without effect, not shown (Discussion).

## Caveats
- Retrospective trough-only TDM data: ka fixed from another study; V/F poorly estimated (RSE 26%, IIV 57%, bootstrap CI
  372-1369). The implied 7.3 h half-life is far shorter than sertraline's usual ~24-26 h (which the paper itself quotes in the
  introduction) and the authors note their CL/V (0.095) differs from two prior PPK studies (0.373 and 0.029).
- Units missing throughout. Time after dose for troughs not reported. Population about 1/3 adolescents.
- Table 1 text layer is scrambled; values above are best reconstruction.
