# Ben-Menachem 1995: gabapentin CSF/plasma at steady state

## Study
- Two-centre, double-blind, placebo-controlled parallel-group add-on trial; 36 patients (17 F, 19 M; mean age 37,
  16-66 y), intractable complex partial seizures on 1-2 AEDs (CBZ, PHT, PB, CZP, PRM).
- 3-month baseline, then placebo (n=12), gabapentin 900 mg/day (n=16) or 1200 mg/day (n=8) for 3 months.
- Lumbar puncture (fasting, lateral decubitus, 14th-17th mL analysed) at baseline and on the last treatment day.
  Gabapentin in plasma and CSF by liquid chromatography (Hengy & Kölle). CSF GABA, HVA, 5-HIAA, 18 other amino acids.
- CSF gabapentin evaluable in 19 of 24 actively treated patients.

## Model and parameters

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- CSF/plasma ratio rose with time after the last dose: 5.9% at 3 h, 21% at 6 h, 21-34.5% at 9.5-13 h; authors say
  the CSF peak follows the plasma peak. No linear relation between plasma and CSF levels.
- No relation of plasma or CSF gabapentin to seizure frequency; no change in CSF GABA, HVA, 5-HIAA or amino acids.
- No ke0, tPeak, EC50. Not an opioid: no MEAC or potency ratio.

## Covariates
None analysed.

## Caveats
Small numbers; single CSF sample per patient at varying times after dose; internal inconsistency in the lower
bound (abstract 0.056, results 7.3% overall vs 5.9% at 3 h); trial too small for efficacy (authors); industry-funded
(Parke-Davis).
