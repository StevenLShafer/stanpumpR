# Zaouter 2016: McSleepy closed-loop TIVA in cardiac surgery (pilot)

## Study
Single-centre prospective observational pilot (Bordeaux), 20 adults for elective on-pump cardiac surgery; 4 excluded after controller-pump communication errors, 16 analysed (success 80%, 97.5% CI 53-95%). Age 72 (7) yr, LVEF 58 (9)%, anaesthesia time 249 (66) min, CPB time 92 (42) min (Table 2). Premedication midazolam 0.1 mg/kg oral. Morphine 0.2 mg/kg, ketoprofen, acetaminophen and nefopam given 15 min before end of surgery.
Controller: PID with self-adaptive algorithm; propofol on BIS target 45; remifentanil on NociMap (the Analgoscore rescaled to -100..+100 from HR/MAP offsets, Table 1) via correction factor 0 to 1.9 x K1 x K2; rocuronium bolus when TOF ratio >25 and >=5 min since the last.
Induction: remifentanil 0.5 ug/kg/min for 2 min, 0.2 for the third minute, 0.1 thereafter; after 3 min propofol 1 mg/kg over 90 s, then 0.5 mg/kg boluses (up to 3 more) with 60 s (LVEF >=40%) or 90 s (LVEF <40%) waits; rocuronium 0.9 mg/kg when BIS <60. Minimum remifentanil 0.05 ug/kg/min in "other" (vagal/hypovolaemic) states and during CPB.

## Model and parameters
No PK model. Results (text, printed p.889): propofol mean infusion 94 (25) ug/kg/min with 106 (33) changes/h; remifentanil mean 0.12 (0.06) ug/kg/min with 28 (9) changes/h; 3 patients extubated on the table at 17 (1) min. BIS MDPE -9.9 (3.3)% with CPB, MDAPE 14.4 (3.2)%.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Opioid audit (remifentanil): no MEAC / analgesic concentration, no ke0 / t1/2ke0 / tPeak, no potency or equianalgesic ratio, no concentrations. The analgesia end point is the NociMap haemodynamic score (excellent -33..+33, good 34-66, insufficient 67-100); excellent+good 70% of time, insufficient 3%, "other" 27%. BIS target 45 for propofol; no EC50 or ke0.

## Covariates
Inputs: weight, height, age, sex, ASA, beta-blocker use, atrial fibrillation, LVEF <40%. No covariate equations.

## Caveats
n = 20, no control group, mild hypothermia only; older population (72 yr) with poorer BIS control than the non-cardiac trial (PMID_23427212); 4 patients dropped for technical failure; Hemmerling is a co-author.
