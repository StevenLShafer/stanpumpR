# de Keijzer 2026: norepinephrine dose versus MAP, awake and under propofol/remifentanil

## Study
- VASOCONTROL, the same trial as Li 2024 (PMID 39465453; NL9312, UMC Groningen, February-June 2022). 36 healthy
  volunteers, 18-70 y, 1:1 sex, 12 per age band (PDF p2). Table 1 (PDF p4): age 42 [18 to 70]; weight 71 (11) kg;
  height 174 (10) cm; BMI 23.2 [22.3 to 24.4]; screening MAP 91 (11) mmHg. One volunteer replaced (37 started).
- Norepinephrine tartrate (Centrafarm) 20 ug/mL by peripheral vein at 0.04, 0.08, 0.12, 0.16, 0.20 ug/kg/min, 15 min
  each, awake; after washout, repeated under propofol (Eleveld, age-related EC50) and remifentanil (Eleveld, Ce
  4.0 ng/mL), with a 30 s 50 mA 100 Hz tetanic stimulus at the end of each step (PDF p2-3). The dose basis is not
  stated.
- Radial arterial pressure; FloTrac/HemoSphere for CI and SVI; arm stop-flow Parm. Values are 30 s medians starting
  60 s before each blood draw (PDF p3-4). BIS under anaesthesia 39 [32 to 47].
- Linear mixed models with random intercepts and slopes; dose as predictor beat plasma concentration (AIC 2297 vs
  2405); polynomials were worse; age and sex did not improve the fit (PDF p4).

## Model and parameters
MAP = intercept + slope x infusion rate (ug/kg/min), per phase, with a phase interaction (PDF p1, p4-5, rendered):
```yaml

  - {name: slope awake, value: 103, unit: mmHg per ug/kg/min, source: "Abstract; Results PDF p4", note: "95% CI 81-124"}
  - {name: slope increase under anaesthesia, value: 119, unit: mmHg per ug/kg/min, source: "Abstract; Results PDF p4", note: "95% CI 119-120"}
  - {name: slope under anaesthesia, value: 222, unit: mmHg per ug/kg/min, source: "Abstract; Results PDF p5", note: "approximately, cumulative (103 + 119)"}
  - {name: awake intercept, value: 88, unit: mmHg, source: "Abstract; Results PDF p4", note: "95% CI 85-91"}
  - {name: MAP fall at induction, value: -34, unit: mmHg, source: "Abstract; Results PDF p4", note: "95% CI -37 to -31"}
  - {name: dose requirement under anaesthesia, value: "(target MAP - 54)/222", unit: ug/kg/min, source: "Abstract; Results PDF p5", note: "65 mmHg -> 0.05 (0.02-0.08)"}
```
Per 0.01 ug/kg/min: +1.0 mmHg (0.8-1.2) awake and an additional 1.2 mmHg (1.2-1.2) under anaesthesia (PDF p5). 88 - 34
= 54, the intercept of the requirement formula (derived).

Table 2 (PDF p7, rendered), medians: plasma NE (nmol/L) awake 1.5 [1.3 to 2.2] at 0, then 8.3, 17.7, 25.8, 31.9, 41.4;
under anaesthesia 11.2, 22.3, 31.3, 41.5, 51.9 at 0.04-0.20. MAP awake 87, 89, 93, 95, 98, 101; under anaesthesia 61,
77, 86, 93, 95 mmHg. Plasma concentrations were higher under anaesthesia at every rate (P < 0.001).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: MAP (radial arterial line) at the end of each 15-min step, i.e. near steady state; no time course, no ke0,
no EC50. Secondary: HR, CI and SVI (pulse wave), dP/dtmax, Parm; steeper dose-response under anaesthesia for
dP/dtmax, SVI and CI. Not an opioid.

## Covariates
None retained (age and sex tested).

## Caveats
- Linear only within the pressures observed; healthy volunteers; positive-pressure ventilation under anaesthesia
  only. Pulse-contour CI and SVI.
- Remifentanil target printed as 4.0 ng/mL here and 3.6 ng/mL in Li 2024 for the same trial.
- The 95% CI of 119 to 120 for the slope increase is much narrower than that of the awake slope; as printed.
- Table 1 prints smoking as "6 +/- 20" (and 2 +/- 16, 1 +/- 8, 3 +/- 25) although its footnote says n (%); as printed.
