# Willmann 2018: integrated population PK of rivaroxaban across indications

## Study
- Pooled sparse PK from 7 Bayer/Janssen trials (Table 1, PDF p.3 = printed p.311): VTE prevention after hip or
  knee replacement (ODIXa-Hip2, ODIXa-OD-Hip, ODIXa-Knee; phase II), VTE treatment (ODIXa-DVT, EINSTEIN DVT
  dose-ranging; phase II), ACS (ACS TIMI-46; phase II) and AF (ROCKET AF; phase III). 5041 enrolled, **4918
  patients with PK**, **22,843 observations**. ROCKET AF contributed only **161 patients / 800 observations**;
  ACS TIMI-46 2251 / 9376.
- Doses: 2.5 mg o.d. to 30 mg b.i.d. (text, p.6); Table 1 also lists 40 mg o.d. arms; Figure 1a shows patient
  counts per dose 2.5 mg 382, 5 mg 1022, 7.5 mg 175, 10 mg 1304, 15 mg 203, 20 mg 1039, 30 mg 530, 40 mg 386.
- Population, all studies (Table 1, mean (SD)): age 60.53 (11.82) y, weight 82.48 (16.87) kg, CrCL 97.74 (33.97)
  mL/min, Tietz-truncated CrCL 97.17 (32.34) mL/min, lean body mass 57.05 (10.00) kg, SCr 0.93 (0.25) mg/dL, BSA
  1.93 (0.22) m2, female 39.3% (22.2-62.7% by study). In ROCKET AF Table 1 prints CrCL and truncated CrCL as the
  same 81.76 (32.06).
- The immediate postsurgical phase of the VTE-prevention studies was excluded (patients split into slow and
  fast absorbers on the first day after surgery) (p.2, printed 310).
- Assay LC-MS/MS, LLOQ 0.5 ug/L; BLQ data excluded. NONMEM 7.3, FOCE with interaction; PsN 4.2.0.
- The paper does not state whether the trial doses were taken with or without food.

## Model and parameters
One-compartment, first-order absorption, CL/F, V/F, ka; proportional residual error on untransformed data. Final
model is run 12 of Table 2 (PDF p.4). Continuous covariates as P = P_TV (X_i / X)^theta, "X is the population mean
or median" (Methods, p.4); categorical covariates written in Methods as P_TV(1 + theta I), but Table 3 lists
the categorical values as multiplicative factors ("Proportion"; the text calls 0.85 for AF a factor, and Figure 1b
plots AF at about 0.85 fold).

Reference category for the study factors is VTE treatment (= 1). Footnote c: relative bioavailability fixed at 1.0
for a 10 mg dose. The dose-F equation itself is not printed in the article (it is Eq. 1 of the Supplementary
Materials).

**Supplement, Equation 1 (sup0004, seven OMML equations; supplement, 2026-10-27):** k10 = (CL/F)/(V/F);
F = Fmin + (Fmax - Fmin) exp(-(ln2/D50) DOSE); ka = ka,TV exp(eta);
CL/F = CL_TV/F (CrCL/93)^theta_CrCL (WT/81)^theta_WT COMED STUDY exp(eta);
V/F = V_TV/F (WT/81)^theta_WT (AGE/61)^theta_AGE theta_SEX^SEX exp(eta). COMED is written as a piecewise choice
(1, or one of the P-gp / strong / medium / weak CYP3A4 inhibitor / CYP3A4 inducer thetas); STUDY is 1 for DVT
(studies 11223, 11528), theta_AF (3001), theta_ACS (2001), theta_VTE<=72h for "VTE (Studies 10933, 10945 and
11527)", and theta_VTE>72h "after first dose". The equation prints CrCL with **no truncation**; the cap is only in
the control stream (below) and the article's Methods. "10933" is printed where the control stream and Figure S2
caption give 10944 (ODIXa-Hip2).

**Supplement, NONMEM control stream (sup0009.txt; supplement, 2026-10-27):** header "Based on: 11", output
FILE=run12.tab, so it is the final run 12 of Table 2. ADVAN2 TRANS2, NONMEM 7.3, METHOD=1 INTER. In $PK:
- `BSA2=BSA; IF (BSA.LT.0) BSA2=1.92` (missing BSA imputed as 1.92 m2); `CrCL_MAX=140*BSA2/1.73`;
  `IF (CrCL.GT.CrCL_MAX) CrCL_CORR=CrCL_MAX`. Upper cap only; no lower bound. CrCL and BSA are data columns, so
  neither the CrCL equation nor the weight in it, nor the BSA formula, is stated. Columns CrCL_BSA and CrCL_WT are
  read but not used.
- `TVCL = THETA(2)*((CrCL_CORR/93)**THETA(4))*((WT/81)**THETA(13))*COMM*INDEFF`: the truncated CrCL is centred at
  93, weight at 81.
- `TVV = THETA(3)*(WT/81)**THETA(5)*((AGE/61)**THETA(6))*THETA(7)**(SEX-1)` (SEX coded 1/2 here, 0/1 in Eq. 1).
  No floor or cap on age or weight.
- INDEFF = 1 by default; THETA(14) if STU 3001 (AF), THETA(15) if 2001 (ACS); for STU 10944, 10945, 11527,
  THETA(16) if TIME <= 72 and THETA(17) if TIME > 72 (TIME is the data's time, h; Eq. 1 calls it time after first
  dose).
- COMM is the **product** of the five comedication factors, each applied when its flag is 1 (Eq. 1's piecewise
  form does not show that several can combine).
- `F1 = (F1MIN +(F1MAX -F1MIN)*EXP(-LOG(2)/D50*DOSE))*1000` (comment: mg to mcg). DOSE is a data column, distinct
  from AMT; whether it is the dose per administration or per day is not stated.
- THETA order: 1 KA, 2 CL, 3 V, 4 CrCL_CL, 5 WT_V, 6 AGE_V, 7 SEX_V, 8 P-gp, 9 strong CYP3A4i, 10 moderate,
  11 weak, 12 inducer, 13 WT_CL, 14 AF, 15 ACS, 16 VTE<=3 d, 17 VTE>3 d, 18 F1MAX (1.25 FIXED), 19 F1MIN (bounds
  0-1), 20 D50 (bounds 0-100 mg); AF/ACS/VTE bounded 0-5. The $THETA values in the file are **initial estimates**,
  not the final ones (e.g. TCL 5.18939 L/h, F1MIN 0.466504, D50 14.6984, CrCL power 0.3, WT_CL 0.1, AF 0.854984);
  the final estimates are Table 3's. $OMEGA (0.631765; block 0.168254, 0.0678267, 0.0391407) and $SIGMA 0.203389
  are close to Table 3's final values.
- Residual error proportional (W = IPRED, floor 0.0001).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None: PK only. No anti-Xa, PT or outcome model. Not an opioid; no MEAC, ke0 or potency ratio.

## Covariates
- CL/F: Tietz-truncated CrCL (Cockcroft-Gault capped at 140 mL/min x BSA/1.73 m2; BSA formula not named; which
  weight enters Cockcroft-Gault not stated), weight (negative power, said to correct the weight already inside
  Cockcroft-Gault), comedications, study population. Age on CL/F was tested and removed (run 10, dOFV +1.3).
- V/F: weight, age, sex.
- F: dose.
- (supplement, 2026-10-27) Control stream: truncated CrCL = min(CrCL, 140 x BSA/1.73), BSA imputed 1.92 m2 when
  missing; centring 93 mL/min, 81 kg, 61 y; female V factor theta^(SEX-1); comedication factors multiply when
  several apply; VTE-prevention factor switches at TIME 72 h. No age or weight bounds.
- Exposure simulations (AF, steady state): severe vs normal renal function median AUC0-24 +53%, Ctrough 2.1-fold,
  Cmax +35%; age groups changed medians by 15% / 10% / 23% (AUC / Cmax / Ctrough), BMI groups by 7.5% / 7.6% /
  44% (p.7, printed 315).

## Caveats
- Sparse sampling; one-compartment where healthy-volunteer data needed two; pcVPC shows slight Cmax
  under-prediction (authors). ka shrinkage 32.8%.
- Strong CYP3A4 inhibitors were in 6 of 5041 patients; the model's 1.02-fold AUC with them contrasts with the
  2.6-fold ketoconazole DDI result (Discussion, p.8).
- The AF factor rests on 161 ROCKET AF patients (800 samples).
- F is relative to 10 mg and was fitted over 2.5-40 mg; the form falls only to Fmin 0.59, so it predicts exposure
  still rising with dose above 40 mg (my inference; no data there).
- Food status of the trial doses is not reported; the supplements do not report it either (supplement,
  2026-10-27).
- The supplement's Eq. 1 omits the Tietz cap that the control stream applies, and lists study 10933 where the
  control stream has 10944 (supplement, 2026-10-27).
- The control stream's $THETA values are initial estimates, not Table 3's finals; quote Table 3, not the stream,
  for parameter values.
