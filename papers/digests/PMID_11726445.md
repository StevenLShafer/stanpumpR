# Higuchi 2001: early pregnancy and the propofol C50 for loss of consciousness

## Study
- Prospective, randomized target concentrations; 72 ASA I women aged 19-39 y, BMI ≤ 30, no medications
  (including oral contraceptives): 36 pregnant at 6-12 wk gestation for suction termination, 36 nonpregnant
  for elective orthopaedic or ENT surgery (p 1566, PDF p 2).
- Analysed: 34 nonpregnant, 33 pregnant (5 excluded because their two samples differed by more than 35%) (p 1567).
- Demographics (Table 3, PDF p 3, median (range)): age 26 (19-39) vs 30 (19-38) y; height 158 (151-168) vs
  158 (150-170) cm; weight 50 (39-65) vs 50 (42-68) kg; gestation 8 (6-12) wk. Japanese women.
- Lidocaine 0.5 mg/kg IV, then effect-site-targeted TCI (RUGLOOP, Graseby 3500) with the Marsh parameters and
  ke0 0.25/min (Table 1). Random targets 1.5-4.5 ug/mL in 0.5 steps (Table 2: 3, 7, 8, 8, 6, 3, 2 patients).
- Target held 10 min; venous (forearm, opposite arm) serum samples at 3 and 8 min after equilibration; verbal
  command plus shoulder rub, twice; no eye opening = loss of consciousness. Observer blinded.
- Only the post-assessment sample of pairs within 35% was used. Logistic regression (STATISTICA) of
  P(no response) = C^γ/(C50^γ + C^γ).
- Assay: HPLC with fluorescence (ex 276, em 310 nm), thymol internal standard, linear 50 ng/mL-10 ug/mL,
  detection limit 10 ng/mL; CV 4.6% and 2.1% at 1 and 10 ug/mL, 2.2% between days (pp 1566-1567).
- Power: designed for 31/group to detect 0.5 ug/mL (SD 0.7); the authors state post hoc power was only 6%
  and ~1600 more per group would be needed (p 1568).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: no eye opening to verbal command with shoulder rub (loss of consciousness) after 10 min at a
  steady TCI target.
- C50 for LOC on **measured venous serum** concentration: 2.1 ± 0.2 (95% CI 1.8-2.4) ug/mL nonpregnant vs
  2.0 ± 0.2 (1.6-2.4) ug/mL pregnant (Table 3; mean ± SE); no significant difference. γ not reported.
- ke0 0.25/min was an input to the TCI, not a result; no tPeak or hysteresis estimated.
- TCI performance (Marsh, venous serum): MDPE −31.5% / MDAPE 34.7% nonpregnant, −32.3% / 33.2% pregnant (p 1567),
  i.e. measured concentrations ran about a third below target.
- The authors attribute their C50 being lower than earlier reports (2.2-4.4 ug/mL; Vuyk 1992 3.4 ug/mL in
  women) to venous serum versus arterial whole blood or plasma sampling (p 1568).

## Covariates
Pregnancy (first trimester) only; no effect on C50. Age, weight, height did not differ between groups.

## Caveats
- First trimester only (median 8 wk); says nothing about term.
- Underpowered (6% power stated by the authors); a difference of the size seen with volatile agents could be missed.
- Venous serum, single sample timed up to ~2 min before the assessment; lidocaine pre-dose.
- "No difference" is pregnant vs these nonpregnant women in the same study (same assay, same TCI).
