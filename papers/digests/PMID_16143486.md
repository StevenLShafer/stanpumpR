# Papini 2006: lorazepam kinetics in term parturients (single oral 2 mg)

## Study
- 10 healthy parturients, 18-37 y (mean 23.3), gestational age 36-40.1 weeks (mean 38.41), weight before delivery
  mean 76.8 kg (95% CI 67.4-86.2), height mean 161.7 cm (Table 1, p3 / printed 399); normal hepatic and renal
  function; co-medication in labour included bupivacaine, fentanyl or sufentanil, lidocaine, oxytocin, diclofenac,
  dipyrone, cefazolin, nifedipine (Table 1).
- Single 2 mg racemic lorazepam tablet orally 2-9.2 h before delivery (8 vaginal, 2 caesarean). Maternal venous
  blood at 0, 0.5, 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12, 30, 36, 48 h; urine in 12 h intervals to 48 h; umbilical vein at clamping
  (8 women) (p3).
- Assay: LC-MS/MS on a Chiralcel OD-R column; lorazepam racemises in vitro, so the isomers are reported summed
  (isomeric mixture); LOQ 0.2 ng/mL per enantiomer, linear 0.4-40 ng/mL; glucuronide by β-glucuronidase difference.
  Total (bound + free) concentrations.
- Analysis: "two-compartment open model" fit plus trapezoidal AUC (WinNonlin 4.0); Cl/F = dose/AUC, Vd/F = Cl/β.

Literature comparators quoted by the authors (Discussion, p5-6): healthy volunteers' oral CL/F 0.84-1.80
mL/min/kg, V/F 93-116 L, t½ about 16 h; urinary unchanged < 0.5%. The authors conclude pregnancy raises V/F and
CL/F and shortens t½, and that fetal concentrations are 50-100% of maternal.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None (no sedation or neonatal outcomes measured).

## Covariates
None analysed; a single term-pregnancy group.

## Caveats
- No non-pregnant control: the pregnancy effect rests on historical literature values.
- n = 10, single dose, in labour with many co-medications; oral bioavailability in labour not measured, so CL/F and
  V/F may partly reflect F (the long absorption half-life, 3.2 h against literature values under 30 min that the
  introduction quotes, suggests slowed absorption in labour; my observation, not the authors').
- Total concentrations only; enantiomers not separable (in vitro racemisation).
- Two-compartment fit stated but only one disposition rate (β) is reported.
