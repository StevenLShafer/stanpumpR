# Minami 2007: irinotecan, UGT1A polymorphisms and SN-38 glucuronidation in Japanese

PDF pages = printed pages minus 496 (PDF p.1 = printed 497).

## Study
177 Japanese cancer patients (mean age 60.5, range 26-78; 135 male/42 female; Table 1) at two National Cancer Center hospitals; irinotecan as single agent (n about 55 for toxicity analysis) or in combination (mostly platinum). Dose: 90-min IV infusion, 100 mg/m2 weekly or 150 mg/m2 biweekly (reduced in combinations). Plasma irinotecan, SN-38 and SN-38G by HPLC at pre-dose, 0 and 20 min, 1, 2, 4, 8, 24 h after the end of the first infusion; AUC by trapezoidal rule (WinNonlin 4.01). AUC ratio SN-38G/SN-38 evaluated in 176 patients. No compartmental model is fitted. Genotypes: UGT1A1 (*6 211G>A G71R; *28 TA insertion; *60; *27), 1A7, 1A9, 1A10 and Block C haplotypes and diplotypes.

Dose-AUC slope of SN-38 vs irinotecan dose (mg/m2; Fig 3c, AUC in h*umol/L) is 1.4-fold (one *6/*28 haplotype) and 2.4-fold (two) that of patients without (p. 502); SN-38/irinotecan AUC ratio also rises with the number of *6/*28 haplotypes (Fig 3b, graph only). Allele frequencies in this cohort (Fig 1): *6 0.167, *28 about 0.138, *60 0.147.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Toxicity, not a concentration-effect model; no ke0 or MEAC. Single-agent patients (Table 5, PDF p.7): grade 3-4 neutropenia 3/21 (14.3%), 7/29 (24.1%), 4/5 (80.0%) for 0, 1, 2 haplotypes with *6 or *28 (trend p 0.0117; Fisher +/+ vs rest p 0.0124); grade 3 diarrhoea 3 (14.3%), 2 (6.90%), 1 (20.0%), not significant. With cisplatin (n=62): neutropenia 20/35 (57.1%), 14/20 (70.0%), 7/7 (100%) (trend p 0.0315). Grade 3+ neutropenia 100% (3/3) in *28/*6 (D1/B2) vs 11.8% in A1/A1 (p 0.0088). Table 6 (n=53, R2 0.3942): nadir absolute neutrophil count falls by 369.1 per *6/*28 (units not printed on the page), p 0.0146. Table 4 (n=176, R2 0.410): log AUC ratio, coefficient -0.189 for '*6 or *28', p < 0.0001. The authors suggest halving the dose in *6/*28 homozygotes but say prospective confirmation is needed.

## Covariates
Genotype is the covariate. Multiple regression of log AUC ratio also found age, albumin, GOT/ALP and creatinine significant (Table 4).

## Caveats
Observational, mixed regimens and tumour types, doses in mg/m2, sparse single-agent subgroups (no *6/*6 among monotherapy patients). Several diplotype groups n <= 9. No concentration-time parameters (CL, V) are given, so nothing here can replace a PK model.

## Verification against stanpumpR
`R/drugs_irinotecan.R` is a Klein 2002 three-compartment model that forms SN-38; its header (line 59) says UGT1A1*28 was not studied and no covariate is applied. This paper supplies the genotype effect the model lacks but gives no CL/V that could be compared. Direction: SN-38G/SN-38 AUC ratio falls with *6/*28 (median ratio 6.13 -> about 2), i.e. less glucuronidation of SN-38 and greater SN-38 exposure (Fig 3c). The effect is on the Japanese population studied, not on Klein's cohort. Nothing in the repo cites it, so there is nothing to verify. Verdict: not-applicable.
