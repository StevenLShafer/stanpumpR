# Bejjani 2009: memory processing under propofol-remifentanil TCI in cardiac surgery

## Study
Double-blind randomized trial, single university hospital (Erasme, Brussels), elective cardiac surgery with
cardiopulmonary bypass (CPB), enrolment 1 Sep 2003 to 15 Apr 2004. 46 enrolled, 38 analysed (1 death, 7 prolonged
ICU stay). Two groups of 19 heard a looped CD of 20 French words (list A or list B, each word repeated 3 times
in random order, 1 word/s) from induction to end of surgery through headphones. Tested the day after surgery
with a word-stem completion test (implicit plus explicit) and a free recall test (explicit); each group's
results on the list it did not hear served as the control (Tables 2 and 4, PDF p. 2-3).
Premedication: alprazolam 0.5 mg orally 30 min before arrival. Anaesthesia: propofol and remifentanil
effect-site TCI run by Infusion Toolbox with the Schnider (propofol) and Minto (remifentanil) models; propofol
Ce raised stepwise from 0.5 ug/mL until loss of consciousness (LOC; loss of eyelash reflex and of response to
command); cisatracurium; BIS (A-2000 v3.21) displayed to the anaesthesiologist, no BIS target set; targets at
the anaesthesiologist's discretion, maintained through CPB (mild hypothermia, always above 34 C).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: memory (word-stem completion, free recall), not a drug effect curve. Results (Table 4, PDF p. 3):
word-stem completion correct on the heard vs unheard list, list A 3.42% vs 3.15% (p = 0.9534, 95% CI of
difference -1.79 to +2.33), list B 13.15% vs 14.73% (p = 0.4221, CI -6.21 to +3.05); no free recall of any word,
no explicit recall, no dreaming. Post hoc power 5% (list A) and 8% (list B): underpowered (p. 178).
Opioid audit (remifentanil): no MEAC or analgesic concentration, no ke0, t1/2ke0 or tPeak, no potency ratio
is stated. The only remifentanil numbers are the observed lowest Ce, 2.8 +/- 1.5 and 3.2 +/- 1.8 ng/mL
(Table 3, effect-site by the Minto model, so a model prediction, not an assay). LOC propofol Ce 1.5 ug/mL with
remifentanil running, after alprazolam premedication (not a propofol-alone EC50).

## Covariates
None analysed. Groups matched on age, weight, height, sex (Table 3).

## Caveats
Small sample, no power calculation, negative result; authors say a larger sample might show a difference (p. 180).
BIS was not blinded, so propofol was titrated to BIS and BIS monitoring itself may explain the result; alprazolam
premedication; very long listening time (217 min, many repetitions); eight of 46 enrolled patients dropped out.
Effect-site concentrations are TCI-model predictions; no blood concentrations were measured.
