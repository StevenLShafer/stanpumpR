# Zhang 2005: forcing-function vs simultaneous fitting of multi-response PK data

## Study
Simulation study (1000 simulated studies per condition) plus one real data set, comparing two ways of fitting a four-module physiological flow model (venous V, arterial A, non-eliminating tissue N, eliminating tissue E; IV bolus into V). SIM fits all responses simultaneously; FFA (forcing function approach) fits each module separately, replacing its input by linear interpolation of the observed donor-module concentrations, then solves algebraically for the 10 model parameters (Table I, p. 288 PDF-printed; paper pages 283-305 map to PDF pages 1-23). Fitting by NONMEM, maximum likelihood. Partition coefficients set to 1; parameters and designs sampled by Latin hypercube from the ranges in Table II (p. 290). Error model: proportional plus additive normal.

Real data: thiopental 20 mg/kg infused over 75 min to 73 male Wistar rats (mean 345 g), arterial samples to 420 min, tissues of animals killed at 14 times (Ebling data, ref 18); data averaged across animals, rapidly perfused tissues represented by liver, plus muscle and fat (p. 296-297).

## Model and parameters
No drug parameters are estimated for use. Structural model is generic: mass balance dCi/dt = (1/Vi)[sum Qji Cj/Pj - (sum Qji + CLi) Ci/Pi] (Eq. 1, p. 287). Simulation ranges (Table II, text layer scrambled; not relied on): dose 10, 5-15 observations, sampling 0-4 time units, total blood flow fixed at 5.

## Caveats
Key findings (p. 283-284, 293-301): with the model correctly specified, FFA parameter errors are about 2 times and prediction errors (IPE) about 10 times those of SIM (SIM 1-2%, FFA 10% or more); with misspecification, FFA IPE is 2-4 times SIM. SIM failed to reject the wrong N model about twice as often as FFA (Table III, normalised 1.75 vs 1.00 in case 1; 8.15 vs 1.25 in case 2). Recommendation: use FFA for model building, SIM for the final fit; a ratio of RS1 to RS2 residuals (RMSR) above 2.0 flags untrustworthy FFA estimates (p. 301). Single generic individual simulated; feedback only through recirculation. Page references use the printed journal pages (283-305); the text layer's pages correspond to the same order.
