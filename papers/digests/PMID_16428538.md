# Wietasch 2006: propofol TCI performance with remifentanil, and a new propofol PK set

## Study
Prospective clinical study, 54 adults (ASA 1-3, 6/36/12 split of ASA 1/2/3 per Table 3), elective general surgery longer than 1 h, total IV anaesthesia. Propofol TCI (Marsh set, initial plasma target 5 ug/mL, titrated to BIS 50 +/- 10) plus remifentanil TCI (Minto set, initial target 5 ng/mL, titrated to heart rate and arterial pressure). Propofol was randomised between LCT (Diprivan 1%) and MCT/LCT (Propofol-Lipuro 1%), 27 each. Table 1 (PDF p4): age 56.6 +/- 12.8 (LCT) and 60.1 +/- 14.3 (MCT/LCT) years, height about 172 cm, weight 75.9 +/- 12.5 and 76.3 +/- 12.4 kg; 36 men and 18 women overall (Table 3). Arterial blood, 621 of 624 propofol samples analysed (HPLC, Plummer method, LOQ 0.05 ug/mL, plasma, total propofol), at baseline, loss of consciousness, intubation, 15/30/60 min, skin incision and then up to 300 min, plus end of infusion and extubation. Mean infusion 230 +/- 86 min; mean doses 1951 +/- 645 mg propofol and 3824 +/- 2543 ug remifentanil. NONMEM V population PK (proportional error, exponential IIV) on dosing histories reduced by a virtual-mean-infusion algorithm (threshold 6.4 mg/min); non-compartmental analysis as well. Post hoc split into Group 1 (patients 1-27, used to fit a new set) and Group 2 (28-54, used to test it).

## Model and parameters
Mammillary 3-compartment model; elimination from the central compartment. Table 2 (PDF p5, printed 434), whole-group 3-compartment fit, Vc and CL are the headline values. V3 was fixed at 163 L because a free fit was numerically unstable: V3 = Vss (non-compartmental) minus V1 minus V2 (text, PDF p4). The unfixed fit was a 2-compartment model (first column).

For comparison, Table 2 lists Marsh (n=18): Vc 17.1, CL 2.04, V2 34.8, Q2 1.92, V3 217, Q3 0.72; Schnider (n=24, calculated for a 76 kg patient): Vc 4.27, CL 1.89, V2 18.9, Q2 1.29, V3 238, Q3 0.84 (units L and L/min).
Performance of the TCI (Table text, PDF p4): measured mean propofol 5.8 +/- 1.3 ug/mL against 3.3 +/- 0.4 predicted. Marsh set MdPE 58.6%, MdAPE 60.7%; Schnider set recalculated MdPE 47.9%, MdAPE 50.3%. Group 2 (PDF p5): new Group 1 set MdPE 2.4%, MdAPE 21.2%; Marsh 57.3% and 58.5%; Schnider 49.5% and 50.4%. Sign convention (Eq. 1): PE = (measured minus predicted) over predicted, so positive means predicted concentration too low.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No propofol or remifentanil PD was estimated; no ke0, t1/2 ke0, tPeak, EC50 or MEAC reported. BIS and SEF95 are only descriptive (Table 1: intraoperative BIS 50.5 +/- 10.3 and 50.6 +/- 11.6 by formulation).

Opioid audit (remifentanil): no MEAC, no ke0/hysteresis, no equianalgesic or potency ratio. Only dosing facts: remifentanil initial plasma target 5 ng/mL, titrated to haemodynamics; mean predicted remifentanil 5.3 +/- 3.6 (LCT) and 5.1 +/- 1.8 (MCT/LCT) ng/mL (Table 1, PDF p4), Minto 1997 PK set, remifentanil never assayed. The paper's pharmacokinetic claim: propofol clearance and central volume are lower with remifentanil than in other sets.

## Covariates
Regression of individual PK variables against weight, height, age, gender, ASA status and surgery type (text, PDF p4): none significant for the compartmental parameters. Non-compartmental comparison (Table 3, PDF p5, Mann-Whitney with Bonferroni): female AUC normalised to dose 4.2 +/- 1.1 vs male 5.2 +/- 1.0 mg.h/L/h (P < 0.05); clearance ASA 3 1.44 +/- 0.33 vs ASA 2 1.79 +/- 0.32 L/min (P < 0.05); abdominal vs non-abdominal, smoker status and propofol formulation not different. The formulation as covariate did not change the objective function (901.5 vs 904.9).

## Caveats
- Short post-infusion sampling, so V3 and terminal clearance are weakly determined (V3 was fixed); authors state extrapolated AUC was under 5% of total.
- No propofol-alone control: the lower clearance is attributed to remifentanil co-administration (speculated haemodynamic mechanism) but compared against other studies' volunteers and patients (Marsh, Schnider), not a within-study reference.
- Arterial sampling with slow-bolus TCI induction (maximum 133 mg/min) gives a small Vc and Q2, which authors link to sampling site and scheme.
- Mean patient was 57-60 years old, ASA 1-3, receiving 7.5 mg midazolam and flunitrazepam premedication; surgery changes physiology.
- New set was fitted and tested within one study (27 vs 27 patients), no external validation; initial concentrations (loss of consciousness sample) still underpredicted.
- Funded in part by B. Braun (Propofol-Lipuro maker).
