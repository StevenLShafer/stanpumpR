# Kates 1978: dobutamine PK in severe heart failure

## Study
- 7 adults (6 men, 1 woman), congestive cardiomyopathy, NYHA class IV, mean age 54 (31-67) y, normal renal and hepatic tests, on digoxin (Table I, printed p. 538). Weights 62.9-85.8 kg (mean derived 73.1 kg), BSA 1.72-2.02 m2 (mean derived 1.86 m2). Edema grade 1 (trace) to 4 (anasarca).
- Dobutamine HCl by constant IV infusion (Harvard pump), stepped every 20 min: 2.5, 5.0, 7.5, 10.0 ug/kg/min; then held at the optimal haemodynamic rate for 3-7 days.
- Pulmonary-artery (mixed venous) blood, 10 mL, at the end of each 20-min step (20, 40, 60, 80 min) and at 0, 1, 2, 4, 6, 10 min after stopping. Plasma, HPLC with fluorescence detection (McKennon and Kates, in press at the time).
- Analysis: non-population. Steady-state plasma concentration vs rate for CL; log-linear washout for half-life; Vd = CL/kel (Eq. 2). One-compartment model, Eq. 3.

## Model and parameters
One-compartment, first-order elimination, per patient. Table III (confirmed on rendered page; mean +/- SD, n = 7):

| | t1/2 (min) | Vd (L/kg) | CL (L/min/m2) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mean +/- SD | 2.37 +/- 0.70 | 0.202 +/- 0.084 | 2.35 +/- 1.01 |
| Range | 1.39-3.42 | 0.093-0.323 | 1.51-4.48 |

Patient G has CL 4.48; excluding G mean CL is 2.0 +/- 0.41 L/min/m2 (text, p. 539). Vd as % of body weight 9.3-32.3%, mean 20.2 +/- 8.4% (p. 539). Mean absolute CL 4.43 L/min, 113% of pre-study mean cardiac output 3.92 L/min (p. 540; CL exceeds cardiac output, so extra-hepatic/blood-based removal is implied by the authors).
Derived: kel = 0.693/t1/2 = 0.29/min (mean t1/2); CL per kg = 4.4 L/min / 73 kg is about 60 mL/min/kg (rough, from means); Vd absolute about 14.8 L (0.202 x 73.1). Patient B check: CL 1.51 x 1.72 m2 = 2.60 L/min; kel = 0.693/2.69 = 0.258/min; Vd = 10.1 L = 0.161 L/kg (matches Table III).

Table II, steady-state plasma concentration (mean, ng/mL; CV%): 2.5 ug/kg/min 46.4 (55.5); 5.0 ug/kg/min 99.8 (34.1); 7.5 ug/kg/min 135.7 (29.7); 10.0 ug/kg/min 167.2 (33.2). Linear in rate (r = 0.999 in Fig. 1, patient B; intercept -1.25, not different from zero): no saturation.
Steady state reached by about 10-12 min (p. 540).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No effect measurement was reported (haemodynamics are mentioned only as stabilising by about 10 min, consistent with the PK). No ke0, t1/2ke0, tPeak, hysteresis, EC50 or MEAC. Not an opioid.

## Covariates
Authors note, from 7 patients, an apparent relation of Vd to degree of edema (Fig. 4, grade 0-4); no equation. Correcting Vd for weight or BSA did not reduce variability. CL was unrelated to cardiac output or organ blood flow. No age or sex analysis.

## Caveats
- n = 7, all NYHA IV heart failure, on digoxin; healthy-subject or non-failure values are not given. Infusion times short (80 min) before the washout; washout sampled to 10 min only, with a first-order half-life of about 2 min, so no slower terminal phase could have been seen at the assay limit.
- Blood from the pulmonary artery (mixed venous), not arterial.
- CL is normalised to BSA but Vd to weight; mixing the two requires each patient's BSA and weight.
- The text prints the assay sensitivity as "10 mg/m!" (surely ng/mL, mangled in the text layer); not confirmed on the page image.
