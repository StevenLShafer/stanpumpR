# Greenblatt 2006: modified-release vs immediate-release zolpidem, EEG/DSST/sedation and kinetics

## Study
Randomized, double-blind, double-dummy, placebo-controlled 3-way crossover, single centre (Clinilabs, New York),
Oct 2003 - Mar 2004. 81 healthy adults 19-45 y enrolled; 70 completers analysed: age 29.7 +/- 0.8 y, height 170 cm,
weight 71 +/- 1.3 kg (mean +/- SE), BMI 24.4; 38 M / 32 F; 41 Caucasian, 15 African-American, 8 Hispanic, 4 Asian.
Treatments: zolpidem IR (Ambien 10 mg), zolpidem MR (Ambien CR 12.5 mg), placebo; given in the morning about 2 h
after breakfast; washout 7-14 days. Plasma and EEG at 0, 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2, 2.5, 3, 3.33 ... 6, 6.5, 7, 7.5, 8, 9, 10,
12 h (22 post-dose points). LC-MS/MS, linear 0.5-100 ng/mL, LLOQ 0.5 ng/mL. Model-independent kinetics (no
absorption rate fitted). PD: EEG relative beta (12-30 Hz) amplitude, 8 leads; DSST; self- and observer-rated
sedation VAS.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
EEG beta amplitude change vs plasma concentration (aggregate means): no clockwise or counterclockwise hysteresis;
fitted y = 0.0366 x^1.13 (IR, r2 = 0.98) and y = 0.0723 x^0.97 (MR) (Fig. 6). EEG effect area 0-8 h: IR 24.2,
MR 29.6, placebo -5.8 (Table I). DSST AUCE 0-8 h: IR -37.4, MR -44.7; self-rated sedation 0-8 h IR 145, MR 168
(Table II). No ke0, EC50 or Emax reported. Not an opioid; no MEAC.

## Covariates
None significant for t1/2 or weight-normalized clearance (gender, ethnicity, sequence).

## Caveats
Daytime dosing about 2 h after breakfast (food delays and lowers peak; tmax 2.0 h is longer than fasting studies).
Body weight capped at 90 kg. Values are mean +/- SE, not SD. Sponsor-funded (Sanofi). MR vs IR confounds dose and
formulation.
