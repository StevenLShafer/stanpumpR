# Hiles 2003: ex vivo placental transfer of pramlintide and exenatide

## Study
Ex vivo dual perfusion of a single isolated cotyledon from normal term placentas (vaginal or caesarean
delivery), Amylin Pharmaceuticals with UT Southwestern (PDF p. 2, printed 624). Fetal artery and vein
cannulated; maternal and fetal circuits each 150 mL of Earle's minimal essential medium. Integrity and
perfusion constants checked with 14C-antipyrine (≥40% maternal-to-fetal transfer in single pass; Challier
criteria; every placenta met them, p. 2). Open loop (non-recirculating) to get transfer fraction (TF),
clearance and clearance index (Ci, test drug clearance relative to antipyrine), then closed loop
(recirculating) for accumulation. Samples at 15, 30, 45, 60, 90, 120 min.

Six placentas per peptide: three near the anticipated maximum human plasma concentration and three at
10-50 times that (nominal pramlintide 1200 and 32 000 pg/mL; exenatide 300 and 3000 pg/mL; Tables 1-4).
Human insulin about 5000 µU/mL added to every perfusion as a non-crossing control. Assays: pramlintide
IEMA (LLOQ 18 pg/mL; Table 2 "LowStd" 20 pg/mL), exenatide IEMA (LLOQ 1.5 pg/mL; Table 4 LowStd
2.5 pg/mL), insulin RIA (2 µU/mL). Number of placentas used in total and donor characteristics not given.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. Not an opioid paper; no MEAC, ke0 or potency. End point is placental transfer only.

## Covariates
None studied.

## Caveats
- Ex vivo term placenta, single cotyledon, 2 h; says nothing about earlier gestation or maternal
  pharmacokinetics in pregnancy.
- Pramlintide concentrations fell in the closed loop (Table 2), which the authors attribute to proteolysis
  without protease inhibitor; open-loop maternal values are erratic (e.g. placenta 4 rose to 95 218 pg/mL at
  60 min).
- Below-LLOQ fetal values imputed as zero, so ratios are lower bounds in the low-concentration arms.
- Sample mix-ups (Tables 1-2 footnote) and a contaminated sample (Table 4) excluded.
- Industry-sponsored (Amylin authors).
