# Chen 2009: TCI vs manual propofol infusion at matched BIS/MLAEP

## Study
Randomised (sealed envelope), 50 ASA 1-2 adults, elective ENT surgery, Kiel. TCI n=25, MCI n=25. Table 1 (PDF p.3, printed 930, text layer): age 39+-14 (TCI) vs 42+-14 (MCI) y; weight 81+-16 vs 78+-14 kg; height 175 cm both; sex M/F 12/13 vs 15/10. Premedication midazolam 7.5 mg orally. TIVA propofol + remifentanil; rocuronium 0.6 mg/kg once. BIS (BIS-XP) and midlatency auditory evoked potentials (Pa, Nb latencies) used to titrate to BIS 40-60 (also MAP within 20% of baseline).
- TCI: Diprifusor software on Alaris Asena PK pump, Marsh propofol model (plasma-targeted); induction target 4 ug/mL, remifentanil 0.3 ug/kg/min. Target stepped by 0.5 ug/mL.
- MCI: bolus 2 mg/kg then 5 mg/kg/h; steps of 1 mg/kg/h; a 20 mg rescue bolus if BIS stayed >60.
- Venous (antecubital, contralateral arm) blood at awake baseline, intubation, 15 min after intubation, extubation; EDTA plasma, HPLC; LOQ 0.05 ug/mL; inter-assay CV 6.1%, intra-assay 3.2%; recovery 98-100%.

## Model and parameters
No model was fitted. The TCI arm ran the published Marsh set (not reproduced in the paper).
```yaml
parameters: []   # no pharmacokinetic parameters estimated; Marsh model used as supplied by the pump
```
Dose and performance results (Table 2, PDF p.4, read on the image; mean+-SD, TCI vs MCI):
- Propofol induction dose 2.09+-0.36 vs 2.06+-0.16 mg/kg; induction time 232+-43 vs 240+-52 s.
- Anaesthesia time 120+-51 vs 113+-42 min; total propofol dose 5.57+-0.96 vs 5.79+-0.87 mg/kg/h (as printed).
- Remifentanil during remaining anaesthesia 0.40+-0.07 vs 0.41+-0.06 ug/kg/min.
- Recovery (Table 3, image): eyes open 7.6+-3.3 vs 7.8+-2.6 min; extubation 8.5 vs 9.0; PACU discharge 12.6 vs 13.0; ward 62.1 vs 62.1 min.
- Measured plasma propofol (Table 4, PDF p.6, text layer, mean+-SD ug/mL): intubation TCI 3.1+-1.5, MCI 2.9+-1.1; 15 min after intubation TCI 2.8+-1.2, MCI 2.7+-0.9; extubation TCI 1.5+-0.5, MCI 1.7+-0.7. Predicted (TCI): 2.7+-0.6 at intubation, 2.2+-0.2 at 15 min. Measured vs predicted at 15 min differs (P<0.05).
- Predictive performance, TCI group (PDF p.4): all samples (n=44) MDPE "more than 24" (SD 54)%, MDAPE 42 (41)%; samples at 15 min (n=22) MDPE "more than 35" (56)%, MDAPE 40 (52)%. Wording suggests measured above predicted (positive bias); the error formula is 100*(Cm-Cp)/Cp. Measured at 15 min ranged 1.8-6.5 ug/mL vs predicted 2.0-2.5. Measured vs predicted r=0.14 (P=0.36 in Fig. 5 caption, 0.37 in text).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- No ke0, t1/2 ke0, tPeak or EC50 reported. No opioid MEAC reported (remifentanil was a co-drug dosed by rate; no remifentanil concentrations measured). No potency ratio.
- End points: BIS and MLAEP latencies. BIS 40-60 maintained; mean intra-anaesthesia BIS 44+-3 (TCI) vs 44+-4 (MCI). BIS at eye opening 73+-4 both groups; no patient regained consciousness with BIS <62.
- Measured total plasma propofol vs BIS: r=-0.56 over all points; no correlation within BIS 40-60 (r=-0.18, P=0.13; text layer reads "-018"). Pa and Nb latency vs propofol: r=0.48 and 0.46.
- Pa latency rise >15 ms and Nb prolongation >70 ms during anaesthesia; thresholds quoted from other work (Nb 60 ms, Pa >12 ms).

## Covariates
None analysed. Interindividual variability in measured concentration at a given Marsh prediction was large.

## Caveats
Single anaesthesiologist; ENT surgery (artefact risk); only three plasma samples per patient, so no within-patient PK/PD analysis; the Marsh set has no age covariate; remifentanil co-administration confounds BIS vs concentration. Text typos: remifentanil step "0.1 mg/kg/min" should presumably be ug; "r = -018".
