# Short 1992: midazolam, propofol and alfentanil interactions (hypnosis and tetanus end points)

## Study
- 400 Chinese women, 18-40 yr, ASA I-II, within 20% of ideal weight, unpremedicated, elective gynaecological surgery. Three phases over 10 months; phase 1-3 mean (SD) age 31.4 (5.4), 30.6 (6.4), 32.1 (5.0) yr, weight 51.6 (10.3), 50.3 (8.3), 52.5 (9.2) kg (p163). Midazolam-propofol data were reported in Short and Chui 1991 (PMID_1751266).
- Groups of 10 per dose; five doses of midazolam or alfentanil, seven of propofol, each alone; then midazolam-propofol, midazolam-alfentanil, propofol-alfentanil and the three-drug combination at fixed dose ratios (starting at 50% of the individual hypnotic ED50 for pairs, 33% for the triple). Bolus over 10 s, 10 mL saline flush.
- Order: midazolam 2 min before propofol or alfentanil; alfentanil before propofol. Assessment 4 min after midazolam, 2 min after propofol or alfentanil ("approximate time to peak effect"; assumed, not measured).
- End points: hypnosis = no eye opening to verbal command; anaesthesia = no response to a 5-s tetanus (50 Hz, 80 mA, 0.25 ms) over the ulnar nerve, tested in hypnotic patients.
- Analysis: ED50 by maximum likelihood; hypnosis by a 10-parameter response-surface "Model 1" (weighted least squares; 280 patients contributed after dropping 0%/100% groups); anaesthesia by logistic shift of the propofol curve. No concentrations measured.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- Opioid audit (alfentanil): no MEAC, analgesic concentration, ke0, t½ke0, tPeak or hysteresis was measured; no concentrations were taken. Assessment at 2 min after alfentanil is stated to be about its bolus time to peak effect, without source or measurement (p162).
- End points: loss of response to voice (hypnosis) and to tetanus (anaesthesia), dose-based. Alfentanil alone: hypnosis ED50 0.094 mg/kg (Table III); no anaesthesia ED50, because muscular rigidity at >=0.1 mg/kg made tetanus responses uninterpretable (p165).
- Potency ratios at the hypnotic end point (dose, mg/kg): midazolam:propofol about 1:7.4 and alfentanil:propofol about 1:11 (derived from Table III), and the fitted potency P_B, P_C depend on level (eqs 1-2). Not equianalgesic ratios.
- Interactions (hypnosis): all pairs synergistic; three-drug term negative, so no extra synergy beyond pairs (Discussion). Anaesthesia: propofol ED50 reduced 52% (midazolam), 73% (alfentanil), 82% (both), Table VI.

## Covariates
None analysed beyond demonstrating equal age and weight across groups.

## Caveats
- Dose rather than concentration; one time point after bolus; no PK interaction excluded.
- Young Chinese women only; hypnosis analysis uses groups of 10; midazolam and alfentanil lack individual anaesthesia ED50, so the anaesthesia synergy cannot be quantitated like hypnosis (authors).
- Alfentanil rigidity confounds the tetanus end point.
- Numerical inconsistencies listed in open_questions; Table VI CI for P-A does not bracket its ED50.
