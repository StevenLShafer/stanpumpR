# Divoll 1981: age and sex and temazepam disposition

## Study
- 32 healthy volunteers, 24-84 y, in four groups: young men (n = 7), young women (n = 7), elderly men
  (n = 8), elderly women (n = 10). Five elderly subjects took cardiovascular medicines.
- Single 30 mg oral temazepam capsule (Restoril, US hard capsule) after an overnight fast; fasting 3 h more.
- Venous plasma at 0.25-48 h (17 times). Electron-capture GLC (internal standard 3-hydroxyprazepam).
- Free fraction by equilibrium dialysis at 37 °C of one non-fasting sample; binding independent of
  concentration over 160-4160 ng/mL.
- Non-compartmental terminal phase (one- or two-exponential fits with absorption, lag if needed;
  satisfactory iterative fits in only 11 of 32). CL = dose/AUC **assuming complete absorption** (F = 1,
  by analogy with lorazepam; absolute bioavailability unknown). Vd (area) = CL/β.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None measured. No ke0, no concentration-effect data.

## Covariates
- Sex: clearance lower and t½ longer in women at any age.
- Age: no significant effect on total t½ or CL; free fraction rises with age (r = 0.45), partly via
  falling albumin; unbound CL tends to fall with age (r ≈ -0.43, p = 0.1, NS).
- No equations given.

## Caveats
- Clearance is CL/F with F assumed 1; hard-capsule absorption was slow and erratic (Tmax up to 12 h).
- Table I printed in a layout the text layer scrambled; the reconstruction above is consistent with
  every pooled value the text gives, but individual cells should be re-read from the PDF before reuse.
- Small groups; five elderly on cardiac drugs.
