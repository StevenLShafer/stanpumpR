# Absalom 2003: BIS closed-loop propofol TCI, minor surgery

## Study
Prospective, uncontrolled performance study of a BIS-driven closed-loop controller that sets the target blood propofol concentration of a TCI system (p.737-738). 20 ASA I-II adults, 18-80 y (mean 43, range 30-72), 12 F / 8 M, weight 68 (SD 11) kg, height 169 (9) cm, BMI <30, body-surface surgery under LMA. Induction by manual TCI: propofol 4 ug/mL (2.5 in the elderly) raised by 0.5 ug/mL every 30 s to loss of consciousness, remifentanil TCI 2 ng/mL then 4 ng/mL after LMA; propofol then lowered to about 0.5 ug/mL above estimated effect-site. Closed loop started once surgery began, at the BIS judged clinically adequate; remifentanil held at 4 ng/mL (p.738). Patients 1-10: PID with gains P 0.25 to 0.2, I 0.5 to 0.75 (changes from the earlier study); patients 11-20 also had effect-site steering (target blood concentration moved to the estimated effect-site concentration, then beyond if needed). Performance by Varvel MDPE, MDAPE, wobble, offset (p.738-739).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point is BIS (EEG). Table 1 (p.739), median (range), calculated Cp / Ce ug/mL: loss of consciousness BIS 74 (50-91), Cp 6 (4-8), Ce 2.2 (1.1-3.9); ready for intubation BIS 44, Cp 5.6, Ce 3.4; ready for incision BIS 51 (29-72), Cp 4, Ce 3.9 (2.9-4.9); start of automatic control BIS 50, Cp 3 (2.4-4.5), Ce 3.3 (2.4-4.8); eye opening BIS 74 (54-96), Cp 1.3, Ce 1.6 (1-3.3); responds to command BIS 77, Cp 1.2, Ce 1.6; states date of birth BIS 92 (60-98), Cp 1.2, Ce 1.6. These are model-calculated, with remifentanil 4 ng/mL on board at the closed-loop stage, so they are not propofol-alone EC50 values. No ke0, t1/2ke0 or tPeak is reported; the Ce values come from an unnamed model. The paper says BIS 40-60 is the manufacturer-recommended range. Remifentanil: no MEAC, no ke0, no potency ratio.

## Covariates
None analysed beyond algorithm version (blood-targeted vs effect-site-steered).

## Caveats
Small n; steered group younger and healthier, tested under different conditions, so improvement is not conclusive (p.740); no control arm; one patient moved when remifentanil was switched off in error; BIS drifted in one patient (45-62, Cp 2.2-3.5 ug/mL).
