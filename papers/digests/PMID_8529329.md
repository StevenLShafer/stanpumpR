# Lo 1995: losartan and EXP3174 after IV losartan, IV EXP3174 and oral losartan

PDF page n is printed page 640 + n (PDF p1 = 641, p9 = 649).

## Study
Two separate studies in two different groups of healthy men (Methods, p3), 18 subjects in all:
- **Study 1** (n = 12; age 21-36 y; mean weight 75.6 kg): randomized two-period crossover, 50 mg losartan
  potassium tablet orally and 20 mg IV over 20 min, at least 7 days apart, fasted. Assayed potencies 49.1 mg
  (tablet) and 19.7 mg (IV); Table I data normalised to 50.0 and 20.0 mg. Plasma to 36 h oral and 30 h IV
  (dense early sampling), urine to 48 h.
- **Study 2** (n = 6, "six different healthy male subjects"; age 22-30 y; mean weight 78.6 kg): open
  three-period crossover, 100 mg 14C-losartan oral solution, 30 mg IV losartan over 20 min, and 20 mg EXP3174 IV
  over 20 min (as hydrochloride; mean actual dose 16.6 mg free acid). Mean actual losartan doses 90.3 mg oral and
  26.4 mg IV. Plasma to 48 h (losartan) and 36 h (IV EXP3174), urine to 96 h.
- So IV EXP3174 was given to the 6 men of Study 2 only, and no subject received all of the 20 mg IV, 30 mg IV,
  tablet and solution doses.
- Assay: HPLC with UV detection, losartan as potassium salt and EXP3174 as free acid; LOQ 5 ng/mL plasma
  (p3-4). Blood/plasma ratio 0.53 (losartan) and 0.58 (EXP3174); plasma binding 98.7% and 99.8% (p6).
- Analysis entirely non-compartmental: linear-up/log-down trapezoids, λz by log-linear regression, harmonic-mean
  half-lives (Lam), Vss by Perrier and Mayersohn, renal clearance, conversion fractions (p4-5). No compartmental
  model and no distribution half-life or exponential intercepts are reported.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No pharmacodynamic measurement. EXP3174 is described as active and important to the duration of action. Hepatic
extraction ratio of losartan estimated at 0.68 from a blood clearance of about 1020 mL/min and liver blood flow
1500 mL/min, predicting F 0.32 against the observed 0.36 arithmetic (p8). Not an opioid; no MEAC, ke0 or potency
ratio.

## Covariates
None studied (healthy young men only).

## Caveats
- Internal reporting differences, all reconciled by arithmetic: EXP3174 tmax is 4 h in the Study 1 Results (Table
  I mean 4.1 h) but 3½ h in the abstract and Discussion, which is the 18-subject mean (derived: (12 × 4.1 + 6 ×
  2.3)/18 = 3.5); the abstract's CL 610 mL/min, Vss 34 L and t1/2 2.1 h are pooled means; "14%" conversion is Table
  IV's 14.3% (IV) or 13.6% (oral).
- The two studies differ 1.6-fold in losartan Vss (28.4 vs 46.6 L) and 1.8-fold in IV half-life (1.84 vs 3.2 h),
  with different doses (20 vs 30 mg) and plasma sampling (30 vs 48 h after IV). The paper does not comment.
- Large between-subject variability: tablet F 12.1-66.6%, Cmax 49-733 ng/mL.
- Only 6 subjects for EXP3174 IV and for the conversion fractions.
