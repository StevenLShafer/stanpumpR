# Stanczyk 1983: norethindrone and ethinylestradiol, solution vs tablet

## Study
Open, randomised Latin-square crossover bioequivalence study in 24 healthy women (age from 18 y, upper limit illegible in the text layer (selection criterion: under 50 y), mean 31.5; weight 52.2-77.6 kg, mean 60.7). Each took 1.0 mg norethindrone (NET) + 0.12 mg ethinylestradiol (EE2) as (a) 100 mL ethanol:water 20:80 solution, (b) two tablets of 0.5 mg NET + 0.06 mg EE2, after a 12 h fast, 7-14 days washout (days 6-24 of cycle). Plasma at 0, 0.5, 1, 1.5, 3, 6, 12, 24, 48 h; 48 h samples mostly below detection so data used to 24 h. Plasma by radioimmunoassay: NET sensitivity 50 pg/mL, EE2 20 pg/mL (PDF p.3). PK model fitted by NONLIN to the tablet data only.

## Model and parameters
Two-compartment open model with first-order absorption, central elimination; fit weighted by 1/variance. 4 of 24 subjects did not fit, so n=20. F not known (no IV dose), so V1 is V1/F; an overestimate of V1 given first-pass metabolism (PDF p.7-8). Table III (PDF p.7, printed p.247), tablets, mean +/- SE:

Half-lives from mean Table III values (as stated in text): absorption 0.21 h (NET), 0.30 h (EE2); alpha 0.63 h, 1.19 h; beta 8.3 h, 10.8 h. Consistency (derived): alpha+beta = 1.183 vs k12+k21+kel = 1.188 for NET; 0.644 vs 0.645 for EE2. Whole-dose-level facts: ka, k21 not different between drugs; alpha, k12, kel, V1/F differ (p<0.05); beta borderline (p=0.040).

Noncompartmental (Tables I and II, PDF p.6, mean +/- SE, n=24), solution vs tablet:
- NET: Tmax 1.17 +/- 0.107 vs 1.17 +/- 0.133 h; Cpmax 14.3 +/- 0.89 vs 15.7 +/- 1.26 ng/mL; AUC 68 +/- 4 vs 84 +/- 6 ng*h/mL (tablet significantly higher); t1/2 7.8 +/- 0.48 vs 8.1 +/- 0.39 h. Tablet concentration (ng/mL): 0.5 h 11.1, 1 h 14.3, 1.5 h 12.0, 3 h 6.1, 6 h 3.49, 12 h 1.89, 24 h 0.72. The solution was significantly lower at 6, 12 and 24 h (the authors could not explain this; not bioequivalent).
- EE2: Tmax 1.54 vs 1.52 h; Cpmax 566 +/- 41 vs 574 +/- 43 pg/mL; AUC 4879 +/- 340 vs 4850 +/- 370 pg*h/mL; t1/2 9.1 +/- 0.71 vs 9.4 +/- 0.65 h; bioequivalent. Tablet concentration (pg/mL): 0.5 h 251, 1 h 504, 1.5 h 525, 3 h 409, 6 h 206, 12 h 107, 24 h 53.
- Combined overall t1/2: EE2 about 9.8 h, NET about 8.1 h (borderline, p=0.076).
- The units: AUC of NET is ng/mL x hr; the EE2 AUC is pg/mL x hr; AUC(0-inf) = trapezoid to 24 h + Cp24/beta.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No pharmacodynamic end point, no ke0, no MEAC, and no potency ratio. Not an opioid paper.

## Covariates
None analysed.

## Caveats
Four subjects could not be fitted; few points after 12 h, so a three-compartment model cannot be excluded; NET kinetics are known to be non-linear (so a linear model applies only near the 1 mg dose); no IV data so F unknown (earlier reports: NET F 47-70% at 1 mg, EE2 38% at 50 ug, quoted from the literature on PDF p.8-9); RIA (cross-reactivity not discussed here); PK analysis was of the tablet data only. The authors note the solution NET result is unexplained.
