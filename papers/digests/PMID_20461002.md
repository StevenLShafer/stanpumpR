# Olofsen 2010: naloxone reversal of morphine and M6G respiratory depression

## Study
Single-blind, placebo-controlled, randomised volunteer study, Leiden. 56 healthy volunteers aged 18-34, BMI below 28, women on oral
contraception (p 1418). Morphine study n = 24 (9 M, 15 F; age 22 (19-27) yr; weight 69 +/- 12 kg; height 176 +/- 9 cm); M6G study
n = 32 (15 M, 17 F; age 22 (18-34); 71 +/- 9 kg; 175 +/- 8 cm) (Table 2, PDF p 4). Values are mean +/- SD.

Morphine 0.15 mg/kg IV at t = 0, then placebo, 200 ug or 400 ug naloxone IV at t = 30 min (n = 8 each). M6G 0.3 mg/kg IV at t = 0, then
placebo, 25, 100 or 400 ug naloxone at t = 55 min (n = 8 each). Infusions over more than 90 s. Naloxone timing chosen to give about equal
depression (0.69 vs 0.67 of baseline ventilation just before naloxone, p 1421).

End point: breath-to-breath minute ventilation at constant end-tidal PCO2 (46-49 mmHg, titrated so baseline ventilation was 25 +/- 2 L/min)
and end-tidal O2 110 mmHg (end-tidal forcing). **No blood was drawn** (sampling stimulated breathing); morphine, M6G and naloxone
concentrations were taken from earlier PK models in this lab, reanalysed per 70 kg (p 1419). NONMEM VI; log-normal parameters except gamma
(inverse logit, bounded 0-20); additive residual error.

## Model and parameters
Mechanism-based PK-PD: effect-site (biophase) equilibration by t1/2ke0, then competitive binding at one receptor pool. Agonist M:
d[MR]/dt = kon[M][R] - koff[MR]. Naloxone N is assumed to equilibrate instantly (koff large; in vitro dissociation t1/2 under 1 min, ref 14),
giving [NR] = ([N]/C50,N)^gamma / (1 + ([N]/C50,N)^gamma) x (1 - [MR]) (Eq 3), with C50,N = koff,N/kon,N. Ventilation = V0 x (1 - [MR]) (Eq 4):
the ventilation fraction removed equals the agonist-occupied receptor fraction. Concentrations of N in nM, M in uM.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Opioid audit (end point throughout: ventilation at constant end-tidal PCO2 about 48 mmHg, O2 110 mmHg; not analgesia):
- **MEAC / minimum effective concentration:** none. KD (koff/kon) is the apparent potency for ventilatory depression: morphine 160 nM, M6G 880 nM
  (text p 1422; the text calls this 5.5-fold more potent for morphine; derived ratio 880/160 = 5.5). The authors say these are the same order as
  earlier morphine and M6G C50 values for ventilation and analgesia (refs 10-13). Assay: modelled, with assumed (not measured) arterial plasma
  concentrations; units nM; total concentration implied.
- **ke0 / t1/2ke0:** morphine 1.24 h (derived ke0 = ln2/74.4 min = 0.0093 /min); M6G 2.72 h (derived 0.0042 /min), Table 4. The text says M6G biophase
  equilibration is about 2x slower (p 1422). Naloxone t1/2ke0 11.2 min (morphine study) and 5.42 min (M6G study); the text gives a range
  of 5.4-11.2 min and calls it consistent with earlier buprenorphine-reversal findings of 5-8 min (p 1425). No tPeak reported.
- **Receptor kinetics:** koff morphine 0.138 /min (t1/2 5 min), M6G 0.0327 /min (t1/2 21 min); kon 0.853 and 0.0371 /(uM min) (Table 4). Naloxone
  koff is assumed fast, not estimated.
- **Hysteresis in the data:** reversal developed more slowly for M6G; time to maximum reversal after naloxone 18.3 +/- 2.1 min for morphine versus
  49.3 +/- 7.6 min for M6G (P < 0.001; Results text, Table 3). Simulation (Figs 5-6): reversal speed is set by the agonist's kon and koff; naloxone dose,
  C50 and t1/2ke0 affect magnitude and duration, not speed.
- **Potency ratios:** naloxone is 3-4x more potent (C50) against M6G than morphine (0.484 vs 1.84 nM); morphine KD is 5.5x lower than M6G's (above).
  M6G 0.3 mg/kg is stated equipotent to morphine 0.15 mg/kg (p 1419, from refs 10-13; a 2:1 mass ratio for ventilatory effect at the chosen dose).
- Naloxone doses giving full reversal: 200 ug for morphine, 100 ug for M6G (p 1426). Peak ventilation with naloxone returned to near baseline
  (relative VMAX 0.95 +/- 0.01 pooled naloxone groups in morphine study per Table 3, text layer; not checked on the image).

## Covariates
Volumes and clearances scaled to 70 kg (Table 1). No covariate in the PD model. Possible sex or oral-contraceptive effects on naloxone response not
tested (p 1426).

## Caveats
Authors: no plasma sampling, so PK was simulated from earlier models (the volunteers largely took part in those); controlled hyperoxia and
isocapnic end-tidal conditions are not perioperative reality; only ventilatory drive was studied, not arousal or airway tone. The 3-4x
difference in naloxone potency between the two studies was unexpected; explanations offered include M6G enhancing naloxone binding or different
receptors. Mine: gamma of 4-7 for a naloxone "C50" means C50 is not a dissociation constant in the strict sense. The table prints
sigma^2 with the unit l/min.

## Verification against stanpumpR
No citation to verify; compared as an independent check. All findings go to the open questions.
- **Naloxone effect site** (R/drugs_naloxone.R:73-85, 116): library ke0 from Yassen 2007 0.106 /min (t1/2 6.5 min), carried as tPeak 3.58 min. Olofsen's naloxone t1/2ke0 is 5.42 min (M6G study) and
  11.2 min (morphine study); Yassen's falls between. Not a contradiction of the library, but the morphine-study value is 1.7x longer.
- **Naloxone KD** (inst/extdata/opioidReceptorConstants.csv:3): 1.87 ng/mL (Yassen). Olofsen 1.84 nM (0.60 ng/mL, derived) against morphine; the numerical
  coincidence of 1.84 and 1.87 is across different units (nM vs ng/mL), so check units when comparing.
- **Naloxone PK** (R/drugs_naloxone.R:148-153): Dowling-based, clearance about 1.5 L/min and total volume about 38 L; Olofsen/Yassen disposition
  has CL 3.45-3.60 L/min and V about 114-123 L. Different studies; Olofsen's PK was assumed, not fitted here.
- **Morphine** (R/drugs_morphine.R:52-55): tPeak 93.8 min is a library value, about 138 min t1/2ke0 on its own disposition; the header notes 1.24 h for
  ventilation. That matches Olofsen's morphine t1/2ke0 (Table 4, PDF p 7) exactly; the header does not name the source. Olofsen's PK (Table 1) was
  not compared with Lotsch's model used by the library.
- **M6G:** the library has no M6G drug file (searched R/ for M6G, glucuronide: only morphine, codeine and others that mention it in comments).
