# Heise 2015: faster-acting insulin aspart vs insulin aspart, glucose clamp in type 1 diabetes

## Study
Randomised, double-blind, single-centre (Profil, Neuss), three-period complete crossover phase 1 trial (NN1218-3978,
NCT01618188). 52 white adults with type 1 diabetes randomised (43 men, 9 women), 51 completed; Table 1 (PDF p3): age
40.3 (SD 12.0) y, weight 76.9 (10.2) kg, BMI 24.2 (2.2) kg/m2, diabetes 20.2 (11.4) y, HbA1c 7.3 (0.7)%, all fasting
C-peptide < 0.3 nmol/L. Single 0.2 U/kg SC (lower abdomen) of faster-acting aspart, insulin aspart (NovoRapid/NovoLog),
or a third formulation not reported; 3-12 day washout. 12-h Biostator euglycaemic clamp at 5.5 mmol/L. Free serum
insulin aspart by aspart-specific ELISA (LLOQ 20 pmol/L), 29 samples over 12 h. Non-compartmental end points; LS means
from linear mixed models (log scale for AUC, Cmax, GIRmax); compartmental fits used only to compute onset of
appearance and the AUC before the first quantifiable sample (PDF p3). Sponsor Novo Nordisk.

## Model and parameters
No population or compartmental parameters are reported. Table 2 (PDF p4), free serum insulin aspart, N = 51 each, LS
mean (s.e.m. or CV), faster aspart vs aspart, ratio (95% CI):
- onset of appearance 4.9 vs 11.2 min, 0.43 (0.36-0.51); t50%Cmax 20.7 vs 31.6 min, 0.65 (0.59-0.72); tmax 62.9 vs
  69.7 min, 0.90 (0.79-1.03), not significant.
- AUC0-15min 14.0 vs 3.1 pmol h/L, 4.53 (3.62-5.66); AUC0-30min 59.9 vs 29.2, 2.05; AUC0-1h 196.5 vs 153.4, 1.28;
  AUC0-1.5h 328.8 vs 295.4, 1.11 (1.01-1.22); AUC0-2h 441.9 vs 424.5, 1.04 (0.95-1.14), not significant.
- AUC0-12h 755.7 vs 786.9 pmol h/L, 0.96 (0.87-1.06); Cmax 318.5 vs 324.4 pmol/L, 0.98 (0.90-1.07).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: glucose infusion rate (GIR) in the clamp. Table 3 (PDF p5), faster aspart vs aspart:
t50%GIRmax 38.3 vs 46.1 min, 0.83 (0.73-0.94); tGIRmax 124.3 vs 135.2 min, 0.92 (0.84-1.01), not significant;
AUCGIR,0-30min 56.2 vs 38.0 mg/kg, 1.48 (1.13-2.02); 0-1h 183.7 vs 140.2, 1.31; 0-1.5h 360.4 vs 308.3, 1.17; primary end
point AUCGIR,0-2h 554.5 vs 502.2 mg/kg, 1.10 (1.00-1.22), not significant; AUCGIR,0-12h 1375.2 vs 1404.7 mg/kg, 0.98;
GIRmax 7.2 vs 7.1 mg/kg/min, 1.02. The abstract's numbers agree with Tables 2-3. No concentration-effect model.

## Covariates
None analysed.

## Caveats
Single dose under clamp conditions in lean, mostly male type 1 diabetes; the faster formulation is the development
product (nicotinamide and arginine excipients); PK end points only, no parameters a simulator could use. Sponsor-
authored.
