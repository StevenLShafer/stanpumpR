# Anderson 2013: propofol and reaction time to a vibrating handset

## Study
Pilot pd-study, ASA 1-2 adults having elective general anaesthesia (PDF p2-3). Phase 1: 10 patients, two baseline reaction times, Ce increments 0.5 ug/mL. Phase 2 (revised protocol): 20 patients (13 men, 7 women), age 35 (9) y, weight 82 (17) kg, three baseline reaction times (2nd and 3rd averaged), Ce raised by 0.2 ug/mL every 2 min from a start of 1 ug/mL until OAAS 1 (unresponsive). Graseby 3500 TCI, Marsh PK model with t1/2 ke0 2.6 min (ref 11, Marsh 1991, a children's model paper); effect-site concentrations are calculated, not measured. Handset vibrated each 1 min; reaction time = time to press the button once, in ms; loss of response = no response or > 6 s. OAAS recorded with each measurement. Probit analysis for dose-response; no power calculation.

## Model and parameters
No PK model was fitted; the paper uses the published Marsh model inside the device and reports no PK parameters.

No ke0 estimated; the device used t1/2 ke0 = 2.6 min (stated, not derived from these data). No opioid, so no MEAC audit item.

- End points: reaction time to handset vibration (ms), loss of response to handset, OAAS score, loss of verbal contact (OAAS 2 or less per the companion paper's definition; this paper reports OAAS 1 as the stopping point).
- Reaction time rose slowly then rapidly with Ce; in phase 1 the reaction time at the increment before loss of response was 188 (96)% of baseline.
- Two patients lost verbal contact at the same Ce as loss of response. The Ce50 gap is 0.7 ug/mL (derived text value, stated by the authors as more than three 0.2 increments).
- Learning effect: 2nd baseline averaged 81% of the 1st (95% CI 65-98%, p = 0.03); 3rd was 102% of the 2nd (p = 0.91). In 7 of 20 patients reaction time fell early, up to Ce 1 ug/mL.

## Covariates
None analysed.

## Caveats
Small pilot, no power calculation; calculated not measured Ce; Marsh model with ke0 2.6 min, not the Eleveld model used in stanpumpR; Ce stepped every 2 min, so the effect site is near but not at equilibrium; oxygen given by mask throughout; Ce50 (probit) and mean-per-patient Ce differ (1.6 vs 2.0; 2.3 vs 2.4) and the paper does not reconcile them.
