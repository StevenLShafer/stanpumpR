# Kelman 1966: nomograms for time and temperature corrections of blood gases

## Study
Methods paper. Builds nomograms (computed on a Leeds University digital computer) to correct measured blood Po2, Pco2, pH and base excess for (a) metabolism in shed blood between sampling and analysis and (b) the temperature difference between subject and electrode. No patients or drugs. Assumes normal leucocyte and reticulocyte counts and Hb 14.8 g/100 ml.

## Model and parameters
Table I (PDF p. 2, printed 1485) pools literature rates in shed blood at 37-38 C; the values used:
```yaml
parameters:
  - {name: O2_consumption, value: 9.0e-5, unit: ml/ml/min, source: "Table I mean, p. 1485", note: "37 C; individual studies 6.05e-5 to 10.5e-5"}
  - {name: Pco2_rise, value: 0.10, unit: mmHg/min, source: "text p. 1485 (Table I mean 0.097)", note: "37 C, independent of Pco2 and Po2"}
  - {name: pH_fall, value: 0.0009, unit: pH/min, source: "Table I mean, p. 1485", note: "37 C, linear in time"}
  - {name: base_deficit_rise, value: 0.03, unit: mEq/L/min, source: "text p. 1486 (Table I mean 0.028)", note: "37 C"}
  - {name: Pco2_temp_f, value: 0.019, unit: per degC (log10 basis), source: "p. 1486", note: "gas tension ratio T1/T2 = 10^(f (T1 - T2)); calculated 0.0185 at pH 7.40 and 34-40 C; measured 0.019 at 18-36.5 C"}
  - {name: pH_temp_coefficient, value: -0.0147, unit: pH/degC, source: "p. 1487, citing Rosenthal 1948", note: "Fig 5 scale; Fig 6 refines it"}
```
Po2 temperature factor: f = 0.0052 + 0.0268 (1 - e^(-0.3x)), x = percent desaturation (exponent is small print on p. 1486; not confirmed). Adamsons et al. coefficient (p. 1487): 0.0146 - 0.005 (7.40 - pH38) + 0.00005 (20 - [CO2]), [CO2] plasma mmol/L; Fig 6 gives it against pH and Pco2. Metabolic rates at 0 C are about 6 % of those at 37 C (Fig 1); 10 % is used for cooled samples. Rate temperature dependence is Arrhenius. Syringe cooling curves (Fig 2) assume ambient about 22 C. Worked examples at pp. 1487-1490 (e.g. 30 C: Pco2 x 0.74, Po2 x 0.68, pH +0.103; text layer).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. Not an opioid paper: no MEAC, ke0, tPeak or potency ratio.

## Covariates
Corrections depend on elapsed time, syringe type and temperature, oxyhemoglobin saturation (Po2), pH and Pco2 (pH coefficient), Hb, white and reticulocyte counts.

## Caveats
Authors say corrections are approximate and advise immediate analysis; fluoride not considered; the dissociation curve is a mean curve and varies between people.
