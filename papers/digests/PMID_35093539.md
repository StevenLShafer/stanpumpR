# Heffernan 2022: ceftriaxone against MSSA in a hollow-fibre infection model

## Study
Short communication. In vitro dynamic hollow-fibre infection model (HFIM; polysulfone cartridges C2011, FiberCell),
7 days, **one** clinical MSSA isolate (#CTAP54), modal broth-microdilution MIC **4 mg/L** (CLSI/EUCAST method, two
occasions x four replicates; p. 2-3). Initial inoculum 1 x 10^6 CFU/mL. One HFIM experiment per regimen.

Exposure: unbound ceftriaxone concentrations simulated from the Garot et al. 2011 critically ill septic population
model, terminal half-life adjusted to creatinine clearance 100 mL/min, giving an HFIM half-life of 11.55 h (p. 2).
Regimens 1 g q24h, 2 g q24h, 1 g q12h, 2 g q12h, each infused over 30 min. Drug assay: UHPLC-UV (254 nm) in broth,
linear 1-100 mg/L, intra-batch accuracy/precision within 7.5%/7.8%. Bacteria counted on plain agar and on agar with
16 mg/L ceftriaxone (4 x MIC). Model: Pmetrics 1.5.2, two-subpopulation (susceptible + less-susceptible) growth/kill
model with Hill-type killing (Eq. 1-3); Monte Carlo simulations with the Garot model.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: total bacterial density (CFU/mL) over 168 h (Fig. 1, p. 3).
- 1 g q24h: no appreciable effect; growth near control.
- 1 g q12h: growth like control in the first 12 h, then about 1-2 log10 reduction, lessening after 48 h
  (Fig. 1B shows regrowth to about 10^10-10^11 CFU/mL).
- 2 g q24h: about 1 log10 reduction over 24 h; similar to control after 96 h.
- 2 g q12h: about 1 log10 reduction over 24 h, maintained to 168 h (Fig. 1D: about 10^4-10^6 CFU/mL). This is the
  best result: sustained suppression below the inoculum, but nowhere near a 3-log10 (bactericidal) kill.
- No colonies on 16 mg/L ceftriaxone agar for any regimen (no resistance detected).
- Simulations (Supplementary Table S1, not in this PDF): probability of stasis, 1-log or 2-log kill in 24 h
  negligible below 2 g q12h at any renal function; 2 g q8h may be needed at CrCl ~100 mL/min for 24-h stasis.
Authors conclude ceftriaxone has limited killing even at 2 g twice daily with normal renal function, and recommend a
specific anti-staphylococcal agent. Cites EUCAST (2021) advice of at least 4 g/day for MSSA, and Zelenitsky 2018
%fT>MIC targets of 55/75/100% for stasis/1-log/3-log kill. Not an opioid paper: no MEAC, ke0 or potency ratio.

## Covariates
Creatinine clearance only, via the Garot model in simulations (one CrCl, 100 mL/min, in the HFIM).

## Caveats
One isolate, one experiment per regimen (no replicates); no immune system; 2 g q24h exposure overshot its target;
EC50 and kill rates apply to this isolate only. Supplementary material (Fig. S1-S3, Table S1) is not in the
archived PDF.

## Verification against stanpumpR
- Note (lines 237-240): in a hollow-fibre model of an MSSA with MIC 4 mg/L, 2 g twice daily "achieved only
  bacteriostasis". Paper: MIC 4 mg/L (p. 2), and 2 g q12h gave a sustained ~1-log10 reduction, the authors' own word
  being limited killing. **Matches in substance**: the row's purpose (ceftriaxone is not bactericidal against MSSA, so
  it is excluded from the MBC band) is supported; strictly the paper shows about 1 log10 below inoculum, slightly
  more than stasis, far short of 3 log10.
- `MIC50 = 4, MIC90 = 4` (line 236) are attributed to EUCAST's 357-isolate distribution and Biedenbach 2016, not to
  this paper; this paper's single isolate (MIC 4) is consistent.
- The EUCAST claim that efficacy could not be reached at 2 g twice daily or 4 g once daily is EUCAST's, not this
  paper's (this paper did not test 4 g once daily). Not checked here.
- PK comparison: the library's ceftriaxone model (`R/drugs_ceftriaxone.R`, Sanz-Codina 2023, six healthy men, total
  plasma: CL 1.21 L/h, Vc 5.76 L, Q 2.92 L/h, Vp 2.97 L) has a terminal half-life of about 5.3 h (derived: beta from
  k10 0.210, k12 0.507, k21 0.983 per h). The 11.55 h HFIM half-life here is longer because it is from Garot's
  critically ill septic patients (unbound, CrCl-adjusted), a different population; it is not used by the library.
  Note `R/drugs_ceftriaxone.R:46,67` cites a different Heffernan 2022 paper (Antimicrob Agents Chemother
  66:e02189-21, critically ill PK), not this one.
