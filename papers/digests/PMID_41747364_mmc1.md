# Hodge 2026 (supplement): methods, structure selection and covariate search of the sugammadex-rocuronium model

## Study
The supplement to the Hodge 2026 article (10 women at term, elective caesarean under general anaesthesia,
rocuronium then sugammadex 200 mg; see PMID_41747364.md). It adds no data or new parameter estimates. It gives:
- **Appendix A (PDF p1-2): methods.**
  - Monolix 2024R1 (SAEM), built on the published Ploeger and Kleijn model of free rocuronium, free sugammadex
    and the complex.
  - Complexation in the central compartment only, with the complex given sugammadex's PK.
  - Complex formation by kon and koff, parameterised as Kd = koff/kon with Kd 0.0559 µM and koff 2.04 h⁻¹ fixed
    (from Kleijn).
  - Concentrations converted to µM: rocuronium MW 529.7742 g/mol, sugammadex 2178 g/mol. The measured plasma
    concentration of each drug is modelled as free plus complex.
  - Log-normal IIV (exponential). Additive, proportional or combined residual error tried.
  - Selection by OFV, AIC and BICc (printed "corrected Bayesian infusion criteria", a typo).
  - Covariates explored: age, total and lean body weight, sex, BMI, serum creatinine, eGFR, haemoglobin, ALT,
    AST, GGT, ALP, bilirubin, albumin. Only those correlated at p < 0.05 were tested, stepwise: forward
    dOFV > 3.84, backward > 10.8.
  - Continuous covariates as power functions centred on the population median; categorical ones as fractional
    changes.
  - Evaluation: goodness-of-fit, VPC (500 simulations), nonparametric bootstrap (1000 replicates).
- **Appendix B (PDF p3-8): the final model.** Table S1, Figure S1 (structure), Table S2 (covariates), Figures
  S2-S3 (goodness-of-fit for rocuronium and sugammadex) and Figure S4 (individual fits, all 10 subjects).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. The supplement has no PD model (the article says there were too few train-of-four measurements).

## Covariates
- **Table S2 (p4-5):** the final model has total body weight on V1S, V1R and CLS. eGFR on CLS improved the OFV
  by only 3.34, below the 3.84 criterion, so it was not kept.
- No other covariate appears in Table S2. The others listed in Appendix A were presumably screened out at the
  correlation step, which is not tabulated.
- The exponents (1.82, 2.40, 0.89) and the centring value (median 78 kg) are in the article, not the supplement.

## Caveats
- Ten subjects. The covariate steps are small OFV differences on 131 observations.
- Figure S4 shows each subject's data ending about 6.5-9.5 h after rocuronium (subjects 3 and 5 at about
  2-2.5 h). The 24-h samples the article lists do not appear.
- Table S1's duplicate row label (see above).
- Figure S1's caption has small slips: k13,R is named twice, and "first peripheral" is used for sugammadex's
  single peripheral compartment.
- No serum creatinine or eGFR values are given. The article reports eGFR > 90 for all, so the renal covariate
  test had almost no range.
