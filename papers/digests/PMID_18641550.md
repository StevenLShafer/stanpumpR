# Rivas 2008: lamotrigine CL/F with valproate and inducers (German and Spanish TDM)

## Study
Retrospective TDM data, **600 adults** with epilepsy (497 Bonn, 103 Salamanca), normal renal and hepatic
function, Lamictal once or twice daily for > 1 month at a fixed dose; age 41.2 ± 17.1 y, dose 328.83 ± 183.51
mg/day (p 1). **1699** steady-state, mostly trough, concentrations (1495 German, 204 Spanish). Table 1 (p 2):
median weight 76.0 kg (Germany) and 70.0 kg (Spain); median LTG 6.4 and 4.1 mg/L; median dose 350 and
200 mg/day. Comedication frequencies are given as concentration points (German %): monotherapy 13.51, +VPA
30.37, +PHT 5.28, +CBZ 14.72, +PB or PRM 4.35, two or more inducers (IND) 22.01, +LEV 1.00, VPA + inducers
(VIND) 8.76. HPLC-UV (Spain), Chromsystems HPLC kit (Germany). NONMEM V, ADVAN2/TRANS2, FOCE with Laplace;
F 1, ka 1.3 /h and V 1.5 L/kg **fixed** (from Grasela 1999), so only CL/F was estimated; IIV on CL only.

## Model and parameters
Final model (p 4): CL (L/h) = theta1 x TBW x e^(-theta2 x VPA) x e^(theta3 x PHT) x e^(theta4 x (PB or PRM))
x e^(theta5 x CBZ) x e^(theta6 x IND), each indicator 0/1.

(IND), and nothing in the paper gives 0.070. Unresolved; Table 4 is used here.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. The paper uses 3-14 mg/L as the accepted therapeutic range and 9 mg/L as a target (pp 3, 5).

## Covariates
Weight (proportional) and comedication. Age, sex, origin (country) and levetiracetam were not significant.
Elderly (> 65 y; 7.4% of samples) post hoc CL 0.024 L/h/kg, 15% below younger adults, not retained. Valproate
plus inducers (VIND) was tested (Table 2, model 2) and dropped because its CI included zero; the authors say
induction and inhibition then roughly balance, near monotherapy CL (p 5).

## Caveats
Sparse troughs; V, ka and F fixed. No valproate or inducer concentrations or doses enter the model (all
categorical). IND pools any two or more of PHT, PB, PRM and CBZ; the paper does not break it down by
combination. The CBZ term is imprecise (SE 29.6%). Internal validation only (64 patients, 164 levels; MPE
0.56 ± 1.76 and 0.61 ± 2.03 mg/L in mono- and polytherapy).
