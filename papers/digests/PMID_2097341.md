# Gerdin 1990: maternal kinetics of morphine during labour

## Study
- Two groups of women (p.479, Table I on PDF p.2 / printed 480):
  - 6 non-pregnant female medical students, 24-32 y, 59-73 kg (64.2 +/- 5.3 kg); single IV morphine hydrochloride 0.07-0.15 mg/kg; 4 of them received the same dose IM two weeks later (their IV and IM values were averaged, p.481).
  - 13 healthy nulliparous parturients at term (38-42 wk), 16-35 y, 60-106 kg (80.5 +/- 13.5 kg); single 0.15 mg/kg in the first stage of labour, IM (n = 9) or IV (n = 4). Oxytocin IV in 6, local anaesthetics in 7. All delivered vaginally.
- Table I doses: parturients 9-15 mg (derived: 0.108-0.154 mg/kg; subjects 10 and 12 got 0.108 and 0.133 mg/kg, not 0.15); non-pregnant 4.5-10 mg (derived: 0.075-0.147 mg/kg).
- Sampling: venous plasma at about 2-5, 10, 20, 30, 45, 60, 90, 120, 150, 180, 240, 360 min, plus at delivery; umbilical vein and artery at birth. Dose-to-delivery interval 1.4-15.5 h.
- Assay: HPLC for morphine and M3G (specific; recovery 88% and 87%), detection limit 5 ng/mL (1 ng/mL = 3.5 nmol/L), plasma (p.481).
- Analysis: polyexponential fit (Siphar, weighted 1/y) to get ke; model-independent (moment) NCA: Cl = Dose/AUC, Vd = Cl/ke, AUC by linear trapezoids extrapolated with Clast/ke. IM bioavailability judged complete, so IM and IV parturients were pooled and Cl computed for IM doses too (p.481).

## Model and parameters
Non-compartmental; no compartmental model reported. Group values (mean +/- SD, abstract and Results p.481-482, confirmed by recomputing from the Table I image):

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- **MEAC / minimum analgesic concentration: none reported.** Analgesia was assessed only as most parturients being satisfied (p.483); no pain scores or concentration-effect data.
- **ke0 / t1/2ke0 / tPeak / hysteresis: none reported**; no effect end point (no pupil, EEG, ventilation or analgesia measurement).
- **Equianalgesic or potency ratio: none.**
- Fetal: umbilical arterial morphine was undetectable (< 5 ng/mL) in 6 of 7 neonates sampled; in subject 8 (born 1.4 h after the dose) UA 33 ng/mL vs maternal 13 ng/mL (Table I; text p.482-483). Apgar scores mostly 9-10.

## Covariates
Pregnancy (labour) only, as a group comparison. No weight or other covariate model; clearance is reported in absolute units although the groups differed in weight.

## Caveats
- Small groups (13 vs 6), unpaired; the comparator is non-pregnant women in this study, not any published model.
- The groups differed in weight (80.5 vs 64.2 kg); the absolute ratio is 1.70, the per-kg ratio 1.33 (derived).
- Short sampling (to 360 min, or until delivery) with a 5 ng/mL limit: the "elimination" half-lives are probably not the terminal phase, and the authors note their clearances (in both groups) exceed earlier studies and hepatic blood flow (p.483-484).
- IM and IV parturients pooled on an assumption of complete IM bioavailability.
- Labour, single dose; results apply to term/labour, not earlier pregnancy.

## Verification against stanpumpR
- **Factor 1.7** (`R/drugs_morphine.R:131`; registry :7; docs :74): 3.4/2.0 = 1.70 (derived 3358/1976 = 1.699 from the Table I image). **Matches** as a transcription of the absolute ratio. But the groups differ by 16 kg and the per-kg ratio is 1.33, while the code applies 1.7 on top of the patient's own size scaling (`R/drugs_morphine.R:189-196`). Whether that double-counts is an open question, which is why the card is partly-verified.
- **Baseline**: the comparator is this study's non-pregnant women (2.0 L/min), not the library's Lotsch 2002 model (1.23 L/min at the reference, `R/drugs_morphine.R:149`). The library applies the relative factor to its own baseline: 1.23 x 1.7 = 2.10 L/min, below the parturients' measured 3.4 L/min. The registry's comparator field states the comparator correctly; the code header does not mention it.
- **"Volumes and distribution clearances unchanged"** (`R/drugs_morphine.R:103-105`; registry): the paper reports only that the elimination-phase Vd was similar (derived means 200 vs 216 L). It has no compartmental volumes or distribution clearances, so keeping them unchanged is a modelling choice the paper does not contradict. **Cannot verify** beyond that.
- **"Decreased half-life"**: 43 vs 84 min. **Matches.**
- **Registry finding** text (3.4 vs 2.0, 43 vs 84, Vd unchanged, faster/larger M3G formation): **matches** the paper. The UGT2B7-induction sentence is not from this paper. `evidence_read` says "abstract"; the full text has now been read.
- **Third trimester** (registry `trimester`): the study was in labour at 38-42 weeks. **Matches** (term).
- MEAC (8 ng/mL) and tPeak (93.8 min) in `R/drugs_morphine.R:153-154` do not come from this paper, which has no PD.
