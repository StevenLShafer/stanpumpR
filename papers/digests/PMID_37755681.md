# Poweleit 2023: escitalopram and sertraline popPK in children and adolescents

## Study
Retrospective opportunistic-sampling study, Cincinnati Children's inpatient psychiatry, 2019-2022. Remnant plasma
from clinical blood draws within 24 h of a dose; dosing history from the EHR (administration records, prescriptions,
notes). Patients had clinical CYP2C19/CYP2D6 genotyping.
- Escitalopram: 288 patients, 315 samples (after exclusions from 303/330); age median 15 (10-18) y; weight median
  66.6 (22.9-223.1) kg; BSA 1.74 (0.91-3.07) m²; 72% female; daily dose median 10 (1-30) mg; 1 (1-4) samples/patient
  (Table 1). CYP2C19 PM 8, IM 75, NM 124, RM 78, UM 3 (Table 3).
- Sertraline: 255 patients, 265 samples; age 14 (5-18) y; weight 62.8 (21.9-138.2) kg; dose 75 (12.5-250) mg/day.
- Assay: UHPLC-MS/MS; LLOQ 0.03 ng/mL escitalopram, 0.1 ng/mL sertraline (100 µL plasma).
- NONMEM 7.5, FOCE-I, PsN/Pirana; simulations in MwPharm++. Most samples about 12 h and 24 h after the last dose.

## Model and parameters
One compartment, first-order absorption, proportional residual error, for both drugs. Ka fixed (absorption not
identifiable): 0.8 /h escitalopram, 0.5 /h sertraline (p. 1623).

Escitalopram (Table 2, final model, typical values; RSE% in brackets):
CL/F = 14.2 L/h/1.73 m² × θCYP2C19 × (BSA/1.73) × θinhibitor; V/F = 428 L/1.73 m² (V/F also normalised to BSA).
Base model (no covariates): CL/F 13.1 L/h, V/F 420 L.

Post hoc individual values, escitalopram (Table 3, median [IQR]): CL/F PM 4.08, IM 9.83, NM 13.81, RM 15.85,
UM 17.94 L/h; t½ PM 60.99 h, NM 21.81 h. Normalised Ctrough at 20 mg/day, BSA 1.73: PM 151.1, NM 38.0 ng/mL.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD model, no ke0. Simulated troughs compared with the adult AGNP reference range (escitalopram 15-80 ng/mL,
lab alert 160; sertraline 10-150 ng/mL, alert 300). Dose-reduction suggestion for PMs: 50-75% escitalopram, 25-50%
sertraline (abstract).

## Covariates
BSA (DuBois) linearly on escitalopram CL/F and V/F (normalised to 1.73 m²); CYP2C19 phenotype (5 levels for
escitalopram, 3 for sertraline) and CYP2C19 inhibitor on CL/F; weight power model for sertraline. Age, sex, race,
CYP2D6, THC/CBD not retained.

## Caveats
Mostly one sample per patient near 12 or 24 h, so Ka fixed and V/F poorly identified (shrinkage 68% escitalopram,
80% sertraline). Few PM (8) and UM (3). EHR dosing history uncertainty. Authors note their escitalopram CL/F and V/F
are lower than adult/adolescent literature values (20-40 L/h, 800-1200 L). Paediatric only (5-18 y).
