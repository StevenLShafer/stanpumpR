# Sakurai 2013: model-estimated D2 occupancy and cognition in CATIE

## Study
- Secondary analysis of CATIE: 410 subjects on risperidone, olanzapine or ziprasidone with plasma levels, neurocognitive testing at month 2, PANSS and SAS at month 1. Age 40.9 +/- 10.3 y (18-62), 73.9% male. Mean doses on test day: risperidone 3.9 +/- 1.3 mg, olanzapine 19.7 +/- 7.0 mg, ziprasidone 100.5 +/- 57.9 mg.
- Peak and trough plasma concentrations on the test day from established CATIE population PK models (one-compartment, first-order absorption; risperidone with a mixture on clearance; empirical Bayes estimates): 1236 risperidone/9-OH concentrations from 490 subjects, 1527 olanzapine (523), 568 ziprasidone (233).
- Occupancy from the Uchida 2011 one-site model; mean of peak and trough occupancy used; subjects split into four equal occupancy groups; multivariate GLM.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- Occupancy groups: 15.5-62.7%, 62.7-71.8%, 71.9-77.2%, 77.2-85.8% (n 102-103 each).
- Occupancy significantly associated with vigilance and the summary cognitive score, nonlinearly; impairment mainly above about 77% (dichotomisation thresholds 77-81% significant).
- Discussion: with the unconstrained model, 77% corresponds to 81%, 79% and 83% under the Emax-fixed models for risperidone, olanzapine and ziprasidone.
- No ke0 or time course. Not an opioid.

## Covariates
Age and education affected all domains; PANSS total affected processing speed.

## Caveats
Occupancy estimated, not measured; mean of peak and trough, not at test time; Uchida's model was 97% striatal data; free-drug formula comparison done only for olanzapine.
