# Nijstad 2023: pediatric population PK of aprepitant (extemporaneous suspension, capsules, IV fosaprepitant)

## Study
- Prospective observational study (NTR7720), Princess Maxima Center, Netherlands. Inclusion 0.5-18 years with a
  central line; Down syndrome and CYP3A4 substrates/inhibitors (7 days) or inducers (30 days) excluded.
- n = 49 (Table 1): median age 10.0 y (0.7-17.9), weight 32.5 kg (8.4-66.3), 20 female (41%).
  - Capsules n = 18: 14.4 y (10.4-17.9), 50.7 kg (40.5-66.3), 88 samples.
  - Extemporaneous 10 mg/mL suspension n = 26: 6.0 y (0.7-14.1), 22.1 kg (8.4-41.7), 123 samples.
  - IV fosaprepitant n = 5: 13.3 y (4.4-13.8), 41.7 kg (19.5-52.4), 24 samples; 1 female.
- Dosing: 3 mg/kg day 1 (aprepitant max 125 mg, fosaprepitant max 115 mg), 2 mg/kg days 2-3 (max 80 mg).
  Fosaprepitant assumed to convert rapidly and completely to aprepitant (Methods); no molar conversion stated.
- Sampling: up to 6 samples over 24 h (0.5, 1.5, 4, 6, 12, 24 h) plus a trough on day 2/3; 235 samples, 2 < LLOQ;
  86% of patients sampled on day 1.
- Assay: validated LC-MS/MS, plasma (total) aprepitant, LLOQ 0.1 ng/mL; first BLQ set to 0.05 ng/mL.
- NONMEM 7.3, FOCE-I, PsN 4.7, SIR for parameter uncertainty.

## Model and parameters
One compartment, linear elimination, three transit compartments for oral absorption (MAT estimated), allometric
weight a priori (exponents fixed 0.75 on CL, 1 on V). Two-compartment (Chain 2020 structure) tested and did not
improve fit. IIV on CL, MAT, F1; IOV on CL; proportional residual error. Joint fit of oral and IV data, so CL and
V are absolute (F estimated against the IV group). Table 2 (typical values, 95% CI by SIR):

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No antiemetic effect, receptor occupancy, ke0 or effect-site model; AUCs compared only with historical
cohorts. Not an opioid; no MEAC or potency ratio.

## Covariates
Body weight only (allometric, fixed exponents). Formulation on MAT (suspension x 0.606). Age/maturation on CL
tested (following Chain 2020) and not significant; only 3 patients < 2 years.

## Caveats
Small IV group (5 children, 4.4-13.8 y); fed state not recorded; no PD; one-compartment may miss early
distribution (sampling starts at 0.5 h); few infants; stability of the nanoparticle formulation not assessed;
Fig. 1 and GOF plots not readable/available here.
