# Allard 2020: population PK of eight factor VIII products

## Study
Retrospective multicentre (13 French centres) analysis of routine-care data, 2012-2019. 258 patients (87 children, 171 adults): 244 severe, 11 moderate, 3 minor haemophilia A, no inhibitors. 935 FVIII:C concentrations, median 4 samples per patient (1-11), taken pre-dose and 15 min to 96 h after infusion. 10.6% of samples were below the LLOQ (0.004-0.01 IU/mL, median 0.01). FVIII:C was measured by one-stage clotting assay, with three reagent/analyser pairs depending on centre. Products: Elocta n=136 (the only extended half-life product, EHL), Advate 44, Kogenate 34, Refacto 18, Factane 8, Kovaltry 7, Novoeight 6, Afstyla 5. Median dose 2750 IU (500-5000). Median age 30 y (3-77); median weight 64 kg (15.1-130) (Table 1, text layer). Monolix 2019R1 (SAEM); BLQ data handled as censored. Steady state was imposed, with four virtual earlier doses, to absorb endogenous and residual FVIII. 1000 bootstraps.

## Model and parameters
Two-compartment linear model, combined error, log-normal IIV. Table 2, PDF p6 (image confirmed). Units are mL and mL/h, not per kg.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD. The end point is FVIII:C activity (IU/mL). Half-life is derived from individual Bayes estimates: mean EHL/SHL ratio 1.48 after switching to Elocta (44 switchers, text p9-10); the half-life fell in 4 of 44 patients (9%). Median Elocta half-life 14.8 h, against 19 h in A-LONG (text p10). No MEAC, ke0 or equipotency ratio. Targets of 0.01 and 0.02 IU/mL are used only in the worked example (p11).

## Covariates
Weight, age and Elocta status on Cl; weight on V1. Brand and structure were not kept. FFM, vWF and ABO were tested secondarily and left out (Table S2, not in the PDF).

## Caveats
- Retrospective sparse data; 25% of patients had a single sample.
- Afstyla, Factane, Kovaltry and Novoeight each have 10 or fewer patients.
- Refacto may be better described by a one-compartment model.
- The abstract calls Q a "fixed random effect", but Table 2 gives Q an RSE, so it was estimated.
- Adding vWF or ABO made the age effect non-significant.
