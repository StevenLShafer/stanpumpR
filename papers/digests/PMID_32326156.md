# Allard 2020: one population model for eight factor VIII concentrates

## Study
- Retrospective, 13 French haemophilia centres, 2012-2019 (Methods 2.1). 258 patients, 87 children and adolescents and 171 adults, age 30 (3-77) years, weight 64 (15.1-130) kg (Table 1). Severe 244, moderate 11, minor 3; no inhibitor during the PK study. Sex not printed (haemophilia A, X-linked).
- Products (Table 1): Elocta 136, Advate 44, Kogenate 34, Refacto 18, Factane 8, Kovaltry 7, Novoeight 6, Afstyla 5. Dose 2750 (500-5000) IU.
- FVIII:C by the one-stage clotting assay (three reagent/analyser pairs), 935 levels, 1-11 per patient (median 4), 15 min to 96 h after a dose; LLOQ 0.004-0.01 IU/mL, 10.6% BLQ, handled by a truncated distribution.
- Predose levels handled by simulating four prior doses to steady state; an unknown predose in a severe patient taken as BLQ (< 0.01 IU/mL). An endogenous production term was tested and not kept (Results 3.2).
- Monolix 2019R1, SAEM; covariates centred on the medians; 1000 bootstraps.

## Model and parameters
Two compartments, linear elimination, combined error. Table 2 and the equations under it:

    CL = 204 mL/h x (age/30)^-0.214 x (weight/64)^0.75 x exp(-0.394 EHL)
    V1 = 2640 mL x (weight/64)^0.827
    Q  = 135 mL/h;  V2 = 339 mL

EHL = 1 for Elocta, 0 otherwise. The weight exponent on CL fixed at 0.75 (estimated 0.779, RSE 9%). Brand (over-parameterised for the small groups) and structure (no gain) were tested and not kept (Results 3.3). vWF, ABO and FFM were explored but not kept, being missing for 130, 51 and 61 patients (Methods 2.2.2; Table S2, not read).

## Checks
- At the medians the model gives CL 3.19 mL/kg/h, Vss 46.5 mL/kg, half-lives 1.5 and 10.4 h; ADVATE's label (Table 6, 20 adults): CL 3 +/- 1 mL/kg/h, Vss 0.4 dL/kg, half-life 12.0 +/- 4.2 h, recovery 2.6 +/- 0.5 IU/dL per IU/kg (model 2.42 at the end of a bolus).
- Switching to Elocta raised the individual half-lives 1.48-fold on average (Results 3.6); exp(0.394) = 1.48 on clearance.

## How stanpumpR uses it

Age held at 3 years. Plotted in IU/dL as the Patient Profile's Factor VIII baseline plus the infused activity (R/factorBaselines.R); blank, 0.5 IU/dL. Band: ADVATE's major-surgery target, 80-120 IU/dL.
