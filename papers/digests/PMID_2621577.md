# Boucher 1989: fosphenytoin (ACC-9653) IV and IM pharmacokinetics in patients

## Study
- 10 adult outpatients (9 M, 1 F) on chronic oral phenytoin monotherapy (Dilantin Kapseals 100-200 mg twice daily,
  stable predose levels); age 26-38 y (mean 30.6), weight 61.0-83.6 kg (mean 72.3 ± 7.35).
- Week 1: IV ACC-9653 (fosphenytoin), dose equivalent to the patient's twice-daily phenytoin dose (150-300 mg of
  prodrug, most 240 mg; 75 mg/mL prodrug ≡ 50 mg/mL phenytoin sodium), infused at 75 mg/min in place of the morning
  oral dose. Week 2: same dose IM into gluteus maximus.
- Plasma at 0, 5, 10, 15, 20, 30, 45 min, 1, 2, 3, 6, 12 h (IV); 0, 1, 1.5, 2, 2.5, 3, 3.5, 6, 12 h (IM).
  HPLC (UV 214 nm) for prodrug (0.1-200 µg/mL) and total phenytoin (0.05-25 µg/mL).
- Individual one- and two-compartment fits (weighted nonlinear least squares, ADAPT-type; AIC), plus
  non-compartmental analysis. IM F = AUC(im)/AUC(iv).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No effect or concentration-response data. Not an opioid.

## Covariates
None analysed (n = 10).

## Caveats
- Patients on chronic phenytoin (enzyme-induced state possible); young adults, almost all men.
- IM F highly variable (68.7-134.1%), attributed to few measurable IM concentrations.
- Phenytoin itself was not modelled; free phenytoin not measured; protein-binding displacement not studied.
- The 8.0 min half-life is the α phase of the prodrug in 8 of 10 patients, interpreted by the authors as conversion.
