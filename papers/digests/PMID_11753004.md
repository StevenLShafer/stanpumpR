# Absalom 2002: BIS closed-loop propofol anaesthesia

## Study
Ten adults (ASA I-II, age 18-80, BMI <= 30), elective hip or knee replacement, combined epidural (10 mL 0.5% bupivacaine to T8) plus general anaesthesia with propofol; no neuromuscular blocker, spontaneous breathing via laryngeal mask, no systemic opioid (PDF p1, 3; printed 67, 69). Table 2 (PDF p3): age 67 (11) yr, 4 M / 6 F, weight 79 (11) kg, height 168 (9) cm, mean (SD).
Controller: BIS (A-1000 monitor) read every 5 s, PID algorithm, target propofol concentration adjusted every 30 s from the sum of the previous six adjustments; maximum change 2 ug/mL (ASA I-II) or 1 ug/mL (ASA III); overall ceiling 15 ug/mL; default minimum 1 ug/mL; automatic reduction of 0.2 or 0.3 ug/mL every 3 min as a safety feature (PDF p2-3). The actuator was a plasma-targeted TCI using the Diprifusor pharmacokinetic parameters (PDF p2). Induction was manual TCI, start target 2 ug/mL, raised by 0.5 ug/mL steps to loss of consciousness. Closed-loop control started during surgery with the BIS at that moment as set point.
Performance measures: MDPE, MDAPE, wobble (Varvel 1992 definitions), mean offset = measured BIS minus set point (PDF p3).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: BIS (hypnotic depth), clinical adequacy, and movement. No EC50, Emax or gamma was estimated. No ke0 was estimated in this study; the Discussion (PDF p6, printed 72) cites others: Billard 1997 keo 0.2 /min (alfentanil/propofol/midazolam EEG paper, ref 18; derived: t1/2 keo = ln2/0.2 = 3.5 min) and Schnider 1999 time to peak effect 1.6 min, and says the peak-effect delay after a plasma change is of the order of 1.6-4.5 min (ref 20, Struys 2000). Calculated Ce values in Table 3 come from an effect-site model that the paper does not name or parameterise. Hysteresis explanation offered for BIS/target oscillation in 3 patients (4, 5, 10): plasma-targeted TCI ignores effect-site lag. No MEAC or analgesic concentration (propofol is not an opioid); no potency ratio. Observation: one patient moved at plasma target 2.2 ug/mL, BIS rose 50 to 84 in 90 s, and responded to a target rise to 5 ug/mL.

## Covariates
None analysed. Two patients had no sedative premedication (patients 6 and 8); performance was said to be similar to the rest.

## Caveats
n = 10, no comparison group, epidural block above T8 only, no opioid, hence stimulation above the block was the cause of the one failure; set points were set per patient from BIS at "adequate anaesthesia"; constants were fixed (software did not allow gain changes); authors suggest effect-site targeting or adaptive control.
