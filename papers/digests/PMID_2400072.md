# Ali-Melkkilä 1990 (Anaesthesia): IM glycopyrronium in parturients

## Study
- **Patients:** 8 women having caesarean section for fetopelvic disproportion (Table 1, p635). Age 30.4 (4.5) y,
  height 163.1 (6.1) cm, weight 69.8 (5.8) kg, ASA 1.
- **Dose:** glycopyrronium 6 µg/kg into the right deltoid, about 60 min before induction. The dose basis (bromide
  salt or cation) is not stated.
- **Anaesthesia:** combined spinal-epidural with bupivacaine, crystalloid 2000 mL, and an etilefrine infusion (mean
  2.8 mg).
- **Sampling:**
  - Venous plasma (K-EDTA) at the times in Fig. 1, out to 8 h.
  - Urine by catheter (0-1, 1-3, 3-6 and 6-8 h).
  - Lumbar CSF at about 60 min.
  - Maternal vein, umbilical vein and artery, and amniotic fluid at delivery, 85.5 (25.2) min after the dose.
- **Assay:** radioreceptor assay (tritiated N-methylscopolamine on rat-brain muscarinic receptors). Sensitivity 60
  pg/mL; precision under 15% at the limit and under 8% at 600 pg/mL. It measures anything that binds muscarinic
  receptors.
- **Analysis:** AUC by the trapezoidal rule. Total body clearance = dose/AUC, assuming complete absorption.
- **PD measures:** heart rate, BP, sedation VAS and dry-mouth VAS.

- **CSF:** below detection.
- **Cord concentrations:** UV and UA fell with time from dose to delivery (r = -0.723 and -0.712).
- **Comparators** (Discussion p636), from the same group's earlier work:
  - The IM study in nonpregnant gynaecological surgical patients (ref 4, Br J Anaesth, "in press"; see
    `PMID_2378771.md`): Tmax 16 min, systemic clearance 0.58 L/h/kg.
  - An IV study (ref 5, Acta Anaesthesiol Scand 1989;33:513): systemic clearance 0.54 L/h/kg. The population is not
    described here.
- **The authors' explanation:** the shorter Tmax, larger clearance and much shorter t½ possibly reflect pregnancy's
  higher cardiac output, plasma volume, and hepatic and renal clearance.
- **Arithmetic checks (derived):**
  - Mean dose 6 × 69.8 = 419 µg; 419 / 5.61 = 74.7 L/h = 1.07 L/h/kg. This agrees with the mean of individual
    values, 1.12.
  - V ≈ CL × t½ / ln2 = 1.12 × 0.557 / 0.693 ≈ 0.90 L/kg.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- Heart rate rose significantly by 10 min (P < 0.05). Dry-mouth VAS rose by 30 min (P < 0.05).
- BP and sedation did not change significantly.
- There was no significant correlation between concentration and effect. The authors attribute this to a lag of
  some minutes between plasma concentration and effect.
- No ke0 or EC50.

## Covariates
None. One group only.

## Caveats
- **Excretion at 3 h:** the abstract says 48.3%, but Table 2 gives 33.68 + 15.66 = 49.34% (derived).
- **AUC:** truncated at 8 h. This is negligible given a t½ of 33 min.
- **Absorption:** IM absorption is assumed complete, so CL is really CL/F.
- **Assay:** the radioreceptor assay counts active metabolites and enantiomers.
- **Comparison:** across studies. The earlier IM comparator group was older (mean 61 y) and had vaginal
  hysterectomy; see `PMID_2378771.md`.
- **Sampling:** the 8 h window cannot show the long terminal phase seen in later LC-MS/MS work.
