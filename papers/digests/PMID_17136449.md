# Vermeulen 2007: parent-metabolite population PK of oral risperidone and 9-hydroxyrisperidone

## Study
- 407 patients (5359 concentrations) from 4 Phase 1 studies (74 patients with schizophrenia/schizoaffective disorder; serial sampling; single 4 mg doses or 3 mg BID) and 3 Phase 3 bipolar mania trials (333 patients; 1-6 mg once daily; sparse, 3 samples each) (Table I). Index 70% / qualification 30% split.
- Assays: RIA (active moiety and risperidone; LOQ 0.20 and 0.10 ng/mL; 9-OH by difference) or LC-MS/MS (LOQ 0.10 ng/mL each); cross-validated. Plasma.
- NONMEM V, ADVAN5, FO method. Exponential IIV/IOV, additive residual error (Table II reports residual variability as %SD).

## Model and parameters
Shared depot receives the dose by zero-order input (duration D1) after a lag, then first-order KA split into risperidone central (1 - FP) and 9-OH central (FP). Two-compartment disposition for each; risperidone cleared to 9-OH (CLPM) and to other metabolites (CLP); 9-OH cleared by CLM. V4/F fixed equal to V2/F (not identifiable). Three-way mixture (PM/IM/EM) on FP and CLPM in lieu of CYP2D6 genotype.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None (PK only). Active moiety quasi-clearance simulated; carbamazepine raised it about 1.4-fold; small effects of phenotype, race and total protein judged clinically irrelevant. No receptor occupancy or ke0. Not an opioid.

## Covariates
Carbamazepine on CLP (2.84 to 6.49 L/h) and CLM (5.99 to 6.22 L/h); study type (single vs multiple dose) on the IM/EM mixture proportions. Weight, LBM, BMI, age, sex, race, creatinine clearance, albumin, AST: not significant.

## Caveats
FO estimation; V4 = V2 assumption makes FP an apparent fraction; phenotype assigned by mixture, no genotype; very large absorption variability; creatinine clearance mostly normal.
