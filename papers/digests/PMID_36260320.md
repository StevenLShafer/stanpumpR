# Li 2023: population PK of levetiracetam in pregnant women with epilepsy (Shenyang)

## Study
- Retrospective single-centre TDM study, Shengjing Hospital, Shenyang, China, January 2017 to July 2021 (p. 1153). Pregnant women with epilepsy on levetiracetam (Keppra) at a constant dose for > 1 week; no nonpregnant group.
- 37 women, 166 concentrations (1-9 per woman, median 5). Table 1 (rendered, PDF p. 4), median (range): age 30 (21-37) y, height 160 (150-169) cm, weight 65 (47-90) kg, BMI 24.61, GA 23.71 (4.14-39.43) weeks; samples in trimester 1 / 2 / 3: 31 / 72 / 63. Daily dose 1250 (250-3250) mg; concentration 6.78 (1.20-33.00) ug/mL; sampling 11.32 (0.58-30.67) h after dose. Monotherapy 22 (59.5%); inducer co-medication (carbamazepine, oxcarbazepine) 8, non-inducer (lamotrigine) 7. Serum creatinine 43.4 umol/L; Cockcroft-Gault CrCL 173.31 mL/min.
- Mostly steady-state troughs; plasma levetiracetam by HPLC-MS/MS, 0.5-128 ug/mL, LLOQ 0.5 ug/mL, CV < 15%.
- NONMEM 7.3.0 (FOCE-I), PsN, Pirana; one compartment, first-order absorption, ka FIXED at 2.44 /h (Pigeolet 2007); proportional residual error. 26 covariates screened; GA tried both continuous and by trimester (T1 < 14 wk, T2 14-28, T3 > 28). NPDE (2000) and bootstrap (2000; 1997 successful). Monte Carlo dosing (27 regimens) against a therapeutic range of 3.80-13.60 ug/mL, the 5th-95th percentiles of the cohort's individual target concentrations.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No concentration-effect model. Therapeutic window for the simulations, 3.80-13.60 ug/mL, is cohort-derived (lower than the ILAE 12-46 ug/mL, which the authors consider inappropriate in pregnancy). Text-stated second-trimester recommendations: 1000 mg/day < 50 kg, 1250 mg/day 56-60 kg, 1500 mg/day 61-65 kg, 1750 mg/day 66-71 kg, 2000 mg/day > 71 kg (p. 1157); 500 mg/day in T1 underdoses at any weight.

## Covariates
TBW (power, centred on 65 kg) and trimester on CL/F. GA as a continuous covariate was significant but the trimester model was preferred (AIC 549.547 vs 557.974; GA-model RSEs > 30%). Not significant: age, inducers, CrCL, eGFR, ALT, bilirubin (Table 2). The authors attribute the absent renal effect to young women with uniformly high CrCL.

## Caveats
- No nonpregnant or postpartum reference: the trimester terms are relative to the first trimester, which is already pregnant; the authors note clearance rises from the first trimester.
- 37 women, single centre, retrospective, no external validation; ka fixed, V/F poorly identified (bootstrap upper bound 130.79 L).
- The third-trimester effect is not distinguishable from 1 in the bootstrap (0.94-1.74).
- Supplement (2026-10-27): CrCL (Cockcroft-Gault) correlates 0.9 with TBW in this cohort (Figure S2), so once TBW is
  in the model a renal term has little left to explain; the authors' "uniformly high CrCL" is not the only reason
  it was not retained. The Figure S1B histogram counts 36 women, the text 37.
- The authors' comparison of 3.82 L/h with nonpregnant adult popPK values (2.74-4.33 L/h) is across studies and populations.
