# Hemiclor (chlorthalidone 12.5 mg) US label, 2025

## Study
This is a DailyMed SPL for HEMICLOR, chlorthalidone 12.5 mg tablets (Ingenus; NDA 218647; marketing start
03/17/2025; revised 3/2025). It is the chlorthalidone label the registry links to, so there is no source file
problem.
- **Indication:** hypertension in adults.
- **Dosage (2.1, p3):** start at 12.5 or 25 mg orally with food once daily. Double every 2-4 weeks as needed, up to
  100 mg once daily. Doses above 100 mg usually add nothing.
- **Description (11, p7):** racemic, MW 338.8 g/mol.
- **Efficacy (14, p8):** the label rests efficacy of 12.5 mg on Sica et al. 2012, the same factorial trial whose PK
  Tsai 2016 modelled.

## Model and parameters

- **Distribution:** the label links the binding to an increased affinity for erythrocyte carbonic anhydrase. The
  sentence is awkwardly worded, and it gives no blood:plasma ratio.
- **Excretion:** mainly as unchanged drug in urine. Non-renal routes are not clarified.
- **Not given:** no Tmax, Cmax, AUC, CL, V or bioavailability.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- **Diuresis (12.2):** onset at a mean of 2.6 h, lasting up to 72 h, with dose-related hypokalaemia and rises in
  uric acid and glucose.
- No concentration-effect relation and no ke0.

## Covariates
- **Renal:** substantially renally excreted, so use caution in renal impairment and the elderly (8.5).
- **Hepatic:** electrolyte shifts may precipitate hepatic coma (8.6).
- **Pediatrics:** not established (8.4).

## Caveats
- Descriptive label text only.
- The erythrocyte statement is qualitative.
