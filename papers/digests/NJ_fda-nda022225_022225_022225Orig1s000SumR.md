# FDA Summary Review, Bridion (sugammadex), NDA 022225, 2015

## Study
Not a study: the division's summary review for the fourth review cycle, which led to approval (section 13,
"Approval"). It reprints the reviewer's April 21, 2015 memo and, inside it, tables from the July 18, 2008 review.
Regulatory history (PDF p. 3): not-approvable letter July 31, 2008 (hypersensitivity/anaphylaxis and coagulation);
complete response with study P06042 (Dec 2012); complete response letter Sept 20, 2013 after site inspections
found protocol deviations in P06042; study P101 submitted Oct 22, 2014; current submission June 19, 2015.

Pivotal efficacy studies (PDF p. 23, rendered table):

| Study | Scenario | Sugammadex | Comparators | n randomised | Primary end point |
|---|---|---|---|---:|---|
| 301 (Europe) | "shallow" block, return of T2 | 2 mg/kg | neostigmine (rocuronium and vecuronium arms) | 196 | T4/T1 0.9 |
| 302 (US) | "profound" block, 1-2 post-tetanic counts | 4 mg/kg | neostigmine (rocuronium and vecuronium arms) | 182 | T4/T1 0.9 |
| 310 (Europe) | "shallow" block, return of T2 | 2 mg/kg | cisatracurium/neostigmine | 84 | T4/T1 0.9 |
| 303 (US, Canada) | "immediate" reversal, 3 min after rocuronium | 16 mg/kg | succinylcholine, no reversal | 115 | T1 0.1 |

## Model and parameters
No PK model. Results table (PDF p. 23), time in min:s, statistic not stated, all p < 0.0001:
- 301: sugammadex 1:29 (rocuronium), 2:48 (vecuronium); comparator 18:30, 16:48.
- 302: sugammadex 2:52 (rocuronium), 4:28 (vecuronium); comparator 50:22, 66:12.
- 303: sugammadex 4:22; comparator 7:04 (spontaneous recovery from succinylcholine).
- 310: sugammadex 2:02; comparator 8:46.

The efficacy summary (PDF p. 24) gives 303 as 4.3 vs 7.2 min "on average" to T1 10%, and, as secondary end points,
5.4 min to T4/T1 0.9 after sugammadex vs 10.9 min to T1 90% after succinylcholine, adding that the clinical
relevance of T1 10% was not shown.

Renal impairment (Dr. Nallani's review, reproduced on PDF p. 6): sugammadex 4 mg/kg in Part 2, n = 6 per group;
AUC0-inf GMR (90% CI) vs healthy 2.42 (1.84, 3.17) moderate (CLcr 30-<50) and 5.42 (4.12, 7.11) severe (CLcr <30);
Cmax GMR 0.92 (0.72, 1.18) and 0.94 (0.73, 1.21). Part 1 was not analysed because of dosing irregularities.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End points are neuromuscular: T4/T1 (train-of-four) ratio 0.9, or T1 return to 10% (study 303). No plasma
concentration-effect relation. Hypersensitivity (DPARP consult quoted, PDF p. 9): in P101, 299 healthy volunteers
given sugammadex, one anaphylaxis (0.33%), on the first dose of 16 mg/kg; DPARP concludes sugammadex causes
anaphylaxis and hypersensitivity events.

## Covariates
Renal function (above). Nothing else quantified in the pages read.

## Caveats
- The results table does not say whether its times are means, geometric means or medians; its sugammadex times
  differ from the 2024 label's medians (e.g. 302 rocuronium 2:52 here vs 2.7 min in the label), and the text gives
  303 as 4.3 vs 7.2 min where the table has 4:22 vs 7:04. Use each document's numbers with its own statistic.
- The comparator column lists one time per NMBA row; its assignment to the rocuronium and vecuronium neostigmine
  arms is by row position.
- Image-only pages were not read.
