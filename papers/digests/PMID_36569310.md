# Wang 2022: joint apparent-oral PK of venlafaxine and O-desmethylvenlafaxine (ODV)

## Study
- Study 1: single-centre, single-dose, randomized crossover bioequivalence study; 24 healthy Chinese men, age
  23.13 +/- 2.47 (18-27) y, weight 62.5 +/- 6.9 (54-80) kg; 50 mg venlafaxine hydrochloride (2 x 25 mg capsules from two
  manufacturers; Table 1 calls the formulation rapid-release tablets), fasted, 7-day washout; 15 samples pre-dose to
  36 h; venlafaxine only measured (LC-MS/MS, calibrators 0.2-200 ng/mL); 664 concentrations. Fig. 2 (left) plots
  venlafaxine only.
- Study 2: retrospective TDM, 127 psychiatric inpatients (Guangzhou, 2018-2021), 44.9 % male, age 37.86 +/- 17.59
  (14-86) y, weight 61.95 +/- 11.02 (38-93) kg; daily dose 132.86 +/- 64.20 (25-300) mg; 83.62 % sustained-release,
  16.38 % rapid-release; mostly morning troughs; VEN and ODV by LC-MS/MS (4-400 and 20-2,000 ng/mL); 804 concentrations.
- NONMEM 7.3, ADVAN6 (TOL 6), FOCE-I; PsN 4.2.0; GOF and NPDE evaluation. Concentrations modelled in µmol/L (Fig. 2
  axes, read from the page image).

## Model and parameters
One compartment each for VEN and ODV (Fig. 1, read from image): Depot -> VEN at Ka x (1 - FP); Depot -> ODV at Ka x FP;
VEN -> ODV by K23 (the VEN compartment's only exit); ODV out by K30. FP = fraction of the absorbed dose converted to ODV
in first pass. Ka fixed at 0.63 /h from an unpublished two-compartment VEN model (0.5x and 2x tested, mild effect).
K23 and K30 are not tabulated.

- Text (p. 8): healthy CL 81 L/h VEN, 22 L/h ODV; volumes 628 and 238 L; patients' VEN CL 31 L/h.
- Discussion: healthy VEN half-life 5.4 h and absorption time 1.6 h; V/F 10.05 L/kg. (derived: ln2 x 628/80.9 = 5.38 h;

- The IIV column is headed %CV but holds values like 0.219 and 1.38 (presumably omega or CV as a fraction).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD. Introduction cites the AGNP therapeutic reference range for VEN + ODV, 100-400 ng/mL, laboratory alert
800 ng/mL (Hiemke 2018). Not an opioid; no MEAC, ke0 or potency ratio.

## Covariates
Final: morbid (patient) state on CL/F; amisulpride on CL/F and CLM/F. Tested and not retained: formulation, sex, age,
body weight, smoking, drinking, valproate, quetiapine, clozapine, olanzapine, risperidone. CYP2D6/CYP2C19 genotype not
available.

## Caveats
- Ka fixed from an unpublished model; FP and K23 identifiability rest on troughs plus a VEN-only rich study.
- Patients' data are mostly troughs; within-interval shape comes from healthy single-dose VEN profiles.
- IV data favour three (VEN) and two (ODV) compartments; one compartment used for simplicity (authors).
- Doses were venlafaxine hydrochloride in Study 1; no salt/base conversion is stated.
- Chinese population; mechanism of the morbidity and amisulpride effects unknown.

## Verification against stanpumpR
- Table 2 transcription (`R/drugs_venlafaxine.R:24-30, 109-113`; `R/drugs_desvenlafaxine.R:23-24`): 80.9 L/h, 628 L,
  22.1 L/h, 238 L, Ka 0.63 /h fixed, FP 0.048, morbid x(1-0.617), amisulpride x(1-0.392) and x(1+0.593). **matches**.
- Unit conversion: cl1 = L/h / 60 to L/min; ka 0.63/60 per min (`:147, :156`). **matches**.
- K23 = CL/V (`:32-48, :178`): Fig. 1 (image) gives the VEN compartment a single exit, K23 to ODV, so CL/F is the
  conversion; consistent with the stated 5.4 h half-life. **matches**.
- First pass (`:50-57, :158, :179`): Fig. 1 splits the depot Ka(1-FP) to VEN and Ka*FP to ODV; bioavailability 0.952
  plus firstPassFraction 0.048 reproduces it. **matches**.
- Molar basis (`:59-64`): Fig. 2 axes are µmol/L, so conversion mole for mole; mwRatio 263.38/277.40 = 0.9495 correct
  for the bases. **matches**. The note that doses are base "as labelled" is the package's choice; the paper's Study 1
  doses were the hydrochloride, and whether it converted to base is not stated (possible ~13 % scale in CL/F, V/F).
- Population (`:66-74`): patients' CL/F 30.98 L/h, t½ 14.0 h; ODV 7.5 h; ODV clearance not affected by health status.
  **matches**.
- Study description (`:17-22`; help `venlafaxine.md`): 24 healthy Chinese men, 50 mg, 15 samples to 36 h, VEN only;
  127 inpatients 14-86 y, 25-300 mg/day, 84 % sustained release. **matches** (the 50 mg was the hydrochloride).
- "Ka is the immediate-release value" (`:78-80`): the paper says only that Ka came from an unpublished two-compartment
  VEN model; that it is IR is plausible (Study 1) but not stated. **cannot verify**.
- Help `venlafaxine.md` (150 mg/day): patient averages 192 ng/mL VEN and 269 ODV, sum 460 **match** (derived:
  0.952 x 150 / (30.98 x 24) = 0.192 mg/L; 150 x 0.9495 / (22.1 x 24) = 0.2685 mg/L). "The same dose in the healthy
  volunteers' model averages about 300": (derived: 74 + 269 = 342 ng/mL) loosely stated; about 340 would be accurate.
- **Discrepancy, citation**: `R/drugs_venlafaxine.R:102` and `:117` (the `reference` string shown in the app) give the
  first author as "Wang X"; the first author is Zhanzhang Wang ("Wang Z"); Xipei Wang is fourth author. Help pages use
  "Wang and colleagues" and are unaffected.
