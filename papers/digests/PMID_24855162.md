# Goudie 2014: insulin aspart PK across type 1 diabetic pregnancy (letter)

PDF p1 = e121, p2 = e122. A research letter (eLetter, observations) on **insulin aspart** by CSII, which stanpumpR does
not model.

## Study
- Two studies: study 1 (ISRCTN62568875), 10 women in early (12-16 weeks) and late (28-32 weeks) gestation, sedentary;
  study 2 (ISRCTN50385583), 12 women twice in mid gestation (19 and 23 weeks), with postprandial walking and 50 min of
  treadmill walking after breakfast (PDF p1).
- n = 22: age 32 (4.5) y; diabetes 18 (8.7) y; gestation 22 (6.5) weeks; BMI 27.0 (3.3); HbA1c 7.1% (1.0). 88 five-hour
  post-meal profiles (40 study 1, 48 study 2); 1,302 plasma insulin measurements, immunochemiluminometric assay (PDF p1).
- "A two-compartment model" estimated tmax, MCR, accumulation rate Ia and postprandial concentration Ib; the letter gives
  no equations (the full method is in the companion Stat Med paper, Goudie 2015, PMID 26013427).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None (PK only).

## Covariates
tmax: study-2 breakfast (probability 0.97), gestation (0.76), diabetes duration (0.68). MCR: no factor above 0.5. Ia: study-2
breakfast, peak bolus rate, total daily dose (Table 1). Text: about 50% slower absorption at 38 than at 8 weeks of
gestation (derived check: 1.016^30 = 1.61).

## Caveats
- MCR units: the letter labels MCR mL/kg/min (text and Table 1) with values 0.022-0.027, while Goudie 2015 Table II gives
  near-identical values (0.022-0.028) in L/kg/min. 0.025 L/kg/min is 25 mL/kg/min. The two labels cannot both be right;
  which is intended is not stated, so left unresolved here.
- Within-patient CV is printed as "tmax 29% and MCR 29% and 23%" (PDF p1), an extra value with no label.
- The gestation slope (1.6% per week) differs from Goudie 2015's 1.7% (a later, fuller analysis).
- Aspart only; small n; conclusions on exercise rest on one study arm.

## Verification against stanpumpR
- **docs/pregnancy.md:180-181**: citation (authors, title, Diabetes Care 2014;37:e121-e122, PMID, DOI) **matches**. The
  description is only partly right: early and late measurements in the same women exist only in study 1 (10 women);
  study 2 is mid gestation, and the gestational effect is reported as a covariate slope, not an early-vs-late
  comparison. The entry is still listed as "to obtain" though the registry records it as read. **partly-verified**.
- **inst/extdata/pregnancyRegistry.csv:116-123** (`to_obtain`): "letter, no usable model" and "aspart, not a Tham insulin"
  **match**. "The equations are in Haidar 2013" is **not supported** by the letter: it cites Haidar 2013 (ref 5) only for
  the non-pregnant variability comparison, and the model equations are printed in Goudie 2015 (Eq 1-2), which the same
  registry row cites. Minor **discrepancy**; no numeric value in the code depends on it.
