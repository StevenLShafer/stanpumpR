# Kruizinga 2022: clonazepam population PK in plasma and saliva

## Study
Open-label single-dose study, CHDR Leiden, June-July 2020 (NL8792). 20 healthy subjects aged 18-30: Table 1 (text
layer jumbled; read by magnitude) age 22.4 (SD 2.8) y, 45% male (9 of 20, Results), 100% Caucasian, weight 67.8
(8.3) kg, height 175.1 (7.3) cm, BMI 22.2 (2.4). Clonazepam oral solution (Rivotril drops in lemonade), 0.5 mg
(n = 10) or 1.0 mg (n = 10). Paired plasma and saliva at 0.5, 1, 2, 4, 6, 8, 24, 48 h. LC-MS/MS; plasma calibrated
range 2-72.3 ug/L (LLOQ 2 ug/L), saliva LOQ 0.1 ug/L; no post-dose sample below LOQ. NONMEM 7.3, sequential
plasma-then-saliva approach, then simultaneous fit; simulations in R/mrgsolve.

## Model and parameters
Plasma: two-compartment (dOFV -17.34 vs one), first-order absorption with a two-population mixture on ka; no lag
reported. Allometry fixed: CL and Q x (WT/70)^0.75, volumes x (WT/70)^1. Only oral data, so all are apparent
(relative to F). Saliva: contamination compartment (1 mL, first-order elimination) plus saturable saliva:plasma
ratio (eq 2-3). Table 2 estimates (column alignment of SE/RSE is garbled in the text layer; estimates are clear):

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No ke0, EC50 or therapeutic range reported; the study is PK only.

## Covariates
Weight only (fixed allometry). Albumin tested for the saliva ratio, no effect. "No covariates for the subgroups" of
the absorption mixture. Sex was not reported as tested.

## Caveats
Oral solution, not tablets; single dose; sampling only to 48 h (short relative to a ~40 h half-life, likely to bias
the terminal phase); some subjects at Cmax by the first 30 min sample, so absorption poorly characterised; young
healthy adults only; total plasma concentrations. Saliva contaminated for 4 h after the solution.
