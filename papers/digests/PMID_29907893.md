# Carlsson Petri 2018: semaglutide s.c. population PK in the SUSTAIN trials

## Study
Pooled population PK analysis of five phase 3 trials of once-weekly subcutaneous semaglutide in adults with
type 2 diabetes: **SUSTAIN 1, 2, 3, 6 and SUSTAIN-Japan** (Table 1, PDF p3 / printed 1535; Methods p2 / 1534;
confirmed by the column headings of ESM Table S1).
Maintenance doses 0.5 or 1.0 mg weekly after escalation (0.25 mg x 4 weeks, then 0.5 mg; 1.0 mg after a further
4 weeks); SUSTAIN 3 used 1.0 mg only. Treatment 30-104 weeks. Sparse sampling at about weeks 4, 8, 16, 30 and 56
(SUSTAIN 6 also week 2 and end of treatment), no timing restriction relative to the dose (p4 / 1536).
- 1683 scheduled, 1612 analysed, 6781 concentrations, mean 4.2 per subject; 8.3% of samples excluded (p6 / 1538).
- Table 2 (p7 / 1539): 927 men (57.5%), 685 women; age 18-64 y 1203, 65-74 y 353, >= 75 y 56 (range 20-86 y);
  weight mean 86.2 (SD 22.5) kg, range 39.7-198.3; BMI 31.1 (7.1), range 16.3-72.8; White 838, Asian 658, Black 73;
  Hispanic 241; renal function normal 997, mild 533, moderate 49, severe 33 (eGFR categories; equation not named);
  0.5 mg 634, 1.0 mg 978.
- ESM Table S1 (supplement, 2026-10-27) splits these by trial: n 237 / 417 / 366 / 118 / 474 (SUSTAIN 1 / 2 / 3 /
  6 / Japan); mean age 57 (SD 10.6) y overall, 65.4 y in SUSTAIN 6; mean weight 93.8 / 89.4 / 96.2 / 88.3 / 71.3 kg;
  SUSTAIN-Japan is 100% Asian and contributes 474 of the 658 Asian subjects; all 33 severe renal impairment and 28
  of 49 moderate are in SUSTAIN 6; SUSTAIN 3 is 100% 1.0 mg. Duration of diabetes 8.1 (6.6) y, range 0-48.9; HbA1c
  8.2 (1.0)%, range 5.9-13.1. American Indian/Alaska Native (2) and unknown race (41) were merged with white for the
  covariate analysis (footnote a). Footnote b gives the renal cut-offs in "mL/min" (the article gives mL/min/1.73
  m2) and again names no equation. Table S1 gives **no count by injection site**.
- Assay: LC-MS/MS after protein precipitation, LLOQ 0.729 nmol/L (p4 / 1536).
- NONMEM 7.1.2, FOCE+I, proportional residual error, untransformed concentrations; pre-specified full model
  approach (base model without covariates, full model with all covariates); VPC with PsN (p5-6 / 1537-1538).

## Model and parameters
One compartment, first-order absorption and elimination; covariates on CL/F only. ka fixed from richly sampled
clinical pharmacology data (Novo Nordisk, data on file). Reference subject: white non-Hispanic woman under 65 y,
85 kg (pre-specified, the expected approximate median), normal renal function, abdominal injection, 1.0 mg weekly
(p5 / 1537). The row labels of ESM Table S3 (male; ages 65-74 and > 74; 0.5 mg; Black; Asian; Hispanic; thigh;
upper arm; mild, moderate, severe renal) are exactly the complements of that reference (supplement, 2026-10-27);
the 85 kg is stated only in the article (p5), the supplement does not restate it.

Covariate model (p5 / 1537, rendered): CL/F = CLtyp x E_dose x E_weight x E_sex x E_age x E_GFR x E_race x
E_ethnicity x E_inj.site x exp(eta); E_weight = (weight / 85 kg)^theta_wt; every categorical effect theta^indicator.
Age groups in the text are "< 64, 65-74, >= 75" (p5); Table 2 and Table S1 label the first group 18-64 years;
Table S3 labels the oldest "> 74 years". No control stream is in the supplement.

**Base model, ESM Table S2** (supplement, 2026-10-27): ka 0.0286 /h fixed; CL/F 0.0480 L/h (95% CI 0.0471-0.0488,
RSE 0.899%, IIV 26.6% CV, shrinkage 7.9%); V/F 12.5 L (11.5-13.4, RSE 3.76%, IIV 30.4%, shrinkage 70.2%);
proportional residual error 23.5% CV (shrinkage 10.4%).

**Full model, ESM Table S3** (supplement, 2026-10-27): ka 0.0286 /h fixed; CL/F 0.0478 L/h (0.0468-0.0488, RSE
1.06%, IIV 12.9% CV, shrinkage 25%); V/F 12.2 L (12.1-12.4, RSE 0.487%, IIV 37.3%, shrinkage 58.1%); proportional
residual error 23.8% CV (shrinkage 8.0%). Covariate estimates (multiplicative on CL/F; the weight row is the
exponent), with 95% CI and RSE: listed in the yaml block below.

**Sensitivity, ESM Table S4** (supplement, 2026-10-27): with ka fixed at 0.0215 (-25%) or 0.0358 /h (+25%), CL/F
is 0.0479 / 0.0478 L/h and every covariate estimate moves by at most 0.004 (weight exponent 0.778 / 0.772; Black
0.973 / 0.970); **V/F moves to 10.3 / 13.6 L** (derived: -16% / +11%). Excluding |CWRES| > 4: all estimates within
0.007 of the final (Black 0.981, Hispanic 1.07). The article's statement (p6 / 1538) is that there were no relevant
differences "in terms of exposure", which Table S4 supports for CL/F (hence Cavg) but not for V/F.

Where the text and the table describe Cavg differently: the Results text (p6-7 / 1538-1539) calls 15.8 and 29.8
nmol/L the Cavg "for the reference subject profile", but Table 3 is titled a summary of model-derived exposures from
the PK population and gives each as a geometric mean with a 95% CI and a range over 634 and 978 subjects. The
table's labelling is the more specific. The two readings are numerically close (derived: the reference subject's
1 mg / (0.0478 L/h x 168 h) = 124.5 ng/mL = 30.3 nmol/L at 4113.58 g/mol, and the population mean weight is
86.2 kg).

Fig. 2 against Table S3 (derived, supplement 2026-10-27): every relative exposure in Fig. 2 equals 1/(Table S3
factor) to its printed two decimals (male 0.962, 65-74 1.012, > 74 1.041, Black 1.027, Asian 1.011, Hispanic 0.943,
upper arm 0.926, mild 1.055, moderate 1.047, severe 1.087; (85/55)^0.774 = 1.40, (85/127)^0.774 = 0.733) except
thigh: 1/1.04 = 0.962 against Fig. 2's 0.97. The two printed values are both reachable only if the unrounded thigh
factor lies in 1.035-1.036 (derived: 1/0.965 = 1.0363). The supplement gives nothing more on this.

Other derived checks: half-life ln2 x 12.2 / 0.0478 = 177 h (the paper says "approximately 1 week", p11 / 1543;
75% of steady state after 2 weekly doses). The Discussion (p10 / 1542) quotes CL/F about 0.05 L/h and V/F about
12.5 L from richly sampled clinical pharmacology trials (data on file).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No HbA1c, glucose, body-weight or exposure-response model is in this paper or its supplement; the Conclusion
defers that to a later exposure-response analysis. Simulations: a missed dose lowers the next trough by 48%; a dose
5 days late lowers the trough 37% and raises the peak 14% (Fig. 5, p11 / 1543). 29 subjects had anti-semaglutide
antibodies, with no apparent effect on exposure (data on file).

## Covariates
Tested on CL/F: dose, baseline weight (continuous), sex, age group, renal group (eGFR >= 90, 60-89, 30-59, < 30
mL/min/1.73 m2 in the article; "mL/min" in ESM Table S1 footnote b), race, ethnicity, injection site (most used
site; abdomen reference, thigh and upper arm estimated). Only body weight was judged important (90% CI of relative
exposure outside 0.80-1.25). The coefficients are in ESM Table S3 (above). Exposure was constant over 2 years (Fig.
S1, ESM; caption only read). The authors estimate that the average ~5% weight loss on treatment raises exposure
about 4% (10% loss: 7.8%).

Injection site (supplement, 2026-10-27): the supplement's only content on it is the two Table S3 rows (thigh 1.04,
95% CI 0.993-1.08; upper arm 1.08, 1.03-1.12, both on CL/F relative to the abdomen) and the same values unchanged in
all three Table S4 sensitivity runs. It gives no distribution of subjects by site and no text.

## Caveats
- Sparse steady-state sampling; V/F poorly informed (shrinkage 58.1% in the full model, Table S3); ka fixed for
  everyone, and V/F is the one estimate that follows ka (Table S4); dosing history only for the two doses before
  each sample; all samples assumed at steady state except SUSTAIN 6 week 2.
- Severe renal impairment came from SUSTAIN 6 only (n = 33, Table S1); Asian race is mostly SUSTAIN-Japan (474 of
  658), so trial and race are partly confounded.
- The supplement holds no control stream, so THETA order and coding could not be checked beyond the article's
  equations on p5 and the Table S3 row labels.
- Industry-sponsored (Novo Nordisk); all authors are employees.
