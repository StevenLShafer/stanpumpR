# Irwin 1997: patient-maintained propofol sedation by TCI

## Study
- 36 unpremedicated Chinese adults (Hong Kong) having local/regional anaesthesia, ASA I:II:III = 16:10:10, 25 M : 11 F, age mean 54.4 (20-75) y, weight mean 58.2 (22-79) kg, surgery 111.6 (10-280) min (Table 2, PDF p.2 / printed 526). Vascular 20, urology 8, orthopaedic 8; epidural, subarachnoid, caudal, brachial plexus or local (Table 3).
- Ohmeda 9000 pump with a backbar running a propofol PK model (their ref 10, White & Kenny 1990; parameters not given in this paper) using age and weight. The pump displays a calculated effect-site concentration.
- Start target 1 ug/mL. Patient raises the target by 0.2 ug/mL with a double press, 2 min lockout, maximum 3 ug/mL. With no demand the target falls 0.2 ug/mL after 6 min (first 20 min) or 12 min (21-60 min, then every 12 min) down to a floor of 0.2 ug/mL.
- Sedation by modified Steward score (Table 1) every 10 min; 1-4 blood samples per patient (propofol by HPLC, Plummer 1987; "blood" concentration), 60 measured vs target pairs.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: modified Steward sedation score 0-9 (Table 1). Score 6 or more judged adequate by the authors.
- Target (predicted blood) concentration by score, median (range), ug/mL (Table 4, confirmed on rendered page): score 3 n=1 1.1; 4 n=1 1.1; 5 n=4 1.1 (0.8-1.3); 6 n=42 0.8 (0.2-2.0); 7 n=115 0.9 (0.2-2.4); 8 n=134 0.8 (0.2-2.0); 9 n=74 0.7 (0.0-2.2). Median target 0.8-0.9 ug/mL for scores 6-8 (abstract). The authors suggest 0.8 ug/mL as a starting target.
- These are predicted (TCI-model) concentrations, not measured; measured concentrations ran below them (bias -47%).
- No ke0, t1/2ke0, tPeak, MEAC or potency ratio is reported. Table 4's caption mentions effect concentrations but only the target column is printed.
- Respiratory rate fell from about 19 to about 14 breaths/min over 120 min (Fig 2); lowest recorded 10. 8/36 (22%) needed oxygen for SpO2 < 92%. Little change in BP or HR (Figs 3-4).

## Covariates
None analysed. Age and weight are inputs to the TCI model only.

## Caveats
- Predicted not measured concentrations; the TCI model over-predicted markedly (bias -47%, inaccuracy 48%, r 0.508), worse than in earlier evaluations by the same group (their refs 10, 11). The sedation-target relationship therefore applies to this model's scale.
- Older, mixed-ASA Chinese population; patients self-titrated, so targets reflect preference as well as effect. Several samples per patient treated as independent (n=60 from 36 patients).
