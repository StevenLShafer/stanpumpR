# Genis-Najera 2024: bioequivalence of two clonazepam 2 mg tablets

## Study
Phase I randomized open-label single-dose two-period crossover, 21-day washout, DEBBIOM (Mexico), Nov-Dec 2021,
Sanofi-funded. 30 healthy Mexican volunteers enrolled (20 M, 10 F), 29 in the PK analysis; age 29.80 +/- 8.97 y,
weight 63.87 +/- 10.94 kg, height 1.66 +/- 0.09 m, BMI 23.06 +/- 2.44 (Table 2). Fasted >= 10 h; one 2 mg tablet with
250 mL water; test = Sanofi Aventis Mexico, reference = Roche Mexico. 22 samples per period: 0, 0.5, 1, 1.33, 1.67,
2, 2.33, 2.67, 3, 3.33, 3.67, 4, 4.5, 5, 6, 8, 10, 12, 16, 24, 48, 72 h. Plasma by UFLC-MS/MS, linear 0.25-50 ng/mL
(LLOQ 0.25). Non-compartmental analysis, Phoenix WinNonlin 8.1.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None measured. Adverse events: 7 (1 dizziness, 6 asymptomatic hypotension at 1.5-2.5 h).
Background statements (Introduction, from refs): bioavailability >80%, Tmax 1-4 h, protein binding about 85%,
t1/2 about 30-40 h, distribution half-life about 0.5-1 h.

## Covariates
None analysed; authors state no sex influence was observed (no analysis shown).

## Caveats
Bioequivalence design: NCA only, truncated at 72 h (about 1.6 half-lives), so AUC0-inf is extrapolated heavily
(about a third). Sanofi-authored. Healthy, mostly male Mexican adults.
