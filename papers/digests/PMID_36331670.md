# Atrux-Tallau 2022: oral immediate-release morphine formulations (ODT vs tablet, capsule, solution)

## Study
Three randomized, single-dose, laboratory-blinded phase I crossovers in fasted healthy adults (Altasciences, Laval,
Canada; sponsor Ethypharm). Eligibility 18-50 y, weight ≥ 60 kg, BMI 18.5-30 (men) / 20-30 (women), non-smokers.
- Pilot (Nov-Dec 2018): 4-period, 4-sequence; 20 enrolled, 17 completed. 30 mg morphine sulfate as ODT (1 × 30 mg),
  Sevredol tablets (3 × 10 mg), Actiskenan capsule (30 mg), Oramorph solution (30 mg/5 mL). Samples 0.08-24 h.
- Pivotal 30 mg: fully replicated 2-treatment 4-period crossover; 42 enrolled, 39 completed (PK population n = 41,
  Table 1); ODT 1 × 30 mg vs Sevredol 3 × 10 mg; 80 dosings per treatment in Table 2. Samples 0.08-36 h.
- Pivotal 10 mg: same design; 42 enrolled, 40 completed (PK n = 42); 2 × 5 mg ODT vs 10 mg Sevredol; 82 dosings.
Naltrexone 50 mg PO 1 h before dosing in both 30 mg studies; none in the 10 mg study. 7-day washout.
Demographics (Table 1, mean (SD)): weight 71.2 (8.5), 75.6 (9.4), 68.1 (12.4) kg; age 33, 36, 41 y.
Assay: HPLC-MS/MS of plasma morphine, LLOQ 50.0 pg/mL. Noncompartmental analysis, Phoenix WinNonlin 8.0.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD measured. No MEAC, no ke0. Discussion cites Lötsch 2005 review for morphine t½ke0 1.6 to 4.8 h (not this
study's data). No potency ratio. 30 mg doses given under naltrexone block, so AEs there are not opioid-effect data.

## Covariates
None analysed.

## Caveats
Healthy fasted volunteers, industry-sponsored; naltrexone co-administration at 30 mg; pilot n = 17; solution
compared only in the pilot; two extreme Tmax values from one vomiting subject. Doses are mg morphine sulfate.
