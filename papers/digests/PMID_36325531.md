# Eichentopf 2022: therapeutic reference range for escitalopram

## Study
Systematic review and meta-analysis (AGNP TDM task force; PROSPERO CRD42020215873) of escitalopram (ESC)
blood levels versus clinical effect, side effects, moderating factors and SERT occupancy. Four databases
searched October 2020, updated March 2022. 31 articles / 30 studies (2006-2021): 9 concentration/effect,
12 moderating factors, 9 SERT occupancy (PDF p2-3). Random-effects meta-analysis in R 4.0.3 (metafor,
meta). Evidence for a concentration/effect relationship graded low (level C) (PDF p2, p4). No PK model.

## Model and parameters
No PK model. Pooled values (serum/plasma, ng/mL):

- Proposed target range **20-40 ng/mL** for antidepressant efficacy (abstract PDF p2; discussion p7-8;
  conclusion p9). Lower limit from Florio 2017 ROC threshold 20.5 ng/mL (AUC 0.817, 70 depressed patients;
  PDF p4, p7). Upper limit described as a therapeutic maximum, not a tolerability limit (p8-9).
- Combined median 23.7 ng/mL, IQR 15-39 ng/mL across 7 studies (n = 4,295), trough 24 h after intake
  (PDF p7, confirmed on image).
- Combined mean 31 ng/mL (95% CI 27, 36) at mean dose 16 mg/day, 12 studies, n = 5,031 (PDF p6, image).
- Responder IQRs (median): 21-44 (29) ng/mL, n = 360; 24-54 (36) ng/mL, n = 34 (p7).
- Mean dose did not correlate with mean concentration across 12 studies (p = 0.07) (p6).
- Mean C/D ratios in naturalistic TDM studies 1.09-2.47 (ng/mL)/(mg/day); guideline (Hiemke 2018) 1.05
  (Table 4, PDF p8, read from rotated image): combined mean C/D Reis (22) 1.23, Reis (21) 1.09,
  Scherf-Clavel 2.22, Warrings 2.47, Hiemke 1.05. Expected at 20 mg/day: 25, 22, 44, 49, 21 ng/mL.
- Previous AGNP 2017 range 15-80 ng/mL; expected steady-state means on 10-20 mg/day 11-21 ng/mL
  (PDF p1-2, image p1).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Not an opioid: no MEAC, ke0, t½ke0, tPeak or potency ratio. End points: HAMD-21 response (Florio), SERT
occupancy by PET. Table 2 (PDF p6, image): EC80 Arakawa 16.0 ng/mL (thalamus, healthy, single dose);
Kim 2017 17.2 (putamen) and 11.6 (dorsal raphe) (healthy, single dose); Lanzenberger 17.5 (thalamus, MDD,
steady state, from Baldinger 2014). EC80 computed from reported EC50. Concentrations above 17 ng/mL give
>80% SERT occupancy (p7). No concentration-response for side effects except dry mouth (level C).

## Covariates
CYP2C19 (Table 3, PDF p7): non-functional alleles +47%/+150% (1 or 2 copies, Bråten); *2/*2 +230%
(Jukić); def/def +470%, *1/def +90% (Rudberg 2008); *17 carriers lower. Sex: +9% to +40% in women, +80% C/D
in one. Age: +91% over 65 y at 10 mg/day (Reis), +40% C/D over 65 y (Waade). Smoking −24%. Body weight: no
effect (4 studies).

## Caveats
Mostly naturalistic, flexibly dosed TDM data; no RCT designed for concentration-effect; lower limit rests
on one cohort (n = 70). Table 2 footnote marks Kim 2017 as SPECT, though Kim 2017 used [11C]DASB PET (as
its own title in ref. 35 says): a likely labelling error in the paper. Table 4 is printed rotated; the text
layer scrambles it.
