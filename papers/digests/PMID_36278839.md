# Tang 2023: population PK of tenecteplase

## Study
- TIMI 10B (phase II), acute myocardial infarction; PK subset of 103 tenecteplase patients, 30, 40 or 50 mg as one IV bolus (the 50 mg arm suspended for intracranial haemorrhage and replaced by 40 mg). Samples at baseline and 2, 30, 60, 90, 120, 180 and 360 min; 785 observations, all above the LLOQ (8 ng/mL, sandwich ELISA, which reads endogenous t-PA and possibly bound drug).
- Median weight 81.8 kg (quartiles 52.2-71.9-81.8-92.8-188.5 kg, Figure 4), median CRCL-NM 82.6 mL/min/70 kg. Demographics in Table S1 (not read). All patients on aspirin and heparin.
- NONMEM 7.4.4 FOCE-I; covariates by SCM; external check in N1811s (acute ischaemic stroke, 0.1-0.5 mg/kg, one sample at 1 h, 75 patients).

## Model and parameters
Two compartments, linear. Fixed allometric exponents (1 on volumes, 0.75 on clearances; run 13). The ELISA's endogenous signal is a baseline BS added to each prediction (Equation 2). Final model, run 34 (Table 1; Equation 9):

    CL = (91.3 + 0.322 x (CRCL-NM - 82.6)) x (WT/70)^0.75   mL/min
    Vc = 3610 x WT/70 mL     Q = 10.0 x (WT/70)^0.75 mL/min     Vp = 914 x WT/70 mL

CRCL-NM: Cockcroft-Gault normalised to 70 kg. The paper computes a 29.2% share for the term at the median, but its Discussion (p. 206) treats the association with caution: a renal contribution is biologically unlikely (liver metabolism the major pathway; about 65 kDa), and stepwise covariate selection can over-estimate an effect or select a collinear covariate. Its alternative model (age and race on CL) gave nearly the same typical CL and Vc (Tables S3, S4).

## Checks
- CL 103 mL/min at 81.8 kg (Discussion): 91.3 x (81.8/70)^0.75 = 102.6.
- Simulated median AUCs at 81.8 kg (Results): 0.1 mg/kg 79.7, 0.5 mg/kg 398, 50 mg 487 ug.min/mL; dose/CL gives 79.4, 397, 485.
- External check: the model over-predicted N1811s means 1.39-fold, not explained by CRCL-NM or weight (p. 207). N1811s dose-normalised mean 837 vs TIMI 10B 1146 ng/mL at 60 min (Figure 6).
- Label (TNKase, BLA 103909, revised 2/2025, 12.3): Vc 4.22-5.43 L, Vss 6.12-8.01 L, t1/2 90-130 min, CL 99-119 mL/min; "Liver metabolism is the major clearance mechanism". Model at 81.8 kg: CL 103 mL/min, Vc 4.22 L, Vss 5.29 L, terminal t1/2 77 min (derived): the clearance agrees, the terminal phase is shorter than the label's.
