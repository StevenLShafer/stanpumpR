# Hirai 2025: digoxin apparent clearance with CLcr and N-desethylamiodarone

PDF page n = printed page 2826 + n.

## Study
Same centre and team as Hirai 2022 (Tokyo Women's Medical University, Jan 2008-Nov 2016): 368 adults with AF and
HF on oral digoxin, 3288 trough concentrations (at least 6 h post dose); 48 (13%) on amiodarone (median 100 mg/day),
with 651 plasma amiodarone/desethylamiodarone (DEA) levels (HPLC-UV) from after 180 days of amiodarone. Table 1
(medians [IQR]): women 141 (38%); age 69 [59-77] y; weight 56 [48-65] kg; SCr 0.93 [0.75-1.12] mg/dL; CLcr 57.3
[41.5-77.2] mL/min; dose 0.125 mg in 280 (76%). Median SDC 0.75 [0.54-1.02] ng/mL; amiodarone 610, DEA 644 ng/mL.
COBAS digoxin assay (LOD 0.3 ng/mL). Phoenix NLME 8.4; IIV on CL/F only; additive residual error; forward p < .05,
backward p < .01; amiodarone/DEA tested as linear time-varying covariates.

## Model and parameters
One compartment, first-order absorption; Vd/F FIXED 6.0 L/kg and ka FIXED 1.0 /h (troughs only). Final (Table 2,
PDF p5 rendered): CL/F = 5.92 x (CLcr/Median)^0.281 x (1 - 0.00030 x DEA [ng/mL]). The median is not printed in the
equation; Table 1's median CLcr is 57.3 mL/min.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None; toxic-range cut-offs 0.9 and 1.2 ng/mL (Table 4).

## Covariates
CLcr (power), plasma DEA (linear; preferred to amiodarone by OFV). Sex, age, weight excluded as CLcr inputs.

## Caveats
Troughs only, V and ka fixed; few amiodarone patients (48); additive error; amiodarone dose low (Japanese
practice); simulated means include IIV, so they exceed typical-patient values.
