# Earp 2017: esomeprazole pediatric approval by exposure matching

FDA reviewers' account of how oral and IV esomeprazole doses for children (1 month to 17 years) were chosen. No concentration-time table
or population PK parameter table is printed; the population PK models are described in words and cited to FDA reviews
(refs 17-19, PAGE 2010 abstract 1905).

## Study
- Pooled sponsor studies: oral esomeprazole PK in 117 children (birth to 17 y); IV esomeprazole (3-minute injections, once daily 4 days)
  in 50 children (1 month to 17 y); adult comparators 24 (oral) and 42 (IV, 3-min or 30-min) subjects at 20 or 40 mg (p. 274 / printed 274).
  6 to 21 children per dose group; predose plus 4-20 postdose samples each.
- Doses (Table 1, printed p. 274): mg/kg doses of 0.5, 0.25 and 1.0 for the younger ages and fixed 5/10, 10/20 and 20/40 mg low/high
  oral doses and 10/20 mg IV for 12-17 y and other bands. The text layer scrambles the table, so which dose belongs to which age
  band and route was NOT read; see open_questions.
- Intragastric pH: 52 neonates/infants and 66 adults; none in children 1-18 y.
- Models: oral = linear 1-compartment with first-order absorption (NONMEM V, 117 subjects); IV = linear 2-compartment (NONMEM, 50 subjects).
  Individual Bayesian post-hoc AUC and Cmax used for simulation.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End points: change from baseline in % of first 24 h with gastric pH > 4, and change in median gastric pH over 24 h, plotted against AUC
  (Fig. 1, p. 275): infants and adults overlay, similar PK-PD.
- No ke0, t1/2ke0, tPeak, EC50, Emax or MEAC reported. No potency ratio. Concentrations in nmol/L (AUC in nmol-h/mL or umol-h/L as labelled in the figures).
- Target exposure: adult 20 mg AUC (90% CI of observed adult 20 mg data), on the plateau of the pH response (Supplemental 3, not seen).
- Prose Cmax statement: geometric-mean Cmax in children on 0.5 mg/kg, 10 mg and 20 mg IV 3-minute injection is 3.2-, 2.6- and 3.1-fold the adult
  20 mg 3-minute value, attributed to smaller volume of distribution (p. 275). Lengthening infusion to 10 min matches adult 40 mg 3-min Cmax;
  to 30 min matches adult 20 mg Cmax (Fig. 2, p. 275-276); levels fall to adult 20 mg level within 2.4 min post-dose for a 10-min infusion.
- Oral weight-based regimen for 1 mo-<2 y: 2.5 mg (3-5 kg), 5 mg (>5-7.5 kg), 10 mg (>7.5-12 kg), once daily (p. 276).
- IV regimen: weight-based with a 55 kg cut point for children >1 y; 0.5 mg/kg for 1 mo-<1 y (p. 275).

## Covariates
Body weight (CL, V); age on CL in the FDA-modified oral model. No equation printed.

## Caveats
- Review-style paper: no PK parameter table, no SEs; models are in FDA reviews not in the article.
- pH data only in infants and adults, none 1-18 y; adult-child similarity is inferred from infants.
- Intended for erosive esophagitis, not GERD generally (as stated by the authors).
