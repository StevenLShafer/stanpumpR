# Scheen 2015: incretin-based therapies in chronic kidney disease (PK review)

## Study
Narrative (not systematic) review; Medline January 2005 to 1 July 2014 (p2). Tabulates the dedicated renal
impairment PK studies of DPP-4 inhibitors (Table 1, pp4-6) and GLP-1 receptor agonists (Table 2, p12), with
clinical-use summaries and dosing recommendations (Tables 3-4). Renal categories by creatinine clearance: normal
> 80, mild 51-80, moderate 31-50, severe ≤ 30 mL/min, ESRD on haemodialysis (p3). Values are means ± SD (a) or
geometric means [CV%] (b); GMR = geometric mean ratio vs normal.

Other content: exenatide LAR (once weekly) in mild (n 56) and moderate (n 10) CKD vs 84 normal (FDA report,
unpublished): average steady-state concentration +24% mild, +53% moderate; predicted up to 2-fold at CrCL 30

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None quantitative. Clinical HbA1c and safety outcomes summarised; recommendations: exenatide not in severe CKD or
ESRD, liraglutide with caution beyond mild CKD, lixisenatide not in severe CKD/ESRD, linagliptin no adjustment,
vildagliptin 50 mg once daily in moderate/severe CKD.

## Covariates
Renal function categories only (Cockcroft-Gault CrCL in most studies).

## Caveats
- Secondary source: every number is copied from the original studies (several only as abstracts: lixisenatide Liu
  et al., alogliptin).
- The exenatide pooled CL/F sentence (p11) repeats Linnebjerg's abstract, in which mild and moderate are
  transposed relative to Linnebjerg's Table 5 and Results; Scheen's Table 2 holds only the single-study values.
- Scheen's exenatide GMR rows (0.81, 0.97, 3.37 for AUC) are Linnebjerg's dose-weight-normalised Table 4 ratios,
  shown next to un-normalised AUCs at different doses.
