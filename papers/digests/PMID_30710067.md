# Gong 2019: vinorelbine safety and PK in liver dysfunction

## Study
Single-institution phase I pilot (NCT00540982, City of Hope), April 1997 to April 2009. 47 patients with treatment-refractory solid tumours (median age 58, range 32-82; 23 F, 24 M; colorectal 36.2%), ECOG 0-1, creatinine <1.5 mg/dL. Weekly vinorelbine as a short IV infusion (10 min maximum) at 7.5-30 mg/m2, assigned by liver function (NCI-ODWG style): normal (bilirubin <1.5 mg/dL, AST/ALT and ALK <1.5 x ULN), mild (bilirubin <1.5 with AST/ALT 1.5-2.5 x ULN or ALK 1.5-3 x ULN), moderate (bilirubin 1.5-3.0 mg/dL and/or AST/ALT >2.5 x or ALK >3 x ULN), severe (bilirubin >3.0 mg/dL). Dose groups: 30 mg/m2 normal; 20 mg/m2 mild and severe; 15 mg/m2 mild and moderate; 30 mg/m2 mild and moderate; 7.5 mg/m2 severe (Tables 3-4). PK after the first dose only: plasma before and at 1, 1.25, 1.5, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12, 24 h after the START of infusion, by HPLC or LC-MS/MS (Jehl 1991); noncompartmental AUC0-24 by linear trapezoids, normalised to 30 mg/m2. Indocyanine green (0.5 mg/kg IV push) clearance measured the day before in 25 patients.

No clearance, volume or half-life reported. Safety (47 patients): 368 treatment-related AEs at incidence of 2% or more; 108 grade 3-4, myelosuppression 34.3% of them, neutropenia 21.3%; 15 grade 4 events; no grade 5. By group, 33 (30.6%) grade 3-4 events at 20 mg/m2 severe, 22 (20.4%) at 15 mg/m2 moderate, 21 (19.4%) at 30 mg/m2 moderate, 9 (8.3%) at 7.5 mg/m2 severe.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No concentration-effect model, ke0, EC50 or MEAC. Toxicity (neutropenia, grade 3-4 AEs) is reported by dose and liver group only, not against exposure. ICG clearance fell with worse liver tests (bilirubin p = .0008; AST p = .008) but did not correlate with vinorelbine PK (p = .30). Free drug and the active metabolite 4-O-deacetylvinorelbine were not measured. Not an opioid paper.

## Covariates
Liver-function group only (and ICG clearance, not predictive). No BSA, weight, sex or renal covariate analysis; BSA not reported.

## Caveats
- Small and unbalanced: PK in 30 of 47 patients, 4 each in mild and severe groups; groups at different doses; no formal statistics on AE rates.
- Assay changed over the 1997-2009 enrolment (HPLC or LC-MS/MS). Single occasion sampling to 24 h.
- Median AUC in the normal group was lower than in the impaired groups combined (p = .06), so a modest clearance reduction cannot be excluded; the authors conclude PK is not significantly affected.
- Plasma, not whole blood; total, not free, drug.
