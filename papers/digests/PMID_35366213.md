# Morse 2022: acetaminophen and ibuprofen popPK with normal fat mass, formulation and food

## Study
- Pooled data from 4 AFT Pharmaceuticals studies (Maxigesic products), 116 healthy adults: age 24 (18-49) y,
  weight 70.8 (49-116) kg, height 173 (156-199) cm, BMI 23.9 (18.6-31.4), 93 M / 23 F (Table 1, median (range)).
  - Study 1 (MXIV-01, n = 30, 5-way crossover): IV acetaminophen 1000 mg +/- ibuprofen 300 mg, half-dose IV
    combination, two tablets (500/150 each); IV over 15 min; fasted.
  - Study 2 (MXIV-06, n = 30, 4-way): IV combination, Ofirmev 1000 mg over 15 min, Caldolor (ibuprofen 400 mg)
    over 30 min, three 325/97.5 tablets; fasted.
  - Studies 3 and 4 (MX-14a fasted, MX-14b fed, n = 28 each, 4-way): suspension, sachet, two tablets, three
    325 tablets (about 1000/300 mg).
- 6095 acetaminophen and 6046 ibuprofen concentrations; LC-MS/MS, LLOQ 0.05 mg/L for both (BLQ set to zero).
- NONMEM 7.5 FOCE-I; 100 bootstrap replicates; pcVPC.
- Size: Fsize = (WT/70)^EXP, EXP 3/4 for clearances, 1 for volumes (Eq. 1). FFM by Janmahasatian (Eq. 2; men
  WHSmax 42.92, WHS50 30.93; women 37.99, 35.98 kg/m2). NFM = FFM + Ffat x (TBW - FFM) (Eqs. 3-4).
  NFMstd uses FFM 56.1 kg for a 70 kg, 1.76 m man; CL = CLstd x (NFM/NFMstd)^3/4 (Eq. 5).

## Model and parameters
Two-compartment, first-order elimination, first-order absorption with lag for oral forms; fed/fasted factors
on absorption half-life and lag by formulation (fasted tablet is the reference). Estimates (bootstrap columns are
scrambled in the text layer; estimates and 95% CIs read by row).

Table 4 (simulated medians, 10th-90th percentiles; acetaminophen 1000 mg, ibuprofen 300 mg; stated to be
calculated from CL, V, ka and F): acetaminophen tablet fasted Cmax 12.8 mg/L at 0.61 h (fed 10.9 at 0.88 h);
ibuprofen tablet fasted 24.1 mg/L at 0.94 h (fed 20.1 at 1.2 h).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD estimated. Simulations used the Hannam 2018 combined acetaminophen/ibuprofen model (Supplementary Table S4,
not in this text): time to a 2-point VAS reduction differed by only 5-10 min across formulations. No ke0, EC50,
MEAC or potency ratio reported in the main text.

## Covariates
Acetaminophen: CL on NFM (Ffat 0.816), volumes on TBW. Ibuprofen: CL on NFM (Ffat 0.863), "volume of distribution"
on NFM (Ffat 0.718; given once in Table 3). Which size descriptor Q2 uses is not stated beyond Eq. 1 (TBW with
exponent 3/4). Feeding and formulation act on absorption half-life and lag.

## Caveats
- Abstract errors: it gives "central volume" as 43.5 (acetaminophen) and 10.5 (ibuprofen) L/h/70 kg; Table 2/3
  show these are Q2; V1 is 43.7 and 6.05 L.
- Abstract/discussion give F as 86% / 94% (abstract) and 87% / 93% (discussion) vs 0.859 / 0.941 in the tables.
- Bootstrap-median column in Tables 2-3 is misaligned in the PDF text; ibuprofen F_FED_LAG sachet 0.178 and
  RUVprop 24.0% (CI 19.9-22.1) look like typesetting errors.
- Healthy young adults, mostly men; BMI to 31.4 only, so obesity extrapolation is untested (authors say so).
- Ibuprofen F and lag poorly estimated (authors: inadequate early sampling). Total racemic ibuprofen.
- Table 4 Cmax values appear to be computed without the peripheral compartment (see Verification).
- Industry-funded (AFT Pharmaceuticals; two authors employees).
