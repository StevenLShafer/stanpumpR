# Feng 2006: paroxetine two-compartment Michaelis-Menten model in late-life depression (MTLD-2)

## Study
- MTLD-2 trial (Pittsburgh): 171 elderly subjects with nonpsychotic, nonbipolar major depression, aged >= 70
  (three aged 69); age 77.1 +/- 5.7 y (range 69-95); weight 72.0 +/- 16.4 kg (32.9-137.0); 58 male, 113 female;
  156 Caucasian, 15 African-American (Table 3, Results).
- Oral paroxetine started at 10 mg/day, titrated to 10-40 mg/day; acute (up to 26 wk), continuation (16 wk) and
  2-year maintenance phases. 1970 plasma concentrations, about 10 per subject, untimed relative to dose (dose
  times recorded for inpatients, self-reported by outpatients; Fig. 1 histogram over 0-40 h).
- Assay: HPLC-UV, LOQ 5 ng/mL, linear 5-500 ng/mL, inter-assay CV 3.4-5.4%.
- CYP2D6 genotyped in 68 subjects (PCR; *2, *4, *5, *10, *17, duplications): PM 1, IM 26 (abstract says 28),
  EM 36, UM 5; 103 missing (Table 2, Results).
- NONMEM V, ADVAN9, FO estimation; covariate retained if dOFV > 3.84.

## Model and parameters
Two compartments, first-order absorption, Michaelis-Menten elimination from the central compartment (better than
one compartment: AIC 17149.9 vs 17265.0; MM better than linear by dOFV 114.0; mixed linear + MM not better).

Base model (Table 5): Vm 208 ug/h, Km 157 ug/L, V2 254 L, V3 2350 L, Q 1.33 L/h, ka 9.24 /h.
Final-model code as printed (Results): TVVM by phenotype, then TVVM1 = TVVM x (WT/75)^theta11; TVV2 = theta3 +
(1-SEX) x theta12. Post hoc V2: males 461.30 +/- 259.75 L, females 346.41 +/- 255.81 L.
Age: median Vm 275 ug/h (>= 80 y) vs 419 ug/h (< 80 y), not significant.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None; no PD, ke0 or concentration-response analysis (response data deferred to a future study). Not an opioid.

## Covariates
CYP2D6 phenotype on Vm (dOFV -137.9); weight on Vm (dOFV -62.66 after phenotype and sex; Table 5 labels it V2);
sex on V2 (dOFV -107.1). Age not significant. Race significant only without genotype.

## Caveats
Elderly only; FO method; untimed sampling with self-reported dose times; genotype missing in 60%; PM n = 1;
large SEs (V3 81%, EM Vm 49.5%); no F or dose-scaling term printed; the weight covariate is attributed to V2 in
Table 5 but to Vm in the printed code and text; abstract (IM n = 28) and Results/Table 2 (IM n = 26) disagree.
Units issue (derived): EM Vm 454 ug/h = 10.9 mg/day of maximal elimination, below the 10-40 mg/day doses given,
yet Fig. 2a/2c population predictions stay within 0-300 to 0-400 ng/mL.
