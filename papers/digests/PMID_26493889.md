# Wang 2016: cefazolin MICs and outcome in E. coli bacteraemia

## Study
Retrospective single-centre study, Taichung Veterans General Hospital, Taiwan, January-December 2013 (IRB CE14247).
506 non-duplicate blood E. coli isolates; 320 were cefazolin-susceptible by disk diffusion (CLSI 2009) and 186
resistant (PDF p2, printed 769). After excluding patients not given cefazolin (or given another beta-lactam beyond 2
days), age <=18 y, malignancy, neutropenia, AIDS, or no E. coli at the source of infection (Figure 1, PDF p3),
43 patients/isolates remained. Adults, mean age 68.1 (MIC <=2 group); mostly women (Table 1). Source: urinary tract 41
(95%), biliary 2 (5%). Cefazolin MICs by CLSI broth microdilution, cation-adjusted Mueller-Hinton, inoculum about
5 x 10^4 CFU/mL per well, 16-20 h at 35 C, range 0.01-256 ug/mL; QC E. coli ATCC 25922 and P. aeruginosa ATCC 27853.
Dosing: 1 g q6h, 1 g q8h, or reduced (1 g q12h in 6, 1 g q24h in 3) for renal impairment (Table 2). No
pharmacokinetic sampling.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Clinical end point: cure / failure / indeterminate at end of therapy; bacteriological response on day 7 after stopping
therapy. Clinical cure (Table 1, PDF p4, printed 771): MIC <=2, 33/34 (97%; one indeterminate); MIC 4, 6/6; MIC 8, 3/3.
Bacteriological success (eradication + presumed eradication) 100% of 43. In-hospital mortality 2/43 (4.6%), both in the
MIC <=2 group. Table 2 cure by regimen and MIC: all 100% except 1 g q12h at MIC <=1, 2/3 (67%).
The Discussion quotes (from the Sandoz label, ref 21) mean serum peak about 188 ug/mL and about 4 ug/mL at 8 h after
1 g IV in volunteers, and the CLSI Monte Carlo 50% fT>MIC target attainment (refs 3-4): 94-100% at MIC 1 and 64-83% at
MIC 2 for 1 g q8h; 94-100% at MIC 2 for 2 g q8h; 42-51% (1 g q6h) and 65-84% (2 g q8h) at MIC 4 (PDF p5, printed 772).
These are secondary citations, not the paper's own data. No MBC, no ke0, no concentration-effect model.

## Covariates
None modelled.

## Caveats
n = 43, single centre, retrospective; patients with severe sepsis, malignancy or neutropenia excluded; 95% urinary
source, where cefazolin reaches high concentrations; only 9 isolates at MIC 4-8. The isolates were pre-selected as
cefazolin-susceptible by the old disk criteria (CLSI 2009), so the distribution is not that of all E. coli: 37% of the
506 were disk-resistant and excluded (derived: 186/506).
