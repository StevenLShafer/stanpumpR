# Olofsen 2022, supplement: buprenorphine-fentanyl ventilation model, no-Kalman-filter fit

## Study
Supplement to a PK/PD model of buprenorphine reducing fentanyl-induced respiratory depression (Leiden / Centre for Human Drug Research; Indivior). Per the table footnote (PDF p. 2): opioid-naive volunteers had occasions 1 (placebo + fentanyl), 2 (buprenorphine + fentanyl) and 3 (buprenorphine only); chronic opioid users had occasion 4 (placebo + fentanyl) and 5 (buprenorphine + fentanyl). Ventilation was measured as 1-min averages with apnea episodes also modelled (Figs 4-5 captions). The supplement holds no design details (n, doses, assay); see the main paper. Supplemental Fig 1 shows goodness-of-fit for buprenorphine PK, fentanyl PK and PD (plasma Cp); Figs 2-3 are VPCs of the PK models; Fig 6 compares residual autocorrelation without and with a Kalman filter (correlated vs uncorrelated noise); Fig 7 simulates a typical chronic opioid user given four fentanyl IV doses (0.25, 0.35, 0.50, 0.70 mg per 70 kg) on top of buprenorphine plasma 0 (placebo), 1, 2 and 5 ng/mL.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point for every parameter: ventilation (L/min, 1-min averages) with apnea probability; no analgesia or pupil data in this file.
- ke0 (effect-site equilibration rate; the table does not state the effect compartment is plasma-linked, but the footnote defines both as effect-site rate constants): buprenorphine 0.0024 /min (t1/2 about 289 min, derived); fentanyl 0.037 /min (t1/2 about 18.7 min, derived). No tPeak given. Plasma Cp (Fig 1 caption); the assay (total vs free, whole blood vs plasma) is not in this file.
- Receptor kinetics: buprenorphine k_ON 0.46 (naive) and 0.12 (chronic) mL/ng/min, k_OFF 0.016 /min. Apparent K = k_OFF/k_ON (derived) 0.035 ng/mL naive, 0.13 ng/mL chronic.
- Fentanyl potency on ventilation: C50,F 0.49 (naive) and 1.88 ng/mL (chronic), a 3.8-fold ratio (derived); alpha_F 1.12.
- No MEAC / minimum effective or analgesic concentration, and no equianalgesic ratio, in this file.

## Covariates
Population only (opioid-naive vs chronic opioid users) on k_ON,B, alpha_B and C50,F; occasion effects on baseline ventilation and sigma. No weight, age or sex covariates in this supplement.

## Caveats
This is the model without the Kalman filter, which the authors compare to one with it (Fig 6: the residuals without the filter are autocorrelated). The values may therefore differ from the main paper's final estimates, which this file does not contain. omega^2 of V_B and k_ON,B are fixed at 0.001. k_OFF,B and ke0,B have large IIV (CV 188% and 113%) and ke0,F 93%. Table has odd label layout (see open_questions).
