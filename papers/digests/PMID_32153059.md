# Abdel Jalil 2020: systematic review of paediatric digoxin population PK

## Study
- **Registration and search:** PROSPERO CRD42018105207. PubMed, Web of Science, Scopus and Science Direct from
  inception to January 2019 (pp1268-1269).
- **Inclusion:** nonlinear mixed-effects analyses in patients aged 16 or under, in English.
- **Selection:** 68 full texts screened, 8 studies included, published 2001-2014 (p1270). Together they recruited
  718 patients (30-172 per study) and 1385 samples.
- **Synthesis:** descriptive. Typical clearance was computed per study at the mean covariates, divided by mean
  weight, and simulated for 10⁶ subjects in R (p1269).
- **Quality checks:** the ClinPK checklist plus Dartois items; model evaluation graded after Brendel.

## Model and parameters
- **Structure:** 7 of the 8 models are one-compartment, fitted to TDM troughs. The only two-compartment model is
  el-Desoky 2005 ("Desoky 2005", ref 27), the only study with 2 samples per dosing interval (p1270).
- **Clearance:** typical apparent clearance ranged from 0.24 to 0.56 L/h/kg, with IIV 7.0-35.1% (abstract; p1270).
- **Volume:** Vd/F was 15.7 L/kg in neonates and infants and 18.8 L/kg in children aged 2-14 (p1275).

Remarks on the el-Desoky row (my reading):
- Table 3 and Table 4 give two different clearance equations for the same model: multiplicative θ1[1 + θ5(SCr −
  0.6)] in Table 3, and subtractive 0.388 − 0.78(SCr − 0.6) in Table 4.
- Read in L/h/kg, as Table 4 prints it, the subtractive form reaches zero at SCr 1.10 mg/dL. That is inside the
  studied 0.2-1.3 range, so the equation cannot be right as printed (derived).
- The typical 0.36 L/h/kg follows from the subtractive form at SCr 0.63 (0.388 − 0.023 = 0.365; derived).
- Vss 9.8 is not V1 + V2.
- V1 is marked "(fixed)". The primary paper's text fixes the IIV of V1 and V2 (and the value of V2), not V1
  (PMID_16041195 text, lines 314-319; not re-rendered here).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No included study related digoxin concentration to an effect (p1278).

## Covariates
- **Age** predicts clearance in neonates and infants (Gong, Yukawa 2007, Suematsu, Martín-Suárez).
- **Weight:** exponent 1.17 (Yukawa 2011), 0.645 (Yukawa 2007) or fixed at 0.75 (Gong).
- **CHF** lowers clearance by 9.5-23.5% and raised Vd/F by about 75% in Preechagoon.
- **Spironolactone** lowered clearance by 10.3% in Suematsu.
- **SCr** was in two final models (Suematsu, el-Desoky 2005). In Suematsu, clearance falls from 0.557 to 0.463
  L/h/kg as SCr goes from 0.3 to 1 mg/dL.
- **Concentration:** Yukawa found clearance nonlinear above 1.7 ng/mL (pp1277-1278).

## Caveats
- Descriptive synthesis of 8 heterogeneous TDM studies, English only, with no US and one European population.
- The tables re-type the source equations and are not reliable for parameter values. The el-Desoky row shows how
  (above).
