# Toffoli 2001: population PK/PD of oral etoposide

## Study
Prospective, open-label, randomised crossover bioavailability study in 50 adults with advanced solid tumours (hepatocellular carcinoma 17, non-small-cell lung 19, gastric 5, breast 6, other 3; Table 1, median age 63-71 y by tumour group, creatinine clearance medians 38-76 mL/min by group). All patients treated 1994-1998 were recruited; no exclusions, no dose changes. Dose: 100 mg etoposide soft-gel capsule orally daily for 14 days every 3 weeks; one oral dose was replaced by a 50 mg IV infusion over 1 h on day 1 or day 7 (randomised); 48 h washout around it. Samples on day 1 and day 7 at 0, 1, 2, 4, 6, 24 h; a limited 0/1/4 h set on day 10 used only in the fit. 728 samples. Total etoposide by HPLC with fluorescence detection; unbound fraction by ultrafiltration of the Cmax sample (single fu per patient). GFR from creatinine clearance by the Jelliffe method. Software P-Pharm (EM algorithm, population PK-PD). Covariates tested by multiple linear regression: age, creatinine clearance, BSA, bilirubin, albumin, MEGX test.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: percent fall in absolute neutrophil count from baseline to nadir, E = 100 x (baseline - nadir)/baseline. Emax model E = Emax x AUC^c / (AUC^c + AUC50^c) with Emax fixed at 100% (p. 514, Eq. 1; fit shown in Fig. 3). Exposure variable is unbound oral AUC (mg/L*h): AUC50 = 1.80 mg/L*h (CV 27%), Hill constant (gamma) 1.47 (CV 20%) (p. 516). Mean nadir ANC 2050/mm3 (median 1650, range 140-5600), mean fall 56% (range 9-98%), nadir about day 16 (range 8-30) (p. 516). Correlations of %ANC fall with exposure (Table 3, p. 516): unbound AUC p.o. r=0.50 (P=0.0007), unbound AUC IV r=0.54 (P=0.0003), dose/m2 r=0.45, creatinine clearance r=0.39, clearance r=0.36, C24 r=0.30. Unbound AUC p.o. was 2.48 +/- 1.81 in grade 0-2 vs 4.03 +/- 2.3 mg/L*h in grade 3-4 haematological toxicity (P<0.01). Mucositis (grade 1-2, 12 patients): unbound AUC 3.8 +/- 2.3 vs 2.4 +/- 1.5 mg/L*h (P=0.02). Response (28 evaluable): in lung cancer, unbound AUC 5.9 mg/L*h in the 2 partial responders vs 2.1 (stable) and 2.3 (progressive) (P=0.01); no response in liver cancer. No ke0, effect-site or hysteresis analysis (cytotoxic, AUC-driven effect). No MEAC or therapeutic concentration is defined; the authors say the optimal exposure is unknown. Not an opioid paper.

## Covariates
Clearance rises with creatinine clearance (Eq. 2); the bilirubin effect on CL (2.5 vs 1.6 L/h for bilirubin > 1.5 mg/dL, n=9 vs 41, P=0.0004) disappeared once creatinine clearance was included (those patients had higher CLCR, 80.1 vs 67.0 mL/min). Age acts through CLCR (Eq. 3: CLCR falls with age; decoded). V1 correlates with BSA and unbound fraction. F no covariate except tumour type. No albumin or MEGX effect (albumin was normal in all patients).

## Caveats
Heterogeneous population, mostly palliative; CLCR explains only part of the variability (r2 0.32; CV 25% to 18%); the authors call it poorly predictive of exposure. Single fu measured at Cmax. Few responders (2 partial). Absorption not saturable at 100 mg (higher doses saturate, per authors). Pharmacodynamic variability was independent of PK and unexplained by covariates; the study was powered to detect only a 25% difference in %ANC between bone-metastasis groups.

The library's etoposide models are all intravenous: etoposideKroghMadsen (R/drugs_etoposideKroghMadsen.R:78-86, adults), etoposidePediatric (Urien 2011) and etoposideDuong (R/drugs_etoposideDuong.R:28-34, children). - Toffoli's clearance is lower at every CLCR: at CLCR 100 mL/min Toffoli gives 0.74 + 0.57 = 1.31 L/h against 0.99 + 0.996 = 1.99 L/h (derived). The renal slope is 0.0057 vs 0.00996 L/h per mL/min. Different CLCR methods (Jelliffe vs Cockcroft-Gault), different populations (palliative solid tumours vs AML), and Toffoli's CL is fitted jointly with an oral-bioavailability term, so the two are not like-for-like; direction only.

- Both papers find about half or more of clearance tied to renal function, consistent with the library's header ("about half the clearance is renal").
