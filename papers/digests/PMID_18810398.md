# Walter-Sack 2009: absolute bioavailability of oral methylene blue

## Study
- **Design:** phase I, single centre, open, randomised. Part A was an intra-individual crossover: 50 mg methylene
  blue (MB) IV and 500 mg MB orally, on days 1 and 8 (1-week washout). Part B, day 15, was a randomised parallel
  comparison of chloroquine (CQ) 750 mg orally alone or with 500 mg oral MB (pp180-181, Fig. 1).
- **Population:** 16 healthy volunteers (9 men, 7 women). Age 26.3 ± 4.9 y, weight 67.1 ± 10.4 kg, height 173 ±
  7 cm, BMI 22.2 ± 2.1 (p181). Subject 9 dropped out before day 2; subject 10 did not take part in Part B. G6PD
  deficiency and renal insufficiency were exclusions.
- **Doses:** IV 50 mg in 10 mL saline over 9.5 min, then a 10 mL flush. Oral 20 mL of a 25 mg/mL aqueous solution
  (500 mg) with 180 mL water, fasting (p181).
- **Sampling:** plasma (heparin) and whole blood (EDTA). IV: 0 (end of injection), 5, 10, 15, 25, 35, 45, 60 min
  and 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 10, 23.5, 24 h. Oral: 0.5-10 h, 23.5 and 24 h. Part B added 48 and 72 h (p181).
- **Assay:** LC-MS/MS (cation exchange), validated to FDA standards. Linear 75-10,000 ng/mL; LOQ 75 ng/mL in
  plasma and whole blood (p182). It measures total MB. The authors suggest earlier assays measured only unbound
  MB, with a difference of about 100:1 (p186).
- **Analysis:** non-compartmental (WinNonlin 5.2). Linear trapezoidal AUC; t1/2 = ln2/λz from at most 7 points
  (only 3 in three subjects after oral MB). F = dose-corrected AUC0-∞ ratio within each subject (p182).

Cross-checks and inconsistencies:
- The extrapolated share of AUC was 31.3 ± 16.5% (IV) and 33.6 ± 11.8% (oral) with 24 h sampling, and 11.7% with
  72 h sampling in Part B (p183). The authors accept it because F from AUC0-24 and from AUC0-∞ agree (p186).
- The Part B (MB + CQ) block of Table 1 heads its second AUC column "AUC0-24". The text (p183) and Table 2 give
  AUC0-72 for Part B. The Table 1 heading looks like a typesetting slip, but the paper does not say.
- Within the same 8 subjects, plasma AUC0-∞ was 45,449 ± 22,039 alone and 76,897 ± 46,037 with CQ (p ≤ 0.016).
  Whole blood was 50,387 ± 35,138 and 45,075 ± 21,989 (not significant) (p183).
- The Discussion's "about two thirds" absorbed (p186) is looser than the tabled 72.3%.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. This is a bioavailability study with no effect end point. Safety: nausea after 14 of 23 oral doses (bitter
taste), vomiting on 3 occasions after oral and once after IV dosing, mild diarrhoea and dysuria, and reversible
blue staining (pp185-186). No therapeutic or toxic concentration is given.

## Covariates
None analysed; n = 15-16 healthy adults.

## Caveats
- NCA only, with 24 h sampling of a drug whose terminal half-life is about 18 h, so a third of the AUC is
  extrapolated. Three oral subjects had only 3 points in their λz fit.
- Healthy young adults; one IV dose level (50 mg, about 0.75 mg/kg).
- Two subjects vomited within 30 min (subject 1 in Part A, subject 11 in Part B). The authors judged their data
  reliable (pp183, 187).
- The CQ interaction (higher plasma MB, unchanged whole blood) is unexplained.
