# Liu 2006: closed-loop propofol induction guided by BIS

## Study
Randomized, two-arm, single-centre feasibility study (Hopital Foch), 40 adults (20 per arm) scheduled for elective surgery under general anaesthesia; ASA I-III (Table 1, PDF p.3). Exclusions: under 18 y, pregnancy, neurological disorder, predicted difficult airway, ASA IV. Block randomization in blocks of 10.
- Closed-loop arm: proportional-differential controller on BIS, set point BIS 50, using the Schnider propofol model; delay between target changes set by effect-site equilibration time. Gain constant chosen with the simulator in Infusion Toolbox 95 v4.8 and a pilot (value not given).
- Manual arm: anaesthesiologist titrated an effect-site propofol target on the Marsh model (the Diprifusor set), to BIS 50, raising the target only after 95% equilibration of the effect site.
- Both arms: remifentanil by TCI on the Minto model, effect-site target 2 ng/mL held constant; propofol started at 1.5 ug/mL; not premedicated; spontaneous ventilation on oxygen for 3 min first, then gentle manual ventilation. Data at T0 (remifentanil Ce 2 ng/mL equilibrated), T1 (BIS 50 reached), T2-T4 (1, 2, 3 min after T1). BIS A-2000 XP, smoothing 15 s. No assay (no concentrations measured).

## Model and parameters
No new pharmacokinetic model. Mean +/- SD, read from rendered PDF p.3 (printed 467):
- Table 1 (n = 20 per arm, TCI vs closed-loop): age 56 +/- 14 vs 60 +/- 20 y; female/male 14/6 vs 11/9; height 164 +/- 10 vs 169 +/- 8 cm; weight 64 +/- 16 vs 69 +/- 11 kg.
- Duration of induction (baseline to T1): closed-loop 381 +/- 106 s vs TCI 490 +/- 131 s (P 0.004), text p.467.
- BIS performance error T2-T4: -9 +/- 13% closed-loop vs -16 +/- 20% TCI (P 0.035), text p.467.
- Table 2, propofol consumption, mg/kg (TCI vs closed-loop; P): T0 0.7 +/- 0.2 vs 0.4 +/- 0.1 (0.001); T1 2.1 +/- 0.6 vs 1.7 +/- 0.6 (0.035); T2 2.2 +/- 0.6 vs 1.9 +/- 0.6 (ns); T3 2.3 +/- 0.6 vs 2.0 +/- 0.7 (ns); T4 2.5 +/- 0.6 vs 2.2 +/- 0.7 (ns).
- Ephedrine boluses: 4 TCI, 3 closed-loop.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: BIS 50 (hypnosis), propofol Ce at BIS 50 not reported as a concentration; propofol dose given as mg/kg only (Table 2). The paper reports no propofol Ce at loss of BIS 50 and no EC50.
- Opioid audit (remifentanil): no MEAC / minimum effective or analgesic concentration reported. The 2 ng/mL remifentanil effect-site target is a protocol choice, not a measured threshold; no assay (concentrations are model-calculated, Minto TCI). No ke0 / t1/2ke0 / tPeak is reported for remifentanil, and no equianalgesic or potency ratio.
- Propofol effect-site timing quoted in the Discussion (PDF p.4, printed 468), cited from other work, not measured here: Schnider set t1/2ke0 1.8 min and time to peak effect 1.69 min; Diprifusor (Marsh) set t1/2ke0 2.6 min and time to peak effect 4 min. End point for those literature values: BIS/EEG as in the cited Schnider work (not restated here).

## Covariates
The closed-loop controller needs sex, age, height and weight (Schnider/Minto covariates). Authors attribute the lower early propofol consumption in the closed-loop arm to Schnider's age and BMI adjustment versus Marsh's weight-only model (mean age over 55 y); not tested (no Schnider TCI control arm).

## Caveats
Small (n = 20 per arm), unblinded, single centre, prototype on Windows 98. Control arm used Marsh while controller used Schnider, so the model and the control method are confounded (authors say so). Induction without nociceptive stimulus (no laryngoscopy). Gain constant value not given. No concentrations measured. Fig. 1 axis and exact BIS values only as box plots.

## Verification against stanpumpR
- Not cited in the repo, so no location to verify.
- Independent comparison: the paper's Schnider propofol time to peak effect, 1.69 min (PDF p.4), against R/drugs_propofol.R:276, tPeak 1.600 with a comment citing Schnider 1999. The numbers differ by 0.09 min (about 5%); the repo's comment attributes its value directly to Schnider, so this is recorded as a [source] question rather than a discrepancy. The paper's Schnider and Marsh values are quoted second-hand.
- Remifentanil: library MEAC 1 ng/mL (R/drugs_remifentanil.R:117) is not supported or contradicted by this paper, which uses a fixed Ce of 2 ng/mL as an induction setting, not a MEAC. The library's remifentanil tPeak 1.6 min (line 116) is not compared: the paper gives no remifentanil tPeak.
- Verdict: cannot verify beyond the one propofol tPeak comparison.
