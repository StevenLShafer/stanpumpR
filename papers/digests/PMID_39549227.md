# Mohmaed Ali 2024: doxorubicin population PK across all ages

## Study
Pooled population PK of doxorubicin in 168 patients aged 0.11-90 y (median 53.2 y), 555 plasma samples. Sources: a prospective Dutch study of elderly patients >= 70 y (n=47; 25-75 mg/m2 over 0.25 or 0.5 h; flexible sampling from end of infusion to 24 h), a prospective Dutch study of children < 18 y (n=31; local protocols, 4-6 samples at 2, 4, 6, 8, 24, 48 h, plus a trough if dosed within 7 days), UK children (n=32; NCCPG TDM 2018) and adults from Joerger 2007 (n=59, breast cancer; 24 of them elderly). Age groups: infants/toddlers 25 (18 under 1 y), children 22, adolescents 16, adults 45, elderly 60. Assay: LC-MS/MS (Dutch studies LLOQ 1.0 ng/mL, daunorubicin internal standard; UK LLOQ 5.0 ng/mL, idarubicin internal standard); samples below LLOQ discarded (15). Software NONMEM 7.5.0, FOCE-I, PsN 5.2.6; parameter uncertainty by sampling importance resampling; pcVPC. Doses in the data: 0.625-1.66 mg/kg in infants and 20-75 mg/m2 otherwise; infusion 0.05-51 h in infants (median 2.0 h).

## Model and parameters
Three-compartment model, first-order elimination, proportional residual error. Body-weight allometry with fixed exponents (0.75 for CL and inter-compartmental clearances, 1 for volumes), normalised to 70 kg (dOFV 358 for BW vs 368 for BSA and 378 for lean body weight as alternatives; BW chosen for stability). Age added as a sigmoid maturation function on V1 and V2 (Eq. 3, Hill fixed at 1, TM50 in weeks) and a maximal-effect decline on V3 (Eq. 4, TM50 in years). No maturation or ageing effect on CL (dOFV 0.44 and 2.38; categorical and power-function age on CL also not retained). Final-model dOFV 40.9 for the age terms. ## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. Pure PK; no effect, toxicity or cardiotoxicity exposure-response analysis. The authors recommend, from simulation, keeping current dosing (1 mg/kg for infants < 10 kg, BSA-based otherwise). Simulated median AUC (1000 patients per group; 1 mg/kg for infants < 10 kg, 30 mg/m2 for the rest, 4 h infusion in infants and children, 0.25 h in adults and elderly): infants/toddlers 756, children 1021, adolescents 507, adults 563, elderly 543 ug*h/L; at 60 mg/m2 adults 1081 and elderly 1149 ug*h/L (p. 1717). Doxorubicinol was not modelled. Not an opioid paper; no MEAC, ke0 or potency ratio.

## Covariates
Body weight (fixed allometry) on every parameter; age as above. No sex, BSA, renal or hepatic term retained in the final model (BSA and lean body weight were alternatives to BW). Missing weights/heights were imputed (study-group median or TNO growth curve).

## Caveats
Authors' own: only about 10% under 1 y; no gestational age; selection bias among fit elderly; doxorubicinol not modelled; slight under-prediction in adults and slight misspecification in adults/elderly at late times (few late samples); TM50 for infants and IIV on V2 poorly estimated (RSE 70.5% and 125.2%). Noticed: sampling stopped at 24-48 h, so the huge V3 (1063 L) is a deep slow compartment from a short observation window; clearance, not Vss, is the robust quantity (as the library already treats Joerger's 11-h terminal half-life, R/drugs_doxorubicinJoerger.R:36-40).

## Verification against stanpumpR
No code cites this paper. Comparison with the library's doxorubicin models, as an independent check:
- Adults, doxorubicinJoerger (R/drugs_doxorubicinJoerger.R:25-31): CL 47.6 L/h at BSA 1.8 m2 (library divides by the base fraction 0.9371, so 50.8 L/h per hydrochloride-dosed mg), V1 12.3 L (13.1 in the library), Q 60.3 L/h, V2 421 L. Mohmaed's adult CL of 48.0 L/h (typical adult, about 69.5 kg) and V1 12.9 L agree with Joerger's within about 1-9%; the paper's other compartments differ (Q2 11.3/V2 23.7 and Q3 50/V3 1063 vs Joerger's single Q 60.3/V2 421), as expected from longer sampling (to 24-48 h vs 24 h) and the extra compartment. Whether Mohmaed's mg are base or hydrochloride is unstated (open_questions), so the 6% difference cannot be attributed.
- Children, doxorubicinPediatric (R/drugs_doxorubicinPediatric.R:23-34, 102-116): Voller's CL is 24.1 L/h/m2 at the median child (BSA 0.77 m2, age 5.32 y), i.e. 18.6 L/h (derived: 24.1 x 0.77); Mohmaed's allometric CL for a child of 17.9 kg is 17.4 L/h (paper, p. 1716). These agree. Mohmaed Ali found no maturation effect on CL once body weight is allometrically scaled, and attributes the age effects in Voller 2015 and Kunarajah 2017 to correlation between size and age and to using a single exponent for CL and V1 (p. 1719). The library's doxorubicinPediatric applies a power-of-age term on clearance ((1 + (age/5.32)^0.286)/2, R/drugs_doxorubicinPediatric.R:106, from Voller) and linear BSA scaling, which this paper contradicts as a structure; it does not say the Voller CL values are wrong. A modelling choice for Shafer, not a transcription discrepancy.
- Mohmaed's V1 for an infant of 7.5 kg is 0.72 L (paper, p. 1716) against the library's Voller V1 = 9.34 L/m2 x scale; not computed because the library needs height and the infant's BSA.
