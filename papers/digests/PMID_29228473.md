# Nichols 2018: desvenlafaxine population PK, Korean versus US

## Study
Population PK of desvenlafaxine (extended-release tablet, as the succinate) built on an earlier model (ref 35, a 2009 poster) fitted to sparse samples from MDD patients (6 phase 3 and 1 phase 2 study) plus rich samples from 9 phase 1 studies. This paper adds 806 samples from a randomized, placebo-controlled single- and multiple-dose study in 30 healthy Korean adults (18-55 y, BMI 17.5-30.5 kg/m2, weight over 50 kg; 10 per dose level at 50, 100 and 200 mg; single dose day 1, then once daily days 4-8; samples to 72 h on days 1 and 8). Plasma desvenlafaxine by LC-MS/MS, LLOQ 2.0 ng/mL. Combined data set: 3987 samples from 289 healthy subjects and 4243 samples from 2001 MDD patients. Analysed groups (PDF p.5): MDD 1822 patients (140 Korean, 1224 US, 227 Japanese, 231 Chinese) and 226 healthy (30 Korean, 149 US, 17 Japanese, 30 Chinese). NONMEM 7.20, ADVAN2. Korean status tested as CL = theta1 x (1 + theta2 x Korean), separately on CL/F and V/F; external validation by standardized visual predictive check (12 observations, 1.5%, below the 5th percentile and 13, 1.6%, above the 95th).

Demographics, Table 1 (PDF p.5): US N 1451, age 43.0 (SD 14.35), weight 85.9 kg (22.67), CrCl 125.8 mL/min; Korean N 170, age 42.9, weight 60.5 kg (10.66, range 40.0-95.0), CrCl 99.6 mL/min.

## Model and parameters
Open one-compartment linear model with parallel first-order and zero-order inputs (extended release), lag time. k0 = dose x (1 - F1)/D2. Table 2 (PDF p.6, printed p.446), typical values of the final model on the combined data (US plus Korean). CL/F and V/F are apparent (oral dose, F not separable).

CL/F = [7.23 x (CrCl/110) + 12.3 x (1 - 0.262 x HEP) x (ALK/74)^-0.206 x (TBIL/0.5)^0.04 x (ALT/21)^0.129] x (WT/70)^0.219 x (Dose/100)^0.063 x (1 - 0.169 x Gender) x (1 + 0.09 x PT)
V/F = 282 x (WT/70)^0.872 x (1 + 0.099 x MD) x (1 + 1.28 x PT)
(Gender 0 male, 1 female; PT 0 healthy, 1 MDD patient; MD multiple dose; HEP 0 normal or Child-Pugh A, 1 Child-Pugh B or C; Table 2 footnotes.)

Reference-subject values (70 kg, CrCl 110, normal liver, male, 100 mg, healthy): CL/F 7.23 + 12.3 = 19.53 L/h (the text gives this as 19.53 x (WT/70)^0.219, p.5); V/F 282 L (Table 2, text p.5). Half-life (derived: ln2 x 282/19.53) about 10.0 h; the introduction says approximately 11 h.
Renal clearance 8.80 L/h (147 mL/min), about 39% of total, nonrenal 13.8 L/h (231 mL/min) for the worked example in the text (WT 81 kg, p.5).

Geometric-mean comparison, Table 3 (PDF p.7): healthy Korean CL/F 16.78 L/h, V/F 245 L (n 30); US 18.54 L/h and 293 L (n 149, ratio to Korean 1.1 and 1.2; V/F P<.0001); Japanese 16.37 and 206 (n 17); Chinese 17.86 and 214 (n 30). MDD Korean 15.28 L/h and 513 L (n 140); US 19.1 and 731 (n 1224, ratios 1.25 and 1.425, both P<.0001); Japanese 15.83 and 524 (n 227); Chinese 16.66 and 508 (n 231). Weight-normalized (text p.5-6): healthy Korean/US CL/F 0.267/0.246 L/h/kg, V/F 3.897/3.895 L/kg; MDD Korean/US CL/F 0.259/0.226 L/h/kg, V/F 8.711/8.667 L/kg.
Model-predicted weight-normalized values (Figure 2 legend): healthy 0.257 L/h/kg and 3.99 L/kg; MDD 0.259 L/h/kg and 8.88 L/kg.
Steady-state AUC of the median profile at 50 mg once daily (Figure 3): healthy US 2931, Korean 3088 h.ng/mL; MDD US 2573, Korean 3130 h.ng/mL.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No concentration-effect, EC50, ke0 or therapeutic range reported. Not an opioid; opioid audit not applicable.

## Covariates
WT, gender, dose, PT, ALK, ALT, HEP, TBIL and CrCl on CL/F; WT, MD and PT on V/F (equations above). Korean status: geometric-mean CL/F and V/F were 9.1% and 16.7% lower in healthy Korean subjects than US (Table 3 ratios 1.1 and 1.2 against Korean; text p.5), against a 20% higher US mean weight (75.3 vs 63.0 kg); the comparator is the US subjects of the same combined data set. After weight was in the model, no Korean effect remained, the authors conclude. In MDD, CL/F and V/F were 20% and 30% lower in Korean patients than US (p.6), again against US patients, whose mean weight was 43% higher.

## Caveats
Apparent parameters only (no IV data in this paper; absolute bioavailability is ref 38). Poster-based base model (ref 35). MDD patients had much larger V/F than healthy subjects (PT effect 1.28); the PT-related CL/F and V/F terms mix disease with sparse-sampling design. Healthy Korean subjects n=30 with weight over 50 kg required. Four typed values in the text layer of Table 2 were scrambled in rows (ka, F1, D2, Tlag); they were read from the image.

## Verification against stanpumpR
Not cited, so nothing to verify as a transcription. Comparison with R/drugs_desvenlafaxine.R:
- The library's CL1 is 22.1 L/h (line 23) and V1 238 L (line 24), described in its header (lines 11-19) as venlafaxine-scale apparent values (divided by venlafaxine's unmeasured bioavailability) that do not describe desvenlafaxine taken as a drug. This paper is the oral drug itself: CL/F 19.53 L/h and V/F 282 L at 70 kg for a healthy male at 100 mg, CrCl 110. Against that, the library's clearance is about 13% higher (22.1/19.53) and its volume about 16% lower (238/282); half-life 7.5 h in the library (header line 12) against about 10 h derived here. The header already predicts the two should differ; the size of the difference is consistent with that.
- The library scales by its own size factors (FFM or cohort), whereas this paper scales by total weight to the power 0.219 (CL/F) and 0.872 (V/F); not comparable without a decision.
- The paper's MDD patients have 2.28x the V/F of healthy subjects (PT effect); the library says no health-status difference (Wang 2022, header line 12). Different data sets; noted, not resolved.
