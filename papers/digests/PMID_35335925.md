# Li 2022: Haloperidol PK in critically ill adults with delirium

## Study
Single-centre, Erasmus MC adult ICU, Oct 2014-Apr 2017. **22 critically ill adults** with delirium (12 M / 10 F; median age 67, 48-77 y; weight 80, 52-137 kg; BMI 27; APACHE III 80.5; all Caucasian). Haloperidol **1 mg i.v. every 8 h** (0.5 mg from age 80; 2 mg if agitated), total 1.5-6 mg/day, tapered by ICDSC. Sampling: troughs on days 2-6, plus day-2 profile (0-1, 2-3, 4-5, 6-8 h). 145 samples, 6 censored (taken during infusion) leaving **139**. Assay: LC-MS/MS, LLOQ 0.5 ug/L, ULOQ 20 ug/L. Matrix described as serum collected in EDTA tubes (Methods), but the Discussion says whole-blood concentrations were measured; inconsistent. CYP2D6: 12 EM, 7 IM, 3 PM; CYP3A4: 18 EM, 4 IM. Admission CRP median 171 (4.1-368) mg/L. NONMEM 7.4 FOCE-I, log-transformed data, additive error; stepwise covariate modelling (forward p < 0.05, backward p < 0.01).

CRP reduced OFV by 7.49 and explained 31% of IIV on CL; the negative CRP-CL relation flattens above about 100 mg/L (Fig. 3).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No concentration-delirium, sedation or QTc relationship studied; the authors state the therapeutic target is unknown. Free fraction not measured.

## Covariates
Tested: age, sex, height, weight, BMI, BSA, CYP3A4, CYP2D6, CRP, creatinine, albumin, bilirubin, APACHE III, SOFA, ASAT, ALAT, interacting drugs, WBC, platelets. Only CRP on CL retained.

## Caveats
Small n; low doses; sparse sampling, so no peripheral compartment could be identified (other published models are two-compartment); matrix ambiguity (serum vs whole blood). Discussion compares with published CL 42.4-88 L/h and V 2060-3169 L (refs 29 Yukawa 2002 and 43 Pilla Reddy 2013, cited together) and with Franken 2017 terminally ill patients (CL 29.3 L/h, V 1260 L).
