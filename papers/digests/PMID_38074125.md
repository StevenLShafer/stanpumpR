# Park 2023: everolimus population PK in focal cortical dysplasia

## Study
- Data from a single-centre (Severance Hospital, Seoul) double-blind randomised crossover trial of everolimus (Afinitor Disperz, tablets for oral suspension) vs placebo in refractory seizures with focal cortical dysplasia type II (NCT03198949), 2017–2020; phases: 4 wk baseline, core I 12 wk, core II 12 wk, extension 29 wk (PDF p2).
- n = 22 (Table 1, PDF p4): age median 13.5 y (IQR 12–17.75; range 4–32); weight median 50 kg (13–86); BSA median 1.5 m² (0.6–2); albumin 4.6 g/dL; 9 male (40.9%). CYP3A4 inducer co-medication: strong 1, moderate 0, weak 14; inhibitors: strong 2, moderate 1, weak 14. All on ≥1 other antiepileptic drug.
- Dose: start 4.5 mg/m²/day once daily (from EXIST-1), TDM-adjusted in 2 mg steps to a trough target of 5–15 ng/mL (PDF p2).
- Sampling: troughs at weeks 2, 3, 4, 8, 14, 15, 16, 20, 25, 28, 40; extra samples 1–4 h post-dose at weeks 24/28/40. 152 concentrations analysed (PDF p2–4).
- Assay: HPLC-MS/MS on **whole blood** (EDTA tubes); LLOQ 1.1 ng/mL; validated 1.1–41.6 ng/mL; inter/intra-assay CV < 7.3% (PDF p3; whole-blood matrix stated PDF p5).
- NONMEM 7.4.1, FOCE-I, PsN 4.7.0, Pirana, Xpose4; 5000-replicate bootstrap; pc-VPC (1000 sims) (PDF p3).

## Model and parameters
One-compartment, first-order absorption (ADVAN2 TRANS2); exponential IIV; proportional residual error. Final model (Table 2, PDF p5, confirmed on rendered page):
TVCL = 12.5 + 9.71 × (BSA/1.5) L/h; TVV = 293 L; TVKA = 0.585 /h. Parameters are apparent (oral, CL/F and V/F); F not estimated.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- No PD model. Target whole-blood trough 5–15 ng/mL, **assumed** from TSC (Franz 2013, EXIST-1); the authors state FCD targets and end points are not established (PDF p6, p8). Introduction cites EXIST-3 low/high trough ranges 3–7 and 9–15 ng/mL (PDF p2).
- Simulated steady-state troughs (Figure 3, PDF p8): 4.5 mg/m² gives troughs near or just above 5 ng/mL at BSA ≥ 1.5 m²; below target at BSA 0.5–0.7 m².
- Table 3 (PDF p8): 0.5 ≤ BSA < 1 m² → 7–9 mg/m² initial dose; 1.5 ≤ BSA → 6–7 mg/m².
- Not an opioid; no MEAC, ke0 or potency ratio.

## Covariates
BSA on CL (linear, centred at 1.5 m²) only. Albumin on CL was dropped at backward elimination (ΔOFV 4.941 < 6.63). RBC on CL/V reduced OFV but was excluded because the assay is whole blood. CYP3A4 inducers/inhibitors not estimable (PDF p5, p8).

## Caveats
- n = 22, sparse mostly-trough data; ka poorly informed (IIV fixed to 0); one-compartment chosen for simplicity although other everolimus models are two-compartment (PDF p6–7).
- V/F 95% CI 72–1454 L (bootstrap): very imprecise.
- Internal inconsistencies: Table 3 has no row for 1 ≤ BSA < 1.5 m² while the discussion says 6–7 mg/m² for BSA 1–2 m²; the abstract says 4.5 mg/m² suffices for BSA > 1.5 m², while Table 3 recommends 6–7 mg/m² there.
- Combes 2018 (TSC) ka cited as 10.8 /h, vs 0.585 /h here.
