# Papathanasiou 2019: high-dose IV naloxone and naloxone-3-glucuronide PK

## Study
8 healthy men (9 eligible, 1 excluded), mean age 25.9 y (24.4-28.9), height 182 cm (177-194), weight 79.6 kg (61-92);
one BMI 30.4. NCT01992146. Naloxone 3.25 mg/kg total IV over 75 min in three bolus + 24-min infusion steps (Table 1:
0.02 + 0.23, 0.06 + 0.69, 0.18 + 2.07 mg/kg). Venous samples (22 per subject) to 334 min after the end of infusion;
166 naloxone and 166 N3G concentrations, none BLQ. LC-MS/MS. NONMEM 7.3, FOCE-I, ADVAN6; PsN SCM covariate search.
Also tested Dowling 2008 and Yassen 2007 predictive performance (Varvel metrics).

## Model and parameters
Naloxone three-compartment, linear (Michaelis-Menten did not improve fit); N3G two-compartment, all naloxone cleared to
N3G (assumption), no formation delay. Proportional residual error. Table 2:

Complex model (absolute): CL 3.84 L/min (3.48-4.23), V1 34.44 L, Q1 3.93 L/min, V2 70.15 L with a linear weight
coefficient 0.044 (exact form not printed), Q2 0.92 L/min, V3 82.12 L; N3G CL 0.6 L/min, V1 22.42 L, Q 0.33 L/min,
V2 20.88 L. IIV (simple): CL 11.4%, V1 112.2%, V2 40%; CL N3G 28.1%, V1 N3G 24.5%; residual 22.6% (naloxone),
17.1% (N3G) (column order as printed).
Inconsistency: Discussion gives simple-model CL for 70 kg as 4.42 L/min, but 0.049 × 70 = 3.43 L/min (derived).
Predictive performance (Table 3): Dowling MDPE -32.5% (over-predicts), MDAPE 32.5%, wobble 33.4%; Yassen MDPE 1.2%,
MDAPE 7%, wobble 19.9%.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No ke0, no MEAC, no potency. COWS monitored only. Cites PET: 100 µg/kg naloxone fully blocks carfentanil binding.

## Covariates
Simple: all volumes and clearances ∝ total weight. Complex: weight linear on V2 only (IIV on V2 45% → 16%).

## Caveats
n = 8 young men; very high doses (concentrations > 30× clinical); weight range 61-92 kg; author caveat against
extrapolation. N3G volumes not identifiable (no N3G dosing). Table 2 text layer is jumbled; the CL-at-70-kg sentence
does not match the table.
