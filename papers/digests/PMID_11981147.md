# Bailey 2002: clevidipine dose-finding, PK and MAP-threshold PD after cardiac surgery

## Study
- Randomised, placebo-controlled dose-finding study at five US centres (p1087).
- 91 adults enrolled after CABG or valve surgery: LVEF > 30%, pulmonary artery catheter, ICU MAP > 90 mmHg on two
  readings, sedated with propofol at a constant rate during the study period.
- Data from 85 patients (Results, p1088). Demographics (Table 1): age 63 (12) y, weight 81 (15) kg, height
  174 (9) cm, 73 M / 18 F.
- Randomised to placebo or 0.32, 1.37, 3.19 or 9.58 µg/kg/min. The protocol was later amended to add 0.05 and
  0.18 µg/kg/min.
- Protocol: 12-min titration to the randomised dose, a 10-min observation (one dose change allowed if MAP was
  > 105 or < 65), then 100 min at a fixed rate, then a 20-min washout. Optional maintenance followed for up to
  12 h.
- Samples: 2 mL arterial, at 10-88 min of the fixed infusion and 0.5-20 min after the stop, then hourly. Tubes
  contained SDS. GC-MS assay, detection limit 0.2282 µg/L; the method reference is a whole-blood method (ref 12).
- 577 concentrations from 61 patients.
- PK analysis: NONMEM FO and conditional estimation, log-normal IIV, two- vs three-compartment models, with and
  without weight proportionality.

## Model and parameters
- Three compartments, elimination from the central compartment. This beat two compartments by 188 OFV points,
  and log-normal error beat additive by 44.
- **Weight proportionality did not improve the fit**, so the parameters are weight-independent. Values per kg are
  normalised to the mean weight.

**Derived checks:**
- V1 + V2 + V3 = 32.39 L, so the abstract's "volume of distribution of 32.4 l" is Vss.
- The micro-constants give an eigenvalue sum of 1.305/min, against 1.306 from the disposition function.
- 9.7 µg/L × 4.3 L/min = 41.7 µg/min, as printed (p1090). 26.3 × 4.3 = 113.1 µg/min, against the printed 114.09.
- Measured/predicted fit: median absolute prediction error 0.313. Prediction error grew during washout (Fig 5).
- The CSHT is under 2 min up to 12 h, and the 80% decrement time plateaus at about 10 min (Fig 7).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- **End point:** a binary fall in MAP of ≥10% or ≥20% from baseline after 10 min of steady infusion, against the
  concentration measured at that time.
- **Model (p1088):** P_x = C^a(x) / (C^a(x) + C50,x^a(x)), a sigmoid in concentration with exponent a(x), fitted
  in NONMEM without inter-patient variability. No ke0.
- **Haemodynamics:**
  - MAP fell 25.5% (14.9) at 1.37 and 32.7% (17.8) at 3.19 µg/kg/min (Table 4).
  - HR, CVP, PAOP, CI and PVR did not change with dose.
  - SVR fell at the two higher doses, but the fall was not significantly different from placebo.
  - Responders (≥10% MAP fall) by randomised dose (Table 5): 0/11 on placebo, 1/11 at 0.05, 4/13 at 0.18, 6/10
    at 0.32, 9/12 at 1.37, 19/20 at 3.19 and 14/14 at 9.58.
- The authors suggest starting at 0.32-1.37 µg/kg/min. About 0.5 µg/kg/min reaches the 10% C50 at steady state,
  and about 1.4 reaches the 20% C50.

## Covariates
- Weight was tested and not retained. No other covariates were studied.
- The authors caution against extrapolating outside the narrow weight range.

## Caveats
- **Matrix:** the abstract and Methods call the samples "plasma", and Results p1089 refers to "blood
  concentration". Yet arterial blood went straight into SDS tubes, and the assay reference is a blood method. The
  paper itself does not settle the matrix.
- Concurrent propofol (a vasodilator that blunts the baroreflex) and uncontrolled intraoperative β-blockade; 15
  patients were atrially paced.
- 14 patients were randomised to 9.58 µg/kg/min but only one received it. Six were excluded for needing more than
  one dose change.
- Source slip: the Discussion (p1090) says Figure 8 uses "table 5" parameters; it means Table 6.
