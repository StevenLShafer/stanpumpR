# Kuhnert 1980: Meperidine disposition in mother, neonate, nonpregnant females

## Study
- Single IV 50 mg meperidine bolus (as needed for pain) in 18 labouring women (12 with usable kinetics); 8 nonpregnant women (7 with kinetics; nurses/hospital staff, studied early in menstrual cycle, no oral contraceptives); 30 neonates whose mothers had received meperidine (urine only). Text p1 (PDF 2).
- Plasma sampled for at least 3 h (pregnant mean 215 ± 28 min, until delivery; nonpregnant 256 ± 28 min); urine 3 days (adults timed 12-h pools, neonates pooled 24 h; expressed as µg drug per mg creatinine). Pudendal lidocaine was given at delivery in most.
- Assay: GC with nitrogen-phosphorus detector (Mather and Tucker method), 1 ml plasma, internal standard W-12174; meperidine and normeperidine, plasma (not whole blood, not free).
- Model: 2-compartment open model, computer-fitted; r 0.823 to 0.991 (mean 0.944 ± 0.06).
- Mean age: pregnant 21 ± 5 yr, nonpregnant 24 ± 5 yr (Table I). Weight not reported.

Urinary excretion (Figs 2 and 3, graphs only): in adults, normeperidine exceeded meperidine in each 12-h period except the first in nonpregnant women; neonates excreted more parent drug than metabolite for 2 days, more normeperidine by day 3, and more drug per mg creatinine than adults each day.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Opioid audit: none. No MEAC or analgesic concentration, no ke0 / t½ke0 / tPeak / hysteresis, no equianalgesic or potency ratio. Kinetics only (plasma concentration, µg/mL, Fig. 1).

## Covariates
Pregnancy (labour) vs nonpregnant: no statistically significant difference in any constant (Table I; text p3). The authors note a possible slight underestimate of t½α in the pregnant group from the shorter sampling. Neonatal maturation: qualitative only (urine patterns).

## Caveats
Small groups (12 vs 7), between-subject SDs large (e.g. nonpregnant V1 60 ± 38 L, K_E 2.11 ± 3.0), so little power to detect a difference; no weight; compared with previously published values from the authors' lab, not randomised. Pregnant women sampled in labour (not a stable third-trimester state); pudendal lidocaine co-administered.
