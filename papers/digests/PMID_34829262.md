# Sakamoto 2021: fluconazole PopPK in hematological malignancy

## Study
Single-centre (Yokohama City University Hospital), Nov 2018-Mar 2020, adults (age >=16) given prophylactic fluconazole 200 mg orally once daily during chemotherapy or HSCT. 54 patients (31 M / 23 F), 119 plasma concentrations used (125 collected; 6 dropped for changing renal function). Sampling 1-4 points per patient (trough, 2, 4 or 12 h after dose). HPLC-UV (266 nm); assay accuracy 86.9-100.7%, intra-/inter-day CV <5.3%/<7.5%; total plasma concentration. Software Phoenix NLME (FOCE-ELS), stepwise covariate search, VPC and 1000 bootstraps. Table 1 medians: weight 57.6 kg (39.8-99.1), age 53 (20-77), CG-CLcr 87.1 mL/min (31.1-193.6), eGFRcre 71.2 mL/min (25.9-148.5).

## Model and parameters
One-compartment, first-order absorption and elimination (Table 2, PDF p.5, final model, typical values with SE / CV%).
CL/F = theta1 x (CLcr/5.2)^theta2 x e^0.16, CLcr = Cockcroft-Gault in L/h normalised to 5.2 L/h (the population median; 87.1 mL/min = 5.2 L/h, derived).
Vd/F = theta3 x (BW/57.6)^theta4.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No ke0 or effect-site data. PK/PD target: free AUC/MIC = 50, protein binding taken as 12%, target MIC 2.0 ug/mL (CLSI M60); PTA >=90% on 10,000 simulated patients (weights 40-70 kg, CLcr 40-140 mL/min). Breakpoint MICs (abstract, p.1): 200 mg/day 0.5-1.0 ug/mL on day 1 and 1.0-2.0 at steady state; 400 mg/day 1.0-2.0 and 2.0-4.0. Recommended: loading 400-700 mg/day, maintenance 200-400 mg/day (Table 4, p.9; grid not read). Simulated peak did not exceed 80 ug/mL (data not shown); convulsions are cited at >80 ug/mL. No MEAC. Literature half-life quoted: 31 h.

## Covariates
CLcr (Cockcroft-Gault) on CL/F; body weight on V/F. Age, height, BSA, BMI, eGFRcre also tested. Sex and albumin not used.

## Caveats
Single centre, hematological patients only, one dose level (200 mg), sparse samples, dosing recommendations not clinically tested (authors). The "CL/F is increased" claim compares with other published studies (0.55-0.79 L/h) and healthy-volunteer Vd/F 55.4-59.4 L, not with any stanpumpR model. Oral data only: F is not separable, so CL and V are apparent.
