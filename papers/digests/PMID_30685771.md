# Madsen 2019: 5-HT2A occupancy, plasma psilocin and psychedelic intensity

## Study
Open-label PET study, Copenhagen. 8 healthy volunteers (3 female; age 33.0 +/- 7.1 y), 5 with earlier
psychedelic use. Single oral psilocybin 3-30 mg (3 mg capsules; doses 3, 6, 12, 15, 18, 24, 24, 30 mg;
0.05-0.3 mg/kg), participants blind to dose. [11C]Cimbi-36 (5-HT2A agonist radioligand) PET at baseline
(49 +/- 12 days earlier), PET 1 starting 58 +/- 13 min after the dose, and in subjects 1-5 PET 2 at
344 +/- 41 min; each scan 120 min. SRTM with cerebellum reference, neocortex BPND; occupancy = % fall in
BPND from baseline. Venous psilocin at radioligand injection and every 20 min during scans, UPLC-MS/MS
(LOQ 0.5 µg/kg, LOD 0.1 µg/kg); mean within-scan psilocin = trapezoid AUC / 120 min. Intensity 0-10 Likert
every 20 min. Fits in GraphPad Prism.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- **Occupancy (neocortical 5-HT2A, PET)** vs mean within-scan plasma psilocin, single-site model
  Occ = Occmax·Cp/(EC50 + Cp): Occmax 76.6% [95% CI 67.3; 88.0], EC50 1.95 µg/L, R² 0.92 (Results p4;
  Fig. 3). The EC50 CI is printed [1.17; 3.15] in the Results text and [1.16; 3.15] in the Fig. 3 legend;
  both are on the same page. Sensitivity for subject 1's PET-2 values: Occmax 75.5 vs 77.9%, EC50 1.81 vs
  2.12 µg/L (LOQ vs LOD); 0.3 µg/kg used. EC50 1.95 µg/L stated as about 10 nM (p5).
- Per-subject EC50 by PET: 4.5 +/- 1.9 µg/L (PET 1) and 6.2 +/- 6.0 µg/L (PET 2), not different (paired t,
  mean difference -1.7, 95% CI -10.2 to 6.7, p = 0.6; p3). This rising/near-peak vs falling-limb comparison
  is the paper's only implicit test of hysteresis; n = 5 and wide CI.
- **Subjective intensity** (0-10) vs psilocin, Emax model: Intensity_max 10.8 [8.6; 14.7], EC50 4.5
  [2.1; 9.8] µg/L, R² 0.35 (p4, Fig. 4b). Intensity vs occupancy, quadratic: β1 -0.02 [-0.13; 0.1],
  β2 0.002 [0.0006; 0.003], R² 0.81 (Fig. 4a).
- Derived prediction in the discussion: Brown 2017's mean Cmax about 15 ng/mL after 25 mg corresponds to
  69% occupancy (density 1.02 g/mL assumed).
- No ke0, t½ke0 or tPeak; no MEAC; no potency ratio. Microdose suggestion 0.5-2 mg (p5).

## Covariates
None modelled.

## Caveats
n = 8 (5 with a second scan); not placebo-controlled; agonist radioligand may overstate high-affinity-state
occupancy; Occmax below 100% unexplained (internalization, 5-HT changes, model assumptions); head motion up
to 35 mm in two subjects; within-scan means over 120 min blur any short lag; assay detail in supplement.

## Verification against stanpumpR
- **Single-site binding, occupancies up to 72%, 8 volunteers** (R/drugs_psilocybin.R:77-80, help :35): the
  quoted phrase is in the abstract and 72.4% is the maximum in Table 1. **Matches.** "A direct function of
  plasma rather than an equilibration delay" is the library's inference: the paper fits occupancy to mean
  within-scan plasma and does not fit an effect compartment, but its unchanged EC50 between PET 1 and the
  falling-limb PET 2 supports no long delay (weakly, n = 5). Reasonable; noted, not a discrepancy.
- **No ke0/EC50/Emax coded**: true; Madsen's EC50s are occupancy and intensity end points, not plotted.
  **Matches.**
- **docs/illicit-drugs-specification-review.md:106-109**: the primary paper now settles both items: the
  intensity EC50 4.5 µg/L [2.1; 9.8] and R² 0.35 are printed in Results (PDF p4); the occupancy EC50 lower
  limit is printed both ways by the paper itself (1.17 in text, 1.16 in Fig. 3 legend). The doc's
  "traces only to secondary sources" is out of date, not wrong about the numbers. Nothing is coded, so no
  model impact.
- **Independent check of the Holze 2023 model** (not cited for this): the library predicts about 11.7 ng/mL
  peak free psilocin for Holze's 15 mg arm (13.8 mg assayed, R/drugs_psilocybin.R:64); Madsen's 15 mg
  subject had Cmax 11.7 µg/L, and the 12-30 mg subjects 11.8-19.3 µg/L, peaks within about 1-2.5 h,
  consistent with Holze's 2 h. Dose-proportional prediction for 30 mg entered as psilocybin (derived:
  11.7 x 30/13.8 ≈ 25 ng/mL) is above Madsen's 19.3 µg/L in one subject, within the stated
  between-subject variability. Assay scale (free vs total) is open; see `open_questions`.
