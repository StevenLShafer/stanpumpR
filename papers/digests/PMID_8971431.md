# Mandema 1996: population PK of controlled-release vs immediate-release oral oxycodone

## Study
Single-dose randomised, analytically blinded, two-way crossover, 1-week washout: 24 healthy fasting men (23
completed), 30 y (21-45), 179 cm, 76 kg (63-91). Two 10 mg CR tablets (OxyContin) vs 20 mg IR oral solution
(Roxicodone). Samples 0.25-36 h (21 times). GC/MS, LOQ 0.2 ng/mL. Validation: separate 21 volunteers, 10 mg CR q12h or
5 mg IR solution q6h for 4 days. NONMEM IV, first-order method; log-residual model; 912 concentrations.
No intravenous reference, so parameters are apparent (CL/F, Vd/F) and IR F is not known.

## Model and parameters
One-compartment disposition; IR: first-order absorption with lag; CR: bi-exponential absorption with the same lag
and relative bioavailability Frel. Table 2 (population mean (SE); IIV variance), rows assigned by order and checked
against the text (kc1 t½ 37 min, kc2 t½ 6.2 h, f1 38%):

Table 1 (arithmetic mean ± SD, n = 23), CR vs IR 20 mg: AUC0-36 199.7 ± 65.3 vs 194.4 ± 23.4 ng·h/mL (ratio 102.7%,
90% CI 89.5-115.9); Cmax 18.6 ± 6.1 vs 41.6 ± 13.2 ng/mL; tmax 2.62 ± 1.07 vs 1.30 ± 0.63 h (ranges CR 1-6, IR
0.2-2.5 h); apparent t½ 7.99 ± 2.96 vs 3.21 ± 0.87 h. CL/F 110 L/h = 1.1-1.4 L/kg/h after weight adjustment.
Derived: typical IR peak from the model = ln(ka/k)/(ka - k) + tlag, k = 110/593 = 0.186 /h, gives 0.78 h + 0.21 h ≈ 59 min.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No analgesic, pupil or other effect was measured; adverse events only (Table 3).
- MEAC: **none reported**. Figure 5 is observed vs predicted mean concentrations during 4 days of repeated dosing,
  not an effect or MEAC plot.
- ke0 / tPeak: none.
- Potency: none.

## Covariates
None (weight mentioned only for comparison of CL with other studies).

## Caveats
Healthy men only; oral only, no IV, so CL and V are divided by unknown F; first-order NONMEM; the IR ka is very
variable (IIV 105%).
