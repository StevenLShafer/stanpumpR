# Vellinga 2025, SDC 3: haemodynamics and SpO2 by TCI step

Supplement to the main paper, which has its own card (`PMID_40067039.md`). The repo does not cite this file (context: no locations, no drugs).

## Study
- Same crossover study as the main paper (24 healthy volunteers, remimazolam effect-site TCI step-up then step-down, with remifentanil plasma TCI of 0, 0.5, or in session 3 of 0.1, 1.0, 2.0 or 4.0 ng/mL). See the main card for design and assay.
- This supplement gives mean [95% CI] of HR (bpm), MAP (mm Hg), SpO2 (%) and EtCO2 (mm Hg) at each remimazolam TCI target step (ng/mL), step-up rows then step-down rows (PDF pp. 1-3, Tables 1-6), and pp. 4-18 plot arterial SpO2 against time for each volunteer and session, with ephedrine 5 mg boluses and the times of the PD assessments (laryngoscopy tolerance, MOAA/S, tetanic stimulus) marked.
- Table layout is by remimazolam target. Targets in session 3 differ between remifentanil groups (Tables 3-6).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No ke0, C50, MEAC or potency ratio. Selected means (Table 1 and 2 verified on the rendered page; the others from the text layer). Remimazolam target ng/mL; step-up peak (2000) values:
- Session 1 (no remifentanil, Table 1): MAP 90.8 at target 0, 69.3 at 2000, 83.2 at the final 0; HR 66.0 to 83.2 at 2000, 72.3 at the end; SpO2 lowest 97.1 (1300 up); EtCO2 22.6-26.4.
- Session 2 (0.5 ng/mL remifentanil, Table 2): MAP 88.6 at 0, nadir 65.7 at 1300 up, 68.8 at 2000; HR 77.8 at 2000; SpO2 lowest 96.5 (300 up); EtCO2 rises from 25.8 to 32-36 once remifentanil is running.
- Session 3, 0.1 ng/mL (Table 3, targets 125-1700): MAP nadir 63.0 at 925 up; SpO2 lowest 96.6 (525 up); EtCO2 about 33-36.
- Session 3, 1.0 ng/mL (Table 4, targets 50-1300): MAP nadir 58.8 at 1300; SpO2 lowest 95.7 (400 up); EtCO2 up to 43.8 at 1300 up.
- Session 3, 2.0 ng/mL (Table 5, targets 100-1350 as printed; n = 4) and 4.0 ng/mL (Table 6, n = 2): intervals are very wide (some negative or over 100%) because of 1-4 observations per cell; Table 5 footnote says one EtCO2 cell had only 2 measurements and Table 6 says its first row is 1 observation with no EtCO2 data. No conclusion drawn here.
- Respiratory depression in these data is mild: lowest group mean SpO2 about 95.4% in the text layer of Table 5 (an interval row whose HR/MAP/SpO2 alignment is ambiguous in the text), otherwise above 95.7%. The authors' interpretation is in the main paper, not here.

## Covariates

## Caveats
- Means of small groups; at 2.0 and 4.0 ng/mL the 95% CIs are not interpretable (n = 4 and 2).
- In Tables 5 and 6 the text layer splits HR from the other columns across lines; I did not confirm row alignment on a page image, so do not use those rows without checking.
- Ephedrine boluses (Fig. 1 captions) confound MAP and HR; the tables do not say whether post-bolus values were excluded.
- Step order is time-ordered, so step-down values carry both lower concentration and time since start.
