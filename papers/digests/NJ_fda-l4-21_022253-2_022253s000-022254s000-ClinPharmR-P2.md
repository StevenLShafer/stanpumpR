# FDA 2008 (lacosamide NDA 22-253/22-254 Clin Pharm review, Part 2): individual study reviews and QT consult

## Study
This file is Part 2 of the FDA Office of Clinical Pharmacology review of the original lacosamide (SPM 927, LCM;
Schwarz Biosciences) NDA. It is the appendix of individual study reviews (section 4.2) plus the QT-IRT consult (4.3).
It holds no population-PK or exposure-response analysis; those are not in this part.

Studies reviewed (all oral, healthy volunteers unless stated; assay LC-MS/MS, plasma LOQ 0.01 ug/mL for LCM and
SPM 12809, urine LOQ 0.2 ug/mL, p 163):
- **SP587** single ascending dose 400/600/800 mg, healthy men, n = 12/12/9 (p 150).
- **SP836** 7-day multiple dose 100 mg qd, 200 mg qd, 200 mg bid; 21 Caucasian men, age 32 (19-39) y (pp 151-153).
- **SP588** 300 mg and 500 mg single dose, then bid for 13.5 days; 33 Caucasian men; 500 mg bid poorly tolerated,
  several reduced to 400 mg bid (pp 154-157, 249).
- **Renal impairment** (Part 1: single 100 mg in CLcr groups >=80, 50-80, 30-50, <30 mL/min, n = 8 each; Part 2:
  ESRD on haemodialysis, n = 8; all White; 2 sites in Germany, 2004) (pp 159-174).
- **SP642** hepatic impairment, 100 mg bid 4.5 days, Child-Pugh B vs matched healthy, n = 8 each (pp 174-183).
- **Elderly/sex**: 100 mg single then bid; elderly men (>=65 y, n = 11-12), elderly women (n = 12), young men
  18-45 y (n = 12) (pp 184-190).
- **Ethnicity**: 200 mg bid; 12 Asian, 12 Black, 12 White men (mean weights 70.9, 68.1, 77.0 kg) (pp 193-197).
- **SP600** food effect, 300 mg, 24 Caucasian men, age 30.5 y (pp 199-200).
- **SP863** omeprazole; **SP644** digoxin; **SP660** metformin; **SP599** oral contraceptive (pp 201-238).
- **SP640** thorough QT: 247 healthy subjects, LCM 400 or 800 mg/day, moxifloxacin, placebo (pp 239-250).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- No exposure-response (seizure) model, no Emax, no therapeutic range in this part.
- QT (SP640, pp 239-240): no QTc prolongation; lacosamide shortened QTcI (time-matched, placebo-adjusted change at
  tmax on day 6: -9.4 ms at 400 mg/day, -7.4 ms at 800 mg/day). A log-linear mixed-effects concentration-QTcI model
  was fitted (negative slope; coefficients not given in this part). The sponsor reported PR prolongation with
  concentration; the QT-IRT did not review it.
- Adverse effects dose-limiting: dizziness, nausea, vomiting (SP640 AE table p 242: dizziness 10% at 400 mg/day,
  54.9% at 800 mg/day; diplopia 0 vs 9.9%).
- Not an opioid; no MEAC, ke0 or potency ratio.

## Covariates
- **Renal function** (100 mg single dose, Tables 4-5, pp 165-167; geometric means): CL/F 2.13 L/h (CLcr >= 80),
  1.68 (50-80), 1.74 (30-50), 1.34 (<30); CLrenal 0.590, 0.354, 0.277, 0.143 L/h; t1/2 13.2, 18.2, 15.4, 18.3 h.
  AUC ratio vs normal 1.27 (mild), 1.22 (moderate), 1.59 (severe). Cmax ratios 1.10-1.14. AUC fell linearly with
  CLcr (Fig 2, p 166). SPM 12809 AUC rose 4.6-fold in severe impairment (Table 7, p 169).
- **Haemodialysis** (Table 8, p 170): 4-h high-flux dialysis lowered AUC about 50% (43.19 vs 23.19 ug.h/mL);
  extraction 57.4%; dialysis clearance 140.8 mL/min; 50.9 mg removed; t1/2 off dialysis 19.6 h.
- **Hepatic** (SP642, Table 2, p 180; Child-Pugh B): AUC(0-12)ss 85.89 vs 53.25 ug.h/mL (1.6-fold), Cmax 8.75 vs
  5.83, t1/2 24.1 vs 14.8 h; reviewer attributes part to coexisting low CLcr in 3 subjects (Fig 3, p 182).
- **Age/sex** (Tables 7-10, pp 189-190): steady-state AUC 54.7 (elderly men), 61.9 (elderly women), 41.2 ug.h/mL
  (young men); weight-normalised AUC ratios elderly men/young 1.26, elderly women/elderly men 0.98, elderly
  women/young 1.23. So the sex difference disappears on per-kg normalisation.
- **Race** (Tables 2-3, pp 195-196): AUC Asian 105.87, Black 104.79, White 94.95 ug.h/mL; weight-normalised ratios
  1.005 and 1.001; t1/2 15.8-16.0 h.
- **DDIs** (sponsor summary p 246): carbamazepine (SP603, SP618) changed lacosamide AUC and Cmax by <10%; valproate
  none; omeprazole +10% lacosamide AUC (SP863: CL/F 2.238 to 1.872 L/h, Table 4 p 209); metformin +6% AUC;
  digoxin none.

## Caveats
- Healthy, mostly young Caucasian men; no patients with epilepsy; NCA only.
- 100 mg renal study used a single dose with a short sampling window for the metabolite.
- Redacted (b(4)) in places, including the proposed maximum dose.
- The exposure-response analysis for seizures is not in this part; Parts 1 or 3 would have to be checked.
- The carbamazepine conclusion is from healthy-volunteer DDI trials (lacosamide added to CBZ at steady state);
  a population analysis in patients can give a different answer.
