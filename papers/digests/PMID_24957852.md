# Kreuer 2014: PK models for exhaled propofol (lung compartment vs ke0lung)

## Study
One adult patient, abdominal surgery, propofol TCI (Marsh model, constant target 3 ug/mL) plus remifentanil TCI (Minto, plasma mode). Exhaled propofol measured every minute for 346 min by ion-mobility spectrometer with multicapillary column (P-IMS), as volts (peak height) and ppb (calibration curve). Models fitted in Excel Solver to maximise R2. Single patient; no blood propofol measured.

## Model and parameters
Calculated plasma propofol from the Marsh set (Table 1, PDF p. 2): V1 = 0.228 x weight (kg) [L]; k10 0.1190, k12 0.1140, k21 0.0550, k13 0.0419, k31 0.0033 (unit not printed; presumably min^-1). V2 = V1*k12/k21, V3 = V1*k13/k31 (Fig 1).
Approach 1: added lung compartment, VLung 17 L (virtual), ClLung 3.75 L/min, k1L 0.209 min^-1, kL1 0.138 min^-1; second-order polynomial links lung conc to breath: R2 0.99 (ppb), 0.87 (volt). Approach 2: first-order delay of plasma concentration, ke0lung 0.27 min^-1, R2 0.96 (ppb), 0.82 (volt). Without a delay, plasma vs breath gives R2 0.32 (volt) / 0.41 (ppb), with a hysteresis loop (Fig 5).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
ke0lung = 0.27 min^-1 is plasma-to-exhaled-breath equilibration, not an effect-site ke0 (no EEG or clinical end point). Equivalent t1/2 (derived: ln2/0.27) = 2.6 min. No MEAC, no potency ratio. Remifentanil was only co-administered; no opioid PD reported.

## Covariates
Weight only, via Marsh V1.

## Caveats
One patient; plasma concentrations are Marsh-predicted, not measured (authors cite ~25% model error); polynomial calibration to volts/ppb; propofol lung "volume" is virtual.
