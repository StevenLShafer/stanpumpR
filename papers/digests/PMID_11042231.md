# Ingwersen 2000: tiagabine population PK, epilepsy monotherapy

## Study
Retrospective sparse-data population analysis of a phase III multicentre (39 centres, 12 countries), randomised,
double-blind trial of tiagabine vs carbamazepine monotherapy in newly diagnosed partial seizures (ages 12-85
eligible). 146 randomised to tiagabine; 135 had concentrations; 5 dropped for missing covariates -> 130 patients
(143/146 Caucasian). Table 1 (n = 130): age 40.1 +/- 18.4 y (13-84), weight 72.0 +/- 14.5 kg (44.3-120), height
171.0 +/- 10.1 cm (148-205); male/female 83/47. Dosing 2.5, 5, 7.5 or 10 mg b.i.d. (doses as tiagabine base;
HCl to base factor 0.91); 6-week titration, 44-week assessment. Sampling at weeks 3-55 with time of previous dose
recorded; doses assumed at 12-h intervals and at steady state; samples > 24 h post-dose excluded. 593 plasma
concentrations (1-7 per patient, median 5). HPLC with solid-phase extraction (Gustavson and Chu 1992), LLOQ
1 ng/mL. NONMEM IV (FO), ADVAN2/TRANS2, exponential IIV with full covariance, exponential residual error.

## Model and parameters
One compartment, first-order absorption and elimination, apparent oral (CL/f, V/f, ka). Final model (Table 4,
mean +/- SE):

- TVCL = 6.10 (+/- 0.16) x (HT/170)^(2.13 +/- 0.46) L/h
- TVV = 52.0 (+/- 3.8) x (HT/170)^(3.44 +/- 1.05) L
- TVKA = 1.25 +/- 0.26 /h

V/F 52.0 vs 62.0: the 52.0 L value appears three times in the body (Table 4, Results, Discussion) and is the one
consistent with the half-life (derived: 0.693 x 52.0/6.10 = 5.9 h; with 62.0 L it would be 7.0 h). The abstract's
62.0 L is very likely a typo. Empirical Bayes ranges: CL/f 2.99-10.4 L/h, V/f 15.6-137 L, ka 0.23-5.1 /h.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No concentration-effect analysis, no ke0, no therapeutic range proposed. Not an opioid paper.

## Covariates
Height only, on CL/F and V/F (power models above, normalised to 170 cm; each dOBJ > 34, P < 0.001). Weight, gender,
age, smoking, alcohol, bilirubin, AST, ALT, albumin and creatinine not retained; weight and gender added on top of
height were not significant. Enzyme inducers not allowed in the trial.

## Caveats
Sparse outpatient data; doses assumed regular and at steady state; no electronic compliance monitoring; FO method.
Few pathological liver/renal values, so those covariate tests have little power. Authors note their CL/F (6.10 L/h)
and V/F (52.0 L) are lower than earlier estimates (Samara 1998 noninduced 12.6 L/h; healthy volunteers 8.3-14.2
L/h; V 67-188 L) for unknown reasons, while half-life is in the expected range. Abstract/body inconsistency on V/F.
