# Vinik 1994: propofol-midazolam-alfentanil hypnotic synergism

## Study
- 130 unpremedicated ASA I-II adult patients, randomised, double-blind, in subgroups of 5, each given one fixed IV bolus dose or one fixed-ratio combination (p354-355). Groups of 20 patients each: M, P, A, MP, PA, MA, MPA (Table 2, p356); age 35-40 y, weight 72-76 kg, no group differences.
- **The paper does not add up, and the discrepancy is its own.** Seven groups of 20 is 140, while the abstract and Methods both say 130 patients. Table 2 settles that the groups really are 20 each, because its male/female counts sum to 20 in every row: M 11/9, P 9/11, A 10/10, MP 13/7, PA 11/9, MA 10/10, MPA 8/12. So both numbers are transcribed correctly here and the paper is internally inconsistent by ten patients; it is not an error in this card. CodeRabbit raised the arithmetic on PR #903 (2026-11-25) and suggested 15 patients each in the midazolam and alfentanil groups, which Table 2 contradicts. Anyone using the group sizes should say which of the two the paper's own analysis rests on; the ED50 fits are by dose subgroup rather than by total, so the count does not propagate into the ED50 values this card pins.
- End point: loss of ability to open eyes to two consecutive verbal commands 5 s apart (p355). Probit analysis of dose-response, ED50 with 95% confidence limits; isobolographic and fractional analysis; R = 1 / sum of ED50 fractions (R > 1 is synergism).
- Timing from injection to end-point test: midazolam 3 min, alfentanil 2.5 min, propofol 2 min, chosen from the authors' earlier times-to-peak-effect (p355). The paper states no numerical tPeak or ke0.
- Doses are IV boluses in mg/kg (Table 1, p355), not concentrations. No drug assay.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- Synergism R (Table 3): MP 1.7, PA 1.3, MA 2.8, MPA 2.5 (combined data). Abstract and Results text (this study alone) give 1.8, 1.4, 2.8, 2.6 (p354, p356). The triple was not significantly different from MA (p357-358).
- Alfentanil here is a hypnotic-potency study on eye opening, not analgesia: no MEAC or analgesic concentration, no ke0, no t1/2 ke0, no plasma/effect-site concentration, no equianalgesic ratio. Potency statements are bolus ED50s only: alfentanil 0.14 mg/kg alone vs 0.02-0.05 mg/kg in combinations; midazolam 0.27 mg/kg alone.
- Fractional ED50 in the triple: midazolam 0.12, propofol 0.15, alfentanil 0.12 (sum 0.40) (Table 3).
- The pre-test timing intervals (2-3 min) are the authors' empirical peak-effect times for bolus doses, not fitted values.

## Caveats
- Bolus ED50s in mg/kg for a single binary end point; not transferable to infusion concentrations without a PK model.
- 5 patients per dose subgroup; wide confidence limits (midazolam ED50 0.08-0.90 mg/kg in this study alone).
- Mechanism (PK vs PD) not determined by the authors (p357).
- Text-layer and printed values of R differ between the Results (1.8, 1.4, 2.6) and Table 3/Discussion (1.7, 1.3, 2.5) because the Results use this study alone and the table pools earlier data; both are recorded above.
