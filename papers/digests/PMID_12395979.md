# Kivikko 2002: levosimendan and metabolites, 24-h infusion in heart failure

## Study
Open, non-randomized phase II, 2 centres (Helsinki, Tallinn). 12 patients NYHA III-IV (11 III, 1 IV), age 62 +/- 9 y, weight 78 +/- 21 kg, height 173 +/- 9 cm, 10 M/2 F, EF 30 +/- 6% (Table 1). Levosimendan 0.2 µg/kg/min (the text layer's "mg" is the Symbol font's µ; see the resolved item) for 24 h on day 2. Venous plasma at 0, 2, 4, 8, 12, 24, 24.5, 25, 26, 28, 36, 48, 60 h, then daily to day 10, last at 13-15 days. LC-MS/MS; LLOQ 0.200 ng/mL levosimendan, 1.00 OR-1855, 0.500 OR-1896. Non-compartmental analysis, WinNonlin 1.5; CL = Dose/AUC0-inf, Vz = Dose/(AUC0-inf x lambda). Holter heart rate and BP followed 2 weeks.

Text (p4) rounds the half-lives to 1.3, 77 and 75 h. The text-layer copy of Table 2 was scrambled; the values above are from the image. No compartmental model. (derived: CL 3.71 mL/min/kg x 78 kg = 17.4 L/h; Vz 0.39 L/kg x 78 kg = 30 L.) Metabolites peak about 2-3 days after the infusion ends; levosimendan is gone from plasma by about 1.5 days (Figure 1).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No ke0, EC50 or MEAC; not an opioid. Observed effects: mean maximum heart-rate rise 10 +/- 8 bpm (Holter, p < 0.005), max fall in systolic/diastolic BP 12 +/- 14 / 8 +/- 10 mmHg, QTc maximum rise 27 +/- 25 ms at end of infusion. Effects persisted up to 3 days (HR, SBP) and tracked metabolite concentrations, not parent; 12-24 h after stopping, HR was up 13 +/- 8 bpm while parent was gone. Authors infer OR-1896 is the active metabolite (from preclinical work, not shown here). No concentration-effect model.

## Covariates
None analysed.

## Caveats
n = 12, no placebo, non-invasive end points, NCA only, dose-unit misprint in text (see open question), 2 patients had very low metabolite levels in the first 12 h, Vz is from AUC.
