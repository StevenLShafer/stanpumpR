# van den Elsen 1998: 0.1 MAC sevoflurane, desflurane, isoflurane and the hypercapnic ventilatory response

## Study
- 25 healthy volunteers (12 women, 13 men, 22-37 yr, nonsmokers). 16 did isoflurane and desflurane, 15 sevoflurane; 7 did all three. Sessions at least 2 weeks apart; control studies preceded drug studies (2-3 control and 2-3 drug each).
- End-tidal forcing: PETCO2 stepped up 1-1.5 kPa for about 8 min, then down for 8 min, PETO2 held at 14.5 kPa (normoxia). Responses split by a two-compartment model into a fast peripheral and a slow central component (GP, GC, apnoeic threshold B, time constants, delays).
- Target end-tidal concentrations: isoflurane 0.125%, desflurane 0.72%, sevoflurane 0.22% (stated approx. 0.1 MAC); measured mean (SD) 0.13 (0.04)%, 0.70 (0.04)%, 0.22 (0.04)% (Results, PDF p.3). Equilibration 20 min (isoflurane), 15 min (others).
- Sedation scored on the Leiden OAA/S scale (Table 1) at the start and end of each run.

Ratio GP/(GC+GP): isoflurane 0.34 -> 0.22 (P=0.01, 35% decrease); sevoflurane 0.28 -> 0.22 (ns); desflurane 0.27 -> 0.28 (ns).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: ventilatory CO2 sensitivity of the peripheral (GP) and central (GC) chemoreflex loops in normoxia. No analgesia end point; not an opioid paper.
- Isoflurane and sevoflurane at about 0.1 MAC cut peripheral CO2 sensitivity (45%, 27%) without changing central sensitivity or B. Desflurane had no significant effect (authors note a type II error cannot be excluded; GP fell in 9 of 16 subjects).
- Sedation: three subjects (all female) reached LOAA/S 3 in each agent; the others were 1 or 2. At score 3 central sensitivity was also depressed (isoflurane/desflurane DeltaGC; sevoflurane Deltaratio; P<0.05, Fig. 3).
- No MEAC, ke0, t1/2ke0, tPeak or hysteresis reported; wash-in kinetics not modelled.

## Covariates
Sedation score vs change from control (regression); sex noted only for the three score-3 subjects.

## Caveats
Subjective sedation scale, scores 1 and 2 hard to separate; only three subjects at score 3; small n; end-tidal concentrations set by manual vaporizer control; the desflurane null may be underpowered.
