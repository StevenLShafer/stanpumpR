# Kuizenga 2022: SmartPilot View in clinical practice

## Study
Two-centre (Bern, Groningen) prospective observational study, 2011-2017, NCT01467167. Sequential design: CONTROL phase first, then SPV phase with SmartPilot View v2.0 (Dräger) available. 493 enrolled, 358 analysed (CONTROL 170, SPV 188; volatile-anaesthetic cases excluded post hoc). ASA 1-3, age 18-90, BMI <35, mostly female gynaecological surgery under propofol TIVA with opioids at the anaesthetist's discretion; BIS blinded. SmartPilot shows demographically adjusted effect-site concentrations (Ce) and response-surface interaction predictions: PTOL (probability of tolerance of laryngoscopy), PTOSS, and NSRI (100 = minimal, 0 = maximal tolerance; NSRI 80/50/20 = PTOL 0.1/0.5/0.9). Primary end point: Anaesthesia Quality Score (% of 5-min MAP/BIS pairs with MAP 60-80 mm Hg and BIS 40-60). In CONTROL, fentanyl and sufentanil Ce were converted post hoc to remifentanil-equivalent Ce (CeREMIeq) using the same conversion factors as SmartPilot (refs 12-17); the factors themselves are not given in this paper.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- No MEAC, no analgesic concentration, no ke0, t½ke0, tPeak or hysteresis analysis is reported.
- Potency ratios: fentanyl and sufentanil are expressed as remifentanil-equivalent Ce using SmartPilot's factors (refs 12-17: Scott 1987, Minto 1997, Scott 1991, Gilron 1998, Bouillon 2003 x2); numeric ratios are not stated in this paper. The end point of the underlying interaction model is tolerance of laryngoscopy (Bouillon response surface).
- Observed Ce at extubation: propofol about 0.97-0.98 ug/mL (Table 2), compared by the authors with about 1.0 [0.8-1.7] ug/mL from Lee 2009 (printed p.968); remifentanil-equivalent 2.18 (CONTROL) vs 2.86 ng/mL (SPV). These are model-predicted Ce in routine care, not measured concentrations and not a MEAC.
- Intraoperative MAP and BIS AUC outside limits were lower with SPV (MAP above 80 AUC 11.1 vs 2.4, p=.001; BIS below 40 AUC 8.0 vs 1.0, p=.001, from the image-checked Table 2). Early PACU NRS 2.96 vs 1.97 (p=.001, text).

## Covariates
Demographically adjusted Ce is displayed; the covariate model is not described here. Centre differed for several outcomes (Bern showed larger effects).

## Caveats
Non-randomised sequential cohorts (sex, ASA and surgery type differed), mostly young female gynaecological patients, TIVA only, research fellow present in every case, MAP target range arbitrary, BIS blinded but MAP not. Authors conclude no change in average titration; small gains in haemodynamic and BIS control and early recovery. The text layer of Table 2 scrambled row labels and wrote the propofol unit as "mg ml-1"; the page image shows ug/mL.
