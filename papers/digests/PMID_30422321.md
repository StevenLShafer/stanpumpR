# Du 2019: two-compartment magnesium in preeclampsia, and simulated regimens

## Study
Reanalysis of the preeclamptic subset of Brookfield 2016 (PMID 26767791; Stanford IRB): 92 women with preeclampsia
of the original 111 (the 19 non-preeclamptic women were not used) (PDF p2). All received MgSO4.7H2O 4 g IV over
20 min then 2 g/h, continued 24 h after delivery; pre-delivery duration varied. Samples at baseline, 0.5, 1, 2, 4 h
and every 6 h during infusion, and 1, 3, 6, 9, 12 h after it stopped. 623 samples: 370 during and 253 after
treatment; 270 (43.3%) before and 353 (56.4%) after birth, 8 at delivery (PDF p5). Table 2 (PDF p5, rendered): age
30.0 (SD 7.3) y, 19-44; weight 90.3 (20.2) kg, 57-157; height 160.8 (7.2) cm; BMI 34.8 (6.5); baseline serum Mg
18.3 (2.2) mg/L, 14-25; serum creatinine 0.82 (0.29) mg/dL, 0.4-2.1; gestational age 34.73 (4.31) wk, 21.0-40.3.
Medians are not printed; covariates were centred on the median (85 kg, 0.8 mg/dL, as the equations show). Assay:
Siemens Dimension RxL Max, LLOQ 0.2 mmol/L; data in mg/L. NONMEM 7.3, FOCE-I. Dependent variable: change from each
woman's baseline. Creatinine imputed by LOCF (or carried back).

## Model and parameters
Two compartments, linear elimination, IV into the central compartment; 2-compartment beat 1-compartment by about 65
OFV points (PDF p8). Weight exponents fixed (0.75 on CL and Q, 1 on volumes); creatinine power on CL. Final model
(PDF p5-6): CL = 3.72 x (Cr/0.8)^theta x (WT/85)^0.75 x exp(eta + AP x eta_IOV); Vc = 15.4 x WT/85; Q = 3.66 x
(WT/85)^0.75; Vp = 17.0 x WT/85. AP is 1 antepartum, 0 postpartum and multiplies only the IOV term: there is no
fixed antepartum effect, although the Results text says CL was "further adjusted for" antepartum or postpartum
status (PDF p5). Age and gestational age were not significant. The dose unit used in the fit (salt or elemental Mg)
is not stated.

Simulations (fixed effects only, baseline 0.74 mmol/L = 18 mg/L added; PDF p5): Table 4 (PDF p10, rendered) gives
the maintenance doses matching the typical woman's 24-h average (Zuspan 1.6 mmol/L; Pritchard 1.8 mmol/L). Zuspan
g/h at Cr 0.5 / 0.8 / 1.2: 65 kg 1.0/0.8/0.6, 75 kg 1.2/0.9/0.7, 85 kg 1.3/1.0/0.8, 95 kg 1.5/1.1/0.9, 105 kg
1.6/1.2/1.0. Pritchard g q4h: 65 kg 5.3/3.2/1.7 ... 105 kg 9.8/6.8/4.9. Pritchard peak-to-trough 1.3-1.7 (8 g q6h
1.7-2.2; 10 g q8h 2.0-2.9) (PDF p6).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD model. Ranges used: putative therapeutic 1.5-2.5 mmol/L (simulation criterion) while the Introduction cites
2-3.5 mmol/L and a minimum of 2 mmol/L; toxicity from 3.5 mmol/L (patellar reflex loss 3.5-5, cardiac conduction
>7.5, arrest 12.5, respiratory depression about 12 mmol/L; PDF p10, all cited from Okusanya 2016). AUC used
arbitrarily as the comparison metric. No ke0.

## Covariates
Weight (allometric, fixed exponents, reference 85 kg) on all four; serum creatinine (mg/dL, ref 0.8) on CL only.

## Caveats
Change-from-baseline model assumes a constant endogenous baseline. All IV; IM absorption borrowed. Preeclamptic
women only, mostly obese (mean BMI 34.8). Table 3 lists Vc and Q IIV only for Vc; no IIV on Q or Vp.
