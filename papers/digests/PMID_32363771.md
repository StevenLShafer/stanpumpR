# Kang 2020: adalimumab-adbm vs Humira population PK

## Study
Three studies were modelled separately. Phase 1 (NCT02045979): 324 healthy men, single 40 mg SC dose of adalimumab-adbm, US-Humira or EU-Humira, intensive sampling to day 71, 7255 concentrations. Phase 3 base (NCT02137226): 644 RA patients on methotrexate, 40 mg SC every 2 weeks for 48 weeks, re-randomised at week 24 (Humira to Humira or to adalimumab-adbm), sparse sampling, 4342 concentrations. Phase 3 extension (NCT02640612): 430 RA patients, open-label adalimumab-adbm for 48 weeks, 2192 concentrations. Assay was ELISA on serum (text says "neat human plasma"), range 25-2000 ng/mL; ADA by 3-tier electrochemiluminescence. NONMEM 7.3. Phase 1 used FOCEI. The phase 3 models used full Bayesian MCMC, with the phase 1 estimates as informative priors on every PK parameter except CL/F.

## Model and parameters
Two-compartment, sequential zero-order then first-order absorption (D1, ka), linear elimination. Clearance and Q scale with (WT/70)^0.75; Vc and Vp scale with WT/70 (exponents fixed). All parameters are apparent (/F), in L and L/h, for a 70 kg reference subject. IIV values are variances, with CV in brackets. Tables 2-4 were read on page images.

Between-subject variability on CL/F is 0.137 (CV 38.4%) in phase 1, 0.122 (36.0%) in the base study and 0.114 (34.8%) in the extension. Vc/F is 0.533 (83.9%), 0.927 (124%) and 0.585 (89.2%). The base-study model also has inter-occasion variability on bioavailability, 0.0479 (CV 22.1%). Residual error is combined: proportional 0.00668, 0.0112 and 0.0367; additive 0.0198, 0.529 and 0.399, with the printed SD 0.141, 0.727 and 0.632 mg/L (Tables 2-4). Relative treatment effect on CL/F: Humira vs adalimumab-adbm 1.02 (0.981-1.05); switching arms Humira:Humira:BI 1.00 (0.940-1.08) and Humira:BI:BI 0.997 (0.922-1.08) (text, p10).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD. The end point is serum adalimumab concentration, with ADA titre as a covariate. No MEAC, ke0 or potency ratio. A covariate shifting clearance by more than 20% was treated as clinically meaningful.

## Covariates
Weight is allometric (fixed exponents). ADA presence and titre raise clearance: an ADA-negative subject has 0.421 of the reference clearance in phase 1, and ADA titre 64 gives about 28% more clearance than titre 16 in the base study. CRP and baseline rheumatoid factor raise clearance and albumin lowers it, but the effects are small. Healthy-volunteer CL/F is higher than that of the RA patients (0.0278 against 0.0244 and 0.0195 L/h); the paper attributes the extension study's lower value to selection of responders.

## Caveats
- The base-study VPC shows a bias in the medians, which the authors could not remove. It is identical across arms.
- The phase 3 volumes and Q/F rest on priors from healthy men, because the sparse data cannot inform them.
- All doses were 40 mg SC, so no dose-range information.
- The text mislabels one switching arm as "Humira:BI:BI" twice in the methods (p4); Tables and Results give Humira:Humira:BI and Humira:BI:BI.
