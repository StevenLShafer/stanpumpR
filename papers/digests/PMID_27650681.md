# Cirincione 2017: population PK of immediate-release exenatide (linear + Michaelis-Menten elimination)

## Study
- Retrospective pooled analysis of 8 studies (Table 1, PDF p3 / printed 519): SC weight-based doses 0.02-0.4 mcg/kg
  in T2DM (studies 1-4; n 6, 23, 8, 12), 5-day continuous IV infusion in healthy volunteers (studies 5 and 6,
  n 73 + 19 = 92, 2235 observations; a thorough-QT study with supratherapeutic concentrations), 10 mcg SC and 1 mcg
  IV bolus in T2DM (study 7, n 24), and 5 and 10 mcg SC in renal impairment (study 8, n 30; Linnebjerg 2007, ref 12).
  Profiles had at least 6 samples between 10 min and 18 h.
- Total 195 subjects, 5318 observations; 22% female, 67% Caucasian; age mean 48 (SD 12.9), 18-76 y; weight
  52.6-162 kg, mean 86 (16.2); eGFR mean 88.7 (31.4), as low as 4.1 mL/min/1.73 m2; renal class counts normal 129,
  mild 47, moderate 10, severe 1, ESRD 8 (p5 / 521). About 4% outliers and 3% BLQ excluded.
- Assay: two-site sandwich ELISA, exenatide-specific capture antibody (p3). NONMEM VI, FOCE-I; log-transformed
  additive residual error. External validation: 669 concentrations from 80 phase III subjects (5 then 5 or 10 mcg
  bid), sparse 0-3 h; slight overprediction.

## Model and parameters
Two compartments, parallel linear and Michaelis-Menten elimination from the central compartment; SC absorption
sequential: zero order for F(1 − fr)·Dose over τ, then a saturable depot (Eqs 1-5, PDF p4 / 520):
dA1/dt = −k1·A1 − K0, A1(0) = Dose; dA2/dt = k1·A1 + K0 − (Cld/Vc + Cl/Vc + Vmax/(Km·Vc + A2))·A2 + (Cld/Vp)·A3;
K0 = F(1 − fr)·Dose/τ for t ≤ τ, else 0; k1 = 0 for t ≤ τ, else F·fr·ka_max/(Km_ka + A1).
(fr enters both the amount left in the depot and the rate expression.) Absolute F estimated about 100% and fixed
at 1 (p4). τ fixed at 1.35 h from a sensitivity analysis (p4).

Reported derived results (p6 / 522): total clearance at 210 pg/mL 5.1, 6.3, 7.5 L/h at eGFR 50, 75, 100 (checked:
4.58·(50/80)^0.838 + 1.55/(567+210 pg/mL) = 3.09 + 2.0 = 5.1 L/h); about 75% of clearance linear at normal renal
function; observed tmax about 2 h after SC. Simulations (7500 subjects, Fig. 5): Cmax 256 pg/mL at normal renal
function and 90 kg; +7% (273) and +24% (321) Cmax and +12% and +48% AUC with mild (eGFR 75) and moderate (45)
impairment; Cmax 287 pg/mL at 75 kg, −18% (237) at 100 kg and −34% (189) at 125 kg. Saturation of absorption is not
expected at 5 and 10 mcg. Background (introduction, from the label): Cmax about 211 pg/mL at about 2 h after 10
mcg, apparent clearance 9.1 L/h, half-life 2.4 h.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD model. The discussion (p8 / 524) cites a minimum effective concentration of 50 pg/mL (ref 32) to argue that
heavier patients (about 125 kg) still exceed it; not derived in this paper. No glucose, insulin or gastric
emptying modelled.

## Covariates
MDRD eGFR (Eq 6: 175·Scr^−1.154·Age^−0.203·0.742 if female·1.212 if African American) on linear Cl only; total
body weight on Vc. Age, sex, race, IBW and BMI tested (univariate, ΔOFV ≥ 10.83) and not retained. Antibodies not
modelled (short exposures).

## Caveats
- The Michaelis-Menten pathway has no renal term; Km and absorption parameters imprecise (Km_ka RSE 54.9%,
  ka_max 42.5%); IIV on Km 96%.
- τ fixed; the equations are written for a single dose.
- Few severe/ESRD subjects (1 and 8). Vc exponent 2.67 is steep. External validation slightly overpredicted.
- Outliers (about 4%) and BLQ (3%) excluded.
