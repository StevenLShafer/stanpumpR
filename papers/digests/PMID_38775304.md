# D'Agate 2024: aciclovir population PK in neonates

## Study
Re-analysis of two earlier open-label IV aciclovir PK studies in neonates (Sampson 2014 data; NICHD BPCA database). Study 1: 23-42 wk GA, <61 d postnatal, suspected systemic infection. Study 2: 23-34 wk GA, <45 d, suspected systemic HSV (PDF p3). 32 infants, 92 plasma samples; 9 excluded (contaminated or drawn during infusion); final 83 samples from 28 infants (p5). Table 1 (median, range): GA 30 (23-40) wk, PMA 31 (25-41) wk, PNA 3 (1-30) d, weight 1420 (373-5720) g, SCr 0.9 (0.3-1.8) mg/dL; 17 F / 15 M. Original dosing regimens are in Table S1 (not in the PDF). Nonlinear mixed effects; bootstrap 2000, SVPC, NPDE, mirror plots. Assay not described here.

## Model and parameters
One-compartment, zero-order infusion input, first-order elimination (p5). CL = θ1·(WT/1.37)^0.75·(PMA/(θ3+PMA)) + CLCR (L/h); V = θ2·(WT/1.37)·DIS·θ4 (L). WT in kg, PMA in weeks, 1.37 kg is the median weight. CLCR is a time-varying Schwartz estimate (k = 0.33 if GA <36 wk, else 0.45; height cm / SCr mg/L), converted from mL/min/1.73 m² to L/h with Gehan-George BSA (pp3-4). The methods equation shows a Hill exponent; the final Table 2 equation has none (effectively Hill = 1) (p6).

Authors' comparison: median CLCR 15.8 vs median post-hoc total CL 71.0 mL/min/1.73 m², so CLCR is about one fourth of total CL (p6). Abstract: median CL 0.2 to 1.0 L/h for PMA <34 vs >=34 wk.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No PD, ke0 or concentration-effect data. Simulated steady-state AUC0-24 and Cmax by age group are in Table 3 (p9); units were not confirmed on the image and are not recorded here. Not an opioid paper.

## Covariates
Allometric weight (CL exponent 0.75, V exponent 1), PMA maturation (sigmoid, PMA50 = 50 wk fixed), CLCR additive on CL, systemic infection (study as proxy) on V. PNA, race and co-medication were not significant (p10).

## Caveats
n = 28; study used as proxy for disease status (confounded with other study differences); no repeat renal measures; CLCR rather than measured GFR; NPDE deviated from normal; IIV large (CL 75.5%, V 95.3%). Supporting tables S1-S5 are not in the PDF.
