# Su 2023: remifentanil-propofol haemodynamic interaction model

## Study
- Re-analysis of two healthy-volunteer trials, no surgical stimulus. Remifentanil alone: Weerink et al., NCT03143972, n = 30, Minto/Eleveld-driven TCI step-up to effect-site targets 1, 2, 3, 5, 7 ng/mL (Eleveld PK), 12 min each, arterial line BP (p223).
- Propofol alone (n = 36) and propofol plus remifentanil: Kuizenga et al., NCT02043938. Schnider-model effect-site targets 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2.5, 3.5, 4.5, 6, 7.5 mcg/mL up then down; on a second day, with remifentanil 2 or 4 ng/mL (Minto model) in two randomised groups. Non-invasive BP every minute. Subjects stratified by age and sex (median ages of the three age groups 22, 47, 60 or 66 y).
- Plasma concentrations used in the model were individual post hoc Eleveld PK for remifentanil and for propofol; no PK interaction was seen (p228). Measured plasma concentrations are in the supplement.
- NONMEM 7.5, FOCE-I; general pharmacodynamic interaction (GPDI) framework on a mechanism-based model of TPR, SV and HR with MAP feedback (extension of Su 2022 propofol model). Pulse pressure used as surrogate of SV (SV/PP = 1.5).

## Model and parameters
Structure (Box 2 and 3, PDF p6, read on the image): turnover for TPR, SV* and HR* (kin, shared kout, MAP feedback power FB); MAP = HR x SV x TPR. Remifentanil acts on kout (TPR: Emax with Hill 1; HR and SV: linear). Propofol acts on kin of TPR (sigmoid Emax) and SV (Emax). Interactions: remifentanil raises the propofol Emax on TPR (INT_TPR x C_remi/(EC50_remi + C_remi)); propofol raises the SV and HR remifentanil slopes (INT_SV, INT_HR with EC50_INT_HR). Propofol Emax on SV scales as exp(0.033*(age-35)).

Results as stated: propofol alone maximal effect on TPR production -78% (-77.8%), reduced to 47.5% and 31.2% with remifentanil 2 and 4 ng/mL (derived check: -0.78 + 2/(4.59+2) = -0.476; -0.78 + 4/(4.59+4) = -0.314, reproduced from Table 2 and eq. 12). Typical baselines MAP 75 mm Hg, HR 56, SV 83 mL (text; Table 2 BaseSV is 82.2). At propofol 1.1 mcg/mL MAP fell 5.7%, 14.6%, 21.5% with remifentanil 0, 2, 4 ng/mL; HR rose 52.3%, 12.3%, 1.9% (p227).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End points: MAP, HR, pulse pressure (as SV surrogate), via TPR/SV/HR turnover. Remifentanil is an opioid; this is a haemodynamic effect, not analgesia or EEG.
- Remifentanil alone: EC50 on TPR 4.59 ng/mL (plasma, Table 2; Hill 1 and Emax fixed). Plasma concentration-driven; effect-site targets from Minto's PK-PD were used to dose but not fitted.
- No MEAC or analgesic concentration. No ke0, t1/2 ke0, tPeak or hysteresis reported or fitted. No equianalgesic or potency ratio. Interaction is described as antagonistic on HR, synergistic on SV, mixed on MAP.
- Simulation of FDA-label dosing (propofol 1.5 mg/kg over 60 s then 0.1 mg/kg/min with remifentanil 0.1 mcg/kg/min), 70 kg men: median MAP fell 32.2%, 36.1%, 39.6% at ages 22, 47, 60 (the text elsewhere says 66); SV fell 14.0%, 23.6%, 32.3% (p227).

## Covariates
Age on the propofol Emax for SV (eq. 17, Table 2); age, weight, height, sex and BMI were explored for the others and not retained in the final table.

## Caveats
Healthy volunteers, no surgical stimulation, no vasoactive drugs; invasive BP in the remifentanil-alone group and non-invasive in the others; model is for post-induction haemodynamics and needs prospective validation (authors, p232). Remifentanil effect fitted to plasma concentration with Hill fixed at 1 because of unstable estimation. Table 2 lists some effects as "%" though they are fractions. The age of the oldest simulated subject is 60 in one place and 66 in another (PDF p1 text vs p6/p7).
