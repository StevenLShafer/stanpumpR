# Eckhardt 2000: gabapentin and morphine interaction in volunteers (cold pressor)

## Study
Randomised, double-dummy, double-blind 4-way crossover (days 1, 8, 15, 22), 12 healthy men. Medication I at 08:00
(morphine sulfate 60 mg controlled-release or placebo), medication II at 10:00 (gabapentin 600 mg or placebo).
Serum at 0-25 h (0.5, 1, 1.5, 2, 2.5, 3, 3.5, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10, 12, 24, 25 h), urine 0-25 h. Gabapentin by HPLC-UV
(TNBS derivatisation); morphine and glucuronides by HPLC-ESI-MS. NCA (TopFit 2.1). Cold pressor pain threshold and
tolerance at 0-6 h; adverse effects by VAS.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: cold pressor pain tolerance and threshold, AUC of % change from baseline over 0-6 h.
Pain tolerance: placebo+placebo 4.7 %·h (95% CI −16.7 to 26.1); placebo+GBP 18.9 (−2.5 to 40.3, NS);
morphine+placebo 40.6 (19.2-62.0); morphine+GBP 75.5 (54.0-96.9), significantly greater than morphine+placebo.
Opioid audit: no MEAC or minimum effective concentration; no ke0, t½ke0, tPeak or hysteresis; no equianalgesic or
potency ratio. Morphine concentrations are serum, total, in pmol/mL.

## Covariates
None.

## Caveats
12 healthy men, single doses, experimental (cold pressor) pain; gabapentin given 2 h after morphine; the PK
interaction (higher gabapentin AUC, possibly slower gut transit) confounds the PD interaction (authors).
