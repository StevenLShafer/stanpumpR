# Regardh 1974: metoprolol pharmacokinetics in healthy volunteers (3H-labelled, IV and oral)

## Study
- Five healthy volunteers, 23-28 y, 62-70 kg (mean 66 kg, Table II); sex not stated.
- Crossover: 5 mg 3H-metoprolol tartrate (60 uCi/mg) orally (100 mL weakly acidic solution) and IV as a constant infusion over 10 min, after an overnight fast (PDF p. 3).
- Blood from a cubital vein; plasma stored at -20 C; urine collected for 72 h.
- Assay: intact metoprolol by hexane extraction then scintillation counting; checked against gas-liquid chromatography (equal in plasma after both routes; after the oral dose the extraction overstated urinary parent drug by about two-fold). Metabolites estimated from total radioactivity, expressed as metoprolol. Matrix is plasma, total (not free). 13% protein binding is quoted from unpublished work (PDF p. 14).
- Blood/plasma ratio 1.23 at 10 ng/mL plasma, 1.09 at 23, 1.08 at 77 (Table I, text layer); the text rounds to 1.2 at low concentrations.
- Models: one- and two-compartment, Gauss-Newton on a PDP-8 and iterative stepping; AUC by numerical integration.

## Model and parameters
IV, two-compartment (Table II, PDF p. 7, image read; H.D. followed a one-compartment curve, so the rate constants are for n = 4):
- Mean (SEM): k12 2.2 (0.7) /h; k21 2.1 (0.4) /h; ke (renal) 0.050 (0.007) /h; kf (metabolic formation) 0.40 (0.04) /h; Vc 2.2 (0.3) L/kg; Vd,beta 5.6 (0.2) L/kg; t1/2 alpha 0.20 (0.05) h; t1/2 beta 3.2 (0.1) h.
- Individual Vc 1.3, 2.5, 2.3, 2.5 L/kg; Vd,beta 5.3, 6.3, 5.9, 5.0, 5.5 L/kg; weights 65, 70, 65, 62, 66 kg. The text gives absolute ranges: Vc 85-175 L, Vd,beta 310-440 L (PDF p. 8).
- Fig. 5 legend (PDF p. 10, text layer): ke 0.25 /h, k12 2.2, k21 2.1, kel 0.45 /h (kel = ke + kf).

Oral (Table III, PDF p. 9, image read): one-compartment fit ka 3.41 /h (iterative) or 3.61 +/- 0.46 (Gauss-Newton), KE 0.25-0.26 /h, lag time 0.16-0.18 h; the two-compartment fit reduced the tissue compartment to nothing (k12 ten-fold lower than IV). Absorption half-life 12 min in the text, 10 min in the abstract.

Bioavailability (Table IV, PDF p. 11, image read): AUC oral 25.7 vs IV 66.4 ng.h/mL (means), per-subject ratio 19.0-54.4%, mean 37.8% (SEM 6.2); text 0.38; abstract "about 40%".

Elimination: t1/2 3.2 h IV, 2.7 h oral (3.1 h from samples beyond 1.5 h). Renal clearance of parent drug 95 mL/min uncorrected, 109 mL/min after correcting for 13% binding (Table V, PDF p. 14); about 10% of the IV dose is excreted unchanged. Hepatic blood flow back-calculated 2.05 L/min, 1.5 L/min after correcting for blood/plasma and renal loss. Metabolite t1/2 5.2 h after IV vs 3.1 h oral (Table V).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None reported; the authors say the concentration-effect relation will be published separately. No ke0, tPeak, EC50, MEAC or therapeutic range. Not an opioid, so no opioid audit values.

## Covariates
None modelled (n = 5, narrow weight range). Renal clearance and urinary pH are discussed qualitatively.

## Caveats
- Tritiated tracer; the hexane extraction is not selective for the parent in urine after oral dosing, though it is in plasma (checked against GLC).
- Dose of 5 mg is small; sampling after the oral dose was too sparse to resolve distribution, so the oral fit is one-compartment.
- Mean rate constants are from four subjects; Table II means use n = 4 for the rate constants and n = 5 for t1/2 beta.
- The metabolite half-life difference by route is attributed by the authors to different pathways, and they call it preliminary.
- 1974 radiolabel assay of intact drug, not LC-MS/MS.

- Clearance: 65 L/h (derived from rate constants and Vc at 66 kg) against 65.8 L/h. **matches** (modulo the base/tartrate question).
- Total volume: Vc x (1 + k12/k21) = 2.2 x 2.05 = 4.5 L/kg, about 297 L at 66 kg (derived), against Lammers V1 + V2 = 284.6 L. **matches**.
- V1: 2.2 L/kg x 66 = 145 L against 187 L (Lammers is 29% larger; per kg at 80 kg 2.3 L/kg). Within the spread of Table II (1.3-2.5 L/kg).
- Terminal half-life: 3.2 h against 3.4 h from the Lammers parameters (derived). **matches**.
- Bioavailability: 0.38 (5 mg solution, n = 5) against 0.45 (100 mg tablet, 12 men); the library applies 0.45 (line 139). Different formulation and dose; the direction is consistent (first-pass about 55-62% lost), no conflict.
- ka: 3.4-3.6 /h with a 0.16-0.18 h lag for a solution, against 1.19 /h and tlag 0 for the tablet (line 201). A solution absorbs faster than a tablet, so expected; the library has no solution formulation.
- tPeak 5 min (line 140) rests on Ritchie 1998; Regardh has no effect data. **cannot verify**.
- Registry row: the statement that Regardh was unavailable is now out of date (the PDF is archived); nothing in it is contradicted. The Zamir ke 1.01 /h claim cannot be traced to Regardh (open question).
