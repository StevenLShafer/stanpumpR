# Duong 2013: metformin population PK across renal function (IR and XR)

## Study
- Pooled data, three studies (Table 1, PDF p. 5, printed p. 377; median (range)):
  - A: 120 T2DM patients (98 new plus 22 CKD patients published before), age 65 (39-86), TBW 86 (53-165) kg,
    CLCR 67 (15-127) mL/min, dose 1,500 (250-3,000) mg/day, IR or XR, steady state, sparse 1-6 samples
    (16 intensively sampled). 52 (43%) had CLCR < 60 mL/min, 13 below 30.
  - B: 16 healthy Caucasians (Timmins 2005), age 27, TBW 70 kg, CLCR 81 (63-102); IR 2,000 mg/day and XR
    500-2,000 mg/day at steady state, rich sampling.
  - C: 169 healthy Malaysians, single-dose bioequivalence studies, 500 (500-1,000) mg, TBW 58 kg, CLCR 90 (57-178).
  - Total printed as 305 in Table 1 but 304 in the text (PDF p. 4).
- CLCR by Cockcroft-Gault using lean body weight (Table 1 footnote; Janmahasatian).
- Assay: HPLC-UV plasma, LLOQ 0.05 mg/L, calibration 0.05-10 mg/L.
- NONMEM VI, FOCE-I; 4,895 concentrations; bootstrap n = 1,000; 57 transporter SNPs tested (study A only).

## Model and parameters
Two compartments; first-order absorption with lag for IR, zero-order input (duration D) for XR. All
apparent (F for IR and 2,000 mg XR fixed at 1). Table 2, PDF p. 6 (printed p. 378), confirmed on the rendered page.

Eq. 1: CL/F = theta_CL x (CLCR/6) x exp(PPV_CL), CLCR in L/h (6 L/h = 100 mL/min, derived).
Eq. 2: V1/F = theta_V1 x (TBW/70) x exp(PPV_V1). Q/F, V2/F and ka carry no covariates. No non-renal
clearance term (intercept of CL/F vs CLCR not different from 0).

Residual error by study (Table 2): A additive 0.018 mg/L, proportional 0.271; B 0.004 mg/L, 0.245;
C 0.001 mg/L, 0.131. Correlations CL-V1 0.505, CL-Q 0.710, CL-V2 0.688. Eta shrinkage 9.1-18.6%.

Observed (PDF p. 6): median CL/F 760 mL/min (A), 1,201 (B), 1,066 (C); CL/F/CLCR ratio 12.3 (5.6-42.5).
Cav,ss 1.28 mg/L (0.2-7.7) in A, 0.9 (0.6-1.1) in B. Simulated half-lives (PDF p. 9): about 13, 8 and 4.5 h
at CLCR 15, 30, 60 mL/min; about 3 h (IR, monophasic) at 120; XR biphasic 1.2 and 7 h.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- No PK/PD model. Upper concentration limit used for dosing: 5 mg/L (95th percentile of simulated Cmax),
  described as "considered elevated" from refs 16-17; the authors say the therapeutic range is unclear (PDF p. 2).
- Recommended maximum daily doses (IR or XR): 500, 1,000, 2,000, 3,000 mg at CLCR 15, 30, 60, 120 mL/min
  (Fig. 3, PDF p. 7, 9). 2,000 mg XR at CLCR 60 gave 5.4 mg/L (95th percentile); 3,000 mg XR at normal
  function 5.2 mg/L. Median Cav,ss at these doses 1.5 mg/L (1.1-1.5).
- Cav,ss vs HbA1c: weak positive correlation in monotherapy only (R^2 = 0.187); none with lactate.
- Not an opioid; no MEAC, ke0, tPeak or potency ratio.

## Covariates
CLCR (Cockcroft-Gault with LBW) linear on CL/F; TBW linear on V1/F. Not significant: age (once CLCR in),
diuretics (p = 0.104), any of 57 SNPs in OCT1/2/3, MATE1, PMAT (including reduced-function OCT1 alleles).

## Caveats
- The covariate reference is called the "median population value" (6 L/h for CLCR, 70 kg), but Table 1's
  median CLCR is 81 mL/min (4.9 L/h, derived) and median TBW 65 kg: the references are round numbers.
- F is apparent; IR F fixed to 1; XR F is relative to 2,000 mg XR. Compliance in study A self-reported.
- Mixed formulations, single-dose and steady-state data; healthy subjects not genotyped.

## Verification against stanpumpR
Independent comparison only (the paper is not used), so **not-applicable**; no discrepancy in any use of
this paper. Comparison with the library's Chae 2012 model (`R/drugs_metformin.R:20-22, 137-139`):
- **CL/F**: Chae 52.6 L/h x (CLcr/106.5)^0.782 (healthy Koreans, CLcr 90-123, single 500 mg). Duong
  72.7 L/h x CLCR/100 (derived). At CLCR 100 mL/min Chae gives about 50 L/h (derived), so Duong is about
  1.45x higher; at 15 mL/min both give about 11 L/h (derived: Chae 11.4, Duong 10.9). Duong's CLCR uses lean
  weight, which lowers CLCR for a given creatinine relative to total-weight Cockcroft-Gault; the library uses
  Cockcroft-Gault at its pharmacokinetic weight (switch on) or total weight (`R/drugs_metformin.R:127-129`),
  so the two CLCR scales are not the same and the 1.45x is not a like-for-like gap. Duong's healthy-subject
  medians (study B 72 L/h, study C 64 L/h, derived from mL/min) are also above Chae.
- **Renal impairment**: the library header says the 0.782 power is an extrapolation below CLcr 90
  (`R/drugs_metformin.R:30-33`). Duong, fitted on CLCR 15-127, found CL/F proportional to CLCR (exponent 1,
  no non-renal term). Between them the predicted fall in CL/F from normal to severe impairment differs
  (Chae ratio 0.23 vs Duong 0.15 from 100 to 15 mL/min, derived), with the CLCR-definition caveat above.
- **Distribution**: Chae V/F 113 L, one compartment, half-life 1.5 h at 70 kg (`R/drugs_metformin.R:51`);
  Duong two compartments, V1/F 149 L + V2/F 182 L, simulated IR half-life about 3 h at CLCR 120.
- **Absorption**: Chae ka 0.41 /h, no lag; Duong ka 0.35 /h with a 0.38 h lag.
- **Concentration ceiling**: Duong's 5 mg/L is an upper limit for dosing simulations, not a therapeutic range;
  consistent with `therapeuticRanges.csv:110` (none established).
- **XR**: the library does not model XR (`R/drugs_metformin.R:35-37`; registry row 51 points to a PAGE
  abstract). Duong gives a complete XR input (zero-order D 9.55 h, dose-dependent relative F), a possible
  source if XR is added.
