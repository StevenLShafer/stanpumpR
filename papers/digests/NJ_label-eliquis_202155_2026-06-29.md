# ELIQUIS label 2026: apixaban

## Study
US prescribing information for apixaban, a factor Xa inhibitor; 2.5 and 5 mg tablets, 0.5 mg tablets for oral
suspension and a 0.15 mg sprinkle capsule. The document is what its name says (Eliquis, NDA 202155, revised 06/2026).

## Model and parameters
No compartmental model. Dosing (2.1-2.6, PDF pp. 4-6):
- NVAF: 5 mg bid; 2.5 mg bid with at least two of age >= 80 y, weight <= 60 kg, serum creatinine >= 1.5 mg/dL.
- DVT prophylaxis after hip/knee replacement: 2.5 mg bid, first dose 12-24 h after surgery (35 / 12 days).
- DVT/PE treatment: 10 mg bid for 7 days, then 5 mg bid; recurrence reduction 2.5 mg bid after >= 6 months.
- Pediatric VTE (Table 1, PDF p. 5): weight-tiered, 0.3 mg bid (2.6-<4 kg) up to 10 mg bid then 5 mg bid (>= 35 kg).
- Surgery (2.4): stop at least 48 h before procedures with moderate/high bleeding risk, at least 24 h for low risk.
- Combined P-gp and strong CYP3A4 inhibitors: halve 5 or 10 mg bid doses; avoid with 2.5 mg bid (2.6).

About 25% of an oral dose is recovered as metabolites (mainly CYP3A4; minor 1A2, 2C8, 2C9, 2C19, 2J2); no active
circulating metabolites. Crushed tablets in water or via NG tube give similar exposure; in applesauce Cmax -20%, AUC
-16%. Aqueous solubility ~0.04 mg/mL (11, PDF p. 24).

Figure 2 (PDF p. 28, forest plot, values read graphically, no numbers printed): ketoconazole AUC ~2-fold,
clarithromycin, diltiazem, naproxen ~1.4-1.6-fold; rifampin AUC ~0.5-fold. Figure 3 (PDF p. 29, graphical): AUC
higher in severe renal impairment (CrCl 15 mL/min, regression) and ESRD; no dose adjustment for any renal group.
ESRD single 5 mg dose post-dialysis: AUC +36%; dosed 2 h before dialysis +17%; on dialysis -14% (p. 29-30).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Anti-FXa activity (Rotachrom Heparin chromogenic assay) rises with concentration, similar in healthy and AF subjects;
the label says it is not recommended for assessing anticoagulant effect (12.2, PDF p. 25). Pediatric anti-FXa vs
concentration slope "close to 1" (p. 26). PCC reverses ETP in 4 h vs 45 h with placebo. No QTc effect up to 50 mg.
No therapeutic range, no ke0. Not an opioid. Overdose: activated charcoal at 2 and 6 h after 20 mg cut AUC by 50% and
27% (10).

## Covariates
Renal (8.6, PDF pp. 22-23): NVAF dose reduction by the two-of-three rule; no adjustment in other indications,
including ESRD on dialysis; pediatric not recommended below eGFR 30 mL/min/1.73 m2 (>= 2 y) or the Table 12
thresholds (< 2 y). Hepatic (8.7, p. 24): no adjustment in Child-Pugh A; no recommendation for B; not recommended for
C. Sex and race: no meaningful difference (12.3, p. 29).

## Caveats
Label summary values, not a population model: the 3.3 L/h "total clearance" and the 21 L Vss are not stated to be
oral-apparent or IV; with F 0.5 they do not reconcile with the population CL/F (about 3.1-3.6 L/h in Cirincione
2018) without assumptions. Recorded, not resolved here. Figures 2 and 3 carry no numeric values.
