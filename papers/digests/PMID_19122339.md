# Honda 2008: population PK of landiolol hydrochloride in healthy men

## Study
- Three Phase I studies in Japan (Table 1, PDF p2/printed 448), 47 healthy **men**, 420 blood concentrations, aged 20-33 y, 50.7-77.2 kg (range only; no means given). Written consent, IRB approval.
- Doses are landiolol hydrochloride in ug/kg/min (the text layer drops the micro sign; confirmed ug on the rendered Table 1):
  - Study 1: 60-min infusions at 5, 10, 20, 40, 80 (pilot, 2 per rate); 40 or 80 (single, n=10); 80 x2 ten hours apart (n=5).
  - Study 2: 1-min load 250 then 40 for 60 min (n=5); 1-min load 500 then 80 for 60 min (n=5).
  - Study 3: dose escalation, LM group (n=6): L = 30 load 1 min then 10 for 10 min, then M = 60 then 20 for 10 min; MH group (n=6): M then H = 125 then 40 for 10 min. Placebo n=4 (not analysed).
- Sampling: venous blood, up to 24 h; 420 points = 61+90+51+48+51+57+62 (Table 1, sums checked). Samples were taken from a vein; the paper does not say which arm relative to the infusion in Methods (the Discussion says contralateral forearm).
- Assay: HPLC-UV after ethanol extraction of blood, C18 SPE, ethyl-acetate extraction; quantitation range printed as 50-104 ng/mL in the text layer (PDF p3, not rendered; the upper limit may be 10^4 with a lost superscript, so not relied on).
- NONMEM V 1.1, FOCE-I; one-, two-, three-compartment compared; AIC 5003.740, 4863.628, 4864.979 for the full data. Bootstrap (200 sets, 196 converged) and leave-one-out cross validation.
- Covariates were not explored (parameters already per kg; healthy young men).

## Model and parameters
Two-compartment model with an absorption **lag time**, parameters per kg. Exponential IIV on CL and V1 only; exponential residual error. Final model excludes four terminal-phase points (30 and 60 min after the end of infusion in subject 10, 30 min in subjects 25 and 35), judged outliers by leave-one-out; AIC one- vs two-compartment 4877.973 vs 4827.113; three-compartment did not converge (PDF p5, Results).

Full-data model before outlier removal, Table 2 (PDF p4): CL 35.5, V1 130 (mL/kg), Q 3.23 mL/min/kg, V2 78.9 mL/kg, lag 0.717 min; omega2 CL 0.0415 (20.4%), V1 0.0793 (28.2%); sigma2 0.0587. Superseded by Table 3. Note the text layer scrambles this table; the values above are from the rendered page.

Bootstrap, Table 3: means within +/-15% of the estimates except Q (-31.7%) and omega2 V1 (-41.1%); the authors say Q and omega2 V1 remain rather unstable.

**Those two percentages are the paper's own, and so is their sign.** Table 3's Difference column is
(final estimate - bootstrap mean) / final estimate, which the printed rows confirm: TVCL 36.6
against 36.4 is 0.5%, TVV1 101 against 94.0 is 6.9%, and Q 16.1 against 21.2 is (16.1 - 21.2)/16.1 =
-31.7% exactly. So a NEGATIVE difference means the bootstrap mean lies ABOVE the estimate, which is
the case for both Q (21.2 against 16.1) and omega2 V1 (0.214 against a bootstrap mean near 0.30).
Read the other way round the same comparison is +31.7%, and a reader who assumes the commoner
(bootstrap - estimate)/estimate convention will take the sign backwards (CodeRabbit raised exactly
that reading on PR #903, 2026-11-25; the value is left as the paper prints it and the convention is
named instead).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No effect measurement or concentration-response analysis, no ke0, t1/2ke0, tPeak or hysteresis, no therapeutic range. The paper gives landiolol elimination half-life as about 4 min (ref 2, a Japanese Phase I report) and beta1/beta2 selectivity 255 vs esmolol 33 (PDF p2). Not an opioid; no MEAC/equianalgesic content.

## Covariates
None tested (see Study). The authors warn that elderly, obese, hepatic or renal impairment and pregnancy are not represented (Discussion, PDF p8).

## Caveats
- Young healthy MEN ONLY, awake, with venous sampling. The paper describes them as healthy male subjects in studies conducted in Japan and states no ethnicity; "Japanese" is an inference from the location, which this card first made and the registry row still makes (CodeRabbit, PR #903, 2026-11-25).
- Q and omega2 V1 unstable in bootstrap; the central volume is driven by 1-2 minute concentrations after the load.
- Lag time (0.82 min) is a venous-sampling artefact in the authors' reading (forearm infusion, contralateral sampling); the authors cite lags of 0.96 min (propofol) and 1.88 min (alfentanil) as comparable.
- Four terminal points removed from the final fit.
- Matrix is 'blood'; basis (hydrochloride vs base) of concentrations not stated.

## Verification against stanpumpR
- Values quoted at R/drugs_landiolol.R:36-38 and vasoactiveRegistry.R:215 (CL 36.6, V1 101, Q 16.1, V2 55.6 mL/kg or mL/min/kg, lag 0.82 min; 47 healthy men): **match** Table 3. Units per kg and minutes match.
- The matrix: **this card's discrepancy has been acted on and is now resolved.** The paper says blood throughout -- 420 blood concentration data points, landiolol extracted from blood in ethanol -- and never calls its samples plasma. When this card was written the registry row read 'plasma'; on current master (f237b705) it reads **'whole blood (venous)'**, and `R/drugs_landiololHonda.R` describes the model as venous whole blood. The paper does not use the phrase "whole blood", so that refinement goes beyond what it states, but the direction was right and the repository and the paper now agree.
- vasoactiveRegistry.R:215 says age and weight, needed by the selector, are only in the full text: the paper gives only ranges (20-33 y, 50.7-77.2 kg), no means or SDs.
- A sibling candidate for comparison: CL 30.7 and Q 48.3 **mL/min/kg**, V1 65.0 and V2 54.4 **mL/kg** (this card first gave all four as mL/kg, a volume unit for two clearances). Against Honda: CL 16% lower; V1 36% lower (Honda 101); Q about 3x higher (Honda 16.1, but Honda's Q is the unstable one); V2 2% lower; Vss 119 vs 157 mL/kg (derived). The library header's remark that clearances agree within 20% and central volumes differ (65 to 101) is borne out.
- Lag: the library leaves it out (R/drugs_landiolol.R:52-57); Honda's lag is 0.82 min vs 0.633 in Kunisawa; curves would be about 49 s early on Honda's value (derived).
- tPeak 2 min (R/drugs_landiolol.R:102) comes from Krumpl 2017, not this paper; this paper reports none.
