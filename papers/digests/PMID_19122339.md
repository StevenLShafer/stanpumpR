# Honda 2008: landiolol population PK in 47 healthy men

## Study
- Pooled Ono Pharmaceutical phase 1 studies in Japan (Table 1, PDF p2): 47 healthy men, 20-33 years, 50.7-77.2 kg (Methods). Study 1: 60-min infusions of 5-80 mcg/kg/min (pilot, single dose, and two infusions 10 h apart); study 2: a 1-min load of 250 or 500 then 40 or 80 mcg/kg/min for 60 min; study 3: escalating 1-min loads and 10-min infusions.
- 420 venous whole-blood concentrations of landiolol hydrochloride; blood extracted into ethanol, solid-phase extraction, HPLC with UV, quantified from 50 ng/mL.
- NONMEM V, FOCE with interaction. One, two and three compartments compared by AIC (5003.7, 4863.6, 4865.0); two compartments with a lag. Covariates not explored: parameters per kg, laboratory values all normal.
- Model evaluation by bootstrap (200 data sets, 196 converged) and leave-one-out cross-validation. Four outlying points were removed before the final model: subject 10 at 30 and 60 min after the end of an infusion, and subjects 25 and 35 at 30 min.

## Model and parameters
Two compartments per kilogram with a lag on the infusion; exponential IIV on CL and V1; exponential residual error.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. Not an opioid; no MEAC.

## Covariates
None explored (see Study).

## Caveats
- Healthy awake young men only; venous whole blood, where both Kunisawa studies measured arterial plasma with esterases stopped.
- Q and its IIV were the least stable parameters in the bootstrap (median 15.8 against a mean of 21.2).
- The dose and concentration are both landiolol hydrochloride, so the per-kg parameters do not depend on the salt.
