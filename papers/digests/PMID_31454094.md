# Jain 2019: omarigliptin population PK-PD (DPP-4 inhibition)

## Study
Population PK (and sequential PK-PD) of once-weekly oral omarigliptin from 13 phase 1 studies, 1 phase 2b (study 006) and 4 phase 3 studies (011, 015, 019, 020), base-study data only (Methods, printed p.2761-2763; Table 1).
- PK: 9827 concentrations from 1387 subjects (321 healthy, 1066 T2DM), single and multiple doses 0.25-400 mg q.w. (Table 2, printed p.2765). Phase 1 rich sampling; phase 2b and 3 sparse. Mean age 54.1 (SD 13.3) y overall (first row of Table 2 is age; the text layer shifts row labels, so read the row order on the rendered page before reusing), weight 82.0 (24.3) kg overall. Renal categories: normal 476, mild 747, moderate 105, severe 39, ESRD 20.
- PD: plasma DPP-4 activity (mOD/min) from 6 phase 1 studies and study 006; 5435 observations from 800 individuals (78% T2DM). Modelled as measured activity, not percent inhibition.
- Software: NONMEM 7.1.2; SAS 9.2 and KIWI for data handling.
- Concentrations are total plasma omarigliptin, in nM in the model. Assay is cited to earlier papers, not described here.

## Model and parameters
Oral two-compartment model with first-order absorption after a zero-order input (D1), linear unbound clearance, and saturable binding to DPP-4 (reversible, competitive) in both central and peripheral compartments. The paper's total plasma concentration is unbound plus bound. Allometric weight scaling with fixed exponents 0.75 (CL, Q) and 1 (volumes); eGFR power effect on unbound CL/F. Reference weight and eGFR for the allometric and eGFR terms are not stated in the main text (see open questions). Final estimates are printed to two figures on the log scale, so back-transformed values carry about 5% rounding error (derived).

Exposure results (printed p.2768): steady-state AUC0-168 with 25 mg q.w. is 1.17x (mild), 1.41x (moderate), 1.82x (severe), 2.94x (ESRD) that of normal renal function; 12.5 mg q.w. in severe and ESRD gives exposure within 1.5-fold of normal on 25 mg. Accumulation across doses minimal (observed week 3/week 1 ratios 1.03-1.35 for AUC, 0.87-1.36 Cmax). Terminal half-life >100 h (intro, printed p.2760).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- Direct-effect sigmoid Imax model on plasma DPP-4 activity (Table 4, PDF p.8, printed 2766): baseline 20.1 mOD/min; IC50 3.83 nM (%SEM 2.65, IIV 37.3 %CV); Imax fixed 1.00; Hill coefficient 1.10 (%SEM 2.34); residual error additive 2.16 mOD/min plus proportional 0.0616. IC80 13.6 nM (text, printed p.2765).
- Concentration is total plasma omarigliptin, not unbound; the authors state the effect was not tied to unbound concentration in the saturable compartment. End point: plasma DPP-4 enzyme activity. No effect-site delay: no ke0, t1/2ke0 or hysteresis model (direct effect).
- Covariates on baseline: Asian race +0.137, T2DM status +0.0876, female sex +0.0740 (Table 4, log-type effects; text says all differences <14%); baseline in T2DM 9.4% higher than healthy.
- 25 mg q.w. keeps median DPP-4 inhibition above 85% over the dosing interval in T2DM (printed p.2768); more than 80% inhibition is described as associated with full clinical response; observed week-12 inhibition 80.7% (phase 2). Missed dose scenarios can lower inhibition below 80% until the next dose (printed p.2769).
- No MEAC or analgesic concentration (not an opioid). Potency ratios: none.

## Covariates
PK: weight (allometric, fixed exponents), eGFR on unbound CL/F (exponent 0.44), patient status on V2 (T2DM 9.1% higher), food and formulation on D1. Not significant: race, ethnicity, age, sex, comedication. Simulated weight 40-180 kg keeps steady-state AUC0-168 roughly within 0.5-2.0x of a typical patient (printed p.2768). Haemodialysis removes about 5-15% (data not shown).

## Caveats
- Large IIV and eta-shrinkage on several PK terms (17-77%); KON %SEM 54%.
- DPP-4 assay residual error exceeds 200% CV at the lowest activity (1 mOD/min); possible censoring.
- The apparent renal-impairment increase is modest (1.82x severe) compared with sitagliptin, alogliptin and trelagliptin (Table 5, printed p.2771: alogliptin ratios about 1.2, ~2, ~3-4 for mild, moderate, severe; those alogliptin ratios are quoted from other work, with CLr 160 mL/min and fu 0.80 in Table 6, not from this analysis).
- Sponsor-authored (Merck).
