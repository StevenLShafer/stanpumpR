# Palahniuk 1974: MAC is lower in pregnant ewes near term

## Study
Laboratory note (animal study). Six pregnant ewes, all within three weeks of term (breeding date and x-ray), and six
nonpregnant ewes, mixed breeds from the same flock (PDF p1, printed p82). Each animal received isoflurane, halothane
and methoxyflurane in sequence, at least 48 h apart, with no other drug than the agent and oxygen. Mask induction,
intubation, controlled ventilation (12 breaths/min, 7-10 mL/kg). End-tidal sampling near the distal tip of the
tube; isoflurane and methoxyflurane by infrared analyser, halothane by ultraviolet analyser. MAC determined in
triplicate; stimulus a surgical clamp applied to the ear for one minute; end-tidal concentration held at least
15 min before each test; positive response = any movement of limbs, head or trunk. Normothermic (temperature varied
less than 0.5 C); systolic pressure varied less than 10% where measured (one third of animals). Student's unpaired
t test.

## Model and parameters

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: gross movement to a one-minute ear clamp (a surgical clamp to the ear, not the tail). Stated reductions
in pregnancy: halothane 25%, isoflurane 40%, methoxyflurane 32% (abstract, Results, Table 1). Arithmetic on the
group means (derived): halothane 1 - 0.73/0.97 = 24.7%; isoflurane 1 - 1.01/1.58 = 36.1%; methoxyflurane
1 - 0.18/0.26 = 30.8%. The isoflurane figure of 40% does not follow from the two printed means (36%); the paper
does not say how "Change" was computed (for example from individual animals), so the cause is unresolved. No ke0,
EC50 or potency ratio. The authors suggest progesterone (tenfold to twentyfold higher in late pregnancy) as a
possible cause, without measuring it.

## Covariates
Pregnancy (last three weeks of gestation) as a group effect only.

## Caveats
- Sheep, not humans; six animals per group; different animals in the two groups.
- Ear-clamp stimulus; order of agents fixed (isoflurane, halothane, methoxyflurane).
- The stated isoflurane change (-40%) is larger than the ratio of the printed means (-36%).
- Hormone levels not measured.
