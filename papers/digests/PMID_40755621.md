# Chatzopoulou 2025: alpha-stat vs pH-stat in hypothermic circulatory arrest

## Study
Systematic review (PRISMA 2020) of acid-base management (alpha-stat vs pH-stat) during hypothermic circulatory arrest
for aortic arch/ascending aorta surgery. Databases: PubMed, Cochrane, CINAHL, ScienceDirect, 2010-2025 (p.2).
Flow (p.3): 42 records, 10 duplicates removed, 32 screened, 14 excluded on abstract, 18 full texts, 14 excluded,
3 added from reference lists, 7 included. Table 1 (PDF p.3) lists 7 items (3 RCTs, 1 crossover, 3 reviews/meta-analyses)
in children and adults. The text describes the set as RCTs although several are reviews or non-randomised.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No drug PD, ke0, MEAC or potency ratios. Outcomes are cerebral blood flow, SjvO2, CEO2, CMRO2 and neurological outcome.
Qualitative conclusion: pH-stat gives faster cooling and higher cerebral oxygen delivery in children; alpha-stat
preserves autoregulation and is favoured in adults; a hybrid (pH-stat cooling, alpha-stat rewarming) is suggested.
Figure 6 (PDF p.8) describes the Vienna protocol: 28-32 C moderate hypothermia with alpha-stat.

## Covariates
None (age group only, qualitatively).

## Caveats
Heterogeneous, small studies; surrogate end points; English-only; no large multicentre trials (p.8). The study-type labels in
Table 1 do not always match the cited references (e.g., the Voicu 2014 reference is a crossover study in post-arrest
patients, not circulatory arrest surgery).
