# Laffont 2024: IN naloxone vs IN nalmefene in the Mann (FDA) overdose model

## Study
Simulation study (Indivior-funded; authors employed by Indivior / Simulations Plus). Re-implements the Mann 2022 FDA
translational model (PK → competitive µ-receptor binding → ventilatory drives → gas exchange / cardiac arrest),
adds population PK sub-models for IN nalmefene 3 mg HCl and IN naloxone 4 mg HCl, validates against the Ellison
remifentanil respiratory-depression study, then simulates 2000 virtual chronic users and opioid-naive subjects
after IV fentanyl 1.63 / 2.97 mg or carfentanil 0.012 / 0.022 mg.
The IN naloxone PK model used only the Ellison pharmacodynamic study (healthy, opioid-experienced non-dependent
volunteers breathing 7% CO2 by mask; remifentanil 0.5 µg/kg bolus + 0.175 µg/kg/min, naloxone 4 mg IN at 25 min).
Number of subjects and sampling are in Supplementary Tables 1-2, not in the article.

## Model and parameters
IN naloxone (Table 2): two-compartment, linear elimination, parallel zero-order and lagged first-order absorption.

No absolute bioavailability (no IV reference in the naloxone data). Note the source mixes apparent (CL/F, Vc/F) and
absolute (Yassen Q, Vp fixed) parameters. IN nalmefene (Table 1): CL/F 63.7 L/h × (WT/74.7)^0.572, Vc/F 15.2 L,
Q/F 81.3 L/h, Vp/F 522 L, INKA 0.497 /h, D2 0.302 h, zero-order fraction 0.0485, lag 0.0615 h, FR IN vs IM 0.834.
Derived: mean input time of the naloxone mixture = 0.183 × 0.689/2 + 0.817 × (0.0717 + 1/0.998) = 0.940 h.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Mann model ke0 for naloxone (and assumed for nalmefene) 0.001774 s⁻¹ (= 0.106 min⁻¹, derived), plasma-to-brain;
naloxone binding steepness n 0.86; remifentanil ke0 0.0218 s⁻¹ (t½ 0.53 min, Olofsen), remifentanil kon 8.08E-06,
koff 2.08E-03 s⁻¹, n 0.70 (0.75 after adjustment). End point: minute ventilation; simulated cardiac arrest (total
blood flow 0.01 L/min). Example (Table 3, chronic users, fentanyl 1.63 mg): no antagonist 52.1%, IM naloxone 2 mg
29.5%, IN naloxone 4 mg 19.2%, 4 × 4 mg 3.8%, IN nalmefene 3 mg 2.2%. No MEAC; no potency ratio stated (nalmefene
binding scaled from Cassel). Naloxone t½ quoted ~2 h (cited).

## Covariates
None for naloxone. Weight on nalmefene CL/F (not clinically relevant per authors).

## Caveats
Sponsor-funded comparison favouring the sponsor's product; simulation, not outcome data; naloxone PK from one
hypercapnic mask study; antagonist given 1 min after ventilation fell to 40% (optimistic); IIV on Vc/F 240%.
n and sampling of the naloxone PK data not in the main text.
