# Woillard 2011: tacrolimus popPK, Prograf and Advagraf (renal transplant)

## Study
- Two PK studies pooled. Study 1 (Prograf, immediate release, twice daily): 32 de novo adult renal transplant recipients, 145 profiles at week 1, week 2, month 1, 3 and 6. Initial dose 0.1 mg/kg/day, then adjusted to trough targets (10-15 ng/mL for 6 weeks, then 5-10). Study 2 (Advagraf, once daily): 41 stable adults more than 12 months after transplant, converted from ciclosporin more than 6 months earlier, one profile each (PDF p.2).
- Table 1 (PDF p.3, medians (range)): Prograf / Advagraf. Age 55 (18-69) / 53 (28-77) y. Sex M/F 19/13 and 19/22. Weight 65 (46-97) / 69 (45-116) kg (P = 0.0217). Haematocrit 32.3 (20.9-46.6) / 38.5 (26.5-45.1) %. Tacrolimus dose 4 (0.5-9) / 4 (2-10) mg/day. Prednisolone 20 (0-94) / 2.5 (0-10) mg/day. CYP3A5 *1/*1 0 / 1, *1/*3 1 / 4, *3/*3 31 / 36 (so 67 of 73 non-expressers).
- Sampling: Prograf pre-dose and 0.33, 0.66, 1, 1.5, 2, 3, 4, 6, 9 h (plus 12 h at weeks 1-2); Advagraf the same plus 12 and 24 h. Dosed 0.5 h before a meal. Whole-blood assay by turbulent-flow chromatography-MS/MS, LLOQ 1 ug/L, calibrated to 100 ug/L, bias -4.4 to 0.6% (PDF p.3, printed 393).
- NONMEM VI with Wings for NONMEM, FOCE. Exponential IIV and IOV. A Bayesian estimator was built from the 0, 1 and 3 h samples (building set n = 49, validation set n = 24).

## Model and parameters
Two compartments, first-order elimination, absorption by an Erlang model with three transit compartments (ADVAN5 SS5). All parameters are apparent (divided by an unmeasured oral bioavailability F). Concentrations are whole blood, ng/mL. Dose unit is mg. A mixture model on Ktr was tried (Table 3: Ktr 5.74 /h for 69% of patients and 1.94 /h for the rest) and rejected in favour of the non-mixture model with a study covariate; the final model is Table 4.

Table 4 (PDF p.7, printed 397), whole data set, typical values (SE):
- Ktr = th1 x th2^study: th1 3.34 /h (0.51), th2 1.53 (0.40); IIV 24%, IOV 33%.
- CL/F = th3 x (HT/35)^th4 x th5^CYP: th3 21.2 L/h (1.3), th4 -1.14 (0.54), th5 2.00 (0.73); IIV 28%, IOV 31%.
- Q/F 79 L/h (4); IIV 54%.
- Vc/F = th6 x th7^study: th6 486 L (11), th7 0.29 (0.10); IIV 31%, IOV 75%.
- Vp/F 271 L (7); IIV 60%.
- study = 0 for Advagraf, 1 for Prograf. CYP = 0 for CYP3A5 non-expressers (*3/*3), 1 for expressers. HT is haematocrit in %. Proportional error 11.3%, additive 0.71 ng/mL.
- Model-building set (n = 49): Ktr 3.39 x 1.50^study; CL/F 21.6 x (HT/35)^-1.32 x 2.09^CYP; Q/F 82; Vc/F 463 x 0.30^study; Vp/F 329.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. This is a PK paper; no efficacy or toxicity end point was modelled. Target ranges quoted in the Introduction (PDF p.2): trough 10-15 then 5-10 ng/mL in the Prograf study, and a consensus AUC(0-12 h) of 150-200 ng.h/mL for Prograf in adult renal transplant (the authors cite a European consensus). Not an opioid; no MEAC, ke0, tPeak or potency ratio.

## Covariates
- CL/F: haematocrit (inverse, power -1.14 on HT/35) and CYP3A5 expresser (x 2.00). Reported in the Discussion (PDF p.9): CL/F 21 vs 42 L/h for non-expressers vs expressers, and a haematocrit rise from 30% to 40% lowers CL/F from 25.3 to 18.2 L/h in non-expressers (derived check: 21.2 x (30/35)^-1.14 = 25.3 and x (40/35)^-1.14 = 18.2, both agree).
- Vc/F and Ktr: formulation (study factor), confounded with time since transplant (Advagraf patients all stable, median 9 years; Prograf profiles all within 6 months).
- Body weight, age, sex, prednisolone above 10 mg/day, creatinine were tested; weight and the others dropped (backward elimination, OFV rise of 10.83 needed).

## Caveats
- The two formulations were studied in different patients at different times after transplant, so the formulation effect on Vc/F and Ktr cannot be separated from post-transplant time (authors' own point).
- No intravenous data: all parameters apparent.
- Only 6 of 73 patients were CYP3A5 expressers; th5 has SE 0.73 on 2.00.
- For 7 Prograf patients (21 profiles) the pre-dose value stood in for the 12 h concentration in the reference AUC (validation only).
- Interacting drugs other than azoles were not recorded in the Prograf study.
- Prednisolone dose differs sharply between studies (median 20 vs 2.5 mg/day, Table 1) and was not retained as a covariate.
- Prograf-only estimator performance (validation, n = 44 profiles): AUC bias 1.3%, RMSE 8.5% (Table 5, PDF p.10).
