# Murdoch 2000: patient-maintained propofol sedation, safety in volunteers

## Study
Ten healthy adult volunteers (age 18-40; median 31 [23-40]; weight 77.5 kg [57-86]; 9 M : 1 F; Table 1) were told to try to anaesthetise themselves with a patient-maintained propofol target-controlled infusion (TCI). Hardware was a Graseby 3400 pump driven by a backbar microprocessor running a propofol PK model (the paper cites White and Kenny 1990, ref 5, and shows an "effect site" figure from White 1999, ref 6; the text names no parameter set, and age and weight are entered). Starting target 1 ug/mL; each double press of the button within 1 s raised the target by 0.2 ug/mL; lockout 2 min after a successful press; maximum target 3 ug/mL; target stepped down toward a minimum of 0.2 ug/mL after 6 min with no successful press (refs 3, 4). The infusion was 1% propofol with lignocaine 20 mg per 50 mL. The study began once the displayed effect-site concentration was within 0.1 ug/mL of the target.
Monitoring: HR and SpO2 continuous; NIBP, ventilatory frequency and modified Steward score every 5 min. Oversedation = loss of response to verbal command. Stop criteria: unresponsive, three failed double presses or 3 min without one, airway loss, SpO2 below 94%, or target at 3 ug/mL. A keyword was read at the end and recall tested 1 h later.

Outcomes: two of 10 became oversedated (80% not), one unrousable to pain at 1.4 ug/mL with loss of eyelash reflex; two did not respond to speech. No significant SpO2 fall or airway loss. In 7 subjects the maximum Ct was within 0.2 ug/mL of the Ct at which they first failed to double press. Three could not double press at maximum sedation but could single press. No spontaneous keyword recall; one recognised it from a list of 10.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No ke0, t1/2ke0, tPeak or EC50 reported. The end points are clinical (Steward score, response to command, keyword recall). Effect-site values were displayed by the system but not reported. The authors suggest the 2 min lockout and 0.2 ug/mL increments do not allow blood-effect-site equilibration. Not an opioid paper.

## Covariates
Age and weight entered into the TCI model; no covariate analysis.

## Caveats
n = 10, healthy, mostly male, no surgical stimulus. Predicted (not measured) concentrations. The PK set is not named, so the predicted concentrations cannot be tied to a specific model.
