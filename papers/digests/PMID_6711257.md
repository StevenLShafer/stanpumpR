# Kanto 1984: midazolam after delivery at caesarean section under epidural

## Study
11 healthy women at term, elective caesarean section under lumbar epidural bupivacaine 0.5% 20-25 mL (plus caudal lidocaine 2% 4-6 mL), age 21-36 y (mean 31), weight 63-89 kg (mean 76), height 154-172 cm (mean 164); 6 primiparas, 5 multiparas (PDF p1, printed 116). Only women who wanted a sedative were included. Midazolam hydrochloride **0.075 mg/kg IV after the baby was delivered** (p1). Sedation (1-5 scale, rated by J.K.), respiratory rate, heart rate and blood pressure followed for 3 h; recall asked about ~2 h after dosing (p1). In **6 of the 11**, serum was sampled up to 6 h (measurable only to 5 h; lower limit 1 ng/mL) (Fig. 1, p2, printed 117).

Assay: **radioreceptor assay** (benzodiazepine receptor, ³H-flunitrazepam displacement), calibrated with midazolam standards 3.26-3257.7 ng/mL, so it measures **midazolam plus its active metabolites** as midazolam-equivalent binding activity, not parent drug (p1-2). Conjugated metabolites are not detected (p3). PK by a two-compartment open model (citing Allonen 1981); software not stated. Statistics: Wilcoxon, Spearman.

## Model and parameters
Two-compartment, individual fits; Table 1 (PDF p3, printed 118), confirmed on the rendered page. Per-kg units; "Vdα" and "Vdβ" as labelled (Vdβ is the area/terminal volume).

The authors call the clearance higher and the half-life shorter **than earlier gas-chromatography (parent-drug) studies**, citing 5.8 to 8.9 mL/kg/min and 1.5-2.5 h (refs 9-11, nonpregnant subjects, p3). They offer supine hepatic blood flow, epidural sympathetic block, or altered hepatic metabolism in pregnancy as explanations; there was no control group.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- Sedation: all 11 were at grade 4 (asleep, not easily arousable) or 5 (unable to communicate) at 3 min; most at grade 2 by 60-90 min; none sedated at 3 h (p2).
- Concentration-sedation correlation up to 3 h, r = 0.861 to 0.973 (Spearman; Fig. 1). No EC50, ke0 or concentration-effect model.
- Anterograde amnesia 30-60 min in 10 of 11; no retrograde amnesia (p2). No abnormal cardiorespiratory values.

## Covariates
None analysed (n = 6 for PK).

## Caveats
- The assay counts receptor-active metabolites (notably 1-hydroxymethylmidazolam) as midazolam, so these are not parent-drug parameters.
- n = 6, sampled to 6 h with concentrations measurable only to 5 h, which likely shortens t½β and inflates clearance.
- Dosed after delivery, under epidural block, supine, after fluid loading: the immediate postpartum state, not pregnancy, and no nonpregnant comparison within the study.
- Wide individual spread (CL 7.2 to 19.3 mL/min/kg).
