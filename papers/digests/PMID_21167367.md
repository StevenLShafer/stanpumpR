# Pruthi 2011: methylene blue PK after subareolar injection, nonpregnant women

## Study
10 nonpregnant women (pre- or postmenopausal; mean age 49.6 y, range 30-67; 9 White, 1 East Asian; normal
creatinine) having sentinel node biopsy for breast cancer, Mayo Clinic, June-December 2006 (Table 1, PDF p3).
Weight and height not reported. Dose: 0.5 mL methylene blue diluted in 3.5 mL saline, total 5 mg, subareolar
(PDF p2). Blood (EDTA) before and 0.25, 0.5, 1, 2, 4, 8, 24 h; urine 0-24 and 24-48 h. Assay: LC-MS/MS on
plasma, red cells and urine; methanolic zinc sulfate reoxidises leuco-MB, so the analyte is TOTAL methylene blue
(oxidised plus reduced) (PDF pp 2-4). LLOQ 100 pg/mL, linear to 1 ug/mL. Noncompartmental analysis, WinNonlin 4.1.
8 of 10 had mastectomy, which removed the injection site (nipple-areolar complex) during the sampling period.

Consistency (derived): 5 mg / 164 mL/min = 30.5 ug.min/mL, close to the AUC median 32.2; 144 L x ln2 / 11.1 h =
9.0 L/h, close to CL/F 9.84 L/h.

Fetal estimate (PDF p5, printed 74): assuming 100% of the subareolar dose reaches the circulation and using Link
1996's IV organ-distribution data (about 5% of dose per organ at 3 h), up to 5% of the dose, 0.25 mg, could reach
the fetus. Pregnancy adjustments are qualitative only (weight +25%, body water +30%, cardiac output +40%, plasma
volume +50-70%, GFR +40-65%); no pregnant subject was studied and no pregnancy PK parameter is computed.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No concentration-effect data, no ke0. Not an opioid. Toxicity context only: intra-amniotic doses of 2.5-4 mg
and above linked to intestinal atresia, haemolysis and fetal or neonatal death (PDF pp 2, 4).

## Covariates
None analysed.

## Caveats
Small n; subareolar, not intravenous, so CL/F and V/F carry an unknown F (the authors assume complete absorption
for the fetal estimate). Mastectomy removed the depot in 8 of 10, possibly lowering exposure (authors' caveat).
Sampling to 24 h only against an 11-45 h half-life, so the terminal phase and AUC extrapolation are uncertain.
Total (oxidised plus leuco) methylene blue measured. Weight not given. Table 2 AUC range misprinted.
