# Ogungbenro 2016: IL-1Ra dose-ranging design and updated population PK

## Study
Design (simulation plus optimal sampling with PopDes) and population PK analysis of an open-label multicentre dose-ranging study of IV anakinra (IL-1Ra) in SAH patients (Salford and Cambridge, Dec 2007-Dec 2008). Prior model: Gueorguieva 2008 (PMID_17875190), re-estimated in NONMEM VI (FO) and used to simulate 1000 subjects per regimen over boluses of 100-1000 mg and 4 h infusions of 1-10 mg/kg/h; target CSF concentration 100 ng/mL within about 30 min (target from rat neuroprotection studies; 100 +/- 82 ng/mL mean +/- SD). Chosen regimen 500 mg bolus + 10 mg/kg/h for 4 h (lowest CSF Cmax that reaches the target); four lower regimens added for safety (Table 2). Study: 25 patients, 5 per regimen. Updated analysis combined these with the 6 analysed patients of the earlier study (100 mg bolus + 2 mg/kg/h x 24 h), 31 subjects; 699 samples new (358 plasma, 341 CSF) plus 256 old (124 plasma, 132 CSF). NONMEM VII FOCE-I, two-compartment plasma + one-compartment CSF (V_CSF fixed to V1; no return from CSF), baseline endogenous IL-1Ra included (Eq. 1), exponential BSV, proportional residual error; 1000 bootstrap runs (914 minimised). Assay: not restated here (ng/mL IL-1Ra; total).

Vss (derived: V1+V2) = 9.30 L updated. The "previous" column (CL 7.8, V1 4.43, CLD 1.14, V2 8.89) differs from Gueorguieva's published Table 1 (6.96, 5.14, 0.63, 4.96) because those were re-estimated here (FO, NONMEM VI).

Optimal sampling (Table 3): 10, 300, 360, 530(540 in text), 1440 min; final 15-point design.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD model, ke0, EC50 or MEAC. Target CSF concentration 100 ng/mL (preclinical neuroprotection, total IL-1Ra, CSF, rat). No potency ratio (not an opioid).

## Covariates
None tested; baseline endogenous IL-1Ra in plasma and CSF modelled as an additive offset (Eq. 1).

## Caveats
Previous-model data had only 6 patients; "previous" column here is a re-fit, not Gueorguieva's printed values; bootstrap success 914/1000; CSF compartment volume fixed to V1; no weight scaling, though doses were per kg; no free vs total distinction.

## Used by stanpumpR

CL, V1, CLD and V2** as a parallel system serving intravenous doses only, anchored since 2026-11-28 at Galea 2011's
