# Ogungbenro 2016: anakinra (IL-1Ra) IV dose-ranging in subarachnoid haemorrhage

## Study
- Design of a phase II dose-ranging study by optimal design (PopED, from the earlier model), then an updated population analysis of the combined data.
- n = 31 adults with subarachnoid haemorrhage: 6 from the earlier study (100 mg IV bolus, then 2 mg/kg/h for 24 h; Gueorguieva 2008) and 25 from the dose-ranging study (Salford and Cambridge, December 2007 to December 2008), five per regimen of a bolus with a simultaneous 4-h infusion: 100 mg + 4, 200 + 2, 300 + 2, 400 + 6 and 500 mg + 10 mg/kg/h (Table 2). Demographics are in Galea 2011 (reference 15), not printed here.
- Plasma and CSF (external ventricular drain) sampled together at 15 time points; IL-1Ra by ELISA.

## Model and parameters
Two compartments with first-order elimination for plasma; one CSF compartment filled from plasma at CLIN and emptied at CLOUT, with no return to plasma (assumed). NONMEM VII; exponential between-subject variability; residual error proportional to an additive endogenous baseline (Eq. 1). Table 1, updated model:

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. The target was a CSF concentration of 100 ng/mL (from rats) within about 30 minutes; Table 2 gives the simulated and observed times to it.

## Covariates
None tested or retained.

## Caveats
- Subarachnoid haemorrhage, not sepsis; renal function not reported.
- The CSF compartment is not drawn by stanpumpR.

## Used by stanpumpR
