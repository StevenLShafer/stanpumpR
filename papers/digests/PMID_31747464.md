# Sempio 2020: population PK of smoked THC, 11-OH-THC and THCCOOH

## Study
- Re-analysis of a published NIDA Phase I crossover (Huestis 1992, ref 14; data in that paper). 6 healthy male
  cannabis users, mean age 31.3 y (29-36), mean weight 77.6 kg (64.8-93.4) (p613, Results).
- Cigarettes of 0, 15.8 (low) or 33.8 mg (high) THC smoked at weekly intervals by computer-paced protocol
  (2-s inhale, 10-s hold, 72-s exhale/rest, 8 puffs) (p612).
- Plasma THC, 11-OH-THC, THCCOOH by GC-MS, LLOQ 0.5 ng/mL each; sampling to 168 h, including during smoking.
  Metabolite concentrations normalised to THC molecular weight (THCCOOH x 0.913 = 314.45/344.4) (p612).
- Phoenix NLME 8.1, FOCE ELS; log-normal IIV; proportional residual error for THC; BLQ by M3 (p612-613).
- **Inhaled bioavailability was not estimated: it was assumed to be 0.25** "for simplicity", following refs 10, 16
  (p613). A relative bioavailability f between the two doses was estimated.
- Sequential: individual THC Bayes estimates fixed, then metabolites fitted. Assumptions: THC CLe = conversion to
  11-OH-THC + direct conversion to THCCOOH (via a fixed delay); 11-OH-THC elimination = its conversion to THCCOOH;
  11-OH-THC and THCCOOH share the central volume (p613). No covariates tested (n = 6).

## Model and parameters
Three-compartment THC model (Figure 1); 11-OH-THC three-compartment; THCCOOH one-compartment sharing the
11-OH-THC central volume. Table 2 (p4, printed 614), typical value ± SEE, confirmed on the page image.
All volumes and clearances are conditional on the assumed F = 0.25 (dose entering the model = 0.25 x smoked dose).

Model selection (Table 1, text layer): THC -2LL 2200 / 1989 / 1863 for 1/2/3 compartments (per Results text),
1785 with interoccasion variability, 1781 (AIC 1813) with the dose-ratio parameter.
Terminal half-life of THC stated as about 3.5 days (p615), from the typical values.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No effect measurements, no ke0/tPeak, no therapeutic or toxic concentration. The Introduction notes
11-OH-THC is equipotent to THC (p612, not a result of this study).

## Covariates
None tested (n = 6, demographically similar). Dose effect: f = 0.89 on relative bioavailability; high-dose split 0.88
on THC->11-OH-THC conversion. 11-OH-THC was nonlinear between doses (similar concentrations after both).

## Caveats
- n = 6 men; SEEs very small, authors attribute to small n; no bootstrap (p615).
- All absolute parameters hinge on the assumed F = 0.25. The authors argue it is close because their CLe 0.72 L/min
  matches Heuberger 2015's absolute 0.65 L/min (comparator: Heuberger's IV/oral/inhaled fit); their Vss 3401 L is far
  larger than Heuberger's 236 L (p615).
- Internal inconsistencies: Discussion gives Vss 581.1 L (11-OH-THC) and 22.8 L (THCCOOH) against the abstract's
  415.2 and 29.1 L (Table 2 sums to 415.2); Discussion says 74% of THC goes to 11-OH-THC against the abstract's 50%
  (= 0.36/0.72); Table 2 footnote calls f the high:low bioavailability ratio, the Results text "low:high".
- The worked steady-state example (p618) multiplies THCCOOH by 43.2 L/h, which is THC's CLe (0.72 L/min), not
  THCCOOH's 0.12 L/min (= 7.2 L/h, derived), and uses 0.8875 as the MW ratio where Methods give 0.913.
- 11-OH-THC low dose systematically underpredicted; THC late points near the LLOQ (none measurable at 12 h, low dose).
