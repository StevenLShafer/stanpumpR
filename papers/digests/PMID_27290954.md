# Wang 2016: TCI vs MCI for asleep-awake-asleep epilepsy surgery

## Study
Single-centre prospective study (Xuanwu Hospital, Beijing), July 2011-Sept 2013, 71 consecutive ASA I-II patients aged 18-60. Groups: TCI-E n=18 and MCI-E n=16 (eloquent-area foci, LMA removed for language testing); TCI-M n=17 and MCI-M n=20 (motor-area foci, LMA kept). The text says 34 eloquent and 37 motor cases. 26 unpublished preliminary cases were used to choose targets for Chinese patients.
All patients got dexmedetomidine 0.1 ug/kg/h without a loading dose, in all phases.
- Phase 1 induction. TCI: propofol Marsh model, plasma target 3 ug/mL; remifentanil Minto model, plasma target 4 ng/mL (plasma rather than effect-site, to avoid overshoot); cis-atracurium 0.2 mg/kg. MCI: propofol 2 mg/kg, remifentanil 1 ug/kg, cis-atracurium 0.2 mg/kg.
- Phase 2 maintenance. TCI propofol 1-3 ug/mL, remifentanil 2-4 ng/mL; MCI propofol 2-4 mg/kg/h, remifentanil 0.05-0.2 ug/kg/min.
- Phase 3 awake, from 10 min before wake-up. TCI propofol 0.5-1 ug/mL, remifentanil 1.5-2 ng/mL; MCI propofol 1-2 mg/kg/h, remifentanil 0.05-0.1 ug/kg/min. Wake-up attempted at BIS 60-75 (motor groups) or 70-95 (eloquent groups).
- Phase 4 closure: previous concentrations restored.

## Model and parameters
No PK model fitted; Marsh (propofol) and Minto (remifentanil) were run by a Chinese TCI device (Silugao).

Results:
- Awakening time (reduction of drugs to purposeful motor or verbal response; PDF p.4, image): TCI-E 12.82+-6.93 vs MCI-E 29.9+-9.04 min (P=.000); TCI-M 16.8+-5.19 vs MCI-M 30.91+-15.32 min (P=.010).
- BIS in the awake phase highest in TCI-E at all time points; TCI-M above MCI-M at seven time points (figures only).
- Adverse events (Table 2, PDF p.7, text layer): tachycardia (HR>110) TCI-E 3 (16.7%), MCI-E 9 (52.9%; P=.001), TCI-M 1 (6.3%), MCI-M 2 (10%); hypertension 1, 5 (29.4%; P=.064), 1, 1. Percentages for MCI-E match n=17 although n=16 is given (derived: 9/17=52.9%, 9/16=56%). Respiratory depression (SaO2<90%) 4, 4, 0, 0.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No ke0, t1/2 ke0, tPeak, EC50, MEAC or equianalgesic ratio reported. No drug concentrations were measured. End points are BIS, awakening time and haemodynamics. The authors cite Xu 2009 for lower propofol/remifentanil C50 (and BIS) at loss of consciousness in Chinese patients than in published white populations but give no values here.

## Covariates
None analysed (race discussed, not quantified; the authors say it was not accounted for).

## Caveats
Drug delivery not blinded; targets in the TCI arm came from preliminary work while MCI rates were by experience, so TCI vs MCI is confounded with dose level; drug amounts not compared; muscle relaxant unrestricted in phase 4; wake-up BIS thresholds differ by group; Table 1 and some prose unreadable in the text layer.
