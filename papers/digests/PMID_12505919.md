# De Castro 2003: TCI vs weight-based remifentanil in carotid surgery

## Study
Prospective randomized trial, 46 ASA II-III patients for carotid endarterectomy (23 per group), Paris. All had propofol TCI (CNS target 3 ug/mL, then 1.6 after intubation; actual body weight; BIS 40-60), atracurium 0.5 mg/kg, 50% N2O/O2, midazolam 5 mg oral premedication. Group I (RIVA): remifentanil 0.5 ug/kg/min over 60 s for induction, 0.25 ug/kg/min for intubation, then steps of 0.05 ug/kg/min by hemodynamics. Group II (TCIR): Minto model on Rugloop, effect-site target 4 ng/mL at induction, then steps of 1 ng/mL. Events: SBP <80 mmHg >1 min = hypotension; SBP >160, HR >90, HR <40 likewise. Unpaired t / chi-squared tests. No drug concentrations were measured.

## Model and parameters
No model was fitted; the Minto 1997 PK/PD model (refs 30, 31) was used as published. No parameters are reported.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- No MEAC, ke0, t1/2ke0, tPeak or potency ratio reported (opioid audit: none).
- Effect-site target (Minto model, predicted not measured): 4 ng/mL at induction before first laryngoscopy; intraoperative requirement 2-5 ng/mL (Discussion, PDF p4-5, printed p37). Surgery described as moderately painful. The authors cite Drover 1998 for 4.1-7.5 ng/mL (plasma, abdominal surgery) and say their CNS target was about half that of Guignard 2000.
- Results (Table 2, PDF p4): intraoperative hypotension 16 (RIVA) vs 6 (TCIR), P<0.001; hypertension 0 vs 2; tachycardia 0 vs 0; bradycardia 0 vs 1; postoperative beta-blocker 16 vs 10, P=0.04; morphine titration 4 vs 3, ns. Table 3: remifentanil total 1390 +/- 555 vs 700 +/- 290 ug (P<0.001), 12 +/- 4 vs 6 +/- 2 ug/min; propofol 637 +/- 196 vs 665 +/- 275 mg, ns.

## Covariates
Table 1: age 68 +/- 8 vs 70 +/- 9 yr; weight 72 +/- 12 vs 71 +/- 14 kg; male/female 19/4 vs 18/5. Groups similar.

## Caveats
Not blinded as to mode of delivery in any stated way; no concentration measurements; the dose difference confounds mode of delivery (authors attribute the benefit to lower cumulative dose and slower delivery). The text layer scrambles Tables 1-2 (values above are from the rendered page).
