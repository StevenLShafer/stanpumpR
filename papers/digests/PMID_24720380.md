# Glen 2014: predictive performance of Diprifusor (Marsh), Schnider and White propofol models

## Study
- Computer re-simulation of an earlier clinical study (Swinhoe 1998, ref 8): 41 of 46 adult surgical patients given propofol by Diprifusor TCI, with arterial blood propofol measured by HPLC (530 samples in all). Ages 18-80 in three age groups; patients more than 20% over ideal weight were excluded.
- Induction target 4 ug/mL (two younger groups) or 3 ug/mL (oldest); alfentanil 30 ug/kg before induction; N2O/O2 ventilation. Samples before and 2 and 5 min after start, around each target increase (>25%) or decrease (>1.5 ug/mL), 5 min after stopping, and at eye opening (13 sampling events, Figure 1 legend).
- Method: TIVAtrainer reproduced each patient's target profile with the Diprifusor model. The Schnider and White parameter sets were then applied to that same infusion-rate profile to get predicted concentrations. Performance error PE% = (measured - predicted) / predicted x 100 (Varvel 1992); per-patient MDPE (bias) and MDAPE (inaccuracy). Wilcoxon signed rank, Bonferroni (p < 0.017); repeated-measures ANOVA for age x model and sex x model.
- The Marsh k12 in TIVAtrainer was changed from 0.112 to 0.114 /min (page 2 text says this is the Diprifusor value and the Marsh publication has a typo); the corrected set is called "Diprifusor".
- Caveat on design: the infusion was driven by the Diprifusor model, so measured concentrations reflect Diprifusor delivery; the Schnider and White concentrations are simulated predictions for a profile those models did not drive.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD end point. No ke0, t½ke0, tPeak or effect-site model is estimated (the Discussion only comments that studies comparing effect-site concentrations at induction and recovery wrongly assume the model's bias is the same at both times). Not an opioid paper; no MEAC or potency ratio. Alfentanil was given with induction.

## Covariates
White model: age and sex on V1 and Cl1 (equations in ref 7, not here). Schnider: V1 fixed 4.27 L; age on V2 and Cl2; weight, height and LBM on Cl1. In this paper's subgroup analysis (Table 5, text layer only): in 56-80 y and in males the White model had less bias than both others; in females Schnider was more biased than Diprifusor or White. Individual MDPE correlated negatively with individual clearance (r = -0.87 Diprifusor, -0.79 Schnider, -0.84 White).

|---|---|---|---|
| MDPE % | 16 (-3 to 31 [27-84]) | 15 (4-37 [-23 to 73]) | 5 (-11 to 19 [-33 to 60]) |
| MDAPE % | 26 (21-41 [11-84]) | 23 (14-73 [7-73]) | 19 (16-32 [6-60]) |

As printed, the Diprifusor MDPE range shows "27-84" without a minus sign; Table 5 and the other columns suggest the lower limit is probably negative, but that is not confirmed. White MDPE p < 0.0001 vs both; White MDAPE p < 0.0001 vs Diprifusor. Table 4 (text layer): median PE steady target Diprifusor 24, Schnider 17, White 11; rising target 37, 13, 14; falling target -10, 23, -9. Positive PE = measured above predicted (models under-predict).

## Caveats
- Retrospective simulation; blood (not plasma) propofol by HPLC; patients on alfentanil, N2O and other drugs. The authors note the first-sample overshoot after target changes may reflect incomplete mixing.
- The White model was derived by one of the authors (White) on a related TCI dataset (113 patients, ref 7), and authors Glen and White have Diprifusor ties; Glen declares consultancy for Anaesthesia Technology Ltd. The comparison favours a model built on Marsh-style TCI data from similar patients.
- Delivered drug was set by the Diprifusor model, so the Schnider and White "performance" is a simulation on that profile.
- Subgroup sex imbalance (27 M : 14 F; older group 16 M : 3 F).
