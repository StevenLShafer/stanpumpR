# Neef 1994: apomorphine population PK, SC and intranasal, NPEM vs other programs

## Study
Eight patients with Parkinson's disease on levodopa >5 y with on-off fluctuations (Table 1, PDF p. 2, printed p. 144): age 56.5 +/- 12.0 y (29-69), 6 M / 2 F, weight 67.3 +/- 6.9 kg (51-78; per patient 75, 55, 78, 65, 74, 70, 51, 70), height 170 cm (158-180). SC bolus 1-5 mg (mean 1.86 +/- 1.15 mg, per Table 3 footnote), 14 data sets; intranasal 1-4 mg in six of them (mean 2.58 +/- 1.78 mg), 12 data sets. Plasma/serum sampled 2 h via IV catheter: every 5 or 10 min in the first hour, every 15 or 30 min in the second. Assay: apomorphine in plasma with an explicit polynomial assay-SD function (ref 31); total vs free not stated. Programs: USC*PACK NPEM (SC only, treated as a brief IV infusion under 5 min), BOXES/PC-ADAPT (weighted least squares), MW/PHARM (KINFIT, KINBES deconvolution, Bayes). Methods compared: naive pooling, standard two-stage, NPEM.

## Model and parameters
Parameters: Vslope (L/kg; VOL = Vslope x weight), Kel (1/h; Kel = Ki + Ks x creatinine clearance, Ks fixed 0) and Ka (1/h). Table 2 (PDF p. 3, printed p. 145, read on image):

Derived (my calculation): clearance = Kel x Vslope x 67.3 kg = 1.5341 x 1.7408 x 67.3 = about 180 L/h (mixes an NPEM mean with a median); naive pooling: 1.8415 x 1.7319 x 67.3 = about 215 L/h. Absorption half-life about 2 min (authors). Other program results (Table 3, SC, means): Vslope 1.2372-1.7420 L/kg and Kel 2.1312-3.4645/h with BOXES/ADAPT and MW/PHARM (the paper's own text says the Kel values differ significantly from NPEM); Ka by KINFIT 141/h was judged not real (few absorption-phase points). Note: the printed Kel of Table 3 is also labelled "Kel" while the text calls the Vslope column "h-1" in one place; see the [check] question.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No effect, ke0 or concentration-response. Discussion cites Gancher (ref 33) elimination half-life 33.6 +/- 3.9 min (Kel 1.2375/h) and V 0.5-3.5 L/kg. Not an opioid paper: no MEAC, ke0, tPeak or potency ratio.

## Covariates
Weight scales volume (Vslope per kg). Creatinine clearance term in the Kel equation was fixed to zero, so no renal effect was estimated.

## Caveats
Eight patients; SC treated as an IV infusion in NPEM; Ka and the IN route not estimable by NPEM; the program-comparison purpose means parameters differ by method (the authors' own Table 3-4 results disagree). Fuller PK report is ref 27, not in this archive stem.
