# Lalovic 2006: oral oxycodone PK, metabolites, and miosis PK-PD

## Study
16 healthy white volunteers (8 M / 8 F), 21-30 y, weight 73 ± 12 kg; no CYP3A-altering drugs, antidepressants or
grapefruit; fasted. Pilot: 4 subjects (2 M, 2 F) given 10, 15 and 20 mg oral oxycodone on separate days; main study:
12 subjects (6 M, 6 F) given 15 mg. All 16 contribute 15 mg data. Venous plasma at 0, 10, 30, 45, 60, 75, 90, 120,
180, 240, 360, 480, 720 min (plus 24 h pupil); urine 0-48 h. LC-MS for oxycodone and 9 metabolites (interday CV < 10%).
Dark-adapted pupil diameter (Pupilscan II infrared pupillometer) 5 min before each blood draw; VAS for alertness, nausea,
pruritus, mood. Noncompartmental PK (WinNonlin 4.01). PK-PD in SAAM II driven by forcing functions of observed plasma
concentrations; initial ke0 from the KE0 program (Shafer; Unadkat nonparametric loop collapse). Also in vitro receptor
binding (Ki) and GTPγS activation at human µ receptor, and rat brain/plasma distribution.

lag; 5 by a 2-compartment model (values not reported; lag not reported). Table III, 15 mg, n 16, mean ± SD:

Urine (48 h): total recovery 72 ± 19% of dose; unchanged oxycodone 8.9% (hydrolysed) / 8.0% (free); N-demethylation
products 45%, O-demethylation (oxymorphone + oxymorphols) 11%, 6-keto reduction ~8%; total oxymorphone 10.7%, free 0.33%.
The authors quote systemic oral availability of 60-80% from the literature and IV clearance of about 0.8 L/min.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: pupil diameter (miosis). Counter-clockwise hysteresis in all subjects. Parent-only effect-compartment model
with classical Emax (gamma fixed at 1; free gamma 0.9-2.8, CI included 1 in 18/24), Emax = E0. Joint
oxycodone-noroxymorphone model failed to converge or gave CIs including 0. Table IV (15 mg, n 16):
- EC50 30 ng/mL (SD 15, CV 50%, range 12-71) - plasma/effect-site oxycodone, total plasma, miosis.
- E0 9.2 mm (SD 1.3).
- ke0 0.1 /min (SD 0.09, CV 95%, range 0.03-0.4); t½ke0 11 min (SD 6, CV 55%, range 2-26). In 3 subjects ke0 went very
  high (t½ke0 < 5 min, CV > 80%), i.e. no discernible delay.
- No tPeak reported. No MEAC or analgesic concentration (analgesia not measured).
- Maximum pupil constriction did not exceed 60% of baseline at 20 mg; pupils back to baseline by 12 h; pupil AUEC
  linear in dose (pilot).
- Potency statements: IV oxycodone equianalgesic dose ratio to IV morphine of 1.3 (introduction, cited); cancer PCA
  morphine consumption 80% of oxycodone's (Discussion, cited Kalso). In vitro hMOR Ki (Table I): oxycodone 16.0 nM,
  oxymorphone 0.36, noroxymorphone 5.7, noroxycodone 57.1, morphine 3.19, DAMGO 0.21 nM; GTPγS EC50 oxycodone 343 nM,
  oxymorphone 42.8, noroxymorphone 167, morphine 94.2 nM (column alignment reconstructed). Rat brain/plasma ratio:
  oxycodone 2.07, oxymorphone 0.23, noroxycodone 0.10, noroxymorphone 0.008 (Table V).

## Covariates
Sex: CL/F per kg higher in women (p = 0.047); pupil AUEC smaller in men (p = 0.046); PD parameters not different by sex
or weight. Narrow age range (21-30 y). CYP2D6 not genotyped.

## The dose basis: no conversion, and the arithmetic that shows it
The paper names neither a salt nor a product, only "a single 15-mg oral dose of oxycodone" (Fig 2 caption, PDF p9).
US oxycodone products are labelled as the hydrochloride, which is also the basis stanpumpR doses, so the question is
whether CL/F was computed on the 15 mg as given or on the 13.45 mg of base in it. **The same second-order test used
on Liukas 2011 answers it, and the answer is: as given.**

- Table III (PDF p9, n = 16, 15 mg): CL/F 1.44 +/- 0.31 L/min, AUC 10.8 +/- 2.09 ug.min/mL. Expected mean CL/F from
  the dose and the AUC is 15/10.8 x (1 + (2.09/10.8)^2) = **1.441** against the reported 1.44; on the base it would
  be 1.292. The reverse direction agrees: 15 x (1 + (0.31/1.44)^2)/1.44 = 10.90 against the reported 10.8.
- Table II (PDF p8, the 4 pilot subjects at 10, 15 and 20 mg) repeats it three times: on the dose as labelled the
  expected CL/F is 1.373, 1.324 and 1.352 against the reported 1.36, 1.31 and 1.33; on the base it would be 1.231,
  1.187 and 1.212, low by 10% at every dose.

So **this paper takes no conversion**, and it is the one of the four that did not need a ruling - its own numbers
settle it. The contrast with the three IV studies is the point: the Finnish studies all used one product, Oxanest,
whose trihydrate basis is invisible unless you know the product, while Lalovic's arithmetic is self-describing.

## Caveats
Oral only (no IV arm, so F and the disposition are not separable); healthy young adults; absorption lag fitted but not
reported; Table III Cmax is the mean of individual peaks, not the peak of the mean curve; PD fitted to plasma forcing
functions, so ke0 is independent of any PK model; 3 subjects with unidentifiable ke0 inflate mean ke0; miosis, not
analgesia.
