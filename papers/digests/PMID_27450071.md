# Parkinson 2016: integrated glucose-insulin model in metformin-treated type 2 diabetes

## Study
- Pooled pre-treatment mixed-meal tolerance test data from four AstraZeneca/Amylin trials (BCA403 NCT00477581,
  H8OUS-GWAY NCT00135330, D6420C00001 NCT02367066, 2993-112 NCT00039013) in type 2 diabetics on stable metformin
  monotherapy (Table 1, PDF p2, p1615).
- 305 patients, 3030 glucose and 2898 insulin samples (p1615). Table 2 (p1617): T2DM duration 6.2 y (0.2-36.0), age 54.5 y
  (27-75), HbA1c 8.2% (6.5-11.6), BMI 32.6 kg/m² (21.2-52.9), weight 91.7 kg (51.9-154.9), 147 M / 158 F; metformin dose
  1507 mg/day (500-2550), mean (range).
- Glucose challenges: standardized meal 75 g glucose, liquid meal (Boost) 40 g, liquid meal (Ensure Plus) 100 g.
- Model: Jauslin/Silber integrated glucose-insulin (IGI) model in NONMEM 7.3.0; disposition parameters fixed to the
  original model (ref 13, Jauslin 2011); covariates tested by likelihood ratio, ΔOFV ≥ 10.83 (α 0.001), power form
  TVP = P1 × (cov/median)^θ, medians HbA1c 7.9%, duration 5 y, BMI 32.3 kg/m² (p1615-1616).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Not an opioid paper. End points are plasma glucose and insulin after a meal; KEOG and KEOI are effect-compartment rate
constants of the glucose-insulin feedback (fixed from ref 13), not drug ke0 values. No drug concentration-effect
relationship, no metformin exposure-response, no MEAC or potency ratio.

## Covariates
Baseline HbA1c on GSS (+1.1), CLGI (-0.744) and SINCR (-2.25); baseline BMI on ISS (+2.02), power form centred on the
medians above; absorption rate estimated separately per glucose dose (Table 3; p1617-1618). Disease duration was not
retained.

## Caveats
Metformin is not studied as a drug: there are no metformin concentrations. Data are pre-dose baselines from trials of other
agents. Unit of ISS printed as "mU". Supplementary tables not in the PDF.
