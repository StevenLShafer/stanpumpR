# Bruhn 2002: EEG parameter artifact robustness and baseline stability

## Study
- Re-analysis of EEG from a propofol volunteer study (the Schnider 1999 age/propofol PD dataset, ref 11 in the paper). 16 healthy volunteers aged 25-65 y, each studied twice (32 sessions).
- Baseline EEG recorded lying quietly, eyes closed, median 18 min (range 9.1-40.9). Then propofol 2 mg/kg bolus over a median 18 s (range 13-24). Only baseline and the bolus period analysed. Maximum effect taken at 1-3 min after bolus.
- EEG: frontal montage Fp3-Cz, digitised 128 Hz, 12 bit, 8-s epochs (1,024 samples). Bispectral index not computed (data already filtered/digitised).
- Parameters compared: SEF95, delta ratio (power 0.5-4 Hz as percent of total), canonical univariate parameter (CUP; ten 3-Hz bins 0-30 Hz, log power, propofol weights from the source dataset), Shannon entropy, approximate entropy (N = 1,024, m = 2, r = 0.2 x SD).
- Metrics: (1) baseline variability without artifact rejection = (median baseline - median max effect)/SD of baseline; (2) the same after discarding the top and bottom 10% of baseline epochs; (3) baseline stability within and between individuals = mean (baseline - effect) over volunteers / SD of the individual median baselines. Wilcoxon tests.

## Model and parameters
No pharmacokinetic or pharmacodynamic model is fitted.

Results (text, PDF pp3-4; mean +/- SEM of the ratios; figures not read):
- Without artifact rejection: Shannon entropy 3.08 +/- 0.39, CUP 2.61 +/- 0.21, approximate entropy 2.48 +/- 0.44, delta ratio 2.09 +/- 0.23, SEF95 1.69 +/- 0.32.
- With artifact rejection: approximate entropy 6.92 +/- 1.27, Shannon entropy 5.91 +/- 0.61, CUP 4.97 +/- 0.37, delta ratio 3.24 +/- 0.39, SEF95 3.17 +/- 0.62. Rejection multiplied the ratio by 2.79 (approximate entropy), 1.92 (Shannon), 1.91 (CUP), 1.87 (SEF95), 1.55 (delta).
- Baseline stability (effect over inter/intra-individual baseline SD): approximate entropy 5.19, CUP 2.89, delta ratio 2.60, Shannon entropy 2.37, SEF95 2.22.
- Using only the first session of each volunteer changed none of these materially.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No ke0, EC50 or concentration-effect model is reported; no propofol concentrations are given. The end point is the EEG descriptor's change from baseline to the maximum effect 1-3 min after a 2 mg/kg propofol bolus. The paper argues that baseline variation distorts E0 and EC50 estimates in a sigmoid Emax model, and that Shannon entropy and CUP suit potency studies where each subject is their own baseline, while approximate entropy suits absolute-value use. Not an opioid paper; no MEAC, ke0, tPeak or potency ratio.

## Covariates
None examined (age, weight and sex not analysed; only the volunteer age range 25-65 y is stated).

## Caveats
- Normalised to the maximum effect of a single propofol dose (2 mg/kg); the authors note this favours parameters most sensitive to propofol and may not generalise to other drugs or doses.
- The 10% trimming rule is arbitrary (authors acknowledge); small sample (16 volunteers, two sessions each); frontal single-channel EEG; BIS not assessed.
- Baseline drift from vigilance is not separated from artifact.
