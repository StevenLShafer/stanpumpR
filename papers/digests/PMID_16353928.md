# Bhattaram 2005: Pharmacometrics in 42 FDA NDAs

## Study
Internal FDA survey of New Drug Applications submitted 2000-2004 to the cardio-renal, oncology and neuropharmacology divisions. Of about 244 NDAs, 42 had a pharmacometrics consult. Clinical pharmacology team leaders ranked each review's impact on approval and on labeling as pivotal / supportive / no contribution / not applicable. Seven case studies follow (nesiritide, apomorphine, zoledronic acid, busulfan, sotalol, an unnamed rheumatologic drug, oxcarbazepine). Page numbers: PDF 1-10 = journal E503-E512.

Survey results (text, pp. E505, E510):
- Approval-related issues in 26 NDAs: pivotal 14 (54%), supportive 12 (46%).
- Labeling in 37 NDAs: pivotal 21 (57%), supportive 11 (30%), no contribution 5 (14%).
- Of the 14 pivotal approval reviews: 5 identified a need for more trials, 6 reduced the burden of more trials, 3 neither.
- Of 32 labeling reviews: 15 touched Dosage and Administration, 12 safety sections, 20 Clinical Pharmacology.
- About 90% of approval-affecting analyses and 60% of labeling-affecting analyses were done by FDA staff.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Not an opioid paper: no MEAC, ke0, t½ke0, tPeak or potency ratio is reported. Other end points in the case studies: nesiritide PCWP and systolic BP (bolus 2 mg/kg then 0.01 mg/kg/min; BP effect lags PCWP; units as printed, the text elsewhere prints 'mg/min/kg'); apomorphine UPDRS and blood pressure (little added benefit beyond 6 mg; no titration before 90 min; renal impairment start 1 mg); sotalol pediatric dose factor (1-month-old multiplier 0.68, so 30 x 0.68 = about 20 mg/m2 three times daily; the text layer prints '520', a garbled value); oxcarbazepine pediatric effect about 85% of adult (equivalence testing).

## Covariates
Busulfan: pediatric dose given in two weight bands (12 kg cut) by the label. Zoledronic acid: baseline creatinine clearance predicts renal deterioration risk. Sotalol: age under 2 years (renal maturation), then body surface area.

## Caveats
Opinion survey of FDA clinical pharmacology staff only (authors say so, E510). Analyses details deliberately not given. Busulfan pediatric regimen was not tested in clinical trials. No primary busulfan PK parameters (CL, V) are reported.
