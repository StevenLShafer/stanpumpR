# Fischer 2003: fosphenytoin clinical pharmacokinetics (review)

## Study
Narrative review of fosphenytoin (phosphate ester prodrug of phenytoin) PK in healthy volunteers, epilepsy and
neurosurgical patients, children, renal/hepatic disease; comparison with IV phenytoin and other acute AEDs.

## Model and parameters
- Dose expression: 75 mg fosphenytoin sodium ≡ 50 mg phenytoin sodium; doses in phenytoin sodium equivalents (PE)
  (p. 36). MW 406.24 (Table I).
- Protein binding 93-98%, saturable: unbound fraction 2-3% at 40-60 mg/L, 5-7% at 140-200 mg/L (p. 38).
- Vd ≈ 0.04 L/kg (100-200 mg PE at 50 mg PE/min) to 0.13 L/kg (1200 mg PE at 150 mg PE/min) (p. 38).
- Elimination entirely by conversion to phenytoin (blood/tissue phosphatases; < 5% unchanged in urine).
  **Conversion (elimination) half-life 7-15 min** after IV (abstract; p. 40). Means by study (Table II): 8.1, 8.0
  (Boucher, n = 10 patients), 9.3, 8.4, 12.9, 8.8, 15 (1200 mg PE), 8.1, 7.9, 7.8, 8.3 min; renal failure 9.2,
  cirrhosis 4.5 min. Earlier studies 8-12.9 min, later 7.8-15 min (p. 40). In vitro whole-blood t½ 3.2 h.
- Apparent clearance of fosphenytoin rises with dose/rate: ~12.9 L/h to 22.8 L/h (1200 mg PE at 150 mg PE/min);
  half-life independent of dose and rate (p. 40-41).
- Phenytoin bioavailability from IV fosphenytoin ≈ 98-99% of IV phenytoin (AUC ratio 0.96 in a stable-isotope
  study) (p. 41-42).
- IM: fosphenytoin peaks ~30 min, apparent t½ 20-40 min (absorption-limited); derived phenytoin peaks 1.5-4 h
  (abstract "2-4 h"); bioavailability 100.5% (CV 20.2%) vs IV fosphenytoin (10 patients, Boucher) and 101.2%
  (CV 5.5%) vs IV phenytoin (12 volunteers) (p. 44). Oral phenytoin bioavailability 85-95% (p. 45).
- Children: conversion t½ 7.1 min (5-10 y) vs 7.9 min (adults); 8.3 min (range 2.5-18.5) in 49 children
  1 day-16.7 y (p. 46).
- Diazepam co-administration: fosphenytoin t½ 12.9 ± 3.06 alone vs 9.53 ± 1.93 min (p. 49).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD model. Therapeutic total phenytoin 10-20 mg/L, unbound 1-2 mg/L (p. 44-45). Time to therapeutic level (Table
IV): fosphenytoin IV 150 mg PE/min 3-8 min, 100 → 8-12, 50 → 14-18, IM 20-40 min; phenytoin IV 50 mg/min 6-9 min,
PO 240-480 min. Fosphenytoin displaces phenytoin from albumin (phenytoin unbound fraction 7.8% → 15% after 750 mg PE
at 50 mg PE/min, elevated for ~1 h). Not an opioid.

## Covariates
No age, race, sex effect on fosphenytoin PK (5-98 y); derived-phenytoin clearance ~20% lower > 70 y (p. 45-46).
Renal/hepatic disease: higher unbound fosphenytoin (12.1% / 6.8% vs 5.0%), faster clearance; infusion rate reduction
25-50% advised.

## Caveats
Review; much data on file with manufacturer; immunoassay cross-reactivity (1.2-6-fold overestimate) means phenytoin
should be measured ≥ 2 h after IV / 4 h after IM fosphenytoin.
