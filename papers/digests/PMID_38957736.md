# Zhang 2024: aripiprazole population PK and fluoxetine interaction

## Study
Retrospective TDM study, Xuzhou Oriental Hospital, July 2020 to June 2022. 119 Chinese adults with schizophrenia
(57 men, 62 women), age 44.29 ± 13.03 y (19.00-69.38), weight 66.77 ± 11.68 kg (median 67.00, range 41.00-115.00);
dosage forms tablet / ODT / solution 95 / 35 / 1 (12 used two). Sparse trough concentrations from routine TDM.
Parent aripiprazole only (dehydroaripiprazole not modelled). NONMEM 7, FOCE-I. Many co-medications screened (Table 2);
**only 3 patients took fluoxetine**.

## Model and parameters
One compartment, first-order oral absorption, ka fixed from literature (ref 14). Final model (Eq 6-7, Table 3):

CL/F = 3.23 x (WT/70)^0.75 x (1 - 0.286 x FLU)  L/h;  V/F = 157 x (WT/70)  L

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD model. Target window 120-270 ng/mL (Hart et al. meta-analysis), used for Monte Carlo dosing (1000 virtual
patients per scenario, 0.1-0.8 mg/kg/day, 40-120 kg). Recommendations (Table 4): without fluoxetine, once daily
0.3 mg/kg/day (40-95 kg) or 0.2 (95-120 kg); twice daily 0.3 mg/kg/day; with fluoxetine, once daily 0.2; twice daily
0.3 (40-60 kg) or 0.2 (60-120 kg). AGNP window 100-350 ng/mL also cited. Not an opioid.

## Covariates
Weight (fixed allometric exponents 0.75 CL, 1 V; reference 70 kg) and fluoxetine (binary on CL/F). No CYP2D6
genotype. Stepwise forward p < 0.01, backward p < 0.001.

## Caveats
Sparse troughs so V/F and ka poorly informed (ka fixed); fluoxetine effect estimated from 3 patients; single centre,
Chinese population; authors acknowledge limited predictive ability.
