# Zabransky 1969: cephalexin in vitro MIC/MBC, volunteer serum levels, an endocarditis case

## Study
Three parts (Mayo Clinic, 1969):
1. **In vitro**: agar-dilution MICs for 146 clinical isolates (Table 1; plates at 1, 5, 10, 20 µg/mL, inoculum 10^5/mL,
   18 h). For 12 selected strains, MIC by agar, Trypticase Soy broth and horse serum (doubling dilutions from 100
   µg/mL, 24 h, 10% CO2) (Table 2) and MBC by subculture of 0.05 mL to thioglycollate broth for 72 h, at three
   inocula (10^5, 10^3, 10 organisms) in broth and serum (Table 3).
2. **Volunteers**: 10 healthy young male medical residents, normal creatinine (0.6-1.15 mg/100 mL), single oral 500 mg
   capsule; 9 of 10 had breakfast 15-45 min before (subject 2 fasting). Serum at 0.5, 1, 2, 4, 6, 8 h; urine 0-8 h.
   Assay: cup-plate microbiological assay, Staphylococcus 910 as organism (2.0 µg reference gave 23.7 mm). Weight not
   given; limit of detection not given.
3. **Case**: 62-year-old man, coagulase-negative staphylococcal endocarditis, cured with oral cefalexin 4 g/day x 2 days
   then 6 g/day x 26 days (164 g total).

## Model and parameters
No pharmacokinetic model is fitted. Observed data (Table 4, PDF p4, printed 879, confirmed on page image), serum
cefalexin µg/mL after 500 mg, mean of 10:

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mean | 3.4 | 13.7 | 8.5 | 2.5 | 0.7 | 0 |

Individual 1 h peaks 0 (subject 8, peak 5.8 at 2-4 h) to 25.2 µg/mL. Urinary recovery 0-8 h mean 81.2% of dose
(range 54.0-97.2%; Table 5, PDF p5, confirmed). Derived (mine): apparent terminal half-life from the 4 h and 6 h means,
ln(2.5/0.7)/2 = 0.64 /h, t1/2 about 1.1 h.

Case (Table 9, text only): 1 h post-dose serum levels 4 to 56 µg/mL on 1 g every 4-6 h; urine 3,000 µg/mL on 6 g/day.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End points are in vitro MIC (growth inhibition) and MBC (no growth on 72 h subculture), µg/mL. Confirmed on page images:

- **Table 1 (agar MIC, strains at 1/5/10/20/>20 µg/mL)**: S. aureus penicillin-sensitive (22): 18 at 5, 4 at 10.
  S. aureus penicillin-resistant (10): 1 at 5, 8 at 10, 1 at 20. Coagulase-negative staphylococci (3): 3 at 5.
  E. coli (41): 4/18/11 at 5/10/20, 8 >20. Klebsiella (6): 4/1/1 at 5/10/20. Enterobacter (12): 1 at 20, 11 >20.
  P. aeruginosa 28 and S. faecalis 13 all >20. Viridans streptococci (5): 2 at 1, 3 at 5.
- **Table 2 (MIC agar / broth / serum)**: S. aureus 1: 5 / 6.2 / 3.1; S. aureus 2: 10 / 6.2 / 6.2. E. coli 1-4 broth
  3.1-6.2, serum 6.2-12.5. Klebsiella 5 / 6.2 / 6.2. Enterobacter 20 / 12.5 / 12.5. P. mirabilis 1-2 broth 12.5.
  S. mitis 1-2 broth 3.1 and 6.2, serum 0.8.
- **Table 3 (MBC, serum at 10^5 / 10^3 / 10; broth at 10^5 / 10^3 / 10)**: S. aureus 1: 12.5/3.1/1.6; 6.2/6.2/3.1.
  S. aureus 2 (penicillinase producer): 25/12.5/6.2; 25/6.2/6.2. E. coli 1-4: >100 at 10^5 in both media; broth 10^3
  6.2-25. Klebsiella: 12.5/12.5/12.5; 25/6.2/6.2. Enterobacter: >100/50/12.5; >100/12.5/6.2. P. mirabilis 1-2: >100 at
  10^5; broth 10^3 25 and 50. S. mitis 1: 25/12.5/12.5; 25/6.2/3.1. S. mitis 2: 12.5/12.5/12.5; 12.5/6.2/6.2.
- Derived MBC/MIC in broth for S. aureus (Table 3 / Table 2 broth MIC 6.2): strain 1, 1 at 10^5 and 10^3; strain 2,
  4 at 10^5 and 1 at 10^3. Authors: inoculum strongly affects the MBC, especially for Gram-negatives; MBCs in serum
  mostly within one dilution of broth; serum binding not evident.
- **Table 8 (text only, unconfirmed)**: endocarditis coagulase-negative staphylococcus, cefalexin agar MIC 5.0, tube
  MIC 3.1, MBC 3.1 µg/mL; penicillin G MBC 0.38; methicillin and vancomycin tube MIC 1.6, MBC 25.0.
- **Table 6 (text only)**: 1 h sera of 5 volunteers did not reach bactericidal titres against the three test strains.
- No ke0, MEAC or potency ratio (not an opioid paper).

## Covariates
None. Food: 9 of 10 volunteers fed; two low absorbers (subjects 4 and 8) noted.

## Caveats
- Coarse agar dilution steps (1, 5, 10, 20) and an MBC series of only two S. aureus strains; methicillin status not
  reported (1969). Broth inoculum unit unstated (see open question).
- Microbiological assay; no limit of quantification; "0" at 8 h is below detection.
- Fed volunteers delay and blunt the peak; weight not given.
- The archived PDF's page images for printed pages 881-883 are duplicates of 878 (see open question).

  supports x1 for the MBC50; the x2 MBC90 sits between the two inocula. Zabransky's absolute S. aureus MICs (agar 5-10,
  broth 6.2) are 1.5-3x Haynes's MIC90 of 4, in line with the file's note that other series put the MIC90 at 8-16.
  **cannot verify** (different organisms/era; decision question).
- **Gram-negative row** (`R/antibioticMbc.R:183-190`, excluded from the band): Zabransky's E. coli MBC >100 µg/mL at
  10^5 supports leaving them out of a plasma band. Consistent.
