# Germovsek 2019: PKPD modelling in paediatric drug development, standardized CL scaling

## Study
Narrative review (open access, published online 19 Apr 2018). Covers paediatric regulation (Table 1), extrapolation and the FDA decision tree (Fig. 1), why to use population modelling (Table 2), scaling of PK (allometry, size and maturation), dose selection, PD scaling, model evaluation, trial design and dose optimisation. No new data and no drug-specific PK table.

## Model and parameters
No parameter table. The recommended standard (printed p. 44, Eq. 2): CLchild = CLadult x (WTchild/70)^b x PMA^Hill / (PMA50^Hill + PMA^Hill), with b fixed at 0.75 (advocated, especially when the weight range is small), PMA in weeks, PMA50 the PMA at 50% maturity, Hill a shape parameter. Eq. 3 (dose scaling): Dosechild = Doseadult x (WTchild/70)^b. Equation 1: y = a x WT^b.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD values of its own. Opioid audit: no opioid is analysed; no MEAC, ke0, t1/2ke0, tPeak or potency ratio is reported. PD statements (printed p. 46): the alveolar concentration for 50% no-response to incision first rises then falls with age (inhalational anaesthetics); for propofol, Peeters et al. found the target concentration in infants the same as in adults, with dose differences due to PK maturation (ref 124). The authors call for PD maturation modelling rather than assuming equal efficacy at equal exposure.

## Covariates
Weight (allometric), PMA (maturation, with extra postnatal-age scaling sometimes needed), organ function (e.g. serum creatinine for renal drugs). For obese children lean body weight is suggested and FFM mentioned; the child FFM model (ref 119) covers only ages over 3 years and is unusable in neonates.

## Caveats
Review; the 18-approach comparison it relies on is a separate paper (ref 96). No single exponent is agreed. Kleiber noted exponent choice matters only when subject weights differ at least ninefold. The authors caution extrapolation outside the covariate range of the model-building data. FDA paediatric PK sample-size rule quoted: 95% CI within 60-140% of the geometric mean CL and V, at least 80% power (printed p. 42).
