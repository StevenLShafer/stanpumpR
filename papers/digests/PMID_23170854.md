# Allegaert 2013: paracetamol concentration-effect relation in neonates

## Study
- Secondary analysis of the PARANEO study (NCT00969176; single centre, Leuven, open label). 60 neonates
  (< 29 days postnatal) received IV paracetamol; 19 received it as the only analgesic for mild-moderate pain and
  were analysed.
- The 19 (Table 1, median (range)): GA 38 (26-41) wk, postnatal age 2 (1-17) d, PMA 38 (27-42) wk, preterm 5/19,
  weight at inclusion 2.84 (0.9-3.96) kg. Indications: medical, subarachnoid bleed, NEC, alprostadil,
  traumatic/postoperative (table layout garbled).
- Dose: IV loading 20 mg/kg; pain and PK analysed for 6 h after the load (before the first 5-10 mg/kg q6h dose).
- 43 plasma samples (HPLC; LLOQ 0.08 ug/mL) from the 19; 142 Leuven Neonatal Pain Scores (LNPS, 0-14) before and at
  0.5, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 h.
- NONMEM VII, FOCE-interaction; PK as previously published (Allegaert 2011); ADVAN6 with an effect compartment.

## Model and parameters
PK not re-reported here (taken from Allegaert, Palmer, Anderson 2011). PD model (Eq. 2): Effect = E0 x (1 - Ce/(Ce + EC50)),
since Emax approached 0 (so Emax equals E0).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: LNPS (multidimensional neonatal pain scale, 0-14). Median score 4 before dosing, 1 at 30 min,
  0 from 2 h onward; ANOVA trend P = 0.02.
- T1/2keo 1.58 h, stated (Table 2 footnote) to be for a 70 kg person, scaled to a neonate by (WT/70)^0.25.
  The footnote says a 3 kg neonate's would be 1 h 12 min and the discussion compares 71 min (neonate) with 24 min
  for Anderson 2001 in a 3 kg neonate. Internal inconsistency: (3/70)^0.25 x 1.58 h = 43 min (derived), not 71-72 min;
  71 min would correspond to about 2.6 h at 70 kg (derived). Anderson's 53 min x (3/70)^0.25 = 24 min (derived)
  matches the paper's 24 min.
- 10 mg/L effect-site concentration predicted to reduce LNPS by 3.4 units (derived check: 4.15 x 10/12.07 = 3.44).
- No MEAC, no potency ratio.

## Covariates
Weight via allometric scaling of T1/2keo (0.25 exponent); no other PD covariates.

## Caveats
- n = 19, open label, unblinded, sparse data; shrinkage > 30% and large SEs; the authors call the analysis
  rudimentary.
- Different pain scale (LNPS) and heterogeneous pain causes vs Anderson 2001 (VAS after tonsillectomy).
- Pain scores approached zero, so the Emax structure is poorly identified.
- The 70 kg vs neonatal T1/2keo figures in the paper do not agree with its own 0.25 scaling (above).
