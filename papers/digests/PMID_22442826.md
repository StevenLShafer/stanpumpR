# Landersdorfer 2012: vildagliptin target-mediated drug disposition (TMDD) model

## Study
- **Design:** randomised, placebo-controlled, double-blind, four-way crossover. Oral vildagliptin 10, 25 and
  100 mg and placebo twice daily for 28 days each (p2). The NCA of the same data was published by He 2007.
- **Population:** 13 patients with type 2 diabetes (12 completed all four periods). Weight 91 (65-116) kg,
  height 166 (148-183) cm, age 53.5 (37-64) years, 6 men and 7 women (p5).
- **Dose times:** breakfast and dinner about 30 min after the doses (p2). On the observation day the two doses
  were 10 h apart (648 and 658 h for 10 mg; Figure 2 caption, p5).
- **Sampling:** day 28, vildagliptin at pre-dose and 0.25-24 h (13 times), DPP-4 activity at 17 times (p2).
- **Assay:** vildagliptin by LC-MS/MS, LLQ 2 ng/mL; DPP-4 activity by fluorogenic substrate, LLQ 0.24
  mU/mL/min (p2).
- **IV data:** a single 25 mg IV dose in healthy volunteers (absolute bioavailability study) was co-modelled
  during development to separate absorption from disposition. F was then fixed to that estimate (pp2-3).
- **Software:** NONMEM VI FOCE-I for development; the final simultaneous PK/DPP-4 (and GLP-1, glucose, insulin
  in the companion paper) model in S-ADAPT 1.56, MC-PEM, Beal M3 for BLQ. SE by bootstrap method 1 (no refits),
  200 runs (p3, p10).

## Model and parameters
Structure (Figure 1, p3; equations pp3-4, rendered p4):
- Two sequential absorption compartments, AG1 → AG2 → central, with ka1 = ka2 + dka (dka > 0). This replaced a
  lag time for numerical stability.
- Two disposition compartments (central VC, peripheral VP) with linear clearance CL and intercompartmental CLic.
- In each compartment, vildagliptin binds free DPP-4 (Rmax − DR) at rate (Rmax − DR) × k2 × C/(Kd + C), the
  slow-tight two-step binding. The complex DR dissociates intact at koff and is hydrolysed at kdeg to an inactive
  metabolite, which frees the enzyme. C is AC/VC or AP/VP.
- DPP-4 activity in plasma = (RmaxC − DRC) × cf1.

**Clearance from simulation (p7):**
- About 74 L/h at a 1 µg dose, where saturable clearance by DPP-4 is about half the total.
- 42.4 L/h after a single 100 mg dose, where the DPP-4 share is about 14%.
- 36.4 L/h as the concentration tends to infinity.
- NCA (He 2007, quoted p7): terminal t1/2 1.32 h at 10 mg and 2.43 h at 100 mg; dose/AUC clearance 84.0 L/h at
  10 mg and 53.5 L/h at 100 mg.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- **End point:** plasma DPP-4 activity, through free central DPP-4 × cf1 (p4). Placebo DPP-4 activity was roughly
  constant; three implausibly low placebo values were excluded (p6).
- **Inhibition:** 100 mg twice daily inhibited DPP-4 almost completely over 24 h; the simulated complex is almost
  constant at 100 mg in both compartments (pp6, 8).
- **Derived binding values at 5 ng/mL vildagliptin (pp9-10):** kon 0.26 nM⁻¹·h⁻¹, against 0.504 in vitro; Ki
  2.4 nM, against an in vitro plasma IC50 of 2.7 nM. koff 0.61 h⁻¹ lies inside the in vitro range of 0.3-0.8 h⁻¹;
  k2 23.4 h⁻¹ is below the in vitro 72 h⁻¹.
- GLP-1, glucose and insulin are in the companion paper (ref 9), not here. No ke0 and no effect compartment: the
  link is mechanistic, through binding.

## Covariates
None reported. The paper gives no weight or renal terms; n = 13.

## Caveats
- 13 patients, one centre. Between-occasion variability could not be included (p4). One subject had unexplained
  very high concentrations after 25 mg, which were kept (p5).
- RmaxC and RmaxP are apparent amounts and may not be the true enzyme amounts (p7). The tissue DPP-4 carries most
  of the PK nonlinearity.
- **Internal inconsistency:** Results (p7) says a "lag time" described the absorption delay, but the Methods
  (p3) and equations use a second absorption compartment instead.
- The SEs come from a bootstrap without refits.
