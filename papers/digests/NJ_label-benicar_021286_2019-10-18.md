# Benicar (olmesartan medoxomil) label, NDA 021286, 10/2019

## Study
Not a study. Regulatory label for the prodrug ester olmesartan medoxomil (MW 558.59; tablets 5, 20, 40 mg), an
AT1 receptor blocker for hypertension in adults and children 6 y and over. Boxed warning: fetal toxicity.

## Model and parameters
No population model. Section 12.3 (PDF p12-13):
- Prodrug completely bioactivated by ester hydrolysis during absorption; suspension bioequivalent to tablets.
- Absolute bioavailability of olmesartan about 26%; Cmax at 1 to 2 hours; no food effect.
- Volume of distribution about 17 L; 99% protein bound; does not enter red cells.
- Virtually no further metabolism. Total plasma clearance 1.3 L/h, renal 0.6 L/h; 35% to 50% of the absorbed dose
  in urine, the rest in faeces via bile.
- Biphasic elimination, terminal half-life about 13 hours; linear to 320 mg single and 80 mg multiple doses;
  steady state in 3 to 5 days, no accumulation once daily.
- The label does not say whether 17 L and 1.3 L/h are intravenous (systemic) values or which volume term is meant.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
12.2 (p12): 2.5 to 40 mg inhibit the pressor response to angiotensin I infusion, dose-related in duration, over
90% at 24 h above 40 mg. No concentration-effect relation and no therapeutic range.

## Dosing (2.1-2.2, PDF p3)
Adults 20 mg once daily, up to 40 mg after 2 weeks; doses above 40 mg no greater effect; twice daily no advantage.
Children 6 y and over: 10 mg (20 to under 35 kg, maximum 20 mg) or 20 mg (35 kg and over, maximum 40 mg); not
recommended under 1 year.

## Covariates (12.3, p13-14; 8.5-8.7, p10)
Elderly (65 and over): AUCss 33% higher, renal clearance about 30% lower. Children 1-16 y: clearance similar to
adults when adjusted by weight. Women: AUC and Cmax 10-15% higher. Moderate hepatic impairment: AUC about 60%
higher. Severe renal impairment (CrCl under 20 mL/min): AUC about tripled after repeated doses; no initial dose
adjustment recommended. Colesevelam lowers AUC 39% (15% if olmesartan is given 4 h before).

## Caveats
- Summary values without n, variability or route of the source study.
- The sex difference (10-15% higher exposure in women) is consistent in direction with a lower clearance in women.
