# Zhang 2018: PBPK model of sublingual buprenorphine in pregnancy

## Study
Modelling study, no new subjects. A full-PBPK model of IV and sublingual (SL) buprenorphine built earlier by
the same group in healthy non-pregnant adults (Kalluri, ref 28) in SimCyp v15.1, with a combined
feto-placental compartment (perfusion-limited, Kp from corrected Poulin-Theil) and gestational changes in
cardiac output, plasma and red-cell volume, haematocrit, albumin and CYP3A4 abundance (Table 2, PDF p4).
Drug-specific parameters (Kp, enzyme kinetics, absorption) were left as in the non-pregnant model. GFR was
not changed (a 50% rise gave identical profiles; sensitivity analysis Figure 2). Simulation: 8 mg SL twice
daily to steady state, 100 virtual women (10 trials x 10) per trimester and postpartum; WinNonlin 6.4 for
steady-state exposure. Validation: Bastian 2017 (ref 19; Caritis group) — women on stable twice-daily SL
buprenorphine for at least 7 days, each studied postpartum and in the 2nd and/or 3rd trimester, 10 samples
over 12 h, HPLC-MS/MS, total plasma. Acceptance criterion: predicted mean AUC within +/-50% of observed.

## Model and parameters
Non-pregnant drug profile (Table A1, PDF p11; Table 1, PDF p3):
- MW 467.64 g/mol; log P 4.98; diprotic base, pKa 9.62, 8.31; B/P 0.55; fu 0.03 (literature/optimised).
- SL: first-order absorption, fa 0.80, ka 2.34 /h, lag 0.7 h, Qgut 8.12 L/h, fuGut 1.
- Full PBPK, Vss predicted 2.48 L/kg (Table A1); Table 1 lists Vss predicted 2.48 and observed 2.77 L/kg
  (its footnotes a/b appear attached the wrong way round: "observed" cites Poulin-Theil, "predicted" cites
  Bullingham). Kp: adipose 0.0044, bone/additional 35 (optimised), brain 3.41, gut 2.69, feto-placenta 1.31,
  heart 0.83, kidney 1.29, liver 2.13, lung 0.29, pancreas 2.20, muscle 1.31, skin 1.60, spleen 1.31;
  Kp scalar 0.225 (optimised).
- Elimination by recombinant enzyme kinetics: CYP3A4 Vmax 10.4 pmol/min/pmol, Km 13.6 (unit printed
  "µmol h-1", evidently µmol/L); CYP2C8 Vmax 1.4, Km 12.4 µmol/L; UGT1A1 / 1A3 / 2B7 CLint 0.0162 / 0.0155 /
  0.0116 µL/min/pmol.
- Literature non-pregnant PK (Table A2, PDF p12): IV CL 50-62.5 L/h; SL bioavailability 36 +/- 13%; Tmax
  0.75-1.5 h; after 8 mg single SL dose Cmax 2.88 +/- 1.14 ng/mL, AUC0-inf 28.39 +/- 10.22 ng.h/mL. Text p9:
  IV clearance about 60 L/h, hepatic extraction about 0.67 (against hepatic blood flow 1.5 L/min).

Pregnancy scalars (Table 2, PDF p4; non-pregnant / T1 / T2 / T3): cardiac output 1 / 1.35 / 1.4 / 1.5;
plasma volume 1 / 1.125 / 1.325 / 1.50; RBC volume 1 / 1 / 1 / 1.3; haematocrit 38 / 35 / 34 / 33 %; albumin
49 / 36 / 36 / 36 g/L; CYP3A4 137 / 185 / 185 / 189 pmol/mg protein (+35, +35, +38%).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- No ke0, t½ke0, tPeak or hysteresis reported.
- Threshold: 1 ng/mL total plasma buprenorphine, taken from Greenwald 2007 (ref 18; PET in non-pregnant
  opioid-dependent volunteers), described as about 50-60% mu-receptor occupancy (p2) / 50% (p9) and the
  minimum to suppress withdrawal. End point: withdrawal suppression in opioid use disorder, not analgesia;
  not measured in this paper; no pregnant PK-PD data exist (p10). It is not an analgesic MEAC.
- Using it (Figure 6, PDF p10; text p8): at 12 h after 8 mg SL BID, 88% (postpartum), 58% (T1), 39% (T2) and
  12% (T3) of virtual women stay above 1 ng/mL; 70% of T3 women fall below it by 6 h.
- No potency or equianalgesic ratio.

## Covariates
Gestational stage via the Table 2 scalars only; no weight or age covariate is reported for the pregnancy
simulation.

## Caveats
- Validated against a single small study (n = 4 per trimester in Table 4) from the same group; T1 is pure
  prediction. Observed group sizes differ between Tables 3 and 4.
- Tmax poorly captured (predicted about half an hour later; women broke the SL tablet).
- Placental metabolism and transporters ignored; feto-placental compartment not validated.
- Postpartum is simulated as a non-pregnant healthy female population, not the same women.
- Bioavailability and absorption are held at non-pregnant values: the predicted change is entirely in
  hepatic clearance (CYP3A4 abundance, hepatic blood flow) and binding.

## Verification against stanpumpR
- **Header claim (R/drugs_buprenorphine.R:252-253)**: the PBPK predicts lower exposure in every trimester
  and matches Bastian's observed means within 25% except T3 Cav (-26.3%). **Matches.** The paper also
  supports the library's mapping of the factor onto elimination clearance rather than F: the PBPK holds
  absorption and drug parameters fixed and reproduces the change through CYP3A4 abundance and hepatic flow.
- **Magnitude**: not used for the factor (1.83 is from Zhu 2025, against postpartum, same women). Zhang's
  CL/F ratio against postpartum is 1.87x in T2 and 2.31x in T3 (derived, Table 4); the library's 1.83
  sits at Zhang's T2 value and below its T3 value. Comparator is the same (postpartum), so this is a real
  difference in size, recorded as a `[decision]` question, not an error.
- **Non-pregnant disposition**: Bjornsson CL 52.1 L/h at 35 y, 72.4 kg (`R/drugs_buprenorphine.R:19`,
  :355) lies inside Zhang's literature range 50-62.5 L/h (Table A2). **Consistent.**

  (Zhu 2018) reproduces the lower exposure", but the source column (and the R header) correctly cites
  Zhang H et al. 2018 (PMID 29873094); the first author is Zhang, not Zhu. **Discrepancy (attribution
  typo).** The same row also dates Bastian "2016" (published 2017) and Zhu "2024" (R header: 2025); those
