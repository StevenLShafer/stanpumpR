# Pfaller 2017: ceftaroline and comparators against US S. pneumoniae (AWARE 2014)

## Study
In vitro surveillance study (AWARE programme, sponsored by Allergan; JMI Laboratories). 2,614 clinically relevant
S. pneumoniae isolates, one per infectious episode, from 135 US medical centres in all nine Census divisions,
January to December 2014 (PDF p. 2 / printed 572). Sources: community-acquired respiratory infection 956 (36.6%),
healthcare-associated pneumonia 945 (36.2%), bloodstream 525 (20.1%), skin and skin structure 66 (2.5%), other 122
(4.7%). Ages: <=5 y 448 (17.1%), 6-17 y 211 (8.1%), 18-49 y 485 (18.6%), 50-64 y 668 (25.6%), >=65 y 708 (27.1%),
not reported 94 (3.6%).

Method: CLSI broth microdilution (M07-A10) in cation-adjusted Mueller-Hinton broth with 2.5-5% lysed horse blood;
QC strain ATCC 49619; interpretation by CLSI M100-S26 (2016). MDR = nonsusceptible to >=2 of penicillin
(MIC >=4), ceftriaxone (>=2), erythromycin (>=0.5), levofloxacin (>=4), tetracycline (>=2), TMP-SMX (>=1) (all
ug/mL). One ceftaroline-nonsusceptible isolate (853008; MIC 1 ug/mL) was whole-genome sequenced (serotype 35B,
single-locus variant of ST377; PBP2x/2b/1a and MurM substitutions). MIC only: **no MBC, no killing data**.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point is visible growth inhibition in broth (MIC). No MBC, time-kill, PK/PD index, ke0 or concentration-effect
model. Not an opioid paper: no MEAC, ke0 or potency ratio. Ceftaroline is reported eightfold more potent than
ceftriaxone by MIC90 (0.12 vs 1 ug/mL), against the same 2,614 isolates.

## Covariates
None (subsets by resistance phenotype only).

## Caveats
Surveillance of clinical isolates from 2014 US centres; sponsor-funded. Ceftriaxone dilution range starts at 0.06,
so the MIC50 is censored (true MIC50 may be lower). %S depends on breakpoint (meningitis <=0.5 vs nonmeningitis <=1).
No bactericidal data, so it cannot by itself support any MBC value.
