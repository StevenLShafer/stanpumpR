# Westmoreland 1994: fentanyl and alfentanil reduction of isoflurane MAC

## Study
Emory. 81 unpremedicated adults, ASA I-III, elective surgery, 39.7 ± 11.5 yr, 74.4 ± 14.6 kg (mean ± SD; p. 24);
79 analysed (2 excluded for concentrations inconsistent with the dose given). Randomized to 9 groups: no opioid;
fentanyl CACI targets 1, 3, 6, 8 ng/mL; alfentanil 70, 210, 400, 1000 ng/mL (abstract also lists 0). Induction
thiopental up to 5 mg/kg, succinylcholine 1.5 mg/kg, isoflurane in O2, controlled ventilation. CACI (Kenny/White
software) started at loss of consciousness. End-tidal isoflurane set per group by a modified Dixon up-and-down
(10% steps). Steady state ≥ 10 min, then skin incision; movement (gross limb/head/body, not cough/chew/swallow)
within 60 s. Venous plasma at ~5 min before, at, and 5 min after incision; RIA (fentanyl sensitivity 0.1 ng/mL,
CV 6.1/7.2%; alfentanil 1 ng/mL, CV 7.0/7.5%). Analysis on the mean of the three opioid concentrations; maximum-
likelihood logistic regression on ln(opioid + 0.01) and isoflurane. Mean thiopental-to-incision 19.9 min;
STANPUMP-simulated residual thiopental 4.6 µg/mL.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Opioid audit:
- End point: movement to skin incision under steady end-tidal isoflurane (MAC reduction), not analgesia; no MEAC.
- 50% isoflurane MAC reduction at fentanyl 0.5 ng/mL (95% CI 0-4.6) and alfentanil 28.8 ng/mL (95% CI 0-70.9);
  total venous plasma by RIA, mean of pre-, at- and post-incision samples, adult surgical patients (abstract, p. 25).
- Potency ratio alfentanil:fentanyl 58:1 for MAC reduction (abstract, p. 25); compared with 75:1 for EEG slowing
  (Scott 1985, cited p. 26).
- Ceiling: fentanyl 3 ng/mL gave about 70% MAC reduction with little more at higher concentrations; no movement with
  alfentanil > 500 ng/mL even at 0.1% isoflurane (p. 26).
- No ke0, t½ke0, tPeak or hysteresis estimated.

## Covariates
Age and weight tested; not significant (p. 25).

## Caveats
Very wide confidence intervals (both lower bounds 0); plasma, not effect-site, concentrations (≥ 10 min equilibration);
CACI prediction error (one fentanyl patient 4-14 ng/mL); thiopental induction; 2 patients excluded post hoc;
50% reduction is relative to the regression intercept (1.4% / 1.3%), not the Dixon MAC 1.25%.
