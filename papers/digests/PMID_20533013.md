# Hemmerling 2010: rule-based closed-loop propofol (BIS) vs manual

## Study
Randomized controlled trial (NCT01019746), Montreal General Hospital, Aug 2007-May 2008. 40 adults (18-85 y) for elective abdominal, thoracic, urologic, or orthopedic surgery >30 min; closed-loop n=20 (4 F/16 M, age 54 ± 20 y, weight 79 ± 7 kg, ASA I/II/III 2/6/12), manual control n=20 (5 F/15 M, 59 ± 16 y, 75 ± 13 kg, ASA 1/7/12) (Table 1, p.731). Mean ± SD.
- Induction: fentanyl 2.5 µg/kg and propofol (text says "1.5 µg/kg"; presumably mg/kg, see open question), rocuronium 0.6 mg/kg. Fentanyl 1.5 µg/kg 5 min before incision and repeated boluses until the last 45 min. Total fentanyl 4.8 ± 0.2 (closed-loop) vs 5.1 ± 0.4 µg/kg (control), n.s.
- Controller: rule-based adaptive algorithm, not model-based (no PK model). BIS (Vista) target 45, updated every 5 s, 20-s moving average; dose = previous dose x Km·Kv·Kh·Ka·Kw (error, variability, trend, age, weight). Valid BIS: SQI >40% and EMG <40 dB. Graseby 3400 syringe pump.
- Primary outcome: % time BIS within ≤10% / 11-20% / 21-30% / >30% of target (excellent / good / poor / inadequate). Secondary: Varvel indices (MDPE, MDAPE, wobble, divergence), global performance index (GPI). Tertiary: emergence (end of propofol to extubation).

Results (Table 3 and Fig. 4, p.731, rendered): excellent / good / poor / inadequate control 55 / 29 / 9 / 7% (closed-loop) vs 33 / 33 / 15 / 19% (control). MDPE -1.1 ± 5.3 vs -10.7 ± 13.1% (P=0.004); MDAPE 9.1 ± 1.9 vs 15.7 ± 7.4% (P<0.0001); wobble 8.5 ± 1.9 vs 12.5 ± 8.5% (P=0.043); divergence 0.0012 ± 0.0644 vs -0.1097 ± 0.2228 %/min (P=0.102); GPI 155 ± 148 vs 40 ± 39 (P<0.0001). Mean propofol dose 120 ± 28 vs 115 ± 27 µg/kg/min (n.s., p.730-731). Duration of anesthesia 143 ± 57 vs 157 ± 81 min. Emergence 9.0 ± 3.7 vs 12 ± 3.3 min (P=0.03, p.732). Ten simulated cases: mean dose 143 ± 13 µg/kg/min.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point is the BIS (target 45); no propofol or fentanyl concentrations measured or modelled. No ke0, t½ke0, tPeak, hysteresis, EC50, or MEAC/analgesic concentration reported. No potency or equianalgesic ratio stated. Authors note the control group's median BIS undershot (MDPE -10.7%, "median BIS of 35" stated in text p.733) while the closed-loop median was about 44.

## Covariates
Age and weight scale the controller's allowable infusion and bolus limits (Ka, Kw); the functions are not given.

## Caveats
Control group managed by one anesthesiologist who was aiming for tight BIS control (possible Hawthorne effect, acknowledged); open-label for dosing; extubation assessor blinded; emergence also depends on fentanyl; abstract says poor/inadequate P<0.01, text gives P=0.001 for inadequate control; text states "propofol 1.5 µg/kg" at induction (unit apparently misprinted).
