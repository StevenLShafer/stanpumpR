# Holze 2024: oral LSD 85 and 170 µg, PK, PK-PD and urinary recovery

## Study
- Double-blind, randomized, placebo-controlled crossover with 5 conditions (LSD 85 µg, LSD 170 µg, psilocybin
  15 and 30 mg, placebo); only LSD and placebo analysed here. Washout ≥ 10 days. NCT03604744.
- 28 healthy adults (14 M / 14 F), age 35 ± 9.4 y (25-52), weight 72 ± 12 kg (55-104), BMI 23 ± 2.7 (PDF p. 2).
- Drug: LSD **base** in 96% ethanol oral solution, 85 µg per unit (analytically 84.5 ± 0.98 µg); 85 µg base
  ≈ 124 µg LSD tartrate (paper's statement). Given at 09:00, about 30 min after a standard breakfast
  (**fed state**).
- Plasma (lithium heparin) at 0, 0.25-24 h (18 times); urine 0-8, 8-16, 16-24 h. UHPLC-MS/MS, LLOQ 10 pg/mL
  for LSD and O-H-LSD in plasma (urine 10 and 50 pg/mL).
- Analysis: Phoenix WinNonlin 8.3; NCA, then a **one-compartment model, first-order absorption and elimination,
  no lag time**, fitted per individual (two-compartment did not improve AIC/fit). PD: sequential individual
  PK-PD link (ke0), sigmoid Emax on VAS scores (Emax bounded 0-100%).

- Derived t½ke0 (0.693/mean ke0): any drug effect 18 min (85 µg) and 13 min (170 µg); range across end points
  12-22 min. tPeak of effect not reported as such; any drug effect peaked at 2.5 ± 0.5 h (85) and 2.2 ± 0.6 h
  (170); onset 0.6 and 0.4 h; duration 9.3 ± 3.2 and 11 ± 3.7 h (10% of individual max threshold; PDF p. 5).
- Anticlockwise hysteresis for all VAS end points, no acute tolerance (Fig. S12, not in this PDF).
- Not an opioid: no MEAC, no potency ratio.

## Covariates
None. Doses analysed separately; parameters similar across doses.

## Caveats
- Healthy, mostly young, normal-BMI participants; fed state; individual (two-stage) fits rather than a
  population model; apparent oral parameters with unknown bioavailability.
- EC50 rises with dose (1.3 → 2.0 ng/mL for any drug effect), so a single PD set is not dose-independent.
- Possible misprint in Table 1 Vz/F range (open question).
