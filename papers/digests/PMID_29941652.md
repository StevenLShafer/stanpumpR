# Zheng 2018: azithromycin PopPK in children with CAP

## Study
Prospective, multicentre (4 Chinese hospitals), open-label opportunistic-sampling PK study, 2015-2017. 95 children aged 2-12 y with suspected or confirmed community-acquired pneumonia receiving IV azithromycin 10 mg/kg once daily, 60-min infusion (PDF p7). Age mean 6.2 (SD 2.6) y, range 2.1-11.7; weight mean 23.9 (9.8) kg, median 21.5, range 11.0-51.0; 53 M / 42 F; ALT median 20.3 IU/L (range 3.3-374.4) (Table 1, PDF p3). 140 measurable concentrations. Plasma assay by LC-MS/MS, calibration 10-2,000 ng/mL, LLOQ 10 ng/mL (PDF p8). NONMEM 7.2, FOCE-I. External validation on 28 other children (28 samples): MPE 1.37%, MAE 3.84% (PDF p4).

## Model and parameters
Two-compartment, linear elimination (ADVAN3 TRANS4), IV infusion. Allometric weight scaling fixed a priori (0.75 on CL and Q, 1 on volumes), centred on the median weight 21.5 kg. ALT > 40 IU/L is a binary covariate on CL (Table 3, PDF p5). Exponential IIV on CL, V1, Q (V2 IIV mentioned in text but not tabulated). All values below are typical values for a 21.5 kg child (absolute L or L/h, not per kg).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No concentration-effect model. Target: free-drug fAUC0-24/MIC90 of 3 h, MIC 2 mg/L, 30% protein binding (fAUC = 0.30 x AUC), taken from preterm-infant work (PDF p5-8). "Overdose" defined as AUC0-24 > 30 mg.h/L (PDF p8). Steady-state AUC for 10 mg/kg: median 8.14, range 3.65-18.50 mg.h/L (PDF p3). Not an opioid; no MEAC, ke0, tPeak or potency ratio.

## Covariates
Weight (allometric, fixed). ALT > 40 IU/L: CL x 0.761. Age on V2 and sex on CL lowered OFV in forward steps but were dropped in backward elimination; AST, BUN, creatinine, height not retained (Table 2, PDF p3-4).

## Caveats
Opportunistic sparse sampling (140 samples, 95 children); no intracellular or tissue concentrations; V1 poorly estimated (RSE 43.8%); residual error RSE 161.6%; protein binding of 30% assumed, not measured; the target of fAUC/MIC 3 h comes from neonates; the dose recommendation targets about 50% attainment. Simulated loading dose 15 mg/kg then 10 mg/kg gave 55.2% (day 1) and 53.2% (steady state) attainment, with 5.8% overdose risk; for ALT > 40 the dose is reduced 15% (PDF p4).
