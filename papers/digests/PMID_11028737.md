# Hoymork 2000: measured versus predicted remifentanil and propofol TCI, BIS

## Study
- 20 unpremedicated ASA 1-2 patients (10 men, 10 women), age 20-70 y (44.8 ± 12.0), BMI 20-30, laparoscopic cholecystectomy. Two excluded, leaving 18 (9 men, 9 women).
- Remifentanil by a non-commercial TCI (Engbers group, Leiden) to a blood target of 7.5 ng/mL throughout; propofol by Diprifusor, blood target 5 µg/mL at induction then 3 µg/mL after intubation. Rocuronium 0.6 mg/kg. BIS (Aspect 1050) recorded.
- Arterial samples before intubation, 1 min after start of surgery, stable surgery (30 min), end of surgery, and at emergence (response to verbal command).
- Assays: remifentanil in whole blood by LC/MS/MS, LOQ 0.1 ng/mL, CV under 15% (0.1-40 ng/mL), citric acid in tubes to stop esterase; propofol in plasma by HPLC with fluorescence detection, LOQ 2 ng/mL, CV under 5.5%.

## Model and parameters
The remifentanil TCI parameters (appendix, from Glaxo Wellcome unpublished data, PDF p6): Vc = 0.072 x weight, k10 0.494, k12 0.339, k21 0.188, k13 0.013, k31 0.01 (all /min), t½ke0 0.99 min. Propofol used the Diprifusor (Marsh) set, not tabulated.

The authors cite Wietasch (abstracts) for a possible 40% reduction of propofol clearance with remifentanil and a 56% propofol error with remifentanil; these are secondhand abstract claims.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- Opioid audit (remifentanil): no MEAC or analgesic concentration is derived. The only remifentanil concentration-effect statement is a literature one: Vuyk 1997 values of remifentanil 7.7 ng/mL with propofol 2.7 µg/mL for no response to surgery in 95% of patients (discussion, PDF p6); the targets used here (7.5 ng/mL, 3 µg/mL) are close. Not measured in this study.
- ke0: the TCI algorithm used t½ke0 = 0.99 min for remifentanil (appendix, end point not stated; ke0 = ln2/0.99 = 0.70 /min, derived). No ke0, tPeak or hysteresis was measured in the study. No potency ratio.
- BIS: baseline 96 ± 2, loss of consciousness 67 ± 18, before intubation 40 ± 7, stable surgery 42 ± 7, at emergence 72 ± 13 (Table 2, PDF p4). Mean BIS during stable surgery 42 ± 7.
- No correlation between measured propofol (plasma) and BIS, or remifentanil (whole blood) and BIS, at any of the five sampling times (Fig. 3). Measured remifentanil correlated inversely with systolic BP at end of surgery (r² 0.4, p = 0.004) and at emergence (r² 0.2, p = 0.04).
- Emergence (time from stopping infusions to response to voice): 15.8 ± 4.7 min overall; women 12.6 ± 2.5 vs men 19.0 ± 4.2 min (p = 0.001, Table 3). End-of-surgery propofol 4.0 vs 4.8 µg/mL (p = 0.13); remifentanil 6.7 vs 5.6 ng/mL (p = 0.26); at emergence propofol 1.3 vs 1.2, remifentanil 1.0 vs 0.7 ng/mL (p = 0.08).

## Covariates
The remifentanil algorithm used body weight (and age) only; the authors note a later prototype (Remifusor, Minto kinetics) adds sex and lean body mass. Sex was a covariate of emergence time only (above).

## Caveats
- n = 18, deep anaesthesia (BIS about 40), propofol plus remifentanil together; no pure single-drug effect.
- Remifentanil blood sampling at emergence only in 8 patients (software problems).
- BIS lags about 30 s (authors), so BIS at induction and emergence is poorly timed.
- Whole blood (remifentanil) versus plasma (propofol): measured values are not interchangeable with other studies' matrices.
