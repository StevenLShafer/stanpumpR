# Zhou 2019: decitabine population PK, adults and children with AML

## Study
- Pooled 5 adult studies (AML and MDS; DACO-016, -017, -018, -020, E7373-A001-101) and 2 pediatric AML studies (E7373-G000-202, DACOGENAML2004): 71 adults and 28 children (1-16 y), 840 concentrations (695 adult, 145 pediatric) (Table 1, PDF p4-5).
- Dosing: 20 mg/m2 IV over 1 h daily x 5 days (most studies); DACO-018 15 mg/m2 over 3 h three times daily for 3 days. Sparse sampling to 4 h after the start of the infusion (to 8 h in DACO-018).
- Plasma decitabine, LC-MS/MS, LLQ 1 ng/mL; concentrations below LLQ treated as missing.
- Adults: median age 70 y, weight 78.2 kg, BSA 1.93 m2; children: median age 6.0 y, weight 20.6 kg, BSA 0.82 m2 (Table 2, PDF p5; weight and BSA read from the page image, the text layer swapped the columns).
- NONMEM 7.3, PsN 3.4.2 (SCM covariate search, bootstrap 500), Xpose 4.4.0.

## Model and parameters
Linear 2-compartment model with elimination from the central compartment; allometric scaling on BSA (centred on 1.73 m2): CL and Q exponent 0.75 (fixed, chosen over 0.67 and 1 and over weight as size metric), V1 and V2 exponent 1. IIV exponential on CL and V1; IIV on Q and V2 not estimable, entered as multiples of eta-CL. Proportional residual error, a separate term for DACO-018 during infusion.

- Children by age, <1 mo to 2 y / 2-6 / 6-12 / 12-16 y: CL 194.0 / 183.0 / 161.1 / 151.5 L/h/m2; AUC 153.8 / 162.9 / 185.3 / 195.5; Vss 52.7 / 53.4 / 50.7 / 54.5 L/m2; Cmax 146.0 / 153.7 / 174.2 / 181.5 ng/mL; t1/2 beta 0.62 / 0.66 / 0.73 / 0.81 h. Tmax 1 h in all (end of infusion).
- Adult CL by regimen in an earlier internal analysis (70 kg, BSA 1.73 m2): 298 L/h (95% CI 249-359) for 1 h infusions, 201 L/h (168-241) for 3 h (p8); the cause is unknown.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No effect measure, ke0, MEAC, EC50 or potency ratio. Not an opioid paper. The paper notes pediatric efficacy was not demonstrated in DACOGENAML2004 (reported separately).

## Covariates
Allometric BSA on CL, Q, V1, V2 only. After this, age, age class (adult vs child), sex, race, dosing regimen, creatinine clearance, bilirubin and disease (AML vs MDS) were not significant (SCM, alpha 0.05 forward, 0.001 backward). Creatinine and bilirubin were unavailable for the two Phase 1/2 studies E7373-A001-101 and E7373-G000-202.

## Caveats
- Sparse sampling, very high IIV (CL 63%, V1 81%); IIV on Q and V2 assumed correlated with CL rather than estimated.
- Only 1 child under 2 y; the authors ask for more data in that group.
- Apparent CL differs by about a third between the 1 h and 3 h regimens in earlier analyses; nonlinearity unexplained.
- Simulated Table 4 means are skewed by IIV: mean CL 143.9 L/h/m2 in adults exceeds the typical 208.32/1.73 = 120.4 L/h/m2 (derived).
- Data not shareable.
