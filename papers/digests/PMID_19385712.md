# Lobo 2009: pooled population PK of oral duloxetine in 594 patients

## Study
- Meta-dataset of five Lilly phase II/III trials (HMAQ, HMAU, HMAV, SAAW, HMEF): 594 patients, 2002 sparse plasma
  concentrations. Indications: MDD 223, diabetic peripheral neuropathic pain 112, stress urinary incontinence 128
  (women only), fibromyalgia 131.
- Overall (Table I, mean +/- SD (range)): age 48.8 +/- 12.0 yr (18.7-84.3); weight 82.0 +/- 21.4 kg (41.0-192.0);
  74% female, 79% nonsmokers, 78% Caucasian.
- Doses 20-60 mg once or twice daily, up to 120 mg/day. Sampling at study visits, 0-36 h after the last dose (Fig. 1).
- Plasma (heparin) by HPLC-MS/MS, curve 0.5-100 ng/mL (validated to 1000 with dilution); two laboratories.
- NONMEM V, FOCE-I; forward p < 0.01, backward p < 0.001; objective-function mapping and leverage analysis.

## Model and parameters
One compartment, first-order absorption, apparent oral parameters (Table II):

| Parameter | Estimate | SEE % | 95% CI |
|---|---|---|---|
| ka | 0.168 /h | 14.8 | 0.113-0.231 |
| CL/F | 45.1 L/h | 3.26 | 42.4-48.0 |
| age on CL/F (theta8) | -0.00725 per yr | 31.7 | -0.0113 to -0.00303 |
| dose on CL/F (theta9) | -0.00446 per mg | 20.3 | -0.00610 to -0.00283 |
| Vd/F | 814 L | 13.3 | 587-1064 |
| Hispanic on Vd/F (theta7) | 1.02 | 38.8 | 0.369-2.01 |
| smoking on F (theta5) | -0.298 | 13.9 | -0.373 to -0.213 |
| sex (male) on F (theta6) | -0.389 | 9.13 | -0.453 to -0.319 |
| IIV CL/F | 58.9% | 8.39 | - |
| IIV Vd/F | 96.6% | 15.9 | - |
| Residual | 30.8% proportional, 5.17 ng/mL additive | | |

Equations (Table II footnotes):
- F = 1 x (1 + IND_male x theta6) x (1 + IND_smoker x theta5); IND = 1 for male or smoker.
- CL/F = 45.1 x [1 + (Age - 49) x theta8] x exp[theta9 x (Dose - 40)] (dose in mg; the paper does not say whether
  per administration or per day).
- Vd/F = 814 x (1 + theta7) in Hispanic patients.
So the reference (typical) patient is a female nonsmoker, 49 yr, 40 mg.

Derived: male nonsmoker CL/F = 45.1 / 0.611 = 73.8 L/h; female t1/2 = ln2 x 814 / 45.1 = 12.5 h.
Simulations (Results): women Cav,ss 64% higher than men; nonsmokers 43% higher than smokers; female nonsmokers
about 2.3 x male smokers; Hispanic Cmax 10% lower, Tmax 1.5 h later; age 29 vs 49: Cav,ss -13%, age 69: +17%.
Doubling dose 30 -> 60 mg gives 2.3-fold, 60 -> 120 mg 2.6-fold (Results) or 2.7-fold (Discussion) Cav,ss.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. Not an opioid; no ke0, no MEAC.

## Covariates
Sex and smoking on F (bioavailability, attributed to CYP1A2), age and dose on CL/F, Hispanic ethnicity on Vd/F.
Not significant: body weight, dosing regimen, disease, renal function (Cockcroft-Gault categories). CYP2D6
genotype not available.

## Caveats
Sparse data from industry trials; all authors Lilly employees. Covariates reduce IIV only from 67% to 59% (CL/F) and
123% to 97% (Vd/F); authors conclude no dose adjustment is warranted. The dose term's unit basis is not stated.
Few Asian (n = 17) and African American patients.
