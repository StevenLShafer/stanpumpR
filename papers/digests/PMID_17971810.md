# Magnusson 2008: turnover model of carbamazepine autoinduction and CYP3A4/CYP1A2/Pgp induction

## Study
- 7 healthy non-smoking Caucasian men, 20-45 y (weights not given). All CYP3A5*3/*3; no CYP3A4 variants.
- Carbamazepine (Tegretol tablets) 200 mg once daily at 20:00 for 2 days, then 400 mg for 14 days (one subject
  reduced to 200 mg for nausea); days 0-15.
- Oral probe cocktail at 08:00 on days -3, 1, 2, 3, 5, 11, 16, 17, 18, 19, 24, 30: midazolam 2.5 or 5.0 mg,
  caffeine 100 mg, digoxin 0.13 mg. Samples pre-dose, 4 and 8 h (plus 0.5, 1, 2 h on days -3, 1, 16).
- Assay: HPLC-MS/MS for midazolam, 1-OH-midazolam, caffeine, paraxanthine, carbamazepine and CBZ-E (plasma).
- NONMEM VIb, FOCE, log-transformed data; sequential (carbamazepine submodel fixed, then probes); bootstrap SEs;
  dOFV 10 for significance.

## Model and parameters
Carbamazepine: one compartment, first-order absorption; inducible clearance CL_CBZ1 forming CBZ-E plus uninducible
CL_CBZ2 fixed at 4 x CL_CBZ1 before induction; one compartment for CBZ-E. Induction: linear stimulus,
induction = slope x (amount CBZ + CBZ-E), turnover dA_enzyme/dt = Rin(1 + induction) - kout A_enzyme with
A_enzyme = 1 at baseline and CLint proportional to A_enzyme (eqs 4, 6). CBZ autoinduction and hepatic midazolam
(CYP3A4) induction share one kout in the final model. Table 1 (estimate, RSE %):

Discussion: pre-induced carbamazepine half-life 40 h. Digoxin (Pgp) unaffected. Caffeine CL 8.4 -> 10.4 L/h;
paraxanthine 6.9 -> 9.9 L/h. Derived: fraction of a step induction complete after 10 days at t1/2 69.8 h =
1 - 2^(-240/69.8) = 0.91 (ignores the rise of the stimulus with carbamazepine accumulation).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: enzyme activity inferred from probe-substrate and carbamazepine clearances (no clinical effect). No
ke0, no concentration-effect model for seizures. Not an opioid; no MEAC or potency ratio.

## Covariates
None (7 subjects).

## Caveats
n = 7 healthy men, short (16-day) exposure at 400 mg/day; induction slope linear (Emax not identifiable over the
narrow dose range); CBZ-E parameters confounded with the fraction formed; IIV in the carbamazepine submodel fixed
to 0 in the caffeine model; induced CL/F 2.4 L/h here is lower than maintenance-population values.
