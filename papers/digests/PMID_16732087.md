# Harris 2006: propofol + sevoflurane additivity for LOC and skin incision

## Study
- Dixon up-down design, 36 ASA I-II adults enrolled (18-55 yr) for elective surgery with a skin incision > 1 inch. They yielded 13 LOC crossover pairs and 10 incision crossover pairs (12 male, 21 female, per Results p.3); crossover-pair patients: age 40 +/- 9 yr, 168 +/- 13 cm, 74 +/- 15 kg (mean +/- SD).
- Sevoflurane held fixed (mask, semiclosed circuit) at end-tidal 0.45% for LOC (half LOC50 sevo 0.89%) and 0.85% for incision (half MAC, MAC 1.71%). Propofol was target-controlled by STANPUMP with the Schnider PK set to an effect-site target, stepped 10% up or down from the previous patient's response. First propofol target 1.4 ug/mL (LOC) and 7.6 ug/mL (incision). Equilibration at least 10 min.
- LOC = modified OAA/S score < 2 (no response to loud voice plus mild prodding/shaking). Movement = gross movement of limbs, head or body to skin incision. BIS (A2000) recorded.
- Control: 22 more patients (5 crossover pairs) given propofol alone with STANPUMP to re-establish the propofol-alone intercepts.
- Analysis: isobologram and t test against simple additivity (half of the propofol-alone value).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End points: LOC (OAA/S < 2) and no movement to skin incision. Not an opioid paper.
- For both end points the observed combined potency equals simple additivity (no synergy); 90% power to detect a 19% (LOC) or 25% (incision) departure.
- Concentration basis is the propofol effect-site target (Schnider ke0 inside STANPUMP) at near-steady state, not a measured plasma level. The abstract calls the incision value an "effective plasma concentration"; the Methods and Discussion make it the effect-site target.
- BIS: responders at LOC had higher pre-test BIS (76 +/- 12) than nonresponders (65 +/- 9), Table 1; BIS 70 was the break point. BIS at LOC50 for the combination 71 +/- 16 vs 69 +/- 13 propofol alone.
- No ke0, t1/2ke0, tPeak, EC50/gamma or MEAC reported (the ke0 is the Schnider one used by STANPUMP, not restated).

## Covariates
Sex, age, weight tested in repeated-measures ANOVA on BIS; none significant except responder status.

## Caveats
Single point on the response surface (one sevoflurane level per end point); quantal, one observation per patient, not blinded; authors flag the "giant rat" criticism. Propofol-alone incision intercept (10.7) differs from the earlier 15.2.

## Verification against stanpumpR
- Nothing in R/ or inst/extdata cites this paper. The gas engine (R/gasProperties.R) sums MAC of volatile agents and nitrous oxide only; propofol is not added. Additivity is what the paper found, so the lack of a propofol term is a modelling choice, not contradicted by it (see open question).
- Sevoflurane MAC: paper 1.71% (Mapleson 1996, ref 8); R/gasProperties.R:104 has 1.80 as the age-40 value. Cannot verify the difference from this paper (open question).
- Propofol model: the Cp50 values are tied to Schnider effect-site targets. R/drugs_propofol.R:78-79 keeps Schnider as propofolSchnider() in R/alternativeModels.R, so the numbers would not transfer unchanged to another propofol model.
