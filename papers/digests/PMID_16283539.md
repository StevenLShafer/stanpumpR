# Stanski 2005: Getting the Dose Right (EUFEPS meeting report)

## Study
Narrative report of a 2002 (9-12 Dec, Basel) EUFEPS/ECPM workshop on dose finding in clinical drug development. No patients, drug, assay or model. PDF pages 1-13 (journal pp. 199-211).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. Not an opioid paper: no MEAC, ke0, t½ke0, tPeak or potency ratio.

Points the report makes (facts only):
- Cites a retrospective review of 499 FDA new molecular entities approved 1980-1999: 354 had evaluable data, about 1 in 5 had a post-approval dose change, 20% of those increases and 80% decreases. Dose-change likelihood was 3x higher for 1995-1999 approvals than 1980-1984 (p. 201).
- The summary states current development gets the suggested dose wrong about 20% of the time (p. 210).
- Recommends earlier PK/PD data (early Phase 2), a "learn-confirm" approach, semi-mechanistic PK/PD models, adaptive designs, clinical trial simulation, a priori and a posteriori (Bayesian) dose individualisation, and repeated-measures designs to estimate individual dose-response.
