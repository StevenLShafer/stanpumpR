# Sharabiani 2026: GARIX model of oxygen extraction ratio in paediatric CPB

## Study
Retrospective analysis of minute-by-minute heart-lung-machine and monitor data, Great Ormond Street Hospital, 2019-2021, all patients <18 y (max age 18.23 y). Patients with postoperative AKI (KDIGO >=1) were excluded from training: 286 patients, 293 CPB procedures, 20,443 valid minutes (p. 1006). Inputs: cardiac index (CI), Hb, SaO2, SvO2, temperature. Age groups used for subgroups: neonate <1 month, infant 1-6 months, toddler 6 months-3 y, child >3 y. No drug is studied; no anaesthetic or opioid concentration is reported.

## Model and parameters
GARIX: a generalised additive time-series model for the minute-to-minute change in logit(OER), fit by backfitting (R `gam` package). Equation 1: change in logit(OER) = ETG + ATG + XTG + error (Gaussian, constant variance). The ETG (equation 2) is k x [log(VO2i) - log(tVO2i)], where VO2i = 10 x CI x (1.34 x Hb x SaO2/100) x OER, indexed to BSA (mL/min/m2). tVO2i is the latent oxygen demand. bGARIX (eq. 3): log(tVO2i) = g0 + g1 x Temp (constant Q10). GARIX (eq. 4): intercept is linear in log(age), log(weight) and their product; temperature enters as a natural spline. Maximum lag N = 5 chosen by 10x repeated 5-fold CV on out-of-sample R-squared (Fig 4A, p. 1010-1011). Lagged coefficients are plotted (Fig 4), not tabulated.

Other reported values: baseline simulation state log(CI)=1.00, log(Hb)=2.31, log(SaO2)=4.60, Temp 33.1 C, OER 0.22; step changes sized to give OER 0.17 (Fig 6A, p. 1014). Fox82 replication conditions: Temp 20 C, SaO2 99%, Hb 7.1 g/dL, CI 2.2 L/min/m2, 17 virtual subjects, five 10-min CI plateaus (p. 1008). Likelihood-ratio p-values: age+weight p=1e-9, spline p=9e-11, GARIX vs bGARIX p=2e-19, bGARIX vs no-ETG p=2e-46 (p. 1012). Adding sex did not help (p=0.8 bGARIX, p=0.5 GARIX).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Not a pharmacology paper. No MEAC, ke0, t1/2ke0, tPeak, EC50, or potency ratio is reported. The nearest thing to a time constant is Fig 6A: OER after a temperature step settles within minutes; after CI or Hb steps it takes tens of minutes (read from curves, no numeric time constants stated).

## Covariates
log(age), log(weight) and their interaction modify the intercept of log oxygen demand; temperature by natural spline. Sex adds nothing. Coefficient values are in the Supplement (not read).

## Caveats
Single centre, retrospective, children only (extreme states such as deep hypothermic arrest not covered); homoscedastic Gaussian errors although residuals show heteroscedasticity and clustering; dissolved O2 ignored (about 1.6% of content); no hard OER ceiling; CI, Hb, SaO2, Temp treated as fully exogenous; Fox82 comparison has wide CIs (Table 2).
