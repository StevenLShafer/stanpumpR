# Sanchez-Alcaraz 1998: placental transfer of propofol at caesarean

## Study
10 healthy ASA I-II women at elective caesarean (Table 1, PDF p2): age 32.7 +/- 5.5 y (20-41), weight 66.9 +/- 3.8 kg (60-75), height 160.2 +/- 4.2 cm, dose 138 +/- 9.8 mg. Induction propofol 2 mg/kg IV over 35-45 s after atropine 0.5 mg and vecuronium 0.1 mg/kg; maintenance O2/N2O; fentanyl 1.5-2 ug/kg and oxytocin 30 IU after delivery. Samples (1 mL blood to plasma, -20 C): maternal at 1 min after end of injection (Co) and at cord clamping (MV), plus umbilical vein (UV) and artery (UA). Plasma propofol by HPLC after coupling with 2,6-dichloroquinone-4-chlorimide, UV 276 nm, sensitivity 0.025 ug/mL, CV <6% (p2). Delivery 8.4 +/- 2.2 min (4-10) after induction (Table 2).

## Model and parameters
No population model; per-subject values "estimated from maternal levels" (method not described). Table 3 (plasma, ug/mL, mean +/- SD): Co 5.01 +/- 1.06; MV 1.47 +/- 0.35; UV 0.32 +/- 0.10; UA 0.22 +/- 0.08. Table 4 (PDF p4, printed p22): t1/2 4.72 +/- 0.94 min; Vc 0.44 +/- 0.12 L/kg; UV/MV 0.22 +/- 0.04 (range 0.15-0.30); UA/UV 0.68 +/- 0.13 (printed with a ug/mL unit in the text, a ratio). Regressions: UV = -0.02 + 0.23 MV (r 0.83); UA = -0.05 + 0.18 MV (r 0.78).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Hypnosis within 75 s in all. Neonatal Apgar 7.3 +/- 2.3 (1 min), 9.3 +/- 1.1 (5 min); 1-min scores of 3, 4 and 5 in three neonates; all 5-min scores >= 7. Apgar vs cord propofol: r 0.48 (UV), 0.49 (UA). No concentration-effect model, no ke0 or MEAC.

## Covariates
None modelled; UV/MV depends on the induction-to-delivery interval (authors).

## Caveats
n = 10; one dose; delivery during the distribution phase, so cord/maternal ratios are time-dependent, not steady-state. Vc of 0.44 L/kg is called higher than 0.28 L/kg in surgical patients (Schuttler abstract, ref 12): the comparator is another study's non-pregnant patients, not the library's model. Plasma, not whole blood. Neonates included weights of 1025 g and 2050 g.
