# Majid 2014: eribulin population PK and tumour-size PK/PD in breast cancer

## Study
Combined population PK analysis of 7 phase 1 studies (advanced solid tumours, n=129), one phase 2 (MBC, n=211) and one phase 3 (Study 301, NCT00337103, MBC, n=173): 513 patients, 4093 plasma observations; 74.9% had breast cancer (p4). Dose eribulin mesylate 1.4 mg/m2 (= 1.23 mg/m2 free base) as a 2-5 min IV infusion on days 1 and 8 of 21-day cycles in the phase 3 study; doses converted to free-base equivalents for modelling. Phase 3 had 4 sparse samples per patient in cycle 1. Assay: LC-MS/MS on plasma, LLOQ 0.2 ng/mL, concentrations below LLOQ excluded. NONMEM 7.2, FOCE, log-transformed data. A tumour growth-inhibition model used 585 observations from 142 phase 3 patients (RECIST sum of longest diameters), and exploratory Cox and parametric survival models used the eribulin arm (n=163 with PK, n=521 per protocol, n=409 with a week-6 scan).

## Model and parameters
Three-compartment, linear elimination from the central compartment, dose-independent; clearance and dose-normalised AUC comparable across studies and AUC dose-proportional. Allometric weight scaling: CL, Q2, Q3 on WGT^0.75; V1, V2, V3 on WGT^1. Final CL covariates: albumin, alkaline phosphatase, bilirubin (Table 1, PDF p5, printed 1138). ## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Tumour model: dy/dt = KG*y - KD*Exposure(t)*exp(-lambda*t)*y, with y the sum of longest diameters and exposure the individual AUC (dose/clearance, ng.h/mL) at the time of assessment. Median exposure 785 ng.h/mL gave about 0.013 /week shrinkage versus growth 0.0022 /week; median predicted size fell from 78 mm to 50 mm (36%) at week 36. IIV on KG 182% and KD 157%; no covariate predicted the effect. Week-6 tumour change predicted survival (log-rank P=.008 in Fig 3B; the abstract says P=.0055), exposure quartiles did not (log-rank P=.9). No MEAC, ke0 or effect-site model; not an opioid.

## Covariates
Albumin (+), ALP (-) and bilirubin (-) on clearance explain 7.3% of IIV in CL (abstract); weight allometric. No effect of age, sex, race, tumour type, ECOG, creatinine clearance. Text states reduced clearance with moderate hepatic impairment from an earlier study.

## Caveats
Cytotoxic, oncology patients; sparse phase 3 sampling; very high PD IIV; the table's printed row order is confusing (see open_questions). Supplementary tables not read. Companies authors (Eisai).
