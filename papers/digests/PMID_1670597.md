# Khuenl-Brady 1991: vecuronium and atracurium block in postpartum women

## Study
- Prospective comparison, Innsbruck. 60 ASA I-II women (PDF p. 1-2, printed pp. 110-111). 30 were within 4 days
  of delivery, scheduled for laparoscopic tubal sterilisation; the rest had elective gynaecological surgery of
  similar length (about 30-50 min). Table 1 gives n = 30 per drug; the split within each drug (postpartum vs
  nonpregnant) is not printed (presumably 15 each, not stated).
- Anaesthesia: no premedication; thiopental 4-5 mg/kg or methohexital 1.5-2 mg/kg plus fentanyl 0.003 mg/kg;
  maintenance N2O/O2 2:1, more fentanyl as needed. No volatile agent.
- Doses: vecuronium bromide 0.1 mg/kg or atracurium besylate 0.5 mg/kg, single IV bolus (per kg of actual,
  i.e. postpartum, body weight: no other weight basis is described).
- Monitoring: TOF 2 Hz every 20 s at the ulnar nerve, evoked EMG of the hypothenar muscles (Datex).
  Reversal with neostigmine 30 µg/kg + atropine 10 µg/kg once the fourth twitch reappeared.
- End points: onset = injection to 95% twitch depression; clinical duration = injection to first reappearance of
  the fourth TOF twitch (not 25% T1 recovery).
- Rat in-vitro arm: phrenic nerve-hemidiaphragm, nonpregnant (n = 5) and 2-12 h postpartum (n = 7) Wistar rats,
  cumulative vecuronium doses.
- Statistics: unpaired Student's t-test. No plasma concentrations measured.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Results (PDF p. 2, printed p. 111; abstract p. 1; mean ± SD):

| End point | Postpartum | Nonpregnant | P |
|---|---|---|---|
| Vecuronium onset (min) | 3 ± 1 | 2 ± 0.7 | not stated as significant |
| Vecuronium clinical duration (min) | 49 ± 10 | 32 ± 6 | < 0.001 |
| Atracurium onset (min) | 3 ± 1 | 2 ± 0.7 | not stated as significant |
| Atracurium clinical duration (min) | 37 ± 4 | 36 ± 6 | NS |

- Comparator: concurrent nonpregnant women of similar age and weight having gynaecological surgery (different
  individuals), same anaesthetic. Vecuronium duration ratio postpartum/nonpregnant 49/32 = 1.53 (derived).
- Rat diaphragm (p. 2): vecuronium ED25, ED50, ED75 2.8, 3.7, 4.7 µmol/L postpartum vs 3.3, 4.3, 5.1 µmol/L
  nonpregnant, not significantly different (Figure 2).
- No ke0, EC50 or plasma concentration. Not an opioid paper.

## Covariates
Postpartum state (≤ 4 days) vs nonpregnant. Weight was similar; postpartum vecuronium women were in fact lighter
(61 vs 66 kg, Table 1), so a relative overdose per kg of a heavier body does not obviously explain the result,
though the authors (p. 3) raise dosing by ideal body weight as a possible preventive measure.

## Caveats
- Group sizes within each drug not printed; small groups.
- Duration defined as return of the fourth TOF twitch, a different end point from 25% T1 used by other studies.
- Mechanism (reduced hepatic blood flow or hormone competition for hepatic uptake) is the authors' speculation;
  no PK, liver flow or hormone levels were measured.
- The rat in-vitro arm says only that muscle sensitivity is unchanged in rats, which the authors note may not
  transfer to humans.
