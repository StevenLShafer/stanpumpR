# Hinderling 1985 (Part II): atropine heart rate and saliva flow linked to the peripheral compartment

This card replaces an earlier "wrong document" card. Before 2026-10-27 this PDF was archived under PMID_4032240, the
stem for Part I. That card's Part II facts were re-checked against this PDF and corrected where needed.

## Study
- These are the same three healthy men and six experiments as Part I (PMID_4032240), and
  the same doses: 1.35 and 2.15 mg atropine IV, each as a 3-min infusion, in random order.
- Table I (PDF p4) gives the measured doses: A 1383 and 2290 ug, B 1353 and 2136, C 1326 and 2026. It also gives
  weight and height: A 66 kg/173 cm, B 72/174, C 79/180. The Results (PDF p3) state that all dosages are the base.
  Part II does not give the subjects' ages.
- Heart rate was recorded by ECG (lead V2 or II), as 60 / mean R-R of 20 consecutive beats. Saliva flow used a
  published method with 2-min sampling.
- Heart rate was followed for at least 4.5 h, and saliva flow for at least 7.5 h. The first experiments ran to 10
  and 8.5 h. Control values were taken over the 2 h before the dose.
- Subject B had one single-blind placebo session. Heart rate was constant for 250 min. Saliva flow was constant for
  150 min, then rose 30%; the authors judged this unrepresentative and used uncorrected data.
- Analysis: a simultaneous TOPFIT fit of the PK and PD data, using post-infusion effect data only. Durations, effect
  areas and D(90) were also derived with model-independent methods.

## Model and parameters
The PK part is Part I's linear two-compartment IV-infusion model, with central P, peripheral T and elimination from
P. The two effect compartments, T_HR and T_SF, are kinetically identical to T. The effects are modified Hill
functions of the **amount** of atropine in T (ug):
- E_HR = (Emax - E0) T^g / (T^g + T50^g) + E0 (eq. 1, PDF p2).
- E_SF = E0 T50^g / (T^g + T50^g) (eq. 2).

Transduction is assumed instantaneous, and only atropine is assumed active. Table I (PDF p4, printed 714, rendered)
gives the overall mean +/- SD of n = 6. Values in parentheses in the table are graphic estimates.

Other results:
- Peak heart rate was 192% (+/- 25) of control at the lower dose and 217% (+/- 33) at the higher. These were 67%
  (+/- 3.0) and 59% (+/- 15) of the theoretical maximum (PDF p3).
- The minimum saliva flow was 8.4% (+/- 8.0) of baseline at the lower dose and 3.4% (+/- 3.9) at the higher, which is
  87% and 86% of the theoretical maximum (PDF p5).
- During the infusion, subjects A and B had about 0.5 min of atrioventricular dissociation 1-2 min after the start.
  Heart rate began to rise on average 1.5 min after the start. Before that, A and B were unchanged and C slowed by
  7-22% at both doses. No terminal bradycardia occurred.
- The effect-amount curves were not superimposable across doses: they were steeper at the lower dose (Figs. 2-3).
- Effect area against dose was linear over 1-3 mg IV, with AUE_HR = -4374 + 5.33 D (ug), r = 0.997 (Fig. 4).
- From the effect areas, the IM absorption extent was 0.78 and 0.70 (literature data). From urinary data it was 0.89.
  The oral extent was 0.49 (PDF p5).
- A beta of 0.00542 /min came from literature effect durations against ln dose. The fitted beta was 0.00527 /min
  (PDF p5), and the Part I t1/2 beta is quoted as 132 (+/- 31) min (PDF p6).

Printed equation 10 (PDF p2) has C50ss = [k_TP/k_PT x T50] V_P, which multiplies by V_P. Table I's values are
reproduced only by dividing (derived: 1139 x 0.0557 / (0.560 x 19.1) = 5.9 ng/mL, against 6.2, which is the mean of
the individual values). Dimensionally, ug x L would not be a concentration. The printed form looks like a
typesetting error in the equation, not in the tabulated values.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End points: heart rate (increase, beats/min) and saliva flow (decrease). There is no ke0 and no effect-site
  rate constant. The effect is linked to the amount in the peripheral compartment, which the authors explain as slow
  access of atropine to the receptors.
- Time to peak: maximum heart rate came 7.5 (+/- 2.5) min after the end of the 3-min infusion, and minimum saliva flow
  7.0 (+/- 4.0) min after. The authors note that the literature range for heart-rate time-to-peak is 2-16 min.
- Potency: C50ss is 6.2 ng/mL for heart rate and 3.7 ng/mL for saliva flow (total plasma atropine, racemate, GC-MS).
- The Hill coefficient is about 3 for both end points.
- There is no MEAC, which does not apply to atropine.

## Covariates
None was modelled. Dose itself changed the effect-amount relationship: T90_HR and C50ss_HR were significantly larger
at the higher dose.

## Caveats
- n = 3 healthy young men with high vagal tone.
- The PD is dose-dependent and not superimposable across doses. The authors propose a feedback mechanism.
- The peripheral-amount link is structurally different from an effect-site (ke0) model.
- Post-infusion data only.
- Racemic atropine was measured.
- The single placebo run showed a 30% late drift in saliva flow.
- Printed equation 10 has the V_P term inverted (see above).
- Saliva E0 is printed in "mL", although the figure axis is mL/min.
