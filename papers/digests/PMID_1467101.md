# Esener 1992: thiopentone and etomidate in maternal and umbilical plasma, and colostrum, at caesarean section

## Study
- 40 healthy term women at elective caesarean section (Samsun, Turkey) were given a single IV induction dose (PDF p1, printed 586):
  - thiopentone 5 mg/kg (n = 20), or
  - etomidate 0.3 mg/kg (n = 20).
- Table I (PDF p2), mean (range or SEM):

  | Group | Age | Weight | Gestation | Induction-delivery interval |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Etomidate | 28.2 (19-41) y | 74 (2) kg | 40.1 (0.7) weeks | 7.0 (0.9) min |
  | Thiopentone | 28.3 (22-38) y | 75 (2) kg | 39.9 (0.6) weeks | 7.3 (2.1) min |

- Premedication was atropine. Anaesthesia: suxamethonium 1.5 mg/kg, then 50% N2O with halothane ≤0.5% until delivery, and 66% N2O with halothane as needed afterwards.
- Sampling, timed from the end of injection:
  - Etomidate: maternal blood at 5, 15, 30 and 60 min and 2 h; colostrum at 30 min, 2 h and 4 h.
  - Thiopentone: maternal blood at 5 min and 2, 4, 9 and 12 h; colostrum at 30 min, 4 h and 9 h.
  - Umbilical vein blood at delivery.
- Matrix: maternal venous plasma (site implied, not stated).
- Assays: both were **UV spectrophotometric**, not chromatographic.
  - Etomidate: pentane extraction (Ellis and Beck HPLC extraction), absorbance at 235 nm (printed "µm"), with KF added to stop esterase activity.
  - Thiopentone: n-hexane extraction, spectrophotometric.
  - No limit of quantification, specificity or precision is reported.
- Statistics: mean (SEM), Student's t test. No pharmacokinetic model was fitted.

## Model and parameters
No PK parameters were estimated. All values below are observed means (SEM), with the range where given, read on the rendered images.

**Etomidate, 0.3 mg/kg:**
- Maternal plasma at 5 min: 1242.0 (146) ng/mL, range 340-2500. This was the sample closest to delivery.
- Later maternal values, using the Summary's alignment (see open_questions for the conflicting Results paragraph):
  - 15 min: 434.0 (82), range 0-1400
  - 30 min: 64.2 (10), range 0-145
  - 60 min: 7.0 (2.6), range 0-35
  - 2 h: undetectable in all samples
- Results also lists 0.5 (0.1) ng/mL, range 0.2-1.2, with no time point it can belong to.
- Umbilical vein: 51.7 (10.3) ng/mL, range 0-190. Umbilical:maternal ratio 1:24 (against the 5-min maternal sample).
- Colostrum:
  - 30 min: 79.3 (21.5) ng/mL, range 0-420
  - 2 h: 16.2 (4.1) ng/mL in the Results (16.3 in the Summary), range 0-60; detectable in 9 of 20
  - 4 h: undetectable
- Colostrum:plasma ratio 1.2 at 30 min.

**Thiopentone, 5 mg/kg:**
- Maternal plasma: 6.09 (0.4) µg/mL at 5 min (range 4-12.1), 2.64 (0.3) at 2 h, 1.35 (0.2) at 4 h, 0.86 (0.1) at 9 h and 0.59 (0.1) at 12 h.
- Umbilical vein: 4.72 (0.4) µg/mL, range 2.3-10.2. Umbilical:maternal ratio 1:1.3.
- Colostrum: 1.98 (0.2) µg/mL at 30 min, 0.91 (0.1) at 4 h and 0.59 (0.1) at 9 h. Colostrum:plasma ratios 0.67 at 4 h and 0.68 at 9 h.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None measured. There is no EEG, no clinical end point, no ke0 and no neonatal outcome data in this paper.

## Covariates
None. There is no non-pregnant control group, so the paper cannot show a pregnancy effect on clearance.

## Caveats
- The assay is non-specific UV spectrophotometry for both drugs, with no validation data reported.
- The etomidate time course is internally inconsistent (see open_questions).
- Maternal sampling stops at 2 h for etomidate.
- The umbilical sample was drawn at about 7 min, against the maternal 5-min sample.
- There is no non-pregnant comparator, and the means are given with SEM.
