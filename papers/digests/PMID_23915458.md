# Urien 2013: anakinra population PK/PD in children with SJIA and autoinflammatory syndromes

## Study
- Pooled data (PDF p2-3): 22 SJIA patients from the ANAJIS phase IIB trial (2 mg/kg SC once daily, max 100 mg)
  plus 65 patients with autoinflammatory conditions treated afterwards with 2-10 mg/kg/day SC. The highest doses
  went to low-weight CAPS patients who had not responded to lower doses. The second group comprised 20 CAPS
  (14 CINCA/NOMID, 6 Muckle-Wells), 3 mevalonate kinase deficiency, 2 TRAPS, 1 FMF, and undetermined conditions.
- n = 87 (sex given as 32 girls and 52 boys; see open question), 148 concentrations, 4 BLQ concentrations treated
  as left-censored (p3). Sampling was sparse: one or more occasions per patient, mostly 0-26 h after dose (Figure 1).
- SJIA group: 2.26-16.8 y (median 7.6), 10-83 kg (median 21). Others: 0.73-21 y (median 8), 4.3-60 kg (median 21) (p3).
- Assay: plasma (heparinised whole blood, separated immediately, frozen at -20 °C); R&D Systems IL-1ra sandwich
  ELISA; LOQ 40 ng/mL (= 0.04 mg/L) (p2). The ELISA measures IL-1ra immunoreactivity. Whether endogenous IL-1ra
  contributes is not discussed.
- PD: CRP, 195 observations in the 22 ANAJIS patients, log10-transformed; PK estimates fixed for the PD fit
  (sequential) (p2, p4).
- Software: Monolix 3.2 (SAEM + MCMC); NPDE for evaluation; LRT/AIC/BIC for model selection (p2-3).

## Model and parameters
One compartment with first-order absorption and linear elimination; parameters are apparent values (F unknown,
SC route). One-compartment with nonlinear elimination, two-compartment, zero-order and transit absorption were tested
and not retained (p2-3). BSV was estimated on CL/F, and only between-occasion variability on V/F. Residual error is
additive (p3).

Covariate equations (text, PDF p3; BW in kg): CL/F = 0.847 × BW^0.47 (L/h); V/F = 2.581 × BW^0.76 (L).
These reproduce Table 1's 70-kg values (derived: 0.847 × 70^0.47 = 6.24; 2.581 × 70^0.76 = 65.2).

Adding BW reduced CL/F variability from 0.41 to 0.28 and V/F variability from 1.34 to 0.475, and AIC/BIC by more than 15 (p3).

Derived at 70 kg: k = 6.24/65.2 = 0.096 h-1, elimination t½ ≈ 7.2 h. Absorption t½ = ln2/0.38 ≈ 1.8 h.
ka is about 4x k at 70 kg, so the terminal decline reflects elimination, not absorption (derived).
At 20 kg: CL/F ≈ 3.46 L/h, V/F ≈ 25.2 L.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: serum CRP (mg/L) in the 22 SJIA patients, followed for about 400 days (Figure 2, p4).
- Model: an indirect response (turnover) model in which anakinra inhibits CRP production,
  dR/dt = kTR·R0·[1 − C/(C50 + C)] − kTR·R (eq. 1, p2). Imax is fixed at 1 and there is no Hill coefficient.
  A mixture model assigns patients to eq. 1 (responders, high baseline) or to eq. 2, where the drug term is
  multiplied by Resis = exp(−kRESI·t), a time-dependent loss of effect (p2).
- C50 = 0.03 mg/L (Table 2). Nearly all observed concentrations were above it (p5). The text takes C90 as about
  10 × C50 (p4); for a hyperbolic Emax model the exact value is 9 × C50 (derived).
- Observed baselines (median [range]): 120 [44, 230] mg/L in responders and 43 [2.5, 152] mg/L in the resistance
  group, p = 0.02 (p4). The proportion with significant "resistance" was 62%, with onset at about 60 days (p4).
  Table 2 gives the responder proportion as 0.37, which is consistent.
- No effect of concomitant anti-inflammatory treatment (corticoids, NSAIDs) on the PD (p4).
- Dosing target: mean Css 0.4 mg/L (Figure 3 simulations, p5). Figure 4 (p5) shows the mg/kg/day dose needed to
  reach it. Read from the figure: 3.9 at the low-weight end, 2.4 at 10 kg, 1 at 50 kg, 0.7 near 100 kg.
  These follow from the PK equation (derived: CL/F × 0.4 mg/L × 24 h ÷ BW gives 2.4 mg/kg/day at 10 kg and
  1.02 at 50 kg). Suggested regimens: 3 mg/kg/day below 10 kg, 2 mg/kg/day for 10-50 kg, a flat 100 mg/day
  above 50 kg (p5).
- No ke0, no effect-site, no MEAC: the drug is not an opioid and the effect is an indirect response measured in days.

## Covariates
Body weight on CL/F (exponent 0.47) and V/F (0.76), with estimated exponents. The authors kept these rather than
fixing 0.75/1 (p5). Age was significant before BW was added but not after. Sex and co-medication had no effect (p3).

## Caveats
- Sparse, mostly trough-to-peak sampling over 0-26 h, 148 concentrations in 87 patients. V/F carries large
  between-occasion variability (0.47).
- F is unknown, so CL and V are apparent SC values. The ELISA measures IL-1ra immunoreactivity.
- Heterogeneous disease population. The PD comes from only 22 SJIA patients, and the authors could not find a
  baseline cut-off between the two subgroups (p4).
- Printing and arithmetic inconsistencies are listed under open_questions (ηKTR '081', the kRESI uncertainty
  column, the girls/boys count, 60 days vs kRESI).
- The dose recommendation rests on simulated CRP curves (Figure 3), not on a prospective test.
