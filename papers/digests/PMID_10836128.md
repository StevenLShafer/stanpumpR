# Sheiner 2000: PK/PD modeling in drug development

## Study
Conceptual review (journal pages 67-95). No patients, doses, assays or fitted parameters. It frames PK/PD
modelling as the "learning" half of drug development (phase 1 to 2b) and the confirmatory trial as the "confirming"
half. It defines mechanistic vs empirical and descriptive vs predictive models, and the notation [X] covariates,
[D|Dn,X] deviation from protocol, [Y|D,X] outcome (PK/PD) model. It argues for hierarchical (individual-parameter)
models over marginal models, discusses informative dropout (pattern-mixture vs selection models), lists uses of
modelling (Table 1, p. 79; Table 2, pp. 82-87) and proposes the "in numero" study.

## Model and parameters
No model parameters. One illustrative equation: a one-compartment bolus model, concentration = (Dose/V)·exp(-(CL/V)·t),
used to show why a predictive model makes observations exchangeable (p. 76).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No ke0, EC50, MEAC or potency ratios. Conceptually it says the exposure variable driving a PD model depends on the
rate-limiting step; if distribution to the biophase is rate limiting, a model for that process is needed (p. 74).
Table 2 mentions as examples only (no numbers): remifentanil dose adjustment in special populations
(Minto 1997, ref 63), ketorolac analgesia dose-time-response (Mandema 1996, ref 60), EEG measures of alfentanil,
propofol, midazolam (Billard 1997, ref 38). Opioid audit: none stated.
