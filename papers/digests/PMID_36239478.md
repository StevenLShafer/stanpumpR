# Franzese 2023: ceftazidime-avibactam renal dose adjustment in children

## Study
Simulation and probability-of-target-attainment (PTA) study, using the previously published combined adult and pediatric population PK models of ceftazidime and avibactam (Franzese 2021, ref 9 of the paper; the parameter tables are in that paper, not here). Data behind the models: 18 phase I-III adult trials, one phase I single-dose pediatric study (NCT01893346) and two phase II multiple-dose pediatric studies (cIAI NCT02475733, cUTI NCT02497781). Ceftazidime dataset 9,628 observations from 2,130 subjects (154 children); avibactam 14,223 observations from 2,403 subjects (153 children) (PDF p6). Adults 18-89 y, baseline CrCL 8-488 mL/min (ceftazidime) and 11-739 (avibactam); children 3 months-17 y, NCrCL 43-489 mL/min/1.73 m2. Total (free plus bound) plasma concentrations. NONMEM 7.3 FOCE-I; simulations in R. Only about 12% of adults and 1% of children (2 patients, 1.3%) had NCrCL < 50.
Simulations: 1,000 subjects per age, renal group and indication, non-parametric, using children's post hoc random effects and resampled demographics; adults resampled from phase III (271 cUTI, 353 cIAI, 161 HAP/VAP). NCrCL groups (mL/min/1.73 m2): >80 normal, 51-80 mild, 31-50 moderate, 16-30 severe 1, 6-15 severe 2, <=5 ESRD. Doses are 2-h infusions of ceftazidime-avibactam (ratio 4:1).

## Model and parameters
Two-compartment disposition, first-order elimination, allometric scaling for all subjects (PDF p4). No typical-value table (V, CL, Q) is given in this paper. Renal submodels (PDF p4):
- Ceftazidime CL renal-function factor (NCrCL in mL/min/1.73 m2, children over 2 y and adults): NCrCL x 0.01030 below 100; 100 x 0.01030 + (NCrCL - 100) x 0.00125 at or above 100. Slope is about 1% per 1 mL/min below 100 and about 1% per 10 mL/min above (the paper's words).
- Avibactam: (NCrCL/80)^0.99 below 80 (no ESRD); 1 + (NCrCL - 80) x 0.00344 at or above 80 (3.4% per 10 mL/min). Flat above NCrCL 150 for both drugs.
- Children of 2 y or younger: maturation F(t) = 100% x PMA^Hill / (PMA^Hill + TM50^Hill), TM50 = 47.7 weeks, Hill = 3.4 (fixed to Rhodin 2009); PMA = postnatal age + 40 weeks; normal adult CrCL taken as 121 mL/min (assumed per 1.73 m2).
- ESRD avibactam off-dialysis CL is about 7% of typical (0.721 L/h for a typical 70-kg adult), vs 0.8 L/h noncompartmental in anuric patients (ref 19). Ceftazidime non-renal CL in the ESRD sensitivity analysis 0.408 L/h (6.8 mL/min), taken from Leroy 1984 assuming a 930 mg dose (15 mg/kg x 62 kg).
- For under-2-year-olds the CL adjustment factor intervals were avibactam [0.55, 0.88] mild, [0.33, 0.55] moderate, [0.17, 0.33] severe 1; ceftazidime [0.49, 0.78], [0.29, 0.49], [0.15, 0.29].
- Free fractions used for PTA: ceftazidime 85%, avibactam 92%.

Approved doses (Table 1, PDF p2), ceftazidime-avibactam, 2-h infusion: adults >50 mL/min 2000-500 mg q8h; 31-50 1000-250 q8h; 16-30 750-187.5 q12h; 6-15 q24h; ESRD q48h (same 750-187.5 mg). Children 2 to <18 y: >50 50-12.5 mg/kg q8h (max 2000-500); 31-50 25-6.25 mg/kg q8h (max 1000-250); 16-30 18.75-4.75 mg/kg q12h; 6-15 q24h; ESRD q48h. 6 months to <2 y: same as above (and 16-30 18.75-4.75 mg/kg q12h, 6-15 q24h, ESRD q48h; US does not approve <2 y with CrCL <51). 3 to <6 months: >50 40-10 mg/kg q8h; 31-50 20-5 q8h; 16-30 15-3.75 q12h.

Selected simulated results (geometric mean (CV%), steady state; Tables 2-6, PDF p7-11): adults with normal renal function on 2000-500 mg q8h, cIAI: ceftazidime Cmax 58.9 mg/L, AUC0-24 602 mg h/L; avibactam Cmax 10.5, AUC0-24 107; joint PTA 95%. Pediatric 12-<18 y normal function, cIAI: ceftazidime Cmax 64.6, AUC0-24 618; avibactam Cmax 12.3, AUC0-24 121; PTA 96%. Pediatric ESRD cIAI (AUC0-48 shown): 12-<18 y ceftazidime 1,118, avibactam 246; joint PTA 96% (2 to <6 y cIAI ESRD 92%). The tables hold every age, renal and indication cell; not transcribed here.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
PK/PD target: 50% fT>8 mg/L for ceftazidime and 50% fT>1 mg/L for avibactam, achieved simultaneously (joint PTA), on free concentrations (PDF p5). Joint PTA 95-99% in adults at approved doses (Table 2). No ke0, EC50, MEAC or potency ratio; not an opioid.

## Covariates
Body weight (allometric) for all; NCrCL (bedside Schwartz, children >2 y; Cockcroft-Gault for adults in dose labelling) for CL; postmenstrual-age renal maturation at 2 y and under; indication (cIAI, cUTI, HAP/VAP) in the simulations; ESRD indicator for avibactam CL.

## Caveats
As given by the authors (PDF p12): no pediatric HAP/VAP PK data in the models; very few children with renal impairment (2); none under 2 y in the cIAI trial; no critically ill children; ceftazidime renal-impairment data in adults came from the literature; ESRD ceftazidime exposure rests on an assumed non-renal CL and an assumed dose. Doses for under-2-year-olds with renal impairment, and HAP/VAP in children, rest on simulation only.
