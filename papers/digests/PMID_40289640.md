# McCann 2025: Ku diazepam model re-estimated with real-world paediatric (mostly obese) data

## Study
- POP01 (NCT01431326), standard-of-care dosing at 14 centres: 61 participants analysed, 2.5-20.6 y (median 12.0), weight 75.0 kg (12.0-149), 80% with obesity (Table 1); 87 diazepam + 82 nordiazepam concentrations; 52 received oral doses (75 of 87 observations after oral). Oral 0.5-15 mg, IV 0.6-7.0 mg. No one had both IV and oral data, so F could not be estimated.
- Combined with the PSS dataset behind Ku 2018 (87 children, 162 concentrations, IV only).
- HPLC-MS/MS, 1-1000 ng/mL. NONMEM 7.5, FOCE-I.
- Ku model externally evaluated (Table 2: PRED MPE 66%, MAPE 96.4%, F30 34.5%), then re-estimated.

## Model and parameters
Ku two-compartment structure, oral first-order absorption added with Ka fixed 0.5 /h (from digitised Friedman 1992 data) and F fixed 100% (assumption from Greenblatt 1989 and label). Allometric exponents fixed 0.75 (CL, Q) and 1 (volumes).

Derived (my simulation, 70 kg, 10 mg PO, Ka 0.5 /h): peak 88 ng/mL at 3.6 h (Vc 58.2) or 93 ng/mL at 3.4 h (Vc 52.8); with Ka 1.99 /h, 123 ng/mL at 1.2 h. Ku parameters with Ka 0.5: 96 ng/mL.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Target 200-600 ng/mL at 10 min post IV dose (from Ku; authors note limited evidence). Simulations: 0.2 mg/kg capped at 8 mg under-exposes older children with obesity; uncapped dosing (up to 32 mg) reaches target in all.

## Covariates
Total body weight (allometric). No obesity covariate retained.

## Caveats
Sparse, SOC data; F and Ka assumed; nordiazepam parameters not identifiable; distribution parameters poorly informed by POP01 alone.
