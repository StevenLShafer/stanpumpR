# Pottel 2021: the EKFC full-age-spectrum creatinine eGFR equation

## Study
Cross-sectional analysis of pooled measured-GFR data from 13 cohorts (19 629 participants, all non-Black, European
Kidney Function Consortium). Development + internal validation: 7 cohorts, 11 251 participants, split 75/25
(n = 8473 / 2778). External validation: 6 cohorts, 8378 participants (1254 children 2 to <18 y; 972 aged 18-<40,
3585 aged 40-<65, 2567 aged >=65). mGFR by plasma or urinary clearance of iohexol, inulin, 51Cr-EDTA or iothalamate.
SCr IDMS-traceable (CRIC corrected). Least-squares regression of log mGFR on log SCr, age, sex; SAS 9.4.
Mean mGFR 76.9 / 77.7 / 78.9 mL/min/1.73 m2 (development / internal / external); mean age 42.4 y (SD 25.2) and
50.9 y (SD 22.3); ~56% and 47% male.

## Model and parameters
EKFC equation (Figure 1, p. 184), eGFR in mL/min/1.73 m2, SCr and Q in µmol/L (divide by 88.4 for mg/dL):
- age 2-40 y: 107.3 x (SCr/Q)^-0.322 if SCr/Q < 1; 107.3 x (SCr/Q)^-1.132 if SCr/Q >= 1
- age > 40 y: the same times 0.990^(Age - 40)

Q = median SCr of healthy people of that age and sex (Figure 1):
- ages 2-25 y, males: ln(Q) = 3.200 + 0.259 Age - 0.543 ln(Age) - 0.00763 Age^2 + 0.0000790 Age^3
- ages 2-25 y, females: ln(Q) = 3.080 + 0.177 Age - 0.223 ln(Age) - 0.00596 Age^2 + 0.0000686 Age^3
- ages > 25 y: Q = 80 µmol/L (0.90 mg/dL) males, 62 µmol/L (0.70 mg/dL) females

Derived Q values (derived: from the Figure 1 polynomials / 88.4): boys 27.4 µmol/L at 2 y, 0.353 mg/dL at 5 y,
0.818 mg/dL at 18 y; girls 25.9 µmol/L at 2 y, 0.676 mg/dL at 18 y.

Performance (Table 1, external validation): median bias -1.2 (children), 0.8 (18-<40), -1.1 (40-<65), -1.2 (>=65)
mL/min/1.73 m2; P30 79.7%, 89.6%, 89.5%, 85.3%. Overall adult bias -0.9, P30 88.0% (Table 2). EKFC age coefficient
0.990 lies between FAS 0.988 and CKD-EPI 0.993 (p. 186-187).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Not applicable (renal-function estimating equation; no drug).

## Covariates
SCr, age, sex (via Q); height only in an optional height-based Q (Supplement, not in this PDF).

## Caveats
- Developed in White populations only; no Black patients.
- Q polynomials are defined for 2-25 y only; nothing below 2 y.
- Bias about -11 mL/min/1.73 m2 in children under 5 (p. 187); high bias in ages 2-4 (Table 2).
- Imprecision highest when SCr is in the normal range; overestimates GFR with low muscle mass.
- The worked example on p. 188 gives a 5-year-old a normal SCr of 33.6 µmol/L (0.38 mg/dL), slightly above the
  Figure 1 Q at age 5 (31.2 boys, 32.0 girls; derived) - presumably a height-based Q; not explained in the main text.
- Proteinuria/hypoalbuminemia not assessed.
