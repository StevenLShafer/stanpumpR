# Moriguchi 2025: NET occupancy and availability during duloxetine treatment of MDD (PET)

## Study
- Longitudinal study (UMIN000008251), Chiba, Oct 2012 - Mar 2016. 15 drug-naive (>= 1 yr) patients with DSM-IV MDD,
  age 39.7 +/- 10.3 yr (20-54), 9 men; baseline HAM-D 21.3 +/- 4.8 (Table 1). Weight not reported.
- (S,S)-[18F]FMeNER-D2 PET at baseline, after 4-6 weeks of duloxetine 20-60 mg/day (mean 34 +/- 13.5 mg), and in 7
  responders after a 2-month washout (mean 9.9 months on drug).
- Thalamus (Oxford connectivity atlas subregions), locus coeruleus; **caudate** reference (the 2017 study used white
  matter); AUC ratio 120-180 min. Plasma duloxetine by GC-MS (LLOQ 0.1 ng/mL) just before and after the second scan.
- Occupancy model 100 x C / (C + EC50) (Emax fixed 100%), GraphPad Prism 6. Test-retest in 5 healthy subjects.

Individual plasma concentrations and occupancies are not tabulated (Fig. 1 shows dose vs occupancy only, as the
legend reads). Occupancy about 30-40% across 20-60 mg/day.
Thalamic (AUC ratio - 1): 0.52 +/- 0.10 before vs 0.55 +/- 0.11 after treatment (n = 7, not significant). Change in
NET availability correlated with post-treatment HAM-D (r = 0.83, P = .018), not with HAM-D change. Test-retest:
Cronbach alpha 0.973, ICC single 0.770, average 0.870.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: thalamic NET occupancy (PET biomarker) at steady state in MDD; EC50 71.21 ng/mL total plasma duloxetine.
No significant correlation of occupancy with HAM-D at 4-6 weeks. No ke0. Not an opioid.

## Covariates
None.

## Caveats
n = 15 (7 completing all scans); high dropout; doses limited to the Japanese maximum of 60 mg/day, so occupancy
never approaches Emax and EC50 rests on Emax = 100%; reference region differs from the 2017 study, so the two EC50s
are not strictly comparable. Plasma data not shown.
