# Hoeben 2016: population PK of canagliflozin

## Study
- Pooled analysis of 14 studies (9 phase I, 2 phase II, 3 phase III; Table 1, PDF pp. 4-5): final dataset 9061
  samples from 1616 people (healthy volunteers, non-diabetic renal impairment, overweight/obese non-diabetics, type 2
  diabetes). Doses 25 mg (study 9) to 1600 mg (study 1, single dose), mostly 50-300 mg once daily, 300 mg bid in two
  studies.
- Stepwise build (section 2.2, p. 3): phase I model on 5715 rich samples from 245 volunteers (absorption and
  distribution covariates); then 8813 samples from 1526 people (pre-final model, covariates on ke); external evaluation
  on 182 samples from 174 people (studies 13, 14); final re-run on all 9061 / 1616.
- Population (section 3.1, p. 6): phase I median age 45 y, 63% men, 59% white, median baseline eGFR 102 mL/min/1.73 m2,
  40% T2DM. Pooled: median age 55 y, 71% white, median eGFR 87, 75% T2DM, sexes balanced. Weight, BMI medians and ranges
  are only "in Supplementary material" (not in this PDF).
- (supplement, 2026-10-27) ESM 2 Table 2 (demographics, per study and totals), median (range):
  - Phase I total, N = 245 (studies 1-9): age 45.0 y (18.0-77.0); weight 75.8 kg (47.6-151), mean 77.1; BMI 26.1
    kg/m2 (18.0-44.5), mean 26.9; MDRD eGFR 102 mL/min/1.73 m2 (9.0-194), mean 97.1; men 154 (63%).
  - Pooled total, N = 1616: age 55.0 y (18.0-90.0); weight 86.0 kg (42.5-167), mean 88.0; BMI 31.1 kg/m2
    (18.0-54.7), mean 31.7; eGFR 87.0 (9.0-194), mean 86.3; men 769 (48%), women 847 (52%); diabetic 1219 (75%).
  - Formulation and food: all 1371 phase II/III subjects (studies 10-14) took over-encapsulated tablets; the
    non-encapsulated tablet was taken by 174 phase I subjects only (11% of the pooled population). 1592 (99%) were
    fed; only study 2 (24 women) was fasted. UGT1A9*3 carriers 21 (1%); no genotype for 895 (55%).
  - Per-study counts (ESM 2 Table 1, analysed) sum to 245 (phase I) and 1616 (all); the white share, pooled
    (942 + 203)/1616 = 70.9% and phase I (116 + 28)/245 = 58.8% (derived), agrees with section 3.1's 71% and 59%.
  - ESM 2 Table 5 (covariate model development, an embedded image whose own caption reads "Table 7"): base model
    run525 OFV -8346.8; eGFR, age, dose and race on ke tested; age (+7.9) and race (+1.3) removed on backward deletion;
    pre-final run544 = run541 (eGFR and dose on ke), re-run on the final dataset as final run549, OFV -8516.6; adding
    particle size on ke dOFV -0.5, UGT1A9 genotype on ke -15.8 (not kept).
- Assay: LC-MS/MS, total unchanged canagliflozin in plasma, LLOQ 5.00 ng/mL (1.00 ng/mL in study 9) (p. 2-3). BLQ and
  pre-dose samples excluded (5.6%).
- NONMEM VII, FOCE-I, ADVAN4 TRANS1 (rate constants), exponential IIV, additive error on log scale with a separate
  residual for phase II/III data (p. 3, 5). Covariates by Eq. 1, TVP = theta_x (cov / cov median)^theta_y (p. 5);
  forward inclusion and backward deletion at dOFV 10.83 (p < 0.001).

## Model and parameters
Two compartments, lag, then zero-order release over D1 into a gut depot, then first-order absorption; first-order
elimination from the central compartment (Fig. 1, p. 7). Final model Table 5 (p. 10, rendered): absorption and
distribution parameters, their covariates and IIV were fixed at the phase I estimates (Table 3, p. 8); only ke and its
covariates were estimated on the pooled data. CL/F = Vc/F x ke (section 2.5, p. 6).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. Plasma concentration only; no ke0, no glucose, UGE or HbA1c model in this paper.

## Covariates
- Vc/F: sex (categorical, women 82.6 L), weight^0.583, age^-0.167 (Table 5). Exposure effects (Table 6, p. 11,
  rendered): women/men AUC ratio 1.22; age > 60 / < 50 y 1.29; weight < 78.2 / > 95.2 kg 1.33.
- ka and Tlag: BMI, one exponent 1.41 (Table 5). Text (section 3.2, p. 6) and Table 2 (p. 6, rendered): median ka about
  twice as high at BMI < 24.2 as > 28.0 kg/m2 (ratio 1.99), median Tlag about twice as long at BMI > 28.0 (ratio 2.00),
  so ka falls and Tlag rises with BMI. The sign convention for applying the single exponent to each is not printed.
  (supplement, 2026-10-27) ESM 2 Table 4 reprints these ratios with the footnotes bound correctly (b: ka, BMI < 24.2 /
  > 28.0, n = 82 each, 1.99 [1.35-3.32]; c: Tlag, BMI > 28.0 / < 24.2, 2.00 [0.98-7.54]), confirming the direction.
  ESM 2 Tables 3 and 6 still give one value, 1.41, as theta_y of Eq. 1 for "body mass index on ka and Tlag"; neither
  supplement contains control-stream code, so how the sign is split between ka and Tlag is still not printed.
- (supplement, 2026-10-27) ESM 1: under TRANS1 the covariates sit on V and K; the equivalent TRANS3 model has
  TVCL = TVK x TVV, so CL/F carries the weight, sex and age effects of Vc/F as well as eGFR and dose (ESM 1, Eq. 6
  and last paragraph).
- Tlag: over-encapsulation (0.262 vs 0.147 h; observed medians ~16 vs ~8 min, ratio 1.93; AUC only 6% higher).
- ke: MDRD eGFR^0.261 and total daily dose^-0.0631. Renal exposure ratios vs normal (>= 90): mild 1.11, moderate 1.40,
  severe (n = 18) 1.29 (Table 6). Daily dose 300/50 mg 1.16, 300/100 mg 1.07 (Table 6).
- UGT1A9*3 carriers (n = 21, ~1%): dOFV -15.8 and AUC ratio 1.26, but not kept in the final model (section 3.3, p. 7).
- Not significant on ke: age, sex, fed status, race, glycaemic status, UGT substrates, particle size (Table 4, p. 9).

## Caveats
- Absorption parameters are poorly informed (IIV 123% and 79%; strong ka-Tlag inverse correlation); the phase I model
  over-predicted the elimination phase (Fig. 2), resolved once phase II/III data entered.
- Vc/F was not re-estimated on the pooled data; its covariates rest on 245 phase I subjects.
- The medians used in Eq. 1 (weight, BMI, total daily dose) are not printed in the paper; nor is which dataset's
  median each covariate uses. (supplement, 2026-10-27) ESM 2 Table 2 prints the demographic medians of both datasets
  (phase I: weight 75.8 kg, BMI 26.1, age 45.0, eGFR 102; pooled: 86.0, 31.1, 55.0, 87.0), but neither supplement
  says which of them Eq. 1 divides by, and neither prints a median (or any distribution) of total daily dose. ESM 2
  Table 7 gives only subject counts at 50, 100 and 300 mg (164, 610, 589). The covariate development (ESM 2 Table 5)
  is consistent with ke's covariates being built on the pooled data and Vc/F's and the absorption's on phase I, but
  that does not establish which median the control stream coded.
- (supplement, 2026-10-27) The marketed non-encapsulated tablet's Tlag (0.147 h) rests on the 174 phase I subjects who
  took it: every phase II/III subject took over-encapsulated tablets (ESM 2 Table 2). Almost all data are fed (99%).
- Table 2's superscripts are crossed relative to its footnotes (the BMI-on-Tlag cell carries "c", whose footnote
  describes over-encapsulation, and vice versa); seen on the rendered page, a source typesetting error. The values agree
  with the text. (supplement, 2026-10-27) The same table in ESM 2 (its Table 4) binds b and c correctly; there the
  over-encapsulation cell carries "d" but its footnote is printed with a second "c".
- (supplement, 2026-10-27) ESM 2 numbers its tables differently from the paper: ESM 2 Table 3 = paper Table 3,
  Table 4 = paper Table 2, Table 6 = paper Table 5, Table 7 = paper Table 6; the covariate-development image filed as
  ESM 2 Table 5 is captioned "Table 7" inside the image.
