# Schüttler 1988: TIVA with propofol and alfentanil by computer-assisted infusion

## Study
Open clinical series, Bonn. 20 ASA 1-2 patients, 18-52 y, 52-85 kg, general surgery. Premedication flunitrazepam 2 mg PO the evening before and 1 mg PO 1 h before. Vecuronium 2 mg, then computer-controlled propofol targeting a **blood** level of 2.5 ug/mL; alfentanil started 30 s later targeting a **plasma** level of 100 ng/mL for 5 min, restarted at skin incision and titrated to stimulation; vecuronium 3 mg, intubation 2 min later; ventilation with oxygen in air (FiO2 0.5 as printed, garbled). Propofol raised in 0.25 ug/mL steps for lightening; alfentanil generally stopped 30 min before the end of surgery; propofol held until the last skin suture. Device: the anaesthetist sets a single target on one dial, like a vaporiser; algorithm from Schwilden 1981 (ref 9); PK sets from the group's earlier studies (refs 3, 10). Propofol by HPLC (whole blood), alfentanil by RIA (plasma).

Surgery 104 (43) min. Propofol induction: about 65 mg in the first minute, then about 10 mg/min declining exponentially to 5 mg/min (p. 6). Largest deviations: alfentanil within 50% at 200-300 ng/mL; propofol within 60% at 2.5-3.0 ug/mL.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Clinical end points only: adequacy of hypnosis, response to noxious stimulation, response to verbal command, extubation and orientation. No ke0, EC50 or concentration-response modelling. Opioid audit: no MEAC; alfentanil plasma concentrations for major vs minor surgical stimulation with propofol 2.4 ug/mL are 285 vs 148 ng/mL (Table 1); no potency ratio.

## Covariates
None; alfentanil set given without weight correction (authors argue a small-group set is widely applicable).

## Caveats
Uncontrolled series of 20; flunitrazepam premedication; propofol bias (m/p 0.88) attributed to a volunteer PK set or alfentanil interaction; Table 1 garbled in the text layer.
