# Catafau 2006: paroxetine serotonin-transporter occupancy by 123I-ADAM SPECT in depression

## Study
- 10 Caucasian MDD outpatients (4 men, 6 women), age 36 +/- 10.8 y (20-53); HAMD-17 >= 18; drug-free >= 6 months;
  benzodiazepines (10-20 mg/day diazepam equivalent) allowed. Weight not reported.
- SPECT before treatment (3.2 +/- 2.5 days after inclusion) and after 4-6 weeks (39.0 +/- 6.1 days) of
  **paroxetine 20 mg/day**. 9 completed; patient 9 had only the baseline scan.
- 10 previously studied healthy volunteers (7 M, 3 F; 36.2 y) as a comparison group.
- 123I-ADAM 170.7 +/- 25.3 MBq IV, acquisition at 240 min; MRI coregistration; tissue-ratio method with
  cerebellum reference: SUR = (R - C)/C; occupancy = 100 x (SURpre - SURpost)/SURpre.
- One plasma sample per patient during the post-treatment SPECT session; paroxetine by HPLC with fluorescence.
  Timing relative to dose not stated.

## Model and parameters
No PK model. Emax model of mean occupancy (average of midbrain, thalamus, striatum) vs plasma paroxetine (Methods;
Results; Fig. 3, n = 9):
%SERTocc = %SERToccmax x Cp / (Cp50 + Cp).

Mean occupancy (Table 2): midbrain 66.4 +/- 9.5% (53-80), thalamus 63.0 +/- 9.6% (48-74), striatum 61.3 +/- 10.8%
(42-75; abstract prints 10.9); no regional difference (p = 0.562). Individual values in Table 1. SUR in drug-free
MDD did not differ from healthy volunteers. Occupancy using HV or MDD group baselines gave similar values.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: SERT occupancy by 123I-ADAM SPECT (biomarker). Emax 70.5%, Cp50 2.7 ng/mL (total plasma). Authors note
the fit is limited because all patients took 20 mg/day, so the data sit on the flat part of the curve; Cp50 close
to Kent 2002 (2.9 ng/mL, PET). Seven of nine responded (HAMD >= 50% fall); no correlation between occupancy and
HAMD change (R2 0.006-0.057). No ke0 or time course. Not an opioid.

## Covariates
None.

## Caveats
n = 9 for the fit; one dose level; one plasma sample per patient, timing vs dose not stated; Emax below 100%
attributed by the authors to technical rather than biological factors; SPECT test-retest variability 13-19%.
