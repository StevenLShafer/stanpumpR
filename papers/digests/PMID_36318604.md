# Hannam 2023: celecoxib, valdecoxib, rofecoxib plasma-CSF kinetics and a celecoxib analgesic ke0

## Study
Secondary analysis of Dembo 2005: 10 healthy adults (40% female, 25 ± 4 y, 73 ± 14 kg) given celecoxib 200 mg,
valdecoxib 40 mg and rofecoxib 50 mg orally together; plasma and CSF at 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 10 h
(LC-MS; celecoxib LLOQ 1 µg/L plasma, 0.5 µg/L CSF). Unbound fraction from one ultrafiltered sample per subject;
celecoxib binding 99.7 ± 0.1%. NONMEM 7.5, FOCE-I, ADVAN6; allometric standardisation to 70 kg (CL exponent 3/4,
V 1); 100 bootstraps. PD: separate re-analysis of mean pain relief (0-4) digitised from Malmstrom 2002 (third-molar
extraction, n = 482 total trial, celecoxib 200 and 400 mg arms; 24 mean observations 0-12 h), with PK fixed to
Table 1, Emax model + effect compartment + placebo model.

0.619 /h = 0.0103 /min.)

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Analgesic hysteresis (counterclockwise) collapsed by an effect compartment: T1/2keo 1.12 h, Ce50 242 µg/L total,
Emax 3.25 on the 0-4 pain-relief scale (dental surgery, adults, mean data). CSF equilibration (0.84 h) is faster
than the analgesic keo; authors state CSF is not the effect compartment but approximates it better than plasma.
Simulated targets: adult 200 mg then 100 mg bid → mean steady-state total plasma 174 µg/L, CSF 1.1 µg/L
(~3/10 VAS reduction); 25 kg child 6 then 3 mg/kg bid → 282 and 1.7 µg/L. In vitro COX-2 IC50 15.5 µg/L cited as
unsuitable. Not an opioid; no MEAC.

## Covariates
Allometric weight scaling (fixed exponents); CYP2C9*3 assumed -30% CL in simulations (not estimated).

## Caveats
n = 10, 10 h sampling, one-compartment; single unbound sample per subject; PD from digitised mean data of another
study with PK borrowed from this one; no concentration-response data from the same patients.
