# Leegwater 2025: popPK of trimethoprim, sulfamethoxazole and N-acetyl-sulfamethoxazole (eGFR, CRRT)

## Study
Retrospective, multicentre (LUMC, Erasmus MC, UMCG; the Netherlands) cohort of hospitalized adults (>=18 y) given IV or oral cotrimoxazole 2016-2021, routine TDM data (p2-3; printed 185-187). 168 patients (16 LUMC, 116 Erasmus MC, 36 UMCG). 64.3% male, age 58.2 +/- 15.2 y, weight 76.7 +/- 16.2 kg, height 174.0 +/- 10.3 cm, BMI 25.3 +/- 5.0, serum creatinine 119.7 +/- 91.8 umol/L, albumin 26.6 +/- 6.8 g/L, eGFR (CKD-EPI) 70.8 +/- 33.2 mL/min/1.73 m2 (median 70), 18 (10.7%) on CRRT, 44.6% oral / 55.4% IV initial route (Table 1). Excluded: ECMO, intermittent haemodialysis. 348 SMX and N-acetyl-SMX concentrations (SMX 2-380 mg/L; NAc-SMX 2-173.4 mg/L); TMP measured in 52 patients (LUMC + UMCG), 137 concentrations (0.2-15.6 mg/L), peak and trough. Plasma, HPLC-DAD or LC-MS/MS (total concentration; no free fraction). NONMEM 7.4, FOCE-I, PsN 5.0.0; 500 bootstrap, pcVPC; Monte Carlo n = 1000.

## Model and parameters
Trimethoprim: one-compartment, first-order absorption, F fixed to 1 (estimated ~100% first), first-order elimination, proportional error. Sulfamethoxazole + N-acetyl-SMX: integrated one-compartment model each, F fixed to 1, 40% of SMX clearance converted to the metabolite (assumed from the literature, no urine data), proportional error. Allometric weight, age and BMI were tested; eGFR and CRRT were the only retained covariates (on CL of all three). All values are typical population estimates; clearances are apparent (CL/F, but F = 1), absolute L/h, not per kg.

Clearance formulas (Table 2/3 footnotes, PDF p5): TMP no CRRT = 4.21 x (eGFR/68)^0.317; with CRRT = 4.21 x 1.12. SMX no CRRT = 0.97 x (eGFR/68)^0.27; with CRRT = 1.34 x 2.2 (as printed in the footnote; 1.34 is the metabolite's CL, so this is probably a typo for 0.97 x 2.2 = 2.13 L/h, which is what the text's "2.2 times higher" implies). NAc-SMX no CRRT = 1.34 x (eGFR/68)^0.797; with CRRT = 1.34 x 0.683. eGFR in mL/min/1.73 m2. Parameters are in Tables 2 and 3 (PDF p5, read on the rendered page).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD model and no ke0. Not an opioid. Targets used for simulation (Cmax in the dosing interval, p2 and p3; originally from two small PCP studies): SMX >100 mg/L and TMP >5 mg/L minimum for Pneumocystis pneumonia; toxicity thresholds SMX >200 mg/L, NAc-SMX >75 mg/L, TMP >15 mg/L. All plasma concentrations (total drug).

Simulated results (n = 1000, IV, first 5 days; Table 4, PDF p8): dose needed for exposure equal to eGFR 70 is 100% at eGFR 50, 83.3% at eGFR 30, 66.7% at eGFR 10; on CRRT 200% for SMX and 100% for TMP. Median patient (eGFR 70): b.i.d. 2400 mg gave 89.0% in range for TMP and 68.7% for SMX/NAc-SMX; t.i.d. 1920 mg 90.9% and 63.0%.

## Covariates
eGFR (CKD-EPI) as power function normalized to 68 mL/min/1.73 m2 on CL of all three analytes; CRRT as a multiplier on CL. Weight, age, BMI tested, not retained. CRRT: SMX CL 2.2x (95% CI 2.0-2.4 in text); NAc-SMX CL 0.68x; TMP CL 1.12x (ns, CI 0.77-1.57). The comparator for the CRRT multipliers is the same model's no-CRRT clearance at eGFR 68 (the text says "median eGFR of 70" patients).

## Caveats
Retrospective TDM with uncertain dosing and sampling times; few samples per patient; CKD-EPI eGFR is poor in ICU and trimethoprim raises creatinine (10-30%) by blocking tubular secretion; CRRT settings not modelled (18 patients); ka for TMP imprecise (RSE 54%); no NAT2/CYP2C19 genotype or urine pH; no urine data for the metabolite fraction.
