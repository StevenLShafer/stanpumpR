# Tulloch 2002: Adderall XR bioavailability vs Adderall IR twice daily; food and sprinkle effects

## Study
Shire-sponsored, randomised, open-label crossovers in healthy adults 18-55 y, weight within 10% of ideal.
- Study A: n = 20 (13 M / 7 F, mean age 40.4 y, mean 167.8 lb, 69.8 in); 4-way Latin square, 7-day washouts.
  Single 20 mg of XR test formulations A, B or C versus Adderall IR 10 mg twice 4 h apart (second dose at lunch).
  All dosed ~20 min after a standard high-fat breakfast. 17 samples to 48 h.
- Study B: n = 21 (11 M / 10 F, mean 35 y); 3-way crossover of SLI381 (= formulation A) 30 mg intact fasted,
  intact after high-fat breakfast, or sprinkled on applesauce; samples to 60 h.
- Assay: chiral HPLC-MS/MS, d- and l-amphetamine, linear 0.5-50 ng/mL, LOQ 0.5 ng/mL, CV <= 5.28%.
- Noncompartmental PK; ANOVA, Dunnett, 90% CI average bioequivalence (0.80-1.25).
- Formulation: capsule of two bead types 50:50; immediate-release beads and delayed-release beads designed to
  release about 4 h after dosing. Adderall d:l = 3:1.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD measured beyond pulse/BP (pulse +10-11 bpm at 24 h in Study B). Authors note behavioural effects are
greatest in the absorption phase and correlate poorly with concentration (literature). No EC50, no ke0.

## Covariates
None.

## Caveats
Healthy adults, mostly fed; sponsor study; no children; noncompartmental only. Release timing of the delayed
beads (~4 h) is a design statement, not measured in vivo.
