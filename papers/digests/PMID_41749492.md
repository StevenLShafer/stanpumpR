# Daugherty 2026: sublingual cyclobenzaprine (TNX-102 SL), two Phase 1 studies

## Study
Study 1 (NCT01889173): single-centre, randomised, single-dose, open-label, parallel, fasting, Quebec City. 24 healthy adults (18-65 y, BMI 18.5-30) randomised, 6 per arm: sublingual cyclobenzaprine HCl 2.8 mg with K2HPO4 (Formulation A, later TNX-102 SL), with Na2HPO4 (B), with trisodium citrate (C), or oral IR HCl 5 mg. Mean age 36.2 y, 58% female (Table 1, PDF p.6). Plasma sampling -0.5 h to 48 h (28 times); urine pooled 0-24 and 24-48 h. Assay HPLC-MS/MS (API 5000), 0.200 mL plasma, range 50-10,000 pg/mL, 1/x^2 weighting. NCA (linear trapezoid) plus a model-based analysis (two-compartment mammillary, first-order absorption with lag) fitted to average profiles of 6 subjects per arm because individual fits failed (48 h sampling vs terminal t1/2 of 30-35 h).
Study 2 (NCT04164719): three-period, six-sequence crossover, 16 dosed (15 completed), mean age 52.7 y (36-62), 8 F/8 M, BMI 25.9 (Table 4, PDF p.7). TNX-102 SL 2.8 mg fasting, 5.6 mg (2 x 2.8) fasting, 5.6 mg fed (high-fat meal 30 min before); 28-day washout; sampling to 360 h (37 times).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No pharmacodynamic measurement. Safety only: oral hypoesthesia and somnolence most common (Study 1 Table 3 PDF p.7; Study 2 Table 6 PDF p.9); no serious AEs. The authors mention in-vitro Nav1.7/1.8 sodium-channel inhibition by cyclobenzaprine (99.9%, 95.8% at 30 uM, unpublished) as the presumed cause of oral numbness. No MEAC, ke0, EC50 or therapeutic range reported. Not an opioid.

## Covariates
None modelled. Food: high-fat meal had no meaningful effect on AUC or Cmax at 5.6 mg SL (Table 5). Sex or age effects not analysed.

## Caveats
Small n (6 per arm in Study 1, no crossover, between-subject comparison); healthy volunteers; Study 1 sampled only 48 h against a terminal half-life of about 25-35 h and norcyclobenzaprine about 60 h, so AUC0-inf and Frel are extrapolated (residual area 23-27%) and its half-lives (24-27 h) are shorter than Study 2 (35-38 h). Frel is calculated from group means, not per subject. The 154% relative bioavailability is against oral IR 5 mg; absolute bioavailability is not given. Dosing in these studies was in the morning (the Phase 3 regimen is bedtime). Authors are employees of the sponsor.
