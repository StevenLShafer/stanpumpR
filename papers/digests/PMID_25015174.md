# Nasser 2014: RBP-6000 (SUBLOCADE) PK and buprenorphine mu-receptor occupancy model

## Study
- **PK**: single-centre, open-label, sequential single-ascending-dose study; 36 opioid-dependent (DSM-IV-TR) subjects, RBP-6000 (buprenorphine base 200 mg/mL in ATRIGEL) 50, 100 or 200 mg SC once. Age 35.5 y [CV 34%], 86% male, 80% white, 17% Black, 3% American Indian/Alaskan Native (Table 1). Weight not reported. Plasma to day 150; LC-MS/MS, buprenorphine 0.0250-5.0 ng/mL, norbuprenorphine 0.0200-4.00 ng/mL. 2,797 observations (66 BLQ as missing; 28 |CWRES| > 3 removed → 2,769). NONMEM 7.2 FOCE-I, ADVAN5; 100-replicate bootstrap; VPC.
- **PK/occupancy**: individual data from two earlier Greenwald studies (refs 18, 19): 15 heroin-dependent subjects; trial 1 (n 5) sublingual maintenance at 32, 16, 2, 0 mg/day with [11C]-carfentanil PET 4 h post-dose; trial 2 (n 10) 16 mg/day SL with PET at 4, 28, 52, 76 h after the last dose. 59 paired PK/occupancy points. Whole-brain mu-receptor occupancy (μORO) = 100 − receptor availability.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: brain mu-opioid receptor occupancy by [11C]-carfentanil PET (whole brain), against **total plasma buprenorphine** concentration (sublingual maintenance data); no ke0 (direct model, assumed no equilibration delay).
- About linear up to ~2 ng/mL; plateau of 70-90% occupancy at 2-3 ng/mL. **2 ng/mL gives ~70% μORO** (Key Points; p. 820); "C ≥ 70%" target chosen because a prior study (Greenwald 2007, ref 19) put the threshold for withdrawal suppression and agonist blockade at 50-60% occupancy, with added benefit at 70%.
- Withdrawal scores and hydromorphone (24 mg) agonist-effect blockade related linearly to receptor availability (Fig. 4b); at 2 ng/mL withdrawal and agonist scores ≤ 2 (of 64).
- Simulation: 200 mg RBP-6000 monthly gives 2-3 ng/mL average concentrations and ≥ 70% occupancy (abstract; Fig. 5).
- **MEAC**: none (no analgesia end point). **Potency ratio**: none. Sublingual bioavailability cited as 50-60% (p. 816, background).
- Derived from Eq. 1: 70% at 2.19 ng/mL; 60% at 1.28 ng/mL; 50% at 0.81 ng/mL; 1.25 ng/mL gives 59.5%; 3 ng/mL gives 74.7%.

## Covariates
None retained (age, sex, race, dose tested).

## Caveats
- Small n (36 PK; 15 occupancy, 59 points); few observations at saturation (> 3-4 ng/mL); IIV on Emax not estimable; authors note generalisability limited.
- Occupancy data came from sublingual maintenance in heroin-dependent volunteers, not from RBP-6000.
- Industry-sponsored (Reckitt Benckiser).
