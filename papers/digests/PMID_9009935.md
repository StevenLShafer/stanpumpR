# Minto 1997: remifentanil PK/PD, influence of age and gender (model development)

## Study
- 65 healthy ASA 1-2 volunteers, 38 men / 27 women, aged 20-85 yr (Table 1: young 20-40 yr n = 32, middle 41-65
  n = 15, elderly > 65 n = 18). Pooled from four phases: 10 men 22-38 yr, 1-8 ug/kg/min for 20 min (Egan 1993);
  10 men 23-39 yr, 2 or 3 ug/kg/min for 10 min (Egan 1996 alfentanil cross-over); 10 women 20-33 yr; 35 men and
  women 40-85 yr, 3 ug/kg/min until no further EEG change (4-15 min) (p. 11). Weight about 40-120 kg, LBM about
  30-80 kg (Fig. 1).
- Prospective test set: 15 more volunteers (11 M, 4 F), 41-84 yr, LBM 41-74 kg, EEG only.
- Frequent 3-mL arterial samples to 4 h after infusion; esterases inactivated with acetonitrile; GC high-resolution
  MS, quantitation limit 0.1 ng/mL, CV < 15% (p. 11). Blood (whole-blood) concentrations.
- NONMEM FOCE with eta-epsilon interaction; log-normal IIV; constant-CV residual (PK), additive (PD). Covariates
  screened with a generalized additive model (S-PLUS) on Bayesian estimates: age, sex, weight, height, BSA
  (DuBois), LBM (James: men 1.1 W - 128 (W/H)^2, women 1.07 W - 148 (W/H)^2, W kg, H cm; p. 12).
- PD: EEG spectral edge (SE95), sigmoid Emax with effect site; 4 excluded (noisy/no EEG), 61 in PD analysis.

Age 20 -> 85 yr lowers V1 about 25% and CL1 about 33% (abstract, p. 14).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: EEG spectral edge (SE95), not analgesia. Effect = E0 + (Emax - E0) Ce^γ/(Ce^γ + EC50^γ).
- ke0 0.516 /min (simple); 0.595 - 0.007 (age - 40) /min (complex; typical value for a 40-yr-old). t½ke0 about
  doubles and EC50 about halves from 20 to 85 yr (p. 16, Fig. 6). No tPeak reported.
- EC50 11.2 ng/mL (simple), 13.1 - 0.148 (age - 40) ng/mL (complex), blood concentration, SE95.
- No MEAC; no equianalgesic or potency ratio. Authors recommend halving bolus doses in the elderly and reducing
  infusion rates to about one third (66% x 50%) (p. 21).

## Covariates
Age on all volumes and clearances except V3 (linear term on V3 dropped); LBM (James) on V1, V2, CL1; age on ke0
and EC50. No effect of sex once LBM was included; no nonlinear relations found. LBM-proportional scaling
outperformed weight-proportional (Table 2).

## Caveats
- Healthy volunteers; high infusion rates chosen to reach maximal EEG effect; SE95 is not a clinical end point.
- Half the individuals lacked a measurable third phase (concentrations below detection within 4 h).
- James LBM is not monotone in weight (fails in obesity); the complex model's linear covariate terms can go
  negative outside the studied range.
- Complex PD model improved precision prospectively but increased bias (median PE 0.5% vs 7%).
