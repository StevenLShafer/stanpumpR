# Kerbusch 2001: ketoconazole and rifampin on ifosfamide metabolism

Not to be confused with Kerbusch 2001, Cancer Chemother Pharmacol 48:53-61 (PMID 11488525), a different paper that the registry row `ifosfamide__sclc__kerbusch2001` uses.

## Study
Double-randomised, 2-way crossover, 16 adults with incurable cancers (mean age 55 y, range 42-70; 10 men), Maastricht, April 1998 - April 2000 (PDF p2-3, p6). Eligibility: creatinine clearance at least 60 mL/min, bilirubin at most 25 umol/L, AST at most 50 and ALT at most 60 U/L. Each patient had 2 courses 3 weeks apart: ifosfamide 3 g/m2 as a 24-h IV infusion (reduced from the usual 5 g/m2 for fear of neurotoxicity), alone or with a modulator. 8 patients got ketoconazole 200 mg orally twice daily (1 day before plus 3 days with ifosfamide); 8 got rifampin 300 mg orally twice daily (3 days before plus 3 days with). Each modulator was given in course 1 to 4 patients and in course 2 to 4. Sample size set by practicality (90% power for a 30% change in initial CL, SD 27%). Mesna and saline hydration given; no dexamethasone, barbiturates, cimetidine, macrolides or lorazepam allowed.

Sampling: venous plasma (lithium heparin, on ice) before and at 6, 12, 18, 24, 25, 27, 30, 36 and 48 h after the start of the infusion; urine for 72 h. Semicarbazide added to stabilise 4-hydroxyifosfamide. Assays: GC-NPD for ifosfamide and 2-/3-dechloroethylifosfamide (LLOQ 0.192 umol/L); HPLC-UV for 4-hydroxyifosfamide (LLOQ 0.361 umol/L). Plasma, total drug. Ketoconazole and rifampin concentrations were not measured.

Model/software: NONMEM V 1.1 (first-order estimation). Ifosfamide: one compartment with autoinduction; metabolites fitted sequentially from Bayes post hoc ifosfamide parameters. Modulator effect: P_pop = theta_control x (theta_keto + theta_rif + 1), significant if the 95% CI of theta excludes zero (PDF p5).

## Model and parameters
Equations (PDF p5): during infusion dA/dt = R - CL x Aifo/Vifo; afterwards without R. CL = CLini,ifo x Aenz. dAenz/dt = Kenz,out - Kenz,out x Aenz x (1 - Cifo/(Cifo + IC50)), enzyme half-life T1/2enz = ln2/Kenz,out. Metabolite: dAm/dt = Fm x CL x Aifo/Vifo - Km x Am; Fm and Vm not separable so F* = Fm/Vm (L-1) is estimated. AUC(metabolite) = D x F*/Km. Volumes assumed unchanged by modulators.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No clinical end point. Metabolite exposure is the surrogate: 4-hydroxyifosfamide as the marker of activation/antitumour activity, 2- and 3-dechloroethylifosfamide as markers of the neurotoxic (chloroacetaldehyde) route. Neither modulator raised 4-hydroxyifosfamide exposure or lowered the fraction going to the dechloroethylated metabolites; the authors conclude no therapeutic gain (PDF p9-10). Autoinduction: T1/2enz 25.9 h and IC50 13.2 umol/L here, against 12.7 h and 30.7 umol/L in their earlier 72-h study (PDF p9). No ke0, tPeak, MEAC or potency ratio (not an opioid).

## Covariates
Modulator only (ketoconazole, rifampin) on each parameter as a fractional change. No body-size covariate; dose in g/m2 but BSA, weight and height are not reported.

## Caveats
n=8 per modulator, power set by practicality; IC50 and Kenz,out had no IIV estimated and modulator effects on them could not be tested; IC50 SEM is 74% of the value. Volumes were assumed unchanged. Modulator concentrations not measured. Only a 24-h infusion at 3 g/m2 (lower than the usual dose), mixed tumour types. 4-hydroxyifosfamide F* and K highly correlated.

## Verification against stanpumpR
No code cites the paper. Independent comparison with the library's ifosfamide model (`R/drugs_ifosfamide.R:67-72`, from Kerbusch 2000, PMID 10848719: CL 2.94 L/h initial, V 43.5 L, Kenz,out 0.0546 /h, IC50 30.7 umol/L; 72-h infusions of 9-12 g/m2 in 15 sarcoma patients): this paper's control CLini 3.44 L/h is 1.17x the library's 2.94, V 42.7 L is 0.98x of 43.5 L, Kenz,out 0.0268 is 0.49x of 0.0546 /h (T1/2enz 25.9 vs 12.7 h), IC50 13.2 is 0.43x of 30.7 umol/L. Same units in both (L/h, L, 1/h, umol/L; the library converts to L/min and mg/L at `R/drugs_ifosfamide.R:103,138,141`, no unit error found). CL and V agree within 17%; the autoinduction terms differ, which the authors explain by sampling (to 48 h) and the shorter infusion (PDF p9), and the Kenz,out and IC50 SEMs here are wide. The model structure is the same (one compartment, enzyme pool with drug-inhibited degradation, Aenz(0)=1). The library has no ketoconazole or rifampin factor; see the open questions. Verdict: **not-applicable** (uncited; comparison only).
