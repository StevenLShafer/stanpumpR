# Kim E 2012: aripiprazole plasma PK and striatal D2/3 occupancy with an effect compartment ([11C]raclopride)

## Study
- 18 healthy right-handed Korean men, age 22.9 +/- 2.4 y, height 174.6 +/- 4.9 cm, weight 69.6 +/- 6.3 kg.
- Single-blind, single oral dose, parallel groups: 2 mg (n = 4), 5 mg (4), 10 mg (5), 30 mg (5), fasting >= 4 h.
- Plasma aripiprazole at 0, 0.5, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 11, 20, 24, 29, 45, 49, 120 h. Parent only; dehydroaripiprazole
  not reported. Assay method not described in the text layer.
- [11C]raclopride PET predose and at 3, 45, 120 h; striatum (putamen + caudate), cerebellum reference, SRTM BPND;
  occupancy = % reduction of BPND from each subject's own baseline.
- NONMEM VII, FOCE-I. Sequential PK then PD (individual PK estimates fixed in the PD fit). PK: ADVAN4, proportional
  error. PK-PD: ADVAN6, additive error. Plasma-only Emax fit also done for comparison.

Results: dose-normalised Cmax 3.4 +/- 0.9 ng/mL per mg; Tmax 4.0 +/- 2.9 h; NCA t1/2 44.9 +/- 16.8 h. Authors:
central-compartment half-life 0.693 x V1/CL = 15.5 h.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: striatal D2/3 receptor occupancy by [11C]raclopride PET (antagonist radioligand), 4 scans per subject.
- Effect compartment (Sheiner link): occupancy = Emax x Ce / (EC50 + Ce), Emax 100%, EC50 8.63 ng/mL (parent
  aripiprazole plasma equivalents), ke0 0.725 /h; t1/2ke0 = 0.96 h (authors; derived 0.693/0.725 = 0.956 h).
  No tPeak reported. Derived: bolus tPeak of Ce in Kim 2012's own two-compartment PK = 2.19 h.
- Hysteresis observed between plasma concentration and occupancy (Fig. 5A, simulated 2 mg); plasma-only fit gives the higher EC50 11.1 ng/mL.
- Mean peak occupancy: 30.4 +/- 11.1% (2 mg), 54.4 +/- 9.1% (5 mg), 72.3 +/- 6.1% (10 mg), 81.9 +/- 5.9% (30 mg).
- Not an opioid; no MEAC or potency ratio.

## Covariates
None.

## Caveats
Single dose, healthy young men, 4-5 per dose group; only 4 PET points per subject (authors note hysteresis is hard
to see); sequential PK/PD; applicability to repeated dosing explicitly unconfirmed by the authors; parent only;
assay not described here.

## Verification against stanpumpR
- ke0 0.725 /h (aripiprazole.R:69, /60 to 1/min at :126): Table 1. **matches**; help's 57-min half-time
  (0.956 h) **matches** the paper's 0.96 h.
- EC50 8.63 ng/mL, Emax 100%, effect site driven by parent only (aripiprazole.R:58-62; antipsychotics.R:124-126):
  **matches**; "do not add dehydroaripiprazole" is right (the paper measured and modelled parent only). The
  "ke0 and Emax not checked" notes (aripiprazole.R:12, antipsychotics.R:129-130, help :17, docs/antipsychotics.md:43)
  can be cleared.
- Help: occupancy 80% at 34.5 ng/mL (= 4 x 8.63): derived, **matches**.
- 18 healthy men: **matches**.
- **PK/PD transplant: discrepancy (method).** The ke0 was estimated on Kim 2012's own two-compartment PK
  (CL/F 5.8, V1/F 130, Q/F 37.1, V2/F 274 L, ka 0.293 /h) in healthy volunteers, but stanpumpR applies it, as a
  rate constant, to Kim JR 2008's one-compartment PK (V/F 192 L, CL/F 3.15 L/h, ka 1.06 /h). The repo's own rule
  (Minto 2003, carry tPeak when PK and PD come from different papers) is not followed. Derived: Kim 2012's model
  gives a bolus Ce tPeak of 2.19 h; in Kim 2008's one-compartment model ke0 0.725 /h gives a bolus tPeak of about
  5.3 h, and matching 2.19 h would need ke0 about 2.26 /h (t1/2ke0 0.31 h). At steady state the choice hardly
  matters (Ce = Cp), but early after a dose, or after a dose change, the plotted Ce lags more than the source
  model implies. The header says ke0 is "supplied directly", so this is a deliberate choice; it should be stated
  and justified, or carried as tPeak. Absorption also differs: ka 0.293 /h here vs 1.06 /h fixed in Kim 2008.
- Kim 2012's apparent clearance in healthy Korean men (5.8 L/h) is higher than Kim 2008's Group I patients
  (3.15 L/h); not used by stanpumpR, noted for context.
