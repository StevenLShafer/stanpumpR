# Noonan 1985: dose- and time-dependent nitroglycerin clearance in healthy volunteers

## Study
Six healthy volunteers (UCSF; age, weight and sex not given). Tridil 100 ug/mL in D5W from a 50 mL glass syringe on a
Harvard pump through a Tridilset (adsorbs < 1%, ref 17 Baaske) and a Teflon obturator in a peripheral arm vein,
supine. Nominal 10, 20, 40 and again 10 ug/min, 40 min each (PDF p3). Subject M.D. became hypotensive at 40 ug/min and
received only 10 and 20; subject R.D. was studied twice. Venous blood from the **opposite arm** at 10, 20, 30, 40 min of
each rate, and 1, 2, 4, 6, 10, 15, 20, 40, 60, 120, 180, 240 min after the last stop. Capillary GC-ECD, LLOQ
25 pg/mL GTN (CV 7.8%), 100 pg/mL dinitrates (metabolites in 4 of 6). Delivered rates checked in vitro by HPLC
(40.6 +/- 1.7 ug/min over 105 min; 10.2, 20.1, 39.7, 10.2 ug/min; PDF p5) and per subject (Table I). Css = AUC/interval
and CL = rate x interval/AUC over the last 30 min of each rate (PDF p4).

and Table III (PDF p9), means (+/- SD) over 7 subject-occasions (6 at high and second low):

Table III's second row is labelled "R.D. #1" again (source typo for R.D. #2; Table I and II say #2). CL at the high rate
and the second low rate did not differ (p = 0.75); first low vs 20 and vs second low differed (p < 0.05) (PDF p9).
Clearance at the first 10 ug/min was 2-5 times that at the final 10 in all but R.D. #2 (text, PDF p9; Table III gives
1.8-5.8, derived). Css rose more than proportionally with rate and showed hysteresis on the way down; the authors
propose end-product (dinitrate) inhibition or saturable vascular binding. 1,2-GDN/GTN 5-15-fold; 1,2-/1,3-GDN about 7-8
(Table IV, PDF p11).

Discussion cross-references (PDF p11, rendered): McNiff 1981 venous CL 54.5 +/- 21.1 (29.8-87.3) L/min at 15.5-21.5
ug/min for 32 min; Armstrong (ref 20) arterial CL 6.2 +/- 2.7 L/min after 21 ug/min for 120 min; an Armstrong patient
whose Css rose > 35-fold (2.0 to > 70 ng/mL) for an 8-fold rate increase (21 to 160 ug/min).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None measured beyond one subject's hypotension at 40 ug/min.

## Covariates
None.

## Caveats
Six healthy subjects, venous sampling, 40-min steps (not steady state for the dinitrates); "steady-state" values were
"highly unsteady". The half-life is from the fall after the last (second 10 ug/min) step, i.e. after 160 min of drug.
