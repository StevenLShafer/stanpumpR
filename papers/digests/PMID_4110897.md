# Nicoloff 1972: T4 and T3 peripheral turnover kinetics in man

## Study
- 31 subjects in groups: 8 euthyroid, 6 primary hypothyroid (spontaneous adult thyroid failure), 7 thyrotoxic Graves', 3 hypothyroid after treated Graves', 3 factitial thyrotoxicosis, 4 idiopathic high TBG, 2 idiopathic low TBG (p.2-3, Table I). Sex is not printed per subject.
- Thyroid blocked with KI (plus methimazole in hyperthyroid). Pulse of 131I-T4 intravenously, then 2-4 days later a pulse of 40-100 uCi 125I-T3 intravenously. Serum drawn every 1-2 h for 24-36 h starting 16-20 h after the T3 pulse; urine collected about 2-hourly (p.2).
- Serum T3 and T4 separated from iodoproteins and iodide on a Dowex 1-2x anion column; results as % of injected dose per litre on semilog plots; single-exponential (one-compartment) calculation of fractional turnover, distribution space (DS), clearance and production rate by the method of Sterling and Chodos (p.3).
- Five subjects (2 controls, 2 hypothyroid, 1 hyperthyroid) also had a constant infusion of 125I-T3 for MCR (p.3).
- Serum T3 concentration by radioimmunoassay (total, unextracted serum; p.3).

## Model and parameters
One compartment for each hormone, fitted to the post-equilibration decline (T3 equilibration 22 h euthyroid, 33 h in primary hypothyroid; Table I last column).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None: no effect-site, ke0, EC50 or therapeutic range. The paper reports total serum T3 and T4 only as baseline levels. Not an opioid paper.

## Covariates
No fitted covariates. Groups compared by thyroid status and TBG: T3 clearance falls from 26.1 (euthyroid) to 18.5 L/day (primary hypothyroid) and rises to 60 L/day (thyrotoxic); T3 DS falls to 26.0 L with high TBG and rises to 64.5 L with low TBG (text p.7).

## Caveats
- Sampling started 16-20 h after the T3 pulse, so the first day (distribution and the 22-33 h equilibration) is not described; the single-compartment DS may overstate the true space by about 20% (authors' estimate, p.9), and MCR by constant infusion agreed in the 5 subjects.
- Small groups (6 primary hypothyroid); sex not given per subject; tracer doses, not therapeutic doses; steady state tracer kinetics in a state that changes with treatment.
- The three Graves' patients made hypothyroid by methimazole show near-normal T3 turnover (kT3 57%/day, clearance 24 L/day) unlike primary hypothyroid.
- Total hormone by RIA or iodine column; T3 values in older methods noted as artefact-prone by the authors.
