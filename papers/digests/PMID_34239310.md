# Li 2021: unbound vancomycin fraction in ICU and non-ICU patients (LC-MS/MS)

## Study
Retrospective, non-interventional, single centre (Affiliated Hospital of Qingdao University, China), archived TDM
plasma samples April 2018 - December 2019. 190 samples from 152 adults (95 ICU / 117 samples; 57 non-ICU / 73
samples), intermittent IV infusion; age median 57 (18-90) y, 101 M / 51 F, BMI median 24.21 kg/m2 (Table 1).
Albumin median 33.8 g/L (20.68-64.50); total bilirubin median 10.0 umol/L (2.50-258.06); 12 dialysis patients (all
non-ICU). Samples stored at 2-8 C until end of measurement day.
Free drug: Centrifree ultrafiltration (30 kDa cut-off), 600 uL plasma, 2000 x g at 37 C for 30 min; no pH control
reported. Total and ultrafiltrate vancomycin by LC-MS/MS (triazolam IS); LLOQ 0.3125 mg/L, ULOQ 20 mg/L; inter-/
intraday CV < 12.6%. fu = ultrafiltrate / total x 100. Validation cohort 13 ICU + 6 non-ICU patients.
Statistics: SPSS 19, MedCalc 20; Spearman correlation; multiple linear (mixed) regression.

## Model and parameters
No PK model. Binding results (Results text, p. 2546):
- Total vancomycin: mean 16.57 +/- 14.13 mg/L (median 12.44; range 1.79-104.50), all samples.
- Unbound vancomycin: mean 7.78 +/- 7.44 mg/L (median 5.54; range 0.11-49.44).
- Unbound fraction (all): mean 45.80% +/- 18.69% (median 46.01%; range 2.13-99.45%).
- ICU: 44.47% +/- 17.15% (median 45.19%; 2.13-92.07%); non-ICU: 47.94% +/- 20.88% (median 49.01%; 7.60-99.45%);
  ICU vs non-ICU P = 0.359 (Mann-Whitney).
- Spearman R unbound vs total: 0.860 all, 0.871 ICU, 0.866 non-ICU (Fig. 3).
- Prediction equations (Results p. 2547): ICU unbound = 0.395 x total + 0.019 x total bilirubin (umol/L) + 0.468
  (R2 0.771); non-ICU unbound = 0.526 x total - 0.527 (R2 0.749); all in mg/L.
- Table 2 standardized betas: total vancomycin 0.863 (all), 0.864 (ICU), 0.866 (non-ICU); BMI -0.102 (all);
  total bilirubin 0.110 (ICU).
- Validation: ICU R2 0.907, mean bias -0.051, precision 0.292; non-ICU R2 0.756, bias 0.114, precision 0.241.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD. The paper cites AUC/MIC >= 400 and older trough 15-20 mg/L targets as background only. Not an opioid.

## Covariates
Total vancomycin (dominant); total bilirubin in ICU patients; BMI in the pooled model (not in final equations).
Albumin, total protein, creatinine, cystatin C, dialysis, highly bound co-medications: no significant effect.

## Caveats
Retrospective, single centre, archived refrigerated samples; ultrafiltration without stated pH buffering (later
work, e.g. Dejaco 2026, attributes wide fu scatter to such methodological factors). Very wide fu range (2-99%) is
implausible biologically and suggests analytical imprecision. Small validation cohorts (13 and 6). Table 2 text
layout misaligns BMI and bilirubin rows (values read per Results text). IgA not measured.
