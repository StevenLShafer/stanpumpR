# Lauderdale 1986: in vitro activity of LY164846, with cephalexin as the S. aureus comparator

## Study
In vitro susceptibility study (Mayo Clinic). 492 clinical isolates (respiratory and dermal pathogens) tested
by agar dilution and disk diffusion against LY164846 (an investigational oral cephalosporin structurally like
cephalexin) and amoxicillin-clavulanic acid (Table 1, PDF p2, printed p561). Broth macrodilution (Taylor
method) in Mueller-Hinton broth for MBC, inoculum, pH (6.4-8.4) and serum (10, 50%) studies, using four
strains each of H. influenzae and S. aureus, including S. aureus ATCC 29213. MBC defined as the lowest
concentration giving 99.9% or more killing. Time-kill: LY164846 vs amoxicillin-clavulanate against
H. influenzae, and LY164846 vs **cephalexin** against S. aureus, at 1, 2 and 4 times the MIC, nominal inoculum
10^5 CFU/mL, sampled at 0, 2, 4, 8 and 24 h (p1-2).

Cephalexin was tested only in the S. aureus MBC attempts and time-kills; the 492-isolate MIC survey (Table 1)
has no cephalexin column.

## Model and parameters
No PK or PD model. Cephalexin-relevant results:

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: 99.9% (3 log10) kill at 24 h in time-kill tubes. No PK/PD index, no ke0, no concentration-time
link to serum levels of cephalexin. Not an opioid paper.

## Covariates
None (in vitro). Inoculum 10^4-10^6 CFU/mL, pH and serum had minimal effect on LY164846 (not tested for
cephalexin).

## Caveats
- Only two S. aureus strains in the cephalexin time-kills; cephalexin MBCs could not be read.
- The ATCC 29213 cephalexin MIC (8 µg/mL) is high relative to usual MSSA values; the time-kill multiples are
  per strain, so 4x MIC means 32 µg/mL for that strain and 4 µg/mL for strain 3.
- Sponsored by Eli Lilly (maker of LY164846 and the cephalexin supplied).

## Verification against stanpumpR
- `R/antibioticMbc.R:167-168`: "four times the MIC of cephalexin '99.9 to 100% bactericidal to S. aureus in
  24 h'" (Lauderdale 1986). The quoted phrase matches the abstract on the page image (p1); citation (journal,
  year, volume, pages) matches. **matches**.
- `R/antibioticMbc.R:168-169`: "MBC/MIC by class analogy, as neither source tested cefalexin". Inaccurate for
  this paper: Lauderdale did test cephalexin against S. aureus, both by broth MBC (uninterpretable: skipped
  tubes or paradoxical effect, p3) and by time-kill (Figs. 3-4). What is true is that no usable cefalexin
  MBC/MIC ratio came out of it. **discrepancy** (wording only).
- `R/antibioticMbc.R:156-157`: MBC90 = MIC x 2 (cephradine analogy; Shafer's choice of 2026-11-01). This
  paper's own time-kill evidence for cephalexin points the other way in one of its two strains: at 2x MIC
  strain 3 regrew by 24 h, and the authors state bactericidal activity for cephalexin only at 4x MIC, against
  2x for LY164846. Time-kill at 24 h is not the same end point as a tube MBC, and n = 2, so this is raised as
  a `[decision]` question rather than called an error.
- `R/antibioticMbc.R:161-162` says other series put the MSSA MIC90 at 8 to 16 mg/L; the ATCC 29213 MIC of 8
  µg/mL here is consistent with that note (one strain, not an MIC90).
- No cephalexin PK in this paper; `R/drugs_cefalexin.R` is not compared.
