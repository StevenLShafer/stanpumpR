# Vellinga 2025, SDC 4: measured versus target concentrations

Supplement to the main paper (card `PMID_40067039.md`). Not cited by the repo (context: no locations).

## Study
One figure (PDF p. 1), six panels of measured plasma concentration against TCI target (ng/mL, log axes), with the line of unity:
- A and B: remimazolam (black) and metabolite CNS7054 (grey) in session 2 (remifentanil 0.5 ng/mL), step-up and step-down.
- C and D: the same for session 3.
- E and F: remifentanil, sessions 2 and 3 combined, step-up and step-down; targets visible at 0.10, 0.50, 1.00, 2.00 and 4.00 ng/mL.
- Orange lines are the TCI targets; short horizontal lines are geometric mean measured concentrations.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No ke0, C50, MEAC or potency ratio.

Qualitative reading of the image (not quantified): measured remimazolam sits close to the line of unity for targets 100-2000 ng/mL in both phases; CNS7054 is well above remimazolam, rises with target in step-up, and plateaus around 5000-10000 ng/mL in step-down and in session 3 step-down, consistent with accumulation. Remifentanil points scatter around unity, more widely at 0.5-2.0 ng/mL. The numerical TCI error (median prediction error remimazolam +5.6% and +16%, remifentanil -8.1% and -11%) is in the main paper (see its card), not in this file.
