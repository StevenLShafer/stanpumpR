# Hong 2011: atazanavir population PK, adults and children, paediatric dose selection

## Study
Pooled population PK (Table 1, PDF p. 2): three adult HIV studies (AI424008, n = 13, ATV 400 mg QD;
AI424089, n = 27, ATV 400 mg QD or ATV/RTV 300/100 mg QD; AI424137, n = 11, ATV/RTV 300/100 mg QD) and the
paediatric PACTG1020 (n = 176, ATV 100-1,200 mg QD alone or 48-600 mg QD + RTV 26-100 mg QD, starting
310 mg/m2, titrated to exposure targets). Steady-state intensive sampling over 24 h. 227 patients,
3,939 concentrations (620 adult, 3,319 paediatric). Table 3 (PDF p. 2): weight mean 70.7 kg (49.7-97.1)
adults, 33.5 (2.6-122) children; age 36.3 y (22-64) adults, 9.17 (0.33-21) children; 104 female, 123 male;
117 with RTV; 64 on powder; 91 from Africa. Assay LC-MS/MS (plasma). NONMEM VI, FOCE; covariates by Wald's
approximation then forward/backward stepwise (cutoff dOFV 8.279 = ln 3,939).

## Model and parameters
One compartment, first-order absorption with lag, first-order elimination, oral (apparent) parameters,
"C0-delinked": the predose concentration C0 is a separate parameter decaying as C0 e^(-ke t) to absorb
nonadherence (eq. 1). Covariates: power model on continuous (reference 70 kg, 18 y), (1 + theta) on
categorical. Table 4 (PDF p. 4), mean +/- SE:

Elimination half-lives reported 5.3 h alone and 9.0 h with RTV (p. 5750; derived check: ln2/0.130 = 5.3 h,
5.3/0.591 = 9.0 h). The authors compare with Colombo 2006 (CL/F 12.9 L/h alone, 7.0 L/h with RTV,
V/F 88.3 L, ka 0.405 /h; half-lives 4.6 and 8.8 h): "higher" clearance and volume here are relative to that
study, the half-lives agree. Model evaluation by VPC (supplement) and posterior predictive check (Fig. 3).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD model. Targets (p. 5751): paediatric GM AUC within 80-125% of adult, GM Cmin >500 ng/mL (about 75%
of adult), GM Cmax <6,230 ng/mL (150% of adult). Efficacy anchor from adult exposure-response (refs 2, 21,
conference posters): Cmin >130 ng/mL gives >90% probability of HIV RNA <400 copies/mL and >75% of
<50 copies/mL at week 48. Safety: asymptomatic PR prolongation more frequent with Cmax >=7,000 ng/mL in
children (42% vs 14% of 101). Not an opioid paper: no MEAC, ke0 or potency ratio.

## Covariates
CL/F = 34.6 x (WT/70)^0.600 x (1 + 0.145 Africa) x (1 - 0.115 female) x (1 - 0.409 RTV) L/h;
V/F = 266 x (WT/70)^0.706 L; ka = 2.04 x (AGE/18)^-0.822 /h; Frel = (1 - 0.355 powder) x (1 + 1.32 RTV).
How ke relates to CL/F when the weight exponents differ (0.600 vs 0.706) is not stated; presumably ke = CL/V.
RTV dose (26-100 mg) explained no extra CL/F variation (dOFV 0.118).

## Caveats
- Apparent (oral) parameters only; no IV data. Steady-state data, C0 delinked to absorb nonadherence.
- Sponsor (Bristol-Myers Squibb) analysis; efficacy targets from posters.
- Few infants (one under 1 year in group 1); simulations restricted to >=15 kg.
- Supplement (VPC, Table S1) not read.
