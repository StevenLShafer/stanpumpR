# Shigetome 2025: paroxetine popPK and a MADRS improvement model driven by first-week AUC

## Study
- Retrospective, Japanese patients with MDD (DSM-IV), Hirosaki University Hospital and Dokkyo Medical University;
  drawn from an earlier cohort (Nishimura 2016). Antidepressant-naive, stable dose >= 9 days, no interacting drugs,
  normal renal/hepatic function; suspected non-adherence excluded.
- PK set: 179 patients (of 327), 329 steady-state concentrations, 1.8 +/- 0.99 per patient (1-5) (Table 1).
  Male 40.8%; age 41.5 +/- 14.2 y (16-80); weight 58.6 +/- 10.5 kg (40-85); dose 22.3 +/- 11.1 mg/day (10-40);
  plasma paroxetine 51.9 +/- 49.0 ng/mL (2.6-286.5); CYP2D6 IM 121, EM 58, PM 0.
- Dosing: Paxil 10-20 mg/day start, +10 mg/day weekly to the maximum tolerated, once daily. HPLC assay.
- PK/PD set: 50 patients with MADRS at weeks 0, 1, 2, 4, 6 and TCI personality scores (Table 2): age 46.6 y,
  weight 56.3 kg, dose 32.2 mg/day; MADRS 39.6 (baseline) to 13.7 (week 6).
- NONMEM 7.5.1, FOCE-I; machine learning (LR, DT, KNN, SVM, RF, MLP; SHAP) for remission (MADRS < 10) at week 6.

## Model and parameters
PK: one compartment **without an absorption process** and without saturable metabolism (best fit; trough data).
V/F fixed at 1010 L = 17.2 L/kg (Kaye 1989) x mean weight. Exponential IIV on CL, proportional residual error.
Sex dropped for collinearity with weight. CYP2D6 not significant.

PD model: EFF = Emax x Time / (ET50 + Time), EFF = % reduction of MADRS from baseline. Proportional IIV on Emax and
ET50; ET50 shrinkage slightly above 30%. Observed improvement 25.9, 45.7, 57.5, 64.2% at weeks 1, 2, 4, 6.
Remission prediction: ROC-AUC 0.973 (PK/PD model), best ML (SVM) 0.761.
Derived: t1/2 = ln2 x 1010/23.6 = 29.7 h at the reference covariates.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: MADRS improvement over 6 weeks (an Emax-in-time model, not a concentration-effect model). Exposure enters
as cumulative AUC over the first week; ET50 depends on the measured week-1 MADRS. No ke0, no EC50, no therapeutic
concentration proposed. Not an opioid.

## Covariates
PK: age and total body weight on CL/F. PD: AUC0-1week on Emax, MADRS at week 1 on ET50. TCI scores not retained.

## Caveats
Retrospective; n = 50 for PK/PD; sparse troughs, absorption omitted and V/F fixed; Japanese only; no CYP2D6 PM
or duplication detected; possible ML overfitting; AUC and time units of the PD equations are not stated in the
main text (supplementary code not in the PDF).
