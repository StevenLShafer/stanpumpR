# Chen 2025: ceftazidime-avibactam PopPK and PTA in CRKP patients

## Study
- Prospective single-centre PopPK study, Fujian Medical University Union Hospital, July 2021-Sept 2023 (PDF p. 2).
- 45 adults with carbapenem-resistant K. pneumoniae infection, 91 steady-state plasma samples (33 trough, 31 peak). Sparse sampling, 1-3 samples per patient, at least the 6th dose. Median age 59 y (18-94); median weight 62 kg (35-80); 36 M / 9 F. 15 on CRRT. Median Cockcroft-Gault CrCL is shown as 71.3 mL/min (Table 1; also the covariate reference value). 29 had CrCL < 90, 7 had CrCL >= 130 (ARC).
- Critically ill = APACHE II > 15 (25 patients); non-critically ill 20.
- Dosing: CAZ-AVI 1.25-2.5 g (4:1 ratio) as 2-h infusions, q8h to q24h by renal function (Table 1).
- Assay: LC-MS/MS, total plasma concentration (free fraction assumed 90% for CAZ and 92% for AVI in simulation).
- Software: Phoenix NLME 8.1, FOCE-ELS. Proportional residual error. Stepwise forward (OFV drop > 3.84) and backward (> 10.83) covariate selection.

CrCL lowered CL variability from 72.73% to 55.71% (CAZ) and from 82.40% to 66.69% (AVI) (p. 5). Bootstrap (1000) 95% CIs for CAZ CL 2.29-3.58, V 13.84-23 (Table 2).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Not an opioid paper. Antibacterial PK/PD targets used in Monte Carlo: non-critically ill 50% fT > MIC (CAZ) and 50% fT > 1 mg/L (AVI); critically ill 100% fT > 4 x MIC (CAZ) and 100% fT > 4 mg/L (AVI). MIC breakpoint 8 mg/L; PTA > 90% required for both agents (p. 4). No ke0, MEAC or potency ratio.

## Covariates
CrCL (Cockcroft-Gault) on CL, the only covariate retained. CRRT, APACHE II, albumin, liver tests and the other screened variables were not significant.

## Caveats
Authors give: single centre, n = 45, 64.4% renally impaired, no isolate MIC data, unexplored covariates, doses for stable renal function only, small CRRT subset (patients on CRRT had median CrCL 69.3 mL/min). Our additions: total concentrations only, no two-compartment fit (authors attribute this to sparse data); CL and V are absolute (no weight scaling). Compared with the authors' own cited healthy-volunteer values (CL 8.14 and 13.93 L/h, V 19.98 and 24.22 L) the CL is lower; their comparator is that volunteer study, not stanpumpR.
