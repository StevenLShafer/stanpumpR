# Levey 2009: the CKD-EPI creatinine equation

## Study
Cross-sectional pooled analysis. Development: 10 studies, 8254 people, split randomly into development (n 5504) and
internal validation (n 2750); external validation: 16 studies, 3896 people. Measured GFR = urinary iothalamate clearance
(development) and iothalamate or other markers (external). Serum creatinine recalibrated to standardized (Roche
enzymatic, IDMS-traceable) values at the Cleveland Clinic. Linear regression on log GFR with piecewise-linear splines
in log creatinine and age. Development cohort mean age 47 (SD 15) y; mean measured GFR 68 (40) mL/min/1.73 m^2,
range 2-190. Prevalence estimates in NHANES 1999-2006 (n 16,032, age ≥ 20). Adults only.

## Model and parameters
Single equation (Table 2 footnote), GFR in mL/min/1.73 m^2, Scr in mg/dL (standardized):
GFR = 141 × min(Scr/κ, 1)^α × max(Scr/κ, 1)^-1.209 × 0.993^Age × 1.018 [if female] × 1.159 [if black];
κ = 0.7 (F), 0.9 (M); α = -0.329 (F), -0.411 (M). Knots 62 µmol/L (0.7 mg/dL) women, 80 µmol/L (0.9 mg/dL) men.

Comparator MDRD Study equation as used here: 175 × Scr^-1.154 × age^-0.203 × 1.212 [black] × 0.742 [female] (IDMS form).
Performance (external validation, Table 3): median bias 2.5 vs 5.5 mL/min/1.73 m^2 (CKD-EPI vs MDRD); at eGFR ≥ 60:
3.5 vs 10.6; at < 60: 2.1 vs 3.4; P30 84.1 vs 80.6%. NHANES median eGFR 94.5 vs 85.0 mL/min/1.73 m^2.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Not applicable (renal function equation). No drug PD.

## Covariates
Standardized serum creatinine, age, sex, race. Weight, diabetes and transplant status did not improve performance.

## Caveats
Few participants > 70 y and few non-black minorities; developed in adults; creatinine must be IDMS-standardized;
imprecision remains (non-GFR determinants of creatinine, muscle mass). Indexed to 1.73 m^2.
