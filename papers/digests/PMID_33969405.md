# Dorn 2021: cefazolin and metronidazole in obese vs non-obese surgical patients (plasma and ISF)

## Study
Prospective, single-centre controlled PK study (Leipzig; EudraCT 2012-004383-22). 15 obese patients (bariatric
surgery; BMI 39.5-69.3; one class II, 14 class III) and 15 non-obese patients (elective laparoscopic/open abdominal
surgery; BMI 18.7-29.8), matched for sex and age (+/- 5 y). Table 1 (medians, layout scrambled; best reading):
obese (excluding one outlier) weight 155 kg (123-200), height 173 cm (156-185); non-obese weight 78 kg (50-96),
height 168 cm (160-192); eGFR (CKD-EPI) ~93-96 mL/min in both; albumin ~44 g/L.
Dose: 2 g cefazolin + 0.5 g metronidazole as a short IV infusion (median 25 min, 21-30), median 30 min before
incision. Plasma sampled predose and 0.5 (end of infusion), 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8 h; subcutaneous microdialysis
(upper arms) to 8 h, retrodialysis calibration. Assay HPLC-UV (total; free after ultrafiltration), QC imprecision/
inaccuracy within 4%. NCA in Phoenix WinNonlin 8; lambda-z from ~4-8 h.

**Metronidazole unbound fraction** (Methods, p. 2115): measured by ultrafiltration in the 0.5, 1, 4 and 8 h samples;
independent of concentration; 96.4% +/- 4.4% in 16 patients; the mean used for all free-concentration curves.
Spiked pooled healthy plasma QC: 96.6% +/- 2.8% at 10 mg/L, 98.1% +/- 2.4% at 2.0 mg/L.

Authors' caveat: 8 h sampling too short for terminal t1/2/AUC; extrapolated AUC8-inf averaged 54% of AUCinf.
Historical healthy-subject values quoted (from Lamp 1999): Vss 0.64-0.74 L/kg, t1/2 7.3-7.9 h, CL 3.9-5.0 L/h.

Cefazolin (Table 2, for completeness): non-obese total Cmax 174 +/- 31 mg/L, fu at Cmax 34.7% +/- 4.8%, fu at 8 h
20.0% +/- 4.4%; CL 4.63 +/- 0.94 L/h; Vss 14.2 +/- 2.3 L; concentration-dependent binding.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD model. ISF and free plasma concentrations compared with the EUCAST v11.0 (2021) MIC breakpoint for
Gram-negative anaerobes, 4 mg/L (used because no ECOFF exists); non-obese ISF stayed > 4 mg/L to 8 h, obese ISF
3-3.5 mg/L throughout. For cefazolin, E. coli ECOFF 4 mg/L and S. aureus ECOFF 2 mg/L.

## Covariates
Obesity (group comparison only): higher Vss, lower Cmax, no significant CL difference for metronidazole.

## Caveats
Short sampling (8 h) for a drug with t1/2 ~9-12 h; NCA only; variable surgery type/duration and vasopressor use in
controls; microdialysis probes remote from surgical site; ISF recovery lower in obese. fu measured in 16 of 30.
