# Sezaby label 2022: neonatal intravenous phenobarbital

## Study
Regulatory label (PLR format), initial US approval 2022, for neonatal seizures in term and preterm infants. Product:
100 mg phenobarbital sodium lyophilised powder per vial (equivalent to 91.35 mg phenobarbital; sodium salt MW
254.22), reconstituted in 10 mL saline to 10 mg/mL (2.2, 11). Not approved for adolescents or adults.
Efficacy trial (Study 1, NCT01720667; 14, p17): neonates under 14 days, gestational age 36-44 weeks,
randomised to phenobarbital (n = 42) or levetiracetam (n = 64); seizure termination over 24 h without a second
drug 73% vs 25% (efficacy set 33 vs 61; Table 5, p18).

## Model and parameters
No model and no PK parameters estimated in the label. Dosing (2.1, Table 1, p4): loading 20 mg/kg IV over 15
min; if seizures persist or recur at least 15 min after it, a second load of 20 mg/kg (term) or 10 or 20 mg/kg
(preterm); maximum total load 40 mg/kg; maintenance 4.5 mg/kg/day starting 8-12 h after the first load, as 1.5
mg/kg every 8 h or 2.25 mg/kg every 12 h, up to 5 days. Doses are of phenobarbital sodium.

Time is not stated as from which dose; n per column not given. Term-preterm difference after the first load "not
predicted" to be clinically significant (modelled, 12.3); metabolism mainly CYP2C9 (negligible at birth), less
CYP2C19, 2E1, UGTs (p16). Therapeutic hypothermia: no clinically significant PK difference (p16).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Exposure-response and the time course of response in neonates are unknown (12.2, p15). **No therapeutic or
target concentration range** anywhere in the label. QTc effect not adequately characterised. Not an opioid.

## Covariates
Gestational age (term vs preterm second load; modelled, no equation); sex, race, renal and hepatic impairment
unknown in neonates (p16).

## Caveats
Observed concentrations only, means without n, from one trial; the preterm statements are model predictions with
no model given. Doses as the sodium salt (100 mg salt = 91.35 mg acid).
