# Duan 2024: levetiracetam population PK with a pregnancy covariate (West China Hospital)

## Study
- Two prospective observational studies at West China Hospital, Chengdu (Study 1, women of childbearing age with epilepsy, ChiCTR2100046318; Study 2, plasma/saliva/hair concentrations, ChiCTR2000032089) (p. 1386).
- 238 patients, 322 concentrations (1.4 per person): 216 women (171 nonpregnant, 45 pregnant: 17 / 19 / 9 in trimesters 1 / 2 / 3, with 21 / 24 / 11 samples) and 22 men (Results p. 1387; Table 1, rendered PDF p. 4). Nonpregnant women: age 28.6 +/- 5.3 y, weight 57.2 +/- 9.5 kg, BMI 22.0; T1 51.6 +/- 10.6 kg; T2 61.8 +/- 9.1 kg; T3 63.5 +/- 9.1 kg. Daily dose: nonpregnant 1187.5 +/- 480.3 mg; T1 1000.0; T2 1361.1; T3 1272.7 mg. Monotherapy 44.0% (nonpregnant), 52.4 / 29.2 / 36.4% in T1 / T2 / T3.
- Trough samples before the morning dose after >= 7 days on a constant dose (EDTA plasma); UPLC-MS/MS with LEV-d6, range 0.1-50 ug/mL (printed "mg/mL", p. 1386).
- NONMEM 7.5, FOCE-I, PsN; one compartment, first-order absorption; ka fixed from Pigeolet 2007; proportional error. Forward P < 0.05, backward P < 0.001. Bootstrap 1000, pcVPC. Simulations of dose needed to hold the pre-pregnancy concentration across gestation, capped at 3000 mg/day.
- No postpartum samples.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No concentration-effect model. Seizures in the prior 3 months: 34 nonpregnant women, 5 / 6 / 2 in T1-T3, 8 men; most of them had levetiracetam below the "effective range" (not defined numerically in the main text). Simulation (Fig. 4): from a 1000 mg/day pre-pregnancy dose, 3-4 adjustments in pregnancy; 1500 mg/day, 5-6; example 50 kg woman: 1000-1250 mg/day in T1, 1500 mg/day at 19-28 weeks, 1750 mg/day after 28 weeks.

## Covariates
Pregnancy by trimester (categorical shift) and body weight (power) on CL/F. BMI significant but dropped for collinearity with weight. Not significant: age, height, daily dose, brand (original or generic), compliance, sex. Renal function was not tested as a covariate (serum creatinine collected).

## Caveats
- Authors: no postpartum samples; southwest Chinese population; only 20.83% of women pregnant; dosing simulations not clinically verified; V/F IIV poorly estimated.
- Small third-trimester group (9 women, 11 samples); sparse troughs (1.4 per person); pregnant and nonpregnant women are mostly different people (a between-subject comparison), with men included in the reference.
- Internal inconsistencies: CL/F 3.31 (Eq. 4) vs 3.43 (Table 2, text, abstract); ka 4.80 (text) vs 4.90 (Table 2); a bootstrap CI for the third-trimester factor (1.50-2.03) that barely contains its estimate, and a negative lower bound for the residual error.
