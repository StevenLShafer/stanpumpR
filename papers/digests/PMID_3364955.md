# Pelletier 1988: reproducibility of S. aureus MBCs for oxacillin, cephalothin and vancomycin

## Study
In-vitro microbiology; no patients, no pharmacokinetics. 41 fresh clinical methicillin-susceptible
S. aureus isolates (Wichita VA Medical Center), the control strain ATCC 25923, and 23 "reputedly tolerant"
strains from eight centres (including Evans and SPRMC 8502/C13). Tube macrodilution MIC and MBC by a
modification of the Taylor method: Ca/Mg-supplemented Mueller-Hinton broth, 0.5-mL inoculum to about
5 x 10^5 CFU/mL, clear tubes vortexed at 20 h, MIC read at 24 h, 0.1-mL subculture of clear tubes; MBC =
lowest concentration giving >99.9% kill (95% confidence limit per Pearson 1980); tubes re-incubated and both
read again at 48 h. All MBC tests in triplicate. Killing rates at 10 ug/mL over 0-8 h for four strains (Fig. 2).
Drugs: oxacillin, cephalothin, vancomycin. (Methods, PDF pp1-2, printed pp374-375.)

## Model and parameters
No PK. Microbiological results (all ug/mL = mg/L, protein-free broth):

ATCC 25923 control, repeated runs (Results, PDF p2, printed p375; text layer):
- Oxacillin (36 runs): 24-h median MBC 0.78, MIC 0.39; 92% of MBCs within one twofold dilution.
- Cephalothin (41 runs): 24-h median MBC 3.12, MIC 0.39, 49% of MBCs within one dilution of the median;
  48-h median MBC 0.39, MIC 0.39, 88% within one dilution.
- Vancomycin (48 runs): 24-h median MBC 50, MIC 1.56, 8% within one dilution; 48-h median MBC 3.12,
  MIC 1.56, 98% within one dilution.

30 (vancomycin); tolerant 21, 23, 19. Of the 41 fresh isolates only 36 (30 for vancomycin) appear in Table 1;
the paper does not say why.

Table 2 (PDF p3): within-run triplicate reproducibility, all >=80% within twofold; lowest 24-h vancomycin MBC
in nontolerant strains (80% within 2x, 85% within 4x).

Fig. 2 (PDF p4): killing rates at 10 ug/mL; oxacillin killed faster than cephalothin or vancomycin.
Only the Evans strain had MBC > 4 x MIC for both cephalothin and vancomycin at 48 h.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: >99.9% kill of the inoculum (MBC) in broth, at 24 h and 48 h; MIC = lowest clear tube at 24 h.
Not an opioid paper: no MEAC, ke0 or potency ratio. Authors' conclusion: S. aureus MBC almost always equals the
MIC, and MBC testing should not be a routine clinical laboratory test.

## Covariates
None (strain category tolerant vs nontolerant; incubation time 24 vs 48 h; Ca/Mg supplementation did not change
MBCs in three strains).

## Caveats
- Cephalothin, not cefazolin or cefalexin; methicillin-susceptible S. aureus only.
- Standard inoculum (5 x 10^5 CFU/mL) only; protein-free broth, so values are free drug.
- MIC/MBC distributions (MIC50/90) are not reported, only MBC/MIC ratio categories.
- Oxacillin carry-over at >=50 ug/mL; no beta-lactamase used to inactivate it.
- Cephalothin 24-h MBCs were poorly reproducible on the control strain (49% within a dilution of the median).

- Line 106-108 (cefazolin) and 167-169 (cefalexin): 36 nontolerant isolates, 64% <=2x, 83% <=4x at 48 h —
  Table 1, cephalothin 48 h, nontolerant: 23 (64), 30 (83), n = 36. **Matches** (ratio categories <= 2 and <= 4
  correctly read as "within twofold/fourfold"). "Fresh clinical" is right (the nontolerant arm is the fresh
  Wichita isolates), though the paper collected 41 and Table 1 shows 36. The citation (vol 32, pp374-377) matches.
- Cefazolin Note (lines 93-96), "cephalothin's tail is longer still": supported — at 48 h 17% of nontolerant
  isolates exceed 4 x MIC (all within 32 x); at 24 h 22% exceed 4 x and 8% exceed 32 x. MBC90 = MIC x 4 for
  cefazolin is therefore a floor relative to cephalothin, as the Note implies. **Matches.**
- Cefalexin MBC90 = MIC90 x 1 (line 157): the paper does not support x 1 for cephalothin at the 90th percentile
  (only 64% within 2x). The row's Basis rests on cephradine (Jones 1977) and the Note does not reconcile the two;
  raised as an open `[decision]`, not a transcription error.
- Header (lines 28-29) defines the MBC as 99.9% kill in 24 h; the figures quoted are the paper's 48-h values.
  The 24-h nontolerant cephalothin figures are 64% / 78% / 92%. Not a discrepancy in what is quoted, but the
  24-h tail is wider.
- No cephalothin or oxacillin model exists in the library (`drugDefaults_global.csv` has neither), so no
