# Simpson 2006: population PK of DHA after rectal artesunate in moderately severe malaria

## Study
- Pooled WHO/TDR Phase II (rich sampling) and Phase III (sparse) studies; n = 179 total (Table 1, PDF p 3,
  printed 2115): Phase II Bangkok adults n = 12 (16-50 y) and Ghana children n = 11 (2-7 y); Phase III Mae-Sot
  n = 44 (11 mo-15 y), Malawi n = 86 (16 mo-10 y), South Africa n = 26 (16-58 y).
- Moderately severe P. falciparum malaria (parasitaemia 0.1-27%), unable to take oral drugs, without features of
  severe malaria.
- Weight by site, mean (SD) [range] (Table 2): Bangkok 48.0 (7.06) [40-60]; Ghana 14.91 (3.61) [11-23]; Mae-Sot
  19.13 (9.30) [9.0-41.5]; Malawi 14.23 (3.73) [7.6-25.4]; South Africa 61.92 (10.0) [40-86] kg. Percent male 39-62.
- Dose: single intra-rectal artesunate (ARS), nominal 10 mg/kg as 50 mg and 200 mg suppositories (Mepha); actual
  mean dose by site 9.2-11.0 mg/kg (Table 2).
- Sampling over 24 h: Phase II at fixed times to 12 h; Mae-Sot and Malawi 3 randomised samples in 3 time blocks
  (later amended to 0.25-1.9, 2-5.9, 6-12 h); South Africa 1-8 h (Table 1).
- Assay: plasma ARS and DHA by HPLC with reductive electrochemical detection, single laboratory; minimum detectable
  8 ng/mL; LOQ 50 ng/mL (20 ng/mL at Mae-Sot); upper limit 3,200 ng/mL (PDF p 3). Concentrations below LOQ and
  above range were kept in the fit.
- Software: S-PLUS 6.2 NLME. Log-normal IIV, CV approximated by the square root of the variance.

## Model and parameters
- Artesunate itself: no model could be fitted (sparse, erratic data). Observed individual ARS peak, median (range)
  269 (11-4,720) ng/mL (PDF p 5, text layer).
- DHA: one-compartment, first-order appearance with lag, assuming complete ARS-to-DHA conversion; appearance rate
  constant and lag were **fixed**, not estimated. 424 DHA levels from 164 patients.
- Base model (abstract and PDF p 5): CL/F 2.64 L/kg/h (IIV 66%), V/F 2.75 L/kg (IIV 96%), t1/2 43 min.
  (derived: ln2 x 2.75 / 2.64 = 0.722 h = 43.3 min, consistent.)
- Final model, Table 5 (PDF p 8, printed 2120; confirmed on the rendered page):

| Parameter | Estimate (SE) |
|---|---|
| ka (/h), fixed | 0.2 |
| Increase in CL/F for a male (L/kg/h) | 1.14 (0.40) |
| CL/F male / female (L/kg/h) | 3.17 / 2.03 |
| Increase in V/F per unit (L/70 kg) | 5.77 (0.55) |
| Intercept (V/F) | 0.57 (1.68) |
| V/F (L/kg) at 15 / 30 / 60 / 70 kg | 1.81 / 3.04 / 5.52 / 6.34 |
| Lag time (h), fixed | 0.14 |
| IIV CL/F, V/F (%CV) | 62, 75 |
| Intra-individual (residual) variability (%CV) | 93 |

  The tabulated V/F values reproduce V/F (L/kg) = 0.57 + 5.77 x (WT/70) (derived: 0.57 + 5.77 x 15/70 = 1.81;
  x 30/70 = 3.04; x 60/70 = 5.52; x 70/70 = 6.34). Methods (PDF p 4) say weight entered allometrically,
  (WT/70) on V/F and (WT/70)^0.75 on CL/F, but the final CL/F in Table 5 carries no weight term; the paper does not
  reconcile this.
- Residual error log-normal; site and assay-limit covariates on residual error did not improve the fit.
- Population-predicted DHA Cmax at about 3 h (Discussion, PDF p 9). Because the fixed ka (0.2/h) is slower than
  elimination, the model is flip-flop: the apparent DHA half-life in plasma is set by the 0.2/h appearance, not by
  the 43-min elimination (authors say absorption is rate limiting).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Not an opioid paper. PD end points: treatment outcome to day 28, early rescue, PC60 (failure of parasitaemia to fall
40% by 12 h), PCT50 and PCT90. No concentration-effect model, EC50 or target concentration. Posterior CL/F and
AUC0-6h were not associated with any end point; higher V/F was associated with early rescue after adjustment
(p = 0.01; geometric mean 3.42 vs 2.69 L/kg, PDF p 8), which the authors note could equally mean lower F. ARS and DHA
are stated to have approximately equal antimalarial activity (PDF p 8).

## Covariates
Sex on CL/F (additive, +1.14 L/kg/h for males) and weight on V/F (linear, above). Age, region (SE Asia vs Africa),
suppository number, parasitaemia, lactate, glucose, PCV not retained. Adding sex and weight reduced IIV of CL/F
66% to 62% and of V/F 96% to 75%.

## Caveats
- F unknown: all parameters are apparent (/F), and ka and lag were fixed, so V/F also absorbs any variation in
  absorption rate (authors' own caveat, PDF p 9-10).
- Dose basis for DHA (ARS mg vs DHA-equivalent) not stated; see open question.
- Very high residual variability (93%) and IIV; sparse sampling in Phase III; a one-compartment DHA model may reflect
  sampling density (authors cite two-compartment DHA after parenteral ARS).
- Per-kg V/F rising steeply with weight (1.8 L/kg at 15 kg to 6.3 L/kg at 70 kg) is an empirical linear fit over
  7.6-86 kg; outliers excluded (1 ARS, 3 DHA levels).
- Patients with moderately severe malaria, not healthy volunteers and not severe malaria.
