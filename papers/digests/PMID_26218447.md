# Niebecker 2015: edoxaban popPK, 13 phase 1 studies pooled with Hokusai-VTE

## Study
13 phase 1 studies (443 healthy subjects; renal impairment study 1 at 15 mg, the rest 60 mg; P-gp inhibitor DDI
studies 2-6, 8-13; study 6 the dronedarone DDI study, the only one run fed; Table 1, PDF p3-4) pooled with Hokusai-VTE
(NCT00986154; 9531 sparse samples from 3707 VTE patients; pre-dose and 1-3 h at month 3, any time at month 12).
Final data 17 406 concentrations from 4130 individuals; 777 below LLOQ excluded (PDF p6). Table 2 (PDF p8, rendered):
Hokusai age median 57.0 (mean 55.6; 10th-90th 32.6-76.0) y, weight 80.5 (82.9; 60-108) kg, CLcr 99.0 (103;
57.5-151) mL/min, 58.3% male, 20.1% Asian; phase 1 age 30.0 (32.2) y, weight 79.3 kg, CLcr 131 (130; min-max
14.1-247), 80.4% male. Protocol dose 60 mg once daily, halved to 30 mg for weight <= 60 kg, Cockcroft-Gault CLcr
30-50 mL/min or selected P-gp inhibitors. LC-MS/MS, LLOQ 0.764 ng/mL (1 ng/mL in the renal study). NONMEM 7.2,
FOCE-I, log-transformed data.

## Model and parameters
Linear two-compartment, first-order absorption with lag. Equations 2-5 (PDF p7, rendered): CLnr/F = theta1 x
(WT/70)^(3/4); CLr/F = theta_slope1 x CLcr; if CLcr > 90, CLr/F = (theta_slope1 - theta_slope2) x 90 + theta_slope2 x
CLcr; CL/F = CLnr/F + CLr/F. CLcr (Cockcroft-Gault, mL/min) truncated at 150 (PDF p5). The Results (PDF p10) say
weight was included on CLnr/F, Q/F, Vc/F and Vp/F; CLcr on CLr/F. Exponents are fixed but are printed only in the
Table 3 footnote, which writes CL/F and Vp/F with (WT/70)^(3/4) and Vc/F and Q/F with (WT/70)^1. Phase 3 (patients):
slope 2 x (1 + 2.74), stated as 0.0321 in phase 1 and 0.120 in patients in the Discussion (PDF p12); Q/F x (1 +
0.646). Asians Vc/F x 1.226. Table 3 (PDF p9, rendered), final model:

Initial phase 1 model (Table 3): CLnr/F 17.1, Vc/F 213, Vp/F 91.1, Q/F 5.28, ka 3.39, slopes 0.155 and 0.0569;
individual P-gp inhibitors raised CL 16.5-52.7% and F 66.7-181%. Full model adds phase 3 P-gp on F -11.5% (text:
CL/F 11.5% decreased; PDF p10). Phase 3 data raised CL/F 15.8% (PDF p7).

Supplement (supplement, 2026-10-27): four files, all figures, no parameter table, no equations, no control stream.
sup0004.docx holds the three legends. Supplemental Figure 1 (sup0001.tiff) is a prediction-corrected VPC of the model
built on phase 1 data only, one panel per phase 1 arm (study 1 by CLcr group <= 30, 30-50, 50-80, > 80 mL/min; study 6
-/+ dronedarone; studies 2-5 -/+ ketoconazole, quinidine, verapamil, erythromycin; studies 7-13) and a bottom row by
weight (<= 67, 67-77, 77-85, > 85 kg); log concentration against time after dose (h). Supplemental Figures 2 (sup0002)
and 3 (sup0003) are pcVPCs of the FINAL model against CLcr (mL/min) and weight (kg), pooled over all sampling times:
observed 5th/50th/95th percentiles (dashed/solid) against the 95% CIs of the simulated percentiles (shaded). Read off
the images (approximate): against CLcr the observed median rises from about 36 ng/mL at about 20 mL/min to about 53 at
40 and about 92 at about 205 mL/min, a smooth line with no visible change of slope at 90 or at 150; from 40 mL/min up
it runs just above the simulated-median band. The CLcr axis runs to about 205 mL/min, past the model's truncation at
150. Against weight the observed median lies on the simulated band from about 48 to 98 kg and above it at the last
bin (about 51 vs about 44-47 ng/mL at about 148 kg); the observed 5th and 95th percentiles lie at or below the
simulated bands across most of both covariate ranges.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None: no anti-Xa or clotting model. Exposure end points (Figure 4, PDF p11, read from the box plots, approximate):
non-dose-reduced 60 mg median Cmax about 210 ng/mL, Cmin about 15, Css,av about 67 (IQR about 60-76); dose-reduced
groups lower. Figure 3 (PDF p10): the average patient's steady-state profile peaks near 220 ng/mL at about 1 h.

## Covariates
Weight (allometric, fixed exponents), Cockcroft-Gault CLcr (piece-wise, cap 150), study phase (slope 2, Q/F), Asian
race (Vc/F); P-gp inhibitors in the full model only. Age, sex and other races dropped on clinical-significance
criteria (>= 20% change).

## Caveats
High eta-shrinkage (23-59% IIV, up to 79% IOV); absorption informed by phase 1 only; IOV fixed for phase 3. Equations
2 and 3 both call their coefficient theta1. The footnote's weight-exponent pairing (Vp/F 0.75, Q/F 1) is the reverse
of the usual convention and is not restated in the text; it is the only statement of the exponents (unresolved).
The Table 3 slope-2 footnote multiplies by the scaling parameter without "(1 + ...)"; the Discussion's 0.120 shows
the factor is 3.74.
