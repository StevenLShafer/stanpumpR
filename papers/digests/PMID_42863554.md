# Maisonneuve 2026: idarubicin and idarubicinol joint population PK in AML

## Study
- 27 adults (13 F, 14 M) with AML in the BIG-1 phase II/III trial, Angers, 2018-2022. Median age 49 y (21-61), median BSA 1.80 m2 (1.33-2.13), median dose 16.3 mg (12.0-19.7) (Table 1, p3).
- Idarubicin 9 mg/m2/day IV over 30 min for 5 days with cytarabine 200 mg/m2/day; PK sampled day 1 only: pre-dose and 5 min, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 1, 2, 4, 6, 12, 24 h after the end of infusion (p2). 461 concentrations, 8 idarubicin and 1 idarubicinol below LLOQ.
- Plasma (lithium heparin), HPLC with fluorescence detection; range 1-50 ng/mL, LLOQ 1 ng/mL (p2). Total plasma concentration.
- NCA with PKanalix; population model with Monolix 2024R1 (SAEM), BICc selection, exponential IIV, proportional error, COSSAC covariate search, bootstrap n = 200, PFIM sampling-design optimisation (p2-3).
- NCA (Table 2, p4): idarubicin AUC0-tlast median 70.47 ng*h/mL (35.92-107.97), Cmax 23.33 ng/mL (9.25-41.30); idarubicinol AUC 95.73 (36.71-218.85), Cmax 5.21 (2.34-13.18); AUC parent/metabolite ratio 0.73 (0.43-1.22). (The text layer interleaves the columns; the pairing follows the row order, AUC row then Cmax row.)

## Model and parameters
Idarubicin: three compartments (V1; V2 via Q1; V3 via Q2). Idarubicinol: two compartments (V4 = V1, assumed; V5 via Q3). Parent eliminated only by conversion: first-order kp1m from V1 to V4 and kp3m from V3 to V5. Metabolite eliminated from V4 at k40, FIXED at 0.02 /h by a grid search (0.0005-0.50 /h) because no samples after 24 h (p4, p6-7). Proportional error. IIV on kp1m, V1, V2, V3, Q1, Q2, V5 with correlation between kp1m and V1 retained; no IIV on Q3 or kp3m (omega below 15%). Covariates (PDF p5 page image): ln(V3i) = ln(817.4) + 2.08 ln(BSA/1.76); ln(V5i) = ln(2155.7) - 0.3 ln(lymphocyte count/1.43) (Giga/L). Final model, Table 3 (p5, page image):

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD end point. Idarubicinol is described as active in vitro (cytotoxicity comparable to the parent, from the introduction, citing other papers); no concentration-effect relation, ke0 or effect-site model is estimated. Literature half-lives quoted: idarubicin terminal about 16 h; idarubicinol about 50 h (30-80 h) (p1-2). Not an opioid paper: no MEAC, ke0, tPeak or potency ratio.

## Covariates
BSA on V3 (exponent 2.08) and baseline lymphocyte count on V5 (exponent -0.3), both labelled exploratory and needing confirmation (abstract, p7). NCA regression r2 0.325-0.568 for various covariates; no covariate explained idarubicinol AUC (p4).

## Caveats
- n = 27, day 1 only, 24 h sampling, so idarubicinol terminal elimination is not identified; k40 was fixed and the authors say exposure extrapolated to infinity is not established (p6-7).
- Metabolite compartment V4 = V1 is an assumption; the model forms about half of the metabolite in the peripheral compartment (kp3m x V3 transfer).
- Concentrations are total plasma; the data are confidential (data availability statement).
- Single-group optimal sampling schedule: 0.58, 1.5, 4, 9, 24 h after start of infusion (p5).
