# Léger 2004: population PK of oral and IV topotecan, with covariates

## Study
- Pooled population analysis, 190 patients (Table 1, PDF p.2): 72 had topotecan as a 30-min IV infusion and 118 orally (gelatine capsules). Five clinical trials (173 patients) plus 17 patients on therapeutic drug monitoring (Table 2).
- Age mean 55 (18-76) y; body weight mean 70 (42-117) kg; BSA mean 1.80 (1.36-2.44) m2 (Dubois); serum creatinine mean 87 (41-162) umol/L; Cockcroft-Gault CrCl mean 80 (33-167) mL/min. WHO performance status 0/1/2/3 in 79/98/11/2 patients. Mostly ovarian cancer and other solid tumours; some combined with cisplatin.
- Doses (Table 2): IV 0.15-2.7 mg/m2/day for 5-13 days; oral 0.2-1.5 mg/m2/day (the oral rows are scrambled in the text layer; the p.1 text gives 0.15-2.7 mg/m2/day oral, 5-21 days).
- 2064 plasma samples (2-21 per patient in cycle 1, median 15; 2-18 in cycle 2, median 7). Total topotecan (lactone plus carboxylate) in plasma by HPLC; LLOQ 0.5 ng/mL (Toulouse) and 0.1 ng/mL (Rotterdam).
- NONMEM V level 1.1, FOCE with interaction, PREDPP. 17 covariates tested. Interoccasion variability between cycles 1 and 2. A limited-sampling strategy was built on the oral data (15 training and 15 validation patients).

## Model and parameters
Two-compartment, linear elimination from the central compartment; oral data with first-order absorption and a lag time. Pooled IV and oral data. Table 3 (PDF p.4, printed p.346), cycle 1, mean (95% CI half-width) and %CV:

| Parameter | Value | %CV |
|---|---|---|
| CL (L/h) = (th1 + th2 x CrCl) x (1 - th3 x PS) | th1 12.8 (4.8); th2 2.1 (1.0); th3 0.12 (0.09) | IIV 29; interday 18 |
| V1 (L) = th4 x body weight (kg) | th4 0.58 (0.13) | IIV 39; interday 49 |
| V2 (L) | 45.5 (7.0) | 46 |
| Q (L/h) | 49.2 (16.9) | 80 |
| F (%) | 32.4 (3.9) | IIV 22; interday 28 |
| Ka (1/h) | 1.7 (0.6) | 64 |
| Lag time (h) | 0.17 (0.03) | 13 |

- CrCl is Cockcroft-Gault in **L/h** (Table 3 footnote a); PS is WHO performance status. Absolute (not per-kg) CL.
- Alternative covariate models (Table 3, change in objective function against the final model): CL = th1 gives th1 20.1 (+62); CL = th1 + th2 x CrCl gives 11.8 and 1.90 (+20); CL = th1 x (1 - th3 x PS) gives 21.8 and 0.11 (+47); V1 = th4 alone gives 35.2 L (+27). All P < 0.001.
- Residual error (p.3): combined proportional plus additive, separate for IV (11% and 0.64 ug/L) and oral (17% and 0.09 ug/L) data.
- Intercycle variability (p.3): CL 18%, V1 31%, F 22%. Fit to both cycles with updated covariates: CL = (11.4 + 2.5 x CrCl) x (1 - 0.06 x PS) and V1 = 0.50 x weight (p.3 text; no table).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No effect-site model, no ke0, no concentration target. Exposure (AUC) is described only as related to neutropenia, citing other work (p.5). Not an opioid paper; no MEAC, potency or hysteresis to record.

## Covariates
- Significant: Cockcroft-Gault CrCl and WHO PS on CL; body weight on V1 (BSA also correlated with V1 but more weakly, p.3).
- Not significant: age, albumin, bilirubin, ALT, AST, creatinine, sex, cisplatin co-treatment, and others (p.3, p.5). No liver test correlated with F.
- The authors read th1 12.8 L/h as non-renal CL. They read th2 = 2.1 as renal elimination exceeding GFR because of tubular secretion (p.5).

## Caveats
- Pooled IV and oral data; the oral half (118 patients) carries F, Ka and lag time, but CL, V1, V2 and Q are shared across routes.
- Different HPLC labs for IV (Toulouse) and oral (Rotterdam) data; the additive error differs by route, which the authors partly attribute to the LLOQ difference.
- Limited-sampling validation had only 15 patients. Table 4: 1.5/4/6 h gives bias +2.3% and rmse 9.3%; 1.5/6 h gives +2.2% and 10.0%. Range of prediction error -17.0 to +45.2% and -17.0 to +45.3%.
- The authors' final CL equation depends on PS, which is subjective and was not significant in two earlier smaller analyses (p.5).

## Verification against stanpumpR
The paper is not cited, so no transcription check applies. As an independent check on `R/drugs_topotecan.R` (Mould 2002 values: non-renal CL 5.77 L/h, renal 12.8 L/h x RF, V1 19.3 L, V2 45.7 L, Q 90.4 L/h, all at 70 kg, lines 74-82):
- **CL at a comparable patient.** The library's own header gives 18.57 L/h at 70 kg, CLCR 70 mL/min, ECOG 1 (lines 37-38). Léger at CrCl 70 mL/min (4.2 L/h) and PS 1 gives (12.8 + 2.1 x 4.2) x 0.88 = 19.0 L/h (derived). Agrees within about 2%. The two PS scales (WHO vs ECOG) are not necessarily the same, so this is only an approximate check.
- **Non-renal intercept** differs by design (12.8 L/h here against 5.77 L/h in Mould), because the renal slopes differ too (here 2.1 per L/h CrCl; Mould's 12.8 per unit RF). The two parameterisations only compare as a whole.
- **V2** 45.5 L here against 45.7 L (Mould): equal. **V1** 40.6 L at 70 kg (derived, 0.58 x 70) against 19.3 L: about twice as large (see open questions). **Q** 49.2 L/h against 90.4 L/h at 70 kg: lower here.
- Léger is a pooled IV plus oral fit; the library applies Mould IV-only to IV doses, which is the cleaner choice. The library has no oral topotecan model; F 32.4% and Ka 1.7/h are available here if oral dosing is ever wanted.
