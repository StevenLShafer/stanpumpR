# Chunilal 2002: in vitro APTT and anti-Xa response of third-trimester plasma to UFH

## Study
Cross-sectional in vitro study, McMaster University, Hamilton (PDF pp1-3, printed 92-94). Healthy pregnant
women at 32-36 weeks: 15 enrolled, 13 analysed; non-pregnant female hospital employees: 16 enrolled, 15
analysed (3 samples mis-handled and excluded); 8 of 16 controls on combined oral contraceptives. Age 30 ± 4.5
vs 30 ± 4.3 y (Table 1). Citrated platelet-poor plasma stored at −70 °C was spiked with UFH (Hepalean, one vial,
10 U/mL stock) to 0, 0.2, 0.4, 0.6, 0.8 U/mL. APTT: Thrombosil I on ACL 300. Anti-factor Xa: chromogenic
(Stachrom Heparin), in duplicate. Non-specific binding: anti-Xa before and after low-affinity heparin (LAH) to
displace protein-bound UFH (46 µg/mL in methods; 45 µg/mL in the Table 2 legend). Fibrinogen (Clauss), FVIII
(one-stage), vWF Ag (ELISA). Student's t-test, Pearson correlation, stepwise multiple regression (SPSS 8).
No subject received heparin: there are no in vivo PK data.

## Model and parameters
No PK model or PK parameters. Results (page images, PDF pp3-4):

Table 1 (printed p94), mean (SD): fibrinogen 5.1 (0.8) vs 2.8 (0.45) g/L; vWF Ag 2.7 (0.48) vs 1.1 (0.66) U/mL;
FVIII 2.7 (0.57) vs 1.2 (0.52) U/mL; all P < 0.001 (pregnant vs non-pregnant).

Table 2 (printed p95), UFH 0.4 U/mL added: measured anti-Xa 0.37 (0.08) vs 0.50 (0.07) U/mL; after LAH 0.72 (0.05)
vs 0.77 (0.05) U/mL; non-specific binding 47 (12.1)% vs 35 (7.8)%; P < 0.01.

Slopes (log APTT vs added heparin): 0.76 ± 0.18 vs 1.12 ± 0.09 s/U/mL, P = 0.005 (results text and Fig. 1;
the abstract says 1.2 for non-pregnant). Measured vs expected anti-Xa: 1.05 vs 1.23 (Fig. 2). Log APTT vs measured
anti-Xa: 0.72 vs 0.92 in the text, 0.73 vs 0.92 in the Fig. 3 legend. Table 3 Pearson r with the log APTT-heparin
slope: FVIII 0.7, fibrinogen 0.7, vWF 0.62, % non-specific binding 0.72 (footnote reads "p <0.5", presumably
p < 0.05). Stepwise regression: fibrinogen, FVIII and non-specific binding, adjusted R² 0.676; 0.68 without FVIII.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End points: APTT (s) and chromogenic anti-factor Xa activity (U/mL) in spiked plasma. Therapeutic ranges are
quoted from earlier work, not measured here: heparin 0.2-0.4 U/mL by protamine titration or 0.3-0.7 U/mL anti-Xa
(p92, ref 3); the authors suggest an anti-Xa target of 0.35-0.7 U/mL in pregnancy and 0.5-1.0 U/mL for LMWH
(p96). No ke0, EC50 or time course. Not an opioid paper.

## Covariates
Pregnancy (third trimester); fibrinogen, FVIII, vWF Ag and non-specific binding as predictors of the APTT slope.

## Caveats
- In vitro only: no endothelial binding or clearance; the authors note it cannot replicate the ex vivo situation.
- Small groups (13 vs 15); half the controls on oral contraceptives (the authors report the difference holds
  against either subgroup, P = 0.006).
- Internal inconsistencies: non-pregnant slope 1.2 (abstract) vs 1.12 (results); Fig. 3 slope 0.72 vs 0.73; LAH
  46 vs 45 µg/mL; Table 3 footnote "p <0.5".
- Measured anti-Xa in non-pregnant plasma (0.50) exceeded the 0.4 U/mL added; the authors attribute this to
  differential binding.
- The comparator for "lower" APTT response and anti-Xa is healthy non-pregnant women's plasma in this study.
