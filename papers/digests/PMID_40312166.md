# Eleveld 2025: remimazolam pooled PK-PD (MOAA/S, BIS) with age, sex, opioid and hepatic covariates

## Study
- Pooled analysis of 20 studies (PAION and UMCG data; list in Supplementary Table S1), 1930 sessions.
- PK model: 933 individuals (568 M / 365 F), age 6-93 y, weight 21-171 kg; 7633 remimazolam observations
  (3737 arterial, 3896 venous). CNS7054 (inactive metabolite): 905 individuals, 7420 observations.
- Three studies (ECMO, ICU: CNS7056-010, ONO-2745-04, REHSCU_ICU) excluded from model building, analysed post hoc.
- Concentrations in the first 2 min (remimazolam) and 3 min (CNS7054) after dosing excluded.
- MOAA/S: 12,670 observations, 449 individuals; BIS: 118,833 observations, 613 individuals (10-s median
  filtered, 15-s processing delay assumed).
- NONMEM 7.5, FOCE-I (LAPLACE for MOAA/S); sequential PK -> metabolite -> PD; covariate entry needed
  dAIC < -13.8. Reference individual 70 kg, 35 y male, no opioids, normal hepatic/renal function.
- Venous samples modelled as a 13.2% / 86.8% mix of a first-order delayed compartment and central (Fven).
- **Supplementary Table S1 (supplement, 2026-10-27)**: the 20 studies with per-study counts. Columns are
  "Individuals: All data" then "Sessions" for Remimazolam, CNS7054, MOAA/S, BIS. The session columns sum to
  the text's counts: remimazolam 933, CNS7054 905, BIS 613 (derived). The MOAA/S column sums to 447 (derived),
  against 449 in the text (p. 4); a 2-session internal difference, not used by the repo. CNS7056-025
  (Vellinga 2024) contributes 72 sessions from 24 individuals, so the paper's "individuals" for PK are
  session counts. The three post hoc studies (CNS7056-010 ECMO 62, ONO-2745-04 ICU 49, REHSCU_ICU 30) carry
  no sessions in the development columns.
- Supplementary Figure S1: 933 individuals, 568 male / 365 female, age 6-93 y, weight 20.5-171 kg.

## Model and parameters
Three-compartment mammillary remimazolam; metabolite (100% formed) via depot -> two-compartment CNS7054.
Equations (p. 209-210): Fsize = WGT/70; V1 = V1ref Fsize; V2 = V2ref Fsize; V3 = V3ref Fsize FV3,age FV3,sex
FV3,Pugh; CL = CLref Fsize^0.75 FCL,sex FCL,opiates; Q2 = Q2ref (V2/V2ref)^0.75; Q3 = Q3ref (V3/V3ref)^0.75.
F_age = exp(K/1000 x (AGE-35)); F_sex = exp(K/100) if female; F_opiates = exp(K/100) if present;
F_Pugh = exp(K/100) if Pugh-Child > 8. Table 1 (estimate, 99% profile CI, IIV omega^2, CV%):

**NONMEM control stream (supplement, 2026-10-27).** The supplement prints the $PK, $DES, $THETA and $ERR
blocks of the final combined model (all THETAs FIXED, i.e. the sequential estimates carried into one stream).
What it settles:
- Remimazolam PK lines: `VSIZ=WGT/70`, `CSIZ=(WGT/70)**0.75`; `V1=VSIZ*EXP(THETA(1)+ETA(1))`,
  `V2=VSIZ*EXP(THETA(2)+ETA(2))`, `V3=VSIZ*EXP(THETA(3)+ETA(3)+KAV3+KSV3+KHV3)`,
  `CL=CSIZ*EXP(THETA(4)+ETA(4)*ETCLV+KSCL+KOCL)`, `Q2=((V2/EXP(THETA(2)))**0.75)*EXP(THETA(5)+ETA(5))`,
  `Q3=((V3/EXP(THETA(3)))**0.75)*EXP(THETA(6)+ETA(6))`. So Q2 and Q3 follow the individual V2 and V3
  (size, age, sex and Pugh terms and the ETAs included) to the 0.75 power.
- Covariate terms: `KAV3=THETA(11)/1000*(AGE-35)`, `KSCL=THETA(12)/100*(M1F2-1)`,
  `KSV3=THETA(13)/100*(M1F2-1)`, `KOCL=THETA(14)/100*(OA1P2-1)`, `KHV3=THETA(15)/100*(P1GT8-1)`; flags coded
  M1F2 1 male / 2 female, OA1P2 1 no opioid / 2 opioid, P1GT8 1 normal / 2 Pugh-Child > 8, H1ESRD2 1 normal /
  2 ESRD. The printed header sets OA1P2=2 (opioids present), P1GT8=1, H1ESRD2=1, AVFLAG=3 (arterial and
  venous) as example values.
- No caps, floors or truncations on weight or age anywhere in the stream; no maturation term.
- Venous variance: `IF (AVFLAG.EQ.2) ETCLV=THETA(16)` (1.86, multiplies ETA(4) for venous-only subjects).
- Venous delay `VKE0=EXP(THETA(10))*((WGT/70)**(-0.25))`: Kdelay 0.0144 /min carries a weight^-0.25 term
  that the main text's equations do not show. Venous prediction `VPRED = VAFRA*A(4) + (1-VAFRA)*APRED` with
  `VAFRA=EXP(THETA(9))` = 0.132 (the stream's comment on that line calls it "arterial error"; it is the
  venous fraction, as the THETA(9) comment "venous log(fraction)" says).
- Metabolite: `MRAT=425.3/439.3` (molecular-weight correction, 100% conversion) on the flux `A(1)*K10`;
  `VSCA=(WGT/70)**THETA(27)` (Kscale 0.518) on V1M and V2M, `VSCA**0.75` on CLM, `VSCA**(-0.25)` on the
  depot rate; renal `KRCL=THETA(26)/100*(H1ESRD2-1)` (ESRD CLM x exp(-2.183) = 0.113), opioids
  `KMOCL=THETA(28)/100` (x0.72), hepatic on Q2M `THETA(29)/100` (x6.57), age on Q2M `THETA(30)/1000*(AGE-35)`.
- Effect sites: `DADT(9) = MKE0*(A(1)/V1 - A(9))` with `MKE0=EXP(THETA(32))` (no size or age term);
  `DADT(10) = BKE0*(A(1)/V1 - A(10))` with BIS ke0 carrying an age term THETA(47)/1000*(AGE-35). Both are
  driven by the ARTERIAL (central) concentration. Effect-site amounts are divided by 1000 before the PD
  model (CEFFM=A(9)/1000), so Ce50 values are in ug/mL (mg/L) when the plasma prediction is in ng/mL.
- THETA values and their exponentials (derived) agree with Table 1 and with the p. 211 simplified TCI
  equations to every printed digit.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Table 1 PD block is scrambled in the text layer; the values below were re-aligned by matching each estimate
to its CI. **Confirmed (supplement, 2026-10-27)** against the $THETA comments and exponentials of the control
stream: every value below matches.
- MOAA/S (proportional odds, competitive CNS7054 interaction): ke0 0.298 /min (0.290-0.310), no age effect on
  ke0 (theoretical scaling exponent removed); Ce50ref 0.182 ug/mL (0.151-0.221); Ca50 11.9 ug/mL (10.0-14.3);
  KCe50~age -7.63 (per 1000/yr); DEFF 19.9; b0 -16.6; opioids shift score 1 -> 0 (Kd01~opiates 1.67).
  (Units print as "mg ml-1" in the text layer, but the figures are in ug/mL; ug/mL is meant.)
  Supplement $ERR: `PEFFM=DEFF*(CEFFM/PE50)/(1 + CEFFM/PE50 + CMET/ME50M)` added to the logits; the predicted
  MOAA/S is the probability-weighted score.
- BIS (sigmoid Emax, gamma not estimated): ke0ref 0.145 /min (0.128-0.164), Ce50ref 0.982 ug/mL (0.909-1.06),
  baseline 93.7, Ca50 8.41 ug/mL, KCe50~age -16.4, Kke0~age -10.6 (both per 1000/yr). Supplement $ERR:
  `BPRED = BASE - BASE*PEFFB`, PEFFB = (Ce/BE50)/(1 + Ce/BE50 + Cmet/ME50B), i.e. gamma 1 and Emax 100% of
  baseline; additive error SD 6.78.
- Time to peak effect about 2.5 min (Discussion; Supplementary Fig S20), compared with 3.0 min (Rex),
  1-4 min (Antonik), 2.6 min (Masui and Hagihira).
  - **Supplementary Figure S20 (supplement, 2026-10-27)**: individual predicted times to peak MOAA/S effect
    against age, all between about 2.1 and 2.8 min, the bulk 2.2-2.6 min, slightly lower at the age
    extremes. No summary statistic is printed on the figure.
  - Derived (this card, RK4 integration of a bolus through the three-compartment model and the MOAA/S effect
    site, typical values from the $THETA block, 70 kg 35 y man): **tPeak 2.39 min** with ke0 0.298269 /min
    (2.39 also with the rounded Table 1 values). The same calculation with the BIS ke0 0.145 /min gives 3.48
    min. For an 80-y 70 kg woman (V3 and CL covariates applied) the MOAA/S tPeak is 2.20 min.
- Proposed TCI targets (Fig 2): sedation (MOAA/S 2-3) at 1.75 x MOAA/S Ce50; anaesthesia (>90% MOAA/S 0) at
  5.25 x MOAA/S Ce50, both with opioids present (derived at the 35-y reference: 0.32 and 0.96 ug/mL).
- Opioids lower remimazolam CL by ~13% (Discussion).

## Covariates
Total body weight (Fsize = WGT/70; CL exponent 0.75; Q via volume ratios), age on V3 (and on PD Ce50/ke0),
sex on CL and V3, binary opioid on CL, Pugh-Child > 8 on V3; ESRD on CNS7054 CL. No maturation function.
Fat-free mass not tested (height missing for some subjects). The control stream (supplement, 2026-10-27)
confirms these forms exactly, with no caps on weight or age, and adds that the venous-delay rate and the
CNS7054 depot rate scale with weight^-0.25 (the CNS7054 depot with (WGT/70)^(0.518 x -0.25)).

## Caveats
- Venous prediction worse (MdAPE 26.0% vs 16.9% arterial); ICU/ECMO predictions poor.
- Opioids binary only; competitive CNS7054 tolerance mechanism admitted to be non-mechanistic.
- Few subjects under 20 y; industry-sponsored data (TS is a PAION employee).
- The control stream's comments carry small slips (a stray "))" on the ME50M line; the VAFRA line labelled
  "arterial error"); none changes a number.
- The effect sites are driven by the arterial (central) concentration; the repo's plasma curve is likewise
  the central compartment, so the plotted Cp is an arterial prediction.
