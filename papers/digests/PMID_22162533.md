# Knebel 2012: istradefylline exposure-response for OFF time, dyskinesia, dizziness and nausea

Istradefylline is not a stanpumpR drug and the paper is not cited in the repository; `drugs` is empty.

## Study
- Six phase 2/3 trials in PD patients with motor complications on levodopa/carbidopa; designs in the PK paper (Knebel
  2011, PMID 20200269). Exposure = individual predicted AUCss from that PK model (PDF p2).
- Efficacy (percentage of awake time OFF): 1760 patients (1181 istradefylline, 579 placebo), 9108 measurements (PDF p3).
  UPDRS subscale 2 mean 17.6 (median 17, 1-40); time since onset of motor complications, baseline OFF time and other
  disease-duration covariates in Table I (PDF p4; its rows are misaligned in the text layer). Co-medication: dopamine
  agonists 63%, COMT inhibitors 36%, amantadine 26%, selegiline 13% (PDF p4).
- Safety: 1198 istradefylline and 591 placebo patients (= 1789; PDF p9); one observation each except 73 patients in
  6002-US-001 (174 observations).
- NONMEM VI, FOCE-INT; full-covariate-model approach; stratified bootstrap 1000; predictive check (PDF p2-3).

## Model and parameters
Percentage OFF time (equations PDF p5, Table II PDF p6):
POFF = E0 x [1 + EmaxP x Time/(ET50 + Time)] + EmaxI x AUCss/(EC50 + AUCss), residual exponential plus additive; the
placebo / disease-progression term (DP-PR) is proportional to E0 and the istradefylline term is additive.

Full covariate list for E0, EmaxP and EmaxI (UPDRS-2/17, BOFF/6.3, TOMC/2.8, dopamine agonist, COMT inhibitor,
selegiline, amantadine) in Table II. The AE models are base models; full covariate models were too imprecise (PDF p9).

**Base vs final.** The abstract and the discussion (PDF p1, p12) give the typical maximal decrease as 5.79% (95% CI
4.09-7.49%) and half-maximal AUC as 1690 ng*h/mL (199-3180). These are the **base-model** estimates; their CIs equal
estimate +/- 1.96 x %SE (derived: 1690 x (1 +/- 1.96 x 0.45) = 199-3180; 5.79 x (1 +/- 1.96 x 0.15) = 4.09-7.49), not the
bootstrap. The final full model in Table II gives EmaxI -3.57 (CI -5.37 to -1.41) and EC50 1860 (640-4670) for the
reference covariates; the paper does not say which model the simulations used beyond "the population PK-PD parameters
from the bootstrap analysis" (PDF p3).

Model-predicted AE probability by dose (Table V, PDF p10; %, 95% CI): dyskinesia placebo 15.4, 20 mg 22.5, 40 mg 24.1,
60 mg 24.3; dizziness 6.21 / 9.30 / 10.7 / 10.9; nausea 6.75 / 9.82 / 11.7 / 13.0. The text calls 24.3% the maximum
dyskinesia probability and 10.9% the maximum dizziness probability; these are the 60-mg predictions in Table V (80 mg
is extrapolated slightly higher).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End points: percentage OFF time (efficacy); dyskinesia, dizziness and nausea as AEs (logistic). Observed OFF time vs
  AUCss plateaued near 2500 ng*h/mL, about the median exposure at 10 mg (PDF p4).
- Integrated simulation (Table VI, PDF p13): change in % OFF time -2.2 (5 mg), -3.2 (10), -4.0 (20), -4.7 (40),
  -4.9 (60), -5.1 (80, extrapolated); patients with >= 30 min decrease 57.5-68.9%.
- Smokers: response 18% (12-26%) lower at 20 mg/day and 12% (7.3-17%) lower at 40 mg/day; about 60 mg/day in a smoker
  matches 40 mg/day in a nonsmoker (PDF p12, Figure 5).
- Recommended start 20 mg/day, up to 40 mg/day; little added efficacy above 40 mg/day (PDF p13).
- Not an opioid: no MEAC, ke0 or potency ratio. No effect-site model; exposure is AUCss.

## Covariates
On the PD side, COMT inhibitor co-medication was the most influential (EmaxI x 1.88), but in the opposite direction on
the DP-PR term, and a subgroup analysis did not confirm it (p = .939; PDF p12). PK covariates (smoking, CYP3A4
inhibitors) act through AUCss.

## Caveats
- Abstract quotes the base-model EmaxI and EC50, not the final model (above).
- Safety n: the abstract says 1798 patients; the AE section gives 1198 + 591 = 1789 (PDF p1, p9). Unresolved.
- Table VI nausea at 5 mg prints 8.2 (8.5, 10.8), a lower bound above the point estimate; Table V gives 8.20
  (6.50-10.8) for the same quantity (PDF p10, p13). A source typo in Table VI, on the rendered page.
- The dizziness text defines EC50DZ as the AUC giving 50% of "EmaxDYS" (PDF p9): a label slip.
- Hill coefficients and nausea parameters poorly estimated (SE 75-403%).
- Sponsor-funded analysis.
