# Chen 1991: codeine disposition and partial clearances, single and chronic dosing

## Study
- 8 healthy subjects (7 M, 1 F), age 27.3 ± 5.2 y (25-37), weight 67.9 ± 11.9 kg (48-83); 4 smokers. Dextromethorphan phenotyping after the study: subject 2 poor metaboliser, the other 7 extensive.
- Single dose: 30 mg codeine phosphate tablet with 100 mL water, fasted; blood and saliva 0-12 h; urine 0-48 h.
- Chronic: 30 mg every 8 h for seven doses; sampling over the last 8 h interval.
- HPLC (fluorescence) for codeine, norcodeine, morphine (LOQ 2 ng/mL) and codeine-6-glucuronide (direct); morphine glucuronides in urine directly.
- AUC by linear trapezoid to 12 h plus C(12)/λz; partial clearances = urinary amount of each metabolite family / codeine AUC; intrinsic clearance = dose/AUC. Mean ± SD.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- No PD measured. No ke0, tPeak, hysteresis or MEAC.
- No potency ratio measured. Cited: C6G μ-receptor affinity about 300 times lower than morphine and M6G, similar to codeine (Irvine 1989); M6G potency equal to or above morphine (literature).

## Covariates
CYP2D6 (dextromethorphan) phenotype, one PM only. Renal clearance of codeine inversely related to urine pH (r = 0.81).

## Caveats
- n = 8 with a single PM, so no statistical EM/PM comparison is possible; the authors note the PM's plasma codeine profile was no different.
- Oral only; "intrinsic clearance" is dose/AUC with no IV reference.
- Partial clearance to morphine counts urinary morphine and its glucuronides, so it includes any presystemic formation.
