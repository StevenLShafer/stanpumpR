# Garraza-Obaldia 2024: propofol/remifentanil effects on MAP and HR

## Study
Prospective data from 80 outpatient gynecologic surgery patients (ASA I-II, female, BMI <= 35); 3 chronic hypertensives excluded, 77 analysed (training n=56 hysteroscopy, test n=21 other surgery). Table 1 (p.7): age 43 (12) y, height 1.61 m, weight 62 (10) kg, BMI 24 (4) (total). Propofol and remifentanil by TCI at effect-site targets (Schnider propofol with ke0 0.456 /min; Minto remifentanil) in 4 groups (propofol only, remifentanil only, each varied with the other fixed); 25 min step-up then 15 min step-down. Noxious stimuli: esophageal instrumentation, LMA insertion, tetanus (60 mA, 5 s), hysteroscopy. MAP by oscillometry every 1 min, HR by ECG. NONMEM 7.4 FOCEI, PsN. Drug concentrations are model-PREDICTED plasma values, not measured.

## Model and parameters
Turn-over (indirect response) model for MAP and HR with feedback of HR on MAP (FB_HR), inhibition of kin by propofol (MAP only) and remifentanil (HR only; no direct MAP effect), and a stimulus term h = 1 + theta*exp(-kStim*(t-tStim)) on MAP kin. Propofol gamma fixed to 1 (not different from 1). Effect-site (ke0) models were NOT supported by the data: plasma concentrations drive the effect. ## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Not an opioid analgesia paper, but remifentanil is an opioid: end points are MAP and HR only. C50 (remifentanil, HR) 4.57 ng/mL predicted plasma, Emax 0.69. No MEAC/analgesic concentration reported. No ke0, t1/2ke0, tPeak or hysteresis reported for remifentanil (or propofol) in this paper: the effect-compartment was tested and rejected (p.7); the only ke0 mentioned (0.456 /min) belongs to the Schnider model used to run the TCI. No equianalgesic/potency ratio. No propofol-remifentanil interaction on MAP detected (p > 0.05). Simulations (Fig. 1/4, p.6, p.11) show similar hypotension risk across combos titrated to the same suppression of motor response.

## Covariates
Age, weight, BMI, surgery duration, position changes, stimulus type tested; none significant (p.7).

## Caveats
Predicted not measured concentrations; population-model-driven TCI targets; hysteroscopy stimulus the only identifiable one; IIV on kout very large (about 130-143%); women only; non-invasive MAP at 1-min intervals; propofol concentrations in ug/mL, so C50 of 8.79 is high relative to usual hypnotic Ce50 values.
