# Dvorackova 2026: ganciclovir/valganciclovir popPK in lung transplant

## Study
Prospective open-label PK study, Prague (Motol), Jan 2020 to Jul 2024. 110 adult lung transplant recipients (LUTR); 379 ganciclovir (GCV) concentrations, 194 after oral valganciclovir (VGCV) and 185 after IV GCV; median 3 (1-12) samples per patient. Table 2 (PDF p5, printed 173), median (IQR): weight 75 (67-86) kg; age 55 (46-62) y; 70 M / 40 F; cystic fibrosis 9 (8%); azole co-treatment 31 (28%); eGFR (CKD-EPI 2021, mL/min/1.73 m2) 85.2 (16.8-153) [min-max]; time since transplant median 14.5 d (1-1936). Exclusions: other organ transplant, renal replacement.
Dosing: IV GCV 5 mg/kg q12h as 60 min infusion for 14 days then VGCV, renally adjusted (Table 1, PDF p3: e.g. eGFR > 90 mL/min/1.73 m2: GCV 500 mg q12h, VGCV 900 mg q12h; 30-60: GCV 200 mg q12h, VGCV 900 mg morning + 450 mg evening; 15-30: GCV 100 mg q12h, VGCV 450 mg q12h). Samples mostly at steady state: trough and at least two later samples the same day. Serum GCV by LC-MS (details in the online supplement, not read). NONMEM 7.4.0, FOCE-I; VGCV dose converted to GCV equivalent by molecular weight (354.362 / 255.23, i.e. x 0.720; derived). Bootstrap (500) and NPDE validation; Monte Carlo (Simulx, 110,000 virtual patients) for probability of target attainment (PTA).

No IIV on kA, V2, Q (insufficient data). Text (PDF p4): each 1 mL/min/1.73 m2 fall in eGFR lowers CL by 0.06 L/h (derived check: 4.96/85.2 = 0.058). Derived: CL at eGFR 85.2 = 7.01 L/h; at the lowest observed eGFR 16.8 = 3.02 L/h. The authors warn against extrapolating below eGFR 16.8.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD model. PK/PD targets taken from literature and used for PTA: AUC24h > 50 mg.h/L (prophylaxis), AUC24h 80-120 mg.h/L (treatment), also AUC24h 40-60, Ctrough 1-2 mg/L (prophylaxis) and 2-4 mg/L (treatment) (PDF p2-4); targets are for plasma GCV, which the authors note may not reflect intracellular ganciclovir triphosphate. PTA with standard dosing (text, PDF p5-6, Figs 2-5): VGCV prophylaxis 1% at eGFR 16.8-24, 27-44% at higher eGFR; GCV prophylaxis < 5% below eGFR 50, 21% at 50-69, 50% at > 70; treatment PTA about 21-35% at higher eGFR. Observed GCV 0.1-19.2 mg/L. No ke0 or hysteresis; not an opioid (audit: none).

## Covariates
Only eGFR (CKD-EPI 2021, linear, on CL; OFV -45.655) was significant. Tested and not retained: weight (CL, V1), sex, cystic fibrosis, azole antifungals, inter-occasion variability. Comparator note: the intro quotes Perrottet (cited paper) as 44% higher CL in renal and 27% lower in heart transplant than in LUTR.

## Caveats
Mostly trough-heavy sparse sampling; V2 and Q poorly determined (V2 bootstrap CI 12-960 L); lowest eGFR 16.8; plasma not intracellular exposure; PTA depends on literature targets that the cited studies could not link to toxicity; the supplement carries the assay and extra figures.

## Verification against stanpumpR
Independent comparison, not a check of a citation (repo constants `R/drugs_ganciclovir.R:115-137`):
- Dose basis: the repo converts VGCV to GCV with 0.720 (line 137), the same MW ratio as here (255.23/354.362 = 0.7202, derived). Matches.
- F: repo 0.594 (Valcyte label, line 135); this paper 0.575 derived, bootstrap 0.58. Agree within 4%.
- CL: repo 7.49 L/h x CrCl/57 (Cockcroft-Gault, mL/min, zero intercept; lines 115-116, 167). This paper CL = 2.05 + 4.96 x eGFR/85.2 (L/h, CKD-EPI, per 1.73 m2). At 85 mL/min the repo gives 11.2 L/h (derived) against 7.01 here, and at 17 mL/min 2.2 against 3.0 (derived). The two renal-function measures are not interchangeable, and the cohorts differ (kidney/liver/heart, mean 66 kg vs LUTR 75 kg), so this is a population/definition difference, not a transcription error; this card cannot say which is closer for a given patient.
- V1: repo 31.9 L vs 43.1 here; V2 32.0 vs 140; Q 10.2 vs 2.1 L/h; ka 0.895 vs 0.334 /h; lag 0.382 vs 0.563 h. The peripheral and absorption parameters differ several-fold; this paper's V2 and Q have wide uncertainty. Repo's size scaling of volumes by fat-free mass (lines 153-155) is not tested here (weight not significant, as in Caldes).
- Units: no per-kg or time-unit conversion is needed; repo converts h to min correctly for Caldes values (lines 167-172), not applicable to this paper.
