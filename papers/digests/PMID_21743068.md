# Sepulveda 2011: paediatric propofol PK models in healthy infants and toddlers

## Study
Prospective external validation of eight published paediatric propofol PK models in 41 ASA I-II children, 18 girls and 23 boys, age 3-26 months, cleft lip/palate surgery. Mean (range): age 9.9 (3.2-26) months, weight 8.2 (5.2-11.4) kg, height 68.5 (57-79) cm (Table 1, PDF p3/printed 595). No premedication. Sevoflurane induction, remifentanil 0.2 mcg/kg/min adjusted (mean consumption 0.26 (0.07) mcg/kg/min), sevoflurane stopped and washed down to 0.5 MAC or less for 5 min, then propofol 2.5 mg/kg bolus and 8 mg/kg/h infusion (mean duration 99 (31) min). 543 arterial samples (2 mL) at 1, 2, 3, 5, 10, 20, 40, 60 min after the bolus, at end of surgery, and 1, 3, 5, 30, 60, 120 min after stopping. PLASMA propofol by HPLC (Seno method), centrifuged within 2 h; calibration 0.1-10 mcg/mL, LOQ 0.1, LOD 0.01 mcg/mL. Predictions simulated in NONMEM VI from each child's weight and dose; Varvel PE/MDPE/MDAPE; acceptable = MDPE within +/-20% and MDAPE <30%. Positive PE = model underestimates.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None: only plasma propofol concentration. No ke0, tPeak, EC50 or MEAC. The authors note BIS and entropy perform poorly under 1 yr, so no PD model could be built. Remifentanil (about 0.2-0.26 mcg/kg/min) was co-administered, and sevoflurane was washed down to 0.5 MAC or less first; no opioid concentrations were measured.

## Covariates
Weight only (as used for all eight models here). Age and height are described, not used. The authors suggest allometric scaling and clearance maturation should be tried.

## Caveats
Short sampling (mean infusion 99 min, 2 h recovery) may hide differences in models derived from long sampling. Plasma assay here versus whole-blood assays in most source studies; the authors say plasma can run 5-10% higher after 1-2 h before centrifuging. Most models over-predict the early concentration (initial V1 too large, probably from venous sampling in source studies). Observed decay after stopping was faster than predicted (higher clearance in small infants). Co-administered sevoflurane and remifentanil might raise propofol concentrations (cited from adult work). Single centre, cleft surgery, healthy children only, 3-26 months. Contrasts with Absalom 2003 (PMID_14504151), where cardiac patients showed slower decay.
