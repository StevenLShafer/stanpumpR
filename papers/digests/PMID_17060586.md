# Christensen 2006: oxcarbazepine (MHD) concentrations in pregnancy

## Study
Retrospective chart review, Aarhus University Hospital (Denmark), of women with epilepsy registered in EURAP and
the local EpiBase (PDF p1). **9 pregnancies in 7 women** taking oxcarbazepine (BID or TID), mono- or polytherapy
(lamotrigine, topiramate/clobazam co-medication in two). Two abortions and one pregnancy without in-pregnancy
MHD were excluded; Patient 3 (caesarean at week 22) was included. Trough (pre-dose) serum MHD
(10-hydroxycarbazepine, the monohydroxy derivative) was retrieved before, during and after pregnancy. Trimesters
from the last menstrual period: days 1-84, 85-197, 198 to birth. MHD < 5 µmol/L (n = 2) treated as noncompliance
and excluded. **Assay**: HPLC, within-run CV 1.4-3.6%; **total** (bound + unbound) **racemic** MHD in serum;
oxcarbazepine itself not measured. End point: dose-corrected concentration (MHD µmol/L / daily dose mg),
averaged within a trimester when several samples existed. Statistics: one-way ANOVA, paired t-tests (Stata 9.0).
Weights, ages and gestational ages at sampling are not given.

## Model and parameters
No PK model. Results (abstract and Results, PDF p1, confirmed on the page image): dose-corrected MHD concentration
as a percentage of the same woman's pre-pregnancy value (pregnancies with a pre-pregnancy sample, n = 7):

| Period | Mean % of pre-pregnancy | SD | p vs before (n) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1st trimester | 72% | 13% | 0.003 (6) |
| 2nd trimester | 74% | 17% | 0.02 (7) |
| 3rd trimester | 64% | 6% | 0.0002 (7) |
| After pregnancy (median 56 d, range 11-390) | 108% | 18% | 0.33 |

ANOVA across periods p = 0.0016. The Discussion (PDF p2) restates this as a fall in concentration of up to about
36% in the third trimester, attributed to raised clearance. The Table (PDF p2, page image) gives per-pregnancy
mean MHD (µmol/L) and mean daily oxcarbazepine dose (mg), e.g. pregnancy 1: 49/38/38/37/38 µmol/L on
900/900/1,050/1,050/900 mg; pregnancy 5: 100/92/85/100/120 µmol/L on 2,400/2,400/2,600/3,375/2,850 mg.
Recomputed from the Table's period means for the 7 pregnancies with pre-pregnancy and third-trimester values
(derived: ratios 0.65, 0.63, 0.70, 0.71, 0.79, 0.53, 0.64; mean 0.665, SD 0.08), close to but not the same as
the reported 64% (SD 6%), which the authors computed from per-sample concentration/dose ratios.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Not an opioid. No concentration-effect analysis: seizures are tabulated per period (Table, continued) without
statistical testing; the authors see no clear change in seizure frequency. No therapeutic range is stated.

## Covariates
Pregnancy (by trimester) only. MHD protein binding is about 40% (Discussion), so the authors think lower binding
unlikely to explain the fall.

## Caveats
Small (7 women, 9 pregnancies; 7 pairs per comparison), retrospective, TDM samples at clinicians' discretion,
doses changed during pregnancy (most increased), two pregnancies from the same woman counted separately (a
first-pregnancy-only analysis was said to agree). Total racemic MHD only: no enantiomers, no unbound
concentration. The ratio is a change in concentration/dose (apparent CL/F), so it cannot separate clearance from
absorption or adherence; the authors argue against hyperemesis because the fall was largest in late pregnancy.
Postpartum sampling was not systematic, so the return to baseline is not timed.

## Verification against stanpumpR
- **64% of baseline in the third trimester** (R/drugs_eslicarbazepine.R:61-63; pregnancyRegistry.csv:108): the
  paper's abstract and Results give 64% (SD 6%). **Matches.**
- **1 / 0.64 = 1.56** (R/drugs_eslicarbazepine.R:71): arithmetic correct (1.5625). The comparator is the same women's
  pre-pregnancy dose-corrected concentration, so the ratio is a relative change and applying it as a multiplier to
  the Falcao non-pregnant CL/F (2.43 L/h, line 130) is a consistent use of a within-subject ratio, as long as
  Falcao's population is the right baseline (a non-pregnant adult). Using 1/mean ratio rather than the mean of
  individual 1/ratios is a small approximation. **Matches.**
- **"9 pregnancies"** (R/drugs_eslicarbazepine.R:63; registry line 108): the study had 9 pregnancies, but the 64%
  is from the 7 with a pre-pregnancy value. Not an error, slightly generous.
- Registry `design: "paired NCA"`: the study is a retrospective paired comparison of trough concentration/dose
  ratios, not a non-compartmental analysis. Minor wording; the registry's `comparator` and `trimester` fields
  match the paper.
- Surrogacy (MHD → eslicarbazepine): this paper measured total racemic MHD only, so it supports the size of the
  change in MHD, not its transfer to the S-enantiomer; the repo states the surrogate and its low confidence
  openly (lines 53-77). See the open question.
- Factor 1.6 applied with the Pregnant flag regardless of trimester; the paper's 1st/2nd-trimester values (72%,
  74%, i.e. ×1.39, ×1.35, derived) would give a smaller factor early in pregnancy. The repo labels its factor
  third-trimester (registry `trimester: third`), which is consistent with this source.
- tests lines 30-31: check only the reference string; consistent.
- `inst/help/drugs/eslicarbazepine.md:15` does not mention pregnancy or this paper at 9aec7d8d; nothing to verify
  there.
Overall: **partly-verified**: every number the repo takes from this paper matches, but the paper supports the change
in total racemic MHD only, not its use for eslicarbazepine (open question).
