# Friedman 1992: dose, concentration and EEG/DSST effect of oral diazepam

## Study
Randomised, double-blind, single-dose, four-way crossover (placebo, 2, 5, 10 mg oral diazepam), ≥ 2 weeks
washout. 11 healthy volunteers (8 men, 3 women, 19-35 y); weight not given (PDF p. 3, printed 141). Fasted
overnight, dosed about 8 AM with 180 mL water, fasting to 4 h. Venous plasma at 1/4, 1/2, 3/4, 1, 1.5, 2,
2.5, 3, 4, 6, 8 and 12 h. Diazepam and desmethyldiazepam by GC with electron capture (QC CV ≤ 11%).
Effects at the same times: DSST (90 s, placebo-corrected), Randt memory test, self and observer sedation
ratings, and EEG (O2-P4, eyes closed, FFT 0.75-31 Hz) as percentage of total amplitude in 13-31 Hz
(sigma + beta), expressed as change from each trial's baseline. Linear regression, repeated-measures ANOVA,
nonlinear least squares (SAS PROC NLIN).

## Model and parameters
No compartmental model; 12-h sampling was too short for a complete profile (Discussion). Table I (PDF p. 3,
printed 141, image; mean ± SEM, n = 11):

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- **EEG (13-31 Hz % amplitude, change from baseline).** Baseline about 15-16% (Table II: 14.9-16.0 ± 1.6-2.3,
  NS between trials). No change with placebo; 2 mg significant only at 2 h; 5 mg increased 1/4-2.5 h (max
  +7.3%); 10 mg 3/4-12 h except 6 h (max +15.2%). Mean concentration vs mean EEG change: linear
  y = 0.064x − 1.0 (r = 0.93, AIC 39.61) or y = 0.0167x^1.243 (AIC 38.21) (Fig. 6, image). No maximum
  reached, so no Emax or EC50 could be estimated. Diazepam plus desmethyldiazepam did not improve the fit.
- **Hysteresis:** none significant after 10 mg (Fig. 9); the authors attribute the absence of
  counterclockwise hysteresis to very rapid plasma-brain equilibration. **No ke0, t½ke0 or tPeak reported.**
- **DSST:** 2 mg indistinguishable from placebo; 5 mg depressed at most times 1/4-4 h; deeper with 10 mg.
  Placebo-corrected DSST vs concentration linear, r = −0.69 (Fig. 4); r = −0.89 against EEG change (Fig. 4
  right panel, text layer only).
- **Observer-rated sedation:** increased 1-2 h after 5 mg and 1/4-3 h after 10 mg (Fig. 7); correlated with
  concentration r = 0.65, EEG r = 0.72, DSST r = 0.84. Self-ratings and Randt test showed no consistent
  change.
- No therapeutic range, MEAC or potency ratio stated. Not an opioid paper.

## Covariates
None examined (n = 11, sex and weight not analysed).

## Caveats
- Single doses, healthy young volunteers, no anxiety disorder; 12-h sampling, no half-life or clearance.
- Practice effects on the DSST (placebo trial improved at 3/4, 8 and 12 h; Fig. 2), handled by placebo
  subtraction.
- Concentration-effect fits are on mean data across doses and times, not individual fits.
- Highest concentrations about 300 ng/mL, so the curve's upper part is not seen.
