# Vellinga 2025 SDC5: remimazolam / CNS7054 population PK, refit on three sessions

## Study
- Supplement to the Anesthesiology paper (see PMID_40067039.md for the trial: 24 healthy volunteers, three sessions, remimazolam effect-site TCI, remifentanil TCI in sessions 2 and 3).
- Samples eligible (PDF p. 1): remimazolam and CNS7054 551, 549, 552 in sessions 1, 2, 3; remifentanil 316 (session 2) and 318 (session 3). Below LLOQ and excluded from fitting: remimazolam 9, 7, 4; CNS7054 28, 26, 33; remifentanil 3 and 5 (sessions 2, 3).
- NONMEM 7.5.0, FOCE with interaction; R 4.0.3 for processing.
- Start: the joint remimazolam-CNS7054 model of session 1 (Vellinga, submitted at the time) refitted to all sessions; 80% of remimazolam assumed converted to CNS7054 (fixed, from the Byfavo EPAR). VPC judged reasonable.
- Remifentanil was not re-modelled: the Eleveld 2017 model was used with structural parameters fixed; the residual error alone was re-estimated: additive SD 0.03 ng/mL, proportional CV 23.4%. MdAPE of that model on these data 20.4% (range 8.9-54.6%).
- Interaction tests: no remifentanil effect on remimazolam clearance (delta OFV 0.0); strong effect on apparent CNS7054 clearance (delta OFV -315.4). A later re-test of an effect on remimazolam clearance improved fit (delta OFV -68.1) but raised the condition number to 8555.7 (versus 156.5), so it was dropped.

## Model and parameters
- Remimazolam: three-compartment (V1, V2, V3, CL, Q2, Q3). 80% of the CL goes to metabolite via a transit compartment (rate KTR) into the CNS7054 central compartment (V5, apparent clearance CLM). Elimination of the non-metabolised 20% is CL*(1-FM)/V1.
- Allometric scaling (code, PDF p. 20): volumes (WT/70)^1, clearances (WT/70)^0.75, KTR (WT/70)^-0.25 (70-kg reference). Parameter units are per 70 kg. No age, sex or other covariates on the remimazolam or CNS7054 parameters.
- Remifentanil inhibits CNS7054 elimination: CLM is multiplied by (1 - INH), INH = C^gamma/(C^gamma + IC50^gamma), C = remifentanil plasma concentration (ng/mL) from the Eleveld model (code, PDF p. 23).
- Concentrations in the output: CP = A1/V1*1000 (ng/mL), CM = A5/V5*1000*(425.3/439.3) (ng/mL, metabolite molecular weight 425.3 over parent 439.3). Remimazolam residual error is proportional only (additive fixed 0).
- Table 2 (PDF p. 6, read on the rendered page): final model, 95% CI from log-likelihood profiling. IIV is CV%; shrinkage %.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- No PD in this supplement (PD is SDC6). No MEAC, ke0, t1/2ke0, tPeak, hysteresis or potency ratio is reported for any drug. The only potency-like value is the remifentanil IC50 on CNS7054 apparent clearance, 8.0 ng/mL (Table 2), a pharmacokinetic interaction on plasma concentrations, not an effect end point.
- Audit: none (remifentanil is an opioid, but only its PK is used, from Eleveld 2017; no effect-site or analgesic concentration here).

## Covariates
- Body weight by allometry (above). Remifentanil concentration on CNS7054 clearance. Remifentanil sub-model carries its own Eleveld covariates (age, sex, height, weight via fat-free mass and maturation; code PDF pp. 20-22).

## Caveats
- The fraction metabolised is assumed (0.8), so CNS7054 CLM, V5 and KTR are apparent; the authors say a wrong fraction affects the metabolite parameters.
- Remifentanil PK was not estimated (single target per volunteer per session). Samples below LLOQ were dropped rather than modelled in the final estimation.
- Some structural trend in CWRES vs time for remimazolam in session 3 (authors); less visible in the pcVPC.
- IIV on Q3 and KTR is large (57.5%, 67.0%).
