# Bertin 2026 ESM1: levosimendan, OR-1855, OR-1896 supplementary material

Supplement to the main paper digested in `PMID_41288922.md`; this card records only what the supplement adds.

## Study
Same 21 VA-ECMO patients (IDs 1-15 adults, 16-21 neonates/infants, p8). Plasma LOQs (pp. 9, 11, 13 and `$ERROR`, p21): levosimendan 0.00036 nmol/mL, OR-1855 0.00049 nmol/mL, OR-1896 0.00041 nmol/mL (the control stream's ln values -7.938, -7.617, -7.805 agree). Values below the LOQ are handled by the M3 likelihood. NONMEM ADVAN13, 5 compartments (central, peripheral, transit, OR-1855, OR-1896), LAPLACIAN with INTER, proportional error on the log scale.

ETA on Q, K40, K50, K54 fixed to 0. Differential equations (p20-21): A1' = -(K12+K13+K10) A1 + K21 A2; A2' = K12 A1 - K21 A2; A3' = K13 A1 - K34 A3; A4' = K34 A3 - (K45+K40) A4 + K54 A5; A5' = K45 A4 - (K50+K54) A5. K12 = Q/V1, K21 = Q/V2. Age flag: AGE in years, Q1 = 1 if AGE <= 1.

Supplementary 1 (p3): the OR-1896 -> OR-1855 back-conversion rate is derived as keM2 x f / (1 - f) = 0.01 x 0.55 / (1 - 0.55) = 0.012 /h, where f = 0.55 is the fraction of OR-1896 converted, inferred as 100% - 45% found unchanged in urine (Puttonen et al., ref 1). (derived: 0.01 x 0.55 / 0.45 = 0.0122.) The fixed elimination constant 0.01 /h for both metabolites is stated as fixed in the model; its source is not given in the supplement.

Simulation scenarios shown (pp. 16-19): 70 kg adult 0.05 ug/kg/min for 1 h, 0.1 for 3 h, 0.2 for 20 h; 4 kg neonate 0.2 ug/kg/min for 48 h; adult 48 h infusions; neonate 0.1 ug/kg/min for 72 h or 96 h; two 48 h infusions 1 week apart.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No ke0, tPeak, MEAC or potency ratio (not an opioid).

## Covariates
Univariate screen (p3-4): significant at DeltaOFV < -3.84 were body weight and height on keM1 and keM2, age on V2 and ktransit, CRRT on keM1 and keM2, ECMO flow on keM2, number of comedications on kM2 and kM2-M1, sex on keM2, and neonate/infant status on ktransit, kM2 and keM2; most had RSE > 50% and were not kept. Body weight on keM1 was the most significant (DeltaOFV -17.03 as read from the scrambled table) and entered the step-2 analysis; neonate/infant status on kM2 was retained in the final model (step 2, p4). Per-row RSE/DeltaOFV values: see open question.

## Caveats
- n = 21; fixed metabolite elimination and fixed back-conversion; V4 = V5 = V1 is an identifiability assumption.
- Main paper Table 2 rounds K13 to 0.01 /h and kM2-M1 to 0.01; the control stream gives 0.0127 and 0.012.
