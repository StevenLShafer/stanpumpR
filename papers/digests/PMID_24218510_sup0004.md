# McCune 2014 supplement, Table S7: worked initial IV busulfan dose

## Study
Supplementary file to McCune 2014 (main card `PMID_24218510.md`). One page, Supplemental Table 7: a step-by-step
prediction of the initial once-daily IV busulfan dose for a target Css of 770 ng/mL using the NFM model, for two
simulated patients. No data or patients of its own.

## Model and parameters
Inputs (Table S7, PDF p1, confirmed on the rendered page):
- Case 1: 1 y, ABW 10 kg, height 0.76 m, female. Case 2: 40 y, ABW 70 kg, height 1.76 m, male.

Steps and printed results (case 1 / case 2):
1. FFM = WHSmax·HT²·ABW/(WHS50·HT² + ABW); female WHSmax 37.99, WHS50 35.98; male 42.92, 30.93 → **8.89 kg / 49.7 kg**.
2. NFM = FFM + Ffat·(ABW − FFM), FfatCL 0.509, FfatV 0.203 → NFM_CL 9.43 / 60.0 kg; NFM_V 9.217 / 53.8 kg.
3. Fsize = (NFM/70)^Pwr → CL 0.222 / 0.891; V 0.132 / 0.769.
4. Fmat from PMA, "TM50 of 46 weeks", Hill 2.3 (infants < 2 y) → 0.831 / 1.00.
5. CL_GRP = CLpop·Fmat·Fsize with **CLpop 12.4 L/h/70 kg NFM** → 2.23 / 11.05.
6. Individual parameters: CL 2.2 / 10.7 L/h; V 6.6 / 38.4 L; half-life 2.05 / 2.47 h.
7. Dose = Css (mg/L) × CL_GRP (L/h) × dosing interval (h) → **41.3 mg / 204 mg** per day.

Derived checks of the table's own arithmetic (all derived by this card):
- Steps 2-4 follow from the printed FFMs: (9.43/70)^0.75 = 0.222, 9.217/70 = 0.132, (60/70)^0.75 = 0.891,
  53.8/70 = 0.769; PMA 40 + 52 = 92 wk gives Fmat 0.831. Case 2's NFMs follow exactly from FFM 49.7; case 1's
  (9.43, 9.217) do not quite (8.89 gives 9.45 and 9.12).
- Step 1 does **not** follow from the printed equation and constants: they give 7.13 kg (case 1) and 56.1 kg (case 2).
- Step 5: 12.4 × 0.831 × 0.222 = 2.29 (printed 2.23); 12.4 × 0.891 = 11.05 (printed 11.05).
- Step 6 vs 5: case 2 CL 10.7 vs 11.05; half-life 2.47 h matches ln2·38.4/10.7 = 2.49 rather than /11.05 = 2.41.
  Case 1: ln2·6.6/2.23 = 2.05 h. The table is internally inconsistent by about 3% for case 2.
- Step 7: 0.77 × 2.23 × 24 = 41.2 mg; 0.77 × 11.05 × 24 = 204 mg; the dose uses step 5's CL.
- V in step 6 implies about 50 L per 70 kg NFM_V (6.6/0.132, 38.4/0.769) and no female factor; Table 2's
  V1 + V2 = 43.8 L per 59 kg is 52.0 L per 70 kg, and Table 2 has a 1.07 female factor on V.
- CLpop 12.4 L/h per 70 kg NFM is Table 2's 11.4 L/h per 62 kg rescaled: 11.4 × (70/62)^0.75 = 12.49.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Not an opioid or PD paper. Only an exposure target: Css 770 ng/mL, chosen as an example and individualisable
(footnote a). No ke0, MEAC or potency ratio.

## Covariates
As in the main paper: FFM (sex, height, ABW), NFM with parameter-specific fat fractions, allometric Fsize,
PMA maturation for infants < 2 y. Sex enters only through the FFM constants in this table (no female V factor).

## Caveats
- The FFM values do not follow the printed equation (see above); the source of 8.89 and 49.7 kg is not stated.
- Small internal inconsistencies between steps 5 and 6 (case 2 CL), and between steps 2 and 1 (case 1 NFM).
- Reference size here is 70 kg NFM, whereas Table 2 of the article uses 62 kg (CL) and 59 kg (V) NFM.
