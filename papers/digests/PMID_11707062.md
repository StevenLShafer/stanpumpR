# Yukawa 2001: two-compartment apparent digoxin model in Japanese patients

PDF page n = printed page 772 + n (PDF 1 = p773).

## Study
Retrospective routine TDM, Kyushu Kousei-Nenkin Hospital. Modelling group 106 patients with heart failure and
atrial fibrillation (43 men, 63 women), 246 serum concentrations; validation group 45 patients (18 men, 27 women),
66 concentrations. All on once-daily digoxin tablets (Digosin). Table I (PDF p3, rendered; values are mean ± SD
and range, modelling / validation): age 60.5 ± 12.4 (21.3-83) / 62.3 ± 10.4 (39-80) y; weight 50.4 ± 10.2
(31.5-88.8) / 51.5 ± 10.6 (34.5-79) kg; dose 192.7 ± 62.5 (125-250) / 197.0 ± 62.3 (125-250) mcg/day; serum
digoxin 1.19 ± 0.59 (0.32-4.01) / 0.95 ± 0.41 (0.26-1.91) mcg/L; Cockcroft-Gault CLcr 56.2 ± 22.6 (11.0-130.9) /
70.9 ± 26.3 (31.9-141.1) mL/min. CHF 14 / 11, spironolactone 23 / 26, calcium antagonists 19 / 14 patients.
Patients on amiodarone, quinidine, cholestyramine or phenytoin excluded. Assay FPIA (TDx), LLQ 0.2 mcg/L, CV
< 10% at 0.5-5.0 mcg/L. NONMEM V (ADVAN3 TRANS4); no absorption phase fitted ("lack of information", although
many samples were 1-4 h post dose), so each tablet was a bolus into the central compartment (p774). All
parameters are apparent (/F) (p774). Proportional IIV and residual error; covariate entry at p < 0.005.

## Model and parameters
Final model (eq 10, Table IV, PDF p5 rendered): CL/F (L/h) = (θ1 TBW + θ5 CLcr) θ6^SPI θ7^CCB; V1/F = θ2 TBW,
V2/F = θ4 TBW, Q/F = θ3 TBW (TBW kg, CLcr mL/min).

Table VI (PDF p7, rendered), 70 kg, present study: CL/F 13.72 L/h at CLcr 100 and 3.08 at CLcr 5 (no SPI/CCB);
10.56 / 2.37 with SPI, 10.76 / 2.41 with CCB, 8.28 / 1.86 with both; maintenance dose for an average 1 mcg/L
= CL x 24 h (329, 254, 258, 199; 74, 57, 58, 45 mcg/day; F taken as 1). (derived: 0.036 x 70 + 0.112 x 100 =
13.72 and + 0.112 x 5 = 3.08, so the table reproduces eq 10.) Text p778: population mean CL 0.185 L/h/kg for a
60 kg patient with CLcr 80 (derived 11.12 L/h / 60 kg = 0.185, consistent).
Hypothesis tests (Table II/III): CHF did not improve the fit (θ6 0.937, LLD 3.37, p > 0.05); full-model
deletions: TBW LLD 24.1, CLcr 114.3, SPI 16.9, CCB 9.5.
Validation (45 patients): ME -0.03 ± 0.28, MAE 0.21 ± 0.19 mcg/L.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD. Therapeutic range 0.5-2.0 mcg/L quoted from the literature (p773, ref 1). No ke0.

## Covariates
TBW (linear, all parameters), Cockcroft-Gault CLcr (renal term), spironolactone x 0.770 (23% lower), calcium
antagonists (diltiazem, nicardipine, nifedipine, verapamil) x 0.784 (21.6% lower). CHF not retained.

## Caveats
V1/F and V2/F poorly determined: the authors say the volumes exceed earlier estimates (V1 0.48, Vss 8.5 L/kg) for
want of early samples; V2's CI includes zero. The spironolactone CI includes 1 despite LLD 16.9, and the authors
note FPIA cross-reactivity with spironolactone metabolites as a possible artefact. Small, light (mean 50 kg)
Japanese population; only 13% had CHF. No absorption model; bolus-into-V1 structure.
