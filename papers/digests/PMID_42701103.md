# Iihara 2026: model-predicted NK1 occupancy, netupitant vs aprepitant

## Study
Retrospective PK/PD modelling of published mean data (Helsinn-sponsored). PK: mean plasma curves only, fitted with
the Excel PKSolver add-in. Netupitant: single oral NEPA 300 mg in 24 fasted healthy adults (Calcagnile 2015), fitted
with a 2-compartment oral model with lag. Aprepitant: single oral 125 mg in 25 fasted healthy subjects (Majumdar
2006), fitted with a **1-compartment oral model, first-order absorption, no lag**. PD: striatal NK1 receptor
occupancy (RO) by PET. Netupitant: sigmoid Emax from Spinelli 2013 ([11C]GR205171, n = 6). Aprepitant: simple Emax
(gamma = 1) refitted to baseline and trough RO from Bergström 2004 ([18F]SPA-RQ, 16 healthy volunteers after 14 days of
dosing), because the originally published parameters "appeared to lack sufficient accuracy". Simulations over 20 days:
netupitant 300 mg once; aprepitant 165 mg once; aprepitant 125/80/80 mg.

are in the supplement (not in this PDF).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: **striatal NK1 receptor occupancy** by PET (a surrogate, not emesis):
- Aprepitant: simple Emax, **Emax 98.1%, EC50 10.4 ng/mL**, R² 0.9958 (Results). Derived: 90% RO at
  Cp = 90 x 10.4 / (98.1 - 90) = 115.6 ng/mL.
- Netupitant: sigmoid Emax, Emax 93.0%, EC50 10.2 ng/mL, gamma 1.1.
- No ke0 or hysteresis; occupancy is treated as a direct function of plasma concentration.
- Predicted RO (Fig 4/Results): all regimens > 90% at 3 h; days 5/7/10/20: netupitant 76/70/60/21%; aprepitant 3-day
  81/39/3/negligible; aprepitant 165 mg 45/10/<1/negligible. Day-10 aprepitant concentrations 0.34 (3-day) and
  0.06 ng/mL (165 mg).
- Thresholds discussed: > 90% RO for aprepitant's maximal efficacy (Bergström 2004); about 50% suggested for
  netupitant. Not an opioid; no MEAC.

## Covariates
None (mean data).

## Caveats
Mean-curve fits from different studies; small PET samples; different tracers; healthy volunteers extrapolated to
cancer patients; PK linearity assumed for 80/165 mg; sponsor (Helsinn, netupitant's maker) involved in design and
writing. RO is a surrogate.
