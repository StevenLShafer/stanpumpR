# Wilson 1987: early midazolam kinetics in pregnant and nonpregnant women

## Study
- **Design:** five parallel groups. Each patient received 5 mg midazolam IV over 10 s (p1058). Healthy
  parturients were more than 38 weeks pregnant, parity 4 or less, with an uncomplicated history.
  Gynaecological patients were of comparable age. All received oral ranitidine 150 mg 2 h before. Obstetric
  patients were supine with a left lateral tilt.
- **Groups** (Table 1, p1059, mean (SEM)):

| Group | n | Age (y) | Weight (kg) |
|---|---|---|---|
| I Labour (oxytocin-induced) | 12 | 25 (1) | 71 (3) |
| II Caesarean section (midazolam 5 min before GA) | 8 | 26 (2) | 74 (3) |
| III Awaiting caesarean section (3 h before surgery) | 10 | 25 (2) | 73 (3) |
| IV Gynaecological (hysterectomy) | 10 | 30 (2) | 60 (5) |
| V Gynaecological with oxytocin infusion | 10 | 31 (2) | 60 (3) |

  Groups I-III were significantly heavier than IV-V (P < 0.05). Group II was limited to 8 after two drowsy
  infants. Height is not reported.
- **Anaesthesia:** general anaesthesia (thiopentone, suxamethonium, pancuronium, N2O/O2, halothane) was induced 5
  min after midazolam in both gynaecological groups and in group II (p1058).
- **Sampling:** venous plasma at 2, 5, 10, 15, 30, 45, 60, 90 and 120 min, with maternal and umbilical venous
  blood at delivery (p1058).
- **Assay:** gas-liquid chromatography, CV 6.3% at 50 and 4.0% at 100 ng/mL, detection limit 1 ng/mL. Total drug
  only.
- **Analysis:** non-compartmental, confined to 0-2 h. AUC0-2h by the trapezoidal rule. t1/2 by log-linear
  regression from 45 min. Clearance and Vd(area) by standard NCA. ANOVA.

Footnotes to Table 3:
- The labour AUC is significantly higher than in groups II (P < 0.001), III and IV (P < 0.05).
- The labour Vd is significantly lower than in II (P < 0.01) and IV (P < 0.05).
- The group II clearance is significantly higher than every other group (P < 0.01-0.001).
- The labour clearance is the lowest, but no significance is marked against it.
- Half-lives did not differ between groups.

**Early plasma levels** (Table 2, p1059, ng/mL at 2/5/10 min):
- Labour: 411 (23) / 321 (32) / 230 (23).
- Combined caesarean: 236 (18) / 171 (13) / 131 (8).
- Combined gynaecological: 212 (14) / 175 (8) / 150 (7).
- Labour was higher than both comparisons, P < 0.001 at every time.

Oxytocin did not change the gynaecological levels (Fig. 2). Groups II and III did not differ in plasma levels
(p1058).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- No pharmacodynamic model. In group II, two babies needed active resuscitation.
- Apgar scores are in Table 4 (1-min average 5.8 in group II, 6.5 in group III and 7.5 in labour).
- The umbilical/maternal vein ratio fell as the injection-to-delivery interval lengthened (Table 5): 0.76 at
  20 min, 0.62 at 190 min (caesarean) and 0.30 at 201 min (labour).

## Covariates
Pregnancy state is the comparison. No weight or age covariate analysis.

## Caveats
- Only 2 h of sampling, because of the small dose and the assay limit. The authors note that the half-lives are
  short-study estimates (p1061).
- Total drug only; protein binding not measured.
- The labour group was induced with oxytocin and studied in a real-life setting. The authors suggest that
  haemodynamic change with contractions, failure to keep the lateral tilt, or a rise in binding may explain the
  small Vd and low clearance. They have no explanation for the high clearance in group II.
- The groups are not weight-matched (pregnant about 72 kg, gynaecological 60 kg).
