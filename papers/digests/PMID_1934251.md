# Buesa 1991: high-dose dacarbazine PK

## Study
Phase I-II patients at Hospital General de Asturias, Oviedo; n not stated as one number (dose groups of 1, 1, 5, 3, 3, 3, 1 = 17 patients, derived from Table 1 / Fig. 1 legend). Normal marrow, liver (bilirubin <= 1.2 mg/dl) and renal function (creatinine clearance >= 60 mL/min). Dacarbazine (DTIC) 850-1,980 mg/m2 as a 10-20-min IV infusion (30 min at 1,980), in 200 mL saline, light-protected, with calcium gluconate 5 mL of 10% at 0, 10 and 20 min. Blood at 0, 5, 15, 30, 45 min and 1, 2, 4, 6, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24 h; urine for 24 h. HPLC with UV (DTIC 326 nm, AICA 270 nm); no internal standard; lower limit 23 ng/mL DTIC, 34 ng/mL AICA. Method of residuals (Wagner), values corrected for infusion duration. Renal clearance from excretion rate against midpoint plasma concentration.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No effect site, ke0, tPeak, MEAC or potency data; not an opioid. Dose-limiting toxicity of 1,980 mg/m2 was hypotension (cited from the group's phase I), recommended dose 1,200 mg/m2.

## Covariates
None analysed beyond dose (DTIC dose vs fraction excreted, r = 0.712). Volumes and clearances given per kg.

## Caveats
High-dose (850-1,980 mg/m2), 10-30-min infusions, one to five patients per dose; mixed phase I-II patients; photodegradation could alter data (authors); no internal standard; third phase in 2 patients not modelled; other metabolites not measured. Authors compare with low-dose literature: Vc and VB higher than low-dose values 0.202 and 0.622 L/kg, t1/2a 3.5-fold the low-dose 2.9 min, t1/2b near the upper end of 38-190 min; AICA clearance about 10 times lower than at low dose (reference: the Breithaupt 1982 low-dose study, not the library).

## Verification against stanpumpR
The library's dacarbazine (R/drugs_dacarbazine.R, Breithaupt 1982, bolus 2.65-6.85 mg/kg, six patients, PMID_7172404) gives V1 0.178 and V2 0.454 L/kg (total 0.632 L/kg), CL 15.4 mL/min/kg (= 0.924 L/kg/h), Q 21.9 mL/min/kg (R/drugs_dacarbazine.R:36). Against this paper's means: V1 (Vc) is 2.4 times larger here (0.42 vs 0.178 L/kg), VB 1.49 vs Breithaupt's Vss 0.632 L/kg, CL 0.58 L/kg/h (9.7 mL/min/kg, derived) is 0.63 of the library's, half-lives 10 min and 2 h here (derived t1/2a 0.17 h) vs 2.4-3.6 min and 31-52 min in Breithaupt (R/drugs_dacarbazine.R:68-75). The direction (larger volumes, slower clearance, longer terminal half-life at about 23-53 mg/kg doses) is what the authors themselves report against low-dose studies and is consistent with possible saturable renal secretion, though they could not demonstrate nonlinearity. The library's model carries no dose dependence and states a normal-kidney curve at low dose (R/drugs_dacarbazine.R:49-50); it would under-predict concentrations and duration after a 1,000-2,000 mg/m2 dose according to this paper. The library's AICA is not modelled (header line 46). Verdict: not-applicable (no citation); informational comparison only.
