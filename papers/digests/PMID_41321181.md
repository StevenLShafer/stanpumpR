# Henthorn 2026 (online 2025): IV methadone enantiomer population PK

## Study
Secondary analysis of a previously published study (Kharasch 2015, NCT01648283). 64 healthy volunteers, 34 M / 30 F,
age 29 ± 8 y, weight 74 ± 13 kg (Table 1, mean ± SD); 42 White, 10 Black, 10 Asian, 2 other. CYP2B6 genotypes:
*1/*1 21, *1/*6 20 (Table 2 footnote), *6/*6 17, *4/*6 or *4/*1 4, *5/*5 2. CYP2C19 phenotypes tested in a subset.
Dose: racemic methadone HCl 6.0 mg (5.4 mg base) IV over 10 s. Venous samples 2, 5, 10, 20, 30 min, 1, 1.5, 2, 3, 4,
6, 8, 12, 24, 36, 48, 72, 96 h; urine to 96 h. Chiral LC-MS/MS of R(-)/S(+) methadone and EDDP in plasma and urine.
1,214 plasma samples, 256 urine collections. Phoenix NLME 8.6, FOCE ELS; M3 for BLQ; log-normal IIV;
proportional residual error. Whether the model dose was entered as salt or base, or per enantiomer, is not stated.

## Model and parameters
Three-compartment model per enantiomer (3- vs 2-compartment: OFV drop 9946.7). S(+) parameters are R(-) values times
fitted S/R ratios (volumes, intercompartmental clearances, renal clearance, clearance to EDDP). Elimination clearance
= renal + metabolism to EDDP + "other" (unmeasured). EDDP: 2-compartment, linked by a 2-compartment delay (Table 3).

Derived (from Table 2): total CL R = 0.103 L/min; S = 0.024×1.30 + 0.026×0.57 + 0.045 = 0.0910 L/min.
Results text: R clearance 25% renal, 23% EDDP, 51% other; S 16%, 34%, 50%. Terminal half-life 2.5 d (R), 1.7 d (S),
ratio 0.69 (Discussion, p.746). IIV of clearance 36% (R), 67% (S). MDPE 5.6/6.1%, MDAPE 13.9/14.6% (first 6 h).
Urinary recovery 0-96 h: R-methadone 20 ± 7%, S 15 ± 6%, R-EDDP 13 ± 5%, S-EDDP 23 ± 8% of dose.
EDDP (Table 3): ClE-R-eddp 0.29, ClE-S-eddp 0.28 L/min; V2 72.5/70.4 L; Vc 0.42/0.14 L; delays 0.69/0.42 min.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None measured. No MEAC, no ke0/t½ke0/tPeak, no hysteresis. Potency: introduction states R(-) mu-receptor affinity
and analgesic potency 30- to 50-fold greater than S(+) (citing ref 7; not this study's data). Oral bioavailability
"approximately 85%" quoted in the introduction without data.

## Covariates
Total body weight on V3 only: θ = θTV · WT^1.23 (Eq. 4 written uncentred; the centring weight is not given).
CYP2B6 genotype on CL to EDDP (exponential, Eq. 3; *1/*1 reference). Tested and rejected: sex, race, CYP2C19.
Weight not significant for clearance (Discussion). Age not reported as tested. Authors recommend using wild-type
parameters without genotype for practical use.

## Caveats
Healthy young volunteers, single 6 mg dose; no AAG, protein binding, creatinine clearance or hematocrit data;
small *4/*5 groups. Reference weight for the V3 power term not stated. Model dose basis (salt vs base) not stated.
Quoted terminal half-lives (2.5/1.7 d) are longer than the typical-value model gives (derived below).
