# Zhang 2023: aztreonam + amoxicillin/clavulanate vs NDM-producing E. coli and K. pneumoniae

## Study
- In vitro plus simulation study. Checkerboard MICs for 18 clinical NDM-producing isolates (9 K. pneumoniae, 9 E. coli) plus QC strains. MPC was measured for 7 isolates (3 K. pneumoniae and 4 E. coli per Table 3; the Methods text says 4 and 3). Time-kill assays used K. pneumoniae LW5 and E. coli 37 at MIC and 2x MIC, with samples at 0, 2, 4, 6 and 8 h (p.3-4).
- Simulation: 10,000 steady-state profiles over 24 h for each regimen (RxODE in R 4.1.2). CrCL was uniform 10-150 mL/min, normalized to 1.73 m2. Males and females in equal proportion (p.3-4).
- The text (p.7) says ELF penetration was 40% for aztreonam, but Methods (p.4) says 55%. This is an internal inconsistency; neither affects stanpumpR.

## Model and parameters
Simulation inputs as printed (p.4). These are recorded from the text layer only and are not confirmed on a page image.
- Aztreonam: two-compartment model, from the REJUVENATE popPK. CL = 4.73 x (CrCL/100)^0.43 L/h (CV 24.1%). Vc = 7.43 x (WT/70)^1.99 L (CV 50.9%). Vp = 6.44 L (CV 27.7%). Q = 9.26 L/h. Protein binding 56%.
- Amoxicillin: two-compartment model, CL = 10.3 x CrCL/102 (CV 39.9%), Vc 13.5 (CV 38.7%), Vp 14.1, Q 15.7. Units are not stated in the text layer. Protein binding 17%.
- Clavulanate: two-compartment model, CL = 6.8 x CrCL/102 (CV 57.8%), Vc 7.6 (CV 34.7%), Vp 11.6, Q 10.4. Units are not stated in the text layer. Protein binding about 25%.
- ELF penetration: aztreonam 55% (Methods) or 40% (Results), amoxicillin/clavulanate 30%.
- Dosing regimens (Table 1, 3 h infusions):
  - CrCL >50-150 mL/min: aztreonam 2 g load then 1.5 g q6h; amoxicillin/clavulanate 1/0.2 g q6h.
  - CrCL >30-50 mL/min: aztreonam 2 g load then 750 mg q6h; amoxicillin/clavulanate 1/0.2 g, then 500/100 mg q12h.
  - CrCL 10-30 mL/min: aztreonam 2 g load then 500 mg q8h.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- This is an antimicrobial PK/PD study. There are no MEAC or ke0 values, and the paper is not an opioid paper.
- Targets: aztreonam 60% fT>MIC (2-log kill, E. coli); amoxicillin/clavulanate 50% fT>MIC. Mutant restriction was measured by fT>MPC and fT in the mutant selection window (MSW).
- MICs and MPCs are in Tables 2 and 3 (p.6-7). The combination lowered the MPC to <=8 mg/L, a fall of at least 32-fold.
- Time-kill: the combination gave about a 2 log10 kill at 4 h (p.5).

## Covariates
In the simulations, CrCL drives CL (all three drugs) and body weight drives aztreonam Vc.

## Caveats
- In vitro and simulation only, with no clinical outcomes. Few isolates.
- Many isolates had MICs of >128 mg/L for the single agents.
- The combination does not cover isolates with AmpC or KPC-2. The authors say clavulanate IC50 is >100 uM for AmpC and KPC-2 (Table 6).
- Population PK model details are in the supplement, which is not in this PDF.
