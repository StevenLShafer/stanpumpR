# Sugiyama 2010: gemcitabine and dFdU population PK, CDA genotypes, Japanese patients

## Study
250 Japanese cancer patients given 30-min IV gemcitabine 800 or 1000 mg/m2 (Sep 2002-Jul 2004), plus 2 patients
added later after life-threatening toxicity (one homozygous CDA*3 given 454 mg/m2); 4 excluded for bimodal
concentration-time curves; 248 analysed. Table I (PDF p2, printed p550): 162 male / 86 female; dose 1000 mg/m2 (n
243), 800 (4), 454 (1); pancreatic 207, lung 35, mesothelioma 6; monotherapy 182, combination 66 (cisplatin 26,
carboplatin 16, fluorouracil 4, S-1 10, vinorelbine 10); mean +/- SD [range]: age 62.67 +/- 9.04 [35-80] y, BSA
1.56 +/- 0.17 [1.14-1.97] m2, weight 54.56 +/- 9.76 [30-80.3] kg, serum creatinine 0.70 +/- 0.19 [0.4-1.5] mg/dL.
BSA formula not stated. Samples before and at 0, 15, 30, 60, 90, 120, 240 min after the end of infusion (to 4.5 h
from start). Plasma gemcitabine and dFdU by HPLC-photodiode array/MS (methods in ref 12); 1973 gemcitabine and 1975
dFdU concentrations. NONMEM V level 1.1, ADVAN5; FO for building, FOCE for the final model; stepwise covariates at
p < 0.001. Genotypes: CDA (-31delC, 79A>C *2, IVS1+37G>A, 208G>A *3), DCK (-360C>G, 364C>T), SLC29A1/hENT1 (7 SNPs).
CDA*3 diplotypes non/non : non/*3 : *3/*3 = 230 : 16 : 2. Dose basis (base vs hydrochloride) not stated.

## Model and parameters
Gemcitabine two compartments, eliminated entirely to dFdU (fm fixed to 1, citing > 90% urinary recovery as dFdU);
dFdU two compartments. V1 fixed at 12.60 L (from the gemcitabine-only step) because Vm became implausible otherwise.
Proportional + additive residual error. No bootstrap or VPC (covariates too unevenly distributed).

Table IV (PDF p7, printed p555), final model 13; IIV %CV in the note:

CL1 = 73.70 x BSA x (1 - 0.639 x *3homo) x (1 - 0.171 x *3hetero) x (1 + 0.0749 x delC) x (1 + 0.191 x S-1)
CLm1 = 11.00 x BSA x (1 - 0.00855 x (AGE - 62.67)) x (1 - 0.732 x (Cre - 0.70))
Vm1 = 15.00 x BSA x (1 - 0.00806 x (AGE - 62.67)) x (1 + 0.239 x Sex)   (Sex 1 male, 0 female)

Derived (typical non-*3, delC 0, no S-1, female, age 62.67, Cre 0.70, BSA 1.56): CL1 115.0 L/h; CLm1 17.2 L/h;
Vm1 23.4 L (29.0 L male); dFdU total volume about 55-61 L. Text p7: *3 homozygotes -64%, heterozygotes -17%; delC
+7.5% per allele; S-1 +19%; CLm -8.6% per 10 y older and -7.3% per 0.1 mg/dL creatinine; Vm1 -8.1% per 10 y, +24%
male. Model-independent CL in *3/*3 was 80% below the non-*3 average (ref 12).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None modelled. No effect site, ke0, concentration target or therapeutic range (not an opioid; no MEAC, tPeak or
potency ratio). Toxicity is discussed only as case history: all three *3/*3 patients had life-threatening
toxicity including grade 4 neutropenia.

## Covariates
Retained: BSA (proportional) on CL1, CLm1, Vm1; CDA*3 (non-linear gene-dose: separate homo/hetero terms) and
CDA -31delC (count) and S-1 co-administration on CL1; age and creatinine on CLm1; age and sex on Vm1. Not retained:
BSA or weight on V1; age, sex, cisplatin on CL1; DCK and hENT1 polymorphisms; CDA *2 and IVS1+37G>A (collinear with
-31delC, which gave the smallest OFV).

## Caveats
dFdU sampled for only 4.5 h despite its longer half-life. V1 fixed. Only 2 *3/*3 and 10 S-1 patients drive those
terms; S-1 was taken hours before gemcitabine, mechanism speculative. No external or bootstrap validation.
Comparatives in the Discussion: CL 115 L/h, V1 12.60 L, V2 9.54 L called comparable to Jiang 2008 (162 L/h, 15 L,
15 L); CL "slightly smaller" and V1 larger than Tham 2008 (222.8 L/h, 2.96 L). The 222.8 L/h is Tham's
deamination (dFdU-formation) clearance, 0.73 x 305.2, not Tham's total CL.
