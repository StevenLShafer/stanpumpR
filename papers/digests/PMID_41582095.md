# Baklouti 2026: amoxicillin popPK in plasma and breast milk

## Study
25 French breastfeeding mothers (age 35 ± 4 y, weight 64.9 ± 12.1 kg, eGFR 114.7 ± 8.5 mL/min/1.73 m2) on oral immediate-release amoxicillin (with or without clavulanate) for at least 2 days: 2 x 1 g/day in n=6 and 3 x 1 g/day in n=19 (mean 2,760 ± 436 mg/day; Table 1, p5). Infants 5.5 ± 4.9 months (166 ± 148 days), 6.7 ± 2.7 kg (Table 2). At steady state, three nearly paired plasma and foremilk samples (about 20 mL, electric pump) between two doses at 15-30 min, 1-2 h and 3-4 h post-dose: 75 plasma and 75 milk samples, none below LLOQ. LC-MS/MS, LLOQ 0.2 mg/L plasma and 0.01 mg/L milk (total drug). Monolix 2024R1.

## Model and parameters
Two-compartment: gut (first-order ka) to central (plasma) compartment with elimination ke, plus a milk compartment fed by a unidirectional first-order transfer from plasma (kmilk) and eliminated by kmilk_e. Plasma C = X/Vc. Milk volume could not be estimated and was arbitrarily set equal to Vc (PDF p6-7). Proportional error for both matrices. No covariate retained (age, weight, height, exclusivity, eGFR, albumin, infant age/weight tested). Table 3 (PDF p6, printed 1838; read from the rendered image):

Derived: CL/F = Ke x V/F = 0.31 x 82.38 = 25.5 L/h (derived; the text, p8, says median apparent clearance 24 L, with the unit given there as L rather than L/h). Plasma half-life from Ke = 2.2 h (derived).

Milk results (Table 4, PDF p8, printed 1840): measured, median milk/plasma ratio 6.0% (Q1 4.5, Q3 9.6, min 0.8, max 94.0); RID 0.08% of adult dose (0.06-0.10, max 0.40) and 0.03% of paediatric dose (0.02-0.05, max 0.10); assumed milk intake 150 mL/kg/day, paediatric reference 100 mg/kg/day. Monte Carlo (10,000, 6 g/day as 2 g q8h): milk/plasma concentration ratio 4.8% (4.1-5.2, 3.3-5.4), AUC ratio 8.5% (6.6-11.1, 1.9-36.9), RID 0.03% adult, 0.03% paediatric (max 0.07 and 0.04). Maximum daily infant intake from the model 0.022 mg/day (p11).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No PD model, no ke0, no effect-site or minimum effective concentration. Not an opioid. Safety end point only: no adverse events in the infants and no milk-production change attributed to amoxicillin (Table 1).

## Covariates
None retained in the final model.

## Caveats
Structural model is not physiological: milk is eliminated continuously rather than at feeds, kmilk is constant, and milk volume is fixed to Vc, so milk amounts are model-dependent. Foremilk from a partial pump collection (authors call it a worst case). Sampling window only 15 min to 4 h after the dose; ka of 0.17/h is much lower than the roughly 1/h the authors quote for adults. V/F 82 L is higher than their expected 46 L from healthy volunteers (comparator: Arancibia 1980 adults, their ref 32), possibly body water or lower F. Ke is lower than the adult values they cite, and the apparent clearance is compared with clearances in pregnancy and postpartum (ref 39). Outlier mother N5 (ratio 94%) retained. Small n (25), French outpatients, mothers of preterm infants excluded.
