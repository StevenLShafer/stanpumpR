# Egan 2004: remifentanil bolus in volunteers (safety, PK, algometry, age)

## Study
- Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, two-period crossover, dose escalation; 1 h washout. 64 subjects: 48 younger (18-59 y, mean 28.2, range 20-48) and 16 older (60+ y, mean 67.4, range 60-75). Weight 72.5 kg (SD 12.5) younger, 74.9 (12.2) older; within 50% of ideal body weight (Table 2, PDF p4).
- Fixed unit doses (not per kg), 2-3 s bolus, 25 ug steps from 25 to 200 ug, 6 subjects per level; escalation stopped when 4 of 6 had respiratory intervention score (RIS) >= 3 (older group stopped at 75 ug, all 4 had RIS 4; younger reached 200 ug) (Table 3-4).
- Arterial samples only from subjects given 75 ug or more (1-60 min; 273 concentrations, 48 dosing records). Remifentanil in blood (citric-acid-stabilised) by HPLC-UV, validated 0.1-100 ng/mL, interassay CV under 15% above 0.1 ng/mL. Two-stage WinNonlin, then WinNonmix population model, linear kinetics assumed.

## Model and parameters
Two-compartment, clearance/volume parameterization, log-normal IIV (CV%), proportional residual error (PDF p6 / printed 340). Younger subjects only (no older subject was sampled).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- Analgesia: pressure algometry (leg tibia plunger, about 2 psi/s, cap 60 psi); onset = >15% above baseline, duration = time above 15% above baseline. Table 5 (PDF p5): younger group maximum % increase mean 62.5 / 73.2 / 105.4 at 50 / 100 / 200 ug (placebo 10.3); T-max mean 2.7 / 3.3 / 3 min; duration mean 6.3 / 7 / 8 min; onset 0.8 / 2 / 1.7 min. Older group: maximum % increase 31.9 / 66.3 / 92.7 at 25 / 50 / 75 ug (placebo 25.3); duration 5 / 9.7 / 9.5 min. Figure 1 gives maximum % increase by dose.
- Respiratory: RIS (Table 1) and minimum SpO2; minimum SpO2 within 2-4 min; older cohort significantly worse than younger at 75 ug (P=0.02 SpO2, P=0.01 RIS). Apnoea in 4 subjects (68 y at 75 ug; 36 y at 150 ug; 27 y at 75 ug; 21 y at 200 ug), each lasting 1 min or less.
- No MEAC is measured or reported. No concentration-effect relationship is fitted: PD was recorded against dose, not concentration.
- ke0 / t1/2ke0 / tPeak: not estimated here. The simulations used a ke0 "from our previous work" (ref 10, Egan 1996 remifentanil vs alfentanil; value not given in this paper), targeting effect-site 3-6 ng/mL "associated with analgesia and spontaneous ventilation" (ref 9, Egan 2001, EEG-clinical potency; blood not plasma). Simulated effect-site ranges: 100 ug then 50 ug every 2.5 min gave about 3 to 5.5 ng/mL; 100 ug then 25 ug every 1.5 min gave just over 3 to just over 4 ng/mL (Fig 4).
- Potency ratio: none stated.
- Assay matrix: whole-blood concentrations (arterial blood, esterase-stabilised).

## Covariates
Age group (under/over 60) was compared for PD only; no covariate in the PK model, and older subjects did not contribute concentrations. Weight not used (fixed ug doses).

## Caveats
- PK from only the subjects given 75 ug or more, sparse after the first minutes (concentrations fall below assay limit quickly); first-minute over-prediction.
- Healthy volunteers breathing room air; algometry is a single pain model; the authors say repeat-bolus safety was not studied.
- Text-layer units are corrupted ("mg" for ug), so do not trust the text layer for doses.
