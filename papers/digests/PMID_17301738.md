# Hamberg 2007: warfarin PK-PD with CYP2C9, VKORC1 and age

## Study
150 Italian patients on long-term warfarin (Thrombosis Center, Padova) from two studies (Table 1, PDF p2):
Study I, n = 57 (15 women; median age 72 [46-87] y, weight 81 [53-120] kg), a single 10 mg racemic dose with S/R
samples at 12, 36 and 60 h and INR at 0, 12, 36, 60 h, then one steady-state sample; Study II, n = 93 (36 women; age 70
[22-84], weight 74 [45-110]), one steady-state sample. Overall median age 71 (22-87) y, weight 80 (45-120) kg, median
maintenance dose 29.375 mg/week (6.25-78.75), steady-state INR 2.28 (1.36-4.37). Steady-state samples assumed at 13 h
after dose. CYP2C9 (all): *1/*1 88, *1/*2 26, *1/*3 25, *2/*2 5, *2/*3 4, *3/*3 2. VKORC1 -1639G>A (Study I only):
GG 22, GA 23, AA 11 (one missing, handled as GG). Total S- and R-warfarin by HPLC (LOD 0.005 and 0.01 mg/mL as
printed). NONMEM VIb, FOCE-I, log-transformed data; PK fitted first, PD on the individual predicted S concentrations
(sequential). PD database: Study I only, 228 single-dose and 56 steady-state INRs from 57 patients.

## Model and parameters
S-warfarin: two compartments, first-order absorption (ADVAN4), ka fixed at 2 /h (no absorption-phase data; 0.5-5
tested). CL_S = theta x (1 + theta_age (AGE - 71)) x (1 - theta_genotype) (Eqs 2-3). R-warfarin: one compartment
(ADVAN2), ka fixed at 2 /h, CL_R with the same age form (Eq 6). Weight and sex tested, not significant. Apparent (oral)
parameters; dose racemic.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: INR. E = 1 - Emax C_S^gamma / (EC50^gamma + C_S^gamma) drives two parallel chains (Eq 8, PDF p10):
dA1/dT = ktr1 (1 - E-term) - ktr1 A1, A2-A6 transit with ktr1; dA7/dT = ktr2 (1 - E-term) - ktr2 A7; initial values 1;
ktr1 = 1/MTT1 and ktr2 = 1/MTT2 as printed (per compartment, not n/MTT). INR = BASE + INRmax (1 - A6 A7)^lambda (Eq 10),
BASE the patient's observed baseline INR. S-warfarin only: adding R (additive or competitive) not significant
(P > 0.05). VKORC1 on EC50 the only PD covariate (dOFV 16.4); age and sex tested on EC50. Table 5 (PDF p5) predicted
doses for steady-state INR 2.5: 9.08 mg/day (50 y, *1/*1, GG) to 0.47 mg/day (90 y, *3/*3, AA); for *2/*3 with AA,
1.35 / 1.14 / 0.94 mg/day at 50 / 70 / 90 y. No ke0; the delay is the transit chains.

## Covariates
CYP2C9 diplotype and age on CL_S; age on CL_R; VKORC1 on EC50. Weight, sex not significant on PK; body size "could not be
justified" though tested.

## Caveats
PD from 57 patients of Study I only (VKORC1 data only there; the 2 *3/*3 and 4 *2/*3 patients are all in Study II, so the PD fit includes none); no induction-phase
INR beyond 60 h after a single dose, so the induction curves of Figure 3 are extrapolations the authors flag; no
absorption data (ka fixed); steady-state sampling times assumed; INRmax fixed; the authors caution against mechanistic
interpretation of the chains. omega V2 99% and omega MTT2 102% very large.
