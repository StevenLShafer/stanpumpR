# Kunisawa 2015 (J Anesth): landiolol TCI and population PK in gynaecologic patients

## Study
- Nine women (Table 1, PDF p4: median 55 years, 37-71; 62.8 kg, 47.1-73.0; lean body mass 43.1 kg; creatinine 0.55 mg/dL; cholinesterase 303 IU/L) having gynaecologic surgery, ASA 1-2; inclusion 18-80 years and under 80 kg (UMIN000007034). Received 1 February 2014, published online September 2014.
- Propofol (Diprifusor) and fentanyl TCI, with a thoracolumbar epidural catheter. Landiolol hydrochloride by STANPUMP TCI using Honda 2008's parameters without the lag, to plasma targets of 500 then 1,000 ng/mL for 30 min each, through an 18-G forearm cannula.
- Radial arterial samples at 1, 2, 5 and 25 min after each start or change and 1, 2, 5, 10, 15 and 20 min after the stop: 1 mL of whole blood into chilled ethanol and neostigmine with EDTA (esterases stopped); plasma by HPLC with fluorescence (Suno). 126 concentrations.
- NONMEM. Two compartments with a lag; three did not converge. Weight, lean body mass and age tested, none retained.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Heart rate fell significantly 2 min after the start and stayed below baseline until 20 min after the end; blood pressure did not change significantly (Fig. 3). No concentration-effect model.

## Covariates
Weight, lean body mass and age tested; none retained (nine patients).

## Caveats
- n = 9, all women; Q's standard error equals its estimate.
- The residual error (38.1%) is large against the tiny IIV, so the between-patient variability is mostly absorbed in the residual.
- Plasma with esterases stopped, as in Kunisawa's 2015 peripheral arterial disease study (PMID 25653534).
