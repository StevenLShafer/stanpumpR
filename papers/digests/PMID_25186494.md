# Kunisawa 2014/2015: landiolol TCI and population PK in gynecologic patients

(Published online 2014, in print J Anesth 2015; the repo header calls it "Kunisawa 2014".)

## Study
- Nine women (Table 1, PDF p. 4; printed p. 201) having gynecologic surgery, ASA 1-2, aged 18-80, weight < 80 kg. Median age 55 y (37-71), weight 62.8 kg (47.1-73.0), lean body mass 43.1 kg (34.6-47.0), creatinine clearance 111 mL/min (87.4-166, Cockcroft-Gault), albumin 4.2 g/dL, cholinesterase 303 IU/L (272-338). Exclusions: arrhythmia, alpha-methyldopa, clonidine, beta-blockers.
- Anesthesia: propofol TCI (BIS 40-60), fentanyl TCI to effect-site 2 ng/mL (STANPUMP, Shafer parameters), vecuronium, epidural ropivacaine.
- Landiolol hydrochloride by STANPUMP-driven Harvard pump 22 using the Honda 2008 healthy-volunteer 2-compartment parameters (lag omitted because STANPUMP cannot take one; the predicted curve is therefore shifted 0.820 min early, Fig 1/2 legends). Targets: 500 ng/mL for 30 min then 1,000 ng/mL for 30 min (about 50% and 100% of the concentration at the highest clinical dose, p. 199).
- Sampling (arterial, 20-G radial): 1, 2, 5, 25 min after each start/change and 1, 2, 5, 10, 15, 20 min after stopping. Whole blood into chilled ethanol plus neostigmine with EDTA (to stop pseudocholinesterase hydrolysis); plasma by centrifuge, stored at -20 C. HPLC with fluorescence detection (Suno 2009, ref 6). Matrix: plasma. 126 concentrations total.
- Analysis: NONMEM V, FOCE with interaction; 1-, 2- and 3-compartment models tried; covariates body weight, lean body mass, age tested stepwise (delta OFV > 3.84). Validation: VPC (1,000 simulations), CWRES, MDPE/MDAPE versus the Honda model.

## Model and parameters
Two compartments with a lag time (AIC 1381.442 for 2-compartment versus 1382.897 for 1-compartment, both with lag; 3-compartment did not converge). Lag judged necessary. No covariate retained (weight, LBM, age; labs not tested as covariates). Parameters are per kg as printed; the paper names no basis (weight) beyond "mL/kg", presumably total body weight (not stated).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No concentration-effect model, no ke0, tPeak, EC50 or MEAC. Heart rate (Fig 3, p. 5) fell significantly from the 2-minute reading (first sample after start) and stayed below baseline to 20 min after stopping; SBP/DBP did not change significantly (patients were on epidural and fentanyl; ephedrine 8.9 +/- 10.8 mg and phenylephrine 0.21 +/- 0.15 mg given). No atropine needed. Not an opioid paper; no potency ratios.

## Covariates
None retained (weight, lean body mass, age tested). Mild lab abnormalities in 1 of 9 not modelled. All women, so no sex effect testable.

## Caveats
- n = 9, all women, normal sinus rhythm, not tachycardic; authors note PK may differ in a clinical setting and that landiolol PK may be slightly non-linear (the 1,000 ng/mL target was under-predicted).
- Q very poorly determined; the Q/V2 combination differs strongly from Honda's.
- Authors state Honda's model under-predicted concentrations, attributing it to lower V1 here.
- Lag time is fitted but not usable in STANPUMP; authors drew curves shifted early.
- Authors (Ono Pharmaceutical employees ran the PK analysis) disclose industry ties.
