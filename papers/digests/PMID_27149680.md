# Bapat 2016: apixaban transfer across the dually perfused human placenta

**Gideon Koren is a co-author.** Under the repository's rule (`AGENTS.md:161`, Shafer 2026-11-16) no paper he
co-authored is cited or used; the audit of 2026-11-16 removed this one from the repository. This card records the
paper's content for the archive only.

## Study
Ex vivo dual perfusion of a single placental cotyledon (Hospital for Sick Children / St Michael's Hospital,
Toronto). Term placentae (> 38 weeks) from healthy singleton pregnancies, caesarean or vaginal delivery, mothers on no
medication. Of 25 placentae screened, 5 cotyledons from 5 placentae were perfused successfully (PDF p. 3 / printed
1438): maternal age 33.4 ± 6.5 y, gestational age 39.6 ± 0.9 wk, cotyledon weight 24.21 ± 10.32 g (mean ± SD);
maternal and fetal flows 12.84 ± 0.40 and 2.63 ± 0.40 mL/min.

- Perfusate: M199 medium with dextran (maternal 7.5 g/L, fetal 30.0 g/L), glucose, heparin, kanamycin; **no plasma
  proteins**. Antipyrine 1 mM as a passive-diffusion marker (PDF p. 2 / 1437).
- 1-h control phase, then apixaban added to the maternal reservoir at 150 ng/mL (stated as within the therapeutic
  range, refs. 18-19); samples at 0, 10, 20, 30, 60, 90, 120, 150, 180 min.
- Assay: UHPLC-MS/MS (AB Sciex QTrap 5500), LOD 1.4 and LOQ 4.4 ng/mL, intra- and inter-day CV both 10%.

## Model and parameters
No pharmacokinetic model of the mother. Results (PDF p. 3, Fig. 1 on p. 4), median (IQR):

- Maternal concentration at experiment start 108 ng/mL (98.7-115), below the 150 ng/mL added (loss in the system,
  e.g. tissue binding); it fell further over the first hour and then held roughly steady.
- At 180 min: fetal concentration 39.0 ng/mL (36.8-40.6); fetal-to-maternal ratio 0.77 (0.76-0.81).
- Antipyrine F:M 0.96 (0.86-0.99) at 3 h; viability markers (Table 1, p. 4) did not change significantly
  (P 0.20-0.83).
- Predicted in vivo F:M of **total** apixaban by the Garland equation with Hill-Abramson binding adjustment, taking
  binding as 87% in non-pregnant adults and the ionisation term as 1: 0.90 if albumin is the sole binder
  ([P]F/[P] 0.866, [P]M/[P] 0.733), 0.35 if alpha-1 acid glycoprotein is (0.37, 1.0) (PDF pp. 3-4). The authors favour
  0.90, apixaban being mainly albumin-bound.
- Background figures quoted from the product monograph (ref. 8): peak 3-4 h after a tablet, plasma protein binding
  87-93%.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None measured. The discussion notes fetal and neonatal factor X at 20-40% of adult levels (refs. 28-29) and suggests
coagulation monitoring of exposed infants. Not an opioid paper; no MEAC, ke0 or potency ratio.

## Covariates
None.

## Caveats
- n = 5 term cotyledons; first- and second-trimester transfer not addressed (authors).
- Protein-free perfusate: the 0.77 ratio is for unbound drug, and the in vivo prediction rests on assumed binding
  proteins and non-pregnant binding ratios; the two scenarios span 0.35-0.90.
- Static system without maternal elimination; it does not reproduce time-varying in vivo concentrations (authors).
- Apixaban is a P-gp and BCRP substrate (ref. 32); a rat study found fetal blood about 35% of maternal (ref. 35).
- Co-authored by Gideon Koren (see top).
