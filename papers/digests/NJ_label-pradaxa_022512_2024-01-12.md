# Pradaxa label 2023: dabigatran etexilate capsules (NDA 022512)

## Study
Not a study: US prescribing information for dabigatran etexilate capsules (75, 110, 150 mg), Revised 11/2023
(PDF p1 and last page), posted by FDA 2024-01-12. Initial US approval 2010. Sources inside the label: RE-LY
(AF), RE-COVER (DVT/PE), RE-NOVATE / RE-NOVATE II (hip replacement), a single-dose 150 mg renal-impairment study,
DIVERSITY and Study 2 (paediatrics), healthy-volunteer interaction studies.

Adult dosing (section 2.2, PDF p3-4):
- AF: CrCl > 30 mL/min 150 mg BID; CrCl 15-30 mL/min 75 mg BID; CrCl < 15 or dialysis: none; CrCl 30-50 with
  dronedarone or systemic ketoconazole 75 mg BID; CrCl < 30 with P-gp inhibitors: avoid.
- DVT/PE treatment (after 5-10 days parenteral anticoagulation) and recurrence reduction: CrCl > 30 mL/min 150 mg BID.
- Hip replacement prophylaxis: CrCl > 30 mL/min 110 mg 1-4 h after surgery once haemostasis is achieved, then
  220 mg once daily for 28-35 days (220 mg daily from the start if not begun on the day of surgery).
- Paediatric capsules 8 to < 18 y by weight (Table 1, PDF p4): 75 to 260 mg BID across six weight bands.
- Stopping before surgery (2.8, PDF p5, rendered): 1-2 days (CrCl >= 50) or 3-5 days (CrCl < 50); longer for
  major surgery, spinal puncture or neuraxial catheter. Switching to a parenteral anticoagulant: wait 12 h
  (CrCl >= 30) or 24 h (CrCl < 30) after the last dose (2.7).

## Model and parameters
No model. Section 12.3 (PDF p16-17, rendered); PK refers to dabigatran plus its active acyl glucuronides:
- Dose-proportional 10 to 400 mg; accumulation factor about 2 with BID dosing.
- Absolute oral bioavailability about 3% to 7%; Tmax 1 h fasted; high-fat meal delays Tmax about 2 h, no change
  in bioavailability. Opened capsule (pellets without shell) +75% bioavailability; oral pellets +37% vs capsules at
  steady state in adults.
- Protein binding about 35%; RBC:plasma (total radioactivity) < 0.3; Vd 50 to 70 L.
- Renal clearance 80% of total after IV dosing; t1/2 12 to 17 h in healthy adults; 12 to 14 h in paediatrics (popPK simulation).
- Steady-state geometric mean trough: 97.9 ng/mL (10th-90th 63.7-151) in paediatric patients 8-18 y on capsules;
  59.7 ng/mL (26.3-146) in adults with DVT/PE.
- Table 10 (single 150 mg dose, PDF p17, rendered):

| Renal function | CrCl (mL/min) | AUC | Cmax | t1/2 (h) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Normal | >= 80 | 1x | 1x | 13 |
| Mild | 50-80 | 1.5x | 1.1x | 15 |
| Moderate | 30-50 | 3.2x | 1.7x | 18 |
| Severe | 15-30 | 6.3x | 2.1x | 27 |

- Haemodialysis (section 10): about 49% removed over 4 h at blood flow 200 mL/min, about 57% at 300 mL/min
  (dialysate 700 mL/min); 7% to 15% redistribution afterwards.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- No therapeutic range. Section 2.4 (PDF p5, rendered): in adults the extent of anticoagulation generally does
  not need to be assessed; when necessary use aPTT or ECT, not INR.
- RE-LY 150 mg: median (10th-90th) trough aPTT 52 (40-76) s; trough ECT 63 (44-103) s (12.2, PDF p16, rendered).
  aPTT measured centrally (Roche PTT reagent); other methods may differ.
- Figure 2 (PDF p16): simulated mean aPTT time courses by CrCl band over 96 h after stopping, no CIs; the label
  says no advice can be given on the aPTT recovery needed for any clinical setting.
- dTT and ECT rise linearly with concentration; aPTT nonlinearly (paediatric data, 12.2). INR insensitive.
- Hip surgery: aPTT Emax and baseline aPTT higher shortly after surgery than at >= 3 days (12.2).
- DIVERSITY paediatric trial (14.4): concentration measured before the 7th dose, one titration allowed to a
  target of 50-250 ng/mL; this is a trial titration target, not a labelled therapeutic range.
- No QTc prolongation up to 600 mg. Neuraxial (5.3): exact time to a sufficiently low effect is unknown.

## Covariates
Renal function (Table 10; dosing by CrCl, Cockcroft-Gault not specified in the parts read). P-gp inhibitors
raise exposure (dronedarone, ketoconazole, Figure 3.1); rifampicin lowers it. Moderate hepatic impairment: no
consistent change. Paediatric renal function by Schwartz eGFR = 0.413 x height (cm) / SCr (mg/dL).

## Caveats
- Severe renal impairment doses rest on PK modelling, not trial data (Table 10 footnote).
- Figure 2 curves are simulations of mean values; Figures 3.1/3.2 point estimates are graphical and were not transcribed.
- Label figures for Vd and t1/2 are ranges without variability.
