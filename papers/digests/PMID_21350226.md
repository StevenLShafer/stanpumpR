# Absalom 2011: Closed loop anesthesia editorial

## Study
Editorial accompanying Liu et al. (Anesth Analg 2011;112:546-57), a randomized multicenter comparison of a BIS-guided dual-loop PID system co-administering propofol and remifentanil with manual control. No data of its own. PDF pages 1-3 (printed 516-518).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Not an opioid PK/PD study; no MEAC, ke0, t1/2 ke0, tPeak or potency ratio is given (audit: none). Qualitative points: (1) The Schnider propofol and Minto remifentanil models supply ke0 values assuming all plasma-effect hysteresis is a transfer delay, so effect is path- and state-independent and symmetric; the authors say this may be false. (2) Cited evidence: sevoflurane/spectral entropy EC50 and slope differ between induction and recovery; brain concentrations at loss and return of consciousness differed in an animal study (neural inertia). (3) In Liu et al.'s dual-loop arm, remifentanil target concentrations were commonly above 10 ng/mL (printed p. 517, described as outside many clinicians' comfort range). (4) Controller rule: BIS error above 4 changes both drugs, with remifentanil adjusted more often; error between 2 and 4 changes only remifentanil. (5) The choice of propofol PK model is called controversial.
