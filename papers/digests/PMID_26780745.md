# Smythe 2016: Anticoagulation Forum guidance on heparin, LMWH and fondaparinux for VTE

## Study
Consensus guidance document (Anticoagulation Forum), not a primary study. Authors posed practical questions per
agent (Table 1, PDF p2), reviewed literature (PubMed/Medline, emphasis on cohort studies and RCTs of the prior
10 years), and gave a consensus "Guidance Statement" for each; summarised in Table 5 (PDF p16-18). Adults with
VTE. PDF page = printed page − 164.

## Model and parameters
No PK model. Dosing and concentration statements:

- **UFH dosing** (Table 2, PDF p3): IV fixed 5000 units bolus then 1250-1280 units/h (Hull); IV weight-based
  80 units/kg then 18 units/kg/h (Raschke); SC fixed 333 units/kg then 250 units/kg q12h (Kearon, FIDO); SC adjusted
  5000 units IV then 17,500 units q12h adjusted to aPTT (Prandoni). Heparin Vd approximates blood volume, about
  60 mL/kg (PDF p4, printed 168). Total body weight suggested. Heparin resistance commonly defined as > 35,000
  units/day (PDF p6).
- **UFH monitoring/range** (PDF p5-6, printed 169-170): aPTT or anti-Xa; historical aPTT range 1.5-2.5 × control
  (Raschke 46-79 s = 1.5-2.3 × control); protamine titration 0.2-0.4 units/mL ≈ anti-Xa about 0.3-0.7 units/mL.
  Anti-Xa 0.3-0.7 U/mL is widely accepted despite limited data; the most recent ACCP guidelines specify no desired
  therapeutic range for VTE. Guidance: anti-Xa target 0.3-0.7 units/mL when anti-Xa monitoring is used. Check every
  6 h until 2 consecutive therapeutic values, then daily.
- **LMWH** (PDF p8-12): enoxaparin 1 mg/kg SC q12h preferred (1.5 mg/kg daily discouraged); 1 mg/kg daily if CrCl
  < 30 mL/min; avoid if CrCl < 20 mL/min or on RRT. Dalteparin 200 units/kg daily or 100 units/kg q12h. Total body
  weight, no dose capping. Peaks occur 3-5 h after a dose (measure at steady state). Target anti-Xa levels are not
  clinically validated. Observed enoxaparin peaks 0.6-1 IU/mL (twice daily) and > 1 IU/mL (once daily); bleeding
  increased with dalteparin infusion mean levels > 0.8 u/mL; in an ACS study troughs > 0.5 IU/mL were called
  elevated (PDF p11, printed 175, confirmed on page image). Routine anti-Xa monitoring not suggested. Protamine
  1 mg per 100 anti-Xa units (1 mg enoxaparin ≈ 100 anti-Xa units), max 50 mg, reverses 60-80% (PDF p12).
- **Fondaparinux** (PDF p13-15): < 50 kg 5 mg, 50-100 kg 7.5 mg, > 100 kg 10 mg SC daily (MATISSE); avoid if CrCl
  < 30 mL/min; clearance reduced 25% at CrCl 30-60 mL/min. PT/aPTT insensitive (PT +1.2 s at 0.8 μg/mL). Peak anti-Xa
  about 3 h after dosing. Target levels not established; treatment-dose peaks 0.6-1.5 μg/mL, typically 1.20-1.26
  μg/mL; troughs 0.46-0.62 μg/mL; these are observed values, not targets (PDF p14, printed 178, confirmed on page
  image). Assay must be chromogenic anti-Xa calibrated with fondaparinux.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End points discussed are recurrent VTE and major bleeding from cited trials; aPTT and anti-Xa are surrogate
monitoring measures. No concentration-effect model, EC50 or ke0. Not an opioid; no MEAC/potency data.

## Covariates
Total body weight (dosing basis for all three); renal function by Cockcroft-Gault CrCl (LMWH, fondaparinux);
obesity and low weight discussed qualitatively.

## Caveats
Consensus guidance with weak supporting evidence for most monitoring ranges, as the authors state; anti-Xa
ranges for LMWH and fondaparinux are observed values, not therapeutic targets. aPTT ranges are reagent-specific.
Funded in part by Boehringer Ingelheim, Daiichi Sankyo and Janssen (PDF p19).
