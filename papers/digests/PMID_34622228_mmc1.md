# Overgaard 2021b supplement (mmc1): oral semaglutide PK Table S1, NONMEM control streams

This file is the supplemental information alone (14 pages); `PMID_34622228_mmc2.pdf` is the article plus the same
supplement in one 28-page file. The article is digested in `PMID_34622228.md`. PDF pages: p. 1-2 title pages,
p. 3 Table S1, pp. 4-11 Figures S1-S5G (one figure per page, each with its legend), pp. 12-13 Methods S1 (oral model
control stream), pp. 13-14 Methods S2 (s.c. model control stream).

## Study
Supplement to a population PK and exposure-response analysis of oral semaglutide (Rybelsus tablets, 3, 7, 14 mg once
daily) in PIONEER 1, 2, 3, 5, 8 and 9 (type 2 diabetes), compared with s.c. semaglutide (SUSTAIN). Main text (see
`PMID_34622228_mmc2` for the page-checked numbers): 10,527 oral concentrations from 2,345 subjects, total
(protein-bound) semaglutide by LC-MS, LLOQ 0.729 nmol/L, 914 values below LLOQ set to LLOQ/2; NONMEM 7.3. One sample
per visit (week 4, 8, 14, 26 etc.), time of last dose recorded. Weight reference in the model 85 kg.

## Model and parameters
Two-compartment model with first-order oral absorption (NONMEM ADVAN4 TRANS4), prespecified from the clinical
pharmacology model; **only bioavailability and its covariates were estimated, every disposition parameter is fixed**
(Table S1 caption, PDF p. 3). Table S1 (PDF p. 3, read on the page image), final model, reference subject White
non-Hispanic female 18-64 y, 85 kg, no upper GI disease, 14 mg:

Derived in the control stream (Methods S1): AUC = BA x dose / CL x (1/MW); Cavg = AUC / 24 (nmol/L, daily dose).
Covariates multiply F as theta^indicator; weight enters as (BW/85)^theta, eGFR as (eGFR/100)^theta (fixed at 0).
Methods S1 initial values for the dropped covariates (age, Black race, Hispanic, eGFR, dose) are 1 and FIXED, i.e. not
in the final model; the final covariates were sex, Asian, body weight, GI disease and two trial effects.
Text-layer note: in the text extraction the `;`-comments of Methods S1 are shifted one line against their `$THETA`
values; the rendered page (PDF p. 12) has each comment on its own line, so use the image.

Methods S2 (s.c. model, ADVAN2 TRANS2, one compartment; not final estimates, these are `$THETA` initial values):
ka 0.0286 /h FIXED, CL/F 0.0479 L/h, V/F 5.7 L, weight exponent on CL/F 0.774, male 1.04, age 65-74 0.988,
age >74 0.960, 0.5 mg dose 1.0, Black 0.974, Asian 0.988, Hispanic 1.07, thigh 1.04, upper arm 1.08, renal impairment
mild/moderate/severe all 0.95; IIV omega CL 0.0165, V 0.162; sigma 0.05; Cavg = dose / CL / 168 h (text layer only).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No ke0, t1/2ke0, tPeak or hysteresis (steady-state Cavg exposure; weekly/daily dosing). Exposure-response figures only
(S2-S5): HbA1c change, proportion reaching HbA1c <7% and <=6.5%, % body weight change, nausea and vomiting versus
quantiles of Cavg at week 26, with fitted lines; covariates retained per end point stated in each legend (HbA1c:
baseline HbA1c, trial population; weight: baseline HbA1c, sex; nausea: trial population, sex, Hispanic, upper GI,
Asian; vomiting: trial population, sex). No EC50 or Emax values are printed in the supplement. No MEAC, no potency
ratio. Not an opioid.

## Covariates
On exposure (Figure S1, relative to the reference subject, 90% CIs): sex, Asian race, body weight (5th and 95th
percentiles 56 and 129 kg) and upper GI disease were significant; age, Black race, Hispanic ethnicity and renal
impairment were not. No numbers are printed on the figure.

## Caveats
Total, not free, semaglutide. The disposition parameters are borrowed and fixed from a clinical pharmacology model, so
Table S1's CI cover F and its covariates only. Sparse single samples at the investigator's chosen times. Table S1's
trial labels disagree with the text (open question). Methods S1/S2 are control streams, so quoted numerals that are
not FIX are starting values.
