# Jeong 2024: gender and levocetirizine PK/PD

## Study
Bioequivalence-derived data (control formulation only): 24 healthy Korean men and 16 women, single 5 mg levocetirizine tablet (UCB, lot 319826), plasma sampled, trial 25 May-12 June 2022 (PDF p3, p17). Inclusion BMI 18-30 kg/m2, weight over 50 kg (men) or 45 kg (women); renal function normal (CrCl 80-190 mL/min, eGFR 70-150 mL/min/1.73 m2, p15). NCA, then population PK in Phoenix NLME 8.4 (FOCE-ELS with eta-epsilon interaction). Assay and sampling details are in the supplement (not read). PD part uses external data from a 6-11 year old paediatric study (ref 12 in the paper): wheal and flare size after histamine challenge, fitted with an indirect-response (input inhibition) model linked to the central compartment; no Ce/ke0 effect compartment values appear in the PDF.

## Model and parameters
Two-compartment disposition (central-peripheral) with five sequential first-order absorption compartments (Ka1-Ka5) and lag time; log-additive residual error; exponential IIV on all parameters (PDF p7-8). Values stated in the text (apparent oral, per dose, absolute not per kg; PDF p8):

```yaml
  - {name: Vc/F, value: 1.07, unit: L, source: "text p8", note: typical value}

  - {name: Vp/F, value: 28.12, unit: L, source: "text p8", note: typical value}
  - {name: CLp/F, value: 13.80, unit: L/h, source: "text p8", note: "intercompartmental clearance, typical value"}
```

Covariates in the final model: BSA, albumin (negative) and gender on Vp/F; BSA on CLp/F; body weight on Ka5. Coefficients are only in Table S7 (not read). Simulation (Table 1, PDF p14, read on the page image), 5 mg, gender-mean covariates: single dose Cmax male 212.00, female 220.52 ng/mL; AUCall male 1624.90, female 1659.09 h.ng/mL; 24 h multiple dosing, steady state (216-240 h) AUCall 1702.49 both sexes. Wheal and flare changes are percent of baseline (100 = no inhibition), minimum value 0.77 (male) and 0.65 (female) for flare after a single dose.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: histamine-induced wheal and flare size (skin), indirect response model, children 6-11 y data. Not an opioid; no MEAC, no ke0/t half/tPeak reported (PD parameters are in Table S8, not read). Effect persisted at about 60-100% inhibition with 12 h interval dosing; recovery to baseline just before dose with 72 h interval (p13). Gender difference in flare inhibition at 24 h steady state was significant (p < 0.05), other comparisons not (p13).

## Covariates
See above. Gender effect: lower Vp/F in women, higher Cmax and faster plasma decline in women vs men in this same study population (NCA Table S2, simulation; comparator is the male group of the same trial). Renal and hepatic indices were not significant in a normal-function cohort. Label renal dosing quoted: 5 mg daily at CrCl 50-80, every 2 days at 30-50, every 3 days at 10-30 mL/min (p15).

## Caveats
Small n (24 M, 16 F), healthy Koreans, single dose, one formulation; PD data from children applied to adults (authors' own limitation, p16); five absorption compartments with Ka and Tlag IIV is heavily parameterised; PK parameters tabulated only in the supplement.
