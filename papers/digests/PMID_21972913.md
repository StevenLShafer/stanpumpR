# Adapa 2012: TCI pump safety and accuracy in a 3 T MRI scanner

## Study
Bench study, not a pharmacokinetic one. Two Alaris Asena PK and two Fresenius Injectomat TIVA Agilia TCI pumps ran propofol 1% (Marsh model; Agilia uses a modified Marsh) in a 3 T Siemens Verio scanner at the 10 G and 50 G field lines, with and without a T2 sequence (large RF pulses), pump parallel or transverse to the field (PDF p3-4, printed p34-35). Default patient 40 y, 70 kg, 180 cm; targets 0.5 and 6.0 ug/mL. Delivery measured gravimetrically through 8 m of tubing (propofol density 0.9985 mg/mL as printed) and compared with the pump display: cumulative error at 5-min intervals and 10-min interval error. Each figure in Tables 1-3 is the mean of 4 runs (2 per pump). Also tested: projectile force, MR image artefact, alarms, occlusion pressure limits (500 and 900 mmHg or L5/L10). Older systems (Graseby 3500, Fresenius Base Primea, Graseby 3500 driven by Stanpump) were tested outside the scanner with 2 m lines.

- Text: at 6 ug/mL pumps displayed 70.6 mL (Alaris) and 68.6 mL (Agilia) at 30 min, measured 70.1 and 68.3 mL; at 0.5 ug/mL displayed 5.9 mL, measured 5.77 and 5.76 mL. Volumes displayed by all pumps and by Stanpump, Rugloop and TIVATrainer agreed within 1% of the group mean (Marsh model).
- Safety: no deflection at 5, 10 or 50 G; a palpable force at about 450 G from the pole-clamp screw (absent when removed). Faint RF artefacts on PD/T2 and FLAIR images with Alaris only. Occlusion alarms triggered at lower pressures in the field (Figs 2-3). Authors recommend an MR Conditional rating to the 50 G line for this scanner.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No ke0, MEAC, EC50 or concentration end point; propofol targets of 0.5 and 6.0 ug/mL were chosen to represent sedation and anaesthesia, not measured effects. Not an opioid paper.

## Covariates
None. Fixed default patient (40 y, 70 kg, 180 cm).

## Caveats
Two runs per condition and two pumps per type; one scanner (field gradient is scanner-specific); time to occlusion alarm and post-occlusion bolus not measured; the paper's accuracy is of the pump's delivery against its own display and model, not against plasma concentration.
