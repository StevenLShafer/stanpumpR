# Arden 1986: etomidate PK/PD across age

## Study
21 ASA I-II surgical patients (20 men, 1 woman), 22-82 y, weight 81.9 ± 11.5 kg (p. 19). Etomidate IV infusion
5-10 mg/min (7.5 mg/min in the examples) until an EEG stage 3 pattern, then stopped; recovery about 20 min, then
thiopental/N2O/enflurane anaesthesia. Arterial whole-blood samples every 0.5 min during infusion, 1-3 min in
recovery, and at 20, 25, 30, 45, 60, 90, 120 min; venous at 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10, 12 h. HPLC on whole blood, sensitive
to 2 ng/mL, CV 5.4% at 5 ng/mL. PK in 20 patients, PD in 16 (17 rows in Table 1, one EEG only). Individual fits:
ELSFIT (extended least squares), 2- vs 3-compartment; three compartments preferred in all. EEG median frequency
(2.56 s epochs, 11-epoch moving mean) with an inhibitory sigmoid Emax effect-compartment model.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: EEG median frequency (Table 1, n = 16 analysed, means ± SD): T½ke0 **1.6 ± 0.5 min** (range 0.9-2.4;
text 0.92-2.4), IC50 **388 ± 144 ng/mL whole blood** (plasma equivalent 430 ± 160 ng/mL, p. 24), E0 9.2 ± 0.96 Hz,
Emax 7.2 ± 1.2 Hz, γ 3.8 ± 2.1. This is a t½ke0, not a time to peak effect; no tPeak is reported. Neither T½ke0 nor
IC50 depended on age. Discussion cites Fragen (307 ng/mL plasma at awakening) and Schüttler (IC50 310 ng/mL,
310 ng/mL at unresponsiveness).

## Covariates
Age: dose to stage 3 fell with age (r² = 0.68; 0.725 mg/kg at 22 y to 0.134 at 80 y); V1 fell with age (r = 0.56,
−42% from 22 to 80 y); clearance fell about 2 mL/kg/min per decade (r² = 0.28). Only correlation coefficients are
given: the authors deliberately published no age equation. Weight: no correlation with dose requirement.

## Caveats
Individual (two-stage) estimates, small n, almost all men; whole-blood assay; infusion over 3-9 min so V1 is shaped
by distribution during infusion; PD measured under succinylcholine for myoclonus in some patients; hypertension and
tachycardia treated with nitroprusside/propranolol.
