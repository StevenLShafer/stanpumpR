# Dolder 2017: oral LSD pharmacokinetics and PK-PD in healthy subjects

## Study
Pooled analysis of two double-blind, placebo-controlled, two-period crossover studies (washout >= 7 days; NCT02308969, NCT01878942) (PDF p. 2, Methods 2.1).
- Study 1: LSD 100 ug, n = 24 (12 M, 12 F), age 33 +/- 11 y (25-60), weight 68 +/- 8 kg (55-85). PK of this dose shown for the first time.
- Study 2: LSD 200 ug, n = 16 (8 M, 8 F), age 29 +/- 6 y (25-51), weight 72 +/- 12 kg (52-98). Previously reported (Dolder 2015, half-life 3.6 h by NCA); samples re-assayed with a more sensitive method and re-analysed here (PDF p. 2-3).
- Healthy volunteers, mostly hallucinogen naive (29 of 40). Drug: d-lysergic acid diethylamide hydrate (HPLC purity > 99%, Lipomed), single oral capsule of 100 or 200 ug (p. 3, 2.4). The paper does not say whether the nominal dose is expressed as hydrate or anhydrous base.
- Plasma (lithium heparin) at 0, 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2, 2.5, 3, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 16, 24 h; the 0.5, 1.5 and 2.5 h samples omitted in Study 1. LC-MS/MS, LLOQ 0.05 ng/mL (Study 1) and 0.01 ng/mL (Study 2), different laboratories (p. 3).
- Phoenix WinNonlin 6.4; individual (per-subject) compartmental fits, summarised as geometric means; not a population (mixed-effects) model.

## Model and parameters
One-compartment, first-order input, first-order elimination, **no lag time**; a two-compartment model did not relevantly improve the fit and had worse AIC in every subject (p. 4, 2.6). ## Pharmacodynamics and end points

Emax units are the end point's own (VAS %, beats/min, degrees C, mm Hg; the column heading says %); upper Emax bounds were 100% (VAS), 100/min (HR), 2 C, 50 and 80 mm Hg (p. 4). t1/2ke0 is the mean of individual ln2/ke0, so it is not ln2 / mean ke0. "Bad drug effect" could not be fitted in 8 (33%) and 5 (31%) subjects; vital signs in 1-3 subjects per outcome (p. 8).
- Effect-time (model-predicted "any drug effect", 10% threshold): 100 ug onset 0.8 +/- 0.4 h, offset 9.0 +/- 2.0 h, duration 8.2 +/- 2.1 h, time to peak 2.8 +/- 0.8 h; 200 ug onset 0.4 +/- 0.3 h, offset 11.6 +/- 4.2 h, duration 11.2 +/- 4.2 h, peak 2.5 +/- 1.2 h (Results 3.2, PDF p. 6).
- Moderate counterclockwise hysteresis, no clockwise hysteresis (no acute tolerance). Across subjects within a dose group, Cmax did not correlate with peak "any drug effect" (r = 0.38, p = 0.08 at 100 ug; r = 0, p = 0.9 at 200 ug); pooled r = 0.38, p < 0.05, n = 40 (p. 8).
- Discussion (p. 10): using k and ke0, an IV bolus is predicted to give peak "any drug effect" at about 70 and 50 min (100 and 200 ug parameters). No MEAC, no potency ratio (not an opioid).

## Covariates
None in the model. Multiple regression of effects on concentration, body weight and sex found no association (p. 8).

## Caveats
- Abstract versus Results: the abstract gives subjective effect duration 11.6 +/- 1.7 h for 200 ug; Results 3.2 (PDF p. 6) give duration 11.2 +/- 4.2 h and offset 11.6 +/- 4.2 h. The abstract's half-life CI (2.2-3.4 h) is the 200 ug arm's; the 100 ug arm's is 2.4-3.0 h (Table 1).
- Two doses in different subjects, assayed in different laboratories with different LLOQ (authors' limitations).
- Geometric means of individual fits, not population typical values; 100 ug ranges are wide (V 24-270 L, CL/F 5.2-103 L/h).
- Authors state their model predicts lower Cmax than observed, and the 200 ug concentrations here are lower than in their 2015 report (p. 9). The 2.6 h half-life is shorter than the 3.6 h NCA value of the same 200 ug subjects.
- Elimination stated to be first order up to 12 h only.
