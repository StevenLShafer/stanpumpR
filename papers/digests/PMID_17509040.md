# Berges 2007: anti-Xa population PK of prophylactic enoxaparin in patients over 75 (PROPHRE.75)

Page convention: printed pp. 428-438 = PDF pp. 1-11 (PDF page = printed page − 427).

## Study
Prospective cohort at the University Hospital of Saint-Etienne (internal medicine, geriatrics, orthopaedic
anaesthesia); consecutive patients over 75 needing VTE prophylaxis, enoxaparin 4000 IU SC once daily. 189
patients; mean treatment 7 days; 451 anti-Xa activities (mean 2.4 per patient), 56 below LOQ excluded; most
samples at trough (printed p. 430 / PDF p. 3). Assay Biophen heparin chromogenic, LOQ 0.05 IU/mL, interassay CV
<2.5% (printed p. 429). Table 1 (printed p. 431 / PDF p. 4, rendered): age 82 ± 5 y (75-95), median 81; women
62%; weight 66 ± 14 kg (38-108), 22% under 55 kg; obese 8%; acute medical 63%, orthopaedic surgery 15%, stroke
22%; CrCl Cockcroft-Gault 52 ± 17 mL/min (24-93), 50% under 50; CrCl simplified MDRD 69 ± 20 mL/min (27-127),
18% under 50. Source errors in Table 1, on the rendered page: the serum creatinine row prints "52 ± 17 ml min-1
(24-93)", the Cockcroft-Gault values with clearance units, so no creatinine value is reported. The abstract says
22% weighed <50 kg; Table 1 says <55 kg (abstract p. 428 vs Table 1 p. 431; unresolved which is right).
One major bleed (intracranial, 0.53%), three symptomatic thromboses (1.59%).

NONMEM V (5.1), FOCE interaction; covariate screening by regression, trees and GAM; entry ΔOFV > 3.84, backward
elimination at P < 0.01 (ΔOFV 6.6). VPC from 200 simulated datasets (Figure 2).

## Model and parameters
Two-compartment, first-order input, log-normal IIV, proportional residual error; an endogenous anti-Xa term
over-parameterised the model; block CL-V2. Table 2 (printed p. 432 / PDF p. 5, rendered) shows gender on CL was
entered in forward selection but dropped in backward elimination ("No"); the final model has none. Table 3
(printed p. 434 / PDF p. 7, rendered):

CL (L/h) = 0.70 × (wt/65)^0.78 × (ClCr/69)^0.25; V2 (L) = 6.43 × (wt/65)^1.25

The text-layer version of Table 3 shifts every value down one row (it puts 0.78 against theta1 and 8.18 against
Q); the rendered page gives the assignments above. Units: dose in anti-Xa IU, activity in IU/mL, so the volumes
are in litres of IU/L ÷ 1000 (the paper does not state the conversion). Derived at the reference patient (my
calculation): disposition half-lives 3.9 h and 27.2 h, the slow one 10.6% of bolus amplitude and 45% of AUC;
Vss 14.6 L; a single 4000 IU dose peaks near 0.39 IU/mL at about 2.9 h.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD model. Individual Bayesian peak activity 3-5 h after injection: 0.10-1.20 IU/mL, median 0.44; 29% of
patients had a peak >0.5, 10% >0.8, 4% (n = 7, mean 50 kg, mean MDRD 74 mL/min) >1.0 IU/mL; the patient with the
major bleed had a peak of 0.35 IU/mL at 4 h (printed p. 431). The 1.0 IU/mL bleeding threshold is called
empirical, defined without clinical evaluation (printed p. 434, ref 37). Simulated terminal half-life difference
between a 45 kg woman with CrCl 25 and a 75 kg man with CrCl 69 (MDRD) is under 1.5 h (printed p. 434). The
authors conclude routine anti-Xa monitoring is not needed in this setting.

## Covariates
Weight and simplified-MDRD creatinine clearance on CL; weight on V2. Serum creatinine alone was not significant;
Cockcroft-Gault entered but MDRD replaced it (Table 2). IIV fell from 30% to 26% (CL) and 28% to 15% (V2). The
paper cites Levey 1999 and 2000 (refs 16-17) for MDRD, does not print the coefficient, and reports MDRD in mL/min
without saying whether it de-indexed from 1.73 m^2. Table 4 (printed p. 436) compares eight earlier enoxaparin
models (mostly curative dosing).

## Caveats
Very sparse data (under 3 samples per patient, mostly troughs); the model "did not properly capture" the first 48
h (printed p. 431). Single dose level, elderly, mostly medical patients. Q and Ka without IIV. V3 IIV 93%.
