# Nieuwenhuijs 2003: remifentanil-propofol interaction on ventilation, haemodynamics and BIS

## Study
- 22 healthy male volunteers, 19-25 yr (PDF p1). Arterial catheter; EEG/BIS (A-2000, version 3.3).
- Remifentanil alone, propofol alone (separate sessions, 1 h rest between), then combinations, all at steady-state target-controlled concentrations (propofol: Psion palm-top, three-compartment set; remifentanil: Remifusor). Some subjects had 2-3 combination experiments. 94 responses in total.
- Hypercapnic ventilatory response by dynamic end-tidal forcing (3-8 PETCO2 steps of 3-19 mmHg, each at least 8 min, PETO2 120 mmHg); V'i = S x (PETCO2 - B). Studies began about 10 min after targets were reached, said to be more than 5-10 equilibration half-lives, so brain and blood were assumed in equilibrium.
- Arterial **blood** concentrations (propofol HPLC after potassium oxalate; remifentanil tandem MS after citric-acid esterase inactivation). Measured ranges: remifentanil 0-2 ng/mL; propofol 0-2.0 ug/mL (one value 2.6).
- Variables: resting inspired ventilation V'i, resting PETCO2, CO2 slope S, ventilation at PETCO2 55 mmHg (V'55), MAP, HR, BIS. NONMEM population response-surface fits (Minto interaction function, spline I(Q)); 15 models compared by AIC with a 1000-sample bootstrap.

## Model and parameters
Single-drug effect E = E0 x [1 - (C/C50)^gamma] (eq 2; gamma < 1 allowed, so apnoea occurs at a finite concentration); combined via Loewe-type U = Cr/C50,r + Cp/C50,p with interaction I(Q), reparameterised by lambda, the percent change at the mid-range concentrations Ch,r = 1 ng/mL and Ch,p = 1 ug/mL. Model 7 (nonlinear, symmetric synergy, Qmax 0.5, gamma_r = gamma_p) was chosen most often for all respiratory variables (Table 1, PDF p5: V'i 531, V'55 431, PETCO2 357, S 357 of 1000 bootstraps). Heart rate: model 2 (240) / model 7 (210) close; MAP: model 1 (664, additive linear).

Derived values quoted in the text: combined 1 ng/mL remifentanil + 1 ug/mL propofol gives 58% depression of resting V'i (additive expectation about 36%, derived by simple addition of 28% and 13%), 82% of V'55, 23% rise of PETCO2 and 69% of S. Model predicts apnoea at, for example, 1.6 ng/mL remifentanil with 2.0 ug/mL propofol (Fig 5 caption).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Opioid audit (remifentanil):
- **MEAC / analgesic concentration:** none; this is a respiratory/cardiovascular study without any analgesic end point. The text (printed 321) compares: the V'55 C50 (0.7 ng/mL) is 4-5 times smaller than the C50 for 50% probability of adequate anaesthesia in abdominal surgery with 66% N2O (Drover 1998, as quoted), and 10 times smaller than the C50 for spectral-edge EEG changes (Minto 1997), i.e. ventilation is more sensitive to remifentanil than analgesia or EEG.
- **ke0 / t1/2ke0 / tPeak / hysteresis:** none estimated. The study is steady state; the authors say the time course of respiratory effects is not addressed. Equilibration was assumed after about 10 min, justified by 5-10 times the remifentanil and propofol blood-effect-site half-lives (no value given).
- **Potency ratio:** remifentanil is stated to be about 80-100 times more potent than alfentanil for depressing V'55 (refs 6, 26: Dahan 2001 alfentanil-sevoflurane, Babenco 2000), not measured here.
- End points and shape: remifentanil shifts the V'i-CO2 curve in parallel (about 12 mmHg at 1 ng/mL in Fig 2's example subject) with little slope change; propofol reduces the slope (66% at 1.5 ug/mL in that subject). BIS is unaffected by remifentanil up to 2 ng/mL, even with propofol; propofol lowers BIS linearly, 19% at 1 ug/mL (text on printed 317 also says 25% decrease at 1.4 ug/mL).
- Concentrations are arterial blood, remifentanil ng/mL and propofol ug/mL; in this paper's modelling they are treated as the effect-site concentrations at equilibrium.

## Covariates
None modelled (22 young men). The authors note earlier work with morphine suggesting sex differences in slope depression; no covariate equation.

## Caveats
- Concentration range is below the C50s, so C50s are extrapolated (hence lambda parameterisation); they must not be read as measured potencies.
- Healthy male volunteers aged 19-25, steady state only, with no stimulation; extrapolation to patients, to women, to bolus/non-steady-state dosing is untested (authors' own caveat).
- Gamma and C50 estimates are tied to the model with equal gamma for both drugs, imposed by AIC choice. Model selection used boundary-failure runs as "not supported", which biases toward simpler models (authors state this).
- Table 2 text-layer values are scrambled; the rendered page was used for the transcription above.
