# Weber 2021: Gas Man 4.2 parameters and decrement times in simulated obesity

## Study
- Simulation only (Gas Man 4.2), no subjects. Models of 70, 100, 125, 150 kg built by adding fat to the 70 kg standard; fat flow
  scaled as F_fat,new = F_fat,old x (V_fat,new / V_fat,old)^0.75 (Eq. 1); CO = 70 kg CO plus excess fat flow.
- Desflurane, sevoflurane, isoflurane held at 1.0 MAC alveolar for 1-19 h (228 simulations), then open circuit, FGF 10 L/min,
  inspired 0; times to 67% (awakening), 90% (recovery), 95% (resolution) decrement in the VRG.
- Structure (Methods p344): a flow-limited four-compartment mammillary model (alveolar gas, VRG, muscle group, fat group)
  connected to the circle system.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
MAC values only (Table 1, no age stated). VRG used as CNS surrogate; 67% decrement chosen to approximate MAC-awake (one-third MAC
for isoflurane and desflurane). Awakening times: desflurane about 3:57-4:30, sevoflurane 5:19-6:42, isoflurane 6:03-10:12 min:s
(Results/Table 3). Recovery 8-31 (des), 11-95 (sevo), 16-260 (iso) min; resolution 13-130, 19-247, 64-584 min.

## Covariates
Weight via added fat only; VRG and muscle unchanged with weight in these obese models (contrast: Gas Man's own weight scaling of a
"big" patient is not used here).

## Caveats
Simulation of an unvalidated-for-obesity model; allometric fat-flow scaling acknowledged as without physiological evidence; no
inter-tissue diffusion; Gas Man 4.2 limits runs to 20 h; nitrous oxide not tabulated. Table 1 isoflurane tissue/blood values are
inconsistent with its own blood/gas value (see above).
