# Schmitt 2009: cystatin C carboplatin clearance formula (the "modified Thomas formula")

## Study
- Prospective multicentre study, 10 French cancer centres. 357 adults on carboplatin under established
  protocols: 70 on carboplatin alone, the rest with paclitaxel (194), etoposide (31), gemcitabine (17) or
  others. Primary sites: ovary 156, uterus 44, lung 36, head and neck 15, other 106 (PDF p2, printed p3634).
- Table 1 (PDF p3, printed p3635; page image), mean (range): age 60 (21-87) y; weight 65 (40-137) kg; BSA
  1.70 (1.30-2.37) m2 (DuBois); Scr 77 (25-433) µmol/L; serum cystatin C 0.90 (0.44-3.49) mg/L; Cockcroft-Gault
  CrCl 80 (10-216) mL/min; 264 female, 93 male. The rich-sampling database (143 patients; 88 F, 55 M) was age
  60 (23-84), weight 68 (40-112), BSA 1.75 (1.28-2.24), Scr 108 (55-353), CrCl 67 (14-168), cysC not available.
- Dosing: carboplatin by 30- or 60-min infusion in 5% dextrose, 170-1,600 mg (mean 557 mg). Doses were set by
  Calvert/Cockcroft-Gault, Chatelut or BSA, depending on the centre.
- Sampling (first cycle): before the dose, 5 min before the end of infusion, and 1 h and 4 h after the end.
  This is a limited sampling strategy, giving 1,057 concentrations from 0.44 to 69.16 mg/L (median 13.34)
  (printed p3635).
- Assay: plasma ultrafiltered (Amicon MPS1, YMT membrane, 4 °C), then platinum measured by flameless atomic
  absorption spectrophotometry in six laboratories. QC CVs were 4.30%, 2.26% and 7.18% at 100, 3,000 and
  90,000 ng/mL. That is, **ultrafilterable platinum**, not intact carboplatin by HPLC. Cystatin C was measured
  centrally by particle-enhanced nephelometric immunoassay (Dade-Behring BN-ProSpec). Scr was measured locally
  by Jaffé, modified Jaffé, spectroreflectometric or enzymatic methods.
- Model: NONMEM VI, FOCE-I, two compartments, proportional inter-patient and residual error, with a residual
  component for each centre.

## Model and parameters
- **Disposition**: the sparse data were pooled with 143 richly sampled patients (refs 9, 11). Typical Vc and Vp
  were proportional to BSA, with no covariate on CL or Q. Each patient's Vc, Vp and Q were fixed at their POSTHOC
  values for the covariate analysis. **The typical Vc, Vp and Q values are not printed.** Shrinkage was 1.2% for
  CL, 27.7% for Vc and 68.2% for Vp. Residual error by centre was 7.2-21.7% (printed p3635).
- **Clearance** (Table 2, PDF p5, printed p3637; page image), from the 357 patients alone:
  CL (mL/min) = θ1 · (Scr/75)^θ2 · (cysC/1.0)^θ3 · (ABW/65)^θ4 · (age/56)^θ5 · θ6^sex,
  with Scr in µmol/L, cysC in mg/L, ABW (actual body weight) in kg, age in years, and sex = 0 male, 1 female.

- Removing each covariate raised the objective function by: sex +34, age +54, cysC +62, Scr +75, ABW +79 (all
  P < 0.001). IBW or LBM in place of ABW fitted worse. Against Thomas 2005 (45 patients) the exponents changed by
  -12% (Scr), +18% (cysC), +6% (ABW) and -5% (age); the female factor was the same (0.854 there).
- Example (derived): woman aged 60 y, 65 kg, Scr 77 µmol/L, cysC 0.90 mg/L gives CL 100 mL/min (6.0 L/h).
- Performance (Table 3, PDF p6, printed p3638; page image), modified Thomas formula vs Calvert-Cockcroft-Gault:

| Group | MAPE % | MPE % | % with abs error > 20% |
|---|---|---|---|
| All (357) | 15 vs 17 | +1 vs -6 | 25 vs 38 |
| Obese (43) | 12 vs 14 | -3 vs +1 | 23 vs 30 |
| Normal (285) | 15 vs 18 | +1 vs -7 | 25 vs 39 |
| Underweight (29) | 15 vs 21 | -2 vs -11 | 28 vs 45 |

  The Thomas formula's parameters were re-estimated on these data and Calvert's were not, which the authors
  concede is not a fair comparison.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No effect compartment, no toxicity or response end point. The target is AUC (dosing by Calvert-style AUC);
no target value is given.

## Covariates
Scr (µmol/L), plasma cystatin C (mg/L, nephelometric or turbidimetric; ELISA values need a 0.66 normalisation,
per the Discussion), actual body weight, age and sex, all on clearance as above. BSA on Vc and Vp. The authors
limit use to the ranges studied: age 21-87 y, ABW 40-137 kg, Scr 25-433 µmol/L (printed p3639).

## Caveats
- The concentrations are ultrafilterable platinum by AAS, so platinum species other than intact carboplatin are
  counted.
- Sparse sampling (3 samples per patient after the dose). The distribution parameters come from another
  database and are not reported, so the paper cannot by itself fix a concentration-time curve.
- Scr assays varied between centres (no relationship with bias was found; centre MPEs ranged from -10.1% to
  +12.6%).
- 74% women, mostly ovarian cancer.
- R² 0.59 for observed vs predicted CL (Fig. 2).
