# Kim 2018: nitroglycerin through polyolefin, polyurethane and PVC administration sets

## Study
Randomised, open-label 3 x 3 crossover (Severance Hospital, Seoul; PDF p2). Nine healthy Korean men, 19-50 y,
BMI 18.5-25 (enrolled: age 33.1 +/- 7.1 (26-45) y, weight 72.7 +/- 8.1 (62.3-85.9) kg, BMI 22.9; Table 2, PDF p4).
Nitroglycerin (Bayer) diluted to 100 ug/mL (PDF p1, p2, p6 as printed; the text layer reads "mg") and infused at
12 mL/h = 20 ug/min for 30 min (PDF p6) through a 100 cm set of PVC (plasticiser DEHT), polyurethane (PU) or
polyolefin (PO), all from one maker, into a 23-gauge forearm-vein catheter; washout >= 5 days (Table 1). Blood at 2, 5,
10, 20, 30 min after the start and 3, 6, 10 min after the end; **the sampling site is not stated** (the infusion was
into a forearm vein; BP was taken on "the other" arm). Plasma by LC-MS/MS, calibration 0.1-50.0 ng/mL, precision
< 20%, accuracy 80-120% (PDF p2). Non-compartmental analysis (WinNonlin 7.0); Cmax and tmax observed, AUClast by linear
trapezoids. One subject (group C) fainted during his first period, with the PO set, and was withdrawn: **PK in 8**,
BP in 8 (PDF p4-5). Most post-infusion samples were below the limit of quantification, so no lambda-z, half-life or
clearance was calculated (PDF p2-3, p6-7).

text (0.14, 0.31), both on the rendered PDF p5; the Cmax ratios agree. The arithmetic-mean ratios (1.78/36.17 = 0.05,

Figure 2 (PDF p4, rendered; mean, 0-30 min only, no post-infusion points): PO about 0.48 ng/mL at 2 min, about 1.05 at
5 min, 1.1-1.25 from 10 to 30 min; PVC about 0.1, 0.29, then 0.22-0.25; PU 0.03-0.11 throughout (read from the
figure). Text: plasma "reached steady state after approximately 5 minutes" in all sets (PDF p4).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
MAP (= (SBP + 2 DBP)/3) by cuff; % change from baseline, repeated-measures mixed model (PDF p3, Figure 3 on PDF p6).
PU differed significantly from PO and from PVC at most points during infusion; **PO and PVC did not differ except at
the end of infusion** (PDF p5). Figure 3: PO about -15% from 8 to 30 min, PVC about -10%, PU about -3 to -6% (read
from the figure). Adverse events (Table 4, PDF p6): one headache (3 min) and one syncope (about 2 s), both with PO.
No ke0, EC50 or concentration-effect relation. The Methods give two BP schedules (every 5 min, PDF p2 left column; and
2, 4, 6 ... 30 min and 4-32 min after, PDF p2 right column).

## Covariates
None.

## Caveats
Preliminary, n = 8 analysed, healthy young men, one manufacturer's 100 cm sets, one rate (20 ug/min), 30 min only.
The tube concentration was not measured (authors' first limitation, PDF p6); the loss is inferred to be sorption, not
measured. Sampling site unstated. Post-infusion data unusable (below LOQ), so the offset was not quantified. The
statement that longer tubing and slower flow increase sorption "proportionately" (PDF p6) cites refs 26-27, which are
studies of nicardipine and diazepam, not nitroglycerin. The authors cite in-vitro PVC sorption of 18-43% (ref 6),
far less than the in-vivo exposure ratios here.
