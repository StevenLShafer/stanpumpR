# da Silva Neto 2021: amoxicillin, metronidazole, gentamicin PopPK in colorectal surgery prophylaxis

## Study
20 adults undergoing elective colorectal surgery, Glasgow Royal Infirmary (9 M / 11 F; Table 1: age 58 y
(18-81), weight 71.2 kg (47.6-101.9), height 1.67 m (1.44-1.79), BMI 26.7 (19.4-40.7), SCr 66 umol/L (53-97),
Cockcroft-Gault CLcr 99 mL/min (50-166), albumin 37.5 g/L (32-44); 11 laparoscopic / 9 open; surgery 4.5 h
(1.2-6.0)). Doses within 1 h of incision: amoxicillin 1000 mg IV, **metronidazole 500 mg IV**, gentamicin 3 mg/kg
IBW banded by height; re-dosing at 8 h (metronidazole) or for blood loss > 1.5 L. Samples: pre-dose, 1 h and 2 h
after dose, at skin closure, and around extra doses; 99 samples, median 5 (3-6) per patient; 76 metronidazole
concentrations (6.5-24.4 mg/L), 3 removed for sampling errors. Assay: LC-MS (Orbitrap Exactive), serum, linear
0.1-6.4 mg/L (samples diluted), intra-run CV < 5% for metronidazole. NONMEM 7.4.2 FOCE-I, PsN bootstrap (1000),
VPC. Covariates tested: age, sex, height, TBW, IBW, AJBW = IBW + 0.4 x (TBW - IBW), (TBW/70)^0.75, CLcr, albumin.
IBW reference is Devine 1974 (ref 8).

## Model and parameters
One compartment for all three drugs (first sample >= 1 h after dose, so distribution not identifiable; Discussion).
Metronidazole final model (text p. 3213-3214; Table 2 footnote): CL (L/h) = 3.22 x (TBW/70)^0.75;
V (L) = 0.556 x AJBW (kg). Covariates reduced BSV in CL from 31.3% to 26.7% and in V from 25.3% to 10.8%;
OFV fell 35.4. Proportional residual error.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Target %fT>MIC with PTA >= 90% (Monte Carlo, 1000 patients from the dataset). EUCAST v11.0 breakpoint for the
B. fragilis group: 4 mg/L. Free concentration assumed with 15% binding. 500 mg PTA 89.3% at 8 h (Table 3); failures
mainly in patients > 85 kg; 500 mg (1000 mg if > 85 kg) gives 95.8% at 8 h. Hydroxymetronidazole (about 65% of
parent activity against B. fragilis group, Haller 1982) not included.

## Covariates
CL: (TBW/70)^0.75. V: AJBW (Devine IBW). No renal or hepatic covariate (no hepatic/renal impairment; CLcr >= 50).

## Caveats
n = 20; sparse sampling from 1 h, so no distribution phase; serum not tissue; protein binding from literature;
not for patients > 100 kg, CLcr < 50, or hepatic impairment (authors). Table 2 layout scrambled in the text layer.

## Verification against stanpumpR
- CL 3.22 L/h x (TBW/70)^0.75 (R:14, 151, 154) and V 0.556 L/kg AJBW (R:15, 150, 153; help:3). Paper: identical.
  Units: L/h -> /60 to L/min correct. **matches**
- AJBW = IBW + 0.4 (TBW - IBW), IBW by Devine (R:16, 109-113). Paper: same formula; IBW reference is Devine 1974.
  **matches**
- Population: 20 adults, 18-81 y, 47.6-101.9 kg, 144-179 cm (R:42-44; help:3). Table 1: same. **matches**
- "sampling from one hour after the dose"; early distribution phase cannot be excluded (R:11, 18-20; help:19).
  Paper Discussion says the same. **matches**
- MIC 4 mg/L for B. fragilis group used for target attainment (R:64-67; antibioticThresholds:82-83). Paper: EUCAST
  v11.0, 4 mg/L. **matches**
- Hydroxymetronidazole about 65% as potent (R:56-57; help:19). Paper Discussion (citing Haller 1982). **matches**
- "Half-time at 70 kg is 8.4 h" (R:20; help:3; test:56). This is ln2 x 0.556 x 70 / 3.22 = 8.38 h, i.e. it takes
  AJBW = 70 kg (a 70-kg patient at his ideal weight). For the library's 70 kg / 170 cm reference man, AJBW is
  67.56 kg (R:37-38), V 37.56 L, t1/2 8.08 h (derived). The paper's mean individual t1/2 is 7.5 h (4.4-11.0).
  **minor discrepancy** (wording: 8.4 h is not the model's half-time for a typical 70-kg patient of ordinary height).
- Note: the paper assumed fu 0.85 (15% bound) for its PTA; stanpumpR uses Dorn's 0.96 (R:72-76), deliberately and
  said so. Not a discrepancy.
