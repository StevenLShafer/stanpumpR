# Standing 2011: pooled pediatric diclofenac PK (IV, suspension, suppository, adult dispersible tablet)

## Study
- Individual-data meta-analysis of studies found in a Cochrane review, plus one unpublished adult study (Table 1):
  - Korpela 1990: IV 0.5 mg/kg over 5 or 15 min; 10 children, minor surgery; 65 samples; age 5 (4-6) y,
    20 (18-22) kg; gas chromatography, LOQ 6.5 ug/L.
  - Van der Marel 2004: suppository 2 mg/kg then 1 mg/kg 8 h later; 26 children (tonsillectomy); 85 samples;
    4 (2-8) y, 21 (14-30) kg; HPLC, LOQ 5 ug/L.
  - Azuma 1982: suppository 25 mg; 5 children; 20 samples; 10 (7-14) y, 33 (24-50) kg; gas chromatography.
  - Standing 2008: oral suspension 1 mg/kg; 70 children (minor surgery); 205 samples; 3 (1-12) y, 17 (9-37) kg;
    HPLC/MS, LOQ 10.1 ug/L.
  - Rosemont Pharmaceuticals (UNPUBLISHED bioequivalence study): 30 adult volunteers, 50 mg suspension,
    dispersible tablet, enteric-coated tablet, randomised crossover; 706 samples; 21 (18-28) y, 71 (48-94) kg;
    HPLC/MS, LOQ 10 ug/L. Enteric-coated data not used.
- Children: 111 (1-14 y), 375 samples. 10.7% of adult late samples (< LOQ) removed.
- Mass in nanomoles (MW 318.13 sodium salt, 296.15 free acid). NONMEM VII: iterative two-stage then SAEM, then
  importance sampling for OFV/SE. Disposition from the IV data alone, then fixed while absorption was fitted for
  each formulation. Allometric scaling: CL and Q by (WT/70)^0.75, volumes by WT/70 (Discussion).

Derived (70 kg): eigenvalues 7.59, 1.26, 0.209 /h; half-lives 5.5 min, 33 min, **3.3 h** terminal; the terminal
phase carries 11.6% of the AUC after a bolus; Vss 14.95 L (0.21 L/kg). Discussion: Korpela's NCA CL 0.462
L/h/kg (32.3 L/h at 70 kg if linear); allometric 16.5 L/h agrees with adult literature 16.6-17.5 L/h. Adult
bioequivalence data: dispersible tablet median tmax 1.0 h (0.5-3.0), enteric-coated 2.5 h (2.0-6).
Dose recommendations (AUC0-12 matched to an adult 50 mg dispersible tablet, adult mean weight 71.7 kg): IV
0.3 mg/kg, suppository 0.5 mg/kg, oral 1 mg/kg.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No concentration-effect model. Exposure (AUC) used as the surrogate; adult NNT vs dose for > 50% pain relief over
4-6 h (McQuay and Moore; Fig. 1) shows a ceiling above 50 mg. Pediatric NNT 3.6 (2.5-6.3) to avoid rescue
analgesia (Cochrane). No MEAC, ke0 or tPeak. Not an opioid. Relative potency stated only qualitatively
(diclofenac about twice as effective as paracetamol by NNT).

## Covariates
Weight (allometric, exponents fixed); no maturation (CYP2C9 adult-like by 1 year, authors).

## Caveats
IV disposition from 10 children aged 4-6 (65 samples); adult disposition is an allometric extrapolation; no
subject had both IV and extravascular data; adult dispersible-tablet data unpublished; erratic absorption with
multiple peaks; high AUC prediction imprecision (median relative error 2.3%, 95% CI -67% to 125%).
