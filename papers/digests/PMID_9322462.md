# Vuyk 1997: opioid-propofol interactions (short review)

## Study
Narrative review (4 pages, supplement) of pharmacokinetic and pharmacodynamic interactions between propofol and fentanyl, alfentanil, sufentanil and remifentanil. No new data; results are quoted from the author's group and others (refs 1-13). Printed pages 23S-26S; PDF pages 1-4.

## Model and parameters
No parameter table. Reported pharmacokinetic findings (qualitative unless stated):
- Measured propofol blood concentrations exceeded TCI-predicted ones increasingly with higher target (refs 1, 2); the author suggests propofol lowers cardiac output and liver blood flow.
- Alfentanil at plasma 40 ng/mL raised blood propofol by 20% (Pavlin, ref 3). Fentanyl and alfentanil increased propofol V1 and clearance in a NONMEM analysis (Schuttler, abstract). Fentanyl pretreatment cut first-pass pulmonary propofol uptake in cats from 60% to 40% (ref 4).
- Propofol raised plasma alfentanil concentrations (refs 3, 5) and inhibits alfentanil and sufentanil metabolism in vitro (refs 6, 7); midazolam and dexmedetomidine inhibit alfentanil metabolism.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Opioid audit (all values quoted from other papers; no ke0, t1/2ke0, tPeak, hysteresis, MEAC as such, or equianalgesic ratio is given):
- Propofol blood concentration for loss of consciousness reduced by 40% in 50% of patients (EC50) by plasma fentanyl 3 ng/mL or plasma alfentanil 122 ng/mL (p. 25S, induction; refs 9, 10). Assay type (plasma vs whole blood) and matrix details not given here.
- Skin incision: propofol EC50 for blunting motor response fell as plasma fentanyl rose 0 to 3 ng/mL, with no further drop above 3 ng/mL (ceiling, p. 25S).
- Alfentanil requirement fell more than ten-fold when propofol rose from 2 to 10 ug/mL in women having gynaecological surgery (p. 25S).
- Emergence: with plasma alfentanil about 150 ng/mL, patients wake at blood propofol 0.5-1 ug/mL; with alfentanil below 50 ng/mL, at 2-3 ug/mL (p. 25S).
- Optimal propofol-alfentanil pair for a 5 h procedure: propofol 3.5 ug/mL with alfentanil 85 ng/mL (the concentrations with 50% probability of no response to lower abdominal surgery stimulus that also give fastest recovery); median time to consciousness after stopping 16 min (p. 25S, Fig 2 caption).
- Optimal propofol concentration about 3.5 ug/mL with fentanyl, sufentanil or alfentanil and 2.5-3 ug/mL with remifentanil (abstract; p. 25S). Source is conference abstracts (refs 12, 13).
- Alfentanil 40 ng/mL with propofol 0.1-1 ug/mL: resting minute ventilation down about 25%, end-tidal CO2 41 to 46 mmHg (p. 24S).

## Caveats
Secondary report; the key optimisation results rest on abstracts and on one gynaecological surgery study in women. Interaction concentrations are plasma for opioids and blood for propofol as stated by the author; the review does not report assay methods.
