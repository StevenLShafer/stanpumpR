# Le Guen 2014: dexmedetomidine sparing of propofol and remifentanil under BIS closed loop

## Study
Single-centre (Hopital Foch, Suresnes), double-blind, placebo-controlled randomized trial (NCT00921284), adults ASA I-II, non-haemorrhagic elective surgery. 66 allocated, 28 per group analysed (PDF p. 3, Fig. 1). Age 55 +/- 13 (saline) vs 56 +/- 10 yr; BMI 25 +/- 4 vs 26 +/- 4; 71% vs 81% male (Table 1, mean +/- SD).

Dexmedetomidine 1 ug/kg over 10 min, then 0.5 ug/kg/h until skin closure; saline placebo. Induction and maintenance by the Liu dual PID closed-loop controller (BIS set point 50, target 40-60), run on Infusion Toolbox 95 with Schnider (propofol) and Minto (remifentanil) effect-site models. Maintenance limits: propofol Ce 1-5 ug/mL, remifentanil 3-12 ng/mL. Minimum interval between target changes equals each drug's time to peak effect in its PK model. No premedication. Morphine 0.1 mg/kg and paracetamol 1 g given 45 min before end of surgery. Primary outcome: propofol and remifentanil requirement.

## Model and parameters
No PK model fitted. Dexmedetomidine PK is not reported; plasma concentrations were not measured.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point is BIS (EEG) held at 40-60 by closed loop; the sparing figures are consumption needed to do that. No ke0, t1/2ke0, tPeak value, MEAC, analgesic concentration or opioid potency ratio is reported. Propofol:remifentanil dose ratio (weight-normalized): induction 0.7 (0.5-0.8) saline vs 0.9 (0.7-1.1) dex, P=0.02; maintenance 48 (39-52) vs 50 (41-60), P=0.04 (text p. 4, Fig. 4; Fig. 4 shows maintenance medians about 48 and 50). Dexmedetomidine's analgesic effect was only inferred from the delayed first morphine request; the authors note dexmedetomidine half-life of about 2 h (citing Arain 2004) and that BIS may misread dexmedetomidine spindle EEG as light anaesthesia, which could understate propofol sparing.

## Covariates
None analysed beyond the model inputs (sex, age, weight, height).

## Caveats
Authors: results depend on the controller gains (patented by Liu and Chazot, Hopital Foch); underpowered for PONV; ASA I-II only; no analgesic monitor. Dexmedetomidine dose was a fixed weight-based regimen, so the sparing reflects that regimen only. Sparing percentages are of the dose consumed, not shifts in Ce50. The abstract gives 95% CI 18-40 for maintenance propofol while the text gives 11-40; the abstract also says induction propofol sparing 23% and the Discussion says 30%.
