# Hishe 2023: urate-transporter genotype and oxypurinol PK

## Study
Population PK (NONMEM 7.3) of plasma oxypurinol in people with gout taking oral allopurinol 50-900 mg daily. Pooled data from five earlier NZ studies plus a genetics study. 325 people / 3666 concentrations were available; 2841 concentrations from 300 people were analysed (PDF p. 2-3). Excluded: dialysis, missing data, oxypurinol <20 umol/L (assumed non-adherence). Assay: HPLC-UV, CV <10.4%, LOQ 0.7 umol/L (0.11 mg/L). Genotypes: ABCG2 rs2231142 and rs10011796, GLUT9, NPT1, URAT1, OAT4, MRP4. Weight, sex and age summaries are in supplementary Table S4 (not in the PDF).

## Model and parameters
One-compartment model with first-order absorption; dose is allopurinol, so CL/F and V/F are oxypurinol apparent values (conversion not modelled separately). Final model (PDF p. 4): CL/F = CL x (CLcr/6 L/h)^RFexp x d_diuretic^(diuretic 0/1) x e_ethnicity (Pacific = 1) x (WT/70)^WTexp; V/F = V x WT/70. CLcr is Cockcroft-Gault, normalised to 6 L/h (100 mL/min) per 70 kg. WT is total body weight.

Univariate genotype effects on CL (PDF p. 3-4): ABCG2 rs2231142 1.24 [1.08, 1.40]; NPT1 1.22 [1.02, 1.42]; others not significant. Neither was retained in the multivariate model.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No effect-site model, ke0 or hysteresis. No target oxypurinol concentration is given (a separate paper on minimum oxypurinol for a serum urate target is cited, with no value). No potency ratio. (Not an opioid.)

## Covariates
CrCl (power), diuretic use, Pacific ethnicity on CL; weight on CL (allometric exponent 0.34, estimated) and V (linear). Tested and dropped: genotypes, FFM, NFM, BMI, sex, concomitant drugs.

## Caveats
Adherence not measured; five heterogeneous studies; missing continuous covariates set to the median; ABCC4 genotype missing in 60%; ka and its IIV fixed; V eta-shrinkage 40.7%. Parent allopurinol and its conversion are not modelled.
