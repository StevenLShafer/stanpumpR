# Mould 1995: midazolam vs diazepam PK/PD on the Digit Symbol Substitution Test

## Study
12 healthy men, 19-34 y, weight 80 +/- 4 kg (OCR "80 2 4"; read as mean +/- SD). Randomized, third-party-blinded
four-way crossover, >= 1 week washout: midazolam 0.03 and 0.07 mg/kg, diazepam 0.10 and 0.20 mg/kg, each as a
**90-second** continuous IV injection by syringe pump (weight-based doses). Blood (5 mL) from an indwelling catheter
"distal to the infusion line" (arterial vs venous not stated explicitly; no arterial line described) at 0, 0.5, 1,
1.5, 2, 2.5, 3, 3.5, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10, 20, 40, 60, 90, 120, 180 min (early samples collected over 25 s, timed at
midpoint). Sampling ended at 3 h. Assay: GC/NCI-MS; LOQ midazolam 1.1 ng/mL, 1-OH-midazolam 0.127 ng/mL, diazepam
2 ng/mL. PD: DSST (3 practice sessions, baseline 30 min pre-dose, 10-min test from 1 min before dose scored per 20 s,
then 5-min tests at 30, 60, 90, 120, 180 min).
Analysis: semiparametric (Verotta-Sheiner): biexponential PK (coefficients not reported), parametric ke0 link,
nonparametric PD; Emax read from collapsed loops, EC50 interpolated; noncompartmental Cmax, tmax, AUC, t1/2, CL, Vd.
Diazepam t1/2, CL, Vd not calculable (3-h sampling).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: DSST (correct substitutions; Emax = 100% inhibition), venous plasma via semiparametric ke0 link.
Table I (mean +/- SD; per-dose t1/2ke0 and range alignment uncertain in OCR, pooled values confirmed by text):
- Midazolam: ke0 0.165 +/- 0.131 (0.03 mg/kg), 0.267 +/- 0.282 (0.07), pooled 0.213 +/- 0.216 /min; t1/2ke0
  harmonic means ~4.2 and ~2.6 min by dose, **pooled 3.2 min** (range 0.8-16.3); Emax 88.4%, 93.5%, pooled 90.9%;
  EC50 16.1 +/- 18.4, 20.9 +/- 18.1, **pooled 18.1 +/- 17.8 ng/mL**.
- Diazepam: ke0 0.630 +/- 0.508, 0.472 +/- 0.355, pooled 0.555 +/- 0.439 /min; t1/2ke0 ~1.1 and ~1.5 by dose,
  **pooled 1.2 min**; Emax 72.0%, 94.1%, pooled 82.6%; EC50 97.6 +/- 62.3, 132 +/- 183, **pooled 116 +/- 137 ng/mL**.
- Midazolam t1/2ke0 about 2.5 times diazepam's; midazolam about **6 times more potent** (EC50). Cites Bührer 1990
  part II EEG values: t1/2ke0 4.8 vs 1.6 min; EEG EC50 152 +/- 148 (midazolam) and 958 +/- 200 ng/mL (diazepam),
  about 7 times the DSST EC50s.
No MEAC (not an analgesic).

## Covariates
None (weight-based dosing only).

## Caveats
Young healthy men; 3-h sampling (diazepam elimination undefined); large between-subject variability (EC50 CV
87-139%); only 7 of 12 reached Emax at the high doses; DSST learning effects; OCR tables scrambled; PK biexponential
coefficients not reported, so no compartmental model can be taken from this paper.
