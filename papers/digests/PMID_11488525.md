# Kerbusch 2001: population PK of ifosfamide and its metabolites with autoinduction

## Study
- Phase I study of ifosfamide + paclitaxel + carboplatin in resistant small-cell lung cancer, University Hospital Groningen (Methods, PDF p. 2-3, printed 54-55).
- 14 patients (9 M, 5 F), mean age 58 y (44-69), weight 76 kg (54-91), BSA 1.93 m2 (1.54-2.10); normal renal and liver function required.
- Ifosfamide 1-h IV infusion: 1500 mg/m2 on days 1 and 2 (n=4); 1000 mg/m2 days 1 and 2 (n=4); 2000 mg/m2 day 1 (n=2); 3000 mg/m2 day 1 (n=4). Paclitaxel 175 mg/m2 (3 h) and carboplatin AUC 6 (30 min) given after the last ifosfamide dose; mesna equal to ifosfamide dose.
- Sampling: 0-48 h (15 samples) for 2-day schedules; 0, 1, 2, 5, 24 h for 1-day schedules. Plasma.
- Assay: GC-NPD for ifosfamide, 2-DCE and 3-DCE (LLQ 0.192 uM); HPLC of the semicarbazone derivative for 4-hydroxyifosfamide (LLQ 0.361 uM).
- Analysis: NONMEM, exponential IIV, proportional + additive residual error; Xpose.

## Model and parameters
One-compartment ifosfamide model with concentration-dependent autoinduction (Kerbusch 2000 model): an enzyme compartment whose degradation (Kenz,out) is inhibited by ifosfamide (IC50), so CL(t) = CLinit x Aenz rises during exposure. Metabolites: Fm x CL(t) x Cifo formation, first-order elimination Km; only F* = Fm/Vm identifiable. Table 1 and Table 2, PDF p. 5 (printed 57), confirmed on page image:

Individual CL(t) rises from roughly 2-5 L/h to about 5-6 L/h by 24-30 h (Fig. 3, read from graph); one patient about 8-12 L/h. Implied Vm range for 2-DCE 0.6-20.3 L and 3-DCE 9.2-118 L (Discussion, p. 6).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None: no PD, no toxicity-concentration relation. Not an opioid paper: no MEAC, ke0 or potency ratio.

## Covariates
None reported. Splitting renal from inducible clearance did not improve the fit.

## Caveats
Small n (14; urine in 10); sparse sampling in 1-day schedules; IIV on Kenz,out and IC50 not estimable; ifosfamide model and Bayesian estimates carried over sequentially into metabolite fits; co-medication given after ifosfamide.
