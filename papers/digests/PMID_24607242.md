# He 2014: population PK of oral baclofen in children with cerebral palsy

## Study
- Open-label ascending-dose study (NICHD-2005-13-2, NCT00607542), 9 US sites. 61 enrolled, 49 with PK data
  (Table I, PDF p15, text layer): age median 8.3 y (2.2-17.7), weight median 22.2 kg (11.2-84.8), gestational age
  30.0 wk (23-41), serum creatinine 0.4 mg/dL (0.2-0.9), CLCR 96.7 mL/min (47.9-191); 27 M / 22 F; 78% White.
  Spastic CP, GMFCS II-V; 73% with GI motility/GERD symptoms.
- Oral racemic baclofen started 2.5 mg TID, escalated every 2 weeks to maximum tolerated dose or limit (≤ 10 mg TID
  under 6 y; otherwise up to 20 mg QID = 80 mg/d). Tablets (5 and 10 mg) or 2 mg/mL liquid (2 subjects at PK visit).
  Unequal intervals 7/7/10 h (TID) and 5/5/5/9 h (QID).
- PK visit after 10-12 weeks titration, at steady state: pre-dose plus 13 samples over 16 h after the second daily dose.
- R- and S-baclofen by chiral derivatisation LC-MS/MS (Covance), range 1-500 ng/mL per enantiomer; no sample BLQ.
- NONMEM 7.1 FOCE-I; 1,200 concentrations; model built on R-baclofen, checked against S, then re-fitted to both.

## Model and parameters
2-compartment, linear elimination, transit-compartment absorption (Savic): ktr = (n+1)/MTT, then first-order ka
(Appendix Eq. 1-3, PDF p9). All parameters apparent (/F). Weight normalised to the median 22.2 kg.

Covariate equations (p.1184-1185): CL/F = 5.98·(WT/22.2)^0.529; Vc/F = 14.3·(WT/22.2); Vp/F = 8.94·(WT/22.2)
(volumes linear in weight); MTT = 0.410 + 0.0656·TDOS60 + 0.522·TDOS80.
Derived at 22.2 kg: CL/F 0.269 L/h/kg; Vss/F (14.3+8.94)/22.2 = 1.05 L/kg (matches the Discussion's 1.05).

NCA (Table II, PDF p16, steady state, n = 43): R-baclofen Cmax/dose 6.25 (SD 2.60) ng/mL/mg, Tmax 2.08 (1.05) h,
AUCτ/dose 25.4 (10.7) ng·h/mL/mg, t½ 4.48 (1.67) h (range 2.03-12.0); S-baclofen 5.63, 2.86 h, 25.3, 4.32 h.
R and S profiles indistinguishable (AUC slope 0.99, Cmax slope 0.90). Literature: fraction unbound 65-70%, 15% hepatic
deamination, oral bioavailability 70-85%, renal clearance 10-17 L/h (adults; cited, p.1182, 1185).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD reported: no efficacy/spasticity concentration relation, no therapeutic or toxic range, no ke0. Not an opioid
paper. Authors recommend 2 mg/kg/day as a weight-based dose (p.1187); efficacy was to be reported separately.

## Covariates
Weight on CL/F (exponent 0.529) and both volumes (linear); daily-dose group on MTT. Age and CLCR not significant
after weight. ABCC9 rs11046232 AT (n = 3) had CL/F 0.51 ± 0.05 vs 0.25 ± 0.07 L/h/kg for TT, but was not retained
in the model. No sex effect. Age-dependence of CL/kg (r² = 0.459) disappears with the 0.529 exponent (Fig. 8).

## Caveats
- Children 2-17 y with CP, mostly premature-born, normal renal function; no renal-impairment data despite renal
  elimination.
- Oral only, apparent parameters; F not estimated. Single steady-state profile per child.
- Vp/F RSE 0.113% is implausibly precise. Abstract and Discussion figures differ from Table III (see `open_questions`).
- "Higher clearance" for ABCC9 AT is against the study's own TT children, n = 3.
