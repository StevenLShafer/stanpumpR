# McQuay 1980: clinical effects of buprenorphine during and after operation

## Study
- 20 patients for elective total hip replacement, Oxford; inclusion 45-75 yr, weight < 80 kg; no serious disease, no antihypertensives except thiazides (PDF p.1, printed 1013).
- Two groups of 10 (6 M : 4 F each). Table I (PDF p.2, printed 1014, mean ± SEM): group V age 67.2 ± 2 yr, weight 68.5 ± 3.7 kg; group M age 62.7 ± 2.9 yr, weight 64.7 ± 3.4 kg; surgery 95.3 ± 10.1 vs 84.5 ± 5.5 min; blood loss 539 ± 94 vs 363 ± 55 mL. (derived: unweighted mean of the two groups 64.95 yr, 66.6 kg.)
- Anaesthesia: diazepam 10 mg oral premed; thiopentone 4 mg/kg, pancuronium 0.1 mg/kg; N2O/O2 with halothane 0.5%, ventilated to normocapnia.
- Dosing: buprenorphine 0.3 mg IV over 1 min at induction (all). At 3 h a second 0.3 mg: group V IV over 1 min; group M IM (vastus lateralis). Second dose withheld unless patient opened eyes to command.
- Analgesia measurement: 6 h after the first dose (= 3 h after the second) patients were connected to a demand system delivering diamorphine 0.25 mg per press; the only postoperative analgesic until the next morning; records 11.5-18.6 h (PDF p.2-3).
- Arterial blood gases, cortisol, prolactin, glucose; plasma buprenorphine by RIA (Bartlett 1980); kinetics reported separately in Bullingham 1980 (PMID 7438685).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- **Duration of analgesia (end point: time to the 5th diamorphine demand, PCA-type demand system):** median 480 min after the second dose, all patients pooled (PDF p.3, printed 1015; restated p.5, printed 1017). Time to 2nd, 3rd, 4th demand and regression to the zero-demand axis gave very similar medians (not tabulated). No significant difference in duration between groups V and M (non-parametric).
- **No correlation** between peak plasma buprenorphine concentration and duration, in either group; no correlation (Spearman) between % IM absorption and duration (PDF p.3, printed 1015).
- Demand rate, 60-600 min of connection (Table II, diamorphine mg × 10^-3 per patient per min, mean ± SEM): group V males 8.6 ± 0.2, females 4.8 ± 0.2, total 7.0 ± 0.3; group M males 6.5 ± 0.2, females 2.0 ± 0.1, total 4.7 ± 0.1; mean males 7.6 ± 0.2, females 3.4 ± 0.2.
- Slope ratios (Table III): male/female V 1.78, M 3.25, mean 2.24; group V/group M males 1.32, females 2.41, total 1.48.
- Range of demand: one group V patient made no demands, another 88 (= diamorphine 22 mg).
- **MEAC:** none measured or reported. No plasma concentration at demand is given. Discussion (PDF p.5, printed 1017) cites unpublished sublingual work with excellent analgesia at plasma concentrations never above 1 ng/mL (assay/matrix: plasma RIA, total drug implied; not otherwise specified).
- **ke0 / t½ke0 / tPeak / hysteresis:** none reported; the authors attribute long action to slow receptor dissociation (Hambrook and Rance 1976), effect continuing at plasma levels where none would be expected.
- **Potency/equianalgesic ratio:** none stated.
- Ventilation: PaCO2 rose postoperatively (Table IV: 5.45 ± 0.21 kPa at 30 min intraop; 6.65-6.98 kPa at 150-360 min) with no further rise after the second dose; authors suggest a flat dose-response.

## Covariates
Sex (women lower demand rate, Table II/III) and route (IM lower demand than IV). No plasma-concentration difference between sexes (text, p.5).

## Caveats
- n = 20, elderly, after halothane/diazepam anaesthesia; two doses, not a single-dose study.
- Demand device delivered diamorphine, so the 5th demand follows 4 earlier 0.25 mg doses (1 mg total).
- Duration is a pooled median of IV and IM second-dose groups; individual times not tabulated.
- Plasma levels at demand time are not reported; the paper gives no concentration-effect relation.

- **Decisions (Shafer, 2026-10-28), closing this card's two open questions.** (1) The band simulation gives group M's second dose IM, as McQuay gave it, to see whether the findings match the PK. That needed an intramuscular route, fitted to Bullingham 1980's IM/IV plasma ratios (ka_IM 0.1407 /min, F_IM 0.947; see PMID_7438685). At the reference patient, the effect site 480 min after the second dose is 0.201 ng/mL (IV) and 0.198 (IM), and the peaks are 1.02 and 0.96 ng/mL. Both round to the band's 0.2 and 1, so the equal durations McQuay found in the two groups are what the PK predicts. (2) The reference patient stays male, because "sex rarely matters". The height (170 cm) remains an assumption.
