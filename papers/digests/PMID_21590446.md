# La-Beck 2012: factors affecting PLD pharmacokinetics

## Study
Pooled analysis of PK studies from three phase I/II trials of pegylated liposomal doxorubicin (PLD, Doxil), 70 patients: study 1, 22 solid tumour; study 2, 36 AIDS-related Kaposi's sarcoma; study 3, 12 solid tumour (PDF p. 2-3). Doses 10-60 mg/m2 IV every 28 days (studies 1, 2); study 3 randomised to 30/60/45 or 60/30/45 mg/m2 in cycles 1-3. Cycle 1 PK sampling in studies 1-2 (baseline to 28 days); cycles 1-3 in study 3. Plasma sum-total doxorubicin (encapsulated plus released) by HPLC with fluorescence detection; clearance by non-compartmental analysis in WinNonlin, as mL/h/m2 and mL/h. Monocyte counts available only for study 3. BSA dosing tested against Baker's criteria (R2 >= 0.25, P < 0.01, >= 15% reduction in variability).

Patient demographics (Table 1): BSA 1.83 +/- 0.20 m2 overall (Kaposi's 1.91, solid tumour 1.74); age 50.6 +/- 14.2 years (28-77); ABW/IBW 1.11 +/- 0.26. BSA regression on clearance in mL/h: R2 = 0.25; variability reduced only 8.6%, so Baker's criteria were not met (p. 45).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD end point, ke0, MEAC or therapeutic range. Not an opioid. Related statements: a longer PLD half-life is linked (cited from others) to more skin toxicity; a fall in pre-cycle monocyte count from cycle 1 to 3 went with a larger fall in clearance (-11.5 vs -5.3 mL/h, P = 0.09, not significant; printed SDs carry minus signs, a typographical slip) (p. 47).

## Covariates
- Age: < 60 vs >= 60, 54.6 vs 23.3 mL/h/m2 (P < 0.0001); in solid tumour alone 34.1 vs 23.3, P = 0.34 (Table 2 and text). All Kaposi's patients are males under 60, so age and tumour type are confounded.
- Sex: females 23.7, males 55.6 (P < 0.0001); in solid tumour alone 23.7 vs 34.9 (P = 0.016). Male < 60 is the high group (60.3 +/- 25.6); the other three groups are 27.4, 27.6, 20.8.
- Body composition (ABW/IBW, BMI): no relationship (r2 0.22 and 0.13). Monocytes: not significant.

## Caveats
Study not powered for age or monocyte effects; Kaposi's patients received low doses (about 20 mg/m2) and the authors say low doses clear faster than 40-60 mg/m2, so dose, disease, age and sex are confounded. Clearance is per m2 of the dosed BSA, a ratio of means across studies. Clearance fell from cycle 1 to 3 (study 3). Clearance is of sum-total drug, not free doxorubicin. The paper quotes an earlier PLD prototype formulation with faster clearance (80-90 mL/h) than the marketed one (30-40 mL/h) (p. 49).

## Verification against stanpumpR
Not cited, so nothing to verify line by line. Independent comparison with R/drugs_doxorubicinLiposomal.R (Hsu 2018 Table I): the library's release clearance 0.0212 L/h (21.2 mL/h, lines 93-94) is close to study 3's cycle-1 sum-total clearance of 23.7 mL/h, but below the all-patient and solid-tumour means converted to absolute values (28.0 mL/h/m2 x 1.74 m2 is about 49 mL/h, derived from means of per-m2 values, so approximate). Direction: the library curve is a slower-clearing one than this population's average, as expected for a 50 mg/m2 dose (dose-dependent clearance, header lines 62-66). The paper's sex and age effects are not applied in the library and cannot be separated from disease and dose here (open questions).
