# PMID_40067039 SDC2: trial-design simulation

## Study
Simulation study to choose the design of the trial in SDC1 (pp.1-2). Primary objective: the dose-exposure-response of remimazolam for MOAA/S 2 and 3 and the effect of remifentanil on it.

- Simulation model: proportional odds logistic regression for MOAA/S. Remimazolam EC50s for MOAA/S <=4, <=3 and <=1 were derived by simulation from the Zhou 2021 Markov model (stationarity assumed, opioid interaction ignored). EC50s for MOAA/S <=2 and =0 and the Hill coefficient came from Schuttler 2020. A hierarchical interaction term (as in Kuizenga 2019) assumed remifentanil 2.0 ng/mL reduces remimazolam EC50s by 50%, with interaction Hill coefficient 1. These are simulation assumptions, not estimates; numeric EC50s are not given.
- 1,000 simulations per design, refitted; designs compared on bias, type I error (failure to identify BSV in EC50) and eta-shrinkage.
- Designs (Table 1, p.3): Design 1 = Kuizenga-type, 30 patients, 2 periods; Design 2 = criss-cross (Short 2002), 60 patients, 1 period; Design 3 = single-session cross-over (Bouillon 2004), 60 patients, 1 period; Design 4 (final) = 24 patients, 3 periods. Remimazolam targets 150, 300, 400, 800, 1300, 2000 ng/mL; remifentanil 0.5 (n=24), 2.0 (n=12), 4.0 (n=12) ng/mL in design 4.
- Stated result: shrinkage on BSV of remimazolam EC50 was acceptable in designs 1 and 3 (>80% of simulations <20%) and unacceptable in design 2. Design 3 was dropped as impractical. At least 24 volunteers needed for design 4.
