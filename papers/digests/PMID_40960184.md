# Wu 2025: population PK of lacosamide in Chinese children and adults with epilepsy

## Study
Retrospective TDM study, Beijing Tiantan Hospital, November 2021 to February 2025 (PDF p2, printed 359). 391 patients
with epilepsy, 482 plasma concentrations, mostly steady-state troughs. Age 32 y median (3-87); weight 65 kg median
(18-110); height 168 cm; BSA 1.75 m²; 67 children (78 samples, 17.14%) (Table 1, PDF p3, printed 360; PDF p5).
Sex: Table 1 gives "Male/Female 218/173", but the Results text says 218 women and 173 men (PDF p3), a contradiction.
Oral lacosamide (LCM) tablets once or twice daily, 150 mg/day median (25-800), 2.01 mg/kg/day median (0.14-11.43).
Urea 4.60 mmol/L median (1.80-31.10); creatinine 55.45 µmol/L; eGFR 140.45 mL/min (Cockcroft-Gault in adults, 2008
Schwartz in children). LCM concentration 4.27 mg/L median (0.65-31.90). Co-medication: valproate 115 (29.41%),
oxcarbazepine 54, lamotrigine 45, topiramate 29, carbamazepine 15 (3.84%), phenobarbital 16 (4.09%), IND1 30
(7.67%), IND2 78; 45.5% on LCM monotherapy.
Assay: stated as fluorescence polarization immunoassay on a Siemens Centaur XP, quantification range about 1-15 mg/L,
but the cited method (ref 19) is a UHPLC-MS/MS paper (PDF p2): which assay was used is unclear. Total plasma.
Phoenix NLME 8.3, FOCE-ELS; stepwise covariates (forward ΔOFV 6.64, P < 0.01; backward 10.83, P < 0.001);
bootstrap 1000, VPC, NPDE.

## Model and parameters
One compartment, first-order absorption and elimination; two-compartment was worse (AIC 2181.33 vs 2139.26; PDF p5).
Ka fixed at 0.13 /h (estimated from these data; fixing at 2.45 gave OFV 2182.37 vs 2129.26); IIV on Ka fixed at zero.
Proportional residual error. Final model (Equation 5, PDF p4, printed 361):

CL (L/h) = 1.19 x (BW/65)^0.6 x (Urea/4.6)^-0.29 x e^IND1, with IND1 = 0.35 if co-medicated with phenobarbital or
carbamazepine, otherwise 0. e^0.35 = 1.419 (derived), the paper's "1.42-fold".

Base model: Vd 10.28 L, CL 1.21 L/h, IIV Vd 27.01%, IIV CL 22.34%, σ 0.33. Table 3 labels the covariate coefficients
"(L/h)", but they are exponents. Derived (not in the paper): k = 1.19/9.95 = 0.12 /h, t½ about 5.8 h, close to the
fixed Ka 0.13 /h (flip-flop territory); the paper's own introduction gives the terminal half-life as about 13 h.

**Table 2** (PDF p4): forward BW on CL ΔOFV -28.14; IND1 -12.38; urea -14.16; BW on Vd -7.25 (later removed,
+7.25, P > 0.001). Backward: removing urea +13.93, IND1 +14.53, BW +22.79. Age was not significant (ΔOFV -0.22 on CL,
-1.05 on Vd). BW beat BSA (OFV 2076.59 vs 2087.39); IND1 beat IND2 (adding oxcarbazepine; 2087.96 vs 2090.64).

**Table 4** (PDF p7, printed 364): simulated q12h doses giving a mean trough of about 1 and about 10 mg/L. No IND1,
urea 4: 30 kg 11.5 / 106.0 mg; 50 kg 15.5 / 153.0; 70 kg 19.5 / 183.5. With IND1, urea 4: 30 kg 16.5 / 159.5; 50 kg
24.5 / 220.5; 70 kg 30.0 / 290.0. The Results text (PDF p4) says 1.5 mg for the 30 kg child where Table 4 says 11.5.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD measured (listed as a limitation, PDF p8). Therapeutic range used for the simulations: 1-10 mg/L (citing the
AGNP 2017 consensus, Hiemke 2018). No ke0.

## Covariates
Body weight (power 0.6, centred on 65 kg), plasma urea (power -0.29, centred on 4.6 mmol/L), IND1 (phenobarbital or
carbamazepine, e^0.35). Not significant: age, oxcarbazepine (alone or within IND2), valproate. Phenytoin and felbamate
could not be tested (no patients).

## Caveats
- **Comparator of the 1.42-fold.** Patients on phenobarbital or carbamazepine against the rest of this cohort (on
  LCM alone or with valproate, oxcarbazepine, lamotrigine or topiramate), not against healthy volunteers. Only 30
  IND1 patients, about half on each drug, so the term is not drug-specific.
- Mostly troughs: Ka fixed, Vd poorly identified; the Vd of 9.95 L (about 0.15 L/kg at 65 kg, derived) is far below
  lacosamide's usual about 0.6 L/kg, and its printed %RSE (68.7) does not fit its CI.
- Internal inconsistencies: sex counts (Table 1 vs text), assay description vs its reference, 1.5 vs 11.5 mg
  (text vs Table 4).
- Single centre, Chinese patients, no external validation.
