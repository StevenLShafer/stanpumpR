# Kuizenga 2018: Test of neural inertia in humans (propofol / sevoflurane +/- remifentanil)

## Study
- Four-period randomized crossover in 36 healthy ASA 1 volunteers (16 M / 20 F; age mean 43.3, range 20-70 yr; weight mean 75.3, range 48-104 kg; BMI 24.5), stratified by age and sex (Table 1, PDF p2; demographics p6). Each had four sessions at least 1 week apart: propofol alone (P), sevoflurane alone (S), propofol + remifentanil (PR), sevoflurane + remifentanil (SR). Remifentanil effect-site target 2 or 4 ng/mL (half of the PR/SR sessions each).
- Staircase step-up then step-down in pseudo steady state: propofol Ce targets 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2.5, 3.5, 4.5, 6, 7.5 ug/mL (first 8 volunteers had 1.5, 2, 2.5, 3, 4, 6, 8); sevoflurane end-tidal 0.2 then 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2.5, 3.5, 4, 4.5 vol% (first 8: 0.5-5 vol%). Each step held 12 min after the predicted Ce was reached, then MOAA/S and an electrical-stimulus (Neurometer, A-delta, 4 mA or 1.5x pain threshold) response.
- Propofol and remifentanil by TCI (RUGLOOPII). Propofol: Schnider model with ke0 computed to give time to peak effect 1.6 min; remifentanil: Minto model (PDF p3). Discussion (PDF p11) gives the propofol ke0 used as 0.456 /min (Schnider).
- Assay: propofol plasma GC-MS (LLOQ 0.25 mg/L, CV <11%), arterial EDTA samples (so plasma, total). Remifentanil by LC-MS/MS in acidified EDTA plasma, drawn in the first three steps only. 887 propofol and 204 remifentanil concentrations (PDF p6).
- End points: LOR-ROR (MOAA/S 3 or higher to 2 or lower; reverse for ROR), PAIN (motor response to electrical stimulus), PSI, SEF95. NONMEM 7.3; sigmoid Emax with measured plasma propofol or end-tidal sevoflurane used as the effect-site surrogate (steady state assumed).
- TCI performance (PDF p11): propofol MDPE 8.7%, MDAPE 20%; remifentanil MDPE 21%, MDAPE 25% (as printed, "median performance error and median absolute performance error ... 8.7% and 20% for propofol and 21% and 25% for remifentanil").

## Model and parameters

LOR/PAIN fitted with E0 = 0, Emax = 1 fixed. Typical values (RSE %) from Table 2 (PDF p10, printed 534); propofol C50 in ug/mL (plasma), sevoflurane C50 in vol% end-tidal:

Derived (not in paper): the remifentanil-adjusted LOR-ROR propofol C50 = 1.72 x (1 + theta1) = 1.24 ug/mL at 2 ng/mL, and 1.72 x (1 + theta1 x (1 + theta2)) = 0.87 ug/mL at 4 ng/mL (from Eq 2-3 and Table 2). Sublinear: doubling remifentanil does not double the reduction (text PDF p7).

Neural-inertia terms (induction C50 higher than recovery C50; q3 = alone, q4 = 2 ng/mL, q5 = 4 ng/mL; Table 2): propofol all NS for every end point. Sevoflurane LOR-ROR: q4 0.184 (RSE 40.3%), q5 0.402 (41.3%); PAIN: q5 0.599 (43.6%); PSI: q3 0.125 (34.1%), q4 0.343 (29.1%); SEF all NS.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- Not an opioid-potency paper, but remifentanil is in it. No MEAC or analgesic concentration is reported for remifentanil. Remifentanil was held at a targeted (predicted) Ce of 2 or 4 ng/mL (Minto model) and is used only as a modifier of the hypnotic C50; no remifentanil ke0, t1/2ke0 or tPeak is estimated, and no equianalgesic ratio is given. Remifentanil measured plasma concentrations (arterial, total, acidified EDTA plasma) were used to confirm steady state only (PDF p4).
- Propofol: TCI ke0 set for tPeak 1.6 min (Schnider); the quoted ke0 is 0.456 /min (PDF p11). These were inputs for the design, not fitted. Fitted C50 are against measured plasma concentration at pseudo steady state (12 min equilibration, more than 45 min on some step-downs), so no hysteresis was modelled.
- Hypnotic C50 for LOR-ROR 1.72 ug/mL propofol and 0.66 vol% sevoflurane; authors compare these with Schnider 1999 (1.68 ug/mL) and Katoh 1998 (0.65%) (PDF p11).
- Remifentanil lowers C50 of propofol/sevoflurane for LOR-ROR, PAIN, PSI, SEF; 2 to 4 ng/mL gave a sublinear further decrease for LOR-ROR and PAIN and no further decrease for PSI and SEF.

## Covariates
Age and sex used only for stratification (Table 1). No covariate model on C50.

## Caveats
- Authors' own limitation: equilibration (12 min after target) was based on propofol ke0 0.456 /min; for sevoflurane ke0 is poorly known, so equilibration was shorter and an apparent sevoflurane inertia could be a PK artefact (PDF p11). Plasma, not effect-site, concentration is the model input.
- Propofol/sevoflurane targets were lowered after the first 8 volunteers because too few responded (PDF p4).
- Remifentanil concentrations were measured in only 204 samples (first three steps), so the remifentanil exposure is mostly the predicted value.
- Spontaneous breathing, airway aids and LMA in many sessions (PDF p6).
