# Koopman 2024: rFIX-Fc (ALPROLIX) including young children

## Study
- Real-world data, OPTI-CLOT TARGET (Netherlands) and the UK-EHL Outcomes Registry. 37 patients with haemophilia B, 35 severe (FIX < 1 IU/dL), 2 non-severe (baseline 1 IU/dL). Age 15 (2-71) years, 19 under 18 and 14 under 12; weight 65.4 (12-103) kg; height 169 (85-192) cm; BMI 22.0 (Table 1). Sex not printed.
- Dose 36 (10-132) IU/kg; 5 (3-7) samples per PK profile, 15 min to 168 h; levels during bleeds or surgery excluded. One-stage assay, corrected for the endogenous baseline and the decaying residual of the previous dose (Methods 2.2, equations 1-3).
- External evaluation of Diao 2014's three-compartment model: median prediction error -48.8%, worst in children. A new model built in NONMEM.

## Model and parameters
Two compartments; a third did not improve the fit. All parameters allometrically scaled to 73 kg (fixed exponents). Age on CL linear, the only covariate kept (LBW, FFM, height, BMI and centre explored). Table 2 and its equations:

    CL = 1.41 dL/h x (BW/73)^0.75 x (1 - 0.0047 x (AGE - 15.8))
    V1 = 73.1 dL x (BW/73)^1.00
    Q  = 2.77 dL/h x (BW/73)^0.75
    V2 = 80.1 dL x (BW/73)^1.00

## Checks
- At 73 kg and 15.8 years: half-lives 8.3 and 87.1 h, CL 1.93 mL/h/kg, Vss 210 mL/kg (the text, 153 dL), recovery 1.00 IU/dL per IU/kg. ALPROLIX label (Table 4, adults): half-life 86-97 h, CL 2.6-3.3 mL/h/kg, Vss 236-327 mL/kg, recovery 1.02-1.12. Half-life and recovery agree; clearance is lower, as the authors found against Diao (1.41 vs 2.39 dL/h).
- Results 3.3.2 gives a 73-kg patient's typical clearance as 1.89 dL/h at 20 years and 1.36 dL/h at 70. The equation gives 1.38 and 1.05 (the same 24% fall). The table, the equations and Results 3.3.3's 1.41 dL/h at 16 years agree with each other, so the sentence's absolute values look like a slip; the model uses the table.
- Terminal half-lives by age (Table S3, not read): 70 h under 12, 76 h 12-17, 88 h adults (Results 3.3.3).
