# Steinhoff 2024: cenobamate levels and response, Kork TDM series

## Study
Part of CENKORK, a prospective, single-centre, open-label non-interventional study at the Kork Epilepsy Center,
Germany (p3). Adults with focal-onset seizures starting add-on cenobamate June 2021-June 2022, once-daily evening dosing,
venous blood in the morning, stable co-medication for 4 weeks and no cenobamate dose change for 4 weeks before the
sample; renal or hepatic impairment excluded (p3). Titration 12.5, 25, 50 mg (2 weeks each) then 100 mg, then
individual, maximum 400 mg/day (p4). Only seizure-free patients (3 months) and non-responders (no effect or <50%
reduction) were analysed; 50% responders were excluded by design (p4). Assay: in-house LC-MS on serum (100 uL),
[13C6]-cenobamate internal standard, linear 0.1-100, approved range 0.5-50 ug/mL, LLOQ 0.5 ug/mL, precision <5%; no
interference from other ASMs (p4-5). Statistics: means +/- SD, linear regression and Pearson correlation of level on
dose, ANCOVA adjusted for baseline seizure frequency (p5).

101 patients: 36 seizure-free, 65 non-responders; 31 with adverse events (p5). Table 1 (p6, rendered): mean age 37.5
(seizure-free) and 37.9 y (non-responders), range 19-76; about 10 previous ASMs; concomitant ASMs mean 1.5-1.6, range
0-3 (non-responders) and 1-3 (seizure-free).

The fitted line in Figure 1 (p7, rendered) runs from roughly 9.5 mg/L at 100 mg to roughly 30 mg/L at 400 mg (read
from the figure; no equation printed). Pooled over all 101 patients the mean level is 19.8 mg/L at a mean 253.5 mg/day,
0.078 mg/L per mg/day (derived from Table 2 means and n). Within-day variation: in two in-patients sampled at four
times of day at steady state, levels varied within 10% (p9).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End points: seizure freedom over 3 months against non-response, and clinician-assessed adverse events. Neither dose
nor level differed significantly between seizure-free and non-responders, or between patients with and without
adverse events (ANCOVA, p8; Table 2 footnote). Seven patients with neurotoxic events (dizziness, ataxia, blurred or
double vision) had 200-400 mg/day and 13.6-54.6 mg/L (p8). No therapeutic range could be defined; the authors favour
documenting each patient's own level when seizure-free, and do not recommend routine TDM (p9, p11).

## Covariates
None analysed beyond the response groups.

## Caveats
Single centre, n 101, 50% responders excluded; non-responders could include patients on no other ASM (range 0-3).
Samples are morning levels after evening dosing (about 12-16 h post-dose), not strict troughs, and the 10% within-day
figure rests on 2 patients. Serum assay, although the text says plasma. Co-medications not analysed for their effect
on cenobamate.
