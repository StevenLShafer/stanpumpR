# Bouillon 2003: remifentanil ventilatory depression, non-steady state

## Study
- 10 healthy volunteers (5 men, 5 women), age 33.5 yr (median, range 23-43), weight 69.3 kg (median, range 50-100) (p780). Subset of a larger propofol-remifentanil study (companion: PMID_12606904). Fasted 6 h. Volunteer 1 excluded (spuriously low baseline PaCO2); volunteer 16 had no CO2 response (equipment failure), so 8 volunteers contributed CO2 response curves (Fig 2 legend, n = 8). No volunteer more than lightly sedated.
- Step 1: modified Read rebreathing (bags filled with oxygen, no CO2 at the start; bags removed at volunteer request after about 5 min), minute ventilation by pressure-differential spirometer, arterial PaCO2 every 1-2 min.
- Step 2: remifentanil by STANPUMP TCI on plasma concentration, Minto 1997 covariate-adjusted PK, ascending steps (each held 15 min; 20 min for volunteers 1 and 2) until PeCO2 > 65 mmHg or apnoea > 60 s, then passive fall to 1 ng/ml. Peak targets 3-9 ng/ml (Table 1: 3, 4.5, 6, 3, 7.5, 9, 3, 9, 3, 6 ng/ml across the listed volunteers).
- Sampling: arterial blood for remifentanil (GC-MS, assay per Bouillon 2002) and PaCO2 (i-STAT) at 2, 5, 10, 15 min from the start, before each step-up, and at 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 15, 30 min after the last change.
- Analysis: NONMEM V, FOCE with eta-epsilon interaction, additive error, log-likelihood ratio at P < 0.01.

Model equations (p781-782): alveolar ventilation = Valv(0) x [1 - Cp^g/(C50^g + Cp^g)] x (PecCO2/PecCO2(0))^F, inserted in a one-compartment CO2 mass balance (eq 12). The sigmoid Emax fit was 25 log-likelihood points better than a power function (eq 13), which was dropped.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: ventilatory depression, shown as PaCO2 rise (arterial CO2 tension) and minute/alveolar ventilation, non-steady state, no inspired CO2. Not analgesia, pupil or EEG.
- C50 for ventilatory depression: 0.92 ng/ml (plasma, remifentanil concentration in blood samples, GC-MS), defined as the concentration that halves ventilation at constant PaCO2 (Table 4 footnote; this condition does not occur in a spontaneously breathing patient). Compared by the authors with isohypercapnic C50s of 1.12 ng/ml (Babenco, ref 5) and 1.17 ng/ml (Glass, ref 4) (p785). Alfentanil comparator: indirect-response C50 60.3 ng/ml, isohypercapnic 49.5 and 75.3 ng/ml (p785).
- Steady-state consequence (p786-787): at Cp = C50 ventilation falls only to 88% of baseline (0.5^(1/(F+1)) with F 4.3); at 5 x C50 to 67% with PaCO2 rising about 1.5-fold (about 60 from 40 mmHg).
- Opioid audit: MEAC/analgesic concentration: none. ke0 for remifentanil: none; an effect-compartment ke0 for remifentanil was tried (eq 10) and did not improve the fit, which the authors attribute to equilibration being faster than CO2 kinetics (p785). The only ke0 in the paper is the CO2 ke0 0.92 /min (end point: minute ventilation vs end-tidal CO2 hysteresis, Table 2). No tPeak. No equianalgesic or potency ratio beyond the alfentanil C50 comparison above.
- Simulation (Fig 4): a 70 ug bolus drives ventilation almost to apnoea; an infusion giving the same peak concentration gives far less depression because PaCO2 rises first.

## Covariates
None; no age, sex or weight effect on the PD parameters was tested. Interindividual variability only (CVs in Tables 2-4).

## Caveats
- n = 8 usable (PD) of 10 volunteers, healthy and young. Large CVs (C50 54.8%); the authors say population predictions are poor for individuals (p784).
- Plasma, not effect-site, concentration drives the model; no remifentanil equilibration delay was identifiable.
- C50 refers to a constant-PaCO2 situation; authors caution against extrapolating above the studied concentrations (about 9 ng/ml peak).
- Units in Tables 2-4 print l/min for rate constants; read as 1/min.

## Verification against stanpumpR
Nothing cites this paper. Cross-checks, none of them a discrepancy:
- MEAC: the library's remifentanil MEAC is 1 ng/ml (R/drugs_remifentanil.R:117) with typical 1.2 and range 0.8-2.0; this paper's C50 0.92 ng/ml is a ventilatory-depression C50 for a different end point (not analgesia), so it neither confirms nor contradicts the MEAC.
- tPeak: the library uses 1.6 min (R/drugs_remifentanil.R:116). This paper reports no remifentanil ke0 or tPeak, so it cannot check it.
- PK: the paper's own fit (V1 13.50 L, Cl1 2.57 L/min) differs from the Minto 1997 set kept in R/alternativeModels.R:267-272 (V1 5.1 L at age 40, LBM 55 kg; Cl1 2.6 L/min); the paper's Cl1 2.57 L/min is close, V1 is not. The study used Minto PK only to drive the pump, so the difference is a property of the fit, not an error in the repo.
