# Lu 2001: selecting reliable PK data under possible noncompliance

## Study
Statistical methods paper with simulations and one worked example. No anaesthetic drug. The aim is to flag PK
sampling occasions whose recalled dose history is unreliable, so they can be dropped before a population PK or
PK-PD analysis.

- Method ("Mixture-Model", pp. 346-350): each occasion's last scheduled dose is T (taken at the stated time), NT (not taken) or U (taken at an unknown time). The occasion likelihood is a mixture of the three, Eq. 5 (p. 349). Observations below the quantification limit (BQL) are handled as censored (Tobit type I) in Eqs. 2-3. U is uniform over 0 to 10 population half-lives before the first sample. An occasion is labelled by whichever of the three terms is largest at the individual-level Bayes (MAP) optimum. Fitted in NONMEM, with a grid search for starting values (p. 351).
- Requirements (p. 346, p. 359): approximate population PK known; at least 1-2 samples per occasion; short half-life with dosing interval of at least 3 half-lives; doses taken are of stated size; sample times accurate.
- Comparators (p. 350): All-T (treat every observation as taken), No-BQL (keep only non-BQL observations), Mixture-Model.
- Simulation (pp. 351-356): one-compartment bolus model, Cl and V lognormal with mean 0.693 and 1 (CV about 25%), unit dose, error variance sigma1^2 + sigma2^2*C^2 with sigma=(0.04, 0.1), QL 0.1, 1000 subjects, p(T)/p(NT)/p(U) = 0.50/0.25/0.25.
- Worked example (pp. 356-358): ACTG 175 zidovudine, 200 mg three times daily; 601 patients, 703 plasma samples (551 patients one occasion, 51 two), 114 sites; RIA assay with quantification limit 5 ng/mL, interassay precision 11.7% and bias 8.9%.

## Caveats
- The authors state the superiority claim rests on one model setting, a limited set of alternatives and no robustness study (p. 359). The benefit is modest with only one sample per occasion (p. 359).
- p(T) and p(NT) are better thought of as tuning parameters (p. 359).
- The search can hit a local optimum: in the ACTG example one individual's labels are reversed (p. 358).
- Applies only to drugs with a short half-life and known population PK.
