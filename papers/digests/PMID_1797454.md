# Stratton 1991: time-kill bactericidal activity of metronidazole, clindamycin and cephamycins against the B. fragilis group

## Study
In vitro microbiology; no pharmacokinetics. 24 clinical isolates of the B. fragilis group: 12 with cefoxitin MIC
≥32 µg/mL (B. fragilis 2, B. thetaiotaomicron 5, B. distasonis 2, B. ovatus 3) and 12 B. fragilis with low cefoxitin
MICs; plus quality-control strains B. fragilis ATCC 25285 and B. thetaiotaomicron ATCC 29741 (26 strains in all)
(Methods, PDF p2 / printed p378).
- Agents: cefoxitin, cefotetan, clindamycin, metronidazole (standard powders).
- MIC: NCCLS broth microdilution, anaerobic broth MIC medium, twofold 0.125-256 µg/mL, 10^5 CFU/well, 48 h at 35°C,
  duplicate.
- Time-kill: BACTEC 7D anaerobic blood-culture bottles (30 mL prereduced enriched soybean-casein digest broth),
  0.25-256 µg/mL in twofold steps, inoculum 10^6-10^7 CFU/mL (Table 2 title gives the final inoculum as 5 × 10^6
  CFU/mL), 35°C on an orbital shaker, sampling at 0, 4, 12, 24 h; detection limit 50 colonies/mL. **Killing = ≥3
  log10 (≥99.9%) fall in CFU/mL at 24 h.** Each isolate in duplicate on at least two occasions. Survivors retested
  for MIC (within ±1 dilution of original).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: ≥3 log10 CFU/mL reduction at 24 h in broth time-kill (an MBC-like measure, not the CLSI macrodilution
MBC). Metronidazole was also the most rapidly bactericidal (Figure 1, B. ovatus PN233, metronidazole MIC 0.5: 1-4
µg/mL fell from ~10^6 to ≤10^3 CFU/mL within 4 h; 0.5 µg/mL held near 10^5). Not an opioid paper: no MEAC, ke0 or
potency ratio.

## Covariates
None (in vitro).

## Caveats
- 26 strains only, selected for cefoxitin resistance (half the cohort), so not a surveillance sample.
- Inoculum ~5 × 10^6 CFU/mL, ten times the usual 5 × 10^5; BACTEC broth rather than standard MBC medium.
- Metronidazole was not tested above the concentration that killed everything (ND above 4).
- No clinical breakpoint or PK/PD linkage beyond saying such concentrations are achievable orally (citing Adam 1981,
  Gerding 1986).
