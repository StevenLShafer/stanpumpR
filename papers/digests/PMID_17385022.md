# Hong 2007: population PK/PD of four corticosteroids on lymphocytes

## Study
Randomized five-way placebo-controlled crossover, 2-week washouts (design detailed in Mager 2003, ref 7). Five healthy
men, age 32.2 +/- 7.6 y, weight 73.6 +/- 10.7 kg. Single "presumably equivalent" doses, stratified by body weight:
IV hydrocortisone sodium succinate (Solu-Cortef) mean 149 mg, IV dexamethasone phosphate (Decadron) mean 5.7 mg,
IV methylprednisolone sodium succinate (Solu-Medrol) mean 29 mg, oral prednisolone tablets mean 37 mg (Fig 2 example
subject: DEX 6 mg, MPL 30 mg, PNL 38 mg). Whether the DEX dose is expressed as phosphate or base is not stated.
Samples at 0, 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2, 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 28, 32 h. Plasma steroids by normal-phase HPLC, LLOQ 10 ng/mL for all
steroids, CV < 12%. Population analysis by MC-PEM in S-ADAPT 1.51; variance model with sigma2 fixed to 2.

## Model and parameters
PK: one- and two-compartment mammillary models "as previously described" (ref 7). Table I (per hour; CL/F and V/F
with F = 1 for IV DEX and MPL; mean (%CV of estimate), IIV %CV):

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Indirect-response models (no effect compartment). IC50 values, ng/mL plasma (population means):
- Cortisol suppression (Table II): DEX 0.167, MPL 1.59, PNL 9.54 (2.38 free-corrected), HC/cortisol 5.52; cortisol kout
  0.300/h (IOV 37.9%); PNL stimulation S 0.0110 mL/ng.
- Lymphocyte trafficking (Table III): DEX 8.5, MPL 19.6, PNL 31.8, cortisol 91.1; kin,L 1125 cells/uL/h, kbe 0.283/h.
- Ex vivo WBLP (Table IV): DEX 0.112, MPL 0.984, PNL 3.93, cortisol 4.72; transit kt 0.217/h (MTT about 4.6 h).
- In vitro WBLP IC50: DEX 2.51, MPL 11.5, PNL 21.9, HC 51.8 ng/mL; ex vivo potencies about 10-fold greater than in vitro.
Lymphocyte nadir about 5 h post dose; DEX suppressed cortisol over the full 32 h.

## Covariates
None.

## Caveats
n = 5 men; single doses; PK structural details deferred to Mager 2003; LLOQ 10 ng/mL limits the DEX terminal phase
(6 mg doses); PD IIV large (41-170%). The author-manuscript Table I is scrambled in the text layer.
