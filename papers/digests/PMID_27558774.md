# Choi 2017: Population pharmacokinetics and analgesic potency of oxycodone

## Study
Two prospective trials at Asan Medical Center, Seoul (PDF p.3): ASA 1-2 adults for elective open stomach, colorectal or hepatobiliary surgery, Aug 2014-Mar 2015; excluded long-term opioid users, hepatic/cardiopulmonary/renal disease, chronic pain (and laparoscopic cases in the potency study). Anaesthesia was propofol + remifentanil by effect-site TCI (Asan Pump), BIS < 60.

- **PK study** (n = 54 analysed, 849 concentrations; Table 1, PDF p.5): age 58 +/- 11 y, weight 65 +/- 11 kg, height 164 +/- 8 cm, LBM 47.2 +/- 9.6 kg (Janmahasatian), 34 M / 20 F. A single IV bolus of oxycodone hydrochloride 0.1 mg/kg (Oxynorm 10 mg/mL) before incision. Arterial samples at 0, 2.5, 5, 10, 15, 20, 30, 45, 60 min and 1.5, 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 24 h. EDTA plasma, LC-MS/MS (oxycodone-d6 internal standard), calibration 0.2-1000 ng/mL (PDF p.4). Total plasma concentration; no free fraction measured. NONMEM VII; bootstrap (2000), predictive check, randomisation test.
- **Potency study** (n = 50 analysed, 200 concentrations): 0.1 mg/kg IV at least 30 min before end of surgery. In the PACU, pain was scored by VAS every 10 min at rest and with 20 N wound compression. At the first VAS >= 3 at rest or >= 5 on compression a venous sample gave the MEC. Then 2 mg IV (3 mg if > 80 kg) every 10 min until VAS < 3 at rest and < 5 on compression, and a second sample gave the MEAC. The cycle was repeated once (first and second MEC / MEAC). MEC/MEAC were also fitted by logistic sigmoid Emax (Cp50, gamma) in NONMEM.
- Non-compartmental (PDF p.5): AUClast 11.6 +/- 3.1, AUCinf 12.1 +/- 3.3 min.ug/mL; lambda-z 0.16 +/- 0.04 /h.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: analgesia judged by VAS pain (rest < 3, wound compression < 5) after open abdominal surgery under propofol-remifentanil. Concentration measured: total oxycodone in plasma (venous samples in the PACU; EDTA; LC-MS/MS; not free; not whole blood). The paper labels it "oxycodone hydrochloride" concentration (salt/base basis unstated).

- **MEC** (first onset of pain, VAS >= 3 rest or >= 5 compression): median 31.5 ng/mL (95% CI 19.2-42.8), second 63.4 (15.6-120.1) (Figure 3; PDF p.5). The first MEC samples were taken 7.7 (4.9-12.7) min after PACU arrival, the second 64.8 (53.4-90.7) min (PDF p.9).
- **MEAC** (first pain relief after 2-3 mg titration steps): median 74.1 ng/mL (29.2-128.3), second 76.1 (32.9-132.7). First MEAC samples came 54.5 +/- 21.8 min and second 88.0 +/- 28.0 min after arrival (PDF p.9). Doses needed: 8 (6-12) mg for the first MEAC and 2 (2-4) mg for the second (PDF p.5). Authors conclude MEAC about 75 ng/mL.
- **Logistic Emax** (Eq. 1, Fig. 4; PDF p.5): Cp50 = 59.9 ng/mL (SE 2.40), gamma 3.73 (SE 0.729), IIV 182%. The authors call this the "analgesic potency", about 60 ng/mL.
- **Comparators the authors cite** (PDF p.9, from other papers): cardiac surgery MEC 6-12 and MEAC 15-25 ng/mL (Pesonen); laparoscopic cholecystectomy MEC 11-57 and MEAC 14-91 ng/mL (Kokki 2012). The authors attribute their higher values to surgery type and perioperative care.
- **ke0 / t1/2 ke0 / tPeak / hysteresis**: none reported. The plasma concentration is used directly; no effect-site model was fitted, and samples were taken at pain-state changes without a waiting interval.
- **Equianalgesic / potency ratio to another opioid**: none stated.

## Covariates
LBM (Janmahasatian formula) on V1, V2, V3; age on CL (CL falls with age: from 1.58 - (age/58)^0.203, about 0.78 L/min at 20 y to 0.51 L/min at 80 y, derived). Tested and not retained: sex, weight, height, BSA, BMI, IBW, blood pressure, heart rate, fluids, urine, blood loss. Delta-OFV for each step is in the text (PDF pp.5-6): LBM on V1 20.74, on V3 36.81, age on CL 4.69, LBM on V2 16.81.

## Caveats
Surgical patients (propofol and remifentanil on board, surgical stress, fluids and blood loss), not volunteers; ASA 1-2; Korean cohort (the authors raise CYP2D6 allele differences); metabolites not measured; the oxycodone bolus 0.1 mg/kg is higher than a clinical bolus; MEAC samples are venous but PK samples are arterial; the MEAC and MEC distributions are very wide (95% CI 29-128); the study is sponsored by Mundipharma; the bootstrap CIs for V3 theta (113-150 000) and some theta6/theta8 are very wide, so the covariate slopes are poorly determined. Supplementary Table 2 is not in this PDF.

## Verification against stanpumpR
No repo citation, so no transcription to check. Comparison with `R/drugs_oxycodone.R` (checked out at master 96311057):

- **MEAC**: library MEAC = 12 ng/mL (line 463; typical 14.4, lower typical 9.6, upper typical 24). Choi's MEAC 74.1 / 76.1 ng/mL and Cp50 59.9 ng/mL are 5-6x higher, and the library's own MEC reference (Kokki 2012, 21 ng/mL; header lines 253-262) sits between. The library header weighs Kokki and Olkkola 1994 but not Choi. Choi's end point is open abdominal surgery in patients just off propofol-remifentanil with VAS and wound compression, which the authors say gives higher values than laparoscopic surgery or cardiac surgery with fentanyl. The direction is therefore a difference of end point and population, not a transcription error. See open question 3.
- **Disposition** (derived, 65 kg, 58 y, FFM scaling off; the library's FFM scaling would change this): library Vss = 3.27 L/kg x 65 = 213 L against Choi 162 L (0.76x); library CL = 0.0147 x 65 x ageFactor 0.852 = 0.81 L/min against Choi 0.58 L/min (0.71x); library V1 = 0.131 x 213 = 28 L against Choi 11.8 L. So the library predicts a larger volume, a larger central volume and a faster clearance than Choi's Korean surgical patients. The library's terminal half-life (3.4 h in header line 133) can be compared with Choi's lambda-z 0.16 /h (t1/2 about 4.3 h, derived: ln2 / 0.16), which is longer. Possible reasons include the dose basis (open question 1), surgical state, and ethnicity, none settled by the paper. The library's age effect (0.00527 per year above 30, line 421) points the same way as Choi's age exponent.
- **Central volume**: Choi sampled arterial blood from 2.5 min, and the library header (lines 133-135) says early concentrations imply a central volume of 15 to over 100 L; Choi's V1 of 11.8 L (at LBM 47) is at the low end of that range, consistent with early arterial sampling.
- **Effect site**: Choi has no ke0, so nothing here bears on tPeak 12.36 min or t1/2 ke0 11 min (library lines 460-461, from Lalovic 2006, pupil).
- **Verdict**: partly-verified. Paper numbers were confirmed on a rendered page (Table 3, Figures 3-4); the stanpumpR comparison is of an uncited source and rests on the unresolved dose basis.
