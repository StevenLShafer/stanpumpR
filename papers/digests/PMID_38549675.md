# Liu 2024: cetirizine population PK in Chinese children, off-label doses

## Study
Prospective single-centre cohort, Peking University Third Hospital, enrolled April 2020-April 2021 (p. 2). 63 children (38 male, 25 female) under 14 y given oral cetirizine for allergic disease: mean age 6.34 +/- 3.7 y, weight 26.4 +/- 15.7 kg, height 120.1 +/- 27.7 cm (p. 4). 39 received label dose, 24 more than the label dose (Table 1 p. 4: ages 0.5-2 y n=10, 2-6 y n=22, 6-12 y n=33 by group; the count of those exceeding the label is 5, 17 and 2). At most 3 plasma samples per child (EDTA, stored at -80 C); assay HPLC-MS/MS from the group's earlier method (p. 3). Exclusions: ALT/AST over 2x upper limit, creatinine above normal or eGFR below 10 mL/min, electrolyte abnormality, steroid use (p. 2). NONMEM 7.4.4, FOCE-I, R 4.2.1.

## Model and parameters
Base model is the published Pitsiu 2004 model (Caucasian children 6 months-12 years): one compartment, first-order absorption with flip-flop (Ka defined as typical Ka plus CL/V), CL/F linear in age and sex, V/F linear in age (p. 3). External evaluation showed misspecification across the exposure range (p. 4, Supp. Fig. S2). Absorption parameters were kept fixed from Pitsiu because few absorption-phase samples exist. The final model re-estimated CL_AGE, V, IIV of CL/F and V/F, and residual error (OFV decrease -31.71). Final values are only in Supplementary Table S1 (not in PDF). Text: age scaling factor on CL/F 0.28 here vs 0.12 in Pitsiu (p. 8, comparator is the published Pitsiu model). Bootstrap (1000) median difference from final estimate about 5%; NPDE approximately normal (p. 4).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD end point; no ke0, EC50 or therapeutic concentration is given. Safety only: no adverse reactions in either dose group; ALT, AST, urea, creatinine comparable (Tables 5-6, p. 7; AST p=0.03 in Table 5, 0.09 in Table 6). Not an opioid; no opioid audit items.

## Covariates
Age (CL/F and V/F), sex (CL/F), from Pitsiu. Weight-based dosing simulations were not done because weight covariate information was lacking (p. 8).

## Caveats
Small sample, sparse sampling, absorption parameters not estimated, only five children above 0.25 mg/kg (p. 4, 8). Simulated 12 virtual children, ages 1-12, one per age, at maximum label dose (Table 2, p. 4). Stated median Cmax,ss range 160-309 and Cmin,ss 19-86 (units printed ug/mL, see open question); highest Cmax,ss at age 7 (about 307), lowest at age 6 (p. 5, 8). Off-label table 3 (p. 6): doses 5-9 mg bid (0.26-0.45 mg/kg); patient 66 median Cmax,ss 580.68 and Cmin,ss 325.40. A 5-year-old, 20 kg, received 30 mg (1.5 mg/kg) by error; measured 1725 at 2.5 h and 111 the next day (p. 7).
