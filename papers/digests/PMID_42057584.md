# Pan 2026: adalimumab PKPD and TDM simulation in psoriasis

## Study
Real-world UK cohort (BSTOP and PSORT-D, linked to BADBIR), adalimumab monotherapy in psoriasis. n = 544 met inclusion; 543 gave 946 serum samples, 539 gave 1700 PASI values, first treatment year (PDF p4). 5.9% of samples (56) were below the quantification limit and were set to half the limit, 0.005 ug/mL (p3). Median age 44.3 y, 63.2% male, median weight 88.7 kg (range 42.6-170), median baseline PASI 12.2 (Table 1, p4). Dose 40 mg SC (80 mg loading, then q2w); missing dosing records were assumed to be standard q2w with full adherence (p3, p6). ELISA assay, detection limit 0.01 ug/mL, serum; anti-drug antibody (ADA) by radioimmunoassay, positive at >= 30 AU/mL (p3). NONMEM 7.6, sequential PK then PD. PD fitted on baseline PASI >= 10 (n = 367, 1171 observations) (p4).

PD: indirect-response turnover of PASI; serum concentration inhibits lesion formation (kin) through an Emax model (Figure 1). The weight exponent on CL, left free, came out 0.67 (95% CI 0.35-1.00) (p6). Sensitivity analysis on 349 samples with full dosing records: CL/F 0.358 L/day (0.319-0.397) vs 0.386 (0.357-0.413) (p6). The covariate-functional forms (linear, power, exponential) and reference values for waist and ADA are in the NONMEM code in the supplement, not in the PDF.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Not an opioid paper; no MEAC, ke0 or equianalgesic ratio. Effect end point is PASI (lesion score; effect compartment not used, response linked directly to serum concentration through the turnover model). EC50 0.95 ug/mL, Emax fixed at 1 (full inhibition of kin). Lesion turnover half-life 17.3 days (kout 0.04/day) (Table 3, p7; text p6). Previously defined therapeutic range for trough 3.2-7.0 ug/mL (PASI75-based, ref 17), used here as a "conservative" benchmark for PASI90 (p8-9). TDM simulation (n = 1000 per regimen): week 5 trough < 3.2 ug/mL gives weekly dosing from week 6; at week 17 PASI90 status and trough vs 7 ug/mL guide q3w or weekly dosing. Outcome at 6 months: PASI90 28.3% to 38.9%, PASI75 62.4% to 70.4%, total dose +25.9% (p7).

## Covariates
On CL: weight (0.75, fixed), ADA concentration, female sex, waist circumference, hypertension (all p < 0.01). On V: weight (1, fixed). No covariates found for EC50 or kout (p9). Comparators: clearance higher in females here, whereas an RA study (ref 30) found higher clearance in men (p8-9). The ADA coefficient 0.37 differs from van Huizen's 0.87, attributed to their methotrexate co-therapy (p9).

## Caveats
Pragmatic sampling, mostly steady state, with samples neither trough nor peak, so V was fixed (flip-flop concern, p8). Missing injection dates assumed q2w with perfect adherence; missing covariates imputed by median or mode. No placebo arm. Large unexplained BSV on kout and EC50. TDM benefit is simulated, not trial-tested. Only 60% of BADBIR centres took part. A Table 2 footnote mentions "rituximab" for ka, an apparent template error in the paper.
