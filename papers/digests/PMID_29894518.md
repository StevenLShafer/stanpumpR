# Kloprogge 2018: WWARN pooled lumefantrine PK/PD meta-analysis

## Study
Individual-patient-data meta-analysis, WWARN repository. Search PubMed/Embase/etc. for studies 1990-2012; 31 studies with 4,546 PK patients identified, 26 studies with 4,122 PK patients contributed (Fig 1, PDF p 7). Artemether-lumefantrine (AL) given with fat in the model-building data. Lumefantrine measured in venous plasma (2,312 patients), venous blood (595), capillary plasma (191), capillary blood (840) (PDF p 6). Model building used the 1,347 patients with at least 2 venous-plasma samples after intact tablets (5,949 samples, median 3 per patient [2-26], Table 1 text layer); 400 intact-tablet patients with one sample were an external validation set. The age and weight columns of Table 1 are scrambled in the text layer and were not read on the page image (about 37% of the PK-model patients were under 10 years, text p 6). Pregnant women 3.1% of the PK-model data (median gestational age 23 weeks, range 13-38). NONMEM 7.3, ln-transformed concentrations, bootstrap n=1,000. A separate lumefantrine/desbutyl-lumefantrine model used 159 patients. A time-to-event PK/PD model used 3,486 patients with PCR-corrected recrudescence to day 42 (93 events, 2.67%).

## Model and parameters
Lumefantrine: two compartments, first-order absorption, additive error on ln concentrations; no third compartment (PDF p 10). Parameters centred on a non-pregnant 42 kg patient with admission parasitaemia 15,800 /uL. Allometry: CL and Q scale (WT/42)^(3/4), V scale (WT/42) (Table 2 footnote, PDF p 12). F fixed to 1 with 70.3% IIV and a Box-Cox shape. Dose-limited absorption: F = theta x (1 - Dosage/(Dose50 + Dosage)). Parasitaemia: F = theta x exp(theta_para x (ln parasitaemia - 4.2)) (the text-layer exponent '4.2' is as printed). Pregnancy: ka = theta x (1 + theta_preg).

Table 4 (PDF p 18, text layer only, column alignment scrambled, NOT image-confirmed): lumefantrine/desbutyl-lumefantrine model, 159 patients: ka 0.0409 /h; CL_LF/F 1.56 L/h; VC_LF/F 21.2 L; Q_LF/F 0.381 L/h; VP_LF/F 53.8 L; CL_DLF/F 78.4 L/h; VC_DLF/F 2,470 L; Q_DLF/F 104 L/h; VP_DLF/F 8,650 L; Dose50 3.86 mg/kg (fixed); pregnancy on ka 0.513; parasitaemia on F -0.226; sigma_LF 0.251, sigma_DLF 0.0560.

Derived day-7 effects (text, PDF p 10): day-7 venous plasma lumefantrine 24.2% and 13.4% lower in children <15 kg and 15-25 kg, and 20.2% lower in pregnant women (second/third trimester), each against non-pregnant adults given the standard six-dose regimen. Matrix/formulation differences against venous plasma after intact tablets (PDF p 14, text): venous blood 11.3% lower, capillary plasma 18.3% lower, capillary blood 23.8% lower, dispersible tablets 6.31% higher, crushed tablets 26.0% higher (from one sample per patient, flagged by the authors as unreliable).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No MEAC, ke0 or opioid-type effect-site quantity (not an opioid paper). Therapeutic exposure target is the day-7 lumefantrine concentration: values of 175, 200 and 280 ng/mL are cited from other studies (PDF p 5). Median day-7 concentration in non-pregnant adults after the standard regimen: 596 ng/mL (Fig 3 caption, PDF p 13) and 801 ng/mL (Fig 5 caption, PDF p 16); median AUC 647,025 h.ng/mL and Cmax 6,731 ng/mL (Fig 5 caption).

Time-to-event PK/PD (Table 3, PDF p 14, text layer only, not image-confirmed): Gompertz hazard with sigmoid Emax on lumefantrine (all data): baseline hazard 0.00600 /day, hazard half-life 12.8 days, IC50 92.6 ng/mL, slope 1.87 (all with poor precision). Constant-baseline-hazard Emax models: African children <15 kg IC50 194 ng/mL, 15-25 kg 9.79, Southeast Asian pregnant women 1,580 (text-layer order; precision very poor). The authors say these are apparent values (no artemether/DHA effect included) and did not use the model for dose simulation.

## Covariates
Weight (allometric on CL, Q, V), pregnancy (on ka), pre-treatment parasitaemia and dose per kg (on F). Weight-for-age z-score, age as maturation, and baseline temperature were not significant; haemoglobin (74% available) correlated with Q and VP but was not in the final model (PDF p 10).

## Caveats
Model uses venous-plasma data after intact tablets with fat; the simulations apply only to that. No artemether/dihydroartemisinin data. Few infants (4 below 1 year). Dose-limited absorption was fitted on sparse data. The text and Table 2 disagree on Dose50 (3.42 vs 3.86 mg/kg). Pooled day-7 medians differ between Fig 3 and Fig 5 captions (596 vs 801 ng/mL). In silico: a 5-day twice-daily regimen gave the highest target attainment; thrice-daily for 3 days was next; an extra tablet per dose did not equalise exposure (PDF pp 16-17).
