# Monfort 2024: sertraline popPK in pregnancy and breastfeeding (plasma, milk, cord blood)

## Study
- SSRI-Breast Milk prospective multicentre cohort (NCT01796132; Lausanne, Geneva, Morges, Lyon, Nancy).
- 38 women on sertraline (2 excluded for undetectable levels, likely non-adherence); age median 34 (22-39); weight median 73.0 kg
  at term (51.9-103.0), 71.4 kg week 1 postpartum, 64.0 kg month 1 (Table 1).
- Doses: median 50 mg/day; 25 mg (n 10), 50 (23), 75 (1), 100 (3), 125 (1), 150 (2).
- Samples: 89 maternal plasma (34 at delivery, 35 week 1, 20 weeks 4-6), 29 cord blood, 107 breast milk (fore/hindmilk pairs).
  Time after dose and steady state self-reported.
- Assay: HPLC-ESI-MS; LLOQ 1 ng/mL plasma, 5 ng/mL milk. Milk fat/protein/carbohydrate by Miris analyser.
- CYP2C19 phenotypes (n 35): PM 2, IM 5, NM 15, UM 13. CYP2D6, CYP3A4, CYP3A5, POR also genotyped.
- NONMEM 7.4 FOCE-I ADVAN6, PsN 5.3.0; bootstrap 2000; pc-VPC.

## Model and parameters
One-compartment maternal model, first-order absorption and elimination (two-compartment not better, dOFV -1.72). Milk described by
a scaling factor MPR on plasma; a fetal (cord) compartment of negligible volume linked by kMC/kCM. IIV only on CL. Proportional RUV.

**Supplement S2, NONMEM control stream "final model" (sup0001.docx; supplement, 2026-10-27).** ADVAN6, four
compartments (depot, plasma, cord, milk). The $THETA initial values are exactly Table 2's final estimates, in this
order: θ1 CL PM 69.6, θ2 V 2320, θ3 K12 (= ka) 0.315, θ4 MPR 1.42, θ5 K23 (= kMC) 0.141, θ6 K32 (= kCM) 0.383,
θ7 FAT on MPR 0.931, θ8 CL non-PM 119. So the table's row assignment above is confirmed.
- CL: `IF (CYP2C19.EQ.0) TVCL = THETA(1)`; `IF (CYP2C19.EQ.1.OR.CYP2C19.LT.0) TVCL = THETA(8)`, so a woman with a
  missing phenotype (coded below 0) takes the non-PM clearance. CL = TVCL × exp(η1). No weight, age, gestational or
  sampling-period term anywhere in $PK.
- Milk: MFAT = 2.59 (g/100 mL, the centring value), FFAT = (FAT − 2.59)/2.59, MPR = θ4 × (1 + θ7 × FFAT); milk
  prediction = plasma concentration × MPR (no milk differential equation). Doubling fat gives MPR × 1.931, a 93.1%
  rise (derived), where the paper's text says 95%.
- Cord: DADT(3) = K23 × A(2) − K32 × A(3), with no −K23 × A(2) term in DADT(2), so the cord compartment draws no
  mass from plasma (the paper's "negligible volume"); it is read with S3 = V2/1000, the same scale as plasma.
- Scaling S2 = V2/1000: dose in mg, V in L, concentrations in ng/mL.
- $OMEGA 0.172 (√ = 41.5%, Table 2's IIV CL); $SIGMA 0.143, 0.09, 0.184, commented plasma, cord, milk (√ = 37.8%,
  30%, 42.9%, Table 2). But $ERROR applies EPS(2) to milk and EPS(3) to cord, the reverse of the $SIGMA comments.
- As printed, `V2 = THETA(2)` is followed by `V2 = TVV2`, and TVV2 is never defined. The supplement calls the file
  a template; the printed lines would not run as written.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD. Infant exposure: median infant dose 6.9 µg/kg/day at 50 mg maternal dose, RID 0.95% (PM 1.2%, non-PM 0.7%); cord blood
median 3.29-33.23 ng/mL for 25-150 mg/day; C/P ratio median 0.39 (0.14-0.69) (Table 3). Doubling milk fat 2.59 -> 5.18 g/100 mL
raised MPR 95%. Cites therapeutic range 10-150 ng/mL. Not an opioid.

## Covariates
CYP2C19 PM vs others on CL (retained for clinical relevance although not significant at backward step, dOFV -2.94); milk fat on
MPR. Weight, age, sampling period, CYP2D6, CYP3A4/5, POR not retained.

## Caveats
Supplement S2 (2026-10-27): the printed control stream has an undefined TVV2 and $SIGMA comments that disagree with
the EPS assignments in $ERROR (above); missing CYP2C19 phenotype is assigned the non-PM clearance. Small n, only 2 CYP2C19 PMs; self-reported dose times; weight missing for many; peripartum physiology changes; CYP2B6 not tested.

## Verification against stanpumpR
- R/drugs_sertraline.R:122 and inst/help/drugs/sertraline.md:26: "Monfort 2024 (pregnancy)" / "of pregnancy (Monfort 2024)".
  Paper: perinatal (late pregnancy and up to 6 weeks postpartum) women; also breast milk and cord blood. **matches** (the paper
  is broader than "pregnancy": it is mainly about postpartum milk transfer). Short form only in the repo; full reference above.
- **Supplement, 2026-10-27.** `data-raw/pregnancyRegistry.R:807` and `pregnancyRegistry.csv:70` say Monfort found no
  gestational-age or pregnancy covariate on CL/F: **matches** S2, whose $PK has no such term (the paper tested the
  sampling period and dropped it). The same sentence says the only covariate was "CYP2C19 phenotype, which did not
  survive in the final model": **discrepancy**. The supplement's control stream, titled "final model", keeps CL by
  CYP2C19 (θ1 69.6 L/h PM, θ8 119 L/h others), and the paper's Results say the covariate failed backward deletion
  (dOFV −2.94) but was kept in the final model for its clinical size (42% lower CL in PMs). Wording only: the
  registry applies Westin's factor, and Monfort is used for no number, so nothing plotted changes.
  `R/drugs_sertraline.R:125` and `:148` ("not mixed in") **match**.
- **Letter, 2026-10-27:** Krutsch 2026 (Br J Clin Pharmacol 92:2423-2424, archived as PMID_39030897_letter.pdf, card
  PMID_39030897_letter.md) argues that the Miris infrared analyser's documented negative fat bias from sertraline may
  confound this paper's milk-fat effect on the milk-to-plasma ratio (+95% per doubling). stanpumpR models no milk,
  so nothing here depends on it.
