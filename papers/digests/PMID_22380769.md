# Chae 2012: metformin one-compartment PK with a signal-transduction glucose model

## Study
Single-dose study in **42 healthy male** volunteers (Methods, PDF p2 / printed 816: "42 healthy male subjects", 25.58 +/- 3.55 y, 68.63 +/- 8.14 kg). Table 1 (p2): age 26 (4) y, median 27, range 21-31; weight 69 (8) kg, range 61-78; height 1.8 (0.1) m, 1.6-1.8; FPG 98 (7) mg/dL; CLcr 107 (16) mL/min, median 106, range 90-123; total bilirubin 1.1 (0.3) mg/dL; Hb 16 (0.8) g/dL. The paper does not say how CLcr was obtained (measured or estimated). Country Korea (study site Daejeon); ethnicity not stated as such.

Design: 12 h fast, one 500 mg immediate-release tablet (Diabex, Daewoong) with 200 mL water; 12 g sugar 20 min after the dose; no food until 4 h. Plasma metformin and glucose at 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2, 2.5, 3, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12 h; 1008 observations (504 PK / 504 PD). A control (no-drug) study day one week later gave the glucose baseline; effect = % change from control glucose. Assay LC-MS/MS, LLOQ 0.05 mcg/mL; glucose by glucose-oxidase. NONMEM VI, FOCE, sequential PK then PD; bootstrap (1000), VPC.

## Model and parameters
One compartment, first-order absorption and elimination, no lag (p3). Final PK model (p5): Ka = 0.41 x exp(eta1); CL/F = 52.6 x (CLCR/106.5)^0.782; V = 113 x exp(eta3). CLcr gave dOFV -9.22 (P < 0.01); eta shrinkage for CL/F 26.22%.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: % change in plasma glucose from the subject's own no-drug control day, single dose, healthy men, after a 12 g sugar load. Model (p4): DR = Emax x Cp^r / (EC50^r + Cp^r), then three transit compartments M1-M3 with common mean transit time tau; response R = M3; proportional error. No effect compartment, no ke0: the delay is a transduction chain (tau 0.5 h each). Signal transduction beat an indirect-response model by dOFV -14.21 (Discussion, p5). Comparisons quoted: EC50 2.26 mcg/mL (Lee 2004) and 4.23 mcg/mL (Hong 2008). Monte Carlo in T2DM (500 mg bid, 2 weeks) predicted FPG falls of about 30.2 and 24.0 mg/dL from baselines 178.1 and 142.1 (p7).

## Covariates
Creatinine clearance on CL/F (power 0.782, reference 106.5 mL/min). Screened and rejected: gender (all were male), age, height, weight, total bilirubin, Hb. No weight term on V/F.

## Caveats
- Inconsistencies in the source: (1) V/F RSE 56.6% (Table 2, abstract) against 4.18% (text p5, the same figure as CL/F's). The bootstrap interval 100-126 L implies an RSE of about 5.9% (derived: (126-100)/3.92/113), which agrees with neither printed value. (2) IIV of CL/F: Table 2 gives 29.7%, but the text (p5) says covariates cut it from 47% to 41%. (3) PK residual error: 23.0 ng/mL in Table 2 against "CV ... 23%" in the text. (4) Hill: 0.55 in Table 2, 0.547 in the abstract (rounding).
- Men only, aged 21-31, CLcr 90-123 mL/min, fasted, single 500 mg dose. Using the model in women, older adults, renal impairment or at other doses (bioavailability falls with dose) goes beyond the data.
- The CLcr method is not stated.
- Immediate-release tablet only; no extended-release data. The PD is an acute single-dose response, not chronic glycaemic control.
