# Elkomy 2022: floating sustained-release vs conventional metronidazole tablets in six volunteers

## Study
Formulation paper: HPMC-matrix, non-effervescent, gastro-retentive floating tablets (800 mg, 150 mg metronidazole)
developed in vitro (buoyancy, Higuchi release in 0.1 N HCl). The reference was a **laboratory-made conventional
800 mg tablet containing 150 mg metronidazole** with Avicel and magnesium stearate, direct compression (section
2.2.6), not a commercial product.
In vivo: 6 healthy adult men (Table 2: age 31 +/- 1.8 y, 29-34; weight 66 +/- 4.4 kg, 60-72), fasted overnight and
>= 2 h; single oral 150 mg dose of each formulation with 200 mL water, two-way crossover, 2-week washout (Minia
University ethics HV12/2020). Serum sampled at 0.5, 1.5, 2, 2.5, 3, 5, 8, 12, 14, 24 h. Assay: HPLC-UV 320 nm, C18,
TCA protein precipitation; validated (intra-/inter-day accuracy ~96-106%, precision up to ~13%).
Analysis: individual one-compartment first-order absorption/elimination fits in WinNonlin 2.0 (ka, K); Cmax/Tmax
observed; AUC by linear trapezoid; MRT = 1/ka + 1/K. No lag time reported.

## Model and parameters
Table 8 (mean +/- SD, n = 6; b = significantly different from conventional):

| Parameter | Floating | Conventional |
|---|---|---|
| Cmax (ug/mL) | 2.490 +/- 0.653 | 2.060 +/- 0.473 |
| Tmax (h) | 5.66 +/- 2.804 b | 2.5 +/- 2.756 |
| ka (1/h) | 0.78 +/- 0.319 b | **1.336 +/- 0.273** |
| K (1/h) | 0.03 +/- 0.003 | 0.070 +/- 0.005 |
| t1/2 (h) | 9.52 +/- 0.510 | 9.837 +/- 0.787 |
| AUC0-24 (ug.h/mL) | 34.155 +/- 7.099 b | 28.478 +/- 6.394 |
| AUC0-inf (ug.h/mL) | 42.658 +/- 8.952 b | 35.535 +/- 7.664 |
| MRT (h) | 15.917 +/- 0.876 b | 14.342 +/- 0.541 |

Internal inconsistency: floating K 0.03/h does not match its t1/2 9.52 h (ln2/0.03 = 23 h); the conventional K and
t1/2 agree (ln2/0.070 = 9.9 h). Relative bioavailability floating/conventional about 1.2.
Derived (conventional): CL/F = 150 mg / 35.535 = 4.22 L/h; V/F = 4.22/0.070 = 60 L; model tmax = ln(ka/K)/(ka - K) =
2.3 h (derived).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None; no PD. Rationale is local H. pylori therapy.

## Covariates
None.

## Caveats
n = 6 young men; 150 mg (below the clinical 400-500 mg); laboratory-made tablets, not commercial; no IV reference
(no absolute F); sparse early sampling (0.5 then 1.5 h), so ka is weakly determined; large Tmax SD; Table 8 internal
inconsistency (floating K vs t1/2). Serum, total concentrations.
