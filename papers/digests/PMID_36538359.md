# Simons 2023: oliceridine vs morphine, respiratory PK-PD in older volunteers

## Study
Single-centre (Leiden), double-blind, randomised, four-arm crossover, 18 healthy volunteers (9 men, 9 women), age 56-87 y (mean 71), BMI 20-34 kg/m2 (mean 26), 5 with BMI >30 (PDF p.7-8). 17 completed (one withdrew after visit 2; data kept). Each visit >= 1 week apart: IV over 60 s of oliceridine 0.5 mg or 2 mg, or morphine HCl 2 mg or 8 mg (PDF p.3). Doses chosen as "equianalgesic" from literature ratios (see below). Arterial line; arterial plasma samples at 0 (pre), 2, 5, 10, 15, 30, 45 min and 1, 1.5, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 h. Assay: LC-MS/MS on K2EDTA plasma; oliceridine LLOQ 0.05 ng/mL (ULOQ 50); morphine and M6G LLOQ 0.5 ng/mL (ULOQ 250). CYP2D6 genotyped. Hypercapnic rebreathing ventilatory response (hyperoxic, FiO2 ~ 1 then rebreathe 7% CO2 in O2) at baseline and 30 min, 1-6 h. Primary end point VE55 = ventilation at extrapolated end-tidal PCO2 55 mmHg, from slope and apnoeic-threshold fit (PDF p.4). NONMEM 7 (ITS, SAEM, IMP, NUTS Bayes); M6G not modelled (potency ~1:20 for ventilation). Antinociception (cold pressor, electrical) was measured but discarded because older subjects could not score it (PDF p.9-10).

## Model and parameters
Three-compartment PK for each drug, volumes and clearances allometric (exponent 1 for volumes, 0.75 for clearances), expressed per 70 kg (so the values are absolute for a 70-kg person, not per kg). Infusion duration D1 estimated (fixed to 1 min typical, variability estimated). Oliceridine clearance depends on CYP2D6 status: 10 normal, 4 intermediate, 3 poor, 1 ultrarapid metabolisers; the three poor metabolisers had CL1 about 50% lower (33.1 vs 17.8 L/h, derived -46%). Intermediate/ultrarapid did not differ significantly. Weight via allometry lowered OFV by 43 (morphine) and 24 (oliceridine). - **MEAC / minimum effective or analgesic concentration:** none. Analgesia data were not analysed (PDF p.9-10). The C50 above is a respiratory-depression potency, not an analgesic one.
- **ke0 / t1/2ke0 / tPeak:** on VE55 (ventilation, hypercapnic rebreathing). Oliceridine t1/2ke0 44.3 min, tPeak 10.5 min; morphine t1/2ke0 214 min, tPeak 56.0 min (text, PDF p.7). The abstract's "factor of 5" is 214/44.3 = 4.8 (derived). The authors call these in line with earlier respiratory studies (refs 13, 21, 31, 32); no value for any other end point is given. Hysteresis: bolus onset of depression within 30 min; 2 mg oliceridine and 2 mg morphine returned to baseline within about 3 h, 8 mg morphine more than 6 h (Fig 1, PDF p.5; results text PDF p.6).
- **Potency / equianalgesic ratios:** the doses were taken as equianalgesic, 0.5 mg oliceridine = 2 mg morphine and 2 mg = 8 mg (ratio 4:1), from literature (PDF p.3): oliceridine 6.7x more potent than morphine in cold pressor, 3.3x in pupil constriction (phase 1, adults <50 y), 4x for pain intensity (phase 2 postoperative). Respiratory potency ratio C50 oliceridine / C50 morphine = 1.4 here (29.9/21.5 = 1.39) versus 1.6 in the younger men of ref 13 (PDF p.10). The authors state C50s are lower than in younger volunteers (they cite about 45 ng/mL for morphine in 20-year-olds, ref 39) and suggest increased respiratory sensitivity with age, but made no direct age comparison.
- Simulation (PDF p.8-9): PCA-like dosing to a steady 65% ventilation depression: morphine 10 mg bolus then 17 x 1 mg (27 mg/24 h); oliceridine 1.5 mg bolus then 20 x 0.5 mg in normal (11.5 mg), 11 x 0.5 mg in poor metabolisers (7 mg), about 40% less.

## Covariates
Body weight by allometry (volumes ^1, clearances ^0.75, 70 kg reference). CYP2D6 metaboliser status on oliceridine CL1 only (poor: 17.8 vs 33.1 L/h at 70 kg). Age/BMI/sex not tested as covariates; they were part of the sample design (older, some obese, half women). All had GFR >60 mL/min and normal liver function.

## Caveats
Small n (18), sample-size not formally computed; only three poor metabolisers and one ultrarapid; analgesia could not be measured, so no utility function or risk-benefit comparison with morphine in the old (authors' own statement); no direct comparison with a young cohort; healthy volunteers, single doses (2 mg oliceridine, 8 mg morphine at most), so effect sizes at clinical dose may differ; oliceridine V1 1.1 L is unusually small (see open questions); parameters reported as median +/- SEE. Text inconsistency: Discussion gives morphine C50 21.1, Table 2 and abstract 21.5. Funded partly by Trevena (oliceridine maker), three authors are or were Trevena employees.

## Verification against stanpumpR
Not cited, so there is no citation to verify; the paper was compared with the library's own models (R/drugs_oliceridine.R, R/drugs_morphine.R, inst/extdata/drugDefaults_global.csv).
- **Oliceridine PK** (R/drugs_oliceridine.R:19-22, Dahan 2020, two compartments): V1 28, V2 29.1 L, CL 31.7, Q 37.5 L/h (at 70 kg reference, scaled by FFM). Simons: CL1 33.1 L/h (+4% vs library, derived), Vss 52.0 vs 57.1 L (derived), but V1 1.1 vs 28 L and a three-compartment structure, so early-time plasma concentrations after a bolus would differ greatly. Clearances agree; the paper does not contradict the library's clearance. Both are healthy volunteers, Dahan young men (19-50 y), Simons 56-87 y.
- **Oliceridine tPeak** (R/drugs_oliceridine.R:52): library 4.4 min, set as a stated assumption (t1/2ke0 1 min, "too fast to measure" for analgesia). Simons gives tPeak 10.5 min and t1/2ke0 44.3 min on VE55, consistent with the library's own note that 0.25 h (Dahan 2020) is the ventilatory t1/2ke0; this paper is a second, older-adult ventilatory value, not an analgesic one, and the library's tPeak was not meant to be one.
- **Oliceridine concentrations** (inst/extdata/drugDefaults_global.csv:25: typical/endCe 27.4, MEAC 27.9, band 18.3-37.5 ng/mL): library 27.4 is Dahan 2020's respiratory potency (25% fall in ventilatory response) and 27.9 analgesic, in young men. Simons C50 29.9 ng/mL is a 50% fall in VE55, in older adults; different definition and population, same order of magnitude. No MEAC in this paper.
- **Morphine PK** (R/drugs_morphine.R:183-188, Lotsch 2002 Model A absolute: V1 17.5, V2 85.8, V3 195.6 L; CL 74.0, Q2 133.7 L/h): Simons CL1 80.1 L/h (+8% vs library, derived), Vss 100.6 vs 298.9 L (derived), V1 3.3 vs 17.5. The paper itself attributes its smaller volumes to age (older vs the 26-year-old volunteers of ref 36); the direction is therefore the paper's population versus Lotsch's (and the library's) young adults. The library does not model age on volumes.
- **Morphine tPeak/MEAC** (R/drugs_morphine.R:190-191): library tPeak 93.8 min (t1/2ke0 about 138 min per the library's note) and MEAC 8 ng/mL. Simons: tPeak 56.0 min, t1/2ke0 214 min on VE55, a ventilatory end point, in older adults; the paper's tPeak 56.0 min comes from its own (smaller-volume) PK, so it is not directly comparable with the library's 93.8 min. The library's header already cites ventilation t1/2ke0 1.24 h (Dahan 2020) and 2.6-2.8 h for pain/pupil, so 214 min (3.6 h) is longer than any of those. No MEAC in this paper (C50 21.5 ng/mL is a ventilatory potency, not an analgesic threshold).
- **Verdict:** no discrepancy in anything the repo claims from this paper (it claims nothing). The PK/PD differences above are cross-study, unreconciled, and left as open questions.
