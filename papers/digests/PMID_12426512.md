# Stempak 2002: single-dose and steady-state celecoxib NCA in children with cancer

## Study
Pilot phase 1/2 antiangiogenic trial. 11 patients (8 M, 3 F), age 11.3 ± 3.9 y (6.9-16.2), weight 39.5 ± 14.5 kg
(23.1-57.8), BSA 1.20 ± 0.30 m² (Table I); 10 recurrent solid tumours, 1 refractory ALL; normal hepatic/renal
function. Celecoxib 250 mg/m² orally bid (rounded to 100 mg; 200-400 mg). Week 1: celecoxib alone, single-dose PK
from the first dose; week 2: vinblastine or cyclophosphamide added, steady-state PK on day 1 of week 2 (9 patients).
Samples 0, 0.5, 1, 3, 6, 12 h. HPLC-fluorescence (standards 50-2500 ng/mL). NCA (WinNonlin 1.0), parameters per kg.

Steady state vs single dose: lower CL/F and longer t½ (p < 0.05), possibly a drug interaction with the
chemotherapy started that day. Adult comparison (FDA NDA 20-998, 400 mg bid, n = 8): t½ 8.9 ± 3.6 h, CL/F
0.5 ± 0.1 L/h/kg.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. Not an opioid.

## Covariates
Exploratory: t½ rose with age after a single dose (R = 0.63, p = 0.04); steady-state CL/F fell with age
(R = -0.64, p = 0.06). No sex or <12 vs teen difference.

## Caveats
12 h sampling only, so AUC∞ and t½ (3.7 h) rely on short extrapolation; children with cancer, heavily pretreated;
steady-state sampling on the day chemotherapy began; small n; one extreme patient (CL/F 4.3 L/h/kg).
