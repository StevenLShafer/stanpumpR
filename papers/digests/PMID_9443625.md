# Nguyen 1998: Population PK of total and unbound etoposide

## Study
Retrospective NONMEM (version IV, ADVAN4/TRANS4) analysis of 100 cancer patients (75 M, 25 F; age 25-85 y; 61 lung cancer), treated at Centre Claudius-Regaud 1986-1996. 57 received i.v. etoposide (1-72 h infusions, 20-360 mg/m2), 43 oral (fasting, 50-200 mg daily; two formulations, no bioavailability difference). 1,044 plasma samples (3-15 per patient over 24 h after the first dose), EDTA plasma, HPLC-UV 229 nm, teniposide internal standard, CV <10%. Unbound fraction by equilibrium dialysis (tritiated etoposide, 37 C, 6 h) in 67 patients: mean 8.4 +/- 4.2%, range 4.1-24.2%; unbound concentration = fu x total. Median patient (Table 2): age 60, BSA 1.75 m2, Scr 65 umol/L, albumin 37 g/L (some Table 2 cells garbled in the text layer, not used). Total and unbound concentrations analysed separately. No patient received both routes.

Interindividual CV left after covariates: CL 26%, Vc 34%, Vp 57%, CLu 34% (p.128). Residual CV 17% (total), 15% (unbound). Mean absolute error of predicted CL 17% (total), 19% (unbound). **Intercompartmental clearance (Q) or k12/k21 is not reported anywhere**, so a two-compartment model cannot be rebuilt from the paper.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD; no ke0 or effect site. No therapeutic or toxic concentration range or MEAC given (the paper cites exposure-neutropenia relationships in other work only). Not an opioid.

## Covariates
Total CL: serum creatinine as WT/Scr (renal part), proteinemia (PRO, g/L), albumin (ALB, g/L), liver metastasis (META, 0/1). Tested and not retained: age (redundant with WT/Scr), sex, BSA, bilirubin, liver enzymes, cisplatin pretreatment (17% lower CL, 23% lower CLu, redundant with Scr). Volumes proportional to BSA (Du Bois). CLu depends on Scr and META but not PRO or ALB. At median covariates the text splits CL into 22 mL/min renal and 15 mL/min nonrenal (p.131).

## Caveats
Retrospective; i.v. and oral groups not randomised and treated as one population; no liver-dysfunction guidance possible (few abnormal bilirubin/ALT/AST); CL/WT/Scr form is empirical; fu measured on pre-dose plasma in only 67 patients, and the unbound analysis used a fixed individual fu.
