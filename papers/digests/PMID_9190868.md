# Bertz 1997: alprazolam sensitivity and acute tolerance in young vs elderly men

## Study
- 25 young (22-35 y, mean 27.5, weight 77.9 kg) and 13 elderly (65-75 y, mean 68.2, weight 80.2 kg) healthy
  nonsmoking white men; women excluded.
- Part 1: alprazolam 2 mg IV over 2 min (open label). Plasma to 48 h; PD for 12 h.
- Part 2 (4 weeks later, double-blind crossover, 13 young + all 13 elderly who met effect criteria): placebo vs an
  individualized IV regimen (30-min load + infusion to 9 h) targeting the bolus-derived EC30 or the most achievable
  with <= 2 mg. Mean 1-9 h concentrations 17.8 (young) and 17.7 (elderly) ng/mL.
- Tests: card sorting (CS), DSST, continuous performance test (CPT), Randt memory test, NRSS (0-4).
- Assay: capillary GC-ECD (CV <= 10% at 0.25-16 ng/mL). Free fraction by equilibrium dialysis: 0.196 young,
  0.192 elderly.
- PK: noncompartmental and two-compartment (PCNONLIN 4.2). PD: per-subject inhibitory sigmoid Emax (E0 = Emax fixed)
  and linear models on **plasma** concentration after the bolus; effect-offset (tolerance) rate constant kt from
  log-linear regression during the infusion.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- EC50s are for percent decrement in CS and DSST (E0 = Emax, so EC50 is the plasma concentration giving a 50%
  decrement), after an IV bolus, without an effect-site term; acute tolerance shifts the curve right, so these are
  early-effect values. The authors note maximum response was not reached in most young men, limiting EC50 reliability.
- Elderly more sensitive at equal concentrations (AUEC/AUC ratios higher; greater MaxOE on all tests) and slower
  offset (acute tolerance) during plateau infusion.
- Illustrative calculation (Discussion): 0.5 mg PO q6h with F 0.9 gives average Css 16.7 (young) and 20.3 (elderly)
  ng/mL, with CS decrements of 11.8% and 28.7%.
- No ke0 or hysteresis analysis reported. No MEAC or potency ratio.

## Covariates
Age (young vs elderly men): lower CL, longer t1/2, lower EC50, slower tolerance in the elderly.

## Caveats
- Men only; healthy volunteers; IV alprazolam in propylene glycol.
- Plasma (not effect-site) concentration-effect relationships, with acute tolerance and no hysteresis model.
- EC50 in the young poorly defined (Emax rarely reached); only 13 of 25 young qualified for part 2.
