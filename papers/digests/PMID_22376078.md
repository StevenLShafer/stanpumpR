# Suyagh 2012: canrenone population PK after IV potassium canrenoate in children

## Study
- Prospective, sparse-sampling clinical study; 23 neonates/infants/children (2 days to 10 years; 20 were <1 year, the
  others 2, 6 and 10 years) given IV potassium canrenoate (K-canrenoate) at clinician-chosen doses in the NICU (Royal
  Jubilee Maternity Service, Belfast) or medical/intensive care wards (Royal Belfast Hospital for Sick Children).
- Table 1 (PDF p4, printed p867; continuous values median (range), although the column header says "Mean (range)"):
  101 samples, 4 (1-8) per patient; K-canrenoate dose 10.06 (3.77-70.43) µmol; measured canrenone 0.85
  (0.12-2.26) µmol/L; GA at birth 37 (25-41) wk; PNA 71 (2-3738) days = 0.195 (0.005-10.241) y; PMA 46.4
  (37.3-574.0) wk; weight 4.00 (2.16-28.00) kg; serum creatinine 32 (16-56) µmol/L; albumin 33 (29-40) (printed
  unit "mg dl-1", presumably g/L); haematocrit 33 (26-40)%; male:female 15:8; indication heart failure 8 (34.8%),
  PICU retained fluids 15 (65.2%); very preterm 2, preterm 9 (all at 36 wk), full term 12.
- Dose is given in µmol of K-canrenoate per dose; no mg/kg dosing regimen is reported.
- Assay: HPLC-UV of plasma canrenone only (LOD 6.0 ng/mL, LOQ 25.0 ng/mL; inter-day imprecision 2.4-12.6% RSD).
  Neither canrenoate (parent) nor glucuronide measured. EDTA plasma.
- NONMEM VI, FOCE-I, ADVAN6; PsN VPC, Xpose, Wings for NONMEM bootstrap (1000; 996 converged); jack-knife influence
  analysis.

## Model and parameters
One-compartment canrenone model with first-order formation from K-canrenoate (kf) and first-order elimination (ke).
Assumes the whole K-canrenoate dose becomes canrenone (conversion is actually reversible and some is glucuronidated),
so CL/F and V/F are, in the authors' words, upper limits of the true values. Weight was an a priori covariate with
fixed allometric exponents; IIV exponential, diagonal; proportional residual error.

Final model (Table 3, PDF p6, printed p869): CL/F (L/h) = 11.4 × (WT/70)^0.75; V/F (L) = 374.2 × (WT/70).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD model, EC50 or ke0. Spearman correlations only (p867): canrenone concentration associated with lower mean BP
in heart failure (P = 0.003), and with lower sodium (P < 0.001) and higher potassium (P = 0.037) in the
retained-fluid patients. Not an opioid paper; no MEAC or potency ratio. Introduction states a spironolactone dose
equivalent of 0.7 × the K-canrenoate dose, citing the BNF for Children and others (refs 2, 6, 7), not measured here.

## Covariates
Weight only (a priori, allometric 0.75 on CL/F, 1 on V/F). GA, PNA, PMA, sex, creatinine, albumin, haematocrit
not significant. IIV fell from 69.64% to 41.06% (CL) and 69.24% to 45.76% (V) with weight included.

## Caveats
- Only canrenone measured; full conversion assumed, so CL/F and V/F overstate true values (authors).
- Sparse sampling; too few early samples for a distribution phase; kf CV% 57.84.
- n = 23, mostly infants; only three children over 1 year (influential at the high end of CL).
- "Lower clearance" than adults is against Krause 1983 adults (17.6 ± 7.6 L/h/70 kg, n = 3 volunteers, a different
  method), not against any model in stanpumpR. The longer t½ vs adults is also against Krause, and the authors say
  the two cannot be compared directly.
- Table 1 header says mean, footnote says median; albumin unit "mg dl-1" looks wrong for a value of 33.
