# Saito 2022: zolpidem in cord blood and breast milk, three cases

## Study
Single-centre prospective observational case series, Tokyo, Sept 2019 - April 2022; 3 mothers (30, 33, 35 y; 58.3, 62.0, 58.5 kg), all taking zolpidem 5 mg once daily. Samples: maternal serum, cord blood, breast milk, infant serum, timed after the maternal dose (Table 1, PDF p. 3). Assay: LC-MS/MS, validated range 0.10-500.0 ng/mL, LLOQ/LLOD 0.05 ng/mL in serum and milk (p. 1035). Serum and milk, not whole blood; total concentration.

Case 1: 5 mg at GA 34-37 w intermittently; delivery 38 w vaginal, 3,091 g. Case 2: panic disorder, 5 mg on postpartum days 1-2 only (narrative says day 2; Table 1 says days 1-2); cesarean 39 w, 3,193 g. Case 3: 5 mg from 37 w; cesarean 39 w 1 d, 2,559 g; oxygen for mild respiratory depression to day 1-2, early-onset jaundice with phototherapy.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No ke0, EC50, MEAC or potency data. Clinical outcomes only: Apgar 8/9 in all; case 3 mild respiratory depression needing oxygen (resolved by day 1-2), Finnegan scores 0, 2, 3; normal 1-month checkups.

## Covariates
None modelled; pregnancy/lactation exposure only.

## Caveats
n = 3, short follow-up (1 month), no premature infants, maternal co-medication in cases 2 and 3 (alprazolam, risperidone, sulpiride, others), samples at single time points so no pregnancy-vs-postpartum PK comparison.
