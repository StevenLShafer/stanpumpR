# Brandon 2026: mitoxantrone population PK in children with AML

## Study
Phase III UK/European trial of children with newly diagnosed AML (ISRCTN12389567); 44 patients, 282 plasma samples (45.7% below LLOQ) from 145 dosing events used, from 313 collected. Age 0.9-17 y (median 9.7), weight 9.5-69.5 kg (median 27.5), BSA 0.42-1.84 m2 (median 0.98); 27 male. 41 patients got 12 mg/m2/day, 3 infants (age 0.9-1.4 y, 9.5-9.9 kg) got 0.4 mg/kg/day (given to age <12 months, weight <=10 kg or BSA <0.5 m2). One-hour IV infusion daily for up to 4 days. Sixteen patients also had cytarabine within 7 days. Sampling: pre-dose, end of infusion, 0.5, 1, 2, 6 h on day 1, pre-dose day 2, 48 and 72 h after the last infusion (p3). Assay: HPLC-PDA on plasma, linear 5-1000 ng/mL, LLOQ 5 ng/mL. NONMEM 7.5, FOCE-I then SAEM, M3 for BLLOQ; early samples at the end of infusion with artificially high values were excluded. Source: PDF p3-4, Table 1.

## Model and parameters
Two-compartment, linear; allometric weight scaling with fixed exponents 0.75 (CL, Q) and 1 (V1, V2) around median weight 27.5 kg (Equation 1, p3). IIV on CL and V2, combined error. V1 fixed to 23.2 L from O'Brien 2010 (ref 22). A three-compartment model performed comparably and was not chosen. Units: L and L/h, absolute, at 27.5 kg. Source: Table 2, PDF p6 (printed 1765).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No effect-site or PK-PD model, no ke0/tPeak, no MEAC or concentration target. Exposure (AUC from dose and individual CL) was compared with CTCAE grade 3-4 toxicity: no correlation with AUC, CL or dosing regimen (Mann-Whitney, p<0.05 threshold). AUC: all patients 309 +/- 181 ug.h/L; mg/m2 group 317 +/- 184; mg/kg group 192 +/- 75 (p7, text). Simulated (1000 virtual patients): 234 +/- 171 for 0.4 mg/kg eligible, 388 +/- 234 for 12 mg/m2 eligible, 365 +/- 229 when the former are given 12 mg/m2. Not an opioid paper; no potency ratio.

## Covariates
Body weight only (fixed allometry). BSA instead of allometry, estimated exponents, age (linear, power, maturation), bilirubin and ALT were tested and did not improve the model. Infants on mg/kg dosing had CL comparable to older children, so lower AUC came from the lower dose.

## Caveats
Only 3 infants on mg/kg dosing, all near the weight cut-off; V1 fixed, V2 poorly known (IIV 133%, shrinkage 34%); 45.7% BLLOQ; no cumulative dose or cardiotoxicity data; toxicity assessed only to day 28. Discussion compares CL with Lacayo 2002 (57 L/h/m2) and O'Brien 2010 (CL 16.4 L/h, V1 23.2 L, V2 102 L, Q 8.13 L/h) and says adult CL (11-37 L/h/m2) is similar to this cohort's; those comparisons are the authors' own.
