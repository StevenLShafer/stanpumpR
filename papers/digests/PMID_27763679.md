# Xue 2017: S- and R-warfarin PK and PCA-turnover INR model

## Study
264 Chinese patients started on warfarin once daily after cardiac surgery (valve replacement, aortic replacement,
TEVAR), Soochow University (Table 1, PDF p5): 148 M / 116 F; height 164.2 +/- 8.2 cm, weight 61.8 +/- 11.4 kg (95%
interval 40.0-86.0), BMI 22.8; ages in decade bands (15 under 30, 25 in 30-39 [printed "30-49"], 55 in 40-49, 74 in
50-59, 72 in 60-69, 23 at 70 or over); mechanical valve 73.1%, bioprosthetic 22.3%, none 4.5%. 2858 S- and R-warfarin
concentrations (total, UPLC-MS/MS, linear 25-1000 ng/mL) and 2163 INR values; median 10 concentrations and 8 INRs per
patient; median follow-up 8.8 weeks; target INR 1.5-2.5; ISI 1.05. Amiodarone 42.8%, fluconazole 6.4%, IV vitamin K1
28.8% (warfarin effect set to zero while on K1). Genotypes by pyrosequencing (Table 2): CYP2C9 *1/*1 94.3%, *1/*3 5.7%,
no *3/*3; VKORC1 -1639 AA 82.6%, GA 17.0%, GG 0.4%; CYP4F2 CC 56.1%, CT 37.8%, TT 6.1%. NONMEM 7.3, FOCE-I, sequential
PPP&D approach; covariates selected by bootstrap CI.

## Model and parameters
PK (PDF p3, p6): one compartment each for S and R, first-order absorption with absorption half-life fixed at 0.385 h
(ka 1.8 /h), F = 1, ADVAN5, fitted simultaneously. Size: NFM = FFM + Ffat (TBM - FFM) (Eq 6) with Janmahasatian FFM
(ref 29); CL scaled by (NFM/NFMstd)^0.75, V by NFM/NFMstd (Eqs 8-9); Ffat not different from 0, so FFM alone. The
standard subject (70 kg, 176 cm man) is given as FFM 56.6 kg in Methods (PDF p4) and in the Discussion (PDF p9), but
Table 3's footnote (PDF p6) and Figure 3's caption (PDF p10) say 56.1 kg.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: clinical INR. PD = Emax / (1 + (C_S / C50_app)^-Hill) inhibits PCA synthesis (Eq 2); dPCA/dt = Rsyn x
Fback x (1 - PD) - kPCA x PCA, kPCA = ln2/T2PCA, Rsyn = kPCA x PCA0 (Eq 3); feedback Fback = (PCA/PCA0)^gamma tested and
rejected (gamma CI -0.411 to 0.651; OFV change 1.084). INR = A/PCA + B (Eq 5): in vitro A, B = 0.482, 0.409 (healthy)
and 0.560, 0.386 (patients), significantly different from theory; in vivo no better than A = 1, B = 0 (P = 0.714), so
the theory-based INR = 1/PCA is recommended (PDF p7, p9). Equation 2 as printed puts C_R/RIC50 added to C50_S in the
denominator; the erratum (PMID 28597521) corrects it to C50_S x (1 + C_R/RIC50). A potency model for R gave a negative
R potency (-0.126). Worked example (Discussion, PDF p9): with the bootstrap RIC50 2.36 mg/L, 5 mg/day in a standard
patient raises the apparent C50 of S-warfarin to 0.530 mg/L, a 36% increase; the C_R is not printed.
(derived: 0.530 / 0.391 = 1.36, so the example is built on the bootstrap C50 0.391; implied C_R = 0.36 x 2.36 = 0.85 mg/L.)
In the text the parenthetical "(72.2%)" after VKORC1 AA and "(76.6%)" after CYP4F2 CC/CT are the bootstrap C50 factors
0.722 and 0.766, not genotype frequencies (PDF p7). Comparators quoted: Hamberg C50S 1.92 (AA), 3.01 (GA), 4.10 mg/L (GG)
(refs 16, 38), other reports 0.468-0.842 mg/L. No ke0, no effect site.

## Covariates
FFM allometry (above); amiodarone, fluconazole, CYP2C9 *1/*3, bioprosthetic valve, female sex on PK; VKORC1 AA and CYP4F2
CC/CT on C50_S. No significant age effect on PK, T2PCA, C50 or Hill; no sex effect on PD; POR*28 and PORrs286 not
retained. (supplement, 2026-10-27) The age-decade factors on CL (S and R together) and V (S and R together) were
nevertheless kept in the minimal PK model, 40-49 y the reference (sup0002.docx Table S3; PDF p6). For a missing
genotype the authors give re-estimated factors (Table S6), e.g. C50_S x 0.783 when only CYP4F2 is unknown.

## Caveats
Cardiac-surgery Chinese population with 91% VKORC1 A alleles; only 15 CYP2C9 *1/*3 and one VKORC1 GG patient; no
absorption-phase data; sparse clinical sampling; RIC50 imprecise (RSE 72%); supplementary covariate values (age decades
etc.) needed to reproduce the PKPD-stage individual predictions are not in the paper (supplement, 2026-10-27: nor in
sup0001.docx, which gives the age-decade effects only as Figures S3-S4, relative to 45 y; sup0002.docx Tables S3-S5
give the full-model values, but the minimal model's age factors are printed nowhere as a table, and Figure S3's
values match neither Table S3 column). Table S3 marks FFEM_VS NS although its CI (1.072-1.968) excludes 1, and
leaves FAGE70_V (CI 1.018-1.574) unmarked. Figure S2 shows the full
model over-predicting the upper tail (95th percentile) of the INR. FFM reference 56.6 vs 56.1 kg
inconsistency; Equation 2 misprinted (erratum).
