# McGough 2003: d- and l-amphetamine PK of Adderall XR (SLI381) in children

## Study
- 51 children with ADHD (98% combined subtype), 6-12 y, prepubertal; four US centres; analog classroom PK/PD study. Table 1: 44 boys (86%) / 7 girls; age 9.5 (1.9) y; weight 37.8 (13.1) kg, 21.7-73.0; height 138.7 (12.4) cm, 115.6-165.6.
- Visit 1: single oral 20 mg SLI381 (Adderall XR) at 7:30 AM. Then 5-week double-blind crossover (placebo, Adderall 10 mg, SLI381 10/20/30 mg), once daily for a week each, plus a makeup/repeat week; visit 7 PK at steady state after the final week.
- Sampling predose and 0.5, 1.5, 3, 4.5, 6, 7.5, 9, 10.5, 12, 24 h. d- and l-amphetamine by LC-MS (central lab).
- Noncompartmental (uncorrected data) and a random-effects two-compartment model (maximum likelihood); errors are SEM unless stated.

## Model and parameters
Single 20 mg SLI381, noncompartmental (text p. 686; n = 48): d-amphetamine Tmax 6.8 (0.5) h, Cmax 48.8 (2.0) ng/mL, AUC0-24 703.9 (27.5), AUC0-inf 936.7 (50.4); l-amphetamine Tmax 6.9 (0.5) h, Cmax 14.8 (0.6), AUC0-24 216.2 (8.6), AUC0-inf 309.0 (18.2). AUC units printed "ng/mL/hr" (i.e. ng.h/mL). NCA CVs 27-47%; d:l Cmax 3.3:1.

Table 2 (two-compartment, n = 51, mean (SEM), CV): d Tmax 7.09 (0.41) h, Cmax 40.10 (1.57), AUC0-24 664.9 (27.65), AUC0-inf 893.3 (62.24), CV 27.9-48.8%; l Tmax 7.72, Cmax 11.89, AUC0-24 203.6, AUC0-inf 293.2 (CV up to 56.4%).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No concentration-response relationship identified (Discussion); no EC50, ke0 or therapeutic range. Efficacy reported separately (McCracken 2003). Anorexia dose-dependent; no link between concentration and sleep disturbance. Not an opioid paper.

## Covariates
Regression (not a population covariate model): log AUC0-24 = 3.24 + 0.082 (African American) - 0.0272 age - 0.122 BMI (R2 0.46); log Cmax similar with race, age, BMI; girls' Tmax longer (likely one outlier). No weight term.

## Caveats
Children only; small steady-state groups; two samplings per child; high variability (CV 28-56%); SLI381 is designed to mimic two Adderall doses 4 h apart (Introduction).
