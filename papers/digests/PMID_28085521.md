# Knop 2017: exenatide review (PK of BID and QW formulations, clinical use)

## Study
Narrative review (no new data, no systematic search described) of exenatide twice daily (Byetta, BID) and once
weekly microspheres (Bydureon, QW): pharmacokinetics (section 2, about one column), DURATION phase 3 efficacy
(Table 1), safety, new formulations (QW suspension for autoinjection, ITCA 650 implant) and ongoing trials
(Tables 2-3).

## Model and parameters
No model. Summary PK statements (section 2, printed p555 = PDF p2, continuing p556):
- Exenatide BID described as dose proportional; single SC 2.5 and 5 mcg: Cmax 56 and 85 pg/mL, AUC 159 and
  340 pg·h/mL; tmax about 2 h. Source cited: ref 23, **McCormack PL, Drugs 2014;74:325-351** (a secondary review).
- Elimination half-life of BID 2.4 h (refs 4, 24).
- QW: same peptide in poly(D,L-lactide-co-glycolide) microspheres; release in three stages: initial (first 48 h),
  diffusion (about 2 weeks), erosion (about 7 weeks) (Fig. 1a, ref 24 DeYoung 2011). Weekly dosing overlaps
  injections so release becomes near-constant (Fig. 1b, a superposition of single-dose profiles from Brunton 2016).
- QW concentrations of 50 pg/mL are sufficient for fasting glucose reduction after 2 weeks of 2 mg weekly; steady
  state after 6-7 weeks; measurable at least 8.5 weeks after a single injection; declines over about 3 months after
  stopping (refs 25 Fineman 2011, 26 Kim 2007). PK similar in Asian and Caucasian populations (refs 27, 28).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD model. 50 pg/mL named as sufficient for fasting-glucose lowering on QW (above). Clinical end points
summarised: DURATION trials HbA1c −1.3 to −1.9% over 24-30 weeks; weight −2.0 to −3.7 kg; antibody incidence and
titres (peak 6-22 weeks).

## Covariates
Renal impairment (section 6.2.4, printed 566): half-life substantially increased and clearance significantly
reduced (p < .001) in end-stage renal disease; mild-to-moderate impairment similar to healthy controls, no dose
adjustment (ref 87, Linnebjerg 2007). Elderly ≥ 75 y with renal impairment: higher, not significantly, exposure
after 5 or 10 mcg (ref 90). No numerical clearances.

## Caveats
- Secondary source: the single-dose numbers come from another review (McCormack 2014), not a primary study.
- "Dose proportional" sits beside Cmax 56 → 85 pg/mL for a doubled dose (ratio 1.5) and AUC 159 → 340 (2.1).
- Industry-linked authorship and editorial support are declared.
