# Levey 2007: the MDRD Study equation re-expressed for IDMS-standardized creatinine

## Study
Laboratory calibration study (CKD Epidemiology Collaboration), no patients dosed. Three split-sample steps
(Fig. 1, PDF p2): (1) CAP LN24 survey samples with NIST LC-IDMS-assigned values measured on the Roche
enzymatic assay at the Cleveland Clinic Research Laboratory (CCRL) (Table 1, p3); (2) a CCRL calibration panel
of 40 pooled sera measured on Roche enzymatic vs the Beckman Synchron CX3 kinetic alkaline picrate (Jaffe)
assay used in the MDRD Study; (3) 253 frozen MDRD Study samples (third baseline visit) re-assayed on the CX3
in 2004 vs their original 1989-1991 CX3 values. Ordinary least squares; non-significant intercepts dropped;
SE of the final factor by the delta method.

## Model and parameters
Calibration chain (p4, Fig. 1 legend): Roche vs IDMS slope 1.00 (SE 0.004; intercept -3.01 umol/L, omitted
for simplicity); Roche vs CX3 slope 0.906 (SE 0.004, r2 0.996 [Fig. 3: 0.9994]); CX3 2004 vs original slope
1.046 (SE 0.0024 in text, 0.002 in Fig. 4 legend, r2 0.9986 [Fig. 4: 0.9870]). Product 1.00 x 0.906 x 1.046 =
0.95 (SE 0.005): standardized Scr = 0.95 x original MDRD Study Scr (Table 2 eq. 1, PDF p6, printed p771).

Table 2 (PDF p6, read on the rendered page):
- Eq. 2 (published, before calibration): GFR = 186 x Scr^-1.154 x age^-0.203 x 1.212 (if black) x 0.742 (if female).
- Eq. 3 (re-expressed): GFR = 175 x standardized Scr^-1.154 x age^-0.203 x 1.212 (if black) x 0.742 (if female).
- Footnote b, SI units: GFR = 30849 x standardized Scr(umol/L)^-1.154 x age^-0.203 x 1.212 x 0.742.
- Units: GFR mL/min/1.73 m^2 (indexed); Scr mg/dL; age years.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Not applicable (not a drug study). No PK, ke0, MEAC or potency data.

## Covariates
Serum creatinine, age, sex, race (black / non-black), as in the equation above. No weight or BSA term: the
output is already indexed to 1.73 m^2.

## Caveats
Two-step calibration through regression slopes rather than direct re-assay against IDMS; intercepts omitted;
possible storage effects on frozen samples (authors judge them small). The authors note worse performance at
higher GFR and suggest reporting numeric values only below 60 mL/min/1.73 m^2 clinically (p6). Equation
developed in CKD populations; not validated here in children, pregnancy or the elderly.

## Verification against stanpumpR
- R/renalFunction.R:321: `175 * scr^(-1.154) * age^(-0.203) * (0.742 if female)` - **matches** Table 2 eq. 3
  (coefficient, both exponents, female factor). Scr is in mg/dL (assumed normal 0.8/0.9 mg/dL,
  covariates.md:74), the paper's unit. Output is mL/min/1.73 m^2, used indexed as the roxygen states, as the paper
  defines it.
- Race term 1.212 omitted (roxygen line 303-304 says so: race is not a Profile field): a documented choice, not
  a transcription error; non-black patients get the paper's equation exactly.
- Age floor 18 (line 320) is the repo's own choice; the paper says nothing about children.
- tests/testthat/test-renalFunction.R:142-146 and test-drugs-exenatide.R:5 use 175, -1.154, -0.203, 0.742:
  **match**.
- The help/header text calling it the "simplified MDRD" / "four-variable IDMS-traceable" form is accurate. The
  context note's doubt whether the 1999 MDRD paper is meant: the 1999 paper (ref 5) is the six-variable
  equation and the 186 four-variable form is ref 6 (2000 abstract); the coefficient 175 the code uses comes
  from this paper, so the citation is correct.
- Whether each downstream model was fitted with the 175 or 186 form is not settled by this paper (open question).
