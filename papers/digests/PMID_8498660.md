# Grunwald 1993: droperidol PK in anaesthetised children

## Study
15 ASA I-II children for tonsillectomy/adenoidectomy enrolled; 3 excluded (catheter failure); 12 analysed
(Table 1): 3.5-12.0 yr (mean 7.4 +/- 2.4), 15.5-75.0 kg (30.3 +/- 17.8), 6 M / 6 F. No premedication;
halothane/N2O induction, atropine 0.02 mg/kg, vecuronium 0.1 mg/kg; maintenance N2O 70%, morphine 0.1 mg/kg,
halothane. Droperidol 0.05 mg/kg IV bolus. Venous plasma at 1, 2, 4, 6, 10, 20, 40, 60, 120, 180, 240, 300,
360 min. Radioimmunoassay (Janssen kit, Fischler method), detection limit 0.2 ng/mL. Noncompartmental:
individual biexponential fits (weight 1/fitted^2) for beta; trapezoidal AUC/AUMC; Cl = dose/AUC,
Vdss = Cl x MRT; normalised per kg. Extrapolated AUC < 10% of total.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None measured. Authors discuss that the ~100 min half-life does not explain prolonged antiemesis and
speculate on slow CNS equilibration. No ke0. (Not an opioid paper.)

## Covariates
No correlation of t1/2, MRT, Vdss or Cl with age, weight or fluid volume (r^2 < 0.25). No sex differences in
demographics.

## Caveats
n = 12, 6 h sampling, halothane/morphine anaesthesia, wide weight range (one 75 kg 12-year-old). The
discussion misquotes Fischler's adult Vss as 2.4 L/kg (Fischler: 1.40 L/kg Vss, 2.04 Vdbeta), which
exaggerates the child-adult volume contrast; Lehmann is cited as "Van Reer".
