# Liu 2001: hypothermia and isoflurane MAC in children

## Study
Prospective, unpremedicated children 4-10 yr with left-to-right shunt (ASD/VSD) having open-heart surgery with hypothermic bypass, n = 33, randomised by nasopharyngeal temperature at skin incision: A 37 degC (n = 10), B 34 (n = 10), C 31 (n = 13). Inhalation induction (N2O/O2 then isoflurane 0.2-5%, N2O stopped), succinylcholine for intubation, isoflurane in O2. End-tidal isoflurane (Datex) and temperature held stable at least 15 min before skin incision. MAC by Dixon up-and-down: first patient in each group at an arbitrary end-tidal (A 1.70, B 1.50, C 1.30 vol%), each next patient changed by 15% according to movement (flexion/withdrawal of an extremity) in the previous one within 1 min of incision. MAC = mean of the end-tidal concentrations just permitting and just preventing movement. Olive oil:gas partition coefficient measured at 37, 34, 31 degC by tonometry and gas chromatography (n = 8 per temperature). Demographics Table 1 (PDF p.2 / printed 430): age 6.1, 6.1, 5.8 yr; weight 18.2, 17.8, 17.8 kg; M/F 8/2, 5/5, 7/6.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: movement on skin incision (quantal), isoflurane in oxygen, end-tidal concentration as the brain-partial-pressure surrogate, children with intracardiac mixing. MAC 1.69 vol% at 37 degC is similar to Cameron's 1.60% in children (cited in the text). MAC x olive oil:gas falls with cooling, so the authors conclude the Meyer-Overton lipid-solubility argument alone does not explain the fall. No ke0 or effect-site data. No opioid content (opioid audit: none).

## Covariates
Body temperature (nasopharyngeal) is the only covariate; linear in temperature over 31-37 degC. No age or weight dependence examined (groups matched).

## Caveats
Three temperature groups only (31-37 degC); 33 children aged 4-10, congenital shunt disease, not adults; N2O used for induction before isoflurane, the stable-for-15-min period is short for deep-tissue equilibration at low temperature; MAC by up-and-down with only about 10 patients per group; surface cooling. The paper's own 5.1%/degC and 16.2 degC zero disagree with its regression (see open questions).
