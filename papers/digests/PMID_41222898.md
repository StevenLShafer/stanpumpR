# Yu 2026: TDF/TAF semi-mechanistic PK in pregnancy and postpartum

## Study
- Secondary NONMEM 7.5 (Laplacian) analysis of deidentified data from four trials in women: CONRAD 137 (single and multiple dose; TDF 300 mg, TAF 25 mg, TAF 10 mg; non-pregnant, HIV-negative), DOT-DBS (TDF 300 mg, non-pregnant), IMPAACT P1026s (46 pregnant women with HIV on TDF; 25 on TAF without cobicistat) and IMPAACT 2026 (28 pregnant on TAF without cobicistat) (Table 1, PDF p6). Pregnant data: second trimester, third trimester, 6-12 weeks postpartum; no first-trimester data.
- Analytes: plasma TAF, plasma TFV, TFV-diphosphate (TFV-dp) in PBMC and in dried blood spots (DBS). Concentrations converted to micromol/L (PBMC volume 282 fL/cell; DBS from RBC volume and cells per 3-mm punch). BLQ by Beal M3 (plasma, DBS) and M1 (PBMC). TDF plasma levels cannot be measured (half-life about 24 s), so 300 mg TDF is modelled as an equivalent 136 mg TFV dose entering via Ka2.
- No bootstrap; uncertainty is RSE from the covariance step.

## Model and parameters
Eight-compartment model (Fig. 1, PDF p8): TAF depot -> TAF plasma (1 cmt, V2, CL1); a fraction F_TFV of absorbed TAF becomes TFV, of which Ffast appears at once in plasma TFV and the rest passes through a transit compartment (Kslow); TDF depot -> plasma TFV (2 cmt: V5, V6, Q, CL2); PBMC and DBS TFV-dp as biophase compartments with first-order formation and elimination. ## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD end point, ke0, tPeak, MEAC, or potency ratio (not an opioid paper). Intracellular TFV-dp is the "effect-site-like" biophase: half-life after TDF is 433 h in DBS (about 18 days) and 60 h in PBMC (text; see open question). Simulated steady-state troughs (TDF 300 mg or TAF 25 mg daily, 1000 virtual women; PDF p7-8) relative to non-pregnant: TDF plasma TFV -30.6% (2nd trimester) and -18.0% (3rd); TDF PBMC TFV-dp -20.3% and -11.8%; TDF DBS TFV-dp -20.0% and -11.6%; TAF plasma TFV -36.0% and -17.7%; TAF PBMC TFV-dp -17.6% and -5.3%. TAF gives about 5x higher PBMC TFV-dp than TDF (abstract, PDF p1). No therapeutic or toxic concentration targets are given.

## Covariates
Only pregnancy stage (indicator terms TRI2, TRI3, PP, additive effects on the non-pregnant value). TDF: TFV apparent clearance +24.9% (2nd trimester), +13.1% (3rd), -9.3% postpartum, each relative to the non-pregnant state (PDF p7). TAF: bioavailability -17.3% (2nd), -5.1% (3rd), +18% postpartum, relative to non-pregnant women (PDF p7). Comparator is the non-pregnant women in CONRAD 137 and DOT-DBS, in the same joint fit.

## Caveats
Authors note: no full covariate search (creatinine, weight not tested); no first-trimester data; unit conversions need cell-volume assumptions; TFV monoester not measured; no DBS data from pregnant women so hemodilution untested; linear kinetics cannot explain PBMC vs DBS discordance in pregnancy; HIV status pooled; no bootstrap. 35.9% of TDF-arm plasma TFV and about 62-70% of TAF observations in pregnancy datasets are BLQ (Table 1).
