# Meylan 1986: MBC versus actual killing of viridans streptococci by amoxicillin and vancomycin

## Study
In-vitro study. 24 viridans group streptococcal strains from blood cultures of endocarditis patients: 21 isolated
1978-1981 in Lausanne, 3 from Geneva (PDF p1, printed 418). Species (API 20S): 10 S. mitis, 7 S. sanguis,
2 S. salivarius, 5 unspeciated. Macrobroth dilution in Mueller-Hinton broth + 1% IsoVitalex, twofold dilutions
of amoxicillin and vancomycin, final inoculum about 10^6 CFU/mL (also 10^5, 10^4; logarithmic and stationary
phase), 24 h at 35 °C. MIC = lowest concentration with no visible growth. MBC = lowest concentration killing
>99.9% of the inoculum in 24 h; tolerance = MBC/MIC > 32. Carry-over minimised (10 µL spread over a whole plate,
penicillinase plates for amoxicillin). Killing also expressed as delta log CFU per tube (inoculum log10 CFU/mL
minus 24 h survivors), mean of three experiments (PDF p2, printed 419).

## Model and parameters
No pharmacokinetic model. Susceptibility results (Fig. 1 and Results, PDF p2; abstract, PDF p1):

- Vancomycin MIC ≤1 µg/mL for all 24 strains. Fig. 1 bins: about 17 strains in the 0.25-0.5 bin and 7 in the
  1-2 bin (read from the bar heights; derived: cumulative ≈71% at ≤0.5, so MIC90 = 1).
- Vancomycin MBC: low in 3 nontolerant strains (abstract "<1 µg/mL"; Results: equal to or twice the MIC);
  **>128 µg/mL in 21 tolerant strains**, MBC/MIC ratios 128 to 4,000.
- Amoxicillin MIC ≤0.25 µg/mL for all; MBC <0.5 in 6 strains, >128 µg/mL in 18 tolerant strains.
- Mean delta log CFU among tolerant strains ranged from about 3 log10 to <1 log10 (abstract). Representative
  vancomycin strains (Fig. 3, PDF p3): S. sanguis II 0.14, S. mitis 1 1.72, S. mitis 2 2.84. Amoxicillin
  (Fig. 2): 1.05, 2.70, 3.05. Repeat experiments within 0.5 log10. S. sanguis less killed than S. mitis
  (amoxicillin P = 0.013, vancomycin P = 0.037, Fisher exact).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End points: visible growth (MIC), 99.9% kill at 24 h (MBC), and the delta log CFU at 24 h. Maximal killing was
reached at or near the MIC and did not increase with concentration up to 128 µg/mL; the authors argue the MBC
splits a continuous distribution into "susceptible" and "tolerant" arbitrarily. Time-kill (2 or 4 h) correlated
with 24 h reduction (data not shown). Not an opioid paper: no MEAC, ke0 or potency ratio.

## Covariates
Species (S. sanguis less killed than S. mitis). Inoculum phase and density (10^4-10^6) did not change delta log
CFU in 4 of 5 strains tested with amoxicillin (Table 1, PDF p4).

## Caveats
Strains from 1978-1981, single centre region, macrobroth method of the time; MBC distribution read from a
histogram; the authors themselves regard the MBC as poorly reproducible near the 99.9% cutoff. No serum,
protein binding or in-vivo data.
