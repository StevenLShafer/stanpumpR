# Jeffcoat 1989: cocaine disposition after IV, intranasal and smoked doses

## Study
- 16 healthy male paid volunteers, recreational cocaine users (mostly intranasal, 1-5 y, 2-10 times/y); age
  24.3 +/- 0.9 y (19-32), weight 75.7 +/- 2.1 kg (61-88), height 180.8 +/- 3.3 cm (144-198), all printed as SD
  (PDF p.1, printed 153). Each subject studied once, by one route only (to limit radiation): iv n = 4, ni n = 6,
  smoked n = 6. Bioavailability is therefore between subjects.
- IV: 23.0 mg [4-3H]cocaine HCl (= 20.5 mg cocaine base) in 3 mL saline over 1 min, antecubital vein (PDF p.2).
- Intranasal: 106 mg [4-3H]cocaine HCl (= 94.6 mg base) as two lines, one per nostril, through a glass tube over
  30 s; delivered dose by tritium subtraction 94.3 +/- 0.5 mg base (PDF p.2).
- Smoked: 50 mg [4-3H]cocaine free base in a glass pipe in a ~250 C oil bath, inhaled every 30 s for 5 min
  (10-s puff, 15-s hold); inhaled dose 39.5 +/- 1.6 mg base equivalents (PDF p.2).
- Sampling: venous blood (EDTA + NaF, plasma at 4 C) frequently for 3 h, then to 48 h; urine to 72 h; feces 72 h.
- Assay: SEP-PAK C18 extraction then HPLC with fractions counted for tritium (cocaine and benzoylecgonine); recovery
  of cocaine 92.3 +/- 3.4% over 3-433 ng/mL, CV 3.9% (PDF p.4). Concentrations in ng/mL plasma (Table 3).
- Model: iv mono- or biexponential; ni and si one-compartment first-order absorption (eq. 4); nonlinear least squares;
  AUC by trapezoids plus C_last/beta; F from AUC ratios corrected for individual half-lives (eqs. 2-8, PDF p.3).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None reported. Heart rate and blood pressure were recorded every minute for 60 min but are not reported
(PDF p.1). No ke0, EC50 or threshold concentration. Protein binding (PDF p.3): 14% to 11% bound to 4% HSA over
4 ng/mL-9.3 ug/mL; 34% to 24% to 0.1% alpha1-AGP over 5-300 ng/mL; AGP: n = 2.03 sites, KD 0.30 mmol/L.
Blood/plasma ratio 0.89 (1 ng/mL) to 1.12 (7.5 ug/mL). Urinary recovery 64-69% of dose in 72 h, < 1% as cocaine
(Table 1).

## Covariates
None modelled. Clearance and volume reported per kg only.

## Caveats
- Small groups (4/6/6), each subject one route: F is a between-group ratio (range up to 103%).
- iv distribution phase seen in 3 of 4 subjects; ni and si fitted with one compartment.
- Single-dose recreational users, young men only. Weight SD probably an SEM (open question).
- Smoked bioavailability is of the inhaled amount; only about 44% of inhaled material was cocaine in simulated
  smoking (ref 12, PDF p.6).

## Verification against stanpumpR
- **ka_IN = ln(2)/11.7 /min** (`R/drugs_cocaine.R:130`; test line 50): paper 11.7 min (abstract, PDF p.4); from
  Table 4, 0.693/3.54 h = 11.7 min (derived). Unit (min) correct. **Matches.**
- **F_IN 0.80** (`R/drugs_cocaine.R:131`): 80 +/- 13% (PDF p.6). **Matches** as a transcription. The 80% was
  computed against Jeffcoat's own iv group (CL 23.0 mL/min/kg, about 1.74 L/min at 75.7 kg, derived), and is
  applied here to Chow's disposition.
- **iv distribution t1/2 11 min, elimination 78 min** (`R/drugs_cocaine.R:55-57`, `cocaine.md:13`,
  `illicitRouteRegistry.csv:8`): 11.1 min (3 of 4 subjects) and 78 min (PDF p.4). **Matches.**
- **Smoked absorption about 1.1 min** (`illicitRouteRegistry.csv:10`, `cocaine.md:29`, spec review :29): 1.1 min
  (Table 4 mean, 1.3 min by mean-data fit). **Matches.** Not implemented.
- **"exposure ... is Jeffcoat's"; single path "reproduces the overall intranasal exposure"**
  (`R/drugs_cocaine.R:82-83`, `cocaine.md:21, 23`): **discrepancy.** Jeffcoat's absorption on Chow's disposition does
  not reproduce Jeffcoat's own nasal data (derived, see open questions): predicted peak 353 vs 220 ng/mL observed,
  peak at 30 vs 44 min, much faster decline (Chow-based t1/2 43.5 min vs Jeffcoat's 78-80 min after ni), AUC 0.81x.
  The 1.6x peak is the composite's, not Jeffcoat's. Mean-curve peaks understate individual Cmax somewhat
  (individual tmax 43 +/- 28 min), but not by this much. The test's 372 ng/mL for 100 mg (`test-drugs-cocaine.R:87`)
  is the model's own arithmetic, consistent with this.
- **Disposition comparison** (uncited for these quantities): Jeffcoat iv V-beta 2.70 L/kg and CL 23.0 mL/min/kg vs
  the model's V 131.8 L (1.88 L/kg at 70 kg) and CL 2.10 L/min (30 mL/min/kg at 70 kg) (derived): Jeffcoat's volume
  is about 1.4x larger and clearance about 0.77x, hence his longer half-life. The header cites only Chow and Javaid
  for the half-life and does not mention Jeffcoat's 78 min as a check against the 43.5 min.
- Dose basis: see open questions (HCl vs base).
