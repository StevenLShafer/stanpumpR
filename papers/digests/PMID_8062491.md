# Blum 1994: gabapentin pharmacokinetics across renal function

## Study
- Open, single-dose, three-centre study (Buffalo, Richmond, Freiburg); 60 subjects (35 M, 25 F), 18-75 y, ≥50 kg,
  none on dialysis. Groups by measured CLcR: ≥60 (n≈15), 30-59 (n≈15), <30 mL/min (n≈30, ≥6 under 15).
- 400 mg oral gabapentin after 8 h fast, fasting to 4 h. Plasma to 72 h (groups 1-2), to 168 h (CLcR ≤30) and
  264 h (<15); urine collected in intervals to the same horizons.
- HPLC with UV detection after TNBS derivatization; plasma calibration 0.02-5.00 µg/mL.
- NCA: λz from terminal log-linear regression; CL/F = Dose/AUC; CLR = Ae/AUC. CLcR = endogenous (24 h urine
  creatinine with 12 h plasma creatinine).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None (PK only; adverse events listed: dizziness 10, fatigue 2, headache 2, somnolence 1). Not an opioid: no MEAC,
ke0, tPeak or potency ratio. Table II gives dose adjustment: ≥60 → 1200 mg/day (400 tid); 30-59 → 600 (300 bid);
15-29 → 300 (300 qd); <15 → 150 (300 qod).

## Covariates
CLcR (endogenous) linearly on CL/F, CLR and λz. Volume and absorption reported as unchanged by renal function;
no metabolism detected even in severe impairment.

## Caveats
NCA, single 400 mg dose; group 3 spans a wide range; one subject withdrawn at 48 h (anuric renal failure);
CLcR measured, not estimated; urinary recovery (~43-49%) taken as F at 400 mg; industry-funded.
