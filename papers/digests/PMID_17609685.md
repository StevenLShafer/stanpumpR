# Urban 2008: OCTN1 genotype and gabapentin renal clearance

## Study
- In vitro: HEK-293 cells stably expressing OCTN1-503L (reference) or OCTN1-L503F; gabapentin uptake Na⁺-independent,
  trans-stimulated by L-ergothioneine; variant has reduced transport.
- Clinical: healthy 18-40 y subjects from the SOPHIE cohort (San Francisco), homozygous 503L/503L (n=11, 10 analysed)
  or 503F/503F (n=9); normal renal function. Single 400 mg oral dose, fasting, meals from 2 h. Plasma 0.25-36 h,
  urine in intervals to 36 h. LC/MS/MS, LLOQ 75 ng/mL.
- One-compartment model fitted jointly to plasma and urine in WinNonlin; total clearance assumed = CLR; F estimated
  by the model and by Ae/Dose (nearly identical). CLcR = 24 h measured creatinine clearance.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. Not an opioid: no MEAC, ke0, tPeak or potency ratio. fu > 97% stated in the introduction.

## Covariates
CLcR alone explained R² 0.33 of CLR, genotype 0.22, both together 56%; genotype the only predictor of net
secretion (R² 0.35). Age, height, weight, sex not significant. F not affected by genotype.

## Caveats
Small n per genotype; single 400 mg dose; homozygotes only (enriched genotype design); mean ± SE, not SD.
Demographic table partly garbled in the text layer.
