# Zuppa 2019 supplement mmc5: NONMEM control stream, allometric model

## Study
Supplement to the infant cardiac-surgery dexmedetomidine study. Main card, with the mapping of all 24 supplementary
files and what they show: `PMID_31623840.md`.

## Model and parameters
NONMEM control stream `final_allometric.ctl` (Appendix 5): ADVAN3 TRANS4, two periods split at `TIME.GT.CPBT` (after CPB)
vs before (pre-bypass and bypass together), 10 THETAs: CL with (WT/70)^0.75 and, after CPB,
Age/(THETA(9)+Age); V1 during CPB x (TEMP/37)^THETA(10); proportional plus additive error; 8 ETAs (a BLOCK(4) and 4 diagonal).

## Caveats
This is not the Table 7 model, which has three periods and 14 THETAs: it is the pre-review two-period version, superseded after review (Shafer, personal communication 2026-10-27; see the main card). The `$THETA` block is
identical in the allometric and linear files and its comment labels are shifted one line, so the numbers are
initial estimates, not results; do not take parameters from it.
