# Macquaire 2002: propofol TCI induction in ASA III cardiac patients

## Study
Prospective two-group study, 44 ASA III adults (age < 75 y, LVEF > 30%, lean body mass within 25% of ideal) scheduled for cardiac surgery, two senior anaesthetists, Hopital Erasme, Brussels. Premedication alprazolam 0.5 mg orally the night before and 1 h pre-op (p.1011). Propofol was given by an effect-site-targeting TCI (home-made "TOOLBOX", Shafer-Gregg algorithm) using the Marsh PK set as in the Diprifusor, t1/2 keo 2.6 min (abstract; 2.67 min p.1010). Ce started at 0.5 ug/mL and was raised by 0.5 ug/mL steps, each step only after 95% of the previous Ce was reached; bolus computed at a maximum pump rate of 1200 mL/h. Group 1 (n = 21): gradient between Cp and Ce limited to 1 ug/mL. Group 2 (n = 23): no limit. End point: loss of consciousness (LOC) = no response to verbal command and mild shaking, assessed every 15 s (MOAAS < 2, Table 2). BIS (Aspect A-1000, v3.2) recorded 30 s after LOC; arterial pressure from a femoral catheter. No blood samples: all concentrations are calculated, not measured.

Demographics (Table 3, mean +/- SD): age 67 +/- 11 and 68 +/- 11 y; weight 74 +/- 11 and 75 +/- 15 kg; height 164 and 167 cm; M/F 12/9 and 13/10; LVEF 44 +/- 15.4% (group 1) vs 56 +/- 11.4% (group 2), P < 0.01.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: loss of response to voice and mild shaking (MOAAS < 2), plus BIS 30 s later. Concentrations at LOC are model-calculated (Marsh PK, keo 0.266 /min), so Ce and Cp are only as good as that set.
- Pooled mean Cp at LOC about 1.9 ug/mL (both groups) and Ce 1.08 to 1.43 ug/mL, in alprazolam-premedicated ASA III patients, mean age about 67 y. The authors compare this with higher LOC concentrations in young healthy patients from other studies (Cp 3.40/4.34 at 50/90% in Vuyk's women; Ce 4.55 median, Wakeling; 4.4 Cp/1.9 Ce, Struys; all quoted in the discussion, p.1014).
- No ke0 was estimated. Quoted: Marsh keo 0.266 /min (t1/2 2.6-2.67 min), Schnider keo 0.385 /min (1.8 min), Struys's finding that keo above 0.239 /min tracked BIS better (p.1014).
- Simulated Cp-Ce gradient never above 1.65 ug/mL with the Marsh set for the stepwise scheme; with Schnider's set at Ce target 2.5 the maximum gradient is under 3.5 (Figs 4-5, p.1014-1015).
- Opioid audit: not an opioid paper. No MEAC, ke0 for an opioid or potency ratio.
- Hemodynamics: systolic and diastolic pressure fell significantly at LOC in both groups (P < 0.001), under 20%, no hypotension (SAP < 100 mmHg) in either group (Table 5, p.1013-1014, text layer only; table layout scrambled so not transcribed).

## Covariates
None modelled. The authors suggest alprazolam synergy and the lower LVEF in group 1 (slower distribution, longer time to LOC) as explanations for the low LOC concentrations and the group difference in Ce.

## Caveats
Calculated, not measured, concentrations; Marsh underpredicts measured propofol in cardiac and elderly patients (positive bias 21.2% before bypass, refs 5, 6 in text), so the true Cp at LOC may be higher. Non-randomised comparison with an LVEF imbalance. Benzodiazepine premedication confounds the concentration. Small n. Ce at LOC depends on the keo assumed.
