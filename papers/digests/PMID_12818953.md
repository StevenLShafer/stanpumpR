# Coda 2003: intranasal hydromorphone bioavailability in volunteers

## Study
Open-label, randomised, three-way crossover, 1-week washouts. 24 healthy non-smoking volunteers (13 M, 11 F),
18-36 y (25.3 ± 5.3), 51-94 kg (men 76.3 ± 9.1, women 66.9 ± 9.3), all within ±20% of ideal weight; fasted.
Treatments (all hydromorphone **HCl**): A 2.0 mg IV infused over 10 min; B 2.0 mg IN (one 0.1 mL spray, 1 mg, in
each nostril); C 1.0 mg IN (one spray). IN solution 10 mg/mL, pH 4.0 citrate buffer (same composition as
Dilaudid-HP). Delivered doses by pump weight: 1.046 mg and 2.069 mg (not used in the PK). Samples 0, 5, 10, 15,
20, 30, 45 min, 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12, 16 h. Plasma LC-MS/MS, LLOQ 20 pg/mL. Noncompartmental (WinNonlin 3.2);
F = Dose_IV·AUC_IN/(Dose_IN·AUC_IV).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD end point measured; the authors say the study was not designed to measure analgesia. Adverse events only
(somnolence 38%/63%, dizziness 33%/58% after 1/2 mg IN; bad taste 88% of IN doses; no respiratory depression).
- MEAC: none reported.
- ke0 / t½ke0 / tPeak / hysteresis: none reported.
- Potency: introduction cites hydromorphone as 3-7.5 times morphine (refs 19-23), not measured here. Cites oral F
  about 51% (CV 59%) from Parab 1988 and oral tmax 1 h, rectal 1.5 h.

## Covariates
None analysed.

## Caveats
Young healthy volunteers; physician-administered sprays aimed at the turbinates; IV given as a 10 min infusion
(so IV tmax 10 min); doses are hydromorphone HCl; F computed from mean AUC0-inf with n = 23. Single dose only.

## Verification against stanpumpR
- **Transcription** (`R/drugs_hydromorphone.R:110-113`, test :49-50): 24 volunteers, 1 and 2 mg IN vs 2 mg IV, F
  52.4% and 57.5%, median tmax 20 and 25 min: **matches** Table 1. F_IN 0.55 matches the paper's "approximately
  55%"; a 20 min peak matches the 1 mg median (2 mg median is 25 min). **matches**.
- **Help** (`hydromorphone.md:15`, `absorption.md:37-38`): "about 20 minutes, from Coda's data", "bioavailability
  about 0.55": **matches**.
- **Consistency of F with the model's clearance**: **discrepancy (method, not transcription)**. Coda's IV clearance
  (derived) is about 1.94 L/min, close to Davis 2004 (113 L/h = 1.88 L/min) and Lohela 2021 (1.88 L/min), whereas
  the model uses Drover's 1.30 L/min (`v1Ref * k10` = 11.2 × 0.116). The file's own oral argument
  (`R/drugs_hydromorphone.R:58-63`) is that exposure is F/CL and that an absolute F from a faster-clearing cohort
  must be rescaled. Applied to IN: model AUC for 2 mg IN = 0.55 × 2000 µg / 1.299 L/min ≈ 14.1 ng·h/mL against Coda's
  9.58 (about 1.47× high, derived at the reference clearance); the CL/F-matched value would be 1.299 / 3.48 ≈ 0.37.
  The peak (model 2.93 ng/mL) is matched to the measured mean Cmax (3.0-3.5), so the curve matches early and
  overstates the tail. Report for the owner to decide; not fixed here.
- Doses: paper doses are hydromorphone HCl, as is labelled practice; no salt correction issue.
