# Cheung 1999: IV bolus nicardipine during anaesthesia for cardiac surgery

## Study
Prospective, randomised, double-blind, self-controlled, four-arm dose-ranging study. 47 enrolled, 40 met
haemodynamic criteria and were randomised, 10 per group: 0.25, 0.50, 1.00, 2.00 mg nicardipine by rapid injection
(< 5 s) into the right atrial port of the pulmonary artery catheter, after sternal wire closure, when SBP > 120 mmHg
(p2). Anaesthesia midazolam, fentanyl 15-75 ug/kg, pancuronium, isoflurane 0.4-1.0% held constant; no other IV
anaesthetic 15 min before to 30 min after. Table 1 (p4): age 65-71 y (group means), weight 74-86 kg, mostly male
(8:2, 8:2, 9:1, 7:3); 32 CABG, 3 redo CABG, 4 AVR, 1 mitral repair. Mixed-venous samples (PA catheter) at 2, 5, 7,
10, 20, 30, 45, 60, 90, 120, 180, 240 min. HPLC-UV, LOD 1.6 ng/mL, LOQ 5.4 ng/mL. Radial arterial pressure and HR
as the average of three beats every 15 s for 240 s, then every 60 s. SBP held 85-140 mmHg: SBP < 85 treated with
phenylephrine 100 ug boluses, SBP > 140 with nitroglycerin or nitroprusside (p2).

Consistency checks (derived): ln2 x Vcentral / CL = 1.8, 2.2, 3.5, 5.5 min, close to the printed redistribution
half-times (2.0, 2.5, 3.5, 5.4), so that row behaves as a central-volume elimination time, not an independent
rate. CL x t1/2(elim) / ln2 = 0.57, 0.42, 0.57, 0.98 L/kg, 1.3-2.2 times the printed Varea. Central volumes 0.02-0.12
L/kg are below plasma volume. Clearance roughly doubles from 0.25 to 1-2 mg. Sampling ended at 4 h. Mean
concentration curves only (Fig 3); no individual parameters.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: radial arterial SBP/DBP/MAP decrease from a 45-s baseline. Maximum MAP decrease (mean +/- sd) 15+/-5,
21+/-3, 24+/-4, 30+/-2 mmHg for 0.25, 0.5, 1, 2 mg (dose effect P < 0.01); SBP 18+/-2, 32+/-5, 36+/-4, 51+/-4 mmHg
(p3). **Time to maximum decrease in MAP** 66+/-10, 63+/-7, 56+/-10, 101+/-11 s (dose effect P < 0.01) (p3); mean of
the four group means 71.5 s (derived). Abstract: maximum response within 100 s; conclusion: maximum response at
about 60 s for 0.5-1 mg. Linear relation of mean MAP change to mean plasma concentration over 0-600 s: y = 4.79 -
0.23x, r = 0.88 (Fig 3 inset; x presumably ng/mL). No ke0, no Emax/EC50 model. HR unchanged (P = 0.18); CO up 0.3
L/min only in the 1 mg group at 600 s. Hypotension (SBP < 85) in 0/10, 2/10, 3/10, 6/10; authors note phenylephrine
rescue may have caused the maximum response and its time to be underestimated in the higher dose groups (p6). No
MEAC, no potency ratio.

## Covariates
None.

## Caveats
Anaesthetised cardiac surgical patients, central (right atrial) injection, pulmonary-artery sampling, group-mean
fits only, dose-dependent apparent clearance, 4-h sampling, vasopressor/vasodilator interventions during the
study, no control group (authors, p6). Table 2 volume units misprinted.
