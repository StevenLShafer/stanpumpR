# Björnsson 2023: population PK of IV, sublingual and CAM2038 depot buprenorphine

## Study
- Four Camurus trials (two phase 1 in healthy volunteers, two phase 2 in OUD): IV, SL and SC CAM2038 weekly (Q1W) and monthly (Q4W). Healthy participants received an IV dose before CAM2038; OUD participants had no IV data (p. 1438).
- Initial model: 236 participants (147 healthy, 62%; 89 OUD), 10,260 concentrations; update added 160 mg Q4W (16 participants). Final: **252 participants, 10,658 observations**.
- Table 1 (initial model, all): age median 35.0 y (18-65); weight 72.2 kg (50.4-127); BMI 24.4; CLCR median 112 mL/min; ~85% normal renal function, ~15% mild impairment.
- Plasma LC-MS/MS, 0.0250-10.0 ng/mL; BLQ excluded. NONMEM 7.3.0/7.5.0, FOCEI, log-transformed data, additive error on log scale; SCM covariate search (p < 0.01 forward, < 0.001 backward); pcVPCs. No external validation.
- Simulations for a typical healthy male, 35 y, 72.4 kg.

## Model and parameters
Three-compartment disposition, first-order elimination. SL: two parallel pathways, (1) lag + zero-order input (DSL1) into a depot absorbed first-order (ka,SL1), fraction FSL1 of the bioavailable dose; (2) first-order (ka,SL2). CAM2038 Q1W: zero-order-then-first-order + first-order; Q4W: two first-order pathways; F for CAM2038 fixed to 1, IV F = 1. Table 3 (estimate, RSE%; IIV CV%):

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- No PD model, no ke0, no MEAC (not an analgesia study); no potency ratio.
- Discussion (p. 1440) quotes literature thresholds (refs 11, 33): **≥ 1.25 ng/mL** for suppression of opioid withdrawal; **≥ 1.2 ng/mL or ≥ 2-3 ng/mL** for blocking opioid reinforcement; mean ~1.2 ng/mL generally sufficient to block 18 mg hydromorphone subjective effects. (The ≥ signs are lost in the text layer; context makes them thresholds.) All total plasma buprenorphine in OUD.
- CAM2038 bioavailability 6-8× SL; norbuprenorphine/buprenorphine exposure 5-7× lower after CAM2038 (unpublished).

## Covariates
Age and weight on CL; population (OUD vs healthy) on Vc and Fq1w1; sex on Fq1w1. Not significant: race (Black vs other), CLCR (normal/mild only), others. Weight on FSL and Vc dropped (RSE > 50%).

## Caveats
- Adults 18-65 only; age power model unbounded at young ages.
- Vc for OUD identified without IV data; IIV on Vc 78.6%.
- Sponsored by Camurus; no external validation; model-predicted steady-state values somewhat lower than NCA values.
- SL dose range of the data is in the supplement (not read here).
