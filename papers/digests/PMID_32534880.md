# Chavez-Castillo 2020: lamotrigine popPK in Mexican adults

## Study
- Observational cross-sectional study, Epilepsy Clinic, Hospital Central, San Luis Potosi, Mexico (p. 2903).
- Adult outpatients (> 18 y) on oral LTG mono- or polytherapy for >= 2 weeks; pregnancy, renal or hepatic
  insufficiency excluded.
- n = 73, one sample each, taken from 2 h after the dose to 30 min before the next (Table 1): 20 M / 53 F; age 31
  (18-78) y; weight 65 (46-100) kg; height 1.61 m; serum creatinine 0.74 (0.5-1.10) mg/dL; CLcr (Cockcroft-Gault)
  128.54 (55.47-163.45) mL/min; LTG dose 300 (100-600) mg/day; 5 smokers. Co-medication includes carbamazepine
  (12, 16.4 %), valproate, phenytoin, primidone, oxcarbazepine, topiramate, levetiracetam, lacosamide (the text layer
  misaligns the remaining counts). UGT2B7-161 CC/CT/TT 58.9/38.4/2.7 %.
- HPLC-UV, linear 0.5-30 mg/L. NONMEM 7.3, FOCE, ADVAN2 TRANS2; bootstrap 1000; VPC.

## Model and parameters
One compartment, first-order absorption. F, ka and V fixed to literature values (Grasela 1999, ref 18) because of
one sparse sample per patient; only CL/F estimated (p. 2903).

CL/F (L/h) = 1.82 x (1 - 0.465 VPA) x (1 + 0.841 CBZ); V/F (L/kg) = 1.8 x weight (Table 2 footnote).
Table 3 (at mean weight 66 kg): CL 0.027 L/h/kg monotherapy, 0.015 with VPA, 0.050 with CBZ, 0.027 with both;
recommended doses 4.5-6.0, 3.0, 9.0-11.0 and 4.5-6.0 mg/kg/day.
(derived: monotherapy half-life with V 1.8 L/kg and CL 1.82 L/h = 48.0 h at 70 kg, 44.6 h at the 65 kg median.)

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. Uses the 2.5-15 mg/L trough reference interval (Patsalos 2008, ref 12) as the dosing target. Not an
opioid paper.

## Covariates
Co-medication with valproate (-46 %) and carbamazepine (+84 %); no continuous covariate significant. UGT2B7-161 trend
(CT/TT 23 % lower CL) not retained.

## Caveats
- One sample per patient; ka, V and F not estimated (fixed); V/F 1.8 L/kg is above the 1.28-1.36 L/kg the
  Introduction itself quotes for patients and Cohen's 1.2 L/kg.
- Small n (73); mostly women; single centre.
- The model holds no weight term on CL, though V is per kg.
