# Lyauk 2016: population PK of d-methylphenidate in healthy Danish adults with CES1 covariates

## Study
Two Bispebjerg studies (NCT02135263, NCT02147535), 122 healthy Caucasian adults, single oral 10 mg racemic Ritalin IR
given 1 h after a standardised breakfast (after an overnight fast; two subjects without the meal). Study I: 44 subjects,
samples pre-dose and 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2, 2.5, 3, 4, 6, 8, 10, 24, 33 h. Study II: 78 subjects, one sample at 3 h.
503 d-MPH concentrations (enantioselective assay, details in supplement). NONMEM 7.3 FOCE-I; covariates: weight, BMI,
sex, nine CES1A1 non-synonymous SNPs, CES1 diplotype; forward p < 0.05, backward p < 0.005; missing genotypes handled by
the EXTRA method (missing-data indicator parameters); 2000-sample bootstrap; permutation tests. Demographics in the
supplement (not in the PDF).

## Model and parameters
Three transit compartments (N estimated 3.05, fixed at 3; ktr = (N + 1)/MTT) into an absorption depot, ka, then
two-compartment disposition, first-order elimination (Figure 1). Typical-value equations (p. 340):
MTT = θ1 (1 + Gender θ8); CL/F = θ4 (1 + rs71647871 θ9)(1 + oneCES1A2 θ11)(1 + twoCES1A2 θ12)(1 + rs115629050 θ14)
(WT/70)^0.75; Vc/F = θ5 (WT/70); Q/F = θ6 (WT/70)^0.75; Vp/F = θ7 (WT/70). Gender = 1 for female.

Full IIV variance-covariance on MTT, CL/F, Vc/F; off-diagonals not printed. Simulated AUC0-inf increases vs wild-type
70 kg man: rs71647871 GA +143%, two CES1A2 +70%, rs115629050 TG +68%, one CES1A2 +22%; 50 kg +29%, 100 kg -24%;
combinations 2- to 7-fold (pp. 340-342).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No ke0, no concentration-effect model, no therapeutic range.

## Covariates
Weight (fixed allometry), sex on MTT, CES1 rs71647871 (G143E), rs115629050 (A270S), CES1A2 copy number on CL/F.

## Caveats
- Single 10 mg dose, healthy Caucasian adults, dosed 1 h after breakfast (fed state).
- Dose record (racemic 10 mg vs 5 mg d-MPH) is not stated in the main text.
- 42% missing rs115629050 genotypes; all TG carriers and many single-CES1A2 carriers were in the sparsely sampled
  Study II; authors call the CES1 simulations preliminary.
- Wide bootstrap CIs on ka, Vc/F, Q/F, Vp/F.
