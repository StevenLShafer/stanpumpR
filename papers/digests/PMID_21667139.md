# Sherwin 2011: optimal sparse-sampling design for pioglitazone in septic children

## Study
- A simulation and optimal-design methods paper. No new patients were studied.
- Prior data: Christensen et al. 2005 (ref 12). These are single- and multiple-dose pioglitazone PK (15, 30, 45 mg) in
  36 adolescents with type 2 diabetes. The mean concentration-time data were used, with n = 12 per dose in Fig. 1.
- NCA was done in WinNonlin 5.1. Population PK was done in NONMEM 7.1 (FOCE with interaction), with PDx-Pop and Xpose.
- Simulated datasets: n = 20 per dose.
- Three paediatric age/weight groups (weights and ages from CCHMC PICU sepsis records and the CDC growth charts),
  each with 20 subjects and 200 observations:
  - Group 1: 3.8 y (2-6 y), 14.4 kg (7-28 kg)
  - Group 2: 9.6 y (6.1-11.9 y), 36.5 kg (28.1-48 kg)
  - Group 3: 15.5 y (12-17 y), 61.6 kg (48.1-80 kg)
- Optimal design was done in WinPOPT 1.2 and PFIM 3.2.

Other numbers:
- **NCA of a single 15 mg dose** (PDF p. 3, printed 435): half-life 7.94 h, Cmax 454 ng/mL.
- **Table 1** (PDF p. 4, printed 436), mean (range). Christensen (literature):
  - half-life 8-9 h (4-17) at every dose
  - 15 mg: Cmax 478 (333-886) ng/mL, AUC0-24 4506 (2849-7522) ng·h/mL
  - 30 mg: Cmax 587 (120-1225) ng/mL, AUC0-24 5618 (1076-8868) ng·h/mL
  - 45 mg: Cmax 1053 (701-1715) ng/mL, AUC0-24 10438 (6297-16897) ng·h/mL
- **Table 1, simulated values** (same page):
  - 15 mg: t½ 7.9 (4.9-11.5) h, Cmax 498 (357-870) ng/mL, AUC 4933 (2534-8094) ng·h/mL
  - 30 mg: t½ 8.3 (5.29-13.3) h, Cmax 629 (343-1156) ng/mL, AUC 5563 (2134-9142) ng·h/mL
  - 45 mg: t½ 9.3 (5.18-16.95) h, Cmax 1087 (589-1743) ng/mL, AUC 10663 (4784-20134) ng·h/mL
- **Allometric-model AUC0-24 at 15 mg** (PDF p. 7, printed 439): 4407 (1725-8153) ng·h/mL in the 2-6 y group and
  5105 (1925-8953) in the 6.1-11.9 y group. At 30 mg the 12-17 y group gives 6557 (2784-9953).
- **Christensen's adolescent values, as quoted in the Discussion** (PDF p. 13, printed 445):
  - AUC: 4474 ng·h/mL at 15 mg, 7845 at 30 mg and 11370 at 45 mg
  - CL/F, mean (SD): 3.23 (1.08) L/h at 15 mg, 5.53 (5.39) at 30 mg and 3.92 (1.19) at 45 mg
  - These AUCs differ from those in Table 1, and the text does not say why.
- **Background figures from the literature** (PDF p. 3): tmax is about 1.5 h (0.5-3 h) over doses of 2-60 mg.
  Single-dose Cmax runs from 101 to 3500 ng/mL (ref 13).
- **Dosing proposed**: 15 mg daily for under 48 kg (groups 1 and 2) and 30 mg daily for 48-80 kg (PDF p. 8).
- **Optimal sampling times**: 0.5 h (fixed), 2, 6 and 21 h. The windows are 1 h 40 min-2 h 40 min, 5 h 50 min-7 h
  and 14 h-23 h 30 min. Simulated annealing gave 0.5, 2.2, 6.5 and 20.5 h (PDF p. 11-12, Fig. 6).
- **Sample size for 20% CV on the PK parameters** (PDF p. 10):
  - Group 1: 18 subjects at 50% BSV, 24 at 80%
  - Group 2: 13 at 50%, 20 at 80%
  - Group 3: 6 at 50%, 20 at 80%

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. This is an antidiabetic and anti-inflammatory drug, not an opioid. There is no ke0, no MEAC, no target
concentration and no potency ratio. The exposure target is AUC comparable with Christensen's adolescents at 15 mg.
The paper mentions active metabolites M-III and M-IV (and M-II) but does not model them.

## Covariates
Weight was the only significant covariate on CL/F and Vd/F, through power-model allometry (Eq. 1, PDF p. 6).
The exponents are described as fixed to their theoretical values, but the numbers are not printed. Age, height,
sex, length of stay and mortality score were not significant. Maturation was omitted on the argument that CYP2C8 and
CYP3A4 are mature by 1-2 years.

## Caveats
- All paediatric "data" are simulated from summary or mean adolescent data. The authors say this cannot support
  dosing recommendations by itself.
- No septic patient was studied. Sepsis effects on PK are unknown.
- The printed Vd/F values and ka units look misprinted (see open_questions), and citation numbering is inconsistent
  (Christensen cited as [16] and [12]). One sentence on PDF p. 7 says the allometric fraction of adult dose "is
  estimated at ." with the value missing. Group 3's dose is printed as "3 mg" on PDF p. 11, apparently 30 mg.
- The weights of the source adolescents are not reported here.

## Verification against stanpumpR
**not applicable**: nothing in the repo cites this paper. As an independent comparison, in line with the README rule,
the library's pioglitazone model was checked against the adolescent data reported here.
- **The library's model.** `R/drugs_pioglitazone.R:67-69` uses CL/F 2.12 L/h, Vc/F 21.4 L, Q/F 0.349 L/h,
  Vp/F 26.3 L and ka 1.82/h, divided by 60 for per-minute units. Its cohort is 73.1 kg (line 52).
- **Clearance.** This paper's base-model CL/F, fitted to Christensen's mean data from adolescents with type 2 diabetes,
  is 3.03 L/h. Christensen's own 15 mg CL/F, quoted here, is 3.23 L/h. The library's 2.12 L/h is 0.70x and 0.66x of
  these (derived). So the library's adult clearance is LOWER than these adolescents' clearance. The comparator is a
  different population: diabetic adolescents, mean-data fits, weights not given here. Healthy adults versus
  adolescents is not a claim that the library's model is wrong.
- **Exposure at 15 mg.** The Kadam model as coded (unscaled, F = 1, Euler simulation in the scratchpad) gives
  AUC0-24 about 5761 ng·h/mL, Cmax about 582 ng/mL at about 1.6 h, and AUCinf 7075 ng·h/mL (derived). The paper
  gives 4506 ng·h/mL and 478 ng/mL for Christensen's 15 mg group. The model is about 1.28x and 1.22x higher,
  consistent with the lower CL/F.
- **Half-life.** The model's half-lives are about 5.9 h (alpha) and 62 h (terminal, small amplitude) (derived).
  Christensen reports 8-9 h (4-17) by NCA, which lies between the two and is not directly comparable.
- **Dose units.** `inst/extdata/drugDefaults_global.csv:152` gives ng/mL with "mg PO qd" dosing, consistent with
  this paper's ng/mL and mg doses.
- **Therapeutic range.** `inst/extdata/therapeuticRanges.csv:117` gives none established, which agrees with this paper:
  it states no concentration target.

No transcription in the repo depends on this paper, so there is nothing to mark as a discrepancy.
