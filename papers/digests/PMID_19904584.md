# de Winter 2009: protein binding of MPA and MPAG, renal transplant

## Study
Pooled data from two Erasmus MC studies in de novo renal transplant recipients: a randomised concentration-controlled trial (RCCT; ciclosporin + steroids; HPLC for total MPA, free MPA by ultrafiltration, total MPAG) and the IMPDH-activity study (tacrolimus + steroids, MMF 1000 mg bid start; LC-MS/MS; total and free MPA and MPAG). 75 patients, 93 profiles (CsA 48, tacrolimus 45); 489 tMPA, 489 fMPA, 488 tMPAG, 210 fMPAG samples. Table 1 (PDF p.8) medians: age 51/53 y, weight 67/78 kg, albumin 0.51 mmol/L both, CrCL (Cockcroft-Gault) 44/45 mL/min, MMF 1350/1000 mg bid (CsA/tacrolimus). Doses and concentrations converted to micromoles (MMF 433.5, MPA 320.3, MPAG 496.5 g/mol). NONMEM VI, FO, log-transformed data. Standard errors could not be obtained (final run did not minimise, rounding errors; 55 h run time).

## Model and parameters
Free MPA (fMPA): 2-compartment with lag and first-order absorption; free MPAG (fMPAG): 1 compartment; both bind competitively and saturably to a binding compartment of capacity BMAX; total = free + bound. MPAG enters a gallbladder compartment (EHC) that empties into fMPA central at TGB for DGB. Parameters are apparent (V/F, CL/F). Table 2 (PDF p.9, printed p.549), typical values, IPV in %:

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD fitted. No ke0, t1/2ke0, tPeak, EC50, MEAC. Exposure targets quoted: tMPA AUC0-12 30-60 mg*h/L with ciclosporin (ref 7). fMPA is taken as the active moiety; fMPA AUC in simulation 0.82 to 0.96 mg*h/L (CsA) and 0.84 to 0.93 (tacrolimus) as albumin falls 0.6 to 0.4 mmol/L (text, PDF p.17).

## Covariates
Albumin on BMAX (power, ref 0.5 mmol/L); CrCL on CL fMPAG; ciclosporin on k57 (EHC nearly abolished). No CrCL effect on fMPA CL. Simulated (1 g MMF bid): albumin 0.6 to 0.4 mmol/L lowers tMPA AUC 30.1 to 17.7 (CsA) and 31.1 to 20.4 (tacrolimus) mg*h/L; free fraction MPA 2.4-5.3% (CsA), 2.7-4.1% (tacro). CrCL 50 to 10 mL/min: tMPAG AUC 831 to 3794 (CsA), 723 to 2647 (tacro) mg*h/L; tMPA AUC 23.9 to 21.5 (CsA, falls) and 25.1 to 31.6 (tacro, rises).

## Caveats
No SEs; competitive-binding structure complex; BMAX (35100 umol) far above plasma albumin capacity (about 1530 umol), attributed to other proteins/interstitial albumin; gallbladder timing fixed because no samples 4-10 h; small underprediction of peaks; IPV on k57 fixed at 71% from literature.

## Verification against stanpumpR
No citation to check. As an independent comparison with R/drugs_mycophenolate.R (header lines 27-43, 58-81): the direction agrees. Here lower albumin lowers tMPA AUC (higher apparent total clearance) by less binding; van Hest's CL and V1 carry negative albumin powers (-1.07, -1.13) so lower albumin raises total CL and V1. The CsA-treated simulation here has tMPA AUC fall slightly with falling CrCL; the repo's CL exponent -0.12 on CLCR also raises CL as CrCL falls. The repo's remarks that unbound concentration is not modelled and that EHC is absent, and that tacrolimus patients have higher exposure, are all supported by this paper (EHC k57 0.0796 /h without CsA vs 0.000159 with; tacrolimus tMPA AUC rises with renal impairment). The repo's 30-60 mg*h/L target matches ref 7 as quoted here. A free-MPA model from this paper would not be a drop-in for the total-MPA one: parameters are in micromoles, apparent, with free-drug CL 747 L/h.
