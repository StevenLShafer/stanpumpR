# Grossherr 2009: propofol in exhaled air vs arterial plasma, cardiac surgery

## Study
Pilot, 12 adults for cardiac surgery, randomised to Group L (3 mg/kg/h, n=6) or Group H (6 mg/kg/h, n=6). Table 1 (PDF p 3), mean (range) age, mean (SD) height and weight: Group H 1 F/5 M, age 58 (21-79) y, 176 (7.0) cm, 83 (19.7) kg; Group L 2 F/4 M, 69 (65-76) y, 168 (6.2) cm, 80 (14.1) kg. Induction etomidate 0.3 mg/kg, sufentanil 0.5 mcg/kg, pancuronium 0.1 mg/kg; sufentanil 1 mcg/kg/h until weaning from bypass. Continuous propofol infusion started after a propofol-free baseline (T0); samples at 10, 20, 30, 40 min (T1-T4, before bypass); infusion continued at 3 mg/kg/h in both groups in ICU until weaning (T5) and eye opening (T6). Arterial plasma propofol by reversed-phase HPLC with fluorescence (range 0.001-8.0 mcg/mL; inter-assay CV 8.7% at 0.05 and 4.5% at 1.00 mcg/mL). Breath: 100 mL of mixed inspired/expired gas on Tenax tubes, thermal desorption, GC-MS (inter-assay CV 12.1%, recovery 106%). Assay is on plasma, not whole blood. Each breath/plasma value normalised to its own T4 value.

## Model and parameters
No PK model fitted. Reported concentrations (mean (SD), mcg/mL, plasma, Results p 610): T2 L 1.6 (0.38) vs H 2.6 (0.52); T3 L 1.3 (0.32) vs H 2.5 (0.53); T4 L 1.4 (0.41) vs H 2.6 (0.27). ## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD end point, no ke0/tPeak, no MEAC, no potency ratio. Finding: breath propofol rose and fell more slowly than plasma (no equilibrium in the first 20 min; at eye opening breath unchanged vs T4 while plasma fell); ratios to T4 in Fig 2. Discussion quotes Harrison: breath 50 and 5 ppb associated with plasma 8 and 4 mcg/mL (predicted). Not an opioid paper (sufentanil only a co-drug).

## Covariates
None analysed; groups compared by Mann-Whitney U, small n, P-values unadjusted.

## Caveats
n=6 per group; cardiac surgery, bypass haemodilution, hypothermia and different ventilation in ICU; mixed inspired/expired gas rather than alveolar (authors say true alveolar values may be higher); internal inconsistency in the stated ranges (see open question).
