# Eickhoff 1981: in-vitro activity of Ro 13-9904 (ceftriaxone)

## Study
In-vitro susceptibility study; no patients dosed, no pharmacokinetics. Ro 13-9904 (ceftriaxone, MW 598.5 as the
disodium salt, Fig. 1) compared with cephalothin, cefamandole, cefoxitin, cefotaxime and moxalactam against 591
clinical isolates (1977-1979, University of Colorado): 50 E. coli, 50 Klebsiella, 36 Enterobacter, 23 Serratia,
50 P. rettgeri, 43 P. mirabilis, 34 Salmonella, 10 Shigella, 50 P. aeruginosa, 50 S. aureus, 50 S. epidermidis,
50 group D streptococci, 15 each S. pyogenes, S. pneumoniae, H. influenzae, N. gonorrhoeae, N. meningitidis, and
20 B. fragilis (PDF p1, printed p435).
Methods (PDF p2, printed p436): microtiter broth dilution in Mueller-Hinton broth (Trypticase soy for group D
streptococci), final inoculum about 10^6 CFU/mL, 35 °C overnight; MBC by replicator subculture of about 0.003 mL,
MBC = lowest concentration with no growth or one colony. Agar dilution for streptococci, Neisseria, Haemophilus,
B. fragilis. Inoculum effect: 10 strains per species at 10^-2 vs standard 10^-4 dilution (Table 2 columns labelled
10^5 and 10^7 CFU/mL). Supported by a Hoffmann-La Roche grant.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End points: MIC (no visible growth) and MBC (no growth or one colony on subculture), in protein-free broth, so
free drug. Abstract (PDF p1): MBCs generally no more than twofold above MICs, the exception being large S. aureus
inocula. Other findings (PDF p5, printed p439): beta-lactamase-positive S. epidermidis had a geometric mean MIC of
8 vs 2 µg/mL for weakly positive or negative strains; raising the pH from 6 to 8 raised the S. aureus geometric mean
MIC of ceftriaxone 14-fold; P. aeruginosa geometric mean MIC 30.8 at pH 6 and 13.4 µg/mL at pH 8; no difference
between Mueller-Hinton and Trypticase soy broth. No PK, no ke0. Not an opioid paper.

## Covariates
Test conditions only: inoculum size (Table 2), pH, beta-lactamase production, medium.

## Caveats
- 1977-1979 isolates, before ceftriaxone's clinical use; modern resistance (ESBL, AmpC) is absent.
- MBC data are geometric means of 10 strains per species; no MBC50, MBC90 or per-strain MBC/MIC distribution is given,
  so the "twofold" statement describes the typical strain, not the 90th percentile.
- MICs reported on a 3.1/6.3/12.5 dilution series (not the modern 0.25/0.5/1 scale).
- Sponsored by the manufacturer.
