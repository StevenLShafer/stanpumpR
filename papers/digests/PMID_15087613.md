# Mertens 2004: alfentanil's effect on propofol pharmacokinetics

## Study
Eight healthy male volunteers (age 24 +/- 3 yr, weight 70 +/- 4 kg, height 1.87 +/- 0.03 m; within 30% of ideal weight), randomized two-way crossover, 3 weeks apart (p 796, 797). Both sessions: propofol 1 mg/kg IV over 1 min then 3 mg/kg/h for 59 min (session A control). Session B added an alfentanil TCI (target 80 ng/mL, three-compartment Maitre parameters) from 10 min before propofol until 6 h after it stopped. In two volunteers propofol was stopped at 40 min in both sessions (excess sedation). Samples: venous (contralateral forearm) blood propofol at 1-60 min and then to 360 min after stopping (428 samples), by HPLC (CV <= 3%, LOD 11.7 ng/mL, WHOLE BLOOD); plasma alfentanil by GC (CV <= 4%, LOD 0.8 ng/mL). Cardiac output by thoracic bioimpedance. Analysis: NONMEM V, FOCE with interaction, Bayesian/NONMEM three-step approach; covariates entered as Theta = TV x exp(eta + sum alpha x (cov - median)); selection by AIC. Mean plasma alfentanil per volunteer 78.2-117.1 ng/mL; median 92.7 ng/mL (p 797-799). Measured whole-blood propofol was 18% higher during infusion and 16% higher after it with alfentanil (p 798).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No pharmacodynamic end point was modelled. Opioid audit: alfentanil only as a covariate; no MEAC, no ke0/t1/2ke0/tPeak, no equianalgesic ratio reported. Alfentanil plasma concentrations studied were 78.2-117.1 ng/mL (median 92.7; target 80), a range the authors say supports no conclusion outside it (p 799). Simulations (p 799, figs 6-7): propofol concentration with alfentanil was 5% higher at 3 min rising to 18% at 60 min of infusion (model-predicted mean 17%), and 10-25% higher over 6 h afterwards; propofol context-sensitive half-time increased by up to 2 min for 0-90 min TCIs.

## Covariates
Plasma alfentanil (time-independent, mean per session), cardiac output and heart rate (time-dependent, interval means). Alfentanil lowered Cl1, Cl2 and V2 (not V1, not lag). Final model: CO on Cl1, Cl2, V1, V2; HR on Cl1, Cl2, V2; alfentanil on Cl1 and V2 (p 800). Only HR differed between sessions (63 vs 58 beats/min, P = 0.02; Table 1, p 798). Simulated 2 mg/kg, 12/10/8 mg/kg/h scheme with the HR-only model gave mean blood propofol 3.6, 3.1, 2.7 mcg/mL at HR 50, 60, 70 (p 803).

## Caveats
Eight young healthy men; venous sampling (lag-time model, V1 larger than most arterial studies, Vss smaller, p 800); whole-blood assay; two-compartment structure; alfentanil range narrow; CO by bioimpedance; the effect of alfentanil cannot be separated from HR/CO changes that propofol itself induces; Table 2 coefficient labels ambiguous (open questions).
