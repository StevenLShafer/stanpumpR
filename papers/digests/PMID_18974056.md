# Chen 2008: dose dependency of cisatracurium PK/PD in dogs

## Study
- Animal study, not human: 8 adult male beagles (7.5-10.5 kg), pentobarbital anaesthesia (30 mg/kg then supplements of 3-5 mg/kg), mechanically ventilated, temperature controlled.
- Two-period randomised crossover, washout between doses. Cisatracurium (Nimbex) 0.15 mg/kg (1.5 x ED95) and 0.6 mg/kg (6 x ED95) i.v. bolus over 2 s. (The mg/kg doses are from the Table 3 and 4 headings; the Introduction's human study used 0.075/0.15/0.3 mg/kg for 1.5/3/6 x ED95, so the dog ED95 here implies about 0.1 mg/kg, derived.)
- Arterial samples every 6 s for the first minute, then 1.5, 3, 5, 7, 15, 30, 45, 60, 80 min and later if recovery was incomplete. Plasma (acidified, iced) by HPLC with fluorescence detection, LOQ 6.5 ng/mL, CV 11% (3.3% for the diluted early samples), linear to 2500 ng/mL. Plasma, not whole blood; total drug.
- Effect: first twitch T1 of the TOF, force transducer, forelimb (medial antebrachial cutaneous nerve), expressed as percent depression from baseline.
- Analysis: individual non-parametric PK/PD in NONMEM VI (concentrations interpolated linearly when rising, log-linearly when falling, convolved with ke0 exp(-ke0 t)), sigmoid Emax effect model; ke0, EC50, gamma fitted jointly for both doses (shared) or separately per dose. Paired t-test with resampling, ANOVA for sequence/period (no effect).

## Model and parameters
All values are mean (SD), n = 8 dogs, low dose 0.15 mg/kg vs high dose 0.6 mg/kg.
- Table 4 (PDF p. 5, printed 792), non-compartmental PK normalised to a 0.1 mg/kg dose: Cmax/D 4678 (1806) vs 4485 (767) ng/mL (P 0.766); Tmax 0.26 (0.06) vs 0.25 (0.11) min; AUC0-inf/D 9593 (1258) vs 10 383 (1655) ng min/mL; AUC0-1min/D 1448 (296) vs 1483 (249); kel 0.0402 (0.0042) vs 0.0409 (0.0096) /min. Terminal half-life 17.6 and 17.8 min (text). No dose dependency in PK, so PK linear.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- ke0 on the T1 twitch (force, forelimb) fell from 0.1278 to 0.0600 /min with the 4-fold higher dose (P 0.002; every dog lower, Fig. 6A); t1/2 ke0 = 5.4 min and 11.6 min (derived: ln 2 / ke0). TECmax rose 6.13 to 10.69 min while the clinical onset time shortened (2.14 to 0.98 min).
- EC50 (effect compartment, T1 depression, ng/mL) 235 vs 323, a 38% increase after the high dose; gamma 7.70 unchanged; Emax estimated 102%. Concentrations are plasma cisatracurium by HPLC in dogs.
- Authors' conclusion: both are dose dependent for a descriptive model, possibly an artefact of near-instant maximum block after the high dose; parameters from an intubating dose are more reliable, and a model fitted at the high dose underestimates peak effect for an intubating dose (Fig. 8), the reverse over-predicting speed (Fig. 7).
- Cited comparison: Bergeron 2001 (humans, three doses) found ke0 falling with dose and IC50 rising; Schmith 1997 population fit, doses up to 8 x ED95, did not test dose as a covariate.
- MEAC / analgesic concentration and equianalgesic ratio: not applicable (neuromuscular blocker, no opioid).

## Covariates
None modelled; only dose group (crossover). Parameters normalised to 0.1 mg/kg for display.

## Caveats
- Dogs under pentobarbital, absolute values (EC50 235-323 ng/mL; plasma kel 0.04 /min) are not human values; dose-dependence of ke0 is the finding being used, not the numbers.
- Non-parametric, individual two-stage fit with a descriptive model, no hypotheses tested mechanistically; authors cannot separate real effect from model artefact (many data points at 0 or 100%).
- Gamma is shared across doses because separate values gave no better fit (Table 5), so the ke0 and EC50 shifts are conditional on that choice.

## Verification against stanpumpR
No repo location to verify. Independent comparison with `R/drugs_cisatracurium.R`:
- The model uses Sorooshian's human ke0 of 0.071 /min (young) and 0.060 /min (elderly), converted to tPeak 12.29 and 12.91 min (R/drugs_cisatracurium.R:56-60, 99). Chen's dog ke0 0.0600 after 6 x ED95 equals the elderly human value only by coincidence; at 1.5 x ED95 the dog ke0 is 0.1278, about 1.8 times the young human 0.071. Different species and a different PK model, so not a check on the library's ke0. Sorooshian's patients received 0.1 mg/kg (about 2 x ED95); the paper's dose-dependence implies ke0 depends on bolus size, so using a bolus-derived tPeak for infusions or very large boluses is an approximation (a qualitative point, no repo discrepancy).
- Potency: repo C50 98 ng/mL (human, Sorooshian; repo thresholds 130/165/200 ng/mL, line 99-102 and `drugDefaults_global.csv`: 130, 200, 165) vs dog EC50 235-323 ng/mL; the dog is about 2.4-3.3 times higher (derived), a species difference, not a conflict.
- PK: dog terminal half-life 17.6-17.8 min vs repo human beta half-life 28.4/36.3 min and Ornstein/Lien values 21.5-25.5 min (header, lines 25-50). Dogs eliminate faster than the human values in the model. Not applicable to the human model.
- Verdict: not-applicable (paper not cited, animal data).
