# Hannam 2018: acetaminophen-ibuprofen-tramadol analgesia after adenotonsillectomy

## Study
- Multicentre (NZ 179, Australia 18, Mexico 60) sponsor (AFT Pharmaceuticals) trial, ACTRN12615000456550. Children 2-12 y, >= 10 kg, elective (adeno)tonsillectomy; 251 analysed.
- Randomised to fixed-ratio oral combination suspension (acetaminophen:ibuprofen 3.3:1): "low" loading 24 + 7.2 mg/kg then 12 + 3.6 mg/kg 4-6 hourly; "high" loading 30 + 9 mg/kg then 15 + 4.5 mg/kg; max 4 doses/24 h, up to 11 days. Loading ~30 min before surgery.
- Up to 5 plasma samples (intra-op, end of surgery, 1-2, 3-4, 5-6 h): 1168 ibuprofen and 1168 acetaminophen concentrations; LC-MS/MS, LLOQ 50 mcg/L.
- Pain: Parent's Postoperative Pain Measurement (PPPM, 0-15) on swallowing, 2-hourly on postoperative day 1; 3886 scores. Tramadol rescue (0.5-1 mg/kg) in 80 children, included as a third drug with literature PK (Allegaert 2015) and estimated absorption.
- Pooled with adult dental PRID data (Mehlisch 2010, scaled to PPPM equivalents) to compare child vs adult PD.
- NONMEM 7.3, PPPD sequential method, FOCE-I, ADVAN13; 1000 bootstraps.

## Model and parameters
PK: one compartment, first-order absorption (Tabs) and elimination, theory-based allometry, no maturation (all > 2 y). Table 1 typical values (columns re-assembled; plausible decoding):

- Ibuprofen: CL 3.83 L/h/70 kg (3.55-4.01), V 16.5 L/70 kg (14.77-18.13), Tabs 0.316 h (0.22-0.40).
- Tramadol: CL 33.9 FIX, V 185 FIX, Tabs 1.61 h (0.707-1.98).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Greco additive model of normalised effect-site units U = Ce/C50 for ibuprofen, acetaminophen, tramadol; fractional Emax with shared Hill; pain score = E0 (15) x (1 - drug effect) x (1 - disease effect). Disease (pain resolution) Emax 0.983, Hill 5.9, T50 46 h. End point: PPPM on swallowing, postoperative day 1 (children), plus adult dental PRID.
- Ibuprofen: t1/2keo 1.04 h (0.75-1.77), C50 3.95 mg/L (2.57-7.53).
- Acetaminophen: t1/2keo 0.34 h (0.23-1.98), C50 7.06 mg/L.
- Tramadol (opioid audit): C50 0.0703 mg/L (0.048-1.07) in Table 2, but 0.088 (0.043-1.01) mg/L in the discussion; t1/2keo 1.77 h (Table 2) / 1.78 h (abstract) (1.06-1.96); end point PPPM pain score. Total tramadol from literature PK; no M1 measured. No MEAC estimated here; discussion cites literature: tramadol ~1/6 to 1/10 as potent as morphine (Lehmann 1990) and 0.1 mg/L tramadol + 15 mcg/L M1 for 95% probability of adequate relief (Garrido 2006).
- Fractional Emax 0.648 (~65% pain reduction), Hill 1.48, beta fixed 0 (CI -0.2 to 1.2). No child-adult PD difference detected.
- Shrinkage high for the t1/2keo parameters.

## Covariates
Allometric size only.

## Caveats
- ke0s are from sparse oral data with high shrinkage; PK one-compartment.
- Internal inconsistencies: discussion quotes C50 ACET 7.39 and C50 IBU 3.39 mg/L vs Table 2 7.06 and 3.95; tramadol C50 0.0703 vs 0.088.
- Parent-rated pain; sponsor-funded.
