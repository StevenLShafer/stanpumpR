# Salerno 1989: IV diltiazem against placebo for atrial fibrillation and flutter

## Study
- **Design:** double-blind, randomised, parallel, placebo-controlled, at 6 centres, April 1986 to May 1987. Marion
  Laboratories supported it, and Dias was their employee.
- **Patients (Table I, p3):** 113 adults with atrial fibrillation (89) or flutter (24). Ventricular rate was at
  least 120 for 15 min and SBP at least 90 mm Hg; NYHA III-IV failure was excluded. 83 were men, mean age 64 ± 13 y.
  57 were randomised to placebo and 56 to diltiazem.
- **Dosing:** dose A was 0.25 mg/kg IV over 2 min. If tolerated but ineffective, dose B (0.35 mg/kg over 2 min)
  followed 15 min later. Mean doses were 19 ± 6 mg (A) and 25 ± 7 mg (B) double-blind, and 19 ± 4 and 27 ± 6 mg
  open-label.
- **Open label:** placebo non-responders could have open-label diltiazem; 49 did.
- **Measurements:** heart rate (hand-counted 1-min rhythm strips) and BP after the 2-min infusion, then every 5 min to
  15 min. That gives samples at 2, 7, 12 and 17 min from the start of the infusion (Table III).
- **Response:** conversion to sinus rhythm, a rate under 100, or a fall of at least 20%.
- **Not measured:** drug concentrations.

## Model and parameters
No pharmacokinetic data. This is an efficacy trial.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- **Response rates (Table II, p4):**
  - Double-blind dose A: 42 of 56 (75%) on diltiazem against 4 of 57 (7%) on placebo.
  - A + B combined: 52 of 56 (93%) against 7 of 57 (12%), p < 0.001.
  - All diltiazem-treated patients: 99 of 105 (94%).
  - Fibrillation 79 of 82 (96%) and flutter 20 of 23 (87%), not significantly different.
  - No effect of age (60 or over against under 60) or of concomitant AV-nodal drugs.
- **Time to peak effect:** the median time from the start of the infusion to the maximal fall in heart rate was
  4.3 min (p5 Results; repeated in the abstract and discussion). The paper does not say how a median of 4.3 min was
  obtained from measurements at 2, 7, 12 and 17 min, nor which patients it covers.
- **Mean heart rate (Table III, double-blind):**
  - Diltiazem dose A: 149 ± 17 at baseline, 123 ± 25 at 2 min, 113 ± 26 at 7, 115 ± 27 at 12 and 114 ± 25 at
    17 min.
  - Dose B: 139, then 121, 114, 115 and 119.
  - Placebo A: 148, then 144, 143, 145 and 143.
  - The mean nadir is at 7 min, with 12 and 17 min within 2 bpm.
  - Some fall had already happened by the end of the 2-min infusion (Figure 3).
  - The mean fall on dose A was 22%, against 3% on placebo.
- **Blood pressure:**
  - SBP fell 11 mm Hg on dose A (126 to 115), largest at the end of the infusion.
  - The mean change was -8% on diltiazem against -2% on placebo for SBP, and -10% against +1% for DBP.
- **Side effects:** 7 of 105 had asymptomatic hypotension (SBP < 90).
- **Not reported:** concentrations, ke0 and any PK-PD model.

## Covariates
None modelled. The subgroups (rhythm, age, concomitant drugs) showed no difference in response.

## Caveats
- The 4.3-min median falls between the 2- and 7-min measurements, and the paper does not explain how it was
  computed.
- The mean heart-rate data reach their lowest at 7 min, but are nearly flat from 7 to 17 min.
- Dose was weight-based (0.25 mg/kg), about 19 mg on average, not a fixed 20 mg.
- Excluded: severe failure, hypotension, sick sinus and recent MI.
- Six protocol violations were kept in the intent-to-treat analysis.
