# Korth-Bradley 1996: population PK of IV amiodarone in refractory VT/VF

## Study
- Phase III efficacy trial of IV amiodarone for hemodynamically destabilising ventricular tachyarrhythmias (Wyeth-Ayerst; ref 1, Levine 1990 abstract).
- n = 245 patients, 2,006 serum concentrations. Age 64 ± 11 y, height 172 ± 9 cm, weight 78 ± 17 kg (mean ± SD), 73% men; ejection fraction 33 ± 14% in the 173 with a measurement (p. 716). Patients with chronic renal failure excluded (p. 718).
- Dosing (p. 716): randomised to three groups: rapid infusion 75, 150 or 300 mg over 10 min, then 0.5, 1 or 2 mg/min for 6 h, then half that rate (0.25, 0.5 or 1 mg/min) for 18 h. After 24 h, open treatment at the investigator's discretion (IV, or IV + oral); supplemental 150 mg over 10-15 min allowed for breakthrough arrhythmias.
- Sampling: immediately after the rapid infusion, 20-40 min, 1, 6, 12, 24 h, then every 24 h while on drug; planned 14, 30, 60, 90 days after stopping. Highly variable in practice; samples after oral doses excluded (oral timing not recorded). Mean sampling duration 6.3 days; some patients up to 100 days (p. 718).
- Assay: HPLC (Lesne & Pellegrin), serum, linear 50-5,000 ng/mL, interday CV < 10%. Desethylamiodarone not reported.
- Model: NONMEM version 3, two-compartment; all parameters normalised to body weight throughout; proportional IIV; additive-proportional residual error.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No ke0, no effect-site model, no concentration-response. Clinical end point: protocol-defined success = 24 h of treatment with no VT/VF after the 6-h infusion; exposure (Cmax, C24, AUC) did not differ by response (Table II). Highest reliably measured concentrations 35-40 mg/L in some patients (p. 718). Not an opioid: no MEAC or potency ratio.

## Covariates
Weight by per-kg normalisation only (a later pooled analysis, ref 4, found weight not significant; it was kept for comparability). Age > 70 y and serum creatinine > 3 mg/dL changed the objective function but not the parameter estimates; gender, height, alkaline phosphatase, ejection fraction and therapeutic response did not contribute.

## Caveats
- Critically ill patients; continuous infusions obscure the multi-exponential disposition (2 compartments adequate; more not supported).
- Clearance probably overestimated: mean 6.3-day sampling vs terminal t½ 14-58 days; pre-steady-state data include distribution.
- Small V1 attributed to sampling immediately after the rapid infusion; reduced ejection fraction may slow distribution.
- Bias: sickest patients more likely to die before late samples.
- Typical value % CVs and IIV interpretation are not fully defined in the text.
