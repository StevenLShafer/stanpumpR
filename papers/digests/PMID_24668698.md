# Kulo 2014: weight, pregnancy and contraceptives on IV paracetamol clearance

## Study
Pooled non-compartmental analysis of 73 IV paracetamol profiles in young women, each after a 2 g IV loading dose with
plasma at 1, 2, 4 and 6 h (HPLC). Cohort 1: 36 women at caesarean delivery (2 g over 15 min after delivery). Cohort 2:
8 of them re-studied 10-15 weeks after delivery and 7 of the 8 again 1 year after delivery (15 profiles; 4/8 and 2/7 on
oral contraceptives). Cohort 3: 8 healthy volunteers not on contraceptives. Cohort 4: 14 Gregoire 2007 volunteers on
contraceptives. Non-pregnant total n = 37: on OC 20 (14 Gregoire + 6 of cohort 2), not on OC 17 (9 of cohort 2 + 8 of
cohort 3). Elimination rate from at least 3 final points; AUC by trapezoids plus extrapolation; CL = dose / AUC0-inf;
**Vd = CL / ke** (an area volume, not a steady-state volume). Medians and ranges; Mann-Whitney, rank correlation,
multiple regression (MedCalc).

Derived ratios: delivery against non-pregnant women not on OC, CL 19.6/12.7 = 1.54, CL/BSA 10.46/6.9 = 1.52, Vd
56/46.8 = 1.20, Vd/kg 0.69/0.74 = 0.93. CL correlated with weight (r = 0.6, r2 = 0.36, P < 0.0001). Multiple regression,
all 73: weight (P = 0.0043) and pregnancy (P = 0.02), multiple r = 0.56; non-pregnant: weight (P = 0.009) and OC
(P = 0.03), r = 0.51. CL (L/h and L/h/m2) and Vd (L) higher at delivery than in all non-pregnant subgroups (P < 0.05);
no test of Vd per kilogram is reported. OC vs no OC, CL/BSA P = 0.023.

The "+80%" (glucuronidation +144%, renal +53%, oxidation +78%) and "11.7 to 6.4 L/h/m2" figures in this paper's
Introduction are citations of Kulo 2013 (PMID 22845052) and the paired Kulo 2012 (PMID 22675252), not results of this
analysis.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None measured. The Discussion asserts a link between median concentration and analgesia (cited).

## Covariates
Weight, pregnancy (delivery) and oral contraceptives on clearance, by regression; no covariate equations.

## Caveats
Sparse 6 h sampling for a non-compartmental estimate; Vd is CL/ke; Gregoire data from a different laboratory and assay;
the 15 post-partum profiles are pooled into the non-pregnant groups although Allegaert 2015 found early post-partum
glucuronidation low.
