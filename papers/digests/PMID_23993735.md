# Chen 2014: sufentanil Ce for burn dressing changes (with propofol)

## Study
- Randomised, 80 burn patients (49 M, 31 F), 20-50% TBSA, age 23-50 y, ASA 1-2, weight 45-100 kg (exclusion), at one Chinese hospital, 2009-2011. Four groups of 20 by target sufentanil effect-site concentration (Ce): 0.2, 0.3, 0.4, 0.5 ng/mL (Table 1: ages 31-35 y, weights 59-65 kg, TBSA 35-39%, dressing change 30-35 min; text layer). Text in Results 3.1 says "Eighteen patients" (misprint for eighty).
- Both drugs by TCI (Orchestra pumps) started 5 min before the dressing change: sufentanil with the Bovill 1984 model, propofol with the Marsh model at a Ce of 1.2 "mg/ml" (see open questions). No premedication, no other analgesia, O2 3 L/min.
- Titration rule: target Ce kept if VAS 2-4, Ramsay 2-3, SpO2 >90%; raised by 0.05 ng/mL at 5-min intervals for pain/anxiety; lowered by 0.05 ng/mL if VAS <2, Ramsay >4 or SpO2 <90%. A patient who needed an increase counted as a failure ("adequate analgesia" = no increase).
- Phases analysed separately: uncovering the inner dressing layers, and wound management. Probit regression of the fraction adequate vs initial target Ce (SPSS 13). No sufentanil plasma samples; concentrations are pump-predicted.

## Model and parameters
No PK model was fitted. The paper only runs Bovill's published model in the pump. Sufentanil dose actually given per patient (Fig 3, read from the bar chart, approximate): about 29, 29.5, 32, 32.6 ug for the 0.2, 0.3, 0.4, 0.5 groups; 0.4 and 0.5 higher than 0.2 (p<0.05).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: probability of "adequate analgesia" (no up-titration of target Ce) with VAS 2-4 and Ramsay 2-3, by Probit analysis on the initial target Ce. Population: adult burn patients, 20-50% TBSA, with concurrent propofol Ce 1.2 (unit as printed mg/mL). Concentrations are pump-predicted effect-site values, not assayed; no total/free or blood/plasma distinction.

| Phase | EC50 ng/mL (95% CI) | EC95 ng/mL (95% CI) | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Uncovering inner dressing layers | 0.278 (0.231-0.318) | 0.394 (0.366-0.530) | Abstract, Fig 1, PDF p. 3 (printed 457) |
| Wound management | 0.349 (0.299-0.366) | 0.465 (0.430-0.563) | Abstract, Fig 2, PDF p. 3 (printed 457) |

Table 2 (no adjustment / decrease / increase, n of 20, printed p. 457), uncovering: no adjustment 7, 10, 6, 3 for 0.2, 0.3, 0.4, 0.5; decrease 0, 4, 13, 17; increase 13, 5, 1, 0. Wound management: no adjustment 2, 5, 13, 15; decrease 0, 0, 3, 5; increase 18, 14, 4, 0.
Table 3 (printed p. 458): respiratory depression (SpO2 <90%) 0, 0, 1, 3 of 20 at 0.2, 0.3, 0.4, 0.5 (4 in all, corrected by verbal prompting); satisfaction medians 5, 5, 5.5, 6 (patients) and 5, 5, 6, 6 (doctors), higher at 0.4 and 0.5 than at 0.2 (p<0.01).
The fitted curves in Figs 1-2 do not start at zero probability at 0.2 ng/mL (about 0.35 and 0.1), so the probit EC50 is an extrapolated, model-dependent value.
- ke0 / t½ke0 / tPeak / hysteresis: none reported. Bovill's ke0 not stated.
- MEAC: none claimed as such; the EC50 above is the closest analogue (procedural pain, with propofol).
- Potency ratio: the Introduction says sufentanil is about 10 times fentanyl (text, no source or end point).

## Covariates
None analysed. Burn size (TBSA), burn depth (observed, not analysed), age and weight did not enter the model.

## Caveats
- Single centre, n=20 per group, all targets 0.2-0.5 ng/mL so the lower asymptote is not defined; CIs are asymmetric and EC95 CIs are wide.
- Co-administered propofol (and burn-related opioid tolerance, not assessed) means the EC50 is not an opioid-alone value; a procedural, stimulus-dependent end point, not a resting-pain MEAC.
- Concentrations are model predictions, depend on Bovill's model and unstated ke0, in burn patients whose PK may differ (authors say so).
- Misprints noted above (80 vs "Eighteen"; "mg/ml" propofol; "sufentanyl").

## Verification against stanpumpR
No citation to check, so the verdict is not-applicable; but the library models sufentanil, so the comparison follows.
- `R/drugs_sufentanil.R:63` models sufentanil on Gepts 1995 (not Bovill 1984, which Chen used for targeting); MEAC 0.056 ng/mL (`R/drugs_sufentanil.R:59`, `inst/extdata/drugDefaults_global.csv` row sufentanil: Lower 0.0448, Upper 0.112, Typical 0.0672, MEAC 0.056); tPeak 5.6 min (`R/drugs_sufentanil.R:58`, EEG).
- Chen's EC50 for procedural analgesia (0.278-0.349 ng/mL) and EC95 (0.394-0.465 ng/mL) are about 5-6x (derived: 0.278/0.056 = 5.0; 0.349/0.056 = 6.2) the library MEAC, and above its "Upper" 0.112. Different end point (burn dressing-change pain, with propofol, Bovill-model Ce), so this is not a contradiction; the library MEAC is not a procedural-pain concentration. Nothing in the library should change on this paper alone; a different PK model (Bovill vs Gepts) means the Ce values are not directly interchangeable.
- No ke0/tPeak in the paper, so the library's tPeak 5.6 min cannot be checked.
