# Reif 2013: population PK of ethinylestradiol and drospirenone in extended-cycle regimens

PDF pages equal printed pages (e1 article, pp. 1-13).

## Study
- Phase III, multicentre, randomised, open-label, parallel-group trial (protocol 308683, NCT00266032); about 1100
  healthy young women taking ethinylestradiol (EE) 20 µg / drospirenone (DRSP) 3 mg once daily (the text layer
  prints "20 mg"; the page shows µg, PDF p. 1). Three regimens (Table 1, p. 3): flexible extended 24-120/4 days
  (flexibleMIB, 684 treated), conventional 24/4 with placebo (229), fixed extended 120/4 (221); 1134 treated.
- Sampling (p. 2): two samples at Week 3 (days 15-21) and two at Week 27, the second 45-120 min after the first;
  99% of samples 0-36 h after the last dose. Serum. 4218 EE samples from 1109 women and 4042 DRSP samples from 1096.
- Population (Table 2, p. 4, EE dataset, n = 1109): age mean 24.8 (SD 4.4), median 24.0, 5th-95th 19.0-34.0 y;
  weight mean 63.0 (8.6), median 62.0, 51.0-79.8 kg; BMI median 22.0 kg/m²; >98% Caucasian.
- Assays: EE by GC-MS, LOQ 5.0 pg/mL, inter-assay CV 4.4-7.9%; DRSP by radioimmunoassay, LOQ 0.10 ng/mL,
  CV 6.1-8.0%, accuracy 88-94% (p. 2).
- NONMEM VI.2, FOCE with interaction; covariate forward p ≤ 0.01, backward p ≤ 0.001. Structural models started
  from a Phase I model (Blode 2012).

## Model and parameters
All apparent (oral) parameters; F fixed to 1 (relative). Typical woman: 24 y, 62 kg.

**EE** (Appendix 1, p. 12): three compartments, first-order absorption with lag, first-order elimination,
proportional error, IIV on CL only. V2/F = V4/F assumed.

**DRSP** (Appendix 2, p. 13): two compartments, first-order absorption with lag, proportional + additive error,
IIV on CL/F and F (correlated), IOV on F.

Printing slips in the appendix footnotes (read on the page images): the DRSP covariate line reads
"CO2 = (1 CL_BW* (BW–62))" with the "+" missing, and the DRSP and EE equations name the Week-27 terms
`TVCL_visit5` and `F_week27` where the tables say `Diff_CL_week27` and `Diff_F_week27`. Taking the percentages
as fractions in a linear (weight) or log-linear (age) factor reproduces Table 3 (derived: EE 20 µg / 25.3 L/h =
790 pg·h/mL vs 789.8; age 34 vs 19 gives 11.3% lower exposure vs 11.2% printed; DRSP 3 mg / 3.52 L/h = 852
ng·h/mL vs 850.6; Week 27 DRSP CL 3.52 x 0.9345 = 3.29 L/h as printed).

Exposures (Table 4, p. 7; AUC0-24,ss): EE total median 808, geometric mean 822 pg·h/mL, 5th-95th 506-1434,
geoCV 31.9%; DRSP total median 874, geometric mean 876 ng·h/mL, 569-1345, geoCV 27.9%. No difference between
regimens (objective function fell only 3.032 for EE and 1.256 for DRSP with regimen as covariate).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No concentration-effect model, therapeutic range, ke0 or potency ratio; efficacy (ovulation inhibition,
bleeding) is in the companion clinical papers (Klipping 2012), not modelled here. Not an opioid.

## Covariates
- EE CL/F: body weight (+0.591%/kg about 62 kg) and age (+20.8% per unit ln age about 24 y). Table 3: EE
  AUC 844.3 (51 kg), 789.8 (62 kg), 715.0 (79.8 kg) pg·h/mL; by age 829.9 (19 y), 789.8 (24 y), 736.7 (34 y).
- DRSP CL/F: body weight only (+0.672%/kg). Table 3: DRSP AUC 917.6 (51 kg), 850.6 (62 kg), 760.5 (79.8 kg).
- Covariates explained only 0.9% (EE) and 1.2% (DRSP) of variability. Alcohol, BMI tested; smoking, ethnicity and
  co-medication not tested (< 10% prevalence or one ethnic group).
- Week 27 vs Week 3: EE relative F +8.15%; DRSP CL/F -6.55%. The comparator is the same women's Week 3 visit.

## Caveats
- The text (p. 6) says DRSP exposure at 51 kg was 17.2% "higher" than at 79.8 kg; Table 3 gives 917.6 vs 760.5,
  which is 20.7% higher, or 17.1% lower read the other way (derived). The percentage was taken on the 51 kg value.
- Sparse sampling (2 samples per visit), few absorption-phase samples (p. 4): ka, lag and the EE peripheral
  structure rest largely on the Phase I prior model.
- EE exposure here (median 808 pg·h/mL) is about 3.6 times the authors' own Phase I studies (220 and 225 pg·h/mL,
  Blode 2012); the authors attribute it to EE's known variability (p. 10). The comparison is against those Phase I
  volunteers, not against any other model.
- Healthy, young, >98% Caucasian women; sponsor-authored (Bayer).
- Parameters are apparent (/F); absolute bioavailability not estimated (EE literature range 25-65% quoted, p. 10).
