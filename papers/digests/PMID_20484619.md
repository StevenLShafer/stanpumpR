# Knebel 2011: pediatric population PK of pantoprazole (birth to 16 years)

## Study
Pooled population PK of 6 Wyeth trials: 2 single-dose IV trials (0.8 or 1.6 mg/kg; 22 patients aged 1-16 y) and 4
single- and multiple-dose oral trials (1.25, 2.5, 20 or 40 mg fixed, or 0.6 or 1.2 mg/kg; GERD patients, 119 aged
birth-11 months and 61 aged 1-16 y). Oral forms: delayed-release tablet and granule ("spheroid"). Total 202 patients,
922 plasma concentrations (p. 336). 121 male / 81 female; 77 preterm (gestational age < 38 wk); CYP2C19 phenotype
4 poor, 165 extensive, 33 unknown (3 of 4 PMs preterm, all < 7 months). Race: 146 White, 38 African American, 4 Asian,
4 Hispanic, 10 other (Table II).
Table I (PDF p. 5): weight 1.57-127 kg (median 7.93, mean 19.2); age 0.025-16 y (median 0.65, mean 3.69); BSA
0.13-2.5 m2. Assay LC-MS/MS, LLOQ 10 ng/mL (stated "in serum" though plasma was measured, p. 334). BLQ values excluded.
NONMEM VI, FOCEI; 1000-replicate stratified nonparametric bootstrap; 500-replicate predictive check.

## Model and parameters
Two-compartment, first-order absorption with lag time; separate Ka and F1 for tablet and granule; F1 tablet fixed 1
(so CL is a partial CL/F for oral data; IV data anchor CL). Allometry to a 10 kg reference, exponents fixed 0.75 (CL,
Q) and 1 (V2, V3). CL (eq 6, PDF p. 6):
CL = θ1·(WT/10)^0.75 · AGE^Hill/(AGE^Hill + AG50^Hill) · θ15^male · θ16^PM · θ17^AfricanAmerican · exp(η);
for preterm infants AG50 is multiplied by θ14 (AG50Ppreterm). Emax fixed 1. Reference: female, full term, EM/unknown
CYP2C19, non-African American, 10 kg, IV or tablet.

Discussion (p. 341): 70 kg adult, not PM: CL 8.3 L/h, Vss (V2 + V3) 13.3 L (derived check: 1.93 × 7^0.75 = 8.31;
(1.3 + 0.596) × 7 = 13.27), against the NDA's 8 L/h and 11 L. Pediatric CL fell between earlier pediatric means
(1.4 and 3.0 L/h, refs 10 and 31); Vss was lower than their 2.2 and 2.5 L.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No acid-suppression or other PD end point; dose comparison uses simulated AUCss against the adult 40 mg oral
AUC range (Fig. 6). Not an opioid.

## Covariates
Weight (fixed allometry on CL, Q, V2, V3); age (sigmoid Emax maturation on CL, separate AG50 for preterm via factor
1.38; allometrically scaled CL near adult value by about 1 year); CYP2C19 poor metabolizer ×0.0716 (derived: 92.8%
lower; paper: point and whole CI more than 70% lower); male ×1.06 (clinically unimportant); African American ×1.29
(indeterminate). Only two categories for CYP2C19: poor vs extensive/unknown.

## Caveats
- Only 4 poor metabolizers, 3 preterm; the PM factor is wide (41% SE).
- Predictive check: some overprediction of tablet and underprediction of granule data (Fig. 1); simulations used
  conditional individual estimates instead of Monte Carlo.
- Granule F1 0.295 is likely confounded with incomplete intake in infants (authors, p. 344).
- Covariance CL-V2 poorly estimated (115% SE); additive residual bootstrap CI 8.27-1940.
- Discussion gives the granule IOV CI as 37.7-61.4% CV, which does not match the Table IV variance CI (0.142-0.519 →
  37.7-72.0% CV, derived); 61.4% equals √0.377, the σ4.4 upper bound. Internal inconsistency, minor.
- Industry-sponsored (Wyeth employees and consultants). Oldest patient 16 y; adult use is extrapolation.
