# Dansirikul 2006: Bayesian popPK of sirolimus in kidney transplant

## Study
- Retrospective routine-care data from 25 adult kidney transplant recipients (14 M / 11 F) at the Queen Elizabeth Hospital, Woodville, once-daily oral sirolimus, mean dose 6 (SD 3) mg/day (range 2-20). 315 whole-blood concentrations, 3-20 per patient, mostly near the 24 h trough (median sampling time 23.7 h, range 1.6-77 h), taken a median of 278 days after transplant (2 days to 10.7 years). 13 values were below the 2.5 ug/L LLOQ (treated as censored between 0 and the LLOQ); 14 values came from 3 patients suspected of non-compliance (a compliance factor was estimated). Formulation (tablet or solution) is not stated (PDF p.5-6).
- Table 1 (PDF p.5), mean +/- SD (range) per patient: age 44 +/- 13 (22-73) y; weight 73 +/- 15 (50-115) kg; BMI 27 +/- 5.5; haematocrit 0.33 +/- 0.05 (0.2-0.51); albumin 37 +/- 4.6 g/L; Cockcroft-Gault CLcr 45.3 +/- 21.6 mL/min. Co-medication: mycophenolate + steroid 15, ciclosporin + steroid 3, tacrolimus + steroid 4, steroid alone 2, mycophenolate alone 1. 21 of 25 were "rescue" patients.
- Assay: HPLC-UV, calibrated 2.5-50 ug/L, LLOQ 2.5 ug/L. Target troughs 4-12 ug/L with ciclosporin, 12-20 ug/L without (PDF p.2).
- Method: fully Bayesian hierarchical model, PKBUGS 1.1 / WinBUGS 1.3, two MCMC chains (20 000 samples each after 4000 burn-in). Informative priors from 14 literature data sets (8 studies; two-compartment values from Ferron 1997 and Brattstrom 1997, rich single-dose profiles to 144 h in 36 and 12 patients, PDF p.11), meta-analysed with inverse-variance-type weighting by N (healthy-volunteer and hepatic-impairment data weighted a further 0.5; the diltiazem arm excluded).

## Model and parameters
Two compartments, first-order absorption, proportional residual error (SD 0.3768), with a compliance factor. Apparent parameters (divided by F), whole blood, ug/L. Deviance (Table 3, PDF p.6): 1-cpt proportional 1730; 2-cpt proportional 1717; plus compliance factor 1681; plus age on CL 1681. Posterior probability of the two-compartment model 0.975 (Mix 0.891), odds ratio 8.2.

Table 4 (PDF p.10, printed 429), posterior medians (95% credible interval):
- CL/F 12.94 L/h (9.46, 16.43), the median of the exponentiated typical-individual ln(CL) over posterior draws (footnote a). The Abstract and Results give 12.5 L/h at age 44.
- Q/F 20.4 L/h (13.1, 32.4); Vc/F 117 L (99.4, 138); Vp/F 583 L (459, 736); ka* 2.195 /h (2.123, 2.269), with ka = ka* + CL/Vc, ka about 2.305 /h (footnote b).
- ln(CL_i) = th1 + th2 x age_i / age_median, age_median 44 y; th1 = 2.827 (2.408, 3.249), th2 = -0.2987 (-0.726, 0.119); exp(th1) = 16.9 (11.1, 25.8), exp(th2) = 0.742 (0.484, 1.13) (footnote c).
- BSV (variance on log scale): CL 0.1903, Q 0.1035, Vc 0.305, Vp 0.0654, ka* 0.1449; covariances near zero.
- Derived (from the equation): CL/F at 44 y = 16.9 x 0.742 = 12.5 L/h; at 22 y 14.6; at 73 y 11.1; at 80 y 9.8 (outside the data).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None; PK only. Target trough ranges quoted for dosing (PDF p.2): 4-12 ug/L with ciclosporin, 12-20 ug/L without, whole blood by HPLC-UV. No efficacy or toxicity relation modelled. Not an opioid; no MEAC, ke0, tPeak or potency ratio.

## Covariates
- Screened on CL only: age, total bilirubin, albumin, haematocrit, weight, post-transplant days, gender. Age retained (log-linear on age/44; probability 0.734 of at least a 20% change in CL between youngest and oldest patient; BSV of CL fell 0.1925 to 0.1903). Bilirubin and post-transplant days were driven by one extreme value each; albumin, weight, gender minimal; haematocrit did not lower deviance (1682) and cut residual variance only 14%.
- With less informative priors theta2 was -0.4664 (-0.9974, 0.0765), PDF p.8-9: the age slope is steeper but equally uncertain (PDF p.7-8, printed 426-427).
- Other studies (Ferron 1997; Boni 2005 for temsirolimus-derived sirolimus) found no age effect (PDF p.12).

## Caveats
- Sparse trough-dominated data; Vc and ka are prior-determined, only CL, Q and Vp moved from their priors (PDF p.11).
- The prior CL (13.6 L/h) mixes healthy-volunteer, kidney-transplant and hepatic-impairment data with ad hoc 0.5 weighting; no SEs were available for the priors.
- Only 25% older than 50 y; age may be a surrogate for something else (authors).
- Concomitant ciclosporin (3 patients) and tacrolimus (4) not modelled as covariates.
- Formulation unspecified, so F is unknown; all values apparent.
- Haematocrit effect on the whole-blood concentration (red-cell binding) was not retained.
