# Milosheska 2016: lamotrigine population PK with UGT/transporter genotypes

## Study
- Prospective study, adult outpatients with epilepsy on stable oral lamotrigine (IR) for at least 2 months,
  University Medical Centre Ljubljana, Slovenia. Exclusions: chronic renal or hepatic disease, pregnancy (p. 401).
- n = 100: 54 monotherapy, 46 with other AEDs. 70 women / 30 men; age median 39.8 (20.7-80.4) y; weight 70
  (50-124) kg; BMI 24.5 (18.7-41.3); height 1.67 (1.52-1.90) m; Crs 74 (39-125) umol/L; CLcr (Cockcroft-Gault)
  107.6 (40.84-246) mL/min; LTG dose 200 (50-600) mg/day (Table 1, median (range)).
- Co-medication (Table 1): inducers carbamazepine 4, oxcarbazepine 6, phenytoin 2, phenobarbital 2, oral
  contraceptives 2; inhibitors valproic acid 13, sertraline 2. Smokers 17 / non-smokers 59 (24 % missing).
- Two samples per patient (approximate trough and 2-4 h peak at steady state): 195 LTG and 195 LTG-N-2-glucuronide
  concentrations (LTG 0.7-23.18 mg/L). HPLC-UV, LLOQ 0.25 mg/L for both (p. 401, 402).
- NONMEM 7.3, FOCE-I, ADVAN2 one compartment with first-order absorption; priors from a random-effects
  meta-analysis of 11 published popPK studies (Table 3) via NONMEM $PRIOR; bootstrap 1000.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. Reference range 2.5-15 mg/L quoted from Patsalos 2008 (ref 10, Introduction). Not an opioid paper.

## Covariates
Final model (Table 4 footnote):
CL (L/h) = 2.40 x (1 - 0.579 Inh) x (Wt/70)^0.938 x (1 + 0.340 Tob) x (1 + 0.546 Ind) x (1 - 0.0358 UGT2B7-161CT)
x (1 - 0.204 UGT2B7-161TT) x (1 + 0.194 UGT2B7 372AG) x (1 + 1.17 UGT2B7 372GG) x (1 + 0.00328 (CLcr - 110))
V (L) = 76.2 x (1 + 0.0181 (Wt - 70))
Inh = valproic acid or sertraline; Ind = carbamazepine, phenobarbital or phenytoin (oxcarbazepine not an inducer
here); Tob = smoking. Discussion quotes the weight exponent as 0.932 (Table 4: 0.938). UGT1A4, SLC22A1 and ABCB1 not
significant.

## Caveats
- Sparse sampling (2 per patient); ka and IIV(ka), IIV(V) largely carried by priors (authors' sensitivity analysis:
  shifting prior means by +/-50 % moves ka and V estimates by -18 to +30 %, CL by -24 to +37 %).
- Mixed co-medication: base-model values are an average over 54 % monotherapy, 13 % valproate, 8 % inducers.
- Mostly women (70 %); one centre; Slovenian adults.
