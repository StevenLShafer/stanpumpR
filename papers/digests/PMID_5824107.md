# Fabian 1969: half-life, volume and clearance of AVP, LVP and oxytocin in man (bioassay)

## Study
Charing Cross Hospital, London. Healthy volunteers 20-48 y. AVP arm: six subjects aged 20-22 (five men, one woman)
given arginine vasopressin 120 mU/min IV for 20 min (syringe pump, forearm vein); blood 2 min before stopping and
about 4 and 8, or 5 and 10 min after (PDF p3). AVP was a partially purified bovine pituitary extract (Parke-Davis
arginine Pitressin, 40 U/mg, 10 U/mL ampoules), and the same preparation was the bioassay standard (PDF p4). Assay:
antidiuretic activity in the water-loaded ethanol-anaesthetised rat. Lysine vasopressin (single injection n = 6,
infusion n = 4) and oxytocin arms also studied; antidiuretic potency in 35 water-loaded men; ultrafiltration
binding; in-vitro stability. Medians with 95% confidence limits; weights not reported.

## Model and parameters
Single-exponential decline after the infusion, semi-log linear over the 4 to 10 min covered. Each subject gave
TWO post-infusion samples (about 4 and 8, or 5 and 10 min), so no subject was followed continuously: Fig. 5 (PDF p7)
pools the individual points of ten subjects (four LVP, six AVP) "as the samples were all taken at different times",
expressed as percentages of the value at 4 MIN "to obtain the best fit", so nothing before 4 min enters the fit.
The Discussion's "10 min samples" (PDF p11) are the latest samples, not ten minutes of sampling.
Infusion clearance from the steady state; V from clearance and the time constant. Table 1 (PDF p7, rendered):

Derived: V/CL = 8.5 min, so a one-compartment half-life of 5.9 min from the two medians, inside the 3.9-9.5 limits.
V is about two thirds of extracellular volume (authors).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Antidiuresis in overhydrated men (100 mU/70 kg AVP or LVP; 1 U oxytocin): AVP's peak inhibition of urine flow in the
second half hour, still significant at 2-2.5 h; LVP's effect over within 60 min (Fig. 5, PDF p7-8). No pressor data,
no ke0. Threshold antidiuretic dose about 1-10 mU (cited, PDF p12).

## Covariates
None; weights not reported.

## Caveats
Authors call the half-lives approximations: infusions limited to about 20 min (about 90% of equilibrium), one
pre-stop sample and only two after it, the latest at 10 min, so a slower phase could be missed (PDF p10-11).
The curve is therefore characterised between 4 and 10 min after the infusion stops and nowhere else, which is what
limits comparison with a study sampling a different window (Baumann 1976, PMID_1262458, excludes the first 10 min). Bioassay. Extract
rather than synthetic AVP.
