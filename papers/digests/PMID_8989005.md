# Burm 1997: enantiomer pharmacokinetics of intravenous racemic mepivacaine

## Study
10 healthy non-smoking male volunteers, 20-35 y, 75-93 kg. Racemic mepivacaine HCl 60 mg (3 mg/mL) IV at 6 mg/min over
10 min. Venous (contralateral antecubital) samples at 2, 5, 10, 15, 20, 30 min, every 15 min to 120, every 30 min to
240, hourly to 8 h. Enantioselective HPLC (LOQ 5 ng/mL each enantiomer). Protein binding by equilibrium dialysis of
blank plasma spiked to 400 ng/mL of each enantiomer. Non-compartmental: AUC (linear up / log down trapezoid),
CL = dose/AUC, MRT = AUMC/AUC - T/2, Vss = CL x MRT, V = CL/λz; λz from 180-240 min onward. Dose per enantiomer 30 mg
(derived: racemate; solution confirmed truly racemic).

Ratios R/S: fu 1.43, CL 2.25, CLu 1.58, Vss 1.79 (Table 1). End-of-infusion total concentrations R 505 ± 168, S 673 ± 207
ng/mL; unbound 177 ± 58 and 167 ± 53 ng/mL. Total-concentration terminal V (Vz) row is not legible in the text layer.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Not an opioid; no effect measures. No toxicity; haemodynamics unchanged. No ke0, no concentration-effect relation.

## Covariates
None (10 young men only).

## Caveats
- Small n, young healthy men, single low dose (60 mg); venous sampling.
- Non-compartmental: no central volume or distribution clearances reported.
- Protein binding measured in spiked blank plasma at 400 ng/mL; binding is concentration dependent (authors cite higher
  unbound fractions at higher concentrations), so fu may not hold at block doses.
- Vree 1992 (block with epinephrine, arterial) found higher CL and much larger Vss than this study.
