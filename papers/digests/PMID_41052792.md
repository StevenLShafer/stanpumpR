# Ding 2026: artemether, dihydroartemisinin and lumefantrine (TRACII and TACT-CV)

## Study
- Pooled pharmacokinetics from two trials of triple artemisinin combination therapy against
  artemether-lumefantrine alone: TRACII (575 patients in the lumefantrine analysis) and TACT-CV (310).
- 885 patients with uncomplicated *P. falciparum* malaria, 443 given artemether-lumefantrine and 442 with
  amodiaquine added.
  - Age 17.0-25.0 years by arm (range 1.9-65), median by arm.
  - Weight 41.5-52.2 kg by arm (range 9.0-101).
  - Medians (min-max), Table 1.
- Dense sampling in 79 patients (41 TRACII, Bangladesh; 38 TACT-CV, Vietnam), children under 20 kg excluded:
  1, 2, 4, 6, 8, 12, 24 and 64 h, days 4, 7, 14 and 28 (52 h as well in TRACII). Artemether and
  dihydroartemisinin were not measured from day 4.
- Sparse sampling in the rest: baseline, day 7 and at recurrence.
- LC-MS/MS. Lumefantrine's lower limit 9.71 ng/mL; the artemether assay is in the paper's reference 14.
- NONMEM 7.5, FOCE-I. Below-limit samples: discarded (M1) for artemether and dihydroartemisinin, censored
  (M3) for lumefantrine.
- Weight was put on every clearance and volume with exponents fixed at 0.75 and 1 before any other
  covariate was tested.

## Model and parameters
Artemether and dihydroartemisinin, a joint parent-metabolite model (Table 2):
- Artemether: two transit compartments "with an identical rate constant between compartments", then two
  compartments.
- Dihydroartemisinin: one compartment. The whole of artemether's elimination forms it ("completely
  metabolized", Methods 2.6).
- Clearance rises with each dose: "[(1 + θ*[OCC-1])*CL]", the dose occasion from 1 to 6.
- The authors: the time dependence "is not suitable for extrapolation" beyond 3 days.

Lumefantrine (Table 4): five transit compartments, then two compartments. Desbutyl-lumefantrine was fitted
afterwards, sequentially.

All values are for a typical 45 kg adult. Lumefantrine's reference is also a baseline parasite density of
10^4.56 /µL, a temperature of 37.5 °C and a dose of 9.8 mg/kg.

Secondary parameters, medians (95% CI) of the post hoc estimates, artemether-lumefantrine arm:

| Quantity | Value |
|---|---|

| artemether AUC | 2,850 h·ng/mL |
| artemether Cmax | 256 ng/mL |
| dihydroartemisinin AUC | 1,870 h·ng/mL |

The abstract prints the day-7 values in µg/mL; Table 4 prints ng/mL.

## Covariates
- Amodiaquine changed no parameter of artemether, dihydroartemisinin or lumefantrine.
- Lumefantrine: parasite density, the dose in mg/kg and temperature on F, and a study effect on Vc
  (Table 4). The authors call the linear dose term unfit for extrapolation beyond the doses studied.
- Desbutyl-lumefantrine: age on clearance as a maturation (age to half maturation 10.1 years).

## Caveats
- **Transit-chain convention not printed.** The text gives the number of transit compartments and the mean
  transit time, not the equation. The group's usual (N + 1)/MTT reading reproduces the medians better
  (derived: see below).
- **Dose basis of the metabolite not stated** (mass or moles). Mass reproduces the median dihydroartemisinin
  area (derived).
- **The lumefantrine Cmax** (open question above).

## Verification against stanpumpR
- **Every typical value above:** matches Tables 2 and 4, with weight scaled to 45 kg.
- **Transit chains:** read as the dose depot plus N transit compartments, every step at (N + 1)/MTT. For the
  typical 45 kg patient on six doses of 80/480 mg, stanpumpR gives an artemether first-dose peak of 239
  ng/mL at 2.0 h and a dihydroartemisinin peak of 128 ng/mL, against the medians 256 and 135. The reading
  with N compartments at N/MTT gives 220 and 116 (derived, 2026-11-24).
- **Areas:** artemether about 3,000 and dihydroartemisinin 1,890 h·ng/mL (medians 2,850 and 1,870);
  lumefantrine day 7, 462 ng/mL, and AUC to 21 days, 591 h·µg/mL (medians 452 and 600). Derived.
