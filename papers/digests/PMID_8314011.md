# Howey 1994: LYSPRO vs regular insulin, SC and IV, glucose clamp

## Study
Open, randomized crossover, n = 10 healthy non-diabetic men (age 35.2 +/- 5.3 y, height 1.77 m, weight 70.9 +/- 5.9 kg, BMI 22.6; p.397). Four treatments, 10 U each at 100 U/mL, at least 5 days apart: zinc-free lispro SC (A), zinc-containing lispro SC (B), human regular SC (C), human regular IV (D). Biostator euglycaemic clamp, 12 h, target 0.28 mM below fasting glucose. Serum insulin and C-peptide by RIA (Coat-A-Count; lispro and insulin displace equally), sampled 0 to 12 h. AUC from C-peptide-corrected insulin. Noncompartmental PK (terminal log-linear beta, MRT by moments). GIR averaged over 5 min for the first 6 h, 20 min after.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point is the glucose infusion rate (GIR) during the euglycaemic clamp (Table 1, printed p.399). No ke0 or effect-site model is reported.
- Rmax (mmol/min): lispro SC 3.10 +/- 1.19; zinc lispro SC 3.08 +/- 1.14; regular SC 2.20 +/- 1.01; regular IV 4.02 +/- 1.38.
- TRmax, time to peak GIR (min): lispro SC 99 +/- 39; zinc lispro 116 +/- 43; regular SC 179 +/- 93; regular IV 23 +/- 5.
- Gtot to 12 h (mmol): 450.8, 476.6, 454.7, 280.6 respectively.
- The 23-min IV regular TRmax is a time to peak effect after a bolus, i.e. a tPeak-type measure on the GIR end point. No hysteresis analysis is given.
- Authors judge lispro and regular equipotent (equal Gtot and AUC); no potency ratio is stated.

## Covariates
None analysed. Weight-based Cl/F and V/F only.

## Caveats
n = 10 men, single 10 U dose, absolute dose (not per kg; about 0.14 U/kg at 70.9 kg, derived). SC t1/2 and V/F are flip-flop contaminated per the authors; only the IV regular beta reflects disposition. Large SD on IV MRT (32 +/- 46) and V/F. Biostator GIR is cyclic and was averaged.
