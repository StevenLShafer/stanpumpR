# Ding 2026: PBPK oliceridine breast-milk exposure and relative infant dose after cesarean

## Study
Simulation study only; no patients, no measured milk or plasma data. PK-Sim v11.0 (Open Systems Pharmacology) with the OSP postpartum physiology database (PKSimDB_v11.sqlite, Lactation-PBPK-model repository); default postpartum individual (maternal weight 66.24 kg, age and height at template medians, not stated in the text). The oliceridine compound model was built from the FDA label, PubChem and DrugBank (non-default inputs in Supplementary Table S1, not in the PDF). Molecular weight 386.6 g/mol; plasma protein binding about 77% (introduction, p2).

Dosing: a reconstructed 24 h post-cesarean PCIA demand-dose-only sequence. Target dose = median 24 h cesarean morphine PCA dose from the literature divided by 5 (label statement that 1 mg oliceridine is about equianalgesic to 5 mg IV morphine, for guidance only). Bolus 0.35 mg, lockout 6 min, no background infusion, no loading dose. Actual dose delivered 13.3 mg over 0-24 h (Table 1), versus label maximum daily dose 27 mg. Hourly distribution from morphine PCA consumption patterns (Table S2, Fig 2).

Milk exposure was not simulated: it is a post-processing step with an assumed milk-to-plasma AUC ratio M/P = 1.0 (sensitivity 0.5-2.0). Infant milk intake 150 mL/kg/day (135-200 sensitivity). RID = infant daily dose / maternal weight-normalised dose. An exposure scaling factor ESF = observed / model-predicted AUC0-inf in a single-dose adult IV scenario (1.5 mg, healthy men, CP130-1003, ref 31; observed AUC 43.8 ng.h/mL from a Chinese phase I report, ref 32; model 53.61 ng.h/mL) = 0.817, applied only to RID.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No ke0, t1/2 ke0, tPeak, EC50, MEAC or analgesic concentration is reported, and no effect end point was modelled. The only potency ratio is the label equianalgesic guidance 1 mg oliceridine ~ 5 mg IV morphine (Methods, PDF p4, quoted from the FDA label, "for guidance only"; used to convert the morphine PCA dose). The RID reference threshold used is the empirical 10%. M/P benchmarking: a simplified pH-partition calculation for morphine gave M/P 2.31 (plasma unbound fraction 0.65, milk unbound fraction 1.0 assumed) versus about 2.5 label AUC-based M/P (7.6% difference); this is a check of the method, not of oliceridine.

## Covariates
Only maternal body weight enters RID (one-way sensitivity 52.99-79.49 kg, i.e. +/-20%; high-exposure scenario 100 kg with dose held constant). No pregnancy or postpartum covariate equations for clearance or volume are reported. No maternal pregnancy or postpartum clearance change is estimated.

## Caveats
- No measured oliceridine milk or postpartum plasma data; M/P is assumed.
- Dose regimen is reconstructed from morphine PCA data and a label equianalgesic ratio, not an actual oliceridine regimen.
- ESF is from healthy adult men after IV dosing, not postpartum women; the authors say it is not a validation of postpartum PK.
- Mean daily milk concentration only; no time course within feeds.
- Plasma concentration basis (total vs unbound) and the actual compartmental parameters are not in the main text.
- Authors report no conflicts; funding from the Wu Jieping Medical Foundation and Natural Science Foundation of Dongying.
