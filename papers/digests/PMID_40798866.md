# Ekobena 2025: bictegravir popPK in real-world people with HIV

## Study
- Retrospective TDM data, Swiss HIV Cohort Study / Lausanne University Hospital, July 2019 to July 2024. 708 steady-state plasma concentrations from 572 people with HIV (815 collected; 107 excluded: 42 undetectable, 40 missing time, 25 uncertain information). Median 1 sample per person (range 1-4); time after last dose 0.25-32 h.
- Population (Table 1, PDF p.4): 69% male; age median 51 y (19-81); weight median 74 kg (37-135); height 173 cm; BMI 24.5 (12.5-44.5); albumin 45 g/L (28-55); 3 pregnant. Dose: standard 50 mg once daily (fixed-dose with emtricitabine/tenofovir alafenamide).
- Assay: UHPLC-MS/MS, LLOQ 15 ng/mL, total plasma concentration. Monolix 2024R1 (SAEM), steady state assumed (reference t1/2 17.3 h).

## Model and parameters
One-compartment, first-order absorption and elimination, proportional error. ka fixed at 0.64 /h (initial estimate RSE 38%, unstable on bootstrap). IIV on CL only. Table 2 (PDF p.4, rendered page); parameters are apparent (CL/F, V/F).

Final model (Table 2): CLi = 0.46 * exp(0.35*ln(BW/70) - 0.20*(Age-51)/51 + eta_i), CL in L/h.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- No PD model, no ke0. Exposure target: trough 760 ng/mL (inhibitory quotient 4.7 x protein-adjusted EC95 of 162 ng/mL; cited from a Gilead exposure-response abstract, ref 14), total plasma concentration; no upper limit.
- Simulated 50 mg QD steady-state trough (Table 3, PDF p.6; ng/mL): typical patient median 2781, P5-P95 1477-4597 (min 906, max 6721); age 20 y, 135 kg median 1593 (P5 797, P95 2906) - 43% below typical (derived: 1593/2781 = 0.57, matches text); age 65-80, BW 40-95 kg median 3267; age 20-65, BW 95-135 kg median 1991. About 5% of 20 y/135 kg fall below 760.
- Not an opioid paper: no MEAC or potency ratio.

## Covariates
Body weight (log-linear, dBICc -21.0) and age (linear, dBICc -3.1) on CL; BMI dropped after multivariate step. 100 kg at 51 y: CL +13%; 80 y at 70 kg: CL -11%. Sex, ethnicity, albumin, inducers/inhibitors, divalent cations tested; no clear effect (few on interacting drugs: 15 inhibitor samples, 4 inducer samples, Table 1).

## Caveats
Sparse routine TDM (mostly one sample per person), absorption not identifiable (ka fixed), no external validation, many missing covariates, no PK-PD analysis. Manufacturer popPK for comparison (as quoted, p.2783): CL 0.504 L/h, V 11.6 L, ka 2.6 /h with lag 0.235 h; this paper's CL 0.46 L/h and V 10.9 L are within about 9% and 6%.
