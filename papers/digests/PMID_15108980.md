# Struys 2004: simulated comparison of two BIS-guided propofol closed-loop controllers

PDF pages 1-8 = printed pages 640-647.

## Study
Simulation study, no patients. Two published BIS-driven propofol controllers were run (RUGLOOP II framework) against a patient simulator on a second computer: a model-based, patient-individualised effect-site controller (Struys 2001; Emax curve fitted during induction, effect-site TCI) and a PID controller (Absalom 2002; plasma-concentration control; gains tuned earlier for an auditory evoked potential target of 35). Both act at least every 5 s (p 641).

Simulator (p 642): propofol plasma and effect-site concentrations from the Schnider PK-PD model (refs 8, 9); effect-site concentration converted to BIS with an Emax model; BIS delayed by a per-patient delay; normal noise with mean 0, SD 3 added; a stimulus-dependent offset (Fig 2) added. One case lasts exactly 1 h; 10 virtual patients x 3 BIS targets (30, 50, 70) x 2 controllers = 60 runs. Performance was scored only in maintenance (from 10 min after start of induction), because the PID controller cannot run induction.

Metrics (Varvel 1992): performance error PE = (measured - target)/target x 100 (Eq 1); MDPE (bias), MDAPE (inaccuracy), divergence (slope of PE against time) and wobble (median |PE - MDPE|). "Accurate" control = BIS within +/-10 of target. Paired t test.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point is the BIS (not an opioid paper). Per-patient sigmoid Emax parameters are in Table 1; no ke0, t1/2ke0 or tPeak is stated: effect-site kinetics come from Schnider (refs 8, 9) without values. No MEAC and no potency ratio.

## Covariates
None; the 10 virtual patients carry no age or weight, and Schnider covariates are not described.

## Caveats
- Only 10 virtual patients, one stimulus profile; the simulator uses the same Emax model type as the model-based controller, which the authors say may favour it.
- The PID controller was run as published, with plasma-site control and gains tuned for an AEP index, not retuned for BIS.
- Both controllers undershoot the target (MDPE negative); authors attribute it to controlling infusion but not elimination.
- Table 3 labels divergence "/s" while the text says percent per minute (p 642); the PDF does not settle which.
- The text-layer copy of Table 3 is scrambled; the values above are from the rendered page.
