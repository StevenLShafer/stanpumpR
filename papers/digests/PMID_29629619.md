# Soulele 2019: total ezetimibe population PK with enterohepatic recirculation

## Study
Population PK of total ezetimibe (parent + glucuronide, summed) from a two-period crossover bioequivalence study (test vs Ezetrol 10 mg tablets), single oral 10 mg dose, fasted, 14-day washout (PDF p. 3). 36 enrolled; 28 male subjects analysed (Table 1, PDF p. 7): age mean 27 y (19-37), weight 59.6 kg (47.3-76.7), height 165.4 cm, BMI 21.8 kg/m2. 26 samples per period to 96 h; 96 h truncated (about 95% below LLOQ), data used to 72 h; 1508 concentrations. Standard meals at 4, 8, 12, 24 h. Assay: HPLC-MS/MS, LLOQ 0.1 ng/mL, range 0.1-15.0 ng/mL, K3-EDTA plasma. Monolix 2016R1, SAEM. Test and reference pooled; period (IOV) and treatment tested as covariates.

## Model and parameters
Four compartments: GI depot, central (blood + liver), peripheral, gallbladder (GB). First-order absorption ka from GI; central to peripheral (Q); central to GB (kb); elimination kel from central (renal + faecal together). GB empties as a bolus into GI at three fixed meal-linked times, duration fixed 0.75 h, release rate fixed 21 h-1 (not estimated); GI drug is re-absorbed with the same ka. Equations 1-6 on PDF p. 7. All volumes and Q are apparent (/F) because no IV data. Combined error model C = f + (a + b f) e.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No PD, ke0, EC50, MEAC or potency ratio. Not an opioid. Context only: Cmax about 1 h; secondary peaks at 4-5, 11-13 and 23-25 h (Figure 1, text p. 9); terminal t1/2 about 22 h is quoted from earlier literature, not estimated here.

## Covariates
Tested: weight, age, height, BMI, AST, ALT, bilirubin fractions, creatinine, albumin, haemoglobin, urea, IOV, treatment. Allometric scaling was a priori (70 kg; exponents 1 for volumes, 0.75 for clearance). None significant; final model has no covariates (all-male, narrow weight range).

## Caveats
Total (parent + glucuronide) concentration, not free parent; apparent parameters (F unknown); healthy male volunteers only (ethnicity and site not stated); EHC timing and release rate fixed from meal schedule (authors acknowledge this can bias other estimates); a high BSV on Q/F (86.6%) and kb (RSE 88% on BSV). The paper's Table 2 footnote labels kb "L/h".
