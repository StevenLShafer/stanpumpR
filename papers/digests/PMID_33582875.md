# Morimoto 2021: SmartPilot View and recovery from desflurane

## Study
Single-centre prospective randomized trial (envelope randomization), Ube Industries Central Hospital, Japan; UMIN000019891. 34 ASA I-II adults (20-70 y) enrolled for elective, mostly orthopaedic surgery (predicted 1-5 h); 4 excluded for surgery under 30 min, leaving 15 per group (Table 1, PDF p. 3; the abstract and Methods still say 15 and 17). Table 1: age 48 +/- 18 (C) vs 50 +/- 16 (SPV) y, weight 63 +/- 10 vs 65 +/- 14 kg, anaesthesia time 119 +/- 51 vs 124 +/- 40 min.

Anaesthesia: propofol 1-2 mg/kg and rocuronium 0.6 mg/kg induction; desflurane (end-tidal about 4.2%) with remifentanil infusion and some fentanyl, titrated to BIS 40-60 and to MAC 90 (NSRI below 20), on a Perseus A500 workstation (fresh gas 2 L/min, 60% O2). Effect-site desflurane, remifentanil and fentanyl concentrations were calculated by Draeger SmartPilot View (SPV); the paper gives no PK/PD model parameters for them. In group SPV, about 10 min before the end of surgery desflurane and remifentanil were reduced to reach MAC 90 (NSRI 20); group C was left unchanged. Sugammadex 2-4 mg/kg at the end; fresh gas 10 L/min after stopping desflurane. Primary outcome: time to eye opening after stopping desflurane. Sample size 17/group assumed from an earlier 284 +/- 60 s.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Not an opioid PK/PD study; no MEAC, no ke0/t1/2ke0/tPeak and no equianalgesic ratio are reported (audit: none). NSRI (Luginbuhl 2010, ref 5) is a 0-100 index of the probability of responding to laryngoscopy; the paper equates MAC 90 with NSRI 20. Effect-site concentrations are SPV outputs, not measured; the time-course settings behind them are not given. Concentrations at eye opening were statistically similar between groups; the SPV group got there sooner because the effect-site desflurane was lower at the start of emergence.

## Covariates
None analysed.

## Caveats
Authors list: 80-93% of patients had peripheral nerve blocks (so opioid effect is hard to judge), awakening assessor not blinded, no neuromuscular monitoring, SPV used only at the end of surgery, SPV is population-based and was combined with BIS. Internal inconsistencies in the paper: abstract gives eye-opening 292 +/- 53 / 218 +/- 44 s and orientation 316 +/- 57 s, whereas Table 2 and Results give 292 +/- 33 / 218 +/- 47 and 319 +/- 59 (Results text says 218 +/- 46); the abstract and Methods state 17 SPV patients, Table 1 says 15.
