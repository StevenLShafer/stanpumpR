# Sasaki 1996: multiple-dose losartan in essential hypertension

## Study
Open, single-blind, sequential design (PDF pp1-3). Nine Japanese inpatients with essential hypertension (supine
SBP >= 150 and DBP >= 90 mmHg), 6 men, 3 women, mean age 52 y (33-69), mean weight 68 kg (47-86); no medication for
>= 2 weeks; no severe renal or hepatic disease. Standardised meals (25 g protein, 15 g lipid, 3 g NaCl).
Placebo once daily days -7 to -1, then losartan 50 mg orally at 09:00 days 1-9. BP and pulse (automated, supine)
and plasma samples at -1, 1, 2, 4, 8, 12 and 23 h after dosing on day -1 (BP only, placebo), day 1 and day 7.
Losartan and E-3174 (EXP3174) by HPLC. Non-compartmental analysis: Cmax and tmax observed, AUC0-23 by linear
trapezoid, t1/2 = ln2/ke from log-linear terminal regression. ANOVA / paired t test. Uric acid and creatinine
clearance before, during and after treatment.

## Model and parameters
No compartmental model. Table I (printed p404 / PDF p2; values confirmed on the rendered page), mean ± SD, n = 9,
50 mg oral:

|---|---|---|
| Losartan Cmax (ng/mL) | 207 ± 57 | 167 ± 98 |
| Losartan tmax (h) | 3 ± 1 | 3 ± 1 |
| E-3174 Cmax (ng/mL) | 393 ± 117 | 397 ± 122 |
| E-3174 tmax (h) | 5 ± 2 | 5 ± 2 |
| E-3174 AUC0-23 (ng·h/mL) | 2965 ± 754 | 3288 ± 1078 |
| E-3174 t1/2 (h) | 4.0 ± 1.1 | 4.5 ± 0.9 |

Losartan AUC and t1/2 were not reported: losartan was detectable at only a few time points in 5 patients after each
dose (PDF p3). None of the day-1 vs day-7 differences was significant.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: supine SBP, DBP and pulse on day -1 (placebo), day 1 and day 7 (Figure 2, PDF p3); ΔBP in Figure 3 is
  mean pressure on placebo minus mean pressure on losartan.
- SBP and DBP fell after both doses (P < 0.01, ANOVA) and were still lower at 23 h than on placebo. The fall after
  the 7th dose was significantly greater than after the 1st (P < 0.05, ANOVA) (PDF p3). The size of the day-7 excess
  is shown only graphically.
- Pulse rate unchanged.
- No significant correlation between losartan or E-3174 concentration and ΔSBP or ΔDBP (PDF p3).
- Hysteresis (Figure 3, PDF p4, day-1 and day-7 data pooled): the effect rose to 2 h, dipped at 4 h, and rose again at
  8 h (P < 0.05 vs 4 h) while losartan concentration fell; maximal ΔBP about 22 mmHg systolic at 8 h (read from the
  figure, approximate), when E-3174 was near its peak. Authors attribute the late effect to E-3174.
- No ke0, EC50, Emax or concentration-effect model. Potency: E-3174 is cited (from ref. 3, not measured here) as
  having at least tenfold higher AT1 affinity than losartan.
- Authors' explanations for the larger day-7 effect: not accumulation (none observed); possibly enhanced
  aldosterone suppression (not measured).
- Uric acid: plasma uric acid fell and ClUA/Clcr rose during treatment (Figure 4, n = 8), returning to baseline
  6 days after stopping.
- Not an opioid paper.

## Covariates
None examined (authors note age and sex were not studied).

## Caveats
- n = 9; no parallel placebo group (the authors say so), so period/order effects are not separated from the
  day-7 enhancement.
- Losartan PK is incomplete (AUC and t1/2 not computable in 5 of 9); "no PK change" for the parent rests on Cmax
  and tmax only, and losartan Cmax was numerically lower on day 7 (167 vs 207 ng/mL).
- Sampling only to 23 h shortens the apparent E-3174 half-life.
- Japanese inpatients on a controlled 3 g NaCl diet.
