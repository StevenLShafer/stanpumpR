# Liu 2016: closed-loop propofol editorial

## Study
Three-page editorial (no data of its own) accompanying Puri et al. 2016 (Anesth Analg 122:106-14), a randomized multicentre trial of closed-loop (CLADS) versus manual propofol administration guided by BIS. Per the editorial: 242 ASA I-II patients, elective noncardiac surgery, 6 centres in India, BIS set point 50. Percentage of maintenance time with BIS 40-60: 17%-67% in the manual group versus 80%-85% in the closed-loop group (editorial p. 4). Manual providers made >10 propofol target changes per hour.

## Caveats
Opinion piece. It compares closed-loop with the manual comparator in trials, and uses an aviation-autopilot analogy for the risk transfer after introducing automation. The authors declare closed-loop patents/company interests (Liu: gain-constant/control-algorithm patent, MedSteer; Rinehart: unrelated fluid patent).
