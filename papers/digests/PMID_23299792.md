# Ferron 2013: cabazitaxel population PK

## Study
Pooled population PK of cabazitaxel from five studies (three phase I: TED6188, TED6189, TED6190; phase II ARD6191 in taxane-resistant breast cancer; phase III EFC6193 TROPIC in mCRPC), 170 patients (110 men, 60 women), 2,322 measurable concentrations (Table 1, Table 3, p3-6). Dose 10-30 mg/m2 as a 1-h IV infusion, every 3 weeks or weekly x4 of a 5-week cycle (only cycle 1 day 1 used for weekly trials). Mean age 60.4 y, weight 75.3 kg (range 35-133), BSA 1.85 m2 (median 1.84, range 1.30-2.53) (Table 3, all studies). Plasma (lithium heparin), LC-MS/MS, LOQ 1.0 ng/mL; samples below LOQ excluded (322, 76% of them pre-dose); 67 samples removed as outliers (1 obvious + 66 statistical, 2.8%). NONMEM VI, FOCE-I. Total (not free) plasma concentration. Cabazitaxel is 89-92% protein bound (Introduction).

## Model and parameters
Linear three-compartment model, first-order elimination from the central compartment, parameterised as CL, V1 and micro rate constants (ADVAN11 TRANS1). IIV on CL, V1, K12, K13, K31 (not K21); IOV (cycle = occasion) on CL and V1; proportional residual error. Final covariates: CL = 48.5 x (BSA/1.84) x (1 - 0.543 x TT1) L/h, TT1 = 1 for breast cancer (Eq. 9, p7). BSA effect is linear (power term not retained). Breast-cancer effect is confounded with study (34 of 37 breast patients are from ARD6191); the authors say a study effect fits better than a tumour-type effect (Discussion p10). Sex, weight, height, BMI, age, race, renal and hepatic function, CYP inducers not retained. Terminal half-life, Vss and CL here are larger/longer/lower than the earlier individual phase I fits; the authors attribute this to better-estimated terminal phase (p10).

CL at BSA 1.50 and 2.22 m2 (5th/95th percentile): 39.5 and 58.5 L/h (p7). Empirical Bayes means per m2 are in Table 5 (e.g. all patients CL 24.2-34.5 L/h/m2 by study; text-layer scrambled, not used).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None reported. No effect-site model, ke0, EC50 or therapeutic range. (Not an opioid.)

## Covariates
BSA (linear on CL), breast cancer on CL (see above). Univariate screens also flagged weight, height, ALT, inducers on CL and BSA, weight, Caucasian race, GI tumour on V1 (p7); none kept after BSA. Mild (n=59) and moderate (n=14) renal impairment did not alter PK; only 1 patient had CrCl <30 mL/min (p9).

## Caveats
Study-confounded breast-cancer effect with a very wide CI (9.3-97.9% reduction). Doses are BSA-based, so BSA-on-CL is largely absorbed by dosing. Bootstrap: 712 of 1,000 runs succeeded. Excluding 66 outlier samples matters: re-including them lowered CL by 31%. No AAG measured. Few patients with elevated transaminases.
