# Kim 2015: EOAA/S, propofol concentration vs sedation and noxious stimulation

## Study
- 20 healthy volunteers (10 F, 10 M), ASA I-II, age 18-55, BMI 18-29 kg/m2 (p1-2 / printed 570-573). Characteristics are in Supplementary Table S1 (not in PDF).
- Fentanyl TCI, effect-site target 0.8 ng/mL held throughout (Shafer 1990 kinetics, run in Stanpump). Propofol effect-site TCI from 0.5 ug/mL in 0.25 ug/mL steps (Schnider 1998 kinetics) until unresponsive to 50 mA tetanic stimulation (TES) at two consecutive targets.
- 5 min equilibration after each target; then BIS, MOAA/S, then TES (about 5 mA every 5 s up to 50 mA for 5 s), then post-TES BIS and MOAA/S. Trapezius squeeze about 0.7 kg/cm2 (10 psi) for 5 s. New score 0E = no response to 50 mA TES; 0T = no response to trapezius squeeze.
- Concentrations are model-predicted (not measured). NONMEM 7.2, naive pooled (no IIV), bootstrap 2000 sets.

## Model and parameters
Proportional-odds sigmoid model of P(score <= s) on predicted propofol Ce; Ce50,s per score; common gamma; STIM multiplier on Ce50 after TES (not applied to 0E). BIS fitted with an inhibitory sigmoid Emax. All Ce50 in ug/mL (Table 2, PDF p5 / printed 573; the figure axes say "mg ml-1", an apparent typo for ug/mL).

Performance: 63% exact and 86% within-one-score predictions; prediction probability Pk 0.84 (SE 0.001) (p573).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End points: MOAA/S-style ordinal score (5 to 0E) and BIS, both against propofol Ce at a fixed fentanyl Ce of 0.8 ng/mL.
- No ke0, t1/2ke0 or tPeak is reported or fitted (pseudo-steady-state design; Ce is the Schnider-model effect-site prediction, whose ke0 the paper does not state).
- Raw data (text p573): no subject unresponsive to 50 mA TES below 2.5 ug/mL; first unresponsiveness to squeeze at 1.75 ug/mL; most unresponsive to TES at about 3.5 ug/mL or more; all by about 5 ug/mL. Ce50 for 0E is stated as 1.7 times that for 0T (p575).
- Fentanyl: only a fixed co-administered target (0.8 ng/mL, effect site, predicted from Shafer 1990). No MEAC, no analgesic concentration, no fentanyl potency ratio. The paper says propofol Ce50s depend on opioid concentration (response-surface interaction), so the values apply at this fentanyl level only.

## Covariates
None modelled (naive pooled, no IIV); STIM is the only modifier.

## Caveats
- Predicted not measured concentrations; Schnider and Shafer kinetics are imperfect (authors' limitation).
- Small n; single fentanyl level; electrical stimulus brief; BIS never reached maximal effect (BIS in high teens/20s), so BIS Emax is influenced; post-TES BIS used a maximum over 90 s, which may exaggerate the shift.
- Authors call the work preliminary; the score is for research, not clinical care.
