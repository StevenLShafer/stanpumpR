# Kamal 2013: oseltamivir and oseltamivir carboxylate population PK, ages 1-78 years

## Study
(Printed page = PDF page + 3469.)
- Pooled analysis of 13 Roche clinical studies (Table 1, PDF p2 / printed p3471): healthy adults (single
  20-1,000 mg and multiple 50-500 mg BID ascending dose), adults inoculated with influenza A or B, adult patients with
  influenza (75 or 150 mg BID x 5 d), healthy geriatric subjects (>=65 y, 75 mg BID), renally impaired adults (100 mg),
  and four paediatric studies (2 mg/kg suspension BID, or 30/45 mg single dose in 1-5 y olds). Neonates and infants
  (<1 y) and haemodialysis patients were not included (PDF p3).
- n = 390 (241 M, 149 F); median age 21 y (range 1-78); median weight 64.5 kg (8-115); median CLCR 95.1
  mL/min/1.73 m2 (13.9-178); 297 normal, 73 mild, 19 moderate, 1 severe renal dysfunction (PDF p3-4).
- Oral route only (no IV data). Intensive sampling (>=5 samples) in 10 of 13 studies; sparse in PV15616 (3),
  JV16284 (2), WV15758 (3; ~7 at selected sites in 5 children).
- 3,881 oseltamivir and 4,402 OC plasma concentrations. Assay HPLC/MS/MS; LLOQ 1 ng/mL (oseltamivir), 8.8 ng/mL (OC);
  CV +/-3% and +/-6% (PDF p3).
- NONMEM 7.1.2, ADVAN13, FOCE-I. Covariate selection forward alpha 0.01, backward alpha 0.001 (decrease in MVOF of
  10.8). Weight centred on 70 kg; other continuous covariates on their median. CLCR: Cockcroft-Gault with IBW when
  weight > IBW, normalised to 1.73 m2, in adults >=18 y; revised Schwartz (0.413 x height cm / SCr) in 1-17 y; SCr
  floored at 0.7 (adults) or 0.2 mg/dL (children). Infection status was not tested as a covariate.
- Funded by Hoffmann-La Roche; most authors Roche or consultants.

## Model and parameters
Oseltamivir (prodrug, OP): oral depot -> central (Vcp) <-> peripheral (Vp), with all of CLp converting OP to OC; OC: one
compartment (Vcm) with first-order elimination CLm (Fig 1, PDF p4). All OP terms are apparent (/F, F = fraction
absorbed); OC terms are additionally conditioned on the fraction converted to OC (assumed complete). A two-compartment
OC model gave only a modest MVOF drop and was not kept.

Table 2 (PDF p4, printed p3473), final estimates, %SEM in brackets; confirmed on the rendered page:
- ka 0.775 h-1 (3.74)
- CLp/F (L/h) = 519 x (WT/70)^0.838 (coefficient 3.99, exponent 4.67)
- Vcp/F 421 L (6.05); CLd/F 120 L/h (4.95); Vp/F 2,800 L (7.46)
- CLm/F (L/h) = 20.7 x (WT/70)^0.560 x (CLCR/95)^0.487 (3.36, 6.63, 9.01)
- Vcm/F (L) = 238 x (WT/70)^0.830 - 2.25 x (age - 21) (5.16, 18.7, 31.2); additive linear age term, age in years
- IIV (%CV): ka 30.7, CLp/F 42.1, Vcp/F 69.3, CLd/F 62.1, Vp/F 63.7, CLm/F 38.3, Vcm/F 65.3
- Covariance CLp/F-CLm/F 0.0987 (r2 0.372); Vp/F-Vcm/F 0.218 (r2 0.274). (The table labels the second as Vp/F, the
  text on PDF p5 says Vcp/F and Vcm/F.)
- Residual: OP CCV 40.5%; OC CCV 14.0% plus additive 17.9 ng/mL
- Eta shrinkage (PDF p5): ka 46.9%, CLp/F 11.9%, Vcp/F 33.7%, CLd/F 23.4%, Vp/F 35.5%, CLm/F 7.16%, Vcm/F 11.4%.

Derived for a 70 kg, 21 y adult, CLCR 95: OC elimination rate constant 20.7/238 = 0.087 h-1, t1/2 about 8.0 h
(derived; the introduction quotes 6-10 h from earlier work). OP: k10 = 519/421 = 1.23 h-1 (derived).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. Not an opioid; no PD model, no ke0, no target concentration. Simulations only (Fig 4, PDF p6, 75 mg q12h x 5 d):
CLCR 30 vs 120 mL/min/1.73 m2 gives about 2-fold OC exposure; weight 50 to 90 kg changes maximum OC exposure 1.38-fold
(18 y); age 18 vs 55 y changes maximum OC exposure 1.08- to 1.17-fold. Population vs individual concordance for
steady-state OC Cmax r2 0.807 and AUC0-24 r2 0.713 (Fig 5). The PK-PD analysis is in a companion paper (Rayner 2013,
AAC 57:3478, ref 28).

## Covariates
Weight (power, centred 70 kg) on CLp/F, CLm/F, Vcm/F; CLCR (power, centred 95 mL/min/1.73 m2) on CLm/F; age (linear,
centred 21 y) on Vcm/F. Weight on CLp/F was the first and strongest (MVOF -308); it reduced CLp/F IIV from 70% to 42% CV.
Estimated, not fixed, allometric exponents (an allometric 0.75/1 approach was considered and not used). No age
maturation term on CLp/F for children >1 y. Predicted CLm/F fall in moderate renal dysfunction (CLCR 30-60 vs 120):
0.51- to 0.71-fold (PDF p7).

## Caveats
- All parameters are apparent (oral only); OC parameters assume complete conversion, and the authors note CLm/F in normal
  renal function reflects formation-rate-limited kinetics rather than true renal clearance.
- Only 1 subject with CLCR < 30; no children < 1 y, no dialysis; covariate model should not be extrapolated there.
- Vcm/F age term is additive in litres, so for small children it is applied to a much smaller weight-scaled volume
  (e.g. at 10 kg the weight term is about 47 L, and age 3 y adds +40.5 L; derived). The paper does not discuss this.
- High shrinkage on ka (46.9%). Sponsor-funded analysis.
- Supplementary material (diagnostic plots) not in the archived PDF.
