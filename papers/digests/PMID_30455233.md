# Ramharter 2019: mefloquine IPTp PopPK in pregnant Gabonese women

## Study
Ancillary PK study of the MIPPAD randomised trial, two Gabonese sites. 263 pregnant women (Table 1, PDF p3): age mean 24.0 (SD 6.75) y, weight mean 58.3 (10.8) kg, median 55.9, range 39.3-108; BMI mean 23.2; gestational age at entry mean 17.8 wk (range 8-28); Hb mean 10.2 g/dL; HIV-negative, asymptomatic. Two IPTp doses at least 1 month apart: single 15 mg/kg or split 7.5 mg/kg on 2 consecutive days (129 full-dose, 134 split-dose women). 37 rich-PK (5-14 samples) and 226 sparse-PK women. 924 (-)MQ, 923 (+)MQ and 924 CMQ plasma concentrations; cord samples from 126-128 women. 5.2-21.2% of plasma samples below LLOQ (75.4% for (+)MQ cord). NONMEM 7.3; bootstrap n = 250. Concentrations in nmol/L.

## Model and parameters
Each of (-)mefloquine, (+)mefloquine and the metabolite carboxymefloquine (CMQ) is a two-compartment model; first-order absorption for the enantiomers, first-order conversion to CMQ; cord concentrations = plasma x 1 (fixed); CMQ autoinduces its own clearance via two chained turnover models (Fig 2, PDF p4). ## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD model or ke0. Proposed chemoprophylaxis thresholds of plasma mefloquine 500 to 620 ng/mL (PDF p9), equal to 1,321 and 1,638 nmol/L as printed under Fig 4-5 (PDF p7-8). Curative recrudescence-era range 546-730 ng/mL (1,400-1,900 nmol/L) quoted from the literature. Matrix and total vs enantiomer basis not stated in the main text ("total meflo-quine" for the simulation trough). Not an opioid; no MEAC or potency ratio. Observed peaks after dose 1: (-)MQ 611, (+)MQ 357, CMQ 402 nmol/L (medians). At delivery plasma medians: (-)MQ 85, (+)MQ 8.3, CMQ 35.6 nmol/L, below the thresholds (PDF p3, p6).

## Covariates
Baseline weight on central volumes only (exponent 1.33, reference 55 kg). Split dose raises relative bioavailability 5%. Gestational age change in weight did not improve fit. No covariates retained on clearance.

## Caveats
Sparse sampling; absorption phase poorly characterised (no lag or enterohepatic model); high IIV on V (119%); 90% CIs wide for V1 and Emax; pregnant, healthy, asymptomatic women only, so apparent half-life (about 620 h for (-)MQ) is much longer than 173 h in Thai pregnant women with malaria. The paper's own simulation: 825 mg fixed dose, or 1,000 mg load then 250 mg weekly (trough above 500 ng/mL for under 64 kg).
