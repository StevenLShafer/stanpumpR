# Björk 2021: CKD-EPI with age-adjusted creatinine (CKD-EPI40)

## Study
- Method: creatinine of patients aged 2-39 y is rescaled to an equivalent level at age 40, using sex-specific creatinine growth curves.
  - The growth curves come from a pooled reference cohort of 83,157 samples, ages 2-40, from Kortrijk, Liège and Uppsala.
  - The rescaled value goes into the original CKD-EPI equation with age set to 40 ("CKD-EPI40").
- Validation (EKFC database): 4005 children (2-17 y) and 3309 young adults (18-39 y) with measured GFR (inulin, iohexol, iothalamate, 51Cr-EDTA). Creatinine assays were IDMS-traceable.
- Comparators: CKiD (bedside Schwartz), Schwartz-Lyon and original CKD-EPI.

## Model and parameters
Growth curves (Methods; Cr in µmol/L, age in years):
- Males: ln(Cr) = 3.200 + 0.259·Age − 0.543·ln(Age) − 0.00763·Age² + 0.0000790·Age³
- Females: ln(Cr) = 3.080 + 0.177·Age − 0.223·ln(Age) − 0.00596·Age² + 0.0000686·Age³

Adjustment: ln(Ĉr) = ln(Cr) + [curve(40) − curve(Age)]. In other words, Ĉr = Cr × Q(40)/Q(Age).

- Median creatinine at 40 y in the reference cohort: women 63 µmol/L (44-103), men 80 µmol/L (58-116).
- CKD-EPI40 uses the original coefficients with max(age, 40), e.g. men with Ĉr ≤ 80 µmol/L: 141 × (Ĉr/80)^−0.411 × 0.993^max(Age,40).

Results (Table 2, P30):
- Children, mGFR < 75: CKD-EPI40 74.6% vs CKD-EPI 12.2%, CKiD 71.8%, Schwartz-Lyon 80.8%.
- Children, mGFR ≥ 75: CKD-EPI40 87.5% vs 33.4%, 82.1% and 86.7%.
- Young adults: CKD-EPI40 75.6% and 91.9%, vs CKD-EPI 55.6% and 83.3%.
- Accuracy was substandard for every equation at ages 2-5.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Not applicable.

## Covariates
Age and sex, through the growth curves.

## Caveats
- Validated only for 2-39 y and only with CKD-EPI (and, in an earlier paper, LMR). Not Cockcroft-Gault.
- Mainly European cohorts.
- Assumes that a child's growth curve shape holds when renal function is reduced.
- Few patients with mGFR below 60.

## Verification against stanpumpR
- **Growth-curve coefficients**: identical to those in R/renalFunction.R:81-83 and :103-105. **matches**.
- **Principle** (divide by the normal value for age, multiply by an adult value): **matches**. It is the same ratio form as Björk's Q(40)/Q(Age).
- **Differences from Björk's method** (the repo says "after", but does not spell these out). **partly-verified**:
  1. **Adult anchor.** stanpumpR multiplies by its assumed adult creatinine, 1.0 mg/dL (88.4 µmol/L) for men and 0.8 (70.7) for women. Björk anchors at the growth curve's value at 40 y: about 82 µmol/L for men and 66 for women by the equations (derived), or reported medians of 80 and 63.
     - A child at the normal creatinine for age therefore gets an adult-scale creatinine about 8-12% higher than Björk's, and a renal-function estimate correspondingly lower.
     - This is internally consistent with stanpumpR's adult convention, but it is not Björk's number.
  2. **Age range.** Björk adjusts ages 2-39. stanpumpR adjusts only under 18, and also applies the method under 2 y (with Boer's infant values), where Björk did not validate it.
  3. **Age term.** Björk sets age to 40 in CKD-EPI. stanpumpR keeps the actual age (relevant to gentamicin's CKD-EPI).
  4. **Equations.** Björk validated CKD-EPI only. stanpumpR also applies the adjustment to Cockcroft-Gault (an extrapolation; the repo's header notes that CG overestimates in children).
- **Citation** (R/renalFunction.R:32 and :161, "Bjork et al., Kidney Int 2021;99:940-947"): **matches**.
- **Verdict: partly-verified.** The coefficients and principle match; the anchor, age range, CKD-EPI age term and extension to Cockcroft-Gault are stanpumpR adaptations not stated as such.
