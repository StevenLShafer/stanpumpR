# Wach 2026: population PK of IR, CR and IPX203 levodopa/carbidopa in healthy volunteers

## Study
Randomised, single-centre, open-label, single-dose, three-treatment, three-period crossover phase 1 study (IPX203-B16-05) in healthy Asian male volunteers, aged 20-42 y, BMI 18.7-29.7 kg/m2; the number of participants is not stated in the main text. Sequences 1:1:1, six-day washout. Single oral doses, fasted at least 10 h, with 240 mL water, swallowed whole: IPX203 carbidopa 70 mg / levodopa 280 mg; IR (Sinemet) carbidopa 25 mg / levodopa 100 mg; CR (Sinemet CR) carbidopa 25 mg / levodopa 100 mg. Fasting continued at least 4 h after dosing. Plasma levodopa and carbidopa by validated LC-MS/MS (total concentration, plasma, stabiliser added). Non-compartmental parameters were derived in the original study; sampling times are not given in this paper. Population model by nonlinear mixed effects (software not named in the main text), exponential inter-individual variability, additive/proportional/combined residual error (PDF p.2-3).

## Model and parameters
Common systemic disposition model shared by all three formulations (Fig. 1, PDF p.3); formulation differences are confined to absorption. One and two compartment structures with first-order absorption/elimination were explored. IR and CR: single absorption depot. IPX203: two parallel depots (an IR component and an ER component with rate-limited release and an optional lag time). Figure 1 labels bioavailability terms for IR carbidopa, IR levodopa and ER levodopa (FCDIR, FLDIR, FLDER), a release rate (kERLD), absorption k01, degradation kdeg, elimination k10, transit k12/k21, and a brain compartment with DOPA synthesis (ke), plus mucoadhesive-polymer and COMT/pyridoxal phosphate labels (as read from the legend text; figure not viewed). Carbidopa was adequately described by first-order absorption for all formulations with no formulation-specific disposition.

No parameter values, no tables of estimates, and no variability estimates are printed in the main text.

Observed qualitative features: IR levodopa peaks at about 0.5 h then declines steeply; CR is delayed with a lower peak; IPX203 is biphasic (early IR peak plus sustained ER phase); carbidopa peaks about 2-3 h after all formulations (PDF p.3).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Not an opioid. No PD was measured (healthy volunteers, PK only); the authors state direct PK-PD relationships were not evaluated (PDF p.7). The concentration "range" is a literature surrogate: plasma levodopa about 750-1000 ng/mL is described as a threshold for the initiation of antiparkinsonian effects, higher concentrations being linked to peak-related adverse effects, and the range is used to colour the simulation figure (Fig. 3, green/orange/red zones, from Hauser 2018, Mao 2013, Mao and Modi 2016, Nelson 1990) (PDF p.2-4; abstract on PDF p.1, confirmed on the rendered page). Units ng/mL = mg/L (derived: 0.75-1.0 mg/L). Matrix and assay of the cited threshold (total plasma levodopa) are not stated for the source studies. No ke0, EC50, MEAC or hysteresis value; no equianalgesic ratio. The only dose-equivalence statement: simulated regimens needed about 2 times the total daily levodopa of IR to match Cmax on a milligram basis, and clinical conversion to IPX203 uses a factor of 2.8 (from the Crexont US label, PDF p.6-7); the authors say these are exposure relationships, not dosing advice.

## Covariates
None reported in the main text.

## Caveats
Single dose, healthy Asian men (young), fasting; no PD patients; PK-PD not evaluated; threshold range borrowed from literature; the authors call the findings preliminary pending Phase 2 patient analysis. Authors are consultants/employees of the sponsor (Zambon); the phase 1 study was funded by Impax (PDF p.5).
