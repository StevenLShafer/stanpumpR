# Kacirova 2016: clonazepam dependence, TDM during withdrawal (case report)

## Study
Single case report (Ostrava, Czech Republic). A 40-year-old man, 110 kg, 175 cm, BMI 35.6, nonsmoker, with
schizophrenia/anxiety history and benzodiazepine dependence; self-reported intake about 60 mg/day clonazepam and
10 mg/day alprazolam. Comedication gabapentin 600 mg tid (raised to 900 mg tid), citalopram 20 mg, sulpiride,
betaxolol. Clonazepam 4 mg tid for the first 8 days, then tapered by about 2 mg every 2-3 days; discharged on day 16
on 5 mg/day (2-1-2 mg). Eight trough serum samples; assay HPLC (Boukhabza 1990 method); individual PK estimates by
MWPharm 3.30 (Bayesian TDM software). No ethics approval (routine care).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PK/PD model. Concentration ranges quoted from other sources (page 2):
- Epilepsy: serum 20-70 ng/mL reported with therapeutic doses; laboratory alert level 80 ng/mL (refs 15, 16:
  Patsalos 2008 ILAE; Hiemke 2011 AGNP).
- Anxiolytic/hypnotic use: therapeutic reference range 4-80 ng/mL, alert level 100 ng/mL (ref 16, AGNP 2011).
- The authors' own department range: 20-80 ng/mL (Methods).
- >100 ng/mL: drowsiness, ataxia possible (ref 10); 1000 ng/mL comatose-lethal (Regenthal, ref 11); 69 ng/mL in a
  child with cyclic coma (ref 9). Alprazolam reference 5-50 ng/mL, alert 100 ng/mL.
No ke0, EC50 or MEAC.

## Covariates
None (single patient).

## Caveats
One patient with extreme tolerance; concentrations are serum troughs; PK parameters are Bayesian individual estimates
from routine software with priors not reported. The reference ranges are secondary quotations, not original data.
The PDF text layer misaligns the Table 1 and Table 2 rows.
