# van den Berg 2021: four propofol-remifentanil Ce pairs on the 90% tolerance-of-laryngoscopy isobole

## Study
Prospective, double-blind, randomised interventional trial, University Medical Center Groningen, 80 patients (20 per group; 87 randomised, 7 replaced) aged 18-90 y, ASA I-III, BMI <= 35, elective neurosurgical or maxillofacial surgery. Studied before surgery. Premedication: paracetamol 1000 mg; no sedative.
Each group received effect-site TCI (RUGLOOP II, Asena GH pumps) to one Ce pair predicted to give P_TOL = 90%; PK/PD models were Schnider (propofol) and Minto (remifentanil), with the Schnider ke0 fixed at 0.456 /min (p.3). After 11 min of equilibration, 1 min of baseline, then laryngoscopy (10 s) and 3 min of observation. End points: HR, SAP, CI, SVI, SVRI (ccNexfin), BIS and suppression ratio, motor response, and rescue (ephedrine 5 mg, atropine 0.5 mg). The drugs were not assayed; all concentrations are model-predicted effect-site values.

## Model and parameters
No new model. The Bouillon interaction model is used in the Hannivoort 2016 form (PDF p.3, printed p.817, Eqs 1-2):
P_TOL = U^gamma/(1 + U^gamma), with U = (Ce_PROP/Ce50_PROP) x (1 + Ce_REMI/Ce50_REMI).

Results (running text, PDF p.5-6, printed p.819-820): median baseline HR 71, 68, 60, 60 beats/min in groups A-D (lower with more remifentanil, p < 0.000); BIS lower in A and B than in C and D; motor responders after laryngoscopy 2, 1, 4, 8 of 20 (A-D); HR responders (> 20% rise) 5, 6, 7, 9 (25, 30, 35, 45%); SAP responders 16, 17, 8, 9; BIS arousal (> 30% rise) 25, 40, 40, 10%; rescue ephedrine for hypotension before baseline in 7, 4, 10, 5 patients. Group C had the lowest median change in HR and SAP.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- Primary end point: predicted probability of tolerance of laryngoscopy (P_TOL, a dichotomous motor/arousal response), plus haemodynamic and BIS effects. Not an analgesia end point.
- Potency parameters: Ce50 propofol 8.48 ug/mL and Ce50 remifentanil 1.16 ng/mL are effect-site, model-predicted, not assayed, and are not MEACs. No MEAC, minimum effective or analgesic concentration is reported.
- ke0: propofol 0.456 /min fixed (Schnider). No remifentanil ke0 is stated numerically (Minto model used; Minto's age-dependent ke0 is not reproduced here). No t1/2 ke0 or tPeak is reported. The authors allowed 11 min of equilibration to limit effect-site prediction error (citing Coppens 2010).
- Equipotent combinations: the four Ce pairs are said to lie on one isobole (P_TOL 90%). The potency ratio of the two drugs on this isobole is Ce50 propofol : Ce50 remifentanil = 8.48 ug/mL : 1.16 ng/mL (derived: 7.3 ug/mL propofol per ng/mL remifentanil at the Ce50 scale).
- Finding: observed effects were not equipotent: heart rate fell and BIS differed across groups, and 8 of 20 in group D moved, so the surface did not predict the observed response equally well across the isobole (authors' conclusion; the study was not powered to validate P_TOL).

## Covariates
None analysed; age 18-90 y, BMI <= 35 were eligibility ranges only. Schnider and Minto covariates enter through the models.

## Caveats
- Not powered for P_TOL accuracy; the authors call claims on P_TOL preliminary.
- Concentrations are predicted, not measured; Schnider and Minto were used as in the Bouillon experiment.
- Non-steady-state induction effects (hypotension, ephedrine) are excluded from analysis.
- Group D had many patients with BIS > 60 and some opened their eyes when the mask was removed, so the high responder count there may reflect inadequate hypnosis rather than failure of the surface.
- Tables 1-2 are scrambled in the text layer; numbers above come from the running text or are flagged.

## Verification against stanpumpR
No citation to verify. Independent comparison:
- R/modelInteraction.R:19-23 uses Bouillon 2004 Table 4 parameters: Ce50 remifentanil 1.01 ng/mL, Ce50 propofol 6.68 ug/mL, steepness 0.72 (opioid) and 6.9 (propofol), preopioid intensity 0.83. This paper uses Ce50 propofol 8.48 ug/mL, Ce50 remifentanil 1.16 ng/mL and a single gamma of 3.46 in a multiplicative U form. The two parameterisations differ, so the numbers do not transcribe from one to the other.
- Evaluated at the paper's four Ce pairs (derived by me; it could not be taken from either paper), the repo's pNR complement gives P(tolerance) of about 1.000, 1.000, 0.999 and 0.996 (groups A-D), against 0.90 by the paper's parameters and an observed tolerance of 90, 95, 80 and 60% (2, 1, 4, 8 of 20 moved). I did not trace whether the repo's steep propofol exponent (6.9) is the Bouillon Table 4 value; recorded as an open question, not a verdict.
- Units: the repo takes propofol in ug/mL and remifentanil in ng/mL (R/modelInteraction.R:4-6), matching this paper.
- The repo's remifentanil MEAC (1 ng/mL, R/drugs_remifentanil.R:117) is unrelated to Ce50 remifentanil 1.16 ng/mL here, which is a potency scaling in an interaction surface.
- Propofol tPeak 1.6 min in the repo (R/drugs_propofol.R:276) goes with Schnider's ke0; this paper's 0.456 /min is the Schnider value (consistent; tPeak not recomputed).
