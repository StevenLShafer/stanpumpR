# Britt 2016: vancomycin AUC24/MBC and 30-day mortality in MRSA bacteraemia

## Study
Retrospective single-centre cohort, University of Kansas Hospital, September 2012 to June 2014 (PDF p2, printed 3071).
Adults (18 or older) with MRSA bacteraemia treated with intravenous vancomycin; excluded: haemodialysis (n = 34),
vancomycin under 48 h (n = 13), polymicrobial bacteraemia. 100 identified, **53 analysed**. Primary outcome 30-day
all-cause mortality: 11/53 (20.8%). Mean age 58.5 ± 15.9 (survivors) and 70.6 ± 16.6 (deaths) years (Table 1, p3);
weight not reported; vancomycin doses not reported. Mean duration of therapy 8.73 days (SD 5.23).

Microbiology (p2): first blood isolate per patient, stored at -70 °C. MIC by CLSI broth microdilution (0.125 to
64 µg/mL, inoculum about 1 × 10^6 CFU/mL) and by Etest. MBC by CLSI microdilution (M26-A) with carry-over control
(aliquots streaked and cross-streaked); MBC = lowest concentration with at least 99.9% kill after 24 h at 35 °C.
Tolerance = MBC/MIC of 32 or more.

Pharmacokinetics (p2): individual AUCs by MAP-Bayesian estimation in ADAPT 5, prior = mean vector and full covariance
of a published two-compartment vancomycin model (refs 14-15, Pryka 1989 and Rodvold 1989). AUC24 = AUC0-96 minus
AUC0-72 (i.e. AUC over 72-96 h), taken as the steady-state 24-h exposure. Concentrations are serum vancomycin as
monitored clinically (total drug; the assay is not described). Statistics: CART for breakpoints, Poisson regression,
Kaplan-Meier, Cox regression (SAS 9.2).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: 30-day all-cause mortality. Not an opioid paper; no ke0, MEAC or potency ratio.
- CART breakpoints (p2): **AUC24/MBC 176**; AUC24/MIC(BMD) 334; none found for AUC24/MIC(Etest).
- Univariable mortality (p3, printed 3072): AUC24/MBC 176 or more 9.4% (3/32) vs 38.1% (8/21), P = 0.017;
  AUC24/MIC(BMD) 334 or more 7.7% (2/26) vs 33.3% (9/27), P = 0.021; AUC24/MIC(BMD) 400 or more 14.3% (6/42) vs
  45.5% (5/11), P = 0.037.
- Table 4 (p4, printed 3073), adjusted risk ratios for 30-day mortality: AUC24/MBC under 176, 3.68 (1.17-11.56),
  P = 0.025 (with age 85+, malignancy, PBS 4+, cirrhosis); AUC24/MIC(BMD) under 334, 5.12 (0.85-30.67), P = 0.074;
  AUC/MIC(BMD) under 400, 1.95 (0.92-4.15), P = 0.084.
- Kaplan-Meier: AUC24/MBC 176 or more, log-rank P = 0.011. Cox: aHR 7.32 (1.66-32.29), P = 0.009, adjusted for
  APACHE II (aHR 1.09) and PBS 4+ (aHR 6.80) (p3).
- Prior vancomycin exposure within 30 days was associated with a rising MIC (P = 0.033) and MBC (P = 0.001).

All AUCs are total serum drug; the MIC and MBC are broth (protein-free) values, so the ratios mix total exposure
with free-drug potency, as do the guideline AUC/MIC targets.

## Covariates
None modelled for PK. Confounders in the outcome models: age 85+, malignancy, Pitt bacteraemia score 4+, cirrhosis,
MRSA pneumonia, APACHE II.

## Caveats
- Retrospective, 53 patients, 11 deaths; authors note possible type II error for AUC/MIC.
- Haemodialysis patients excluded; doses, weights and sampling times not reported, so the AUCs cannot be checked.
- AUC24 is the 72-96 h window, not a true steady state for every patient.
- The Cox result is printed as an aHR of 7.32 for the 176-or-more group "and improved survival"; a hazard ratio
  above 1 for improved survival suggests the ratio is for the under-176 group (reference category not stated).
  Not used by stanpumpR.
- Cause of death unknown; MBC tolerance rates are institution-specific (authors).

## Verification against stanpumpR
- **antibiotics.md:60** says this is the one clinical study to relate outcome to AUC over the MBC. The paper
  relates 30-day mortality to AUC24/MBC and its authors call it the first to set such a breakpoint (p3). That the
  library calls it "the one" study is the library's claim, and this paper cannot confirm it. **matches**.
  The paper is fully readable. Its MBC results (MBC50 1, MBC90 64 µg/mL in 53 MRSA bacteraemia isolates) are now
  available for the vancomycin band. They are not used.
- Independent check of the vancomycin MBC row (`R/antibioticMbc.R:362-371`: MIC50 1, MIC90 1, MBC50 2, MBC90 8,
  Honda 2011). The MICs agree (1/1). Britt's MBC50 is one dilution lower (1 vs 2). Its MBC90 of 64 is far higher
  because 24.5% of isolates were tolerant. The library leaves tolerant isolates out of the band
  (`R/antibioticMbc.R:372-374`, "excluded"). Britt's 24.5% fits the help text's "up to a quarter of MRSA"
  (`antibiotics.md:33`) and the row note's "4 to 27%" (`R/antibioticMbc.R:365-366`). No discrepancy.
- The breakpoint of 176 uses total-drug AUC over a broth MBC. The library draws its MBC band in free drug
  (`antibiotics.md:22`), so it should not put 176 on the free-drug curve. The library does not use 176, so nothing
  needs fixing.
- PK: the vancomycin model (`R/drugs_vancomycin.R`) cannot be compared because the paper gives no doses, weights
  or parameters. The cohort mean AUC24 of 681 mg·h/L is only a description of this cohort.
