# Singhvi 1988: disposition of fosinopril sodium and fosinoprilat (SQ 27,519) in healthy men

## Study
- Open three-way crossover, 1-week washouts. Nine healthy male Caucasians, 20-34 y (mean 27), 64.3-93.2 kg
  (mean 77.0), 168-184 cm (mean 178) (Methods, PDF p2, printed 10).
- Doses: fosinoprilat (SQ 27,519) 7.5 mg 14C IV over 3 min, stated equimolar to 10 mg fosinopril sodium;
  fosinopril sodium 10 mg 14C orally as a solution and as a dry-filled capsule. About 50 uCi per dose.
- Plasma sampled to 48 h; urine and faeces for 4 days. Assay: radioactivity with thin-layer
  radiochromatography and HPLC to separate prodrug, diacid and metabolites; detection limit about 1 ng/mL
  (Discussion, PDF p7). Concentrations expressed as SQ 27,519 equivalents.
- Analysis: non-compartmental (Lagrange AUC, extrapolated from the 16 h concentration) and a biexponential
  fit of the IV data in NONLIN, weighted 1/C^2. Dispersion is mean +/- SEM, n = 9.

## Model and parameters
Biexponential (two-compartment) disposition after IV fosinoprilat; values are means of individual analyses.

Absorption and bioavailability (abstract; Results, PDF p5):
- Oral absorption of fosinopril (from total-radioactivity AUC): 32% solution, 36% capsule; from urinary
  excretion 29% and 36%.
- Absolute bioavailability of SQ 27,519 from plasma AUC: 25% solution, 29% capsule; from urine 21% and 25%.
  Capsule vs solution not significantly different (paired t, P > 0.05).
- Plasma radioactivity after oral dosing: 70-80% SQ 27,519 at all times, the rest mainly the acyl
  glucuronide; abstract gives about 75% diacid, glucuronide 15-20%, p-hydroxy analogue about 5%. Intact
  prodrug 11% and 3% of plasma radioactivity at 20 and 40 min, under 1% from 1-24 h.
- Excretion of the IV dose over 96 h: 44% urine, 46% faeces (biliary). After oral dosing: urine 13%
  (solution) and 16% (capsule), faeces 73% and 78%.
- Protein binding of radioactivity: 95% at 1 and 3 h after IV; 89% (1 h) and 92% (3 h) after oral.
  Association with blood cells under 6%.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No ACE inhibition, blood pressure or other effect was measured; no ke0, EC50 or therapeutic range.
Safety only: one subject's SGPT rose from 57 to 85 IU/L 4 days after the IV dose and normalised.

## Covariates
None examined (nine young healthy men).

## Caveats
- n = 9, young healthy men only; SEM, not SD.
- Sampling and assay sensitivity (about 1 ng/mL) did not capture the long terminal phase; the 3.7-4.3 h
  half-lives are explicitly underestimates, and AUC extrapolation from 16 h may understate AUC(0-inf).
- Radiolabel study: Cmax and AUC(0,24) rows for "total radioactivity" are not the diacid; only the
  SQ 27,519 rows are.
- Table 2's "3.7 +/- 0.02" for t1/2 lambda2 is probably a misprint of 0.2 (the model-independent row is
  3.7 +/- 0.2); immaterial to the repo.
- No dose proportionality data (single 10 mg oral dose).
