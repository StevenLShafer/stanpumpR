# Yu 2026: lacosamide population PK in Chinese adults with epilepsy

## Study
Retrospective TDM study, Second Affiliated Hospital of Zhejiang University, April 2022 - February 2024.
180 adult inpatients with epilepsy (> 18 y), 294 plasma concentrations, **all troughs** (Results).
Table 1: 79 male / 101 female (printed 43.9% / 56.6%; 101/180 is 56.1%), age median 32.9 y (IQR
24.9-51.2, range 18-82.8), weight 65 kg (55.3-75.0, range 36-130), daily dose 100 mg/d (100-150, range
50-225), SCr 66.0 umol/L (57.1-77.8), Cockcroft-Gault CrCl 115 mL/min (95.5-137, range 25.2-308).
Co-medication: levetiracetam 44.4%, oxcarbazepine 28.3%, valproate 26.7%, lamotrigine 23.3%,
carbamazepine 15.6%, others. Assay not described in the text layer. NONMEM 7.5.0, FOCE-I; bootstrap
(n = 1000), NPC, VPC; Monte Carlo AUC simulations (1000 virtual patients per scenario).

## Model and parameters
One compartment, first-order absorption and elimination. Because only troughs were available, V fixed at
0.6 L/kg and ka fixed at 6.47 /h from the literature (refs 7, 15). Final model (Table 2; equation 1):

CL/F = 1.86 x 1.48^CBZ x 0.875^SEX x (CLCR/119)^0.311, SEX = 1 for women, CLCR Cockcroft-Gault (mL/min).

Condition number 37.938. The text says levetiracetam with CBZ was a preliminary covariate; the final
model has CBZ only. Note the centring value 119 differs from the cohort median CrCl (115).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None measured. No exposure-response, no therapeutic range derived (the authors note a suggested trough
range lacks clinical evidence, ref 25). Simulation: with CBZ, 40-60% more dose needed for the same
steady-state AUC (Fig 2).

## Covariates
CBZ co-medication (x1.48), female sex (x0.875), Cockcroft-Gault CLCR power 0.311 on CL/F. Age, weight,
other labs and other ASMs screened, not retained. No covariates on V (fixed per kg).

## Caveats
Trough-only data: V and ka not estimable (fixed), no IIV on V; absorption profile not informed by data.
Retrospective; no external validation; CBZ dose not collected; seizure status not collected. Small
inconsistencies: sex percentage, the Table 2 CI for CL/F. Single Chinese centre.
