# Jospin 2007: DFA of EEG as a depth-of-anesthesia measure

## Study
- Signal-processing paper. Data reused from an earlier Ghent study (Vereecke 2005, ref 14): 17 women, 18-60 y, ASA I, ambulatory gynecological surgery, weight 70-130% of ideal (PDF p1-2).
- Propofol infusion fixed at 300 mL/h (concentration not stated in the text layer) by RUGLOOP, until burst-suppression ratio >= 80% or MAP < 50 mmHg. Recording 10-15 min, before surgery.
- Effect-site propofol from a three-compartment PK-PD model plus effect site (refs 16-17, Schnider 1998/1999), with ke0 set to give a time to peak effect of 1.6 min after bolus (p1).
- EEG 900 Hz, 16 bit (A-Line); BIS A-2000 (15 s smoothing); AAI (AEP index). States from AAI: awake > 60, sedated 30-60, anesthetized < 30; deep = burst suppression.
- 712 ten-second segments: 144 awake, 164 sedated, 404 anesthetized (p3).

## Model and parameters
No PK model fitted. DFA scaling-exponent indexes (slopes on short time scales, 6.7 ms-158 ms) rise with depth; Mann-Whitney p-values and leave-one-out discriminant percentages (best index: 81.6% awake, 75.5% sedated, 78.5% sedated, 90.8% anesthetized, p4) are reported; correlations with AAI, BIS and effect-site propofol in Table III (not read).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- Only PD-relevant number: effect-site propofol was computed with tPeak 1.6 min (end point: the model's effect site, calibrated to bolus time to peak; the paper gives no ke0 value, no EC50, no hysteresis analysis).
- Not an opioid paper: no MEAC, ke0 or potency ratio.

## Covariates
None.

## Caveats
Abstract-level evaluation only; DFA indexes were correlated with, not calibrated against, drug concentration. Women only, n = 17, mostly anesthetized segments.
