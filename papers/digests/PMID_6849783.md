# Rubin 1983: IV labetalol disposition and BP response in pregnancy

## Study
Ten women aged 18-37 y with hypertension arising in the last trimester (gestation 33-38 weeks) about to start
labetalol; seven of them restudied 3-4 months postpartum (normotensive, off treatment); ten non-pregnant normotensive
women aged 18-35 y as controls (PDF p1-2). Labetalol 50 mg IV over 2 min. Venous samples 0-8 h (0, 5, 10, 15, 20,
30, 40 min, 1-8 h). HPLC with fluorescence (sensitivity 10 ng/mL, CV 5%). BP and HR supine and standing at 30-min
intervals to 3 h, then hourly to 8 h. Biexponential fit (Marquardt, reciprocal weighting); CL = dose/AUC
(model-independent); Vss from the coefficients, two-compartment model. Effect compartment after Sheiner 1979 with a
linear E = m Ce + i (PDF p2-3). Results as median (range), Wilcoxon tests.

No group differences in CL, Vss or t1/2, absolute or per kg. In the seven paired women, CL 31.3 (21.9-38.8) during and
34.8 (27.6-51.4) mL/min/kg after pregnancy, NS (PDF p3). Weights are not reported. The abstract calls the volumes
"volumes of distribution"; Table 1 shows they are Vss.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- Effect compartment fitted in the pregnant group only (BP change too small postpartum and in controls). keq (ke0) is
  defined but not reported; no t1/2ke0 or tPeak.
- Table 2 (PDF p4, rendered), per subject m (mmHg per ng/mL), i and r2. Supine systolic m in six women: -0.33, -0.34,
  -0.14, -0.42, -0.91, -0.49 (median -0.38, derived; abstract 0.38, 0.14-0.91). Supine diastolic m: -0.46, -0.26,
  -1.14, -0.21, -0.99, -0.78: median -0.62 (derived), mean -0.64. The abstract gives diastolic 0.36 (0.21-1.14); the
  Discussion gives mean values 0.45 (supine SBP), 0.78 (standing SBP), 0.64 (supine DBP), 0.43 (standing DBP) (PDF p5).
  The abstract's 0.36 matches neither the median nor the mean of Table 2's diastolic slopes; unresolved.
- Pregnant women: prompt fall in BP sustained over 8 h (Fig. 2, PDF p3).

## Covariates
Pregnancy (group comparison): no effect on CL, Vss or t1/2 of IV labetalol.

## Caveats
Small groups; postpartum study at 3-4 months; controls are labelled "age-matched" in Table 1, but no weights are given
for any group; IV only, so it says nothing of first-pass changes. One subject (MM) has supine systolic r2 0.00.
