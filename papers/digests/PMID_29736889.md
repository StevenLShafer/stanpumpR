# Kuchimanchi 2018: evolocumab population PK and exposure-response

## Study
Pooled analysis of 11 clinical studies (phase 1a/1b, four phase 2, five phase 3; Table 1, PDF p. 3). 5474 patients in the pooled data; 3414 who received evolocumab in the final popPK analysis (Table 2); 1312-1314 from the four phase 2 studies in the exposure-response analysis (text gives both numbers). 16,179 evolocumab concentrations. Doses 7-420 mg, IV (21 and 420 mg, phase 1a) and SC (7-420 mg; weekly, Q2W, monthly regimens). Healthy volunteers and hypercholesterolemia patients (statin combination, statin-intolerant, monotherapy, HeFH). Final dataset (Table 2, text layer, scrambled; SDs not read): age range 18-80 years; weight mean 84.2 kg, range 41-175 kg; 50% female (1706 of 3414). Assay: validated ELISA for UNBOUND evolocumab in serum, LLOQ 0.8 ug/mL, ULOQ 10 ug/mL (PDF p. 2). About 19% of the data were below LLOQ, handled by M3. Software: NONMEM 7.2, SAEM and IMP for popPK, FOCEI then IMP for exposure-response.

## Model and parameters
One compartment, first-order SC absorption (ka), SC bioavailability F, parallel linear clearance CL and Michaelis-Menten elimination (Vmax, km) as the approximation of target-mediated drug disposition (Fig. 1). A quasi-steady-state TMDD fit was tried and abandoned as over-parameterized. ka and F fixed from phase 1a; km fixed after phase 1-2 estimation; in the phase 3 update Vmax and km were fixed to the phase 1-2 values. BSV log-normal, full block except ka and km. Residual error additive plus proportional.

Covariate form (Methods, PDF p. 4): continuous, P = TVP x (cov/median)^theta x exp(eta); categorical, P = TVP x theta^indicator x exp(eta). Reference patient: 84 kg male, no other lipid-lowering drug, baseline PCSK9 5.9 nM (425 ng/mL).

Derived values stated in the Discussion (PDF p. 13): km about 4 ug/mL (Discussion says linear below 0.4 ug/mL, 10-fold under km); elimination half-life about 1.9 days when the nonlinear pathway is unsaturated (from Vmax/km), about 34 days when it is saturated (linear pathway); effective half-life 11.4 days (140 mg Q2W) and 16.8 days (420 mg QM); Vmax equal to 7.7 mg/day; nonlinear share of a single dose about 77% (140 mg) and 51% (420 mg). Observed unbound Cmax 18.6 ug/mL (140 mg) and 59 ug/mL (420 mg) are cited from reference [34], not from this analysis.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Emax model on LDL-C: Eff = Emax x AUC(wk8-12) / (EC50 x REG^i + AUC(wk8-12)), additive form Y = BASL + Eff selected (Table 4). Response is the mean of weeks 10 and 12 LDL-C. Maximal predicted fall 99.7 mg/dL, from a 134 mg/dL baseline, or 66%. 140 mg Q2W and 420 mg QM give about 80% of the maximal response. EC50 51.5 (ug/mL)*day for Q2W, 118.5 for QM. No ke0, hysteresis or effect-site model (this is an AUC-based, not a time-course, exposure-response; the longitudinal indirect-response model is cited as published elsewhere, ref. 12). No MEAC, no therapeutic concentration range, no potency ratio. Not an opioid.

## Covariates
PK: body weight on CL, V, Vmax; female sex on V; statin and statin+ezetimibe on Vmax; baseline PCSK9 on Vmax. Renal function (CrCl, MDRD), age, race, HeFH, diabetes were tested or considered and are not in the final model. Exposure-response: statin (on baseline LDL-C and Emax), ezetimibe, HeFH (on baseline LDL-C; exponents 0.797, 0.768, 1.28 for baseline, statin exponent 0.937 on Emax). Only body weight at 40 and 140 kg moved AUC(wk8-12) markedly (Fig. 7); LDL-C response stayed within 74-126% of the reference patient (Abstract, Fig. 8).

## Caveats
Authors: a one-compartment model may bias results (IV data sparse); high concentrations, especially in the monthly regimen, were under-predicted, but AUC(wk8-12) and the week-12 trough were well predicted. Fixed ka, F, km, and (phase 3) Vmax. Weight effects on CL and Vmax have RSE 30-33%. Unbound drug assayed, serum. Phase 3 data not used to update the exposure-response model. Amgen authors.
