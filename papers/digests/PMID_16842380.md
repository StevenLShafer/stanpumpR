# Joerger 2006: determinants of high-dose methotrexate and 7-OH-methotrexate elimination

## Study
Population PK and covariate analysis of high-dose IV methotrexate (MTX) and its metabolite 7-hydroxy-MTX (7-OH-MTX) in 76 adults with solid tumours (and a few lymphoma/leukaemia) treated at the Netherlands Cancer Institute: 304 courses. 21 patients (34 courses) had intensive sampling (end of infusion, +3, +6, +8 h) and 55 patients (270 courses) had routine 24 h and 48 h samples. Dose 300 mg/m2 to 12 g/m2 over 1-24 h; 61 patients (80%) received 1000-5000 mg/m2, and the infusion lasted 1-6 h in 61 patients. Tumour types: 29 mesothelioma, 20 gastro-oesophageal, 12 NHL, 10 head and neck, 2 choriocarcinoma, 1 each ALL, trophoblastic tumour, osteosarcoma (PDF p5). Entry required serum creatinine < 120 umol/L, bilirubin < 25 umol/L, WHO status 0-2. Urine alkalinised before MTX; leucovorin rescue from 24 h.
Plasma MTX and 7-OH-MTX by reversed-phase HPLC; LLOQ 0.04 umol/L; CV <= 7%. NONMEM V, FO method, log-transformed concentrations, ADVAN5. Proportional IIV and interoccasion variability.
Table 1 (PDF p4, rendered; medians, range): age 51.1 y (17.1-77.0); 62 male / 14 female; BSA 1.94 m2 (1.56-2.45); albumin 40.0 g/L (16-71); bilirubin 6.0 umol/L (2-60); serum creatinine 77.0 umol/L (32-433); Cockcroft-Gault CLcr 87.5 mL/min (40-140), truncated at 140. Weight is not tabulated.

## Model and parameters
MTX: linear three-compartment model, first-order elimination from the central compartment. 7-OH-MTX: linear two-compartment, elimination from its central compartment. Fraction of MTX converted to 7-OH-MTX fixed at 10% (literature; 2%-50% gave a worse fit). Five-compartment model overall (Figure 2, PDF p5). ## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD model and no ke0. Not an opioid. Concentration thresholds cited from the literature in the Introduction (PDF p2): leucovorin rescue intensified when plasma MTX > 0.1 umol/L at 48 h or any value > 10 umol/L; in this study rescue was stopped at <= 0.04 umol/L at 48 h, continued 24 h more for 0.04-0.1, intensified at >= 0.1 umol/L. MTX >= 0.1 umol/L at 48 h in 78.1% of patients on benzimidazoles, 71.4% after NSAIDs, 33.5% with neither (PDF p8). All concentrations are total plasma by HPLC.
Table 3 (PDF p8, text layer; geometric mean umol/L, 95% CI): with benzimidazoles (n=13) MTX 24 h 2.01 (1.44-2.81), 48 h 0.25 (0.17-0.35); without (n=63) 0.66 (0.52-0.84), 0.12 (0.09-0.15); ratios 3.03 and 2.10. 7-OH-MTX 24 h 4.47 vs 2.52, 48 h 1.11 vs 0.72. Prior NSAIDs (n=6) MTX 24 h 0.98 vs 0.77 (P 0.26), 48 h 0.17 vs 0.13 (P 0.21).

## Covariates
Retained: creatinine clearance (Cockcroft-Gault, truncated at 140 mL/min), concurrent benzimidazoles (10 omeprazole, 3 lansoprazole), prior NSAIDs (6 patients; diclofenac 5, ibuprofen 1; stopped on the day of MTX), each on both CL_MTX and CL_7OH. Together they lowered the objective function by 174.5, IIV in CL_MTX from 35.2% to 19.6%. Paper's stated effects: benzimidazoles -27% CL_MTX, -39% CL_7OH; NSAIDs -16% and -38% (see open question on the 7-OH figures). Not significant: age, sex, weight, BSA, bilirubin, AST, ALT, ALP, third-space fluid (15 patients), corticosteroids, vinca alkaloids, 5-FU, L-asparaginase. Cisplatin (2 patients) and ifosfamide (1) could not be tested (too few).

## Caveats
Only 13 patients on benzimidazoles and 6 on NSAIDs; the NSAID group had 6 with unknown reasons for stopping. Doses and infusion durations were heterogeneous; most patients had only 24 h and 48 h samples, so the distribution volumes rest on 21 patients. FO estimation. Males 62/14 females. CLcr was truncated at 140. Effects of the two PPIs or the two NSAIDs were not separable. Metabolite fraction fixed, not estimated. Two outliers with very low clearance (one fatal renal failure) are in the full data set; covariate results held with and without them.

## Verification against stanpumpR
Not applicable to a citation. For comparison with the library's own models: methotrexateIbarra (R/drugs_methotrexateIbarra.R:30-31) uses CL = 5.92 L/h x (WT/70)^0.75 x (SCRpop/SCR)^0.694 x (ALB/4)^0.594, V1 14.4 L, V2 3.55 L, V3 2.09 L (total 20.0 L). Joerger's typical CL_MTX 8.85 L/h (at CLcr 87 mL/min, no comedication) is 1.5 times Ibarra's 5.92 L/h at the reference patient, and the Joerger total volume of distribution (23.0 + 185 + 5.34 = 213 L, derived) is about ten times Ibarra's. The populations differ (Joerger: 76 adults, high-dose, median 51 y, 62 male; Ibarra: 243 adults, 18-88 y), the Joerger model has no weight or albumin covariate, and the two structures differ (the large Joerger periph-1 volume of 185 L has an RSE of 25.5%), so the comparison is indicative only. Neither model nor the Taylor pediatric model uses PPI or NSAID covariates; this paper is the only archived quantitative source for those interactions (CL_MTX -2.45 and -1.46 L/h on 8.85). No discrepancy against the code can be stated, because the code does not cite this paper.
