# Shah 2021: remifentanil TCI for awake fibreoptic intubation (letter)

## Study
Retrospective service evaluation, one head-and-neck anaesthetist, Royal Marsden Hospital, awake tracheal intubation by flexible bronchoscopy (ATI:FB), Sept 2010 to Nov 2019. n = 188 charts (PDF p.1). Technique: incremental remifentanil TCI until the patient is relaxed and obeys commands, minimal topical local anaesthetic via the bronchoscope, 1 L/min oxygen insufflation. Only starting dose, highest dose, any midazolam and complications were recorded; no assay, no PK or PD modelling, no model named (guideline cites the Minto model for its Ces advice).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point is clinical (relaxed, comfortable, obeying commands), not a measured concentration-response. Table 1 (PDF p.1, printed p.284), remifentanil target "Cpt" in ng/mL, median (IQR [range]):

| Group | n (%) | Starting target | Highest target |
|---|---|---|---|
| Remifentanil TCI alone | 137 (73%) | 5 (5-5 [2-7]) | 5 (5-7 [3-9]) |
| Midazolam bolus before TCI | 47 (25%) | 3 (3-4 [2-7]) | 5 (4-6 [2.5-9]) |
| Midazolam bolus after TCI start | 4 (2%) | 4 (3-5 [3-5]) | 5 (4.75-6 [3-9]) |

- 125 (91%) of the remifentanil-alone group had Cpt of 5 ng/mL or above at intubation. Midazolam before TCI was 0.5-2.0 mg.
- No sedation complications, no naloxone given.
- The DAS guideline quoted in the letter recommends Ces 1-3 ng/mL (Minto model); the authors say their doses are higher and safe.
- Audit: MEAC/analgesic concentration: none reported. ke0 / t½ke0 / tPeak / hysteresis: none. Potency or equianalgesic ratio: none.

## Covariates
None analysed (age, weight, sex, BMI not reported).

## Caveats
Retrospective, single operator, charts only; targets are pump settings, not measured concentrations; sampling of spontaneous ventilation and SpO2 not reported; whether the target was plasma or effect-site is not clear (see open question).
