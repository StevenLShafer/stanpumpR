# Yang 2023: valsartan population PK in healthy Chinese subjects (from sacubitril/valsartan)

## Study
Retrospective population analysis of a randomized, open-label, two-preparation, four-period crossover
bioequivalence study of sacubitril/valsartan tablets (sacubitril 97 mg / valsartan 103 mg) at Lanzhou University
Second Hospital (p.2). Only the first single oral dose of the reference product (Entresto) was used. 78 healthy
Chinese adults, 52 M / 26 F, 38 fasted / 40 fed (high-fat meal); median age 24 y (19-40), height 167 cm
(145.5-184), weight 60 kg (45-81), BMI 21.3 (18.6-26), creatinine 72 µmol/L (43-98), ALT 14 U/L (6-58),
AST 18 U/L (7-48) (Table 1, PDF p.4, rendered). Sampling to 48 h (20 times fasted, 22 fed); 7-day washout.
1539 plasma concentrations (p.4). Assay HPLC-MS/MS, linear 0.01-10 µg/mL, LLOQ printed as 5.000 ng/mL (the linear
range starts at 10 ng/mL; as printed); BLQ set to 0 (p.2-3). Phoenix NLME 8.1, FOCE-ELS; additive residual error;
forward addition (dOFV > 3.84) / backward elimination (> 6.63). Validation by GOF, 1000-sample bootstrap and VPC.
No dose-normalisation to conventional valsartan is discussed (Entresto's valsartan is more bioavailable than
Diovan's; not stated in the paper).

## Model and parameters
Two compartments, first-order absorption with lag, first-order elimination (Table 2, p.5: -2LL 2884.9 one-cmt no
lag; 2008.4 one-cmt + lag; 2036.2 two-cmt no lag; 2003.3 two-cmt + lag, selected). The text (p.4) says the
one-compartment model fitted similarly; two compartments were kept by precedent and a "biphasic decline".
Final estimates, Table 4 (p.5, rendered); Table 3 gives the same typical values with IIV variances:

**Supplement (sup0001.docx, read 2026-10-27).** It holds goodness-of-fit figures and the covariate search, and no
parameter table, equations or code.
- Table S1 (forward addition, criterion delta -2LL > 3.84): base 2003.3; food on Ka chosen first (1988.9), then ALT
  (1971.4), then AST (1967.9), then CRE (1957.1). Single-step AST on CL/F alone raised -2LL to 2004.5. Rows 29-33
  label the carried covariates "CL/F-BMI" where the chosen set is ALT (as printed); step numbers 23 repeat.
- Table S2 (backward elimination, criterion > 6.63): removing food, AST, ALT or CRE from the final model gives 1988.4,
  1975.6, 1977.6 and 1967.9, i.e. increases of 31.3, 18.5, 20.5 and 10.8 against 1957.1 (derived). No BMI, age, sex or
  weight term survives, consistent with Table 3.
- Figure S2 (base two-compartment model with lag, rendered): PRED takes discrete values up to about 3.3 ug/mL;
  Figure S3 (final model): PRED reaches about 5.2 ug/mL, observations about 12.3 ug/mL (read from the figures).
- Derived bound: with the printed V/F 50.79 L and no covariate on V/F, no PRED for 103 mg can exceed
  103/50.79 = 2.03 mg/L, whatever the clearance (V2/F 0.51 L and absorption only lower it). The final model's PREDs
  to about 5.2 ug/mL are above that bound. The base model's typical PRED peak, about 3.3 ug/mL, cannot be compared with
  it, because the base model's parameters are not printed.

Bootstrap means (Table 4): Ka 0.19, V/F 52.2, V2/F 0.52, CL/F 5.02, CL2/F 4.03 (CI 3.26-4.48, much narrower
than the model CI 0.29-7.60), Tlag 0.31, AST effect 0.21 (against 0.36 estimated). Residual error magnitude not
reported. Table 3's individual equations print V2/F_ind = V/F x exp(eta) and CL2/F_ind = CL/F x exp(eta), i.e.
built on the central parameters (as printed; probably a typesetting slip, not resolvable from the text).

Derived checks (mine): with V2/F 0.51 L the model is effectively one-compartment; k = 5.07/50.79 = 0.0998/h,
t1/2 about 6.9 h; fed Ka 0.19 gives tmax = ln(0.19/0.0998)/(0.19-0.0998) + 0.32 = 7.46 h and Cmax for 103 mg
about 0.99 mg/L; fasting Ka 0.31/h gives tmax about 5.7 h; AUC = 103/5.07 = 20.3 mg.h/L (derived: one-compartment
approximation). Figure 1 VPC (p.6, rendered; fed and fasted pooled): observed and predicted median peak about
2.8-3.0 µg/mL at about 1.25-2.5 h, 95th percentile about 6.5 µg/mL; the 5th percentile goes negative (additive error).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No blood pressure or other PD measured or modelled.

## Covariates
Food on Ka (fasting 1.63-fold the fed value); CRE, AST and ALT on CL/F by power models normalised to 70.5 µmol/L,
18.0 U/L and 17.2 U/L (Table 3). Age, sex, BMI and weight were not retained (p.4, p.6). The Methods (p.3)
describe allometric BMI scaling of clearances (exponent 0.75, BMI/21.7) and volumes (1.0) that does not appear in
the final model (Table 3). The Discussion notes that the label says renal impairment does not change exposure,
in tension with the retained creatinine effect.

## Caveats
Healthy young Chinese adults, single dose, a combination product (valsartan in Entresto), narrow covariate ranges.
Additive-only residual error with BLQ set to 0. CL2/F has a 26-fold CI (0.29-7.60) and a bootstrap CI that
excludes most of it. Methods describe a BMI model the final model lacks; Table 3 individual equations look
mis-typed. Printed typical values do not reproduce the VPC peak (see derived checks). The text states (p.4) that
"AST, AST, and CRE" affected CL/F (typo for AST, ALT).
