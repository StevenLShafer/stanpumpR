# Yeung 1993: IV and oral chlorpromazine and metabolite kinetics in healthy men (non-compartmental)

## Study
- 11 healthy young men (12 screened), 20-30 y, 60.0-89.1 kg.
- Crossover, 2-week washouts: 10 mg IV bolus (Largactil injectable) and single oral doses of 25, 50, 100 mg
  (Largactil liquid); only the 25 and 50 mg oral doses randomised. Fasted.
- Samples 0-48 h (0.25 ... 48 h). Plasma CPZ, CPZ sulfoxide, CPZ N-oxide, free and conjugated 7-hydroxy-CPZ by
  extraction radioimmunoassays (validated against HPLC).
- Non-compartmental / statistical-moment analysis; ANOVA (PC-SAS). No compartmental or population model.

Oral CPZ (Tables 4-7): tmax 2.0, 1.7, 1.4 h; Cmax 4.31, 11.9, 37.9 ng/mL; AUC 27.8, 81.8, 247 ng.h/mL; t1/2(k)
5.48, 9.52, 11.05 h for 25, 50, 100 mg. Dose-AUC relation non-linear (parabolic in 9/11). Between-subject CV of
apparent oral clearance 43.2%, 62.2%, 81.2% by dose. Triphasic decline in some subjects after IV, biphasic in
others. Conjugated 7-OH-CPZ AUC about twice the parent's.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None measured (marked sedation after 10 mg IV noted, not quantified). Not an opioid; no MEAC, ke0 or potency
ratio.

## Covariates
None. Weight correction did not reduce variability in V or Vss.

## Caveats
n = 11 healthy men; single small IV dose; RIA assays (CPZNO stability CV 28%); dose-dependent, highly variable
oral F (first-pass); no compartmental parameters (no V1, Q, rate constants); OCR text.
