# Heise 1998: premixed lispro / NPL insulin time-action profiles

## Study
Open, single-centre, randomized five-way crossover (Latin square), n = 30 healthy volunteers (12 women, 18 men), age 27 +/- 2 y, BMI 23.0 +/- 2.3 kg/m2 (PDF p.1; the abstract text layer reads 11 +/- 1, see open question). Each received 0.3 U/kg SC (mean 21.3 +/- 3.3 U), U100, in a paraumbilical skinfold, of: insulin lispro (IL), high mixture 75/25 lispro/NPL (HM), mid 50/50 (MM), low 25/75 (LM), or NPL alone. Euglycaemic clamp (Biostator, 5.0 mmol/L), GIR followed 22 h (10 h for lispro). Serum insulin by RIA with lispro as the standard; C-peptide measured. GIR fitted with an exponential after baseline subtraction; PK summary by polynomial fit with graphical estimates and trapezoidal AUC. Study funded by Eli Lilly.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: GIR in the euglycaemic clamp (Table 1). No ke0, EC50 or hysteresis analysis. Time to peak GIR (tmax) is listed above; early t50% is the time to half-maximal GIR on the rise, late t50% on the decline. GIRmax rose less than proportionally to the lispro fraction; the overall AUC0-1320 of GIR (3.7, 3.8, 4.0, 3.8 g/kg for HM, MM, LM, NPL; P = 0.62) did not differ. Lispro AUC to 1320 min is not available (only to 600 min). The paper does not state a potency ratio.

## Covariates
Sex: stated not to differ (no data shown). Dose is per kg.

## Caveats
Healthy volunteers, fasted, supine, single dose. Serum insulin tmax for NPL is very variable (200 +/- 162 min). Lispro serum AUC beyond 360 min is not comparable (short study). PK summaries come from polynomial fits, not a model. The Table 1 text layer is scrambled; numbers above were taken from the rendered page.
