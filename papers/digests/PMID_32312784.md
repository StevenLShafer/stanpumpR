# Koshimichi 2020: baloxavir acid population PK, high-risk influenza patients

## Study
Pooled population PK of baloxavir acid (active metabolite of the oral prodrug baloxavir marboxil, Xofluza) from 13 studies: phase 1 healthy subjects, phase 2 and phase 3 otherwise-healthy (OwH) influenza patients, and the phase 3 high-risk (HR) study (CAPSTONE-2). Abstract: 11,846 concentrations from 1,827 subjects (664 HR patients, 2,341 concentrations); Methods says 11,848 data points (a 2-point inconsistency in the paper). Subjects (Table 1): healthy Asian 231, healthy non-Asian 46, patients Asian 844, non-Asian 706. Weight median 61-83 kg, range 36.0-217.3 kg; age 12-85 yr. Single oral doses; fed state split fasted/intermediate/fed. Assay: validated LC-MS/MS on plasma. NONMEM 7.3, FOCE-I; covariate screen p<0.05, backward deletion p<0.01; bootstrap n=5000; pc-VPC.

## Model and parameters

Equations (Table 2 footnote): CL/F = 10.8 x (BW/67.7)^0.362 x 0.519^Asian; Vc/F = 565 x (BW/67.7)^0.833 x 0.564^Asian; Ka = 1.03 x 0.682^gender (0 male, 1 female). IIV on CL/F, Vc/F, Vp1/F (29.3%), Vp2/F (35.4%), Ka; CL-Vc covariance 0.209. Shrinkage of Vp1/F and Vp2/F above 60%.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No ke0 or hysteresis (antiviral; not an opioid). Exposure-response in HR patients, time to improvement of symptoms (TTIIS) and day-2 virus-titer change against Cmax, AUC and C24 (observed concentration 20-28 h postdose) bands (Tables 4, 5). C24 >=20 ng/mL gave a shorter TTIIS than placebo and median day-2 titer change of -3.8 to -4.5 log10 TCID50/mL (type A) and -2.3 to -4.0 (type B); C24 <20 ng/mL had few patients (9 type A, 6 type B). Table 3 (post hoc Bayesian) mean Cmax/AUC/C24 for the 40 mg and 80 mg groups by race. Lowest-dose (10 mg) phase 2 exposure: Cmax 27.8 ng/mL, AUC 2,105 ng.h/mL, C24 15.1 ng/mL.

## Covariates
Body weight (allometric, exponents above), race (Asian) on CL/F and Vc/F, sex on Ka. Food on F, age, organ function and the risk factors for complications were tested and dropped. HR status did not change PK. Authors note the race effects on CL/F and Vc/F are similar, so they may reflect bioavailability, not clearance.

## Caveats
Parameters are apparent (/F); the exponent 0.362 on CL/F is below the 0.75 allometric value and 0.833 below 1 (authors note this). Wide IIV on Ka. Tables 3-5 scrambled in the text layer.
