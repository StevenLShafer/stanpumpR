# Ilias 2000: antithrombin III in adults with severe sepsis

## Study
- Prospective, open, randomised, two parallel groups, phase II; eleven academic ICUs in Austria, Belgium, Denmark, Germany, Norway and Sweden.
- n = 33 with severe sepsis (32 in the pharmacokinetic analysis: 14 intermittent, 18 continuous); age median 64 years, range 18-85; 23 men, 10 women; 6 non-operative, 13 emergency and 14 elective postoperative; APACHE II median 17 (8-28), SAPS II median 47 (Table 1). No weight or height printed.
- Product: Kybernin P (Centeon Pharma, Marburg), plasma-derived human antithrombin III. Loading dose 6000 IU, then 1000 IU every 4 h, or 250 IU/h, for 4 days; 30 000 IU in all. Low-dose heparin allowed for prophylaxis and line flushing.
- Baseline functional AT III activity median 63% (range 20-100%, n = 32; Table 1).
- Sampling: before, at 15, 30, 60 and 120 min after the first bolus, 4-96 h, 4 and 14 h after the last dose, and on day 7. Functional (chromogenic) and immunological assays in one central laboratory; they agreed (Figure 1), so only activity was analysed.

## Model and parameters
Each patient fitted by non-linear regression (HOEREP-PC) to one compartment with zero-order input, after the pre-infusion activity was subtracted from every later sample; intermittent dosing by superposition. Not a population model: Table 3 summarises the individual fits. Volume and clearance "standardized to 70 kg body weight".

By regimen (Table 3): intermittent half-life median 21.1 h, Vss 4.6 L, CL 2.5 mL/min; continuous 17.0 h, 4.4 L, 3.0 mL/min.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None modelled. Levels above 120% were the target and were reached in all but one patient. Mortality was higher with Vss above 4.5 L (response below 1.7% per IU/kg; Figure 6, Table 5), which the authors read as capillary leak.

## Covariates
None in a model. Heparin in eight patients did not change the kinetics (Results).

## Caveats
- The medians are internally consistent (0.693 x 4.5 L / 2.7 mL/min = 19.3 h, against 18.6 h); the means are not (15.3 h against 18.9), the mean of a ratio not being the ratio of means.
- The day-7 samples sometimes exceeded the fit, read as recovering endogenous synthesis, which the model does not carry.
- Sepsis: antithrombin is consumed; the half-life is far shorter than the 2.5-3.8 days the Thrombate III label gives in hereditary deficiency.
- The abstract says the median response was 1.75% per IU/kg; Table 3 gives 1.70 as the median and 1.75 as the mean.
