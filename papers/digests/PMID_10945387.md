# Ilias 2000: antithrombin III PK in severe sepsis

## Study
Prospective, open, randomised, 2 parallel groups, phase II, 11 ICUs in 6 European countries (PDF p. 1-3). 33 adults with severe sepsis (median age 64 y, range 18-85; 23 male, 10 female; Table 1); 32 had PK data (14 intermittent, 18 continuous). Median APACHE II 17, SAPS II 47. Baseline AT III activity median 56% (intermittent) and 65% (continuous), range 20-100% (Table 2). Body weight is not reported; Table 3 volumes and clearances are "standardized to 70 kg" (method not given).
Drug: AT III concentrate (Kybernin P, Centeon), IV. Loading dose 6,000 IU, then either 1,000 IU every 4 h (intermittent boluses) or 250 IU/h continuous, for 4 days; total 30,000 IU. Heparin was limited (8 patients had some; the text says no PK effect).
Samples: pre-infusion; 15, 30, 60, 120 min after the end of the first bolus; 4, 12, 24, 36 ... 96 h; 4 and 14 h after the last infusion; day 7. Plasma AT III by chromogenic functional (activity) assay (Berichrom) and immunological (antigen) assay (Liatest); the two agreed (Fig. 1), so PK used activity only. Pre-infusion level was subtracted before fitting.
Model: one compartment, C(t)=Css(1-exp(-lambda t)) during infusion and mono-exponential decay after; superposition for the intermittent scheme; HOEREP-PC nonlinear regression on each patient (no population model).

Mean half-life 95% CI (Table 4, text layer): 18.9 h (16.0-21.7) overall; 20.3 (15.7-24.9) intermittent; 17.8 (13.9-21.7) continuous. Mean response 1.75 (1.60-1.90) overall.
Healthy-volunteer half-life quoted from other studies: 2.3-4.8 days (Introduction and Discussion).
(Derived: median CL 2.7 mL/min with median Vss 4.5 L gives V/CL of about 27.8 h for the one-compartment half-life ln2 x V/CL, against the reported 18.6 h; the paper does not reconcile the medians of individual ratios with the ratio of medians.)

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD model, no ke0, no EC50, no MEAC. The target was plasma AT III functional activity above 120% of normal for 4 days; both regimens reached it in all but one patient (median levels 168-232% during treatment, none below 100%; Table 2 and Results). Baseline AT III below 60% went with higher 28-day mortality (Table 5; 42% vs 24% in the Discussion). Not an opioid; no equianalgesic ratio.

## Covariates
None modelled. Vss and CL scaled to 70 kg by the authors. Heparin co-administration: no PK effect, stated without numbers. Vss above 4.5 L or response below 1.7% per IU/kg went with worse survival (Fig. 6, Table 5), read by the authors as possible capillary leak.

## Caveats
Individual fits to a one-compartment model; day-7 levels exceeded the fit in some patients, attributed by the authors to recovering synthesis. Wide variability (CV 41-59% for elimination parameters). Small n, open label, no placebo. Dose was in IU, not per kg; "response" is the authors' per-kg normalisation. Mortality subgroup findings are exploratory.

## Verification against stanpumpR
No repo claim rests on this paper, so the verdict is not-applicable. Independent comparison with `R/drugs_antithrombin.R:106-147` (Moffett 2017, children; CL 0.917 dL/h x (W/70)^0.75, VD 67.9 dL x W/70 x (BASE/60)^-0.389, heparin held at 0):
- Model at 70 kg, baseline 60 IU/dL (derived): CL 0.0917 L/h = 1.53 mL/min, V 6.79 L, half-life 51.3 h, recovery 1.03 IU/dL per IU/kg (70 IU / 67.9 dL).
- Ilias (septic adults, medians at 70 kg): CL 2.7 mL/min, Vss 4.5 L, half-life 18.6 h, response 1.70-1.75% per IU/kg.
- Direction: the library's adult curve has a smaller CL (about 0.57x), a larger V (about 1.5x), a half-life about 2.8x longer and a recovery about 0.6x of this cohort's. The comparator is septic patients with consumption (baseline 56-65%); the paper itself finds a half-life far shorter than healthy volunteers (2.3-4.8 days), so a longer half-life for a non-consuming adult is the expected direction. The header at `drugs_antithrombin.R:66-76` already predicts a slower fall than consuming patients show, though it contrasts the Thrombate label, not this paper. Both express activity as % of normal (IU/dL), so recovery is comparable.
- The model's 60 IU/dL default baseline (`drugs_antithrombin.R:119`) is close to the Ilias medians (56% and 65%).
