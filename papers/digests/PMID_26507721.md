# Geiser 2016: dulaglutide clinical PK and phase 2/3 population PK

## Study
Three analyses (Table 1, PDF p3 / printed 627):
- **Phase 1 meta-analysis** (studies 1-8; 189 healthy subjects and 73 patients with T2D; Table 3: N = 262, 27.9%
  T2D, mean weight 83.3 kg in the diabetic and 81.5 kg in the non-diabetic subjects): noncompartmental (WinNonlin),
  doses 0.5-1.5 mg (1.5 mg about 87% of the data). Includes an absolute bioavailability crossover (16 healthy
  subjects, 1.5 mg SC vs 0.1 mg IV) and an injection-site crossover (39 subjects).
- **Phase 2 population analysis** (studies 9-13; 987 patients, 3515 samples; 0.1-3.0 mg weekly).
- **Phase 3 population analysis** (studies 13-15, AWARD-5, -1, -3; **1067 patients with T2D**, 3317 samples;
  0.75 and 1.5 mg weekly; Table 3: 47% female, age 55 (20-86) y, weight 94 (46-157) kg, BMI 33 (22-54), CrCl 121
  (47-297) mL/min).
- Assay: radioimmunoassay against the GLP-1 analogue portion (also detects native GLP-1), quantification range 5-50
  ng/mL (p2 / 626).
- NONMEM 7.2, FOCE with interaction; informative priors on the structural parameters from the IV data of the absolute
  bioavailability study (p4 / 628).

## Model and parameters
Two compartments, first-order absorption and elimination (p5 / 629). Phase 2: Emax effect of dose on bioavailability,
F1 = F0 - FMAX x DOSE / (DOSE + FD50), FD50 0.19 mg (SEE 9.46%), FMAX 59.7%, constrained so that F at 1.5 mg = 47%
(p5-6). Phase 3 final model, Table 6 (p7 / 631, rendered):

Table 6 does not give a 0.75 mg F1 or a phase 3 dose term, although the Discussion (p9 / 633) says the dose effect
was put into the absorption component. Covariate effect on IIV: weight explained under 6% of CL variability.

Table 4 (p6 / 630, rendered; NCA at steady state in T2D, geometric mean (CV%)): 0.75 mg (one study, N = 11):
AUCtau 6730 (32) ng·h/mL, Cmax 51.6 (30) ng/mL, tmax median 48 h (24-72.5), t1/2 5.5 (18) d (N = 9), CL/F 0.111
(32) L/h, Vz/F 19.2 (19) L (N = 9). 1.5 mg (two studies, N = 15): AUCtau 14,000 (30), Cmax 114 (35), tmax 48
(24-72), t1/2 4.7 (14) d, CL/F 0.107 (30), Vz/F 17.4 (28). The abstract's "48 h" to Cmax and "5 days" half-life
come from this steady-state table; no single-dose tmax is reported.

Other results: intra-individual CV after a single 1.5 mg dose 11.9% (AUCtau) and 16.1% (Cmax); accumulation ratio
1.56 at 1.5 mg; steady state between the 2nd and 4th dose (Abstract); single-dose exposure less than dose
proportional over 0.5-1.5 mg (Fig. 1). Healthy vs T2D single 1.5 mg: ratio 0.923 (AUCinf) and 0.857 (Cmax). Table 5
injection site (N 39-44): AUCinf ratio vs abdomen 0.973 (arm), 0.989 (thigh); Cmax 0.984 (arm), 0.890 (thigh,
90% CI 0.838-0.944).

Derived checks (mine): micro-constants give lambda_z = 0.00384 /h (t1/2 180 h = 7.5 d) and absorption t1/2 90 h
(3.8 d); at 83 kg F = 0.511 and steady-state AUCtau = 12.9 mg·h/L; a simulated steady-state Cmax of 92 ng/mL at
44 h, and single-dose tmax 59 h.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None modelled here. Missed-dose simulations (Fig. 5): a dose taken 3 days before the next gives a transient ~20%
higher concentration after the next dose; recommendation to take a missed dose if at least 72 h remain.

## Covariates
Tested (Table 2): dose, age, BMI, weight, CrCl, macroalbuminuria, serum creatinine, sex, race, DPP-4 inhibitor use,
ethnicity, smoking. Final phase 3: body weight on F1 only, because weight had "similar effects on both clearance and
the volume of distribution" (p6 / 630). Hispanic origin was on CL in phase 2 only. Fig. 4 reference patient: 93 kg,
56 y, male, white; no dose adjustment recommended for any factor.

## Caveats
- RIA also detects native GLP-1. F was fixed from 16 healthy subjects; F1 is labelled "relative bioavailability" in
  Table 6 but fixed at the absolute value.
- Q has SEE 38.4% and Vc IIV SEE 57.2%: weakly informed.
- The fitted dose range is 0.75-1.5 mg (phase 3); 3 and 4.5 mg are outside it.
- Industry-sponsored (Eli Lilly); all authors employees.
