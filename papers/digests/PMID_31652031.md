# Jacobs 2019: capecitabine and four metabolites, population PK

## Study
Pooled analysis of seven studies (Table 1, PDF page 2) of oral capecitabine in cancer patients, rich sampling over 0-8 h after dose on days 1, 22/43 or treatment day 1; 237 patients (159 men, 78 women; mean age 57.5 y, range 27.8-77.8), 8,988 observations; 63 patients had two occasions, one had three. Median dose 1,650 mg (range 300-2,600). Gastric surgery: none 154, total gastrectomy 24, partial gastrectomy 44, esophagogastrectomy 15 (Table 2, PDF page 3). DPYD*2A heterozygous 21 (8.9%). Capecitabine was given within 30 minutes after food. Plasma assayed by LC-MS/MS (capecitabine, dFCR, dFUR LLoQ 27.8, 40.8, 40.6 nmol/L in assay 1 and 139, 204, 203 nmol/L in assay 2; 5-FU 384 and FBAL 467 nmol/L); 560 below-LLoQ values (5.0, 4.7, 4.8, 10.1, 7.4% by analyte) imputed as LLoQ/2. NONMEM 7.3, FOCE-I, sequential then simultaneous fitting (pages 3, 8).

## Model and parameters
Figure 1 (PDF page 3): gut, four transit compartments (ktr), capecitabine two-compartment, then dFCR two-compartment, dFUR and 5-FU as single rate-constant steps (flip-flop; volumes ran to zero and were not estimated), FBAL two-compartment. Bioavailability and the fractions converted are not identifiable, so everything is apparent (/F). 31.6% of capecitabine occasions (95 of 301) peaked at the first sample and were handled with a zero-order input to the first observation. ## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None; no effect model. Not an opioid paper, so no MEAC, ke0 or potency ratio. Concentrations are plasma. Observed average peaks: capecitabine about 0.5 h, dFCR/dFUR/5-FU about 1 h, FBAL about 2.5 h (page 2 text). Simulated (2,000 mg, 1,000 patients; page 6) median capecitabine tmax 0.8 h (90% PI 0.3-3.3) reference, 0.7 (0.3-2.4) partial gastrectomy, 0.4 (0.2-1.0) total gastrectomy; median Cmax 11.0 / 13.0 / 16.7 (printed "mmol/L", see open_questions).

## Covariates
Gastrectomy on ktr (multipliers 1.46 and 3.14); DPYD*2A heterozygosity on k 5-FU>FBAL (x0.785); age and female sex on CL/F FBAL (Eq 3 power of age over the population mean 57.5 y, with exponent -0.97; female x0.757). DPYD c.2846A>T, c.1236G>A and CDA c.79A>C had no significant effect. Missing categorical covariates get their own estimate (Eq 5). Comparator for "faster" absorption after gastrectomy: patients with no gastrectomy in the same data set.

## Caveats
Absorption highly variable; the transit model slightly underpredicted median Cmax; a zero-order input was needed for 29% / 31.8% / 50% of curves in non-gastrectomy / partial / total (page 6); only 21 DPYD*2A carriers; food type not recorded; bioavailability and metabolic fractions not identifiable, so all values are apparent; simulations had to replace the zero-order input by random assignment with duration capped at 1 h.

## Used by stanpumpR
Not cited (context locations empty). The library does model capecitabine (R/drugs_capecitabine.R) and 5-FU formed from it (capecitabine5FU), from Gieschke 2003.

## Verification against stanpumpR
No repo citation to check; compared independently.
- Absorption: library first-order ka 1.09 /h (R/drugs_capecitabine.R:88, lag 0, line 138). Jacobs uses four transit compartments with ktr 5.08/h, mean transit time 0.98 h (derived from text, p3) in non-gastrectomy patients; mean absorption time of a first-order ka of 1.09 /h is 0.92 h (derived), so comparable on average; the shapes differ.
- Chain: library dose enters 5'-DFUR in one step, one compartment V1 90.6 L, CL1 75.8 L/h (k 0.837/h), then 5-FU V 17.8 L, CL 1190 L/h (k 66.9/h; lines 89-90). Jacobs puts capecitabine and dFCR (two-compartment each) upstream of dFUR, and estimates dFUR and 5-FU only as rate constants (129/h and 777/h), flip-flop, no volumes (Table 3, PDF p4). These cannot be compared directly: Jacobs' absolute dFUR amount is unidentifiable, and its apparent dFUR terminal decay follows dFCR (derived 1.36/h) rather than a dFUR elimination, which is faster than the library's 0.837/h. Direction of difference: the paper's terminal decay is faster than the library's dFUR k, though against a different (Gieschke, colorectal-only, 1250 mg/m2 twice daily) population.
- 5-FU: both have a very short half-life that follows formation (library 37 s per its header, line 49; Jacobs 3.2 s derived from 777/h); agree in kind.
- Library has no gastrectomy or DPYD*2A effects (header says no covariates beyond ALP and size); Jacobs gives x0.785 on 5-FU elimination in heterozygous DPYD*2A carriers and faster absorption after gastrectomy, neither applied. The note in the library header ("no band") is consistent with the paper, which gives no concentration-effect.
- Doses: capecitabine is oral in mg (library unit mg PO, drugDefaults_global.csv:284); the paper's dose is mg per patient, no per-m2 or per-kg basis, consistent.
Verdict: **partly-verified** (uncited; kinetics cannot be matched value to value; unit and structure questions listed above).
