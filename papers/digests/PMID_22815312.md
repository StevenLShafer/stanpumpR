# Dickmann 2013: estradiol induces CYP2B6 in human hepatocytes

## Study
In vitro. Cryopreserved hepatocytes from 3 female donors treated 48 h with estradiol, rifampicin (0.025-25 µM)
or carbamazepine (0.39-200 µM), triplicate. End points: CYP2B6 mRNA (TaqMan) and bupropion hydroxylation
(LC-MS/MS). 3-parameter Emax fits (GraphPad Prism). In vivo fold induction predicted as
1 + [I]·Emax/([I] + EC50), estradiol 10-1000 nM, no scaling factor. Comparator for every fold value: vehicle-
treated hepatocytes from the same donor. No methadone was studied.

Rifampicin activity EC50 1.3 ± 0.7 µM, Emax 16.9 ± 12.7; carbamazepine 145 ± 65 µM, 21.5 ± 14.3 (Table 1).
Abstract: at 100 nM, CYP2B6 activity predicted to rise 1.5-3-fold (combining activity and mRNA). Pregnancy
estradiol cited as reaching up to 50 nM (Soldin 2005), giving a predicted ~2-fold rise in expression (p272).
Discussion: this 2-fold matches the ~2-fold methadone clearance rise of Pond 1985 and Wolff 2005 (p273).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Opioid audit: no MEAC, no ke0/t½ke0/tPeak, no potency ratio; the paper has no clinical or methadone data.

## Covariates
Estradiol concentration only (in vitro).

## Caveats
Three donors, wide donor variability (activity Emax 2.2 to 44); hepatocyte vs plasma estradiol uncertain; the
2-fold methadone link is inferred, not measured; CYP3A4 (~2-fold, Hebert 2008) and renal clearance also cited.
