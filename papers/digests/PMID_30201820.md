# Thakkar 2018: population pharmacokinetics of tafenoquine

## Study
Population PK of oral tafenoquine from six GSK studies. Estimation set: 675 subjects, 5,286 plasma observations from five studies: DDI (healthy), SIL (healthy), TQT (healthy), DETECTIVE part 1 (phase 2B, capsule; patients) and DETECTIVE part 2 (phase 3, tablet; patients). Healthy volunteers 193 (28.6%), patients 482 (71.4%) with P. vivax malaria (Table 2, PDF p 3). External validation: GATHER (phase 3, 300 mg tablet, 166 subjects, 1,001 samples, Table 1 PDF p 2). The Table 1 rows are scrambled in the text layer, but the five estimation studies sum to 675 subjects (24 + 14 + 155 + 223 + 259, derived) and 5,286 samples (283 + 232 + 2,215 + 1,067 + 1,489, derived), leaving 166 and 1,001 for GATHER. Single oral doses of 50 to 1,200 mg of tafenoquine (the 1,200 mg TQT dose was 400 mg daily for 3 days). Demographics (Table 2, medians/ranges): age 35.0 y (15.0-79.0), weight 69.3 kg (37.2-138), BMI 24.3 (15.5-47.0), 171 women (25.3%), 504 men; formulation tablet 297 (44.0%), capsule 378 (56.0%). Assay: LC-MS/MS on plasma, LLQ 2 ng/mL (0.5 ng/mL in the SIL study), upper limit 3,000 ng/mL; below-limit samples (<4%) were excluded. NONMEM 7.3, FOCE-I, log-transformed concentrations, bootstrap n=500, VPCs by mrgsolve. Concentrations ranged 2.13-1,013 ng/mL.

## Model and parameters
Two compartments, lagged first-order absorption, first-order elimination; apparent (/F) parameters. Allometric weight scaling with exponents fixed at 0.75 (CL/F, Q/F) and 1 (V2/F, V3/F) (Methods, PDF p 10); the reference weight is not printed. Formulation on F1 (tablet = 1 reference) and Ka; health status (healthy volunteers versus patients) on V2/F and V3/F. Residual error additive on the log scale. Covariance step aborted, so precision is from the bootstrap. Table 3 (PDF p 4, read on the page image), final run (bootstrap median; 90% CI):

Relative bioavailability, capsule versus tablet (Results, PDF p 6-7): 86% (0.86), bootstrap 90% interval 0.83-0.90; post hoc 300 mg AUC0-60 and Cmax ratios within 80-125% (Table 4, text layer: e.g. tablet DETECTIVE part 2 versus capsule DETECTIVE part 1 AUC 1.07 (1.00-1.14), Cmax 1.00 (0.93-1.08); the rows of Table 4 are scrambled in the text layer and were not read on the image). Terminal half-life about 15 days (Introduction, PDF p 2); healthy volunteers about 30% higher V2 (Discussion, PDF p 9, '1.3').

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. This is a PK paper; no PD, MEAC, ke0, therapeutic range or effect-site quantity is reported (not an opioid).

## Covariates
Body weight (allometric, fixed exponents), formulation (F1 and Ka), health status (V2/F, V3/F). Age, gender and ethnicity were tested and had no relevant effect (PDF p 5). Dropping formulation on Ka lowered OBJFV by 20.4 but it was kept to describe Cmax (PDF p 5).

## Caveats
Health status is confounded with chloroquine co-medication and with the studies (healthy volunteers took tafenoquine alone). Food state of the studies not stated and no food term fitted. Single doses only (mostly 300 mg). Shrinkage on ALAG1 54.3%. Peak and AUC comparisons are model-based. The text and Table 3 differ on the capsule F (0.833 vs 0.863) and the V2 healthy ratio (about 1.3 in the Discussion vs 1.35).
