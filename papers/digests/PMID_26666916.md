# Ayyoub 2016: pyronaridine population PK in children

## Study
Pooled one phase II (SP-C-003-05, Gabon, rich sampling to day 21) and five phase III trials of pyronaridine-artesunate (3:1, Pyramax) in children with uncomplicated malaria; tablets and granules, 3 daily doses, pyronaridine tetraphosphate about 6 to 12 mg/kg (PDF p2-3). Phase III sampling was sparse (1-2 samples: day 0-3 and day 4-42). Table 1 (PDF p3): about 349 subjects, 1,085 observations used, 165 (13.2%) below LOQ excluded. Median age 7 y, median weight 20 kg, 52.4% male (text, p5). Assay: whole-blood LC-MS, LLOQ 5.7 ng/mL, linear to 855 ng/mL. NONMEM 7, FOCE, log-transformed data; dose converted from tetraphosphate to base with factor 0.57 (p2).

## Model and parameters
Two-compartment, first-order absorption and elimination, oral (apparent) parameters. Allometric scaling fixed, weight centred at 20 kg: exponent 1 on volumes, 0.75 on clearances. Table 2 (PDF p4, printed 1453), typical values:

Covariate equations (p5): V3/F = 3230 x (weight/20) x (age/7)^0.624 x exp(eta); Ka = 17.9 x (1 + formulation x 1.63) x exp(eta). Mean elimination half-life 12.3 days (text, p5). All rates per day. Doses are base: 34.2 mg per 60 mg tetraphosphate sachet (Table 3).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD and no concentration target stated. Not an opioid; no MEAC, ke0 or potency ratio.

## Covariates
Tested: creatinine clearance, ALT, sex, age, formulation, haemoglobin. Retained: age on V3/F and granule formulation on Ka. Weight is a fixed allometric covariate. The authors say the age effect does not change exposure, which depends on CL/F.

## Caveats
Sparse phase III sampling; the granule effect on Ka is confounded with age and rests on few absorption-phase points; IIV on Q fixed to 0; shrinkage 48.8% (CL), 15.2% (V2), 63.8% (V3), 71.6% (Ka); 13.5% of samples below LOQ were excluded, not modelled; bootstrap convergence 85.9%. Whole-blood total concentrations (pyronaridine concentrates in red cells).
