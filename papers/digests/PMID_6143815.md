# Greenblatt 1984: temazepam with and without cimetidine

## Study
- 9 healthy men and women, 21-67 y (weights not given), randomised crossover at least 1 week apart.
- 30 mg oral temazepam (Restoril hard capsule, US) fasting, alone or during cimetidine 300 mg every 6 h
  (from 12 h before to 48 h after).
- Plasma at 5 min-48 h (19 times). Electron-capture GC. Free fraction by equilibrium dialysis at 37 °C
  with [14C]temazepam.
- CL = dose/AUC assuming the whole dose available (F = 1); Vd = CL/β.
