# Hammer 2009: propofol EC50 for EGD in children, with and without dexmedetomidine

## Study
- Two consecutive groups of 12 children, ages 3-10 y, ASA-type exclusions (no chronic sedatives or opioids, no obesity, no major organ disease), scheduled for esophagogastroduodenoscopy (EGD) at Stanford. No premedication, no topical pharyngeal anaesthesia (p139).
- Control group (propofol alone) was published earlier (ref 20, Hammer 2001) and run by the same investigators under the same plan; the DEX group was studied consecutively (not randomised) (p139-140).
- DEX group: dexmedetomidine 1 ug/kg over 10 min, then 0.5 ug/kg/h for the procedure. Propofol TCI started 5 min into the dexmedetomidine load; both groups assessed after 5 min of propofol (p139).
- Propofol delivered by STANPUMP TCI using the Kataria paediatric propofol model (refs 18, 19). The "plasma concentration" is therefore the model-predicted target, not a measured level. No assay.
- Target concentrations chosen by Dixon up-and-down, levels (Table 1, p140): 1.0, 1.25, 1.6, 2.0, 2.5, 3.2, 4.0, 5.0, 6.3 ug/mL (log dose 0 to 0.8 in steps of 0.1). Abstract gives the targets as 2.8-4.0 ug/mL.
- End point: non-responder = no rise of MAP or HR of 20% or more above baseline and no significant movement at endoscope insertion; responder otherwise (p139).
- Analysis: logistic (Hill) model P(no response) = Cp^n / (Cp^n + EC50^n), NONMEM VI Laplacian, log-likelihood-profile CI, 1000 bootstrap replicates (p140).
- Demographics (Table 2, p141): age control 7.2 +/- 2.6 y (3-10), DEX 6.5 +/- 2.1 (4-10); weight control 25.3 +/- 11.2 kg (9.7-52.8), DEX 26.8 +/- 7.70 (18-42); M/F 7/5 and 8/4.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- Table 3 (p141, read on the page image). Propofol EC50 (SE): control 3.7 (0.4), DEX 3.5 (0.2) ug/mL. Bootstrap EC50 (95% CI): 3.7 (2.8-4.5) and 3.5 (3.0-4.0). Symmetrical 95% CI: 2.8-4.5 and 3.1-3.8. Non-symmetrical (profile) 95% CI: 3.0-5.0 and 2.9-4.1. Responders/non-responders: 9/3 control, 6/6 DEX.
- Steepness n and its estimate are not reported in the text or tables.
- Conclusion: no significant shift of the propofol concentration-response curve; authors call the 0.2 ug/mL left shift clinically insignificant (Fig 1, p141).
- Context given in the Discussion (p142): propofol alone 3.7 vs 2.8 ug/mL with remifentanil 25 ng/kg/min (Drover 2004, ref 16); adult loss-of-consciousness values 2.3-2.7 ug/mL cited from other studies; 8.72 ug/mL for LMA insertion in adult men cited from Higuchi.
- No ke0, t1/2 ke0, or tPeak for propofol or dexmedetomidine; the EC50 is on predicted plasma concentration with only 5 min of propofol exposure, so effect-site equilibration was probably incomplete (the paper does not say). Not an opioid paper; no MEAC or potency ratio.
- No dexmedetomidine concentration was measured or predicted.

## Covariates
None analysed (age and weight only described; no covariate model).

## Caveats
- Small n (12 per group), non-randomised, control group historical-consecutive; authors state low power for logistic goodness of fit (p143).
- EC50 is conditional on the Kataria model's predicted concentrations; it does not transfer to another model (e.g. Eleveld) without re-expression.
- Painful stimulus (endoscope insertion) in children, an end point of movement/haemodynamics, not loss of consciousness.
- Weight in the control group goes up to 52.8 kg for a 3-10 y range; the table reports it as printed.
- The adverse-event rows (Table 3) differ between the text layer and the page image; the image was used: bradycardia 0/3, hypotension 2/0, respiratory depression 4/1, vomiting 0/1, total 6 (50%) and 5 (42%) (control/DEX).
