# Pennell 2022 (MONEAD supplement): carbamazepine box plots, cohort flow, postpartum mixed-effect models

## Study
No new study. The supplement adds to the parent article (card `PMID_35157004.md`; MONEAD, 326 pregnant and 104
nonpregnant women with epilepsy, dose-normalized concentration (DNC) in µg/L/mg).

  The final counts of both figures equal the main article's Table 1 women counts.
- **eFigure 1 (p. 2)** illustrates the mixed-effect parameterisation on the lamotrigine data: β0 the control intercept,
  β0 + β2 the pregnant intercept, β1 the control slope, β1 + β3 the pregnant slope, against study week (gestational
  age for the pregnant women, weeks since enrolment for the controls). Its y axis is in (µg/mL)/mg, 0.00-0.04, which is
  0-40 µg/L/mg.

Footnotes (p. 6): β0 is the DNC at the nonpregnant state in both cohorts; a significant β2 means the pregnant
cohort's postpartum DNC differs from the controls'; a significant β3 means a different change over time. Model

different quantity from the main article's postpartum medians (for example zonisamide 51.07 against the median
40.12).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. DNCs only; no seizure outcome, no concentration-effect relation.

## Covariates
Cohort (β2, β3), time (β1) and, for lamotrigine and levetiracetam only, estrogen-containing hormonal medication
(eTable). In the postpartum data estrogen lowered lamotrigine DNC by 3.36 (P = .01) and did not affect levetiracetam
(P = .52).

## Caveats
- Carbamazepine numbers are small (T1 n = 5; 16 at postpartum) and the box-plot medians are printed to one decimal.
- The eTable's slope unit "(1/week)" is a header label; the quantity is DNC per week (eFigure 1).
- The eFigure 4 legend names topiramate, which has no panel.
- The supplement holds trimester medians for carbamazepine only; the other drugs' trimester medians are in the main
  article's Figures 1-3.

## Verification against stanpumpR
- **Carbamazepine baseline: discrepancy** (descriptive, no number is computed from it). The registry
  (`data-raw/pregnancyRegistry.R:1104`, csv `:97`) gives "11.56 to 7.97" as postpartum to lowest, as the main article
  prints it. eFigure 4 shows a postpartum median of 9.6 and a first-trimester median of 11.6. Against the figure's
  postpartum box the largest fall is 16.7% (T2), and the only significant one 14.6% (T3); 17.3% is consistent with a
  9.6 baseline, and 31% (7.97/11.56) is not supported by any box. So the notes' "17-31%" (`:1113`) can be narrowed to
  about 15-17% (derived). The registry applies no factor (`cl_factor = NA`), so the plotted model is unaffected.
- **Unbound carbamazepine and the epoxide unchanged** (`:1104`): eFigure 4 marks no significant difference for either.
  **matches**. Trimester `third` (`:1107`): the only significant difference is T3 against postpartum; the lowest
  median is T2. **matches** the registry's choice of trimester.
