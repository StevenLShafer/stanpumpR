# Zhang 2021: ceftolozane and tazobactam in plasma and ELF, adults including ventilated pneumonia

## Study
- Pooled population analysis of 16 clinical studies (Merck), extending the earlier ceftolozane/tazobactam models with the phase 3 ASPECT-NP study (ventilated HABP/VABP; 3 g C/T, 1-h infusion every 8 h).
- Ceftolozane plasma data set: 968 adults (Table 1): 277 healthy participants (28.6%; "included patients with renal impairment without infection"), 176 cUTI, 174 cIAI, 331 pneumonia (305 from ASPECT-NP, all ventilated), 10 "other infection" (the augmented-renal-clearance cohort of MK-7625A-007, critically ill). Age 18.0-98.0 years (mean 53.2, SD 19.7); weight 33.5-173.0 kg (mean 74.7, SD 17.8); CrCL 6.3-531.3 mL/min (mean 109.9) by Cockcroft-Gault; 583 men, 385 women; 66.1% White, 21.7% Japanese, 12.2% other. No height or BMI printed.
- Tazobactam: 835 adults.
- NONMEM; forward selection at alpha 0.01, backward elimination at 0.001.

## Model and parameters
Two compartments, zero-order input, first-order elimination. Table 2, ceftolozane, with its printed equations:

    CL = 4.84 x (CrCL/100)^0.701 x 1.18^cUTI x 1.43^cIAI x 0.32^ESRD   L/h
    Vc = 9.23 x (WTKG/70)^0.684 x 1.25^cUTI x 1.59^cIAI x 2.00^Pneumonia x 2.14^OINF x 1.30^ESRD   L
    Q  = 3.13 L/h
    Vp = 4.78 x (WTKG/70)^0.484   L

Tazobactam: CL 16.6 x (CrCL/100)^0.623 x 0.626^ESRD; Vc 13.1 x (WT/70)^0.629 x 1.49^cIAI x 2.17^Pneumonia x 2.49^OINF x 0.749^ESRD; Q 4.05; Vp 4.89 x (WT/70)^0.530 x 1.25^cUTI x 1.34^cIAI x 2.06^Pneumonia.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No effect site. The ELF is a hypothetical link compartment (K1E, KE0) fitted to 51 participants; not used.

## Covariates
CrCL (Cockcroft-Gault; the weight in it is not named) on CL; weight on Vc and Vp; infection type on CL and Vc; ESRD on CL and Vc. Race not retained. Weight on CL not identified, "likely because body weight was partially accounted for in the Cockcroft-Gault equation".

## Caveats
- Every pneumonia patient was ventilated; non-ventilated pneumonia is not described.
- "Other infection" is 10 patients (1.0%).
- ESRD is a categorical term on top of CrCL; the Discussion attributes it partly to Cockcroft-Gault overestimating CrCL in ESRD.
- Q and Vp highly correlated; IIV on Vp poorly estimated.
