# Oggianu 2023: trazodone and gabapentin writhing PK/PD in mice

## Study
Mouse acetic-acid writhing test (male CD-1, 20-25 g; 0.7% acetic acid 16 uL/g i.p.; writhes counted in 5-min bins over 30 min; drugs given 1 h before the acid). Trazodone by oral gavage 0.075-4.5 mg/kg; gabapentin i.p. 1.5-15 mg/kg; combinations (trazodone+gabapentin) 0.075+1.5, 0.15+1.5, 0.5+5, 1.5+15 mg/kg (PDF p3). PK satellite groups, n = 12 per group, sparse sampling, 12 times to 24 h: trazodone 1, 3, 10 mg/kg; gabapentin 10, 30, 100 mg/kg. Human trazodone PK from a phase I crossover (n = 23, 50 mg IR and 30/60/90 mg drops); human gabapentin PK taken from Carlsson 2009 (ref 17: 16 neuropathic-pain patients, mean 1968 mg/day, median CrCl 90.5 mL/min) (PDF p3). NONMEM VI, FOCE with interaction.

## Model and parameters
Mouse PK, Table 1 (PDF p6, confirmed on the page image): both drugs 2-compartment with zero-order absorption (duration D1). Typical values; V1/F is per kg. Human trazodone: 2-compartment, first-order absorption with lag (parameters in the supplement).

Writhing PD model (PDF pp4-5; Table 2 on p6): a transit-compartment chain (n = 9). The drug reduces the stimulus in the first compartment by k2 x C; the additive combination is k2A CA + k2B CB and the interaction form adds gamma CA CB.

Additive-model k2 in the low-dose combination: trazodone 12.7, gabapentin 0.0808 (Table 4a). At 0.5+5 and 1.5+15 mg/kg gamma was about 0 and both models gave OBJF 1429.21 (text p6, Table 5).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
The end point is writhe count after acetic acid in mice, not human pain. k2 is a potency index acting on the stimulus, so there is no EC50, Emax, ke0, t1/2ke0 or tPeak, no hysteresis analysis, no MEAC or analgesic concentration, and no equianalgesic ratio. The only potency comparison is the k2 ratio, trazodone about 237 times gabapentin (derived: 10.9 / 0.0459); the L/umol/min units imply concentration in umol/L, and whether the PD link used total or free plasma concentration is not stated.
Table 6 (PDF p10, image confirmed) gives simulated human percent inhibition of the stimulus (means): gabapentin alone 5.4, 10.6, 16.4, 22.2, 28.2 at 50, 100, 200, 400, 800 mg; trazodone alone 4.93 (0.5 mg), 9.20 (1 mg), 27.6 (5 mg), 37.5 (10 mg), 56.2 (25 mg), 69.41 (50 mg); 0.5-10 mg trazodone with 50 or 100 mg gabapentin 21.7-81.7. The simulation assumes mouse k2 carries to humans, with a trazodone protein-binding correction (97% human vs 89% mouse, internal unpublished data) and none for gabapentin.

## Covariates
Formulation (50 mg IR tablet vs 30/60/90 mg drops) was tested on human trazodone PK. Human gabapentin PK uses dose-dependent bioavailability (hyperbolic function) from Carlsson 2009.

## Caveats
Acute mouse pain model; the authors state the same k2 across species is untestable until human data exist. Gabapentin was i.p. and trazodone oral. Table 1b gabapentin k has SE 58.8% and its variance terms SE >100%. The interaction finding rests on two low-dose groups. The Table 1 and 3 values are mouse values and are not human exposure-response.
