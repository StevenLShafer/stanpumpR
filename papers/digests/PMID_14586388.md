# Harris 2003: bioavailability of intranasal and smoked methamphetamine

## Study
- Eight male, non-dependent, experienced methamphetamine users, mean age 40 years (range 31-45), within 30% of
  ideal body weight; body weight itself **not reported**. All smoked tobacco and used caffeine. No women
  (PDF p2-3, printed 476, 478).
- Balanced crossover of two inpatient sessions about 1 week apart. Each session: either **intranasal**
  d-methamphetamine 50 mg (base equivalent; 10% HCl solution in saline, sprayed as a fine mist to the
  nasopharynx with two 0.25 mL Accuspray syringes) or **smoked** d-methamphetamine 40 mg (base equivalent; two
  20 mg pipe loads, each two deep untimed inhalations, 10 min apart, borosilicate pipe in an aluminium block at
  265 +/- 10 °C), **plus** simultaneous intravenous d3-methamphetamine 10 mg as a 15-min constant-rate infusion
  (PDF p2-3, printed 476-477). Dose basis of the 10 mg d3 IV dose not stated.
- Preliminary check in 4 men: 5 mg d3 + 5 mg d0 IV gave identical curves (Fig 1), so labelling does not alter
  kinetics.
- Plasma at -5 min, 10, 15, 30, 45 min, 1, 1.5, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 12, 18, 24, 36, 48 and 72 h; urine 0-4, 4-24,
  24-48, 48-72 h. Plasma site (venous/arterial) not stated.
- Assay: GC-MS (N-propyl derivatives), d0 and d3 methamphetamine and amphetamine in plasma and urine. Plasma
  intraday CV 4.3% at 1 ng/mL to 2.4% at 250 ng/mL. Urine LOQ for methamphetamine 10 ng/mL; for amphetamine 1 ng/mL.
- Analysis: **non-compartmental** (WinNonlin 3.0); linear-up/log-down trapezoids; MRT corrected for the 15-min
  infusion; F = IV CL x extravascular AUC(0-inf) / extravascular dose; renal clearance from 0-24 h data.

## Model and parameters
No compartmental model. Means +/- SD, from rendered Tables I-VI (PDF p6-8, printed 480-482). The tables run
across two facing pages; the right-hand half of each sits on printed 481.

- Smoked: mean 22.2 +/- 8.4 mg extracted from the 40 mg (range 11.3-36.4 mg, 28-91%); F of the delivered dose
  67% (text, PDF p5, printed 479; no SD given).
- Abstract rounds these to CL 272 mL/h/kg, Vss 4.2 L/kg, MRT 16 h, IV t1/2 11.4 h, tmax 2.7 h (intranasal) and
  2.5 h (smoked). (derived: 11.4 = mean of 11.8 and 11.0; 4.2 ~ mean of 4.30 and 4.02 = 4.16; 16 ~ mean of 16.6
  and 15.5 = 16.05.)
- tmax was earlier for smoked after excluding one outlier (P < .03).
- Renal clearance (Table V): methamphetamine 94.4-102 mL/h/kg, amphetamine 957-1270 mL/h/kg, ratio about
  10-13. Fraction of methamphetamine dose in urine (CLr/CL, Table VI) 34.4-39.3%, ranging about 10% to 90%
  between subjects; urine pH not controlled.
- **Table VI lists IV CL as 284 +/- 59.0 mL/h/kg for the IV-with-nasal session**, not Table I's 272 +/- 73.3
  (see open_questions).
- Hepatic extraction about 19% (authors' calculation, assuming hepatic blood flow 25 mL/min/kg, blood:plasma 1).
- Amphetamine (metabolite) t1/2 15.2-16.9 h (Tables III-IV; abstract 16.2 h); amphetamine/methamphetamine plasma
  AUC ratio 15.5-19.3%.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- Not an opioid; no MEAC, ke0, tPeak or potency ratio reported.
- Effects measured: heart rate, BP, rate-pressure product, respiratory rate, verbal 0-100 intoxication/craving,
  VAS (good/bad drug effect, desire, quality). Intranasal: SBP 121 -> 141, DBP 76 -> 86 mm Hg, HR 73 -> 94/min;
  smoked: SBP 121 -> 137, DBP 73 -> 83, HR 76 -> 106 (printed 480-481). No route difference; the authors say the
  10 mg IV dose confounds the effect data and no placebo was given. No concentration-effect analysis.

## Covariates
None analysed. Per-kg normalisation of CL and V is reported, but weight is not given and no weight-scaling was
tested. All subjects male.

## Caveats
- n = 8, all male, experienced users; no weights reported.
- Non-compartmental summaries only; no micro-constants, no absorption rate constant, no lag.
- tmax is the mean of individual tmax values, not the peak of a fitted curve.
- Intranasal input was a sprayed solution, not snorted powder.
- Smoked delivery depended on a heated-block pipe at 265 °C and 2 puffs per load; the authors note Cook et al
  (about 300 °C) found 90% bioavailability of a similar delivered dose.
- Comparative claims: "similar to previously reported values" (CL, Vss, MRT) means similar to Cook et al's
  smoked/IV study (ref 21), not to any model.
- The text layer's lambda z unit "L/h" is a misprint for 1/h (0.0628/h gives 11.0 h; derived).
- Internal inconsistency of IV CL between Table I (272) and Table VI (284) for one session.
