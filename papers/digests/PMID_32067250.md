# Langenhorst 2020: glutathione-depletion model of the fall in busulfan clearance

## Study
- Retrospective TDM data, UMC Utrecht, allogeneic HCT, September 2005 to January 2017; 385 patients (387 patient-transplant combinations; one transplanted twice), 3994 samples. Table 1 (PDF p5): weight 50 kg (3.7-130), age 14 years (0.16-73); 0-12 y 159, 12-20 y 91, 20-40 y 35, 40-60 y 59, 60+ y 43; 233 male, 154 female; samples per patient 15 (5-24) (median, range).
- Busulfan (Busilvex) IV over 3 h, once daily on days -5 to -2, TDM-targeted to a cumulative AUC of 90 mg·h/L (current), 80 (before 2011), 60 (reduced intensity) or 30 (Fanconi anaemia); with fludarabine (+/- clofarabine) or cyclophosphamide; rATG in 78% (premedicated with paracetamol 60 mg/kg up to 4 g, clemastine, prednisolone).
- Sampling usually 5 min and 1, 2, 3 h after the infusion, some 4-20 h; day 1 and/or 2, and day 4. Plasma; LC-MS, or HPLC-UV in children before September 2008.
- NONMEM 7.3 (SAEM then IMP); SIR for 95% CI; pcVPC.

## Model and parameters
Two-compartment base (Bartelink 2012). Clearance has a weight-changing allometric exponent (eq. 2, see open_questions); V1 a fixed estimated exponent; V2 and Q scaled with fixed exponents 1 and 0.75 on weight/43 kg. Table 2 (PDF p5, printed 1503), confirmed on the rendered page:

Empirical model (stepwise day 1 vs day > 1): V1 23.4 L/43 kg (22-24), V1 exponent 0.869 (0.84-0.88), CL day 1 7.58 L/h/43 kg (7.5-7.9), CL exponent l 1.03 (0.95-1.1), p -0.138 (-0.17 to -0.11), V2 4.83 L/43 kg (4-5.8), Q 5.6 L/h/43 kg (4-8.1), "CL decrement after day 1 (%)" 0.0676 (0.05-0.082) — printed under a % heading but a fraction: the text gives 7% (95% CI 5-8%). IIV: V1 13.8%, CL 19.4% (correlation with V1 74.9%), V2 27.4%; IOV CL 12.4%, V1 12%; proportional error HPLC 9%, LC-MS 6.6%.

Semi-mechanistic model (no step; a relative GSH amount, 1 at baseline, depleted in proportion to busulfan metabolism; eqs. 3-7): V1 23.3, exponent 0.863, CL at T = 0 7.61 L/h/43 kg (7.4-7.8), l 1.04, p -0.14, V2 4.73, Q 5.9, S_GSH 0.00259 h/mg for age ≤ 40 (0.0017-0.0032), age effect 0.0419 per year over 40 (0.0062-0.081), S_GSH,i = S_GSH,pop × (1 + (age - 40) × slope), slope 0 at 40 or younger (eq. 7). IIV V1 13.8%, CL 19.5% (corr 68.4%), V2 28.6%; IOV CL 12.1%, V1 11.5%; error HPLC 9.1%, LC-MS 6.6%. Elimination is k10 × A_GSH (eq. 3); A_GSH falls by S_GSH/V1 × A_GSH × k10 × A_bu1 (eq. 6, printed without a minus sign).

Derived at 70 kg (reading eq. 2 as (BW/43)^(l·BW^p); derived): empirical CL exponent 1.03 × 70^-0.138 = 0.573, CL day 1 = 7.58 × (70/43)^0.573 = 10.0 L/h; V1 = 23.4 × 1.628^0.869 = 35.7 L; V2 = 7.9 L; Q = 5.6 × 1.628^0.75 = 8.1 L/h.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD model, ke0 or concentration band. Exposure target is a cumulative AUC0-∞ of 90 mg·h/L (≈22000 µmol·min; window 80-100 mg·h/L from refs 3-5); TDM simulation target 81-99 mg·h/L. Target attainment in 258 simulated patients: empirical 74%, semi-mechanistic 75%, with age 76%; adults 76% vs 80% (Fig. 6). No exposure-outcome relation found in this cohort.

## Covariates
Weight (all structural parameters, as above); age over 40 on S_GSH (eq. 7, P = 0.009). rATG (surrogate for paracetamol) on the time effect: no improvement.

## Caveats
- No GSH measured: the depletion compartment is theoretical, with busulfan metabolism as a surrogate and GSH resynthesis neglected (eq. 5 simplified to eq. 6).
- Text and Table 2 disagree in places: the Results text gives IIV "14%, 19%, 28%" for CL, V1, V2 (the table has V1 13.8%, CL 19.4%), correlation 71% (table 74.9%) and IOV 11% (table 12-12.4%); the decrement heading says % for a fraction; eq. 2 is printed without the /43 inside the power; eq. 6 lacks a minus sign. The Results quote the day-1-to-4 range as 87% increase to -44% (the abstract says -82% to 44%).
- Single centre; retrospective; mostly children (65% under 20).
