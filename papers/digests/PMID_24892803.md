# Sahinovic 2014: CVI as a measure of nociception/antinociception balance

## Study
Randomised, 12-group design (3 BIS targets x 4 remifentanil targets, n = 10 per group, 120 analysed; Fig 1 p289). ASA I-II, age 18-65, weight 70-130% of ideal body weight. Propofol was titrated to BIS 70, 50 or 30 by a closed-loop system, then switched to open-loop TCI at the achieved effect-site concentration (CePROP) 30 s before the OAA/S evaluation. Remifentanil effect-site targets were 0, 2, 4 or 6 ng/mL. Propofol used the Schnider model and remifentanil the Minto model (p289; the paper's reference numbers 16-19 are not read). Remifentanil infusion started at -1080 s and propofol at -900 s relative to the start of OAA/S. A 30 s tetanic stimulus (100 Hz, 60 mA, ulnar nerve) was applied 60-90 s after OAA/S start. Patients with OAA/S 5 got no tetanus and counted as responders. Tolerant meant OAA/S 0 and no movement. Outcomes: BIS, CVI (offline, v2.1), HR, MAP. CVI is dimensionless, 0-10, lower is better. Statistics: prediction probability (PK), thresholds by Matthews correlation coefficient (MCC).

## Model and parameters
No pharmacokinetic model was fitted. The paper only uses published models to compute predicted concentrations.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- Opioid audit (remifentanil): no MEAC or analgesic concentration is reported. The 0/2/4/6 ng/mL values are targets (predicted effect-site, Minto model), not measured concentrations. No ke0, t1/2ke0, tPeak or hysteresis is reported. No potency or equianalgesic ratio is stated. No concentration-effect curve was fitted.
- Main finding: no concentration-effect relation between CeREMI and baseline CVI (Fig 4, p293; rho = -0.16, P = 0.087). CVI depended on BIS (rho = 0.64, P < 0.001). A significant CVI drop appeared only for 0 vs 6 ng/mL at BIS 50 (Table 2 footnote, P = 0.0464, p294).
- Heart rate and MAP fell with remifentanil at baseline and response (Table 2).
- Discrimination of responders vs nonresponders (Table 4, p296), PK (SE):

| Baseline | 0.85 (0.04) | 0.84 (0.04) | 0.69 | 0.69 | 0.72 | 0.73 |
| Response | 0.89 (0.04) | 0.94 (0.03) | 0.75 | 0.77 | not given | not given |
| Delta | 0.72 (0.05) | 0.87 (0.05) | 0.73 | 0.70 | not given | not given |

- Optimal thresholds by MCC (p293): baseline BIS about 60 and CVI about 1.8 (MCC about 0.55). Stimulation period: BIS 66 (MCC 0.55) and CVI 2.5 (MCC 0.8). For delta, the best combined PPV/NPV is at delta CVI 0.8 (MCC about 0.9); delta BIS reaches only MCC 0.4.
- Tolerant/responsive counts per group of 10 (Table 1, p290, read from the text layer, whose rows are interleaved): BIS 70 at REMI 0/2/4/6 = 1/9, 5/4, 8/2, 9/1. BIS 50 at REMI 0 = 3/7, and 10/0 at REMI 2/4/6. BIS 30 at REMI 0 = 9/1, and 10/0 at REMI 2/4/6.

## Covariates
None modelled. Randomised design; patient characteristics said to be similar between groups (data not shown).

## Caveats
Opioid exposure is a predicted effect-site target. Stimulus was a single standardised tetanus combined with the OAA/S sequence, in awake-to-deeply-sedated patients before surgery. Combined OAA/S plus tetanic response means "responder" mixes hypnotic and nociceptive arousal. Convenience sample of 10 per group. Authors conclude CVI is an arousability index, not an independent nociception index, and that it is strongly influenced by hypnotic depth. Funding included Covidien research support; one author was a Covidien employee.
