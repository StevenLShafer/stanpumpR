# Bamgboye 2026: population PK of IV topiramate in patients

## Study
- 20 adults on maintenance oral topiramate for epilepsy or migraine (13 F / 7 M, all White); age 39.8 ± 12.1 y
  (26-74); weight median 85.2 (54.5-150.3) kg, mean 92.3; height 169 cm; CrCL (Cockcroft-Gault) median 102.5
  (46-206) mL/min in Table 1 (107.6 in the Results text).
- 7 (35%) on enzyme inducers (4 carbamazepine, 1 phenytoin, 1 oxcarbazepine, 1 carbamazepine + phenytoin).
- Single 25 mg **13C stable-isotope-labelled** IV topiramate (10 mg/mL in 10% Captisol) over 10 min with the usual
  morning oral dose. Samples predose, 5, 15, 30 min, 1, 2, 4, 6, 12, 24, 48, 72, 96 h; 246 concentrations.
- LC-MS (separate m/z for oral, labelled IV, internal standard). Plasma, total.
- NONMEM 7.5.1, FOCEI; 1-, 2-, 3-compartment linear models; allometric weight fixed (0.75 clearances, 1 volumes);
  stepwise covariates (age, sex, inducer, CrCL); bootstrap n = 1000 (925 converged); pcVPC.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No therapeutic range or effect model.

## Covariates
Weight (fixed allometry); enzyme-inducer status ×1.63 on CL. Age, sex and CrCL not significant (CrCL near
normal; authors caution about renal impairment).

## Caveats
n = 20; single 25 mg dose on top of oral therapy (oral and IV assayed separately); V3 CI very wide; Q3 poorly
estimated; non-stratified bootstrap; author (Cloyd) holds royalties on injectable topiramate.
