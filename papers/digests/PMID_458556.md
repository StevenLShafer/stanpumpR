# Kramer 1979: digoxin serum kinetics and systolic-time-interval response after 1 mg IV

## Study
- 12 healthy male volunteers; mean weight 72.4 kg (SD 7.9), BSA 1.93 m² (0.11) (Table II footnote a, PDF p10 / printed p56). Age not reported.
- Digoxin (Lanoxin injection) 1.0 mg rapid IV bolus (<30 s); fasting 12 h before to 4 h after (PDF p3 / printed p49).
- Serum sampling 2 min to 96 h (23 samples); systolic time intervals (ECG, phonocardiogram, carotid pulse) at 10 min to 96 h (PDF p4 / printed p50).
- End point: |ΔQS2I|, change in electromechanical systole corrected for heart rate (a noninvasive inotropy index), corrected for diurnal variation at 4, 8 and 12 h using untreated controls (−3.4, −10.1, −14.9 ms).
- Assay: ¹²⁵I radioimmunoassay (Schwarz/Mann), modified; extra 0.2 ng/mL standard; each subject's blank serum used for the standard curve.
- Analysis: the 12 subjects' data were AVERAGED and fitted as one data set (no individual fits); three-compartment mammillary model (elimination from central), NONLIN, weighted by reciprocal variance. A second fit modelled serum and response simultaneously.

Table I (PDF p6, printed p52; confirmed on the page image — the text layer misaligns the ΔQS2I column): mean serum digoxin (SD) e.g. 63.8 (30.0) ng/mL at 2 min, 8.57 (2.21) at 30 min, 2.79 (0.76) at 2 h, 0.86 (0.37) at 8 h, 0.58 (0.16) at 24 h, 0.29 (0.12) at 48 h, 0.22 (0.20) at 96 h. Mean |ΔQS2I| 14.0 ms at 10 min, rising to a maximum 27.9 (9.2) ms at 180 min, 26.7 at 480 min, then 20.6 at 24 h, 21.8 at 48 h, 16.8 at 72 h, 11.9 at 96 h.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: |ΔQS2I| (ms), shortening of heart-rate-corrected electromechanical systole, taken as inotropy.
- No ke0, t½ke0 or tPeak is reported. Instead the effect is linked to the amount in the deep compartment (compartment 2, % of dose): linear fit (serum-only kinetics) slope 0.271 ms per % dose, intercept 10.9 ms, r 0.91 (printed p53); the preferred form is eq. 5, R = Rmax·K·A2/(1 + K·A2), with Rmax 37.8 ms and K 0.035 (%dose)⁻¹ (Table II). Simultaneous fit r 0.970 (printed p57).
- Derived: the deep compartment's return rate constant k21 0.058-0.060 /h corresponds to a half-time of about 11.6-12 h, the timescale of the hysteresis; the observed mean response peaks at about 3 h and persists to 96 h (Table I).
- Not an opioid; no MEAC, no potency ratio.

## Covariates
None (averaged data, one healthy-male group).

## Caveats
- Naive-averaged data from 12 subjects; the authors say the relationship was not shown in individuals and that the narrow ΔQS2I range cannot separate linear from nonlinear forms.
- RIA may cross-react with digoxin metabolites (authors' caveat).
- SDs in Table II are parameter standard errors of the averaged-data fit labelled "standard deviations"; K's SD (0.033) nearly equals its value.
- Healthy young-ish men, renal function not reported; age not reported.
- The authors warn the deep-compartment link need not mean the receptor is in that compartment.

## Verification against stanpumpR
- **Description of the data (header lines 59-63): matches.** 1 mg IV bolus, 12 healthy men, end point shortening of electromechanical systole (ΔQS2I) — all as in the paper (PDF p1, p3).
- **13 h equilibration half-time: cannot verify from this paper.** Kramer 1979 reports no ke0; the 13 h is Weiss and Kang's refit. It is plausible against this paper's own deep-compartment rate constant (derived t½ of k21 ≈ 12 h), but that is not the same quantity. See open question.
- **Independent comparison with the library's IV model (Cheng 1997, `R/drugs_digoxinIV.R:131-137`), not cited as such:** Cheng at CLcr 100-140 mL/min gives CL 0.0976-0.110 L/h/kg = 1.63-1.83 mL/min/kg (derived) and Vss 6.4-6.9 L/kg; Kramer's healthy men had CL 3.56-3.66 mL/min/kg (about 2x higher) and Vss 8.0-9.6 L/kg, terminal t½ 36-42 h against the header's 46 h for Cheng's system. Direction: Kramer's clearance is higher. Differences in assay (RIA vs Cheng's fluorescence polarization), naive-averaging, and population (healthy men vs patients with a range of renal function) may account for part; the model's header does not anticipate this comparison. Not a discrepancy in the code, since the code does not claim Kramer's values.
- The tPeak in the code (87 min, `R/drugs_digoxinIV.R:140`) is for rate control and is not from this paper; Kramer's |ΔQS2I| peaks at about 180 min and stays near-maximal to 8 h (Table I), consistent with the header's statement that inotropy is far slower.
- Units: Kramer reports ng/mL serum (= the library's ng units for digoxinIV, drugDefaults_global.csv:196); V1 in absolute litres (17.0 L, ≈0.23 L/kg derived) vs Cheng's 0.6 L/kg — Kramer's three-compartment central volume is smaller.
