# Severinghaus 1979: blood O2 dissociation equations

## Study
A methods note: empirical equations for the human whole-blood O2 dissociation curve at 37 C, pH 7.4, fitted to the "standard" curve of Roughton and Severinghaus 1973 (ref 6, Table 2 columns 1-2, interpolated to Table 1). No subjects, no assay.

## Model and parameters
Symbols: S fractional saturation; PO2 in Torr at 37 C; C content in mL O2 (STPD) per mL blood; P50 the PO2 at S = 0.5, pH 7.4.

- Eq 1 (S from PO2, PDF p1, printed p599): S = ( ( (PO2^3 + 150 PO2)^-1 x 23,400 ) + 1 )^-1. Greatest error +0.55% saturation at 98.77%; mean absolute error 0.26% saturation (less than half the Adair equation's); P50 by Eq 1 is 26.86 against 26.67 in the standard curve. Abstract: within +-0.0055 fractional saturation over 0 < S < 1.
- Eq 2 (PO2 from S, valid S < 0.965): ln PO2 = 0.385 ln (S^-1 - 1)^-1 + 3.32 - (72 S)^-1 - 0.17 (S^6) (abstract; body prints (S^6)/6). Mean absolute error 0.16 Torr, maximum 0.6 Torr; 37 Torr below the standard at S = 0.99. Eqs 1 and 2 are not exact inverses, so they must not be chained in an iteration (p602).
- Eq 3 temperature coefficient: dlnPO2/dT = 0.058((0.243 x PO2/100)^3.88 + 1)^-1 + 0.013 (per C; 7.4%/C at low saturation, 1.3%/C at high PO2, p600).
- Eq 4 Bohr: PO2(7.4) = PO2 x e^(1.1 (pH - 7.4)); the abstract gives dlnPO2/dpH = (PO2/26.6)^0.184 - 2.2; Eq 5 adds +0.003 BE with (PO2/26.7).
- Eq 6 content: C = 0.0134 x Hb x S + 0.000031 x PO2 (37 C), C in mL/mL; the paper does not state the Hb unit; 0.0134 mL/mL per unit Hb implies g/dL (derived: 1.34 mL O2/g, 0.0031 mL/dL/Torr).
- Eq 7 iteration for A-V content differences; Eq 8 P50 = 26.7 x PO2(obs)/PO2(std).

Table 1 (p600) lists the standard curve, e.g. PO2 26 Torr 48.27%, 100 Torr 97.49%, 150 Torr 98.77%; Eq 1 at 100 Torr gives 0.9775 (derived).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Not applicable (no drug). Not an opioid; no MEAC, ke0 or potency ratio.

## Covariates
Temperature (Eq 3) and pH/base excess (Eqs 4-5) shift PO2 at a given saturation; Eq 1 itself holds only at 37 C, pH 7.4, normal 2,3-DPG. The author notes little experimental verification of the temperature correction (p600).

## Caveats
Curve for normal adult blood; PO2 above about 150 Torr is extrapolation of the standard curve where S is near 1 and Eq 1 reads slightly low (error under 0.006 S). Eq 2 breaks down above S = 0.965.
