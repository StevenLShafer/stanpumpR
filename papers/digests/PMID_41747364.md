# Hodge 2026: sugammadex and rocuronium popPK at caesarean, and breast milk

## Study
- **Design:** prospective observational study at the Royal Brisbane and Women's Hospital, 2022-2024 (ACTRN12621001449820).
  The target was 15; recruitment stopped at 10.
- **Inclusion:** elective caesarean delivery under general anaesthesia, at term.
- **Exclusions:** renal impairment (serum creatinine > 70 µmol/L), liver impairment, and drugs that displace
  rocuronium from the complex.
- **Patients** (Table 1, p3):

| Characteristic | Value |
|---|---|
| Age | 30.4 ± 6.3 y |
| Total body weight | median 78.0 [IQR 68.5, 99.8] kg |
| Lean body weight | 44.4 [40.1, 49.2] kg |
| BMI | 28.9 [26.7, 37.7] kg/m²; 3 with BMI > 40 |
| Gestation | 37.7 [35.5, 38.3] wk |
| ASA | 3 in 9 women |
| eGFR | > 90 [> 90, > 90] mL/min/1.73 m² |
| Albumin | 27 [26, 30] g/L |

  Serum creatinine itself is not tabulated. Indications for GA were mostly CNS or spinal pathology (6).
- **Doses:**
  - Rocuronium at induction, by anaesthetist preference: 1.47-2.30 mg/kg LBW, 0.73-1.46 mg/kg TBW, median 2.02
    mg/kg LBW. One woman had two extra doses.
  - Sugammadex 200 mg in every woman (2.56 [2.02, 2.93] mg/kg TBW). One woman had a second 200 mg in the PACU.
- **Sampling:**
  - Blood (venous or arterial): before induction, then 5, 10, 15, 20, 30 and 60 min, 6 h and 24 h after
    sugammadex. That gave 66 rocuronium and 65 sugammadex concentrations.
  - Urine to 6 h. Breast milk at 1 and 6 h.
- **Assay:** UHPLC-MS/MS of total plasma rocuronium and sugammadex. Sugammadex LLOQ 1 mg/L (0.46 µM). Five
  sugammadex samples were below the LLOQ and handled by the M4 method.
- **Model:** Monolix 2024R1 (SAEM), built on the Ploeger 2009 and Kleijn 2011 structure for free rocuronium, free
  sugammadex and the complex. Dosing simulations were run in Simulx.

## Model and parameters
- **Structure:** sugammadex and the complex are two-compartment with first-order elimination. Rocuronium is
  three-compartment.
- **Covariates:** total body weight on sugammadex CL and V1 and on rocuronium V1, as a power of TBW/78 (p3).
- **Complex kinetics:** Kd and koff were fixed from the literature (koff from Kleijn).
- Table 2 (p4) gives population estimates (RSE%) with the bootstrap median and 95% CI.

- **Supplement (Appendix A-B; supplement, 2026-10-27):**
  - Complexation occurs in the central compartment only, and the complex is given sugammadex's PK (its own
    V1S, V2S, kel,S; Figure S1). So Table 2's sugammadex V1, V2, Q and CL describe both free sugammadex and the
    complex.
  - The measured plasma concentration of each drug is modelled as free drug plus complex, in µM (molecular
    weights above). So the fitted CL and V apply to total sugammadex, as the library's Kleijn model does.
  - Kd 0.0559 µM and koff 2.04 h⁻¹ fixed (from Kleijn), as in Table 2.
  - IIV only on rocuronium CL and V1; combined error for rocuronium, proportional for sugammadex.
  - Table S1: the selected structure is the shaded 3-cmt rocuronium / 2-cmt sugammadex row (OFV 170.56). A
    third row, also labelled 3 CMT / 2 CMT (OFV 166.25, BICc 248.59), is not explained.
- **The abstract's "volume of distribution" values are total volumes (derived):**
  - Sugammadex: 13.9 L = V1 + V2 = 6.82 + 7.04 = 13.86.
  - Rocuronium: 17.9 L = 9.21 + 5.91 + 2.80 = 17.92.
- **Sugammadex IIV:** could not be estimated after adding TBW (high RSE and shrinkage), so it was dropped.
- **Breast milk:**
  - 14 of 16 samples were undetectable and 2 were under 1 mg/L (both from the 45-kg woman).
  - Milk/plasma ratio and relative infant dose were not reportable.
- **Simulated rocuronium Cmax** (Table 3, median, µM): 1.2 mg/kg LBW 10.6; 2 mg/kg LBW 17.7; 1.2 mg/kg TBW 19.3;
  2 mg/kg TBW 32.2.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- Train-of-four ratio was 0.40 [0.02, 0.64] before sugammadex, 0.95 after, and 1.00 in the PACU.
- There were too few TOF measurements for a PD model, so no PD parameters are given.

## Covariates
Total body weight only (equations above, centred on the median 78 kg). LBW, renal function and other covariates
were tested and not retained. **Supplement (2026-10-27):** Appendix A lists the covariates explored (age, total
and lean body weight, sex, BMI, serum creatinine, eGFR, haemoglobin, ALT, AST, GGT, ALP, bilirubin, albumin):
power functions about the median for continuous covariates; forward inclusion at dOFV > 3.84, backward
elimination at > 10.8. Table S2 (PDF p4-5): weight on V1S -8.66, then V1R -20.38, then CLS -11.78 (final, OFV
129.74); eGFR on CLS added -3.34 (not significant, not retained). Backward: removing weight on V1S +45.74,
on V1R +20.87, on CLS +11.78. No eGFR or creatinine term is in the final model; with eGFR > 90 in every woman
(Table 1) the data had almost no renal range.

## Caveats
- **No control group.** "Upper end of published ranges" is a comparison with other studies.
- **Sample:** n = 10 (not 15), mostly ASA 3, elective only. Mixed arterial and venous samples.
- **Sampling gap:** none between 1 and 6 h. Fig. 1 plots data only to about 7-9 h after rocuronium, although 24-h
  samples are listed (8 at T24 h). Supplement Figure S4 (individual fits, PDF p8) also stops at about 6.5-9.5 h
  after rocuronium, and subjects 3 and 5 have data to only about 2-2.5 h.
- **Rocuronium Q2:** RSE 3.97%, but a bootstrap CI of 0.47-149, so it is poorly identified.
- **Fixed values:** Kd and koff were fixed. Doses and IIV are as above.
- **Parity wording:** the text says "four (40%) were nulliparous"; Table 1 lists "Primiparous, n 4 (40%)".
- **Renal function:** the exclusion of creatinine > 70 µmol/L (0.79 mg/dL) means every woman had a low-normal
  creatinine.
