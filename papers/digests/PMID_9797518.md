# Mortier 1998: closed-loop propofol with BIS

## Study
Ten ASA I-II adults (Table 2, PDF p3 / printed p751: age 48.7 +/- 7.0 y, 4 M / 6 F, weight 78.1 +/- 12.2 kg, height 173.1 +/- 10.6 cm) had orthopaedic lower-limb surgery under spinal block (0.5% bupivacaine, L3-L4), oral diazepam 10 mg premedication, spontaneous breathing. Propofol was given by a closed loop with BIS (Aspect A-1000, 4-s epochs) as controlled variable; sedation goal OAA/S <= 1. Actuator: Graseby 3400 syringe pump driven by a PC. Induction: effect-site-targeted TCI through Stanpump (three-compartment propofol model plus effect compartment), target Ce started at 2 ug/mL, +0.5 ug/mL each minute until OAA/S <= 1; the BIS at that moment became the individual set point and the loop closed. The controller computes a Ce which Stanpump converts to infusion rates. The paper gives no PK or PD parameter values and does not name the PK set used inside Stanpump.

## Model and parameters
No parameter table. Results (text, PDF p3): time to OAA/S <= 1 250.7 (59.8) s; BIS then 63.9 (6.2); loop closed at 318.7 (51.3) s and stayed closed 28.8 (13.3) min; recovery after stopping 8.3 (3.1) min; BIS at OAA/S >= 2 was 76.0 (7.5); induction dose 1.5 (0.4) mg/kg; maintenance 5.2 (2.7) mg/kg/h (all mean (SD)). Two patients apnoeic at induction, no manual ventilation needed, SpO2 never below 90%. Some patients moved (Table 3), no awareness or recall.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point is BIS (hypnotic effect), with OAA/S for the sedation target. No ke0, t1/2 ke0, tPeak, EC50 or hysteresis value is reported; no concentration-effect model is fitted. The calculated plasma and effect-site concentrations are plotted (Figs 1-2) but not tabulated. Not an opioid paper: no MEAC or potency ratio.

## Covariates
None analysed.

## Caveats
n = 10, spinal anaesthesia (chosen to avoid analgesic influence on BIS), sedation not surgical anaesthesia. Concentrations are model-calculated, not measured. Pk set and ke0 inside Stanpump not stated.
