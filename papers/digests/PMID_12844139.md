# Yang 2003: anakinra PK in renal impairment and dialysis

## Study
Three open-label single-dose studies by Amgen (anakinra = recombinant human IL-1 receptor antagonist, 17.3 kDa), PDF pp. 2-3.
- Study A (PDF p. 3): 12 healthy men, 3-period crossover of three production sources; 1 mg/kg IV over 1 min; samples 0.02-24 h. Only the source used in study B is reported.
- Study B: 20 ESRD subjects (10 haemodialysis, 10 CAPD), 1 mg/kg IV over 1 min; samples to 96 h; dialysate, urine and dialyzer arterial/venous lines sampled.
- Study C: 30 men and women in 5 groups of 6 by creatinine clearance (CLcr): normal >80 mL/min, mild 50-80, moderate 30-49, severe <30, ESRD on HD. Single 100 mg SC dose; samples to 48 h, to 72 h (moderate) or 96 h (severe, ESRD). CLcr from a 24 h urine collection before dosing, normalised to 1.73 m2.
- Assay: antibody-capture ELISA on EDTA plasma, LLOQ 1.0 (A), 0.94 (B), 20 (C) ng/mL; precision 2.7-21.0% within-assay, accuracy 94-104%.
- Analysis: noncompartmental (WinNonlin 3.1) for all; SAAM II 1.1.2 compartmental fit only to estimate Vc after IV (3 compartments in healthy, 2 in ESRD).
- Baseline (Tables I-II, medians): healthy IV group age 27 y, 82 kg; HD 67 y, 72 kg; CAPD 52 y, 71 kg. SC groups 46-68 y, 72-92 kg; CLcr 95 / 65 / 41 / 16 mL/min (normal to severe).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None measured. No effect, IL-1, CRP or clinical end point; no ke0, tPeak or hysteresis; no therapeutic concentration. The discussion cites a dose-response (ACR20 plateau at 1-2 mg/kg, ref 6) and preclinical data that time above a threshold matters more than Cmax or AUC (ref 19), without giving a concentration. Not an opioid; no MEAC or potency ratio.

## Covariates
Creatinine clearance on CL/F (SC study): log(CL/F) = 1.65 + 0.0062 x CLcr, r2 = 0.718, regression on the 24 non-ESRD subjects (Fig 3, PDF p. 7); ESRD drawn at an assumed CLcr of 2 mL/min for the plot only. A nonrenal clearance remains at CLcr = 0 (discussion, PDF p. 8). Volumes were similar in ESRD and healthy subjects (P .06-.86). Dialysis (HD or CAPD) does not change clearance appreciably.

## Caveats
- n = 6 per renal group; the SC groups differ in age (46-68 y median) and sex mix.
- Only CL/F and half-life exist after SC; no V/F is reported, and the flip-flop makes SC half-lives uninformative about clearance.
- IV (healthy, study A) and SC (study C) are different subjects: CL/F 170 vs CL 137 mL/min gives an implied F near 0.8 (derived, cross-study; the label's 95% is a separate measurement).
- Concentrations below the LLOQ were set to zero; the ELISA had large within-assay imprecision (up to 21%); the HD extraction ratio was negative.
- Whether study C's normal-function group is "healthy" is not stated; they are enrolled as men and women grouped by CLcr.
- Authors are Amgen employees and shareholders; Amgen funded the work.
