# de Jonge 2005: phenytoin induces cyclophosphamide and thiotepa metabolism (case report)

## Study
Single-patient case report (short communication, PDF pp. 1-4; printed 507-510). 42-year-old man, 167 cm, 80 kg, relapsing
non-seminomatous germ-cell cancer, given two 4-day CTC high-dose courses (cyclophosphamide 1,500 mg/m2/day as a 1-h
infusion, carboplatin by Calvert to AUC 5 mg/min/mL/day, thiotepa 120 mg/m2/day as two 30-min infusions 12 h apart),
courses 28 days apart. Phenytoin 150 mg twice daily orally was started 5 days before course 2 (seizures); course 1 had none.
Sampling on day 1 of each course (before, and at 30, 60, 90, 120, 150, 180, 210, 280, 390, 660 min). Plasma
cyclophosphamide (CP), 4-hydroxycyclophosphamide (4OHCP), thiotepa, tepa by LC-MS/MS; carboplatin by atomic absorption.
AUCs by Bayesian analysis with the authors' published integrated popPK model (de Jonge 2004). Fig. 1 plots the day-1 curves
(CP 2,835 mg, thiotepa 115 mg per dose in the caption).

Derived (mine): CP AUC ratio 2,194/6,637 = 0.33, so apparent CP clearance about 3.0x higher on phenytoin if the dose
was the same and kinetics linear (day-1 CP dose 2,835 mg both courses is implied by Fig. 1; not stated for course 1
in the text). The paper does not give a clearance value. The text also says "133% vs 126%" for the increased rate of
metabolite formation; what these percentages are is not defined (PDF p. 2).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No pharmacodynamics, no ke0, no MEAC (not an opioid). Toxicity: grade I mucositis and diarrhoea in both courses; the dose
cut on days 3-4 was aimed at a predefined target exposure (median of a reference population) and kept 4OHCP and tepa
in the "predefined therapeutic window" (data not shown). Phenytoin plasma 4-5.5 mg/L (optimum 8-18 mg/L stated), so the
induction was seen at a subtherapeutic phenytoin level.

## Covariates
Co-medication (phenytoin) is the only factor examined. The authors cite Slattery 1996 for CP clearance 112% higher and
CP half-life 54% lower in transplant patients on phenytoin with busulfan, against patients given CP with radiotherapy
(a different comparison group, not the same patient).

## Caveats
n = 1; interaction size "not predictable" per the authors; course 2 was 4 weeks after course 1, so any residual
CP/thiotepa autoinduction would also differ between courses; phenytoin levels subtherapeutic; Bayesian AUCs rely on a
model that itself contains CP-thiotepa interaction and autoinduction.
