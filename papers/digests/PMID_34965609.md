# Lu 2022: cetagliptin multiple oral doses, healthy volunteers

## Study
Single-centre, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled study with an open-label sitagliptin positive control, incremental dose, Nanjing (Methods 2.1-2.2, PDF p2-3). 48 healthy subjects (30 cetagliptin, 9 placebo, 9 sitagliptin 100 mg); median age 25 (19-39) y, weight 62.05 (47.5-81.5) kg, BMI 21.97 (Results 3.1, PDF p5). Cohorts A/B/C: 10 cetagliptin + 3 placebo + 3 sitagliptin each; cetagliptin 50, 100, 200 mg once daily for 7 days with 240 mL water, fasting on days 1 and 7. PK samples (plasma, EDTA): pre-dose and 0.5-24 h on day 1, pre-dose days 5-6, to 120 h on day 7. LC-MS/MS, range 0.5-5000 ng/mL. Non-compartmental analysis, Phoenix WinNonlin 8.0. No population model.

Sitagliptin 100 mg (comparator): day 1 AUC0-24 2900, Cmax 407; day 7 AUC0-24,ss 3010, Cmax,ss 391 (Table 1). Dose proportionality (Table 2, p6): estimated ratios 1.077-1.344 against a criterion interval of 0.839-1.161, so exposure rose more than in proportion to dose. Steady state reached by about day 5 on trough concentrations (Fig 2).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: plasma DPP-4 enzyme activity, % inhibition from baseline (fluorescence assay). Emax fit (Fig 5, text p8): EC50 5.29 ng/mL for cetagliptin (sitagliptin 7.03 ng/mL); 80% inhibition at 34.87 ng/mL (sitagliptin 48.79). Plasma concentration, total (free not stated). Day-7 inhibition was above 80% over 24 h at all doses: Rmax,ss 89.4/90.4/91.4% and E24h,ss 78.8/82.7/88.1% for 50/100/200 mg (Table 3, p6). Active GLP-1 AUEC0-24 rose 2.3-3.1x (day 1) and 3.1-3.6x (day 7) against placebo (text p8; Table 4). Glucose and insulin in Tables 5-6. No ke0, hysteresis or tPeak reported. Not an opioid: no MEAC or potency ratio.

## Covariates
None analysed. Healthy normoglycaemic adults only.

## Caveats
Small n (10 per dose), healthy volunteers, no population model, nonlinear PK (more than dose-proportional). Table 1 row labelling questionable (open question). Authors note the sample may be too small to show glucose differences.
