# Wilens 2002: population PK of fluoxetine in children and adolescents

## Study
- 21 outpatients (11 boys, 10 girls), age 12.6 +/- 3.0 y (range 6-17): 10 children (10.0 +/- 1.9 y; 39.9 +/- 12.8 kg; 142.1 cm) and 11 adolescents (15 +/- 1.2 y; 67.3 +/- 16.1 kg; 162.9 cm). Major depression (18), OCD (2), both (1). Five also took methylphenidate.
- Fluoxetine 20 mg once daily, fixed, for 58-62 days (open label, after 2-28 day washout).
- Sparse sampling: at least 6 samples 8-12 h after dose, up to 4 optional; 168 concentrations in total.
- Assay: LC/MS (Lilly), plasma, fluoxetine and norfluoxetine, range 1.0-500 ng/mL.
- Model: NONMEM V, ADVAN2 TRANS2, FO / FOCE; only fluoxetine modelled (norfluoxetine described, not modelled).

## Model and parameters
One compartment, first-order absorption (ka fixed); two compartments rejected. Base model (no covariates): CL/F 11.8 L/h, V/F 1480 L. Final model (Table 2):
```yaml
parameters:
  - {name: ka, value: 0.666, unit: 1/h, source: "Table 2", note: fixed}
  - {name: CL/F, value: 0.181, unit: L/h/kg, source: "Table 2", note: "theta1 x weight; %SEE 12.6; IIV 52%"}
  - {name: V/F, value: 37.4, unit: L/kg, source: "Table 2", note: "theta2 x weight; %SEE 5.56; IIV 20.5%"}
  - {name: residual, value: 18, unit: "% proportional", source: "Table 2", note: "%SEE 22"}
```
Derived (not in paper): t1/2 = 0.693 x 37.4 / 0.181 = 143 h (about 6 days), weight-independent in this linear form.

Observed steady state (after about 4 weeks; Table 1, mean, SD, range): fluoxetine all 127 ng/mL (75.0; 28-312), children 171, adolescents 86.3; norfluoxetine all 152 (76.3; 48-310), children 195 (89.2), adolescents 113 (30.4). Abstract gives norfluoxetine 151. Weight-normalised values, printed as ng/mL with the normalisation not defined: fluoxetine 292 vs 257 and norfluoxetine 331 vs 373 (child vs adolescent).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. Authors state no concentration-efficacy relationship is established for fluoxetine. No ke0, no therapeutic range proposed.

## Covariates
Weight (linear, on both CL/F and V/F) retained; age, BMI, BSA also significant individually; sex not significant; age + weight model did not converge. CYP2D6 not genotyped.

## Caveats
- n = 21, sparse trough-region sampling, ka fixed, norfluoxetine not modelled; fluoxetine's time-dependent (CYP2D6 autoinhibition) kinetics folded into apparent values.
- Unexplained IIV in CL/F about 50%.
- Second-to-last author's name printed "Faird" in PubMed; the paper prints "FAIRD".
