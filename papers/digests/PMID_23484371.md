# Sakata 2013: MIC and MBC of parenteral antibiotics for paediatric invasive pneumococci (Japanese)

## Study
In vitro susceptibility survey, in Japanese with an English abstract (PDF p5). 76 *Streptococcus pneumoniae*
isolates from children with invasive pneumococcal disease (IPD) seen at Asahikawa Kosei Hospital and other
paediatric units in Hokkaido, January 2006 to December 2011 (p1). 73 isolates from blood, 3 from CSF (p1–2).
Patients 4 months to 6 years; 60 (79.1%) aged 1 year or younger. Diagnoses: occult bacteraemia 38 (50.0%),
pneumonia 34 (44.7%), meningitis 3 (3.9%), sepsis 1 (1.3%); one death (sepsis) (p1–2).

Methods (p1): MIC by broth microdilution per the Japanese Society of Chemotherapy standard method, for
penicillin G, ampicillin, cefotaxime, ceftriaxone, panipenem, meropenem, doripenem and vancomycin. MBC: 10 µL from
wells at or above the MIC plated on non-selective agar, 35 °C aerobic for 20–24 h; MBC is the concentration
reducing the inoculum by at least 99.9%. Penicillin category by PCG MIC: PRSP ≥ 2 µg/mL, PSSP < 0.1 µg/mL.
PBP genotype by PCR kit. Serotypes by Statens Serum Institute antisera.

Genotype (p2): gPSSP 9 (11.8%), gPISP 35 (46.1%), gPRSP 32 (42.1%), so the collection is about 88%
penicillin-nonsusceptible by genotype. By MIC: PSSP 35 (46.1%), PISP 22 (28.9%), PRSP 19 (25.0%).
Commonest serotypes 6B 20, 19F 13, 14 9, 23F 8 (Fig 1, p2); PCV7 serotypes 55 (72.4%).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point is in vitro: growth inhibition (MIC) and ≥99.9% kill (MBC). The discussion (p4) states that a CSF
concentration of at least 10 times the organism's MBC is considered necessary in meningitis (no source data
in this paper). No PK, no target attainment.

## Covariates
None (in vitro). Vaccine serotypes were more often resistant than non-vaccine serotypes (p < 0.05, p2).

## Caveats
Single region (Hokkaido), small n (76), paediatric invasive isolates heavily enriched for penicillin-nonsusceptible
genotypes, so MBCs are high relative to an unselected population. Japanese standard microdilution method, not CLSI.
MIC50/MBC50 not reported; histogram readings above are approximate.
