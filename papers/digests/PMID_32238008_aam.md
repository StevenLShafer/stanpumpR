# Schoretsanitis 2020: antidepressant concentrations in the third trimester, systematic review and meta-analysis

## Study
PRISMA systematic review (Embase, Medline, August 2019) plus one unpublished case from the Trondheim TDM database
(PDF pp3-5). Inclusion: within-subject design, plasma/serum antidepressant levels in the third trimester (week 26
onward) and at baseline (before pregnancy or more than 4 weeks after delivery), steady state, trough or presumed
trough. 169 records, 14 full texts, 11 studies included, 11 antidepressants.
- Primary outcome: **alteration ratio** = dose-adjusted concentration (ng/mL per mg/day) in the 3rd trimester
  divided by baseline; a mean of individual ratios (individual-participant style), or a sample-size-weighted mean
  of cohort ratios where only cohort data existed (PDF p4).
- Secondary: random-effects (DerSimonian-Laird) mean difference (MD) of dose-adjusted concentrations, baseline
  minus 3rd trimester (positive = lower in pregnancy), R `meta`; only with >=3 studies. AUCs divided by the
  interval used as a proxy for trough.
- Raw data of the largest cohort (Westin 2017, ref [7]) supplied by co-author AA Westin (PDF p3).
- Quality: ClinPK checklist, mean score 13.7.
- Active moiety (parent + active metabolite) for clomipramine, imipramine, fluoxetine and venlafaxine (Table 1
  footnote); citalopram and fluoxetine enantiomers summed where reported separately.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD. Reference ranges quoted from Hiemke 2018 (AGNP 2017): fluoxetine active moiety 120-500, fluvoxamine 60-230,
imipramine 175-300, nortriptyline 70-170, paroxetine 20-65, sertraline 10-150, venlafaxine active moiety 100-400,
clomipramine active moiety 230-450 ng/mL; trimipramine printed "150-300 mg/mL" (sic, PDF p10). Not an opioid paper.

## Covariates
None modelled. Discussion: CYP2D6 induction and CYP2C19/CYP1A2 decrease in pregnancy; citalopram fall
attributed largely to inactive R-citalopram (two patients with enantiomer data, ref [6]); paroxetine direction
CYP2D6-genotype dependent (Ververs 2009, not pooled).

## Caveats
- Not independent of Westin 2017: Westin is an author and supplied that cohort's raw data; [7] contributes to every
  SSRI/venlafaxine pooled value.
- Combined ratios are means of individual ratios (skewed by large ratios); inverse-variance MDs can disagree
  (sertraline: ratio 1.38, MD null).
- Table 1 n is cohort size; included patients can be fewer (footnote). Escitalopram n is 13 in the text but cohorts
  sum to 14.
- Sertraline 1.38 cannot be reproduced from the cohort means weighted by the printed n (8, 6, 12 gives 1.26;
  derived), presumably because of individual-patient pooling with missing values; citalopram 0.70 does reproduce
  (0.58x11 + 1.23x2 + 0.73x12)/25 = 0.70 (derived).
- Trough timing unstandardised (8-30 h in one study); adherence not assessed; co-medication in some cases; small n.
- Accepted manuscript; numbers from the PMC version only.
