# Alvarez 2022: HCQ and three metabolites, COVID-19 population PK and PK/PD

## Study
- Retrospective, single-centre (Garches, France) study; 100 hospitalised COVID-19 patients (ICU and medicine wards), 34 F / 66 M. Age 60.7 ± 15.9 y, weight 83.6 ± 20.1 kg, BMI 28.9 ± 5.4 (Results 2.1, PDF p3). Table 1 is scrambled in the text layer; not re-read as an image.
- Dosing (Methods 4.1, PDF p14-15): HCQ (Plaquenil 200 mg) 200 mg bid or tid, optionally a 400 mg bid loading dose on day 1; some patients also had azithromycin 250 mg bid on day 1 then 250 mg qd. Route oral.
- Sampling about every 2 days when possible; 333 whole-blood concentrations of HCQ and metabolites (1-9 per patient). Assay: validated turbulent-flow LC-MS, **whole blood** (lithium heparin), HCQ-D4 internal standard (Methods 4.2).
- Analysis: NONMEM 7.4.1, FOCE-I, ADVAN5/TRANS1, Wings for NONMEM, R 4.0.3; concentrations modelled in µmol/L, converted to µg/L for graphics; exponential IIV; bootstrap n = 500; VPC 500 replicates.

## Model and parameters
Final model (Table 2, PDF p6, read on the rendered page): HCQ with first-order absorption and lag, one clearance for the parent, and three metabolite compartments (DesCQ, DesHCQ, DiDesCQ) each fed by a clearance from HCQ and eliminated by its own clearance. Each metabolite volume is fixed to the parent's VP/F. Lag and KA were not estimable and were fixed to the published values of Lim 2009 (ref 16) after a sensitivity analysis. IIV on CL/F and VP/F of HCQ and on each metabolite CL. Residual error proportional plus additive. No covariate (age, weight, height, BMI, sex, azithromycin, ICU) was significant. Shrinkage 37% (CL/F) and 4% (VP/F).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- Not an opioid; no MEAC, ke0, tPeak or equipotency applies. No effect-site model was fitted.
- PD end points were clinical, not concentration-effect curves: length of stay, death, and a composite poor outcome (death, ICU transfer, or stay >= 10 d). PD data were missing for 35 of the 100 patients (they had a single sample).
- Exposure measure: model-predicted whole-blood HCQ **trough at 48 h and 72 h** (BHCQ) from the final parameters.
- Results (abstract and 2.1.2, PDF p1, p9-12): length of stay vs BHCQ at 48 h r2 = 0.12, p = 0.0052; at 72 h r2 = 0.16, p = 0.0012. Outcome: good 369 ± 181 vs poor 285 ± 144 µg/L at 48 h (p = 0.0441); 407 ± 207 vs 311 ± 174 at 72 h (p = 0.0502). Deaths unrelated to trough (p = 0.972 and 0.836). Length of stay unrelated to HCQ clearance (p = 0.836).
- Threshold: with BHCQ > 200 µg/L, 87% (48 h) or 91% (72 h) of patients had stay < 25 d, versus 63% or 62% with BHCQ below 200 µg/L (abstract, Figure 7).
- Reference concentrations quoted from other work (Discussion, PDF p14): suggested therapeutic whole-blood range 500-2000 µg/L in SLE (ref 28); cardiac toxicity concern at > 2000 µg/L (Methods 4.2); in vitro SARS-CoV-2 EC50 values 241 to 1400 µg/L at 48 h and 72 h (refs 30, 31) and 5800 µg/L in one study (ref 32). Observed steady-state whole-blood SLE concentrations from ref 29: HCQ 1336 ± 621, DesHCQ 1037 ± 721, DesCQ 115 ± 54 µg/L. No therapeutic range for COVID-19 is defined by the authors.
- Simulation (Figure 8, PDF p13, text only): five regimens; all but 200 mg bid had under 25% of patients below 200 µg/L at 48 h or 72 h; 200 mg tid or a 400 mg bid day-1 loading dose is needed to exceed 200 µg/L at 48 h; with 400 mg bid some patients exceed 2000 µg/L.

## Covariates
None retained for any parameter (age, weight, height, BMI, sex, azithromycin co-administration, ICU vs ward tested by power model; p < 0.05 forward, p < 0.001 backward).

## Caveats
- Retrospective; samples mostly trough-like at irregular times; not at steady state. Absorption (KA, lag) fixed from literature, so absorption is not informed by these data.
- Whole-blood concentrations, so values are not interchangeable with plasma or serum values (the authors cite plasma being lower and more variable, refs 12, 13).
- Apparent parameters (CL/F, VP/F): oral bioavailability not estimated. VP/F = 1850 L is apparent and absolute, not per kg.
- PD relationships are weak (r2 0.12-0.16), rest on model-predicted troughs, and the outcome definition includes length of stay >= 10 d, which overlaps with the length-of-stay end point.
- Discussion quotes some blood concentrations in µg/mL where the Results use µg/L (PDF p14); read as µg/L.
