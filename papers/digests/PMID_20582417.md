# Bonate 2011: clofarabine and 6-ketoclofarabine population PK, adults and children

## Study
- Pooled five phase I/II studies; 1898 quantifiable clofarabine concentrations from 144 patients (91 adults, 53 children), age 2.8-81.5 y, weight 14.1-135.3 kg, 81 M / 63 F (PDF p.7, printed 881).
- Studies (Table 1, PDF p.10): ID99-383 (n=12), CLO-212 (n=22), CLO-222 (n=19), CLO-151 (n=68, adults, solid tumours, IV weekly 4-148 mg/m2 over 0.5-2 h), CLO-152 (n=23, adults, oral tablets daily x5, 1-5 mg/m2). External validation set CLO-221: 12 adults with AML, 40 mg/m2 over 1 h daily x5, 102 samples.
- Assay: plasma HPLC-MS/MS, linear range 1-500 ng/mL (clofarabine); 6-ketoclofarabine 0.1-20 ng/mL. Total plasma concentration.
- NONMEM VI, FOCE (hybrid: IOV on CL by first-order approximation), log-transform-both-sides residual error stratified by study. Bootstrap (1000, balanced IV/oral), jackknife, condition number 54, external validation.
- 6-Ketoclofarabine: non-compartmental only, 9 adults from CLO-151 at 103, 129, 148 mg/m2 (total dose 237-284 mg).

## Model and parameters
Two-compartment, linear elimination, first-order oral absorption (MAT = 1/ka). Weight allometry fixed a priori (0.75 on CL and Q, 1.0 on V) with a 70 kg reference (Eq. 5, PDF p.8). Age is scaled to 30 y, eCrCL to 90 mL/min/1.73 m2. Parameter units in Table 2 are **mL/h and mL** (not L/h and L). Residual: sigma1^2 = 0.0904 (31%), sigma2^2 = 0.163 (42%, study ID99-383).

Final model (Eq. 5, read from page image):
- CL = theta2 (Wt/70)^0.75 (Age/30)^theta7 exp(eta+kappa) if eCrCL missing; CL = theta9 (Wt/70)^0.75 (Age/30)^theta7 (eCrCL/90)^theta10 exp(eta+kappa) if eCrCL observed
- V2 = theta3 (Wt/70) (Age/30)^theta8 exp(eta+kappa)
- Q = theta4 (Wt/70)^0.75 exp(eta_Q); V3 = theta5 (Wt/70) exp(eta_Q x theta11 + kappa)
- F1 = theta6 exp(eta_F1); MAT = theta1

6-Ketoclofarabine (non-compartmental, PDF pp.11-14): peaks about 1.1 h after the start of infusion; mean AUC(0-last) 346 ng.h/mL (range 108-854); mean clofarabine AUC(0-6) 6238 ng.h/mL (2545-12693); metabolite ratio mean <5%, max 8%; apparent half-life 4.9 h (3.9-6.2); no accumulation (pre-dose below LLOQ on Days 8 and 15).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. This is a PK paper (no effect-site model, ke0, EC50 or concentration-effect relationship). It is not an opioid paper, so no MEAC/ke0/tPeak/potency ratio applies.

## Covariates
- Weight: fixed allometric (0.75 CL/Q, 1.0 V/V3). Age: on CL (exponent -0.181) and V2 (-0.560). eCrCL: on CL (exponent 0.533), capped at 200 mL/min/1.73 m2, by MDRD in adults (CLO-151/152) and Schwartz in children (PDF p.6).
- Tested and not retained: serum creatinine, albumin, race (Caucasian vs not), disease type, diabetes, sex. Weight was not screened.
- Simulations (PDF pp.10-11): AUC rises with falling eCrCL; only at moderate (30-60) or severe (<30 mL/min/1.73 m2) impairment does AUC rise by more than 20%; authors suggest about a 25% dose reduction at moderate impairment.
- Adults have higher exposure than children at the same BSA-normalised dose: comparator is children in this dataset.

## Caveats
- Few oral subjects (CLO-152 n=23): no BSV or IOV for F1.
- Paper says weight is the allometric covariate, but dosing was by BSA.
- Bootstrap bias about 25% for Corr(V2,Q) and about 17% for IOV V2; 92.7% of bootstrap runs minimised.
- Mean relative error in the external set was +47.1% (SD 281.2%), median 2.7%; the model missed the highest observed concentration.
- Table 2 is in mL/h and mL while Table 3 is in L/h and L.
- Table 3's per-kg CL for the 3-year-old differs from the abstract (see the open question).
- Adults are patients with advanced cancer; hepatic function was not a screened covariate (albumin was screened and not retained).
