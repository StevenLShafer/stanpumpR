# Sagent bumetanide injection US label (DailyMed), 2026

## Study
This is the generic Bumetanide Injection USP label from Sagent (ANDA 074441). It is the bumetanide injection
product the registry names, so there is no source file problem.
- **Description (p1):** 4 mL and 10 mL vials, 0.25 mg/mL, for IV or IM use. Each mL holds bumetanide 0.25 mg,
  NaCl 8.5 mg, ammonium acetate 4 mg, edetate disodium 0.1 mg and benzyl alcohol 10 mg, pH 6.8-7.8. MW 364.42.
- **Indication:** edema of heart failure and of hepatic and renal disease.
- **Dosage (p8):** start at 0.5-1 mg IV or IM, with IV given over 1-2 min. Repeat at 2-3 h intervals, at most
  10 mg/day. Switch to oral as soon as possible.

  within minutes, with maximum diuresis at 15-30 min.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- **Potency:** 1 mg ≈ 40 mg furosemide (p1). The ototoxicity warning (p4) says potency is about 40-60 times
  furosemide.
- IV diuresis peaks at 15-30 min. There are no concentration-effect data.

## Covariates
Age (neonates, infants and the elderly), as in the other bumetanide labels.

## Caveats
- A generic label with no adult IV clearance or volume.
