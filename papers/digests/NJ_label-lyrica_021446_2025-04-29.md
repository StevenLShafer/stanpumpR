# Lyrica (pregabalin) label (2025)

## Study
Not a study: US label for immediate-release pregabalin (capsules 25-300 mg; oral solution 20 mg/mL). The
12.3 statements summarise healthy-volunteer, renal-impairment and population PK studies.

## Model and parameters
- Absorption (12.3): fasting peak within 1.5 h; oral bioavailability >= 90%, independent of dose; Cmax and AUC
  linear over 25-300 mg single and 75-900 mg/day multiple doses; steady state within 24-48 h.
- Food: Cmax down about 25-30%, Tmax up to about 3 h, total absorption unchanged.
- Distribution: not bound to plasma proteins; apparent V about 0.5 L/kg; system L transporter substrate.
- Elimination: negligible metabolism; ~90% of dose in urine unchanged (N-methyl metabolite 0.9%); mean
  half-life 6.3 h with normal renal function ("about 6 hours" in the 12.3 opening); mean renal clearance
  67.0-80.9 mL/min in young healthy subjects (implies tubular reabsorption); elimination nearly proportional
  to CLcr.
- Haemodialysis: a 4 h session lowers plasma concentration about 50%.
- Pediatrics (3 mo to <17 y, n = 358): tmax 0.5-2 h fasted; CL/F and V/F rise with weight; t1/2 3-4 h up to
  6 y, 4-6 h from 7 y; CL/F nearly proportional to CLcr, as in adults; CL/F per kg ~40% higher under 30 kg.
- Renal dosing (2.7, Table 2; Cockcroft-Gault): total daily dose for CLcr >= 60 / 30-60 / 15-30 / <15 mL/min:
  150/75/25-50/25; 300/150/75/25-50; 450/225/100-150/50-75; 600/300/150/75 mg/day; supplemental dose after
  each 4 h haemodialysis.
- Indication doses: DPN max 300 mg/day; PHN 150-600; epilepsy adults 150-600, pediatrics 2.5-14 mg/kg/day;
  fibromyalgia 300-450; spinal-cord-injury pain 150-600.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No therapeutic, analgesic or toxic plasma concentration range anywhere in the label (searched the full text for
mcg/mL, mg/L and ng/mL: none for pregabalin). Respiratory depression warning (5.5) with CNS depressants
including opioids, and in respiratory impairment and the elderly. Schedule V. No ke0. Not an opioid.

## Covariates
CLcr (Cockcroft-Gault) the key covariate; age acts through CLcr; weight in children; sex and race no
clinically relevant effect.

## Caveats
Label figures are summary means without the full study detail.
