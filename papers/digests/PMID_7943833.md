# Gin 1994: isoflurane MAC is lower at 8-12 weeks of pregnancy

## Study
Prospective comparison, two groups of 10 ASA 1 women in Hong Kong (Prince of Wales Hospital). Pregnant group:
termination of pregnancy with laparoscopic sterilization at 10 (8-12) weeks by dates, fetus confirmed on pathology.
Control group: nonpregnant women for laparoscopic gynaecological surgery (negative pregnancy test, menses within
5 weeks). Exclusions: reflux symptoms, any medication including oral contraceptives. Demographics, median (range)
(PDF p2, Results): nonpregnant age 34.5 (31-45) yr, weight 54 (48-67) kg, height 1.62 (1.51-1.70) m; pregnant
35.5 (21-46) yr, 59 (41-65) kg, 1.60 (1.50-1.69) m.

No premedication; inhalational induction with isoflurane in oxygen (Magill circuit), tracheal intubation,
end-tidal CO2 held at 5.0%. Gas analysis by a Bruel and Kjaer 1304 photoacoustic monitor. Stimulus: 10-s, 50-Hz,
80-mA transcutaneous tetanus to the ulnar nerve at the wrist (constant-current stimulator); positive response =
movement of a nonstimulated limb (not swallowing or coughing). 10-min equilibration at each end-tidal
concentration; up-down from 1% with final steps of 0.05% until a move / no-move / move sequence (or the reverse).
Individual MAC = mean of the two bracketing concentrations; group MAC = median of individual values. A single
blinded observer. Progesterone by solid-phase I-125 radioimmunoassay before induction (interassay CV 10% at 5.0
nM, 7.2% at 47.0 nM; LOD 0.2 nM). Statistics: Mann-Whitney; Kendall rank correlation of MAC with progesterone.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: movement in response to a supramaximal tetanic ulnar-nerve stimulus, not skin incision. Isoflurane MAC
0.775% (0.675-0.825) pregnant vs 1.075% (1.025-1.175) nonpregnant, a 28% reduction (P < 0.001). No ke0, EC50,
MEAC or potency ratio. Progesterone was higher in the pregnant group, but its Kendall rank correlation with MAC was
not significant in either group (nonpregnant P = 0.09, pregnant P = 0.32); one pregnant woman aged 45 had MAC
0.675% with progesterone 0.8 nM (PDF p3). Discussion: the nonpregnant tetanic MAC agrees with Zbinden's tetanic
1.03%, which Zbinden found slightly below his incision MAC of 1.16%.

## Covariates
Pregnancy (8-12 weeks) as a group effect. Progesterone examined, no significant relation.

## Caveats
- Only isoflurane, only 8-12 weeks of gestation; the authors decline to extrapolate to other stages of pregnancy.
- Tetanic-stimulus MAC, not incision MAC; small groups (10 + 10); medians and ranges only.
- Women of Chinese ethnicity, low body weight (median 54-59 kg).
- Cause of the reduction not established (progesterone not correlated; pregnancy-induced analgesia discussed).
