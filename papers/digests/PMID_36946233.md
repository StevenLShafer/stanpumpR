# Burkat 2023: PBPK/PD model of alprazolam brain concentrations and GABA-A potentiation

## Study
- Modelling study only (single author). Plasma data digitized (WebPlotDigitizer) from published IV, IR and XR
  studies (Smith 1984, Lin 1988, Greenblatt and Wright 1993, Wright et al.). PBPK in PK-Sim 10 (virtual White male,
  30 y, 80.35 kg, 178.5 cm); PD in MonolixSuite 2021R (SAEM).
- Population simulations: 100 virtual individuals (75 M / 25 F, 25-55 y, 55-86 kg, BMI 18.5-24.9).
- IR dissolution Weibull (shape 0.92, 5 min, no lag); XR (shape 0.9, 300 min).
- Receptor potentiation: recombinant GABA-A receptor EC10 currents from Masneuf et al. fitted to a logistic,
  y = A2 + (A1 - A2)/(1 + ([alprazolam]/EC50)^p), applied to predicted unbound interstitial brain concentration.

Simulated unbound interstitial brain peaks: 4-33 nM for IR 0.25-2 mg, 3-54 nM for XR 0.5-10 mg; repeated dosing
59 nM (IR 2 mg) and 122 nM (XR 10 mg). 59 nM = 18.2 ng/mL unbound (derived, MW 308.8).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End points: EEG beta 13-30 Hz (direct Emax, data from Venkatakrishnan 2005), saccade peak velocity and mood
  (indirect response), cognitive speed slowing and DSST (quadratic models), all driven by predicted unbound brain
  interstitial concentration; no ke0 (the PBPK brain compartment supplies the delay).
- IR effects show counterclockwise hysteresis vs brain concentration; XR DSST clockwise.
- Not an opioid; no MEAC or potency ratio.

## Covariates
None estimated (single virtual male; population simulation varies anatomy only).

## Caveats
- Single-author modelling of digitized literature data; PBPK fit judged "within 2-fold".
- Receptor potentiation is in vitro (recombinant receptors), mapped to predicted unbound brain concentrations.
- The abstract's alpha1 potentiation at 59 nM (92%) does not follow from the stated logistic (79%, derived);
  alpha2 and alpha5 do.
- Partition-coefficient value as printed (126) is unexplained.
