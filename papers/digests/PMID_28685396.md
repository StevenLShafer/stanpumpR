# Connarn 2017: bupropion IR, SR and ER in a six-way crossover

## Study
- Randomised six-period, six-treatment crossover, fasting, single doses of bupropion HCl IR 75 mg, IR 100 mg, SR 100 mg,
  SR 150 mg, ER 150 mg and ER 300 mg; washout >= 10 days (NCT02078180; FDA-funded).
- 33 enrolled, 30 completed all periods; 17 F / 16 M; ages 25-55, BMI 18.5-35; 72.7% Caucasian (Table II; the
  table's characteristics column is misaligned in the text layer, weight/height not reliably readable).
- Sampling 0-96 h (0, 0.5, 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8 [12 for ER], 24, 48, 72, 96 h). Plasma bupropion, hydroxy-, threohydro-
  and erythrohydrobupropion by LC-MS/MS (venlafaxine IS).
- NCA in Phoenix WinNonlin 6.4; population PK of bupropion (parent only) in NONMEM 7.3; genotyping CYP2B6, CYP2C19,
  AKR7A3.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None: no PD measured. Not an opioid; no MEAC, ke0, tPeak or potency ratio.

## Covariates
No SNP in CYP2B6, CYP2C19 or AKR7A3 was significant on CL/F or V/F; AUC(metabolite)/AUC(parent) did not differ by
genotype or formulation.

## Caveats
- Healthy volunteers, single doses, fasting. Small n for pharmacogenomics.
- The ER product is called "ER" throughout; the BE question concerns generics referencing Wellbutrin XL, but the
  paper text does not name the ER tablets used (supplement not read).
- Large IIV on ka (96%) because one ka was shared by all formulations.

## Verification against stanpumpR
- Values 1, 0.955 and 0.68: paper text p. 1519 gives SR 95.5% and ER 68% (Table IV(B) 0.96 and 0.68). **matches**.
- Attribution: the repo's "Zhang and colleagues ... their 2017 ... crossover" points to this paper, whose first author
  is Connarn (Zhang X is the 13th of 14 authors). No journal/DOI in the repo. **discrepancy (citation only)**:
  should be cited as Connarn et al., AAPS J 2017;19:1513-1522.
- "Overpredicted by about a third": the model is the SR tablet's (F 0.955 vs IR); an XL dose gives
  0.68/0.955 = 0.71 of the SR exposure, i.e. the model overpredicts by 1/0.71 = 1.40-fold (derived). "About a third"
  is a loose rounding of 29-40%; acceptable but imprecise. **matches (approximately)**.
- Context: this paper's population CL/F, 154 L/h, is close to the model's (Ghimire) 148.4 L/h; ka 0.47/h (shared,
  all formulations) vs the model's SR-specific 0.29/h. Not used by the repo.
