# Kulo 2017: S- and R-ketorolac PK at delivery, post-partum and in volunteers

## Study
Leuven (NCT01291472, EudraCT 2011-000367-27). Single IV bolus over 30 s of ketorolac tromethamine 30 mg (Taradyl,
stated equal to 20.345 mg ketorolac, 67%) to 39 women shortly after caesarean delivery (multimodal analgesia with
locoregional anaesthesia and IV paracetamol), 8 of them again in the later post-partum period (mean week 21, 17.1-22.6),
and 8 healthy female volunteers. Plasma at 1, 2, 4, 6, 8 h; enantiomer-specific HPLC-UV after solid-phase extraction.
Non-compartmental IV-bolus analysis in PKSolver: AUC0-inf, t1/2, Vss, Vss/BW, CL, CL/BW, CL/BSA (Haycock BSA). Unpaired
comparison uses the 31 delivery women not re-studied (Kruskal-Wallis); paired comparison the 8 (Wilcoxon). Medians
(min-max). 47 women, 235 profiles.

Derived dose check: CL x AUC = 6.49 x 1.56 = 10.1 mg for S and 2.14 x 4.76 = 10.2 mg for R, i.e. half of the 20.345 mg
free-acid dose per enantiomer, so the clearances are per enantiomer on the free-acid dose.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None measured; the paper states analgesia is attributed almost exclusively to S-ketorolac (cited). Introduction cites
S/R AUC ratio 0.36 in adults and albumin binding 98.4% (S) vs 99.2% (R).

## Covariates
State (delivery, post-partum, volunteer); weight and BSA normalisation only.

## Caveats
8 h sampling (authors argue 2 half-lives for R, 3-4 for S); small post-partum and volunteer groups; total (not unbound)
concentrations though binding falls in pregnancy; surgery confounds the delivery values.
