# Bouillon 2002: propofol-remifentanil pharmacokinetic interaction

## Study
- 20 healthy volunteers (10 men, 10 women), median age 33.5 y (20-43), median weight 69.3 kg (50-120). Stanford IRB, consent. Randomized open-label, one session per subject of about 180 min of drug administration (p. 1352-1356, PDF pp. 2-7).
- Drugs by target-controlled infusion (Harvard pump, STANPUMP). Propofol kinetics: the non-weight-adjusted Schnider set; remifentanil: the weight-adjusted Minto set (PDF p. 1). The first drug was stepped up and down (to apnea or PETCO2 > 65 mmHg for respiratory depression), then lowered to 1 ug/ml propofol or 1 ng/ml remifentanil; the second drug was held constant (propofol 0-4 ug/ml; remifentanil 0-4 ng/ml, by randomization list) and the first drug stepped up again to loss of response to laryngoscopy. Both infusions then stopped together (PDF pp. 2-3).
- Table 1 (PDF p. 2, printed p. 1351), read on the page image: propofol-first group (n = 10): peak propofol alone 6-12 ug/ml, peak with remifentanil 2-12 ug/ml, constant remifentanil 0-4 ng/ml (one subject's target was 2 instead of 1 ng/ml by error). Remifentanil-first group (n = 10): peak remifentanil alone 3-9 ng/ml, peak with propofol 1-40 ng/ml (subjects 1 and 2, with propofol 0, reached 24 and 40 ng/ml), constant propofol 0-4 ug/ml.
- 18 of 20 received propofol (16 both with and without remifentanil); 18 of 20 received remifentanil (8 both with and without propofol) (Fig. 2).
- Sampling: arterial. Propofol in plasma (centrifuged), to 6 h after the stop; remifentanil in whole blood with citric acid, to 40 min after the stop. Assay GC-MS for both. Propofol limit of quantitation 0.1 ug/ml, linear to 15 ug/ml, interday CV 10.4% at 0.1 and 4.0% at 10 ug/ml. Remifentanil limit of quantitation 0.25 ng/ml, linear 0.25-30 ng/ml, interday CV 8.5% at 5 and 9.3% at 20 ng/ml (PDF p. 3).
- Analysis: NONMEM V, FOCE with interaction; exponential interindividual variability, proportional residual error; covariates age, weight, gender tested (none significant for either drug). Interaction tested as a step-change scalar on a parameter, likelihood ratio P < 0.01 (PDF pp. 5-6).

Derived with-propofol remifentanil values (derived: column 2 x (1 + scalar), with the column read as the without-propofol value): V1 13.50 x 0.59 = 7.97 L; Cl2 1.31 x 0.59 = 0.77 L/min; Cl1 2.57 x 0.85 = 2.18 L/min; V2 unchanged 8.64 L.

Interaction findings: remifentanil did not significantly alter propofol kinetics in the population model; exploratory signed-rank hints (lower V1, higher Cl2 of propofol with remifentanil, Fig. 6) were not confirmed (PDF p. 8). Propofol lowered remifentanil V1 and Cl2 (both -41%) and Cl1 (-15%); the Cl1 effect had P = 0.055 in the exploratory test and was kept for the population test; the objective function fell by 36.9 (Table 4 shows 43.0 and 6.1 as the two objective-function values) (printed p. 1358). Not concentration-dependent in the range studied (propofol up to 4 ug/ml). Simulation (Fig. 9): with a 1 ug/kg bolus, 0.4 ug/kg/min for 2.5 min, then 0.2 ug/kg/min for 60 min, remifentanil concentrations after the bolus are much higher with propofol, only slightly higher in maintenance, and recovery is unchanged.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Opioid audit (remifentanil):
- MEAC / minimum effective or analgesic concentration: none reported.
- ke0, t1/2 ke0, tPeak, hysteresis: none reported. The remifentanil TCI targeted an effect-site concentration using the Minto model's own ke0 (the paper does not state the value). The study's pharmacodynamic end points (respiratory depression by PETCO2 > 65 mmHg or apnea > 60 s; loss of response to laryngoscopy) are analysed in companion papers (see PMID_14739795 for propofol respiratory depression), not here.
- Equianalgesic or potency ratio: none.
- Remifentanil peak concentrations given in Table 1 (printed p. 1351): 3-9 ng/ml alone, up to 24 and 40 ng/ml in two subjects with no propofol.

For propofol, no pharmacodynamic parameters are reported in this paper.

## Covariates
Age and weight entered as deviation from the study median (theta_i = theta_TV x (1 + theta_d x (Cov - Cov_median))); gender tested too. None significant for either drug, so the tabulated models carry no covariates (p. 1355-1357). Subjects were healthy adults only.

## Caveats
- Design was driven by pharmacodynamics, not the classic crossover: the second drug was always added after the first (no random sequence), so time and the second drug are confounded; the authors discuss this and reject a stationarity objection (printed p. 1361).
- Remifentanil was sampled only to 40 min after stopping and fitted to two compartments in blood; its V1 (10.5 L) is about twice the value of the Minto 1997 model at the library's reference patient (see below), plausibly because no third compartment was fitted.
- The with-propofol remifentanil data were few (8 subjects had both conditions); one remifentanil target was set at 2 instead of 1 ng/ml by error.
- The authors cannot size the interaction they might have missed for propofol (no simulation-power analysis); a propofol-concentration effect was not found.
- Table 4 has no column headings (see open_questions).

## Verification against stanpumpR
- Interaction: R/drugs_remifentanil.R (Eleveld 2017 below BMI 30 and in children, Kim 2017 for obese adults) and R/alternativeModels.R:261 (Minto 1997 companion) have no propofol-dependent term. The paper's finding (V1 and Cl2 -41%, Cl1 -15% of remifentanil with propofol) is therefore not applied. Nothing in the paper says the library is wrong; the paper itself calls the effect clinically relevant only for boluses. Verdict: not-applicable (decision recorded in open_questions).
- Propofol, independent comparison. Library propofol is Eleveld 2018 (R/drugs_propofol.R:277); the Schnider 1998 companion is R/alternativeModels.R:213-226: V1 4.27 L, V3 238 L, Cl3 0.836 L/min, V2 = 18.9 - 0.391 (age - 53), Cl2 = 1.29 - 0.024 (age - 53). At the paper's median age 33.5 y these give V2 about 26.5 L and Cl2 about 1.76 L/min (derived), against Table 3's 31.6 L and 3.25 L/min. Table 3 V1 3.78 L, V3 209 L, Cl3 1.09 L/min are close to the Schnider values; Cl1 3.04 L/min is higher than the Schnider Cl1 formula gives for an average adult (about 1.9-2.0 L/min at 69 kg; derived, needs the lean body mass of the median subject, which the paper does not give). This paper's volunteers and the Schnider 1998 volunteers were studied at the same Stanford laboratory with a TCI, but the two fits differ; the Bouillon set is not the library's model.
- Remifentanil, independent comparison. Minto 1997 companion at age 40, LBM 55: V1 5.1 L, V2 9.82 L, V3 5.42 L, Cl1 2.6 L/min, Cl2 2.05 L/min (alternativeModels.R:267-272). Table 4 column 1 (blood, two compartments): V1 10.50, V2 8.22, Cl1 2.34, Cl2 0.82. Cl1 is close; V1 is about double and Cl2 lower, consistent with the paper's short sampling and two-compartment fit. The library's Eleveld remifentanil at the reference patient: V1 5.81 L, Cl1 2.58 L/min (drugs_remifentanil.R:154-158 comments). Cl1 2.57 in Table 4 column 2 (if that is the without-propofol value) equals Eleveld's reference 2.58 to rounding. Direction of differences is noted; no code change implied.
- The paper's propofol and remifentanil PK are not used by any location, so there is no transcription to check.
