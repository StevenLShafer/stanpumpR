# van de Vusse 2022: PK of methyldopa, labetalol and nifedipine in pregnancy (systematic review)

## Study
Systematic review (PROSPERO CRD42019128415); Embase, Medline, Web of Science, Cochrane, Google Scholar (the abstract
dates the search 27 July 2021, the methods 2 August 2022). 1459 unique records, 67 full texts, 30 PK studies retained:
2 methyldopa, 12 labetalol, 16 nifedipine (3 of them PBPK). No meta-analysis; data heterogeneous. Study list in
Table 1 (PDF p4-7). Nicardipine is not covered.

Rubin 1983 (IV labetalol 50 mg; Table 1 lists 20 subjects, 33-38 weeks): Vd and CL not significantly different between
pregnant, postpartum and normotensive groups (p7). Manninen 1991: nifedipine serum 4.3 ± 3.1 ng/mL in 11
third-trimester women vs 12.0 ± 2.9 in 6 nonpregnant controls at 10 mg three times daily (p8). Haas 2013: CL/F
differed by CYP3A5 expression (the review prints the units as µg/mL, evidently an error).

Fetal/maternal ratios: methyldopa total 0.99 and 0.63 (Table 2); labetalol about 0.5 (one outlier about 1), 0.67;
nifedipine 0.53-0.93 in vivo, 0.054 ex vivo (Table 4, p9).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None quantified. The authors note dosing is empirical, guided by maternal blood pressure.

## Covariates
Gestational age (labetalol CL/F, Fischer 2014); CYP3A5 genotype and CYP3A inhibitors (nifedipine, Haas 2012/2013);
type 2 diabetes had no effect on nifedipine PK (Filgueira 2017).

## Caveats
Small, heterogeneous studies; most data third trimester or at delivery; oral apparent clearances (CL/F) mix
bioavailability and clearance; the "almost doubled" nifedipine CL/F is a PBPK result checked against one observed
dataset; search date inconsistent between abstract and methods. No IV nifedipine or any nicardipine data.
