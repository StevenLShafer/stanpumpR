# Melin 2024: dapagliflozin population PK in HFrEF and type 2 diabetes

## Study
- Short communication, AstraZeneca (all authors employees). Data: DAPA-HF (NCT03036124; HFrEF with or without type 2
  diabetes, 10 mg, one pre-dose sample at steady state, Visit 7, day 360 +/- 14), four adult type 2 diabetes studies
  (NCT00162305, NCT00528372, NCT00736879, NCT00673231), adolescents 10-17 y with type 2 diabetes (NCT01525238) and
  healthy subjects (MB102002; the main text's "MB10200211" is MB102002 followed by citation 11, per Table S1 and the
  supplement's methods) (PDF p2).
- Table S1 (supplement, 2026-10-27), doses and "Number of PK patients" (before the exclusions of Table S3):
  DAPA-HF 10 mg (5 mg if needed), 1869, one pre-dose sample at week 52; MB102002 2.5/10/20/50/100 mg qd, 30, serial up
  to 24 h on days 1, 7, 14; NCT00162305 5/25/100 mg qd, 39, serial up to 24 h days 1 and 14; NCT00528372 2.5/5/10 mg,
  480, pre-dose, 60, 180 min; NCT00673231 2.5/5/10 mg, 567, pre-dose, 60, 180 min; NCT00736879 1/2.5/5 mg, 183,
  pre-dose and 30-180 min; NCT01525238 2.5/5/10 mg single dose, 24, up to 11 samples over 48 h. Adult T2DM sum 1269
  (derived: 39 + 480 + 567 + 183). Dose range 1-100 mg.
- Table S3 (supplement): samples used, DAPA-HF 1497 (80.1%), adult T2DM 8011, adolescents 226, all 9735 (84.1%);
  BLQ 793 (6.85%) excluded; 152 DAPA-HF and 353 adult samples excluded as "high pre-dose" (post-dose) concentrations.
  Table S5: 1869 DAPA-HF patients with PK sampling vs 499 without (median weight 79 kg both; eGFR 65 [24-137] vs 61).
- 9735 quantifiable concentrations from 2744 patients; of 11 571 PK samples 81.8% adult T2DM, 16.2% DAPA-HF, 2%
  adolescents (PDF p3). PK samples were unavailable in 504 DAPA-HF patients.

## Model and parameters
The previous sponsor model, refitted with the added data, not developed de novo: two compartments, first-order
absorption, a fixed lag time, first-order elimination (PDF p2, p6). eGFR changed from MDRD to CKD-EPI (Levey 2009,
the paper's reference 12) in adults; bedside Schwartz in adolescents. Covariates retained from the earlier T2DM model:
eGFR on CL/F, sex on CL/F (higher in men), body weight on Vc/F.

Final model (supplement, 2026-10-27): Table S4 ("final model based on healthy subjects and patients from the DAPA-HF
study, adult T2DM studies, adolescent T2DM study") and the control stream sup0003 ($PROBLEM "dapagliflozin HF+T2D";
ADVAN4 TRANS4, so V2 is the central and V3 the peripheral volume). Control-stream equations:
- `KA = THETA(1)*EXP(ETA(1))`; `CL = THETA(2)*EXP(ETA(2))*(BEGFR/83)**THETA(6)*EXP(THETA(7)*(1-SEXM))`, comment
  "ref group: male, healthy"; `V2 = THETA(3)*(BBWT/89)**THETA(8)` (no ETA); `V3 = THETA(4)*EXP(ETA(3))`;
  `Q = THETA(5)`; `ALAG1 = 0.248` hard-coded (not a THETA); `S2 = V2`; `AUCS = DOSE*1000/CL` [ng.h/mL].
- No caps or bounds on any covariate; eGFR enters as a plain power with reference 83, weight with reference 89 kg.
  The HF and T2D columns are in $INPUT but unused in $PK. These references are not the analysed medians of Table 1
  (weight 83 kg, eGFR 77); the source does not say where 83 and 89 come from.
- Residual error: `Y = LOG(F+EP1) + F*ERR(1)/(F+EP1) + EP1*ERR(2)/(F+EP1)`, EP1 = THETA(9) bounded (0, 1); for PAT 4
  (adolescents) `Y = LOG(F) + ERR(3)`. Not used by a typical-value simulation.
- The supplement's Equation 1 (OMML) writes the sex term as a linear factor, (SEXM + SEX~CL); with SEX~CL = -0.174
  that would give a negative clearance in women, so the control stream's exponential form is the operative one.

Previous models (Table S2): adult data only, Ka 2.97/h, CL/F 22.9, Vc/F 73.9, Vp/F 113, Q/F 8.85, CrCL~CL 0.552,
sex -0.161, BWT~Vc 0.705, EP1 0.725, TLAG 0.248 h fixed; adult + adolescent (MDRD eGFR), Ka 2.6, CL/F 23, Vc/F 74,
Vp/F 109, Q/F 8.86, eGFR~CL 0.499, sex -0.165, BWT~Vc 0.546; BSV Vp/F 0.147 / 0.113.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None (only one dose in DAPA-HF, so no exposure-response). Model-predicted 10 mg-normalised AUC: 1.2-/1.1-fold higher in
HFrEF with/without T2DM than adult T2DM; 1.2-fold in < 70 kg vs 70-100 kg; 1.2-fold female vs male; 1.1-fold >= 75 vs
65-74 y; 1.5- and 1.8-fold at eGFR 45-59 and 30-44 vs >= 90 (PDF p5-6). Table S6 gives the medians (e.g. adult T2DM
454, HFrEF without T2DM 518, with 536, male 468, female 544 ng.h/mL). None judged clinically relevant. Not an opioid.

## Covariates
eGFR (CKD-EPI 2009 adults, bedside Schwartz adolescents) and sex on CL/F; weight on Vc/F, as the control stream
writes them (above). HFrEF tested and dropped. HFrEF correlated with eGFR (r = -0.49).

## Caveats
- One trough per HFrEF patient around week 52; possible selection bias (the authors' check suggests minor).
- Not a de novo model. BSV of Ka fixed at 8.52 (from the model without HFrEF data) because troughs do not inform Ka.
- Table S4's RSE column for Ka (0.100%) and CL/F (0.200%) is not consistent with its own 95% intervals (1.90-2.48;
  22.6-23.9); the source does not explain it.
- The supplement's printed Equation 1 does not match the control stream's sex term (see above).
- The example dataset's header (TAFD, DV, LDV) differs from the control stream's $INPUT names (TIME, DV2, DV);
  NONMEM reads by position, so this does not change the model.
