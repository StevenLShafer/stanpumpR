# Samara 1998: tiagabine population PK in epilepsy, effect of enzyme inducers

## Study
Retrospective population analysis of sparse samples from two open-label, long-term add-on safety studies (118
centres). 2,147 plasma samples from 511 patients (268 from study 1, 243 from study 2; 239 F, 272 M; about 90%
white; 90 smokers). Mean (SD) weight 73.8 (20.7) kg, age 32.1 (12.3) y (range 11-77), height 168.5 (11.3) cm
(Table 1, abstract). Total daily dose 2 mg once daily to 80 mg in four doses. Samples eligible if <= 24 h after the
previous dose, >= 2 days at a dose, and >= 6 weeks after any change in inducing AEDs. Only 15 patients took
tiagabine alone. HPLC with electrochemical detection, LOQ about 1 ng/mL, QC CV < 8%. NONMEM version 4,
one-compartment with first-order absorption, multiple-dose superposition equation assuming full compliance;
exponential IIV on CL/F, V/F and ka (independent), proportional residual error. Post hoc CL/F explored by
ANCOVA in SAS/JMP.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No efficacy or concentration-effect analysis; no ke0. Not an opioid paper.

## Covariates
Enzyme-inducing AED (CBZ, PHT, PB, primidone): separate CL/F, +67%; effect similar across inducers and not
additive with two or more inducers. Weight on V/F only (CL/F unaffected by weight). Age, sex, race, smoking,
bilirubin, SGOT, SGPT not important; albumin and weight weak regressors on post hoc CL/F explaining little variance.

## Caveats
Sparse, uncontrolled dosing history; 100% compliance assumed except for the last dose; few patients on
monotherapy (15); 90% white. Independent random effects. No central V/F or ka value is reported (only post hoc mean V/F by subgroup, Table 3).
