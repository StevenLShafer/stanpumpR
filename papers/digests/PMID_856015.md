# Wust 1977: nitroimidazole MIC and MBC against 114 anaerobes

## Study
In vitro microbiology; no patients, no pharmacokinetics. 114 anaerobic strains (9 reference strains from VPI, 105
clinical isolates, Zurich): 39 *B. fragilis* (subspecies incl. thetaiotaomicron, distasonis, vulgatus), 2
*B. melaninogenicus*, 13 *Fusobacterium*, 9 *C. perfringens*, 14 other clostridia, 24 *Peptococcus*/
*Peptostreptococcus*, 4 *Veillonella*, 2 *Eubacterium*, 7 *Propionibacterium acnes* (p. 631-632; Table 1).
Drugs: metronidazole, ornidazole, tinidazole.

- **MIC**: agar dilution, twofold dilutions 100 to 0.1 ug/mL, inoculum about 2 x 10^3 to 2 x 10^4 bacteria per spot,
  48 h anaerobic at 37 C; MIC = lowest concentration with no growth, a barely visible haze or one colony (p. 632).
  Four media: BHI agar with and without 5% sheep blood, brucella agar + blood, Mueller-Hinton + blood.
- **MBC**: replica plating from the BHIA (no blood) MIC plate onto drug-free BHIA; MBC = lowest concentration with no
  growth or a single colony after 48 h (p. 632). Not a 99.9%-kill broth MBC. Not done for 9 swarming clostridia, so
  MBC n = 105 (Table 1 footnote b).
- Broth-disk (Wilkins-Thiel, 30-ug disk giving 6 ug/mL) and VPI agar-diffusion tests compared with the MIC.

The "inhibited by 3.1, killed by 6.3" statement (abstract; p. 633) covers all three drugs together and excludes
*P. acnes*. For **metronidazole alone**, every strain except *P. acnes* was inhibited and killed at <= 3.1 ug/mL; the
6.3 step is reached only by ornidazole and tinidazole (Table 1). MBC was usually equal to the MIC, sometimes one or
two dilutions higher, rarely one lower (p. 633).

Table 2 (PDF p. 4; geometric mean MIC of 105 strains, ug/mL): metronidazole 0.56 (BHIA), 0.55 (BHIA + blood),
0.87 (brucella), 0.72 (Mueller-Hinton); ornidazole 0.50/0.49/0.84/0.59; tinidazole 0.54/0.53/0.79/0.60. No significant
difference between drugs; brucella and MH slightly higher than BHIA (P < 1%).
Table 3: broth-disk agreement with MIC 97.4% (metronidazole), 94.7% (others); 7 resistant strains (P. acnes) all
correctly called. Table 4: reproducibility on one *B. fragilis* strain x7, metronidazole MIC mean 0.73 (SD 0.47,
range 0.4-1.6) ug/mL.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End points are in vitro growth inhibition (MIC) and killing on replica plating (MBC). The author chose 6 ug/mL as the
susceptible/resistant line because serum easily reaches it (p. 636; supporting serum data are cited, not shown).
No time-kill, no PK/PD index, no concentration in patients.

## Covariates
Medium (four agars) only; species.

## Caveats
- MBC by replica plating (no growth or one colony on a velvet-pad transfer) is far cruder than a 99.9%-kill broth MBC;
  the author notes some MBCs below the MIC.
- Small numbers per species (n 2-39); 1970s taxonomy (*B. fragilis* subspecies now separate species; *P. acnes* now
  *Cutibacterium acnes*; *B. melaninogenicus* now *Prevotella*/*Porphyromonas*).
- MBC not measured for 9 of 14 "other" clostridia.

- **Wording, discrepancy (minor)**: `R/antibioticMbc.R:351-352` says 114 anaerobes were inhibited by 3.1 and killed by
  6.3. The paper says all strains *except the 7 P. acnes* (MIC and MBC > 100 ug/mL), and MBC was measured in 105, not
  114 (Table 1). The 6.3 figure is the pooled three-drug figure; for metronidazole alone every non-*P. acnes* strain was
  killed by 3.1 ug/mL. Accurate form: 107 of 114 inhibited, and 98 of 105 killed, by 3.1 ug/mL metronidazole. The
  library's next row (`:353-355`) already excludes *C. acnes* as intrinsically resistant, so no number is affected.
- **Row values vs Wust (independent check)**: the row's MBC90 = 2 and MIC90 = 1 are not taken from Wust. For Wust's
  strains in this row's groups (*B. melaninogenicus*, *Fusobacterium*, clostridia, peptococci, plus *Veillonella* and
  *Eubacterium*), 57 of 59 MBC-tested strains were killed at 1.6 ug/mL (96.6%) and all at 3.1 (derived from Table 1
  percentages x n). So Wust's MBC90 is <= 1.6 ug/mL, at or below the row's 2: **consistent**. In the same groups 62 of 68
  strains were inhibited at 0.8 ug/mL (91%; derived), so Wust's MIC90 is 0.8, consistent with the row's 1.
- **B. fragilis row (`:334-345`, not citing Wust)**: MBC50 1, MBC90 4. Wust's *B. fragilis* MBC50 is 0.8 (67% killed),
  MBC90 1.6 (90%), all killed at 3.1 (Table 1). The library's values are one to two dilutions higher, the conservative
  direction; Wust's crude replica-plate MBC would be expected to read lower than a 99.9%-kill MBC.
