# Keppra label 2024: levetiracetam tablets and oral solution

## Study
An FDA label for oral products only: 250/500/750/1000 mg tablets and a 100 mg/mL oral solution. **This label covers no injection.** Keppra injection is a separate NDA (021872), and its 1:1 IV-oral conversion is not in this document.

Dosing (2.2-2.4): adults start at 500 mg bid, rising by 1000 mg/day every 2 weeks to a maximum of 3000 mg/day. Pediatric dosing is by weight, e.g. 4 to < 16 y: 10 mg/kg bid rising to 30 mg/kg bid.

Renal dosing (2.5, Table 1) uses Cockcroft-Gault normalised to 1.73 m² BSA:

| CLcr (mL/min/1.73 m²) | Dose |
|---|---|
| > 80 | 500-1500 mg q12h |
| 50-80 | 500-1000 mg q12h |
| 30-50 | 250-750 mg q12h |
| < 30 | 250-500 mg q12h |
| ESRD on dialysis | 500-1000 mg q24h, plus 250-500 mg after dialysis |

## Model and parameters
No compartmental model. Section 12.3 gives the following.

- Absorption: peak about 1 h after oral dosing (fasted). Oral bioavailability of the tablets is 100%, and tablet and solution are bioequivalent. Food delays Tmax by 1.5 h and lowers Cmax by 20%, without changing extent.
- Linear over 500-5000 mg; steady state in 2 days on bid dosing; under 10% protein-bound.
- Metabolism: not extensive and not CYP-dependent. Enzymatic hydrolysis of the acetamide group gives the inactive ucb L057, 24% of the dose. Minor metabolites account for 2% and 1%.
- Elimination: t½ 7 ± 1 h in adults, independent of dose and repeated administration. Renal excretion of unchanged drug accounts for 66% of the dose. Total body clearance is 0.96 mL/min/kg and renal clearance 0.6 mL/min/kg, by glomerular filtration with partial tubular reabsorption. ucb L057 is cleared renally at 4 mL/min/kg.
- Children: 6-12 y have about 40% higher weight-adjusted CL/F. At 4-12 y, Tmax is about 1 h and t½ about 5 h. At 1 month to < 4 y, t½ is 5.3 h vs 7.2 h in adults, and CL/F 1.5 vs 0.96 mL/min/kg.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Pharmacodynamics: the mechanism is SV2A binding (12.1). No QTc prolongation at 1000 or 5000 mg (12.2). The label gives no exposure-response model and no therapeutic or toxic concentration range. Overdose cases (the highest dose in clinical trials was 6000 mg/day) caused somnolence, agitation, respiratory depression and coma. Hemodialysis removes about 50% in 4 h. Not an opioid: no MEAC, ke0 or tPeak.

## Covariates
- Renal function. Total body clearance falls 40% in mild (CLcr 50-80), 50% in moderate (30-50) and 60% in severe (< 30 mL/min) impairment, and 70% in anuric ESRD, all compared with CLcr > 80. Clearance correlates with creatinine clearance.
- Elderly (61-88 y, CLcr 30-74): clearance 38% lower and t½ 2.5 h longer.
- Sex: Cmax and AUC 20% higher in women, but clearance adjusted for weight is comparable.
- Hepatic function: unchanged in Child-Pugh A and B. In Child-Pugh C, total clearance is 50% of normal, mostly from lower renal clearance.
- Interactions: enzyme-inducing antiseizure drugs raise CL/F about 22% in children. There are no other relevant interactions.
- Obese children: small exposure differences, judged not clinically meaningful.

## Caveats
A label summary: means only, small special-population studies (elderly n=16, sex n=11/12). The renal-impairment reductions are group means without CLcr values. The label gives no IV data. Keppra XR is a different label.

## Verification against stanpumpR
- **R/drugs_levetiracetam.R:20-21** (66% unchanged, 24% hydrolysis). The label gives 66% renal as unchanged drug and ucb L057 at 24% of the dose. **Matches.** The code uses 0.66 as the fraction of clearance that is renal. The label's own clearances give 0.6/0.96 = 0.625 (derived), which is close to fe of 0.66.
- **R/drugs_levetiracetam.R:25 and levetiracetam.md:3** (proportional fall with renal function). The label says clearance correlates with CLcr: reductions of 40/50/60% for mild/moderate/severe and 70% when anuric. With a fixed non-renal 34%, the model gives smaller reductions in mild-moderate impairment (derived, against the 107 mL/min reference): about 26% at CLcr 65, 41% at 40 and 54% at 20, with a floor of 66% when anuric. The direction and the anuric end agree, but the mild-group fall is underpredicted. **Partly matches.**
- **R/drugs_levetiracetam.R:27-30, :107, and levetiracetam.md:7** (injection interchangeable mg for mg, "label's 1:1 conversion"). This label (NDA 021035/021505) covers tablets and oral solution only. It contains no injection, no IV data and no conversion statement; it supports only oral F = 100%. The 1:1 statement belongs to the Keppra injection label (NDA 021872), which should be cited instead. **Discrepancy** in sourcing; the clinical claim itself is not in question.
- **R/drugs_levetiracetam.R:37** ("Half-life 8.0 h (label 6-8 h)"). The label says 7 ± 1 h, i.e. 6-8, so the label value is correctly transcribed. The model value does not follow from the code. At the reference patient the code gives V 65.3 L and CL 3.9 L/h (test-drugs-levetiracetam.R pins both with the switch off), so t½ = 0.693 × 65.3 / 3.9 = 11.6 h (derived), not 8.0 h. The label's own figures imply V of about 41 L (0.58 L/kg) for t½ 7 h at CL 4.0 L/h. Rhee's V/F of 65.3 L is therefore about 1.6 times the label-implied volume. The model half-life sits well above the label's 6-8 h, and both the header check and help line 7 ("about 8 hours") are wrong. **Discrepancy** (`R/drugs_levetiracetam.R:37`, `inst/help/drugs/levetiracetam.md:7`).
- The clearance itself is consistent with the label: 3.9 L/h vs 0.96 mL/min/kg × 70 kg = 4.03 L/h (derived).
