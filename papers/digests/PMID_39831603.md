# Li 2025: Semi-mechanistic oral THC model and cannabis per se limits

## Study
Model-based meta-analysis, no new subjects. **Digitised mean concentration-time curves** from 10 published studies:
7 single oral doses (5-90 mg; dronabinol capsules, brownies, hemp oil etc.) and 3 single IV doses (1.6-5 mg), sampling
4-54 h (Table S2, not in file). Occasional-user arms only where studies had both; fasted arm only of the fed/fasted
study. Whole-blood data converted to plasma with factors 0.71 (THC), 0.73 (11-OH-THC), 0.65 (THC-COOH). Mass, not
molar, concentrations. NONMEM 7.4.3 FOCEI, ADVAN14; **inter-study variability only (no inter-individual variability
can be estimated from means)**. External check against Goodwin 2006 (0.13, 0.157, 2.5, 4.93 mg TID for 5 days).
Simulations: 1000 virtual subjects per group, oral 2.5-100 mg, single dose (occasional) or BID for 14 days (frequent),
48 h after the last dose.

## Model and parameters
Final structure (Figure 1 and equations 1-11, PDF p.4, image-confirmed): oral depot (F, ka) -> THC central; **THC has
two peripheral compartments and an adipose pool connected to peripheral 2** through PS and a partition coefficient Kp
(eq. 6-7: flux PS x (C_peri2 - C_adipose/Kp)); THC cleared to 11-OH-THC (CL_THC), which has the same four-compartment
structure (adipose off its peripheral 2, PS2, Kp2); 11-OH-THC cleared with fraction Fm to THC-COOH (two compartments)
and 1-Fm excreted renally. The Results text (p.542) describes Q2 and Q4 as the flows that "connected the adipose
compartments to the central compartments", consistent with the route through peripheral 2.

Every RSE other than V_THC's is at most 8.2%, most below 1%. Results text values agree with Table 1. Derived (base R
in scratchpad, IV bolus into THC central, adipose off peripheral 2): Vss = 204 + 30.2 + 88.3 + 91.8 x 14 = 1608 L;
disposition half-lives 0.29, 1.09, 2.95 and 202.9 h, the slowest carrying 5.3% of AUC; 10 mg oral (F 0.144) peaks at
3.60 ng/mL at 1.06 h.

The Methods describe a combined proportional plus additive residual error (eq. 13), but Table 1 lists only
proportional variances. The text says inter-study variability was put on "CL, V2, V3 and V4", while Table 1 lists it
on V_THC, CL_THC, V_11-OH-THC and V_THC-COOH.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD data or model. Impairment is assumed, not measured: an "impairment window" 1-8 h after an oral dose and a
"minimal impairment dose" of 10 mg or less. Whole-blood per se limits 1, 2, 5 ng/mL are taken as plasma 1.4, 2.8,
7 ng/mL (divided by 0.71). Table 2 (p.545) gives the simulated probability of exceeding each limit: e.g. occasional
users at 1.4 ng/mL, 2.0-32.8% at 1-8 h for 2.5-10 mg and 53.7-73.0% for 20-100 mg; at 7 ng/mL, 0-0.7% for 2.5-10 mg.
Conclusions: the 1 ng/mL whole-blood limit is least effective (false positives); the 2 and 5 ng/mL limits are
inconclusive for lack of PD data. Not an opioid; no MEAC, no ke0.

## Covariates
None (user frequency enters only as a dosing regimen in the simulations).

## Caveats
Authors: built from digitised means of studies with different formulations; could not fully capture THC accumulation
for lack of long sampling; fixed impairment window; no PD. Noticed: V_THC is effectively unidentified (RSE 186%) while
the other RSEs are implausibly small, as expected when fitting a handful of mean curves; inter-study shrinkage 37.5% on
V_THC-COOH; the implied 203 h terminal half-life rests on an adipose pool sampled for at most 54 h.
