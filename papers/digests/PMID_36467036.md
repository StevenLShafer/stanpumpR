# Laffont 2022: buprenorphine exposure-response in opioid use disorder (BUP-XR)

## Study
Sponsor (Indivior) exposure-response analysis of two trials of monthly subcutaneous extended-release buprenorphine
(BUP-XR, Sublocade) (PDF p3-5). Phase 2 (NCT02044094): 39 non-treatment-seeking adults (18-55 y) with moderate-severe
OUD, stabilised on transmucosal buprenorphine 8-24 mg/day for 2 weeks, then BUP-XR 300 mg on days 1 and 29;
hydromorphone 0, 6 or 18 mg IM challenges weekly with drug-liking VAS and a drug-vs-money choice task. Phase 3
(NCT02357901): 504 randomised (489 analysed) treatment-seeking adults (18-65 y): 300/100 mg (n 203), 300/300 mg
(n 201), placebo (n 100) for 6 months; urine screens plus self-report, craving VAS, COWS, SOWS weekly. Plasma
buprenorphine by LC-MS/MS (LLOQ 0.05 ng/mL); in Phase 3 concentrations were individual predictions from the Jones 2021
population PK model. Mixed-effects logistic (binary/ordinal) Emax models and a Gompertz dropout model; NONMEM 7.3.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Opioid audit:
- **MEAC**: none (end points are OUD outcomes, not analgesia).
- **Occupancy mapping** (from Nasser 2014, not re-estimated): 2-3 ng/mL = 68-75% mu-receptor occupancy; 4 ng/mL about
  78%; withdrawal controlled from 1 ng/mL (about 55%) in earlier laboratory studies (Kuhlman 1998, Greenwald 2007,
  2014) (PDF p12). Abstract: 2-3 ng/mL corresponds to at least 70% occupancy.
- **ke0 / hysteresis**: not estimated; delays could not be modelled with weekly sampling. Cites Yassen 2007: biophase
  distribution half-life 75 min, receptor dissociation half-life 68 min (PDF p13).
- **Blockade**: drug liking not above placebo for hydromorphone 6 and 18 mg throughout BUP-XR; not achieved for 18 mg
  during transmucosal run-in at a mean 1.2 ng/mL; at >= 2 ng/mL fewer than 10% of placebo-corrected scores >20 mm.
- No potency ratio.

## Covariates
Abstinence EC50: injecting use (x3.57), OPRD1 rs678849 (TC -71%, TT -94%), race (-11%, imprecise); Emax: employment
(+43%), race (-31%). Craving Emax: BMI. Dropout: craving, race, age and CGI-S (placebo only for the last two).

## Caveats
- Sponsor-authored; Phase 3 concentrations are model predictions; the low end of the curves rests mainly on early
  post-injection data.
- The sublingual trough/average/peak figures are the label's, cited in a footnote.
- Phase 3 dropout 33-34% active vs 64% placebo.
