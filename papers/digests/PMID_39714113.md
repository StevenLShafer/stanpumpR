# Barry 2025: pregnancy PBPK model of lacosamide (Simcyp), evaluated on MONEAD

## Study
Whole-body PBPK model in Simcyp v22. Nonpregnant model built from literature inputs (Table S1, supplement):
BCS class 1, first-order absorption from population-PK estimates, intrinsic clearance from in vivo data, with
CYP3A4, CYP2C9 and CYP2C19 **assumed to contribute equally** (no data). Pregnancy model: Simcyp pregnancy
population with its gestational changes in weight, hepatic and renal function, blood volume and CYP activity;
absorption assumed unchanged. Evaluation data: single 200 mg doses in 16 healthy men (digitised from the
literature); sparse samples from **MONEAD, 11 nonpregnant and 16 pregnant women** on lacosamide at several
doses, with 5, 8, 9 and 6 samples near 10, 20, 30 and 40 weeks (pp 2-3). Simulations: 10 trials x 15 women,
18-45 y, mostly 200 mg twice daily at steady state, at 10, 20, 30 and 40 weeks; dose-change, CYP-phenotype and
GFR (50-140 mL/min) sensitivity runs.

## Model and parameters
No parameter table in the main text. Reported results (Results pp 4-5; Discussion p 7):
- Nonpregnant model: AUC AFE 1.01, AAFE 1.11; Cmax AFE 0.87, AAFE 1.18 (criterion 0.8-1.25); 100% of
  single-dose and 92% of multiple-dose observations inside the 90% interval.
- Pregnant model: 83% of MONEAD observations inside the 90% interval (no AFE possible: no observed AUC/Cmax).
- **Clearance at 40 weeks +48.2% vs nonpregnant** (Table S2; text p 5; abstract). Cmax -30%, AUC -32.5% at
  40 weeks; Tmax unchanged. Check (derived): 1/(1 - 0.325) = 1.48, consistent with +48.2%.
- Typical concentration -10.9% at 10 weeks and -25% at 30 weeks (Discussion p 7).
- Background quoted from the literature (p 2): t½ about 13 h, V 0.6 L/kg, CL about 2.5 L/h in adults, about
  60% metabolised (CYP2C9, 2C19, 3A4), the rest mainly renal.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. Dose simulations (Fig 4): for 200 mg twice daily before pregnancy, +50 mg once daily at 10 weeks, or
+50 mg twice daily at 20 or 30 weeks, keeps concentrations near preconception; +100 mg twice daily overshoots.

## Covariates
Gestational age (through Simcyp physiology). CYP2C9 PM/IM: higher AUC than EM; CYP2C19 EM at 40 weeks
approaches nonpregnant UM (Fig 5). Baseline GFR does not change the percentage fall in AUC (about 30% at GFR
50 and at 120 mL/min; Fig 6, Discussion p 8).

## Caveats
A simulation, not an estimate: equal CYP contributions assumed, absorption assumed unchanged in pregnancy,
variability of absorption and renal clearance assumed; evaluation against sparse MONEAD data only, with few
samples before 10 weeks; no observed pregnancy AUC or Cmax. The numeric outputs are in the unarchived
supplement (Table S2).
