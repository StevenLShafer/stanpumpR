# Kulo 2012 (IJOA): racemic IV ketorolac after caesarean delivery

## Study
Prospective single-centre open-label (EudraCT 2011-00367-27, NCT01291472). Ketorolac tromethamine 30 mg IV bolus
(stated equal to 20.345 mg ketorolac) at the end of caesarean delivery (repeated at 8 and 16 h clinically; PK on the first
dose). Group IP: 39 women; Group LP: 8 of them re-studied 4-5 months post-partum (20.3 +/- 2.2 weeks; 4 breastfeeding);
Group C: 8 unrelated healthy non-pregnant female volunteers. Plasma at 1, 2, 4, 6, 8 h; racemic HPLC-UV (LLOQ 0.025
ug/mL). Non-compartmental: ke from at least 3 final points, AUC0-8 by trapezoids + C8/ke, CL = dose / AUC0-inf, Vd =
CL/ke. The paper does not state whether the dose in CL is the salt (30 mg) or the free acid (20.345 mg).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None measured. Discussion suggests a 50-100% larger loading dose and 6-hourly dosing (an inference, not tested).

## Covariates
In Group IP, BSA correlated with CL in L/h (r = 0.38) but not with CL/BSA; gestational age, pre-term vs term, twins and
co-morbidity had no significant effect.

## Caveats
Racemic assay; NCA on 8 h sampling; surgery and co-medication at delivery; controls and LP n = 8; dose basis for CL not
stated.
