# Arixtra label 2024: fondaparinux prescribing information (NDA 021345)

The PDF is the expected document: the US Arixtra label, initial approval 2001, revised 12/2024, 47 pages (one
page per text chunk). Page numbers below are PDF pages, which equal the printed page numbers.

## Study
Label, not a study. Indications: DVT prophylaxis after hip fracture (including extended), hip replacement, knee
replacement or abdominal surgery; treatment of DVT/PE with warfarin; paediatric VTE treatment from 1 year and
10 kg (added 12/2024) (p. 1).

Moderate hepatic impairment (Child-Pugh B, single 7.5 mg): Cmax −22%, AUC −39%, PD unchanged, more haemorrhage
(p. 26). No gender effect; Japanese and Black subjects not different (pp. 26-27). Anticoagulant effect persists
2-4 days after stopping with normal renal function, "at least 3 to 5 half-lives" (5.3 p. 12; 8.6 p. 23).

Dosing (section 2, pp. 4-6): prophylaxis 2.5 mg SC daily, first dose no earlier than 6-8 h after surgery, 5-9
days (hip fracture extended up to 24 more days); treatment 5 mg (<50 kg), 7.5 mg (50-100 kg), 10 mg (>100 kg)
daily for at least 5 days until INR 2-3; paediatric 0.1 mg/kg daily, peak target 0.5-1 mg/L 2-4 h after the 2nd or
3rd dose, maximum 7.5 mg/day. Table 3 (p. 6, rendered): anti-Xa <0.3 mg/L increase 0.03 mg/kg; 0.3-0.49 increase
0.01; 0.5-1 no change; 1.01-1.2 decrease 0.01; >1.2 decrease 0.03 (to the nearest 0.1 mg). Contraindicated in CrCl
<30 mL/min and in prophylaxis under 50 kg (p. 1).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Anti-Xa activity calibrated with fondaparinux only, expressed in mg; heparin/LMWH standards are not valid; activity
peaks about 3 h after a dose (12.2 p. 25; 5.6 p. 14). Paediatric therapeutic range 0.5-1 mg/L (2.5, 12.4). Major
bleeding rises with renal impairment (Table 4 p. 12), weight <50 kg (5.4% vs 2.1% orthopaedic, p. 13) and age
(Table 9 p. 23).

## Covariates
Renal function, age, weight (above). Pregnancy (8.1, pp. 20-21): no pregnancy PK; crosses the placenta: in five
women on 2.5 mg/day in the third trimester, four had raised cord anti-Xa activity, cord plasma about 1/10 of
maternal; no adverse infant effects. Lactation: no human data (p. 22).

## Caveats
The label gives no clearance in L/h or mL/min and no compartmental parameters; renal, age and weight effects are
categorical percentages from prophylaxis trials.

## Verification against stanpumpR
- Label half-life 17-21 h: **matches** the label (12.3 p. 25). Whether the model's curve behaves so is a derived
  check: at Delavenne's reference patient a single 2.5 mg dose falls to half its peak 17.4 h after the peak, and
  the day-7 peak/trough is 0.43/0.21 mg/L against the label's 0.39-0.5 / 0.14-0.19 (derived: my simulation of the
  code's parameters). **matches** (the trough is slightly above the label range).
- Not cited by the code but relevant to it: the code says clearance is "about 15% lower in moderate ... renal
  impairment" (`R/drugs_fondaparinux.R:36-37`, help line 15). The label gives 40% lower for CrCl 30-50 and 55% for
  <30; Delavenne gives 25% and 43%. Neither source supports 15% (reported under PMID_20539905).
- The model's steady-state volume (23.7 L, derived from Delavenne) is about twice the label's 7-11 L apparent
  volume in healthy adults; an observation, not a code claim.
- "fondaparinux is essentially completely absorbed" (`R/drugs_fondaparinux.R:40`, help line 7): **matches** the
  label's 100% absolute bioavailability.
