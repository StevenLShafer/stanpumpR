# Alqahtani 2021: micafungin PopPK in patients with and without cancer

## Study
Prospective single-centre observational PK study, King Saud University Medical City, Riyadh. 19 adult inpatients (10 with cancer, 9 without), 133 plasma samples (7 per patient: 1, 2, 4, 6, 8, 12, 24 h from start of infusion). Micafungin 100-150 mg once daily in 100 mL saline over 60 min; all cancer patients 100 mg/day, non-cancer 7 at 100 mg and 2 at 150 mg (p4). Plasma total micafungin by HPLC-UV (273 nm), anidulafungin internal standard; total, not free. Table 1 (p4, mean SD): age 47.3 (12.3) cancer vs 51.1 (19.1); weight 63.4 (18.2) vs 69.8 (15.7) kg; height 162.2 vs 163.1 cm; CLCr 103 vs 99 mL/min; 60% vs 67% male. Monolix 4.4 (SAEM), both groups co-modelled; covariates tested stepwise (-2LL). Monte Carlo (n = 1000, R) for 100, 150, 200 mg/day in 50, 70, 100 kg patients with AST/ALT 2-3 x ULM; target total plasma AUC0-24/MIC 3000 (non-parapsilosis Candida) and 285 (C. parapsilosis), success if PTA > 90% (p4).

## Model and parameters
Two-compartment, linear elimination from the central compartment, combined (proportional + additive) error. Table 2 (PDF p6, read on the page image): typical values; IIV as CV%; RSE in brackets. Parameters are absolute (L, L/h), not per kg. The paper says weight, AST and ALT affected CL, but the printed values are for the unadjusted model with no covariate equation given; it does not say at what covariate values the Table 2 estimates apply.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No effect-site model, ke0, EC50 or MEAC (not an opioid). The PD end point is the PK/PD index total plasma AUC0-24/MIC (targets 3000 and 285, taken from Andes 2011, ref 31, p4). No concentration threshold other than that.

## Covariates
Significant on CL: body weight, AST, ALT (both groups). On V1: BMI, body weight, total bilirubin, albumin. Age, height, sex, creatinine, CLCr, SOFA not retained (p4-5). Cancer status acts on CL only (1.2 vs 0.6 L/h). The "higher CL in cancer" is a comparison between the two groups of this study (n = 10 vs 9); the discussion also says CL in both groups was lower than the 0.84 L/h reported in other populations (p9, refs 22-25, 32-39). Simulated AST/ALT changes did not change PTA; weight did (Table 3).

## Caveats
n = 19; the authors note total not unbound drug, heterogeneous and terminally ill patients, no safety or outcome data (p10). Only 100 mg once daily in the cancer arm. Covariate equations not printed. Discussion text on parapsilosis breakpoints is stated for pooled weights and does not map one-to-one onto the weight-specific Table 3 rows. The estimated CL (0.6-1.2 L/h) is low compared with the ICU values cited in the repository's own literature search (Martial 2017 CL 1.10 L/h, V1 17.6 L; docs/literature-search-2026-11-16.md:391).
