# Unichem doxazosin tablets US label (DailyMed), 2024

## Study
This is the generic immediate-release doxazosin mesylate label from Unichem (ANDA 212329). It is the doxazosin IR
tablet the registry names, so there is no source file problem.
- **Strengths:** doxazosin mesylate equivalent to 1, 2, 4 or 8 mg of doxazosin free base (3, p4).
- **Indications:** BPH and hypertension.
- **Dosage (2.1-2.3, p3-4):**
  - Monitor BP for at least 6 h after the first dose and after each increase. Restart at the initial dose after a
    gap of several days.
  - BPH: 1 mg daily, titrated at 1-2 week intervals to 2, 4 and then 8 mg (maximum 8 mg).
  - Hypertension: 1 mg daily, doubled as needed up to 16 mg once daily.

**Other PK facts (12.3, p12):**
- Kinetics are linear and dose-proportional at 2-16 mg QD at steady state.
- Secondary peaks suggest enterohepatic recycling.
- Metabolised mainly by CYP3A4, with minor CYP2D6 and CYP2C9 contributions. The active metabolites are not
  characterised.

**Not given:** no CL or V.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- **Hypertension (12.2, p11):** maximum BP reductions usually come 2-6 h after dosing, with a small rise in standing
  heart rate. The effect is greater standing. In normotensive men with BPH there was no clinically significant BP
  fall.
- **Metabolites:** the parent drug is mainly responsible for the effect. Metabolites, of which 6'-hydroxy is the
  most potent, contribute little.
- No concentration-effect parameters and no ke0.

## Covariates
- **Elderly:** half-life and oral clearance similar to younger subjects (12.3).
- **Renal impairment:** no significant change.
- **Hepatic impairment:** Child-Pugh A raised exposure by 40%. B and C are unknown, and severe impairment is not
  recommended (8.6).
- **Pediatrics:** not established (8.4).
