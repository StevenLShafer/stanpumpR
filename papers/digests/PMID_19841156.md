# Jin 2010: escitalopram population PK in US and Italian adults with major depression

## Study
- SPECTRUM trial (NCT00073697), Pittsburgh (n = 105) and Pisa (n = 67): 172 adults with nonpsychotic major depression,
  aged 20-65; escitalopram 5, 10, 15 or 20 mg/day for at least 32 weeks (alone or added to IPT).
- Table I: age 39.52 +/- 11.73 yr (20.41-65); weight 76.25 +/- 19.45 kg (31.9-139.7); BMI 26.93 +/- 6.20; 69% female;
  93% white. Pittsburgh about 14 kg heavier than Pisa.
- 473 plasma concentrations (320 + 153), weeks 4, 12, 24, 36; mean time after dose about 12 h (SD 11-12 h), broad
  spread. 73 Pittsburgh patients had MEMS dose timing.
- HPLC-UV (210 nm), linear 2.5-500 ng/mL. CYP2C19 *2, *3, *17 by TaqMan in 128 patients (Rudberg classes): RM 5, EM 77,
  IM 43, PM 3, missing 44. *3 not detected.
- NONMEM 5.1.1, FOCE-I; forward selection at p < .05.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. Not an opioid; no ke0.

## Covariates
CYP2C19 class (RM/EM vs IM/PM vs missing), age (power -0.336) and weight (power 0.333) on CL/F; BMI (power 1.11) on
V/F. Sex, race and site not significant once these were included. No difference found between RM and EM (n = 5).

## Caveats
Genotype missing in 44/172; very few RM and PM; mostly white women; SEs of age and weight terms large (42%, 54%);
Table III extraction leaves some SE/row assignments uncertain (the SE column has fewer entries than rows).
