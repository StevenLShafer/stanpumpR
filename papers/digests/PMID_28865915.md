# Mahajan 2017: CLADS vs propofol TCI (Marsh) in left-ventricular systolic dysfunction

## Study
Randomised, unblinded trial, single centre (Chandigarh), n = 40 ASA III/IV adults aged 18-65 y, LVEF <= 45%, BMI 15-30, open cardiac surgery (CABG or valve; 15/5 vs 13/7). 20 per arm: CLADS (BIS-guided closed-loop propofol, induction plus maintenance, "high/very-high risk" rate limits) vs manual propofol TCI with Diprifusor (Marsh model, plasma targeting). All got oral alprazolam 0.25 mg, fentanyl 3 ug/kg over 3 min before propofol, then fentanyl 1 ug/kg boluses as needed, vecuronium. Target BIS 50. Primary outcome: % of valid time BIS within +/-10 of 50. Demographics (Table 1): age 56.1 vs 51.75 y, weight 65.2 vs 62.85 kg, height 166.4 vs 162.9 cm, LVEF 38 vs 39%.

TCI induction used Dixon up-and-down: first target 1.8 ug/mL, +/-0.2 ug/mL by previous patient's success (MOAA/S < 3 then BIS 50 within 2 min, inside 5 min) or failure. Maintenance TCI adjusted by hand in 0.2 ug/mL steps when BIS >60 or <40, at least 3 min apart.

## Model and parameters
No PK model was fitted. The TCI arm used the Marsh model as built into the Diprifusor/Master TCI pump (no parameters printed). CLADS is a proprietary PID/PK-PD controller (patent cited); no parameters printed.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: BIS 50 after loss of consciousness (MOAA/S < 3); propofol TCI target concentration (plasma, Marsh model, calculated not measured); induction phase only.
- EC50 for BIS 50 on induction = 1.62 +/- 0.17 ug/mL by Dixon (6 failure-to-success pairs); probit EC50 1.62 (95% CI 1.45-1.79) ug/mL; EC95 1.87 (95% CI 1.73-2.96) ug/mL (abstract, PDF p 1; results). The EC95 upper CI as printed is far from the point estimate; not re-derivable.
- Population: low-EF cardiac surgery patients, fentanyl 3 ug/kg and alprazolam premedicated. The authors compare 1.62 with 1.9 ug/mL at LOC in Macquaire 2002 (ASA III cardiac, Marsh).
- No ke0, t1/2 ke0, tPeak or hysteresis reported. No MEAC. Not an opioid paper (fentanyl is co-medication only; no fentanyl concentrations).
- Performance (off-CPB, Fig 3, PDF p 6): % time within +/-10 of target 86 (82.75-89.75) vs 73 (61.75-82.25), p = 0.001; MDPE -4 (-4.5 to -1.5) vs -12 (-18 to -3.5), p = 0.024; MDAPE 10 (9.5-14) vs 15 (10-18.5), p = 0.021; global score 22.73 vs 32.16, p = 0.017.
- Propofol consumption (Table 2, row labelled mg/kg/h): 2.11 +/- 0.65 CLADS vs 2.6 +/- 0.54 TCI, p = 0.014. Induction dose 0.94 +/- 0.28 vs 0.82 +/- 0.19 mg/kg (p = 0.114); see open question.

## Covariates
LVEF only as a population selector. Authors attribute the low EC50 to low EF (39%) and note Marsh under-predicts concentrations when cardiac output is low (reduced hepatic clearance, further reduced by opioids). No quantitative covariate equation.

## Caveats
Small n, unblinded (Hawthorne effect), Marsh only (Schnider deliberately avoided for fear of high plasma peaks), results not extrapolable to other TCI models. EC50/EC95 come from 6 transitions of a 20-patient up-down series with fentanyl and alprazolam on board. Groups differed in age (median 61.5 vs 53 y) and EF.
