# Strathe 2023: semaglutide exposure and weight-loss model

## Study
Population PK plus semi-mechanistic exposure-response (weight) model of subcutaneous semaglutide in overweight or obesity with or without T2D. Development data: phase 2 dose-ranging trial (once daily 0.05-0.4 mg, 52 wk, N=957 randomized), STEP 1 (2.4 mg weekly, 68 wk, N=1961), STEP 2 (T2D; 2.4 and 1.0 mg weekly, N=1210) (Table 1, PDF p3). Evaluation on independent STEP 3, 4, 5. PK analysis used 2580 participants; exposure-response set 3818 participants aged 18-86, 2487 women, mean weight 104.8 kg (SD 22.6), BMI 37.5 (PDF p4). NONMEM 7.3, PsN 4.6.0. The assay is not described in the text.

## Model and parameters
Population PK: one compartment, first-order absorption and elimination, all covariates on apparent clearance; separate analyses for phase 2 and phase 3; parameters are in the supplement (Table S2) and not in the PDF. Weight was the most important covariate (exposure falls as weight rises); sex, age, race, ethnicity, renal function, injection site and glycaemic status were minor (PDF p4, Figure S1). Reference participant: non-Hispanic white woman 18 to <65 y, normoglycaemic, 110 kg, normal renal function, abdominal injection.

Exposure-response: indirect-response model plus a small fast direct component; placebo and lifestyle effect as a time-dependent direct function; weight = baseline + semaglutide effects + lifestyle effect (PDF p3). Covariates on Emax: sex (women lose more at similar exposure), T2D, baseline HbA1c (PDF p5). Equations and parameters are in the supplement.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: percent body-weight change (and BMI). No ke0, tPeak or hysteresis time constant in the main text (the indirect-response delay is in the supplement). The text says exposure from 0.05-0.4 mg daily informs a 50% effective concentration for weight loss but gives no value. Exposure-response appeared independent of dosing regimen (daily vs weekly) in people without T2D. No MEAC, no potency ratio. Prediction performance (Figure 3, PDF p6): week-52 weight MAE in STEP 3 6.84 kg with week-8 data, 4.89 with week-16, 3.3 with week-28; ROC AUC for >=10/15/20% loss 0.44-0.70 from baseline, 0.77-0.85 with week 8, 0.86-0.91 with week 16 (Figure 4, text p6-7).

## Covariates
On PK: weight (dominant), others minor. On weight loss: sex, T2D status, baseline HbA1c; age, race, ethnicity, BMI, region little effect.

## Caveats
Trial populations only, not real-world data (authors). Parameters live in the supplement. Sponsor-employed authors. The text and Figure 3 differ slightly on STEP 3 week-16 MAE (text 4.86, figure 4.89).
