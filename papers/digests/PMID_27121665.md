# Song 2013: model-supported development of the olmesartan/amlodipine/hydrochlorothiazide triple combination (CS-8635)

## Study
- Modeling and simulation (NONMEM VI, FOCE; S-Plus 8.1), in three phases. Phase 1, before the Phase III trial: PopPK and exposure-response (E-R) models from olmesartan (OM) monotherapy and the OM/AML and OM/HCTZ dual combinations were used to predict the triple combination (Table 1, PDF p.4). Phase 2, after the trial: models rebuilt on CS8635-A-U301 (TRINITY, n = 2458), 866-318 (n = 495) and CS8663-A-U301 (COACH, n = 1920), plus 13 Phase I studies with intensive PK. Phase 3: simulations of add-on and dose-escalation in non-responders to dual therapy (Table 5).
- Population (Table 2, PDF p.5): 4873 hypertensive adults, mean age 54.8 y (SD 11), mean weight 94.9 kg (SD 22), 2625 M : 2248 F, 14.1% diabetic. PK subjects: 555 in COACH and 916 in TRINITY.
- Drugs: OM 20 or 40 mg, amlodipine (AML) 5 or 10 mg, hydrochlorothiazide (HCTZ) 12.5 or 25 mg, once daily, oral. Only the 40/10/25 mg strength was tested in TRINITY.
- Assay: plasma LC/MS/MS (0.2 mL); validated range 1-1000 ng/mL for OM and HCTZ, 0.05-50 ng/mL for AML; LOQ 1, 0.05 and 1 ng/mL (PDF p.4, printed p.106 per text layer). Total plasma concentrations.
- PD end point: seated trough diastolic and systolic BP (SeDBP, SeSBP), taken before the day's dose at the primary visit (week 12, 8, 8 and 2 in the four studies).
- PopPK was used only to give each subject a post hoc steady-state AUC (AUCss = Dose / apparent clearance); E-R was fitted on AUCss, not on time-matched concentrations.

## Model and parameters
Two-compartment disposition for each drug (the structural model is stated; its parameters are in supplementary Tables S3-S5, not in this PDF). The covariate equations are in the main text (PDF p.5, printed p.107, Eq. 8-10; CL/F is apparent, L/h):

- OM: CL/F = 6.32 x (CLcr/111)^0.425. CLcr by Cockcroft-Gault.
- AML: CL/F = 23.4 x (Age/50.9)^-0.349.
- HCTZ: CL/F = 20.3 x exp(-0.219 x Female) x (CLcr/117.5)^0.499 x (Age/49.5)^-0.214.

E-R model (Eq. 1-5, PDF p.4): Response = placebo + MonoOM + MonoAML + MonoHCTZ + interaction terms (OM x AML, OM x HCTZ, AML x HCTZ, triple, each a product of the mono effects). OM and AML are saturable (Emax x AUC / (AUC + EAUC50)); HCTZ is linear in AUC. Covariates are power (Eq. 6) or linear (Eq. 7). ## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: seated trough SBP/DBP change from baseline (mmHg), a clinical BP effect, driven by steady-state AUC. No effect-site model: no ke0, t1/2ke0, tPeak or hysteresis. No MEAC or analgesic concentration (not an opioid or analgesic paper). No therapeutic concentration range is given for any of the three drugs.
- EAUC50 values are exposures (ng/mL x h of AUCss), not concentrations.
- The model has the combined effect somewhat below the sum of the mono effects (positive interaction terms on the negative mono effects).

## Covariates
- PK: OM clearance on CLcr; AML clearance falls with age; HCTZ clearance on CLcr, age and sex (female 0.80x, derived: exp(-0.219) = 0.803).
- E-R: baseline BP (larger effect at higher baseline), Black race on OM effect, body weight on AML effect (lower effect at higher weight), study-specific placebo response, plus age, sex and Hispanic race on the placebo effect.

## Caveats
- Industry-funded model-based development paper; the full PopPK parameter tables are supplementary only, so V and Q are not given in the PDF.
- The Black-race percentages in the text do not match the Table 3 coefficients (see open_questions).
- Table 3's footnote letters for the inter-subject variability row look transposed (a/b/c); the values 14.0 and 8.56 are as printed.
- The E-R model is on steady-state exposure at trough BP; it says nothing about the time course of BP after a single dose.

## Verification against stanpumpR
No citation to verify. Independent comparison with the library's models:

- **Olmesartan** (R/drugs_olmesartan.R:128-188, 65-70). Song's population-average OM CL/F is 6.32 L/h at CLcr 111 mL/min in hypertensive patients of mixed sex. The library's Yoshihara model, evaluated for a hypertensive 70 kg, 50-year-old at SCr 80 umol/L, gives 7.32 x exp(-0.239) = 5.76 L/h for a woman and 6.66 L/h for a man (the model header says the same, lines 76-77); the Song value lies between them, as expected for a mixed-sex cohort (derived: 5.76 and 6.66 from the file's own constants). Consistent in size and the same renal direction (Song exponent +0.425 on CLcr vs Yoshihara -0.344 on creatinine). Whether Song's dose basis is the medoxomil is not stated (open question), so the match is of magnitude only. Verdict: consistent, cannot verify the dose basis.
- **Hydrochlorothiazide** (R/drugs_hydrochlorothiazide.R:203-286). The library's total CL/F at CLcr 120 mL/min is 18.3 + 2.44 = 20.74 L/h (Van Wart; file header says the same, lines 133-138). Song's Eq. 10 gives 20.3 L/h for a man at CLcr 117.5 and age 49.5, and 16.3 L/h for a woman (derived: 20.3 x 0.803); at 120 mL/min the man is 20.5 L/h (derived: 20.3 x (120/117.5)^0.499). Agrees with the library within about 1% for men. Direction of difference: Song's female clearance is 20% lower than male at equal CLcr, which the library does not model (it has no sex term; its only covariate is CLcr). The comparison is against the library's own Van Wart model, which pooled mixed-sex study means. Not a discrepancy in a cited claim; noted as a possible refinement. Also the library CLcr power (1.38 on the renal part only) differs in form from Song's total-clearance exponent (0.499), consistent since the non-renal part dilutes the exponent.
- **Amlodipine**: no model in the library; Song's 23.4 L/h at age 50.9 and the age exponent -0.349 are recorded for use when one is added.
- Unit check: all Song clearances are L/h and apparent (CL/F); the library converts L/h to L/min by dividing by 60, so these would need the same conversion.
