# Hamp 2018: S-ketamine lowers the MAC of sevoflurane

## Study
Single-centre, randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial, Vienna, 2014-2018. 60 women (20 per group), ASA 1-3, age 30-65 y, BMI 18-30, breast surgery or inguinal hernia repair (skin incision 3-15 cm) under sevoflurane via laryngeal mask. Table 1 (PDF p4): age 48-50 y, weight 65-68 kg, height 166-167 cm, groups did not differ.
Inhalation induction (8% sevoflurane), then end-tidal sevoflurane held constant at least 15 min before incision. Study drug started after LMA insertion: placebo saline; low dose S-ketamine 0.5 mg/kg bolus over 30 s then 0.5 mg/kg/h; high dose 1 mg/kg bolus then 1 mg/kg/h (per-kg doses, S-enantiomer). MAC by up-and-down titration (first patient 1.6%, step 0.2%), end point gross purposeful movement within 1 min of skin incision; Choi estimator with 5000-sample bootstrap. Arterial blood sample at incision for S-ketamine by GC-MS (plasma, 100 uL, internal standard ketamine; calibration range 0.2-10 per mL, prefix not confirmed). BIS, HR, BP recorded every 2 min.

MAC reduction vs placebo: about 59% (low dose, difference -1.3 percentage points) and 77% (high dose, -1.7); high vs low 44% (-0.4) (printed p1246). The discussion says up to 76%.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: movement to skin incision, MAC (sevoflurane end-tidal, % at 1 atm). No ke0, tPeak, hysteresis or EC50 for ketamine is estimated; no ketamine-alone group, so the paper says synergy vs additivity cannot be concluded. BIS 2 min before incision was higher with ketamine (placebo 40 (6), low about 78, high about 79 on the text layer; Table 2 is scrambled and was not read on an image), attributed partly to the lower sevoflurane. Not an opioid paper; no MEAC or potency ratio.

## Covariates
None analysed. Women only; age restricted because MAC falls with age (about 20% between 30 and 65 y per the authors).

## Caveats
Women only; two ketamine doses; wide spread in plasma S-ketamine (SD about equal to the mean in the high-dose group); only MAC (not MAC95/99); n=20 per group is small for up-and-down designs; MAC reduction is a drug-combination result at one time (about 19 min after LMA insertion), plasma concentration measured once at incision; ketamine is S-enantiomer, so doses are not racemic-ketamine doses.

## Verification against stanpumpR
No repo location cites this paper. R/drugs_ketamine.R is a Domino 1984 racemic-ketamine PK model (analgesic band 0.1-0.16 mcg/mL, MEAC 0); it carries no sevoflurane interaction, and the repo's gas files (R/gasProperties.R etc.) contain no ketamine-MAC term that this paper would feed. Nothing to compare. The S-ketamine dose basis (mg/kg, mg/kg/h) and the possible 2x potency of S- vs racemic ketamine (stated by the authors from Geisslinger 1993) would matter if anyone mapped these doses onto the racemic model.
