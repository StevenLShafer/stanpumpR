# Sethi 2021: CLADS closed-loop propofol vs manual TIVA in thoracic surgery

## Study
Prospective, single-blind, randomized, single-centre trial (Sir Ganga Ram Hospital, New Delhi; NCT03307551). 30 adults 18-65 y, ASA II-III, elective one-lung-ventilation thoracic surgery; 28 analysed (14 per arm; 1 per arm lost to infusion-pump malfunction). Table 1 (PDF p.4): age 39 vs 38 y, weight 72 +/- 12 vs 70 +/- 11 kg, height 164 vs 167 cm, male:female 11:3 vs 10:4, anaesthesia time 386 vs 392 min.
CLADS group: propofol rate set by a BIS-feedback closed loop, target BIS 50. Manual group: propofol by a non-target-controlled infusion pump titrated by the anaesthesiologist to BIS 50. All: fentanyl preinduction (dose as printed "3 mg/kg", almost certainly ug/kg, unit symbol lost in print) and a fentanyl infusion after intubation ("1 mg/kg/h", likely ug/kg/h), atracurium, thoracic epidural for open cases, acetaminophen 1 g. No blood concentrations were measured; no PK or PD model is fitted, and the closed-loop algorithm's PK model is not described.
Primary outcome: % of time BIS within +/-10 of 50. Secondary: Varvel performance measures (MDPE, MDAPE, wobble, global score), propofol use, workload (infusion adjustments/h), haemodynamics, recovery, OAA/S sedation, awareness (modified Brice).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point is BIS (target 50, "adequate" 40-60). No ke0, EC50, Ce or Cp is reported. No difference in time to eye opening or extubation, OAA/S, or haemodynamics. One manual-arm patient had explicit recall (Table 4).

## Covariates
None modelled; arms balanced on baseline (standardized difference, Table 1).

## Caveats
n = 14/arm, powered for a 20% difference in time-in-range; propofol dose reduction is unadjusted for co-administered fentanyl/epidural; the manual arm used a non-TCI pump, so the comparator is manual rate titration, not TCI. One author holds the CLADS patent. Table 2 as extracted contains apparent label/value misalignment (e.g. a baseline-BIS row showing 1.27 vs 1.78, which are the induction doses).
