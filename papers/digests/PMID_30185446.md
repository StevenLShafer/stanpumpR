# Voinescu 2018: antiepileptic drug clearance by trimester

## Study
Prospective observational cohort, Emory Clinic, December 2002-November 2007 (PDF p2). Women with epilepsy enrolled
before conception or before 16 weeks' gestation, followed through pregnancy and the first postpartum year with dose,
adherence and seizure diaries; visits every 1-3 months; all draws at steady state (at least 5 days after a dose
change), time since last dose recorded but not standardised. 40 women, 44 pregnancies analysed (PDF p3). Clearance
analysis included monotherapy or non-interacting polytherapy. Assays: levetiracetam research samples by HPLC-UV
(Chromsystems) in one laboratory, plus clinical-laboratory results for all drugs (oxcarbazepine as its monohydroxy
derivative, MHD). **Apparent oral clearance = daily dose (mg/kg) / serum concentration (mg/L)**, i.e. weight-normalised
(PDF p3). Baseline = preconception or more than 4 weeks postpartum. Log clearance by mixed linear regression with a
random intercept per woman, adjusted for age, race and hours post-dose (quadratic terms). Seizure analysis: 15 women on
monotherapy, ratio to target concentration (RTC) against seizure worsening.

The Table (PDF p4, rendered): women / pregnancies levetiracetam 16 / 18, topiramate 8 / 10, valproate 5 / 5, phenytoin
7 / 7, oxcarbazepine 4 / 4; mean age 25.5-29.5 y; complete baseline plus three trimesters in 9, 4, 3, 3 and 2
pregnancies. Concentrations: oxcarbazepine 29, topiramate 46, phenytoin 32, valproate 27; levetiracetam 106 clinical and
74 research samples (PDF p4-5).

The levetiracetam second-trimester CI is printed as 1.68-1.73, which excludes its own estimate 1.421: a source error
(the true interval is not recoverable). The abstract gives p = 0.0001 for levetiracetam, the Results p < 0.0001. The
Discussion's 1.42-2.02 for the first trimester is close to, but not identical with, the printed CI 1.42-2.06. Peak
levetiracetam clearance varied 1.6-4.4-fold between women with more than 5 research samples (PDF p4).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Seizure frequency against RTC (average trimester concentration / baseline): worsening (6/15 women) was associated with
lower RTC in the first (p 0.012) and second (p 0.042) trimesters and over the whole pregnancy (p 0.004), not the third;
RTC below 0.65 went with worsening (Figure 4, PDF p7).

## Covariates
Age significant for all drugs; race for levetiracetam; hours post-dose a significant covariate.

## Caveats
Very small groups for every drug except levetiracetam (n 4-7 pregnancies), clinical-laboratory assays from several
laboratories, draws at varying times after the dose, doses adjusted clinically (the clearance accounts for dose), and
postpartum baselines for some women. Clearance is per kg: in late pregnancy, when weight has risen, the ratio of
absolute clearance would be larger than the ratio printed (inference; weights are not reported).
