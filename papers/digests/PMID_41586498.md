# Dejaco 2026: vancomycin protein binding in 228 in-patients

## Study
Prospective observational, University Hospital Regensburg, April-August 2023; leftover Li-heparin TDM plasma.
768 samples from 231 adults collected; 706 samples from 228 patients analysed (62 excluded). Age median 63
(18-86) y; 68% male; creatinine median 1.08 mg/dL; eGFR median 66 mL/min; albumin median 30.2 g/L (15.3-47.5);
total protein 56.4 g/L; CRP 64.4 mg/L (Table 1). ICU 367 samples / 96 patients; non-ICU 339 / 132 (Table 2).
Free drug: ultrafiltration (Vivafree 500, 30 kD Hydrosart), 300 uL plasma plus 10 uL 3 M potassium phosphate (pH
7.4) or 2 M HEPES (pH 7.5 at room temperature); 10 min at 100 x g/37 C, then 20 min at 1,000 x g/37 C. Post-
ultrafiltration plasma pH 7.54 +/- 0.12 (phosphate, n = 492) and 7.64 +/- 0.07 (HEPES, n = 152). Total and free by
HPLC-UV (PDA, 240 nm), ICH M10 validated; routine total by Roche VANC3 (KIMS) immunoassay. Statistics: GraphPad
Prism 10; random-intercept linear mixed model in R 4.5.1 (subject and occasion random effects, 3-day occasion gap).

## Model and parameters
No PK model; binding results:
- fu all samples (n = 706): mean 72.2 +/- 5.5% (CV 7.7%, range 53-93%) (Results, p. 3).
- fu per-patient means (n = 228): 72.2 +/- 4.6% (CV 6.4%, range 53-84%).
- Table 2 (median, range): fu all 72.1% (52.9-92.9); ICU 72.3% (52.9-92.2); non-ICU 71.8% (53.1-92.9); ICU vs
  non-ICU P = 0.465. VANtotal all 14.9 mg/L (3.85-57.4); VANfree 10.8 (2.86-42.4).
- Intra-individual CV (73 patients with >= 4 samples): median 5.3% (1.1-14.5%). (Abstract says 5.7%; Results 5.3%.)
- Free vs total R = 0.9846; slope of fu vs concentration not different from zero.
- Mixed model: overall CV 7.0% = 2.2% inter-individual, 3.8% inter-occasion, 5.5% residual; phosphate buffer
  raised fu by 5.8% (95% CI 4.9-6.7) vs HEPES; ward effect P = 0.013 (vascular surgery 66.9% vs neurosurgery 73.5%).
- By buffer: patients 73.6 +/- 7.8% (phosphate) vs 68.1 +/- 4.1% (HEPES); spiked QC 69.6 +/- 1.7% vs 66.7 +/-
  2.9% (Limitations; Methods). Stability study in spiked healthy serum: 72.5 +/- 1.8% (n = 30).
- HPLC vs immunoassay total: bias +0.221%, LoA -16.4 to 16.9%.
- Literature cited in Discussion: Stove 2015 fu 74.4 +/- 6.6% by ultrafiltration (unbuffered) and 72.0 +/- 5.7% by
  micro-equilibrium dialysis (HEPES).
- Conclusion: about 70% is a good estimator of fu in clinical practice.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD measured. Background: AUC/MIC or trough targets, referenced to unbound drug. Not an opioid.

## Covariates
None clinically relevant: fu independent of total concentration, albumin, total protein, age, sex, creatinine,
bilirubin, CRP, ICU status. Buffer type (methodological) and ward specialty were statistically significant.

## Caveats
Single centre; methodological change mid-study (phosphate to HEPES); incomplete covariate data (albumin in 387 samples, total protein in 92, IgA in 12/9). The mixed model was
fitted in three stages (Linear mixed-effects modeling, PDF/printed p. 4): the initial complete-case full-covariate
model had only 49 observations from 40 subjects, judged too small (little albumin/total protein overlap); dropping
total protein gave 284 observations from 138 subjects (189 occasions); after removing non-significant fixed effects
the final model used all 706 observations from 228 subjects (330 occasions). So (inferred from that sequence) the mixed-model
test of albumin rests on the 284-observation fit, and total protein was tested only in the 49-observation fit and the
pooled correlation (92 samples, Fig. 3). Only 4 mg/L LLOQ for the
immunoassay. Free fraction is ultrafiltration, not equilibrium dialysis, though prior work showed agreement.
Abstract (5.7%) and Results (5.3%) disagree on the intra-individual median CV.

## Verification against stanpumpR
- fu 0.70 recommended; mean 0.722 (range 0.53-0.93); 706 samples / 228 adult in-patients; ICU and non-ICU;
  independent of total concentration (3.9-57 mg/L), albumin and total protein; 37 C. **matches** (Results, Table 2).
- "pH 7.4" (R/drugs_vancomycin.R:66-67; vancomycin.md:11; antibioticThresholds.R:114): the phosphate buffer was pH
  7.4 but HEPES was pH 7.5 and measured plasma pH after ultrafiltration was 7.54-7.64. Approximately right;
  **matches** in substance.
- "0.725 in spiked healthy serum" (R/drugs_vancomycin.R:68): stability study 72.5 +/- 1.8% (n = 30). **matches**
  (the per-run spiked QC was lower, 0.696 phosphate / 0.667 HEPES; not mentioned in the repo).
- "Stove 2015 found 0.725 by equilibrium dialysis" (R/drugs_vancomycin.R:68-69; inst/help/drugs/vancomycin.md:11;
  R/antibioticThresholds.R:116): per this paper, Stove 2015 gave 72.0 +/- 5.7% by equilibrium dialysis (and 74.4%
  by ultrafiltration). 0.725 appears to conflate Dejaco's spiked-serum value. **discrepancy** (should read 0.72;
  verify against Stove 2015 itself).
- Threshold 1/0.70 = 1.43 mg/L, CSV endCe 1.4 (derived). **matches**.
- "The label's about 55% bound comes from ultrafiltration at room temperature or high g-force": paper attributes
  falsely low free concentrations in prior studies to low-temperature centrifugation, membrane cut-off/g-force and
  adsorbing membranes; it cites the SPC range 30-55% bound. Broadly supported; **matches** in substance.
