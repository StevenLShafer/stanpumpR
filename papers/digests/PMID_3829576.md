# Inturrisi 1987: methadone PK and analgesic PK-PD in chronic pain

## Study
8 opioid-tolerant patients with chronic pain (5 F, 3 M; 31-67 y, mean 50; 66.1 ± 27.9 kg; 5 with cancer); 3 studied
twice (11 studies, the repeat studies excluded from PK means). Single IV methadone HCl 10-30 mg (doses as HCl),
single-blind, given when pain was moderate to severe. Plasma at 2, 5, 10, 15, 30, 60, 120, 180, 240, 480, 1440,
2880 min. RIA (Ling), sensitivity 0.003 µg/mL. Pain intensity difference (VAS) and photographed pupil size to
240-300 min. Triexponential fits per patient (NONLIN). PK-PD: Sheiner effect compartment, Hill equation on
fraction of maximal pain-intensity difference.

## Model and parameters
Table II / text (harmonic means unless stated): t½π 1.2 min, t½α 25.8 min, t½β 23 h; Vc 0.16 ± 0.08 L/kg (mean;
range 0.02-1.34); Vβ (area) 3.59 ± 1.16 L/kg; plasma CL 81 mL/min; blood CL 106 mL/min; renal CL 3.9 mL/min (3.7%
± 3.0% of dose unchanged in 24 h urine); hepatic CL 102 mL/min; hepatic ER 0.089 ± 0.047 (Q 1500 mL/min assumed);
blood/plasma 0.75 ± 0.03; red cell/plasma 0.32; protein binding ~88% (89.4% at 0.073 µg/mL, 85.8% at 0.661),
free fraction ~12%. Predicted oral F ~90% from ER. Individual micro-rate constants are not reported.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: analgesia (pain intensity difference, VAS, as fraction of maximum), plus miosis (not modelled). 2 of 8
patients had no analgesia or miosis; 6 responders (7 fits).
- **t½ke0 (analgesia)**: harmonic mean 3.6 min, range 1.3-23.1 min (Table III). Individual (reassigned): pt 1 6.3,
  2B 1.3, 5 2.7, 6A 23.1, 6B 6.9, 7 2.9, 8 7.7 min (arithmetic mean 7.3, derived).
- Observed time to peak: analgesia and miosis peaked 2 to 5 min after the IV dose (Results). No tPeak is computed
  in the paper.
- **Css(50) analgesia**: 0.04-1.13 µg/mL, mean 0.29 ± 0.38 µg/mL (total plasma methadone, racemic, RIA); gamma
  ~2.
- **MEAC**: not measured. The paper quotes **0.06 µg/mL** as Gourlay et al.'s minimum effective analgesic
  concentration in relatively non-tolerant postoperative patients (p. 399, refs 3, 7), contrasting it with its own
  Css50 of 0.29 in tolerant patients. Gourlay's matrix (blood vs plasma) is not stated here.
- Potency: methadone nearly equipotent with morphine IM in cancer patients (intro, ref 1); not measured.

## Covariates
None modelled; prior opioid exposure not correlated with Css50 (small n).

## Caveats
Very small, heterogeneous, opioid-tolerant cohort; single-blind; RIA; bolus design limits PD precision (authors
say so); 2 non-responders excluded from PD; tables scrambled in the text layer.

## Verification against stanpumpR
- **tPeak 11.3 min attributed to Inturrisi**: **discrepancy**. The paper gives t½ke0 3.6 min (harmonic) and an
  observed peak of analgesia at 2-5 min. Simulating the former Inturrisi-based model (V1 0.18 L/kg, k10 0.00806,
  k12 0.408, k13 0.112, k21 0.06, k31 0.00114 /min, from git history) with ke0 = ln2/3.6 gives tPeak ≈ 3.4 min
  (derived); the arithmetic-mean t½ke0 7.3 min gives ≈ 4.9 min; tPeak 11.3 min needs t½ke0 ≈ 16 min. So 11.3 min is not
  derivable from this paper; its origin is undocumented. Help and investigators.md repeat the attribution.
- **MEAC 60 ng/mL racemic**: **partly verified**. 0.06 µg/mL appears in the paper, but as Gourlay's postoperative
  value quoted second-hand, not an Inturrisi measurement; the repo should cite Gourlay (1982/1984) as the source.
  Inturrisi's own analgesic Css50 in tolerant patients is about 5 times higher (0.29 µg/mL).
- Former disposition (context: V1 0.18 L/kg): the paper's mean Vc is 0.16 L/kg; 0.18 is patient 1's value, and the
  paper reports no micro-rate constants. Historical only; not in master.
- Old ke0/tPeak handling: per AGENTS.md rule, a tPeak carried from a different PK model should come from a
  re-solved ke0; here the source ke0 (t½ 3.6 min) is not what is carried.
