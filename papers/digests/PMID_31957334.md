# Langenhorst 2020: clinical trial simulation of fludarabine dosing

## Study
Clinical trial simulation (not a PK study) of randomised trials comparing current practice (160 mg/m2 as 40 mg/m2/day on days -5 to -2) with covariate-based or TDM-guided fludarabine dosing in allogeneic HCT. Framework: the published 2-compartment fludarabine PK model (ref 7, Langenhorst 2019) to simulate AUC(t0-inf); cause-specific hazard models for non-relapse mortality (NRM), relapse and graft failure from ref 10 (Blood Adv 2019); new Markov survival transitions. Simulation set: 194 adults (median age 46, range 20-74; weight 74 kg, 36-130; leukaemia/lymphoma 76%, MDS 24%), Table 1 (PDF p5, printed 276). 25 clearance sets per subject, with interoccasion variability; five concentrations on day 1 at about 1, 5, 6, 7, 8 h; about 8% of TDM samples dropped at random. Trials simulated 1000 times to 1 year after HCT, 50-220 patients per arm; power = fraction of trials with P < 0.05.

## Model and parameters
The PK model is cited, not re-estimated. The paper gives the dosing equation (PDF p4, printed 275, read on the image):

Cumulative dose (mg) = 20 mg*h/L x (0.782 x eGFR [L/h] + 3.24 L/h) x (BW/70 kg)^0.75, divided equally over 4 days. Dose is "in mg of the fludarabine base (administered as phosphate)"; eGFR is "in L/hour/70 kg"; BW in kg.

Table 1 (PDF p5): estimation set median AUC 24.7 mg*h/L (13.9-66); simulation set under current practice 26.8 (16.8-44, 2.5th-97.5th percentile), TDM 20.3 (17.3-24.2), covariate-based 20.3 (13.4-30); simulation-set creatinine clearance 120 mL/min/1.73 m2 (44-140). Median cumulative dose: TDM 122 mg/m2 (76-186), covariate-based 125 (86.7-142).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Exposure-response (ref 10, covariates re-selected here, PDF p5): NRM rises with AUC (polynomial spline, P < 0.001); graft failure rises at low AUC (linear, P = 0.04); relapse is not related to AUC. The only target is cumulative AUC. Results (PDF p7-8): optimal trial, TDM vs current practice, NRM end point, n = 70 per arm, power 82.3%; NRM 21% to 5.7%, overall mortality 39% to 26%, relapse 23% to 26%. Covariate-based vs current practice needs 120 per arm for NRM. Sensitivity analyses (Table 2, scenario-to-value assignment unconfirmed): power 73.8-82.4% across NRM RR 0.9, graft-failure RR 1.1, relapse penalty (Eq. 3) and 10% TDM failure (text says 78.8%). Not an opioid: no MEAC, ke0 or potency ratio.

## Covariates
Eq. 1: eGFR (L/h per 70 kg) and body weight (allometric 0.75 on clearance). The eGFR equation (Cockcroft-Gault or Schwartz, age caps) is not given here.

## Caveats
Conclusions depend on exposure-response models fitted to a mixed population (192 patients including children and benign disease); the authors tested several departures. Dose basis (base vs phosphate) is defined only by the word "base".
