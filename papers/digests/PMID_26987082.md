# Dowell 2016: CDC guideline for opioids in chronic pain (2016 MME table)

## Study
Clinical practice guideline (CDC, GRADE-type recommendation categories A/B and evidence types 1-4) for primary-care prescribing of opioids for chronic pain outside active cancer, palliative and end-of-life care. Note: the archived file is the JAMA publication of the same guideline (JAMA 2016;315(15):1624-1645, doi 10.1001/jama.2016.1464), not the MMWR typeset version the PMID points to; the content is the same guideline.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No MEAC, ke0, tPeak or concentration data. Dose thresholds (Recommendation 5): reassess at **>= 50 MME/day**; avoid or carefully justify **>= 90 MME/day**; naloxone offered at >= 50 MME/day or with other risks (Recommendation 8). Overdose risk rises with dose (e.g. 6.14-fold at >= 450 MME/day in one cited study). Methadone conversion factor increases with dose (checklist caution). The MME factors are equianalgesic potency ratios relative to oral morphine, based on mu-agonist activity.

## Covariates
None.

## Caveats
Conversion factors are adapted from other tables without primary data; they are for risk thresholds, not dose conversion. Methadone tiers stop at 80 mg/d.
