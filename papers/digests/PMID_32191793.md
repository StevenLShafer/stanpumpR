# Rybak 2020: revised vancomycin TDM consensus (ASHP/IDSA/PIDS/SIDP)

## Study
Consensus guideline and literature review (PubMed/Embase search), graded recommendations (A-C, I-III), Table 2
lists the primary recommendations. Scope: serious MRSA infections in adults, children, neonates, obesity, dialysis.

## Model and parameters
No PK model. Relations stated:
- AUC24 (mg·h/L) is the average steady-state concentration × 24; AUC24 estimated as total daily dose ÷ vancomycin
  CL (in the pediatric Bayesian discussion).
- Continuous infusion: target Css 20-25 mg/L, AUC24 = Css × 24 (rec. 10).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- Efficacy/safety target: individualised AUC/MIC_BMD 400-600 (MIC_BMD 1 mg/L) for suspected or definite serious
  MRSA infection (rec. 1, A-II), ideally achieved within 24-48 h (rec. 2).
- **Trough-only monitoring with a 15-20 mg/L target is no longer recommended** (rec. 3, A-II), on efficacy and
  nephrotoxicity data.
- Monitoring: Bayesian software with 1-2 levels (preferred), or two-level first-order equations (rec. 8).
- MIC: assume 1 mg/L empirically; with MIC_BMD > 1 mg/L the AUC/MIC ≥ 400 target is unlikely with usual doses; do
  not lower doses when MIC < 1 (rec. 5).
- Intermittent haemodialysis: predialysis 15-20 mg/L likely gives AUC 400-600 over the previous 24 h (rec. 14).
- AKI risk rises with AUC (above about 600) and with troughs ≥ 15-20 mg/L (review text).
- The AUC is of total vancomycin; protein binding is not part of the target.

## Covariates
Dosing by actual body weight; renal function, obesity, dialysis, age group handled by recommendation, not a model.

## Caveats
Consensus document; graded mostly A-II/B-II; target assumes MIC by broth microdilution; automated MIC methods vary.
