# Bührer 1990 (part II): EEG PK/PD of midazolam and diazepam

## Study
- 3 healthy non-smoking male volunteers (Table I: ages 36, 40, 36 y; weights 85, 78, 78 kg), each studied repeatedly, > 4 weeks apart, random order.
- Midazolam 7.5, 15, 25 mg infused at 5 mg/min; diazepam 15, 30, 50 mg at 10 mg/min (doses assumed equipotent a priori, p. 556). Not every subject received every dose.
- Radial-arterial samples every minute during infusion and for 5 min after, then at increasing intervals until EEG recording stopped (subject awake).
- Assays: midazolam GC-ECD (Himberg, Helsinki); diazepam GC-ECD (Greenblatt, Boston); parent drug separated from metabolites.
- End point: fronto-occipital EEG, aperiodic waveform analysis, voltage per second (µV); part I (48:544-554) chose this parameter. Also loss/return of response to "move your toes".
- Analysis: (1) nonparametric PK/PD (Unadkat/Sheiner; ke0 chosen to collapse hysteresis) and (2) parametric: three-compartment PK fitted to Cp (zero-order input), then ke0, Emax, Cp50, N fitted to effect with PK fixed (sigmoid Emax; MKMODEL). Low-dose experiments had Emax fixed to the high-dose estimate.

## Model and parameters
Individual three-compartment PK parameters are not reported (fitted but not printed).

Descriptive (Table II): EEG onset (5% change) 0.5-1 min after start of infusion; half-peak 1-2 min. Peak EEG time in subject S: diazepam 2.8, 8.0, 8.3 min (15, 30, 50 mg; Fig. 3); midazolam 3, 15, 23 min (Fig. 2). Loss of responsiveness 2-4 min after start of infusion, independent of dose; 15 mg diazepam did not cause unresponsiveness, 7.5 mg midazolam did (6 min).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: EEG voltage (aperiodic analysis), arterial plasma, total drug.
- Steady-state potency ratio midazolam:diazepam 5.5-6.3 (Cp50 ratio; p. 565); authors distinguish this from bolus dose potency (3-6x cited).
- Midazolam's equilibration about three times slower than diazepam's; one midazolam experiment in subject P gave a diazepam-like t½ke0, unexplained and included in the table.
- Comparison values quoted: Greenblatt 1989 beta-band EC50 269 (diazepam) and 35.2 ng/mL (midazolam); Koopmans midazolam t½ke0 36.6 min (oral, venous).

## Covariates
None (n = 3).

## Caveats
- Three subjects; too few for formal variability analysis (authors).
- Infusion rates not equipotent (2:1 rate assumed; later evidence 3-6:1), which may blur onset comparisons (authors).
- Effect-site concentrations are apparent (partition coefficient fixed at 1).
- Parametric and nonparametric t½ke0 differ by ~ 20%; which one a user carries matters for tPeak.
