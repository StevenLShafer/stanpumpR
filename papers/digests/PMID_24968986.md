# Hill 2014: actinomycin D population PK in children

## Study
- Two UK pharmacology studies, 158 patients under 21 y recruited 2004-2012 (median age 4.6 y, range 0.3-19.8; 78 M / 80 F); Wilms tumour 48%, rhabdomyosarcoma 30%, Ewing's 18% (p. 744).
- Act D by short IV infusion (1-5 min), 0.4-1.6 mg/m2, absolute dose 0.19-2.5 mg, capped at 2 mg in larger children; 0.02-0.05 mg/kg for infants under 1 y or under 10 kg (pp. 743-744).
- Samples (2 mL, heparinised, central line, line flushed with saline): pre-dose, 5, 15, 30 min, 2, 4, 8, 24, 26 h. Plasma LC-MS/MS, LLOQ 0.25 ng/mL, range 0.25-100 ng/mL (p. 743). Plasma stored at -20 C; samples older than 6 months were judged unstable and 14 patients' data excluded; another 14 excluded for contamination.
- PK set: 650 samples, 117 patients. NONMEM 7.2, ADVAN11/TRANS3, FOCE-I; PsN bootstrap and VPC (2,000 simulated datasets).
- Genotyping of ABCB1 1236C>T, 2677G>T/A, 3435C>T in 140 patients.

## Model and parameters
Three-compartment, linear. All CL/Q scaled by (WT/70)^0.75 and all V by (WT/70)^1.0 (exponents fixed), parameters expressed for a 70 kg patient. Proportional residual error. Median empirical-Bayes individual values at actual weights (text, p. 746; derived by the authors, not scaled to 70 kg): CL 5.3 L/h (range 1.5-23.5), Q2 5.5, Q3 13.9 L/h, V1 1.9 L, V2 4.4 L, V3 108 L. The abstract's "13.9 L/h/70 kg and 7.5 L/70 kg" are the Table 2 CL and V1. Note that Q3 13.9 L/h (individual median) and CL 13.9 L/h/70 kg (typical) share a number but are different quantities.

Other facts: observed concentration at 5 min median 128 ug/L (64.7-186, n=13); at 15 min 24.4 ug/L (5.3-122, n=101); at 24 h 1.8 ug/L (0.7-4.8, n=73). Mean AUC at 1.5 mg/m2: 9.3 mg·min/L (capped, BSA > 1.3 m2, n=12) vs 12.8 (uncapped, n=32), P=0.0003 (Fig. 4).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No effect-site model, ke0, EC50 or concentration-response relation. No relation was found between CL, V1, dose or AUC and grade 3-4 toxicity (p. 747). Not an opioid; no MEAC. Not applicable.

## Covariates
Body weight by fixed allometry only (above). Serum creatinine (median 37 umol/L), catheter type, ABCB1 genotypes, toxicity grade were examined outside NONMEM on the etas and had no effect after weight (catheter-type effect vanished once weight was included). No age or sex effect reported beyond weight.

## Caveats
- Covariates were screened outside NONMEM on empirical-Bayes etas because of missing data.
- Few patients per rare genotype (2677 TA); the authors say the null genotype result does not rule out an effect.
- Median CL/V1 were described as similar to but generally higher than Edwards 2012 (that comparator is another study's estimate, not the stanpumpR library).
- Weights are not tabulated in the main text (Table 1 text layer is scrambled); the model is size-scaled.
- Terminal volume V3 is large (388 L/70 kg): late concentrations of 1-5 ug/L at 24 h.
