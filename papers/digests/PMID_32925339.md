# Sahinovic 2020: propofol Ce vs BIS / qCON hysteresis

## Study
Prospective observational PD analysis, 165 patients randomly drawn from a larger database (Hospital Clinic Barcelona, Sep 2016-Jul 2017), ASA 1-3, outpatient gynaecologic and abdominal surgery, propofol-remifentanil TIVA by effect-site TCI (Fresenius Base Primea pumps): Schnider model for propofol, Minto for remifentanil. Typical case: remifentanil Ce 1.5 ng/mL first, propofol Ce target 4.5-6 ug/mL to loss of response, remifentanil then 4 ng/mL (laryngeal mask) or 6 ng/mL (intubation). 9 of 165 were male; 6 were excluded for data errors (3.6%). Data logged by RUGLOOP II; one tenth of the BIS/qCON samples was randomly kept for modelling. Demographics (Table 1, PDF p.5, image-read): age 53.1 +/- 15.4 yr (16-82); weight 66.8 +/- 12.5 kg (42-119); height 159 cm (140-176); LBM 44.9 +/- 4.95 kg; BSA 1.68 +/- 0.15 (printed unit kg/m2, a misprint for m2). Median session CePropofol 2.81 +/- 0.53 ug/mL (range 1-4.5); CeRemifentanil 2.66 +/- 0.65 ng/mL (1-4.47); session length 71.7 +/- 25.2 min. No concentrations were measured: Ce values are predictions of the pump models. Monitors: BIS (Medtronic) and qCON (Quantium).

## Model and parameters
Direct PD model: sigmoid Emax, PD = PD0 x (1 - Emax x Ce^g / (Ce50,INT^g + Ce^g)), with PD0 fixed at 100 and Emax at 1. Ce50,INT = Ce50 x (1 - POTmax x CeRemi^gR / (Ce50,Remi^gR + CeRemi^gR)). IIV lognormal on Ce50 and gamma; additive residual error; NONMEM 7.3 FOCE. Lag-time PD model: the predicted Ce of each drug is time-shifted (Ce at t minus the lag) on a grid of lags (propofol 10-70 s, remifentanil 0-300 s) and the lags chosen by objective function. Fit (PDF p.5-6): median individual RMSE, direct model BIS 9.38 (IQR 7.92-11.23), qCON 8.41 (7.04-10.2); lag-time model BIS 7.87 (6.49-9.90), qCON 6.56 (5.28-8.57) (abstract). Median RMSE fall 11.9% (IQR 4.58-19.4%) for BIS and 20.3% (7.90-31.0%) for qCON. The likelihood profiles put the lag estimates within +/-1 s.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End points: BIS and qCON index (processed frontal EEG), not the Schnider CUP (canonical univariate parameter) that the Schnider model was fitted to.
- Hysteresis: the direct model leaves a loop between Schnider Ce and BIS/qCON (Figure 1, PDF p.5); the fitted extra lags are 49 s (BIS) and 53 s (qCON) for propofol. The paper gives no ke0, t1/2 ke0 or tPeak of its own: the Schnider effect-site ke0 used in the Figure 3 simulation is 0.456 /min (Figure 3 legend, PDF p.7, text layer).
- Ce50 for propofol falls from 6.24 to 3.66 ug/mL (BIS) and 8.62 to 3.62 (qCON) once lag is allowed, i.e. the apparent Ce50 is inflated by unmodelled delay; gamma 0.75 to 0.90 (BIS), 0.59 to 0.93 (qCON). Population: 156 women and 9 men, propofol with remifentanil; the Ce50 is "conditional on this sex ratio" per the authors. Concentration basis: model-predicted effect-site, ug/mL.
- Remifentanil (opioid audit): remifentanil Ce enters only as a potentiator of the propofol Ce50. Ce50,Remi 1.07 ng/mL (BIS lag), 0.89 ng/mL (qCON lag); POTmax 0.60 (BIS) / 0.57 (qCON) = maximal fractional reduction of propofol Ce50 (derived: 40-43% of its Ce50 left at full effect); remifentanil lag 97 s (BIS) and 245 s (qCON), with wide RSE (qCON Ce50,Remi RSE 15.8%, gammaR RSE 46%). Basis: Minto-model predicted Ce, ng/mL; no MEAC, analgesic concentration, measured concentration, assay or equianalgesic ratio is reported. The remifentanil lag is not a ke0: it is the time shift that best collapses the loop on BIS/qCON, and the paper does not claim it as a remifentanil effect-site equilibration constant.
- Monitor processing delay is the authors' explanation (index calculation 14-155 s cited from Pilge and Zanner); it is not a pharmacologic ke0.

## Covariates
None estimated (age dependence of the lag is noted as untested). Only the population lag was estimated, not per patient.

## Caveats
Predicted not measured concentrations, so Ce50 inherits any error in the Schnider and Minto models (and the lag partly absorbs kinetic misspecification). Only 9 men. Observational clinical data, not steady state, with remifentanil co-administered and boluses of rocuronium. Lag grid fixed at 10-70 s (propofol) and 0-300 s (remifentanil); remifentanil lag poorly determined for qCON. Models were not used to build a new PK-PD model, and monitor-specific. Internal inconsistencies: POTmax (text 0.59/0.56 vs Table 2 0.60/0.57), loop direction (clockwise vs counterclockwise), BSA unit.
