# Bertin 2026: levosimendan, OR-1855 and OR-1896 on VA-ECMO

## Study
- Prospective observational study, two Swiss ICUs (CHUV Lausanne, HUG Geneva). 21 patients on veno-arterial ECMO given levosimendan as clinical care: 15 adults (12 men; age 62, 18-75 y; weight 79, 52-125 kg) and 6 neonates and infants (24 days, 13-164; 3.4 kg, 2.7-5.8) (Table 1).
- ECMO for cardiac arrest, cardiogenic shock or failure to wean from bypass; 20% of adults on CVVHD; albumin 28 g/L; 5 adults and 5 children died in the ICU.
- Arterial plasma; UHPLC-MS/MS, LLOQ 0.1 ng/mL for all three; M3 method for data below the LLOQ.
- NONMEM; doses in umol/h and concentrations in nmol/mL to avoid molecular-weight discrepancies.

## Model and parameters
Levosimendan: two compartments, CL and V1 scaled by body weight (CL exponent fixed at 0.75, V1 0.57 estimated; reference 70 kg). One transit compartment (ktransit in = ktransit out) leads to OR-1855, which is eliminated (keM1, fixed 0.01 /h) or acetylated to OR-1896 (kM2); OR-1896 is eliminated (keM2, fixed 0.01 /h) or converted back (kM2-M1, fixed). V3 = V4 = V1 (not identifiable). Table 2 (final estimate, RSE):

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None modelled. OR-1896 is the long-acting active metabolite; no effect site or tPeak.

## Covariates
Body weight (on CL, V1, keM1); childhood on kM2. Tested and not retained: age, CRRT, albumin, bilirubin, ECMO flow, time on ECMO, antibiotics.

## Caveats
- n = 21, critically ill on ECMO; the parameter estimates are printed rounded.
- The metabolites' elimination was not observed and is fixed; their volumes are an identifiability assumption.
- Jonsson 2003 (190 adults with heart failure, parent only) is the larger adult source; not in the archive.
