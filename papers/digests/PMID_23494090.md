# Dussaussoy 2014: dual closed-loop TCI propofol and remifentanil, anesthesiologist workload

## Study
Single-centre (Angers), prospective, randomized, single-blind trial (NCT00392158). Adults 18-80 y, ASA I-III (the text prints "B III"), elective vascular or thoracic surgery. n = 18 per group: TCI Loop (BIS-driven PID controller, Infusion Toolbox 95 v4.11, adjusting propofol and remifentanil effect-site targets) versus TCI Manual (skilled anesthesiologist adjusting the same TCI to BIS 40-60). Premedication hydroxyzine 75 mg orally. Both groups used TCI on the Schnider propofol and Minto remifentanil population PK sets. Controller aimed at BIS 50 during induction and 40-60 during maintenance. Sample size: 15 patients for 95% power to detect a 40% fall in BIS watching, alpha 0.01. Primary outcome: workload (looks at the BIS monitor, manual target changes, counted by an independent observer). Results: Table 1 (p.37) demographics, Fig. 1-2 (pp.38-39).

Workload: in TCI Loop, no manual adjustment during induction in 15/18 patients (one adjustment in two, two in one); none during surgery in 10/18; the Manual group always needed adjustments (up to 24 in one patient). BIS-monitor looks fell on average 53% with the loop (p.37).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point is BIS (EEG), not analgesia. Opioid audit: no MEAC or analgesic concentration, no ke0, t1/2 ke0 or tPeak, and no equianalgesic or potency ratio is reported. Remifentanil is controlled through effect-site targets from the Minto model; the target values above are the only remifentanil concentrations given, they are controller settings and not an effect threshold.

## Covariates
Patient sex, age, weight and height entered the Schnider and Minto models; no covariate analysis. Manual group slightly sicker (ASA III 12/18 vs 7/18); mean age 63 +- 11 (Loop) vs 67 +- 15 (Manual); BMI 25 in both.

## Caveats
Small pilot, single centre, two experienced investigators, a human observer (no video), single blinding only; MAP differed between groups (confounded by ASA status); emergence not studied; two of the authors patented the controller algorithm.
