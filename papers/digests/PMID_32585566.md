# Zhou 2020: remimazolam population PK/PD in general anesthesia

## Study
- Pooled analysis of 11 Phase 1-3 trials (PK) and 8 trials (PK-PD) by Paion/Nuventra. PK: 4,448 observations from 689 subjects (61% male; mostly Asian, 19% White, 9% Black or other; ASA 50% I, 36% II, 14% III). PK-PD: 24,612 BIS observations from 604 subjects (59% male; 8% White; 29% BMI 25-30, 2% BMI > 30) (PDF pp. 4-5).
- General anesthesia studies: remimazolam 0.1-30 mg/kg/h for induction, then 1 mg/kg/h (range 0.5-3) for maintenance, adjusted to BIS 40-60, with remifentanil 0.01-1 microg/kg/min. ICU study: induction 0.10-0.50 mg/kg/h, maintenance up to 2.0 mg/kg/h, up to 4.5 days. Healthy volunteers: 1 mg/kg/h for 1 h or boluses 0.1-0.5 mg/kg or 5-8 mg. Healthy-volunteer studies had no opioid (PDF pp. 2, 4-5).
- Mixed arterial and venous samples (3 studies arterial only, 5 venous only, 3 both); they were put in one compartment with separate residual errors because simultaneous samples in the first 5 min were too few (n = 1) (PDF p. 4).
- NONMEM, bootstrap; sequential PK then PK-PD. Simulations of 1,000 virtual subjects in R 3.3.1 (1:1 sex, 3:1 Asian:non-Asian), remifentanil 0.2 microg/kg/min in OR (0-6 h), 0.05 microg/kg/min post-surgery (PDF pp. 3-4, Table 1).

## Model and parameters
- Three compartments, allometric exponents 0.75 on clearances and 1 on volumes, reference 70 kg (total weight). Covariates: ASA 3 on V1 and V2, extracorporeal circulation (EC) on CL and V1, female on CL, and a time-dependent fall in CL (sigmoid Emax). No effect of age or of cumulative dose on CL (PDF p. 4).
- Table 2 (PDF p. 4, rendered). Units L/h and L per 70 kg; I add L/min (derived: /60).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point is BIS, effect compartment with inhibitory sigmoid Emax (equation 2, PDF p. 2). keo 8.08 /h (0.135 /min, derived). No t1/2ke0 or tPeak is reported. keo is 17% faster with BMI > 25 and 48% slower in Asians (text, PDF p. 5; Table 3 multiplier 0.520).
- IC50 remimazolam 0.52 microg/mL is for the first dose; it grows 31.2% per cumulative mg/kg (acute tolerance, as the authors read it). The authors say the size of this tolerance does not match clinical data, which stayed at a constant 1 mg/kg/h.
- Remifentanil synergy was the largest covariate. Remifentanil concentration was not measured, so its infusion rate (microg/kg/min) stands in: IC50 0.758 microg/kg/min, IMAX_BIS 12.7. No MEAC, no analgesic end point and no equianalgesic ratio. Remifentanil ke0 not reported.
- IMAX_BIS was fixed at 73.3 because protocol-driven BIS 40-60 would otherwise yield an estimate of about 40 (PDF p. 5).
- Simulated sedation: 6 mg/kg/h until loss of consciousness, then 1 mg/kg/h for general anesthesia (BIS 40-60 "adequate") and 0.25 mg/kg/h for post-surgical sedation (BIS 60-80 "adequate"), with the lower 0.5 and 0.125 mg/kg/h for sensitive scenario #3 (Table 1).

## Covariates
- PK: ASA 3 (V1 lower, V2 higher), EC (CL up, V1 up), female (CL +11%), time (CL falls). No age effect. PD: BMI > 25 and Asian race on keo; cumulative dose and remifentanil rate on IC50; no age or ASA effect on IC50.

## Caveats
- About 72% of subjects Asian. Only 7 ICU subjects with samples after 24 h inform the time-dependent CL (IT50 31.5 h, IIV 82%).
- IMAX_BIS fixed and the model described by the authors as "fit for purpose" and conservative; BIS only, no suppression ratio.
- Remifentanil effect is on infusion rate, and betaEmax bootstrap CI spans 0.634-75.9.
- Remimazolam arterial/venous differences not modelled physiologically.
- Several data sources are unpublished or "data on file".
