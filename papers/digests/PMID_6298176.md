# Delaplane 1983: ceftriaxone against group B streptococci, in vitro and in infant rats

## Study
- **In vitro**: 24 clinical isolates of type III group B streptococci (GBS) from neonates (bacteraemia, meningitis,
  osteomyelitis). Broth dilution in Todd-Hewitt broth, inoculum 10^5 cfu/mL, overnight at 35 °C in 5% CO2.
  MIC = lowest concentration with no visible growth; MBC = lowest concentration killing 99.9% of the inoculum
  (PDF p2, printed p70).
- **In vivo**: 4- and 5-day-old Sprague-Dawley rats, bacteraemia/meningitis induced by intraperitoneal inoculation
  with 4 strains (strain #84 at 10^1 cfu, others 10^3 cfu). Treatment: ceftriaxone 2 mg/kg ip every 8 h for 5 doses
  vs ip saline controls.
- **PK**: a sixth dose given to 4 rats; blood and CSF (0.01 mL each) at 0.5, 2, 3.5 and 6 h. Agar diffusion bioassay
  with E. coli 1346; lowest reliable concentration 0.1 mg/L. Total (not free) drug.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- Not an opioid paper; no MEAC, ke0 or potency ratio.
- Efficacy (Table I, PDF p3): 57/60 inoculated rats bacteraemic (≥10^3 cfu/mL); 38 bacteraemia alone, 19 with
  meningitis. Treated 45 (30 + 15), all survived; controls 12 (8 + 4), all died within 26-48 h (χ² P < 0.001).
  Blood and CSF sterile at 8 and 54 h after the last dose in all treated animals.
- The dose was chosen to give peak CSF levels 2-4 times the MBC of the test strains (Discussion, PDF p4).

## Covariates
None.

## Caveats
- MIC/MBC given only as ranges for 24 strains of a single serotype (III); no distribution, no MIC50/90 or MBC50/90.
- Non-standard dilution series (0.05, 0.1, 0.2, 0.4 mg/L) rather than the 2^n scale.
- PK in 4 infected infant rats, bioassay, total drug; not transferable to human PK. The authors compare the rat
  half-life to 4.5 h in rabbits and 8 h in adult humans (Seddon 1980), i.e. the rat value is shorter than human.
