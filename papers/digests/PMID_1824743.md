# Shafer 1991: PK/PD simulation and rational opioid selection

## Study
Computer simulation only (no new subjects). Fentanyl and alfentanil PK from Scott and Stanski 1987; sufentanil PK
from Hudson 1989 (aortic surgery); ke0 for fentanyl and alfentanil from Scott and Stanski, sufentanil from Scott,
Cooke and Stanski 1991. Effect site as a fourth compartment (volume 1/1000 of V1). Euler solution, 1-s step, C on an
80386 under MS-DOS (p. 54). Simulated: bolus (plasma as % of initial; effect site as % of peak), bolus plus 10-min
infusion holding peak Ce, and effect-site-targeted infusions of up to 600 min with the time for 10-90% decreases in
Ce after stopping (the decrement curves later called context-sensitive decrement times).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Opioid audit:
- ke0 (EEG spectral edge, the end point of the source studies): fentanyl 0.147, alfentanil 0.77, sufentanil 0.227 /min
  (Table 1). Intro text (p. 53) gives equilibration half-times of 5-6 min for fentanyl and sufentanil and 1.5 min for
  alfentanil, which do not match the Table 1 ke0s (derived t½ke0: 4.7, 0.9, 3.05 min).
- Time to peak effect-site concentration after a bolus (simulated, Fig. 2, p. 55): fentanyl 3.6 min (Ce peak 17% of
  initial Cp), alfentanil 1.4 min (37%), sufentanil 5.6 min (20%; peak very flat). Authors note these agree with the
  measured time of peak ventilatory depression (p. 61, ref 25).
- IC50 for EEG depression (Table 3, p. 59): fentanyl 7.8 (Scott & Stanski), 6.9 (Scott 1985), 8.1 (Scott 1991) ng/mL;
  alfentanil 480 and 520 ng/mL; sufentanil 0.69 ng/mL; gamma 4.3 / 4.8 / 6.2.
- Potency ratio fentanyl : alfentanil : sufentanil ≈ 1 : 1/70 : 9, from both Table 2 clinical concentrations and the
  IC50s (pp. 58-59).
- Table 2 (p. 59), concentrations (ng/mL; effect-site, partly simulated from doses) that ablate responses or permit
  ventilation: adequate ventilation on emergence fentanyl 1.5, alfentanil 125, sufentanil 0.25; maintenance with
  N2O/potent vapor fentanyl 1.5-4, alfentanil 100-300, sufentanil 0.25-0.5; O2/N2O only 1.5-10, 100-750, 0.25-1.0;
  O2 only 15-60, 1000-4000, 2-8 (10-60 in one report); intubation with thiopental 3-5, 250-400, 0.4-0.6. These are
  literature syntheses, not a measured MEAC.
- Recovery: after a 10-h infusion a 20% fall in fentanyl Ce takes 45 min, about 4 times sufentanil/alfentanil;
  sufentanil faster than alfentanil for 50% decrease under 8 h and 80% decrease under 3 h (pp. 56-57).

## Covariates
None (simulations at typical values; sufentanil per 70 kg).

## Caveats
Simulation only; EEG is not a measure of analgesia; sufentanil PK from aortic surgery (Bovill alternative compared,
Fig. 7); Euler integration; the intro equilibration half-times differ from Table 1. The paper does not propose
carrying tPeak instead of ke0 between PK models — that argument is Minto 2003.
