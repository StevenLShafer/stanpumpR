# Baos 2025: the GAP trial of perioperative gabapentin

## Study
- Multicentre (7 UK NHS hospitals), double-blind, placebo-controlled, pragmatic RCT, April 2018 - May 2022.
- Adults ≥18 y having non-emergency cardiac, thoracic or abdominal surgery, expected stay ≥2 days. Excluded: on
  antiepileptics, eGFR <30 mL/min/1.73 m², weight <50 kg, gabapentin allergy.
- 1,196 randomized (500 cardiac, 346 thoracic, 350 abdominal); 600 gabapentin, 596 placebo; analysis 1,195.
  Median age 68-69 y (Table 1).
- Gabapentin 600 mg preoperatively, then 300 mg twice daily for 2 days once able to swallow; identical placebo.
- Primary: length of stay (end of surgery to discharge). Secondary: opioid use (IV morphine equivalents), NRS pain,
  Brief Pain Inventory at 4 weeks and 4 months, SAEs, EQ-5D-5L, SF-12.

## Model and parameters
No PK. Results:
- Length of stay: median 5.94 (IQR 4.08-8.04) vs 6.15 (4.22-8.97) days; HR 1.07 (0.95-1.20), P = 0.26 (Table 2).
- Opioids: no difference after cardiac surgery; thoracic day 1 geometric mean 9.4 vs 13.4 mg (ratio 0.73, 0.54-0.99);
  abdominal day 1 8.5 vs 13.8 mg (ratio 0.67, 0.50-0.90); none after discharge.
- NRS at 1 h: −0.81 (rest) and −0.82 (movement); at 120 h ≈ 0. Never >1 point difference.
- Pain present at 4 weeks 63.4% vs 53.3%, at 4 months 40.6% vs 33.2% (gabapentin vs placebo); severity similar.
- SAEs 195 (32.5%) vs 189 (31.7%); somnolence 1.8% vs 0.7%; respiratory depression 2 vs 1.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Clinical end points only; no concentrations measured. Not an opioid; no MEAC, ke0, tPeak or potency ratio.
Opioid use reported as IV morphine equivalents (conversion factors in the supplement, not read).

## Covariates
Stratified by specialty and site; subgroups by sex, minimally invasive vs open, pre/post COVID-19 (none different).

## Caveats
Single fixed dose and 2-day course; target sample (1,500) reduced to 1,020 minimum after COVID-19; 19-22%
protocol deviations; elderly/frail and other surgery types not studied (authors).
