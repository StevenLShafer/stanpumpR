# Fediuk 2021a: ertugliflozin population PK in healthy subjects and type 2 diabetes

## Study
Pooled population PK analysis of 15 studies (9 phase 1, 2 phase 2, 4 phase 3, sparse sampling in phase 2/3), 13,691 plasma concentrations from 2276 subjects (PDF p. 2-3).
- Table 1 (PDF p. 4): weight 86.9 +/- 19.7 kg, median 84.8 (42.6-197); age 55.7 +/- 11.6 y, median 57 (18-87); eGFR (4-variable MDRD) 85.9 +/- 24.3, median 86.6 (6.8-196) mL/min/1.73 m2; 1287 (56.5%) male; White 71.8%, Black 8.74%, Asian 13.8%, other 5.62%; healthy 192 (8.4%), T2DM 2084 (91.6%); fasted 275, fed 473, without regard to food 1697 (counts exceed 2276 by design).
- Renal function: about 44% normal, 41% mild, 14% moderate, 1% severe impairment (p. 4).
- Oral ertugliflozin; HPLC-MS/MS, LLOQ 0.5 ng/mL (14 studies) or 0.1 ng/mL (1); BLQ (5% of records) removed. 24 outliers excluded for undue influence on Vc/F.
- NONMEM 7.3, FOCE-I; full model estimation of covariates; bootstrap n = 1035.

## Model and parameters
Two-compartment, first-order absorption with lag time, first-order elimination (p. 3). Weight on CL/F, Vc/F, Vp/F, Q/F by allometry with exponents fixed at 0.75 (clearances) and 1.0 (volumes) (p. 4). eGFR values above 120 set to 120; age excluded from CL/F because collinear with eGFR (r = -0.531). Reference: 65-year-old healthy white man, 85 kg, eGFR 90, fasted (p. 7). IIV finally on CL/F only.

Supplement (supplement, 2026-10-27), sup0001.docx Equation 8 (final model, OMML), verbatim in structure:
CL/F = theta_TV x (BWT/85)^0.75 x (eGFR/90)^theta_eGFR x theta_PTST x theta_NSEX x theta_RACE2 x theta_RACE3 x
theta_RACE4, times exp(eta); Vc/F = theta_TV x (BWT/85) x (AGE/65)^theta_AGE x theta_NSEX x theta_RACE2-4 (no eta);
Vp/F = theta_TV x (BWT/85); Q/F = theta_TV x (BWT/85)^0.75; ka = theta_TV x theta_FOOD1^FOODEFF1 x
theta_FOOD2^FOODEFF2; F1 = 1 - FOODEFF1 x theta_F1,FOOD1 - FOODEFF2 x theta_F1,FOOD2. Equation 6 defines every
categorical term as theta^indicator (indicator 0 or 1), so the printed theta_PTST etc. in Equation 8 are factors applied
when the indicator is 1; Equation 7 makes the food effect on F1 a fractional DECREASE (0.0683 fed = 6.8% lower).
Indicators: PTST healthy 0 / T2DM 1, NSEX male 0 / female 1, RACE2 Black, RACE3 Asian, RACE4 other, FOODEFF1 fed,
FOODEFF2 without regard to food. Equation 4: residual error additive on log concentration; Equation 1: log-normal IIV.
The 120 cap on eGFR is stated in the Covariate Evaluation text, not written into Equation 8. Table S1: 15 studies
(I-1 to I-9, II-1, II-2, III-1 to III-4), subjects per study 6-736, doses 0.5-300 mg; phase 3 studies dosed without
regard to food and contributed Phase A data only. Table S2: sampling schemes; LLOQ 0.1 ng/mL in I-1 only, 0.5
elsewhere. Table S3: base-model steps (IIV on all five parameters failed, Vc/F IIV 1070 %CV; lag added, OFV -347.181;
food on ka and F1, -262.055; separate phase 1 and phase 2/3 residuals, -1674.818, final base model; estimated weight
exponents, -43.522, not adopted).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None modelled. Efficacy said to depend on AUC (average steady-state concentration), not Cmax. Background (introduction, p. 1-2): absolute bioavailability about 100%, protein binding 93.6%, glucuronidation 86%, oxidation 12%, renal excretion about 1.5%, NCA terminal half-life 11-17 h, steady state 4-6 days.

## Covariates
Figure 3 (PDF p. 8) gives typical-value ratios against the reference: CL/F at eGFR 45 / 60 / 120 = 0.730 / 0.832 / 1.14; T2DM 0.904; female 0.962; Asian 1.08. AUCtau ratios 1.37 / 1.20 / 0.88 for those eGFRs, T2DM 1.11, female 1.04, Asian 0.93. Vc/F: age 60 y 1.06, age 75 y 0.966, female 1.36, Asian 2.12.
- Derived checks: (45/90)^0.455 = 0.730, (60/90)^0.455 = 0.832, (120/90)^0.455 = 1.14, so the eGFR effect is a power of eGFR/90. (75/65)^-0.243 = 0.966 matches the age-75 point; (60/65)^-0.243 = 1.02 does not match the printed 1.06 for age 60, and no simple form found reproduces both; unresolved.
  (supplement, 2026-10-27) Equations 5 and 8 confirm the power form (AGE/65)^theta for age, so the equation gives 1.02
  at 60 y and 0.966 at 75 y; the Figure 3 point of 1.06 at 60 y is not what Equation 8 gives (still unresolved; the
  supplement does not discuss Figure 3).
- Weight over 59.5-123 kg (5th-95th percentiles): CL/F 9.18-15.8 L/h, Vc/F 4.58-9.46 L, Vp/F 75-155 L, Q/F 5.95-10.3 L/h (derived check: 12.0 x (59.5/85)^0.75 = 9.18, so the weight reference is 85 kg).
- Food decreased ka about 27% and F1 about 7%; without regard to food 34% and 8% (p. 7).

## Caveats
- The abstract says CL/F was "slightly higher in Asians" and Vc/F higher in women and Asians; consistent with Table 2.
- Phase 2/3 residual error 83.6%, systematic DV-vs-PRED trends for 5 and 15 mg attributed to dosing-time misrecording; IIV only on CL/F.
- Age effect on Vc/F and food on F1 imprecise (text p. 5).
- Equations in the supplementary appendix, not archived. (supplement, 2026-10-27) Now archived and read:
  sup0001.docx Equations 1-8; Table S3 shows the fixed allometric exponents were kept although estimating them lowered
  the OFV by 43.5 (0.566, 0.466).
- Sponsor-authored.
