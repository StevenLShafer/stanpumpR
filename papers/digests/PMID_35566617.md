# Vandemoortele 2022: general purpose PKPD models for TCI (narrative review)

## Study
Narrative review (no new data; no systematic search described) of "general purpose" PKPD models in commercial or imminent TCI pumps: Eleveld propofol (2018), Eleveld remifentanil (2017), Kim-Obara-Egan remifentanil (2017), Hannivoort-Colin dexmedetomidine (2015/2017) and Morse dexmedetomidine (2020). It restates their published equations (Table 3), the demographic ranges of the source populations (Table 1), the size descriptors (Table 2: James LBM, Janmahasatian FFM, Al-Sallami FFM, Morse NFM) and prospective-validation results. Everything here is secondary: the numbers are the review's copy of other papers' values.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Opioid audit (remifentanil):
- **MEAC / minimum effective or analgesic concentration:** none reported.
- **ke0:** Eleveld remifentanil ke0 = 1.09 * Fage(-0.0289) (Table 3, PDF p6). That is 1.09 at age 35, falling with age. The review gives no unit; the end point is EEG spectral edge frequency, in adults only (text, PDF p8). The review notes this has not been prospectively validated, especially in children. Kim-Obara-Egan has no ke0 (no EEG data; plasma-controlled only, PDF p9).
- **tPeak / hysteresis:** no tPeak reported for remifentanil.
- **Potency / equianalgesic ratios:** none.

Propofol: Eleveld ke0 = 0.146 * (wt/70)^-0.25 (Table 3, PDF p6), scaled to weight, end point BIS. Ce50 for BIS 3.4 ug/mL at 20 y and 2.3 ug/mL at 80 y (text, PDF p8); E50 equation 3.08 * Fage(-0.00635). The review says the -0.25 weight exponent did not significantly improve the fit.

Dexmedetomidine: the Colin 2017 PD models (BIS, MOAA/S, MAP, HR) are described qualitatively; no ke0 or Ce50 numbers are given. MAP is biphasic: below baseline under 2 ng/mL, rising above that. Two effect sites with separate ke0 and Ce50 are used. Recommended maximum infusion rate is 6 ug/kg/h. A MOAA/S of 3 or less is mentioned as a target (PDF p10-11).

## Covariates
Allometry (volumes power 1, clearances power 3/4, CL2 and CL3 scaled to V2 and V3), fat-free mass by Al-Sallami (propofol, remifentanil, dexmedetomidine clearances), Janmahasatian FFM (Kim), normal fat mass (Morse volumes), postmenstrual-age maturation, concomitant opioids (propofol V3, CL1), sex (propofol CL, remifentanil V2/CL).

## Caveats
Secondary source; Table 3 abbreviates the original models (no fixed "ref" values, no Fopiates ages, no Morse FfatV and no Morse maturation function). The review is selective by its own statement. The Kim V2 formula is printed with the age term outside the power term, matching the code, but the review does not say whether the original puts it there.
