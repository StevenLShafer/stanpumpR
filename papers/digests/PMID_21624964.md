# Struys 2011: optimizing IV drug administration with PK/PD

## Study
Narrative review (no new data) of PK/PD-guided TCI, effect-site control, hypnotic effect monitors, closed-loop control, and hypnotic-opioid response-surface interactions and advisory displays (Smart Pilot View, GE Navigator).

## Model and parameters
No parameter tables. Fig 2 (printed p40) shows a three-compartment model with an effect-site compartment (keo) and sigmoidal Emax effect. States that commercial TCI systems use one model each for remifentanil (Minto), sufentanil (Gepts) and alfentanil (Maitre), and for propofol the Marsh and Schnider adult models and the Kataria and Paedfusor paediatric models (printed p40).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No ke0, t1/2ke0, tPeak, MEAC, EC50 or equianalgesic ratio values reported. Mentions that fentanyl is converted to remifentanil equivalents in Smart Pilot View (Fig 3 legend, printed p43), without giving the ratio. Opioid audit: none.

## Covariates
Notes that models are specific to their study populations and caution is needed in children, the elderly and obese patients (printed p40); the Cortinez 2010 allometric propofol model with total body weight is cited.

## Caveats
Review and opinion; the authors have industry grants (GE, Drager, Ethicon) and one is co-owner of RUGLOOP.
