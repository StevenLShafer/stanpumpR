# Bienczak 2017: nevirapine PK in African children

## Study
Population PK, NONMEM 7.3 (FOCE-I), steady-state nevirapine (oral, twice daily, WHO weight-band dosing; fixed-dose combination tablets 50/100/200 mg nevirapine). 414 African children (Uganda, Zambia), 3305 samples (539 intensive from CHAPAS-1, 2766 sparse from CHAPAS-3); age 0.3-15.0 y (median 2.92), weight 3.5-29.6 kg (median 12.2) (Table 1). Genotype available in 324 (CYP2B6 516G>T and 983T>C; missing ones imputed by mixture model). Assays: CHAPAS-1 UHPLC-UV, linear 0.1-10 mg/L; CHAPAS-3 LC-MS/MS, linear 0.0195-20 mg/L (plasma, total drug). 246 samples excluded (unclear dosing 111, outliers 87, BLQ 48).

## Model and parameters
One-compartment disposition, three transit compartments plus first-order absorption, well-stirred liver model (first-pass). Intrinsic clearance CLint by CYP2B6 metabolizer group, with a cosine diurnal term. Pre-hepatic bioavailability FpreH rises with age (exponential). All CL and V allometrically scaled to 14.5 kg. ## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD model, no ke0, no tPeak. Efficacy target used: therapeutic range 3-8 mg/L trough (plasma), taken from the La Porte 2006 TDM guideline (adults); the authors state it has not been evaluated in children or Africans (p. 197). Table 2 counts evening Cmin below 3, within 3-8 and above 8 mg/L (e.g. EM <3: 77 (16.6%); SM >8: 222 (73.0%)). Simulated: evening Cmin <3 mg/L in 43% of EM and 26% of IM in the 4-6 kg band on WHO dosing (p. 194-195).

## Covariates
Allometric weight on all CL and V. CYP2B6 metabolizer group on CLint (dOFV -217, df 3, explains 85% of remaining BSV CLint). Diurnal cosine on CLint (dOFV -91, df 2): amplitude 29%, zenith about noon; morning/evening Cmin ratios 1.09-1.15 depending on dosing time, AUC0-12 1.03-1.07. Age on FpreH (not on CLint). No other covariate significant (sex, WAZ, HAZ, formulation, NRTI, site, other SNPs tested). Unequal dose splitting: median Cmin AM/PM 0.93 (larger dose AM), 1.13 (equal), 1.41 (larger PM).

## Caveats
Authors: self-reported dosing times for sparse data may inflate the diurnal effect; food not recorded; therapeutic range not validated in children. The USM group has only 2 genotyped children (Table 2). Maturation of clearance not identified; the age effect is placed on bioavailability. Bootstrap only n=50. Between-occasion variability in MTT is very large (199.7%). Nonmem model equations are in the unavailable Appendix S1.
