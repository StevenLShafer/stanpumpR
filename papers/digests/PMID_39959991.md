# Chun 2025: enantiomer- and unbound-specific population PK/PD of tramadol in neuropathic pain

## Study
- Secondary analysis of a Brazilian single-dose study (Ribeirao Preto) in patients with chronic neuropathic pain > 3 months and VAS pain above 40/100, with diabetic neuropathy (T1DM, T2DM) or nerve compression.
- 30 patients, 2145 PK observations; 27 patients, 402 PD observations. 14 men / 16 women, age 19-59 y, BMI 17.2-38.9 kg/m2 (Table S2, not archived). Diabetic subgroups poorly controlled, median HbA1c 10.7% (8-15.7).
- Single oral tramadol 100 mg (Tramal) after 8 h fast, 200 mL water, standard meal at 2 h. Blood to 24 h.
- Enantioselective LC-MS/MS of tramadol, M1 (O-desmethyl) and M2 (N-desmethyl), total and unbound (ultrafiltration). CYP2D6 in vivo activity by metoprolol/alpha-hydroxymetoprolol urinary metabolic ratio over 0-8 h; eGFR by CKD-EPI 2021.
- Pain: VAS 0-100 mm at baseline and each PK sampling time, rescaled to 0-1, logit-transformed.
- NONMEM 7.4.4, FOCE-I, sequential (PK fixed to post hoc estimates before PD), non-parametric bootstrap, VPC. Three non-responders excluded.

## Model and parameters
PK: each tramadol enantiomer two compartments with Weibull absorption (shape, scale, location) and linear elimination; each M1 enantiomer one compartment with linear formation, linear elimination, a first-pass branch (fraction fp, rate Kmet1) and enterohepatic circulation; M2 likewise. Total and unbound modelled jointly with an estimated binding parameter Kb so fu stays in (0,1). Identifiability fixes: M1 volume scaled allometrically (exponent fixed to 1) from rat values Vc(R-M1) 0.1 L and Vc(S-M1) 0.255 L at 0.235 kg to 72.2 kg; 30% of the dose assumed renally excreted unchanged.

Covariate forms: power for continuous covariates, referenced to median BMI 26.045 kg/m2 and median CYP2D6 in vivo activity 0.610; proportional for diabetes.
Simulated exposures (500 subjects, 100 mg): total (1R,2R)-M1 AUC reduced 67% (T1DM) and 14% (T2DM) vs non-diabetic; unbound (1R,2R)-M1 AUC unchanged; fu of tramadol and M1 higher in both diabetes groups.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: VAS pain score, single oral dose, chronic neuropathic pain. Inhibitory Emax (Imax) with an effect compartment driven by predicted apparent **unbound (1R,2R)-M1**.
- ke0 0.0398 /h on unbound (1R,2R)-M1, i.e. an equilibration half-time of 17.4 h (derived); bootstrap 95% CI spans 0.0004 to 0.233 /h.
- IC50 (unbound (1R,2R)-M1): 2.36 nmol/L in Table 1, 2.91 nmol/L in the text (stated as 725.61 ng/L, i.e. 0.73 ng/mL); T1DM 0.156, T2DM 0.515 nmol/L.
- No MEAC in the usual sense; no equianalgesic ratio. Affinities quoted from the literature (secondary): (1R,2R)-M1 Ki 3.4 nM, racemic tramadol 2.4 uM, (1S,2S)-M1 240 nM (Gillen 2000 and refs 6-8).
- Dosing conclusion: once daily may suffice in diabetes, twice daily for nerve-compression neuropathy.

## Covariates
Diabetes (T1DM, T2DM) on fu of tramadol and M1 and on IC50; BMI on unbound central volume; CYP2D6 in vivo activity on M1 (and M2) formation.

## Caveats
- 30 patients, single dose, one centre, VAS over 24 h: a chronic-pain end point measured once.
- IC50 bootstrap interval 0.03-22.07 nmol/L, a 700-fold range; text and Table 1 give different central values (2.91 vs 2.36).
- ke0 bootstrap interval includes essentially zero; the 17.4 h half-time is a slow response, not an acute onset.
- M1 volume fixed from rat data with the allometric exponent fixed at 1, and 30% renal excretion assumed, so the M1 scale is assumption driven.
- Driver is the unbound (1R,2R) enantiomer, which requires both a free fraction and an enantiomer ratio to compare with a total racemic M1 model.
