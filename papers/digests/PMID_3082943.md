# Nau 1986: valproate serum binding in mother-fetus pairs, weeks 13-41

## Study
- **Design**: cross-sectional, **in vitro** binding study (PDF p2, printed p216). Paired maternal venous and
  fetal/neonatal blood sampled **at delivery** (or termination) from **65 fetus-mother pairs, 13-41 weeks**; women
  aged 15-41 y. The mothers were **not taking valproate**: serum (50 µL) was spiked with valproate to a total of
  **50 µg/mL**, ultrafiltered (Amicon YMT micromethod), and free and total measured by liquid scintillation
  counting (radiolabel implied). Albumin and alpha1-acid glycoprotein by radial immunodiffusion; free fatty acids
  (FFA) colorimetrically.
- **Groups** (PDF p1-2): 18 pregnancies ended at 13-21 weeks (11 prostaglandin-induced terminations, 7 spontaneous
  abortions); 36 premature deliveries at 22-36 weeks; 11 term deliveries at 37-41 weeks. 17 mothers received
  synthetic oxytocin; many other drugs (sulprostone 15, pethidine 15, fenoterol 16, metoclopramide 10, epidural
  bupivacaine 8, ...). Maternal pathology included infection (8) and insulin-dependent diabetes (2).
- No weights, no doses, no pharmacokinetics; no non-pregnant control group.

## Model and parameters
No PK model. Binding results (free fraction, % of total, at 50 µg/mL added valproate):

| Maternal fu, weeks 31-41, no oxytocin (N 20) | 15.7 ± 4.4% | Table I, PDF p4 |

| Fetal fu, ~week 20 / term | ~20% / ≤10% | Results; abstract |

Regressions (Table II, PDF p4): maternal fu vs gestational age N 63, r .16, **NS**; vs albumin N 61, r -.093, NS;
vs FFA N 60, r .39, P < .001; vs alpha1-AGP N 63, r -.063, NS. Fetal fu vs gestational age N 60, r -.68; vs
albumin N 60, r -.77 (both P < .001). Maternal albumin vs gestational age N 63, r .24, P < .05 (positive as
printed, though the text says albumin tended to fall); maternal FFA vs gestational age N 62, r .25, P < .05.

Discussion: valproate half-life given as 8-12 h (no source cited for it); at steady state the unbound
concentration is set by dosing rate and unbound clearance, so a rise in fu lowers the total and leaves the free
level unchanged; with intermittent dosing free peaks may rise transiently.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None measured. No concentration-effect, therapeutic range or toxicity data. The authors argue the maternal free
concentration governs effect and toxicity in mother and fetus.

## Covariates
Fetal fu falls with fetal albumin (r -.77). Maternal fu rises with maternal FFA (r .39) and with oxytocin
(Table I). Gestational age has no significant effect on maternal fu in regression.

## Caveats
- In vitro, one spiked concentration (50 µg/mL, the bottom of the 50-100 band); valproate binding is
  concentration-dependent, so fu at clinical levels may differ.
- Every sample was taken at delivery or termination, with labour drugs (oxytocin, prostaglandins, pethidine) on
  board; the "early pregnancy" group is abortions/terminations, not continuing pregnancies.
- The "10% to 20%" rise is the abstract's summary; the regression on gestational age is not significant and the
  scatter is wide.
- No non-pregnant control: the paper says values up to 56% are never seen in non-pregnant patients but measures
  none.
- The model's half-life, 0.693 × 14 / 0.646 = 15.0 h at 70 kg (derived), is longer than the paper's unreferenced
  8-12 h; not a pregnancy point.

## Verification against stanpumpR
- **65 pairs, weeks 13-41; maternal fu 10% early to 20% term** (R/drugs_valproate.R:84-86, registry :98):
  **matches** the abstract. But the repo presents it as a measured doubling; the paper's own regression is not
  significant (Table II) and the term mean without oxytocin is 15.7% (Table I). See open question 1.
- **fu x 2.0 → total CL/F x 1.6** (R/drugs_valproate.R:147-150, 195-202): the arithmetic 1.25/2.0 = 0.625,
  1/0.625 = 1.6 is correct. The 2.0 is the abstract's endpoints, including oxytocin-exposed mothers. The header's
  own caveat (lines 130-133: sampled at delivery, FFA and oxytocin, 2.0 overstates) **matches** the paper
  (Table I). With the paper's no-oxytocin late value the ratio is ~1.57 (derived). Factor retained for other
  reasons (Johannessen Landmark); not changed here.
- **Comparator**: the repo applies the ratio against its non-pregnant model; the paper's baseline is early
  pregnancy at abortion/termination, with no non-pregnant group (open question 2).
- **"free fraction higher still in labour (oxytocin and free fatty acids)"** (R/drugs_valproate.R:165-166;
  help :19; registry notes): **matches** Table I (24.6 vs 15.7%, FFA 1,085 vs 756 µM).
- **Registry design "cross-sectional NCA (binding)"** (pregnancyRegistry.csv:98): **discrepancy**. No
  non-compartmental analysis was done and the women were not taking valproate; it is in-vitro ultrafiltration of
  serum spiked to 50 µg/mL. The repo nowhere says the binding was measured in vitro at one added concentration.
- **Reference string** (R/drugs_valproate.R:252-253): citation and DOI **match**.
- No PK parameter of the Teixeira-da-Silva model is drawn from this paper.
