# Rupp 1987: Pancuronium and vecuronium PK/PD in younger and elderly adults

## Study
ASA I-II elective surgical patients (gynaecologic, urologic, ENT or peripheral extremity surgery, minimal blood loss),
with normal renal, hepatic and laboratory values, no interfering medication. **Vecuronium: 5 younger adults aged
30-40 (36 +/- 4 yr, 61 +/- 10 kg) and 6 elderly aged 70-84 (77 +/- 5 yr, 66 +/- 12 kg)**; pancuronium: 7 younger aged
32-57 (43 +/- 10 yr, 66 +/- 9 kg) and 6 elderly aged 70-79 (73 +/- 3 yr, 72 +/- 9 kg) (Table 1, PDF p.2,
image-confirmed). The abstract's "12 young adults (30-57 yr)" pools both drugs. Sex not given. Data from 4 of the 5
younger vecuronium patients were reported previously (ref 16, Cronnelly 1983).

Anaesthesia: thiopental 2-4 mg/kg, halothane in 60% nitrous oxide, end-tidal halothane age-adjusted to 75-85% MAC
(0.6-0.7% young, 0.5% elderly); intubation without relaxant; oesophageal temperature above 35.5 C. Relaxant
**infused at about 2.5 ug/kg/min until twitch fell to 30-20% of control (7-16 min)**, not given as a bolus. Venous
samples to 360 min. Assay: selective ion-monitoring mass spectrometry, parent compound only, sensitivity 2 ng/mL.
Analysis: individual 2- and 3-compartment fits (infusion), 3-compartment selected for all groups (Boxenbaum);
twitch then fitted **to each patient's PK estimates** (Sheiner et al. effect-compartment method), giving Cpss50,
gamma and ke0. Unpaired t tests, P < 0.05.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: adductor pollicis twitch tension (ulnar nerve stimulation, force transducer) during and after the infusion.
Cpss50 is the steady-state plasma concentration for 50% twitch depression; ke0 the plasma-effect site equilibration
constant; gamma the sigmoidicity. No tPeak, no t1/2ke0 reported (t1/2ke0 = ln 2/0.169 = 4.1 min, derived). Age did not
change ke0, Cpss50 or gamma. Authors conclude elderly sensitivity is unchanged; the roughly 30% lower vecuronium Cl and
Vdss are attributed to its biliary route, and they contrast this with d'Hollander's finding of a 34% lower
steady-state dose requirement and a doubled 25-75% recovery time after 60 years. Not an opioid; no MEAC.

## Covariates
Age, as a two-group comparison only (30-40 vs 70-84 yr for vecuronium); no continuous relationship.

## Caveats
Very small groups (5 and 6); healthy, carefully selected elderly. Halothane-N2O potentiation. Infusion to only 70-80%
block, so the upper concentration-effect region is extrapolated. PD was fitted sequentially to the individual PK, so
ke0 is tied to these kinetics. The tabled values are means of individual estimates; a "typical" model rebuilt from the
means is not itself a fitted model. Cpss50 SD in the young (37 on 92) is large.
