# Katoh 1999: fentanyl reduction of sevoflurane MAC and MAC-BAR

## Study
- Hamamatsu University Hospital, Japan. ASA 1, age 20-50 yr, elective abdominal, limb or body-surface surgery; BMI > 30,
  cardiopulmonary/renal disease, vasoactive drugs, drug/alcohol abuse excluded. No premedication.
- 226 randomised: sevoflurane group (n = 96) and sevoflurane + 66 vol% N2O group (n = 86), each split into five
  fentanyl target subgroups (0, 1, 2, 4, 8 ng/mL); 44 added later for MAC-BAR (sevoflurane: fentanyl 0 n = 22,
  4 n = 10, 8 n = 4; sevo/N2O: 0 n = 8). Predetermined end-tidal sevoflurane per patient (up-and-down style scheme,
  Fig. 1).
- Analysed: sevoflurane group 118 (53 M / 65 F), age 41.6 ± 9.4 yr, weight 55.0 ± 12.4 kg; sevo/N2O group 91 (42 M /
  49 F), age 42.7 ± 10.7 yr, weight 57.2 ± 14.2 kg (p. 401). Exclusions: concentration change > ±35% pre/post incision,
  ephedrine for hypotension, awake before incision.
- Fentanyl by computer-controlled infusion (NEC 9801 + ATOM 1235 pump) with Shafer 1990 parameters; target doubled for
  the first 6 min, citing a blood-brain equilibration half-time of 6.6 min (Scott 1991). End-tidal held constant
  > 20 min; incision on average 50 min (45-75) after the infusion started.
- End points at skin incision: somatic = purposeful movement within 60 s (cough, chew, swallow excluded); sympathetic
  (BAR) = > 15% rise in heart rate or MAP over pre-incision (oscillometry, 1-min intervals for 5 min).
- Blood 5 min before and within 30 s after incision; serum (clotted 15 min, -70 °C); fentanyl by radioimmunoassay,
  LLOQ 0.13 ng/mL, CV < 10%. Only post-incision concentrations analysed (range 0.00-9.66 ng/mL).
- Analysis: Waud method for no-fentanyl MAC; multiple logistic regression on ln(concentrations) with an interaction
  term for the fentanyl reductions.

Above 3.1 ng/mL fentanyl, MAC-BAR fell below MAC (difference < 0.2 vol%). With N2O no ceiling was seen. Below
0.3 vol% sevoflurane patients were awake, so not tested.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Opioid audit:
- MEAC / analgesic concentration: none measured. The discussion calls 1.5 ng/mL fentanyl a "normal analgesic
  concentration" without respiratory depression (p. 403), not a measured MEAC.
- ke0: not estimated here. The infusion used a 6.6 min blood-brain equilibration half-time from Scott 1991 (EEG); no
  tPeak, no hysteresis measured.
- Potency ratio: none.
- End points: movement (MAC) and > 15% HR/MAP rise (MAC-BAR) at skin incision, with steady-state measured serum
  fentanyl (RIA). Ceiling effect for MAC and MAC-BAR without N2O; 6 ng/mL adds only 13% (MAC) and 9% (MAC-BAR) beyond
  3 ng/mL.

## Covariates
None modelled. Age 20-50, Japanese adults, mean weight about 55 kg.

## Caveats
Single-centre Japanese ASA 1 adults; serum RIA (not plasma GC/MS); logistic coefficients not reported, so curve values
other than the stated points must be read from figures; MAC-BAR determination biased by excluding hypotensive patients
(authors). The 74% at 6 ng/mL is not stated directly; it is 61% plus the "additional 13%" in the abstract.
