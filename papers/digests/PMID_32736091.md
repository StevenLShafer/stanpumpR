# Karatza 2020: irbesartan two-compartment model with delay-differential absorption

## Study
Open-label, two-period, two-sequence crossover bioequivalence study (PDF p2): 32 healthy Asian male volunteers, 18-45 y, BMI 18.5-24.9 kg/m2; single 300 mg oral irbesartan, test (Verisfield UK) and reference (Aprovel, Sanofi), 7-day washout, fasted, 200 mL water. 27 blood samples per period (pre-dose to 72 h). LC-MS/MS plasma assay, LLOQ 20.7 ng/mL, range 20.7-7217.6 ng/mL (total irbesartan, plasma; protein binding not modelled). Final dataset 64 profiles, 1664 concentrations in ng/mL (PDF p3). Monolix 2018R2, SAEM; lognormal parameters, exponential IIV; bootstrap 1000 sets (Rsmlx); VPC, NPDE. Most profiles showed two peaks.

## Model and parameters
Two-compartment disposition with first-order elimination, all parameters apparent (/F; oral data only). Absorption is a delay differential equation, dA0/dt = -ka*A0(t-T), A0(0)=Dose, A0(t)=0 for -T<=t<=0 (eq. 1, PDF p3); inflow to central ka*A0(t-T). kel=CL/Vc, k12=Q/Vc, k21=Q/Vp. Combined error model. Delay model beat first-order, zero-order, lag, sinusoidal, 1-3 transit compartments, gastric-emptying and enterohepatic-recirculation variants on -2LL/AIC/BIC (Table 1: Delayka -2LL 22505.33; first-order ka 22652.3; lag time 22581.27). Final model -2LL 22481.5, AIC 22517.5, BIC 22556.36 (PDF p3; the combination "Stomach-Gut-Liver + DelayBile" in Table 1 is 22551.25, i.e. the final model is the delay-ka model refined, not a Table 1 row).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None fitted. Simulations only (PDF p4, p7 Fig. 5): the new PK joined to the Hedaya and Helmy 2015 PD model (sigmoid Emax, effect compartment, healthy volunteers); SBP and DBP stayed flat (about 111 and 74 mmHg) whatever the delay or dose, which the authors attribute to normal baseline pressure. No ke0, EC50, MEAC or therapeutic range is reported by this paper. Not an opioid; audit items: none.

## Covariates
Weight, height, age and BMI tested linearly and allometrically on all parameters, plus occasion and treatment effects; none significant (PDF p2-3). The paper prints only eligibility ranges, not the cohort's mean weight, height or age.

## Caveats
Healthy young Asian men only; apparent (oral) parameters, F confounded with V and CL; no external validation (authors say so, PDF p8); bootstrap table says "95% CIs" in text but columns are 90%; Table 3 percent columns inconsistent (see open questions); Discussion quotes SmPC CL 9.42-10.56 L/h and V 53-93 L as close to the estimates (CL/F 13.5 L/h is apparent, so not directly comparable without F). The delay equation is stated with a zero history for -T..0 (eq. 1) though the supplement code is not available here.
