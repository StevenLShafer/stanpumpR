# Bindellini 2024: joint ACTH-cortisol and hydrocortisone PK model

## Study
- Two crossover phase I trials in healthy men (NCT02777268, NCT01960530), South East Wales REC.
  - Trial 1 (n = 16): dexamethasone (DEX) suppression, then single 0.5, 2, 5, 10 mg hydrocortisone immediate-release granules at 07:00; total cortisol pre-dose, half-hourly to 8 h, hourly to 12 h.
  - Trial 2 (n = 14, 1 excluded for inadequate ACTH suppression, so 13): 24 h endogenous ACTH/cortisol profile; then DEX only, DEX + 20 mg granules, DEX + 20 mg IV bolus. Total cortisol at 0.25-12 h; unbound cortisol pre-dose and 2 h.
- Analysed n = 29; body weight median 81.7 (64.7-102) kg, age 38.5 (21-60) y (Table 1). 1865 total cortisol (interventional), 325 endogenous total cortisol, 310 ACTH concentrations.
- NONMEM 7.4.3 (FOCE family not stated in text), PsN, SIR for parameter precision; VPCs.
- Workflow: Step 1 refine absorption of a previously published hydrocortisone PK model (Melin 2018, ref 20: two compartments, CBG + albumin binding, theory-based allometry); Step 2 endogenous ACTH/cortisol model; Step 3 joint re-estimation.

## Model and parameters
Disposition (from ref 20, refined): two compartments; CL and Q act on unbound cortisol; CBG binding with Kd fixed, nonspecific (albumin) binding NS fixed; allometry exponent 0.75 for flows, 1 for volumes, 70 kg reference (Table 2 footnote). The paper's discussion gives the unbound-cortisol elimination rate constant as 49.3 /h, i.e. CL/Vc = 106/2.15 (derived check: 49.3), confirming CL acts on unbound cortisol with Vc as the central volume.

Oral absorption (ABS2): transit chain with estimated number of transit compartments (Ntr), Ktr = (Ntr+1)/MTT, into a depot with first-order Ka; MTT has a power dependence on dose (exponent 0.179), MTT 0.57 h at 0.5 mg and 1.11 h at 20 mg (p 815).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No clinical effect site. PD is the HPA axis: ACTH-driven cortisol production (sigmoid Emax) and unbound-cortisol feedback inhibition of ACTH pulsatile secretion (sigmoid Imax). Not an opioid.

## Covariates
Allometry (theory-based, fixed exponents) on CL, Q, Vc, Vp; body weight (power 6.53) on SA1. No other covariates retained.

## Caveats
- Healthy DEX-suppressed men only; endogenous submodel from 13 subjects.
- Ka imprecise (RSE >= 50%).
- F 0.344 is low relative to paired-route studies (Johnson 2018 on what appears to be the same trial-2 subjects found F ~1 on total cortisol); the paper does not discuss the discrepancy.
- MTT extrapolation above 20 mg is outside the data.
- Bmax not reported in the paper itself.
