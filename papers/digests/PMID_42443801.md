# Bai 2026: placental-cell permeability of four DPP-4 inhibitors (in vitro)

## Study
- **Design:** in vitro only; no patients, no pregnant women dosed, no pharmacokinetics (pp3-5).
- **Models:** BeWo (choriocarcinoma) cells, HTR-8/SVneo (first-trimester extravillous trophoblast) cells, and
  primary human trophoblast cells from term placentas (38-40 weeks, CK7 positive >90%). CHO cells overexpressing
  human OAT4, ENT1/2 or CNT1/2/3 were used for the transporter tests.
- **Drugs:** sitagliptin, vildagliptin, linagliptin, alogliptin.
- **Assays:**
  - Accumulation at 1, 10 and 50 µM for 10 min, and concentration-dependent uptake over 1-500 µM for 3 min at
    4 and 37 °C.
  - BeWo Transwell transport, AP→BL and BL→AP, for 15-180 min, at about the adult Cmax of each drug:
    sitagliptin 1 µM, vildagliptin 1 µM, linagliptin 10 nM, alogliptin 0.3 µM (p4).
  - Inhibitor, siRNA knockdown and overexpression experiments.
- **Analysis:** LC-MS/MS in cell lysates, validated to FDA guidance. Linear ranges 1-200 nM (sitagliptin,
  vildagliptin) and 1-500 nM (linagliptin, alogliptin); accuracy 88.87-108.21%, precision 0.52-7.94% (Tables
  1-2, pp5-7). Vildagliptin and linagliptin showed matrix suppression to 57-74%, corrected by the
  stable-isotope internal standard (Table 3, p7).
- Mean ± SD of n ≥ 3 independent experiments.

**Results:**
- **Accumulation:** linagliptin > alogliptin > sitagliptin > vildagliptin in all three cell types and at all
  concentrations (Figs 3-4, pp8-9).
- **Carrier-mediated uptake:** uptake at 37 °C was far above that at 4 °C for all four drugs, which suggests
  carrier-mediated uptake. For linagliptin the uptake at 37 °C saturated (p8).
- **Transport across BeWo:** linear over 180 min in both directions, Papp above 2 × 10⁻⁶ cm/s for all four.
  The order was linagliptin ≈ alogliptin ≈ sitagliptin > vildagliptin. Recovery after 180 min was 72-95% (p9).
- **Transporters:** ENTs, CNTs and OAT4 were not shown to carry any of the four (pp10-12). The authors give
  linagliptin's known transporters, OCT2 and P-gp, and vildagliptin's interaction with P-gp, as literature (p12).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. The authors say the work offers no dosing advice for gestational diabetes (p13).

## Covariates
Not applicable.

## Caveats
- Cell lines express transporters differently from the placenta. Experiments in the primary trophoblasts ran at
  24 h, before syncytialisation (p13). The authors warn that the in vitro results need care before being applied
  in vivo.
- Papp values are given only as a bar chart; the numbers above are read from it.
- The paper states that human placental-transfer and pregnancy safety data for these drugs are lacking (abstract,
  p3). It cites one RCT of sitagliptin in the second trimester (206 participants) and an observational cohort with
  no excess of major malformations (p12). It gives no human PK in pregnancy for linagliptin or vildagliptin.
