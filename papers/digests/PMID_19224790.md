# Moerman 2009: manual vs TCI remifentanil with propofol, colonoscopy

## Study
Prospective, randomized, double-blind, Ghent. 60 unpremedicated ASA I-II outpatients aged 18-65 for elective colonoscopy; 57 analysed (3 protocol violations). Groups: MCI remifentanil (n=18), TCI remifentanil (n=18), placebo saline (n=21) (Table 1; the text says 19/20 randomised before exclusions). MCI: 0.125 ug/kg/min for 2 min (0.25 ug/kg) then 0.05 ug/kg/min, total body weight. TCI: plasma target 1 ng/mL. Propofol TCI started 2 min later at 4 ug/mL until loss of consciousness, then the plasma target set to the effect-site concentration at LOC and adjusted by 0.5 ug/mL steps for deep sedation with spontaneous ventilation. Rugloop II; Schnider propofol, Minto remifentanil. Predicted concentrations only; no assay. Table 1: age 39.4 +/- 12.5, 41.0 +/- 13.6, 39.9 +/- 10.7 yr; weight 68.7 +/- 12.1, 66.4 +/- 14.5, 73.7 +/- 14.5 kg (MCI, TCI, placebo).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- Opioid audit: no MEAC measured. Remifentanil target 1 ng/mL (TCI, predicted plasma) and MCI regimen simulated to give a mean effect-site about 1 ng/mL; this is a study dose, not a defined effective concentration.
- ke0 for remifentanil: the Minto age function above (not estimated here). Propofol tPeak 1.6 min. No hysteresis or potency ratio reported.
- End points: apnea 8 (MCI) vs 2 (TCI) vs 0 (placebo), P=0.003; bradypnea RR <6: 8/5/0; obstructive respiration 9/10/2; purposeful movement 2/5/10 (Table 3). Apnea subgroup in MCI: Cpremi_max 1.797 +/- 0.324 vs 1.490 +/- 0.193 ng/mL without apnea (Results). Propofol total 318 +/- 94 (MCI), 289 +/- 81 (TCI), 381 +/- 162 mg (placebo; P=0.056 TCI vs placebo), Table 2. Ceprop at LOC 4.79 +/- 1.31, 4.41 +/- 0.63, 4.59 +/- 0.80 ug/mL; Ceprop at recovery 1.80, 1.69, 2.13 ug/mL (Table 4).

## Covariates
Age in the remifentanil ke0 only; no covariate analysis.

## Caveats
Concentrations are model predictions, not measured. The TCI and MCI remifentanil doses differ (MCI overshoot), so mode of delivery and dose are confounded (authors say so). Ordinary colonoscopy patients; authors disclaim clinical recommendations. The text layer scrambles Tables 1-2 (values above are from the rendered page).
