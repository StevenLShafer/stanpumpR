# Charles 2006: sparse third-trimester population PK of metformin with a fetal compartment

## Study
Opportunistic sampling within an Australian trial of metformin vs insulin in pregnancy (Adelaide). 27 women (29 pregnancies) with NIDDM (n = 7) or GDM (n = 20); 5 had PCOS. Table 1 (PDF p2 / printed 68): only **12 women gave maternal samples** (8 maternal + cord, 4 maternal only; 17 cord only); 28 maternal samples, median 2 (1-7) per patient, at 8.1 (0.25-41.2) h after the last dose; age 32 (26-43) y; weight 88 (50-150) kg; median dose 2000 (1000-3000) mg/day; maternal concentration 1.2 (0.1-2.9) mg/L. 21 cord samples, 13.4 (2.5-41.4) h after the last dose, 0.8 (0.1-2.6) mg/L; birth weight 3.39 kg. HPLC-UV, LOQ 0.08 mg/L. Half the women had other medicines (listed, p2).

NONMEM V, FOCE (with interaction per abstract); ADVAN4 TRANS4 reparameterised: maternal central compartment V2, a fetal compartment V3 = V2 x 4/WT, Q = V3 x KEQ, placental partition coefficient PMF. Ka fixed at 0.3 /h and F fixed at 0.5 (lack of absorption-phase samples), TEQ fixed at 5 min (sensitivity 30 s to 30 min, little change).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. Fetal exposure only: PMF 1.07 (cord about equal to maternal).

## Covariates
Maternal age and current weight did not reduce the OFV significantly (alpha 0.01); allometric weight scaling (0.75 on CL, 1 on V) was nonetheless used because it reduced the variability of V2 and PMF (p3). No renal-function covariate tested.

## Caveats
- The conclusion (PK "similar to" nonpregnant patients, so no dose change) rests on a cross-study comparison with Tucker 1981 (n = 4 healthy, 1.5 g: 28.4 L/h and 6 h; n = 4 T2DM, 1 g: 46.6 L/h and 5.3 h), assuming F = 0.5 throughout (Discussion p5). There is no nonpregnant control group.
- Only 12 women and 28 maternal samples, sparse and unstructured; ka and F fixed, so CL is really CL/F x 0.5 and inherits any error in the assumed F (Liao 2020 measured F 0.53-0.64 in pregnancy).
- Table 2 prints KEQ in h^-1, but its value is the per-minute rate for a 5-min half-time (derived above).
