# Eleveld 2017: allometric remifentanil PK-PD model

## Study
- Pooled reanalysis of three published data sets (Table 1): Minto 1997 (65 volunteers, 20-85 yr, 45-106 kg; 1,992 PK
  and 3,989 SEF observations), Ross 2001 (36 children 0-17 yr, 2-96 kg; 196 PK), Mertens 2003 (30 patients
  25-57 yr, 52-92 kg; 446 PK, TCI). Total 131 individuals (55 M, 76 F), 5 days to 85 yr, 2.5-106 kg,
  49-193 cm; 2,634 arterial concentrations.
- NONMEM 7.3 FOCE-I; log transform-both-sides residual error, separate per data set; sequential PK then PD
  (post hoc individual PK drives Ce). Model building by AIC (drop >= 10 to add) plus repeated twofold
  cross-validation, performance averaged over five age subgroups. Reference individual 70 kg, 35 yr, 170 cm male.
- PD: EEG spectral edge frequency (SEF), Minto data only (adults).
- No obese individuals analysed (p. 1012-1013).

## Model and parameters
Three compartments; size = Al-Sallami FFM / FFMref; volumes linear in size, CL to size^0.75; Q2, Q3 by
compartmental allometry, (V2/V2ref)^0.75 and (V3/V3ref)^0.75 (p. 1010 equations).

- Al-Sallami FFM: males (0.88 + 0.12/(1 + (AGE/13.4)^-12.7)) x 9270 TBW/(6680 + 216 BMI); females
  (1.11 - 0.11/(1 + (AGE/7.1)^-1.1)) x 9270 TBW/(8780 + 244 BMI) (p. 1006).
- faging(x) = exp(x (AGE - 35)); fsigmoid(x, E50, λ) = x^λ/(x^λ + E50^λ).
- KMAT = fsigmoid(TBW, Θ1, 2); KSEX = 1 (male), 1 + Θ5 fsigmoid(AGE, 12, 6)(1 - fsigmoid(AGE, 45, 6)) (female).
- V1 = V1ref SIZE faging(Θ2); V2 = V2ref SIZE faging(Θ3) KSEX; V3 = V3ref SIZE faging(Θ4) exp(Θ6 (TBW - 70));
  CL = CLref SIZE^0.75 (KMAT/KMATref) KSEX faging(Θ3); Q2 = Q2ref (V2/V2ref)^0.75 faging(Θ2) KSEX;
  Q3 = Q3ref (V3/V3ref)^0.75 faging(Θ2).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: EEG SEF only (Minto volunteers); sigmoid Emax with effect compartment.
- ke0 1.09 /min at 35 yr, decreasing with age as exp(-0.0289 (AGE - 35)) (derived: 0.396 /min at 70 yr).
  t½ke0 not stated (derived: ln2/1.09 = 0.64 min). No tPeak reported; the authors favour an integrated PK-PD
  model over tPeak-based methods (p. 1017).
- Ce50 12.7 ng/mL for SEF, noted as above clinical TCI targets of 3-8 ng/mL (p. 1015). No MEAC, no analgesic end
  point, no potency ratio. Ce50 age term tested and rejected (p. 1011).
- Fig. 9: effect-site target of 4 ng/mL is reached about 1 min later in the elderly.

## Covariates
FFM (Al-Sallami), age (three exponential age terms for PK, one for ke0), sex (KSEX), weight (maturation and V3
correction). Maturation 50% at Θ1 2.88 kg in Table 3; the discussion (p. 1012) says about 3.6 kg (internal
inconsistency; possibly a KMAT/KMATref or PMA-related restatement).

## Caveats
- No obese data; extrapolation to obesity untested (p. 1012).
- PD only from adults in one data set; ke0 in children is extrapolated.
- No prospective validation; SEF is a tenuous surrogate for clinical effect.
- Heights imputed for 4 + 2 individuals.
