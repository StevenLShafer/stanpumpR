# Chatelut 1995: carboplatin clearance from weight, age, sex and serum creatinine

## Study
70 adult cancer patients (age 23-84 y) on carboplatin in combination regimens, 1-hour IV infusion, 184-950 mg; 89 cycles. Samples before dosing, at end of infusion, and at 0.5, 1, 4, 8, 24 h after; plasma ultrafiltrate carboplatin (ultrafilterable platinum) by flameless atomic absorption (Methods, PDF p1-2). NONMEM IV, two-compartment model, constant infusion, first-order elimination, proportional error. Formula derived on 34 patients (46 cycles), evaluated prospectively on 36 other patients (43 cycles). GFR was measured by Cr-51-EDTA (most patients) or Tc-99m-DTPA scintigraphy (first 10 patients) and compared as GFR + 25 mL/min.

## Model and parameters
Final formula (Results, PDF p3, printed 575, confirmed on the page image):
CL (mL/min) = θ1·weight + [θ2·weight·(1 − θ3·age)·(1 − θ4·sex)] / Scr, with weight in kg, age in years, sex 0 male and 1 female, Scr in micromolar (µM).

Predictive performance (Table 3, PDF p4, text layer only, scrambled; not recorded). Text states: retrospective percent error −28 to 27% (median −4%); prospective median percent error 2% (range −25 to 30%), median absolute percent error 10% (range 0-30%) (Abstract and Results). Proposed upper bound for CL: 202 mL/min (Discussion). The objective-function increase for dropping weight from the first term was 38.7.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No concentration-effect model. The paper motivates AUC targeting: dose (mg) = CL (mL/min) × target AUC (mg·min/mL) (Discussion); cites a response probability rising from 20% to over 40% as AUC rose from 2 to 5 mg·min/mL (Introduction, citing Jodrell). Non-renal clearance: formula gives 9.4 mL/min at 70 kg (derived: 0.134 × 70 = 9.38, matches the text) against 32.8 ± 12.8 mL/min measured from urine collection in 27 patients (Discussion). Not an opioid; no MEAC or ke0.

## Covariates
Weight, age, sex and serum creatinine; body surface area, height, serum proteins, albumin and cisplatin pretreatment did not improve the fit. Mean percent error −5% ± 12% (pretreated) vs −1% ± 13% (not). Excluded from validation: children and haemodialysis patients.

## Caveats
Observed CL comes from ultrafilterable platinum, so it is the unbound-drug clearance; population includes mixed tumour types and regimens; Scr assay method not stated in the text read; the θ3 interval is wide (age distribution uneven); the model reports only mean individual Vdss, no fitted volumes; the non-renal term is probably underestimated (authors).
