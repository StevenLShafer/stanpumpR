# Yoshihara 2005: olmesartan population PK (Sankyo, 12 trials)

## Study
Retrospective NONMEM V analysis (FO method; FOCE did not converge) of 12 phase I-III trials in the US, Europe and
Japan (Table I, PDF p3): 89 healthy volunteers and 383 hypertensive patients (337 hypertension only, 34 with renal
and 12 with hepatic impairment), 7911 olmesartan concentrations. 355 Westerners (338 Caucasian, 17 Hispanic, all
Hispanics in the hepatic trial) and 117 Japanese (derived from Table I). Olmesartan medoxomil orally once daily:
single 10 mg (two trials), multiple 2.5-80 mg for 1-12 weeks (nine), titrated 5-40 mg for 1 year (one); 5-29 samples
per participant. In the five-way crossover (trial 5) the 30 men were treated as separate individuals in each period,
so Table II counts 209 healthy volunteers and 592 in all. Assay HPLC with fluorescence, LLOQ 0.2 ng/mL. Table II
(PDF p6) gives age, weight and laboratory values as mean ± SD only (hypertension-only patients 54.2 ± 12.2 y,
77.8 ± 15.6 kg; healthy 30.6 ± 11.8 y, all male); **no age range is printed**. Bootstrap B = 200.

## Model and parameters
Two compartments, first-order absorption with lag (ADVAN4 TRANS1), parameterised in CL/F, ka, ke, k12, k21, ALAG;
exponential IIV on all six; exponential residual error. Table IV (PDF p8, rendered), NONMEM estimate (SE):

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None: no PD model, no blood pressure. Not an opioid. The discussion cites absolute bioavailability of olmesartan
medoxomil about 26-29% and about 40% renal excretion of systemic olmesartan (PDF p11, p13; from ref. 10).

## Covariates
Equation 5 (PDF p6-7): CL/F = 7.32 x (age/50)^-0.117 x (BWt/70)^0.310 x (SCr/80)^-0.344 x exp(0.145 x sex) x
exp(-0.239 x pv) x exp(-0.030 x race), SCr in µmol/L. Equation 3 (p5, rendered) is covariate/median, as in Eq. 5
(the text layer prints it inverted). Liver tests (ALT, AST, ALP, TB, GGT) not significant at alpha 0.01. The
paper's own sensitivity numbers (p8, p11) all follow from Eq. 5 (derived check): 75 y vs 50 y -4.6%; 50 vs 70 kg
-9.9%; SCr 265-450 vs 80 µmol/L -33.8% to -44.8% (4.4 L/h at 265); hypertensive vs healthy -21.3%; female -13.5%;
Japanese -3.0%. Fig. 6 (p13) bars use 50 vs 80 kg and 75 vs 35 y.

## Caveats
- **Source typo**: the sentence giving the typical values (PDF p7, rendered) assigns to a 60 kg man (woman) CL/F
  6.66 (5.76) L/h. Eq. 5 gives 6.66 and 5.76 only at 70 kg (derived: 7.32 x e^0.145 x e^-0.239 = 6.663; at 60 kg
  6.35), and Fig. 6 and the Fig. 3 caption both state 70 kg for the 6.66 L/h reference.
- Table II labels total bilirubin in U/L while Eq. 3 gives its median as 12 µmol/L.
- FO estimation; ka is poorly determined between individuals (omega2 1.64). The dose basis (prodrug mg) is
  implied by "normalised to a dose of olmesartan medoxomil 20mg" (p5) and the medoxomil dose list, not stated as a
  modelling convention.
