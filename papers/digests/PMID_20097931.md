# Frey 2010: population PK of tocilizumab in rheumatoid arthritis

## Study
- Pooled phase III trials OPTION, TOWARD, RADIATE and AMBITION (p. 2 / 755). Patients had moderate to severe RA. Tocilizumab 4 or 8 mg/kg (by body weight) was given as a 1-h IV infusion every 4 weeks for 24 weeks, alone or with DMARDs.
- Sampling:
  - Sparse: pre-infusion samples at weeks 12 and 24 in all patients.
  - Rich, in about 20% of patients: 8 more samples. These were pre-infusion at weeks 4, 8 and 16; within 15 min after the infusion at weeks 4 and 12; and 2 weeks after the infusion at weeks 2, 6 and 14.
- Assay: serum ELISA, LLOQ 0.1 µg/mL.
- Data: 7415 serum samples from 1793 patients (p. 5 / 758). 82% were women and 77% white; median body weight 70 kg and median age 52 y. 18 patients developed anti-tocilizumab antibodies (HAHA).
  - Per-trial demographics are in Table I (PDF p. 5), whose text layer is scrambled; not used.
- Software and estimation:
  - NONMEM VI level 1, FO on log-transformed data. FOCE could not be applied consistently.
  - Covariates were screened by GAM with bootstrap (Xpose/S-PLUS), then tested by stepwise forward addition and backward elimination at 1%, with randomisation tests.
  - The covariance step failed; SEs come from a nonparametric bootstrap, in which 149 of 200 replicates succeeded.

## Model and parameters
Two-compartment disposition with parallel linear (first-order) and Michaelis-Menten elimination; IIV (exponential) on CL, V1, V2, VM; combined additive and proportional residual error.

Other values:
- IIV correlations (Table II, r): CL:V1 0.6, CL:V2 −0.1, CL:VM −0.5, V1:V2 0.5, V1:VM 0.2, V2:VM 0.2.
- Shrinkage (p. 8 / 761): CL 28%, V1 38%, V2 27%, VM 52%.
- IIV fell from the base model: V2 from 145% to 66%, VM from 89% to 54%.
- Units: the paper gives CL as 0.3 L/d, which is 12.5 mL/h (printed as 13). For 70 kg it says about 0.2 mL/h/kg (derived: 0.18). VM 7.5 mg/d is 0.31 mg/h (derived), consistent with the mg/h axes of Fig. 1.
- Derived (this card, linear part only, from the rounded Table II values): half-lives 3.9 and 21.0 d, Vss 6.4 L. These agree with the paper's terminal half-life of about 21 d.
- Concentration dependence (Fig. 5A, p. 9 / 762): nonlinear CL dominates below 25 µg/mL, the two pathways contribute equally near 25 µg/mL, and linear CL dominates above 50 µg/mL.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No ke0, no concentration-effect or exposure-response model, no target or therapeutic concentration. Not an opioid. The nonlinear CL is interpreted as target-mediated (IL-6 receptor binding), but no receptor occupancy is modelled. Time-varying IL-6, sIL-6R and HAHA showed no trend in the weighted residuals.

## Covariates
Table III (PDF p. 7 / 760; rendered), power models normalised to cohort medians; sex and smoking proportional:
- CL = 0.3 × (BSA/1.8)^0.7, BSA range 1.3-2.7 m2, −20/+31%.
- Sex on CL: women −16% (reference men).
- CL = 0.3 × (HDL-C/54)^−0.2 (mg/dL), range 23-135, +15/−14%.
- CL = 0.3 × (LRF/4.7)^0.1, LRF = log of rheumatoid factor, range 2.7-9.4, −4.9/+6.5%.
- V1 = 3.5 × (PROT/74)^−1.1, total protein in g/L, range 57-96, +33/−25%.
- V1 = 3.5 × (ALBU/38)^0.7, albumin in g/L, range 24-50, −27/+21%.
- VM = 7.5 × (ALBU/38)^−0.4, +22/−11%.
- VM = 7.5 × (CRCL/106)^0.2, CrCL in mL/min, range 27-317, −27/+29%.
- Smoking on VM: +11% (18% of patients smoked).

Not retained: age, race, body weight and BMI (correlated with BSA; BSA kept), concomitant methotrexate, other DMARDs, NSAIDs, corticosteroids, and analgesics (paracetamol, tramadol, codeine; 524 patients).

## Caveats
- RA patients on a fixed per-kg dose; trough-heavy sparse sampling; FO estimation, which can inflate covariate significance (the authors ran randomisation tests).
- The covariance step failed; precision comes from a bootstrap with 149 of 200 replicates successful.
- Fixed effects are printed to 1-2 significant figures (open question).
- Residual-error row labels have their σ subscripts swapped in Table II.
- All authors were Roche employees; Roche sponsored the work.
