# Ralph 1978: inactivation of metronidazole by anaerobic and aerobic bacteria

## Study
In-vitro microbiology; no patients and no pharmacokinetics.
- **Organisms (PDF p1, printed p. 377):** six clinical anaerobic isolates, one strain each (Bacteroides fragilis,
  Fusobacterium nucleatum, Eubacterium lentum, Peptostreptococcus anaerobius, Clostridium perfringens,
  Propionibacterium acnes), and four aerobes (Staphylococcus aureus, Escherichia coli, Proteus morganii,
  Streptococcus faecalis).
- **MIC/MBC:** tube dilution in brucella broth with hemin, in an anaerobic glove box, inoculum about 10^5 CFU/mL.
  MBC is the lowest concentration with no growth on subculture to brucella agar with hemin.
- **Time-kill curves:** stationary-phase cultures (5 x 10^7 to 5 x 10^10 CFU/mL for the anaerobes; about 10^9 to
  10^10 for the aerobes), metronidazole 10 µg/mL added at zero time, counts every 2 h to 8 h and at 24 h. Mixed
  cultures of B. fragilis with each aerobe were also run.
- **Drug assay:** filtered culture fluid, bioassay (authors' earlier method, ref. 11), accuracy ±10%; detection
  limit given as <0.5 µg/mL (PDF p2).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: viable count (log CFU/mL) in time-kill curves, and bioassayed metronidazole remaining in broth.
- 10 µg/mL cut the five susceptible anaerobes by 2 to 5 logs within 4 h; killing was over by 6 to 8 h (PDF p2).
- Susceptible anaerobes inactivated the drug quickly: none detectable (<0.5 µg/mL) after 4 h for C. perfringens,
  F. nucleatum and P. anaerobius; <1 µg/mL by 8 h and none by 24 h for B. fragilis and E. lentum (PDF p2). The
  authors tie the end of killing to the concentration falling below the MBC, usually by 4 h (PDF p6).
- P. acnes: no killing and no inactivation (Figs 1, 2).
- Aerobes: no killing; S. faecalis, P. morganii and E. coli inactivated the drug more slowly (to <1, <1 and
  <2 µg/mL by 24 h); S. aureus did not (PDF p3). Cell-free filtrates did not inactivate it (PDF p4).
- Mixed cultures: aerobes did not blunt killing of B. fragilis in vitro (Figs 5, 6).
- No ke0, EC50 or clinical concentration target; this is not an opioid paper.

## Covariates
None (in vitro).

## Caveats
- One strain per species: Table 1 cannot give an MIC90 or MBC90.
- The authors summarise susceptible anaerobes as MIC ≤1 and MBC ≤3 µg/mL (PDF p2): that bound is set by the single
  C. perfringens strain.
- Bioassay, not chromatography; time-kill used very large stationary-phase inocula, chosen to maximise inactivation.
- The P. acnes result is one resistant strain; the paper does not claim it for the species or for related genera.
