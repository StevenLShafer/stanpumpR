# Liu 2024: ciprofol PopPK in ICU, surgical and healthy subjects

## Study
- Pooled PopPK of 12 trials: 6 healthy-volunteer, 4 elective-surgery, 2 mechanically ventilated ICU (HSK3486-205 and -307) (Abstract, PDF p1; Table 1, PDF p5). N = 462 adults (350 non-ICU, 112 ICU), 3918 concentrations (Table 1). 232 of 4148 samples (5.59%) were below LLOQ and dropped (PDF p4).
- Table 1 (medians): age 38 y (18-77), weight 61.7 kg (41.6-97.9), BMI 22.9 (16.9-30.0). ICU subjects: age 57 y (19-77), weight 63.5 kg (42-90). 236 female / 226 male. 85% Asian. Weights up to 97.9 kg only; BMI at most 30.
- ICU dosing: loading 0.1-0.2 mg/kg, then 0.3 mg/kg/h, titrated to RASS -2 to +1, range 0.06-8.0 mg/kg/h (PDF p2).
- Assay: plasma, HPLC-MS/MS, LLOQ 5 ng/mL, CV of precision <= 9.2%, QC accuracy 95-108% (PDF p3). Total plasma concentration (protein binding not discussed).
- Software: NONMEM 7.4.3, PsN 4.2; forward p < 0.01, backward p < 0.001; bootstrap, pcVPC, NPC (PDF p3).

## Model and parameters
Three-compartment linear model, first-order elimination, IV only. Exponential IIV; additive error on log scale (proportional residual error, separate for venous vs arterial samples). Typical values (Table 2, PDF p7, confirmed on page image) are for the reference subject: 62 kg, 33 y, male, arterial sample, long-term infusion, ICU patient (FLG = 0, ICU = 0).

Covariate equations (PDF p4), with the parameters on the log scale:
- CL = exp(4.39 + 0.578 ln(WT/62) - 0.195 [venous sample] + 0.089 [female] + 0.229 FLG - 0.237 ICU)
- V1 = exp(1.26 + 0.496 ln(age/33)); Q2 = exp(4.30); V2 = exp(3.95 - 2.08 FLG); Q3 = exp(3.35 + 0.802 FLG); V3 = exp(6.04 - 1.62 FLG)
- FLG = 0 ICU = 0: long-term infusion in ICU patients. FLG = 0 ICU = 1: long-term infusion, non-ICU (studies 102, 204, 306). FLG = 1 ICU = 1: short-term infusion, non-ICU.
- Arithmetic check (derived): exp(4.39) = 80.6 vs tabulated 80.9; exp(1.26) = 3.53; exp(3.95) = 51.9; exp(6.04) = 420. The multiplicative effects in Table 2 (0.823, 1.09, 0.789, 1.26, 0.125, 2.23, 0.198) equal exp of the equation coefficients.

Derived exposure facts the authors report (PDF p5-6): 0.1 mg/kg over 4 min then 0.3 mg/kg/h for 24 h gives typical AUC0-24h 4606 ng*h/mL and Cmax 500.3 ng/mL for 62 kg, 33 y. Cmax at the end of a 0.1 mg/kg load = 464 ng/mL (0.2 mg/kg: 928); end-of-maintenance Cmax for 0.06/0.1/0.2/0.3/0.8 mg/kg/h = 41.6/69.1/138/207/550 ng/mL.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- No ke0, t1/2ke0, tPeak, EC50 or hysteresis analysis. No EEG/BIS model.
- The only concentration-effect statement is empirical: the model-predicted plasma concentration range matching the target sedation (RASS -2 to +1) in the ICU studies 205 and 307 was 77.0-215.6 ng/mL (90% prediction interval, 5th-95th percentile), median 143.3 ng/mL (Fig 3-5 legends, PDF p6-8; text PDF p6). It is a post-hoc individual (Bayes) concentration at the time of RASS measurement, total plasma, mechanically ventilated adults. Not a MAC or EC50.
- Potency: ciprofol is described as about 4-5 times as potent as propofol (Introduction/Discussion, citing other work; not measured here).
- Exposure-safety: no meaningful relation between exposure (loading dose, Cmax, Cavg, AUC0-10h) and hypotension (19/112 ICU patients) (PDF p6-7). An inverse Cavg/AUC0-10h association with p < 0.05 was found but is attributed to dose-selection confounding.

## Covariates
Weight on CL (exponent 0.578, centred 62 kg); age on V1; sex, sampling site, ICU vs non-ICU and long- vs short-term-infusion study flag as above. Not significant: race, bilirubin, ALP, AST, ALT, total protein, creatinine clearance. Sensitivity analysis: weight is the most influential; effects of all covariates on AUC0-24h and Cmax are under about 21%. A 40 kg patient has 29.3% lower AUC0-24h; a 116 kg (BMI 40, extrapolated) patient has 59.2% higher Cmax (PDF p8-9). Non-ICU subjects have 20.9% higher AUC0-24h than ICU subjects (the authors can't explain why).

## Caveats
- Weight range 41.6-97.9 kg and BMI <= 30; obese predictions are extrapolation, and the authors suggest lean-body-weight dosing may be needed.
- Reference subject is an ICU patient with arterial sampling; healthy volunteer and surgical short-term infusion estimates need the FLG and ICU terms.
- High eta-shrinkage for V1, Q2, V2, Q3 and V3 (30.9-50.3%); IIV on V1 is 75%.
- The ICU-vs-non-ICU effect has no mechanistic explanation. Supplement (Table S1-S5) not available.
