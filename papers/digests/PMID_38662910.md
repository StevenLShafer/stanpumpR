# Shafer 2024: Vocacapsaicin for postsurgical pain

## Study
Phase 2, multicenter (3 sites), triple-blind, placebo-controlled, dose-ranging trial (NCT03599089) in bunionectomy. n=147 randomized 1:1:1:1 (Table 1: 36 at 0.05 mg/ml, 36 at 0.15, 38 at 0.30, 37 placebo). Mean age 46.4 y, 84% female, mean weight 78.8 kg (SD 16.2), BMI 28.5. Single intraoperative surgical-site dose of 14 ml (0.7, 2.1 or 4.2 mg vocacapsaicin, or vehicle), split between osteotomy surface, deep soft tissue, capsule and wound surface (printed p.251). All patients got 30 mg ketorolac, 1 g acetaminophen and a Mayo block with 20-30 ml 0.5% bupivacaine. Only opioid rescue was allowed 0-96 h (5 mg oxycodone; 1 mg oxycodone = 1.5 mg oral morphine equivalent, p.252, ref. 33).

## Model and parameters
No PK model or concentrations are reported. The introduction says vocacapsaicin converts to capsaicin with half-life under 5 min at physiologic pH, and capsaicin and the cyclic urea reach maximum plasma levels at 1-2 h (p.251; no data given).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Primary: pain-at-rest NRS AUC 0-96 h, 0.30 mg/ml vs placebo reduced by 134 NRS x h (median 33%, 95% CI 10-52%, d=0.61, P=0.005; p.253). Lower doses: 21% (0.05) and 20% (0.15), concentration trend P=0.013. Opioid-free 0-96 h: 26% vs 5% (P=0.025). Opioid consumption 0-96 h reduced by 28 OME, median 50% (26-67%), P=0.002; 24% (0.05) and 33% (0.15). Week-1 pain AUC reduced 37%. Hazard ratio for continued opioid use, vocacapsaicin 0.30 mg/ml against placebo, 0.56 (95% CI 0.39-0.89; P=0.009, log-rank), from the Kaplan-Meier analysis of time to opioid cessation (Fig. 4); median time to cessation 23 h earlier (95% CI -4 to 52 h; P=0.074). Mean NRS at 48 h: 4.6 placebo vs 2.8 at 0.30 mg/ml. Adverse events did not differ between groups (Table 2). No MEAC, ke0, tPeak or concentration-effect relationship (the dose-response is on administered concentration only). The only potency conversion is oxycodone 1 mg = 1.5 mg OME. Opioids here are rescue medication, not the test drug.

## Caveats
Sponsor-funded; Shafer is a consultant and equity holder (p.258). Opioids were the only rescue 0-96 h by design. No local anesthetic at the study-drug site, so early burning pain. Concentrations above 0.30 mg/ml not tested. Wound dehiscence events clustered at one site (Table 2 footnote).
