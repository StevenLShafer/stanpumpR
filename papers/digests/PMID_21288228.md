# Volmanen 2011: Timing of remifentanil PCA bolus in early labour

## Study
Double-blind, randomised cross-over trial, Oulu University Hospital (PDF p.2). 50 healthy term parturients, uncomplicated singleton pregnancies, recruited 15 Sep 2004 to 9 Oct 2008 in early first stage (cervix under 7 cm), no opioid in prior 4 h, no regional block, average contraction interval over 4 min. 41 analysed (Group 1 n = 22, Group 2 n = 19; Fig 2, PDF p.4). Table 1 (image confirmed): age 28 (SD 6.2) y, range 19-45; height 165 (5.3) cm; weight 82 (12.8) kg, range 62-105; gestation 40 (1.2) weeks; induction 15 (37%); primipara/multipara 28/13; cervix before 2.8 (1) cm, after 3.9 (1.8) cm; last dose to delivery 385 (285) min.

Drug: remifentanil 0.4 ug/kg (median effective PCA bolus in the authors' earlier dose-finding study, ref 11) as a 20 ug/mL solution, infused over 1 min into a saline carrier line at about 100 mL/h. Lockout 1 min for the instant device; none for the delayed device. No background infusion, no total dose limit. Two PCA pumps on one trigger: one gave the bolus at the trigger ("instant"), the other after a delay set to the mean of three prior contraction intervals minus 140 s ("delayed"; Fig 1, PDF p.2), the 140 s being the time assumed optimal for blood-effect-site equilibration. Two periods of 6-8 contractions, washout of at least 2 contractions, 20 min follow-up. Pain 0-10 NRS after every contraction, pain relief 0-4, sedation and nausea 0-3 verbal scales. Primary outcome: pain intensity difference (PID) over contractions 4-6. No drug concentrations were measured.

## Model and parameters
No PK model. Observed consumption (Table 2, PDF p.5, image confirmed; median, 25th-75th percentile, ug/kg/min): first period Group 1 0.067 (0.057-0.078), Group 2 0.079 (0.053-0.10); second period Group 1 0.074 (0.058-0.086), Group 2 0.084 (0.065-0.11). Pooled text values (PDF p.4): 0.067 (0.055-0.090) first period, 0.077 (0.063-0.095) second, P = 0.007. Proportion of contractions with a PCA bolus 0.8-1.0 (median).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Opioid audit (remifentanil):
- MEAC / minimum analgesic concentration: none reported; no concentrations were measured. The only dose reference is the 0.4 ug/kg median effective PCA bolus from the authors' earlier study (ref 11), a dose, not a concentration.
- ke0 / t1/2 ke0 / tPeak: none estimated. Cited background: blood-effect-site equilibration half-time 0.5-1.6 min, ventilatory depression starting at 30 s and peaking at 2.5 min after a short IV bolus (PDF p.2, refs 8-10). End point of those references is ventilation (Olofsen, Babenco) and EEG (Egan), per the references named, not measured here.
- Potency ratios: none.
- Results on pain: pooled pain score during contractions 4-6 was 3.3 (2-5) in period 1 and 5.3 (3.4-6.2) in period 2 (P < 0.001), a period effect the authors attribute probably to acute opioid-induced hyperalgesia (speculative). By period, no difference between instant and delayed (Table 2: PID period 1 Group 1 2.5 vs Group 2 3.3, P = 0.388; period 2 2.5 vs 2.3, P = 0.724). Post hoc subgroup with contraction interval of at least 4 min (n = 19), after discounting excess "bad demands" in the delayed device: PID 2 (0.5-3.2) instant vs 2.5 (1.3-4) delayed, P = 0.01. Sedation 2.0 (1.0-3.0) vs 2.0 (0.75-3.0), P = 0.061; oxygen supplement needed 9/41 (22%) in each regimen (Table 3, PDF p.5).

## Covariates
None analysed for PK. Primiparity, labour progress and period order were considered only descriptively; sample size calculation assumed a 2-point pain difference, SD 1.8, 90% power, 25% dropout.

## Caveats
The authors state a period effect forced separate analysis of periods, weakening power; fixed 0.4 ug/kg was too large for many (patients skipped boluses; 49% and 39% skipped at least one in periods 1 and 2); the longer lockout of the instant device and the refractory time of the delayed device produced dosing imbalance; the subgroup finding is post hoc; short exposure (about 30 min) in early labour. Desaturation in 22%. Four babies delivered during the study; five Apgar under 8 at 1 min, all over 1 h after the last dose.
