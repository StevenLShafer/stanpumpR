# Sangle 2018: sitagliptin 100 mg PK/PD in healthy Indian men, fasted and fed

## Study
- Randomised, single-dose, open-label, three-treatment, three-period, three-sequence crossover bioavailability
  study (Wockhardt-funded); sitagliptin (Januvia 100 mg, "test product C") studied alongside two in-house
  formulations whose data are not reported. Separate fasted and fed studies, 18 participants each; 1-week washout
  (PDF p2, printed 562).
- Design inconsistency in the paper: Methods (p2) say that in each period only six participants received
  sitagliptin, whereas Results and Table 2 report n = 18 fasted and n = 15 fed for sitagliptin.
- Population (Table 1, p4): healthy Indian men 18–45 y eligible; mean age 22.4 y (SD 4.31; range 18–34), weight
  62.7 kg (SD 7.53; range 51–77), height 169.8 cm, BMI 21.74 kg/m² (SD 2.20); fasting glucose 85.01 mg/dL.
  Table 1's text-layer columns are shifted; values above are as stated in the Results text (p5).
- Fed arm: high-fat, high-energy breakfast before dosing. Overnight fast ≥ 8 h.
- PK sampling: pre-dose and 0.33–72 h (26 post-dose samples). Plasma sitagliptin by SPE and LC-MS/MS (details in the
  supplement, not archived). NCA in Phoenix WinNonlin.
- PD: plasma DPP-IV activity (Gly-Pro-AMC substrate) at the same times; active GLP-1 and insulin (ELISA, fasted study
  only) at baseline, 4 h (pre-meal) and 4.5, 5, 6 h.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End point: plasma DPP-IV inhibition (% from baseline). Simple Emax model pooled across participants and time
  points (Gauss-Newton): EC50 24.0 (2.2) nM fasted, 26.4 (2.87) nmol/L fed (p = 0.0106) (text p5; Table 4 p6).
  Emax and Hill coefficient not reported. No hysteresis/ke0 analysed.
- WAI(0–24 h) (weighted average inhibition): 89.6 (2.2)% fasted vs 87.7 (2.7)% fed (p = 0.0332); ≥ 80% throughout
  24 h (Table 4; Fig. 3). Fig. 4 marks 80% inhibition as the preclinical target for near-maximal glucose lowering.
- Post-meal (meal at 4 h) weighted-average active GLP-1 11.1 pmol/L (printed "(9.88)", described as GMR) vs 2.53 in
  a non-Indian comparator; insulin AUC 0–2 h 47.9 (24.9) μIU/mL (Table 5, p8).

## Covariates
Food only: fasted vs fed. AUC 10% and Cmax 31% higher fasted; Tmax and t½ unchanged. No other covariates.

## Caveats
- Healthy young men only, single 100 mg dose, no placebo (authors' limitations).
- Contradictory n (6 vs 18 sitagliptin recipients per period, p2 vs p4–5); three fed participants dropped.
- Cmax p-value 0.012 in Table 2 vs 0.011 in text; fed t½ 10.68 (table) vs 10.6 (text).
- Interethnic comparison is against historical studies of different doses and designs, without statistics.
- Text-layer Tables 1 and 2 are scrambled; values here were taken from the rendered page 5.
