# Dahan 2001: alfentanil-sevoflurane response surfaces on ventilation, heart rate and BIS

## Study
- Nine healthy male volunteers, 18-25 yr, no tobacco/illicit drug use. Single-day experiment, 5-6 h (PDF p 4).
- Dynamic end-tidal forcing: isocapnic hypoxic step (PETO2 110 -> 50 mmHg, 3 min at 50) at PETCO2 about 48 mmHg, 5-7 mmHg above resting; two hypoxic studies per treatment level. 109 paired hypoxic studies in all (Table 1).
- Sevoflurane 0-0.4 end-tidal % (12 min equilibration), alfentanil by target-controlled infusion to blood/plasma targets 10, 20, 30, 40, 50 ng/ml (Maitre 1987 population PK), and combinations (Table 1). Hypoxic tests 5-10 min after target reached. Measured plasma alfentanil averaged 12.7, 21.3, 27.4, 35.6 ng/ml for targets 10, 20, 30, 40 (p 5).
- Alfentanil assayed by capillary gas chromatography, plasma (total); sevoflurane at the mouth.
- Outcomes: normoxic Vi, VT, RR, HR, BIS; hypoxic sensitivities (delta E / delta SpO2). NONMEM V, population approach.

## Model and parameters
Response surface after Minto 2000: E = E0 * (1 - [(UA+US)^gamma / 2 * I(Q)]) (equation 6, written f(x) = alpha(1 - x^gamma)), with U = C/C50, Q = UA/(UA+US), I(Q) a two-parameter spline (Imax, Qmax; Appendix 1). gamma was not different from 1 for every variable, so concentration-effect relations are linear over the range studied. C25/C50 are concentrations giving 25%/50% reduction of the variable (Table 2, population value ± SE; %CV is interindividual).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- End points: steady-state ventilation at fixed PETCO2 about 48 mmHg, hypoxic ventilatory sensitivity, heart rate, BIS. No analgesia end point.
- MEAC / minimum effective or analgesic concentration: none reported. The nearest quantities are C25/C50 for respiratory depression above (plasma alfentanil, ng/ml, total; healthy young men; GC assay).
- ke0 / t½ke0 / tPeak / hysteresis: none measured (steady state, 5-10 min after target). The authors assume plasma-brain equilibration t½ of 1 min, taken from Scott 1985 (EEG; ref 14), not measured here (p 3).
- Potency: alfentanil C25 for resting Vi 37.7 ng/ml vs hypoxic drive 15.7 ng/ml (about 2.4 times more sensitive); sevoflurane 0.73 vs 0.14 ET% (about 5 times); the paper says C25 values differ 2-8 times. Synergy for resting Vi and HR (Imax 1.92 and 2.47 at Q about 0.7); additive for hypoxic Vi and HR, VT and BIS. Example: 25% reduction of Vi by 13.4 ng/ml alfentanil + 0.12 ET% sevoflurane (p 1, 4).
- Alfentanil did not change BIS even with sevoflurane.
- No equianalgesic ratio.

## Covariates
None (all male, one age band).

## Caveats
- Only a small part of the surface was sampled for Vi and HR (Table 1 sparse above 30 ng/ml); C50 values are extrapolations and the authors warn against extrapolating, with HR especially weak (CV 108%, SE about 58% of the estimate).
- Fixed PETCO2 about 48 mmHg, steady state, healthy men, no sedation score. No blood-gas dynamics.
- Alfentanil target (blood/plasma) concentrations given as TCI; the measured plasma values ran below target at 30-40 ng/ml (27.4, 35.6).
