# Marsot 2017: population PK of smoked THC in occasional smokers

## Study
- 12 healthy Caucasian men, occasional cannabis smokers and tobacco smokers (3-8 cigarettes/day); Table 1 (PDF p3, printed 51): age 22.5 y (20-28), weight 70.4 kg (62.5-91.0), height 180 cm (173-187), creatinine 91.3 µM, AST 18.6 IU/L (7.0-28.0), ALT 22.3 IU/L (6.0-45.0). Table 1 "Value" column is not labelled mean or median.
- One cannabis cigarette, 0.5 g of 4% THC (about 20 mg THC nominal), mixed with tobacco, smoked under a paced procedure (deep inhalation, ~4 s hold) over about 20 min (p2). Placebo crossover (tobacco only); order blinded.
- Venous plasma (EDTA) at baseline, 1, 5, 10, 15, 20, 30 min, 1, 2, 4, 6, 8, 12, 24, 48, 72 h (p2). GC/MS, linear 1-20 ng/mL, LOQ 1.0 ng/mL; interassay accuracy 92.8-112.4%, imprecision 3.9-13.1%.
- 99 THC concentrations used, up to 79.8 µg/L; many samples after ~10 h were below LOQ and excluded (p5).
- NONMEM 7.3, FOCE; proportional residual error; exponential IIV on k10 and V1 only.

## Model and parameters
Three compartments, first-order elimination from the central compartment, parameterised in micro-constants and V1, input treated as a bolus (Discussion p5). Table 2 (PDF p4, printed 52), confirmed on the rendered page:

The abstract rounds k21 to 0.022 and, like section 3.2, names the last two constants k23 and k32 (see open question). The 0.408 typical k10 is θk10 × the Table 1 ALT of 22.3 IU/L (derived: 0.0183 × 22.3 = 0.408). All parameters are apparent: the paper does not state a bioavailability or how the dose was entered.

Derived (not printed in the paper): CL = 0.408 × 19.1 = 7.79 L/h (the paper's Discussion, p4, states 7.8 L/h); ln2/k10 = 102 min (the paper's "half-life of 102 min" is therefore the k10 half-life, not a terminal one). Mammillary reading: Vss = 3,509 L with k21 = 0.0224 (3,572 L with the abstract's 0.022); exponents 5.80, 0.825 and 0.00202 h⁻¹, half-lives 0.12 h, 0.84 h and ~343 h. Catenary reading (k23/k32): Vss ≈ 7,012 L.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None measured or modelled. No ke0, no EC50, no effect end point. Not an opioid paper. The introduction (p1) cites literature values only: psychoactive effects peaking at 15-30 min, and systemic bioavailability of smoked THC of about 10-35%.

## Covariates
ALT on k10, linear through the origin: k10 = 0.0183 × ALT (IU/L). Adding it dropped OFV by 8.649 (section 3.3, p3; Fig. 1). The ALT range was normal (6-45 IU/L). The authors say this needs further investigation. Weight, age and renal markers were not retained.

## Caveats
- n = 12, men only, a single dose level, apparent parameters with an unstated dose/F treatment.
- Most samples after ~10 h fell below the 1 ng/mL LOQ and were excluded, so the terminal phase is poorly informed; the authors say the terminal half-life was underestimated. Even so, the mammillary reading implies ~343 h (derived), against the 20-36 h literature values they cite. The fitted terminal phase is barely supported by data.
- IIV shrinkage of 57.9% (k10) and 83.9% (V1). The bootstrap mean ωk10 (0.665) is far from the estimate (0.244).
- Internal inconsistencies: k13/k31 vs k23/k32; 1000 bootstrap data sets (methods, p3) vs 500 replicates (results, p3); sampling to 72 h (p2) vs data "up to a 48 h" (p5).
- In Fig. 1 ALT is on the y axis and k10 on the x axis.

## Verification against stanpumpR
- **Library model**: there is no `R/drugs_thc.R` and no THC/cannabis row in `inst/extdata/drugDefaults_global.csv`, so there is nothing to compare numerically. The paper reports no IV data, which is consistent with the repo's refusal to put it on an IV or intranasal route.
- **docs/illicit-drugs-specification-review.md:166**: "three compartments in micro-constants, central volume 19.1 L (39.7% RSE)". **Matches** Table 2. The five rate constants it lists (0.408, 4.070, 0.022, 1.070, 1.060 /h) match the abstract. The row says the assignment "is an inference from the order". Table 2 settles it: k10, k12, k21, k13, k31, the same order the repo assumed. The abstract and section 3.2 instead name k23/k32 (open question). "Subjects smoked ad libitum" matches the abstract's wording, although the methods describe a paced procedure.
- **docs/illicit-drugs-specification-review.md:178**: "Marsot's implied 3,572 L" Vss/F. **Discrepancy (minor)**: 3,572 L is reproduced only with the abstract's rounded k21 = 0.022 (derived 3,571.9 L). Table 2's k21 = 0.0224 gives 3,509 L (derived), 1.8% lower. Under the catenary labelling the abstract uses, it would be ~7,012 L. It is correct that this is Vss/F rather than Vss, since the paper never states F.
- **docs/illicit-drugs-specification-review.md:175**: Marsot is grouped with the models that are apparent on unmeasured pulmonary bioavailability. **Matches in substance**: no F was measured. The paper does not say how the dose was coded (open question).
- **inst/help/models/illicit-drugs.md:59** (context says :58): "three-compartment fit in 12 occasional smokers parameterised in micro-constants on a 19.1 L central volume". **Matches** (Table 1, Table 2).
- Side note: the repo gives Heuberger 2015's clearance as 38.8 L/h. Marsot's Discussion (p4) quotes Heuberger as 38.7 L/h. That is Marsot's paraphrase and cannot be checked here.
