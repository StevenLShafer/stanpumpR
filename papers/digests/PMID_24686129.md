# Beleyn 2014: estradiol and weight as covariates of paracetamol clearance in young women

## Study
Pooled non-compartmental analysis (Leuven, Belgium; EUDRACT 2010-020164-37), PDF pp 2-3 (printed 212-213).
All subjects received a single **2 g IV paracetamol loading dose over 15 min**; plasma sampled at 1, 2, 4 and 6 h
(lithium heparin), HPLC assay.
- 49 women at caesarean delivery (5 excluded for < 3 samples; 44 analysed), gestational age ~26-41 weeks (Fig. 3).
- 8 of these restudied 10-15 weeks postpartum; 7 again at ~1 year postpartum (4/8 and 2/7 on oral contraceptives).
- 8 non-pregnant female controls aged 27-37, no OCs.
- 14 historical non-pregnant women aged 19-32, all on OCs, raw data from Gregoire et al. 2007 (same 2 g IV dose,
  same sampling times); no hormones measured.
- Estradiol and progesterone by ELISA with electro-chemiluminescence (Roche Modular E-170).
- 325 samples, 81 profiles. CL = dose/AUC0-inf, normalised to BSA (Haycock); V = CL/ke (an area volume); t1/2 from ke.
  Medians (range). Statistics: Kruskal-Wallis, Friedman, Mann-Whitney, Spearman, multiple linear regression (Statistica 8).

Paired subgroup (text, PDF p3): median CL at delivery 11.8 L/h/m2 (the 8 restudied women) vs 6.42 at 15 weeks and
7.13 at ~1 year (both p < 0.05); no difference between the two postpartum visits, nor by OC use.

Regression (PDF p4, printed 214): CL (L/h/m2) = 0.8609 + 0.1248 x weight (kg) + 0.0003 x estradiol (pg/mL),
R2adj 0.41; weight p = 0.0462, estradiol p < 0.0001.

Derived values (medians multiplied, so only indicative): ratio delivery/controls 11.9/8.4 = 1.42; delivery/Gregoire
11.9/9.9 = 1.20; absolute CL at delivery 11.9 x 1.95 = ~23.2 L/h, controls 8.4 x 1.78 = ~15.0 L/h (ratio ~1.55
unnormalised). Fig. 1 (means) reads about 13 vs 8.5 L/h/m2.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None measured. No ke0, EC50 or analgesic concentration; the discussion infers shorter analgesia from higher clearance
without data. Not an opioid.

## Covariates
Estradiol (Spearman R 0.494 with CL) and progesterone (R 0.474); weight and estradiol retained in the regression above.
Gestational age not associated (R -0.179, p 0.244; term vs preterm p 0.452). No OC effect detected in non-pregnant women.

## Caveats
- Sampling only to 6 h with 4 points; NCA, so AUC extrapolation is a large share and V = CL/ke is an area volume.
- Delivery samples are within hours of caesarean surgery (surgery, fluids, blood loss mixed with pregnancy).
- Control group n = 8; the OC group is historical, from another centre, without hormone data.
- CL normalised to BSA; the pregnant women's BSA includes gravid weight, so the per-m2 ratio (1.42) is smaller
  than the absolute one (~1.55, derived).
- Figure 1 significance labels disagree with the text (see open_questions).
- The comparator for "higher clearance" is the 8 non-OC controls (p < 0.05) or the Gregoire OC women (p < 0.01),
  and the paired comparison is against the same women postpartum, whose clearance is itself below the controls.
