# Fattinger 1991: population pharmacokinetics of oral quinidine

## Study
60 arrhythmia patients (46 M, 14 F; median age 65.5 y, range 28-82; median weight 70.5 kg, range 45-105; median CLcr 62.5 mL/min, range 17 to >100), 260 serum concentrations (56 at steady state, 204 not; median 4 per patient), mostly routine monitoring. Oral quinidine sulphate and/or slow-release bisulphate (Kinidin duriles). Doses corrected to quinidine base: x0.663 bisulphate, x0.829 sulphate (PDF p3). HPLC assay specific for quinidine (CV 1.5% within run, 4.2% between run). NONMEM. Validation in 30 further patients (28 of 30, 93%, within the 90% interval; PDF p4-5).

Authors' derived values (PDF p4): half-lives 2.22 and 10.1 h and oral CL 18.1 L/h without kidney disease; renal fraction 31% at CLcr 100 mL/min. CLcr (PDF p3 image) = (150 - age) x body weight / serum creatinine (umol/L), +10% men, -10% women (modified Cockcroft-Gault, Dettli 1983).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD. Recommended range 2-5 mg/L (Follath 1983), used for the dosing simulation. Not an opioid; no MEAC/ke0/potency ratio.

## Covariates
Only CLcr (renal CL) and severe heart or liver failure (nonrenal CL about halved) were kept. Body weight, sex, age, nifedipine, mild/moderate heart or liver dysfunction: no effect (PDF p4).

## Caveats
Only CLcr >15 mL/min; few severe HF/LF patients (about 5), not validated; a possible V1 effect of severe failure was not retained. Dosing regimens (600 mg sulphate, then bisulphate 500 mg q8h or q12h, 250 mg q8h in severe failure; Fig 6) come from Monte Carlo simulation.
