# Mim 2023: model-based meta-analysis of gliclazide PK and glucose-insulin PD

## Study
No new subjects. PK: PubMed and Scopus search (September 2021), 258 articles, 21 digitisable studies (WebPlotDigitizer
4.5): 11 IR in healthy volunteers, 2 IR in type 2 diabetes, 5 MR in volunteers, 2 IR in both, 1 IR and MR; 77 arms
(58 IR volunteer, 7 IR T2D, 12 MR volunteer), arm sizes 6-41 (mean 18), plus 39 individual IR profiles from three
studies (PDF p4-5). PD: individual postprandial glucose (every 30 min to 6 h) and insulin (fasting and 30 min) from
Naidoo 2006, 13 South African T2D patients, placebo then 40, 80 and 160 mg IR once daily in 2-week periods with a meal
test at the end of each (PDF p3). Table 1 (PDF p3, rendered): age 52.0 (11) y, 37-77; BMI 29.5 (5.9), 18.7-41.1; FPG
11.6 (3.4) mM; FPI 18.5 (13) mU/L; 54% women; 69% Asian, 16% Black. NONMEM 7.4, ADVAN6, FOCE-I; NONMEM PRIOR for the
disposition parameters (Brendel 2006) and the IGI model (Silber 2007, Jauslin 2011).

## Model and parameters
PK: one compartment, first-order elimination, transit-compartment absorption; disposition shared between IR and MR,
absorption (ka, MTrT, their IIV) separate, MR slower; relative F estimated 0.997 and then fixed equal (PDF p5). No
covariate significant (concomitant metformin, health status tested). The PD analysis used the IR model (Table 3,
fixed values, ISV ignored). The MR absorption values and the full PK table (Table B2) are in the online appendix only.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Integrated glucose-insulin model, placebo parameters re-estimated on Naidoo's placebo arm and fixed (Table 3 glucose
and insulin rows, e.g. GSS 11.8 mM, CLI 73.2 L/h). Six sites of action tested (Table 2, PDF p6); the sigmoidal model on
inhibition of insulin elimination had the lowest AIC (-445), marginally better than stimulation of insulin secretion
(-439); no secondary effect; effect direct, no delay. End points of simulation: steady-state HbA1c from 24-h glucose
AUC (Nathan regression) and glucose < 3 mmol/L. Simulated results (Results PDF p5-6, Discussion PDF p8): about 25%
reach HbA1c < 7% untreated, a maximum of 40% with treatment, 95% of the maximum at 320 mg/day IR; at 120 mg, 32% IR vs
33% MR reach HbA1c < 7%, with 8.7% vs 7.2% having glucose < 3 mmol/L; MR 270 mg gives 39.7% at the risk of IR 160 mg.
The Abstract instead gives a maximum of 44% reaching HbA1c < 7%, 11% with glucose < 3 mmol/L and 35 min of low glucose
in the most sensitive 5%; the Discussion gives "up to 12%" and the Caveats 30 min. These figures do not agree with one
another; the paper does not say which is right (unresolved).

## Covariates
None in the final PK or PD model.

## Caveats
Digitised aggregate data; doses above 160 mg extrapolated under assumed linearity; steady state assumed from
single-dose data. PD from 13 patients with two insulin samples each. PK sources from Asia and Europe, PD in South
Africa. Within-paper conflicts: number of transit compartments 5.17 (text) vs 5.50 (Table 3); headline efficacy and
hypoglycaemia figures differ between Abstract, Results and Discussion. Simulated glucose < 3 mmol/L is not clinical
hypoglycaemia (no symptoms, no rescue).
