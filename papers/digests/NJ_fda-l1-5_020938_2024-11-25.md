# Mobic label 2024: oral meloxicam

## Study
Not a study: US prescribing information for Mobic tablets 7.5 and 15 mg (osteoarthritis, rheumatoid
arthritis, JRA). 12.3 summarises PK "from various studies" (Table 4 footnote 1); assay not given.
The repo does not name the label; the 30 mg capsule vs 30 mg IV bolus F of 89% is this label's wording.

## Model and parameters
No compartmental model. 12.3 Absorption (label p. 12): absolute bioavailability of meloxicam capsules 89%
after a single 30 mg oral dose vs 30 mg IV bolus. IV dose-proportional 5-60 mg; oral dose-proportional
7.5-15 mg on multiple dosing. Mean Cmax 4-5 h after a 7.5 mg tablet fasted (prolonged absorption).
Steady state by day 5. Second peak at 12-14 h (biliary recycling). Suspension bioequivalent to capsules;
capsules bioequivalent to Mobic tablets. High-fat meal: capsule Cmax +22%, AUC unchanged, tmax 5-6 h.

Metabolism and excretion as in the ANJESO label (CYP2C9 major, CYP3A4 minor; four inactive metabolites;
0.2% urine / 1.6% feces unchanged; cholestyramine halves AUC after IV). Specific populations: paediatric
t½ 15.2 (10.1) h at 2-6 y and 13.0 (3.0) h at 7-16 y; body weight the only covariate on CL/F in children.
Elderly females AUCss +47%, Cmax,ss +32% vs younger females (weight-normalised). Sex: single-dose t½
19.5 h female vs 23.4 h male (steady state 17.9 vs 21.4 h). Renal impairment: total concentrations fall
and clearance rises, free AUC similar. Hemodialysis: free fraction 1% vs 0.3%; not dialysable.

Dosing (2.1-2.5): 7.5 mg once daily, up to 15 mg/day maximum; 7.5 mg/day maximum on hemodialysis;
JRA 7.5 mg once daily for ≥60 kg. Not interchangeable with other oral meloxicam formulations (2.6).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No concentration-effect relationship, no analgesic concentration, no ke0.

## Covariates
Sex, age (elderly females), renal function, body weight (children), qualitative only.

## Caveats
- F 0.89 is from one 30 mg capsule study; n not given.
- Table 4 pools different studies, doses and fed states.
