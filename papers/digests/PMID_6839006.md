# Javaid 1983: cocaine kinetics after intravenous and intranasal dosing

## Study
- Four adult volunteers with a history of cocaine use and no other known psychiatric or physical illness,
  admitted to a clinical research unit for two weeks and tested once daily (Methods, PDF p2). Age, sex and weight
  not reported.
- Intravenous: cocaine **hydrochloride** 32 mg in 1 mL saline over 60 s. Intranasal: cocaine hydrochloride 64 or
  96 mg mixed with lactose to 100 mg of powder, snorted through a 5 cm straw within 1 min (p1-2). Doses are salt
  mass throughout.
- Plasma (heparin + sodium fluoride 2.5 mg/mL blood to block esterase hydrolysis), GLC with nitrogen or electron
  capture detection; LLOQ 5 ng/mL; CV 8.7% within-day at 25 ng/mL, 13.2% day-to-day (p3). Sampling to 120 min
  (Figures 1-5); exact times not listed.
- Analysis: intravenous one-compartment, C0 by back-extrapolation; intranasal one-compartment with first-order
  absorption, ka and ke by the method of residuals; AUC trapezoidal to 120 min plus C120/ke; F = dose-adjusted
  AUC ratio intranasal/intravenous; V = F·D/(ke·AUC) (p3-4).

different. F significantly lower at 64 than 96 mg (paired t, p < 0.05); the authors attribute it to the larger
amount of drug in the fixed 100 mg powder.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No effect measured, no ke0 / t½ke0 / tPeak, no effective or toxic concentration, no potency ratio.

## Covariates
None examined (n = 4; weight not reported). Dose dependence of intranasal F is the only systematic effect.

## Caveats
- n = 4, experienced users, sex/weight/age not given; SEMs, not SDs.
- Intravenous dose is small (32 mg HCl); the authors discuss reports of biphasic, longer decline at 100-200 mg IV,
  so the one-compartment fit may be dose-specific.
- Sampling only to 120 min, so the terminal phase is short relative to the half-lives; the residual area is
  extrapolated from C120.
- Text and Table 1 disagree slightly on the mean intravenous half-life (41.4 min vs 0.71 h = 42.6 min).
- F is computed against each subject's single intravenous AUC; intra-subject variability is large.
- The derived clearance uses a ratio of means; individual values (derived) are 2.19, 3.40, 1.93, 2.29 L/min on the
  HCl basis.
