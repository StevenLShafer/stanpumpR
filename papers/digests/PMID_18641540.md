# Dowling 2008: population PK of IV, IM and IN naloxone

## Study
Open-label crossover, 6 healthy men, median age 25 y (24-45), weight 80 kg (75-100), height 1.78 m (1.75-1.93).
Fixed order: 0.8 mg IV, 0.8 mg IM (gluteal), 0.8 mg IN, 2 mg IV, 2 mg IN; ≥ 2-day washout. IN by Mucosal Atomiser
Device with the 400 µg/mL injectable solution (5 mL for 2 mg), half per nostril, subject at 45°. Samples 5, 10, 15,
30, 45, 60, 90, 120, 180, 240 min. HPLC; LOD 0.3, LOQ 1 µg/L (Fig. 2 caption: 1 ng/mL). Only values above LOQ used,
first BLQ set to LOQ/2. 0.8 mg IN never quantifiable; 2 mg IN quantifiable in 2 subjects only. Final data: 128
concentrations (82 IV, 39 IM, 7 IN). NONMEM VI, FOCE; Wings for NONMEM.

## Model and parameters
Three-compartment, first-order absorption for IM and IN, F(IV) fixed to 1; combined error (small fixed additive);
BSV on CL and V2 (central) only. Final covariate model (p.492):
CL = TVCL × (LBW2005/70)^0.75 × exp(η1); V2 = TVV2 × (WT/70) × exp(η2).

Simulated median tmax: IM 12 min, IN 6-9 min (Fig. 3). Adding weight and LBW2005 to CL: ΔOFV -4.136.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD measured; no ke0, no EC50. Discussion (p.494) quotes Kaufman 1981 (reversal of morphine respiratory depression):
about 550 µg naloxone gives a morphine dose ratio of 5 (20 mg acts like 4 mg), 1100 µg reduces it to 2 mg; and that
0.05-0.1 mg naloxone may suffice for antagonism. Not this study's data.

## Covariates
LBW2005 = Janmahasatian 2005 lean body weight (ref. 31) on CL, allometric 0.75, normalised to 70 kg LBW. Total weight
on central volume, linear, normalised to 70 kg. Sex not tested (all male).

## Caveats
n = 6 men, narrow demographics; BSV on CL likely underestimated (authors). IN data are 7 samples from 2 subjects;
F IN 4% may be overestimated and reflects a dilute 5 mL dose largely swallowed. Fixed dosing order. 4-h sampling
limits the terminal phase. CL is referenced to LBW 70 kg, which is a heavy (non-reference) man.
