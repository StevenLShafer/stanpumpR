# Fragen 1999: dexmedetomidine and sevoflurane MAC in 55-70 year olds

## Study
Prospective, randomised, placebo-controlled; IRB approval and written consent. 45 adults, age 55-70 yr, 15 per group, elective surgery with at least a 3-inch skin incision, unpremedicated. Table 1 (PDF p3, mean ± SD): age 62.9 ± 4.4 / 64.6 ± 4.1 / 61.7 ± 4.6 yr (placebo / 0.3 ng/mL / 0.6 ng/mL); height 171.0 / 171.4 / 171.0 cm; weight 81.0 ± 15.9 / 78.7 ± 12.8 / 76.2 ± 15.4 kg; F/M 7/8, 6/9, 9/6. No group differences.
Dexmedetomidine by Harvard 22 pump programmed by STANPUMP to targets of 0 (saline), 0.3 or 0.6 ng/mL, for at least 15 min before induction (infusion about 1 h in all groups). Induction with sevoflurane in oxygen by face mask (no IV induction drug or opioid before the MAC reading); after intubation the target end-tidal sevoflurane was held at least 15 min (about 30 min of sevoflurane in all groups) before skin incision. Starting concentrations 2% (placebo), 1.5% (0.3 ng/mL), 1% (0.6 ng/mL); Dixon up-down steps of 0.25%. Movement = purposeful movement observed by two people for 1 min after incision.
Venous plasma samples before the infusion and at incision; GC-MS, lower limit 10 pg/mL. Gas monitor Datex Ultima. Esophageal temperature kept above 36 C; ETCO2 35-45 mmHg.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: movement to skin incision; sevoflurane MAC as end-tidal %, at the dexmedetomidine plasma concentration at incision. PDF p3 (printed 468):

```yaml
  - {name: MAC sevoflurane Dixon-Mood, value: 1.79, unit: "% end-tidal", source: "p468", note: "0.39 ng/mL group, CI 2.08-1.5"}
  - {name: MAC sevoflurane Dixon-Mood, value: 1.46, unit: "% end-tidal", source: "p468", note: "0.70 ng/mL group, CI 1.62-1.3"}
  - {name: MAC sevoflurane logistic regression, value: 1.83, unit: "% end-tidal", source: "p468", note: "placebo, CI 2.0-1.68"}
  - {name: MAC sevoflurane logistic regression, value: 1.78, unit: "% end-tidal", source: "p468", note: "0.39 ng/mL group, CI 1.99-1.6"}
  - {name: MAC sevoflurane logistic regression, value: 1.51, unit: "% end-tidal", source: "p468", note: "0.70 ng/mL group, CI 1.69-1.36"}
```
- Reduction at 0.70 ng/mL: 17% (abstract; derived 1 - 1.51/1.83 = 17.5%, and 1 - 1.46/1.77 = 17.5% by Dixon-Mood), p=0.02; no difference at 0.39 ng/mL (p=0.67).
- Authors compare with isoflurane (Aantaa 1997: 47% reduction at 0.69 ng/mL, with alfentanil and thiopental at induction) and say the sevoflurane effect is smaller; they attribute Aantaa's larger effect to those co-drugs, not to a difference in plasma concentration, and note that a drug-specific difference was not tested.
- No ke0, t1/2 ke0, tPeak, hysteresis, EC50 or Emax. No opioid in this study (before the MAC reading), so no MEAC or equianalgesic ratio; no total/free or blood/plasma distinction beyond plasma assay.

## Covariates
None analysed (groups matched on age, size, sex, ASA).

## Caveats
Small groups (15); one plasma sample at incision. Measured concentrations higher than targets (0.39 vs 0.3, 0.70 vs 0.6). Dixon up-down and logistic regression both used, with the authors noting that logistic regression assumes independence the design violates. Ages 55-70 only. Funded in part by Abbott. The PDF's last page is the reprinted abstract of Aantaa 1997 (PMID_9158354), printed p470.
