# Simon 2002: lidocaine vs mepivacaine plasma kinetics after axillary brachial plexus block with epinephrine

## Study
Randomised double-blind, 30 ASA I-II day-case hand/forearm surgery patients, 15 per drug. Lidocaine group 10 M / 5 F,
80.4 ± 11.7 kg, 1.73 m, 55.5 ± 14.2 y. Mepivacaine group 7 M / 8 F, 73.5 ± 13.5 kg in the text (72.8 ± 13.9 in
Table 3), 1.74 m, 49.7 ± 12.3 y. Nerve-stimulator perivascular axillary block, 40 mL of 1.5% solution (600 mg) with
adrenaline 5 µg/mL, injected over ~30 s; t = 0 at end of injection. 18 radial-arterial samples: 0, 5, 10, 15, 20, 25,
30, 40, 50, 60, 75, 90, 105, 120, 135, 150, 165, 180 min. HPLC-UV (LOQ 0.30 µg/mL mepivacaine; MEGX 0.02 µg/mL),
total (bound + unbound) racemic drug in plasma. MW/Pharm: two-compartment (lidocaine) or one-compartment (mepivacaine)
with first-order absorption, plus non-compartmental analysis, all with F = 1.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Not an opioid. Sensory block by pinprick (Hollmén 3-point scale); motor block by hand dynamometer: complete in all within
20 min; lidocaine mean motor-block curve t½ 0.098 min and 4.0 min phases; no concentration-effect model, no ke0. Peak
concentrations below quoted toxic levels of 5-10 µg/mL. Three lidocaine patients had incomplete sensory block.

## Covariates
None.

## Caveats
- Only 3 h sampling; AUC∞ extrapolation large (AUC3h is 52% of AUC∞ for lidocaine, 31% for mepivacaine).
- Clearances assume F = 1 after tissue injection.
- Text-layer tables are misaligned; mepivacaine V/MRT row assignment is inferred. Body weight for the mepivacaine
  group differs between text (73.5) and Table 3 (72.8).
- Cmax/tmax are means of individual fitted values, not the peak of the mean curve.
- Drug section prints adrenaline as "5 µg l-1"; elsewhere 5 µg/mL.
