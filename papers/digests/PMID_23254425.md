# Han 2013: fluconazole population PK in burn patients

PDF page = printed page - 1005.

## Study
- 60 adult burn patients (50 male), BICU of Hangang Sacred Heart Hospital, Dec 2008-May 2010. Age mean 50.3 (20-82) y, weight 65.6 (40-90) kg, TBSA burned 50.3 (11-95)%, creatinine clearance (Cockcroft-Gault) 123.5 (21.6-282.7) mL/min, 29 with sepsis, 20 with edema, 15 on continuous renal replacement therapy (CRRT), time since burn 23.0 (7-88) days, APACHE II 7.6.
- Fluconazole 100-400 mg IV over 10-40 min every 24 h (5-17 days of therapy before sampling); 8 samples at 0, 3, 5, 9, 24, 27, 48 and 51 h; 409 observations. Plasma total drug by LC-MS/MS, LLOQ 0.04 ug/mL.
- NONMEM 7.2, FOCE-I; one compartment, first-order elimination, exponential between-subject variability, proportional residual error (final).

## Model and parameters
Final model (Table 3, PDF p4; estimate, % RSE):
CL = (1 - CRRT) x (theta1 + CLCR x theta4 + TBI x theta7 + SEPS x theta9) + CRRT x theta3; V = theta2 + (WT/65) x theta5 + EDEM x theta6 + TBI x theta8. CLCR enters scaled (the table calls theta4 the proportionality constant between CL and CLCR/120). TBI = 1 if within 30 days of injury, else 0.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD end point measured. Therapeutic success was simulated as fAUC/MIC >25 h (also 50 shown), 10,000 virtual patients, fAUC = AUC x 0.88 (12% protein binding assumed, from the literature), MIC sampled from 45 local Candida isolates (0.25 ug/mL 15.6%, 0.5 40.0%, 1 24.4%, 2 20.0%). Result: 400 mg/day recommended for MIC <=2 ug/mL, especially <30 days post-burn or on CRRT. Targets 25, 50 and 100 are discussed. Not an opioid; no MEAC or ke0.

## Covariates
CRRT, CLCR, time since injury (<30 d), sepsis on CL; weight, edema, time since injury on V (Table 2 delta-OFV steps: CRRT -19.0, CLCR -15.5, WT -14.6, EDEM -9.2, TBI to CL -8.5, TBI to V -4.9, SEPS -4.8). Albumin (mean 2.5 g/dL), sex, age, TBSA, APACHE II were not retained.

## Caveats
- Single center, Korean patients, 60 subjects; MIC data not from the study patients, so target attainment was not tied to clinical outcome (authors).
- Bootstrap for several parameters is wide (e.g. theta1 0.116-1.03); mean bootstrap theta9 is printed with an odd interval (-0.0649 to -0.730).
- Authors' comparison: CL and V larger than in non-burn patients and healthy adults (literature, not given as numbers here; Boucher burn patients 1.52 L/h, 46.8 L); comparators are other published studies, not a stanpumpR model.
- The label "L/kg" for theta5 conflicts with the formula (see open questions).
