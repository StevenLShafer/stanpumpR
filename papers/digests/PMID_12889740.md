# Blesch 2003: PK/PD and PBPK modelling in capecitabine development

PDF pages 1-29 = printed pages 195-223 (PDF page = printed page - 194).

## Study
Review of Roche's modelling programme for capecitabine (oral triple prodrug of 5-fluorouracil, 5-FU), drawing on
earlier primary reports. Components:
- **Conceptual simulation** (printed p198, ACSL): 12-week regimen, intermittent (1500 mg bid, 2 wk on/1 wk off) vs
  continuous (1000 mg bid); assumed 100% bioavailability, 30% CV PK variability, Gompertz tumour growth, Emax kill
  (EC50 IIV 150% CV), indirect-response epidermis model (80% CV). 50 simulated subjects.
- **Logistic regression, Phase I** (pp198-200): two Phase I trials, exposure (dose, AUC0-12h and Cmax at day 14 of
  capecitabine, 5'-DFCR, 5'-DFUR, 5-FU, FBAL) vs dose-limiting toxicity.
- **Population PK (NONMEM), Phase III** (pp200-202): structural model built on 2 Phase I entry-into-human studies
  (n = 49) and a bioequivalence study (n = 24); applied to sparse data from 481 colorectal-cancer patients on
  1250 mg/m2 bid (2 wk on/1 wk off), sampled day 1 of cycles 2 and 4. Index (2/3) / validation (1/3) split.
- **Logistic and Cox concentration-effect analysis, Phase III** (pp205-208).
- **PBPK model** (pp208-214; from Tsukamoto 2001, ref 30): 5 compartments (GI, blood, liver, tumour, non-eliminating
  tissue), Michaelis-Menten sequential metabolism with in-vitro Km/Vmax; fitted with Napp. Also a mouse xenograft
  application (pp214-218, ref 42).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Not an opioid or anaesthetic paper; no MEAC, ke0, t½ke0, tPeak or potency ratio.
- Phase I logistic model predicted MTD 3200 mg/m2/day vs clinical MTD 3000 mg/m2/day (p200). 5'-DFUR and FBAL
  AUC/Cmax predicted DLT; 5-FU poorly; capecitabine and 5'-DFCR not.
- Phase III: 5 of 42 exposure-effect relationships significant (p205): FBAL AUC + grade 3-4 diarrhoea; 5-FU AUC +
  hyperbilirubinaemia; FBAL Cmax negatively with hyperbilirubinaemia; 5-FU AUC negatively with time to progression
  (explained by baseline ALP confounding, p208); 5'-DFUR Cmax positively with survival (median 355 d lowest quartile
  vs 423 and 458 d, p206). Authors conclude plasma exposure poorly predicts outcomes.
- PBPK (human, 70 kg, 20 g tumour, 829-1255 mg/m2): tumour:blood 5-FU AUC ratio 11.2-18.6, tumour:GI 5.3-7.7 (p212);
  DPD Km 0.38-0.45 µM; tumour 5-FU Cmax ~40 µM (p214).

## Covariates
Table 2 (PDF p11, printed p205), confirmed on page image: ALP x2 -> CL2 -11% (5-FU AUC +12%); ALP x5 -> CL2 -24%
(AUC +31%); CLCR x0.5 -> CL3 -35% (FBAL AUC +53%); CLCR x0.5 -> V3 -24% (FBAL Cmax +41%); BSA x1.3 -> V3 +24% (FBAL
Cmax -19%). Note: the text (p202) says a 54% increase in FBAL AUC; Table 2 says 53%. No covariate on CL1.

## Caveats
- Review; primary detail in refs 21, 28, 30, 42.
- TLAG typical value ~2 s with ISV/IOV of ~50000 %CV is effectively unidentified; KA unit printed "l/h".
- Linear PK assumed although EIH data showed nonlinear 5-FU kinetics (p201); low concentrations poorly described.
- Cancer patients only; capecitabine parent and 5'-DFCR not modelled in the popPK.
