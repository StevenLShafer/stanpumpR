# Mongardon 2009: predicted propofol Ce at loss of consciousness in early pregnancy

## Study
- Prospective comparison, Hôpital Bichat, Paris, July to December 2005. 120 women enrolled and 112 analysed:
  57 at elective termination of pregnancy (TOP) and 55 non-pregnant controls at transvaginal oocyte retrieval
  for IVF. All ASA I-II; BMI > 30 kg/m2 excluded (PDF p2, printed p91; p3, printed p92).
- Table 1 (PDF p3, printed p92; confirmed on the page image), mean ± SD, TOP vs IVF: age 27 ± 8 vs 32 ± 4 y
  (P < 0.0001); height 166 ± 6 vs 165 ± 5 cm; weight 59 ± 11 vs 61 ± 10 kg; lean body mass 44 ± 5 vs 44 ± 5 kg
  (James formula); fetal age 10.6 ± 1.6 (unit not printed; weeks implied by the Discussion's median of 11 wk);
  blood progesterone 69 ± 3 vs 42 ± 4 nmol/L; anaesthesia 14 ± 4 vs 19 ± 9 min; surgery 7 ± 3 vs 11 ± 5 min;
  time to eye opening 7 ± 3 vs 8 ± 6 min.
- Induction: 1% propofol (Diprivan) by continuous infusion at 200 mL/h (Methods, printed p91), through a Base
  Primea pump (Fresenius-Vial) that displayed the Schnider-model predicted effect-site concentration (Ce). Loss
  of consciousness (LOC) = no reply when asked her name in a normal voice, tested every 15 s, no touch. After LOC
  effect-site TCI was titrated to BIS 45-55. Alfentanil 10 µg/kg just before surgery; IV acetaminophen 1 g; no
  lidocaine. At emergence, eye opening (EO) on request every 15 s.
- The Ce at LOC and at EO was read off the pump; alfentanil Ce at EO was calculated afterwards with pkpdtools
  using the Maitre model. **No propofol concentration was measured**: every Ce is model-predicted. Progesterone by
  chemiluminescent immunoassay (Elecsys) on arrival in recovery.
- Statistics: Student's t-test; sample size of 46 per group to detect a 10% Ce difference (pilot of 21 TOP:
  4.5 ± 0.76 µg/mL).

## Model and parameters
No pharmacokinetic model was fitted. Ce came from the Schnider 1998 model running in the pump; alfentanil Ce from
Maitre 1987.
```yaml
parameters: []  # no PK parameters estimated; all concentrations are Schnider- (propofol) or Maitre- (alfentanil) model predictions
```

- End points: LOC (loss of verbal contact) and EO (eye opening to command). These are mean predicted Ce at
  the moment of each event, not a C50 from a concentration-response fit. No ke0, t½ke0, Emax, gamma or EC50 was
  estimated. The ke0 is whatever the pump's Schnider implementation used, which the paper does not state.
- BIS at LOC 80 ± 8 vs 78 ± 9 (P = 0.20); at EO 71 ± 9 vs 70 ± 10 (P = 0.57) (Results, printed p92).
- Ratio TOP/IVF of Ce at LOC 0.916, i.e. 8% lower (derived: 4.59/5.01); dose ratio 0.923 (derived).
- Progesterone did not correlate with dose or Ce at LOC: r = 0.02 / 0.02 within TOP, r = 0.05 / 0.11 pooled
  (Fig. 1, printed p92).
- Not an opioid paper. Alfentanil enters only as a co-drug, as a predicted Ce at EO.

## Covariates
Pregnancy (first trimester, by group) was the only factor compared. Progesterone was tested by simple regression
and was not associated. The groups also differed in age (27 vs 32 y), which was not adjusted for. Note that age
is itself a Schnider covariate (ke0 and clearances).

## Caveats
- All concentrations are Schnider predictions for non-pregnant adults. The authors accept that pregnancy may make
  the model inaccurate, so a lower predicted Ce could be kinetic (cardiac output, ke0) or a true change in
  sensitivity. The design cannot tell these apart (Discussion, printed p93). The measured dose and time to LOC
  both fell, which supports a real difference.
- Not blinded: the analyst (JG) knew the pregnancy state.
- Internal inconsistencies in the paper:
  - The abstract says 200 mL/min and the Methods say 200 mL/h. The Methods are consistent with the data: 1% at
    200 mL/h is 33.3 mg/min, against 108.57 mg in 193.8 s = 33.6 mg/min (derived).
  - Exclusions are given as "two" plus "eight", which makes ten, against 120 - 112 = 8.
  - The Discussion places 4.59 µg/mL in "our IVF control group", when Table 2 gives 4.59 for TOP and 5.01 for IVF.
  - The Results text prints the IVF SD at EO as 0.3, where Table 2 has 0.36.
  None of these touches a number the repo uses.
- The EO comparison was underpowered: by the authors' own estimate it needed 125 per group. Alfentanil Ce at EO
  was higher in TOP, owing to shorter surgery.
- Early pregnancy only (about 11 weeks). It says nothing about term.
