# Creinin 1997: methotrexate PK after IM 50 and 60 mg/m2 in early pregnancy

## Study
Randomized (sealed envelopes, unblinded) trial, 20 healthy women requesting abortion at <=49 days' gestation, BSA 1.60-1.90 m2, no folate, normal blood counts, AST, creatinine <=1.5 mg/dL. Group 1 (n=10) methotrexate 50 mg/m2 IM; group 2 (n=10) 60 mg/m2 IM. Blood hourly for 8 h, every 2 h to 24 h, then daily to day 9. Methotrexate by fluorescence polarization immunoassay (TDx Flx), minimal detectable 0.02 umol/L (serum; total drug). Misoprostol 800 ug vaginal on day 8. AUC by linear trapezoid, 0-24 h (levels reached zero, so no extrapolation); clearance = dose / AUC. No compartmental model, no software PK fit.
Table I (PDF p. 3, printed p. 1446; mean +/- SD): age 24.1 +/- 5.6 and 26.3 +/- 6.0 y; BSA 1.73 +/- 0.10 and 1.72 +/- 0.11 m2; gestation 44.0 +/- 3.9 and 38.7 +/- 4.4 d (group 1, group 2). Weight not given. Approximate doses given in the Discussion: 86 to 103 mg.

Concentrations above 5.0 umol/L: 2 patients (group 1), 9 (group 2); all below 5.0 by 4 h. Undetectable (<0.02 umol/L): group 1 by 18-24 h in 4 patients and 48 h in 6; group 2 by 48 h in 9 and 72 h in 1. Fig. 1 (PDF p. 5) shows mean levels to 24 h. No volume, half-life, ka or bioavailability reported.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD model. The only concentration benchmark is 5.0 umol/L at 24 h as a toxicity threshold after high-dose therapy (cited from ref 1, not measured here); neither dose exceeded it at 24 h. Clinical end point: complete abortion without surgery 9/10 (group 1) and 10/10 (group 2), p=0.99. Not an opioid paper: no MEAC, ke0, tPeak or potency ratio.

## Covariates
None analysed. BSA window 1.60-1.90 m2; authors say smaller or larger women may differ. Dose scaled by BSA, so absolute mg differs between women.

## Caveats
n=10 per group; sampling starts at 1 h so Cmax and Tmax are coarse; healthy early-pregnant women; no nonpregnant control in the study; the lower clearance at 60 than at 50 mg/m2 is unexplained (authors suggest dose-dependent clearance or an effect on filtration). Individual clearances were not normal; the group difference was re-tested after log transform.
