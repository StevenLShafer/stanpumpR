# Lim 2016: topiramate concentration and Symbol-Digit Modalities Test

## Study
Sequential PK-PD analysis pooling three randomised crossover studies in healthy native-English speakers
(Table 1): 38 subjects (24 M / 16 F), median weight 77.6 kg (52.2-112.3), median age 26.5 y (19-55). Study I
(UMN, n = 10 with SDMT): 100 mg IV (15 min) and oral crossover, rich sampling to 120 h, SDMT at 0.25, 2 and 6 h.
Study II (UF, n = 9) 100 mg oral vs placebo; Study III (UMN, n = 11 at 100 mg, n = 8 at 200 mg) oral vs placebo
(and lorazepam); one blood sample right after the SDMT at about 1-1.5 h. LC-MS (LLOQ 0.04 ug/mL Study I; 0.375
ug/mL Studies II-III). NONMEM 7.2. The PK model is the published Ahmed 2015 model (ref 16); individual empirical
Bayes PK estimates drove the PD fit. 189 SDMT scores.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: Symbol-Digit Modalities Test (working memory, attention, psychomotor speed), direct inhibitory Emax on
predicted plasma concentration (eq 5), no effect compartment, no ke0. At EC50 2.85 ug/mL SDMT falls 25.5%.
Discussion (p 720-721): typical peak after 100 mg is 2 ug/mL, predicting a 21% fall; for Meador 2005's average
steady-state concentration of 9.3 ug/mL on 300 mg/day the model predicts a 39% fall vs 25.4% observed (possible
tolerance). Emax model AIC 1114.8 vs exponential 1123.4 and linear 1118.8. Not an opioid paper.

## Covariates
Age on baseline SDMT (-1.13% per year, explaining 24% of IIV in E0); practice effect after more than one prior
test (+7%). Sex, race and study not significant.

## Caveats
Single doses (100-200 mg), narrow concentration range; IIV on Emax and EC50 not estimable; EC50 CI wide
(0.43-5.26); healthy young adults; extrapolation to chronic therapy uncertain.
