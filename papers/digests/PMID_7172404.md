# Breithaupt 1982: dacarbazine (DTIC) and AIC pharmacokinetics

## Study
Eight adults with malignant melanoma (pts 1-4) or sarcoma (pts 5-8), normal haematology/chemistry (Table 1, PDF p. 2). Ages 22-57 y, weights 55-92 kg, 6 M / 2 F (pt 2 and pt 6 female). DTIC 2.65-6.85 mg/kg (225-600 mg) daily for 5 days, as IV bolus (pts 1-4, 7, 8) or continuous infusions of 0.5-6 h (pts 5-8); courses repeated every 4 weeks. Pts 3 and 4 had a cycle preceded by BCG immunotherapy (excluded from the means). Blood sampled 0-6 h after bolus; urine at 4-h intervals. Plasma assayed directly by paired-ion HPLC; limit of sensitivity 60 ng/mL, linear 0.06-600 ug/mL, intra-assay precision < 7% (PDF p. 2). Half-lives by Gauss-Newton nonlinear fit; CL = dose/AUC(0-inf); Vss = CL x mean transit time T (noncompartmental). Also isolated dog liver and human extremity perfusion (not relevant to systemic PK).

## Model and parameters
Biphasic decline "compatible with a two-compartment model" (Discussion); no compartmental micro-constants are printed. Table 2 (PDF p. 3; per-patient mean of 5 daily bolus courses, units per kg body weight):

Other facts: fast phase is about 28 +/- 7% (SE) of AUC (Results), 21-35% (Discussion). Peak plasma 10-30 ug/mL after bolus (Fig. 1). Day-to-day within-patient SD < 12%. Renal CL of DTIC 5.2-10.9 mL/kg/min, about 50% extrarenal; urinary recovery DTIC 46-52%, AIC 9-18% (Table 4, n = 3). AIC: peak up to 5 ug/mL at 15 min, monoexponential, t1/2 43-116 min (Table 3); AIC volume terms not meaningful (fraction converted not determined). Infusions of 0.5-6 h gave DTIC AUC comparable to bolus (96-127%, Table 5); Cmax 0.66-6.2 ug/mL; steady state reached by 4 h. Linear kinetics inferred; no saturation.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No PD end point; the paper states the minimal therapeutic plasma concentration of DTIC is unknown (Discussion). No MEAC, ke0, tPeak or potency ratio. Not an opioid.

## Covariates
None analysed. BCG pretreatment: no significant change in DTIC PK (paired t-test, 5% level; Table 2 footnote a). Dose schedule (bolus vs 0.5-6 h infusion): AUC comparable (Table 5).

## Caveats
Six non-BCG patients (n = 6 for the model); heterogeneous cancer patients, mixed prior therapy, 22-57 y. Dose-normalised to total body weight only. Vss is noncompartmental; no two-compartment fit was reported, so any V1/V2/Q is derived by a third party. SEs are across the 5 daily courses within one patient, not across patients. Pt 7's Vc 0.281 has no SE printed.
