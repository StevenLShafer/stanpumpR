# Huang 1986: ketoconazole pharmacokinetics and dose proportionality

## Study
Two-phase study in healthy, nonsmoking male college students at Rutgers (PDF p. 1, printed p. 206).
- Phase 1: 24 men (18-25 y, mean 20; 61.2-95.3 kg, mean 76.4; 167.6-188 cm, mean 180) in a randomized 3-way crossover, 1-week washout. 200 mg ketoconazole as tablet, suspension (20 mg/mL) or aqueous solution (20 mg/mL), each with 200 mL water, after an overnight fast. 23 completed (one withdrew after one treatment, unrelated to drug; excluded).
- Phase 2: 12 of the phase-1 men received 400 mg/20 mL and 800 mg/40 mL solution in randomized crossover; the 200 mg solution value from phase 1 is the third dose level.
- Sampling: 10 mL heparinized venous blood before and at 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12, 24, 36, 48 h. Plasma frozen.
- Assay: reverse-phase HPLC, UV 254 nm, terconazole internal standard; heptane-isoamyl alcohol extraction. Lower limit of detection 2 ng/mL (30% CV); CV 9.8% at 10 ng/mL. Total plasma concentration (no free fraction measured).
- Analysis: non-compartmental. kel from terminal log-linear regression, t1/2 = 0.693/kel, AUC by linear trapezoid plus extrapolation, CL/F = dose/AUC, V/F = (CL/F)/kel. No compartmental model fitted.

## Model and parameters
No structural model. Observed distribution is biphasic (33 of 69 profiles multiexponential): initial t1/2 1.7 (±0.6), 1.5 (±0.4), 1.6 (±0.5) h for tablet, suspension, solution, over first 8-12 h; terminal t1/2 below (PDF p. 3).

Dose proportionality, solution, n = 12 (Table 3 and text, p. 208): Cmax 5.38 (±1.67), 11.77 (±2.35), 21.83 (±2.84) ug/mL at 200, 400, 800 mg; Tmax 0.92 (±0.29), 1.08 (±0.15), 2.04 (±0.66) h. ## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No effect measured; no ke0, MEAC, EC50. Not an opioid paper. Introduction notes a 200 mg tablet gives Cmax 3-4.5 ug/mL, "therapeutic activity" for several hours (quoted from reference, no threshold given); 99% plasma protein binding at 1 ug/mL (in vitro, cited reference 7, not measured here).

## Covariates
None modelled. Subject weight, age given only as ranges. Single sex (male). Fasted; the authors suggest the greater tablet variability reflects gastric-pH-dependent dissolution (pH < 3).

## Caveats
- Healthy young men, fasted; no IV reference so CL and V are apparent; V/F has an SD of 78% of the mean.
- Dose-dependent kinetics mean a linear PK model fitted at 200 mg would mis-predict 400-800 mg.
- Internal inconsistencies in printing: relative F tablet/suspension are 81.2/89.0 (±33.5/±23.1) in the abstract, 81.0/89.0 (±34.0/±23.0) in the text, 0.81/0.89 in Table 2; CL/F at 800 mg 80.0 (abstract) vs 80.8 (text); mean CL/F in the abstract is 209 vs 209.9 in the text.
- The 200 mg CL/F in the phase-2 12 men (244.9) differs from the 23-man value (209.9): different subsets.
