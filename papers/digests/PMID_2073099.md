# Caron 1990: ceftriaxone-sulbactam against a TEM-3 K. pneumoniae in rabbit endocarditis

## Study
In vitro susceptibility plus a rabbit aortic-valve endocarditis model (Perlman-Freedman catheter technique).
- Organisms: K. pneumoniae CF104, a blood isolate carrying plasmid pCF104 that encodes TEM-3 (formerly CTX-1),
  and a spontaneous susceptible variant that had lost the plasmid; both make low-level chromosomal SHV-1.
- MIC/MBC by tube macrodilution in cation-supplemented Mueller-Hinton broth, log-phase cultures at final inocula
  of 5 x 10^5 and 1 x 10^7 CFU/mL; MIC = no visible turbidity at 24 h, 37 °C; MBC = lowest concentration giving a
  99.9% kill on subculture of 0.01 mL (PDF p1-2, printed pp2070-2071).
- Sulbactam PK: 4 non-infected female New Zealand White rabbits, 200 mg/kg i.m. single dose, serum at 0.25-12 h;
  HPLC (Rogers et al. method), limit 0.1 µg/mL. Vegetation sulbactam in 8 infected rabbits, 1 h (n = 2) or 12 h
  (n = 6) after the 8th BID dose.
- Ceftriaxone PK not measured here: taken from the group's earlier rabbit studies (refs 12, 17).
- In vivo: 92 rabbits randomised to 8 groups; ~10^9 CFU i.v. on day 2; treatment days 5-8 (4 days); killed on
  day 9 at trough. Regimens: ceftriaxone 30 mg/kg i.m. once daily; sulbactam 200 mg/kg i.m. BID or every 6 h; and
  the combinations.
- Ex vivo beta-lactamase activity of 18 vegetations from 6 untreated rabbits (nitrocefin 100 µM, 30 °C).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
End point: log10 CFU/g of vegetation and sterile vegetations after 4 days (Table 3, PDF p3, rendered):
resistant strain control 7.0 ± 1.8 (0/11 sterile); ceftriaxone OD 6.9 ± 1.8 (0/9); sulbactam BID 6.6 ± 2.2 (1/9);
sulbactam QID 4.1 ± 1.7* (3/6); ceftriaxone OD + sulbactam BID 5.4 ± 2.3 (2/9); ceftriaxone + sulbactam QID
4.1 ± 2.0* (2/5). Susceptible strain: control 8.1 ± 1.6 (0/7); ceftriaxone OD 4.9 ± 2.3* (2/8). (* P < 0.01 vs
control.) The authors cite a peak vegetation concentration/MBC ratio above 100 as needed for cephalosporin kill
in E. coli endocarditis (refs 12, 17); here the ceftriaxone vegetation/MBC ratio for the TEM-3 strain was 0.22.
No ke0, EC50 or MEAC (not an analgesic paper).

## Covariates
None.

## Caveats
- One isolate, rabbits; ceftriaxone PK borrowed from earlier studies, not measured.
- Table 3's last row reads "Ceftriaxone (30) BID + sulbactam (200) QID", whereas Methods (p2) describe ceftriaxone
  once daily in that group; a misprint in one place (not used by stanpumpR).
- MBC 128 mg/L is for one TEM-3 strain at standard inoculum; at 10^7 CFU/mL the MBC exceeded 1,000 mg/L.
