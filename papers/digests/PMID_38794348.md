# Pejcic 2024: clopidogrel and CLO-CA joint semi-physiological PopPK

## Study
Two 2-way crossover bioequivalence studies (generic 75 mg tablet vs Plavix), single 150 mg dose (2 tablets), fasting, healthy Caucasian adults: Study 1 n = 24 (21-53 y, BMI 19-26), Study 2 n = 26 (19-42 y, BMI 19-29) (PDF p3). Pooled n = 50: 29 M / 21 F, age 31.94 ± 8.51 y, weight 74.1 ± 13.56 kg (47-100) (Table 1, text layer). Sampling to 48 h (14 samples/period) and 36 h (17/period). Plasma, HPLC-MS, LLOQ 0.5 ng/mL (CLO) and 0.1 µg/mL (CLO-CA); BLLOQ data excluded. 841 CLO and 1149 CLO-CA concentrations. NONMEM 7.5 (p3). Mean Cmax 7.2 ng/mL (CLO) and 4.9 µg/mL (CLO-CA) (p4).

## Model and parameters
CLO: one-compartment, absorption via two transit compartments (Erlang), liver compartment (Vh 1.5 L, Qh 50 L/h fixed, taken from Jiang) through which all parent drug is metabolised; CLO-CA: two-compartment. Fraction to active thiol (FaM) fixed at 12% (sensitivity at 10% and 15% changed OFV by <2). CLO-CA formation scaled by 0.9565 (molecular weights 321.82 / 307.80). Allometry: CL exponent 0.75, V exponent 1 (per 70 kg). IIV log-normal; IOV on F and MTT; study-specific proportional error (pp3-4).

None: no platelet response or active-metabolite (CLO-TH) concentrations modelled. No ke0, EC50 or MEAC. Not an opioid paper.

Allometric weight only (no stepwise covariate search; homogeneous healthy volunteers). No CYP2C19 or CES1 genotype data.

## Caveats
