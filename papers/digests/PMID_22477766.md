# Schipani 2012: SLCO1B1 521T>C and lopinavir population PK

## Study
- Retrospective population PK from the Liverpool Therapeutic Drug Monitoring Registry (PDF p2, printed 862).
- n = 375 HIV-positive adults on lopinavir/ritonavir 400/100 mg tablet-based regimens (the dosing interval of the
  cohort is not stated in Methods; the simulations and VPC use 400/100 mg twice daily). 82% male; median age 40 y
  (range 19-66); median weight 72 kg (range 45-117). All had viral load <50 copies/ml. Excluded: pregnancy,
  undetectable lopinavir (non-adherence), enzyme inducers. No ethnicity data (a stated limitation).
- 594 plasma samples at random times after dose (sparse; few in the absorption phase). Concentrations 0.114 to
  22.432 mg/l (PDF p3).
- Assay: validated HPLC-MS/MS on heat-inactivated plasma (58 °C, 40 min); LLOQ 95 ng/ml; total plasma lopinavir.
- Genotype SLCO1B1 521T>C (rs4149056) by real-time qPCR: 295 TT (78%), 73 CT (20%), 7 CC (2%); minor allele 11%.
- NONMEM VI, FOCE with interaction; PsN 5.1 bootstrap (200 replicates); VPC (1,000 sets); external validation in
  42 samples from 6 patients (bias 2.1%, 95% CI -1.1 to 5.3).

## Model and parameters
One-compartment, first-order absorption and elimination, no lag time; IIV on CL/F only (exponential);
proportional residual error. Table 1 (PDF p4, printed 864), final model, estimate (RSE%):

Basic (no covariate) model, Table 1: CL/F 5.29 l/h, V/F 42.4 l, ka 0.18 h-1, IIV 40%, residual 32.2%.

Derived (not stated as such in the paper): k = CL/V = 5.67/45.5 = 0.125 h-1, t½ ≈ 5.6 h (derived); 521TC and
521CC lower CL/F by 0.791/5.67 = 14% and 2.09/5.67 = 37% (derived; the paper's Discussion states 14% and 37%).
Weight: +0.457 l/h per 10 kg = 8.1% of 5.67 (derived); the Discussion says 8.8% per 10 kg, which matches the
rounded "0.5 l/h per 10 kg" of the Results, not the Table 1 estimate.

Comparators: the 14% and 37% decreases are relative to 521TT patients in this same cohort. The Discussion
compares them with Lubomirov 2010 (14% for TC, 21% for CC) and Kohlrausch 2010 (+49% Ctrough in CC; this study's
simulation +57% vs TT).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD model. A minimum effective concentration (MEC) for lopinavir of 1 mg/l in treatment-naive patients is
taken from the literature (the authors call it "alleged"/"proposed") and used as a trough threshold in
simulations (Figures 3-4, PDF p5-6). Total plasma concentration.
Simulated results (using an external sequential lopinavir-ritonavir model, reference 25, with an Imax ritonavir
inhibition of lopinavir CL/F, plus this study's genotype effects):
- 400/100 mg twice daily, steady-state Ctrough median (P5-P95): TT 5.0 (1.1-13.2), TC 5.11 (1.4-15.6),
  CC 7.4 (2.0-22.1) mg/l (PDF p4).
- 400/100 mg with the evening dose missed (24 h trough): CC mean 1.23 mg/l (95% CI 1.16, 1.29), 40% below MEC;
  TC 0.43 mg/l (0.39, 0.46), 83% below MEC.
- 200/50 mg twice daily: CC mean Ctrough 2.36 mg/l (2.32, 2.41), 2% below MEC; TC 1.49 (1.46, 1.53), 18% below.
Not an opioid paper: no MEAC, ke0 or potency ratio.

## Covariates
Body weight (additive, per kg about median) and SLCO1B1 521T>C genotype (additive l/h shifts) on CL/F. Age and
sex entered in forward selection but were dropped at backward elimination. Weight: ΔOFV 20.5; genotype: ΔOFV 15.

## Caveats
- Sparse TDM data with selection bias; few absorption samples, so ka and V/F are poorly informed (the bootstrap
  CI for V/F spans 16.7-74.3 l) and V/F was "slightly underestimated" per the authors.
- Only 7 CC homozygotes; no ethnicity data; ritonavir concentrations were not measured, so all ritonavir effects
  come from an external healthy-volunteer model.
- Apparent (oral) parameters only; bioavailability unknown. Typical values apply under ritonavir boosting.
- Text-versus-table inconsistencies on basic-model values and the weight percentage (see open questions).
- The Table 1 footnote on RSE of the IIV term is garbled ("coefficient of validation").
