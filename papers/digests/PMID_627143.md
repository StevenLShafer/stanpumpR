# Myers 1978: Gentamicin binding to serum and plasma proteins

## Study
In vitro equilibrium dialysis (cellulose tubing, 2 mL serum or heparinised plasma vs 10 mL buffer, 0.05 M tris in 0.15 M NaCl, pH 7.4, 37 C, 15 h; equilibration 6 h). 14C-gentamicin (Schering), initially 6 ug/mL in the buffer (one experiment 6, 12, 18 ug/mL); liquid scintillation counting. Sera from 6 healthy volunteers and 3 uraemic haemodialysis patients (creatinine 9.6-13.4 mg/dL). Buffer Ca/Mg: none; physiological (2.5 mM Ca, 1.0 mM Mg); about four times physiological (9.5 mM Ca, 3.8 mM Mg). Heparin 0-72 USP units/mL. Binding to tubing about 3% (in absence of Ca/Mg), to glassware 1%.

Binding independent of free concentration 6-18 ug/mL; rose with heparin from 19.1% to 55.2% at 72 USP U/mL (Fig. 2).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. The authors judge that 20% binding does not alter glomerular filtration as the main route, and raise heparin as a confounder in plasma samples.

## Covariates
Divalent cations (inverse relation), heparin (direct), uraemia (no effect at a given Ca/Mg).

## Caveats
Small n (Fig. 1 uses 2 normal, 3 uraemic); radiolabel purity > 98% but no separation of degradation products; equilibrium dialysis volume shift ~5%. Contradicts Gordon 1972 (ultrafiltration, zero) and Ramirez-Ronda 1975 (zero at physiological cations).
