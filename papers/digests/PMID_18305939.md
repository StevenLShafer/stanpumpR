# Tham 2008: first-order formation of gemcitabine triphosphate; gemcitabine-dFdU-dFdCTP population model

## Study
- Prospective PK substudy of a randomised phase II trial (NCT00212043) in Asian adults with stage IIIB/IV
  non-small-cell lung cancer (p2, printed 56). Gemcitabine 750 mg/m2 over 75 min (10 mg/m2/min, n = 28) or
  1,000 mg/m2 over 30 min (n = 28), days 1 and 8 every 3 weeks; carboplatin (target AUC 5 min·mg/mL) as a 1-h
  infusion just before gemcitabine on day 1. Dose basis (base vs hydrochloride) not stated.
- Table 1 (p5, printed 59, page image): n = 56; age 58.2 ± 9.7 y (range 39-74, p8); 39 male / 17 female;
  45 Chinese, 10 Malay, 1 other; weight 58.1 ± 11.9 kg; height 1.61 ± 0.08 m; CLcr 74.7 ± 18.9 mL/min;
  stage IV 47, IIIB 9; KPS 90-100% in 45.
- Sampling, day 1 of cycle 1 (later cycle if missed): baseline, 10 and 30 min into the infusion, 10 min before
  its end, then 30, 60, 120 min after. Venous blood into heparinised tubes preloaded with tetrahydrouridine
  (cytidine deaminase inhibitor). Plasma gemcitabine and dFdU by isocratic ion-pair reversed-phase HPLC;
  dFdCTP in peripheral mononuclear cells by anion-exchange HPLC (LOD 0.2 µM, linear 0.4-10 µM), corrected for
  cell count and mean cell volume.
- Data: 327 gemcitabine and 328 dFdU plasma concentrations (56 patients), 180 intracellular dFdCTP (33 patients).
- NONMEM VI, FOCE with interaction; sequential (PPP&D) then simultaneous fit; 1,000-sample nonparametric
  bootstrap (medians and 5th-95th percentiles); VPC from 150 simulated datasets.

## Model and parameters
Figure 1b: gemcitabine two compartments (V1G, V2G, Q). Gemcitabine clearance split by fixed fractions from the
Abbruzzese 1991 mass balance: 23% to intracellular dFdCTP (F2T), 4% unchanged (F2O), 73% to dFdU (F2U);
CLG = CLG2T/F2T. dFdU one compartment (VU, CLU2O); dFdCTP one intracellular compartment (VT, CLT2O).
Size: standardised normal fat weight (SNFWT, Eqs. 1-5) with fat fraction FFAT estimated at 0, so the size
descriptor reduces to lean body mass relative to a 70-kg, 1.76-m standard; it is used "in an allometric model"
citing Holford 1996, but no exponents are printed (p4). No other covariate was retained.

BSV column and the dFdCTP/dFdU rows by one line; the values below are from the page image.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD model, no effect site, no ke0, no target concentration. The question is formation kinetics: the
Michaelis-Menten model on a reduced 10-patient dataset (60 gemcitabine, 60 dFdU, 40 dFdCTP observations) did
not lower the objective function below the first-order model; Km (263 µM) is about 3.6 times the highest
gemcitabine concentration measured (72.2 µM), so formation of dFdCTP is effectively linear at clinical doses.
Not an opioid paper: no MEAC, ke0 or potency ratio.

## Covariates
SNFWT (lean body mass, FFAT = 0) on clearances and volumes, preferred over total-weight allometry
(ΔOBJ −25.68, df 4) and over no size model (ΔOBJ −15.7, df 1). Age, sex, KPS, stage, CLcr, race and smoking
tested, none retained. Eq. 1 as printed in this file is LBM = LBMMAX × HT² × WT/(WT50 + WT × HT²), HT stated
"in cm" while the standard uses 1.76 m; LBMMAX and WT50 are not given numerically.

## Caveats
- The 23/4/73% split of clearance is assumed from another study's mass balance (Abbruzzese 1991), not
  identified here; so CLG and CLG2U are scalings of the fitted CLG2T.
- V1G is poorly identified (90% CI 0.20-7.8 L, BSV 2.4). Sampling only to 2 h after the infusion, so dFdU
  (t½ ~1.7 h, derived) is followed for barely one half-life.
- Carboplatin co-administered; its effect cannot be separated. Mononuclear cells may not represent tumour cells.
- Michaelis-Menten test on 10 patients only. Asian population, mean weight 58 kg.
- Equations 1 and 3 as printed are erroneous (a later erratum exists, not in this file).
- Fig. 2 x-axis is labelled "Time (min)" with ticks 0-3; it must be hours (text and Fig. 3 use hours).
