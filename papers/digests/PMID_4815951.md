# Runkel 1974: less-than-proportional naproxen exposure at high doses, from saturable albumin binding

## Study
Syntex. Healthy men.
- Experiment 1: 24 men, 23-47 y, 68-89 kg, 6 groups of 4 at 125, 250, 375, 500, 625, 750 mg naproxen capsules:
  single dose day 1, same dose bid days 2-4, single dose day 5. Plasma 0-24 h on days 1 and 5, troughs days 2-4.
- Experiment 2: 6 men, 2 groups of 3, 250 or 900 mg with 20 uCi 3H-naproxen on days 1 and 5, unlabeled bid days
  2-4; plasma, complete urine and stools.
- Assay: GLC (methyl ester, FID); radioactivity counting; binding by ultrafiltration (whole plasma, 21 C).
- Model-independent: 24 h trapezoidal AUC; t1/2 from 8-24 h slope; renal clearance = UV/P (total or unbound P).

## Model and parameters
No compartmental model.
- Dose-AUC (Fig 2): day-1 24 h AUC linear from the origin up to 500 mg, slope 1.9 area units per mg; deviation
  above 500 mg, with minimal increment from 750 to 900 mg. Day-5 curve roughly parallel and higher.
- Steady state (Fig 1, text): the 72 h level at 250 mg bid is twice that at 125 mg bid; proportionality lost at
  375 mg bid and above; 500, 625, 750 mg bid approach an asymptote. Plateau reached by ~72 h of bid dosing.
- Earlier tolerance study (Introduction): 12 h levels on 600 mg tid only 25% higher than on 250 mg tid.
- Table I, t1/2 (h, mean +/- SD), day 1 / day 5: 125 mg 10.3 +/- 7.5 / 14.0 +/- 1.8; 250 mg 13.0 +/- 2.6 /
  13.3 +/- 2.0; 375 mg 15.0 +/- 1.2 / 15.0 +/- 1.7; 500 mg 12.5 +/- 1.0 / 13.5 +/- 1.0; remaining rows (625, 750,
  900 mg) give 14.3-14.5 h day 1 and 13.0-16.5 h day 5 (row assignment ambiguous in the text layer). t1/2
  independent of dose and of 4 days' dosing. Slopes steeper 4-8 h after the peak than 8-24 h.
- Table II, in vitro binding (n = 5 plasmas): % free 0.37 at 23.0 ug/mL total, 0.47 at 48.8, 0.69 at 94.8, 0.95
  at 150.2, 1.16 at 194.4, 1.42 at 273.1, 1.94 at 387.6, 2.67 at 473.5 (text says 2.4% at 473).
- Table III: 96 h 3H recovery urine 87% / feces 2.3% (250 mg) and 100% / 1.4% (900 mg): absorption complete.
- Renal excretion rate of radioactivity vs total plasma concentration is parabolic (rate = b Cp + c Cp^2,
  Fig 3); against unbound concentration it is a straight line through the origin (Fig 4): clearance relative to
  unbound drug constant.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. Authors suggest little advantage in exceeding 500 mg bid.

## Covariates
None.

## Caveats
Groups of 3-4; renal clearance based on total radioactivity (naproxen equivalents, mostly conjugates), not
parent drug; binding at 21 C, not 37 C; t1/2 from only 8 and 24 h points.

## Verification against stanpumpR
- Mechanism (unbound fraction rises with concentration; total clearance rises; total exposure less than
  dose-proportional; clearance constant with respect to unbound drug): supported by Table II, Fig 2 and Fig 3-4.
  **matches**, with the caveat that Runkel's "constant clearance" is renal clearance of total radioactivity
  related to unbound naproxen, not a measured unbound clearance of the parent drug.
- Threshold "above about 500 mg a day" (R/drugs_naproxen.R:62-63; inst/help/drugs/naproxen.md:25): Runkel puts
  the break at single doses above 500 mg and, at steady state, at 375 mg bid and above (750 mg/day or more), with
  linearity "below 500 mg twice a day". The repo's per-day threshold is lower than Runkel's (it may come from
  Davies 1997, not checked here). **discrepancy (minor)**: if Runkel is the source, it should read about 500 mg a
  dose, or 750-1000 mg a day.
