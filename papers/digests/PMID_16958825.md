# Yun 2006: glimepiride PK-PD (insulin and glucose) in healthy volunteers

## Study
- 6 healthy Korean men, mean age 25 y (22-25), mean weight 69.67 kg (55-83) (PDF p. 2, printed p. 470).
- Crossover: single oral glimepiride 2 mg (Amaryl tablet) in period 1, placebo in period 2, 6-day washout.
  Fasted ≥ 10 h; 12 g sugar cubes immediately after the dose; standardised meal at 4 h.
- Plasma sampled at 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2, 2.5, 3, 4, 5, 7 and 9 h. Glimepiride by LC/MS/MS (glibenclamide internal
  standard; LLOQ not stated); glucose by hexokinase; insulin by RIA.
- Sequential PK then PD, individual maximum-likelihood fits in ADAPT II; mean ± SEM reported.

## Model and parameters
Two-compartment model with absorption lag, Michaelis-Menten absorption from the gut (Vmax12, Km12) and
Michaelis-Menten elimination from the central compartment (Vmax2e, Km2e), first-order K23/K32 (Eqs 1-3, Fig. 1,
PDF p. 3). Km and Vmax are expressed in amounts (ng, ng/h). **No central volume (V2) is reported**, although
Eq. 7 uses x2/V2.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
- Insulin: indirect response, glimepiride stimulating Kin by a sigmoid function without Hill factor,
  S1 = 1 + Smax·C/(SC50 + C) (Eqs 4, 7, 9). End point: plasma insulin above placebo (mIU/L).
- Glucose: "glucose disappearance" model with liver (5) and glucose (6) compartments, insulin stimulating
  K56 and elimination through S2 = 1 + x4 (Eqs 5, 6, 8). End point: plasma glucose below placebo (mg/dL).
- Table 2 (PDF p. 6, printed p. 474; mean ± SEM, n = 6): Kin 4.05 ± 1.01 (unit printed "mIU h/L"),
  R0 4.54 ± 1.08 mIU/L, SC50 78.89 ± 33.66 ng/mL, Smax 21.17 ± 10.20, Kel 0.012 ± 0.005 /h,
  K56 0.007 ± 0.002 /h, K65 0.37 ± 0.20 /h; Kout = Kin/R0 = 0.89 /h (secondary).
- Observed effects (p. 5): maximum insulin rise 9.98 mIU/L over placebo, significant 1-4 h; glucose fall maximal
  about 2 h (21.00 mg/dL, results text; the abstract gives 19.33 mg/dL and 2.5 h), significant 1.5-5 h.
- No ke0, no therapeutic or toxic concentration range. Not an opioid paper.

## Covariates
None (6 subjects).

## Caveats
- n = 6 healthy young men; 9 h sampling, so the terminal half-life (2.55 h) may reflect only the early phase
  (the paper does not discuss this).
- Internal inconsistencies: abstract says Cmax at "approximately 4 h" but Table 1 Tmax is 2.58 h; abstract glucose
  maximum 19.33 vs 21.00 mg/dL in Results; Kin unit "mIU h/L" should be a rate (mIU/L/h) for Kout = Kin/R0 to be /h.
- The compartmental PK cannot be reconstructed: no V2 is given, and Vmax2e 0.14 ng/h at Km2e 0.006 ng would
  eliminate at most 0.14 ng/h from a 2 mg dose, which cannot reproduce CL/F 2.26 L/h. The table's MM elimination
  parameters therefore look mis-scaled or mis-labelled.
- Sugar load and 4 h meal confound the glucose and insulin profiles (handled by placebo subtraction).
- Funded by a generic manufacturer (SAM-NAM Pharm).
