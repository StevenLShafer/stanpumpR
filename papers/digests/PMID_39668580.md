# Khwarg 2024: SLC22A1 1022C>T and proguanil PK in Koreans

## Study
Retrospective genotyping plus post hoc population PK analysis of a CYP2C19-mediated DDI study (NCT04568772, Yang 2023). 16 healthy Korean CYP2C19 *1/*1 normal metabolizers (19-50 y, BMI 19.0-30.0); 13 consented to SLC22A1 genotyping (all male; age 34.31 ± 7.38 y, height 172.85 ± 7.37 cm, weight 76.12 ± 9.82 kg, BMI 25.52 ± 3.14). Single oral dose atovaquone/proguanil 250/100 mg, alone or with tegoprazan 50 mg (esomeprazole and vonoprazan arms excluded). Plasma proguanil and cycloguanil; 160 and 159 observations from the 16 subjects. Six SNPs typed (TaqMan); only 1022C>T (rs2282143) varied: 9 CC, 4 CT (Table 1, PDF p. 5). Three subjects with missing genotype assigned by mixture model (fixed 0.7 CC / 0.3 CT). NONMEM 7.5 FOCE-I, PsN SCM, 1000 bootstraps; VPC.

## Model and parameters
Proguanil: 2-compartment, first-order absorption into a well-stirred liver compartment; hepatic extraction EH = fub*CLint*FUP^GENO / (QBH + fub*CLint*FUP^GENO); CLBH = EH*QBH; fub = fu/(CB/CP) with fu 0.25 and CB/CP 2.7 fixed; liver volume 1 L and QBH 90 L/h scaled to weight (VH linear, QBH ^0.75, 70 kg reference); QH = QBH*(1 - 0.45). Cycloguanil: 1-compartment with 2 transit compartments for efflux from liver; Vc equal to proguanil Vc (molecular-weight conversion 290.19/288.17). Observed (Table 2, PDF p. 6, mean ± SD, total of both treatments): proguanil CC AUClast 1205.29 ± 359.36 h*ug/L, Cmax 78.97 ± 14.94 ug/L, t1/2 15.90 ± 2.07 h, Tmax median 3.00 h; CT 1502.00 ± 399.4, 93.34 ± 19.81, 18.07 ± 3.96 h, 2.50 h. Cycloguanil CC AUClast 445.08 ± 80.22, Cmax 29.17 ± 6.75, t1/2 11.24 ± 1.28 h, Tmax 6.00 h; CT 279.48 ± 156.75, 17.48 ± 10.69, 13.02 ± 1.88 h. Metabolic ratio (cycloguanil/proguanil AUClast) CC 0.38 ± 0.13, CT 0.21 ± 0.19. Ratios CT/CC: proguanil about 1.2x, cycloguanil about 0.6x (stated in text). No formal statistics (n = 4 CT).

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
None. No effect model, no MEAC, no ke0. Not an opioid.

## Covariates
SCM tested weight, eGFR on CL/F; AST, ALT, weight on CLint; weight on Vc: none retained (p. 6). Genotype acts only through FUP on hepatic extraction.

## Caveats
Retrospective, 13 genotyped subjects, 4 with CT, all healthy males, CYP2C19 normal metabolizers only, FUP RSE 54%. Fixed fu and CB/CP taken from literature. Cycloguanil efflux structure is empirical. Combined data from two treatment arms (tegoprazan did not change exposure markedly in Table 2). Alleles: 1022C>T frequency 0.168 estimated (Hardy-Weinberg), 16.7% in Koreans in cited reference.
