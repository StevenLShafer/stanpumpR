# Bertilsson 1986: autoinduction of carbamazepine epoxidation (review of the authors' stable-isotope studies)

## Study
A short review (4 pages, *J Clin Pharmacol* 1986;26:459-462, a symposium issue on stable isotopes in pharmacology) of the
Karolinska group's own carbamazepine (CBZ) work. No new data; it gathers four earlier study sets:

1. **Early single-dose work** (PDF p1-2, printed 459-460): 8 healthy subjects, single oral CBZ, mean half-life 36 h
   (range 24-46 h). In 4 patients, single-dose mean half-life 36 h; after 15-21 days of multiple dosing the
   post-steady-state half-life was 21 h (mean).
2. **Three children** aged 10-13 y with new complex partial seizures (PDF p2-3; original: Bertilsson 1980, Clin Pharmacol
   Ther 27:83). An equimolar CBZ + tetradeuterium CBZ (CBZ-D4) single dose, followed for 4 days, then maintenance CBZ
   from day 5; CBZ-D4 replaced part of a maintenance dose on three later occasions. Assay: GC mass fragmentography
   (m/z 236 CBZ, 240 CBZ-D4, internal standard 10,11-dihydro-CBZ m/z 238; Fig. 1). No isotope effect (Table I; also
   shown in rats).
3. **Two adults with alcohol abuse** treated for withdrawal (PDF p2-4): 600 mg on admission as 300 mg CBZ + 300 mg
   CBZ-D4 in suspension, then 200 mg CBZ tablets tid (8, 14, 20 h) for about 5 days, then a single 300 mg CBZ test dose
   7 days after the last maintenance dose (Fig. 3).
4. **Metabolic pathways** (PDF p4, Table II, from Eichelbaum et al. 1985, Clin Pharmacokinet 10:80): 15N-CBZ in
   patients; clearance to each metabolite in three groups.

Weights not given; doses are absolute (mg). Plasma (total) CBZ throughout.

No compartmental model. Reported quantities:

The clearance unit is misprinted: 0.693 x 1.17 / 25.4 = 0.0319 L/kg/h (derived), and Fig. 2's axis runs 0-0.07 L/kg/h,
so the printed values are x10^-2 L/kg/h (3.19 = 0.0319 L/kg/h = 31.9 mL/kg/h, derived).

**Autoinduction time course (children)**: CBZ-D4 clearance had already risen by day 6 (the second maintenance dose); it
had doubled by days 21-36 of the study (PDF p2, printed 460), which is 17-32 days of treatment (abstract, PDF p1;
dosing began on day 5), and did not rise further over the next months (four months in the abstract, five in the
body). Fig. 2 (PDF p3): clearance about 0.024-0.03 L/kg/h before treatment and about 0.047-0.066 L/kg/h induced
(read off the figure; derived ratio about 1.8-2.8 by patient).

**Adults (Fig. 3, PDF p3; text PDF p3-4)**: CBZ-D4 half-life after the first 600 mg dose 20 and 26 h (patients 1, 2);
post-steady-state CBZ half-life after stopping on day 5, 23 h in both; single 300 mg test dose one week later, 46 and
45 h. Cited single-dose half-lives in alcoholics vs healthy controls, 33 and 40 h (Pynnönen 1978).

**Table II** (PDF p4, printed 462; mean ± SD, mL/h/kg):

| Group | n | Total CBZ CL | → trans-CBZ-diol | → 9-OH-CBZ | → 3-OH-CBZ | → 2-OH-CBZ |

derived). The epoxide-diol pathway carries most of the induction.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
No PD measured. No therapeutic range, ke0 or concentration-effect relation. The authors note (PDF p4) that the
CBZ-10,11-epoxide rises with induction and has its own effect in trigeminal neuralgia (citing Tomson 1984).

## Covariates
Time on treatment (autoinduction, complete within about a month in children, within the first doses in the two adults,
gone within a week of stopping); co-medication with other antiepileptics (Table II, group 3, a further x2 over
monotherapy). Alcoholism was judged not to explain the early induction (single-dose half-lives 33 vs 40 h in
alcoholics vs controls, cited).

## Caveats
- A review of the authors' earlier work; n = 3 children and 2 adults for the within-subject autoinduction data.
- Table I clearance unit misprinted (x10^-2 L/kg/h; see above).
- Time-course numbers differ between abstract and body only in counting (17-32 days of treatment = days 21-36 of the
  study) and in the length of follow-up (four vs five months).
- The adult "clearance doubles" is inferred from half-lives (23 vs 45-46 h); volume was not reported for them, so the
  half-life ratio equals the clearance ratio only if volume is unchanged.
- Table II compares different people (healthy volunteers vs patients), so its x2.8 is not a within-subject fold.
- All clearances are apparent oral.
