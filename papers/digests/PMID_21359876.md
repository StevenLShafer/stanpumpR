# Boyce 2011: duloxetine placental transfer and milk, single case

## Study
Case report (PDF pp1-3, printed 169-172). One multiparous woman, 31.4 y, 57 kg, recurrent melancholic depression,
on duloxetine hydrochloride (Cymbalta) 60 mg once daily throughout her third pregnancy and lactation. Healthy male
infant, 3.00 kg, 40 weeks; Apgar 9 and 10; no withdrawal signs. At birth: maternal and cord serum (4 mL each). Day
18 postpartum (infant 4.5 kg): milk samples (5 mL) before the morning dose and 8 more over 22.5 h (two split
fore/hind collections at about 3.16 and 7.16 h); maternal and infant heparinised plasma at 7.6 h after the dose.
Assay: validated HPLC (Briggs 2009 method); creamatocrit by Lucas 1978. Non-compartmental analysis (TopFit 2.0);
infant dose by the Bennett 1996 method with milk intake 0.15 L/kg/day.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Not an opioid. No concentration-effect data. Clinical end points only: no neonatal withdrawal or neurobehavioural
effects at birth; healthy infant at 18 days; mother without depressive symptoms.

## Covariates
None. Comparisons (PDF p3): cord/maternal 0.12 is said to be low against venlafaxine 0.72, desvenlafaxine 1.08 and
most SSRIs 0.33-0.86, similar to paroxetine 0.15 (all from Rampono 2009, a different cohort). RID 0.81% is higher
than Lobo 2008 (0.14%, women who had stopped breastfeeding) and similar to Briggs 2009 (0.82%).

## Caveats
n = 1. Delivery samples have no recorded time after dose. Milk study at 18 days postpartum, not in pregnancy. No
gestational-change data at all. Industry-funded (Eli Lilly); first author on a duloxetine advisory board.
