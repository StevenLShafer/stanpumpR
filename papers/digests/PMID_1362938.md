# Cook 1992: oral S-methamphetamine kinetics before and after 13 days of daily dosing

## Study
- 8 paid male volunteers familiar with recreational amphetamine use; weight 73.5 +/- 2.9 (SD) kg, age 19-32 y, healthy, abstinent from drugs 2 weeks before and throughout (EMIT urine screens) (PDF p2, printed p857, Subjects).
- Day 1: single oral challenge dose of S-(+)-methamphetamine-d3 hydrochloride (deuterium on the chain end), weighed into a gelatin capsule, swallowed with 50 mL water; then 13 days of 10 mg each morning of Desoxyn Gradumet (slow-release S-methamphetamine HCl); day 15: second challenge dose (p857).
- Two challenge dose levels: 0.125 and 0.250 mg/kg (of the hydrochloride); 6 subjects per level, 4 took part in both, about 3 months apart (p857).
- Plasma sampling -1, 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2, 2.5, 3, 3.5, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10, 24, 28, 32, 48 h; saliva and urine also collected (p857).
- Assay: GC/MS, methane positive chemical ionisation, pentafluorobenzoyl derivatives; d5-methamphetamine (and d6-amphetamine) internal standards; standards 1-25 ng/mL; replicate SD 1-7% for methamphetamine (p857). Plasma, not whole blood.
- Model: one compartment, first-order input with lag time, fitted per subject with ADAPT II; a two-compartment model "gave a poor fit" (PDF p4, printed p859). Urine: cumulative-excretion fit with SAS NLIN.

## Pharmacodynamics and end points
Not an opioid paper. No ke0, EC50 or effect model; subjective and cardiovascular effects were monitored but reported elsewhere (Perez-Reyes 1991, ref 14). The authors conclude that tolerance to the heart-rate effect is not explained by changed kinetics, since the only significant change (higher C1 and Cmax at 0.250 mg/kg on day 15, about 8.5% and 12.5%) would raise, not lower, concentrations (abstract; p859-860). No MEAC or potency ratio.

## Covariates
None modelled. Dose affected % excreted unchanged (54% low vs 35% high on day 1); renal clearance depended on urine pH, flow and dose (p860). Urine pH not controlled.

## Caveats
- Small n (6 per dose level), males only, recreational users, mean weight only (per-group weights not given).
- Oral only: clearance is apparent (F assumed 1); dose expressed as hydrochloride and the dose basis for clearance not stated.
- Deuterated isotopomer; authors argue no isotope effect on the day 1 vs 15 comparison.
- Day 15 values are on a background of ~6 ng/mL unlabelled methamphetamine from Gradumet (p858); day 1 values are the drug-naive (2-week abstinent) estimates.
- Table 2 (power analysis, p861) is badly scrambled in the text layer and was not used.
